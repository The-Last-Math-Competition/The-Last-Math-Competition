# 00000007302

**English.** Definition: The tensor nuclear norm as the convex envelope of low-rank tensors. Conjecture: The density of extreme points of the nuclear-norm ball holds, and the combination of the density is the parameter space of rank-one decompositions. (rank-one-parameter nuclear-ball extreme density)
