\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the asymptotic dimension of
$\mathrm{Conf}_n(\mathbb{R}^d)$ is exactly $dn - 1$, coinciding
with the coarse dimension, and the fractal correction term is
always zero.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Configuration spaces of Euclidean space are open manifolds of
dimension $dn$: the ordered configuration is the open set
$\{x_i \ne x_j\} \subset \mathbb{R}^{dn}$, and the unordered
quotient is a $dn$-manifold (the free $S_n$-action is by
diffeomorphisms).  The asymptotic dimension of
$\mathbb{R}^k$ is $k$, and asdim is homotopy-invariant for these
spaces.  At the smallest instances:
\[
\operatorname{asdim} \mathrm{Conf}_1(\mathbb{R}) =
\operatorname{asdim} \mathbb{R} = 1 \ne 0 = dn - 1;
\]
\[
\mathrm{Conf}_2(\mathbb{R}) = \{x \ne y\} = \{x < y\} \cup \{x > y\},
\quad (x, y) \mapsto (x, y - x):
\]
each component is linearly homeomorphic to $(0,\infty) \times
\mathbb{R} \cong \mathbb{R}^2$, so
$\operatorname{asdim} \mathrm{Conf}_2(\mathbb{R}) = 2 \ne 1 =
dn - 1$.  The correct value is $dn$ --- always strictly larger
than the claimed $dn - 1$.  Since the conjecture explicitly fixes
the fractal correction term to \emph{zero}, the off-by-one failure
at the base case cannot be repaired within its own terms.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the dimension comparisons at the two smallest
instances; that $\operatorname{asdim} \mathbb{R}^k = k$ and that

$dn - 1$ (with zero correction) is refuted at the base cases and
