# 00000007847

**English.** Definition: The approximation threshold of randomized rounding: the independent rounding ratio r(A) for the LP relaxation of the integer program max{cx : Ax <= b, x in {0,1}^n}, as a function of the row and column counts of A. Conjecture: The expectation of r(A) is exactly 1 - (1-1/m)^m with m the number of constraints, and no deterministic rounding exceeds 1 - 1/e; the randomized advantage vanishes at m = 2 and is strictly positive and increasing toward e^{-1} for m at least 3. (randomized rounding exact expectation)

**中文。** 定义：随机舍入的近似阈值：整数规划 max{cx: Ax≤b, x∈{0,1}^n} 的 LP 松弛的独立舍入比值 r(A)（A 的行数与列数的函数）。猜想：r(A) 的期望的精确刻画为 1-(1-1/m)^m（m 为约束数），且任何确定性舍入的比值不超过 1-1/e；随机化优势的分离在 m=2 时为零，m≥3 时严格为正且递增趋于 e^{-1}。
