# 00000000228

**English.** Definition: Stern's diatomic sequence satisfies s(0) = 0, s(1) = 1, s(2n) = s(n), s(2n+1) = s(n) + s(n+1). Conjecture: The distribution of s(n²)/3^{log₂ n} converges to an explicit fractal limit which is continuous and nowhere differentiable; the spectral dimension of the fractal is explicitly related to the turning density of the path of n² on the diatomic tree. (Stern square fractal limit)

**中文。** 定义：Stern 双原子序列满足 s(0)=0、s(1)=1、s(2n)=s(n)、s(2n+1)=s(n)+s(n+1)。猜想：s(n²)/3^{log₂n} 的分布收敛到显式分形极限且极限函数连续而无处可微；分形的谱维数与 n² 在双原子树上的路径的转折密度显式相关。（Stern 平方分形极限）
