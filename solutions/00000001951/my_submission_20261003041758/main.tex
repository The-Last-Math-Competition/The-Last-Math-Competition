\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the smallest index of a proper subgroup of
$\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ ($n \ge 3$) is $2^n \binom{n}{2}$.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation at $n = 3$}

The classical map $\mathrm{Aut}(F_3) \to \mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb{Z})$
(the action on $H_1(F_3,\mathbb{Z})$) kills inner automorphisms and
descends to $\mathrm{Out}(F_3)$. Composing with the determinant
$\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb{Z}) \to \{\pm 1\}$ --- surjective via
$\mathrm{diag}(-1,1,1)$ --- gives a surjective homomorphism to a group
of order 2, whose kernel is a proper subgroup of index 2. Hence the
smallest index is $\le 2 < 24 = 2^3\binom{3}{2}$: the claimed formula
fails at $n = 3$.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} performs the exact arithmetic ($2^3\binom{3}{2}
= 24$ vs the index-2 kernel). The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1)
certifies $24$, $2 < 24$, and $2 \ne 24$; both audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}. The determinant construction
and its descent are classical and cited.

\section{Boundary}

Only the claimed smallest-index formula at $n = 3$ is refuted; the CSP
clause is not addressed.
