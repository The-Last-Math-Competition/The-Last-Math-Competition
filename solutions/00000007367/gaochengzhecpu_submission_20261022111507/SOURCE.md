# 00000007367

**English.** Definition: The average-vector-field method with energy conservation. Conjecture: The exact identity of discrete gradients holds, and the realization of the identity is the averaging operator. (averaged-operator AVF energy conservation)

**中文。** 定义：平均向量场方法：能量守恒。猜想：AVF 的保能量：方法的离散梯度的精确恒等且等的实现为平均算子。（AVF 平均算子保能量离散梯度方法精确恒等实现）
