\begin{abstract}
The bilateral full shift on three symbols is a
surjective sliding-block automorphism with minimal
radius one. Its actual dynamical cylinder covering
number at time $n$ is $3^n$, giving topological entropy
at least $\log3>\log2$. This violates the proposed
bound $h\leq r\log2$ under the source's unrestricted
finite-alphabet statement. The Lean proof directly
uses Mathlib's topological entropy, not a word-count
quantity merely named entropy.
\end{abstract}

\section*{The actual compact system and local rules}
Let $\mathcal A=\{0,1,2\}$ have its discrete topology,
and use the full configuration space
\[
                      X=\mathcal A^{\mathbb Z}
\]
with its product topology and product uniformity.
It is compact. This is the full shift, also a shift
of finite type with an empty list of forbidden words.
The exact bilingual source does not restrict the
alphabet to two symbols.

Define $F:X\to X$ and $G:X\to X$ by
\[
                  F(x)_k=x_{k+1},\qquad G(x)_k=x_{k-1}.
\]
They are continuous because each output coordinate
is a coordinate projection. Direct substitution
gives $FG=GF=\operatorname{id}$, so $F$ is a
homeomorphism and is surjective. It commutes with
the underlying shift, being that shift itself;
therefore it is an automorphism of this full shift.

A radius-$r$ sliding-block rule uses the symmetric
window $k-r,\ldots,k+r$ to produce output at $k$.
For $F$, the radius-one rule sends $(a,b,c)$ to
$c$; the inverse rule sends it to $a$. Thus both
directions have radius one.

This is also the true minimum radius. Let $x$ be
the all-zero configuration and let $y$ be zero
except that $y_1=1$. They agree at coordinate zero,
but $F(x)_0=0$ and $F(y)_0=1$. A radius-zero rule
could not distinguish them. Hence the minimal
radius of $F$ is exactly one, not merely a padded
choice of a larger window.

\section*{Actual dynamical covering numbers}
Use the genuine uniform entourage
\[
                      U=\{(x,y):x_0=y_0\}.
\]
It is an entourage because the alphabet is discrete
and the zero-coordinate projection is uniformly
continuous. The actual iterates satisfy
$F^j(x)_k=x_{k+j}$ for every $j\geq0$.
Consequently the $n$-step dynamical ball is exactly
\[
 B_n(x,U)=\{y:(F^j x,F^j y)\in U\ (0\leq j<n)\}
       =\{y:y_j=x_j\ (0\leq j<n)\}.
\]
Thus these dynamical balls are precisely the actual
length-$n$ cylinders in coordinates $0,\ldots,n-1$.

Every word $w\in\mathcal A^n$ extends to a genuine
bilateral configuration by setting all coordinates
outside this block to zero. Reading the block back
recovers $w$. Hence all $3^n$ words occur in the
full system, not just in a proposed counting model.

Let $r_n(U)$ be the minimal size of a finite family
of such dynamical balls covering all of $X$.
In any cover, each center has one length-$n$ word,
and that ball covers only configurations with that
same word. Since every word occurs, the map from
centers to their words must be surjective; therefore
there are at least $3^n$ centers.

Conversely, choose the zero-extended configuration
for every word. These form a cover of all $X$,
and the extension map is injective because reading
recovers the word. This cover has exactly $3^n$
centers. We have proved the genuine minimum
\[
                             r_n(U)=3^n.
\]
The equality even holds at $n=0$, when one ball
covers the nonempty configuration space.

\section*{The genuine entropy obstruction}
For the compact uniform system, Bowen--Dinaburg
topological entropy is
\[
 h_{\rm top}(F)=\sup_{V\in\mathcal U_X}
       \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log r_n(V).
\]
Applying this definition to the actual entourage $U$
and its exact minimum covering number gives
\[
           h_{\rm top}(F)\geq\log3>\log2=1\cdot\log2.
\]
Only this lower bound is needed; the formal proof
does not claim an unproved upper bound on the
total entropy. All logarithms use the same base,
chosen to be the natural base in Lean.

\section*{Conclusion, formalization and reproducibility}
\begin{theorem}
A surjective sliding-block automorphism of minimal
radius one need not satisfy $h\leq\log2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual three-symbol shift $F$ has minimal radius
one and genuine topological entropy at least
$\log3>\log2$, by the covering argument above.
\end{proof}
This refutes the first asserted bound as written.
A binary-alphabet restriction would be an additional
hypothesis, absent from both source versions.
The equality-family and entropy-spectrum clauses
need not be separately refuted once the proposed
universal inequality is false.
