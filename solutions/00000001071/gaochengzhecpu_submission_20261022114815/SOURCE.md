# 00000001071

**English.** Definition: The quadratic-residue graph (adjacency by ±1). Conjecture: The deviation of the smallest eigenvalue of QR(p) from the limit −2√p is at most p^{−1/4+ε}, and the spectral gap is uniform via Weil sums (QR spectral uniformity).

**中文。** 定义：二次剩余图(±1 邻接)。猜想：QR(p) 的最小特征值的极限 −2√p 的偏差 ≤ p^{−1/4+ε} 且谱间隙由 Weil 和均匀(QR 谱均匀)。
