# 00000004228

**English.** Definition: The tree decomposition of the Euler characteristic is the combinatorial formula expressing it through tree counts. Conjecture: The correction term is exactly the product of the torsion coefficients of each dimension, and when all torsion vanishes the Euler characteristic equals the sign-alternating sum of tree counts. (Euler-tree corrected torsion-product formula)

**中文。** 定义：欧拉特征的树分解指以树计数表达的组合公式。猜想：修正项恰为各维挠系数的乘积,挠全部消失时欧拉特征等于树计数的符号交错和。（欧拉-树修正挠积公式）
