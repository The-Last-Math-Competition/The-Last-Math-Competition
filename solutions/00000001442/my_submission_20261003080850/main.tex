\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the expected cover time of random walks on
bipartite random graphs is $2|E| \cdot H_{|V|/2}$ with an explicit
correction (the exact cover time).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

At the complete bipartite instance $K_{10,10}$ ($|E| = 100$,
$|V| = 20$) the conjectured value is
\[
2|E| \cdot H_{|V|/2} = 200 \cdot H_{10} = 200 \cdot \frac{7381}{2520}
= \frac{36905}{63} \approx 585.79 .
\]
The true expected cover time is computed exactly by solving the
coverage-state Markov chain --- states $(i, j, \text{side})$ with $i$ =
covered left vertices, $j$ = covered right vertices (240 non-absorbing
states) --- by Gaussian elimination over exact fractions:
\[
E[\mathrm{cover}] = \frac{132922481672135822466559649}{1926224885145229235070504}
\approx 69.007 ,
\]
with zero residual on all $240$ equations.  This matches the
simulation ($\approx 68$--$69$) while the formula overshoots by a
factor of $\approx 8.5$: it is neither the exact cover time nor close
to it.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} builds the $240$-state linear system over exact
fractions, solves it by Gaussian elimination, re-verifies zero
residual on every equation, and compares with the formula anchors.
The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
$H_{10} = 7381/2520$, the instance anchors $|E| = 100$ and $2|E| =
200$, the formula value $36905/63$, and the comparisons
$36905 > 63 \cdot 500$ and $H_{10} < 3$; all five audited theorems
report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the formula-side arithmetic; the exact
expectation is carried by the script's exact-fraction elimination
with zero-residual verification (an exact certificate).  The formula
fails as an exact value and as an approximation at this instance.
