# 00000006561

**English.** Definition: Symplectic splitting is the scheme of composing symplectic maps. Conjecture: Symplecticity is preserved under composition; the verification of the closed commutative diagram is pullback preservation; and the closed commutative diagram is verified in the composition category. (pullback preservation of symplectic composition)

**中文。** 定义：保辛分裂为辛映射复合的格式。猜想：辛性在复合下保持；且交换图闭合的验证为拉回保持；且闭合的交换图在合成范畴验证。（辛复合拉回保持）
