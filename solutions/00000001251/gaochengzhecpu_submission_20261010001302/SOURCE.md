# 00000001251

**English.** Definition: k-exclusion is the exclusion process with capacity k per site. Conjecture: The stationary state of k-exclusion is of product type only when k equals one; and the isolation of product-form stationarity is shown by the three-point deficit of correlation functions. (product stationary state isolated)

**中文。** 定义：k-步排斥为每格容量为 k 的排斥过程。猜想：k-排斥的稳态为乘积型仅在 k 等于一时；且乘积性的孤立由关联函数的三点亏损给出。（乘积稳态孤立）
