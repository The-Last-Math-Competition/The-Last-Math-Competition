# 00000009634

**English.** Definition: Identifiability of hidden Markov models: the rank of the Hankel matrix H(w) of the output process is the minimal realization dimension. Conjecture: The minimal hidden state count of a sofic shift's output process equals the rank of its Hankel matrix, decidable in polynomial time from observed words of length 2n+1; and realizations with rank deficit exactly one (n-1) form a geometrically isolated exceptional family. (Hankel rank identifiability)

**中文。** 定义：隐 Markov 模型的辨识：输出过程的 Hankel 矩阵 H(w) 的秩为最小实现维数。猜想：sofic 移位输出过程的最小隐状态数等于其 Hankel 矩阵的秩且可由长度 2n+1 的观测词在多项式时间判定；秩亏缺一（恰好 n−1）的实现为几何孤立的例外族。（Hankel 秩可辨识）
