\begin{abstract}
For the source's order direction, the qubit completely
depolarizing channel $D$ has a strict successor: the completely
dephasing channel $Z$. We prove $D\preceq_{\rm ln}Z$ for
every finite input ensemble, including noncommuting states,
and $Z\not\preceq_{\rm ln}D$. Thus $D$ is not even a maximal
element. The obstruction survives quotienting by mutual
less-noisy equivalence. Lean checks actual complex matrices,
normalized Kraus families and spectral von Neumann entropy.
\end{abstract}

\section*{The order and the two actual channels}
For an ensemble $\mathcal E=(p_i,\rho_i)$ of qubit density
matrices, let
\[
 \chi(\mathcal E;\Phi)=
 S\left(\sum_i p_i\Phi(\rho_i)\right)
 -\sum_i p_iS(\Phi(\rho_i)),\qquad
 S(A)=-\sum_j\lambda_j(A)\log\lambda_j(A).
\]
The weights are nonnegative and sum to one; $0\log0=0$.
We follow the exact direction in the conjecture:
$\Phi\preceq_{\rm ln}\Psi$ means
$\chi(\mathcal E;\Phi)\leq\chi(\mathcal E;\Psi)$ for every
finite ensemble. Logarithms are natural, which does not
change the order. The standard definitions are recalled in
\cite[Sections 5.2 and 5.3.3]{watrous}.

For any complex $2\times2$ matrix $A$, define
\[
 D(A)=\frac{\tr A}{2}I_2,\qquad
 Z(A)=\begin{pmatrix}A_{00}&0\\0&A_{11}\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Both are channels on the same input and output spaces. Let
$E_{ab}$ be the matrix units, $a,b\in\{0,1\}$. A Kraus
family for $Z$ is $E_{00},E_{11}$. A Kraus family for $D$
contains two copies of $E_{ab}/2$ for every pair $(a,b)$.
For each family,
\[
 \sum_j K_j^*K_j=I_2,\qquad
 \Phi(A)=\sum_jK_jAK_j^*\quad\hbox{for every complex }A.
\]
These identities are checked in Lean. They give trace
preservation by cyclicity of trace, and complete positivity
because every matrix amplification is a sum of conjugations
by $I\otimes K_j$. This is the standard finite Kraus
characterization, as in \cite[Section 2.2]{watrous}.

\section*{A strict comparison, for all input ensembles}
Every density state has trace one, so $D(\rho_i)=I_2/2$.
Consequently $\chi(\mathcal E;D)=0$ for every ensemble.

For $Z$, write $a_i=(\rho_i)_{00}$ and $b_i=(\rho_i)_{11}$.
These entries are real and nonnegative because $\rho_i$
is positive semidefinite. The output is
$\operatorname{diag}(a_i,b_i)$, and its actual eigenvalues
are $a_i,b_i$, with multiplicity. Put $f(x)=-x\log x$ for
$x>0$ and $f(0)=0$. The function $f$ is concave on
$[0,\infty)$. Finite Jensen inequalities give
\[
 \sum_i p_i f(a_i)\leq f\left(\sum_i p_i a_i\right),
 \qquad
 \sum_i p_i f(b_i)\leq f\left(\sum_i p_i b_i\right).
\]
Adding them proves $\chi(\mathcal E;Z)\geq0$. No
commutativity or diagonal assumption was made on the input
states. Thus $D\preceq_{\rm ln}Z$.

Now use the equiprobable ensemble $E_{00},E_{11}$.
Dephasing leaves each pure state unchanged. Its individual
output entropies are zero and its average output is $I_2/2$,
of entropy $\log2$. Hence
\[
 \chi(\mathcal E;Z)=\log2>0=\chi(\mathcal E;D),
\]
so $Z\not\preceq_{\rm ln}D$.

\begin{theorem}
The completely depolarizing channel is not a maximal element
of the less-noisy relation in the direction defined in the
source, even after identifying mutually equivalent channels.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two channel certificates and the strict comparison
$D\preceq_{\rm ln}Z$, $Z\not\preceq_{\rm ln}D$ exhibit a
strict successor. Therefore $D$ is not maximal. The same
strict comparison holds between their equivalence classes.
\end{proof}

There is also a secondary issue with a partial order on
literal channels. The two reset channels
$R_a(A)=\tr(A)E_{aa}$, $a=0,1$, are distinct and have
Kraus families $E_{a0},E_{a1}$. Both give Holevo information
zero on every ensemble. Thus $R_0\preceq_{\rm ln}R_1$ and
$R_1\preceq_{\rm ln}R_0$, despite $R_0\ne R_1$.
Quotienting repairs this secondary failure of antisymmetry,
but cannot repair the strict-successor obstruction above.
The separate broadcast-capacity assertion is not needed
for this disproof and is not assessed here.

\section*{Formalization, checks and reproduction}
Density states in Lean are actual complex $2\times2$
matrices with Mathlib positive semidefiniteness and trace
one. Entropy is defined from Mathlib's Hermitian eigenvalues.
The diagonal entropy formula is proved using spectral
membership and trace invariance under unitary conjugation,
so Shannon entropy is derived rather than assigned in place
of von Neumann entropy.

The channel predicate uses the exact normalized finite
Kraus characterization. The displayed formulas are verified
on arbitrary complex input matrices. The ensemble type
has arbitrary finite size, genuine density inputs and
arbitrary nonnegative real weights of sum one. The Jensen
argument therefore quantifies over all allowed ensembles.
The explicitly constructed orthogonal ensemble witnesses
strictness.

The main theorem is \texttt{depolarize\_not\_maximal}:
it bundles the actual channel certificates and both order
comparisons. A separate theorem proves failure of
antisymmetry for the two actual reset maps. No general
capacity formula or quantum data-processing theorem is
assumed in the proof.
