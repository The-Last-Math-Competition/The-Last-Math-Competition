\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} there exist two graphs whose counts of all
connected subgraphs coincide while their adjacency spectra differ;
the minimal vertex number for such a separation is $6$ or $7$, and
all cases can be completely enumerated.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

At $n = 5$ take $G_1 = K_3 \cup 2K_1$ (triangle plus two isolates)
and $G_2 = P_3 \cup K_2$ (path plus an edge).  Connected induced
subgraphs by order: $G_1$ has $5$ vertices, $3$ edges, exactly one
connected triple (the triangle), and nothing larger; $G_2$ has $5$
vertices, $3$ edges ($2 + 1$), exactly one connected triple (the
path), and nothing larger.  The count vectors $(5, 3, 1, 0, 0)$
coincide.  The spectra do not:
\[
p_1(x) = x^2(x-2)(x+1)^2, \qquad
p_2(x) = x(x^2 - 2)(x^2 - 1):
\]
$p_1(2) = 0$ (the triangle's eigenvalue $2$) while $p_2(2) =
2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 = 12 \ne 0$, and the multiplicity of $-1$ is $2$
versus $1$.  Since exhaustive enumeration over all
$2^{\binom{n}{2}}$ graphs at $n = 2, 3, 4$ yields no separation,
the minimal vertex number is exactly $5$ --- not ``$6$ or $7$''.
(The existence clause survives; the minimal-number and enumeration
claims are refuted.  Under the non-induced reading the sweep finds
no separation up to $n = 7$ at all.)

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} exhibits the certified pair (identical
counts, distinct eigenvalues) and exhausts $n = 2, 3, 4$.  The
Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
\texttt{count\_vertices}, \texttt{count\_edges},
\texttt{count\_triples}, \texttt{spec1\_two} ($(2-2) = 0$),
\texttt{spec2\_no\_two}/\texttt{polys\_differ}
($2 \cdot (4-2) \cdot (4-1) = 12 \ne 0$),
\texttt{multiplicity\_gap} ($2 = 1+1$ vs $1 \ne 1+1$), and
\texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All eight audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the count identity and the spectral anchors at
the certified pair; the $n \le 4$ enumeration and the eigenvalue
numerics are in the script.  The minimal-number claim is refuted.
