# Conjecture 00000007159 — Resolution Package

## Verdict: **FALSE** (Disproved)

Conjecture `00000007159` proposes that for multiplicative inequalities of matrices (associated with matrix multiplicative trace / min-max extrema, abbreviated MMT), the complete system of trace-product inequalities holds and is generated by the integer simplex lattice $\Delta_k^{(m)} = \{\alpha = (\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_m) \in \mathbb{N}^m : \alpha_1 + \dots + \alpha_m = k\}$.

We prove that this conjecture is **false** across **every** canonical mathematical formulation of a simplex-lattice multiplicative trace-product system—ordered-product positivity, lattice-midpoint log-convexity, Frobenius-norm (Hermitian sandwich) log-convexity, lattice-semigroup trace-product submultiplicativity, word-grouping / Lieb–Thirring extrema, and both ordered and word-averaged (Hurwitz/BMV) symmetric matrix Muirhead majorization systems—already for $2 \times 2$ strictly positive definite symmetric integer Gram matrices $A_i = L_i^T L_i \in M_2(\mathbb{Z})$ with $\det L_i \neq 0$.

## Mathematical Summary

For $m$ positive semidefinite matrices $A_1, \dots, A_m \succeq 0$ and $\alpha \in \Delta_k^{(m)}$, let $F(\alpha) = \mathrm{tr}(A_1^{\alpha_1} \cdots A_m^{\alpha_m})$ denote the ordered product trace and $G(\alpha) = \|A_1^{\alpha_1} \cdots A_m^{\alpha_m}\|_F^2 = \mathrm{tr}((A_1^{\alpha_1} \cdots A_m^{\alpha_m})^T (A_1^{\alpha_1} \cdots A_m^{\alpha_m}))$ denote the squared Frobenius norm.

### 1. Why Commuting and Two-Matrix Regimes Look Deceptive (Positive Theorems)
- **Commuting PSD matrices ($m \ge 1$ arbitrary, all $n, k$):** When $A_1, \dots, A_m$ commute, every simplex-lattice system holds simultaneously: $F(\alpha) \ge 0$, midpoint log-convexity $F(\beta)^2 \le F(\alpha) F(\gamma)$ whenever $2\beta = \alpha + \gamma$ in $\Delta_k^{(m)}$, submultiplicativity $F(\alpha + \beta) \le F(\alpha) F(\beta)$, word invariance $\mathrm{tr}(W) = F(\alpha(W))$, and Muirhead majorization monotonicity along elementary lattice transfers.
- **Noncommuting two-matrix hierarchy ($m = 2$, all $n$):** Even without commutativity, for arbitrary Gram PSD matrices $A = X^T X$ and $B = Y^T Y$:
  - Degree-$2$ positivity and submultiplicativity $0 \le \mathrm{tr}(AB) \le \mathrm{tr}(A)\,\mathrm{tr}(B)$ hold;
  - Degree-$4$ Lieb–Thirring and degree-$5$ word grouping hold via exact commutator identities $2\,\mathrm{tr}(A^2 B^2) - 2\,\mathrm{tr}((AB)^2) = \|[A, B]\|_F^2 \ge 0$ and $\mathrm{tr}(A^3 B^2) - \mathrm{tr}(A^2 B A B) = \|X [A, B]\|_F^2 \ge 0$;
  - **Universal log-convexity on $\Delta_k^{(2)}$:** $\mathrm{tr}(A^{p+1} B^{q+1})^2 \le \mathrm{tr}(A^{p+2} B^q)\,\mathrm{tr}(A^p B^{q+2})$ holds for all $p, q \ge 0$, implying two-matrix Ordered Muirhead Schur-convexity across $\Delta_k^{(2)}$.

### 2. Universal Breakdown in $M_2(\mathbb{Z})$ Across All Candidate Systems
Every candidate system collapses on $2 \times 2$ strictly positive definite integer Gram matrices $A_i = L_i^T L_i \in M_2(\mathbb{Z})$ ($\det L_i \neq 0$):
- **Failure of lattice-semigroup submultiplicativity ($m = 2, k = 4$ and $m = 3, k = 3$):** For unimodular $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, $\mathrm{tr}(A^2 B^2) = 11$ exceeds $\mathrm{tr}(AB)^2 = 3^2 = 9$. For $m = 3$ with $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, $B = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $C = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 3 \\ 3 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, $\mathrm{tr}(ABC) = 79$ exceeds $\mathrm{tr}(AB)\,\mathrm{tr}(C) = 77$.
- **Failure of interior positivity ($m = 3, k = 3$):** At $\alpha = (1, 1, 1) \in \Delta_3^{(3)}$, unimodular SPD matrices $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, $B = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $C = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 \\ -3 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$ give $\mathrm{tr}(ABC) = -2$ (strictly negative).
- **Failure of simplex-lattice midpoint log-convexity ($m = 3, k = 4, 5$):** Even when all traces are strictly positive, at $\beta = (1, 2, 1) = \frac{1}{2}((2, 1, 1) + (0, 3, 1)) \in \Delta_4^{(3)}$ we obtain $F(1,2,1)^2 = 36^2 = 1296$, exceeding $F(2,1,1)\,F(0,3,1) = 39 \cdot 33 = 1287$; strictly inside the interior of $\Delta_5^{(3)}$ at $\beta = (2, 1, 2) = \frac{1}{2}((3, 1, 1) + (1, 1, 3))$ with unimodular SPD matrices, $F(2,1,2)^2 = 459^2 = 210681$ exceeds $F(3,1,1)\,F(1,1,3) = 207 \cdot 1014 = 209898$.
- **Failure of Frobenius-sandwich log-convexity ($m = 3, k = 3$):** Even for $G(\alpha) \ge 0$, at $\beta = (1, 1, 1) = \frac{1}{2}((2, 0, 1) + (0, 2, 1)) \in \Delta_3^{(3)}$ with unimodular SPD matrices, $G(1,1,1)^2 = 43^2 = 1849$ exceeds $G(2,0,1)\,G(0,2,1) = 7 \cdot 70 = 490$.
- **Failure of three-matrix Lieb–Thirring / word-grouping extrema ($m = 3, k = 6$):** At $\alpha = (2, 2, 2) \in \Delta_6^{(3)}$, $\mathrm{tr}((ABC)^2) = 193$ and $\|ABC\|_F^2 = 337$ both exceed the grouped trace $\mathrm{tr}(A^2 B^2 C^2) = 175$.
- **Failure of both $S_3$-symmetrized Muirhead systems ($m = 3, k = 5, 6$):** Along $\alpha = (3, 2, 1) \succ (2, 2, 2)$ in $\Delta_6^{(3)}$, Ordered Muirhead gives $M^{\mathrm{ord}}_{(3,2,1)} = -8606$, strictly smaller than $M^{\mathrm{ord}}_{(2,2,2)} = -6900$ (and $147776$ vs. $147792$ on strictly positive SPD traces along $\alpha = (4, 1, 1) \succ (3, 2, 1)$). Along $\alpha = (3, 1, 1) \succ (2, 2, 1)$ in $\Delta_5^{(3)}$ for unimodular SPD matrices, the word-averaged Hurwitz/BMV trace $P^{\mathrm{sym}}_{(3,1,1)} = 7224$ is strictly smaller than $P^{\mathrm{sym}}_{(2,2,1)} = 7360$.
