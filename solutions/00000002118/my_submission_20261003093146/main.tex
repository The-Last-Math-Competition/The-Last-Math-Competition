\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Definition:} the Quicksort cost $Q_n$ is the number of
comparisons. \textit{Conjecture:} the third cumulant is
$\kappa_3(n) = 2n - 6\log n + C$ (with explicit constant $C$); the
linear main coefficient of $\kappa_3$ is $2$.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Quicksort splits at a uniform position $J \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$:
$C_n = (n-1) + C_J + C_{n-1-J}$ with independent subproblems.  Solving
the exact moment recurrences over Fractions gives
\[
\kappa_3(n) = M_3 - 3 M_1 M_2 + 2 M_1^3:
\qquad \kappa_3(50) = 22084.80 \approx 442 \cdot 50,
\]
with the ratio $\kappa_3(n)/n$ \emph{growing}: $0.35, 6.40, 48.2,
133.9, 264.9, 441.7$ at $n = 5, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50$.  The third
cumulant is $\Theta(n^3)$, not the claimed linear form
$2n - 6\log n + C$: the claimed linear main coefficient $2$ is wrong
by two orders of magnitude at $n = 50$, and the gap grows with $n$.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} solves the exact Fraction moment recurrences up
to $n = 54$, tabulates $\kappa_3$ and $\kappa_3/n$, checks the strict
growth of the ratios, and confirms $\kappa_3(50) \approx 22085$ with
ratio $441.7 \pm 0.2$.  The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1)
certifies the claimed main-coefficient anchor $2 \cdot 50 = 100$, the
measured rounded ratio $442 \ne 2$, the comparisons $442 > 100$ and
$442 > 3 \cdot 50$; all four audited theorems report \texttt{does not
depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the claimed-coefficient anchor and the rounded
measured-ratio contrast; the exact $\kappa_3$ values are carried by
the script's Fraction-exact moment recurrences.  The
linear-main-coefficient claim is refuted at $n = 50$.
