\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Definition:} the Hausdorff dimension $d_H(\gamma)$ of LQG
metric spaces is given by the metric construction of the
exponentialized GFF. \textit{Conjecture:}
$d_H(\gamma) = 2 + \gamma^2/4 + \gamma^4/64 + O(\gamma^6)$; the known
value $d_H(\sqrt{8/3}) = 3$ is consistent with this expansion; and
each coefficient of the expansion is explicitly determined by
correlation functions of the gravitational collision operators.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

At the conjecture's own test point $\gamma^2 = 8/3$ --- where
$d_H = 3$ by the Brownian-map identification of the
$\sqrt{8/3}$-LQG metric --- the claimed expansion evaluates (in
ninths) as
\[
2 = \tfrac{18}{9}, \qquad
\frac{\gamma^2}{4} = \frac{8/3}{4} = \tfrac{6}{9}, \qquad
\frac{\gamma^4}{64} = \frac{64/9}{64} = \tfrac{1}{9},
\]
\[
\text{claimed value} = \frac{18 + 6 + 1}{9} = \frac{25}{9}
\approx 2.778 \;\ne\; \frac{27}{9} = 3 .
\]
The ``consistency'' claim is arithmetically false.  Moreover the
claimed first coefficient $\gamma^2/4$ is exactly half the classical
$\gamma^2/2$: the known expansion $d_H(\gamma) = 2 + \gamma^2/2 +
o(\gamma^2)$ at $\gamma^2 = 8/3$ gives $\tfrac{10}{3} + o(4/3) =
\tfrac{30}{9} + o(9) > \tfrac{27}{9}$, the coefficient consistent
with the known value.  The gap $3 - 25/9 = 2/9 \approx 0.222$ cannot
be bridged by the $O(\gamma^6)$ term at this point (scale
$(8/3)^3 = 512/27$ in $\gamma^6$, i.e.\ $512/729$ in ninths ---
orders below $2/9 = 162/729$).
