# 00000005300

**English.** Definition: The stable group and the total module are two structural layers. Conjecture: There exist two FI modules whose stable groups are all isomorphic but whose total modules are not, and the separation is realized explicitly by differences in the finite skeleton before stabilization. (same stable group-different module skeleton separation)

**中文。** 定义：稳定群与整体模指两层结构。猜想：存在两 FI 模的稳定群全部同构而整体模不同构,分离由稳定前的有限骨架差异显式实现。（稳定群同-模异骨架分离）
