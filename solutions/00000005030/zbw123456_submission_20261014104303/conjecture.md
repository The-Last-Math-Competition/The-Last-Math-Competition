# 00000005030

**English.** Definition: Expressive power and convergence laws are two logical properties. Conjecture: There exist two logics of the same expressive power with different convergence-law behavior on random structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of counting extensions. (same expressive power-different convergence law separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：表达力与收敛律指两层逻辑性质。猜想：存在两逻辑表达力相同而随机结构上的收敛律行为不同,分离由计数扩张的显式对实现。（表达力同-收敛律异分离对）
