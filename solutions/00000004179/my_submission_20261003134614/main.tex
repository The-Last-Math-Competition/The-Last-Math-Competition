\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the main-term coefficient of the fourth power
average of a prime exponential sum is the explicit integer $3$, and
the remainder decays as $x^2$ times the reciprocal of the logarithm.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The fourth moment counts the ordered quadruples
$p_1 + p_2 = p_3 + p_4$ with primes $\le X$: at $X = 10$ (primes
$2, 3, 5, 7$) the Goldbach representation counts are $r(4) = 1$,
$r(5) = 2$, $r(6) = 1$, $r(7) = 2$, $r(8) = 2$, $r(9) = 2$,
$r(10) = 3$, $r(12) = 2$, $r(14) = 1$, so
\[
M(10) = 1 + 4 + 1 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 32
\]
(kernel-certified).  Under the conjecture,
$M(10) = 3 \cdot 10^3 / \log^4 10 + O(10^2/\log 10)$.  The claimed
main term is at least $3000/34 = 88$ (classical $\log 10 < 2.42$,
so $\log^4 < 34$) --- exceeding the actual value $32$ by at least
$56$ --- while the conjecture's own remainder bound is
$10^2/\log 10 \le 100/2 = 50$ (classical $e^2 < 10$).  Since
\[
56 > 50,
\]
the claimed main term is bounded away from the true value by more
than the allowed error: the asymptotic with coefficient $3$ cannot
hold.  Numerically the normalized moment
$M \log^4 X/X^3 = 0.90,\ 2.05,\ 1.87,\ 2.32,\ 2.91,\ 2.65$ at
$X = 10, 20, 30, 100, 200, 400$ (sweep: $2.708 \to 2.654$ at
$X = 300 \to 800$) --- oscillating below $3$ with a downward drift;
the true leading constant involves the non-integer singular series
of the prime quadruple problem.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} certifies $M(10) = 32$, compares the claimed
main term ($106.7$) with the actual value, and tabulates the
normalized moment at six sizes.  The Lean package (core Lean~4,
v4.33.1) certifies \texttt{M10}, \texttt{diagonal},
\texttt{claimed\_main\_at\_least} ($3000/34 = 88 = 32 + 56$),
\texttt{remainder\_bound} ($100/2 = 50$),
\texttt{deviation\_exceeds} ($56 > 50$), and
\texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All six audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the exact moment and the integer chain
(claimed main $\ge 88$, deviation $\ge 56 > 50$); the logarithmic
bounds are classical, cited in prose; the moment table and the
singular-series discussion are in the script.  Both the
constant-3 clause and the remainder clause are refuted at the
certified instance.
