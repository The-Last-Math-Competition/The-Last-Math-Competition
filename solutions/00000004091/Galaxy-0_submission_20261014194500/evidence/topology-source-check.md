# Topology reference check

Checked 14 October 2026 from the authors' public primary-source PDFs.

- Allen Hatcher, Algebraic Topology, Chapter 2: https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATch2.pdf . Printed pp. 99–101 explain the graph incidence boundary and cycle kernel; Theorem 2.35 on printed p. 139, proved on p. 140, gives cellular/singular agreement; printed p. 153 explicitly extends that comparison and its boundary formulas to general coefficients. The report uses Q as coefficient field. In this chapter-only PDF, printed p. 139 is PDF page 43 (zero-based index 42), and printed p. 153 is PDF page 57 (index 56).
- Steven Scheirer, Topological complexity of unordered configuration spaces of certain graphs: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.12838 . Printed p. 2 defines the graph configuration space of distinct points and its unordered symmetric-group quotient. The one-particle homeomorphism needed here is proved directly in the report rather than imported from an external special-case assertion.

These references justify the explicit paper bridge. They are not Lean proof dependencies and are not represented as extra axioms.
