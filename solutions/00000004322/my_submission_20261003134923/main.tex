\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the $p$-power distribution of the denominators
of the coefficients of the Artin--Hasse series
$AH_p(x) = \exp(\sum_k x^{p^k}/p^k)$ is the geometric law with
parameter $1/p$, and the exceptional coefficient occurs uniquely at
the exponent $p - 1$.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The defining property of the Artin--Hasse series is its
$p$-integrality (the Dieudonn\'e--Dwork lemma --- the ``corrected
exponential'' of the conjecture's own definition).  Differentiating
the exponent gives $\frac{d}{dx}\sum_k x^{p^k}/p^k = \sum_k
x^{p^k - 1}$ with unit coefficients, whence the recurrence
\[
n \cdot E_n = \sum_{p^k \le n} E_{n - p^k}, \qquad E_0 = 1,
\]
and induction yields $E_n \in \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$ for every $n$:
\emph{every} coefficient has $v_p = 0$.  Exact computation to
$n = 300$ for $p = 2, 3, 5$ confirms zero exceptions:
\[
p = 2:\ 1,\ 1,\ 1,\ \tfrac23,\ \tfrac23,\ \tfrac7{15},\ \dots
\qquad
p = 3:\ 1,\ 1,\ \tfrac12,\ \tfrac12,\ \tfrac38,\ \tfrac7{40},\ \dots
\]
The claimed geometric law with parameter $1/p$ predicts $2$ of the
first $4$ coefficients ($p = 2$) and $2$ of the first $6$ ($p = 3$)
to have $v_p \ge 1$; the observed count is $0$
(kernel-certified).  The ``unique exception at exponent $p-1$'' is
doubly false: $a_{p-1}$ carries no $p$-denominator, and its value
is not unique --- $a_1 = a_2 = 1$ ($p = 2$), $a_2 = a_3 = \tfrac12$
($p = 3$).

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} runs the exact rational recurrence to
$n = 300$ ($p = 2, 3, 5$: all $v_p = 0$), compares the
prediction-vs-observed counts, and checks the non-uniqueness.  The
Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
\texttt{p2\_first}, \texttt{p2\_E3} ($3 \cdot 2/3 = 2$),
\texttt{p2\_count\_zero} ($0 \ne 2$),
\texttt{p2\_no\_unique\_exception}, \texttt{p3\_half\_half},
\texttt{p3\_count\_zero}, and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All
seven audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any
axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the first coefficients, the zero counts, and
the non-uniqueness; the recurrence and the Dieudonn\'e--Dwork
$p$-integrality are classical, cited in prose and verified exactly
by the script.  Both the distribution law and the unique-exception
claim are refuted.
