# 00000000806

**English.** Definition: The spectral gap γ(Ω) = λ₂(Ω) − λ₁(Ω) and its ratio to the first eigenvalue R(Ω) = γ/λ₁. Conjecture: sup R(Ω) is attained by the union of two equal-volume disjoint disks, with value R = 3/4; every connected domain has R strictly smaller.

**中文。** 定义：谱间隙 γ(Ω)=λ₂(Ω)−λ₁(Ω) 与首特征值之比 R(Ω)=γ/λ₁。猜想：sup R(Ω) 由两个等体积不相交圆盘的并取得,值为 R=3/4;一切连通域的 R 严格更小。
