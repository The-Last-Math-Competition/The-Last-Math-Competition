\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the expected condition number of the norm matrix
of random interpolation on smooth varieties is $m^{-1/2}$ ($m$ the
sample count; a negative half power).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The condition number of any nonsingular matrix satisfies
$\kappa = \sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min} \ge 1$ (classical). Hence
$E[\kappa] \ge 1$ for every distribution on nonsingular norm matrices
and every sample count, while the claimed closed form $m^{-1/2} < 1$
for every $m \ge 2$ (at $m = 4$: $1/2$). The claimed closed form sits
strictly below a universal lower bound: it cannot be the expectation of
$\kappa$.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} samples random $4\times 4$ matrices, computes
$\kappa$ by singular values, and confirms $\kappa \ge 1$ in every
sample with mean $\approx 78 \gg 1/2$. The Lean package (core Lean~4,
v4.33.1) certifies the exact anchors ($1 < 2$; $1/2 \ne 1$); all three
audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}. The
condition-number lower bound $\kappa \ge 1$ is classical and cited.

\section{Boundary}

Only the displayed closed form is refuted.
