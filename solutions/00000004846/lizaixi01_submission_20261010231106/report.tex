an actual finitely generated group and subgroup with distortion equivalent
to the rounded polynomial $n^{3/2}$. This refutes the conjunction without
separately resolving its hyperbolic or Baumslag--Solitar assertions.

Let $G$ have eight unrestricted integer coordinates $(p,q,r,s,t,a,b,c)$.
For a second element $(P,Q,R,S,T,A,B,C)$ its product has coordinates
\[
(p+P,q+Q,r+R,s+S+pQ,t+T+qR,a+A,b+B,c+C+pT+sR+aB).
\]
The identity is zero. The inverse coordinates are
\[
(-p,-q,-r,-s+pq,-t+qr,-a,-b,-c+pt+sr-pqr+ab).
\]
Lean proves the actual group laws, rather than assuming them. The integral
Heisenberg group with product $(a,b,c)(A,B,C)=(a+A,b+B,c+C+aB)$ embeds
injectively as the actual subgroup $H$ with $p=q=r=s=t=0$.
The ambient symmetric alphabet consists of the unit elements in the
$p,q,r,a,b$ directions and their inverses, denoted $X,Y,Z,A,B$.
The intrinsic alphabet is $A,B$ and their inverses.
Actual commutator identities and coordinate normal forms prove generation
of every ambient element and every subgroup element.

Use attained minimum lengths in these actual alphabets. The formal
definition uses the least natural length of a representing finite word.
The complete generation theorems eliminate its outside-generated-range
fallback. Finite enumeration of all ambient words of length at most $n$
then gives a finite nonempty subgroup ball and its attained maximum
\[
D(n)=\max\{|h|_H:h\in H,\ |h|_G\leq n\}.
\]
Lean proves minimum attainment, exact ball membership, maximum attainment,
maximality and monotonicity of this genuine distortion function.

Induction on every ambient word of length $L$, including negative letters,
bounds the five weight-one coordinates by $L$, $s,t$ by $L^2$, and $c$ by
$2L^3$. For an embedded subgroup element in radius $n\geq1$, the correction

Consequently
\[
m^3\leq D(10m)^2\quad(m\geq0),\qquad
\lfloor\sqrt{\lfloor n/10\rfloor^3}\rfloor\leq D(n)\quad(n\geq0).
\]
Both inequalities concern genuine minima and attained maxima.
