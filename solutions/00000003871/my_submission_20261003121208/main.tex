\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} $E(G_{\max})\,\beta\pi N/2 - \log N$ converges
to a constant $c_\beta$ strictly increasing in $\beta$, with
$c_2 = 0$ ($\beta$-Hermite maximal gap).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

For $\beta$-repelling eigenvalues the maximal \emph{unfolded} gap
grows like $(\log N)^{1/\beta}$ (extreme-value law) --- not like
$\log N$, which is the Poisson ($\beta = 0$) tail. The raw maximal
gap is therefore $E[G_{\max}] \sim (\log N)^{1/\beta}/N$, and the
conjecture's statistic becomes
\[
\frac{\beta\pi}{2}\,(\log N)^{1/\beta} - \log N .
\]
This \emph{diverges} for every $\beta$: at $\beta = 1$ it equals
$(\tfrac{\pi}{2} - 1)\log N \to +\infty$ (since $\pi > 2$); at
$\beta = 2$ it is $\pi\sqrt{\log N} - \log N \to -\infty$ (log~$N$
beats $\pi\sqrt{\log N}$ once $\sqrt{\log N} > \pi$, i.e. $\log N >
\pi^2 \approx 9.87$; at $N = 2^{15}$, $\ln N = 15\ln 2 = 10.39$,
kernel-anchored via $(22/7)^2 = 484/49 < 10$ and $\ln 2 > 2/3$); at
$\beta = 4$ it crosses at $\log N = (2\pi)^{4/3} = 11.59$ ($N
\approx 108{,}464$) and goes to $-\infty$. No constant $c_\beta$
exists at any $\beta$: ``$c_2 = 0$'' is false, and ``strictly
increasing'' is false wholesale ($c_1 = +\infty$ vs $c_2 = -\infty$
would be \emph{decreasing}). Structurally, the normalization
$\beta\pi N/2$ is the \emph{circular} mean spacing --- inapplicable
to the real-line Hermite ensemble, whose mean spacing
$1/(\beta\rho(x))$ is position-dependent. Simulation
(Dumitriu--Edelman model, 15 samples per cell) confirms the
measured statistic in the thousands, growing with $N$ at every
$\beta \in \{1,2,4\}$.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} simulates the statistic at $\beta \in
\{1,2,4\}$, $N \in \{200, 400, 800\}$, tabulates the asymptotic
form, and checks the crossing points ($\ln 2^{15} > \pi^2$,
$(2\pi)^{4/3} = 11.59$). The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1)
certifies \texttt{pi\_lt\_22\_7} ($22 \cdot 22 < 10 \cdot 7 \cdot
7$), \texttt{pi\_gt\_two} ($2 < 4$), \texttt{two\_pow\_15},
\texttt{beta1\_grows}, \texttt{beta2\_falls}, \texttt{beta4\_falls},
and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted}. All seven audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the rational $\pi$ anchors, $2^{15}$, and the
divergence conclusions as stated; the extreme-value law
$(\log N)^{1/\beta}$ and the $\ln 2$ bounds are classical, cited in
prose and confirmed by simulation. The existence of $c_\beta$, the
value $c_2 = 0$, and the monotonicity clause are all refuted.
