- **Clause 1 resolution (FALSE):**
  - Proved that a strict spectral gap ($\gamma_{\mathrm{mod}}(A) > 0$ or $\gamma_{\mathrm{re}}(A) > 0$) requires neither irreducibility, nor primitivity, nor a single essential Frobenius class (almost irreducibility allowing arbitrary nilpotent/zero blocks), nor block-supported primitivity:

    - $A_{\mathrm{per}} = \mathrm{diag}(3, P_2)$ has two nonzero components, one of which is imprimitive of period $2$, yet $\sigma(A_{\mathrm{per}}) = \{3, 1, -1\}$ gives $\gamma_{\mathrm{mod}} = \gamma_{\mathrm{re}} = 2 > 0$.
    - $A_{\mathrm{cyc}} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ is irreducible of period $2$ (not primitive) yet has $\gamma_{\mathrm{re}} = 4 > 0$.
  - Proved the exact Frobenius normal form criterion (`main.pdf`, Theorem 2.2), showing that almost-irreducible primitivity erroneously replaces the true dominance condition $\rho(B_r) < \rho(A)$ ($r \neq r_0$) by $\rho(B_r) = 0$.
- **Clause 2 resolution (FALSE across all three positivity moduli and both bound directions):**
  - **Entrywise floor $m(A) = \min_{i,j} a_{ij}$:** Vanishes on primitive matrices with a zero entry ($A_{\mathrm{prim}} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, $\gamma_{\mathrm{mod}} = 2$); fails as an upper bound on symmetric positive matrices ($S_K = \begin{pmatrix} K^2 & K \\ K & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $m = 1$, $\gamma = K^2 + 1 \to \infty$); and fails even as the sharp lower bound on strictly positive matrices $A > 0$, since universal strict separation $\gamma_{\mathrm{mod}}(A) > m(A)$ holds for every $n \ge 2$ (`strictlyPositive_gap_gt_posModulus`) and $\inf_{m(A)=m} \gamma_{\mathrm{mod}}(A) = 2m \neq m$ for $n = 2$ (`twoByTwo_gap_ge_two_posModulus`).
  - **Markov–Doeblin modulus $\delta(A) = \sum_j \min_i a_{ij}$:** Proved as a true lower bound for constant-row-sum matrices (`constantRowSum_gap_ge_doeblin`), but disproved as a lower bound on symmetric positive matrices for every $n \ge 3$ ($A_{\mathrm{low},3}$ with $\gamma = 5 < 6 = \delta$, $A_{\mathrm{low},4}$ with $\gamma = 3 < 4 = \delta$, and $\gamma(J_n + a(E_{11}+E_{22}))/\delta \to 2/n \to 0$) and disproved as an upper bound even on symmetric doubly stochastic positive matrices ($P_{\mathrm{stoch}}$ with $m = \frac{1}{10} < \delta = \frac{2}{5} < \gamma = \frac{4}{5}$).
  - **Scale-invariant moduli $\mu_{\mathrm{ratio}} = m/M$ and Birkhoff–Hopf $\kappa_{\mathrm{BH}}$:** Refuted for unnormalized $\gamma(A)$ by positive scaling $A \mapsto cA$, and refuted even for the relative spectral gap $\gamma_{\mathrm{mod}}(A)/\rho(A)$ via the exact integer-eigenvalue symmetric family $A_{\mathrm{rat}}(p) = \begin{pmatrix} p^2 + 2p - 3 & 2p \\ 2p & 2p + 1 \end{pmatrix}$ ($p \ge 2$), where $\gamma_{\mathrm{mod}}/\rho \to 1$ while $\mu_{\mathrm{ratio}} \to 0$ and $\kappa_{\mathrm{BH}} \to 0$ (`ratioGapFamily_arbitrage`).
