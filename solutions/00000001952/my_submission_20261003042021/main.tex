\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the minimal index of a finite-index subgroup of
$\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is $2^n\binom{n}{2}$.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation at $n = 3$}

The action on $H_1(F_3,\mathbb{Z})$ gives
$\mathrm{Out}(F_3) \to \mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb{Z})$; composing with the
determinant gives a surjection to $\{\pm 1\}$ via
$\mathrm{diag}(-1,1,1)$, whose kernel is an index-2 proper subgroup.
The minimal index is $\le 2 < 24 = 2^3\binom{3}{2}$: the claimed
formula fails at $n = 3$.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} performs the exact arithmetic. The Lean package
(core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies $24$ and $2 < 24$; both audited
theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}. The determinant
construction is classical and cited.

\section{Boundary}

Only the claimed minimal-index formula at $n = 3$ is refuted.
