\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the EHZ capacity of $\Delta^1 \times
\Delta^{n-1}$ is $1/\sqrt{n}$ (a radical rational number).
\end{quote}

\section{The counterexample at $n = 2$}

$\Delta^1 \times \Delta^1$ is the unit square. It contains the inscribed
disk of radius $1/2$; EHZ capacity is monotone; and the disk of radius
$r$ has EHZ capacity $\pi r^2$. Hence
\[
c_{EHZ}(\Delta^1 \times \Delta^1) \ \ge\ \pi \cdot \tfrac14\ >\ \tfrac34\ >\ \tfrac{1}{\sqrt 2},
\]
using Archimedes' $\pi > 3$ and $(3/4)^2 = 9/16 > 1/2$ (i.e.\ $9 > 8$).
The claimed value $1/\sqrt{2}$ is strictly exceeded: the closed form is
false at the first product case.
