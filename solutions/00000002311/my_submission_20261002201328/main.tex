\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} The derangement proportion [in a transitive group] is
bounded below by $(1/|\Omega|)\cdot c$ with $c = 1/2$ (Boston--Shalev type);
\emph{tightness of the corrected constant is verified by Frobenius groups}.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The Frobenius group $G = C_7 \rtimes C_6 = \mathrm{AGL}(1,7)$ acts
transitively on $\Omega = \mathbb{Z}/7$ ($|G| = 42$). Its elements
$x \mapsto ax + b$ with $a \in \{1..6\}$, $b \in \mathbb{Z}/7$:
translations ($a = 1$, $b \ne 0$) are fixed-point-free ($6$ elements), and
every map with $a \ne 1$ has exactly one fixed point. The derangement
proportion is therefore
\[
  \frac{6}{42} = \frac17 = 2 \cdot \frac{1}{14}, \qquad
  c_{\text{realized}} = \frac17 \cdot 7 = 1 \ne \frac12 .
\]
Tightness of $c = 1/2$ would require proportion $1/14$ ($3$ of $42$):
Frobenius groups do not verify it. (In $C_p \rtimes C_m$ the realized
constant is $(p-1)/m$, never pinned to $1/2$ at the smallest example.)

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} enumerates all 42 maps (and $S_3$ on 3 points).
The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies by kernel enumeration
that exactly 6 of the 42 elements are derangements, plus the order and
$6 \ne 3$ --- all \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The lower bound $\ge \frac12 \cdot \frac1{|\Omega|}$ is \emph{not}
disputed ($\frac17 \ge \frac1{14}$); only the ``tightness verified by
Frobenius groups'' clause is refuted.
