# 00000000781

**English.** Definition: The lattice of closed subgroups of 1 + pZ_p is the combinatorial structure of the multiplicative group. Conjecture: This subgroup lattice is isomorphic to a quotient lattice of the integers; the isomorphism is the valuation stratification; and the isomorphism preserves the stratified structure of the valuation filtration. (unit group subgroup lattice classification)
