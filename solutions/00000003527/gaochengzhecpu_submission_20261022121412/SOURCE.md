# 00000003527

**English.** Definition: Convergence of ordinal exponentiation: normal functions. Conjecture: The fixed-point chain of normal functions: the fixed-point sequence of a normal function is closed unbounded, with the ω-th term of the sequence the transfinite iteration limit. (normal function fixed point closed unbounded chain)
