\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the maximal hitting time of the complete graph is
$n - 1$, and adding edges decreases hitting time submodularly.
\end{quote}

\section{The counterexample}

Let $P_3$ be the path $1{-}2{-}3$ and $G = P_3 + \{1,3\}$ the triangle.
On $P_3$, $H(1 \to 2) = 1$ (vertex 1's unique neighbor is 2). On the
triangle the hitting times satisfy $H_1 = 1 + H_3/2$, $H_3 = 1 +
H_1/2$, with unique solution $H_1 = H_3 = 2$ (determinant $3 \ne 0$).
So adding the edge $\{1,3\}$ \emph{increased} $H(1 \to 2)$ from $1$ to
$2 > 1$: hitting time is not edge-monotone, and the submodularity
clause is moot.
