\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the measure of the attracting basin of a
$p$-adic contraction is an explicit fraction of the ball volume; the
denominator of the fraction is the order of the multiplier mod $p$,
and the boundary of the basin is the closure of periodic points.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Take $f(x) = 5x + x^2 = x(5+x)$ on $\mathbb{Z}_5$, with attracting
fixed point $0$ and multiplier $f'(0) = 5$.

\textbf{No denominator.} The multiplier mod $p$ is $5 \equiv 0
\pmod 5$, and $0$ has \emph{no} multiplicative order modulo $5$
($0^k = 0 \ne 1$ for all $k \ge 1$): the conjecture's denominator
does not exist at this instance.  Even the charitable reading
$\mathrm{ord}(0) = 1$ gives the fraction $1/1 \ne$ the truth:

\textbf{The basin is $5\mathbb{Z}_5$, measure $1/5$.}  If $x \in
5\mathbb{Z}_5$ then $|f(x)| \le |x|/5 \to 0$; if $x \notin
5\mathbb{Z}_5$ then $|f(x)| = |x| = 1$ forever.  The basin is
exactly $5\mathbb{Z}_5$: measure $1/5$ of the ball, denominator
$5 = p$.  On the grid models $\mathbb{Z}/5^k$ the basin is exactly
the set of multiples of $5$ --- a $1/5$-fraction at depths $k = 2,
3, 4$ ($5/25$, $25/125$, $125/625$).

\textbf{The boundary is not a periodic closure.}  The basin-boundary
shell $5\mathbb{Z}_5 \setminus 25\mathbb{Z}_5$ consists of points
with $f(x) = x(5+x) \equiv 0 \pmod{25}$ (whenever $x \equiv 0 \bmod
5$): one step into the basin, never to return.  Mod $25$ the
periodic points are $\{0,\ 21 = -4,\ 1, 6, 11, 16\}$ (two fixed
points and a 4-cycle), all \emph{off} the shell
$\{5, 10, 15, 20\}$ --- whose points satisfy $f(x) = 0 \ne x$.  The
boundary of the basin is not the closure of periodic points.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} verifies the non-existence of the order of $0$
mod $5$ ($k = 1..99$), the basin $=$ multiples of $5$ exactly at
depths $2..4$ (the $1/5$-fraction), and the enumeration of periodic
points mod $25$ against the shell.  The Lean package (core Lean~4,
v4.33.1) certifies the depth-2 instance: \texttt{multiplier\_zero\_mod\_p},
\texttt{zero\_has\_no\_order} ($\forall k \ge 1,\ 0^k \ne 1$),
\texttt{basin\_fraction} ($5 \cdot 5 = 25$),
\texttt{denominator\_mismatch} ($5 \ne 1$),
\texttt{shell\_maps\_to\_zero}, \texttt{shell\_not\_periodic}, and
\texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All seven audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the depth-2 grid instance; the passage to
$\mathbb{Z}_5$ (the $5\mathbb{Z}_5$ identification and its measure)
is classical non-archimedean dynamics, cited in prose and verified
by the script at depths $2..4$.  All three clauses --- the explicit
fraction's denominator, and the boundary description --- are
refuted at this instance.
