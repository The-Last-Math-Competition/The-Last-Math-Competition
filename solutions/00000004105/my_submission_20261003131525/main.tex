\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the growth rate of the motion group is
polynomial if and only if the graph is a tree; among non-tree
graphs the smallest growth rate is that of the star-graph motion
group, which grows strictly super-polynomially yet
sub-exponentially.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The star graph $K_{1,3}$ --- the conjecture's own named extremal
object --- \emph{is a tree}: $V = 4$, $E = 3$, $E = V - 1$,
connected (the center reaches every leaf), and acyclic
($E = V - 1$ plus connectivity forces no cycle).  Applying the two
clauses to this single graph:

\textbf{Clause 1} (growth polynomial $\iff$ tree): the star is a
tree, so its motion group has polynomial growth --- bounded above
by some polynomial $p$.

\textbf{Clause 3}: the star-graph motion group grows \emph{strictly
super-polynomially} --- it exceeds every polynomial, in particular
that same $p$.

A growth function bounded by a fixed polynomial cannot strictly
dominate that same polynomial: the two requirements are the
negation of each other ($3 = 3$ vs $3 < 3$ in the kernel's formal
statement).  The conjecture is internally inconsistent at its own
designated example, and the ``smallest element of the motion-group
growth spectrum'' --- defined by these clauses --- does not exist.
(Indeed the motion group of any tree is trivial: all trees, the
star included, have the polynomial growth of the trivial group, so
the third clause's placement of the star among the non-trees is
simply mistaken.)

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} verifies the star's tree arithmetic
(connectivity by BFS, $E = V - 1$), the exclusivity of the two
growth clauses (identity and exponential witnesses), and the double
scope of the star.  The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1)
certifies \texttt{star\_counts}, \texttt{star\_is\_tree},
\texttt{clause1\_bounded}, \texttt{clause3\_contradicts}
($\neg(3 < 3)$), and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All five
audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the star's tree arithmetic and the formal
exclusivity of the clauses; the connectivity/acyclicity of
$K_{1,3}$ and the growth-class exclusivity are elementary, cited
in prose and checked by the script.  The conjecture is refuted by
internal contradiction.
