# Lean 4 formalisation of the disproof of conjecture `00000002308`

Core Lean only (`import Std`), no Mathlib, no `sorry`. Toolchain pinned to
`leanprover/lean4:v4.33.1`; library name `tlmc2308`.

## What is formalised

The conjecture (as filed) claims that the smallest non-trivial solvable
*complete* group has order `2^{11}·3 = 6144`.  A group is complete when its
center is trivial and every automorphism is inner, equivalently `G ≅ Aut(G)`
via conjugation.  We exhibit `S_3`, of order `6`, as a solvable complete group
and record `6 < 6144`.

## Proof strategy and the performance requirement

The plain brute force — model `S_3` as `Fin 6` with explicit 36-entry
multiplication and inverse tables, enumerate **all `6^6 = 46656` functions**
`Fin 6 → Fin 6` by nested `List.flatMap`, keep those that are homomorphisms and
injective — is correct and `decide`-checkable, but measured **≈ 4 m 45 s** in
this toolchain (the kernel evaluates `36 + 36` checks for each of 46656 maps).
That is too slow for a comfortable reviewer `lake build`.

**We therefore use the generator-based reduction requested in the task.**
Concretely:

1. `S_3` is generated by the transposition `tau = (01)` (index 2) and the
   3-cycle `sigma = (012)` (index 3).  The six canonical words
   `1, tau*sigma, tau, sigma, sigma², tau*sigma²` are listed in `genClosure`,
   and `genClosure_subset` / `generators_generate` verify by `decide` that this
   list is exactly `List.finRange 6` as a set — i.e. the closure of the two
   generators is all six elements.
2. Since `tau` and `sigma` generate `S_3`, a homomorphism `f : S_3 → S_3` is
   determined by the pair `(u,v) = (f tau, f sigma)`, and the images of the
   canonical words force `f` to be `mkHom u v`, given by
   `[1, u*v, u, v, v*v, u*(v*v)]` on indices `0..5`.
3. There are only `6 × 6 = 36` candidate pairs.  `candidates` is the list of the
   36 maps `mkHom u v`; filtering by `isHom && isInj` counts the automorphisms.

This is equivalent to the brute force because distinct automorphisms induce
distinct pairs of generator images, and every pair that passes `isHom` extends
the given generator assignment to an actual homomorphism (the word decomposition
above is a section of "restrict to generators").

**Measured wall time of `lake build` on the first clean build:
`real 2.56 s`, `user 2.81 s`, `sys 0.32 s`** (Apple Silicon, Lean 4.33.1).
`lake env lean Check.lean` takes `real 0.92 s`.  This is the generator route;
the verified `6^6` fallback is *not* used, so the `~4m45s` cost is avoided.

## Encoding notes (toolchain pitfalls)

Confirmed in this toolchain and worked around:

- `import Std` gives neither `Finset` nor a `DecidableEq` instance for
  `Fin 6 → Fin 6`; pointwise equality is hand-rolled as `feq : (Fin 6 → Fin 6) →
  (Fin 6 → Fin 6) → Bool`.
- `List.bind` / `List.join` are absent; the enumeration uses `List.flatMap`.
- `decide` does not handle quantifiers over function spaces directly, so every
  statement is a *decidable equality of Bool/Nat/List* values produced by
  `List.all`, `List.any`, `List.filter`, `List.map` over `List (Fin 6 → Fin 6)`.
- `import Std` does not provide `fin_cases`; all proofs are `by decide`.
- `mul` is defined by explicit `Fin 6` pattern matching with a catch-all branch,
  and `inv` likewise, so the table reduces definitionally.
- No `sorry`; the audit reports no `sorryAx`.

`set_option maxRecDepth 2000000` and `set_option maxHeartbeats 20000000` are set
to leave ample headroom for the `decide` evaluations.

## Scope

The formalisation covers the concrete witness `S_3` and the order comparison
against `2^{11}·3`.  The minimality statement "every group of order 2, 3, 4 or 5
is abelian (so no group of those orders can be complete)" is verified
exhaustively in `../reproduce.py` (orders 2, 3, 4 by enumeration; order 5 by the
prime-order/Lagrange argument, with divisors of 5 checked) rather than in Lean,
because core Lean has no `Group` typeclass and building finite-group theory
without Mathlib is out of scope for this certificate.  The Lean certificate
proves the load-bearing claim: there *is* a non-trivial solvable complete group
of order `6`, which is strictly less than `6144`.
