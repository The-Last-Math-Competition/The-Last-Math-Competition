\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} for sums of seven cubic powers of primes the
local obstruction classes are exactly the three non-representable
classes modulo 9, and adding one variable removes all
obstructions.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Prime cubes mod $9$ are exactly $\{0, 1, 8\}$: $3^3 = 27 \equiv
0$, and for $p \not\equiv 0 \pmod 3$ the cube map on the cyclic
group $(\mathbb{Z}/9)^*$ of order $6$ lands in $\{\pm 1\}$
($2^3 = 8 = -1$, $4^3 = 64 = 1$).  A seven-term sum is
$k \cdot 1 + m \cdot 8$ with $k + m \le 7$; the reachable classes
are $k - m$ for $0 \le k - m \le 7$, together with $8 = -1$ (one
term $8$, six terms $0$): \emph{all nine} classes mod $9$.  There
are \textbf{zero} local obstruction classes at seven variables ---
not three.  The genuine obstruction belongs to three variables,
which miss exactly \textbf{two} classes $\{4, 5\}$ (reachable:
$k - m$ with $k + m \le 3$, i.e.\ $\{-3, \dots, 3\}$), and four
variables already remove every obstruction:

\begin{tabular}{c|l}
$s$ variables & missing classes mod $9$\\ \hline
3 & $\{4, 5\}$ --- exactly two\\
4 & $\varnothing$ --- none\\
5, 6, 7 & $\varnothing$ --- none
\end{tabular}

Both the claimed count (three classes) and the claimed
variable-count (seven with three obstructions, eight clearing
them) misdescribe the actual residue arithmetic.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} computes the cube-residue set, the exhaustive
seven-term coverage, and the missing-class table for
$s = 3..7$.  The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
\texttt{cube\_residues} ($8, 0, 1 \bmod 9$), \texttt{seven\_cover}
(explicit $(k, m)$ witnesses for all nine classes via cases on the
residue), \texttt{no\_obstruction}, \texttt{three\_missing}, and
\texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All five audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the cube residues, the nine explicit
witnesses with $k + m \le 7$, and the three-term missing set; the
exhaustive cover table is in the script.  Both the obstruction
table and the variable-count claim are refuted at modulus $9$.
