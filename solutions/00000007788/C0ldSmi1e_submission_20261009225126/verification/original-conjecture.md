# 00000007788

**English.** Definition: The random polytope K_N = conv(X_1,...,X_N) with X_i independent uniform on K. Conjecture: For the ball B^n the deficit 1 - E vol(K_N)/vol(B) is asymptotically c_n N^{-2/(n-1)}, with c_n computable from the precise asymptotics of the one-point spherical measure and c_n/c_{n-1} tending to sqrt(pi/2); for the simplex the exponent is unchanged while the constant doubles, the difference being an interpretable combinatorial quantity of corner-measure contributions. (random polytope deficit constants)

**中文。** 定义：随机多胞形 K_N=conv(X_1,…,X_N)（X_i 独立均匀于 K）。猜想：对球 B^n，E vol(K_N)/vol(B) 的缺失量 1-E vol(K_N)/vol(B) 渐近为 c_n N^{-2/(n-1)}，常数 c_n 可由球面单点测度的精确渐进计算，且 c_n/c_{n-1}→√(π/2)；对单纯形相应指数不变而常数翻倍，两个常数之差恰为角点测度贡献的可解释组合量。
