Mathematical proof reviewed
For the normalized-area law on the unit disk, the event E_r that all N points lie in a concentric radius-r disk has probability (r^2)^N. On E_r the convex hull lies in that disk and its normalized area is at most r^2. Almost surely its normalized area is at most 1. The expectation therefore gives
    D_N >= (1-r^2)(r^2)^N.
With t=1/[2(N+1)] and r=sqrt(1-t), Bernoulli's inequality yields (1-t)^N >= 1-Nt >= 1/2. Thus
    D_N >= 1/[4(N+1)],
    N^2*D_N >= N/8 for N>=1.
The quadratic normalization diverges to +infinity, excludes every finite real normalized limit, and makes the ratio to any positive c*N^(-2) diverge. This contradicts the source assertion. Every step is elementary and valid; the coarse lower bound does not need to give the true sharp random-polytope rate.
