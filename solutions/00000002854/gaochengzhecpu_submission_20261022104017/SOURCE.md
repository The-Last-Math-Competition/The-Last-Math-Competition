**English.** Definition: Structural equation models (SEM): the algebraic side of causal inference. Conjecture: The algebraic criterion of SEM identifiability: global identifiability holds if and only if the rank deficit of the Jacobian at the generic point is zero, and the deficit is explicitly compensated by additional moment conditions on instrumental variables. (SEM algebraic identifiability criterion)

**中文。** 定义：结构方程模型（SEM）：因果推断的代数侧。猜想：SEM 的可辨识性的代数判据：全局可辨识当且仅当 Jacobi 行列式在泛型点的秩亏损为零且亏损的补偿由工具变量的额外矩条件显式填补。（SEM 代数可辨识判据）
