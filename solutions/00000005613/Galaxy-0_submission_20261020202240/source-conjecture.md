# 00000005613

**English.** Definition: The subresultant conversion relates subresultants to common factors. Conjecture: The third-order minor spectrum is always the explicit spectrum of classical matrix minors, with signs given by the interleaving of roots; a minor is zero if and only if the corresponding principal minor is zero; and the nonzero part of the spectrum is log-concave, the concavity given by the monotonicity of the interleaving signs. (interleaved signs of third-order minor spectra)

**中文。** 定义：子结果换算：子结果式与公因子的关系。猜想：三阶子式谱恒为经典矩阵子式的显式谱且符号由根的交错给出；子式为零当且仅当对应主子式为零；谱的非零部分为对数凹且凹性由交错符号的单调性给出。（三阶子式谱交错符号）
