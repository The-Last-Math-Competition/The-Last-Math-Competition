# 00000008262

**English.** Definition: The spectral condition number ric_cond of the steady state P-infinity of the error-covariance Riccati equation of Kalman-Bucy filtering. Conjecture: ric_cond (the condition number of the steady Riccati) is at most the explicit double ratio (sigma_obs/sigma_sys)^2 (a double-ratio condition law); the rate of convergence of the steady state (transient decay) is the double decay 2|lambda_max(A) minus the observation gain (a double decay formula); and the mismatch radius of the robustness of filtering (stability domain under model mismatch) is the reciprocal 1/(2 cond) (a reciprocal radius law). (Kalman-Bucy Riccati condition double decay)

**中文。** 定义：Kalman–Bucy 滤波的误差协方差 Riccati 方程的稳态 P∞ 的谱条件数 ric_cond。猜想：ric_cond（稳态 Riccati 的条件数）≤ (σ_obs/σ_sys)² 的显式双比（双比条件律）；且稳态的收敛（暂态衰减）的速率为 2·|λ_max(A)-观测增益| 的双衰减（双衰减公式）；滤波的鲁棒性（模型失配下的稳定域）的失配半径 = 1/(2·cond) 的倒数（倒数半径律）。
