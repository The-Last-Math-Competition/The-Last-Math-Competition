\begin{abstract}
The conjectured bound for the spectral condition number of a steady
Kalman--Bucy covariance fails already in two dimensions with equal,
nondegenerate isotropic system and observation noises. The stabilizing
covariance has eigenvalues $2$ and $1/2$, hence condition number $4$,
while the proposed upper bound is $1$.
\end{abstract}

\section*{The claimed bound and the filtering model}
Both language versions assert
\[
 \kappa_2(P_\infty)\leq
       (\sigma_{\rm obs}/\sigma_{\rm sys})^2.
\]
We refute this first conjunct. No conclusion about the other claimed
transient-rate and robustness-radius formulas is needed.

Consider the usual continuous-time linear filtering model
\[
 dX_t=A X_t\,dt+\sigma_{\rm sys}\,dW_t,
 \qquad dY_t=H X_t\,dt+\sigma_{\rm obs}\,dV_t,
\]
with independent standard two-dimensional Brownian motions. The covariance
Riccati equation and its steady equation are
\begin{align*}
 \dot P&=AP+PA^T+\sigma_{\rm sys}^2 I
    -\sigma_{\rm obs}^{-2}PH^THP,\\
 0&=AP_\infty+P_\infty A^T+\sigma_{\rm sys}^2 I
    -\sigma_{\rm obs}^{-2}P_\infty H^THP_\infty.
\end{align*}
Set
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}3/4&0\\0&-3/4\end{pmatrix},\quad
 H=I,\quad \sigma_{\rm sys}=\sigma_{\rm obs}=1,\quad
 P_\infty=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
All noise covariances are strictly positive definite. In particular,
the example does not use a zero-noise or unobservable direction.

\section*{Verification of the steady covariance}
The matrix $P_\infty$ is real symmetric positive definite. Direct
multiplication gives
\[
 AP_\infty+P_\infty A^T
       =\operatorname{diag}(3,-3/4),\qquad
 P_\infty^2=\operatorname{diag}(4,1/4),
\]
so $AP_\infty+P_\infty A^T+I-P_\infty^2=0$.
The closed-loop error matrix is
\[
 A-P_\infty H^TH=A-P_\infty=-\frac54 I.
\]
Both complex eigenvalues therefore have strictly negative real part;
this is a stabilizing positive-definite Riccati solution.

One can also see the actual steady limit without invoking a general
Riccati existence theorem. With $P(0)=0$, put $e(t)=\exp(-5t/2)$ and
\[
 P(t)=\operatorname{diag}\left(
       \frac{2(1-e(t))}{1+4e(t)},
       \frac{(1/2)(1-e(t))}{1+e(t)/4}\right).
\]
Differentiation verifies the covariance equation, and the positive
denominators show it is defined for all $t\geq0$. It converges to the
displayed $P_\infty$. Alternatively, initializing the covariance at
$P_\infty$ gives an exactly stationary solution. These observations
ensure that the example is a genuine steady filtering covariance,
not an unrelated algebraic root.

\section*{Contradiction}
For a real symmetric positive-definite matrix its spectral condition
number is the ratio of its largest to its smallest eigenvalue. Here
the real spectrum is exactly $\{2,1/2\}$, and hence
\[
 \kappa_2(P_\infty)=\frac{2}{1/2}=4
       >1=(\sigma_{\rm obs}/\sigma_{\rm sys})^2.
\]
The same numerical contradiction holds if the symbols for the noises
are interpreted as variances rather than standard deviations, since
both equal one. The conjecture has no hypothesis excluding anisotropic
system dynamics $A$. Such dynamics are precisely what its proposed
noise-only condition-number bound overlooks.

standard real and complex matrix spectra. The predicate

The predicate \texttt{Hurwitz} quantifies over the complex spectrum
of the complexified closed-loop matrix and requires negative real parts.
The predicate \texttt{SpectralCondition} defines the spectral condition
number using the least and greatest elements of the real spectrum and
their ratio, with strictly positive least element.

The development proves positive definiteness, the actual Riccati
equation, the closed-loop stability, the exact real spectrum and condition
number, and negates the universally quantified proposed bound. Its
final theorem is
\begin{center}\texttt{Conjecture8262.conjecture8262\_false}.\end{center}
There is no table substituted for matrix operations or spectra. The

The Brownian filtering model and the explicit time-dependent solution
above are justified on paper. Lean does not formalize stochastic

equation. These are the steady covariances in this fully observable,
nondegenerate example, as the explicit solution independently checks.
