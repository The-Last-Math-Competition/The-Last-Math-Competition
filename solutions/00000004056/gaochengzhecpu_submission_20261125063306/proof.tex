\title{A group-algebra block with distinct Tate and AR periods}

Let \(k=\mathbb F_3\), let \(g\) generate \(C_3\), and set \(u=g-1\).
The group algebra is
\[
  A=kC_3\cong k[u]/(u^3)
\]
because \((g-1)^3=g^3-1=0\) and both algebras have dimension three.
It is local with maximal ideal \((u)\), so the whole algebra is one block.
Taking the coefficient of \(u^2\) in a product gives a symmetric
nondegenerate associative pairing, so \(A\) is symmetric. Write \(S=A/(u)\).

Use the Tate period of \(S\) in the stable extension category, namely the
least \(n>0\) with \(\Omega^n S\cong S\) stably. In the graded Tate
self-extension ring this is the least positive degree of a graded unit.
Indeed, a degree-\(n\) class is represented by a stable map
\(\Omega^n S\to S\), and a graded inverse represents its inverse after
applying the syzygy shift. Here the stable syzygy is invertible because
the group algebra is self-injective.

The quotient \(A\to S\) is a projective cover with kernel \(uA\).
Multiplication by \(u\) gives a projective cover of \(uA\) with kernel
\(u^2A\). Both kernels lie in the radical of the corresponding free
module \(A\). The map \(S\to u^2A\) sending \(1\) to \(u^2\) is an
isomorphism. Hence \(\Omega^2 S\cong S\). Equivalently, the alternating
maps \(u\) and \(u^2\) on copies of \(A\) give an exact complete
resolution. Their kernels and images are \(\ker(u)=u^2A=\operatorname{im}(u^2)\)
and \(\ker(u^2)=uA=\operatorname{im}(u)\). Applying
\(\operatorname{Hom}_A(-,A)\) gives the same alternating maps and remains
exact.

Every map \(S\to uA\) has image in \(u^2A\), the subspace of \(uA\)
annihilated by \(u\). Every map \(uA\to S\) kills \(u^2A=u(uA)\).
Thus every composite \(S\to uA\to S\) is zero. The stable identity of
\(S\) is nonzero. Indeed, every map \(S\to A\) lands in \(u^2A\), and
every map \(A\to S\) kills \(u^2A\). Every finitely generated projective
is a summand of a finite sum of copies of \(A\), so any endomorphism of
\(S\) factoring through a projective is zero. It follows that
\(\Omega S\) is never stably isomorphic to \(S\). The Tate period of
\(S\) is exactly two.

For the Auslander--Reiten translate, the minimal projective presentation
of \(S\) is \(A\xrightarrow{u} A\to S\to0\).
The transpose \(\operatorname{Tr} S\) is the cokernel of the dual map,
which under \(\operatorname{Hom}_A(A,A)\cong A\) is again multiplication by
\(u\). Thus \(\operatorname{Tr} S\cong S\) as right modules, and
\(\tau S=D\operatorname{Tr} S\cong S\), where \(D=\operatorname{Hom}_k(-,k)\).
The AR-translation period is one. The two periods already differ in
the group-algebra block \(kC_3\), refuting the claimed equality.

\paragraph{Block-wide periods.}
The same example also distinguishes the orders of the two functors on
the stable category. View \(A\) as a bimodule via the quotient
\[
  R=A\otimes_k A^{\mathrm{op}}\cong k[x,y]/(x^3,y^3)
        \longrightarrow A,\qquad x,y\longmapsto u .
\]
In characteristic three, \(z=x-y\) changes coordinates to
\(R=k[z,y]/(z^3,y^3)\). The quotient is \(R/(z)\) and the alternating
multiplications by \(z\) and \(z^2\) give an exact bimodule resolution,
with second syzygy \(z^2R\cong A\). This resolution splits as a complex
of right \(A\)-modules with its augmentation, since its successive
kernels are free over \(k[y]/(y^3)\). Tensoring with a left module
therefore gives a natural two-step projective resolution whose second
kernel is the original module. The terms are projective because
\(R\otimes_A M\cong A\otimes_k M\) is free as a left \(A\)-module. Thus
\(\Omega^2\) is naturally the identity stably. Its order is exactly two
by the computation on \(S\). For this symmetric algebra the standard
stable functor formula \(\tau\simeq\Omega^2\) gives order one
\cite[Corollary IV.8.6]{ARS}.

\paragraph{Lean verification.}
The Lean project checks the exact kernel and image formulas in
\(\mathbb F_3^3\), the module maps and the obstruction to a stable
inverse in degree one. It verifies the resulting unequal periods on
the two stable isomorphism classes in the syzygy orbit of \(S\).
The identifications with the group-algebra block and its stable and
Auslander--Reiten categories are given above.
