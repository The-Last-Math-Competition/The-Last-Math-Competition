\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Sparsification of intersective sets: there exists an absolute
$c > 0$ such that whenever $A \subset [N]$ with $|A| \ge N^{1/2-c}$,
the set $\{a^2 : a \in A\}$ is intersective (it has nonempty
intersection with every set of positive density).}
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Within $[N]$, a subset $D$ has positive density $|D|/N > 0$ exactly
when $D \ne \emptyset$.  Hence ``$S$ is intersective'' --- meeting
every positive-density set --- means meeting every nonempty subset of
$[N]$, in particular every singleton $\{x\}$, which forces $S = [N]$
(kernel-certified \texttt{singleton\_reduction}).

The square image, however, satisfies
$|\{a^2 : a \in A\}| \le |A|$, and the hypothesis only gives
$|A| \ge N^{1/2-c}$ with $1/2 - c < 1$.  One instance refutes every
$c \ge 0$ simultaneously:
\[
N = 4, \quad A = \{1, 2\}: \quad |A| = 2 = 4^{1/2} \ge 4^{1/2 - c},
\]
(square image $S = \{1, 4\}$) while the witness $D = \{3\}$ is
nonempty (density $1/4 > 0$) and disjoint from $S$.  So for every
absolute $c > 0$ there is an $A$ satisfying the size hypothesis whose
square image is not intersective.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} enumerates all $15$ nonempty subsets of $[4]$ ---
$\{1,4\}$ fails intersectivity with witnesses $\{2\}, \{3\},
\{2,3\}$ --- confirms the size hypothesis for representative $c$, and
verifies the general principle ``meets every nonempty subset iff
$S = [N]$'' over all $16$ subsets of $[4]$.  The Lean package (core
Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the singleton reduction, the instance
refutation, and the size anchor $2 \cdot 2 = 4$; all four audited
theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The refutation is under the conjecture's own literal definition of
``intersective''; the standard S\'ark\"ozy/Furstenberg
difference-intersection notion is a different statement and is not
addressed.  Under the literal definition the conjecture is refuted in
full for every absolute $c$.
