\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Definition:} the isomonodromy deformation and its monodromy
manifold. \textit{Conjecture:} the count of $\mathbb{Q}$-points of the
PVI monodromy manifold is $N_p = p^2 + ap + 1$ with
$a \in \{-1, 0, 1\}$ (point-count patterns).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The PVI monodromy manifold is classically the Fricke surface
$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - xyz = \kappa$.  Over $\mathbb{F}_5$ with
$\kappa = 1$, the exhaustive count over the $5^3 = 125$ triples gives
\[
N_5 = 6: \quad (0,0,1),\ (0,0,4),\ (0,1,0),\ (0,4,0),\ (1,0,0),\ (4,0,0),
\]
while the pattern $N_p = p^2 + ap + 1$, $a \in \{-1,0,1\}$, at $p = 5$
demands $N_5 \in \{21, 26, 31\}$.  Since $6 \notin \{21, 26, 31\}$,
the point-count pattern is violated.  (Contrast: the degenerate
$\kappa = 0$ surface has $41$ points.)

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} brute-forces the count over
$\mathbb{F}_5^3$ (exactly $6$ points, listed above) and the pattern
values.  The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) defines the membership
predicate (using $-xyz = 4xyz \bmod 5$), counts over
\texttt{List.range 5} at each coordinate, and certifies the count
$= 6$ by kernel reduction, plus the six memberships, two sanity
non-memberships, and the pattern arithmetic; all five audited theorems
report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the complete $\mathbb{F}_5$ enumeration; the
identification ``PVI monodromy manifold = Fricke surface'' is
classical, cited in prose.  The pattern is refuted at $p = 5$,
$\kappa = 1$.
