# 00000000957

**English.** Definition: The frame potential FP measures equiangularity of frame vectors. Conjecture: Local minima of FP occur at r-fold repetitions of orthogonal bases for frames of redundancy r; and the classification of minima is complete at integer redundancy. (frame potential local minimum classification)

**中文。** 定义：帧位势 FP 度量框架列的等角性。猜想：FP 的局部极小在冗余 r 的框架处为 r 重重复正交基；且极小分类在冗余整数时完备。（帧位势局部极小分类）
