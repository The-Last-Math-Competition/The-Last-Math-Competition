\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the fusion rules of string-net anyons are the
Grothendieck ring of the input category, and the rank of the ring is
a lower bound for ground-state degeneracy.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The anyons of a Levin--Wen model on input category $C$ are the
simples of the \emph{Drinfeld center} $Z(C)$ --- not of $C$.  For
the smallest non-trivial input $C = \mathrm{Vec}_{\mathbb{Z}/2}$:

\textbf{Input ring.}  $\mathrm{Vec}_{\mathbb{Z}/2}$ has two simples
$\{1, x\}$ with $x \otimes x = 1$; its Grothendieck ring is
$\mathbb{Z}[\mathbb{Z}/2]$, of rank $2$.

\textbf{Anyon ring.}  $Z(\mathrm{Vec}_{\mathbb{Z}/2}) \cong
\mathrm{Vec}_{\mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2}$ (the toric-code
category) has four simples $\{1, e, m, \psi\}$ with group-law
fusion
\[
e\,e = m\,m = \psi\,\psi = 1, \qquad e\,m = \psi :
\]
a fusion ring of rank $4$ (associativity verified on all
$4^3 = 64$ triples).  Rank $4 \ne 2$: the conjecture's
identification is false --- the string-net excitation spectrum is
governed by the center, which adds the electric $e$, the magnetic
$m$, and their product $\psi$ beyond the input's data.
Correspondingly the torus ground-state degeneracy of this model is
$4$ --- the \emph{center's} rank (the toric-code number); the
lower-bound clause, read on the input rank, holds only vacuously
($2 \le 4$) and rests on the failed identification.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} verifies the $\mathbb{Z}/2$ group law (input
ring, rank 2), the $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^2$ fusion table (four toric laws,
$e\,m = \psi$), associativity on all triples, the rank mismatch, and
the torus GSD $= 4$ = center rank.  The Lean package (core Lean~4,
v4.33.1) certifies \texttt{input\_rank} ($2 = 1+1$),
\texttt{rank4} ($4 = 2 \cdot 2$), \texttt{toric\_laws} (the XOR
group law on 2-bit codes: $\texttt{fuse}\ 1\ 1 = 0$, \dots,
$\texttt{fuse}\ 1\ 2 = 3$), \texttt{four\_simples} (pairwise
distinct), and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted} ($2 \ne 4$).  All five
audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the two ranks, the toric fusion laws, the
distinctness of the four simples, and the mismatch; the
identification of the anyon category as $Z(\mathrm{Vec}_{\mathbb{Z}/2})
\cong \mathrm{Vec}_{V_4}$ is classical (Levin--Wen; Kitaev's quantum
double), cited in prose and reproduced by the script's table.  The
identification clause is refuted.
