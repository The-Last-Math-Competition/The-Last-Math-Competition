# 00000008240

**English.** Definition: The critical discounting of cooperation in repeated games: the explicit formula folk_delta for the critical discount factor of the folk theorem. Conjecture: folk_delta (the critical discount for cooperation) is the explicit ratio of the one-period defection payoff to the cooperative payoff (an explicit ratio formula); the correction of the critical discount under noise (imperfect monitoring) is a linear inflation in the noise variance sigma^2 (a linear inflation law); and the spectrum of the critical discounts over static profiles is a finite union of closed intervals inside (0,1) (a finite interval union law), the interval endpoints being the switching points of defection-punishment (a switching endpoint law). (folk critical discount explicit ratio intervals)

**中文。** 定义：重复博弈的合作的临界贴现：Folk 定理的临界贴现因子 δ_c 的显式公式 folk_delta。猜想：folk_delta（合作的临界贴现）= 单期背叛收益与合作收益的显式比（显式比公式）；且噪声（不完全监测）下的临界贴现的修正为噪声方差 σ² 的线性膨胀（线性膨胀律）；临界贴现的博弈族的谱（一切静态剖面的 δ_c 的值）为介于 0 与 1 之间的有限并闭区间（有限区间并律），区间的端点为背叛-惩罚的切换点（切换端点律）。
