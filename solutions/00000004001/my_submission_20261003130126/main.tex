\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} $E\|S^n\|^2 = n!\,\binom{d+n-1}{n}$ for the
$n$-th level of the Brownian signature, and its generating function
in $n$ is rational.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

For scalar Brownian motion ($d = 1$) the iterated integrals
collapse: $S_n = \int_{t_1 < \dots < t_n} \mathrm{d}W^{\otimes n} =
X_1^n/n!$ (Stratonovich chain rule; Monte Carlo:
$E[S_2^2] = 0.755 \approx 3/4$ over $400{,}000$ samples).  Hence,
using the Gaussian moment $E[X^{2n}] = (2n)!/(2^n n!)$,
\[
E\|S^n\|^2 = \frac{E[X_1^{2n}]}{(n!)^2}
= \frac{(2n)!}{2^n (n!)^3},
\]
while the conjecture's formula at $d = 1$ reads
$n!\binom{n}{n} = n!$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccc}
n & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6\\ \hline
\text{true} & 1 & 1 & \tfrac34 & \tfrac5{12} & \tfrac{35}{192} &
\tfrac{21}{320} & \tfrac{77}{3840}\\
\text{claimed} & 1 & 1 & 2 & 6 & 24 & 120 & 720
\end{array}
\]
--- agreement only at $n = 0, 1$, then overestimates growing
without bound ($8/3$ at $n = 2$, $14.4$ at $n = 3$, $131$ at
$n = 4$).  The generating-function clause fails independently: the
true coefficients have ratio $a_{n+1}/a_n = \frac{2n+1}{(n+1)^2}
\to 0$ --- Bessel-type decay, not C-finite --- while coefficients
of rational generating functions satisfy a linear recurrence with
polynomial-exponential behavior.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} tabulates true vs claimed ($n = 0..6$,
exact rationals), confirms $E[S_2^2] = 3/4$ by Monte Carlo, and
verifies the ratio law exactly for $n = 1..5$.  The Lean package
(core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies \texttt{claimed\_value},
\texttt{gaussian\_moment} ($4! = 24$, $2^3 = 8$, $24/8 = 3$,
$3 < 8$), \texttt{true\_below\_claimed}, \texttt{agree\_only\_early},
\texttt{ratio\_decay} ($5 < 9$), and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.
All six audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any
axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the scaled comparison at $(d, n) = (1, 2)$ and
the ratio-decay anchor; the scalar signature identification, the
Gaussian moments, and the C-finiteness obstruction are classical,
cited in prose and confirmed by the script.  Both the formula and
the rationality clause are refuted at $d = 1$.
