\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Definition:} the relaxation time $t_{\mathrm{rel}}$.
\textit{Conjecture:} for random walks on the binary tree,
$t_{\mathrm{rel}} = (1+o(1))\cdot 4n$ with second term $-2\log n$.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

For the complete binary tree of depth $d$, the similarity-symmetrized
walk matrix has second eigenvalue giving
\[
t_{\mathrm{rel}} = 4\cdot 2^d - 6d + O(1),
\]
measured at $d = 8, 10, 12$: $984.6$, $4045.1$, $16321.4$ (formula:
$976$, $4036$, $16312$); the least-squares second-term coefficient is
$-5.8 \approx -6$.

\emph{Reading $n = $ depth $d$:} the conjectured law $4d + o(d)$ is
linear, but the growth is exponential --- $2^d > 4d$ for all
$d \ge 5$ (kernel-certified by induction). At $d = 12$ the conjectured
$4d = 48$ against the measured $16321.4$, just under the exponential
law $4\cdot 2^{12} = 16384$.

\emph{Reading $n = $ vertices $N = 2^{d+1}-1$:} the leading
coefficient is $t_{\mathrm{rel}}/N \to 2$ (not $4$), and the second
term is $-6d = -6\log_2(N+1) + O(1)$ (coefficient $-6$, not $-2$).

Both readings fail; the conjectured law holds for neither.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} recomputes the eigenvalues independently
(NumPy) at $d = 8, 10, 12$, fits the second-term coefficient, checks
the separation $2^d > 4d$ up to $d = 39$, and evaluates the $n = N$
reading. The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the
exponential-vs-linear separation for all $d \ge 5$ and the instance
anchors; all five audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on
any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The spectral measurements are numerical (script, standard
complete-binary-tree walk conventions); the kernel certifies the
exponential-vs-linear separation and the instance anchors. The
conjecture fails under both readings of $n$.
