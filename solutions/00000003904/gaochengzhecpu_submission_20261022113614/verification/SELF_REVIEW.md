# 3904 — the one-variable Veronese F-signature is identically one

## Exact source and scope

The parent obtained the exact bilingual source `round5/source/00000003904.md` and completed remote duplicate screening before authorizing this problem. The source defines the d-Veronese subring as the subring generated by all degree-d monomials and asserts, without excluding one variable,

    s(V_d) = d^(-n) (1 + c(n)/d + O(d^(-2))).

Take the coefficient field F₂ and n=1. For every d>0 the actual Veronese subalgebra is V_d=F₂[X^d], isomorphic to F₂[T]. Its F-signature is exactly one, whereas the asserted expression tends to zero. This refutes the F-signature clause and therefore the stated conjunction. No counterclaim about strong F-regularity is needed.

The formalization uses the standard graded F-signature definition: the limit of maximal free-summand ranks of actual Frobenius pushforward modules, divided by q raised to the Krull dimension. This reference supplies terminology and the standard definition; the proof does not invoke its F-signature computation theorem. Its Example 3.2 separately treats at least two variables and obtains 1/d, illustrating why a variable-count restriction would not rescue the source's proposed d^(-n) law.
