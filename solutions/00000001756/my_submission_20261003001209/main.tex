\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the maximal order $d(n)$ of a unimodular square
submatrix of the $S_n$ character table equals
$d(n) = \#\{\lambda \vdash n : \mathrm{hook}(\lambda)\ \text{pairwise
coprime}\}$, and a submatrix of that order is selectable from
irreducible-character rows sorted by dimension.
\end{quote}

\section{The counterexample at $n=3$}

$S_3$ character table, rows sorted by dimension (trivial, sign,
standard), columns $[e], [(12)], [(123)]$:
$\chi = \left(\begin{matrix} 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & -1 \end{matrix}\right)$.

\textbf{Formula value 3.} All partitions $(3), (2,1), (1,1,1)$ have
pairwise coprime hook multisets $\{3,2,1\}, \{3,1,1\}, \{3,2,1\}$.

\textbf{True value 2.} The only $3\times3$ submatrix is the whole table
with $\det = 6 \ne \pm 1$; while rows $\{$trivial, standard$\}$,
columns $\{(12),(123)\}$ give $\det = -1$. Hence $d(3) = 2 \ne 3$, and
the dim-sorted order-3 selection does not exist.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} computes all ten square-submatrix determinants and
recomputes the hook multisets from the hook-length formula. The Lean
package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the determinants, the pairwise
coprimality, and $3 \ne 2$; all five audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

Only $n = 3$ is addressed; larger $n$ are not.
