# Refutation of conjecture 00000003843 — 0-Hecke two-sided class-partition

**Verdict: FALSE.** The conjecture fails in both of its halves, with the smallest
witness at `n = 3` (and the second half already at `n = 2`).

## The conjecture (verbatim)

> **Definition**: The 0-Hecke monoid H_n(0) is generated by monoid braid
> relations and absorption relations. **Conjecture**: The number of two-sided
> classes of H_n(0) is exactly the partition number p(n), and the number of left
> classes in each two-sided class equals the number of distinct hook lengths of
> the corresponding partition. *(0-Hecke two-sided class-partition)*

## Why it is false

`H_n(0)` has `n!` elements (indexed by `w ∈ S_n`) and is **J-trivial**: every
principal two-sided ideal is distinct, so there are exactly `n!` two-sided
(Green `J`-) classes, and likewise `n!` left (`L`-) classes. This agrees with the
literature — Ganyushkin–Mazorchuk, *On Kiselman quotients of 0-Hecke monoids*
([arXiv:1006.0316](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.0316)) derive that `H_Γ` is
`J`-trivial.

| n | \|H_n(0)\| | J-classes | L-classes | p(n) | Σ_λ ⊢ n #distinct hook lengths |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2   | 2   | 2   | 2 | 4  |
| 3 | 6   | 6   | 6   | 3 | 8  |
| 4 | 24  | 24  | 24  | 5 | 17 |
| 5 | 120 | 120 | 120 | 7 | 29 |

* **First half fails at n = 3** — 6 two-sided classes vs `p(3) = 3`.
  (At `n = 2` the two counts coincide: `2 = p(2)`. A coincidence, nothing more.)
* **Second half fails at n = 3** — 6 left classes vs `3 + 2 + 3 = 8`.
  It already fails at `n = 2`, where each `J`-class is a singleton (hence
  contains exactly 1 left class) while both partitions of 2 have hook lengths
  `{2, 1}`, i.e. 2 distinct ones. `1 ≠ 2`.

## Robustness of the reading

"Two-sided classes" / "left classes" are read as Green's `J`- and `L`-classes,
which is the standard reading for a monoid (and `D = J` for finite monoids, so
this coincides with the two-sided/left cell reading).

The second half of the conjecture is false under the **alternative
Kazhdan–Lusztig reading** as well. Under that reading the two-sided cells of the
Hecke algebra of `S_n` *are* indexed by partitions (so the first half would be
true for `S_n`), but the number of left cells in the cell `C_λ` is the number
`f^λ` of standard Young tableaux of shape `λ`, **not** the number of distinct
hook lengths. At `n = 2`: `f^(2) = f^(1,1) = 1`, while both partitions have 2
distinct hook lengths. So `1 ≠ 2` either way.

The six principal two-sided ideals of `H_3(0)`, as characteristic vectors on
`(id, s_1, s_2, s_1s_2, s_2s_1, w_0)`:

```
J(id)    = (1,1,1,1,1,1)     J(s_1)   = (0,1,0,1,1,1)
J(s_2)   = (0,0,1,1,1,1)     J(s_1s_2)= (0,0,0,1,0,1)
J(s_2s_1)= (0,0,0,0,1,1)     J(w_0)   = (0,0,0,0,0,1)
```

All six are distinct ⟹ 6 `J`-classes. Note the structural reason: `J`-triviality
means each `J`-class is a singleton, hence contains exactly one left class.
