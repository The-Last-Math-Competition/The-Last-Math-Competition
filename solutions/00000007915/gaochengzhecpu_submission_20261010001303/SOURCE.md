# 00000007915

**English.** Definition: The spectral-graph correspondence of the Ihara zeta: the explicit relation (Hashimoto formula) between the Ihara zeta zeta_X(u) of a k-regular graph X and the graph Laplacian spectrum. Conjecture: The poles of zeta_X(u) (as functions of the nontrivial spectral values of the graph) equidistribute on the free-group spectral radius circle |u| = 1/(2 sqrt(k-1)) on random regular graphs; and the deviation of zeta_X on the critical circle is controlled by the fluctuation of the Cheeger constants of new graphs in bijection covers, with deviation exponent 1/2 consistent with the Alon-Boppana separation constant. (Ihara pole equidistribution circle)

**中文。** 定义：Ihara zeta 的谱图对应：k-正则图 X 的 Ihara zeta ζ_X(u) 与图的 Laplacian 谱的显式关联（Hashimoto 公式）。猜想：ζ_X(u) 的极点（倒数为图的非平凡谱值的函数）在随机正则图上等分布于自由群的谱半径圆 |u|=1/(2√(k-1))；且 ζ_X 在临界圆上的偏差由图的双射覆盖（new graphs）的 Cheeger 常数的波动控制，偏差指数 1/2 与 Alon–Boppana 分离常数一致。
