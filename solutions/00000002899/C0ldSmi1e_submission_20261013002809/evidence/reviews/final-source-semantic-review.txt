2. Statement fidelity and interpretation boundary

displays the asymptotic lower bound q^d(1-c/q), followed by an attainment claim.
ProposedEventualLowerBound formalizes the necessary lower-bound component as

  exists c : Real, exists Q : Nat,
  for every actual finite field F with q=|F|>=Q,
    q^d(1-c/q) <= minimumKakeyaSize F d.

c is fixed as q grows but may depend on the fixed dimension d, as may Q.
The final negation holds for every natural d>=2. The natural threshold is
equivalent to an arbitrary real field-size threshold after taking a natural
upper bound. Arithmetic in the displayed inequality is real, including its
division and subtraction. No natural truncation changes its meaning.

This faithfully captures the displayed coefficient-one, fixed-c reading.
The source does not explicitly resolve the words "of type". If those words
were intended to suppress an arbitrary leading multiplicative constant and
mean only Omega(q^d), this is a different assertion and is not refuted here.
Similarly, c growing with q is outside the reading. This material limitation
was acknowledged in the plan and is prominently present in the draft report.
The PASS requires preserving that scope, not declaring all possible readings
of the informal original false. The undefined attainment method need not be
formalized to contradict its necessary lower-bound clause.

3. Actual affine geometry and exact counting

Ambient is the genuine coordinate vector space Fin d -> F. IsKakeya requires
for every nonzero vector a base a with a+t*v in K for every t in F. The
line_injective and line_card theorems certify that this parameterization has
exactly |F| distinct points. It therefore denotes a full affine line, not a
segment or a bounded sample. Quantification over all nonzero vectors covers
every projective direction; scalar multiples simply reparameterize a line.

The constructed family is exactly the finite set

  {w : F^(n+2) : w_0=0 or exists z, w_1+w_0^2=z^2}.

family_isKakeya uses the whole x_0=0 hyperplane for a direction with v_0=0,
including directions whose first two entries vanish. For v_0!=0 it sets
b=v_1/(2*v_0), base coordinate 1 to b^2, and other base coordinates to zero.
The identity w_1+w_0^2=(b+t*v_0)^2 then supplies every point on the required
line. The explicit hypothesis (2:F)!=0 and v_0!=0 justify division. Nothing
assumes that v_1 or a later coordinate is nonzero. All dimensions n+2 and all
directions in them are covered.

finite_field_square_count symbolically proves a singleton zero fiber and
two-element nonzero fibers {z,-z}. It obtains 2|S|=q+1 for the actual square
image. slice_card proves the translation bijection to that image. familyEquiv
is an explicit equivalence between members of the family and an allowed
first-coordinate/second-coordinate pair together with a free Fin n -> F
function, with both inverse laws checked in the source.

Consequently family_card_product counts q^n actual completions of each pair,
family_card_identity proves 2|K|+q^n=(q^2+2q)q^n, and family_card_real yields

  |K|=((q^2+2q-1)/2)q^n.

The q-1 in the intermediate natural count is backed by q>0 and the explicit
subtraction identity. The final fractional expression lives in the reals.
This chain is an exact count of the actual defined finset. It contains no
numerical surrogate or unsupported optimality claim.

4. True minimum and all finite-field/dimension bounds

minimumKakeyaSize is Nat.find over cardinalities of ALL Kakeya finsets in
the actual ambient space. The universal finset witnesses nonemptiness.
minimum_attained provides a genuine minimizer, and minimum_le_card compares
that minimum with every admissible finset. These are actual source theorems;
the quantity was not defined to be the family size or the proposed formula.

family_lt_proposed_bound applies the real exact count for arbitrary real c
when q>2(c+1). Positivity of q and q^n justifies the strict multiplication and
the denominators. The planar gap is

  q^2(1-c/q)-(q^2+2q-1)/2 = q(q/2-c-1)+1/2 > 0.

normalized_minimum_upper_bound invokes the actual family's Kakeya theorem
and minimum_le_card before dividing by q^(n+2)>0. It concludes a bound on the
genuine minimum, not merely on the constructed family's density. The added
normalized_minimum_upper_bound_in_each_dimension explicitly rewrites every
d>=2 as (d-2)+2. Thus the normalized bound is actually exposed for every such
dimension, including d=2. No claim that the minimum density converges to 1/2
or that the constructed family is optimal is made.

5. Final unbounded counterexamples and exact negation

prime_field_counterexamples takes arbitrary n:Nat, c:Real, and Q:Nat before
choosing its witness. exists_nat_gt chooses N with 2(c+1)<N. Euclid's theorem
is applied at max N (max Q 3), obtaining a prime p with p>=N, p>=Q, and p>=3.
This proves both the real size inequality and p>2, for every supplied c and
threshold. In particular, a negative or nonintegral c is not excluded.

The existential hp:p.Prime installs the ordinary Fact p.Prime instance, so
the witness space is the actual field ZMod p. The source proves (2:ZMod p)!=0:
otherwise the generic casting criterion gives p|2, contradicting p>2.
ZMod.card identifies the actual field cardinality as p. The witness K is the
already constructed family (ZMod p) n, and the theorem's conclusion explicitly
contains IsKakeya K, the genuine minimum comparison, and the strict inequality
for its actual cardinality. It does not existentially quantify an unrelated
number or assume the target bound as a hypothesis.

counterexamples_in_each_dimension has the full required signature:

  for every d:Nat with 2<=d, every c:Real, and every Q:Nat,
  exists an actual prime p>2 with p>=Q and an actual Kakeya

    minimumKakeyaSize (ZMod p) d <= |K| < p^d(1-c/p).

construction. Since Q is arbitrary, this is an unbounded family of field
orders, not a single small-field counterexample or a finite test list.
