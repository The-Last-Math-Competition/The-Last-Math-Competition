# 00000003968

**English.** Definition: A graceful labeling of a caterpillar tree is a bijective labeling whose edge differences are exactly 1 through n-1. Conjecture: The generating function of the number of graceful labelings of n-vertex caterpillar trees is a rational function, with denominator given by the partition function of the caterpillar spine length. (rationality of caterpillar graceful counting)

**中文。** 定义：毛虫树的优雅标号指使边差恰为 1 到 n−1 的双射标号。猜想：n 顶点毛虫树的优雅标号个数的生成函数为有理函数,分母由毛虫脊柱长的分拆函数给出。（毛虫优雅计数有理性）
