\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the complete table of ZFC-provable inequalities is
a finite DAG (about 200 vertices / $\aleph_0$ edges); the table is drawn
explicitly (traversal complete).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

A DAG has at most one directed edge per unordered vertex pair (both
directions would form a directed 2-cycle), so a DAG on $N$ vertices has
at most $\binom{N}{2}$ edges. For $N = 200$:
$\binom{200}{2} = 200 \cdot 199 / 2 = 19900$. A count bounded by 19900
is finite and cannot be $\aleph_0$: the displayed statement --- ``finite
DAG \dots\ $\aleph_0$ edges'' --- is internally contradictory.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} brute-forces acyclic orientations on small $n$
and counts pairs. The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the
pair count 19900, the boundedness, the general identity
$2\binom{n}{2} = n(n-1)$ at $n = 200$, and the refutation; all four
audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

Only the displayed self-contradiction is refuted.
