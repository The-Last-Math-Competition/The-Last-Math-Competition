\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Definition:} $P(t) = \sum b_{2i} t^i$ is the Betti-number
generating function of the Bott--Samelson variety
$BS(s_1,\dots,s_k)$. \textit{Conjecture:} the coefficients of $P(t)$
are strictly increasing up to the middle level, $P(t)$ is divisible by
$(1+t)^{\lfloor k/2 \rfloor}$ with monic quotient, and the quotient's
coefficients have no internal zeros when $s_1 \dots s_k$ is a reduced
word.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

For a reduced word the Bott--Samelson variety is an iterated
$\mathbb{P}^1$-bundle, so by the projective bundle formula
\[
P(t) = (1 + t^2)^k .
\]

\emph{Divisibility fails.} If $(1+t)\cdot Q(t) = (1+t^2)^2$, evaluating
at $t = -1$ gives $0 \cdot Q(-1) = (1+1)^2 = 4$, i.e.\ $0 = 4$ ---
absurd. In general $P(-1) = 2^k \ne 0$ for all $k \ge 1$, with
synthetic-division remainders $4, 16, 64$ at $k = 2, 4, 6$.

\emph{Strict increase fails.} $(1+t^2)^2 = 1 + 0\cdot t + 2t^2 +
0\cdot t^3 + t^4$ has vanishing odd-degree coefficients.

\emph{No internal zeros fails.} The quotient for $k = 6$,
$(1+t)^3(1-t)^6 = 1 - 3t + 0t^2 + 8t^3 - 6t^4 - 6t^5 + 8t^6 + 0t^7
- 3t^8 + t^9$, has internal zeros.

All three clauses fail for reduced words.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} performs the polynomial arithmetic over
$\mathbb{Z}[t]$: $P(-1) = 2^k$ for $k \le 6$, synthetic division
remainders $4, 16, 64$ at $k = 2, 4, 6$, the exact $k = 6$ quotient
list with internal zeros, and the $k = 2$ coefficient zeros. The Lean
package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the $k = 2$ divisibility
failure by the evaluation argument at $t = -1$, using only the clean
\texttt{Int.mul\_zero}; all three audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the $k = 2$ divisibility failure; the general-$k$
evaluation failure ($P(-1) = 2^k$) and the other clause failures are
carried by the exact script arithmetic. The reduced-word
iterated-$\mathbb{P}^1$-bundle structure and the projective bundle
formula are classical, cited in prose.
