- **Multi-basis coverage:** Because the conjecture does not specify the partner basis paired with the multisymmetric monomial basis $\{m_\Lambda\}_{\Lambda \vdash \mathbf{d}}$, we analyzed and disproved the claim across **all** standard multiplicative generator bases of $\Lambda^{(r)}_{\mathbf{d}}$:
  1. **Elementary multisymmetric basis $\{e_\Lambda\}$:** Fails permutation-triangularity already at $r = 3, \mathbf{d} = (1,1,1)$ ($\det = -2$ on a $\{0,1\}$-matrix), fails all three clauses at $r = 2, \mathbf{d} = (2,2)$ ($\det = 3$, non-triangular, and differs from $|\det M(e,m)_{(1,1)}| = 1$), and fails both triangularity and $\pm 2^z$ determinant even within multilinear degree $\mathbf{d} = (1^r)$ at $r = 4$ ($\det M(e,m)_{(1,1,1,1)} = -96 = -2^5 \cdot 3$).
  2. **Complete homogeneous multisymmetric basis $\{h_\Lambda\}$:** Strict positivity $M(h,m)_{\Lambda,\Gamma} \ge 1$ refutes permutation-triangularity whenever $N(\mathbf{d}) \ge 2$ (already at $r = 2, \mathbf{d} = (1,1)$), while $|\det M(h,m)_{\mathbf{d}}| = |\det M(e,m)_{\mathbf{d}}|$ gives $\det M(h,m)_{(2,2)} = 3$ and $\det M(h,m)_{(1,1,1,1)} = 96$.
  3. **Power-sum basis $\{p_\Lambda\}$ and augmented monomial basis $\{\tilde{m}_\Lambda\}$:** Proved lower triangular for all $r, \mathbf{d}$ with $\det = \prod_{\Lambda \vdash \mathbf{d}} \mathrm{aut}(\Lambda)$. Explained the positive $\mathbf{d} \in \{0,1,2\}^r$ power-of-two regime ($\det = 1, 2, 32$ at $\mathbf{d} = (1,1), (2,1), (2,2)$ for $r = 2$, which already refutes pure $r$-dependence), and proved universally for **every** $r \ge 1$ that $6 \mid \det M(p,m)_{(3,1,\dots,1)}$ (e.g., $\det M(p,m)_{(3,1)} = 12$).
  4. **Inverses, polarized scalings, and tensor products $\Lambda^{\otimes r}$:** Checked explicitly in both the paper and `verify.py`; none rescues the conjecture.
