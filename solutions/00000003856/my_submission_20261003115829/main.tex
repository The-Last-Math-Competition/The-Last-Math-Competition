\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} $\pi N\,G_{\max} - \log N$ converges in
distribution to a Gumbel law, with Gumbel scale parameter exactly
$1/\beta$ (CUE: $\beta = 2$).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

A Gumbel limit for a maximal gap arises from a Poisson
(exponential-tail) heuristic: $N \cdot \mathbb{P}(\mathrm{gap} > x
\cdot 2\pi/N) \approx e^{-x}$ gives the centering $x \approx \log
N$. But CUE eigenangles \emph{repel}: the maximal gap obeys the
empirical law
\[
\frac{N}{2\pi}\,G_{\max} \approx \sqrt{\log N}
\quad\text{(coefficient $1.00 \pm 0.01$ at $N = 400, 800, 1600$),}
\]
measured with exact Haar CUE sampling (Mezzadri QR convention):
$2.423 \pm 0.193$, $2.635 \pm 0.190$, $2.710 \pm 0.159$ against
$\sqrt{\log N} = 2.448, 2.585, 2.716$. Hence the conjecture's
statistic
\[
\pi N\,G_{\max} - \log N
\;=\; 2\pi^2 \cdot \frac{N\,G_{\max}}{2\pi} - \log N
\;\approx\; 2\pi^2\sqrt{\log N} - \log N
\]
drifts without bound (means $41.83 \pm 3.80$, $45.32 \pm 3.75$,
$46.11 \pm 3.13$), while the claimed limit Gumbel(scale $1/2$)
assigns $\mathbb{P}(X \ge 44) = e^{-88} < 10^{-38} < 2^{-126}$ to a
region the statistic visits at about half of all samples ---
convergence in distribution is impossible. The scale clause fails
independently: the observed spread ($\approx 3.4$, non-vanishing)
matches neither Gumbel scale $1/2$ ($\mathrm{sd} = \pi/(2\sqrt{6})
\approx 0.641$) nor scale $1$ ($\mathrm{sd} = \pi/\sqrt{6} \approx
1.282$).

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} performs the exact CUE sampling, verifies the
$\sqrt{\log N}$ law at three sizes, measures the divergent
statistic, certifies the tail chain $e^{-88} < 10^{-38} < 2^{-126}$,
and compares the standard deviations. The Lean package (core
Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the exact integer anchors:
\texttt{tail\_bound\_core} ($880 > 874$, i.e.\ $38\ln 10 < 88$ via
$\ln 10 < 23/10$), \texttt{ten38\_gt\_two126} ($2^{126} < 10^{38}$,
exact 39-digit comparison), \texttt{scale\_half},
\texttt{sd\_mismatch}, and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted}. All six
audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the tail chain and the scale anchors; the
empirical $\sqrt{\log N}$ law, the statistic's drift, and the sd
comparison are exact simulations (script). Both the Gumbel-limit
claim ($\log N$ centering) and the exact-scale clause are refuted at
$\beta = 2$; no claim is made about other centerings.
