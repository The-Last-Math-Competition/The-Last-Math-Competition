# 00000007914

**English.** Definition: The covolume spectrum of arithmetic subgroups: the limit-from-below spectrum S_arith of covolumes vol(Gamma\H) of arithmetic Fuchsian groups Gamma. Conjecture: S_arith is a well-ordered set (no descending chains); its k-th element v_k has the Siegel-type asymptotic v_k ~ c k^{-1} (log k)^{-2/3}; and the smallest noncompact arithmetic triangle group is exactly (2,3,7) (Hurwitz rigidity), the next smallest having an explicit trichotomy with covolume ratio 3/7. (arithmetic covolume well-ordering)

**中文。** 定义：自守群的算术子群的协体积谱：算术 Fuchsian 群 Γ 的协体积 vol(Γ\H) 的下极限谱 S_arith。猜想：S_arith 为良序集（无递降列）；其第 k 个元 v_k 的渐近 v_k ~ c·k^{-1}·(log k)^{-2/3}（Siegel 型计数）；且最小非紧算术三角群恰为 (2,3,7)（Hurwitz 刚性），次小者协体积比为 3/7 的显式三分律。
