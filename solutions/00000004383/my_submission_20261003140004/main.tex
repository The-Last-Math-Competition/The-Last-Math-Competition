\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the first five nonzero low-degree homotopy
groups of THH(S) are explicit direct sums of $\mathbb{F}_p$, and
the first nonzero group lies in degree 1 and is cyclic of order 4.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

THH of a ring spectrum $A$ is the realization of the cyclic bar
construction; for the sphere spectrum $S$ --- the initial object
among ring spectra --- the unit maps collapse the construction:
$\mathrm{THH}(S) \simeq S$.  Hence $\pi_*\mathrm{THH}(S) =
\pi_*^{\mathrm{st}}(S)$, the classical stable stems:

\[
\pi_0 = \mathbb{Z} \ (\text{infinite}), \qquad
\pi_1 = \mathbb{Z}/2, \qquad
\pi_3 = \mathbb{Z}/24 .
\]

Every row of the claimed table fails: $\pi_0$ is \emph{infinite},
while every direct sum of $\mathbb{F}_p$'s is finite; the first
nonzero positive-degree group is $\mathbb{Z}/2$ of order $2$, not
a cyclic group of order $4$ ($2 \ne 4$); and $\pi_3 = \mathbb{Z}/24$
has order $24$, decomposing as $\mathbb{Z}/8 \oplus \mathbb{Z}/3$
($8 \cdot 3 = 24$) --- not the claimed $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^2 \oplus
(\mathbb{Z}/3)^2$ of order $4 \cdot 9 = 36$ ($24 \ne 36$).  The
conjecture appears to conflate $\mathrm{THH}(S)$ with
B\"okstedt's $\mathrm{THH}(\mathbb{F}_p)$ computation, where the
$\mathbb{F}_p$-sum structure and order-$4$ phenomena genuinely
occur.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} verifies the group orders against the
claimed table.  The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
\texttt{first\_group\_order} ($2 \ne 4$),
\texttt{order\_mismatch\_3} ($24 \ne 36$),
\texttt{structure\_mismatch} ($4 \cdot 9 = 36$, $8 \cdot 3 = 24$,
$24 \ne 36$), \texttt{pi0\_infinite}, and
\texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All five audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the order arithmetic contradicting the table;
the identification $\mathrm{THH}(S) \simeq S$ and the stable-stem
groups are classical (Toda's table), cited in prose.  All rows of
the claimed table are refuted.
