# Conjecture 00000004507 — Closure System Representations of Galois Inverses

## Problem Statement

From `SOURCE.md`:

> **English.** Definition: A Galois connection is a monotone adjoint pair between posets. Conjecture: The minimal representation of its inverse is a closure system generated by units, the representation length is an explicit count of units, and finite lattices attain the lower bound 2. (closure representation lower bound of Galois inverses)
>
> **中文。** 定义：Galois 联系指偏序集间的单调伴随对。猜想：其逆的最小表示为单元生成的闭包系统,表示长度为单元数的显式计数,有限格达到下界 2。（Galois 逆闭包表示下界）

## Final Verdict: **Disproved (False as Stated)** — with Exact Salvaged Classification

Conjecture `00000004507` is **false as stated** under every standard order-theoretic reading of Galois inverses, closure systems, unit generation, and representation length.

### 1. Summary of Refutations

Let $\bigl(f, g\bigr)$ be a monotone Galois connection between posets $P$ and $Q$, meaning $f : P \to Q$ and $g : Q \to P$ are monotone maps satisfying

$$f(x) \le_Q y \iff x \le_P g(y)$$

for all $x \in P$ and $y \in Q$. Its right adjoint (upper Galois inverse) $g$ satisfies $f \circ g \circ f = f$ and $g \circ f \circ g = g$, and its image $\mathcal{C} = \mathrm{Im}(g) = \mathrm{Fix}(g \circ f)$ is the closed-element set of the closure operator $c = g \circ f$ on $P$.

1. **Failure of the Finite-Lattice Lower Bound $2$ Across All Readings (Readings I–IV):**
   - **Reading I — Minimal abstract meet-generating basis $\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})$** (under arbitrary meets $\bigwedge S$, including $\bigwedge \emptyset = \top_L$):
     - On the $1$-element finite lattice $C_1 = \{0\}$ with $\bigl(\mathrm{id}_{C_1}, \mathrm{id}_{C_1}\bigr)$, $\mathcal{M}(C_1) = \emptyset$ gives length $0 \lt 2$.
     - On the **nontrivial** $2$-element chain $C_2 = \{0, 1\}$ ($0 \lt 1$) even with the **bijective** identity Galois connection $\bigl(\mathrm{id}_{C_2}, \mathrm{id}_{C_2}\bigr)$, $\mathcal{M}(C_2) = \{0\}$ gives length $1 \lt 2$.
     - On **every** pair of finite lattices $L_1, L_2$ (even with $|L_1|, |L_2| \ge 2$), the constant monotone Galois connection $f(x) = \bot_{L_2}$, $g(y) = \top_{L_1}$ has $\mathcal{C} = \{\top_{L_1}\}$ and length $0 \lt 2$.
     - On **every** finite lattice $L$ with $|L| \ge 2$ and any $a \neq \top_L$, there is a **non-constant** monotone Galois connection between $L$ and $C_2$ with $\mathcal{C} = \{a, \top_L\}$ of length $1 \lt 2$.
   - **Reading II — Minimal concrete set-closure ground-set size $|U|$** for $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \bigl(\mathcal{P}(U), \subseteq\bigr)$:
     - Equals $|\mathcal{J}(\mathcal{C})|$ (count of join-irreducible elements), giving $0 \lt 2$ on $C_1$ and $1 \lt 2$ on $C_2$ with $\bigl(\mathrm{id}_{C_2}, \mathrm{id}_{C_2}\bigr)$ via $\mathcal{F} = \{\emptyset, \{1\}\} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(\{1\})$.
   - **Reading III — Minimal nonempty-meet generator count $|\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C}) \cup \{\top_L\}| = |\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})| + 1$ or closed-element count $|\mathcal{C}|$:**
     - Equals $1 \lt 2$ on $C_1$ with $\bigl(\mathrm{id}_{C_1}, \mathrm{id}_{C_1}\bigr)$ and equals $1 \lt 2$ on **every** finite lattice via the constant Galois connection $f(x) = \bot_{L_2}$, $g(y) = \top_{L_1}$.
   - **Reading IV — Minimal unit-implicational basis size $|\Sigma_{\min}(\mathcal{C})|$:**
     - Equals $0 \lt 2$ on every finite Boolean lattice $B_n = \mathcal{P}(\{1, \dots, n\})$ ($n \ge 0$) with $\bigl(\mathrm{id}_{B_n}, \mathrm{id}_{B_n}\bigr)$.

2. **Failure of Minimal Unit Generation on General Posets and Infinite Complete Lattices:**
   - On the $2$-element antichain $A_2 = \{0, 1\}$ with discrete order, $\mathrm{Im}(\mathrm{id}_{A_2}) = A_2$ has neither an empty meet (top) nor a binary meet $0 \wedge 1$.
   - On the complete distributive lattice $\mathrm{Cut}(\mathbb{D}) \cong \bigl([0, 1], \le\bigr)$ of open rounded upper Dedekind cuts of dyadic rationals in $\bigl(0, 1\bigr)$ with $\bigl(\mathrm{id}, \mathrm{id}\bigr)$, principal cuts meet-generate $\mathrm{Cut}(\mathbb{D})$, yet **every** meet-generating family $B$ admits an element $b_0 \in B$ such that $B \setminus \{b_0\}$ still meet-generates $\mathrm{Cut}(\mathbb{D})$ (and zero completely meet-irreducible elements exist).

3. **Non-Invertibility Without Restriction to Closed/Open Elements:**
   - General monotone Galois connections are not two-sided invertible, nor is $\bigl(g, f\bigr)$ a monotone Galois connection from $Q$ to $P$ without passing to order duals $Q^{\mathrm{op}}$ and $P^{\mathrm{op}}$. Instead, $f$ and $g$ restrict to mutually inverse order isomorphisms between $\mathrm{Fix}(g \circ f)$ and $\mathrm{Fix}(f \circ g)$.

### 2. Salvaged Theorems and Sharp Lower Bound Characterization

On any finite lattice $L$ with closure system $\mathcal{C} = \mathrm{Im}(g) \subseteq L$:
- $\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})$ is the **unique inclusion-minimum** (and minimum-cardinality) meet-generating set of $\mathcal{C}$, of exact length $|\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})|$.
- $|\mathcal{J}(\mathcal{C})|$ is the **exact minimum ground-set size** $|U|$ of a concrete set-closure representation $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(U)$ isomorphic to $\mathcal{C}$.
- Exact sharp thresholds hold:

$$|\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})| = 0 \iff |\mathcal{J}(\mathcal{C})| = 0 \iff |\mathcal{C}| = 1,$$

$$|\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})| = 1 \iff |\mathcal{J}(\mathcal{C})| = 1 \iff |\mathcal{C}| = 2,$$

$$|\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})| \ge 2 \iff |\mathcal{J}(\mathcal{C})| \ge 2 \iff |\mathcal{C}| \ge 3.$$

- Moreover, equality $|\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})| = 2$ (and $|\mathcal{J}(\mathcal{C})| = 2$) is attained up to order isomorphism **exclusively** by the $3$-element chain $C_3$ ($|\mathcal{C}| = 3$) and the $4$-element Boolean diamond $B_2$ ($|\mathcal{C}| = 4$).

| Instance | $\|\mathcal{C}\|$ | Reading I: $\|\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C})\|$ | Reading II: $\|\mathcal{J}(\mathcal{C})\|$ | Reading IIIa: $\|\mathcal{M}\| + 1$ | Reading IIIb: $\|\mathcal{C}\|$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $C_1$, $\bigl(\mathrm{id}_{C_1}, \mathrm{id}_{C_1}\bigr)$ | $1$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1$ | $1$ |
| Any $L_1, L_2$, constant $\bigl(\bot, \top\bigr)$ | $1$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1$ | $1$ |

| $C_3$, $\bigl(\mathrm{id}_{C_3}, \mathrm{id}_{C_3}\bigr)$ *(attains $2$)* | $3$ | $2$ | $2$ | $3$ | $3$ |
