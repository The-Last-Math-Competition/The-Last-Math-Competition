The explicit ratio criterion has a definite suﬀicient direction once the gap, eigenvalue con-
vention, and mixing metric are specified. We refute that direction with the following scope.
1. Gap and metric. The gap is the ordinary spectral gap of the reversible transition
operator. Mixing is measured in total variation from the uniform stationary law, with
the maximum over starting states. The source does not itself name a metric or give a
precise window definition. We state our definitions below and prove failure of both a
genuine fixed-threshold width test and the quantified standard TV cutoff criterion.
2. Two eigenvalue readings. We compute separately the multiplicity of the smallest
nontrivial eigenvalue of P and that of the smallest positive eigenvalue of I (cid:0)P. The
first is not silently replaced by the second. Both resulting ratios diverge in the same
counterexample.
