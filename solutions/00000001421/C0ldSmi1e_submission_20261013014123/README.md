# Conjecture 00000001421: a counterexample to the explicit spectral ratio criterion

For every integer `N ≥ 4`, take the exactly half-lazy uniform Cayley walk on
`ZMod 2 × ZMod N` with support
`{(0,h) : h ≠ 0} ∪ {(1,0)}`. Its ordinary spectral gap is `1/N`.
The minimum nontrivial transition eigenvalue has multiplicity `N−1`, so its
multiplicity divided by the gap is `N(N−1) → ∞`. If the minimum instead means
the smallest positive eigenvalue of `I−P`, its multiplicity is one and the
ratio is `N → ∞`. This same family has no total variation cutoff.

The exact distance to its uniform stationary law is `1−1/(2N)` at time zero
and `(1−1/N)^t / 2` for every positive integer time, from every starting state.
Consequently `t_mix(3/4)=1` while `T=t_mix(1/4)≥N/2 → ∞`. The genuine
quarter-to-three-quarter window is `T−1`, and `T/(T−1) → 1`, rather than
infinity. The usual cutoff condition, quantified over all tolerances
`0<ε<1/2`, also fails.

## Scope of the disproof

This refutes the explicit numerical **sufficient direction under the ordinary
spectral gap and standard total variation cutoff interpretation**. The exact
English and Chinese original is preserved in `conjecture.md` and the report.
The original does not define a conditioning operation, a conditional spectral
gap, second-order behavior, a mixing metric/window, or a precise
Diaconis–Saloff-Coste comparison assertion. This submission does not invent
those definitions or claim to disprove an unspecified further restriction.
The characters are actual characters of the group generated by the support;
the support is proved to generate the whole group. Both minimum-eigenvalue
readings described above are handled separately.

## Formal result

`Conjecture1421.verified_counterexample` constructs the closed
`CounterexampleCertificate`. It includes the actual support and stochastic
walk, growing group sizes, the complete actual character basis, characteristic
polynomials and root multiplicities of `P` and `I−P`, the actual matrix-power
and TV formulas, attained least mixing times, both spectral-ratio limits, and
the failure of both cutoff criteria. No profile hypothesis remains in it.

The four final corollaries are `literal_sufficient_criterion_false`,
`gap_mode_sufficient_criterion_false`, `literal_window_sufficient_criterion_false`,
and `gap_mode_window_sufficient_criterion_false`, all in namespace
`Conjecture1421`. They negate the two proposed sufficient implications for
this concrete family. Its verified membership in the stated walk class makes
it a witness against the corresponding universal numerical implication.
