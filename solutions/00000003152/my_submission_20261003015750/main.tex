\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the BM inequality for groups: the lower bound of
the Haar measure of product sets is exponential in the sum of measures,
$\mu(AB) \ge \exp(\mu(A) + \mu(B))$, with equality on cosets.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

$\mu$ is a normalized Haar measure, so $\mu(AB) \le 1$ always. By the
convexity inequality $\exp(t) \ge 1 + t$, the claimed lower bound
satisfies $\exp(\mu(A)+\mu(B)) > 1$ whenever $\mu(A) + \mu(B) > 0$ ---
e.g.\ $\mu(A) = \mu(B) = 1/10$ gives $\exp(1/5) > 6/5 > 1$. The claimed
inequality is unsatisfiable for every such pair of sets: it is not a
Brunn--Minkowski-type statement but an impossible one.
