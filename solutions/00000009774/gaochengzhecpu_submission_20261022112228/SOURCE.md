# 00000009774

**English.** Definition: Szego's strong limit theorem: logarithmic asymptotics of Toeplitz determinants (secondary terms given by Widom theory). Conjecture: The secondary coefficient of Toeplitz determinants on general domains is given entirely by corner angles of the boundary; in the smooth case the secondary term vanishes, corner contributions being local multiplicative factors with no global correction. (Szego secondary corner law)

**中文。** 定义：Szegő 强极限定理：Toeplitz 行列式的对数渐近（次主项由 Widom 理论给出）。猜想：一般区域上 Toeplitz 行列式的次主项系数完全由边界角落的角代数给出；光滑情形次主项消失且角落贡献为局部乘性因子，无整体修正。（Szego 次主项角落律）
