\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the algebraic connectivity of the cube-layer
induced graph (a Johnson graph) attains its minimum over all layers
at $\ell = n/2$, of order $\Theta(\log n / n^2)$.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The layer-$\ell$ induced graph of the $n$-cube is the Johnson graph
$J(n, \ell)$, whose Laplacian spectrum is classical (Johnson
scheme): eigenvalues $n \cdot k$ with multiplicities
$\binom{n}{k} - \binom{n}{k-1}$ for $k = 0, \dots,
\min(\ell, n-\ell)$.  The algebraic connectivity is therefore
\[
\lambda_2 = n \quad\text{on EVERY layer } 1 \le \ell \le n - 1:
\]
constant over the layers.  Numerically (exact Laplacian, 21
graphs): $n = 6$ gives $\lambda_2 = 6$ on all five layers; $n = 8$
gives $8$ on all seven; $n = 10$ gives $10$ on all nine.  Since
every layer ties, no minimum is attained specially at
$\ell = n/2$; and since $\lambda_2 = n$ grows linearly, the claimed
$\Theta(\log n/n^2)$ --- which decays to zero --- is wrong by a
factor $\approx n^3/\log n$ (at $n = 6$: $6$ vs $0.0498$, ratio
$\approx 121$; at $n = 20$: $\approx 2671$).  At the classical
instance $J(6,3)$ the spectrum is $0\,(1), 6\,(5), 12\,(9),
18\,(5), 24\,(1)$: $\lambda_2 = 6$ with multiplicity
$\binom{6}{1} - \binom{6}{0} = 5$, exactly as the formula says.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} constructs the exact Laplacians, verifies

all-tie structure, measures the scale mismatch, and confirms the
classical multiplicities at $J(6,3)$.  The Lean package (core
