\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the third cumulant of $\mathrm{Tr}(U)$ for
Haar-uniform $U \in U(N)$ equals $\kappa_3 = 2\pi i/(3N)$ (nonzero).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Haar measure is invariant under $U \mapsto e^{i\theta}U$, and
$\mathrm{Tr}(e^{i\theta}U) = e^{i\theta}\,\mathrm{Tr}(U)$. Hence
$E[X^3] = e^{3i\theta} E[X^3]$ for every $\theta$; at $\theta =
\pi/3$: $E[X^3] = 0$, and identically $E[X] = 0$. The third cumulant
$\kappa_3 = m_3 - 3m_2 m_1 + 2 m_1^3$ therefore vanishes:
$\kappa_3 = 0$ for every value of the second moment. The claimed
$2\pi i/(3N) \ne 0$ (imaginary part $2\pi/(3N) > 0$) is contradicted.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} Monte-Carlo samples Haar $U(N)$ (QR of Ginibre)
and confirms $E[(\mathrm{Tr}U)^3] \approx 0$. The Lean package (core
Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the general cumulant-vanishing statement
(exact arithmetic, general in $m_2$); all three audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}. The rotation symmetry, cumulant
polynomial, and $\pi > 3$ are classical and cited.

\section{Boundary}

Only the claimed nonzero third cumulant is refuted.
