PROOF REVIEW

Also deck G = deck G by reflexivity. Applying the proposed universal rigidity clause at n=2 to the empty graph on Fin 3 twice therefore gives 2 <= 0. This contradiction proves not_reconstructionRigidity. Its use of the second conjunct proves originalClaim_false without assuming or deciding the classical reconstruction assertion.
