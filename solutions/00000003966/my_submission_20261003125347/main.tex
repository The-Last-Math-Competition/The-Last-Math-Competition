\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the smallest nonplanar graph requiring 3 pages
is $K_{3,3}$; every 1-planar graph needs at most 4 pages, and this
bound is optimal.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Both $K_5$ and $K_{3,3}$ are nonplanar 3-page graphs --- but $K_5$
is smaller: 5 vertices (10 edges) against $K_{3,3}$'s 6 vertices
(9 edges).

\textbf{$K_5$ is nonplanar}: its $10$ edges violate the Euler bound
$e \le 3v - 6 = 9$ (kernel-certified).  \textbf{$K_5$ requires 3
pages}: Bernhart's theorem $\mathrm{bt}(K_n) = \lceil n/2 \rceil$
for $n \ge 4$ gives $\lceil 5/2 \rceil = 3$; independently, the
script exhausts all $120$ circular vertex orders and finds no
2-page embedding ($\mathrm{bt}(K_5) \ge 3$), while the standard
3-page drawing gives $\mathrm{bt}(K_5) \le 3$.  \textbf{$K_5$ has
$5 < 6$ vertices}.  Hence the smallest nonplanar 3-page graph is
$K_5$, not $K_{3,3}$: the conjecture's first clause is false.  (The
3-page nonplanar graphs are exactly those containing a subdivision
of $K_5$ or $K_{3,3}$ --- Bernhart--Kainen --- and $K_5$ is minimal
by vertex count.)  $K_5$ is also 1-planar ($10 \le 4\cdot5 - 8 =
12$), consistent with the second clause, which is not needed for
the refutation.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} exhausts all 120 circular orders of $K_5$
(no 2-page embedding), checks the Euler violation $10 > 9$, the
vertex comparison $5 < 6$, and the 1-planar count $10 \le 12$.  The
Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
\texttt{K5\_edges}, \texttt{K5\_nonplanar}, \texttt{K5\_pages},
\texttt{fewer\_vertices}, \texttt{K33\_basic},
\texttt{one\_planar\_count}, and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All
seven audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any
axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the exact counts and inequalities; the
book-thickness classification $\mathrm{bt}(K_n) = \lceil n/2 \rceil$
is classical (Bernhart), and the 2-page non-embeddability is
verified by exhaustive search.  The ``smallest is $K_{3,3}$''
clause is refuted; no claim is made about 4-page optimality for
1-planar graphs.
