# 00000005838

**English.** Definition: Blocks are the primitive partition units of characters (p-blocks), with the involution case. Conjecture: The blocks of the involution centralizer are given by the real-valued dichotomy, the kernel of the dichotomy being the real-valued criterion; the number of real-valued blocks is half the total; and non-real blocks appear in pairs whose characters are complex conjugates. (real-valued dichotomy criterion of blocks)

**中文。** 定义：块：特征标的本原分割单元（p 块）；对合情形。猜想：对合中心化子的块由实值二分给出且二分的核为实值判别；实值块的个数为全块数之半；非实值块成对出现且对的特征标互为复共轭。（块亏类实值二分判据）
