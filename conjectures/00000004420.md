# 00000004420

**English.** Definition: Cycle counts and tree counts are the two families of local invariants of graph limits. Conjecture: There exist two non-isomorphic bounded-degree graph limits whose cycle counts all agree while their neighborhood distributions differ, the minimal degree of the separation is 3, and it is realized by limits of explicit same-degree graph sequences. (cycle count-neighborhood distribution separation)

**中文。** 定义：圈计数与树计数指图限的两类局部不变量。猜想：存在两个不同构的有界度图限其一切圈计数相同但邻域分布相异,分离的最小度为 3,由显式同度图列极限给出。（圈计数-邻域分布分离）
