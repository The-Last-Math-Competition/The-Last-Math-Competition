# 00000003988

**English.** Definition: A k-CNF tautology is a clause-form tautology in negation normal form. Conjecture: Every k-CNF tautology has a Frege proof of length O(n^{ceil(k/2)}), and the exponent ceil(k/2) is proven optimal by explicit constructions. (optimal kCNF Frege exponent)

**中文。** 定义：k-CNF 恒真式指否定范式下恒真的子句式。猜想：一切 k-CNF 恒真式的 Frege 证明长度为 O(n^{⌈k/2⌉}),且指数 ⌈k/2⌉ 由显式构造证明最优。（kCNF Frege 指数最优）
