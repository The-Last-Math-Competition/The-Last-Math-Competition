# 00000009608

**MSH-28 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: In S^3 the only continuous families of minimal surfaces are the Clifford tori (Lawson rigidity); the continuous-family problem for higher-dimensional spheres. Conjecture: In S^{n+1} the only continuous families of minimal hypersurfaces are the equatorial and Clifford families; any new continuous family must appear at n at least 6 and must be isoparametric, with the exceptions of the isoparametric classification exhausted by the Cartan list. (Lawson generalization of continuous families)

**中文。** 定义：S^3 中连续族极小曲面仅为 Clifford 环面（Lawson 刚性）；高维球面的连续族问题。猜想：S^{n+1} 中连续族极小超曲面仅有赤道族与 Clifford 型两族；任何新连续族必然在 n≥6 出现且必为等参曲面，等参分类的例外由 Cartan 列表穷尽。（连续族 Lawson 推广）
