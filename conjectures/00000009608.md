# 00000009608

**MSH-28 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: S^3 in continuous family minimal surface only is continuous family problem of Clifford ring plane (Lawson rigidity); high dimension ball plane. Conjecture: S^{n+1} in continuous family minimal hypersurface only has family and Clifford type two family; new continuous family necessarily in n≥6 and necessarily is exception of isoparametric surface, isoparametric classification by Cartan list. (continuous family Lawson)

**中文。** 定义：S^3 中连续族极小曲面仅为 Clifford 环面（Lawson 刚性）；高维球面的连续族问题。猜想：S^{n+1} 中连续族极小超曲面仅有赤道族与 Clifford 型两族；任何新连续族必然在 n≥6 出现且必为等参曲面，等参分类的例外由 Cartan 列表穷尽。（连续族 Lawson 推广）
