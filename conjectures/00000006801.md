# 00000006801

**English.** Definition: Basis pursuit is the convexified approximation. Conjecture: The convex recovery of the pursuit holds, the equivalence of the relaxation being necessary and sufficient for sparsity. (sparse-criterion convex relaxation of basis pursuit)

**中文。** 定义：基追踪：凸化逼近。猜想：追踪的凸恢复：逼近的凸松弛且弛的等价为稀疏充要。（基追踪凸松弛逼近凸恢复稀疏充要等价 basis pursuit）
