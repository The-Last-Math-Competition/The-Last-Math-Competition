# 00000002312

**FR-22 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: The n-Engel condition [x, _n y] = 1 is the identity of n-fold commutators. Conjecture: 5-Engel groups are locally nilpotent; the combinatorial reduction of the proof path: via the vanishing of the 5th-power identities of the associated Lie ring (prime by prime at characteristics 2, 3, 5).

**中文。** 定义：n-Engel 条件 [x,_n y]=1 指 n 重换位子的恒等。猜想：5-Engel 群为局部幂零;证明路径的组合约简:由关联 Lie 环的 5 次恒等的消没(在特征 2,3,5 的逐素数)。
