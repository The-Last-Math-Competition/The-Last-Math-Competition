# 00000007630

**GA5-30 △** — source: 239_几何代数III.md

**English.** Definition: Idempotent spectra and zero-divisor spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two Clifford algebras with the same idempotent spectrum but different zero-divisor spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit signature pair with the same idempotents but different zero divisors. (same idempotent-different zero-divisor separation)

**中文。** 定义：幂等谱与零因子谱指两层。猜想：存在两 Clifford 代数的幂等元谱相同而零因子谱不同,分离由同幂等异零因子的显式符号差对实现。（幂等同-零因子异分离）
