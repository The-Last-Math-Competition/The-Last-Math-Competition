# 00000008453

**English.** Definition: The girth of a triangulation is the minimal combinatorial length of a bisimplex chain. Conjecture: Hyperbolic 3-manifolds with girth at least 4 exist in every volume class; the minimal volume with girth at least 5 is an explicit constant not exceeding 3.664. (minimal volume at given girth)

**中文。** 定义：三角剖分的 girth 指双单纯形链的最小组合长度。猜想：girth ≥ 4 的双曲三流形在一切体积类中存在；girth ≥ 5 的最小体积为显式常数且不超过 3.664。（girth 最小体积）
