**English.** Definition: Quasiperiodicity is the local period deviation of the height function. Conjecture: The period upper bound is linearly controlled by the partial quotients of the continued fraction of the rotation number, the bound is tight, and the upper bound is attained by irrational sequences. (continued-fraction linear upper bound of quasiperiods)

**中文。** 定义：拟周期性指高度函数的局部周期偏差。猜想：周期上界由旋转数连分母的部分商线性控制,界紧且上界由无理数序列达到。（拟周期连分母线性上界）
