# 00000009641

**HSS-01 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: High-dimensional universality of Wigner matrices with iid upper-triangular entries: the question of which marginal and joint moment conditions determine spectral statistic limits. Conjecture: Correlation functions of order three and above converge, under only uniformly bounded fourth moments, to explicit kernel processes of the Gaussian unitary ensemble; higher-order kernels are completely given by determinant formulas of the sine kernel, with no new free parameters. (higher-order correlation universality)

**中文。** 定义：Wigner 矩阵的独立同分布上三角元的高维普适性：边缘与联合矩条件决定谱统计极限的问题。猜想：三点及以上相关函数在仅四阶矩一致有界条件下收敛到高斯酉系综的显式核过程；高阶核由 sine 核的行列式公式完全给出，无新自由参数。（高阶相关普适性）
