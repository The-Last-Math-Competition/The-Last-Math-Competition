# 00000007423

**English.** Definition: Plunnecke inequalities with the multiplicativity of sumsets. Conjecture: The graph proof of the inequality holds, and the combination of the proof is the amplification graph. (amplification-graph Plunnecke proof)

**中文。** 定义：Plünnecke 不等式：和集的乘性。猜想：Plünnecke 的图论：不等式的图证明且明的组合为放大图。（Plunnecke 放大图不等式图论证明组合）
