# 00000008820

**CVX-20 ★** — source: 279_凸优化的迭代复杂度.md

**English.** Definition: The condition-number-free geometric descent GD (Bubeck-Lee-Singh type): minimizing iterations for convex objectives controlled by nested shrinkage of ball envelopes at historical best values. Conjecture: The shrinking radius of GD satisfies the recursion r_{k+1} <= r_k(1 - 1/sqrt nu), where nu is the ratio of upper to lower curvature of the objective, with iteration count not explicitly depending on the condition number; each shrink is locked by a single support point of the quadratic upper envelope; geometric descent and Nesterov acceleration are equivalent in asymptotic iteration complexity, with an explicit reparametrization as the equivalence map; and interpolation transfer between the strongly convex and convex domains splits the convergence rate exactly between linear and O(1/k^2). (geometric descent curvature-ratio envelope equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：与条件数无关的几何下降法 GD（Bubeck–Lee–Singh 型）：以历史最优值处球套的嵌套收缩控制凸目标的最小化迭代。猜想：GD 的收缩半径满足递推 r_{k+1} ≤ r_k(1-1/√ν)，ν 为目标的上曲率与下曲率之比，迭代数不显式依赖条件数；每步收缩由二次上包络的单个支撑点锁定；几何下降与 Nesterov 加速在渐近迭代复杂度上等价且等价映射为显式重参数化；强凸与凸两域间的插值迁移使收敛率恰分界为线性率与 O(1/k²) 率。
