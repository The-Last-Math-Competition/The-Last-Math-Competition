# 00000003941

**English.** Definition: Treewidth is the minimum over tree decompositions of the bag width minus one. Conjecture: The exact treewidth of an n-vertex planar graph can be computed in 2^{O(sqrt k)} * n time, where k is the treewidth; under ETH any algorithm requires 2^{Omega(sqrt k)} time, so the exponential constants of the two bounds differ by at most a constant factor. (planar treewidth matching exponent)

**中文。** 定义：树宽指树分解袋宽减一的最小值。猜想：n 顶点平面图的精确树宽可在 2^{O(√k)}·n 时间内求出(k 为树宽),且在 ETH 假设下任何算法需 2^{Ω(√k)} 时间,两端指数常数相差不超过常数因子。（平面树宽匹配指数）
