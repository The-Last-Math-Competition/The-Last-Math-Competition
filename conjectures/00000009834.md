# 00000009834

**English.** Definition: Sparse domination: pointwise control of operators by sparse-family sums. Conjecture: The attainable range of the density parameter alpha of sparse domination is an open interval; the supremum alpha = 1 is unattainable, and the optimal choice of any sparse family realizes the pointwise supremum, with the supremum function Bohr increasing. (sparse density supremum)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏控制：算子被稀疏族和函数的点态控制。猜想：稀疏控制的密度参数 α 的可达范围为开区间；上确界 α=1 不可达且任意稀疏族的最优选择实现点态确界，确界函数为 Bohr 上升。（稀疏密度上确界）
