# 00000004692

**English.** Definition: A subshift is a closed invariant set of symbol sequences, and entropy is the limit of the language growth rate. Conjecture: The entropy values of subshifts of finite type are the logarithms of algebraic numbers, the closure of the entropy value domain of general subshifts is the whole nonnegative axis, and the stratification is characterized explicitly by the language complexity growth type. (stratified characterization of shift entropy values)

**中文。** 定义：子移位指符号列的闭不变集,熵指语言增长率的极限。猜想：有限型子移位的熵值为对数代数数集,一般子移位的熵值域闭包为全非负轴,分层由语言复杂度增长型显式刻画。（移位熵值域分层刻画）
