# 00000009789

**English.** Definition: Approximation properties of operator ideals: finite-rank density in Banach ideals. Conjecture: Except for the trace class, symmetric ideals do not have finite-rank density in the trace-norm topology; the explicit distance of non-density is given by the decay rate of the renormalization function of the ideal at infinity, and the critical ideal is the logarithmic power. (ideal finite-rank density threshold)

**中文。** 定义：算子理想的逼近性质：Banach 理想中的有限秩稠密性。猜想：除迹类外的对称理想在迹范数拓扑下有限秩不稠密；不稠密性的显式距离由理想的重整函数在无穷远的衰减率给出且临界理想为对数幂。（理想有限秩稠密门槛）
