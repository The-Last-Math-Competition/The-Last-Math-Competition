# 00000001090

**English.** Definition: The automorphism group of an MDS code over GF(3) of type [12,6] is the stabilizer of its permutation equivalence class. Conjecture: The maximal order of this automorphism group is 660 (attained by an M₁₁ embedding); the group orders of all other equivalence classes divide 132.

**中文。** 定义：GF(3) 上 [12,6] MDS 码的自同构群为其置换等价类的稳定群。猜想：该自同构群的阶的最大值为 660(由 M₁₁ 的嵌入取得);一切其他等价类的群阶整除 132。
