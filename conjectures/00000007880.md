# 00000007880

**English.** Definition: The tilting spectrum of higher Auslander-Reiten theory: the number n(d) of indecomposable direct summands of d-tilting modules in d-cluster tilting categories. Conjecture: n(d) is determined by the dimension-vector spectrum of the d-AR quiver of the finite-dimensional algebra Lambda, the spectrum being finite if and only if Lambda is d-representation finite; and the count of equivalence classes of d-representation finite algebras (with e the edge count of the fixed quiver) grows polynomially in d as P_e(d), with P_e of degree e-1 and leading coefficient the Cayley tree count (e+1)^{e-2}/e!. (d-representation finite polynomial counting)

**中文。** 定义：higher Auslander–Reiten 的 tilting 谱：d-簇倾斜范畴的 d-tilting 模的不可分解直和项数 n(d)。猜想：n(d) 由有限维代数 Λ 的 d-AR 箭图的维数向量谱决定，谱为有限集当且仅当 Λ 为 d-表示有限；且 d-表示有限代数的等价类的计数（固定箭图 quiver 的边数 e）按 d 的增长为多项式 P_e(d)，P_e 的次数为 e-1，首项系数为 Cayley 树计数 (e+1)^{e-2}/e!。
