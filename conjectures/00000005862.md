**English.** Definition: The classical lemma is the error-bound framework of polynomial approximation. Conjecture: The two-dimensional constant is explicit in the symmetric difference of domains, controlled for simplices by the circumradius-to-inradius ratio, and the kernel of the ratio is shape regularity. (circumradius-inradius ratio of shape regularity in the Bramble-Hilbert lemma)
