# 00000001844

**RM-04 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The hole probability of the Ginibre ensemble is the probability of no eigenvalues in a disk. Conjecture: The hole probability at radius r is asymptotically exp(−c·r⁴ + O(r³)) with c = 1/4 (the constant correction term has coefficient 4/3 on r³); via a Jancovici–Lebowitz–Manificat type Coulomb-gas energy computation.

**中文。** 定义：Ginibre 系综的 hole probability 指圆盘无特征值的概率。猜想：半径 r 的 hole probability 渐近为 exp(−c·r⁴+O(r³)),c=1/4(常数修正项为 r³ 的 4/3 系数);由 Jancovici–Lebowitz–Manificat 型库仑气体能量计算。
