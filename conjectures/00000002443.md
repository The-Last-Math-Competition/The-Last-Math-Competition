**English.** Definition: The hyperjump spectrum of an automorphism action is the realization set of Borel spectral functions on orbits. Conjecture: The hyperjump spectrum of the Aut(Q,<) action equals all Σ^0_α degrees (α < ω₁); the realization density of the spectrum under this action is full (every degree has a representative).

**中文。** 定义：自同构作用的超跃度谱指 Borel 谱函数在轨道上的实现集。猜想：Aut(Q,<) 作用的超跃度谱 = 一切 Σ^0_α 度(α<ω₁);谱在该作用下的实现密度为满(每个度皆有代表)。
