# 00000006308

**English.** Definition: The zeroth derivative is the derivative group expression of the zeta-regularized determinant. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit group expression of the zeroth derivative and the ratio with the classical constant is one after normalization; the kernel of the normalization is the independent summation of connected components; and the tangent space of the derivative is the spectral difference of the harmonic oscillator. (normalized ratio conversion of derivative group expressions)

**中文。** 定义：零阶导数：ζ 正则化行列式的导数群表达。猜想：换算常数为零阶导数的群表达显式且与经典常数的比值一旦归一；归一的核为连通分量的独立求和；导数的切空间为谐振子的谱差。（导数群表达比值归一换算）
