**English.** Definition: The Kauffman bracket homology spectrum is its graded dimension sequence. Conjecture: There exist knots with trivial bracket homology but different spectra, of minimal crossing number 16, whose four-ball genus spectra differ by exactly one. (minimal example of bracket spectrum separation)
