# 00000008838

**English.** Definition: The alternating direction method of multipliers ADMM. Conjecture: ADMM always converges in the two-block case; three or more blocks require Gauss-Southwell type corrections with correction parameter lower bound one half. (ADMM three-block correction lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：乘子交替方向法 ADMM。猜想：ADMM 在两块情形恒收敛；三块以上需 Gauss–Southwell 型修正且修正参数下界为一半。（ADMM 三块修正下界）
