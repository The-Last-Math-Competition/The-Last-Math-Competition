**English.** Definition: Unirationality of M_g means it admits a rational cover. Conjecture: The maximal unirational g is 22 (M₂₂ is unirational, and the unirationality status of M₂₃ gives the upper boundary); once the boundary is determined, unirationality fails for g ≥ 23.
