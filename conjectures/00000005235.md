**English.** Definition: The Mori cone is the effective dual cone of curves. Conjecture: The coincidence criterion of the effective cone with the Mori cone is an explicit family of inequalities, coincidence holds together with the rigidity of the added irreducible divisors, and the criterion is complete and the bound tight. (rigidity criterion of effective-Mori cone coincidence)
