# 00000009105

**English.** Definition: Buffon's needle: the intersection integral with random lines. Conjecture: The asymptotic constant of intersection counting equals an explicit linear expression in length; and the slope of the linear expression is the universal Buffon constant. (Buffon intersection linear expression)

**中文。** 定义：Buffon 投针为随机直线的相交积分。猜想：交点计数的渐近常数等于长度的显式线性式；且线性式的斜率为通用 Buffon 常数。（Buffon 交点线性式）
