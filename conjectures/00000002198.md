# 00000002198

**English.** Definition: The weak order lattice is the poset of permutations under weak order. Conjecture: The count of its maximal antichains is given by an explicit bijection of Eulerian numbers (the bijection via balancing double staircases); the antichain count equals second-order Eulerian numbers.

**中文。** 定义：weak order 格指排列的弱序偏序。猜想：其最大反链的计数由 Eulerian 数的显式双射给出(双射由双阶梯的均衡);反链计数 = 第二类 Eulerian 数。
