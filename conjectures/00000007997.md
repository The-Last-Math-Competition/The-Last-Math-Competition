# 00000007997

**English.** Definition: The doubling constant of p-harmonic measure: the doubling exponent D(n,p) of p-harmonic measure from ball to ball. Conjecture: D <= exp(c(p) n) with an explicit exponent; the optimal value of D is 2^{alpha(p)} (alpha the optimal Holder exponent of p-harmonic functions); and the failure boundary of doubling (the minimal roughness of nondoubling regions) is determined by the critical band of inverse dimension s < n-p of Ahlfors regular measures, inside which the boundary between doubling and nondoubling regions is the integer part of the fractal exponent of snowflake-type boundaries. (p-harmonic doubling snowflake boundary)

**中文。** 定义：p-调和测度的 Doubling 常数：球到球的 p-调和测度的 doubling 指数 D(n,p)。猜想：D ≤ exp(c(p)·n) 的显式指数；且 D 的最优值为 2^{α(p)}（α 为 p-调和函数的最佳 Hölder 指数）；doubling 的失效边界（非 doubling 区域的最小粗糙度）由 Ahlfors 正则测度的逆维数 s<n-p 的临界带决定，带内 doubling 与非 doubling 区域的分界为 snowflake 型边界的分形指数的整数部分。
