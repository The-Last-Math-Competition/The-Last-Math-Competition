# 00000007832

**English.** Definition: The stacky Euler characteristic e(Mbar_{g,n}) of the Deligne-Mumford orbifold. Conjecture: The generating function of the sum over g of e(Mbar_{g,n}) t^g is a polynomial in Fourier coefficients of a quasimodular form of weight (n+1)/2; and the poles at all roots of unity in t are simple with multiplicity exactly 1, the residues given by the one-vertex stable graph contribution at infinity. (stacky Euler quasimodular generation)

**中文。** 定义：栈式 Euler 特征：e(Mbar_{g,n}) 的 orbifold（Deligne–Mumford 栈）Euler 数。猜想：Σ_{g} e(Mbar_{g,n})t^g 的生成函数为权 (n+1)/2 的拟模形式的 Fourier 系数多项式；且系数的极点在 t 的所有单位根处重数恰为 1（简单极点），极点残数由 ∞ 处的 stable graph 的单点图贡献给出。
