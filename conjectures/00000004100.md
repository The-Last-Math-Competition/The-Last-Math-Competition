# 00000004100

**CG4-10 △** — source: 122_组合几何II_配置空间.md

**English.** Definition: Homeomorphic equivalence of graphs is isomorphism after subdivision. Conjecture: There exist two graphs that are not homeomorphically equivalent yet whose configuration spaces are homotopy equivalent for all n, while the separation of homeomorphic inequivalence can already occur at n = 1. (hierarchy of graph equivalence-configuration separation)

**中文。** 定义：图的同胚等价指经细分后同构。猜想：存在两个同胚不等价的图,其对一切 n 的配置空间同伦等价;而同胚不等价的分离在 n = 1 已可发生。（图等价-配置分离层级）
