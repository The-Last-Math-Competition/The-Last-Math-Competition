# 00000004099

**English.** Definition: An orbifold configuration is a configuration space on a global-quotient orbifold, and the age is the conformal weight of the shift numbers. Conjecture: Its Euler characteristic equals the finite sum of the Euler characteristics of the fixed-point sub-orbifolds weighted by powers of the age, with weight exponent exactly the particle number. (age-weighted sum of orbifold configurations)

**中文。** 定义：orbifold 配置指全局商 orbifold 上的配置空间,age 指移位数的共形权。猜想：其 Euler 特征等于各不动点子 orbifold 的 Euler 特征按 age 的幂加权的有限和,权重指数恰为粒子数。（orbifold 配置 age 加权和）
