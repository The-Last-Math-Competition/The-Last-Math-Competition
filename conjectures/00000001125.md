# 00000001125

**English.** Definition: The multiplicity spectrum of the restriction to the finite subalgebra g of an integrable level-ℓ module L(Λ) is the vector of multiplicities of the finite-dimensional irreducibles L(μ). Conjecture: The normalized multiplicity spectrum converges componentwise, as ℓ → ∞, to the character coefficients of χ(V(Λ₀)), with convergence error O(ℓ^{-1/2}).

**中文。** 定义：仿射李代数水平 ℓ 的可积模 L(Λ) 限制到有限子代数 g 分解的重数谱指各有限维不可约 L(μ) 的重数组成的向量。猜想：归一化重数谱在 ℓ→∞ 时逐分量收敛到 χ(V(Λ₀)) 的特征标系数,且收敛误差为 O(ℓ^{-1/2})。
