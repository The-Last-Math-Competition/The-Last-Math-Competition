# 00000001931

**LG-01 ◆** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: An arithmetic lattice is a lattice constructed from the integer ring of a number field. Conjecture: The complete criterion for uniform lattices of SO(3,1) to be arithmetic is quasi-isometric invariance (i.e. Gromov's conjecture: all uniform lattices are arithmetic); the criterion is controlled explicitly by the infimum of the volume spectrum.

**中文。** 定义：算术格指由数域整数环构造的格。猜想：SO(3,1) 中的一致格为算术的完全判据为拟等距不变(即 Gromov 猜想:一切一致格为算术);判据由体积谱的下确界显式控制。
