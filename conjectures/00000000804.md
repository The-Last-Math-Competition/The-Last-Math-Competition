**English.** Definition: For the clamped plate problem ω₁ = inf ∫(Δu)²/∫u², Rayleigh's conjecture asserts the ball is minimal at equal volume. Conjecture: The ball is a strict local minimizer: every domain with Fraenkel asymmetry δ satisfies ω₁(Ω) − ω₁(B) ≥ c·δ² with explicit c > 0; beyond the Rayleigh conjecture itself, local stability holds.

**中文。** 定义：夹紧板问题 ω₁=inf∫(Δu)²/∫u² 的 Rayleigh 猜想断言等体积下球最小。猜想：球是该极小化问题的严格局部极小:任何 Fraenkel 偏差为 δ 的域满足 ω₁(Ω)−ω₁(B) ≥ c·δ²,c>0 显式;Rayleigh 猜想本身之外,局部稳定性成立。
