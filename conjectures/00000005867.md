# 00000005867

**FE2-07 ★** — source: 181_有限元II.md

**English.** Definition: The inf-sup constant is the stability measure of the mixed pair. Conjecture: The optimal conversion formula is explicit in the angle of the two spaces, given at the minimal stable pair as a supremum; the cosine of the angle is the constant, and the constant is the norm of the discrete Fortin operator; and the supremum is attained at isoparametric elements, of order the square of the mesh size. (optimal space-angle conversion of the inf-sup constant)

**中文。** 定义：下确界常数：混合问题（ Stokes 对）的稳定度量（ inf-sup 常数）。猜想：最优换算公式为两空间角度的显式且最小稳定对处给出上确界；角度的余弦为常数且常数为离散 Fortin 算子的范数；上确界在等参元处达到且达到阶为网格尺寸的平方。（下确界空间角度最优换算）
