# 00000005961

**English.** Definition: Banded matrices are bandwidth-restricted random matrices. Conjecture: The bandwidth power-law phase transition of eigenvectors turns from localized to extended near the transition bandwidth, and the kernel of the transition is the spectral-gap closure. (localized-extended transition of banded spectral-gap closure)

**中文。** 定义：带状矩阵指带宽受限的随机矩阵。猜想：特征向量的带宽幂律相变在过渡带宽附近由局域转延展；且相变的核为谱隙闭合。（带状谱隙闭合局域延展相变）
