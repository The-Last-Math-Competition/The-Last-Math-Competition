# 00000004240

**English.** Definition: Perfect-matching equivalence is the isomorphism of the matching structures of two triangulations. Conjecture: There exist two triangulations that are matching equivalent but not higher-tree equivalent, and the smallest separation is a pair of two-dimensional prism triangulations. (minimal matching-tree separation example)

**中文。** 定义：完美匹配化等价指两剖分的匹配结构同构。猜想：存在两剖分的匹配化等价而高维树不等价,最小分离为二维棱柱剖分对。（匹配-高维树分离最小例）
