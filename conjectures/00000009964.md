# 00000009964

**MRG-24 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Non-Archimedean random walks on tori: stationary measures of p-adic expansion maps. Conjecture: The dimension of stationary measures of random walks on p-adic tori is an explicit quotient of drift and Lyapunov exponents, and the self-similarity of the measure is controlled by the Mahler measure of the expansion kernel; the exceptions of the dimension formula occur only at resonant spectra. (non-Archimedean stationary dimension formula)

**中文。** 定义：torus 上的非阿基米德随机行走：p-adic 展开映射的平稳测度。猜想：p-adic 环面上随机行走的平稳测度的维数为漂移与 Lyapunov 指数的显式商且测度的自相似性由展开核的 Mahler 测度控制；维数公式的例外仅发生在共振谱。（非阿基米德平稳维数公式）
