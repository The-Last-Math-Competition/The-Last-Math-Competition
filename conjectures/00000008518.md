**SDG-18 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: The structure of noncommutative ideals of the Weyl algebra. Conjecture: The minimal number of generators of a left ideal of the Weyl algebra is at most binom(n+d,n); equality is attained by generic ideals. (Weyl ideal generator bound)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl 代数的非交换理想的结构。猜想：Weyl 代数的左理想的极小生成元数 ≤ binom(n+d,n)；等号达成为 generic 理想。（Weyl 理想生成元界）
