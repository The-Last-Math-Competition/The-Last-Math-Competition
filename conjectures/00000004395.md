# 00000004395

**English.** Definition: The subgraph density polytope is the feasible region with edge density and four-point density as variables. Conjecture: The vertices of this polytope are exactly three types, the complete graph, the complete bipartite graph, and one explicit intermediate type, with no vertices beyond the three types. (three-type classification of four-point density vertices)

**中文。** 定义：子图密度多面体指以边密度与四点密度为变量的可行域。猜想：该多面体的顶点恰为完全图、完全二部图与一个显式中间型三类,三型之外无任何顶点。（四点密度顶点三分类）
