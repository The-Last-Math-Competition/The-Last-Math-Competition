# 00000004970

**English.** Definition: Waring rank and cactus rank are two decomposition measures. Conjecture: There exist two polynomials with the same Waring rank but different cactus ranks, and the minimal degree of the separation is four, realized by complete intersection ideal pairs. (same Waring-different cactus separation at degree four)

**中文。** 定义：Waring 秩与 cactus 秩指两个分解度量。猜想：存在两多项式的 Waring 秩相同而 cactus 秩不同,分离的最小次数为四且由完全交理想对实现。（Waring 同-cactus 异分离次数四）
