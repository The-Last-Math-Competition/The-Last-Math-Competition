# 00000006162

**English.** Definition: The transfer theorem is the transfer framework from singular terms to coefficients. Conjecture: For trigonometric cases the angle-window width is optimal, the combination of the window being the d-delta condition; the optimal window is a constant fraction of pi/2, the fraction decided by the singular shape; and the error outside the window is exponentially small, the exponent being the distance from the window to the singularity. (optimal angle-window structure of the transfer theorem)

**中文。** 定义：转移定理：奇异项到系数的传递框架（singularity analysis 的 transfer）。猜想：三角函数情形的角度窗宽最优且窗的组合为 d-δ 条件；最优窗为 π/2 的常分数且分数由奇异性状决定；窗外的误差为指数小且小的指数为窗到奇点的距离。（转移定理角度窗最优结构）
