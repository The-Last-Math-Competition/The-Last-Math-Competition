# 00000001497

**HY-17 ★** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Linear response of SRB measures is the differentiable dependence of statistics on parameters. Conjecture: For piecewise expanding families outside uniform hyperbolicity, the linear-response coefficient is a convergent integral of correlation functions along critical orbits; and the convergence rate of the integral equals the diffusive mixing rate. (piecewise-family response integral convergence law)

**中文。** 定义：SRB 测度的线性响应指系统参数扰动下统计量的可微依赖。猜想：一致双曲族之外的分段扩张族的线性响应系数为关联函数沿临界轨线的收敛积分；且积分的收敛速率等于扩散混合率。（分段族响应积分收敛律）
