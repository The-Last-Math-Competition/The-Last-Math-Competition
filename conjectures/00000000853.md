**English.** Definition: Jacobi fields of minimal surfaces. Conjecture: The dimension of L²-Jacobi fields of a complete embedded minimal surface is finite and bounded by a constant times the genus (L² rigidity).

**中文。** 定义：极小曲面的 Jacobi 场。猜想：完备嵌入极小曲面的 L²-Jacobi 场维数有限且 ≤ 常数·亏格(L² 刚性)。
