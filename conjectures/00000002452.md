**English.** Definition: A κ-Suslin tree is a κ-tree with no κ-antichain. Conjecture: κ-Suslin existence is equivalent to the weakening of □_κ (the negation of □(κ,ω)); an explicit statement of the equivalence (a weak tree-square equivalence).

**中文。** 定义：κ-Suslin 树指无 κ-反链的 κ-树。猜想：κ-Suslin 存在 ⟺ □_κ 的弱化(□(κ,ω) 的否定);等价的显式陈述(树-方块的弱等价)。
