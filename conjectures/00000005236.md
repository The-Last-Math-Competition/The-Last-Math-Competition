**MS3-06 ★** — source: 160_代数几何V_模空间.md

**English.** Definition: A complete family is a family of curves covering the moduli space. Conjecture: The complete family of genus three and degree two is constructed from an explicit two-parameter family of double covers, the moduli image is a hypersurface, and completeness is verified by counting. (complete covering family of genus three degree two)

**中文。** 定义：完备族指覆盖模空间的曲线族。猜想：亏格三度二的完备族由二次覆盖的双参数显式族构造,模像为超曲面且完备性经计数验证。（亏格三度二完备覆盖族）
