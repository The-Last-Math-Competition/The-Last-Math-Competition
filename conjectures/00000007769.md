# 00000007769

**ADY-19 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Escape-rate renormalization of the random quadratic family: with parameter c drawn from a density near the Mandelbrot set, the escape time exponent tau(c) is the decay exponent of the orbit escaping a radius. Conjecture: The distribution of tau(c) converges to a Frechet-type extreme value law with shape parameter xi = 1/2; and the expectation of the conformally invariant weighted Brownian motion on the Mandelbrot boundary agrees with the logarithm of the renormalization factor 2, giving a universality proof path for the lambda-constant. (Frechet escape law and lambda universality)

**中文。** 定义：随机二次族的逃逸重整化：取参数 c 依概率密度 ρ 于 Mandelbrot 集的邻域，逃逸时间指数 τ(c) 为轨道逃逸半径的衰减指数。猜想：τ(c) 的分布收敛到 Frechet 型极值律，其形状参数 ξ=1/2；且在 Mandelbrot 边界上加权布朗运动共形不变量的期望与重整化因子 2 的对数一致，给出 λ-常数的普适性证明路径。
