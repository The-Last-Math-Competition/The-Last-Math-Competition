# 00000009897

**GBR-17 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: n-representation infinite algebras: the higher hereditary class of algebras. Conjecture: n-representation infinite algebras are derived equivalent to coherent sheaf categories of n-Fano weighted projective spaces; the correspondence is one-to-one with a finite classification table, the exceptional members being n-repetitions of specific star-shaped quivers. (n-representation infinite Fano correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：n-表示无穷代数：高维遗传性代数类。猜想：n-表示无穷代数与 n-Fano 加权射影空间的凝聚层范畴导等价；对应为一一且分类表有限，例外成员为特定星型箭图的 n-重复。（n-表示无穷-Fano 对应）
