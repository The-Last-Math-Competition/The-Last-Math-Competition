# 00000005785

**IG2-15 ★** — source: 178_统计推断的几何_信息几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Pythagorean extension is the Pythagorean relation in nonflat information geometry. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit second-order curvature correction, the correction at second order being the three-tensor contraction; the sign of the correction is always positive; and in the flat (exponential-family) case the correction vanishes, if and only if the curvature is zero. (curvature second-order correction conversion of Pythagoras)

**中文。** 定义：勾股推广：非平坦几何（信息几何）中的勾股关系。猜想：换算常数为联络曲率的二阶修正显式且修正在二阶为三张量收缩；修正项的符号恒为正；平坦（指数族）情形修正消没且消没当且仅当曲率为零。（勾股曲率二阶修正换算）
