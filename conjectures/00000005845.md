# 00000005845

**English.** Definition: Module-category equivalence is the derived or stable equivalence of block algebras. Conjecture: At rank two it is controlled by the local-system classification, the kernel of the control being fusion isomorphism; the equivalence preserves the Cartan trace and the defect of blocks; and the difference of stable equivalences is given by simple-module translation, with finitely many translations. (fusion-isomorphism control of module-category equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：模范畴等价：块代数的导出等价与稳定等价。猜想：秩二时由局部系分类控制且控制的核为融合同构；等价保持 Cartan 迹与块的亏数；稳定等价的相差由单模平移给出且平移个数有限。（模范畴等价融合同构控制）
