# 00000008593

**MLC-03 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Oracle inequality denotes estimate risk by optimal model risk controls. Conjecture: Oracle constant of wavelet threshold estimate is always two o and remainder term is logn n; and threshold policy not can simultaneously constant and remainder term. (Oracle constant two optimal)

**中文。** 定义：Oracle 不等式指估计子风险被最优子模型风险控制。猜想：小波阈值估计的 Oracle 常数恒为二加小 o 且余项为 logn 除 n；且任何阈值策略不能同时改进常数与余项。（Oracle 常数二最优）
