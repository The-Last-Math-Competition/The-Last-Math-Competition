**English.** Definition: Conservativity is the equivalence of Voevodsky motives with Chow motives. Conjecture: Conservativity holds for all smooth projective varieties over Q; the number-field case reduces to Q (since the extension/restriction composite is the identity, conservativity transfers).

**中文。** 定义：保守性指 Voevodsky 母题与 Chow 母题的等价。猜想：保守性对 Q 上一切光滑射影簇成立;数域情形归约到 Q(由扩张/限制的复合恰为恒等的 conservativity 传递)。
