# 00000009978

**NCR-08 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Intersections of powers of the Jacobson radical: descending sequences of fully bounded Noetherian rings. Conjecture: The descent rate of the intersection of powers of the Jacobson radical of FBN rings is an explicit exponential decay in the dimension of the quotient algebra; the exact constant of the decay is given by the Loewy layer dimensions of the radical, and the intersection vanishes in finitely many steps. (Jacobson power intersection descent rate)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobson 根的幂的交：完全有界 Noetherian 环的下降序列。猜想：FBN 环的 Jacobson 根幂的交下降的速率为商代数维数的显式指数衰减；衰减的精确常数由根的 Loewy 层维数给出且交在有限步为零。（Jacobson 幂交下降速率）
