# 00000008472

**English.** Definition: The joint spectral radius rho(A) of a family of matrices A is the supremum of the geometric means of norms of length-n products. Conjecture: For generic compact matrix families, rho is exactly attained by finite products (a generic attainability law); the minimal dimension of nonattainable families (requiring infinitely long extremal orbits) is 2, realized by hyperbolic plane rotation families (a hyperbolic plane example); and in the attainable case the length of extremal words is bounded by a polynomial-exponential form in (md). (generic finite attainment of joint spectral radius)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵族 A 的联合谱半径 ρ(A) 定义为一切长度 n 乘积范数几何平均的上确界。猜想：通有的紧矩阵族的 ρ 由有限乘积精确达到（通有可达律）；且不可达族（需要无穷长极值轨道）的最小维数为 2，由双曲平面旋转族实现（双曲平面例）；可达情形极值字的长度上界为 (md) 的多项式指数形式。
