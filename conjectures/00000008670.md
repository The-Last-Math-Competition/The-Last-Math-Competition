# 00000008670

**English.** Definition: Nielsen equivalence classes: equivalence classes of generating tuples. Conjecture: The count m(G) of Nielsen classes is unbounded (an unboundedness law); the realizing families are given by PSL(2,p) (a PSL family law); the uniqueness of the T-system (transitivity system) holds with a finite list of exception groups (a finite list law); and the smallest exception on the list is PSL(2,7) (a seven-element exception law). (Nielsen unbounded PSL T-system exceptions)

**中文。** 定义：Nielsen 等价类：生成元组的等价类。猜想： Nielsen 类的计数 m(G) 的无界性（无界律）；且无界的实现族的例为 PSL(2,p) 的（PSL 族律）；T-system（传递系统）的单一性（单一性律）的例外群的列表有限（有限列表律）；列表的最小例外为 PSL(2,7) 的（七元例外律）。
