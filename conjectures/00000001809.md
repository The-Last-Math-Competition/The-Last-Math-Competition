# 00000001809

**English.** Definition: The CR dimension is the dimension of the complex tangent space of a Cauchy–Riemann manifold. Conjecture: The CR embedding of the unit cotangent bundle of a compact complex n-manifold has codimension at least ⌈n/2⌉, controlled by a quantitative form of Rossi's embedding rigidity theorem.

**中文。** 定义：CR 维数指 Cauchy–Riemann 流形上复切空间的维数。猜想：紧复 n-流形的单位余切丛的 CR 嵌入的余维 ≥⌈n/2⌉;由 Rossi 的嵌入刚性定理的定量形式控制。
