# 00000003147

**English.** Definition: The Kirillov model of representations: the explicit space of infinite-dimensional representations. Conjecture: The uniqueness of the Kirillov model: the model space of a GL₂ representation is the Schwartz functions, and the covariance of the Fourier transform is the Gamma factor of the basis vector. (Kirillov model Gamma factor)

**中文。** 定义：表示的 Kirillov 模型：无限维表示的显式空间。猜想：Kirillov 模型的唯一性：GL₂ 表示的模型空间为 Schwartz 函数且 Fourier 变换的共变为基础向量的 Gamma 因子。（Kirillov 模型 Gamma 因子）
