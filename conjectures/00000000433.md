# 00000000433

**English.** Definition: Macdonald's denominator identity expresses the denominator of an affine Lie algebra as a linear combination of monomials; let T(type) denote its number of terms. Conjecture: T(B_n) = T(C_n) = n, T(D_n) = 2n, T(G₂) = 2, T(F₄) = 4, T(E₆) = 6, T(E₇) = 7, T(E₈) = 8; and the number of terms always equals the multiplicity of exponent 1 in the root system.

**中文。** 定义：Macdonald 分母恒等式把仿射 Lie 代数的分母写为单项式线性组合,其项数记 T(型)。猜想：T(B_n)=T(C_n)=n,T(D_n)=2n,T(G₂)=2,T(F₄)=4,T(E₆)=6,T(E₇)=7,T(E₈)=8;且项数恒等于根系指数 1 的重数。
