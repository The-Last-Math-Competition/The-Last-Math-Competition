# 00000000328

**English.** Definition: The uniform partial quotient λ_n(α) is the reciprocal of the approximation constant determined by the first n partial quotients. Conjecture: λ_n(e) exceeds one half of n^{log 2} along an infinite subsequence; and the density of the exceeding subsequence is an explicit constant. (growth of uniform partial quotients of e)

**中文。** 定义：一致部分商 λ_n(α) 为前 n 个部分商构成的逼近常数之逆。猜想：λ_n(e) 沿无穷子列超过二分之一倍的 n 的 log2 次幂；且超出的子列密度为显式常数。（e 的一致部分商增长）
