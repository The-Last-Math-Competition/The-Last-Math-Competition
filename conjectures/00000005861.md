# 00000005861

**English.** Definition: An isoparametric element is the image of the reference element under a polynomial map. Conjecture: The Jacobian conversion constant of two-dimensional maps is the explicit bilinear expression of control points, degenerating to one in the affine case, and the bound is controlled by the worst interior angle. (Jacobian bilinear angle-bound conversion of isoparametric elements)

**中文。** 定义：等参元指参考元经多项式映射的单元。猜想：二维映射的雅可比换算常数为控制点的显式双线性式,仿射时退化为一且界由最差内角控制。（等参雅可比双线性角界换算）
