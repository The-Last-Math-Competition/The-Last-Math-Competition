# 00000004455

**SY3-05 ★** — source: 134_辛几何III_镜像对称深化.md

**English.** Definition: The partially wrapped Fukaya category is the wrapped category with stops. Conjecture: The category equivalence classes given by orderings of rationally weighted stops are completely classified by the quotient of the weight permutations under the dihedral group, and the quotient set is finite. (dihedral quotient classification of rational weights)

**中文。** 定义：部分包裹 Fukaya 范畴指带 stop 的包裹范畴。猜想：有理权重 stop 的排序给出的范畴等价类由权重排列在二面体群下的商完全分类,商集合有限。（有理权重二面体商分类）
