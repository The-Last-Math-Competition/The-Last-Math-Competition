**HG4-05 ★** — source: 164_几何群论IV_双曲几何III.md

**English.** Definition: A minimal action has no nontrivial invariant closed sets. Conjecture: The kernel of the boundary action of a hyperbolic group satisfies the alternative that the kernel is finite or is an explicit normal subgroup; the criterion is complete and verifiable by orbit closures. (alternative for kernels of boundary-minimal actions)

**中文。** 定义：极小作用指无非平凡不变闭集的作用。猜想：双曲群在边界的极小作用核满足二择一:核有限或核为显式正规子群,判据完全且可由轨道闭包验证。（边界极小作用核二择一）
