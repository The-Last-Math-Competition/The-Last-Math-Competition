# 00000008785

**English.** Definition: Random-start Lanczos: iterates approximating extreme eigenpairs of a symmetric matrix A, started from a random unit vector in the Krylov subspace. Conjecture: After k steps, the relative error of Ritz values against extreme eigenvalues is at most C sqrt kappa exp(-2k/sqrt kappa) with high probability; the tail probability decays as a power of the spectral gap for almost every start, with single-step convergence rate exactly 1 - 2/sqrt kappa; restart intervals of 3 sqrt kappa steps are asymptotically optimal, and the ratio of total work of adaptive to fixed restarts converges to 1. (Lanczos random start restart optimal work)

**中文。** 定义：随机起点 Lanczos：以随机单位向量启动 Krylov 子空间，逼近对称矩阵 A 极端特征对的迭代。猜想：k 步后 Ritz 值对极端特征值的相对误差以高概率不超过 C√κ·exp(-2k/√κ)；尾概率对几乎处处起点衰减为谱间隙的幂型且单步收敛率恰为 1-2/√κ；重启间隔取 3√κ 步为渐近最优，自适应重启与固定重启的总工作量之比收敛于 1。
