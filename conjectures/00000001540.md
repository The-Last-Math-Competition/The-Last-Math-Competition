# 00000001540

**KK-30 ★** — source: 36_代数K理论与非交换几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Baum–Connes assembly map with coefficients is the K-theoretic assembly for arbitrary coefficient C*-categories. Conjecture: BC with coefficients holds for all Gromov-hyperbolic groups (the general-group situation with known Gromov-ghost counterexamples does not apply); the proof is given explicitly by controlled homotopy compression on the Rips complex.

**中文。** 定义：带系数的 Baum–Connes 装配映射指任意系数 C*-范畴下的 K-理论装配。猜想：带系数 BC 对一切 Gromov-双曲群成立(已知存在 Gromov 幽灵反例的一般群情形不适用);其证明由 Rips 复形上的受控同伦挤压显式给出。
