# 00000008598

**English.** Definition: The white noise model is observation of Gaussian noise coefficients. Conjecture: The minimax equivalence of density, regression, and white noise models holds strictly under matching smoothness with conversion constant 1. (model equivalence constant one)

**中文。** 定义：白噪声模型指 Gauss 噪声系数观测。猜想：密度、回归与白噪声模型的 minimax 等价在光滑度匹配下严格成立且换算常数为 1。（模型等价常数一）
