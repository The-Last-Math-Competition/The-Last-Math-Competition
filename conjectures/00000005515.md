# 00000005515

**English.** Definition: The mean field of the shortest strategy is its deterministic limit. Conjecture: The shortest-fraction flow is always an explicit differential equation, with closed-form equilibrium, and the two-station solution is an elementary function. (elementary closed form of shortest-flow equilibria)

**中文。** 定义：最短策略的平均场指其确定极限。猜想：最短分数流恒为显式微分方程；平衡点闭式且两站解为初等函数。（最短流初等平衡闭式）
