**English.** Definition: The degeneration formula for relative GW invariants: the fusion product of relative invariants across the degeneration of X along a smooth curve W into components with divisors. Conjecture: On the wrapping-number-two part of the winding class sigma the fusion bilinear form is always symplectic-translation type: the matrix of fusion(sigma=2) is a self-dual basis change of the Fibonacci ring F_2 (an integral basis of Q(sqrt 5)); and for wrapping sigma at least 3 the matrix traces form a Somos-4 type integer sequence whose growth exponent is the largest real eigenvalue. (Fibonacci fusion and Somos traces)

**中文。** 定义：相对 GW 不变量的退化公式分解：光滑曲线 W⊂X 的退化 X→X_W∪D_1∪D_2 的相对不变量的融合积。猜想：融合的双线性型在缠绕类 σ 的缠绕数为 2 的部分恒为辛平移型：fusion(σ=2) 的矩阵为 Fibonacci 环 F_2 的自对偶基变换（Q(√5) 的整基）；更高缠绕 σ≥3 时矩阵的迹构成 Somos-4 型整序列，序列的增长指数为最大的实特征值。
