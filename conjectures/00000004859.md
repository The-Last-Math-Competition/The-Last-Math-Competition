**HG3-19 ◆** — source: 147_几何群论III_双曲群.md

**English.** Definition: The stable commutator length scl is the limit functional of the word length of commutator classes. Conjecture: The scl spectrum of a hyperbolic group is a discrete rational half-lattice with lattice constant given by the spectral gap of a chain complex; the lattice constant is a quasi-isometry invariant; the spectrum of a free group is {0} together with the rational points from 1/2 to infinity, each rational value realized by an explicit word. (rational half-lattice of stable commutator lengths)

**中文。** 定义：稳定交换子长度 scl：交换子类词长的极限泛函。猜想：双曲群的 scl 谱构成有理数的离散半格且格常数由链级数的谱间隙给出；格常数为拟等距不变量；自由群的谱为 {0} 与从 1/2 到无穷的有理点之并且每个有理值由显式字实现。（稳定交换子长度有理半格）
