**English.** Definition: The potential function is the generating functional of the dual coordinates. Conjecture: The Legendre conversion is the explicit difference of convex duality, the difference being the symmetrization of the canonical divergence, and the conversion is unique. (symmetrized difference conversion of potential functions)
