# 00000002144

**English.** Definition: The maximal eigenvalue multiplicity of a tree. Conjecture: The extremum is ⌈(n+1)/3⌉, realized by gluing a path with stars; and the kernel of the realization is the matching number. (tree maximal multiplicity matching-number path-star realization)

**中文。** 定义：树的特征值重数最大值。猜想：极值为 n 加一除三的上取整且由路径粘星形实现；且实现的核为匹配数。（树最大重数匹配数路径粘星实现）
