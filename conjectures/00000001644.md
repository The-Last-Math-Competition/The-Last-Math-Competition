# 00000001644

**HA-14 ◆** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: Atiyah's conjecture is the integrality of l²-Betti numbers (for groups without 2-torsion). Conjecture: Atiyah's conjecture holds for all torsion-free Gromov-hyperbolic groups; the minimal counterexample group (if any) needs at least 3 generators and admits no quotient containing a free subgroup of rank < 2.

**中文。** 定义：Atiyah 猜想指 l²-Betti 数整性(群无 2-挠时)。猜想：Atiyah 猜想对一切无挠 Gromov-双曲群成立;其失败的最小反例群(若存在)的生成元数 ≥3 且不含秩 <2 的自由子群的商。
