# 00000002212

**English.** Definition: The catenary degree c(H) is the diameter of the factorization graph. Conjecture: The catenary degree of the three-generated numerical semigroup ⟨a, b, c⟩ is an explicit function of min(a, b, c) (three-generated catenary).

**中文。** 定义：catenary 度 c(H)(因子化图的直径)。猜想：三生成数值半群 ⟨a, b, c⟩ 的 catenary 度为 min(a, b, c) 的显式函数(三生成 catenary)。
