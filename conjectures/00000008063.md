**English.** Definition: The reverse-mathematical threshold of Ramsey theory: the localization spectrum of Ramsey's theorem RT(k,n) in reverse mathematics (the refinement between RCA_0 and ACA_0). Conjecture: The separation of RT(2,3) from RT(2,2) is genuine (strict inclusion); the localization spectrum of RT(k,n) in n is given entirely by the direct-product closure of diagonal structures (a multiplicative closure law); and the proof-theoretic cost of separating the stabilization SRT^2_2 from Coh(COH) is the middle layer of the omega^omega hierarchy (the ordinal cost of separation), with the measure of the difference between COH+SRT and RT being zero (a model-theoretic zero-difference measure theorem). (RT stabilization Coh ordinal cost)
