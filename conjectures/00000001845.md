# 00000001845

**RM-05 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: Truncated unitary matrices are obtained from uniform Haar matrices by deleting a fixed set of k columns. Conjecture: The limit of the one-point function at the spectral edge is an explicit rational function (the truncated spectral edge); and the pole spectrum of the rational function is the Jacobi weight of the deleted columns.

**中文。** 定义：truncated unitary(均匀 Haar 后固定 k 列删除)。猜想：谱边缘的单点函数的极限为显式有理函数(trunc 谱边缘)；且有理函数的极点谱为删失列的 Jacobi 权重。
