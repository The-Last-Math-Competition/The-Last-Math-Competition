# 00000008011

**English.** Definition: The universality of the asymptotic constant of the tail of self-similar Barenblatt solutions of the porous medium equation (PME): the constant c_p of the truncation radius R(t) of the tail of the Barenblatt solution B_p(x,t). Conjecture: c_p is explicit of type (2/(p-1+n beta))^{1/2} (beta the self-similarity exponent); the free boundary of finite propagation of PME is C^infinity (interface smoothness rigidity) when the initial data is sufficiently smooth; and in the fast-diffusion limit p to 1 the truncation radius diverges as exp(t) (an exponential transition), the critical window of the transition having width |p-1| = O(1/t) (the noncommuting window of limit interchange). (Barenblatt tail universality and FDE window)

**中文。** 定义：多孔介质方程（PME）的自相似 Barenblatt 解的尾部的渐近常数的普适性：Barenblatt 解 B_p(x,t) 的尾的截断半径 R(t) 的常数 c_p。猜想：c_p = (2/(p-1+n·β))^{1/2} 型显式（β 为自相似指数）；且 PME 的有限传播的界面（free boundary）的正则性为 C^{∞}（界面光滑刚性）当初始数据的光滑性充分；界面的 FDE（fast diffusion）极限 p→1 的截断半径发散为 exp(t)（指数转变），转变的临界窗口宽度为 |p-1|=O(1/t)（极限交换的非交换窗口）。
