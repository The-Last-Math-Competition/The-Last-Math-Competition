# 00000002543

**English.** Definition: Uniform L² boundedness of singular integrals on finite-type surfaces. Conjecture: The complete converse of L² boundedness of Calderón–Zygmund kernels on uniformly rectifiable surfaces: boundedness implies uniform rectifiability, and the equivalence constant is a linear function of the singularity order of the kernel. (CZ boundedness complete converse)

**中文。** 定义：奇异积分在有限类型曲面上的一致 L² 有界性。猜想：Calderón–Zygmund 核的一致可校正曲面 L² 有界的完全逆：有界性蕴含一致可校正且等价的量化常数为核的奇性阶的线性函数。（CZ 有界完全逆）
