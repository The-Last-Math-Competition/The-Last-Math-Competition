**English.** Definition: Harmonic weak Maass forms: companions of non-holomorphic modular forms. Conjecture: The shadow of mock: the holomorphic part of a weak Maass form is a mock modular form, with shadow the image of a differential operator, and the series of the image regularized-divergent. (Mock shadow regularized divergent series)
