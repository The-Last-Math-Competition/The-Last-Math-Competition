# 00000004218

**English.** Definition: The weighted tree count is the sum over trees of products of edge weights. Conjecture: The stable distributional limit of the weight exponent is log-normal, with variance an explicit functional of the weight spectrum growing superlinearly with size. (log-normal limit of weighted trees)

**中文。** 定义：加权树计数指边权乘积在树集上的求和。猜想：权指数的稳定分布极限为对数正态,方差由权谱的显式泛函给出且随规模超线性增长。（加权树对数正态极限）
