# 00000007511

**ET3-01 ◆** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The base-change theorem compares the etale cohomology of fibers with specialized fibers. Conjecture: The base-change map is an isomorphism when proper and smoothly relative, the exception being controlled by the torsion part of critical cohomology; and the dimension of the exception is given by the discriminant of the singular fiber. (smooth-torsion base-change exception)

**中文。** 定义：基变换定理比较纤维的平展上同调与特殊化纤维。猜想：基变换映射在 proper 且光滑相对时为同构且例外由临界上同调的扭转部分控制；且例外的维数由奇异纤维的判别式给出。（基变换光滑扭转例外）
