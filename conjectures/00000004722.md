# 00000004722

**English.** Definition: The Brauer group of a curve consists of the classes of the function field regular everywhere on the curve. Conjecture: The l-torsion dimension of the Brauer group of a genus-three curve over a number field has an explicit upper bound, given by the l-rank of the Jacobian and attained by an explicit family of quotients. (torsion dimension upper bound of curve Brauer groups)

**中文。** 定义：曲线的 Brauer 群指函数域中在曲线上处处正则的类。猜想：数域上亏格三曲线的 Brauer 群 ℓ 挠维数有显式上界,界由 Jacobian 的 ℓ 秩给出且由显式商族达到。（曲线 Brauer 挠维数上界）
