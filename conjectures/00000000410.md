# 00000000410

**SA-10 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The number PP(n) of plane partitions is given by MacMahon's product formula. Conjecture: The largest prime factor of PP(n) is smaller than n² for infinitely many n; and the smoothness of the partition numbers is controlled by the prime-exponent structure of the product. (plane partition smoothness)

**中文。** 定义：平面分拆数 PP(n) 由 MacMahon 乘积公式给出。猜想：PP(n) 的最大素因子小于 n² 对无穷多 n 成立；且分拆数的光滑性由乘积的素数指数结构控制。（平面分拆数光滑性）
