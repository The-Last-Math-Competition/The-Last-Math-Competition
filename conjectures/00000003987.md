# 00000003987

**English.** Definition: The proof length of a random 3-CNF is the size of its shortest resolution proof at density alpha. Conjecture: On both sides of the transition point alpha close to 4.26 the proof length is 2^{Theta(n)}; the exponential constant is continuous at the threshold, while the exponent slope undergoes a single kink at the threshold. (exponent kink of random 3SAT proof lengths)

**中文。** 定义：随机 3-CNF 的证明长度指密度 α 时最短消解证明的尺寸。猜想：相变点 α ≈ 4.26 两侧证明长度均为 2^{Θ(n)},且指数常数在阈值处连续,指数斜率在阈值处发生一次折点。（随机 3SAT 证明长度指数折点）
