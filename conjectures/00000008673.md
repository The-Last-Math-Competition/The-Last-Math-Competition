# 00000008673

**English.** Definition: The 2-cohomology of central extensions: computation of the Schur multiplier. Conjecture: The computational complexity of the Schur multiplier for general finite groups is polynomial time (a polynomial law); the 2-rank asymptotics of the multiplier of random groups is trivial (a triviality law); the exceptions with large multipliers are special linear groups (an SL example law); and the multiplier pairs with representation theory in the counting of projective representations (a dual counting law). (Schur multiplier polynomial random triviality)

**中文。** 定义：中心扩展的 2-上同调：Schur 乘子的计算。猜想：Schur 乘子的计算复杂度（一般有限群的）为多项式时间的（多项式律）；且乘子的 2-秩的渐近（随机群的乘子）为平凡（平凡律）；平凡的例外（大乘子）的例为特殊线性群的（SL 例律）；乘子与表示论（射影表示的计数）的对偶计数（对偶计数律）。
