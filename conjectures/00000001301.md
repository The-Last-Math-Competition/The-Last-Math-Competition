# 00000001301

**CC-01 ◆** — source: 29_多复变与复几何.md

**English.** Definition: The corona problem on the ball Bⁿ ⊂ Cⁿ asks whether the maximal ideal space of the ring H∞ of bounded holomorphic functions contains Cⁿ as a dense open set. Conjecture: The corona theorem holds for Bⁿ (n ≥ 2) (the case n = 1 is Carleson's theorem).

**中文。** 定义：球 Bⁿ ⊂ ℂⁿ 上的 corona 问题(有界全纯函数环 H∞ 的极大理想空间是否含 ℂⁿ 为稠密开集)。猜想：corona 定理对 Bⁿ(n ≥ 2)成立(n = 1 为 Carleson 定理)。
