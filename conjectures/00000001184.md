**LT-84 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The Dixon pair-generation probability is the probability that two random elements generate. Conjecture: The pair-generation probability for finite Lie-type groups is bounded below by one minus the sum of reciprocals of q and its square; and the bound is asymptotically tight for large q. (Dixon probability lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Dixon 偶对生成为两随机元素的生成概率。猜想：有限李型群的偶生成概率的下界为一减 q 与平方的倒数和；且界的紧性在 q 大时渐近。（Dixon 概率下界）
