# 00000002231

**NV-01 ★** — source: 60_亚纯函数与NevanlinnaII.md

**English.** Definition: The Nevanlinna deficiency δ(a, f) = 1 − limsup N(a,f)/T(f). Conjecture: There exists a transcendental meromorphic f whose deficiency spectrum {δ(a)} realizes exactly any prescribed closed subset of [0,1] containing 0 (deficiency spectrum realization).

**中文。** 定义：Nevanlinna 缺度 δ(a, f) = 1 − limsup N(a,f)/T(f)。猜想：存在超越亚纯 f 使其缺度谱 {δ(a)} 恰为任意预设闭子集 ⊆ [0, 1] 含 0 的显式实现(缺度谱实现)。
