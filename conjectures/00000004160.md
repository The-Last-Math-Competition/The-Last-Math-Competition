# 00000004160

**English.** Definition: The circle method main term is the product of the singular series and the major arc integral. Conjecture: There exists a family of Diophantine equations whose main term is always negative while the actual number of solutions is infinite, and the smallest degree where this occurs is 3. (negative-main-term infinite-solution separation)

**中文。** 定义：圆法主项指奇异级数与主弧积分的乘积。猜想：存在丢番图方程族其主项恒负而实际解数无穷,出现的最小次数为 3。（负主项无穷解分离）
