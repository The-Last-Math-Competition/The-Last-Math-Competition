**English.** Definition: Metastability with the deficit structure of metastable states. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit ratio of metastable deficits and the two-parameter deficit ratio is bounded; the bound spectrum closes with closed lower bound 1; and the limit of the deficit ratio is the equilibrium constant. (two-parameter deficit-ratio conversion of metastability)

**中文。** 定义：亚稳定性：亚稳态的亏格结构。猜想：换算常数为亚稳亏格的显式比且双参数亏格比有界；界谱封闭且封闭的下界为 1；亏格比的极限为平衡常数。（亚稳亏格双参数比换算常数）
