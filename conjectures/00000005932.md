# 00000005932

**English.** Definition: The space-time covariance is the time-correlation function of the process. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit negative two-thirds power of the time difference, with constant the square of the classical rate; the universality of the power is preserved on all 1:2:3 class models; and the two-model deviation of the constant is zero, the zero deviation being the hallmark of the universality class. (negative two-thirds power constant of the covariance)
