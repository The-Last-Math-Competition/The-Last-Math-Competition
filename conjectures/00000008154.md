# 00000008154

**English.** Definition: The low-temperature fixed point of the renormalization of the Edwards-Anderson spin glass: the density zero_RSB of zero modes of the RSB spectrum of operator spectra at the glassy fixed point. Conjecture: zero_RSB (the exponent of the spectral density of zero eigenmodes at zero) is explicit as theta_RSB (2-d)/2 (a zero-point exponent formula); the regularization dimension of the truncation of the infrared divergence of zero modes (regularization of finite-dimensional zero modes) is d_s = 4/3 (a rigidity of the glassy spectral dimension 4/3); and the universality of the spectral dimension (all glassy fixed points share d_s) is guaranteed by the self-similarity of the replica structure of the RSB hierarchy (a replica self-similarity law). (spin glass spectral dimension 4/3 replica self-similarity)

**中文。** 定义：Edwards–Anderson 自旋玻璃的重整化的低温不动点：玻璃不动点的算子谱 RSB 谱的零模的密度 zero_RSB。猜想：zero_RSB（零特征模的谱密度在零点的指数）为 θ_RSB·(2-d)/2 的显式（零点指数公式）；且零模的infra-red 发散的截断（有限维零模的正则化）的正则化维数为 d_s = 4/3（玻璃谱维数 4/3 刚性）；谱维数的普适性（一切玻璃不动点同 d_s）由 RSB 层级的复制结构的自相似（复制自相似律）保证。
