# 00000007670

**English.** Definition: A q-series f(z)=sum a_n z^n is of Mahler type if there exist an integer d>1 and a polynomial P with f(z^d)=P(z,f(z)). Conjecture: The intersection of Mahler-type series and q-hypergeometric-type series consists exactly of finite combinations of algebraic functions and theta quotients; in particular, a series of convergence radius 1 satisfying both a Mahler equation and a first-order q-difference equation must be a rational function of theta functions. (Mahler q-difference intersection)

**中文。** 定义：q-级数 f(z)=Σ a_n z^n 称为 Mahler 型的，若存在整数 d>1 与多项式 P 使 f(z^d)=P(z,f(z))。猜想：Mahler 型级数与 q-超几何型级数的交集恰由代数函数与 theta 商的有限组合构成；特别地，同时满足一个 Mahler 方程与一个一阶 q-差分方程的收敛半径为 1 的级数必为 theta 函数的有理函数。
