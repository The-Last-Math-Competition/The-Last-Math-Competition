# 00000002400

**FM-20 △** — source: 65_分形几何与测度.md

**English.** Conjecture: There exist two doubling measures μ, ν such that the energy dimension of μ×ν exceeds the sum of their individual energy dimensions (a superadditivity counterexample); the construction maximizes the correlation of the product structure.

**中文。** 猜想：存在两倍增测度 μ,ν 使 μ×ν 的能量维数 >各自能量维数之和(超可加性反例);构造由产品结构的相关性最大化。
