**English.** Definition: Stopping times and optional sampling: the stopping behavior of martingales. Conjecture: The convergence condition of optional sampling: uniformly integrable martingales converge under stopping, with the genus of convergence the tail thickness of the stopping time. (optional sampling stopping tail genus)
