# 00000005219

**English.** Definition: The finite-depth limit is the finite-depth closure structure of the standard invariants of subfactors. Conjecture: The conversion within depth three is always an explicit quotient of the translation lattice of the principal graph; in the affine type-A case the quotient is trivial and fully indexed by the translation lattice; and the index of the quotient is an exponential function of the depth with base 2. (translation-lattice quotient structure at depth three)

**中文。** 定义：有限深度的极限：子因子标准不变量的深度有限闭包结构。猜想：深度三以内的换算恒为主图平移格的显式商；仿射 A 型情形商为平凡且由平移格完全索引；商的指标为深度的指数函数且指数底为 2。（深度三平移格商结构）
