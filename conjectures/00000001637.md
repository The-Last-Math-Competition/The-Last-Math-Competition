# 00000001637

**HA-07 △** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: Spherical objects. Conjecture: The isomorphism classes of spherical objects on a K3 surface are completely covered by the monoid orbits of spherical twists (K3 spherical coverage).

**中文。** 定义：球面对象(spherical object)。猜想：K3 曲面上球面对象的同构类集合由球面自发反射的幺半群轨道完全覆盖(K3 球面覆盖)。
