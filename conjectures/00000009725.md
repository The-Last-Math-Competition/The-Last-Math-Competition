# 00000009725

**English.** Definition: Scattering theory for the Zakharov system: long-time behavior of the coupled wave-Schroedinger system. Conjecture: Solutions of the three-dimensional Zakharov system scatter below the energy threshold, and solitons exist only in one dimension; the threshold function is explicitly given by the uniqueness of the ground-state wave, and the dichotomy is complete. (Zakharov threshold dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：Zakharov 系统的散射理论：波与薛定谔耦合系统的长时间行为。猜想：三维 Zakharov 系统的解在能量阈值以下散射且孤子仅在一维存在；阈值函数由基态波的唯一性显式给出且二分律完全。（Zakharov 阈值二分）
