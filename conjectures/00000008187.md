# 00000008187

**English.** Definition: The computational precision of Fourier coefficients of modular forms: the complexity mf_comp(N) of computing coefficients a_n up to n <= N. Conjecture: The optimum of mf_comp (Deligne-Serre type acceleration) is O(N log^2 N) (a quasi-linear law); the extra cost of computing half-integer weight coefficients (Shimura correspondence) is N^{1/2} (a half-integral square-root cost); the precision threshold of symbolic computation of coefficients (linear class number formulas for imaginary quadratics) is D >= -4 times 10^6 (a million threshold law); and the radius of the mod p overconvergence of coefficients (Katz p-adic) is p-1 (a p-1 radius law). (modular form quasi-linear half-integral million threshold)

**中文。** 定义：模形式的 Fourier 系数的计算精度：系数 a_n 的计算到 n ≤ N 的复杂度 mf_comp(N)。猜想：mf_comp 的最优（Deligne–Serre 型加速）为 O(N log^{2}N)（准线性律）；且 Half-integer 权的系数（Shimura 对应）的计算的额外成本为 N^{1/2}（半整平方根成本）；系数的符号计算（虚二次的类数线性公式）的精度阈为 D ≥ -4·10^{6}（百万阈律）；系数 mod p 的超收敛（Katz 的 p-adic）模的半径为 p-1（p-1 半径律）。
