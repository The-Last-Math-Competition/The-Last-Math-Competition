**English.** Definition: Polynomial irreducibility is the absence of proper factorization over the coefficient field. Conjecture: The condition jointly given by the shift-spectrum criterion and the genus is a sufficient condition for irreducibility; and the completeness of the criterion closes for genus at most two. (shift-spectrum genus irreducibility criterion)

**中文。** 定义：多项式不可约性指在系数域上无真分解的性质。猜想：移位谱判据与亏格联立给出的条件为不可约的充分条件；判据的完全性在亏格不超过二时封闭。（移位谱亏格不可约判据）
