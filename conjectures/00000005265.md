**English.** Definition: The convergence criterion of series solutions is the condition for termwise summation. Conjecture: The convergence threshold is one third, with absolute convergence above and energy divergence below, exactly divergent at the critical point, and the threshold cannot be moved. (one-third threshold of series convergence)

**中文。** 定义：级数解的收敛判据指逐项求和的条件。猜想：收敛阈值为三分之一,上方绝对收敛下方能量发散,临界处恰好发散且阈值不可移动。（级数解收敛三分之阈值）
