**SA-69 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The flow polynomial and tension polynomial are dual graph polynomials. Conjecture: For pairs of planar dual graphs, the two have disjoint real-root sets; and the proof of root separation is by sign reversal on dual cycles. (flow-tension root separation)

**中文。** 定义：流多项式与张力多项式为对偶的图多项式。猜想：平面图对的两者的实根交为空；且根分离的证明由对偶圈的符号反转。（流-张力根分离）
