# 00000000071

**English.** Conjecture: Moments of the zeta function at prime ordinates: (1/π(x))·Σ_{p≤x} |ζ(1/2+ip)|^{2k} has the same main term (the Keating–Snaith constant) and the same second-order term as the continuous average in t.

**中文。** 猜想：ζ 函数在素数纵标处的矩:(1/π(x))·Σ_{p≤x} |ζ(1/2+ip)|^{2k} 与连续 t 平均具有相同的主项(Keating–Snaith 常数)与第二阶项。
