**English.** Definition: Random Chern insulators: topological insulators under disorder. Conjecture: The quenched Chern number is stable under disorder; the local Chern density is inhomogeneous, the quantization precision fluctuations of the random Thouless pump are of Poisson type; and the existence threshold of the three-dimensional generalization is an explicit value of the disorder strength. (quenched Chern stability pump fluctuations)

**中文。** 定义：随机陈绝缘体指无序下的拓扑绝缘体。猜想：淬火陈数在无序下恒稳定；局域陈密度非均匀且随机 Thouless 泵的量子化精度涨落为泊松型，三维推广的存在阈为无序强度的显式值。（淬火陈数稳定泵涨落）
