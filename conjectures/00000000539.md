**CA-39 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The h-polynomial h(t) is the encoding generating function of the Hilbert function of a graded ideal. Conjecture: The h-vector of a quadratic monomial ideal has no three adjacent strictly decreasing terms; this combinatorial positivity is induced by the shellability of the complex. (h-vector without triple descent)

**中文。** 定义：h-多项式 h(t) 为分次理想的希尔伯特函数的编码母函数。猜想：二次单项式理想的 h-向量为无相邻三项严格下降的序列；该组合正性由复形的 shellability 诱导给出。（h-向量无三降）
