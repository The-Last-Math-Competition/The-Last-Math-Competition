# 00000007721

**FRV-01 ★** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The Bercovici-Pata correspondence Pi maps a classically infinitely divisible law mu to its free analogue via the integral formula of Cauchy and R-transforms; the BP convergence rate is the decay of d(nu_n^boxed, Pi(mu^{*n})) in Kolmogorov distance. Conjecture: For compactly supported mu the rate is O(n^{-1}), and the coefficient of n^{-1} is given by the difference of classical and free cumulants at third order; that coefficient is nonzero if and only if the third classical cumulant of mu differs from its free cumulant. (third-cumulant BP rate)

**中文。** 定义：Bercovici–Pata 对应 Π 把经典无穷可分律 μ 映为其自由类比（经 Cauchy 变换与 R-变换的积分公式）。对 μ 的 n 重卷积和测度 ν_n，BP 收敛速率指 d(ν_n^⊞, Π(μ^{*n}))（d 为 Kolmogorov 距离）随 n 的衰减阶。猜想：对支撑紧的 μ，速率为 d = O(n^{-1})，且 n^{-1} 的系数由经典与自由累积量在第三阶的差给出；该系数非零当且仅当 μ 的三阶经典累积量与其自由累积量不等。
