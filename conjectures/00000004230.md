# 00000004230

**English.** Definition: The Hodge spectrum is the full spectrum of the combinatorial Laplacian. Conjecture: There exists a family of complexes whose Hodge spectrum, under continuous deformation, passes through spectra realizable by no complex, and the smallest crossing example is a two-dimensional Mobius band triangulation. (Hodge-non-Hodge spectrum crossing)

**中文。** 定义：Hodge 谱指组合拉普拉斯的全谱。猜想：存在复形族的 Hodge 谱经连续变形穿过不可由任何复形实现的谱,穿越的最小例为二维莫比乌斯带剖分。（Hodge-非 Hodge 谱穿越）
