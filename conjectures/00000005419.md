**English.** Definition: A snowflake domain is a region with self-similar slit boundary. Conjecture: The Fourier dimension of its measure is an explicit linear expression of the parameter, given by self-similar spectral analysis, and strictly smaller than the Hausdorff dimension. (linear formula of snowflake Fourier dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：雪花域指自相似裂边界的区域。猜想：其测度的傅里叶维数为参数的显式线性式,由自相似谱分析给出且严格小于豪斯多夫维数。（雪花傅里叶维数线性式）
