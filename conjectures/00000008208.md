# 00000008208

**ORB-08 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The Beilinson-Bernstein localization of annihilators of representations: the characteristic variety char(pi) of the annihilator of the localized D-module. Conjecture: char(pi) is a union of coadjoint orbit closures (an envelope law); the characteristic variety of discrete series representations is a single orbit closure (a single-variety law); the minimum of the dimension of characteristic varieties is the conversion of type (1/2) dim G minus 1 (a minimal dimension conversion formula); and the dimension spectrum (over all representations) is the integral lattice of (1/2) dim O (a half-integral lattice law). (D-module characteristic orbit closure half-lattice)

**中文。** 定义：表示的零化子的 Beilinson–Bernstein 局部化：D-模的局部化的零化子的特征簇 char(π)。猜想：char(π) 为余伴随闭包的并（包络律）；且零散表示（离散系列）的特征簇为单个轨道闭包（单簇律）；特征簇的维数的极小值 = 1/2·dim G·（-1）型换算（极小维数换算公式）；维数的谱（一切表示的特征簇维数）为半整数格 1/2·dim O 的整格（半整格律）。
