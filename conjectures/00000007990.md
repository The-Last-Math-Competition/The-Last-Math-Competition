# 00000007990

**CD3-30 ★** — source: 251_编码理论III_列表解码与局部译码.md

**English.** Definition: The perfect covering of the Lee metric for codewords: perfect Lee codes (a continuation of the Golab-Weldon problem). Conjecture: Perfect Lee codes PL(n,e) of radius e exist only at (n,e) = (2,2) and n congruent to plus or minus 1 mod 6 (e = 2, known to Golab-Weldon) plus sparse higher-radius exceptions; the step distribution of (n,e) for radius at least 3 is an arithmetic-progression intersection in the prime-mod-6 sense with density (1/2) product over p>3 of (1-2/p^2) (an explicit Euler product); and nonexistence is uniquely determined by a Diophantine approximation obstruction of lattice density (the Markov constant). (perfect Lee Euler product density)

**中文。** 定义：码字的 Lee 度量的完备覆盖：Lee 度量球的完美 Lee 码（Golab–Weldon 问题的延续）。猜想：完美 Lee 码 PL(n,e)（半径 e）仅在 (n,e)=(2,2) 及 n≡±1 (mod 6)（e=2，Golab–Weldon 已知）与更高半径的稀疏例外存在；半径 ≥3 的完美 Lee 码的 (n,e) 的分步分布为素数模 6 意义下的等差数列交集，交集的密度为 1/2·Π_{p>3}(1-2/p²)（显式 Euler 乘积）；非存在性由格点密度的丢番图逼近障碍（Markov 常数）唯一决定。
