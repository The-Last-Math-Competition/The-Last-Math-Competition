# 00000005816

**English.** Definition: Fast diffusion is the equation with exponent below one. Conjecture: The extinction-time formula of the fast exponent is the explicit fraction of the initial mass, given jointly by the dimension and the exponent, and exact. (mass-fraction extinction formula of the fast exponent)

**中文。** 定义：快速扩散指指数小于一的方程。猜想：半指数的灭绝时间公式为初始质量的显式分式,由维数与指数联合给出且精确。（半指数灭绝质量分式公式）
