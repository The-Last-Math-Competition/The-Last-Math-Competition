**HY-24 ★** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Measure equivalence between hyperbolic groups is the group-theoretic generalization of orbit equivalence, with spectrum given by cocycle constants. Conjecture: The admissible spectrum of measure equivalence between non-lattice hyperbolic groups is the explicit discrete set {1/2, 1, 2}; different volumes preclude measure equivalence; spectral values are completely determined by the overlap dimension of the groups.

**中文。** 定义：双曲群间的测量等价指轨道等价的群论推广,其谱为 cocycle 常数。猜想：非格子双曲群间的测量等价的容许谱为离散显式集 {1/2, 1, 2};体积不同时无测量等价;谱值由群的重叠维数完全决定。
