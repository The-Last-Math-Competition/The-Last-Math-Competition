**SDG-07 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: Gevrey sequence denotes analytic function class of growth (n!)^s part level. Conjecture: singularity spectrum of analytic continuation of solution of differential equation of Gevrey class is accumulation point set finite of algebra singularity set of s- density classification type; s = 1. (Gevrey singularity spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Gevrey 序列指按增长 (n!)^s 分级的解析函数类。猜想：Gevrey 类的微分方程的解的解析延拓的奇点谱为 s-密度分类型；s = 1 的代数奇点集的聚点集有限。（Gevrey 奇点谱）
