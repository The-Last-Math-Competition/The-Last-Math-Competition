# 00000008507

**SDG-07 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: A Gevrey sequence is a class of analytic functions graded by growth (n!)^s. Conjecture: The singularity spectrum of the analytic continuation of solutions of differential equations in a Gevrey class is of s-density dichotomy type; and for s = 1 the accumulation set of algebraic singularities is finite. (Gevrey singularity spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Gevrey 序列指按增长 (n!)^s 分级的解析函数类。猜想：Gevrey 类的微分方程的解的解析延拓的奇点谱为 s-密度分类型；s = 1 的代数奇点集的聚点集有限。（Gevrey 奇点谱）
