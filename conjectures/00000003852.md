**RM3-02 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The gap probability F_β(s) denotes the probability that the sine-β point process has no point in an interval of length s. Conjecture: Among the first three coefficients of the complete asymptotic series of log F_β(s), a universal ratio function G(β) exists satisfying the duality equation G(β)·G(2/β) = 1. (beta gap coefficient duality)

**中文。** 定义：gap 概率 F_β(s) 指正弦-β 点过程在长度 s 的区间内无点的概率。猜想：log F_β(s) 的完全渐近级数的首三个系数之间存在普适比函数 G(β),满足对偶方程 G(β)·G(2/β) = 1。（β-gap 系数对偶）
