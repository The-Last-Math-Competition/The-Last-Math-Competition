# 00000008029

**OMN-09 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The o-minimal additivity of the Euler characteristic of definable sets: the difference Delta(X) = chi_def(X) - chi_top(X) between the definable Euler characteristic and the topological Euler characteristic. Conjecture: Delta(X) is given by the count of asymptotic types of the unbounded ends: Delta = sum over ends of (-1)^{k_end}; and the classification list of end types is countable (four types, type 1 to type 4: linear, polynomial, exponential, and mixed); the sign rule for mixed ends is the sign of the logarithm of the polynomial-to-exponential weight ratio (a sign-weight duality). (definable Euler end counting duality)

**中文。** 定义：可定义集合的 Euler 特征的 o-极小可加性：χ_def(X)（可定义 Euler 特征）与拓扑 Euler 特征 χ_top(X) 的差 Δ(X)=χ_def-χ_top。猜想：Δ(X) 由无界端的渐近类型（ends）的计数给出：Δ=Σ_ends (-1)^{k_end}；且端类型的分类列表为可数（type 1 至 type 4 的四类：线性、多项式、指数、混合）；混合端的符号规则为多项式-指数权重比的对数的符号（符号-权重对偶）。
