# 00000002729

**English.** Definition: The local-global principle of Cohen–Macaulayness: heredity of links. Conjecture: The local criterion for CM-ness: a complex is CM if and only if all vertex links are CM plus connectivity; the iteration depth of the criterion is the dimension of the complex, and the depth minimization agrees with the shellification depth. (CM local iteration depth)

**中文。** 定义：Cohen–Macaulay 性的局部-整体原理：链接的遗传。猜想：CM 性的局部判别：复形为 CM 当且仅当一切顶点链接为 CM 加连通性；判别的迭代深度为复形的维数且深度的极小化与 shell 化的深度一致。（CM 局部迭代深度）
