# 00000002309

**FR-19 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: The minimal degree of a permutation group is the smallest number of points moved by a nonidentity element. Conjecture: The complete version of the minimal-degree table of rank-3 primitive groups: the remaining 7 exceptions are completed (the ratio of minimal degree to degree being an explicit function); the table is henceforth complete; and the ratios of the exceptional groups are all rational functions.

**中文。** 定义：置换群的极小度指非恒等元素移动点的最小数。猜想：秩 3 的本原群的极小度表的完全版:表中补全剩余的 7 个例外(极小度与度数的比为显式函数);表自此完备；且例外群的极小度与度数之比皆为有理函数。
