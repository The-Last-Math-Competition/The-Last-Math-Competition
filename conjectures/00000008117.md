# 00000008117

**English.** Definition: The classification index of regularity of noncommutative quadrics (graded algebras on 3 quadratic generators): the parameter space Q_reg of AS-Gorenstein nc quadrics. Conjecture: Q_reg is an algebraic variety with exactly 2 connected components (a two-component law: point and direct-sum components); the intersection of the components (the contraction subvariety) is Cohen-Macaulay of genus 1 (a CM genus-1 law for the intersection); and the count of rational points of the contraction subvariety is 1 (a single-point contraction), that point being a limit of 3-dimensional Sklyanin algebras (a Sklyanin limit rigidity). (nc quadric two-component CM genus one)

**中文。** 定义：非交换二次簇（3 个二次生成元的分次代数）的正则性的分类指数：nc 二次簇的 AS-Gorenstein 的参数空间 Q_reg。猜想：Q_reg 为代数簇且其连通分支恰 2（双分支律：点-直和分支）；且分支的交（收缩簇）为 Cohen–Macaulay 型亏格 1（交的 CM 亏格 1 律）；收缩簇的有理点的计数为 1（单点收缩），该点为 3 维 Sklyanin 的极限（Sklyanin 极限刚性）。
