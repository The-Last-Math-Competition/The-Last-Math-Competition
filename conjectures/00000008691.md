**English.** Definition: Unbalanced transport allows mass variation with a penalized transport. Conjecture: The two limits of the penalization parameter of UOT are classical OT and pure penalization; and the cone lifting of the intermediate Hellinger-Kantorovich distance is an explicit isometry of the square-root metric. (UOT two limits HK cone)
