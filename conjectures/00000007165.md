**EE4-15 ★** — source: 224_特征值极值II.md

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of the Rayleigh quotient with the extremal faces of the quotient. Conjecture: The contact manifolds of extremal faces hold, and the dimension of the manifolds is the multiplicity of the index. (index-multiplicity Rayleigh contact manifolds)

**中文。** 定义：Rayleigh 商的谱：商的极值面。猜想：商面的切触：极值面的切触流形且形的维数为指标的重数。（Rayleigh 商切触流形指标重数维数极值面）
