# 00000007614

**English.** Definition: The Lichnerowicz formula with the curvature-coefficient structure of the Dirac operator. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the curvature coefficient, the coefficient being one quarter of the scalar curvature; the coefficient is exact with test on constant-curvature spaces; and the remainder of the formula is the connection divergence. (curvature-coefficient conversion)

**中文。** 定义：Lichnerowicz 公式：Dirac 算子的曲率项系数结构。猜想：换算常数为曲率项系数且系数为标量曲率的四分之一；系数精确且精确的检验为常曲率空间；公式的余项为联络散度。（曲率系数换算常数结构）
