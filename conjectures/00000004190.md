# 00000004190

**English.** Definition: The volume spectrum and the Laplace spectrum are respectively the width sequence {W_p} and the eigenvalue sequence {lambda_p}. Conjecture: There exist two three-dimensional metrics with identical volume spectra but different Laplace spectra, and the volume spectrum determines no curvature integral of any fixed order. (volume spectrum-curvature separation)

**中文。** 定义：体积谱与 Laplace 谱分别指 {W_p} 与特征值列 {λ_p}。猜想：存在两个三维度量其体积谱完全相同而 Laplace 谱不同,且体积谱不决定任何固定阶曲率积分。（体积谱-曲率分离）
