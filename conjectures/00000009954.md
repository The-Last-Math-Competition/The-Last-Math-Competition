# 00000009954

**MRG-14 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Fourier decay of invariant measures: spectral rigidity of homogeneous measures. Conjecture: The rigidity threshold for Fourier decay of invariant measures: ergodic measures decaying faster than an explicit power law must be Haar; the threshold power is a linear function of the rank of the group, attained by Sobolev obstructions of the diagonal flow. (Fourier decay rigidity threshold)

**中文。** 定义：不变测度的 Fourier 衰减：齐性测度的谱刚性。猜想：不变测度的 Fourier 变换的衰减的刚性阈值：衰减快于显式幂律的遍历测度必为 Haar；阈值幂为群秩的线性函数且由对角流的 Sobolev 障碍达到。（Fourier 衰减刚性阈值）
