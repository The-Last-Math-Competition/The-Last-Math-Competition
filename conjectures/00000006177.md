# 00000006177

**English.** Definition: The matrix-tree theorem counts by Laplacian cofactors. Conjecture: The spanning-tree log density of large lattice graphs is given by a measure-type invariant, the realization of the invariant being the lattice determinant. (lattice-determinant spectral density of spanning trees)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵树定理为 Laplace 余子式计数。猜想：大格子图的生成树对数密度由测度型不变量给出；且不变量的实现为格行列式。（生成树谱密度格行列式）
