# 00000007543

**English.** Definition: Graph equivalence as the complete criterion of mixed-moment equivalence. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the complete criterion, the criterion being the merge of isomorphism plus cospectrality; the failure example of the merge is the cospectral pair; and the complexity of the criterion is linear in subgraph counting. (merged-criterion conversion constant)

**中文。** 定义：图等价：混合矩等价的完整判据。猜想：换算常数为等价的完整判据且判据为同构加同谱的合并；合并的失效例为余图对；判据的复杂度为子图计数的线性。（合并判据换算常数结构）
