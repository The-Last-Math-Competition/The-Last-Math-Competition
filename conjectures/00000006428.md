**English.** Definition: Entropy power with the lattice correction of the entropy-power inequality. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit asymptotics of the correction term and the fine-lattice limit correction is of second-order type; the coefficient is given by the Fisher information; and the sign of the correction is always positive, the positivity realized by the discrete Gauss. (Fisher coefficient conversion of fine-lattice corrections)

**中文。** 定义：熵幂：熵幂不等式的格修正。猜想：换算常数为修正项的显式渐近且细格极限的修正为二阶型；系数由 Fisher 信息给出；修正的符号恒为正且正值由离散 Gauss 实现。（细格修正费歇尔系数换算）
