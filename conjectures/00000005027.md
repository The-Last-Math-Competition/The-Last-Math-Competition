# 00000005027

**English.** Definition: The zero-one law states that the limiting probability of a sentence on random structures converges to 0 or 1. Conjecture: First-order logic preserves the zero-one law on dense random graphs; the failure model of second-order logic is given by an explicit random structure of weighted majority functions, with the failing sentence of even-cardinality type; and the limiting probability of the failing sentence family converges to 1/2 with power-type rate. (even-cardinality sentence of second-order zero-one law failure)

**中文。** 定义：零一律：语句在随机结构上的极限概率收敛到 0 或 1。猜想：一阶逻辑在稠密随机图上保持零一律；二阶逻辑的失效模型由计权多数函数的显式随机结构给出且失效句子为偶基数型；失效句子族的极限概率收敛于 1/2 且收敛速率为幂型。（二阶零一律失效偶基数句）
