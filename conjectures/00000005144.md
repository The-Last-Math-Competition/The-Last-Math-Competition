# 00000005144

**HD2-04 ◆** — source: 157_复几何II_Hodge理论.md

**English.** Definition: An absolute Hodge cycle is a cycle stable under all automorphisms. Conjecture: The lower bound of the Galois orbit size of such cycles at genus two is a logarithmic power of the height, with power one, extended from CM type to general type. (logarithmic lower bound of absolute Hodge orbits)

**中文。** 定义：绝对 Hodge 圈指在一切自同构下稳定的圈。猜想：亏格二的此类圈伽罗瓦轨道尺寸下界为高度的对数幂,幂为一且由 CM 型推广到一般型。（绝对 Hodge 轨道对数下界）
