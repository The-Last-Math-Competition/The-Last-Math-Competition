# 00000007052

**English.** Definition: Rudin's construction with multiplicative independence. Conjecture: The sum-product of Rudin-type sets holds and is optimal. (optimal multiplicative-independence Rudin construction)

**中文。** 定义：Rudin 构造：乘性独立。猜想：乘性独立：Rudin 型集合且合的和积为最优。（Rudin 构造乘性独立最优和积型集合和积独立构造）
