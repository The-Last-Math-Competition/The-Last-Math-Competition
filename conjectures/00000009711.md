# 00000009711

**English.** Definition: Scattering channels of many-body Schroedinger: asymptotic states decompose as channel direct sums of clusters plus free motion. Conjecture: The number of channels of short-range many-body scattering is finite and equals the count of bound-state cluster partitions; the direct sum of channel spaces is dense on the energy surface, and the asymptotic-completeness deficit is zero. (scattering channel finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：多体 Schrödinger 的散射通道：渐近态分解为聚簇加自由运动的通道直和。猜想：短程位势多体散射的通道数有限且等于束缚态聚簇划分的计数；通道空间的直和在能量面上稠密且渐近完备的亏损为零。（散射通道有限性）
