# 00000005980

**English.** Definition: The singular spectrum and the eigenvector statistics are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two non-Hermitian models with the same singular spectrum but different eigenvector statistics, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same spectrum but different polar decompositions. (same singular spectrum-different polar separation)

**中文。** 定义：奇异谱与特征向量统计指两层。猜想：存在两非厄米模型的奇异谱相同而特征向量统计不同,分离由同谱异极分解的显式对实现。（奇异谱同-向量异极分解分离）
