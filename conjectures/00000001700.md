# 00000001700

**EG-10 ★** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: The Rauzy fractal is the invariant set of three-dimensional continued substitutions (Atelier substitutions). Conjecture: Its Hausdorff dimension satisfies the closed form dim_H = (1/2)·log(λ)/log(β), where λ and β are the two associated eigenvalues; this value is strictly between 1 and 3/2.

**中文。** 定义：Rauzy 髓指三维持续替代的不变集合(Atelier 替换)。猜想：其 Hausdorff 维数满足闭式 dim_H = (1/2)·log(λ)/log(β),λ,β 为关联的两个特征值;该值严格大于 1 且小于 3/2。
