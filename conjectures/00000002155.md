**English.** Definition: Jac of a complete multipartite graph is its sandpile group. Conjecture: Jac is a direct sum of cyclic groups with index the greatest common divisor; and the kernel of the factors is the spanning-tree count. (complete multipartite spanning-tree count Jac direct sum)

**中文。** 定义：完全多部图的 Jac 为 sandpile 群。猜想：Jac 为循环群直和且指数为最大公因子；且因子的核为生成树计数。（完全多部生成树计数 Jac 直和）
