# 00000001246

**CG-46 △** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Conjecture: The topological entropy of a one-dimensional cellular automaton is computable by a two-term recursion if and only if the local rule is number-conserving (entropy computability).

**中文。** 猜想：一维 CA 的拓扑熵可由 2 项递推计算当且仅当局部规则为 number-conserving(熵可计算性)。
