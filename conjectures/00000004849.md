# 00000004849

**English.** Definition: The growth series is the generating function of the sphere-counting function. Conjecture: The convergence radii of growth series of hyperbolic groups of fixed generator number form a discrete set of real algebraic numbers with no finite accumulation point and explicit isolation. (discreteness of the growth convergence-radius spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：增长级数指球计数函数的母函数。猜想：固定生成元数的双曲群增长级数收敛半径构成实代数数的离散集,无有限聚点且孤立性显式。（增长收敛半径谱离散性）
