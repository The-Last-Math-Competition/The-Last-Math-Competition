# 00000003048

**IP-08 ★** — source: 87_非线性PDE的可积检验.md

**English.** Definition: Completeness of scattering data: asymptotic completeness of eigenvalues. Conjecture: The completeness criterion for scattering data: completeness of Jost solutions holds if and only if there are no resonances, and the real parts of resonances are isolated spectral obstructions. (Jost completeness resonance obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：散射数据的完全性：特征值的渐近完备。猜想：散射数据的完备性判据：Jost 解的完备性当且仅当无共振且共振的实部为谱障碍的孤立点。（Jost 完备共振障碍）
