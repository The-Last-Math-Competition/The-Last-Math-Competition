**AS-17 ★** — source: 80_代数统计与张量分解的几何.md

**English.** Definition: The algebraic-statistical side of MDS codes: maximal distance separable codes and Segre varieties. Conjecture: The geometric form of the MDS conjecture: the maximal length of arcs is q+1 with the exception q+2 for even q; the statistical counterpart is the tightening of the MDS bound for the variance lower bound of second-moment estimators. (MDS arc length geometric bound)

**中文。** 定义：MDS 码的代数统计侧：最大距离可分码与 Segre 簇。猜想：MDS 猜想的几何形式：弧的极大长度为 q+1 且例外为 q 偶的 q+2 弧；统计侧的对应为二阶矩估计的方差下界的 MDS 界的紧化。（MDS 弧长度几何界）
