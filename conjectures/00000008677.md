**CGT-27 ★** — source: 274_计算群论与字的几何.md

**English.** Definition: Orbit Turing degrees are the computability degrees of orbit equivalence relations of group actions. Conjecture: The Bernstein property holds for free groups; and the realizable degree spectrum is an arithmetic-closed upper cone whose complement is a thin set. (free group Bernstein degree spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：轨道 Turing 度指群作用的轨道等价关系的可计算度。猜想：自由群的 Bernstein 性质恒成立；可实现度谱为算术封闭的上锥且锥的补为薄集。（自由群 Bernstein 度谱）
