**English.** Definition: Landauer transport is the scattering formula of conductance. Conjecture: The transport lower bound of one-dimensional ergodic extended states is an explicit linear expression of the absolutely continuous spectral share, verified at the critical model and tight. (spectral-share linear lower bound of Landauer transport)

**中文。** 定义：Landauer 传输指导电的散射公式。猜想：一维遍历扩展态的传输下界为谱的绝对连续份额的显式线性式,临界模型处验证且紧。（Landauer 传输谱份额线性下界）
