**English.** Definition: Etale cohomology with the foundation of l-adic theory. Conjecture: The finiteness of l-adic cohomology of proper flat schemes holds, and the rank is the l-adicization of Betti numbers. (Betti-l-adic proper-flat finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：平展上同调：ℓ-进理论的基础。猜想：ℓ-进的有限性：proper 平坦的 ℓ-进上同调的有限且限的秩为 Betti 数的 ℓ-进化。（l-进 proper 平坦 Betti 有限秩上同调）
