**English.** Definition: The degeneration of tensors is the limit structure of group orbits. Conjecture: The degeneration order is equivalent to the variety-inclusion order; and the layer spectrum of the equivalence is a finite poset lattice, completely computed at small sizes. (inclusion-equivalent degeneration lattice)
