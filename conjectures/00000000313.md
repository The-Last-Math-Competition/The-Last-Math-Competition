**DA-13 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The Lagrange spectrum L is the set of all Markov constants; spectral points are the isolated values in L. Conjecture: All isolated points of L between 3 and 3√2 are quadratic irrationals; and the isolated points in this interval form a finite, explicitly generated table. (Lagrange isolated points quadratic irrational)

**中文。** 定义：Lagrange 谱 L 为一切 Markov 常数之集，谱点指属于 L 的孤立值。猜想：L 在 3 与 3√2 之间的一切孤立点都是二次无理数；且此区间内孤立点恰为有限个由显式词生成的表。（Lagrange 孤立点二次无理）
