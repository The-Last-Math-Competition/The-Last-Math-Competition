**English.** Definition: The free sphere is the matrix ball of noncommutative function theory. Conjecture: The solvability criterion for Nevanlinna–Pick interpolation on it is positive definiteness of the free Pick matrix; and the kernel of the criterion is the free Szegő kernel. (free sphere Nevanlinna-Pick criterion)

**中文。** 定义：自由球面为 nc 函数论的矩阵球。猜想：其上的 Nevanlinna–Pick 插值可解判据为自由 Pick 矩阵正定；且判据的核为自由 Szegő。（自由球 Nevanlinna-Pick 判据）
