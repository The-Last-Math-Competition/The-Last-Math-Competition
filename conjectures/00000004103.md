# 00000004103

**CG4-13 ★** — source: 122_组合几何II_配置空间.md

**English.** Definition: The torsion subgroup of a graph braid group is the set of all its finite-order elements. Conjecture: The torsion subgroup of a graph braid group is either trivial or cyclic of order 2, the order-2 case occurs if and only if the graph has an involutive star symmetry, and the classification is complete. (two-valued torsion classification of graph braids)

**中文。** 定义：图辫群的挠子群指其全部有限阶元。猜想：图辫群的挠子群或平凡或为 2 阶循环,2 阶出现当且仅当图含对合星形对称,分类完全。（图辫挠二值分类）
