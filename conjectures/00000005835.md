# 00000005835

**English.** Definition: The orthogonality relations are the inner-product laws of characters. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit correction of modular-reduction deficits, the correction vanishing on defect-zero blocks, and the within-block formula is exact. (deficit-correction conversion formula of orthogonality)

**中文。** 定义：正交关系指特征标的内积律。猜想：换算公式为模约化亏损的显式修正,亏零块处修正消失且块内公式精确。（正交亏损修正换算公式）
