# 00000002492

**English.** Definition: Borwein-type conjecture: positivity of triple products. Conjecture: The coefficients of certain triple product expansions are nonnegative, with the counting content given by cyclic counts: the positivity closes under cyclic enumeration. (Borwein conjecture triple-product positivity cyclic counting)

**中文。** 定义：Borwein 型 conjecture：三积正性。猜想：三积的正性：乘积展开的系数正性且性的组合为循环计数。（Borwein 型 conjecture 三积正性乘积展开系数正性循环计数组合 Borwein 循环计数系数正性计数循环系数正性计数循环正性计数）
