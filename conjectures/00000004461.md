**English.** Definition: The BPS invariants are the integer-valued spectrum of DT counts. Conjecture: The genus-zero BPS generating function is an explicit rational combination of modular forms with leading constant 1, and the higher-genus coefficients are given by derivatives of quasimodular forms. (BPS modular-form leading normalization)

**中文。** 定义：BPS 不变量指 DT 计数的整值谱。猜想：亏格零 BPS 母函数为模形式的显式有理组合,首项常数为 1,高亏格系数由拟模形式导数给出。（BPS 模形式首项归一）
