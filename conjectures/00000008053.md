# 00000008053

**English.** Definition: The tower law for termination lengths of Goodstein sequences: the termination step count tau(n) of the Goodstein sequence G(n) (starting at base 2, iteratively raising the base and subtracting one). Conjecture: The exact characterization of tau(n): tau(n) equals the Hercules-Hercules descent length of the notation tree T_n; the maximum of tau over the first n naturals is an elementary approximation of type F_{epsilon_0}(c log n) (F the fast-growing hierarchy); and the double-logarithmic plot of the distribution of tau(n) (uniform n at most N) is a straight line of slope -1/2 (a heavy-tailed power law), the infimum density given by the index distribution of the Hardy hierarchy. (Goodstein epsilon_0 heavy-tail slope)

**中文。** 定义：Goodstein 序列的终止长度的塔律：Goodstein 序列 G(n)（基底 2 起，迭代提升基底并减一）的终止步数 τ(n)。猜想：τ(n) 的精确刻画：τ(n) = 记号树 T_n 的 Hercules–Hercules 下降长度；且 τ 在前 n 个自然数上的最大值为 F_{ε₀}(c·log n) 型初等逼近（F 为快速层级）；τ(n) 的分布（均匀 n≤N）的双对数图像为斜率 -1/2 的直线（重尾幂律），下确界密度由 Hardy 层级的索引分布给出。
