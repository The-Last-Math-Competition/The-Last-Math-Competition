# 00000007314

**English.** Definition: Wishart tensors with sample covariance. Conjecture: The spectral limit of sample tensors holds, and the kernel of the limit is the free convolution of free moments. (free-moment Wishart tensor spectral limit)

**中文。** 定义：Wishart 张量：样本协方差。猜想：Wishart 的谱：样本张量的谱的极限且限的核为自由矩的自由卷积。（Wishart 张量自由卷积自由矩样本谱极限核）
