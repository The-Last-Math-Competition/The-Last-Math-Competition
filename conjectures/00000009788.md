# 00000009788

**English.** Definition: Determinant-trace duality of Hilbert-Schmidt norms: Young's inequality for log-det and trace. Conjecture: The optimal constant of the Young-type dual inequality between log det and trace norm is 1; equality cases are an explicit one-parameter family of Gibbs-type operators, and the family has measure zero in the Schatten classes. (log-det Young duality)

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert–Schmidt 范数的行列式迹对偶：log-det 与迹的 Young 不等式。猜想：log det 与迹范数的 Young 型对偶不等式的最优常数为 1；等号情形为 Gibbs 型算子的显式单参数族且族在 Schatten 类中测度为零。（log-det Young 对偶）
