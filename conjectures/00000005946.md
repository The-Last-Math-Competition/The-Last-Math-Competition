**English.** Definition: The roughness exponent is the growth exponent of interface roughness. Conjecture: The conversion formula gives the explicit attainable domain of the exponent, at most one half in this class, with endpoints controlled by the linear limit and extreme tilt. (attainable-domain formula of the roughness exponent)
