**English.** Definition: A rough space-time wave is the wave equation driven by rough space-time noise. Conjecture: The deficit in the energy identity equals an explicit quadratic form in the cross-variation of the driver, and the deficit becomes exactly zero after the Stratonovich correction. (quadratic form of the rough-wave energy deficit)

**中文。** 定义：时空粗糙波指粗糙时空噪声驱动的波动方程。猜想：能量恒等式的亏损等于驱动交叉变差的显式二次型,且亏损经 Stratonovich 修正后精确为零。（粗波能量亏损二次型）
