# 00000004999

**HL2-09 ◆** — source: 152_微分几何V_特殊和乐.md

**English.** Definition: An irreducible symplectic manifold is a hyper-Kaehler manifold with simple deformation type. Conjecture: The dimension of its deformation space is an explicit linear expression in the second Betti number, the upper bound is realized by symmetric power types of K3, and it is closed in dimension four. (linear upper bound of irreducible symplectic deformation dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：不可约辛流形指单形变类的超凯勒流形。猜想：其形变空间维数为二阶贝蒂数的显式线性式,上界由 K3 幂对称积型实现且四维时封闭。（不可约辛形变维线性上界）
