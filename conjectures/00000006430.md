# 00000006430

**SD3-30 △** — source: 199_几何测度V_对称差问题.md

**English.** Definition: Product conversion and counting polynomials are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two lattice sets with the same product-count conversion but different counting polynomials, and the separation is realized by an explicit lattice pair with the same sum count but different coefficients. (same product count-different polynomial separation)

**中文。** 定义：积换算与计数多项式指两层。猜想：存在两格点集的积计数换算相同而计数多项式不同,分离由同和计数异系数的显式格点对实现。（积计数同-多项式异分离）
