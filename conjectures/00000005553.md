**English.** Definition: Overconvergent sheaves are lisse sheaves beyond the convergence boundary. Conjecture: The radius deficit is the explicit logarithmic expression of the spectral slope; it degenerates to ordinary convergence with zero deficit at unit slope. (logarithmic slope deficit of overconvergence)

**中文。** 定义：过收敛层指越过收敛边界的刚层。猜想：半径亏损为谱斜率的显式对数式；单位斜率时退化为常收敛且亏损为零。（过收敛斜率对数亏损）
