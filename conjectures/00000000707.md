# 00000000707

**PA-07 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: The geometric mean of Browkin partial quotients G_N = (∏_{j≤N}|a_j|)^{1/N}. Conjecture: For almost every ξ ∈ Q_p, G_N converges to a Khinchin-type constant K_p given by the explicit integral formula log K_p = Σ_{a∈reps} log|a|·log(p/(p−|a|)); K_p lies in ((p−1)/2, p/2).

**中文。** 定义：Browkin 部分商的几何平均 G_N=(∏_{j≤N}|a_j|)^{1/N}。猜想：对 a.e. ξ∈Q_p,G_N 收敛到 Khinchin 型常数 K_p,且 K_p 满足积分公式 log K_p = Σ_{a∈reps} log|a|·log(p/(p-|a|)) 的显式赋值;K_p 位于 ((p−1)/2, p/2)。
