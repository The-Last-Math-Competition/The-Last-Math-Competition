# 00000004781

**English.** Definition: A Fano variety is a variety with ample anticanonical line bundle. Conjecture: The total Betti number of smooth five-dimensional Fanos has an explicit upper bound, attained by product varieties and consistent with the finiteness of homotopy types in fixed dimension. (total Betti upper bound of five-dimensional Fanos)

**中文。** 定义：Fano 簇指反典范线丛丰富的簇。猜想：五维光滑 Fano 的全 Betti 数有显式上界,界由积簇达到且与固定维数的同伦型有限性一致。（五维 Fano 全 Betti 上界）
