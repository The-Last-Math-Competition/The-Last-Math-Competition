# 00000005603

**English.** Definition: Average behavior is the expected performance on random input. Conjecture: The two-dimensional average conversion constant is always given by the integral over the uniform measure of the lattice, differing from the worst case by a constant factor. (two-dimensional closed-form integral constant)

**中文。** 定义：平均行为指随机输入的期望性能。猜想：二维的平均换算常数恒由格的均匀测度积分给出；且与最坏情形差常数因子。（二维平均闭式积分常数）
