# 00000000621

**English.** Definition: The flip number counts flips of a knot diagram. Conjecture: The Khovanov rank is at least the one-half power of the flip number; and tightness of the bound is attained by flip dances. (Khovanov rank flip lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：翻转数为纽结图的翻转计数量。猜想：Khovanov 秩至少为翻转数的二分之一次幂；且下界的紧性为翻转让舞。（Khovanov 秩翻转下界）
