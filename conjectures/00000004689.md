# 00000004689

**SG3-29 ◆** — source: 141_交换代数IV_奇点理论.md

**English.** Definition: The number of generators of the integral closure of a three-dimensional singularity is the minimal number of generators of the closure as a module over the original ring, related to the multiplicity e and the embedding dimension. Conjecture: The generator count is at most the quadratic expression (e-1)(e-2)/2, attained by monomial germs; the ratio of the generator count to the multiplicity converges to (d-1)/2 with the lower bound attained by hypersurface germs; and each increment layer of the embedding difference contributes exactly one new generator. (quadratic upper bound of three-dimensional integral closures)

**中文。** 定义：三维奇点的整闭包生成元数：整闭包作为原环模的最小生成元数与重数 e、嵌入维数的关系。猜想：生成元数不超过 (e-1)(e-2)/2 型二次式且界由单项式芽达到；生成元数与重数之比收敛于 (d-1)/2 且下界由超曲面芽达到；嵌入差的每个增量层恰贡献一个新生成元。（三维整闭包二次上界）
