# 00000001968

**English.** Definition: Many-body localization is the spectral localization of one-dimensional spin chains without external noise. Conjecture: There exist one-dimensional spin chains without external noise (random-field XXZ type) whose spectrum is pure point and whose fractionalized eigenvalues have an infinite-lifetime limit (rigorous existence of the MBL phase).

**中文。** 定义：多体局域化指无外噪声的一维自旋链的谱局域。猜想：存在无外噪声的一维自旋链(随机场 XXZ 型),其谱为纯点且分式化特征值的无穷寿命极限存在(MBL 相的严格存在性)。
