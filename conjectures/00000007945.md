# 00000007945

**INT-15 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The uniqueness of quasifunctions under Virasoro constraints: the one-sided control condition on the coefficients of the double Schur expansion of the tau function of the 2D Toda lattice. Conjecture: The Virasoro constraints (W constraints) uniquely determine the generalized Toda tau function with parameters (q,t) within the convergence domain of the series; and the gauge-uniqueness index of the solution (the number of free constants) equals the dimension of the Hurwitz space; the single-pole index of Hurwitz numbers in ELSV-type connections agrees with the count of maximal chains of the elementary Schur support of tau (a double duality). (Toda Virasoro uniqueness Hurwitz dimension)

**中文。** 定义：symmetry 的 Virasoro 约束的拟函数唯一性：2D Toda 格的 τ 函数的双重 Schur 展开的系数单侧控制条件。猜想：Virasoro 约束（W 约束）唯一决定含参数 (q,t) 的广义 Toda τ 函数（在级数收敛域内）；且约束的解的规范唯一性指标（自由常数的个数）等于 Hurwitz 空间的维度；ELSV 型连接的 Hurwitz 数的单极点指标与 τ 的初等 Schur 支撑的极大链计数一致（双对偶）。
