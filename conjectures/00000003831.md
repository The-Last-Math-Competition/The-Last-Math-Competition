# 00000003831

**English.** Definition: The crystal character ch(B) denotes the generating function of vertex weights. Conjecture: For any finite crystal, the coefficients of the Schur expansion of the Weyl symmetrization of ch(B) are nonnegative, and for B = B(λ)⊗B(μ) these coefficients are exactly the classical Littlewood–Richardson coefficients. (symmetrized Schur nonnegativity)

**中文。** 定义：晶体字符 ch(B) 指顶点权的生成函数。猜想：对任意有限晶体,ch(B) 的 Weyl 对称化的 Schur 展开系数非负,且当 B = B(λ)⊗B(μ) 时这些系数恰为经典 Littlewood–Richardson 系数。（对称化 Schur 非负）
