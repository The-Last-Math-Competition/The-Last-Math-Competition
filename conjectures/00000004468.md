**English.** Definition: The motivic realization map is the specialization from motivic DT to numerical DT. Conjecture: The deficit term is an explicit monomial in the generating variables, and the deficit vanishes at torus fixed points and only there. (monomial vanishing of the motivic deficit at fixed points)

**中文。** 定义：motivic 实现映射指 motivic DT 到数值 DT 的特殊化。猜想：亏损项为生成变量的显式单项式,亏损在环面不动点处消失且仅在该处消失。（motivic 亏损单项式固定点消失）
