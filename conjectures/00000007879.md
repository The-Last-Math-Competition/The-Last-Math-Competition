# 00000007879

**HCA-09 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The spatialization of the 2-Segal conjecture: the 2-Segal condition on simplicial spaces is dual to the associativity of Hall algebras; the Hall module H(X) of a 2-Segal space X. Conjecture: The homotopy coherence obstruction to associativity of the composition product of H(X) vanishes when X is a Waldhausen S-construction; and the Grothendieck group of the Hall module of any 2-Segal space is the elementary linearization of the 2-Segal condition, its rank equal to the Euler characteristic of the simplicial set, with degeneration (1-Segalization) corresponding to unification of the rank. (2-Segal Hall module associativity)

**中文。** 定义：2-Segal 猜想的空间化：单纯空间的 2-Segal 条件对偶于 Hall 代数的结合。定义 2-Segal 谱 X 的 Hall 模 H(X)。猜想：H(X) 的合成积的结合性的同伦凝聚障碍在 X 为 Waldhausen S-构造时消没；且任意 2-Segal 空间的 Hall 模的 Grothendieck 群为 2-Segal 条件的初等线性化，其秩等于单纯集的 Euler 特征，退化(1-Segal 化)对应于秩的合一。
