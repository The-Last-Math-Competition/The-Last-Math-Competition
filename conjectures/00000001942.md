# 00000001942

**LG-12 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: An effective Ratner theorem is the quantitative version of the approximation rate of orbit closures. Conjecture: The approximation rate of orbit closures of nilflows under SL₂(R) is t^{-c} with c = 1/4; this exponent is optimal (via the decay of Fourier coefficients of equidistribution).

**中文。** 定义：有效 Ratner 定理指轨道闭包的逼近率的定量版本。猜想：SL₂(R) 幂 nil 流的轨道闭包的逼近率为 t^{-c}(c=1/4);该指数为最优(由等分布的 Fourier 系数的衰减)。
