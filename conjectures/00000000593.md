# 00000000593

**English.** Definition: Rings of invariants k[x_1,…,x_n]^G (G finite). Conjecture: The coefficient sequence of the Molien series is rational with denominator dividing an explicit chain of factors (1 − t^{d}) (modular periodic structure).

**中文。** 定义：不变量环 k[x_1,…,x_n]^G(G 有限)。猜想：Molien 级数的系数序列为有理函数且分母整除 (1 − t^{d}) 的显式链(模周期结构)。
