# 00000003463

**English.** Definition: Circular-arc graphs are intersection graphs of chords, whose local coloring complement characterizes the coloring structure of neighborhoods. Conjecture: The dimension of the local coloring complement of circular-arc graphs is at most four, with the dimension spectrum exactly the integers one through four; the minimal circular-arc graph attaining dimension four is the two-crossing double-prism local configuration. (circular-arc local coloring dimension)

**中文。** 定义：圆图为弦的圆弧交图，其局部染色补刻画邻域的染色结构。猜想：圆图的局部染色补的维数不超过四且维数谱恰为一至四的整数集；达到维数四的极小圆图为交叉数二的双棱型局部构型。（圆图局部染色维数）
