**English.** Definition: The BMS spectral sequence is the spectral sequence from THH to typical homology. Conjecture: The d_2 differential is always given by the linearization of Frobenius, and the first nonzero instance appears at an explicit cohomology class on the second page. (BMS d_2 linearization formula)
