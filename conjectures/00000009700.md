# 00000009700

**English.** Definition: The dimension geometry of algebraically independent sets: Hausdorff and box dimensions of transcendental sets. Conjecture: Every algebraically independent set of transcendental numbers has full Hausdorff dimension 1, and the box dimension spectrum of its complement is an explicit subinterval of [0,1/2]; algebraically independent number sets are full-measure dense G-delta under Lebesgue measure. (algebraic independence dimension spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：代数无关集合的维数几何：超越数集的 Hausdorff 维数与盒维数。猜想：一切代数无关超越数集的 Hausdorff 维数为满维 1 且其补集的盒维数谱为 [0,1/2] 的显式子区间；代数无关数集在 Lebesgue 测度下为满测度稠密 Gδ。（代数无关维数谱）
