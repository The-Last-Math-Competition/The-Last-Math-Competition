# 00000005263

**English.** Definition: The turbulence spectrum is the distribution law of energy over wavenumbers. Conjecture: The spectrum of convex-integral solutions in the inertial range is the explicit negative five-thirds law, converted from the amplitude-frequency iteration law of the perturbations and stable in the transport window. (negative five-thirds conversion of convex-integral spectra)

**中文。** 定义：湍流谱指能量按波数的分布律。猜想：凸积分构造解的谱在惯性区为显式负三分之五律,谱由扰动的振幅频率迭代律换算且在传输窗口稳定。（凸积分谱负三分之五换算）
