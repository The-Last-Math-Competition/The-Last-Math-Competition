# 00000001007

**English.** Conjecture: In even characteristic, every ovoid of Q(4,q) is an elliptic quadric (uniqueness; the odd-characteristic case has Suzuki's counterexamples) — the even-characteristic uniqueness conjecture.

**中文。** 猜想：偶特征 Q(4, q) 的 ovoid 皆为椭圆二次曲面(唯一性,奇特征有 Suzuki 反例)— 偶特征唯一性猜想。
