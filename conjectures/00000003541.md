# 00000003541

**OC3-21 ★** — source: 103_序数与组合数论III.md

**English.** Definition: The measure theory of ordinals: measures on ordinals. Conjecture: The atomicity of ordinal measures: the atomic decomposition of measures on ordinals is countable, with the weight of decomposition the concentration of the measure. (ordinal measure atomic countable decomposition weight)

**中文。** 定义：序数的测度论：序数上的测度。猜想：序数测度的原子性：序数上的测度的原子分解为可数且分解的权为测度的浓度。（序数测度原子可数分解权）
