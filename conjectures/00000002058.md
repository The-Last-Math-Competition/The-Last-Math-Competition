# 00000002058

**UA-08 ★** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Equational Noetherianity means every finite system of equations is equivalent to a finite subsystem. Conjecture: Algebraic sets over M_n(K) are Noetherian if and only if n ≤ 2; concretely, algebraic sets over M₃(K) are non-Noetherian (by a construction of infinite ascending chains of commutator polynomials).

**中文。** 定义：等式 Noether 性指有限方程组等价于有限子组。猜想：M_n(K) 上代数集的 Noether 性 ⟺ n≤2;具体地 M₃(K) 的代数集非 Noether(由换位子多项式的无限升链构造)。
