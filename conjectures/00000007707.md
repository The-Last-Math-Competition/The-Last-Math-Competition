# 00000007707

**English.** Definition: The rank growth of the Cech cohomology of substitution tiling spaces is the supremum growth of r(sigma)=sum dim H^i(Omega_sigma) in the tile count n(sigma). Conjecture: Over all primitive substitutions sigma, r(sigma) <= n(sigma)^2 - n(sigma) + 1, with the bound asymptotically attained by single-tile double substitutions at n=3,4. (quadratic cohomology rank growth)

**中文。** 定义：替换铺砌空间的 Čech 上同调 H^*(Ω_σ) 的秩增长指 r(σ)=Σ_i dim H^i(Ω_σ) 关于 tile 数 n(σ) 的上确界增长。猜想：遍历一切原始替换 σ，r(σ) ≤ n(σ)^2 - n(σ) + 1 且该界由单 tile 双替换族在 n=3,4 时渐近取得。
