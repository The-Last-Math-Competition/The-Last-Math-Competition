**English.** Definition: The avalanche-size distribution is a renormalized statistic of the stationary sandpile. Conjecture: The exponents of the moments of 2D BTW stationary avalanches are 2k−2 plus anomalous corrections; and the explicit table of corrected exponents is given by the operator spectrum of conformal field theory. (BTW avalanche moment exponents)
