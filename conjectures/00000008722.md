# 00000008722

**English.** Definition: Nonergodic extended states are fractal eigenstates with inverse participation ratio between zero and one. Conjecture: The fractal dimension of high-energy states of random regular graphs is always close to one; interactions make the fractal dimension flow to one, and a pure fractal phase exists stably only in high dimension. (near-extended fractal dimension flow)

**中文。** 定义：非遍历扩展态指逆参与率介于零与一的分形本征态。猜想：随机正则图的高能态的分形维恒接近一；相互作用使分形维流向一且纯分形相仅在高维稳定存在。（近扩展态分形维流）
