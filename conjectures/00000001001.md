# 00000001001

**FG-01 ◆** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: A complete (k,2)-arc of PG(2,q) is a k-point set with no three collinear that admits no further point. Conjecture: The number of isomorphism classes of complete (q+1)-arcs is q^{c·q+O(log q)} as q → ∞ (odd q), with c an explicit positive constant; the count is squeezed between the constructive lower bounds of Segre's k-arc theory and exclusion upper bounds.

**中文。** 定义：PG(2,q) 的 complete (k,2)-arc 指不可再添加点且无三点共线的 k 点集。猜想：complete (q+1)-arc 的同构类个数当 q→∞(奇 q)时为 q^{c·q+O(log q)},c 为显式正常数;该计数由 Segre k-弧理论的构造族下界与排除上界夹逼。
