# 00000005241

**MS3-11 ◆** — source: 160_代数几何V_模空间.md

**English.** Definition: The moduli space of stable bundles parametrizes semistable vector bundles on a curve. Conjecture: The dimension of the singular locus of genus three and rank two is given by the count of recombination types, with each type's dimension an explicit combinatorial expression; the maximal-dimension locus is the intersection of two recombination lines, with dimension exactly 2; and the union of singular loci is a proper closed subset of codimension at least 1. (recombination dimensions of the genus-three singular locus)

**中文。** 定义：稳定丛模空间：曲线上半稳定向量丛的模。猜想：亏格三秩二的奇异轨迹维数由重组类型计数给出且每型维数为组合显式式；最大维轨迹为两条重组线之交且维数恰为 2；奇异轨迹的并为真闭子集且余维至少 1。（亏格三奇异轨迹重组维数）
