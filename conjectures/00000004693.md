# 00000004693

**EC4-03 ★** — source: 142_遍历论的组合_熵与计数.md

**English.** Definition: Topological entropy is the exponential growth rate of orbit complexity. Conjecture: The criterion for an interval smooth map to have a positive lower entropy bound is an explicit separation condition on the critical orbits, given by a combination of the branch count and the total variation of the derivative, and it is verifiable. (positive-entropy criterion of interval maps)

**中文。** 定义：拓扑熵指轨道复杂度的指数增长率。猜想：区间光滑映射的拓扑熵有正下界的判据为临界点轨道的显式分离条件,由分支数与导数变差的组合给出且可验证。（区间映射熵正判据）
