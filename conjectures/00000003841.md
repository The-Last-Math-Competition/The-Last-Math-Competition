# 00000003841

**RC-21 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: The plactic monoid P_n denotes words over the alphabet {1,…,n} modulo Knuth equivalence. Conjecture: There exists a constant c(n) such that the number of surjective homomorphisms from P_n to any finite monoid M is at most |M|^{c(n)}, with optimal exponent c(n) ≥ 1 growing unboundedly in n. (plactic quotient count bound)

**中文。** 定义：plactic 幺半群 P_n 指字母 {1,…,n} 上的字模 Knuth 等价。猜想：存在常数 c(n) 使 P_n 到任意有限幺半群 M 的满同态个数不超过 |M|^{c(n)},且最优指数 c(n) ≥ 1 并随 n 无界增长。（plactic 商计数界）
