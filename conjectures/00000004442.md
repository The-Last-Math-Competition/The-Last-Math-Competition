**English.** Definition: The log-gamma polymer is the directed polymer with inverse-Gamma weights. Conjecture: The limiting constant of its free-energy variance is an explicit functional of the second derivative of the Gamma function, and the constant is the renormalized limit and exact. (log-gamma variance Gamma functional)

**中文。** 定义：log-gamma 聚合物指逆 Gamma 权重的定向聚合物。猜想：其自由能方差的极限常数为 Gamma 函数二阶导的显式泛函,常数为重整极限且精确。（log-gamma 方差 Gamma 泛函）
