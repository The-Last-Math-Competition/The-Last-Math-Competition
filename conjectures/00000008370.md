# 00000008370

**TRP-20 ★** — source: 264_热带几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The tropicalization of the Arakelov metric: the determinant green_det of the tropicalization of Green functions (Green functions on graphs). Conjecture: green_det (the determinant of the graph Green equal to the capacity of the Jacobian) is the normalization tau(Gamma) n^{-1} (a tau-over-n normalization law); the domain of algebraicity of Green values (rational values) is integral-weight graphs (an integral-weight rationality law); transcendence of nonintegral (real-weight) values is universal (a universal transcendence law); and the metric of the sum of Arakelov heights (tropical heights, the tropical Faltings) is a quadratic form in the h-vector (an h quadratic metric law). (graph Green capacity integral transcendence Faltings)

**中文。** 定义：热带几何的 Arakelov 的度量的热带化：Green 函数的热带化（图上的 Green）的行列式 green_det。猜想：green_det（图 Green 的行列式 = 雅可比的容度）= τ(Γ)·n^{-1} 的规范化（τ/n 规范律）；且 Green 的值（两点 Green 的谱）的代数性（有理值）的域为整权图（整权有理律）；非整（实权）的超越（值的超越）的普遍（普遍超越律）；Arakelov 高度（热带高度）的和（Faltings 的热带）的度量为 h-向量的二次型（h 二次度量律）。
