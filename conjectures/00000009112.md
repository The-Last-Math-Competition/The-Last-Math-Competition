**English.** Definition: Nikodym sets: sets with missing codirections. Conjecture: Nikodym sets in the plane have dimension two, and full dimension holds in higher dimensions; and the volume concentration of the construction is in tube neighborhoods. (Nikodym full-dimensional construction)

**中文。** 定义：Nikodym 集为余方向缺失的集。猜想：Nikodym 集在平面的维数为二且在高维满维成立；且构造的体积浓度为管邻域。（Nikodym 满维构造）
