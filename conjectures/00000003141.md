# 00000003141

**LG2-11 ★** — source: 90_李群微分方程.md

**English.** Definition: Momentum maps of Lie group actions: symplectic Hamiltonian actions. Conjecture: The convexity of the image of the momentum map (Kostant's convexity theorem): the image is the orthogonal projection of the weight polytope, with the vertices of the projection the extreme points of the Weyl group orbit. (Kostant convexity vertex extremality)

**中文。** 定义：李群作用的动量映射：辛哈密顿作用。猜想：动量映射的像的凸性（Kostant 凸性定理）：像为权多面体的正交投影且投影的顶点为 Weyl 群轨道的极值点。（Kostant 凸性顶点极值）
