# 00000004169

**CM3-19 ★** — source: 124_解析数论III_圆法.md

**English.** Definition: The Kloosterman sum is S(a,b;n) = the sum over x of e((ax + b x^{-1})/n), where x^{-1} is the inverse of x modulo n. Conjecture: The bias constant of its sign distribution is the explicit value -1/2, and the bias decays by negative powers of 2 with the number of prime factors. (bias constant of Kloosterman signs)

**中文。** 定义：Kloosterman 和指 S(a,b;n) = Σe((ax+bx 上划线)/n),x 上划线 为模 n 逆。猜想：其符号分布的偏向常数为 −1/2 型显式值,偏向随素因子个数按 2 的负幂衰减。（Kloosterman 符号偏向常数）
