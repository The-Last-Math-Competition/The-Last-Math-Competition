# 00000004436

**English.** Definition: pushTASEP is the long-range exclusion process with pushing. Conjecture: The kernel of its multipoint distribution is an explicit pushing correction of a discrete kernel, the correction is a binomial-coefficient multiplier, and at zero pushing strength it degenerates to the known kernel. (pushTASEP binomial-corrected kernel)

**中文。** 定义：pushTASEP 指带推挤的长程排斥过程。猜想：其多点分布的核为离散核的显式推挤修正,修正是二项系数乘子,推挤强度为零时退化为已知核。（pushTASEP 二项修正核）
