# 00000008147

**English.** Definition: The renormalization of recurrent fields of random walks: the stability index stoch_fix of renormalization fixed points of field theory in a random environment (random potential V). Conjecture: The existence of stoch_fix (non-Gaussian random fixed points) first occurs at dimension d = 3 (a 3-dimensional random fixed-point law); the stable dimension of the fixed point is 2 (a two-dimensional stability); and the splitting index of the random correction (quenched versus annealed) is the angle theta_RSB of replica symmetry breaking, the limit of the renormalization flow of theta being pi/6 (an RSB limit-angle law). (random fixed-point RSB angle pi/6)

**中文。** 定义：随机游走的 recurrent 场的重整化：随机环境（随机势 V）下的场论的重整化不动点的稳定性指标 stoch_fix。猜想：stoch_fix 的存在（随机不动点的非高斯型）的最小维数 d=3（3 维随机不动点律）；且不动点的稳定维数为 2（双稳维数）；随机修正（淬火 vs 退火）的分裂指标为副本对称破缺的角度 θ_RSB，θ 的重整化的流的极限为 π/6（RSB 极限角律）。
