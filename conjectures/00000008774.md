# 00000008774

**English.** Definition: The Nystrom method: column-sampling approximation of PSD matrices. Conjecture: The expected Nystrom error is explicit (an explicitness law); the concentration between expectation and actuality (probability tails) is exponential (a concentration law); weighted sampling gives improvement (an improvement law); and the limit of improvement (optimal weighting) is 1 (a unit extremal law). (Nystrom explicit concentration weighted extremum)

**中文。** 定义：Nyström 方法：PSD 矩阵的列抽样逼近。猜想：Nyström 的误差的期望的显式（显式律）；且期望与实际（概率尾部）的集中的指数（集中律）；加权的（加权抽样的）的改进（改进律）；改进的极限（最优加权）为 1（单位极律）。
