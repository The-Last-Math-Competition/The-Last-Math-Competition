# 00000001822

**English.** Definition: The SYZ conjecture asserts that CY threefolds admit special Lagrangian torus fibrations. Conjecture: An explicit construction of the SYZ fiberation of the Fermat quintic exists (via Lagrangian sections of diagonal symmetries); the count of special Lagrangian spheres in the real structure equals 2^5 (classified by the product type of real two-spheres).

**中文。** 定义：SYZ 猜想指 CY 三维流形有特殊拉格朗日环面纤维化。猜想：Fermat quintic 的 SYZ 纤维化存在显式构造(由对角对称的 Lagrange 截面);实结构中的特殊拉格朗日球面计数 = 2^5(由实二维球面乘积型分类)。
