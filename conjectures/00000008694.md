# 00000008694

**English.** Definition: The Wasserstein gradient flow is the JKO scheme of energies on P_2. Conjecture: JKO always converges as the time step tends to zero; the convergence rate for regular energies is linear, and the A-stability domain of higher-order schemes contains a neighborhood of the imaginary axis. (JKO linear convergence A-stability)

**中文。** 定义：Wasserstein 梯度流指能量在 P₂ 上的 JKO 格式。猜想：JKO 在时间步趋于零时恒收敛；正则能量的收敛速率为线性且高阶格式的 A-稳定域包含虚轴邻域。（JKO 线性收敛 A 稳定）
