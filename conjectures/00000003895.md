# 00000003895

**English.** Definition: A semistable crystal is the crystalline attachment of a semistable representation; the inertia weights are the weights of inertia-group action. Conjecture: The number of semisimplification types of the mod p reduction of a two-dimensional crystalline representation with all inertia weights 1 is at most 4, and 4 is attainable. (inertia weight mod p type count upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：半稳定晶体指半稳定表示的晶体附件,惯性权指惯性群作用的权。猜想：惯性权全为 1 的二维晶体表示的模 p 约化的半单化型个数不超过 4,且 4 可被达到。（惯性权模 p 型数上界）
