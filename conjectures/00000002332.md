# 00000002332

**NG-12 ★** — source: 63_数值代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The topological degree of a random real polynomial system is the signed count of real solutions. Conjecture: The distribution always converges to a Rademacher convolution; the convolution depth is the dimension n and the moments are explicit. (topological degree Rademacher convolution)

**中文。** 定义：随机实多项式系统的拓扑度指实解的带符号计数。猜想：分布恒收敛到 Rademacher 卷积；且卷积深度为维度 n 而矩为显式。（拓扑度 Rademacher 卷积）
