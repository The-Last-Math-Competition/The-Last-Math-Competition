# 00000001662

**GT-02 ◆** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: Lovász's conjecture states that every connected vertex-transitive graph contains a Hamilton path. Conjecture: Lovász's conjecture holds; strengthened: vertex-transitive graphs without Hamilton paths have degree ≥ (n−1)/2 — any counterexample must be nearly complete.

**中文。** 定义：Lovász 猜想指一切连通点传递图含 Hamilton 路。猜想：Lovász 猜想成立;加强:无 Hamilton 路的点传递图的度数 ≥ (n−1)/2,即反例必近完全图。
