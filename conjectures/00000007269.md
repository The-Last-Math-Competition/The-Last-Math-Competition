# 00000007269

**English.** Definition: The variation of regularization with viscosity elimination of weak solutions. Conjecture: The convergence of weak solutions under viscosity elimination holds at rate a power of the viscosity; and the convergence rate is exactly the one-fourth power of the viscosity parameter. (viscosity-power regularized weak-solution convergence)

**中文。** 定义：正则化的变分：弱解的粘性消去。猜想：粘性消去的弱解收敛且敛的率为粘性的幂。(正则化粘性幂弱解收敛变分消去存在率)；且收敛率恰为粘性参数的四分之一次幂。
