# 00000001483

**HY-03 ◆** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Exponential mixing means correlation functions decay at exponential rates. Conjecture: The geodesic flow on compact pinched negatively curved manifolds of dimension ≥ 3 mixes exponentially (a high-dimensional open problem); the infimum of the mixing exponent λ is explicitly controlled by the minimal Anosov splitting angle θ(x) and the pressure: λ ≥ c·inf θ·h.

**中文。** 定义：指数混合指关联函数以指数速率衰减。猜想：维数 ≥3 的紧 pinched 负曲率流形上测地流指数混合(高维开放);其混合率指数 λ 的下确界由 Anosov 分裂角 θ(x) 的最小值与压强共同显式控制:λ ≥ c·inf θ·h。
