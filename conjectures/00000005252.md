# 00000005252

**English.** Definition: The boundary complement is the complement of the stable domain of the moduli space. Conjecture: Its conversion is an explicit elementary correction by test configurations, the correction is given by the torsion term of the cylinder pairing, and it is closed on stable types. (cylinder-torsion correction conversion of boundary complements)
