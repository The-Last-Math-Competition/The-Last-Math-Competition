# 00000006285

**English.** Definition: The plus construction with the determinant splitting of the noncommutative plus construction. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the determinant splitting and the field-case splitting is canonical; the canonical coefficient is one; and the kernel of the splitting is the commutator subgroup, the perfectness of the kernel being the universality of the construction. (canonical coefficient conversion of determinant splitting)

**中文。** 定义：加构造：K 理论的非交换加构造（plus construction）的行列式分裂。猜想：换算常数为行列式分裂的显式且域情形的分裂典范；典范系数为一；分裂的核为换位子群且核的完满性为构造的通用性。（行列式分裂典范系数换算）
