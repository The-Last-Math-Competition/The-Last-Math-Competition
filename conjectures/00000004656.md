# 00000004656

**English.** Definition: The shadow of a mock modular form is its modular companion in the nonholomorphic part. Conjecture: The shadows of two mock forms are orthogonal if and only if the cross terms of the principal-part sums vanish, and the criterion is explicit and complete in rank-one umbral moonshine. (principal-part criterion of shadow orthogonality)

**中文。** 定义：mock 模形式的阴影指其非全纯部分的模伴侣。猜想：两 mock 的阴影正交当且仅当主部连和交叉项为零,判据显式且在秩一影月射完全。（阴影正交连和判据）
