**English.** Definition: The log-gamma polymer: intermediate-disorder localization of the exactly integrable model. Conjecture: The endpoint localization length of the log-gamma polymer varies as an explicit power of temperature in the intermediate-disorder regime; the power exponent is continuously glued at the two end critical values, and the localization profile is a universal exponential family. (log-gamma localization profile)

**中文。** 定义：log-gamma 聚合物：精确可积模型的中无序局域化。猜想：log-gamma 聚合物端点局域化长度在中无序区按温度的显式幂变化；幂指数在两端的临界值连续拼接且局域化剖面的形状为普适指数族。（log-gamma 局域化剖面）
