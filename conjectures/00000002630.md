# 00000002630

**English.** Definition: Hecke operators on Jacobi forms: convolution liftings at prime indices. Conjecture: The Hecke eigenvalues of random Jacobi cusp forms always follow an SU(2)-type Sato–Tate law; and the symmetry center is determined explicitly by the parity of weight and index. (Jacobi Hecke spectral distribution)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式的 Hecke 算子指素指标的卷积提升。猜想：随机 Jacobi 尖点形式的 Hecke 特征值恒服从 SU(2) 型 Sato–Tate；且对称中心由权与指标宇显式确定。（Jacobi Hecke 谱分布）
