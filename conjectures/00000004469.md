# 00000004469

**English.** Definition: The quiver Grassmannian is the variety of subrepresentations of a representation. Conjecture: Its cluster constants of the Euler characteristic form a table of positive integers, computable explicitly from the dimension vectors, and positivity holds for all affine-type quivers. (affine positivity of quiver cluster constants)

**中文。** 定义：箭图 Grassmannian 指表示的子表示簇。猜想：其 Euler 特征的簇常数为正整数表,表由维数向量显式计算,正性对一切仿射型箭图成立。（箭图簇常数仿射正性）
