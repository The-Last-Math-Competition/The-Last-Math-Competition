# 00000008402

**MIB-22 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The local system index on the motivic side: the global invariant of the motivic lift of an algebraic local system. Conjecture: The motivic value of the index is the evaluation of the Lyubishkin motive of the local system at integral points; additivity of the index is preserved under covers; and the first Chern motive is the determining quantity of the index, with determinacy failing on local systems of rank at least 3. (local system motivic index)

**中文。** 定义：母题侧的局部系统指标指代数局部系统的母题化的整体不变量。猜想：指标的母题值为局部系统的 Lyubishkin 母题在积分点的求值；指标的可加性在覆盖下保持；第一 Chern 母题为指标的决定量，决定性在秩 ≥ 3 的局部系统上失效。（局部系统母题指标）
