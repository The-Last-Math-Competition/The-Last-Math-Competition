# 00000001638

**HA-08 ★** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The Picard group of the stable category StMod(kG) is its group of twist autoequivalence objects. Conjecture: Pic(StMod(kQ₈)) ≅ Z[1/2] ⊕ (Z/2)², a noncommutative generalization of the cyclic-group result Pic(StMod(kC_p)) ≅ Z[1/p]; the twist objects are realized by stable classes of bimodules.

**中文。** 定义：稳定范畴 StMod(kG) 的 Picard 群指其自等价扭对象群。猜想：Pic(StMod(kQ₈)) ≅ Z[1/2]⊕(Z/2)²,四元数群情形为循环群结果 Pic(StMod(kC_p))≅Z[1/p] 的非交换推广;扭对象由二重模的稳定类实现。
