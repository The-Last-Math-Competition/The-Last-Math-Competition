# 00000003951

**G4-11 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: A bramble is a family of pairwise touching connected subgraphs, and its order is the minimum hitting-set size over members of the family minus one. Conjecture: On graphs of treewidth k, the number of isomorphism classes of minimal brambles of order exactly k+1 grows exponentially with k and is completely determined by the decomposition structure of the k-spoke wheel. (counting minimal brambles)

**中文。** 定义：bramble 指两两接触的连通子图族,其阶为族中任取一子图的横截数减一。猜想：树宽 k 图的阶恰为 k+1 的极小 bramble 的同构类个数随 k 指数增长,并由 k 辐条轮结构的分解完全决定。（极小 bramble 计数）
