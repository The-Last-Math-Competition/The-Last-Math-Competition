**English.** Definition: Tracy-Widom limits of corner growth: one-point fluctuations of LPP converge to GUE Tracy-Widom (proven for many models). Conjecture: A complete asymptotic expansion of Tracy-Widom convergence exists: the first three coefficients are explicit universal constants, the third containing a model-dependent dissipation term; the expansion is valid inside the intermediate scale t^{1/9}. (Tracy-Widom finite-size full expansion)

**中文。** 定义：角生长的 Tracy–Widom 极限：LPP 的单点涨落收敛到 GUE Tracy–Widom（多模型已证）。猜想：Tracy–Widom 收敛的完整渐近展开存在：首三项系数为显式普适常数且第三项含模型相关的耗散项；展开的有效区间为中间尺度 t^{1/9} 的内部。（TW 有限尺寸全展开）
