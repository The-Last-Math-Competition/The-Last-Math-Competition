# 00000003253

**English.** Definition: Regular homotopy: the homotopy equivalence of immersions. Conjecture: Regular homotopy invariants: the rotation number of plane curves is a complete invariant, with the higher-dimensional generalization the Smale invariant. (Whitney-Graustein rotation number complete)

**中文。** 定义：正则同痕（regular homotopy）：浸入的同痕等价。猜想：正则同痕不变量：平面曲线的旋转数为完全不变量且高维的推广为 Smale 不变量。（Whitney-Graustein 旋转数完全）
