# 00000009567

**English.** Definition: Random translation surfaces are sampled by random permutation models (random covers of the torus); concentration properties of their Lyapunov spectra are central to large-genus Teichmueller statistics. Conjecture: Under the random permutation model, KZ exponents satisfy a large deviation principle: lambda_1 concentrates in an O(1/g) neighborhood of 1, and the variance of the spectral sum is O(1/g); the empirical spectral distribution converges as g grows to an explicit continuous density (a Marchenko-Pastur type limit law). (random surface spectral concentration)

**中文。** 定义：随机平移曲面由随机置换模型（随机覆盖环面）采样；其 Lyapunov 谱的集中性质是大亏格 Teichmüller 统计的核心。猜想：随机置换模型下 KZ 指数满足大偏差原理：λ_1 集中于 1 的 O(1/g) 邻域，且谱和 Σλ_i 的方差为 O(1/g)；谱的经验分布在 g→∞ 收敛到显式连续密度（Marchenko–Pastur 型极限律）。（随机曲面谱集中）
