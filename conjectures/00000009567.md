**English.** Definition: random translation surface by concentration property of random permutation model (random cover ring plane); its Lyapunov spectrum is core of large genus Teichmüller. Conjecture: random permutation model under KZ exponent satisfies O(1/g) neighborhood, of large deviations: λ_1 concentration in 1 and spectrum and variance of Σλ_i is distribution in g -> ∞ of O(1/g); spectrum converges to explicit continuous density (Marchenko–Pastur type limit). (random surface spectrum concentration)
