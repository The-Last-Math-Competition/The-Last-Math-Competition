# 00000008219

**English.** Definition: The invariant enhancement of Tannakian reconstruction: the complete set tan_inv of representation invariants of the reconstruction group of a rigid tensor category. Conjecture: tan_inv (a complete invariant of rigid tensor categories) is the triple of Grothendieck ring plus dimension functor plus weights (a triple completeness law); the minimal rank of examples where the triple is undecidable (non-isomorphic categories with the same triple) is 2 (a rank-two undecidable example); and the construction of undecidable examples (the braiding of bicategories) has braiding group the 8-element dihedral group D_4 (a D4 braiding example). (Tannakian triple rank-two D4 braiding)

**中文。** 定义：Tannakian 重建的指标增强：刚性张量范畴的重建群的表示不变量的完全组 tan_inv。猜想：tan_inv（刚性张量范畴的完全不变量）为 Grothendieck 环 + 维数函子 + 权重的三重组（三重完全律）；且三重组的不可判定（范畴不同构但三重相同）的例子的最小秩为 2（秩二不可判例）；不可判例的构建（双范畴的扭结）的扭结群为 8 阶二面体群 D₄（D4 扭结例）。
