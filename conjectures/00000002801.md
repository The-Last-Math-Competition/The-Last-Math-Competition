# 00000002801

**LD-01 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: The large deviation principle (LDP): a rate function I controls the exponential decay of tail probabilities. Conjecture: The optimal lower bound for the rate function of the LDP of empirical measures of Markov chains: for irreducible chains the zero of I is unique, and the Hessian of I at the zero is a constant multiple of the Fisher information; the constant is the reciprocal of the spectral gap. (chain empirical-measure rate Hessian)

**中文。** 定义：大偏差原理（LDP）：速率函数 I 控制尾概率的指数衰减。猜想：马尔可夫链的经验测度的 LDP 的速率函数的最优下界：不可约链的 I 的零点唯一且 I 的 Hessian 在零点为 Fisher 信息的常数倍；常数为谱隙的倒数。（链经验测度速率 Hessian）
