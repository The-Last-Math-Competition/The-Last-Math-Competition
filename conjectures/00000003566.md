# 00000003566

**English.** Definition: Hilbert geometry: hyperbolic-type metrics inside convex bodies. Conjecture: The rigidity of Hilbert metrics: Hilbert geometry is hyperbolic if and only if the body is an ellipsoid, with the proof of rigidity the quadratic integrability of the geodesic flow. (Hilbert ellipsoid geodesic quadratic integrability rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert 几何：凸体内部的双曲型度量。猜想：Hilbert 度量的刚性：Hilbert 几何为双曲当且仅当体为椭球且刚性的证明为测地流的二次可积。（Hilbert 椭球测地二次可积刚性）
