# 00000008342

**English.** Definition: The height distribution of integral points on quadrics: the height distribution sph_h of integral points on the sphere x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = m. Conjecture: The asymptotics of sph_h (maximal coordinate of sphere points) is the one-third sqrt(m/3) (a one-third law); recurrence (existence of points with maximal coordinate at least c sqrt m) holds with c = 1/2 (a half-route recurrence law); the density of the proof threshold of recurrence (the exceptional set of m) is nearly full, 1 - delta (a nearly full density law); and the equidistribution error of integral points on the sphere (spherical harmonic decay) has the Linnik exponent 1/2 + theta (a Linnik exponent law). (sphere one-third Linnik equidistribution error)

**中文。** 定义：二次曲面的整点高度分布：球面 x²+y²+z²=m 的整点的高度的分布 sph_h。猜想：sph_h（球面整点的最大坐标）的渐近为 √(m/3) 的三分之一（三分之一律）的常返（存在最大坐标≥c·√m 的整点）的 c=1/2 的半程（半程常返律）；且常返的证明阈（m 的例外集）的密度为 1-δ 的近全（近全密度律）；整点的分布（球面的等分布）的误差（球谐衰减）的指数为 1/2+θ 的 Linnik 型（Linnik 指数律）。
