# 00000007032

**English.** Definition: Erdos-Szemeredi with the sum-product conjecture. Conjecture: The quantitative version of the conjecture holds with the exponent approaching one; and exponent one is attained by the Szemeredi-Trotter limit of point families of convex-body configurations. (near-one quantitative Erdos-Szemeredi exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Erdős–Szemerédi：和积猜想。猜想：ES 的定量：猜想的定量版本且本的指数接近一。(Erdos-Szemeredi 和积猜想定量接近指数版本定量和积一)；且指数一由凸体配置点族的 Szemerédi-Trotter 极限达到。
