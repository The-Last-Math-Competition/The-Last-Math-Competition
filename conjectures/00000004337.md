# 00000004337

**FP2-07 ★** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: Reflection positivity is the positivity of correlations under reflection about a hyperplane. Conjecture: The transfer of reflection positivity for lattice systems always requires coupling at or above an explicit threshold; below the threshold the transfer fails in dimension at least three. (coupling threshold of reflection positivity)

**中文。** 定义：反射正性指测度关于超平面反射的关联正性。猜想：格点系的反射正性传递恒需耦合不低于显式阈值；且低于阈值时传递在维数不低于三时失败。（反射正性耦合阈值）
