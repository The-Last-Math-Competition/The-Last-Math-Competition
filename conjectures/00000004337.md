**FP2-07 ★** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: reflection positive denotes positive of measure with respect to hyperplane reflection. Conjecture: reflection positive of lattice point system always requires coupling at least explicit threshold; and low in threshold in dimension at least three. (reflection positive coupling threshold)

**中文。** 定义：反射正性指测度关于超平面反射的关联正性。猜想：格点系的反射正性传递恒需耦合不低于显式阈值；且低于阈值时传递在维数不低于三时失败。（反射正性耦合阈值）
