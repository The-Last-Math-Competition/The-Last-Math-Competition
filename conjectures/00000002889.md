**English.** Definition: Applications of the index in mathematical physics: the index characterization of anomalies. Conjecture: The index density of gauge anomalies: the Schwinger term of the anomaly is the descent of the Dirac index density, and the n-th term of the descent is the n-th characteristic class of the family index. (anomaly descent index density)

**中文。** 定义：指标的在数学物理的应用：反常的指标刻画。猜想：规范反常的指标密度：反常的 Schwinger 项为 Dirac 指标密度的 descent 且 descent 的第 n 项为族指标的 n 阶示性类。（反常 descent 指标密度）
