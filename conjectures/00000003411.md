**English.** Definition: The entropy rate of Markov chains: the measure of randomness of stationary processes. Conjecture: The continuity of entropy rates: the entropy of transition probabilities has Lipschitz dependence, with the dependence constant the supremum of the minimal distribution value. (entropy rate Lipschitz minimum constant)
