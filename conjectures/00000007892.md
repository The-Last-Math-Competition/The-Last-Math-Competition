# 00000007892

**HCA-22 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The combinatorialization of secondary operations in the Ravenel spectral sequence: the symbol table Sigma_2 of second-order Adams-Novikov operations. Conjecture: Sigma_2 is realized as the homology of signed tree-like combinatorial objects (the spectral complex of orientable even graphs); its Euler-characteristic generating function is the integral of the logarithmic derivative of the modular form q-expansion Delta(q), with coefficients in the p-localization given by the v_1-periodic Toda kernel of the self-map; this yields a complete combinatorial model of the second Ext group, whose dimension spectrum coincides with the order sequence of the Adams d_2 differential. (Adams-Novikov secondary combinatorial model)

**中文。** 定义：Ravenel 谱序列的 secondary operations 的组合化：Adams–Novikov 的二阶运算的符号表 Σ₂。猜想：Σ₂ 可实现为树状带号组合对象的同调（可定向偶图的谱复形）；其 Euler 特征的生成函数为模形式 q-展开 Δ(q) 的对数导数的整化，系数在 (p)-局部时为 v_1-周期律的 Toda 自同态核；由此给出二阶 Ext 群的完全组合模型，且其维数谱与 Adams d_2 微分的秩序列重合。
