# 00000001321

**CC-21 ★** — source: 29_多复变与复几何.md

**English.** Definition: The growth of the Nevanlinna characteristic of entire functions over unions of excluded hyperplanes in Cⁿ. Conjecture: After excluding 2n+1 hyperplanes in general position, the degeneration of the Picard-type theorem is an explicit exponent (a higher-dimensional refinement of Picard).

**中文。** 定义：整函数的 Nevanlinna 特征在 ℂⁿ 的排除超平面并集上的增长。猜想：排除 2n+1 个一般位置超平面后的 Picard 型定理的退化为显式指数(高维 Picard 精确化)。
