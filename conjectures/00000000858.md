# 00000000858

**English.** Definition: Translating solitons are translating self-similar solutions of the mean curvature flow. Conjecture: Complete translating solitons exist in every dimension with a unique bent foliation; and the only exception to bowl-type uniqueness is the two-dimensional spine surface. (translating soliton foliation uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：平移孤子为平均曲率流的平移自相似解。猜想：完备平移孤子在任意维数存在唯一的弯曲叶状结构；且 bowl 类唯一性的例外为二维脊面。（平移孤子叶状唯一）
