# 00000001749

**English.** Definition: Simultaneous Thue equations are systems F(x,y) = a, G(x,y) = b of two binary homogeneous forms of degree ≥ 3. Conjecture: The number of solutions is at most (deg F)·(deg G); the bound is optimal, attained when the composite root structures of F and G are of cyclotomic type.

**中文。** 定义：联立 Thue 方程指两个次数 ≥3 的二元齐次形式 F,G 的方程组 F(x,y)=a,G(x,y)=b。猜想：解数 ≤ (deg F)·(deg G);界为最优,达到时 F,G 的复合根结构为分圆型的。
