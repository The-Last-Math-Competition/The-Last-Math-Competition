**POS-26 ★** — source: 270_偏序集与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: A free distributive lattice is a lattice of ideals. Conjecture: The automorphism group of the free distributive lattice is the symmetric group; its fixed-point lattice is of partition-symmetric type; and the maximal chain length of the fixed-point lattice is exactly the logarithm of the partition number p(n). (free distributive automorphisms)

**中文。** 定义：自由分配格为理想格。猜想：自由分配格的自同构群为对称群；其不动点格为分拆对称型。(自由分配自同构)；且不动点格的极大链长恰为分拆数 p(n) 的对数。
