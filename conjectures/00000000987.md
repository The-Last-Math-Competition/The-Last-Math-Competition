# 00000000987

**English.** Definition: The k-point correlation of random unitary groups is represented by Weingarten-function integration. Conjecture: The deviation from CUE is explicitly controlled by the decay of the Weingarten constants; and the decay rate is the square of reciprocal factorials. (Weingarten decay rate)

**中文。** 定义：随机酉群的 k 点关联由 Weingarten 函数积分表示。猜想：与 CUE 的偏差由 Weingarten 常数的衰减显式控制；且衰减率为阶乘倒数的平方。（Weingarten 衰减率）
