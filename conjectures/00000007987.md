# 00000007987

**English.** Definition: The distinguishing entropy of codes from a cryptographic viewpoint: the capacity gap g(C) (the difference from channel capacity) of the mutual information I(Y;X) of soft-decision decoding of a code C. Conjecture: g(C) = Theta(1/sqrt n) for random linear codes (a universal law for convergence speed); and the leading coefficient of g(C) for structured codes (algebraic constructions) is inversely proportional to the dual distance d-perp: g(C) d-perp tends to c = 2 log 2 (a universal constant); and the spectrum of g at fixed rate R concentrates at the mean (variance of constant 1/n), the concentration width being explicitly controlled by the second moment of the weight enumerator. (soft-decision mutual information gap law)
