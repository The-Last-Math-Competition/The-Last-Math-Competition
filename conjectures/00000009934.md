# 00000009934

**English.** Definition: Concentration of Betti curves: concentration inequalities for homology counts of random complexes. Conjecture: The total variation of Betti curves satisfies Talagrand-type concentration: the probability of deviation beyond t decays as exp(-c t^2/n); the constant c is given by the degree entropy of the simplex dependency graph. (Betti total variation concentration)

**中文。** 定义：Betti 曲线的集中：随机复形的同调计数的集中不等式。猜想：Betti 曲线的全变差量满足 Talagrand 型集中：偏差超过 t 的概率按 exp(−ct²/n) 衰减；常数 c 由单形依赖图的度数熵给出。（Betti 全变差集中）
