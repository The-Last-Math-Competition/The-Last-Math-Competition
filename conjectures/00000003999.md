# 00000003999

**English.** Definition: The Prover-Delayer game is the two-player game that characterizes resolution lower bounds via answer-tree sizes. Conjecture: The optimal Delayer payoff and the logarithm of the shortest resolution proof length differ by at most the constant 2 on all formulas, and 2 is optimal. (tight Prover-Delayer payoff conversion)

**中文。** 定义：Prover–Delayer 博弈指以回答树尺寸刻画消解下界的双人博弈。猜想：最优 Delayer 收益与最短消解证明长度的对数在一切公式上相差至多常数 2,且 2 最优。（Prover-Delayer 收益紧换算）
