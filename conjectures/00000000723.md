# 00000000723

**PA-23 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: The Artin–Hasse exponential is the integer-coefficient correction of the p-adic exponential. Conjecture: Its values at nonzero algebraic points in the domain of convergence are all transcendental; and the transcendence follows from the spectral analysis of the Dwork operator. (Artin-Hasse transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：Artin–Hasse 指数为 p 进指数的整系数修正。猜想：该指数在收敛域内非零代数点的取值都为超越数；且超越性由 Dwork 算子的谱分析给出。（AH 指数超越性）
