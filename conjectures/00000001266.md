# 00000001266

**CG-66 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The Stanley–Wilf limit L of the permutation class Av(1324) is the limit of its counting growth constant. Conjecture: L exists and equals 11.60 ± 0.05; more precisely, L is the convergent of the continued fraction [11; 1, 1, 2, 1, 1, 4, 2] of an explicit algebraic expression, this continued fraction arising from the main term of an urn-model approximation.

**中文。** 定义：排列类 Av(1324) 的 Stanley–Wilf 极限 L 指其计数增长常数的极限。猜想：L 存在且 L = 11.60±0.05;更精确地,L 为某显式代数式的连分数展开 [11; 1, 1, 2, 1, 1, 4, 2] 的收敛值,该连分数由罐模型近似的主项给出。
