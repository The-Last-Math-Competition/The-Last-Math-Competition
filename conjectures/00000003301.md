**English.** Definition: Heun functions: Fuchsian equations with four singularities. Conjecture: Heun's 192 solutions: the local solution system of the four-singularity equation is the eightfold extension of the twenty-four solutions, with the combination of the extension the permutation-group action on singularities. (Heun 192 solutions singularity permutation extension)
