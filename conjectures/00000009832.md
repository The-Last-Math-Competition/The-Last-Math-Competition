**English.** Definition: The Bellman function of H^1-BMO duality: the optimal constant function of the dual inequality. Conjecture: The dual Bellman function of the H^1 atomic norm and BMO is explicitly exponential; the first three Taylor coefficients at the origin uniquely determine the function and agree with the John-Nirenberg constant chain. (duality Bellman explicit)

**中文。** 定义：H¹–BMO 对偶的 Bellman：对偶不等式的最优常数函数。猜想：H¹ 原子范数与 BMO 的对偶 Bellman 函数为指数型显式；函数在原点的 Taylor 展开的前三项系数唯一确定且与 John–Nirenberg 常数链一致。（对偶 Bellman 显式）
