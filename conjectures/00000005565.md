# 00000005565

**English.** Definition: Chain subgroups are the special chain entries of designated structure, such as Thompson chains. Conjecture: The chain-length conversion is an explicit linear expression of the element count, additive for direct products; the slope is 1 in the two-generated case; and the shortest chain length equals the number of composition factors, the count characterizing solvable groups. (linear additive conversion of chain lengths)

**中文。** 定义：链中子群：指定结构的特殊链项（如 Thompson 链）。猜想：链长换算为元素个数的显式线性式且直积情形可加；两生成情形的斜率为 1；链的最短长度等于合成因子的个数且个数为可解群的特征刻画。（链长线性可加换算）
