**English.** Definition: The Cowen–Douglas class is the operator class of joint eigenvector bundles. Conjecture: The classification for n = 2 is given by frame equivalence; and the complete invariant of the equivalence is the frame characteristic class. (Cowen-Douglas frame equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Cowen–Douglas 类为联合特征向量簇算子类。猜想：n=2 的分类由标架等价给出；且等价的完全不变量为标架示性类。（Cowen-Douglas 标架等价）
