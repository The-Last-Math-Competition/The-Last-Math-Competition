**English.** Definition: The Paley-Wiener class PW_def of Fourier transforms of tamed groups: the decay spectrum dec(f) of Fourier transforms of definable compactly supported functions. Conjecture: dec(f) is definable decay (a definable realization of polynomial or exponential decay); the polynomial decay exponent equals the definable regularity exponent of the boundary of the support (a regularity-decay equivalence); exponential decay holds if and only if f is subanalytic (an intrinsic characterization of the Weierstrass domain); and the decay constant is the distance from the singularities of the analytic continuation to the real axis (a singularity-distance-decay duality), computability of the distance being a quantitative version of the model completeness of (R_an,exp) (the distance is a definable constant). (definable Paley-Wiener subanalytic characterization)

**中文。** 定义：驯化群的傅里叶变换的 Paley-Wiener 类 PW_def：可定义紧支撑函数的 Fourier 变换的衰减谱 dec(f)。猜想：dec(f) 为可定义衰减（多项式或指数衰减的可定义实现）；且多项式衰减的指数 = 支撑边界的可定义正则指数（正则-衰减等价）；指数衰减当且仅当 f 为亚解析（Weierstrass 域的内在刻画）；衰减常数为解析延拓的奇点到实轴的距离（奇距-衰减对偶），距离的可计算性为 (R_an,exp) 的模型完备性的定量版（距离为可定义常数）。
