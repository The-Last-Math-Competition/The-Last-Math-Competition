# 00000001864

**RM-24 △** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Conjecture: The boundary of the droplet (Hele–Shaw domain) of random normal matrix ensembles with potential V = |z|^{2k} − t·log|z| is an algebraic curve of degree at most 2k (droplet algebraicity).

**中文。** 猜想：随机正规矩阵系的 droplet(Hele–Shaw 域)在位势 V = |z|^{2k} − t·log|z| 的边界为代数曲线,次数 ≤ 2k(droplet 代数性)。
