**English.** Definition: The Bennequin type inequality for slice potential: tb + |rot| <= 2g_4 + 1. Conjecture: Equality holds if and only if the knot is strongly quasipositive; and the lattice set of Legendrian lifts of equality knots is a surjective image of a half-plane. (strong quasipositive characterization of slice equality)
