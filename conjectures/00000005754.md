# 00000005754

**CP3-14 ★** — source: 177_代数几何VI_特征p几何.md

**English.** Definition: A valuation is the ordered functional structure of a ring, with the rank of the valuation group. Conjecture: The conversion formula is explicit in the rank of the valuation group, given by abelianization for continuous valuations; rank jumps occur at composite valuations, with jump size 1; and the value group of a continuous valuation is a power of Z, with exponent the rank. (abelianization conversion of valuation-group ranks)

**中文。** 定义：赋值：环的序泛函结构；赋值群的秩。猜想：换算公式为赋值群秩的显式且连续赋值时由交换化（阿贝尔化）给出；秩的跳跃在复合赋值处且跳跃幅度为 1；连续赋值的值群为 Z 的幂且幂指数等于秩。（赋值群秩交换化换算）
