# 00000004697

**English.** Definition: The entropy of an algebraic action is the entropy of the group action driven by integer polynomials. Conjecture: The entropy upper bound is the logarithm of the Mahler measure, equality holds for principal-ideal actions, and the higher-dimensional correction is an explicit mutual information term. (algebraic entropy Mahler identity and correction)
