**English.** Definition: Phantoms with nontrivial objects of zero cohomology. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the cohomology deficit and the deficit of true phantoms is nonzero; zero deficit means semiphantom; the deficit spectrum is 2-torsion; and the orthogonal complement of a phantom is fully exceptional. (semiphantom conversion of phantom cohomology deficits)
