# 00000002872

**English.** Definition: The Weitzenböck mechanism: curvature terms and vanishing theorems for kernels. Conjecture: The quantitative Lichnerowicz-type vanishing: under positive scalar curvature the dimension bound for the kernel is controlled by the minimal eigenvalue of the curvature potential, with control an equality when the potential is constant. (Weitzenbock quantitative vanishing)

**中文。** 定义：Sig 的 Weitzenböck 机制：曲率项与核的消没定理。猜想：Lichnerowicz 型消没的定量版：正数量曲率下核的维数上界由曲率位势的最小特征值控制且控制为等式当位势为常数。（Weitzenbock 定量消没）
