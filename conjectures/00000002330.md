# 00000002330

**English.** Conjecture: The flag condition number of numerical Schubert computation converges to a Gaussian distribution; the variance is the Richardson number R of the Schubert problem (equal to the logarithm of the Littlewood–Richardson coefficient); the variance is σ² = R/n (inversely proportional to the sample size).

**中文。** 猜想：数值 Schubert 计算的旗条件数的高斯分布收敛;方差为 Schubert 问题的 Richardson 数 R(等于 Littlewood-Richardson 系数的对数);方差 σ² = R/n(样本规模反比)。
