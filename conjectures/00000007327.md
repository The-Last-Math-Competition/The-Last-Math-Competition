# 00000007327

**English.** Definition: Matched filtering of tensors with optimal detection. Conjecture: The signal-to-noise gain of detection is the spectral radius of the tensor, and the optimum of the radius is the aligned direction. (aligned-direction matched-filter gain)

**中文。** 定义：张量的匹配滤波：最优检测。猜想：匹配的增益：检测的信噪比增益为张量的谱半径且径的最优为对齐方向。（张量匹配对齐方向谱半径检测信噪比增益最优）
