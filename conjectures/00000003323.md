**English.** Definition: Symbolic powers: higher symbolic powers of ideals. Conjecture: Symbolic expectations: the criterion for coincidence of symbolic and ordinary powers of a graph ideal is the absence of isolated odd cycles, with the exponential upper bound of coincidence the structural genus. (symbolic ordinary power coincidence genus bound)
