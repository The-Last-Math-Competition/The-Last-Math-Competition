# 00000002821

**English.** Definition: Compactness and convexity of LDP level sets: the regularity layer of rate functions. Conjecture: Convex-hull rigidity of level sets: level sets of the rate are compact and convex, with the number of extreme points of the hull an exponential-type finite value of the level; the extreme-point structure degenerates for affine rates. (level set extreme point spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：LDP 水平集的紧性与凸性：速率函数的正则层。猜想：水平集的凸包刚性：速率的水平集为紧凸且凸包的极点数为水平值的指数型有限值；极点结构在仿射速率下退化。（水平集极点谱）
