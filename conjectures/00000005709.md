# 00000005709

**English.** Definition: Extension homology is the homology lift with spectral values. Conjecture: In the binary case it is given by the Whitehead exact sequence, closed, and the kernel of the sequence is the homotopy boundary. (Whitehead exact boundary of extension homology)

**中文。** 定义：延展同调为带谱值的同调提升。猜想：二元时由 Whitehead 正合列给出且封闭；且列的核为同伦边缘。（延展 Whitehead 正合边缘）
