# 00000009945

**English.** Definition: The Zimmer program: cocompactness thresholds of higher-rank lattice actions on compact manifolds (partially proven by Brown-Fisher-Hurtado). Conjecture: The cocompact threshold of lattice actions is exact: actions on manifolds of dimension below the rank of the lattice plus two are trivial, and actions at the threshold dimension are a finite list of homogeneous classification; the tightness of the threshold is explicitly realized by local entropy obstructions. (Zimmer cocompact threshold)

**中文。** 定义：Zimmer 程序：高秩格在紧流形上作用的余紧阈值（Brown–Fisher–Hurtado 部分证明）。猜想：格作用的余紧阈值精确：作用空间维数低于格的秩加二时作用平凡且阈值维数处的作用为齐性分类的有限列表；阈值的紧性由局部熵障碍显式实现。（Zimmer 余紧阈值）
