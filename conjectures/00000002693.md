# 00000002693

**English.** Definition: Zariski geometry (the Zilber program): the abstract geometry of commutative closure operators. Conjecture: Independence of the dimension axioms of Zariski geometry: the three dimension axioms are complete modulo finite quotients of infinite-dimensional examples, and the kernel of the quotient is the preperiodic equivalence class. (Zariski geometry dimension independence)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 几何（Zilber 程序）：交换代数闭包算子的抽象几何。猜想：Zariski 几何的维数公理的独立性：维数函数的三公理在无穷维例子的模有限商下完备且商的核为预周期等价类。（Zariski 几何维数独立）
