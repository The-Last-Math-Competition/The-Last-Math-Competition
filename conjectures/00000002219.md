# 00000002219

**MI-19 ★** — source: 59_交换环与乘法理想论.md

**English.** Definition: A Mori domain satisfies the ACC on ideals. Conjecture: The Mori property of the complete integral closure is preserved under finite characteristic extensions; and the criterion is given explicitly by the ramification index of the conductor. (Mori closure extension criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Mori 域指满足理想 ACC 的整环。猜想：完全整闭包的 Mori 性在有限特征扩张下保持；且判据由导子的分歧指数显式给出。（Mori 闭包扩张判据）
