**English.** Definition: ∞-Tannaka duality: the reconstruction of fiber functors. Conjecture: Rigidity of reconstruction: the automorphism group of the fiber functor of a rigid category is an ∞-groupoid, with π₁ of the groupoid the braid group of automorphisms. (Tannaka braid automorphism group)

**中文。** 定义：∞-Tannaka 对偶：纤维函子的重构。猜想：重构的刚性：刚性范畴的纤维函子的自同构群为 ∞-群胚且群胚的π₁ 为自同构的辫子群。（Tannaka 辫子自同构群）
