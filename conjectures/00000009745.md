# 00000009745

**MAD-15 ★** — source: 310_数学的自动发现.md

**English.** Definition: Scaling laws of neural generation of propositional proofs: power laws between model size and proving ability. Conjecture: The success rate of propositional proof generation follows a power law in parameter count P: error rates decay as P^{-1/4}; the exponent 1/4 is an explicit function of the combinatorial branching degree of proof trees and stable under architectural changes. (proof generation scaling law)

**中文。** 定义：命题证明的神经生成的标度律：模型规模与证明能力的幂律关系。猜想：命题逻辑证明生成的成功率随参数量 P 满足幂律：错误率按 P^{−1/4} 衰减；指数 1/4 由证明树的组合分支度的显式函数给出且对架构变化稳定。（证明生成标度律）
