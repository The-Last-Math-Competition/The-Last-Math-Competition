# 00000008611

**English.** Definition: Posterior contraction of nonparametric Bayes. Conjecture: The posterior contraction rate agrees with the frequentist minimax rate if and only if the prior unit ball is a Sobolev compact embedding; otherwise it is corrected by the sieve level. (posterior contraction consistency)

**中文。** 定义：非参贝叶斯的后验收缩。猜想：后验收缩速率与频率 minimax 一致当且仅当先验单位球为 Sobolev 紧嵌入；否则由 sieve 层数修正。（后验收缩一致性）
