**NCR-15 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Nichols algebras: graded Hopf envelopes of braided vector spaces. Conjecture: The root system classification of finite-dimensional Nichols algebras is closed: the root systems are exactly an explicit list of finite and super types (hyperoctahedral and F4 exceptions), with no new infinite families; the correspondence table exhausts pointed Hopf classification. (Nichols root system closed list)

**中文。** 定义：Nichols 代数：辫化向量空间的分次 Hopf 包络。猜想：有限维 Nichols 代数的根系分类封闭：根系恰为有限型与超有限型（超八面体、F4 型例外）的显式列表且无新无限族；对应表穷尽 pointed Hopf 分类。（Nichols 根系封闭列表）
