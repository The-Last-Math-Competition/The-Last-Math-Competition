**NCR-15 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: graded Hopf envelope of Nichols algebra: braid -ization to quantity space. Conjecture: classification closed: of finite dimension Nichols algebra is exactly finite type and explicit list of finite type (eight plane body, F4 type exception) and without new infinite family; corresponds to table pointed Hopf classification. (Nichols closed list)

**中文。** 定义：Nichols 代数：辫化向量空间的分次 Hopf 包络。猜想：有限维 Nichols 代数的根系分类封闭：根系恰为有限型与超有限型（超八面体、F4 型例外）的显式列表且无新无限族；对应表穷尽 pointed Hopf 分类。（Nichols 根系封闭列表）
