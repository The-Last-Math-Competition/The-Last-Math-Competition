**English.** Definition: The exceptional poles of the symmetric fourth L-function are the exception set of functorial poles. Conjecture: The pole list is completely given by dihedral and tetrahedral type representations, the table is closed with no other exception sources, and the residue of an exceptional pole equals the Archimedean local factor of the representation. (complete table of sym4 exceptional poles)

**中文。** 定义：对称四次 L 的例外极点指函子极点的例外集。猜想：该极点列表完全由二面体与四面体型表示给出,表封闭且无其他例外源。(sym4 例外极点完全表)；且例外极点的留数等于表示的 Archimedes 局部因子。
