**English.** Definition: Random majority is the majority-type quantifier of randomized threshold. Conjecture: Its conversion constant to monotone circuits is an explicit value of the size power, the conversion preserves probability error, and the constant is optimal. (monotone-circuit conversion constant of random majority)
