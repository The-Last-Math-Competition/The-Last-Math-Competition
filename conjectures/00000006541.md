# 00000006541

**English.** Definition: The non-Archimedean index is the non-Archimedean spectral count. Conjecture: The spectral count is given by graph models and the count is finite; the threshold of finiteness is the reproducing kernel; and the threshold of finite count is controlled by the kernel dimension. (graph-model spectral count of the non-Archimedean index)

**中文。** 定义：非阿指标为非阿基米德的谱计数。猜想：谱计数由图模型给出且计数有限；且有限的门槛为再生核；且计数有限的门槛由再生核维数控制。（非阿图模型谱计数）
