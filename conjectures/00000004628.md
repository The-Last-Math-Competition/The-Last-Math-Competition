**English.** Definition: The Frankel property is that any two minimal surfaces must intersect. Conjecture: The Frankel property holds on manifolds of positive Ricci curvature, the proof combines the maximum principle with the second variation, and the first counterexample requires sign-changing Ricci curvature. (Frankel confirmed under positive Ricci)

**中文。** 定义：Frankel 性质指任意两极小曲面必相交。猜想：正 Ricci 曲率流形中 Frankel 性质成立,证明由最大值原理与第二变分联合完成,首例外需 Ricci 变号。（Frankel 正 Ricci 确证）
