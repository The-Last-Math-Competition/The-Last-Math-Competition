# 00000006131

**English.** Definition: A derived scheme is the augmented geometric structure in the spectral sense. Conjecture: At transverse intersections it degenerates to the classical intersection with explicit truncation length, the kernel of the length being the vanishing of torsion groups. (torsion truncation length of derived intersections)

**中文。** 定义：导出概形为谱意义的增广几何结构。猜想：横截交时退化为经典交且截断长度显式；且长度的核为挠群消没。（导出交挠群截断长度）
