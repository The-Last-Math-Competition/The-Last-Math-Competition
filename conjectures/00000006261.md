**EG3-11 ◆** — source: 194_遍历论III_熵公式.md

**English.** Definition: The variational principle equates topological entropy with the supremum of measure entropy. Conjecture: Equilibrium states of affine families exist and are unique, the criterion of uniqueness is strict convexity, and the verification of strict convexity is the linearity of the potential. (strictly convex unique affine equilibrium states)

**中文。** 定义：变分原理为拓扑熵等于测度熵上确界。猜想：仿射族的均衡态存在且唯一；且唯一的判据为严格凸；且严格凸的验证为势函数线性。（仿射均衡态严格凸唯一）
