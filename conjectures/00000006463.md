# 00000006463

**English.** Definition: Minorization with the minimum asymptotics of Gaussian processes, the duality gap. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit contraction of the duality gap and the gap vanishes for self-similar sets; the vanishing rate is the iteration ratio; and the regular part of the gap is of Euler-characteristic type. (self-similar vanishing conversion of duality gaps)

**中文。** 定义：极小化：高斯过程的极小值渐近（对偶间隙）。猜想：换算常数为对偶间隙的显式收缩且自相似集的间隙消没；消没率为迭代比；间隙的正则部分为 Euler 示性数型。（对偶间隙自相似消没换算）
