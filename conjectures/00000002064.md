# 00000002064

**English.** Definition: An accessible category is closed under λ-filtered colimits. Conjecture: The lattice of reflective placements of accessible categories and the limit theories of sketches form a Galois correspondence; the bijection of the correspondence is given by the power adjunction of Isbell duality; the size of the lattice is explicitly computable.

**中文。** 定义：可及范畴指 λ-可滤余极限封闭的范畴。猜想：可及范畴的反射位置的格与 sketch 的极限理论为 Galois 对应;对应的双射由 Isbell 对偶的幂伴随给出;格的大小可显式计算。
