# 00000000397

**DA-97 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: S_π(N) is the alternating sum of partial quotients of π by parity. Conjecture: S_π(N) divided by the total of the first N partial quotients of π tends to one half; and the error of convergence obeys a law of the iterated logarithm. (parity-balanced partial quotients of pi)

**中文。** 定义：连分数的奇偶部分商和 S_π(N)。猜想：S_π(N) 除以 π 的前 N 部分商总和趋于二分之一；且收敛的误差为重对数律。（pi 奇偶部分商对半）
