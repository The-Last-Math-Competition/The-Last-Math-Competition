**English.** Definition: Generator-based divergences are divergence families defined by generating functions. Conjecture: The equivalence-class classification is explicitly indexed by the convex-difference invariant of the generator, stable under monotone reparameterization; the complete invariant set is binary for binary divergences; and two divergences are equivalent if and only if their convex-difference invariants coincide. (convex-difference invariant classification of generated divergences)

**中文。** 定义：函数生成散度：由生成函数定义的散度族。猜想：等价类分类由生成元的凸差不变量显式索引且单调重参数化下稳定；不变量的完全集为二元（二元散度的情形）；两个散度等价当且仅当凸差不变量相同。（生成散度凸差不变量分类）
