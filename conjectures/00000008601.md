# 00000008601

**MLC-11 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Aggregation is combining M estimators with optimal weights. Conjecture: The excess risk of aggregation is (1+o(1)) log M/n with the constant 1 optimal; and the boundary between linear and exponential aggregation is of logarithmic-exponential type in M. (aggregation constant-one optimality)

**中文。** 定义：聚合指组合 M 个估计子的最优权重。猜想：聚合的过剩风险为 (1+o(1))log M/n 且常数 1 最优；线性与指数聚合的分界为 M 的对数指数型。（聚合常数一最优）
