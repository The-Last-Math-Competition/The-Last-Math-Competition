**English.** Definition: Orbits of linear groups over finite fields: the orbit-length spectrum of generators. Conjecture: The divisibility stratification of orbit lengths: orbit lengths of irreducible representations are divisible by the prime-factor spectrum of the matrix order, with tightness of the divisibility chain given by the ratio-field structure of eigenvalues. (orbit length divisibility chain)
