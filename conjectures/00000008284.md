# 00000008284

**English.** Definition: The differentiability of flows of rough differential equations: the index rde_diff of Frechet differentiability of solutions of RDEs with respect to initial values. Conjecture: rde_diff (C^infinity dependence for RDEs) holds if and only if the iterated Lie derivatives of the vector fields are bounded (a bounded iteration criterion); the constant of the estimate of the Jacobian of the flow (exponential growth bound) is the product of the Lipschitz constant of the vector field and the variation of the path (a Lipschitz-variation product law); and the minimal dimension of failure of differentiability (bifurcation-type RDEs) is 3 (a three-dimensional bifurcation RDE example), the Hausdorff dimension of the set of nondifferentiability times being the rough dimension 1/alpha (an alpha rough dimension formula). (RDE Frechet bounded iteration rough dimension)

**中文。** 定义：粗糙微分方程的流的可微性：RDE 的解对初值的 Fréchet 可微性的指标 rde_diff。猜想：rde_diff（RDE 的 C^∞ 依赖）当且仅当向量场的李导数的迭代的有界（有界迭代判据）；且流的雅可比的估计（指数增长界）的常数为向量场的 Lipschitz 与路径的变差的乘积（Lipschitz-变差乘积律）；可微性的失效（分岔型 RDE）的最小维数为 3（三维分岔 RDE 例），例的解的不可微时刻的集合的 Hausdorff 维数为 1/α 的粗糙维数（α-粗糙维数公式）。
