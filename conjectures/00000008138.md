# 00000008138

**English.** Definition: The quotient invariants of homogeneous actions of Hopf algebras on noncommutative varieties: the index hg(A,H) of Hopf-Galois extensions of noncommutative quotients. Conjecture: hg (the invariant of faithful flatness of the Hopf-Galois extension) is minimally generated by the quotient dimension d (a d-minimal generation); the criterion for freeness of the Hopf action (geometricity of the quotient) is the polynomial identity Hilbert(A,z)/d for the Hilbert series of the invariant subring (a Hilbert quotient formula); and the minimal Hopf algebra where the formula fails (non-free action) is the 4-dimensional Sweedler Hopf algebra (a Sweedler minimal example), the 4-dimensional exception being isolated among all hyper-translation invariants (a Sweedler isolation law). (Hopf-Galois Hilbert Sweedler isolation)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的 Hopf 代数的齐次作用的商不变量：非交换商的 Hopf-Galois 扩张的指标 hg(A,H)。猜想：hg（Hopf-Galois 的忠实平坦性的不变量）为商的维数 d 的极小生成（d-极小生成）；且 Hopf 作用的自由性（商的几何性）的判据为不变量子环的 Hilbert 级数 = Hilbert(A,z)/d 的多项式等式（Hilbert 商公式）；公式失效（非自由作用）的最小 Hopf 为 Sweedler 4 维 Hopf（Sweedler 极小例），4 维例外在一切超平移不变量中孤（Sweedler 孤立律）。
