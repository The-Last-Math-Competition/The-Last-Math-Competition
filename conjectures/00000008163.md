# 00000008163

**RNG-23 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The recursive renormalization of conformal blocks: the convergence radius zam_rad of Zamolodchikov's recursion (the shape expansion of conformal blocks). Conjecture: The exact value of zam_rad is e^{pi s/L} (an exponential in the elliptic modulus), s the conformal dimension difference and L the elliptic nome; the asymptotics of the radius (high-temperature limit) is 1 (recovery of disk convergence); and the monodromy of the analytic continuation of the radius is a representation of the braid group (a braid-monodromy duality), whose irreducibility criterion is nonresonance of the dimension differences (a nonresonant irreducibility criterion). (Zamolodchikov radius braid monodromy)

**中文。** 定义：共形块的递归重整化：Zamolodchikov 的递归（共形块的形状展开）的收敛半径 zam_rad。猜想：zam_rad 的精确值为 e^{π·s/L}（椭圆模数的指数），s 为共形维数差，L 为椭圆名；且半径的渐近（高温度极限）为 1（圆盘收敛恢复）；半径的解析延拓的 monodromy 的表示为 braid 群的表示（braid-monodromy 对偶），表示的不可约性判据为维数差的非共振（非共振不可约判据）。
