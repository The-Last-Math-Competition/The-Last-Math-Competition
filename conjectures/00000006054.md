**English.** Definition: Game semantics is the game-interpretation framework of strategies. Conjecture: The category of innocent strategies on dialogue trees is a fibration, the kernel of the fiber being history equivalence; innocence is decided by the totality of winning responses; and morphisms between fibers preserve response functions, the uniqueness of preservation given by the history function. (history-equivalent fibration of game dialogues)
