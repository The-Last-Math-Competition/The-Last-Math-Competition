**English.** Definition: Fiber entropy is the conditional-entropy structure of factor fibers. Conjecture: Under the additive law of relative entropy the chain length is finite, the realization of the constant being quotient maps, and the realization of the quotient maps being natural-projection towers. (finite quotient-map chains of fiber additivity)

**中文。** 定义：纤维熵为因子纤维的条件熵结构。猜想：相对熵的加性律下链长有限；且常数的实现为商映射；且商映射的实现为自然投影塔。（纤维加性商映射链有限）
