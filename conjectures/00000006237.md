**English.** Definition: A closed subtopos is the substructure of closed immersions. Conjecture: The coclosure is of idempotent type and commutes with open complements, the combination of the commutation being De Morgan's law. (De Morgan coclosure of closed complements)

**中文。** 定义：闭子拓扑斯为闭浸入的子结构。猜想：余闭包的幂等型给出且与开补交换；且交换的组合为德摩根律。（余闭幂等德摩根开补交换）
