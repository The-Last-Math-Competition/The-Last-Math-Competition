# 00000002689

**English.** Definition: Equidistribution heights: the relation between naive heights of point sequences and equidistribution measures. Conjecture: Asymptotics of orbit height sums: the limit of the mean height of orbits outside the preperiodic set is a linear combination of canonical heights with coefficients the Fourier coefficients of the orbit-frequency measure; the convergence rate of the mean is 1/log n. (orbit height mean asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：等分布高度：点列的 naive 高度与等分布测度的关系。猜想：轨道高度和的渐近：预周期外轨道的高度均值的极限为 canonical 高度的线性组合且组合系数为轨道频率测度的 Fourier 系数；均值的收敛速率为 log n 的倒数。（轨道高度均值渐近）
