# 00000004816

**English.** Definition: The prime number theorem of the Selberg class is the main-term asymptotic of partial sums of coefficients and the error exponent. Conjecture: The optimal error exponent is a linear function of the degree d (for d = 2 it is the one-half power of x times a logarithmic correction); the optimality of the exponent is equivalent to the zero-free strip hypothesis; under GRH the error is of type x^{1/2} log^2 x with explicit coefficient. (error exponent of the Selberg prime number theorem)

**中文。** 定义：Selberg 类的素数定理：系数部分和的主项渐近与误差项指数。猜想：误差项的最优指数为度 d 的线性函数（d=2 时为 x 的二分之一幂乘对数修正）；指数的最优性与零点自由带假设等价；广义 RH 下误差为 x^{1/2}·log²x 型且系数显式。（Selberg 素数定理误差指数）
