**English.** Definition: main term asymptotic of prime number theorem: coefficient part and of Selberg class and error term exponent. Conjecture: optimal exponent of error term is linear function (d=2 when of d is optimal of two one power times logarithm positive); exponent of x and zero self- by hypothesis equivalent; RH under error is x^{1/2} · log²x type and coefficient explicit. (Selberg prime number theorem error exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Selberg 类的素数定理：系数部分和的主项渐近与误差项指数。猜想：误差项的最优指数为度 d 的线性函数（d=2 时为 x 的二分之一幂乘对数修正）；指数的最优性与零点自由带假设等价；广义 RH 下误差为 x^{1/2}·log²x 型且系数显式。（Selberg 素数定理误差指数）
