# 00000004731

**English.** Definition: A gerbe is a second-order stack structure over the group of roots of unity. Conjecture: The classification of third-root gerbes is given explicitly by cohomology representatives together with ribbon automorphism pairs, and the classification is complete up to equivalence of twisted sheaf categories. (representative classification of third-root gerbes)

**中文。** 定义：gerbe 指单位根群的二阶叠结构。猜想：三次单位根 gerbe 的分类由上同调代表与带状自同构对显式给出,分类在扭曲层范畴等价意义下完全。（三次根 gerbe 代表分类）
