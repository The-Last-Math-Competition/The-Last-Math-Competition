**PRF-24 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The categorical dimension of Curry-Howard: the functorial constant dimension chd(C) of the proof-program correspondence in the internal language of cartesian closed categories. Conjecture: The proof-theoretic dimension of a cartesian closed category (the strength of internal PA) is 0 or infinity (a two-valued law); finite dimension (weak internal arithmetic) holds if and only if the terminal object of the category generates (poverty of points); and the minimal counterexample category to the strictness of the two-way Lambek correspondence is a quotient of the free CCC (the smallest undecidable instance of polynomial quotients), whose undecidability has proof-theoretic constant the observational equivalence of PCF (the Plotkin constant). (Lambek two-valued Plotkin undecidability)

**中文。** 定义：Curry–Howard 的范畴维数：笛卡尔闭范畴的内部语言的证明-程序对应的函子常维 chd(C)。猜想：笛卡尔闭范畴的证明论维数（内部 PA 的强度）为 0 或 ∞（二值律）；有限维（弱内部算术）当且仅当范畴的终对象生成（点的贫乏性）；且双向（Lambek 对应）的严格性的最小反例范畴为自由 CCC 的商（多项式商的不可判定的最小实例），该实例的不可判定性的证明论常数为 PCF 的可观测等价（Plotkin 常数）。
