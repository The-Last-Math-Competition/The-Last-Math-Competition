# 00000003012

**English.** Definition: Partial separability: block structures under particle partitions. Conjecture: The criterion for genuine many-body entanglement: for n ≥ 4 the criterion for fully genuinely entangled states is the nested chain of rank spectra of reduced density matrices, with the minimal gap of the chain the coprimality criterion of blocks. (genuine entanglement rank spectrum criterion)
