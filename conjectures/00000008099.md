# 00000008099

**English.** Definition: The depth spectrum of fusion-system ranks: the distribution d_spec(F) of the depth (the depth of characteristic-zero blocks) of P-fusion. Conjecture: d_spec(F) is contained in {0,1,...,log_p |S|} (an integer spectrum rigidity); the count of depth-0 blocks (nilpotent blocks) is at most 1 (a unique nilpotence law); and the average depth (the mean of the spectrum) is at least log_p |S| /2 (an average depth lower bound), tight for the trivial fusion on abelian S (an abelian-trivial tight mean). (block depth unique nilpotent average bound)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的秩的深度谱：P-融合的 depth（特征零的块的深度）的分布 d_spec(F)。猜想：d_spec(F) ⊆ {0,1,…,log_p |S|}（整数谱刚性）；且 depth 0 的块（nilpotent 块）的计数 ≤ 1（唯一幂零律）；depth 的平均（谱的平均值）≥ log_p |S| /2（平均深度下界），下界的紧性由交换 S 的平凡融合取得（交换-平凡平均紧）。
