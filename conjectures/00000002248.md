**English.** Definition: Carleman interpolation is weighted entire-function approximation. Conjecture: The threshold for the minimal measure decay of the complement of Carleman sets on R is explicit at e^{−|x|^{2+ε}} (the Carleman threshold).

**中文。** 定义：Carleman 插值(带权整函数逼近)。猜想：ℝ 上的 Carleman 集的补的最小测度衰减率为 e^{−|x|^{2+ε}} 的阈值显式(Carleman 阈值)。
