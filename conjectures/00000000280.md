# 00000000280

**English.** Definition: Feigenbaum's constant δ ≈ 4.6692 is the universal limiting ratio of successive period-doubling bifurcation parameters, and α ≈ 2.5029 is the scaling factor; both are spectral data of the fixed-point equation of the renormalization operator for the quadratic family. Conjecture: δ and α are both transcendental and algebraically independent; the transcendence is controlled by the isolated spectral gap of the linearization of the renormalization operator, whose convergence rate yields an effective irrationality-measure lower bound for δ. (Feigenbaum transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：Feigenbaum 常数 δ≈4.6692 为倍周期分叉参数间距的普适极限比，α≈2.5029 为标度因子；两者为二次映射族重整化算子的不动点方程的谱数据。猜想：δ 与 α 均为超越数且二者代数无关；超越性由重整化算子的线性化的孤立谱间隙控制，谱间隙的收敛速率给出 δ 的有效无理性度量下界。（Feigenbaum 超越性）
