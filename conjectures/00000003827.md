**RC-07 ◆** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: The perfection of a Kirillov–Reshetikhin crystal B^{r,s} means its level-0 closure forms a perfect crystal. Conjecture: B^{r,s} is perfect if and only if gcd(r,s) = 1 or r labels an end node of the affine Dynkin diagram. (KR perfection criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Kirillov–Reshetikhin 晶体 B^{r,s} 的完美性指其水平 0 闭包构成完美晶体。猜想：B^{r,s} 完美当且仅当 gcd(r,s)=1 或 r 为仿射 Dynkin 图端点的标号。（KR 完美性判据）
