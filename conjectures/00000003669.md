**SG-29 ★** — source: 107_随机几何.md

**English.** Definition: Stochastic counting: the cover times of random coverings. Conjecture: The concentration of cover times: the cover time of random walks on graphs concentrates at an explicit constant, with the constant the harmonic mean of hitting times. (cover time harmonic mean hitting constant concentration)

**中文。** 定义：Stochastic 计数：随机覆盖的覆盖时间。猜想：覆盖时间的集中：图上随机游走的覆盖时间集中于显式常数且常数为 hitting 的调和平均。（覆盖时间调和平均 hitting 常数集中）
