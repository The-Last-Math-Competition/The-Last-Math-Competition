**English.** Definition: Auslander–Reiten (AR) triangles and almost-split sequences. Conjecture: Realizability of finite subgraphs of AR quivers of Artin algebras: every locally finite undirected graph arises as a connected component of the stable AR quiver of some algebra (a direction toward the realization conjecture).
