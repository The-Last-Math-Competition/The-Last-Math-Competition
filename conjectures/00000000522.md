# 00000000522

**CA-22 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The ordered semigroup of purity sequences is the degree-sequence structure of Boij–Söderberg decompositions. Conjecture: The semigroup fails unique factorization if and only if there exists a strictly increasing chain of length at least three; and the chain criterion is decidable in combinatorial polynomial time. (degree-sequence semigroup non-unique factorization criterion)

**中文。** 定义：纯度序列的偏序半群为 Boij–Söderberg 分解的度序列结构。猜想：该半群无唯一因子分解当且仅当存在长度至少三的严格递增链；且链的判定为组合多项式时间。（度序列半群非唯一分解判定）
