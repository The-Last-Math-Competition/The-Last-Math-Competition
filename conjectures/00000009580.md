# 00000009580

**English.** Definition: The Fenchel-Nielsen twist flow is a Hamiltonian flow on modular surfaces, with twist coordinates conjugate to length coordinates; the structure of its orbit closures is unknown. Conjecture: The joint spectral measure of twist flows is Lebesgue-type uniquely ergodic, and every orbit is either dense in the interior of the WP completion or lies on a finite periodic orbit; and the analytic embedding of twist tori is completely decided by the length convexity criterion. (twist flow unique ergodicity)

**中文。** 定义：Fenchel–Nielsen twist 流为模曲面上的哈密顿流，twist 坐标共轭于长度坐标；其轨道闭包结构未知。猜想：twist 流的联合谱测度为 Lebesgue 型唯一遍历，且每条轨道在 WP 完全化的内部稠密或落于有限周期轨；twist 环面的解析嵌入由长度凸性判据完全决定。（twist 流唯一遍历）
