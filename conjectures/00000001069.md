# 00000001069

**English.** Conjecture: The critical number of a multiplicative subgroup H ≤ F_q^* (guaranteeing intersection with H by an integer-sum condition) is explicit in q/|H| (the subgroup critical number).

**中文。** 猜想：乘法子群 H ≤ F_q^* 的 critical number(保证与 H 相交的整和条件)为 q/|H| 的显式(子群 critical number)。
