# 00000005775

**English.** Definition: The intermediate connection is the nonflat-end connection. Conjecture: Its curvature modulus is an explicit multiple of one minus the squared parameter, the multiple given by the zero-order three-tensor, and the formula degenerates to zero at both ends. (one-minus-square law of intermediate curvature)

**中文。** 定义：中间联络指非平坦端的联络。猜想：其曲率模为一减参数平方的显式倍,倍由零阶三张量给出且公式两端退化零。（中间联络曲率一减平方律）
