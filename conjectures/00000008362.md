# 00000008362

**English.** Definition: The barycenter of Lagrangians in tropical mirror symmetry via SYZ: the constant leg_dual of the tropicalization of the focus-barycenter duality (Legendre duality). Conjecture: leg_dual (the constant conversion of Legendre duality) is the 2:1 conversion (a two-one conversion law); the count of duality fixed points (self-dual polytopes) is at most 2 (a double fixed point law); the minimal self-dual polytope is the simplex (a simplex minimality law); and the deviation of double duality from the identity is a translation (a translation deviation law), with the spectrum of translation moduli on the integer lattice (an integer lattice spectrum law). (tropical Legendre simplex self-dual translation)

**中文。** 定义：热带镜像对称的 SYZ 的拉格朗日的重心：焦点-重心对偶（Legendre 对偶）的热带化的常 leg_dual。猜想：leg_dual（Legendre 对偶的常数的换算）为 2:1 的换算（二一换算律）；且对偶的不动点（自对偶多面体）的计数 ≤ 2（双不动点律）；自对偶的极小多面体为单形（单形极小律）；对偶的合成（双重对偶）的恒等的偏差为平移（平移偏差律），平移的模数的谱为整数格点（整格点谱律）。
