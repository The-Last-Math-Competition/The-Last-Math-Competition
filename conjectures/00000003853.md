# 00000003853

**English.** Definition: The spiked β-Laguerre model denotes the β-gas of sample covariances with rank-one drift. Conjecture: At the critical drift, the right tail exponent of the limiting distribution of the largest eigenvalue remains 3/2 while the left tail exponent is 3, and the product of the two is independent of drift for all β > 0. (spiked critical double tail exponent)

**中文。** 定义：spiked β-Laguerre 模型指带秩一漂移的样本协方差 β-气。猜想：临界漂移处最大特征值的极限分布右尾指数仍为 3/2,但左尾指数为 3,两者之积在一切 β>0 上与漂移无关。（spiked 临界双尾指数）
