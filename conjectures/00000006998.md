# 00000006998

**English.** Definition: Lemmas with the relative deficit of Stein-type lemmas. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the relative deficit and conditional complexity gives the window; the endpoint of the window is the entropy of the difference set; and the recursion of the deficit is logarithmic. (conditional-complexity relative deficit conversion)

**中文。** 定义：引理：Stein 型引理的相对亏格。猜想：换算常数为相对亏格的显式且条件复杂度给出窗；窗的端点为差集的熵；亏格的递推为对数型。（相对亏格条件复杂换算常数）
