# 00000009692

**TAG-22 ★** — source: 308_算术超越.md

**English.** Definition: U_2 numbers are Mahler U numbers whose approximation deviation is exactly quadratic; their explicit existence is open. Conjecture: There exist U_2 numbers constructed by explicit series; the measure functions of all U_2 numbers are uniform, explicit functions of Liang-Mahler polynomials, and the construction is closed under iterated exponential towers. (U_2 number explicit construction)

**中文。** 定义：U_2 数为逼近偏差恰为二次的 Mahler U 数；其显式存在性开放。猜想：存在显式级数构造的 U_2 数；一切 U_2 数的测度函数一致且为 Liang–Mahler 多项式的显式函数，构造在迭代指数塔下封闭。（U_2 数显式构造）
