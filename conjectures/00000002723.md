# 00000002723

**English.** Definition: Rational polyhedrality of spheres: simplicial spheres and projection polytopes. Conjecture: The criterion for a simplicial sphere to be combinatorially equivalent to the boundary of a rational polytope: the criterion is the rational CM type of the face ring, decidable for vertex counts at most d+13. (sphere rational polytope criterion)

**中文。** 定义：球面的有理多面体性：单纯球面与投影多面体。猜想：单纯球面组合等价于有理多面体边界的判据：判据为面环的有理 CM 型且判据在顶点数不超过 d+13 可判定。（球面有理多面体判据）
