**PHT-05 ★** — source: 316_持久同调II_统计与稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: Computational complexity of persistent homology: pairing algorithms on filtered boundary matrices. Conjecture: The complexity of persistent homology is equivalent to matrix multiplication complexity: every pairing algorithm requires Omega(n^omega) operations and the standard algorithm already attains it; the equivalence follows from reductions on block structures of filtrations. (persistence matrix-multiplication equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：持久同调计算复杂度：过滤边界矩阵的配对算法。猜想：持久同调的计算复杂度与矩阵乘法复杂度等价：任何配对算法需 Ω(n^ω) 次运算且标准算法已达；等价性由过滤的块结构的归约给出。（持久计算矩阵乘等价）
