# 00000003985

**English.** Definition: IPS is the algebraic proof system whose certificates are ideal-membership polynomials. Conjecture: IPS lower bounds can be decoded as the algebraic immunity degree of polynomial maps; the decoding is lossless within degree (log n)^2, while beyond that threshold the distortion is exponential. (IPS algebraic immunity decoding threshold)

**中文。** 定义：IPS 指以理想隶属多项式为证书的代数证明系统。猜想：IPS 下界可译码为多项式映射的代数免疫度,译码在度 (log n)² 阈内无损,超阈失真为指数级。（IPS 代数免疫译码阈值）
