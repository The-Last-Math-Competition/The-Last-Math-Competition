**English.** Definition: The spectra of solvable operators with spectral solvability. Conjecture: The solvable structure of spectra holds, and the decomposition of the structure is the direct sum of root subspaces. (root-summed solvable spectral decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：可解算子的谱：谱的可解性。猜想：可解谱：算子的谱的可解结构且构的分解为根子空间直和。（可解算子根子空间直和谱可解分解结构谱）
