# 00000008511

**SDG-11 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: The monodromy of a parametrized differential equation is the analytic continuation of solutions along the parameter space. Conjecture: The dimension of the Zariski closure of the parameter monodromy group is maximal in the generic case; and the conservation law of the discretization (specialization) of parameter monodromy groups is lower semicontinuity of the dimension. (parameter monodromy dimension continuity)

**中文。** 定义：参数微分方程的单值指解沿参数空间的解析延拓。猜想：参数单群的 Zariski 闭包的维数在一般情形为最大；且参数单群的离散化（特殊化）的守恒律为维数的下半连续。（参数单群维数连续性）
