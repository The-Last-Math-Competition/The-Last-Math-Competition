# 00000002838

**English.** Definition: Algebraic solutions of moment problems: the semialgebraic structure of moments of quasi-invariant measures. Conjecture: Real triangular decompositions: a semialgebraic characterization of multivariate moment orders exists, whose boundary polynomials are the realizations of Cayley–Bacharach type Chow forms. (moment order semialgebraic boundary)

**中文。** 定义：矩问题的代数解：拟不变测度的矩的半代数结构。猜想：实三角分解：多元矩序的半代数刻画存在且刻画的边界多项式为 Cayley–Bacharach 型 Chow 形式的实化。（矩序半代数边界）
