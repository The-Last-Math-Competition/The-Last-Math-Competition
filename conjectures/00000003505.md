**English.** Definition: Bott–Borel–Weil: the cohomology of bundles on flag manifolds. Conjecture: The vanishing of BBW: the vanishing of cohomology is the displacement of the weight chamber, with the realization of displacement the minimal representatives of the Weyl group. (BBW weight chamber displacement minimal representative vanishing)
