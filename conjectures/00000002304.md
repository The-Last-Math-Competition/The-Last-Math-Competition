# 00000002304

**FR-14 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: The k(GV) conjecture states that semidirect products with a linear action V satisfy k(GV) ≤ k(G)k(V). Conjecture: The classification of equality under general linear actions: exactly when V is faithful irreducible and G is of fixed-point-free type (a complete list of equality classes); otherwise strict inequality; and the list of equality classes is closed by the degree expansion of characters of the fixed-point-free type.

**中文。** 定义：k(GV) 猜想指线性作用 V 上的半直积满足 k(GV)≤k(G)k(V)。猜想：一般线性作用下的等号分类:恰为 V 为忠实不可约且 G 无不动点型(等号类的完全列表);其余严格不等；且等号类的列表由无不动点型的特征标次数展开闭合。
