# 00000004730

**English.** Definition: The order and the Clifford invariant are the two layers of data of a Brauer class. Conjecture: There exist two Brauer classes with the same order but different Clifford invariants, and the separation is realized by an explicit quadratic twist of the even Clifford group. (same order-Clifford separation by twists)

**中文。** 定义：阶与 Clifford 不变量指 Brauer 类的两层数据。猜想：存在两个 Brauer 类的阶相同而 Clifford 不变量不同,分离由偶 Clifford 群的显式二次扭曲实现。（阶同 Clifford 分离扭曲）
