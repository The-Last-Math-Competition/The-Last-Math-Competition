**KK2-07 ★** — source: 131_代数K理论II_迹方法.md

**English.** Definition: The crystalline comparison is the categorical comparison functor between THH and prismatic cohomology. Conjecture: The deficit spectrum of the comparison is an explicit shift of A divided by p, the deficit is zero if and only if the variety is smooth, and the criterion is complete. (smoothness criterion of the crystalline deficit spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：晶态比较指 THH 与棱晶上同调间的范畴比较函子。猜想：比较的亏损谱为 A 除 p 的显式移位,亏损为零当且仅当簇光滑,判据完备。（晶态亏损谱光滑判据）
