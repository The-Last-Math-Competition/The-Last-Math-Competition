**PA-17 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: The Volkenborn integral ∫₀^{p−1} f(x) dx_p (a translation-invariant functional). Conjecture: The Hurwitz correction of ∫ C(x, k) dx_p = (−1)^k/k has no deviation of order p³ when k is prime (prime-indexed Volkenborn).

**中文。** 定义：Volkenborn 积分 ∫₀^{p−1} f(x) dx_p(平移不变泛函)。猜想：∫ C(x, k) dx_p = (−1)^k/k 的 Hurwitz 修正在 k 素时无 p³ 级别的偏差(素指标 Volkenborn)。
