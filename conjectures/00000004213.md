**English.** Definition: A spanning hypersurface is a higher-dimensional tree of a torus triangulation in the dual sense. Conjecture: The count of 2-dimensional spanning hypersurfaces of a torus triangulation equals the factorial of the order of the higher torsion subgroup times the determinant of the Laplacian pseudoinverse, in strict parallel with the matrix-tree theorem for graphs. (hypersurface count and pseudoinverse determinant)

**中文。** 定义：生成超曲面指对偶意义下环面剖分的高维树。猜想：环面剖分的 2-生成超曲面计数等于高维挠子群阶乘乘拉普拉斯伪逆行列式,与图情形的矩阵树公式严格平行。（超曲面计数-伪逆行列式）
