**English.** Definition: Fixed-point semantics: solutions of recursive programs. Conjecture: The approximation of least fixed points: the length of approximation sequences is the order height, with the minimality of height the unfolding depth of loops. (fixed point approximation loop unfolding depth minimal)

**中文。** 定义：不动点语义：递归程序的解。猜想：最小不动点的逼近：逼近序列的长度为序高度且高度的极小为循环的展开深度。（不动点逼近循环展开深度极小）
