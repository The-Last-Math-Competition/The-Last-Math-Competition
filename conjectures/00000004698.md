# 00000004698

**EC4-08 ★** — source: 142_遍历论的组合_熵与计数.md

**English.** Definition: A homogeneous action is the flow of a diagonal group on a homogeneous space. Conjecture: The entropy of a two-parameter diagonal action is an explicit product of the diagonal entries, and the product agrees with the Weyl-vector function of the root system with zero error. (Weyl product formula of diagonal entropies)

**中文。** 定义：齐性作用指对角群在齐性空间的流。猜想：二参数对角作用的熵为对角元素的显式积,积与根系的 Weyl 向量函数一致且误差为零。（齐性对角熵 Weyl 积公式）
