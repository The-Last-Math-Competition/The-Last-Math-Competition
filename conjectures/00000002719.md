# 00000002719

**SC-09 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Double suspension of combinatorial manifolds and topological obstructions. Conjecture: The combinatorial count of sphericity of double suspensions (the Edwards–Cannon theorem): the minimal number of exchanges in shellifying the suspended complex is the maximum of the Betti numbers of the original manifold. (double suspension shellification exchange count)

**中文。** 定义：组合流形的双悬挂（double suspension）与拓扑障碍。猜想：双悬浮的球面性（Edwards–Cannon 定理）的组合计数：悬浮复形的 shell 化的最小交换次数为原始流形的 Betti 数的极大值。（双悬浮壳化交换数）
