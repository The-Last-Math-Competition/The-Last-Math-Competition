# 00000008290

**English.** Definition: The Wasserstein robustness of model-free filtering: the worst-case filtering error wass_fil under distributional uncertainty (Wasserstein balls). Conjecture: wass_fil (the worst error within a Wasserstein ball) is linear, radius epsilon times the observation Lipschitz constant (a linear worst-case law); the worst distributions (extremes on the boundary of the ball) are two-point-supported adversarial perturbations (a two-point adversarial law); the explicit solution of robust filtering (minimax estimation) is the Huberized observation update (a Huber update law); and the explicit Huber truncation threshold is the product epsilon sigma (an epsilon-sigma product threshold). (Wasserstein worst-case Huber two-point adversarial)

**中文。** 定义：无模型滤波的 Wasserstein 鲁棒：分布不确定（Wasserstein 球）下的最坏滤波误差 wass_fil。猜想：wass_fil（Wasserstein 球内的最坏误差）= 半径 ε·观测 Lipschitz 的线性（线性最坏律）；且最坏分布（球的边界上的极值）为两点支撑的对抗扰动（两点对抗律）；鲁棒滤波（minimax 估计）的显式解为 Huber 化的观测更新（Huber 更新律）；Huber 的截断阈的显式为 ε·σ 的乘积（εσ 乘积阈）。
