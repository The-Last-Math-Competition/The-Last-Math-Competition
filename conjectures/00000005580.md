**LP2-20 △** — source: 171_有限群的局部分析与融合.md

**English.** Definition: Strong embedding and global structure are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two groups with isomorphic strongly embedded subgroups but different global structures, and the separation is realized by a field-twist pair of rank-one Lie types. (same strong embedding-different global field-twist separation)

**中文。** 定义：强嵌入与整体结构指两层。猜想：存在两群的强嵌入子群同构而整体结构不同,分离由秩一李型的域扭转对实现。（强嵌入同-整体异域扭分离）
