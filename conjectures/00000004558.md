**English.** Definition: A binomial-coefficient exponential sum is a finite weighted sum of coefficients and algebraic powers. Conjecture: Its transcendence first instance is the value of the weighted-sum function at an irrational algebraic base, the value is transcendental, and the proof is achieved by Mahler's method at finite-order truncation. (first Mahler instance of binomial exponential sums)

**中文。** 定义：二项式系数指数和指系数与代数幂的有限加权和。猜想：其超越首例为无理代数底在权和函数处的值,值超越且证明由 Mahler 方法在有限阶截断达到。（二项指数和 Mahler 首例）
