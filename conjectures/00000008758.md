**English.** Definition: Rognes' spectralization tower. Conjecture: The spectralization constant of each layer of the tower always satisfies that the ratio of adjacent layers is nonincreasing; and the equality case is the Adams sum of the sphere spectrum. (Rognes tower nonincreasing ratio)

**中文。** 定义：Rognes 的谱化塔。猜想：塔的每一层的谱化常数恒满足相邻层的比不增；且不增性的等号为球谱的 Adams 和。（Rognes 谱化塔不增比）
