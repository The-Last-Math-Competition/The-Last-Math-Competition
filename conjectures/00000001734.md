# 00000001734

**DG-14 ★** — source: 43_丢番图几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The trace field of a Hitchin character variety. Conjecture: The integral points of the SL₂ character variety of a fibered hyperbolic 3-manifold group are finite, with a count bounded explicitly by the number of boundary tori (character-variety integral points).

**中文。** 定义：Hitchin 特征簇的迹域。猜想：fibered 双曲 3-流形群的 SL₂ 特征簇的整点有限且计数 ≤ 边界环面个数显式(特征簇整点)。
