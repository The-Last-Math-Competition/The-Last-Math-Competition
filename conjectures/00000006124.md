# 00000006124

**CG5-24 ★** — source: 189_高维概率_集中不等式II.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral gap and isoperimetry give the Cheeger-type relation of two constants. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit square of the isoperimetric constant, invariant under homology-equivalent classes (isospectral graphs); the gap of constant 1/2 is attained by zig-zag graph families; and laziness shrinks the gap by half, the shrinkage optimal. (isoperimetric square spectral-gap half-difference conversion)

**中文。** 定义：谱隙与等周：Cheeger 型不等式的两常数关系。猜想：换算常数为等周常数的显式平方且同调等价类（同谱图）下不变；常数 1/2 的间隙由 zig-zag 图族达到；懒惰化使间隙收缩一半且收缩为最优。（等周平方谱隙差半换算）
