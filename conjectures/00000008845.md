**English.** Definition: The structure of self-dual operators. Conjecture: Self-dual monotone operators are always densely defined antisymmetric extensions; and the classification is by antisymmetric bilinear forms on closed dense subspaces. (self-dual antisymmetric classification)

**中文。** 定义：自对偶算子的结构。猜想：自对偶单调算子恒为稠密定义的反对称扩张；分类由闭稠密子空间上的反对称双线性型给出。（自对偶反对称分类）
