# 00000000626

**KT-26 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The cusp volume of a hyperbolic knot is the volume enclosed by the maximally embedded torus of the complement. Conjecture: Every hyperbolic alternating knot has cusp volume ≥ 4, with equality attained only by the figure-8 complement; in the non-alternating case the infimum is the explicit constant 2v₈.

**中文。** 定义：双曲纽结的 cusp 体积指其余空间极大嵌入环面所围的体积。猜想：一切双曲交替纽结的 cusp 体积 ≥ 4(figure-8 补恰取等),且等号仅由 figure-8 取得;非交替情形下确界为 2v₈ 的显式常数。
