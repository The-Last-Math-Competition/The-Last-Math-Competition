# 00000008097

**FUS-17 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The Steinerberger-type quadratic constraint of fusion systems: the quadratic form q_F (the intersection-rank type) on the lattice of abelian subgroups of F. Conjecture: q_F is positive definite if and only if the exotic part of F vanishes (a positive-definiteness group-source duality); the discriminant of q_F lies in {plus or minus p^k} (a prime-power discriminant rigidity); and the sign of the discriminant equals (-1)^{rank(S)} (a sign-rank formula), the exception to the formula being the Wilk exceptional system (the unique sign-reversal system, with p = 2 and rank 5). (intersection-rank quadratic Wilk exception)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 Steinerberger 型二次约束：F 的交换子群格的二次型（交秩型）q_F。猜想：q_F 为正定的当且仅当 F 的 exotic 部分消失（正定-群源对偶）；且 q_F 的判别式 ∈ {±p^k}（素幂判别刚性）；判别式的符号 = (-1)^{rank(S)}（符号-秩公式），公式的例外为 Wilk 例外系（唯一的符号反例系统，其 p=2 且秩 5）。
