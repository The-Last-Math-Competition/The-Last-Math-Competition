**English.** Definition: The avalanche support of a sandpile with n initial chips on the 3D integer lattice is the carrier of the limiting configuration. Conjecture: The shape factor of the support is exactly an octahedron with an explicit correction; the correction term is a logarithmic power law with exponent one third. (three-dimensional sandpile shape factor)
