# 00000003971

**PC2-01 ★** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: Regular resolution is the subclass of resolution in which each resolution edge is used at most once, and S denotes proof size. Conjecture: The supremum of the separation ratio S_reg(phi)/S_R(phi) over n-variable formulas is Theta(log n), attained at both ends by an explicit family of bounded-width formulas. (regular-general separation order)

**中文。** 定义：正则消解指每条消解边至多使用一次的性质保持子类,S 记证明尺寸。猜想：正则与一般消解的分离比 S_reg(φ)/S_R(φ) 在 n 变量公式上的上确界为 Θ(log n),由显式有界宽度公式族同时达到两端。（正则-一般分离阶）
