# 00000001485

**HY-05 ★** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The zeta function of a suspension of an invertible map is the generating function ζ(z) = ∏(1−z^p)^{-1} of its periodic counts. Conjecture: For every hyperbolic suspension, ζ has radius of convergence 1, and its singular set on the unit circle is exactly the set of roots of unity (a complete characterization of periodic structure); no hyperbolic suspension has radius of convergence > 1.

**中文。** 定义：可逆映射的 suspension 的 zeta 函数指其周期计数的生成函数 ζ(z)=∏(1−z^{p})^{-1}。猜想：一切双曲 suspension 的 ζ 的收敛半径为 1,且单位圆上的奇点集恰为单位根(周期结构的完全刻画);不存在收敛半径 >1 的双曲 suspension。
