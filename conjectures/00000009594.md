# 00000009594

**MSH-14 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: Complete embedded minimal surfaces of finite Morse index in R^3 are classified as planes, catenoids, and helicoids; higher codimension and higher index generalizations are incomplete. Conjecture: Complete embedded minimal surfaces of finite index in R^4 form a finite list of planes, higher-dimensional catenoids, and Clifford types; the surface of index 2 is unique — the double Clifford embedding — with no exceptions. (finite index high codimension classification)

**中文。** 定义：R^3 中有限 Morse 指标的完备嵌入极小曲面分类为平面、悬链面与螺旋面；高余维与高指标推广未完成。猜想：R^4 中有限指标的完备嵌入极小曲面为平面、高维悬链面与 Clifford 型的有限并列表；指标为 2 的曲面唯一为二重 Clifford 嵌入，无例外。（有限指标高余维分类）
