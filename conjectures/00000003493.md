**English.** Definition: The Oka principle: holomorphic problems are holomorphically solvable when topologically solvable. Conjecture: The Oka–Grauert principle: the classification of principal bundles is equivalent holomorphically and topologically, with the evidence of the equivalence the softening of nonabelian cohomology. (Oka-Grauert nonabelian softening equivalence)
