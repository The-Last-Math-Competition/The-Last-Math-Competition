# 00000008658

**CGT-08 ★** — source: 274_计算群论与字的几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Nielsen-Thurston classification of mapping class groups: deciding among periodic, reducible, and pseudo-Anosov. Conjecture: There is a polynomial-time decision algorithm (a polynomial algorithm law); the algebraic degree of the stretch factor of pseudo-Anosovs is unbounded (a degree unboundedness law) but bounded by 2g-1 (a Bell-Webb type bound law); the shortest representative of reducible canonical systems is findable in polynomial time (a polynomial finding law); and quantum speedup of the decision is impossible (an exclusion law). (Nielsen-Thurston polynomial stretch bound)

**中文。** 定义：映射类群的 Nielsen–Thurston 分类：周期、可约、伪 Anosov 三分类的判定。猜想：分类判定的多项式算法（多项式算法律）；且伪 Anosov 的 stretch factor 的代数度可任意大（度无界律）但 ≤ 2g-1 的界（Bell–Webb 型界律）；可约的规范系统的最短代表的多项式发现（多项式发现律）；判定的量子加速的可能性排除（排除律）。
