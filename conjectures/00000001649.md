# 00000001649

**English.** Definition: The topmost nonzero degree of the cohomology of SL_n(Z) is the largest i with H^i(SL_n(Z);Q) ≠ 0. Conjecture: This degree is an explicit function of the vcd: the top nonzero degree equals ⌊(n²−1)/4⌋ − 1 (with vcd = 2n−3); outside the stable range, this degree is controlled by the symplectic geometry of the Borel–Serre boundary.

**中文。** 定义：SL_n(Z) 的上同调的非零最高度指使 H^i(SL_n(Z);Q)≠0 的最大 i。猜想：该度为 vcd 的显式函数:最高非零度 = ⌊(n²−1)/4⌋−1(vcd=2n−3);在稳定区间之外,该度由 Borel–Serre 边界的辛几何拓扑控制。
