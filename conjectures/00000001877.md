# 00000001877

**ML-07 ★** — source: 48_逻辑与模型论II.md

**English.** Definition: The model theory of II₁ factors in continuous logic treats operator-algebra structures in the language of continuous logic. Conjecture: The theory of separable existentially closed II₁ factors is a non-complete simple class (its Keisler–Shelah quantifiers are non-metrizable); continuous model completeness fails.

**中文。** 定义：连续逻辑中 II₁ 因子的模型论指以连续逻辑为语言的算子代数结构。猜想：可分存在封闭 II₁ 因子的理论为非完全简单类(其 Keisler–Shelah 量词非可度量);连续模型完全性失效。
