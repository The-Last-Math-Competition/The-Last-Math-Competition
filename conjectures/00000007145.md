# 00000007145

**English.** Definition: The Hamiltonicity of polytope graphs with skeleton cycles. Conjecture: Non-Hamiltonian examples of four-dimensional polytope graphs exist, and the minimal dimension of the examples is four. (minimal four-dimensional non-Hamiltonian skeletons)

**中文。** 定义：多面体图的哈密顿性：骨架的圈。猜想：哈密顿圈的存在：四维多面体图的非哈密顿例子且例的最小维数为四。（非哈密顿四维骨架最小维数例子）
