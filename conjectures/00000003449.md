**English.** Definition: The convergence radius of the Padé table: analytic continuation. Conjecture: Star-shaped convergence of the Padé table: the convergence domain of the Padé table of Stieltjes functions is the slit plane, with the expansion of the domain Zygmund polynomials. (Pade slit Zygmund star expansion)
