# 00000003926

**English.** Definition: The quantum cap action is the automorphic action of quantum cohomology on HF_*(H). Conjecture: The eigenvalues of the quantum cap action are Laurent polynomials in the Novikov parameter, and their radius of convergence equals exactly the Gromov width of the manifold. (quantum cap convergence radius and width)

**中文。** 定义：量子帽作用指量子同调在 HF_*(H) 上的自同构作用。猜想：量子帽作用的本征值为 Novikov 参数的 Laurent 多项式,其收敛半径恰等于流形的 Gromov 宽度。（量子帽收敛半径-宽度）
