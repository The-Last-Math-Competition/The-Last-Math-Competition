# 00000003870

**English.** Definition: Solvable densities are ensembles whose k-point functions have finite-dimensional closed forms. Conjecture: Beyond β ∈ {1,2,4}, no determinant- or Pfaffian-type solvable β-gas with analytic potential exists; but there exist everywhere-non-analytic potential families with solvable bulk statistics. (non-classical beta solvable boundary)

**中文。** 定义：可解密度指 k-点函数具有限维闭式的系综。猜想：β ∈ {1,2,4} 之外不存在解析位势的行列式或 Pfaffian 型可解 β-气;但存在处处非解析的位势族使 bulk 统计可解。（非经典 β 可解分界）
