# 00000006574

**English.** Definition: The interaction term with the interaction coefficients of splitting. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the interaction term and the interaction coefficients of the fractional steps are exact; the coefficients alternate in sign with period 2; and the sum of the coefficients is conserved, with conservation value 1. (exact interaction-coefficient conversion)

**中文。** 定义：相互作用项：分裂的相互作用系数。猜想：换算常数为相互作用项的显式且分步的相互作用系数精确；系数的符号交替且交替周期为 2；系数的和守恒且守恒值为 1。（相互作用系数精确换算）
