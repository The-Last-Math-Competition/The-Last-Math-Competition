# 00000008365

**English.** Definition: Tropical elimination theory: the Gabrielov-type upper bound structure of tropical images. Conjecture: The complexity upper bound of tropical elimination is always the n-th power of d; the bound is tight, attained by explicit constructions, and controlled by Betti numbers. (tropical elimination Gabrielov power bound)

**中文。** 定义：热带消除论指热带像的 Gabrielov 型上界结构。猜想：热带消除的复杂度上界恒为 d 的 n 次幂；界的紧性由显式构造达到且 Betti 数控制。（热带消除 Gabrielov 幂界）
