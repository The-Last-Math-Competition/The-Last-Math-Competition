**English.** Definition: The topological entropy h(a) of the quadratic family f_a(x) = a−x² is monotone in the parameter (Milnor–Thurston). Conjecture: The fractal dimension of the singular set of h(a) (the non-flat points) is 1/2; the singular spectrum consists exactly of Feigenbaum endpoints and homoclinic tangencies.
