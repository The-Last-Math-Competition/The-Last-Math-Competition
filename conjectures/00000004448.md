**English.** Definition: Open ASEP is ASEP with boundary injection and extraction. Conjecture: The second-order correction of the maximal-current phase flux value is an explicit logarithmic term, and the correction undergoes a square-root-type transition near the diagonal point and is exact. (open ASEP flux logarithmic correction)
