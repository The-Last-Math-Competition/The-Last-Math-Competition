# 00000000493

**English.** Definition: The Fibonacci-weighted lattice assigns weight 1 to right steps and weight x to up steps (with x² = x + 1); the partition function of a family of nonintersecting paths is given by an LGP determinant. Conjecture: The limit free energy F(n) of the partition function of n nonintersecting paths on the 2n×n rectangle satisfies F(n) ~ n²·log φ² with correction O(n log n), φ being the golden ratio.

**中文。** 定义：Fibonacci 权重格指每步向右权 1、向上权 x(x²=x+1 满足)的加权格,其不相交路径族的配分函数由 LGP 行列式给出。猜想：2n×n 矩形上 n 条不相交路径的配分函数的极限自由能 F(n) 满足 F(n) ~ n²·log φ²,修正项为 O(n log n),φ 为黄金比。
