# 00000006297

**English.** Definition: The homotopy-invariance conjecture of higher signatures. Conjecture: The injectivity domain of the assembly map contains hyperbolic groups, the injectivity coefficient is a rational multiple of characteristic classes, and the coefficient of the rational multiple is the pairing value of cohomology classes. (hyperbolic injectivity of assembly)

**中文。** 定义：高阶符号的同伦不变猜想。猜想：装配映射的单性域含双曲群；且单性系数为示性类的有理倍；且有理倍的系数为上同调类的配对值。（装配单性双曲全域）
