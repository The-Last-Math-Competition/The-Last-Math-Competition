# 00000008310

**English.** Definition: The homological trace of geometric intersections: the Lefschetz-Floer trace formula lf_trace. Conjecture: lf_trace (the Floer Lefschetz trace) is the exponential sum of local indices of fixed points (an index sum law); the periodicity of the trace (the generating function of traces of iterates k) is a rational function (a rational generation law); the poles of the rational function are a finite union of roots of unity (a root-of-unity pole law); and pole multiplicities are the minima of fixed point multiplicities (a multiplicity minimality law). (Lefschetz-Floer trace rational roots-of-unity poles)

**中文。** 定义：几何交集的同调迹：Lefschetz–Floer 的迹公式 lf_trace。猜想：lf_trace（Floer 的 Lefschetz 迹）= 不动点的局部指标的指数和（指数和律）；且迹的周期性（迭代映射 k 的迹的生成函数）为有理函数（有理生成律）；有理函数的极点为单位根的有限并（单位根极点律）；极点的重数为不动点的多重性的极小（重数极小律）。
