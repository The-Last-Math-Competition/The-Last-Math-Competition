**English.** Definition: The relative Brauer group is the subgroup of Brauer classes trivial under a given field extension. Conjecture: The l-prime part of the relative Brauer group of a quadratic extension is generated by the kernel of an explicit homomorphism, and the minimal number of generators is an explicit logarithmic function of the class number. (logarithmic generator formula of relative Brauer groups)

**中文。** 定义：相对 Brauer 群指在给定域扩张下平凡的 Brauer 类子群。猜想：二次扩张的相对 Brauer 群 ℓ 素部分由显式同态的核生成,最小生成元数为类数的对数型显式函数。（相对 Brauer 生成对数公式）
