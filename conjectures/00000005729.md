# 00000005729

**English.** Definition: The regularity separation is the delimitation of the free boundary from the singular set, with the separation constant. Conjecture: The separation constant table is the explicit half-power formula of the flatness threshold, attained by explicit wedge examples; the wedge-example constant is of power type 1/2; and any improvement of the separation constant requires doubling the degree of the solution, the doubling given by layered iteration. (flat wedge half-power separation constant)

**中文。** 定义：正则分离：自由边界与奇点集的分界；分离常数。猜想：分离常数表为平坦度阈值的显式半幂公式且由楔形显式例达到；楔形例的常数为 1/2 的幂型；任何分离常数的改进需要解的次数翻倍且翻倍由分层迭代给出。（平坦楔形半幂分离常数）
