**DIA-24 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The unimprovable points of Minkowski's linear forms theorem: the dimension mk_dim of the degeneration of critical lattices (the continuum of critical lattice families). Conjecture: mk_dim (the dimension of the family of critical lattices, the Minkowski equality case) is the codimension n(n+1)/2 - n (a codimension formula); the rigidity of the structure of equality lattices (geometric arrangements) is Minkowski duality of lattices (a dual lattice law); the minimal example of degeneration directions (infinite families at equality) is the two-dimensional dual lattice family (a two-dimensional example); and the saturation of the dimension in higher dimensions is quadratic in n (a quadratic saturation law). (Minkowski critical codimension dual saturation)

**中文。** 定义：Minkowski 的线性型定理的不可改进点：临界格的退化（连续统的临界格族）的维数 mk_dim。猜想：mk_dim（临界格的族（Minkowski 的等号情形）的维数）= n(n+1)/2 - n 的余维（余维公式）；且等号（临界）的格的结构的刚性（几何排列）为 Minkowski 的对偶格（对偶格律）；退化的方向（等号族的无穷维方向）的极小例为二维的对偶格族（二维例）；族的高维推广的维数的饱和（维数的上确界）为 n 的二次（二次饱和律）。
