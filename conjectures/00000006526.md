**English.** Definition: The superconnection is the even-odd mixed Quillen connection. Conjecture: The leading odd-term coefficient of the curvature expansion is invariant once normalized, and the supertrace of the leading term is the characteristic form. (invariant odd leading term of superconnections)

**中文。** 定义：超联络为奇偶混合的 Quillen 联络。猜想：曲率展开的奇次首项系数一旦归一恒不变；且首项的超迹为示性形式。（超联络奇次首项不变）
