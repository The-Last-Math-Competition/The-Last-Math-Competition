**English.** Definition: The Verbshire structure of commutators: nested identities [[…]]. Conjecture: The minimal length of nested commutator identities: no nontrivial nested identity of depth k exists on infinite-dimensional algebras, while on M_n the minimal length is an explicit correction of 2^k. (nested commutator minimal length)
