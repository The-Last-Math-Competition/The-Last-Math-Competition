# 00000001797

**CV-17 ◆** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: The truncation level of the Second Main Theorem (SMT) is the truncated counting function of the Nevanlinna characteristic. Conjecture: The SMT truncation level 1 cannot be improved for holomorphic curves in CP^n (the construction of replacing curves makes any truncation below 1 fail); truncation 1 is optimal; and the failure construction is realized by Fermat curve families of sufficiently large degree.

**中文。** 定义：第二主定理(SMT)的截断水平指 Nevanlinna 特征的截断计数函数。猜想：SMT 的截断水平 1 在 \ℂℙ^n 中的全纯曲线不可改进(替换曲线的构造使任何截断 <1 失效);截断 1 为最优；且失效构造由次数充分大的 Fermat 曲线族实现。
