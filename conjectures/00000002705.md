# 00000002705

**English.** Definition: Arzelà-type vector-valued compactness: families of Banach-valued functions. Conjecture: Compactness of Banach-valued equicontinuous families is equivalent to total boundedness of values plus equicontinuity; the optimal size of the ε-net of total boundedness is an explicit function of the type constant of the Banach space. (vector-valued Arzela equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Arzelà 型向量值紧性：Banach 值函数族。猜想：Banach 值等度连续族的紧性等价于值的完全有界加等度连续；完全有界的 ε-网的最优尺寸为 Banach 空间的类型常数的显式函数。（向量值 Arzela 等价）
