# 00000009622

**English.** Definition: Correlation decay and spectral gaps of Gibbs measures on hyperbolic systems: Ruelle's exponential mixing program. Conjecture: Gibbs measures of mixing SFTs have exponentially decaying correlations for Holder observables, with the optimal exponent equal to an explicit spectral expression of the second-order Legendre modulus of the pressure at the equilibrium state; the decay rate cannot exceed the gradient modulus of the potential, with the supremum attained by constant potentials. (Gibbs exponential mixing rate)

**中文。** 定义：Gibbs 测度在双曲系统上的关联衰减与谱隙：Ruelle 的指数混合纲领。猜想：混合 SFT 的 Gibbs 测度对 Hölder 可观测的关联为指数衰减，最优指数等于压力泛函在均衡态处的 Legendre 二阶模的显式谱表达式；衰减率不可超过势的梯度模，上确界由常数势达到。（Gibbs 指数混合率）
