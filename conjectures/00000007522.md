# 00000007522

**ET3-12 ★** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The Kummer sequence as the etale sheaf of n-th roots of unity. Conjecture: Kummer cohomology encodes divisibility as the eigendecomposition of H^1; and the spectrum of the decomposition is given by the Galois action on roots of unity. (Kummer eigendecomposition)

**中文。** 定义：Kummer 序列为 n 次单位的平展层。猜想：Kummer 上同调把可除性编码为 H¹ 的本征分解；且分解的谱由单位根的 Galois 作用给出。（Kummer 本征分解）
