**English.** Definition: The pseudospectral area is the Lebesgue measure of the level set. Conjecture: At small parameters the area growth is an explicit power law determined by the maximal Jordan block dimension, with explicit coefficient and asymptotically exact. (block-dimension power-law asymptotics of pseudospectral areas)
