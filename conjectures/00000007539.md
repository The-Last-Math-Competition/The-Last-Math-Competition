**ET3-29 ★** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: l-independence as the independence of etale cohomology from primes. Conjecture: The cohomology at sufficiently large primes is isomorphic, the isomorphism spectrum being controlled by the ramification of representation fields; and the exceptions of independence are only a finite set of small primes. (sufficiently-large-isomorphism l-independence)

**中文。** 定义：ℓ-独立性指平展上同调对素数的独立。猜想：充分大素数的上同调为同构且同构的谱由表示域的分歧控制；且独立的例外仅在小素数有限集。（l-独立充分大同构）
