# 00000002826

**LD-26 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: Deviations of long-range-dependent processes: Taqqu type. Conjecture: The deviation-exponent drift of long memory: for processes with Hurst exponent above 1/2 the effective deviation scale is n^{2−2H}, with rate function the squared kernel norm of the fractional Gaussian. (long-memory deviation scale law)

**中文。** 定义：长程依赖过程的偏差：Taqqu 型。猜想：长记忆的偏差指数漂移：Hurst 指数大于 1/2 的过程的有效偏差尺度为 n 的 2−2H 次幂且速率函数为分数高斯的核范数平方。（长记忆偏差尺度律）
