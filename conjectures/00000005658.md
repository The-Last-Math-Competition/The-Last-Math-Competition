# 00000005658

**English.** Definition: The free-field embedding is the graph embedding driven by the Gaussian field. Conjecture: The two-dimensional conversion constant is the explicit exponential of the two-point function of the field, of root-two-over-pi type, and matching the discrete Liouville height difference. (height-difference constant of free-field embeddings)
