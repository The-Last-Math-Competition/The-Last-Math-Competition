# 00000008424

**English.** Definition: The Arf invariant of an orthogonal design is the Witt index of its associated quadratic form. Conjecture: The difference of Witt indices of an orthogonal design under the field extension F_q/F_p is a combination of design parameters; the difference is given by the sum of the quadratic character of q over the design blocks and is always even. (orthogonal design Witt difference)

**中文。** 定义：正交设计的 Arf 不变量指其结合二次型的 Witt 指数。猜想：正交设计在域扩张 F_q/F_p 下的 Witt 指数差为设计参数的组合；差由 q 的二次特征标在设计区组上的和给出且恒为偶数。（正交设计 Witt 差）
