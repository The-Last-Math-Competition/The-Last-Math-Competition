# 00000009102

**English.** Definition: Configuration spaces: the orbit spaces of disjoint points. Conjecture: The volume formula for k-point configurations is a combinatorial factor times a tube domain integral; and the integrand of the integral is a power of ball volumes. (configuration volume tube formula)

**中文。** 定义：构形空间为不相交点的轨道空间。猜想：k 点构形的体积公式为组合因子乘管域积分；且积分的被积函数为球的体积幂。（构形体积管公式）
