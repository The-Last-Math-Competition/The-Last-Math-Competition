# 00000001061

**FG-61 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: A hyperelliptic curve is a curve whose affine equation is quadratic in one variable. Conjecture: The parity of the number of its finite-field points is always given explicitly by the sign of the discriminant of the quadratic character; and the kernel of the explicit formula is Euler's criterion. (hyperelliptic discriminant-sign point-count parity)

**中文。** 定义：超椭圆曲线指仿射方程为二次多项式的曲线。猜想：其有限域点数的奇偶性恒由二次特征的判别式符号显式给出；显式的核为欧拉判据。（超椭圆判别符号点数奇偶）
