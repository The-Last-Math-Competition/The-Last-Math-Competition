# 00000004736

**English.** Definition: The conversion of the Brauer group to etale cohomology is the boundary computation of the Kummer sequence in Gabber-type theorems. Conjecture: In the third-coefficient case the kernel of the boundary map is the mod-three quotient of H^3, computed explicitly by the Kummer boundary; the image of the boundary map lies in the 3-torsion layer and the index of the image is exactly 3; the composition of the quadratic and cubic boundaries is the zero map, and the vanishing is equivalent to the complete vanishing of the square tower. (mod-three kernel formula of the Kummer boundary)

**中文。** 定义：Brauer 群到 étale 上同调的换算：Gabber 型定理的 Kummer 序列边缘计算。猜想：三次系数情形的边缘映射核为 H³ 的模三商且核由 Kummer 边缘显式计算；边缘映射的像落在挠 3 层且像的指标恰为 3；二次与三次边缘的合成为零映射且零性等价于平方塔的完全消没。（Kummer 边缘模三核公式）
