# 00000001077

**English.** Definition: The correspondence between stabilizer codes and finite-field self-dual codes (CRSSH). Conjecture: The F₄-linear correspondence for embedded quantum codes becomes, for asymmetric quantum codes, a weighted version of Hermitian self-duality (the asymmetric correspondence).

**中文。** 定义：Stabilizer 码与有限域自对偶码对应(CRSSH)。猜想：嵌量子码的 F₄ 线性对应在非对称量子码下为 Hermitian 自对偶的加权版(非对称对应)。
