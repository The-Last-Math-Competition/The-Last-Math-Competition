# 00000002281

**MG-21 ★** — source: 61_鞅与随机分析.md

**English.** Definition: Immersion of filtrations means compatible conditional expectations for ℱ ⊂ 𝒢. Conjecture: The compatibility of the initial enlargement of the immersed merge of two Brownian filtrations is an explicit cross condition on quadratic covariations (immersed merge).

**中文。** 定义：滤波的浸入性(immersion,ℱ ⊂ 𝒢 的条件期望相容)。猜想：两个 Brown 滤波的浸入合并的初始扩大(expansion)的相容性为二次变差的显式交叉条件(浸入合并)。
