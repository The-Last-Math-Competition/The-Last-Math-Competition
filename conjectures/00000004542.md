# 00000004542

**English.** Definition: The irrationality exponent is the critical exponent for which the approximation inequality has infinitely many solutions. Conjecture: The irrationality exponent of GS-type logarithmic ratios has the explicit upper bound two plus a small quantity, and within the class there exist members attaining exponents arbitrarily close to two. (upper bound of GS-type irrationality exponents)

**中文。** 定义：无理度指数指使逼近不等式有无穷多解的临界指数。猜想：GS 型对数比值的无理度指数有显式上界二加小量,且该类中存在达到任意接近二的指数的成员。（GS 型无理度指数上界）
