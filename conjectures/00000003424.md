**English.** Definition: The boundary behavior of potential kernels of stochastic processes: Feller chains. Conjecture: The Martin stratification of Feller boundaries: the stratified dimension of the boundary is the spectrum of escape velocities, with the gaps of the spectrum the transverse mixing rates. (Feller Martin escape transverse spectrum)
