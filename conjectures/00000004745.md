# 00000004745

**BR2-25 ★** — source: 143_非交换代数的几何_Brauer群与扭曲.md

**English.** Definition: The transcendental Brauer group of a Kummer surface is the inheritance structure of the transcendental Brauer group of the parent abelian surface under the Kummer quotient. Conjecture: The transcendental Brauer inheritance for small discriminants is given by a binary tree of the parent tower with depth at most 3; the surviving classes are exactly the involution-invariant part, and the quotiented part is fully absorbed by 2-torsion; the transcendental rank of Kummer surfaces of discriminant at most 16 is at most 2, and there is exactly one example of rank 2. (binary tree of Kummer transcendental inheritance)

**中文。** 定义：Kummer 曲面的超越 Brauer 群：母阿贝尔曲面的超越 Brauer 群在 Kummer 商下的继承结构。猜想：小判别式的超越 Brauer 继承由母塔的二叉树给出且树的深度不超过 3；幸存的类恰为对合不变部分且商掉的部分由 2 挠完全吸收；判别式不超过 16 的 Kummer 的超越秩至多为 2 且秩 2 的例恰有一个。（Kummer 超越继承二叉树）
