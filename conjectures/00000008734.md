# 00000008734

**English.** Definition: Coherence order: the maximal scale of quantum coherence maintainable in many-body systems. Conjecture: The coherence order in the thermal phase is unbounded (an unboundedness law); the coherence order in the MBL phase is O(1) (a unit bound law); metrological enhancement in the MBL phase reaches the Heisenberg limit (a realization law); and the enhancement factor is explicit in the disorder strength (an enhancement law). (coherence order Heisenberg metrological enhancement)

**中文。** 定义：相干阶：多体系统中可保持的量子相干最大规模。猜想：热相的相干阶无界（无界律）；且 MBL 相的相干阶为 O(1)（单位界律）；计量学增强在 MBL 相达到 Heisenberg 极限（实现律）；增强因子为无序强度的显式（增强律）。
