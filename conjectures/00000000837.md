# 00000000837

**English.** Definition: The Morrey constant is the extremal factor in potential estimates. Conjecture: The best Morrey constants on the half-space and on the quadrant coincide; and this equality is the minimal example of the failure of domain monotonicity. (Morrey half-space quadrant equality)

**中文。** 定义：Morrey 常数为位势估计的极值因子。猜想：半空间与象限域上的最佳 Morrey 常数相等；且相等性为域单调性失败的最小例子。（Morrey 半空间象限等值）
