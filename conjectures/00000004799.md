**English.** Definition: The Kuznetsov component is the semiorthogonal complement in the derived category. Conjecture: Its numerical torsion correction table is given by the coefficients of the Hochschild order, the table is complete for low-degree Fanos, and the correction is always of second-order torsion. (numerical torsion correction table of Kuznetsov components)
