# 00000008514

**English.** Definition: The Malgrange group of a nonlinear differential equation is its Galois-type invariant of the solution flow. Conjecture: The Lie algebra of the Malgrange group is finite-dimensional; its dimension is at most the dimension of the solution space of the equation; and in the integrable-system case the dimension equals the number of first integrals. (Malgrange group dimension law)

**中文。** 定义：非线性微分方程的 Malgrange 群为其解流的 Galois 型不变量。猜想：Malgrange 群的 Lie 代数为有限维；其维数 ≤ 方程的解空间维数；且在可积系情形维数等于首次积分的个数。（Malgrange 群维数律）
