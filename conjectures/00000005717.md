# 00000005717

**English.** Definition: The well-posedness of the double obstacle problem is existence and uniqueness. Conjecture: The uniqueness threshold is the explicit infimum of the obstacle gap, controlled by the antimonotone difference of the upper and lower obstacles, and the bound is tight. (antimonotone-difference uniqueness threshold of double obstacles)

**中文。** 定义：双障碍的适定性指存在唯一性。猜想：唯一性阈值为障碍间隙的显式下确界,由上下障碍的反单调差控制且界紧。（双障碍反单调差唯一阈值）
