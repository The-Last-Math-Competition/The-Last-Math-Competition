# 00000003503

**English.** Definition: Donaldson–Uhlenbeck–Yau: the existence of anti-self-dual connections. Conjecture: The DUY correspondence: the correspondence of stable bundles with Hermitian–Einstein metrics, with the uniqueness of the correspondence discrete modulo the reductive group. (DUY Hermite-Einstein reductive uniqueness)
