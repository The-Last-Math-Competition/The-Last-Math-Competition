**English.** Definition: The optimal bounds A, B of a frame {fₙ}. Conjecture: Under perturbations of Λ = Z by ±ε, the Lipschitz constant of the optimal frame ratio B/A of the exponential frames {e^{iλₙx}} in L²(0,1) is explicit in ε (exponential frame stability).
