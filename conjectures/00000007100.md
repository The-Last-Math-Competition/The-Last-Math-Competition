# 00000007100

**English.** Definition: The geometric interpretation of acceleration with the continuous limit. Conjecture: The differential-equation limit of acceleration holds, the combination of the limit being the variational viewpoint. (variational continuous-limit acceleration)

**中文。** 定义：加速的几何解释：连续极限。猜想：连续的极限：加速的微分方程极限且限的组合为变分观点。（加速几何解释连续极限变分观点微分方程极限组合加速变分观点极限方程微分观点加速极限）
