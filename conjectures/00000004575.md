**English.** Definition: The pants graph has pants decompositions as vertices and single exchanges as edges. Conjecture: Its hyperbolicity constant is an explicit linear function of the genus, the lower bound is attained by nested splitting sequences, and the slope is optimal. (genus-linear hyperbolicity bound of the pants graph)
