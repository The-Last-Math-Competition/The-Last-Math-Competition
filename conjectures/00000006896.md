# 00000006896

**English.** Definition: The length of degeneration chains is the longest descending chain of the poset. Conjecture: The maximal length of degeneration chains has an explicit upper bound counted by rank jumps; and the length spectrum is finite at fixed size. (rank-jump bound on degeneration-chain length)

**中文。** 定义：退化链的长度为偏序的最长降链。猜想：退化链的最大长度有显式上界且界由秩跳跃计数；且长度谱在固定尺寸有限。（退化链长度秩跳跃界）
