# 00000008060

**PRF-10 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The proof-theoretic algebra of interpolants of Frege systems: the size i(A to B) of the constructive interpolant of Craig's interpolation theorem (the minimal size of an interpolant formula). Conjecture: The interpolant size for propositional Frege systems is exponentially incompressible: there exist A, B with i(A to B) at least 2^{c|A|}; and the gap between upper and lower bounds on interpolant size is system-invariant (all Frege systems of the same order); the non-uniform lower bound for interpolants of quantified Boolean formulas (QBF) agrees with monotone circuit lower bounds (the anchoring separation): the interpolation-to-monotone-circuit reduction preserves order (an order-preserving reduction). (Craig interpolant monotone order preservation)

**中文。** 定义：Frege 系统的插入子的证明论代数：Craig 插值定理的构造性插入子的规模 i(A→B)（插值式的最小规模）。猜想：命题 Frege 系统的插入子规模为指数级不可压缩：存在 A,B 使 i(A→B) ≥ 2^{c|A|}；且插值规模的上下界间隙的系统不变性（一切 Frege 系统同阶）；量词布尔公式 QBF 的插子的非一致性下界与单调电路下界（为分离的锚点）一致：插值-单调电路的归约保阶（阶保持归约）。
