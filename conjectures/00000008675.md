**English.** Definition: Dehn filling adds relations to a relatively hyperbolic structure of a hyperbolic group to kill peripheral subgroups. Conjecture: The optimal lower bound on filling density is always given by the spectral gap of the relative pseudo-characteristic; and hyperbolicity after filling is strictly preserved below that bound, with the recovered picture complete. (Dehn filling spectral gap density bound)

**中文。** 定义：Dehn 填充指双曲群相对双曲结构下添加关系消去例外子群。猜想：填充密度的最优下界恒由相对伪特征数的谱间隙给出；且填充后的双曲性在密度低于该下界时严格保持而还原图完备。（Dehn 填充谱间隙密度下界）
