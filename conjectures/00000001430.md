**English.** Definition: Internal defects of rotor-router aggregation are the pointwise deviations of its occupied set from an inscribed disk. Conjecture: The defects of rotor-router aggregation on Z² started at the origin are uniformly bounded (an O(log n) bound is known; boundedness is open); the true value of the bound is within ±3.

**中文。** 定义：rotor-router 聚集的内部缺陷指其占据集与内切圆盘的逐点偏差。猜想：ℤ² 上从原点出发的 rotor-router 聚集的缺陷一致有界(已知 O(log n) 界,有界性开放);界的真实值为 ±3 以内。
