# 00000005154

**English.** Definition: Special subdomains are the Shimura-type full-measure special algebraic subdomains of the period domain. Conjecture: The special compact subdomains of the rank-two symmetric domain are given by an explicit finite list from the classification of embeddings; the intersection of two special subdomains is again special; and a maximal special subdomain has codimension one if and only if the embedding is given by a quaternion order. (embedding list of rank-two special subdomains)

**中文。** 定义：特殊子域：周期域中 Shimura 型全测度特殊代数子域。猜想：秩二对称域的特殊紧子域由嵌入分类给出显式有限列表；两个特殊子域的交仍为特殊；极大特殊子域余维为一当且仅当嵌入由四元数阶给出。（秩二特殊子域嵌入列表）
