**English.** Definition: The information criterion is the penalty term of model selection. Conjecture: The curvature correction of the volume penalty is given by the marginal-likelihood saddle point of nested families, and the kernel of the saddle point is the Schwarz asymptotics. (Schwarz asymptotic saddle point of criteria)
