# 00000009747

**MAD-17 ★** — source: 310_数学的自动发现.md

**English.** Definition: Reconstructing defining relations from numerical data: the error floor of inverse symbolic regression. Conjecture: The minimal error of reconstructing algebraic relations from observations is an explicit spectral function of the product of noise, condition number, and relation degree; the error floor does not decay to zero as observations grow, with the residual exactly noise times the Vandermonde condition number. (inverse regression error floor)

**中文。** 定义：数值数据重建定义关系：逆符号回归的误差底限。猜想：由观测重建代数关系的最小误差为噪声、条件数与关系次数乘积的显式谱函数；误差底限在观测数趋于无穷时不衰减到零且残留恰为噪声乘 Vandermonde 条件数。（逆回归误差底限）
