**English.** Definition: A geodesic automaton is a finite automaton recognizing geodesic words. Conjecture: The minimal state count is an explicit bilinear expression of the thinness constant and the generator number, attained at extremal groups, and the count is exact. (bilinear state count of geodesic automata)
