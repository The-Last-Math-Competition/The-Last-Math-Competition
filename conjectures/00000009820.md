**English.** Definition: algorithm lower bound of computation complexity: vertex scaling objective curvature of discrete homogenization. Conjecture: polynomial time computation of discrete homogenization can in number of vertices and complexity lower bound of arbitrary algorithm is exponent of linear; polynomial is two and by convexity optimal realized of curvature equation. (homogenization complexity lower bound)
