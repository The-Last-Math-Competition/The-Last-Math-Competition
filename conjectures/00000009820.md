# 00000009820

**DDG-30 △** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Computational complexity of discrete uniformization: algorithmic lower bounds for vertex scaling to target curvatures. Conjecture: Discrete uniformization is computable in polynomial time in the vertex count, and every algorithm has a linear lower bound; the polynomial exponent is two, optimally realized by the convexity of the curvature equations. (uniformization complexity lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：离散均匀化的计算复杂度：顶点缩放到目标曲率的算法下界。猜想：离散均匀化可在顶点数的多项式时间计算且任意算法的复杂度下界为线性；多项式的指数为二且由曲率方程的凸性最优实现。（均匀化复杂度下界）
