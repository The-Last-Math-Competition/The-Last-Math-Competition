# 00000000442

**English.** Definition: Ribbon Schur functions form the ribbon basis of NCSF. Conjecture: The ribbon-basis expansion coefficients of arbitrary ribbon products are nonnegative; and the combinatorial interpretation of positivity is the counting of ribbon tableaux. (ribbon multiplication positivity)

**中文。** 定义：ribbon Schur 函数为 NCSF 的带状基。猜想：任意带状多重积的带状基展开系数非负；且正性的组合解释为带状表的计数。（ribbon 乘法正性）
