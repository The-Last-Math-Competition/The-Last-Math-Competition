**TIL-20 ◆** — source: 242_离散几何II_镶嵌与铺砌.md

**English.** Definition: The maximal spectral multiplicity msm of a minimal substitution dynamical system is the maximal multiplicity of spectral measures in its unitary spectral decomposition. Conjecture: Every primitive substitution tiling system has finite msm; msm=1 if and only if the part of the substitution matrix spectrum on the unit circle is contained in {1,-1}; and primitive substitutions with arbitrarily large msm exist, the tile count needed growing linearly in msm. (maximal spectral multiplicity of substitutions)

**中文。** 定义：极小替换动力系统的极大谱重数 msm 指其酉谱分解中谱测度的最大重数。猜想：一切原始替换铺砌系统具有有限 msm；且 msm=1 当且仅当替换矩阵的谱在单位圆上的部分至多包含 {±1}；存在 msm 任意大的原始替换，其构造中所需的 tile 数与 msm 呈线性关系。
