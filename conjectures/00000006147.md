**DAG2-17 ◆** — source: 190_导出代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: Derived duality is the relative dualizing complex of proper pairs. Conjecture: For complete intersections the dualizing is the shift of the canonical algebra, the kernel of the shift always being the weight of the cotangent spectrum; the weight sum equals the relative dimension; and the vanishing threshold of the dualizing is at singularity codimension two, Cohen-Macaulay type below. (cotangent-spectrum kernel of dualizing shifts)

**中文。** 定义：导出对偶：完备偶的相对对偶化复形。猜想：完全交时对偶化为规范代数的移位且移位的核恒为余切谱的权；权的和等于相对维数；对偶化的消没阈在奇点余维二处且阈下为 Cohen–Macaulay 型。（对偶化移位余切谱核）
