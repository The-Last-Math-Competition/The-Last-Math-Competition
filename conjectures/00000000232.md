# 00000000232

**English.** Definition: The Ramsey number R(s, t) is the least n such that every red-blue coloring contains a red K_s or a blue K_t. Conjecture: The asymptotic constant of the diagonal Ramsey numbers is a series in negative powers of the square root of two; and the bound is approached within the random-coloring window. (diagonal Ramsey random-coloring window constant)

**中文。** 定义：Ramsey 数 R(s,t) 为最小 n 使任意红蓝染色含红 K_s 或蓝 K_t。猜想：对角 Ramsey 数的渐近常数为根号二乘四的负幂级数；且界的核为随机染色窗。（对角 Ramsey 随机染色窗常数根号二）
