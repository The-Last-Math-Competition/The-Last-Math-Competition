# 00000009940

**English.** Definition: Time-varying persistence diagrams: topological time-series models of dynamical systems. Conjecture: The mixing rate of time-varying persistence diagram processes equals the spectral gap of the generating dynamical system, and mixing transfer preserves the Markov property of topological features; the explicit constant of the mixing rate is the supremum of filtration parameter derivatives. (time-varying persistence mixing rate)

**中文。** 定义：时变持久图：动力系统生成的时间序列的拓扑时序模型。猜想：时变持久图过程的混合率等于生成动力系统的谱隙且混合传递保持拓扑特征的马尔可夫性；混合率的显式常数为过滤参数的导数上确界。（时变持久混合率）
