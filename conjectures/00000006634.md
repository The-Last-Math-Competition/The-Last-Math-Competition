# 00000006634

**English.** Definition: Uniqueness with the contraction condition of infinite-volume-state uniqueness. Conjecture: The contraction spectrum of the interaction matrix is explicit and the closed generator is spectral radius one; the generation of spectral radius one is uniform universality; and the contraction constant is the spectral radius. (closed spectrum structure of contraction matrices)

**中文。** 定义：唯一性：无限体积态的唯一性收缩条件。猜想：交互矩阵的收缩谱显式且封闭的生成元为谱半径一；谱半径一的生成为一致普适；收缩的常数为谱半径。（收缩矩阵谱封闭结构）
