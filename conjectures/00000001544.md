# 00000001544

**GM-04 ★** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: An m-rich line contains ≥ m points; the 3D point-line-plane incidence count is the total number of incidence triples. Conjecture: In three dimensions the optimal upper bound for incidences is the explicit product form I ≤ C·(m^{1/2}n^{3/4} + m + n²) of Szemerédi–Trotter type, with C = 2^{11}; the form is attained by cell decompositions parameterized by rich-line density.

**中文。** 定义：m-富线指含 ≥m 点的直线;3D 点-线-面关联计数指关联三元组总数。猜想：三维中关联数 I 的最优上界为 I ≤ C·(m^{1/2}n^{3/4}+m+n²) 的 Szemerédi–Trotter 型显式乘积形式,C=2^{11};形式由富线密度参数化的胞腔分解达到。
