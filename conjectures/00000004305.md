**English.** Definition: The p-adic Brumer-Stark units are the special systems of units with p-adic attachment. Conjecture: The smallest prime generating their field is at most a constant multiple of the logarithm of the discriminant, and the bound is attained optimally by an explicit CM family. (logarithmic bound of the smallest BS generating prime)

**中文。** 定义：p-进 Brumer–Stark 单位指 p-进附着的特殊单位系。猜想：其生成域的最小素数不超过判别式对数的常数倍,界由显式 CM 族达到最优。（BS 最小生成素对数界）
