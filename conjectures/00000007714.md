**English.** Definition: The spectral dimension d_s of the graph approximation sequence G_n of the chair tiling is defined via the return-probability decay lim (-2 log p_n)/log n. Conjecture: For the chair tiling d_s = 2 exactly, and more precisely the return probability is p_n = c n^{-1}(log n)^{-1/2+o(1)}, the logarithmic correction exponent -1/2 arising from the resonance between the second eigenvalue 2 of the substitution matrix and the fourth eigenvalue.
