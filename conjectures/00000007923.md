# 00000007923

**English.** Definition: The pole spectrum of Rankin-Selberg integrals: the pole set of the Rankin-Selberg L-function L(s, pi x pi') of two cusp representations occurs only at s = 1 when pi is isomorphic to pi'. Conjecture: A quantitative form of the uniform vanishing of error poles: when pi and pi' are distinct but arithmetically equivalent (coefficients almost identical), the first separating prime p_sep (the first prime where coefficients differ) satisfies p_sep <= Q(pi)^epsilon (strong separation); and the expected size of p_sep is of uniform order log Q, with the separation rate distribution following a Poisson-type model (the prime hit problem). (strong separation of arithmetically equivalent representations)

**中文。** 定义：Rankin–Selberg 积的极点谱：两个尖点表示的 Rankin–Selberg L(s,π×π') 的极点集仅在 π≅π'（s=1）出现。猜想：一致版本的误差极点消没的定量形式：当 π 与 π' 为不同但算术等价的（系数几乎一致），首个分离素数 p_sep（首个系数不同的素数）满足 p_sep ≤ Q(π)^{ε}（强分离）；且 p_sep 的期望规模为 log Q 的一致阶，分离率的分布为泊松型模型（素数命中问题）。
