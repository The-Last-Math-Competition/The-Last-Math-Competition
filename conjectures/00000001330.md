# 00000001330

**English.** Conjecture: The ratio of the Bergman metric to the Weil–Petersson metric on Teichmüller space has, at boundary points, asymptotics given by an explicit quadratic form (a boundary comparison of the two metrics).

**中文。** 猜想：Teichmüller 空间的 Bergman 度量与 Weil–Petersson 度量的比值在境界点处的渐近为显式二次型(两度量的边界比较)。
