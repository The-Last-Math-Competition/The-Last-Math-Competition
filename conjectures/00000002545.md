# 00000002545

**English.** Definition: The converse of the density theorem: upper density 1 implies rectifiability. Conjecture: H^k-measure sets of upper density 1 are k-rectifiable, and the quantitative rate of rectifiability under uniform density approach to 1 is an explicit power of the Besicovitch distance; the exponent is 2^{-(k+1)}, attained by snowflake counterexamples. (density converse quantitative)

**中文。** 定义：密度定理的逆：上密度为 1 蕴含可校正性。猜想：上密度为 1 的 H^k 测度集必为 k 可校正且可校正性在密度一致逼近 1 时的量化速率为 Besicovitch 距离的显式幂；幂指数为 2 的负 k+1 次幂且由雪花反例达到。（密度逆定理量化）
