# 00000001416

**HT-26 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: The LS category cat(X) is the minimal number of contractible open sets covering X. Conjecture: For quaternionic projective spaces, cat(HP^n) = n+1; for the modified version of Toomer's conjecture (upper category), the bound cat₁(HP^{2n}) = 4n is tight, attained explicitly by the cup length of the cell structure.

**中文。** 定义：LS 范畴 cat(X) 指覆盖 X 所需可缩开集的最小个数。猜想：对四元数射影空间,cat(HP^n) = n+1;对 Toomer 猜想的修改版(上范畴),cat₁(HP^{2n}) = 4n 的界为紧,由胞腔结构的杯长显式达到。
