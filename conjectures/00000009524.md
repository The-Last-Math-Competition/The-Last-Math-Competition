# 00000009524

**QIC-04 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: The coherent information I_c(rho,phi) = S(phi(rho)) - S(phi^c(rho)); the quantum capacity Q(phi) is its regularization, and the single-letterization question is whether sup_rho I_c(rho,phi) already equals Q(phi). Conjecture: For Haar-random d-dimensional channels, with probability tending to one, sup_rho I_c is at most Q(phi) which is at most sup_rho I_c + O(1/d), so random channels approximately single-letterize; and the intersection of this random class with the proven degradable class has measure zero. (LSD single-letterization)

**中文。** 定义：相干信息 I_c(ρ,φ)=S(φ(ρ))−S(φ^c(ρ))；量子容量 Q(φ) 是 I_c 的正则化，单字母化问题即 sup_ρ I_c(ρ,φ) 是否已等于 Q(φ)。猜想：对 Haar 随机选取的 d 维信道，以趋于 1 的概率 sup_ρ I_c ≤ Q(φ) ≤ sup_ρ I_c + O(1/d)，即随机信道近似单字母化；且该随机类与单字母化已证的 degradable 类之交为零测集。（LSD 单字母化）
