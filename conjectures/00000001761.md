# 00000001761

**RT-11 ◆** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: James's conjecture states that Specht modules are semisimple when the p-restricted weight is at most p/2. Conjecture: James's conjecture holds, with no counterexamples outside its range (large p with p ≥ 2n); the first counterexample (if any) has (p,n) with p < 2n and n ≥ 16.

**中文。** 定义：James 猜想指 Specht 模在 p-限制权 ≤p/2 时半单。猜想：James 猜想成立且其范围之外(p≥2n 的大 p)无反例;第一个反例(若存在)的 (p,n) 满足 p<2n 且 n≥16。
