# 00000009542

**QIC-22 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: Composite channel capacity is the reliable rate when the sender only knows that the true channel lies in a set W; random-coding capacity and deterministic-coding capacity can differ. Conjecture: The entanglement-assisted capacity of a finite composite quantum set {phi_i} is the minimax formula min_i max_rho I(rho, phi_i), and the formula extends continuously to compact composite sets; and the random-coding error exponent is the worst-channel exponent min_i E(R, phi_i). (composite capacity minimax)

**中文。** 定义：复合信道容量指发送端仅知真实信道属于集合 W 的条件下的可靠速率；随机编码容量与确定编码容量可分离。猜想：有限复合量子集 {φ_i} 的纠缠辅助容量为极小极大公式 min_i max_ρ I(ρ,φ_i)，该公式延拓到紧复合集时连续；随机编码错误指数为 min_i E(R,φ_i) 的最坏信道指数。（复合容量极小极大）
