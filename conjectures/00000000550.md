# 00000000550

**CA-50 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The ext multiplicity Ext^1(M,M). Conjecture: For finite-representation-type algebras, the extremal dimension of the ext-cone equals the representation dimension (an equality between finitistic and ext dimensions, for a special class of biserial algebras).

**中文。** 定义：ext 重数 Ext^1(M,M)。猜想：有限表示代数的 ext-锥的极值维数 = rep 维数(Finitistic 维数与 ext 维数的等式,对一类特殊的 biserial 代数)。
