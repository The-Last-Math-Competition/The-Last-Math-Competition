# 00000001956

**English.** Definition: The spectral zeta function of a hyperbolic lattice is the regularization of power sums of Laplace eigenvalues. Conjecture: The leading term of its residue is given explicitly by the integral of a heat-kernel coefficient (a boundary integral of a₁); the type of the residue is isomorphic across all arithmetic hyperbolic lattices.

**中文。** 定义：双曲格的谱 zeta 函数指 Laplace 特征值的幂和的正规化。猜想：其残数的首项由热核系数的积分显式(a₁ 的边界积分);残数的类型在一切算术双曲格上为同构。
