# 00000005140

**NL2-30 △** — source: 156_数值线性代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The mean error and the tail distribution are two layers of statistical behavior. Conjecture: There exist two random algorithms with the same mean error but different tail-distribution exponents, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of two types of random projections. (same mean error-different tail exponent separation)

**中文。** 定义：均值误差与尾分布指两层统计行为。猜想：存在两随机算法的均值误差相同而尾分布指数不同,分离由两类随机投影的显式对实现。（均值误差同-尾指数异分离）
