# 00000004125

**English.** Definition: The random algebra is the forcing of positive-measure Borel sets modulo null sets. Conjecture: Its automorphism group is simple and its maximal solvable subgroups are trivial; the complete ccc Boolean algebras with simple automorphism group are exactly the random algebra and the Cohen algebra. (simplicity of the random algebra automorphism group)

**中文。** 定义：随机代数指正测度 Borel 集模零测的力迫。猜想：其自同构群为单群且可解子群极大者平凡;完备 ccc 布尔代数中具单自同构群的恰为随机与 Cohen 两个。（随机代数自同构单性）
