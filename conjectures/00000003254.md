# 00000003254

**English.** Definition: The Smale invariant: the regular homotopy classes of sphere immersions. Conjecture: The Smale invariant of immersions of the two-sphere into three-space is the Z₂ of the second homotopy group, with the geometric realization of the invariant the explicit movie of sphere eversion. (Smale sphere eversion movie realization)
