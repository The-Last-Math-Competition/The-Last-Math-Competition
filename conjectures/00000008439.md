# 00000008439

**English.** Definition: The dual distance spectrum of the design space: the distance distribution of the dual code. Conjecture: The multiplicities of the first t nonzero values of the dual spectrum are polynomials in lambda; the polynomials are given explicitly by the coefficients of the Krawtchouk expansion and are positive. (positivity of dual spectrum multiplicities)

**中文。** 定义：设计空间的对偶距离谱指对偶码的距离分布。猜想：对偶谱的前 t 个非零值的重数为 λ 的多项式；多项式由 Krawtchouk 展开的系数显式给出且为正。（对偶谱重数正性）
