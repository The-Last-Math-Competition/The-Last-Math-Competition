# 00000004038

**English.** Definition: A tau-periodic point is an indecomposable module with tau^n M isomorphic to M, n being the period. Conjecture: The realizable periods are exactly the degrees corresponding to the roots of unity among the roots of the Coxeter polynomial, and each period is realized in some explicit tube. (tau-periods and Coxeter roots of unity)

**中文。** 定义：τ-周期点指 τ^n M ≅ M 的不可分解模,n 为周期。猜想：可实现的周期全体恰为 Coxeter 多项式的单位根对应的次数集合,每个周期在某显式管中实现。（τ-周期-Coxeter 单位根）
