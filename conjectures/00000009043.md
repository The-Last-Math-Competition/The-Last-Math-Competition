# 00000009043

**English.** Definition: The Carleman inequality: the weighted inequality for log-concave functions. Conjecture: The optimal constant of the decay exponent of means is explicitly given by the semicircle law; optimality saturates at the Gaussian kernel; and saturation occurs exactly for the standard Gaussian density alone. (Carleman optimal decay constant)

**中文。** 定义：Carleman 不等式为对数凹函数的加权不等式。猜想：平均值的衰减指数的最优常数由半圆律显式给出；且最优性在 Gauss 核饱和；且饱和情形恰为标准 Gauss 密度的独占。（Carleman 最优衰减常数）
