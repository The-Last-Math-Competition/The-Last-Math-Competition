# 00000007818

**English.** Definition: The Mori flip chain of Mbar_g: the number F(g) of flips in the MMP from Mbar_g to a minimal model. Conjecture: F(g) is the explicit linear form 2g-4; and the center of each flip is the image of a critical genus stratum curve, with flips displaying a period-3 law for g at least 24 and center-dimension sequences obeying a three-term recursion consistent with the threshold combinatorics of the Hilbert curve. (periodic Mori flip chain of Mbar_g)

**中文。** 定义：Mbar_g 的 Mori 翻转链：MMP 从 Mbar_g 到其极小模型模型的翻转次数 F(g)。猜想：F(g) = 2g-4 型显式值；且每个翻转的中心为临界亏格分拆曲线的像，翻转在 g≥24 后呈现周期律（周期 3），中心维数序列满足三项递推，与 Hilbert 曲线的阈值组合一致。
