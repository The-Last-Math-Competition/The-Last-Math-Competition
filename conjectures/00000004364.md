**English.** Definition: The Nikolaus-Scholze TC is the topological cyclic homology via the invariant-point formula. Conjecture: The TC homotopy of any p-complete ring is always reconstructed from the three pieces of data of THH coefficients, Frobenius, and Verschiebung, and the reconstruction formula can be executed effectively. (NS three-data reconstruction of TC)

**中文。** 定义：Nikolaus–Scholze 的 TC 指经不变点公式的拓扑循环同调。猜想：任意 p 完备环的 TC 同伦恒由 THH 系数与 Frobenius 及 Verschiebung 三段数据重建；且重建公式可有效执行。（NS 三数据 TC 重建）
