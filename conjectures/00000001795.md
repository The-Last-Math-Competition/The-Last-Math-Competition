# 00000001795

**English.** Conjecture: The convergence rate of approximation orders of the Néron–Severi classes of singular Hermitian metrics is polynomial under the topological Zariski topology (an approximation rate for singular metrics).

**中文。** 猜想：奇异 Hermitian 度量的 Neron–Severi 类的逼近序的收敛速率在拓扑 Zariski 下为多项式(奇异度量的逼近率)。
