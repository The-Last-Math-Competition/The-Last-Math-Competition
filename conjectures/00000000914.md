**English.** Definition: The comparison radius rc(A) measures the layered structure of projection comparison in the Cuntz semigroup. Conjecture: A Z-stable nuclear algebra of comparison radius zero satisfies projective cancellation; and the universality of cancellation is given by the function-system structure of the Cuntz semigroup. (comparison radius zero projective cancellation)

**中文。** 定义：比较半径 rc(A) 度量投影比较的 Cuntz 半群层结构。猜想：比较半径为零的 Z-稳定核代数满足射影消没；且消没的普适性由 Cuntz 半群的函数系统给出。（比较半径零射影消没）
