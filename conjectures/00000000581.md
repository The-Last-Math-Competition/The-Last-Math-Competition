# 00000000581

**English.** Definition: The Chow ring A^*(M) of a matroid. Conjecture (separation of realizability from HL): There exists a non-realizable matroid M whose Chow ring A^*(M) still satisfies the hard Lefschetz property (exhaustively verifiable among low-rank matroids).

**中文。** 猜想：定义:Chow 环 A^*(M)(拟阵)。猜想(实现性与 HL 的分离):存在非可实现拟阵 M 使 Chow 环 A^*(M) 仍满足 hard Lefschetz 性质(可在小秩拟阵中穷举验证)。
