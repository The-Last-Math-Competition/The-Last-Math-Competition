# 00000007873

**English.** Definition: Descent for noncommutative motives: the descent property of a localizing invariant E of small stable infinity-categories with respect to the cdh topology, expressed by descent homotopy fiber sequences E(sPerf_X) to E(Perf_{X_i}). Conjecture: K-theory (nonconnective K) satisfies cdh descent; and the K_{-1}=0 conjecture is equivalent to the lattice conjecture holding integrally (without rationalization) for all smooth varieties; together they give that K_0(cPerf_X) is a torsion-free integral invariant. (cdh descent and integral lattice conjecture)

**中文。** 定义：非交换动机的下降：非交换谱（localizing invariant of small stable ∞-categories）E 对 cdh 型拓扑的下降性质指 E(sPerf_X)→E(Perf_{X_i}) 的 Descent 同调纤维序列。猜想：K-理论（非连通 K）对 cdh 拓扑下降成立；且 K_{-1}=0 猜想的成立等价于格点预言（lattice conjecture）在整系数（非有理化）层面对一切光滑簇成立；两者共同给出 K_{0}(cPerf_X) 为无挠的整不变量。
