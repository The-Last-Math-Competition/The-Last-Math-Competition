# 00000008172

**English.** Definition: The Skolem bound for the search for integral points on elliptic curves: the search upper bound skolem(k) for integer solutions of the Mordell curve y^2 = x^3 + k. Conjecture: skolem(k) = O(log|k|) as a solution-count bound (a logarithmic solution-count law); the upper bound on the x-coordinates of solutions is |k|^{1+epsilon} (a polynomial improvement of Baker-type explicit bounds); and the density of k where Baker-type upper bounds are actually attained (bound saturation) is zero (a zero-density saturation law), the list of minimal saturating k being explicit with at most 10 elements (a finite saturation list). (Mordell Skolem logarithmic Baker saturation)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆曲线的 integral 点的搜索的 Skolem 界：Mordell 曲线 y²=x³+k 的整数解的搜索上界 skolem(k)。猜想：skolem(k) = O(log|k|) 的解数界（对数解数律）；且解的 x-坐标的上界为 |k|^{1+ε}（Baker 型显式界的多项式改进）；Baker 型上界的实际可达（界饱和）的 k 的密度为零（零密度饱和律），饱和例的最小 k 的列表为不超过 10 个的显式（饱和有限列表）。
