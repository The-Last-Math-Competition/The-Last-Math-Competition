**English.** Definition: The log canonical divisor volume spectrum of M_{0,n}: the volume extrema of the log canonical models M_{0,n}^{delta} and their threshold values delta_k. Conjecture: The Ehrhart counting of the Harvey-Lawson auxiliary polytope (the hive polytope of M_{0,n}) gives all delta thresholds, and the number of thresholds is exactly n-4; and the f-vector of each transition polytope is a log-concave sequence, the transition difference being a three-dimensional rotation count (12 or 20). (hive-threshold log canonical spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：M_{0,n} 的对数典范除子体积谱：log canonical 模型 M_{0,n}^{δ}（δ 为斜率参数）的体积极值与δ 的分界值 δ_k。猜想：M_{0,n} 的 Harvey–Lawson 辅助多面体（M_0,n 的 hive 多胞形）的 Ehrhart 计数给全部 δ 分界，分界数恰为 n-4；且每个跃迁的多面体的 f-向量满足对数凹序列，跃迁处的差为 3 维旋转数（12 或 20）。
