**English.** Definition: Canonical heights and periodic structure are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two maps with the same canonical height function but different periodic point structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit conjugate pair, with period difference controlled by the perturbation term. (same height function-different periodic conjugate separation)
