# 00000007757

**ADY-07 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Periodic-point spectral orthogonality of two maps f, g in Q(z) of degree at least 2 in a commuting semigroup concerns the intersection of their periodic sets P_f and P_g on P^1(Qbar). Conjecture: The intersection P_f intersect P_g is finite with cardinality controlled by a uniform function of deg f times deg g; and the intersection is empty when the maps have no common iterate, decidable by weighted identities between the preperiodic heights h_f and h_g. (periodic orthogonality criterion)

**中文。** 定义：两映射的周期点谱正交性：设 f,g∈Q(z) 为交换半群中不同的次数 ≥2 映射，P_f 与 P_g 为其在 P^1(Qbar) 上的周期点集。猜想：P_f ∩ P_g 为有限集，其基数被 deg f · deg g 的一致函数控制；且该交集在两映射无公共迭代时为空，此判定可由预周期高度 h_f 与 h_g 的加权恒等式验证。
