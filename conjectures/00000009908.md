# 00000009908

**GBR-28 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Weighted projective lines: quiver realizations of derived categories of coherent sheaves in the Geigle-Lenzing sense. Conjecture: A complete correspondence table exists between derived categories of singular weighted projective lines and derived categories of tubular type algebras; the table is exhausted by pairings of weight types with singularity types, with exactly four supersingular exceptional members. (singular weighted line correspondence table)

**中文。** 定义：加权射影直线：Geigle–Lenzing 的凝聚层导范畴的箭图实现。猜想：奇异加权射影直线的导范畴与管型代数的导范畴的完全对应表存在；表由权型与奇点型的配对穷尽且配对的例外成员恰为四个超奇异情形。（奇异加权直线对应表）
