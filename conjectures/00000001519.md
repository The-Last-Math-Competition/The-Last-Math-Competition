# 00000001519

**English.** Definition: The Farrell–Jones conjecture asserts that the K/L-theory of group rings is computed by virtual-subgroup assembly. Conjecture: Out(F_n) satisfies the K-theoretic part of the FJ conjecture for all n; the cases n ≤ 4 are completed by explicit verification on their cell models, and n ≥ 5 follows by relative hyperbolicity transfer.

**中文。** 定义：Farrell–Jones 猜想断言群环的 K/L-理论由虚拟子环装配计算。猜想：Out(F_n) 满足 FJ 猜想的 K-理论部分(一切 n);n≤4 的情形可由其胞腔模型的显式核对完成,n≥5 由相对超双曲性传递。
