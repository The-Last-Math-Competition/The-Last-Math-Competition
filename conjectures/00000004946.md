# 00000004946

**English.** Definition: The Goodwillie tower is the polynomial approximation tower of the identity functor. Conjecture: Its connectivity formula is an explicit jumping law between layers, optimal and attained by the connectivity of free generalized Lie algebras. (optimal connectivity jumping law of Goodwillie)

**中文。** 定义：Goodwillie 塔指恒等函子的多项式逼近塔。猜想：其连通度公式为层间的显式跳变律,律由自由广义李代数的连通性达到最优。（Goodwillie 连通跳最优律）
