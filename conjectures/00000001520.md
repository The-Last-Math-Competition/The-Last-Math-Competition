# 00000001520

**English.** Definition: The Whitehead group Wh(Γ) = K₁(ZΓ)^{±}/±Γ. Conjecture: For Gromov-hyperbolic groups Γ, Wh(Γ) = 0 (torsion-free and trivial); the vanishing of the 2-torsion part is characterized separately by the amenability criterion of the boundary action, without the full FJ conjecture.

**中文。** 定义：Whitehead 群 Wh(Γ)=K₁(ZΓ)^{±}/±Γ。猜想：对 Gromov-双曲群 Γ,Wh(Γ)=0(挠自由且平凡);2-挠部分的消失由边界作用的顺从性判据单独刻画,不需全 FJ。
