# 00000000603

**English.** Definition: The specialization P_K(1,z) of the HOMFLY polynomial P_K(a,z) is the Conway polynomial ∇_K(z). Conjecture: For the torus knot T(p,q), the coefficient sequence of ∇(z) is nonnegative, has no internal zeros, and is unimodal on the logarithmic scale; the peak position is ⌊(p−1)(q−1)/4⌋.

**中文。** 定义：HOMFLY 多项式 P_K(a,z) 的特殊化 P_K(1,z) 即 Conway 多项式 ∇_K(z)。猜想：对 torus 结 T(p,q),∇(z) 的系数序列非负、无内部零,且在对数尺度下构成单峰序列;峰的位置为 ⌊(p−1)(q−1)/4⌋。
