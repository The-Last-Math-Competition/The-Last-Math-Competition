# 00000006538

**English.** Definition: The naturality domain of the homomorphism is the closed structure of commutative diagrams. Conjecture: The commutative diagrams close and the domain is complete; the check of completeness is functoriality; and the criterion of domain completeness is the functorial pentagon identity. (closed naturality domain of the homomorphism)

**中文。** 定义：同态的自然性域为交换图封闭结构。猜想：交换图封闭且域完全；且完全性的检验为函子性；且域完全性的判据为函子五角恒等。（同态自然性域封闭）
