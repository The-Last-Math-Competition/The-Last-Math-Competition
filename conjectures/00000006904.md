# 00000006904

**English.** Definition: The proof window of explicit rigidity is the range of degree constraints. Conjecture: Shift-elimination methods give the endpoints of the proof window and the window ends are explicit functions of the degree; and the expansion of the window implies a breakthrough of the lower bound. (shift-elimination endpoints of the proof window)

**中文。** 定义：显式刚性的证明窗为度数约束范围。猜想：移位消没法给出证明窗的端点且窗端为度数的显式函数；且窗口的扩张蕴含下界突破。（移位消没证明窗端点）
