# 00000002221

**English.** Definition: The constructible topology on Spec R. Conjecture: The cardinality of the Stone space of the Booleanization (complemented-closure algebra) of the spectrum is at most 2^{2^{d}} with d = dim, explicitly (Booleanization cardinality).

**中文。** 定义：Spec R 的 constructible 拓扑。猜想：谱的布尔化(补闭包代数)的 Stone 空间的基数 ≤ 2^{2^{d}} 的 d = dim 的显式(布尔化基数)。
