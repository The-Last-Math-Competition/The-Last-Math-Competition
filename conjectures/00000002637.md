**English.** Definition: The spectral connection of Jacobi forms with elliptic cohomology: the topological realization. Conjecture: The Jacobi realization of the coefficient sheaf of elliptic cohomology: the Jacobi lift of the Witten genus is a weak Jacobi form of index 1, and the modularity deficit of the lift is compensated explicitly by the analytic Eichler integral of the μ function. (elliptic cohomology Jacobi realization)
