**English.** Definition: Well-posedness: continuous dependence of solutions. Conjecture: The metric of well-posedness: the sufficiency of continuity of solution maps is strong monotonicity, with the modulus of continuity the Lipschitz constant. (well-posed strong monotone Lipschitz continuity sufficient modulus)

**中文。** 定义：适定性：解的连续依赖。猜想：适定的度量：解映射的连续的充分为强单调且连续的模为 Lipschitz 常数。（适定强单调 Lipschitz 连续充分模）
