# 00000007897

**English.** Definition: The Furburg-type inequality for categorical entropy: the convexity in the weight parameter t of the categorical entropy h_t(F) of an exact autoequivalence F. Conjecture: h_t(F) is a convex function of t whose right derivative at t = 0 equals the Gromov-Yomdin type spectral radius log rho(F*); the equality cases (linear entropy) are completely classified as abelian combinations of Serre-functor shifts and spherical twists; and the categories with linear entropy are exactly the Perf of derived discrete varieties (classification rigidity), giving a complete geometric characterization of entropy linearity. (categorical entropy linearity classification)

**中文。** 定义：范畴熵的 Furberg 型不等式：自洽（exact）自等价 F 的范畴熵 h_t(F) 关于权参数 t 的凸性。猜想：h_t(F) 为 t 的凸函数且在 t=0 处的右导数等于 F 的 Gromov–Yomdin 型谱半径 log ρ(F*)；等号情形（线性熵）完全分类为 F 为 Serre 函子平移或球扭转的阿贝尔组合；且线性情形的范畴恰为导出离散簇的 Perf（分类刚性），给出熵线性性的几何完全刻画。
