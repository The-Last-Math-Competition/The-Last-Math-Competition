# 00000009591

**English.** Definition: The structure of the Willmore energy spectrum after the Willmore conjecture (Marques-Neves proved torus Willmore energy at least 2 pi squared). Conjecture: The Willmore energy of closed surfaces takes only finitely many critical values between 2 pi squared and 8 pi; Willmore critical surfaces below 8 pi form a complete list of genus at most 1; and the second gap of the energy spectrum is an explicit irrational multiple of 2 pi squared. (Willmore spectrum finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：Willmore 猜想（Marques–Neves 已证：环面 Willmore 能量 ≥ 2π²）之后的 Willmore 能量谱结构。猜想：闭曲面的 Willmore 能量在 2π² 与 8π 之间仅含有限多个临界值；8π 以下的 Willmore 临界曲面为亏格 ≤1 的完全列表，能量谱的第二间隙为 2π² 的显式无理倍数。（Willmore 谱有限性）
