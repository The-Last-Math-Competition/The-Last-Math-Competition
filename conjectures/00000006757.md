**English.** Definition: The triangular extension is the poset structure of triangulated categories. Conjecture: The braid-subgroup chain gives the structure and the structure is complete; the order of the chain is inclusion; and the inclusion order of the chain agrees with the braid positive order. (braid-subgroup chain structure of triangle extensions)

**中文。** 定义：三角推广为三角范畴的偏序结构。猜想：辫群子格链给出结构且结构完全；且链的序为包含；且链的包含序与辫正序一致。（三角辫群子格链结构）
