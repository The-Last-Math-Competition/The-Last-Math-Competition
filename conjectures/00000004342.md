# 00000004342

**FP2-12 ◆** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: Nelson hypercontractivity is the L^p to L^q boundedness of the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup. Conjecture: The optimal hypercontractivity constant is a rational function of p and q independent of dimension, attained on Wiener measure and normalized at p = q. (optimal constant function of hypercontractivity)

**中文。** 定义：Nelson 超有界性指 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 半群的 L^p 到 L^q 有界性。猜想：最优超有界常数为与维数无关的 p、q 有理函数,在维纳测度上达到且在 p 等于 q 处归一。（超有界最优常数函数）
