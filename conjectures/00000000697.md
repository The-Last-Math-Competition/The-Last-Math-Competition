# 00000000697

**English.** Definition: The exceptional slopes of a hyperbolic knot are those whose Dehn surgery outcomes are non-hyperbolic. Conjecture: The maximum possible number of exceptional slopes is 10, attained by an explicit family of braided knots with minimal cusp area; no hyperbolic knot has 11 exceptional slopes.

**中文。** 定义：双曲纽结的例外坡指 Dehn 手术结果非双曲的斜率。猜想：例外坡的最大可能数为 10,由 cusp 面积极小的编织纽结的显式族达到;不存在具 11 个例外坡的双曲纽结。
