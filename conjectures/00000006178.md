# 00000006178

**English.** Definition: The matrix-tree theorem gives the spanning-tree count by Laplacian determinants, for doubly periodic lattice graphs. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit regularized product of doubly periodic lattice points, a closed special value for the triangular lattice; the tree-count exponent of the square flat torus converges to 4G/pi, with G the Catalan constant; and the divergence term of the regularization is logarithmic, with coefficient the lattice point count. (regularized special-value conversion of doubly periodic lattices)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵树定理：生成树数的拉普拉斯行列式；双周期格图。猜想：换算常数为双周期格点的正则化乘积显式且三角格时为闭式特殊值；方格环面的树数指数收敛于 4G/π（G 为 Catalan 常数）；正则化的发散项为对数型且系数为格点数。（双周期正则化特殊值换算）
