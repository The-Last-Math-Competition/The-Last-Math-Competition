# 00000000216

**English.** Definition: The mod-3 sequence of the Euler zigzag numbers. Conjecture: This sequence is 3-automatic with an explicit number of automaton states; and the automaticity is settled through the denominator of the generating function. (zigzag generating function denominator mod 3 automaticity)

**中文。** 定义：Euler zigzag 数的模三序列。猜想：该序列为三自动的且自动机的状态数为显式；且自动的核为母函数分母。（zigzag 母函数分母模三自动）
