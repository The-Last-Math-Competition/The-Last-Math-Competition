**SQ-27 ★** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: The Beatty sequence a_n = ⌊πn⌋ (π the circle constant). Conjecture: The count of primes among a_n is (N/log N)·(1 + c/π² + o(1)) with c explicit; the control of the second-order term depends only on the irrationality measure μ(π) = 2, and any degeneration of the measure directly reflects in a counting deviation. (Beatty prime second-order term)

**中文。** 定义：Beatty 序列 a_n=⌊πn⌋（π 为圆周率）。猜想：a_n 中的素数计数为 (N/log N)·(1+c/π²+o(1)) 且修正常数 c 显式；二阶项的控制仅依赖 π 的无理性度量 μ(π)=2，度量的任何退化直接反映为计数偏差。（Beatty 素数二阶项）
