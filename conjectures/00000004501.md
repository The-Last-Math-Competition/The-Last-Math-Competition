# 00000004501

**English.** Definition: A Barr covering is a surjective geometric morphism from a topos onto a Boolean topos. Conjecture: A covering sufficient for vector bundle decomposition is a tower of two-point categories, with tower height the logarithm of the bundle rank, and this height is optimal and cannot be lowered. (Barr covering rank-logarithmic tower height)

**中文。** 定义：Barr 覆盖指拓扑斯到布尔拓扑斯的满几何态射覆盖。猜想：使向量丛分解的充分覆盖为二点范畴塔,塔高为丛秩的对数,该高度最优不可降。（Barr 覆盖秩对数塔高）
