**SP-12 ★** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: The matching spectrum of a quantum graph is the part of its spectrum determined by the multiset of edge lengths. Conjecture: There exist star-graph pairs with at most 4 edges whose edge multisets differ but full spectra coincide (the minimal scale of a quantum-graph matching-spectrum counterexample); star graphs with at most 3 edges have edge lengths uniquely determined by the spectrum.

**中文。** 定义：量子图的匹配谱指由其边长的多重集部分决定的谱。猜想：存在边数 ≤4 的星图对,其边长多重集不同但全谱相同(量子图匹配谱反例的最小规模);且边数 ≤3 的星图由谱唯一确定边长。
