**English.** Definition: Geometric quantization: the representation realization of symplectic manifolds. Conjecture: Invariance of polarization: the isomorphism of quantizations under different real polarizations is given by cobordism, with the phase of the isomorphism the Chern–Simons correction of the metric. (polarization cobordism isomorphism phase correction)
