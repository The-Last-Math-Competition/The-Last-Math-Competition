# 00000008682

**English.** Definition: Benamou-Brenier dynamic programming: the minimization of the continuity equation for the W_2 distance. Conjecture: The optimal velocity field of the dynamic program has W^{1,infty} (Lipschitz) regularity (a Lipschitz law); the Lipschitz exception (non-Lipschitz fields) occurs for initial data with non-strictly-convex support (a nonconvex exception law); the field is unique for generic initial data (a uniqueness law); and the bifurcation of multivalued fields occurs first in dimension 2 (a two-dimensional bifurcation law). (Benamou-Brenier Lipschitz uniqueness bifurcation)

**中文。** 定义：Benamou–Brenier 动态规划：W₂ 距离的连续性方程的极小化。猜想：动态规划的极小场的正则性（最优速度场的 Sobolev 正则性）的 W^{1,∞} 的（Lipschitz 律）；且 Lipschitz 的例外（非 Lipschitz 场）的初值为非严格凸支撑的（非凸例外律）；场的唯一性（一般初值的唯一）的唯一（唯一律）；唯一的高维（多值场）的分叉（分叉律）的分叉维度为 2 的（二维分叉律）。
