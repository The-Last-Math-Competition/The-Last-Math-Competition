**English.** Definition: A braid representation is the matrix action of the braid group on a module category. Conjecture: The braid image is finite if and only if all twist orders are finite and the fusion matrices are uniformly power-bounded, and the criterion is explicit and verifiable in finitely many steps. (twist criterion for finite braid images)

**中文。** 定义：辫表示指模范畴的辫群矩阵作用。猜想：辫像有限当且仅当全体旋量阶有限且融合矩阵一致幂零有界,判据显式且有限步可验。（辫像有限旋量判据）
