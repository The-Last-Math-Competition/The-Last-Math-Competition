# 00000008583

**English.** Definition: The Hoffman chromatic lower bound for infinite graphs: the extension to infinite graphs of the chromatic lower bound from the smallest eigenvalue and maximal degree. Conjecture: The Hoffman type lower bound for the chromatic number of infinite graphs holds (a lower bound law); saturation is attained by affine plane graphs (an affine plane law); graph families of higher-dimensional affine geometry attain asymptotic saturation (a high-dimensional saturation law); and the second-largest eigenvalue of saturated graphs is an explicit algebraic number (a second-eigenvalue law). (infinite Hoffman affine saturation eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：无限图的 Hoffman 色数下界：由最小本征值与最大度给出的色数下界在无限图的推广。猜想：无限图的色数的 Hoffman 型下界成立（下界律）；且下界的饱和由仿射平面图取得（仿射平面律）；高维仿射几何的图族达到渐近饱和（高维饱和律）；饱和图的谱的二大本征值为显式代数数（二大本征值律）。
