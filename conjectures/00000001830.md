# 00000001830

**English.** Definition: The Artin–Tate formula (the relation between Brauer group order and period-index). Conjecture: The 2-part of the Artin–Tate constant of a K3 surface is an explicit table: the asymptotic formula for the order of Sha(X)[2^∞] is corrected by the Ekedahl construction (an explicit classification of the 2-part).

**中文。** 定义：Artin–Tate 公式(Brauer 群阶与周期指数的关系)。猜想：K3 的 Artin–Tate 常数的 2-部分为显式表:Sha(X)[2^∞] 的阶的渐近公式由 Ekedahl 造法修正(2-部分的显式分类)。
