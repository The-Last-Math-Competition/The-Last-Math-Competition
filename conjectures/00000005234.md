# 00000005234

**MS3-04 ★** — source: 160_代数几何V_模空间.md

**English.** Definition: The effective cone is the positive closed cone of effective divisor classes. Conjecture: The effective-cone boundary of the genus-four curve moduli space is spanned by an explicit family of cubic divisor curves, with boundary equation a combination of variety geometry; the extreme rays of the cone are finite, each given by a codimension-one locus; and the closure of the boundary rational points is exactly the whole boundary of the cone. (cubic boundary of the genus-four effective cone)

**中文。** 定义：有效锥：有效除子类的正闭锥。猜想：亏格四曲线模空间的有效锥边界由显式三次曲线除子族张成且边界方程为簇几何的组合式；锥的极端射为有限条且每条由余维一轨迹给出；边界有理点的闭包恰为锥的整个边界。（亏格四有效锥三次边界）
