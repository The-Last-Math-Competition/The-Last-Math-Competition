**English.** Definition: Transitive closure logic is first-order logic plus the transitive closure operator. Conjecture: Its separation from first-order is given by an explicit path-jumping graph family, the separating sentence is connectivity, the first-order side needs superpolynomial size, and the family is logarithmically optimal. (logarithmic separation family of transitive closure versus first-order)
