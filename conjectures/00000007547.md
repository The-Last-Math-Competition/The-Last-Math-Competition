# 00000007547

**FF3-07 ★** — source: 237_自由群因子II.md

**English.** Definition: Free products with the correction of free-product decomposition of operators. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the correction of free decomposition and the correction term of finite kernels is torsion; the term spectrum is finite with spectral length the rank of the factor; and the correction vanishes at torsion-free kernels. (torsion-corrected free-decomposition conversion)

**中文。** 定义：自由积：算子的自由积分解修正。猜想：换算常数为自由分解的修正且有限核的修正项为挠；项谱有限且谱的长度为因子的秩；修正的消没在无挠核处。（自由修正换算常数结构）
