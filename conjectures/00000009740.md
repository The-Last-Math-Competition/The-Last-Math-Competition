# 00000009740

**English.** Definition: Finite-size scaling of random k-SAT solving times: universal exponents of the transition window. Conjecture: The solving-time window width of random 3-SAT near the saturation density shrinks as n^{-1/3}; the scaling exponent 1/3 agrees with the KPZ class of critical phenomena and is universal over all DPLL-type algorithms. (SAT finite-size scaling)

**中文。** 定义：随机 k-SAT 求解时间的有限尺寸标度：相变窗口的普适指数。猜想：随机 3-SAT 在饱和密度附近的求解时间窗口宽度按 n^{−1/3} 收缩；标度指数 1/3 与临界现象的 KPZ 类一致且对一切 DPLL 型算法普适。（SAT 有限尺寸标度）
