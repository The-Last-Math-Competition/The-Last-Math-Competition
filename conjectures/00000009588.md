# 00000009588

**English.** Definition: The a priori curvature estimate for embedded minimal hypersurfaces: a conjectural uniform bound on ||A||^2 depending on Ricci lower bounds and volume non-collapsedness. Conjecture: In closed manifolds with Ricci at least -(n-1) and volume at least v, all embedded minimal hypersurfaces satisfy ||A||^2 <= C(n,v); the volume hypothesis cannot be removed, with degenerate counterexamples given by neck-pinch chain sequences. (embedded a priori curvature estimate)

**中文。** 定义：嵌入极小超曲面的先验曲率估计：||A||² 的一致界依赖 Ricci 下界与体积非塌缩的猜想。猜想：Ricci ≥ −(n−1) 且体积 ≥ v 的闭流形中一切嵌入极小超曲面满足 ||A||² ≤ C(n,v)；体积假设不可去，由 neck-pinch 链序列给出退化反例。（嵌入先验曲率估计）
