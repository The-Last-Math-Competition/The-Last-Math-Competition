**MSH-08 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: uniform bound dependence Ricci lower bound of a priori curvature estimate: ||A||² of embedding minimal hypersurface and conjecture of volume non- collapse. Conjecture: Ricci ≥ −(n−1) and closed manifold in all embedding minimal hypersurface of volume ≥ v satisfies ||A||² ≤ C(n,v); volume hypothesis not, by neck-pinch sequence gives degenerate counterexample. (embedding a priori curvature estimate)

**中文。** 定义：嵌入极小超曲面的先验曲率估计：||A||² 的一致界依赖 Ricci 下界与体积非塌缩的猜想。猜想：Ricci ≥ −(n−1) 且体积 ≥ v 的闭流形中一切嵌入极小超曲面满足 ||A||² ≤ C(n,v)；体积假设不可去，由 neck-pinch 链序列给出退化反例。（嵌入先验曲率估计）
