# 00000009947

**MRG-07 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Arithmeticity: lattices of rank at least two are arithmetic (Margulis); intrinsic criteria for rank-one arithmetic lattices are unknown. Conjecture: Arithmeticity of rank-one lattices is decided by spectral invariants of traces: two lattices are isospectral if and only if commensurable, and the isospectrality classes of arithmetic lattices are finite in count; the decision algorithm is an explicit comparison of trace formulas. (rank-one arithmetic spectral criterion)

**中文。** 定义：算术性：秩至少二的格为算术（Margulis）；秩一算术格的内在判据未知。猜想：秩一格的算术性由迹的谱不变量判别：两个格同谱当且仅当同痕且算术格的同谱类的计数有限；判别算法为迹公式的显式比较。（秩一算术谱判据）
