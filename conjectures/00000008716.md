**MBL-06 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: Level statistics: the distribution of adjacent gap ratios. Conjecture: The gap ratio distribution in the MBL phase always converges to Poisson type; the thermal phase converges to Wigner-Dyson with the transition region of intermediate half-Poisson statistics, changing continuously with parameters. (gap ratio Poisson-Wigner-Dyson transition)

**中文。** 定义：能级统计指相邻能隙比的分布。猜想：MBL 相的能隙比分布恒收敛到 Poisson 型；热相收敛到 Wigner-Dyson 且过渡区为半 Poisson 中间统计，转变随参数连续。（能隙比 Poisson-WD 过渡）
