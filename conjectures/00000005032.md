**English.** Definition: Counting first-order is the first-order extension with cardinality quantifiers. Conjecture: Its separation from first-order is always given by twin-clique parity sentences, with the counting side polynomially expressible and the first-order side needing superpolynomial size. (twin-clique separation of counting first-order)
