**EG3-02 ◆** — source: 194_遍历论III_熵公式.md

**English.** Definition: The conditional-entropy infimum is the minimal conditional entropy of generating partitions. Conjecture: In the countably generated case the value equals the classical entropy; the deficit order is logarithmic; and the kernel of the logarithmic deficit is the repeated-counting correction. (logarithmic deficit order of countable generation)

**中文。** 定义：条件熵下确界为生成分割的最小条件熵。猜想：计数生成情形的值等于经典熵；且亏损阶为对数；且对数亏损的核为重复计数修正。（计数生成亏损对数阶）
