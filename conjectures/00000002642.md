# 00000002642

**English.** Definition: CM values of Jacobi forms: special values at CM points (τ,z). Conjecture: Algebraicity of CM values: the values of all integral Jacobi forms at CM points are algebraic numbers generating a number field of degree the class number times the number of index factors; the Galois orbits of the norms follow a Sato–Tate type distribution. (Jacobi CM value algebraicity)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式的 CM 值：在 CM 点 (τ,z) 的特殊取值。猜想：CM 值的代数性：一切整 Jacobi 形式在 CM 点的值为代数数且生成的数域次数为类数乘指标因子数；值的范数的 Galois 轨迹为 Sato–Tate 型分布。（Jacobi CM 值代数性）
