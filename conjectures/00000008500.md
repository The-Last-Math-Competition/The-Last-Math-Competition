# 00000008500

**English.** Definition: The finite-code isomorphism criterion for Bernoulli shifts under Ornstein's d-bar distance. Conjecture: Finite-code isomorphism holds if and only if the entropies are equal (entropy as a complete invariant law); the d-bar distance between K but non-Bernoulli systems and the Bernoulli class has a positive lower bound (a positive lower bound law); the explicit lower bound is a linear expression in the entropy difference (an entropy difference linear law); and the tightness of the lower bound is attained by explicit couplings of two-entropy systems (a two-entropy tight system law). (Ornstein finite-code entropy complete invariant)

**中文。** 定义：Ornstein 的 d̄ 距离下 Bernoulli 移位的有限码同构判据。猜想：有限码同构当且仅当熵相等（熵完全不变量律）；K 但非 Bernoulli 的系统与 Bernoulli 类的 d̄ 距离有正下界（正下界律）；下界的显式为熵差的线性式（熵差线性律）；下界的紧性由双熵系统的显式耦合取得（双紧系统律）。
