# 00000003473

**GA3-13 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: Flow-irreducible graphs have no nontrivial flow-polynomial factors and are the obstruction structure of Tutte's three-flow conjecture. Conjecture: A graph is flow-irreducible if and only if the compression isomorphism classes of its two-edge cuts and three-vertex cuts form a minimal family; the joint constraints of girth and degree of irreducible graphs are given by the complete list of three-flow-critical configurations. (flow irreducibility criterion)

**中文。** 定义：流不可约图为无非平凡流多项式因子的图，为 Tutte 三流猜想的阻碍结构。猜想：图流不可约当且仅当其二边割与三顶割的压缩同构类为极小族；不可约图的围长与度的联合约束由三流临界构型的完全列表给出。（流不可约判据）
