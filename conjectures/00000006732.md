# 00000006732

**English.** Definition: The dimension-spectrum structure of the complement formula. Conjecture: The vanishing equations give the dimension spectrum and the spectrum is finite; the threshold of finiteness is the reflection-sequence length; and the generators of the spectrum are given explicitly by the reflection vectors. (vanishing-equation dimension spectrum of complements)
