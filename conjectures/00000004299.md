**English.** Definition: The pure-injective hull is the minimal pure-injective extension. Conjecture: The quotient dimension spectrum of the hull over the group is exactly the power-of-2 lattice, the exponents given explicitly by the rank difference, and the spectrum becomes the full lattice as the rank grows. (power-of-2 quotient dimension spectrum of hulls)
