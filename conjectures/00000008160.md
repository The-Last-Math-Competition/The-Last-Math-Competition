**English.** Definition: The Balog-Niedermaier ultraviolet-infrared bridge: the integrability loss constant integr_loss of the realization of integrable boundary conditions under the nonperturbative RG. Conjecture: integr_loss (the loss rate of the RG evolution of integrable functionals) is strictly quadratic (a quadratic loss law); the list of boundary conditions with zero loss (complete preservation of integrability) is of reflection type (the complete list: reflection, periodic, and Neumann); the minimal loss constant outside the list (breaking boundaries) is c_min = 1/24 (a 1/24 minimal loss, agreeing with the Virasoro central charge deficit); and the loss constants are quantized as integer multiples of 1/(12m) (a loss quantization law). (Balog-Niedermaier quadratic loss 1/24)

**中文。** 定义：Balog–Niedermaier 的紫外-红外桥：非微扰 RG 的可积边界条件的实现的可积性损失常数 integr_loss。猜想：integr_loss（可积泛函的 RG 演化的损失率）为严格二次（二次损失律）；且损失的零点（完全可积保持）的边界条件的列表为反射型（完全列表：反射、周期、Neumann 三类）；三列表之外的边界（破坏边界）的损失的最小常数 c_min = 1/24（1/24 最小损失，与 Virasoro 的 c-差额一致）；损失常数的量子化为 1/(12m) 的整数倍（损失量子化律）。
