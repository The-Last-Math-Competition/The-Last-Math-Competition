# 00000009907

**English.** Definition: Periodicity laws of AR sequences: the period structure of almost-split sequences of self-injective algebras. Conjecture: The syzygy period of AR sequences of a self-injective algebra is determined by the sign of the determinant of the Cartan matrix: period 1 when the determinant is +1, period 2 when -1; the degenerate zero-determinant case branches by the dimension of the kernel. (AR period Cartan criterion)

**中文。** 定义：AR 序列的周期律：自内射代数的几乎分裂序列的周期结构。猜想：自内射代数的 AR 序列的 syzygy 周期由 Cartan 矩阵的行列式的符号决定：行列式为正一时周期为 1，为负一时周期为 2；退化行列式零的情形按核的维数分叉。（AR 周期 Cartan 判据）
