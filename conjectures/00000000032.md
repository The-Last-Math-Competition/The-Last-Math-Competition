# 00000000032

**AC-06 ★** — source: 02_组合数论与加性组合.md

**English.** Conjecture: The primes contain Hilbert cubes a₀ + Σ_{i∈S} d_i (S ranging over all subsets) of dimension tending to infinity; moreover, the largest dimension attained among primes in [1,N] is Θ(log log N).

**中文。** 猜想：素数集合包含维数趋于无穷的 Hilbert 方块 a₀ + Σ_{i∈S} d_i(S 取遍子集);且 [1,N] 内素数中最大维数为 Θ(log log N)。
