**English.** Definition: Lambert series: generating functions of divisor sums. Conjecture: The identity network of Lambert: the coefficients of the Lambert expansion of Eisenstein series are divisor functions, with the closure of the expansion the Ramanujan-type modular equations. (Lambert Ramanujan modular equation closure)
