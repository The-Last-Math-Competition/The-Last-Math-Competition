# 00000008202

**English.** Definition: The geometric index of polarizations of coadjoint orbits: the Maslov index mas(O) of the Lagrangian distribution of a polarization (maximal isotropic subalgebra) of an orbit O of a real nilpotent algebra g. Conjecture: mas(O) is an integral lattice point of the symplectic form of the orbit (an integral lattice law); the cancellation of the Maslov class (trivialization of the monodromy of the orbit) holds if and only if G is of string type (a string trivialization criterion); and the sum of the indices (over all minimal orbits) equals the count of the discrete series of the unitary dual (a count-index sum correspondence). (Maslov integral lattice string trivialization)

**中文。** 定义：余伴随轨道的极化子的几何指标：实幂零代数 g 的轨道 O 的极化（极大迷向子代数）的 Lagrange 分布的 Maslov 指标 mas(O)。猜想：mas(O) 为轨道的辛形式的整格点（整格点律）；且 Maslov 类的消除（轨道的单值性平凡化）当且仅当 G 为 string 类型（string 平凡化判据）；指标的和（一切极小轨道的和）= Ĝ 的离散系列的计数（计数-指标和对应）。
