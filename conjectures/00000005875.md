**FE2-15 ★** — source: 181_有限元II.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal marking parameter is the efficiency threshold of the selection. Conjecture: The optimal formula is the explicit ratio of total to marked error, the infimum is attained at global marking, and the parameter threshold is one half. (global infimum half-parameter threshold of marking)

**中文。** 定义：最优标记参数指挑选的效率阈值。猜想：最优公式为总误差与标记误差的显式比,下确界在全局标记且参数阈为半。（标记全局下确界半参数阈）
