**English.** Definition: Randomized trace estimation: Hutchinson's trace. Conjecture: The variance of Hutchinson is 2(||A||_F^2 - sum a_ii^2) (a variance law); the variance reduction of Hutch++ is a sqrt factor (a root factor law); optimal probing combinations exist (a combination law); and probing has a lower bound (a bound law). (Hutchinson variance Hutch++ probing bound)

**中文。** 定义：随机迹估计：Hutchinson 的迹。猜想：Hutchinson 的方差为 2(||A||_F²-Σa_ii²) 的（方差律）；且方差的减少（Hutch++ 的）的 √ 因子（根因子律）；最优探测（probing）的组合（组合律）；组合的下界（探测的）的（界律）。
