# 00000001667

**GT-07 ★** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: The game chromatic number (Dinski–Zhu activation coloring). Conjecture: The tight value of the activation chromatic number of planar graphs is at most 8 (planar activation coloring); and the tight value eight is attained by the activation pairing of Eulerian triangulations.

**中文。** 定义：染色博弈数(Dinski–Zhu 活动染色)。猜想：平面图的活动染色数 ≤ 8 的紧值确定(平面活动染色)；且紧值八由 Euler 三角剖分的活动对达到。
