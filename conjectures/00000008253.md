# 00000008253

**EGT-23 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: Complementarity pricing for multiple items: the pricing complexity comp_price of the complementarity parameter gamma of combinatorial valuations. Conjecture: comp_price (the revenue-welfare gap under complementarity) is the product gamma log gamma (a gamma-log product law); the failure of approximation guarantees under strong complementarity (gamma to n, a no-guarantee threshold) starts at gamma at least n/2 (a half-count failure threshold); and the explicit gain of bundling sales under complementarity is the logarithm of the matching number of the complementarity graph (a matching-gain formula). (complementarity gamma-log matching bundling gain)

**中文。** 定义：多物品的互补性定价：组合估价的互补参数 γ 的定价复杂度 comp_price。猜想：comp_price（互补性下的收入-福利差距）= γ·log γ 的乘积（γ-log 乘积律）；且强互补（γ → n）的近似保障的失效（无保障阈）为 γ ≥ n/2（半数失效阈）；互补下的捆绑销售的增益的显式为互补图的匹配数的对数（匹配-增益公式）。
