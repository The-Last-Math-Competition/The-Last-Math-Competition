# 00000003102

**English.** Definition: Group boundaries: the topological structure of the compactification remainder of hyperbolic groups. Conjecture: The dimension rigidity of boundaries: the boundary dimension of a hyperbolic group and the divergence exponent satisfy a dimension-sum formula, with the exception being the 1-dimensional boundary of surface groups. (boundary dimension-sum formula exception)
