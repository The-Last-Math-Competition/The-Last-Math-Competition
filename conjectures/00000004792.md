**English.** Definition: The quantum cohomology of a three-dimensional Fano is the eigenvalue spectrum of the quantum deformation ring of the cohomology. Conjecture: The eigenvalues are computed explicitly by a Gamma convergence criterion of Zagier type and the spectral moduli are algebraic numbers; the quantum spectrum of a prime Fano is finite with length equal to the dimension of the cohomology; the Mahler measure of the spectral moduli has lower bound 1, attained by the spectrum of projective space. (Gamma criterion of the Fano quantum spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：三维 Fano 的量子上同调：上同调的量子形变环的本征值谱。猜想：本征值由 Gamma 收敛判据（Zagier 型）显式计算且谱模为代数数；素 Fano 的量子谱有限且谱的长等于上同调的维数；谱模的 Mahler 测度有下界 1 且下界由射影空间的谱达到。（Fano 量子谱 Gamma 判据）
