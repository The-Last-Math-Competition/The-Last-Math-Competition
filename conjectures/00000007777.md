# 00000007777

**English.** Definition: Gap distribution of periodic-point heights: m_n = min of canonical heights over points of exact period n. Conjecture: For every non-PCF f one has the hyperbolic inequality m_n >= c d^n/n with c depending only on f; and the bound is optimal, given by the asymptotics of partial sums of the geometric Bottcher series, with approximating sequences realized by the smallest roots of dynatomic special-value polynomials. (hyperbolic height gap inequality)

**中文。** 定义：周期点高度的间隙分布：f 的恰周期 n 点的极小高度 m_n=min{hat h(P): P 为恰周期 n 点}。猜想：m_n 满足双曲不等式 m_n ≥ c·d^n/n 对一切非 PCF 的 f 成立，c 为仅依赖 f 的常数；且该界为最优，由 Böttcher 坐标几何级数的部分和的渐近给出，等号逼近序列由一列 dynatomic 特值多项式的最小根实现。
