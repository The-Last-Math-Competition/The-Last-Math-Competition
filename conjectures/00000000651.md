# 00000000651

**English.** Definition: Writhe minimization. Conjecture: Beyond the minimality of crossing number for positive knots (all-positive diagrams), there exist semi-positive knots whose minimal crossing diagram is not positive (existence of the failure of positivity rigidity).

**中文。** 定义：writhe 极小化。猜想：正纽结(全正图)的交叉数极小性外,存在半正纽结使其最小交叉图非正(正性刚性失败的存在)。
