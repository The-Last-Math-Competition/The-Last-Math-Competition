# 00000006975

**NF2-05 ★** — source: 218_非线性傅里叶分析.md

**English.** Definition: Inversion with the nonlinear inverse Fourier transform. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the regularized spectrum and the error law is Holder type; the exponent of the law is the regularity of the potential; and the regularization window is the reciprocal frequency. (Holder-law conversion of regularized inversion)

**中文。** 定义：反演：非线性傅里叶逆变换。猜想：换算常数为正则化谱的显式且误差律为 Holder 型；律的指数为位势的正则性；正则化的窗为频率倒数。（反演正则赫尔德律换算常数）
