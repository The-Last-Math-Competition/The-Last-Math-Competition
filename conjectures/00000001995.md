**English.** Definition: Hilbert's sixth problem (the derivation of kinetics from mechanics) and Lanford's short-time result. Conjecture: The convergence radius of the BBGKY hierarchy for hard-sphere systems has the explicit lower bound t < c·λ^{-1} (the mean free time); extended to Lambert times with a constant of the c = 1/100 type.

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert 第六问题(力学到动理学的推导)Lanford 的短时结果。猜想：硬球系统的 BBGKY 级数的收敛半径的显式下界为 t<c·λ^{-1}(平均自由程);扩展到 Lambert 时间的 c=1/100 型常数。
