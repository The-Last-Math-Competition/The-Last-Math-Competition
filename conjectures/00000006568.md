# 00000006568

**English.** Definition: Gauge splitting with the fractional structure of gauge fields. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the leading gauge drift and the drift is of charge-error type; the order is exact, with exact order 1; and the vanishing of the drift is at gauge-invariant discretization. (charge-error conversion of gauge drift)

**中文。** 定义：规范分裂：规范场的分步结构。猜想：换算常数为规范漂移首阶的显式且漂移为电荷误差型；阶精确且精确的阶为 1；漂移的消没在规范不变离散化处。（规范漂移电荷误差换算）
