# 00000005394

**English.** Definition: The minimal-point orbit is the iteration set of height-minimal points. Conjecture: The orbit lower bound for small discriminants is an explicit polynomial of the minimal height, given by the second-order term of the equidistribution deficit, and compatible with the classical constant. (second-order deficit lower bound of minimal orbits)

**中文。** 定义：极小点轨道指高度极小点的迭代集。猜想：小判别式的轨道下界为极小高度的多项式显式,由等分布亏损的二阶项给出且与经典常数相容。（极小轨道二阶亏损下界）
