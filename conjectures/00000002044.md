# 00000002044

**English.** Conjecture: The partial sums of the Rudin–Shapiro sequence along squares are o(x) (its random phase controlled by the quadratic structure of Weyl sums of the square sequence); the convergence rate is O(x^{1/2+ε}).

**中文。** 猜想：Rudin–Shapiro 序列沿平方数的部分和为 o(x)(其随机相位由平方序列的 Weyl 和的二次结构控制);收敛率为 O(x^{1/2+ε})。
