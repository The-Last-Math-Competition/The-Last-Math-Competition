**English.** Definition: Pseudospectra are the perturbation images of the level sets of the spectrum. Conjecture: Under a fixed Jordan-type constraint, the minimization of the pseudospectral area is an explicit power law in the reciprocal of the block dimension, the minimizing construction is explicit, and the power law is exact. (Jordan power-law minimization of structured pseudospectra)
