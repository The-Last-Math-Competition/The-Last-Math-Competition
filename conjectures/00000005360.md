**English.** Definition: The series and the boundary are two group invariants. Conjecture: There exist two hyperbolic groups with identical series but non-isomorphic boundaries, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of groups with the same growth but different fractal boundaries. (same series-different boundary group pair separation)
