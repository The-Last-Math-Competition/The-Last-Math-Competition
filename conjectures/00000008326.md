**DIA-06 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The transfinite hierarchy of U-numbers in Mahler's classification: the deviation constant of minimal polynomial approximation of U_m numbers. Conjecture: The explicit value (the infimum of approximation constants of U_m numbers) follows the odd number law w_m = 2m - 1 (an odd number law); algebraic independence of U numbers (two U numbers being algebraically independent) is universal (an algebraic independence universality law); the spectrum of the hierarchy of subclasses of Liouville numbers (U_infinity, the stratification of super-Liouville numbers) is totally ordered (a total order spectrum law); and adjacent strata are realized densely in (0,1) (a dense realization law). (U-number odd law super-Liouville stratification)

**中文。** 定义：Mahler 分类的 U-数的超限层级：U_m 数的极小多项式逼近的偏差常 u_m 常数。猜想：u_m 的显式（U_m 数的逼近常数的下确界）为 w_m=2m-1 的奇数律（奇数律）；且 U 数的代数无关（两个 U 数的代数无关性）的普遍性（代数无关普遍律）；Liouville 数（U_∞）的子类（超 Liouville 的分层）的层级的谱为全序（全序谱律）；层与层的实现在 (0,1) 的稠密（稠密实现律）。
