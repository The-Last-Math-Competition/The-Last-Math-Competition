# 00000007677

**QDE-17 △** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: The q-Zeilberger algorithm inputs a q-hypergeometric term t(n,k) and outputs a certificate for G(n+1,k)-G(n,k)=D_q^* t(n,k). Conjecture: For every q-hypergeometric term over a rational function field, the algorithm terminates within order d<=deg(t)+2; and the supremum of termination steps over all terms of degree <=D is a linear function of D. (linear termination bound for q-Zeilberger)

**中文。** 定义：q-Zeilberger 算法输入 q-超几何项 t(n,k)，输出使 G(n+1,k)-G(n,k)=D_q^* t(n,k) 的证书，其中 D_q^* 为移位算子。猜想：对每个固定的有理函数域上的 q-超几何项 t(n,k)，算法在阶数 d≤deg(t)+2 内终止；且终止步数的上确界在遍历所有次数 ≤D 的 t 时为 D 的线性函数。
