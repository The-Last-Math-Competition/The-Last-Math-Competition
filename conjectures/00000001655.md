**English.** Definition: The center of the preprojective algebra Π(Q) is known to be the invariant ring of the corresponding Kleinian group. Conjecture: The dimension of H¹(Π(Q)) is (#Q₀−3)·h with h the Coxeter number of the corresponding affine ADE type; beyond the known center result, the cohomology dimensions of higher centers are given explicitly by Ext over the quantum invariant ring.

**中文。** 定义：预投影代数 Π(Q) 的中心已知为对应 Kleinian 群的不变量环。猜想：H¹(Π(Q)) 的维数为 (#Q₀−3)·h,h 为相应仿射 ADE 型的 Coxeter 数;除已知中心结果外,高次中心的上同调维数由量子不变量环的 Ext 显式。
