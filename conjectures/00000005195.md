# 00000005195

**English.** Definition: The Picard spectrum of a symmetric monoidal category is the spectrification of invertible objects. Conjecture: The dimension table of two-dimensional Picard spectra is explicitly indexed by pairs of homotopy groups, complete within group rank two with closed classification, and the torsion part of the pair is always controlled by the prime-factor spectrum of the Brauer group. (pair dimension table of two-dimensional Picard spectra)

**中文。** 定义：对称单项范畴的皮卡谱指可逆对象的谱化。猜想：二维皮卡谱的维数表由同伦群二元组显式索引,群秩二以内完全且分类封闭。(二维皮卡谱二元组维数表)；且二元组扭转部分恒由 Brauer 群素因子谱控制。
