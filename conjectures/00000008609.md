# 00000008609

**English.** Definition: Bahadur efficiency is the exponential rate of tests. Conjecture: The location Bahadur slope of the sign test is 2-asymptotically optimal; and no rank test has slope exceeding its Pitman counterpart. (optimal Bahadur slope)

**中文。** 定义：Bahadur 效率指检验的指数速率。猜想：符号检验的位置 Bahadur 斜率 2-渐近最优；任何秩检验的斜率不超过其 Pitman 对应。（Bahadur 斜率最优）
