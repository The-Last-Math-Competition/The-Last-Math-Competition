# 00000008577

**English.** Definition: Recursive weights of weighted shift operators and the self-similar structure of their spectra. Conjecture: The spectrum of recursively homogeneous weights is a Cantor-type self-similar set (a Cantor type law); the fractal dimension of the spectrum can be tuned continuously between 0 and 1 (a continuous spectrum line law); the spectrum line is explicitly controlled by the compression ratio of the weights (a compression ratio control law); and the dimension of the spectral measure coincides with the Hausdorff dimension at the critical ratio (a critical coincidence law). (weighted shift recursive Cantor continuous tuning)

**中文。** 定义：加权移位算子的递归权重与谱的自相似结构。猜想：递归齐次权重的谱为 Cantor 型自相似集（Cantor 型律）；且谱的分形维数可在 0 与 1 间连续调节（连续谱系律）；谱系由权重的压缩比显式控制（压缩比控制律）；谱测度的维数与 Hausdorff 维数在临界比处重合（临界重合律）。
