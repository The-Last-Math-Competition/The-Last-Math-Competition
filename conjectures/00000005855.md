**English.** Definition: Semidihedral defect is the block of the mixed-type defect group. Conjecture: It is enumerated into four subtypes by the two-valued distinction of the height distribution, the kernel of the distinction being the height jump; the inertia index set of the four subtypes is {1,2,3,4}; and two subtypes share the trunk count, exactly one sharing pair. (four-subtype height jump of semidihedral defect)
