**English.** Definition: Nesterenko's method is the technique for proving algebraic independence of modular function values. Conjecture: Its new instance is the explicit quadruple of values of weight-two and half-weight modular forms, the quadruple is algebraically independent, and the method extends to signature three. (Nesterenko signature-three instance)
