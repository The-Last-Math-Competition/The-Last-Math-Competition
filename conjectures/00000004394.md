# 00000004394

**English.** Definition: BS-convergence is the neighborhood convergence of bounded-degree graphs, and the spectral measure is the normalized adjacency spectrum. Conjecture: The convergence rate of the spectral measure is controlled by an explicit hyperbolic bound in the separability parameter of the graph sequence, and the bound is optimal for uniformly tree-like graph sequences. (separable hyperbolic bound of BS spectral convergence)

**中文。** 定义：BS-收敛指有界度图的邻域收敛,谱测度指归一化邻接谱。猜想：谱测度的收敛速度由图列可分性参数的显式双曲控制,界在一致树图列达到最优。（BS 谱收敛可分双曲界）
