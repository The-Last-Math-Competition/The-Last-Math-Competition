# 00000008004

**English.** Definition: The solvability threshold beyond the Cordes condition: the ellipticity ratio epsilon_C(n) for C^{1,1} solutions of the non-divergence equation a_ij D_ij u = f. Conjecture: epsilon_C(n) = (n-1)/(n+1) (the exact Cordes threshold); above the threshold (ratio at most epsilon_C) solutions are injective (unique) in the L^p framework; and below the threshold the minimal roughness coefficient of counterexamples is an explicit piecewise-constant perturbation of epsilon_C + 1/(3n) (an explicit minimal counterexample), whose singularity type is a quarter-power law corner singularity. (Cordes threshold exact value)

**中文。** 定义：Cordes 条件的边界外的可解性阈值：非-divergence 方程 a_ij D_ij u=f 的 C^{1,1} 解的椭圆率比 ε_C(n)。猜想：ε_C(n) = (n-1)/(n+1)（Cordes 阈值的精确值）；且阈值之上（比 ≤ε_C）的解在 L^p 框架的单射（唯一性）；阈值下的反例的最小粗糙系数为 ε_C+1/(3n) 的显式扰动的分段常系数（极小反例的显式化），反例的奇性类型为角点奇性的 1/4-幂律。
