**English.** Definition: The Dehn surgery volume deficit is the difference of the volumes before and after surgery. Conjecture: The asymptotic coefficient of the deficit is the explicit constant of the cusp volume divided by four pi, and the coefficient is uniform over all surgery coefficients and exact. (uniform coefficient of the Dehn deficit)

**中文。** 定义：Dehn 手术体积亏损指手术前后体积之差。猜想：亏损渐近系数为尖点体积除以四派型显式常数,系数对一切手术系数一致且精确。（Dehn 亏损一致系数）
