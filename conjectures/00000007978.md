**English.** Definition: The capacity of private information retrieval (PIR): the download-rate upper bound C_PIR(t) of PIR with t database replicas. Conjecture: C_PIR(t) = 1/(1 + t^{-1}) (achievability of the known upper bound, complete determination of the t-server PIR capacity); and the capacity of PIR with adversarial servers (BYZ-routable PIR) is (1-epsilon)/(1 + 1/t + 1/t^2) (a fault-tolerant correction); and the minimal word length for query complexity is a prime-power bound of (t+1), the existence of structured codes being realized by the unique construction of RS subspace codes. (PIR capacity adversarial correction)

**中文。** 定义：私信息检索（PIR）的容量：数据库副本数 t 的 PIR 的下载率上界 C_PIR(t)。猜想：C_PIR(t) = 1/(1+t^{-1})（已知上界的可达性，t 服务器 PIR 容量的完全确定）；且带有敌意服务器（BYZ-routable PIR）的容量为 (1-ε)/(1+1/t+1/t²)（容错修正）；查询复杂度的极小字长为 (t+1) 的素数幂界（结构码的存在性由 RS 子空间码的唯一构造实现）。
