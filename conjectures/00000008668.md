# 00000008668

**CGT-18 ★** — source: 274_计算群论与字的几何.md

**English.** Definition: Zelmanov's representation variant: irreducibility of representations of infinite-dimensional Burnside groups. Conjecture: The dimensions of irreducible representations of large prime Burnside groups are unbounded (an unboundedness law); the constructing families of explicit small dimensions have dimension polynomial in p (a polynomial dimension law); the improvement of the explicit upper bound for the order in the restricted Burnside problem (finiteness at fixed exponent and generator number) is double-exponential (a double-exponential law); and the tightness of the double exponential against the actual order is polynomial (a polynomial magnitude law). (Zelmanov Burnside representation dimension bounds)

**中文。** 定义：表示变体的 Zelmanov：无限维 Burnside 的表示的不可约。猜想：大素数 Burnside 群的不可约表示的维数无界（无界律）；且无界的构造（显式小维数的族）的维数为 p 的多项式（多项式维数律）；限制 Burnside（固定指数与生成数的有限性）的阶的显式上界的改进（改进律）为双指数的（双指数律）；双指数的紧性（实际阶的量级）为多项式的（多项式量级律）。
