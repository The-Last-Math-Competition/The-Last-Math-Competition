# 00000002752

**PI-12 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: Coefficient growth of identities: the asymptotics of codimension sequences c_n(A). Conjecture: The exponential growth law: c_n(A) ~ c·n^t·α^n with t a half-integer composed of the PI exponent and a half-integer correction; the preperiod length is the component sum of the Kemer index vector of the algebra. (codimension power-law parameters)

**中文。** 定义：恒等式的系数增长：codimension 序列 c_n(A) 的渐近。猜想：codimension 的指数增长律：c_n(A) ~ c·n^t·α^n 且指数 t 为半整数由 PI 指数与一个半整修正组成；预周期的长度为代数的 Kemer 指标向量的分量和。（codimension 幂律参数）
