# 00000006203

**NL5-13 ◆** — source: 192_色散方程II_非线性薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: Multi-solitons are combined-type soliton solutions, N-solitons. Conjecture: The integrable framework is given by scattering compatibility and the phase-shift lattice is complete; the pairwise decomposition of phase shifts is multiplicative with zero deficit; and the Poisson structure of scattering data is preserved under the soliton flow, the preservation given by the inversion formula. (integrable phase-shift lattice of multi-solitons)

**中文。** 定义：多孤立子：组合型孤子解（N-soliton）。猜想：可积框架由散射相容性给出且相移格为完全；相移的两两分解为乘性且乘性亏损为零；散射数据的 Poisson 结构在孤子流下保持且保持由反演公式给出。（多孤子可积相移格散射相容）
