# 00000001277

**CG-77 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The entropy-rate extremum of a k-th order Markov chain is the maximal entropy under given transition constraints. Conjecture: The transition support of the entropy-maximizing chain is a partial permutation matrix; and the extremum is given explicitly by the permutation-block decomposition of the admissible matrix. (transition-constraint entropy maximum)

**中文。** 定义：k 阶马尔可夫链的熵率极值为给定转移约束下的最大熵。猜想：熵极大链的转移支撑为部分置换矩阵；且极值由允许矩阵的置换块分解显式给出。（转移约束熵极大）
