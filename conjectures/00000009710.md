# 00000009710

**SCA-10 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Modified wave operators for long-range potentials: wave operators under Coulomb-type decaying potentials require phase corrections. Conjecture: The modified wave operators of three-dimensional Coulomb potentials are asymptotically complete: completeness holds when the charge is below a critical value, and the failure at critical charge is given by an explicit mechanism of resonant capture. (modified wave operator completeness)

**中文。** 定义：长程位势的修正波算子：Coulomb 型衰减位势下波算子需相位修正。猜想：三维 Coulomb 位势的修正波算子渐近完备：完备性在电荷小于临界值时成立且临界电荷处的失效由共振俘获的显式机制给出。（修正波算子完备性）
