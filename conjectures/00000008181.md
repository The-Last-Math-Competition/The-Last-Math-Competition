# 00000008181

**CNT-11 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: The attack surface of Weil descent on abelian varieties: the minimality weil_min of the dimension of systems from the Weil restriction of the DLP on Jacobians. Conjecture: weil_min (the effective dimension of Weil descent) is at least g+1 (a one-beyond law); the lower bound on the genus of the hyperelliptic cover of the descent system is 2^{g-1} (a genus exponent law); and the optimal system of the attack (the hyperelliptic version of index calculus) has genus 2g-1 at the balance point (a 2g-1 balance law), the balance constant being the tightness at 1 (a single tight balance). (Weil descent genus 2g-1 balance law)

**中文。** 定义：阿贝尔簇上的 Weil 下降的攻击面：Jacobi 簇的 DLP 的 Weil 限制的系统的维数最小性 weil_min。猜想：weil_min（Weil 下降的有效维数）≥ g+1（超一维律）；且下降系统的超椭圆覆盖的亏格的下界为 2^{g-1}（亏格指数律）；攻击（索引计算的超椭圆版）的最优系统的亏格 = 2g-1 的平衡点（2g-1 平衡律），平衡的常数为 1 的紧化（单紧平衡）。
