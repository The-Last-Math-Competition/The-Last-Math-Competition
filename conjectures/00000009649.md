# 00000009649

**English.** Definition: Asymptotic distribution of eigenvector components: individual components tend to Gaussian after normalization (the isotropic local law). Conjecture: The joint distribution of any fixed k components of eigenvectors is asymptotically Gaussian, with the elliptic equivalence class completely determined by the fourth-moment parameter; and the Berry-Esseen bound is n^{-1/2+o(1)} with optimal exponent. (component joint Gaussian law)

**中文。** 定义：特征向量分量的渐近分布：单个分量在重整化后趋向高斯（isotropic 局部律）。猜想：特征向量任意固定 k 个分量的联合分布为渐近高斯且椭圆等价类完全由四阶矩参数确定； Berry–Esseen 界为 n^{−1/2+o(1)} 且指数最优。（分量联合高斯律）
