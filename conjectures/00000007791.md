# 00000007791

**ACG-11 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The mixing time T_mix(epsilon) of hit-and-run random walks on isotropic convex bodies K. Conjecture: T_mix(epsilon) = Theta(n^2 log(n/epsilon)) uniformly over all isotropic bodies, matching the known lower bound and the ball upper bound; and the worst constant is attained at the simplex, its exact value the explicit expression of the second eigenvalue of the hit-and-run chain on the simplex, an algebraic number uniquely determined by the recursion of iterated expectations. (uniform hit-and-run mixing)

**中文。** 定义：hit-and-run 随机游走在迷向凸体 K 上的混合时间 T_mix(ε)。猜想：T_mix(ε) = Θ(n² log(n/ε)) 对一切迷向体一致成立（匹配已知下界与球的上界）；且系数常数对单纯形取到最坏值，其精确常数为单纯形上 hit-and-run 链的第二特征值的显式表达式，即由迭代期望的递推方程唯一确定的代数数。
