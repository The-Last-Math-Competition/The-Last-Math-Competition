# 00000006116

**English.** Definition: Noise stability is the expectation preservation of Boolean functions under noise, majority stability. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit upper envelope of majority functions, majority most stable within the critical band; the band width is the square root of the noise parameter; and the envelope is attained at recursive majorities, with value one minus the band width. (majority upper-envelope critical band conversion)

**中文。** 定义：噪声稳定性：布尔函数在噪声扰动下的期望保持（多数稳定性）。猜想：换算常数为多数函数的显式上包络且临界带内多数最稳定；带的宽度为噪声参数的平方根；包络的达到在递归多数处且达到值为 1 减去带宽。（多数上包络临界带换算）
