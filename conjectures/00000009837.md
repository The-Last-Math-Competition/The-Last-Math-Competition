# 00000009837

**English.** Definition: Two-weight norms of Hardy operators: the one-dimensional exact computation was given by Nazarov-Podkorytov. Conjecture: The two-weight norms of d-dimensional Hardy-type operators reduce to finite testing families; the cardinality of the family is an explicit function of the covering complexity of the weight support, and finiteness is equivalent to boundedness. (Hardy two-weight finite testing)

**中文。** 定义：Hardy 算子的两权范数：一维情形的精确计算已由 Nazarov–Podkorytov 给出。猜想：d 维 Hardy 型算子的两权范数可化为有限检测组；检测组的基数为权支撑的覆盖复杂度的显式函数且有限性等价于有界性。（Hardy 两权有限检测）
