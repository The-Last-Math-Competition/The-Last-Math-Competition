**WEX-17 ★** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: exact computation already of two weight norm: one dimension of Hardy operator by Nazarov–Podkorytov gives. Conjecture: two weight norm of d dimension Hardy type operator is cardinality of finite set; set is explicit function of cover complexity of weight support and finite is equivalent to bounded. (Hardy two weight finite)

**中文。** 定义：Hardy 算子的两权范数：一维情形的精确计算已由 Nazarov–Podkorytov 给出。猜想：d 维 Hardy 型算子的两权范数可化为有限检测组；检测组的基数为权支撑的覆盖复杂度的显式函数且有限性等价于有界性。（Hardy 两权有限检测）
