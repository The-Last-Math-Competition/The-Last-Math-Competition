# 00000001133

**English.** Definition: The 0-Hecke algebra H_n(0) acts on the cohomology of flag varieties as an analogue. Conjecture: The dimension of the image of the projector equals the rank of the Schubert class (Hecke projector dimension).

**中文。** 定义：0-Hecke 代数 H_n(0) 的类似物作用于旗簇的上同调。猜想： projector 的像的维数为 Schubert 类的秩(Hecke projector 维数)。
