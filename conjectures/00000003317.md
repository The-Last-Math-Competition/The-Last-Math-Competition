# 00000003317

**English.** Definition: Gorenstein cell complexes: the combinatoratorial version of duality. Conjecture: The Gorenstein criterion: a cell complex is Gorenstein if and only if the local cohomology of the face ring concentrates in the top degree, with the combinatoratorial side of the criterion Dehn–Sommerville. (Gorenstein Dehn-Sommerville criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Gorenstein 胞腔复形：对偶性的组合版本。猜想：Gorenstein 判据：胞腔球为 Gorenstein 当且仅当面环的局部上同调集中于顶层且判据的组合侧为 Dehn–Sommerville。（Gorenstein Dehn-Sommerville 判据）
