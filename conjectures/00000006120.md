# 00000006120

**CG5-20 △** — source: 189_高维概率_集中不等式II.md

**English.** Definition: Noise stability and the spectrum are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two Boolean functions with identical noise stability but different spectral distributions, and the separation is realized by an explicit perturbation with the same stability but different low-degree weights. (same stability-different low-degree spectral separation)

**中文。** 定义：噪声稳定性与谱指两层。猜想：存在两布尔函数的一切噪声稳定性相同而谱分布不同,分离由同稳定异低度权的显式扰动实现。（稳定同-谱异低度权分离）
