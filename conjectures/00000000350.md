**English.** Definition: The deep-hole depth ℓ(Λ) of a lattice is the minimal distance from the farthest point to the lattice. Conjecture: The deep-hole ratio ℓ/λ₁ of the Leech lattice equals √2; and the ratio is an exact algebraic number uniquely determined by the minimal-vector configuration. (Leech deep-hole ratio square root of two)
