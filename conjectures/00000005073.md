**English.** Definition: The area law is the boundary scaling of entanglement. Conjecture: The area-law constant of the one-dimensional many-body localized phase is an explicit functional of the reciprocal decay rate of localized bits, the value is uniformly bounded, and the constant controls optimally. (area-law constant functional of many-body localization)

**中文。** 定义：面积律指纠缠的边界标度。猜想：一维多体局域相的面积律常数为局域化比特衰减率倒数的显式泛函,值一致有界且常数控制最优。（多体局域面积律常数泛函）
