# 00000008485

**English.** Definition: The Pareto frontier of two continuous functions (f,g) on an ergodic system: the northeast boundary of the image of the set of pairs (integral f d nu, integral g d nu) over ergodic measures. Conjecture: The Pareto frontier is always a compact convex curve; strict convexity holds if and only if the parametrized family t f + (1-t) g has a unique equilibrium state; the endpoints are exactly the equidistributions on the locked orbits maximizing f and g separately; and the middle segment is parametrized by the Legendre dual of the pressure function t mapping to P(tf + (1-t)g), with at most 2 endpoints in total. (Pareto frontier Legendre pressure endpoints)

**中文。** 定义：遍历系统上两连续函数 (f,g) 的 Pareto 前沿：遍历测度积分对 (∫f dν, ∫g dν) 像集的北东边界。猜想：Pareto 前沿恒为凸紧曲线；严格凸性当且仅当参数族 t f+(1-t) g 有唯一均衡态；端点恰为 f 与 g 各自单目标极大化锁定轨道上的等分布；中段由压力函数 t↦P(tf+(1-t)g) 的 Legendre 对偶参数化且端点总数不超过 2。
