# 00000002007

**PD-17 ◆** — source: 52_偏微分方程II.md

**English.** Definition: The soliton resolution conjecture asserts that long-time dynamics decompose asymptotically into radiation plus solitons plus breathers. Conjecture: The decomposition for general mass-supercritical NLS converges at the exponential rate e^{−t^{3/2}}; and the breather term is always zero below the mass threshold. (supercritical decomposition breather vanishing)

**中文。** 定义：孤立子分解猜想指长时间动力学为辐射加孤子加呼吸子的渐近分解。猜想：一般质量超临界 NLS 的分解指数收敛率为 e 的负 t 二分之三次幂；且呼吸子项在质量阈值下方恒为零。（超临界分解呼吸子消没）
