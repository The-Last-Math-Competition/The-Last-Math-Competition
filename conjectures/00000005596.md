**EL2-06 ★** — source: 172_精确线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Residue reconstruction recovers integers from congruences. Conjecture: The reconstruction constant of determinants is the explicit half-threshold of the modulus product, controlled by the classical bound of the determinant, with super-exponentially small error probability. (half-threshold reconstruction constant of determinant residues)

**中文。** 定义：余式重建指经同余恢复整数的方法。猜想：行列式的重建常数为模数积的显式半阈,由行列式的经典界控制且误差概率超指数小。（行列式余式半阈重建常数）
