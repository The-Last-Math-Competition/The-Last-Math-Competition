# 00000008307

**FLO-17 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The Poisson-Sigma rigidity of Lagrangian intersections: the intersection forcing force_int for Lagrangian boundaries of symplectic manifolds with boundary. Conjecture: force_int (the minimal obstruction forcing intersection) is the nontriviality of the relative homology of the boundary (a relative nontriviality law); the quantification of the forcing (lower bound on intersection counts) is the sum of relative Betti numbers (a relative Betti law); and the exception (embeddings without intersection) holds if and only if the boundary is null-homologous (a null-homologous criterion law). (Poisson-Sigma relative Betti intersection forcing)

**中文。** 定义：Lagrangian 相交的 Poisson–Sigma 的刚性：带边辛流形的 Lagrangian 边界的相交强制 force_int。猜想：force_int（强制相交的最小障碍）= 边界的相对同调的非平凡（相对非平凡律）；且强制的量化（相交的计数下界）为相对 Betti 的和（相对 Betti 律）；例外（无相交的嵌入）的充要为边界的 null-homologous（零调判据律）。
