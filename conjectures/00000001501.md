**HY-21 ◆** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The classification conjecture for Anosov diffeomorphisms asserts they exist only on infranil manifolds. Conjecture: The existence of codimension-1 order-preserving Anosov diffeomorphisms is completely characterized by a Lie-algebra cohomology obstruction: existence holds if and only if the induced normal cohomology class lies in an explicit finite list.

**中文。** 定义：Anosov 微分同胚的分类猜想断言其仅存在于 infranil 流形。猜想：余维 1 保序 Anosov 微分同胚的存在性由李代数上同调障碍完全刻画:存在当且仅当其诱导作用的_nomal 上同调类在显式有限列表中。
