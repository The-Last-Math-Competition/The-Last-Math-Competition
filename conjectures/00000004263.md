**GT2-23 ★** — source: 127_数学物理II_规范理论的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The pentagon equation is the self-consistency equation of the four-point functions, and the F matrix is its solution matrix. Conjecture: The minimal dimension for solving the F matrices is 3, and the F matrices of all rigid fusion categories of dimension at least 3 can be solved explicitly by Brauer diagram recursion. (minimal dimension and recursive solution of F matrices)

**中文。** 定义：结合子方程指四点函数的自洽方程,F-矩阵指其解矩阵。猜想：F-矩阵求解的极小维数为 3,一切维数不低于 3 的刚性融合范畴的 F-矩阵可由 Brauer 图递归显式解出。（F-矩阵极小维与递归解）
