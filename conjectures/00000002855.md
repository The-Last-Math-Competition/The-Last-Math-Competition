# 00000002855

**English.** Definition: The geometry of mixed-integer quadratic programming: the algebraic complexity of SQP. Conjecture: Combinatorial rigidity of QP polytopes: the face count of the image polytope of a quadratic map is a permutation number, and the Hasse diagram of the face lattice is dual to the intersection lattice of a hyperplane arrangement. (QP face lattice permutation duality)

**中文。** 定义：混合整数的二次规划几何：SQP 的代数复杂度。猜想：QP 多面体的组合刚性：二次映射的像多面体的面数为排列数且面格的 Hasse 图与超平面排列的交格对偶。（QP 面格排列对偶）
