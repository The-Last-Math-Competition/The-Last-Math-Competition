**PQ-30 △** — source: 68_整数分拆与q级数II.md

**English.** Definition: Wilf's conjecture for partitions. Conjecture: The arithmetic-progression structure of partition congruences has density zero: the set of moduli M for which p(n) satisfies a nontrivial congruence mod M has density zero among all M. (partition Wilf conjecture congruence arithmetic progressions density zero)
