# 00000002315

**English.** Definition: The power structure of a p-group is the power-map type of its subgroup lattice. Conjecture: The power structure for coclass ≤ 2 is a PORC-type explicit periodic table (period p^r); the table comes from the classification of power sections at the traversal endpoints of coclass trees.

**中文。** 定义：p-群的幂结构指子群格的幂映射型。猜想：coclass ≤2 的幂结构为 PORC 型显式周期表(周期 p^{r});表由 coclass 树的遍历终点的幂截面的分类。
