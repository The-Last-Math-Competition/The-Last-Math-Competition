**English.** Definition: Tate cohomology is the extension cohomology of the stable category. Conjecture: For blocks of group algebras the Tate period and the AR-translation period are always equal, and the smallest counterexample beyond group algebras is the four-dimensional exterior algebra. (boundary of the Tate-AR period equality)
