# 00000008481

**English.** Definition: Posterior variance minimization for Markov chain equilibria with symmetric Dirichlet priors on transition probabilities. Conjecture: The minimizing prior parameter is alpha = 1/2, the Jeffreys prior (a Jeffreys law); this optimality is universal under squared loss and exponential-family observations; and the variance increment from prior mismatch is the explicit (alpha - 1/2)^2/(n+1) (an explicit mismatch law). (Jeffreys optimal prior explicit mismatch)

**中文。** 定义：转移概率带对称 Dirichlet 先验的马尔可夫链平衡态的后验方差极小化问题。猜想：极小先验参数为 α=1/2，即 Jeffreys 先验（Jeffreys 律）；且该最优性在平方损失与指数族观测下普适；先验错配的方差增量为 (α-1/2)²/(n+1) 的显式式（错配显式律）。
