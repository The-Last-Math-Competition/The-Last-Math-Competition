**English.** Definition: a posteriori variance minimize problem of Markov chain equilibrium state of transition probability symmetric Dirichlet a priori. Conjecture: minimal a priori parameter is α=1/2, namely Jeffreys a priori (Jeffreys); and the optimal in square loss and variance increment of exponential family under universal; a priori is explicit expression (explicit) of (α-1/2)²/(n+1).

**中文。** 定义：转移概率带对称 Dirichlet 先验的马尔可夫链平衡态的后验方差极小化问题。猜想：极小先验参数为 α=1/2，即 Jeffreys 先验（Jeffreys 律）；且该最优性在平方损失与指数族观测下普适；先验错配的方差增量为 (α-1/2)²/(n+1) 的显式式（错配显式律）。
