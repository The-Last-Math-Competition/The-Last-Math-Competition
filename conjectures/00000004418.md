# 00000004418

**English.** Definition: The Schatten class is the operator ideal of p-summable sequences. Conjecture: The graphon integral operator belongs to the Schatten class of order p if and only if the p-th power sum of its spectrum is finite, the conversion constant is 1, and the boundary exponent is attained by interval kernels. (conversion index boundary of Schatten classes)

**中文。** 定义：Schatten 类指算子的 p 可和序列理想。猜想：graphon 积分算子属于 p 阶 Schatten 类当且仅当其谱的 p 次和有限,换算常数为 1,边界指标由区间核达到。（Schatten 类换算指标边界）
