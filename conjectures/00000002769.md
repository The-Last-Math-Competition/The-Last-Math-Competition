# 00000002769

**English.** Definition: The geometric side of identity semantics: representation varieties. Conjecture: The correspondence between irreducible components of representation varieties and PI: the dimension spectrum of irreducible components of the n-point representation variety of an algebra A has a unique maximum, the maximal dimension being the square correction of the PI exponent of A. (representation variety component dimension spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：恒等式语义的几何侧：表示簇（representation variety）。猜想：表示簇的不可约分量与 PI 的对应：代数 A 的 n 点表示簇的不可约分量的维数谱满足极大元唯一且极大维数为 A 的 PI 指数的平方修正。（表示簇分量维数谱）
