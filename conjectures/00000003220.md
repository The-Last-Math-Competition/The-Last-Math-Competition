# 00000003220

**English.** Definition: Algorithms for multiplicative bases: structure computation of group algebras. Conjecture: Augmented multiplicative bases: the multiplicative basis of a group algebra over a splitting field is a scalar correction of group elements, with the correction algorithm the inverse Fourier transform. (multiplicative basis Fourier inverse algorithm)

**中文。** 定义：乘法基的算法：群代数的结构计算。猜想：增广乘法基：分裂域上群代数的乘法基为群元素的标量修正且修正的算法为 Fourier 逆变换。（乘法基 Fourier 逆算法）
