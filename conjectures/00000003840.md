# 00000003840

**English.** Definition: The inverse limit of crystals denotes the graph along an injection chain B(λ₁) ↪ B(λ₂) ↪ … . Conjecture: There exists a chain of dominant weights where each B(λ_k) is connected but the inverse limit is disconnected; for type A_n, any chain keeps the inverse limit connected. (inverse limit connectivity threshold)

**中文。** 定义：晶体的反向极限指沿单射链 B(λ₁) ↪ B(λ₂) ↪ … 的逆向极限图。猜想：存在支配权链使每个 B(λ_k) 连通而反向极限不连通;但对型 A_n 的任何链反向极限保持连通。（反向极限连通阈值）
