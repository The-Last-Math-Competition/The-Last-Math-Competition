# 00000007237

**English.** Definition: Marklof-Strombergsson with free path lengths. Conjecture: The distribution of free path lengths on the torus holds, and the kernel of the distribution is the window measure of the homogeneous space. (window-measure Marklof-Strombergsson free-path kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Marklof–Strömbergsson：自由程长度。猜想：自由程的分布：环面上的自由程的分布且布的核为齐性空间的窗测度。（Marklof-Strombergsson 窗测度齐性自由程分布核环面）
