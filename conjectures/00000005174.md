**English.** Definition: Bridgeland stability is the data of a heart with a phase function. Conjecture: The feasible domain of stability conditions of the derived category is an open cone in the whole space, the infinity version is parametrized explicitly by geometric-type structures, and the domain is connected. (connectedness of the feasible open cone of infinite stability)
