# 00000009577

**English.** Definition: The separating curve graph has separating simple closed curves as vertices; its coarse geometry and end structure reflect the Torelli group. Conjecture: Every end of the separating curve graph is a Cantor set, and its quasi-isometry group is the exact centralizing extension of the mapping class group — automatic rigidity holds; and the hyperbolicity of the product graph of the non-separating and separating curve graphs fails exactly at genus at most 2. (separating curve graph rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：分离曲线图以分离简单闭曲线为顶点；其粗几何与末端结构反映 Torelli 群。猜想：分离曲线图的每个末端为 Cantor 集，其拟等距群为映射类群的精确中心化扩张，自动刚性成立；非分离曲线图与分离曲线图的积图的双曲性恰在亏格 ≤2 失效。（分离曲线图刚性）
