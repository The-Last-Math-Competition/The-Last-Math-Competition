# 00000001616

**SP-16 ◆** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: Spectral statistics of rational polygonal billiards refer to the gap distribution of their Laplace eigenvalues. Conjecture: For general rational triangles the spectral gaps are Poisson; for the isosceles right triangle, the first k gaps are characterized by the explicit quadratic form Q(x,y) = x²+y² of the Eisenstein integers' norm, with distribution concentrating on the prime-factor counts of Q-values.

**中文。** 定义：有理多边形弹子的谱统计指其 Laplace 特征值的间隙分布。猜想：一般有理三角形的谱间距为 Poisson;等腰直角三角形的首 k 个间隙由 Eisenstein 整环的范数形式 Q(x,y)=x²+y² 的显式二次型刻画,其分布集中于 Q 值的素因子计数。
