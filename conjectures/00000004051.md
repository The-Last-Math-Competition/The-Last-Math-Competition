**English.** Definition: The stable category of a group algebra kG is the quotient category modulo projectives. Conjecture: The blocks of the stable AR-quiver correspond one-to-one with the defect groups, and the AR-type of each block is given explicitly by the p-structure of its defect group. (stable blocks, defect groups, and AR types of kG)
