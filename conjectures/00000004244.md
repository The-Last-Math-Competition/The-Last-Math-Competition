# 00000004244

**English.** Definition: SW wall crossing is the jump of invariants across a stability wall. Conjecture: The wall-crossing table for b^+ at most 3 is complete, the change factors are rational functions of elementary factors, the number of elementary factors is half the dimension, and there are no other factors. (SW wall-crossing elementary factor table)

**中文。** 定义：SW 壁穿越指不变量跨稳定壁的跳变。猜想：bc⁺ 不超过 3 时的壁穿越表完整,变化因子为初等因式的有理函数,初等因式个数为维数的一半且无其他因子。（SW 壁穿越初等因式表）
