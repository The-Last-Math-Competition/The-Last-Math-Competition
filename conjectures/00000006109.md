**CG5-09 ★** — source: 189_高维概率_集中不等式II.md

**English.** Definition: Dimension-freeness is the removability of dimension factors in concentration bounds. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit vanishing rate of dimension dependence, the reciprocal correction derived from the log-Sobolev constant; the correction is of reciprocal log d type; and the minimal construction where it is non-removable is the sum of independent coordinates, whose lower bound still contains the double log of log d. (reciprocal correction of dimension dependence)

**中文。** 定义：维数无关性：集中界中维数因子的可去性。猜想：换算常数为维数依赖的显式消失率且由对数常数推得倒数修正；修正项为 log d 的倒数型；不可去的极小构造为坐标独立和且其下界仍含 log d 的双对数。（维数依赖倒数修正换算）
