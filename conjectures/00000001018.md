**English.** Definition: A coset leader is the minimum-weight representative of a coset. Conjecture: The second moment of the leader-weight distribution of RM(m,r) is explicit via Krawtchouk polynomials; and the kernel of the polynomials is the zero lattice. (RM leader Krawtchouk zero-lattice second moment)
