# 00000008006

**English.** Definition: The Ginzburg-Landau free boundary of superconductivity: the asymptotics of the thickness constant of the interface between the saturation band of |u| (the region where u is close to 1) and the mixed band, with interface thickness of order kappa^{-1} (kappa the GL parameter). Conjecture: As kappa grows, the support of the limit measure of the interface (vortex band) is an isoperimetric set of minimal connection metric (the isoperimetric limit of vortex bands); the leading coefficient of the mixed energy H_c1 is explicit of type pi log kappa + gamma (gamma the Euler constant plus corrections); and the integer-valued distribution of the vortex count N(kappa) ~ H Area/(2 pi) log kappa is equidistributed (vanishing of Benford-type bias in vortex counting). (GL vortex isoperimetric equidistribution)

**中文。** 定义：超导的 Ginzburg–Landau 自由边界：|u| 的饱和带（u≈1 的区域）与混合带的界面的厚度常数的渐近：界面厚度 ~ κ^{-1}（κ 为 GL 参数）。猜想：κ→∞ 的界面（vortex 带）的极限测度的支撑为极小度量的等集合（vortex 带的等周极限）；且混合能 H_c₁ 的第一项系数为 π·log κ + γ 型显式（γ 为 Euler 常数 + 修正）；vortex 数 N(κ)~ H·Area/(2π)·log κ 的整数化分布为等分布（vortex 计数的 Benford 型偏差消没）。
