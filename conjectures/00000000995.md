# 00000000995

**English.** Definition: Set-valued versions of Banach's fixed point. Conjecture: Fixed-point existence for set-valued nonexpansive maps holds on hyperconvex metric spaces and cannot be extended to general complete spaces (Aronszajn non-extension).

**中文。** 定义：Banach 不动点的集值映射。猜想：集值非扩张映射的不动点存在性在超凸度量空间成立且不可延拓到一般完备(Aronshain 不延拓)。
