# 00000005141

**English.** Definition: A CM Hodge structure is a special structure determined by a cyclotomic field. Conjecture: For rank-three CM structures the special points in the period domain are zero-dimensional with point count an explicit product of class numbers, and the formula is closed for all CM types. (class-number product formula of CM special points)

**中文。** 定义：CM Hodge 结构指由分圆域确定的特殊结构。猜想：秩三的 CM 结构在周期域的特殊点为零维且点数为类数的显式乘积,公式对一切 CM 型封闭。（CM 特殊点类数乘积公式）
