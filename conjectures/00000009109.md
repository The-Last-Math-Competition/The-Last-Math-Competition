# 00000009109

**English.** Definition: The principal kinematic formula: the expected volume of intersections of two sets. Conjecture: The coefficients of the formula are products of intrinsic volumes and are closed under iteration over submanifolds; and the combination of the closure is the binomial lattice. (kinematic coefficient closure lattice)

**中文。** 定义：主运动学公式为两集合相交的期望体积。猜想：公式的系数为内在体积乘积且在子流形迭代下封闭；且封闭的组合为二项式格。（运动学系数封闭格）
