**FP2-03 ◆** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: Stochastic parallel transport is the horizontal-lift transfer of Brownian motion on a manifold. Conjecture: The divergence correction term of the transport generator is the potential of half the scalar curvature, the correction degenerates to zero on Cartan-Hadamard manifolds, and it is universal. (curvature potential of stochastic parallel transport)

**中文。** 定义：随机平行移动指流形上布朗运动的水平提升传输。猜想：移动生成元的散度修正项为标量曲率一半的位势,修正在 Cartan–Hadamard 流形上退化为零且普适。（随机平行移动曲率位势）
