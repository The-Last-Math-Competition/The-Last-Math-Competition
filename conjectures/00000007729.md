# 00000007729

**FRV-09 ★** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The eigenvector overlap of a deformed Wigner matrix W_n + P_n, with P_n a fixed finite-rank perturbation with outlying spectral points, is the squared inner product of the outlier eigenvector with the principal eigenvector of P_n, its limit given by finite-rank deformation theory. Conjecture: The overlap distribution is universal for Wigner ensembles with matching first four moments; and the limit value as a function of deformation strength lambda has cube-root singularity at the critical point with critical exponent exactly 1/3, strictly dual to the square-root law at free-convolution density edges. (1/3 critical exponent of eigenvector overlaps)

**中文。** 定义：形变 Wigner 矩阵 W_n + P_n（P_n 为固定有限秩扰动的谱离群点）的特征向量重叠指离群特征向量与 P_n 的主特征向量的内积平方，其极限由自由概率的有限秩形变理论给出。猜想：重叠分布对满足匹配前四阶矩的 Wigner 系综是普适的；且其极限值作为形变强度 λ 的函数在临界点 λ_c 处有立方根奇性，临界指数恰为 1/3，与自由卷积密度边缘的平方根律形成严格对偶。
