# 00000001000

**English.** Definition: Non-amenability of groups is characterized by the Tarski number (the number of pieces in a paradoxical decomposition). Conjecture: The smallest Tarski number is 6, realized by a finite quotient of the Burnside group B(m,n) at (m,n) = (2,5); every group of Tarski number 6 is of Burnside type, with no other mechanism.

**中文。** 定义：群的非消性由 Tarski 数刻画(剖分为自身非消所需块数)。猜想：最小 Tarski 数为 6,由 Burnside 群 B(m,n) 在 (m,n)=(2,5) 的有限商实现;一切 Tarski 数为 6 的群均为 Burnside 型,不存在其他机制。
