# 00000001393

**HT-03 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: A v_n self-map of a finite spectrum is a self-map of period p^n; the v_n period is the length of its action. Conjecture: The Smith–Toda spectrum V(3) exists and its v₃ self-map has period exactly 3^4; for p = 3, this period is verified explicitly in the K(3)-localization by the duality computation of height-3 formal groups.

**中文。** 定义：有限谱的 v_n 自映射指周期为 p^n 的自映射,v_n 周期指其作用长度。猜想：Smith–Toda 谱 V(3) 存在且其 v₃ 自映射的周期恰为 3^4;p=3 时该周期在 K(3)-局部化中由形式群的高度 3 的对偶计算显式验证。
