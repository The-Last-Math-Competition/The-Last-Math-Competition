# 00000008827

**English.** Definition: Duality of robust optimization: Lagrangian duality under worst-case constraints with compact convex constraint set U and its regularization corrections. Conjecture: When U is compact convex and a Slater-type interior condition holds, robust duality always holds with zero duality gap; without Slater, the gap of the xi-norm-squared regularized dual converges to the original gap as xi tends to 0; robust separability — U being a Cartesian product — holds if and only if the dual decomposes constraintwise; and the distributionally robust dual for Wasserstein-ball uncertainty sets equals the regularized empirical risk with regularization coefficient exactly the Wasserstein radius. (robust duality Slater separability Wasserstein radius)

**中文。** 定义：鲁棒优化的对偶：最坏情形约束下约束集 U 为紧凸的拉格朗日对偶及其正则化修正。猜想：U 为紧凸且满足 Slater 类内点条件时鲁棒对偶恒成立且对偶隙为零；无 Slater 条件时加 ξ‖y‖² 的正则化对偶之隙随 ξ→0 收敛于原对偶隙；鲁棒可分性——U 为坐标笛卡尔积——当且仅当对偶可逐约束分解；Wasserstein 球不确定集的分布鲁棒对偶等价于正则化经验风险且正则化系数恰为 Wasserstein 半径。
