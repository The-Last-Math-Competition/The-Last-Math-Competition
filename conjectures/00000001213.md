# 00000001213

**CG-13 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Sylver Coinage: two players alternately name positive integers that may not be nonnegative integer combinations of previously named ones; naming 1 loses. Conjecture: The first player wins, and every winning first move is composite; no prime first move is winning — after a prime opening the second player has a winning response.

**中文。** 定义：Sylver Coinage 指两人轮流命名正整数,不可命名为已命名数的非负整组合,命名 1 者输。猜想：先手必胜,且一切必胜首着均为合数;不存在素数首着必胜,即首着为素数时后手有必胜应对。
