# 00000007872

**English.** Definition: The classification of solid rings in condensed mathematics: discrete rings S that become free modules after solidification. Conjecture: The equivalence classes of solid rings are in bijection with the admissible pairs (n,m) of integers (n dividing m), hence form a countable set; and the semiring generated by tensor-product closure is isomorphic to the monoid semiring of (N,+) direct sum (Z/2), giving a fully discrete invariant of the condensed world. (solid ring classification semiring)

**中文。** 定义：凝聚态数学中的 solid 环（solid abelian group 环结构）的分类：solid 化后成为自由模的离散环 S。猜想：solid 环的等价类与整数的基本配对 (n,m)（n 整除 m 的容许型）一一对应，从而为可数集；且 solid 环的张量积封闭性生成的半环同构于 (N,+)⊕(Z/2) 的 Monoid 半环，给出凝聚态世界的一个完全离散不变量。
