# 00000002152

**English.** Definition: Random lifts. Conjecture: The distribution of new eigenvalues of a random n-lift of a base graph converges to Kesten–McKay outside the countable atoms of the base spectrum (lift spectral limit).

**中文。** 定义：随机提升(random lift)。猜想：base 图的随机 n-lift 的新特征值的分布收敛到 base 谱的可数原子之外的 Kesten–McKay(提升谱极限)。
