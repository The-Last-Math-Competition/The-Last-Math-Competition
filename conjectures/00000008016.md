# 00000008016

**DEL-26 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The regularization threshold for hyperbolic-ellipt mixtures: the energy criterion e_hyp for elliptization of degenerate hyperbolic-elliptic equations (such as stationary waves of p-systems). Conjecture: e_hyp is given by the sign of the Oseledets-type splitting index (the Lyapunov exponent difference): elliptization when lambda_1 lambda_2 < 0 (opposite-sign exponents); the width of the transition zone of mixed-type behavior near the threshold is O(|lambda_1 lambda_2|^{1/2}); and inside the transition zone the scattering data of singularity transport of solutions (the reflection coefficient of the mixed boundary value problem) is the Neumann series of a Fredholm second-kind kernel, with convergence radius exactly twice the threshold distance (convergence radius rigidity). (mixed-type elliptization Lyapunov threshold)

**中文。** 定义：双曲-椭圆混合的正则化阈值：退化双曲-椭圆方程（如 p-系统的驻波）的椭圆化的能量准则 e_hyp。猜想：e_hyp 由 Oseledets 型的裂解指数（Lyapunov 指数差）的符号给出：椭圆化当 λ₁·λ₂<0（指数异号）；且阈值附近的混合型行为的过渡区宽度为 O(|λ₁λ₂|^{1/2})；过渡区内的解的奇性传输的散射数据（混合边值问题的反射系数）为 Fredholm 型第二类核的 Neumann 级数，级数的收敛半径恰为阈值距离的两倍（收敛半径刚性）。
