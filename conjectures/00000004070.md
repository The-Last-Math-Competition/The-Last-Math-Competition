# 00000004070

**English.** Definition: The finite-order invariants of a bifurcation are all the coefficients of the normal forms of each order. Conjecture: There exist arbitrarily close two-parameter points whose finite-order invariants of all orders coincide while the orbit topologies differ, with minimal codimension 3. (finite-order invariant-topology separation)

**中文。** 定义：分叉的有限阶不变量指规范形各阶系数全体。猜想：存在任意接近的两参数点其一切有限阶不变量相同而轨道拓扑不同,最小余维为 3。（有限阶不变量-拓扑分离）
