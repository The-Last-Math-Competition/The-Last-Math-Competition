# 00000002103

**SY-23 ★** — source: 55_辛几何与镜像对称.md

**English.** Definition: The Dubrovin connection is the family of flat connections on quantum cohomology. Conjecture: The Dubrovin monodromy group of the quantum cohomology of a modular vector bundle is Γ(2) (a subgroup of the modular group); the weights of the monodromy representation are given explicitly by E₂-type quasimodular periods.

**中文。** 定义：Dubrovin 联络指量子上同调的平坦联络族。猜想：模向量丛的量子上同调的 Dubrovin 单值群为 Γ(2)(模群子群);单值表示的权为由 E₂ 型的拟模形式周期显式。
