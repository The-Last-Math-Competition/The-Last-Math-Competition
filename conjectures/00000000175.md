**English.** Definition: P(s) is the prime zeta function. Conjecture: Infinitely many of the values {P(2k+1) : k ≥ 1} are irrational; moreover, the density of irrational values among the odd arguments is positive. (infinitely many irrational odd prime-zeta values)
