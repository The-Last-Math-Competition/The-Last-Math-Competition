# 00000009013

**English.** Definition: The Waldspurger index: coefficient squares versus L-values. Conjecture: Its conversion is given by torus periods of quadratic numbers, with constant 1. (Waldspurger torus period)

**中文。** 定义：Waldspurger 指数指系数平方与 L 值。猜想：其换算由二次数的 Torus 周期给出且常数为 1。（Waldspurger Torus 周期）
