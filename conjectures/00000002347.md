# 00000002347

**English.** Definition: The Bertini theorem (numerical version) concerns the smoothness of intersections of random hypersurfaces. Conjecture: The probability of numerical smoothness is 1 − Σ_i d_i^{-m} (a leading-term formula); the correction comes from volume estimates of the singular loci of the hypersurface degrees.

**中文。** 定义：Bertini 定理(数值版)指随机超曲面相交的光滑性。猜想：数值光滑性的概率为 1-\sum_i d_i^{-m}(首项公式);修正项由各超曲度的奇异轨迹的体积估计。
