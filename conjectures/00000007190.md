# 00000007190

**English.** Definition: Linear equivalence of divisors with complete linear systems. Conjecture: The complete system is the projective space, and the dimension of the space is the Riemann-Roch difference. (Riemann-Roch linear-system dimension)

**中文。** 定义：除子的线性等价：完全线性系。猜想：线性系的维数：完全系的射影空间且间的维数为 Riemann–Roch 差。（线性系 Riemann-Roch 差完全射影维数）
