**RC-05 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: A monomial crystal denotes an arrow structure on the alphabet monoid satisfying crystal axioms. Conjecture: Irreducible finite monomial crystals over finite-type alphabets correspond one-to-one with dominant integral weights, vertex counts given by the Weyl dimension formula, and only finitely many irreducibles over a fixed alphabet. (monomial crystal classification)

**中文。** 定义：幺半群晶体指字母幺半群上满足晶体公理的带箭头结构。猜想：有限型字母集上不可约有限幺半群晶体与支配整权一一对应,其顶点数由 Weyl 维数公式给出,且固定字母集上不可约者只有有限个。（幺半群晶体分类）
