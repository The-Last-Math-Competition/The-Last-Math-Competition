# 00000000811

**PV-11 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The minimal complexity of isospectral pairs is the lower bound on the number of edges needed to realize Gordon–Webb–Wilcox type isospectral pairs. Conjecture: For any isospectral non-congruent planar domain pair, the sum of boundary segment counts is at least 16, attained by the known minimal examples (8 segments each); no isospectral pair with fewer segments exists.

**中文。** 定义：等谱域对的最小复杂度指实现 Gordon–Webb–Wilcox 型等谱对所需的边数下界。猜想：任何等谱非等距平面域对的边界分段数之和 ≥ 16,且已知的最小例(各 8 段)达到该下界;不存在更少分段的等谱对。
