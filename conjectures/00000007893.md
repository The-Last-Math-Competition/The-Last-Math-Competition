# 00000007893

**HCA-23 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The shape measure of derived enhancements of tame stacks: the spectrum s(X) of the homotopy sheaf shift of the derived structure (the Tor-amplitude spectrum of the cotangent complex). Conjecture: s(X) is a mixed spectrum of [-1,0] and [0,1] type, the mixing type being completely classified by singularity type: pure [0,0] (trivial) and mixed [-1,1] (hourglass type) are the only maximal types; and the concentration measure m = h^{-1}(l)(1-l) of the hourglass type (l the derived genus) has supremum 1/4, attained at the derived symmetric point. (derived stack hourglass concentration)

**中文。** 定义：tame 栈的导出增强 的形状测度：导出结构的同伦层 shift 的谱 s(X)（余切复形的 Tor-振幅谱）。猜想：s(X) 为 [-1,0] 与 [0,1] 的混合谱，混合型完全由奇点类型分类：纯 [0,0]（平凡）与混合 [-1,1]（沙漏型）两种仅有的极大型，且沙漏型的测度集中度 m = h^{-1}(l)(1-l)（l 为导出亏格）的上确界为 1/4，在导出对称点取到。
