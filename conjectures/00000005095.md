# 00000005095

**English.** Definition: The Betti moduli space is the character variety of local systems, with its E-polynomial. Conjecture: The E-polynomial of GL_2 at genus three is an explicit rational combination of generating functions given by the weight grading; the weight-zero part agrees with the weight spectrum of the mixed Hodge structure; and the mixed coefficients are nonnegative, splitting into two branches by parity vanishing. (rational E-polynomial of GL_2 at genus three)

**中文。** 定义：Betti 模空间：局部系统的特征簇；其 E 多项式。猜想：GL₂ 亏格三的 E 多项式为母函数的显式有理组合且由权分次给出；权零部分与混合 Hodge 结构的权谱一致；混合项系数非负且按奇偶消没分为两支。（GL2g3 Betti 多项式有理式）
