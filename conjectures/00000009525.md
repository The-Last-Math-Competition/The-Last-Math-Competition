**English.** Definition: denotes channel pair; Smith–Yard of Q(φ)=Q(ψ)=0 Q(φ⊗ψ)>0 gives example, zero capacity channel contains not solution type of dimension (2,4) and solution type two class. Conjecture: most small of channel pair always input dimension is 6 and tensor product of cannot be lowered further, two element qubit channel if all zero capacity then joint capacity is always zero; always dimension D fixed when most Q(φ⊗ψ) is log(D/2)−O(1). (most small dimension)

**中文。** 定义：超激活指 Q(φ)=Q(ψ)=0 但 Q(φ⊗ψ)>0 的信道对；Smith–Yard 给出维数 (2,4) 的例子，零容量信道含不可降解型与反可降解型两类。猜想：超激活信道对的最小总输入维数为 6 且不可再降，两个 qubit 信道的张量积若都零容量则联合容量恒为零；总维数 D 固定时最大激活增益 Q(φ⊗ψ) 为 log(D/2)−O(1)。（超激活最小维数）
