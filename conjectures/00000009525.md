# 00000009525

**QIC-05 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: Superactivation refers to channel pairs with Q(phi) = Q(psi) = 0 but Q(phi tensor psi) > 0; Smith-Yard gave examples of dimensions (2,4), with zero-capacity channels of both non-degradable and anti-degradable types. Conjecture: The minimal total input dimension of superactivating pairs is 6 and cannot be reduced, the tensor product of two zero-capacity qubit channels always has zero joint capacity; and at fixed total dimension D the maximal activation gain Q(phi tensor psi) is log(D/2) - O(1). (superactivation minimal dimension)

**中文。** 定义：超激活指 Q(φ)=Q(ψ)=0 但 Q(φ⊗ψ)>0 的信道对；Smith–Yard 给出维数 (2,4) 的例子，零容量信道含不可降解型与反可降解型两类。猜想：超激活信道对的最小总输入维数为 6 且不可再降，两个 qubit 信道的张量积若都零容量则联合容量恒为零；总维数 D 固定时最大激活增益 Q(φ⊗ψ) 为 log(D/2)−O(1)。（超激活最小维数）
