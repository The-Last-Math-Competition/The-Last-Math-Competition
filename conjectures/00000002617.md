# 00000002617

**English.** Definition: Incidence algebras of locally finite posets: the structure of convolution algebras. Conjecture: The Jacobson radical of an incidence algebra is zero, with semisimplicity evidenced by pointwise invertibility of the Möbius function; the vanishing of the radical follows from local finiteness of the unit decomposition. (incidence algebra semisimplicity)

**中文。** 定义：局部有限偏序集的关联代数：卷积代数的结构。猜想：关联代数的 Jacobson 根为零且半单性的证据为 Möbius 函数的逐点可逆；根的消没由单位分解的局部有限性给出。（关联代数半单性）
