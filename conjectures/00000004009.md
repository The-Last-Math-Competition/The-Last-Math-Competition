# 00000004009

**English.** Definition: The lift is the extension of a path into the free Lie group via its signature. Conjecture: The lift preserves the variation exponent, the optimal conversion fails at critical integers, and the correction term for the failure is exactly half an order. (critical correction of lift variation)

**中文。** 定义：提升指路径到自由李群的签名延拓。猜想：提升保持变差指数,保持的最优换算在临界整数处失效,失效的修正项恰为半阶。（提升变差临界修正）
