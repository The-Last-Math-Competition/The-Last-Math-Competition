# 00000008135

**English.** Definition: The 2-categorical lifting of birational noncommutative morphisms: the Whitehead 2-type wh2(A) with Azumaya torsor classes as the pivot. Conjecture: pi_1 of wh2 is isomorphic to the Quillen group K_1 of the central variety; pi_2 is the direct sum of the negative K-group K_{-1} with Z/2, the Z/2 correction vanishing when the center is regular; and the first k-invariant is the pairing of the Azumaya class with Stokes data, vanishing in de Rham cohomology if and only if the Hodge spectral sequence of the stack degenerates. (Whitehead 2-type Azumaya Stokes pairing)

**中文。** 定义：双有理非交换态射的 2-范畴提升：以 Azumama 挠子类为轴心的 Whitehead 2-型 wh2(A)。猜想：wh2 的 π₁ 同构于中心簇的 Quillen 群 K₁；π₂ 等于负 K 群 K_{-1} 与 Z/2 的直和且该 Z/2 修正项在中心正则时消没；第一 k-不变量为 Azumaya 类与 Stokes 数据的配对且其在 de Rham 上同调为零当且仅当叠的 Hodge 谱退化。
