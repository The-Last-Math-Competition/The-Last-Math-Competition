# 00000009875

**English.** Definition: Inviscid Burgers turbulence: the energy spectrum of solutions with white-noise initial data. Conjecture: The energy spectrum of white-noise-velocity Burgers is asymptotically k^{-2} times an explicit amplitude at high wavenumbers; the amplitude is explicitly given by the square of the initial spectral density, and the spectral slope is invariant in time. (Burgers spectral slope and amplitude)

**中文。** 定义：无粘 Burgers 湍流：白噪声初值下的解的能量谱。猜想：白噪声速度 Burgers 的能量谱在高波数渐近为 k^{−2} 乘显式振幅；振幅由初始谱密度的平方显式给出且谱斜率在一切时刻不变。（Burgers 谱斜率与振幅）
