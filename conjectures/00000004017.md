# 00000004017

**English.** Definition: BPHZ renormalization is the canonical subtraction scheme that removes divergences in regularity structures. Conjecture: The BPHZ constants of rough PDEs are polynomials in rational numbers and zeta values, and the first five constants vary quasi-polynomially with the spatial dimension. (structure of BPHZ constants for rough PDEs)

**中文。** 定义：BPHZ 重整化指正则性结构中减除发散的规范方案。猜想：粗 PDE 的 BPHZ 常数为有理数与 ζ 值的多项式,前五个常数随空间维数拟多项式变化。（粗 PDE 的 BPHZ 常数结构）
