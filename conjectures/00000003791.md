# 00000003791

**English.** Definition: Variational inequalities: structural problems of finding u so that inner-product inequalities hold. Conjecture: Existence and uniqueness of solutions: existence and uniqueness under strong monotonicity and coercivity, with the rate of uniqueness the constant ratio. (variational strong monotone coercive unique existence rate)

**中文。** 定义：变分不等式：寻找 u 使内积不等式成立的结构问题。猜想：解的存在唯一：强单调与强制条件的解的存在唯一且唯一性的率为常数比。（变分强单调强制唯一存在率）
