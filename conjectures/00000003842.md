**RC-22 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: The hypoplactic monoid denotes the refinement quotient of Knuth equivalence by adjoining hypoplactic relations, whose classes are characterized by quasi-symmetric leading-term exponent vectors. Conjecture: The number of hypoplactic classes of length at most ℓ over an n-letter alphabet equals the number of plane partitions in a 2×n×ℓ box. (hypo class-plane partition count)

**中文。** 定义：hypoplactic 幺半群指在 Knuth 等价上再并入超对称关系所得的加细商,等价类由拟对称首项指数向量刻画。猜想：长度 ℓ、字母至多 n 的 hypoplactic 类的个数恰等于盒子 2×n×ℓ 内平面分拆的个数。（hypo 类-平面分拆计数）
