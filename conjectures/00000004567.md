# 00000004567

**English.** Definition: Deligne periods are the period vectors of critical L-values. Conjecture: Its transcendence first instance is the period ratio of the L-values of an explicit Hecke character, the ratio is transcendental, and the criterion is an explicit parity condition on the weight difference of the character. (parity transcendence criterion of Deligne periods)

**中文。** 定义：Deligne 周期指临界 L 值的周期向量。猜想：其超越首例为显式 Hecke 特征的 L 值周期比,比值超越且判据为特征权差的奇偶显式条件。（Deligne 周期奇偶超越判据）
