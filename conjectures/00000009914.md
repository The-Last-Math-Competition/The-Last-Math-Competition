**English.** Definition: Persistence landscapes: isometric-type embeddings of persistence diagrams into function spaces. Conjecture: The entropy integral of the norm of the reproducing kernel Hilbert space of persistence landscapes converges, and the lower bound of the integral is the entropy of the diagonal distance distribution of the birth-death plane; the kernel of the two spaces is an explicit triangular basis. (landscape RKHS entropy integral)

**中文。** 定义：持久景观：持久图到函数空间的等距型嵌入。猜想：持久景观的再生核 Hilbert 空间的范数的熵积分收敛且积分的下界为出生-死亡平面的对角线距离分布的熵；两空间的核为显式三角基。（景观 RKHS 熵积分）
