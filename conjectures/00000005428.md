# 00000005428

**English.** Definition: The elliptic measure is the hitting distribution of a second-order elliptic operator. Conjecture: The optimal constant of the boundary conversion is given by an explicit functional of the Lipschitz constant, one at flat boundaries, and the functional is monotone. (Lipschitz functional constant of boundary conversions)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆测度指二阶椭圆算子的击中分布。猜想：边界换算的最优常数由利普希茨常数的显式泛函给出,平坦边界处为一且泛函单调。（边界换算利普希茨泛函常数）
