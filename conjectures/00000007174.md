# 00000007174

**English.** Definition: The extrema of the condition number with spectral-ratio control. Conjecture: The lower bound of the spectral condition number is the antisymmetric norm, and the realization of the bound is the normal pair. (normal-pair condition-number lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：条件数的极值：谱比的控制。猜想：条件数的谱下界：谱的条件数的下界为反对称范数且界的实现为正规对。（条件数反对称正规对实现下界谱）
