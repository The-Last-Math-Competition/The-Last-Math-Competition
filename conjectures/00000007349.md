# 00000007349

**English.** Definition: The hierarchy of internal sets with inner and outer. Conjecture: The algebraic closure of internal sets holds, and the exception of the closure is the standard-part map. (standard-part internal closure exception)

**中文。** 定义：内部集合的层次：内与外。猜想：内外的分界：内集的代数封闭且闭的例外为标准部分映射。（内外标准部分映射内集代数封闭例外分界）
