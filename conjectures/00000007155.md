# 00000007155

**English.** Definition: The min-max principle, Courant-Fischer. Conjecture: The branching characterization of the k-th eigenvalue holds, and the combination of the characterization is the intersection of subspaces. (subspace-intersection Courant-Fischer branching)

**中文。** 定义：极大-极小原理：Courant–Fischer。猜想：极值指标的分支：第 k 特征值的分支刻画且刻的组合为子空间的交。（Courant-Fischer 子空间交分支指标刻画）
