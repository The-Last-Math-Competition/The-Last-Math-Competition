# 00000004670

**SG3-10 △** — source: 141_交换代数IV_奇点理论.md

**English.** Definition: The Milnor number and the topological type are the two layers of invariants of a singularity. Conjecture: There exist two hypersurface singularities with the same Milnor number and the same spectrum but different topological types, and the minimal number of variables of the separation is three, realized by an explicit pair of singularities. (same mu and spectrum-topology separation in three variables)

**中文。** 定义：Milnor 数与拓扑型指奇点的两层不变量。猜想：存在两个超曲面奇点的 Milnor 数与谱均相同而拓扑型不同,分离的最小变量数为三且由显式奇点对实现。（μ 谱同-拓扑分离变量三）
