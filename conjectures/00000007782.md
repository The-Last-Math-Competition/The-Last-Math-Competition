# 00000007782

**ACG-02 ◆** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral gap lambda_1 of an isotropic log-concave measure mu is the first nonzero eigenvalue of its Neumann-type problem, and the KLS constant psi_n is the infimum of Cheeger isoperimetric constants. Conjecture: The optimal order of psi_n is n^{-1/2}, i.e. a universal c>0 gives psi_n >= c n^{-1/2}; and on the ball the asymptotic constant of psi is phi(Phi^{-1}(1/4)) with standard normal density phi and distribution Phi, exactly the bottleneck estimate limit. (KLS order and ball constant)

**中文。** 定义：迷向 log-concave 测度 μ 的谱隙 λ_1 为其 Neumann 型特征值问题第一非零特征值；KLS 常数 ψ_n 为 Cheeger 等周常数的下确界。猜想：ψ_n 的最优阶为 n^{-1/2}，即存在普适 c>0 使 ψ_n ≥ c n^{-1/2}；且在球体上 ψ 的渐近常数为 φ(Φ^{-1}(1/4))（φ、Φ 为标准正态密度与分布），该常数恰为瓶颈估计的极限值。
