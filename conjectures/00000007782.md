# 00000007782

**ACG-02 ◆** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: spectral gap λ_1 of isotropy log-concave measure μ is its Neumann type eigenvalue problem the- one non- zero eigenvalue; KLS constant ψ_n is infimum of Cheeger isoperimetric constant. Conjecture: optimal order of ψ_n is n^{-1/2}, namely exists universal c>0 such that ψ_n ≥ c n^{-1/2}; and asymptotic constant of in ball body on ψ is φ(Φ^{-1}(1/4)) (φ, Φ is standard positive state density and distribution), the constant is limit of exactly bottleneck estimate.

**中文。** 定义：迷向 log-concave 测度 μ 的谱隙 λ_1 为其 Neumann 型特征值问题第一非零特征值；KLS 常数 ψ_n 为 Cheeger 等周常数的下确界。猜想：ψ_n 的最优阶为 n^{-1/2}，即存在普适 c>0 使 ψ_n ≥ c n^{-1/2}；且在球体上 ψ 的渐近常数为 φ(Φ^{-1}(1/4))（φ、Φ 为标准正态密度与分布），该常数恰为瓶颈估计的极限值。
