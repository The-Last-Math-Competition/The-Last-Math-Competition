**English.** Definition: Carleson embedding: boundedness of embeddings when measures satisfy the Carleson condition (exact in the scalar case); the matrix-valued version is open. Conjecture: The matrix-valued Carleson embedding is bounded if and only if the family of testing inequalities for the matrix measure is bounded; the constant is the linear product of the testing constant and dimension, and the dimension factor cannot be removed. (matrix Carleson characterization)

**中文。** 定义：Carleson 嵌入：测度满足 Carleson 条件时嵌入有界（标量情形精确）；矩阵值版本开放。猜想：矩阵值 Carleson 嵌入有界当且仅当矩阵测度的检测不等式族有界；常数为测试常数与维数的线性积且维数因子不可去。（矩阵 Carleson 特征）
