**English.** Definition: The Fukaya category is the Lagrangian Floer category of symplectic manifolds. Conjecture: Split generation of the category holds by the exceptional sequence of the symplectic cone structure, and the length of the sequence is the exceptional dual of mirror symmetry. (cone-exceptional mirror-dual split generation)
