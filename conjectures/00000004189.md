**English.** Definition: A canceling vertex is a pair of branch points canceling each other in a min-max sequence. Conjecture: The number of canceling vertices is at most a linear function of the parameter-family dimension p, and the upper bound is attained optimally by the antipodal-sweeping family of the sphere. (linear upper bound on canceling vertices)

**中文。** 定义：canceling 顶点指极小极大序列中相互抵消的分支点。猜想：canceling 顶点个数至多为参数族维数 p 的线性函数,上界由球面对径扫描族达到最优。（canceling 顶点线性上界）
