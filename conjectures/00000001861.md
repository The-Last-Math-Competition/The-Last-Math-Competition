**RM-21 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The elliptic ensemble is the Gaussian matrix model with correlation parameter τ ∈ [0,1]. Conjecture: The variance of the singular-value fluctuations around the ring is the exact quadratic polynomial in τ, σ² = (1+τ²)/2; and the covariance structure of the CLT is interpolated by the one-parameter family in τ.

**中文。** 定义：椭圆系综指相关参数 τ∈[0,1] 的高斯矩阵模型。猜想：奇异值围绕环的涨落方差为 τ 的二次多项式 σ² = (1+τ²)/2(精确);CLT 的协方差结构由 τ 单参数族插值。
