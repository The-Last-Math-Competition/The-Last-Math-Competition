# 00000009969

**MRG-29 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: structure of ergodic invariant measure space of ergodic measure: ×b map of expansion map. Conjecture: counting entropy part sheaf: every sheaf of isomorphism class of ×b ergodic measure is finite of algebra class and entropy spectrum of non- algebra set; sheaf of zero is logarithm lattice and lattice point is prime factor power of b. (expansion measure entropy part sheaf)

**中文。** 定义：展开映射的遍历测度：×b 映射的遍历不变测度空间的结构。猜想：×b 遍历测度的同构类的计数按熵分层：每层为代数类的有限并加零测的非代数薄集；层间的熵谱为对数格且格点为 b 的素因子幂。（展开测度熵分层）
