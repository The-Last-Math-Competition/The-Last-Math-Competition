# 00000009969

**MRG-29 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Ergodic measures of expanding maps: the structure of the space of ergodic invariant measures of the times-b map. Conjecture: The isomorphism classes of times-b ergodic measures are stratified by entropy: each stratum is a finite union of algebraic classes plus a measure-zero thin set of nonalgebraic ones; the entropy spectrum between strata is a logarithmic lattice with lattice points the prime power factors of b. (expanding measure entropy stratification)

**中文。** 定义：展开映射的遍历测度：×b 映射的遍历不变测度空间的结构。猜想：×b 遍历测度的同构类的计数按熵分层：每层为代数类的有限并加零测的非代数薄集；层间的熵谱为对数格且格点为 b 的素因子幂。（展开测度熵分层）
