# 00000009814

**DDG-24 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Discrete Calabi-Yau metrics of 3-manifolds: flat discrete connections on cell decompositions. Conjecture: The necessary condition for a regular cell decomposition of a 3-manifold to admit an everywhere-flat discrete metric is vanishing Euler characteristic; the condition is sufficient when the manifold is a torus power or finite quotient thereof, and the moduli space of discrete flat metrics has dimension equal to the first Betti number. (discrete Calabi-Yau existence)

**中文。** 定义：三维流形的离散 Calabi–Yau 度量：胞腔分解上的平坦离散联络。猜想：三维流形的正则胞腔分解容许处处平坦的离散度量的必要条件为欧拉示性数为零；条件在流形为环面幂或其有限商时充分且离散平坦度量的模空间维数等于第一 Betti 数。（离散 Calabi-Yau 存在性）
