**English.** Definition: The state graph of the cellular-automaton representation of p-adic continued fractions is the combinatorial model of the expansion. Conjecture: Acyclicity of the state graph implies termination of the algorithm; and the combinatorial criterion for termination is the strict decrease of the state cardinality. (algorithm termination acyclicity criterion)

**中文。** 定义：p 进连分数的元胞自动机表示的状态图为展开的组合模型。猜想：状态图无环蕴含算法终止；且终止的组合判据为状态的势严格下降。（算法终止无环判据）
