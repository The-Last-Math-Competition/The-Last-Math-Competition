# 00000004720

**English.** Definition: Factor counting and rescaling are the two equivalence layers of words. Conjecture: There exist two infinite words whose finite factor counts all coincide yet which are not equivalent up to rescaling, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of a Toeplitz word and its mirror. (factor counting-rescaling separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：因子计数与重标指词的两个等价层面。猜想：存在两无限词的一切有限因子计数相同而非重标等价,分离由 Toeplitz 与镜像词的显式对实现。（因子计数-重标分离对）
