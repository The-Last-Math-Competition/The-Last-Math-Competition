# 00000001887

**English.** Definition: The difference-closure is the model closure over difference fields. Conjecture: The difference-closure of a geometric-rank difference field (non-periodic geometric rank) has SU-rank 1; this closure is a minimal difference field (no intermediate difference-closed subfields).

**中文。** 定义：差闭包指差分域上的模型闭包。猜想：几何级差域(非周期几何级)的差闭包的 SU-秩为 1;该闭包为极小的差分域(无中间差闭子域)。
