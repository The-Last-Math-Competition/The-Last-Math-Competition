**English.** Definition: The remainder of the deficit formula is the higher-order correction of the isoperimetric deficit. Conjecture: The remainder is always a higher power of the asymmetry; the optimal exponent of the power is two; and the exponent window closes within dimension plus one. (asymmetry power of deficit remainders)
