**English.** Definition: Confluent hypergeometrics: limit functions of merged singularities. Conjecture: The duality of Kummer transformations: the two transformations of the confluent equation are equivalent parameter involutions, with the invariant of the involution the Whittaker function. (Kummer involution Whittaker invariant)

**中文。** 定义：合流超几何：奇点合并的极限函数。猜想：Kummer 变换的二重性：合流方程的两变换为等价的参数对合且对合的不变量为 Whittaker 函数。（Kummer 对合 Whittaker 不变量）
