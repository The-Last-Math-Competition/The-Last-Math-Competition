# 00000000314

**English.** Definition: The Markov constant M(α) = limsup 1/‖qα‖ measures the quality of worst approximation. Conjecture: The set {M(√p) : p prime} is dense in the half-open interval from √5 to 3; and the density follows from the transition statistics of the continued-fraction partial quotients of square roots of primes. (prime square root Markov density)

**中文。** 定义：Markov 常数 M(α)=limsup 1/‖qα‖ 度量最坏逼近质量。猜想：{M(√p): p 素数} 在从 √5 到 3 的半开区间中稠密；且稠密性由素数平方根的连分数部分商的转移统计给出。（素数平方根 Markov 稠密）
