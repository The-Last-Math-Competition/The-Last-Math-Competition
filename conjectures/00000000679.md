# 00000000679

**KT-79 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: Equilibria of the gradient flow of the Möbius energy are critical knots of the energy functional. Conjecture: For every crossing number c, at least c/4 knot types with crossing number ≤ c admit Möbius-energy critical representatives, i.e. the critical-point count grows linearly with complexity; every critical point has bounded Morse index.

**中文。** 定义：Möbius 能量的梯度流的平衡点指能量泛函的临界纽结。猜想：对每个交叉数 c,交叉数 ≤ c 的纽结型中至少有 c/4 个型存在 Möbius 能量临界代表,即临界点计数随复杂度线性增长;每个临界点的 Morse 指标有界。
