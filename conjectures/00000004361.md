**KK2-01 ◆** — source: 131_代数K理论II_迹方法.md

**English.** Definition: THH is topological Hochschild homology, and the Bokstedt computation determines its coefficient ring. Conjecture: The coefficients of THH(Z_p; F_p) form an explicit polynomial-tensored-exterior algebra over F_p, with polynomial generator of degree 2p-2, and the table is complete with no undetermined classes. (complete table of THH Z_p coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：THH 指拓扑 Hochschild 同调,Bökstedt 计算指其系数环的确定。猜想：THH(ℤ_p; 𝔽_p) 的系数为 𝔽_p 上的显式多项式张外代数,多项式生成元度为 2p−2,表完全且无未定类。（THH Zp 系数完全表）
