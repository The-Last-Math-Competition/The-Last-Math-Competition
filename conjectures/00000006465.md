# 00000006465

**English.** Definition: Tails with the tail-probability structure of Gaussian fields. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the optimal value of the tail exponent given by the measure-entropy pair; the exponent is unique with zero deviation; and the matching of the entropy pair is a duality relation. (measure-pair conversion of the uniform optimal tail exponent)
