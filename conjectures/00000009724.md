# 00000009724

**English.** Definition: The S-matrix of multi-soliton scattering: the analytic structure of asymptotic maps of soliton collisions. Conjecture: The S-matrix of Klein-Gordon multi-soliton scattering is analytic in energy bands with poles a finite set of resonances; the resonance count of each energy band is given by an explicit formula of the spectral counting of the potential. (multi-soliton S-matrix analyticity)

**中文。** 定义：多孤子散射的 S 矩阵：孤子碰撞的渐近映射的解析结构。猜想：Klein–Gordon 多孤子散射的 S 矩阵在能量带内解析且极点为共振的有限集；每个能量带的共振个数由位势的谱计数的显式公式给出。（多孤子 S 矩阵解析）
