# 00000001406

**English.** Definition: The Torelli group is the subgroup of the mapping class group acting trivially on cohomology. Conjecture: The closure of any subgroup is given explicitly by the Zariski closure of its Johnson-homomorphism image; and the closure operation is idempotent. (Torelli closure Johnson idempotence)

**中文。** 定义：Torelli 群指作用在上同调平凡的映射类子群。猜想：任意子群的闭包由 Johnson 同态像的 Zariski 闭包显式给出；且闭包运算为幂等。（Torelli 闭包 Johnson 幂等）
