# 00000001413

**HT-23 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: Waldhausen's splitting A(X) → K(Z) is the stable splitting of algebraic K-theory spectra. Conjecture: The stable automorphism group of this splitting is a direct sum of copies of Z/2 (generated by the interchange of assembly and Whitehead); no other stable automorphisms exist.

**中文。** 定义：Waldhausen 的分裂 A(X)→K(Z) 指代数 K-理论谱的稳定分裂。猜想：该分裂的稳定自同构群为 Z/2 的直和(由装配与 Whitehead 的对换生成);不存在其他稳定自同构。
