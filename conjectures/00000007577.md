**English.** Definition: The resolution function of covers measures the granularity of intersection structure. Conjecture: The explicit characterization of the resolution domain is given by the spectrum of the cover-overlap matrix; and the spectral gap is the connectivity threshold. (resolution-spectral-gap connectivity)
