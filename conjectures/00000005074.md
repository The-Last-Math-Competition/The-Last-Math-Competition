**English.** Definition: Localized bits are the effective spin variables. Conjecture: Their decay exponent is an explicit exponential product of interaction strength and localization length, the order recursion is closed, and the asymptotic rate is the reciprocal localization length. (exponential-product order recursion of localized bits)
