# 00000002902

**English.** Definition: Permutation polynomials: polynomials inducing bijections of finite fields. Conjecture: The complete spectrum of permutation polynomials: the existence criterion for permutation polynomials of given degree d is a divisibility condition on the prime-factor spectrum of d, completely listed for d below one hundred. (permutation polynomial degree spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：置换多项式：诱导有限域双射的多项式。猜想：置换多项式的完全谱：给定次数 d 的置换多项式的存在域的判据为 d 的素因子谱的整除条件且判据在 d 小于一百完全列表。（置换多项式次数谱）
