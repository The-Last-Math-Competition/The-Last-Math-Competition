**English.** Definition: Induced densities are the constraints of induced-subgraph densities. Conjecture: The induced-density lattice of four-vertex graphs is complete, the combination of the lattice being the flag algebra; the extremal values of the lattice are explicit intervals; and densities outside the intervals are characterized by triangle deficits, the characterization being a system of linear inequalities. (complete flag-algebra lattice of four-vertex induced densities)
