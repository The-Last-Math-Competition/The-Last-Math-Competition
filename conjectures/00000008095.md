# 00000008095

**FUS-15 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The codimension rigidity of p-prime automorphisms of fusion systems: the fixed-point codimension cod(F) of the linear action of the p-prime part of Out_F(S) on the mod p group. Conjecture: cod(F) >= 1 (a nonzero codimension rigidity); the classification of minimal codimension systems with cod(F) = 1 consists of simple group fusions (a prime system list); and the duality between the codimension spectrum and exoticness: systems with cod(F) >= 3 are exotic candidates (a 3-codimension exotic criterion), the counterexample to the criterion (high codimension without exoticness) being the local system of the exceptional Weyl group E_8 (a unique E_8 exception law). (codimension-three exotic E_8 exception)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 p'-自同构的余维刚性：Out_F(S) 的 p'-部分的线性作用的模 p 群上不动点余维数 cod(F)。猜想：cod(F) ≥ 1（余维非零刚性）；且 cod(F)=1 的极小余维系统的分类为单群融合（素系统列表）；余维数谱与 fusion 的 exotic 性的对偶：cod(F) ≥ 3 的系统为 exotic 候选（3-余维 exotic 判据），判据的反例（非 exotic 的高余维）为 exceptional Weyl 群 E_8 的局部系（E_8 唯一例外律）。
