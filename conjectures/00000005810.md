**English.** Definition: The spectrum and the asymptotic cone are two geometric layers. Conjecture: There exist two shrinkers with the same spectrum but different asymptotic cones, and the separation is realized by an explicit gluing pair with the same spectrum but different asymptotics. (same spectrum-different asymptotic cone gluing separation)

**中文。** 定义：谱与渐近锥指两层几何数据。猜想：存在两收缩子的谱相同而渐近锥不同,分离由同谱异渐近的显式粘合对实现。（谱同-渐近锥异粘合分离）
