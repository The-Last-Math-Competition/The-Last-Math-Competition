**English.** Definition: The volume spectrum is the width sequence, and the non-Weyl term is the remainder of the Weyl law. Conjecture: The normalized distribution of the remainder always tends to a Poisson-type limit along p, and the variance constant depends only on the dimension. (Poissonization of the volume spectrum remainder)

**中文。** 定义：体积谱指宽度序列，非 Weyl 项指 Weyl 律的余项。猜想：余项的规范分布沿 p 恒趋于 Poisson 型极限；且方差常数仅依赖维数。（体积谱余项 Poisson 化）
