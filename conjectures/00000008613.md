# 00000008613

**English.** Definition: Estimation under differential privacy constraints. Conjecture: The loss under privacy budget epsilon is of type (d/(n^2 epsilon^2))^{1/2} with the Laplace mechanism optimal; and the nonparametric loss is controlled by the effective dimension. (privacy loss Laplace optimality)

**中文。** 定义：差分隐私约束下的估计。猜想：隐私预算 ε 的亏损为 (d/(n²ε²))^{1/2} 型且 Laplace 机制最优；非参数亏损由有效维控制。（隐私亏损 Laplace 最优）
