# 00000001054

**English.** Definition: The Weil coefficient a₁ of a curve (= −q+1+#C(F_q)). Conjecture: Over families of curves of fixed genus g, the maximum of a₁ outside the hyperelliptic locus is (g·√q) times an explicit trigonometric value (an extremal first coefficient).

**中文。** 定义：曲线的 Weil 系数 a₁(= −q+1+#C(F_q))。猜想：固定亏格 g 的曲线族上 a₁ 的最大值在超椭圆曲线外为 (g·√q)·显式三角函数值(首系数极值)。
