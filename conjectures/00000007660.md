# 00000007660

**English.** Definition: Reflection spectra and stability spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two absorbing boundaries with the same reflection spectrum but different stability spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit pole shift with the same reflection but different dissipation. (same reflection-different stability shift separation)

**中文。** 定义：反射谱与稳定谱指两层。猜想：存在两吸收边界的反射谱相同而稳定谱不同,分离由同反射异耗散的显式极点移位实现。（反射同-稳定异移位分离）
