**English.** Definition: The natural proof barrier is the Razborov-Rudich counterfactual framework for natural lower bound methods. Conjecture: The barrier admits an algebraized extension: any lower bound method that is natural and explicit for IPS implies the nonexistence of one-way functions; the algebraized framework and the Boolean framework separate at the degree threshold. (algebraized characterization of the natural barrier)

**中文。** 定义：自然证明障碍指 Razborov–Rudich 的自然下界方法反证框架。猜想：障碍可代数化推广:任何对 IPS 自然且显式的下界方法都蕴含单向函数不存在;代数化框架与布尔框架在度阈处分离。（自然障碍代数化刻画）
