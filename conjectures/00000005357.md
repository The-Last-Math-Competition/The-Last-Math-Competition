# 00000005357

**English.** Definition: Cluster stability is the robustness of the partition to perturbations. Conjecture: The stability threshold of three classes is an explicit quadratic in the reciprocal of the spectral gap, below the threshold the partition is unique, and the upper bound cannot be lowered. (gap-quadratic stability threshold of three-class clustering)

**中文。** 定义：聚类稳定性指分块对扰动的稳健性。猜想：三类的稳定阈值为谱间隙倒数的显式二次式,阈值下划分唯一且上界不可再降。（三类聚类间隙二次稳定阈）
