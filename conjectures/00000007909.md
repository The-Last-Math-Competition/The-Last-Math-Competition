# 00000007909

**English.** Definition: The Jacquet-Rallis trace comparison in the local Langlands correspondence: the homological transfer factor Delta of correlated isomorphic members of the odd unitary group U(V) and GL_N. Conjecture: The concentrated error term of the matching identity of the relative trace formula is controlled by the spectral deviation of the formal degree, whose constant agrees with the residue of the analytic continuation pole of the Plancherel measure; and the local volume normalization coefficient of the norm-variety version of the arithmetic fundamental lemma (ALT) is a power of 2 with exponent the integer part of half the unipotent orbit dimension. (relative trace formal degree deviation)

**中文。** 定义：局部 Langlands 对应的 Jacquet–Rallis 迹比较：奇酉群 U(V) 与 GL_N 的相关同构成员的同调转移因子 Δ。猜想：相对迹公式的匹配恒等式的误差集中项由 formal degree 的谱偏差控制，偏差常数与 Plancherel 测度的解析延拓极点的残数一致；且算术基本引理（ALT）的范数簇版本的局部体积归一化系数为 2 的幂，幂指数由 unipotent 轨道维数的一半取整。
