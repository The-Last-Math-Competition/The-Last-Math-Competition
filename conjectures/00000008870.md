# 00000008870

**English.** Definition: Chilling and warming: the arithmetic of Go endgame scoring. Conjecture: The G-value number field of Go positions contains infinitesimal levels; and the kernel of the levels is the infinitesimal lattice. (Go value infinitesimal lattice levels)

**中文。** 定义：chilling 与 warming 为 Go 后缀计分的算术。猜想：Go 位置的 G-值数域包含无穷小层次；且层次的核为 infinitesimal 格。（Go-值 infinitesimal 格无穷小层次）
