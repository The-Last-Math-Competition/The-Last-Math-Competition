# 00000003972

**English.** Definition: PCR is the polynomial resolution system with the ring extension by Boolean axioms. Conjecture: The degree d and size s of PCR satisfy the tradeoff s >= exp(Omega(d^2/n)), and the Tseitin formula family makes both bounds tight simultaneously, so they cannot be improved. (tight PCR degree-size tradeoff)

**中文。** 定义：PCR 指带布尔公理环扩张的多项式消解系统。猜想：PCR 的度 d 与尺寸 s 满足权衡 s ≥ exp(Ω(d²/n)),且 Tseitin 公式族使两端界同时取等,故不可改进。（PCR 度-尺寸紧权衡）
