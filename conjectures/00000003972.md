**PC2-02 ★** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: PCR denotes polynomial solution system of ring extension. Conjecture: d of PCR and size s satisfies weight s ≥ exp(Ω(d²/n)), and Tseitin formula family such that two bound simultaneously, hence cannot be improved. (PCR - size compact weight)

**中文。** 定义：PCR 指带布尔公理环扩张的多项式消解系统。猜想：PCR 的度 d 与尺寸 s 满足权衡 s ≥ exp(Ω(d²/n)),且 Tseitin 公式族使两端界同时取等,故不可改进。（PCR 度-尺寸紧权衡）
