# 00000002414

**English.** Definition: The cohomological dimension is the virtual cohomological dimension of a group. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of finitely generated proper subfields is unbounded; and the construction of unboundedness is iterated cyclic extensions. (proper subfield vcd unbounded spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：上同调维数为群的上同调虚维数。猜想：有限生成真子域的维数谱无界；且无界的构造为迭代循环扩张。（真子域 vcd 无界谱）
