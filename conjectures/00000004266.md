# 00000004266

**English.** Definition: The unit component of the tensor category of a VOA is the group of invertible objects. Conjecture: The realizable group spectrum consists of direct products of cyclic and dihedral groups, and the order of the group divides the square of the global dimension of the VOA. (group spectrum restriction of the VOA unit component)

**中文。** 定义：VOA 张量范畴的单位分支指可逆对象群。猜想：可实现群谱为循环群与二面体群的直积,群的阶整除 VOA 全局维数的平方。（VOA 单位分支群谱限制）
