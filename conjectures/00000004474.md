**English.** Definition: The Gamma series is the mirror period series of a Fano variety. Conjecture: The Gamma series of P^3 is an explicit hypergeometric product, the coefficient table is complete, and it matches the Gromov-Witten potential through the mirror map up to degree 10. (P^3 Gamma table matching up to degree 10)
