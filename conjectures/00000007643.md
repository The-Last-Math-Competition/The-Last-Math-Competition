# 00000007643

**English.** Definition: The vanishing viscosity of quasilinear hyperbolic equations as the dissipative regularization of shocks. Conjecture: The convergence rate of the viscosity limit is controlled by shock strength; and the convergence error is the boundary-layer thickness. (boundary-layer viscosity-vanishing convergence)

**中文。** 定义：拟线性双曲的黏性消失为激波的耗散正则。猜想：黏性极限的收敛速率由激波强度控制；且收敛的误差为边界层厚度。（黏性消失边界层收敛）
