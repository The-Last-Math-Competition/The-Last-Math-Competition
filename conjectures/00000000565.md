# 00000000565

**CA-65 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The exchange graph of commutative diagrams. Conjecture: The automorphism group of the exchange graph of finite type is isomorphic to the Weyl group semidirect the graph automorphisms (the type-dependent part is known; state the extension to affine types).

**中文。** 定义：交换图 exchange graph。猜想：有限型 exchange graph 的自同构群 ≅ Weyl 群 ⋊ 图自同构(已知 (与类型有关的) 部分;陈述对仿射型推广)。
