# 00000004890

**MT2-20 △** — source: 148_数学的元问题.md

**English.** Definition: Consistency strength and combinatorial complexity are the two dimensions of independent statements. Conjecture: There exist two independent statements of the same consistency strength with different minimal proof-certificate sizes, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of a large cardinal and its combinatorial companion. (same strength-certificate size separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：一致性强度与组合复杂度指独立陈述的两维。猜想：存在两独立陈述的一致性强度相同而最小证明证书尺寸相异,分离由大基数与其组合伴随的显式对实现。（强度同-证书尺寸分离对）
