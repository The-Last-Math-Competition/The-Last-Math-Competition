# 00000004030

**English.** Definition: Noise-limit-cycle aggregation is the phenomenon that the limits of RDE solution sets under different noise supports coincide. Conjecture: There exist two supports for which the solutions aggregate onto the same limit cycle although the supports differ, and the aggregation exponent is given explicitly by the Melnikov integral of the vector field. (limit-cycle aggregation exponent)

**中文。** 定义：噪声-极限环聚集指不同噪声支撑下 RDE 解集的极限重合现象。猜想：存在两支撑使解聚集于同一极限环而支撑不同,聚集指数由向量场的 Melnikov 积分显式给出。（极限环聚集指数）
