# 00000001100

**English.** Definition: The orbits of maximal subgroups of the finite-field affine group refer to the orbit structure of the natural action. Conjecture: The orbit count of a maximal subgroup is always q plus a uniformly bounded constant term; and the kernel of the constant is the subfield index. (affine maximal-subgroup subfield-index orbits)

**中文。** 定义：有限域仿射群的极大子群轨道指其在自然作用下的轨道结构。猜想：极大子群的轨道计数恒为 q 加一致有界常数项；常数的核为子域指标。（仿射极大子群子域指标轨道）
