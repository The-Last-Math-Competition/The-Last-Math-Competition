**LT-98 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: Zonal polynomials are the spherical functions at Jack parameter two. Conjecture: The LR coefficients of Zonal polynomials are combinatorial numbers given by nonnegative alternating sums of even integers; and the combinatorial realization of the coefficients is the counting of matching pairs. (Zonal-LR positivity)

**中文。** 定义：Zonal 多项式为 Jack 参数二的球函数。猜想：Zonal 的 LR 系数为非负偶交错和的组合数；且系数的组合实现为匹配对的计数。（Zonal-LR 正性）
