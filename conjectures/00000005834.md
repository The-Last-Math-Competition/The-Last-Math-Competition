# 00000005834

**MR3-04 ★** — source: 180_模表示II.md

**English.** Definition: Modular characters are the characters in positive characteristic (Brauer characters), compared with characteristic-zero values. Conjecture: At the prime three, the difference of regular-class values with characteristic-zero values is carried by a single irreducible character; the absolute value of the difference is at most 1; and the combination of the difference is a defect-one group, whose order divides the third power of the group order. (defect-one difference of regular-class values at the prime three)

**中文。** 定义：模特征标：正特征下的特征标（Brauer 特征标）与特征零值的对照。猜想：素三的正则类值与特征零值的差由单一不可约特征标承载；差的绝对值不超过 1；差的组合为亏一群且亏的阶整除群阶的三次幂。（素三正则类值亏一差）
