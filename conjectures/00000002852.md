# 00000002852

**English.** Definition: Waring decomposition: sums-of-powers decompositions of symmetric tensors. Conjecture: Genericity of Waring rank: the rank of a general binary form is ⌈(d+1)/2⌉, and generic ranks in the multivariate case follow a Kruskal-type addition law; the exception to addition is the absence of real points on quadrics. (Waring generic rank addition)

**中文。** 定义：Waring 分解：对称张量的和的幂分解。猜想：Waring 秩的一般性：一般二元形式的秩为 ⌈(d+1)/2⌉ 且一般秩的取值在多元情形为 Kruskal 型加法定律；加法的例外为二次簇的实点缺失。（Waring 一般秩加法）
