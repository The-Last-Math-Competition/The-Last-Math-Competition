# 00000002694

**English.** Definition: Degeneration of dynamics: homogenization limits of polynomial maps. Conjecture: Dynamical limits of homogenized families: the Green measures of degree-degenerate families converge to a Fubini–Study type measure at rate a negative logarithmic power of the degree difference; the support of the limit measure is the degenerate image curve. (homogenization Green measure limit)

**中文。** 定义：fsrc 动力学退化：多项式映射的齐次化极限。猜想：齐次化族的动力学极限：度退化族的 Green 测度收敛到 Fubini–Study 型测度且收敛速率为度差的负对数幂；极限测度的支集为退化的像曲线。（齐次化 Green 测度极限）
