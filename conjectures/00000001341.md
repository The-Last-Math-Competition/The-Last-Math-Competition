# 00000001341

**English.** Definition: The Lang–Trotter conjecture predicts #{p ≤ x : a_p(E) = t} ~ C(E,t)·√x/log x with C(E,t) explicit. Conjecture: The three constants C(E,0), C(E,1) and C(E,−1) are transcendental for every elliptic curve E without complex multiplication; transcendence follows from the linear independence of the Gamma-factor products.

**中文。** 定义：Lang–Trotter 猜想预言 #{p≤x: a_p(E)=t} ~ C(E,t)·√x/log x,C(E,t) 为显式常数。猜想：C(E,0)、C(E,1) 与 C(E,−1) 三个常数对一切无复乘 E 均为超越数;超越性由其 Gamma 因子乘积的线性无关性给出。
