# 00000004027

**English.** Definition: The Levy area is the area process of the second-level signature of a planar driver. Conjecture: Under rough drivers the distribution of the sign of the area converges to a symmetric law if and only if the second level of the driver has zero mean, and the asymmetry is given by an explicit functional of that mean. (sign law of the rough Levy area)

**中文。** 定义：Lévy 区域指平面驱动第二层签名的面积过程。猜想：粗糙驱动下面积符号分布收敛于对称律当且仅当驱动第二层均值为零,不对称度由该均值的显式泛函给出。（粗 Lévy 面积符号律）
