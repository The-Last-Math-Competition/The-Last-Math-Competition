# 00000003479

**English.** Definition: Harmonious coloring is a proper coloring making edge-endpoint color sums pairwise distinct. Conjecture: The harmonious chromatic number of the random graph G(n,p) concentrates at (1+o(1)) times the square root of np with concentration constant 1; the concentration holds uniformly for p at least log n/n, and the low-probability region degenerates to a linear multiple of the classical chromatic number. (harmonic chromatic concentration law)

**中文。** 定义：和谐染色为使每条边两端色和互异的表现染色。猜想：随机图 G(n,p) 的和谐色数集中于根号下 np 的一加小 o 倍且集中常数为 1；集中现象在 p 至少 log n/n 一致成立且低概率区退化为经典色数的线性倍。（和谐色数集中律）
