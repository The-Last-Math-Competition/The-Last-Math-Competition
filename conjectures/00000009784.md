**English.** Definition: regularization of eta invariant: spectrum not symmetric of Dirac operator. Conjecture: eta invariant with respect to metric perturbed bounded difference always Lipschitz constant of quantity is of explicit function; spectral gap vanishes when eta of spectral gap reciprocal is exactly integer spectrum flow and without part number positive. (eta difference Lipschitz)

**中文。** 定义：Dirac 算子的 eta 不变量：谱不对称性的正则化。猜想：eta 不变量关于度量扰动的有界变差总量的 Lipschitz 常数为谱隙倒数的显式函数；谱隙消失时 eta 的跳变恰为整数谱流且无分数修正。（eta 变差 Lipschitz 律）
