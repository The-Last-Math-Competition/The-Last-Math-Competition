# 00000009784

**OIE-24 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: The eta invariant of Dirac operators: regularization of spectral asymmetry. Conjecture: The Lipschitz constant of the eta invariant with respect to total bounded variation of metric perturbations is an explicit function of the inverse spectral gap; when the spectral gap vanishes, the jump of eta is exactly the integer spectral flow, with no fractional correction. (eta variation Lipschitz law)

**中文。** 定义：Dirac 算子的 eta 不变量：谱不对称性的正则化。猜想：eta 不变量关于度量扰动的有界变差总量的 Lipschitz 常数为谱隙倒数的显式函数；谱隙消失时 eta 的跳变恰为整数谱流且无分数修正。（eta 变差 Lipschitz 律）
