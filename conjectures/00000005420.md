# 00000005420

**English.** Definition: Harmonic measure and boundary dimension are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two domains with the same measure equivalence class but different boundary dimensions, and the separation is realized by explicit slit-pruning surgery. (same measure-different boundary dimension pruning separation)

**中文。** 定义：调和测度与边界维数指两层。猜想：存在两域的测度等价类相同而边界维数不同,分离由裂隙修剪的显式手术实现。（测度同-边界维数异修剪分离）
