# 00000008174

**English.** Definition: The convergence of the root Hermite factor of BKZ lattice reduction: the b to infinity asymptotics of the output quality delta_b (root-Hermite factor) of BKZ-b. Conjecture: The exact asymptotics of delta_b is delta_b = (b/(2 pi e))^{1/(2b)} (pi b)^{1/(4b^2)} (a double correction formula); the matching threshold between experiment and asymptotics is b >= 60 (a sixty matching threshold); the deviation for b < 60 is 1 + 1/b^2 (a square deviation law); and the total number of core SVP calls of BKZ is n^2 b (a calls-dimension-block product law), the product constant being tight (a tight product constant). (BKZ root-Hermite double correction formula)

**中文。** 定义：格基规约的 BKZ 的根 Hermite 因子的收敛：BKZ-b 的输出质量 δ_b（root-Hermite factor）的 b→∞ 的渐近。猜想：δ_b 的精确渐近为 δ_b = (b/(2πe))^{1/(2b)}·(πb)^{1/(4b²)}（双修正公式）；且 δ_b 的实际（实验）与渐近的匹配阈为 b ≥ 60（六十匹配阈）；b<60 的偏差为 1+1/b²（平方偏差律）；BKZ 的核心 SVP 的调用的总数为 n²·b（调用-维数-块积律），积常数为紧（紧积常数）。
