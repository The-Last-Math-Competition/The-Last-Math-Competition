**English.** Definition: The spectral stratification with the layer-depth structure of the spectrum. Conjecture: The depth spectrum is finite and the bound of finiteness is the eccentric tower height; the saturation of the eccentric tower height is the composition sequence; and the depth upper bound is the variety dimension, attained by projective spaces. (finite eccentric-tower depth of stratifications)

**中文。** 定义：谱分层：谱的层深结构。猜想：深度谱有限且有限的界为偏心塔高；偏心塔高的饱和为合成序列；深度的上界为簇维数且界由射影空间达到。（分层深度偏心塔高有限）
