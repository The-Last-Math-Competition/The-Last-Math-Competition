# 00000008602

**MLC-12 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Debiased Lasso is the Lasso with a noise correction. Conjecture: The Berry-Esseen error of the debiased Lasso is s^{3/2} log^{3/2} p / n^2 and optimal. (optimal debiased Berry-Esseen error)

**中文。** 定义：去偏 Lasso 指噪声校正后的 Lasso。猜想：去偏 Lasso 的 Berry–Esseen 误差为 s^{3/2}log^{3/2}p/n² 且最优。（去偏 BE 误差最优）
