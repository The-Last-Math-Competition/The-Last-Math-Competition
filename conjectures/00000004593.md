# 00000004593

**English.** Definition: The Paterson-Sullivan measure is the family of conformal measures on the boundary. Conjecture: The limit of the PS dimension in a deformation family is an explicit logarithmic function of the spectral radius, and the limit exists and is continuous under convex-cocompact deformations. (PS dimension spectral-radius logarithm law)

**中文。** 定义：Paterson–Sullivan 测度指边界上的共形测度族。猜想：PS 维数在形变族的极限为谱半径的对数型显式函数,极限存在且关于凸余紧形变连续。（PS 维数谱半径对数律）
