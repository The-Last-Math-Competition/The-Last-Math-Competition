**English.** Definition: Two-time-scale stochastic approximation is the coupled iteration scheme of two step sizes. Conjecture: At scale ratio two the convergence rate is the slow-scale rate times an explicit correction, the correction is one step, and the constant matches exactly. (one-step correction constant of two-scale convergence)

**中文。** 定义：双时间尺度随机逼近指两步长的耦合迭代格式。猜想：尺度比为二时收敛速率为慢尺度速率乘显式修正,修正为一步且常数精确匹配。（双尺度收敛一步修正常数）
