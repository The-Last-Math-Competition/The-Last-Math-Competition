# 00000000579

**CA-79 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The dual-difference spectrum is the coefficient sign spectrum of the difference P_M − P_{M*} between the KL polynomials of a matroid M and its dual M*. Conjecture: For every graphic matroid M(G), the dual-difference spectrum and the flat count of the cographic matroid M*(G) satisfy: the value of the difference polynomial at t = 1 is an explicit integer multiple of |χ_G(−1)|, the multiplier being a spanning-tree deletion count.

**中文。** 定义：拟阵 M 的 KL 多项式与其对偶 M* 的 KL 多项式的差 P_M−P_{M*} 的系数符号谱称对偶差谱。猜想：对一切图拟阵 M(G),对偶差谱与余图拟阵 M*(G) 的平坦计数满足:差多项式在 t=1 处的值为 |χ_G(−1)| 的显式整倍,倍数为生成树的去项计数。
