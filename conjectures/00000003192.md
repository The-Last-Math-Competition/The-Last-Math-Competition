**English.** Definition: The Buchberger algorithm: the elimination criterion of S-polynomials. Conjecture: The completeness of useless-pair criteria: the second-type elimination criterion together with the Gebauer–Möller criterion is complete, with the verification of completeness the vanishing of local leading terms. (Buchberger useless pair complete criterion)
