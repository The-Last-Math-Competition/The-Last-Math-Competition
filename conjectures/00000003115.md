**English.** Definition: The boundary of outer space: the compactification of Out(F_n). Conjecture: Tree rigidity of the outer boundary: boundary points are very small F_n-trees, with the hierarchy of free factor systems as the coordinates of the boundary. (outer boundary tree coordinates rigidity)
