# 00000006293

**English.** Definition: The topology of K-groups of operator rings. Conjecture: The semicontinuous jumps of continuous-field groups are controlled by characteristic classes, the spectrum of the jumps being the torsion subgroups. (characteristic control of continuous-field semicontinuous jumps)

**中文。** 定义：算子环的群的拓扑结构。猜想：连续场群的半连续跳跃由示性类控制；且跳跃的谱为挠子群。（连续场半连续跳跃示性控制）
