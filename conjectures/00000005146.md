# 00000005146

**HD2-06 ★** — source: 157_复几何II_Hodge理论.md

**English.** Definition: Under the master conjecture of the algebraicity of Hodge cycles, one studies the positive-direction domain structure of three-dimensional abelian varieties. Conjecture: The partial positive-direction domain of three-dimensional abelian varieties consists of explicit families of direct products and covers; the family parameter spaces are algebraic and verified via motive decompositions; and the rank contributed by each family is the sum of the motive-factor ranks with zero deficit of the sum. (family structure of partial positive-direction Hodge domains)

**中文。** 定义：Hodge 圈代数性主猜想（Hodge 猜想）下的三维交换簇正向域结构。猜想：三维交换簇的部分正向域由直积与覆叠的显式族组成；族参数空间为代数且经母题分解验证；每族贡献的秩为母因子秩之和且和式的亏损项恒为零。（Hodge 部分正向域族结构）
