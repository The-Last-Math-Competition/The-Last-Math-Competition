# 00000004947

**English.** Definition: Polynomial functors are the Taylor terms of Goodwillie calculus. Conjecture: The graded cohomology dimension table of third-order functors is the homology dimension of the crossed Lie algebra, closed by graded degree and consistent with the low-dimensional homotopy of spheres. (crossed Lie dimension table of third-order polynomials)

**中文。** 定义：多项式函子指 Goodwillie 微积分的泰勒项。猜想：三阶函子的分次上同调维数表为交叉李代数的同调维数,表按分次度封闭且与球的低维同伦一致。（三阶多项式交叉李维数表）
