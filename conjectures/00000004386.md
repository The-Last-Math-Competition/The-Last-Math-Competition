# 00000004386

**English.** Definition: The K of a valuation ring is the algebraic K-group of a complete discrete valuation ring. Conjecture: The third group is given explicitly by the kernel of the symbolic power map, and the order of the kernel is an explicit count of the l-torsion of the unit group. (K_3 symbolic-kernel formula of valuation rings)
