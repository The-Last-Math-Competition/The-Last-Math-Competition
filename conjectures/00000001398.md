# 00000001398

**HT-08 △** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: The k-th layer of the Goodwillie identity functor tower for Sⁿ is P_k(Sⁿ) with connectivity c_k. Conjecture: c_k = 1 + Σ_{i=1}^{k}(2i−1) = k²+1 for odd n; c_k = 1 + Σ(2i) = k(k+1)+1 for even n; the convergence rate of the tower is exactly a quadratic polynomial.

**中文。** 定义：Goodwillie 恒等式函子塔对 Sⁿ 的第 k 层指 P_k(Sⁿ) 的连通度 c_k。猜想：c_k = 1 + Σ_{i=1}^{k}(2i−1) = k²+1 对奇数 n;c_k = 1+Σ(2i)=k(k+1)+1 对偶数 n;塔的收敛速率恰为二次多项式。
