**HSS-22 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: eigenvalue process: n repulsion diffusion of Dyson Brownian motion and free probability limit of n -> ∞. Conjecture: eigenvalue process functional converges to self- by Brownian motion; self- by Hölder exponent of Brownian motion sample path is 1/2−ε for all ε becomes and local when of 1/2 not possibly; eigenvalue collision is zero. (self- by Hölder exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Dyson 布朗运动的特征值过程：n 粒子排斥扩散与 n→∞ 的自由概率极限。猜想：特征值过程泛函收敛到自由布朗运动；自由布朗运动样本路径的 Hölder 指数为 1/2−ε 对一切 ε 成立且 1/2 不可能；特征值碰撞的局部时为零。（自由布朗 Hölder 指数）
