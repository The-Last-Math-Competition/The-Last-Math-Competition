# 00000009662

**HSS-22 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The eigenvalue process of Dyson Brownian motion: n-particle repulsion diffusion and its free-probability limit as n grows. Conjecture: The eigenvalue process converges functionally to free Brownian motion; sample paths of free Brownian motion have Holder exponent 1/2 - epsilon for every epsilon but not 1/2; and the local time of eigenvalue collisions is zero. (free Brownian Holder exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Dyson 布朗运动的特征值过程：n 粒子排斥扩散与 n→∞ 的自由概率极限。猜想：特征值过程泛函收敛到自由布朗运动；自由布朗运动样本路径的 Hölder 指数为 1/2−ε 对一切 ε 成立且 1/2 不可能；特征值碰撞的局部时为零。（自由布朗 Hölder 指数）
