# 00000006202

**English.** Definition: Soliton stability is the preservation of bound states under perturbation, orbital stability. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit structure of the linearized spectrum, with the minimal dimension of the zero eigenspace and the spectral gap explicit; the dimension of the zero eigenspace is the conservation-law count plus one; and the spectral-gap lower bound is given by the Bogomolny-type construction. (linearized spectral-structure conversion of solitons)

**中文。** 定义：孤子稳定性：微扰下束缚态的保持（轨道稳定性）。猜想：换算公式为线性化谱的显式结构且零特征的最小维数与谱间隙显式；零特征的维数为守恒律数加一；谱隙的下界由 Bogomolny 型构造给出。（孤子线性化谱结构换算）
