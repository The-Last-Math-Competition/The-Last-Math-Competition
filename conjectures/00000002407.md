**English.** Definition: A self-affine tile is a translation tiling set. Conjecture: The dimension of the tile boundary is a logarithmic ratio of spectral radii; and the ratio is realized on an integer lattice. (self-affine tile boundary spectral-radius ratio)

**中文。** 定义：自仿射 tile 为平移铺砌集。猜想：tile 边界的维数为谱半径的对数比；且比的实现为整数格。（自仿射 tile 边界谱半径比）
