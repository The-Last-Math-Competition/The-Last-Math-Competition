# 00000005078

**English.** Definition: The nonlinear random Schrodinger equation is the model with both nonlinearity and random potential. Conjecture: Its localization-threshold nonlinearity is an explicit product of potential strength and spectral gap, above the threshold the wave packet spreads, and the transition is sharp. (threshold-product criterion of nonlinear localization)

**中文。** 定义：非线性随机薛定谔指非线性与随机位势并存的模型。猜想：其局域化阈值非线性为位势强度与谱隙的显式积,阈值以上波包展开且转变尖锐。（非线性局域化阈值积判据）
