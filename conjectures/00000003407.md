**English.** Definition: The cutoff phenomenon: the transition window of mixing times. Conjecture: The universality criterion of cutoff: the existence of cutoff for product chains is the non-simplicity of the single-chain spectral gap, with window width the subleading term of the characteristic polynomial. (product cutoff subleading window)

**中文。** 定义：谱约化（cutoff）现象：混合时间的相变窗。猜想：cutoff 的普适判据：乘积链的 cutoff 的存在性为单链谱隙的非简并且窗口宽度为特征多项式的次主项。（乘积 cutoff 次主项窗口）
