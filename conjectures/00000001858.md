# 00000001858

**English.** Definition: The second eigenvalue λ₂ of a random d-regular graph. Conjecture: The finite-size correction of the mean of λ₂ is n^{-2/3} (TW fluctuations); the correction constant is of the type (mean of TW-β=1) − (d−1)^{1/2} (the constant being −1.2065·(d−1)^{1/2}).

**中文。** 定义：随机 d-正则图的第二特征值 λ₂。猜想：λ₂ 的均值的有限尺寸修正为 n^{-2/3}(TW 波动);修正常数 = TW-β=1 的均值 −(d−1)^{1/2}(常数为 −1.2065·(d−1)^{1/2} 型)。
