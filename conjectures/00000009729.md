# 00000009729

**SCA-29 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Global attractors of energy-critical equations: scattering coexisting with solitons from a dynamical systems viewpoint. Conjecture: The global attractor of energy-critical NLS is finite-dimensional with dimension equal to the soliton manifold dimension plus one; the attractor is a single point on the radiation complement, with convergence rate exponential times polynomial. (global attractor dimension)

**中文。** 定义：能量临界方程的全局吸引子：动力学系统视角的散射与孤子并存。猜想：能量临界 NLS 的整体吸引子为有限维且维数等于孤子流形维数加一；吸引子在辐射补空间上为单点且收敛速率为指数乘多项式。（全局吸引子维数）
