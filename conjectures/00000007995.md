**English.** Definition: Haroptimal constant C(n,p) of nack inequality sup_B u ≤ C inf_B u of Harnack constant: nonnegative p- harmonic solution of degenerate equation. Conjecture: explicit of C(n,p) by of one dimension model solution (Barenblatt solution) gives: C = ((1+β)/(1-β))^{(p-1)/p} (β is one dimension exponent); and the constant is average convexity under, constant of plane of optimal (one dimension rigidity); high dimension strictly only in solution is minimum of difference functional of volume to function.

**中文。** 定义：双退化方程的 Harnack 常数：非负 p-调和解的 Harnack 不等式 sup_B u ≤ C inf_B u 的最优常数 C(n,p)。猜想：C(n,p) 的显式值由一维模型解（Barenblatt 型分离解）的比值给出：C = ((1+β)/(1-β))^{(p-1)/p}（β 为一维指数）；且该常数为最优（一维刚性）；高维严格改进仅在解的等高面的平均凸性条件下发生，改进常数为体积径向函数的变差泛函的极小值。
