# 00000007995

**English.** Definition: The Harnack constant of doubly degenerate equations: the optimal constant C(n,p) in the Harnack inequality sup_B u <= C inf_B u for nonnegative p-harmonic solutions. Conjecture: The explicit value of C(n,p) is given by the ratio of one-dimensional model solutions (Barenblatt-type separated solutions): C = ((1+beta)/(1-beta))^{(p-1)/p} with beta the one-dimensional exponent; and this constant is optimal (one-dimensional rigidity); strict improvement in higher dimensions occurs only under mean convexity of the level surfaces of the solution, the improved constant being the minimum of a variation functional of the volume radial function. (p-harmonic Barenblatt Harnack optimality)

**中文。** 定义：双退化方程的 Harnack 常数：非负 p-调和解的 Harnack 不等式 sup_B u ≤ C inf_B u 的最优常数 C(n,p)。猜想：C(n,p) 的显式值由一维模型解（Barenblatt 型分离解）的比值给出：C = ((1+β)/(1-β))^{(p-1)/p}（β 为一维指数）；且该常数为最优（一维刚性）；高维严格改进仅在解的等高面的平均凸性条件下发生，改进常数为体积径向函数的变差泛函的极小值。
