# 00000009983

**NCR-13 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Group algebras of torsion-free groups: zero divisors and spectral properties. Conjecture: The spectral radius and l^2 norm of group algebras of torsion-free groups satisfy an Atiyah-type lower bound: the spectral radius of a nonzero element is at least an explicit exponential function of the l^2 norm; the bound is attained by the class of the L^2 zero divisor conjecture and is independent of amenability. (group algebra spectral radius lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：无挠群的群代数：零除子与谱性质。猜想：无挠群代数的谱半径与 ℓ² 范数满足 Atiyah 型下界：非零元的谱半径至少为 ℓ² 范数的显式指数函数；下界由群的 L² 零除子猜想类达到且界限与顺从性无关。（群代数谱半径下界）
