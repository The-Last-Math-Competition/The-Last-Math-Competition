# 00000007492

**MP6-12 ★** — source: 235_母题与周期II.md

**English.** Definition: The de Rham realization is the period-integral structure of algebraic differentials. Conjecture: The Hodge filtration of the realization is always strictly compatible under functors; and the obstruction to compatibility concentrates in weight-one extensions. (weight-one-obstructed de Rham filtration strictness)

**中文。** 定义：de Rham 实现指代数微分的周期积分结构。猜想：实现的 Hodge 滤过在函子作用下恒严格相容；且相容性的障碍集中在权一的扩张。（de Rham 滤过权一障碍）
