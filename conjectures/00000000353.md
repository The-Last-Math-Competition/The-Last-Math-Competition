# 00000000353

**English.** Definition: The shells of the E₈ lattice are the sets of lattice points of norm 2m. Conjecture: Every shell spans Q⁸; and the minimum number of shell points needed to span is eight, selectable explicitly from the minimal-vector configuration. (E8 shells span the rational space)
