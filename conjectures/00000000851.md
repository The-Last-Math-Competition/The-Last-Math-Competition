**English.** Definition: The double-membrane problem is a variational problem with two obstacle layers. Conjecture: The intersection set of the free boundaries is discrete, with count bounded by a dimensional constant; and the local structure at contact points is symmetrically doubled. (double membrane contact discreteness)

**中文。** 定义：双膜问题为两层障碍的变分问题。猜想：自由边界的交点集为离散且计数不超过维数常数；且接触点的局部结构为对称二重。（双膜接触离散）
