# 00000004986

**English.** Definition: The optimal sampling density is the weighted distribution of least squares. Conjecture: The optimal density in dimension three is an explicit normalization of the reciprocal of the Christoffel function, closed on tensor domains and achieving the optimal condition number. (three-dimensional Christoffel optimal density)

**中文。** 定义：最优采样密度指最小二乘的加权分布。猜想：三维的最优密度为克里斯托费尔函数倒数的显式归一,张量域下封闭且达到条件数最优。（三维克里斯托费尔最优密度）
