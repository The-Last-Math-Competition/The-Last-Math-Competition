# 00000007930

**ATC-30 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The q-difference bound for trace functions: the decay spectrum of the p-Laplacian type difference sum over x of K(x) psi(f(x)h(x)) for an l-adic trace function K from F_p to C. Conjecture: Uniformly over K (slope at most 1, no trivial phase) and f, h (degree at most d), the root mean square of the one-variable sum S(K,f,h;p) is C p^{1/2} (universality of square-root cancellation); and the distribution of S converges to a Sato-Tate type semicircle law when the monodromy group of K is SU(2), and to the uniform phase (circle law) when the monodromy group contains the noncompact part of SL_2; the transition function of the phase change is given by the purity formula of Deligne weights. (trace function square-root universality)

**中文。** 定义：迹函数的 q-差分界：ℓ-adic 迹函数 K: F_p→C 的 p-Laplacian 型差分 Σ_{x} K(x)·ψ(f(x)h(x)) 的衰减谱。猜想：一致于 K（斜率 ≤1，无平凡相位）与 f,h（次数 ≤d），迹函数的单变量和 S(K,f,h;p) 的均方根为 C·p^{1/2}（平方根消灭的普适）；且 S 的分布收敛到 Sato–Tate 型半圆律当 K 的单色（monodromy）群为 SU(2)，收敛到均匀相（圆律）当单色群包含 SL₂ 的非紧部分；相变的转移函数由 Deligne 权重的纯净度公式给出。
