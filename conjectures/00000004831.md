**English.** Definition: The GL_3 standard L-function is the L-function of a cubic group representation. Conjecture: The exponent-sum bound of its triple coefficient sum is the explicit one third plus epsilon, given by stationary-phase analysis of cubic phases and matching the optimal bound. (triple-sum exponent bound of GL_3 coefficients)
