**GA5-12 ★** — source: 239_几何代数III.md

**English.** Definition: Irreducible modules with the irreducible matrix-representation dimension law of Clifford algebras. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the irreducible dimension law and the irreducible realization dimension is exactly 2 to the n/2; the law is exact with deviation by complexification; and the count of realizations is the center dimension. (irreducible-dimension conversion)

**中文。** 定义：不可约模：Clifford 代数的不可约矩阵表示维数律。猜想：换算常数为不可约维数律且不可约实现的维数恰为 2 的二分之 n 次幂；律精确且精确的偏差为复化；实现的个数为中心维数。（不可约维数换算常数结构）
