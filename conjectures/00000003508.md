**English.** Definition: Riemann–Roch–Hirzebruch: the Euler-characteristic formula of bundles. Conjecture: The nonabelian version of RRH: the characteristic numbers of principal bundles realized by the index, with the kernel of realization the Chern–Weil form of characteristic forms.
