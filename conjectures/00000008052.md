# 00000008052

**PRF-02 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The hyper-exponential constant of cut elimination: the proof-length inflation rate beta(S) of cut elimination in sequent calculus (the worst ratio of cut-free proof length to elementary proof length). Conjecture: The exact growth rate of beta(S) is given by the height h of the standard notation tree of the ordinal epsilon_0: beta(S) = tower_h(poly(|S|)); the lower bound on beta is uniform across all proof systems (Hilbert-type, natural deduction, sequent), an invariance of the inflation rate; and the Girard parallel elimination strategy for ordinal notations attains half the upper bound (a strategy half-optimality law). (cut elimination tower inflation invariance)

**中文。** 定义：切割消去的超指数常数：序列演算中 cut 消去定理的证明长度膨胀率 β(S)（初等证明到无 cut 证明的长度比的最坏值）。猜想：β(S) 的精确增长率由序数 ε₀ 的标准记号树的高度 h 给出：β(S)=tower_{h}(poly(|S|))；且任何证明系统（Hilbert 型、自然演绎、sequent）的 β 的下界一致（膨胀率的系统不变性）；序数记号的 Girard 泛allel 消去策略达到上界的一半（策略最优半律）。
