**English.** Definition: Spectral flow: the topological invariant counting the crossings of spectra by operator families. Conjecture: The axiomatization of spectral flow: spectral flow is a homotopy invariant satisfying additivity and norm continuity; the axioms uniquely determine spectral flow, with uniqueness proved via the K₁ characteristic class of the Fredholm semigroup. (spectral flow axiom uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：谱流（spectral flow）：算子族穿越谱的计数的拓扑不变量。猜想：谱流的公理化：谱流为同伦不变量且满足加法性与范数连续性；公理唯一确定谱流且确定性的证明经由 Fredholm 半群的 K₁ 的示性类。（谱流公理唯一性）
