# 00000005887

**English.** Definition: The virtual element method is the element method without explicit basis functions. Conjecture: On polygonal elements the dimension of the projected polynomial space is uniform, and the kernel of the uniformity is the virtual degrees of freedom. (virtual-degree polygonal dimension uniformity of virtual elements)

**中文。** 定义：虚元方法指无需显式基函数的单元法。猜想：多边形单元上投影多项式空间的维数一致；且一致的核为虚拟自由度。（虚元虚拟自由度多边形维数一致）
