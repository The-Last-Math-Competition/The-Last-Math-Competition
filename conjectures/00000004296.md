# 00000004296

**English.** Definition: Mixing is the amalgamation construction of p-groups, and a balanced subgroup is the middle group of a balanced extension. Conjecture: The minimal balanced-subgroup construction of a mixing is given uniquely by the termwise pairing of the Ulm factors, the uniqueness holds for length omega and fails beyond. (uniqueness boundary of balanced mixing constructions)

**中文。** 定义：混合指 p-群的合并构造,平衡子群指平衡扩张的中项群。猜想：混合的平衡子群极小构造由 Ulm 因子的逐项配对唯一给出,唯一性在长度 ω 情形成立并在更长时失效。（混合平衡构造唯一性边界）
