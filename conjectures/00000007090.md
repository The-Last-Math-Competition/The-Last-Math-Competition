**English.** Definition: The zero-genus symplectic isotopy group with the topology of the Hamiltonian group. Conjecture: The higher homotopy of the group is the homotopy of Floer homology, and the spectrum of the homology is the isotopy obstruction. (Floer-spectral Hamiltonian higher homotopy)
