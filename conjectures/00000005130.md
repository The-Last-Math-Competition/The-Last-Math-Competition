**English.** Definition: Spectral clustering and iteration trajectories are two layers of algorithm behavior. Conjecture: There exist two preconditioners with the same spectral clustering but different iteration trajectories, and the separation is realized by an explicit rotation of the initial direction. (same spectral clustering-different trajectory separation)
