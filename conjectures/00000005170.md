# 00000005170

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative spectra and periods are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two noncommutative structures with the same spectrum but different period integrals, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of categories with the same spectrum but different periods. (same noncommutative spectrum-different period categorical separation)

**中文。** 定义：非交换谱与周期指两层。猜想：存在两非交换结构的谱相同而周期积分不同,分离由同谱异周期的显式范畴对实现。（非交换谱同-周期异范畴分离）
