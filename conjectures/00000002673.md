# 00000002673

**English.** Definition: Pointwise Poincaré: the local side of lower Ricci bounds. Conjecture: The pointwise version of the inequality, with doubling constants. (pointwise Poincare doubling)

**中文。** 定义：点态 Poincaré：Ricci 下界的局部。猜想：局部的 Poincaré：不等式的点态版本且本的常数为倍增。（点态 Poincaré 倍增不等式常数局部版本 Ricci 下界点态）
