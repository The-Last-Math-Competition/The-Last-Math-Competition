**TCH-09 ★** — source: 304_Teichmuller动力.md

**English.** Definition: Prym eigenform loci are special families of Teichmueller curves defined by Prym variation structures; McMullen completed the classification for genus at most 5, and finiteness at higher genus is open. Conjecture: For genus at least 6 only a finite exceptional list of Prym eigenform Teichmueller curves remains; and the number of curves of each fixed Prym type is given by an explicit polynomial in spin structure combinatorics. (Prym curve finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：Prym 特征形式轨迹为由 Prym 变分结构定义的特殊 Teichmüller 曲线族；McMullen 完成亏格 ≤5 的分类，更高亏格的有限性未决。猜想：亏格 ≥6 的 Prym 特征形式 Teichmüller 曲线仅剩有限例外列表；每个固定 Prym 型的曲线个数由旋量结构组合的显式多项式给出。（Prym 曲线有限性）
