**MFH-06 ★** — source: 286_半整权模形式.md

**English.** Definition: The Saito-Kurokawa lift: the lift from weight 3/2 to weight 2. Conjecture: The torsion structure of the non-cuspidal part is controlled by the singular weight of Siegel modular forms; and the controlling weight is one. (Saito-Kurokawa singular weight torsion control)

**中文。** 定义：Saito–Kurokawa 提升为权二分之三到二的提升。猜想：非尖点部分的挠结构由 Siegel 模形式的奇异权控制；且控制的权为一。（SK 提升奇异权挠控制）
