**English.** Definition: Comparison with the strictness deficit of the Gaussian comparison principle. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the strictness deficit and the deficit at equal-variance meeting is of spectral-gap type; the meeting moment is the variance intersection; and the limit of the deficit is an area-type functional. (spectral-gap conversion of strictness deficits)
