**English.** Definition: Dynamical Mordell–Lang: finiteness of intersections of orbits with subvarieties (the Ghioca–Tucker theorem). Conjecture: A quantitative upper bound on intersection density: the count of intersection points of an orbit with a codimension-r subvariety is a degree-r polynomial in log n along the iterates, with coefficients explicit in the degree vector of the subvariety. (DML intersection count polynomial law)

**中文。** 定义：Dynamical Mordell–Lang：轨道与子簇的交的有限性（Ghioca–Tucker 定理）。猜想：交的密度的定量上界：轨道与余维 r 子簇的交点的计数沿迭代 n 为 log n 的 r 次多项式且多项式系数由子簇的次数向量显式给出。（DML 交计数多项式律）
