# 00000006222

**English.** Definition: Accessible categories are closed under filtered colimits. Conjecture: The doubly accessible threshold is uniform for adjoint pairs and the minimal chain is stable, the combination of the stability being the lifting sheaf. (lifting-sheaf stability of doubly accessible minimal chains)

**中文。** 定义：可及范畴为滤余极限封闭的范畴类。猜想：双可达阈在伴随对一致且极小链稳定；且稳定的组合为提升层。（双可达极小链提升层稳定）
