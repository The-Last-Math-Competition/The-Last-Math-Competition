**English.** Definition: K(Z) is the algebraic K-spectrum of the integers. Conjecture: Beyond the known low-degree homotopy, the next nonzero odd-degree homotopy appears in degrees that are multiples of p^2 - 1, the degree structure is uniform for all odd primes p, and the degrees are exactly the v_p-self-map periods of the Adams part of Quillen's operation. (next occurrence degree of the odd homotopy of K(Z))

**中文。** 定义：K(ℤ) 指整数的代数 K 谱。猜想：在已知的低次同伦之外,下一非零奇次同伦出现在 p² − 1 的倍数度,该度数结构对一切奇素数 p 统一。(K(Z) 奇次下一出现度)；且该度数恰为 Quillen 操作 Adams 部分的 v_p 自映射周期。
