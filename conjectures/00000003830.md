# 00000003830

**RC-10 △** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: A normal crystal is a crystal satisfying Stembridge raising and switching axioms. Conjecture: Finite normal crystals are determined up to isomorphism by their characters; removing normality, 3-vertex crystals with equal characters but non-isomorphic graphs already exist, so the normal axioms are the weakest local axioms for character determinacy. (normal axiom weakest property)

**中文。** 定义：正规晶体指满足 Stembridge 提升与交换公理的晶体。猜想：有限正规晶体由其字符唯一决定同构类;而取消正规性后 3 顶点晶体已出现字符相同但不同构的反例,故正规公理组是字符决定性的最弱局部公理组。（正规公理最弱性）
