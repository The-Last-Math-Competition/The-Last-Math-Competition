**English.** Definition: The Langevin threshold for Diophantine applications of Pila-Wilkie: the optimal exponent kappa_AO of the counting threshold H_AO for tamed proofs of Andre-Oort. Conjecture: kappa_AO = 0 (sufficiency of a logarithmic counting bound); the gap constant for the distribution of special points below the threshold is the logarithmic derivative of the Mass formula (a gap-mass duality); the minimum of the gap distribution is attained at the cusps of compact Shimura varieties, the explicit cusp gap constant being 2 pi/sqrt|D| (D the discriminant), whose Diophantine type is controlled by the regulator of the imaginary quadratic field. (Andre-Oort cusp gap mass duality)

**中文。** 定义：Pila–Wilkie 的丢番图应用的朗之万（Langevin）阈：André–Oort 的驯化证明的计数阈值 H_AO 的最优指数κ_AO。猜想：κ_AO=0（对数计数界的充分性）；且阈值下的特殊点分布的间隙常数为 Mass 公式的对数导数（间隙-质量对偶）；间隙分布的极小值在紧 Shimura 纹的尖点（cusp）取得，尖点间隙的显式常数为 2π/√|D|（D 为判别式），常数的 Diophantine 型由虚二次域的 Regulator 控制。
