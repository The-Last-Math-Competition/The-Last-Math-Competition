# 00000009864

**KPU-14 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: The moment problem of the Cole-Hopf solution: determinacy of the moments of the exponentiated KPZ solution. Conjecture: The distribution of the Cole-Hopf solution of the KPZ equation is uniquely determined by its moments of all orders; the uniqueness of the moment problem is guaranteed by two-sided bounds on negative moments, with the exponent of the bounds any positive number. (KPZ moment problem uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：Cole–Hopf 解的矩问题：指数变换后 KPZ 方程解的矩的确定性。猜想：KPZ 方程 Cole–Hopf 解的分布由其各阶矩唯一确定；矩问题的唯一性由负矩的双侧界保证且界的指数为任意正数。（KPZ 矩问题唯一性）
