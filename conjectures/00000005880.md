**English.** Definition: The efficiency constant and the adaptive spectrum are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two estimators with the same efficiency but different adaptive spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit weight pair with the same efficiency but different marking. (same efficiency-different adaptive spectrum weight separation)
