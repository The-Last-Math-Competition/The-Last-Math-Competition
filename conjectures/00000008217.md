**English.** Definition: The intersection form of l-adic sheaves in Deligne-Lusztig theory: the character table it_form(G) of the intersection form of DL varieties. Conjecture: The characters of it_form are integer combinations of characters of G^F (an integer combination law); the combination coefficients (multiplicities of intersection numbers) are nonnegative (a nonnegative multiplicity law); and the minimal group realizing negative values (indefiniteness of the intersection form) is the Suzuki group (a Suzuki indefinite example), with indefinite signature of second order (1, -q) (a Suzuki second-order signature). (Deligne-Lusztig intersection Suzuki indefiniteness)

**中文。** 定义：Deligne–Lusztig 理论的 ℓ-adic 层的交截形式：DL 变种的交截形式的特征标表 it_form(G)。猜想：it_form 的特征标为 G^F 的特征标的整系数组合（整组合律）；且组合系数（交截数的重数）≥ 0（非负重数律）；负值的实现（交截形式的不定型）的最小群为 Suzuki 群（Suzuki 不定例），不定的符号差 = (1, -q) 的二阶（Suzuki 二阶符号差）。
