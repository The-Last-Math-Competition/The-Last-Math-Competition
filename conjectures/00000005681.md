# 00000005681

**English.** Definition: A 2-group is the groupoid-like structure with object and morphism layers, and a 2-representation is its action on 2-vector spaces. Conjecture: The irreducible-representation classification of three-object strict 2-groups is given by the explicit two-entry table of characteristic numbers with automorphism pairs, finite and complete for fixed homotopy type. (two-entry classification table of three-object 2-representations)

**中文。** 定义：双群指带对象与态射两层的群胚化结构,双层表示指其在双层向量空间的作用。猜想：三对象严格双群的不可约表示分类由示性数与自同构对的显式二元表给出,同伦型固定时有限且完全。（三对象双层表示二元分类表）
