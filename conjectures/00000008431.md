# 00000008431

**English.** Definition: The q-analogue of t-designs: designs on subspaces of a vector space. Conjecture: t-(v,k,1)_q Steiner systems exist at q = 2 with k at most v/2 exactly under divisibility conditions plus an explicit lower bound; and the first nonexistence exception is (v,k) = (8,4). (existence domain of q-Steiner systems)

**中文。** 定义：t-设计的一种模拟的 q-模拟指向量空间子空间下的设计。猜想：t-(v,k,1)_q Steiner 系统在 q = 2 与 k ≤ v/2 的存在域为整除条件加显式下界；第一个非存在例外为 (v,k) = (8,4)。（q-Steiner 存在域）
