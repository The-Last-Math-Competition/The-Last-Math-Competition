# 00000007933

**English.** Definition: The spectral-parametric rigidity of Yang-Baxter operators: the solution set of the R-matrix R(u) of the six-vertex model up to gauge equivalence and scalar multiplication. Conjecture: The family of nondegenerate symmetric six-vertex R-matrices is two-dimensional (the parameter Delta and a gauge); the nonsymmetric family is four-dimensional; any family of R-matrices parametrized by an algebraic curve degenerates to one of these families (dimension rigidity); and the parameter dimension of the complete solutions of the higher spectral-parameter case (color R-matrices) at vertex dimension at least 3 is 2n^2+2n (n the vertex count), the degrees of freedom corresponding one-to-one to the coboundary classification of quantum groups. (six-vertex dimension rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Yang–Baxter 算子的谱参数化刚性：六顶点模型的 R-矩阵 R(u) 的解集（在规范等价与乘法标量下）。猜想：非退化对称六顶点 R-矩阵的族为二维（参数 Δ 与规范）；非对称族为四维；任何代数曲线参数化的 R-矩阵族必退化为上述族（维数刚性）；且高谱参数情形（color R-matrix）的完全解在顶点维数 ≥ 3 时的参数维数为 2n²+2n（n 为顶点数），其自由度与量子群的余边界分类一一对应。
