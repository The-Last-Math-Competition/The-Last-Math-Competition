# 00000007752

**English.** Definition: The Bottcher coordinate phi_c of f(z)=z^2+c is the unique univalent analytic function near infinity with phi_c(f(z)) = phi_c(z)^2. Conjecture: For every algebraic c (with 0 non-escaping), the ratio phi_c(z)/z at algebraic points z of sufficiently large modulus is transcendental; and for two distinct parameters c_1, c_2, the ratios phi_{c_1}(z)/phi_{c_2}(z) are algebraically independent at algebraic points of the common domain. (transcendence of Bottcher coordinate ratios)

**中文。** 定义：f(z)=z^2+c 的 Böttcher 坐标 φ_c 为 ∞ 附近满足 φ_c(f(z))=φ_c(z)^2 的唯一单值解析函数。猜想：对每个代数数 c（c 使 0 不逃逸），φ_c 在代数点 z（|z| 充分大）的取值 φ_c(z) 与 z 的比值为超越数；且对两个不同参数 c_1,c_2，比值 φ_{c_1}(z)/φ_{c_2}(z) 在公共定义域的代数点处代数无关。
