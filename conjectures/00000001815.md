**AG2-05 ★** — source: 46_代数几何II.md

**English.** Definition: A very free curve on a Fano threefold is a rational curve whose normal bundle has no negative summands. Conjecture: The minimal degree of very free curves through two points on every Fano threefold has a completely explicit table (one value per Fano class); the minimum is 1 (a line P¹), the maximum 3 (double-hypersurface type).

**中文。** 定义：Fano 三维流形的非常自由曲线指其法丛无负分量的有理曲线。猜想：一切 Fano 三维流形上过两点的非常自由曲线的最小次数有完全显式表(每类 Fano 对应一值);最小值为 1(线性 P¹),最大值为 3(双曲面型)。
