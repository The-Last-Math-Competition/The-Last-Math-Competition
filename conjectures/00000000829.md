# 00000000829

**English.** Definition: The Moser–Trudinger constant. Conjecture: Extremal functions exist on arbitrary bounded domains and satisfy the convex-solution structure of an explicit Euler–Lagrange nonlinear equation (existence of MT extremizers on general domains; known for some symmetric domains).

**中文。** 定义：Moser–Trudinger 常数。猜想：任意有界域上极值函数存在且满足显式 Euler–Lagrange 非线性方程的凸解结构(MT 极值存在的一般域版本;已知部分对称域)。
