# 00000007730

**FRV-10 △** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The operator-valued free central limit theorem gives the convergence rate to semicircular elements over an operator-valued algebra B. Conjecture: For centered self-adjoint tuples that are free over B with bounded fourth moments, the moment convergence rate is O(n^{-1}); the rate constant is controlled by the fourth-order operator-valued cumulant, and when B is the diagonal matrix algebra the constant equals the difference of the classical fourth cumulant and a colored Motzkin path count. (colored-Motzkin operator-valued CLT rate)

**中文。** 定义：算子值自由中心极限定理给出收敛到半圆元（在算子值代数 B 上）的速度。猜想：对具有有界四阶矩的、B 上自由且中心化的自伴元组，矩收敛速度为 O(n^{-1})；速度常数由算子值累积量的四阶项控制，且当 B 取为对角矩阵代数时该常数可表为经典四阶 cumulant 与某类有色 Motzkin 路径计数之差。
