# 00000005749

**English.** Definition: A tame group is a quotient group without wild ramification. Conjecture: At the prime two the maximal rank of generators is given by the linear expression of the ramification point count, and the kernel of the linearity is the Artin-Schreier tower. (ramification-linear rank of tame groups at the prime two)
