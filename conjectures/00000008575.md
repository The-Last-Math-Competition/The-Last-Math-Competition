# 00000008575

**English.** Definition: Infinite Ramanujan graphs: infinite d-regular graphs whose spectral radius attains the infimum 2 sqrt(d-1). Conjecture: Infinite Ramanujan graphs exist and can be constructed as double-coset graphs of the tree (a double-coset construction law); their spectrum is pure point plus the spectral-radius circle (a pure point plus circle law); the density of the point spectrum is given by the trace formula of the lattice (a trace formula density law); and the existence threshold of higher-dimensional generalizations (graphs over buildings) is rank 2 of the affine building (a rank-two threshold law). (infinite Ramanujan double-coset trace density)

**中文。** 定义：无限 Ramanujan 图：谱半径达到 2√(d-1) 下确界的无限 d-正则图。猜想：无限 Ramanujan 图存在且可由树的双陪集图构造（双陪集构造律）；且其谱为纯点加谱半径圆（纯点加圆律）；点谱的密度由格的迹公式给出（迹公式密度律）；高维推广（建筑上的图）的存在阈为仿射建筑的秩 2（秩二阈律）。
