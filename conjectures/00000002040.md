# 00000002040

**English.** Definition: A 3-independent set has no solutions of x + y = z. Conjecture: The density limit of maximal 3-independent sets in [n] exists and is at most 1/4 (with correction 0); the limit is exactly 1/4 (attained by the natural ternary cascade construction).

**中文。** 定义：3-独立集指无 x+y=z 的解的集合。猜想：[n] 中最大 3-独立集的密度极限存在且 ≤1/4(修正 = 0);极限为 1/4 的精确值(达到者的构造由三进制的自然层叠)。
