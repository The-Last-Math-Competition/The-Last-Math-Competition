**GBR-11 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Deformed preprojective algebras: finiteness of representation dimension of Pi^lambda (Crawley-Boevey's criterion is known). Conjecture: The Poisson leaf decomposition of the center of the deformed preprojective algebra corresponds one-to-one with parameter spaces of indecomposable representations; the dimension formula of leaves is an explicit quadratic function of the dimension vector, and symplectic leaves exactly contain closed orbits. (preprojective Poisson leaf correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：形变预投射代数：Π^λ 的表示维数的有限性（Crawley-Boevey 判据已知）。猜想：形变预投射代数的中心的 Poisson 叶分解与不可分解表示的参数空间一一对应；叶的维数公式为维数向量的显式二次函数且辛叶恰含闭轨道。（预投射 Poisson 叶对应）
