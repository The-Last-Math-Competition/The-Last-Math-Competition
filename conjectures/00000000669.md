**KT-69 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The r-function of a Montesinos knot is a Schubert-type quantity. Conjecture: The r-function is given by the branching structure of the branched cover; and its combination is a sum of genera. (Montesinos r-function genus sum)

**中文。** 定义：Montesinos 结的 r-函数为 Schubert 型量。猜想：r-函数由分支覆盖的分歧结构给出；且给出的组合为亏格和。（Montesinos r-函数亏格和）
