# 00000000824

**PV-24 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The interior zero density of a Dirichlet eigenfunction is the energy share of neighborhoods of the zero set. Conjecture: The interior zero decay rate is identically an explicit power law of the distance function; and the associated constant is the ratio of the first eigenvalue. (interior zero decay rate)

**中文。** 定义：Dirichlet 特征函数的内零点密度为零集邻域能量份额。猜想：内零衰减率恒等于距离函数的显式幂律；且相关的常数为第一特征值之比。（内零点衰减率）
