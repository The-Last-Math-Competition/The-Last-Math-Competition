# 00000005604

**English.** Definition: Strong reduction is the complete reduction of successive minima. Conjecture: The computable dimension upper bound of complete reduction is four, from dimension five the equivalence decision hardens, and the boundary is controlled by quadratic-form equivalence. (four-dimensional computability boundary of strong reduction)

**中文。** 定义：强规约指逐次极小的完全规约。猜想：完全规约的可计算维数上界为四,五维起等价判定转难且分界由二次型等价性控制。（强规约四维可算分界）
