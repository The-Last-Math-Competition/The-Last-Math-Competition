**English.** Definition: Strong dynamical localization is the exponential decay of all moments. Conjecture: Under one-dimensional Bernoulli potentials the lower bound of the decay exponent is an explicit functional of the Lyapunov exponent, given by the multiplicative measure and tight. (Lyapunov lower bound of the dynamical decay exponent)

**中文。** 定义：强动力学局域化指一切矩的指数衰减。猜想：一维组合位势下衰减指数的下界为李雅普诺夫指数的显式泛函,由乘性测度给出且界紧。（动力学衰减指数李雅普诺夫下界）
