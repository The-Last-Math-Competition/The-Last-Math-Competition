**English.** Definition: Nonstandard economics with equilibria of hypermany agents. Conjecture: The hyperfinite count of agents holds, and the limit of the count is the continuum equilibrium. (continuum-limit hypermany-agent equilibrium)

**中文。** 定义：非标准经济：超多主体的均衡。猜想：超多主体：主体的超有限数且数的极限为连续均衡。（超多主体连续均衡超有限数极限主体）
