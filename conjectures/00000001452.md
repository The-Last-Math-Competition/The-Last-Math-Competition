# 00000001452

**English.** Definition: The convex floating body is the intersection of all halfspaces cutting off depth δ. Conjecture: For smooth convex bodies, the exact relation between the second coefficient of the volume difference of the floating body and the affine surface area A(K) is explicit in d = 3 (a three-dimensional floating-body formula).

**中文。** 定义：凸浮体(floating body,深度 δ 切除的交集)。猜想：光滑凸体的浮体的体积差的第二项系数与仿射表面积 A(K) 的精确关系式在 d = 3 显式(三维浮体公式)。
