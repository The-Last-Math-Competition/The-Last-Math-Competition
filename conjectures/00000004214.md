# 00000004214

**English.** Definition: The cellular matrix-tree theorem is the higher-dimensional count via minors of the cellular Laplacian. Conjecture: The multiplicity factor of the count is an explicit factorial product in the numbers of faces, depending only on the numbers of cells of each dimension and not on the gluing. (multiplicity factor formula of cellular trees)

**中文。** 定义：胞腔矩阵树定理指胞腔拉普拉斯子式的高维计数。猜想：计数的重数因子为面数的阶乘显式乘积,仅依赖各维胞腔数而不依赖胶合方式。（胞腔树重数因子公式）
