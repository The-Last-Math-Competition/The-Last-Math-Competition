**English.** Definition: A semi-universal deformation is the minimal family controlling all first-order deformations, and the socle is the local algebra of its base. Conjecture: The realizable values of the socle dimension are all integers from zero to the difference, each realized by an explicit family of monomial ideals. (full-interval realization of socle dimensions)
