# 00000004094

**English.** Definition: Homological stability is the stabilization of Betti numbers as the particle number grows, and the stable slope is the ratio at which stabilization begins. Conjecture: The stable slope of configurations on a genus g surface is the explicit value of the form 2g - 2, and it cannot be improved. (stable slope of surface configurations)

**中文。** 定义：同调稳定性指 Betti 数在粒子数增大后的稳定化,稳定斜率指稳定化起始的比率。猜想：亏格 g 曲面配置的稳定斜率为 2g − 2 型显式值,且不可再改进。（曲面配置稳定斜率）
