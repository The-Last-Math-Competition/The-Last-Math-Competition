# 00000002235

**NV-05 ★** — source: 60_亚纯函数与NevanlinnaII.md

**English.** Definition: Ritt's second theorem (the classification of equal-degree monomial decompositions). Conjecture: A complete list of second-type exceptional pairs exists as a finite verification table for degree products ≤ 400 (a Ritt pair table); and the termination of the table is guaranteed by the vanishing of associativity obstructions of polynomial composition.

**中文。** 定义：Ritt 第二定理(等次数单项分解的分类)。猜想：第二类例外对的完全列表在度数乘积 ≤ 400 的有限核对表(Ritt 对表)；且核对表的终止由多项式复合的结合律消没保证。
