# 00000007439

**CA5-19 ★** — source: 233_组合的算术II.md

**English.** Definition: Sum-free sets, Schur type. Conjecture: The maximal density of sum-free sets is realized on the odd integers; and the density limit is exactly the Euler product of the interval endpoints modulo three. (odd-realized Schur sum-free density)

**中文。** 定义：sum-free 集：Schur 型。猜想：Schur 的密度：无和集的极大密度且度的实现在奇数集。(Schur 无和集奇数集极大密度密度实现)；且密度极限恰为区间端点模三的 Euler 乘积。
