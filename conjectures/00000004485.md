**English.** Definition: A Lawvere-Tierney topology is a closure operator on the subobject classifier. Conjecture: The count of nontrivial maximal elements of its complete lattice is an explicit double-exponential cardinal function of the set of separated objects, and the formula holds for all sheaf toposes. (double-exponential count of LT maximal elements)
