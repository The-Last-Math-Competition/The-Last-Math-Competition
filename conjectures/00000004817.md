# 00000004817

**English.** Definition: The Sato-Tate measure is the limiting distribution of Frobenius angles. Conjecture: The optimal exponent of the deviation for non-CM curves is the explicit one half plus epsilon, attained by angle-sum estimates and not compressible. (optimal ST deviation exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Sato–Tate 测度指弗罗贝尼乌斯角的极限分布。猜想：非 CM 曲线的偏差的最佳指数为二分之一加 ε 型显式值,由角和估计达到且不可再压。（ST 偏差最优指数）
