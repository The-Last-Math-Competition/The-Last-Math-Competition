**English.** Definition: Stability conditions are the combined data of heart and phase. Conjecture: The three-dimensional existence window is given by classical inequalities with explicit ends, and the combination of the window is the Bogomolov inequality. (Bogomolov window of three-dimensional stability)

**中文。** 定义：稳定条件为心与相位的组合数据。猜想：三维的存在窗由经典不等式给出且窗端显式；且窗的组合为 Bogomolov。（三维稳定 Bogomolov 构造窗）
