# 00000004489

**English.** Definition: The Artin gluing topos is the gluing construction along a geometric morphism. Conjecture: The classified fiber conversion of its objects is the chained composition of three left adjoints of the gluing functor, the chain length is exactly 3 and cannot be shortened. (gluing three-adjoint chain identity)

**中文。** 定义：粘合拓扑斯指沿几何态射的 Artin 粘合构造。猜想：其对象的分类纤维换算由粘合函子的三个左伴随链式合成,链长恰为 3 且不可缩短。（粘合三伴随链恒等）
