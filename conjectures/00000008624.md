**INP-04 ★** — source: 273_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: Macdonald processes are processes whose compatible families are Macdonald polynomials. Conjecture: The fluctuation covariance of their q-Whittaker limit is an explicit Plancherel-type functional, and the leading particle degenerates to a one-dimensional universal law. (Macdonald covariance degeneration)

**中文。** 定义：Macdonald 过程指以 Macdonald 多项式为相容族的过程。猜想：其 q-Whittaker 极限的涨落协方差为 Plancherel 型显式泛函且领头粒子退化为一维普适律。（Macdonald 协方差退化）
