# 00000008655

**CGT-05 ★** — source: 274_计算群论与字的几何.md

**English.** Definition: The conjugacy problem: deciding whether two words in a group are conjugate. Conjecture: The conjugacy problem for hyperbolic groups has a linear-time algorithm; the smallest presentation with solvable word problem but unsolvable conjugacy problem is of HNN extension type; and the solvable domain of tame conjugacy is exactly the class of finite extensions of hyperbolic plus nilpotent groups. (hyperbolic conjugacy linear solvability)

**中文。** 定义：共轭问题指判定群中两字是否共轭。猜想：双曲群的共轭问题有线性时间算法；字问题可解而共轭不可解的最小呈现为 HNN 扩张型，tame 共轭的可解域恰为双曲加幂零的有限扩张类。（双曲共轭线性可解）
