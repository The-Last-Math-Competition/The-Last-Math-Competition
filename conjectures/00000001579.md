# 00000001579

**IS-09 ★** — source: 38_可积系统与精确可解模型.md

**English.** Definition: Algebraic solutions of Painlevé VI are solutions in parameter classes with finite monodromy. Conjecture: The degree of algebraic solutions for the remaining unclassified parameters is at most twenty-four; and the complete list is closed by the Galois inverse images of icosahedral cosets. (PVI algebraic solutions capped at degree 24)

**中文。** 定义：Painlevé VI 的代数解指有限单值的参数类解。猜想：剩余未分类参数的代数解次数不超过二十四；且完整列表由 icosahedral 陪集的 Galois 逆像封闭。（PVI 代数解 24 次封顶）
