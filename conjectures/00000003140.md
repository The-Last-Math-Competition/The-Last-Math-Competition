**English.** Definition: Weyl groups and differential equations: reflection groups of singularity theory. Conjecture: The monodromy of hypergeometric systems is the reflection representation of the Weyl group, with the invariant ring of the representation the polynomials of Chevalley's theorem. (hypergeometric monodromy Chevalley invariants)
