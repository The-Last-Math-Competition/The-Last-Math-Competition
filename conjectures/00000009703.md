**SCA-03 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Two-dimensional equivariant wave maps into the sphere: the dynamics of the 1-corotational class is energy-critical; the blowup-scattering dichotomy is partially known. Conjecture: Solutions of 1-corotational wave maps are globally classified into scattering, finite-time blowup, and pseudoconformal blowup — a trichotomy; the rate of non-pseudoconformal blowup is exactly the double logarithm law with universal constant correction. (corotational wave map trichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：二维等波映照到球面：1-共同旋转类的动力学为能量临界问题；爆破与散射二分已知部分。猜想：1-共同旋转波映照的解全局分类为散射、有限时间爆破与伪共形爆破三类；非伪共形爆破的速率恰为对数双指数律且修正项为普适常数。（共转波映照三分律）
