**RT-26 △** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: The Atiyah–Bott duality of sl(2|1) is the geometric correspondence of its representation category. Conjecture: The weight spectrum of the mixed Hodge structure of the duality is {0, 2, 4, …} (purely even); the spectrum is explicit in the dimensions of the supersymmetric vanishing cycles.

**中文。** 定义：sl(2|1) 的 Atiyah–Bott 对偶指其表示范畴的几何对应。猜想：对偶的混合 Hodge 结构的权谱为 \{0,2,4,…\}(纯偶);谱由超对称消没循环的维数显式。
