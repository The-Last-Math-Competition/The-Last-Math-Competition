# 00000008483

**English.** Definition: The Barabanov norm of a linear system: the extremal norm for which the joint spectral radius is exactly attained by finite products. Conjecture: Barabanov norms exist for compact matrix families (an existence law); the norm is unique up to norm equivalence (a uniqueness law); uniqueness-breaking examples are given by rotation-symmetric matrix families (a symmetric family example); and the convergence rate of computing the extremal norm is exponential in the spectral gap (a spectral gap exponent law). (Barabanov norm existence uniqueness gap)

**中文。** 定义：线性系统的 Barabanov 范数：使联合谱半径由有限乘积精确实现的极值范数。猜想：紧矩阵族的 Barabanov 范数恒存在（存在律）；且该范数在规范等价下唯一（唯一律）；唯一性破缺的例子由旋转对称矩阵族给出（对称族例）；极值范数的计算的收敛速率为谱隙的指数（谱隙指数律）。
