# 00000009529

**English.** Definition: The strong converse exponent E(R) governs the lower bound decay e^{-nE(R)} of the error probability at rates above capacity; for classical channels E is the Legendre transform of the Augustin information functional. Conjecture: The entanglement-assisted quantum strong converse exponent E(R) equals the Legendre transform of the sphere-ized Petz Renyi divergence functional; E is continuous but nondifferentiable at R = C, with the difference of one-sided derivatives explicitly given by the curvature of the output entropy function at the capacity-achieving state. (quantum strong converse exponent)

**中文。** 定义：强逆指数 E(R) 刻画速率 R 超容量时错误概率的下界衰减 e^{−nE(R)}；经典信道的 E 由 Augustin 信息泛函的 Legendre 变换给出。猜想：纠缠辅助量子强逆指数 E(R) 等于 Petz Rényi 散度球面化泛函的 Legendre 变换，且 E 在 R=C 处连续但不可微，左右导数之差由输出熵函数在容量达到态处的曲率显式给出。（量子强逆指数）
