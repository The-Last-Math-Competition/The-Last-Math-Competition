# 00000008035

**OMN-15 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: Tame probability: the Fubini mismatch index F_m (the quantitative failure of definable Fubini) for definable probability measures on definable sets (Keisler's program). Conjecture: F_m = 0 (definable Fubini always holds, complete taming of probability); the convergence rate of the law of large numbers for definable measures is optimal among definable functions (a definable version of the 1/sqrt n rate); and the tamed version of the Wald identity for stopping times of definable martingales holds throughout the class of definable stopping times (tamed Wald), failing only at non-definable stopping times (a minimal violation of the axiom of choice: non-definability of choice functions). (tame Fubini Wald definability threshold)

**中文。** 定义：驯概率：可定义集上的可定义概率测度（Keisler 主义）的 Fubini 失配指数 F_m（可定义 Fubini 的失效的定量）。猜想：F_m = 0（可定义 Fubini 恒成立，驯概率的完全驯化）；且可定义测度的大数定律的收敛速率为可定义函数的最优（速率为 1/√n 的可定义化）；鞅的可定义化的停止时间的 Wald 恒等式的驯化版在可定义停时的类恒成立（Wald 驯化），恒等式的失效仅在非可定义停时（选择公理的极小破坏：选择函数的非可定义性）下发生。
