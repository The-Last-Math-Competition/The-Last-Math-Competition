# 00000008061

**PRF-11 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The regularity criterion for cyclic proof systems of infinite proof theory: the complexity cyc(T) of the automata well-ordering decision for cyclic proofs. Conjecture: cyc(T) (deciding global well-ordering of cyclic proofs) is Pi-1-1-complete; the fragment with automatic decision (the docyclic fragment) is expressively equivalent to the no-counterexample interpretation (the combinatorial equivalence of Kreisel's NCI); and the minimal theory separating cyclic proofs from inductive proofs is the Pi-2 fragment of Peano with parameters (a separation hierarchy), within which the size advantage of cyclic proofs is double-exponential (a tight bound on the compression advantage). (cyclic proof Pi-1-1 no-counterexample equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：无限证明论的循环证明系统的正则性判据：循环证明（cyclic proof）的自动机的良序判定的复杂度 cyc(T)。猜想：cyc(T)（判断循环证明全局良序）为 Π₁¹-完全；且带自动判定的片段（docyclic fragment）的表达能力等价于 no-counterexample 解释（Kreisel 的 NC I 的组合等价）；循环证明与归纳证明的分离的最小理论为带参数的 Peano 的 Π₂ 片段（分离层级），片段内循环证明的规模优势为双指数（压缩优势的紧界）。
