# 00000008272

**SFL-12 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The propagation of chaos in the McKean-Vlasov limit of particle systems: the rate chaos_rate of chaos propagation for empirical measures of interacting N-particle systems. Conjecture: chaos_rate (the Wasserstein rate of chaos propagation) is N^{-1/2} (an optimal transport square-root law); the critical strength for the failure of propagation under singular interactions (attractive-repulsive kernels, clustering breaking the chaos) is the 2/3 critical value of the L^1 norm of the kernel (a 2/3 clustering critical law); and the phase diagram of the number of clusters in the post-clustering steady state (multi-cluster distributions) is piecewise constant in the kernel parameter (a piecewise cluster count law). (McKean-Vlasov square-root two-thirds clustering)

**中文。** 定义：粒子系统的 McKean–Vlasov 极限的传播混沌：相互作用 N 粒子的经验测度的混沌传播的速率 chaos_rate。猜想：chaos_rate（混沌传播的 Wasserstein 速率）= N^{-1/2}（最优传输平方根律）；且相互作用的奇异（吸引-排斥核）的传播的失效（混沌破缺的成群）的临界强度为核的 L¹ 范数的 2/3 临界（2/3 成群临界律）；成群后的稳态（多群分布）的群的个数的相图为核参数的（折线）分段常数（分段群数律）。
