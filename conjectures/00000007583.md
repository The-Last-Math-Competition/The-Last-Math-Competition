# 00000007583

**English.** Definition: Circular coordinates as one-dimensional cycle recovery of persistent homology. Conjecture: The recovery error of circular coordinates is controlled by the canonical representative of the cocycle; and the optimality of the representative is harmonic type. (harmonic-representative circular coordinates)

**中文。** 定义：圆坐标为持续同调的一维循环恢复。猜想：圆坐标的恢复误差由 cocycle 的典范代表控制；且代表的最优性为调和型。（圆坐标调和代表）
