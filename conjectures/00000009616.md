# 00000009616

**SMA-06 ★** — source: 306_Smale空间与符号动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The beta-transformation T_beta on the unit interval maps x to the fractional part of beta x; for Pisot beta the pure pointedness of the spectrum is the Pisot conjecture. Conjecture: The beta-shift has pure point spectrum if and only if beta is a Pisot unit; in the pure point case the spectral frequency group is a unit group coset of Z[beta^{-1}], and the Pisot criterion is verifiable through the discrete Fourier coefficients of the Parry measure. (beta-shift pure point spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：β-变换 T_β 在单位区间上定义为 x 映到 βx 的小数部分；β 为 Pisot 数时谱的纯点性是 Pisot 猜想主题。猜想：β-移位具有纯点谱当且仅当 β 为 Pisot 单位；纯点情形的谱频率群为 Z[β^{-1}] 的单位群陪集，Pisot 判据通过 Parry 测度的离散 Fourier 系数可验。（beta 移位纯点谱）
