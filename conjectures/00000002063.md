# 00000002063

**English.** Definition: Vopěnka's principle states that there is no large discrete full subcategory. Conjecture: VP is consistent with the existence of supercompact cardinals (but not conversely necessary); the smallest counterexample category (if VP fails) has undefinable complexity (requiring second-order quantifiers).

**中文。** 定义：Vopěnka 原理指不存在大离散的满子范畴。猜想：VP 与超紧基数的存在相容(反之不必要);VP 的最小反例范畴(若 VP 失败)的表述复杂度为不可定义(需二阶量词)。
