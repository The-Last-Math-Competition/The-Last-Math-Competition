**English.** Definition: Strong convergence is the norm convergence of the joint spectrum. Conjecture: The spectral norm convergence rate is, for all beta parameters, the explicit negative one-half power of the dimension times a double logarithm, controlled by the dimension in free polynomial bounds, and universal. (universal double-logarithm rate of strong convergence)
