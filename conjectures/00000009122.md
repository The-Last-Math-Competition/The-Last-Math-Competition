**English.** Definition: Visible lines: unobstructed lines of sight. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of the obstruction measure is full; the exception to full dimension is direct sums of convex obstacles; and the verification of full dimensionality is complete for piecewise smooth obstacle classes. (visible line full spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：可视线为无遮挡的视线族。猜想：遮挡测度的维数谱为满维；且满维的例外为凸障碍直和；且满维性的验证在分段光滑障碍类完成。（可视线满维谱）
