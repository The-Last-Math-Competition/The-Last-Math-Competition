# 00000008489

**English.** Definition: The local deviation spectrum of balanced words: the supremum of local window deviations of letter proportions of frequencies. Conjecture: The deviation supremum of balanced words is 1 (a unit supremum law); the worst word is the Fibonacci word (a Fibonacci worst law); the family of next-worst words consists of Tribonacci type words (a Tribonacci secondary spectrum law); and the complete classification of the worst family is Pisot-type low-complexity words (a Pisot classification law). (balanced word Fibonacci Tribonacci Pisot classification)

**中文。** 定义：平衡词的局部偏差谱：频率字母比例的局部窗口偏差的全体上确界。猜想：平衡词的偏差上确界为 1（单位上确界律）；且最坏词为 Fibonacci 词（Fibonacci 最坏律）；次坏词的族由 Tribonacci 型词构成（Tribonacci 次谱律）；最坏族的完全分类为 Pisot 型低复杂度词（Pisot 分类律）。
