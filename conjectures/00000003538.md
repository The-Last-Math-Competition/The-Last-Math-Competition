# 00000003538

**English.** Definition: Kurepa trees: tall tree combinatorics. Conjecture: The Kurepa problem: the existence of tall trees is independent, with the combinatorial side of independence the separation of branch counts. (Kurepa branch count separation independence)

**中文。** 定义：Kurepa 树：高树组合。猜想：Kurepa 的问题：高树的存在为独立且独立的组合侧为分支计数的分离。（Kurepa 分支计数分离独立）
