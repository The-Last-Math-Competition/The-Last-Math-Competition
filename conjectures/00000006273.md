**English.** Definition: Very weak Bernoulli is weakened independence. Conjecture: Block distances decay polynomially, the exponent controlled by the mixing rate; the kernel of the control is the spectral gap; and the bound of the spectral gap is the return-time distribution. (spectral-gap control of polynomial block distances)

**中文。** 定义：非常弱 Bernoulli 为弱化独立性。猜想：块距离多项式衰减且指数由混合率控制；且控制的核为谱隙；且谱隙的界为返回时间分布。（块距离多项式谱隙控制）
