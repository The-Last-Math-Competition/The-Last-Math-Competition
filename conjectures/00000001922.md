# 00000001922

**English.** Definition: The majorant problem compares norms of controlling functions. Conjecture: The optimal U² constant of a majorant of the squares is always one plus ε; and ε is locked by the logarithmic density of square gaps. (majorant squares logarithmic density)

**中文。** 定义：majorant 问题指控制函数的范数比较。猜想：平方数集的 majorant 在 U² 的最优常数恒为一加 ε；且 ε 由平方间隙的对数密度锁定。（majorant 平方对数密度）
