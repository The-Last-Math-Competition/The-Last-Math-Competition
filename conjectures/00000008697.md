# 00000008697

**OTW-17 ★** — source: 275_最优传输与Wasserstein几何.md

**English.** Definition: Fractional transport: cost |x-y|^p with exponent not equal to 2. Conjecture: The structure of maps for p > 1 is a generalization of monotone maps (a generalization law); p = 1 gives the monotonicity criterion (a monotonicity law); p < 1 gives the arithmetic structure of fractions (an arithmetic law); and p to infinity gives bottleneck transport (a bottleneck law). (fractional transport monotone arithmetic bottleneck)

**中文。** 定义：分数传输：代价 |x-y|^p 的非 2 指数。猜想：p>1 的映射的结构（单调映射的推广）的（推广律）；且 p=1 的（单调性判据）的（单调律）；p<1 的（分数的算术结构）的（算术律）；p→∞ 的（瓶颈传输）的瓶颈的（瓶颈律）。
