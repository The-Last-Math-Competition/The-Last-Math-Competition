**DC3-05 ★** — source: 205_代数几何IX_导出范畴II.md

**English.** Definition: The tilt condition with the tilt structure of stability conditions. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the tilt-parameter domain and the domain endpoints are given by slope vanishing; the interval closes with the closed endpoint being the Zassenhaus threshold; and the length of the domain is a rigidity invariant. (slope-vanishing conversion of tilt domains)

**中文。** 定义：倾角条件：稳定性条件的倾角结构。猜想：换算常数为倾角参数域的显式且域端点由斜率消没给出；区间封闭且封闭的端点为 Zassenhaus 阈值；域的长度为刚性不变量。（倾角域斜率消没换算常数）
