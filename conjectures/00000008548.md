# 00000008548

**English.** Definition: A q-analogue lattice is the lattice of subspaces over a field. Conjecture: The Sperner number of a subspace lattice is the maximum Gaussian binomial coefficient; and its Mobius function is an explicit q-power. (q-lattice Sperner Gaussian)

**中文。** 定义：q-模拟格指域上子空间格。猜想：子空间格的 Sperner 数为 Gaussian 二项式系数最大值；其 Möbius 函数为显式 q-幂。（q-格 Sperner Gaussian）
