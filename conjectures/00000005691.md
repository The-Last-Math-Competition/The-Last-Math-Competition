**TW2-11 ★** — source: 175_范畴的表示_2表示论.md

**English.** Definition: A categorified representation replaces vector spaces with categories. Conjecture: The weight-decomposition lattice is spanned by the standard objects of highest-weight categories, the table is finite, and the completion of the finiteness is the dominant-weight lattice. (dominant-weight standard span of categorification)

**中文。** 定义：范畴化表示为以范畴代替向量空间的表示。猜想：权分解格由最高权范畴的标准对象张成且表有限；且有限的组合为支配权格。（范畴化支配权标准张成）
