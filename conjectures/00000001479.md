# 00000001479

**English.** Definition: Algebraic interiors (Zariski closures of convex hulls of varieties). Conjecture: The degree of the closure is explicitly bounded by a polynomial in the degree and dimension of the variety (a closure degree bound); and the degree of the bound is exactly the overlap dimension of the variety plus one.

**中文。** 定义：代数 interiors(convex hulls of varieties 的 Zariski 闭包)。猜想：闭包次数被簇次数与维数的多项式显式界(闭包次数界)；且界的次数恰为簇重叠维数加一。
