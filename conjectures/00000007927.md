# 00000007927

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic invariant of Schur indices: the Schur index m(pi) of an automorphic representation pi (the reciprocal of the minimal degree of the field of realization). Conjecture: m(pi) lies in {1,2} always (quaternionic type excluded), and m(pi) = 2 if and only if the local components of pi carry the arithmetic obstruction of half-integral weight lifts of discrete series at an even number of places; and the multiplicativity formula m(pi tensor chi) = m(pi) times chi with the quaternionic correction (the Hasse invariant product formula) is automatically satisfied globally (a local-global principle). (Schur index Hasse product law)

**中文。** 定义：Schur 指标的算术不变量：自守表示 π 的 Schur 指标 m(π)（实现域的最小次数的倒数量）。猜想：m(π)∈{1,2} 恒成立（四元数型除外），且 m(π)=2 当且仅当 π 的局部分量在偶数个位上为离散序列的半整权提升的算术障碍；m(π) 的乘性公式 m(π⊗χ)=m(π)·χ 的四元性修正（Hasse 不变量积公式）在整体水平自动满足（局部-整体原理）。
