# 00000008028

**OMN-08 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The maximal invariant of definable compactifications of definable groups: the dimension skeleton d(G) of the maximal definable quotient G/G-degrees of an o-minimal group G. Conjecture: A definable group G has a definable quotient (G/G-degrees a definable finite extension of a Lie group) with the dimension spectrum of the kernel G-degrees integral (Pillay's conjecture, proven); the quantified extension: the decay law of the approximation radius of the fibers of the quotient map is 2^{-d} (d the cell depth); and the homological dimension of every definable group agrees with the dimension of its Lie-ization (homological rigidity), the only exception being the torsion part of the nilpotent kernel (a primary classification of torsion coefficients). (definable group Pillay homological rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：可定义群的可定义紧化的极大不变量：o-极小群 G 的极大可定义商 G/G° 的维数骨架 d(G)。猜想：可定义群 G 为可定义商（G/G° 为 Lie 群的可定义有限扩张）的核 G° 的维数分界谱为整数（Pillay 猜想已证）；延拓的定量化：商映射的纤维的逼近半径的衰减律为 2^{-d}（d 为 cell 深度）；且一切可定义群的同调维数与 Lie 化的维数一致（同调刚性），一致性的例外为幂零核的挠部分（挠系数的准素分类）。
