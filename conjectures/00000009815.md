# 00000009815

**English.** Definition: Discrete Ricci-Yamabe map: the attainable range of target discrete curvatures. Conjecture: The set of discrete curvatures realizable by vertex scaling is a convex polytope; its faces are labeled by maximal genus partition functions, and the boundary concentration modes of curvature correspond to degeneration loci of circle patterns. (attainable curvature polytope)

**中文。** 定义：离散 Ricci–Yamabe 映射：目标离散曲率的可达范围。猜想：顶点缩放可实现的离散曲率集合为凸多面体；其面由极大亏格分划函数标记且边界的曲率集中模式对应圆模式的退化轨迹。（曲率可达多面体）
