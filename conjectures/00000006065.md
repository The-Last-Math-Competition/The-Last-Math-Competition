**English.** Definition: The system is the higher-order recursion framework. Conjecture: Multiplicity one gives primitive recursion, unbounded multiplicity gives the full system, the kernel of the system being the recursion tower, and the levels of the recursion tower are the nesting-depth counting. (recursion-tower primitive recursion of systems)
