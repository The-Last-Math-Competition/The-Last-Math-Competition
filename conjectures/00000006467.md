**English.** Definition: Gaps with the sample-path gap structure. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the maximal-gap set class and the gap of elliptic processes is controlled by the anisotropy ratio; the ratio is optimal, the optimal ratio being the ratio of principal-axis lengths; and the spectrum of the gap degenerates to a single point for rotation-invariant processes. (anisotropy-ratio conversion of elliptic gaps)
