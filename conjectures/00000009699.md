**English.** Definition: p-adic logarithmic linear forms: Baker-type lower bounds under p-adic valuations. Conjecture: Lower bounds for p-adic logarithmic linear forms are of the same order (log B)^{-kappa} as the complex case; the same-order property holds under explicit conditions on the ramified prime set, and the ramification condition cannot be removed. (p-adic Baker same order)

**中文。** 定义：p-adic 对数线性型：p-adic 赋值下的 Baker 型下界。猜想：p-adic 对数线性型的下界与复情形同阶 (log B)^{−κ}；同阶性在分歧素数集合的显式条件下成立且分歧条件不可去。（p-adic Baker 同阶）
