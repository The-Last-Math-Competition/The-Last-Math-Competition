# 00000005571

**English.** Definition: A signalizer functor is the compatible assignment on local subgroups, with its completeness. Conjecture: The completeness conversion of balanced functors is the explicit criterion of the connectivity of the compatibility graph, given at rank two by triangular closure; the number of connected components is the completeness deficit, at most the rank; and the obstruction to triangular closure is noncommutative 2-groups on abelian graphs, vanishing if and only if the graph is a flag triangulation. (triangular-closure completeness criterion of balanced functors)
