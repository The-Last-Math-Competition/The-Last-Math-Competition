# 00000008271

**SFL-11 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The Szego limit of linear prediction of stationary processes: the error szego_err of finite approximation of the Kolmogorov-Szego formula for prediction error variance sigma^2. Conjecture: szego_err (convergence of finite-window prediction error) is sigma^2 (1 + c/n + O(n^{-2})) (a 1/n correction law); the correction constant c is the sum of squares of the Fourier coefficients of the logarithm of the spectral density (a Fourier square-sum law); and the critical index of the failure for long-range dependence (nonabsolute summability, slowing of the correction) is Hurst H = 1/2 (a half-Hurst critical law), the slowing rate being the Hurst correction n^{-(1-2H)}. (Szego finite window Fourier Hurst correction)

**中文。** 定义：平稳过程的线性预测的 Szegő 极限：预测误差方差 σ² 的 Kolmogorov–Szegő 公式的有限逼近的误差 szego_err。猜想：szego_err（有限窗口的预测误差的收敛）= σ²·(1+c/n+O(n^{-2}))（1/n 修正律）；且修正常数 c 为谱密度的对数的 Fourier 系数的平方和（Fourier 平方和律）；长程相关（非绝对可和）的失效（修正的慢化）的临界指数为 Hurst H=1/2（半 Hurst 临界律），慢化的速率为 n^{-(1-2H)} 的 Hurst 修正。
