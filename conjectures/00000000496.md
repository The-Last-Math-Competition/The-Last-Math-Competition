**English.** Definition: Fully packed loops and link patterns. Conjecture: For every n, the Wheel-polynomial representation of link patterns yields total positivity of the structure constants (partially the proven direction of the Razumov–Stroganov correspondence for FPLs).

**中文。** 定义：fully packed loops 与链接型(link pattern)。猜想：任意 n 的 link pattern 的"Wheel polynomial"表示给出结构常数全正(部分为 FPL 的 Razumov–Stroganov 对应,已证方向)。
