**English.** Definition: The condition formula with the macro-element counting bound. Conjecture: The bound is given by graph-theoretic matching and is tight; the saturation of matching is perfect matching; and the saturation of perfect matching is verified on macro-block triangulations. (tight graph-matching bound of macro elements)

**中文。** 定义：条件公式的宏元计数界。猜想：界由图论匹配给出且紧；且匹配的饱和为完美匹配；且完美匹配的饱和在宏块三角剖分验证。（宏元图论匹配紧界）
