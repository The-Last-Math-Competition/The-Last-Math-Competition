# 00000005281

**English.** Definition: The vacuum interface is the free boundary of compressible flow. Conjecture: The physical vacuum interface of the quadratic adiabatic exponent converges at the negative one-half power of time, matched by wave decay, and the interface velocity is uniformly Lipschitz. (half-decay law of vacuum interfaces)

**中文。** 定义：真空界面指可压缩流的自由边界。猜想：平方绝热指数的物理真空界面按时间的负二分之一次幂收敛,由波动衰减匹配且界面速度一致利普希茨。（真空界面半衰减律）
