**English.** Definition: The stability of a mean-field game is the robustness of the equilibrium. Conjecture: The spectral threshold of the linear-quadratic case is an explicit function of the displacement term, it is one half for monotone coupling, and beyond the threshold the unique equilibrium perishes. (mean-field spectral threshold and equilibrium collapse)

**中文。** 定义：平均场博弈的稳定性指均衡的抗扰性。猜想：线性二次情形的谱阈值为位移项的显式函数,单调耦合时为二分之一且超阈值唯一均衡破灭。（平均场谱阈值均衡破灭）
