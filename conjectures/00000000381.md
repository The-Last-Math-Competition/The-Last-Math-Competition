**DA-81 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal Dirichlet-improvement constant ℓ(m,n) for matrices is the extremal value for which the improved inequality always holds. Conjecture: For every matrix pair (m,n), the improvable set has dimension strictly smaller than the product mn; the dimension deficit is controlled by the parity structure of matrix continued fractions and the deficit is uniform. (matrix improvable dimension deficit)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵 Dirichlet 改进的最优常数 ℓ(m,n) 为使改进不等式恒成立的极值。猜想：一切矩阵对 (m,n) 的可改进集的维数均严格小于乘积 mn；维数亏格由矩阵连分数的奇偶结构控制且亏损为一致量。（矩阵可改进维数亏损）
