# 00000008025

**English.** Definition: The quantification of the Kleinberg density point theorem in tame topology: the density constant delta_tam of the density point theorem near boundaries of definable sets in o-minimal structures. Conjecture: The two-scale limit of the local volume density of definable sets exists (a tamed Lusin-type theorem); the density function is a definable 1-Lipschitz function; and the limit of the density constant in the critical band where the dimension of the singular (nonsmooth) point set is at most n-2 is 1/2 (half-density rigidity), the dimension spectrum of half-density point sets being an integer staircase (discreteness of dimensions of tamed sets). (tamed density half-rigidity staircase)

**中文。** 定义：tame topology 的 Kleinberg 稠密点定理的定量化：o-极小结构中的可定义集的边界附近的密度点定理的密度常数 δ_tam。猜想：可定义集的局部体积密度的双尺度极限存在（Lusin 型定理的驯化）；且密度函数为可定义的 1-Lipschitz 函数；密度常数在奇点（非光滑点）集的维数 ≤ n-2 的临界带的极限值为 1/2（半密度刚性），半密度点集的维数谱为整数台阶（驯化集的维数的离散性）。
