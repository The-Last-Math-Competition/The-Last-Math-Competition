# 00000003003

**English.** Definition: Solvability chains of second-order differential equations: the Galois criterion for second-order solvability. Conjecture: The tower structure of second-order solvability: the Galois groups of equation chains are solvable, with tower length the derived length of the group; minimization of the length is given by the nilpotent radical. (second-order tower derived length)

**中文。** 定义：二次微分方程的可解链：二次可解性的 Galois 判据。猜想：二次可解的塔结构：方程链的 Galois 群为可解群且塔的长度为群的导出列长度；长度的极小化由幂零根基给出。（二次塔导出长度）
