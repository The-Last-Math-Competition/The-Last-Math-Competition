# 00000007523

**ET3-13 ◆** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: Deligne's purity theorem guarantees the exactness of eigenvalue weights. Conjecture: The equality domain of weight equal to the weight index w is given by the zero-slope condition; and the equality domain is the whole space for smooth projective varieties. (zero-slope weight-equality domain)

**中文。** 定义：Deligne 纯性定理保证特征值的权精确。猜想：权等于权指标 w 的等号域由斜率零条件给出；且等号域在光滑射影簇为全空间。（权等号斜率零域）
