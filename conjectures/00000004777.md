# 00000004777

**FP3-27 ★** — source: 144_随机矩阵IV_自由概率.md

**English.** Definition: A free martingale is a sequence with free increments. Conjecture: Its large deviation rate function is the explicit Legendre transform of the cumulant generating functional, the constant is one when beta is two, and the function is convex and symmetric. (Legendre formula of free martingale rates)

**中文。** 定义：自由鞅指具自由增量的序列。猜想：其大偏差速率函数为累积量生成泛函的显式 Legendre 变换,β 为二时常数为一且函数凸对称。（自由鞅速率 Legendre 公式）
