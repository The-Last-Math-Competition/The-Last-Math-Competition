# 00000004708

**English.** Definition: A nilsequence is the sampled sequence of a function on a nilpotent flow. Conjecture: Its minimal complexity is explicitly controlled by a linear law in the order of the bracket polynomials, the first difference of the complexity is always one, and the bound is optimal. (linear complexity law of nilsequences)

**中文。** 定义：nilsequence 指幂零流函数的采样序列。猜想：其最小复杂度由括号多项式阶数的线性律显式控制,复杂度一阶差恒为一且界最优。（nilsequence 复杂度线性律）
