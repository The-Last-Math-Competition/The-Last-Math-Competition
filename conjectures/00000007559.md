# 00000007559

**English.** Definition: Unique decomposition as the uniqueness assertion of prime decomposition. Conjecture: The prime-factor count law is a finite table; the recursion of the law is rank halving; and the rank-halving recursion is on the prime-decomposition tree. (rank-halving prime-factor count law)

**中文。** 定义：唯一分解为素分解唯一断言。猜想：素因子个数律为有限表；且律的递推为秩折半；且秩折半的递推在素分解树。（素因子个数秩折半律）
