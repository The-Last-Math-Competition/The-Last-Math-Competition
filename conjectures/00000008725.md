**English.** Definition: Discrete time crystals: subharmonic response from spontaneous breaking of discrete time translation symmetry. Conjecture: MBL-stabilized discrete time crystals exist rigorously (a rigorous stability law); the universal constant of the period-doubling route is the Feigenbaum constant (a Feigenbaum law); Floquet MBL phases exist (a Floquet law); and the boundary between Floquet thermalization and MBL is an explicit function of the driving frequency (a boundary law). (discrete time crystal Feigenbaum Floquet boundary)
