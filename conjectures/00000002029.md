# 00000002029

**AD-09 ◆** — source: 53_加性数论II.md

**English.** Definition: The Chowla two-point correlation is (1/x)Σ_{n≤x}λ(n)λ(n+1). Conjecture: The mean is o(1); moreover it is −c/(log x)^{1/2+ε} (an explicit decay); the decay agrees with the random multiplicative model in the Matomäki–Radziwiłł theorem.

**中文。** 定义：Chowla 二点相关指 (1/x)Σ_{n≤x}λ(n)λ(n+1)。猜想：均值为 o(1);进一步为 −c/(log x)^{1/2+ε}(显式衰减);衰减与随机乘性模型的 Matomäki–Radziwiłł 定理一致。
