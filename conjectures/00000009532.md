**English.** Definition: The Petz recovery map R(.) = sigma^{1/2} phi-dagger((phi(sigma))^{-1/2} . (phi(sigma))^{-1/2}) sigma^{1/2} partially reverses the channel action on the pair (rho,sigma); Fawzi-Renner type inequalities connect the relative entropy contraction to the recovery fidelity error. Conjecture: The relative entropy contraction delta and the optimal recovery error epsilon satisfy the two-sided bound epsilon = Theta(sqrt delta); the square-root exponent is attained by small-angle unitary rotation channel families as theta tends to 0, and any linear recovery rate epsilon = O(delta) necessarily fails on noncommuting state pairs.
