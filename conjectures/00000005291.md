# 00000005291

**English.** Definition: Representation stability is the stable pattern of sequences of symmetric-group representations. Conjecture: The stable starting point of the automorphism representations of curves is an explicit linear expression of the genus, given by the branch monomials of repeated covers, and attained by hyperelliptic families. (linear starting point of automorphism representation stability)

**中文。** 定义：表示稳定性指对称群表示序列的稳定模式。猜想：曲线自同构表示的稳定起点为亏格的显式线性式,由重复覆盖的分支单项式给出且超椭圆族达到。（自同构表示稳定线性起点）
