# 00000001496

**English.** Definition: The C² closing lemma states that closed orbits of any system can be produced by C²-small perturbations. Conjecture: The C² closing lemma holds; whether its analytic-category version fails is equivalent to: whether periodic orbits of analytic systems are dense in the analytic topology (a weak form).

**中文。** 定义：C² 收尾引理指任意系统的闭轨道可被 C²-小扰动产生。猜想：C² 收尾引理成立;其解析范畴版本失败与否等价于:解析系统的周期轨道是否在解析拓扑下稠密(弱形式)。
