**English.** Definition: Analytic torsion: the regularized determinant functional of Ray–Singer. Conjecture: The metric derivative of weighted torsion always has an explicit formula; and the formula contains only boundary spectral-deviation terms with no interior contribution. (weighted torsion derivative formula)
