**English.** Definition: The Tumura–Clunie type concerns the finite-value distribution of multiplicative difference equations. Conjecture: Beyond the complete classification of solutions of f² = P(e^z), the solutions of f·f(z+c) = P(e^z) are explicitly quasiperiodic (difference TC classification).

**中文。** 定义：Tumura–Clunie 型(乘法差分方程的有限值分布)。猜想：方程 f² = P(e^z) 的解的完全分类之外,f·f(z+c) = P(e^z) 的解为拟周期的显式(差分 TC 分类)。
