**English.** Conjecture: The cutoff window of simple random walks on Ramanujan complexes is O(1), and the window constant is determined by the multiplicity of the degenerate eigenvalues of the spectral gap (high-dimensional cutoff).

**中文。** 猜想：Ramanujan 复形上的简单随机游走的 cutoff 窗为 O(1) 且谱隙的退化特征值的重数决定窗口常数(高维截断)。
