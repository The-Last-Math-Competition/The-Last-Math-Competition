# 00000002363

**English.** Definition: The AC function calculus is the absolutely continuous function calculus. Conjecture: Under the AC calculus, the closure of Besov classes is an operator algebra; and the spectrum of the closure is the continuous part of the point spectrum. (AC calculus Besov closure)

**中文。** 定义：AC 函数演算为绝对连续函数演算。猜想：AC 演算下 Besov 类的闭包为算子代数；且闭包的谱为点谱连续部分。（AC 演算 Besov 闭包）
