# 00000006632

**English.** Definition: The kernel locality criterion of the equations. Conjecture: Uniform decay is necessary and sufficient with explicit decay rate; the bound of the rate is the spectral radius; and the bound of the spectral radius is the reciprocal of the constant. (necessary-sufficient uniform decay of kernel locality)

**中文。** 定义：方程公式的核局域判据。猜想：一致衰减充要且衰减率显式；且率的界为谱半径；且谱半径的界为常数的逆。（核准局域一致衰减充要）
