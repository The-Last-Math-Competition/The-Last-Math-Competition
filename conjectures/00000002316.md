# 00000002316

**FR-26 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: An Aut-p-group is a finite p-group whose automorphism group is a p-group. Conjecture: A uniform construction family exists (via iterated constructions {P}_n): the order of its automorphism group equals p^{(n²−n)/2}·|{P}_n|^{explicit}; the family covers all types of Aut-p-groups.

**中文。** 定义：Aut-p-群指自同构群为 p-群的有限 p-群。猜想：存在统一构造族(通过迭代构造 {P}_n):其自同构群的阶 = p^{(n²-n)/2}·|{P}_n|^{\text{某显式}};族覆盖一切 Aut-p-群的型。
