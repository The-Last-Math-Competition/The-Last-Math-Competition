**English.** Definition: The virtual genus is the integral count on the virtual fundamental class. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit integral of the virtual class, agreeing with the classical genus in the convex case; the difference term is a functional on torsion groups, of order dividing the valuation of the discriminant; and rationalization removes all torsion differences, the removal invariant under family translation. (torsion-difference conversion of virtual genus integrals)
