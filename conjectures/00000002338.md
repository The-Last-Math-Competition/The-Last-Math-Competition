# 00000002338

**English.** Conjecture: The rank threshold of numerical factorization is an explicit gap of singular values: the ratio of the k-th to the (k+1)-st singular value exceeds d² (the square of the degree); the threshold criterion is the relative proportion of the gap.

**中文。** 猜想：数值因式分解的秩阈值为奇异值的显式间隔:第 k 与第 k+1 奇异值的比值 > d²(度数的平方);阈值判据为间隔的相对比例。
