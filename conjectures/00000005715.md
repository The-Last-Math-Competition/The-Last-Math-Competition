# 00000005715

**English.** Definition: The critical frequency values are the jump points of the frequency of polynomial germs. Conjecture: The three-dimensional critical values form an explicit sequence, given by the lowest mixed order of spherical harmonics, and the values are exact. (spherical-harmonic mixed-order critical sequence in three dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：频率临界值指多项式芽的频率跳跃点。猜想：三维的临界值为显式序列,由球谐的最低混合阶给出且值精确。（三维球谐混合阶临界序列）
