# 00000005299

**English.** Definition: A VI module is a module over the category of finite vector spaces. Conjecture: The finitely generated characteristic domain in characteristic two is an explicit lattice of polynomial counting functions, given by monomial quotient counting, and decidable in polynomial time. (counting-lattice decidability of VI modules in characteristic two)

**中文。** 定义：VI 模指有限向量空间范畴的模。猜想：特征二的有限生成特征域为多项式计数函数的显式格,由单项式商计数给出且判定多项式时间。（VI 特征二计数格判定）
