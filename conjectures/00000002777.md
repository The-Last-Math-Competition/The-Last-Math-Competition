**English.** Definition: Distortion: the distance between a space and its renormings. Conjecture: The continuous spectrum of distortion: the distortion of separable infinite-dimensional spaces can be infinite, with ℓ₂ the minimally distorted space; the first infinite distortion value is the stable limit of an explicit sequence, and the limit space is of Gowers–Maurey type. (distortion spectrum minimal infinite value)

**中文。** 定义：distortion（畸变）：空间与其重赋范的距离。猜想：畸变的连续谱：可分无穷维空间的畸变值可达无穷且极小畸变空间为 ℓ₂；畸变的第一无限值为显式序列的稳定极限且极限空间为 Gowers–Maurey 型。（畸变谱极小无限值）
