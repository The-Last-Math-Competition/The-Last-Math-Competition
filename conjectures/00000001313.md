# 00000001313

**CC-13 ◆** — source: 29_多复变与复几何.md

**English.** Definition: Levi-flat hypersurfaces (the curvature vanishes in all complex tangent directions). Conjecture: There is no smooth Levi-flat hypersurface in CP² (Brunella proved the analytic case; the smooth case is open).

**中文。** 定义：Levi 平坦超曲面(复切方向全曲率为零)。猜想：ℂℙ² 中不存在光滑 Levi 平坦超曲面(Brunella 已证解析情形;光滑情形开放)。
