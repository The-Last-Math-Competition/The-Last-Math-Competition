# 00000005122

**English.** Definition: Stagnation is the phenomenon that the residual does not decrease at some step. Conjecture: The three-dimensional stagnation criterion is the orthogonality condition of the initial vector with an invariant subspace, the condition is the zero set of a quadratic form, and the measure is explicitly computable. (orthogonal quadratic criterion of three-dimensional stagnation)

**中文。** 定义：停滞指残差在某步不下降的现象。猜想：三维停滞判据为初始向量与不变子空间的正交条件,条件为二次型零集且测度显式可算。（三维停滞正交二次判据）
