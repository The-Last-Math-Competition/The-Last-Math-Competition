# 00000009696

**English.** Definition: CM periods of high-genus curves: the position of period values of Abelian varieties in a CM field within the period field. Conjecture: Periods of Jacobians with complex multiplication generate the corresponding-type subfield of the holomorphic period field; the number of generators is exactly half the degree of the CM field, and generation is stable under Galois conjugation. (CM period generation)

**中文。** 定义：高亏格曲线的 CM 周期：Abel 曲线在某 CM 域的周期值的周期域地位。猜想：带复乘的 Jacobian 的周期生成全纯周期域的对应型子域；生成元的个数恰为 CM 域次数的一半且生成性在 Galois 共轭下稳定。（CM 周期生成性）
