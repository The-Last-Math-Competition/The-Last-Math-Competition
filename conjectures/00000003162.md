**English.** Definition: RIP (restricted isometry property): the condition that matrices preserve norms of sparse vectors. Conjecture: The optimal constants of RIP: at the phase transition the RIP constant is the explicit threshold δ_k(c), derived by the volume comparison of lattice sphere coverings. (RIP phase transition volume threshold)

**中文。** 定义：RIP（有限等距性质）：矩阵保持稀疏向量范数的条件。猜想：RIP 的最优常数：相变处的 RIP 常数为显式阈值 δ_k(c) 且阈值的推导为格球覆盖的体积比较。（RIP 相变体积阈值）
