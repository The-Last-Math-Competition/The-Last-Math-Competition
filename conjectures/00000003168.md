**English.** Definition: Dictionary learning: learning sparse representation bases from samples. Conjecture: The sample threshold of identifiability: the sample count for local identification of a dictionary is an explicit exponent of the dictionary coherence, with the tightness of the threshold the separation of signature vectors. (dictionary learning coherence exponent threshold)

**中文。** 定义：字典学习：从样本学习稀疏表示基。猜想：辨识的样本阈值：字典的局部辨识的样本数为字典相干度的显式指数且阈值的紧性为签名向量的分离。（字典学习相干指数阈值）
