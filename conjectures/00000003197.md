# 00000003197

**English.** Definition: Homogenized Bézout bounds: the counting of isolated zeros. Conjecture: The algorithmic correction of multiple roots: the local multiplicity of a zero is the intersection multiplicity, with the computation of multiplicity the length of the local algebra. (Bezout intersection multiplicity local length)

**中文。** 定义：齐次化的 Bezout 界：孤立零点的计数。猜想：重根的算法修正：零点的局部重数为交重数且重数的计算为局部代数的长度。（Bezout 交重数局部长度）
