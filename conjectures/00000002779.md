# 00000002779

**English.** Definition: Finite representability and ultrapowers: the structure of the ultrapower X^U of a space X. Conjecture: The principal-ideal lattice of ultrapowers: the finitely representable elements of an ultrapower are the image of a closure operator, and the minimal generators of the image are the complete list of asymptotic types of the space. (ultrapower finite representability lattice)

**中文。** 定义：有限表示与超幂：空间 X 的超幂 X^U 的结构。猜想：超幂的主理想格：超幂的有限表示元集为闭包算子的像且像的极小生成元为空间的渐近型完全列表。（超幂有限表示格）
