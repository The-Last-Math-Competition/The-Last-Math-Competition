# 00000004181

**GM3-01 ◆** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: The Almgren-Pitts p-width W_p is the min-max volume over p-parameter families in the space of integral currents. Conjecture: A closed three-manifold satisfies the Weyl law W_p asymptotic to c * p^{1/3} * Vol^{2/3}, with the constant c explicit and metric-independent, and the remainder is little-o of p^{1/3}. (determination of the volume spectrum Weyl constant)

**中文。** 定义：Almgren–Pitts 的 p-宽度 W_p 指整值流量空间上 p 参数族的极小极大体积。猜想：闭三维流形满足 Weyl 律 W_p 渐近于 c·p^{1/3}·Vol^{2/3},常数 c 显式且与度量无关,余项为小 o(p^{1/3})。（体积谱 Weyl 常数确定）
