# 00000009765

**English.** Definition: The Weyl-von Neumann theorem: normal operators are diagonalizable by small compact perturbations; the quantitative rate of the perturbation is unknown. Conjecture: The minimal perturbation norm of the n-th approximate diagonalization of a normal operator is an explicit trade-off sum of the spectral sequence; the corresponding quantity for nonnormal operators is controlled by the deviation of numerical radius from singular values, and the deviation can be infinite. (diagonalization perturbation rate)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl–von Neumann 定理：正规算子可被小紧扰动对角化；扰动的定量速率未知。猜想：正规算子的第 n 逼近对角化的最小扰动范数为谱列的显式折衷和；非正规算子的相应量由数值半径与奇异值的偏差控制且偏差可达无穷。（对角化扰动速率）
