**English.** Definition: A twisted sheaf is a coherent sheaf with Brauer phase. Conjecture: The number of connected components of the moduli space of twisted sheaves equals the lattice-point count of the twisted Chern character, the formula is explicit at low index, and the components are pairwise equidimensional. (lattice-point count of twisted moduli components)

**中文。** 定义：扭曲层指带 Brauer 相位的凝聚层。猜想：扭曲层模空间的连通分量数等于扭曲陈特征的格点计数,公式在低指标显式且分量两两同维。（扭曲模分量格点计数）
