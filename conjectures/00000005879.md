# 00000005879

**English.** Definition: The efficiency constant is the ratio of the estimator to the true error. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit supremum of the local load, attained at explicit oscillation terms, and the bound is tight. (load-supremum oscillation conversion formula of efficiency)
