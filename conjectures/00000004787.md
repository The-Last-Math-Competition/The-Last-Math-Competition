# 00000004787

**English.** Definition: The Picard rank of smooth three-dimensional Fano varieties. Conjecture: The upper bound of the Picard rank is 10, and the rank-10 varieties are realized by explicit toric-type constructions with finitely many isomorphism classes; the total number of isomorphism classes of rank at least 8 is a finite explicit number; the total number of deformation families of rank 1 is 17, each family characterized by its index. (Picard rank upper bound and counts of three-dimensional Fanos)

**中文。** 定义：三维光滑 Fano 簇的 Picard 秩。猜想：Picard 秩的上界为 10 且秩 10 的簇由显式 Toric 型构造实现、同构类为有限；秩至少 8 的同构类总数为有限显式数；秩 1 的变形族总数为 17 且每个族由其索引刻画。（三维 Fano 秩上界与计数）
