**English.** Definition: The Euler characteristic of the union of high-energy nodal domains is a topological statistic of the nodal set. Conjecture: Its sign is predicted by the Gaussian functional of the Berry wave field with error λ^{1/2−ε}; and the universal class of the prediction is a one-parameter Gaussian field. (nodal domain topology Berry prediction)

**中文。** 定义：高能节域并的 Euler 示性数为节域拓扑统计。猜想：其符号由 Berry 波场的高斯泛函预测且误差为 λ^{1/2−ε}；且预测的普适类为单参数高斯场。（节域拓扑 Berry 预测）
