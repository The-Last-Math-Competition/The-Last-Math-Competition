**English.** Definition: The Donaldson-Thomas generating function Z_X(q) counts weighted Euler characteristics of moduli spaces of ideal sheaves on a threefold; the DT/PT correspondence identifies it with the stable pair generating function up to an Euler factor. Conjecture: Relative DT invariants decomposed across the two sides of a slope divisor satisfy the fusion rules of a symmetric pants decomposition, the fusion coefficients forming a three-dimensional TQFT whose pairing space dimension is given by the signature of the Hilbert lattice; and the fusion rules uniquely determine the quasimodular weight and the modular positions of the poles of Z_X(q). (DT pants fusion TQFT)

**中文。** 定义：Donaldson–Thomas 不变量的整体母函数 Z_X(q) 计数三维簇 X 上理想层模空间的加权 Euler 特征。DT/PT 对应断言 DT 与稳定对不变量生成函数相等（乘 Euler 积分修正）。猜想：相对 DT 不变量在斜面除子的两侧分解满足对称 pants 分解的融合律，融合系数构成 3 维 TQFT，其配对空间维数由 Hilbert 格的号差给出；该融合律唯一确定 Z_X(q) 的拟模性权重与极点的模位置。
