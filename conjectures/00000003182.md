**English.** Definition: Sparse cuts of random graphs: the spectrum of structured sparsity. Conjecture: The spectral existence of sparse cuts: a graph is an expander if and only if all small cuts have large spectral gap, with the minimal realization of the gap regular graphs. (sparse cut expander criterion)

**中文。** 定义：随机图的稀疏割：结构化稀疏的谱。猜想：稀疏割的谱存在：图为 expander 当且仅当一切小割的谱间隙大且间隙的极小实现为正则构图。（稀疏割 expander 判据）
