# 00000007889

**English.** Definition: The trace method for categorical weights: the noncommutative unitization tau(C) = HH_0(Perf(C)) of the Doplicher-Richards trace of an exact category. Conjecture: For every essentially small additive category, the Hattori-Stallings trace on the K-theory of tau(C) is surjective; and tau preserves localization sequences, its kernel being the bicoequalizer image of double localization; the trace spectral sequence of categorical weights has on its second page a 5-term exact sequence that is exactly the shadow of the fundamental localization column of noncommutative motives. (categorical trace localization shadow)

**中文。** 定义：范畴权的迹方法：精确范畴的 Doplicher–Richards 迹 τ(C)=HH_0(Perf(C)) 的非交换单位化。猜想：对一切本质小加性范畴，τ(C) 的 K-理论的 Hattori–Stallings 迹为满射；且 τ 保持局部化列，其核为二重局部化的双协合像；范畴权的谱化（trace spectral sequence）在第二页的 5-项正合列恰为 Noncommutative Motives 的基本局部化列的影子。
