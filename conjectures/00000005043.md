**English.** Definition: Locality is the property that first-order formulas decompose as local disjunctions. Conjecture: The optimal locality-radius formula is an explicit exponent product of the quantifier number and the degree, proven optimal by matching lower bounds of random regular graphs. (optimal exponent-product formula of the locality radius)
