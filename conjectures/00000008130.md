# 00000008130

**NAG-20 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The contact geometry of categorical compactifications of noncommutative varieties: the compactification invariant ct_nc of the contact structure (Y,xi) at infinity of an nc variety. Conjecture: ct_nc is a categorical obstruction to compactification and an invariant of symplectic homotopy type; the existence of the contact structure (existence of a Stein filling) is equivalent to the agreement of the smooth obstruction theory of the derived category of the variety (a filling-obstruction agreement); and the minimum of the dimension spectrum of fillings is Stein (a minimal Stein law), the sum of the Betti numbers of the minimal filling being at most the Euler characteristic of the Hochschild homology of the nc variety (a Betti-HH Euler bound). (contact Stein filling Hochschild bound)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的范畴紧化的接触几何：nc 簇的无限远处的接触结构 (Y,ξ) 的紧化不变量 ct_nc。猜想：ct_nc 为紧化的范畴障碍且为辛同伦型不变量；且接触结构的存在（Stein 填充的存在）等价于簇的导出范畴的 smooth 障碍理论的一致（填充-障碍一致）；填充的维数谱的极小值为 Stein 的（极小 Stein 律），极小填充的 Betti 数的和 ≤ nc 簇的 Hochschild 同调的 Euler 特征（Betti-HH Euler 界）。
