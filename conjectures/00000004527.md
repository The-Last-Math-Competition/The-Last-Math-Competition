**English.** Definition: Warning propagation is the simplified transfer scheme of Boolean messages. Conjecture: Near the random 3-SAT threshold the number of its fixed points is a linear exponential power of 2, and the pre-exponential factor is given explicitly by the tree sub-solution count. (exponential count of WP fixed points)

**中文。** 定义：警告传播指布尔消息的简化传递格式。猜想：随机 3-SAT 阈值附近其不动点个数为 2 的线性指数次,指数前因子由树上子解计数显式给出。（WP 不动点指数计数）
