**English.** Definition: An exponential period is the algebraic-chain integral of exponential differentials. Conjecture: The dimension of the exponential periods of the projective line minus three points is two, with basis an explicit pair of Gamma values, closed for parameters satisfying the arithmetic group condition. (Gamma basis of three-point exponential periods)

**中文。** 定义：指数周期指指数微分的代数链积分。猜想：射影直线去三点的指数周期维数为二,基为显式 Gamma 值对,且对满足算术群条件的参数封闭。（三点指数周期 Gamma 基）
