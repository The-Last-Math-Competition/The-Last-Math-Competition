# 00000009846

**WEX-26 ★** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: Unconditionality of the Haar system: properties of the Haar basis in weighted L^p. Conjecture: The unconditional basis constant of the Haar system in weighted L^p is polynomial in the weight constant, of degree an explicit function of p; the degree is exact, attained by staircases of dyadic two-block weights. (Haar unconditional exact degree)

**中文。** 定义：Haar 系的无条件性：加权 L^p 中 Haar 基的性质。猜想：Haar 系在加权 L^p 的无条件基常数为权常数的多项式且次数为 p 的显式函数；多项式次数精确且由二进两块权的阶梯达到。（Haar 无条件精确次数）
