**English.** Definition: The Recamán sequence is defined by the backtracking-doubling recurrence. Conjecture: Every nonnegative integer occurs only finitely often in the sequence; and the finiteness is settled by interval covering. (Recaman finite occurrence via interval covering)

**中文。** 定义：Recamán 序列为回退加倍的递推。猜想：序列的每一非负整数出现有限次；且有限的核为区间覆盖。（Recaman 区间覆盖出现有限）
