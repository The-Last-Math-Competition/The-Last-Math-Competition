# 00000008145

**RNG-05 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The Borel summability of the renormalization constants of the perturbative expansion of the Wightman index at the Gaussian fixed point: borel(F). Conjecture: borel (Borel summability) holds in supercritical dimensions (d > 4) and in the infrared region at d = 4 (the Borel domain); the real part of the imaginary parts of the singularities in the Borel plane (instanton singularities) is 2 pi over a factor (a Le Guillou-Zinn-Justin type singularity formula), the distribution of singularities in the Borel plane forming a logarithmic lattice (a log-lattice singularity law); and the minimal model failing Borel summability (resonance) is the supersymmetric model with fermions (a SUSY resonance exception). (Borel instanton log-lattice SUSY exception)

**中文。** 定义：Gaussian 不动点的 Wigthman 指标的扰动展开的重整化常数的 Borel 可和性：borel(F)。猜想：borel（Borel 可和）在超临界维数（d>4）与 d=4 的红外区域成立（Borel 域）；且 Borel 平面的奇点（瞬时子奇点）的虚部的实部为 2π/因子（Le Guillou–Zinn-Justin 型奇点公式），奇点的密度在 Borel 平面上的分布为对数格点（对数格点奇点律）；非 Borel 可和（共振）的最小模型为带费米子的超对称模型（SUSY 共振例外）。
