# 00000004858

**English.** Definition: A free subgroup is a rank-two nonabelian free subgroup. Conjecture: The free subgroup of a torsion-free hyperbolic group is given by a ping-pong combination of two explicit words, the word-length upper bound is an explicit exponential in the hyperbolicity constant, and the construction is polynomial time. (explicit ping-pong construction bound of free subgroups)

**中文。** 定义：自由子群指秩二非交换自由子群。猜想：无挠双曲群的自由子群由两个显式词的乒乓组合给出,词长上界为双曲常数的显式指数且构造多项式时间。（自由子群乒乓显式构造界）
