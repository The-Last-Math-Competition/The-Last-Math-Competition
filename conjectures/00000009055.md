**English.** Definition: The Schoen-Yau monotonicity formula: monotone quantities of area. Conjecture: The remainder of the monotonicity formula is always the potential term; and the sign of the remainder is controlled by scalar curvature. (Schoen-Yau monotonicity potential remainder)

**中文。** 定义：Schoen–Yau 单调公式为面积的单调量。猜想：单调公式的余项恒为位势项；且余项的符号由数量曲率控制。（Schoen-Yau 单调余项位势）
