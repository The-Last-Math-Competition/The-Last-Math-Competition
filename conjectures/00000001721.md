# 00000001721

**DG-01 ◆** — source: 43_丢番图几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Vojta's main conjecture (the height-density conjecture) on the blowup of P² at r general-position points asserts finiteness of integral points (the case r ≥ 10 being equivalent to abc). Conjecture: The finiteness of integral points at r = 9 does not require abc; this case can exclude all parabolic exceptions by an explicit construction of automorphic forms.

**中文。** 定义：Vojta 主猜想(高字典猜想)对 ℙ² 在 r 个一般位置点的爆破积分点有限性(需 r≥10 等价于 abc)的剩余情形。猜想：r=9 的积分点有限性不依赖 abc;该情形可由自守形式的显式构造排除一切抛物例外。
