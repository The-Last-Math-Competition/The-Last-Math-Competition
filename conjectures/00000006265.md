# 00000006265

**EG3-15 ◆** — source: 194_遍历论III_熵公式.md

**English.** Definition: Postcritically finite maps with repeller spectral values. Conjecture: The spectral value of the repeller is a logarithmic algebraic number, realized as the golden value in the quadratic family; the degree of the logarithmic algebraic number is finite and divides the period count; and the entropy of the golden value is the log of the golden ratio, an algebraic unit. (logarithmic-algebraic golden value of postcritical maps)

**中文。** 定义：特殊点（后临界）：后临界轨道有限的映射；排斥子谱值。猜想：排斥子的谱值为对数代数数且二次族的实现为黄金值；对数代数数的次数为有限且次数整除周期数；黄金值的熵为 log 黄金比且比值为代数单位。（特殊点对数代数黄金值）
