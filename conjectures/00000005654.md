**English.** Definition: Uniform convergence is the uniform rate of pointwise mixing. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit exponential of the maximal degree deviation, controlled by the local-tree approximation error, and it degenerates to one for regular graphs. (tree-approximation conversion constant of uniform mixing)

**中文。** 定义：一致收敛指逐点混合的一致速率。猜想：换算常数为最大度偏差的显式指数,由局部树逼近误差控制且正则图时退化为一。（一致混合树逼近换算常数）
