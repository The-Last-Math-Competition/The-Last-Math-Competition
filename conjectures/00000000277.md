# 00000000277

**English.** Definition: M(T) is the maximum multiplicity of a Laplace eigenvalue on a planar flat torus T = C/Λ, and M its supremum over all flat tori. Conjecture: M = 6 (6 is known to be attainable, and upper bounds ≥ 6 are constrained by congruence structure); and multiplicity six is attained only on the lattice torus with maximal automorphism group of order thirty-two.

**中文。** 定义：平面平坦环面 T = ℂ/Λ 的 Laplace 算子特征值重数的最大值 M(T)(对所有平坦环面取上确界 M)。猜想：M = 6(已知 6 可达且 ≥ 6 的上界受限于同余结构)；且重数六仅在自同构群极大的三十二阶格点格达到。
