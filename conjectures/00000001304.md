# 00000001304

**CC-04 ★** — source: 29_多复变与复几何.md

**English.** Definition: Compactness of the Bergman-space Toeplitz operator T_f on the Hartogs triangle T is characterized by the boundary behavior of the symbol f. Conjecture: T_f is compact if and only if f vanishes on the essential boundary |z₁| = |z₂| and satisfies the b-entropy condition ∫|f|²·log log(1/|z₂|)^{-2} → 0 on the double-logarithmic neighborhood of the inner boundary |z₁| = 0; this is the first instance of a Suárez-type characterization on a nonsmooth domain.

**中文。** 定义：Hartogs 三角 T 的 Bergman 空间 Toeplitz 算子 T_f 的紧性以符号 f 的边界行为刻画。猜想：T_f 紧当且仅当 f 在 T 的本质边界 |z₁|=|z₂| 上消没且在内边界 |z₁|=0 的双对数邻域上满足 b-熵条件 ∫|f|²·log log(1/|z₂|)^{-2} → 0;该判据是 Suárez 型刻画到非光滑域的第一个实例。
