# 00000000162

**C2-09 ★** — source: 12_组合代数与几何II.md

**English.** Conjecture: Convex polygons with prime-square side lengths: for every n ≥ 3 there exists a planar convex n-gon whose side vectors all have prime squared lengths (two-squares vectors for primes p ≡ 1 mod 4) and sum to zero.

**中文。** 猜想：素数平方边长的凸多边形:对每个 n ≥ 3,存在平面凸 n 边形,其各边向量长度平方均为素数(p ≡ 1 mod 4 的二平方和向量),且向量之和为零。
