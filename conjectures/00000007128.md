**English.** Definition: Scissors congruence as the decomposition equivalence of polytopes. Conjecture: The complete invariant of congruence is volume plus the Dehn invariant, and the functional of the invariant is tensor-valued on dihedral angles. (tensor-valued Dehn complete congruence invariant)
