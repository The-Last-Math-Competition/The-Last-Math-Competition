# 00000001243

**English.** Definition: The central column of Wolfram's rule 30 is the output sequence of column 0 from a single 1 as initial condition. Conjecture: This sequence is not eventually periodic, and among its factors of length < 2^k none contains a square: the central column is square-free over all prefixes of length 2^k; this rules out any short-period structure.

**中文。** 定义：Wolfram rule 30 的中心列指初值为单 1 时第 0 列的输出序列。猜想：该序列非最终周期,且其长度 < 2^k 的因子中不含平方:即中心列在一切前 2^k 项中 square-free;这排除了任何短周期结构。
