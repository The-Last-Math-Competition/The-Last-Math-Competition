**GM3-12 ★** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: The AC interface is the transition layer of the solution, and the second variation is the quadratic form of the linearized operator. Conjecture: The negative-eigenvalue count converges, as epsilon tends to zero, to the Morse index of the limiting minimal surface plus a self-intersection correction, the correction being a nonnegative integer determined solely by the self-intersection count of the interface. (AC negative-spectrum convergence and self-intersection correction)

**中文。** 定义：AC 界面指解的过渡层,第二变分指线性化算子的二次型。猜想：负特征值计数在 ε 趋于零时收敛于极限极小曲面的 Morse 指标加自交修正,修正为非负整数且仅由界面自交数决定。（AC 负谱收敛-自交修正）
