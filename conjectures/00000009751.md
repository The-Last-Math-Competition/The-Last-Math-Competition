**English.** Definition: Average complexity of Groebner bases: element degrees of bases of generic ideals in random coordinates. Conjecture: The maximal degree of Groebner basis elements of generic ideals in random coordinates is of double-exponential order d^{2^{n-1}} in the worst case but drops to d^{O(n)} on average; the average-versus-worst gap is explicitly given by the measure of degenerate ideals. (Groebner average complexity)
