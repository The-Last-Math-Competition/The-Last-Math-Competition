**English.** Definition: The stability bound is the dimension-dependence phenomenon of group constructions. Conjecture: The stability bound of special ring classes is twice the dimension minus three; the bound is non-lowering and attained; and the attainment is realized in the minimal representation of special rings. (2n-3 stability bound of special rings)

**中文。** 定义：稳定界为群构造的维数依赖现象。猜想：特殊环类的稳定界为二倍维数减三；且界不可降且达到；且界的达到在特殊环的极小表示中实现。（特殊环稳定界 2n-3）
