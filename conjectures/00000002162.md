# 00000002162

**English.** Definition: The Floquet spectrum of a periodic graph is its band structure over the Brillouin zone; band gaps are open intervals free of eigenvalues. Conjecture: The band-gap count is an explicit covering of the BZ (a finite union with boundaries); the covering is given by the Morse theory of the lattice's energy surface.

**中文。** 定义：周期图的 Floquet 谱指其在 Brillouin 区上的带结构;带隙指无特征值的开区间。猜想：带隙计数为 BZ 的显式覆盖(带边界的有限并);覆盖由 lattice 的能量面的 Morse 理论给出。
