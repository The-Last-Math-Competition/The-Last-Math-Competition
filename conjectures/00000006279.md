# 00000006279

**English.** Definition: Loose Bernoulli is the equivalence class of name counting, very weak Bernoulli. Conjecture: The cutting-and-stacking counterexample breaks the logarithmic window, the combination of the breakage being window shift; the kernel of the shift is the name-frequency difference, bounded; and the breakage window of cutting-and-stacking examples is an explicit interval. (cutting-stacking breakage structure of name logarithm windows)

**中文。** 定义：松 Bernoulli：名字计数的等价类（very weak Bernoulli）。猜想：对数窗的卡型例子破裂且破裂的组合为窗移位；移位的核为名字频率差且差有界；卡型例子（cutting and stacking）的破裂窗为显式区间。（名字对数窗卡型破裂结构）
