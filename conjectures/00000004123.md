**FA2-03 ◆** — source: 123_高等数理逻辑_强制法与公理.md

**English.** Definition: The P-ideal dichotomy is the alternative theorem stating that a P-ideal either contains a countable intersection side. Conjecture: Every natural refinement of the dichotomy is of the same consistency strength as PFA, and any consistency proof of a refinement requires a supercompact cardinal. (strength stratification of the P-ideal dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：P-理想二分指 P-理想或含可数交侧的二择一定理。猜想：二分的一切自然细化版本恒与 PFA 等强度；且任何细化的一致性证明都需要超紧基数。（P-理想二分强度分层）
