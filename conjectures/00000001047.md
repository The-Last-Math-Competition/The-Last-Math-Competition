# 00000001047

**FG-47 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: An o-polynomial (oval polynomial). Conjecture: o-polynomials over F_q exist only for even q, with count of order q^{q/2} (a direction toward o-polynomial counting).

**中文。** 定义：o-polynomial(oval 多项式)。猜想：F_q 的 o-多项式仅在 q 偶存在且个数 ~ q^{q/2} 计数(o-polynomial 计数方向)。
