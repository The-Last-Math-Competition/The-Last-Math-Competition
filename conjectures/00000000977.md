# 00000000977

**English.** Definition: Uniqueness of spectral synthesis is the set version of the uncertainty principle. Conjecture: On compact groups, the chain length of ideal closures for uniqueness sets of spectral synthesis is at most two; and the extremal chain length is controlled by the depth of the coset-null sets. (synthesis chain length upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：谱合成的唯一性为不确定原理的集合版。猜想：紧群上谱合成唯一性集合的理想闭包链长度不超过二；且链长的极值由余零集深度控制。（合成链长度上界）
