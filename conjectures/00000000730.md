# 00000000730

**PA-30 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: Gerth's correction compares the plus and minus parts of the 3-part of the class group. Conjecture: The plus and minus 3-ranks are jointly distributed independently; and the refinement of independence holds for families of imaginary quadratic fields. (Gerth independence refined)

**中文。** 定义：Gerth 修正为三部分类群的正负部分的比较。猜想：正负三秩的联合分布独立；且独立性的精化在虚二次域族成立。（Gerth 独立性精化）
