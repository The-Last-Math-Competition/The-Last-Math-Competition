# 00000009713

**SCA-13 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Type-II blowup rates of three-dimensional focusing nonlinear waves: bounded-energy blowup speeds. Conjecture: The rate of type-II blowup is (1+o(1))/t with secondary correction the universal negative -c/t^2; the correction constant is explicitly given by the first eigenvalue of the soliton linearization and is universal. (type-II blowup correction law)

**中文。** 定义：三维聚焦非线性波的二型爆破速率：能量有界的爆破速度。猜想：type-II 爆破的速率为 (1+o(1))/t 且次阶修正为 −c/t² 的普适负修正；修正常数由孤子线性化的第一特征值显式给出并普适。（二型爆破修正律）
