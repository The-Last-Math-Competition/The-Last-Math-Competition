# 00000004077

**BF2-17 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: A heteroclinic cycle is a closed chain of orbits connecting a list of saddles. Conjecture: Stability is always decided by the product of the eigenvalue ratios of the saddles being less than one, and at codimension-two degeneracy the criterion is replaced by the first-order Melnikov integral. (product-Melnikov criterion for heteroclinic cycles)

**中文。** 定义：异宿环指连接鞍点列的闭轨链。猜想：稳定性恒由各鞍点特征值比的乘积小于一判定；且余维二退化时判据由一阶 Melnikov 积分替代。（异宿环乘积 Melnikov 判据）
