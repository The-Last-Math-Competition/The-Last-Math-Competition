# 00000004077

**BF2-17 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: ring denotes closed orbit of saddle point sequence. Conjecture: stability always by product of each saddle point eigenvalue ratio less than one; and codimension two degenerate by one order Melnikov integral. (ring product Melnikov)

**中文。** 定义：异宿环指连接鞍点列的闭轨链。猜想：稳定性恒由各鞍点特征值比的乘积小于一判定；且余维二退化时判据由一阶 Melnikov 积分替代。（异宿环乘积 Melnikov 判据）
