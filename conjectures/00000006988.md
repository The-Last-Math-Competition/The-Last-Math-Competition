# 00000006988

**English.** Definition: The concentration scale law with the bubble-concentration structure. Conjecture: The bubble-law exponent is explicit and the closed verification is the dilation invariant; the matching of the invariant is mass conservation; and the critical value of the exponent is half the dimension. (dilation-invariant concentration bubble law)

**中文。** 定义：集中尺度律：气泡集中结构。猜想：气泡律指数显式且封闭的验证为伸缩不变量；伸缩不变量的匹配为质量守恒；指数的临界值为维数之半。（集中气泡律伸缩不变结构）
