# 00000001657

**English.** Definition: The graded center of a Calabi–Yau category. Conjecture: The Amiot cluster category C_A has Serre dimension an integer of dim/2 type, and its graded center is a cyclic group action (the Amiot center).

**中文。** 定义：Calabi–Yau 范畴的分次中心。猜想：Amiot 簇范畴 C_A 的 Serre 维数为 dim/2 型整数值且其分次中心为循环群作用(Amiot 中心)。
