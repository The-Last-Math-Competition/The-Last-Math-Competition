# 00000001374

**DS-14 ★** — source: 31_描述集合论与递归论.md

**English.** Definition: A ∀∃-problem is a mass problem of the form "for every X there is Y with (X,Y) ∈ W" with W Borel; its Muchnik spectrum is the set of weak-reduction degrees of such problems. Conjecture: The Muchnik spectrum of a ∀∃-problem is always a complete distributive lattice, whose lattice operations are realized explicitly by unions and intersections of problems; and the maximal elements of the spectrum are exactly the Mass problem degrees of basis-generic sets.

**中文。** 定义：∀∃-问题指形如"对一切 X 存在 Y:(X,Y)∈W"的质量问题,W 为 Borel;其 Muchnik 谱指该类问题的弱归约度集合。猜想：∀∃-问题的 Muchnik 谱恒为完备分配格;格运算由问题的并交显式实现；且谱的极大元恰为基泛型的 Mass 问题度。
