**English.** Definition: The hypergeometric function ₂F₁: solutions of the Gauss hypergeometric equation. Conjecture: Kummer's twenty-four solutions of hypergeometrics: the connection matrices of the solution system are explicit matrix elements of the monodromy group, with rationality of the elements parametrized by the Schwarz table. (Kummer 24 solutions connection matrices)
