**English.** Definition: Transparent boundaries as fully transmitting artificial boundaries. Conjecture: The realization spectrum of transparent boundaries is finitely many exact transmission operators; and the construction of the spectrum is a Pade approximation sequence. (Pade-realized transparent boundary spectrum)
