# 00000002062

**UA-12 △** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Conjecture: There exists a locally finite variety whose prime-order parts realize any prescribed residue-class spectrum (i.e. for any subset of primes, one can realize exactly that spectrum); the construction is exhausted by subquotients of automorphism varieties of affine spaces.

**中文。** 猜想：存在局部有限簇,其成员的素数阶部分为任意预设的剩余类谱(即给定任何素数子集,可实现恰在此谱上取值);构造由仿射空间的自同构簇的子商穷举。
