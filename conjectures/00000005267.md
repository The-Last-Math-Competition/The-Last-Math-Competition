**English.** Definition: The minimal regularity is the boundary smoothness guaranteeing well-posedness. Conjecture: Its conversion constant is an explicit reciprocal of the boundary arclength, equality at circular sheets, compatible with the Lipschitz deficit of the transport velocity.
