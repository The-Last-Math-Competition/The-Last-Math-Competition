**English.** Definition: The model checking of monadic second-order logic is its sentence decision problem. Conjecture: The linear-time lower bound on trees is given by an explicit rank-three sentence family that forces any algorithm to read all nodes, and the evidence is of information-theoretic type. (linear lower-bound evidence of monadic second-order tree checking)
