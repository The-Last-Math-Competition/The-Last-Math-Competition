**English.** Definition: periodic orbit distribution: closed geodesic measure converges to volume (Duke–Rudnick–Sarnak theorem). Conjecture: quantity limit of sequence of rigidity: periodic orbit measure of quantity limit of period measure and classical limit and error of is explicit logarithm power; error of volume by power controls of spectral gap. (period quantity limit rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：周期轨道等分布：闭测地线测度收敛到体积（Duke–Rudnick–Sarnak 定理）。猜想：周期测度的量子极限的刚性：周期轨道测度提升的序列的量子极限与经典极限重合且重合的误差为体积的显式对数幂；误差由谱隙的幂控制。（周期量子极限刚性）
