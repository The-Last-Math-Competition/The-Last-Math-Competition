# 00000009956

**English.** Definition: Equidistribution of periodic orbits: closed geodesic measures converge to volume (the Duke-Rudnick-Sarnak theorem). Conjecture: Rigidity of quantum limits of periodic measures: the quantum limits of sequences of lifted periodic-orbit measures coincide with classical limits, with coincidence error an explicit logarithmic power of the volume; the error is controlled by a power of the spectral gap. (periodic quantum limit rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：周期轨道等分布：闭测地线测度收敛到体积（Duke–Rudnick–Sarnak 定理）。猜想：周期测度的量子极限的刚性：周期轨道测度提升的序列的量子极限与经典极限重合且重合的误差为体积的显式对数幂；误差由谱隙的幂控制。（周期量子极限刚性）
