# 00000001847

**English.** Definition: The spiked model is a Wishart matrix plus a rank-2 perturbation. Conjecture: The joint fluctuations of the two outlying eigenvalues of a rank-2 spike follow a two-dimensional generalization of the TW distribution (with correlation coefficient −1/2); each marginal is TW-β.

**中文。** 定义：spiked 模型指 Wishart 矩阵加秩 2 扰动。猜想：rank-2 spike 的两个外部特征值的联合涨落为二维推广 TW 分布(相关系数为 −1/2);边缘各为 TW-β。
