# 00000008311

**FLO-21 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The Nielsen classes of symplectic fixed points: the minimal representative count nielsen_min of Nielsen equivalence classes of fixed points. Conjecture: nielsen_min (essential Nielsen classes of fixed points of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms) is at least the cup length (a Nielsen lower bound law); the essentiality of classes (criterion for nonempty classes) is given by nonzero carriers of Floer cohomology (a carrier criterion law); and the agreement of the Nielsen number with cup length (the equality domain) is monotone manifolds (a monotone equality domain law), the smallest inequality example being the non-monotone Enriques surface (an Enriques example). (Nielsen cup length monotone Enriques exception)

**中文。** 定义：辛不动点的 Nielsen 类：不动点的 Nielsen 等价类的极小代表数 nielsen_min。猜想：nielsen_min（Hamilton 微分同胚的不动点的本质 Nielsen 类）≥ cup 长度（Nielsen 下界律）；且类的本质性（非空类的判据）由 Floer 上同调的非零承载（承载判据律）；Nielsen 数与 cup 长度的一致（等式域）为单调流形（单调等式域律），等式的例外（不等）的最小例为非单调的 Enriques（Enriques 例）。
