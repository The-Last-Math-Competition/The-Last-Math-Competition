# 00000000991

**OA-91 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The quantum expansion coefficient is the spectral dilation of quantum channels in the worst average case. Conjecture: No universal gap separates quantum from classical expansion: for every ε > 0 there is a graph family where quantum expansion exceeds classical expansion by at most ε; the construction diagonalizes quantum noise, and in the 3-regular case the quantum advantage decays as 2^{-n}.

**中文。** 定义：量子膨胀系数指量子传输在最大平均情形下的谱扩张。猜想：量子膨胀与经典膨胀不存在普适间隙:对一切 ε>0,存在图族使量子膨胀超出经典膨胀不超过 ε;构造由量子噪声对角化给出,且 3-正则情形量子优势 ≤ 2^{-n} 衰减。
