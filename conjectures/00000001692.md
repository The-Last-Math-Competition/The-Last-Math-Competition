# 00000001692

**English.** Definition: The multiple recurrence average with linear shifts E_{n<N}f₁(a₁n+h₁n)f₂(a₂n+h₂n). Conjecture: For pairwise distinct (a_i,h_i) in general position, the average converges to the multiple term μ(A₀ ∩ T^{h₁}A₁ ∩ T^{h₂}A₂); when (a_i,h_i) fall into a resonance hyperplane, the limit contains a prime local correction factor β_∞.

**中文。** 定义：线性位移多重递归平均 E_{n<N}f₁(a₁n+h₁n)f₂(a₂n+h₂n)。猜想：对互不相同的一般位置 (a_i,h_i),该平均收敛于 μ(A₀∩T^{h₁}A₁∩T^{h₂}A₂) 的多重项;当 (a_i,h_i) 落入共振超平面时,极限含素数局部修正因子 β_∞。
