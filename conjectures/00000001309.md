# 00000001309

**English.** Definition: The Carathéodory metric c_Ω. Conjecture: There exists a domain where the infimum of the ratio of c_Ω to the Kobayashi metric is 0 while the quotient domain's automorphism group is nontrivial (an explicit separation of the two metrics).

**中文。** 定义：Carathéodory 度量 c_Ω。猜想：存在域使 c_Ω 与 Kobayashi 度量的比值的下确界为 0 而商域自同构群非平凡(两度量的显式分离)。
