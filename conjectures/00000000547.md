# 00000000547

**CA-47 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The graded dimensions of a filtered complex are alternating sums of graded Bettis. Conjecture: Unimodality of graded dimensions is equivalent to the Lefschetz property of the dual complex; and the equivalence is preserved in the bigraded case. (unimodal-Lefschetz equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：过滤复形的分次维数为分次 Betti 的交替和。猜想：分次维数的单峰性等价于对偶复形的 Lefschetz 性质；且等价性在二分次时保持。（单峰-Lefschetz 等价）
