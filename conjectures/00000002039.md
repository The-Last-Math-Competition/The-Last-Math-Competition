# 00000002039

**English.** Definition: An MSTD set (More Sums Than Differences) satisfies |A+A| > |A−A|. Conjecture: The proportion of MSTD sets among uniform random subsets of [n] converges to an explicit limit α (α ∈ (10^{-4}, 1)); α is given by the integral of a limiting density function (computable).

**中文。** 定义：MSTD 集(More Sums Than Differences)指 |A+A|>|A−A| 的集合。猜想：[n] 的均匀随机子集中 MSTD 的比例收敛于显式极限 α(α∈(10^{-4},1));α 由极限密度函数的积分给出(可计算)。
