**FM2-03 ★** — source: 153_有限模型论.md

**English.** Definition: Compact separation is the separation property of structures realized by polynomial-size sentences. Conjecture: On three-color-type combinatorial structures there exist compact separating sentences of inflationary fixed-point logic with constant quantifier number; the length of the separating sentences is at most the diameter plus a constant; and each layer of the separation hierarchy is explicitly indexed by the local covering number, strictly increasing. (three-color compact separation sentences of fixed points)

**中文。** 定义：紧分离：由多项式规模句子实现的结构分离性质。猜想：三色型组合结构上存在膨胀不动点逻辑的紧分离句子且量词数为常数；分离所需句子长度不超过结构直径加常数；分离谱系每层由局部覆盖数显式索引且索引严格增。（不动点三色紧分离句子）
