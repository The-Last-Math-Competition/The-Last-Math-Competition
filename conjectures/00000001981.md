# 00000001981

**MP-21 ★** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: qLDPC codes are quantum error-correcting codes with low-density parity checks. Conjecture: There exists a polynomial-time decoder for asymptotically good qLDPC codes (correcting a linear number of errors); the algorithm is constructed explicitly from the embedded structure of expander graphs.

**中文。** 定义：qLDPC 码指低密度校验的量子纠错码。猜想：存在渐近优良的 qLDPC 码的多项式时间译码器(可纠正线性数量的错误);算法由膨胀图的嵌入结构显式构造。
