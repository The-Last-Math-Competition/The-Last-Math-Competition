**F3-02 ★** — source: 82_有限域上的几何III.md

**English.** Definition: k-arcs: point sets with no three collinear, with maximal arcs of order q+2 (hyperovals). Conjecture: The equivalence classification of hyperovals: for q an even power of 2 the number of equivalence classes of hyperovals is finite with logarithmic-order asymptotics in q; completeness of the classification is given by the closure of monomial types. (hyperoval equivalence class asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：k-弧：无三点共线的点集，最大弧的阶为 q+2（hyperoval）。猜想：hyperoval 的等价分类：q 为 2 的偶次幂时 hyperoval 的等价类为有限个且类数的渐近为 q 的对数阶；分类的完全性由单项式型的封闭性给出。（hyperoval 等价类渐近）
