# 00000009879

**English.** Definition: Gibbs property of KPZ line ensembles: Brownian-bridge conditioning of noncrossing line ensembles (proven by Corwin-Hammond). Conjecture: Height line ensembles of nonintegrable models converge to a universal line ensemble satisfying spatial local Gibbs properties; the interaction potential of the Gibbs property is uniquely determined by the Gaussian curvature functional of the microscopic model. (line ensemble functional Gibbs limit)

**中文。** 定义：KPZ 线系综的 Gibbs 性：非交叉线系的布朗桥条件（Corwin–Hammond 已证）。猜想：非可积模型的高度线系综收敛到满足空间局部 Gibbs 性的普适线系综；Gibbs 性的交互势由微观模型的高斯曲率泛函唯一决定。（线系综泛函 Gibbs 极限）
