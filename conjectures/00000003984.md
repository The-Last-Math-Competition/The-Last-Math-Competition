# 00000003984

**PC2-14 ★** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: A pseudodistribution is a fake expectation functional compatible with a given SOS degree. Conjecture: The local spectral gap condition is universally sufficient for the existence of degree-d pseudodistributions, and on random regular graphs it yields pseudodistributions of degree d <= n^{1/2-epsilon}, covering every known explicit graph family. (universality of pseudodistribution spectral gaps)

**中文。** 定义：伪分布指与给定 SOS 度相容的假期望泛函。猜想：局部谱间隙条件对度 d 伪分布的存在普遍充分,且在随机正则图上给出 d ≤ n^{1/2−ε} 的伪分布,覆盖一切已知的显式图族。（伪分布谱间隙普适性）
