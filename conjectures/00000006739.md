# 00000006739

**English.** Definition: The leg decomposition is the direct-sum stratification of tilting modules. Conjecture: The explicit bound of the leg count is tight in the hereditary case and the bound is complete; the combination of tightness is the arm-length sum; and the combinatorial structure of the bound is decided by the maximal length of partial arms. (hereditary tight bound of leg decompositions)

**中文。** 定义：腿分解为倾斜模的直和分层。猜想：腿数的显式界在遗传情形紧且界完全；且紧的组合为臂长和；且界的组合结构由偏臂的极大长度决定。（腿分解遗传紧界）
