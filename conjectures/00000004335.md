# 00000004335

**English.** Definition: The Feynman path integral is the formal oscillatory-phase functional integral, and the Stokes constants are the jump coefficients of its analytic continuation. Conjecture: The Stokes constants are algebraic numbers satisfying global resurgence relations, and the universal criterion is the isolation and nondegeneracy of the critical points. (universal algebraicity criterion of Feynman Stokes data)

**中文。** 定义：Feynman 路径积分指形式振荡相位泛函积分,Stokes 常数指其解析延拓的跳变系数。猜想：Stokes 常数为代数数且满足 resurgence 整体关系,普适判据为临界点的孤立与非退化性。（Feynman Stokes 代数普适判据）
