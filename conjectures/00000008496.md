# 00000008496

**English.** Definition: The convergence rate of the annealed central limit theorem for optimization in random environments (the Bolthausen self-normalized bound). Conjecture: The optimal convergence rate of the annealed CLT is n^{-1/2} (a square-root law); the difference between quenched and annealed limiting variances is always positive (a positive difference law); the explicit form of the difference is twice the correlation length of the environment (an explicit correlation length law); and the unique case of zero difference is the independent environment (an independent zero-difference law). (annealed CLT correlation length independence)

**中文。** 定义：随机环境优化的退火中心极限定理（Bolthausen 自归一化界）的收敛速率。猜想：退火 CLT 的最优收敛速率为 n^{-1/2}（平方根律）；淬火与退火极限方差之差恒正（正差律）；该差的显式为环境相关长度的两倍（相关长度显式律）；零差的唯一情形为独立环境（独立零差律）。
