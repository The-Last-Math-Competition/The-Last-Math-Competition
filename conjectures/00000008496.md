**ERO-26 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: convergent rate of central limit theorem (Bolthausen self- normalization bound) of random environment optimization. Conjecture: optimal convergent rate of CLT is n^{-1/2} (square root); and limit variance difference always explicit of positive (positive difference); the difference is unique of two times (correlation length explicit); zero difference of environment correlation length is independent environment (independent zero difference).

**中文。** 定义：随机环境优化的退火中心极限定理（Bolthausen 自归一化界）的收敛速率。猜想：退火 CLT 的最优收敛速率为 n^{-1/2}（平方根律）；淬火与退火极限方差之差恒正（正差律）；该差的显式为环境相关长度的两倍（相关长度显式律）；零差的唯一情形为独立环境（独立零差律）。
