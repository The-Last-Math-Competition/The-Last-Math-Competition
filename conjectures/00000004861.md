**English.** Definition: The boundary measure dimension is the dimension of the Patterson measure. Conjecture: The Hausdorff dimension spectrum of the boundaries of hyperbolic groups is dense in the positive real axis, and the quotient of the spectrum modulo measure equivalence is a finite explicit set. (density and finite quotient of the boundary dimension spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：边界测度维数指帕特森测度的维数。猜想：双曲群边界的豪斯多夫维数谱在正实轴稠密,谱模测度等价的商为有限显式集。（边界维数谱稠密与有限商）
