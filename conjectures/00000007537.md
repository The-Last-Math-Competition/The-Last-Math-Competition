**English.** Definition: The Tate conjecture as the equality framework of weights and algebraic cycles. Conjecture: The weight domain of the equality is located at codimension one by Kummer systems; and the location of higher-weight domains is the polarization endomorphism. (Tate-equality weight-domain location)
