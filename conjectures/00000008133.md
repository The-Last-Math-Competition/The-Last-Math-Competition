# 00000008133

**NAG-23 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Chan-Ingalls equivalence of minimal orders of noncommutative varieties: the equivalence class CI_nc of affine posets of valuations of the nc function field. Conjecture: The complete invariant of CI equivalence (poset equivalence) is the divisor lattice of the central function field (a lattice completeness law); the transitivity of the Cohen-Macaulay property under localization of nc valuations (CM transitivity) holds at rank at most 2 (a rank-2 CM transitivity law); and the minimal rank-3 exception to transitivity (non-CM localization) is the exceptional system at the first infinitely near point of an exceptional curve (an infinitely-near exception law). (Chan-Ingalls poset lattice CM transitivity)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的极小 OK 的 Chan–Ingalls 等价：nc 函数域的 valuations 的仿射有序集的等价类 CI_nc。猜想：CI 等价（有序集等价）的完备不变量为中心函数域的 divisor 格（格完备律）；且 nc valuations 的局部化的 Cohen–Macaulay 性的传递（CM 传递）在秩 ≤2 成立（2-秩 CM 传递律）；传递的例外（非 CM 局部化）的最小秩 3 的例子为 exceptional curve 的第一无穷近点的例外系（无穷近例外律）。
