**F3-03 ★** — source: 82_有限域上的几何III.md

**English.** Definition: Generalized quadrangles (GQ): rank-2 incidence geometries satisfying the quadrangle axioms. Conjecture: The strengthening of the Feit–Higman parameter constraints for GQ: a gap theorem for the feasible domain of parameter pairs, with the complete gap list finite for st below one thousand; the strengthening path is the integralization of spectral constraints. (GQ parameter gap theorem)

**中文。** 定义：广义四边形（GQ）：点线关联满足四边形公理的秩 2 几何。猜想：GQ 的 Feit–Higman 参数约束的加强：参数对的可行域的间隙定理且间隙的完全列表在 st 小于一千有限；加强的路径为谱约束的整数化。（GQ 参数间隙定理）
