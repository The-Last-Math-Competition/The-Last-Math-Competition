**English.** Definition: Thickness is the stable structure of set intersections. Conjecture: The product threshold of thicknesses is nonempty when the gap product is below one; the threshold is always one after normalization; and the normalization constant is the maximal thickness quotient. (normalized nonempty threshold of thickness products)
