# 00000007162

**English.** Definition: Critical points of the eigenvalue map with the variation of extrema. Conjecture: The critical points of convex spectral functions are normal matrices, and the minimizer is the eigenvalue ordering. (normal-critical convex spectral rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：特征值映射的临界点：极值的变分。猜想：谱函数的刚性：凸谱函数的临界为正规矩阵且阵的极小为特征值的排序。（凸谱函数正规临界特征值排序极小刚性）
