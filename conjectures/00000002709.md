# 00000002709

**English.** Definition: Zariski factorization and dynamical degrees of maps. Conjecture: The limit of the dynamical degree sequence ν(f^n) exists, and it is an algebraic irrational only in the scattered class of non-invertible self-maps; the algebraic degree of the limit is bounded by the Picard number. (dynamical degree limit rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 因子分解与映射的动力学次数。猜想：动力学次数序列 ν(f^n) 的极限存在且极限为代数无理数只出现在不可逆自映射的散点类；极限的代数次数上界为 Picard 数。（动力学次数极限刚性）
