# 00000007686

**QDE-26 ◆** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: The p-adic continuity of the partition function p(n) is the recursive periodicity of p(n) mod p^a; the q-analogue p_q(n) is defined as the coefficient sum of the Gaussian polynomial [2n choose n]_q. Conjecture: The sequence p_q(n) in N[q]/(q-1)^k is eventually periodic with minimal period an explicit function of p^a k; in particular k=1 reduces to the classical period divided by p-1. (q-analogue partition periodicity)

**中文。** 定义：分拆函数 p(n) 的 p-进连续性指 p(n) 模 p^a 满足的递推周期性。q-模拟 p_q(n) 定义为高斯多项式 [2n choose n]_q 的系数和。猜想：p_q(n) mod (q-1)^k 意义下（即在 N[q]/(q-1)^k 中）的序列最终周期，且最小周期为 p^a k 的显式函数；特别 k=1 时退化为 p(n) mod p 的经典周期除以 (p-1)。
