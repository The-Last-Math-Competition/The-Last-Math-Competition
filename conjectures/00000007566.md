**English.** Definition: The tower-convergence law of inclusion formulas. Conjecture: The convergence spectral threshold of towers is explicit; the criterion of the threshold is index divergence; and the criterion of index divergence is unbounded spectral radius. (index-divergence tower-convergence threshold)

**中文。** 定义：包含公式的塔收敛律。猜想：塔收敛的谱阈显式；且阈的判据为指标发散；且指标发散的判据为谱半径无界。（塔收敛指标发散阈）
