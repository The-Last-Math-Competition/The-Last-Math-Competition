**OL-12 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: Continuous lattices and the Scott topology: directedly complete lattices defined via the way-below relation. Conjecture: Lawson compactness of continuous lattices has a spectral characterization: Lawson-compact if and only if the spectrum is stably compact; the proof path is a directed refinement of the Hofmann–Mislove theorem. (Lawson compact spectral characterization)

**中文。** 定义：连续格与 Scott 拓扑：way-below 关系定义的定向完备格。猜想：连续格的 Lawson 紧性有谱刻画：Lawson 紧当且仅当谱空间为 stably 紧；刻画的证明路径为 Hofmann–Mislove 定理的定向细化。（Lawson 紧谱刻画）
