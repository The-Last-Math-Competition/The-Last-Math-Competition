# 00000005371

**English.** Definition: Arithmetic lattices are lattices constructed from number fields. Conjecture: The minimal trace-field discriminant table in dimension three is an explicit list of imaginary quadratic fields, the first row is the minimal-discriminant field, and the ordering is complete. (three-dimensional minimal trace-field discriminant list)

**中文。** 定义：算术格指由数域构作的格。猜想：三维的最小迹域判别式表为虚二次域的显式列表,首行为最小判别式域且排序完全。（三维最小迹域判别式列表）
