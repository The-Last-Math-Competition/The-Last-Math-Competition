# 00000002193

**English.** Definition: A random poset is the intersection of uniformly random linear orders. Conjecture: The dimension of a random poset always concentrates on two or three; and the switching threshold from two to three is given explicitly by a density parameter. (random poset dimension switching threshold)

**中文。** 定义：随机偏序指均匀线性序对的交。猜想：随机偏序的维数恒集中于二或三；且从二到三的切换阈值由密度参数显式给出。（随机偏序维数切换阈值）
