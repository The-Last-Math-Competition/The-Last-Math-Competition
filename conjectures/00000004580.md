**English.** Definition: The Bers boundary dimension and the volume spectrum are two hyperbolic invariants. Conjecture: There exist two hyperbolic three-manifolds with the same boundary fractal dimension but different volume spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of Dehn surgeries. (Bers dimension-volume spectrum separation)

**中文。** 定义：Bers 边界维数与体积谱指两个双曲不变量。猜想：存在两个双曲三维流形的边界分形维数相同而体积谱不同,分离由显式 Dehn 手术对实现。（Bers 维数-体积谱分离）
