**HG3-02 ★** — source: 147_几何群论III_双曲群.md

**English.** Definition: The squared Betti numbers are the homology dimensions with square coefficients. Conjecture: The squared Betti numbers of a hyperbolic group vanish outside the middle dimension, and the middle value coincides with the boundary dimension plus one. (middle-dimensional concentration of hyperbolic squared Betti numbers)

**中文。** 定义：平方贝蒂数指作用的同调维数。猜想：双曲群的平方贝蒂数恒除中维外全部消没；且中维值与边界维数加一吻合。（双曲群平方贝蒂数中维集中）
