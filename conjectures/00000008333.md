# 00000008333

**DIA-13 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic of the Markov spectrum of quadratic forms: the distribution mark_dist of Markov numbers (solutions of x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 3xyz). Conjecture: mark_dist (the distribution density of Markov numbers) grows as c x^{1/2} (a half-exponential growth law); the density of the prime factor spectrum (distribution of prime Markov numbers) is log-log sparse (a log-log sparsity law); the completeness of the Markov tree (Cohn tree) branching (Fricke iteration) — the tree covers all Markov numbers — strengthens to unique representation (Frobenius uniqueness, a Frobenius uniqueness law); and the stationary distribution of random walks on the tree (random generation of Markov numbers) has the 1/3-2/3 bias (a biased stationarity law). (Markov half-exponential Frobenius biased walk)

**中文。** 定义：二次型的 Markov 谱的算术：Markov 数（x²+y²+z²=3xyz 的解）的分布 mark_dist。猜想：mark_dist（Markov 数的分布密度）~ c·x^{1/2} 的增长（半指数增长律）；且 Markov 数的素因子谱（素 Markov 数的分布）的密度为 log-log 型稀疏（log-log 稀疏律）；Markov 树（Cohn 树）的分支（Fricke 的迭代）的完全性（树覆盖一切 Markov 数）的已证的强化（唯一表示）为 Frobenius 唯一性（Frobenius 唯一律）；树的随机游走（Markov 数的随机生成）的分布的稳态为 1/3-2/3 的偏置（偏置稳态律）。
