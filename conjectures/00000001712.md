# 00000001712

**EG-22 ★** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: The equidistribution rate of a nilsequence is the deviation decay of its partial sums under Diophantine conditions. Conjecture: The deviation is at most C·N^{−1/k+ε} with k the order of the nilflow; 1/k is the optimal exponent, given explicitly by the convergent denominators of the Diophantine-type vector of that order.

**中文。** 定义：nil-序列的等分布率指其部分和在 Diophantine 条件下的偏差衰减。猜想：偏差 ≤ C·N^{−1/k+ε},k 为 nil-流的阶;1/k 为最优指数,由该阶 Diophantine 型的向量收敛分母显式。
