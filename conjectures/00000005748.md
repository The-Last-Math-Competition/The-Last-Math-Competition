# 00000005748

**English.** Definition: The etale fundamental group is the topological group after removing several points. Conjecture: The rank formula at genus two is the explicit value of the tame bound, given by counting the section genus and ramification divisors, and matching the classical bound. (tame-bound rank formula at genus two)
