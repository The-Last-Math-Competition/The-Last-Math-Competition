# 00000008088

**English.** Definition: The fusion lifting of the stable elements theorem for Morava E-theory: the fusion invariants E_inv(F) of the action of nilpotent elements of pi_* E. Conjecture: The stable image of the E-theory cohomology H*(S;E) is exactly H*(F;E) (the precise fusion-system form of Hopkins-Kuhn-Ravenel stability); the order of the image of the Brauer group of the action of the fusion system on the Azumaya algebra of E-theory equals the block count of F (a Brauer-block counting law); and the prime factor spectrum of the ramification index of the Azumaya invariant is contained in {p,l} (a two-prime ramification rigidity). (HKR stable image Brauer block count)

**中文。** 定义：Morava E-理论的稳定元素定理的融合提升：π_*E 的幂零元素的行动的融合不变量 E_inv(F)。猜想：E-理论的上同调 H*(S;E) 的稳定像恰为 H*(F;E)（Hopkins–Kuhn–Ravenel 的稳定性的融合系统的精确形式）；且融合系统在 E-理论的 Azumaya 代数上的作用的 Brauer 群像的阶 = F 的块数（Brauer-块计数律）；Azumaya 不变量的分歧指数的素因子谱 ⊆ {p,ℓ}（双素分歧刚性）。
