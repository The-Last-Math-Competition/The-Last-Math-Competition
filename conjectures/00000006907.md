**English.** Definition: The projection dimension is the minimal number of projections in a decomposition. Conjecture: The size law is given explicitly by a degree polynomial and the completeness of the law is verified at small sizes; and the degree of the polynomial is a treewidth-type parameter. (size law of projection dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：投影维数为分解的极小投影数。猜想：尺寸律由度数多项式显式给出且律的完全性在小尺寸验证；且多项式的次数为树宽型参数。（投影维数尺寸律）
