**English.** Definition: orthogonal: f of distribution measure and g is PCF map, μ_f, μ_g of two element different is Berkovich distribution limit of its periodic point. Conjecture: μ_f and μ_g each other singular if and only if f and g without iteration; and in each other absolutely continuous under, Radon-Nikodym derivative is bounded analytic function, its bound by coefficient ratio of Böttcher coordinates of two map gives dimension of, part is 1.

**中文。** 定义：等分布测度的正交：f 与 g 为两个不同的 PCF 映射，μ_f、μ_g 为其预周期点的 Berkovich 等分布极限。猜想：μ_f 与 μ_g 互相奇异当且仅当 f 与 g 无公共迭代；且在互相绝对连续情形下，Radon-Nikodym 导数为有界解析函数，其界由两映射的 Böttcher 坐标的系数比给出，公共部分的维数为 1。
