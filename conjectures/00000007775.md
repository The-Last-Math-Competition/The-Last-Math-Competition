# 00000007775

**ADY-25 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Orthogonality of equidistribution measures: for two distinct PCF maps f, g with Berkovich equidistribution limits mu_f, mu_g of their preperiodic points. Conjecture: mu_f and mu_g are mutually singular if and only if f and g have no common iterate; and in the mutually absolutely continuous case the Radon-Nikodym derivative is a bounded analytic function with bound given by the ratio of Bottcher coefficients of the two maps, the common part having dimension 1. (singularity criterion of dynamical measures)

**中文。** 定义：等分布测度的正交：f 与 g 为两个不同的 PCF 映射，μ_f、μ_g 为其预周期点的 Berkovich 等分布极限。猜想：μ_f 与 μ_g 互相奇异当且仅当 f 与 g 无公共迭代；且在互相绝对连续情形下，Radon-Nikodym 导数为有界解析函数，其界由两映射的 Böttcher 坐标的系数比给出，公共部分的维数为 1。
