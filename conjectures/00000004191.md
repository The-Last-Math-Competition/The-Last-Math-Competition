# 00000004191

**GM3-11 ★** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: The Allen-Cahn equation is epsilon^2 Delta u + u - u^3 = 0, and a min-max solution is a critical solution of it. Conjecture: The Morse index of an AC min-max solution converges exactly to the corresponding min-max level p as epsilon tends to zero, with no additional jumps. (AC index-min-max level equality)

**中文。** 定义：Allen–Cahn 方程指 ε²Δu + u − u³ = 0,极小极大解指其临界解。猜想：AC 极小极大解的 Morse 指标在 ε 趋于零时恰收敛于对应极小极大水平 p,无额外跳跃。（AC 指标-极小极大水平等式）
