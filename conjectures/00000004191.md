# 00000004191

**GM3-11 ★** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Allen–Cahn equation denotes ε²Δu + u − u³ = 0, minimax solution denotes its critical solution. Conjecture: Morse index in ε tends to zero when exactly of AC minimax solution converges to corresponds to minimax p, without jump. (AC index - minimax identity)

**中文。** 定义：Allen–Cahn 方程指 ε²Δu + u − u³ = 0,极小极大解指其临界解。猜想：AC 极小极大解的 Morse 指标在 ε 趋于零时恰收敛于对应极小极大水平 p,无额外跳跃。（AC 指标-极小极大水平等式）
