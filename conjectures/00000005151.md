# 00000005151

**English.** Definition: A period domain is the classifying space of polarized pure Hodge structures, with its automorphism group. Conjecture: The automorphism group of the rank-three weight-two period domain is an explicit quotient of a real algebraic group given by the Hodge isometry group; the number of connected components of the quotient is at most 2; and the group table at low weight is finite and completely determined by outer automorphisms. (low-weight automorphism classification of period domains)

**中文。** 定义：周期域：极化纯 Hodge 结构的分类空间；其自同构群。猜想：秩三权二的周期域自同构群为实代数群的显式商且由 Hodge 等距群给出；商的连通分支数不超过 2；低权情形的群表为有限且由外自同构完全决定。（周期域低权自同构分类）
