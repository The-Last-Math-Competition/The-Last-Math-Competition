# 00000004433

**English.** Definition: An ascending Young tableau is the ascending-chain structure of standard tableaux. Conjecture: Its uniform random limit shape exists, is the envelope of an Euler-Lagrange solution of a variational problem, and the envelope is given by an explicit parametric curve. (variational envelope limit of ascending tableaux)

**中文。** 定义：上升 Young 图表指标准图表的升链结构。猜想：其均匀随机极限形状存在,为变分问题的 Euler–Lagrange 解的包络,包络由显式参数曲线给出。（上升图表变分包络极限）
