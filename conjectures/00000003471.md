# 00000003471

**English.** Definition: The Hedetniemi conjecture asserts that the tensor-product chromatic number equals the smaller chromatic number; Shitov gave counterexamples, but the fractional version remains open. Conjecture: Fractional Hedetniemi holds: the fractional tensor-product chromatic number equals the smaller fractional chromatic number; the minimal order of Shitov-type counterexamples in the fractional framework exceeds 2^{20}, with the proof path of nonexistence locked in the Xu inequality chain. (fractional tensor product chromatic number)

**中文。** 定义：Hedetniemi 猜想断言张量积色数等于较小色数，Shitov 已给反例；分数版本仍开放。猜想：分数 Hedetniemi 成立：分数张量积色数等于较小分数色数；分数框架下 Shitov 型反例的最小阶超过 2^{20} 且不存在性的证明路径锁定在 Xu 不等式链。（分数张量积色数）
