# 00000008030

**English.** Definition: The quasianalytic closure of Hardy fields: the maximal quasianalytic extension H^{qa} of a Hardy field H (the maximal domain preserving quasianalyticity). Conjecture: The extension H^{qa}/H is purely transcendental (no algebraic adjunctions); the maximal quasianalytic Hardy field exists (an application of Zorn) with uniqueness up to categorical equivalence; and the realization of the order structure (the order completion of H^{qa}) is given by fibers of the standard part map in a saturated model of the hyperreals (uniformity of fiber cardinality), the uniformity constant being exponential in the cardinality of the language. (Hardy quasianalytic purely transcendental closure)

**中文。** 定义：Hardy 场的拟解析闭包：Hardy 场 H 的极大拟解析扩张 H^{qa}（保持拟解析性的极大域）。猜想：H^{qa}/H 的扩张为纯超越（无代数添加）；且极大拟解析 Hardy 域的存在性（Zorn 应用）的唯一性在范畴等价下成立；扩张的序结构（H^{qa} 的序完备化）的实现为超实数域的饱和模型中的标准部分映射的纤维（纤维的基数一致），一致常数为语言基数的指数。
