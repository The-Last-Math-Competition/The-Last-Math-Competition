# 00000006605

**English.** Definition: The noncommutative spectrum is the generalized space of tensor ideals. Conjecture: The construction domain of the spectrum is given by representation dimensions and the domain is finite; the kernel of the domain is the splitting word length; and the bound of the splitting word length is the splitting dimension. (representation-dimension splitting-word domain)

**中文。** 定义：非交换谱为张量理想的推广空间。猜想：谱构造域由表示维数给出且域有限；且域的核为分裂字长；且分裂字长的界为分裂维数。（表示维数分裂字长域）
