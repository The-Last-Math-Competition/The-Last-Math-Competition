# 00000008568

**NSP-08 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: problem of spectral gap in point edge under of infinite graph. Conjecture: point such that spectral gap non- (point); unique exception is isolated point (point exception); edge spectral gap, minimal counterexample is spectrum explicit of tree (antitree) graph (tree); tree is interval and and (Cantor formula) of Cantor part.

**中文。** 定义：无限图的谱隙在删点删边下的单调性问题。猜想：删点使谱隙非增（删点单调律）；唯一例外为删除孤立点（孤点例外律）；删边可增谱隙，极小反例为反树（antitree）图（反树律）；反树的谱显式为区间与 Cantor 部分的并（Cantor 公式律）。
