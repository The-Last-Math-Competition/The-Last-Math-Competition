# 00000008568

**English.** Definition: The monotonicity of spectral gaps of infinite graphs under vertex and edge deletion. Conjecture: Deleting vertices never increases the spectral gap (a vertex deletion monotonicity law), the only exception being deletion of isolated vertices (an isolated vertex exception law); deleting edges can increase the gap, with the minimal counterexample being antitree graphs (an antitree law); and the spectrum of antitrees is explicitly the union of an interval and a Cantor part (a Cantor formula law). (infinite graph gap monotonicity antitree)

**中文。** 定义：无限图的谱隙在删点删边下的单调性问题。猜想：删点使谱隙非增（删点单调律）；唯一例外为删除孤立点（孤点例外律）；删边可增谱隙，极小反例为反树（antitree）图（反树律）；反树的谱显式为区间与 Cantor 部分的并（Cantor 公式律）。
