**English.** Definition: The quasiminimal covering of Zilber pseudo-exponential models: the bilinear form deg_Z of the commutative diagram G tensor G to G for the pregeometry closure operator cl of the pseudo-exponential field Z. Conjecture: The existential model of the pseudo-exponential field is unique in the categorical sense (categoricity of quasiminimality); the free part of the kernel of the pseudo-exponential (the exponential period group) is a rank-1 free module (kernel rigidity); and the cardinality of the model (the saturated model of the countable language) is aleph_1, with the torsion part of the kernel of every model a subgroup of the primary decomposition of the standard torsion group Q/Z (a torsion group classification). (pseudo-exponential quasiminimal categoricity)

**中文。** 定义：Zilber 伪指数模型的拟极小覆盖：伪指数域 Z 的预几何闭包算子 cl 的交换图表 G⊗G→G 的双线性型 deg_Z。猜想：伪指数域的存在性模型唯一的范畴范畴（拟极小性的范畴化）；且伪指数的核（指数周期群）的自由部分为一秩 1 自由模（核刚性）；模型的势（可数语言的饱和模型）为 ℵ₁，且一切模型的核的扭部分为标准扭群 Q/Z 的准素分解的子群（扭群分类）。
