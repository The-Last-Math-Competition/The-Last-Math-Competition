# 00000008662

**English.** Definition: Growth equivalence classes are asymptotic comparison classes of growth functions of two groups. Conjecture: The equivalence classes are uncountable with a countable representative system given by fundamental group families; the partial order of the system is dense with no adjacent pairs, and the dimension spectrum of gaps is strictly increasing. (growth equivalence dense spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：增长等价类指两群增长函数的渐近比较类。猜想：等价类为不可数且可数代表系由基本群系给出；系的偏序稠密而相邻对不存在，间隙的维数谱严格递增。（增长等价类稠密谱）
