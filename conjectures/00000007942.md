# 00000007942

**INT-12 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The rigidity of solutions of the reflection equation for integrable boundary conditions: the classification index b(G) of solutions K(u) of the reflection equation. Conjecture: For a simple Lie group G with symmetric space G/H of codimension-one H, the number of families of polynomial solutions K(u) is 2^{rank G - rank H}; every K solution has order (polynomial degree in u) at most 2; and order-2 solutions occur only when the isotropy representation of H contains a three-dimensional irreducible component (an SU(2) embedding), the occurrence condition corresponding one-to-one to the exceptional duality of the holomorphic classification of symmetric spaces. (reflection equation rank-difference family count)

**中文。** 定义：可积边界条件的反射方程的解的刚性：边界反射矩阵 K(u) 在反射方程下的解的分类指标 b(G)。猜想：对单李群 G（余边界 H 的对称空间 G/H），K(u) 的多项式解的族数为 2^{rank G - rank H}；且任意 K 解的阶（u 的多项式阶数）≤ 2；阶 2 的解仅在 H 的迷向表示含三维不可约分量（SU(2) 嵌入）时出现，出现条件与对称空间的全纯分类的例外对偶一一对应。
