# 00000004704

**English.** Definition: Integer polynomial recurrence is point recurrence along polynomial times. Conjecture: The recurrence density lower bound of cubic polynomials is an explicit combination of the degree and the difference-set density, optimal under the uniform distribution hypothesis. (density bound of cubic polynomial recurrence)

**中文。** 定义：整多项式回归指沿多项式时间的点回归。猜想：三次多项式的回归密度下界为次数与差集密度的显式组合,组合在均匀分布假设下最优。（三次多项式回归密度界）
