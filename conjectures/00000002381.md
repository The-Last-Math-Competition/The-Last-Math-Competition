**English.** Definition: The L^q spectrum is the generating function of Rényi dimensions. Conjecture: Under the open set condition, the derivative of the L^q spectrum at the box dimension is the entropy-to-dimension ratio; and the ratio is realized in the equal-weight case. (Lq spectrum derivative entropy-dimension ratio)

**中文。** 定义：L^q 谱为 Rényi 维数的母函数。猜想：开集条件下 L^q 谱在盒维数处的导数为熵除维数比；且比的实现为等权情形。（Lq 谱导数熵维比）
