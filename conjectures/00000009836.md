# 00000009836

**English.** Definition: Weighted pointwise bounds for p-Laplacians: weighted versions of Wolff potential estimates. Conjecture: The weight-modulated Wolff potential bound for p-Laplacian solutions holds with an explicit power of the weight constant; the power exponent returns to the classical at p = 2, and the constant remains finite at the endpoint p to infinity. (weighted Wolff potential)

**中文。** 定义：p-Laplace 的加权点态界：Wolff 位势估计的权版本。猜想：p-Laplace 解的权调制 Wolff 位势界成立且常数为权常数的显式幂；幂指数在 p=2 回到经典且端点 p→∞ 的常数有限。（加权 Wolff 位势）
