# 00000004559

**English.** Definition: a to the power b is the power with algebraic base and algebraic exponent. Conjecture: When a and b are both algebraic, a to the b and b to the a are algebraically independent if and only if an explicit condition holds on the irrationality of the ratio of the two logarithms, and the criterion is complete when ab is not one. (logarithm criterion of two-power algebraic independence)

**中文。** 定义：a 的 b 次幂指代数底与代数指数的幂。猜想：a 与 b 均为代数时 a 的 b 次幂与 b 的 a 次幂代数无关当且仅当两对数比无理的显式条件,判据在 ab 不为一时完全。（双幂代数无关对数判据）
