# 00000001534

**English.** Definition: The p-tower of THH of K(F_p) is the inverse-limit structure of its p-complete homotopy; the Amitsur complement is the associated cobar complex. Conjecture: There exists a v₂-periodic class in THH(K(F_p)) whose image in the Amitsur complement is an explicit nonzero detecting element; this detection transplants the proof template of the 1→2 redshift to 2→3.
