**English.** Definition: The activation-network landscape is the loss terrain with nonlinear activations. Conjecture: The explicit connectivity of minimal clusters is a single cluster for overparametrized two-layer networks, and the combination of the single cluster is the moduli-space connectivity. (moduli-space single-cluster connectivity of activation networks)
