# 00000002095

**English.** Definition: The HF rank of a local-system Lagrangian is the rank of its Floer cohomology. Conjecture: The HF rank of an incompressible Lagrangian of genus g is bounded below by 2g (tight); the bound is controlled by the dimension of the monotone homology of the local system.

**中文。** 定义：局部系拉格朗日的 HF 秩指 Floer 上同调的秩。猜想：亏格 g 的非可压缩拉格朗日的 HF 秩下界为 2g(紧);下界由局部系的单调同调的维数控制。
