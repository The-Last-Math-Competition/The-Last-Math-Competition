# 00000001967

**English.** Definition: Lee–Yang zeros are the zeros of the partition function in the complex-temperature plane. Conjecture: The angular density of the Lee–Yang zeros of the ferromagnetic Ising model near the critical point is θ^{−σ} with the explicit exponent σ of the (1/δ) type; the density is controlled by the bootstrap decay of correlation functions.

**中文。** 定义：Lee–Yang 零点指配分函数在温度复平面上的零点集。猜想：铁磁 Ising 的 Lee–Yang 零点在临界点附近的角密度为 θ^{-σ}(σ=(1/δ)型显式指数);密度由关联函数的 bootstrap 衰减控制。
