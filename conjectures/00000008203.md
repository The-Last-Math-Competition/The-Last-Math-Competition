# 00000008203

**English.** Definition: The limiting measure of infinitesimal characters of unitary representations: the density pl(G) of the Plancherel decomposition of the distribution measure mu_pi (orbit measure) of a representation pi. Conjecture: The support of pl (the Plancherel measure) on the coadjoint orbit space is exactly the set of integral orbits (an integral support law); the explicit density (the Harish-Chandra formula for semisimple groups) has correction term the Weyl denominator of root subsystems (a Weyl correction formula); and the p-adic analogue of Plancherel (Plancherel for p-adic groups) has constant c(G) explicit of type |W|^{-1} 2^{rank} (a p-adic constant formula). (Plancherel integral support Weyl correction)

**中文。** 定义：酉表示的无穷小特征标的极限测度：表示 π 的分布测度 μ_π（轨道测度）的 Plancherel 分解的密度 pl(G)。猜想：pl（Plancherel 测度）在余伴随轨道空间上的支撑 = 积分轨道的全体（积分支撑律）；且密度的显式（半单群的 Harish-Chandra 公式）的修正项为根子系统的 Weyl 分母（Weyl 修正公式）；Plancherel 的 p-进类比（p-进群的 Plancherel）的公式的常数 c(G) = |W|^{-1}·2^{rank} 型显式（p-进常数公式）。
