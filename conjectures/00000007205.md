**English.** Definition: Weil with the number-field side of geometry. Conjecture: The rationality of the zeta function is the l-adic-interchanged root of unity, and the weight of the root is the eigenvalue weight of Frobenius. (l-adic Weil zeta rationality)

**中文。** 定义：Weil。猜想：数域侧的几何。猜想：有理数的刚性：ζ 函数的有理性为 l-进交换单位根且根的权为 Frobenius 的特征值权。（Weil 有理 l-进交换单位根 ζ 刚性特征权）
