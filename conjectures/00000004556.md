**English.** Definition: Elliptic logarithms are the values of the inverse functions of elliptic integrals. Conjecture: The lower bound of their linear forms is an explicit exponential type of the height, in the complex multiplication case the exponent improves by half, and the improvement is exact and cannot be improved further. (halved CM exponent of elliptic logarithms)
