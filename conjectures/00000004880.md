# 00000004880

**English.** Definition: Equivalent systems and extraction complexity are two structural layers. Conjecture: There exist two mutually translatable formal systems such that the extraction complexity exponent of the same combinatorial theorem differs, and the separation is realized by an explicit translation between linear and logarithmic arithmetic. (equivalent system-extraction exponent separation)

**中文。** 定义：等价系统与提取复杂度指两层结构。猜想：存在两个可相互翻译的形式系统使同一组合定理的提取复杂度指数不同,分离由线性与对数算术的显式翻译实现。（等价系统-提取指数分离）
