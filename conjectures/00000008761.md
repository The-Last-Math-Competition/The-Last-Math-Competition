# 00000008761

**MKT-21 ★** — source: 277_代数K理论与MilnorK.md

**English.** Definition: The determinant map det sends GL(R) to the unit group of R, and SK_1 is its kernel. Conjecture: SK_1 vanishes when the reduced nilradical of a commutative ring is nilpotent-annihilated; and the minimal number of generators of noncommutative counterexamples is two. (SK_1 vanishing and two-generator counterexample)

**中文。** 定义：行列式映射 det 把 GL(R) 映到 R 的单位群，SK_1 为其核。猜想：SK_1 在交换环的约化幂零零化时消没；且非交换情形的反例最小生成数为二。（SK1 消没域与二生成反例）
