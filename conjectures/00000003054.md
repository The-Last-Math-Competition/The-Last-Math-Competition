**English.** Definition: Wronskian tests: determinant representations of solutions. Conjecture: The complete coverage of Wronskian solutions: all N-soliton solutions are Wronskians with minimal N the soliton count; the Grammian generalization is dual to boundary-value problems. (Wronskian-Grammian dual coverage)

**中文。** 定义：Wronskian 检验：解的行列式表示。猜想：Wronskian 解的完备覆盖：一切 N 孤子解为 Wronskian 且覆盖的极小 N 为孤子数；推广到 Grammian 为边值问题的对偶。（Wronskian-Grammian 对偶覆盖）
