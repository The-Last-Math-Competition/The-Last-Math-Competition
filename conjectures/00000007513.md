**English.** Definition: The decomposition-support structure of p-adic cohomology. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the support spectrum of the decomposition, and the support-independence criterion is necessary and sufficient; the kernel of the criterion is constant-sheaf splitting; the union of supports is the whole spectrum with zero redundancy. (support-independence conversion criterion)

**中文。** 定义：p 进上同调的分解支撑结构。猜想：换算常数为分解的支撑谱且支撑无关判据为充分必要；判据的核为常值层分裂；支撑的并集为整个谱且并的冗余为零。（支撑无关判据换算常数）
