# 00000007838

**MSE-28 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: combinatorial rigidity of realized point number) of realized R(H) (group H in general curve on of Weierstrass group of M_g. Conjecture: realized R(H) = Π_i (ν_i+1) - C(H), where ν_i of H is structure parameter, C(H) of group is coupling positive; and extreme value in ball group ({0,2,4,…}) of R(H) attains maximum of infinite (every point realized), finite realized is 2g-1, is exactly Weierstrass point number.

**中文。** 定义：M_g 的 Weierstrass 半群的实现度 R(H)（半群 H 在一般曲线上的实现点数）的组合刚性。猜想：H 的实现度 R(H) = Π_i (ν_i+1) - C(H)，其中 ν_i 为半群隙的结构参数，C(H) 为耦合修正；且 R(H) 的极值在超椭球星半群（{0,2,4,…}）达到无穷（每点实现），有限实现的极大值为 2g-1，恰为 Weierstrass 点数。
