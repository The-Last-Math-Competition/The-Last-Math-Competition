# 00000007838

**MSE-28 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The realization degree R(H) of a Weierstrass semigroup H on M_g: the number of points of a general curve at which H is realized. Conjecture: R(H) = product of (nu_i + 1) minus C(H), with nu_i the structural parameters of the semigroup gaps and C(H) a coupling correction; and the extremal value of R(H) is infinite at the hyperelliptic star semigroup {0,2,4,...} (realized at every point), while the maximum finite realization is 2g-1, exactly the number of Weierstrass points. (Weierstrass semigroup realization degree)

**中文。** 定义：M_g 的 Weierstrass 半群的实现度 R(H)（半群 H 在一般曲线上的实现点数）的组合刚性。猜想：H 的实现度 R(H) = Π_i (ν_i+1) - C(H)，其中 ν_i 为半群隙的结构参数，C(H) 为耦合修正；且 R(H) 的极值在超椭球星半群（{0,2,4,…}）达到无穷（每点实现），有限实现的极大值为 2g-1，恰为 Weierstrass 点数。
