# 00000001560

**English.** Definition: The thinnest lattice covering density θ₄ of R⁴ is the infimal volume ratio of lattice coverings by unit balls. Conjecture: θ₄ is given by the D₄* lattice, with value an explicit algebraic number; every other four-dimensional lattice has strictly larger covering density.

**中文。** 定义：ℝ⁴ 的最薄格覆盖密度 θ₄ 指单位球格覆盖的体积比下确界。猜想：θ₄ 由 D₄* 格给出,值为 θ₄ = (5^{2}·√5)/4·(1+… ) 的显式代数数;一切其他四维格覆盖密度严格更大。
