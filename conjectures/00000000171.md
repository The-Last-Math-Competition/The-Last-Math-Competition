# 00000000171

**English.** Definition: The Gauss–Kuzmin–Wirsing constant λ ≈ 0.3037 is the subleading eigenvalue of the continued-fraction transfer operator. Conjecture: The set of spectral conjugates of λ has empty intersection with the set of 2^{-k}-type combinations of the imaginary parts of the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function; the separation follows from spectral rigidity of the two operators, evidenced by the coprimality of their best simultaneous approximation exponents. (GKW–zeta spectral separation)

**中文。** 定义：Gauss–Kuzmin–Wirsing 常数 λ≈0.3037 为连分数转移算子的次大特征值。猜想：λ 的谱共轭点集与 Riemann ζ 非平凡零点虚部的 2^{-k} 型组合集的交为空集；分离性由两个算子的谱刚性分别给出，谱刚性的证据为共轭值的最佳同时逼近指数互素。（GKW-zeta 谱分离）
