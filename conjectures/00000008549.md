**English.** Definition: Maximal and minimal elements of a bounded lattice. Conjecture: The length of maximal chains in a graded lattice is constant; and the minimal example of a partition lattice with variable length is the diamond lattice. (constancy of maximal chain length)

**中文。** 定义：有界格的极大元与极小元。猜想：极大链的长度在有序格为常数；长度可变的分拆格的最小例为菱形格。（极大链长恒定性）
