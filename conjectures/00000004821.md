**English.** Definition: The Waldspurger bridge is the square identity between half-integral weight coefficients and twisted central L-values. Conjecture: The conversion at weight three halves is always normalized by the period of the Shimura correspondence, and the two-direction conversion constants are computable. (Waldspurger period normalization)

**中文。** 定义：Waldspurger 桥指半整权系数与扭转中心 L 值的平方恒等。猜想：权二分之三的换算恒由 Shimura 对应的周期归一；且双向换算常数可计算。（Waldspurger 周期归一）
