# 00000004233

**HT2-23 ★** — source: 126_组合代数拓扑_高阶树.md

**English.** Definition: The barcode of persistent homology is the set of life intervals of the homology of a filtered complex. Conjecture: The sum of the death times of the barcode is at least the weight of the shortest spanning tree, with equality attained for sphere triangulations in which trees and bars correspond one-to-one. (barcode-spanning tree weight inequality)

**中文。** 定义：持续同调的条形码指滤波复形同调的生存区间集。猜想：条形码死亡时间之和不小于最短生成树权,等号在树与条形码一一对应的球面剖分达到。（条形码-生成树权不等式）
