# 00000004606

**English.** Definition: The standard triple bubble is the minimal cluster with three chambers. Conjecture: In three-dimensional Euclidean space the standard triple bubble with equal volumes exists and is unique, its interfaces are constant-mean-curvature spherical patches, and the triple intersection angles are always one hundred twenty degrees. (existence and uniqueness of the equal-volume triple bubble)

**中文。** 定义：标准三泡指三腔室的极小泡团。猜想：三维欧氏空间中等分体积的标准三泡解存在唯一,界面为常平均曲率球片且三重交角恒为一百二十度。（三泡等分解存在唯一）
