# 00000004000

**English.** Definition: A proof system hierarchy is an infinite chain ordered by polynomial simulation. Conjecture: There exists a strict infinite chain whose adjacent separations are given by one unified explicit family of formulas, and every adjacent separation is superpolynomial. (strict infinite hierarchy chain)

**中文。** 定义：证明系统层级指按多项式模拟排列的无穷链。猜想：存在严格无穷链使相邻分离由统一的显式公式族给出,且每个相邻分离均为超多项式。（严格无穷层级链）
