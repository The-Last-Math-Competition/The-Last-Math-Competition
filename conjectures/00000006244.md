# 00000006244

**English.** Definition: Unboundedness is the weakened structure of the Grothendieck universe axiom, the size problem. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit weakening window of the size axiom and the axiom difference of weak universes is decidable; the window ends are given by reflection distances, the distance being measurable-cardinal differences; and the minimal weakening system is a conservative extension of ZFC, the conservativity given by the reflection principle. (reflection-distance window conversion of size weakening)

**中文。** 定义：无界：Grothendieck 宇宙公理的弱化结构（尺寸问题）。猜想：换算常数为尺寸公理的显式弱化窗且弱宇宙的公理差可判别；窗端由反射距离给出且距离为可测基数差；弱化的极小系统为 ZFC 的保守扩张且扩张的保守性由反射原理给出。（尺寸弱化反射距离窗换算）
