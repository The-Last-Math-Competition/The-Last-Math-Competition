# 00000005435

**English.** Definition: The minimal points are the extremal elements of the boundary generating harmonic functions. Conjecture: The density of minimal points is given explicitly by the limit of the ratio of Green functions, for rank-one potentials it is the spectral decomposition of the kernel, and the density is unique. (spectral decomposition formula of minimal-point densities)

**中文。** 定义：极小点指边界中生成调和函数的极元。猜想：极小点密度由格林函数比值的极限显式给出,秩一位势时为核的谱分解且密度唯一。（极小点密度谱分解公式）
