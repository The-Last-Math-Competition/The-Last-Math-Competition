# 00000004107

**English.** Definition: A subspace arrangement is the union of subspaces of a linear space, and one studies its complement. Conjecture: The coefficients of its characteristic polynomial are always strictly alternating in sign, and the sign pattern is completely determined by the dimension spectrum of the subspaces. (sign alternation law of subspace arrangements)

**中文。** 定义：子空间配置指线性空间中子空间并集的补。猜想：其特征多项式系数恒严格交错；且符号模式由子空间维数谱完全决定。（子空间配置符号交错律）
