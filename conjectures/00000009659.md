# 00000009659

**HSS-19 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The second-largest eigenvalue of random d-regular graphs: Friedman's theorem gives lambda_2 tending to 2 sqrt(d-1); its fluctuations are unknown. Conjecture: n^{2/3}(lambda_2 - 2 sqrt(d-1)) converges to an explicit limit distribution; the difference between this distribution and GOE Tracy-Widom is an explicit curvature-corrected kernel, and the fluctuation order n^{-1/6} is exact. (second eigenvalue fluctuation)

**中文。** 定义：随机 d-正则图的第二大特征值：Friedman 定理给出 λ_2 → 2√(d−1)；其波动未知。猜想：n^{2/3}(λ_2 − 2√(d−1)) 收敛到显式极限分布；该分布与 GOE 的 Tracy–Widom 之差为曲率修正的显式核，波动阶 n^{−1/6} 精确。（第二大特征值波动）
