**English.** Definition: the- two large eigenvalue: Friedman theorem of random d- regular graph gives λ_2 -> 2√(d−1); its fluctuation unknown. Conjecture: n^{2/3}(λ_2 − 2√(d−1)) converges to explicit limit distribution; the distribution and Tracy–Widom difference of GOE is explicit kernel, fluctuation order n^{−1/6} exact of curvature positive. (the- two large eigenvalue fluctuation)
