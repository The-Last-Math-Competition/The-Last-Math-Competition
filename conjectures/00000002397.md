**English.** Conjecture: A quantitative Marstrand theorem: the uniform bound on the density of projected measures in typical directions is O(t^{-(dim_H−α)}) (α the projection parameter); the projection-direction exponent is explicit.

**中文。** 猜想：Marstrand 定量版:典型方向的投影测度的密度的一致界为 O(t^{-(dim_H-α)})(α 为投影参数);投影方向的指数显式。
