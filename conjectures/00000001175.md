# 00000001175

**English.** Definition: The perfection of KR crystals is a graded condition of classical-crystal isomorphism. Conjecture: KR crystals are perfect for every affine type and every index; the remaining exceptions occur only at specific ranks of twisted affine types. (KR crystals completely perfect)

**中文。** 定义：KR 晶体的完美性为古典晶体同构的分次条件。猜想：KR 晶体对一切仿射型与一切指标均为完美晶体；且剩余例外仅出现在扭仿射型的特定秩。（KR 晶体完全完美）
