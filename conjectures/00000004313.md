**English.** Definition: NFS is the number field sieve, and L(1/3) is the subexponential complexity notation. Conjecture: The optimal polynomial selection of NFS attains the lower bound of the 1/3 power of the constant 64/9, and any further improvement must go beyond the sieve framework.
