# 00000001658

**English.** Conjecture: The count of derived-equivalence classes of skew group algebras under cyclic group actions is at most 2 (skew group derived-class counting); and the counting upper bound is given by the Morris algebra structure of Clifford algebras.

**中文。** 猜想：斜群代数的导出等价类的计数在循环群作用下 ≤ 2(斜群导出类计数)；且计数的上界由 Clifford 代数的 Morris 代数结构给出。
