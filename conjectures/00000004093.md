# 00000004093

**English.** Definition: The colored configuration space is the configuration space of labeled particles. Conjecture: For graphs the colored and uncolored Euler characteristics are converted through the chromatic polynomial, with values an explicit combination of the chromatic polynomial evaluated at -n and at -1. (colored-uncolored chromatic conversion formula)

**中文。** 定义：有色配置空间指粒子带标号的配置空间。猜想：图情形有色与无色 Euler 特征由染色多项式换算,取值为染色多项式在 −n 与 −1 处取值的显式组合。（有色-无色染色换算公式）
