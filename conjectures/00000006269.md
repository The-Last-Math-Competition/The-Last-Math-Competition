**English.** Definition: Fiber integration is the summation structure of fiber entropy, the fibration formula of entropy. Conjecture: The remainder of the sum is given by boundary entropy, the combination of the remainder being the boundary partition; the weight of the partition is the side-boundary count, nonnegative; and the remainder vanishes if and only if the fibration is isentropic. (boundary-entropy remainder structure of fiber integrals)
