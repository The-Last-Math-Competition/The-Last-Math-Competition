# 00000005555

**English.** Definition: Crystalline cohomology is the structure of infinitesimal invariants. Conjecture: The realization of modules is uniquely given by matching of the cotangent spectrum, realized by decomposition for smooth varieties, and the slope is always preserved. (cotangent-spectrum matching of crystalline cohomology)

**中文。** 定义：结晶上同调指无穷小不变量的结构。猜想：模的实理由余切谱的匹配唯一给出；光滑簇时由分解实现且斜率恒保持。（结晶余切谱匹配）
