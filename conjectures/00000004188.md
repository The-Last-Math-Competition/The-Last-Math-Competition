**GM3-08 ★** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: A bumpy metric is one whose minimal surfaces admit no degenerate Jacobi fields. Conjecture: The min-max surfaces of a bumpy metric give a family of pairwise disjoint embedded minimal surfaces, the family cardinality within each isotopy class is finite and equals the count of the width spectrum of that class. (counting disjoint families for bumpy metrics)

**中文。** 定义：bumpy 度量指极小曲面无退化 Jacobi 场的度量。猜想：bumpy 度量的极小极大曲面给出两两不交的嵌入极小曲面族,同痕类内的族基数有限且等于该类宽度谱的计数。（bumpy 不交族计数）
