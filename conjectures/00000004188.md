**English.** Definition: bumpy metric denotes metric of minimal surface without degenerate Jacobi. Conjecture: minimax surface of bumpy metric gives family cardinality finite of embedding minimal surface family, isotopy class in of two two not intersection and equals counting of the class width spectrum. (bumpy not intersection family counting)
