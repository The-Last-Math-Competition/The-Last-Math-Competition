# 00000003911

**English.** Definition: Hamiltonian Floer homology HF is the Morse homology of the action functional on a closed symplectic manifold, and the PSS isomorphism is its canonical isomorphism with quantum homology. Conjecture: The modulus of continuity of the PSS isomorphism is independent of the Hamiltonian H and is uniformly controlled by one explicit constant depending only on the symplectic diameter of the manifold. (uniform PSS modulus of continuity)

**中文。** 定义：Hamilton Floer 同调 HF 指闭辛流形上作用泛函的 Morse 同调,PSS 同构指其与量子同调的典范同构。猜想：PSS 同构的连续模与 Hamilton 量 H 无关,仅由流形的辛直径的一个显式常数一致控制。（PSS 一致连续模）
