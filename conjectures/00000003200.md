**English.** Definition: Dimension theory: Krull dimension and Noether normalization. Conjecture: The linear-algebraic computation of dimension: the maximal length of regular sequences equals the depth of the homogeneous ideal, with the evaluation of depth the Ext vanishing layer. (Noether normalization Ext evaluation)
