# 00000003925

**English.** Definition: The closed-open map CO is the ring homomorphism from quantum cohomology to the Hochschild cohomology of the Fukaya category. Conjecture: CO is injective whenever a rational Lagrangian exists, and the image of CO generates the entire Hochschild cohomology if and only if quantum cohomology is semisimple. (closed-open injection and semisimple surjective image)

**中文。** 定义：闭开映射 CO 指量子同调到 Fukaya 范畴的 Hochschild 上同调的环同态。猜想：存在有理 Lagrange 时 CO 单射;且 CO 的像生成整个 Hochschild 上同调当且仅当量子同调半单。（闭开单射-半单满像）
