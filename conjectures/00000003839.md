# 00000003839

**English.** Definition: The dual canonical basis denotes the image of the canonical basis in the dual quantum group. Conjecture: In type A_n, leading coefficients of dual canonical basis elements in string coordinates are always 1; the smallest finite type with a coefficient other than 1 is B₂, and its minimal non-1 coefficient is 2. (dual basis leading coefficient)

**中文。** 定义：对偶典范基指典范基在对偶量子群中的像。猜想：型 A_n 的对偶典范基元素在字符串参数坐标下的首项系数恒为 1;出现系数非 1 的最小有限型为 B₂,且其非 1 系数的最小值为 2。（对偶基首项系数）
