# 00000002365

**English.** Definition: Kato stability is the perturbation bound for fractional powers. Conjecture: The stability threshold is one half; and the perturbation constant is of Lipschitz type below the threshold. (Kato stability threshold one half)

**中文。** 定义：Kato 平稳性为分数幂的扰动界。猜想：平稳的阈值为二分之一；且扰动常数在阈值下为 Lipschitz 型。（Kato 平稳阈值半）
