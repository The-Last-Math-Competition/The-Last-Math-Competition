**English.** Definition: martingale approximation denotes discrete martingale sequence approximation. Conjecture: RDE solution limit of two item martingale approximation if and only if convergent of approximation not preserves the- two sheaf Lévy area. (martingale limit - area)

**中文。** 定义：鞅逼近指以离散鞅序列逼近粗糙驱动。猜想：两条鞅逼近驱动的 RDE 解极限相异当且仅当逼近不保持第二层 Lévy 面积的收敛。（鞅极限-面积判据）
