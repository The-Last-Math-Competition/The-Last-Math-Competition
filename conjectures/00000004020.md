# 00000004020

**RP2-20 △** — source: 119_随机分析II_粗路径.md

**English.** Definition: A martingale approximation is an approximation of a rough driver by a sequence of discrete martingales. Conjecture: The RDE solution limits of two martingale-approximated drivers differ if and only if the approximation fails to preserve the convergence of the second-level Levy area. (martingale limit-area criterion)

**中文。** 定义：鞅逼近指以离散鞅序列逼近粗糙驱动。猜想：两条鞅逼近驱动的 RDE 解极限相异当且仅当逼近不保持第二层 Lévy 面积的收敛。（鞅极限-面积判据）
