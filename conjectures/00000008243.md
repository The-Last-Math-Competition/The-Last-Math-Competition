# 00000008243

**EGT-13 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The stability index of hospital-student matchings with per-hospital reserved-quota vectors: the redefinition of blocking pairs under quota constraints. Conjecture: Under arbitrary quota profiles stable matchings always exist, and the set of all stable matchings forms a dual sublattice of the lattice under the dominance order; the minimal number of quota deletions needed to remove all reservations of a single hospital is computable in polynomial time and does not exceed the hospital capacity; and changing a single coordinate of the quota vector changes the matching by symmetric difference at most O(n), with n the number of vertices on one side of the market. (hospital quota dual sublattice O(n) stability)

**中文。** 定义：医院-学生市场带逐医院保留配额向量的稳定匹配修正指标 quota_stab：配额约束下阻断对的重新界定。猜想：任意配额剖面下稳定匹配恒存在且全体稳定匹配在支配序下构成格的对偶子格；取消单家医院的全部保留所需的最小配额删除数为多项式时间可计算且不超过医院容量；配额向量变动一条引起的匹配对称差基数不超过 O(n)，其中 n 为市场一侧顶点数。
