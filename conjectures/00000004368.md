**English.** Definition: Prismatic THH is the topological Hochschild homology of primitized rings, and the filtered grading is the grading of its canonical filtration. Conjecture: The first graded piece is the polynomial ring of the Hodge-Tate grading, the leading constant is 1, and the higher coefficients are given explicitly by the Hodge numbers. (Hodge leading term of prismatic THH)

**中文。** 定义：棱晶 THH 指棱镜化环的拓扑 Hochschild 同调,滤波分次指其典范滤子的分次。猜想：首分次为 Hodge–Tate 分次的多项式环,首项常数为 1,高次系数由 Hodge 数显式给出。（棱晶 THH Hodge 首项）
