# 00000008274

**English.** Definition: The optimal coding of the recursion of the information state of filters: the recursion geo_rec of geodesic distances in the information geometry (Fisher metric) of Bayesian updates. Conjecture: geo_rec (the geodesic recursion of the information state) is the logarithmic coordinates of the local Fisher metric (a logarithmic coordinate law); the minimal dimension of the compression of the recursion (dimension of sufficient statistics) equals the parameter dimension (a minimal sufficient dimension law); and the spectral decay of the L^2 error of truncation approximations of nonparametric filtering (finite-dimensional projections of infinite-dimensional states) is the 2-alpha power of the eigenvalues (a 2 alpha spectral decay law). (information geometry logarithmic sufficient truncation)

**中文。** 定义：滤波的信息状态的递归的最优编码：贝叶斯更新的信息几何（Fisher 度量）的测地距离的递推 geo_rec。猜想：geo_rec（信息状态的测地递推）= 局部 Fisher 度量的对数坐标（对数坐标律）；且递推的压缩（充分统计量的维数）的最小维数 = 参数维数（最小充分维数律）；非参数滤波（无穷维状态）的近似的截断（有限维投影）的 L² 误差的谱衰减为特征值的 2α 次幂（2α 谱衰减律）。
