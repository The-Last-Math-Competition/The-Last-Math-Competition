**English.** Definition: The Jacobi operator is the linearized operator of a minimal surface. Conjecture: Its first eigenvalue has a universal explicit upper bound given by a combination of the coordinate functions and the mean curvature, with equality only for the equatorial minimal sphere. (universal upper bound of the first Jacobi eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 算子指极小曲面的线性化算子。猜想：其第一特征值有普适显式上界,由坐标函数与平均曲率的组合给出,等号仅在球面极小曲面达到。（Jacobi 首值普适上界）
