# 00000001072

**FG-72 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: Let G(n,k,q) denote the number of k-dimensional planes of F_q^n. Conjecture: The prime-factor spectrum of the Gaussian-binomial formula G(n,k,q) = q^{(n−k)k}·[n choose k]_q consists exactly of the primes ≤ q^n − 1; every prime factor divides some q^i − 1 with i ≤ n, and conversely each such prime occurs for suitable (n,k).

**中文。** 定义：F_q^n 中 k 维平面的个数记 G(n,k,q)。猜想：G(n,k,q)=q^{(n-k)k}·[n choose k]_q 的 Gaussian 二项式公式之素因子谱恰为 ≤ q^{n}-1 的素数全体;该计数的一切素因子整除某个 q^i−1(i≤n),且反之每个这样的素数在适当 (n,k) 出现。
