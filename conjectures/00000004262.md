# 00000004262

**English.** Definition: The Schellekens list is the list of 71 candidate classes of rank-24 self-dual VOAs. Conjecture: The orbit-decomposition construction realizes all candidate classes, and the minimal order of the twist group of the remaining unrealized classes is a prime at least 5. (minimal twist of the remaining Schellekens classes)

**中文。** 定义：Schellekens 表指秩 24 自对偶 VOA 的 71 个候选类。猜想：候选类的轨道分解构造全部实现,剩余未实现类的最小扭转群阶为不小于 5 的素数。（Schellekens 剩余类最小扭转）
