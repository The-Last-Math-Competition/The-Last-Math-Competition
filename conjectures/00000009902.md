# 00000009902

**English.** Definition: Representation dimension: Auslander algebras and the lower-bound structure of representation dimension. Conjecture: The difference between the representation dimension of an algebra and its derived dimension has upper bound 2, and the bound is tight; tightness is attained by explicit families of minimal Auslander algebras, where the difference is exactly 2. (representation-derived dimension difference bound)

**中文。** 定义：表示维数：Auslander 代数与表示维数的下界结构。猜想：代数的表示维数与其导出维数之差有上界 2 且界为紧；紧性由极小 Auslander 代数的显式族达到且差值在此族恰为 2。（表示-导出维数差上界）
