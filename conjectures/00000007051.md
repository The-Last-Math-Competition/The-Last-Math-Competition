# 00000007051

**English.** Definition: The inverse theorem of sum-product with structurization. Conjecture: The structurization of small sum-product holds, the classification of the structurization being subgroup neighborhoods. (subgroup-neighborhood inverse sum-product structurization)

**中文。** 定义：和积的逆定理：结构化。猜想：逆和积：小和积的结构化且化的分类为子群近邻。（逆和积子群近邻小和积结构化分类结构和积逆定理近邻）
