# 00000009110

**English.** Definition: Gauss-Bonnet: the topological identity of curvature. Conjecture: The boundary correction term always equals the boundary integral of geodesic curvature; and the higher-dimensional extension of the correction is Chern-Lashof. (Gauss-Bonnet boundary correction)

**中文。** 定义：Gauss–Bonnet 为曲率的拓扑恒等式。猜想：边界的修正项恒等于测地曲率的边界积分；且修正的高维推广为 Chern–Lashof。（Gauss-Bonnet 边界修正）
