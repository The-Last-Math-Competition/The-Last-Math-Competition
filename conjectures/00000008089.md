# 00000008089

**English.** Definition: The Kulshammer-Robinson-Sambale (KRS) bound for fusion systems: the lower bound on the character rank rk_chi(F) of the character table of a fusion system. Conjecture: rk_chi(F) is at least the iterated logarithm of |S:F| (a double-logarithmic lower bound), attained as a constant at defect-zero blocks (trivial fusion); the tightness of the upper bound of the character rank is given by the dimension spectrum of Gelfand-Graev characters of rank-1 Lie type groups (a Gelfand tight upper bound); and the intermediate value spectrum of the character rank is the complete interval of integers [c,C] (spectrum completeness). (KRS double-log Gelfand tight bounds)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 Külshammer–Robinson–Sambale (KRS) 界：融合系统的特征表的秩的下界（特征秩） rk_χ(F)。猜想：rk_χ(F) ≥ |S:F| 的对数的对数（双对数下界）且下界在亏 0 的块（平凡融合）取得为常数；且特征秩的上界的紧性由秩 1 的 Lie 型群的 Gelfand–Graev 特征的维数谱给出（Gelfand 紧上界）；特征秩的中间值谱为整数的完全区间 [c, C] 的实现（谱完全性）。
