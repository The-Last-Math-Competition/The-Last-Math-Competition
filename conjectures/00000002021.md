# 00000002021

**English.** Definition: G(k) is the smallest s such that every sufficiently large integer is a sum of s k-th powers. Conjecture: G(5) = 17; the currently known bound G(5) ≤ 17 (or on partial ranges) has 17 as the exact target; and the lower bound for G(5) is guaranteed by the local density obstruction modulo eleven.

**中文。** 定义：G(k) 指每个充分大整数可表为 s 个 k 次幂的最小 s。猜想：G(5)=17;当前已知 G(5)≤17(或部分区间),精确值 17 为目标；且 G(5) 的下界由模十一的局部密度障碍保证。
