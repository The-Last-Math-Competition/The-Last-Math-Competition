**English.** Definition: Star-autonomy is the symmetric structure closed under duality, the star-autonomous category. Conjecture: The compact-closed case degenerates to self-dual, the combination of the degeneration being the coduality vanishing; the criterion of degeneration is that the natural involution isomorphism is the identity; and in the nondegenerate case the tensor is nonsymmetric, the symmetric deficit given by cyclic permutation. (coduality-vanishing degeneration of compact-closed star-autonomy)
