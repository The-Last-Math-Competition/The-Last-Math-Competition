**English.** Definition: The statistical geometry of Bernstein bases: the algebraic limit of Bayesian posteriors. Conjecture: The algebraic degree of Bernstein approximation: the polynomial degree of the posterior mean is a linear function of the sample size, and the growth of coefficients is controlled by the Bernstein–Vasilescu rational extension. (Bernstein posterior algebraic degree)

**中文。** 定义：Bernstein 基的统计几何：贝叶斯后验的代数极限。猜想：Bernstein 逼近的代数度：后验均值的多项式次数为样本量的线性函数且系数的增长由 Bernstein–Vasilescu 有理扩张控制。（Bernstein 后验代数度）
