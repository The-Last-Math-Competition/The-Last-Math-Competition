**English.** Definition: Coincidence degree measures repeated positions of permutations. Conjecture: The conversion of coincidence degree with stability is explicitly linear at low order, the coefficients are given by orbit counting, and the growth order is preserved in both directions. (linear orbit conversion of coincidence degree)

**中文。** 定义：重合度指置换重复位置的度量。猜想：重合度与稳定性的换算在低阶为线性显式,系数由轨道计数给出且双向保持增长阶。（重合度线性轨道换算式）
