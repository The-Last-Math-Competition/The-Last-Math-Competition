**MP6-15 ★** — source: 235_母题与周期II.md

**English.** Definition: The motivic index is the framework of the motivic side of index theorems. Conjecture: The motivic spectrum of the index is always the graded structure of the weight filtration, the spectral gap is always one; and the gap spectrum is compatible with the rank generating function of motives. (weight-graded motivic index spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：母题的指标指指标定理的母题侧表述框架。猜想：指标的母题谱恒为权滤过的分次结构且谱的间隙恒为一；间隙谱与母题的秩生成函数相容。（母题指标权谱间隙一）
