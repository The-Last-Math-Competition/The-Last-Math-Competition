**GZ-24 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Image-set rigidity of the Cauchy transform: analytic capacity versus the area of the transform's image. Conjecture: The image area of the Cauchy transform controls analytic capacity: γ(E) ≤ C·Area(C_E) with asymptotic equality on purely Cantor-type sets; the boundary dimension of the image set is the exponent dual of analytic capacity. (Cauchy image area control)

**中文。** 定义：Cauchy 变换的象集刚性：解析容量与变换值域面积。猜想：Cauchy 变换的象面积控制解析容量：γ(E) ≤ C·Area(C_E) 且等号在纯 Cantor 型集渐近成立；象集的边界维数为解析容量的指数对偶。（Cauchy 象面积控制）
