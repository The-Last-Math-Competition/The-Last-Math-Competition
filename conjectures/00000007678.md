# 00000007678

**QDE-18 ◆** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: A quantum modular form in Zagier's sense is a function f:Q to C such that for each gamma in Gamma_0(N) the error function h_gamma(x)=f(x)-(cx+d)^{-k}f(gamma x) is analytic on intervals of R\Q and extends to the half-plane. Conjecture: The quantum modular forms with q-expansion coefficients satisfying constant-coefficient linear recursions form a finitely generated ring whose generator count equals the dimension sum of the dual spaces of weight k and 1-k mock modular forms; and the regularity of the error function is exactly the Hoelder exponent-one bound. (finitely generated ring of recursive quantum modular forms)

**中文。** 定义：Zagier 意义下的量子模形式指函数 f:Q→C，使得对每个 γ=(a b c d)∈Γ_0(N)，错切函数 h_γ(x)=f(x)-(cx+d)^{-k} f(γx) 在 R\Q 的每个区间上解析并延拓到整个半平面。猜想：q-展开系数满足常系数线性递推的量子模形式构成有限生成的环，其生成元个数恰等于影子空间中权为 k 与 1-k 的 mock 模形式对偶空间的维数之和显式的结构刻画；且错切函数的正则性恰为 Hölde 指数一的界。
