**English.** Definition: graph of parameter dependence: solution map S(p,λ) parameter (p,λ) of variational contains 0∈A(x)+λ x-p and regularity. Conjecture: graph of solution map always closed; continuous if and only if A strong and parameter field is connected; solution map is sufficient of Hausdorff Lipschitz is inverse of A is cocoercive and Lipschitz constant is not of of reciprocal; parameter unit path one solution curve of exactly strong constant does not exceed dimension of space of A.
