# 00000008850

**English.** Definition: Parameter dependence of the variational inclusion 0 in A(x) + lambda x - p: the graph and regularity of the solution map S(p,lambda) in the parameters. Conjecture: The graph of the solution map is always closed; single-valued continuity holds if and only if A is strongly monotone and the parameter domain is connected; a sufficient condition for Hausdorff Lipschitz regularity is that the inverse of A is cocoercive with Lipschitz constant exactly the reciprocal of the cocoercivity constant; and the non-closure of the lifted solution curve after one loop of a unit-circle parameter path does not exceed the dimension of the deficiency space of A. (variational inclusion solution map regularity lift)

**中文。** 定义：变分包含 0∈A(x)+λ x-p 的参数依赖：解映射 S(p,λ) 随参数 (p,λ) 的图与正则性。猜想：解映射的图恒闭；单值连续当且仅当 A 强单调且参数域为连通；解映射为 Hausdorff Lipschitz 的充分条件是 A 的逆为 cocoercive 且 Lipschitz 常数恰为余强制性常数的倒数；参数沿单位圆路径一圈后解曲线的提升产生的不闭合度不超过 A 的亏损空间的维数。
