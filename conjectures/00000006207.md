# 00000006207

**English.** Definition: Inverse scattering is the inversion method of spectral data. Conjecture: The reflection-plus-pole map is stable and the inverse map recovers the initial value, the combination of the recovery being Gelfand-Levitan. (GL map recovery of inverse scattering)

**中文。** 定义：逆散射为谱数据的反演方法。猜想：反射加极点的映射稳定且逆映射恢复初值；且恢复的组合为 Gelfand–Levitan。（逆散射 GL 映射恢复初值）
