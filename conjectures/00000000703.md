# 00000000703

**English.** Definition: In Schneider's p-adic continued fractions the partial quotients are powers of p, and the convergent denominators satisfy |q_{n+1}/q_n|_p → p^{-1}. Conjecture: For almost every ξ ∈ Q_p, log_p |q_n|_p/n converges to a constant κ_p whose analogue value of log_p φ is 1; this constant is independent of the digit set chosen by the algorithm.

**中文。** 定义：Schneider p 进连分数的部分商取 p 的幂,收敛子分母 q_n 满足 |q_{n+1}/q_n|_p→p^{-1} 型极限。猜想：对 a.e. ξ∈Q_p,log_p |q_n|_p/n 收敛到常数 κ_p,且 κ_p = log_p φ 的类比值为 1;该常数与算法选择的 digit 集无关。
