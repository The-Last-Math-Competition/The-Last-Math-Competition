**English.** Definition: Coordinate descent: update methods with random or cyclic coordinates. Conjecture: The expected rate of random coordinates follows the explicit law of coordinate Lipschitz constant ratios; and accelerated coordinates parallelize (a parallelization law). (random coordinate Lipschitz ratio law)

**中文。** 定义：坐标下降为随机与循环坐标的更新方法。猜想：随机坐标的期望率为坐标 Lipschitz 常数比的显式律；且加速坐标的并行化为并行律。（随机坐标 Lipschitz 比律）
