**English.** Definition: Konane and toads-and-frogs: value spectra of typical partizan games. Conjecture: The value spectrum realizes dense subsets of real surreals (a density law); the rate of densification (size versus complexity) is controlled (a rate law); small games have no repeated values (a no-repetition law); and the first repetition occurs at a minimal repeated-value game (a first-example law). (konane toads-frogs dense spectrum first repetition)

**中文。** 定义：konane / toads-and-frogs：典型 partizan 游戏的值谱。猜想：值谱的（实 surreal 的稠密实现）的（稠密律）；且稠密的速率（尺寸-复杂度）的（速率律）；速率的下界（小游戏的无重复值）的（无重复律）；重复的首例（最小重复值的游戏）的（首例律）。
