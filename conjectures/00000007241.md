**English.** Definition: The direct method with the classical method of the calculus of variations. Conjecture: The sufficiency of convergence of minimizing sequences is convexity plus coercivity, and the constant of the sufficiency is Young's inequality. (Young-inequality direct-method compactness)

**中文。** 定义：直接法：变分法的经典方法。猜想：直接法的紧性：极小化序列的收敛的充分为凸性加强制性且分的常数为 Young 不等式。（直接法 Young 不等式凸性强制极小化收敛常数充分）
