**English.** Definition: The Weil-Petersson metric is the symplectically invariant metric of Teichmuller space. Conjecture: Its Ricci curvature is an explicit negative-definite combination, the compactification boundary has quadratic cone singularities, and the singularity coefficients are given explicitly by the alternation numbers. (WP Ricci quadratic cone formula)

**中文。** 定义：Weil–Petersson 度量指 Teichmüller 空间的辛不变度量。猜想：其 Ricci 曲率为负定的显式组合式,完备化边界处呈二次锥奇性,奇性系数由交错数显式给出。（WP Ricci 二次锥公式）
