**English.** Definition: The barcode is the interval-decomposition representation of persistence modules. Conjecture: The interval-type lattice of three dimensions is indexed by the birth-death dimension difference, the combination of the lattice being the simplicial decomposition; the rank of the lattice is three; and the additivity of the index is preserved under interval direct sums, with constant 1. (birth-death dimension simplicial lattice in three dimensions)
