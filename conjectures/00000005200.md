# 00000005200

**ST2-30 △** — source: 158_高阶范畴II_稳定无穷范畴.md

**English.** Definition: Homology groups and category equivalence classes are two layers of invariants. Conjecture: There exist two categorified invariants whose homology groups all agree but whose category equivalence classes differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of condensation complexes with the same homology but different homotopy. (same homology-different category equivalence separation)

**中文。** 定义：同调群与范畴等价类指两层不变量。猜想：存在两范畴化不变量的一切同调群相同而范畴等价类不同,分离由同调不同伦的显式凝集群对实现。（同调群同-范畴等价异分离）
