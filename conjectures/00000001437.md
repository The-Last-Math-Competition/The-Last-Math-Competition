**English.** Definition: The contact process on a Galton–Watson tree is the infection dynamics on the tree. Conjecture: The ratio of the strong-survival to weak-survival thresholds is always an explicit function of the mean μ; and the ratio tends to one as μ → ∞. (GW tree strong-weak threshold ratio)

**中文。** 定义：接触过程在 Galton–Watson 树指树上感染动力学。猜想：强弱生存临界的比值恒为均值 μ 的显式函数；且比值在 μ 趋于无穷时趋于一。（GW 树强弱临界比）
