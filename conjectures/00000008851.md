# 00000008851

**English.** Definition: The association map between sets and functions in abstract convex analysis: the antitone correspondence between indicator functions and support functions of closed convex sets. Conjecture: The association map is an antitone bijection whose composition is the identity; objects fixed by double association are exactly graphs of symmetric operators — those whose subdifferentials are symmetric monotone relations; association and conjugation commute under Fenchel duality with the exchange diagram commuting up to isomorphism; and the number of antitone lattice homomorphisms equals the factorial of the dimension of the underlying space. (antitone association symmetric fixed symmetric monotone)

**中文。** 定义：抽象凸分析中集合与函数的关联映射：闭凸集的示性函数与支撑函数之间的反序对应。猜想：关联映射构成反序双射且复合恰为恒等；自反关联（两次复合不动）的对象恰为对称算子的图——次微分为对称单调关系者；在 Fenchel 对偶下关联与共轭可交换且交换图在同构意义下交换；反序格同态的个数等于底层空间维数的阶乘。
