**English.** Definition: Multilevel Monte Carlo is the simulation averaging method of hierarchical refinement. Conjecture: The optimal sample allocation of three levels is an explicit geometric ratio by the variance decay rate, the ratio is four, and it is optimal by the doubling law. (optimal geometric ratio four of three levels)
