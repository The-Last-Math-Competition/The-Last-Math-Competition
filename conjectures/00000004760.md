**English.** Definition: Distribution and free entropy are the two invariants of a noncommutative space. Conjecture: There exist two noncommutative variables with the same marginal distributions but different free entropies, and the separation is realized by an explicit construction with empty microstate sets. (same distribution-free entropy microstate separation)

**中文。** 定义：分布与自由熵指非交换空间的两个不变量。猜想：存在两个非交换变量的边缘分布相同而自由熵不同,分离由微状态空集的显式构造实现。（分布同-自由熵微状态分离）
