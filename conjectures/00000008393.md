# 00000008393

**English.** Definition: The change of variables formula for motivic integrals: the Jacobian correction for variable substitution under the motivic measure. Conjecture: The motivic lift of the Jacobian of the change of variables is the image of a motivic differential operator on the motivic measure; on weighted homogeneous singularities the operator is the restriction of the spectrum of the Euler operator at critical values; and the formula is valid if and only if the map is quasi-finite. (motivic change-of-variables operator)

**中文。** 定义：母题积分的换元公式指在母题测度下变量替换的 Jacobian 修正。猜想：换元公式的 Jacobian 的母题化为母题测度的母题微分算子的像；该算子在加权齐次奇点上为 Euler 算子的谱在临界值上的限制；公式的有效性当且仅当映射为拟有限的。（母题换元算子）
