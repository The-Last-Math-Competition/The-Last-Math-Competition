# 00000003835

**English.** Definition: The weight polytope P_λ denotes the convex hull of the weight set of the highest-weight crystal B(λ). Conjecture: The limiting shape of weight density supported on P_λ has piecewise-polynomial density, zero on the boundary and strictly positive inside, with the number of pieces at most the order of the Weyl group. (weight density limiting shape)

**中文。** 定义：权多面体 P_λ 指最高权晶体 B(λ) 的权集凸包。猜想：以 P_λ 支撑为限的权密度极限形状在边界为零、内部为严格正的分片多项式密度,且分片个数不超过 Weyl 群的阶。（权密度极限形状）
