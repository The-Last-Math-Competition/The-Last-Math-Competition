# 00000009852

**English.** Definition: Fluctuation and correlation exponents: the height fluctuation t^{1/3} and correlation length t^{2/3} of the KPZ class form the 1:2 ratio. Conjecture: The subleading correction of the correlation length exponent is logarithmic: the correlation length carries a (log t)^{1/9} correction to t^{2/3}; the correction exponent 1/9 is uniquely determined by the tail asymptotics of the Airy kernel. (correlation length logarithmic correction)

**中文。** 定义：涨落指数与相关指数：KPZ 类的高度涨落 t^{1/3} 与相关长度 t^{2/3} 构成 1:2 比例。猜想：相关长度指数的次阶修正为对数型：相关长度带 t^{2/3} 乘 (log t) 的 1/9 幂修正；修正指数 1/9 由 Airy 核的尾部渐近唯一确定。（相关长度对数修正）
