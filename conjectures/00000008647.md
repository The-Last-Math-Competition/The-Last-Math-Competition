# 00000008647

**English.** Definition: Fusion models are Chebyshev-type weighted counts. Conjecture: The k-th power sums of fusion numbers have closed forms with closed coefficients; and the combination of the closure is a trace identity. (fusion trace identity Chebyshev power closed form)

**中文。** 定义：融合模型指 Chebyshev 型加权计数。猜想：融合数的 k 幂和有闭式且系数封闭；且封闭的组合为迹恒等。（融合迹恒等 Chebyshev 幂闭式）
