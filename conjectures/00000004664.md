**English.** Definition: The difference of the Milnor number and the Tyurina number is the difference of the two invariants. Conjecture: The realizable set of the difference is the explicit interval satisfying that twice the Tyurina number is at least the Milnor number, the endpoints are characterized by weighted homogeneous types, and the interval cannot be enlarged. (doubled constraint interval of the difference)

**中文。** 定义：Milnor 数与 Tyurina 数之差指两不变量的差。猜想：差值的可实现集为满足两倍 Tyurina 数不小于 Milnor 数的显式区间,端点由加权齐次型刻画且不可再扩。（差值区间二倍约束）
