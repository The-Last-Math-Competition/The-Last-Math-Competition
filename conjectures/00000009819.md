# 00000009819

**English.** Definition: Symmetry breaking of random Delaunay triangulations: long-range order and entropy measures. Conjecture: Random Delaunay triangulations under the two-dimensional exponential model undergo hexagonal symmetry breaking at a critical temperature; the joint area-edge-count distribution in the broken phase is bimodal, with peak separation growing at the Ising exponent beta^{1/8} near criticality. (Delaunay symmetry breaking)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Delaunay 三角化的对称破缺：长程序与熵测度。猜想：二维指数模型下的随机 Delaunay 三角剖分在临界温度处发生 六边形对称破缺；破缺相的面积-边数联合分布为双峰且峰间距在临界点按 β^{1/8} 的 Ising 指数增长。（Delaunay 对称破缺）
