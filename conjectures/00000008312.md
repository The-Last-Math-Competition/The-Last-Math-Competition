**English.** Definition: The microlocal constancy of constructible sheaves: the index mc_lag of the Lagrangian nature of singular supports (characteristic varieties). Conjecture: mc_lag (the involutivity of the characteristic variety) is homogeneous Hamilton-Jacobi (an HJ homogeneity law); the count of the minimal generation of the category by singular sheaves (a minimal generation law) equals the number of strata of the skeleton (a strata counting law); and the localization cost of verifying generation (the generation criterion) is exponential in the codimension of singularities (a codimension exponent cost law). (constructible involutivity skeleton strata localization)

**中文。** 定义：可构造层（constructible sheaf）的 microlocal 常数：奇异支撑（characteristic variety）的 Lagrangian 性的指标 mc_lag。猜想：mc_lag（characteristic variety 的 involutivity）为齐性 Hamilton–Jacobi（HJ 齐性律）；且层范畴的生成（全局生成）的特异层的极小生成（极小生成律）的计数 = skeleton 的 strata 数（strata 计数律）；生成的验证（生成判据）的局部化的 cost 为奇点的余维的指数（余维指数 cost 律）。
