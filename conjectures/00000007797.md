**English.** Definition: complexity: convex body of volume estimate by becomes lattice oracle gives, randomization algorithm estimate vol(K) pair error ε. Conjecture: optimal complexity is n/ε² of Θ(n log n + n²/ε²) · log(1/δ) (δ probability), namely known and all of log between is not possibly; deterministic algorithm requires (1+1/ε)^{n-1} and exponent cannot be improved.

**中文。** 定义：体积估计的查询复杂度：凸体由成员资格 oracle 给出，随机化算法估计 vol(K) 至相对误差 ε。猜想：最优查询复杂度为 Θ(n log n + n²/ε²)·log(1/δ)（δ 失败概率），即改进已知的 n/ε² 与 log 之间的任何缝隙均为不可能；确定性算法需 (1+1/ε)^{n-1} 次查询且指数不可改进。
