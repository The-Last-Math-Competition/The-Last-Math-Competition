**English.** Definition: Shock formation as the gradient blow-up of characteristic convergence. Conjecture: The order of gradient blow-up is exactly reciprocal type 1/(t*-t); and the exactness of the order is by the characteristic compression identity. (reciprocal-order shock gradient blow-up)

**中文。** 定义：激波形成为特征汇聚的梯度爆破。猜想：梯度爆破的阶恰为倒数型 (1/(t*−t))；且阶的精确性由特征压缩恒等式。（激波梯度倒数爆破阶）
