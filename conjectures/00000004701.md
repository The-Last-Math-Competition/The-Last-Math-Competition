**English.** Definition: Sarkozy's theorem is the recurrence theorem along sets of squared differences. Conjecture: The density lower-bound exponent of its quantitative version can be improved to explicitly exceed one quarter, and the improvement is achieved by a new energy-increment argument and it converges. (quantitative exponent improvement of Sarkozy)

**中文。** 定义：Sarkozy 定理指沿平方差集的回归定理。猜想：其定量版本的密度下界指数可改进到显式超过四分之一,改进由新的能量增量论证达到且收敛。（Sarkozy 定量指数改进）
