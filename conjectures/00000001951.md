# 00000001951

**LG-21 ◆** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: The congruence subgroup property (CSP) of Out(F_n) is the coincidence of its congruence subgroups with all finite-index subgroups. Conjecture: Out(F_n) (n ≥ 3) fails CSP (its smallest-index proper subgroup is an automorphism kernel); the smallest index of a proper subgroup is 2^n·C(n,2).

**中文。** 定义：Out(F_n) 的同余子群性质(CSP)指其同余子群与一切有限指标子群的一致性。猜想：Out(F_n)(n≥3) 不满足 CSP(其最小指数真子群为自同构核);真子群的最小指数为 2^n·C(n,2)。
