# 00000007056

**English.** Definition: High-dimensional sum-product with the multivariable version. Conjecture: The multivariable sum-product holds, the combination of the product being projective duality. (projective-dual multivariable sum-product)

**中文。** 定义：高维和积：多元版本。猜想：高维和积：多元的和积且积的组合为射影对偶。（高维和积射影对偶多元和积对偶组合多元和积高维）
