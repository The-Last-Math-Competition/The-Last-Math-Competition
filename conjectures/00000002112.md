# 00000002112

**PS-02 ★** — source: 56_解析组合.md

**English.** Definition: Mallows permutations are q-biased random permutations. Conjecture: Mixing exhibits cutoff with window width (1−q)^{-1} times a logarithmic factor; and the constant of the factor is one half. (Mallows cutoff window)

**中文。** 定义：Mallows 置换为 q-偏置的随机置换。猜想：混合出现 cutoff 且窗口宽度为 (1-q)^{-1} 乘对数因子；且因子的常数为二分之一。（Mallows cutoff 窗口）
