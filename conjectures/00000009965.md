**MRG-25 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Diffraction of model sets: pure point diffraction spectra of cut-and-project constructions (pure pointedness is known). Conjecture: The quantitative form of the equivalence of pure pointedness of the diffraction spectrum with Meyer property: pure pointedness is linearly equivalent to the growth rate of the doubling radius, with the linear constant explicit in the measure of the window boundary. (pure point diffraction Meyer quantitative equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：模型集的衍射：cut-and-project 构造的纯点衍射谱（已知纯点性）。猜想：衍射谱的纯点性与 Meyer 性的等价的定量形式：纯点性与加倍半径的增长率线性等价且线性常数为窗口边界的测度显式。（纯点衍射-Meyer 定量等价）
