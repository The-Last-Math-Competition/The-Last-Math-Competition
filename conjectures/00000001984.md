# 00000001984

**English.** Definition: Z_n is the domain-wall partition function of the six-vertex model. Conjecture: The second coefficient of the asymptotics of Z_n/Z_{n−1} is always one quarter times the central charge; and the coefficient is given by the Wiener–Hopf asymptotics of the Izergin kernel. (domain-wall partition Wiener-Hopf second term)

**中文。** 定义：六顶点模型的域壁配分函数 Z_n。猜想：Z_n 与 Z_{n−1} 的比的渐近第二项系数恒为四分之一乘中心荷；且系数由 Izergin 核的 Wiener–Hopf 渐近给出。（域壁配分 Wiener-Hopf 二项）
