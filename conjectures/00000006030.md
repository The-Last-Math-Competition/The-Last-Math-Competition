**English.** Definition: The exponent spectrum and the diffusion rate are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two systems with the same exponent spectrum but different energy diffusion rates, and the separation is realized by an explicit time-reversible pair with the same spectrum but different correlation decay. (same exponent spectrum-different correlation diffusion separation)

**中文。** 定义：指数谱与扩散率指两层。猜想：存在两系统的指数谱相同而能量扩散率不同,分离由同谱异关联衰减的显式时间可逆对实现。（指数谱同-扩散率异关联分离）
