# 00000002711

**English.** Definition: The f-vector of a simplicial complex: the sequence (f₀,…,f_{d−1}) of face numbers. Conjecture: A complete characterization of the realizable domain of f-vectors: the f-vector set of d-dimensional complexes is the integer-point envelope of a combinatorial polytope, with boundary faces the equality faces of Stanley's graded inequalities; the complexes of the equality faces form the overlapping shellable family. (f-vector realizability characterization)

**中文。** 定义：单纯复形的 f-向量：各维面数的序列 (f₀,…,f_{d−1})。猜想：f-向量可实现域的完全刻画：d 维复形的 f-向量集为组合多面体的整点包络且包络的边界面为 Stanley 分次不等式的等号面；等号面的复形分类为重叠 shellable 族。（f-向量可实现刻画）
