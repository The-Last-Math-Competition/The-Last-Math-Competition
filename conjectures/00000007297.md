# 00000007297

**English.** Definition: The Plancherel measure with random partitions. Conjecture: The limit shape of random partitions exists, and the functional of the shape is the minimum of the variational functional. (variational-minimum Plancherel limit shape)

**中文。** 定义：Plancherel 测度：随机分拆。猜想：随机分拆的极限形状且状的函数为变分泛函的极小。（Plancherel 变分泛函极限形状随机分拆函数极小）
