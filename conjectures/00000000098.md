# 00000000098

**English.** Conjecture: Serre's uniformity question: there exists an absolute constant C(E) such that for every elliptic curve E/Q without complex multiplication and every ℓ > C(E), the ℓ-torsion Galois representation ρ_{E,ℓ} surjects onto GL₂(F_ℓ).

**中文。** 猜想：Serre 一致性:存在绝对常数 C(E),使得对无复乘 E/Q 与所有 ℓ > C(E),ℓ-torsion Galois 表示 ρ_{E,ℓ} 满射到 GL₂(F_ℓ)。
