**BF2-07 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: A Chenciner bifurcation is a saddle-node bifurcation of invariant tori, and the Farey tree is the rational tree of locking ratios. Conjecture: The ratios of the sizes of adjacent windows deep in the Farey tree converge to a universal constant, which in the two-dimensional case is the square of the reciprocal of the golden ratio. (window-ratio limit of Chenciner bifurcations)

**中文。** 定义：Chenciner 分叉指不变环面的鞍结分叉,Farey 树指锁频比的有理树。猜想：Farey 树深层相邻窗口尺寸之比收敛于普适常数,二维情形常数为黄金比倒数的平方。（Chenciner 窗口比极限）
