**OA-22 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: A noncommutative CW complex is the algebraic skeleton of iterated cohesive extensions. Conjecture: The K₁ group of an NCCW is free abelian with rank equal to the cycle rank of the connecting graph; and the cycle-count formula is preserved additively under iterated extensions. (NCCW K1 cycle rank formula)

**中文。** 定义：非交换 CW 复形为迭代凝聚扩张的代数骨架。猜想：NCCW 的 K₁ 群为自由交换群且秩等于连接图的圈秩；且圈数公式在迭代扩张下加性保持。（NCCW K1 圈秩公式）
