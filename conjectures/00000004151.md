# 00000004151

**English.** Definition: The singular series is the Euler-product correction factor of the main term in the circle method. Conjecture: The remainder term of the count of three-prime representations has an oscillating phase spectrum, given exactly by the neighboring phases of the approximating fractions, with an amplitude lower bound of square-root oscillation type that cannot be weakened. (oscillation spectrum of the three-prime remainder)

**中文。** 定义：奇异级数指圆法中主项的 Euler 积修正因子。猜想：三素数表示数的余项具振荡相位谱,谱恰由逼近分数的邻近相位给出,振幅下界为平方根振荡型且不可减弱。（三素数余项振荡谱）
