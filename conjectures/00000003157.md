# 00000003157

**English.** Definition: The Wigner–Eckart theorem: symmetry and selection rules. Conjecture: The complete list of selection rules: nonzero matrix elements of tensor operators have angular momentum coupling by Clebsch–Gordan coefficients, with the symmetry of the coefficients the permutation parity of the 3j symbols. (Wigner selection 3j parity)

**中文。** 定义：Wigner–Eckart 定理：对称性与选择定则。猜想：选择定则的完全列表：张量算符的非零矩阵元的角动量耦合由 Clebsch–Gordan 系数且系数的对称为 3j 符号的置换宇称。（Wigner 选择 3j 宇称）
