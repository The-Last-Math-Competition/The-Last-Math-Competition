# 00000006837

**English.** Definition: The invariants of the adjoint operator. Conjecture: The invariant subspaces of the adjoint hold, the combination of the subspaces being orthogonal complements. (orthogonal-complement adjoint invariants)

**中文。** 定义：对偶算子的不变。猜想：对偶的不变：伴随的不变子空间且间的组合为正交补。（对偶算子正交补伴随不变子空间对偶组合不变正交补伴随子空间对偶补）
