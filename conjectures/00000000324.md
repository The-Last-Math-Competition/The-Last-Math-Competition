**English.** Definition: The Jarník spectrum is the set of all Jarník exponents v(α) (orders of quadratic rational approximation). Conjecture: The Lebesgue density of the spectrum near each point of [2,3] is a continuous function; the density has a logarithmic singularity at the endpoint 2 and reaches the saturation value one at the endpoint 3. (Jarnik spectrum density continuous)

**中文。** 定义：Jarník 谱为一切 Jarník 指数 v(α)（二次有理逼近的逼近阶）之集。猜想：谱在闭区间 [2,3] 中每点附近的 Lebesgue 测度密度为连续函数；密度函数在端点二处有对数奇异且在端点三处达到饱和值一。（Jarnik 谱密度连续）
