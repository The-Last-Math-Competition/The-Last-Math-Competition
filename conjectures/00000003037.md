**English.** Definition: The spectral form factor (SFF) of many-body systems: the fingerprint of quantum chaos. Conjecture: The ramp-plateau structure of SFF: the slope of the ramp is a linear function of inverse temperature, and the plateau saturation value is the reciprocal of the Hilbert dimension; the ramp-inflection time is the Thouless time of the spectrum. (SFF Thouless inflection time)

**中文。** 定义：多体系统的谱形因子（SFF）：量子混沌的指纹。猜想：SFF 的斜坡-平台结构：斜坡的斜率为温度倒数的线性函数且平台的饱和值为希尔伯特维数的倒数；斜坡-拐点的时间为谱的 Thouless 时间。（SFF Thouless 拐点时间）
