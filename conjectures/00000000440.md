# 00000000440

**SA-40 △** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt polynomial PBW_n(π) counts decomposable words of the length-n permutation π; the total is PBW_n = Σ_π PBW_n(π). Conjecture: PBW_n admits an exact rational combinatorial formula (not merely a recursion), and PBW_n/n! → 1/√3; this limiting constant is explicitly related to a spectral integral of a tridiagonal matrix.

**中文。** 定义：Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt 多项式 PBW_n(π) 计数长 n 排列 π 的可分解字,总计数 PBW_n=Σ_π PBW_n(π)。猜想：PBW_n 存在精确有理组合式(而非仅递推),且 PBW_n/n! → 1/√3;该极限常数与 3-对角矩阵的谱积分显式相关。
