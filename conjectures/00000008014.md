# 00000008014

**English.** Definition: The flatness threshold of the Gamma-limit of the Bernoulli energy functional: the minimal scale epsilon*(n) for coarsening the Modica-Mortola interface energy of E_epsilon(u) = integral epsilon |grad u|^2 + W(u)/epsilon. Conjecture: epsilon* is an explicit threshold of the ratio of interface thickness to domain diameter (the single-scale interface limit); the flattening of the Gamma-limit (the interchange of the limit with mean curvature flow of the interface) fails at scales above epsilon*, the critical failure index being a combinatorial quantity of type 2^n (the neighborhood covering number of the phase boundary); and the convergence order of the limit interchange for energy-minimizing configurations is epsilon^{1/2} log(1/epsilon) (the optimal log-corrected rate). (Modica-Mortola flatness critical scale)

**中文。** 定义：Bernoulli 能量泛函的 Γ-极限的平坦性阈值：E_ε(u)=∫ε|∇u|²+W(u)/ε 的 Modica–Mortola 的界面能量的粗化的最小尺度 ε*(n)。猜想：ε* 为界面厚度与域直径的比值的显式分界（界面单尺度极限）；Γ-极限的平坦化（界面的平均曲率流的极限交换）在 ε* 之上的尺度失效，失效的临界指标为 2^{n} 型组合量（相界的邻域覆盖数）；能量最小构型的界面的平均曲率流的极限交换的收敛阶为 ε^{1/2}·log(1/ε)（log 修正的最优速率）。
