**English.** Definition: The Lovasz theta functional is the semidefinite programming functional of graphs. Conjecture: Its graphon version is an explicit interpolation function between the spectral radius and the chromatic-number parameter, normalized and monotone at the two ends on complete-graph graphons. (graphon theta interpolation function)

**中文。** 定义：Lovasz θ 泛函指图的半定规划泛函。猜想：其 graphon 版为谱半径与色数参数间的显式插值函数,插值在完全图 graphon 两端归一且单调。（graphon θ 插值函数）
