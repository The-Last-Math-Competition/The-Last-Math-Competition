# 00000008746

**MKT-06 ★** — source: 277_代数K理论与MilnorK.md

**English.** Definition: Waldhausen A-theory: the K-spectrum of the category of simple finite spectra over X. Conjecture: A(X) always splits as the product of the infinite suspension spectrum of X with WH(X); the additivity theorem for the splitting is compatible with bifunctoriality; and stable J homology is spectrally given by Lichtenbaum special values. (Waldhausen splitting additivity theorem)

**中文。** 定义：Waldhausen A-理论指简单有限谱范畴 over X 的 K 谱。猜想：A(X) 恒分裂为 Ω^∞Σ^∞X 与 WH(X) 的积；且分裂的加法定理在双函子性下相容，稳定 J 同调由 Lichtenbaum 特殊值的谱化给出。（Waldhausen 分裂加法定理）
