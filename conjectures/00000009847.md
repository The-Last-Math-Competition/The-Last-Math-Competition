# 00000009847

**WEX-27 ★** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: Rubio-type Littlewood-Paley inequalities: control of projections onto disjoint frequencies. Conjecture: The constant of the weighted Rubio inequality as a function of p is piecewise: for p at least 2 the constant is bounded and converges to 1, while for p below 2 it diverges as an inverse power of p - 1; the divergence exponent is exact. (Rubio weighted piecewise constant)

**中文。** 定义：Littlewood–Paley 的 Rubio 型不等式：不相交频率投影的控制。猜想：加权 Rubio 不等式的常数与 p 的关系为分段函数：p≥2 时常数有界收敛到 1 且 p<2 时按 p−1 的逆幂发散；发散指数精确。（Rubio 加权分段常数）
