# 00000008239

**English.** Definition: The complexity class of computing Nash equilibria: the constant complexity ppad_const of the PPAD-completeness of two-player games. Conjecture: The lower bound of ppad_const (the constant of support enumeration) is exponential 2^n (an exponential lower bound law); the complexity of enumerating sparse supports (support size k) is n^{2k} (a 2k power law); and the gap separating the existence of polynomial algorithms for constant-epsilon approximate equilibria from PPAD-completeness of the exact problem is the polynomial feasibility threshold epsilon at least 10^{-4} (a ten-thousandth feasibility threshold), tightness given by the Eckhardt construction for bimatrix games (an Eckhardt tight construction). (PPAD support enumeration ten-thousandth threshold)

**中文。** 定义：纳什均衡的计算复杂性的类：双人博弈的纳什的 PPAD 完全性的常数复杂度 ppad_const。猜想：ppad_const（支持枚举的复杂度常数）的下界为 2^{n} 的指数（指数下界律）；且稀疏支持（支撑大小 k）的枚举的复杂度为 n^{2k}（2k 幂律）；近似均衡（ε-纳什）的多项式算法的存在（ε 为常数）与精确的 PPAD 完全的分离的间隙（常数-精确间隙）为 ε ≥ 10^{-4} 的多项式可行阈（万分之一可行阈），阈的紧性由 Bimatrix 的 Eckhardt 构造（Eckhardt 紧构造）。
