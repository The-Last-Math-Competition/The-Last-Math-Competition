**English.** Definition: A cyclic algebra is the algebra constructed from a cyclic extension and the symbol of an element. Conjecture: The equivalent normal form of a quartic cyclic algebra is an explicit two-parameter family, exactly two invariants remain modulo joint transformations, and the parametrization is single. (two-parameter normal form of quartic cyclic algebras)
