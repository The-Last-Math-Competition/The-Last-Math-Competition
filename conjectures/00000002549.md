**GZ-19 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: The quasisymmetric equivalence classification of Ahlfors regular (AR) measures. Conjecture: The QS equivalence classes of AR(k) measures are completely determined by segments at k = 1; in higher dimensions the QS classes are separated by the David–Semmes condition, with separation degree given by the diversity index of tangent cones. (AR quasisymmetric classification)

**中文。** 定义：Ahlfors 正则（AR）测度的拟对称等价分类。猜想：AR(k) 测度的 QS 等价类在 k=1 由线段完全决定；高维的 QS 类由 David–Semmes 条件分离且分离度为切锥的多样性指数。（AR 拟对称分类）
