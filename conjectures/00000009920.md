# 00000009920

**English.** Definition: Persistent homology testing: minimax detection of topological signals. Conjecture: The minimax separation rate of persistent homology testing is the n to the minus one-half power of the signal-to-noise ratio; the separation rate is uniquely determined by the overlap region of the distributions of the longest persistent bar and noise bars, and the test is a level test based on the longest bar. (persistence detection minimax rate)

**中文。** 定义：持久同调检验：拓扑信号的极小极大检测。猜想：持久同调检验的极小极大分离率为信噪比的 n 的负二分之一次幂；分离率由最长持久条与噪声条的分布重叠区域唯一决定且检验为基于最长条的水平检验。（持久检测极小极大率）
