# 00000002125

**English.** Definition: The free energy of the antiferroelectric limit of the six-vertex model. Conjecture: The denominator of the free energy is a rational multiple of π; the multiple is one quarter; and the denominator of the multiple is a combination of four with square roots. (six-vertex antiferroelectric free energy rational denominator)

**中文。** 定义：六顶点模型反对称极限的自由能。猜想：自由能的分母为 π 的有理倍数；且倍数为四分之；且倍数的分母为四与平方根的组合。（六顶点反自由能有理分母）
