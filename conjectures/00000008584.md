# 00000008584

**NSP-24 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The Friedrichs extension of semibounded symmetric operators and the Rayleigh-Ritz minimax principle. Conjecture: The eigenvalues of the Friedrichs extension are characterized by the minimax principle (a principle law); Rayleigh-Ritz approximations converge from above (an above convergence law); the convergence rate is controlled by the Polya frequency condition (a P-rate law); and the saturation constant of triangulation approximations is the square of the mesh ratio (a mesh ratio square law). (Friedrichs minimax Polya mesh saturation)

**中文。** 定义：半有界对称算子的 Friedrichs 扩张与 Rayleigh–Ritz 极小极大原理。猜想：Friedrichs 扩张的特征值由极小极大原理刻画（原理律）；且 Rayleigh–Ritz 逼近从上方收敛（上方收敛律）；收敛速率由 Pólya 频率条件控制（P-速率律）；三角剖分逼近的饱和常数为网格比的平方（网格比平方律）。
