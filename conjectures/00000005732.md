# 00000005732

**English.** Definition: Uniqueness is the singleness of the minimal set. Conjecture: The convex-domain criterion is the explicit condition on the boundary curvature integral, unique for strictly convex domains; the weakly convex exception table is finite, the exceptions given by symmetric two-solutions of flat boundary arcs; and the intersection of the two solutions in the exceptional domain is a hyperplane with normal parallel to the flat arc. (strictly convex uniqueness with weakly convex exceptions)

**中文。** 定义：唯一性：极小集的单一性。猜想：凸域的判据为边界曲率积分的显式条件且严格凸时唯一；弱凸的例外表为有限且例外由平坦边界弧的对称二解给出；两个解的交在例外域为超平面且平面的法向平行于平坦弧。（严格凸唯一弱凸例外判据）
