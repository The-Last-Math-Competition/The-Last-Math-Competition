**English.** Definition: The zero-divisor conjecture for group algebras of Kaplansky asserts absence of zero divisors over torsion-free groups. Conjecture: Residually finite torsion-free groups of rank two satisfy the conjecture; and the verification is settled through the Malcev–Neumann series field. (Kaplansky via Malcev-Neumann, rank two)

**中文。** 定义：群代数零因子猜想为 Kaplanky 挠自由无零因子。猜想：秩二的残差有限挠自由群满足猜想；且验证的核为 Malcev–Neumann 级数域。（Kaplansky-Malcev-Neumann 秩二验证）
