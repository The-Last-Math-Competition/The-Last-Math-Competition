# 00000000436

**English.** Definition: The Kashaev invariant of a torus knot is a hypergeometric-type q-series. Conjecture: This series never vanishes inside the unit disk; and the connection between nonvanishing and the volume conjecture is given by the leading asymptotic term. (Kashaev series nonvanishing)

**中文。** 定义：环面结的 Kashaev 不变量为超几何型 q-级数。猜想：该级数在单位圆内恒无零点；且无零点性与体积猜想的连接由渐近首项给出。（Kashaev 级数无零点）
