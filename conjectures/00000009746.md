**MAD-16 ★** — source: 310_数学的自动发现.md

**English.** Definition: Interestingness measures of automated conjectures: significance quantification of machine-generated conjectures. Conjecture: Interestingness is negatively linear in the logarithm of proof length; the constant slope is universal and stable across domains, given by the information-theoretic trade-off between logical depth and information content of the conjecture. (interestingness proof length law)

**中文。** 定义：自动猜想的兴趣度度量：机器生成猜想的显著性量化。猜想：兴趣度与证明长度的对数呈负线性关系；常数斜率为普适值且跨领域稳定，由猜想的逻辑深度与信息含量的信息论折衷给出。（兴趣度-证明长度律）
