# 00000003978

**English.** Definition: The Lovasz-Schrijver rank is the number of rounds of lifting of the semidefinite cone. Conjecture: The LS rank lower bound for unsatisfiable formulas equals the vertex expansion number minus 2, the conversion constant 2 is optimal, and the extremal examples are complete bipartite cut graphs. (LS rank-expansion conversion)

**中文。** 定义：Lovasz–Schrijver rank 指半定锥逐轮提升的轮数。猜想：不可满足公式的 LS rank 下界为顶点扩张数减 2,换算常数 2 最优,且极值例为完全二部剖分图。（LS rank-扩张换算）
