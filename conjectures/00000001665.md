# 00000001665

**GT-05 ◆** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: The burning number b(G) of a graph G is the minimal number of rounds needed to burn all vertices progressively. Conjecture: b(G) ≤ ⌈√n⌉ + 1 (paths attain ⌈√n⌉); the characterization of tight examples: Cartesian products of the Petersen graph with paths have burning number ⌈√n⌉ + 1, with no higher-order tight family.

**中文。** 定义：图 G 的燃烧数 b(G) 指逐步点燃顶点使全图燃尽的最少轮数。猜想：b(G)≤⌈√n⌉+1(路径达到 ⌈√n⌉);紧例的刻画:Petersen 图与路径的笛卡尔积使燃烧数为 ⌈√n⌉+1,无更高阶的紧族。
