# 00000003010

**GD-30 △** — source: 85_微分方程的Galois理论.md

**English.** Definition: Differential valuation theory: valuations and extensions over differential fields. Conjecture: The extension theorem for differential valuations: a differential valuation extends to algebraic differential extensions, with the number of extensions the count of differential prime ideals; the ramification index of an extension is the valuation gradient of the derivation. (differential valuation extension ramification index)

**中文。** 定义：微分赋值论：微分域上的赋值与延拓。猜想：微分赋值的延拓定理：微分赋值可延拓到代数微分扩张且延拓的个数为微分素理想的计数；延拓的分歧指标为导子的赋值梯度。（微分赋值延拓分歧指标）
