# 00000004156

**English.** Definition: The Weyl exponent is the decay exponent of the uniform bound of the Weyl sum. Conjecture: The uniform-bound exponent for degree-k polynomial sums can always be improved to 1 - 1/(k(k-1)), and this exponent cannot be compressed further on the optimal arcs. (reciprocal-quadratic law of the Weyl exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl 指数指 Weyl 和一致界的衰减指数。猜想：k 次多项式和的一致界指数恒可改进到一减 k(k−1) 分之一；且该指数在最优弧上不可再压。（Weyl 指数倒数二次律）
