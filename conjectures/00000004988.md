# 00000004988

**English.** Definition: The restricted isometry property is the matrix condition of sparse recovery. Conjecture: The satisfying construction of polynomial compressed sensing is given by an explicit random matrix on nodes, with column count a logarithmic polynomial of the sparsity and constant at most two. (explicit polynomial RIP construction)

**中文。** 定义：有限等距性质指稀疏恢复的矩阵条件。猜想：多项式压缩感知的满足构造由节点上的显式随机矩阵给出,列数为稀疏度的对数多项式且常数不超二。（多项式有限等距显式构造）
