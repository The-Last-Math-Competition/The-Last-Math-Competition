# 00000008628

**INP-08 ★** — source: 273_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: height transition kernel of universal limit process; initial data of KPZ fixed point: 1:2:3 universality class. Conjecture: height point distribution is Airy cosine kernel Fredholm determinant and asymptotic of determinant is exponent of Tracy–Widom GUE distribution; kernel is positive of three one; determinant of time is Neumann series of deterministic kernel and series leading term coefficient is 1. (KPZ Airy cosine kernel)

**中文。** 定义：KPZ 不动点：1:2:3 普适类的普适时空极限过程；窄楔初值的高度转移核。猜想：高度单点分布为 Airy 余弦核 Fredholm 行列式且行列式的长时渐近为 Tracy–Widom GUE 分布；核的尺度指数为时间的三分之一；行列式的修正为确定核的 Neumann 级数且级数首项系数为 1。（KPZ 窄楔 Airy 余弦核）
