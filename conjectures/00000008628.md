# 00000008628

**English.** Definition: The KPZ fixed point: the universal space-time limit process of the 1:2:3 universality class; the height transition kernel for narrow-wedge initial data. Conjecture: The one-point height distribution is a Fredholm determinant of the Airy cosine kernel whose long-time asymptotics is the Tracy-Widom GUE distribution; the scaling exponent of the kernel is one third of time; and the correction of the determinant is a Neumann series of a deterministic kernel with leading coefficient 1. (KPZ narrow wedge Airy cosine kernel)

**中文。** 定义：KPZ 不动点：1:2:3 普适类的普适时空极限过程；窄楔初值的高度转移核。猜想：高度单点分布为 Airy 余弦核 Fredholm 行列式且行列式的长时渐近为 Tracy–Widom GUE 分布；核的尺度指数为时间的三分之一；行列式的修正为确定核的 Neumann 级数且级数首项系数为 1。（KPZ 窄楔 Airy 余弦核）
