# 00000009015

**MFH-05 ★** — source: 286_半整权模形式.md

**English.** Definition: The Cohen-Eisenstein series: the series of weight k + 1/2. Conjecture: The class number formula of its coefficients extends to and closes on totally real fields; and the combination of the closure is the discriminant product. (Cohen class number totally real discriminant product)

**中文。** 定义：Cohen–Eisenstein 级数为权 k+1/2 的级数。猜想：系数的类数公式推广到全实域且封闭；且封闭的组合为判别式积。（Cohen 类数全实域判别积）
