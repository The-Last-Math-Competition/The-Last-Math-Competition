**English.** Definition: Cohen–Eisenstein series is series of weight k+1/2. Conjecture: class number formula real field of coefficient and closed; and combinatorial of closed is discriminant product. (Cohen class number field product)

**中文。** 定义：Cohen–Eisenstein 级数为权 k+1/2 的级数。猜想：系数的类数公式推广到全实域且封闭；且封闭的组合为判别式积。（Cohen 类数全实域判别积）
