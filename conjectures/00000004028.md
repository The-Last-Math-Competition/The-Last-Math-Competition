**RP2-28 ★** — source: 119_随机分析II_粗路径.md

**English.** Definition: The jump count of a flow is the number of jumps of the derivative of the signature. Conjecture: The expected jump count of a stationary flow is an explicit functional of the spectral density; it is finite for jump-weight exponent p >= 1 and diverges for p < 1, with the threshold 1 optimal. (threshold of expected flow jumps)

**中文。** 定义：流的跳跃计数指签名导数的跳变次数。猜想：平稳流的跳跃期望为谱密度的显式泛函,在跳权指数 p ≥ 1 有限而 p < 1 发散,阈值 1 最优。（流跳跃期望阈值）
