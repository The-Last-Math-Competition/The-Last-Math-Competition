**OTW-05 ★** — source: 275_最优传输与Wasserstein几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Monge-Ampere equation is the degenerate elliptic equation det D^2 u = f/g composed with grad u. Conjecture: The regularity threshold of Alexandrov solutions is C^{1,alpha}; and the combination of the threshold is the support density. (Monge-Ampere support density C1alpha threshold)

**中文。** 定义：Monge–Ampère 方程为退化椭圆方程 det D²u=f/g∘∇u。猜想：Alexandrov 解的正则性门槛在 C^{1,α} 上；且门槛的组合为支撑密度。（MA-支撑密度 C1a 正则门槛）
