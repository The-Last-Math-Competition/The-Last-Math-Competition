# 00000004144

**English.** Definition: A Wadge degree is an equivalence class under continuous reduction, and a level automorphism is a layer-preserving bijection. Conjecture: The maximal automorphism group of a Wadge level always has order 2, generated by the dual-complement operation, and this agrees in the AD^+ world and in the axiom-of-choice world. (order 2 of Wadge level automorphisms)

**中文。** 定义：Wadge 度指连续归约下的等价类,层自同构指保层的双射。猜想：Wadge 层的极大自同构群的阶恒为 2,由对偶补运算生成,在 AD⁺ 与选择公理两世界一致。（Wadge 层自同构阶 2）
