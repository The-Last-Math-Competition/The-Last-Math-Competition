# 00000005700

**English.** Definition: The ring and the category are two structural layers. Conjecture: There exist two categories with isomorphic rings but non-equivalent categories, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same fusion rules but different braidings. (same ring-different category braid separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：环与范畴指两层结构。猜想：存在两范畴的环同构而范畴不等价,分离由融合规则同辫不同的显式对实现。（环同-范畴异辫差分离对）
