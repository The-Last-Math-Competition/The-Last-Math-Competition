# 00000008809

**English.** Definition: The alternating direction method of multipliers ADMM for two-block convex optimization min f(x) + g(z) subject to Ax + Bz = c: alternating subproblem solves with multiplier ascent on the quadratic augmented term. Conjecture: In the two-block case ADMM always converges to the solution set for closed proper convex objectives with residual O(1/k); for three or more blocks there exist explicit divergent linear operator instances; the minimal fix is random block ordering or Gauss-Southwell block selection, with extra iteration cost at most (1 - 1/s) times (s the number of blocks); and linearized ADMM with step-size product alpha beta converges if and only if alpha beta lies in the open interval (0,2). (ADMM two-block three-block divergence randomized fix)

**中文。** 定义：两块凸优化 min f(x)+g(z) 约束 Ax+Bz=c 的交替方向乘子法 ADMM：子问题交替求解与二次增广项的乘子上升。猜想：两块情形 ADMM 对闭真凸目标恒收敛到解集且残差为 O(1/k)；三块以上存在显式发散的线性算子实例；最小修复为随机块序或 Gauss–Southwell 选块，其额外迭代代价不超过 (1-1/s) 倍（s 为块数）；线性化 ADMM 的步长积 αβ 达到收敛当且仅当 αβ 落在开区间 (0,2) 内。
