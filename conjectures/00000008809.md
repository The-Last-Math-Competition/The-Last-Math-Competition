**English.** Definition: alternating direction multiplier method ADMM: problem intersection solution of two block convex optimization min f(x)+g(z) constraint Ax+Bz=c and multiplier on of quadratic term. Conjecture: two block ADMM pair closed genuine convex objective always converges to solution set and residual is linear operator; most of O(1/k); three block on exists explicit diverges is random block or Gauss–Southwell block, its iteration cost does not exceed (1-1/s) times (s is step size product αβ of block number); linearization ADMM attains convergent if and only if αβ lies in open interval (0,2) in.

**中文。** 定义：两块凸优化 min f(x)+g(z) 约束 Ax+Bz=c 的交替方向乘子法 ADMM：子问题交替求解与二次增广项的乘子上升。猜想：两块情形 ADMM 对闭真凸目标恒收敛到解集且残差为 O(1/k)；三块以上存在显式发散的线性算子实例；最小修复为随机块序或 Gauss–Southwell 选块，其额外迭代代价不超过 (1-1/s) 倍（s 为块数）；线性化 ADMM 的步长积 αβ 达到收敛当且仅当 αβ 落在开区间 (0,2) 内。
