# 00000009719

**English.** Definition: Non-blowup of mass-supercritical defocusing NLS: global existence below the energy dimension. Conjecture: All finite-energy solutions of mass-supercritical defocusing NLS exist globally, and blowup is impossible; the proof path of non-blowup combines coercivity of fourth-order Morawetz identities with local energy conservation. (supercritical non-blowup)

**中文。** 定义：质量超临界 defocusing NLS 的无爆破性：能量之下维数的整体存在。猜想：一切质量超临界 defocusing NLS 的有限能量解整体存在且爆破不可能；无爆破性的证明路径由四阶 Morawetz 恒等式的强制性与局部能量守恒的联合实现。（超临界无爆破）
