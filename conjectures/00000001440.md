# 00000001440

**MC-20 ★** — source: 33_概率论II_马氏链与粒子系统.md

**English.** Conjecture: On random regular graphs, the energy density of zero-temperature Ising converges to an explicit limit with Gaussian fluctuations (the zero-temperature limit); and the variance of the Gaussian fluctuations is exactly the strictly convex derivative of the energy density.

**中文。** 猜想：随机正则图上零温 Ising 的能量密度收敛于显式极限且涨落为高斯(零温极限)；且高斯涨落的方差恰为能量密度的严格凸导数。
