# 00000008509

**SDG-09 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: The differential version of the Jacobian conjecture concerns polynomial maps whose partial vector fields have index 1. Conjecture: Counterexamples to the differential Jacobian type problem require dimension at least 4; and in dimension two the differential Jacobian problem is equivalent to the classical Jacobian conjecture. (differential Jacobian dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobian 猜想的微分版本指 ∂ 向量场的指数为 1 的多项式映射。猜想：微分 Jacobian 型问题的反例维数 ≥ 4；且二维时微分 Jacobian 问题等价于经典 Jacobian 猜想。（微分 Jacobian 维数）
