**KZ2-06 ◆** — source: 183_KPZII.md

**English.** Definition: The fixed point is the limit space-time process of the universality class, the KPZ fixed point. Conjecture: For lattice models the local time is of subprocess type, the formula closed by the space-time scaling constant; the Holder exponent of the local time is space 1/2 minus a universal correction; and the scaling constant is the same for all nearest-neighbor models, the sameness being evidence of universality. (local space-time scaling of the fixed point)

**中文。** 定义：不动点：普适类的极限时空过程（KPZ 不动点）。猜想：点格模型情形局部时间为子过程型且公式由空时标度常数封闭；局部时的 Holder 指数为空间 1/2 减且减量为普适修正；标度常数在一切紧邻模型上相同且相同性为普适的证据。（不动点局域时空标度）
