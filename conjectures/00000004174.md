**CM3-24 ◆** — source: 124_解析数论III_圆法.md

**English.** Definition: The Vinogradov system is the system of two-sided congruences in the variables and their k-th powers. Conjecture: The equality configuration of the solution-count upper bound consists only of the main diagonal and its permutations, and any off-diagonal configuration lowers the count by at least one order. (uniqueness of the Vinogradov equality configuration)

**中文。** 定义：Vinogradov 系统指变量与其 k 次幂的双边同余方程组。猜想：解计数上界的等号构型仅由主对角及其置换构成,任何次对角构型使计数至少下降一阶。（Vinogradov 等号构型唯一）
