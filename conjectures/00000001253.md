# 00000001253

**CG-53 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Ballistic annihilation A+A → ∅ is the annihilation process of counter-propagating particles. Conjecture: The surviving density for two-velocity distributions is t^{-1/2} with a constant correction; and the correction constant is given by the expansion of the characteristic function of the velocity distribution at zero. (ballistic annihilation survival exponent)

**中文。** 定义：弹道湮灭 A+A→∅ 为相向粒子的湮灭过程。猜想：双速度分布的幸存密度为 t^{-1/2} 的常数修正；且修正常数由速度分布的特征函数在零点的展开给出。（弹道湮灭幸存指数）
