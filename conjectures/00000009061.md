# 00000009061

**English.** Definition: Fenchel-Borsuk: total curvature. Conjecture: The lower bound 2 pi on the total curvature of closed curves holds, and the Borsuk lower bound 4 pi for closed surfaces is uniquely attained. (Fenchel-Borsuk lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Fenchel-Borsuk 指全曲率。猜想：其闭曲线的全曲率下界 2π 且闭曲面的 Borsuk 下界 4π 唯一达到。（Fenchel-Borsuk 下界）
