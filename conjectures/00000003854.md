**English.** Definition: Rigidity denotes the optimal order of deviation of the j-th eigenvalue (counted from the spectral edge) from its classical location. Conjecture: The optimal rigidity bound for the quadratic-potential β-gas is N^{−2/3}j^{−1/6} times (log N)^{1/2}, with the logarithmic factor irremovable. (beta rigidity logarithmic optimality)

**中文。** 定义：刚性指第 j 个(自谱端数起)特征值与其经典位置的最优偏差量级。猜想：二次位势 β-气的刚性最优界为 N^{−2/3}j^{−1/6} 乘 (log N)^{1/2},其中对数因子不可去除。（β-刚性的对数最优）
