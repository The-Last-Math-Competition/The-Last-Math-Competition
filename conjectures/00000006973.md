# 00000006973

**English.** Definition: The direct method with the perturbation expansion of nonlinear transport equations. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the nonlinear correction order and the quadratic leading coefficient is explicit; the coefficient is the bilinear form of scattering data; and the correction order is integer powers of 1/t. (quadratic leading coefficient of linearized corrections)

**中文。** 定义：直接法：非线性传输方程的扰动展开。猜想：换算常数为非线性修正阶的显式且二次首系数显式；系数为散射数据的双线性型；修正的阶为 1/t 的整数幂。（线性化修正二次首系数换算）
