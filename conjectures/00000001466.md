# 00000001466

**English.** Definition: Bregman divergences. Conjecture: The correction term of the optimal triangle-type inequality for Bregman divergences is given explicitly by the dual KL divergence (the Bregman triangle correction term); and the correction degenerates to exactly zero for exponential-family divergences.

**中文。** 定义：Bregman 散度。猜想：Bregman 散度的最优三角型不等式的修正项由对偶 KL 散度显式给出(Bregman 三角修正项)；且修正项在指数族散度下恰退化为零。
