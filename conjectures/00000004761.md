**English.** Definition: The Mobius function is the inverse zeta function of the lattice of noncrossing partitions. Conjecture: The extension of its summation identity is an explicit signed sum over nested cycles, the sign is the two-valued parity of cycle lengths, and the total sum is always zero. (parity zero-sum of NC Mobius cycles)
