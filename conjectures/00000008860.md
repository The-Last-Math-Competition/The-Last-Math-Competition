# 00000008860

**English.** Definition: Primal-dual splitting of saddle point problems. Conjecture: The optimal window of the multiplier step size is always [1/sqrt L, 1]; and the convergence rate within the window is exactly O(1/t). (multiplier step optimal window)

**中文。** 定义：鞍点问题的原始对偶分裂。猜想：乘子步长的最优窗恒为 [1/√L, 1]；且窗口内的收敛率恰为 O(1/t)。（乘子步长最优窗）
