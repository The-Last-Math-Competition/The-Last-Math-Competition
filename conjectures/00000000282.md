# 00000000282

**English.** Definition: h(a) is the topological entropy of the real quadratic family f_a(x) = ax(1−x), a ∈ [0,4]. Conjecture: As a function of a, h(a) is monotonically nondecreasing on the interval from 1+√5 to 4 (the entropy-equals-growth-rate identity of Lyubich is proven; monotonicity for the whole family remains open).

**中文。** 定义：实二次映射族 f_a(x) = ax(1−x), a ∈ [0,4] 的拓扑熵 h(a)。猜想：h(a) 作为 a 的函数在从 1+√5 到 4 的区间上为单调不减(H 恒等式已证熵=增长率的 Lyubich 公式;单调性对整族仍开放)。
