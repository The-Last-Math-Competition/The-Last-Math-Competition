**English.** Definition: The cyclotomic trace is the trace map to topological Hochschild homology. Conjecture: The localized sheaf is homotopy equivalent in the complete case, the kernel of the equivalence being the completion difference, and the kernel of the completion difference being the torsion part of the p-adic completion. (localized completion equivalence of the cyclotomic trace)

**中文。** 定义：分圆迹为到拓扑 Hochschild 的迹映射。猜想：相对化层在完备情形同伦等价；且等价的核为完备化差；且完备化差的核为 p 进完备的挠部分。（分圆迹相对化完备层等价）
