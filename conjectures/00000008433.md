# 00000008433

**DFG-23 ★** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The Wilson asymptotics of t-designs: their existence for large v. Conjecture: Once v is at least exp(exp(t)), existence is decided solely by the divisibility conditions; and the bound can be improved to v at least t^{ct} with c an absolute constant. (improvement of the Wilson existence bound)

**中文。** 定义：t-设计的Wilson 渐近指其在大 v 下的存在。猜想：v ≥ exp(exp(t)) 后存在性仅由整除条件决定；界可改进为 v ≥ t^{ct} 且 c 为绝对常数。（Wilson 存在界改进）
