# 00000001418

**English.** Definition: HH^*(C^*(X)) is the Hochschild cohomology of the cohomological dg algebra, with E₂-structure being the Gerstenhaber structure of the center action. Conjecture: The equality dim HH^{even} − dim HH^{odd} = χ(LX) holds between the dimensions of HH^*(C^*(X)) and those of the minimal model of the Koszul dual in rational homotopy; the equality holds for all formal spaces.

**中文。** 定义：HH^*(C^*(X)) 为上同调 dg 代数的 Hochschild 上同调,其 E₂-结构指中心作用的 Gerstenhaber 结构。猜想：dim HH^*(C^*(X)) 与 rational homotopy 的 Koszul 对偶的极小模型的维数满足等式 dim HH^even − dim HH^odd = χ(LX);该等式对一切形式空间成立。
