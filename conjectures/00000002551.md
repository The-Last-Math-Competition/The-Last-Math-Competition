# 00000002551

**English.** Definition: Density jumps (Besicovitch): the gap between upper density and lower limit. Conjecture: The measure supported on the set where upper and lower densities differ is zero; the packing dimension of the jump set can reach full dimension, and the two-density gap is controlled by the non-planarity index of the tangent measures. (density jump packing dimension)

**中文。** 定义：密度跃迁（Besicovitch）：上密度与下极限的间隙。猜想：上密度与下密度不相等的点集支撑的测度为零；跃迁集的 packing 维数可达满维且两密度间隙由切测度的非平面性指标控制。（密度跃迁 packing 维）
