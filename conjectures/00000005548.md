# 00000005548

**DM2-18 ★** — source: 170_表示论的几何II_D模.md

**English.** Definition: Twisted D-modules are module structures with line-bundle twists. Conjecture: The classification of twisted modules is always given by the explicit function of the Chern classes of the twisting line bundles; it is closed at Picard rank two, with rigidity preserved. (Picard-rank-two classification of twisted modules by Chern classes)

**中文。** 定义：扭 D-模指带线丛扭转的模结构。猜想：扭模的分类恒由扭转线丛陈类的显式函数给出；皮卡秩二时分类封闭且刚性保持不变。（扭模陈类皮卡二分类）
