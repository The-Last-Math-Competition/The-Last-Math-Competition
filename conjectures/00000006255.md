# 00000006255

**English.** Definition: Upper semicontinuity is the upper convergence of entropy for invariant measures, with the exceptional structure of divergent systems. Conjecture: The exceptions of divergent systems are controlled by finite critical orbits, the combination of the exceptions being atomic measures; the count of atomic measures is the cardinality of the postcritical set, finite; and the limit of exceptions is period-orbit equidistribution. (critical-orbit atomic control of divergent exceptions)

**中文。** 定义：上半连续性：熵对不变测度的上半收敛；散度系统的例外结构。猜想：散度系统的例外由临界轨道有限控制且例外的组合为原子测度；原子测度的个数为后临界集的基数且基数有限；例外的极限为周期轨等分布。（散度例外临界轨道原子控制）
