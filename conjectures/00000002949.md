**English.** Definition: The interpolation property of fragments of arithmetic: the Craig interpolation theorem. Conjecture: The stratification of fragment interpolation: the interpolation property of Idelta_0 + exp is equivalently characterized by the folding property of countably saturated models, and the stratification boundary is closedness under modular multiplication. (fragment interpolation stratification boundary)
