**English.** Definition: The Tsfasman–Vladut–Zink bound: the Gilbert–Varshamov breakthrough for AG codes. Conjecture: The saturation genus of the TVZ breakthrough: the minimal genus-to-length ratio needed for the breakthrough is the explicit value of the reciprocal of √q − 1, and the moduli-space dimension of saturating curves is counted by connected components of Hurwitz spaces. (TVZ saturation genus moduli space)
