# 00000001597

**IS-27 ★** — source: 38_可积系统与精确可解模型.md

**English.** Definition: Inhomogeneous Toda (with weights). Conjecture: The recursion coefficients of the Toda orbit of the Freud weight exp(−|x|^m) are asymptotically n^{1/m}·(1 + c·n^{−m/2} log n) with an explicit correction (the Freud correction).

**中文。** 定义：非齐次 Toda(权重)。猜想：Freud 权重 exp(−|x|^m) 的 Toda 轨道的递推系数渐近为 n^{1/m}·(1 + c·n^{−m/2} log n) 的显式修正(Freud 修正)。
