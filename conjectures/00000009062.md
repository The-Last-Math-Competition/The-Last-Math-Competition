# 00000009062

**English.** Definition: Crofton-type formulas: projection counting of submanifolds. Conjecture: The counting of average projections is always an invariant integral; and the measure of the integral is the affine Grassmannian. (Crofton submanifold invariant integral)

**中文。** 定义：Crofton 型公式为子流形投影计数。猜想：平均投影的计数恒为不变量积分；且积分的测度为仿射 Grassmann。（crofton 子流形不变量积分）
