# 00000007193

**SC6-13 ★** — source: 225_概形论II.md

**English.** Definition: Formal schemes with the geometry of completions. Conjecture: The equivalence of coherent sheaves on formal schemes with convergent sheaves holds, and the bridge of the equivalence is completion. (completion-bridged formal GAGA)

**中文。** 定义：形式概形：完备化的几何。猜想：形式 GAGA：形式概形的凝聚层与收敛层的等价且价的桥为完备化。（形式 GAGA 收敛完备凝聚等价桥）
