**TCH-22 ★** — source: 304_Teichmuller动力.md

**English.** Definition: The connected components of quadratic differential strata Q(d_1,...,d_n) are classified by Lanneau into at most three (principal, hyperelliptic, exceptional); a unified form of the existence criterion for hyperelliptic components is incomplete. Conjecture: A Q stratum contains a hyperelliptic component if and only if the congruence class of the singularity order sequence mod 4 lies in an explicit finite table; the total number of exceptional components is finite, with the last exceptional stratum within genus 6. (Q component congruence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：二次微分 stratum Q(d_1,…,d_n) 的连通分量由 Lanneau 分类为至多三个（主、双曲、例外）；双曲分量的存在判据的统一形式未完成。猜想：Q stratum 含双曲分量当且仅当奇点阶数序列模 4 的同余类落在显式有限表中；例外分量的总数有限且最后一个例外 stratum 在亏格 6 内。（Q 分量同余判据）
