# 00000003576

**English.** Definition: Differential geometry on Banach spaces: connection structures. Conjecture: The flatness of Banach connections: the monodromy of flat connections is linear holonomy, with the group of holonomy abelian. (Banach flat linear holonomy abelian group)

**中文。** 定义：Banach 空间上的微分几何：联络结构。猜想：Banach 联络的平坦：平坦联络的单值为线性 holonomy 且 holonomy 的群为交换。（Banach 平坦线性 holonomy 交换群）
