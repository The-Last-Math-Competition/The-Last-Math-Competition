# 00000005240

**English.** Definition: The tautological ring and the total cohomology are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two moduli spaces with isomorphic tautological rings but non-isomorphic total cohomology, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of classes tautologically trivial but globally nontrivial. (same tautological-different global class separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：重言环与整体上同调指两层。猜想：存在两模空间的重言环同构而整体上同调不同构,分离由重言平凡整体非平凡类的显式对实现。（重言同-整体异类分离对）
