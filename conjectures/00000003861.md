# 00000003861

**RM3-11 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The Jack structure constants c_{λμ}^{ν}(α) are the coefficients in the expansion J_λ·J_μ = Σc_{λμ}^{ν}(α)J_ν. Conjecture: All structure constants are rational functions of α whose reduced denominators have all prime factors at most the total box count of the partition |ν|. (Jack structure constant denominator bound)

**中文。** 定义：Jack 多项式结构常数 c_{λμ}^{ν}(α) 指展开 J_λ·J_μ = Σc_{λμ}^{ν}(α)J_ν 的系数。猜想：一切结构常数为 α 的有理函数,其既约分母的素因子均不超过分拆 |ν| 的总盒数。（Jack 结构常数分母界）
