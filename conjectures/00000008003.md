# 00000008003

**DEL-13 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The constant of the Aleksandrov maximum principle for uniformly elliptic equations in non-divergence form: the constant K_A(n) of the sup bound for solutions of det D^2 u >= f. Conjecture: K_A(n) = (n!/n^n)(1 + O(1/n)) (an asymptotic sharpening of the Aleksandrov constant); the constant is attained (asymptotic extremal domains) at parabolic-type domains (strip domains), and the high-dimensional generalization of extremal domains is unique, of Lorentz type (equal-section Lorentz domains); and improvement of the lower bound (minimality of the constant) follows from injectivity of the compactification of equi-contact surfaces in the geometric interpretation of Det(D^2 u). (Aleksandrov constant Lorentz extremality)

**中文。** 定义：非-divergence 形式的均匀椭圆方程的 Aleksandrov 极大值原理的常数：det D²u ≥ f 的解的 sup 界的常数 K_A(n)。猜想：K_A(n) = n!/n^n ·(1+O(1/n))（Aleksandrov 常数的渐近精确化）；且常数的达到（渐近极值区域）为抛物型域（长条域），极值区域的高维推广为 Lorentz 型（等截面的 Lorentz 域）唯一；下界的改进（常数的最小性）由 Det(D²u) 的几何解释的等接触面紧化的单射性给出。
