# 00000009708

**English.** Definition: Soliton resolution for mass-subcritical focusing NLS: solutions below threshold converge to solitons plus radiation. Conjecture: All global solutions below threshold decompose into a soliton plus scattering radiation, with soliton parameter convergence exponent t^{-1/2}; the convergence exponent is explicitly given by the spectral gap of the linearized operator and cannot be improved. (subcritical soliton decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：质量次临界聚焦 NLS 的孤子分辨率：阈值以下的解收敛到孤子加辐射。猜想：阈值以下的一切整体解分解为孤子加散射辐射且孤子参数收敛指数为 t^{−1/2}；收敛指数由线性化算子的谱隙显式给出且不可改进。（次临界孤子分解）
