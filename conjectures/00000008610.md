# 00000008610

**English.** Definition: The Gaussian sequence model is noisy observation of coefficients. Conjecture: The minimax constant of diagonal estimators on l_2 balls is uniquely given by linear shrinkage ordered by eigenvalues; and the hard-thresholding constant for l_0 balls requires a softening correction. (unique diagonal shrinkage estimator)
