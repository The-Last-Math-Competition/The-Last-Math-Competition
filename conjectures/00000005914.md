# 00000005914

**English.** Definition: The collapse radius is the parameter at which the complex contracts to a point set. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit ratio of diameter to perimeter, of half type for isometrically embedded manifolds; the inequality of the ratio is of Puiseux type with coefficient 1/2; and the optimal collapse sequence is given by regular triangulations, with edge-length ratio converging to 1. (diameter-perimeter ratio conversion of collapse)

**中文。** 定义：崩溃半径：复形收缩为点集的参数（collapse）。猜想：换算公式为直径与周长的显式比且等距嵌入流形时为半型值；比值的不等式为 Puiseux 型且系数为 1/2；崩溃的最优序列由正三角剖分给出且剖分的边长比收敛于 1。（崩溃直径周长比换算）
