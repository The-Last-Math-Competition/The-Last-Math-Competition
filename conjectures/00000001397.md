# 00000001397

**HT-07 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: The torsion exponent of unstable homotopy. Conjecture: The odd-primary torsion exponent of π_{n+k}(Sⁿ) is at most p^{k/(2p−2)}, and this is optimal (exponent optimality).

**中文。** 定义：不稳定同伦的挠指数。猜想：π_{n+k}(Sⁿ) 的奇素挠指数 ≤ p^{k/(2p−2)} 的最优性(指数最优性)。
