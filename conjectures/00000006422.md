# 00000006422

**English.** Definition: Discrete symmetric difference is the discrete analogue of sumset structure. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the sumset structure and small sumsets are characterized by dimension; the deficit is the dimension difference; and the minimal positive value of the dimension difference is 1, attained by affine images. (dimension-deficit conversion of small sumsets)

**中文。** 定义：离散对称差：和集结构的离散模拟。猜想：换算常数为和集结构的显式且小和集由维数刻画；亏格为维数差；维数差的极小正值为 1 且正值由仿射像达到。（小和集维数亏格换算）
