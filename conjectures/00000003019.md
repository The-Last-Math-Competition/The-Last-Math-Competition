**English.** Definition: Many-body localization (MBL): strongly disordered nonergodic phases. Conjecture: The stability threshold of MBL: the critical disorder strength for the one-dimensional MBL-to-thermalization transition is finite, and the scale invariance of the critical region is given by Aubry–André-type dual automorphisms. (MBL critical dual automorphism)
