**English.** Definition: algebra growth: Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of torsion-free power zero group and growth function. Conjecture: growth function of torsion-free power zero group algebra is exact power law: growth is power of always rank of group and -fold level term of power law is logarithm period function; period is explicit function of construction constant of lattice. (power zero group growth exact power law)

**中文。** 定义：无挠幂零群的代数增长：Gelfand–Kirillov 维数与增长函数。猜想：无挠幂零群代数的增长函数为精确幂律：增长率为群的总秩的幂且幂律的次级项为对数周期函数；周期为格的构造常数的显式函数。（幂零群增长精确幂律）
