# 00000009995

**English.** Definition: Algebraic growth of torsion-free nilpotent groups: Gelfand-Kirillov dimension and growth functions. Conjecture: The growth function of the group algebra of a torsion-free nilpotent group is an exact power law: the growth rate is a power of the total rank of the group, and the secondary term of the power law is a log-periodic function; the period is an explicit function of the structure constants of the lattice. (nilpotent group growth exact power law)

**中文。** 定义：无挠幂零群的代数增长：Gelfand–Kirillov 维数与增长函数。猜想：无挠幂零群代数的增长函数为精确幂律：增长率为群的总秩的幂且幂律的次级项为对数周期函数；周期为格的构造常数的显式函数。（幂零群增长精确幂律）
