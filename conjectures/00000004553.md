# 00000004553

**English.** Definition: The Skolem-Mahler-Lech theorem is the structure theorem of the set of zero terms of a recurrence. Conjecture: The upper bound of the common-ratio exponent of degenerate subsequences is the recurrence order minus one, and the bound is optimal and attained by binomial sum-difference type sequences. (SML degenerate exponent as order minus one)

**中文。** 定义：Skolem–Mahler–Lech 定理指递推零项集的结构定理。猜想：退化子序列的公比指数上界为递推阶减一,界最优且由二项和差型序列达到。（SML 退化指数阶减一）
