# 00000007613

**English.** Definition: The Dirac operator as first-order differential structure. Conjecture: The square law D^2 equals Laplacian plus curvature term; and the spectrum of the curvature term is the characteristic class. (Dirac square-law curvature characteristic term)

**中文。** 定义：Dirac 算子为一阶微分结构。猜想：平方律 D² 等于 Laplace 加曲率项；且曲率项的谱为示性类。（Dirac 平方律曲率示性项）
