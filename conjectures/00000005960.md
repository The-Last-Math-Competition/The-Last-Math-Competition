**English.** Definition: The spectrum and the eigenvector statistics are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two ensembles with the same eigenvalue spectrum but different eigenvector statistics, and the separation is realized by an explicit free parametrization with the same spectrum but different orientation. (same spectrum-different vector orientation separation)

**中文。** 定义：谱与特征向量统计指两层。猜想：存在两系综的特征值谱相同而特征向量统计不同,分离由同谱异定向的显式自由参数化实现。（谱同-向量统计异定向分离）
