# 00000009121

**English.** Definition: The Furstenberg-Sarkozy problem: square-difference constrained sets. Conjecture: The constant of the metric law of high-dimensional square-difference sets is an explicit density; and the recursion of the density is diagonal decomposition. (square-difference metric constant)

**中文。** 定义：Furstenberg–Sarkozy 问题为平方差约束集。猜想：高维平方差集的度量律的常数为显式密度；且密度的递推为对角分解。（平方差度量常数）
