# 00000001789

**CV-09 ◆** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: Demailly's equisingular convergence conjecture concerns convergence control of sequences of roots of psh functions. Conjecture: For rational exponents the convergence rate is polynomial: ‖u_k^{1/k} − v‖_{L¹} ≤ C·k^{−α}, with α explicit in the denominator of the Q-structure of the exponent.

**中文。** 定义：Demailly 的等奇异收敛猜想指 psh 函数根的序列收敛控制。猜想：指数为有理数时收敛速率为多项式:‖u_k^{1/k}−v‖_{L^1} ≤ C·k^{-α},α 由指数的 Q-结构分母显式。
