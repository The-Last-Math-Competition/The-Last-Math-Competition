# 00000009592

**MSH-12 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The total curvature classification of complete embedded minimal surfaces in R^3: total curvature at most 4 pi classifies planes and catenoids; the classification at larger total curvature is incomplete. Conjecture: Complete embedded minimal surfaces of total curvature at most 8 pi are completely classified as a finite list of planes, catenoids, and three-ended surfaces; and the next member of the list has total curvature exactly 12 pi with three ends. (total curvature classification extension)

**中文。** 定义：R^3 中完备嵌入极小曲面的全曲率分类：全曲率 ≤4π 分类为平面与悬链面；更大全曲率的分类未完成。猜想：全曲率 ≤ 8π 的完备嵌入极小曲面完全分类为平面、悬链面与三端曲面的有限列表；列表的下一个成员全曲率恰为 12π 且端数为 3。（全曲率分类扩展）
