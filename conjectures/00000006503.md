**English.** Definition: Stiefel-Whitney numbers with unoriented cobordism. Conjecture: The cobordism of Stiefel-Whitney numbers means the numbers are cobordism invariants, and the combination of the invariance is the cobordism ring. (cobordism-ring invariance of Stiefel-Whitney numbers)

**中文。** 定义：Stiefel–Whitney 数：非定向配边。猜想：S-W 数的配边：数的配边不变且变的组合为 cobordism 环。（Stiefel-Whitney 数非定向配边配边不变组合 S-W 数 cobordism 环数不环配边数不变环）
