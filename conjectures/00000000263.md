# 00000000263

**AA-10 ◆** — source: 16_代数与算术对象.md

**English.** Definition: An n²+1-type prime is a prime of the form one plus a square. Conjecture: There are infinitely many; and the counting function is asymptotically a constant times x^{1/2}/log x. (Landau's fourth problem counting law)

**中文。** 定义：n²加一型素数指形如平方数加一的素数。猜想：有无穷多个；且计数渐近为 x 的二分之一次幂除 logx 的常数倍。（Landau 第四问题计数律）
