# 00000007952

**INT-22 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The integrability breaking of Kelvin-Helmholtz type free boundaries: the linear stability index of interior fluid boundaries with surface tension and the classification of fully integrable (reflectionless) potentials. Conjecture: Reflectionless potentials (in the sense of the potential function of the Lax pair) are of rational decay type (Kay-Moses type); and the integrable initial-value edge (the set of integrable initial data) in the infinite-dimensional initial-value space is a finite-dimensional analytic submanifold whose dimension is the sum of soliton and radiation degrees of freedom; the normal stability index of the edge is the sign-change count of the Wronskian of the Sturm sequence (an integrabilization of the Sturm criterion). (KH reflectionless Sturm edge)

**中文。** 定义：Kelvin–Helmholtz 型自由边界的可积性破坏：带表面张力的流体内边界的线性稳定性指标与全可积（无反射）势的分类。猜想：无反射势（在 Lax 对的势函数意义下）为有理衰减型（Kay–Moses 型）；且自由边界问题的初值边缘（可积初值集）在无穷维初值空间中为有限维解析子流形，其维度为孤子自由度与辐射自由度之和；边缘的法向稳定性指数为 Strum 序列的 Wronskian 的符号变化数（Sturm 判据的可积化）。
