# 00000002864

**English.** Definition: The index of Toeplitz operators: Fredholm theory of symbol restrictions on Hardy spaces. Conjecture: The multiplicativity of the Toeplitz index: the index of a product of symbols is the sum of factor indices, with deviation given by the algebra of connected components of the symbols; the deviation vanishes for simply-connected symbols. (Toeplitz index multiplicativity)

**中文。** 定义：Toeplitz 算子的指标：Hardy 空间上符号限制的 Fredholm 理论。猜想：Toeplitz 指标的乘性：符号的乘积的指标为因子指标之和且和的偏差由符号的连通分量代数给出；偏差的消没为单叶符号。（Toeplitz 指标乘性）
