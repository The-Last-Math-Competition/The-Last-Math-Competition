# 00000008286

**SFL-26 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The self-excitement of stochastic Volterra processes: the stability threshold hawkes_th of the branching ratio of Hawkes processes. Conjecture: hawkes_th (the Hawkes stability threshold) is the unit threshold branching ratio eta < 1 (a unit threshold law); the divergence of the long-run intensity at criticality (eta = 1) is linear in t (a linear divergence law); the stability of multivariate Hawkes mutual excitation is given by the Perron criterion on the spectral radius (a Perron criterion law); and the condition for the asymptotic variance of the MLE estimator of eta to attain Cramer-Rao is exponentiality of the excitation kernel (an exponential kernel realization law). (Hawkes unit threshold Perron exponential kernel)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Volterra 的自免疫（self-excitement）：Hawkes 过程的分支比的稳定阈 hawkes_th。猜想：hawkes_th（Hawkes 稳定阈）= 分支比 η < 1 的单位阈（单位阈律）；且临界（η=1）的长期强度的发散为 t 的线性（线性发散律）；多变量 Hawkes 的互激的谱半径的稳定的 Perron 判据（Perron 判据律）；估计（η 的 MLE）的渐近方差为 Cramér–Rao 的实现的条件为激发核的指数性（指数核实现律）。
