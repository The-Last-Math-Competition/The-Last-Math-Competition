# 00000003224

**SE-04 ★** — source: 93_随机环境的谱理论.md

**English.** Definition: The IDS (integrated density of states) of ergodic operators: eigenvalue counts per unit volume. Conjecture: The Lipschitz regularity of IDS: the IDS is Hölder continuous with exponent an explicit function of the disorder; smoothness saturates at Lifshitz tails. (IDS Lifshitz tail saturation exponent)

**中文。** 定义：遍历算子的 IDS（integrated density of states）：每单位体积的特征值计数。猜想：IDS 的 Lipschitz 正则性：IDS 为 Hölder 连续且指数为无序的显式函数；光滑性在 Lifshitz 尾部饱和。（IDS Lifshitz 尾部饱和指数）
