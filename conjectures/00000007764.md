# 00000007764

**English.** Definition: The Feigenbaum constant delta_F is the limit ratio of period-doubling bifurcation parameter spacings of the quadratic family. Conjecture: delta_F is transcendental; more generally, the set of all nonintegrable universality constants delta_U of real-analytic one-parameter families is algebraically independent over Q, and is algebraically related to the Feigenbaum alpha (scale contraction): alpha and delta_F satisfy a field relation of transcendence degree exactly 2. (transcendence of universality constants)

**中文。** 定义：Feigenbaum 常数 δ_F 为二次族倍周期分岔参数间距的极限比。猜想：δ_F 为超越数；进一步对实解析一参数族的一般倍周期普适性常数 δ_U，一切非可积普适常数的集合在 Q 上代数无关，且与 Feigenbaum α（尺度收缩常数）代数相关：α 与 δ_F 满足一个超越次数恰为 2 的域关系。
