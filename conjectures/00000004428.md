**English.** Definition: The KPZ fixed point is the universal-class limit space-time process. Conjecture: The conversion constant of the space-time covariance for narrow-wedge initial data is of the explicit type 1/24, and the constant is independent of the choice of gravitational normalization and exact. (KPZ space-time covariance normalization invariant)
