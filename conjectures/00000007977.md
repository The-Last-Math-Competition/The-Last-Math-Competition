# 00000007977

**English.** Definition: The symmetrized extremal of the linear programming for code weight counting: the integer gap of the Delsarte LP bound (the ratio of the LP value to the true A(n,d)). Conjecture: The Delsarte LP gap is polynomially bounded (n^{O(1)}), with exact order Theta(n) given by explicit constructions of fractional LP solutions of MRRW second type; the rounding obstruction to integerizing the LP is the Diophantine type of the roots of the Krawtchouk polynomials (the algebraic degree of the roots), lossless rounding (LP = IP) at algebraic degree at least 3, and a Theta(n) gap at degree at most 2 (the elliptic exception). (Delsarte LP Krawtchouk degree gap)

**中文。** 定义：码字的重量计数的线性规划的对称化极值：Delsarte LP 界的整数间隙（LP 值与真实 A(n,d) 的比）。猜想：Delsarte LP 的间隙为多项式有界（n^{O(1)}），且间隙的精确阶为 Θ(n)（由 MRRW 二型的 LP 解的分数性的显式构造给出）；LP 的整数化的舍入障碍为 Krawtchouk 多项式的根的 Diophantine 型（根的代数度），代数度 ≥ 3 时舍入无损（LP=IP），度 ≤ 2 时存在 Θ(n) 间隙（Elliptic 型例外）。
