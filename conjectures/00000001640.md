# 00000001640

**HA-10 ★** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: Broué's abelian defect group conjecture states that the derived category of a block is equivalent, via a bimodule, to that of its Brauer correspondent. Conjecture: For swap 2-blocks with defect group C_p × C_p of rank 2, an explicit bimodule exists, realized by the t-structure on rank-1 sheaves of flag spaces; the construction is uniform for all primes p.

**中文。** 定义：Broué 交换亏群猜想指块的导出范畴与其不动子块导出范畴经双模等价。猜想：亏群为秩 2 循环群 C_p×C_p 的轮换 2-块存在显式双模:由旗空间的秩 1 层的 t-结构实现;该构造对一切素 p 一致。
