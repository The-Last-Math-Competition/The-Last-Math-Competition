# 00000001796

**CV-16 ★** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: The pole-order spectrum of the Green function is the limit order of Green functions in C^n at poles. Conjecture: The spectrum of realizable orders is a closed set containing all values at least 2 together with infinity; every value α in the spectrum is realized by the singularity of a weighted homogeneous hypersurface, constructed via sections of its Newton diagram.

**中文。** 定义：Green 函数的极点阶谱指 C^n 中 Green 函数在极点的极限阶数。猜想：可实现阶数的谱为闭集,含一切不小于 2 的值与无穷;谱中任何值 α 可由加权齐次超曲面的奇性实现,通过其 Newton 图的截面构造。
