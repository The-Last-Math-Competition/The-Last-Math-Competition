# 00000006082

**English.** Definition: The escape rate quantifies the escape along negative-curvature directions. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit power of the minimal negative curvature, negative-quadratic times logarithmic within the smooth window; the window width is the square root of the curvature ratio; and the escape time is at most two times the logarithm, the constant normalized by curvature. (negative-quadratic-times-log escape constant)

**中文。** 定义：逃逸速率：负曲率方向的逃逸量化。猜想：换算常数为最小负曲率的显式幂且光滑窗内为负二次乘对数型；窗的宽度为曲率比的平方根；逃逸时间不超过二乘对数且常数由曲率归一。（逃逸负二次乘对数常数）
