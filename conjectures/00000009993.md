# 00000009993

**English.** Definition: Symbol lengths of Brauer groups: the shortest algebraic expression length of n-torsion classes. Conjecture: The symbol length of n-torsion classes of the Brauer group over a local field does not exceed n minus one, and the bound is tight; tightness is attained by towers of cyclic algebra degrees, and over general fields the symbol length is stratified by coherent sheaf cohomological dimension. (symbol length local bound)

**中文。** 定义：Brauer 群的符号长度：n-挠类的代数表达最短长度。猜想：局部域上 Brauer 群的 n-挠类的符号长度不超过 n 减一且界为紧；紧性由循环代数的次数塔达到且一般域的符号长度按凝聚层上同调维数分层。（符号长度局部界）
