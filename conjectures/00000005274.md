**FL2-14 ★** — source: 161_非线性偏微分II_流体II.md

**English.** Definition: The four-fifths law is the universal turbulence law of third-order structure functions. Conjecture: Its optimal constant is exactly four fifths, the correction term is an explicit integral of the intermittent energy spectrum, and the correction tends to zero with the Reynolds number. (four-fifths constant with intermittent integral correction)

**中文。** 定义：四五律指三阶结构函数的湍流普适律。猜想：其最优常数恰为五分之四,修正项为间歇能谱的显式积分且修正随雷诺数趋于零。（四五律常数间歇积分修正）
