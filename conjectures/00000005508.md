**QN2-08 ★** — source: 169_排队网络的图极限.md

**English.** Definition: The bottleneck is the first station to saturate. Conjecture: The identification criterion is explicit in the minimal element of the residual-capacity vector, given by the lexicographic order of the traffic margins; the number of tied bottlenecks is the multiplicity of the minimal element, at most the logarithm of the station count; and the bottleneck switching count is monotone in the load, with step 1. (traffic-margin lexicographic criterion of bottlenecks)

**中文。** 定义：瓶颈：最先饱和的站点。猜想：识别判据为剩余容量向量的最小元显式且由流量余量的字典序给出；并列瓶颈的个数为最小元的重数且重数不超过站数的对数；瓶颈的切换次数随负载单调且单调步长为 1。（瓶颈流量余量字典判据）
