# 00000006313

**English.** Definition: Doubly stochastic matrices have row and column sums one. Conjecture: The limit support is given by the minimal diagonal cover with linear convergence exponent, the kernel of the exponent being the support lattice. (diagonal-cover exponent of limit supports)

**中文。** 定义：双随机为行列和均为一的矩阵。猜想：极限支撑由极小对角覆盖给出且收敛指数线性；且指数的核为支撑格。（极限支撑对角覆盖指数）
