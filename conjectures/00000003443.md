**English.** Definition: Best rational approximation: Chebyshev rational approximation. Conjecture: The alternation of Chebyshev rationals: the error of the best approximation has n+2 alternating extremal points, with the distribution of points weight-balanced. (rational alternation weight balanced extremal distribution)

**中文。** 定义：最佳有理逼近：Chebyshev 有理逼近。猜想：Chebyshev 有理的交错：最佳逼近的误差的交错极值点为 n+2 且点的分布为权平衡。（有理交错权平衡极值分布）
