# 00000009723

**SCA-23 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Wave operators of massless fields on asymptotically flat backgrounds: Friedlander-type existence and completeness. Conjecture: Wave operators of massless fields on asymptotically flat backgrounds exist and are asymptotically complete; completeness is equivalent to product estimates of the null geometry, and the failure case is exactly the existence of stable clusters of trapped geodesics. (massless wave operator completeness)

**中文。** 定义：渐近平坦无质量场的波算子：Friedlander 型存在性与完备性。猜想：渐近平坦背景无质量场的波算子存在且渐近完备；完备性等价于类零几何的乘积估计且失效情形恰为俘获测地线的稳定簇存在。（无质量波算子完备）
