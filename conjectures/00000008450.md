**KH3-10 ★** — source: 267_纽结不变量与低维拓扑III.md

**English.** Definition: The Milnor invariant mu-bar_{i1...ik} is the length-k homotopy invariant of a link. Conjecture: Milnor invariants of length at most 3 form a complete invariant of links; the first incomplete example at length 4 is the Whitehead-type three-component extension; and the completeness spectrum is closed under the Sato-Levine stratification. (Milnor completeness stratification)

**中文。** 定义：Milnor 不变量 μ̄_{i1…ik} 指链环的长度 k 同伦不变量。猜想：长度 ≤ 3 的 Milnor 不变量为链环完全不变量；长度 4 的第一个非完全例为 Whitehead 型三分量扩展；完全性谱按 Sato–Levine 分层封闭。（Milnor 完全性分层）
