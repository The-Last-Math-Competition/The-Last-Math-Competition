# 00000005340

**LH2-20 △** — source: 163_范畴化不变量_链接同调.md

**English.** Definition: Colored homology and triple grading are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two links whose colored homologies all agree but whose triple gradings differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of outer twists. (same coloring-different triple grading twist separation)

**中文。** 定义：染色同调与三重分次指两层。猜想：存在两链环的全部染色同调相同而三重分次不同,分离由外扭转的显式对实现。（染色同-三重分次异扭转分离）
