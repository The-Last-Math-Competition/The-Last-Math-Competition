# 00000001028

**FG-28 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: A resolvable S(2,4,v) (Kirkman quadruple system) is a design whose blocks partition into parallel classes. Conjecture: There is an explicit threshold v₀ = 316 such that KQS(2,4,v) exist for all v ≡ 4 mod 12 with v ≥ v₀; below v₀ there are exactly 21 exceptional values.

**中文。** 定义：可分解 S(2,4,v)(Kirkman 四元系)指区组可划分为平行类的设计。猜想：存在显式阈值 v₀=316,使一切 v≡4 mod 12 且 v≥v₀ 的 KQS(2,4,v) 存在;v₀ 以下恰有 21 个例外值。
