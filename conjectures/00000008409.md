# 00000008409

**MIB-29 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: Local rigidity on the motivic side: vanishing of the dimension of the motivic deformation space. Conjecture: The motivic deformation space is the dual of Ext^1, and rigidity holds if and only if Ext^1 = 0; rigid motives are direct summands of semisimple objects in the category of motives; and the first non-semisimple rigid motive has weight 2 and is given by the transcendental motive of a K3 surface. (motivic rigidity Ext criterion)

**中文。** 定义：母题侧的局部刚性指母题形变空间的维数零。猜想：母题形变空间为 Ext¹ 的对偶，刚性当且仅当 Ext¹ = 0；刚性母题在母题范畴中为半单对象的直和项；第一个非半单刚性母题的权为 2 且由 K3 曲面的超越母题给出。（母题刚性 Ext 判据）
