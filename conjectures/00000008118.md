# 00000008118

**English.** Definition: The noncommutative minimal model program (nc MMP) for noncommutative varieties: the existence threshold mmp_nc for minimal models in birational noncommutative equivalence classes. Conjecture: The nc MMP runs and terminates within the class of 3-dimensional AS-Gorenstein algebras (a noncommutative version of the termination theorem); the obstruction to uniqueness of flips (the existence of noncommutative flips) is given by the projective geometry of the point module space (a projective-geometry flip duality); and the minimality criterion for 3-dimensional Sklyanin is that its point module space is an elliptic curve (an elliptic minimality criterion), the proof path being iterative termination of semiorthogonal decompositions of exceptional sets in the derived category. (nc MMP Sklyanin elliptic minimality)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的非交换极小模型纲领（nc MMP）：双有理非交换等价类的极小模型的存在阈 mmp_nc。猜想：nc MMP 在 3 维 AS-Gorenstein 的类中运行且终止（终止定理的非交换版）；且翻转的唯一性的障碍（非交换 flip 的存在性）由点模空间的投影几何给出（投影几何-翻转对偶）；3 维 Sklyanin 的极小性判据为其点模空间为椭圆曲线（椭圆极小判据），判据的证明路径为导出范畴的例外集的半正交分解的迭代终止。
