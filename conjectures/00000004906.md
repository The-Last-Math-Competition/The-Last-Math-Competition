# 00000004906

**MD3-06 ★** — source: 149_随机过程III_马氏决策.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal bases of the two-action MDP linear program are the vertex bases of optimal solutions. Conjecture: The count satisfies an explicit Fibonacci-type recursion with initial values from the elementary MDP; the ratio of the characteristic roots of the recursion is the golden ratio and the asymptotic count is of type phi^n; the count recursion of three-action MDPs is of order three with characteristic polynomial irreducible over the rationals. (Fibonacci count of optimal bases)

**中文。** 定义：两动作 MDP 线性规划的最优基：最优解顶点基的计数。猜想：计数满足斐波那契型显式递推且初值由基本 MDP 给出；递推特征根之比为黄金比且渐近计数为 φⁿ 型；三动作 MDP 的计数递推为三阶且特征多项式在有理数域上不可约。（最优基斐波那契计数）
