**English.** Definition: Trivial extension algebras: periodicity of trivial extensions of an algebra by its dual bimodule. Conjecture: A trivial extension algebra is periodic if and only if the original algebra is derived equivalent to the repetitive algebra of a hereditary algebra; the period is the quiver period of the original quiver, and the period sequence is given by the order of the Coxeter transformation. (trivial extension periodicity criterion)
