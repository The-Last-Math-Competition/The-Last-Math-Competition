**English.** Definition: trivial extension algebra: algebra and period of trivial extension of its dual bimodule. Conjecture: trivial extension algebra is period algebra if and only if algebra is algebra; period of equivalent to hereditary algebra is quiver period of quiver and period sequence by order of Coxeter gives. (trivial extension period)
