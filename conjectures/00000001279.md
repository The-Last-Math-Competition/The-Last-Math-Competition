# 00000001279

**English.** Definition: The maximal equicontinuous factor of a substitutive subshift is its maximal group reduction. Conjecture: The dimension of the MEF equals the number of real eigenvalues of the substitution's characteristic space; and the dimension formula is given by the multiplicity of the spectral radius. (MEF dimension formula)

**中文。** 定义：替换子移位的极大等度连续因子为其最大群性简化。猜想：MEF 的维数等于替换特征空间的实特征数；且维数公式由谱半径的重数给出。（MEF 维数公式）
