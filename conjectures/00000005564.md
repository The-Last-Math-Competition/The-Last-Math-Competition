**English.** Definition: A special abelian group carries a designated subgroup chain. Conjecture: The minimal rank is given explicitly by the third-root-of-unity type criterion, and at the prime three the smallest example is an order-nine extension with semidirect-product kernel. (third-root order-nine kernel of special abelian groups)
