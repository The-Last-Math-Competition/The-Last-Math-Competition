# 00000002620

**OL-30 △** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: Face lattices of polytopes: the face poset of a convex polytope. Conjecture: The face lattice of a 3-polytope is determined by its graph; the face lattice of a 4-polytope is jointly recovered from the graph and the middle layer of the dual graph, with a linear-time recovery algorithm. (high-dimensional face lattice recovery)

**中文。** 定义：多面体的面格：凸多面体的面偏序。猜想：三维多面体的面格由图决定；四维多面体的面格由图与对偶图的中间层联合恢复且恢复算法为线性时间。（高维面格恢复）
