**English.** Definition: Kakeya sets contain a segment in every direction. Conjecture: Their Hausdorff dimension in R^n is n, and the Minkowski dimension of Besicovitch sets is n. (Kakeya full dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Kakeya 指含一切方向的集合。猜想：其 Hausdorff 维数在 R^n 为 n 且 Besicovitch 集的 Minkowski 摘为 n。（Kakeya 维数满维）
