# 00000007372

**English.** Definition: Spectral methods with harmonic extension. Conjecture: The superconvergence points of interpolation hold, and the distribution of the points is Gauss type. (Gauss-distributed spectral superconvergence)

**中文。** 定义：谱方法：谐波的延拓。猜想：谱方法的超收敛：插值的超收敛点且点的分布为 Gauss 型。（谱方法 Gauss 型超收敛插值点分布超收敛）
