**English.** Definition: Exact controllability of NS is the minimal time to steer the system to an arbitrary state. Conjecture: The minimal control time for small-data NS is (1+ε)·diam/v_max (the wave speed given by the real part of the Stokes spectrum); the explicit wave speed is λ₁^{-1/2}.

**中文。** 定义：NS 的精确可控性指驱动系统到任意状态的最小时间。猜想：小数据的 NS 的最小控制时间为 (1+ε)·diam·/v_max(波速由 Stokes 谱的实部);显式波速为 λ₁^{-1/2}。
