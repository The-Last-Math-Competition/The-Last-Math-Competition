**PRF-08 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of generic semantics of intuitionistic logic: the branching complexity b(HA) of generic models of the Kripke semantics of intuitionistic arithmetic HA. Conjecture: The minimal branching width of b(HA) is aleph_0 (necessity of infinite branching); realizability of generic models is preserved by the Kripke-to-realizability translation while the reverse fails (one-directionality); quantitatively, there exist realizable statements invalid in Kripke semantics whose minimal examples have Kolmogorov complexity lower-bounded by c log n (a complexity-semantics duality); and the optimal framework for the provable-negation axiomatization of Church's thesis CT_0 is the equivalence of the consistency of the extended intermediate logic KC+CT with the pure logical equivalence of the domain of ECT (a consistency equivalence law). (Kripke realizability one-way complexity duality)

**中文。** 定义：直觉主义逻辑的泛域语义的谱：直觉主义算术 HA 的 Kripke 语义的泛（generic）模型的分支复杂度 b(HA)。猜想：b(HA) 的极小分支宽度为 ℵ₀（无穷分支必要性）；且泛模型的可实现性（Kripke→realizability 的翻译）保持而逆向破坏（单向性）；破坏的定量：存在可实现语句不可 Kripke 有效，其极小例子的 Kolmogorov 复杂度为 c·log n 的下界（复杂度-语义对偶）；Church 论题 CT₀ 的可证否定的公理化的最优框架为扩展的中间逻辑 KC+CT 的协调性等价于 ECT 的域的纯逻辑等价（协调等价律）。
