# 00000000725

**English.** Definition: Leopoldt's conjecture asserts that the p-adic regulator of a number field K is nonzero. Conjecture: For real cyclotomic fields K = Q(ζ_m)^+, Leopoldt at p holds under a criterion explicitly stated via the prime factors of m: it holds automatically when gcd(m, p) = 1 and every prime factor ℓ of φ(m) satisfies ℓ ∤ (p−1).

**中文。** 定义：Leopoldt 猜想断言数域 K 的 p 进调节子非零。猜想：对实循环域 K=Q(ζ_m)^+,Leopoldt 在 p 处成立的充分必要条件由 m 的素因子集显式刻画:当 gcd(m,p)=1 且 φ(m) 的每个素因子 ℓ 满足 ℓ∤(p−1) 时自动成立。
