**CM-07 ★** — source: 71_计算复杂性中的数学.md

**English.** Definition: A read-k branching program reads each variable at most k times. Conjecture: Explicit functions require read-k BP size superpolynomial; and the evidence for the excess is Nechiporuk-type separation. (read-k BP superpolynomial lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：read-k 分支程序为每变量至多读 k 次的程序。猜想：显式函数需 read-k BP 尺寸为超多项式；且超出的证据为 Nechipipuk 型分离。（read-k BP 超多项式下界）
