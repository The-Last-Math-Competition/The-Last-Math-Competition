# 00000001431

**English.** Definition: Internal DLA (IDLA). Conjecture: The fluctuations of IDLA in Z³ are optimally O(log n), and the distribution converges to the tail of the GFF (high-dimensional IDLA fluctuations).

**中文。** 定义：内部 DLA(IDLA)。猜想：IDLA 在 ℤ³ 的涨落为 O(log n) 的最优且分布收敛于 GFF 的尾(高维 IDLA 涨落)。
