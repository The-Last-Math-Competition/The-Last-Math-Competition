**English.** Definition: The weight coupling is the correspondence of growth models with directed polymers, the rho coupling. Conjecture: Under logarithmic scaling the weight coupling preserves the fixed point, the kernel of the preservation being the exponential-geometric interpolation; the error of the interpolation is logarithmically small, of order 1/log t; and the scaling constant of the coupling is equal on the two end models, with zero deviation. (exponential-geometric interpolation of the weight coupling)

**中文。** 定义：权耦合：生长模型与定向聚合的对应（rho 耦合）。猜想：尺度对数化下权耦合保持不动点且保持的核为指数几何插值；插值的误差为对数小量且小量的阶为 1/log t；耦合的标度常数在两端模型上相等且相等偏差为零。（权耦合指数几何插值）
