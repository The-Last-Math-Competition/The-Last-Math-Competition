# 00000001620

**SP-20 ★** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral zeta function of the Cantor string is the analytic continuation of the power sums of its Dirichlet–Neumann frequencies. Conjecture: All residues at its poles equal 1 (coinciding with the poles of the geometric zeta); after renormalization, the difference between spectral and geometric zeta is entire, producing no extra poles.

**中文。** 定义：Cantor 弦的谱 ζ 函数指其 Dirichlet–Neumann 频率的幂和的解析延拓。猜想：其一切极点的残数皆为 1(与几何 ζ 的极点重合);重整化后的谱 ζ 与几何 ζ 之差为整函数,不产生额外极点。
