# 00000007639

**English.** Definition: The Glimm scheme as random-sampling shock tracking. Conjecture: The monotone decrease of the Glimm functional implies convergence; and the estimate of the decrease is the interaction functional. (Glimm interaction-functional monotonicity)

**中文。** 定义：Glimm 格式为随机取样的激波追踪。猜想：Glimm 泛函的单调下降蕴含收敛；且下降量的估计为交互泛函。（Glimm 交互泛函单调）
