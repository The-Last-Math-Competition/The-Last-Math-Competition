# 00000002621

**English.** Definition: Jacobi forms: functions φ(τ,z) on the group J_{k,m} satisfying transformation laws, with both elliptic and modular variables. Conjecture: The dimension formula of integral-weight integral-index Jacobi forms is Zagier's explicit formula; at genus one, the zero-density region of the weight-index dimension table is a quadratic constraint in weight and index, with parabolic boundary. (Jacobi dimension formula zero region)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式：群 J_{k,m} 上满足变换律的函数 φ(τ,z)，兼具椭圆与模变量。猜想：整权整指标 Jacobi 形式的维数公式为 Zagier 显式公式；亏格一时的权-指标维数表的零密度区域为权与指标的二次约束且约束边界为抛物线。（Jacobi 维数公式零区）
