# 00000007615

**English.** Definition: The Hodge star as the duality-map structure. Conjecture: The double-star sign is a power of minus one with exponent a dimension combination; the combination exponent is the product of codimensions; and the sign law gives the self-duality condition in middle dimension with condition codimension one. (exact double-star sign law)

**中文。** 定义：Hodge 星：对偶映射结构。猜想：双星符号为负一的幂且幂的指数为维数组合；组合的指数为余维乘积；符号律在中间维数给出自对偶条件且条件的余维为一。（双星符号负一幂精确律）
