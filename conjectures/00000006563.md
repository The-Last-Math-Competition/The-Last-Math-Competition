**English.** Definition: Time reversibility is the time-reversal symmetric structure. Conjecture: The necessary and sufficient criterion of reversibility is the palindromic coefficient; the criterion is complete and the verification is linear; and the linear verification of the palindromic criterion is coefficient mirroring. (palindromic necessity of time reversibility)

**中文。** 定义：时间可逆为时间反演对称结构。猜想：可逆判据的充要条件为回文系数；且判据完全且验证为线性；且回文判据的线性验证为系数镜像。（时间可逆回文充要）
