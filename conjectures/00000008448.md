# 00000008448

**English.** Definition: The box invariant of a knot is the Mahler-measure type cohomological quantity of its Alexander polynomial. Conjecture: The box invariant is multiplicative under connected sums; the box equals 1 if and only if the Alexander polynomial is the multiplicative unit; and multiplicality fails first on links, the correction term being the linking number. (multiplicativity of the box invariant)

**中文。** 定义：结的 Alexander 多项式的 Mahler 测度型上同调量称为箱不变量。猜想：箱不变量在连通和下可乘；箱 = 1 当且仅当 Alexander 多项式为乘法单位元；乘法性在链环上首次失效且修正项为链环绕数。（箱不变量乘法性）
