**English.** Definition: Aubry-Mather with the minimal orbit structure. Conjecture: The Cantor families of minimal orbits exist, and the rotation numbers of the families fill intervals. (interval-rotation Aubry-Mather Cantor family)

**中文。** 定义：Aubry–Mather：极小轨道结构。猜想：Aubry–Mather 集：极小轨道的 Cantor 族且族的旋转数为区间。（Aubry-Mather Cantor 极小轨道区间旋转数族）
