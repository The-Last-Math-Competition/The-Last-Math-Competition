# 00000002707

**AD2-27 ★** — source: 75_代数动力学_ArzelaZariski.md

**English.** Definition: Orbit structure of cyclic extensions: endomorphisms of polynomial rings. Conjecture: The Zariski closure of a cyclic orbit is a union of algebraic varieties, and the dimension spectrum of the union is the lattice-point stratification of the Newton polygon of the generating polynomial; the height difference of the stratification is the polynomial degree. (cyclic orbit Newton stratification)

**中文。** 定义：循环扩张的轨道结构：多项式环的自同态。猜想：循环轨道的 Zariski 闭包为代数簇的并且并的维数谱为生成多项式的 Newton 多边形的整点分层；分层的高度差为多项式次数。（循环轨道 Newton 分层）
