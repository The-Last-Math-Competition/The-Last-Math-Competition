**English.** Definition: Total-degree approximation is the approximation with total degree as budget. Conjecture: The sparse optimal error exponent of a quintic budget is an explicit fraction of the mixed smoothness, attained by the matching lower bound of the hyperbolic cross. (fractional error exponent of the quintic total-degree budget)
