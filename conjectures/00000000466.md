**SA-66 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The Tutte polynomial at (1,1) is the spanning-tree number, and at (2,0) the number of acyclic orientations. Conjecture: On cubic graph families, the ratio of the number of acyclic orientations to the spanning-tree number concentrates around two raised to the v/2 power times an exponential correction; and the correction constant is Euler's constant. (AO-to-tree ratio concentration law)

**中文。** 定义：Tutte 多项式在 (1,1) 为生成树数且在 (2,0) 为无圈定向数。猜想：无圈定向数与生成树数之比在三正则图族集中于二的 v/2 次幂乘指数修正；且修正常数为欧拉常数。（AO-树比集中律）
