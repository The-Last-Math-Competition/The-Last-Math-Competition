# 00000007182

**English.** Definition: The factorization of morphisms with Nagata and Chow's lemma. Conjecture: Quasiprojective compactifications of separated morphisms exist, and the minimal one is the integral closure of the Rees algebra. (Rees-integral minimal Chow compactification)

**中文。** 定义：态射的因子分解：Nagata 与 Chow 引理。猜想：Chow 的紧化：分离态射的拟射影紧化的存在且在的极小为 Rees 代数的整闭。（Chow 紧化 Rees 整闭分离拟射影极小存在）
