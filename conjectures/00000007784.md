# 00000007784

**English.** Definition: The high-dimensional Sylvester problem: for a convex body K in dimension n with independent uniform samples K_1,...,K_{n+1}, the random simplex volume V = |conv(K_1,...,K_{n+1})|. Conjecture: The infimum of E[V]/|K| over all K is an explicit constant of type ((n+1)/n!)^n c_n, attained asymptotically by the simplex; and for the simplex the distribution of V is an exact Beta-type distribution whose density parameters sum to (n+1)/2 with (1,...,1), giving the log-normal limit center at high dimension. (Sylvester expectation infimum)

**中文。** 定义：Sylvester 问题的高维化：K 为 n 维凸体，K_1,…,K_{n+1} 为独立均匀样点，随机单形体积 V=|conv(K_1,…,K_{n+1})|。猜想：E[V]/|K| 的下确界（遍历一切 K）为 ((n+1)/n!)^{n}·c_n 型显式常数在单纯形渐近达到；且 V 的分布在单纯形情形为 Beta 型精确分布，其密度参数 (1,…,1) 与 (n+1)/2 之和在维数高时给出对数正态极限中心。
