**English.** Definition: high dimension -ization: K of Sylvester problem is n dimension convex body, K_1,…,K_{n+1} is independent all point, random volume V=|conv(K_1,…,K_{n+1})|. Conjecture: infimum (ergodic all K) of E[V]/|K| is ((n+1)/n!)^{n} · c_n type explicit constant in is asymptotically attained; and distribution in of V is Beta type exact distribution, its density parameter (1,…,1) and (n+1)/2 the sum of in dimension gives logarithm positive state limit center.

**中文。** 定义：Sylvester 问题的高维化：K 为 n 维凸体，K_1,…,K_{n+1} 为独立均匀样点，随机单形体积 V=|conv(K_1,…,K_{n+1})|。猜想：E[V]/|K| 的下确界（遍历一切 K）为 ((n+1)/n!)^{n}·c_n 型显式常数在单纯形渐近达到；且 V 的分布在单纯形情形为 Beta 型精确分布，其密度参数 (1,…,1) 与 (n+1)/2 之和在维数高时给出对数正态极限中心。
