**MF-09 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: Skew Jacobi forms: the antisymmetric variant of odd weight. Conjecture: The dimension law of the skew Jacobi ring: the dimension of the antisymmetric space is an explicit combination of the symmetric-space dimension and the half-integral-weight modular dimension; the sign changes of the difference occur only in the weight congruence class 3k−1. (skew dimension law)

**中文。** 定义：skew（反称）Jacobi 形式：奇权的反对称变体。猜想：skew Jacobi 环的维数律：反称空间的维数为对称空间维数与半整权模空间维数之差的显式组合量；维数差的符号变化仅出现在权 3k−1 同余类。（skew 维数律）
