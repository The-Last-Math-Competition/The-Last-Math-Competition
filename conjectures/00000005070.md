**SE2-20 △** — source: 154_随机环境II_去局域化.md

**English.** Definition: Fractional-moment exponents and dynamical rates are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two random systems with the same fractional-moment exponent but different dynamical localization rates, and the separation is realized by an explicit family of sparse potentials. (same fractional moment-different dynamical rate separation)

**中文。** 定义：分数矩指数与动力学速率指两层。猜想：存在两随机系统的分数矩指数相同而动力学局域化速率不同,分离由稀疏位势的显式族实现。（分数矩同-动力学速率异分离）
