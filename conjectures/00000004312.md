**English.** Definition: The class polynomial is the norm polynomial of CM ideals. Conjecture: The coefficient height of the quintic class polynomial is approximately the formula exp times pi times sqrt(d) times the explicit constant c_5, with error of type the reciprocal of a power of d. (quintic constant of class polynomial heights)
