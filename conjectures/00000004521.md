**English.** Definition: The cavity method is the self-consistent message method under the tree approximation, and the complexity zero is the root of the entropy density function. Conjecture: The zero density of random k-SAT always fluctuates according to the central limit, and the variance is given by the second moment of the solution count. (central limit formula of the cavity zero)
