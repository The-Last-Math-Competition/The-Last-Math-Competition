# 00000001853

**RM-13 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The sign distribution of the determinant of a Wigner matrix. Conjecture: The distribution of det(Wigner)/E|det| converges to a Rademacher convolution (a convolution of infinitely many ±1's): the series coefficients are explicit; this limit differs from the sign law of the real Ginibre ensemble.

**中文。** 定义：Wigner 矩阵的行列式符号分布。猜想：det(Wigner)/E|det| 的分布收敛于 Rademacher 卷积(±1 的无限序列卷积):级数展开的系数为显式;该极限不同于实 Ginibre 的符号律。
