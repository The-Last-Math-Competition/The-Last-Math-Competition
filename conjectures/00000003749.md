# 00000003749

**English.** Definition: Ramsey theory of ordinals: ordinal versions of partition relations. Conjecture: The compactness of ordinal arrows: positive limits of ordinal partition relations, with the taking of limits uncountable regulars. (ordinal arrow uncountable regular compactness limit)

**中文。** 定义：序数的 Ramsey 理论：划分关系的序数版。猜想：序数箭头的紧性：序数划分关系的正极限且极限的取法为不可数正则。（序数箭头不可数正则紧性极限）
