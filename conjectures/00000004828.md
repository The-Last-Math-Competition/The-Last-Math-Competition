# 00000004828

**English.** Definition: Eisenstein series are noncuspidal modular forms with divisor-type coefficient functions. Conjecture: The variance of their joint central limit theorem is an explicit power law in one half of the prime exponent, given by the correlation of the divisor kernel with Gaussian limit. (CLT variance power law of Eisenstein divisors)

**中文。** 定义：Eisenstein 级数指非尖模形式,系数为除数型函数。猜想：其系数的联合中心极限定理方差为素数指数二分之一的显式幂律,由除数核相关性给出且极限为高斯。（Eisenstein 除数 CLT 方差幂律）
