**English.** Definition: Defect groups are the local subgroups of prime-power order of a block, with the classification of abelian defect groups. Conjecture: The blocks of abelian defect groups are indexed by rank partitions, the kernel of the index being the fusion-system structure on the block; at rank two the fusion system is one of two (group-realized or exotic), the existence of exotics delimited by odd characteristic; and the partition length is at most the rank of the defect group. (fusion rank-partition indexing of abelian defect)
