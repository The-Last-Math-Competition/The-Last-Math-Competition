**English.** Definition: The contour method with the aggregation correction of Peierls contours. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the convergence radius of the aggregation correction given by the branching deficit; the deficit spectrum closes and the closed recursion is tree counting; and the reciprocal of the radius is the critical temperature. (aggregation convergence branching-deficit conversion)

**中文。** 定义：轮廓法：Peierls 轮廓的聚合修正。猜想：换算常数为聚合修正的收敛半径且由分叉亏格给出；亏谱封闭且封闭的递推为树的计数；半径的倒数为临界温度。（聚合收敛分叉亏谱换算）
