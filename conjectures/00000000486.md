# 00000000486

**SA-86 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The zero-entry proportion z(n) of the character table of S_n is the proportion of cells with χ_λ(μ) = 0. Conjecture: z(n) = 1 − Θ(n^{-1/2}); more precisely 1 − z(n) = c·n^{-1/2} + O(n^{-1}) with c an explicit constant of the form √(2/π)·∫exp(−t²/2)dt; this is a quantitative form of the Miller–Gamburd effect.

**中文。** 定义：S_n 特征标表的 0 项比例 z(n) 指表中 χ_λ(μ)=0 的格子占总数 n!^{…}的比例。猜想：z(n) = 1 − Θ(n^{-1/2}),更精确地 1−z(n) = c·n^{-1/2}+O(n^{-1}),常数 c = √(2/π)·∫exp(−t²/2)dt 型显式;这是 Miller–Gamburd 效应的定量形式。
