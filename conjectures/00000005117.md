**English.** Definition: The condition number is the relative amplification rate of perturbations. Conjecture: The mean of the Frechet condition number on Gaussian matrices is an explicit linear expression of the logarithm of the dimension, consistent with the expected pseudospectral area and asymptotically exact. (log-linear expectation of Frechet condition numbers)

**中文。** 定义：条件数指扰动的相对放大率。猜想：高斯矩阵上弗雷歇条件数的均值为维数对数的显式线性式,与伪谱期望面积一致且渐近精确。（弗雷歇条件数对数线性期望）
