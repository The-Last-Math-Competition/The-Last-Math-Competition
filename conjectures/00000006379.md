# 00000006379

**English.** Definition: The penalty formula with the iterative contraction structure. Conjecture: The contraction rate is of fraction type with tight bound; the numerator of the fraction is the spectral gap; and the numerator of the spectral gap is the discrete inf-sup constant. (tight fraction bound of iterative contraction)

**中文。** 定义：罚公式的迭代收缩结构。猜想：收缩率为分式型且界紧；且分式的分子为谱间隙；且谱间隙的分子为离散 inf-sup 常数。（迭代收缩分式紧界）
