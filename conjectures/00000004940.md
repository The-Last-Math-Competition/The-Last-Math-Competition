# 00000004940

**English.** Definition: Morava K-theory and stable homotopy are two layers of invariants. Conjecture: There exist two spectra whose Morava K-theories are all isomorphic but whose stable homotopy groups differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of spectra with the same K-types but different Adams differentials. (same K-theory-stable homotopy separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：Morava K 同调与稳定同伦指两层不变量。猜想：存在两谱的一切 Morava K 理论同构而稳定同伦群不同,分离由同 K 型异 Adams 微分的显式谱对实现。（K 同调同-稳定同伦分离对）
