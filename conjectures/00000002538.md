# 00000002538

**GZ-08 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Nonhomogeneous restriction theory: weighted versions of restriction estimates for surface measures. Conjecture: The parabolic scaling obstruction of nonhomogeneous restriction: the parabolic-scaling deficit of the restriction exponents is given explicitly by a trade-off formula in the Frostman exponent of the measure; the trade-off curve is piecewise linear at conical singularities with integer breakpoints. (nonhomogeneous trade-off curve)

**中文。** 定义：非齐次限制理论：曲面测度的限制估计的加权版本。猜想：非齐次限制的抛物缩放障碍：限制指数的抛物缩放亏损由测度的 Frostman 指数的折衷公式显式给出；折衷曲线在锥形奇点处为分段线性且折点为整指数。（非齐次折衷曲线）
