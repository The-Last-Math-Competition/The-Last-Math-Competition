# 00000007663

**QDE-03 ◆** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: For the first-order q-difference system Y(qz)=A(z)Y(z), the difference Galois group G_A is the smallest linear algebraic group over C(z) preserving the solution space. Conjecture: If A(z) in Mat_m(C(z)) satisfies |det A(0)|=1 and det A(z) is not a constant power in C(z), then G_A contains SL_m unless A is diagonalizable by a gauge transformation over C(z); in particular for m=2 the irreducible case always gives G_A = GL_2. (difference Galois genericity criterion)

**中文。** 定义：一阶 q-差分方程组 Y(qz)=A(z)Y(z) 的差分 Galois 群 G_A 定义为 C(z) 上保持方程解空间的最小线性代数群。猜想：若 A(z) ∈ Mat_m(C(z)) 满足 |det A(0)|=1 且 det A(z) 非 C(z) 中的常数幂，则 G_A 含 SL_m，除非 A 可经 C(z) 上的规范变换对角化；特别地 m=2 时不可约情形的 G_A 恒为 GL_2。
