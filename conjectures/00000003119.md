# 00000003119

**English.** Definition: Dynamics on the boundary: convergence group actions. Conjecture: The dual rigidity of convergence groups: convergence actions on the boundary are equivalent to hyperbolic groups (Yamasaki), with the combinatorial side of the equivalence the separation of uniformly finite orbits. (convergence duality uniform separation)

**中文。** 定义：边界上的动力学：收敛群作用。猜想：收敛群的对偶刚性：边界的收敛作用与双曲群的等价（Yamasaki）且等价的组合侧为 uniformly 有限轨道的分离。（收敛对偶均匀分离）
