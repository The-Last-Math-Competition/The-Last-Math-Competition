# 00000003993

**English.** Definition: The clique-coclique formula is the contradictory formula asserting the coexistence of a k-clique and an m-independent set. Conjecture: The SOS degree lower bound of this formula is Omega(sqrt k) when k <= sqrt(n)/2, and it matches the upper bound from an explicit spectrally embedded pseudodistribution with equality. (matching clique SOS degree)

**中文。** 定义：clique-coclique 公式指 k 团与 m 独立集不共存的矛盾公式。猜想：该公式在 SOS 的度下界为 Ω(√k)(k ≤ √n/2 时),且与显式谱嵌入伪分布的上界匹配为等号。（clique SOS 度匹配）
