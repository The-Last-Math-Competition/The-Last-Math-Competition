# 00000000913

**OA-13 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The Kirchberg–Phillips classification asserts that purely infinite simple nuclear algebras are completely classified by the K-theoretic pair. Conjecture: The fewest layers of the Elliott invariant needed to extend this classification to general nuclear algebras is three — ordered K₀, K₁, and the trace pairing; with fewer (say K-groups plus traces alone) there is an explicit counterexample pair, constructed by graph pasting of (O₂⊗M₂^{⊕k}).

**中文。** 定义：Kirchberg–Phillips 分类断言纯无限单核代数由 K-理论二元组完全分类。猜想：把该分类推广到一般核代数所需的 Elliott 不变量的最少层数为三层:有序 K₀、K₁ 与迹配对;少于此(如仅 K 群加迹)时存在显式反例对,由 (O₂⊗M₂^{⊕k}) 的图粘合构造。
