# 00000004930

**MD3-30 △** — source: 149_随机过程III_马氏决策.md

**English.** Definition: Discounted value and average reward are two layers of optimality measures. Conjecture: There exist two MDPs with identical values for all discount factors but different average rewards, and the separation is realized by an explicit construction concentrating the discount weights on transients. (same discounted values-average reward separation)

**中文。** 定义：折扣值与平均奖励指两层最优度量。猜想：存在两 MDP 的一切折扣因子值相同而平均奖励不同,分离由折扣权重集中于瞬态的显式构造实现。（折扣全同-平均奖励分离）
