**English.** Definition: The common zeros of ζ′ and ζ are the points in the critical strip where their ordinates coincide. Conjecture: The common zeros of ζ′ and ζ are exactly the multiple zeros of ζ (known to be equivalent to RH); moreover, under RH the gap statistics of the zeros of ζ″ couple with the GUE point process with parameter 1.

**中文。** 定义：ζ′ 的零点与 ζ 的零点的公共点指两者在临界带内虚部重合的点。猜想：ζ′ 与 ζ 的公共零点恰为 ζ 的重零点(已知等价于 RH);进一步,ζ″ 的零点虚部的间隙统计在 RH 下与 GUE 点过程的耦合参数为 1。
