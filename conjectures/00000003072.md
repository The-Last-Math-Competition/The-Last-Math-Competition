# 00000003072

**English.** Definition: Complete Segal spaces: the segmented-condition model of simplicial spaces. Conjecture: Minimality of Rezk completion: complete Segal spaces are the minimal completion of Segal spaces, and the equivalence vanishing of completion occurs at fully discrete objects. (Rezk completion minimality)

**中文。** 定义：完全 Segal 空间：单纯空间的分段条件模型。猜想：Rezk 完备化的极小性：完全 Segal 空间为 Segal 空间的极小完备化且完备化的等价消没在对象完全离散处。（Rezk 完备化极小性）
