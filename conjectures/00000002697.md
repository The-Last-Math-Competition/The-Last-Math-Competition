# 00000002697

**English.** Definition: The dynamical side of Zariski tangent spaces: the evolution of tangent spaces along orbits. Conjecture: The growth law of orbit tangent spaces: the Lyapunov exponent of the norm growth of tangent vectors equals the logarithmic average of the Jacobi spectrum of the map, and the degeneracy of the exponent is the order of the orbit's symmetry group. (orbit tangent space Lyapunov law)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 切空间的动力学侧：切空间沿轨道的演化。猜想：轨道切空间的增长律：切向量的范数增长的 Lyapunov 指数等于映射的 Jacobi 谱的对数平均且指数的简并度为轨道的对称群阶。（轨道切空间 Lyapunov 律）
