# 00000006375

**English.** Definition: Stabilization with the pseudoresidual stabilization parameter of mixed elements. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the optimal domain of the parameter, sharp at both ends in the pseudo-time interpretation; the sharp ends are powers of the mesh size; and the convergence rate within the domain is uniform, independent of the parameter. (sharp two-ended conversion of the optimal parameter domain)

**中文。** 定义：稳定化：混合元的伪残差稳定化参数。猜想：换算常数为参数最优域的显式且伪时间解释的域两端锐利；锐利的两端为网格尺寸的幂；域内的收敛率一致且率与参数无关。（参数最优域两端锐利换算）
