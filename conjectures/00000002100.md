# 00000002100

**SY-20 ★** — source: 55_辛几何与镜像对称.md

**English.** Definition: The scattering diagram of a log CY is the combinatorial object of broken-line structure. Conjecture: The broken-line structure of the scattering diagram is finite (modulo the lattice of Σ); the count of breakpoints is exhausted by the supporting lattice of the walls of the dominating function (a finite table).

**中文。** 定义：log CY 的散射图指断线结构的组合对象。猜想：散射图的断线结构为有限(Σ 模格);断点集的计数由支配函数的 wall 的支撑格穷举(有限表)。
