**English.** Definition: A noncommutative projective space is the geometric quotient structure under a Brauer class. Conjecture: The conversion constant of its classification is the square of the Brauer index, at index two it is realized by explicit quiver geometry, and the conversion is invertible. (index-square conversion of noncommutative projective spaces)
