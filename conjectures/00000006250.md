# 00000006250

**English.** Definition: The shape and the sheaf category are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two toposes with homotopy-equivalent shapes but non-equivalent sheaf categories, and the separation is realized by an explicit point-free pair with the same shape but different point thickness. (same shape-different sheaf-category point-free separation)

**中文。** 定义：形状与层范畴指两层。猜想：存在两拓扑斯的形状同伦等价而层范畴不等价,分离由同形状异点厚的显式无点对实现。（形状同-层范畴异无点分离）
