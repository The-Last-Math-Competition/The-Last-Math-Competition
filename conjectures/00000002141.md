# 00000002141

**GA-01 ★** — source: 57_代数图论II.md

**English.** Definition: Cospectral regular graphs are non-isomorphic regular graphs with the same adjacency spectrum. Conjecture: The minimal order of a cospectral regular pair is 10 (the Saltire pair); the cospectral regular pair of order 10 is unique (classification uniqueness).

**中文。** 定义：同谱正则图指非同构但邻接谱相同的正则图。猜想：最小同谱正则图对的阶为 10(Saltire 对);10 阶的同谱正则图对只有一对(分类唯一)。
