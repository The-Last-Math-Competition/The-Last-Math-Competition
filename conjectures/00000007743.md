**English.** Definition: Rectangular free probability concerns the deterministic rectangular equivalence of matrix pairs and the rectangular convolution boxed_rect defined via S-type functionals of singular-value distributions. Conjecture: The rectangular Bercovici-Pata correspondence converges at rate O(|lambda-1|+1/n) in the Beta limit lambda=d/n; and the rectangular free stable family degenerates to classical stable laws as lambda tends to 0, the degeneration rate being a linear function of the classical-free characteristic-exponent difference. (rectangular BP rate)

**中文。** 定义：矩形自由概率中的确定性矩形等价刻画 (A,B) 矩形对到标量对 (a,b) 的谱等价；矩形卷积 ⊠_rect 由奇异值分布的 S-型泛函定义。猜想：矩形 Bercovici–Pata 对应在 Beta 系极限 (λ=d/n→λ) 下的收敛速率为 O(|λ-1|+1/n)；且矩形自由稳定律族在 λ→0 时退化为经典稳定律，其退化速率恰为经典与自由特征指数差的线性函数。
