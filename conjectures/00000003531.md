**OC3-11 ★** — source: 103_序数与组合数论III.md

**English.** Definition: Combinatorial partition relations: infinite Ramsey relations. Conjecture: The compactification of partition relations: finite approximations of infinite partition relations, with the convergence of approximations the compactness theorem. (partition compactness finite approximation convergence)

**中文。** 定义：组合划分关系：无穷 Ramsey 关系。猜想：划分关系的紧化：无穷划分关系的有限近似且近似的收敛为紧性定理。（划分紧性有限近似收敛）
