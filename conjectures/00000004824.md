**English.** Definition: Hecke eigenvalues are the Hecke eigenvalues of cusp forms. Conjecture: The exponent of the deviation of their limiting distribution under six-moment analysis is the explicit negative one-half power of the prime, optimal and not improvable. (optimal six-moment Hecke deviation exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Hecke 特征值指尖形式的赫克本征值。猜想：其极限分布偏差在六矩解析下的指数为素数负二分之一次幂型显式,指数最优不可改进。（Hecke 六矩偏差最优指数）
