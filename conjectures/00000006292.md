**English.** Definition: Continuous geometry with the dimension function of von Neumann continuous geometries. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the dimension function and the value range of continuous geometry is the unit interval; the function is unique, the deviation of uniqueness being the automorphism of the lattice of composed subalgebras; and the additivity of the dimension function is preserved under orthogonal complement. (unique dimension-function conversion of continuous geometry)

**中文。** 定义：连续几何：von Neumann 连续几何的维数函数。猜想：换算常数为维数函数的显式且连续几何的值域为单位区间；函数唯一且唯一的偏差为合成子代数格的自同构；维数函数的加性在正交补下保持。（连续几何维数函数唯一换算）
