**English.** Definition: The Peierls argument is the phase-transition proof framework of contour counting. Conjecture: The lower bound of the contour-counting constant is three in two dimensions and the shortest contour attains the bound, the combination of the bound being square-shell counting. (Peierls contour-constant lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Peierls 论证为轮廓计数的相变证明框架。猜想：轮廓计数常数的下界在二维为三且最短轮廓达界；且界的组合为方形壳计数。（Peierls 轮廓常数下界）
