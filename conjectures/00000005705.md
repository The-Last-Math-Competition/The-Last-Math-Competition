# 00000005705

**TW2-25 ★** — source: 175_范畴的表示_2表示论.md

**English.** Definition: The four-dimensional invariant is the two-level four-dimensional topological quantity of Crane-Yetter type. Conjecture: For spheres the invariant is the square of the index, the combination of the square being the leading term of the genus expansion; the next coefficient of the expansion is a linear function of the Euler characteristic; and the invariant is unchanged under surgery on the manifold, the obstruction to invariance being the torsion of the second cohomology. (index-square leading term of four-dimensional spheres)

**中文。** 定义：四维不变量：双层构造的四维拓扑量（Crane–Yetter 型）。猜想：球面时不变量为指标的平方且平方的组合为亏格展开首项；展开的次项系数为 Euler 示性数的线性函数；不变量在流形的重联操作下不变且不变性的障碍为第二上同调的挠。（四维球面指标平方首项）
