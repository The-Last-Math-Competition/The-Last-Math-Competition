# 00000001944

**English.** Definition: The horocycle section is the set of horocycle intersection points on short geodesics. Conjecture: Its equidistribution rate is exp(−c·ℓ) (ℓ the geodesic length); the constant c = 1/2 (via the spectral gap of the transfer operator).

**中文。** 定义：horocycle 截面指短测地线上的 horocycle 交点集。猜想：其等分布率为 exp(−c\ell)(l 为测地线长);常数 c=1/2(由 transfer 算子的谱隙)。
