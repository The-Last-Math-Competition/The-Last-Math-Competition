# 00000006200

**English.** Definition: Critical exponents and blow-up mechanisms are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two equations with the same critical exponent but different blow-up mechanisms, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same exponent but different signs. (same exponent-different sign blow-up separation)

**中文。** 定义：临界指数与爆破机制指两层。猜想：存在两方程的临界指数相同而爆破机制不同,分离由同指数异符号的显式对实现。（指数同-爆破机制异符号分离）
