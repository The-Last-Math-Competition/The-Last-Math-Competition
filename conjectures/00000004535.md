# 00000004535

**English.** Definition: A planted clique is a clique of a given size hidden in a random graph. Conjecture: The information-theoretic detection threshold is 2 times log base 2 of n, the polynomial-algorithm threshold is at least sqrt(n), and the gap between the two thresholds is characterized by an explicit function of the spectral gap. (spectral-gap separation of the planted clique thresholds)

**中文。** 定义：种植团指随机图中隐藏的给定大小团。猜想：信息论检测阈值为 2 倍以二为底的对数 n,多项式算法阈值不低于根号 n,两阈值的间隔由谱隙的显式函数刻画。（种植团双阈值谱隙间隔）
