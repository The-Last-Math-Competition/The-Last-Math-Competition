**English.** Definition: β(4) = Σ (−1)^n/(2n+1)⁴ is the value of the Dirichlet beta function at four. Conjecture: At least one of the even beta values β(2k) is transcendental; the proof path is the algebraic independence of modular forms. (even beta values via modular forms)

**中文。** 定义：β(4)=Σ(−1)^n/(2n+1)⁴ 为 Dirichlet beta 在四的值。猜想：β(2k) 对每个 k 至于一超越；超越性的证明路径为模形式的代数无关性。（beta-偶值超越模形式路径）
