# 00000004728

**English.** Definition: The Tate-Shafarevich group is the difference between the global Brauer group and the product of the local ones. Conjecture: For quadratic fields of small discriminant the group can be completely computed, its order is an explicit integer table, and it is canonically isomorphic to the corresponding torsion subgroup of an elliptic curve. (complete table of small-discriminant TS groups)

**中文。** 定义：Tate–Shafarevich 群指整体 Brauer 与局部之差。猜想：小判别式二次域的该群可完全计算,阶为显式整数表且与椭圆曲线相应挠子群典范同构。（小判别式 TS 完全表）
