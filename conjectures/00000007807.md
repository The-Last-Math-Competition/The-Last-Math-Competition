# 00000007807

**ACG-27 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: Log-Sobolev universality of the surface-area ratio: the logarithmic Sobolev constant alpha_K of the boundary measure sigma_K of a convex body K. Conjecture: alpha_K >= c/ratio(K) for all convex bodies, where the denominator is the surface-to-volume ratio; on smooth strictly convex bodies the reciprocal of alpha_K approximates the ratio of mean-curvature integrals to an explicit constant at order O(curvature variance); and the prism family attains the minimum c* = 2 of alpha_K times the ratio, given by the spectrum of the one-dimensional thin-plate model. (surface-ratio log-Sobolev universality)

**中文。** 定义：表面积比的 log-Sobolev 普适性：凸体 K 的边界测度 σ_K 满足的对数 Sobolev 常数 α_K。猜想：α_K ≥ c/∂(K)（∂ 为表面体积比）对一切凸体成立；且在光滑严格凸体上 α_K 的倒数以显式常数逼近平均曲率积分的比值，逼近阶 O(曲率方差)；棱柱体族达到 α_K·∂(K) 的极小值 c*=2（由薄板的一维模型的谱给出）。
