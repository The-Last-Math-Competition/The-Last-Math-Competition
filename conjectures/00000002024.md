# 00000002024

**AD-04 ★** — source: 53_加性数论II.md

**English.** Definition: g(k) is the Waring number for all positive integers (every n is a sum of s k-th powers). Conjecture: The exceptions to g(k) = 2^k + ⌊(3/2)^k⌋ − 2 are finite, with the largest exception k = 4 (g(4) = 19 > 2⁴ + ⌊(3/2)⁴⌋ − 2); for k ≥ 5 the formula always holds.

**中文。** 定义：g(k) 指一切正整数的 Waring 数(所有 n 可表为 s 个 k 次幂)。猜想：g(k) = 2^k + ⌊(3/2)^k⌋ − 2 的反例有限且最大反例为 k = 4(g(4) = 19 > 2⁴+⌊(3/2)⁴⌋−2);k ≥ 5 后公式恒成立。
