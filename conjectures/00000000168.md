**English.** Definition: S' = ∫₀¹ xˣ dx = Σ (−1)^{n+1} n^{−n} is the alternating sophomore partner. Conjecture: S and S' are linearly independent over Q: for integers a, b not both zero, aS + bS' is irrational; the independence follows from the sign-locking mechanism of same-denominator alternating approximations. (linear independence of the two sophomore constants)
