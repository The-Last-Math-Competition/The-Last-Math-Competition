**English.** Definition: The irrationality measure μ(ξ) is the best-approximation exponent. Conjecture: μ(ln 2) is exactly two; and the tradeoff between degree and height of the approximating polynomials is given by the Padé structure of the exponential integral. (ln2 irrationality measure exactly two)

**中文。** 定义：无理度 μ(ξ) 为最佳逼近指数。猜想：μ(ln 2) 恰为二；且逼近多项式的次数与高度的折衷由指数积分的 Padé 结构给出。（ln2 无理度恰为二）
