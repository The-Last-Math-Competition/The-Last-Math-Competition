# 00000004579

**English.** Definition: The core boundary is the fractal surface of the convex core boundary. Conjecture: Its Hausdorff dimension is strictly greater than two if and only if the bending measure is nonzero, the dimension increases by an explicit function of the bending density, and the supremum is below three. (dimension two-to-three law of the core boundary)

**中文。** 定义：核边界指凸核边界的分形曲面。猜想：其 Hausdorff 维数严格大于二当且仅当弯曲测度非零,维数随弯曲密度的显式函数递增且上确界小于三。（核边界维数二至三律）
