# 00000005940

**English.** Definition: First- and second-order spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two systems with the same first-order fluctuation spectrum but different second-order spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit noise pair with the same leading law but different mixing. (same first-order-different second-order noise separation)

**中文。** 定义：一阶与二阶谱指两层。猜想：存在两系统的一阶涨落谱相同而二阶谱不同,分离由同首律异混合的显式噪声对实现。（一阶谱同-二阶谱异噪声分离）
