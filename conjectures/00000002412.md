**English.** Definition: The étale fundamental group is the algebraic fundamental group of a scheme. Conjecture: The spectrum of finite quotients of a smooth projective curve is controlled by cofiniteness; and the control window is the conductor spectrum. (curve étale fundamental group cofinite control)
