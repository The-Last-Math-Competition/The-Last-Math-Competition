**DDG-15 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Tutte embeddings are barycentric embeddings with convex fixed boundary. Conjecture: The Lipschitz constant of Tutte embedding deformations is always controlled by the second eigenvalue of the triangulation; and it is optimal at heaviest-weight balanced meshes. (Tutte deformation Lipschitz bound)

**中文。** 定义：Tutte 嵌入指边界凸固定下的重心嵌入。猜想：Tutte 嵌入形变的 Lipschitz 常数恒由三角剖分第二特征值控制；且最重权平衡网格处最优。（Tutte 形变 Lipschitz 界）
