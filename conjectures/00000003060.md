**English.** Definition: The Jacobi identity test for Hamiltonian structures: structural integrability. Conjecture: Algorithmic verification of Jacobi: verification of the Jacobi identity of a Poisson bracket is the vanishing of the Schouten bracket, and the verification of vanishing is the reduction of polynomial identities. (Schouten vanishing reduction verification)
