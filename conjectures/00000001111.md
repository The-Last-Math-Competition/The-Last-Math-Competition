# 00000001111

**English.** Definition: Descendant subgroups (of the alcove group of an affine Weyl group). Conjecture: For any affine Weyl group, the ratio of the descendant count to the total alcove count is 1/h (descendant density).

**中文。** 定义：Descendant 子群(仿射 Weyl 群的 alcove 群)。猜想：任意仿射 Weyl 群的 Descendant 计数与全体 alcove 计数的比为 1/h(后代密度)。
