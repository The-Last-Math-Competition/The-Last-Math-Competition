# 00000007493

**English.** Definition: Mixed motives are motive structures with weight filtration. Conjecture: The rank of mixed extension groups is always controlled by the image of the regulator map; and the rank equals the rank of L-values under Beilinson's conjecture. (regulator-controlled mixed-extension rank)
