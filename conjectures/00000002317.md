# 00000002317

**English.** Definition: Direct-sum decompositions of abelian p-groups (Krull–Schmidt uniqueness). Conjecture: The table of possible types of decomposition invariants is an explicit quasipolynomial in partition pairs (the quasipolynomial period being the smallest power of p); the table comes from the partial-order enumeration of partition functions.

**中文。** 定义：交换 p-群的直和分解(Krull–Schmidt 唯一)。猜想：分解不变量的可能型表为分拆对的显式拟多项式(准多项式的周期为 p 的最小幂);表由分拆函数的偏序枚举。
