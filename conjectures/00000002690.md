**AD2-10 ★** — source: 75_代数动力学_ArzelaZariski.md

**English.** Definition: Zariski's conjecture: completeness determined by the topology of the local ring. Conjecture: Removing the Zariski paradox for topologically isomorphic complete local rings: the minimal degree of coefficient-field extension of a representative of a topological equivalence class is a bounded function of the discriminant, and the representative is unique up to Frobenius twist. (Zariski local ring representative)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 猜想（局部环的拓扑决定的完备性）。猜想：拓扑同构的完备局部环的 Zariski 佯谬消除：拓扑等价类的代表环的最小系数域扩张次数为判别式的有界函数且代表元在同构意义下唯一至 Frobenius 扭。（Zariski 局部环代表元）
