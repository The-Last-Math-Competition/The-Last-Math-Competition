# 00000009881

**GBR-01 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Kac polynomials: counting polynomials of absolutely indecomposable representations of quivers at given dimension vectors; positivity is proven but combinatorial interpretations are scattered. Conjecture: All coefficients of Kac polynomials admit a unified combinatorial interpretation: coefficients count explicit varieties of stable flags satisfying specific chain conditions; the interpretation degenerates to root multiplicities in affine type and matches the canonical basis coefficients of quantum groups. (Kac coefficient unified combinatorial interpretation)

**中文。** 定义：Kac 多项式：箭图在给定维数向量的绝对不可分解表示的计数多项式；正性已证但组合解释分散。猜想：Kac 多项式的一切系数有统一组合解释：系数计数满足特定链条件的稳定旗的显式簇；解释在仿射型退化为根重数且与量子群的典范基系数吻合。（Kac 系数统一组合解释）
