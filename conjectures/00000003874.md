# 00000003874

**English.** Definition: The m-point function ρ_m is the simultaneous density. Conjecture: For fixed m and any β > 0, the bulk ρ_m of analytic-potential β-gases is given from the sine-β ρ_m and the local density by a universal convolution-type functional, with error O(1/N) independent of m. (m-point local universality error)

**中文。** 定义：m-点函数 ρ_m 指同时密度。猜想：对固定 m 与任意 β>0,解析位势 β-气的 bulk ρ_m 由正弦-β 的 ρ_m 与局部密度经普适卷积型泛函给出,误差 O(1/N) 且不依赖 m。（m-点局部普适误差）
