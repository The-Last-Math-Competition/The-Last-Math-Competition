**English.** Definition: The four-ball unknotting number u_4(K) is the minimal weighted sum of crossing changes of signed (weight plus or minus 1/2) self-crossings trivializing the knot. Conjecture: u_4(K) = (u(K) + nu(K))/2, where nu(K) is a Rasmussen type correction invariant; u_4 is a half-integer and additive under connected sums. (half-value formula for four-ball unknotting)

**中文。** 定义：四类 unknotting 数 u₄(K) 指经带符号（权 ±1/2）自交叉变化化为平凡结的最小权和。猜想：u₄(K) = (u(K)+ν(K))/2，ν(K) 为 Rasmussen 型修正不变量，u₄ 为半整数且在连通和下加性。（四类 unknotting 半值公式）
