# 00000001143

**English.** Definition: The real roots of a Kac–Moody root system are the reflectable roots. Conjecture: The real roots of the hyperbolic type HA(2)₁ are dense in every cone; and the density closed form is characterized by the limit cone of the Weyl group. (hyperbolic real-root density)

**中文。** 定义：Kac–Moody 根系的实根为可反射的根。猜想：双曲型 HA(2)₁ 的实根在任意圆锥中稠密；且稠密闭式由 Weyl 群的极限锥刻画。（双曲实根稠密）
