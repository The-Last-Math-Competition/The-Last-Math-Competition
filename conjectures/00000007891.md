# 00000007891

**English.** Definition: The elliptic-cohomology morphism of spectral algebraic geometry: the torsion index f(E) of the orientation morphism M_Ell to M_Spectra of an elliptic spectrum E (a Goerss-Hopkins-Miller-Lurie object). Conjecture: f(E) is a function of the height h of the elliptic curve: f = 24 at h = 1 (the number of factors of the modular form discriminant), and in general f = phi_h (h+1); and the torsion index jumps at exactly two points of the height-2 supersingular locus, j = 0 and j = 1728, with jumps 3 and 2 respectively, matching the orders of the automorphism groups. (elliptic orientation torsion jumps)

**中文。** 定义：spectral algebraic geometry 的椭圆上同调态射：椭圆谱 E 的定理态射 M_Ell→M_Spectra（Goerss–Hopkins–Miller–Lurie 对象）的挠性指数 f(E)。猜想：f(E) 为椭圆曲线的高度 h 的函数：h=1 时 f=24（模形式判别的因子数），一般 h 时 f=φ_{h}·(h+1)；且挠性在高度 2 的超奇点轨迹上的跳跃点为j=0 与 j=1728（恰两处），跳跃量分别 3 与 2，与自同构群的阶一致。
