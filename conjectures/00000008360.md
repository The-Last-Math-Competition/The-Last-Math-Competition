**English.** Definition: The Kolchin form of tropical completion: the semialgebraic position kol_trop of the tropicalization of differential polynomials (tropicalization in Kolchin semantics). Conjecture: kol_trop (the image of differential tropicalization) is semialgebraically closed (a semialgebraic closure law); the dimension conversion of the image (tropicalization of Kolchin dimension) is linear (a linear conversion law); the minimal differential equation breaking closure (nonclosed image) is of Darboux type (a Darboux exception law); and the meaning of the closure additions (boundary points of the image) is differential singularities (a singularity meaning law). (Kolchin tropical semialgebraic Darboux closure)

**中文。** 定义：热带完备化的 Kolchin 形式：微分多项式的热带化（Kolchin 语义的热带化）的半代数位 kol_trop。猜想：kol_trop（微分热带化的像）为半代数的闭（半代闭律）；且像的维数（Kolchin 维数的热带化）的换算为线性换算（线性换算律）；闭包的例外（非闭的像）的最小微分方程为 Darboux 型（Darboux 例外律）；闭包的添加（像的边界的添加）的点的意义为微分奇点（奇点意义律）。
