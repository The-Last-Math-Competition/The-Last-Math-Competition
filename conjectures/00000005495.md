**English.** Definition: The minimal models are rational conformal field theories of central charge below one. Conjecture: The higher-order symmetry is always given explicitly by the fusion lattice of degenerate representations, complete for the three series, and the lattice table is closed with no gaps. (fusion-lattice three series of higher symmetries)

**中文。** 定义：极小模型指中心荷小于一的有理共形场论。猜想：高阶对称恒由退化表示的融合格显式给出；三系列完全且格表封闭无缺。（高阶对称融合格三系列）
