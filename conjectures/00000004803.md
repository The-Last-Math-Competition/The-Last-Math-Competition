# 00000004803

**English.** Definition: The deformation tangent space is the cohomology space of first-order deformations. Conjecture: The deformation dimension upper bound of high Picard rank three-dimensional Fanos is an explicit quadratic expression in the Hodge numbers, attained by fan-type structures and not exceedable. (quadratic deformation upper bound of high rank)

**中文。** 定义：形变切空间指一阶形变的上同调空间。猜想：高 Picard 秩三维 Fano 的形变维上界为 Hodge 数的显式二次式,界由扇形结构达到且不可超。（高秩形变维二次上界）
