# 00000005973

**English.** Definition: Singular vectors are the orthogonal pairs of rectangular decompositions. Conjecture: The coupling law of left and right vectors is symmetric in the square case, with phase alignment in the rectangular limit, and the kernel of the alignment is the circular law. (circular-law phase alignment of singular vectors)

**中文。** 定义：奇异向量指矩形分解的正交向量对。猜想：左右向量的耦合律在方形时对称且矩形极限为相位对齐；且对齐的核为圆律。（奇异向量圆律相位对齐方形对称）
