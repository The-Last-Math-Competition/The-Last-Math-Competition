**English.** Definition: k- tree denotes support of Kalai under k dimension group in kernel minimal. Conjecture: counting of simply connected 5- manifold triangulation in 3- tree by each dimension Betti number and explicit polynomial of torsion coefficient gives, polynomial -fold number is binomial coefficient of triangulation number of faces. (3- tree counting Betti polynomial)
