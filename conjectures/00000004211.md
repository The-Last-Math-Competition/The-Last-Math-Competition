# 00000004211

**English.** Definition: A k-tree is a supporting subcomplex whose chain group has minimal kernel in the sense of Kalai. Conjecture: For triangulations of simply connected 5-manifolds, the count of 3-trees is an explicit polynomial in the Betti numbers and torsion coefficients of each dimension, of degree a combination of the numbers of faces. (3-tree count Betti polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：k-树指 Kalai 意义下 k 维链群中核极小的支撑子复形。猜想：单连通 5-流形剖分中 3-树的计数由各维 Betti 数与挠系数的显式多项式给出,多项式次数为剖分面数的组合数。（3-树计数 Betti 多项式）
