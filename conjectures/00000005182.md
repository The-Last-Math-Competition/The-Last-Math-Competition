# 00000005182

**ST2-12 ★** — source: 158_高阶范畴II_稳定无穷范畴.md

**English.** Definition: A phantom category is a nonzero stable category with zero K-theory. Conjecture: The minimal realization of phantoms on rational varieties is a direct summand of the surface of two direct products, with minimal skeleton of type two; the nonvanishing of the phantom orthogonal complement is given by the motive decomposition of the product factors; and the thickness of any phantom is at most the dimension of the factors. (minimal type-two realization of rational phantoms)

**中文。** 定义：幻影范畴：不为零但 K 理论为零的稳定范畴。猜想：有理簇上幻影的最小实现为两直积表面的直和分量且最小骨架为二型；幻影正交补的非零性由直积因子的母题分解给出；任何幻影的厚度不超过因子的维数。（有理幻影最小二型实现）
