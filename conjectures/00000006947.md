**English.** Definition: The worst-case RKHS error is the integration error of the worst function on the kernel space. Conjecture: The squared error equals the kernel diagonal minus the sum of the reproducing kernels; and the characterization decomposes componentwise for additive kernels. (squared characterization of RKHS worst-case error)

**中文。** 定义：RKHS 的最坏情形误差指核空间上最坏函数的积分误差。猜想：误差的平方等于核的对角减去再生核的求和；且该刻画对加性核逐分量分解。（RKHS 最坏误差平方刻画）
