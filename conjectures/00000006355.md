# 00000006355

**English.** Definition: A quasi-Hopf algebra is the structure with nontrivial associator. Conjecture: The leading term of the normal-form series of the associator is one and unique; the uniqueness is gauge fixing; and the freedom of gauge fixing is the Drinfeld link. (unique normal form of associators)

**中文。** 定义：拟霍普夫为结合子非平凡的结构。猜想：结合子的规范型级数首项为一且唯一；且唯一性为规范固定；且规范固定的自由度为 Drinfeld 联结。（结合子规范型唯一）
