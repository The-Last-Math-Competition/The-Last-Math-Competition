# 00000007919

**ATC-19 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The logarithmic energy form of Lindelof: the logarithmic integral I(T) = integral from 0 to T of log|L(1/2+it,pi)| dt along the critical line. Conjecture: I(T) = O(T log log T/log T) (a low bound for logarithmic energy); and I(T)/T tends to 0 with main correction the Mertens-type partial sum of sum_p (1/p) Re lambda_pi(p^2), whose divergence rate is an explicit multiple of log log T, consistent with the random matrix eigenvalue energy xi = 1 + log(N)/2. (logarithmic energy Mertens correction)

**中文。** 定义：Lindelöf 的对数能量形式：L(s,π) 在临界线的值的对数积分 I(T)=∫_0^T log|L(1/2+it,π)|dt。猜想：I(T) = O(T·log log T/log T)（对数能量低度有界）；且 I(T)/T 的极限为 0，主修正项为 Σ_p 1/p·Re λ_π(p²) 的 Mertens 型部分和，发散速度为 log log T 的显式倍数（与随机矩阵特征能量 ξ=1+log(N)/2 的一致）。
