**English.** Definition: The ramification groups G_i of the cyclotomic Z_p-extension form a filtered tower. Conjecture: The rank-drop jumps follow the explicit formula i = (p−1)p^n; and the jump sequence's regularity is corrected at singular points. (ramification jump formula)
