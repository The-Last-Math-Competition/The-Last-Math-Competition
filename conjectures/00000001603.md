**SP-03 ★** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: The Fibonacci Hamiltonian is the discrete Schrödinger operator whose potential takes ±λ following the Fibonacci word. Conjecture: At λ = 1, the box dimension of the spectrum is 1 (upper bound 1 and lower bound > 0.9 are known); refined statement: the deviation from 1 is exactly 0, the spectrum is a fat fractal of zero full measure whose thickness constant τ satisfies τ²·τ_reg = 1/4 (Damanin–Gorodets-type exactness).

**中文。** 定义：Fibonacci Hamiltonian 指势按 Fibonacci 词取 ±λ 的离散 Schrödinger 算子。猜想：λ=1 时谱的盒维数为 1(已知上界 1 与下界 >0.9);修正陈述:维数与 1 的偏差精确为 0,谱为全测度零的胖分形,其厚度常数 τ 满足 τ²·τ_reg=1/4(Daman–Gorodets 型精确)。
