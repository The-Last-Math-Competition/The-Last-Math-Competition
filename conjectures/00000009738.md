# 00000009738

**English.** Definition: Size distributions of minimal counterexample models of first-order formulas: the statistical structure of automated counterexample search. Conjecture: The minimal model sizes of satisfiable but invalid first-order formulas follow heavy-tailed distributions with tail exponent 3/2; counts of minimal models over domain size n are asymptotically a decreasing n^{-1/2} power law. (minimal counterexample size distribution)

**中文。** 定义：一阶公式最小反例模型的规模分布：自动反例搜索的统计结构。猜想：可满足但非有效公式的一阶最小模型规模服从重尾分布且尾指数为 3/2；最小模型在论域大小 n 上的计数渐近为 n^{−1/2} 的递减幂律。（最小反例规模分布）
