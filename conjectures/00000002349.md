# 00000002349

**English.** Definition: Verifiable tracking in fixed-precision environments is homotopy tracking in double precision. Conjecture: In fixed precision (2^{-53}), the maximal trackable degree is D_max = 2^{53/γ}/e (γ the γ-number; an explicit conversion); tracking fails for degrees above this bound.

**中文。** 定义：固定精度环境的可验证追踪指双精度下的同伦追踪。猜想：固定精度(2^{-53})下的最大可追踪度 D_max = 2^{53/γ}/e(γ 为 γ-数;显式换算);度超过此界时追踪失败。
