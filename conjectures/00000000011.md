**English.** Conjecture: Let τ be the Ramanujan τ function; then τ(p) (mod p) is uniformly distributed over the residue classes allowed by the Swinnerton–Dyer congruences.

**中文。** 猜想：设 τ 为 Ramanujan τ 函数,则 τ(p) (mod p) 在 Swinnerton–Dyer 同余所允许的剩余类中一致分布。
