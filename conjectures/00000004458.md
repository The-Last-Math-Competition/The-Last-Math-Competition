# 00000004458

**English.** Definition: The singular support of a microlocal sheaf is the conormal Lagrangian set of the sheaf construction. Conjecture: The correspondence of singular-support equivalence classes with Legendrian submanifolds is complete at the derived level, given by a Riemann-Hilbert type functor and essentially surjective. (complete microlocal-Legendrian RH correspondence)
