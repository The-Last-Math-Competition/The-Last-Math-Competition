# 00000006209

**English.** Definition: Weak turbulence is the slow transport phenomenon of Fourier energy. Conjecture: The periodic truncation layers of resonance polygons are finite with explicit inter-layer transfer rates, the kernel of the rate being phase drift. (phase-drift transfer rate of weak-turbulence resonance layers)

**中文。** 定义：弱湍流为 Fourier 能量的慢输运现象。猜想：共振多边形的周期截断层有限且层间转移率显式；且率的核为相位漂移。（弱湍流共振层相位漂移转移率）
