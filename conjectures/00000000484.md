**SA-84 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: charge is the Lascoux–Schützenberger cocharge statistic. Conjecture: The charge distribution for fixed weight is asymptotically normal with explicit variance; and the variance is a function of the multiplicities of the weight parts. (charge asymptotic normality)

**中文。** 定义：charge 为 Lascoux–Schützenberger 的共电荷统计。猜想：charge 分布在固定权下渐近正态且方差显式；且方差为权部分的重数函数。（charge 渐近正态）
