# 00000001153

**English.** Definition: The Whitehead product of a graded Lie algebra (L, d) is a secondary operation on homology. Conjecture: Every nontrivial Whitehead product on H^*(L) lifts to a Samelson product in H^*(L); that is, no essentially cohomological Whitehead elements exist; equivalently, the Whitehead spectrum is a direct summand of the Samelson spectrum.

**中文。** 定义：分次李代数 (L,d) 的 Whitehead 乘积为同调上的二级运算 [,[ ]]。猜想：一切在 H^*(L) 上的非平凡 Whitehead 乘积均可提升为 H^*(L) 中的 Samelson 积,即不存在只在上同调层出现的本质 Whitehead 元素;等价地 Whitehead 谱是 Samelson 谱的直和项。
