**English.** Definition: The orbit closure is the smallest algebraic closed set. Conjecture: In the curve case the closure is explicitly dichotomous, a finite set or a finite union of translates, given by the periodic rigidity criterion, and the criterion is complete. (rigid dichotomy criterion of orbit closures)
