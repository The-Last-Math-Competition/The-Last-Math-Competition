**NG-07 ★** — source: 63_数值代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: Regeneration is the numerical method of adding equations successively and branching. Conjecture: The expected number of regeneration steps under random quadratization is D^{n}·2^{-n} (an explicit functional of the Bernstein basis); the expectation is computed from the Bernoulli product of branching ratios.

**中文。** 定义：再生法(regeneration)指逐次添加方程并分支的数值方法。猜想：随机二次化的再生步骤的期望数为 D^{n}·2^{-n}(Bernstein 基的显式泛函);期望由分支比的 Bernoulli 乘积计算。
