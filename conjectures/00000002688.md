**English.** Definition: Adelic dynamics: joint iteration of p-adic and real topologies. Conjecture: Multiplicativity of adelic invariant measures: the product of invariant measures of the branches is adelic-invariant with total entropy the sum of the branch entropies; the deficit of the local-global entropy formula is a finite sum over ramified primes. (adelic dynamical entropy decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：Adelic 动力学：p-adic 与实拓扑的联合迭代。猜想：adelic 不变测度的乘性：各拓扑分支的不变测度的乘积为 adelic 不变测度且分支熵之和等于整体熵；熵的局部-全局公式的亏损项为分歧素数的有限和。（adelic 动力熵分解）
