# 00000001870

**English.** Definition: The CLT for the trace distribution of random unitary matrices (known). Conjecture: The third cumulant decays as N^{-1}·c₃ with c₃ = (2πi)/3 (from the series expansion of the cumulant-generating function); the decay exponent 1 is exact.

**中文。** 定义：随机幺正矩阵的迹分布的 CLT(已知)。猜想：三阶累积量在 N→∞ 衰减为 N^{-1}·c₃,c₃ = (2π i)/3(由 cumulant 生成函数的级数展开);衰减指数 1 为精确。
