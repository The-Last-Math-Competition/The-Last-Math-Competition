**MBL-13 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: The many-body mobility edge: the energy-dependent localization threshold curve. Conjecture: Quasiperiodic interacting systems always have a many-body mobility edge; the edge is an analytic curve universal under interaction deformations, and in the random case it is granulated by renormalization. (many-body mobility edge analytic universality)

**中文。** 定义：多体迁移率边指能量依赖的局域化阈值曲线。猜想：准周期相互作用系统恒存在多体迁移率边；边为解析曲线且对相互作用变形普适不变，随机情形被重整化颗粒化。（多体迁移率边解析普适）
