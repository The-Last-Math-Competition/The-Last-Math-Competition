# 00000008452

**English.** Definition: The length of a sutured manifold hierarchy is its normalized number of decomposition steps. Conjecture: The length is at most twice the complexity, and the bound is optimal; the manifolds attaining half the bound form an explicit family. (linear bound on hierarchy length)

**中文。** 定义：sutured 流形 hierarchy 的长度指其规范化分解步数。猜想：长度 ≤ 2·复杂度且界最优；达到一半长度的流形构成显式族。（hierarchy 长度线性界）
