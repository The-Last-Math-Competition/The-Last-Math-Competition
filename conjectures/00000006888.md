**GC6-08 ◆** — source: 215_计算复杂性的几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The hive method for tensor-product coefficients is the combinatorial computation of Littlewood-Richardson coefficients. Conjecture: The hive formula gives the integer-point counting of coefficients and the counting domain is explicitly constrained by partition boxes; and the complexity of the formula is polynomial in the partition size. (hive integer-point LR formula)

**中文。** 定义：张量积系数的半图方法为 Littlewood–Richardson 的组合计算。猜想：半图公式给出系数的整数点计数且计数域由分拆盒显式约束；且公式的复杂度为分拆大小的多项式。（半图整数点 LR 公式）
