**English.** Definition: graph normal of tensor product coefficient is combinatorial computation of Littlewood–Richardson. Conjecture: graph formula gives integer point counting of coefficient and counting field by partition explicit constraint; and complexity of formula is polynomial of partition size. (graph integer point LR formula)
