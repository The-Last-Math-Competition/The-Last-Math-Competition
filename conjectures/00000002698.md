# 00000002698

**English.** Definition: Amoebas and the non-Archimedean side of algebraic dynamics: tropicalization. Conjecture: The dynamical commutation of tropicalization: the periodic points of the tropicalization of an iterated map agree with the valuations of the original periodic points; the deficit of the agreement appears only on the ramification layer, whose height is log p. (tropical dynamics periodic correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：Amoeba 与代数动力学的非阿基米德侧：tropical 化。猜想：tropical 化的动力学交换：迭代映射的 tropical 化的周期点与原映射周期点的赋值一致；一致的亏损仅出现在分歧层且层的高度为 p 的对数。（tropical 动力周期对应）
