# 00000007732

**English.** Definition: Independent Haar unitary matrices U_1,...,U_k converge jointly, in the n to infinity limit over star-product polynomials, to a free Haar unitary family. Conjecture: The convergence rate in expected trace norm is O(k^2/n); and for a fixed polynomial P the leading term of the variance is given by the difference between noncrossing and crossing pairing counts of P, this difference decaying at order (d!)^{-1} in the polynomial degree d. (Haar unitary polynomial convergence rate)

**中文。** 定义：k 个独立的 Haar 酉矩阵 U_1,…,U_k 的族在 n→∞ 时关于星积多项式的联合分布收敛到自由 Haar 酉族。猜想：收敛速度（在迹范数的期望下）为 O(k^2/n)；且对固定的多项式 P，其方差的主项由 P 的非交叉配对数与交叉配对数之差给出，该差值随多项式次数 d 的增长以 (d!)^{-1} 阶衰减。
