**English.** Definition: The geometric separation of the Kalai-Smorodinsky and Nash solutions of bargaining: the distance geometry sep_geom between the two solutions. Conjecture: sep_geom (the maximal relative distance between the two solutions) is 1/2 (a half-distance upper bound); the extremal bargaining sets attaining 1/2 are L-shaped feasible sets (an L-shaped extremal set law); the axiomatic completeness of convex combinations of the two solutions (characterization of mixtures) is the pair scale-covariance plus Pareto (a two-axiom characterization law); and the iterative convergence rate of the fixed point of mixed solutions is 1/2 (a half-step convergence law). (Kalai-Smorodinsky Nash L-shaped half-distance)
