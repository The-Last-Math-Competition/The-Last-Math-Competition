# 00000005931

**English.** Definition: The spatial limit process is the universal process in the spatial direction. Conjecture: Multipoint functions are determinant products with explicit kernels, and the two-point coherence-length factor is two to the two-thirds power, the kernel of the factor being the Airy kernel. (Airy-kernel determinant coherence length of multipoint functions)

**中文。** 定义：空间极限过程指空间方向的普适过程。猜想：多点为行列式显式核积且二点相干长度因子为二的三分之二次幂；且因子的核为 Airy 核。（多点 Airy 核行列式相干长度）
