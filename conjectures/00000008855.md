# 00000008855

**English.** Definition: Splitting frameworks for sparse optimization. Conjecture: The proximal operator of sparse penalties is always soft thresholding; and the convergence rate of thresholded splitting is O(1/t) plus a logarithmic correction. (proximal soft threshold logarithmic correction)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏优化的分裂框架。猜想：稀疏罚的邻近算子恒为软阈值；且阈值化的分裂收敛率为 O(1/t) 加对数修正。（邻近软阈值对数修正）
