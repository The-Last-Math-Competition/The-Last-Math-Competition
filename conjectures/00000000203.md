**SQ-03 ◆** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: The Ulam sequence U satisfies 1, 2 ∈ U, and for n > 2, n belongs to U if and only if n has exactly one representation as a sum of two distinct Ulam numbers. Conjecture: The counting function satisfies #(U ∩ [1,N]) = N^{α+o(1)}, where the exponent α is a new constant explicitly equal to the power-law exponent of the additive energy of the recursion; the existence of α is equivalent to the existence of the spectral-radius limit of the Ulam graph family. (Ulam density exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Ulam 序列 U 满足 1,2∈U 且 n>2 时 n 属于 U 当且仅当 n 恰有一次表示为两相异 Ulam 数之和。猜想：U 的计数函数满足 U∩[1,N] 的规模为 N^{α+o(1)}，指数 α 为新常数且与递推的加性能量的幂律指数显式相等；α 的存在性等价于 Ulam 图谱的谱半径极限存在。（Ulam 密度指数）
