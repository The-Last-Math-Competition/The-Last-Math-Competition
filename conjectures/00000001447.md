# 00000001447

**MC-27 ★** — source: 33_概率论II_马氏链与粒子系统.md

**English.** Definition: The boundary length of the uniform spanning tree (UST) is the length of the closure loop around the origin in its planar embedding (the rescaled limit is known to be CLE₂). Conjecture: After rescaling, the tail exponent of the k-th largest boundary loop length of the UST is 5/4, i.e. P(L_k > r) ~ c_k·r^{-5/4}, with c_k explicit from the loop intensity of CLE₂.

**中文。** 定义：均匀生成树(UST)的边界长度指其平面嵌入中环绕原点的闭包回路长度(已知重标度极限为 CLE₂)。猜想：UST 的第 k 大边界回路的长度重标度后的尾指数为 5/4,即 P(L_k > r) ~ c_k·r^{-5/4},c_k 由 CLE₂ 的环强度显式给出。
