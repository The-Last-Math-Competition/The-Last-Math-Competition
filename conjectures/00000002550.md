# 00000002550

**English.** Definition: The traveling salesman function f(E): the infimum length of curves covering E. Conjecture: The spectrum of the traveling salesman function on the plane is onto the nonnegative reals; the pre-heap structure of the spectrum on a fractal E is a monotone Cantor-type function whose jump points are the symmetrization indices of E. (traveling salesman function spectrum structure)

**中文。** 定义：旅行商函数 f(E)：覆盖 E 的曲线长度下确界。猜想：旅行商函数的谱在平面为非负实数满射；谱的预堆结构在分形 E 上为单调上升的 Cantor 型函数且函数的跳变点为 E 的对称化指标。（旅行商函数谱结构）
