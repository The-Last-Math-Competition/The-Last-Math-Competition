# 00000008010

**English.** Definition: The equivalence classes of viscosity solutions of Hamilton-Jacobi with nonsmooth Hamiltonians: the uniqueness class U_H for Lipschitz nonsmooth H(p,x). Conjecture: Under convexity of H in p, viscosity solutions are unique in the uniformly continuous initial-value class; the optimal solvable framework for nonconvex H is characterized by the Caratheodory completeness of the characteristic flow: solvable if and only if the characteristic flow exists globally (no blow-up); and the minimal dimension of characteristic blow-up (the counterexample dimension for nonconvex H) is 2 with double-exponential blow-up rate (Chirikov-type acceleration of characteristic accumulation). (HJ Caratheodory completeness criterion)

**中文。** 定义：非光滑位势的 Hamilton–Jacobi 的粘性解的等价类：Hamilton 量 H(p,x) 的 Lipschitz 非光滑情形的粘性解的唯一性类 U_H。猜想：H 的凸性（p 向）下粘性解在一致连续初值类唯一；唯一性的最优框架（非凸 H）的可解类由特征流的 Carathéodory 完全性刻画：可解当且仅当特征流的全局存在（无爆破）；特征爆破的最小维数（非凸 H 的反例维数）为 2 且爆破率为双重指数（特征累积的 Chirikov 型加速）。
