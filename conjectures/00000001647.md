# 00000001647

**English.** Definition: The bounded-cohomology triangle class. Conjecture: The norm of the straightened volume cohomology class of a hyperbolic triangulation is a rational multiple of v₃, the multiplier being a combinatorial count of vertex configurations (the triangle-class norm).

**中文。** 定义：有界上同调的三角类。猜想：双曲三角剖分的正则体积上同调类的范数为有理数倍 v₃ 且倍数为顶点配置的组合计数(三角类范数)。
