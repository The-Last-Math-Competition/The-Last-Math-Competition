# 00000006344

**English.** Definition: The crystal basis is the zero-parameter combinatorial skeleton. Conjecture: The combinatorial realization of the operators is given by semistandard tableaux; the completeness of the tableaux is lattice isomorphism; and the verification of lattice isomorphism is the convergence of the signature rule. (complete semistandard realization of crystals)
