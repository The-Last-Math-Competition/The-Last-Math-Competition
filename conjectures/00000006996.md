**English.** Definition: The inverse structure of norm formulas. Conjecture: Large norm implies low complexity; the quantification of the inverse is the entropy deficit; and the quantification of the entropy deficit is given by the dimension deficit. (entropy-deficit inverse of norms)

**中文。** 定义：范数公式的逆结构。猜想：范数大蕴含低复杂；且逆的量化为熵亏；且熵亏的量化由维数亏损给出。（范数逆低复杂熵亏）
