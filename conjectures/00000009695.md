# 00000009695

**English.** Definition: Zilber pseudo-exponential fields: model-theoretic constructions of exponential fields satisfying Schanuel's axioms. Conjecture: The automorphism group of a pseudo-exponential field is an unsolvable group whose commutator structure is characterized by direct sums of prime-order cyclic groups; and the existence of automorphisms is equivalent to the construction of a Schanuel-independent basis over the complex field. (pseudo-exponential field automorphisms)

**中文。** 定义：Zilber 伪指数域：满足 Schanuel 公理的指数域的模型论构造。猜想：伪指数域的自同构群为不可解群且其换位子结构由素数阶循环群的直和刻画；自同构的存在性等价于复数域上 Schanuel 独立基的构造。（伪指数域自同构）
