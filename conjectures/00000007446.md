# 00000007446

**English.** Definition: Freiman dimension with the dimension of additive combinatorics. Conjecture: The isometric invariance of dimension holds, and the realization of the invariance is the lattice convex hull. (lattice-hull Freiman-dimension rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Freiman 维数：加性组合的维数。猜想：Freiman 维的刚性：维数的等距不变且变的实现为格凸包。（Freiman 维格凸包维数等距不变实现刚性）
