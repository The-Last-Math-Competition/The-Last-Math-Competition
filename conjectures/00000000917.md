# 00000000917

**English.** Definition: The Bott element β ∈ K₂(C(S²)) is the basic torsion element of the sphere spectrum. Conjecture: The torsion part of the K-groups of higher Bott towers is completely encoded by the dihedral group of a combinatorial configuration; and the twisting of the encoding is the signature of the dihedral order. (Bott torsion dihedral combinatorial encoding)
