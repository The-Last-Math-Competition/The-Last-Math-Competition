# 00000002165

**English.** Definition: A high-dimensional expander is a simplicial complex with spectral expansion; the constants of the Cheeger inequality are the gaps of the two-sided inequality. Conjecture: The tight constant of the high-dimensional Cheeger inequality is 1/2 (attained by combinatorial expanders); the lower-bound constant improves to 1/2.

**中文。** 定义：高维 expander 指单纯复形的谱扩展性质;Cheeger 不等式的常数指两向不等式的间隙。猜想：高维 Cheeger 不等式的紧常数为 1/2(组合刚体达到);下界常数的改进至 1/2。
