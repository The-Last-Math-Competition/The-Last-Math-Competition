# 00000006941

**English.** Definition: Quasi-Monte Carlo with the integration of low-discrepancy sequences. Conjecture: The optimality of discrepancy holds, the lower bound of star discrepancy being realized by randomization. (randomized lower bound of star discrepancy)

**中文。** 定义：拟蒙特卡罗：低差异序列的积分。猜想：差异的最优：星差异的下界且界的实现为随机化。（拟蒙特卡罗星差异下界随机化低差异积分差异最优序列实现）
