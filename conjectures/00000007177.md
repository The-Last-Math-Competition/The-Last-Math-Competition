# 00000007177

**English.** Definition: Generalized eigenvalues with matrix pencil extrema. Conjecture: The scaling of generalized eigenvalues is the quotient of the two spectra, and the extremum of the quotient is the definite pair. (definite-pair pencil spectral quotient)

**中文。** 定义：广义特征值：矩阵束的极值。猜想：束谱的联合：广义特征值的刻度为两谱的商且商的极值为定度对。（广义束定度对极值商两谱刻度联合）
