# 00000005328

**English.** Definition: The diagram category is the mother-space structure of categorified invariants, the bubble-diagram algebra. Conjecture: The two-object matrices are always explicit matrices of two types of commuting diagrams, invariant under peg equivalence; the dimensions of the matrices are Fibonacci type, with growth rate the golden ratio; and the basis change between commuting diagrams is monomial, with the monomial exponents given by the winding numbers of loops. (two-object commuting diagrams of diagram categories)

**中文。** 定义：图形范畴：范畴化不变的母空间结构（泡泡图代数）。猜想：二对象的矩阵恒为两类交换图的显式矩阵且钉等价下不变；矩阵的维数为 Fibonacci 型且增长率为黄金比；交换图间的基变换为单项式且单项式的指数由圈的缠绕数给出。（图形范畴二对象交换图）
