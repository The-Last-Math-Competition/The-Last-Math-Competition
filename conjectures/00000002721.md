# 00000002721

**SC-11 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Simplicial trees: acyclic higher-dimensional complexes (cycles are boundary-less closed chains). Conjecture: The correction of d-face count minus (d−1)-face count for d-trees is the Euler genus, with the counting formula the genus-corrected higher Kirchhoff matrix-tree theorem; the correction is an explicit linear combination of torsion coefficients of chain groups. (higher tree genus counting)

**中文。** 定义：单纯树为无圈的高维复形（圈为无边的闭链）。猜想：d 维树的 d-面数减 (d−1)-面数的修正为 Euler 亏格且亏格的计数公式为高维 Kirchhoff 矩阵树定理的亏格修正版；修正项由链群挠系数的显式线性组合给出。（高维树亏格计数）
