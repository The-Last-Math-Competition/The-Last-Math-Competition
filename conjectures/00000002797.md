# 00000002797

**English.** Definition: The duality of uniform convexity and smoothness: modulus equivalence under ultrapowers. Conjecture: The transfer of the modulus of uniform convexity under ultrapowers: the modulus is invariant under ultrapowers, giving a one-to-one correspondence between asymptotic types and smooth types; the deviation of the correspondence is the Hessian spectral norm of the convexity modulus. (ultrapower convexity modulus transfer law)

**中文。** 定义：均匀凸的光滑对偶：超幂框架下的模等价。猜想：超幂的一致凸性模的传递：模在超幂下不变且不变性给出渐近型与光滑型的一一对应；对应的偏差为凸性模的 Hessian 谱范数。（超幂凸模传递律）
