# 00000003028

**QM-18 ★** — source: 86_多体量子数学.md

**English.** Definition: Quantum simulation of gauge theories: quantum link models. Conjecture: The continuum limit of quantum links: the link model converges to the gauge theory in the large-representation limit, with convergence error an explicit inverse power of the representation dimension. (quantum link continuum limit error)

**中文。** 定义：规范理论的量子模拟：量子链接模型。猜想：量子链接的连续极限：链接模型在大表示极限收敛到规范理论且收敛误差为表示维数的显式逆幂。（量子链接连续极限误差）
