# 00000008688

**English.** Definition: Transport inequalities: the hierarchy of Talagrand T_2 and log-Sobolev. Conjecture: T_2 and LS are equivalent (the Gozlan law); the optimal constants of the equivalence are attained by Gaussians (a Gaussian law); the strict deformation (strict T_2) is strict (a strictness law); and the failure of strictness occurs in heavy-tailed domains (a heavy-tail law). (Talagrand Gozlan Gaussian strictness)

**中文。** 定义：传输不等式：Talagrand T₂ 与 log-Sobolev 的层级。猜想：T₂ 与 LS 的等价（Gozlan 律）；且等价的常数（最优常数的实现）的高斯的（高斯律）；变形的（严格 T₂）的 strict 的（严格律）；严格的失效（非严格域）的重尾域（重尾律）。
