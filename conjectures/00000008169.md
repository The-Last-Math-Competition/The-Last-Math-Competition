# 00000008169

**RNG-29 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic realization of the renormalization group: the arithmetic entropy arith_e(RG) of the p-adic decomposition of RG transformations. Conjecture: The entropy of arith_e is the p-adic normalization of log b (b the scale factor, a p-adic entropy formula); the criterion for the reversibility of the entropy (arithmetic reversibility of the RG) is the p-adic norm of b equaling 1 (a unit norm criterion); and the root spectrum of the arithmetic invariant (p-adic characteristic polynomial) of reversible RG consists of Weil numbers (a Weil number spectrum law), the weights of the spectrum being pure 2 (a pure weight law). (arithmetic RG p-adic Weil pure weight)

**中文。** 定义：重整化群的算术实现：RG 变换的 p-进分解的算术熵 arith_e(RG)。猜想：arith_e 的熵为 log b（b 为尺度因子）的 p-进规范（p-进熵公式）；且熵的可逆性（RG 的算术可逆）的判据为 b 的 p-进范数的 1（单位范数判据）；可逆 RG 的算术不变量（p-进特征多项式）的根的谱为 Weil 数（Weil 数谱律），谱的权为纯 2（纯权律）。
