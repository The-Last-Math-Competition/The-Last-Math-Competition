# 00000002285

**English.** Definition: An orthogonal martingale pair is a pair of continuous martingales with identically zero quadratic covariation. Conjecture: The realization spectrum of joint distributions is completely characterized by the sign of the predictable quadratic covariation, with no counterexamples under the two-dimensional Brownian kernel. (orthogonal martingale spectrum sign characterization)

**中文。** 定义：正交鞅对指二次协变差恒为零的连续鞅对。猜想：联合分布的实现谱恒由可料二次协变差的符号完全刻画且在二维布朗核下无反例。（正交鞅谱符号刻画）
