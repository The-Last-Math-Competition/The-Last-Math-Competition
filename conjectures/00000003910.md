# 00000003910

**English.** Definition: The p-limit of an F-pure threshold is the limiting behavior of the threshold as the prime varies. Conjecture: There exists a fixed family of ideals whose fpt converges along different prime subsequences to distinct values, whereas for a fixed monomial ideal the fpt always converges to the characteristic-zero log threshold. (fpt subsequence separation and monomial convergence)

**中文。** 定义：fpt 的 p-极限指 F-纯阈值沿素数变化的极限行为。猜想：存在固定理想族使其 fpt 沿不同素数子列收敛到相异值;而固定单项式理想的 fpt 恒收敛于特征零对数阈值。（fpt 子列分离与单项式收敛）
