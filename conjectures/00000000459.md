**SA-59 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The Kreweras complement. Conjecture: There is a rank-preserving bijection between the fixed points of the Kreweras involution in NC_n and the 3-noncrossing isolated-point-free parts (counted by the Riordan numbers).

**中文。** 定义：Kreweras complement。猜想：NC_n 中 Kreweras 对合的不动点与 3-非交叉无孤立点部分之间存在保秩双射(计数为 Riordan 数)。
