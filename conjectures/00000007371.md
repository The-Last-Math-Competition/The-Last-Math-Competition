# 00000007371

**English.** Definition: Shooting methods for boundary value problems. Conjecture: The Newton correction of initial slopes holds, and the Jacobian of the correction is the variation of the flow. (flow-varied shooting Newton sensitivity)

**中文。** 定义：边界值问题的打靶法。猜想：打靶的灵敏度：初始斜率的牛顿校正且正的雅可比为流的变分。（打靶法牛顿校准变分雅可比两点边界初始斜率灵敏度）
