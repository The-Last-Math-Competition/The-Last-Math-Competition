# 00000002640

**English.** Definition: p-adic Jacobi forms: p-adic interpolation theory. Conjecture: The dimension of the p-adic Jacobi space is a continuous function on the weight-index lattice, the continuity breaking when p divides the index; the repair layer of the break is the Jacobi version of Hida-type control theorems. (p-adic Jacobi control theorem)

**中文。** 定义：p-adic Jacobi 形式：p 进插值理论。猜想：p-adic Jacobi 空间的维数为权-指标格上的连续函数且连续性在 p 整除指标时破裂；破裂的修补层为 Hida 型控制定理的 Jacobi 版本。（p-adic Jacobi 控制定理）
