# 00000007744

**English.** Definition: The spectral empirical measure of random d-regular graphs (uniform model) converges to the Kesten-McKay law KM_d; the second-order limit is the joint limit process of normalized trace polynomials times sqrt n. Conjecture: The limit is Gaussian with covariance Sigma(p,q) given by (d+1)-regular constrained counts of noncrossing return path pairings of p and q; for p=q=x^2 one has Var = 4 - 12/d + 8/d^2, degenerating to the Wigner value 4 of GUE fluctuations as d grows. (Kesten-McKay second-order fluctuations)

**中文。** 定义：随机 d-正则图（一致模型）的谱经验测度收敛到 Kesten–McKay 律 KM_d；其二阶极限分布指规范化迹多项式 (tr p(A) - E)·√n 的联合极限过程。猜想：该极限为高斯族，协方差函数 Σ(p,q) 由 p,q 的非交叉返回路径配对的 (d+1)-正则约束计数给出；特别对 p=q=x²，Var = 4 - 12/d + 8/d²，且当 d→∞ 时退化为 GUE 波动的 Wigner 值 4。
