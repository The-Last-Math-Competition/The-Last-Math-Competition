**FRV-24 ★** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: spectrum measure of random d- regular graph (uniform model) converges to Kesten–McKay KM_d; its two order limit distribution denotes joint limit process of normalization trace polynomial (tr p(A) - E) · √n. Conjecture: the limit is Gaussian family, covariance function Σ(p,q) by (d+1)- regular constraint counting of non- intersection cycle pairing of p,q gives; in particular pair p=q=x², Var = 4 - 12/d + 8/d², and when d -> ∞ when degenerate is Wigner 4 of GUE fluctuation.

**中文。** 定义：随机 d-正则图（一致模型）的谱经验测度收敛到 Kesten–McKay 律 KM_d；其二阶极限分布指规范化迹多项式 (tr p(A) - E)·√n 的联合极限过程。猜想：该极限为高斯族，协方差函数 Σ(p,q) 由 p,q 的非交叉返回路径配对的 (d+1)-正则约束计数给出；特别对 p=q=x²，Var = 4 - 12/d + 8/d²，且当 d→∞ 时退化为 GUE 波动的 Wigner 值 4。
