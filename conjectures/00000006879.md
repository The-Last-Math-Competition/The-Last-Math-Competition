# 00000006879

**English.** Definition: The zeros of noncommutative polynomials with free zeros. Conjecture: The free zeros of polynomials hold, the combination of the zeros being matrix spectra. (matrix-spectrum free zeros of polynomials)

**中文。** 定义：非交换多项式的零点：自由零点。猜想：自由的零点：多项式的自由零点且点的组合为矩阵谱。（非交换多项式矩阵谱自由零点多项式组合自由零点谱矩阵多项式零点谱自由矩阵）
