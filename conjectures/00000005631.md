# 00000005631

**CP2-11 ◆** — source: 173_组合几何III_凸位置.md

**English.** Definition: The colored intersection theorem, of colorful Helly type, is the intersection theorem with colors. Conjecture: In the three-dimensional case the color count and the intersection multiplicity form an explicit pair, degenerating to the classical Helly value 4 in the monochromatic case; the feasible domain of the pair is staircase-shaped, with jumps at prime color counts; and the lower bound of the multiplicity grows logarithmically in the color count with explicit rate. (three-dimensional color-multiplicity pair)

**中文。** 定义：染色交定理（彩色 Helly 型）：带颜色的相交定理。猜想：三维情形色数与交重数构成显式二元组且单色时退化为经典 Helly 值 4；二元组的可行域为阶梯型且阶梯的跳变在色数为素数处；重数的下界随色数对数增长且增长率显式。（染色三维色数重数二元组）
