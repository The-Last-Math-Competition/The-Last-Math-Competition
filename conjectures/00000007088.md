**English.** Definition: The growth law of Floer complexes with the exponent of boundary maps. Conjecture: The growth exponent of boundary maps is the symplectic scale of chains, and the saturation of the scale is monotonicity. (monotone-saturated symplectic-scale boundary growth)

**中文。** 定义：Floer 复形的增长律：边映射的指数。猜想：Floer 复形的增长：边映射的增长的指数为链的辛尺度且尺度的饱和为单调性。（Floer 边映射辛尺度单调饱和指数增长）
