# 00000001594

**IS-24 ★** — source: 38_可积系统与精确可解模型.md

**English.** Definition: The scaling limit of Ising diagonal correlations is given by the Fredholm determinant of the Painlevé VI tau function. Conjecture: The kernel of this determinant is an explicit hypergeometric kernel (a combination of Bessel algebraic kernels), replacing the Jacobi elliptic kernel; the spectral scaling of the kernel is t^{1/2}, explicitly linked to the λ parameterization of PVI.

**中文。** 定义：Ising 对角关联的标度极限由 Painlevé VI 的 τ 函数的 Fredholm 行列式给出。猜想：该行列式的核为显式超几何核(Bessel 代数核的组合),代替 Jacobi 椭圆核;核的谱标度为 t^{1/2},与 PVI 的 λ 参数化显式关联。
