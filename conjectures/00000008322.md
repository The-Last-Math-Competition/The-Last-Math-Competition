# 00000008322

**English.** Definition: The Schmidt winning property of the set Bad of badly approximable numbers (irrationals with a positive lower bound c(alpha)): sch_win. Conjecture: Any finite cross product Bad(p_1) x ... x Bad(p_k) is winning (a cross product winning law); and the strengthening of Bad to absolute winning is a proper subset of Bad (an absolute winning proper containment law); the elements of the difference set (Bad but not absolutely winning) are constructed by parametrized Cantor splicing (a Cantor splicing example); and the difference set has dimension 1 (a full-dimensional difference law). (badly approximable cross product Cantor splicing)

**中文。** 定义：坏可逼近数集 Bad（常数下界 c(α)>0 的无理数）的 Schmidt 赢集性：Bad 在 Schmidt 博弈下的赢性 sch_win。猜想：Bad 的任意有限叉积 Bad(p₁)×…×Bad(p_k) 为赢（叉积赢律）；且 Bad 在绝对赢（absolute winning）的强化（绝对赢集）为 Bad 的真子集（绝对赢真包含律）；包含的差集（Bad 但非绝对赢）的元素的构造为参数化的 Cantor 拼接（Cantor 拼接例）；差集的维数为 1（满维差集律）。
