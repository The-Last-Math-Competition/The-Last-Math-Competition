# 00000009750

**English.** Definition: Algorithmization of differential Galois theory: computational criteria for Liouvillian solvability. Conjecture: Liouvillian solvability of linear differential equations is decidable in polynomial time; the complexity of the decision algorithm is polynomial in the product of equation order and coefficient degree, with the exponent controlled by the nesting depth of the Risch structure. (differential Galois polynomial algorithm)

**中文。** 定义：微分 Galois 理论的算法化：Liouvillian 可解性的计算判据。猜想：线性微分方程的 Liouvillian 可解性可在多项式时间判定；判定算法的复杂度为方程阶与系数次数乘积的多项式且指数由 Risch 结构的嵌套深度控制。（微分 Galois 多项式算法）
