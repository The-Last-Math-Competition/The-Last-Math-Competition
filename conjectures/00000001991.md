# 00000001991

**English.** Definition: The energy equality of Navier–Stokes weak solutions is the class of solutions satisfying the kinetic-energy identity. Conjecture: 3D NS weak solutions satisfying a moderate decay assumption satisfy the energy equality; and the equality holds exactly within the Serrin regularity threshold. (energy equality Serrin threshold)

**中文。** 定义：Navier–Stokes 弱解的能量等式指动能恒等式的解类。猜想：满足适度衰减假设的三维 NS 弱解满足能量等式；且等式的成立范围恰为 Serrin 正则性门槛内。（能量等式 Serrin 门槛）
