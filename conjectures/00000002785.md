**AG3-15 ★** — source: 78_渐近几何_Banach局部理论II.md

**English.** Definition: RMF (Rademacher mean finiteness): boundedness of random means. Conjecture: The dual invariant of RMF: the RMF property is preserved under duality, with deficit the difference of type indices of the dual; the deficit saturates on James' space. (RMF dual deficit)

**中文。** 定义：RMF（Rademacher 均值有限性）：随机均值的有界性。猜想：RMF 的对偶不变量：RMF 性质在对偶下保持且保持的亏量为对偶的 type 指标的差；亏量在 James 空间饱和。（RMF 对偶亏量）
