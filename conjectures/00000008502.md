# 00000008502

**SDG-02 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: dimension d(G) of Zariski closure of differential Galois group. Conjecture: d(G) ≤ n² (n is equation order) and if and only if dimension spectrum of G of G = GL_n; quadratic system of equations y″=ay is {1,2,3,4} and every by explicit family realized of coefficient field. (differential Galois dimension spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：微分 Galois 群的 Zariski 闭包的维数 d(G)。猜想：d(G) ≤ n²（n 为方程阶）且等号当且仅当 G = GL_n；二次方程组 y″=ay 的 G 的维数谱为 {1,2,3,4} 且每个值由系数域的显式族实现。（微分 Galois 维数谱）
