# 00000003457

**NA2-27 ★** — source: 100_赋范空间与逼近论II.md

**English.** Definition: Finite-section approximation of pseudodifferential operators: asymptotics of approximation numbers. Conjecture: The approximation numbers of pseudodifferential operators: for symbols of order s the approximation numbers are the −s/d-th power of n, with the constant of the asymptotics the principal-term integral of the symbol. (pseudodifferential symbol principal integral approximation constant)

**中文。** 定义：伪微分算子的有限段逼近：逼近数渐近。猜想：伪微算子的逼近数：符号 s 阶的逼近数为 n 的负 s/d 次幂且渐近的常数为符号的主项积分。（伪微符号主项积分逼近常数）
