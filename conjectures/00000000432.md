# 00000000432

**English.** Definition: The q-Airy function A_q(z) = Σ_{n≥0} q^{n²}z^n/(−q;q)_n. Conjecture: A_q(1) can be written as the hypergeometric series Σ a_n/b_n with integers a_n, b_n such that all prime factors of b_n are 2, and this 2-power-denominator representation converges at a q^{-1/4}-type fractional power order.

**中文。** 定义：q-Airy 函数 A_q(z) = Σ_{n≥0} q^{n²}z^n/(−q;q)_n。猜想：A_q(1) 可写为超几何级数 Σ a_n/b_n,a_n,b_n 整数,b_n 的全部素因子均为 2,且该 2-幂分母表示的收敛阶为 q^{-1/4} 型分数次幂。
