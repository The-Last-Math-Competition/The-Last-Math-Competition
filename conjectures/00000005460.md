**CN2-20 △** — source: 167_计算数论_算法复杂性.md

**English.** Definition: The shortest-vector complexity and the reduction trajectory are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two lattices with the same complexity but different reduction trajectories, and the separation is realized by an explicit lattice pair with the same spectrum but different bases. (same shortest-vector complexity-different trajectory separation)

**中文。** 定义：最短向量复杂度与规约轨迹指两层。猜想：存在两格的复杂度相同而规约轨迹不同,分离由等谱异基的显式格对实现。（最短向量复杂度同-轨迹异分离）
