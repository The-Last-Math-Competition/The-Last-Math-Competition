# 00000004301

**English.** Definition: The j-invariant of CM theory is the modular invariant of the complex multiplication of an imaginary quadratic field. Conjecture: In the prime-ideal factorization of the j-value of a field of class number h, the smallest norm of a prime satisfies an upper bound of type h to the 1/2+epsilon, and this exponent cannot be improved. (exponent bound of the smallest splitting prime for CM)

**中文。** 定义：CM 的 j-不变量指虚二次域复乘理论的模不变量。猜想：类数 h 域的 j 值素理想分解中范数最小素数满足 h 的 1/2 加 ε 次幂型上界,且该指数不可改进。（CM 最小分裂素指数界）
