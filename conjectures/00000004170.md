# 00000004170

**CM3-20 △** — source: 124_解析数论III_圆法.md

**English.** Definition: The singular series and the main term are respectively the local Euler product and the global arc integral. Conjecture: There exists a family of equations whose singular series converges everywhere while the main-term integral diverges, the smallest degree where this occurs is 4, and the divergence is of logarithmic type. (singular series-main term divergence separation)

**中文。** 定义：奇异级数与主项分别指局部 Euler 积与整体弧积分。猜想：存在方程族其奇异级数处处收敛而主项积分发散,出现的最小次数为 4 且发散为对数型。（奇异级-主项发散分离）
