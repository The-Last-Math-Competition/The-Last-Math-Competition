**NLA-21 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: randomized algorithm condition number average of stability. Conjecture: average condition number is dimension linear and ratio of most is dimension; and core of ratio is Wishart spectrum. (average condition number Wishart spectrum linear)

**中文。** 定义：稳定性的随机算法条件数平均。猜想：平均条件数为维数线性且最坏情形的比为根号维数；且比的核心为 Wishart 谱。（平均条件数 Wishart 谱线性根比）
