# 00000008791

**NLA-21 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Averaged condition numbers of randomized algorithms for stability. Conjecture: The average condition number is linear in dimension with worst-case ratio the square root of dimension; and the core of the ratio is the Wishart spectrum. (average condition Wishart spectrum linear root ratio)

**中文。** 定义：稳定性的随机算法条件数平均。猜想：平均条件数为维数线性且最坏情形的比为根号维数；且比的核心为 Wishart 谱。（平均条件数 Wishart 谱线性根比）
