# 00000008165

**English.** Definition: The conformal embedding of the renormalization of site percolation: the embedding invariant perc_emb of the RG flow of critical percolation. Conjecture: The matching constant of perc_emb (the renormalized embedding of percolation invariants) with SLE_6 (the embedding constant) is sqrt 3/2; the correction of the mismatch of the embedding (lattice-continuum distortion) is 1/L (a first-order correction law), with correction coefficient the zeta value zeta(z) of the lattice coordination number (a coordination zeta law); and the coefficient of the higher-order distortion term (order L^{-2}) is a normalization of the Euler constant gamma (a gamma-normalized second-order law). (percolation SLE embedding coordination zeta)

**中文。** 定义：固渗流的重整化的共形嵌入：临界渗流的 RG 流的嵌入不变量 perc_emb。猜想：perc_emb（渗流的不变量的重整化嵌入）的 SLE_6 的匹配常数（嵌入常数）为 √3/2（黄金-√3 常数）；且嵌入的失配（格点-连续的畸变）的修正为 1/L（一阶修正律）；修正系数为格点配位数的 zeta 值 ζ(z)（配位 zeta 律）；畸变的高阶（L^{-2} 项）的系数为 Euler 常数 γ 的规范化（γ 规范化二阶律）。
