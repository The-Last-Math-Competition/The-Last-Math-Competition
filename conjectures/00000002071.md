# 00000002071

**UA-21 ★** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: The formal codimension of a fusion category is the reciprocal of the dimensions of its indecomposable objects. Conjecture: The formal codimensions of near-group fusion categories are integers dividing the square of the category dimension; the classification is uniquely determined by the prime factors of the codimension.

**中文。** 定义：融合范畴的形式余码指范畴中不可分解对象维数的倒数。猜想：near-group 融合范畴的形式余码皆为整数且整除范畴维数的平方;分类由余码的素因子唯一确定。
