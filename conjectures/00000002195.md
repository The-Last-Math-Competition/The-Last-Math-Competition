# 00000002195

**EC-25 ★** — source: 58_极值集合论与组合.md

**English.** Definition: The speed of the substitution product of hereditary properties. Conjecture: The speed of the substitution product of perfect-graph properties is e^{(c+o(1))nlog n} with c = 1/2 (a formula); the speed exponent follows from the singularity of the generating function of modular decompositions.

**中文。** 定义：遗传性质的替换乘积的速度。猜想：完美图性的替换乘积的速度为 e^{(c+o(1))nlog n},c = 1/2(公式);速度指数由模体分解的生成函数的奇性。
