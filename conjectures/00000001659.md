# 00000001659

**English.** Conjecture: In the suspension interval [A[1], A] of a negative Calabi–Yau category, the stable hearts correspond bijectively to interval t-structures (suspension-heart bijection); and the correspondence is uniquely realized by the tilting functors of interval hearts.

**中文。** 猜想：负 Calabi–Yau 范畴的悬浮区间 [A[1], A] 内的稳定心与区间 t-结构一一对应(悬挂-心一一性)；且对应由区间心的倾斜函子唯一实现。
