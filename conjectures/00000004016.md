# 00000004016

**RP2-16 ★** — source: 119_随机分析II_粗路径.md

**English.** Definition: The Marcus correction is the geometric-type integration correction for paths with jumps. Conjecture: The composition of the corrections of the jump and continuous parts requires no additional universal constant, the single-jump correction is exactly the action of the vector-field flow e^J, and multiple jumps compose in jump order. (universality of single Marcus jumps)

**中文。** 定义：Marcus 校正指带跳路径的几何式积分修正。猜想：跳与连续部分的校正复合不需额外普适常数,单跳校正恰为向量场流 e^J 的作用,多重跳按跳序复合。（Marcus 单跳普适性）
