# 00000007835

**MSE-25 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The quasimodular bound of twisted Gromov-Witten potentials: the cohomological weight truncation of the expansion coefficients of the GW potential of an orbifold curve [C/mu_r]. Conjecture: The GW potential of a weighted projective line P(a,b) is the embedded image of the ring of quasimodular forms on Gamma(1) times chi, with weight exactly the spectral value of the inflated age; and the coefficient growth is subexponential of factorial type with discriminant index (a+b)/(ab). (weighted projective line GW quasimodularity)

**中文。** 定义：twisted Gromov–Witten 位势的岑界模性：orbifold 曲线 [C/μ_r] 的 GW 势的展开系数的上同调权截断。猜想：对加权射影线 P(a,b) 的 GW 势为 ring of quasi-modular forms on Γ(1)·χ 的嵌入像，其权次恰为 inflated age 的谱值；且系数增长率为 (n!)^{-1} 型亚指数，判别指数为 (a+b)/(ab)。
