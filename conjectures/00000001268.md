# 00000001268

**English.** Definition: The palindromic defect is the count of defect letters of rich-in-palindromes words. Conjecture: Every infinite word can be repaired to a rich word by at most one letter insertion; and the minimality of the repair position is uniquely given by the defect center. (defect repair by a single insertion)

**中文。** 定义：回文缺陷为富回文词的缺陷字母计数。猜想：任何无穷词可由至多一次字母插入修补为富回文；且修补位置的极小性由缺陷中心唯一给出。（缺陷修补单次插入）
