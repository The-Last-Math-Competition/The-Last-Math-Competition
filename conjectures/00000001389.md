# 00000001389

**English.** Conjecture: A generalization of Borel determinacy (known): the least large-cardinal strength equivalent to determinacy for games with Π¹₁ payoff is infinitely many Woodin cardinals (determinacy strength).

**中文。** 猜想：Borel 决定性(已知)的可推广:博弈的 payoff 为 Π¹₁ 时决定性的超大基数的等价刻画的最小强度为 infinitely many Woodins(决定性强度)。
