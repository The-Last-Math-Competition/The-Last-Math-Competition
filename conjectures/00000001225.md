**CG-25 △** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The firefighter problem (protecting one vertex per step). Conjecture: The trees whose firefighter survival count is at least n/2 are exactly the caterpillar class (a half-survival structure).

**中文。** 定义：firefighter 问题(每步保护一点)。猜想：树的 firefighter 存活数 ≥ n/2 的树的结构为毛虫类(存活半数结构)。
