# 00000005934

**English.** Definition: The distribution tail is the tail asymptotics of the limit law. Conjecture: The left tail is negative three halves and the right tail negative three, with exact coefficients, the combination of the exactness being the saddle-point exponents. (exact saddle-point coefficients of both tails)

**中文。** 定义：分布尾部指极限律的尾渐近。猜想：左负二分之三与右负三型值且系数精确；且精确的组合为鞍点指数。（尾鞍点指数左右精确系数）
