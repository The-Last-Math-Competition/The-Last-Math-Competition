**English.** Definition: The pushforward is the direct image F_* of the structure sheaf through Frobenius. Conjecture: The direct-sum decomposition of split surfaces is given by the explicit spectrum of line bundles, with coefficients the combination of multisecant degrees (rank functions); at large characteristic the decomposition is of binomial type, the type given by the Newton polygon; and the multiplicity generating function of the decomposition is rational with poles at roots of unity. (multisecant spectrum of pushforward direct sums)

**中文。** 定义：挤压：结构层经弗罗贝尼乌斯的前推（F_*）。猜想：分裂曲面的直和分解由线丛的显式谱给出且系数为多重再生度（秩函数）的组合；大特征下分解为二项式型且型由牛顿多边形给出；分解的重数生成函数为有理函数且极点为单位根。（挤压直和多重再生度谱）
