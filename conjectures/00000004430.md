# 00000004430

**English.** Definition: One-point distributions and joint distributions are the marginals and the full law of a process. Conjecture: There exist two integrable systems whose one-point distributions all coincide while their two-point joint distributions differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of zero-range processes. (one-point-joint separation by a zero-range pair)

**中文。** 定义：单点分布与联合分布指过程的边缘与整体律。猜想：存在两个可积系统的全部单点分布相同而两点联合分布不同,分离由零程过程的显式对实现。（单点-联合分离零程对）
