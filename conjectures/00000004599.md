**HA3-29 ★** — source: 138_双曲几何的解析面.md

**English.** Definition: Train tracks are the switch-graph models of curves, and the weight space is their nonnegative weight cone. Conjecture: The count of integral weight lattice points is an explicit evaluation of the track characteristic polynomial at positive integers, closed and computable step by step. (characteristic integer counting of train tracks)

**中文。** 定义：列车轨道指曲线的开关图模型,权空间指其非负权锥。猜想：整权格点的计数为轨道示性多项式在正整数处的显式求值,求值封闭且可逐步计算。（列车轨道示性整数计数）
