# 00000004345

**FP2-15 ★** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: McCann displacement convexity is the convexity condition of functionals along Wasserstein geodesics. Conjecture: Strict displacement convexity requires an epsilon-minimal correction of the Hessian of the potential, and the minimal epsilon is an explicit geometric quantity of the curvature of the support and cannot be smaller. (minimal strictification correction of McCann)

**中文。** 定义：McCann 位移凸性指 Wasserstein 测地线上泛函的凸性条件。猜想：严格位移凸性需势 Hessian 的 ε 最小修正,最小的 ε 为支撑曲率的显式几何量且不可再小。（McCann 严格化最小修正）
