# 00000001370

**English.** Conjecture: The theorem that every non-hyperfinite countable Borel equivalence relation E satisfies E ≥_B E₀ can be proved by a single Borel-circuit construction (a unified proof of the mixed dichotomy).

**中文。** 猜想：任何非超有限的计数 Borel 等价关系 E 满足 E ≥_B E₀ 的证明可由单一 Borel 电路构造给出(混合二分的统一证明)。
