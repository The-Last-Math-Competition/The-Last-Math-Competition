# 00000008612

**English.** Definition: Distributed estimation means splitting samples across m machines. Conjecture: The rate loss under B bits of communication is of type (mB/n)^{1/2}; and the optimal one-bit-per-machine encoding is of sign type. (distributed communication loss)

**中文。** 定义：分布式估计指 m 机样本分割。猜想：通信 B 比特的速率亏损为 (mB/n)^{1/2} 型；一维通信的最优编码为符号型。（分布式通信亏损）
