# 00000002001

**PD-11 ◆** — source: 52_偏微分方程II.md

**English.** Definition: Kerr stability is the nonlinear stability of rotating black-hole metrics under Einstein vacuum. Conjecture: Slowly rotating Kerr (a ≪ M) is nonlinearly stable; for extremal Kerr (a = M), the blue-shift instability grows at the rate e^{ct^{1/2}} (the exact growth exponent 1/2).

**中文。** 定义：Kerr 稳定性指带旋转黑洞度量在 Einstein 真空下的非线性稳定性。猜想：小旋转 Kerr(a≪ M) 为非线性稳定;极端 Kerr(a=M) 的蓝移不稳定性增长率为 e^{c t^{1/2}}(精确增长指数 1/2)。
