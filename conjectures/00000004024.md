# 00000004024

**English.** Definition: The rough path metric space is the signature group under the p-variation distance. Conjecture: The spectral radius of the compactification of its unit ball is given by the growth radius of the free Lie algebra, with the explicit value a combinatorial quantity of the form (d-1)^{1/2}. (compactification spectral radius of rough paths)
