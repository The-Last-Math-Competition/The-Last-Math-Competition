# 00000008955

**ISO-05 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: A Backlund transform: a constructive map from solutions to solutions. Conjecture: The automorphism group of its iteration tree is the corresponding affine Weyl group, and the composed word length equals the depth. (Backlund Weyl word length)

**中文。** 定义：Bäcklund 变换指解到解的构造映射。猜想：其迭代生成树的自同构群为相应仿射 Weyl 群且合成字长等于深度。（Bäcklund Weyl 字长）
