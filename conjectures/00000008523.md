# 00000008523

**SDG-23 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: Parametrized differential Galois theory studies the variation of Galois groups of a family of differential equations with parameters. Conjecture: The Galois group is upper semicontinuous over the parameter space; the codimension of jumping strata is at least 1 and the number of jumps is finite. (parameter Galois continuity)

**中文。** 定义：参数化微分 Galois 理论研究一族微分方程的 Galois 群随参数变化。猜想：Galois 群在参数空间为上连续；跳跃层的余维数 ≥ 1 且跳跃的次数有限。（参数 Galois 连续性）
