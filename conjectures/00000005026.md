**English.** Definition: A universal sentence characterizes a unique structure up to isomorphism. Conjecture: The smallest universal sentence with four variables is constructed explicitly from a four-component encoding of invariants, of polynomial size, and four variables is the lower bound. (explicit construction of the four-variable minimal universal sentence)

**中文。** 定义：通用句子指在同构意义下刻画唯一结构的句子。猜想：四变量的最小通用句子由不变量的四元编码显式构造,构造多项式尺寸且变量数四为下界。（四变量最小通用句子显式构造）
