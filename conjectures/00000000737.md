# 00000000737

**PA-37 △** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: The noncommutative Iwasawa main conjecture. Conjecture: For general p-solvable extensions, the characteristic ideals of the Pontryagin dual form an increasing union (a noncommutative order limit).

**中文。** 定义：非交换 Iwasawa 主猜想。猜想：对一般 p-可解扩张,Pontryagin 对偶的特征理想为递增并(非交换顺序极限)。
