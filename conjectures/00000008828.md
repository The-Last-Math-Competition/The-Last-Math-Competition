# 00000008828

**English.** Definition: Subgradient randomization: convergence with noisy directions. Conjecture: Random subgradient convergence has the square-root reciprocal rate; decreasing step sizes are optimal; and the decreasing steps are realized by the harmonic sequence. (random subgradient root-rate optimal steps)

**中文。** 定义：次梯度随机化为噪声方向的收敛。猜想：随机次梯度的收敛为根号倒数码率；且递减步长为最优；且递减步长的实现为调和序列。（随机次梯度根率最优步长）
