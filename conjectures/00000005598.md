# 00000005598

**EL2-08 ★** — source: 172_精确线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: A sparse determinant has few nonzero entries. Conjecture: The complexity conversion is an explicit power of the nonzero count, the power is controlled by the treewidth of the support graph, and path graphs attain the bound. (treewidth-power complexity conversion of sparse determinants)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏行列式指非零元少的行列式。猜想：计算复杂度换算为非零元数的显式幂,幂由支持图的树宽控制且路径图达到界。（稀疏树宽幂复杂度换算）
