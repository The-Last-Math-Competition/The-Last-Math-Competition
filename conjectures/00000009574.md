# 00000009574

**English.** Definition: The asymptotic cone of Teichmueller space depends on the choice of ultrafilter; its uniqueness is equivalent to quasi-isometric rigidity in coarse geometry. Conjecture: Any two asymptotic cones of Teichmueller space are isomorphic, and isomorphic to the cone lattice union of the WP asymptotic cone; and the non-Hilbertianity of the asymptotic cone has an explicit obstruction from the branching geodesic structure of the curve graph. (asymptotic cone uniqueness)
