# 00000006822

**English.** Definition: Lomonosov's theorem with compact-operator commutation. Conjecture: Operators commuting with a compact operator exist, the invariance of the operator being triangularization. (triangularizing Lomonosov commutants)

**中文。** 定义：Lomonosov 定理：紧算子交换。猜想：Lomonosov 的存在：与紧算子交换的算子且算子的不变为三角化。（Lomonosov 定理紧算子交换三角化不变存在组合交换三角化紧算子不变三角算子）
