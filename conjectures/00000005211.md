# 00000005211

**English.** Definition: Tensor rigidity is the extreme property of incompressibility, the absence of dimension-reducing embeddings. Conjecture: The rigidity classification of two-dimensional matrix spaces is an explicit two-valued criterion given by the irreducibility of symbolic polynomials; the rigidity criterion is invariant under field extensions; and the smallest counterexample to rigidity is given by 2x2 symbolic spaces, with space count 2. (two-dimensional symbolic irreducibility criterion of rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：张量刚性：不可压缩（无降维嵌入）的极端性质。猜想：二维矩阵空间的刚性分类为二值的显式判据且由符号多项式的不可约性给出；刚性判据在域扩张下不变；非刚性的最小反例由 2×2 符号空间给出且空间数为 2。（二维刚性符号不可约判据）
