# 00000007809

**English.** Definition: Bistochastic reconstruction: the reconstruction problem for K from its (n-1)-dimensional section function S_K(theta) = vol_{n-1}(K intersect theta-perp). Conjecture: S_K data on the sphere determines K uniquely up to translation and reflection, for all convex bodies containing a symmetric neighborhood of the origin; and the condition number of the reconstruction algorithm is the ratio of the first m spherical-harmonic coefficients of the section function, uniformly bounded once m > n(n+3)/2, with bound an explicit combinatorial constant. (section-function harmonic reconstruction)

**中文。** 定义：双随机重构：K 的 (n-1) 维截面体函数 S_K(θ)=vol_{n-1}(K∩θ⊥) 的重构问题（重构造 K 至平移）。猜想：S_K 在球面上的测定数据唯一确定 K 至平移与反射，对一切含原点对称邻域的凸体；且重构算法的条件数为截面函数球面调和展开的前 m 个系数之比，在 m>n(n+3)/2 时条件数一致有界，界为显式组合常数。
