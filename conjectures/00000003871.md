# 00000003871

**RM3-21 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The maximal gap G_max of β-Hermite is the largest normalized adjacent eigenvalue difference. Conjecture: E(G_max)·βπN/2 − log N converges to a constant c_β strictly increasing in β, with c_2 = 0. (maximal gap center constant)

**中文。** 定义：β-Hermite 的最大间隔 G_max 指归一化后最大的相邻特征值差。猜想：E(G_max)·βπN/2 − log N 收敛于常数 c_β,且 c_β 关于 β 严格递增,c_2 = 0。（最大间隔中心常数）
