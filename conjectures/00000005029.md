# 00000005029

**English.** Definition: Sparse random graphs are the random graphs G(n, c/n) of constant expected degree. Conjecture: The convergent limiting theory at the critical sparsity c = 1 is the explicit sentence set of the forest axioms; the quantifier depth is unbounded with finitely many sentences per level; for supercritical c > 1 the limiting theory contains the giant-component sentence of logarithmic quantifier depth. (critical sparse forest limiting theory)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏随机图：期望度常数的随机图 G(n,c/n)。猜想：临界稀疏 c=1 的收敛极限理论为森林公理的显式句子集；量词深度无界而每层句子为有限；超临界 c>1 时极限理论含巨分量句子且其量词深度为对数型。（临界稀疏森林极限理论）
