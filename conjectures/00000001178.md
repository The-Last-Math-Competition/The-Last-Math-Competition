# 00000001178

**LT-78 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The Racah normalization of the 6j-symbols of SU(2)_q is the normalization identity Σ|6j|². Conjecture: A closed form exists: each 6j-symbol is a single summation of a generalized hypergeometric series {}_4φ_3 of three q-Clebsch–Gordan coefficients; at roots of unity the series degenerates to a polynomial identity, giving a combinatorial proof of the Racah normalization.

**中文。** 定义：SU(2)_q 的 6j-符号的 Racah 归一化指 Σ|6j|² 的归一化恒等式。猜想：存在 6j 符号的如下闭式:每个 6j 为三个 q-Clebsch–Gordan 系数的广义超几何级数 {}_4φ_3 的单重求和,且该级数在单位根处退化为多项式恒等式,给出 Racah 归一的组合证明。
