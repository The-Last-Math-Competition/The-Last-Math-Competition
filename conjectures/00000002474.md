# 00000002474

**English.** Definition: Asymptotics of q-series: the saddle-point method. Conjecture: The saddle-point asymptotics of q-series generating functions follows the Meinardus scheme: the main term is determined by the singular behavior near q = 1, with the error term controlled by the Dirichlet series of exponents. (q-series asymptotics saddle-point Meinardus)
