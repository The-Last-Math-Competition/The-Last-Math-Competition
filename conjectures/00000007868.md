# 00000007868

**RAM-28 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The rounding error exponent of randomized matrix multiplication: the energy epsilon_n of the output error of randomized Strassen-type algorithms (randomized block splits and recursion branches) relative to the Frobenius norm. Conjecture: Randomized branching lowers the growth exponent of epsilon_n from the deterministic 2^{log_2 n times 1/2} to 2^{log_2 n times 1/4} (a variance-halving law for the error); and every randomized branching strategy has exponent lower bound (1/4) log_2 n, attained by uniform random mirror branching (swapping multiplication order with equal probability). (Strassen randomized error halving)

**中文。** 定义：随机矩阵乘法的舍入误差指数：随机化 Strassen 型算法（随机的块分割与递归分支）输出误差的能量 ε_n（相对 Frobenius 范数）。猜想：随机化分支使 ε_n 的增长指数从确定性的 2^{log₂n·(1/2)} 降为 2^{log₂n·(1/4)}（误差方差减半律）；且任何随机化分支策略的指数下界为 1/4·log₂ n，达到该下界的策略为均匀随机镜像分支（等概率交换乘法顺序）。
