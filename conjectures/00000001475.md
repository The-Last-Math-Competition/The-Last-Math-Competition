# 00000001475

**CX-25 ★** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The moment surface is the convex hull of the image of the Veronese-type map (s,t) ↦ (1,s,t,s²,st,t²). Conjecture: The combinatorial types of all 2-faces of this convex hull have an explicit complete list: exactly the union of the 2-faces of the cyclic polytope C(n,3) with one additional oblique class; the list is complete over all parameter ranges.

**中文。** 定义：矩曲面指 Veronese 型映射 (s,t)↦(1,s,t,s²,st,t²) 的像的凸包。猜想：该凸包的全部 2-面(二维面)的组合类型有显式完全列表:恰为循环多胞体 C(n,3) 的 2-面与一个额外倾斜类之并;列表对一切参数域完备。
