# 00000008115

**NAG-05 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Brauer rank br(X) of Azumaya algebras over PI-equivalent (polynomial identity equivalent) noncommutative varieties. Conjecture: The supremum of the ranks of Azumaya algebras over a noncommutative variety (the maximal Azumaya rank) equals the exponent of the Brauer group of the center (an exponent-maximal-rank equality); the domain of existence of maximal Azumaya algebras (a central open set) has codimension at least 2 (a codimension-2 existence law); and the descent law of Brauer rank under blow-up: br(Bl_X) <= br(X)/p (a single-prime descent), the exception being sign blow-ups (a sign exception), whose minimal center is a rational curve (a rational exception law). (Azumaya exponent maximal-rank blow-up descent)

**中文。** 定义：PI-等价（多项式恒等式等价）的非交换簇的 Azumaya 代数的 Brauer 秩 br(X)。猜想：非交换簇的 Azumaya 代数的秩的上确界（极大 Azumaya 秩）= 中心的指数 exp(Brauer 群)（指数-极大秩等式）；且极大 Azumaya 代数的存在域（中心开集）的余维 ≥ 2（余 2 存在律）；Blow-up 下的 Brauer 秩的下降律：br(Bl_X) ≤ br(X)/p（单素下降），下降的例外为符号爆破（符号例外），符号例外的极小中心为有理曲线（有理例外律）。
