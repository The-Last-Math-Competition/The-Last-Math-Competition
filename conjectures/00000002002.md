**PD-12 ★** — source: 52_偏微分方程II.md

**English.** Definition: The Penrose inequality with electromagnetic charge is m ≥ √(E²+Q²) (the area-energy-charge relation). Conjecture: Its quasilocal version holds for spherically symmetric stable data; the global version can be proved by the charge-corrected inverse mean curvature flow.
