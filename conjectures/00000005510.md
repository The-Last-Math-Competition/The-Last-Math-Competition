**QN2-10 △** — source: 169_排队网络的图极限.md

**English.** Definition: The stationary distribution and the departure process are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two networks with the same stationary distribution but non-isomorphic departure processes, and the separation is realized by an explicit reparameterized pair with service dependence. (same stationary-different departure reparameterization separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：平稳分布与离开过程指两层。猜想：存在两网络的平稳分布相同而离开过程不同构,分离由服务相依的显式重参数对实现。（平稳同-离开异重参数分离对）
