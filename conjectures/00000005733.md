# 00000005733

**English.** Definition: The volume-constrained variational problem is the shape minimization with fixed volume. Conjecture: The multiplier is always the explicit spectral value of the minimal-ratio extremum, the spectral-value table is closed, and the extremum is uniquely attained. (unique extremum of the volume multiplier spectral values)

**中文。** 定义：容积约束变分指体积固定的形状极小化问题。猜想：乘子恒为极小比极值的显式谱值；谱值表封闭且极值达到唯一。（容积乘子谱值唯一极值）
