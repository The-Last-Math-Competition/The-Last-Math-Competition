# 00000001893

**English.** Conjecture: Nishioka's Mahler fields (function fields satisfying Mahler equations) have a model-theoretically minimal closure (closures of all subfields remain of Mahler type); the closure is exhausted by radical extensions of difference algebra.

**中文。** 猜想：Nishioka 的 Mahler 域(满足 Mahler 方程的函数域)有模型论的极小闭包(一切子域的闭包仍为 Mahler 型);闭包由差分代数的根式扩张穷举。
