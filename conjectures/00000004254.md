# 00000004254

**English.** Definition: The MNOP equivalence is the all-genus correspondence between DT and GW invariants. Conjecture: Via the GV intermediary invariants the conversion constant is 1, the higher-genus correction terms come only from families of rational curves, and the correction is given explicitly by the Hodge star of the BPS spectrum. (MNOP higher-genus correction structure)

**中文。** 定义：MNOP 等价指 DT 与 GW 不变量的全亏格对应。猜想：经 GV 中介不变量的换算常数为 1,高亏格修正项仅来自有理曲线族,修正由 BPS 谱的 Hodge 星显式给出。（MNOP 高亏格修正结构）
