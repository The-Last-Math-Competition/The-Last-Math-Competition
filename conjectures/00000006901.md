# 00000006901

**English.** Definition: Matrix rigidity is the minimal number of modifications under a rank constraint. Conjecture: A construction spectrum for explicit rigidity lower bounds exists and the candidate spectrum is finite; and attaining the super-logarithmic-squared target is the flagship problem of GCT. (candidate spectrum of explicit rigidity constructions)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵刚性为秩约束下的最小修改数。猜想：显式刚性下界的构造谱存在且候选谱有限；且超对数平方目标的达成为 GCT 的标志问题。（刚性显式构造候选谱）
