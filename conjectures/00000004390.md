# 00000004390

**KK2-30 △** — source: 131_代数K理论II_迹方法.md

**English.** Definition: Even and odd K-groups are the two halves of the K-theory grading. Conjecture: There exists a family of spectra all of whose even K-groups are isomorphic while the torsion structures of the odd K-groups differ, and the separation is constructed explicitly via towers of Z divided by p extensions. (even-odd K separation by extension towers)

**中文。** 定义：偶与奇 K 群指 K 理论分次的两个半侧。猜想：存在谱族其一切偶 K 群同构而奇 K 群的挠结构不同,分离由 ℤ 除 p 扩张塔显式构造。（偶-奇 K 分离扩塔）
