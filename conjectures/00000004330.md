# 00000004330

**AN4-30 △** — source: 129_数论IV_代数数论显式方法.md

**English.** Definition: Congruence counting is the sequence of class group counts modulo each power of p. Conjecture: There exist two number fields whose congruence counts for the same prime p all agree while their Iwasawa mu and lambda invariants differ, and the smallest prime is 5. (minimal prime of congruence-Iwasawa separation)

**中文。** 定义：同余计数指模 p 各次幂的类群计数列。猜想：存在两个数域对同一素 p 的一切同余计数相等而 Iwasawa 的 μ 与 λ 不变量不同,最小素数为 5。（同余-Iwasawa 分离最小素）
