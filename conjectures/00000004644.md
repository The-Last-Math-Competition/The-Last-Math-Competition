# 00000004644

**English.** Definition: Symplectic fermions are the VOAs freely generated in odd degree. Conjecture: The failure of semisimplicity of their representation category occurs only in the zero-charge layer, the smallest non-semisimple block has dimension four, and it is isomorphic to the fusion kernel of the first member of the triplet family. (four-dimensional breaking block of symplectic fermions)
