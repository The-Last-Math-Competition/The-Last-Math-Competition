# 00000001512

**KK-02 ★** — source: 36_代数K理论与非交换几何.md

**English.** Definition: The tame kernel K₂^T(O_F) is the kernel of K₂ modulo the integrality map. Conjecture: The 2-primary part of its order is always given by an explicit formula in the discriminant and the type of ramification at 2; the formula is simplest when 2 is totally ramified. (tame kernel 2-part ramification formula)

**中文。** 定义：tame 核 K₂^T(O_F) 指 K₂ 模整性映射的核。猜想：其阶的二准素部分恒由判别式与素二分歧型的显式公式给出；且公式在 2 完全分歧时最简。（tame 核 2 部分分歧公式）
