**English.** Definition: random preconditioner is sketch preconditioner. Conjecture: expectation of condition number is one ε and probability is exponent small; and kernel of exponent is subspace distance. (sketch preconditioner space distance exponent)

**中文。** 定义：随机预条件为 sketch 预条件子。猜想：条件数的期望为一加 ε 且失败概率为指数小；且指数的核为子空间距离。（sketch 预条件子空间距离指数失败）
