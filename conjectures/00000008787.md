# 00000008787

**NLA-17 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Randomized preconditioning: sketch preconditioners. Conjecture: The expected condition number is one plus epsilon with exponentially small failure probability; and the kernel of the exponent is the subspace distance. (sketch preconditioner subspace distance exponential failure)

**中文。** 定义：随机预条件为 sketch 预条件子。猜想：条件数的期望为一加 ε 且失败概率为指数小；且指数的核为子空间距离。（sketch 预条件子空间距离指数失败）
