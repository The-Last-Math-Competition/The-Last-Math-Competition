**LC-07 ◆** — source: 18_逻辑计算与拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: A slice knot bounds a disk in the four-ball; a ribbon knot bounds a ribbon disk. Conjecture: Slice and ribbon are equivalent; and the equivalence is certified through Rasmussen's s-invariant. (slice equals ribbon via Rasmussen invariant)

**中文。** 定义：slice 纽结为四球圆盘边界且 ribbon 为带圆盘。猜想：slice 与 ribbon 等价；且等价的核为 Rasmussen s 不变量。（slice-ribbon Rasmussen 不变量等价）
