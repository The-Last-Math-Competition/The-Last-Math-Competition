# 00000002597

**OL-07 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: The weak order of a Coxeter group: the element ordering by length function. Conjecture: Combinatorial rigidity of finite-interval lattices of weak orders of affine Weyl groups: the length-generating function of interval counts is a rational function with roots of unity as poles; the CW complex of an interval is a ball whose dimension is given by the cardinality of the descent set. (weak order interval rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Coxeter 群的弱序：以长度函数排序的群元素偏序。猜想：仿射 Weyl 群的弱序的有限区间格的组合刚性：区间个数的按长度生成函数为有理函数且极点为单位根；区间的 CW 复形为球且球维数由下降集的势给出。（弱序区间刚性）
