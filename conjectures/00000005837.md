**English.** Definition: The defect-group order is the local scale of the block, with the conversion of the order-four case. Conjecture: The conversion formula of order-four defect groups is explicit in the transitive fusion type, single-point fusion for cyclic groups; the number of transitive types is 2, the dichotomy delimited by dihedral fusion; and single-point-fusion blocks are equivalent to cyclic-defect blocks, the equivalence preserving the Cartan trace. (transitive-type conversion of order-four fusion)
