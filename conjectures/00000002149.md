# 00000002149

**English.** Definition: The Colin de Verdière parameter μ(G) is the spectral-rigidity invariant of a graph. Conjecture: μ(̄G) ≤ |V| − ω(G) − 1 (an upper bound for the complement); the bound is tight (attained by the complete graph with empty complement).

**中文。** 定义：colin de Verdière 参数 μ(G) 指图的谱刚性不变量。猜想：μ(̄ G) ≤ |V|-ω(G)-1(补图的上界);界为紧(由空补的完全图达到)。
