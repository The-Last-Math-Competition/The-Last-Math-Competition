# 00000001937

**LG-07 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: The subgroup growth zeta function is the Dirichlet series of subgroup-index counts. Conjecture: The abscissa of convergence of the zeta function of an arithmetic linear group is dim G/(dim G+1) (an explicit rational), uniquely determined by the algebraic dimension of the group.

**中文。** 定义：子群生长 ζ 函数指子群指数计数的 Dirichlet 级数。猜想：算术线性群的 ζ 的收敛横坐标为 dim G/(dim G+1)(显式有理数);横坐标由群的代数维数唯一确定。
