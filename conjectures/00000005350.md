# 00000005350

**English.** Definition: Categorified invariants and closures are the braid and knot layers. Conjecture: There exist two braids whose categorified invariants are all isomorphic but whose closed knots differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of Markov moves. (same categorification-different closure Markov separation)

**中文。** 定义：范畴化不变量与闭包指辫子与纽结两层。猜想：存在两辫子的全部范畴化不变量同构而闭包纽结不同,分离由马尔可夫移动的显式对实现。（范畴化同-闭包异马尔可夫分离）
