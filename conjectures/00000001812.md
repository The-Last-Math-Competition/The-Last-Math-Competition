**English.** Definition: Abundance means some positive multiple of the semi-canonical bundle is effective. Conjecture: Abundance holds in dimension 4 (dimension 3 is known); the four-dimensional case follows from the classification of minimal fibration structures (base a curve/surface/threefold), requiring no independent technique.
