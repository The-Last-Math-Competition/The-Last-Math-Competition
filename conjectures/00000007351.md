# 00000007351

**English.** Definition: The geometry of infinitesimals with nonstandard differential geometry. Conjecture: The tangent space of infinitesimal manifolds exists, and the realization of the space is the quotient of monads. (monad-quotient infinitesimal tangent space)

**中文。** 定义：无穷小的几何：非标准微分几何。猜想：无穷小的无穷小：无穷小流形的切空间且间的实现为单子的商。（无穷小流形单子商切空间无穷小实现非标准微分几何）
