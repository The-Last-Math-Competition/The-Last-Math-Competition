# 00000007862

**English.** Definition: The algorithmic success probability of the Lovasz local lemma: the expected number T(Phi) of restart rounds of randomized resampling on the variable-event hypergraph. Conjecture: T(Phi) is at most the explicit bound (sum p_i)/((1-ep*) product(1-p_i)) with p* the largest event probability; and the bound diverges as (1-ep*)^{-2} (a quadratic divergence law) as the critical product ep* tends to 1, the exponent 2 being the branching-process survival exponent of the critical restart cascade; adaptive event selection lowers the exponent to 1. (LLL resampling quadratic divergence)

**中文。** 定义：Lovász 局部引理的算法化成功概率：变量-事件超图上随机重采样的期望重启轮数 T(Φ)。猜想：T(Φ) 的上界为 (Σp_i)/((1-ep*)·Π(1-p_i)) 的显式式（p* 为最大事件概率）；且该界在临界交积 ep*→1 时发散为 (1-ep*)^{-2}（平方发散律），其指数 2 为临界重启级联的分支过程存活指数；自适应事件选择使指数降至 1。
