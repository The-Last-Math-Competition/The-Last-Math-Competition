**English.** Definition: A two-bridge knot is a double bridge knot whose symmetry is controlled by the structure of the continued fraction expansion. Conjecture: Symmetry exists if and only if the continued fraction expansion of p/q is palindromic; and the smallest crossing number of an asymmetric two-bridge knot is 12. (continued fraction symmetry criterion for two-bridge knots)

**中文。** 定义：two-bridge 结指双桥结，其对称性由连分数展开结构控制。猜想：对称性存在当且仅当 p/q 的连分数展开为回文型；非对称 two-bridge 结的最小交叉数为 12。（two-bridge 连分数对称判据）
