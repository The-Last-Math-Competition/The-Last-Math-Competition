**GBR-05 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Ringel-Hall algebras and quantum groups: the algebra isomorphism structure given by Green's correspondence. Conjecture: The multiplication structure constants of the monomial basis of the twisted Hall algebra are explicit polynomials in flagged Hall numbers; the structure constants agree with those of the PBW basis of the quantum group at critical parameters. (twisted Hall structure constants)

**中文。** 定义：Ringel–Hall 代数与量子群：Green 对应给出的代数同构结构。猜想：斜 Hall 代数的单项基的乘法结构常数为 flagged Hall 数的显式多项式；结构常数与量子群的 PBW 基的结构常数在临界参量处一致。（斜 Hall 结构常数）
