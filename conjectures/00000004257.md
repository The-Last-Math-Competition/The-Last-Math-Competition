**English.** Definition: The minimal generators of the Hall algebra are the sets of indecomposable generating objects. Conjecture: The lower bound of the degree spectrum of the generators is an explicit formula as the reciprocal of an exponential-type generating function, and the bound is optimal, attained by families of rigid objects. (degree spectrum lower bound of Hall generators)

**中文。** 定义：Hall 代数的极小生成元指不可分解生成对象集。猜想：生成元的度谱下界为指数型母函数倒数的显式公式,下界由刚性对象族达到最优。（Hall 生成元度谱下界）
