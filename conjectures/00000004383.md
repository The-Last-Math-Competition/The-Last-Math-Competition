# 00000004383

**English.** Definition: The THH of the sphere spectrum is the topological Hochschild homology of the unit spectrum itself. Conjecture: Its first five nonzero low-degree homotopy groups are explicit direct sums of F_p, and the first nonzero group lies in degree 1 and is cyclic of order 4. (explicit table of the first five THH groups of the sphere)
