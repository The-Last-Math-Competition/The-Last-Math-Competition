# 00000005292

**RS2-02 ◆** — source: 162_组合的代数_表示稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: An FI module is a module structure over the category of finite injections. Conjecture: The stable range of finitely generated FI modules is an explicit linear combination of generation degree and presentation degree, with constant one after normalization, and the range cannot be shortened. (linear optimality of FI stable ranges)

**中文。** 定义：FI 模指有限单射范畴上的模结构。猜想：有限生成的稳定范围为生成度与呈现度的显式线性组合,常数为一旦归一且范围不可再缩。（FI 稳定范围线性最优）
