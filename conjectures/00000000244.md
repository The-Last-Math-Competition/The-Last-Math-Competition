**English.** Definition: A nowhere-zero flow is an integer edge-flow with no zero values. Conjecture: Every bridgeless graph admits a nowhere-zero 5-flow; and the bound five is settled via decomposition into modular prime flows. (Tutte 5-flow via modular prime flow decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：处处非零流为边值流的无零流。猜想：每个无桥图有处处非零五流；且五的核为模素流分解。（Tutte 五流模素流分解无桥）
