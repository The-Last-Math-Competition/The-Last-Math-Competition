# 00000008361

**English.** Definition: The Berkovich skeleton comparison for tropicalization: the map sk_map from the skeleton SK(X) of the analytic space X^an to the tropicalization Trop(X). Conjecture: The fibers of sk_map (retraction from skeleton to tropicalization) are trees (a tree fiber law); the dimension of a generic fiber is 0 (a zero-dimensional generic fiber law); the isometry domain of the map (1-Lipschitz for the Tate distance) is the motivically normalized locus (a normalized isometry domain law); and the isometry-breaking locus has measure zero (a zero-measure breaking law). (Berkovich skeleton tree fibers normalized isometry)

**中文。** 定义：Trop 的 Berkovich 的骨架比较：解析空间 X^an 的骨架 SK(X) 与热带化 Trop(X) 的映射 sk_map。猜想：sk_map（骨架到热带化的收缩）的纤维（点的逆像）为树（树纤维律）；且纤维的维数（一般纤维的维数）= 0（零维一般纤维律）；映射的保距（Tate 距离的 1-Lipschitz）的等距域为母题正规化域（正规等距域律）；等距的破缺（非等距点）的测度为零（零测破缺律）。
