# 00000001329

**English.** Definition: The non-Archimedean Fubini–Study potential is the Boucksom–Jonsson potential. Conjecture: Subdifferentiability of the energy always yields a chain of identities between singularity types and algebraic invariants; and each link of the chain is an inequality. (energy-invariant identity chain)

**中文。** 定义：非阿基米德 Fubini–Study 位势指 Boucksom–Jonsson 位势。猜想：能量的次可微性恒给出奇异度与代数不变量的等式链；且链的每一环为一不等式。（能量-不变量等式链）
