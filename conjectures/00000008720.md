**MBL-10 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: exponential growth of many body Lyapunov exponent: Fock space on transition matrix product. Conjecture: many body Lyapunov exponent of one dimension random many body system always positive (positive); and its and exponent can polynomial computation (compu of table); exponent in MBL phase transition at of relations body (many body Thouless); small system of Thouless formula is explicit ().

**中文。** 定义：多体 Lyapunov 指数：Fock 空间上转移矩阵乘积的指数增长率。猜想：一维随机多体系统的多体 Lyapunov 指数恒正（正性律）；且其与 Thouless 公式的关系推广到多体（多体 Thouless 律）；小系统的指数可多项式计算（可计算律）；指数在 MBL 相变处的跳变为显式（跳变律）。
