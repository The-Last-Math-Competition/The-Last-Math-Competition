# 00000008720

**MBL-10 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: Many-body Lyapunov exponent: the exponential growth rate of products of transfer matrices on Fock space. Conjecture: The many-body Lyapunov exponent of one-dimensional random many-body systems is always positive (a positivity law); its relation with the Thouless formula extends to many bodies (a many-body Thouless law); the exponent for small systems is polynomially computable (a computability law); and the jump of the exponent at the MBL transition is explicit (a jump law). (many-body Lyapunov Thouless positivity jump)

**中文。** 定义：多体 Lyapunov 指数：Fock 空间上转移矩阵乘积的指数增长率。猜想：一维随机多体系统的多体 Lyapunov 指数恒正（正性律）；且其与 Thouless 公式的关系推广到多体（多体 Thouless 律）；小系统的指数可多项式计算（可计算律）；指数在 MBL 相变处的跳变为显式（跳变律）。
