**English.** Definition: The pro-etale fundamental group is the fundamental group under pro-etale contraction. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit count of generators, given for genus curves by base-point-free representations; the generator count is 2g plus the rank of relations; and base-point-freeness holds if and only if the base is Deligne-Mumford, with stack points exceptional. (base-point-free counting of the pro-etale fundamental group)

**中文。** 定义：仿射线基本群：收缩（pro-étale）下的基本群。猜想：换算公式为生成元的显式计数且亏格曲线时由基点无关的表示给出；生成元数为 2g 加秩的关系数；基点无关性当且仅当底为 Deligne–Mumford 且例外为栈点。（仿射线基群基点无关计数）
