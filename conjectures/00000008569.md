# 00000008569

**NSP-09 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The Hille-Yosida-Lumer-Phillips characterization of generators of C_0 semigroups. Conjecture: The bound constant of the quantitative version of the dissipativity condition of generators is e^{omega t} (an e^{omega t} law); the bound is attained by translation semigroups (a translation saturation law); non-dense domains can break the bound (a non-dense breaking law); and the recovery constant of regularization of the breaking is 1 (a unit recovery law). (Hille-Yosida dissipativity translation saturation)

**中文。** 定义：C₀ 半群的生成元的 Hille–Yosida–Lumer–Phillips 刻画。猜想：生成元的耗散条件的定量版的界常数为 e^{ωt}（e^ωt 律）；且该界由平移半群取得（平移饱和律）；非稠密定义域可突破该界（非稠密破缺律）；破缺的正则化的恢复常数为 1（单位恢复律）。
