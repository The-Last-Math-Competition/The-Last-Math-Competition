# 00000007357

**English.** Definition: Nonstandard compactification with monad compactification. Conjecture: The infinitesimal realization of compactification holds, and the boundary of the realization is the monad at infinity. (infinity-monad compactification boundary)

**中文。** 定义：非标准的紧化：单子紧化。猜想：单子紧化：空间的紧化的无穷小实现且现的边界为无穷远单子。（单子紧化无穷远单子边界空间无穷小实现紧化）
