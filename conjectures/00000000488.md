# 00000000488

**SA-88 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: M_n = max_λ f_λ is the largest dimension of an irreducible character of S_n. Conjecture: M_n/√(n!) → e^{-1/2}·√2, and the limit shape of the maximizing λ* is a modified Logan–Shepp curve with first-row length n − Θ(√n·log n), the constants given by the unique solution of the Euler–Lagrange equation of the variational problem.

**中文。** 定义：S_n 的不可约特征标维数 f_λ 的最大值 M_n = max_λ f_λ。猜想：M_n/√(n!) → e^{-1/2}·√2,且达到最大值的形状 λ* 的极限形状为修改的 Logan–Shepp 曲线,其首行长度为 n−Θ(√n·log n),常数由变分问题的 Euler–Lagrange 方程唯一解给出。
