# 00000007733

**English.** Definition: The free Stein discrepancy Sigma(X) measures the deviation of the distribution of a self-adjoint operator X from the semicircle law via free Fisher information. Conjecture: Among all distributions supported on a fixed interval [-2,2] without atoms, Sigma is minimized to 0 at the semicircle law, with next-smallest value attained by an explicit family of Jacobi-type distributions; among discrete measures the minimizer of Sigma is the three-point distribution with mass 1/4 at plus or minus sqrt 2 and mass 1/2 at 0. (minimizers of free Stein discrepancy)

**中文。** 定义：自由 Stein 不一致度 Σ(X) 度量自伴算子 X 的分布与半圆律的自由 Fisher 信息偏离。猜想：在所有支撑于固定区间 [-2,2] 且无原子的分布中，Σ 的最小值在半圆律处取得为 0，次小值由一族显式的 Jacobi 型分布取得；离散测度中 Σ 的极小值点为在 ±√2 处质量 1/4 与在 0 处质量 1/2 的三点分布。
