# 00000000255

**AA-02 ◆** — source: 16_代数与算术对象.md

**English.** Definition: The Tate–Shafarevich group Sha(E) of an elliptic curve. Conjecture: Sha(E) is finite; and when the root number of E is +1, #Sha(E) is a perfect square, while for root number −1 it is twice a perfect square (the BSD parity prediction).

**中文。** 定义：椭圆曲线的 Tate–Shafarevich 群 Sha(E)。猜想：Sha(E) 有限,且当 E 的根数为 +1 时 #Sha(E) 为完全平方,根数 −1 时为平方的二倍(BSD 平性预测)。
