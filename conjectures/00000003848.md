# 00000003848

**RC-28 ◆** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: Two-sided cells of the affine 0-Hecke monoid are its two-sided equivalence classes. Conjecture: Two-sided cells of type A_n^{(1)} correspond one-to-one with (n+1)-core partitions, and the number of left cells in the corresponding cell equals the number of distinct parts of the partition obtained by removing all (n+1)-hooks from that core. (affine cell-core dictionary)

**中文。** 定义：仿射 0-Hecke 幺半群的双边胞腔指其双边等价类。猜想：型 A_n^{(1)} 的双边胞腔与 (n+1)-核分拆一一对应,且对应胞腔的左胞腔个数恰等于该核分拆去掉全部 (n+1)-钩后所得分拆的不同部分个数。（仿射胞腔-核心字典）
