# 00000009903

**English.** Definition: Elementary generation of stable equivalences: quiver relations within stable equivalence classes. Conjecture: Two stably equivalent quivers are connected by chains of elementary one-vertex-gluing operations; the shortest chain length is an explicit function of the prime factor multiplicities of the Cartan determinants of the two algebras. (stable equivalence elementary chain)

**中文。** 定义：稳定等价的初等生成：稳定等价类的箭图关系。猜想：稳定等价的两个箭图由单顶点粘合的初等运算链连接；链的最短长度为两代数的 Cartan 行列式的素因子重数的显式函数。（稳定等价初等链）
