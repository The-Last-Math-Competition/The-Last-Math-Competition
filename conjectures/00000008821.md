**English.** Definition: Optimal recovery: sample-optimal reconstruction. Conjecture: The worst-case lower bound of optimal recovery holds (a lower bound law); the bound is attained by linear algorithms (a linearity law); nonlinear (sparse) improvements exist (a sparsity law); and the sparse quantification extends to phase retrieval (a phase law). (optimal recovery linear sparse phase)

**中文。** 定义：最优恢复（optimal recovery）：样本最优的重构。猜想：最优恢复的 (worst-case 的下界) 的（下界律）；且下界的达到（线性算法）的（线性律）；非线性的改进（稀疏的）的（稀疏律）；稀疏的量化（相位恢复的）的（相位律）。
