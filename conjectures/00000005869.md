# 00000005869

**English.** Definition: Pressure stabilization is the control structure of pressure modes. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit spectral window of the stability weight, of mesh-size order at the critical weight; the window width grows linearly with the polynomial degree; and pressure modes outside the window are spurious, with spurious-mode count a constant multiple of the degree. (spectral-window critical weight of pressure stabilization)

**中文。** 定义：压力稳定：压力模态的控制结构。猜想：换算公式为稳定权重的显式谱窗且临界权重处为网格尺寸阶；谱窗的宽度随多项式次数线性增长；窗外的压力模态为 spurious 且 spurious 模态数为次数的常数倍。（压力稳定谱窗临界权重）
