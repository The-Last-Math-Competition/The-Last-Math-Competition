**English.** Definition: Geometrization is the metric reconstruction of energy surfaces. Conjecture: The explicit conformal form of the Jacobi metric singularizes at zero energy, the hill-region boundary is given by explicit hypersurfaces, and the kernel of the singularity is the turning surface. (Jacobi turning-surface zero-energy singularity hill boundary)

**中文。** 定义：几何化指能量面的度量重构。猜想：雅可比度量的显式共形在零能量处奇点化且山区域边界由显式超曲面给出；且奇点的核为转折面。（雅可比转折面零能奇点山边界）
