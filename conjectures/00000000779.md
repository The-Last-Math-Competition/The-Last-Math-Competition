**PA-79 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: The p-adic determinant is the norm functional of the Dieudonné algebra. Conjecture: The spectrum of det D(A) is a recursive rational combination of the original eigenvalues; and the p-adic rigidity of the spectrum follows from finiteness. (Dieudonne spectrum rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：p 进行列式为 Dieudonné 代数的范数泛函。猜想：det D(A) 的谱为原始特征值的递归有理组合；且谱的 p 进刚性由有限性给出。（Dieudonne 谱刚性）
