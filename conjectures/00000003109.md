**English.** Definition: Filling functions: the equivalent invariants of Dehn functions. Conjecture: The gap theorem for Dehn functions: the Dehn function of a hyperbolic group is linear, and the gap immediately above is realized by explicit quadratic groups; the realizing classification is the minimal examples of CAT(0) cubulated groups. (Dehn gap adjacent realization)
