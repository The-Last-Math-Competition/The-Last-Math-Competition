**English.** Definition: Entropy conjugacy is a natural abstract isomorphism preserving entropy-measures; the completeness of its invariants is open. Conjecture: The complete invariants of entropy conjugacy classes are the pair of the ordered dimension group and the periodic count sequence (K_0^+, p_n); the invariants are complete: two mixing SFTs are entropy conjugate if and only if both data agree. (entropy conjugacy complete invariants)
