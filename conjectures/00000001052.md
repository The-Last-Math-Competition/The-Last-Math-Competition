# 00000001052

**FG-52 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: The Hermitian curve H_q: y^q + y = x^{q+1} is a maximal curve of genus (q−1)²/4. Conjecture: It is the unique maximal curve of genus (q−1)²/4: every q-maximal curve of genus (q−1)²/4 is isomorphic to H_q; the second-largest genus is realized by hyperelliptic subquotients of H_q.

**中文。** 定义：Hermitian 曲线 H_q:y^q+y=x^{q+1} 为亏格 (q−1)²/4 的极大曲线。猜想：它是唯一亏格达 (q−1)²/4 的极大曲线:任何亏格 (q−1)²/4 的 q-极大曲线均同构于 H_q;次大亏格的极大曲线由 H_q 的超椭圆子商实现。
