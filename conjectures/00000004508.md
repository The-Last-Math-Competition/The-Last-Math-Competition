# 00000004508

**TL2-28 ◆** — source: 135_范畴逻辑_拓扑斯.md

**English.** Definition: A geometric morphism is a functor between toposes preserving finite limits and arbitrary colimits, and hyperconnected means its inverse image is surjective. Conjecture: The decomposition classes of hyperconnected geometric morphisms are exactly the two-stage decompositions, the decomposition is unique, and the class is closed under composition. (unique two-stage decomposition of hyperconnected morphisms)

**中文。** 定义：几何态射指拓扑斯间保有限极限与任意余极限的函子,超连通指其逆像满。猜想：超连通几何态射的分解等式类恰为二段分解,分解唯一且等式类对复合封闭。（超连通二段分解唯一）
