# 00000000956

**English.** Definition: Continuous frame decomposition expresses a frame as a continuous superposition of positive measures. Conjecture: Every tight frame admits such a decomposition with unique energy splitting; and the exceptions to uniqueness are countably redundant frames. (continuous frame decomposition uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：连续帧分解指框架表为正测度的连续叠加。猜想：任意紧框架可表为正测度的连续叠加且能量分解唯一；且唯一性的例外为可数冗余框架。（连续帧分解唯一）
