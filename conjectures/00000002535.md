# 00000002535

**English.** Definition: Kakeya sets: subsets of R^n containing unit segments in every direction. Conjecture: The Hausdorff dimension of Kakeya sets is n; for a Kakeya set with dimension deficit δ, the tube-volume lower bound improves to δ^{-n/2}, attained by multi-scale interpolation of polynomial methods. (Kakeya tube volume exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Kakeya 集：R^n 中含一切方向单位线段的集合。猜想：Kakeya 集的 Hausdorff 维数为 n；n−维数亏量为 δ 的 Kakeya 集的管体积下界为 δ^{-n/2} 的改进指数且指数由多项式方法的多重插值达到。（Kakeya 管体积指数）
