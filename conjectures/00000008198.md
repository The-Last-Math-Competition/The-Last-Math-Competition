# 00000008198

**English.** Definition: The explicit computation of heights of Heegner points: the coefficient heegner_c(D) of the main term of the height formula for Heegner points. Conjecture: The explicit form of heegner_c (the analytic constant of the Gross-Zagier formula coefficient) is the normalization sqrt|D|/pi times L'(1,chi_D) (1/2 + o(1)) (a GZ normalization); the amplification of the rounding control of the computation (numerical heights of Heegner points of D) is |D|^{1/4} (a quarter amplification law); and the complexity of computationally verifying the rank-one criterion (the criterion that the Heegner system has rank 1) is |D|^{1/2+epsilon} (a half-power verification law). (Heegner GZ normalization quarter amplification)

**中文。** 定义：Heegner 点的高度的显式计算：Heegner 点的高度公式的主项的系数 heegner_c(D)。猜想：heegner_c 的显式（Gross–Zagier 公式的系数的解析常数）= √|D|/π·L'(1,χ_D)·(1/2+o(1)) 的规范化（GZ 规范）；且计算（D 的 Heegner 点的高度的数值）的舍入的控制的放大率为 |D|^{1/4}（四分之一放大律）；Heegner 点的系（点群的秩）为 1 的判据（秩一判据）的计算验证的复杂度为 |D|^{1/2+ε}（半次验证律）。
