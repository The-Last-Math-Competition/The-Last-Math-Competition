# 00000004996

**English.** Definition: A twisted connection is the connection system of a traceless Hermitian bundle. Conjecture: The existence threshold of its solutions is an explicit cone condition of the balanced class, given by the slope inequality of stable bundles, and within the cone solutions exist. (balanced cone existence threshold of twisted connections)
