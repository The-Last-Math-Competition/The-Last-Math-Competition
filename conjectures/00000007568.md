**English.** Definition: The index law with the continuous-law structure of subfactor indices. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the subfactor law and the continuous law below 4; the spectral structure is explicit, the spectrum being the complement of the Jones set; the parameter of the continuous segment is quadratic; and the isolated points of the spectrum lie at 4cos^2(pi/n) values. (subfactor continuous-law conversion)
