# 00000004321

**English.** Definition: An explicit reciprocity law is a direct computation formula for local symbols. Conjecture: The quintic reciprocity symbol formula can be minimized to exactly 3 explicit terms, and the lower bound is guaranteed by the algebraic independence degree of the symbol function. (three-term minimal quintic reciprocity formula)

**中文。** 定义：显式互反律指局部符号的直接计算公式。猜想：五次互反符号公式可极小化为恰 3 项显式和,下界由符号函数的代数独立度保证。（五次互反三项极小公式）
