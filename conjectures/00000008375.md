# 00000008375

**TRP-25 ★** — source: 264_热带几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The multiplicity of tropical points: the Hilbert-Samuel multiplicity structure of initial ideals. Conjecture: The multiplicative transfer of multiplicities is preserved along nested chains; and the transfer constant, after normalization, is identically one. (multiplicity multiplicative transfer unit law)

**中文。** 定义：热带点的重数指初始理想的 Hilbert–Samuel 重数结构。猜想：重数的乘法传递在嵌套链下恒保持；传递常数在归一化后恒等于一。（重数乘法传递单位律）
