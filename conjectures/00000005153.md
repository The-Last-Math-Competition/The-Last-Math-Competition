**English.** Definition: The codimension distribution is the codimension statistics of the loci. Conjecture: As the genus tends to infinity the codimension distribution is asymptotically Poisson, with parameter the leading coefficient of the generating series and convergence rate squared. (Poisson asymptotics of locus codimensions)

**中文。** 定义：余维数分布指位置的余维统计。猜想：亏格趋于无穷时余维分布渐近为泊松型,参数为生成级数首系数且收敛速率平方。（位置余维泊松渐近律）
