# 00000006169

**English.** Definition: The saddle-point formula is the saddle asymptotics of coefficient integrals. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit series of second-order corrections, closed in the first three terms at classical scale; the optimal truncation is at the fourth term with exponentially small remainder; and at singularity collision the corrections unify with the Airy transition, the unification window width being the cube root of the collision distance. (first-three-term closure of the saddle second-order series)

**中文。** 定义：鞍点公式：系数积分的鞍点渐近。猜想：换算常数为二阶修正的显式级数且经典尺度下前三项封闭；最优截断在第四项且余项指数小；奇异碰撞时修正与 Airy 过渡统一且统一的窗宽为碰撞距离的立方根。（鞍点二阶级数前三项封闭）
