**English.** Definition: The maximum-principle barrier is the obstacle for constructing minimal graphs by the barrier method. Conjecture: Its explicit construction is given by the wedge of two strictly convex hypersurfaces, it is effective in dimension at most seven, and the boundary data are continuously attainable. (explicit construction of wedge barriers)

**中文。** 定义：最大值原理障碍指屏障法构造极小图的障碍物。猜想：其显式构造由两严格凸超曲面的楔形给出,维数不超过七有效且边界数据连续可达。（楔形障碍显式构造）
