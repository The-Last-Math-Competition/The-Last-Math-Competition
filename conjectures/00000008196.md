# 00000008196

**CNT-26 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: The von Staudt distribution of Bernoulli denominators: the count staudt(k) of the set of Staudt primes. Conjecture: The expectation of staudt(k) (the number of prime factors of the denominator of B_k) is log log k (a double-logarithmic expectation); the Staudt hits of primes p (p - 1 dividing k) are independent (an independent hit law); the distribution of the complement of the complete characterization {p : p-1 divides k} (large prime factors of the numerator) is Poisson-Gumbel (a numerator Gumbel law); and the expectation of the largest prime factor of the numerator is a constant exponential in k (an exponential expectation law). (von Staudt independence numerator Gumbel law)

**中文。** 定义：Bernoulli 多项式的 von Staudt 分布：分母（Staudt 素数集）的计数 staudt(k)。猜想：staudt(k)（B_k 的分母的素因子数）的期望为 log log k（双重对数期望）；且素数 p 的 Staudt 命中（p-1 | k）的关联为独立（独立命中律）；k 的 Staudt 集 = {p: p-1|k} 的完全刻画（已知）的补（分子的大素因子）的分布为 Poisson–Gumbel（分子 Gumbel 律）；分子的最大素因子的期望为 k 的常数的指数（指数期望律）。
