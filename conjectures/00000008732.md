# 00000008732

**English.** Definition: Krylov complexity: the growth of expansion coefficients of operators in the Lanczos basis. Conjecture: In the thermal phase the Krylov complexity of local operators grows exponentially without bound (an unboundedness law); in the MBL phase the complexity saturates (a saturation law); the saturation value is a volume law in the operator support (an area saturation law); and the Krylov dimension distribution is universal (a distribution law). (Krylov complexity unbounded saturation universal)

**中文。** 定义：Krylov 复杂度：算子在 Lanczos 基下展开系数的增长。猜想：热相中局域算子的 Krylov 复杂度无界指数增长（无界律）；且 MBL 相中复杂度饱和（饱和律）；饱和值为算子支撑的体积律（面积饱和律）；Krylov 维数分布为普适（分布律）。
