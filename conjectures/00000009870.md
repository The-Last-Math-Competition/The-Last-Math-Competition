# 00000009870

**English.** Definition: Open-boundary ASEP: the stationary structure of boundary injection and extraction. Conjecture: The stationary current-density curve of open-boundary ASEP is an integrable exact curve completely determined by the phase diagram of boundary parameters; the current jumps by an explicit value along first-order lines of the diagram, and fluctuations at critical points are Tracy-Widom type. (open boundary current phase diagram)
