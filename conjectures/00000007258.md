**English.** Definition: The Jacobi metric with the quantization of the action. Conjecture: The conformal transformation of minimizing geodesics holds, and the factor of the transformation is the energy potential. (energy-potential Jacobi conformal factor)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 度量：作用度量化。猜想：Jacobi 的度量变换：极小化测地的共形变换且换的因子为能量的势。（Jacobi 共形能量势因子极小化测度变换）
