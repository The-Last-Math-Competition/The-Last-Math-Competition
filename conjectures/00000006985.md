**English.** Definition: Compensated compactness is the recovery of products under weak convergence. Conjecture: Rank-one recovery is given by geometric conditions; the criterion of the condition is the determinant identity; and the verification of the determinant criterion is the two-by-two system. (determinant criterion of compensated compactness)

**中文。** 定义：补偿紧性为弱收敛的积恢复。猜想：秩一恢复由几何条件给出；且条件的判据为行列式恒等；且行列式判据的验证为二乘二系统。（补偿紧性行列式判据）
