# 00000008136

**NAG-26 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The termination index of noncommutative Grobner bases (Bergman's diamond lemma for noncommutative varieties): term(A), the termination index of term orders. Conjecture: term(A) is well-ordered if and only if the Hilbert series of A is a rational function (a rationality-termination equivalence); the polynomial complexity of constructing the optimal term order (the minimal reduction order) equals the degree of the denominator of the Hilbert series (a denominator-degree complexity); and the minimal example of non-termination (infinite reduction) is a twist deformation of the Bergman exceptional ring (the minimal example with irrational Hilbert series, a twist minimal-example law). (Bergman termination rational Hilbert equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的 Bergman 的菱形引理的非交换 Gröbner 基的终止指标：term(A)（项序的终止指标）。猜想：term(A) 为良序当且仅当 A 的 Hilbert 级数为有理函数（有理-终止等价）；且最优项序（最小归约序）的构造的多项式复杂度 = Hilbert 级数的分母的次数（分母次数复杂度）；非终止（无穷归约）的极小例子为 Bergman 例外环（无理 Hilbert 的极小例）的 twist 形变（twist 极小例律）。
