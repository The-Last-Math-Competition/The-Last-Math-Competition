# 00000005226

**English.** Definition: The joint error exponent is the error decay rate of joint moments. Conjecture: The error exponent of the two-matrix model is explicit of dimension-reciprocal type, controlled by the first-order correction of crossed moments, and consistent with the single-variable case. (first-crossing-controlled exponent of two-matrix joint errors)

**中文。** 定义：联合误差指数指联合矩的误差衰减率。猜想：二元模型的误差指数为维数倒数型显式,由交叉矩首阶修正控制且与单变量一致。（二元联合误差首交叉控制指数）
