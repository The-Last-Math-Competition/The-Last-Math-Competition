# 00000007352

**IL3-22 ★** — source: 230_无穷小逻辑.md

**English.** Definition: Nonstandard numerical analysis with infinitesimal error control. Conjecture: The infinitesimal selection of discretization step sizes holds, and the error of the selection is infinitesimal. (infinitesimal-step nonstandard discretization)

**中文。** 定义：非标准数值分析：误差的无穷小控制。猜想：无穷小的步长：离散化的步长的无穷小选取且取的误差为无穷小。（无穷小步长离散化误差无穷小选取非标准数值）
