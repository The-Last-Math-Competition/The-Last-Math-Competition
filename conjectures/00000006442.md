**English.** Definition: Coefficient distribution is the value-distribution structure of expansion coefficients. Conjecture: A parametrix-type distribution gives the asymptotic distribution of coefficients, the kernel of the distribution being the Fourier transform of the potential measure; the decay of the transform is polynomial; and the drift of the center of the distribution is zero. (potential-measure coefficient distribution)

**中文。** 定义：系数分布：展开系数的值分布结构。猜想：参数型分布给出系数的渐近分布且分布的核为位势测度的 Fourier 变换；变换的衰减为多项式；分布的中心的漂移为零。（参数位势测度系数分布）
