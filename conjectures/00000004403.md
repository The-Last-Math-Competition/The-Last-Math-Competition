# 00000004403

**GL2-13 ◆** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: The Frieze-Kannan error is the cut-norm error of the weak regular matrix approximation. Conjecture: Its optimal constant is 1/2, attained by an explicit construction of uniform blocks, and any smaller constant fails for general graphs. (FK optimal error constant 1/2)

**中文。** 定义：Frieze–Kannan 误差指弱正则矩阵逼近的切割范数误差。猜想：其最优常数为 1/2,由均匀分块的显式构造达到,任何更小常数对一般图失败。（FK 最优误差常数 1/2）
