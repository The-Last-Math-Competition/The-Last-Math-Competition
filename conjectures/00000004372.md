# 00000004372

**English.** Definition: The destruction of Bokstedt periodicity is the vanishing of the coefficient periodic element on general rings. Conjecture: The first example of destroyed periodicity for a nonconnected spectrum is the sum of the unit-split spectra S^0 and S^0, whose THH has no periodicity, the loss originates from the splitting, and every connected p-complete ring retains periodicity. (splitting as first example of destroyed periodicity)

**中文。** 定义：Bökstedt 周期性的破坏指系数周期元在一般环上消失。猜想：非连通谱的周期破坏首例为单位分裂谱 S⁰ 与 S⁰ 的和,其 THH 无周期且缺失源于分裂,一切连通 p 完备环保留周期。（周期破坏分裂首例）
