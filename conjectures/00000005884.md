# 00000005884

**English.** Definition: Aliasing is the error of mistaking high for low frequencies. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit tail sum of truncated spectral coefficients, corrected at Gibbs layers by a logarithmic factor, and closed. (spectral tail-sum Gibbs logarithmic correction of aliasing)

**中文。** 定义：混叠指高频误认为低频的误差。猜想：换算公式为截断谱系数的显式尾和,吉布斯层处由对数因子修正且封闭。（混叠谱尾和吉布斯对数修正式）
