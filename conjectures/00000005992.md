# 00000005992

**English.** Definition: The rigidity formula is the quantified structure of the infinitesimal-deformation criterion. Conjecture: The three-dimensional conversion constant is the explicit combination of tangent-space dimensions, generally rigid at higher order for smooth hypersurfaces; the rigidity threshold is degree 4, with H^1 vanishing above; and the tangent-space dimension upper bound is of degree-cubic type, attained by quartic hypersurfaces. (tangent-space rigidity conversion in three dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：刚性公式：无穷小形变判据（刚性）的量化结构。猜想：三维换算常数为切空间维数的显式组合且光滑超曲面时高次普遍刚性；刚性阈值为次数 4 且阈值上 H¹ 消没；切空间维数的上界为次数立方型且界由四次曲面达到。（三维切空间组合刚性换算）
