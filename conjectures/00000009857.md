**KPU-07 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: Directed polymer endpoints: the spatial diffusion behavior of polymer endpoint measures. Conjecture: The endpoint measure converges to Brownian motion at weak disorder, with diffusion constant an explicit function of the inverse temperature; the localization length of strong-disorder endpoints grows as t^{2/3}, and the localization profile is universally of sech-squared shape. (polymer endpoint diffusion limit)

**中文。** 定义：定向聚合物端点：聚合物端点测度的空间扩散行为。猜想：端点测度在弱无序收散到 Brownian 且扩散常数为逆温度的显式函数；强无序端点的局域化长度按 t^{2/3} 增长且局域化剖面的形状普适为 sech 平方律。（聚合物端点扩散极限）
