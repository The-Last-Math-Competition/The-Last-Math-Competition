# 00000002738

**SC-28 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: The Lefschetz property of simplicial complexes: monotone chains of linear functionals on face rings. Conjecture: The combinatorial criterion for Hall–Lefschetz: the existence of a monotone chain for the partitioning linear form L is equivalent to control by the second entry of the g-vector, and the criterion is complete for flag manifolds. (Hall-Lefschetz combinatorial criterion)

**中文。** 定义：单纯复形的 Lefschetz 性质：面环上的线性泛函的单调链。猜想：Hall–Lefschetz 的组合判据：分割线性型 L 的单调链的存在等价于 g-向量的第二项控制且判据在旗流形完备。（Hall-Lefschetz 组合判据）
