**PH2-16 ◆** — source: 182_持久同调II.md

**English.** Definition: A zigzag filtration alternates forward and backward arrows. Conjecture: The extension of interval types is indexed by the forward-backward alternation count, the kernel of the index being the block structure; the parity of the alternation count determines the endpoint types of intervals, the determination being a bijection; and the conserved quantity of the extension is the total interval count, equal to the difference before and after zigzagging. (block-structure interval indexing of zigzag alternations)

**中文。** 定义：双向过滤：正反箭头交替的过滤（zigzag）。猜想：区间类型的扩张由正反向交替数索引且索引的核为块结构；交替数的奇偶决定区间的端点类型且决定为一一对应；扩张的守恒量为区间总数且总数等于交错化前后之差。（双向交替块结构区间索引）
