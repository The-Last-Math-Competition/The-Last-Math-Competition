# 00000004253

**English.** Definition: The DT invariants are the Behrend-function weighted virtual counts of moduli spaces. Conjecture: The Behrend local weights are given by an explicit sign function of the Milnor numbers of the singularities, and for A_1 singularities they are exactly the dimension sign. (Behrend local weight Milnor formula)

**中文。** 定义：DT 不变量指模空间的 Behrend 函数加权虚计数。猜想：Behrend 局部权重由奇点 Milnor 数的显式符号函数给出,奇点型 A₁ 时恰为维数符号。（Behrend 局部权重 Milnor 公式）
