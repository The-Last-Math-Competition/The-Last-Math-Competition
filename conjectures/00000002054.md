# 00000002054

**English.** Definition: The congruence commutator [θ,θ] is the commutator operation of modular varieties. Conjecture: The decision of the condition [θ,θ] = θ∧[1,1] is controlled by a finite classification table of polynomial identities; the table has at most 3 row types (abelian/rectangular/kernel).
