# 00000003154

**LG2-24 ★** — source: 90_李群微分方程.md

**English.** Definition: Representation rings: the lattice structure of characters. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of representation rings: the rank of the ring equals the number of irreducible representations, and the spectrum of the ring is the Fourier transform of the weight lattice. (representation ring weight lattice Fourier spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：表示环（representation ring）：特征标的格结构。猜想：表示环的维数谱：环的秩等于不可约表示数且环的谱为权格的 Fourier 变换。（表示环权格 Fourier 谱）
