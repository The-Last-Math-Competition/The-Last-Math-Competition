# 00000002885

**IX-25 ★** — source: 81_微分算子的指标II.md

**English.** Definition: The index homomorphism of operator algebras: the characteristic values of Fredholm modules. Conjecture: The index pairing is always invariant under homotopies of the unit; and the proof goes through the closedness of cup products in cyclic cohomology. (index pairing homotopy invariance)

**中文。** 定义：算子代数的指标同态指 Fredholm 模的示性数值。猜想：数值指标的配对恒在单位的同伦下不变；且证明经由循环上同调的杯积封闭性。（指标配对同伦不变）
