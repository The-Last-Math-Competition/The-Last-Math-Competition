# 00000000254

**AA-01 △◆** — source: 16_代数与算术对象.md

**English.** Definition: The Mordell-Weil rank of E(Q) for an elliptic curve E over Q. Conjecture: There exists an elliptic curve of rank at least 29 (the current published record is a curve of rank 28; unboundedness of ranks is a famous open direction).

**中文。** 定义：椭圆曲线 E/ℚ 的 Mordell–Weil 秩 rank E(ℚ)。猜想：存在秩 ≥ 29 的椭圆曲线(当前已发表纪录为秩 28 的曲线;秩无界是著名开放方向)。
