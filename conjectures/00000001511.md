**KK-01 ★** — source: 36_代数K理论与非交换几何.md

**English.** Definition: K_n(Z) is the Quillen K-group of the integers. Conjecture: The group type of K₉(Z) is Z/2 ⊕ Z/2 ⊕ Z/2 (a concrete instance of Lichtenbaum's conjecture at n = 9); the structure is obtained by computing étale K-theory at 2 and at odd primes separately and gluing.

**中文。** 定义：K_n(Z) 为整数的 Quillen K-群。猜想：K₉(Z) 的群型为 Z/2⊕Z/2⊕Z/2(Lichtenbaum 预言在 n=9 的具体化);其结构由 étale K-理论在 2 与奇素分别计算后粘合得出。
