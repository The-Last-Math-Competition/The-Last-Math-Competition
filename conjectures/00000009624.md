**SMA-14 ★** — source: 306_Smale空间与符号动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The Curtis-Hedlund-Lyndon theorem gives automorphisms as sliding block codes of radius r; the joint constraint of radius and entropy is unknown. Conjecture: The radius r and entropy h of surjective automorphisms satisfy the linear upper bound h <= r log 2; automorphisms asymptotically attaining equality are elementary rule families, and the entropy spectrum of extremal systems is discrete. (radius-entropy linear law)

**中文。** 定义：Curtis–Hedlund–Lyndon 定理给出自同构为半径 r 的滑动块代码；半径与熵的联合约束未知。猜想：满射自同构的半径 r 与熵 h 满足 h ≤ r·log 2 的线性上界；等号渐近达到的极值自同构为初等规则族，极值系统的熵谱为离散集。（半径-熵线性律）
