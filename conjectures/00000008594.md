# 00000008594

**English.** Definition: Robust estimation is estimation under epsilon-contaminated neighborhoods. Conjecture: The minimax loss for location parameters under epsilon contamination is of constant type epsilon^2; and the exact constant is given by the Huber least favorable distribution. (contamination loss Huber constant)

**中文。** 定义：稳健估计指 ε-污染邻域下的估计。猜想：位置参数在 ε-污染下 minimax 亏损为 ε² 常数型；精确常数由 Huber 最不利分布给出。（污染亏损 Huber 常数）
