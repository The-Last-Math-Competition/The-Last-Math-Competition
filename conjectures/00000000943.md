**OA-43 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Conjecture: The Grothendieck constant sequence K_G(ℓ∞ⁿ) satisfies the adjacent difference bound K_G(ℓ∞ⁿ) − K_G(ℓ∞^{n−1}) ≥ c/n² for an explicit positive constant c (a convergence-speed lower bound).

**中文。** 猜想：Grothendieck 常数序列 K_G(ℓ∞ⁿ) 满足相邻差 K_G(ℓ∞ⁿ) − K_G(ℓ∞^{n−1}) ≥ c/n²,c 为显式正常数(收敛速度下界)。
