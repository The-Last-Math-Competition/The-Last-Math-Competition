# 00000008348

**English.** Definition: The hyperbolic Bogomolov exception: the distribution bog_exc of points of minimal height (tau-exceptional points). Conjecture: The complete list of bog_exc (Bogomolov exceptions, missing lower bounds for small-height points) is torsion and special subgroups (a special list law); the completeness of the list (no other exceptions) holds for semiabelian varieties (a semiabelian completeness law); the quantified Bogomolov (explicit lower bound c(epsilon) for heights) has quadratic constant epsilon^2 (a quadratic constant law); and the precise constant of the small-quadratic correction (the minimal-epsilon domain) is the two-power 2^{-n} (a two-power constant law). (Bogomolov torsion list semiabelian quadratic)

**中文。** 定义：双曲的 Bogomolov 例外：高度极小（τ-例外）的点的分布 bog_exc。猜想：bog_exc（Bogomolov 例外（小高度的点的下界缺失）的代数子群的完全列表）的列表为 torsion 与特殊子群（特殊列表律）；且列表的完备性（无其他例外）在半阿贝尔簇成立（半阿贝尔完备律）；量化的 Bogomolov（高度的下界的显式 c(ε)）的常数为 ε² 的二次（二次常数律）；小的二次的修正（极小 ε 的域）的精确常数为 2^{-n} 的二幂（二幂常数律）。
