# 00000000224

**English.** Definition: A Fortunate number is the least prime of the form primorial plus m. Conjecture: All Fortunate numbers are prime; and the primality is settled through the least-primitive-root window. (Fortunate least primitive root window primality)

**中文。** 定义：Fortunate 数为素数乘积加 m 的极小素。猜想：一切 Fortunate 数都是素数；且素数的组合为最小原根窗。（Fortunate 最小原根窗素数）
