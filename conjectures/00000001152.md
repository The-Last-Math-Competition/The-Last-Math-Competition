# 00000001152

**LT-52 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The moduli space of Lie-algebra structure constants on a torus is the quotient space of bracket constants. Conjecture: The moduli space of three-dimensional real Lie algebras is an explicit nine-dimensional variety with an explicit number of singular points; and the classification of the singularities is the intersection of the solvable and simple direct-sum strata. (three-dimensional Lie algebra moduli)

**中文。** 定义：环面上李代数结构常数模空间为括号常数的商空间。猜想：三维实李代数的模空间为九维显式簇且奇异点数显式；且奇点的分类为可解与单直和的交。（三维李代数模空间）
