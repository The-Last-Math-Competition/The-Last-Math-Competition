# 00000002010

**English.** Definition: The Calabi flow is a fourth-order conformal geometric flow. Conjecture: The singular set of its Cheeger–Gromov subsequential limits has dimension at most n−2; singularities are codimension ≥ 2 regularity obstructions with no isolated point singularities.

**中文。** 定义：Calabi 流指四阶共形几何流。猜想：其 Cheeger–Gromov 子列极限的奇点集维数 ≤n−2;奇点为余维 ≥2 的正则性障碍,无孤立点奇点。
