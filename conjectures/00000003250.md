**English.** Definition: Spectral anomalies of phonon glasses: disorder-induced vibrations. Conjecture: The anomalous density of glassy states: the low-frequency density of states of disorder grows as a power of frequency, with the universality class the phonon-diffuse-scattering dichotomy. (glass density of states phonon diffuse scattering dichotomy)
