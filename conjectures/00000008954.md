**ISO-04 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: The Hirota bilinear equation: the tau function equation. Conjecture: The algebraic entropy of its residue structure is zero if and only if the system is integrable, and the entropy spectrum consists of Pisot numbers. (Hirota entropy Pisot law)

**中文。** 定义：Hirota 双线性方程指 τ 函数方程。猜想：其余项结构的代数熵为零当且仅当系统可积且熵谱为 Pisot 数。（Hirota 熵 Pisot 律）
