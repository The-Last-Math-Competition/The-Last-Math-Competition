**English.** Definition: Metric projections of convex bodies: the structure of nearest points. Conjecture: The continuity of metric projections: Chebyshev sets have continuous projections, with the rigidity of continuity the image of strict convexity. (Chebyshev strict convexity projection continuity rigidity)
