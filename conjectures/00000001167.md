# 00000001167

**LT-67 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The boundary-value function of homomorphisms of a rank ≥ 2 lattice Γ into a p-adic group is the measure value on the Furstenberg boundary. Conjecture: For all sufficiently regular representations, the boundary-value function is algebraic (valued in algebraic measures on the boundary), and this property is equivalent to p-adic Margulis superrigidity; both properties fail in rank 1.

**中文。** 定义：秩 ≥ 2 格 Γ 到 p-adic 群的同调表示的边界值函数指其在 Furstenberg 边界上的测度值。猜想：一切足够高正则的表示的边界值函数为代数的(取值于边界的代数测度),且该性质等价于 p-adic 端的 Margulis 超刚性;秩 1 时两性质均不成立。
