# 00000004307

**English.** Definition: An Euler system is a compatible family of elements of cyclotomic-unit type. Conjecture: The minimal rank of an Euler system for the higher K-group is 2k-1, and when k is coprime to p-1 the rank is exactly 1, the formula being complete. (minimal rank formula of Euler systems)

**中文。** 定义：欧拉系指分圆单位型的相容元素族。猜想：高阶 K 群的欧拉系最小秩为 2k−1,当 k 与 p−1 互素时秩恰为 1,公式完全。（欧拉系最小秩公式）
