# 00000008238

**English.** Definition: The balanced-weight condition for nonemptiness of cores: the minimal violation scarf_min of the Scarf condition on balanced weights for nonempty cores of cooperative games. Conjecture: The cardinality of scarf_min (a minimal unbalanced family) is at most n+1 (a Farkas bound); the structure of minimal violations is the barycentric subdivision of a simplex (a barycentric structure law); the convergence rate of the duality of cores and equilibria of markets (the Edgeworth limit core equals equilibria) is 1/sqrt m (a square-root convergence law, m the number of copies); and the sign of the convergence error (the core-equilibrium gap of replicated economies) is always containment of equilibria in the core (a containment direction law). (Scarf barycentric Farkas square-root core)

**中文。** 定义：核心的非空性的平衡权条件：合作博弈的核心的非空的平衡权的 Scarf 条件的极小违例 scarf_min。猜想：scarf_min（最小不可平衡族）的基数 ≤ n+1（Farkas 界）；且极小违例的结构为单纯形的重心细分（重心结构律）；市场的核心与均衡的对偶（core=equilibria 的 Edgeworth 极限）的收敛速率为 1/√m（平方根收敛律，m 为复制数）；收敛的误差（复制经济的核-均衡间隙）的符号恒为核包含均衡（包含方向律）。
