# 00000008241

**EGT-11 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The fragility of explicit VCG payments in mechanism design: the revenue share vcg_share of coalition manipulation (coalitional misreporting) of VCG. Conjecture: vcg_share (the coalition VCG gain) is subadditive, at most |C| times the single-agent gain (a subadditivity law); the minimal example of VCG revenue nonmonotonicity is the two-good auction with 3 agents and 2 items (a two-item nonmonotone example); and the determination in the Green-Laffont impossibility (uniqueness of all VCG-type manipulation-immune mechanisms) is the one-parameter subclass of VCG (a one-parameter uniqueness law). (VCG coalition subadditivity Green-Laffont uniqueness)
