# 00000001711

**English.** Definition: The minimal spectral-gap representation of SL₃(R) is the representation corresponding to the smallest nonzero Casimir constant. Conjecture: Its matrix coefficients decay like e^{-t/(2 log 3)}; the exponent is given by the explicit Casselman–Wallach value, and the spectral-gap representation is the minimal nontrivial representation itself.

**中文。** 定义：SL₃(R) 的最小谱隙表示指其非平凡表示的最小 Casimir 常数对应的表示。猜想：该表示的矩阵系数衰减为 e^{-t/(2\log 3)};指数由 Casselman–Wallach 的显式值给出,谱隙表示为最小非平凡表示本身。
