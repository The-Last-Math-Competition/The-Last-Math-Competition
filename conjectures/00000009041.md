# 00000009041

**English.** Definition: The Willmore functional: the integral of squared mean curvature. Conjecture: The Willmore conjecture for compact surfaces in n dimensions attains its minimum on the Clifford torus, with value 2 pi squared in three-space. (Willmore minimum Clifford)

**中文。** 定义：Willmore 泛函指平均曲率平方积分。猜想：n 维紧曲面的 Willmore 猜想在 Clifford 环面上取得极小且在三维空间其值为 2π²。（Willmore 极小 Clifford）
