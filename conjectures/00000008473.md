# 00000008473

**ERO-03 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: Maximization of Birkhoff averages: the gap between the double supremum beta(f) = limsup (1/n) sum f(T^i x) over invariant measures and initial values and the approximation by periodic orbits. Conjecture: For mixing systems, the difference between beta(f) and the optimal periodic-orbit mean is O(1/n) with constant explicitly depending on the variation of f; on expanding maps the periodic approximation rate is exactly the harmonic order 1/n; and for nonexpanding systems the slowdown factor equals the reciprocal of the topological entropy with saturation lower bound 1/2, attained by the spectral radius of linearized periodic points. (periodic approximation of Birkhoff suprema)

**中文。** 定义：Birkhoff 平均的极大化：连续函数 f 沿不变测度与初值的双重上确界 β(f)=limsup (1/n)Σf(T^i x) 与周期轨道逼近的差距。猜想：混合系统的 β(f) 与最优周期轨道均值之差为 O(1/n) 且常数显式依赖 f 的变差；扩张映射上周期逼近速率恰为调和阶 1/n；非扩张系统的减速因子等于拓扑熵的倒数且饱和下界为 1/2，界由线性化周期点的谱半径达到。
