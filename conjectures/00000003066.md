**IP-26 ★** — source: 87_非线性PDE的可积检验.md

**English.** Definition: Self-similar solution tests: the structure of scale-invariant solutions. Conjecture: Self-similar profiles: the oscillation of the profile equation of a self-similar solution vanishes if and only if the equation is integrable, and the count of oscillations is the rotation number of the Poincare map. (self-similar oscillation rotation number criterion)

**中文。** 定义：自相似解的检验：标度不变解的结构。猜想：自相似剖面：自相似解的剖面方程的洛界振动为零当且仅当方程可积且振动的计数为 Poincare 映射的旋转数。（自相似振动旋转数判据）
