**English.** Definition: The asymptotics of n-widths: the width spectrum of compact sets. Conjecture: The regular variation of the width spectrum: the power decay of widths is regular variation, with the exponent of variation the box-dimension deficit of the set. (width regular variation box dimension exponent)
