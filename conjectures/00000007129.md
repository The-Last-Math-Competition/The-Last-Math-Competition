# 00000007129

**English.** Definition: The combinatorics of linear programming with the vertex structure of polytopes. Conjecture: The worst-case step count of the simplex method is the diameter, and the upper bound of the diameter is monomial. (monomial diameter bound for simplex worst cases)

**中文。** 定义：线性规划的组合：多面体的顶点结构。猜想：LP 的直径界：单纯形法的最坏步数为直径且径的上界为单项式。（单纯形法直径单项式最坏步数上界）
