# 00000008715

**English.** Definition: Bipartite entanglement entropy of energy eigenstates: the scaling of the von Neumann entropy of eigenstates with subsystem size after bisecting the chain. Conjecture: Typical eigenstate entropy in the thermal phase satisfies the volume law S = s_th |A| + O(1) with fluctuations of order O(sqrt|A|); eigenstate entropy in the MBL phase does not exceed the area-law constant C times log 2, where C equals the resonance localization length; and the scaling exponent of entropy in the transition region lies between 0 and 1/2, synchronizing with the transition of the level spacing ratio from Wigner-Dyson to Poisson. (eigenstate entanglement volume-area synchronization)

**中文。** 定义：能量本征态的二分纠缠熵：把链切成两半后本征态 von Neumann 熵随子链尺寸的标度。猜想：热相典型本征态熵满足 S = s_th·|A|+O(1) 的体积律且涨落阶为 O(√|A|)；MBL 相本征态熵不超过面积律常数 C 与 log 2 的乘积且 C 等于共振局域长度；过渡区熵的标度指数落在 0 与 1/2 之间且与能级间距比从 Wigner–Dyson 到泊松的过渡同步变化。
