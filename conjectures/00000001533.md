**KK-23 ★** — source: 36_代数K理论与非交换几何.md

**English.** Definition: A localization invariant of noncommutative motives is a functor compatible with localization sequences. Conjecture: The spectrum of invariants is completely classified by the K₀ of the monoid of natural transformations; and the classification has no redundant equivalence classes. (complete K₀ classification of invariants)
