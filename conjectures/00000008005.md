# 00000008005

**English.** Definition: Homogenization of Bernoulli-type free boundary problems: the explicitness of the capillary constant of the effective free boundary for the Bernoulli problem in periodic-coefficient composite media. Conjecture: The effective capillary constant is an explicit formula of minimum (non-averaged) type over the microscopic constants: cos theta_eff = inf over directions of the directional saturation value of cos theta; the regularity of the free boundary in the homogenized limit is completely classified by the sign of the effective constant (regular when cos theta_eff is nonzero); and the homogenization convergence rate is epsilon^{1/2} in the H^1 topology and optimal (two-scale examples). (Bernoulli homogenization infimum capillarity)

**中文。** 定义：自由边界问题的 Bernoulli 型的均质化（homogenization）：周期系数复合介质的 Bernoulli 问题的有效自由边界的毛细常数的显式性。猜想：有效毛细常数为微观常数的最小（非平均）型的显式公式：cos θ_eff = inf_方向 (cos θ 的方向饱和值)；且均质化极限的自由边界的正则性由有效常数的符号完全分类（正则当 cos θ_eff≠0）；均质化的收敛速率为 ε^{1/2}（H^1 拓扑）且速率为最优（双尺度例子）。
