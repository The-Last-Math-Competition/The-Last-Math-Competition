# 00000009868

**English.** Definition: RSK and GUE minors: the exact correspondence of Poisson LPP and random matrix minor processes. Conjecture: Under the RSK correspondence, the multidimensional process of Poisson LPP and Brownian last passage is equivalent to the GUE minor process; the finite-size error of the equivalence is an explicit Poissonization correction that does not destroy the joint distribution structure. (RSK-GUE error control)

**中文。** 定义：RSK 与 GUE 子式：泊松 LPP 与随机矩阵子式过程的精确对应。猜想：RSK 对应下的泊松 LPP 与布朗最末通过的多维过程等价于 GUE 子式过程；等价的有限尺寸误差为泊松化的显式修正且修正不破坏联合分布的结构。（RSK-GUE 误差控制）
