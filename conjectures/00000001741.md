# 00000001741

**DG-21 ◆** — source: 43_丢番图几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Hall's conjecture states |x³−y²| ≫ √x for all positive integers x, y. Conjecture: The reverse direction of Hall's conjecture: there exist infinitely many pairs with 0 < |x³−y²| < √x·(log x)^{1/4}; the logarithmic correction is optimal (the exponent 1/4 cannot be increased).

**中文。** 定义：Hall 猜想指 |x³−y²| ≫√x(对一切正整数 x,y)。猜想：Hall 猜想的反方向:存在无穷多对使 0<|x³−y²|<√x·(log x)^{1/4};该对数修正是最优的(指数 1/4 不可再增大)。
