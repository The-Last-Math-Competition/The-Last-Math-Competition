# 00000000481

**English.** Definition: A Young bouquet is the data structure decomposing a word (permutation) into chains of standard tableaux (rather than pairs); its rank function ρ(B) is a norm-type function of the chain-length vector of the irreducible components of B. Conjecture: ρ is submodular on the merge poset of bouquets, i.e. ρ(B₁)+ρ(B₂) ≥ ρ(B₁∨B₂)+ρ(B₁∧B₂), with equality characterizing the decomposability of free Lie algebra quotients.

**中文。** 定义：Young 花束指把字(排列)分解为标准表链(而非对)的数据结构,其秩函数 ρ(B) 为花束 B 的不可约分量链长向量的范数型函数。猜想：ρ 在花束的合并偏序上是子模的,即 ρ(B₁)+ρ(B₂) ≥ ρ(B₁∨B₂)+ρ(B₁∧B₂),等号刻画免费李代数商的可分解性。
