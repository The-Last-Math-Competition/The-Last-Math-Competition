# 00000000846

**English.** Definition: The optimal transport cost W_p(μ, ν) between fractal sets K, L is defined when μ, ν are the natural Hausdorff measures on K, L. Conjecture: The cost functional scales as W_p ~ C·dist(K,L)^{1−(s/p)}, where s is the larger Hausdorff dimension of K and L; this scaling is verified explicitly on self-similar sets via the equivalence class of heat kernel estimates.

**中文。** 定义：分形集 K,L 之间的最优输运代价 W_p(μ,ν) 在 μ,ν 为 K,L 上自然 Hausdorff 测度时定义。猜想：代价泛函的标度为 W_p ~ C·dist(K,L)^{1−(s/p)},s 为 K,L 的较大 Hausdorff 维数;该标度在自相似集上由热核估计的等价类显式验证。
