**English.** Definition: ∞-stacks: sheaves valued in ∞-categories. Conjecture: Descent rigidity of stacks: the localization of presheaves satisfying descent is hypercomplete, and levelwise verification of descent is a graded homotopy limit. (stack descent levelwise verification)

**中文。** 定义：∞-叠（∞-stack）：取值于 ∞-范畴的层。猜想：叠的下降刚性：满足下降的预叠的局部化为超完备且下降的逐级验证为分级同伦极限。（叠下降逐级验证）
