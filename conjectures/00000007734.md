# 00000007734

**FRV-14 ★** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The free cumulants kappa_m(c^k) of the k-th power of a circular element c count noncrossing pairings with k-coloring constraints. Conjecture: In kappa_m(c^k) = sum N(m,j,k) C_j with Catalan numbers C_j, the coefficients N(m,j,k) are explicit polynomials in k; and for fixed k as m grows, the generating function of kappa_m(c^k) is the composition of a square root of a quadratic polynomial. (polynomial structure of colored pairing counts)

**中文。** 定义：圆元 c=(s_1+i s_2)/√2（s_1,s_2 自由半圆）的 k 次幂 c^k 的 *-矩由平面图计数给出；其自由累积量 κ_m(c^k) 计数带 k-染色约束的非交叉配对。猜想：κ_m(c^k) = Σ_{j} N(m,j,k) C_j（C_j 为 Catalan 数）中系数 N(m,j,k) 为 k 的显式多项式，且当 k 固定、m→∞ 时 κ_m(c^k) 的生成函数为二次多项式的平方根的复合。
