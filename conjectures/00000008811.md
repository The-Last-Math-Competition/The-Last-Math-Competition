**CVX-11 ★** — source: 279_凸优化的迭代复杂度.md

**English.** Definition: The geometry of condition numbers: comparison of spectral and distance conditions. Conjecture: The distance condition number is given by the worst feasible point distance; and Renegar's perturbation bound is linear (a linear law). (Renegar distance condition perturbation law)

**中文。** 定义：条件数的几何为谱条件与距离条件的比较。猜想：距离条件数由最坏可行点距离给出；且 Renegar 的扰动界为线性律。（Renegar 距离条件数扰动律）
