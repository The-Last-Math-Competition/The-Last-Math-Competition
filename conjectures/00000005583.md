# 00000005583

**English.** Definition: Components are subnormal quasisimple subgroups, with the conversion of layered central products. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit order of the layered central product, given at two layers by the coprime vanishing of the centers; the constant is 1 for coprime centers; otherwise it is a prime power, with exponent the logarithm of the order of the center intersection. (coprime-order conversion of component central products)

**中文。** 定义：分量：次正规的拟单子群；层中心积的换算。猜想：换算常数为层中心积的阶显式且两层情形由中心的互素消没给出；互素时常数为 1；非互素时常数为素数幂且幂指数为中心交的阶的对数。（分量中心积互素阶换算）
