# 00000007918

**English.** Definition: A variant of the Colmez inverse correspondence in p-adic Langlands: the half-integral weight pointer w(pi) of the module M(pi) of locally analytic vectors of representations of GL_2(Q_p). Conjecture: The weight spectrum of M(pi) is completely determined by the multiplicities of the Hodge-Sen weights of the (phi,Gamma)-module D(pi): w(pi) = max(mult(w_i)); and the number of irreducible subquotients of M(pi) is at most dim D(pi)^Gamma, with equality if and only if the local component of pi is supersingular; in the equality case the subquotient types correspond one-to-one to chains of lattices of crystabelian subrepresentations. (Colmez module weight multiplicity law)

**中文。** 定义：p-adic Langlands 的 Colmez 逆对应的变体：GL₂(Q_p) 的单位表示的 locally analytic 向量构成的模 M(π) 的半整权指针 w(π)。猜想：M(π) 的权谱完全由 (φ,Γ)-模 D(π) 的 Hodge–Sen 权的重数决定：w(π)=max(mult(w_i))；且 M(π) 的不可约子商数 ≤ dim D(π)^{Γ} 的维数，等号当且仅当 π 的局部分量为 supersingular；等号情形的子商类型为一一对应于 crystabelian 子表示的格点链。
