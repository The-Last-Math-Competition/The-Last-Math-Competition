**English.** Definition: locally analytic to module M(π) of half-integral weight of quantity constitutes of unit representation of body: GL₂(Q_p) of Colmez inverse corresponds to of p-adic Langlands denotes w(π). Conjecture: weight spectrum completely of M(π) by multiplicity: w(π)=max(mult(w_i)); of Hodge–Sen weight of (φ,Γ)- module D(π) and dimension, of irreducible quotient number ≤ dim D(π)^{Γ} of M(π) if and only if local part quantity of π is quotient of supersingular; is lattice point of one one corresponds to in crystabelian representation.

**中文。** 定义：p-adic Langlands 的 Colmez 逆对应的变体：GL₂(Q_p) 的单位表示的 locally analytic 向量构成的模 M(π) 的半整权指针 w(π)。猜想：M(π) 的权谱完全由 (φ,Γ)-模 D(π) 的 Hodge–Sen 权的重数决定：w(π)=max(mult(w_i))；且 M(π) 的不可约子商数 ≤ dim D(π)^{Γ} 的维数，等号当且仅当 π 的局部分量为 supersingular；等号情形的子商类型为一一对应于 crystabelian 子表示的格点链。
