**English.** Definition: Integer multiplication is the multiplication of multi-digit integers. Conjecture: In the circuit model, the nonlinear lower bound of multiplication is the log-linear term plus an explicit growth remainder, the combinatorial formulation of the lower bound is given by the rank of the transfer matrix, and it improves the existing bounds. (improved transfer-matrix rank lower bound for multiplication)

**中文。** 定义：大数乘法指多位整数的乘法运算。猜想：电路模型下乘法的非线性下界为对数线性项加显式增长余项,下界的组合表述由传输子矩阵的秩给出且改进现有界。（乘法传输子秩下界改进）
