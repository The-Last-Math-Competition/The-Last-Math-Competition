# 00000004295

**English.** Definition: A directly indecomposable group is a group with no nontrivial direct-sum decomposition. Conjecture: The rank spectrum of torsion-free directly indecomposable groups is all positive integers, the minimal realization field for each rank is a pure quadratic extension of the rationals, and the degree lower bound 2 is optimal. (rank spectrum realization fields of direct indecomposables)

**中文。** 定义：直不可分解群指无非平凡直和分解的群。猜想：无挠直不可分解群的秩谱为全部正整数,每秩的最小实现域为有理数的纯二次扩张,次数下界 2 最优。（直不可分解秩谱实现域）
