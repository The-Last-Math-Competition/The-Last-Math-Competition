# 00000001593

**English.** Definition: A Y-system is the family of functional equations attached to an affine Lie algebra, whose constant identities are dilogarithm identities. Conjecture: The constant terms of the twisted-type D_{n+1}^{(2)} Y-system have an explicit dilogarithm evaluation: the constant is a combination of eigenvalues of (1/2)·n·(n+1)·L(χ,2); apart from the types with known algebraic proofs, this is the last unevaluated family.

**中文。** 定义：Y-系统指仿射 Lie 代数伴随的函数方程族,其常数恒等式为 dilog 恒等式。猜想：扭类型 D_{n+1}^{(2)} 的 Y-系统常数项有显式 dilog 求值:常数为 (1/2)·n·(n+1)·L(χ,2) 的特征值组合;除已知代数证明的类型外,这是最后未求值的族。
