**English.** Definition: The Lie algebra of a differential algebraic group. Conjecture: The classification of linear differential algebraic groups reduces to the classification of differential Lie algebras; and the solvability decision for differential Lie algebras is effective in finite dimension. (differential Lie algebra classification)

**中文。** 定义：微分代数群的 Lie 代数。猜想：线性微分代数群的分类可约化为微分 Lie 代数的分类；且微分 Lie 代数可解性判定在有限维时有效。（微分 Lie 代数分类）
