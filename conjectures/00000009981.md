# 00000009981

**NCR-11 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Splitting of A-infinity structures: formality criteria of derived categories. Conjecture: The complete set of Massey obstructions to formality of derived categories is finite, with finiteness decided by the dimensions of the first n obstruction groups; n is the least common multiple of the directed cycle lengths of the quiver. (formality Massey finite obstructions)

**中文。** 定义：A_∞ 结构的分裂：导范畴的形式性判据。猜想：导范畴形式性的 Massey 障碍的完全集有限且有限性由前 n 阶障碍群的维数判定；n 为箭图的有向圈长的最小公倍数。（形式性 Massey 有限障碍）
