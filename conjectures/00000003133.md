# 00000003133

**English.** Definition: Lie systems: systems whose vector fields span a finite-dimensional Lie algebra. Conjecture: The factorization of Lie systems: solutions are right-translation products of one-parameter subgroups with a fundamental solution, and the number of factors equals the Lie algebra dimension. (Lie system right-translation factorization)

**中文。** 定义：李系统：向量场张成有限维李代数的系统。猜想：李系统的因子分解：系统解为单参数子群与基本解的右平移积且积的因子个数等于李代数维数。（李系统右平移因子分解）
