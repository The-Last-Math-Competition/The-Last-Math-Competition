# 00000002049

**English.** Definition: The integer complexity ‖n‖ is the shortest expression of n using additions and multiplications of 1's. Conjecture: In the optimal expression of any n, the number of multiplications deviates from the ideal value by O(loglog n); the multiplication defect is controlled by the depth of the factor structure of n.

**中文。** 定义：整数复杂度 ‖n‖ 指以 1 的加法和乘法表示 n 的最短表达。猜想：任意 n 的最优表达中乘法次数与理想值的差为 O(loglog n);乘法缺陷由 n 的因子结构的深度控制。
