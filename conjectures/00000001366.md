# 00000001366

**DS-06 △** — source: 31_描述集合论与递归论.md

**English.** Definition: A sofic group is a group embeddable as an ultraproduct limit of permutation groups. Conjecture: A purely combinatorial soficity criterion exists — G is non-sofic if and only if some tower of finite quotients of G realizes a violation of an anomalous-signature inequality; the criterion is logically independent of the Elek–Szabó ultraproduct method and verifiable object-by-object on finite data.

**中文。** 定义：sofic 群指可嵌入置换群的超积极限的群。猜想：存在纯组合的 sofic 性判据——G 非 sofic 当且仅当 G 的某有限商塔中存在可实现的反常签名不等式违反;该判据与 Elek–Szabó 的超积方法逻辑独立,可在有限对象上逐个验证。
