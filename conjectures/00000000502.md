# 00000000502

**English.** Definition: For the edge ideal I(G) of a graph G, the regularity of powers reg I(G)^t is eventually linear in t, with slope denoted a(G). Conjecture: For every bipartite graph G, a(G) = max{ν(G)−1, ⌈(m(G)+1)/3⌉}, where ν(G) is the matching number and m(G) the induced matching number; moreover, for chordal bipartite graphs the formula simplifies to a(G) = ν(G)−1.

**中文。** 定义：对图 G 的边理想 I(G),幂 I(G)^t 的正则度 reg I(G)^t 关于 t 最终为线性,其斜率记 a(G)。猜想：对任意二部图 G,a(G) = max{ν(G)−1, ⌈(m(G)+1)/3⌉},其中 ν(G) 为最大匹配数,m(G) 为诱导匹配数;且该公式在弦二部图上可简化为 a(G)=ν(G)−1。
