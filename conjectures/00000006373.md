# 00000006373

**MX2-03 ★** — source: 198_有限元III_混合元.md

**English.** Definition: The projection with the mesh-dependence bound of mixed-scheme projection operators. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit mesh dependence of the operator norm, uniform for shape-regular families; the minimal constant of uniformity is the stability constant of the Fortin projection; and the bound degenerates as the angle tends to zero, the degeneration rate being the reciprocal of the sine of the angle. (optimal regular uniform conversion of operator norms)

**中文。** 定义：投影：混合格式的投影算子的网格依赖界。猜想：换算常数为算子范数的显式网格依赖且形状正则族的界一致；一致的极小常数为 Fortin 投影的稳定性常数；界的退化在角度趋零时且退化率为角度的正弦倒数。（算子范数正则一致最优换算）
