**INP-01 ★** — source: 273_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: TASEJ denotes not symmetric simple repulsion process. Conjecture: reversible measure of transition probability and joint distribution of map class group action under Hurwitz number are equal; position and constant of its many time joint distribution is 1. (TASEJ-Hurwitz corresponds to)

**中文。** 定义：TASEJ 指全不对称简单排斥过程。猜想：转移概率的可逆测度与映射类群作用下Hurwitz 数相等；粒子位置的联合分布与其多时间联合分布的换算常数为 1。（TASEJ-Hurwitz 对应）
