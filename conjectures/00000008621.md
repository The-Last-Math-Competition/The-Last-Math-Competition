# 00000008621

**INP-01 ★** — source: 273_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: TASEP is the totally asymmetric simple exclusion process. Conjecture: The reversible measures of transition probabilities agree with Hurwitz numbers under mapping class group actions; and the conversion constant between the joint distribution of particle positions and their multi-time joint distribution is 1. (TASEP-Hurwitz correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：TASEJ 指全不对称简单排斥过程。猜想：转移概率的可逆测度与映射类群作用下Hurwitz 数相等；粒子位置的联合分布与其多时间联合分布的换算常数为 1。（TASEJ-Hurwitz 对应）
