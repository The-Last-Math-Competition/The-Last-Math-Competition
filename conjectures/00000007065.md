**English.** Definition: Wrapped Fukaya with the noncompact wrapped category. Conjecture: The automorphisms of the wrapped category are the representation of the symplectic homotopy group, and the kernel of the representation is the displacement-negligible objects. (displacement-kernel wrapped automorphism representation)
