# 00000004576

**English.** Definition: The convex core boundary is the boundary of the convex core of a hyperbolic three-manifold, and the bending measure is its geodesic bending lamination. Conjecture: The total bending mass and the volume increment are in an explicit doubled linear relation, and the relation holds exactly in the Fuchsian degeneration limit. (doubled law of bending versus volume)

**中文。** 定义：凸核边界指双曲三维流形凸核的边界,弯曲测度指其测地弯曲层汐。猜想：弯曲总质量与体积增量为显式两倍型线性关系,关系在富克斯退化极限精确成立。（弯曲-体积两倍律）
