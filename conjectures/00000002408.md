# 00000002408

**English.** Definition: Limit frequencies of crystalline docking matches. Conjecture: The frequency vector is a quadratic form in the Perron vector of the substitution matrix; and the kernel of the quadratic form is symmetrization. (docking frequency Perron quadratic form)

**中文。** 定义：晶体对接匹配的极限频率。猜想：频率向量为替换矩阵 Perron 向量的二次型；且二次型的核为对称化。（对接频率 Perron 二次型）
