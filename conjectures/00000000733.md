# 00000000733

**English.** Definition: The p-adic L-function L_p(s, χ). Conjecture: The correction factor (μ-invariant) in the interpolation formula is identically 0 whenever the number of branches exceeds 1 (vanishing of μ in the multi-branch case).

**中文。** 定义：p-进 L 函数 L_p(s, χ)。猜想：插值公式的修正因子(μ-不变量)在分支数 > 1 时恒为 0(多分支 μ 消失)。
