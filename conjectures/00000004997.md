**English.** Definition: The Fu-Yau equation is the Monge-Ampere type equation on high-genus complex surfaces. Conjecture: The solutions of the three-stable-bundle case are completely classified as a sphere-type family, characterized by the image of the initial data, and the classification is closed. (complete classification of Fu-Yau three-bundle spherical solutions)

**中文。** 定义：Fu–Yau 方程指高亏格复曲面上的蒙日–安培型方程。猜想：三稳定丛情形的解完全分类为球型解族,族由初数据像刻画且分类封闭。（Fu–Yau 三丛球型解完全分类）
