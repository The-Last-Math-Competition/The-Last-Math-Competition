**English.** Definition: Extremal geometry: the extremal structure of random points. Conjecture: Extremal random simplices: the asymptotics of maximal-volume simplices are Gamma-weighted extreme points, with the ordering of weights the Gumbel law. (extremal simplex Gamma weight Gumbel ordering)
