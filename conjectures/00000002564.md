# 00000002564

**English.** Definition: The tensor rank of a trilinear form is the minimum number of multiplications. Conjecture: There exists an explicit family of trilinear forms with rank at least (1+1/64)·n² (ε = 1/64 explicit); the construction is via skew transformations of the matrix-multiplication tensor. (explicit trilinear rank improvement)

**中文。** 定义：三线性型的张量秩指最少乘法数。猜想：显式三线性型族其秩 ≥(1+1/64)· n²(ε = 1/64 显式);构造由矩阵乘张量的斜变换。
