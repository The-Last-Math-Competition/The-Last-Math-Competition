# 00000003145

**English.** Definition: Lie pseudogroups: the geometry of infinite-dimensional Lie groups. Conjecture: Finite-type Lie pseudogroups: the Cartan–Kähler integral of a pseudogroup exists, with the finiteness of the integral the Cartan criterion on structure constants. (pseudogroup Cartan-Kahler finiteness criterion)
