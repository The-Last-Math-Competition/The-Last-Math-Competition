**English.** Definition: The remainder term when compactness fails in the critical Sobolev embedding is the correction between the deficit of the best constant and the energy concentration of the bubble family. Conjecture: At the critical exponent p* = N, the third-order correction is given explicitly by the energy of the Bessel potential G_s*u: the sharp inequality reads ||u||_{p*} ≤ S||∇u||₂ + C·E_Bessel(u), with C explicit via the logarithmic capacity of ∫G_s.

**中文。** 定义：临界 Sobolev 嵌入缺失紧性时的余项指最佳常数之差与 bubble 族能量集中之间的修正项。猜想：在临界指标 p*=N 时,三阶修正项由 Bessel 位势 G_s*u 的能量显式给出:最佳不等式为 ||u||_{p*} ≤ S||∇u||_2 + C·E_Bessel(u),且 C 由 ∫G_s 的对数容量显式。
