# 00000005812

**English.** Definition: Finite propagation is the compactly supported interface advance of porous-medium type. Conjecture: For the cubic exponent (m = 3) the free-boundary regularity is the explicit combination of Lipschitz plus half-power, degenerating to pure half-power at interface contact; the half-power exponent is half of m-1; and the waiting time is a power-type functional of the initial data, with exponent 1/(m-1). (free-boundary half-power combination of the cubic exponent)

**中文。** 定义：有限传播：紧支撑的界面推进（多孔介质型）。猜想：立方指数（m=3）的自由边界正则为 Lipschitz 加半幂的显式组合且界面接触处退化为纯半幂；半幂指数为 m-1 的一半；等待时间（界面滞留）为初始数据的幂型泛函且指数为 1/(m-1)。（立方指数自由边界半幂组合）
