**English.** Definition: An algebraic Riccati equation is the quadratic matrix equation of control theory. Conjecture: The criterion for existence and stability of the solution is that the Hamiltonian spectrum has no imaginary-axis roots plus stabilizability, necessary and sufficient and polynomially verifiable. (polynomial stability criterion of Riccati equations)

**中文。** 定义：代数 Riccati 方程指控制论中的二次矩阵方程。猜想：解存在且稳定的判据为哈密顿谱无虚轴根加可稳定化条件,判据必要充分且多项式可验。（Riccati 稳定充要多项式判据）
