# 00000002618

**English.** Definition: Canonical join representations of lattices: irredundant decompositions of join dependencies. Conjecture: A finite lattice is semidistributive if and only if every element has a unique canonical join representation; the existence of the representation is equivalent to congruence separation with separation constant 1. (canonical join criterion)

**中文。** 定义：格的 canonical join 表示：join 依赖的不可约分解。猜想：有限格为半分配当且仅当每个元素有唯一 canonical join 表示；表示的存在性等价于同余分离性且分离常数为 1。（canonical join 判据）
