# 00000009775

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative Lorentz spaces L^{p,q}: renormalized interpolation of operator ideals. Conjecture: The repair constant for the endpoint failure of real interpolation of noncommutative Lorentz spaces is an explicit function of min(p,q); after repair the interpolation exactly includes weak Schatten classes with logarithmic loss constant. (noncommutative Lorentz interpolation)

**中文。** 定义：非交换 Lorentz 空间 L^{p,q}：算子理想的重整化插值。猜想：非交换 Lorentz 空间实插值端点失效的修复常数为 min(p,q) 的显式函数；修复后插值精确包含弱 Schatten 类且损失常数为对数型。（非交换 Lorentz 插值）
