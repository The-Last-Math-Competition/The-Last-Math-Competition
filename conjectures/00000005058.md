**English.** Definition: Homogenization is the quantitative form of quantum ergodicity. Conjecture: The conversion constant of partial homogenization is an explicit polynomial in the reciprocal of the spectral gap, of degree half the dimension, matching the free case. (spectral-gap polynomial conversion of homogenization)
