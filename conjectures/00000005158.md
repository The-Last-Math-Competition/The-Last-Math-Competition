# 00000005158

**HD2-18 ◆** — source: 157_复几何II_Hodge理论.md

**English.** Definition: Algebraic subvarieties of the period domain are the closures of images of the period map. Conjecture: One-dimensional algebraic subvarieties split into special curves and explicit nonhomogeneous curves, the classification following from an Ax-Schanuel type transcendence principle; the special closure of a nonhomogeneous curve is a finite point set; and the intersection pattern of the two classes is finite with all intersection points special. (two-class classification of period-domain curves)

**中文。** 定义：周期域的代数子簇：周期映射像的闭包。猜想：一维代数子簇分为特殊曲线与显式非齐次曲线两类且分类由 Ax–Schanuel 型超越原理推出；非齐次曲线的特殊闭包为有限点集；两类的交模式为有限且交点均为特殊点。（周期域曲线二类分类）
