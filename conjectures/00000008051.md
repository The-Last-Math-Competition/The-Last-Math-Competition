**English.** Definition: The proof-theoretic ordinal |T| of a theory T (the ordinal strength of the Pi-1-1 fragment), agreeing with the maximal length of transfinite induction schemata representable over well-founded recursive paths of T. Conjecture: The proof-theoretic ordinal of the subsystem Pi-1-1-CA_0 of second-order arithmetic, given by explicit notations above Bachmann-Howard (the known psi(Omega_omega) analysis), can be further compressed: the minimal ordinal-notation length of any recursive well-order representation of |Pi-1-1-CA_0| is a tower-type growth function f_{epsilon_0} in n, with no representation shorter than an Omega-layer tower (representation minimality); and this minimality is equivalent to the density level of the halting problem of aurora Turing machines embedding T. (Pi-1-1-CA_0 notation tower minimality)
