# 00000004090

**English.** Definition: The connectivity of a bifurcation diagram is the connected topology of the stable branch diagram. Conjecture: There exist arbitrarily small parameter perturbations of a bifurcation family that change the connectivity of the bifurcation diagram while preserving the spectrum, with the smallest example a three-dimensional odd-symmetric system. (spectrum-preserving connectivity separation)

**中文。** 定义：分叉图的连通性指稳定分支图的连通拓扑。猜想：存在分叉族任意小的参数扰动在保持谱不变下改变分叉图连通性,最小例子为三维奇对称系统。（谱保持连通分离）
