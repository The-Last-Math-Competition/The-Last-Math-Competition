# 00000002379

**OF-29 ★** — source: 64_算子理论II_函数演算与自由分析.md

**English.** Definition: A regular dilation is an extension to a normal operator. Conjecture: The minimal dimension compensation for the absence of regular dilations of polydisk contractions is n·2^n; and the compensation is realized by the Sz.-Nagy–Foiaş displacement. (regular dilation absence dimension compensation)

**中文。** 定义：正则膨胀为到正规算子的延拓。猜想：多圆盘压缩的正则膨胀缺失的最小维数补偿为 n·2^n；且补偿的实现为 Sz.-Nagy–Foiaș 落差。（正则膨胀缺失维数补偿）
