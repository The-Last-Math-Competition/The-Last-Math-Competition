# 00000004265

**GT2-25 ★** — source: 127_数学物理II_规范理论的数学.md

**English.** Definition: A framed VOA is a vertex algebra with SO(8) framing structure. Conjecture: Its attainable central charges form a complete lattice of half-odd-integer multiples of 24, the smallest central charge is 12, and the lattice gap widens linearly with the central charge. (attainable lattice of framed central charges)

**中文。** 定义：framed VOA 指带 SO(8) 框架结构的顶点代数。猜想：其中心荷可达值构成 24 的半奇数倍完全格,最小中心荷为 12,格间隙随中心荷线性扩大。（framed 中心荷可达格）
