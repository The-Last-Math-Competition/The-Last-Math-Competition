**English.** Definition: The gl constant (Gordon–Lewis): the connection between unconditional bases and operator ideals. Conjecture: The multiplicativity of the gl constant: the gl constant of a tensor product is the product of the two factors with a correction, the correction being the mixed-type index of the kernel method; multiplicativity degenerates on Hilbert factors. (Gordon-Lewis multiplicativity)

**中文。** 定义：gl 常数（Gordon–Lewis）：无条件基与算子理想的连接。猜想：gl 常数的乘性：张量积的 gl 常数为两因子的乘积修正且修正项为核方法的混合型指标；乘性在 Hilbert 因子退化。（Gordon-Lewis 乘性）
