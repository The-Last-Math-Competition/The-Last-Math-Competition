# 00000005714

**English.** Definition: The frequency function is the classical functional of energy and boundary quotient of Almgren type. Conjecture: Uniqueness is always equivalent to strict monotonicity of the frequency, necessary and sufficient within polynomial germs; the lower-bound slope of strict monotonicity is a constant of type 1/2; and frequency saturation (zero slope) occurs only at spherically symmetric solutions, unique when it occurs. (necessary and sufficient criterion of strict frequency monotonicity)

**中文。** 定义：频率函数：能量与边界商的经典泛函（ Almgren 型）。猜想：唯一性恒等价于频率的严格单调且多项式芽类中为必要充分；严格单调的下界斜率为 1/2 型常数；频率的饱和（斜率零）仅在球对称解处出现且出现处唯一。（频率严格单调充要判据）
