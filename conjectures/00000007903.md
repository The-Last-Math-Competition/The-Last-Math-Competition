**English.** Definition: Stabilization of the elliptic part of the trace formula: the entanglement coefficients a(gamma) in the stable decomposition of the elliptic term TR^ell(f) of the Arthur-Selberg trace formula. Conjecture: The stabilization coefficients satisfy a global area identity of the type product over v of a(gamma_v) = sum over delta of kappa(delta); and the deep extension of the (proven) fundamental lemma: the minimal ramification count of the stable barycenter trajectory is a power of 2 (completely controlled by the root-edge graph structure of the radical); the spectral-side weights on the ramification trajectories are integers with absolute value at most half the order of the central group. (elliptic stabilization power-of-two ramification)
