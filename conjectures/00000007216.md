# 00000007216

**English.** Definition: The Linnik problem with representations in arithmetic progressions. Conjecture: The ergodic proof of the equidistribution of representations holds, and the engine of the proof is the action of semisimple groups. (semisimple-engine Linnik equidistribution)

**中文。** 定义：Linnik 问题：等差数列的表示。猜想：Linnik 的遍历侧：表示的等分布的遍历证明且明的引擎为半单群的作用。（Linnik 半单群等分布遍历证明引擎）
