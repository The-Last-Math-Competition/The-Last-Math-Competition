**English.** Definition: Pseudospectra as the measure of nonnormality. Conjecture: The area growth of pseudospectra holds, and the constant of the growth is Kreiss. (Kreiss-constant pseudospectral explosion)

**中文。** 定义：伪谱：非正规性的度量。猜想：伪谱的爆炸：伪谱的面积增长且长的常数为 Kreiss。（伪谱 Kreiss 面积爆炸非正规性度量增长常数）
