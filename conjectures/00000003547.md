**English.** Definition: The Erdős–Rado arrow relations: partition cardinals. Conjecture: The tightness of arrow relations: the gap between failure of infinite partition relations and success at higher cardinals is separability, with the extremum of the gap exponential towers. (arrow relation exponential tower gap extremum)
