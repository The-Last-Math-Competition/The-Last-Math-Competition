**English.** Definition: Engulfing and unstable manifolds: the geometry of hyperbolic dynamical systems. Conjecture: The engulfing of unstable manifolds: the invariant foliations of hyperbolic sets admit engulfing extensions, with the dimension of the extension the complement of the foliation dimension. (invariant foliation engulfing complementary extension)

**中文。** 定义：套叠与不稳定流形：双曲动力系统的几何。猜想：不稳定流形的套叠：双曲集的不变叶层的套叠扩展且扩展的维数为叶层维数的余补。（不变叶层套叠余补扩展）
