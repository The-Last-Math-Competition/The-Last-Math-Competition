**MSH-03 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The min-max width spectrum W_k is defined by infima of maximal volumes over one-parameter families; the multiplicity of the realizing surfaces is the multiplicity-one conjecture. Conjecture: For Baire-generic metrics every width W_k is realized by a single multiplicity embedded minimal hypersurface, and the single-multiplicity set is open and dense; exceptional metrics (forcing multiplicity) form an analytically thin set of infinite codimension. (multiplicity-one stability)

**中文。** 定义：min-max 宽度谱 W_k 由单参数族的最大体积下确界定义；宽度实现曲面的重数（multiplicity）问题为多重重一猜想。猜想：Baire 一般度量下每个宽度 W_k 由单重嵌入极小超曲面实现，单重性集合为开稠密；例外度量（强迫多重性）为余维无穷的解析薄集。（多重重一稳定性）
