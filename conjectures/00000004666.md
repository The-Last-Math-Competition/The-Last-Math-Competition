# 00000004666

**English.** Definition: The Durfee inequality is the lower bound of the Milnor number in terms of bidegrees. Conjecture: Its optimal improvement is the original bound plus an explicit strengthening term in nu, the strengthening cannot be increased, and it is attained by explicit double points. (nu-improved Durfee inequality)

**中文。** 定义：Durfee 不等式指 Milnor 数关于双次数的下界。猜想：其最优改进为原界加 ν 的显式加强项,加强项不可再增且由显式二重奇点达到。（Durfee 不等式加 ν 改进）
