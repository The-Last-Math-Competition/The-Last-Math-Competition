# 00000003272

**English.** Definition: Codimension-one immersions and the Euler characteristic: the orientability criterion. Conjecture: The Euler obstruction of codimension-one immersion: a manifold immerses with codimension one if and only if the Euler characteristic is even, with the geometry of the obstruction the Hopf index of the normal field. (codimension one Euler even Hopf index obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：余一浸入与 Euler 示性数：可定向判据。猜想：余一浸入的 Euler 障碍：流形余一浸入当且仅当 Euler 示性数为偶且障碍的几何为法向场的 Hopf 指数。（余一 Euler 偶 Hopf 指数障碍）
