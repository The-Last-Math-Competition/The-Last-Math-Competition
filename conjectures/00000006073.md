# 00000006073

**English.** Definition: Spurious minima are stationary points with zero gradient but not minima, with their quantified structure. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit formula of the saddle-point index after smoothing, the saddle index at least one; the Morse-type decomposition of the index has lower bound the logarithm of the critical-point count; and the attraction basin of spurious minima has measure zero, strictly in the analytic case. (lower-bound formula of smoothed saddle indices)

**中文。** 定义：伪极小：梯度为零但非极小的驻点；其量化结构。猜想：换算常数为光滑化后鞍点指标的显式公式且鞍点指标不小于一；指标的 Morse 型分解的下界为临界点数对数；伪极小的吸引域测度为零且零性在解析情形严格。（光滑化鞍点指标下界公式）
