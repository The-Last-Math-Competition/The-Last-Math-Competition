# 00000002068

**UA-18 ★** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Morita equivalence is the equivalence type of categories (Dukarm theory). Conjecture: The correspondence between Morita classes of rings and Morita classes of the associated affine-variety categories is surjective; the fibers are exactly Picard classes (torsors of the Picard group).

**中文。** 定义：Morita 等价指范畴的等价型(Dukarm 理论)。猜想：环的 Morita 类与对应仿射簇范畴的 Morita 类的对应为满射;纤维恰为 Picard 类(Picard 群的扭转)。
