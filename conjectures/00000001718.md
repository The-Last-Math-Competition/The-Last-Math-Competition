# 00000001718

**EG-28 ★** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Conjecture: The discrepancy growth of balanced words of algebraic integers of cubic fields is O(log N) (cubic balanced discrepancy); and the discrepancy constant is exactly the infimum of the embedding ratio of the totally real cubic field.

**中文。** 猜想：三次域的代数整数的平衡词的偏差增长为 O(log N)(三次平衡偏差)；且偏差常数恰为全实三次域的嵌入比的下确界。
