# 00000002011

**English.** Definition: Periodic homogenization is the convergence of solutions with rapidly oscillating coefficients to homogenized coefficients. Conjecture: The optimal convergence rate for nonsmooth coefficients is ε² (the correction being the second-order contribution of the doubly periodic boundary layer); the exact exponent 2 (matching the smooth case).

**中文。** 定义：周期均匀化指系数快速振荡的解收敛到均匀化系数。猜想：非光滑系数的最优收敛率为 ε²(修正项为双周期的边界层的二阶贡献);率的精确指数 2(与光滑情形一致)。
