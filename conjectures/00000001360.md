# 00000001360

**English.** Conjecture: For any fixed cusp form f, the distribution of log L(sym³ f, 1) — the value at s = 1 of the symmetric-cube L-function under cubic twists — is Gaussian with variance (1/2)log log q (extreme-value statistics of cubic twists).

**中文。** 猜想：任意固定模形式 f 的对称立方 L 函数在 s = 1 的值 log L(sym³ f, 1) 的分布为高斯,方差 (1/2)log log q(立方扭曲的极值统计)。
