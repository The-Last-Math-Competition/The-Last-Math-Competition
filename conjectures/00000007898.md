# 00000007898

**English.** Definition: The projective dimension spectrum of multicoherent sheaves: the extremal generation length p_n(X) of projective objects in the n-fold multicoherent category Coh^{[n]}(X). Conjecture: p_n(X) = p_1(X) + n for every projective variety (a linear translation law); and p_1(X) = dim(X) (a strengthening of Serre's theorem); the two-periodicity of the diagonalized decomposition of Ext groups between layers is strictly broken for n at least 2, the defect dimension equaling the Hodge number h^{n,0} of X. (multicoherent linear projective law)

**中文。** 定义：多层拟层（multicoherent sheaves）的投射维数谱：凝聚层的 n-多层化 Coh^{[n]}(X) 的投射对象的生成长的极值 p_n(X)。猜想：p_n(X) = p_1(X)+n 对一切射影簇成立（线性平移律）；且 p_1(X)=dim(X)（Serre 定理的强化）；多层化的 Ext 群在层间的对角化分解的 2-周期性在 n≥2 时严格破坏，破缺项的维度等于 X 的 Hodge 数 h^{n,0}。
