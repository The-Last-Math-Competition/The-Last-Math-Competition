# 00000004881

**English.** Definition: The word problem is the decision problem of equivalence of group words. Conjecture: The infimum lattice of the undecidability degree spectrum is characterized by explicit types of cancellation conditions, all decidable at hyperbolic groups, with the boundary given by solvable small cancellation, and the spectrum infimum is exactly the inverse-logarithmic lattice of the Dehn function exponent. (cancellation-type characterization of the word problem degree spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：字问题指群词等价的判定问题。猜想：其不可判定度谱的下确界格由消除条件的显式型刻画,双曲群处全部可判定,边界由可解小消除给出。(字问题度谱消除型刻画)；且度谱下确界恰为 Dehn 函数指数的逆对数格。
