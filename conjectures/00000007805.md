# 00000007805

**English.** Definition: The optimal exponent of Brunn-Minkowski stability: a deficit epsilon in vol(K+L)^{1/n} >= vol(K)^{1/n} + vol(L)^{1/n} should force K and L, after rigid motion, close to congruent simplices. Conjecture: The optimal exponent for the stability measure (a monotone function of Hausdorff distance) is epsilon^{1/(n+1)}; and this exponent is asymptotically attained by a randomized extremal construction for the simplex, taking L to be the minimal body of the epsilon-neighborhood of K. (simplex-sharp BM stability exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Brunn–Minkowski 稳定性的最优指数：vol(K+L)^{1/n} ≥ vol(K)^{1/n}+vol(L)^{1/n} 的亏缺 ε 蕴含 K,L 经刚体运动后逼近同余的单形体。猜想：稳定性度量（Hausdorff 距离的单调函数）的最优指数为 ε^{1/(n+1)}；且该指数由单纯形的一个随机化极值构造渐近达到，其构造取 L 为 K 的 ε-邻域的极小体。
