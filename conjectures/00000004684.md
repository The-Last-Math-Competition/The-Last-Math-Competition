# 00000004684

**English.** Definition: Right equivalence is the equivalence of functions under changes of variables. Conjecture: The normal form of this singularity type is an explicit two-parameter family, the family modulo equivalence retains exactly two parameters, and the boundary case degenerates to a cusp union. (two-parameter normal form with cusp boundary)
