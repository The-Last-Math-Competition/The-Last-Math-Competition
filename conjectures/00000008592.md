# 00000008592

**MLC-02 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: adaptive estimate denotes estimate of simultaneously for all Sobolev ball minimax. Conjecture: logarithm loss factor of Lepski normal always optimal and smooth tends to infinite when tends to one; and loss of adaptive estimate at least fixed multiple of logn. (adaptive logarithm loss optimal)

**中文。** 定义：自适应估计指同时对一切 Sobolev 球接近极小极大的估计子。猜想：Lepski 型方法的对数损失因子恒最优且光滑度趋于无穷时趋于一；且任何自适应估计子的额外损失不低于 logn 的固定倍数。（自适应对数损失最优）
