# 00000008592

**MLC-02 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Adaptive estimation: estimators simultaneously near-minimax over all Sobolev balls. Conjecture: The logarithmic loss factor of Lepski type methods is always optimal and tends to one as the smoothness grows; and the extra loss of any adaptive estimator is at least a fixed multiple of log n. (optimal adaptive logarithmic loss)

**中文。** 定义：自适应估计指同时对一切 Sobolev 球接近极小极大的估计子。猜想：Lepski 型方法的对数损失因子恒最优且光滑度趋于无穷时趋于一；且任何自适应估计子的额外损失不低于 logn 的固定倍数。（自适应对数损失最优）
