# 00000007761

**ADY-11 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The preperiodic graph Pi_p(f) is the functional graph of f on F_p, with invariant rho_p = longest tail length over maximal cycle length. Conjecture: For fixed quadratic f as p grows, rho_p converges in distribution to an absolutized form of the Beta distribution with parameters (1/2,1/2); and the expectation of rho_p tends to pi/8 + 1/8, deviating from the random mapping value 1/pi exactly by a quadratic-character correction from the discriminant sign. (Beta limit of preperiodic graph ratios)

**中文。** 定义：预周期图 Π_p(f) 为 f 在 F_p 上的函数图；其不变量 ρ_p=（最长尾长）/（最大圈长）。猜想：对固定的二次 f 与 p→∞，ρ_p 依分布收敛到参数为 (1/2,1/2) 的 Beta 分布的绝对值化形式；且 E[ρ_p]→π/8+1/8，与随机映射模型的 1/π 偏差恰为判别式符号的二次特征修正。
