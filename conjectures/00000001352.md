# 00000001352

**AN2-22 △** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Conjecture: The Linnik constant can be taken as 2: min{p ≡ a mod q} ≪ φ(q)·log²q for all coprime (a, q); and the constant 2 is optimal — there are infinitely many (a, q) for which the first congruent prime is at least c·φ(q)·log²q.

**中文。** 猜想：Linnik 常数可取 2:min{p≡a mod q} ≪ φ(q)·log²q 对一切互素 (a,q);且常数 2 为最优——存在无穷多 (a,q) 使首个同余素数 ≥ c·φ(q)·log²q。
