# 00000002262

**MG-02 ★** — source: 61_鞅与随机分析.md

**English.** Definition: The BDG inequality E(sup|f|^p) ≤ C_p E⟨f⟩_∞^{p/2}. Conjecture: The exact value of C_p is known asymptotically only for p ≥ 2; new: the exact constant at p = 4 (the BDG p=4 constant).

**中文。** 定义：BDG 不等式 E(sup|f|^p) ≤ C_p E⟨f⟩^{p/2}_∞。猜想：C_p 的精确值已知仅在 p ≥ 2 的渐近;新:p = 4 的精确常数(BDG p=4 常数)。
