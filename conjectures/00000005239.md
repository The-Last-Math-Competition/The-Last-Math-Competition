# 00000005239

**English.** Definition: Duality is the restriction property of the pairing on the subring. Conjecture: The success or failure of tautological ring duality at genus twenty-three is decided by the kernel of explicit quadratic relations, the threshold is twenty-three, and there are no intermediate genera. (genus twenty-three threshold of tautological duality)
