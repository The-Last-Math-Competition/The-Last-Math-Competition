**English.** Definition: Contact nonsqueezing: the nonsqueezing constant cs_sqz of contact manifolds. Conjecture: cs_sqz (the critical width of contact nonsqueezing) is the normalized 1 for the standard S^{2n+1} (a unit width law); the dimension of the squeezing exception (failure of nonsqueezing) is the 3-dimensional exceptional domain (a three-dimensional exceptional domain law); the quantification of the exception (squeezable pre-Poincare domains) has volume constant pi/2 (a pi/2 constant law); and the high-dimensional (n at least 2) recovery of nonsqueezing (Eliashberg-Kim-Polterovich type) has modulation correction by a positive boojum constant (a boojum constant law). (contact nonsqueezing pi/2 boojum constant)

**中文。** 定义：切触非挤压：切触流形的非挤压常数 cs_sqz。猜想：cs_sqz（切触非挤压的临界宽度）= 标准 S^{2n+1} 的 1（单位宽度律）的正规化；且挤压的例外（非挤压的失效）的维数为 3 维的例外域（三维例外域律）；例外的量化（可挤压的预 Poincaré 域）的体积常数为 π/2（π/2 常数律）；非挤压的高维（n ≥ 2）的恢复（Eliashberg–Kim–Polterovich 型）的调制修正为 boojum 的正常数（boojum 常数律）。
