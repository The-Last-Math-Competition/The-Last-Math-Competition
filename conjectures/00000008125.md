# 00000008125

**English.** Definition: The relation between the Rouquier dimension dim_R D^b(qgr-A) of the noncommutative projective variety qgr-A of a connected graded algebra and the central Krull dimension d. Conjecture: For commutative-type graded algebras dim_R is exactly d-1, attained by a Beilinson type exceptional collection; the qgr of the two-point Smith algebra has dim_R exactly 2; in general dim_R does not exceed d+1 and the lower bound dim_R >= d-1 is given by the length of graded minimal free resolutions, both bounds being attained by explicit exceptional constructions. (Rouquier dimension exceptional tight bounds)

**中文。** 定义：连通分次域 A 的非交换射影簇 qgr-A 的 Rouquier 维数 dim_R D^b(qgr-A) 与中心 Krull 维数 d 的关系式。猜想：交换型分次域的 dim_R 恰为 d-1 且由 Beilinson 型例外组达成；Smith 代数双点的 qgr 有 dim_R 恰为 2；一般地 dim_R 不超过 d+1 且下界 dim_R ≥ d-1 由分次极小自由分解的长度给出，两端界均由显式例外组构造达到。
