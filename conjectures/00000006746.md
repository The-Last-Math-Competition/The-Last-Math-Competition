**English.** Definition: Duality with the dimension-map structure of Coxeter functors. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the dimension map and the two components commute; the image is a curve of genus zero; and the period of the map is half the Coxeter number. (dimension-curve duality conversion)

**中文。** 定义：对偶：Coxeter 函子的维数映射结构。猜想：换算常数为维数映射的显式且两分量交换；像为曲线且曲线的亏格为零；映射的周期为 Coxeter 数之半。（维数曲线对偶换算常数）
