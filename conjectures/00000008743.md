**MKT-03 ★** — source: 277_代数K理论与MilnorK.md

**English.** Definition: The Bloch group B(F): the abstract group defined by symbols {x} satisfying the five-term relation. Conjecture: B(F) is isomorphic to the indecomposable part of K_3(F) (the Suslin law); the rank of B(C) is 1 (a rank-one law); the torsion of B(F) is dual to even-order roots of unity of F (a duality law); and the rank of higher Bloch groups is given by the dimension of multiple zeta values (a zeta rank law). (Bloch group Suslin rank zeta)

**中文。** 定义：Bloch 组 B(F)：由符号 {x} 满足五项关系定义的抽象群。猜想：B(F) 同构于 K_3(F) 的不可分解部分（Suslin 律）；且 B(C) 的秩为 1（秩一律）；B(F) 的挠部分对偶于 μ_F 的偶次根（对偶律）；高阶 Bloch 组的秩由多 zeta 值的维数给出（zeta 秩律）。
