# 00000005161

**English.** Definition: Mixed Hodge structures carry a weight filtration, with the first-order extension groups. Conjecture: The conversion of first-order extensions is the intermediate-Jacobian quotient of double extensions, an elliptic curve in the simplest type; the dimension of the quotient equals the sum of weight-one Hodge numbers; and the image of the extension monodromy group is an explicit arithmetic subgroup of the symplectic group of finite index. (intermediate-Jacobian quotient of mixed extensions)

**中文。** 定义：混合 Hodge 结构：带权滤过的分层结构；一阶扩张群。猜想：一阶扩张的换算为双扩张的中间雅可比商且最简单型时商为椭圆曲线；商的维数等于权一 Hodge 数之和；扩张单值群的像为辛群的显式算术子群且指标为有限。（混合扩张中间雅可比商）
