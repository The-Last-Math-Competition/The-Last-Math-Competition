**English.** Definition: Secant genus: asymptotic varieties of curves. Conjecture: The secant genus formula: the genus of the asymptotic variety is the interpolation polynomial of the abundance function, with the degree of the polynomial the winding number. (secant abundance interpolation winding degree)
