# 00000003398

**English.** Definition: The noncommutative version of the local index formula: the JLO cocycle. Conjecture: The equivalence of JLO: the JLO cocycle is equivalent to periodic cyclic cohomology as an entire homotopy class, with representatives of the equivalence local coefficients. (JLO local coefficient homotopy equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：局部指标公式的非交换版：JLO 上闭链。猜想：JLO 的等价性：JLO 上闭链与周期循环上同调的等价为整个同伦类且等价的代表为局部系数。（JLO 局部系数同伦等价）
