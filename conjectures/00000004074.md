# 00000004074

**English.** Definition: The geometric Lorenz attractor is the attractor of an affine expanding map on a Poincare section. Conjecture: Its fractal dimension is a strictly increasing function of the parameter with a supremum below 2.1, attained at an explicit limiting parameter. (supremum of the Lorenz dimension)

**中文。** 定义：几何 Lorenz 吸引子指 Poincaré 截面上仿射扩张映射的吸引子。猜想：其分形维数为参数的严格递增函数且存在小于 2.1 的上确界,上确界在显式极限参数处达到。（Lorenz 维数上确界）
