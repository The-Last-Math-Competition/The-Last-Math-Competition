# 00000008661

**English.** Definition: The Bass-Serre tree of an amalgamated product is the action tree of the split group. Conjecture: Solvability of subgroup word problems always passes to solvability of the word problem of the amalgamated product; exception examples are given by subgroups with undecidable membership problem, and the characterization of the decidable subclass is stabilizers of finite-index intersections. (Serre word problem passage criterion)

**中文。** 定义：amalgam 积的 Bass–Serre 树指分裂群的作用树。猜想：子群字问题可解恒传递到 amalgam 积的字可解；例外例由成员问题不可判定的子群给出而成员可解子类的刻画为有限指数交的稳定子。（Serre 字问题传递判据）
