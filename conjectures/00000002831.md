# 00000002831

**English.** Definition: Algebraic statistics: implicit polynomial parametrizations of probability models and the combinatorial geometry of statistical inference. Conjecture: Algebraic invariants of hidden Markov models: the image of the transition-emission parameter space in observed distributions is an algebraic variety whose ideal is generated by explicit invariants; the degree spectrum of the invariants is a linear function of the tree parameters. (HMM algebraic invariant spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：代数统计：概率模型的隐多项式参数化与统计推断的组合几何。猜想：隐马尔可夫模型的代数不变量：转移-发射参数空间到观测分布的映射的像为代数簇且簇的理想由显式不变量生成；不变量的次数谱为树参数的线性函数。（HMM 代数不变量谱）
