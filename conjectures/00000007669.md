# 00000007669

**English.** Definition: The Bailey binary tree chain consists of all identities generated from a seed pair by moving at each step in one of the two directions a to aq or b to bq, with a_m the node count at level m. Conjecture: a_m = Fib(m+2), the number of distinct identities being exactly Fib(m+2)-1; and every level-m identity has at least m+1 factors on the product side, with equality exactly on purely one-directional chains. (Fibonacci counting of the Bailey tree)

**中文。** 定义：Bailey 二叉树链指从种子对出发,每步沿参数 a→aq 或 b→bq 两个方向之一移动生成的恒等式全体,第 m 层节点数记 a_m。猜想：a_m = Fib(m+2),其中不同恒等式恰有 Fib(m+2)−1 条;每个第 m 层恒等式乘积侧的因子个数至少为 m+1,等号恰在纯单方向链上取得。（Bailey 二叉树 Fibonacci 计数）
