# 00000001171

**LT-71 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Conjecture: The multiplication structure constants of the canonical basis are Laurent polynomials with positive coefficients, and their degree support lies in the length interval [ℓ(x)+ℓ(y)−2k, ℓ(x)+ℓ(y)], where k is the common Demazure support depth; the support width in rank 2 is given by the length difference of affine Weyl translations.

**中文。** 猜想：典范基的乘法结构常数为正系数 Laurent 多项式,且其度支撑集包含于长度区间 [ℓ(x)+ℓ(y)−2k, ℓ(x)+ℓ(y)],k 为公共 Demazure 支撑深度;支撑宽度在秩 2 由仿射 Weyl 平移的长度差给出。
