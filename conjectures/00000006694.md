**English.** Definition: The entropy-profile convex deficit of the geodesic formula. Conjecture: The convex deficit is given by measure regularity and the deficit spectrum closes; the minimal element of the spectrum is the flat measure; and the minimality of the flat measure is decided by entropy maximization. (closed deficit spectrum of entropy profiles)
