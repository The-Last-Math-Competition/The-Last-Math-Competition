# 00000002834

**AS-04 ★** — source: 80_代数统计与张量分解的几何.md

**English.** Definition: Uniqueness of CP decomposition: identifiability of tensor rank decompositions. Conjecture: The strengthened Kruskal condition for CP uniqueness: uniqueness at rank r holds when the sum of Kruskal ranks of factor matrices satisfies an explicit inequality strictly weaker than the classical one; the improvement gap is given by a mixed three-matrix criterion. (Kruskal strengthened condition)

**中文。** 定义：CP 分解的唯一性：张量的秩分解的可辨识性。猜想：CP 唯一性的 Kruskal 条件的加强版：秩为 r 的唯一性当因子矩阵的 Kruskal 秩之和满足显式不等式且新条件严格弱于经典条件；改进的间隙由三矩阵混合判据给出。（Kruskal 加强条件）
