**English.** Definition: virtual cohomology dimension denotes cohomology dimension of finite index subgroup. Conjecture: its and boundary dimension difference by explicit counting of torsion part gives, difference is zero if and only if torsion-free and boundary is ball plane type. (cohomology dimension - boundary difference torsion formula)
