**English.** Definition: The virtual cohomological dimension is the cohomological dimension of a finite-index subgroup. Conjecture: Its difference with the boundary dimension is given by an explicit count of the torsion part, and the difference is zero if and only if the group is torsion-free with sphere-type boundary. (torsion formula of the cohomology dimension-boundary difference)

**中文。** 定义：虚拟上同调维数指有限指标子群的上同调维数。猜想：其与边界维数之差由挠部分的显式计数给出,差为零当且仅当无挠且边界为球面型。（上同调维数-边界差挠公式）
