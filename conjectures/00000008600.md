# 00000008600

**MLC-10 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: structure hypothesis denotes or convex constraint. Conjecture: recurrence rate is n^{−2/3} constant 1; convex recurrence d ≤ 3 is n^{−4/(d+4)} and critical jump of d ≥ 4 dimension. (structure rate critical dimension)

**中文。** 定义：结构假设指单调或凸约束。猜想：单调回归速率为 n^{−2/3} 常数 1；凸回归 d ≤ 3 为 n^{−4/(d+4)} 且 d ≥ 4 出现维数诅咒的临界跳跃。（结构速率临界维）
