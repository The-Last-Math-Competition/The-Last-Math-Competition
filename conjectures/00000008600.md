# 00000008600

**English.** Definition: Structural assumptions mean monotonicity or convexity constraints. Conjecture: The monotone regression rate is n^{-2/3} with constant 1; and convex regression has rate n^{-4/(d+4)} for d at most 3, with the critical jump of the curse of dimensionality appearing at d at least 4. (structural rate critical dimension)

**中文。** 定义：结构假设指单调或凸约束。猜想：单调回归速率为 n^{−2/3} 常数 1；凸回归 d ≤ 3 为 n^{−4/(d+4)} 且 d ≥ 4 出现维数诅咒的临界跳跃。（结构速率临界维）
