**English.** Definition: The infinite cascade of Kosterlitz-Thouless flows: the asymptotics pitch_KT of the pitch of the spiral flow of the KT differential system (the flow of the couplings g,h). Conjecture: The asymptotics of pitch_KT (at long flow times) is 1/t (a logarithmic pitch decay); the half-angle of the invariant cone of the spiral flow (the cone invariant of the flow) is pi/8 (an invariant cone half-angle law); and the explicit value of the constant c in the exponential divergence xi ~ exp(c/sqrt T) of the correlation length under temperature perturbation at the KT transition is given by the imaginary part of the linearized spectrum at the fixed point (a spectral imaginary-part constant). (KT logarithmic pitch pi/8 invariant cone)

**中文。** 定义：Kosterlitz–Thouless 流的无限级联：KT 流的微分方程组（耦合 g,h 的流）的螺旋流的螺距的渐近 pitch_KT。猜想：pitch_KT 的渐近（长流时间）为 1/t（对数螺距衰减）；且螺旋流的不变锥（流的锥不变量）的半角为 π/8（不变锥半角律）；KT 过渡的温度扰动的相关长度 ξ ~ exp(c/√T)（指数发散）的常数 c 的显式值由固定点的线性化谱的虚部（谱虚部常数）给出。
