# 00000007158

**English.** Definition: Bendixson's theorem with real-part bounds of eigenvalues. Conjecture: The imaginary-part bandwidth of the spectrum is the norm of the antisymmetric part, and the minimizer of the norm is the normal matrix. (antisymmetric Bendixson imaginary-part bandwidth)

**中文。** 定义：Bendixson 定理：特征值的实部界。猜想：虚部的界：谱的虚部带宽为反对称部分的范数且数的极小为正规矩阵。（Bendixson 反对称正规极小范数虚部带宽）
