**English.** Definition: An elliptic divisibility sequence consists of the values of division polynomials. Conjecture: The prime divisibility pattern of the sequence is governed by a quadratic character spectrum; and the spectrum is controlled by the associated Galois representation. (EDS Galois representation quadratic character divisibility)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆 divisibility 序列为除子多项式值。猜想：序列的素整除模为二次特征谱；且谱的核为 Galois 表示。（EDS-Galois 表示二次特征整除谱）
