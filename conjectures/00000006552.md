# 00000006552

**English.** Definition: Strang splitting with the middle-step structure of the symmetric second-order splitting. Conjecture: The commutator coefficient of the second-order error leading term is exact and the closed combination is nested commutators; the coefficient is 1/24; and the nested-commutator count is three, controlled by the Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff order. (Strang second-order commutator coefficient)

**中文。** 定义：Strang 分裂：对称二阶分裂的中间步结构。猜想：二阶误差首项的换位子系数精确且封闭的组合为嵌套交换；系数为 1/24；嵌套交换的个数为三且个数由 Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff 阶数控制。（Strang 二阶换位子系数）
