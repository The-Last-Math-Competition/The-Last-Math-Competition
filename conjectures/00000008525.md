# 00000008525

**SDG-25 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: The Janet decomposition of Noetherian differential rings. Conjecture: The involutivity of the quasilinear systems of a Janet decomposition can be decided in finitely many steps; the step count is bounded by the Spencer delta-function. (finite decision of Janet involutivity)

**中文。** 定义：Noetherian 微分环的 Janet 分解。猜想：Janet 分解的拟线性组的 involutivity 的判定可在有限步内完成；步数由 Spencer δ-函数的上界给出。（Janet involutivity 有限判定）
