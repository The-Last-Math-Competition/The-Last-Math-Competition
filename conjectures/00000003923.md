**English.** Definition: The components m_k of an A-infinity structure are the higher multiplication operators. Conjecture: In the Fukaya algebra of an embedded two-dimensional torus the smallest k with nonzero m_k is 3, while the first example in which higher multiplication does not degenerate occurs in the algebra of prism orbits, with k = 5. (first example of A-infinity higher multiplication)

**中文。** 定义：A∞-结构的 mₖ 指高阶乘法算子。猜想：嵌入二维环面的 Fukaya 代数中非零 mₖ 的最小 k 为 3,而高阶乘法不退化的首例出现于棱镜轨道的代数且 k = 5。（A∞ 高阶乘法首例）
