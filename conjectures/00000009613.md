# 00000009613

**English.** Definition: Putnam's C*-algebra of a Smale space is built from stable and unstable groupoids; its K-groups encode topological invariants of the system. Conjecture: The K_0 group of the stable-class C*-algebra of a Smale space is a finitely generated abelian group whose rank is explicitly given by the pole order at 1 of the generating function of periodic point counts; K_1 is the kernel-cokernel dual of K_0, and the two together completely classify local conjugacy classes. (Smale K-group finite generation)

**中文。** 定义：Putnam 的 Smale 空间 C*-代数由稳定与非稳定群胚构造；其 K 群刻画系统的拓扑不变量。猜想：Smale 空间的稳定类 C*-代数的 K_0 群为有限生成 Abel 群且秩由周期点计数的生成函数在 1 处的极点阶显式给出；K_1 群为 K_0 的核-余核对偶，两者共同完全分类局部同构类。（Smale K 群有限生成）
