**BR2-12 ★** — source: 143_非交换代数的几何_Brauer群与扭曲.md

**English.** Definition: The dimension of the moduli stack of twisted sheaves is the bilinear relation of the expected dimension of the stable twisted sheaf moduli space with the Chern character and the Brauer phase. Conjecture: The dimension is a bilinear expression in (rank, c_1, c_2) and the phase theta, degenerating at theta = 0 to the classical Le Potier dimension; the sign of the bilinear expression is determined by the order of the phase, flipping at order 2; the dimension of every irreducible component equals the expected dimension, with smoothness given by the openness of reduced stability. (bilinear dimension formula of twisted moduli)

**中文。** 定义：扭曲层模叠的维数：稳定扭曲层模空间的期望维数与陈特征、Brauer 相位的双线性关系。猜想：维数为 (rank,c₁,c₂) 与相位 θ 的双线性式且 θ=0 时退化为经典 Le Potier 维数；双线性式的符号由相位的阶决定且阶为 2 时符号翻转；不可约分量的维数恒等于期望维数且光滑性由约化稳定性的开性给出。（扭曲模维数双线性公式）
