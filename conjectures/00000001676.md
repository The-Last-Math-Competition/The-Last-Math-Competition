# 00000001676

**English.** Definition: The isoperimetric profile of the n×n torus grid is the minimum boundary function of its vertex subsets. Conjecture: The exact formula is ip(m) = ⌈4√(nm−m²)⌉ for all m ≤ n²/2; s(t) is attained by diagonal cuts, with no other minimizing configuration.

**中文。** 定义：n×n 环面格图的等周剖面指其顶点子集边界的最小值函数。猜想：等周剖面的精确公式为 ip(m)=⌈4√(nm−m²)⌉ 对一切 m≤n²/2;s(t) 由对角线切割达到,无其他极小构型。
