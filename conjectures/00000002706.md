**English.** Definition: Dynamical stability of Zariski resolution: commutation of singularity resolution with map lifting. Conjecture: The stability threshold of lifted maps: after the resolution degree exceeds twice the dimension, the lifted map is dynamically stable, with the criterion the strictly descending chain of images of the exceptional divisor. (resolution lifting stability threshold)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 解消的动力学稳定性：奇点解消与映射提升的交换。猜想：提升映射的稳定性门限：解消次数超过维数两倍后提升映射的动力学稳定且稳定性的判据为例外除数的像的严格下降链。（解消提升稳定门限）
