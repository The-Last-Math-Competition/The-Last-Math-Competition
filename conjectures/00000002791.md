# 00000002791

**English.** Definition: Quasi-reflexivity of James' space: codimension-one reflexivization. Conjecture: The distortion spectrum of quasi-reflexive spaces: the distortion of James' space is infinite, and the scale of the first distortion step is an explicit quadratic function; the distortion of the generalized quasi-reflexive family grows linearly in codimension. (James distortion quadratic law)

**中文。** 定义：James 空间的准自反性：余维一的自反化。猜想：准自反空间的畸变谱：James 空间的畸变为无穷且第一畸变步的尺度为标准的显式二次函数；准自反的推广族的畸变按余维数线性增长。（James 畸变二次律）
