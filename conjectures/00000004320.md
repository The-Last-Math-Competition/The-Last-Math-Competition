**English.** Definition: The odd and even parts of a class group are the 2-part and the odd-prime part. Conjecture: There exist pairs of fields with adjacent discriminants for which the odd and even parts are anticorrelated, with strength measured explicitly by the deviation of the joint distribution of the 3-rank and the 2-rank from the independent model. (anticorrelation of odd-even parts and joint distribution deviation)

**中文。** 定义：类群奇部与偶部指 2 部分与奇素部分。猜想：存在相邻判别式域对使奇偶部呈反相关,强度由 3 秩与 2 秩的联合分布对独立模型的偏离显式度量。（奇偶反相关联合分布偏离）
