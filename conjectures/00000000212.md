# 00000000212

**SQ-12 ★** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: The output of the lucky sieve (a positional sieve, not a divisibility sieve). Conjecture: The limit of the ratio between the proportion of primes among lucky numbers and the natural prime density exists and is an explicit Euler-product constant; the convergence rate of the ratio is N^{−1/2+ε}, with the independence deficit of the sieve concentrated at small lucky numbers. (lucky prime retention rate)

**中文。** 定义：幸运数筛的输出序列（定义同上：位置筛而非整除筛）。猜想：幸运数中素数的比例与自然素数密度之比的极限存在且为显式 Euler 乘积常数；比例的收敛速度为 N^{−1/2+ε} 且筛的独立性亏损集中在小幸运数处。（幸运素数保持率）
