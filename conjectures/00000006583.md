**English.** Definition: Kernel representation with the dimension lower bound of kernel objects. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the lower bound of kernel dimensions and the lower bound of indecomposable kernels is given by dimension; equality holds for the structure sheaf; the tight example of the bound is the diagonal kernel; and the dimension deficit is the semiphantom. (structure-sheaf equality of kernel dimension bounds)
