# 00000007657

**HY4-27 ★** — source: 240_微分算子IV_双曲型.md

**English.** Definition: Fluid-structure coupling as two-phase energy exchange at interfaces. Conjecture: The energy allocation law of the interface is an explicit function of the impedance ratio; and the extreme of allocation is the impedance-mismatch limit. (impedance-ration interface energy allocation)

**中文。** 定义：流固耦合为界面上的双相能量交换。猜想：界面的能量分配律为阻抗比的显式函数；且分配的极端为阻抗失配极限。（界面能量阻抗分配律）
