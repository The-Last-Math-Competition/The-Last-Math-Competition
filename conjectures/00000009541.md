# 00000009541

**English.** Definition: Gaussian channels are characterized by the attenuation-noise triple (K, lambda, mu); whether their classical capacity is attained by Gaussian inputs is the Gaussian optimality question, proven in the single-mode case. Conjecture: The classical capacity under energy constraint of any finite-dimensional Gaussian channel is attained by Gaussian inputs, with optimal covariance the solution of the matrix equation X = KXK^T + lambda + mu E; the improvement from non-Gaussian inputs is always zero — universal rigidity holds. (Gaussian optimality)

**中文。** 定义：高斯信道由衰减-噪声三元组 (K,λ,μ) 刻画；其经典容量是否由高斯输入达到是高斯最优性问题，单模情形已证。猜想：任意有限维高斯信道在能量约束下的经典容量由高斯输入达到，最优协方差为矩阵方程 X=KXK^T+λ+μE 的解；非高斯输入的改进量恒为零，普遍刚性成立。（高斯最优性）
