# 00000009749

**English.** Definition: Stratified statistics of reverse mathematics: the distribution of logical strengths of theorems among the five subsystems. Conjecture: The proportion of natural theorems falling outside the three main subsystems does not exceed five percent; the exceptional theorems form a finite list, each equivalent to an arithmetic incarnation of some well-ordering principle. (reverse mathematics distribution law)

**中文。** 定义：反推数学的分层统计：定理的逻辑强度谱在五大子系统中的分布。猜想：自然数学定理中落在三大子系统之外的例外比例不超过百分之五；例外定理的列表有限且每条等价于某种良序原理的算术化身。（反推数学分布律）
