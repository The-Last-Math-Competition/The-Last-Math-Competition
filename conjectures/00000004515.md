# 00000004515

**English.** Definition: The random Euclidean MST is the minimum spanning tree on iid points of the unit region. Conjecture: In dimension two the expected total weight is a constant times sqrt(n) times the 1/4 power of log n, the exponent 1/4 is exact and determined by the long-edge density formula. (log-power exponent 1/4 of the planar MST)

**中文。** 定义：随机欧氏 MST 指单位区域 iid 点上的最小生成树。猜想：二维情形总权期望为常数乘根号 n 乘对数 n 的 1/4 次幂,指数 1/4 精确且由长边密度公式确定。（二维 MST 对数幂指数 1/4）
