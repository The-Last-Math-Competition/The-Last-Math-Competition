# 00000007948

**English.** Definition: The uniqueness of the kernel of the d-bar dressing method: the kernel support condition on the Cauchy data (u, u-bar) of the inverse problem for implicit solutions of integrable PDEs. Conjecture: The dressing kernel has finite rank if and only if the initial data is of N-soliton type, the rank being exactly the soliton number; and the pole flow of the kernel (t-dynamics of the poles) is the Moser flow of a Hamiltonian system, whose Liouville coordinates are the Zakharov-Shabat scattering data, with symplectic form of Atiyah-Hitchin type (a sum of residues of dlog lambda wedge dlog mu) degenerating at soliton merging points but preserving sign (positivity). (d-bar kernel finite-rank Moser flow)

**中文。** 定义：d-bar 问题的 dressing 方法的核的唯一性：可积 PDE 的隐式解的反问题的 Cauchy 数据 (u,ū) 的核支撑条件。猜想：dressing 核为有限秩当且仅当初值为 N-孤子型；秩恰为孤子数；且核的极点流（极点的 t-动力学）为 Hamilton 系统的 Moser 流，流的 Liouville 坐标为 Zakharov–Shabat 的散射数据，散射数据的辛形式为 Atiyah–Hitchin 型（dlog λ∧dlog μ 的留数和），形式在孤子合并点退化但符号保持（正性）。
