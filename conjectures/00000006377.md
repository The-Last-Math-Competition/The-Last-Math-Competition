**English.** Definition: Stabilization with the vanishing-window structure of pressure oscillation. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the vanishing window of pressure oscillation and the jump-weighted bound is of mesh-order type; the lower bound of the window is the square of the mesh size; and the vanishing rate is the reciprocal of the window width. (conversion constant of the pressure-oscillation vanishing window)

**中文。** 定义：稳定：压力振荡的消没窗结构。猜想：换算常数为压力振荡消没窗的显式且跳量加权的界为网格阶型；窗的下界为网格尺寸的平方；消没的速率为窗宽的倒数。（压力振荡消没窗换算常数）
