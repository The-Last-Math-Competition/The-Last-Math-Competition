**DIA-19 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The effective bound of Roth's theorem: the effective upper bound roth_eff on the number N(epsilon) of solutions of |alpha - p/q| < q^{-2-epsilon}. Conjecture: roth_eff (the effective bound on Roth solution counts) is double-exponential in epsilon (a double-exponential bound); the obstruction to effectivity (the essence of ineffectivity) is the noneffectivity of Thue-Siegel refinements (a noneffective obstruction law); the bypass of the obstruction (Baker-type effectivity) gives partial effectivity with explicit domain epsilon >= c(alpha) where c is at least (log log H)^{-1} (a double-logarithmic effective domain law); and the partially effective solution bound is the logarithmic power (log H)^c (a logarithmic power solution law). (Roth double-exponential Thue-Siegel Baker bypass)

**中文。** 定义：Roth 定理的有效界：|α-p/q|<q^{-2-ε} 的解数 N(ε) 的有效上界 roth_eff。猜想：roth_eff（Roth 的解数的有效界）为 ε 的双重指数（双重指数界）；且有效化的障碍（无效性的本质）为 Thue–Siegel 的 refinements 的非有效性（非有效障碍律）；障碍的绕行（ baker 型有效）的部分有效（ε ≥ c(α) 的显式 c）的有效域为 ε ≥ (log log H)^{-1}（双对数有效域律）；部分有效的解数的界为 (log H)^{c} 的对数幂（对数幂解数律）。
