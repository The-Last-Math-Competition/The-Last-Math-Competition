# 00000006445

**English.** Definition: Symmetric-power lifting is the automorphic lift of representations. Conjecture: The frontier of the complete family tree of lifts is given by functoriality and closes, the combination of the closure being the Langlands group. (Langlands functorial family tree of symmetric powers)

**中文。** 定义：对称幂提升为表示的自守提升。猜想：提升的完全族谱前沿由函子性给出且封闭；且封闭的组合为 Langlands 群。（对称幂 Langlands 函子族谱）
