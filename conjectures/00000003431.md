**English.** Definition: Kolmogorov widths of Banach spaces: the maximal distance from a set to r-dimensional subspaces. Conjecture: The dual extremum of widths: the Singer dual characterization is the extremum of the dual space, with the realization of the extremum extremal function pairs. (Kolmogorov width Singer dual extremum)
