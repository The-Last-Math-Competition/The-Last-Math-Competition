**AA-20 ★** — source: 16_代数与算术对象.md

**English.** Definition: The distribution of prime factors of aⁿ − bⁿ (with a − b = 1). Conjecture: For each pair (a, b) = (m+1, m), m ≥ 1, the largest prime factor of (m+1)ⁿ − mⁿ exceeds n^{2+ε} for infinitely many n (a polynomial-level strengthening beyond Stewart-type lower bounds).

**中文。** 定义：aⁿ − bⁿ(a − b = 1)的素因子分布。猜想：对每对 (a, b) = (m+1, m),m ≥ 1,序列 (m+1)ⁿ − mⁿ 的最大素因子 P(((m+1)ⁿ − mⁿ)) > n^{2+ε} 对无穷多 n(Stewart 型下界之外的多项式级加强)。
