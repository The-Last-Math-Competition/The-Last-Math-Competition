# 00000008505

**English.** Definition: A D-finite function is a power series with polynomial coefficients satisfying a linear differential equation. Conjecture: The asymptotics of the P-recursive coefficients of a D-finite function are combinations of finitely many Frobenius type forms; the number of terms of the combination is the order of the equation; and the asymptotic constants are products of algebraic numbers and Gamma values. (D-finite asymptotic structure)

**中文。** 定义：D-finite 函数指满足线性微分方程的多项式系数幂级数。猜想：D-finite 函数的 P-递归系数的渐进由有限个 Frobenius 型形式组合；组合的项数为方程的阶；渐近常数为代数数与 Gamma 值的积。（D-finite 渐近结构）
