**English.** Definition: D-finite function denotes satisfies polynomial coefficient power series of linear differential equation. Conjecture: of P- recurrence coefficient of D-finite function by finite element Frobenius expression combinatorial; combinatorial number of terms is order; asymptotic constant of equation is algebraic number and product of Gamma. (D-finite asymptotic structure)

**中文。** 定义：D-finite 函数指满足线性微分方程的多项式系数幂级数。猜想：D-finite 函数的 P-递归系数的渐进由有限个 Frobenius 型形式组合；组合的项数为方程的阶；渐近常数为代数数与 Gamma 值的积。（D-finite 渐近结构）
