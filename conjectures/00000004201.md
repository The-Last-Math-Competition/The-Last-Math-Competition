# 00000004201

**GM3-21 ★** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: A CMC embedding is an embedded surface of constant mean curvature, and spectral rigidity means that the Laplace spectrum determines the embedding. Conjecture: The smallest genus for which spectrally rigid CMC surfaces in the sphere correspond one-to-one with their embeddings is 1, realized by the Clifford torus. (minimal genus of spectrally rigid CMC surfaces)

**中文。** 定义：CMC 嵌入指常平均曲率嵌入曲面,谱刚性指 Laplace 谱决定嵌入。猜想：球面中谱刚性 CMC 曲面谱与嵌入一一对应的最小亏格为 1,由克利福德环面实现。（CMC 谱刚最小亏格）
