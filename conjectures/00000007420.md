# 00000007420

**English.** Definition: Super geometric realization with supersymmetric spaces. Conjecture: The realization of supersymmetric spaces holds, and the kernel of the realization is the induction of super flags. (super-flag-induced supersymmetric realization)

**中文。** 定义：超几何实现：超对称空间。猜想：超几何的刚性：超对称空间的实现且现的核为超 flag 的归纳。（超几何超 flag 超对称空间归纳实现刚性核）
