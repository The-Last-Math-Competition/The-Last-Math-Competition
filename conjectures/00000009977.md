# 00000009977

**NCR-07 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Goldie dimension: the maximal length of direct sums of uniform submodules of Noetherian modules. Conjecture: The Goldie dimension of direct sums of uniform modules satisfies a multiplicativity formula: the dimension of a tensor product is the product of the factor dimensions divided by a central torsion correction; the correction is an invariant of the Brauer equivalence class, and the dimension formula is exact in the Azumaya case. (Goldie dimension multiplicativity formula)

**中文。** 定义：Goldie 维数：Noetherian 模的均匀子模直和的最大长度。猜想：均匀模直和的 Goldie 维数满足乘性公式：张量积的维数为两因子维数之积除以中心扭修正；修正项为 Brauer 等价类的不变量且维数公式在 Azumaya 情形精确。（Goldie 维数乘性公式）
