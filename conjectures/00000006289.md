# 00000006289

**English.** Definition: Trace support is the support structure of noncommutative trace maps, the trace formula for involutive algebras. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in trace support and the support for finite groups is the identity conjugacy class; the trace is zero off the support; and the dimension of the support equals the class count, finite. (identity conjugacy-class conversion of trace support)

**中文。** 定义：迹支撑：非交换迹映射的支撑结构（对合代数的迹公式）。猜想：换算常数为迹支撑的显式且有限群的支撑为恒等共轭类；支撑的余集上迹为零；支撑的维数等于类数且类数为有限。（迹支撑恒等共轭类换算）
