# 00000009627

**English.** Definition: The Hausdorff dimension of hyperbolic invariant sets is determined by the Moran-type pressure equation sum e^{-s times distance} = 1. Conjecture: The dimension of a hyperbolic set is the unique solution of the pressure equation, continuous in the Lipschitz structure; the dimension is invariant under topological conjugacy if and only if the conjugacy is bi-Lipschitz, with the topological-metric dichotomy explicitly separated by Henon attractor families. (hyperbolic dimension Moran formula)

**中文。** 定义：双曲不变集的 Hausdorff 维数由 Moran 型压强方程 Σ e^{−s·距离} = 1 决定。猜想：双曲集的维数为压强方程的唯一解且随 Lipschitz 结构连续；维数在拓扑共轭下不变当且仅当共轭为双 Lipschitz，拓扑-度量的二分由 Henon 吸引子族显式分离。（双曲维数 Moran 公式）
