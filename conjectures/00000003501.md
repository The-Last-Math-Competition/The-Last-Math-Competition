# 00000003501

**English.** Definition: Stability of vector bundles: slope stability. Conjecture: The compactification of stable moduli spaces: the compactification of the moduli of stable bundles consists of S-equivalence classes of semistable bundles, with the separation of classes by Jordan–Hölder factors. (semistable S-equivalence JH factor compactification)

**中文。** 定义：向量丛的稳定性：斜率稳定。猜想：稳定模空间的紧化：稳定丛模空间的紧化为半稳定的 S 等价类且类的分离为 Jordan–Hölder 因子。（半稳定 S 等价 JH 因子紧化）
