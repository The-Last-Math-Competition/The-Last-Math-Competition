# 00000000518

**CA-18 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: For graded ideals I ⊂ R and J ⊂ S, the tensor-product ideal is the ideal defined in the quotient of R⊗S by I⊗S + R⊗J, with Betti table recorded bigraded. Conjecture: The Betti table of I⊗S + R⊗J lies in the Minkowski sum of the Boij–Söderberg cones of I and of J, and the inclusion is an equality when I and J are both purely decomposed.

**中文。** 定义：两个分次理想 I⊂R,J⊂S 的张量积理想指 I⊗S + R⊗J ⊂ R⊗S 的商环中定义的理想,其 Betti 表按双分次记录。猜想：I⊗S+R⊗J 的 Betti 表落在 I 与 J 各自的 Boij–Söderberg 锥的 Minkowski 和内,且当 I,J 均为纯分解时该包含为等式。
