# 00000004751

**English.** Definition: The R-transform is the logarithmic version of the generating function of free convolution. Conjecture: The structure constants of its composition are the binomial correction of the Mobius inversion of noncrossing partitions, and the correction table is given explicitly by block structure and is closed. (binomial correction table of R-composition)
