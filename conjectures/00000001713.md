**EG-23 ★** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: The CLT for rotations of bounded-type α refers to the central limit behavior of the ergodic sums. Conjecture: σ_N = c(f)·√(log N) (the logarithmic correction of Denjoy–Koksma); with c(f) of the explicit type (∫f² · var_θ), and the CLT holds in the non-coboundary case.

**中文。** 定义：有界型 α 的旋转的 CLT 指其遍历和的中心极限行为。猜想：σ_N = c(f)·√(log N)(由 Denjoy–Koksma 的 log 修正);c(f) = (∫f²·var_θ) 型显式,CLT 在非 coboundary 情形成立。
