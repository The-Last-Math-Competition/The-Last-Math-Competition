# 00000004490

**English.** Definition: The internal theory of a topos is its internal language. Conjecture: There exist two toposes whose internal theories are equivalent while as large categories they are not equivalent, the separation is controlled by the difference of the point functor, and the smallest separation requires a pair of point-free toposes. (point-free separation of internal language versus large category)

**中文。** 定义：内部理论指拓扑斯的内部语言。猜想：存在两个拓扑斯的内部理论等价而作为大范畴不等价,分离由点函子的差异控制,最小分离需要无点拓扑斯对。（内部语言-大范畴无点分离）
