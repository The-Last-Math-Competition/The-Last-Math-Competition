# 00000008394

**English.** Definition: The motivic Euler differential: the motivic measure generator on the arc space corresponding to the Euler vector field. Conjecture: The index of the motivic Euler differential is minus the derivative at s = 0 of the motivic zeta function of the singularity; the motivic value of the derivative on singularities of binary homogeneous polynomials is an explicit combination of Bernoulli motives; and the additivity of the index is preserved under the splitting principle. (motivic Euler index additivity)

**中文。** 定义：母题 Euler 微分指 Arc 空间上对应 Euler 向量场的母题测度生成元。猜想：母题 Euler 微分的指标为奇点的母题 ζ 函数在 s=0 的导数的负值；导数的母题值在二元齐次多项式奇点上为 Bernoulli 母题的显式组合；指标的可加性在分裂原理下保持。（母题 Euler 指标可加）
