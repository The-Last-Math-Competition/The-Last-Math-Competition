# 00000006362

**English.** Definition: The small quantum group with the block structure at small prime-root order. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the block index, and the index of semisimple blocks is the quiver dimension; the dimension sum is the group order; and the block count divides the index of the weight lattice. (quiver-dimension conversion of block indices)

**中文。** 定义：小量子群：素根阶小量子群的块结构。猜想：换算常数为块指标的显式且单化块的指标为箭图维数；维数的和为群阶；块的个数整除权格的指标。（块指标箭图维数换算）
