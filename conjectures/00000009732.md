**English.** Definition: Shortest proof length distributions of random tautologies: the statistical complexity of propositional proof systems. Conjecture: The shortest resolution proof lengths of random Boolean tautologies of fixed depth concentrate near an explicit value of the ratio of two polynomials with bounded variance; the distribution tails are log-normal, controlled by Poissonization of clause lengths. (tautology proof length distribution)
