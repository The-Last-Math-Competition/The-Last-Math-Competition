# 00000007969

**English.** Definition: The contact domain of spherical codes: the maximal size A(n,theta) of point sets on the unit sphere S^{n-1} at angular distance at least theta (spherical code capacity). Conjecture: For fixed theta in (0,pi), A(n,theta) = 2^{c(theta) n + o(n)} with c(theta) an explicit entropy integral; the leading coefficient of the small-angle expansion of c(theta) as theta tends to 0 agrees with the Yaglom constant of spherical packing; and the measure equidistribution limit of extremal configurations degenerates at theta = pi/2 into two orthogonal measures (a Plank decomposition), while for theta < pi/2 it is an equiangular tight frame with redundancy exactly the floor of 1/cos theta. (spherical code entropy integral)

**中文。** 定义：球码的接触域：单位球面 S^{n-1} 上的角距 ≥θ 的点集的最大规模 A(n,θ)（球码容量）。猜想：对固定 θ∈(0,π)，A(n,θ)=2^{c(θ)n+o(n)}，其中 c(θ) 为显式熵积分；且 c(θ) 在小角 θ→0 的展开的首系数与球面填充的 Yaglom 常数一致；极值构型的测度等分布极限在 θ=π/2 退化为两个正交测度（Plank 分解），在 θ<π/2 为等距紧框架，紧框架的冗余度恰为 1/cos θ 的下取整。
