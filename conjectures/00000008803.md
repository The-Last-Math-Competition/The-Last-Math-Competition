# 00000008803

**English.** Definition: Lower complexity bounds: the Nemirovski-Yudin adversarial argument. Conjecture: The optimality of the adversarial chain (chain instances) is universal (a universality law); the high-dimensional correction of the initial segment is dimension-dependent (a correction law); the correction constant is in d (a constant law); and the lower bound for nonlinear (noneuclidean geometry) problems is metric-dependent (a metric law). (Nemirovski-Yudin chain universality metric)

**中文。** 定义：lower complexity bounds：Nemirovski–Yudin 的对抗论证。猜想：对抗链（链实例）的最优性的普适（普适律）；且高维的（维数依赖的）初始段的修正（修正律）；修正的常数为 d 的（常数律）；非线性（非欧几何的）的下界的度量的（度量律）。
