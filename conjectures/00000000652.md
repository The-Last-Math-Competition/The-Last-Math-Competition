# 00000000652

**English.** Definition: The sum S(K) of the Kauffman bracket coefficients of an alternating knot counts resolutions by their number of co-born circles. Conjecture: Under connected sums K = K₁#K₂, S is exactly multiplicative: S(K₁#K₂) = S(K₁)S(K₂), and multiplicativity fails for alternating factors only in mixed adequate degrees, finitely often.

**中文。** 定义：交替纽结的 Kauffman 括号系数之和即纽结图在同生圆数下的计数和 S(K)。猜想：在纽结的连通和分解 K=K₁#K₂ 下,S 恰为乘性:S(K₁#K₂)=S(K₁)S(K₂),且该乘性对交替因子的失败只出现在混合 adequate 度时,失败次数有限。
