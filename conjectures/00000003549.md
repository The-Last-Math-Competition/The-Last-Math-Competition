**English.** Definition: Ordinal games: the ordinal generalization of Gale–Stewart. Conjecture: The determinacy spectrum of ordinal games: the transitive closure of determinacy is the cofinality of ordinals, with the hierarchy of closure the measurable tower. (ordinal game cofinality measurable tower determinacy spectrum)
