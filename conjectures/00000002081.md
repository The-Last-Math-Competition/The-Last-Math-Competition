# 00000002081

**SY-01 ★** — source: 55_辛几何与镜像对称.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral invariant c([u],H) is the minimax value of Hamilton Floer cohomology. Conjecture: The spectral norm of the Clifford torus in the four-dimensional closed ball is π/2 (the exact value); this is the closed form of the Clifford torus spectral norm.

**中文。** 定义：谱不变量 c([u],H) 指 Hamilton Floer 上同调的极小极大值。猜想：Clifford 环面在四维闭球中的谱范数为 π/2(精确值);该值即 Clifford 环面谱范数的闭式。
