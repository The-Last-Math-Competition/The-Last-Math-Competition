# 00000009704

**English.** Definition: Energy-critical scattering of four-dimensional Yang-Mills equations: global existence and asymptotic freedom of energy-class solutions. Conjecture: Finite-energy solutions of four-dimensional Yang-Mills exist globally and scatter to free fields; the gauge fixing of the scattering is realizable within the Coulomb class, and the decay of conserved energy-momentum is t^{-2}, optimal. (Yang-Mills energy scattering)

**中文。** 定义：四维杨–米尔斯方程的能量临界散射：能量类解的整体存在与渐近自由。猜想：四维 Yang–Mills 的有限能量解整体存在且散射到自由场；散射的规范固定可在 Coulomb 类内实现且能量动量守恒的衰减为 t^{−2} 最优。（Yang-Mills 能量散射）
