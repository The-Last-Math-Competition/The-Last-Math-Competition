**MD3-22 ★** — source: 149_随机过程III_马氏决策.md

**English.** Definition: The bias span is the range of the bias values. Conjecture: It is at most an explicit product of the ergodic diameter and the maximal reward, equality is attained by the one-directional cycle chain, and the inequality cannot be improved. (bias span-diameter product inequality)

**中文。** 定义：偏差跨度指偏差值的极差。猜想：其不超过遍历直径乘最大奖励的显式积,单向圈链达到等号且不等式不可改进。（偏差跨度-直径积不等式）
