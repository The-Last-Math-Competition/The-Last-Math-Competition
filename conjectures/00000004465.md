# 00000004465

**English.** Definition: The GMN invariants are the hypergeometric BPS spectrum, and the KS identity is the Kontsevich-Soibelman wall-crossing formula. Conjecture: The KS identity can be verified to arbitrary order on the Kronecker quiver, and the verification table gives a strict two-valued structure of the BPS spectrum. (Kronecker KS two-valued spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：GMN 不变量指超几何 BPS 谱,KS 恒等式指 Kontsevich–Soibelman 墙穿越公式。猜想：KS 恒等式在 Kronecker 箭图上可验证到任意阶,验证表给出 BPS 谱的严格二值结构。（Kronecker KS 二值谱）
