# 00000003909

**English.** Definition: A phantom homology class is a cohomology class mapped to zero under homological functors, and the phantom category is the subcategory cut out by its vanishing. Conjecture: The phantom category is a thick subcategory whose minimal number of generators equals the number of F-splitting points of the ring, and it is preserved under perfection. (phantom thick generation cardinality)

**中文。** 定义：phantom 同调指在同调函子下映为零的上同调类,phantom 范畴指其消没所成子范畴。猜想：phantom 范畴为 thick 子范畴,最小生成基数为环的 F-分裂点个数,且在完美化下保持。（phantom thick 生成基数）
