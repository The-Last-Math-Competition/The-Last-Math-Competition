# 00000005900

**PH2-10 △** — source: 182_持久同调II.md

**English.** Definition: One-parameter barcodes and two-parameter completions are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two filtrations whose barcodes are within bottleneck distance epsilon but whose two-parameter completions cannot be epsilon-matched, and the separation is realized by an explicit module pair with indecomposable entanglement. (near-barcode non-matchable two-parameter separation)

**中文。** 定义：单参数条形码与双参数补全指两层。猜想：存在两过滤的条形码在瓶颈距离 ε 内而双参数补全不可 ε 匹配,分离由不可分解纠缠的显式模对实现。（条形码近-双参数不可匹配分离）
