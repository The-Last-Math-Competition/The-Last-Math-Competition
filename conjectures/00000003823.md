# 00000003823

**RC-03 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: The string cone C_g denotes the cone of string parametrizations of a finite simple g under a fixed reduced word, and m(g) the number of irreducible generators of its integral-points semigroup. Conjecture: m(g) is independent of the reduced word, and m(C_n) = m(D_{n+1}) for all n ≥ 3. (string generator duality equality)

**中文。** 定义：字符串锥 C_g 指有限单型 g 在取定简约字下的字符串路线锥,m(g) 记其整点半群的不可约生成元个数。猜想：m(g) 不依赖简约字的选取,且对一切 n ≥ 3 有 m(C_n) = m(D_{n+1})。（字符串生成元对偶相等）
