**EG3-06 ★** — source: 194_遍历论III_熵公式.md

**English.** Definition: Semicontinuity is the semicontinuity of the entropy functional with the recovery genus, the genus version of the structure theorem. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the recovery genus and zero-dimensional systems are counted by split points; the generating function of the count is rational with poles at roots of unity; and the lower bound of the genus is one, attained by double-split points. (split-point counting conversion of recovery genus)

**中文。** 定义：半连续：熵泛函的半连续性与恢复亏格（结构定理的亏格版）。猜想：换算常数为恢复亏格的显式且零维系统由分裂点计数；计数的母函数为有理且极点为单位根；亏格的下界为一且下界由双分裂点达到。（恢复亏格分裂点计数换算）
