# 00000000918

**English.** Definition: Quasi-traces are subadditive weakly-continuous trace functionals. Conjecture: The quasi-trace space of a nuclear algebra is a simplex whose extreme points are factor traces; the simplex has dimension one and a closed extreme-point set. (nuclear algebra quasi-trace simplex)

**中文。** 定义：拟迹为次可加弱连续的迹泛函。猜想：核代数的拟迹空间为单形且其端点为因子迹；且单形性的维数为一且端点集为闭。（核代数拟迹单形）
