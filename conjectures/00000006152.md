**English.** Definition: Point automorphisms of a stack give the stabilizer-group structure of classifying stacks. Conjecture: For classifying stacks the point-automorphism spectrum is the shifted group cohomology, the combination of the cohomology being the weight decomposition; the weight difference of the decomposition is the shift number; and the order of the automorphism group at uniform weight is the base ring of the hypercohomology, a Galois ring. (shifted-cohomology weights of point automorphisms)

**中文。** 定义：叠的点自同构：分类叠的稳定群结构。猜想：分类叠时点自同构谱为移位群上同调且上同调的组合为权分解；分解的权差为移位数；自同构群的阶在权一致时为超上同调的基环且基环为伽罗瓦环。（点自同构移位上同调权）
