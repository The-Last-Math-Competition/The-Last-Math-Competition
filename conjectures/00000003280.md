# 00000003280

**English.** Definition: The Lusternik–Schnirelmann category: the lower bound of critical point counts of manifolds. Conjecture: The immersion upper bound of LS category: the LS category of a manifold is at most the codimension plus one, with the tightness of the bound realized by torus families. (LS category codimension tight upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：Lusternik–Schnirelmann 范畴：流形的临界点计数下界。猜想：LS 范畴的浸入上界：流形的 LS 范畴不超过余维数加一且界的紧性为环面族实现。（LS 范畴余维紧上界）
