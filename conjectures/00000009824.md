# 00000009824

**English.** Definition: Orlicz bump conditions: log-convexified conditions beyond Muckenhoupt controlling weighted inequalities. Conjecture: The entropy integral threshold of bump conditions is the exact sufficient-necessary boundary: finiteness of the entropy integral is sufficient, and any bump below the threshold admits counterexamples; counterexample weights are constructed from nested covers of dyadic trees. (entropy bump threshold boundary)

**中文。** 定义：Orlicz bump 条件：超过 Muckenhoupt 的对数凸化条件控制加权不等式。猜想：bump 条件的熵积分阈值为充分必要的精确分界：熵积分有限为充分且任何低于阈值的 bump 均存在反例；反例权的构造基于二进树的嵌套覆盖。（熵 bump 阈值分界）
