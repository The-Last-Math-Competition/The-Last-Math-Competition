# 00000008740

**MBL-30 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: Annealed and quenched ensembles: the two averaging types for self-averaging of random systems. Conjecture: The two ensembles are always equivalent in high dimension; the low-dimensional breaking is by the symmetry-breaking mechanism with the gap vanishing near criticality; and the breaking threshold of many-body self-averaging is dimension two. (annealed-quenched equivalence threshold dimension two)

**中文。** 定义：退火与淬火系综指随机系统自平均的两类平均。猜想：高维下两系综恒等价；低维的破缺由对称破缺机制给出且间隙在近临界消失，多体自平均的破缺阈为维度二。（退火-淬火等价阈二维）
