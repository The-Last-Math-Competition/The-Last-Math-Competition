# 00000001589

**IS-19 ◆** — source: 38_可积系统与精确可解模型.md

**English.** Definition: Completeness of the Bethe ansatz means the spectrum of the sl₂ spin chain is entirely given by Bethe solutions; the M-string hypothesis organizes singular strings. Conjecture: M-string completeness holds; the smallest counterexample (if any), for chain length N > M = 6, has system-size lower bound 2^{20} — no counterexample exists within finite systems.

**中文。** 定义：Bethe ansatz 的完备性指 sl₂ 自旋链的谱完全由 Bethe 解给出;M-弦假设给出奇异串的组织规则。猜想：M-弦完备性成立;最小反例(若存在)其链长 N > M=6 对应的系统尺寸下界为 2^{20},即有限系统内无反例。
