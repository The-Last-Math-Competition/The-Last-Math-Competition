# 00000008101

**English.** Definition: The vanishing line of the Adams spectral sequence of a fusion system: the slope van(F) of the vanishing line on the E_2 page of the Adams spectral sequence of the cohomology of F (mod p coefficients). Conjecture: The slope of van(F) is 1/(p-1) or 1/(2(p+1)) (a two-valued slope law); the switch of slopes agrees with the rank of the periodic carrier P_per of F (FUS-11, a period-slope correspondence); and the count of signals on the E_2 page (elementary symmetric classes with nonvanishing) equals the minimal atomic coefficients of F (a signal-atom count). (Adams vanishing two-slope period correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 Adams 谱序列的 vanishing 线：F 的上同调（mod p 系数）的 Adams 谱序列的 E₂ 页的 vanishing 线的斜率 van(F)。猜想：van(F) 的斜率为 1/(p-1) 或 1/(2(p+1))（斜率二值律）；且斜率的切换与 F 的周期承载（FUS-11 的 P_per）的秩一致（周期-斜率对应）；E₂ 页的信号（非 vanishing 的初等对称类）的计数 = F 的极小原子系数（信号-原子计数）。
