**English.** Definition: The intermittency constant measures the deviation from scaling laws. Conjecture: The second-order scaling deviation is an explicit algebraic value self-consistent with the four-fifths law, given by the matching of multilayer spectra and consistent with measured intervals. (algebraic self-consistent value of second-order intermittency)

**中文。** 定义：间歇常数指标度律的偏离参数。猜想：二阶标度偏差为四五律自洽的显式代数值,由多层谱的匹配给出且与实测区间一致。（二阶间歇代数自洽值）
