# 00000000492

**English.** Definition: A random version of the hook length formula. Conjecture: The expectation of the multiset of hook lengths of a uniformly random standard tableau equals (Σ hooks)/n², explicitly (n²−1)/3 (a closed-form conjecture, numerically verifiable).

**中文。** 定义：hook length formula 的随机版本。猜想：均匀随机标准表的钩长多重集的期望 = (Σ hooks)/n² 显式为 (n²−1)/3(数值可验证的闭式猜想)。
