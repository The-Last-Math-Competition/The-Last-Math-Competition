# 00000006860

**English.** Definition: Rectangular free convolution with the rectangular version. Conjecture: The rectangular free convolution holds, the combination of the convolution being singular values. (singular-value rectangular free convolution)

**中文。** 定义：矩形自由卷积：矩形版本。猜想：矩形的卷积：矩形的自由卷积且积的组合为奇异值。（矩形自由卷积奇异值矩形自由卷积组合矩形卷积奇异值卷积矩形奇异值）
