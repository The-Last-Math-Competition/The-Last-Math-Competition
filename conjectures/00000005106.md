**English.** Definition: Quantum categorical deformation is the parameter-dependent family of categories, the braided deformation of representation categories. Conjecture: The deformation is explicitly indexed by modified braiding elements, with the index injective in the parameter; at dual parameters the braiding is self-composed with composition constant 1; when the parameter is a root of unity the deformation degenerates to finite type, with the number of types equal to the order of the root. (self-composed braiding of quantum deformations)

**中文。** 定义：量子范畴形变：参数依赖的范畴族（表示范畴的编织形变）。猜想：形变由修饰编织元显式索引且索引为参数的单射；对偶参数处编织自合且合成常数为 1；参数为单位根时形变退化为有限型且型数等于根的阶。（量子形变编织元自合）
