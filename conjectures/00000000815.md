# 00000000815

**English.** Definition: The nodal length L(λ) of an eigenfunction φ_λ is the Hausdorff measure of its zero set. Conjecture: On compact analytic manifolds, the lower bound L(λ) ≥ c·λ^{1/2}·vol improves to L(λ) ≥ c·λ^{1/2}·(1+ε) when a spectral gap exists, with ε explicitly related to the gap; the optimal-constant direction is dual to the Dong–Zelditch upper bound.

**中文。** 定义：特征函数 φ_λ 的节线长度 L(λ) 指其零集的 Hausdorff 测度。猜想：在紧解析流形上 L(λ) ≥ c·λ^{1/2}·vol 的下界可改进为 L(λ) ≥ c·λ^{1/2}·(1+ε),其中谱隙存在时 ε>0 与间隙显式相关;密度的最优常数方向由 Dong–Zelditch 上界对偶确定。
