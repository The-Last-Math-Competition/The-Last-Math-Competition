# 00000009654

**HSS-14 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The asymptotic law of the maximal gap G_n of the sine process over long intervals. Conjecture: n G_n - log n converges to an explicit Gumbel-type limit distribution; the joint distribution of the k largest gaps is given by an explicit intensity functional of a Poisson point process, consistent with the extreme value theory of log-correlated fields. (maximal gap Gumbel law)

**中文。** 定义：sine 过程在长区间上的最大间隙 G_n 的渐近律。猜想：最大间隙满足 nG_n − log n 收敛到 Gumbel 型显式极限分布；前 k 大间隙的联合分布为 Poisson 点过程的显式强度泛函，与对数相关场的极值理论一致。（最大间隙 Gumbel 律）
