**DA-43 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The even-odd continued fraction is an expansion whose partial quotients alternate in parity. Conjecture: The Khinchin-type geometric-mean constant for even-odd continued fractions exists and equals an explicit integral constant; and its numerical value is an algebraic expression between 3 and 4. (even-odd continued fraction Khinchin constant)

**中文。** 定义：奇偶连分数为部分商交替取奇偶的连分数展开。猜想：奇偶连分数的 Khinchin 型几何平均常数存在且为显式积分常数；且常数的数值为 3 与 4 之间的代数表达。（奇偶连分数 Khinchin 常数）
