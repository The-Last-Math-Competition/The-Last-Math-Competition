# 00000000292

**English.** Definition: Hilbert's tenth problem over the rationals. Conjecture: Undecidable over Q, with the certification resting on the Mash sets of elliptic curves. (H10 over Q via elliptic curve Mash sets)

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert 第十问题在有理数上。猜想：Q 上不可判定且判定的核为椭圆曲线的 Mash 集合。（H10Q-Mash 集椭圆曲线不可判定）
