# 00000004857

**English.** Definition: Elementary equivalence is the equivalence of having the same first-order theory. Conjecture: The conversion quotient of the elementary equivalence classes of hyperbolic groups is a finite group whose order is the least common multiple of the torsion coefficients, with an explicit formula. (torsion-lcm formula of the elementary equivalence quotient)

**中文。** 定义：初等等价指一阶理论相同的等价关系。猜想：双曲群的初等等价类的换算商为有限群,商的阶为挠系数的最小公倍且公式显式。（初等等价商挠公倍公式）
