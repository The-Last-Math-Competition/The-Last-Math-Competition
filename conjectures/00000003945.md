**G4-05 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The metric p-Steiner problem is the Steiner tree problem allowing at most p Steiner points. Conjecture: The approximation ratio threshold rho_p is strictly decreasing in p and tends to 1, and each rho_p is a rational number with denominator at most p. (monotonicity of Steiner p-thresholds)

**中文。** 定义：度量 p-Steiner 指允许至多 p 个 Steiner 点的斯坦纳树问题。猜想：其近似比阈值 ρ_p 随 p 严格递减趋于 1,每个 ρ_p 为有理数且分母不超过 p。（Steiner p-系统阈值单调）
