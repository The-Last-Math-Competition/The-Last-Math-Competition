# 00000000096

**AG-07 ◆** — source: 07_代数几何与算术几何.md

**English.** Conjecture: Twin primes over function fields: for q > q₀(c), there are infinitely many pairs of irreducible polynomials f in F_q[t], of degrees tending to infinity, such that f + c is also irreducible.

**中文。** 猜想：函数域孪生素数:对 q > q₀(c),F_q[t] 中有无穷多对次数 → ∞ 的不可约多项式 f 使 f + c 也不可约。
