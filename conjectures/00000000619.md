# 00000000619

**KT-19 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: Rasmussen's s-invariant gives the smooth 4-genus lower bound g₄(K) ≥ s(K)/2. Conjecture: There exists a family of knots K_n with g₄(K_n)/(s(K_n)/2) → 1 but never equal to 1, and the gap g₄ − s/2 grows unboundedly on this family; optimality of s fails universally in the strict sense.

**中文。** 定义：Rasmussen s-不变量给出光滑 4-亏格下界 g₄(K) ≥ s(K)/2。猜想：存在纽结族 K_n 使 g₄(K_n)/(s(K_n)/2) → 1 但恒不等于 1,且差距 g₄−s/2 在该族上无界增长;s 的最优性在严格意义下普遍失败。
