**TR3-09 ★** — source: 137_数论V_超越方法.md

**English.** Definition: Pade approximation is the system of exact rational approximations of a function, and the Hermite identity is its determinant relation. Conjecture: The conversion constant of diagonal Pade is an explicit ratio of Hankel determinants, and for the exponential function this ratio is an integer factorial. (Pade-Hankel factorial conversion)

**中文。** 定义：Padé 逼近指函数的精确有理逼近系,Hermite 恒等式指其行列式关系。猜想：对角 Padé 的换算常数为 Hankel 行列式的显式比,在指数函数情形该比为整数阶乘。（Padé-Hankel 阶乘换算）
