# 00000001471

**CX-21 △** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The self-concordant barrier parameter. Conjecture: The only cones in Rⁿ whose optimal barrier parameter is exactly n are the symmetric cones (a classification of optimal parameters); and the exceptional cones are exactly the homogeneous triangulations of polyhedral cones.

**中文。** 定义：自和谐障碍(self-concordant barrier)参数。猜想：ℝⁿ 中锥的最优障碍参数恰为 n 的锥仅有对称锥(最优参数分类)；且例外锥恰为多面体锥的齐次三角化。
