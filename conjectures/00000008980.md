# 00000008980

**ISO-30 △** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: Integrability breaking: nonintegrability after perturbation. Conjecture: There exist explicit perturbation families whose algebraic entropy approaches log 2 arbitrarily closely but never attains it. (entropy log-2 approaching family)

**中文。** 定义：可积性破坏指扰动后非可积。猜想：存在显式扰动族使代数熵任意接近 log 2 但从不达到。（熵逼近 log2 族）
