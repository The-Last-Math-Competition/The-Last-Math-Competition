# 00000007776

**English.** Definition: Orbit-equivalence rigidity: two polynomials f, g of degree at least 2 are orbit equivalent if a bijection of orbits preserves orbit relations and both directions are restrictions of polynomial maps in the Zariski topology. Conjecture: Orbit equivalence implies f and g are linearly conjugate, with exceptions only in combinations of automorphisms of the Chebyshev and monomial families; the parameters where rigidity fails form an algebraic subset of the two-parameter space with Zariski-open complement. (orbit-equivalence rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：轨道等价刚性：两个次数 ≥2 的多项式 f,g 称轨道等价，若存在双射 φ:Orb_f(x)→Orb_g(y) 保持轨道关系且 φ 与 φ^{-1} 在 Zariski 拓扑下同构于多项式映射的限制。猜想：轨道等价蕴含 f 与 g 线性共轭，例外仅出现在 Chebyshev 与单项式族的组合自同构；刚性失败的参数在二元参数空间中构成代数子集，其补集为 Zariski 开。
