# 00000002254

**English.** Definition: The dilation lemma for quasi-normal families sharing small functions. Conjecture: The nonlinear version of the dilation exponent always has an exact value; and the value is controlled by the Nevanlinna characteristic of the shared functions. (PZ nonlinear exponent)

**中文。** 定义：quasi-normal 族共享小函数的伸缩引理。猜想：非线性版本的伸缩指数恒有精确值；且值由分担函数的 Nevanlinna 特征控制。（PZ 非线性指数）
