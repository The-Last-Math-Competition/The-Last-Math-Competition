# 00000002840

**AS-10 ★** — source: 80_代数统计与张量分解的几何.md

**English.** Definition: The algebraic degree of maximum likelihood: the algebraic count of ML estimation solutions. Conjecture: The recursion of ML degrees: the ML degree of hidden Markov chains is an explicit combination of path polynomials of the transition graph, superlinear in the model dimension but below double exponential. (HMM maximum likelihood degree)

**中文。** 定义：极大似然的代数度：ML 估计的解的代数计数。猜想：ML 度的递推：隐马尔可夫链的 ML 度为转移图的路多项式的显式组合量且度为模型维数的超线性但不超过双指数。（HMM 极大似然度）
