# 00000007917

**ATC-17 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The AC envelope of Langlands parameters: the member count |A_phi| of the Arthur packet A_phi (the automorphic envelope of a parameter phi). Conjecture: |A_phi| is the integer part of half the product over i of dim sigma_i of the representation dimensions of the Arthur SL_2(C) part of phi; the product of Hecke eigenvalue signs over members of the packet is always +1 (sign law); and the distribution of the packet on the spectral side of the trace formula is weighted by the Plancherel measure of irreducible characters of the central extension, with zero weight for non-Plancherel spectrum (non-stable packet members). (Arthur packet count and sign law)

**中文。** 定义：Langlands 参数的 AC 包络：Arthur 包 A_φ（参数 φ 的自守包络）的成员计数 |A_φ|。猜想：|A_φ| 为 φ 的 Arthur SL₂(C) 部分的表示维数的乘积 Π dim σ_i 的一半取整；且包内成员的 Hecke 特征符号乘积恒为（+1）（符号律）；包的分布在迹公式的谱侧的权重为中心扩张的不可约特征标的 Plancherel 测度，非 Plancherel 谱（非稳定包成员）的权重为零。
