**English.** Definition: The Camassa-Holm equation: the peakon soliton system. Conjecture: The N-peakon solution is equivalent to a finite-rank Jacobi matrix Toda flow, with collision scattering as eigenvalue insertion. (Camassa-Holm Jacobi Toda equivalence)
