# 00000003977

**English.** Definition: The r-th round of Sherali-Adams is the round-by-round lifting of the linear programming relaxation. Conjecture: SA_r simulates width-w resolution proofs if and only if r >= w - 2, and the constant 2 is proven optimal by an explicit family of sparse formulas. (SA-width simulation constant)

**中文。** 定义：Sherali–Adams 第 r 轮指线性规划的逐轮提升。猜想：SA_r 模拟宽度 w 的消解证明当且仅当 r ≥ w − 2,且常数 2 由显式稀疏公式族证明最优。（SA-宽度模拟常数）
