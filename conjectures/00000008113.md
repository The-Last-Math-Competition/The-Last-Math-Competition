# 00000008113

**NAG-03 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The noncommutative divisor theory of function fields of noncommutative curves (qgr dimension 1): the divisor class group Cl_nc of an nc curve. Conjecture: Cl_nc is a finitely generated abelian group with rank equal to the transcendence degree of the center field (a central synchronization law); the order of the torsion part is at most h^2 (h the hereditary rank), with equality realized by the weights of the tube category (a tube weight law); and the correction term of noncommutative Riemann-Roch (the chi correction) is a quadratic form on the centralized index (a chi quadratic-form correction). (nc curve divisor central rank law)

**中文。** 定义：非交换曲线（qgr 维数 1）的函数域的非交换除子理论：nc 曲线的 divisor class 群 Cl_nc。猜想：Cl_nc 为有限生成交换群且秩 = 中心域的超越次数（中心同步律）；且扭部分的阶 ≤ h²（h 为 hereditary 秩），等号由 tube 范畴的权重实现（tube 权重律）；非交换 Riemann–Roch 的修正项（χ 修正）为中心化的指标的二次型（χ-二次型修正）。
