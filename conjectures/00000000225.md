# 00000000225

**English.** Definition: Fermat numbers F_n = 2^{2ⁿ}+1 are pairwise coprime. Conjecture: The largest prime factor of F_n satisfies P(F_n)/2^{n+2} → ∞ along an infinite subsequence; the divergent subsequence has positive density, given by a Bateman–Horn correction for the distribution of primes k among the Lucas-type prime divisors k·2^{n+2}+1. (Fermat large prime factor divergence)

**中文。** 定义：Fermat 数 F_n=2^{2ⁿ}+1 两两互素。猜想：F_n 的最大素因子满足 P(F_n)/2^{n+2} 沿无穷子列发散；发散子列的密度为正且由 Lucas 型素因子约束 k·2^{n+2}+1 中 k 的素数分布的 Bateman–Horn 修正给出。（Fermat 大素因子发散）
