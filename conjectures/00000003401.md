**English.** Definition: Mixing of Markov chains: convergence from the initial to the stationary distribution. Conjecture: The spectral characterization of truncated mixing times: the mixing time of lazy chains is the logarithmic correction of the inverse spectral gap, with the correction term the logarithm of the minimal stationary probability. (truncated mixing spectral gap logarithmic correction)
