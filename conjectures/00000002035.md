# 00000002035

**English.** Conjecture: The decoupling of μ(p(n)) (the Möbius function composed with the prime-counting function) is o(π(x)); the squared correlation of this composite also vanishes (double independence of combinatorial and prime exponents).

**中文。** 猜想：μ(p(n))(素数计数函数的复合)与 Möbius 函数的解耦为 o(π(x));该复合的平方相关亦消没(组合指数与素数指数的双重独立性)。
