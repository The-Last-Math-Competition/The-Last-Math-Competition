# 00000001325

**CC-25 ◆** — source: 29_多复变与复几何.md

**English.** Definition: Geodesics between uniform Svěrak norms on the Kähler potential space ℋ are given by Chen's weak solutions. Conjecture: (i) These geodesics are C^{1,1} (a famous open problem); (ii) the geodesic between a pair of polynomial potentials can be approximated by an explicit algebraic sequence at exactly the rate O(ε²).

**中文。** 定义：Kähler 位势空间 ℋ 上的一致 Svěrak 范数间的测地线由 Chen 的弱解给出。猜想：(i) 该测地线为 C^{1,1}(著名开放);(ii) 多项式位势对的测地线可被显式的代数近似序列逼近,逼近速率恰为 O(ε²)。
