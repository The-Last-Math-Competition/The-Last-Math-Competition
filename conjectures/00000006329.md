# 00000006329

**English.** Definition: Totally positive matrices have all minors positive, with the minor spectral structure. Conjecture: The minor spectral lattice of totally positive matrices closes, the kernel of the closure being the minimal generation of the totally positive lattice; the minimal generators are elementary Jacobi matrices; and the multiplicativity of the spectrum is preserved under triangular decomposition, uniquely. (closed totally positive minor spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：全正矩阵：一切子式为正的矩阵；子式谱结构。猜想：全正矩阵的子式谱格封闭且封闭的核恒为全正格的极小生成；极小生成为初等 Jacobi 矩阵；谱的乘性在三角分解下保持且保持为唯一。（全正子式谱全正格封闭）
