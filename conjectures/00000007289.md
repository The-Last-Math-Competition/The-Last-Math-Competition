# 00000007289

**SF4-19 ★** — source: 228_对称函数III.md

**English.** Definition: Glaisher's theorem with the odd-even splits of partitions. Conjecture: The equal counting of odd parts and distinct parts holds, and the combination of the counting is the merging map. (merging-map Glaisher equinumerosity)

**中文。** 定义：Glaisher 定理：分拆的奇偶拆分。猜想：Glaisher 的广义：奇部与重复部的等计数且数的组合为合并映射。（Glaisher 合并映射奇部重复部等计数广义组合）
