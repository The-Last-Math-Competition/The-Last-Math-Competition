**English.** Definition: eigenvalue of eigenvalue inequality: AB of product operator by A and singular value joint controls of B. Conjecture: optimal joint bound of product operator eigenvalue module is optimal of intersection convolution; bound of two factor singular value by direct sum of finite dimension weighted place attains and convolution kernel unique. (product eigenvalue convolution bound)
