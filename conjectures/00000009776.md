# 00000009776

**OIE-16 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Eigenvalue inequalities for product operators: eigenvalues of AB jointly controlled by the singular values of A and B. Conjecture: The optimal joint bound for eigenvalue moduli of products is the alternating convolution of the singular values of the two factors; optimality is attained by direct sums of finite-dimensional weighted shifts, and the convolution kernel is unique. (product eigenvalue convolution bound)

**中文。** 定义：乘积算子的特征值不等式：AB 的特征值由 A 与 B 的奇异值联合控制。猜想：乘积算子特征值模的最优联合界为两因子奇异值的交错卷积；界的最优性由有限维加权移位的直和达到且卷积核唯一。（乘积特征值卷积界）
