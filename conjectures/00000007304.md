# 00000007304

**English.** Definition: Tensor detection of low-rank signals. Conjecture: The detection threshold of the signal-bearing tensor model is the critical noise, and the constant of the noise is the spectral-gap equation. (spectral-gap-equation signal detection threshold)

**中文。** 定义：低秩信号的张量检测。猜想：检测阈值：带信号的张量模型的检测阈为临界噪声且声的常数为谱隙方程。（带信号张量谱隙方程检测阈值临界噪声常数）
