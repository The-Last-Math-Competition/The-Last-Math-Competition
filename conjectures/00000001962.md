# 00000001962

**MP-02 ◆** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: The triviality of 4D φ⁴ means the scaling limit is the Gaussian free field. Conjecture: Triviality holds; and the divergence exponent of the renormalized susceptibility on the 4D φ⁴ lattice is at least 2+ε (with an explicit ε > 0 formula); the two together give a quantitative version of triviality.

**中文。** 定义：4D φ⁴ 的平凡性指标度极限为高斯自由场。猜想：平凡性成立;且 4D φ⁴ 格上的重整磁化率的发散指数 ≥2+ε(ε>0 公式);两项合并给出平凡性的定量版本。
