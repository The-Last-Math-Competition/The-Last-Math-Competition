# 00000007833

**English.** Definition: The Severi degree spectrum: the degrees deg V_delta of the Severi varieties V_{delta,L} (nodal curve loci) of genus delta in a linear system |L|. Conjecture: The generating series of deg V_delta (delta fixed, L of growing degree) has elementary part a hyperbola count of type S_d f(delta,d) with S_d the Schubert central number; the correction to f is (-1)^delta/delta! (H_d - P_d) with H and P the harmonic numbers and power sums; and the result returns the (-1) correction tropically dual to Gromov-Witten disk invariants. (Severi degree harmonic correction)

**中文。** 定义：Severi 度量密度谱：|L| 中亏格 δ 的 Severi 度 V_{δ,L}（节点曲线轨迹）的次数 deg V_δ。猜想：deg V_δ 的生成级数（δ 固定，L 次数→∞）的初等项为 S_d·f(δ,d)（S_d 为 Schubert 中心数）型双曲线计数；f 的修正项为 (-1)^δ/δ!·(H_d−P_d)（H,P 为调和数与幂和），且回归到 Gromov–Witten 盘不变量的 tropically 对偶的 (-1) 修正。
