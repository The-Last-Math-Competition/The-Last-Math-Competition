# 00000007334

**English.** Definition: Hyperfinite sums with the nonstandard realization of infinite sums. Conjecture: The infinitesimal regularization of divergent series holds, and the sum of the regularization is the natural continuation. (naturally-continued Euler regularization)

**中文。** 定义：超有限和：无穷和的非标准实现。猜想：超有限的 Euler：发散级数的无穷小正则化且化的和为自然延拓。（超有限 Euler 自然延拓发散级数无穷小正则化和）
