**ERO-18 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: The equivalence of the definitions of Bowen topological entropy (spanning sets, separated sets, generating partitions). Conjecture: All equivalent definitions give the same value on compact systems (a uniformity law); the correction factor for noncompact systems is the boundary entropy (a boundary factor law); the uniform convergence rate of the finite-scale value spectra of the definitions is 1/n (a harmonic decay law); and the optimality of the decay is attained by full periodic approximation of isometric systems (a full periodic matching law). (Bowen entropy definitional uniformity decay)

**中文。** 定义：Bowen 拓扑熵的等价定义（张成集、分离集、生成划分）的一致性。猜想：一切等价定义在紧系统上给出同值（一致律）；非紧系统的修正因子为边界熵（边界因子律）；各定义的有限尺度值的谱的一致收敛速率为 1/n（调和衰减律）；衰减的最优性由等距系统的满周期逼近取得（满周期匹配律）。
