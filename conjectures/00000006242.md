# 00000006242

**GT5-22 ★** — source: 193_几何范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Classifiers with the torsion of universal-object tangent spaces. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit torsion of the universal-object tangent space and deformation directions are given by the shift operator; the order of the torsion divides the weight of the classifier, the weight finite; and the spectrum of the shift operator is real, with maximal element the deformation dimension. (universal tangent torsion shift conversion)

**中文。** 定义：分类子：万有对象的分类结构；切空间的挠率。猜想：换算常数为万有对象切空间的显式挠率且形变方向由移动算给出；挠率的阶整除分类子的权且权为有限；移动算的谱为实数且谱的极大元为形变的维数。（万有切挠移动系数换算）
