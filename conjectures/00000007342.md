# 00000007342

**English.** Definition: Nonstandard probability with probability measures of events. Conjecture: The infinitesimal deviation of independence holds, and the order of the deviation is the hyperfinite ratio. (hyperfinite-ratio nonstandard independence)

**中文。** 定义：非标准概率：事件的概率测度。猜想：非标准的独立：事件的独立性的无穷小偏差且差的阶为超有限比例。（非标准独立超有限比例事件概率无穷小偏差阶）
