**English.** Definition: The spectral gap gamma_n(p) of the lazy random walk on G(n,p): the gap of the normalized Laplacian. Conjecture: gamma_n(p) = Theta(max(1 - lambda_2(G)/np, np^{-1})) concentrates at its mean with high probability when p is much larger than log n/n; and the concentration width is n^{-1} (superconcentration), whose proof barrier is fully equivalent to the 3/2-power rigidity of the second eigenvalue of GOE (the rigidity separation from Tracy-Widom n^{-2/3} fluctuations). (G(n,p) walk gap superconcentration)

**中文。** 定义：非一致随机图上的随机游走谱隙：G(n,p) 上懒惰随机游走的规范化 Laplacian 谱隙 γ_n(p)。猜想：γ_n(p) = Θ(max(1-λ₂(G)/np, np^{-1})) 在 p≫log n/n 时依高概率集中于均值；且集中宽度为 n^{-1}（超集中），其证明障碍与 GOE 的第二特征值刚性的 3/2-幂律（Tracy–Widom 波动 n^{-2/3} 的刚性分离）完全等价。
