# 00000002456

**English.** Definition: The Tukey classes of elementary chains of ℵ_1. Conjecture: Their count is an explicit function of ℵ_1 (concretely f(α) = |α+ω|); the count is by the enumeration of cofinal subsets of subchains.

**中文。** 定义：ℵ_1 的初等链的 Tukey 类。猜想：其计数为 ℵ_1 的显式函数(具体 f(α)=|α+ω|);计数由链的子链的 cofinal 子集的枚举。
