# 00000007904

**English.** Definition: Subconvexity breaking for L-functions: the breaking amount delta_m below the convexity bound m/4 in the t-direction for the standard L-function L(s,pi) of a cusp representation pi of GL_m (L(1/2+it,pi) <= Q(pi)^{m/4-delta_m+epsilon}). Conjecture: Uniform breaking gives delta_m >= 1/(2m+4); and with t fixed and the village parameter (the slope of the representation) varying, the optimal delta_m is exactly 1/(2m+4), attained asymptotically at the position of extremal Maass forms (the gap endpoint of the Laplace spectrum). (uniform subconvex breaking 1/(2m+4))

**中文。** 定义：L 函数的亚凸性破缺：标准 L 函数 L(s,π)（π 为 GL_m 的尖点表示，解析亏格 g_an）在 t-方向的凸性界 m/4 的破缺量 δ_m（L(1/2+it,π) ≤ Q(π)^{m/4-δ_m+ε}）。猜想：一致破缺 δ_m ≥ 1/(2m+4)；且在 t 固定、village 参数（表示的斜率）变化时最优 δ_m 恰为 1/(2m+4)，由极端 Maass 形式的位置（Laplace 谱的间隙端点）渐近取得。
