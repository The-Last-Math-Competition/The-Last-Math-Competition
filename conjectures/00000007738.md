# 00000007738

**FRV-18 △** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The convergence of Dyson Brownian motion eigenvalue measures to the semicircle law in Wasserstein-2 distance is known to be O(n^{-1}) after smoothing. Conjecture: Without smoothing, for initial data without atom mass, W_2(mu_t^emp, mu_t^sc) <= C(t) n^{-1} with C(t) converging as t grows to the constant C_inf = sqrt 2 over pi; and C_inf is jointly given by the Lipschitz constant of the semicircular transport potential and the rigidity lower bound from eigenvalue repulsion with beta=2. (unsmoothed Dyson convergence constant)

**中文。** 定义：Dyson Brown 运动（Wigner 矩阵的 OU 流）特征值测度在 Wasserstein-2 距离下到半圆律的收敛有界性为 O(n^{-1})（已知的平滑化界）。猜想：无平滑化的原始速率对原子质量为零的初值为 W_2(μ_t^emp, μ_t^sc) ≤ C(t)·n^{-1}，其中 C(t) 在 t→∞ 时收敛到常数 C_∞=√2 π^{-1}；且 C_∞ 由半圆律传输势的 Lipschitz 常数与特征值排斥 β=2 的刚性下界联合给出。
