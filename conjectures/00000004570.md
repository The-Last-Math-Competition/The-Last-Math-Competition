**TR3-30 △** — source: 137_数论V_超越方法.md

**English.** Definition: Schanuel's conjecture is the master conjecture of the algebraic independence of the exponential field. Conjecture: There exist two periods all of whose algebraic combinations have provable transcendence independent of Schanuel, with the separating pair given by an explicit combination of zeta(3) and Gamma(1/4). (Schanuel-independent period pair)

**中文。** 定义：Schanuel 猜想指指数域代数无关性主猜想。猜想：存在两个周期的一切代数组合的超越性可独立于 Schanuel 证明,分离对由 ζ(3) 与 Γ(1/4) 的显式组合给出。（Schanuel 独立周期对）
