**TW2-04 ★** — source: 175_范畴的表示_2表示论.md

**English.** Definition: A crossed module category is the module framework with crossing (braided) structure of Yetter-Drinfeld type. Conjecture: The centrality conversion constant is explicit in the double reduction of the braiding, one for symmetric braiding; the kernel of the double reduction is the image of the center of the pure braid group, a finite group; and the spectrum of the constant is infinite but discrete in the nonsymmetric case, the discrete lattice indexed by the crossing number of braid words. (symmetric constant of crossed double reduction)

**中文。** 定义：交叉模范畴：带交叉（辫）结构的模框架（ Yetter–Drinfeld 型）。猜想：中心性换算常数为辫的双重规约显式且辫对称时常数为 1；双重规约的核为纯辫的中心的像且像为有限群；常数的谱在非对称情形为无穷但离散且离散格由辫字的交叉数索引。（交叉双重规约对称常数）
