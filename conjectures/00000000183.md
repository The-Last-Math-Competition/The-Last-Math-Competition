# 00000000183

**CS-17 ◆** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: ln π is the natural logarithm of π. Conjecture: ln π is transcendental; it is known that ln(algebraic ≠ 1) is transcendental, but the transcendence of π does not transfer directly, and a new exponential-independence argument is required. (ln-pi exponential independence)

**中文。** 定义：ln π 为 π 的自然对数。猜想：ln π 超越；已知 ln(代数不等于一)超越而 π 的超越不直接迁移，需新的指数独立性论证。（ln-pi 指数独立论证）
