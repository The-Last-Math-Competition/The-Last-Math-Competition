# 00000009963

**MRG-23 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The Poisson dimension spectrum of diagonal-flow invariant measures: the dimension structure under randomization parameters. Conjecture: The Poisson dimension spectrum of an invariant measure is an affine interval with endpoints given by the supremum and infimum of the local dimension of the measure; linearity of the spectrum is equivalent to algebraicity of the measure, and nonalgebraic measures have strictly convex spectra. (Poisson dimension spectrum linearity)

**中文。** 定义：对角流不变测度的 Poisson 维数谱：测度的随机化参数下的维数结构。猜想：不变测度的 Poisson 维数谱为仿射区间且端点由测度的局部维数的上下确界给出；谱的线性性等价于测度的代数性且非代数测度的谱严格凸。（Poisson 维数谱线性）
