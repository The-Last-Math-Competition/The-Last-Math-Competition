# 00000009963

**MRG-23 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: dimension structure of randomization parameter under of Poisson dimension spectrum: measure of pair angle flow invariant measure. Conjecture: Poisson dimension spectrum of invariant measure is affine interval and endpoint by on infimum of local dimension of measure gives linear of; spectrum is algebra of equivalent to measure and spectrum strictly convex of non- algebra measure. (Poisson dimension spectrum linear)

**中文。** 定义：对角流不变测度的 Poisson 维数谱：测度的随机化参数下的维数结构。猜想：不变测度的 Poisson 维数谱为仿射区间且端点由测度的局部维数的上下确界给出；谱的线性性等价于测度的代数性且非代数测度的谱严格凸。（Poisson 维数谱线性）
