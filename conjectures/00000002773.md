**AG3-03 ★** — source: 78_渐近几何_Banach局部理论II.md

**English.** Definition: Dvoretzky's theorem: every infinite-dimensional space contains nearly Euclidean low-dimensional sections. Conjecture: The optimal concentration of the Dvoretzky dimension: an n-dimensional space contains a subspace (1−ε)-isometric to ℓ₂^k with k = c·log n the optimal order for general spaces; optimality is attained by Johnson–Lindenstrauss spaces, and the supremum constant concentrates at (1/2)log₂n. (Dvoretzky dimension concentration law)

**中文。** 定义：Dvoretzky 定理：任意无穷维空间含接近欧氏的低维截面。猜想：Dvoretzky 维数的最优集中：n 维空间含 (1−ε) 等距于 ℓ₂^{k} 的子空间且 k = c·log n 为一般空间的最优阶；最优性由 Johnson–Lindenstrauss 空间达到且常数的上确界集中在 1/2·log₂n。（Dvoretzky 维数集中律）
