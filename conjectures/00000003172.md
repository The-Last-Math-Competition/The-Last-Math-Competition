**English.** Definition: Iterative hard thresholding (IHT): greedy sparse recovery. Conjecture: The convergence rate of IHT: linear convergence if and only if the restricted strong convexity-smoothness condition holds, with the ratio of constants the spectral threshold of the acceleration term. (IHT linear convergence spectral threshold)

**中文。** 定义：迭代硬阈值（IHT）：贪心稀疏恢复。猜想：IHT 的收敛速率：线性收敛当且仅当受限强凸-光滑条件成立且常数的比为加速项的谱阈值。（IHT 线性收敛谱阈值）
