# 00000008449

**English.** Definition: Evaluations of the Kauffman bracket at roots of unity. Conjecture: The modulus of an evaluation is an algebraic integer; its absolute value gives a lower bound for the decomposition numbers of families of four-spheres; and the sign is isomorphic to the Tristam-Levine signature. (algebraic integrality of Kauffman root evaluations)

**中文。** 定义：Kauffman 括号在单位根处的赋值。猜想：赋值的模为代数整数；其绝对值给出四维球面族分解数的下界；符号与 Tristam–Levine 签名同构。（Kauffman 根赋值代数整性）
