# 00000005259

**English.** Definition: The parametrized extension is the deformation family of the theta-function quantum well. Conjecture: The constant table at parameter one is the explicit evaluation of theta functions at root points, given by the Fourier transform of the weight space; the evaluations are algebraic numbers of degree at most the weight; and the analytic continuation of the parameter has no poles inside the unit disk, with boundary values modular forms. (root-point evaluation constants at parameter one)

**中文。** 定义：带参量推广：theta 函数量子宫的形变族。猜想：参量取一的常数表为 theta 函数在根点的求值显式且由权空间的傅里叶变换给出；求值为代数数且次数不超过权；参量的解析延拓在单位圆内无极点且边界值为模形式。（参量一根点求值常数）
