# 00000008407

**English.** Definition: The motivic dimension: the minimal generation dimension of representations of the Tannakian group. Conjecture: The motivic dimension obeys an additive-multiplicative law under direct sum and tensor product, and its value equals the level of the corresponding Hodge realization; the maximum dimension for weight-1 motives is the degree of the endomorphism algebra; and for higher-weight motives the dimension takes arbitrary integer values. (motivic dimension value spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：母题的动机维数指 Tannakian 群的表示的最小生成维数。猜想：母题维数在直和与张量下有可加-可乘律，其值等于对应 Hodge 实现的水平；维数的极大值在权 1 母题上为自同态代数的次数；高权母题的维数可取任意整值。（母题维数取值谱）
