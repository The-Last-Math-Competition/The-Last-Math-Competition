**English.** Definition: Local D-modules are modules of differential operators on germ spaces. Conjecture: The classification of local D-modules is completely determined by orbit-closure pairings; the two-orbit case degenerates to the classical duality correspondence, and the classification is closed up to isomorphism. (orbit-closure pairing classification of local D-modules)
