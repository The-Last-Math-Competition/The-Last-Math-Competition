**KT-84 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The virtual knot group is the group invariant of virtual diagrams. Conjecture: The group is finitely generated and infinite if and only if the knot is non-classical; and the criterion's combination is the Gauss diagram. (virtual group Gauss diagram non-classical criterion)

**中文。** 定义：虚拟纽结群为虚拟图表的群不变量。猜想：群为有限生成无限群当且仅当纽结非经典；且判据的组合为高斯图。（虚拟群高斯图非经典判据）
