**English.** Definition: Principal-component stability is the robustness of principal axes to perturbation. Conjecture: The explicit angle bound of the signal-to-noise ratio is the negative one-half power of dimension for strong signals and uniformly distributed angles for weak signals, the kernel of the distribution being the uniform phase. (dimension negative-half-power angle bound of principal components)

**中文。** 定义：主成分稳定性指主轴对扰动的稳健性。猜想：信噪比的显式角界在强信号时为维数负二分之一次幂且弱信号为角一致分布；且分布的核为均匀相位。（主成分角界维数负半幂相位均匀）
