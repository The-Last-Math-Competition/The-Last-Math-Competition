# 00000004763

**English.** Definition: Free cumulants and classical cumulants are the noncommutative and commutative combinatorial systems of moment functionals. Conjecture: Their conversion is always a low-order rational combination with coefficients given by weighted crossing pair counts; the first two nontrivial conversion coefficients are 1 and -1; the conversion matrix is invertible over the field of rational functions, with finite bandwidth of the inverse (finite bandwidth of the Stirling-type triangle). (rational conversion of free-classical cumulants)

**中文。** 定义：自由累积量与经典累积量：矩泛函系的非交换与交换两套组合系统。猜想：两者的换算恒为低阶有理组合且组合系数由带权交叉配对计数给出；首两个非平凡换算系数为 1 与 -1；换算矩阵在有理函数域上可逆且逆的带宽为有限（Stirling 型三角的有限带宽）。（自由-经典累积量有理组合）
