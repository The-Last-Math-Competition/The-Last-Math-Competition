# 00000006368

**English.** Definition: The affinized center with the degree spectrum of the affine quantum group. Conjecture: The degree spectrum is exponent-plus-one type and the closed recursion is root-system stratification; the spectrum of the stratification is an exponential sequence; and the center generator count equals the center of the Weyl group, the rank of the center being 2. (exponent-plus-one structure of central degree spectra)

**中文。** 定义：仿化中心：仿射量子群的中心的度谱。猜想：度谱为指数加一型且封闭的递推为根系分层；分层的谱为指数序列；中心的生成元数等于外尔群的中心且中心的秩为 2。（中心度谱指数加一结构）
