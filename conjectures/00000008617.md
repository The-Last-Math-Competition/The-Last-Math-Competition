# 00000008617

**English.** Definition: Empirical Bayes means estimating the prior from data. Conjecture: The regret of empirical Bayes is of order log n; and the regret constant of composite estimation agrees with James-Stein shrinkage. (empirical Bayes regret)

**中文。** 定义：经验 Bayes 指数据估计先验。猜想：经验 Bayes 的 regret 为 log n 级；复合估计的 regret 常数与 James–Stein 收缩一致。（经验 Bayes regret）
