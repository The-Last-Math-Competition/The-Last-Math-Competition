# 00000008027

**OMN-07 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The taming of Radon-Nikodym derivatives of definable Choquet capacities: the definability class D_mu of the density function d mu/dV of o-minimal measures (definable finite measures). Conjecture: D_mu is the class of definable L^1 functions whose representatives have Lipschitz constants bounded by definable functions; the fractal dimension of the support of a definable measure is an integer (complete discretization of the dimension spectrum); and the exclusion mechanism for non-integer dimensions (genuine fractals) is the iterative convergence of the monotonicity theorem (the monotone limit of cell decomposition dimensions), with convergence rate a linear function of cell depth (a linear convergence law). (definable measure integer dimension rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：可定义的 Choquet 容量的 Radon–Nikodym 导数的驯化：o-极小测度（可定义有限测度）的密度函数 dμ/dV 的可定义性类 D_μ。猜想：D_μ 为可定义 L¹ 函数的等价类且代表的 Lipschitz 常数被可定义函数控制；且可定义测度的支撑的分形维数为整数（维数谱的完全离散化）；非整数维（真分形）的排除的机制为单调性定理的迭代收敛（cell 分解的维数的单调极限），收敛的速率为 cell 的深度的线性函数（线性收敛律）。
