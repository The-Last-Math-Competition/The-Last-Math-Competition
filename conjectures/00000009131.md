**FIM-01 ★** — source: 290_FI模与表示稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: FI-modules: modules over the category of finite sets and injections. Conjecture: Noetherianity of FI-modules: Noetherian in characteristic zero, with the characteristic p generalization on the spectral side. (FI-module spectral-side Noetherianity)

**中文。** 定义：FI-模：有限单射范畴上的模。猜想：FI-模的 Noether 性：特征为零的 Noether 且特征 p 的广义为谱侧。（FI-模谱侧特征零 Noether 特征 p 广义）
