# 00000007953

**English.** Definition: The code of asymptotic positions of multisoliton solutions: the Jacobian determinant of the two-way map between position parameters {x_i(0)} and scattering data {p_i}. Conjecture: The Jacobian is the explicit double product over i<j of (p_i - p_j)^2/(p_i + p_j)^2; and the map is symplectic (relative to the Zakharov-Shabat 2-form); the permutation between the asymptotic configurations of position parameters as t tends to plus and minus infinity is the permutation sigma in S_N of elastic soliton scattering, the inversion number of sigma equaling the velocity ordering, and the total phase shift sum of delta_ij satisfies two-body additivity (no many-body correction, exactness of the elasticity law). (multisoliton Jacobian elasticity law)

**中文。** 定义：多孤子解的渐近位置的密码：N-孤子解的位置参数 {x_i(0)} 与散射数据 {p_i} 的双向映射的 Jacobi 行列式。猜想：Jacobi 行列式为 ∏_{i<j}(p_i-p_j)²/(p_i+p_j)² 的显式双积式；且该映射为辛映射（相对 Zakharov–Shabat 的 2-形式）；位置参数在 t→∞ 与 t→-∞ 的两个渐近配置的置换为孤子的弹性散射的置换 σ∈S_N，σ 的反序数等于速度排序，相位移动总量 Σδ_ij 满足两体可加性（无多体修正，弹性律的精确性）。
