# 00000003488

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic stratification of graph spectra: divisibility layers of Laplacian eigenvalues as algebraic integers. Conjecture: The algebraic-integer ring decomposition layers of Laplacian eigenvalues accumulate under iterated Mycielski construction by Fibonacci-type recursions; layer thickness grows logarithmically, with the limit layer converging to an imaginary quadratic integer ring. (eigenvalue divisibility layer structure)

**中文。** 定义：图谱的算术分层：Laplacian 特征值作为代数整数的整除层结构。猜想：Laplacian 特征值的代数整数环分解层在迭代 Mycielski 构造下按斐波那契型递推累积；层厚增长为对数型且极限层收敛到虚二次整数环。（特征整除层结构）
