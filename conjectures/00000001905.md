**English.** Definition: The sublevel sets of oscillatory integrals are {|e^{iP}| ≥ λ}. Conjecture: The optimal bound for the sublevel-set measure of the rank-2 polynomial P(x,y) is λ^{−c·deg} with an explicit exponent (rank-2 sublevel sets).
