# 00000003537

**English.** Definition: Dense linear orders of ordinals: η_α sets. Conjecture: The saturation of η sets: the existence of η_α sets is the regularity of α, with the saturation degree of sets the density function of ordinals. (eta set regular density saturation)

**中文。** 定义：序数的稠密线性序：η_α 集。猜想：η 集的饱和：η_α 集的存在为 α 的正则性且集的饱和度为序数的稠密函数。（eta 集正则稠密饱和度）
