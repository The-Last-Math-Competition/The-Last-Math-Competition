**English.** Definition: The categorification of diagram algebras is the combinatorial categorical lift. Conjecture: Its conversion is given explicitly by the counting bijection of admissible diagrams, the bijection preserves gradings, and it is closed at extension generators. (counting-bijection conversion of diagram algebras)
