**English.** Definition: The circular law for non-Hermitian random matrices: Ginibre eigenvalues equidistribute on the disk (proven by Tao-Vu); the local statistics at the boundary and the outlier structure. Conjecture: The renormalized local statistics of eigenvalues inside the boundary ring converge to an explicit Ginibre kernel process; outliers from finite-rank deformations undergo a BBP-type transition, and the coupling kernel of outliers with the inner bulk backbone is an explicit elliptic kernel. (circular law boundary kernel process)

**中文。** 定义：非厄米特随机矩阵的圆律：Ginibre 系综特征值一致分布于圆盘（Tao–Vu 已证）；边界的局部统计与离群值结构。猜想：边界环内特征值的重整化局部统计收敛到显式 Ginibre 核过程；有限秩形变产生的离群值满足 BBP 型相变且离群值与内圈骨干的耦合核为显式椭圆核。（圆律边界核过程）
