# 00000002423

**English.** Definition: A self-covering is a nontrivial covering C → C of a curve to itself. Conjecture: The complete classification of self-coverings of genus ≥ 2 curves: exactly the pullback types of coverings C → E of elliptic curves E; there are no first examples outside the classification (rigidity).

**中文。** 定义：自覆盖指非平凡的覆叠 C→C(到自身)。猜想：亏格 ≥2 的自覆盖的完全分类为:恰为由椭圆曲线 E 的覆盖 C→E 的拉回型;分类外无首例(刚性)。
