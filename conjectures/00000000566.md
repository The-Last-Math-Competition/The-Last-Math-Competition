# 00000000566

**English.** Definition: The theta basis is a candidate canonical basis of a cluster algebra, valued on the torus compactification. Conjecture: Integrality of theta functions on the boundary of the compactization is equivalent to total positivity of the structure constants of the canonical basis; and this positivity extends to cluster structures on arbitrary exchange graphs. (theta basis structure-constant positivity)

**中文。** 定义：theta 基为 cluster 代数的典范基候选取值于环面紧化。猜想：theta 函数在紧化边界上的整性等价于典范基结构常数的全正性；该正性扩展到任意交换图的 cluster 结构。（theta 基结构常数正性）
