# 00000007911

**English.** Definition: Equidistribution evidence for subconvexity: the short interval sum sum_{n<=x} lambda_pi(n) of the Dirichlet coefficients of a GL_m cusp representation. Conjecture: sum_{n<=x} lambda_pi(n) = O(x^{1-1/(m^2+1)+epsilon}) uniformly in pi; and the exponent 1/(m^2+1), which interpolates to the classical 1/2 at m = 1, cannot be improved as a parabolic interpolation; any epsilon improvement would imply absolute convergence of Rankin-Selberg L-functions at Re s = 1, equivalent to minimality of the density hypothesis. (coefficient sum uniform exponent)

**中文。** 定义：亚凸界的均匀分布证据：Dirichlet 系数的短区间和 Σ_{n≤x} λ_π(n)（λ_π 为 GL_m 尖点表示的系数）。猜想：Σ_{n≤x}λ_π(n) = O(x^{1-1/(m²+1)+ε})（一致于 π）；且指数 1/(m²+1) 在 m=1 时回到经典 1/2 的抛物线插值不可改进；改进任何 ε 将蕴含 Rankin–Selberg L 函数在 Re s=1 的绝对收敛，等价于零点密度假设的最小化。
