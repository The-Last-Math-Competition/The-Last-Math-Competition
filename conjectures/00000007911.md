**English.** Definition: short interval of all distribution: Dirichlet coefficient of convex bound and Σ_{n≤x} λ_π(n) (λ_π is coefficient) of GL_m cusp representation. Conjecture: Σ_{n≤x}λ_π(n) = O(x^{1-1/(m²+1)+ε}) (uniform in π); and parabolic line interpolation cannot be improved; ε - of exponent 1/(m²+1) in m=1 classical 1/2 implies pair convergent, of Rankin–Selberg L function in Re s=1 is minimizes of equivalent to zero density hypothesis.

**中文。** 定义：亚凸界的均匀分布证据：Dirichlet 系数的短区间和 Σ_{n≤x} λ_π(n)（λ_π 为 GL_m 尖点表示的系数）。猜想：Σ_{n≤x}λ_π(n) = O(x^{1-1/(m²+1)+ε})（一致于 π）；且指数 1/(m²+1) 在 m=1 时回到经典 1/2 的抛物线插值不可改进；改进任何 ε 将蕴含 Rankin–Selberg L 函数在 Re s=1 的绝对收敛，等价于零点密度假设的最小化。
