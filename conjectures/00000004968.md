# 00000004968

**English.** Definition: Real and complex Waring ranks are the minimal numbers of real and complex summands. Conjecture: The realizable domain of the real-complex difference of quartic binary forms is an explicit three-valued partition, decided by the boundary rank, and the domain is closed. (three-part domain of quartic real-complex rank differences)

**中文。** 定义：实与复 Waring 秩指实与复分解的最小项数。猜想：四次二项式的实复差可实现域为三值显式分划,分划由边界秩判定且域封闭。（四次实复秩差三分域）
