**English.** Definition: point line LPP: objective is GOE limit of through time of line segment. Conjecture: LPP limit of point line geometry under is Airy_2 and function kernel representation of explicit composition distribution; composition weight of independent Brownian is Pfaffian type and geometry parameter continuous without phase transition. (point line composition limit)
