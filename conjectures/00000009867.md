**KPU-17 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: Point-to-line LPP: GOE limits of passage times with a segment target. Conjecture: The fluctuation limit of LPP in point-to-half-line geometry is an explicit composite distribution of Airy_2 with an independent half-Brownian motion; the functional kernel representation of the composite weight is Pfaffian, and geometric parameters deform continuously without phase transition. (point-to-halfline composite limit)

**中文。** 定义：点到线 LPP：目标为线段的通过时间的 GOE 极限。猜想：点到半线几何下的 LPP 涨落极限为 Airy_2 与独立半 Brownian 的显式复合分布；复合权重的函数核表示为 Pfaffian 型且几何参数连续变形无相变。（点到半线复合极限）
