# 00000005527

**English.** Definition: The infinite-server system has no queueing. Conjecture: The truncation-error decay constant is the explicit exponential of the mean occupancy, given by the large-deviation rate of the blocking probability, and exact. (large-deviation constant of infinite-server truncation)

**中文。** 定义：无穷服务系统指无排队的系统。猜想：截断误差衰减常数为占用均值的显式指数,由阻塞概率的大偏差率给出且精确。（无穷服务截断大偏差常数）
