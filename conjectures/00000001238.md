**English.** Definition: The invariant-factor sequence of the Jacobi group encodes the torsion structure of the graph Laplacian. Conjecture: Log-concavity of the invariant-factor sequence of the torsion group holds for all graphs; and equality in the concavity holds exactly for direct sums of cycles. (Jacobian invariant factors log-concave)
