**English.** Definition: Pic²-rationality of M_{g,n} is the Q-coefficient trivialization of the second Picard group. Conjecture: The extreme rays of the effective cone of M_{g,n} form a completely explicit table for (g,n) ≤ (10,3); the rays of the table are constructed via generalized Falconer–Pandharipande type sections, each explicit.

**中文。** 定义：M_{g,n} 的 Pic² 有理性指第二 Picard 群的 Q 系数化。猜想：M_{g,n} 的有效锥的极值射线在 (g,n) ≤(10,3) 的范围为完全显式表;表的射线由广义 Falconer–Pandharipande 型截面构造,每条射线显式。
