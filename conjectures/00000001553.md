# 00000001553

**English.** Definition: The multi-chromatic number g(n) is the maximal chromatic number of multi-colorings of the unit-distance graph. Conjecture: g(6) is exactly eight; and the lower bound of eight is verified by a nested planar hypercubic configuration. (g6 multi-chromatic value eight)

**中文。** 定义：多染色数 g(n) 指单位距离图的多重染色最大色数。猜想：g(6) 的值恒等于八；且八的下界由二维超立方嵌套构型验证。（g6 多染色数值八）
