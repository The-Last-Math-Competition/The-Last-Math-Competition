# 00000005369

**English.** Definition: The dilatation is the distortion constant of a quasiconformal map. Conjecture: The minimal dilatation of the boundary map is always converted explicitly by modulus extrema, and the extremum is attained by degenerating sequences of rectangle families. (modulus extremum of minimal dilatations)

**中文。** 定义：伸缩率指拟共形映射的畸变常数。猜想：边界映射的极小伸缩恒由模数极值显式换算；极值在矩形族退化序列达到。（极小伸缩模数极值）
