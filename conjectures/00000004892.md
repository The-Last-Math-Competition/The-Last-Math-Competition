# 00000004892

**English.** Definition: A cross-domain lemma is a lemma transferred between the combinatorial and algebraic domains. Conjecture: The combinatorial side of the minimal covering lemma always converts through the count of the subobject lattice, and the conversion is closed on rank-two lattices. (lattice conversion of the combinatorial-algebraic minimal covering lemma)

**中文。** 定义：跨域引理指组合与代数领域间迁移的引理。猜想：极小覆盖引理的组合侧恒由子对象格的计数换算；且换算在秩二格封闭。（组合-代数极小跨域换算格）
