# 00000008618

**MLC-28 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Optimal reweighting means improvement by loss weighting. Conjecture: The minimax improvement from reweighting does not exceed the square root of the variance ratio; and the heteroscedastic gain is an explicit rational expression. (reweighting gain upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：最优再加权指损失加权的改进。猜想：再加权的 minimax 改进不超过方差比平方根；异方差收益为显式有理式。（再加权收益上界）
