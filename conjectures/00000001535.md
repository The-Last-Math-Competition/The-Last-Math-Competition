# 00000001535

**English.** Definition: The Cuntz semigroup Cu(A) is an object of the completion category of positively ordered monoids. Conjecture: The realizable elements of the Cu-semigroups of real-rank-zero stable algebras are exactly the sequence semigroups satisfying (O5); the minimal non-realizable interval-type object is the complement of dense elements in (0,∞), its structure explicitly characterized by the first gap element ξ.

**中文。** 定义：Cuntz 半群 Cu(A) 为 positively ordered monoid 的完备化范畴的对象。猜想：实秩零稳定代数的 Cu-半群的可实现元恰为满足 (O5) 的序列半群;最小不可实现的区间型对象为 (0,∞) 中的稠密元补集,其结构由第一间隙元素 ξ 显式刻画。
