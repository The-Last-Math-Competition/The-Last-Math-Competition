**English.** Definition: The K moduli space is the K-stability moduli construction of stable objects. Conjecture: The dimension of the three-dimensional K moduli is always given by an explicit count of fixed-point configurations, complete for double-quotient types; the mod 2 reduction of the dimension is decided by the parity of the singularity pairing count; and the ramification locus of the double quotient is a divisor, with degree given by the weights. (fixed-point dimensions of the three-dimensional K moduli)

**中文。** 定义：K 模空间：稳定对象的 K 稳定性模构造。猜想：三维 K 模的维数恒由不动点配置的显式计数给出且双商型完全；维数的模 2 约化由奇点配对数的奇偶决定；双商的分歧轨迹为除子且次数由权和给出。（三维 K 模不动点维数）
