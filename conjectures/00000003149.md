**English.** Definition: Orbit bundles: the quotient structure of action bundles. Conjecture: The stratification of orbit bundles: the stratification of singular orbits is a singular stratification, with the connectivity of the layers the nilpotent cone structure of the action group. (orbit bundle nilpotent cone stratification)
