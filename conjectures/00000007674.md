**English.** Definition: pair q- difference part equation y(qz)-y(z)=z y(z)^{s} (q- type Painlevé I), its algebra entropy κ is defined to be limsup (1/n²) log d_n, d_n is pole order of expression Laurent solution the- n step. Conjecture: κ=log|q|; right-hand side of for all algebraic number q, 0<|q|<1, the equation is z^k y^m back κ = ((k-1)m/(m-1)) log|q|, namely algebra entropy by spectral radius formula of equation gives and and rational number entropy 2 strictly of classical Painlevé I.

**中文。** 定义：对 q-差分方程 y(qz)-y(z)=z y(z)^{s}（q-型 Painlevé I），其代数熵 κ 定义为 limsup (1/n²) log d_n，d_n 为形式 Laurent 解第 n 步的极点阶。猜想：对一切代数数 q，0<|q|<1，该方程的 κ=log|q|；而将右端换为 z^k y^m 后 κ = ((k-1)m/(m-1)) log|q|，即代数熵由方程的谱半径公式给出且与经典 Painlevé I 的有理数熵 2 严格分离。
