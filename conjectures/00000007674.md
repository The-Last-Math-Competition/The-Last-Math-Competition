# 00000007674

**English.** Definition: For the q-difference equation y(qz)-y(z)=z y(z)^s (q-Painleve I type), the algebraic entropy is kappa=limsup (1/n^2) log d_n with d_n the pole order of the formal Laurent solution at step n. Conjecture: For every algebraic q with 0<|q|<1, kappa=log|q|; and with right side z^k y^m one gets kappa = ((k-1)m/(m-1)) log|q|, so the entropy is given by the spectral-radius formula and strictly separates from the rational entropy 2 of classical Painleve I. (algebraic entropy of q-Painleve I)

**中文。** 定义：对 q-差分方程 y(qz)-y(z)=z y(z)^{s}（q-型 Painlevé I），其代数熵 κ 定义为 limsup (1/n²) log d_n，d_n 为形式 Laurent 解第 n 步的极点阶。猜想：对一切代数数 q，0<|q|<1，该方程的 κ=log|q|；而将右端换为 z^k y^m 后 κ = ((k-1)m/(m-1)) log|q|，即代数熵由方程的谱半径公式给出且与经典 Painlevé I 的有理数熵 2 严格分离。
