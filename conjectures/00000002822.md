# 00000002822

**LD-22 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: Mogulskii's theorem: the full-trajectory LDP on path space. Conjecture: Boundary behavior of the trajectory LDP: the rate function has a lower-semicontinuous correction at boundary paths, the correction being the sum of penalty terms for jump-type trajectories. (Mogulskii boundary correction)

**中文。** 定义：Mogulskii 定理：路径空间的全轨迹 LDP。猜想：轨迹 LDP 的边界行为：速率函数在边界轨道的下半连续修正且修正为跳跃型轨线的惩罚项之和。（Mogulskii 边界修正）
