# 00000006300

**English.** Definition: The spectrum and the homology spectrum are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two group rings with the same spectrum but different homology spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit group pair with the same spectrum but different resistance. (same spectrum-different homology resistance separation)

**中文。** 定义：该谱与同调谱指两层。猜想：存在两群环的谱相同而同调谱不同,分离由同谱异抵抗的显式群对实现。（谱同-同调谱异抵抗分离）
