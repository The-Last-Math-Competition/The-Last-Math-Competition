**English.** Definition: A d-cyclic group action is a free action of a finite cyclic group on a triangulation. Conjecture: The prime-factor spectrum of tree counts corresponds one-to-one with the factorization of the class group of the ring of cyclotomic integers, and the class number enters the factorization explicitly. (tree count-cyclotomic class group correspondence)
