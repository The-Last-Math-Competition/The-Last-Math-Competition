# 00000008672

**English.** Definition: Subgroup mismatch of direct products (Michaloglou): distorted subgroups of direct products. Conjecture: The decision of finiteness of fiber products is the 1-2-3 theorem (a 123 law); there is an algorithm separating distorted subgroups (an algorithm law); the complexity of the separation is exponential (an exponential complexity law); and the downgrade of the complexity for hyperbolic components is polynomial (a polynomial downgrade law). (fiber product 1-2-3 exponential separation)

**中文。** 定义：直积的子群失配（Michalovsky）：直积的扭曲子群。猜想：纤维积的有限性的判定（判定律）为 1-2-3 定理的（123 律）；且扭曲子群（扭曲律）的分离的算法（算法律）；分离的复杂度（算法的复杂度）为指数的（指数复杂度律）；复杂度的降级（双曲分量的降级）为多项式的（多项式降级律）。
