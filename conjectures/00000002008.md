**PD-18 ★** — source: 52_偏微分方程II.md

**English.** Definition: Stability of shock interactions is the continuous dependence of solutions after two shocks collide. Conjecture: The interaction of orthogonal shocks in 3D relativistic Euler is Lipschitz stable; and the stability constant is inversely proportional to the product of the two shock strengths. (orthogonal shock interaction Lipschitz law)

**中文。** 定义：冲激波相互作用的稳定性指两激波碰撞后的解连续依赖。猜想：三维相对论 Euler 的正交激波相互作用具有 Lipschitz 稳定性；且稳定性常数与两激波强度的乘积成反比。（正交激波交互 Lipschitz 律）
