# 00000001766

**English.** Definition: The manifold invariants of exceptional orbits of a Lie algebra refer to the invariant-ring structure under the isotropy representation. Conjecture: The manifold invariants of exceptional orbits are algebraic integers: the Hilbert series of the invariant ring on exceptional orbits are all rational functions, with denominators explicit from the third-order Cartan-type invariants.

**中文。** 定义：李代数的例外轨道的歧管不变量指其在迷向表示下的不变量环结构。猜想：例外轨道的歧管不变量为代数整数:不变量环的 Hilbert 级数在例外轨道上全部为有理函数,分母由 Cartan 型三阶不变量显式。
