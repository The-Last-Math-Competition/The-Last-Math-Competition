# 00000005191

**English.** Definition: A two-dimensional topological field theory takes values in vector spaces. Conjecture: The conversion of its isotopy invariants is given explicitly by the algebraic trace pairing, the genus-expansion coefficients are closed, and the normalization constant is one once the dimension is fixed. (trace-pairing genus expansion of two-dimensional field theories)

**中文。** 定义：二维拓扑场论指值于向量空间的场论。猜想：其同痕不变量的换算由代数迹配对显式给出,亏格展开系数封闭且归一常数为一旦固定维数。（二维场论迹配对亏格展开）
