# 00000005324

**English.** Definition: The knight-move conjecture asserts the diagonal-neighborhood concentration of homology. Conjecture: The search band of the smallest counterexample is explicit within one hundred crossings, the negative evidence within the band is given by explicit chains of thickness-two families, and the exclusion is generated layer by layer by the basic ideals of Whitehead doubles. (hundred-crossing search band of knight-move counterexamples)

**中文。** 定义：骑士移动猜想指同调集中对角邻域的断言。猜想：最小反例的交叉数搜索带在一百以内显式,带内的否定证据由厚度二族的显式链给出。(骑士反例百内搜索带)；且排除由 Whitehead 双的基本理想逐层生成。
