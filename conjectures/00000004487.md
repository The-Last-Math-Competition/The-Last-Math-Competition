**English.** Definition: The closed topos is the closed component in the double-adjoint decomposition of a geometric morphism. Conjecture: A closed topos exists if and only if the corresponding Lawvere-Tierney topology has an idempotent coclosure, the criterion is explicit, and the dual statement holds for the open-complement topology. (idempotent coclosure criterion of closed toposes)

**中文。** 定义：闭拓扑斯指几何态射双伴随分解中的闭子项。猜想：闭拓扑斯存在当且仅当对应的 Lawvere–Tierney 拓扑具幂等余闭包,判据显式且在开补拓扑下对偶成立。（闭拓扑斯余闭幂等判据）
