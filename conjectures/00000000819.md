# 00000000819

**English.** Definition: Convex vertices of nodal lines are nodes where the curvature changes sign. Conjecture: On analytic domains, the number of convex vertices of nodal lines is at most C(λ)·λ^{1/2}; and the bound's tightness is attained by circular eigenfunctions. (nodal line convex vertex upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：节线的凸顶点为曲率变号的节点。猜想：解析域上节线凸顶点数不超过 C(λ) 乘 λ^{1/2}；且界的紧性由圆周特征函数达到。（节线凸顶点上界）
