**English.** Definition: Toeplitz sequences are built from dense weaves of periodic layers; the unique ergodicity of regular Toeplitz flows. Conjecture: Regular Toeplitz flows are uniquely ergodic with pure point spectrum, the frequency group being the direct limit of periodic layer groups; non-regular Toeplitz must contain a singular continuous spectral component, and the pure point criterion is equivalent to regularity. (Toeplitz pure point criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Toeplitz 序列由周期层的稠密编织构成；正则 Toeplitz 流的唯一遍历性。猜想：正则 Toeplitz 流唯一遍历且谱为纯点，谱频率群为周期层群的正向极限；非正则 Toeplitz 必含奇异连续谱分量，纯点判据与正则性等价。（Toeplitz 纯点判据）
