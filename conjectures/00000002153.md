# 00000002153

**English.** Definition: A Fiedler vector is an eigenvector of the second eigenvalue; nodal domains are the components where its sign is constant. Conjecture: The number of nodal domains of a Fiedler vector on a tree is at most multiplicity(λ₂) + 1; for graphs of girth at least 5, the nodal-domain count is at most 2√n (a universal bound under the girth condition).

**中文。** 定义：Fiedler 向量指第二特征值的特征向量;节域指其符号恒定的分量。猜想：树上 Fiedler 向量的节域数 ≤λ₂ 的重数+1;高围长(≥5)图的节域数 ≤2√n(围长条件下的普适上界)。
