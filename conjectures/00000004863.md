**English.** Definition: The Patterson measure is the equivariant conformal measure on the boundary. Conjecture: The lower bound of the conformal dimension is an explicit capacity-type function of the separated-set count, attained by the Sierpinski carpet and tight. (separated-count lower bound of the conformal dimension)
