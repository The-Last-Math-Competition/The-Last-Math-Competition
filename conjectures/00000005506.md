**QN2-06 ★** — source: 169_排队网络的图极限.md

**English.** Definition: A classed network is a routing network with customer classes. Conjecture: The symmetry is always given by an explicit pair of class permutations and queue exchanges, conjugate at two classes under first-come-first-served. (class-permutation queue-exchange conjugacy)

**中文。** 定义：带类网络指顾客分型的路由网络。猜想：对称性恒由类置换与队列交换的显式对给出；两类先来先服务处共轭。（类置换队列交换共轭）
