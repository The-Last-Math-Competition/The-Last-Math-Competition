**English.** Definition: Dirac matrices as anticommuting system structure. Conjecture: The existence lower bound of gamma systems is twice the dimension; the tightness of the bound is by the trace identity; and the tightness of the trace identity is given by dimension counting. (double-dimension gamma existence bound)

**中文。** 定义：狄拉克矩阵为反对易系结构。猜想：伽马系的存在下界为维数的二倍；且界的紧性由迹恒等式；且迹恒等式的紧性由维数计数给出。（伽马系存在维数二倍）
