**English.** Definition: Generalized Wythoff: the queen game generalization. Conjecture: P-positions are characterized by Beatty sequences with explicit irrational ratio; and the kernel of the explicit ratio is the golden slope. (Wythoff-Beatty golden slope)

**中文。** 定义：广义 Wythoff 为皇后博弈推广。猜想：P-位置由 Beatty 序列刻画且比率为无理显式；且显式的核为 golden 斜率。（Wythoff-Beatty golden 斜率刻画）
