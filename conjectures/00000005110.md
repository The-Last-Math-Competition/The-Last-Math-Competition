# 00000005110

**English.** Definition: The dual image is the corresponding image on the dual group. Conjecture: There exist two equivalent categories whose images under duality are not isomorphic, and the separation is realized explicitly by two distinct braid twists of unramified sheaves. (equivalent category-dual image braid-twist separation)

**中文。** 定义：对偶像指在对偶群上的对应像。猜想：存在两等价范畴经对偶的像不同构,分离由非分歧层的两个不同辫扭转显式实现。（等价范畴-对偶像辫扭转分离）
