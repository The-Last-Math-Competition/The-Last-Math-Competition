**English.** Definition: Online convex optimization (OCO): regret of sequential decisions. Conjecture: The OCO lower bound is sqrt T (a root-T law); the optimum is attained by Zinkevich's method (an attainment law); adaptive adversaries allow optimism (an optimism law); and optimism improves with predictable sequences (a predictability law). (OCO root-T Zinkevich optimism)

**中文。** 定义：在线凸优化（OCO）：序列决策的遗憾。猜想：OCO 的下界的 (√T) 的（根 T 律）；且最优（Zinkevich 的）的达到（达到律）；自适应对手（乐观的）的（乐观律）；乐观的改进（可预测序列）的（可预测律）。
