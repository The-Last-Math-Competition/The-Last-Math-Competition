# 00000005196

**English.** Definition: The graded Picard is the invertible structure with shifts. Conjecture: The joint conversion of the two homotopy groups is an explicit torsion invariant of skew-symmetric pairs, complete for cyclic groups with unique torsion. (torsion joint conversion of graded Picard groups)
