**English.** Definition: The semi-length elasticity ρ₂(H) is a two-parameter family. Conjecture: The limit spectrum of ρ₂ over families of atomic monoids is the closed interval [1, ρ] with explicit endpoint realizations (semi-length elasticity spectrum).
