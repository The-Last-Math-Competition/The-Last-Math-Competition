**English.** Definition: Palindromic splitting is the symmetric coefficient structure. Conjecture: The even-order law holds and the first odd-order term vanishes; the law table closes in the palindromic family; and the verification of the closed law table is completed on the Suzuki family. (palindromic even-order law with odd-term vanishing)
