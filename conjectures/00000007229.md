# 00000007229

**English.** Definition: The growth of subgroups with lattice counting. Conjecture: The saturation law of subgroup counting holds, and the exponent of the law is the volume entropy. (volume-entropy subgroup saturation law)

**中文。** 定义：子群的增长：格的计数。猜想：格的增长律：子群的计数的饱和律且律的指数为体积熵。（格子群体积熵饱和指数计数增长律）
