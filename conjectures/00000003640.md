**English.** Definition: Reversible systems: the structure of time-reversal symmetry. Conjecture: Periodic orbits of reversible systems: periodic points on fixed curves of reversible maps are symmetric-axis intersections, with the count of intersections the product of symmetric-axis counts. (reversible symmetric axis intersection periodic count product)

**中文。** 定义：可逆系统：时间反演对称的结构。猜想：可逆系统的周期轨：可逆映射的不动曲线的周期点为对称轴交点且交点的计数为对称轴数的乘积。（可逆对称轴交点周期计数乘积）
