# 00000001645

**HA-15 ◆** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: Lück's determinant conjecture is the integrality of Fuglede–Kadison determinants. Conjecture: For torsion-free groups satisfying Atiyah's conjecture, the l²-determinant of a matrix is an algebraic integer; moreover, the set of determinant values of rational matrices forms an explicit family of algebraic units (enumerated by the additive structure of Whitehead torsion).

**中文。** 定义：Lück 行列式猜想指 Fuglede–Kadison 行列式的整值性。猜想：对无挠满足 Atiyah 的群,矩阵的 l²-行列式为代数整数;进一步,一切有理系数矩阵的行列式值的集合构成代数单位的显式族(由 Whitehead 挠的加法结构枚举)。
