# 00000004456

**English.** Definition: The Legendrian sieve is the sieve structure constructed from microlocal sheaf theory. Conjecture: The iterated-connection generators of the sieve DGA form an explicit table of elementary-crossing wedge products, and the table is complete and closed for the standard torus knot. (wedge-product generator table of the sieve DGA)

**中文。** 定义：Legendrian 筛指由微局部层论构造的筛结构。猜想：筛 DGA 的多次连接生成元为初等交叉楔积的显式表,表在标准环面结情形完整且封闭。（筛 DGA 楔积生成元表）
