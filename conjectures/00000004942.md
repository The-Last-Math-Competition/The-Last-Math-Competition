# 00000004942

**English.** Definition: The Bousfield class is the class of acyclic objects of a homology theory, and its lattice is the poset structure of the classes. Conjecture: The lattice of classes above Morava-type homology is classified explicitly by the height total order plus a spike structure, the classification diagram is closed with no foreign classes, and the number of spikes is exactly the dimension of the Lubin-Tate deformation space. (height-order classification of the Bousfield class lattice)

**中文。** 定义：Bousfield 类指同调论的零化对象类,其格指类的偏序结构。猜想：在 Morava 型同调上的类格由高度全序加尖峰结构显式分类,分类图封闭无外来类。(Bousfield 类格高度序分类)；且尖峰个数恰为 Lubin-Tate 形变空间维数。
