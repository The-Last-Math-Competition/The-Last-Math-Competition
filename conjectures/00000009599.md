# 00000009599

**MSH-19 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The relation between minimal-area minimal surfaces and topological genus in closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds. Conjecture: The area of a genus-g minimal surface is at least 4 pi (g-1)(1 - epsilon_g) with epsilon_g tending to 0; and for general hyperbolic metrics the minimal-area minimal surface is unique, with uniqueness exceptions being a finite list of low-symmetry manifolds with geodesic coincidence. (hyperbolic minimal area lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：闭双曲 3-流形中最小面积极小曲面与拓扑亏格的关系。猜想：亏格 g 极小曲面面积 ≥ 4π(g−1)·(1−ε_g) 且 ε_g → 0；一般双曲度量下最小面积极小曲面唯一，唯一性例外为测地重合的低对称流形的有限列表。（双曲极小面积下界）
