# 00000009599

**MSH-19 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: closed hyperbolic 3- manifold in most small area minimal surface and relations of topology genus. Conjecture: genus g minimal surface area ≥ 4π(g−1) · (1−ε_g) and ε_g -> 0; general hyperbolic metric under most small area minimal surface unique, uniqueness exception is finite list of low symmetric manifold of geodesic. (hyperbolic minimal area lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：闭双曲 3-流形中最小面积极小曲面与拓扑亏格的关系。猜想：亏格 g 极小曲面面积 ≥ 4π(g−1)·(1−ε_g) 且 ε_g → 0；一般双曲度量下最小面积极小曲面唯一，唯一性例外为测地重合的低对称流形的有限列表。（双曲极小面积下界）
