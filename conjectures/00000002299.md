# 00000002299

**English.** Definition: A strongly p-embedded subgroup controls all nontrivial p-elements. Conjecture: A complete classification table of almost-simple groups with strong p-embeddings (correction: completing the three exceptions of Lie types B₂ and G₂ in characteristic 2); the table is henceforth complete; and the Lie-bundle structure of the exceptional-group automorphisms is always the twisted triple exception.

**中文。** 定义：强 p-嵌入子群指控制一切非平凡 p-元素的嵌入。猜想：具强 p-嵌入的几乎单群的完全分类表(修正项:补全 Lie 型 B₂ 与 G₂ 的特征 2 的 3 例外);表自此完备；且例外群自同构的 Lie 丛结构恒为扭三例外。
