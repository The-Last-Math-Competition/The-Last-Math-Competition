# 00000007645

**English.** Definition: Resonant systems as hyperbolic systems with coinciding characteristic speeds. Conjecture: The solution structure of the resonance band is mixed type with criterion the characteristic linear degeneracy; and the behavior of the resonance band is absorption type. (resonance-band mixed-type criterion)

**中文。** 定义：共振系统为特征速度重合的双曲系统。猜想：共振带的解结构为混合型且混合的判据为特征线性退化；且共振带的行为为吸收型。（共振带混合型判据）
