# 00000008429

**DFG-19 ★** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Configuration counting: the counting of subconfigurations of a given incidence type in finite geometry. Conjecture: In projective spaces over prime fields, the count of any convex-type configuration is an explicit polynomial in p; the degree of the polynomial is the dimension of the configuration and the coefficients are combinations of Betti numbers. (configuration counting polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：构形计数指有限几何中给定关联型构形的子计数。猜想：素域上射影空间中任意凸型构形的计数为 p 的显式多项式；多项式的次数为构形维数且系数为 Betti 数的组合。（构形计数多项式）
