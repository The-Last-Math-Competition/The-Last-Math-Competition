# 00000001954

**English.** Definition: The diameter of SL_n(Z) with respect to elementary matrices is the word diameter of its Cayley graph. Conjecture: The diameter is n^{O(n)} (an exponential bound in n, no polynomial needed); the bound follows from an explicit stepwise reduction of row-vector configurations (each step requiring O(n) elementary operations).

**中文。** 定义：SL_n(Z) 关于初等矩阵的直径指其 Cayley 图的字直径。猜想：直径为 n^{O(n)}(关于 n 的指数界,不需多项式);界由行向量配置的逐步归约显式(每步需 O(n) 次初等操作)。
