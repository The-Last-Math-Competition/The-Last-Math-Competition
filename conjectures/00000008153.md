# 00000008153

**English.** Definition: The SLE renormalization of multilevel structures: the renormalization flow rg_SLE(kappa) of the parameter kappa of Schramm's conformally invariant curve families. Conjecture: The fixed points of rg_SLE (conformal invariance of the scale flow of kappa) form the four-point set kappa in {2, 8/3, 4, 6} (a four fixed-point set); the direction of connectivity of flows between the four points is determined by the monotonicity of the central charge c(kappa) = (6-kappa)(3kappa-8)/2 kappa (a c-monotone flow law); and the special (second-order fixed point) nature of the point 8/3 is given by the local reversibility of its SLE (a local reversibility second-order law). (SLE four fixed points c-monotone flows)

**中文。** 定义：多层次结构的 SLE-重整化：Schramm 共形不变曲线族的参数 κ 的重整化流 rg_SLE(κ)。猜想：rg_SLE（κ 的尺度流的共形不变性）的不动点为 κ∈{2,8/3,4,6} 的四点集（四不动点集）；且四点之间的流的连通性的方向由中心荷 c(κ)=(6-κ)(3κ-8)/2κ 的单调性给定（c-单调流律）；不动点 8/3 的特殊（二阶不动点）性质由其 SLE 的局部可逆性（局部可逆二阶律）给出。
