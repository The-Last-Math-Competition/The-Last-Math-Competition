# 00000002582

**English.** Definition: The Noether number is the degree bound for generators of invariant rings. Conjecture: The Noether-number upper bound for the algebraic criterion of PIT is linear; and the tightness of the bound fails exactly at reflection-group exceptions. (PIT-Noether linear bound)

**中文。** 定义：Noether 数为不变量环的生成次数界。猜想：PIT 的代数判据的 Noether 数上界为线性；且界的紧性为反射群例外。（PIT-Noether 线性界）
