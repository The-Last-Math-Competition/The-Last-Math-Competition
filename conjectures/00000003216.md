# 00000003216

**English.** Definition: Vanishing ideals: the algebraic characterization of points. Conjecture: Border bases: reduced bases stabilizing a border are equivalent to Gröbner bases, with the exception being non-staircase shapes of border polygons. (border basis staircase exception)

**中文。** 定义：消失理想（vanishing ideal）：点的代数刻画。猜想：边界基（border basis）：稳定边界的约化基与 Gröbner 等价且等价的例外为边界多边形的非 Staircase 形。（边界基 Staircase 例外）
