# 00000001798

**English.** Definition: The invariant algebraic curves of a general foliation of degree d on CP². Conjecture: Their number is exactly 2d²−4d+3; the count is given by the fixed-point formula of characteristic invariants, with the total index sum identically equal to the total Baum–Bott residue. (foliation invariant curve Baum-Bott count)

**中文。** 定义：CP² 中度 d 的一般叶状结构的不变代数曲线。猜想：其条数恰为 2d²−4d+3；且计数由特征不变量的不动点公式给出，指标总量恒等于 Baum-Bott 残差的总体。（叶状不变曲线 Baum-Bott 计数）
