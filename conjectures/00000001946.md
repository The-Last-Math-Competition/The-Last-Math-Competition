# 00000001946

**English.** Definition: The Epstein zeta function is the power sum of lattice-point distances. Conjecture: In dimension five, the minimal Epstein zeta value is always attained by the D₅ lattice; the minimum is a closed form as a rational multiple of ζ(5), and the lattice is unique. (D5 lattice Epstein minimum unique)

**中文。** 定义：Epstein ζ 函数指格点距离的幂和。猜想：五维的最小 Epstein ζ 值恒由 D₅ 格取得；且最小值为 ζ(5) 的有理倍数闭式而格唯一。（D5 格 Epstein 最小唯一）
