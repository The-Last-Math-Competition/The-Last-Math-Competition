# 00000004991

**English.** Definition: A G_2 manifold is a seven-dimensional Riemannian manifold with holonomy group G_2. Conjecture: For compact G_2 manifolds with vanishing second Betti number, the third Betti number has an explicit lower bound and all odd values are realizable, with the table complete by twist-sum constructions. (attainable table of G_2 third Betti numbers at zero second Betti)

**中文。** 定义：G₂ 流形指和乐群为 G₂ 的七维黎曼流形。猜想：二阶贝蒂数为零的紧 G₂ 流形的三阶贝蒂数有显式下界且一切奇值可实现,表由解拧和构造完全。（G₂ 零二贝蒂的三贝蒂可达表）
