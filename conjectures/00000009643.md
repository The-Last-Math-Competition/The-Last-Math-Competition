# 00000009643

**HSS-03 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: Beta-ensembles are defined by the joint density product |lambda_i - lambda_j|^beta e^{-beta sum V(lambda_i)}; for arbitrary positive real beta, the universality structure of gap distributions. Conjecture: The normalized gap distributions of all ensembles with beta > 0 converge to the same universal function; the function degenerates at beta = 2 to the Fredholm determinant of the sine kernel, and for general beta is given by a diffusion Painleve representation, uniform in beta. (beta gap universal function)

**中文。** 定义：β-系综由联合密度 ∏|λ_i−λ_j|^β e^{−βΣV(λ_i)} 定义；β 为任意正实数时间隙分布的普适结构。猜想：一切 β>0 的系综的归一化间隙分布收敛到同一普适函数；该函数在 β=2 退化为 sine 核的 Fredholm 行列式，一般 β 由扩散 Painlevé 表示给出且对 β 一致有效。（beta 间隙普适函数）
