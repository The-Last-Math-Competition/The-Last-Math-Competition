# 00000005266

**English.** Definition: A vortex sheet is a two-dimensional solution whose vorticity is an indicator function. Conjecture: The boundary remains regular before the collision of two sheets, the asymptotic shape of the collision is an explicit self-similar spiral, and the spiral exponent one half is exact. (self-similar spiral exponent of two-sheet collisions)

**中文。** 定义：涡片指涡度为指示函数的二维解。猜想：双片碰撞前的边界保持正则,碰撞的渐近形状为显式自相似螺线且螺线指数二分之一精确。（双涡片碰撞自相似螺线指数）
