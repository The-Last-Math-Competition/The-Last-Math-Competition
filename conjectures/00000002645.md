# 00000002645

**English.** Definition: The level of Jacobi forms: transformation laws on congruence subgroups. Conjecture: The level-N dependence of the dimension formula of Jacobi forms is a multiplicative Euler product, and the main term of the Euler factor at prime powers is a linear function of p; the leading coefficient is given explicitly by the conductor of the character. (level N dimension Euler product)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式的级（level）：同余子群上的变换律。猜想：级 N 的 Jacobi 形式的维数公式的 N 依赖为乘性 Euler 积且 Euler 因子在 N 为素数幂时的主项为 p 的线性函数；主项系数由特征的导子显式给出。（级 N 维数 Euler 积）
