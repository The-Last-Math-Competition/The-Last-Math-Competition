# 00000004444

**English.** Definition: The Busemann function is the direction-difference functional of LPP, and the coexistence spectrum is the set of its limit values. Conjecture: The coexistence spectrum of exponential-weight LPP is a two-valued set, and the growth of the spectrum cardinality with the direction parameter obeys an explicit logarithmic law. (Busemann two-valued spectrum and logarithmic growth)

**中文。** 定义：Busemann 函数指 LPP 的方向差泛函,共存谱指其极限值集合。猜想：指数权 LPP 的共存谱为二值集合,谱基数随方向参数的增长服从显式对数律。（Busemann 二值谱与对数增长）
