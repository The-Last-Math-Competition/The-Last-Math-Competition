# 00000008168

**English.** Definition: The nonlinear renormalization of Goldstone modes: the renormalization flow le_rg of the LE coefficients c_i of the loop renormalization of the nonlinear realization of the effective action. Conjecture: The dimension of the stabilizer manifold of the fixed point of le_rg (stable combinations of LE coefficients) is 2 (a two-dimensional stability law); the existence of the zero of the flow of the coefficient c_4 (fourth-derivative term, a nonrenormalizable cancellation) is the 1/3 coefficient coincidence (a c4 = 1/3 coincidence law); and the universality of the coincidence (agreement across all Goldstone systems) is guaranteed by the loop closure of the Ward identities (a Ward loop closure law). (Goldstone LE two-stable c4 coincidence)

**中文。** 定义：Goldstone 模式的非线性重整化：有效作用量的非线性实现的圈重整化的 LE 系数 c_i 的重整化流 le_rg。猜想：le_rg 的不动点（LE 系数的稳定组合）的稳定子流形的维数为 2（双稳维数律）；且系数 c_4（四阶导数项）的流的零点的存在（非可重整的相消）为 1/3 系数巧合（c4=1/3 巧合律）；巧合的普适性（一切 Goldstone 系统一致）由 Ward 恒等式的圈闭合（Ward 圈闭合律）保证。
