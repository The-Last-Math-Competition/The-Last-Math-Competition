# 00000007082

**English.** Definition: Equivariant Fukaya with the symplectic side of localization. Conjecture: The splitting of the equivariant category is the direct sum of the categories of fixed sets, and the weights of the sum are the weight systems of the action. (weight-system fixed-set equivariant splitting)

**中文。** 定义：等变 Fukaya：局部化的辛侧。猜想：等变的分裂：等变范畴的分裂为不动集的范畴的直和且和的权重为作用的权系。（等变不动集权系直和分裂权重）
