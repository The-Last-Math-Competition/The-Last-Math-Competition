# 00000002277

**MG-17 ★** — source: 61_鞅与随机分析.md

**English.** Definition: Transport inequalities for Lévy processes. Conjecture: The T₁/C constant of Lévy processes with bounded jumps is an explicit renormalization of the Lévy measure (Lévy transport constant).

**中文。** 定义：Lévy 过程的输运不等式。猜想：有界跳的 Lévy 的 T₁/C 常数为 Lévy 测度的显式重整(Lévy 输运常数)。
