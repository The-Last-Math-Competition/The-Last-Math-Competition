# 00000009801

**DDG-11 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Pole counts of quadrilateral meshes: the global structure and topological obstruction of quad meshes. Conjecture: The pole count of quadrilateral meshes of genus-g surfaces satisfies p = 4 - 4g plus branched-cover corrections; minimal-pole meshes are classified by an explicit combinatorial list, with the index sum per pole conserved. (quad mesh pole formula)

**中文。** 定义：四边形网格的极点计数：quad mesh 的全局结构与拓扑障碍。猜想：亏格 g 曲面四边形网格的极点个数满足 p=4−4g 加分支覆盖修正；最小极点网格的分类为显式组合列表且每张极点的指标和守恒。（quad 网格极点公式）
