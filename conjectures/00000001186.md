# 00000001186

**LT-86 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: Let m(k) denote the minimal order of a nonabelian simple group with exactly k distinct prime factors. Conjecture: m(3) = 60 (A₅), m(4) = 504 (PSL₂(8)), m(5) = 660 (PSL₂(11)); and m(k)/m(k−1) ≤ 4 for all k, with equality at k = 4.

**中文。** 定义：恰有 k 个不同素因子的非交换单群的最小阶记 m(k)。猜想：m(3)=60(A₅),m(4)=504(PSL₂(8)),m(5)=660(PSL₂(11));且序列 m(k)/m(k−1) ≤ 4 对一切 k,等号在 k=4 时取得。
