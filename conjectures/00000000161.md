**C2-08 ★△** — source: 12_组合代数与几何II.md

**English.** Conjecture: The order of a random element of GL_n(F_p): with probability tending to 1 (as p → ∞ with fixed n ≥ 2), a random A ∈ GL_n(F_p) has order divisible by a prime factor exceeding p^{n−1}/poly(n).

**中文。** 猜想：随机 GL_n(F_p) 元素的阶:以概率 → 1(p → ∞,固定 n ≥ 2),随机 A ∈ GL_n(F_p) 的阶含 > p^{n−1}/poly(n) 的素因子。
