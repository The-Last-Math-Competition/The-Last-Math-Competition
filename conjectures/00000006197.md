**English.** Definition: The defect measure is the energy-partition structure of weak solutions. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit energy partition of defect measures, with the three regimes (wave, resonant, defect) having conserved proportion sum; the conserved sum is identically the initial energy; and the propagation-speed lower bound of the defect is the speed of light, given by the local energy flux. (conserved energy-partition conversion of defect measures)

**中文。** 定义：缺陷测度：弱解的能量分割结构。猜想：换算常数为缺陷测度的能量分割显式且三模式（波、共振、缺陷）比例和守恒；守恒和恒等于初值能量；缺陷的传播速度下界为光速且由局部能量流给出。（缺陷测度能量分割守恒换算）
