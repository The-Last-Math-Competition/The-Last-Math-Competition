**English.** Definition: Focal quantities: the perturbation criterion of centers. Conjecture: The algebraic independence of focal quantities: the common zero set of the first n focal quantities is the center condition, with the ideal of conditions a complete intersection. (focal quantity common zero set complete intersection center)
