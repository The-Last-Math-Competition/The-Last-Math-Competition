# 00000001145

**English.** Definition: The multiplicity mult(δ) = k of the imaginary root δ of an affine Lie algebra is given by Kac's formula as a coefficient of the partition generating function. Conjecture: The distribution of the p-adic valuation v_p(mult(kδ)) satisfies: for random k ≤ N, {v_p}/log_p N converges to an explicit Beta(1,·) distribution on [0,1], coinciding with the valuation distribution of the partition numbers themselves.

**中文。** 定义：仿射李代数虚根 δ 的重数 mult(δ)=k 由 Kac 公式给出为分拆数生成函数系数。猜想：mult(kδ) 的 p-进赋值 v_p(mult(kδ)) 的分布满足:对随机 k≤N,{v_p}/log_p N 收敛到 [0,1] 上的 Beta(1,·) 型显式分布,与分拆数本身的赋值分布重合。
