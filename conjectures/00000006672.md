**English.** Definition: Linear programming with the LP form of transport. Conjecture: The polytope vertices are spanning trees; the criterion of nondegeneracy is full tree capacity; and the enumeration of tree structures is counted by the spanning-tree matrix-tree theorem. (spanning-tree structure of LP vertices)

**中文。** 定义：线性规划为传输的 LP 形式。猜想：多面体顶点为支撑树；且非退化的判据为树的容量满；且树结构的枚举由生成树矩阵树定理计数。（LP 顶点支撑树结构）
