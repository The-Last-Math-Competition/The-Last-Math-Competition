# 00000004415

**English.** Definition: The weak regular decomposition of hypergraphs is the block-uniformization of uniform hypergraphs. Conjecture: Its lower bound on the number of parts is superpolynomial of exponential type, exp of epsilon to the negative c with c explicit, and the lower bound is attained by random block hypergraph constructions. (superpolynomial lower bound of hypergraph weak regularity)

**中文。** 定义：超图的弱正则分解指一致超图的分块均匀化。猜想：其部分数下界为 ε 的负幂次指数型超多项式 exp 乘 1/ε 的 c 次幂,c 显式,下界由随机块超图构造达到。（超图弱正则超多项式下界）
