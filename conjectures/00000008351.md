# 00000008351

**English.** Definition: The degeneration slopes of tropical curves: the generation w_gen of edge weights of the tropicalization Trop(X_0) of central fibers of families pi: X to B. Conjecture: w_gen (the generating function of tropicalized edge weights) is the combination of flat sections of the uniformization of B (a flat section combination law); the sum of weights (total weight) is the conserved Euler characteristic of X (an Euler conservation law); the minimum nonzero weight is 1 (a unit weight law); and the correction for splitting weights (half-integral weights) is a double cover (a double cover correction law). (tropical edge weight flat Euler conservation)

**中文。** 定义：热带曲线的退化斜率：族 π:X→B 的中心纤维的热带化 Trop(X_0) 的边权生成 w_gen。猜想：w_gen（热带化的边权的生成函数）为 B 的单值化的平坦截面的组合（平坦截组合律）；且权的和（总权）= X 的 Euler 特征的守恒（Euler 守恒律）；权的极小（非零权的下界）为 1（单位权律），权的分裂（半整权）的修正为双重覆盖（双覆盖修正律）。
