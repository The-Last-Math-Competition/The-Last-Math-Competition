# 00000005851

**MR3-21 ★** — source: 180_模表示II.md

**English.** Definition: Cyclic defect groups give blocks with cyclic defect of prime-power order, that is, Brauer tree blocks. Conjecture: When the subgroup count is two, the Ext graph is a single-edged tree, the single edge given by the vanishing of the chain condition; the edge count of the tree equals the multiplicity of the exceptional character minus one; and the multiplicity of the exceptional point is of type e+1, with e the inertia index. (single-edge tree chain vanishing of cyclic defect)

**中文。** 定义：循环亏群：阶为素幂的循环亏群块（Brauer 树块）。猜想：子群数二时 Ext 图为单边树且单边的组合恒为链条件消没；树的边数等于例外特征标重数减一；例外点的重数为 e+1 型且 e 为惯性指数。（循环亏单边树链消没）
