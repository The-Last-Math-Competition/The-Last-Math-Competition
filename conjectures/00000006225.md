# 00000006225

**GT5-05 ★** — source: 193_几何范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Representable functors preserve limits, with the minimal regular cardinal. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the minimal regular cardinal and the rank jumps of limit closures are countable; the jump height is at most omega; and the minimal cardinal is invariant under limit closure, the deviation of invariance being accessibility. (countable rank-jump conversion of limit closures)

**中文。** 定义：可表示函子：保持极限的函子的可表示条件；极小正则基数。猜想：换算常数为极小正则基数的显式且极限闭包的秩跳跃可数；跳跃的高度不超过 ω；极小基数在极限封闭下不变且不变的偏差为可达性。（极限闭包秩跳跃可数换算）
