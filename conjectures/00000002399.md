# 00000002399

**FM-19 ★** — source: 65_分形几何与测度.md

**English.** Definition: Lower bounds for the dimension of harmonic measure on fractals. Conjecture: The harmonic-measure dimension is at least a constant multiple of the box dimension, the constant being one half; and the tightness of the slope is attained on angular domains. (harmonic dimension half-slope angular-domain tightness)

**中文。** 定义：分形上调和测度的维数下界。猜想：调和测度维数至少为盒维数的常数倍且常数为二分之一；且斜率的紧性为角域。（调和维数半斜率角域紧）
