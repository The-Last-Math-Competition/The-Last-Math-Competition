# 00000005437

**English.** Definition: The spectral conversion links the measure with the eigenvalues. Conjecture: The conversion of the first eigenvalue is always explicit in the escape exponent of the measure; linear for convex domains, with constant of dimension type. (linear escape-exponent conversion of the first eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：谱换算指测度与特征值的联系。猜想：首特征值的换算恒由测度逃逸指数显式；凸域时为线性且常数为维数型值。（首特征值逃逸指数线性）
