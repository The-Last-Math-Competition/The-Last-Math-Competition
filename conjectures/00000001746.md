# 00000001746

**English.** Definition: The uniform height bound h_preper(d,D) in arithmetic dynamics is the supremum of heights of preperiodic points of degree-d maps over fields of diameter D. Conjecture: h_preper ≤ c·log d·D² with c = 2^{16}; the bound is tight in log d, attained by Lattès maps.

**中文。** 定义：算术动力学的一致高度界 h_preper(d,D) 指度 d 映射在直径 D 的域上的预周期点高的上确界。猜想：h_preper ≤ c·log d·D^{2},c = 2^{16};界在 Lattès 映射达到 log d 的紧。
