# 00000001868

**English.** Definition: The first-derivative zeros of Dirichlet L-functions in [T, 2T]. Conjecture: The leading coefficient of their GUE deviation is c₁ = 1/(2π) (known for the Riemann ζ); the deviation coefficient for L is corrected by the reciprocal of the logarithm of its conductor.

**中文。** 定义：Dirichlet L 函数在 [T,2T] 的一阶导数零点。猜想：其 GUE 偏差的首项系数为 c_1 = 1/(2π)(Riemann ζ 已知);L 的偏差系数由其 conductor 的对数的倒数修正。
