# 00000001705

**English.** Definition: The word-frequency measure of an admissible substitution. Conjecture: The components of the frequency vector of any primitive uniform substitution are algebraic numbers, with denominators governed by the algebraic integrality of the eigenvectors of the substitution matrix (algebraicity of frequencies).

**中文。** 定义：可容许位移(substitution)的词频率测度。猜想：任意本原一致替换的频率向量的分量为代数数且分母的范数与替换矩阵的特征向量的代数整数性(频率代数性)。
