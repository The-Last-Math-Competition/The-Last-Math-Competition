# 00000003462

**GA3-02 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: Doubly critical graphs: k-coloring edge-critical graphs where deleting any two vertices lowers the chromatic number, with Gallai's bound the edge upper bound of critical graphs. Conjecture: The Gallai upper bound for six-doubly-critical graphs improves to (5v−2)/3, with the improved bound uniquely attained by Kostochka–Yancey extremal graphs; the classification of doubly critical and four-critical merges into one extremal family at girth at least five. (doubly critical edge count tight bound)

**中文。** 定义：双临界图：k-染色边临界图满足任意两点的删除使色数降一，Gallai 界给出临界图边数上界。猜想：六双临界图的 Gallai 上界可改进为 (5v−2)/3 且改进界由 Kostochka–Yancey 型极图唯一取得；双临界与四临界的分类在围长至少五时合并为同一极图族。（双临界边数紧界）
