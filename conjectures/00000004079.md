# 00000004079

**English.** Definition: Takens renormalization is the universal renormalization operator for critical circle maps. Conjecture: The first eigenvalue delta of the spectrum of its linearization is an algebraic number of degree at most 12, the same for all analytic families of critical circle maps. (algebraicity of the first renormalization eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：Takens 重整化指临界圆映射的普适重整化算子。猜想：其线性化谱的第一特征值 δ 为代数数且次数不超过 12,对一切解析临界圆映射族相同。（重整化第一特征值代数性）
