# 00000009033

**MFH-23 ★** — source: 286_半整权模形式.md

**English.** Definition: The Niwa lift: from half-integral to integral weight. Conjecture: The lift preserves cuspidality, and the Fourier coefficients of the kernel are combinations of spherical harmonics; and the weight of the combination is one. (Niwa spherical harmonic kernel cuspidality)

**中文。** 定义：Niwa 提升为半权到整权。猜想：提升保持尖性且核的 Fourier 系数为球谐组合；且组合的权为一。（Niwa 球谐核保尖）
