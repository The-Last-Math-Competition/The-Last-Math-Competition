# 00000009982

**NCR-12 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Invariants of derived Morita equivalence: separation of equivalence classes of perfect complex categories. Conjecture: Two algebras are derived Morita equivalent if and only if isomorphisms of the K-theory spectrum and the Hochschild cohomology ring are compatible on the Tate spectrum; the invariants are complete, and the minimal separating family is K_0 plus the action of HH^1. (derived Morita invariant completeness)

**中文。** 定义：导 Morita 等价的不变量：完美复形范畴的等价类分离。猜想：两个代数导 Morita 等价当且仅当 K 理论谱与 Hochschild 上同调环的同构在 Tate 谱上相容；不变量完备且分离的最小族为 K_0 加 HH^1 的作用。（导 Morita 不变量完备）
