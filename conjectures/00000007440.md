# 00000007440

**English.** Definition: Rado numbers with partition regularity. Conjecture: The partition-regularity criterion of equations holds, and the combination of the criterion is the column sums. (column-summed Rado partition criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Rado 数：划分正则。猜想：Rado 的列条件：方程的划分正则判据且据的组合为列和。（Rado 列和方程划分正则判据组合数列条件）
