# 00000006223

**English.** Definition: Accessibility is the presentation rank of objects, the size of needed presentations, related to representability. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit descent of presentation ranks and the rank of representable categories is bounded; the descent step count is finite with step one; and the minimal rank is attained at the limit completion, with value a regular cardinal. (bounded descent conversion of presentation ranks)

**中文。** 定义：可达性：范畴中对象的展示秩（生成所需展示的规模）与可表示性的关系。猜想：换算常数为展示秩的显式下降且可表示范畴的秩有界；下降步数为有限且步长为 1；秩的极小值在极限完备化处达到且达到值为正则基数。（展示秩有界下降换算常数）
