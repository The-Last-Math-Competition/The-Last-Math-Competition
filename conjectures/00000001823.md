**AG2-13 ★** — source: 46_代数几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The Kuznetsov component is the orthogonal complement in the semiorthogonal decomposition of a derived category. Conjecture: The Hochschild cohomology of the Kuznetsov component of Gushel–Mukai fourfolds is C ⊕ C[2] (nontrivial only in degree 2); the dimension of the center is independent of the specific deformation parameters of the GM variety.

**中文。** 定义：Kuznetsov 分量指导出范畴的半正交分解中的正交补分量。猜想：Gushel–Mukai 四维簇的 Kuznetsov 分量的 Hochschild 上同调为中心 C⊕C[2](不平凡仅在度为 2);中心的维数不依赖于 GM 簇的具体形变参数。
