# 00000005431

**HM2-21 ★** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: The hitting time is the moment Brownian motion first reaches the boundary. Conjecture: The conversion constant of the joint distribution is given explicitly by the kernel function, one after normalization, with closed form in the spherical case. (unit conversion constant of hitting joint kernels)

**中文。** 定义：首中时间指布朗首次到达边界的时刻。猜想：联合分布的换算常数由核函数显式给出,常数为一旦归一且球面情形闭式。（首中联合核换算常一）
