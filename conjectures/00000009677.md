**English.** Definition: Kontsevich-Zagier periods are values of absolutely convergent integrals of algebraic integrands over algebraic domains with algebraic coefficients; the structure of the period field P is unknown. Conjecture: 1/pi is not a period; the multiplicative structure of the period field is a graded lattice: weight-k periods are closed under Q-linear combinations, distinct-weight quotient lattices are nontrivially finitely generated, and quotient generators are explicitly listable from the de Rham cohomology of curves. (period field graded lattice)

**中文。** 定义：Kontsevich–Zagier 周期为代数系数代数域上的代数被积函数的绝对收敛积分值；周期域 P 的结构未知。猜想：1/π 不是周期；周期域的乘性结构为分次格：权 k 周期在 Q 线性组合下封闭且不同权商格为非平凡有限生成，商格的生成元可由曲线的 de Rham 上同调显式列出。（周期域分次格）
