# 00000004886

**English.** Definition: A Ramsey cardinal is a large cardinal with the strong partition property. Conjecture: Its minimal combinatorial equivalent is an explicit family of coloring sentences, the family's provability differs from a measurable cardinal by exactly one layer, and the formulation is closed and redundancy-free. (combinatorial equivalent family of Ramsey cardinals)

**中文。** 定义：拉姆齐基数指具强划分性质的大基数。猜想：其最小组合等价为显式着色语句族,族的可证性与可测基数恰差一层且表述封闭无冗余。（拉姆齐基数组合等价族）
