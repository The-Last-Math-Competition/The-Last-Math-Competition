# 00000008264

**English.** Definition: The normalization of signatures of rough paths (iterated integrals): the correction sig_norm to the Chen identity of signatures of geometric rough paths. Conjecture: The correction for sig_norm (translation invariance of the signature) is the cancellation of the first term (a first-term cancellation law); the explicit expectation of the signature (Gaussian rough paths) is the symplectic symmetry of quadratic Wiener functionals (a symplectic symmetry formula); and in the inverse problem of signatures (path recovery from the signature), the loss (equivalence classes of different paths with the same signature) is tree-like paths (a tree-like law), the metric on the quotient space (the signature metric) being bi-Lipschitz equivalent to the 1-variation metric of paths (a bi-Lipschitz equivalence law). (signature translation tree-like bi-Lipschitz quotient)

**中文。** 定义：粗糙路径的签名（iterated integrals）的重整化：几何粗糙路径的签名的 Chen 恒等式的正规化修正 sig_norm。猜想：sig_norm（签名的移位（translation）不变性）的修正为签名的首项的消去（首项消去律）；且签名的期望（Gaussian 粗糙路径）的显式为二次 Wiener 泛函的辛对称（辛对称公式）；签名的逆问题（由签名恢复路径）的损失（同签名不同路径的等价类）为树状循环（tree-like 律），等价类的商空间的度量（签名度量）与路径的 1-变差度量的双 Lipschitz 等价（双 Lipschitz 等价律）。
