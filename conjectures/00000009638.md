# 00000009638

**SMA-28 ★** — source: 306_Smale空间与符号动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Multiple mixing: convergence of order-k correlations; Bowen proved equilibrium states are mixing of all orders. Conjecture: k-fold mixing of equilibrium states of mixing SFTs decays as a t^{-k alpha} power law, with exponent alpha explicitly given by the Holder exponent of the potential; arbitrary accelerated decay is achievable by flattening the potential, with no universal obstruction to exponential mixing; and the supremum of the exponent alpha is exactly the Holder conjugate exponent of the potential. (k-fold mixing power law)

**中文。** 定义：多重混合：混合阶数 k 的关联收敛；Bowen 证明均衡态全阶混合。猜想：混合 SFT 均衡态的 k 重混合按 t^{−kα} 幂律衰减，指数 α 由势的 Hölder 指数显式给出；任意加速衰减率可通过势的平坦化实现且无指数混合的普适障碍。(k 重混合幂律)；且指数 α 的上确界恰为势的 Hölder 共轭指数。
