# 00000001703

**English.** Definition: The ergodic optimization measure of the beta-transformation is the invariant measure maximizing ∫x dμ. Conjecture: This measure is the Sturmian measure whose slope is the beta-conjugate of β; the continued-fraction expansion of the slope is generated explicitly by digit reversal of the Rényi beta-expansion of β.

**中文。** 定义：β-变换的遍历优化测度指最大化 ∫x dμ 的不变测度。猜想：该测度为以 β-共轭数为 slope 的 Sturmian 测度;slope 的连分数展开由 β 的 Rényi β-expansion 的逐位反转显式生成。
