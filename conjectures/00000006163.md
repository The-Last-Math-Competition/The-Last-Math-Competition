# 00000006163

**English.** Definition: The transfer formula transfers logarithmic singular terms to coefficient asymptotics. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit polynomial basis of logarithmic singular terms with exact coefficients in the standard basis; the dimension of the basis is the multiplicity of the dominant pole; and the contribution of nondominant poles decays geometrically by modulus ratio, the decay constant being the ratio of the two moduli. (complete coefficient conversion of the logarithmic polynomial basis)

**中文。** 定义：转移公式：对数奇异项到系数渐近的传递。猜想：换算常数为对数奇异项的显式多项式基且标准基下系数精确；基的维数为主导极点重数；非主导极点的贡献按模比几何衰减且衰减常数为两模之比。（对数多项式基完全系数换算）
