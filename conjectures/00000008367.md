# 00000008367

**English.** Definition: The ramified strengthening of tropical Riemann-Roch: the remainder rr_rem of the strengthened version of RR for metric graphs. Conjecture: rr_rem (vanishing of the RR remainder) holds on 2-edge-connected graphs (a two-edge remainder vanishing law); the minimal graph with nonzero remainder (maximal remainder) is the banana graph (a banana graph law); the spectrum of remainders (over all graphs) is the two-value set {0,1} (a two-value spectrum law); and the complete classification of winning strategies of the strengthened chip-firing game is by seed sets (a seed classification law). (tropical Riemann-Roch banana two-value seed)

**中文。** 定义：热带 Riemann–Roch 的增强（分歧）：度量图的 RR 的加强版的余项 rr_rem。猜想：rr_rem（RR 的余项的消失）在 2-边连通图成立（2-边消余律）；且余项的非零（最大余）的极小图为香蕉图（香蕉图律）；余项的谱（一切图的余项的值）= {0,1} 的二值（二值谱律）；加强 RR（chip-firing 的增强）的博弈的必胜策略的完全分类为种子集（种子分类律）。
