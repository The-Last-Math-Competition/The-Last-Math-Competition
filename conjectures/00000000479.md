# 00000000479

**SA-79 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The perfect-matching mosaic of Aztec diamond domino tilings stratifies the 2^{n(n+1)/2} matchings by the number of NW-SE main-diagonal lozenges they contain. Conjecture: The 2-adic valuation of the number of matchings in the k-th stratum (with exactly k main-diagonal lozenges) vanishes when k ≡ 0 mod 4, and equals 1 otherwise; the pattern has period 4.

**中文。** 定义：Aztec 金刚图 domino tiling 的完美匹配马赛克指把 2^{n(n+1)/2} 个匹配按其含 NW-SE 主对角菱形数分层。猜想：第 k 层(恰含 k 个主对角菱形)的匹配数的 2-rank(即该数 mod 2 的赋值结构)在 k≡0 mod 4 时消失,其余情形 2-rank = 1,该模式周期为 4。
