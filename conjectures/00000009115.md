**English.** Definition: Projective polyhedra: projection images of convex bodies. Conjecture: The square root of cross-section areas satisfies Brunn-Minkowski type concavity; and equality in the concavity is homothety. (projection Brunn-Minkowski square-root concavity)

**中文。** 定义：射影多面体为凸体的投影像。猜想：截面积的平方根满足 Brunn–Minkowski 型凹性；且凹性的等号为同位相似。（投影 BM 平方根凹）
