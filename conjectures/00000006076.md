# 00000006076

**English.** Definition: A strict saddle is a saddle with nondegenerate negative curvature. Conjecture: The escape window of saddle neighborhoods is controlled by the curvature ratio, with deterministic escape inside the window, and the escape rate is exponential. (curvature-ratio exponential escape window of strict saddles)

**中文。** 定义：严格鞍为负曲率非退化的鞍点。猜想：鞍邻域的逃逸窗由曲率比控制且窗内确定性远离；且远离的速率为指数。（严格鞍曲率比指数远离窗）
