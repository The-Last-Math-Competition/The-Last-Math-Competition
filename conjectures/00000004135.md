# 00000004135

**FA2-15 ★** — source: 123_高等数理逻辑_强制法与公理.md

**English.** Definition: The asterisk axiom is Woodin's P_max extremal axiom. Conjecture: The consistency-strength gap between the asterisk axiom and a supercompact cardinal is exactly one layer: the passage from the supercompact world to the fragment of the asterisk axiom can be achieved by a single Woodin cardinal plus stationary reflection, and the gap cannot be compressed further. (asterisk-supercompact strength gap)

**中文。** 定义：星号公理指 Woodin 的 P_max 极值公理。猜想：星号公理与超紧基数的一致强度差恰为一层:超紧世界到星号的片断可由单 Woodin 加平稳反射达到,差额不可再压缩。（星号-超紧强度差额）
