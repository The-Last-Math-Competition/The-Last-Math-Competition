# 00000004157

**English.** Definition: The optimal constant of the VMVT is the main-term coefficient of the asymptotic of the Vinogradov mean value J_{s,k}(X). Conjecture: The main-term coefficient always equals the number of diagonal solutions, given by an explicit combination of factorials, and the range of validity is exactly s <= k(k+1). (diagonal count of the VMVT main term)

**中文。** 定义：VMVT 的最优常数指 Vinogradov 中值 J_{s,k}(X) 渐近式的主项系数。猜想：主项系数恒等于对角解的个数,由阶乘的显式组合给出,适用范围恰为 s ≤ k(k+1)。（VMVT 主项对角计数）
