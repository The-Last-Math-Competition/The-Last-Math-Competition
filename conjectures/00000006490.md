**English.** Definition: Concentration and superconcentration are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two processes with identical Lipschitz-functional concentration but different superconcentration, and the separation is realized by an explicit coupling pair with the same concentration but different correlation. (same concentration-different superconcentration coupling separation)

**中文。** 定义：集中与超集中指两层。猜想：存在两过程的一切利普希茨泛函集中相同而超集中性质不同,分离由同集中异相关的显式耦合对实现。（集中同-超集中异耦合分离）
