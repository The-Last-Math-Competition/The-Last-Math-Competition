# 00000002849

**AS-19 ★** — source: 80_代数统计与张量分解的几何.md

**English.** Definition: Geometric contraction of tensor networks: the geometric characterization of algorithmic complexity. Conjecture: Complexity rigidity of tensor network contraction: the minimal exponential cost of contraction is exponential in the treewidth of the network graph, and optimal contraction orders correspond one-to-one with treewidth decompositions. (network contraction treewidth rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：张量网络的几何收缩：算法复杂度的几何刻画。猜想：张量网络收缩的复杂度刚性：收缩的最小指数代价为网络图的树宽的指数且最优收缩顺序与 treewidth 分解一一对应。（网络收缩 treewidth 刚性）
