# 00000007335

**English.** Definition: Nonstandard functional analysis with the infinitesimal side of functionals. Conjecture: The infinitesimal extension of linear functionals holds, and the domain of the extension is the internal dual. (internal-dual nonstandard Hahn-Banach)

**中文。** 定义：非标准泛函分析：泛函的无穷小侧。猜想：非标准的 Hahn–Banach：线性泛函的无穷小延拓且拓的域为内部对偶。（非标准 Hahn-Banach 内部对偶线性泛函无穷小延拓域）
