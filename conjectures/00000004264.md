**GT2-24 ★** — source: 127_数学物理II_规范理论的数学.md

**English.** Definition: A coset pair is the pair of a vertex algebra with the quotient structure of a subalgebra. Conjecture: The coset reconstruction constant is an explicit rational function of the conformal weight difference, the reconstruction is unique when the weight difference is an integer, and it has exactly two solutions at half-integers. (two-valuedness of coset reconstruction constants)

**中文。** 定义：coset 对指顶点代数与其子代数的商结构对。猜想：coset 重构常数为共形权差的显式有理式,重构在权差为整数时唯一,在半整数时恰有二解。（coset 重构常数二值性）
