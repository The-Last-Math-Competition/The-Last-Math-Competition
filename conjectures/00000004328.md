# 00000004328

**English.** Definition: The quasi-period is the ratio of the real period and the imaginary period of an elliptic curve. Conjecture: For totally real discriminants up to 50, the quasi-periods of CM elliptic curves are given by closed forms in Gamma values of class invariants, and the first non-closed-form value appears where the discriminant is 1 modulo 8. (boundary of the quasi-period closed-form table)

**中文。** 定义：准周期指椭圆曲线实周期与虚周期的比。猜想：全实域判别式不超过 50 的 CM 椭圆曲线准周期由类不变量的 Gamma 值闭式给出,首个非闭式值出现在判别式模 8 余 1 处。（准周期闭式表的边界）
