**English.** Definition: The anisotropic curvature is the weighted mean curvature. Conjecture: The surface tensor is always given explicitly by the dual norm; the first fundamental-form table is closed; and convexity is preserved. (dual-norm tensor of anisotropic curvature)

**中文。** 定义：各向异性曲率指加权平均曲率。猜想：表面张量恒由对偶范数显式给出；第一基本形表封闭且凸性保持。（各向异性对偶范数张量）
