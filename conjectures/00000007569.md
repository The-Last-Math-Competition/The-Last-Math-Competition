**English.** Definition: The fundamental group as the scaling group of the index. Conjecture: The attainable spectrum of the group is complete in the index-closure group; the topology of the closure is the projective limit; and the projective-limit topology is the inverse limit of the index lattice. (projective-closed index attainable spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：基本群为指标的缩放群。猜想：群的可达谱在指标闭包群完全；且闭包的拓扑为射影极限；且射影极限的拓扑为指标格逆极限。（指标可达谱射影闭包）
