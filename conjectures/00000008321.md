# 00000008321

**English.** Definition: The difference set D = L without M between the Lagrange spectrum L (closure of all limsup values of asymptotic fractions of irrationals) and the Markov spectrum M: its Hausdorff dimension dim_D. Conjecture: The spectral structure of D: the initial segments of L and M coincide (below 3 is the finite Markov segment), and the first bifurcation value nu* after 3 (the first value where L differs from M) is transcendental, a limit of quadratic irrationals (a transcendental bifurcation law); and the Hausdorff dimension of D is the half-dimension 1/2 + o(1) (a half-dimension conjecture); the lower bound on dim_D of 0.54... (the Moreira explicit bound) improves along the Hall ray path (a Hall ray path). (Lagrange-Markov transcendental bifurcation half-dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Lagrange 谱 L（无理数的渐近分数的 limsup 的全体值的闭包）与 Markov 谱 M 的差集 D=L\M 的 Hausdorff 维数 dim_D。猜想：D 的谱结构：L∩M 的初始段重合（<3 部分为 Markov 有限段），3 之后的第一个分叉值 ν*（L≠M 的第一个值）的超越性：ν* 为二次无理数的极限的超越数（超越分叉律）；且 D 的 Hausdorff 维数为 1/2+o(1) 的半维数（半维数猜想）；dim_D 的下界为 0.54… 的 Moreira 显式（Moreira 下界）的改进路径为 Hall 射线（Hall 射线路径）。
