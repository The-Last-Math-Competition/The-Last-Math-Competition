**KH3-15 ★** — source: 267_纽结不变量与低维拓扑III.md

**English.** Definition: A cable knot is a knot embedded along the tubular neighborhood of another knot. Conjecture: The hyperbolic volume of a cable knot is a Dehn-type exponential sum of the volume of the parent knot; the sum is a bivariate polynomial in the cabling parameters (p,q) with a closed form at (2,3). (cable volume polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：电缆结指沿结的管状邻域嵌入的另一结。猜想：电缆结的双曲体积为母结体积的 Dehn 型指数和，该和为电缆参数 (p,q) 的二元多项式且在 (2,3) 处有闭式。（电缆体积多项式）
