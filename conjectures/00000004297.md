# 00000004297

**English.** Definition: Pure-injective refers to pure-injective groups, and the Cantor-Bendixson rank is the hierarchy of isolated points of the derived limit. Conjecture: The uniqueness of the pure-injective decomposition is read off explicitly from the CB rank, the rank being a convergent transfinite sum of isolated-point multiplicities. (CB rank formula of pure-injective decompositions)
