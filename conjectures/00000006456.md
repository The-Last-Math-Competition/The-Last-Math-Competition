**English.** Definition: Precision with the numerical certification scheme. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the certification scheme and the certified error bound of interval arithmetic closes; the closed bound is linear in the interval width; and the failure mode of certification is overlap, the detection of overlap being symbolic computation. (interval certified error-bound conversion constant)
