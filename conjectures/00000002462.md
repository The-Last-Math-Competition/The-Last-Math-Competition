# 00000002462

**English.** Definition: The Markov free topological group is the topologized free group on a space. Conjecture: The cardinality of the Stone–Čech remainder is double exponential; and the combination of the exponent is the number of ultrafilters. (free group Stone-Cech ultrafilter double exponential)

**中文。** 定义：Markov 自由拓扑群为空间上的自由群拓扑化。猜想：Stone–Čech 剩余的基数为双指数；且指数的组合为超滤子数。（自由群 Stone-Cech 超滤双指数）
