**GBR-18 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: subcategory lattice: torsion class and lattice corresponds to of wide subcategory. Conjecture: torsion class lattice of finite dimension algebra is distributive lattice if and only if to partial order of quiver is minimal counterexample of forest partial order; distributive is three vertex to and length bounded of distributive approximation of lattice. (torsion lattice distributive)

**中文。** 定义：宽子范畴格：torsion 类与 wide 子范畴的格对应。猜想：有限维代数的 torsion 类格为分配格当且仅当箭图的定向路偏序为森林偏序；分配性的极小反例为三顶点定向圈且格的分配逼近的长度有界。（torsion 格分配判据）
