# 00000005180

**English.** Definition: K-theory and object spectra are two layers of categorical invariants. Conjecture: There exist two stable categories with isomorphic K-theory spectra but non-isomorphic compact-object spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit construction of phantom pairs. (same K-theory-different object spectra phantom separation)

**中文。** 定义：K 理论与对象谱指两层范畴不变量。猜想：存在两稳定范畴的 K 理论谱同构而紧对象谱不同构,分离由幻影对的显式构造实现。（K 理论同-对象谱异幻影分离）
