# 00000006604

**DC3-24 ★** — source: 205_代数几何IX_导出范畴II.md

**English.** Definition: Deformation is the deformation structure of the category. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the conversion of deformation dimensions and the dimension of the second-order group equals the deformation dimension; the conversion is one; and the obstruction to deformation is the Gerstenhaber bracket, the vanishing of the bracket being equivalent to smoothness. (unit conversion of second-order deformation dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：形变：范畴的形变结构。猜想：换算常数为形变维数的换算且二阶群的维数等于形变维；换算常一；形变的障碍为 Gerstenhaber 括号且括号的消没等价于光滑。（二阶群形变维常一换算）
