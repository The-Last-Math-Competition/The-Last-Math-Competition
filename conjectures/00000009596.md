# 00000009596

**MSH-16 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The classification of tangent cones of the singular set for n = 8 minimal hypersurfaces: whether new cone types exist beyond the Simons cone and hyperplanes is central to regularity theory. Conjecture: Singularity tangent cones are only of hyperplane and Simons cone product types; the singularity density is quantized on the half-integer lattice {1, 3/2, 2, ...}, and density 5/2 singularities do not exist. (tangent cone dichotomy and density lattice)

**中文。** 定义：n=8 极小超曲面的奇点集的切锥分类：Simons 锥与超平面外是否还有新锥型是正则性理论的核心。猜想：奇点切锥只有超平面与 Simons 锥及其乘积型两类；奇点密度量子化到半整数格 {1, 3/2, 2, …}，密度 5/2 的奇点不存在。（切锥二分与密度格）
