**English.** Definition: The local Brauer group is the Brauer group of a local field, and the invariant map is its canonical isomorphism to the quotient group. Conjecture: The kernel of the quadratic explicit map is the dual of the second-class group, the dual is realized explicitly by the kernel of the Hilbert symbol and is finite. (dual of the quadratic kernel of the local invariant)

**中文。** 定义：局部 Brauer 群指局部域的 Brauer 群,不变量映射指其到商群的典范同构。猜想：二次显式映射的核为二阶类群的对偶,对偶由希尔伯特符号的核显式实现且有限。（局部不变量二阶核对偶）
