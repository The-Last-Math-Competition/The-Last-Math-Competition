**English.** Definition: The infinitesimal rigidity of a Weinstein manifold means that the symplectic cohomology determines the homotopy type. Conjecture: The first counterexample to rigidity appears in dimension 6, as an explicit pair of Milnor fibers with isomorphic SH rings but non-homotopic manifolds. (first counterexample dimension 6 of Weinstein rigidity)
