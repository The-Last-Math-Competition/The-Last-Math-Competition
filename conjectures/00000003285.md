# 00000003285

**English.** Definition: q-hypergeometric series: the q-analogues of basic hypergeometrics. Conjecture: The q-Kummer solution system: the monomial transformations of the finite solution system of q-hypergeometrics are R-matrix elements of quantum groups, with the three-term relations of the elements the six-vertex degeneration of Yang–Baxter. (q-Kummer R matrix six-vertex degeneration)

**中文。** 定义：q-超几何级数：基本超几何的 q-类比。猜想：q-Kummer 解系：q-超几何的有限解系的单项式变换为量子群的 R 矩阵元且元的三项关系为 Yang–Baxter 的六顶点退化。（q-Kummer R 矩阵六顶点退化）
