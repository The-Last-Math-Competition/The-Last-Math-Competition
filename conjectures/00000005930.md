# 00000005930

**KZ2-10 △** — source: 183_KPZII.md

**English.** Definition: Scaling exponents and the limit kernel are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two models with identical scaling exponents but different limit kernels, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of directional and transversal noise. (same exponents-different kernel noise separation)

**中文。** 定义：标度指数与极限核指两层。猜想：存在两模型的一切标度指数相同而极限核不同,分离由定向与横向噪声的显式对实现。（指数同-极限核异噪声分离）
