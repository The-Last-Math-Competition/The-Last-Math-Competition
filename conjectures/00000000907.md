# 00000000907

**English.** Definition: The Amitsur complex is the categorical isomorphism structure of the standard invariants of a subfactor. Conjecture: The standard-invariant triple completely determines the weak homotopy class; counterexamples to iceberg completeness occur only in infinite depth; and completeness in finite depth is verified by a flatness criterion. (standard invariants weak homotopy completeness)

**中文。** 定义：Amitsur 复合为子因子标准不变量的范畴同构结构。猜想：标准不变量三元组完全决定弱同痕类；且冰山完全性的反例仅出现在无限深度情形；且该完全性在有限深度情形由平坦性判据验证。（标准不变量弱同痕完全）
