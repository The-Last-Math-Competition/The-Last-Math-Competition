**English.** Definition: Nonsymmetric Macdonald polynomials as asymmetric extensions. Conjecture: The combinatorial model of nonsymmetric Macdonald polynomials holds, and the lattice of the model is the affine permutation. (affine-permutation nonsymmetric lattice)
