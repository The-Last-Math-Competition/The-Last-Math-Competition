# 00000004473

**English.** Definition: The breakpoints of the y-variables of a cluster variety correspond to the break-point counts of the image of the tropicalization map and the discriminant factors of the F-polynomials. Conjecture: The breakpoint count equals the number of discriminant factors, each factor contributing exactly one breakpoint; the factor count of Markov-type discriminants grows logarithmically in the rank with an explicit growth rate; the discreteness of the breakpoints is controlled by the difference of leading exponents of the y-variables, and the exponent differences lie on the integer lattice. (cluster tropical discriminant counting)

**中文。** 定义：cluster 簇的 y 变量折点：tropical 化映射像的折点个数与 F 多项式判别式因子的对应。猜想：折点计数等于判别式因子的个数且每个因子恰贡献一个折点；Markov 型判别式的因子数随秩按对数增长且增长率为显式常数；折点的离散度由 y 变量首项指数差控制且指数差落在整数格上。（cluster tropical 判别式计数）
