**English.** Definition: A brunnian link is a link that becomes trivial upon removing any component. Conjecture: The infimum v_m of hyperbolic volumes of m-component brunnian links is a strictly increasing explicit rational sequence with linear growth. (infimum sequence of brunnian volumes)

**中文。** 定义：brunnian 链环指去掉任一分量即平凡的链环。猜想：m 分量 brunnian 链环的双曲体积下确界 v_m 为严格递增的显式有理序列且增长线性。（brunnian 体积下确界序列）
