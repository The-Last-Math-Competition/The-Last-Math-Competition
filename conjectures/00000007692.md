# 00000007692

**English.** Definition: The Heesch number h(P) of a convex polygon P is the maximum number of coronas that can surround P compatibly, with h(P)=infinity when P tiles the plane. Conjecture: Heesch numbers are unbounded, that is for each k there exists a polygon P_k (convexity unrestricted) with h(P_k)>=k; and the minimal number of sides of a simply connected non-convex P_k with h>=k is 4k+3. (unbounded Heesch numbers)

**中文。** 定义：凸多边形 P 的 Heesch 数 h(P) 为围绕 P 可逐层紧贴相容冠层的最大层数；若可无限延伸则 h(P)=∞（此时 P 铺砌平面）。猜想：Heesch 数无界，即对每个 k 存在凸性不受限的多边形 P_k 使 h(P_k)≥k；且在非凸多边形单连通区域内达到 h≥k 的 P_k 的最小边数为 4k+3。
