**English.** Definition: Meanshift clustering of persistence diagrams as barcode grouping. Conjecture: Barcode-cluster centroids exist and centroid stability is controlled by Frechet variation; and the convexity of the variation is the log-concavity of the measure. (Frechet-stable barcode centroids)

**中文。** 定义：持续图的 Meanshift 聚类为条码分组。猜想：条码聚类的质心存在且质心的稳定性由 Fréchet 变差控制；且变差的凸性为测度的对数凹。（条码质心 Frechet 稳定）
