**English.** Definition: The Aubry-Andre model is the one-dimensional Schroedinger operator with quasiperiodic potential. Conjecture: The critical statistics at the self-dual transition is of critical Poisson type; and with interactions a mobility edge appears whose boundary curve is an analytic function of energy and strength. (Aubry-Andre self-dual critical statistics)

**中文。** 定义：Aubry–André 模型指准周期势的一维薛定谔算子。猜想：自对偶点转变的临界统计为临界 Poisson 型；加入相互作用后出现迁移率边且边界曲线为能量与强度的解析函数。（AA 自对偶临界统计）
