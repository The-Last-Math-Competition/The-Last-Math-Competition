# 00000002065

**UA-15 ★** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: The classifying topos of a local group G is the sheaf topos of BG. Conjecture: The spectrum of geometric morphisms of the sheafified model equals the torsion classes of discrete G-principal bundles (beyond the known point sets, the topological type of the spectrum is explicitly a quotient of Stone spaces).

**中文。** 定义：局部群 G 的分类拓扑斯指 BG 的层拓扑斯。猜想：层化模型的几何态射的谱 = 离散 G-主丛的挠类(除已知点集外,谱的拓扑型显式为 Stone 空间的商)。
