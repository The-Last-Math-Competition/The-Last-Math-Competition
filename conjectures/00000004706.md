# 00000004706

**English.** Definition: The Furstenberg-Zimmer structure theorem is the tower decomposition of ergodic systems. Conjecture: The minimal number of tower layers equals the minimal interleaving degree of the uniform distribution of multiple averages, the identity is explicit, and the construction can be carried out uniformly. (FZ tower-layer-interleaving identity)

**中文。** 定义：Furstenberg–Zimmer 结构定理指遍历系统的塔分解。猜想：塔的最小层数等于多重均值均匀分布的最小交错度,等式显式且构造可一致完成。（FZ 塔层数交错度等式）
