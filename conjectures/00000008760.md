# 00000008760

**English.** Definition: Z/2-graded supergeometry. Conjecture: The 2-torsion part of the super Brauer group is always given by the spinor-norm kernel of Clifford algebra classes; and the rank of the kernel is the number of real places. (super Brauer spinor norm kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Z/2 分次的超几何。猜想：超 Brauer 群的 2-挠部分恒由 Clifford 代数类的旋量范数核给出；且核的秩为实位置的个数。（超 Brauer 旋量范数核）
