# 00000000521

**CA-21 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The alternating Betti sum A_j = Σ_i (−1)^i β_{i,j}(I) of a monomial ideal I. Conjecture: For all edge ideals, the sign of {A_j} changes exactly once as j crosses reg(I), and |A_j| is monotonically nondecreasing up to that point; this gives a combinatorial characterization of the graded Lefschetz property for edge ideals.

**中文。** 定义：单项式理想 I 的 Betti 交替和 A_j = Σ_i (−1)^i β_{i,j}(I)。猜想：对一切边理想,序列 {A_j} 的符号恰在 j 越过 reg(I) 时改变一次,且 |A_j| 在该点之前单调不降;这给出边理想的分次 Lefschetz 性质的一个组合等价刻画。
