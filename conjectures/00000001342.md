# 00000001342

**AN2-12 ★** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Definition: Let N₋(q) denote the number of eigenvalues of the Laplace operator below 1/4 on the newspace of Γ₀(q) (Selberg's eigenvalue conjecture asserts it is always 0). Conjecture: N₋(q) ≪ q^{ε} for every ε > 0; i.e., within the narrow window where Selberg's conjecture could fail, the number of exceptional eigenvalues is subpolynomial.

**中文。** 定义：Γ₀(q) 的 newspace 上 Laplace 算子小于 1/4 的特征值个数记 N₋(q)(Selberg 特征值猜想断言恒为 0)。猜想：N₋(q) ≪ q^{ε} 对一切 ε>0;即在 Selberg 猜想可能失败的窄缝内,例外特征值个数亚多项式。
