**English.** Definition: The interchange limit is the scaling limit of interchange variables. Conjecture: The two-dimensional torus limit is always the explicit expression of the representation dimensions of the interchange algebra, of Brownian type for the uniform measure. (representation-dimension limit of two-dimensional interchange)
