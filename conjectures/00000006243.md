**English.** Definition: Unbounded classification is the classification structure without size restrictions. Conjecture: The failure points of the reflection principle are localized and the conflicts close, the kernel of the closure being the inaccessible tower. (inaccessible-conflict reflection failure tower)

**中文。** 定义：无界分类为无尺寸限制的分类结构。猜想：反射原理的失效点定位且冲突封闭；且封闭的核为不可达塔。（不可达冲突反射失效不可达塔）
