# 00000002598

**English.** Definition: The Bruhat order: the reflection-subword order of a Coxeter group. Conjecture: The Z-polynomial of a Bruhat interval is the generating function of feasible-word counts of the interval; the extremal structure of feasible words gives nonnegative combinatorial interpretations of the first and last coefficients of Z. (Bruhat Z polynomial combinatorics)

**中文。** 定义：Bruhat 序：Coxeter 群的反射子字序。猜想：Bruhat 区间的 Z-多项式为区间的可行字计数的生成函数；可行字的极值结构给出 Z 的首末系数的非负组合解释。（Bruhat Z 多项式组合）
