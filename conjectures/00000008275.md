# 00000008275

**SFL-15 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The two-timescale step sizes of stochastic approximation (RM): the optimal ratio two_scale of fast and slow scales. Conjecture: two_scale (the optimal fast-slow ratio) allows epsilon_slow/epsilon_fast = o(1), arbitrarily slow (an arbitrary slowness law); the explicit optimal (variance-minimizing) ratio is the square of the noise ratio of the fast variable (a noise ratio square law); the order of the bias correction of the two-scale scheme (higher-order correction of averaging) is the 1.5 power of epsilon (a 1.5 correction order law); and Poissonized averaging realizing the correction halves the variance (a halving law). (two-timescale noise ratio square averaging)

**中文。** 定义：随机逼近（RM）的步长的双时间尺度：快慢尺度的比 ε 的最优比 two_scale。猜想：two_scale（快慢的最优比）= ε_slow/ε_fast = o(1) 的任意慢（任意慢律）；且最优（方差最小）的比率的显式为快变量的噪声比的平方（噪声比平方律）；双尺度的偏差修正（平均化的高阶修正）的阶为 ε 的 1.5 次（1.5 修正阶律）；修正的实现（泊坛化的平均）的方差减半（减半律）。
