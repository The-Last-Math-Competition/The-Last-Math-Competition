# 00000000295

**English.** Definition: The structure (R, +, ×, ζ) is the real field expanded by the Riemann zeta function. Conjecture: The first-order theory of this structure is undecidable; the undecidability is settled through first-order definability of the prime spectrum. (zeta prime spectrum definability undecidability)

**中文。** 定义：结构 (R,+,×,ζ) 指带 Riemann zeta 函数的实数结构。猜想：该结构的一阶理论不可判定；不可判定的核为素数谱的一阶可定义性。（zeta 素数谱定义不可判定）
