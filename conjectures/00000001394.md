# 00000001394

**English.** Definition: The E₂ page of the Adams–Novikov spectral sequence. Conjecture: The complete computation of the group structure of E₂^{s,t} in the range t − s ≤ 100 is given by combinations of holomorphic modular forms of elliptic curves (the modular-form bridge).

**中文。** 定义：Adams–Novikov 谱序列的 E₂ 页。猜想：E₂^{s,t} 的群结构在 t − s ≤ 100 的范围的完全计算由椭圆曲线的全纯模形式的组合给出(模形式桥)。
