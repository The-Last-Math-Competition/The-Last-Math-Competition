# 00000004977

**English.** Definition: The root mean square error is the root of the mean squared error. Conjecture: The optimal multilevel constant at convergence rate four is an explicit bilinear expression of the bias and variance rates, optimal at smoothness index four and matching the single-level lower bound. (bilinear optimal constant of multilevel)

**中文。** 定义：均方根误差指平方误差均值的根。猜想：收敛率四的多级最优常数为偏差与方差率的显式双线性式,在光滑指标四处最优且匹配单级下界。（多级双线性最优常数）
