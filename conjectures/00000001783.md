# 00000001783

**English.** Definition: The log-term coefficient of the Fefferman expansion is the first correction term of K_Ω(z,z̄). Conjecture: For a class of "directly defined" Bell domains, the log coefficient is an explicit curvature integral: coefficient = ∫_{∂Ω}(Ricci − (1/2)#σ)·η; this globalizes the local formula of Graham–Hirachi.

**中文。** 定义：Fefferman 展开的 log 项系数指 K_Ω(z,z̄) 的首阶修正项。猜想：一类「直接定义」Bell 域的 log 系数为显式曲率积分:系数 = ∫_∂Ω (Ricci−(1/2)\#σ)·η;由 Graham–Hirachi 的局部公式全局化。
