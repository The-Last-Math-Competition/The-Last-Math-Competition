# 00000004183

**English.** Definition: A sweepout is a parameterized family of surfaces sweeping out the whole manifold. Conjecture: The weakest regularity for compactness is the flat convergence of integral currents, and any weakening of the modulus of continuity loses compactness. (weakest regularity of sweepout compactness)

**中文。** 定义：sweepout 指扫掠整个流形的曲面参数族。猜想：紧性成立的最弱正则性为整值流量的平坦收敛,任何连续模的弱化都将丧失紧性。（sweepout 紧性最弱正则性）
