# 00000009656

**HSS-16 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The logarithmic field of characteristic polynomials: the limit and extreme value distribution of log|det(W - x)| as a random process in x. Conjecture: The logarithmic field converges after renormalization to the Gaussian free field; the distribution of its maximum is the Fyodorov-Bouchaud distribution, with the location process of extrema of Poissonized explicit intensity; and the gap between second and first maxima has an asymptotically exponential distribution. (characteristic polynomial FB distribution)

**中文。** 定义：特征多项式的对数场：log|det(W−x)| 作为 x 的随机过程的极限与极值分布。猜想：对数场在重整化下收敛到高斯自由场；其极大值的分布为 Fyodorov–Bouchaud 分布，且极值点的位置过程为 Poisson 化的显式强度；第二极大与第一极大的间隙渐近分布为指数。（特征多项式 FB 分布）
