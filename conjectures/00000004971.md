**English.** Definition: The hyperbolic cross is the sparse index set constrained by product degree. Conjecture: The optimal quadrature error constant in dimension three is an explicit harmonic product of the anisotropic weights, degenerating to the classical value for equal weights, and the constant is tight. (harmonic product constant of the three-dimensional hyperbolic cross)
