# 00000004475

**English.** Definition: The superconvergent singularities of a Landau-Ginzburg model are the mutation point set. Conjecture: The Stokes constants of their monodromy form a table of rationals, at the A_1 singularity the value is of type 1/24, and higher singularities obey an explicit closed recursion. (rational recursion table of LG Stokes data)

**中文。** 定义：Landau–Ginzburg 模型的超收敛奇点指突变点集。猜想：其单值的 Stokes 常数为有理数表,A₁ 奇点处为 1/24 型值,高阶奇点显式递推且封闭。（LG Stokes 有理数递推表）
