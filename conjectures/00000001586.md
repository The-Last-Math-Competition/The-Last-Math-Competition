# 00000001586

**English.** Definition: The Fredholm determinant representation of the isomonodromy tau function. Conjecture: The kernel of the Fredholm determinant of τ_III is a Bessel kernel with spectral scaling t^{1/3} (Bessel scaling).

**中文。** 定义：isomonodromy τ 的 Fredholm 行列式表示。猜想：τ_III 的 Fredholm 行列式的核为 Bessel 且谱标度为 t^{1/3}(Bessel 标度)。
