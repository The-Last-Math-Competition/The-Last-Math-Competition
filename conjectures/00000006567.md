**English.** Definition: Gauge invariance is the preservation structure of phase symmetry. Conjecture: The equivariant construction is given by averaged characteristics; the completeness of the construction is the characteristic closure; and the convergence of the characteristic closure is the averaging window radius. (averaged-characteristic equivariant construction)
