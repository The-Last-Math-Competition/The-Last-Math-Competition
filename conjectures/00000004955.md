# 00000004955

**English.** Definition: Kervaire invariant one is the exceptional homotopy class of framed manifolds, known to exist in dimensions 2, 6, 14, 30, and 62. Conjecture: Elements of Kervaire invariant one exist only in dimensions 2, 6, 14, 30, and 62 with no further exceptional dimensions; the multiplicity in the homotopy group at each existing dimension is exactly one; and the nonexistence in dimension 126 follows from the completeness of the Adams differential of h_6 squared. (closure of the Kervaire exceptional dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：Kervaire 不变量一：带框流形的例外同伦类（已知维数 2、6、14、30、62 处存在）。猜想：Kervaire 不变量一的元素仅在维数 2、6、14、30、62 存在且不存在其余例外维数；每个存在维数的元素在同伦群中的重数恰为一；126 维的非存在性由 h₆² 的 Adams 微分完全性给出。（Kervaire 例外维数封闭）
