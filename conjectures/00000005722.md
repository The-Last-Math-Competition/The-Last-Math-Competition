**FB3-12 ★** — source: 176_椭圆偏微分III_自由边界问题II.md

**English.** Definition: The transmission condition is the gradient jump condition across the interface, with its coefficient structure. Conjecture: The coefficient of the jump condition is always the explicit product of the ratios of the two-phase gradients, degenerating to one for balanced phases; the sign of the product is decided by the density ratio, the denser phase having the smaller gradient; and the bound of the coefficient is the harmonic mean of the two-phase admittances, attained in layered parallel flows. (degenerate structure of the transmission gradient-ratio product)

**中文。** 定义：传输条件：跨界面梯度跳条件；系数结构。猜想：跳条件的系数恒为两相梯度比的显式积且平衡相时退化为一；积的符号由密度比决定且密度大者的梯度小；系数的界为两相导纳的调和平均且达到在分层平行流。（传输梯度比积退化结构）
