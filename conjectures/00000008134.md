**English.** Definition: The noncommutative Berthelot realization of categorical crystalline cohomology of noncommutative varieties: the rigidity index cr_nc of nc p-adic cohomology. Conjecture: cr_nc (the existence of a complete invariant of the category of p-adic local systems) is equivalent to the p-adic comparison theorem for the centralization of the nc variety (a comparison-complete invariant equivalence); the criterion for rigidity (triviality of the absolute Hodge closure) is rationality of the coefficients of the Frobenius characteristic polynomial of nc de Rham cohomology (a polynomial rigidity criterion); and the exception to the criterion is superconvergent equivariance (a superconvergence exception). (crystalline comparison polynomial rigidity)
