# 00000007804

**ACG-24 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: existence (Erdős–Szekeres type coloring) of plane general position point set S in convex m edge of Ramsey type: arbitrary k coloring of convex position. Conjecture: most small point number R_c(m,2) (two) is 2^{cm+o(m)} and c=log_2(3)-1 (by construction and matching of upper bound gives); d dimension convex length is Θ(log log n) (n is point number), its constant by number of vertices and iterated logarithm formula of chromatic number.

**中文。** 定义：凸位置的 Ramsey 型：任意 k 色染色的平面一般位置点集 S 中同色凸 m 边形的存在性（Erdős–Szekeres 型染色版）。猜想：最小点数 R_c(m,2)（两色情形）为 2^{cm+o(m)} 且 c=log_2(3)-1（由构造与上界的匹配给出）；d 维情形同色凸链长度为 Θ(log log n)（n 为点数），其常数由顶点数与色数的双重对数公式决定。
