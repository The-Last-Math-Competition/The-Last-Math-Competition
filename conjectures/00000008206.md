# 00000008206

**ORB-06 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The shift correction in geometric quantization: the correction shift_q to the integrality condition (the GS conjecture) for the quantization Q(O) of the prequantum line bundle of an orbit O. Conjecture: shift_q is the half-integral correction by the restriction of rho (the Weyl vector, a half-integral correction law); the correction vanishes (unshifted quantization) if and only if O is an adjoint orbit (an adjoint vanishing criterion); and the family of orbits realizing negative quantization dimensions (orbits with zero quantization, Kostant formula) is the even-shift family (an even-shift zero-family law), whose minimal-dimension orbit is the minimal element of the Richardson orbits (a Richardson minimal-zero law). (quantum shift half-integral Richardson zero family)

**中文。** 定义：几何量子化的 Shift 切触：轨道 O 的预量子线丛的量子化 Q(O) 的整性条件（GS 猜想）的修正 shift_q。猜想：shift_q = ρ|_O 的半整修正（ρ 为 Weyl 向量，半整修正律）；且修正的消没（无移位量子化）当且仅当 O 为 adjoint 轨道（adjoint 消没判据）；量子化维数（Kostant 公式）的负值的实现（量子化为零的轨道）的轨道族为偶移位族（偶移位零族律），族的最小维数轨道为 Richardson 轨道的极小元（Richardson 极小零律）。
