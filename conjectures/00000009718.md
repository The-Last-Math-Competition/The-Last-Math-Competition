# 00000009718

**SCA-18 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Equivalence of global spacetime norms and scattering for critical equations: quantitative criteria for scattering. Conjecture: The energy-critical wave equation scatters if and only if the global spacetime norm is finite, and the scattering threshold function of the norm is continuous and increasing; the threshold curve is explicitly segmented by the minimal energy of solitons. (spacetime norm scattering criterion)

**中文。** 定义：临界方程的全局时空范数与散射的等价：散射的定量判据。猜想：能量临界波方程的解散射当且仅当全局时空范数有限且范数的散射阈值函数为连续递增剖度；阈值曲线由孤子的最小能量显式分段。（时空范数散射判据）
