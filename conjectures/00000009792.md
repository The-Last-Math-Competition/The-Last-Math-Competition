# 00000009792

**English.** Definition: Discrete Gauss-Bonnet: the sum of vertex angle deficits of a polyhedral surface equals 2 pi chi (exact identity); extensions with boundary and corners. Conjecture: The boundary term of discrete Gauss-Bonnet with creased boundary is the exact sum of dihedral deficits of creases; the identity has no residual, and the boundary term converges under refinement to the integral of geodesic curvature. (discrete Gauss-Bonnet boundary term)

**中文。** 定义：离散 Gauss–Bonnet：多面体曲面顶点角度亏损总和等于 2πχ（精确恒等式）；带边界与角点的推广。猜想：带边界折痕的离散 Gauss–Bonnet 的边界项为折痕二面角缺损的精确求和；等式无残差且边界项在细分极限下收敛到测地曲率积分。（离散 Gauss-Bonnet 边界项）
