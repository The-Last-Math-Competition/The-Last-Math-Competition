# 00000008282

**SFL-22 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The dual control of Kalman filtering with incomplete observation: the separation princ_sep of the dual roles of control and estimation. Conjecture: princ_sep (the separation principle) holds when noises are independent (decorrelation of system and observation noise, a decorrelation separation law); the correction for correlated failure (noise correlation) is a conservatization of control (a conservatization correction) with magnitude explicit in the correlation coefficient rho (a rho explicit magnitude); the minimal problem where the explicit dual optimum (explicit solution of dual control) becomes nonlinear (nonseparable) is LQG with rho nonzero (an LQG correlated example); and the correction of the optimal control gain for that example is the product rho times the observation gain (a rho-gain product correction). (dual control separation rho-gain correction)

**中文。** 定义：不完全观测的 Kalman 的对偶控制：控制与估计的双重角色的分离 princ_sep。猜想：princ_sep（分离原理）在噪声独立（系统噪声与观测噪声的去相关）成立（去相关分离律）；且相关的失效（噪声相关）的修正为控制的保守化（保守化修正）的幅度为相关系数 ρ 的显式（ρ 显式幅度）；双重的最优（对偶控制的显式解）的非线性（非分离的显式）的最小问题为 LQG 的 ρ≠0（LQG 相关例）；例的最优控制的增益的修正为 ρ·观测增益的积（ρ-增益积修正）。
