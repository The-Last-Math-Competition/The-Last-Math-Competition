# 00000008162

**English.** Definition: The renormalization of random matrices: the spectral stability index rm_rg of the N to infinity limit of the RG of hierarchical couplings J_{ij}/r^{i-j} of eigenvalues. Conjecture: The fixed point of rm_rg (self-similarity of the Wigner semicircle) is stable in a one-foot neighborhood of coupling decay r^{-1} (a one-foot stability law); the stability boundary (instability point) is r_c = e^{-1} (a reciprocal-e critical point); and the steady state after instability (a non-semicircular steady state) is a Levy-type spectrum (a Levy steady-state law), the tail exponent alpha of the steady state agreeing with the tail of the initial coupling (a tail agreement law). (random matrix Levy steady-state e-critical)

**中文。** 定义：随机矩阵的重整化：特征值的层级耦合（层次耦合 J_{ij}/r^{i-j}）的 RG 的 N→∞ 极限的谱稳定指标 rm_rg。猜想：rm_rg 的不动点（Wigner 半圆的自相似性）在耦合衰减 r^{-1} 的一足邻域稳定（一足稳定律）；且稳定边界（失稳点）r_c = e^{-1}（e-倒数临界）；失稳后的稳态（非半圆稳态）为 Lévy 型谱（Lévy 稳态律），稳态的尾部指数 α 由初始耦合的尾部一致（尾部一致律）。
