# 00000005474

**English.** Definition: Triangular solutions are spectral-parameter-dependent multiplicative Yang-Baxter solutions, with unitary-solution matrix entries. Conjecture: The matrix entries of unitary triangular solutions are always given explicitly by the dimensions of quantum group representations, with the entries of the fundamental representation cosine-type algebraic numbers; the extremal spectrum of the entries is finite, with extremal points roots of unity; and unitarity is preserved on the whole torus of the spectral parameter, with preservation constant 1. (quantum-coefficient matrix entries of unitary triangular solutions)

**中文。** 定义：三角解：带谱参数的乘法型 Yang–Baxter 解；酉解的矩阵元。猜想：酉三角解的矩阵元恒由量子群表示维数显式给出且基本表示下的矩阵元为余弦型代数数；矩阵元的极值谱为有限且极值点为单位根；酉性在谱参数的整个环面上保持且保持常数为 1。（三角酉量子系数矩阵元）
