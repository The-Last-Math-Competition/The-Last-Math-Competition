# 00000003008

**English.** Definition: Integrability of nonlinear differential equations: the Galois-ization of the Painlevé test. Conjecture: The Galois obstruction to the Painlevé property: a necessary condition for absence of fixed critical points is nondiscreteness of the Galois group, with the discretization criterion the closure of Bonnet transformations. (Painleve Galois obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：非线性微分方程的可积性：Painlevé 检验的 Galois 化。猜想：Painlevé 性质的 Galois 阻碍：无固定临界点的必要条件为 Galois 群的非离散性且离散化判据为 Bonnet 变换的封闭。（Painleve Galois 阻碍）
