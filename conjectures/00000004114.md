**English.** Definition: A graph complex is a simplicial complex whose faces are graphs. Conjecture: The sign of its Euler characteristic is eventually constantly positive as the number of vertices tends to infinity, and the asymptotic sign is given explicitly by the isolated-edge density function. (eventually positive sign of graph complexes)

**中文。** 定义：图复形指以图为面的单纯复形。猜想：其 Euler 特征的符号在顶点数趋于无穷时最终恒正,渐近符号由孤立边密度函数显式给出。（图复形渐近符号恒正）
