# 00000002306

**English.** Definition: The Schur multiplier M(G) is the kernel of central extensions. Conjecture: The complete table of Schur multipliers of simple groups is completed up to |G| ≤ 10^6: three exceptional covers remain; after completion the order distribution of multipliers in the table is uniform (asymptotically Poisson).

**中文。** 定义：Schur 乘子 M(G) 指中心扩张的核。猜想：单群 Schur 乘子的完备表在 |G|≤10^6 的补全:剩余 3 个例外覆盖;补全后表的乘子的阶分布均匀(渐近 Poisson)。
