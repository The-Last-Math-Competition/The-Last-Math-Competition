**English.** Definition: The weight spectrum is the weight-jump sequence of a mixed structure. Conjecture: The weight-three constraint table is the combination law that adjacent weight differences are at most three, the spectrum table is closed by the codimension of open varieties, and there are no exceptions. (weight-three adjacent-difference constraint spectrum table)
