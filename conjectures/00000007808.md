# 00000007808

**ACG-28 △** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Poisson floating body: the floating body Gamma(Phi_lambda intersect K) of a Poisson point process of intensity lambda restricted to K. Conjecture: The large-lambda expansion of E vol(Gamma(Phi_lambda intersect K)) is vol(Gamma K)(1 - c(K) lambda^{-2/(n+1)} + O(lambda^{-3/(n+1)})), the leading exponent 2/(n+1) agreeing with the random polytope volume deficit; c(K) is constant on affine equivalence classes and vanishes exactly at ellipsoids, the unique bodies where it disappears. (Poisson floating body expansion)

**中文。** 定义：泊松浮身：强度 λ 的泊松点过程 Φ_λ 的浮身体 Γ(Φ_λ∩K)（在 K 上限制）。猜想：E vol(Γ(Φ_λ∩K)) 的 λ→∞ 展开为 vol(ΓK)(1-c(K)λ^{-2/(n+1)}+O(λ^{-3/(n+1)}))，主指数 2/(n+1) 与随机多胞形缺体积指数一致；c(K) 在仿射等价类上为常数，其极值在椭球为 0（唯一消失的体）。
