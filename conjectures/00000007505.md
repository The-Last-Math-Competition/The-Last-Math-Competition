# 00000007505

**English.** Definition: The approximation of periods with transcendence measures. Conjecture: The lower bound of linear forms in periods holds, and the constant of the bound is the motivic height. (motivically-bounded period linear forms)

**中文。** 定义：周期的逼近：超越度量。猜想：周期的超越度量：周期的线性型的下界且界的常数为母题高度。（周期超越度量母题高度线性型下界常数逼近）
