# 00000002161

**English.** Definition: The inertia bound of signed graphs states the independence number is at most the positive inertia index. Conjecture: The minimal genus of graphs attaining the tight bound equals 1 (apart from complete graphs); the genus is computed explicitly via the Euler formula of 2-cell embeddings.

**中文。** 定义：符号图的惯性界指独立数 ≤ 正惯性指数。猜想：达到紧确界的图的最小亏格 = 1(除完全图外);亏格的显式计算由 2-胞腔嵌入的 Euler 公式给出。
