# 00000007661

**QDE-01 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: Let 0<|q|<1; a power series F(z)=sum a_n z^n is of q-hypergeometric type if there are rational functions R(z), S(z) with F(qz)=R(z)F(z)+S(z); the coefficient ratios a_{n+1}/a_n are the Q-geometric ratios. Conjecture: F(z) in Z[[z]] is of q-hypergeometric type with convergence radius 1 if and only if its Q-geometric ratio sequence converges to 1 at rate O(q^n), and this rate cannot be improved to o(q^n) unless F is a polynomial. (sharp Q-geometric ratio convergence rate)

**中文。** 定义：设 0<|q|<1，幂级数 F(z)=Σ_{n≥0} a_n z^n 称为 q-超几何型的，若存在有理函数 R(z),S(z) 使 F(qz)=R(z)F(z)+S(z)；系数比 a_{n+1}/a_n 称为 Q-几何比。猜想：F(z) ∈ Z[[z]] 为 q-超几何型且收敛半径为 1，当且仅当其 Q-几何比序列在 n→∞ 时收敛到 1 的速度为 O(q^n)，且此收敛速度不可改进为 o(q^n)，除非 F 为多项式。
