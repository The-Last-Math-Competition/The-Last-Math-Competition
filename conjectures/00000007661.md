# 00000007661

**QDE-01 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: let 0<|q|<1, power series F(z)=Σ_{n≥0} a_n z^n is, of called q- geometry type if exists rational function R(z),S(z) such that F(qz)=R(z)F(z)+S(z); coefficient ratio a_{n+1}/a_n is called Q- geometry ratio. Conjecture: F(z) ∈ Z[[z]] is q- geometry type and radius of convergence is 1, if and only if its Q- geometry ratio sequence in n -> ∞ when converges speed of to 1 is O(q^n), and this convergence speed cannot be improved is o(q^n), unless F is polynomial.

**中文。** 定义：设 0<|q|<1，幂级数 F(z)=Σ_{n≥0} a_n z^n 称为 q-超几何型的，若存在有理函数 R(z),S(z) 使 F(qz)=R(z)F(z)+S(z)；系数比 a_{n+1}/a_n 称为 Q-几何比。猜想：F(z) ∈ Z[[z]] 为 q-超几何型且收敛半径为 1，当且仅当其 Q-几何比序列在 n→∞ 时收敛到 1 的速度为 O(q^n)，且此收敛速度不可改进为 o(q^n)，除非 F 为多项式。
