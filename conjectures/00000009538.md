# 00000009538

**English.** Definition: The amortized coherent information I_am(phi) = max_rho [I_c(rho,phi) + E(rho_in)], with E an input-side entanglement measure; it gives a unified framework for upper bounds without PPT-breaking capacity. Conjecture: For every channel, I_am(phi) is at most log((1 + ||phi||_{diamond,red})/2), with equality if and only if the Choi state is a partial permutation of a maximally entangled state; this dual norm bound uniformly covers the two major upper bound classes, the degradable bound and the PPT bound. (amortized entanglement dual bound)
