**English.** Definition: The graph braid group is the fundamental group of the configuration space of a graph, and cd denotes the cohomological dimension. Conjecture: cd(B_Gamma) <= 2 if and only if Gamma contains no two vertex-disjoint cycles, and the graphs with cd = 3 admit a complete classification. (dichotomy criterion for the graph braid cohomological dimension)

**中文。** 定义：图辫群指图配置空间的基本群,cd 指上同调维数。猜想：cd(B_Γ) ≤ 2 当且仅当 Γ 不含顶点不交的两个圈,cd = 3 的图有完全分类。（图辫上同调维数二分准则）
