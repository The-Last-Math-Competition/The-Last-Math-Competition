**AR3-13 ★** — source: 120_代数表示论III_AuslanderReiten.md

**English.** Definition: The Loewy length is the length of the radical nilpotence chain. Conjecture: The Loewy length of a monomial algebra and the growth rate of its preprojective component satisfy a logarithmic-type inequality, with equality attained by Koszul monomial algebras. (optimal Loewy length-growth inequality)

**中文。** 定义：Loewy 长指根幂零链长度。猜想：单项式代数的 Loewy 长与 preprojective 分量的生长率满足对数型不等式,等号由 Koszul 单项式代数达到。（Loewy 长-生长率最优不等式）
