# 00000002394

**English.** Definition: The eigenvalue counting of Laplacians of substitution systems. Conjecture: The second-order term of the new-subspace dimension is a power of the second eigenvalue of the substitution matrix; and the combination of the power is the spectral-radius ratio. (substitution spectrum second-order second eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：替换系统 Laplacian 的谱计数。猜想：新子空间维数的二阶项为替换矩阵第二特征值幂；且幂的组合为谱半径比。（替换谱二阶第二特征值）
