# 00000008227

**English.** Definition: The quadratic correction of the Weil index under metaplectic transformations: the splitting wei_split of the Weil index. Conjecture: The constant of wei_split (the quadratic extension decomposition of the Weil index) is an explicit 8th root of unity (an 8th root law); the correction for the symmetry of the splitting (self-duality of the quadratic form x^2 + y^2) is a phase of 1/4 (a quarter phase law); and the minimal commutative obstruction to the existence of the correction of the Weil index for higher (quartic) extensions (noncommutative extensions) is the noncommutative cover of the Heisenberg group (a noncommutative cover example). (Weil index eighth root quarter phase)

**中文。** 定义：分支的 metaplectic 变换的 Weil 指数的二次修正：Weil 指数的分解 wei_split。猜想：wei_split（Weil 指数的二次扩张分解）的常数为 8 次单位根的显式（8 次根律）；且分解的对称（二次型 x²+y² 的自对偶）的修正为 1/4 相位（四分之一相位律）；高阶（4 次扩张）的 Weil 指数的修正的存在（非交换扩张）的极小交换障碍为 Heisenberg 的非交换覆盖（非交换覆盖例）。
