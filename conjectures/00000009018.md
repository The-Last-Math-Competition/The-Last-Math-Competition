# 00000009018

**English.** Definition: The Petersson inner product: the pairing of modular forms. Conjecture: The norms of half-integral weight orthogonal bases are explicit and tied to symmetric power L-values; and the tied ratio is the central value. (half-integral Petersson norm L-value)

**中文。** 定义：Petersson 内积为模形式配对。猜想：半整权正交基的范数显式且与对称幂 L 值挂钩；且挂钩的比为中心值。（半整 Petersson 范数 L 值）
