**English.** Definition: Local representability is the characterization by complete reflections plus generating structures. Conjecture: The union of the regular-cardinal spectra of free generation is stable, the kernel of stability being the rank sheaf; the gluing of the sheaf is injective, the cokernel being the next regular cardinal; and the induced functor of the reflection is conservative, the conservativity given by the covering of the generating structure. (rank-sheaf stability of regular-cardinal unions)

**中文。** 定义：局部可表示性：完备反射加生成结构的刻画。猜想：自由生成的正则基数谱的并集稳定且稳定的核为秩层；层的粘合为单射且余核为下一个正则基数；反射的诱导函子保守且保守性由生成结构的覆盖给出。（正则基数谱并秩层稳定）
