**English.** Definition: The BD Grassmannian is the mobile version of the Grassmannian. Conjecture: The generating table of its equivariant cohomology is indexed by an explicit basis of the double-affine algebra, closed within the third layer, with commuting generation relations. (double-affine basis table of BD equivariant cohomology)
