**English.** Definition: The characteristic exponents are the exponential data of a linear system. Conjecture: The exponent-monodromy conversion of rank two is an explicit matrix of resonance relations, diagonal when nonresonant, with logarithmic corrections at resonances. (resonance-matrix conversion of rank-two exponents)

**中文。** 定义：特征指数指线性系统的指数数据。猜想：秩二的指数单值换算为共振关系的显式矩阵,非共振时对角且共振处对数型修正。（秩二指数共振矩阵换算）
