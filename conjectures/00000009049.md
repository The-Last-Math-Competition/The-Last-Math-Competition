# 00000009049

**SMI-09 ★** — source: 287_子流形几何不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Asymptotic rigidity: the structure at infinity of complete submanifolds. Conjecture: A complete minimal hypersurface has finite index if and only if its total curvature is finite; and the constant of the equivalence is the index density. (asymptotic index total curvature equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：渐近刚性为完备子流形的无穷远结构。猜想：完备极小超曲面的指标有限当且仅当总曲率有限；且等价的常数为指标密度。（渐近指标-总曲率等价）
