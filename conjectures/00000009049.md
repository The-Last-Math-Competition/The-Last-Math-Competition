**English.** Definition: asymptotic rigidity is infinity structure of complete manifold. Conjecture: index finite of complete minimal hypersurface if and only if always curvature finite; and constant of equivalent is index density. (asymptotic index - always curvature equivalent)

**中文。** 定义：渐近刚性为完备子流形的无穷远结构。猜想：完备极小超曲面的指标有限当且仅当总曲率有限；且等价的常数为指标密度。（渐近指标-总曲率等价）
