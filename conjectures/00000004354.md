**English.** Definition: The Wick order is the renormalized product under Gaussian expectation. Conjecture: The combinatorial constants of its product formula form a table of telephone-number-type nonnegative integers, and the differences of adjacent rows of the table are always Fibonacci numbers. (Wick combinatorial telephone-number-Fibonacci table)

**中文。** 定义：Wick 序指高斯期望下的重整化乘积。猜想：其乘积公式的组合常数为电话数型非负整数表,表中相邻行差恒为斐波那契数。（Wick 组合电话数-斐波那契表）
