# 00000000185

**English.** Definition: The algebraic relation between Euler's constant γ and π. Conjecture: γ and π are algebraically independent; moreover, γ and e^γ jointly generate a field of transcendence degree at least two. (gamma-pi algebraic independence)

**中文。** 定义：欧拉常数 γ 与 π 的代数关系。猜想：γ 与 π 代数无关；且 γ 与 e^γ 联合生成超越次数至少为二的域。（gamma-pi 代数无关）
