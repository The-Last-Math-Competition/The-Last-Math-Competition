# 00000005087

**English.** Definition: The block labels of geometric Satake parametrize the zero-weight branches, and one asks for the block structure of the exceptional group G_2. Conjecture: The block labels of G_2 are given by explicit representatives of the quotient of the weight lattice, and the number of blocks is exactly three; the three blocks are isomorphic to the image of the dominant chamber, with chamber volume ratios 1:2:3; the label conversion is preserved under Ishida-type vanishing with preservation constant 1. (three-label block structure of G_2 Satake)

**中文。** 定义：几何 Satake 的块标签：权零分支的参数化；例外群 G₂ 的块结构。猜想：G₂ 的块标签由权格商的显式代表给出且块的个数恰为三；三块同构于支配腔的像且腔体积比为 1:2:3；标签换算在石田型消没下保持且保持常数为 1。（G2 Satake 块三标签结构）
