**English.** Definition: Arithmetic unique ergodicity is the uniqueness of the limit of mass measures. Conjecture: In dimension two, subconvexity forces the entropy of escaping mass measures to vanish via explicit reasoning, thus implying unique ergodicity, and the reasoning is complete for all arithmetic hyperbolic surfaces. (subconvex-QUE zero-entropy implication chain)
