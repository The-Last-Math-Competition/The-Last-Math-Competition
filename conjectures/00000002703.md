**AD2-23 ★** — source: 75_代数动力学_ArzelaZariski.md

**English.** Definition: Zariski's conjecture II: knot isotopy classes determined by the complement's fundamental group (refuted). Conjecture: Quantitative rigidity of complement groups: the separation degree of isotopy classes is given by the coprimality of Alexander polynomials of the fundamental groups, and the coprimality criterion is complete at bridge number at most three. (complement group Alexander rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 猜想 II：曲线同痕类由补基本群决定（已否证）。猜想：补基本群的定量刚性：同痕类的分离度由基本群的 Alexander 多项式的互素性给出且互素判据在桥数不超过三时完备。（补群 Alexander 刚性）
