# 00000005367

**English.** Definition: The lexicographic automaton generates canonical representatives. Conjecture: Its conversion is given explicitly by the finite permutation group action of generator reorderings, and the automaton size is invariant under reordering. (reordering-invariant conversion of lexicographic automata)

**中文。** 定义：字典自动机指生成规范代表的自动机。猜想：其换算由生成元重排的有限置换群作用显式给出,自动机尺寸在重排下不变。（字典自动机重排不变换算）
