# 00000007572

**English.** Definition: The nerve lemma gives the homotopy equivalence of the nerve with the original space. Conjecture: The window of the equivalence is all-intersections-connected, the window ends being given by the convexity of good covers; and the existence of good covers is geodesic spaces. (intersection-connected nerve window)

**中文。** 定义：神经引理给出 nerve 与原空间的同伦等价。猜想：等价的窗口为一切交连通且窗端由好覆盖的凸性给出；且好覆盖的存在为测地空间。（神经引理交连通窗）
