# 00000005155

**English.** Definition: Special points are the points corresponding to special structures. Conjecture: The special-point density of layer three is an explicit Euler product on the weight space, the convergence exponent is the reciprocal of the degree, and the formula is exact. (Euler density product of layer-three special points)
