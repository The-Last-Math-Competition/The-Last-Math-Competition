# 00000001082

**English.** Definition: Gabidulin MRD codes attain the Singleton bound for rank distance. Conjecture: For fixed (m,n,k), the number of equivalence classes of MRD codes has the explicit polynomial main term q^{(k−1)(n−k+1)}·(1+O(q^{-1})); the count is given by the cell decomposition of the variety of nonsingular matrices.

**中文。** 定义：Gabidulin MRD 码为秩距离达到 Singleton 界的码。猜想：固定 (m,n,k) 时 MRD 码的等价类个数为 q^{(k−1)(n−k+1)}·(1+O(q^{-1})) 型显式多项式主项;该计数由非奇异矩阵簇的胞腔分解给出。
