# 00000000638

**KT-38 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The minimal translation number δ_g of the mapping class group of genus g is the infimum of the logs of the dilatations of its pseudo-Anosov elements. Conjecture: The limit lim_{g→∞} g·log δ_g exists and equals log 2, i.e. δ_g ~ 2^{1/g}; and the asymptotic is attained by an explicit family among the Penner–Lanneau-type sequences.

**中文。** 定义：亏格 g 的映射类群的最小平移数 δ_g 指其伪 Anosov 元的 dilatation 对数下确界。猜想：极限 lim_{g→∞} g·log δ_g 存在且等于 log 2,即 δ_g ~ 2^{1/g};且该渐近由 Penner–Lannear 类序列中的一族显式达到。
