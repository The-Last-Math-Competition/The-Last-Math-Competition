**IX3-26 ★** — source: 203_非交换几何III_指标定理的几何侧.md

**English.** Definition: The twisted index with the index of flat twist bundles. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the flatness window and the window ends are given by the vanishing of characteristic classes; the window closes and the kernel of the closure is the rationality of Chern classes; and the obstruction to flatness is the integrality of characteristic classes. (vanishing-window conversion of flatness)

**中文。** 定义：扭曲指标：平坦扭丛的指标。猜想：换算常数为平坦性窗的显式且窗端由示性类消没给出；窗口封闭且封闭的核为陈类的有理性；平坦的障碍为示性类的整性。（平坦窗端消没换算常数）
