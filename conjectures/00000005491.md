# 00000005491

**IF2-21 ◆** — source: 168_数学物理IV_可积场论.md

**English.** Definition: The model with topological term is the continuum limit of the antiferromagnetic chain, of WZW type plus a topological angle. Conjecture: The period formula of the mass gap is an explicit fraction of the topological angle, compatible at the critical angle with the symmetry-breaking transition; the gap vanishes at angle zero, with vanishing order quadratic; and the numerator and denominator of the fraction are both Weyl invariants, with invariant degree difference 1. (topological-angle periodic fraction of the gap)

**中文。** 定义：带拓扑项的模型：反铁磁链的连续极限（WZW 型加拓扑角）。猜想：质量间隙的周期公式为拓扑角的显式分式且临界角处与对称破缺相变相容；间隙在角为零处消没且消没阶为二次；分式的分子分母均为外尔不变量且不变量次数差为 1。（拓扑角周期分式间隙）
