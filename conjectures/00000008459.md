**English.** Definition: The Rasmussen invariant s(K) and the Ozsvath-Szabo invariant tau(K). Conjecture: |s(K)/2 - tau(K)| <= 1; knots with difference exactly 1 are of Whitehead double type, and the spectrum is completely classified by the slice genus difference. (difference-one spectrum of s and tau)
