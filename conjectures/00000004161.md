**CM3-11 ◆** — source: 124_解析数论III_圆法.md

**English.** Definition: The Goldbach exceptional set is the set of even numbers not representable as a sum of two primes. Conjecture: The exceptional set is always controlled by a fixed power of log x, and under the Riemann hypothesis the power exponent can be taken to be two. (logarithmic bound of the Goldbach exceptional set)

**中文。** 定义：Goldbach 例外集指不能写成两素数之和的偶数集。猜想：例外集恒被 logx 的固定幂控制；且黎曼假设下幂指数可取二。（Goldbach 例外集对数界）
