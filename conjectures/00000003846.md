# 00000003846

**RC-26 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: Monoid homology denotes the integral homology of its classifying space. Conjecture: H₁ of the plactic monoid P_n is free abelian of rank n, H₂ free abelian of rank binom(n,2), and all higher homology groups are torsion-free. (plactic homology torsion-free)

**中文。** 定义：幺半群同调指其分类空间的整同调群。猜想：plactic 幺半群 P_n 的 H₁ 为秩 n 的自由交换群,H₂ 为秩 binom(n,2) 的自由交换群,且所有高阶同调群无挠。（plactic 同调无挠）
