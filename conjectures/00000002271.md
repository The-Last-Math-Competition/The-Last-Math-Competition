# 00000002271

**English.** Definition: The expected signature E[σ(X)]_t is the expectation of iterated integrals of the path. Conjecture: For fractional Brownian motion with H < 1/2, the expected signature has an explicit hypergeometric closed form (fBM signature).

**中文。** 定义：期望签名 E[σ(X)]_t(路径的迭代积分期望)。猜想：分数 Brown 运动 H < 1/2 的期望签名的显式超几何闭式(fBM 签名)。
