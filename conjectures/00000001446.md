# 00000001446

**MC-26 ★** — source: 33_概率论II_马氏链与粒子系统.md

**English.** Definition: The boundary one-arm exponent σ₁ of the overall rescaling limit of LERW (loop-erased random walk) is the decay exponent of the probability of boundary connection from the origin to distance 1. Conjecture: σ₁ is an explicit value of the form 2 − (5/(3π))·arcsin-type; precisely the closed form σ₁ = (1/4)·(5 − √3/π); this agrees with the boundary one-arm exponent of SLE₂.

**中文。** 定义：LERW(环擦除随机游走)的整体重标度极限的外边界一臂指数 σ₁ 指从原点到距离 1 的边界连通的概率衰减指数。猜想：σ₁ = 2 − 5/(3π)·arcsin 型显式值,精确地为 σ₁ = (1/4)·(5−√(3)/π) 的闭式;与 SLE₂ 的边界一臂指数一致。
