# 00000004096

**English.** Definition: Elliptic configurations are configuration spaces on an elliptic curve. Conjecture: The generating function of the ranks of their homology of each degree is an infinite product, the main term of the rank is the quadratic expression n(n-1)/2, and the correction terms are quasi-periodic with period 1. (rank generating function of elliptic configurations)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆配置指椭圆曲线上的配置空间。猜想：其各阶同调秩的母函数为无穷乘积,秩的主项为 n(n−1)/2 型二次式,修正项以周期 1 拟周期。（椭圆配置秩母函数）
