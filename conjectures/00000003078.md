**English.** Definition: Truncations of ∞-groupoids: n-truncation and homotopy layers. Conjecture: Splitting of the truncation tower: the layers of the truncation tower of the category of n-types are Postnikov pieces, with gluing of pieces the pullback of k-invariants. (truncation Postnikov gluing)
