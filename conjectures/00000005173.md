**English.** Definition: The compact-object class is preserved under filtered colimits. Conjecture: Its stability threshold is an explicit closure condition of a regular cardinal, namely stability when the extension group of generators is presentable, and the condition is necessary and sufficient. (presentability stability criterion of compact-object classes)

**中文。** 定义：紧对象类指在滤余极限下保持的对象类。猜想：其稳定阈值为正则基数的显式封闭条件,即生成元的扩张群为可呈现时稳定,条件必要充分。（紧对象类呈现稳定判据）
