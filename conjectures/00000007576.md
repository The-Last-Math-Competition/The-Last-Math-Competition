**English.** Definition: The Mapper algorithm as complex reconstruction by open covers plus clustering. Conjecture: The deviation of the Mapper complex from the nerve is jointly controlled by cover resolution and gain; and the minimization of the deviation is at the logarithmic window of the overlap parameter.
