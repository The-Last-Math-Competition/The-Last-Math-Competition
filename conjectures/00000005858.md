**MR3-28 ★** — source: 180_模表示II.md

**English.** Definition: The quaternion invariant is the discriminant data of the block. Conjecture: For the minimal quaternion block the invariant is two-valued, the combination of the two values always being the minimal-module chain-length spectrum; the chain-length difference is 1; and the flip of the invariant corresponds to the Morita equivalence flip of the block, the flip preserving the trace. (chain-length two-valued spectrum of quaternion blocks)

**中文。** 定义：四元数不变量：块的判别数据。猜想：最小四元数块时不变量为二值且二值的组合恒为极小模链长谱；链长的差为 1；不变量的翻转对应块的 Morita 等价翻转且翻转保迹。（四元数链长二值谱）
