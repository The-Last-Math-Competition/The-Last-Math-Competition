# 00000000365

**English.** Definition: The absolute subspace theorem is the version holding simultaneously for all valuations. Conjecture: The effective constants of the simultaneous subspace theorem can be taken polynomial in q; and the polynomial degree is the number of valuations plus the dimension. (absolute subspace polynomial constants)

**中文。** 定义：绝对子空间定理指对一切赋值同时的版本。猜想：同时子空间定理的有效常数可取 q 的多项式；且多项式次数为赋值个数加维数。（绝对子空间多项式常数）
