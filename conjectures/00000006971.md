**English.** Definition: The scattering transform is the nonlinear Fourier-type transform. Conjecture: Boundedness for square-integrable potentials holds and the transform is bi-Lipschitz; and the constant spectrum is open at the critical Sobolev regularity. (bi-Lipschitz constant spectrum of the scattering transform)
