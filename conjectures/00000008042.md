# 00000008042

**English.** Definition: The Phillips group problem for definable automorphism groups: the automorphism rigidity index AR(X) of definable domains (definable subdomains of R^n) whose only definable automorphisms are the identity. Conjecture: AR(X) = 1 (trivial definable automorphisms) holds for general definable domains X; the complete list of exceptional domains consists of semialgebraic symmetric domains (quotients of balls and polytopes); and the quotient by definable equivalence (definable bijections) has finite cardinality with supremum of the cardinality spectrum equal to 2 (binary rigidity), the supremum attained at Mobius-type domains (annular domains). (definable automorphism binary rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：可定义自同构群的 Phillips 群问题：可定义域（如 R^n 的可定义子域）的仅恒等的可定义自同构群的自同构刚性指数 AR(X)。猜想：AR(X)=1（可定义自同构平凡）对一般可定义域 X 成立；例外域的完全列表为半代数对称域（球的商与多面体的商）；且可定义等价（可定义双射）的商的基数为有限，基数谱的上确界为 2（二元刚性），上确界在 Möbius 型域（圆环域）取得。
