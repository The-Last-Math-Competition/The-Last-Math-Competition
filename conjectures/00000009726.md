# 00000009726

**SCA-26 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Multi-soliton interactions of critical generalized KdV: elastic versus inelastic losses of collisions. Conjecture: The inelastic energy loss of multi-soliton collisions of the nonintegrable critical gKdV is exponentially small, exp(-c sqrt T); the loss exponent is an explicit function of soliton velocity differences, and the loss is strictly zero in the integrable limit. (gKdV inelastic loss)

**中文。** 定义：临界广义 KdV 的多孤子相互作用：碰撞的弹性与非弹性损耗。猜想：非可积临界 gKdV 的多孤子碰撞的非弹性能量损失为指数小 exp(−c√T)；损失指数由孤子速度差的显式函数给出且可积极限下严格为零。（gKdV 非弹性损耗）
