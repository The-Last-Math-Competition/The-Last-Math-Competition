# 00000001715

**English.** Definition: The local limit theorem (LLT) for rotations concerns the asymptotic distribution of pointwise counting functions. Conjecture: The LLT has an explicit period-1 correction: the correction term is given by the Fourier coefficients of f through the decaying sequence c_k = (1−e(kα))^{-1}; the correction is nonzero if and only if f has nontrivial low-frequency content.

**中文。** 定义：旋转的局部极限定理(LLT)指逐点计数函数的渐近分布。猜想：LLT 修正为周期 1 的显式函数:修正项由 f 的 Fourier 系数经 c_k = (1−e(kα))^{-1} 的衰减序列给出;修正项非零当且仅当 f 有不平凡的低频成分。
