# 00000009650

**English.** Definition: The empirical distribution of eigenvector overlaps o_ij = <u_i,u_j>^2 of distinct eigenvalues (Bourgade-Dubach computed the limit for GOE/GUE). Conjecture: The empirical distribution of the upper triangle of the overlap matrix converges, over all universality classes, to the same explicit free-probabilistic limit; the density of the limit distribution is an explicit combinatorial formula with exponential tails, and the mean equals the universal scaling 2/n. (overlap distribution universal limit)

**中文。** 定义：不同特征值的特征向量重叠 o_ij=⟨u_i,u_j⟩² 的经验分布（Bourgade–Dubach 对 GOE/GUE 计算了极限）。猜想：重叠矩阵上三角的经验分布对一切普适类收敛到同一显式自由概率极限；极限分布的密度为指数尾的显式组合公式，均值等于 2/n 的普适标度。（重叠分布普适极限）
