**English.** Definition: The entropy of the hard-core model (hard squares) on Z² is the exponential growth rate of its equilibrium measures. Conjecture: A closed form exists: h = log(1.5030480824…), where the constant is the value of an explicit infinite product given by the dual free-fermion solution; the criterion for the entropy to be transcendental also holds.

**中文。** 定义：Z² 硬核模型(hard square)的熵指其平衡态测度指数增长率。猜想：该熵存在闭式:h = log(1.5030480824…) 其中该常数为某显式无穷乘积的值,该乘积由对偶自由费米子解给出;熵为超越数的判据同样成立。
