# 00000003167

**English.** Definition: Sparse PCA: the sparsity-constrained variant of principal components. Conjecture: The computational rigidity of sparse PCA: sparse PCA with support size s is NP-hard, with the multiplicative gap of approximation an explicit power of s/n. (sparse PCA approximation gap power)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏 PCA：主成分的稀疏约束变体。猜想：稀疏主成分的计算刚性：支撑大小为 s 的稀疏 PCA 为 NP 难且逼近的乘性间隙为 s/n 的显式幂。（稀疏 PCA 逼近间隙幂）
