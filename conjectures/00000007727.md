# 00000007727

**English.** Definition: A spectral-generic free position of a deterministic matrix pair (A_n, B_n) is an orthogonal O_n such that O_n A_n O_n^* and B_n are asymptotically free, with O_n from an explicit Diophantine rotation family. Conjecture: The set of spectral-generic free positions has full measure in the rotation group, its complement having Hausdorff dimension dim(SO(n)) - 1; and explicit constructions follow from irrationality conditions on the spectral points of A and B (no nontrivial integer relations among the spectral vector components). (full-measure generic free positions)

**中文。** 定义：确定性矩阵对 (A_n, B_n) 的谱泛型自由位置指存在 Haar 正交 O_n 使 O_n A_n O_n^* 与 B_n 渐近自由，且 O_n 可取自显式的 Diophantine 旋转族。猜想：谱泛型自由位置的集合在旋转群中为全测度集，其补集的 Hausdorff 维数为 dim(SO(n)) - 1；且显式构造可由 A,B 的谱点的无理性条件（谱向量分量间无非平凡整线性关系）保证。
