# 00000002743

**English.** Definition: Kemer theory: T-ideals of characteristic-zero PI algebras are finitely based (solving Specht's problem). Conjecture: The algorithmization of Kemer's representation classification: deciding Z₂-graded T-ideals of superalgebras is doable in polynomial time, with complexity cubic in the number of variables. (Kemer decision polynomial algorithm)

**中文。** 定义：Kemer 理论：特征零 PI 代数的 T-理想由有限基生成（Specht 问题解决）。猜想：Kemer 表示分类的算法化：超代数的 Z₂-分次 T-理想的判别可在多项式时间完成且复杂度为变量数的立方。（Kemer 判别多项式算法）
