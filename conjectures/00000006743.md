# 00000006743

**English.** Definition: Cotilting is the dual injective-type structure. Conjecture: The one-to-one correspondence of torsion pairs is given by modules; the cokernel of the correspondence is cotorsion-free; and the cokernel structure is the direct sum of cotorsion-free modules. (one-to-one torsion-pair cotilting)

**中文。** 定义：余倾斜为对偶的内射型结构。猜想：挠配对的一一对应由模给出；且对应的余核为余挠自由；且对应的余核结构为余挠自由模的直和。（余倾斜挠配对一一）
