# 00000004154

**English.** Definition: The Farey dissection is the arc dissection of the circle method, and the major arc width is the major arc radius. Conjecture: The optimal major arc width is of scale x^{-1+1/k}, and any widening or narrowing loses at least 1/(2k) in the provable exponent pairs. (optimal exponent of the major arc width)

**中文。** 定义：Farey 分割指圆法的弧分割,主弧宽指主弧半径。猜想：主弧最优宽度为 x^{−1+1/k} 型尺度,任何加宽或收窄都使可证指数对至少损失 1/(2k)。（主弧宽度指数最优）
