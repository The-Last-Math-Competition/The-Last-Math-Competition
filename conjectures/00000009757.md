**English.** Definition: Minimal axiom subset search: minimization of axiom dependencies of proofs. Conjecture: Searching for minimal axiom dependency subsets at given logical depth is NP-complete with the logarithmic approximation ratio optimal; the inapproximability gap is preserved from set cover by explicit reduction to arbitrary constant factors. (axiom minimization hardness)

**中文。** 定义：最小公理子集搜索：证明所依赖公理的极小化。猜想：给定逻辑深度的最小公理依赖子集的搜索为 NP 完全且对数近似比为最优；不可近似性间隙由集合覆盖的显式归约保持到任意常数因子。（公理极小化难度）
