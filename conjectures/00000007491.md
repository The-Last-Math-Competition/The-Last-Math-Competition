# 00000007491

**English.** Definition: The monodromy representation with the monodromy of period functions. Conjecture: The motivicity of representations holds, and the criterion of motivicity is the Zariski closure. (Zariski-closed monodromy motivicity)

**中文。** 定义：单值表示：周期函的单值。猜想：单值的母题：表示的母题性且性的判据为 Zariski 闭包。（单值 Zariski 闭包母题表示周期函判据性）
