# 00000003994

**English.** Definition: TC0 proof is the constant-depth proof system with threshold gates. Conjecture: The perfect matching principle for complete bipartite graphs has polynomial-length TC0-Frege proofs, and the required threshold degree is exactly 2, while degree 1, that is AC0, admits no polynomial proof. (matching TC0 threshold degree)

**中文。** 定义：TC0 证明指常数深度带门限门的证明系统。猜想：完全二部图完美匹配原理在 TC0-Frege 具多项式长度证明,且所需门限度恰为 2,度 1 即 AC0 无多项式证明。（匹配 TC0 门限度）
