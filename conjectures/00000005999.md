**English.** Definition: A very free curve is a rational curve of maximal splitting. Conjecture: The minimal-degree upper bound is linearly controlled by degree and dimension, and the exactness of the formula is the maximal splitting. (linear minimal degree of very free curves)

**中文。** 定义：非常自由曲线为极大分裂的有理曲线。猜想：极小度上界由度数与维数线性控制；且公式的精确性为分裂极大。（非常自由曲线线性极小度）
