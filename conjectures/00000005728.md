**English.** Definition: The two-phase problem is the transmission problem with different coefficients on the two sides. Conjecture: The two-dimensional regularity is always an explicit power law of the jump ratio, given by flatness iteration for small perturbations. (flatness-iteration power-law regularity of two phases)

**中文。** 定义：二相问题指两侧系数不同的传输问题。猜想：二维的正则性恒为跳比的显式幂律；小扰动时由平坦度迭代给出。（二相平坦迭代幂律正则）
