**English.** Definition: The Hasse principle is the local-to-global principle. Conjecture: The Hasse failure rate for curves in quadratic families is an explicit rational proportion given by counting Brauer group elements, with leading value 1/16. (Brauer formula of the Hasse failure rate)

**中文。** 定义：Hasse 原则指局部整体原则。猜想：二次族曲线的 Hasse 失败率为显式有理比例,比例由 Brauer 群元素计数给出且首值为 1/16。（Hasse 失败率 Brauer 公式）
