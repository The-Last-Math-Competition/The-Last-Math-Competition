**English.** Definition: On connected components of strata of unit abelian differentials, the Teichmueller geodesic flow is ergodic and exponentially mixing with respect to the Masur-Veech measure; the optimal mixing rate, the decay exponent sigma* of correlation functions, is the core undetermined constant. Conjecture: For every stratum component the optimal mixing exponent sigma* equals the explicit function sigma* = lambda_1 - lambda_2 - epsilon of the Kontsevich-Zorich spectral gap for every epsilon; and the lower bound 1/3 for sigma* is attained by explicit examples controlled by the boundary codimension of the compactification. (optimal exponential mixing rate)
