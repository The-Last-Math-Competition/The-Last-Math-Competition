**English.** Definition: Forman discrete curvature: combinatorial curvature and corresponds to of spectrum. Conjecture: spectrum of Forman curvature and graph Cheeger constant satisfies dual inequality; inequality in regular triangulation manifold on simultaneously and is equivalent to discrete Einstein. (Forman-Cheeger dual)

**中文。** 定义：Forman 离散曲率：组合曲率与谱的对应。猜想：Forman 曲率的谱与图 Cheeger 常数满足对偶不等式；不等式双方在正则剖分流形上同时取等且等价于离散 Einstein 条件。（Forman-Cheeger 对偶）
