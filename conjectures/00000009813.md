# 00000009813

**English.** Definition: Forman discrete curvature: the correspondence between combinatorial curvature and spectra. Conjecture: The spectrum of the Forman curvature and the graph Cheeger constant satisfy a dual inequality; both sides simultaneously attain equality on regular triangulated manifolds, equivalent to the discrete Einstein condition. (Forman-Cheeger duality)

**中文。** 定义：Forman 离散曲率：组合曲率与谱的对应。猜想：Forman 曲率的谱与图 Cheeger 常数满足对偶不等式；不等式双方在正则剖分流形上同时取等且等价于离散 Einstein 条件。（Forman-Cheeger 对偶）
