# 00000007798

**English.** Definition: Consistency of random simplex centroids: the centroid coefficient gamma_N(K) is the limit ratio E[vol(Gamma K_N)]/vol(Gamma K). Conjecture: The correction of gamma_N(K) at large N is 1 - c(K)/N^{2/(n+1)}, the exponent given by the critical index of the Santalo decomposition, with c(K) continuous under affine invariants and minimized to 1/2 at ellipsoids. (centroid coefficient correction exponent)

**中文。** 定义：随机单形重心与浮身点的一致性：K_N 的浮身系数 γ_N(K) 定义为 E[vol(Γ K_N)]/vol(Γ K) 的极限比。猜想：γ_N(K) 在 N→∞ 的修正为 1-c(K)/N^{2/(n+1)}，指数由 Santaló 分解的临界指标给出，c(K) 在仿射不变量下连续且其极小值在椭球取得为 1/2。
