# 00000009806

**English.** Definition: Discrete conformal equivalence by vertex scaling: the Bobenko-Pinkall-Springborn discrete uniformization framework. Conjecture: Discrete uniformization metrics of vertex-scaling classes exist and are unique; the unique metric converges under refinement to the smooth uniformization metric, with convergence order O(h) for circle pattern coordinates and O(h squared) for cross-ratio coordinates. (discrete uniformization unique convergence)

**中文。** 定义：顶点缩放的离散共形等价：Bobenko–Pinkall–Springborn 的离散均匀化框架。猜想：顶点缩放类的离散均匀化度量存在且唯一；唯一度量在细分下收敛到光滑均匀化度量且收敛阶为 O(h) 对圆模式坐标与 O(h²) 对交叉比坐标。（离散均匀化唯一收敛）
