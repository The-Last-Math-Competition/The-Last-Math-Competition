**English.** Definition: discrete of vertex scaling equivalent discrete homogenization frame of: Bobenko–Pinkall–Springborn. Conjecture: discrete homogenization metric exists of vertex scaling class and unique; unique metric in under converges to smooth homogenization metric and order of convergence is O(h) pair module expression coordinates and O(h²) pair intersection coordinates. (discrete homogenization unique convergent)

**中文。** 定义：顶点缩放的离散共形等价：Bobenko–Pinkall–Springborn 的离散均匀化框架。猜想：顶点缩放类的离散均匀化度量存在且唯一；唯一度量在细分下收敛到光滑均匀化度量且收敛阶为 O(h) 对圆模式坐标与 O(h²) 对交叉比坐标。（离散均匀化唯一收敛）
