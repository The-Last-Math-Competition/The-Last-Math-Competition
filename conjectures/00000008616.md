# 00000008616

**MLC-26 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: phase transition of quadratic functional estimate. Conjecture: deviation τ_n satisfies τ_n√n -> ∞ and window in τ_n = n^{−1/4} of rate τ_n²; linear functional. (functional estimate window)

**中文。** 定义：二次泛函估计的相变。猜想：偏差 τ_n 满足 τ_n√n→∞ 时相干且速率 τ_n²；线性泛函的准相干窗在 τ_n = n^{−1/4}。（泛函估计相干窗）
