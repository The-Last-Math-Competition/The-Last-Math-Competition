# 00000008616

**English.** Definition: The phase transition of quadratic functional estimation. Conjecture: When the bias tau_n satisfies tau_n sqrt n to infinity, estimation is efficient at rate tau_n^2; and the near-efficiency window of linear functionals sits at tau_n = n^{-1/4}. (functional estimation efficiency window)

**中文。** 定义：二次泛函估计的相变。猜想：偏差 τ_n 满足 τ_n√n→∞ 时相干且速率 τ_n²；线性泛函的准相干窗在 τ_n = n^{−1/4}。（泛函估计相干窗）
