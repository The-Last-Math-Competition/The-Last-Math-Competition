# 00000004399

**English.** Definition: A common graph is a graph for which the sum of its density and that of its complement has a uniform lower bound over all graphs. Conjecture: There exists a non-complete non-bipartite common graph of order 6, and 6 is the minimal order of such graphs. (minimal new order 6 of common graphs)

**中文。** 定义：common 图指其与补图密度和在一切图中有一致下界的图。猜想：存在阶为 6 的非完全非二部 common 图,且 6 为此类图的最小阶。（common 图最小新阶 6）
