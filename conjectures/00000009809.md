**DDG-19 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Rigidity of embedded surfaces: infinitesimal rigidity of isometrically embedded polyhedral surfaces (the Connelly conjecture direction). Conjecture: Extension of the infinitesimal rigidity of convexly embedded spherical triangulations (known by Cauchy): the rigidity set of star-convex embedded complexes is dense open in the embedding space; flexing examples must contain negative dihedral angles with odd self-intersection count. (star-convex embedding rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：嵌面刚性：等距嵌入折面的无穷小刚性（Connelly 猜想方向）。猜想：凸嵌入的球面三角剖分的无穷小刚性（Cauchy 已知）推广：星形凸嵌入复形的刚性集合在嵌入空间中稠密开；挠曲例子必含负二面角且自交数为奇。（星形凸嵌入刚性）
