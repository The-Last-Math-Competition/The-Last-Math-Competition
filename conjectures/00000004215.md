**English.** Definition: A simplicial spanning tree is a higher-dimensional tree of a simplicial complex, and the Smith normal form classifies the torsion part. Conjecture: The p-torsion prime factors of the simplicial tree Laplacian never exceed the number of vertices of the triangulation, with the bound attained by lens space triangulations. (linear upper bound on simplicial tree torsion primes)
