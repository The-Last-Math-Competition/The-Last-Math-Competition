# 00000007972

**English.** Definition: The universal spectrum of the free distance of convolutional codes: the expected free distance d_free of a convolutional code of memory m and rate 1/2 with random generator polynomials. Conjecture: d_free ~ n delta_GV(rate)(1 + gamma/m) (a convolutional correction of the Gilbert-Varshamov asymptotics), with gamma an explicit constant given by the derivative at zero of the large-deviation rate function of the state graph; the distribution of d_free concentrates within width O(1) (superconcentration); and strict positivity of the convolutional advantage exponent gamma is controlled by the forward drift of an asymmetric random walk. (convolutional free distance GV correction)

**中文。** 定义：卷积码的 free distance 的普遍谱：记忆 m 的卷积码（码率 1/2）的 free distance 的期望 d_free(随机生成多项式)。猜想：d_free ~ n·δ_GV(rate)·(1+γ/m)（Gilbert-Varshamov 渐近的卷积修正）；γ 为显式常数（由状态图的大偏差率函数在零点的导数给出）；且 d_free 的分布集中在宽度 O(1)（超集中）；卷积的优势指数 γ > 0 的严格正性由非对称随机游走的前置漂浮控制。
