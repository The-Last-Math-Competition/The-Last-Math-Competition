# 00000006420

**SD3-20 △** — source: 199_几何测度V_对称差问题.md

**English.** Definition: The isoperimetric deficit and asymmetry are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two sets with the same deficit but different asymmetry, and the separation is realized by an explicit two-circle union with the same deficit but different asymmetry. (same deficit-different asymmetry two-circle separation)

**中文。** 定义：等周亏格与不对称指两层。猜想：存在两集的亏格相同而不对称度不同,分离由同亏格异不对称的显式双圆并实现。（亏格同-不对称异双圆分离）
