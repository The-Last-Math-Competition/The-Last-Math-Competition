# 00000001681

**GT-21 ★** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: Rainbow-triangle-free edge colorings are complete-graph colorings without triangles of three distinct colors. Conjecture: The number of rainbow C₄'s in such colorings is at least (1/24)·n²; the extremal configuration is attained by recursive constructions from balanced 4-partite colorings, and the constant 1/24 is optimal.

**中文。** 定义：无彩虹三角的边染色指不含三色相异三角的完全图染色。猜想：此类染色的彩虹 C₄ 个数 ≥ (1/24)·n²;极值由 4-部平衡染色的递归构造达到,常数 1/24 最优。
