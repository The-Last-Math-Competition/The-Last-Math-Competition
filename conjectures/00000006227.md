**GT5-07 ★** — source: 193_几何范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Infinitesimals are the dimension structure of infinitesimal line objects in synthetic microgeometry. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit dimension of infinitesimal lines and the connection dimension of first-order synthetic differential is of dimension type; the curvature of the connection vanishes if and only if the space is projective, the projectivity given by level vanishing; and the sum of the dimensions is the space dimension, the sum unique. (connection-dimension conversion constant of synthetic differential)

**中文。** 定义：无穷小：综合微几何的无穷小线对象的维数结构。猜想：换算常数为无穷小线的显式维数且一阶综合微的联络维为维数型值；联络的曲率消没当且仅当空间为射影且射影性由层次消没给出；维数的和为空间维数且和式唯一。（综合微联络维数换算常数）
