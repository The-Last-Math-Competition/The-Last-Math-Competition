# 00000007799

**English.** Definition: High-dimensional intersection bodies: the lower bound on the volume of the Busemann intersection body IK when K is isotropic. Conjecture: vol(IK) >= c^n vol(K)^{(n-1)/n} for universal c, equivalent to a lower bound on the L_1 norm of the intersection function of isotropic bodies; the extremal bodies are generalized cylinders K = D plus-or-minus [-1,1] (direct sum), with the dimension ratio boundary at n/2 beyond which the extremal body switches to simplex type. (cylinder-simplex extremal switch)

**中文。** 定义：高维交体渐近：Busemann 交函数体 IK 的体积在 K 为迷向体时的下界。猜想：vol(IK) ≥ c^n vol(K)^{(n-1)/n}（c 普适），等价于迷向体的交函数的 L_1 范数的下界；极值体为广义圆柱体 K=D⊕[-1,1]（直和），其维数比在 n/2 处为分界，越过此界极值体转为单纯形型。
