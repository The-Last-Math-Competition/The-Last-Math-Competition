**English.** Definition: The blow-up rate is the time-asymptotic law of the singularity. Conjecture: The rate constant is the explicit friction coefficient of the critical profile, verified at the classical profile point, and the rate is exact with no higher-order corrections. (friction-constant rate of separation blow-up)

**中文。** 定义：爆破速率指奇性的时间渐近律。猜想：速率常数为临界剖面的摩擦系数显式,经典剖面积处验证且速率精确无高阶修正。（分离爆破摩擦常数速率）
