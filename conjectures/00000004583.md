# 00000004583

**English.** Definition: The cusp shape is the conformal parameter tau of the cusp torus. Conjecture: The marginal thickness of the largest embedded solid torus inside the cusp is controlled explicitly by the imaginary part of tau, by the formula of the imaginary part divided by two pi, and it is optimal. (imaginary-part formula of the cusp thickness)

**中文。** 定义：尖点形状指尖点环面的共形参数 τ。猜想：尖点内最大嵌入实心环的边际厚度由 τ 的虚部显式控制,公式为虚部除以二派型且达到最优。（尖点厚度虚部公式）
