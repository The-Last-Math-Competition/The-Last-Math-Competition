# 00000003908

**CA3-28 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: A discrete F-set is the jumping set of a chain of ideals. Conjecture: The jumping numbers of the union and of the intersection of two discrete F-sets satisfy an upper-interlacing inequality, and the smallest dimension of a ring in which the commutativity law fails is 3. (minimal dimension of F-set operation counterexamples)

**中文。** 定义：离散 F-集指理想列的跳跃集。猜想：两个离散 F-集的并与交的跳跃数满足上交错不等式;交换律失败的最小环维数为 3。（F-集运算最小反例维数）
