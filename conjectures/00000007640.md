**English.** Definition: Shock spectra and entropy-solution spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two equations with the same shock-position spectrum but different entropy-solution spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit viscosity-limit pair with the same shocks but different entropy. (same shock-different entropy viscosity separation)

**中文。** 定义：激波谱与熵解谱指两层。猜想：存在两方程的激波位置谱相同而熵解谱不同,分离由同激波异熵的显式粘性极限对实现。（激波同-熵异粘性分离）
