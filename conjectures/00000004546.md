**English.** Definition: The subspace theorem is the structural theorem of linear subspace approximation. Conjecture: The effective bound of its corollary is an explicit power of the height variable, the power exponent is given by the dimension of the subspace, and it cannot be improved by more than an arbitrarily small amount. (effective power bound of the subspace theorem)

**中文。** 定义：子空间定理指线性子空间逼近的结构定理。猜想：其推论的有效化界为高度变量的显式幂,幂指数由子空间维数给出且不可改进超过任意小量。（子空间定理有效幂界）
