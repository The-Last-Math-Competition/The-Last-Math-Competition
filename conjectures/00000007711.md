# 00000007711

**English.** Definition: The N-dimensional Sturmian structure is the combinatorial complexity p(n) of the stepped surface digitizing an irrational hyperplane H, counting n-cell patterns. Conjecture: For every essentially irrational slope of H, p(n) = c n^{N-1} + O(n^{N-2}), where the constant c equals the volume of the projected parallelotope when a coordinate of the normal vector of H equals 1; and the second-order coefficient of p(n) is given by the denominators of the three nearest rational approximations. (Sturmian complexity in dimension N)

**中文。** 定义：N 维 Sturmian 结构指无理超平面 H 的数字化的 stepped surface 的组合复杂度 p(n)（n-胞腔图案数）。猜想：对每个本性无理斜率的 H，p(n)=c n^{N-1} + O(n^{N-2})，其中常数 c 等于 H 的法向量的任一坐标为 1 时投影平行多面体的体积；且 p(n) 的二阶项系数由三个最近有理逼近的分母给出。
