# 00000001552

**GM-12 ★** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: The two-distance chromatic number is the least number of colors of the plane forbidding both distance one and the square root of two. Conjecture: Its exact value is six or seven; the two candidates are distinguished by triangular-lattice colorings and Moser-type lower-bound graphs respectively. (two-distance chromatic 6-7 decision)

**中文。** 定义：双距离色数指禁用一与根号二两种距离的平面最少色数。猜想：其精确值为六或七；且两种候选值分别由三角格染色与 Moser 型下界图区分。（双距离色数 6-7 判定）
