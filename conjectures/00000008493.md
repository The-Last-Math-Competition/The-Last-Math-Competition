# 00000008493

**English.** Definition: Nonlocked configurations: explicit constructions of functions on interval maps whose unique maximizing measure is an ergodic nonperiodic measure. Conjecture: Nonlocked functions exist (an existence law); their construction is given by Bousch's counterexample technique (a Bousch counterexample law); the regularity domain of the construction is the class of functions with Holder exponent above 1/2 (a half-exponent domain law); and no nonlocked functions exist below exponent 1/2 (a half-exponent rigidity law). (Bousch nonlocked half-exponent rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：非锁定构型：区间映射上极大化测度为唯一遍历非周期测度的显式函数构造。猜想：非锁定函数存在（存在律）；其构造由 Bousch 反例技术给出（Bousch 反例律）；构造的正则性域为 Hölder 指数大于 1/2 的函数类（半指数域律）；指数 1/2 以下的非锁定函数不存在（半指数刚性律）。
