# 00000001417

**English.** Definition: The closed-string coproduct of string topology is the coproduct operation on the homology H_*(LM) of the free loop space. Conjecture: Together with the Chas–Sullivan product, this coproduct forms a BV-algebra structure whose compatible coderivation exists and is unique; the entire structure for compact oriented M is given explicitly by the cyclic symmetry of intersection diagrams.

**中文。** 定义：string topology 的闭弦余积指自由环路空间同调 H_*(LM) 上的余积运算。猜想：该余积与 Chas–Sullivan 积共同构成 BV 代数结构,且其兼容余导子存在并唯一;结构对紧定向 M 的 entirety 由相交图的轮换对称性显式给出。
