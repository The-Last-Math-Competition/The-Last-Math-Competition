# 00000009880

**English.** Definition: The variance constant of stationary LPP: the coefficient of the t^{2/3} term of stationary passage times. Conjecture: The variance constant of stationary LPP has a universal explicit value glued continuously to the known value of the zero-drift special case; the constant varies as an explicit rational function of the drift and flattens out. (stationary variance universal constant)

**中文。** 定义：稳态 LPP 的方差常数：stationary 通过时间的 t^{2/3} 项系数。猜想：稳态 LPP 的方差常数有普适显式值且该值与零漂移特例的已知值连续拼接；常数在漂移不为零时按漂移的显式有理函数变化并趋于平缓。（稳态方差普适常数）
