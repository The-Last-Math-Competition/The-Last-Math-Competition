# 00000003143

**English.** Definition: Stochastic differential equations on Lie groups: Brownian motion and the heat kernel. Conjecture: The asymptotic rigidity of the heat kernel: the short-time expansion coefficients of the heat kernel of a compact group are bi-invariant polynomials, with the generators of the polynomials the Casimir spectrum. (heat kernel bi-invariant polynomial expansion)

**中文。** 定义：李群的随机微分方程：Brown 运动与热核。猜想：热核的渐近刚性：紧群热核的短时展开的系数为 Bi-Inaviant 多项式且多项式的生成元为 Casimir 谱。（热核双不变多项式展开）
