# 00000009800

**DDG-10 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Hexagonal remeshing: reconstruction of triangulations into approximately hexagonal lattices. Conjecture: The necessary total count of defect vertices of hexagonal remeshings of genus-g closed surfaces is the discrete realization of 6 times |2 - 2g|, and the bound is attainable; defect valences are limited to five and seven, with defect pair spacing at least twice the mesh size. (hexagonal mesh defect formula)

**中文。** 定义：六角网格化：三角剖分到近似六角连线的重构。猜想：亏格 g 的闭曲面的六角网格化的必要缺陷顶点总数为 6 倍 2−2g 的绝对值的离散实现且该下界可达到；缺陷的价分布限于五与七且缺陷对的间距下界为网格尺寸的两倍。（六角网格缺陷公式）
