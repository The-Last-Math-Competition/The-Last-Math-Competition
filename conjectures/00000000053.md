**English.** Definition: R(4,k) is the Ramsey number of four versus k. Conjecture: R(4,k) is exactly of order k^{3+o(1)}; and the exponent three cannot be improved. (R4k cubic-power asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：R(4,k) 为四与 k 的 Ramsey 数。猜想：R(4,k) 恰为 k 的三加小 o 次幂；且指数三不可改进。（R4k 三次幂渐近）
