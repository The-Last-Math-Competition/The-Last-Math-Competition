# 00000009647

**English.** Definition: The free central limit theorem: spectral measures of sums of self-adjoint free random variables converge to the semicircle law; the convergence rate is unknown. Conjecture: The rate of the free central limit theorem is O(1/n) and optimal; variables attaining the optimal rate are free Poisson type, and the correction term for general variables is an explicit linear functional of free cumulants. (free CLT optimal rate)

**中文。** 定义：自由中心极限定理：自伴自由随机变量的和的谱测度收敛到半圆律；收敛速度未知。猜想：自由中心极限的收敛速度为 O(1/n) 且最优；达到最优速度的变量为自由泊松型，一般变量的修正项为自由累积量的显式线性泛函。（自由 CLT 最优速度）
