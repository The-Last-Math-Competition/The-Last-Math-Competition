# 00000000351

**English.** Definition: The covering radius of the D_n lattice is determined by its coordination structure. Conjecture: For dimensions up to eight, the D_n lattices are covering-optimal, with the first non-D exception in dimension nine; and the exceptional lattice configuration is given by an E₈ extension. (Dn lattice covering optimal through dimension eight)

**中文。** 定义：D_n 格的覆盖半径由其配位结构决定。猜想：维数不超过八时 D_n 格覆盖最优且维九出现第一个非 D 例外；且例外格的构型由 E₈ 延拓给出。（Dn 格覆盖维八最优）
