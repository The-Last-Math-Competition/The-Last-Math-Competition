# 00000008832

**English.** Definition: The forward-backward-forward three-step splitting algorithm. Conjecture: FBF always converges under joint Lipschitz monotonicity; the step-size domain is an explicit joint constraint on the three step sizes; and adaptive backtracking preserves stability. (FBF three-step convergence step domain)

**中文。** 定义：向前-向后-向前三步分裂算法。猜想：fbf 在联合 Lipschitz 单调假设下恒收敛；步长域为三步长的显式联合约束且自适应回溯保持稳定。（fbf 三步收敛步长域）
