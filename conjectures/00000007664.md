# 00000007664

**QDE-04 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: The Jackson q-Gamma function is Gamma_q(x)=(1-q)^{1-x}(q;q)_infinity/(q^x;q)_infinity, where (a;q)_infinity is the infinite q-Pochhammer product. Conjecture: For every algebraic q in (0,1) and any two distinct positive integers m, n, the numbers Gamma_q(m/n) and Gamma_q(1-m/n) are algebraically independent over Q(q), except when m/n=1/2. (algebraic independence of q-Gamma values)

**中文。** 定义：Jackson q-Gamma 函数 Γ_q(x)=(1-q)^{1-x} (q;q)_∞/(q^x;q)_∞，其中 (a;q)_∞=Π_{k≥0}(1-a q^k)。猜想：对任意代数数 q∈(0,1) 与任意两个不同的正整数 m,n，数 Γ_q(m/n) 与 Γ_q(1-m/n) 在 Q(q) 上代数无关，除非 m/n=1/2。
