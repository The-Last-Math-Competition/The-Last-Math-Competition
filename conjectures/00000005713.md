# 00000005713

**English.** Definition: The blow-up cone is the tangent limit cone at a singularity. Conjecture: The classification table of classical cones is an explicit two-parameter family of symmetric cones, the family separated monotonically by frequency; the frequency values are half-integer lattice points with spacing 1/2; and mixing of two blow-up cones never occurs at equal frequencies, the separation given by the cone apex angle. (two-parameter frequency separation of symmetric cones)

**中文。** 定义：爆破锥：奇点处的切极限锥。猜想：经典锥的分类表为对称锥的二参数显式族且族由频率单调分隔；频率的取值为半整数格点且格距为 1/2；两个爆破锥的混合在频率相同时不出现且分离由锥顶角给出。（对称锥二参数频率分隔）
