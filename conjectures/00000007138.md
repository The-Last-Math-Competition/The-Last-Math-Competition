# 00000007138

**PL4-18 ★** — source: 223_多面体.md

**English.** Definition: The combinatorial rigidity of polytopes with face-lattice isomorphism. Conjecture: Face-lattice isomorphism implies combinatorial equivalence, and the geometric extension of the equivalence is the poset of neighborhoods. (neighborhood-poset face-lattice rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：多面体的组合刚性：面格的同构。猜想：面格的刚性：面格同构蕴含组合等价且价的几何推广为邻域的偏序。（面格偏序邻域组合等价几何推广刚性）
