**English.** Definition: Barron space is the approximable function class of single-hidden-layer networks. Conjecture: Its Fourier effective sparsity bound is an explicit integral of the squared Barron norm, equality is attained at radial functions, and the bound is tight. (Fourier sparsity integral bound of Barron space)
