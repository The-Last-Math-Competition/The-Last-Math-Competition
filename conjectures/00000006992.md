# 00000006992

**English.** Definition: Endpoint deficit with the deficit law of Sobolev-type endpoints. Conjecture: The deficit grows with dimension and the closed recursion is binomial convolution; the generating function is rational type; and the parity of the deficit is locked by the dimension. (binomial-convolution endpoint deficit law)

**中文。** 定义：端点亏格：Sobolev 型端点的亏格律。猜想：亏格随维数且封闭的递推为二项卷积；母函数为有理型；亏格的奇偶由维数锁定。（端点亏格二项卷积律结构）
