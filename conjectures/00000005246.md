**MS3-16 ★** — source: 160_代数几何V_模空间.md

**English.** Definition: The Betti numbers are the dimension sequence of the moduli-space homology. Conjecture: The Betti numbers of rank three and genus two are always given by singularity-stratified weighted polynomials, and the polynomials agree with enumerative counts. (stratified weighted polynomials of rank three genus two)

**中文。** 定义：贝蒂数指模空间同调的维数列。猜想：秩三亏格二的贝蒂数恒由奇异性分层加权的多项式给出；且多项式与枚举计数一致。（秩三亏格二分层加权多项式）
