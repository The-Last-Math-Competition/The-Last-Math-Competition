**English.** Definition: The Gibbs measure is the formal ensemble of energy and mass. Conjecture: The supercritical case is renormalized by normalization with explicit parameter, the combination of the renormalization being the Wick ordering. (Wick-ordering renormalization of supercritical Gibbs measures)

**中文。** 定义：Gibbs 测度为能量质量的形式系综。猜想：超临界情形由规范化重整且参数显式；且重整的组合为 Wick 序。（Gibbs 超临界 Wick 序重整）
