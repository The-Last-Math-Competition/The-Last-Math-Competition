# 00000001033

**FG-33 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: Orthogonal arrays OA(N,k,s,t). Conjecture: The maximum k of OA(s^t,k,s,t) at the Rao bound is attained by linear MDS codes and only then (an OT-MDS equivalence).

**中文。** 定义：正交表 OA(N, k, s, t)。猜想：OA(s^t, k, s, t) 的最大 k(Rao 界处)由线性 MDS 码达成且仅此时(OT-MDS 等价)。
