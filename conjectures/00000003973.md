# 00000003973

**PC2-03 ★** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: The Chvatal-Gomory rank is the minimal number of rounds of iterated Chvatal-Gomory closure needed to reach the integer hull of a polytope. Conjecture: The CG rank of every 0/1 polytope is at most n^2 (1 + o(1)), and there exists a family of partition polytopes attaining quadratic order, so the supremum is Theta(n^2). (supremum order of CG rank)

**中文。** 定义：Chvatal–Gomory rank 指多面体经迭代取整理想到达整数 hull 的最少轮数。猜想：一切 0/1 多面体的 CG rank 至多 n²(1+o(1)),且存在划分多面体族达到二次阶,上确界为 Θ(n²)。（CG rank 上确界阶）
