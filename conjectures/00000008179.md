# 00000008179

**CNT-09 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: Index calculus for exponential integrals and discrete logarithms: the optimal smoothness threshold smooth_thr(p) of index calculus for the DLP over F_p. Conjecture: smooth_thr (the optimal radius of the smoothness base B) is exp(sqrt(log p log log p)) (a subexponential optimality law); the expected number of relations in the collection phase of the index is 2 B log B (a relation-base product law); and the minimal r for migrating the DLP on weak fields with p = 1 mod r (embedding attack) is the one-sixth power of p (a sixth-power migration threshold), the threshold being given by the dual embedding of the Weil pairing (a Weil dual threshold). (DLP subexponential Weil dual threshold)

**中文。** 定义：指数积分与离散对数的指标计算：有限域 F_p 的 DLP 的指标计算的最优平滑阈 smooth_thr(p)。猜想：smooth_thr（平滑基 B 的最优半径）= exp(√(log p·log log p))（次指数最优律）；且指标的收集阶段的关系数的期望 = 2·B·log B（关系-基积律）；DLP 的 p=1 mod r 的弱域（嵌入攻击）的迁移的最小 r 为 p 的 1/6 次（六次迁移阈），阈值由 Weil 配对的对偶嵌入（Weil 对偶阈）给出。
