**English.** Definition: The rigidity measure of symplectic desks: the squeezing threshold sq_th of the rigidity (nonsqueezability) of open symplectic manifolds. Conjecture: sq_th (the critical radius of Gromov squeezing) is half the EZ capacity (a half capacity law); the complete classification of squeezing exceptions (squeezable domains) is the Borman class of symmetric domains (a Borman exceptional class); the minimal member of the class is the 2:1 ellipsoid (a two-one ellipsoid example); and ellipsoids are squeezable if and only if their weight sequences are convex (a weight convexity criterion law). (Gromov squeezing half capacity ellipsoid convexity)

**中文。** 定义：辛 desk 的刚性测度：辛流形（开）的刚性（无挤压性）的挤压阈 sq_th。猜想：sq_th（Gromov 挤压的临界半径）= EZ 容量的一半（半容量律）；且挤压的例外（可挤压的域）的完全分类为对称域的 Borman 例外（Borman 例外类）；类的最小例外为 Ellipsoid 的 2:1（二一椭球例）；椭球的可挤压的充要为重量序列的凸性（重量凸性判据律）。
