# 00000003470

**English.** Definition: A perfect elimination ordering is the vertex-elimination structure of chordal graphs, with the elimination count the number of orderings satisfying the simple successor condition. Conjecture: The elimination-ordering count of chordal graphs is given explicitly as the product of the planar-embedding counts of maximal-clique trees; the logarithmic asymptotics of the count are the combined quantity of leaf count times the logarithm of leaf degree. (elimination count formula)

**中文。** 定义：完美消除排序为弦图的顶点消去结构，消除计数为满足简单后续条件的排序个数。猜想：弦图的消除排序数由极大团树的平面嵌入选数的乘积显式给出；计数的对数渐近为叶子数乘叶子度的对数的组合量。（消除计数公式）
