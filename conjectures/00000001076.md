# 00000001076

**English.** Definition: The distribution of nontrivial sums of multiplicative characters. Conjecture: The third moment is an explicit polynomial in the prime; and the kernel of the polynomial is the Jacobi sum. (character-sum Jacobi third-moment polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：乘法特征的非平凡和分布。猜想：第三矩为素数的多项式显式；且多项式的核为 Jacobi 和。（特征和 Jacobi 第三矩多项式）
