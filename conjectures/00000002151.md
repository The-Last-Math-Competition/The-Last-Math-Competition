# 00000002151

**English.** Definition: A non-bipartite Ramanujan graph is a d-regular non-bipartite graph whose second eigenvalue is at most 2√(d−1). Conjecture: Infinite families exist for every d ≥ 3 (a non-bipartite extension of MSS); the spectral gap of the construction is exactly 2√(d−1); and the Marcus–Spielman–Srivastava matching-polynomial method extends.

**中文。** 定义：非二部 Ramanujan 图指第二特征值 ≤2√(d-1) 的 d-正则非二部图。猜想：每个 d≥3 的无穷族存在(MSS 的非二部推广);构造的谱间隙为 2√(d-1)(精确达到);Marcus–Spielman–Srivastava 的匹配多项式方法可推广。
