**English.** Definition: The spinor space is the sphere bundle of a hyper-Kaehler manifold. Conjecture: Its Hodge parity criterion is an explicit condition of the induced structure, verified at quadratic symmetric product types, and the criterion is complete. (induced criterion of spinor space parity)
