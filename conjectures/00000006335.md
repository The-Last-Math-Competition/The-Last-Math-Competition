**English.** Definition: Conference matrices are the orthogonal one-type with zero diagonal. Conjecture: The existence criterion is explicit of prime type and the classical construction covers a class of primes, the kernel of the covering being quadratic residues. (quadratic-residue prime type of conference matrices)

**中文。** 定义：会议矩阵为零对角的正交一型。猜想：存在判据的素型显式且经典构造覆盖一类素；且覆盖的核为二次剩余。（会议矩阵二次剩余素型）
