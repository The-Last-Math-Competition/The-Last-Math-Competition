# 00000007821

**MSE-11 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Rigid points of the Hurwitz space H_{d,g} of covers with monodromy marking are covers without infinitesimal automorphisms; the Ellenberg-Venkatesh type bound asserts that rigidity is generic for large d. Conjecture: The number of Q-rational connected components of the full monodromy cover H_{d,g}^{full} is at most 2; and for d at least 2g+3 the rigid points are dense on every component in the weak approximation sense, with the degree of the exceptional divisor controlled by an explicit polynomial in d^2 g. (Hurwitz rigidity density bound)

**中文。** 定义：Hurwitz 空间 H_{d,g}（单值群标记的覆盖模空间）的刚体点为无无穷小自同构的覆盖。Ellenberg–Venkatesh 型界断言其单性在 d 大时普遍。猜想：一般单值标记 H_{d,g}^{full} 的 Q-有理连通分量的个数 ≤ 2；且当 d≥2g+3 时 H_{d,g}^{full} 的每个分量上刚体点稠密于弱逼近意义，例外除子的次数被 d²·g 的显式多项式控制。
