# 00000003313

**English.** Definition: The combinatorics of Ext modules: Chow rings and Hodge structures. Conjecture: The combinatoratorial characterization of Ext: the dimensions of graded Ext are counts of combinatorial objects (trees, tableaux), with the generating function of the counts rational. (Ext combinatorial count rational generating)
