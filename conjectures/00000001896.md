# 00000001896

**English.** Definition: cov(κ,κ,ω,2) is the number of families of size < κ needed to cover. Conjecture: The realizable interval of cov at κ = ℵ_ω is [ℵ_{ω+1}, 2^{ℵ_ω}]; the interval endpoints are controlled by explicit pcf-indexed models.

**中文。** 定义：cov(κ,κ,ω,2) 指覆盖 <κ 大小集族所需基数。猜想：κ=ℵ_ω 的 cov 值可实现区间为 [ℵ_{ω+1}, 2^{ℵ_ω}];区间端点由 pcf 索引的显式模型控制。
