# 00000005383

**English.** Definition: The canonical limit is the limit process of height iteration. Conjecture: The convergence rate is the explicit geometric series of the degree, the leading error is the Lipschitz constant of the difference of local functions, and the formula is exact. (geometric convergence-rate formula of canonical limits)

**中文。** 定义：标准极限指高度迭代的极限过程。猜想：收敛速率为度量的几何级数显式,误差首项为局部函数差的利普希茨常数且公式精确。（标准极限几何收敛速率式）
