**English.** Definition: The Yang–Mills mass gap is the minimal nonzero energy in the spectrum of the theory. Conjecture: The 3D Yang–Mills mass gap satisfies m² ≥ c/e⁴ with the explicit constant c = 1/32; a rigorous lower bound solving the 3D precursor of the Clay 4D problem.
