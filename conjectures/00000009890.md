**GBR-10 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Broue equivalence of abelian p-blocks: derived equivalence of blocks with local subpairs for cyclic defect groups. Conjecture: The categorical construction of Broue equivalence for cyclic defect groups is unique up to isomorphism; uniqueness follows from the rigidity of minimal splendid Rickard complexes, and the term count of the complex is the factor count of the defect group order. (cyclic block equivalence uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：交换 p-块的 Broué 等价：亏群循环时块与群的局部子对的导等价。猜想：循环亏群的 Broué 等价的范畴构造在同构意义下唯一；唯一性由极小 splendid Rickard 复形的刚性给出且复形的项数为亏群阶的因子数。（循环块等价唯一性）
