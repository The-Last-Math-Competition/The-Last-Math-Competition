# 00000002297

**FR-07 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: A controller is the controlling-subgroup structure of p-local subgroups in a fusion system. Conjecture: The minimal size of the transfer layer of the controller is at most polynomial (degree 2) in the rank; the bound follows from a layered construction of controller subsystems (avoiding exponential towers).

**中文。** 定义：controller 指融合系统中 p-局部子群的控制子群结构。猜想：控制器的传递层的最小尺寸 ≤ rank 的多项式(次数 2);界由 controller subsystem 的层叠构造(指数塔的避免)。
