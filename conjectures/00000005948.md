**English.** Definition: The last-passage time is the optimal passage time of paths. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit rate of the time constant, fourfold rate in the square limit; the square-rectangle conversion of the time constant is a single constant, the square of 2; and the limit shape of the last-passage surface is deterministic, with parabolic boundary. (fourfold rate conversion of square last-passage times)

**中文。** 定义：末过时间：路径的最优通过时间（last passage）。猜想：换算公式为时间常数的显式率且方形极限时为四倍率；时间常数的方形-矩形换算为单常数且常数为 2 的平方；末过面的极限形状为确定且形状的边界为抛物型。（末过时间方形四倍率换算）
