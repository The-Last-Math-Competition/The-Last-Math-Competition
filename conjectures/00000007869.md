# 00000007869

**RAM-29 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The random-order competitive ratio of online bin packing: the expected competitive ratio R_ro^n under a uniformly random arrival order. Conjecture: Every deterministic online algorithm has R_ro^n >= 3/2 + epsilon_0 for an explicit positive epsilon_0, while Simple-Best-Fit achieves R_ro^n <= 3/2 + O(n^{-1/2}); and the optimal separation of the random-order advantage over the worst order is the explicit jump from 4/3 to 3/2, determined by extremal instances of bimodal volume distributions. (random-order bin packing separation)

**中文。** 定义：在线箱子装箱的随机序 (random-order) 竞争比：在随机均匀到达序下的期望竞争比 R_ro^n。猜想：对一切确定性在线算法 R_ro^n ≥ 3/2+ε₀（ε₀ 为显式正常数），且 Simple-Best-Fit 的 R_ro^n ≤ 3/2+O(n^{-1/2})；随机序优势（相对最坏序）的最优分离为 4/3 → 3/2 的显式跃迁，由双峰体积分布装箱问题的极值实例决定。
