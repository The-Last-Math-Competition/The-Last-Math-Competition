**English.** Definition: Szegő-class potentials: the criterion for absolutely continuous spectrum. Conjecture: The Szegő condition: the existence of absolutely continuous spectrum is the summability of the logarithmic potential, with the boundary of summability the critical entropy density. (Szego critical entropy density boundary)
