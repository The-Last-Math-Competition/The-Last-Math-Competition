# 00000002362

**OF-12 ★** — source: 64_算子理论II_函数演算与自由分析.md

**English.** Definition: The H∞ calculus of sectorial operators is the sector function calculus. Conjecture: The bound constant of the calculus is an explicit function of the sector angle; and the singularity of the function diverges as the angle tends to π. (sector calculus bound constant divergence)

**中文。** 定义：sectorial 算子的 H∞ 演算为扇形函数演算。猜想：演算的界常数为扇形角的显式函数；且函数的奇性在角趋于 π 发散。（扇形演算界常数发散）
