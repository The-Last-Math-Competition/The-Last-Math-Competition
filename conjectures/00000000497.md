# 00000000497

**SA-97 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: A family of osculating paths may share vertices (oscillate) at lattice points but not edges; O_n denotes their count with fixed endpoints. Conjecture: O_n/#ASM_n = 2^{n(n−1)/2}·(1 + c₁n^{-1} + O(n^{-2})) with c₁ = 1/4; that is, each independent choice of an osculating pair contributes a factor 2.

**中文。** 定义：osculating 路径族指在格点处可共享顶点(相切)但边不相交的路径族,其到达固定终点的计数记 O_n。猜想：O_n/#ASM_n = 2^{n(n−1)/2}·(1+c₁n^{-1}+O(n^{-2})),c₁ = 1/4,即相切对的每一独立选择贡献因子 2。
