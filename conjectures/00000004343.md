# 00000004343

**FP2-13 ◆** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: The logarithmic Sobolev inequality is the inequality between entropy and Fisher information. Conjecture: The optimal logarithmic Sobolev constant of the symmetric stable measure of stability exponent alpha is the explicit formula of type 2/(2-alpha), the constant is optimal, and it degenerates to the Gaussian value 2 as alpha tends to zero. (LSI constant formula of stable measures)

**中文。** 定义：对数 Sobolev 不等式指熵与费歇尔信息间的不等式。猜想：稳定指数 α 的对称稳定测度的最优对数 Sobolev 常数为 2/(2−α) 型显式公式,常数最优且在 α 趋于零退化为高斯值 2。（稳定测度 LSI 常数公式）
