**English.** Definition: Centrally finite division rings D: their maximal subfields and dimension structure. Conjecture: The maximal chain length of maximal subfields of a division ring is the sum of multiplicities of the prime factorization of the square root of the dimension; each chain is realized by explicit Galois tower constructions of Cartan-Brauer-Hua type embeddings. (division ring subfield chain length formula)
