**English.** Definition: Multi-operator spectra, Taylor spectra. Conjecture: The joint spectrum of multi-operators holds with the Koszul complex; and the homological dimension of the Koszul complex is exactly the Taylor spectral depth. (Koszul-complex Taylor joint spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：多算子谱：Taylor 谱。猜想：Taylor 谱的联合：多算子的联合谱且谱的 Koszul 复形。(Taylor 谱 Koszul 复形多算子联合谱谱联合)；且 Koszul 复形同调维数恰为 Taylor 谱深度。
