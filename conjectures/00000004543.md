# 00000004543

**English.** Definition: An E-function is a series satisfying exponential growth and a differential equation with integer coefficients. Conjecture: The irrationality exponent of its algebraic values is at most the explicit bound two plus d over n, where d is the order of the equation, and the bound is optimal, attained at Bessel values. (order bound of E-function irrationality)

**中文。** 定义：E 函数指满足指数增长与整系数微分方程的级数。猜想：其代数值的无理度指数不超过二加 d 除以 n 型显式界,d 为方程阶,界在贝塞尔值达到最优。（E 函数无理度阶数界）
