# 00000008713

**MBL-03 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: l-bits: a complete set of exponentially localized diagonal operators in the MBL phase. Conjecture: The rigorous construction of l-bits holds for strongly disordered chains (a construction law); their tail decay rate mu is at least the minimum single-particle localization rate (a decay lower bound law); the spectral gap of mu has a strictly positive lower bound in the deep MBL regime (a positive lower bound law); and the l-bit algebra generates the entire quasi-local operator algebra (a generation law). (l-bit construction tail decay algebra generation)

**中文。** 定义：l-比特：MBL 相中满足指数局域的对角算子完备组。猜想：l-比特的严格构造在强无序链成立（构造律）；且其尾巴衰减率 μ 不小于单粒子局域化率的最小值（衰减下界律）；μ 的谱隙在深 MBL 域有严格正下界（正下界律）；l-比特代数生成整个准局域算子代数（生成律）。
