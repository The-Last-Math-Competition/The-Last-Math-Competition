**English.** Definition: The proof-theoretic strength of the modal mu-calculus: the ordinal mu_ord of well-founded proof systems for fixed-point logic. Conjecture: The proof-theoretic ordinal of the mu-calculus is the third layer omega^omega^omega of the Buchholz hierarchy (third-layer rigidity of the mu-calculus); the inflation rate of eliminating nested fixed points (alternation depth d) is an exponential tower of d-1 layers (an alternation-elimination tower law); and the proof-theoretic conversion between the mu-calculus and the solution complexity of parity games (quasi-polynomial, known) is: the polynomial exponent of the solution complexity agrees with the inflation rate of fixed-point elimination (a solution-elimination consistency law). (mu-calculus third-layer alternation tower)
