# 00000006459

**English.** Definition: Universality is the random-matrix law of the spectrum. Conjecture: The parity symmetry breaking of adjacent spacings is explicit and the combination of the breaking is the symmetry class; even-parity spacings are GOE type while odd parity has an additional repulsion factor 2; and the statistical significance of the breaking grows with spectral height. (parity symmetry-class breaking structure of spacings)

**中文。** 定义：普遍分布：谱的随机矩阵律。猜想：相邻间距的奇偶对称破缺显式且破缺的组合为对称类；偶宇称的间距为 GOE 型而奇宇称附加排斥因子 2；破缺的统计显著性随谱高增加。（间距奇偶对称类破缺结构）
