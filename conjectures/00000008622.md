**INP-02 ★** — source: 273_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: q-TASEP is the exclusion process with deformation parameter q. Conjecture: The closed-form leading term of its n-th moment recursion is a product of reciprocals of q-Pochhammer symbols; and the recursion is stable if and only if q < 1. (q-TASEP moment closed product)

**中文。** 定义：q-TASEH 指带形变参数 q 的排斥过程。猜想：其 n 阶矩递推的闭式首项为 q-Pochhammer 倒数积；递推稳定当且仅当 q < 1。（qTASEH 矩闭式积）
