# 00000008019

**DEL-29 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: Anzellotti pairings and the degenerate domain of Green formulas: the admissible class G_mu of Green formulas for equations of divergence type with measure data div A(grad u) = mu. Conjecture: The complete characterization of G_mu is absolute continuity of mu with respect to p-capacity (the classification of the distribution of the mu-perp cap_p part); the optimal power of the singularity of the Green function is G(x,y) ~ |x-y|^{-(n-p')(1+theta)} (theta a codimension correction); and the integrability threshold of the Green function agrees with the critical exponent of compactness of the Sobolev embedding (a critical coincidence law), the mismatch occurring only in the logarithmic window at n = p' (the conformal dimension, the unique log-corrected dimension). (measure-data Green capacity admissibility)

**中文。** 定义：Anzellotti 配对与 Green 公式的退化域：散度型测度数据的方程 div A(∇u)=μ 的 Green 公式的合理类 G_μ。猜想：G_μ 的完全刻画为 μ 关于 p-容量的绝对连续（μ⊥cap_p 部分的分布归类）；且 Green 函数的奇异性的最优幂 G(x,y) ~ |x-y|^{-(n-p')·(1+θ)}（θ 为余维数修正）；Green 函数的可积性阈值与 Sobolev 嵌入的紧性的临界指数一致（临界重合律），重合的失配仅在 n=p'（共形维数）的对数窗口（log 修正的唯一例外维）。
