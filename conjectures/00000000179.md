# 00000000179

**English.** Definition: The growth of the continued-fraction partial quotients a_n of e^e. Conjecture: limsup log a_n / log n = 1; the upper bound follows from an effective version of the Gelfond-type transcendence measure of e^e, and the lower bound from a rounding construction of exponential approximations. (partial quotient growth of e-e)

**中文。** 定义：e^e 的连分数部分商 a_n 的增长。猜想：limsup log a_n / log n = 1；上界由 e^e 的 Gelfond 型超越度量的有效版本给出，下界由指数逼近的凑整构造达到。（e-e 部分商增长律）
