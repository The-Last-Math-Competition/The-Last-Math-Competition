# 00000002255

**English.** Definition: Borel's identity theorem (frequency grouping of exponential polynomials). Conjecture: The error term of the difference Borel theorem is an explicit negative power of the minimal gap of characteristic frequencies (difference Borel error).

**中文。** 定义：Borel 恒等式定理(指数多项式的级分组)。猜想：差分 Borel 定理的误差项为特征频率的最小间隙的显式负幂(差分 Borel 误差)。
