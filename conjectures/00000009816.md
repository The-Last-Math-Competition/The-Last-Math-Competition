# 00000009816

**English.** Definition: Umbilics of subdivision surfaces: limit regularity of Loop and Catmull-Clark subdivision. Conjecture: The umbilic count of Loop subdivision limit surfaces has an upper bound depending only on the initial mesh; umbilics vanish generation by generation under subdivision or freeze into persistent pairs, and the count of persistent pairs is conserved. (subdivision surface umbilic counting)

**中文。** 定义：细分曲面的脐点：Loop 与 Catmull–Clark 细分的极限正则性。猜想：Loop 细分极限曲面的脐点个数有仅依赖初始网格的上界；脐点在细分下逐代消失或固化为持久对且持久对的计数守恒。（细分曲面脐点计数）
