# 00000007921

**ATC-21 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The Eisenstein mixing of the core number representation of quadratic fields: the analytic refinement of the Cohen-Eisenstein relation between the Fourier coefficients c(|d|) of half-integral weight modular forms and class numbers. Conjecture: The square mean sum_{|d|<=x} c(|d|)^2 = C x log x + O(x) (a logarithmic main term law), with C explicitly expressed by the central value of L(1, sym^2 g); and the supremum of a single c(|d|) is |d|^{1/4+epsilon} (the square-root barrier for convexity breaking), the barrier being asymptotically attained at Erdos-type extremal discriminants. (Cohen coefficient square mean law)

**中文。** 定义：二次域的核数表示的 Eisenstein 混合：半整权模形式的 Fourier 系数 c(|d|) 与类数的 Cohen–Eisenstein 关系的解析精化。猜想：c(|d|) 的平方平均 Σ_{|d|≤x} c(|d|)² = C·x·log x + O(x)（主项对数律），常数 C 由 L(1,sym² g) 的中心值显式表出；且单个 c(|d|) 的上确界为 |d|^{1/4+ε}（凸性破缺的平方根势垒），势垒在 d 为 Erdős 型极端判别式时渐近达到。
