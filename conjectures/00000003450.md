**English.** Definition: Müntz approximation: the density of sparse power bases. Conjecture: The Müntz theorem: the density of the power basis is the divergence of the reciprocal sum, with the necessity of divergence the separation of exponents. (Muentz reciprocal sum divergence exponent separation)
