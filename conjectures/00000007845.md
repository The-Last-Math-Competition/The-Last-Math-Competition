# 00000007845

**RAM-05 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The expected error decay rate R(A) of the randomized Kaczmarz iteration (projection onto randomly selected rows weighted by spectral norm) for Ax = b: R(A) = E[||x_{k+1}-x*||^2/||x_k-x*||^2]. Conjecture: The worst value of R(A) over full-row-rank A is 1 - kappa^{-2} (kappa the condition number), and the bound is exact; the geometric mean improvement rate E[log ratio] attains its optimum at equicontiguous tight frames as a correction of log(1-1/n), with deviation explicitly controlled by the dispersion variance of the row Gram matrix. (Kaczmarz rate and ETF optimality)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Kaczmarz 迭代（依谱范数行加权随机选行投影）求解 Ax=b 的期望误差衰减率 R(A)=E[||x_{k+1}-x*||²/||x_k-x*||²]。猜想：R(A) 的最坏值（遍历行满秩 A）为 1-κ^{-2}（κ 为条件数），且该界精确；几何平均改进率 E[log ratio] 在行向量等角紧框架（ETF）取到最优值 log(1-1/n) 的修正，偏差由行向量 Gram 矩阵的离差方差显式控制。
