**English.** Definition: expectation error decay rate R(A)=E[||x_{k+1}-x*||²/||x_k-x*||²] of random Kaczmarz iteration (spectrum norm row weighted random projection) solution Ax=b. Conjecture: most (ergodic rank A) of R(A) is 1-κ^{-2} (κ is condition number), and positive, deviation of the bound exact; geometric mean E[log ratio] in row to quantity angle tight frame (ETF) optimal log(1-1/n) by difference variance explicit controls of row to quantity Gram matrix.

**中文。** 定义：随机 Kaczmarz 迭代（依谱范数行加权随机选行投影）求解 Ax=b 的期望误差衰减率 R(A)=E[||x_{k+1}-x*||²/||x_k-x*||²]。猜想：R(A) 的最坏值（遍历行满秩 A）为 1-κ^{-2}（κ 为条件数），且该界精确；几何平均改进率 E[log ratio] 在行向量等角紧框架（ETF）取到最优值 log(1-1/n) 的修正，偏差由行向量 Gram 矩阵的离差方差显式控制。
