# 00000007518

**English.** Definition: The etale cohomology of smooth hypersurfaces is characterized by Lefschetz pencils. Conjecture: The nontrivial part of the cohomology is a one-dimensional eigensheaf, the eigenvalue spectrum given explicitly by the Dwork family; and the spectral gap is a p-adic unit root. (eigen-spectrum hypersurface cohomology)

**中文。** 定义：光滑超曲面的平展上同调由 Lefschetz 束刻画。猜想：上同调的非平凡部分为一维本征层且本征值的谱由 Dwork 族显式给出；且谱的间隙为 p-adic 单位根。（超曲面上同调本征谱）
