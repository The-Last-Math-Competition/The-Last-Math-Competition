# 00000002600

**English.** Definition: The Möbius function μ(P): the inverse of the incidence algebra of a poset. Conjecture: The strongest extension of the alternating sign of μ for geometric lattices is the log-concavity of μ on Cauchy posets; the extremal lattice of the concavity is the subspace lattice of projective geometry. (Mobius log-concavity rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Möbius 函数 μ(P)：偏序集的关联代数逆元。猜想：几何格的 μ 交替符号的最强推广为柯西偏序集的 μ 的对数凹性；凹性的极值格为投影几何的子空间格。（Mobius 对数凹刚性）
