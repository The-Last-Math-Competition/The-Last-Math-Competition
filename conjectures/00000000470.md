# 00000000470

**English.** Definition: The random weighted tree polynomial τ_p(G) = Σ_T p^{w(T)}, over spanning trees T of G, where w(T) is a sum of independent random edge weights. Conjecture: For vertex-transitive graph families, the arguments of the complex conjugate roots of p become asymptotically uniformly distributed over the interval from 0 to 2π, and the Kolmogorov distance between the empirical argument measure and the uniform measure is O(v^{-1/2}), where v is the vertex count.

**中文。** 定义：随机加权树多项式 τ_p(G)=Σ_{T}p^{w(T)},T 取遍 G 的生成树,w(T) 为独立随机边权之和。猜想：对顶点传递图族,p 的共轭复根的辐角渐近均匀分布于从 0 到 2π 的区间,且辐角经验测度与均匀测度的 Kolmogorov 距离为 O(v^{-1/2}),v 为顶点数。
