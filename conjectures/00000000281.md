# 00000000281

**English.** Definition: The hyperbolicity density is the relative area of the set of parameters c ∈ M for which z²+c is hyperbolic on the orbit of 0. Conjecture (density of hyperbolicity, a consequence of MLC): The hyperbolicity parameters are dense in M; in the stronger form, the complement has area measure zero.

**中文。** 猜想：定义:双曲性密度:|{c ∈ M : z²+c 在 0 的轨道上双曲}| / area(M ∩ 界框)。猜想(双曲性稠密,MLC 的推论):双曲参数在 M 中稠密;更强形式:其补集面积测度为零。
