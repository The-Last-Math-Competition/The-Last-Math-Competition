**English.** Definition: The randomized escape from the Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem in social choice: the compatibility rand_compat of manipulation-immunity and efficiency for randomized social choice functions. Conjecture: The feasible domain of rand_compat (randomized immunity-efficiency compatibility) is the maximin criterion (a maximin compatibility law); the uniqueness of random dictatorship (the characterization of all compatible mechanisms) is strict (a unique characterization law); and the minimal number of agents at which the conflict between strengthened efficiency (strict Pareto) and immunity becomes explicit is 4 (a four-agent conflict threshold). (randomized dictatorship maximin four-agent conflict)

**中文。** 定义：社会选择的 Gibbard–Satterthwaite 的随机化的逃避：随机社会选择函数的操纵免疫与效率的相容 rand_compat。猜想：rand_compat（随机免疫-效率的相容）的可行域为最大最小准则（maximin 相容律）；且随机独裁（random dictatorship）的唯一性（一切相容机制的表征）的严格（唯一表征律）；效率的强化（严格 Pareto）与免疫的矛盾的显性化的最小代理为 4（四代理矛盾阈）。
