**RP2-26 ◆** — source: 119_随机分析II_粗路径.md

**English.** Definition: Lyons extension theorem denotes continuous extension of cut line infinite order. Conjecture: perturbation stability constant of extension is polynomial, perturbation exponent in order at exactly 1 of truncation order and not. (extension stability order)

**中文。** 定义：Lyons 延拓定理指割线提升到无穷阶的连续延拓。猜想：延拓的扰动稳定性常数为截断阶的多项式,扰动指数在阶跃迁处恰降 1 且不可恢复。（延拓稳定性阶跃）
