# 00000004026

**English.** Definition: The Lyons extension theorem is the continuous extension of secant lifts to infinite order. Conjecture: The perturbation stability constant of the extension is a polynomial in the truncation order, and the perturbation exponent drops by exactly 1 at order transitions and cannot be recovered. (order jump of extension stability)

**中文。** 定义：Lyons 延拓定理指割线提升到无穷阶的连续延拓。猜想：延拓的扰动稳定性常数为截断阶的多项式,扰动指数在阶跃迁处恰降 1 且不可恢复。（延拓稳定性阶跃）
