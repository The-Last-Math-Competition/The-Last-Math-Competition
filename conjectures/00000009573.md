# 00000009573

**English.** Definition: The relation between the visual boundary of Teichmueller space (equivalence classes of geodesic rays) and the Thurston boundary is a classical compactification problem. Conjecture: The natural map from the Teichmueller visual boundary to the Thurston boundary is a discontinuous injection whose continuity locus is the uniqueness locus of geodesic foliations, a full-measure set with complement of strictly smaller dimension; and the Busemann function equivalence classes induced by both boundaries admit countable classification. (visual boundary classification)

**中文。** 定义：Teichmüller 度量的视觉边界（测地射线等价类）与 Thurston 边界的关系是 Teichmüller 空间紧化的经典问题。猜想：Teichmüller 视觉边界与 Thurston 边界之间的自然映射非连续单射，其连续点集为测地叶状结构的唯一性点集，该集合为满测度且余集维数严格小；两类边界诱导的 Busemann 函数等价类均可数分类。（视觉边界分类）
