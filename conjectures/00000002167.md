# 00000002167

**GA-27 ★** — source: 57_代数图论II.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral radius of n-vertex graphs of girth ≥ 5. Conjecture: The spectral radius is attained by Moore-approximate graphs (Brown type or PC-lab type); the classification of extremal graphs holds for all n ≥ 5 (uniqueness of spectral-radius extrema).

**中文。** 定义：围长 ≥5 的 n 点图的谱半径。猜想：谱半径由 Moore-近似图(Brown 型或 PClab 型)达到;极值图的分类对一切 n≥5(谱半径极值唯一性)。
