# 00000008792

**NLA-22 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Sparse recovery: compressed sensing with random measurements. Conjecture: Random (Gaussian) matrices satisfy RIP when m is at least C k log(n/k) (a sampling law); the law is optimal (lower bound, an optimality law); sparse (0/1) projections carry corrections (a correction law); and the correction has sparse bias (a bias law). (RIP Gaussian sampling sparse bias)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏恢复：随机测量的压缩感知。猜想：RIP 的随机矩阵（高斯）的 m≥Ck log(n/k) 的（采样律）；且律的最优性（下界）的（最优律）；稀疏投影（0/1 的）的修正（修正律）；修正的偏差（稀疏偏差）的（偏差律）。
