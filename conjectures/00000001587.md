# 00000001587

**English.** Definition: The thermodynamic entropy of the hard-square model is the limit of the logarithm of its per-site partition function. Conjecture: This entropy admits no Rogers–Ramanujan type infinite product representation (no q-series closed form); the rigorous criterion: the q-difference equation of the entropy transcends all algebraic q-equations, hence the constant is non-D-finite.

**中文。** 定义：硬方格模型的热力学熵指其每格配分函数对数的极限。猜想：该熵无 Rogers–Ramanujan 型无穷乘积表示(即非 q-级数闭式);严格判据:熵的 q-差分方程超越一切代数 q-方程,因而为非 D-finite 常数。
