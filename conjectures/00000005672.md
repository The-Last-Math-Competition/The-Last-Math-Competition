# 00000005672

**English.** Definition: Fixed-point stability is the attracting property of the limit. Conjecture: The stability criterion is the explicit condition on the Jacobian spectral radius, stable when below one, with a unique unstable direction at criticality. (Jacobian-radius stability criterion of fixed points)

**中文。** 定义：不动点稳定性指极限的吸引性质。猜想：稳定判据为雅可比谱半径的显式条件,半径小于一稳定且临界处失稳方向唯一。（不动点雅可比半径稳定判据）
