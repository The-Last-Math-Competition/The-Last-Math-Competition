# 00000009628

**English.** Definition: The homoclinic group H(X,sigma) (points homoclinic to the identity) is a normal subgroup of Aut; the structure of the quotient Aut/H is the core of Boyle's program. Conjecture: The quotient Aut(X,sigma)/H(X,sigma) is solvable for every mixing SFT, with derived length uniformly bounded by 2 in the stable rank of the space; and the quotient is finite if and only if the system is a minimal extension of a cyclic cover. (homoclinic quotient solvability)

**中文。** 定义：同宿群 H(X,σ)（同宿于恒等的点集）为 Aut 的正规子群；商群 Aut/H 的结构是 Boyle 纲领核心。猜想：商群 Aut(X,σ)/H(X,σ) 对一切混合 SFT 为可解群且导出列长度由空间的稳定秩一致有界为 2；商群为有限群当且仅当系统为循环覆盖的极小延拓。（同宿商群可解性）
