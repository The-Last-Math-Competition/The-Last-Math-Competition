# 00000003851

**RM3-01 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: In the Dumitriu–Edelman tridiagonal model of the β-Hermite ensemble, the bulk gap denotes the limiting distribution of adjacent eigenvalue differences s = λ_{k+1} − λ_k away from both edges. Conjecture: The gap density satisfies p_β(s) = C_β s^{β}(1 + o(1)) near zero, with C_β log-convex in β and explicitly computable at β = 2. (beta small gap log-convexity)

**中文。** 定义：β-Hermite 系综的 Dumitriu–Edelman 三对角模型中,体中间隔指远离两端的相邻特征值差 s = λ_{k+1} − λ_k 的极限分布。猜想：间隔分布密度在零点附近满足 p_β(s) = C_β s^{β}(1 + o(1)),且 C_β 关于 β 对数凸,在 β = 2 处可显式求值。（β-小间隔对数凸）
