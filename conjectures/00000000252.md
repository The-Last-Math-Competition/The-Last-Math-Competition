**GE-21 ◆** — source: 15_图与极值参数.md

**English.** Definition: An antimagic labeling is an injection of the edges by 1 to m such that the vertex sums are pairwise distinct. Conjecture: Every connected graph without isolated vertices is antimagic; the determination is settled through a parity-adjustment mechanism. (antimagic parity adjustment)

**中文。** 定义：反魔标号指边以一到 m 单射标号且顶点度和两两不同。猜想：一切无孤立点的连通图均反魔；判定的核为奇偶调节机制。（反魔奇偶调节无孤立点判定）
