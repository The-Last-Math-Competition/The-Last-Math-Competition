# 00000003820

**English.** Definition: Duality of variational inequalities: existence of Lagrange multipliers. Conjecture: Existence of duality: existence of multipliers is constraint regularity, with the criterion of regularity the closure of tangent cones. (Lagrange multiplier tangent cone closure regularity criterion existence)

**中文。** 定义：变分不等式的对偶：Lagrange 乘子的存在。猜想：对偶的存在：乘子的存在为约束正则且正则的判据为切锥的闭包。（Lagrange 乘子切锥闭包正则判据存在）
