# 00000008336

**DIA-16 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The pair correlation breaking threshold for Poissonian statistics of equidistributed sequences: the threshold poi_pair for Poissonian pair correlation of gap statistics. Conjecture: poi_pair (universality of Poissonian pair correlation) holds for all concave sequences alpha_n = n^2 theta with decay (a concave sequence law); the explicit breaking configuration (non-Poisson) is Kronecker phase locking (a phase-locking configuration law); the unlocking of phase locking (the measure of unlocking parameters) is full measure (a full-measure unlocking law); and the Poissonization rate (convergence to Poisson gaps) is n^{-1/2} exponential (an exponential convergence law). (pair correlation phase-locking full-measure Poissonization)

**中文。** 定义：等分布的 Poissonian 对相关的破缺阈值：点列的间隙统计的 Poisson 的对相关（RSA 破缺）的阈 poi_pair。猜想：poi_pair（对相关的 Poisson 型的普遍性）对一切凹序列 α_n=n²·θ 的衰减成立（凹序列成立律）；且破缺（非 Poisson）的显式构型为 Kronecker 的锁相（锁相构型律）；锁相的解锁（解锁参数的测度）的全测度（全测解锁律）；解锁的速率（间隙的 Poisson 化）的收敛速率为 n^{-1/2} 的指数（指数收敛律）。
