**English.** Definition: An affinoid algebra is the convergent power-series algebra of Witt-vector type. Conjecture: The two-dimensional conversion formula is the explicit lattice bound of the valuation radius, given by the extremum of the weight norm; the admissible set of radii is the lattice of points with p-power denominators; and the extremum is attained at equal-weight vectors, with value the harmonic mean of the weights. (two-dimensional valuation lattice radius conversion)

**中文。** 定义：仿刚体代数：收敛幂级数代数（Witt 向量型）。猜想：二维的换算公式为赋值半径的显式格点界且由权范数的极值给出；半径的容许集为 p 的幂分母格点；极值在等权向量处达到且达到值为权的调和平均。（二维赋值格点半径换算）
