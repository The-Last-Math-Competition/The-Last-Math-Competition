# 00000000426

**SA-26 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: A cyclic sieving phenomenon (CSP) triple (X, C, X(q)). Conjecture: There exist CSP instances where the rotation group has prime order p and X(q) takes the value 1 at all p-th roots of unity (a separation of trivial sieving from nontrivial counting).

**中文。** 定义：循环筛化(cyclic sieving, CSP)三元组 (X, C, X(q))。猜想：存在 CSP 实例使旋转群的阶为素数 p 且 X(q) 在 p 次单位根的值全为 1(平凡筛化与不平凡计数的分离)。
