# 00000007823

**MSE-13 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The volume vol(Q(a_1,...,a_k)) of a stratum of quadratic differentials, normalized by the Eskin-Mas counting measure. Conjecture: The ratio of vol(Q(a)) to the volume of the corresponding principal stratum lies in the polynomial ring Q[pi] with degree at most the number of zeros; and the asymptotics of vol as the dimension grows is a double-exponential function of the dimension, its exponential constant agreeing with the free energy of the perfect matching polynomial. (quadratic stratum volume algebraicity)

**中文。** 定义：二次微分 stratum Q(a_1,…,a_k) 的体积 vol(Q(a))（由 Eskin–Mas 计数测度规范）。猜想：vol(Q(a)) / vol(相应主 stratum) ∈ Q[π]（π 的多项式环）且次数 ≤ 零点个数；且 vol 的渐近在维度 →∞ 时为维度的双指数函数，其指数常数与 perfect matching 多项式的自由能一致。
