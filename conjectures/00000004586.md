# 00000004586

**English.** Definition: The systole is the length of the shortest closed geodesic. Conjecture: The systole upper bound for hyperbolic three-manifolds of fixed genus is of the explicit doubled-logarithm-of-genus type, the bound is attained by sequences of link complement spaces and cannot exceed twice that. (logarithmic upper bound of the three-dimensional systole)

**中文。** 定义：Systole 指最短闭测地线长。猜想：固定亏格的双曲三维流形的 systole 上界为二倍对数亏格型显式,界由链环补空间序列达到且不可超两倍。（三维 systole 对数上界）
