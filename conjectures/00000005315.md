**English.** Definition: The mixed-structure stability threshold is the stabilization point of the weight stratification. Conjecture: Within genus five the threshold is the explicit constant of weight two, given by the commutative obstruction, with no higher-weight jumps. (weight-two stability constant of mixed structures)
