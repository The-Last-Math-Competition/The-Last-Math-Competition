# 00000009926

**English.** Definition: Multiparameter persistent homology: the invariant completeness problem of the two-parameter setting. Conjecture: A minimal family of complete invariants exists: the rank invariant plus a two-dimensional degeneracy measure family is complete, with family cardinality at least three; any two-invariant family admits isomorphism classes where separation fails. (multiparameter minimal complete family)

**中文。** 定义：多参数持久同调：双参数的不变量完备性问题。猜想：完备不变量的最小族存在：秩不变量加两维的退化测度族为完备且族的基数不小于三；任何两不变量族均存在分离失败的同构类。（多参数最小完备族）
