# 00000008754

**English.** Definition: Big Witt vectors. Conjecture: The equivalence of big Witt vectors with Hensel pairs always holds; and the kernel of the standard form of the pair is given by integrality of ghost components. (big Witt Hensel ghost integrality)

**中文。** 定义：Witt 向量的大德_vectormap。猜想：大德向量与 Hensel 对的等价恒成立；且对的标准形式的核由幽灵分量的整性给出。（大德 Hensel 幽灵整性）
