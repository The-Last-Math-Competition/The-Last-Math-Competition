# 00000008050

**English.** Definition: The duality of tame topology and irrationality measures: the dual constant mu_tam(f) of the measure of definable domains of transcendental functions (a tame dual of the Mahler measure). Conjecture: mu_tam(f) (a tamed constant of Khintchine-type duality) is at least 2 (a tame-topology version of the lower bound of the Lehmer conjecture); and the tamed realization of equality at the Lehmer number is an approximation sequence of minimal Mahler measures of o-minimal embeddings of algebraic number fields, with convergence rate -log log H (double-logarithmic convergence); the tame-topology framework does not change the Lehmer constant (a tame-algebraic invariance), the mechanism being the definability of the Jensen integral of the Mahler measure (definable constancy of the Jensen integral). (tame Mahler Lehmer invariance)

**中文。** 定义：驯拓扑与无理测度的对偶：超越函数的可定义域的测度（Mahler 测度的驯对偶）的对偶常数 μ_tam(f)。猜想：μ_tam(f)（Khintchine 型对偶的驯化常数）≥ 2（ Lehmer 猜想的驯拓扑版下界）；且等号 Lehmer 数的驯化实现为代数数域的 o-极小嵌入的极小 Mahler 测度的逼近序列，逼近速率为 -log log H（双重对数收敛）；驯拓扑框架不改变 Lehmer 常数（驯-代数不变性），不变性的机制为 Mahler 测度的 Jensen 积分的可定义化（Jensen 积分的可定义常数性）。
