# 00000007089

**English.** Definition: The quasi-equivalence theorem with the Whitehead theorem for A-infinity categories. Conjecture: The equivalence of triangulated A-infinity categories is the equivalence of homology, and the minimum of the equivalence is the generation of closed objects. (closed-object quasi-equivalence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：准等价定理：A∞-范畴的 Whitehead。猜想：准等价定理：三角 A∞-范畴的等价为同调的等价且价的极小为闭对象的生成。（准等价闭对象同调极小生成等价判据）
