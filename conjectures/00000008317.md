# 00000008317

**English.** Definition: The Bott-type correction operator bott_mod for families of Reeb orbits in contact homology: the linearized embedding of family cohomology into the orbit chain complex. Conjecture: bott_mod is the linearization of equivariant cohomology, and the corrected differential is exactly the twisting of the Morse-Bott spectral difference with the Conley-Zehnder index; the resonance threshold for the eigenvalues of the family normal bundle is 1, and beyond resonance the series converges after renormalization by the Ruelle functional; and the imaginary parts of resonance poles are lattice-distributed with density equal to the topological entropy of the Reeb flow divided by 2 pi. (Bott operator resonance Ruelle lattice density)

**中文。** 定义：切触同调中 Bott 型 Reeb 轨道族的修正算子 bott_mod：族上同调到轨道链复形的线性化嵌入。猜想：bott_mod 为等变上同调的线性化且修正后的微分恰为 Morse–Bott 谱差与 Conley–Zehnder 指标的绞合；族法丛特征值的共振阈为 1，超共振情形经 Ruelle 泛函重整后级数收敛；共振极点的虚部按晶格分布且密度等于 Reeb 流拓扑熵除以 2π。
