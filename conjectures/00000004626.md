# 00000004626

**English.** Definition: The maximal genus of free-boundary minimal surfaces is the supremum of the genera in this class. Conjecture: In the unit ball the maximal genus of a free-boundary minimal surface with b boundary components is the explicit 3b-3, and the bound is attained by stacking constructions. (free-boundary genus 3b minus 3 bound)
