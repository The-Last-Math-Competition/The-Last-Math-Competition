# 00000003170

**CS2-10 ★** — source: 91_稀疏化与压缩感知的数学.md

**English.** Definition: Chernoff concentration and sparse estimation: tail bounds for empirical processes. Conjecture: The bracketing entropy of sparse empirical processes: the bracketing count for subclasses of support size s is s·log(ed/s), with the tail bound the square of the entropy root. (bracketing entropy sparse support law)

**中文。** 定义：Chernoff 集中与稀疏估计：经验过程的尾界。猜想：稀疏经验过程的括号熵：支撑大小为 s 的子类的括号数为 s log(ed/s) 且尾界为熵根的平方。（括号熵稀疏支撑律）
