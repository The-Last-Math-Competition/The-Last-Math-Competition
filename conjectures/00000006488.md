# 00000006488

**English.** Definition: The smoothness difference with the variance-window structure of smooth functionals. Conjecture: Both ends of the smoothness-difference variance window are explicit and the combination of closure is the fourth moment; the weight of the fourth moment is the cumulant constant; and the contraction rate of the window is the fourth power of the bandwidth. (closed fourth-moment window of smoothness differences)

**中文。** 定义：平滑差：平滑泛函的方差窗结构。猜想：平滑差方差窗两端显式且封闭的组合为四阶矩；四阶矩的权为累积量常数；窗的收缩率为带宽的四次幂。（平滑差四阶矩窗封闭）
