**English.** Definition: The algebraic stability index sft_alg of symplectic field theory on contact manifolds: the invariance under contact isomorphisms of the BV algebra of holomorphic curve counts. Conjecture: The homological stability threshold of sft_alg is the Morse-Bott families of nondegenerate orbits of the Reeb vector field; under the frequency filtration the spectral sequence degenerates exactly at page 3, and the E_3 page is isomorphic to the graded dual of contact homology; and the correction coefficients lie on the integer lattice generated by normal bundle Euler characteristics and are integer multiples of the Ruelle zeta values of the elliptic complex of periodic orbits. (SFT Morse-Bott page-three Ruelle lattice)
