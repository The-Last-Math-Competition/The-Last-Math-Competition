**English.** Definition: A twisted FI module is the extended structure with twisting. Conjecture: The minimal generation table of rank two is given explicitly by the orbit representatives of the twisting, closed under quadratic twisting, with generator count equal to the orbit count. (orbit minimal generation table of twisted FI modules)
