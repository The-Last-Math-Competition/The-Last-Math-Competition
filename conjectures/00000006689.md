# 00000006689

**English.** Definition: Power diagrams with the tessellation structure of weight lifting. Conjecture: The refinement-coarsening chain is finite and the chain length is the weight-difference sum; the weight-difference sum is the infimum; each step of the chain is a single weight lift, with logarithmic step size. (finite-chain structure of power diagrams)

**中文。** 定义：正则剖分：权提升的剖分结构（power diagram）。猜想：细化粗化链有限且链的长度为权差和；权差和为下确界；链的每步为单权提升且提升的步长为对数型。（正则剖分有限链结构）
