# 00000007954

**INT-24 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The monodromy group of the Stokes net of the analytic continuation of Frobenius manifolds (Dubrovin structure): the image group Mon(M) of the monodromy representation of flat coordinates on the complement of the discriminant (caustic). Conjecture: Mon(M) is a triangle group Delta(p,q,r) with p,q,r determined by the elementary symmetric functions of the spectral side; and the rigidity of Mon (the dimension of the Zariski closure) is a finite group when 1/p+1/q+1/r > 1, affine when equal to 1, and a lattice when less than 1; and the invertibility of the Frobenius manifold (existence of a predual) is equivalent to the vanishing of the weight-3 decomposition of Mon on the Ranada-type Hopf invariant. (Frobenius triangle monodromy trichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：Frobenius 流形（Dubrovin 结构）的解析延拓的 Stokes 网的单值群：判别式（caustic）补集上的平坦坐标的单值表示的像群 Mon(M)。猜想：Mon(M) 为三角形群 Δ(p,q,r)（p,q,r 由谱侧的初等对称函数的判定）；且 Mon 的刚性（Zariski 闭包的维数）当 (1/p+1/q+1/r)>1 时为有限群、=1 时为仿射、<1 时为格；Frobenius 流形的可逆性（存在预对偶）等价于 Mon 的权 3 分解的在 Ranada 型Hopf 不变量上的消失。
