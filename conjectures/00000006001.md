# 00000006001

**English.** Definition: A flip is the sign-correcting operation on derived canonical divisors. Conjecture: The existence of four-dimensional flips holds at terminal singularities, the classification of special flips is closed, and the closed table of special flips recurses by the Shokurov polytopes. (terminal existence and closure of four-dimensional flips)

**中文。** 定义：翻转为导出典范的符号修正操作。猜想：四维翻转的存在在终端奇点处成立；且特殊翻转的分类封闭；且特殊翻转的封闭表由 Shokurov 拼图递推。（四维翻转终端存在封闭）
