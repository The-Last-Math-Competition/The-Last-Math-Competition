# 00000007886

**English.** Definition: The bridging invariant between cyclotomic spectra and the p-adic Langlands program: the p-complete negative homotopy groups of the cyclotomic spectrum TC(R) of a condensed ring R. Conjecture: The negative homotopy groups of TC^{-}(Z_p[C_{p^infinity}]), as a function sheaf on the parameter space of p-adic local Langlands, are the completion of half-integral weight modular forms; and its trace formula under the Galois action agrees with the K-group index of the Breuil (phi,Gamma)-module classification, the equality following from the relative theorem of morphism K-theory. (cyclotomic Langlands trace identity)

**中文。** 定义：cyclotomic 谱与 p-adic Langlands 的桥接不变量：对凝聚态环 R 的 cyclotomic 谱 TC(R) 的 p-完备负同伦群。猜想：TC^{-}(Z_p[C_{p^∞}]) 的负同调群作为 p-adic 局部 Langlands 参数空间的函数层为半整权模形式的完备化；其 Galois 作用下的迹公式与Breuil的 (φ,Γ)-模分类的 K-群指标一致，等式由态射-K 理论的相对化定理给出。
