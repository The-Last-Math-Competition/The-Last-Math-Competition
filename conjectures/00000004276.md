# 00000004276

**English.** Definition: The type of a rank-1 group is the equivalence class of its characteristic sequence, and the type poset is its natural partial order. Conjecture: All maximal dense intervals of the type poset are mutually isomorphic, with a unified sample given by the explicit lattice of subsets of prime exponents. (unified sample of type dense intervals)
