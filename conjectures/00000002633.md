# 00000002633

**English.** Definition: The convergence domain of Fourier–Jacobi expansions of modular forms: tube-domain geometry. Conjecture: The absolute convergence domain of Fourier–Jacobi expansions on the Siegel upper half-plane is completely determined by positivity of the quadratic form of the index; the boundary singularities of the domain are Yamanoi-type critical layers. (FJ convergence domain geometry)

**中文。** 定义：模范式的 Fourier–Jacobi 展开的收敛域：管域几何。猜想：Fourier–Jacobi 展开在 Siegel 上半平面的绝对收敛域由指标的二次型正性完全确定；收敛域的边界奇点为 Yamanoi 型临界层。（FJ 收敛域几何）
