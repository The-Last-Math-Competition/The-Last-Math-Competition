# 00000005168

**English.** Definition: Hodge-Tate structures are the one-sided spectral type of prismatic cohomology. Conjecture: The one-sided weight conversion is the spectral formula of semilinear filtrations with constant spectrum in the unimodular case; the spectrum table at low genus (at most 3) is finite with spectral width at most the genus; and the degeneration points correspond one-to-one with the singularity types of semistable reduction. (one-sided spectral formula of Hodge-Tate weights)

**中文。** 定义：Hodge–Tate 结构：棱镜上同调的特殊单边谱型。猜想：权单边换算为半线性滤子的谱公式且幺模情形谱为常值；低亏格（不超过 3）的谱表为有限且谱宽不超过亏格；谱退化点与半稳定约化的奇点型一一对应。（Hodge–Tate 权单边谱公式）
