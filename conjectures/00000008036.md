**English.** Definition: A tame-topology criterion for Valiant's VP versus VNP: the taming bound VC_tam of the VC dimension of definable curve families (geometric realizations of polynomial-size circuits). Conjecture: The VC dimension of definable curve families is linear in circuit depth (tamed counting); and the geometric realization of VP (polynomiality of the volume measure of definable families) characterizes VP (the volume criterion: VP if and only if definable volume is polynomially bounded); the verification domain of the volume criterion is (R,+,x,exp), the necessity of exp being forced by excluding the realization of the exponential growth of the counting completeness of VNP. (VP definable volume polynomial criterion)
