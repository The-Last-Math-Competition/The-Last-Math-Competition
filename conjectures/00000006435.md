# 00000006435

**MF4-05 ★** — source: 200_模形式III_Maass形.md

**English.** Definition: Small eigenvalues with the counting of small-eigenvalue Maass forms. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit upper bound of the small-value count and the count for any congruence group is bounded; the minimum of the bound is at the modular group with count zero; and the growth of the upper bound is logarithmic in the level. (explicit upper-bound conversion of small-value counting)

**中文。** 定义：小特征：小特征值 Maass 形的计数。猜想：换算常数为小值计数的显式上界且任意同余群的个数有界；界的极小在模群处且个数为零；上界的增长为水平的对数。（小值计数显式上界换算）
