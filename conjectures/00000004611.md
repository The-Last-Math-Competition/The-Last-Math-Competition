**MS2-11 ◆** — source: 139_几何测度IV_极小曲面系统.md

**English.** Definition: A minimal surface system is a coupled multi-phase family of minimal interfaces. Conjecture: The system is regular in its interior in dimension at most seven, an explicit first singularity appears in dimension eight, and the singular set has codimension at least four with the bound attained. (dimension-eight regularity threshold of systems)

**中文。** 定义：极小曲面系统指多相耦合的极小界面组。猜想：维数不超过七时系统内部正则,维数八出现首例显式奇点,奇点集余维至少为四且达界。（系统正则八维阈值）
