# 00000007852

**RAM-12 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The information threshold rho_c(n) of random graph matching: vertex-correspondence recovery for a pair (G_1,G_2) of correlated Erdos-Renyi graphs with correlation rho. Conjecture: The exact recovery threshold is rho_c = (log n)^{-1/2}(2^{1/2}+o(1)); the partial recovery threshold (linearly many correct matches) coincides with the exact threshold, so there is no intermediate phase; and the interpolation law in the number of seeds s (a priori correct matches) is rho_c(s) = rho_c(0)(1-s/n)^{1/2}, with endpoint behavior governed by the broadcast-tree reconstruction constant 1/pi. (correlated graph matching thresholds)

**中文。** 定义：随机图匹配（相关性为 ρ 的 Erdős–Rényi 对（G_1,G_2）的顶点对应恢复）的信息阈值 ρ_c(n)。猜想：精确恢复的阈值为 ρ_c = (log n)^{-1/2}·(2^{1/2}+o(1))；部分恢复（线性规模正确匹配）的阈值与精确阈值重合（无中间相）；种子数（先验正确匹配点数）s 的插值律为 ρ_c(s)=ρ_c(0)·(1-s/n)^{1/2}，端点行为由广播树重构常数 1/π 控制。
