**English.** Definition: random graph matching (correlated is threshold ρ_c(n) of vertex corresponds to) of Erdős–Rényi pair (G_1,G_2) of ρ. Conjecture: threshold of exact is threshold of ρ_c = (log n)^{-1/2} · (2^{1/2}+o(1)); part (linear module positive matching) and interpolation of exact threshold (without middle mutual); number (a priori positive matching point number) s is ρ_c(s)=ρ_c(0) · (1-s/n)^{1/2}, endpoint row is by tree constant 1/π controls.

**中文。** 定义：随机图匹配（相关性为 ρ 的 Erdős–Rényi 对（G_1,G_2）的顶点对应恢复）的信息阈值 ρ_c(n)。猜想：精确恢复的阈值为 ρ_c = (log n)^{-1/2}·(2^{1/2}+o(1))；部分恢复（线性规模正确匹配）的阈值与精确阈值重合（无中间相）；种子数（先验正确匹配点数）s 的插值律为 ρ_c(s)=ρ_c(0)·(1-s/n)^{1/2}，端点行为由广播树重构常数 1/π 控制。
