# 00000003934

**English.** Definition: The Legendrian satellite operation replaces the tubular neighborhood of a knot by a pattern knot. Conjecture: The DGA of the satellite is isomorphic to the derived tensor product of the original DGA and the pattern DGA, with an explicit twisting by the winding number as coefficient. (satellite DGA tensor formula)

**中文。** 定义：Legendrian 卫星操作指将结的管状邻域替换为模式结的操作。猜想：卫星的 DGA 同构于原 DGA 与模式 DGA 的导出张量积附加以缠绕数为系数的显式扭曲。（卫星 DGA 张量公式）
