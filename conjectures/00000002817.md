# 00000002817

**LD-17 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: Deviations of random recursions: heavy-tail LDP of autoregressive processes. Conjecture: The deviation exponent of heavy-tail autoregression: for processes with stable-law tail index α the large deviations are at scale n^{1/α}, with rate function a Legendre-type correction of the Lévy measure of the stable law. (heavy-tail AR deviation exponent)

**中文。** 定义：随机递归的偏差：自回归过程的重尾 LDP。猜想：重尾自回归的偏差指数：稳定律尾指数 α 的过程的大偏差为 n 的 1/α 尺度且速率函数为稳定律 Lévy 测度的 Legendre 型修正。（重尾 AR 偏差指数）
