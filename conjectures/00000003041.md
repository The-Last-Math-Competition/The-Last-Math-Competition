**English.** Definition: Integrability tests: the system of criteria deciding whether nonlinear PDEs are integrable. Conjecture: The trichotomy of integrability: the integrability spectrum of equations is the trichotomy of fully integrable, partially integrable, and chaotic, with the criterion for the trichotomy the joint closure of the Painlevé test and inverse scattering. (integrability trichotomy joint criterion)

**中文。** 定义：可积检验：判定非线性偏微分方程是否可积的判据体系。猜想：可积性的三岐：方程的可积性谱为完全可积、部分可积与混沌三岐且三岐的判据为 Painlevé 检验与逆散射的联合封闭。（可积三岐联合判据）
