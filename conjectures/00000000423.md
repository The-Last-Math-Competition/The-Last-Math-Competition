**English.** Definition: The promotion operator is cyclic sliding on standard tableaux. Conjecture: Promotion orbit lengths divide the tableau size; and the simultaneous equidistribution and orbit counting are given jointly by a bijection. (promotion orbit divisibility)

**中文。** 定义：promotion 算子为标准表的循环滑动。猜想：promotion 轨道长度整除表的尺寸；且同均值性与轨道计数的联合为双射给出。（promotion 轨道整除）
