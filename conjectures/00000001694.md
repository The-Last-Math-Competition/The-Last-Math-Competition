**EG-04 ◆** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: Sarnak's Möbius disjointness is the orthogonality of μ to zero-entropy systems. Conjecture: The Möbius function is orthogonal to the diagonalized Fourier coefficients of diagonal flows of rank ≥ 2; the proof uses horocycle dilation to reduce higher-order phases to Ratner joinings.

**中文。** 定义：Sarnak 的 Möbius 不相交性指 μ 与零熵系统正交。猜想：Möbius 与秩 ≥2 对角流的对角化 Fourier 系数正交;其证明经 horocycle 膨胀技术把高阶相位降秩到 Ratner 连接。
