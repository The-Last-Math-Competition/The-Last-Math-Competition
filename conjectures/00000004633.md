# 00000004633

**English.** Definition: A modular tensor category is a nondegenerate braided semisimple tensor category, and the total Gauss sum is the dimension sum with spin factors. Conjecture: The ratio of two Gauss sums is an explicit root of the discriminant of the category, and Galois-type new rows give a verifiable criterion for non-integer ratios. (Galois discrimination of Gauss sum ratios)

**中文。** 定义：模张量范畴指非退化辫半单张量范畴,总 Gauss 和指带旋量的维权和。猜想：两 Gauss 和之比为范畴判别式的显式根,Galois 型新行给出非整数比的判据且可验证。（Gauss 和比 Galois 判别）
