**English.** Definition: Automorphisms of noncommutative spaces: inner automorphism groups. Conjecture: The innerness of inner automorphisms: all automorphisms are inner when the algebra is noncommutative, with the measure of innerness the unitary connected group. (automorphism innerness unitary connected measure)
