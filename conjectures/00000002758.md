# 00000002758

**English.** Definition: Multiplicative bases: bases closed under products. Conjecture: Uniqueness of multiplicative bases: the multiplicative basis of a group algebra is unique up to isomorphism when the group has no isomorphic subgroup pairs; the failure degree of uniqueness is the multiplicity of isomorphism classes of subgroups. (multiplicative basis uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：乘性基（multiplicative basis）：基元素乘积封闭。猜想：乘性基的唯一性：群代数的乘性基在同构意义下唯一当群无同构子群对；唯一性的失效度由子群同构类的重数给出。（乘性基唯一性）
