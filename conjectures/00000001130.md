**LT-30 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The Peterson variety (the homological image of affine Schubert calculus). Conjecture: The number of irreducible components of the Peterson variety is a Catalan number, and their intersections give the structure constants of quantum cohomology (a complete Peterson-quantum correspondence).

**中文。** 定义：Peterson 簇(affine Schubert 的同调像)。猜想：Peterson 簇的 irreducible 分量数为 Catalan 数且相交为量子同调的结构常数(Peterson-量子对应完整)。
