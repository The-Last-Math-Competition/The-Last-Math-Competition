# 00000000344

**DA-44 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The even continued fraction is an expansion with all partial quotients even. Conjecture: The Hurwitz constant of even continued fractions is an explicit algebraic number related to the golden ratio; and optimality is attained only by golden-ratio tails. (even continued fraction Hurwitz constant)

**中文。** 定义：偶连分数为部分商全为偶数的展开。猜想：偶连分数的 Hurwitz 常数为与黄金比相关的显式代数数；且最优性仅由黄金比尾项达成。（偶连分数 Hurwitz 常数）
