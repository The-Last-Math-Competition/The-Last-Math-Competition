# 00000002907

**English.** Definition: Exponential sums over finite fields: absolute values of Gauss and Jacobi sums. Conjecture: The phase rigidity of exponential sums: the phase of a nondegenerate Gauss sum is an algebraic number whose norm splitting field is an explicit subfield of the cyclotomic field; the degree of the subfield is the modular inverse of the characteristic. (Gauss sum phase splitting field)

**中文。** 定义：有限域上的指数和：Gauss 和与 Jacobi 和的绝对值。猜想：指数和的相角刚性：非退化 Gauss 和的相角为代数数且相角的范数分裂域为分圆域的显式子域；子域的次数为特征的模逆。（Gauss 和相角分裂域）
