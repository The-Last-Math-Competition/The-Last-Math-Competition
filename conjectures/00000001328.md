# 00000001328

**English.** Definition: The Donaldson G-dimension of a Fano variety X is the normalization of the volume ratio inf_{D ∼ −K} vol(D). Conjecture: The infimum of the G-dimension over all n-dimensional Fano varieties is 2/(n+1), attained uniquely by the projective space CPⁿ; no Fano variety has G-dimension strictly below 2/(n+1).

**中文。** 定义：Fano 簇 X 的 Donaldson G-畴指 volume ratio inf_{D ∼ -K} vol(D) 的归一化。猜想：一切 n 维 Fano 簇的 G-畴下确界为 2/(n+1),且该下界由射影空间 ℂℙⁿ 唯一取得;不存在使 G-畴严格小于 2/(n+1) 的 Fano 簇。
