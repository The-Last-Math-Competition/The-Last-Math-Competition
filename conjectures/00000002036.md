# 00000002036

**English.** Definition: DHL[k, m+1] means infinitely many k-tuples containing m+1 primes. Conjecture: The minimal admissible-tuple size k(m) is asymptotically m·log m·exp(m) (the combinatorial limit of the supremum of prime gaps); the asymptotics come from the extremal covering of the Erdős–Rankin construction.

**中文。** 定义：DHL[k, m+1] 指 k 元组中存在 m+1 个素数的无穷次出现。猜想：可容许元组的最小尺寸 k(m) 渐近为 mlog m·\exp(m)(与素数间隙的上确界的组合极限);渐近由 Erdős–Rankin 构造的极值覆盖给出。
