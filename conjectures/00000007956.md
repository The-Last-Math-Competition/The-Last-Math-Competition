# 00000007956

**English.** Definition: The evaluation of vacua in the algebraic correlation functions (Korepin form) of quantum integrable models: the long-distance bound of the String conjecture (asymptotics of the correlation length). Conjecture: The exponential decay rate of the two-point function <sigma_0 sigma_x> as x grows (the maximal imaginary part mu_2 of the second-longest roots) satisfies mu_2 = 2 pi / xi (xi the correlation length); the prefactor is 1/x^theta (theta the anomalous dimension) with an explicit irrational formula in the elliptic modulus k and Gamma values; and the anomalous dimension is 1/2 at the free point k = 0 (Ising type) and diverges logarithmically at the critical point k = 1 (Kondo type). (String conjecture anomalous dimension law)

**中文。** 定义：量子可积模型的代数相关函数（Korepin 形式）的空态（vacua）的求值：若 String 猜想的远距界（asymptotic of correlation length）。猜想：双点相关函数 <σ_0 σ_x> 在 x→∞ 的指数衰减率（第二长的根的最大虚部 μ₂）满足 μ₂ = 2π·ξ^{-1}（ξ 为相关长度）；且前置因子为 1/x^{θ}（θ 为反常维数），θ 的显式式为无理数（由椭圆模数 k 与 Γ 值表出）；反常维数在自由点 k=0 为 1/2（Ising 型），在临界点 k=1 发散为对数（Kondo 型）。
