# 00000008380

**English.** Definition: The tropicalization depth: the equivalence depth of tropicalizations of a variety under different embeddings. Conjecture: The equivalence depth criterion is always isomorphism to a skeleton retraction; and the smallest inequivalent variety is the quadratic embedding of an elliptic curve. (tropicalization depth skeleton retraction criterion)

**中文。** 定义：热带化深度指簇的不同嵌入的热带化等价深度。猜想：等价深度判据恒为同构于骨架的收缩；不等价的最小簇为椭圆曲线的二次嵌入。（热带化深度骨架收缩判据）
