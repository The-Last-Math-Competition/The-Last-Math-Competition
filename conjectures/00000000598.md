# 00000000598

**English.** Definition: The algebraic de Rham cohomology of a branched cover carries the Varchenko bilinear form. Conjecture: The sum of squared volumes of the regions of a hyperplane arrangement is given by an explicit quadratic form in the coefficients of the characteristic polynomial; and positive definiteness of the form is equivalent to the maximality of the arrangement. (Varchenko quadratic form strengthened)

**中文。** 定义：分支覆盖的代数 de Rham 上同调携带 Varchenko 双线性型。猜想：超平面配置各区域的体积平方和由特征多项式系数的显式二次型给出；且二次型的正定性与配置的极大性等价。（Varchenko 二次型加强）
