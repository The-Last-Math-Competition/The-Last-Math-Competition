**English.** Definition: The curvature of the limit shape is the geometric curvature of the contour. Conjecture: The curvature of the classical limit is an explicit rational expression, has a square-root divergence at the frozen boundary, and the formula is globally closed. (global rational formula of limit curvature)

**中文。** 定义：极限形状的曲率指轮廓的几何曲率。猜想：经典极限的曲率为显式有理式,冻结边界处平方根发散且公式全场封闭。（极限曲率有理全场公式）
