# 00000008301

**FLO-11 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Specht modules of symplectic cell decompositions: the index specht_ind of the Specht decomposition of representations of triangulated Lagrangian skeletons. Conjecture: specht_ind (the Specht decomposition of the representation of the skeleton) is the count from the hook length formula for partitions (a hook length counting law); the dimension spectrum of the irreducibles of the decomposition equals the count of standard Young tableaux (a Young counting law); and the image of the highest weight map of the skeleton is a saturated subsemigroup of the weight lattice (a saturated semigroup law), whose Hilbert basis has at most n(n-1)/2 elements (a binomial basis bound). (Specht hook length saturated semigroup skeleton)

**中文。** 定义：辛胞腔剖分的 Specht 模块：三角剖分的 Lagrangian skeleton 的表示的 Specht 分解的指标 specht_ind。猜想：specht_ind（skeleton 的表示的 Specht 分解）= 分割的钩长公式的计数（钩长计数律）；且分解的不可约的维数谱 = 标准 Young 表的计数（Young 计数律）；skeleton 的最高权（权映射）的像为权格的饱和子半群（饱和半群律），半群的 Hilbert 基的元素数 ≤ n·(n-1)/2（二项式基界）。
