# 00000000674

**English.** Definition: The Kinoshita–Terasaka/Conway pair. Conjecture: There exist three or more pairs of mutually prime knots sharing all sl(n) homologies yet having non-homeomorphic complements (constructing families of n-homology-equivalent knots).

**中文。** 定义：Kinoshita–Terasaka/Conway 对。猜想：存在三对以上的互素纽结对,其所有 sl(n) 同调相同但补流形不同(n-同调全等族的构造)。
