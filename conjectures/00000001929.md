# 00000001929

**English.** Definition: The badly approximable set is the complement of the Diophantine-approximable set. Conjecture: The dimension formula for intersections of badly approximable sets under two distinct affine images always has a complete case table; and the table is stratified by the resonance relations of affine slopes. (badly-approximable intersection dimension resonance table)

**中文。** 定义：坏逼近集指 Diophantine 逼近的余集。猜想：两重不同仿射像的坏逼近交的维数公式恒有完全情形表；且表按仿射斜率的共振关系分层。（坏逼近交维数共振分层表）
