**English.** Definition: Stable conversion is the stable descent of sheaves compatible with group actions, that is, equivariant sheaves. Conjecture: The two-dimensional formula is an explicit linear expression of the degree of the characteristic polynomial, degenerating to the dimension power of the characteristic for the trivial sheaf; the slope of the linear expression is half the orbit count; and the obstruction to descent is nonabelian first cohomology, a finite obstruction group. (two-dimensional characteristic linear stable conversion)

**中文。** 定义：稳定换算：层与群作用的相容结构（等变层）的稳定下降。猜想：二维公式为特征多项式次数的显式线性式且平凡层时退化为特征的维数幂；线性式的斜率为轨道数之半；下降的障碍为非交换一阶上同调且障碍群有限。（二维特征线性稳定换算）
