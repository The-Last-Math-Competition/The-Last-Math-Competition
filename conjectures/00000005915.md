# 00000005915

**English.** Definition: The cover pullback construction combines covers with clustering. Conjecture: At the refinement limit the cover multiplicity is homotopy equivalent to the intersection balls, and the kernel of the equivalence is nerve stability. (nerve-stable refinement equivalence of cover pullbacks)

**中文。** 定义：覆盖拉回构造指覆盖与聚类的组合。猜想：增细极限时覆盖重数与交球同伦等价；且等价的核为神经稳定。（覆盖拉回神经稳定增细等价）
