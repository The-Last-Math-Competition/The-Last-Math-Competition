# 00000009821

**WEX-01 ★** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: Weighted norms of commutators [b,H] of Hilbert transforms with BMO functions: Chung-Pereyra gave the two-weight dyadic case. Conjecture: The exact constant of the weighted commutator norm is the square root of the product of the squared weight constant plus the squared BMO norm; the constant is exact in the dyadic model and transfers to the continuous case by averaging over parallel shifts without loss. (commutator weighted exact constant)

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert 变换与 BMO 函数的交换子 [b,H] 的加权范数：Chung–Pereyra 给出双权 dyadic 情形。猜想：交换子的加权范数精确常数为权常数的平方加 BMO 范数平方乘积的平方根；常数在 dyadic 模型精确且经平行移位平均传递到连续情形无损失。（交换子加权精确常数）
