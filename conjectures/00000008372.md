# 00000008372

**English.** Definition: The shape pad_four of the Fourier transform of p-adic distributions in the p to infinity tropical limit: the multiplicative valuation turns oscillations into piecewise-linear forms. Conjecture: The tropical limit of pad_four is always a piecewise-linear step function whose jump points lie exactly on the power lattice of the valuation lattice of p; the difference of adjacent jump amplitudes divides p - 1; the convergence rate is O(1/p), uniform outside a codimension-one neighborhood of the origin; and the origin neighborhood is the unique exception, its radius being the single scale p^{-1}, with no intermediate scale layers. (p-adic Fourier tropical lattice step limit)

**中文。** 定义：p 进分布的傅里叶变换在 p→∞ 热带极限下的形态 pad_four：以乘法赋值把振荡化为分段线性。猜想：pad_four 的热带极限恒为分段线性阶梯函数且跳跃点恰落在赋值格点 p 的幂格上；相邻跳幅之差整除 p-1；收敛速率在原点余维一邻域之外为 O(1/p) 且一致；原点邻域为唯一例外且其半径等于 p^{-1} 的单尺度，无中间尺度层。
