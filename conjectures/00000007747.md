# 00000007747

**English.** Definition: For Haar unitary powers, normalized traces f(U^n) = tr(U^n A U^{-n} B) have abelianized limits. Conjecture: For fixed A, B and n growing, the L^2 norm of tr(U^n A U^{-n} B) - tr(A)tr(B) is the absolute square sum of [a_k b_k] with relative density correction of (n), with decay rate O(n^{-1}); the leading coefficient is the product of the Fourier coefficients of the spectral densities of A and B at frequency n, exhibiting arithmetic-quantum-unique-ergodicity-type resonance. (resonant decay of unitary power traces)

**中文。** 定义：Haar 酉幂次的特征角：U^n 的特征值在单位圆上等分布；规范化迹 f(U^n)=tr(U^n A U^{-n} B) 的极限为交换化泛函。猜想：对固定的 A,B 与 n→∞，tr(U^n A U^{-n} B) - tr(A)tr(B) 的 L² 范数为 Σ_{k≥1} [a_k b_k] 的绝对平方和经 (n) 的相对密度修正，衰减率 O(n^{-1})；其首项系数由 A,B 各自谱密度的 Fourier 系数在频率 n 处的乘积给出，呈现算术量子唯一遍历性类型的共振。
