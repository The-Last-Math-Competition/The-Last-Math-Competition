# 00000000215

**English.** Definition: PL(n), the number of plane partitions, counts column-monotone plane arrays; MacMahon's product formula gives the generating function. Conjecture: The parity of PL(n) is decided by an explicit automaton reading the binary expansion of n: PL(n) is odd if and only if the 2-adic structure of n falls into the automaton's accepting set, and the density of odd values is 1/4 rather than 1/2, the deviation being given by a 2-adic valuation analysis of the numerator of the product formula. (plane partition parity automaton)

**中文。** 定义：平面分拆数 PL(n) 为行列单调平面阵列的计数，MacMahon 乘积公式给出其生成函数。猜想：PL(n) 的奇偶性由 n 的二进制展布的显式自动机判定：PL(n) 为奇当且仅当 n 的 2-adic 结构落入自动机接受集，且奇数密度为 1/4 而非 1/2，偏差由乘积公式分子的 2-adic 赋值分析给出。（平面分拆奇偶自动机）
