**MON-07 ★** — source: 280_单调算子与算子分裂.md

**English.** Definition: Almost sure convergence of stochastic splitting algorithms. Conjecture: The convergence rate of expected trajectories is always O(1/sqrt t); and almost sure convergence is guaranteed by square summability of the martingale-difference noise. (stochastic splitting root-rate convergence)

**中文。** 定义：随机分裂算法的几乎必然收敛。猜想：期望轨道的收敛率恒为 O(1/√t)；几乎必然收敛由鞅差噪声的平方可和保证。（随机分裂根率收敛）
