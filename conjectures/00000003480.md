# 00000003480

**GA3-20 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The fractional Hall condition connects the fractional chromatic number with the linear-programming dual of independence constraints. Conjecture: The difference between the fractional chromatic number and the fractional Hall bound takes every value from zero to one; the family realizing value t is of Mycielski interpolation type, with endpoint one attained only by finite approximations of infinite families. (fractional Hall continuous spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：分数 Hall 条件连接分数染色数与独立数约束的线性规划对偶。猜想：分数色数与分数 Hall 界之差取遍零到一的一切实数值；实现值 t 的图族为 Mycielski 型插值族且端点一仅由无限族的有限逼近达到。（分数 Hall 连续谱）
