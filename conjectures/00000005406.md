# 00000005406

**English.** Definition: A noninvertible map is a non-bijective dynamical system. Conjecture: The period counting of one-dimensional maps is always given by the branch degree of the preimage tree, corrected by the squarefree part of the discriminant. (branch counting formula of noninvertible preimage trees)

**中文。** 定义：不可逆映射指非双射的动力系统。猜想：一维的周期计数恒由前像树分支度给出；且经判别式无平方部分修正。（不可逆前像树分支计数式）
