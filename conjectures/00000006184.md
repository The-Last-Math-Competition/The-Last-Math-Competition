# 00000006184

**English.** Definition: Quasirandomness is the equivalent characterization by deviation parameters. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit window of deviation parameters, the quadratic-deviation window equivalent to the subgraph deviation; the window-matching exponent is 3/2 minus epsilon; and the stratification of refined quasirandom spectra is a strict inclusion chain with chain length a power of 2. (window-matching conversion of quadratic deviations)

**中文。** 定义：拟随机性：偏差参数的等价刻画。猜想：换算常数为偏差参数的显式窗且二次偏差窗与子图偏差等价；窗宽匹配的指数为 3/2 减 ε；拟随机谱的加细窗分层为严格包含链且链长为 2 的幂。（二次偏差窗等价匹配换算）
