# 00000007888

**English.** Definition: The Lurie-Pridham extension of derived deformation theory to the (∞,2)-sheaf setting: the tangent 2-space of the formal moduli problem M_D of a deformation problem D (a functor from Artin rings to spaces). Conjecture: M_D is completely controlled by a differential graded Lie 2-algebra g_D; and the classification obstruction in the 2-cohomology of g_D vanishes in the semisimple case, while in general the dimension of the obstruction group H^3(g_D) equals the number of second-order ramification points of the moduli space, the splitting of the ramification being uniquely given by the third layer of the Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff bracket. (Lie 2-algebra deformation control)

**中文。** 定义：导出形变理论的 Lurie–Pridham 推广到 (∞,2)-层的情形：形变问题 D（函子 Artin 环到空间）的形式模空间 M_D 的切 2-空间。猜想：M_D 由微分分次 Lie 2-代数 g_D 完全控制；且 g_D 的 2-上同调的分类 obstruction 在半单情形消没，一般的 obstruction 群 H³(g_D) 的维数等于模空间的二阶歧点数，歧点的分裂由 Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff 的第三层括号唯一给出。
