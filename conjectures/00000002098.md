# 00000002098

**English.** Definition: Isotopy classification of Lagrangians in CP². Conjecture: The one-point Lagrangian tori are exactly of Clifford type; and the kernel outside this type is the Chekanov classification. (CP2 Clifford-Chekanov torus classification)

**中文。** 定义：CP² 中拉格朗日的 isotopy 分类。猜想：单点拉格朗日环面恰为 Clifford 型；且型外的核为 Chekanov 分类。（CP2-Clifford-Chekanov 环面分类）
