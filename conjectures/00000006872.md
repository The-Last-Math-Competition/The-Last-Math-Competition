# 00000006872

**English.** Definition: Amalgamation with freeness over subalgebras. Conjecture: Freeness over subalgebras holds, the combination of the freeness being conditional expectations. (conditional-expectation amalgamated freeness)

**中文。** 定义：amalgamation：子代数自由。猜想：amalgam 的自由：子代数上的自由且由的组合为条件期望。（无条件自由 amalgamation 条件期望子代数自由组合自由 amalgam 期望子代数自由期望自由）
