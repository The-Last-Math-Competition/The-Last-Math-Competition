# 00000004960

**English.** Definition: Homology groups, homotopy groups, and homotopy types are two layers of topological data. Conjecture: There exist two spaces whose homology groups and homotopy groups are respectively isomorphic but whose homotopy types differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of towers with non-commuting actions. (tower separation with all groups equal but different homotopy types)

**中文。** 定义：同调群同伦群与同伦型指两层拓扑数据。猜想：存在两空间的一切同调群与同伦群分别同构而同伦型不同,分离由作用不交换的显式塔对实现。（群全同-同伦型塔分离）
