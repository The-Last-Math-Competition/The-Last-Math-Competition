# 00000008385

**English.** Definition: The Fubini theorem for motivic measures: the exchange of iterated integrals over fibers of arc space fibrations with the integral over the total space. Conjecture: Motivic Fubini holds without residual on quasi-smooth morphisms; residuals occur if and only if the fiber dimension jumps on special arcs, and the residual is an explicit integer combination of motivic measures of the jumping strata arc sets. (motivic Fubini residual characterization)

**中文。** 定义：母题测度的 Fubini 定理指弧空间纤维化的测度的迭代积分与全空间积分的交换。猜想：母题 Fubini 在拟平滑态射上成立且无残差；残差的出现当且仅当纤维维数在特殊弧上跳跃，残差为跳跃层弧集的母题测度的显式整系数组合。（母题 Fubini 残差刻画）
