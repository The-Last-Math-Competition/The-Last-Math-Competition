**English.** Definition: A polynomial activation network is a network with power functions as activations. Conjecture: Its depth separation is given by an explicit compression construction of trinomial recursions, the lower bound of the width ratio is of exponential type, and the construction attains it exactly. (trinomial compression depth separation of polynomial networks)

**中文。** 定义：多项式激活网络指以幂函数为激活的网络。猜想：其深度分离由三项式递推的显式压缩构造给出,宽度比的下界为指数型且构造精确达到。（多项式网络三项式压缩深度分离）
