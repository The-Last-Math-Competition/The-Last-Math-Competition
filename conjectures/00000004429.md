# 00000004429

**English.** Definition: The Airy process is the Airy-type space-time limit process. Conjecture: The kernel of its cross-scale joint distribution is an explicit composition of the Airy kernel with flat translations, and as the gap tends to zero the composition degenerates to the one-point Airy kernel. (Airy cross-scale translation-composed kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Airy 过程指 Airy 型时空极限过程。猜想：其跨尺度联合的核为 Airy 核的跖平移显式复合,空隙趋于零时复合退化为单点 Airy 核。（Airy 跨尺度平移复合核）
