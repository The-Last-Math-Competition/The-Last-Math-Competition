# 00000006590

**English.** Definition: The stability space and the category are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two categories with isomorphic stability spaces but globally non-isomorphic categories, and the separation is realized by an explicit direct-sum complement with the same stability but different cohomology. (same stability-different category direct-sum separation)

**中文。** 定义：稳定空间与范畴指两层。猜想：存在两范畴的稳定空间同构而整体范畴不同构,分离由同稳定异上同调的显式直和补实现。（稳定同-范畴异直和补分离）
