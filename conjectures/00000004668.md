# 00000004668

**English.** Definition: Equivariant deformation is a deformation preserving symmetry under a group action. Conjecture: The upper bound of the order of a singularity under equivariant deformation is an explicit function of the least common multiple of the weights, and the bound is optimal, attained by flag-type sequences. (least-common-multiple upper bound of equivariant orders)
