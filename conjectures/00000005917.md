**English.** Definition: Persistence landscapes are the functional representation of barcodes. Conjecture: The layerwise norm equivalence is the exact isomorphism of persistence modules, the combination of the isomorphism being the layer-function basis; the conversion of landscape norms with the bottleneck distance is a linear inequality with optimal constant 1; and the orthogonal basis of layer functions is piecewise linear, with completeness of the basis preserved in L^p spaces. (layer-function basis norm isomorphism of landscapes)

**中文。** 定义：持久景观：条形码的函数化表示。猜想：逐层范数等价为持久模的精确同构且同构的组合为层函数基；景观范数与瓶颈距离的换算为线性不等式且常数 1 最优；层函数的正交基为分段线性型且基的完备性在 L^p 空间保持。（景观层函数基范数同构）
