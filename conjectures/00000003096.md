**English.** Definition: Automorphisms of ∞-groupoids: higher symmetry. Conjecture: The spectrum of automorphisms: the homotopy groups of the automorphism spectrum of an ∞-groupoid are stable on the sphere spectrum, with the genus of stabilization the Whitehead tower of the combinatorial group. (automorphism sphere spectrum stabilization genus)
