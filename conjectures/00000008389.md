**MIB-09 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: Motivic invariants: the motivic lifts of birational invariants, such as generalizations of the motivic Euler characteristic and the motivic Hodge-Deligne polynomial. Conjecture: The motivic lift of birational invariants satisfies the blow-up identity [Bl_X E] = [X] + L [E] (L the Lefschetz motive); this identity uniquely determines all motivic lifts under weak factorization; and the canonicity of the lift is equivalent to the existence of the box-tensor structure on the category of motives. (birational motivic lift)

**中文。** 定义：母题不变量指双有理不变量的母题提升，如母题 Euler 特征与母题 Hodge–Deligne 多项式的推广。猜想：双有理不变量的母题化在爆破下满足等式 [Bl_X E] = [X] + L·[E]（L 为 Lefschetz 母题）；该等式唯一确定一切弱因子化下的母题提升；提升的典范性等价于母题范畴的⊠结构的存在。（双有理母题提升）
