# 00000008377

**English.** Definition: The spectral curve of tropical Toda: the genus spec_toda of the spectral curve of the tropical limit of the integrable system (Toda lattice). Conjecture: spec_toda (the spectral curve of tropical Toda) is a claw-free tree (a claw-free tree law); the maximal vertex degree (degree spectrum) is at most 3 (a three-degree upper bound law); the invariant of the tropicalization of the commutation of the Lax pair (max-plus commutation) is the waist constant (a waist constant law); and the spectrum of waists (over all Lax pairs) is the image of Hurwitz numbers (a Hurwitz image law). (tropical Toda claw-free waist Hurwitz image)

**中文。** 定义：热带Toda 的谱曲线：可积系统（Toda 格）的热带极限的谱曲线的亏格 spec_toda。猜想：spec_toda（热带 Toda 的谱曲线）为无爪树（无爪树律）；且树的度（顶点度谱）的极大 ≤ 3（三度上界律）；谱的交换（Lax 对的交换）的热带化（Max-Plus 代数的交换）的不变量为腰常数（腰常数律）；腰的谱（一切 Lax 对的腰）为 Hurwitz 数的像（Hurwitz 像律）。
