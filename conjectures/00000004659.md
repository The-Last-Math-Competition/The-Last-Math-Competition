**English.** Definition: Zagier duality is the pairing of mock shadows with quantum modular forms. Conjecture: The dual conversion is an explicit involutive permutation of coefficient reflections, realized in the simultaneous diagonalization of the coefficient matrix product of the dual pair. (involution conversion of Zagier duality)

**中文。** 定义：Zagier 对偶指 mock 阴影与量子模形式的配对。猜想：对偶换算为系数反射的显式对合置换,置换在对偶对的系数矩阵乘积对角化中实现。（Zagier 对偶对合换算）
