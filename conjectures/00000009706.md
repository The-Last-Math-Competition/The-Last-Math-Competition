# 00000009706

**English.** Definition: The concentration-compactness principle: deficits of profile decompositions of critical equations are controlled by the escape of symmetry-group orbits. Conjecture: The compactness deficit of profile decompositions of energy-critical equations has explicit gradient control: profiles of deficit energy epsilon separate with scale ratio at least exp(c/epsilon); the separation constant is an explicit exponential function of the symmetry group dimension. (concentration-compactness deficit control)

**中文。** 定义：集中紧性原理：临界方程的轮廓分解的亏损由对称群轨道的散逸控制。猜想：能量临界方程的轮廓分解的紧性亏损有显式梯度控制：亏损能量 ε 的轮廓分离尺度满足尺度比 ≥ exp(c/ε)；分离常数为对称群维数的显式指数函数。（集中紧亏损控制）
