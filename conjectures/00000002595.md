# 00000002595

**English.** Definition: Lattice congruences and quotient lattices: the congruence lattice Con(L) of a finite lattice. Conjecture: Con(L) of a finite lattice is distributive; quantitatively conversely: the minimal size of a lattice L realizing a distributive lattice D as Con(L) is a linear function of the prime-ideal lattice of D, and the realizing lattice is the minimal cut lattice. (congruence lattice realization minimal size)

**中文。** 定义：格同余与商格：有限格的同余格 Con(L)。猜想：有限格的同余格为分配格；其逆的量化：分配格 D 可实现为 Con(L) 的最小 L 的尺寸为 D 的素理想格的线性函数且实现格为极小切割格。（同余格实现最小尺寸）
