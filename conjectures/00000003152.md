# 00000003152

**English.** Definition: Subset product measures on Lie groups: the Brunn–Minkowski type. Conjecture: The BM inequality for groups: the lower bound of the Haar measure of product sets is exponential in the sum of measures, with equality exactly on cosets of subgroups. (Haar BM coset equality case)

**中文。** 定义：李群的子集乘积测度：Brunn–Minkowski 型。猜想：群的 BM 不等式：Haar 测度的乘积集下界为测度和的指数型且等号为子群的陪集。（Haar BM 陪集等号情形）
