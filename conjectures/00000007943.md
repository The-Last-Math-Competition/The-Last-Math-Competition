# 00000007943

**English.** Definition: The algebraic geometry of correlation functions at the free-fermion point (critical Delta = 0): the correlation functions of the XX chain as correlations of Schur processes. Conjecture: Every space-time correlation function is a ratio of Schur functions of dimer model (Aztec diamond) correlations; and the discrete measure of the Lawler-Schramm type boundary behavior of level lines converges to SLE_2; the hole generating function of correlations has a large-time limit given by the explicit kernel of a Fredholm determinant of the Airy process. (XX Schur dimer SLE limit)

**中文。** 定义：free-fermion 点（临界 Δ=0）的相关函数的代数几何：XX 链的关联函数为 Schur 过程的相关函数。猜想：任意空间-时间相关函数为 dimer 模型（Aztec 金刚）的相关函数的 Schur 函数比；且平面变差（level lines）的 Lawler–Schramm 型边界行为的离散化测度收敛到 SLE₂；关联的空穴生成函数在时间 →∞ 的极限为 Airy 过程的 Fredholm 行列式的显式核。
