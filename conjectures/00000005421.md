# 00000005421

**English.** Definition: The extremal length is the conformal invariant of curve families. Conjecture: The conversion of annular families is always explicit in the logarithm of the modulus; it degenerates at separated slit domains, and the conversion preserves monotonicity. (logarithmic conversion of annular moduli)

**中文。** 定义：极值长度指曲线族的共形不变量。猜想：环形族的换算恒由模的对数显式；分离型裂域处退化且换算保持单调。（环族模对数换算）
