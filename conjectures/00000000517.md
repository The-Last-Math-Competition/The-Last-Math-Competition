# 00000000517

**CA-17 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The Hilbert basis of the convex cone of Betti tables is its minimal integral generating set. Conjecture: For cones with degree gaps at most four, the Hilbert basis is finite and explicitly listable; and the length of the list is exponential in the degree gap. (Betti cone Hilbert basis finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：Betti 表的凸锥的 Hilbert 基为锥的极小整生成元。猜想：固定度差不超过四的锥的 Hilbert 基为有限且显式列表；且列表的长度为度差的指数。（Betti 锥 Hilbert 基有限）
