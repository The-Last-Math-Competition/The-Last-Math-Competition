# 00000008084

**FUS-04 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The balance of signalizer functors of fusion systems: the balance constant bal(F) between F-closedness and triviality of an F-signalizer functor theta. Conjecture: The fusion analogue of the signalizer theorem for finite groups (Gorenstein-Goldschmidt) holds: every balanced and closed signalizer functor is trivial (signalizer vanishing); the local criterion for vanishing is the rank condition at p-rank at least 3 (rank-3 rigidity); and the minimal exotic system where the fusion signalizer fails is a rank-2 Levi type (the complete list of Levi exceptions being the 2-local duals of Lie type groups). (fusion signalizer rank-three vanishing)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 signalizer 函子的平衡性：F-signalizer 函子 θ 的 F-闭合性与平凡性的平衡常 bal(F)。猜想：有限群的 signalizer 定理（Gorenstein–Goldschmidt）的融合模拟成立：平衡且闭的 signalizer 函子恒平凡（signalizer 消没）；且消没的局部判据为 p-秩 ≥3 的秩条件（秩 3 刚性）；融合的 signalizer 失效的最小 exotic 系为秩 2 的 Levi 型（Levi 例外的完全列表为 Lie 型群的 2-局部对偶）。
