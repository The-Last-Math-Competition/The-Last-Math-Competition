# 00000004566

**English.** Definition: Nori motives are the motive category constructed from diagrams. Conjecture: Its equivalence conversion with the graded motive category is an explicit inclusion chain of Tannakian subgroups, the chain stabilizes at the Tate truncation, and the conversion preserves dimension. (Nori-Tate truncated stable chain)

**中文。** 定义：Nori 母题指由图表构造的母题范畴。猜想：其与分次母题范畴的等价换算为 Tannakian 子群的显式包含链,链在 Tate 截断处稳定且换算保持维数。（Nori-Tate 截断稳定链）
