# 00000008771

**NLA-01 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Randomized numerical linear algebra (RandNLA): a framework of matrix algorithms centered on random sampling and projection. Conjecture: The optimal constant of the approximation error of the random SVD is the explicit 2 (a two-constant law); optimality is attained by the minimax of Gaussian projections (a Gaussian attainment law); the correction for sparse projections is an O(log d) factor (a logarithmic correction law); and the correction saturates as sparsity vanishes (a saturation law). (random SVD Gaussian logarithmic saturation)

**中文。** 定义：随机化数值线性代数（RandNLA）：以随机抽样与投影为核心的矩阵算法框架。猜想：随机 SVD 的逼近误差的最优常数为 2 的显式（二常数律）；且最优性由 Gaussian 投影的极小极大达到（Gaussian 达到律）；稀疏投影的修正为 O(log d) 因子（对数修正律）；修正的饱和在大稀疏度消失（饱和律）。
