**English.** Definition: randomization numerical linear algebra (RandNLA): random and projection is matrix algorithm frame of core. Conjecture: optimal constant of approximation error of random SVD is explicit (two constant); of 2 and optimal by minimax of Gaussian projection attains (Gaussian attains positive of); sparse projection is of O(log d) factor (logarithm positive); positive and in large sparse vanishes (and).

**中文。** 定义：随机化数值线性代数（RandNLA）：以随机抽样与投影为核心的矩阵算法框架。猜想：随机 SVD 的逼近误差的最优常数为 2 的显式（二常数律）；且最优性由 Gaussian 投影的极小极大达到（Gaussian 达到律）；稀疏投影的修正为 O(log d) 因子（对数修正律）；修正的饱和在大稀疏度消失（饱和律）。
