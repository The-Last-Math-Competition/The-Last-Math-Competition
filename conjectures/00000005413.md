**HM2-03 ★** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: A regular boundary is the doubling-type regular structure of the measure. Conjecture: In the regular case the dimension conversion constant is one after normalization, the difference of the boundary dimension and the measure dimension is linearly controlled by the regularity constant, and the bound is tight. (linear control of regular dimension conversion)

**中文。** 定义：正则边界指测度的双倍型正则结构。猜想：正则情形的维数换算常数为一旦归一,边界维数与测度维数之差被正则常数线性控制且界紧。（正则维数换算线性控制）
