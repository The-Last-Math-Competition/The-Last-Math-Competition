# 00000006523

**English.** Definition: The dimension law of heat-kernel coefficients is the weight structure. Conjecture: The coefficients satisfy an integral dimension-type law and close; the verification of the law is gauge fixing; and the self-consistency check of the law is completed on low-dimensional projective spaces. (closed dimension law of heat kernels)

**中文。** 定义：热核系数的维度律为权结构。猜想：系数满足积分维数型律且系数封闭；且律的验证为规范固定；且律的自洽检验在低维射影空间完成。（热核维数律封闭）
