# 00000005076

**English.** Definition: Griffiths subdiffusion is the slow transport caused by rare regions. Conjecture: In dimension one the attainable interval of the exponent is an explicit open interval with endpoints scaled by the chemical potentials of rare regions, monotone and continuous within the interval. (scaling of the Griffiths exponent interval)

**中文。** 定义：Griffiths 亚扩散指稀有区域导致的慢输运。猜想：一维的指数可达域为显式开区间,端点由稀有区域化学势定标且区间内单调连续。（Griffiths 指数可达区间定标）
