**English.** Definition: Waldhausen's A(X) is the stable A-functor of a space. Conjecture: The rational homotopy of A(manifold) always splits as an explicit direct sum of Wh(π) and K(Z); and the splitting is unique up to rational equivalence. (A-functor rational splitting unique)
