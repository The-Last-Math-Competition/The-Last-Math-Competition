# 00000004350

**English.** Definition: The entropy production is the entropy rate along an interpolation path, and the covariance is its fluctuation. Conjecture: There exists a sequence of measures for which the entropy production and the covariance of every interpolation path diverge simultaneously, and the minimal dimension of realization is 2. (simultaneous divergence of entropy production and covariance)

**中文。** 定义：熵产量指沿插值路径的熵变率,协方差指其涨落。猜想：存在测度序列使一切插值路径的熵产量与协方差同时发散,实现的最小维数为 2。（熵产量-协方差同发散）
