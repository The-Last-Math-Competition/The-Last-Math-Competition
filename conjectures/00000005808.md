# 00000005808

**English.** Definition: A self-similar shrinking surface is a shrinking self-similar solution. Conjecture: The two-dimensional classification consists of a finite number of new families beyond the classical three, the new families are finite, and the classification is closed without gaps. (finite closed classification of two-dimensional shrinkers)

**中文。** 定义：自相似收缩曲面指收缩的自相似解。猜想：二维的分类为经典三族外的有限新族,新族数有限且分类封闭无缺。（二维收缩子有限封闭分类）
