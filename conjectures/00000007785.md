# 00000007785

**ACG-05 ◆** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Mahler product M(K) = vol(K) vol(K^circ) with polar K^circ; the Mahler conjecture asserts M(K) >= 4^n in the symmetric case. Conjecture: The symmetric n-dimensional extremal bodies are the Hanner polytopes (equality cases), with strict inequality outside finite products of the Hanner family and finitely many low-dimensional exceptions (n<=3); the nonsymmetric case M(K) >= (n+1)^{n+1}/(n!)^2 is attained uniquely by the simplex; and both statements have stability versions: M(K) <= 4^n(1+epsilon) implies K is O(epsilon^{1/2}) close to a Hanner body. (Mahler extremal classification and stability)

**中文。** 定义：Mahler 乘积 M(K)=vol(K)vol(K°)（K° 为极体）。Mahler 猜想断言对称情形 M(K)≥4^n。猜想：对称 n 维极值体为 Hanner 多面体（等号情形）且除 Hanner 族的有限次乘积与有限个低维例外（n≤3）外严格不等；非对称情形 M(K)≥(n+1)^{n+1}/(n!)^2 由单纯形取得且唯一；两个断言的稳定性版本成立：M(K)≤4^n(1+ε) 蕴含 K 与某 Hanner 体距 O(ε^{1/2})。
