# 00000003975

**English.** Definition: The substitution Frege system sF is a Frege system augmented with the substitution rule. Conjecture: Every sF proof admits an equal-length simulation of depth at most 5, and the depth constant 5 is optimal, in that there exist formulas whose depth-4 simulations suffer superpolynomial length blowup. (sF depth compression constant)

**中文。** 定义：替换 Frege 系统 sF 指附替换规则的 Frege 系统。猜想：sF 的任意证明存在深度至多 5 的等长模拟,深度常数 5 最优,即存在公式其深度 4 模拟长度超多项式膨胀。（sF 深度压缩常数）
