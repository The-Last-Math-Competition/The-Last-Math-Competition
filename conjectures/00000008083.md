# 00000008083

**FUS-03 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The fusion-system version of the Alperin weight conjecture (AWC): the count W(F) of weights of a fusion system (zero-inertia labelings of irreducible characters of automorphism groups). Conjecture: W(F) equals the character count of the characteristic-zero blocks of F (the fusion version of the block-weight duality); the verification complexity of the chain condition on defect groups of blocks of the fusion system (Knorr-Robinson type) is polynomial in the defect group chain length; and the error term of the local-global comparison of weight counts vanishes (zero-error rigidity), the vanishing being guaranteed by the connectivity of the transitive system of the linking system (a connectivity-vanishing correspondence). (fusion Alperin weight zero-error law)

**中文。** 定义：Alperin 权重猜想（AWC）的融合系统版本：融合系统的权重（自同构群的不可约特征的零惯性标记）的计数 W(F)。猜想：W(F) = F 的特征零的块的特征计数（块-权重对偶的融合版）；且融合系统的块的亏群的链条件（Knörr–Robinson 型）的验证的复杂度为亏群链长度的多项式；权重计数的局部-全局比对的误差项消失（零误差刚性），误差的消没由链接系统的传递系的连通性保证（连通-消没对应）。
