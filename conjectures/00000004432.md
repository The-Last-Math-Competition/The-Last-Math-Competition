# 00000004432

**English.** Definition: The Schur measure is the partition measure weighted by Schur functions. Conjecture: The leading Fredholm asymptotic constant of the distribution of the maximal first row is an explicit functional of Gamma(2/3), and the coefficient in the exponential part is universal. (Schur first-row Fredholm leading constant)

**中文。** 定义：Schur 测度指以 Schur 函数为权的分拆测度。猜想：最大首行分布的 Fredholm 渐近首常数为 Γ(2/3) 的显式泛函,指数部分的系数普适。（Schur 首行 Fredholm 首常数）
