**English.** Definition: Wasserstein space P_p(R^d) denotes complete space of finite p order moment measure W_p distance. Conjecture: geodesic of absolutely continuous initial data always bounded concentration atom number of preserves absolutely continuous; exception initial data is explicit linear function of geodesic. (AC geodesic preserves atom linear bound)

**中文。** 定义：Wasserstein 空间 P_p(R^d) 指有限 p 阶矩测度配 W_p 距离的完备空间。猜想：绝对连续初值的测地恒保持绝对连续；例外初值的有界集中原子数为测地弧长的显式线性函数。（AC 测地保持原子线性界）
