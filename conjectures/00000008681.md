# 00000008681

**English.** Definition: The Wasserstein space P_p(R^d) is the complete space of measures with finite p-th moment under the W_p distance. Conjecture: Geodesics from absolutely continuous initial data remain absolutely continuous; and the bounded number of concentrating atoms of exceptional initial data is an explicit linear function of the geodesic arc length. (AC geodesic preservation linear atom bound)

**中文。** 定义：Wasserstein 空间 P_p(R^d) 指有限 p 阶矩测度配 W_p 距离的完备空间。猜想：绝对连续初值的测地恒保持绝对连续；例外初值的有界集中原子数为测地弧长的显式线性函数。（AC 测地保持原子线性界）
