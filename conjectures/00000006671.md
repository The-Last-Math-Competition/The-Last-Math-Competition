**English.** Definition: Linear duality is the dual program of the transport problem. Conjecture: The vanishing of the duality gap always locates the support by the complementary slackness conditions; the table of the location is complete; and the located slack support is given by the complementary difference of the marginal constraints. (complementary-slackness support of the duality gap)

**中文。** 定义：线性对偶为传输问题的对偶规划。猜想：对偶隙的消没恒由互补松弛条件定位支撑；且定位的表完全；且松弛支撑的定位由边际约束的互补差给出。（对偶隙互补松弛支撑）
