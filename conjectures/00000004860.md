# 00000004860

**English.** Definition: The stable commutator length spectrum and the homology spectrum are two group invariants. Conjecture: There exist two hyperbolic groups with the same length spectrum but different homology spectra, and the separation is realized explicitly by homology-identified twisted products. (same length spectrum-homology spectrum separation)

**中文。** 定义：稳定交换子长度谱与同调谱指两个群不变量。猜想：存在两双曲群的该长度谱相同而同调谱不同,分离由同调等同的扭曲积显式实现。（长度谱同-同调谱分离）
