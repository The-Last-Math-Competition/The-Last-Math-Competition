**English.** Definition: The transition matrix is the adjacency matrix of the partition. Conjecture: The spectral radius at genus two is the explicit value of the topological entropy, given by the crossing product of invariants, and identically equal to the volume entropy. (crossing-product identity of transition spectral radii)
