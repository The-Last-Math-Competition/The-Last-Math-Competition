**English.** Definition: The transfer of A-infinity structures with Fukaya multiplications. Conjecture: The counting of the transfer of Fukaya multiplications is the holomorphic counting of homotopy cylinders, and the symplectic side of the counting is tree summation. (homotopy-cylinder tree-summed multiplication transfer)

**中文。** 定义：A∞-结构的转移：Fukaya 的乘法。猜想：转移的闭式：Fukaya 乘法的转移的计数为同伦柱的全纯计数且计数的辛侧为树形求和。（Fukaya 同伦柱树形乘法计数转移）
