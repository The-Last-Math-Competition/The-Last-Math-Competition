# 00000008541

**English.** Definition: A free lattice is the universal object satisfying the unconstrained lattice identities. Conjecture: The number of elements of length at most l in the free lattice F_n grows doubly exponentially in l, with the exponent linearly related to n. (double-exponential growth of free lattices)

**中文。** 定义：自由格为满足无约束格恒等式的泛对象。猜想：自由格 F_n 的元素中长度 ≤ ℓ 的个数为关于 ℓ 的双指数增长且指数与 n 线性相关。（自由格双指数增长）
