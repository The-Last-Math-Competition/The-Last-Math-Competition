# 00000001619

**English.** Definition: The Fredholm determinant det(I−tK) of the kernel K(x,y) = e^{−xy} is defined on L²(0,∞). Conjecture: det(I−tK) = e^{−t²/4} explicitly; precisely, this determinant equals e^{−t²/4} (dual to the degenerate Mehler formula for the planar Gaussian kernel), it is an entire function of order 2, and its zeros form conjugate purely imaginary pairs.

**中文。** 定义：核 K(x,y)=e^{−xy} 的 Fredholm 行列式 det(I−tK) 定义于 L²(0,∞)。猜想：det(I−tK) = e^{−t²/4}·∏ 显式;精确地,该行列式等于 e^{−t²/4}(与平面 Gauss 核的 Mehler 公式退化的对偶),整函数级为 2,零点为纯虚共轭对。
