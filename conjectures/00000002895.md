# 00000002895

**English.** Definition: Sziklai's theorem: lower bounds on point counts of curves meeting every line. Conjecture: The tightening of the Sziklai bound: curves attaining the lower bound are completely reducible unions, classified by a complete list of Fermat-type curves; the closure of the list follows from inflection-count conservation. (Sziklai bound tightened classification)

**中文。** 定义：Sziklai 定理：与每条线相遇的曲线的点数下界。猜想：Sziklai 界的紧化：达到下界的曲线为完全可约的并集且并集的分类为 Fermat 型曲线的完全列表；列表的封闭性由拐点计数守恒。（Sziklai 界紧化分类）
