# 00000008252

**EGT-22 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The short-side advantage in matching: the dictatorship advantage short_adv of the short side of hospital-resident matchings. Conjecture: short_adv (the expected gain of the short side) is 1 - Theta(1/n) (a near-total gain law); the constant of the expected total rank of stable matchings under random preferences (the Knuth-de Bruijn integral constant) is pi^2/6 (an Apery constant law); the tail of the distribution of stable matchings (rank distribution of the proposer-optimal agents) is exponential (an exponential tail law); and the explicit decay rate of the tail is log(3/2) (a 3/2 logarithmic rate). (short-side advantage Apery constant tail rate)

**中文。** 定义：匹配的射线延迟：医院-居民匹配的短线（short side）的独裁优势 short_adv。猜想：short_adv（短线侧的期望收益）= 1-Θ(1/n)（近全收益律）；且随机偏好下的稳定匹配的期望总秩的常数（Knuth-de Bruijn 积分常数）= π²/6（Apéry 常数律）；稳定匹配的分布（proposer-optimal 的代理的秩分布）的尾部为指数（指数尾部律）；尾部的衰减率的显式为 log(3/2)（3/2 对数率）。
