# 00000008582

**NSP-22 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: Keldysh theorem: spectral projections and root subspace systems of resolvents of compact nonselfadjoint operators. Conjecture: Uniform boundedness of spectral projections implies completeness of root subspaces (a completeness law); simple eigenvalues are the generic situation (a generic simplicity law); the exceptions with multiple eigenvalues are finite (a finite exception law); and the algebraic multiplicity of exceptions is bounded by the resolvent rank (a resolvent rank upper bound law). (Keldysh completeness generic simplicity exceptions)

**中文。** 定义：Keldysh 定理：紧非自伴算子豫解式的谱投影与根子空间系。猜想：谱投影的一致有界蕴含根子空间的完备性（完备律）；且本征值为单重是普遍情形（普遍单重律）；多重本征值的例外有限（有限例外律）；例外的代数重数上界为豫限秩（豫限秩上界律）。
