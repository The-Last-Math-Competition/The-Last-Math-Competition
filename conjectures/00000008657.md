# 00000008657

**CGT-07 ★** — source: 274_计算群论与字的几何.md

**English.** Definition: Burnside groups B(m,n): the free group on m generators modulo n-th power relations. Conjecture: Burnside groups of large odd exponent are hyperbolic (a hyperbolicity law); B(2,n) has intermediate growth (an intermediate growth law); the complete list of (m,n) with B(m,n) finite is finite (a finite list law); and solvability of the conjugacy problem for infinite Burnside groups is proven via small cancellation (a small cancellation path law). (Burnside odd hyperbolicity intermediate finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：Burnside 群 B(m,n)：m 生成自由群模 n 次幂关系。猜想：奇大指数 Burnside 群的双曲性（双曲律）；且 B(2,n) 的生长的中间性（中间生长律）；有限 Burnside 群（B(m,n) 有限）的 (m,n) 的完全列表的有限（有限列表律）；无限 Burnside 的共轭问题的可解性（可解律）的证明路径为小消去（小消去路径律）。
