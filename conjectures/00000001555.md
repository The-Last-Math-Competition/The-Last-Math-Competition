**GM-15 ★** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: The ratio of chromatic number to clique number of the random unit-disk graph. Conjecture: The ratio converges to two as n → ∞; and the convergence rate is controlled by the Poissonization of the local condensation window. (random geometric chromatic-clique ratio tends to two)

**中文。** 定义：随机单位圆盘图的色数与团数之比。猜想：比值在 n 趋于无穷时收敛到二；且收敛速率由局部凝聚窗口的泊松化控制。（随机几何色团比趋二）
