# 00000009733

**English.** Definition: Separation of Frege and extended Frege systems: the relative strength of propositional proof systems. Conjecture: There exist explicit polynomial-size formula families for which extended Frege has polynomial proofs while Frege requires superpolynomial; the separating families are given by combinatorial extension encodings of trigonometric sum functions. (Frege separation explicitness)

**中文。** 定义：Frege 系统与扩展 Frege 系统的分离：命题证明系统的相对强度。猜想：存在显式的多项式规模公式族使扩展 Frege 有多项式证明而 Frege 需要超多项式；分离公式族由三角和函数的组合扩展编码给出。（Frege 分离显式化）
