# 00000002737

**English.** Definition: The combinatorial version of Morse theory (Forman): discrete gradient fields. Conjecture: Computing the minimal number of critical points of discrete Morse functions: minimization is NP-hard but linear for shellable complexes; the logarithmic gap of approximation is preserved by the independence-number obstruction of embeddings. (discrete Morse minimal complexity)

**中文。** 定义：Morse 理论的组合版（Forman DMV）：离散梯度场。猜想：离散 Morse 函数的极小临界点数计算：极小数为 NP 困难但对 shellable 复形为线性；近似的对数间隙由嵌入的独立数障碍保持。（离散 Morse 极小复杂度）
