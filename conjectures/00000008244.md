**English.** Definition: The dimension of the median voter theorem for single-peaked preference profiles: the generalization med_dim of the dimension of the median agent. Conjecture: The criterion for med_dim (the high-dimensional existence of a median) is value-restriction of the preference domain (a lattice criterion for restricted domains); the maximal number of agents for the intersection of the equivalent domains of single-crossing and single-peakedness (double-structure domains) is the explicit 2^d d (a double-structure domain formula); and the minimal domain escaping the Arrow impossibility by relaxing its three conditions (domain restriction escape) is the single-peaked domain (a minimal single-peaked escape domain), of size 2^{n-1} (a domain size formula). (median voter double-structure single-peaked escape)
