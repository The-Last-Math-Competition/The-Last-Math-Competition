# 00000007668

**QDE-08 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: For the partial q-binomial (z;q)_N with zeros z_i, the geometric mean of zero moduli is g_N = (prod |z_i|)^{1/N}. Conjecture: For 0<q<1, g_N satisfies g_N = q^{-(N-1)/2}(1+q)^{-1}(1+O(q^{N/2})), the leading correction coefficient being given explicitly by the Jensen mean of theta functions; and for algebraic q this limit constant is transcendental. (geometric-mean law for partial q-binomial zeros)

**中文。** 定义：部分 q-二项式 (z;q)_N 的零点模几何均值 g_N 定义为 Π_{i=1}^N |z_i| 的 N 次根,z_i 为其零点。猜想：对 0<q<1,g_N 满足渐近 g_N = q^{-(N-1)/2}·(1+q)^{-1}·(1+O(q^{N/2})),其中修正主项系数由 theta 函数的 Jensen 均值显式给出;当 q 为代数数时该极限常数为超越数。（部分二项式零点几何均值律）
