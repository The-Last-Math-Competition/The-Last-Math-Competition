# 00000007322

**English.** Definition: The geometric divergence of tensors as statistical distance. Conjecture: The tensor version of the Fisher metric holds, and the curvature of the version is the spectrum of mixing parameters. (mixing-spectrum tensor Fisher divergence)

**中文。** 定义：张量的几何散度：统计距离。猜想：散度的张量化：Fisher 度量的张量版且版的曲率为混合参数的谱。（张量 Fisher 混合参数谱几何散度统计距离曲率版本）
