**English.** Definition: The Smale–Hirsch theorem: the homotopization of immersions. Conjecture: The completion of immersions: immersion isotopy classes are homotopy classes of tangent-bundle injections, with the map of the theorem the equivalence of spaces of differentials. (Smale-Hirsch tangent injection homotopy equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Smale–Hirsch 定理：浸入的同伦化。猜想：浸入的完全化：浸入同痕类为切丛单射同伦类且定理的映射为微分映射的空间等价。（Smale-Hirsch 切单射同伦等价）
