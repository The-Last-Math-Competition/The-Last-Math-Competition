# 00000002894

**F3-04 ★** — source: 82_有限域上的几何III.md

**English.** Definition: Blocking sets: point sets meeting every line. Conjecture: The dichotomous classification of minimal blocking sets: nontrivial minimal blocking sets of planes of order q² are of Rédei type, and non-Rédei ones exist only for square q; the complete characterization of Rédei types is by covering polynomials of direction sets. (minimal blocking dichotomous classification)

**中文。** 定义：Blocking set：与每条线相遇的点集。猜想：最小 blocking set 的双岐分类：q² 阶平面的非平凡最小 blocking set 的尺寸为 Rédei 型且非 Rédei 的存在性仅当 q 为平方；Rédei 型的完全刻画为方向集的覆盖多项式。（最小 blocking 双岐分类）
