# 00000002270

**English.** Conjecture: Under convex renormalization of martingales, the stability perturbation of the Doob L^p maximal inequality constant (p/(p−1))^p is at most of the explicit type (1 + 1/(p−1)²) (Doob stability).

**中文。** 猜想：Doob L^p 极大不等式的常数 (p/(p−1))^p 在鞅的凸重整下的稳定扰动 ≤ (1 + 1/(p−1)^2) 型显式(Doob 稳定性)。
