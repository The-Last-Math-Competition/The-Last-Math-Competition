# 00000005261

**FL2-01 ◆** — source: 161_非线性偏微分II_流体II.md

**English.** Definition: Onsager's conjecture concerns the critical regularity of energy conservation: regularity above one third preserves energy. Conjecture: The critical Besov index of conservation is uniquely determined by the three-index family, with 1/3 as the unique critical value; the membership of the critical layer is decided by interpolation symmetry, and the critical index layer is logarithmically sensitive; and the admissible domain of spectral indices is the closed half-plane with p at most 3, with degeneration on the boundary. (Onsager critical Besov three-index family)

**中文。** 定义：Onsager。猜想：能量守恒的临界正则性——三分之一以上正则保持能量守恒。猜想：守恒的临界 Besov 指标由三指标族唯一决定且 1/3 为唯一临界；临界层的归属由插值对称性判定且临界指标层为对数敏感型；谱指标的容许域为 p 不超过 3 的闭半平面且边界上指标退化。（Onsager 临界 Besov 三指标）
