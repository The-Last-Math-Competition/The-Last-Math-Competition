**English.** Definition: The non-stable range is the interval of particle numbers where homological stabilization fails. Conjecture: For configurations on a genus g surface the first homological difference occurs exactly at n = 2g - 2, and the invariant at that point is given explicitly by Johnson homology. (location of the first non-stable difference)
