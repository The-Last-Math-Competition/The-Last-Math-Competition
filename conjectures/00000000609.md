# 00000000609

**English.** Definition: The Volume conjecture (Kashaev): the N-colored Jones value satisfies ⟨K⟩_N ~ exp(N·vol(K)/2π) to main order. New-direction Conjecture: the complete asymptotic expansion for the family of twist knots carries an N^{1/2} prefactor with explicit constants (qualitative results are known for some knots).

**中文。** 猜想：定义:Volume 猜想(Kashaev):第 N 色 Jones 值满足 ⟨K⟩_N ~ exp(N·vol(K)/2π) 的主项。猜想(新方向):twist 结族的完整渐近展开含 N^{1/2} 预因子且常数显式(已知部分结的定性结果)。
