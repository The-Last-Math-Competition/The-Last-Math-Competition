# 00000008867

**English.** Definition: Misere play: the inverted convention where the last player to move loses. Conjecture: Misere quotients have finite equivalence classes (a finiteness law); the general finiteness conjecture holds for misere quotients of all impartial games (a finiteness conjecture law); the known exceptions (quotients that are infinite) exist (an exception law); and the exceptions are classified by Nim-heap combinations of needles (a classification law). (misere quotient finiteness needle classification)

**中文。** 定义：misère play：最后行动者输的反常玩法。猜想：misère quotient 的（等价类的有限性）的（有限律）；且有限性的一般（一切 impartial 的 misère 商的有限性猜测）的（有限猜测律）；猜测的例外（已知无限商的例）的（例外律）；例外的分类（针（Nim-堆组合）的）的（分类律）。
