# 00000008861

**CGB-01 ★** — source: 281_组合博弈论II.md

**English.** Definition: Combinatorial games (in Conway's sense): two-player alternating perfect-information games without draws, forming recursive classes by the choices of Left and Right. Conjecture: The temperature spectrum of all-small games (the full spectrum of temperature values) is dense (a density law); the upper bound of temperature for all-small games is 1 (a bound law); the bound is tight (games attaining 1, a tightness law); and the tight constructions are of birdcage type (a construction law). (all-small temperature density birdcage tightness)

**中文。** 定义：组合博弈（Conway 意义）：无平局的双人轮流完美信息游戏，按左右玩家的选择构成递归类。猜想：微小（all-small）游戏的温度谱（温度值的全谱）的稠密性（稠密律）；且温度的上界（微小游戏的 1 的界）的（界律）；界的紧（达到 1 的游戏）的（紧律）；紧的构造（鸟巢（birdcage）型）的（构造律）。
