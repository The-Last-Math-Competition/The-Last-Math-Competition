# 00000002713

**English.** Definition: Cohen–Macaulay complexes: complexes whose links of arbitrary vertex chains are balls or homology balls. Conjecture: The Macaulay upper bound for h-vectors of CM complexes is attainable, and the classification of extremal complexes is the reverse-lexicographic elementary family; the construction of the family is unique up to combinatorial isomorphism. (CM h-vector Macaulay tightness)

**中文。** 定义：Cohen–Macaulay 复形：任意点链的链接为球或同调球的复形。猜想：CM 复形的 h-向量的 Macaulay 上界为可达界且极值复形的分类为 reverse lexicographic 初等族；族的构造唯一至组合同构。（CM h-向量 Macaulay 紧性）
