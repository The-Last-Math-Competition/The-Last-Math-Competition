# 00000008816

**English.** Definition: SVRG: gradient methods with variance reduction. Conjecture: The total gradient cost is n to the two-thirds; and the lower bound is matched uniformly over the window. (SVRG two-thirds matching)

**中文。** 定义：SVRG 为方差缩减的梯度方法。猜想：总梯度代价为 n 的三分之二次幂；且下界的匹配为窗一致。（SVRG 三分之二律匹配）
