# 00000009129

**English.** Definition: The regular structure at critical dimension one half. Conjecture: The component decomposition at critical dimension is a direct sum of purely one-dimensional parts; the stabilization of the decomposition is by density gaps; and the uniqueness of the decomposition is strict under the density dichotomy law. (critical dimension pure one-dimensional decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：临界二分之一维度的正则结构。猜想：临界维数的分量分解为纯一维直和；且分解的稳定为密度间隙；且分解的唯一性在密度二值律下严格。（临界维数纯一维分解）
