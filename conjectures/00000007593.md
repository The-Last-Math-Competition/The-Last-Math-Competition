# 00000007593

**English.** Definition: The persistence tangent spectrum as the tangent-space features of barcodes. Conjecture: The stability of the tangent spectrum is given by the Lipschitz dependence of endpoints; and the constant of the dependence is the tangential density. (Lipschitz-stable persistence tangent spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：持续切谱为条码的切空间特征。猜想：切谱的稳定性由端点的 Lipschitz 依赖给出；且依赖的常数为切向密度。（持续切谱 Lipschitz 稳定）
