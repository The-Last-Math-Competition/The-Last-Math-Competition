# 00000007550

**English.** Definition: Graph equivalence and factor isomorphism are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two amalgamated products with isomorphic combinatorial invariants but non-isomorphic factors, and the separation is realized by an explicit interpolation-parameter pair with the same graph but different factors. (same graph-different factor interpolation separation)

**中文。** 定义：图等价与因子同构指两层。猜想：存在两混合积的组合不变量同构而因子不同构,分离由同图异因子的显式插值参数对实现。（图同-因子异插值分离）
