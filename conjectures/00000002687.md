**English.** Definition: General position in the Zariski topology: the structure of dense open sets of parameter spaces. Conjecture: Critical stratification of dynamical parameter spaces: the boundary layer between minimal and stable parameters is the critical-orbit congruence layer of codimension at least two; outside the layer all critical orbits diverge uniformly. (dynamical parameter critical stratification)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 拓扑下的一般位置：参数空间的稠密开集结构。猜想：动力参数空间的临界分层：极小参数与稳定参数的边界层为临界轨迹同余层且层的余维数至少二；层的外部一切参数的临界轨道发散且发散速率一致。（动力参数临界分层）
