# 00000007201

**SC6-21 ★** — source: 225_概形论II.md

**English.** Definition: The Stein factorization of proper morphisms with connected fibers. Conjecture: The factorization into a finite part and a connected-fiber part holds, and the finite part is the semisimple kernel. (semisimple-kernel Stein factorization)

**中文。** 定义：proper 的 Stein 分解：连通纤维。猜想：Stein 的分裂：proper 的有限加纤维连通的分解且解的有限部分为半单核。（Stein 半单核有限分裂连通纤维分解）
