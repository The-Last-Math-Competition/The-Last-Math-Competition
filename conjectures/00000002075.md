# 00000002075

**English.** Definition: The calculus complexity of morphisms of Lawvere theories. Conjecture: The shortest morphism-representative length of the Lawvere theory of groups is polynomial in the target degree (concretely ≤ d⁴); the bound is controlled by the resolution strategy of composite words.

**中文。** 定义：Lawvere 理论的射的演算复杂度。猜想：群的 Lawvere 理论的最短射代表长度按目标度为多项式(具体地 ≤d^{4});界由合成词的消解策略控制。
