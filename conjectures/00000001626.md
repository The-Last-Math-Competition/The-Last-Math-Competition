# 00000001626

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of the random d-regular tree covering refers to the expected spectral measure of its adjacency operator (a modification of the Kesten density). Conjecture: The correction term of the spectral density is an explicit functional of the Laplace transform of the tree-height tail T(h): ρ(E) = ρ_K(E) + T̂(−log|E−E₀|); the correction is completely controlled by the boundary effect of finite trees.

**中文。** 定义：随机 d-正则树覆盖上的谱指其邻接算子的期望谱测度(修正自 Kesten 密度)。猜想：谱密度的修正项为树高度尾分布 T(h) 的 Laplace 变换的显式泛函:ρ(E)=ρ_K(E)+T̂(−log|E−E₀|);修正由有限树的边界效应完全控制。
