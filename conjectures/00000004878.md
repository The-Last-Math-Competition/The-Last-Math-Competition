**English.** Definition: The length-ratio distribution is the statistical law of the ratio of formalized to human proof lengths. Conjecture: The logarithm of the ratio is normally distributed with constant mean and variance decreasing logarithmically in the theorem size, and the fitting parameters are explicit and stable on large samples. (log-normal distribution law of length ratios)

**中文。** 定义：长度比分布指形式化与人类证明长度之比的统计律。猜想：该比的对数服从正态,均值恒定方差按定理规模对数递减,拟合参数显式且大样本稳定。（长度比对数正态分布律）
