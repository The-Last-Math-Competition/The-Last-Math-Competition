# 00000001810

**CV-30 ★** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: The Bers fibration is the map from Teichmüller space to a product of Teichmüller space and moduli. Conjecture: The analytic convexity of the image of the second Bers embedding is genuine; the convexity is explicit from the positivity of the kernel function (on model families).

**中文。** 定义：Bers 纤维化指 Teichmüller 空间到 Teichmüller 空间×moduli 的映射。猜想：第二 Bers 嵌入的像的解析凸性为真;凸性由 kernel 函数的正性(在模型簇上)显式。
