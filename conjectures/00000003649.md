**English.** Definition: The Stienen model: maximal inscribed balls. Conjecture: The packing rate of Stienen balls: the expectation of packing density is an explicit volume ratio, with the dimension dependence of the ratio Gamma functions. (Stienen Gamma volume ratio packing expectation)
