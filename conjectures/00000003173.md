**English.** Definition: Sketching: the randomization of linear algebra. Conjecture: The dimension rigidity of sketches: the optimal dimension of sketches for matrix multiplication is the rank of the target plus a log factor, with the optimum of the factor the inverse failure probability. (sketch dimension rank logarithmic factor)
