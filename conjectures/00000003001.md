# 00000003001

**English.** Definition: Mixed difference-differential Galois theory: Δ-Σ field theory. Conjecture: The splitting of mixed Galois groups: the mixed group splits into difference and differential parts if and only if the commutator deviation of the two operator types is zero, and the elimination of the deviation is the necessary and sufficient condition for commutativization. (mixed group splitting criterion)

**中文。** 定义：差分-微分混合 Galois：Δ-Σ 域理论。猜想：混合 Galois 群的分裂：混合群分裂为差分与微分部分当且仅当两类算子的交换偏差为零且偏差的消除为可交换化的充要条件。（混合群分裂充要）
