# 00000002616

**English.** Definition: Extremal counting of posets: width and Dilworth decompositions. Conjecture: The maximal number of maximal chains of a poset of width w has a Károlyi-type explicit formula; the realizing posets are horizontally balanced segmented constructions, unique up to isomorphism. (width-chain count extremal formula)

**中文。** 定义：偏序集的极值计数：宽度与 Dilworth 分解。猜想：宽度为 w 的偏序的极大链数最大值有 Károlyi 型显式公式；公式的可实现偏序为水平均衡的分段构造且均衡偏序在同构意义下唯一。（宽度-链数极值公式）
