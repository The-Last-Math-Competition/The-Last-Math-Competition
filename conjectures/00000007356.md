# 00000007356

**English.** Definition: Infinitesimal Ramsey theory with hyperfinite combinatorics. Conjecture: The infinitesimal strengthening of finite Ramsey holds, and the constant of the strengthening is hyperfinite. (hyperfinite-constant super-Ramsey strengthening)

**中文。** 定义：无穷小的 Ramsey：超有限组合。猜想：超 Ramsey：有限 Ramsey 的无穷小强化且化的常数为超有限。（超 Ramsey 超有限有限无穷小强化常数组合）
