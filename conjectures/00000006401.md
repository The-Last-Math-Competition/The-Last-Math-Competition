# 00000006401

**SD3-01 ★** — source: 199_几何测度V_对称差问题.md

**English.** Definition: The symmetric-difference inequality compares the measures of the difference parts of two sets. Conjecture: The tight constant of the inequality is given by the explicit triangular strengthened combination, saturating under the isoperimetric combination, and the extremal body of saturation is the equally divided disk. (triangular combination of symmetric-difference tight constants)

**中文。** 定义：对称差不等式比较两集差部分的测度。猜想：不等式的紧常数由三角强化的显式组合给出且在等周组合下饱和；且饱和的极值体为等分圆盘。（对称差紧常数三角组合）
