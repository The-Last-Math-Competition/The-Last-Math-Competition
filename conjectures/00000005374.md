# 00000005374

**English.** Definition: Nonarithmetic lattices are lattices of nonarithmetic origin. Conjecture: Their combinatorial invariants are constructed explicitly by covers of weighted graphs, distinguish common commensurability classes, and are complete for the known low-dimensional examples, with the invariant weights always determined by the volume spectrum of the same commensurability class. (weighted-cover invariant construction for nonarithmetic lattices)

**中文。** 定义：非算术格指非算术来源的格。猜想：其组合不变量由加权图的覆盖显式构建,不变量区分共同度量类且对已知低维例完全。(非算术加权覆盖不变量构建)；且不变量权重恒由相同度量类的体积谱决定。
