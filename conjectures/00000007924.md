# 00000007924

**ATC-24 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The ramification locus density of Gan-Gross-Prasad: the image D_loc in Hecke parameter space of the local ramification conditions for families with nonzero period. Conjecture: D_loc is the image of the spherical-variety space (spherical variety) of the local field, with positive measure if and only if the spherical variety is of GGP type; the density of the complement (representations without period) is an explicit Euler factor of type 1/zeta_loc(2), with global density the product of local factors; and the step corrections of local factors under ramified configurations (multiple ramification) are uniquely determined by the sign classification of the epsilon factor. (GGP ramification Euler density)

**中文。** 定义：Gan–Gross–Prasad 的分歧轨迹密度：周期非零的表示族的局部分歧条件在 Hecke 参数空间的像 D_loc。猜想：D_loc 为局部域的球簇空间（球变体）的像，其测度为正当且仅当球簇为 GGP 型；且像的补集（无周期表示）的密度为 1/ζ_{loc}(2) 型显式 Euler 因子，整体密度为局部因子的乘积；局部因子在分歧构型（多分歧）下的阶跃修正由根数 (epsilon factor) 的符号分类唯一确定。
