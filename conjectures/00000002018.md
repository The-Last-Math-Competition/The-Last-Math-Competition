# 00000002018

**PD-28 ★** — source: 52_偏微分方程II.md

**English.** Conjecture: The critical exponent for global well-posedness of Maxwell–Klein–Gordon in the energy gauge is s_c (energy-critical s = 1); for general gauge fixing, the well-posedness threshold is s ≥ s_c (i.e. no extra loss); improvements of the 1/8-loss type occur only in Coulomb gauge.

**中文。** 猜想：Maxwell–Klein–Gordon 的能量规范的整体适定性的临界指数为 s_c(能量临界 s=1);对一般的规范固定,适定性的阈值为 s≥ s_c(即无额外损失);损失 1/8 型的改善仅出现在 Coulomb 规范。
