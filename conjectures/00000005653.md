**English.** Definition: The heat-kernel transition is the switching point of the stages of the curve. Conjecture: The transition threshold of small graphs is the explicit exponential of the uniformization time, controlled by the squared diameter, and the curve slope at the threshold is one half. (half-slope threshold of small-graph heat-kernel transitions)

**中文。** 定义：热核转移指曲线的阶段切换点。猜想：小图的转移阈值为一致化时间的显式指数,由直径平方控制且阈值处曲线斜率为半。（小图热核转移半斜率阈值）
