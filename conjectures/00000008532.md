# 00000008532

**English.** Definition: The Sperner number of a lattice is the size of its largest antichain. Conjecture: The Sperner number of the free distributive lattice is an explicit central correction of binom(n, floor(n/2)); the correction is of logarithmic order with always positive sign. (free distributive lattice Sperner)

**中文。** 定义：格的 Sperner 数指其最大反链的尺寸。猜想：自由分配格的 Sperner 数为 binom(n,⌊n/2⌋) 的显式中心修正；修正项为对数级且符号恒正。（自由分配格 Sperner）
