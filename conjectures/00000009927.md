**English.** Definition: Equivariant persistent homology: statistics of quotient filtrations under group actions. Conjecture: The stability constant of equivariant persistent homology is the product of the Lipschitz constant of the orbit-space metric and the maximal anisotropy ratio of the group action; the constant degenerates to the nonequivariant case for free actions. (equivariant stability constant)

**中文。** 定义：等变持久同调：群作用下的商过滤的统计。猜想：等变持久同调的稳定性常数为轨道空间度量的 Lipschitz 常数与群作用的最大各向异性比的乘积；常数在自由作用下退化为非等变情形。（等变稳定性常数）
