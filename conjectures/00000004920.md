**MD3-20 △** — source: 149_随机过程III_马氏决策.md

**English.** Definition: Regret asymptotics and trajectory distribution are two layers of statistics. Conjecture: There exist two learning algorithms with the same regret asymptotics but different state trajectory distributions, and the separation is realized by an explicit reordering of exploration order. (same regret-trajectory distribution reordering separation)

**中文。** 定义：悔值渐近与轨迹分布指两层统计。猜想：存在两学习算法的悔值渐近相同而状态轨迹分布不同,分离由探索顺序的显式重排实现。（悔值同-轨迹分布重排分离）
