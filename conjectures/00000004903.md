# 00000004903

**English.** Definition: Blackwell optimality is the stable optimal policy for sufficiently small discounts. Conjecture: Its discount threshold upper bound is an explicit polynomial in the transition and reward parameters, a concrete constant for small state spaces, and the bound is optimal. (Blackwell discount threshold polynomial bound)

**中文。** 定义：Blackwell 最优指折扣充分小时的稳定最优策略。猜想：其折扣阈值上界为转移与奖励参数的显式多项式,小状态空间时为具体常数且界最优。（Blackwell 折扣阈值多项式界）
