# 00000009593

**English.** Definition: The first lower bound on the count of minimal surfaces in closed 3-manifolds: Lyusternik-Schnirelmann give 3; the relation to homology lower bounds is unknown. Conjecture: Every closed 3-manifold has at least b_1(M) + 3 embedded minimal surfaces; the lower bound is attained by hyperbolic geometry of Heegaard decompositions, with no lower bound improvement beyond homology. (minimal surface homology lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：闭 3-流形中极小曲面个数的第一下界：Lyusternik–Schnirelmann 给出 3；与同调的下界关系未知。猜想：每个闭 3-流形的嵌入极小曲面数 ≥ b_1(M)+3；下界由 Heegaard 分解的双曲几何实现等号，无超越同调的下界改进。（极小曲面同调下界）
