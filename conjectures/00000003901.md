# 00000003901

**CA3-21 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: The Frobenius exponent quotient of an ideal I is the quotient I^{[p^e]}/I, and one studies the growth of its length with e. Conjecture: For every ideal in an n-dimensional ring, the leading exponent of this length in p^e is always n-1, and the leading coefficient is the explicit value given by the boundary measure of the Newton polyhedron divided by (n-1)!. (Frobenius bridge leading coefficient)

**中文。** 定义：Frobenius 桥指数商 I^{[p^e]}/I 的长度随 e 的增长。猜想：对 n 维环上任意理想,该长度的首项指数恒为 n−1,首项系数为牛顿多面体边界测度除以 (n−1)! 的显式值。（Frobenius 桥首项系数）
