# 00000003901

**CA3-21 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: growth of length e of Frobenius exponent quotient I^{[p^e]}/I. Conjecture: leading term exponent of pair n dimension ring on arbitrary ideal, the length is always n−1, leading term coefficient is polytope boundary measure divided by explicit of (n−1)!. (Frobenius leading term coefficient)

**中文。** 定义：Frobenius 桥指数商 I^{[p^e]}/I 的长度随 e 的增长。猜想：对 n 维环上任意理想,该长度的首项指数恒为 n−1,首项系数为牛顿多面体边界测度除以 (n−1)! 的显式值。（Frobenius 桥首项系数）
