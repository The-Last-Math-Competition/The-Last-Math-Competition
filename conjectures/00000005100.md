# 00000005100

**English.** Definition: The eigensheaf spectrum and the Betti moduli space are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two local systems with the same eigensheaf spectrum but non-homeomorphic Betti moduli spaces, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of isospectral non-isotopic systems. (same eigensheaf spectrum-different Betti separation pair)
