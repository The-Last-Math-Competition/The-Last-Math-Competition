**English.** Definition: Shape regularity is the quality condition of element geometry. Conjecture: The error is an explicit power of the minimal angle, diverging at degenerate elements, and the kernel of the divergence is anisotropic stretching. (minimal-angle power of anisotropic stretching in shape regularity)
