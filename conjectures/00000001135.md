# 00000001135

**English.** Definition: P(t) = Σ b_{2i} t^i is the Betti-number generating function of the Bott–Samelson variety BS(s₁,…,s_k). Conjecture: The coefficients of P(t) are strictly increasing up to the middle level, P(t) is divisible by (1+t)^{⌊k/2⌋} with monic quotient, and the quotient's coefficients have no internal zeros when s₁…s_k is a reduced word.

**中文。** 定义：Bott–Samelson 簇 BS(s₁,…,s_k) 的 Betti 数生成函数 P(t)=Σ b_{2i} t^i。猜想：P(t) 的系数严格递增直到中间层,且 P(t) 被 (1+t)^{⌊k/2⌋} 整除,商为首一多项式;商多项式的系数在字 s₁…s_k 为约化词时无零间隙。
