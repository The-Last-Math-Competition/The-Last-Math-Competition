# 00000000531

**CA-31 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The set of annihilating primes of Frobenius-invariant submodules of local cohomology forms the structure sheaf of the module. Conjecture: This set is finite and its elements are bounded by a degree-one polynomial in the characteristic; and the explicit bound on prime support is given by the roots of the Bernstein–Sato polynomial. (D-module finiteness of annihilating primes)

**中文。** 定义：局部上同调的 Frobenius 不变子模的零化素数集为局部上同调模的结构层。猜想：该集合有限且其元素被特征的一次多项式界定；且素数支撑的显式界由 Bernstein–Sato 多项式的根给出。（D-模零化素数有限界）
