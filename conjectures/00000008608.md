# 00000008608

**MLC-18 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Interaction detection is testing for the presence of higher-order terms. Conjecture: The minimal detectable amplitude of s-order interactions is n^{-1/2}(log d)^{s/2}; and the logarithmic factor is necessary at s = 2. (interaction detection logarithmic factor)

**中文。** 定义：交互检测指高阶项存在检验。猜想：s 阶交互的最小可检测振幅为 n^{−1/2}(log d)^{s/2}；对数因子在 s=2 必要。（交互检测对数因子）
