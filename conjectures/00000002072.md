# 00000002072

**English.** Definition: Morita equivalence classes of fusion categories are bimodule equivalences between categories. Conjecture: The number of Morita equivalence classes of fusion categories of dimension n is finite if and only if the prime factors of n are at most 2 (a power of two); when n has a prime factor ≥ 3 there are infinitely many classes.

**中文。** 定义：融合范畴的 Morita 等价类指范畴间的双模等价。猜想：维数 n 的融合范畴的 Morita 等价类在同维数有限,当且仅当 n 的素因子 ≤2(二幂);n 含素因子 ≥3 时无限类。
