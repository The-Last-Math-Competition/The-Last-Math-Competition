# 00000000189

**CS-23 ◆** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: Values of the Gamma function at rational points. Conjecture: Γ(1/5) and Γ(2/5) are both transcendental; moreover, their joint transcendence degree together with π is at least two. (Gamma fifths degree two)

**中文。** 定义：Γ 函数在有理点的值。猜想：Γ(1/5) 与 Γ(2/5) 都是超越数；且二者与 π 的联合超越次数至少为二。（Gamma-五分值次数二）
