# 00000002899

**English.** Definition: The Kakeya problem over finite fields: the size of sets containing a line in every direction. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of Kakeya sets: the size of minimal Kakeya sets in d-dimensional space has the explicit asymptotic lower bound of type q^d(1−c/q), attained by tube merging of polynomial methods. (finite Kakeya size asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：有限域上的 Kakeya 问题：含每方向线的集合的尺寸。猜想：Kakeya 集的维数谱：d 维空间的最小 Kakeya 集的尺寸有 q^d(1−c/q) 型显式渐近下界且下界由多项式方法的管合并达到。（有限 Kakeya 尺寸渐近）
