# 00000004994

**English.** Definition: The automorphism group is the structure-preserving isometry group. Conjecture: The dimension upper bound of the automorphism group of a compact Spin(7) manifold with vanishing first Betti number is three, attained by symmetric constructions of torus fibrations and not exceedable. (dimension upper bound three of Spin(7) automorphism groups)

**中文。** 定义：自同构群指保结构的等距群。猜想：一阶贝蒂数为零的紧 Spin(7) 的自同构群维数上界为三,由环面纤维的对称构造达到且不可超越。(Spin(7) 自同构维数上界三)。
