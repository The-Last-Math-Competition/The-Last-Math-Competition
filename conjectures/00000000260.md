# 00000000260

**English.** Definition: u(p) is the fundamental solution of the Pell equation (for primes p ≡ 1 mod 4). Conjecture: For infinitely many primes p, u(p) exceeds p^{1/4+ε}; and such primes are dense in towers of quadratic fields. (Pell fundamental solution quartic growth)

**中文。** 定义：u(p) 为 Pell 方程的基本解（p 模四为一的素数）。猜想：对无穷多 p 有 u(p) 大于 p 的四分之一加 ε 次幂；且此类素数在二次域塔中稠密。（Pell 基本解四次幂增长）
