# 00000004109

**English.** Definition: Compactly supported cohomology is the cohomology with compactly supported sections. Conjecture: For non-compact X the duality correction terms of Conf_n(X) are concentrated in the top two degrees, their dimension equals the number of ends, and the correction is a pure layer of weight 2n. (dimension of the compactly supported duality correction)

**中文。** 定义：紧支撑上同调指具紧支撑截面的上同调。猜想：非紧 X 上 Conf_n(X) 的对偶修正项集中在最高两个度,维数等于末端个数,修正为纯权 2n 层。（紧支撑对偶修正维数）
