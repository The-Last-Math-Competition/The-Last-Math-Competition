# 00000008721

**MBL-11 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: The decoherence lifetime of MBL: the survival time of localization under environmental coupling. Conjecture: Under any nonzero environmental coupling the MBL lifetime is finite (a finite lifetime law); the lifetime grows as exp(W) with disorder strength (an exponential lifetime law); the relaxation function is a stretched exponential with beta = 1/2 (a half-exponential relaxation law); and the asymptotic relaxation slows logarithmically (a logarithmic slowing law). (MBL decoherence exponential lifetime stretched relaxation)

**中文。** 定义：MBL 的退相干寿命：环境耦合下局域化的存活时间。猜想：任意非零环境耦合下 MBL 寿命有限（有限寿命律）；且寿命随无序强度为 exp(W) 增长（指数寿命律）；弛豫函数为 β=1/2 的拉伸指数（半指数弛豫律）；渐近弛豫为对数慢化（对数慢化律）。
