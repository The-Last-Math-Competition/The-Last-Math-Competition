# 00000009521

**English.** Definition: The Holevo capacity of a quantum channel phi, chi(phi) = max over ensembles {p_i, rho_i} of [S(sum p_i rho_i) - sum p_i S(rho_i)] with S the von Neumann entropy; the additivity question asks whether chi(phi tensor psi) = chi(phi) + chi(psi) holds for a given pair — Hastings proved it fails in general. Conjecture: The channels for which additivity holds against every psi form a closed cone Add; for dimensions d at least 4, Add is neither the whole space nor a low-dimensional lattice, its boundary consists of the analyticity-breaking points of chi, and the expected violation sup_psi[chi(phi tensor psi) - chi(phi) - chi(psi)] over random unitary conjugation orbits is Theta(1/d). (Holevo additivity structure domain)

**中文。** 定义：量子信道 φ 的 Holevo 容量 χ(φ)=max over 系综 {p_i,ρ_i} [S(Σp_iρ_i)−Σp_i S(ρ_i)]，S 为冯诺依曼熵；可加性问题问 χ(φ⊗ψ)=χ(φ)+χ(ψ) 是否对给定信道对成立，Hastings 已证一般不成立。猜想：使可加性对一切 ψ 成立的信道构成闭锥 Add；Add 在维数 d≥4 时既非全空间也非低维格，其边界由 χ 的解析性破裂点组成，且违反量 sup_ψ[χ(φ⊗ψ)−χ(φ)−χ(ψ)] 在随机酉共轭轨道上的期望为 Θ(1/d)。（Holevo 可加性结构域）
