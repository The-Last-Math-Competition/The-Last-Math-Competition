**CG-23 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The surround cop number is the least number of cops needed to surround rather than capture. Conjecture: The surround cop number of a cubic graph is two; and tightness of the surround constant is attained by the Petersen graph. (cubic surround constant)

**中文。** 定义：包围警察数指包围而非捕获所需的最少警察数。猜想：三正则图的包围警察数为二；且包围常数的紧性由 Petersen 图达到。（三正则包围常数）
