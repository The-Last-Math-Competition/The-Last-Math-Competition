# 00000007534

**English.** Definition: The invariant criterion as the detection of Galois-action triviality. Conjecture: The invariant subspace of cohomology is computed by the idempotent projection of the group ring; and the rank of the projection is the representation multiplicity. (idempotent-projection invariant criterion)

**中文。** 定义：不变量判据为 Galois 作用平凡性的检测。猜想：上同调的不变量子空间由群环的幂等投影计算；且投影的秩为表示重数。（不变量幂等投影判据）
