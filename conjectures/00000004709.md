**EC4-19 ★** — source: 142_遍历论的组合_熵与计数.md

**English.** Definition: A bracket polynomial is a generalized polynomial containing floor operations. Conjecture: The criterion for uniform distribution of its zeros is an explicit irrationality-type condition on the leading coefficient, and the condition is complete and verified by approximation rates. (irrationality-type criterion of bracket polynomials)

**中文。** 定义：括号多项式指含取整运算的广义多项式。猜想：其零点均匀分布的判据为主系数无理性类型的显式条件,条件完全且由逼近速率验证。（括号多项式无理性类型判据）
