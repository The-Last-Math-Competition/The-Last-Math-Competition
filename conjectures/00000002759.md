# 00000002759

**English.** Definition: The Golod–Shafarevich side of algebras: PI constraints and tower growth. Conjecture: The PI obstruction to GS towers: algebras satisfying a nontrivial PI admit no GS-type infinite tower, with obstruction constant the quadratic-form index of the relation space. (PI-GS tower obstruction constant)
