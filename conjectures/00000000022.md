# 00000000022

**English.** Definition: The saturation scale of Chebotarev counting: the smallest x at which the ratio of π_C(x) to its main term first enters the 1/2-neighborhood and remains there permanently. Conjecture: For zero-free extensions the saturation scale is at most exp(L^{1+o(1)}), where L is the logarithm of the discriminant; if an exceptional zero β exists, the saturation delay is at least (1−β)^{-1} and at most its square; the delay is inversely proportional to the width of the zero-free region with proportionality constant 1; and the difference between the saturation scales of a fixed conjugacy class C and of the unit class is at most a constant multiple of log L.

**中文。** 定义：Chebotarev 计数的饱和尺度：π_C(x) 与主项之比首次进入 1/2 邻域并永久停留的最小 x。猜想：零点自由扩张的饱和尺度不超过 exp(L^{1+o(1)})，L 为判别式对数；存在例外零点 β 时饱和延迟至少 (1-β)^{-1} 且不超过其平方；延迟与零点自由区域宽度成反比且比例常数为 1；固定共轭类 C 的饱和尺度与单位类的饱和尺度之差不超过 log L 的常数倍。
