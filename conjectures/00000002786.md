**English.** Definition: Almost-orthogonal complements of Hilbert subspaces: Bessaga–Pełczyński type decompositions. Conjecture: The dimension lower bound of almost-orthogonal block complements: the dimension of the complement of an ℓ₂ copy is the logarithm of the codimension, with the lower bound given by the inverse of the type constant. (block complement dimension logarithmic lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert 子空间的几乎正交补：Bessaga–Pełczyński 型分解。猜想：几乎正交的块补的维数下界：ℓ₂ 副本的补的维数为余维数的对数且下界由型常数的逆给出。（块补维数对数下界）
