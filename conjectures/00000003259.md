**DT-09 ★** — source: 94_微分拓扑II_浸入与套叠.md

**English.** Definition: Pseudo-isotopy: the weak equivalence of topological structures. Conjecture: The obstruction to pseudo-isotopy: the lifting obstruction from pseudo-isotopy to isotopy is the Tor term of the Whitehead group, with the vanishing of the term the s-invertibility criterion. (pseudo-isotopy Whitehead Tor obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：伪isotopy（伪同痕）：拓扑结构的弱等价。猜想：伪同痕的障碍：伪同痕到同痕的提升障碍为 Whitehead 群的 Tor 项且项的消没为 s-可逆判据。（伪同痕 Whitehead Tor 障碍）
