# 00000004059

**English.** Definition: A relative AR-sequence is an almost-split sequence with respect to a functorially finite subcategory. Conjecture: Relative AR-sequences exist if and only if the relative projective dimension of the subcategory is finite, and this threshold is closed under extensions and cannot be relaxed further. (existence threshold of relative AR-sequences)

**中文。** 定义：相对 AR 序列指对 functorially finite 子范畴的几乎分裂列。猜想：相对 AR 序列存在当且仅当子范畴相对投射维数有限,该阈值对扩张封闭且不可再放宽。（相对 AR 存在阈值）
