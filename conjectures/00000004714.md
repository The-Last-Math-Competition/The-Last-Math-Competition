**EC4-24 ★** — source: 142_遍历论的组合_熵与计数.md

**English.** Definition: A minimal subshift is a system with no proper closed invariant sets. Conjecture: The factor maps between minimal systems can be realized combinatorially by the induction algorithm, the algorithm terminates if and only if the factor is almost one-to-one, and the criterion is complete. (induction realization criterion of minimal factors)

**中文。** 定义：极小子移位指无真闭不变集的系统。猜想：极小系统间的因子图可由诱导算法组合实现,算法终止当且仅当因子为几乎一对一,判据完全。（极小因子诱导实现判据）
