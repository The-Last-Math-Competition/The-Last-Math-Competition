# 00000002020

**English.** Conjecture: The superexponential splitting rate of elliptic resonance pairs is e^{−c·e^{t}} (double exponential); the splitting rate exactly matches the product of the unstable eigenvalues (with c the root of λ₁·λ₂); the relation uniquely determines the splitting.

**中文。** 猜想：椭圆共振对的超指数分裂率为 e^{-c·e^{t}}(双重指数);分裂率与不稳定特征值的乘积精确匹配(指数 c = λ₁·λ₂ 的根);关系式唯一确定分裂。
