**English.** Definition: measure realized of -fold normal: weight sequence of weighted place. Conjecture: weighted place is -fold normal if and only if moment problem of weight sequence has uniqueness of positive measure solution; solution by strictly of logarithm concavity of weight and support of unique measure is explicit recurrence set. (weighted place -fold normal)

**中文。** 定义：加权移位的次正规性：权序列的测度实现。猜想：加权移位为次正规当且仅当权序列的矩问题有正测度解；解的唯一性由权的对数凹性的严格性判定且唯一测度的支撑为显式递推集。（加权移位次正规判据）
