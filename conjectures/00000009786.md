**English.** Definition: Subnormality of weighted shifts: measure realizations of weight sequences. Conjecture: A weighted shift is subnormal if and only if the moment problem of its weight sequence has a positive measure solution; uniqueness of the solution is decided by strictness of log-concavity of the weights, and the support of the unique measure is an explicit recursive set. (weighted shift subnormality criterion)

**中文。** 定义：加权移位的次正规性：权序列的测度实现。猜想：加权移位为次正规当且仅当权序列的矩问题有正测度解；解的唯一性由权的对数凹性的严格性判定且唯一测度的支撑为显式递推集。（加权移位次正规判据）
