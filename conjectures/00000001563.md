# 00000001563

**English.** Definition: Sierksma's conjecture asserts that n points in d dimensions in general colorful position determine at least (d+1)^{n−d−1} rainbow simplices. Conjecture: The second term of this lower bound is O(d^{n−d−2}); the extremal configuration is unique — the neighborhood layering of the (d+1)-arm star.

**中文。** 定义：Sierksma 猜想断言 d 维空间 n 点彩色配置的彩虹单纯形数 ≥ (d+1)^{n-d-1}。猜想：该下界的第二项为 O(d^{n-d-2});极值构型唯一,为 (d+1)-臂星形的邻域层叠。
