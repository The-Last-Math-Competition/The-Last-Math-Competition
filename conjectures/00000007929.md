# 00000007929

**English.** Definition: The shape rigidity of functional equations: the root number epsilon(s) of an L-function of degree d in the Selberg class is a complex number of unit modulus. Conjecture: The analytic shape of epsilon(s) is a constant epsilon_0 times Q^{1-2s} times the normalized product of Gamma factors, where epsilon_0 is a 2d-th root of unity; and the rigidity of epsilon_0 is consistent with Hodge weights: epsilon_0 of non-automorphic (fake) L-functions falls outside the group of roots of unity (irrational phase), so automorphicity in the Selberg class can be decided by epsilon_0 alone (the shape detection principle). (root-of-unity shape detection)

**中文。** 定义：泛函方程的形状刚性：Selberg 类中次数 d 的 L 函数的泛函方程的根数 ε(s) 为单位模复数。猜想：ε(s) 的解析形状为 ε₀·Q^{1-2s}·Π Γ-因子的规范化的常数 ε₀，其中 ε₀ 为 2d 次单位根；且 ε₀ 的刚性与 Hodge 权重一致：非自守（假）的 L 函数的 ε₀ 落在单位根群之外（无理数相位），故 Selberg 类的自守性可仅由 ε₀ 判定（形状检测原理）。
