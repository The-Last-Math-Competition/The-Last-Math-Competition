# 00000002833

**English.** Definition: Secant varieties and rank stratification: the algebraic stratification of tensor spaces by rank. Conjecture: The first defect of the rank stratification: the dimension defect of secant varieties is one for the triple Veronese embedding, and higher defects in the symmetric case vanish for ranks at most 4. (secant defect spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Secant 簇与秩的分层：张量空间按秩的代数分层。猜想：秩分层的第一缺陷：secant 簇的维数亏缺（defect）在 Veronese 三重嵌入为一次且更高缺陷的对称情形在秩不超过 4 消失。（secant 缺陷谱）
