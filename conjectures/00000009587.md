# 00000009587

**MSH-07 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The volume-measure equidistribution of minimal hypersurfaces in closed manifolds with Baire-generic metrics (the Irie-Marques-Neves theorem); the quantitative error of equidistribution is unknown. Conjecture: For general metrics, the deviation of the empirical measure of the first k minimal hypersurfaces for any continuous function is O(k^{-1/(2n+2)}); the exceptional set of quantum limits is empty, and the spectral measure limit is always the volume measure. (equidistribution error rate)

**中文。** 定义：Baire 一般度量的闭流形中极小超曲面的体积测度等分布（Irie–Marques–Neves 定理）；等分布的定量误差未知。猜想：一般度量下前 k 个极小超曲面的经验测度对任意连续函数的偏差为 O(k^{−1/(2n+2)})；量子极限的例外极小曲面集合为空集，谱测度极限恒为体积测度。（等分布误差率）
