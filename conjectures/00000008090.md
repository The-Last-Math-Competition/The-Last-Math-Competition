# 00000008090

**English.** Definition: The Adams decomposition of fusion systems: the fusion analogue of the splitting of self-equivalences of stable p-completed spaces — the Adams sum Sigma_Ad(F) of the spectrum |L|_p-hat. Conjecture: Sigma_Ad(F) (the number of terms of the Adams decomposition) equals the logarithm of |S| (a term-count order-logarithm law); each term of the decomposition is a rank-1 atomic system (an atomic decomposition); and the period of the atomic systems (v_1-periodicity) is p-1 or 2(p+1) (a two-valued period law), the switch between the two values being determined by the parity type of the rank of the fusion system (a parity switch criterion). (fusion Adams atomic two-valued periods)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 Adams 分解：稳定的 p-完备空间的自我等分的分裂的融合类比：融合系统的谱谱 |L|_p^ 的 Adams 和 Σ_Ad(F)。猜想：Σ_Ad(F)（Adams 分解的项数）= |S| 的对数（项数-阶对数律）；且分解的每一项的融合型为秩 1 的原子系（原子分解）；原子系的周期（v_1-周期）为 p-1 或 2(p+1)（周期二值律），二值的切换由融合系统的"奇偶型"（rank 奇偶）决定（奇偶切换判据）。
