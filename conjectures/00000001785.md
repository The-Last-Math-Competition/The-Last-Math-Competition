# 00000001785

**CV-05 ★** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal constant of OT-type extension on the polydisc with weight φ = c·log|z₁|. Conjecture: The optimal constant is an explicit step function of c: C(c) = max(1, 2c)·π (with a transition at c = 1/2); the step function is controlled by the vanishing orders of singular-weight poles.

**中文。** 定义：多圆柱上带权 φ=clog|z₁| 的 OT 型延拓的最优常数。猜想：最优常数为 c 的显式阶梯函数:C(c) = max(1, 2c)·π(过 c=1/2 跃迁);阶梯函数由奇权极点的零点阶数控制。
