# 00000000223

**English.** Definition: Euclid numbers E_n = p_n# + 1 (one plus the product of the first n primes). Conjecture: The least prime factor of E_n exceeds p_n uniformly for all sufficiently large n, and the least prime factors are uniformly distributed on the logarithmic scale over (p_n, p_n²); moreover, for a fixed prime q, the density of n with q dividing E_n is an explicit value of q^{−2} type. (Euclid number least prime factor spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Euclid 数 E_n=p_n#+1（前 n 个素数之积加一）。猜想：E_n 的最小素因子大于 p_n 对充分大 n 一致成立且最小素因子在对数尺度上均匀分布于 (p_n,p_n²)；进一步，固定素数 q 整除 E_n 的 n 的密度为 q^{−2} 型显式值。（Euclid 数最小素因子谱）
