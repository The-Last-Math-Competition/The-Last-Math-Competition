# 00000001590

**English.** Definition: The Bethe algebra of the Gaudin model. Conjecture: The equality of the images of the Bethe algebra and the Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra holds globally on the skew Cherednik commutative family (the Bethe-GT equality).

**中文。** 定义：Gaudin 模的 Bethe 代数。猜想：Bethe 代数与 Gelfand–Tsetlin 代数的像的等式在斜 Cherednik 交换簇上为整体(Bethe-GT 等式)。
