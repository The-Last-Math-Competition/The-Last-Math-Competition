# 00000007958

**INT-28 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The companion rigidity of bi-Hamiltonian structures: the recursivity index r(P) of the Leitner-Faddeev coefficients of two compatible Poisson structures (P_0, P_1) of an integrable system. Conjecture: r(P) is the codimension of rank P_0; and the complete integrability invariant of Gel'fand-Zakharevich systems is determined by the Smith normal form of the Jacobian of the Casimir family; the elementary divisors of the normal form are preserved under bi-Hamiltonian reduction, and their vanishing (trivialization) is exactly the criterion for Lie systems (right-invariant Lie algebra type), giving the complete bi-Hamiltonian classification of Lie systems. (bi-Hamiltonian Smith normal form rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：双 Hamiltonian 结构的相伴刚性：可积系统的两族相容 Poisson 结构 (P₀,P₁) 的 Leitner-Faddeev 系数的可递性指标 r(P)。猜想：r(P) = rank P₀ 的补维；且 Gel'fand–Zakharevich 系统的完全可积性的不变量由 Casimir 函数组的 Jacobi 矩阵的 Smith 标准型决定；标准型的初等因子在双哈密顿约化下保持，其消没（平凡化）恰为 Lie 系统（右不变李代数型）的判据（Lie 系统的双哈密顿完全分类）。
