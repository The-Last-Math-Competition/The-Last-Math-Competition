**English.** Definition: Exotic Stein structures are symplectically distinct structures on the same topology. Conjecture: The Stein structures of R⁸ form a contractible family; and the kernel of the family is the Casimir handles. (R8 Stein Casimir handle contractible family)

**中文。** 定义：exotic Stein 结构指同拓扑不同辛结构。猜想：R⁸ 的 Stein 结构的可缩族；且族的核为 Casimir 把柄。（R8-Stein Casimir 把柄可缩族）
