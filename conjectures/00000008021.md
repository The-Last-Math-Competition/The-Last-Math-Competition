# 00000008021

**OMN-01 ◆** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The compactification bound of the Pila-Wilkie point counting theorem: the compactification exponent kappa(X) of the algebraic point count N(X,H) (rational points of height at most H) of transcendental subvarieties X in R^n definable in an o-minimal structure. Conjecture: For all structures with moderate values, the epsilon-bound N(X,H) <= C H^epsilon can be uniformized (controlled by the relative volume of the semialgebraic exceptional subvariety); the dimension of the optimal exceptional subvariety equals the Zariski dimension of the semialgebraic closure (an explicit volume version of the Zilber-Peterzil conjecture); and the volume constant of the exceptional subvariety is a Sylvester-type elimination constant of integer-coefficient polynomial systems. (Pila-Wilkie moderate volume exceptional bound)

**中文。** 定义：Pila–Wilkie 点计数定理的紧化界：定义在 o-极小结构中的超越子簇 X⊂R^n 的代数点计数 N(X,H)（高度 ≤H 的有理点）的紧化指数 κ(X)。猜想：对一切"适度取值"（moderate values）结构，N(X,H) ≤ C H^{ε} 的 ε-界可一致化（半代数例外簇的相对体积控制）；且最优例外簇的维数为半代数闭包的 Zariski 维数（Zilber–Peterzil 猜想的显式体积版）；例外簇的体积常数为整系数多项式系统的 Sylvester 型消去常数。
