# 00000008237

**EGT-07 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The lattice structure of stable matchings: the lattice invariants of the interval between the men-optimal and women-optimal matchings. Conjecture: The completion of lat_stab (the stable matching lattice, known to be distributive): complements exist (unique complements) if and only if the lattice is boolean (a boolean criterion); the expected height of the lattice for random preference profiles is log n (a logarithmic expected height law); and the density of failures of the alternation of the sign of the Mobius function (a sign alternation law) is e^{-Theta(n)} (an exponentially sparse failure). (stable matching boolean completion logarithmic height)

**中文。** 定义：稳定匹配的格结构：男性最优与女性最优匹配的格的区间 [μ_M, μ_W] 的格不变量 lat_stab。猜想：lat_stab（稳定匹配格）为分配格（分配律已知）的补全：格的补元的存在（唯一补）当且仅当格为布尔（布尔判据）；且稳定匹配的随机剖面的格的期望高度为 log n（对数期望高度律）；格的 Möbius 函数的符号的交替（符号交替律）的破坏（同号 Möbius）的密度为 e^{-Θ(n)}（指数稀疏破坏）。
