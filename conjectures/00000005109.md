**English.** Definition: The localization theorem is the localization construction in the symplectic duality framework. Conjecture: The localization conversion is always given explicitly by the spectral data of the symplectic dual pair, closed for the lowest-rank pairs and preserving deformation equivalence. (symplectic-dual spectral conversion of localization)

**中文。** 定义：局部化定理指辛对偶框架的局部化构造。猜想：局部化换算恒由辛对偶对的谱数据显式给出；最低秩对封闭且保持形变等价。（局部化辛对偶谱换算）
