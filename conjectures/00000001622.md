# 00000001622

**English.** Definition: Multiplicity splitting under Sobolev perturbations refers to the perturbative expansion of eigenvalue clusters. Conjecture: The gaps in the splitting of a k-fold cluster are always of order ε^{1/k}; and the universal constant is a quadratic-form index. (splitting universal epsilon one-over-k law)

**中文。** 定义：Sobolev 摄动下的重数分裂指特征面的微扰展开。猜想：k 重特征面分裂的间隙恒为 ε 的 k 分之一次幂；且普适常数为二次型指标。（分裂普适 ε 1/k 律）
