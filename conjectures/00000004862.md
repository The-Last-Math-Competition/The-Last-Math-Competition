# 00000004862

**English.** Definition: The conformal dimension is the minimal dimension of the boundary over all quasisymmetric metrics. Conjecture: The conformal dimension of a three-generator self-similar boundary is computed explicitly by the pressure exponent of the iterated system, verified and attained for Sierpinski types. (pressure formula of the self-similar conformal dimension)

**中文。** 定义：共形维数指边界在一切拟对称度量下的最小维数。猜想：三生成自相似边界的共形维数由迭代系统的压力指数显式计算,公式在谢尔宾斯基型验证且达到。（自相似共形维数压力公式）
