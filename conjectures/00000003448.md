**English.** Definition: Spline approximation: piecewise polynomial approximation. Conjecture: The approximation order of splines: the error of degree-k splines is the (k+1)-th power of h, with the optimality of the order the interpolation lemma. (spline interpolation lemma power optimal)

**中文。** 定义：样条逼近：分段多项式逼近。猜想：样条的逼近阶：k 次样条的误差为 h 的 k+1 次幂且阶的最优性为本征引理。（样条本征引理幂次最优）
