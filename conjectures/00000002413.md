**GC2-03 ★** — source: 66_Galois覆盖与基本群.md

**English.** Definition: The Neukirch–Uchida theorem asserts that number fields are uniquely determined by their absolute Galois groups. Conjecture: The analogous rigidity holds for local fields; and the exception to rigidity is the pair of real-closed fields. (local Neukirch-Uchida rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Neukirch–Uchida 定理断言数域由绝对 Galois 群唯一确定。猜想：局部域的类似刚性成立；且刚性的例外为实闭域对。（局部 Neukirch-Uchida 刚性）
