**English.** Definition: Recursive construction sets are the classical point-set constructions without empty large shapes of Horton type, with the empty-polygon spectrum. Conjecture: The empty-polygon spectrum count converts to an explicit formula of the recursion depth, with empty quadrilateral count polynomial, empty pentagon linear, and empty hexagon zero; the count is monotone in the depth, with step a constant multiple of the depth; and every 6-point subset always contains an empty pentagon, with Horton type the unique counterexample family. (depth conversion of the recursive-set empty-shape spectrum)
