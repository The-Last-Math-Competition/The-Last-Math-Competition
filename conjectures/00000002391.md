# 00000002391

**FM-11 ★** — source: 65_分形几何与测度.md

**English.** Definition: The centered density is the local box dimension of the support of a doubling measure. Conjecture: The box dimension of the support of a measure with doubling constant C is at least log 2/log C (exact); the constant of the doubling lower bound is tight.

**中文。** 定义：中心密度指倍增测度的支撑的局部盒维数。猜想：倍增常数 C 的测度的支撑的盒维数 ≥log 2/log C(精确);双线性下界的常数达到紧。
