# 00000008739

**MBL-29 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: The entanglement spectrum is the negative logarithm spectrum of reduced density matrix eigenvalues. Conjecture: The thermal-phase entanglement spectrum is always thermal type; the MBL phase is a copy of the local single-particle spectrum with the boundary entanglement spectrum of topological phases containing zero modes; and the spectral gap of random topology is stable. (entanglement spectrum thermal-localized dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：纠缠谱指约化密度矩阵本征值的负对数谱。猜想：热相纠缠谱恒为热型；MBL 相为局域单粒子谱的复制且拓扑相的边界纠缠谱含零模，随机拓扑的谱隙稳定。（纠缠谱热-局域二分）
