# 00000003552

**GF2-02 ★** — source: 104_几何泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The Mahler volume of convex bodies: the product of volume and polar volume. Conjecture: The symmetric version of Mahler's conjecture: the lower bound of the volume product is 8/π² with extremal body the cube; the asymmetric extremum the explicit product of the tetrahedron. (Mahler cube tetrahedron extremal product)

**中文。** 定义：凸体的 Mahler 体积：体积与极体体积的积。猜想：Mahler 猜想的对称版：体积积的下界为 8/π² 且极值体为立方体；非对称的极值为四面体的显式积。（Mahler 立方体四面体极值积）
