# 00000009756

**English.** Definition: Coordinates of minimal counterexamples of geometric conjectures: arithmetic height distributions of automated geometric counterexamples. Conjecture: Coordinate heights of minimal geometric counterexamples follow double-logarithmic distributions; height quantiles are linear in the logical complexity of the conjecture, and the absence of counterexamples below finite height is decidable by a decision procedure. (counterexample coordinate height distribution)

**中文。** 定义：几何猜想的最小反例坐标：自动几何反例的算术高度分布。猜想：几何猜想最小反例的坐标高度服从双对数分布；高度的分位数与猜想的逻辑复杂度呈线性关系且有限高度内无反例的验证为决定性程序。（反例坐标高度分布）
