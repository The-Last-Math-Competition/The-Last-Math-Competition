# 00000008890

**CGB-30 ★** — source: 281_组合博弈论II.md

**English.** Definition: Decomposition primality of additive games: the indecomposability of games. Conjecture: Primality is decidable by a polynomial algorithm (a polynomial law); prime factors have unique decomposition as an ordinal sequence (a unique factorization law); the decomposition length (bound on factor count) holds (a count bound law); and the bound saturates on fully decomposable instances (a saturation law). (game primality polynomial unique factorization)

**中文。** 定义：加性博弈的分解素性：游戏的不可分解。猜想：素性的（判定算法的多项式）的（多项式律）；且素因子的（唯一分解的序数列）的（唯一分解律）；分解的长度（因子的个数界）的（个数界律）；界的饱和（完全分解实例）的（饱和律）。
