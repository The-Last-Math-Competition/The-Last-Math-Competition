# 00000007946

**INT-16 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The XXZ-type chirality of the quantum Racah chain: the branch count b_{m,n} of the spectral decomposition of the braid matrix of the centralizer of the quantum group U_q(sl_2) on the representation V_m tensor V_n. Conjecture: b_{m,n} = min(m,n)+1; and the q-dimensions of the spectral projections generate the irreducible character table of the Hecke algebra (consistent with the A_1 case of Littlewood-Richardson); the eigenvalues of the braid at roots of unity q = e^{i pi/l} form a finite list of l-th roots of unity, the number of list elements being exactly min(m,n,l) - 1 plus a boundary correction of 2. (quantum Racah braid branch law)

**中文。** 定义：量子里奇链的 XXZ 型啁啾（chirality）：量子群 U_q(sl₂) 的中心化子的辫子矩阵在表示 V_m⊗V_n 的谱分解的分支数 b_{m,n}。猜想：b_{m,n}=min(m,n)+1；且谱分解的投影算子的 q-维数生成 Hecke 代数的不可约特征标表（与 Littlewood–Richardson 的 A₁ 型一致）；辫子的特征值在 q 为单位根 e^{iπ/l} 时为 l 次单位根的有限列表，列表的元素个数恰为 min(m,n,l)-1 加 2 的边界修正。
