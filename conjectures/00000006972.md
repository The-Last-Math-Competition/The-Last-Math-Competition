# 00000006972

**English.** Definition: Direct scattering is the forward map from potential to data. Conjecture: The uniform invertibility of data is given by the reflection-coefficient control window, and the radius of the window is the reciprocal of the potential norm. (reflection-coefficient control window of direct scattering)

**中文。** 定义：直接散射为势到数据的正向映射。猜想：数据的一致可逆由反射系数控制窗给出；且窗的半径为势范数倒数。（直接散射反射系数控制窗）
