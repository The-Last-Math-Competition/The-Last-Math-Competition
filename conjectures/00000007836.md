# 00000007836

**MSE-26 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The etale covering spectrum of the p-adic moduli space M_g^{p-adic} of Drinfeld upper half plane type rigid spaces: the semisimplification of representations of its fundamental group. Conjecture: The semisimple classification of l not equal to p representations of the p-adic fundamental group of M_g is given by Langlands parameters of weighted GSp(2g) automorphic forms evaluated at mirror points of the quantum cohomology; and the dimension spectrum of representations is finite for fixed g, counted by a local density formula of ideal Hecke characters. (p-adic fundamental group semisimple spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Drinfeld 上半平面型刚性空间的 p-adic 模空间 M_{g}^{p-adic} 的 Etale 覆盖谱（其基本群的表示半单化）。猜想：M_g 的 p-adic 基本群的 l ≠ p 表示的半单分类由 GSp(2g) 的带权与自守形式的 Langlands 参数在量子上同调的镜像点取值给出；且表示的维数谱在 g 固定时有限，其计数由理想 Hecke 特征的局部密度公式决定。
