# 00000009932

**English.** Definition: Partial matching transport of persistence diagrams: transport distances allowing rejection. Conjecture: The partial matching distance with rejection penalty satisfies the triangle inequality, and the critical value of the penalty parameter is the boundary of the quantile function of the diagonal measure; below the critical value the distance degenerates to full matching. (partial matching triangle repair)

**中文。** 定义：持久图的部分匹配运输：允许拒绝的运输距离。猜想：带拒绝惩罚的部分匹配距离满足三角不等式且惩罚参数的临界值为对角线测度的分位数函数的分界；临界值下方距离退化为全匹配。（部分匹配三角修复）
