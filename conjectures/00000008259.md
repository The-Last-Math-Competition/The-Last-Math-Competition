# 00000008259

**English.** Definition: The moment conditions for testing the Euler equation of intertemporal consumption: the rank hs_rank of the Hansen-Singleton moments of consumption assets. Conjecture: hs_rank (the effective rank of instruments) is the smaller of the numbers of assets and instruments (a smaller-count law); the optimal instruments for the test power of overidentification tests (construction of optimal instruments) are the conditional-moment razors (a razor instrument law); and the consistency rate of the estimated correction coefficient for consumption habits (habit formation) is sqrt T (a sqrt T consistency law). (Hansen-Singleton instrument rank razor)

**中文。** 定义：跨期消费的 Euler 方程的检验的矩条件：消费资产的 Hansen–Singleton 矩的秩 hs_rank。猜想：hs_rank（工具变量的有效秩）= 资产数与工具数的较小者（较小者律）；且矩的过度识别检验的检验势的最优工具（最优工具的构造）为条件矩的剃刀（剃刀工具律）；消费的 Habits（习惯形成）的修正系数的估计的一致性率为 √T（√T 一致律）。
