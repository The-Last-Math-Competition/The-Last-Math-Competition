# 00000004402

**GL2-12 ◆** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: A weak regular decomposition is a partition into blocks with near-uniform inter-block densities, and the number of parts is the number of blocks. Conjecture: The lower bound on the number of parts needed to achieve error epsilon is of type 1/epsilon^2, matching the upper bound of random partitioning up to a logarithmic factor, and the match is optimal. (1/epsilon^2 matching of weak regularity part counts)

**中文。** 定义：弱正则分解指分块使块间密度近均匀的分解,部分数指块数。猜想：达到误差 ε 所需部分数的下界为 1/ε² 型,与随机分块的上界匹配至对数因子,匹配最优。（弱正则部分数 1/ε² 匹配）
