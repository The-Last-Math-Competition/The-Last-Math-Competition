# 00000002699

**English.** Definition: The Zariski–Riemann space: the projective-limit geometry of the set of valuations. Conjecture: Dimension conservation of the Zariski–Riemann space: the dimension of the projective limit equals the transcendence degree of the function field, and conservation is preserved under finite morphisms; the proof path is rank vanishing on the dependency graph of valuation rings. (ZR space dimension conservation)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski–Riemann 空间：赋值集的投射极限几何。猜想：Zariski–Riemann 空间的维数守恒：投射极限的维数等于函数域的超越次数且守恒在有限 morphism 下保持；守恒的证明路径为赋值环的依赖图的秩消没。（ZR 空间维数守恒）
