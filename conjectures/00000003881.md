# 00000003881

**CA3-01 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: An F-split ring is a commutative ring where 1 lies in the Frobenius image. Conjecture: A finitely generated integral domain over F_p is F-split if and only if the structure sheaf of every exceptional divisor of a resolution generates under contraction; and F-splitting is open under small deformations within the Cohen–Macaulay class. (F-split deformation openness)

**中文。** 定义：F-分裂环指 1 属于 Frobenius 像的交换环。猜想：F_p 上有限生成整环为 F-分裂当且仅当其奇点消解的每个例外除子的例层在收缩下生成;且 F-分裂性在 Cohen–Macaulay 类内对小时变开放。（F-分裂的形变开放性）
