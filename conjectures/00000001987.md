**English.** Definition: The Gaussian field on a capacity-regular tree is the tree model of equilibrium lattice fields. Conjecture: The renormalization fixed point of the Gaussian field on capacity-regular trees is always unique; and uniqueness is guaranteed by the transitivity of tree automorphisms. (GFF tree fixed point unique)

**中文。** 定义：容度正则树上的高斯场指平衡态晶格场的树模型。猜想：高斯场的重整不动点在容度正则树上恒唯一；且唯一性由树自同构的传递性保证。（GFF 树不动点唯一）
