# 00000006434

**English.** Definition: Small eigenvalues are spectral points below the critical value. Conjecture: The explicit classification of codimension-one styles is given by invariant combinations and is complete, the weight of the combination being the volume. (invariant-volume classification of small eigenvalues)

**中文。** 定义：小特征值为临界值以下的谱点。猜想：余维一样式的显式分类由不变量组合给出且完全；且组合的权为体积。（小特征值不变量体积分类）
