# 00000008587

**English.** Definition: Adaptive (algebraic) bases of operator algebras: minimal bases making the representation matrices upper triangular. Conjecture: Adaptive bases always exist for commutative algebras (an existence law); the minimal cardinality of a basis equals the cardinality of the maximal ideal space of the algebra (a cardinality law); the dimension upper bound of minimal bases is 2 (a double dimension upper bound law); and the existence criterion for bases of noncommutative algebras is triangular nilpotence (a triangular criterion). (operator algebra adaptive basis triangularity)

**中文。** 定义：算子代数的自适应（代数）基：使表示矩阵为上三角的极小基。猜想：自适应基对交换代数恒存在（存在律）；且基的基数的极小值等于代数的极大理想空间基数（基数律）；极小基的维数上界为 2（双维上界律）；非交换代数的基的存在性判据为三角幕零性（三角判据）。
