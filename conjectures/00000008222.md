# 00000008222

**ORB-22 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The geometric multiplicity of ABV packets: the sign abv_mult of the multiplicities of geometrically constructed packets. Conjecture: abv_mult (the representation multiplicity of an ABV packet) is a signed geometric count (a geometric counting law); the harmonization of signs (the pure multiplicity after positive-negative cancellation) equals the regular multiplicity of the Langlands packet (an ABV-Langlands agreement); and the minimal example of failure of the agreement (unharmonized signs) is the first parameter of a ramified odd unitary group (a first ramified example), with mismatch exactly plus or minus 1 (a unit mismatch law). (ABV packet geometric Langlands agreement)

**中文。** 定义：分支的 ABV-packet 的几何重数：几何构造的 packet 的重数的符号 abv_mult。猜想：abv_mult（ABV packet 的表示重数）为符号化的几何计数（几何计数律）；且符号的调和（正负相消后的纯净重数）= Langlands packet 的正规重数（ABV-Langlands 一致）；一致的例外（符号不调和）的最小例子为 ramified 奇酉群的第一参数（第一 ramified 例），例的失配量为 ±1（单位失配律）。
