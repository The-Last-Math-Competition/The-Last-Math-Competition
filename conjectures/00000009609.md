# 00000009609

**English.** Definition: CMC foliations: the H-value spectrum structure of foliations of closed manifolds by constant mean curvature surfaces. Conjecture: Closed 3-manifolds of positive scalar curvature admit CMC foliations with H-value spectrum a closed interval; interval endpoints are realized by stable CMC surfaces whose index is zero and which are unique for general metrics. (CMC foliation existence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：CMC 叶状结构：闭流形被常平均曲率曲面叶化的 H 值谱结构。猜想：正数量曲率闭 3-流形存在 CMC 叶状结构且 H 值谱为闭区间；区间端点由稳定 CMC 曲面实现，端点曲面的指标为零且在一般度量下唯一。（CMC 叶状存在判据）
