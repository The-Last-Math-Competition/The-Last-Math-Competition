# 00000000288

**English.** Definition: A Whitehead complex is the simplicial complex built from a group presentation ⟨x_i | r_j⟩ by attaching 2-cells, each of whose links is an acyclic graph. Conjecture (standard form of the Whitehead asphericity conjecture): Every Whitehead complex is aspherical, i.e. its universal cover is contractible.

**中文。** 猜想：定义:Whitehead 复形指由群表现 ⟨x_i|r_j⟩ 的 2-胞腔构建的单纯复形,其每个链环为无圈图。猜想(Whitehead 担体猜想的通行表述):每个白头复形是 ASPHERIC 的,即其万有覆盖可缩。
