# 00000009600

**MSH-20 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: Minimal representatives of incompressible surfaces: Schoen proved existence; uniqueness generally fails. Conjecture: For general hyperbolic metrics the minimal representative of every incompressible isotopy class is unique and real-analytic in the metric; non-uniqueness occurs only on the symmetry loci of branched covers, the exceptional set being an analytically thin set of codimension at least 2. (incompressible unique minimality)

**中文。** 定义：不可压缩曲面的极小代表：Schoen 证明存在性；唯一性一般不成立。猜想：一般双曲度量下每个不可压缩同痕类的极小代表唯一且随度量实解析；非唯一性仅在分支覆盖的对称轨迹出现，例外集为余维 ≥2 的解析薄集。（不可压缩唯一极小）
