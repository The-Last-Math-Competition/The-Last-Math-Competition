# 00000008112

**NAG-02 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The rank of the K-group of noncommutative projective space ncP^n = qgr-A (the noncommutative projective scheme of an AS-regular A). Conjecture: rk_K(ncP^n) = n+1 (a classical synchronization law); the order of the torsion part of the K-group (finite torsion) is a factor of the product of the degrees of the defining relations of A (a torsion-degree law); and the sign table of the multiplicative structure of K_0 (ring structure) is a correction of a cyclic sign matrix of order n+1 (sign rigidity of the ring), the correction vanishing if and only if A is Picard-invariantly equivalent to a polynomial ring (a polynomial invariance criterion). (ncP K-group torsion-degree sign rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：非交换射影空间 ncP^n = qgr-A（AS-正则 A 的非交换射影方案）的 K-群的秩 rk_K(ncP^n)。猜想：rk_K(ncP^n) = n+1（经典同步律）；且 K 群的扭部分（有限扭）的阶为 A 的定义关系的次数的乘积的因子（扭-次数律）；K_0 的乘法结构（环结构）的符号表为 n+1 阶的循环符号矩阵修正（环的符号刚性），修正项为零当且仅当 A 为 Picard-不变量等价于多项式环（多项式不变判据）。
