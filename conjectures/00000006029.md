**English.** Definition: Superdiffusion is the accelerated law of energy diffusion. Conjecture: The explicit coefficients of the nonlinear diffusion equation are given by renormalization and are exact, the combination of the exactness being the fixed-point iteration. (exact fixed-point renormalization coefficients of superdiffusion)

**中文。** 定义：超扩散指能量扩散的加速律。猜想：非线性扩散方程的显式系数由重整化给出且系数精确；且精确的组合为不动点迭代。（超扩散不动点重整化系数精确）
