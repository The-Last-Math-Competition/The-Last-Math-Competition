# 00000008209

**ORB-09 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of the Kazhdan-Lusztig two-parameter (q,t) of representations of affine Hecke algebras: the index kl_single of the single-rootedness of KL polynomials. Conjecture: The minimal pair (W,x,y) where kl_single fails (single-rootedness of the leading term of KL polynomials) is a two-parameter nonsingle point of type D_4 (a D4 exception); single-rootedness holds generically (all single-rooted type parameters) on the diagonal (q,q) of (q,t) (a diagonal single-root law); and the two-parameter domain of failure is a hypersurface of codimension 1 (a hypersurface failure law) with degree at most 4 (a quartic surface bound). (KL two-parameter D4 quartic failure)

**中文。** 定义：仿射 Hecke 代数的表示的 Kazhdan–Lusztig 双参数 (q,t) 的谱：KL 多项式的单根性的指标 kl_single。猜想：kl_single（KL 多项式的首项单根性）的失效的最小对 (W, x,y) 为 D₄ 型的双参数非单点（D4 例外）；且单根性的普遍（一切单根型双参数）在 (q,t) 的对角 (q,q) 成立（对角单根律）；单根性失效的双参数域的余维数为 1 的超曲面（超曲面失效律），曲面的次数 ≤ 4（四次曲面界）。
