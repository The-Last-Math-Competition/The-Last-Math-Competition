# 00000004452

**English.** Definition: The obstruction algebra of relative symplectic cohomology is the spectral data of the curved A-infinity structure. Conjecture: On a Weinstein domain with torus boundary, the structure constants of the obstruction algebra are always given by the combinatorial counts of the dual moduli spaces. (combinatorial spectral table of obstruction algebras)
