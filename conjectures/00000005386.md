**AD4-06 ★** — source: 165_代数动力学II_高度与轨道.md

**English.** Definition: The height-difference sequence is the limit sequence of height differences of local functions along orbits. Conjecture: For trivial parameters the limit classification is an explicit two-valued set, each value realized by the escape or boundedness of critical orbits; in the escaping case the limit is identically the constant of the local height pairing; and in the bounded case the limit set is finite, with cardinality at most the critical point count. (two-valued limit classification of difference sequences)

**中文。** 定义：高度差序列：局部函数沿轨道的高度差的极限列。猜想：平凡参数下极限的分类为二值显式集且每个值由临界轨道的逃逸或有界性实现；逃逸情形的极限恒等于局部高度配对的常数；有界情形的极限集为有限且其基数不超过临界点数。（差序列二值极限分类）
