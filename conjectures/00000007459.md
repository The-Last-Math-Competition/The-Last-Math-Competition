# 00000007459

**English.** Definition: The geometry of spectral flow with the index side. Conjecture: The relation of flow and index holds, and the constant of the relation is the Fredholm compromise. (Fredholm-compromised spectral-flow index relation)

**中文。** 定义：谱流的几何：指标侧。猜想：谱流的指标：流与指标的关系且系的常数为 Fredholm 折衷。（谱流 Fredholm 折衷指标流关系常数几何侧）
