# 00000001104

**English.** Definition: Parabolic Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials P^{I}_{x,w} are defined relative to a parabolic subgroup W_I. Conjecture: For every Coxeter system and every I, the polynomial P^{I}_{x,w} has leading coefficient 1 (monicity); the only known exceptions occur when the subgroup generated by I contains affine A₁, and that exception occurs exactly once, in the (B₃, B₂) embedding.

**中文。** 定义：抛物 Kazhdan–Lusztig 多项式 P^{I}_{x,w} 相对抛物子群 W_I 定义。猜想：对一切 Coxeter 系统与一切 I,多项式 P^{I}_{x,w} 的首项系数为 1(首一性);已知仅例外出现在 I 生成的子群含仿射 A₁ 情形,且该例外恰只在 (B₃,B₂) 嵌入中出现一次。
