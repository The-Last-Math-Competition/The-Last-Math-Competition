**English.** Definition: Higher moments of Wigner characteristic polynomials: the number-theoretic-combinatorial correspondence of moments (Catalan-type path counting). Conjecture: All moment limits are explicit combinations of Gamma function ratios; the moment sequences satisfy linear recursions with recursion coefficients explicitly given by self-intersection corrections of paths, and the deviation of universal moments from the Gaussian case concentrates exactly in the leading term. (characteristic polynomial moment formula)

**中文。** 定义：Wigner 特征多项式的高阶矩：矩的数论-组合对应（Catalan 型路径计数）。猜想：一切矩极限为 Γ 函数比值的显式组合；矩序列满足线性递推且递推系数由路径的自交修正显式给出，普适矩与高斯情形的偏差恰集中在首项。（特征多项式矩公式）
