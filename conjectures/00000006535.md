**English.** Definition: The family index is the characteristic structure of the parametrized index bundle. Conjecture: The characteristic classes of the index bundle are given by the classical Chern-Weil forms, and the closure of the giving is the differential forms. (classical characteristic classes of the family index)

**中文。** 定义：族指标为参数化指标束的示性结构。猜想：指标束示性类由陈–Weil 经典形式给出；且给出的闭包为微分形式。（族指标经典示性类）
