# 00000004071

**English.** Definition: The Silnikov phenomenon is that a homoclinic loop of a saddle-focus implies a horseshoe. Conjecture: The topological entropy of the Silnikov horseshoe is always at least log 2, and once the ratio of imaginary to real parts exceeds a universal threshold the entropy lower bound increases strictly. (increasing entropy threshold of the Silnikov horseshoe)

**中文。** 定义：Silnikov 现象指鞍焦点同宿圈蕴含马蹄。猜想：Silnikov 马蹄的拓扑熵恒不小于 log2；且虚部实部比超过普适阈值后熵下界严格递增。（Silnikov 熵递增阈值）
