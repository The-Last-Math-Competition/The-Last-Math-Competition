**HY4-02 ◆** — source: 240_微分算子IV_双曲型.md

**English.** Definition: The method of characteristics solves hyperbolic equations along characteristic curves. Conjecture: The criterion of first intersection of characteristics is the negative gradient of slopes and the criterion is complete; and the intersection position is given by the Riccati equation. (Riccati characteristic-intersection criterion)

**中文。** 定义：特征线法沿特征曲线求解双曲方程。猜想：特征首次相交的判据为斜率梯度的负值且判据完全；且相交位置由 Riccati 方程给出。（特征相交 Riccati 判据）
