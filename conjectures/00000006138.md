# 00000006138

**English.** Definition: A perfect obstruction theory is the two-bundle-type obstruction data of the OT framework. Conjecture: The critical structure is indexed by singularity decompositions, the kernel of the index being the stability criterion; the minimal example of the criterion is nodal surfaces, with nodal contribution 1; and the critical locus has the expected dimension with zero deviation. (singularity-stable indexing of critical structures)

**中文。** 定义：完美障碍理论：双丛型障碍数据（OT 框架）。猜想：临界结构由奇点分解索引且索引的核为稳定判别；判别的极小例为节点曲面且节点的贡献为 1；临界轨迹的维数为期望维数且偏差为零。（临界结构奇点稳定索引）
