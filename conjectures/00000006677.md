**English.** Definition: The auction algorithm with the scaling cascade of discrete transport. Conjecture: Geometric series eliminate the error and the closed geometric ratio is constant; the geometric ratio is the scaling parameter of fixed constant, the parameter a negative power of 10; and the cascade step count is the logarithm of the digit count of the error. (geometric-elimination scaling cascade)

**中文。** 定义：拍卖算法：离散传输的缩放级联。猜想：几何级数消除误差且封闭的几何比为常数；几何比为固定常数的缩放参数且参数为 10 的负幂；级联的步数为误差位数的对数。（缩放级联几何消除结构）
