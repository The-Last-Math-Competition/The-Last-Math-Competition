# 00000000573

**English.** Definition: The θ-uniform matroid θ^{(k)} is a Kruskal–Katona type uniformization construction. Conjecture: Its characteristic polynomial is an explicit hypergeometric-type closed form with alternating coefficient signs; and the extremal case of the alternation pattern is the free matroid. (theta-uniform characteristic hypergeometric closed form)

**中文。** 定义：θ-均匀拟阵 θ^{(k)} 为 Kruskal–Katona 型均匀化构造。猜想：其特征多项式为显式超几何型闭式且系数正负交错；且交错模式的极值为自由拟阵。（θ-均匀特征超几何闭式）
