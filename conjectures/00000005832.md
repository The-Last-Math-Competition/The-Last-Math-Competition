**English.** Definition: The Cartan matrix records the composition relations of irreducible modules within a block. Conjecture: For cyclic defect-group type, the normalized trace value is the subgroup count plus one, the kernel of the normalization being the computation of decomposition numbers; the lower bound of the trace is the rank of the defect group plus 1; and the Cartan determinant is a power of the defect-group order, with exponent 1. (cyclic normalization structure of Cartan traces)

**中文。** 定义：Cartan 矩阵：块内不可约模合成关系的矩阵。猜想：循环亏群型时迹的归一值为子群数加一且归一的核为分解数的计算；迹的下界为亏群的秩加 1；Cartan 行列式为亏群阶的幂且幂指数为 1。（Cartan 迹循环归一结构）
