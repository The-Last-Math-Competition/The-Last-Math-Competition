**English.** Definition: A quantum modular form is an object with modular transformation behavior at real quadratic irrational points. Conjecture: Its coefficient sequence satisfies an explicit binomial-type recursion, the recursion table is complete for the three prototypes, and the recursion order is two. (binomial recursion table of quantum modular forms)
