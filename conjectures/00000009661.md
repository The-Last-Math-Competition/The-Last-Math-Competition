# 00000009661

**HSS-21 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The eigenvalue group correspondence of symmetric power L-function families: Katz-Sarnak theory connects families with universal classes of classical compact groups. Conjecture: For symmetric power families sym^{2k} at fixed weight and growing conductor, the universal group alternates with the parity of k between SO-even and SO-odd; the alternation law is uniquely determined by the sign statistics of root numbers, and first-order density tests completely determine the group type. (symmetric power group type alternation)

**中文。** 定义：对称幂 L 函数族的特征值群对应：Katz–Sarnak 理论将族与经典紧群的普适类连接。猜想：权固定而导子趋于无穷的对称幂族 sym^{2k} 的普适群随 k 的奇偶交替为 SO 偶与 SO 奇；交替律由根数的符号统计唯一决定，一阶密度检验完全确定群型。（对称幂群型交替律）
