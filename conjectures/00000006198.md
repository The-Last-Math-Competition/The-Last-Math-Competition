**English.** Definition: Minimal-mass blow-up is the blow-up solution with exactly critical mass. Conjecture: The symmetric type gives the rate window, renormalization completes the classification, and the combination of completeness is rescaling invariance. (symmetric rescaling-invariant rate window of minimal blow-up)
