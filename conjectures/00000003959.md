# 00000003959

**G4-19 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: High girth means girth at least g. Conjecture: There exist graphs of girth g with chromatic number (1/2 - o(1)) * g/log g; every graph of girth g has chromatic number at most (1 + o(1)) * g/log g, so the factor-2 gap genuinely exists and cannot be eliminated. (factor 2 for chromatic number of high-girth graphs)

**中文。** 定义：高围长指围长至少 g。猜想：存在围长 g、色数达 (1/2 − o(1))·g/log g 的图;任何围长 g 图的色数上界为 (1 + o(1))·g/log g,故因子 2 的间隙真实存在且不可消除。（高围长色数因子 2）
