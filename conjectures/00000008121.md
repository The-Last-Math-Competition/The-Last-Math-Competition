# 00000008121

**English.** Definition: The geometric equivalence classes of twist parameters (theta_ij) of quantum polynomial rings: the dimension dim_tw(n) of the moduli quotient of twist space. Conjecture: dim_tw(n) = (n-1)(n-2)/2 (a dimension formula); the boundary of the moduli space (degenerate twists) corresponds to singularities of the quantum variety (a degeneration-singularity correspondence); and the Picard group of the point module space at a general twist is a sign correction of Z^n (a Picard sign correction law), the correction sign table given by the skew adjacency matrix (a skew sign table formula). (twist moduli dimension skew sign table)

**中文。** 定义：量子多项式环的 twist 参数（θ_ij）的几何等价类：twist 空间的模 quotient 的维数 dim_tw(n)。猜想：dim_tw(n) = (n-1)(n-2)/2（维数公式）；且模空间的边界（退化 twist）对应于量子簇的奇点（退化-奇点对应）；一般 twist 的点模空间的 Picard 群为 Z^n 的符号修正（Picard 符号修正律），修正的符号表由 skew 邻接矩阵给出（skew-符号表公式）。
