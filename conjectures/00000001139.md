# 00000001139

**English.** Definition: Under Beilinson–Bernstein localization, the minimal extension j_{!*}L(w·λ) of D-modules on the flag variety. Conjecture: j_{!*}L(w·λ) and j_!L(w·λ) are interchanged by Verdier duality: the dual of j_{!*} is the minimal extension of j_*, and the successive quotients of its Schubert-stratum filtration all have multiplicity 1; this property characterizes minimal extensions.

**中文。** 定义：Beilinson–Bernstein 局部化下旗簇上 D-模的极小扩张 j_{!*}L(w·λ)。猜想：j_{!*}L(w·λ) 与 j_!L(w·λ) 的 Verdier 对偶互换:j_{!*} 的对偶 = j_* 的极小扩张,且其在 Schubert 层 filtration 的相继商的重数全为 1;该性质刻画极小扩张。
