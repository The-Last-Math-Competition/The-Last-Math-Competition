# 00000009916

**English.** Definition: Rips sparsification: sparse approximations of simplicial complexes preserving persistent homology. Conjecture: The optimal (1+epsilon)-sparse Rips graph has Theta(n/epsilon) edges with constant factor 4; the optimality of the constant is attained by sparsity lower bounds of isometric circle families. (Rips sparsity optimal constant)

**中文。** 定义：Rips 稀疏化：单纯复形的稀疏近似保持持久同调。猜想：最优 (1+ε)-稀疏 Rips 图的边数为 Θ(n/ε) 且常数因子为 4；常数的最优性由等距圆周族的稀疏下界达到。（Rips 稀疏最优常数）
