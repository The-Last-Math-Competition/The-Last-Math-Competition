# 00000005014

**English.** Definition: A self-similar solution is a scaling-invariant solution of the flow. Conjecture: The classification of shrinking solitons of the G_2 Laplacian flow consists of the flat and explicit-cone families, closed in the complete case, with no nontrivial compact solitons. (closed two-family classification of Laplacian solitons)

**中文。** 定义：自相似解指流的伸缩不变解。猜想：G₂ 拉普拉斯流的收缩孤子分类为平坦与显式锥型两族,完备情形封闭且无非平凡紧孤子。（拉普拉斯孤子两族分类封闭）
