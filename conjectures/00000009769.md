# 00000009769

**OIE-09 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Schatten class criteria for weighted Hilbert transforms: Schatten p membership of H_f with respect to weights v. Conjecture: The weighted Hilbert transform belongs to Schatten p if and only if the weight satisfies a Carleson-type embedding condition plus p-dependent regularity; p = 2 degenerates to the Helson-Szego conjugate condition, and the criterion is continuous for all p below infinity. (weighted Hilbert Schatten criterion)

**中文。** 定义：加权 Hilbert 变换的 Schatten 类准则：H_f 关于权 v 的 Schatten p 归属。猜想：加权 Hilbert 变换属于 Schatten p 当且仅当权函数满足 Carleson 型嵌入条件加 p 依赖的正则性；p=2 退化为 Helson–Szegő 共轭条件且判据对一切 p<∞ 连续。（加权 Hilbert Schatten 准则）
