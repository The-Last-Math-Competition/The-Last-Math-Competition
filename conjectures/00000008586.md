# 00000008586

**NSP-26 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The k-th higher rank numerical range Lambda_k(T) of an operator T: the set of lambda where the compression of lambda I - T has rank at most k-1, the higher-order generalization of the classical numerical range. Conjecture: Lambda_k(T) is always convex and compact with Lambda_k(T) contained in Lambda_{k+1}(T); for finite matrices Lambda_k(T) is an intersection of convex sets with boundary generated by k-compression spectral curves; and Lambda_k(T) is nonempty generically once n is at least 2k-1, with extrema given by a polynomial envelope of eigenvalues of degree at most n-k+1. (higher rank numerical range convexity envelope)

**中文。** 定义：算子 T 的 k 高秩数值域 Λ_k(T)：使 λI-T 的压缩有秩不超过 k-1 的 λ 集，即经典数值域的高阶推广。猜想：Λ_k(T) 恒为凸紧集且 Λ_k(T) 含于 Λ_{k+1}(T)；有限矩阵情形 Λ_k(T) 为凸集的交且边界由 k 压缩谱曲线生成；Λ_k(T) 非空阈在 n ≥ 2k-1 时对一般矩阵恒成立且其最值为特征值的多项式包络、包络次数不超过 n-k+1。
