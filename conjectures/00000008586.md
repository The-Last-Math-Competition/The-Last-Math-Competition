Conjecture: Λ_k(T) is always convex compact set and Λ_k(T) contains in Λ_{k+1}(T); finite matrix Λ_k(T) is intersection of convex set and boundary by k contraction spectrum curve generates; Λ_k(T) non- in n ≥ 2k-1 when pair general matrix always becomes and its most is polynomial envelope, envelope -fold number of eigenvalue does not exceed n-k+1.
