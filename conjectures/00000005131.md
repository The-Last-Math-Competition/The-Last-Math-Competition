# 00000005131

**NL2-21 ★** — source: 156_数值线性代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The eigenvalue condition number is the modulus of the reciprocal of the inner product of left and right eigenvectors, together with the perturbation sensitivity of multiple eigenvalues. Conjecture: An eigenvalue of algebraic multiplicity m shifts under epsilon perturbation by the one-over-m power of epsilon, with the exponent decided by the maximal Jordan block size; the pseudospectral area scales as epsilon to the 2/m, with constant containing an explicit power of the condition number; and for nearly orthogonal eigenvectors the reciprocal condition number is an epsilon-type small quantity, with minimal perturbation configuration of lattice type. (power-law response of multiple eigenvalue perturbations)

**中文。** 定义：特征值条件数：左右特征向量内积倒数的模；重特征值的扰动敏感度。猜想：代数重数 m 的特征值在 ε 扰动下偏移为 ε 的 m 分之一次幂型且指数由最大若尔当块尺寸决定；伪谱面积标度为 ε 的 2/m 次幂且常数含条件数的显式幂；特征向量几乎正交时条件数倒数为 ε 型小量且最小扰动配置为格点型。（重特征值扰动幂律响应）
