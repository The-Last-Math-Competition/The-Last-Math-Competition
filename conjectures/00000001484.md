**HY-04 ★** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: A contact Anosov flow is an Anosov flow preserving a contact form. Conjecture: Every smooth contact Anosov flow, apart from locally rank-one symmetric flows, mixes exponentially; and the lower bound on the mixing rate is controlled explicitly by the maximal pressure of the Reeb field. (contact flow universal mixing gap)

**中文。** 定义：接触 Anosov 流指保持接触形式的 Anosov 流。猜想：除局部秩对称流之外一切光滑接触 Anosov 流为指数混合；且混合率的下界由 Reeb 场的最大压强显式控制。（接触流普适混合间隙）
