**HL2-14 ★** — source: 152_微分几何V_特殊和乐.md

**English.** Definition: A Cayley submanifold is a four-dimensional minimal submanifold calibrated by the Cayley calibration in a Spin(7) manifold. Conjecture: The wall-crossing constants of Cayley counts are always given explicitly by the decomposition table of the spinor representation; crossings occur only at the splitting of unions of involutive submanifolds, with binary splitting types; and the total charge is conserved across crossings with zero difference. (spinor constants of Cayley wall crossings)

**中文。** 定义：Cayley 子流形：Spin(7) 流形中 Cayley 校准的四维极小子流形。猜想：Cayley 计数的壁穿越常数恒由旋量表示的分解表显式给出；穿越仅发生在对合子流形的并分裂处且分裂类型为二值；穿越前后总荷守恒且守恒差为零。（Cayley 壁穿越旋量常数）
