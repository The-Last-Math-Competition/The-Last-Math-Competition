# 00000007778

**English.** Definition: Branching hierarchy of iterated Galois representations: the growth of the order a_n of the image of rho_f(G_Q) at tree level n. Conjecture: For nonexceptional polynomials, a_n/a_{n-1} converges to d, i.e. hierarchical exponential growth without deficit; and in the exact formula a_n = 2 d^n epsilon_n the series of deficit factors epsilon_n converges, its sum being the total deficit constant computable from the critical orbits and the prime factors of the discriminant. (deficit series of arboreal representations)

**中文。** 定义：迭代伽罗瓦表示的分支层级：ρ_f(G_Q) 在树 T 的第 n 层的像的阶 a_n 的增长。猜想：对非例外多项式，a_n/a_{n-1} 收敛到 d，即分层指数增长无亏损；且 a_n 的确切公式 a_n=2·d^n·ε_n 中亏损因子 ε_n 的级数 Σ ε_n 收敛，其和为表示的总体亏损常数，可由临界轨道与判别式素因子的组合数据完全计算。
