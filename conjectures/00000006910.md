# 00000006910

**English.** Definition: Shifted-derivative dimension spectra and rigidity are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two polynomials with the same dimension spectrum but different rigidity, and the separation is realized by an explicit monomial family with the same spectrum but different support. (same spectrum-different rigidity support separation)
