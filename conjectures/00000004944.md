**English.** Definition: The telescope conjecture is the equivalence of periodic with height localizations, failing from height two on. Conjecture: The spectral structure of the failure evidence is explicit: the splitting length of the failing layer is at least two, and the lower bound grows linearly with the height. (lower bound of telescope failure splitting length)
