**MF-14 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: Jacobi differential operators: exponent derivatives raising weight and lowering index. Conjecture: The representation theory of the differential-operator ring: all weight-index shift operators are generated by two basic operators, and the algebra of generating relations is a central extension of the Heisenberg algebra; the center of the extension is exactly the index operator. (Jacobi differential operator algebra)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 微分算子：升权降指标的指数导数。猜想：微分算子环的表示论：一切权-指标移位算子由两个基本算子生成且生成关系的代数为 Heisenberg 代数的中心扩张；扩张的中心恰为指标算子。（Jacobi 微分算子代数）
