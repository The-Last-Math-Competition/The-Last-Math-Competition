# 00000002359

**English.** Definition: Free Pick interpolation is matrix-valued nc interpolation. Conjecture: The free Nevanlinna–Pick constant for the parametrization of solutions is 1 (exactly the classical constant on the free ball); the parametrization is the complete parametrization by the free Schur class.

**中文。** 定义：自由 Pick 插值指矩阵值的 nc 插值。猜想：解的参数化的自由 Nevanlinna–Pick 常数为 1(即在自由球上恰为经典常数;参数化为自由 Schur 类的完全参数)。
