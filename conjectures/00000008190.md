# 00000008190

**CNT-20 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: The local-global computation of representation numbers of quadratic forms: the computational complexity rep_loc(n,m) of local obstructions for the representation number r_q(m) of a given quadratic form q. Conjecture: rep_loc is polynomial (a polynomial representation law); the error term of the globalization of local obstructions (verification of the Minkowski-Siegel formula) is m^{n/4-1+epsilon} (a cusp-exponent law); and the generating set of moduli of asymptotic singularities of representation numbers (exceptional moduli of representations) is the determinant of the exceptional sublattice of q (an exception-determinant law). (representation local-global cusp determinant)

**中文。** 定义：二次型的表示数的局部-整体的计算：给定二次型 q 的表示数 r_q(m) 的局部分歧的计算复杂度 rep_loc(n,m)。猜想：rep_loc 为多项式（表示多项式律）；且局部分歧的全局化（Minkowski–Siegel 公式的验证）的误差项（光斗的空间项）为 m^{n/4-1+ε}（光斗指数律）；表示数的渐近的奇点（表示的例外模）的模数的生成集合为 q 的例外子格的 determinant（例外-行列式律）。
