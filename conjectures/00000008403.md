**MIB-23 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The Fourier transform of the motivic measure: the motivic Fourier dual of the arc space measure with respect to the additive structure. Conjecture: The Fourier transform preserves integrability at the motivic level and is an involution (its square is reflection); the fixed motivic measures of the transform are Gauss-type motives; and the support of the Fourier image of non-fixed measures is the support of the motivic measure of the dual singularity. (motivic Fourier involution)

**中文。** 定义：母题测度的 Fourier 变换指弧空间测度对加法结构的母题化 Fourier 对偶。猜想：Fourier 变换在母题层保持可积性且为对合（平方为反射）；变换的不动母题测度为 Gauss 型母题；非不动测度的 Fourier 像的支撑为对偶奇点的母题测度的支撑。（母题 Fourier 对合）
