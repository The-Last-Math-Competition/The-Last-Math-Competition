**ISO-26 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: The free fermion point: models degenerating at critical parameters to free-fermion solvable points. Conjecture: The hole generating function is a Fredholm determinant of the Airy kernel, with the kernel of the determinant the orthogonal polynomial projection; the asymptotics of generation is GUE type with exponent 1/6; and the correlation length at the critical point diverges logarithmically. (free fermion Airy determinant structure)

**中文。** 定义：自由 fermion 点：临界参数处退化为自由费米子可解点的模型。猜想：空穴生成函数为 Airy 核的 Fredholm 行列式且行列式的核为正交多项式投影；生成的渐近为 GUE 型且型的指数为 1/6；临界点的相关长度发散为对数型。（自由 fermion Airy 行列式结构）
