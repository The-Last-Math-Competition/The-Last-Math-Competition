# 00000002635

**English.** Definition: Mock Jacobi forms: non-holomorphic completions with shadows, as z-elliptic functions. Conjecture: The shadow of a mock Jacobi form is a pairing of a modular form with a Jacobi form; the shadow map on the space of all integral-weight mock Jacobi forms is surjective, and the dimension of the fibers is the classical Jacobi space dimension plus one. (mock Jacobi shadow fibers)

**中文。** 定义：mock Jacobi 形式：非全纯完成带阴影的 z-椭圆函数。猜想：mock Jacobi 的阴影为模形式与 Jacobi 形式的配对；一切整权 mock Jacobi 空间的阴影映射为满射且纤维的维数为经典 Jacobi 空间维数加一。（mock Jacobi 阴影纤维）
