# 00000001733

**DG-13 ★** — source: 43_丢番图几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The Manin count of the diagonal cubic surface X: x³+y³+z³+w³ = 0 is the asymptotic of rational points by height. Conjecture: The main-term constant C_X = α(X)·β(X)·τ(X) converges; the relation of C_X with CM-type L-values is explicit: the β(X) factor comes from the logarithmic divergence of the cubic-character L(1,χ).

**中文。** 定义：对角三次曲面 X:x³+y³+z³+w³=0 的 Manin 计数指有理点(按高)的渐近。猜想：主项常数 C_X = α(X)·β(X)·τ(X) 的收敛值存在;C_X 与 CM 型 L 值的关联显式:β(X) 因子由三次特征 L(1,χ) 的对数发散给出。
