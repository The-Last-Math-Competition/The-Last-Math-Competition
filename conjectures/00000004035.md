# 00000004035

**English.** Definition: The knitting algorithm is the procedure that recursively constructs the AR-quiver from the Cartan matrix and known components. Conjecture: In the preprojective component each knitting step produces at most as many indecomposable direct summands as the quiver has vertices, and the bound is attained optimally by the three-arrow quiver. (per-step summand bound for knitting)

**中文。** 定义：knitting 算法指由 Cartan 矩阵与已知分量递归构造 AR-箭图的程序。猜想：preprojective 分量中 knitting 每步生成的不可分解直和项个数不超过箭图顶点数,界由三箭点箭图达到最优。（knitting 每步项数界）
