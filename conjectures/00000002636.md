# 00000002636

**MF-16 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: Upper bounds for Fourier coefficients of Jacobi forms: the cusp case. Conjecture: Cusp Jacobi coefficients satisfy the local bound |c(n,ℓ)| ≤ C·m^{k/2+ε} (k the weight, m the index); the constant of the bound is an explicit function of the Petersson norm, saturating at CM points. (Jacobi coefficient local bound)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式的傅里叶系数的上界：尖点情形。猜想：尖点 Jacobi 系数满足 |c(n,ℓ)| ≤ C m^{k/2+ε}（k 为权，m 为指标）型局部界；界的常数为 Petersson 范数的显式函数且在 CM 点饱和。（Jacobi 系数局部界）
