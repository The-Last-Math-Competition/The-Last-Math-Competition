# 00000004850

**HG3-10 △** — source: 147_几何群论III_双曲群.md

**English.** Definition: Boundary homeomorphism and group isomorphism are two equivalence relations. Conjecture: There exist higher-dimensional hyperbolic groups with homeomorphic boundaries but non-isomorphic groups, and the separation is realized explicitly by central extension pairs with the same boundary but different torsion. (boundary homeomorphism-group separation by extension pairs)

**中文。** 定义：边界同胚与群同构指两层等价关系。猜想：存在高维双曲群边界同胚而群不同构,分离由同边界不同挠的中心扩张对显式实现。（边界同胚-群分离扩张对）
