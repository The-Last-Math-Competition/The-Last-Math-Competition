# 00000000787

**PA-87 △** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: The p-adic period map is a functor from Galois representations to geometric objects. Conjecture: The complement of the image of the period map has measure zero; the evidence for density is the trace distribution on lattices; and the closure of the image is a simplicial decomposition of the spectrum. (period image dense)

**中文。** 定义：p 进周期映射为伽罗瓦表示到几何对象的函子。猜想：期间映射的像的补集测度为零；且稠密性的证据为格的迹分布。；且像闭包为谱的单形分解。（周期像稠密）
