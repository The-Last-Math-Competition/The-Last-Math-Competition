# 00000001421

**English.** Definition: Cutoff means that for a family of random walks the ratio of total mixing time to mixing window tends to infinity. Conjecture: Lazy random walks on abelian groups exhibit cutoff if and only if the second-order behavior of the conditional spectral gap over irreducible characters of the generating support satisfies an explicit criterion: the ratio of the multiplicity of the minimal nontrivial eigenvalue to the spectral gap tends to infinity; the criterion is completely characterized by Diaconis–Saloff-Coste type second-eigenvalue comparison.

**中文。** 定义：cutoff 指随机游走族的总混合时间与混合窗之比趋于无穷。猜想：交换群上惰性随机游走出现 cutoff 当且仅当其生成支撑的不可约特征条件谱隙的二阶行为满足显式判据:最小非平凡特征值的重数与谱隙之比趋于无穷;判据由 Diaconis–Saloff-Coste 型第二特征值比较完全刻画。
