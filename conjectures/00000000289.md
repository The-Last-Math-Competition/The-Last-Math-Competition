**English.** Definition: Andrews–Curtis moves are Nielsen transformations and conjugations on a balanced presentation ⟨x_i | r_j⟩. Conjecture (Andrews–Curtis): Every balanced presentation of the trivial group can be transformed by AC-moves into ⟨x_i | x_i⟩.

**中文。** 猜想：定义:Andrews–Curtis 移动:平衡表现 ⟨x_i | r_j⟩ 上的 Nielsen 变换与共轭。猜想(Andrews–Curtis):平凡群的每个平衡表现可经 AC-移动化为 ⟨x_i | x_i⟩。
