**English.** Definition: Subgraph expectation is the mean structure of subgraph copy counts in random graphs. Conjecture: The correction term of the critical window is explicit, the combination of the correction being Poissonization; the Poisson correction is the leading order of the in-window mean expansion; and the variance of the correction is O(1) in the window, decaying as the square of the window width outside. (critical Poisson correction of subgraph expectations)

**中文。** 定义：子图期望：随机图中子图拷贝数的均值结构。猜想：临界窗的修正项显式且修正的组合为泊松化；泊松修正为窗内均值展开的首阶；修正项的方差在窗内为 O(1) 且窗外的方差按窗宽平方衰减。（子图期望临界泊松修正）
