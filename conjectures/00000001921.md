# 00000001921

**English.** Definition: The inverse theorem for Gowers norms states that large norms imply structure. Conjecture: The quantitative bound of the inversion can always be taken of single-exponential polynomial type; and the bound is attained on the pure Nikodym function family of the Boolean cube. (inversion single-exponential quantitative bound)

**中文。** 定义：Gowers 范数的反演定理指大范数蕴含结构。猜想：反演的定量界恒可取单指数型多项式；且界在布尔立方体的纯尼科德姆函数族上达到。（反演单指数定量界）
