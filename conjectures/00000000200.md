# 00000000200

**CS-34 ★** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: S = Σ_{n≥1} sin(n²)/n (a convergent series of Weyl sums). Conjecture: S is transcendental with μ(S) ≤ 7/2; the transcendence follows from a joint construction combining the equidistribution of n² mod 2π with n!-denominator approximations, and the controlling term of the measure is the square-root cancellation of Gauss sums. (sin(n^2) series transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：S=Σ_{n≥1} sin(n²)/n（Weyl 和的收敛级数）。猜想：S 超越且 μ(S) ≤ 7/2；超越性由 n² mod 2π 的等分布与 n! 分母逼近的联合构造给出，度量的控制项为 Gauss 和的平方根相消。(sin(n^2) 级数超越)。
