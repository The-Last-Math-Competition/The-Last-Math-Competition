# 00000000752

**English.** Conjecture: The entropy of a one-sided shift of finite type on Z_p is log λ, where λ is the real number maximizing the p-adic norm among the roots of the characteristic polynomial (a p-adic Perron–Frobenius theorem).

**中文。** 猜想：ℤ_p 上的单侧平移子移位(SFT)的熵为 log λ(λ 为特征多项式根的 p 进范数最大的实数)(p 进 Perron–Frobenius)。
