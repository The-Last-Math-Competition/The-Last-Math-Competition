**English.** Definition: graph model of Erdős–Rényi random graph G(n,p): edge independent generates. Conjecture: threshold of k- kernel of critical window: G(n,p) of k- kernel is c_k/n and c_k is solution; critical window of explicit Poisson fixed point equation is n^{−1/3} and kernel size distribution of window in converges to process limit. (k- kernel critical window width)

**中文。** 定义：Erdős–Rényi 随机图 G(n,p)：边独立生成的图模型。猜想：k-核出现的临界窗：G(n,p) 的 k-核的涌现阈值为 c_k/n 且 c_k 为显式泊松不动点方程的解；临界窗宽为 n^{−1/3} 且窗内的核尺寸分布收敛到汲空过程极限。（k-核临界窗宽度）
