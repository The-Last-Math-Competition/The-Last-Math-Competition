**English.** Definition: The spectral gap is the second eigenvalue difference of the transition matrix. Conjecture: The spectral gap of the pointwise exponential graph of a surface is an explicit constant multiple of the reciprocal of the volume, controlled by the length of the shortest nonzero homology cycle, and attained by triangulation sequences. (homology-cycle constant bound of exponential-graph spectral gaps)
