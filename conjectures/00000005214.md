**English.** Definition: The fundamental group is the scaling group of the index. Conjecture: The family of examples with nontrivial fundamental group is given by fixed-point constructions of group actions, the first example of index four is a quaternion-type action, and the family is infinite. (index-four quaternion-action fundamental group families)
