# 00000002741

**English.** Definition: Polynomial identities (PI): nonzero polynomials valid on all matrix algebras M_n(K). Conjecture: The minimal degree of an algebraic identity of M_n is 2n (the standard identity s_{2n}), and the moduli space of identities of degree exactly 2n is a finite-dimensional variety; the number of irreducible components is the partition number of n. (standard identity minimal degree)

**中文。** 定义：多项式恒等式（PI）：对一切矩阵代数 M_n(K) 成立的非零多项式。猜想：M_n 的 PI 理论代数恒等式的极小次数为 2n（标准恒等式 s_{2n}）且次数恰为 2n 的恒等式的模空间为有限维簇；簇的不可约分支数为 n 的分拆数。（标准恒等式极小次数）
