# 00000000421

**English.** Definition: The growth-tree statistic a(T) is the number of outer-corner cells that can be added to the tableau T. Conjecture: The expectation of a(T) on a uniformly random tableau converges to two; and the convergence rate is the inverse logarithm of the tableau size. (growth-tree expectation limit two)

**中文。** 定义：生长树统计 a(T) 为表 T 可添加外角格数。猜想：均匀随机表上的期望 a(T) 收敛到二；且收敛的速率为表的大小的逆对数。（生长树期望极限二）
