# 00000001371

**DS-11 ◆** — source: 31_描述集合论与递归论.md

**English.** Definition: Martin's conjecture asserts that the degree structure of Turing-invariant functions under cone measure is well-ordered. Conjecture: The second level of the Martin order consists exactly of the classes of iterates J^α (α < ω₁) of the jump operator J; that is, the jump operator gives a complete characterization of the second level.

**中文。** 定义：Martin 猜想断言 Turing 不变函数在锥测度下的度结构为良序。猜想：Martin 序的第二层恰由跳跃算子 J 的迭代 J^α(α<ω₁) 的类组成;即跳跃算子给出第二层的完全刻画。
