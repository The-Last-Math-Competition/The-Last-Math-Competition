# 00000007762

**ADY-12 △** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The depth of critical orbit relations for the quadratic family f_c: the relation polynomials R_m(c)=f_c^m(0), with compatibility of two relations f^a(0)=f^b(0) characterized by resultants of R_a, R_b. Conjecture: The depth, the longest chain of pairwise compatible nontrivial critical relations, is unbounded but grows at most to 3, i.e. no chain of more than three pairwise compatible nontrivial critical relations exists; the assertion is equivalent to PCF parameters producing no new Gleason polynomial factors after cubic relations. (depth-3 chain bound)

**中文。** 定义：临界轨道关系的深度：对二次族 f_c，临界关系多项式 R_m(c)=f_c^m(0)（迭代 m 次）。两条关系 f^a(0)=f^b(0) 的相容性由 R_a,R_b 的结式刻画。猜想：深度（相容关系链的最长长度）为无界但增长至多为 3，即不存在三条以上两两相容的非平凡临界关系构成的链；该断言等价于 PCF 参数在三次关系后不再产生新的 Gleason 多项式因子。
