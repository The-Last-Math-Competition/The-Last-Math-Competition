# 00000002592

**English.** Definition: A modular lattice satisfies x ≥ z ⟹ x∧(y∨z) = (x∧y)∨z. Conjecture: Rigidity of the sublattice spectra of finite modular lattices: the finite-sublattice lattice of the free modular lattice M_3 is a complete invariant separating all non-isomorphic modular lattices; the minimal nonmodular counterexample to rigidity is the pentagon lattice. (modular lattice sublattice spectrum rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：模格：满足 x≥z 蕴含 x∧(y∨z)=(x∧y)∨z 的格。猜想：有限模格的子格谱的刚性：自由模格 M_3 的有限子格格为分配格的完全不变量且该不变量分离一切非同构模格；刚性失效的最小非模反例为五边形格。（模格子格谱刚性）
