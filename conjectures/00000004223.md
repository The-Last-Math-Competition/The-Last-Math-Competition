# 00000004223

**English.** Definition: The harmonic space is the kernel subspace of the combinatorial Laplacian. Conjecture: The kernel dimension is invariant under barycentric subdivision, and under an arbitrary subdivision it increases by at most one explicit correction term determined solely by the torsion coefficients. (subdivision stability of the harmonic dimension)

**中文。** 定义：调和空间指组合拉普拉斯的核子空间。猜想：核维数在重心细分下不变,在任意细分下至多增加一个仅由挠系数决定的显式修正量。（调和维数细分稳定性）
