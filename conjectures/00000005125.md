**English.** Definition: Incomplete factorization is the triangular approximation dropping small entries. Conjecture: The drop threshold minimizing fill is an explicit negative-power expression of the spectral condition number, given by the balance of residual and sparsity, and the threshold is optimal. (negative-power optimal threshold of incomplete factorization)

**中文。** 定义：不完全分解指丢弃小元的三角近似。猜想：填充最小的丢弃阈值为谱条件数的显式负幂式,由残差与稀疏度的平衡给出且阈值最优。（不完全分解负幂最优阈值）
