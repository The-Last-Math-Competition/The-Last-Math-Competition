**English.** Definition: The Buchmann algorithm is the subexponential method for computing class groups. Conjecture: Computing the class group of a general number field has an L(1/2)-type subexponential lower bound, the only obstacle in the proof is the randomness hypothesis for the relation lattice, and the obstacle can be bypassed by explicit lattice families. (L(1/2) lower bound for class group computation)

**中文。** 定义：Buchmann 算法指子指数的类群计算方法。猜想：一般数域类群计算存在 L(1/2) 型亚指数下界,证明的唯一障碍为关系格随机性假设,且障碍可由显式格族绕开。(类群计算 L(1/2) 下界)。
