**English.** Definition: The hard-core distribution is the weighting of independent sets under activity lambda. Conjecture: For Delta >= 6, the phase transition for polynomial-time approximate counting and sampling occurs at an explicit correction of lambda_c(Delta) = (Delta-1)^{Delta-1}/(Delta-2)^Delta, and the correction factor tends to 1 as Delta grows. (hard-core phase transition correction)

**中文。** 定义：硬核分布指独立集在活度 λ 下的均匀化。猜想：对 Δ ≥ 6,多项式近似计数与采样的相变发生在 λ_c(Δ) = (Δ−1)^{Δ−1}/(Δ−2)^{Δ} 的显式修正处,修正因子随 Δ 增大趋于 1。（硬核相变点修正）
