# 00000008654

**English.** Definition: Decomposition complexity of groups: the Farre-Mosher minimal index dc(G) of the decomposition game of a group. Conjecture: The boundedness of dc: the class of groups of finite decomposition complexity FDC (including hyperbolic, nilpotent, and linear groups) is closed under standard constructions (a closure law); FDC implies the vanishing of higher Novikov cohomology (a Novikov vanishing law); the exceptions to vanishing (non-FDC counterexamples) are constructions with unbounded FDC (an unbounded example law); and the boundary between bounded and unbounded is given by wreath products (a wreath boundary law). (decomposition complexity Novikov wreath boundary)

**中文。** 定义：群的分解复杂度（decomposition complexity）：群的分解游戏的 Farber 极小指标 dc(G)。猜想：dc 的有界性：有限分解复杂度 FDC 的群的类的扩展（含双曲、幂零、线性群）的闭包（闭包律）；且 FDC 的 Novikov 高阶同调的消没（Novikov 消没律）；消没的例外（非 FDC 的反例）的例为无界 FDC 的构造（无界例律）；有界与无界的边界（边界群）的例为 wreath 积的（wreath 边界律）。
