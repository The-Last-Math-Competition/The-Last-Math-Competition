**MSE-10 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The theta-null divisor Theta_0 on the spin moduli space S_g^- is the common zero locus of the odd theta constants. Conjecture: The codimension spectrum of the stratified singular locus of Theta_0 is {1,2,3} union {at least 5}, so codimension 4 is missing; and the fundamental group of the normalization of Theta_0 is the quotient of the theta group inside the level-2 subgroup of Sp(2g,Z), with abelianization of rank 3^{g-1} - 2^g + g. (theta-null singularity stratification)

**中文。** 定义：spin 模空间 S_g^− 的 θ--null 除子 Θ_0 为奇 θ 常数的公共零点。猜想：Θ_0 的奇点的余维数谱（分层奇点集的余维）为 {1,2,3}∪{≥5}（余维 4 缺失）；且 Θ_0 的正规化的基本群为 Sp(2g,Z) 的水平 2 子群中 θ 群的商，其 Abel 化的秩为 3^{g-1}-2^g+g。
