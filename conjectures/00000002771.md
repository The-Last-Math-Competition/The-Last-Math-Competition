**English.** Definition: The asymptotic game: a two-player game generating block sequences to construct asymptotic properties. Conjecture: Determinacy of asymptotic games: the dimension threshold of winning strategies determines the asymptotic type set, the threshold being an explicit inverse function of the modulus of uniform convexity of the space. (asymptotic game strategy threshold)

**中文。** 定义：渐近博弈（asymptotic game）：两人博弈生成块序列以构造渐近性质。猜想：渐近博弈的确定性：博弈的获胜策略的维数阈值确定渐近型集且阈值为空间的一致凸性模的显式逆函数。（渐近博弈策略阈值）
