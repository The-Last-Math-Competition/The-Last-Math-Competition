# 00000007280

**English.** Definition: Macdonald polynomials with the two-parameter extension. Conjecture: The symmetric-function positivity of coefficients holds, and the combination of the positivity is the combinatorics of diagonal partitions. (diagonal-partition Macdonald positivity)

**中文。** 定义：Macdonald 多项式：双参数推广。猜想：Macdonald 正性：系数的对称函数正性且性的组合为双分拆的组合。（Macdonald 双分拆对称函数系数正性组合）
