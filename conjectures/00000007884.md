# 00000007884

**HCA-14 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The compatibility of the Gray tensor product of infinity-categories with presentability: the presentability of the 2-functor category Fun^L(C,D) under the Cartesian closed structure of (∞,2)-categories. Conjecture: For all presentable (∞,1)-categories C and D, Fun^L(C,D) is presentable in the (∞,2)-Gray structure; and its representing object (the dual generated by the Yoneda embedding) is the adjointness machine of bifunctors, the exchange obstruction of the adjunction being controlled at the 3-cell layer of lax-natural transformations by an explicit 2-groupoid whose connected components form the automorphism group Z/2 of the adjoint triple cyclic law. (Gray tensor presentable adjunction)

**中文。** 定义：∞-范畴的 Gray 张量与 presentability 的相容：(∞,2)-范畴的 Cartesian 闭结构下，2-函子范畴 Fun^L(C,D) 的可 present 性。猜想：对一切可 present (∞,1)-范畴 C,D，Fun^L(C,D) 在 (∞,2)-Gray 结构下可 present；且其表示对象（按 Yoneda 嵌入生成的对偶）为双函子的伴随机，伴随的交换障碍在 lax-natural 变换的 3-胞腔层由一个显式 2-群胚控制，其连通分量为伴随的三元循环律的自同构群 Z/2。
