**English.** Definition: The variance-energy constant and the log-Sobolev constant are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two measures with the same former but different latter, and the separation is realized by an explicit parameter family of double-well mixtures. (same variance constant-different log constant double-well separation)

**中文。** 定义：方差能量常数与对数常数指两层。猜想：存在两测度的前者相同而后者不同,分离由双井混合的显式参数族实现。（方差常数同-对数常数异双井分离）
