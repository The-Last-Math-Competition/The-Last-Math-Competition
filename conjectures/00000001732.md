# 00000001732

**English.** Definition: The Peyre constant is the effective local-global constant of the main term of Manin's count. Conjecture: The Peyre constant of a degree-4 del Pezzo surface equals τ times the Brauer factor explicitly: τ is measured by the convergence domain of arc lengths, and the global Brauer correction is |Br(X)/Br₀|; the closed form can be written completely on diagonal dP4 surfaces.

**中文。** 定义：Peyre 常数指 Manin 计数主项的有效局部-整体常数。猜想：4 度 del Pezzo 的 Peyre 常数 = τ·\lim brauer 因子的显式:τ 由弧长的收敛域测定,整体 Brauer 修正值为 |Br(X)/Br_0|;闭式可在对角 dP4 上完整写出。
