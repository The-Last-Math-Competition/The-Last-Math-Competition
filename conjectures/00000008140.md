# 00000008140

**NAG-30 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Oka principle for noncommutative cohomological dimension of noncommutative varieties: the topological-algebraic equivalence oka(A) of nc cohomology. Conjecture: The Oka principle (cohomological equivalence of algebraic and topological) holds for coherent sheaves on nc Stein varieties (affine nc) (an nc Oka-Grauert theorem); the minimal example of failure of the equivalence (algebraic vector bundles differing from topological) is the missing algebraic realization of a nontrivial Azumaya topological class on nc C^n (an Azumaya missing example); and the completion of the missing classes (full realization of algebraic Azumaya classes of topological classes) at dimension at least 3 is given by theorem B of the noncommutative Cartan (a Cartan completion law). (nc Oka Azumaya Cartan completion)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的非交换上同调维数的 Oka 原理：nc 上同调的拓扑-代数等价的 oka(A)。猜想：oka 原理（代数=拓扑的上同调等价）在 nc Stein 簇（affine nc）的凝聚层成立（nc Oka-Grauert）；且等价的失败（代数向量丛 ≠ 拓扑）的最小例子为 nc C^n 的非平凡 Azumaya 拓扑类的代数实现缺失（Azumaya 缺失例）；缺失的补全（代数 Azumaya 的拓扑类的全实现）在维数 ≥3 由非交换 Cartan 的 B 组定理补全（Cartan 补全律）。
