# 00000008497

**ERO-27 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: The ergodic uncertainty principle: the lower bound on the sum of support entropy concentrations of an observable f and its translate f composed with T. Conjecture: A universal c > 0 makes the sum of the two entropies at least c (a lower bound law); the optimal constant c is given by the ergodic dual of the Donoho-Stark inequality (a duality law); the equality-case functions are classified as modulations of characteristic functions (a modulation classification law); and the growth of the lower bound in the multi-translate case is linear (a linear growth law). (ergodic Donoho-Stark modulation duality)

**中文。** 定义：遍历不确定性原理：观测 f 与平移 f∘T 的支撑熵浓度之和的下界。猜想：存在普适 c>0 使两熵之和不小于 c（下界律）；最优常数 c 由 Donoho–Stark 不等式的遍历对偶给出（对偶律）；等号情形的函数分类为特征函数的调制（调制分类律）；多平移情形下界的增长为线性（线性增长律）。
