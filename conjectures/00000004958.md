# 00000004958

**English.** Definition: The Hurewicz homomorphism is the canonical map from homotopy to homology. Conjecture: Its kernel within twenty stems is generated explicitly by the images of the Adams differentials, the generating table is complete, and the kernel has no free part. (Adams-image generation table of the Hurewicz kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Hurewicz 同态指同伦到同调的典范映射。猜想：其核在二十以内 stem 由 Adams 微分的像显式生成,生成表完整且核无自由部分。（Hurewicz 核 Adams 像生成表）
