# 00000008059

**PRF-09 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The automatic verifiability of ordinal notations: the proof complexity v(N) of verifying well-foundedness of a notation system N (such as Buchholz psi). Conjecture: v(N) is unprovable within primitive-recursive systems (an essential upper bound on well-foundedness); the growth rate of proof lengths for verifiable fragments (well-foundedness of the initial alpha segment) follows the tower law of the maximal out-degree m of the notation tree of alpha; and the shortest formalized proof verifying the well-foundedness of psi(Omega_omega) in automatic proof assistants (such as Agda or Coq) has size in the tight range of 10^4 to 10^5 steps (a prediction law for formalization complexity: a double-exponential conversion of the Veblen hierarchy of |T|). (Buchholz well-foundedness formalization prediction)

**中文。** 定义：序数记号的自动可验证性：给定记号系统 N（如 Buchholz ψ）的良序性验证的证明复杂度 v(N)。猜想：v(N) 在原始递归系统内不可证明（良序性的本质上界）；且可验证的片段（前 α 段的良序性）的证明长度的增长率与 α 的记号树的最大出度 m 的塔律一致；自动证明助手（如 AGDA/Coq）验证 ψ(Ω_ω) 良序的最短形式化证明的规模为 10⁴-10⁵ 步的紧区间（形式化复杂度的预测律：|T| 的 Veblen 层级的双指数换算）。
