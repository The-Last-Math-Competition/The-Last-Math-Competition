**English.** Definition: The orbit-closure problem asks for inclusions of orbit closures of two groups. Conjecture: The inclusion criterion for orbit closures of homogeneous spaces is the necessary and sufficient termwise nonnegativity of quantization coefficients; and the criterion is finitely verifiable in the two-center variety case. (quantization-nonnegativity orbit-closure criterion)

**中文。** 定义：轨道闭包问题问两群轨道闭包的包含关系。猜想：齐性空间轨道闭包的包含判据为量子化系数的逐项非负充要条件；且判据在双中心簇情形可有限验证。（轨道闭包量子化非负判据）
