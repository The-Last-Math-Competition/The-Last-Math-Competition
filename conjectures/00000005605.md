**English.** Definition: Reduction invariants are the characteristic quantities of reduced bases. Conjecture: The three-dimensional case is always given explicitly by two quadratic-form invariants, closed within the characteristic-field closure, and the criterion is complete. (complete two-invariant criterion in three dimensions)
