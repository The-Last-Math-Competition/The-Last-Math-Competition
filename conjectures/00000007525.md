# 00000007525

**English.** Definition: The weight filtration as the stratified structure of mixed structures. Conjecture: The weight-piece spectrum satisfies piece weight at most w plus i, the stratification being preserved by the four functors; and the strictness of the preservation is weight monotonicity. (four-functor weight-piece stratification)

**中文。** 定义：权滤过为混合结构的分层结构。猜想：权片的谱满足片权不超过 w 加 i 且分层由四函子保持；且保持的严格性为权重单调。（权片四函子分层）
