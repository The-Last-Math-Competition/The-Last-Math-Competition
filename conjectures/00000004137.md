**English.** Definition: The strong reflection principle SRP is the strong reflection property of stationary sets. Conjecture: The lattice of principles equivalent to SRP is finite up to isomorphism with a unique maximal element, which is the negation-combination of the weak square principle. (unique maximality of the SRP equivalence lattice)

**中文。** 定义：强反射原理 SRP 指平稳集的强反射性质。猜想：SRP 的等价原理格在同构意义下有限且有唯一极大元,极大元为弱平方原理的否定组合。（SRP 等价格极大唯一性）
