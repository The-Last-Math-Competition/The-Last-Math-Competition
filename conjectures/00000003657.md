# 00000003657

**English.** Definition: Stable random measures denote limit measures of renormalized sums. Conjecture: The renormalized limit is always a stable law; the exponent of the law equals the tail exponent of the original measure, and the renormalizing sequence is given by stable functionals. (stable renormalization tail exponent law)

**中文。** 定义：稳定随机测度指重整和的极限测度。猜想：重整极限恒为稳定律；且律的指标等于原测度的尾指数而重整序列由稳定泛函给出。（稳定重整尾指数律）
