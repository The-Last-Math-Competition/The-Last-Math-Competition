# 00000009968

**MRG-28 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Measure rigidity of toral automorphisms: algebraic measure classification of hyperbolic toral automorphisms. Conjecture: Positive-entropy ergodic invariant measures are algebraic (Haar on Lebesgue times radical group orbits) when the Lyapunov spectrum of the measure coincides pointwise with that of the map; measures with spectral mismatch contain nonalgebraic members, all with strictly smaller entropy. (toral positive-entropy algebraic classification)

**中文。** 定义：双环面自同构的测度刚性：hyperbolic toral 自同构的代数测度分类。猜想：正熵遍历不变测度为代数测度（Lebesgue 乘根式群轨道上的 Haar）当测度的 Lyapunov 谱与映射的谱逐点重合；谱失配的测度含非代数成员且成员的熵严格小。（toral 正熵代数分类）
