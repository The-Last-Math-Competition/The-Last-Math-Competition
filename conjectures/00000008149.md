# 00000008149

**English.** Definition: The renormalization of the F-invariant of the six-vertex model: the algebraic index F_inv of the RG of row shifts of cylindrical statistical models. Conjecture: The conservation of F_inv (exact preservation of algebraic relations) holds under all cylindrical transformations (an F conservation law); the conservation error of finite-dimensional truncation at chain length L is e^{-pi L} (an exponential conservation error); and the rate of conservation breaking (breaking under nonintegrable perturbations) agrees with the Lyapunov exponent of arithmetic chaos (a conservation-chaos agreement law). (six-vertex F-invariant exponential conservation)

**中文。** 定义：六顶点模型的 F-不变量的重整化：柱状统计模型的 Row 移位的 RG 的代数指标 F_inv。猜想：F_inv 的守恒（代数关系的精确保持）在一切柱变换下成立（F 守恒律）；且 F-守恒的有限维截断（链长 L 的截断的守恒误差）为 e^{-πL}（指数守恒误差）；守恒的失效（非可积微扰的守恒破缺）的破缺率与算术混沌的 Lyapunov 指数一致（守恒-混沌一致律）。
