# 00000000594

**CA-94 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: Betti numbers of Nakajima quiver varieties and partitions. Conjecture: The Poincare polynomial of the quiver variety for the fixed partition (1^n) equals an explicit q-Hermite polynomial (programmatically verifiable).

**中文。** 定义：Nakajima quiver variety 的 Betti 数与分拆。猜想：固定分拆 (1^n) 的 Quiver variety 的 Poincaré 多项式 = q-Hermite 多项式的显式(可编程验证)。
