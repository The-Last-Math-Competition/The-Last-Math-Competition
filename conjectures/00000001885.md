**English.** Definition: The principal bundle of an o-minimally definable compact group G (Pillay's conjecture is proven: all are definable Lie groups). Conjecture: Rigidity of the o-minimal covers of the quotient space G/H: the covering group is a linear algebraic group of finite rank (dimension ≤ rank G); there are no exceptional covers; and the rank of the covering group exactly equals the definable dimension of the base group.
