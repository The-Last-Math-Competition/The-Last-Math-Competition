# 00000000681

**English.** Definition: Inflection points of plane projected curves are the curvature structure of the Möbius energy. Conjecture: The number of inflection points is linearly controlled by the genus; and the control constant is Möbius-energy minimizing. (inflection points genus-linear Möbius control)
