# 00000001888

**ML-18 ★** — source: 48_逻辑与模型论II.md

**English.** Definition: ACFA (the axiomatization of difference-closed fields, Chatzidakis–Hrushovski). Conjecture: The normal forms of quantifier elimination for ACFA have a complete table: every formula is equivalent to a finite pattern of Boolean combinations of polynomial difference equations; the table is exhausted by Gröbner bases of difference polynomials.

**中文。** 定义：ACFA(差分闭域公理化,Chatzidakis–Hrushovski)。猜想：ACFA 的量词消去的正常形有完备表:一切公式等价于多项式差分方程的布尔组合的有限模式;表由差分多项式的格朗基底穷举。
