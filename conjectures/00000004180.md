# 00000004180

**English.** Definition: The main-term asymptotic series is the order-by-order expansion of the circle method main term in the arc parameter. Conjecture: There exists an admissible family of phases for which the series alternately diverges and converges along the parameter, with the alternation appearing periodically on the logarithmic scale. (alternating-divergence family of main terms)

**中文。** 定义：主项渐近级数指圆法主项按弧参数的逐阶展开。猜想：存在容许相位族使级数沿参数交替发散与收敛,交替在对数尺度上周期出现。（主项交错发散族）
