# 00000007735

**English.** Definition: The free Cramer theorem states that free independence plus semicircular sum forces both summands semicircular; the free stable laws S_alpha are the limit family of normalized free sums. Conjecture: Decomposition uniqueness holds within the free stable family: if X boxed Y lies in S_alpha with X, Y in its domain of attraction, then X and Y are translates of S_alpha; and a free stable law is free infinitely divisible if and only if alpha lies in (0,1), while for alpha above 1 it is not. (free stable decomposition and divisibility boundary)

**中文。** 定义：自由 Cramér 定理指若 X,Y 自由独立且 X⊞Y 为半圆则 X,Y 皆半圆。其稳定律类比：自由稳定律 S_α 为正规化自由和的极限族。猜想：分解唯一性在自由稳定律族内成立：若 X⊞Y 属于 S_α 且 X,Y 都在其吸引域内，则 X,Y 各为 S_α 的平移；且自由稳定律为自由无穷可分当且仅当 α 介于 0 与 1 之间，当 α 大于 1 时自由稳定律不是自由无穷可分的。
