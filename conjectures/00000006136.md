**English.** Definition: The virtual fundamental class is the intrinsic characteristic class of the moduli space. Conjecture: For smooth covers the class is the fundamental class, the correction is given by the obstruction-bundle Euler type, and the combination of the correction is the obstruction annihilation. (obstruction-Euler correction of virtual classes)

**中文。** 定义：虚拟基本类为模空间的内在示性类。猜想：光滑覆盖时类为基本类且修正由障碍丛欧拉型给出；且修正的组合为障碍零化。（虚拟类障碍欧拉修正）
