**English.** Definition: A rigid four-dimensional Fano is a four-dimensional Fano without nontrivial structures. Conjecture: Its new series is constructed from explicit base varieties of double covers by divisors, the rigidity is decided by the polarity of the contraction cone, and the series is infinite; the rational curve counts of its members always satisfy the Fuchs relation of the quantum differential equation. (new series of rigid four-dimensional covers)
