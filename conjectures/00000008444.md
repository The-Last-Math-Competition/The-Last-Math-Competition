# 00000008444

**English.** Definition: An L-space knot is a knot for which some integer surgery yields an L-space; N(K) is the smallest such surgery coefficient. Conjecture: N(K) equals the stability number of the knot plus one; N(K) for three-strand braid knots is computed explicitly from the depth of the braid word; and for general knots, N(K) is computable in polynomial time in the braid length. (endpoint formula for L-space surgeries)

**中文。** 定义：L-space 结指某整数手术为 L-space 的结，N(K) 为该最小手术系数。猜想：N(K) 等于结的稳定数加一；三股辫结的 N(K) 由辫字深度显式计算；一般结的 N(K) 为辫长的多项式时间可算。（L-space 手术端点公式）
