# 00000009997

**NCR-27 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative unique factorization: the noncommutative version of homogeneous prime element factorization. Conjecture: The weak Euclidean criterion of noncommutative UFDs: left coefficient division is feasible if and only if common multiples of homogeneous primes are commutativizable; the criterion is verified explicitly by the Euclidean property of the reduced norm. (noncommutative UFD weak Euclidean criterion)

**中文。** 定义：非交换唯一分解：齐次素元分解的非交换版。猜想：非交换 UFD 的弱欧几里得判据：左系数除法可行当且仅当齐次素元的公倍数为可交换化；判据由约化范的 Euclid 性显式验证。（非交换 UFD 弱欧氏判据）
