**BIR-05 ★** — source: 293_双有理几何III.md

**English.** Definition: A contraction is the morphism along an extremal ray in the minimal model program. Conjecture: The classification of contractions is completely encoded by the ray type, the encoded types being the divisorial and fibering dichotomy; and the criterion for the two contraction types is whether the dimension of the contracted fiber is one. (contraction ray-type encoding divisorial-fibering dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：收缩（contraction）为极小模型纲领中沿极小射线定义的态射。猜想：收缩的分类由射线类型完全编码且编码的类型为除子型与纤维型二分；两类收缩的判据为收缩纤维维数是否为一。（收缩类型射线编码）
