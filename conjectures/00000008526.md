**SDG-26 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: The differential version of etale covers is torsion-free differential extensions. Conjecture: The dimension of a torsion-free differential extension equals the rank of the original extension; and the composition of torsion-free extensions is closed under direct products of differential Galois groups. (torsion-free differential extensions)

**中文。** 定义：平展覆叠的微分版本为无挠微分扩张。猜想：无挠微分扩张的维数等于原扩张的秩；且无挠扩张的复合在微分 Galois 群直积下封闭。（无挠微分扩张）
