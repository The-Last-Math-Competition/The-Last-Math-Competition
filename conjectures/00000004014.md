# 00000004014

**English.** Definition: The Euler approximation of an RDE is the numerical scheme that replaces the driver by grid-point secants. Conjecture: The convergence rate of the strong error is an exponent of the form 1/p - H in the mesh, where H is the Hurst parameter of the driver, and the rate saturates at the truncation of that order. (optimal RDE Euler rate)

**中文。** 定义：RDE 的 Euler 逼近指以格点割线替换驱动的数值格式。猜想：强误差的收敛速率为 mesh 的 (1/p − H) 型指数,H 为驱动 Hurst 参数,且在阶截断处饱和。（RDE Euler 最优速率）
