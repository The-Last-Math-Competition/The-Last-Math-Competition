# 00000001881

**ML-11 ◆** — source: 48_逻辑与模型论II.md

**English.** Definition: Wilkie's conjecture states that the count of algebraic points of height ≤ H on transcendently defined sets in o-minimal structures is O(H^ε). Conjecture: The count of rational points of height ≤ H on the graph of the Pfaffian curve (e^x, e^{e^x}) has a double-logarithmic bound (log H)^c rather than H^ε; the correction comes from the Diophantine approximation of the Lindemann condition.

**中文。** 定义：Wilkie 猜想指 o-极小结构中超越定义集的代数点计数 O(H^ε)。猜想：Pfaffian 曲线 (e^x,e^{e^x}) 的图像的高 ≤H 有理点计数为 (log H)^c 的双对数界(而非 H^ε);修正项由 Lindemann 条件的 Diophantine 近似给出。
