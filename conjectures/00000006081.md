**English.** Definition: The escape rate is the time law of leaving saddles. Conjecture: For spherical perturbations it is of noise-radius negative-quadratic order, and the combination of the order is the radial diffusion. (radial-diffusion noise-radius negative-quadratic escape)

**中文。** 定义：逃逸速率为离开鞍点的时间律。猜想：球形扰动时为噪声半径负二次阶；且阶的组合为径向扩散。（逃逸噪声半径负二次径向扩散）
