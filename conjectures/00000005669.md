# 00000005669

**English.** Definition: Anisotropy is the direction dependence. Conjecture: The Green anisotropy is always the explicit quadratic form of the drift vector, degenerating to isotropy at zero drift. (drift quadratic-form anisotropy)

**中文。** 定义：各向异性指方向依赖性。猜想：格林各向异性恒为漂移向量的显式二次型；零漂移时退化各向同性。（漂移二次型各向异性）
