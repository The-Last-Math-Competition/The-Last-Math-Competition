# 00000002548

**English.** Definition: The dual characterization of p-capacity and Hausdorff measure. Conjecture: The absolute continuity of p-capacity with respect to H^{n−p} is exact on the class of uniform domains: the density of the capacity is an explicit functional of the lower density of the measure, and the functional saturates at the critical measure. (capacity-measure duality density)

**中文。** 定义：p-容度与 Hausdorff 测度的对偶刻画。猜想：p-容度与 H^{n-p} 的绝对连续性在一致域类精确：容度的密度为测度的下密度的显式泛函且泛函在临界测度处饱和。（容度-测度对偶密度）
