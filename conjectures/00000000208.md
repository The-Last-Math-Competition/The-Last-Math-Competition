**SQ-08 ◆** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: The Hofstadter Q-sequence is the meta-Fibonacci recurrence defined by Q(n) = Q(n−Q(n−1)) + Q(n−Q(n−2)). Conjecture: The density of the sequence always exists and is positive; the positive density is settled through the generating-tree structure of its slow decomposition. (Q-sequence positive density via slow decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：Hofstadter Q 序列指满足 Q(n)=Q(n−Q(n−1))+Q(n−Q(n−2)) 的元递推结构。猜想：序列的密度恒存在且为正；正密度的核为慢分解的生成树结构。（Q-序列慢分解正密度存在）
