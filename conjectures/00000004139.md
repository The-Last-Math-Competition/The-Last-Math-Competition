# 00000004139

**FA2-19 ◆** — source: 123_高等数理逻辑_强制法与公理.md

**English.** Definition: The Cichon diagram is the poset diagram of the ten cardinal invariants. Conjecture: The strict inequalities between the ten invariants provable in ZFC are exactly the transitive closure of the diagram, and no ZFC strict inequality outside the diagram exists. (ZFC completeness of the Cichon diagram)

**中文。** 定义：Cichon 图指十个基数不变量的偏序图。猜想：十个不变量之外 ZFC 可证的严格不等式恰为图的传递闭包,不存在任何图外的 ZFC 严格不等式。（Cichon 图 ZFC 完备性）
