**English.** Definition: The differential operators give the linear substitution structure of differential linear logic. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit combination of Taylor coefficients, a polynomial basis under resource calculus; the degree of the basis is the substitution depth; and positivity of the coefficients is preserved on canonical resource terms. (Taylor resource-polynomial-basis conversion)
