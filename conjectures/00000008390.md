# 00000008390

**MIB-10 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The action of the Cremona group on motives: the motivic automorphisms induced by birational self-maps. Conjecture: The image of the Cremona action is a finite group on the part of weight at most 2, of order dividing 12; the rank of weight-2 invariant motives is the birational invariant h^{1,1}; and the kernel is the motivic lift of the birational isotopy identity, the smallest-dimensional example of generators of the kernel being three-dimensional. (Cremona motivic image finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：Cremona 群在母题上的作用指双有理自同构诱导的母题自同构。猜想：Cremona 作用的像在权 ≤ 2 部分为有限群，其阶整除 12；权 2 的不变母题的秩为 h^{1,1} 的双有理不变量；作用的核为双有理同痕恒等元的母题化，且核的生成元的最小维数例子为三维情形。（Cremona 母题像有限）
