# 00000007108

**English.** Definition: Infinite-dimensional first-order methods with functional optimization. Conjecture: The first-order method for functionals holds, the combination of the method being reproducing kernels. (reproducing-kernel infinite-dimensional descent)

**中文。** 定义：无穷维度的一阶：泛函优化。猜想：无穷维的下降：泛函的一阶方法且法的组合为再生核。（无穷维一阶方法泛函优化再生核泛函下降组合无穷维再生核一阶泛函核一阶泛函优化核方法）
