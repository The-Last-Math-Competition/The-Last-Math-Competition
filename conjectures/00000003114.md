**English.** Definition: Right-angled Artin groups (RAAG): groups defined by graphs. Conjecture: The boundary rigidity of RAAGs: the homotopy type of the visual boundary of the group is the suspension of the flag complex, with the rigidity criterion the connectivity of the complement graph. (RAAG boundary flag suspension rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：右角 Artin 群（RAAG）：由图定义的群。猜想：RAAG 的边界刚性：群的视觉边界的同伦型为旗复形的 suspension 且刚性的判据为图的补图的连通性。（RAAG 边界旗悬浮刚性）
