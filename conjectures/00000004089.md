# 00000004089

**English.** Definition: Rigidity of bifurcation diagrams is the equivalence of two bifurcation families under diffeomorphisms of parameter and phase space. Conjecture: The moduli space of equivalence classes of bifurcations of codimension at most 2 has dimension exactly equal to the codimension, and from codimension 3 on it is strictly larger than the codimension. (dimension law of bifurcation moduli spaces)

**中文。** 定义：分叉图的刚性指两分叉族经参数与相空间微分同胚的等价。猜想：余维不超过 2 的分叉等价类模空间维数恰等于余维数,余维 3 起严格大于余维数。（分叉模空间维数律）
