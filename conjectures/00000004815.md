# 00000004815

**English.** Definition: The Selberg class is the class of series satisfying the function-equation axioms, with degree the order of the functional equation. Conjecture: There are no elements with degree between one and two, and all elements of degree below five thirds are twists of the zeta function, so the classification is complete. (Selberg degree gap and low-degree classification)

**中文。** 定义：Selberg 类指满足函数方程等公理的级数类,度指其函数方程阶。猜想：度在一与二之间无元素,度小于三分之五者全为 zeta 的扭转,分类完全。（Selberg 度间隙与低度分类）
