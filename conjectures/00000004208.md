# 00000004208

**English.** Definition: The discretization of min-max is the width approximation on finite element spaces. Conjecture: The optimal exponent of the convergence order is the 1/2 power of the width, attained by piecewise constant elements, and higher-order elements cannot raise this order. (optimal finite element order of min-max)

**中文。** 定义：极小极大的离散化指有限元空间上的宽度逼近。猜想：收敛阶的最优指数为宽度的 1/2 幂,由分段常元达到,高阶元不能提升该阶。（极小极大有限元最优阶）
