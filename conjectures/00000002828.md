**LD-28 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: Numerical verification of LDP: statistical consistency of rate estimation. Conjecture: The consistency window of rate estimation: the empirical rate estimator is consistent within window width n^{-1/5}, and the optimal window is an adaptive function of the deviation scale. (rate estimation consistency window)

**中文。** 定义：LDP 的数值验证：速率估计的统计相合性。猜想：速率估计的相合窗：经验速率估计在窗宽 n 的负 1/5 次幂内相合且相合的最优窗为偏差尺度的自适应函数。（速率估计相合窗）
