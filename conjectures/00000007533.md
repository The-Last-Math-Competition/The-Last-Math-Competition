# 00000007533

**ET3-23 ◆** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: Local constancy as the geometric realization of representations. Conjecture: The trivialization window on geometrically simply connected domains exists with window ends given explicitly by the rigidity criterion; and the exception outside the window is the ramification locus. (trivialization-window local constancy)

**中文。** 定义：局部常值性为表示的几何实现。猜想：几何单连通域上的平凡化窗口存在且窗端由刚性判据显式给出；且窗外的例外为分歧轨迹。（局部常值平凡化窗）
