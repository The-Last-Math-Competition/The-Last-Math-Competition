# 00000009973

**English.** Definition: The Specht problem: whether polynomial identity ideals of PI algebras are finitely based (affirmative in characteristic zero by Kemer). Conjecture: The criterion for finite generation of T-ideals of PI algebras over positive characteristic fields is a finite verification table of standard identities of symbolic calculus systems; the table length is polynomial in the characteristic, and Kemer's characteristic-zero correspondence transfers through corrected functors. (positive characteristic Specht finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：Specht 问题：PI 代数的多项式恒等式理想是否有限基（特征零已由 Kemer 肯定）。猜想：正特征域上 PI 代数的 T-理想为有限生成的判据为符号演算系统的标准恒等式的有限验证表；表的长度为特征的多项式且特征零的 Kemer 对应经修正函子迁移。（正特征 Specht 有限性）
