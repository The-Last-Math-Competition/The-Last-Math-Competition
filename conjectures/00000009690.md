# 00000009690

**English.** Definition: Baker's logarithmic linear forms: lower bounds |Lambda| at least B^{-kappa} for Q-linear combinations of logarithms of algebraic numbers. Conjecture: The optimal exponent is kappa = 1: lower bounds for logarithmic linear forms can be improved to (log B)^{-1-epsilon} for every epsilon > 0, and the exponent 1 cannot be broken; breaking is excluded by explicit constructions of near-degenerate linear forms. (Baker optimal exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Baker 对数线性型：代数数对数的 Q 线性组合的下界 |Λ|≥B^{−κ}。猜想：最优指数为 κ=1：对数线性型下界可改进为 (log B)^{−1−ε} 对任意 ε>0 且指数 1 不可突破；突破将由近退化线性型的显式构造排除。（Baker 最优指数）
