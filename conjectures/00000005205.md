# 00000005205

**English.** Definition: Minimization is the realizing construction of the infimum of dimensions. Conjecture: The dimension-minimizing construction of free algebras is given by an explicit embedding sequence of matrix units, with dimension value one and not further reducible. (minimal embedding dimension one of free algebras)

**中文。** 定义：极小化指维数下确界的实现构造。猜想：自由代数的维数极小构造由矩阵单位的显式嵌入序列给出,维数值为一且不可再降。（自由代数嵌入极小维数一）
