**GBR-06 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Counting complete exceptional sequences: Chapoton's formula for the number of exceptional sequences of Dynkin type. Conjecture: The counting generating function of complete exceptional sequences of non-Dynkin quivers is rational; rationality follows from the self-similar structure of AR translations of preprojective components, and pole moduli are integer multiples of the highest root coefficient. (exceptional sequence rational generating function)

**中文。** 定义：完备例外序列计数：Dynkin 型的例外序列个数的 Chapoton 公式。猜想：非 Dynkin 箭图的完备例外序列的计数生成函数为有理函数；有理性由预投射分量的 AR 平移的自相似结构给出且极点的模为最大根系数的整数倍。（例外序列有理生成函数）
