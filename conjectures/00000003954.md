# 00000003954

**G4-14 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: A vertex-minor orbit is an orbit class of vertices closed in the vertex-minor sense. Conjecture: The graph classes with finitely many orbits are completely characterized by the minimal forbidden orbit list, and the list consists of exactly three members: the cycle orbit, the bullseye orbit, and the bipartite-clique substitution orbit. (minimal forbidden list for vertex-minor orbits)

**中文。** 定义：vertex-minor 轨道指点子式意义下封闭的顶点轨道类。猜想：有限轨道的图类由最小禁用轨道表完全刻画,表恰由圈轨道、牛眼轨道与二部团替换轨道三者构成。（vertex-minor 最小禁用表）
