# 00000006155

**English.** Definition: Infinitesimal deformations are the formal structure of point neighborhoods. Conjecture: For good moduli spaces the completion is faithful with error given by truncation, the kernel of the error being the higher deformation sheaf. (higher truncation error of formal completeness)

**中文。** 定义：无穷小形变指点邻域的形式结构。猜想：好模情形完备忠实且误差由截断给出；误差的核恒为高阶形变层。（形式完备截断高阶误差）
