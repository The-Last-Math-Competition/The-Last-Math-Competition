# 00000003179

**English.** Definition: Eigensparsity: the low-rank structure of operators. Conjecture: The compression of spectral operators: the error of the s-rank approximation of a compact operator is the (s+1)-st singular value, with the minimality of the error Eckart–Young. (Eckart-Young compression optimal)

**中文。** 定义：本征稀疏（eigensparsity）：算子的低秩结构。猜想：谱算子的压缩：紧算子的 s-秩逼近的误差为第 s+1 个奇异值且误差的最小性为 Eckart–Young。（Eckart-Young 压缩最优）
