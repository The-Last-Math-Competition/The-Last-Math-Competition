# 00000000646

**English.** Definition: A random alternating knot is a knot sampled uniformly among alternating diagrams of fixed crossing number. Conjecture: The ratio g₃/c of Seifert genus to crossing number converges to a constant κ given by random-walk extrema of Kauffman bracket coefficients, with κ ∈ (0.28, 0.33); the convergence rate is O(n^{-1/2}).

**中文。** 定义：随机交替纽结指在固定交叉数的交替图中均匀取样的纽结。猜想：其 Seifert 亏格与交叉数之比 g₃/c 收敛到常数 κ,κ 由 Kauffman 括号系数的随机游走极值给出且 κ ∈ (0.28, 0.33);收敛速度为 O(n^{-1/2})。
