# 00000000947

**OA-47 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The modulus of uniform convexity δ_X(ε) measures the uniform convexity of the unit ball. Conjecture: For fixed dimension, the optimal lower envelope of δ(ε) is given by the family ℓ_q^n, with q selected explicitly from ε and n; q tends to two for small ε and to infinity for large ε; and the slopes of the envelope curve at both endpoints are explicit rationals. (uniform convexity modulus optimal envelope)

**中文。** 定义：均匀凸性模 δ_X(ε) 度量单位球的一致凸程度。猜想：固定维数下 δ(ε) 的最优下包络由 ℓ_q^n 的族给出且 q 由 ε 与 n 显式选定；小 ε 时 q 趋于二且大 ε 时 q 趋于无穷；且包络曲线在两端点的斜率为显式有理数。（均匀凸模最优包络）
