# 00000007926

**ATC-26 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The field dependence of the first spectral gap of arithmetic quotients: the relation between the spectral gap lambda_1(M) of a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold M = Gamma\H^3 and the discriminant D_K of its invariant trace field K_M. Conjecture: lambda_1(M) >= c/log^2 D_K (a Burger-Li-Sarnak type uniform lower bound); the logarithmic power cannot be improved to cubic (there are arithmetic manifold families attaining order log^2 via the deepest Maass forms); and the arithmetic supremum of lambda_1 is given by the Bethe-Sommerfeld critical value of the first nontrivial Maass form. (hyperbolic three-manifold log-squared gap)

**中文。** 定义：算术商的谱的第一间隙的域依赖：闭双曲 3-流形 M=Γ\H³ 的谱间隙 λ₁(M) 与其不变迹字段 K_M 的判别式 D_K 的关系。猜想：λ₁(M) ≥ c/log² D_K（Burger–Li–Sarnak 型一致下界）；且该界的对数幂不可改进为三次（存在由最深的 Maass 形式引起的算术流形族达到 log² 的阶）；λ₁ 的算术上确界由第一非平凡 Maass 形式的 Bethe–Sommerfeld 临界值给出。
