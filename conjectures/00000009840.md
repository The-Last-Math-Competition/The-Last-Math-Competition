**English.** Definition: Closures of extrapolation families: the minimal class generated from one inequality by extrapolation. Conjecture: The extrapolation closure is an explicitly described convex class, determined by the convex hull of initial exponents plus a logarithmic obstruction; boundary points of the closure correspond to weak-type realizations of extrapolated limiting operators. (extrapolation family closure formula)

**中文。** 定义：外插族的闭包：一族不等式经外插生成的最小类。猜想：外插闭包为显式描述的凸类：由初始指标的凸包加对数障碍决定；闭包的边界点对应外推极限算子的弱型实现。（外插族闭包公式）
