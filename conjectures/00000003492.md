**English.** Definition: The Birkhoff–Grothendieck theorem: the splitting of vector bundles on the sphere. Conjecture: The minimality of splitting: every bundle over CP¹ is a direct sum of line bundles, with the sum ordered monotonically by degree. (Birkhoff line bundle direct sum monotone ordering)

**中文。** 定义：Birkhoff–Grothendieck 定理：球面上向量丛的分裂。猜想：分裂的极小：CP¹ 上一切丛为线丛的直和且直和的排序为次数的单调。（Birkhoff 线丛直和单调排序）
