# 00000003014

**English.** Definition: Many-body entanglement entropy: the von Neumann entropy of subsystems. Conjecture: The logarithmic correction to the entanglement area law: the ground-state entanglement entropy of gapped local Hamiltonians obeys an area law plus a logarithmic correction, with correction coefficient the universal constant of topological entanglement entropy; the correction vanishes at gapless transitions in favor of logarithmic divergence. (entanglement area law logarithmic correction)

**中文。** 定义：多体纠缠熵：子系统的 von Neumann 熵。猜想：熵面积律的修正：gapped 局域哈密顿量的基态纠缠熵为面积律加对数修正且修正系数为拓扑纠缠熵的普适常数；修正的消没在无能隙相变点让位于对数发散。（纠缠面积律对数修正）
