**English.** Definition: The transmission problem is a free boundary problem with gradient jump conditions. Conjecture: The two-dimensional solution classification splits into convex type and an explicit nonconvex family, separated by the stability of the boundary curvature flow; the parameter of the nonconvex family is the intersection angle, with admissible domain the acute angles; and the critical angle of the delimitation is pi/4, locked by the two-phase matching condition. (curvature delimitation of the transmission dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：传输问题：带梯度跳条件的自由边界问题。猜想：二维的解分类为凸型与显式非凸族二类且由边界曲率流的稳定性分隔；非凸族的参数为交角且角的容许域为锐角；分界的临界角为 π/4 且分界由二相匹配条件锁定。（传输问题二分类曲率分界）
