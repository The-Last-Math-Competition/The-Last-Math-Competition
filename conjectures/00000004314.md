**English.** Definition: The discriminant gap of an imaginary quadratic field is the difference of adjacent discriminants. Conjecture: The limiting distribution of the gaps is the exponential law with parameter the reciprocal of the square root of X, and the maximal gap is of type sqrt(X) times a logarithm with a finite asymptotic ratio. (exponential limit law of imaginary quadratic gaps)

**中文。** 定义：虚二次域判别式间隔指相邻判别式的差。猜想：间隔的极限分布为指数律,参数为 X 平方根的倒数,最大间隔为根号 X 乘对数型且有限渐进。（虚二次间隔指数极限律）
