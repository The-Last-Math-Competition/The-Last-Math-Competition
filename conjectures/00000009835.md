**English.** Definition: Gundy decomposition: the weighted Calderon-Zygmund decomposition tool. Conjecture: The weighted loss constant of Gundy decomposition is an explicit power law in the distribution level; the power exponent is the reciprocal of p, and the loss is exact at the weak-type endpoint. (Gundy weighted loss constant)

**中文。** 定义：Gundy 分解：加权的 Calderón–Zygmund 分解工具。猜想：Gundy 分解的加权损失常数为分布函数水平的显式幂律；幂律指数为 p 的倒数且损失在弱型端点精确。（Gundy 加权损失常数）
