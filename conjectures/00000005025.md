# 00000005025

**English.** Definition: The polynomial capture domain is the expression class where fixed-point iteration terminates polynomially. Conjecture: The domain coincides with polynomial time if and only if the multitone length of iteration sequences is uniformly bounded, and the criterion is explicitly controlled by the inflation sequences. (iteration monotonicity criterion of capture-domain coincidence)

**中文。** 定义：多项式捕获域指不动点迭代多项式终止的表达类。猜想：该域与多项式时间的重合当且仅当迭代序列的多调长度可一致界定,判据由膨胀序列显式控制。（捕获域重合迭代单调判据）
