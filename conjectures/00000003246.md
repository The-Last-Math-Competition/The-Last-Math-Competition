**English.** Definition: Self-similar operators: the spectra of iterated function systems. Conjecture: The self-similarity of spectra: the spectra of self-similar structures are recursively finitely generated, with the spectral type the Perron–Frobenius limit of the substitution system. (self-similar substitution PF spectral limit)
