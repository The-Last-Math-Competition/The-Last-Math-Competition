# 00000001595

**IS-25 ★** — source: 38_可积系统与精确可解模型.md

**English.** Definition: An isoradial dimer is the dimer model on an isoradial lattice. Conjecture: The dimer entropy of a periodic isoradial lattice is an elementary period constant belonging to a number field of periods; and the degree of the field is controlled by the CM criterion of an elliptic curve. (isoradial dimer entropy periodic number field)

**中文。** 定义：等径 dimer 指等径格上的二聚体模型。猜想：周期等径格的 dimer 熵为初等周期常数且属于周期数域；且域的次数由椭圆曲线的 CM 判据控制。（等径 dimer 熵周期数域）
