# 00000007376

**English.** Definition: Heterogeneous multiscale methods with multiscale numerics. Conjecture: The compromise of macro and micro errors holds, and the constant of the compromise is the sampling window. (sampling-window HMM macro-micro compromise)

**中文。** 定义：异质多尺度方法：多尺度数值。猜想：HMM 的误差：宏观误差与微观误差的折衷且衷的常数为抽样窗口。（HMM 抽样窗口宏观微观误差折衷多尺度常数方法）
