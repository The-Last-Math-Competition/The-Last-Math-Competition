# 00000000879

**English.** Definition: The inverse problem for Schrödinger eigenvalues. Conjecture: A potential V ∈ L^∞ is uniquely determined by the full spectrum together with one norming function (Borg–Levinson: one spectrum plus norms).

**中文。** 定义：Schrödinger 的本征值的反问题。猜想：势能 V ∈ L^∞ 由整谱 + 一条范数函数唯一确定(borg–levinson 单谱 + 范数)。
