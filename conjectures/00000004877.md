# 00000004877

**English.** Definition: The formalization burden is the length ratio of the formal proof to the original. Conjecture: The burden factor is an explicit exponential in the domain complexity, with exponent one for algebra and two for analysis, and the formula is verified by statistics over large formal libraries. (domain-exponent formula of the formalization burden)

**中文。** 定义：形式化负担指形式证明与原证明的长度比。猜想：负担因子为领域复杂度的显式指数,代数域指数为一分析域为二,公式经大型形式库统计验证。（形式化负担领域指数公式）
