# 00000005907

**English.** Definition: The zigzag constant is the stability quantity of the framework. Conjecture: The conversion formula is explicit in the image deviation of approximately commutative squares, degenerating to classical stability for exact squares, and the bound is tight. (tight square-deviation conversion formula of zigzag)
