**English.** Definition: The Birkhoff polytope is the convex hull of doubly stochastic matrices. Conjecture: The lower bound of the face count is given by triangular partition counting, the bound is closed, and the kernel of the bound is the permutation-face lattice. (partition lower bound of Birkhoff face counts)

**中文。** 定义：Birkhoff 多面体为双随机矩阵的凸包。猜想：面数的下界由三角分拆计数给出且界封闭；且界的核为置换面格。（Birkhoff 面数分拆下界）
