# 00000002030

**English.** Definition: The triple divisor correlation Σ_{n≤x} d(n)d(n+1)d(n+2). Conjecture: The main term is x·P₂(log x) with a computable quadratic polynomial (triple divisor); and the polynomial coefficients are computed termwise by contour-integral residues.

**中文。** 定义：三重除子相关 Σ_{n≤x} d(n)d(n+1)d(n+2)。猜想：主项为 x·P₂(log x) 的二次多项式且系数可算(三重除子)；且多项式系数由围道积分的残差逐项计算。
