# 00000002070

**UA-20 ★** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: The Lie bialgebra prop is the operad-like category generating bialgebra structures. Conjecture: Its Koszul dual prop admits an explicit quantization giving deformations of Sternberg–Weinstein structures; the quantization parameter agrees with the degree of the prequantum line bundle of the symplectic structure; and the quantization parameter always equals the first Chern class of the prequantum line bundle.

**中文。** 定义：李双代数 prop 指生成双代数结构的操作范畴。猜想：其 Koszul 对偶 prop 的显式量子化给出 Sternberg–Weinstein 结构的形变;量子化参数与辛结构的预量子化线丛度数一致；且量子化参数恒等于预量子化线丛第一 Chern 类。
