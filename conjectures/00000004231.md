# 00000004231

**English.** Definition: Higher tree sampling is Markov chain sampling of the uniform distribution on the set of trees. Conjecture: The mixing time is bounded above by a degree-d polynomial in the size of the triangulation, where d is the dimension, and the bound is optimal, attained by simplicial spheres. (polynomial mixing bound of tree sampling)

**中文。** 定义：高维树采样指树集上均匀分布的马尔可夫链采样。猜想：混合时间以剖分规模的 d 次多项式为上界,d 为维数,上界由单纯球面达到最优。（树采样多项式混合界）
