# 00000005212

**FF2-12 ★** — source: 159_随机矩阵V_自由群因子.md

**English.** Definition: An incompressible tensor is one that cannot be embedded into a lower-dimensional tensor space. Conjecture: The incompressibility classification of second-order cubic tensors is three-valued, jointly indexed by rank and kernel dimension; the boundary of the three types is given by the jump points of rank, with jump magnitude 2; incompressibility implies rigidity, and the smallest failure of the converse is a third-order tensor. (three-valued classification of second-order cubic tensors)

**中文。** 定义：不可压缩张量：不能嵌入更低维空间张量的张量。猜想：二阶立方张量的不可压缩分类为三值型且由秩与核维数联合索引；三值型的边界由秩的跳变点给出且跳变幅度为 2；不可压缩蕴含刚性且反向失效的最小例为三阶张量。（二阶立方张量三值分类）
