# 00000001558

**GM-18 ★** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: A (1,2)-Furstenberg set contains, for each direction, a subset of dimension ≥ 1 in that direction. Conjecture: Its Hausdorff dimension is at least 2 (currently about 1.4 is known); the minimal configurations are approached by product-type constructions of regular sets in two orthogonal directions.

**中文。** 定义：(1,2)-Furstenberg 集指对每个方向含一条该方向的维数 ≥1 子集的点集。猜想：其 Hausdorff 维数下界为 2(当前已知约 1.4);极小构型由两个正交方向的正则集的乘积型构造逼近。
