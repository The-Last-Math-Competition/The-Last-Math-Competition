# 00000008714

**English.** Definition: ETH: local observables of high-energy eigenstates equal microcanonical thermodynamic values. Conjecture: ETH holds universally in typical mixed chaotic-integrable systems (a universality law); ETH fails throughout the MBL phase (a failure law); the subsystem noise correction of the failure is Gaussian (a Gaussian correction law); and the variance constant of the correction is an explicit function of the entropy density (a variance constant law). (ETH universal failure Gaussian variance)

**中文。** 定义：ETH：高能本征态的局域观测值等于微正则热力学值。猜想：ETH 在混沌可积混合的典型系统中普适成立（普适律）；且 MBL 相中 ETH 普遍破缺（破缺律）；破缺的子系统噪声修正为高斯型（高斯修正律）；修正的方差常数为熵密度的显式函数（方差常数律）。
