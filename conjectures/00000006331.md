**CM4-21 ◆** — source: 196_组合矩阵论III.md

**English.** Definition: Hadamard matrices are the fully orthogonal class with entries plus-minus one. Conjecture: The smallest missing order is given by the gap-filling paths of the construction spectrum and the spectrum is finite, the kernel of the filling being the Paley tower. (Paley tower filling of the smallest missing order)

**中文。** 定义：Hadamard 矩阵为元素正负一的完全正交类。猜想：最小缺失阶由构造谱补齐路径给出且谱有限；且补齐的核为 Paley 塔。（最小缺失阶 Paley 塔补齐）
