**English.** Definition: Critical exponents give the joint scaling of mass and energy criticality. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit value of the joint scaling of conservation laws, closed by two-anchor interpolation; the best constant of the interpolation is given by the Aubin-Talenti bubble, the bubble being the unique extremizer; and the dimensional homogeneity of the constant is locked by scaling. (two-anchor interpolation conversion of critical constants)
