**HT2-06 ◆** — source: 126_组合代数拓扑_高阶树.md

**English.** Definition: The duality of higher trees is the pairing of k-tree and codimension-tree counts. Conjecture: The product of the tree and cotree counts satisfies a corrected Euler identity, with correction term an explicit product of vertex torsion coefficients, equal to one for sphere triangulations. (tree-cotree corrected Euler identity)

**中文。** 定义：高维树的对偶性指 k-树与余维树计数的配合。猜想：树与余树计数的乘积满足修正欧拉恒等式,修正项为顶挠系数的显式乘积且在球面剖分时为一。（树-余树欧拉修正恒等式）
