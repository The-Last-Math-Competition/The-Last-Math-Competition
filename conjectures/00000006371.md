# 00000006371

**English.** Definition: Mixed elements are the weak-form discretizations of multifield variables. Conjecture: The stability constant is an explicit function of the pressure-space dimension given by macro-element counting, and the ratio of constants is the dual-norm ratio. (macro-element counting of stability constants)

**中文。** 定义：混合元为多场变量的弱形式离散。猜想：稳定常数为压力空间维数的显式函数且由宏元计数给出；且常数的比值为对偶范数比。（稳定常数宏元计数）
