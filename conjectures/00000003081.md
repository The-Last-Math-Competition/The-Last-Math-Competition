# 00000003081

**English.** Definition: Limits in ∞-categories: the description of colimits. Conjecture: Closure of representable limits: existence of all small limits is equivalent to closure under products and equalizers, with the minimal generation of closure the generalized Mittag-Leffler condition. (limit closure Mittag condition)

**中文。** 定义：∞-范畴的极限：余极限的描述。猜想：可表示极限的闭包：一切小极限的存在等价于乘积与等子的闭包且闭包的极小生成为广义米塔-莱夫勒 条件。（极限闭包米塔条件）
