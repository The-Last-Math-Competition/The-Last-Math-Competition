# 00000008364

**TRP-14 ★** — source: 264_热带几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The triviality of flatness in tropical algebra: the criterion trop_flat for flatness of tropical modules (tropical semimodules). Conjecture: trop_flat (the criterion for flat, torsion-free tropical modules) is the absence of deficits of Sturm fans (a deficit-free criterion law); the minimal module with deficit (torsion) is the 2-dimensional Speyer-Sturmfels module (an S-S module example); the complete classification of modules of rank at most 2 is the union of a finite list and a parametrized family (a finite-plus-parameter union law); and the dimension of the rank-2 parameter space is 3 (a three-dimensional parameter law). (tropical flatness Speyer-Sturmfels rank-two)

**中文。** 定义：热带代数的平直度的平庸：热带模（热带半模）的平坦性的判据 trop_flat。猜想：trop_flat（热带模的平坦（无 torsion）的判据）为 Sturm-fans 的无亏（无亏判据律）；且亏（torsion）的极小模（有亏的最小）为 2 维的 Speyer–Sturmfels 模（S-S 模例）；模的分类（秩 ≤2 的分类）的完备（完备分类）为有限+参数族的并（有限+参数并律）；秩 2 的参数空间的模数（参数的维数）为 3（三维参数律）。
