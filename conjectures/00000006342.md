# 00000006342

**English.** Definition: The canonical basis is the global basis with positivity. Conjecture: The multiplication structure constants are nonnegative; the closed form after root-system decomposition is the monomial exponent; and the combination of the monomial exponent is the order of root vectors. (nonnegative closed canonical multiplication constants)

**中文。** 定义：典范基为具正性的整体基。猜想：乘法结构常数非负；且根系分解后的闭式为单项式指数；且单项式指数的组合为根向量的序。（典范积常数非负闭式）
