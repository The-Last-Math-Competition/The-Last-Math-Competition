# 00000005270

**English.** Definition: The energy distribution and the vorticity spectrum are two layers of statistics. Conjecture: There exist two weak solutions with the same energy-scale distribution but different vorticity spectra, and the separation is realized explicitly by phase reordering of convex integration. (same energy distribution-different vorticity spectrum separation)
