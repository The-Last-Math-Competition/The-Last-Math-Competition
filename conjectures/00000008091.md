# 00000008091

**FUS-11 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The double trivialization of periodicity of fusion systems: the rank spectrum per(F) of the minimal periodic subgroups P_per (the carriers of v_1-periodicity of the fusion). Conjecture: The rank of per(F) is at most 2 (a two-rank rigidity); the realization of rank-2 periodic carriers is given by the complete classification of fusion of p-rank 2 (a Diaz-Glesser-Park-Seeliger type list): exactly 7 fusion systems carry nontrivial v_1-periodicity (a seven-system rigidity); and the product of the orders of the automorphism groups of the seven systems is 2^a 3^b (a small-prime factorization law with a,b explicit). (rank-two seven-system periodicity rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的周期性的双平凡化：F 的极小周期子群 P_per（Fusion 的 v_1-周期的承载子群）的秩谱 per(F)。猜想：per(F) 的秩 ≤ 2（双秩刚性）；且秩 2 的周期承载的实现由 p-秩 2 的 fusion 的完全分类（Díaz–Glesser–Park–Seeliger 型列表）给出：恰 7 种 fusion 系统承载非平凡 v_1 周期（七系刚性）；七系的自同构群的阶的乘积为 2^{a}·3^{b}（小素分解律，a,b 显式）。
