# 00000004162

**CM3-12 ★** — source: 124_解析数论III_圆法.md

**English.** Definition: The distribution of the exceptional set in progressions is the size of the intersection of E(x) with each residue class. Conjecture: E(x) is uniformly distributed among the representable classes of any modulus q, with density upper bound x^{1/2} divided by an explicit function of q, independent of the class. (uniform distribution of the exceptional set in progressions)

**中文。** 定义：例外集的级数分布指 E(x) 与等差数列的相交规模。猜想：E(x) 在任何模 q 的可表示类中分布均匀,密度上界为 x^{1/2} 除以 q 的显式函数且与类无关。（例外集级数均匀分布）
