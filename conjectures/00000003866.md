# 00000003866

**English.** Definition: Banded random matrices are symmetric random matrices nonzero only within bandwidth W. Conjecture: The center of the GOE-to-GUE transition of local statistics is always at W of order √N; the transition-region width is N^{1/6} and the curve is given by a single scaling function. (banded transition single scaling function)

**中文。** 定义：带状随机矩阵指仅带宽 W 内非零的对称随机矩阵。猜想：局部统计从 GOE 到 GUE 的过渡中心恒在 W 为根号 N 处；且过渡区宽为 N 的六分之一次幂而曲线由单一标度函数给出。（带状过渡单一标度函数）
