# 00000005318

**English.** Definition: The stable class of rings is the limit class of tower sequences. Conjecture: The stable-class conversion constant is the explicit evaluation of the zeta function at the critical point, given by the motivic measure, and is a volume-type constant. (zeta-evaluation volume constant of stable classes)
