# 00000004565

**English.** Definition: The motivic Galois group is the Tannakian automorphism group of the category of motives. Conjecture: The minimal number of generators of the identity component of the motivic Galois group of an algebraic variety is an explicit linear expression in the Betti numbers, and the bound is attained by abelian motives. (linear expression for motivic Galois generators)

**中文。** 定义：母题伽罗瓦群指母题范畴的 Tannakian 自同构群。猜想：代数簇的母题伽罗瓦群连通分量的生成元最小个数为 Betti 数的显式线性式,界由阿贝尔母题达到。（母题伽罗瓦生成线性式）
