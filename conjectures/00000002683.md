**English.** Definition: Algebraic dynamical systems (X,f): the dynamics of a self-map f of a projective variety and its iterates. Conjecture: Density of preperiodic points of arithmetic dynamical systems: the Zariski closure of preperiodic points of a d₁-map is a finite union of preperiodic subvarieties, of maximal dimension the ceiling of the logarithm of the top degree; the density deficit is controlled explicitly by the minimum of the canonical height. (arithmetic dynamics Zariski closure)

**中文。** 定义：代数动力系统 (X,f)：射影簇 X 上的自同态 f 与其迭代作用的动力学。猜想：算术动力系统的预周期点密度：d₁ 映射的预周期点的 Zariski 闭包为预周期子簇的有限并且并的极大维数为映射的顶端次数的对数上取整；密度亏损由 canonical 高度的极小值显式控制。（算术动力 Zariski 闭包）
