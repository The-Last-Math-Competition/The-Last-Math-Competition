**English.** Definition: Strichartz endpoint estimates are the critical regularity bounds for dispersive equations. Conjecture: On the periodic torus, the Schrödinger endpoint estimate fails for initial data below an exact threshold; and the counterexample to the failure is a resonance-block construction. (periodic critical endpoint failure)

**中文。** 定义：Strichartz 端点估计为色散方程的临界正则性。猜想：周期环面上薛定谔的端点估计在初值低于阈值时失败且阈值精确；且失败的反例为共振块构造。（周期临界端点失败）
