# 00000001218

**CG-18 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The random triangle game is the maker-breaker game on the edges of G(n,p) with the triangle as maker's goal. Conjecture: There is an explicit threshold p*(n) = n^{-1/2}(log n)^{-1/2} such that for p > p* the maker wins with high probability and for p < p* the breaker wins with high probability; the threshold window has width O(n^{-1/2}).

**中文。** 定义：随机三角博弈指在 G(n,p) 的边集上依次抢占、Maker 目标为三角的博弈。猜想：存在显式阈值 p*(n) = n^{-1/2}(log n)^{-1/2},使 p>p* 时 Maker 以高概率获胜,p<p* 时 Breaker 以高概率获胜;阈值窗宽为 O(n^{-1/2})。
