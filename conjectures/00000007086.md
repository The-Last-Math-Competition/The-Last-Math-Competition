**English.** Definition: Microlocal sheaf theory with the sheaf side of Fukaya. Conjecture: The equivalence of the cotangent Fukaya with the derived category of sheaves holds, and the kernel of the equivalence is the matching of singular supports. (singular-support microlocal equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：微局部层论：Fukaya 的层侧。猜想：微局部等价：余切的 Fukaya 与层的导出范畴的等价且等的核为奇异支撑的匹配。（微局部奇异支撑匹配等价核层导出）
