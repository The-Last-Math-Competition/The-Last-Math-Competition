**English.** Definition: The barycenter-difference structure of the cell formula. Conjecture: The explicit bound of the barycenter difference is given by the weight spectrum and is tight; the attainment of tightness is equal weights; and the attainment of equal weights is verified by symmetric configurations. (tight weight-spectrum barycenter bound of cells)

**中文。** 定义：胞腔公式的重心差结构。猜想：重心差的显式界由权谱给出且界紧；且紧的达到为等权；且等权的达到性由对称构型验证。（胞腔重心权谱紧界）
