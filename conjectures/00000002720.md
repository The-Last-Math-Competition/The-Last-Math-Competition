# 00000002720

**SC-10 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Face rings (Stanley–Reisner rings): the commutative-algebra shadow of simplicial complexes. Conjecture: The depth genus of the Stanley–Reisner ring is dual to the topological genus of the complex; the equality of genera is exact in the Buchsbaum case, and the equality map is an isomorphism of graded Betti tables. (face ring depth genus duality)

**中文。** 定义：face ring（Stanley–Reisner 环）：单纯复形的交换代数影子。猜想：Stanley–Reisner 环的深度亏格与复形的拓扑亏格对偶；亏格的相等在 Buchsbaum 情形精确且相等映射为分次 Betti 数表的同构。（面环深度亏格对偶）
