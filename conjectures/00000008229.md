# 00000008229

**English.** Definition: The monodromy of the KZ connection of Cherednik algebras: the monodromy representation mon_KZ of the KZ equations of the rational Cherednik algebra. Conjecture: mon_KZ (KZ monodromy as affine Hecke representations) is an equivalence (a KZ-Hecke equivalence law); the correspondence of levels of the module categories (blocks of category O versus blocks of the Hecke algebra) preserves dimensions (a block dimension preservation law); and the numerical instability (chaoticization) of the inverse of KZ has Lyapunov exponent the p-adic normalization of log q (a log q normalization law). (KZ monodromy Hecke equivalence log q)

**中文。** 定义：Cherednik 代数的 KZ 联络的单值：有理 Cherednik 代数的 KZ 方程的单值表示 mon_KZ。猜想：mon_KZ（KZ 的单值 = 仿射 Hecke 表示）为等价（KZ-Hecke 等价律）；且等价的模范畴的层次（范畴 O 的块与 Hecke 的块的对应）保持维数（块维数保持律）；KZ 的逆（逆单值）的数值的不稳定性（混沌化）的 Lyapunov 指数为 log q 的 p-adic 规范（log q 规范律）。
