# 00000003952

**English.** Definition: The twin-width is the minimal width over contraction sequences of red-black graphs. Conjecture: The graphs of twin-width at most 1 are exactly the distance-hereditary graphs; recognizing twin-width at most 2 is polynomial time, while recognizing twin-width at most 3 is NP-hard, so the boundary lies exactly at 2. (twin-width recognition boundary)

**中文。** 定义：twin-width 指红黑图收缩序列的最小宽度。猜想：twin-width ≤ 1 的图恰为距离遗传图;twin-width ≤ 2 的识别为多项式时间,而 ≤ 3 的识别为 NP-难,分界恰在 2。（twin-width 识别分界）
