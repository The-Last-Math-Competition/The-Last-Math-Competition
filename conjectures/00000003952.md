**English.** Definition: twin-width denotes most small width of graph contraction sequence. Conjecture: graph of twin-width ≤ 1 is of exactly distance graph;twin-width ≤ 2 is of polynomial time, ≤ 3 is NP-, separates exactly in 2. (twin-width separates)

**中文。** 定义：twin-width 指红黑图收缩序列的最小宽度。猜想：twin-width ≤ 1 的图恰为距离遗传图;twin-width ≤ 2 的识别为多项式时间,而 ≤ 3 的识别为 NP-难,分界恰在 2。（twin-width 识别分界）
