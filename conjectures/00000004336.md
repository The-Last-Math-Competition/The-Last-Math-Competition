# 00000004336

**English.** Definition: Monte Carlo variance reduction is the sampling technique for reducing the variance of integral estimates. Conjecture: The per-sample cost of logarithmic variance reduction is at least half the number of reduced binary digits, and the combination of stratification and control variates is the universally optimal technique. (logarithmic cost lower bound of variance reduction)

**中文。** 定义：Monte Carlo 方差缩减指降低积分估计方差的抽样技术。猜想：方差对数缩减的每样本代价不少于缩减位数的一半,分层与控制变量的组合为普适最优技巧。（方差缩减对数代价下界）
