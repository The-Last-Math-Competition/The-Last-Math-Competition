**English.** Definition: An extremal ray is a generator of the cone theorem. Conjecture: The lattice of ray lengths is controlled by the minimal intersection of curve equivalence classes, finitely generated for rigid varieties, and the generated table is the ray graph. (minimal-intersection finite generation of extremal rays)

**中文。** 定义：极射线为锥定理的生成元。猜想：射线长度的格由曲线等价类最小交控制且刚性簇有限生成；且生成的表为射线图。（极射线最小交有限生成）
