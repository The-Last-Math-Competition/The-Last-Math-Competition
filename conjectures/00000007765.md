**ADY-15 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: zeta function ζ_f(z)=exp(Σ_{n≥1} N_n z^n/n), N_n is etale counting of Fix(f^n) of f. Conjecture: ζ_f of characteristic p field on separable map is reciprocal of rational function, its is Weil number; and in fixed p and pair set of weight of ergodic all d map when, is exactly {p^{w/2}: w∈{0,…,d-1}}, namely weight spectrum completely realized.

**中文。** 定义：动力 zeta 函数 ζ_f(z)=exp(Σ_{n≥1} N_n z^n/n)，N_n 为 f 的 Fix(f^n) 的 etale 计数。猜想：特征 p 域上可分映射的 ζ_f 为有理函数，其分子分母根的倒数为 Weil 数；且在固定 p 与遍历所有度 d 映射时，根的权的绝对值集合恰为 {p^{w/2}: w∈{0,…,d-1}}，即权谱完全实现。
