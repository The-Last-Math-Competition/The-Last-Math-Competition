# 00000007765

**English.** Definition: The dynamical zeta function is zeta_f(z) = exp(sum N_n z^n/n) with N_n the etale count of Fix(f^n). Conjecture: For separable maps over characteristic p fields, zeta_f is a rational function whose reciprocal roots of numerator and denominator are Weil numbers; and for fixed p over all degree-d maps, the set of absolute weights of the roots is exactly {p^{w/2}: w=0,...,d-1}, i.e. the weight spectrum is fully realized. (full weight spectrum of dynamical zeta)

**中文。** 定义：动力 zeta 函数 ζ_f(z)=exp(Σ_{n≥1} N_n z^n/n)，N_n 为 f 的 Fix(f^n) 的 etale 计数。猜想：特征 p 域上可分映射的 ζ_f 为有理函数，其分子分母根的倒数为 Weil 数；且在固定 p 与遍历所有度 d 映射时，根的权的绝对值集合恰为 {p^{w/2}: w∈{0,…,d-1}}，即权谱完全实现。
