**English.** Definition: Overheating is the thermal dilation operation on game temperatures. Conjecture: The semigroup of compositions of dilation operators is commutative with unit the isotherm; and the kernel of the unit is zero temperature. (overheat semigroup zero-temperature commutative unit)

**中文。** 定义：overheating 为游戏的温度热伸缩操作。猜想：伸缩算子复合的半群为交换且单位为恒温；且单位的核为零温。（overheat 半群零温单位交换）
