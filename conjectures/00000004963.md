**English.** Definition: Chebyshev-basis sparse recovery is the reconstruction of sparse Chebyshev expansions from samples. Conjecture: The stability constant is an explicit exponential in the sparsity, attained by alternating node configurations; the exponent of the constant is exactly of type s minus one half; no node configuration has a smaller constant than the alternating one, and the ratio converges to 1 if and only if the nodes are quasi-uniformly distributed. (stability constant of Chebyshev sparse recovery)

**中文。** 定义：Chebyshev 基稀疏恢复：从 Chebyshev 多项式稀疏展开的样本重建。猜想：稳定性常数为稀疏度的显式指数式且由交错节点配置达到；常数的指数恰为 s-1/2 型；任何节点配置的常数不低于交错配置且比值收敛于 1 当且仅当节点为准均匀分布。（Chebyshev 稀疏恢复稳定常数）
