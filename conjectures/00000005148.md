**English.** Definition: Monodromy is the matrix representation of period continuation, and its closure is its algebraic envelope. Conjecture: The candidate table of rank-four closures is an explicit list of subgroups of the motive group, closed under the weight conditions with no exceptional groups. (complete subgroup table of rank-four monodromy closures)
