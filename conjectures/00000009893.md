# 00000009893

**GBR-13 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: The derived Picard group: an algebraic invariant of the autoequivalence group of derived categories. Conjecture: The derived Picard group of a finite-dimensional algebra is finitely generated, with generator count the number of connected components of the quiver plus twice the dimension of the deformation space; the group structure is a semidirect product of a finite group with a lattice. (derived Picard finite generation)

**中文。** 定义：导 Picard 群：导范畴的自等价群的代数不变量。猜想：有限维代数的导 Picard 群为有限生成且生成元个数为箭图的连通分支数加两倍的形变空间维数；群的结构为有限群与格的半直积。（导 Picard 有限生成）
