# 00000003822

**RC-02 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: The crystal diameter diam(B) denotes the maximal graph distance between any two vertices after all arrows are made undirected. Conjecture: Highest-weight crystals of finite type always satisfy diam(B(λ)) = ⟨λ, 2ρ^∨⟩, where ρ^∨ is the dual Weyl vector. (highest weight crystal diameter formula)

**中文。** 定义：晶体直径 diam(B) 指把所有箭头视为无向边后任意两顶点间的最大图距离。猜想：有限型最高权晶体 B(λ) 恒有 diam(B(λ)) = ⟨λ, 2ρ^∨⟩,其中 ρ^∨ 为对偶 Weyl 向。（最高权晶体直径公式）
