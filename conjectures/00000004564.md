# 00000004564

**English.** Definition: Grothendieck's period conjecture is the structural conjecture of period comparison. Conjecture: Its effective conversion bound is an explicit polynomial in the Hodge numbers times the height, with polynomial degree half the weight and not lowerable. (degree of the effective period polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：Grothendieck 周期猜想指周期比较的结构猜想。猜想：其有效化的换算界为 Hodge 数乘高度的显式多项式,多项式次数为权的一半且不可降低。（周期有效多项式次数）
