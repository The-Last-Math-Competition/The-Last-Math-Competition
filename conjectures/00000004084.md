# 00000004084

**English.** Definition: A grazing bifurcation is when a fixed point of a piecewise smooth map reaches the switching boundary. Conjecture: The topological classification of the normal forms of two-dimensional grazing bifurcations splits into exactly three classes by the sign of the trace of the grazing matrix, with no new topology beyond the boundary values plus or minus 1. (three-class classification by grazing trace)

**中文。** 定义：擦边分叉指分段光滑映射的不动点到达切换边界。猜想：二维擦边分叉的规范形拓扑分类由擦边矩阵迹的符号分为恰三类,迹边界值 ±1 之外无新拓扑。（擦边迹三分分类）
