**English.** Definition: Structure preservation is the numerical preservation of invariants. Conjecture: The hierarchy of preserved spectra is decided by the Lie-algebra closure; the completeness of the criterion is weight zero; and the exception of complete decidability is the non-closed ideal case. (Lie-closure criterion of structure preservation)
