# 00000000997

**English.** Definition: Fluctuations of ground-state configurations of the zero-temperature planar Ising model. Conjecture: The law-of-the-iterated-logarithm constant for density fluctuations of ground states is explicit; it agrees with the extremal spectrum of spin glasses; it is consistent with exact integrability of the planar Ising model; and the marginal distribution of the fluctuation spectrum is isomorphic to the Gaussian free field. (Ising fluctuation LIL constant)

**中文。** 定义：零温度 Ising 平面的基态构型的涨落。猜想：基态构型的密度涨落的重对数律常数显式；且常数与自旋玻璃的极值谱一致；且该常数与平面 Ising 的精确可积性自洽；且涨落谱的边缘分布与高斯自由场同构。（Ising 涨落重对数常数）
