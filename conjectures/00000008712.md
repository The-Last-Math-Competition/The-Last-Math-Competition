# 00000008712

**English.** Definition: The many-body localization (MBL) phase: the phase of interacting spin chains where all energy eigenstates are exponentially localized. Conjecture: The one-dimensional strongly disordered MBL phase exists rigorously (a rigorous existence law); the eigenstate entanglement entropy of the MBL-thermal transition jumps from area law to volume law (an entanglement jump law); the characteristic length near the transition diverges as exp(c/|W-W_c|^{1/2}) (a half-exponential divergence law); and the critical exponent on the thermal side is universal (a universality law). (MBL entanglement jump half-exponential divergence)

**中文。** 定义：多体局域化（MBL）相：相互作用自旋链中一切能量本征态均指数局域的相。猜想：一维强无序 MBL 相的严格存在（严格存在律）；且 MBL-热相变的本征态纠缠熵从面积律跳到体积律（纠缠跃迁律）；相变附近的特征长度发散为 exp(c/|W-W_c|^{1/2})（半指数发散律）；热相一侧的临界指数普适（普适律）。
