# 00000006036

**English.** Definition: Nonholonomic conversion is the quantification of nonholonomic constraints. Conjecture: The conversion formula is explicit in the integrability deficit of the constraint distribution, zero when the connection curvature vanishes; the deficit is given by the Frobenius ladder of the curvature, the ladder height at most the codimension of the distribution; and zero deficit is equivalent to a holonomic constraint, the criterion being the involutivity of the distribution. (curvature-vanishing integrability deficit of constraints)

**中文。** 定义：非完整换算：非完整约束的量化。猜想：换算公式为约束分布可积性亏格的显式且联络曲率消没时为零；亏格由曲率的 Frobenius 阶梯给出且阶梯高不超过分布余维；零亏格等价于完整约束且等价的判据为分布的对合性。（约束可积亏格曲率消没）
