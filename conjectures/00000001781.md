# 00000001781

**English.** Definition: Compactness of the d̄-Neumann operator means its canonical solution operator is compact; the worm domain W_k has annulus parameter k. Conjecture: The compactness threshold for W_k is k^{1/3}: domains with k ≤ k^{1/3} satisfy compactness, larger k do not; this interpolates Barrett's noncompact family with Fornæss's compact family.

**中文。** 定义：d̄-Neumann 算子的紧性指其典范解算子为紧算子;worm 域 W_k(k 为 annulus 参数)。猜想：W_k 的 d̄-Neumann 紧性阈值为 k^{1/3}:k≤k^{1/3} 的域满足紧性,k 更大时不紧;由 Barrett 的非紧族与 Fornaess 的紧族插入。
