**English.** Definition: The equality case of the sum formula is the extremal structure of the Brunn-Minkowski inequality. Conjecture: Equality is always attained by parallel bodies and the convex-cone decomposition is complete, the combination of completeness being the normal-cone lattice. (parallel-body convex-cone decomposition of equality)
