**English.** Definition: Defect groups and decomposition matrices are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two blocks with isomorphic defect groups but different decomposition matrices, and the separation is realized by an explicit block pair with the same defect group but different fusion. (same defect group-different decomposition fusion separation)
