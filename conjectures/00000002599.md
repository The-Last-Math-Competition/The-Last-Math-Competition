# 00000002599

**English.** Definition: Young's lattice: the inclusion poset of partitions, whose linear extensions are standard Young tableaux. Conjecture: The Schur-type positivity of d-complete posets uniformly covers the three families of Young, shifted, and Propeller lattices; the unified weight function is the d-th power of hook lengths. (d-complete poset unified positivity)

**中文。** 定义：Young 格：分拆的包含偏序，线性扩张与标准 Young 表一致。猜想：d-完备格的 Schur 型正性统一覆盖 Young、shifted 与 Propeller 格三族；统一的权重函数为 hook 长度的 d 次幂。（d-完备格统一正性）
