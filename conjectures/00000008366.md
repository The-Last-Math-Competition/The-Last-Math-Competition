# 00000008366

**TRP-16 ★** — source: 264_热带几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The tropicalization of moduli spaces and tropical stability: the simplicial structure trop_Mgn of the tropicalization Trop(Mbar_{g,n}). Conjecture: trop_Mgn (the cell structure of the tropicalized moduli space) is a weighted simplicial complex (a simplicial structure law); the weights (cell multiplicities) have a generating function whose coefficients (weight coefficients of the D-modular, modular-form part) are ellipticized partition numbers (an elliptic partition law); and the volume of the tropical moduli space (the tropical psi class integral) is an explicit multiple of the classical psi integral (an explicit multiple law), the factor being the two-three power 2^{3g-3+n} (a two-three power law). (tropical Mbar elliptic partition psi multiple)

**中文。** 定义：热带稳定性与模空间的热带化：Mbar_{g,n} 的热带化 Trop(Mbar_{g,n}) 的单纯结构 trop_Mgn。猜想：trop_Mgn（模空间热带化的胞腔结构）为加权单纯复形（单纯结构律）；且权（胞腔的重数）的生成函数的 D-modular（模形式）的系数（权系数）为分拆数的椭圆化（椭圆分拆律）；热带模空间的体积（tropical psi 类的积分）= 经典 psi 积分的显式倍（显式倍律），倍数为 2^{3g-3+n} 的三二幂（三二幂律）。
