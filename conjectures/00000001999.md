**English.** Definition: The second critical field H_{c2} of GL theory is the critical field strength at which vortices enter the superconductor. Conjecture: The optimal lattice for the lattice-configuration lower bound is the Abrikosov triangular lattice with constant 2π/√3; and the optimal lattice is unique, with no second-optimal configuration. (Abrikosov triangular optimal unique)
