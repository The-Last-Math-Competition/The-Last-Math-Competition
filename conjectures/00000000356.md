**DA-56 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The flatness constant c(n) of lattices is the infimum of the ratio of basis-vector lengths to the shortest vector. Conjecture: c(n) equals n/4 plus a bounded correction; and the correction is identically one for n greater than eight. (flatness constant linear main term)

**中文。** 定义：格的平坦性常数 c(n) 为基向量长与最短向量之比的下确界。猜想：c(n) 等于 n/4 加有界修正；且修正项在 n 大于八时恒等于一。（平坦性常数线性主项）
