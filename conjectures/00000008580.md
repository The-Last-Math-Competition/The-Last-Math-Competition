# 00000008580

**NSP-20 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: Truncated integrals and the Helton-Howe trace: the cyclic trace identity of triangular representations of operators. Conjecture: The truncated integral and the trace agree on infinitesimal generators (a trace law); the Helton-Howe identity for double cycles holds (an H-H law); the extension to multiple cycles is given by the Carey-Pincus identity (a C-P extension law); and the minimal dimension of failure of the identity is 3 (a three-dimensional failure law). (Helton-Howe Carey-Pincus trace identity)

**中文。** 定义：三角积分与 Helton–Howe 迹：算子三角表示的循环迹恒等式。猜想：三角积分与迹在无穷小生成元上一致（迹律）；且双循环的 Helton–Howe 恒等式成立（H-H 律）；多循环的推广由 Carey–Pincus 恒等式给出（C-P 推广律）；恒等式失效的最小维数为 3（三维失效律）。
