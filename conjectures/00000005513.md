# 00000005513

**QN2-13 ★** — source: 169_排队网络的图极限.md

**English.** Definition: Power-of-d sampling samples d queues and joins the shortest, with its drift function. Conjecture: The fixed-point drift of power-of-three is always an explicit cubic, degenerating to linear under light load; the cubic has exactly one real root, the stable queue length; and the derivative of the drift at the fixed point is negative, its absolute value the convergence rate. (cubic drift degeneration of power-of-three)

**中文。** 定义：多选择：采样多条队列取最短的策略；漂移函数。猜想：三选择的固定点漂移恒为显式三次式且轻负载时退化为线性；三次式的实根个数为 1 且根为稳定队长；漂移的导数在固定点处为负且其绝对值为收敛速率。（三选择三次漂移退化）
