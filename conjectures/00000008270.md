# 00000008270

**English.** Definition: The identifiability of hidden Markov models: the uniqueness index hmm_ident of the identifiability of transition-observation parameters (A,B). Conjecture: hmm_ident (generic HMM identifiability) holds within k^{O(1)} observations for polynomial samples (a polynomial sample law); the minimal non-identifiable model (equivalence classes) is the 2-state symmetric one (a 2-state symmetric example); the Lipschitz constant for stable identification (continuous recovery of parameters) is the reciprocal of the spectral gap (a spectral gap Lipschitz law); and the error exponent of optimal decoding of hidden states (Viterbi, large deviations) is the infimum of the Chernoff information of observations (a Chernoff decoding law). (HMM polynomial identifiability Chernoff decoding)

**中文。** 定义：隐藏 Markov 模型的可辨识性：转移-观测参数 (A,B) 的可辨识的唯一性指标 hmm_ident。猜想：hmm_ident（一般 HMM 的可辨识）在 k 个观测内 k^{O(1)} 的多项式样本（多项式样本律）；且不可辨识（等价类）的最小模型为 2 态对称（2 态对称例）；辨识的稳定（参数的连续恢复）的 Lipschitz 常数为谱隙的倒数（谱隙 Lipschitz 律）；隐状态的最优解码（Viterbi）的误差指数（大偏差）为观测的 Chernoff 信息的下确界（Chernoff 解码律）。
