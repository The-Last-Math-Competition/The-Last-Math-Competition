# 00000004308

**AN4-08 ★** — source: 129_数论IV_代数数论显式方法.md

**English.** Definition: The unit Vandermonde determinant of the maximal real subfield of a cyclotomic field is the Vandermonde determinant of the logarithmic coordinates of the units. Conjecture: Its upper bound is an explicit expression of type the positive part of the class number times n^{n/2}, and the bound is attained by cyclotomic fields of prime degree. (cyclotomic Vandermonde upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：分圆域极大实子域的单位 Vandermonde 行列式指单位对数坐标的范德蒙德行列式。猜想：其上界为类数正部分乘 n 的 n/2 次幂型显式式,界由素数次分圆域达到。（分圆 Vandermonde 上界）
