# 00000008276

**English.** Definition: The predictability of fractional Brownian motion (fBm) in filtering: the asymptotic variance fbm_var of the estimator of the Hurst index H. Conjecture: fbm_var (the asymptotic variance of the H estimator) is the explicit 2/T (a 2/T law); the explicit prediction gain for long-range dependence at H > 1/2 (variance reduction for predicting future increments) is the residue at the pole of the spectral density (a residue gain formula); the vanishing of the gain for H < 1/2 (anticorrelation, no long-range gain) has the critical threshold H = 1/2 (a half-critical zero-gain law); and the transfer of roughness for solutions of fBm-driven SDEs (the infimum of roughness) is the H-alpha transfer (an H-alpha transfer law). (fBm variance residue gain H-alpha transfer)

**中文。** 定义：分数布朗运动（fBm）的滤波的可预测性：fBm 的 H 的估计的渐近方差 fbm_var。猜想：fbm_var（H 估计的渐近方差）= 2/T 的显式（2/T 律）；且 H>1/2 的长程相关的可预测增益（未来增量的预测方差缩减）的显式为谱密度的极点的留数（留数增益公式）；H<1/2（反相关）的增益的消失（无长程增益）的阈为 H=1/2 的临界（半临界增益零律）；fBm 驱动的 SDE 的解的粗糙度的传递（粗糙度的下确界）为 H-α 的传递（H-α 传递律）。
