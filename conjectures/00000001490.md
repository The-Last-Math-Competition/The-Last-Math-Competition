# 00000001490

**English.** Definition: The period-doubling renormalization operator R acts on the class of unimodal maps. Conjecture: R is hyperbolic on general (arbitrary Lobachevsky-type) unimodal classes; the dimension of its unstable manifold is always 1, given by the universal conformal structure of the Feigenbaum–Coullet–Tresser fixed direction, independent of the type.

**中文。** 定义：周期倍化重整化算子 R 作用于单峰映射类。猜想：R 在一般(任意 Lobachevsky 类型)单峰类上双曲;其不稳定流形的维数恒为 1,由 Feigenbaum–Coullet–Tresser 不动方向的普适共形结构给出,不随类型改变。
