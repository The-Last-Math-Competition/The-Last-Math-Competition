**English.** Definition: Superconcentration is the phenomenon of variance below linearity. Conjecture: The double-logarithmic variance bound is attained by spin-glass-type models, the kernel of the bound being the spectral gap, and the constant of the spectral gap is the heat-kernel decay rate. (spectral-gap kernel of superconcentration)

**中文。** 定义：超集中为方差低于线性的现象。猜想：方差的双对数型界达于自旋玻璃型模型；且界的核为谱间隙；且谱间隙的常数为热核衰减率。（超集中双对数谱间隙核）
