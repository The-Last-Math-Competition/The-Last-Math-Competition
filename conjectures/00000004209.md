# 00000004209

**English.** Definition: The Morse index is the dimension of the negative space of the second variation. Conjecture: The index density of a closed minimal hypersurface, the ratio of index to area, satisfies a Weyl-type asymptotic with limit the potential-type constant c_n times the integral of |A|^2, where c_n is explicit and attained by the sphere. (index density Weyl potential constant)

**中文。** 定义：Morse 指标指第二变分负空间的维数。猜想：闭极小超曲面的指标密度(指标与面积之比)满足 Weyl 型渐近,极限为 c_n·∫|A|² 型位势常数,c_n 显式且由球面达到。（指标密度 Weyl 位势常数）
