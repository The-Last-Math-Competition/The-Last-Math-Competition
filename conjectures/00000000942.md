# 00000000942

**English.** Definition: The Ribe program (metric constants of finite representability). Conjecture: The metric type p of a Banach space is finitely recoverable from Lipschitz embedding constants of graphs (the complete quantitative realization of Ribe).

**中文。** 定义：Ribe 程序(有限表示的度量常数)。猜想：Banach 空间的度量型 p 可由图的 Lipschitz 嵌入常数有限恢复(Ribe 定量的完整实现)。
