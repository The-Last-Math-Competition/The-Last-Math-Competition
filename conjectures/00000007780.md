# 00000007780

**ADY-30 △** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Common preperiodic sets of mixed power maps: S(d_1,d_2) = Per(z^{d_1}) intersect Per(z^{d_2}) consists of roots of unity. Conjecture: For any quadratic polynomial f together with a power map z^d on P^1(Qbar), the common preperiodic set has cardinality uniformly bounded by 2 v(d) with v(d) the prime factor count of d; and when f is neither Chebyshev nor monomial the set contains only fixed-point-type points, at most 3, decidable by uniform distribution of the fixed-point multipliers of f. (power-map preperiodic intersection)

**中文。** 定义：混合幂映射的公共预周期集：S(d_1,d_2)=Per(z^{d_1})∩Per(z^{d_2}) 由单位根构成。猜想：对 {P^1(Qbar) 中任意二次多项式 f 与幂映射 z^d 的公共预周期集}，其基数被 2·v(d)（v(d) 为 d 的素因子个数）一致控制；且当 f 非 Chebyshev 与单项式时该集合只含不动点类型的点，其个数 ≤3 并可由 f 的不动点乘子的一致分布判定。
