# 00000009851

**KPU-01 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: The KPZ fixed point: the universal limit process of the 1+1-dimensional KPZ universality class (Matetski-Quastel-Remenik proved convergence for TASEP). Conjecture: The totally asymmetric zero-range process from arbitrary gentle initial data converges to the KPZ fixed point, with finite-size corrections a t^{-1/3} power law; the universal constant of the correction is explicitly given by the curvature of the initial data. (zero-range convergence to KPZ fixed point)

**中文。** 定义：KPZ 不动点：1+1 维 KPZ 普适类的普适极限过程（Matetski–Quastel–Remenik 对 TASEP 证明收敛）。猜想：完全不对称零程过程从任意平缓初值收敛到 KPZ 不动点且有限尺寸修正为 t^{−1/3} 的幂律；修正的普适常数由初值的曲率显式给出。（ZRP 收敛 KPZ 不动点）
