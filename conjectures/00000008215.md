**ORB-15 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The unimodular representation and the Segal-Shale-Weil cycle: the slope msw_slope of the infinite-dimensional cycle of the metaplectic representation. Conjecture: msw_slope (the slope of the K-type spectrum of the Weil representation) is 1/2 (a half-slope law); the dimension difference of the two branches of the splitting of the cycle (the Clifford splitting at the prime 2) is at most 1 (a dimension-difference-one law); the kernel of the theta lift of the Weil representation (representations with trivial lift) consists of even characters (an even-character kernel law); and the minimal pair at the wall of the conservation relation of theta is the first wall of (O(1), Sp(2)) (a first-wall example). (Weil half-slope conservation first wall)

**中文。** 定义：幺模表示与 Segal–Shale–Weil 循环：metaplectic 表示的无限维循环的斜率 msw_slope。猜想：msw_slope（Weil 表示的 K-型谱的斜率）= 1/2（半斜率律）；且循环的分裂（素 2 的 Clifford 分裂）的两支的维数差 ≤ 1（维数差一律）；Weil 表示的 θ 提升的核（平凡提升的表示）为偶特征（偶特征核律）；θ 的守恒关系的壁的最小对为 (O(1), Sp(2)) 的第一壁（第一壁例）。
