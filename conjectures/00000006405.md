**English.** Definition: The inscribed structure of the strengthened symmetric difference is the inradius of the symmetric-difference metric of convex bodies. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the existence of the inradius minimum, and a convex body has at least two contact points; the difference value is the rigidity deficit; and the lower bound of the rigidity deficit is 1/2, attained by isosceles triangles. (two-contact rigidity-deficit conversion constant)

**中文。** 定义：对称差强化的内切结构：凸体的对称差度量的内切半径。猜想：换算常数为内切半径极小存在的显式且凸体至少二切点；差值为刚性亏；刚性亏的下界为 1/2 且界由等腰三角形达到。（内切二点刚性亏换算常数）
