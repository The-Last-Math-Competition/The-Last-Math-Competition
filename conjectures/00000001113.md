# 00000001113

**English.** Definition: In characteristic p, the semisimple threshold of a Weyl module V(λ) is the range of λ for which its composition factors have no multiplicity. Conjecture: When λ lies in the closed alcove {λ : ⟨λ+ρ, α∨⟩ ≤ p(p−h+1) for all simple α}, all composition factor multiplicities of V(λ) are 1, and this alcove bound cannot be enlarged for SL₃.

**中文。** 定义：正特征 p 下 Weyl 模 V(λ) 的半单阈值指使其合成因子无重数的 λ 范围。猜想：当 λ 落在权格的闭alcove {λ : ⟨λ+ρ,α∨⟩ ≤ p(p−h+1) 对一切单 α} 内时,V(λ) 的合成因子重数全为 1,且该 alcove 界对 SL₃ 不可再扩大。
