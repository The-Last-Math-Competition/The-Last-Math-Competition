# 00000008456

**English.** Definition: A rational tangle is a two-strand tangle of double-spiral type. Conjecture: The supremum of hyperbolic volumes of rational tangles is an explicit Catalan-constant type value; the unique tangle attaining it is of (3,5) type. (supremum of rational tangle volumes)
