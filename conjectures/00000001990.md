# 00000001990

**MP-30 △** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: A time crystal is the spontaneous breaking of discrete time-translation symmetry. Conjecture: There exist rigorous time crystals in one-dimensional spin chains (period-2 minimal-energy clusters, MBL-stable); the construction uses the absence of many-body resonances in the driven Floquet–Ising system.

**中文。** 定义：时间晶体指离散时间平移对称的自发破缺。猜想：存在一维自旋链的严格时间晶体(周期 2 的极小能簇,MBL 稳定);构造由驱动 Floquet–Ising 的多体共振缺失。
