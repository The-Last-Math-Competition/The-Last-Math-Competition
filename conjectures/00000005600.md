# 00000005600

**English.** Definition: The determinant and the echelon form are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two matrices with the same determinant but non-isomorphic echelon forms, and the separation is realized by an explicit noncommutative pair of unimodular transformations. (same determinant-different echelon unimodular separation)

**中文。** 定义：行列式与梯形型指两层。猜想：存在两矩阵的行列式相同而梯形型不同构,分离由单模变换的显式非交换对实现。（行列式同-梯形异单模分离）
