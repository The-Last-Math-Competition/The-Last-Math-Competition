**English.** Definition: Random walks on contingency tables: Markov chain mixing. Conjecture: The mixing threshold of the contingency-table chain: the mixing time of the heat-bath chain on 2 x n contingency tables is polynomial in the diameter, with polynomial exponent the greatest common divisor of the diagonal. (contingency mixing gcd law)
