# 00000003539

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic geometry of ordinals: roots of ordinal polynomials. Conjecture: The root count of ordinal polynomials: the existence of roots is the valuation condition of coefficients, with the number of roots at most the leading degree of the polynomial. (ordinal polynomial root leading degree upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：序数的算术几何：序数多项式的根。猜想：序数多项式的根数：根的存在为系数的赋值条件且根的个数不超过多项式的首项度。（序数多项式根首项度上界）
