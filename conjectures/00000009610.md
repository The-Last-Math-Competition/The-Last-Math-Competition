# 00000009610

**MSH-30 △** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: Discrete minimal surfaces on triangulations are critical points of the cotan-Laplacian area functional; discrete-to-continuous convergence is a foundational problem of discrete differential geometry. Conjecture: Discrete minimal surfaces on uniformly refined triangulations converge to smooth minimal surfaces at rate O(h), with the limit independent of the conformal structure chosen for the triangulation; and the nondegeneracy condition (interior angles bounded below) is necessary and sufficient. (discrete minimal convergence rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：三角剖分上的离散极小曲面为 cotan Laplacian 面积泛函的临界点；离散到连续的收敛性是离散微分几何的基础问题。猜想：一致加细剖分上的离散极小曲面收敛到光滑极小曲面，收敛速率为 O(h) 且极限与剖分的共形结构选择无关；非退化条件（内角有下界）必要且充分。（离散极小收敛刚性）
