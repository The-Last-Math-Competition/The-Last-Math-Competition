**English.** Definition: discrete minimal surface of three angle triangulation on is convergence of critical point; discrete continuous of cotan Laplacian area functional is foundation problem of discrete differential geometry. Conjecture: discrete minimal surface of uniform triangulation on converges to smooth minimal surface, convergent rate is O(h) and limit and structure without pass; nondegenerate (in angle of triangulation has lower bound) necessary and sufficient. (discrete minimal convergent rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：三角剖分上的离散极小曲面为 cotan Laplacian 面积泛函的临界点；离散到连续的收敛性是离散微分几何的基础问题。猜想：一致加细剖分上的离散极小曲面收敛到光滑极小曲面，收敛速率为 O(h) 且极限与剖分的共形结构选择无关；非退化条件（内角有下界）必要且充分。（离散极小收敛刚性）
