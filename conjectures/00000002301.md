# 00000002301

**English.** Definition: McKay's conjecture is the local-global equality k(G,D) = k(N_G(D)) of character counts. Conjecture: It holds universally (Malle–Späth proved the defect-2 case); the remaining uniform verification for p = 2 proceeds by a system-by-system check of nil-block induction.
