# 00000002108

**English.** Definition: The quantum Kirwan map is the equivariant isometry of the quantum cohomology of a symplectic reduction. Conjecture: The quantum Kirwan map of the diagonal quotient of CP¹×CP¹ has an explicit computation (surjective on the quantum ring); the kernel is given explicitly by the quantum correction terms of the classical moment graph.
