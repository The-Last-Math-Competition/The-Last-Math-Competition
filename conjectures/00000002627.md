**MF-07 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: The connection between degree-2 Siegel modular forms and Jacobi forms: the Maass specific space. Conjecture: The Jacobi characterization of the Maass space: a Siegel cusp form lies in the Maass space if and only if its Fourier–Jacobi coefficients are the explicit symmetrization of index-1 Jacobi forms; the kernel of the symmetrization map is zero. (Maass space Jacobi characterization)

**中文。** 定义：Siegel 2 阶模形式与 Jacobi 形式的联系：Maass 特定空间。猜想：Maass 空间的 Jacobi 刻画：Siegel 尖点形式属于 Maass 空间当且仅当其 Fourier–Jacobi 系数为 index 1 Jacobi 形式的显式对称化；对称化映射的核为零。（Maass 空间 Jacobi 刻画）
