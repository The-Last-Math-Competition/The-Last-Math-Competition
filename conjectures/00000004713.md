# 00000004713

**EC4-23 ◆** — source: 142_遍历论的组合_熵与计数.md

**English.** Definition: A Toeplitz subshift is a system of periodic symbol filling. Conjecture: The attainable domain of the density of its maximal equidistributed sequence is exactly the rational points of an interval, each value is realized by an explicit template, and the template periods are closed. (rational attainable domain of Toeplitz densities)

**中文。** 定义：Toeplitz 子移位指符号周期填充的系统。猜想：其最大等分布序列的密度可达域恰为区间中的有理点,每个值由显式模板实现且模板周期封闭。（Toeplitz 密度有理可达域）
