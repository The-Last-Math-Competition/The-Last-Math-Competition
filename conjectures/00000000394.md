# 00000000394

**DA-94 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The structure of the partial quotients of the continued fraction of ζ(3). Conjecture: The continued fraction of ζ(3) contains infinitely many terms equal to perfect squares; and the density of square terms follows an explicit logarithmic law. (zeta3 continued fraction square terms)

**中文。** 定义：ζ(3) 的连分数展开的部分商结构。猜想：ζ(3) 的连分数包含无穷多项等于完全平方数；且平方项的密度为显式对数律。（zeta3 连分数平方项）
