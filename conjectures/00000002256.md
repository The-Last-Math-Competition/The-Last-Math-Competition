# 00000002256

**English.** Conjecture: The classification of meromorphic f sharing three values with f′ is complete (f–f′ CM sharing); and the exceptional functions are exactly compositions of exponential entire functions with linear polynomials.

**中文。** 猜想：亚纯 f 与 f′ 共享三个值的分类完全(f–f′ CM 分享)；且例外函数恰为指数整函数与一次多项式复合。
