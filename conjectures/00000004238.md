# 00000004238

**English.** Definition: The higher effective resistance is the value of the bilinear form of the Laplacian pseudoinverse. Conjecture: The sum of the resistances of all j-faces is an explicit invariant depending only on the homology and the number of faces, and the sum is independent of the details of the triangulation. (higher resistance summation invariant)

**中文。** 定义：高维有效电阻指拉普拉斯伪逆的双线性型取值。猜想：全体 j-面电阻之和为仅依赖同调与面数的显式不变量,和不依赖剖分细节。（高维电阻求和不变量）
