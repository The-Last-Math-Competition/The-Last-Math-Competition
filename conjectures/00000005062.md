# 00000005062

**English.** Definition: The almost Mathieu operator is the discrete Schrodinger operator with quasiperiodic cosine potential. Conjecture: At the self-dual critical coupling all phases are spectrally critical and the Lyapunov exponent is identically the positive part of the logarithm of the absolute coupling; in the supercritical regime pure point spectrum holds for almost every phase and the spectrum is always of Cantor type; in the subcritical regime the failure of absolutely continuous spectrum occurs only on the all-rational phase orbits. (self-dual critical characterization of the almost Mathieu operator)

**中文。** 定义：几乎 Mathieu 算子：位势为余弦准周期的离散薛定谔算子。猜想：自对偶临界耦合处一切相位均为谱临界且 Lyapunov 指数恒等于耦合绝对值的对数取正部；超临界时纯点谱对几乎处处相位成立且谱恒为 Cantor 型；亚临界的绝对连续谱失效仅发生在全有理相位轨道上。（几乎 Mathieu 自对称临界刻画）
