**English.** Definition: The principle of local reflexivity: the embedding into B(X*,Y*). Conjecture: The quantitative version of local reflexivity: the ε-realization length of the principle is the logarithm of the dimension, and the block-basis choice of the realization is an explicit function of uniform convexity. (local reflexivity quantitative length)
