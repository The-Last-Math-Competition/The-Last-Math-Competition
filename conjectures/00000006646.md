# 00000006646

**English.** Definition: Symbolic summation with the Gosper algorithm. Conjecture: Hypergeometric summation is decided by the Gosper criterion, and the combination of the criterion is the difference field. (difference-field Gosper criterion of hypergeometric summation)

**中文。** 定义：符号求和：Gosper 算法。猜想：超几何求和：Gosper 判据且据的组合为差分域。（符号求和 Gosper 算法超几何求和差分域组合 Gosper 差分域超几何判据求和判据域求和差分判据）
