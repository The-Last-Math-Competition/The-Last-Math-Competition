# 00000008184

**CNT-14 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: The distribution of quadratic irrationals by continued fraction period: the limit law cf_law(X) of the distribution of period lengths l(D) (for D <= X). Conjecture: The limit of cf_law is a corrected Gauss-Kuzmin type discrete law: P(l = m) ~ C (log sqrt D)^m/m! (a Poisson-logarithm law); the asymptotics of the maximum of l (the longest period) is bounded above by O(sqrt D log D) and below by Omega(sqrt D), a tight gap of log D (a logarithmic gap law); and the parity distribution of the period (the criterion of the ambiguous class number) is the symmetric 1/2-1/2 (a parity symmetry law), the deviation term of the symmetry being the 2-rank of genus theory (a genus deviation law). (Gauss-Kuzmin Poisson-logarithm genus deviation)

**中文。** 定义：连分数的周期的二次无理数的分布：周期长度 ℓ(D) 的分布（D ≤ X）的极限律 cf_law(X)。猜想：cf_law 的极限为 Gauss–Kuzmin 型离散律修正：P(ℓ=m) ~ C·(log √D)^m/m!（泊松-对数律）；且 ℓ 的最大值（最长周期）的渐近为 O(√D log D) 的上界（√D log 上界）与 Ω(√D)（下界）的紧间隙 log D（对数间隙律）；周期的奇偶（歧类数的判据）的奇偶分布为 1/2-1/2 的对称（奇偶对称律），对称的偏差项为 genus 理论的 2-秩（genus 偏差律）。
