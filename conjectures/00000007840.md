# 00000007840

**MSE-30 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The distribution of enumerative rationality: the genus spectrum R_g of leading q-coefficients of the rational curve counts N_g(q) on the quintic hypersurface. Conjecture: The asymptotics of R_g are governed by a Bousseir-type recursion: R_g ~ (2g)! A^g g^{-alpha}, where A is the logarithmic singularity constant of the Gromov-Witten potential of the surface S given by a one-sided neighborhood of a line and alpha = 3/2; and the error against the Tatitscheff-Zagier type explicit formula decays exponentially as O(2^{-g}) for g beyond 40. (quintic rational curve genus asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：枚举理性的分布：五次超曲面上的有理曲线的亏格谱 R_g = {N_g(q) 的首 q 系数}。猜想：R_g 的分布渐近由 Boussezq 型递推控制：R_g ~ (2g)!·A^g·g^{-α}，A 为曲面 S=直线单侧邻域的 Gromov–Witten 势的对数奇点常数，α=3/2；且回归到 Tatitscheff–Zagier 型显式公式的误差在 g 大于 40 时为 O(2^{-g}) 的指数衰减。
