**English.** Definition: The relation between the spectral gap of finite level covers of the mapping class group Mod_{g,n} and the first Laplace eigenvalue of modular surfaces is a Brooks-type problem. Conjecture: lambda_1(level-L covers of modular surfaces) times diameter squared converges to an explicit positive constant c(g,n), with c given by the hyperbolic entropy of the curve graph and the WP-isometric double linkage; and the uniform spectral gap lower bound (1/(4 pi squared))(1/g) of level covers is exactly tight. (level cover spectral gap)

**中文。** 定义：映射类群 Mod_{g,n} 的有限商图（level cover）的谱隙与模曲面第一 Laplace 特征值的关系为 Brooks 型问题。猜想：λ_1(模曲面 level L 覆盖)·diam² 收敛到显式正常数 c(g,n)，且 c 由曲线图的双曲熵与 WP 等距的双缩联给出；level 覆盖的谱隙一致下界 1/(4π²)·(1/g) 有精确紧性。（level 覆盖谱隙）
