**English.** Definition: The Bernstein problem studies the regularity of entire minimal graphs. Conjecture: The classification of minimal cones in dimension eight is an explicit finite list of isolated cones; and the exceptional cone at the critical dimension is unique up to isomorphism. (eight-dimensional minimal cone classification)

**中文。** 定义：Bernstein 问题研究整极小图的正则性。猜想：八维的极小锥分类为显式孤立锥列表；且临界维的例外锥在同构意义下唯一。（八维极小锥分类）
