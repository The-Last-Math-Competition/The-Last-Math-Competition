**English.** Definition: The Kummer test is the solvability test for powers of elliptic units. Conjecture: Its threshold constant is an explicit value of type the square of the logarithm of p, the threshold improves linearly over CM fields with constant 2. (threshold formula of the Kummer test)

**中文。** 定义：Kummer 检测指椭圆单位幂的可解性检测。猜想：其阈值常数为 p 对数的平方型显式值,CM 域上阈值线性改进且常数为 2。（Kummer 检测阈值公式）
