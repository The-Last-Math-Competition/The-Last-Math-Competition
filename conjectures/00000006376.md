**English.** Definition: Pressure stabilization is the projection-type stabilization of the pressure direction. Conjecture: The consistency order is recovered by projection, with exact recovery order; the realization of recovery is the orthogonal-complement projection; and the realization of the orthogonal-complement projection is nodal averaging. (exact projection recovery of consistency orders)

**中文。** 定义：压力稳定为压力方向的投影型稳定。猜想：相容阶由投影恢复且恢复阶精确；且恢复的实现为正交补投影；且正交补投影的实现为节点平均。（相容阶投影恢复精确）
