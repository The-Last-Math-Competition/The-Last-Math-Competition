# 00000008191

**CNT-21 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: The computational threshold of the minimum distance of algebraic-geometric codes: the NP-hardness boundary agd_hard(n) of determining the minimum distance of AG codes. Conjecture: The boundary of agd_hard (the family of codes computable in polynomial time) is the special family of rate 1/2 (a half-rate polynomial family); minimum distance computation for general AG codes is NP-hard (a general hardness law); and the criterion for the family of codes tightening the lower bound (attaining the Goppa designed distance) is the complete jumping of the dimension of Riemann-Roch spaces (an RR-jump criterion), the jump threshold being the gaps of the Weierstrass semigroup (a gap-jump duality). (AG code distance half-rate RR jump)

**中文。** 定义：代数几何码的最小距离的计算阈：AG 码的码的最小距离的确定的 NP 硬的边界 agd_hard(n)。猜想：agd_hard 的边界（多项式可算的码的族）为码率 1/2 的特殊族（半码率多项式族）；且一般的 AG 码的距离计算为 NP 困难（一般困难律）；距离的下界（Goppa 的设计距离）的紧化（达到下界）的码的族的判定为 Riemann–Roch 空间的维数的完全跳变（RR-跳变判据），跳变的阈值为 Weierstrass 半群的间隙（间隙-跳变对偶）。
