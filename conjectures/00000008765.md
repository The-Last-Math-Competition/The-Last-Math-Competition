**English.** Definition: The Brown-Gersten spectral sequence: the Zariski convergence of K-theory. Conjecture: The convergence domain always contains all regular Noetherian schemes; and the collapse condition of the motivic AH spectral sequence is given by the vanishing layers of coherent sheaves. (Brown-Gersten AH vanishing collapse)
