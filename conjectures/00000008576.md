# 00000008576

**English.** Definition: m-isometric operators: finite-order generalizations of isometries satisfying binomial combination identities. Conjecture: The spectrum of an m-isometry is contained in the closed unit disk (a disk containment law); the spectral modulus is identically 1 if and only if the operator is an isometry plus unitary (a unitary equality law); every m-isometry decomposes as a finite extension of an isometric part (a decomposition law); and the upper bound on the minimal m is the dimension of the space (a dimension upper bound law). (m-isometry disk unitary decomposition bound)

**中文。** 定义：m-等距算子：满足二项式组合恒等式的有限阶等距推广。猜想：m-等距的谱包含于闭单位圆盘（圆盘包含律）；且谱模长恒为 1 当且仅当算子为等距加酉（酉等号律）；一切 m-等距可分解为等距部分的有限扩张（分解律）；极小 m 的上界为空间维数（维数上界律）。
