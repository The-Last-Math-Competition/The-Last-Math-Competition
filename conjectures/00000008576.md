# 00000008576

**NSP-16 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: m- isometry operator: satisfies finite order isometry of binomial combinatorial identical expression. Conjecture: spectrum contained in closed unit (contains); of m- isometry and spectrum modulus is always 1 if and only if operator is isometry unitary (unitary); all m- isometry separable solution is upper bound of finite extension (decomposition); minimal m of isometry part is space dimension (dimension upper bound).

**中文。** 定义：m-等距算子：满足二项式组合恒等式的有限阶等距推广。猜想：m-等距的谱包含于闭单位圆盘（圆盘包含律）；且谱模长恒为 1 当且仅当算子为等距加酉（酉等号律）；一切 m-等距可分解为等距部分的有限扩张（分解律）；极小 m 的上界为空间维数（维数上界律）。
