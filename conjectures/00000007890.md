# 00000007890

**English.** Definition: The model independence of condensed sets: the equivalence sheaf I_cond of the condensed topos across countable models of ZFC and ZF+DC. Conjecture: I_cond is homotopy equivalent on all epsilon-models (absoluteness), and the supremum of the ordinals needed for transfinite closure is the tight lower neighborhood of the first inaccessible cardinal; the condensed analogue of the Whitehead problem (vanishing of free condensed abelian groups) is undecidable without the supercompact cardinal assumption, its independence proof being transplantable from inner-model constructions at a measurable cardinal. (condensed set absoluteness and Whitehead)

**中文。** 定义：凝聚态集合的模型独立性：在 ZFC 与 ZF+DC 的可数模型下的凝聚态 topos 的等价性层 I_cond。猜想：I_cond 在一切 ε-模型上同伦等价（绝对性），且超限闭包所需的序数上确界为第一个不可及基数的紧下方；白头问题的凝聚态类比（自由凝聚态群的消没）在非超紧基数假设下不可判定，其独立性证明可由可测基数的内模型构造移植。
