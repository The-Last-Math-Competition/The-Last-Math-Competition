# 00000006348

**English.** Definition: The universal braid solution is the mother solution of the braid structure. Conjecture: The convergence window of the factorization is the unit disk; the conversion within the window is always one; and the singularity at the window boundary is the pole of the braid. (factor convergence disk of universal solutions)

**中文。** 定义：泛辫解为辫结构的母解。猜想：因式分解的收敛窗为单位圆盘；且窗内的换算恒为一；且窗边界的奇性为辫子的极点。（泛解因子收敛圆盘）
