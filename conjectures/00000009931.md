# 00000009931

**English.** Definition: Discrete Morse theory: minimal critical pairings of filtered complexes. Conjecture: Computing the minimal critical point count is NP-hard, and a polynomial-time logarithmic approximation exists; the non-improvable gap of the approximation is preserved from subcube covering hardness to arbitrary constant factors. (discrete Morse approximation bound)

**中文。** 定义：离散 Morse 化：过滤复形的极小临界配对。猜想：极小临界点数计算为 NP 困难且存在多项式时间的对数近似；近似的不可改进间隙由子立方体的覆盖硬性保持到任意常数因子。（离散 Morse 近似界）
