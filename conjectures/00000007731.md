# 00000007731

**FRV-11 ★** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The compound free Poisson law with jump distribution nu and intensity lambda has R-transform R(z) = lambda int z/(1-tz) d nu(t). Conjecture: The supremum of atomic masses of the compound free Poisson family is max(lambda^{-1} mass(nu), 1/2); and when nu is a two-point distribution the law has exactly floor(lambda)+1 atoms, with the product of atomic masses an algebraic combination of lambda^{-lambda} and the masses of nu. (atomic structure of compound free Poisson laws)

**中文。** 定义：复合自由 Poisson 律由跳跃分布 ν 与强度 λ 经自由复合 Poisson 构造，其 R-变换为 R(z)=λ∫ z/(1-t z) dν(t)。猜想：复合自由 Poisson 律族的原子质量上确界为 max(λ^{-1}·mass(ν), 1/2)；且当 ν 为两点分布时该律恰有 floor(λ)+1 个原子，原子质量之积的连乘为 λ^{-λ} 与 ν 质量的代数组合。
