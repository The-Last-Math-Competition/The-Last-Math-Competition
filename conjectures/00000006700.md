**OT3-30 △** — source: 208_最优传输II_离散.md

**English.** Definition: Pairwise distances and the barycenter structure are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two measure sets with identical pairwise distances but different barycenter structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit permutation pair with the same distances but different configurations. (same pairwise-different barycenter permutation separation)

**中文。** 定义：两两距离与重心结构指两层。猜想：存在两测度集的一切两两距离相同而重心结构不同,分离由同距异构型的显式置换对实现。（两两距离同-重心异置换分离）
