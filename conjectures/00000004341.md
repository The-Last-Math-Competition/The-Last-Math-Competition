**FP2-11 ◆** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: Kakutani distance denotes Hellinger type integral distance of infinite product measure. Conjecture: such that finite projection error does not exceed most small projection dimension of ε is logarithm type explicit function of entropy and the dimension optimal. (Kakutani most small projection dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Kakutani 距离指无穷乘积测度间的 Hellinger 型积分距离。猜想：使有限投影误差不超过 ε 的最小投影维数为熵亏的对数型显式函数且该维数最优。（Kakutani 最小投影维数）
