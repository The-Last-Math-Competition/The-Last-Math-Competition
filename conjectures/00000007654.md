**English.** Definition: Absorbing boundary conditions as nonreflecting truncations of artificial boundaries. Conjecture: The residual reflectance in the frequency window is a decreasing curve of the explicit window-end function; and the in-window reflectance monotonically tends to zero. (frequency-window residual reflectance)

**中文。** 定义：吸收边界条件为人工边界的无反射截断。猜想：残余反射率在频率窗内恒为窗端显式函数的下降曲线；窗内反射率单调趋于零。（残余反射率频率窗）
