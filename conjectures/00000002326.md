# 00000002326

**English.** Definition: Polyhedral homotopies decompose tracking by the BKK mixed volume. Conjecture: The minimal value of the ratio of the path count of the optimal mixed-volume decomposition to the full Bézout number is n!/n^n (an explicit mixed-cell ratio); the extremal ratio is attained by diagonal mixed structures.

**中文。** 定义：多面体同伦指按 BKK 混合体积分解追踪。猜想：混合体积最优分解的路径数与满 Bézout 数之比的最小值为 n!/n^n(混合单元的显式比);比值的极值由对角混合结构达到。
