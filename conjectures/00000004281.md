**AG5-11 ◆** — source: 128_交换群论.md

**English.** Definition: Ext(A, Z) is the group of central extensions of A. Conjecture: The dimension of its divisible part follows the power-of-2 formula in the torsion-free rank mu of A, and the decision that the non-divisible part is of torsion-complete type is given by the pure projective dimension of A. (divisible dimension formula of Ext)

**中文。** 定义：Ext(A, Z) 指 A 的中心扩张群。猜想：其可除部分维数为 A 无挠秩 μ 上的 2 的 μ 次幂型公式,不可除部分为挠完备型的判定由 A 的纯投射维数给出。（Ext 可除维数公式）
