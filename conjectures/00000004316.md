# 00000004316

**English.** Definition: The local density is the density of representability of a quadratic form at a prime. Conjecture: The infinite product of local densities converges to an explicit constant of rational value 1/2, and the convergence rate is the reciprocal square of the prime parameter. (convergence constant of the local density product)

**中文。** 定义：局部密度指素数处二次型表示的存在密度。猜想：局部密度的无穷乘积收敛于显式常数,常数为 1/2 型有理值,收敛速率为素参数平方倒数。（局部密度乘积收敛常数）
