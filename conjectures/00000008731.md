# 00000008731

**MBL-21 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: Nonequilibrium steady states (NESS): steady states of open chains coupled to baths of different temperatures at the two ends. Conjecture: In the MBL phase the temperature gradient decays exponentially (a decay law); the interface thermal resistance is Kapitza type (a Kapitza law); the many-body Landauer description of thermal-phase NESS holds (a Landauer law); and the transport signatures of the two phases are distinguished by response functions (a distinction law). (NESS Kapitza Landauer response distinction)

**中文。** 定义：非平衡稳态（NESS）：两端接不同温浴的开放链的稳态。猜想：MBL 相中温度梯度指数衰减（衰减律）；且界面热阻为 Kapitza 型（Kapitza 律）；热相 NESS 的多体 Landauer 描述成立（Landauer 律）；两相的输运 signatures 可由响应函数区分（区分律）。
