# 00000003960

**G4-20 △** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: A product lower bound for structural parameters means that the product of two parameters controls some quantity linearly. Conjecture: There exists a parameter pair whose product lower bound can be approached indefinitely yet no finite graph attains equality; the smallest such parameter pair is treewidth and pathwidth. (asymptotically unattained parameter products)

**中文。** 定义：结构参数的乘积下界指两参数之积对某量的线性控制。猜想：存在参数对使其乘积的线性下界可被无限逼近但无任何有限图达到等号;最小这样的参数对为树宽与路宽。（参数乘积渐近无实现）
