**English.** Definition: The saddle-point asymptotics of D-finite generating functions. Conjecture: The first error term is an explicit series with logarithmic corrections; and the series converges exponentially. (D-finite saddle error series)

**中文。** 定义：D-有限生成函数的鞍点渐近。猜想：首个误差项为对数修正的显式级数；且级数的收敛为指数。（D-finite 鞍点误差级数）
