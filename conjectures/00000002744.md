**English.** Definition: Asymptotic theory of identities: asymptotic PI of matrix algebras. Conjecture: The limiting characterization of the asymptotic identity set: the asymptotic spectrum of all identities of M_n is determined by the rank stratification of n×n capacity matrices, and the stratification is a finite lattice. (asymptotic PI spectrum rank stratification)
