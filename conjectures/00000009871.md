# 00000009871

**English.** Definition: The stochastic six-vertex model: scaling limits of height functions. Conjecture: The Fredholm representation of the one-point distribution of the stochastic six-vertex height function has optimal error control, the error decaying as an explicit power of the spectral parameter of vertex weights; the scaling limit is a thinning of the Airy line ensemble. (six-vertex Fredholm error)

**中文。** 定义：随机六顶点：高度函数的标度极限。猜想：随机六顶点模型高度函数的单点分布的 Fredholm 表示有最优误差控制且误差按顶点权重的谱参数的显式幂衰减；标度极限为 Airy 线系综的稀疏化。（六顶点 Fredholm 误差）
