**English.** Definition: The period of a beta-transformation is the orbit length of x = 1. Conjecture: #{β ∈ (1,2) : per_β(1) = n} ~ c·n² with c = 1/ln 2; the counting asymptotics follows from an explicit binary-tree count of FLIP branches.
