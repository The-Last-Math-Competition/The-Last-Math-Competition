# 00000001315

**CC-15 ★** — source: 29_多复变与复几何.md

**English.** Definition: The τ-invariant of the Webster connection (a CR analogue of the Riemannian invariant). Conjecture: A sign change of τ on an embeddable three-dimensional CR manifold implies a nontrivial obstruction to CR holomorphic functions (τ and embedding rigidity).

**中文。** 定义：Webster 联络的 τ-不变量(CR 版模仿 Riemann)。猜想：嵌入型三维 CR 流形的 τ 的符号变化蕴含存在 CR 全纯函数的非平凡障碍(τ 与嵌入刚性)。
