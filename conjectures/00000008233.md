**English.** Definition: The manipulation gains of deferred acceptance (DA) in market design: the gain set of one-sided manipulation (a single agent misreporting preferences). Conjecture: A quantification of the prevalence of nonempty gains (universal existence of manipulation): the density of manipulable preference profiles tends to 1 for large markets (a manipulation density law); the worst manipulation gain (the most profitable misreport) is constructed by top trading (a top-cycle exchange law); and the infimum of the efficiency-loss ratio for the price of strategyproof mechanisms is 1 - 1/e (a reciprocal-e efficiency lower bound), tightness approached asymptotically by serial dictatorship (a dictatorship tightness). (DA manipulation density serial dictatorship bound)

**中文。** 定义：匹配的市场设计的延迟接受（DA）的操纵收益：单边操纵（一个代理谎报偏好）的收益集合 gain_DA。猜想：gain_DA 的非空的普遍性（操纵普遍存在）的定量：可操纵的偏好剖面的密度 → 1（当市场大，操纵密度律）；且操纵收益的最坏（收益最大的谎报）的构造为顶部交换（top-cycle 交换律）；无操纵机制的代价（Strategyproof 的效率损失）的比率的下确界为 1-1/e（e-倒数效率下界），下界的紧性由序列独裁（serial dictatorship）渐近取得（独裁紧性）。
