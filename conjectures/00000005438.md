# 00000005438

**English.** Definition: The spectrum with boundary condition is the spectrum under the normal free condition. Conjecture: The boundary-behavior conversion constant is given explicitly by the heat-kernel boundary layer, attained for convex domains, with constant of reciprocal square-root-of-time type. (heat-kernel conversion constant of boundary layers)

**中文。** 定义：带边界条件的谱指法向自由条件的谱。猜想：边界行为的换算常数由热核边界层显式给出,凸域达到且常数为时间平方根倒数型。（边界层热核换算常数）
