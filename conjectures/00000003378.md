**NC-08 ★** — source: 98_非交换几何II_谱三元组.md

**English.** Definition: The noncommutative residue: the Wodzicki residue. Conjecture: The noncommutative extension of the Wodzicki residue: the residue of pseudodifferential symbols is the residue of higher-dimensional poles, with the noncommutativization of the residue the quantum residue on the Hopf algebra side. (Wodzicki quantum residue Hopf extension)

**中文。** 定义：非交换留数：Wodzicki 留数。猜想：Wodzicki 留数的非交换推广：伪微分符号的留数为高维极点的残差且残差的非交换化为 Hopf 代数侧的量子留数。（Wodzicki 量子留数 Hopf 推广）
