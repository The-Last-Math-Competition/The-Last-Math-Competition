**English.** Definition: N(λ) is the eigenvalue counting function of the Laplacian on the Sierpinski gasket. Conjecture: N(λ) = C·λ^{d_s/2} + c·λ^{d_i/2} + o(λ^{d_i/2}) (a second power-law term with inner exponent d_i and explicit constants).
