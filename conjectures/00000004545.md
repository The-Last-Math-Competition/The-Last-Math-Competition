**English.** Definition: Mahler's method is the systematic proof of transcendence of values of self-similar functions. Conjecture: The solvability criterion of the coefficient field of the Mahler equation is an explicit Frobenius semisimplicity condition, and the condition is complete and decidable in finitely many steps. (Mahler solvability Frobenius criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Mahler 方法指自相似函数值超越性的系统证明。猜想：Mahler 方程系数域的可解判据为 Frobenius 半单的显式条件,条件完全且可有限步判定。（Mahler 可解 Frobenius 判据）
