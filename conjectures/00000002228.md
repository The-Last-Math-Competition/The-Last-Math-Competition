**English.** Definition: The Zariski lattice is the distributive lattice of compact open sets. Conjecture: The sublattice count agrees with coherent-module support; and the agreement spectrum is given explicitly by the covering complex of the compact basis. (Zariski sublattice covering-complex spectrum)
