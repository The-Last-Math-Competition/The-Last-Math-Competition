# 00000000732

**English.** Definition: The unit index ε(m) of the cyclotomic field Q(ζ_m) is the index of its unit group over the norm-one units. Conjecture: As the conductor m → ∞, the supremum of ε(m) grows like φ(m)/2^{ω(m)}·(1+O(2^{−ω(m)})), with exact equality when m is an odd prime power.

**中文。** 定义：循环域 Q(ζ_m) 的单位指标 ε(m) 指其单位群对范数为 1 单位的指数。猜想：当 conductor m→∞ 时,ε(m) 的上确界增长为 φ(m)/2^{ω(m)}·(1+O(2^{-ω(m)})),且该界在 m 为奇素数幂时取得精确等式。
