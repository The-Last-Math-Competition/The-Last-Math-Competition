**English.** Definition: The fine hierarchy of consistency strength of large cardinal axioms: the definability def(lc) of the proof-theoretic constants of relative consistency for the hierarchy below measurability (inaccessible, Mahlo, weakly compact, indescribable, and so on). Conjecture: The exact jump of the consistency strength at each layer is the increment of n in Sigma_n-reflection (a reflection increment law); the arithmetization cost of the jump (the increment of proof length) is one layer of the tower function (a single-tower law); and the iteration fixed-point constant of the fine structure of the inner model L[U] of a measurable cardinal is the order type of Steel's standard parameters (uniqueness of the order type of the Steel constant). (reflection increment single-tower Steel order)
