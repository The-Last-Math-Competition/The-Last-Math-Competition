# 00000008205

**ORB-05 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The reduction of minimal representations: the K-type spectrum kt_min(G) of the minimal representation pi_min of a semisimple group G. Conjecture: The K-type multiplicities of kt_min are 1 (a multiplicity-one law); the highest weights of the K-types form a linear chain in the rank (a linear chain law); the step size of the chain is a (half-)integer multiple of the coefficient of the highest root (a root-coefficient step law); and the stable rank of the image of the reflection representation of the minimal representation (the first theta lift of Howe's correspondence) equals the Witt index minus 1 (a stable rank formula). (minimal representation multiplicity-one root step)

**中文。** 定义：极小表示的约化：半单群 G 的极小表示 π_min 的 K-型谱 kt_min(G)。猜想：kt_min 的 K-型重数为 1（重数一律）；且 K-型的最高权构成 rank 的线性链（线性链律）；链的步长为最高根的系数的（半）整数（根系数步长律）；极小表示的反射表示（Howe 的 θ 对应的首次提升）的像的稳秩 = Witt 指数 - 1（稳秩公式）。
