# 00000001740

**English.** Definition: The Mordell–Weil lattice. Conjecture: The smallest prime factor of the discriminant of the MW lattice of the known rank-28 elliptic curve is at most 7 (arithmetic of the record curve's MW lattice).

**中文。** 定义：Mordell–Weil 格。猜想：已知的秩 28 椭圆曲线的 MW 格的判别式的最小素因子 ≤ 7(记录曲线 MW 格的算术)。
