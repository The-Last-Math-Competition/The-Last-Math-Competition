# 00000002293

**FR-03 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: A centered fusion system is one all of whose objects are centric. Conjecture: A combinatorial proof of the isomorphism uniqueness of its centric linking system (uniqueness on top of Chermak's existence); the unique obstruction group is H²(S; Z(S)) (i.e. H² controls everything).

**中文。** 定义：centered 融合系统指一切对象为中心化的融合系统。猜想：其 centric linking 系统的同构唯一性(Chermak 的存在性之上的唯一性)的组合证明;唯一的 obstruction 群为 H²(S;Z(S))(即 H² 控制一切)。
