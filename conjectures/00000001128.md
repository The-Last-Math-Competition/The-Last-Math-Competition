# 00000001128

**English.** Definition: M(w,j) is the maximal monomial coefficient in the degree-j part of the Schubert polynomial 𝔖_w. Conjecture: M(w,j) equals min(j, ℓ(w), ⌈ℓ(w)/2⌉)! times a lattice-path count C(w), where C(w) has a product formula on 321-avoiding permutations; in particular M(w, ℓ(w)) is an explicit function of the increasing-subsequence count of w.

**中文。** 定义：Schubert 多项式 𝔖_w 的度 j 部分的系数极大值 M(w,j) 指该项中最大单项式系数。猜想：M(w,j) = min(j, ℓ(w), ⌈ℓ(w)/2⌉)! 之外再乘以格路径数 C(w),且 C(w) 在 321-避免置换上有乘积公式;特别 M(w,ℓ(w)) 为 w 的递增子序列计数的显式函数。
