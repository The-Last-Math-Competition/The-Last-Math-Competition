# 00000001784

**CV-04 ◆** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal constant C_n of the Ohsawa–Takegoshi L² extension theorem. Conjecture: The optimal constant for the ball is proven (Błocki); the constant for general pseudoconvex domains is still π: the supremum is attained by the ball case; the optimal constant is uniformly π over all domains.

**中文。** 定义：Ohsawa–Takegoshi L² 延拓定理的最优常数 C_n。猜想：球的最优常数已证(Błocki);一般拟凸域的常数仍为 π:上确界被球的情形达到;最优常数在一切域上一致为 π。
