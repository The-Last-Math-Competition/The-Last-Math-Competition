**English.** Definition: The heat kernel is the fundamental solution of the heat equation. Conjecture: The leading term of the long-time asymptotic of the heat kernel of a hyperbolic manifold is the exponential law of the first eigenvalue, and the next term is a resonance-phase correction with explicit coefficient. (lambda_0 leading law of the long-time heat kernel)
