# 00000007758

**ADY-08 △** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Boundedness of integer orbits: the sequence a_0 in Z, a_{n+1}=f(a_n) for f in Z[x] of degree at least 2. Conjecture: If f has no real fixed point with attracting interval (the real Julia set is totally disconnected), then the integer orbit is finite, and the union O_f of all integer orbits satisfies the count #{O_f intersect [-H,H]} = O(H^{1/2+epsilon}); in particular the orbit values of x^2+1 have density O(H^{-1/2} log H) in [1,H]. (integer orbit counting bound)

**中文。** 定义：整点轨道的有界性：整数序列 a_0∈Z, a_{n+1}=f(a_n)，f∈Z[x] 次数 ≥2。猜想：若 f 无实不动点吸引区间（即实 Julia 集完全不连通），则整点轨道为有限集，且全体整轨道的并集 O_f 满足计数 #{O_f∩[-H,H]} = O(H^{1/2+ε})；特别地 x^2+1 的整轨道像集在 [1,H] 内的密度为 O(H^{-1/2}log H)。
