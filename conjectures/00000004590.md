# 00000004590

**HA3-20 △** — source: 138_双曲几何的解析面.md

**English.** Definition: The homology spectrum and the length spectrum are two geometric spectra. Conjecture: There exist two hyperbolic three-manifolds whose closed-chain counts all agree while their length spectra differ, and the separation is realized by a homology-isometric pair of surgeries. (homology spectrum-length spectrum separation)

**中文。** 定义：同调谱与长度谱指两个几何谱。猜想：存在两个双曲三维流形的一切闭链计数相同而长度谱不同,分离由同调等距的手术对实现。（同调谱-长度谱分离）
