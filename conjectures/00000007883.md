# 00000007883

**HCA-13 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: Cellularity of motives: in the stable category of MW-motives, an object X is cellular if it lies in the thick tensor closure of the sphere shifts; the cellular bound c(X) is the minimal generation order of a weight-flip closed closure. Conjecture: For every smooth projective variety, c(X) <= dim(X); and equality holds exactly for twisted sums of spheres (sphere varieties); and the Morel-type homotopy invariants (mu-homotopy groups) of a cellular spectrum form a complete invariant of its PIQUEL transform (commutation normal form), equivalent to the vanishing of the Z/2[G]-module structure of the associated injective chain complex. (cellular motive generation bound)
