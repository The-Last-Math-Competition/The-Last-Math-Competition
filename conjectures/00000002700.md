# 00000002700

**English.** Definition: Height lower bounds of dynamical systems: explicit bounds for minimal heights. Conjecture: Orbit height lower bounds of algebraic points: the canonical height of a non-preperiodic point is strictly positive with lower bound an explicit combination of the map degree and the discriminant; the bound is tight, attained by Chebyshev-type maps. (canonical height lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：动力系统的高度下界：极小高度的显式界。猜想：代数点的轨道高度下界：非预周期点的 canonical 高度严格正且下界为映射度数与判别式的显式组合；界的紧性由 Chebyshev 型映射达到。（canonical 高度下界）
