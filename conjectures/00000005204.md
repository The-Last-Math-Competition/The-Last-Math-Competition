# 00000005204

**English.** Definition: The Minkowski dimension is the dimension-type invariant of algebras. Conjecture: The dimension of free group factors exists if and only if the interpolation domain is a semilattice, and the dimension conversion formula is given by the limit of compression ratios and is unique. (Minkowski compression-limit dimension formula)

**中文。** 定义：闵可夫斯基维数指代数的维数型不变量。猜想：自由群因子的维数存在当且仅当插值域为半格,维数换算公式由压缩率极限给出且唯一。（闵可夫斯基压缩极限维数公式）
