**DA-26 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The uniform spectrum Ŵ(τ) is the set of numbers whose order of uniform approximation is exactly τ. Conjecture: For τ < 2, the Hausdorff and packing dimensions of Ŵ(τ) differ; and their difference tends to zero as τ → 2. (uniform spectrum dimension gap)

**中文。** 定义：一致谱 Ŵ(τ) 为一致逼近阶恰为 τ 的数集。猜想：τ<2 时 Ŵ(τ) 的 Hausdorff 维数与填充维数相异；且两者之差在 τ→2 时趋于零。（一致谱维数相异）
