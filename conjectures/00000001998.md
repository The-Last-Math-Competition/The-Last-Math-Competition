**English.** Definition: The semi-geostrophic equations are the Lagrangian form of atmospheric dynamics. Conjecture: Uniqueness of weak solutions holds under W^{1,p} (p > 1) regularity of the Lagrangian coordinates, with the critical case p = 1; the threshold is exact (a W^{1,1} nonuniqueness counterexample exists).

**中文。** 定义：半地转方程指大气动力学的 Lagrangian 形式。猜想：弱解的唯一性在 Lagrangian 坐标的 W^{1,p}(p>1) 正则下成立,临界为 p=1;阈值为精确(即 W^{1,1} 不唯一反例存在)。
