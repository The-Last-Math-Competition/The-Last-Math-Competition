# 00000001767

**RT-17 ★** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: Dirac cohomology is the homology of the kernel of the Dirac operator of a (g,K)-module. Conjecture: The first discrete-series family with nonzero Dirac cohomology is the holomorphic discrete series (complemented by the converse of Vogan's conjecture: the Dirac cohomology of every discrete series equals its infinitesimal character); beyond what is proven, the first classification covers holomorphic families of weight ≥ 2.

**中文。** 定义：Dirac 上同调指 (g,K)-模的 Dirac 算子的核的同调。猜想：Dirac 上同调非零的首个离散序列族为全纯离散序列(由 Vogan 猜想的逆定理补充:一切离散序列的 Dirac 上同调=其无穷小特征标)。已证部分之外,首例分类为权 ≥2 的全纯族。
