**OL-22 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: The comparability graph of a poset: the vertex comparability structure. Conjecture: The complement of a comparability graph is perfect; new quantitative form: the chromatic-clique gap of toral comparability graphs is controlled by the rank deficit of a parametrized Hankel matrix. (comparability graph perfect duality)

**中文。** 定义：偏序集的可比图：顶点可比性结构。猜想：可比图的补为完美；新的定量：环面可比图的色数-团数间隙由参数化的 Hankel 矩阵的秩亏控制。（可比图完美对偶）
