**English.** Definition: The blow-up cone and the asymptotic type are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two free boundaries with the same blow-up cone but different first-order asymptotics, and the separation is realized by an explicit transmission pair with the same cone but different coefficients. (same cone-different asymptotic transmission coefficient separation)
