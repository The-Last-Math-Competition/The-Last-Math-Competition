# 00000009853

**KPU-03 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: The slow bond problem: the effect on the macroscopic profile of a local jump-rate 1 - epsilon perturbation in TASEP (Basu-Ganguly-Sly proved the effect exists). Conjecture: The critical strength of the slow bond is zero: every epsilon > 0 changes the macroscopic flow, with the profile correction an explicit jump in upstream density; the correction amplitude relates to epsilon by monotone exponential decay to the stationary profile. (slow bond critical zero strength)

**中文。** 定义：慢键问题：TASEP 中局部跳跃率 1−ε 的扰动对宏观轮廓的影响（Basu–Ganguly–Sly 已证影响存在）。猜想：慢键的临界强度为零：任意 ε>0 均改变宏观流且轮廓修正为上游密度的显式跳跃；修正幅度与 ε 的关系为单调指数衰减到平稳轮廓。（慢键临界零强度）
