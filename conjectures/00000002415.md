# 00000002415

**GC2-05 ★** — source: 66_Galois覆盖与基本群.md

**English.** Conjecture: For every finite group G and number field K ⊃ Q, there exists a regular Galois extension L/K with group G and discriminant ≤ exp(C·[G:Q]²·h(K)) (an explicit bound); the polynomial exponent of the bound is exact.

**中文。** 猜想：对每个有限群 G 与数域 K⊃Q,存在 G 的正则 Galois 扩张 L/K,其判别式 ≤\exp(C·[G:Q]^2· h(K))(显式界);界的多项式指数精确。
