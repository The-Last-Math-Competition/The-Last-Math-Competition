**English.** Definition: Straightening–unstraightening: the correspondence between fibrations and functors. Conjecture: Rigidity of straightening: the counit of the adjunction is a weak equivalence, and the deficit of the equivalence level is the flatness of fibers. (straightening counit flatness deficit)

**中文。** 定义：拉直-反拉直（straightening–unstraightening）：纤维化与函子的对应。猜想：拉直的刚性：伴随的余单位为弱等价且等价的层级的亏损为纤维的平坦性。（拉直余单位平坦亏损）
