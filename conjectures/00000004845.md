# 00000004845

**English.** Definition: The quasiconvex genus of a quasiconvex subgroup of a hyperbolic group is the minimal genus of the subgroup as a surface subgroup. Conjecture: The genus upper bound is an explicit function of the hyperbolicity constant and the codiameter of the subgroup; the bound is attained by quiver-type constructions and separation fails at attainment; quasiconvex subgroups of genus zero do not exist in groups without an indecomposable virtual HNN decomposition. (constant upper bound of quasiconvex genera)

**中文。** 定义：双曲群拟凸子群的拟凸亏格：该子群作为曲面子群的最小亏格。猜想：亏格上界为双曲常数与子群余直径的显式函数；界由箭图型构造达到且达到时子群的分离性失效；亏格为零的拟凸子群在无非分歧虚拟 HNN 分解的群中不存在。（拟凸亏格常数上界）
