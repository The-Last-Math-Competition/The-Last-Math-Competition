# 00000004894

**English.** Definition: Cross-domain counting conversion is the transfer from generating functions to finite field counts. Conjecture: The counting deviation is an exponential correction of the parameters, the low-degree terms are given explicitly by the Hodge numbers, and the deviation is monotone in the degree. (Hodge exponential correction of cross-domain counting)

**中文。** 定义：跨域计数换算指母函数到有限域计数的迁移。猜想：计数偏差为参数的指数修正,低次项由 Hodge 数显式给出且偏差随次数单调。（跨域计数 Hodge 指数修正）
