# 00000005165

**English.** Definition: Twisted noncommutative Hodge theory carries a twisting parameter. Conjecture: Its spectral conversion constant is an explicit exponential function of the twist parameter, degenerating to the usual spectrum at zero parameter and preserving convexity. (exponential conversion of twisted noncommutative spectra)

**中文。** 定义：扭曲非交换 Hodge 指带扭曲参数的理论。猜想：其谱换算常数为扭参数的显式指数函数,零参数退化为通常谱且保持凸性。（扭曲非交换谱指数换算）
