**SDG-16 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: The Stokes phenomenon is the jump behavior of sectorial summations of divergent series solutions. Conjecture: Stokes constants are algebraically independent numbers; in integrable systems they are positive and their product with the intersection angle of separatrices is conserved. (algebraic independence of Stokes constants)

**中文。** 定义：Stokes 现象指发散级数解的扇形求和的跳变。猜想：Stokes 常数为代数无关数；其在可积系中为正数且与其分界线交角的乘积守恒。（Stokes 常数代数无关）
