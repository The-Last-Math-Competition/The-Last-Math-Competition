# 00000006429

**SD3-29 ◆** — source: 199_几何测度V_对称差问题.md

**English.** Definition: Lattice convex bodies are compact convex sets containing lattice-point structure. Conjecture: The deficit of the lattice version of the product inequality is always given by the boundary lattice-point count, and the lattice version recovers the continuous Brunn-Minkowski inequality in the limit. (boundary lattice-point deficit of lattice products)

**中文。** 定义：格点凸体指含整点结构的紧凸集。猜想：乘积不等式的格点版亏格恒由边界整点数给出；格点版的极限恢复连续 Brunn–Minkowski 不等式。（格点积亏格边界整点）
