# 00000004447

**English.** Definition: Stationary KPZ is the KPZ fixed point with stationary initial data. Conjecture: Its two-point covariance decays by an explicit exponential law, with exponent constant given explicitly by the second cumulant of the initial distribution. (stationary KPZ covariance cumulant exponent)

**中文。** 定义：平稳 KPZ 指平稳初值的 KPZ 不动点。猜想：其两点协方差衰减为显式指数律,指数常数由初值分布的二阶累积量显式给出。（平稳 KPZ 协方差累积量指数）
