**English.** Definition: The normalized density product of lattice codes (codings from Euclidean lattices): the quantization goodness (normalized second moment) G(Lambda) of an n-dimensional lattice Lambda. Conjecture: The infimum G_n = 1/12 + Theta(n^{-2}) (lattice realizability of the Zador asymptotics); and lattice sequences attaining 1/12 + O(n^{-2}) have duality gap O(1/n), constructed by laminated-type wound products; at n = 24 the Leech lattice attains the local optimum G = (1/12)(1 + 1/1440), the minimal finite list of corrections being sieved by the Minkowski-Siegel mass formula. (lattice Zador Leech optimality)

**中文。** 定义：格码（Euclidean 格的格点编码）的正规化密度积：n 维格 Λ 的量化良好性（normalized second moment）G(Λ)。猜想：G(Λ) 的下确界 G_n = 1/12 + Θ(n^{-2})（Zador 渐近的格实现性）；且达到 1/12+O(n^{-2}) 的格序列的格对偶亏（duality gap）为 O(1/n)，由缠绕积（laminated 型）构造；在 n=24 由 Leech 格取得局部最优 G=1/12·(1+1/1440)（修正量的极小有限列表由 Minkowski–Siegel 质量公式筛出）。
