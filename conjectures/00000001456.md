# 00000001456

**CX-06 ★** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The Löwner–John ellipsoids. Conjecture: A complete classification of symmetric convex bodies attaining the minimal number n+⌈n/2⌉ of John-ellipsoid contact points (an extremal classification of contact points).

**中文。** 定义：Löwner–John 椭球。猜想：对称凸体达到 John 椭球接触点最小数 n+⌈n/2⌉ 的完全分类(接触点极值分类)。
