# 00000002465

**English.** Definition: The Banach–Mazur game is an infinite game on topological spaces. Conjecture: The winning sets of measurable strategies admit a cardinal-invariant axiomatization; and the combination of the axioms is the strategy equivalence class. (BM measurable strategy equivalence class axiomatization)

**中文。** 定义：Banach–Mazur 博弈为拓扑空间上的无限博弈。猜想：可测策略的必胜集尺度不变量公理化；且公理的组合为策略等价类。（BM 可测策略等价类公理化）
