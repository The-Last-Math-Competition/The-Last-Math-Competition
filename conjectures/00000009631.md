**SMA-21 ★** — source: 306_Smale空间与符号动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The level-2 large deviation principle for empirical measures: the rate function is the Legendre dual of the pressure (known); strict convexity and the quadratic form are unknown. Conjecture: The rate function is strictly convex at the equilibrium state with Hessian an explicit constant multiple of the Fisher information matrix of the Gibbs measure; the quadratic form has no zero directions in phase space, and the convexity modulus matches the correlation decay exponent. (large deviation strict convexity)

**中文。** 定义：经验测度的大偏差原理（level-2）：率函数为压力的 Legendre 对偶（已知）；严格凸性与二次型未知。猜想：率函数在均衡态处严格凸且 Hessian 为 Gibbs 测度的 Fisher 信息矩阵的显式常数倍；二次型在相空间无零方向，凸性模与关联衰减指数一致。（大偏差严格凸性）
