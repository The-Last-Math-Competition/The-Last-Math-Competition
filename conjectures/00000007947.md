# 00000007947

**English.** Definition: The inverse spectrum of peakons of the Camassa-Holm equation: the Stieltjes continued fractions of the dynamics of peaked solutions and finite-rank modifications of Jacobi matrices. Conjecture: The evolution of N-peakon solutions is equivalent to a finite-rank decomposition of the Toda flow of a Jacobi matrix; and the merging law of the scattering data at peakon collisions (blow-up renormalization) is a contraction of continued fractions, the number of contractions equaling the number of merged peakons, and the rearrangement of the peakon mass spectrum is an eigenvalue insertion of the Toda matrix; the collision time set of any solution family outside the merging law (antipeakon-peakon symmetric pairs) is an explicit biquadratic field of algebraic numbers. (peakon Toda contraction merging law)

**中文。** 定义：Camassa–Holm 方程的尖峰（peakon）的逆谱：峰值解的动力学的 Stieltjes 连分数与 Jacobi 矩阵的有限秩修正。猜想：N-尖峰解的演化等价于 Jacobi 矩阵的 Toda 流的有限秩分解；且尖峰碰撞（blow-up 重整化）的散射数据的合并律为连分数的收缩操作，收缩次数等于尖峰的合并数，尖峰质量谱的重排为 Toda 矩阵的特征值插入；任何合并律之外的解族（antipeakon-peakon 对称对）的碰撞时间集合为代数数的显式双二次域。
