# 00000002727

**English.** Definition: Combinatorial Laplacians: the up-down Laplacian spectra of simplicial complexes. Conjecture: The spectrum of the combinatorial Laplacian connects with the count of spanning hypertrees via the higher matrix-tree theorem; the asymptotics of the tree count is a spectral determinant, whose secondary term is the combinatorial shadow of the regularized Ray–Singer quantity. (higher matrix-tree asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：组合 Laplacian：单纯复形的上-下 Laplacian 谱。猜想：组合 Laplacian 的谱与组合树的树数（spanning hypertree）通过高维矩阵树定理联系；树数的渐近为谱行列式且行列式的次级项为正则化 Ray–Singer 量的组合影子。（高维矩阵树渐近）
