# 00000002644

**English.** Definition: The first-kind points of Jacobi forms: half-points z = λ/2 + μτ/2. Conjecture: The half-point value theorem: φ(τ, half-point)·e^{-πimλμτ} is a half-integral-weight modular form of weight k−1/2 with a correction; the correction is uniquely determined by the mod-2 class of (λ,μ). (Jacobi half-point value theorem)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式的第一类点：z=λ/2 + μτ/2 型半点。猜想：半点值定理：φ(τ,半点)·e^{-πi m λμτ} 为半整权模形式且权为 k−1/2 修正；修正由 (λ,μ) 的模 2 类唯一确定。（Jacobi 半点值定理）
