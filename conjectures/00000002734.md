# 00000002734

**English.** Definition: Minimal free resolutions of face rings: the combinatorial Betti table. Conjecture: The recursion of the Betti table of minimal free resolutions: β_{i,j} has the combinatorial interpretation of pair counting at position (i,j), satisfying Herzog–Kühl type proportional relations; the rigidity of the relations saturates in the Gorenstein case. (face ring Betti proportionality rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：face 环的极小自由分解：组合侧的 Betti 表。猜想：极小自由分解的 Betti 表的递推：β_{i,j} 的组合解释为 (i,j) 位置的配对计数且计数满足 Herzog–Kühl 型比例关系；关系的刚性在 Gorenstein 情形饱和。（面环 Betti 比例刚性）
