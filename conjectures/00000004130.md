# 00000004130

**English.** Definition: The two-step iteration forcing is the composite forcing of two quotient algebras. Conjecture: There exists a forcing P whose two-step iteration is equivalent to a single step while no intermediate regular embedding exists, and the smallest such P has cardinality aleph_1. (regular embedding gap in iteration forcing)

**中文。** 定义：叠加重力指两层商代数的复合力迫。猜想：存在力迫 P 使两层叠加重力等价于单层而不存在中间正则嵌入,最小 P 的基数为 ℵ₁。（叠加重力正则嵌入缺口）
