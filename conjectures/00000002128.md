# 00000002128

**PS-18 ★** — source: 56_解析组合.md

**English.** Definition: The q-Potts domain-wall partition function is an Izergin–Korepin determinant. Conjecture: The low-temperature limit has a third-order phase transition; the latent heat of the transition is the leading determinant term; and the latent heat is explicitly a constant multiple of a determinant difference. (Potts domain-wall third-order transition)

**中文。** 定义：q-Potts 域壁配分为 Izergin–Korepin 行列式。猜想：低温极限的相变为三阶；且相变的潜热为行列式首项；且潜热的显式为行列式差的常数倍。（Potts 域壁三阶相变）
