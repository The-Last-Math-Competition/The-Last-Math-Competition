**English.** Definition: Linear negation is the negation operator in phase semantics. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit involution of annihilators, given in finite spaces by the fixed-point count; the fixed-point count is the Mobius count of the subspace lattice; and the fixed points of the involution are dense at self-dual factors. (fixed-point conversion of annihilator involutions)

**中文。** 定义：线性否定：相位语义中的否定算子。猜想：换算常数为零化子的显式对合且有限空间时由不动点数给出；不动点数为子空间格的 Möbius 计数；对合的不动点在自对偶因子处稠密。（零化子对合不动点换算）
