# 00000008281

**SFL-21 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The Sinkhorn correction of particle methods in filtering: the contraction sink_c of the Sinkhorn iteration of particle weights. Conjecture: sink_c (the Sinkhorn contraction rate) is the explicit 1 - 1/n (a 1/n contraction law); the convergence rate in the regularization parameter epsilon of the Sinkhorn limit (entropy regularization of optimal transport) is second order epsilon^2 (an epsilon-squared second-order law); and the constant of the second order (the norm of the Hessian) is the L^2 norm of the difference of marginal densities (an L^2 constant law). (Sinkhorn contraction epsilon-squared L^2 constant)

**中文。** 定义：滤波的粒子方法的 Sinkhorn 修正：粒子权重的 Sinkhorn 迭代的收敛的收缩 sink_c。猜想：sink_c（Sinkhorn 的收缩率）= 1-1/n 的显式（1/n 收缩律）；且 Sinkhorn 的极限（最优传输的熵正则化）的正则化参数 ε 的收敛速率为 ε^{2} 的二阶（ε² 二阶律）；二阶的常数（Hessian 的范数）= 边缘的密度差的 L² 范数（L² 常数律）。
