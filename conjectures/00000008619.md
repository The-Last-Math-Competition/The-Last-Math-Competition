# 00000008619

**MLC-29 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Multitask learning is joint estimation with shared structure. Conjecture: The boundary between sharing and independence is a spectral threshold of task similarity; and the rate jump at the boundary is half the dimension difference. (multitask spectral threshold)

**中文。** 定义：多任务学习指共享结构联合估计。猜想：共享与独立的分界为任务相似度谱阈值；分界的速率跳跃为维数差的一半。（多任务谱阈值）
