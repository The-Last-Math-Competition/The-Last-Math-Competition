# 00000009670

**English.** Definition: Distributional convergence of random matrix bilinear forms <v, Wu>: high-dimensional Berry-Esseen bounds. Conjecture: Bilinear forms converge to Gaussian under uniformly bounded fourth moments at rate n^{-1/2}; optimality of the rate is attained by counterexamples with nonzero third moment, and the heavy-tail stabilized limits are explicit alpha-stable laws. (bilinear form convergence rate)

**中文。** 定义：随机矩阵双线性型 ⟨v,Wu⟩ 的分布收敛：高维 Berry–Esseen 界。猜想：双线性型在四阶矩一致有界条件收敛到高斯且速度为 n^{−1/2}；速度的最优性由三阶矩非零的反例达到，重尾时的稳定化极限为显式 α 稳定律。（双线性型收敛速度）
