**HSS-30 △** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: distribution convergent: high dimension Berry–Esseen bound of random matrix bilinear type ⟨v,Wu⟩. Conjecture: bilinear type in four order moment uniformly bounded converges to Gaussian and speed is optimal of n^{−1/2}; speed by counterexample of three order moment non- zero attains stabilization limit of, heavy-tailed when is explicit α stable. (bilinear type convergence speed)

**中文。** 定义：随机矩阵双线性型 ⟨v,Wu⟩ 的分布收敛：高维 Berry–Esseen 界。猜想：双线性型在四阶矩一致有界条件收敛到高斯且速度为 n^{−1/2}；速度的最优性由三阶矩非零的反例达到，重尾时的稳定化极限为显式 α 稳定律。（双线性型收敛速度）
