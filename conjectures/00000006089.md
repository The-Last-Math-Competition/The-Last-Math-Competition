# 00000006089

**English.** Definition: Nonconvex constraints are manifold-type constraint structures. Conjecture: On smooth compact manifolds the Riemannian and penalty methods are equivalent, the kernel of the equivalence being the tangent projection, and the combination of the tangent projection is the normal-bundle vanishing. (tangent-projection equivalence of manifold Riemannian and penalty methods)

**中文。** 定义：非凸约束为流形型约束结构。猜想：光滑紧流形上黎曼法与罚法等价；且等价的核为切投影；且切投影的组合为法丛消没。（流形黎曼罚法切投影等价）
