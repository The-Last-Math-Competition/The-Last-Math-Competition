# 00000006676

**OT3-06 ◆** — source: 208_最优传输II_离散.md

**English.** Definition: The auction algorithm with bid-price iteration. Conjecture: The convergence rounds are quasi-polynomial with exponent given by the precision ratio; the window of the ratio is logarithmic; and the logarithmic window of the precision ratio is locked by the bid increment. (quasi-polynomial auction convergence rounds)

**中文。** 定义：拍卖算法为投标价格迭代。猜想：收敛轮数为拟多项式且指数由精度比给出；且比的窗为对数；且精度比的对数窗由投标增量锁定。（拍卖收敛拟多项式轮数）
