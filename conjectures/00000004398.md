# 00000004398

**English.** Definition: A forcing graph is a graph whose single density determines all edge statistics. Conjecture: The forcing graphs are exactly the two classes of complete graphs and complete bipartite graphs, the classification is closed, and every candidate outside the two classes can be excluded by a flag algebra certificate. (complete two-class classification of forcing graphs)

**中文。** 定义：forcing 图指其单点密度决定全部边统计的图。猜想：forcing 图恰为完全图与完全二部图两类,分类封闭,两类之外的候选均可由旗代数证书排除。（forcing 二类完全分类）
