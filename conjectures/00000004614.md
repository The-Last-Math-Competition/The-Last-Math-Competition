**English.** Definition: The tangent cone is the limiting cone at a singularity. Conjecture: The tangent cone of a minimal surface is unique in dimension at most three, uniqueness in dimension four is confirmed via the gap theorem, and the first non-unique tangent cone appears in explicit constructions in dimension at least five. (dimension-four formula of tangent-cone uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：切锥指奇点处切极限锥。猜想：维数不超过三的极小曲面切锥唯一,维数四的唯一性经间隙定理确证,首个非唯一切锥出现在维数不低于五的显式构造。（切锥唯一性四维公式）
