# 00000001474

**English.** Definition: The algebraic degree of an SDP (the algebraic degree of its optimal solutions). Conjecture: The algebraic degree of a fixed-rank r generalized SDP is an explicit binomial in the dimension and rank (a closed form; partially known, the general-rank form is open).

**中文。** 定义：SDP 的代数度(最优解的代数次数)。猜想：固定秩 r 的广义 SDP 的代数度为关于维数与秩的显式二项式(闭式,部分已知,一般秩形式开放)。
