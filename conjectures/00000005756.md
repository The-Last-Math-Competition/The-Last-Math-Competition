**English.** Definition: Tilting equivalence is the categorical equivalence inverting the characteristic. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the faithfulness of the tilting functor, one after perfection, and the equivalence is complete on the adic sheaves. (faithful perfectoid unit-constant tilting equivalence)
