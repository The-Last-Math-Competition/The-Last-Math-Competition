# 00000002691

**English.** Definition: Lattice-valued extension of the Arzelà condition: equicontinuous families on complete lattices. Conjecture: Compactness of lattice-valued function families: Dedekind completeness of the value lattice is equivalent to pointwise-bounded implying uniformly compact for equicontinuous families; the equivalence fails for nondistributive lattices, with failure degree the count of N₅ sublattices. (lattice-valued Arzela compactness)

**中文。** 定义：Arzelà 条件的格值推广：完备格上的等度连续族。猜想：格值函数族的紧性：值格的 Dedekind 完备性等价于等度连续族的逐点有界即一致紧；等价性在格非分配时失效且失效度由 N_5 子格计数。（格值 Arzela 紧性）
