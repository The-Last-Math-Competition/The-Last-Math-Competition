# 00000000268

**English.** Definition: The Airy function Ai is the distinguished solution of a second-order differential equation. Conjecture: For every nonzero algebraic α, the value Ai(α) is transcendental; and the proof path is differential Galois theory. (Airy transcendence at algebraic points)

**中文。** 定义：Airy 函数 Ai 指满足二阶方程的特解。猜想：对每个非零代数数 α 值 Ai(α) 恒为超越数；且证明路径为微分 Galois 理论。（Airy 代数点超越）
