# 00000008513

**SDG-13 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: Mahler equation denotes Galois group of functional equation, its coefficient field on of f(qz) expression is Mahler–Galois group. Conjecture: transcendental of solution of Mahler equation by solution of dimension characterizes: dim G = n² of Mahler–Galois group is transcendental and not can product. (Mahler–Galois transcendental)

**中文。** 定义：Mahler 方程指 f(qz) 形式的函数方程，其系数域上的 Galois 群为 Mahler–Galois 群。猜想：Mahler 方程的解的超越性由 Mahler–Galois 群的维数刻画：dim G = n² 的解为超越且不可积。（Mahler–Galois 超越判据）
