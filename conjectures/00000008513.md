# 00000008513

**English.** Definition: A Mahler equation is a functional equation of the form f(qz), whose Galois group over the coefficient field is the Mahler-Galois group. Conjecture: The transcendence of solutions of Mahler equations is characterized by the dimension of the Mahler-Galois group: solutions with dim G = n^2 are transcendental and nonintegrable. (Mahler-Galois transcendence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Mahler 方程指 f(qz) 形式的函数方程，其系数域上的 Galois 群为 Mahler–Galois 群。猜想：Mahler 方程的解的超越性由 Mahler–Galois 群的维数刻画：dim G = n² 的解为超越且不可积。（Mahler–Galois 超越判据）
