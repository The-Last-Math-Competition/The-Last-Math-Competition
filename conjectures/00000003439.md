**English.** Definition: The Stechkin lemma: the lemma of sparse approximation. Conjecture: The optimal decay of Stechkin: the error of s-term approximation is the block sum of sorted coefficients, with the optimum of blocks binary partitions. (Stechkin binary block sparse optimal)

**中文。** 定义：Stechkin 引理：稀疏逼近的引理。猜想：Stechkin 的最优衰减：s-项逼近的误差为排序系数的块和且块的最优为二进制分块。（Stechkin 二进制块稀疏最优）
