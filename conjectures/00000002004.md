# 00000002004

**English.** Definition: The Einstein–Vlasov system is Einstein's equation with a collisionless matter field. Conjecture: The classification of spherically symmetric stationary solutions is given explicitly by the phase-portrait separatrices of anisotropic ODEs: the stable/unstable/no-stationary parameter-space boundaries are explicit curves.

**中文。** 定义：Einstein–Vlasov 系统指无碰撞物质场下的 Einstein 方程。猜想：球对称稳态解的分类由各向异性 ODE 的相图分界线显式:稳定/不稳定/无稳态的参数空间分界为显式曲线。
