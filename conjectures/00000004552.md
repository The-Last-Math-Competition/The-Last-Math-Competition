**TR3-12 ★** — source: 137_数论V_超越方法.md

**English.** Definition: The p-adic logarithm is the Iwasawa logarithm on the p-adic field. Conjecture: The p-adic lower-bound constant of the logarithm inequality is an explicit combination of the residue degree and the ramification index, and in the unramified case it is of the same type as the complex case and exact. (ramification constant of the p-adic logarithm)

**中文。** 定义：p 进对数指 p 进域上的 Iwasawa 对数。猜想：对数不等式的 p 进下界常数为剩余度与分歧指数的显式组合,非分歧情形与复情形同型且精确。（p 进对数分歧常数）
