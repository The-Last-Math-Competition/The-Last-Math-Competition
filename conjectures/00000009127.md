# 00000009127

**English.** Definition: Marstrand's theorem characterizes projection dimensions. Conjecture: The projection dimension in typical directions is always the smaller of the dimension and the target dimension; and the exceptional direction set has strictly smaller dimension. (Marstrand projection dimension law)

**中文。** 定义：Marstrand 定理刻画投影维数。猜想：典型方向的投影维数恒为维数与目标维数的较小者；且例外的方向集维数严格小。（Marstrand 投影维数律）
