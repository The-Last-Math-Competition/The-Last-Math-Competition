**English.** Definition: Posterior concentration is the contraction speed of the posterior distribution. Conjecture: The concentration rate of Gaussian models is the negative one-half power of time, the constant is given by the information, and it matches the asymptotics of maximum likelihood. (information constant of Gaussian posterior concentration)

**中文。** 定义：后验集中指后验分布的收缩速度。猜想：高斯模型的集中速率为时间的负二分之一次幂,常数由信息量给出且匹配极大似然的渐近。（高斯后验集中信息常数）
