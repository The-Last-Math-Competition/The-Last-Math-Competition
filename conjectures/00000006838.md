**English.** Definition: The invariants of rank-two systems with the vector-field side. Conjecture: The invariance of rank-two vector fields holds, the combination of the invariance being cyclic splitting. (cyclic-splitting rank-two invariance)

**中文。** 定义：秩二系统的不变：向量场侧。猜想：秩二的不变：秩二向量场的不变且变的组合为循环裂解。（秩二系统循环裂解秩二向量场不变组合不变裂解循环向量场秩二裂解）
