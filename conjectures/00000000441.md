# 00000000441

**English.** Definition: Multisymmetric functions form the ring of symmetric functions in several sets of variables. Conjecture: The transition matrix of the multisymmetric monomial basis is triangular with determinant a power of two; and the exponent is a function of the number of variable sets. (multisymmetric basis triangular power determinant)

**中文。** 定义：多重对称函数为多组变量的对称函数环。猜想：多重单项式基的转移矩阵为三角且行列式为二的幂；且幂指数为变量组数的函数。（多重基三角幂行列式）
