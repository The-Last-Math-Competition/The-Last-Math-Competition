# 00000001105

**LT-05 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The multiplication structure constants h^k_{ij} of the canonical basis of Soergel bimodules are given by matrix entries of the self-dual Soergel bilinear form. Conjecture: For fixed rank r, the growth of h^k_{ij} in the weight norm is controlled by an r-variate homogeneous polynomial: |h^k_{ij}| ≤ C_r·(i+j)^r, and this bound is attained on the rank-2 dihedral group.

**中文。** 定义：Soergel 双胞胎的典范基的乘法结构常数 h^k_{ij} 由 self-dual Soergel 双线性型的矩阵元素给出。猜想：固定秩 r 时 h^k_{ij} 关于权范数的增长被 r 元齐次多项式控制:|h^k_{ij}| ≤ C_r·(i+j)^{r},且该界在秩 2 二面体群上达到。
