**English.** Definition: The Poincare series is the generating function of group-element counts. Conjecture: For exponential-growth groups the convergence radius and the growth rate are reciprocal identities, the lattice case has double pole order one, and the conversion is universal with no correction. (reciprocal identity of series radii)
