**English.** Definition: A fringe subtree of a random recursive tree is a repeated occurrence of an isomorphism class. Conjecture: The occurrence count of a fixed fringe is (n·p_f) + O(n^{1/2}) (with mean corrected by log n/H); the CLT variance is explicit.

**中文。** 定义：随机递归树的 fringe 子树指同构类的重复出现。猜想：固定 fringe 的出现次数为 (n· p_f)+O(n^{1/2})(均值为 log n/H 修正);CLT 的方差显式。
