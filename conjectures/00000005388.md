**English.** Definition: The torus height is the height function on multiplicative groups. Conjecture: Its conversion is always the explicit spectrum of the exponent matrix; for spectral moduli above one it converges, and the conversion constant is of logarithmic spectral type. (spectral-logarithmic conversion of torus heights)

**中文。** 定义：环面高度指乘法群上的高度函数。猜想：其换算恒为指数矩阵的谱显式；谱模超一时收敛且换算常数为谱的对数型。（环面高度谱对数换算）
