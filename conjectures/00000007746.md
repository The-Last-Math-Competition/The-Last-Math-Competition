**English.** Definition: Multiple free Wigner integrals I_k^free(f) are defined by the chaos decomposition of free Brownian motion, with the Nourdin-Speicher fourth-moment theorem deciding normality. Conjecture: For radial multiple integrals (mixed integrals over several independent free Brownian motions), the normality criterion needs and needs only moments up to the sixth; and the difference of the sixth and fourth moments is always nonnegative, its minimum given by the explicit integral of the Cholesky-type kernel f(x) = x^{k-2}. (sixth-moment criterion for radial free integrals)

**中文。** 定义：多重 Wigner 积分 I_k^free(f) 由自由 Brown 运动的混沌分解定义，其第四矩定理（Nourdin–Speicher）给出正态性由四矩判别。猜想：径向多重积分（对多个独立自由 Brown 运动的混合积分）的正态性判据需要且只需要到第六矩；且第六矩与第四矩之差的符号恒非负，其最小值由 Cholesky 型核 f(x)=x^{k-2} 的显式积分给出。
