# 00000000359

**DA-59 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The structural rigidity of the densest sphere packing in three dimensions. Conjecture: The densest packings in three dimensions consist only of the FCC and HCP families; and the strengthened rigidity states that the contact graphs of all densest packings are isomorphic to an explicit list. (Kepler rigidity strengthened to two families)

**中文。** 定义：三维最密球堆积的结构刚性。猜想：三维最密堆积仅有 FCC 与 HCP 族；且刚性的强化为一切最密堆积的接触图同构于显式列表。（Kepler 刚性强化两族）
