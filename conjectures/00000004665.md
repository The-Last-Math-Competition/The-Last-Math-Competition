**English.** Definition: The spectrum is the weighted eigenvalue sequence of the monotone operator of a singularity. Conjecture: The spectrum is centrally symmetric if and only if the singularity is weighted homogeneous and Gorenstein, and the criterion is complete and fully verified on the low-codimension table. (weighted Gorenstein criterion of spectral symmetry)

**中文。** 定义：谱指奇点单调算子的加权本征值列。猜想：谱的中心对称当且仅当奇点为加权齐次且 Gorenstein,判据完全且在低余维表全部验证。（谱对称加权 Gorenstein 判据）
