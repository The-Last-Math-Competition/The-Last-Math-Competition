# 00000003875

**English.** Definition: The partition function Z_{N,β}(V) is the normalizing constant of the β-gas with potential V. Conjecture: The coefficient a_g(V,β) in the 1/N² expansion of log Z is a degree-2g polynomial in β times a β-independent potential functional, with leading polynomial coefficient β^{2g}/(2g)!. (free energy beta polynomiality)

**中文。** 定义：配分函数 Z_{N,β}(V) 指位势 V 的 β-气的归一化常数。猜想：logZ 的 1/N² 展开系数 a_g(V,β) 为 β 的 2g 次多项式乘以与 β 无关的位势泛函,多项式首项系数为 β^{2g}/(2g)!。（自由能 β-多项式性）
