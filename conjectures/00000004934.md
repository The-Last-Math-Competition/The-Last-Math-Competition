# 00000004934

**English.** Definition: A Smith-Toda spectrum is a finite spectrum with prescribed cofiber structure. Conjecture: At the prime two, the nonexistence of the fifth-type spectrum makes the failure first-example dimension explicit, for sufficiently large primes all types exist, and the boundary is exact. (failure boundary dimension of Smith-Toda)

**中文。** 定义：Smith–Toda 谱指具指定余核结构的有限谱。猜想：素二时第五型谱不存在即失败首例维数显式,素数充分大时各型全部存在,分界精确。（Smith–Toda 失败分界维数）
