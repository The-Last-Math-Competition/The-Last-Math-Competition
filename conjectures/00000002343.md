# 00000002343

**NG-23 ★** — source: 63_数值代数几何.md

**English.** Conjecture: The expected number of coincidences (crossings) of eigenvalue curves of the random matrix pencil A + λB is (π/4)·n (a real/complex distribution ratio); the expectation is computed from the collision probability of the real/complex eigenvalue proportions (1/2 each).

**中文。** 猜想：随机矩阵束 A+λ B 的特征曲线的重合(交叉)数的期望为 (π/4)· n(实/复型分布比);期望由特征值的实/复比例(1/2 each)的碰撞概率计算。
