# 00000001275

**CG-75 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The nonnegative spectrum of an incidence matrix is the set of inner products of row vectors. Conjecture: The nonnegative spectrum has a complete characterization by self-similar sets when the rank is at most two; and higher-rank spectra are finite unions of closed intervals. (low-rank nonnegative spectrum characterization)

**中文。** 定义：关联矩阵的非负谱为行向量内积的取值集。猜想：非负谱为自相似集的完全刻画当秩不超过二；且高秩的谱为闭区间的有限并。（低秩非负谱刻画）
