# 00000007866

**RAM-26 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The fairness deviation of rounded random walks: the maximal deviation D_n = sup|S_n mod 1 - n/2| of the fractional parts of a positive-integer-step random walk in the unit interval. Conjecture: For independent uniform rounding errors with steps a_1,...,a_n one has D_n = O((log n)^{1/2}), asymptotically tight on 2-adic equidistributed steps a_i = 2^i; and any transcendental-step sequence gives D_n = O(1) with constant 1/4, consistent with the star discrepancy of Kronecker sequences. (rounded walk 2-adic extremality)

**中文。** 定义：随机游走取整的公平性偏差：正整数步长的随机游走在单位区间内取整（取小数部分）的最大偏差 D_n=sup|S_n mod 1 - n/2|。猜想：对步长 {a_1,…,a_n} 的独立均匀取整误差，D_n = O((log n)^{1/2})，且该界在 2-adic 型等分布步长（a_i=2^i）上渐近取等；任何超越步长的序列使 D_n = O(1)，常数为 1/4，与 Kronecker 序列的星偏差一致。
