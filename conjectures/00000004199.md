# 00000004199

**English.** Definition: The gluing construction produces new solutions by patching known ones, and the neck is the thin neck of the patching. Conjecture: The asymptotic constant of the gluing energy deficit equals the interface tension times the neck length, and the shortest neck length is the explicit value 2*epsilon*log(1/epsilon). (shortest neck length for gluing)

**中文。** 定义：粘合构造指由已知解拼接新解,neck 指拼接细颈。猜想：粘合能量亏损的渐近常数等于界面张力乘 neck 长度,neck 最短长度为 2ε log(1/ε) 型显式值。（粘合 neck 最短长度）
