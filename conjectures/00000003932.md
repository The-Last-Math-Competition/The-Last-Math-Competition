# 00000003932

**English.** Definition: The augmentation number is the number of augmentations of a DGA to a given field. Conjecture: For a fixed Legendrian, the augmentation number over the prime field F_p is a quasi-polynomial in p whose period divides the crossing number of that Legendrian. (quasi-polynomial period of augmentations)

**中文。** 定义：augmentation 数指 DGA 到给定域的增强个数。猜想：固定 Legendrian 在素域 F_p 上的 augmentation 数为 p 的拟多项式,其周期整除该 Legendrian 的穿越数。（augmentation 拟多项式周期）
