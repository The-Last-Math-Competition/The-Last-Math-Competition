**English.** Definition: Interpolation factors as the intermediate type of amalgamated products. Conjecture: The interpolation-domain spectrum is continuous in the one-parameter family; the continuous spectrum is interval structure; and the endpoints of the interval structure are the parameter extrema. (interval-structured interpolation spectrum)
