# 00000009111

**English.** Definition: Favard length: the average length functional of projections. Conjecture: The Favard length of planar sets satisfies Vitushkin-type delta decay; and the decay exponent is polynomial in the complexity of the set. (Favard Vitushkin decay)

**中文。** 定义：Favard 长度为投影的平均长度泛函。猜想：平面集合的 Favard 长度满足 Vitushkin 型 δ-衰减；且衰减指数为集合复杂度的多项式。（Favard Vitushkin 衰减）
