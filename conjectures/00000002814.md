**LD-14 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: The LDP of Brownian increments: modulus-of-continuity constraints. Conjecture: The interaction of the increment LDP rate with the modulus function: the rate function develops kinks at the modulus-of-continuity layer, and the kink positions are the affine envelope of the optimal modulus. (increment rate kinks)

**中文。** 定义：Brown 运动的增点的 LDP：模连续性约束。猜想：增点 LDP 的速率与模函数的交互：速率函数在模连续性层出现折点且折点位置为最优模的仿射包络。（增点速率折点）
