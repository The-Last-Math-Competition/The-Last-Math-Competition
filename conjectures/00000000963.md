# 00000000963

**English.** Definition: Free entropy χ(a₁, …, aₙ) (Voiculescu). Conjecture: Superadditivity of free entropy for free group generators: χ(a, b) = χ(a) + χ(b) for relation-free pairs (superadditivity).

**中文。** 定义：自由熵 χ(a₁, …, aₙ)(Voiculescu)。猜想：自由群生成元的自由熵超加性:χ(a, b) = χ(a) + χ(b) 对无关系对(超加性)。
