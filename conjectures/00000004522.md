**English.** Definition: Belief propagation is the local-message fixed-point iteration. Conjecture: On random regular graphs its convergence threshold is exactly the condition that the spectral radius of the Jacobian reaches one, and the threshold is computed from the explicit non-backtracking spectrum of the graph. (BP non-backtracking spectral convergence threshold)

**中文。** 定义：置信传播指局部消息不动点迭代。猜想：随机正则图上其收敛阈值恰为雅可比谱半径达一的条件,阈值由图的显式非回谱计算。（BP 非回谱收敛阈值）
