# 00000009056

**English.** Definition: Simons-type equations: the structural equations of the Jacobi operator. Conjecture: The conversion of the spectrum with iterations of coordinate functions is always an explicit polynomial; and the degree of the polynomial is twice the index. (Simons spectral iteration polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：Simons 型方程为 Jacobi 算子的结构方程。猜想：谱与坐标函数的迭代的换算恒为显式多项式；且多项式的次数为指标二。（Simons 谱迭代多项式）
