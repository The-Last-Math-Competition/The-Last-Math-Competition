**English.** Definition: Simons type equation is structure equation of Jacobi operator. Conjecture: spectrum and of iteration of coordinates function is always explicit polynomial; and -fold number of polynomial is index two. (Simons spectrum iteration polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：Simons 型方程为 Jacobi 算子的结构方程。猜想：谱与坐标函数的迭代的换算恒为显式多项式；且多项式的次数为指标二。（Simons 谱迭代多项式）
