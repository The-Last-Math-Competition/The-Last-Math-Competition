**MP-26 ★** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: Metaplectic ice is the metaplectic deformation of the six-vertex model. Conjecture: Its partition function equals a polynomial times a power of two (the exponent being the explicit formula p(N,r), a cyclic sum of Z/r colorings of clique configurations); and the polynomial degree has an exact formula.

**中文。** 定义：metaplectic Ice 指六顶点的 metaplectic 变形。猜想：其配分函数 = 多项式 × 2 的幂(幂次 = 显式公式:p(N,r) 由团构型的 {Z}/r 着色的循环和);多项式次数有精确公式。
