**English.** Definition: The Morava stabilizer group is the arithmetic subgroup of the automorphism group of the height-n formal group and its cohomology. Conjecture: The dimension sequence of the cohomology of the stabilizer group of height two and prime three is an explicit sequence of period 8, with the period given by the commutator structure of the braid-type automorphisms; the nilpotent ideal of the cohomology ring is completely characterized by the braid-action invariants, and the invariant ring is finitely generated. (periodicity of Morava stabilizer cohomology)

**中文。** 定义：Morava 稳定群：高度 n 形式群自同构群的算术子群及其上同调。猜想：高度二素三的稳定群上同调的维数序列为周期 8 的显式序列且周期由辫型自同构的换位结构给出；上同调环的幂零理想由辫作用的不变量完全刻画且不变量环为有限生成。（Morava 稳定群上同调周期）
