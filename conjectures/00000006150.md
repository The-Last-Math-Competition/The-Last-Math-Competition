**English.** Definition: The tangent complex and the derived type are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two derived schemes with homotopy-equivalent tangent complexes but different global types, and the separation is realized by an explicit non-formal pair with the same tangent but different cohomology. (same tangent-different non-formal derived-type separation)

**中文。** 定义：切复形与导出型指两层。猜想：存在两导出概形的切复形同伦等价而整体型不同,分离由同切异上同调的显式非形式对实现。（切复形同-导出型异非形式分离）
