# 00000008289

**English.** Definition: The Zeno index of asynchronous (event-triggered) filtering: the Zeno threshold zeno_th on the limit of triggering intervals (infinitely many triggers). Conjecture: zeno_th (the minimal event-triggering interval) is positive (a positive interval law) with optimal constant one quarter of the noise intensity (a 1/4 interval law); the optimal trade-off curve between mean-square error and trigger rate of triggered filtering is a Pareto front (a Pareto front law); and the explicit parametrization of the front (sweeping the threshold parameter) is the power-law error-rate trade-off E ~ r^{-1/2} (a half-power trade-off law). (event-triggered Zeno interval Pareto half-power)

**中文。** 定义：异步滤波（事件触发）的 Zeno 指标：触发间隔的极限（无穷触发）的 Zeno 阈 zeno_th。猜想：zeno_th（事件触发的最小间隔）> 0（正间隔律）且最小间隔的最优常数为噪声强度的 1/4（1/4 间隔律）；且触发滤波的均方误差与触发率的最优权衡曲线为 Pareto 前沿（Pareto 前沿律）；前沿的显式（阈值参数的扫描）的参数化为误差-率的幂律 trade-off：E ~ r^{-1/2} 的半幂（半幂 trade-off 律）。
