# 00000008017

**English.** Definition: The unique continuation of free boundaries from boundary observation: the uniquely determined class UF of free boundaries from Cauchy data on an open subset of the boundary. Conjecture: The free boundary of the Bernoulli problem is uniquely determined by the Cauchy data of the solution u on an open boundary subset (local rigidity); the quantitative form of determination (stability) is log-Lipschitz type: d(FB_1,FB_2) <= C (data difference)^theta with theta = 1/2 up to a logarithmic correction; and the optimal stability exponent theta* is explicit of type (1/2) 1/(1+n), the counterexample construction being a resonance sequence of the frequency function with resonant frequencies the half-integral sequence starting at 3/2. (free boundary Cauchy log-Lipschitz stability)

**中文。** 定义：边界观测的自由边界的唯一延拓：Cauchy 数据在边界开集上的自由边界的唯一确定类 UF。猜想：Bernoulli 问题的自由边界由解 u 在边界开集的 Cauchy 数据唯一确定（局部刚性）；确定的定量形式（稳定性）为 log-Lipschitz 型：d(FB₁,FB₂) ≤ C·(data 差)^{θ}，θ=1/2 的对数修正；最优稳定指数 θ* = 1/2·1/(1+n) 型显式（反例的构造为频率函数的共振序列，共振频率为 3/2 的半整序列）。
