# 00000008862

**CGB-02 ★** — source: 281_组合博弈论II.md

**English.** Definition: Nimber arithmetic: star equals 0 xor star, additive cancellation of star-n. Conjecture: Nimber multiplication converges through the tower of finite fields F_{2^{2^k}} (a tower law); the tower limit (n to infinity) is the Fock of nimbers (a limit law); the arithmetic of the limit gives explicit nimber squares (a square law); and the application yields a closed formula for nimber products xy (a formula law). (nimber tower limit square formula)

**中文。** 定义：Nimber 运算：*=0⊕*、*n 的加法消去。猜想：Nimber 的乘法的收敛（有限域 F_{2^{2^k}} 的塔）的（塔律）；且塔的极限（n→∞ 的 Fock）的（极限律）；极限的算术（Nimber 平方的显式）的（平方律）；平方的应用（Nim 乘法的 (xy) 的封闭公式）的（公式律）。
