# 00000009640

**English.** Definition: The shift center and the word problem of automorphism groups of SFTs: the correspondence between group-theoretic complexity and entropy computability. Conjecture: The word problem of the automorphism group of a mixing SFT is solvable if and only if the entropy of the system is computable; and the polynomial hierarchy of word problem complexity corresponds one-to-one with the speed of algebraic approximation of Perron numbers. (word problem entropy computability correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：SFT 的移位中心与自同构群的字问题：群论复杂度与熵可计算性的对应。猜想：混合 SFT 的自同构群的字问题可解当且仅当该系统的熵可计算；字问题复杂度的多项式层级与 Perron 数的代数逼近速度一一对应。（字问题-熵可计算对应）
