**English.** Definition: The explicit dimension wir_dim of the set W(psi) of approximable numbers for the approximation function psi in Wirsing's problem. Conjecture: wir_dim (the dimension formula of the Wirsing series) is the conversion 2 minus the convergence exponent of psi (a conversion formula), valid on the monotone domain (a monotone domain formula law); the minimal failure for nonmonotone (oscillating psi) is a two-scale oscillating psi (a two-scale example); the failure is corrected by the pilot function of the Borel-Cantelli guiding set (a pilot function correction law); and the phase transition of the corrected dimension spectrum sits at the threshold 1/2 (a half-threshold jump law). (Wirsing two-scale pilot half-threshold)

**中文。** 定义：Wirsing 问题的显式常：逼近函数 ψ 的可逼近数集 W(ψ) 的维数公式 wir_dim。猜想：wir_dim（Wirsing 級数的维数）= 2-ψ 的收敛指数的换算（换算公式）在单调域成立（单调域公式律）；且非单调（振动 ψ）的失效的最小例为两尺度振动的 ψ（双尺度例）；失效的修正为 Borel–Cantelli 的引导集的引导函数（引导函数修正律）；修正后的维数谱的跳变（相变）的位置为 1/2 的阈值（半阈值跳变律）。
