# 00000009652

**English.** Definition: The spectral measure process of the corner-minor sequence of Wigner matrices: the joint evolution of empirical spectral measures of growing principal minors. Conjecture: The sequence of spectral measures of normalized minors converges to an explicit infinite-dimensional diffusion process; the process is the solution of an SDE with deterministic drift plus free Brownian perturbation, and the Markov property completely characterizes it. (corner minor limit process)

**中文。** 定义：Wigner 矩阵的角 minors 序列的谱测度过程：递增主子式的经验谱测度的联合演化。猜想：归一化 minors 的谱测度序列收敛到显式无穷维扩散过程；过程为确定性漂移加自由布朗扰动的 SDE 解且马尔可夫性完全刻画。（角 minors 极限过程）
