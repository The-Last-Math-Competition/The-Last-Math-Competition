# 00000008903

**English.** Definition: Homomorphism deviation: the departure of counting vectors of truncated subfamilies from limit values. Conjecture: Small homomorphism deviation is always equivalent to subgraph density uniformity; and the constant of the equivalence is given by the minimal truncated family size controlled by the deviation. (homomorphism deviation subgraph uniformity equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：同态偏差指截断子族的计数向量与极限值的偏离。猜想：同态偏差的小性与子图密度均匀性恒等价；且等价的常数由偏差控制的最小截断族规模给出。（同态偏差-子图均匀等价）
