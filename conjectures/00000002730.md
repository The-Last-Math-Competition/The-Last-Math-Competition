# 00000002730

**English.** Definition: Fine grading of face enumeration: refined counting by flag labels. Conjecture: Invertibility of the Euler transform of fine-graded (flag) f-vectors: the transformation matrix is unitriangular with inverse entries the ordered partition numbers; invertibility separates the layered spectrum from topological invariants. (fine f-vector invertibility)

**中文。** 定义：face enumeration 的段结构（fine grading）：由旗标签的细分计数。猜想：细粒度 f-向量（分层 f-向量）的 Euler 变换的可逆性：变换矩阵为单位三角且逆的元素为有序分拆数；可逆性保证分层谱与拓扑不变量的分离。（分层 f-向量可逆）
