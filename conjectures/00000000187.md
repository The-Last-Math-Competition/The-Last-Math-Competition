**English.** Definition: Catalan's constant G = β(2) = Σ (−1)^n/(2n+1)². Conjecture: The Q-linear relation space between G and Γ(1/4)^4/π has dimension exactly 1, generated solely by the elliptic period relation; the transcendence of G is equivalent to the existence of a non-algebraic independent component beyond the vanishing of that relation space. (Catalan period relation dimension)
