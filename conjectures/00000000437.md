**English.** Definition: The shuffle algebra Sh(V) (tensor algebra with shuffle product). Conjecture: The n-th graded dimension of the Lie-primitive space of Sh(V) for dim V = 2 is (1/n)Σ_{d|n} μ(d)·2^{n/d} (Witt's formula), and its parity is determined explicitly by whether n is a power of 2.

**中文。** 定义：shuffle 代数 Sh(V)(张量代数带 shuffle 积)。猜想：Sh(V) 的李代数本原空间的第 n 个分次维数在 dim V = 2 时为 (1/n)Σ_{d|n} μ(d)·2^{n/d}(Witt 公式),其奇偶性由 n 是否为 2 的幂显式给出。
