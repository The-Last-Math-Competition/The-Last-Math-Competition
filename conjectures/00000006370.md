# 00000006370

**QG2-30 △** — source: 197_量子群II.md

**English.** Definition: The braid solution and the representation category are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two quantum groups with the same braid solution but different representation categories, and the separation is realized by an explicit tensor-structure pair with the same braid but different rank. (same braid-different tensor-category separation)

**中文。** 定义：辫解与表示范畴指两层。猜想：存在两量子的辫解相同而表示范畴不同,分离由同辫异秩的显式张量结构对实现。（辫解同-范畴异张量分离）
