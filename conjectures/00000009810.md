# 00000009810

**DDG-20 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Sabitov's theorem: volumes of flexible polyhedra are radical values of edge lengths; bounds on the radical degree. Conjecture: The upper bound on the degree of the minimal polynomial satisfied by the volume of a flexible polyhedron can be improved to a linear function of the vertex count; the linear bound is attained by nested realizations of the Bricard octahedron. (Sabitov radical degree linear bound)

**中文。** 定义：Sabitov 定理：挠曲多面体体积为边长的根式值；根式次数的上界。猜想：挠曲多面体的体积满足的极小多项式次数的上界可改进为顶点数的线性函数；线性界由 Bricard 八面体的嵌套实现达到。（Sabitov 根式次数线性界）
