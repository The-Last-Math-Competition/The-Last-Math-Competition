**English.** Definition: Walls with the wall structure of stability conditions, wall crossing. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit descent of wall thickness and the embedded-wall thickness is given by the mass deficit; the deficit spectrum is a finite point set; and wall intersections are finite points, with point count two thirds of the wall count. (mass-deficit spectrum conversion of wall thickness)
