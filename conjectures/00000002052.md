# 00000002052

**English.** Definition: The arity gap is the jump in the number of variables needed for identities. Conjecture: Beyond the semilattice gap = 1 (Willard), the arity gap of the modular lattice variety is always 2 (the exact value); decided by a McKenzie-type classification table of polynomial identities.
