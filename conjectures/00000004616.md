# 00000004616

**English.** Definition: The Simons cone is the minimal unstable quadratic cone in eight-dimensional Euclidean space. Conjecture: The first negative value of its second-variation spectrum is explicit and scale invariant, the second eigenvalue is exactly zero, and the cone is the critical boundary of stability. (negative value and zero edge of the Simons spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Simons 锥指八维欧氏空间中的极小不稳定二次锥。猜想：其第二变分谱的第一负值显式且缩放不变,第二特征值恰为零,锥为稳定性临界边缘。（Simons 谱负值零边缘）
