# 00000009687

**English.** Definition: Values of the Bessel function J_nu at nonzero algebraic points: Siegel proved transcendence for rational nonintegral nu. Conjecture: The ratio J_{1/3}(alpha)/J_{-1/3}(alpha) is transcendental for every nonzero algebraic alpha, and the ratio equals the elliptic period ratio of the corresponding elliptic curve; the transcendence degree of the ratio is equivalent to the CM criterion of the curve. (Bessel ratio transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：Bessel 函数 J_ν 在非零代数点的值：Siegel 证明有理非整 ν 时超越。猜想：比值 J_{1/3}(α)/J_{−1/3}(α) 对一切非零代数 α 超越且该比值等于对应椭圆曲线的椭圆周期比；比的超越度与曲线的 CM 判据等价。（Bessel 比值超越性）
