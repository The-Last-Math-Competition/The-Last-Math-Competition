# 00000002386

**English.** Definition: The fiber-topological dimension structure of product fractals. Conjecture: For the product of the Cantor set with the Sierpinski gasket, the exceptional directions form a countable set; and the counting spectrum is the tangent lattice. (product fiber exceptional countable directions)

**中文。** 定义：乘积分形的纤维拓扑维数结构。猜想：Cantor 乘 Sierpinski 的例外方向为可数集；且计数谱为切线格。（乘积纤维例外可数方向）
