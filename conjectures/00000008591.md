# 00000008591

**MLC-01 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: The Pinsker constant is the exact minimax constant for estimating differentiable functionals. Conjecture: The minimax rate of density estimation on d-dimensional Sobolev balls of smoothness s is n^{-2s/(2s+d)} with the exact L^2 risk constant attained by the Pinsker estimator; and the ratio of the minimax constant for set estimation to the Gaussian width of the set is always 1. (Pinsker constant width ratio)

**中文。** 定义：Pinsker 常数指可微泛函估计的精确 minimax 常数。猜想：d 维 Sobolev 球 s 上密度估计的 minimax 速率为 n^{−2s/(2s+d)} 且 L² 风险精确常数由 Pinsker 估计子取得；集合估计的 minimax 常数与集合的 Gaussian 宽度之比恒为 1。（Pinsker 常数宽度比）
