# 00000008591

**MLC-01 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Pinsker constant denotes exact minimax constant of differentiable functional estimate. Conjecture: minimax rate of d dimension Sobolev ball s on density estimate is n^{−2s/(2s+d)} and L² risk exact constant by Pinsker estimate attains minimax constant of; set estimate and Gaussian width the ratio of of set is always 1. (Pinsker constant width ratio)

**中文。** 定义：Pinsker 常数指可微泛函估计的精确 minimax 常数。猜想：d 维 Sobolev 球 s 上密度估计的 minimax 速率为 n^{−2s/(2s+d)} 且 L² 风险精确常数由 Pinsker 估计子取得；集合估计的 minimax 常数与集合的 Gaussian 宽度之比恒为 1。（Pinsker 常数宽度比）
