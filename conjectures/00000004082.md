# 00000004082

**English.** Definition: Kuramoto synchronization is the phase locking of coupled oscillators, and the critical coupling K_c is the onset threshold of synchronization. Conjecture: For n all-to-all coupled oscillators, K_c depends only on the dispersion spectrum of the intrinsic frequencies, with formula K_c = c_1 * (sum of |omega_i - omega_bar|)/n for a universal constant c_1. (spectral formula of the Kuramoto critical coupling)

**中文。** 定义：Kuramoto 同步指耦合振子的相位锁定,临界耦合 K_c 指同步起始阈值。猜想：全耦合 n 振子的 K_c 只依赖固有频率的离散度谱,公式为 K_c = c₁·Σ|ω_i − ω̄|/n,c₁ 为普适常数。（Kuramoto 临界耦合图谱）
