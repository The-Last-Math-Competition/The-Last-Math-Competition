# 00000007751

**English.** Definition: For a rational map f of degree d>=2 over a field K, a preperiodic point has finite orbit; the Morton-Silverman uniform boundedness conjecture asserts a uniform bound on preperiodic points of degree d maps of P^N(K), depending only on N and d, with MSD(N,d) the minimal such bound. Conjecture: For N=1, d=2, K=Q, MSD(1,2)=9, attained by a unique map; more generally MSD(N,d) is dominated by the exponential expression (d^{2N+2}-1)/(d-1). (MSD exact value and domination)

**中文。** 定义：域 K 上次数 d≥2 有理映射 f 的预周期点指轨道有限的点；Morton–Silverman 一致有界猜想断言 P^N(K) 上 deg d 映射的预周期点数有一致上界，只依赖 N 与 d。定义 MSD(N,d) 为该上界的最小值。猜想：对 N=1、d=2、K=Q，MSD(1,2)=9 且恰由唯一的映射取到；更一般地 MSD(N,d) 的值由指数式 (d^{2N+2}-1)/(d-1) 型支配项给出。
