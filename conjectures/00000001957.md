# 00000001957

**LG-27 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: The 2-torsion cohomology count of SL_n(Z). Conjecture: The 2-part of H^i(SL_n(Z);Z)_tors grows as 2^{c·n²} as n → ∞ (with c an explicit base from Borel's conjecture); the asymptotics are controlled by the limit of stable cohomology.

**中文。** 定义：SL_n(Z) 的 2-挠上同调计数。猜想：H^i(SL_n(Z);Z)_tors 的 2-部分在 n→∞ 的增长为 2^{c\cdot n^2}(c 为 Borel 猜想的显式底数);渐近由稳定上同调的极限控制。
