# 00000005397

**English.** Definition: Density is the topological denseness of an orbit, with the criterion for torus translations. Conjecture: The translation orbit on the two-dimensional torus is dense if and only if the components of the frequency vector are linearly independent over the rationals, with independence decided by the absence of two integral linear relations; in the critical (semidense) case the closure is a finite union of subtori, with the number of unions equal to the deficiency of the independence rank; and dense orbits are uniquely ergodic. (integer-independence criterion of torus density)

**中文。** 定义：稠密性：轨道在空间中的拓扑稠密；环面平移的判据。猜想：二维环面平移轨道稠密当且仅当频率向量的分量在有理数上线性无关且无关性的判定由两个整线性关系的不存在给出；临界（半稠密）情形的闭包为有限并环面且并的个数等于无关秩的亏数；稠密轨道的分布为唯一遍历。（环面稠密整数无关判据）
