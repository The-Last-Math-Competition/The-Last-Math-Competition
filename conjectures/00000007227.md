# 00000007227

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic side of the trace formula with the identity of spectral sums. Conjecture: The identity of elliptic terms holds, the class numbers of the geometric side expanding as orbital integrals. (orbital-integral elliptic trace identity)

**中文。** 定义：迹公式的算术侧：谱和的恒等。猜想：椭圆项的恒等：几何侧的类数且数的展开为轨道积分。（迹公式轨道积分椭圆几何侧类数恒等展开）
