# 00000005289

**English.** Definition: Magnetic annihilation is the small-scale dissipation process of magnetic fields. Conjecture: Its rate constant is an explicit power product of exponent and diffusion, verified at classical flow values, and the rate grows logarithmically with the magnetic Reynolds number. (exponent-power rate of magnetic annihilation)

**中文。** 定义：磁湮灭指磁场的小尺度耗散过程。猜想：其速率常数为指数与扩散的显式幂积,经典流数值验证且速率随磁雷诺数对数增长。（磁湮灭指数幂积速率）
