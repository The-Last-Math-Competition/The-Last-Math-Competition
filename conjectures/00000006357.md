**QG2-17 ◆** — source: 197_量子群II.md

**English.** Definition: Gauge equivalence is equivalence under twist transformation. Conjecture: Equivalence is resolved by cohomology classes and the classes are complete; the generators of completeness are two-dimensional; and the two-dimensional generators are the components of the link. (cohomology resolution of gauge orbits)

**中文。** 定义：规范等价为扭变换下的等价。猜想：等价由上同调类分辨且类完全；且完全的生成元为二维；且二维生成元为联结子的分量。（规范轨道上同调分辨）
