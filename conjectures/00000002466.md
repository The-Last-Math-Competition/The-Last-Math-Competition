**English.** Definition: Universal subgroup chains of zero-dimensional Polish group actions. Conjecture: The realizable density spectrum of the intermediate classes is a reciprocal lattice; and the combination of the lattice is the orbit-count ratio. (universal chain orbit-ratio reciprocal density spectrum)
