# 00000002251

**NV-21 ★** — source: 60_亚纯函数与NevanlinnaII.md

**English.** Definition: The difference operator Δ_c f = f(z+c) − f(z). Conjecture: The kernel of Δ_c has dimension 1, and the spectrum of Δ_c on meromorphic function spaces (differences of difference polynomials) is explicit of the {2 sin(kc/2)} type (difference spectrum).

**中文。** 定义：差分算子 Δ_c f = f(z+c) − f(z)。猜想：Δ_c 的核的维数为 1,且 Δ_c 在亚纯函数空间的谱(差分多项式的差分)为 {2 sin(kc/2)} 型显式(差分谱)。
