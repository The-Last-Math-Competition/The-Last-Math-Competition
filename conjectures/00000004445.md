**English.** Definition: The tails of LPP are the left and right tail probabilities of the passage time. Conjecture: The leading Fredholm-expansion constants of the right and left tails are the explicit separated laws 2/3 and 1/3, and the constants are universal and exact. (2/3-1/3 separation of LPP tail leading terms)

**中文。** 定义：LPP 的尾指通过时间的左右尾概率。猜想：其 Fredholm 展开首项常数在右尾与左尾分别为 2/3 与 1/3 的显式分离律,常数普适且精确。（LPP 尾首项 2/3-1/3 分离）
