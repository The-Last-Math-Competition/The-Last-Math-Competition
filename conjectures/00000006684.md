**OT3-14 ★** — source: 208_最优传输II_离散.md

**English.** Definition: Weighting with the volume functional of weight functions. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit gradient of the volume functional and the gradient is the measure difference; the second-order form is positive semidefinite; and the convexity of the functional is preserved under weight lifting. (measure-difference gradient conversion of volume functionals)

**中文。** 定义：加权：权函数的体积泛函。猜想：换算常数为体积泛函的梯度显式且梯度为测度差；二阶半正；泛函的凸性在权提升下保持。（体积泛函梯度测度差换算）
