# 00000007842

**English.** Definition: The blanket time constant theta(G) of a simple random walk on a graph: the ratio E[T_blanket]/E[T_cov] of blanket time to cover time. Conjecture: For all n-vertex graphs, theta(G) lies in [4/3, 4] with both ends asymptotically attainable: the lower end 4/3 by complete graphs and cycles, the upper end 4 by lollipop-type graphs; and theta concentrates to log 3/2 on tree-like graphs, the critical branching degree separating the two behaviors being 2. (blanket-to-cover ratio spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：图上简单随机游走的覆盖时间 T_cov 与命中时间最大值 h_max 的比值序列定义为毯时间常数 θ(G)=E[T_{blanket}]/E[T_cov]。猜想：对一切 n 点图，θ(G) ∈ [4/3, 4]，两端均渐近可达；下端 4/3 由完全图与圈取得，上端 4 由棒棒糖型图取得；且 θ 的分布集中在类树图上为 log 3/2，将两类行为分离的临界分支度为 2。
