**MON-11 ★** — source: 280_单调算子与算子分裂.md

**English.** Definition: Iteration of asymptotically nonexpansive maps. Conjecture: Weak convergence of asymptotically nonexpansive sequences always holds in Hilbert space; and strong convergence exceptions are delimited by the demiclosedness principle. (asymptotically nonexpansive demiclosed boundary)

**中文。** 定义：渐近非扩张映射的迭代。猜想：渐近非扩张序列的弱收敛恒成立于 Hilbert 空间；强收敛的例外由半闭原理分界。（渐近非扩张半闭分界）
