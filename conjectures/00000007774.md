**English.** Definition: The bifurcation measure mu_bif on parameter space is supported on points where the multiplier of some periodic point crosses modulus 1. Conjecture: The mu_bif-mass of the bifurcation locus {c: (f_c^n)'(x) = root of unity} decays as O(theta^n) in the decorrelation coefficient theta of f, and the optimal decay exponent theta is exactly exp(-h_top), the topological entropy of the real quadratic family at the central parameter, establishing an explicit identity between bifurcation measure and entropy. (bifurcation-entropy identity)

**中文。** 定义：分岔测度的局部谱：参数空间上使某周期点乘子模过 1 的点的分岔测度 μ_bif。猜想：μ_bif 在二次族的分岔轨迹 {c: (f_c^n)′(x)=单位根} 上的限制的质量衰减为 O(θ^n)（θ 为 f 的去相关系数），且该衰减最优指数 θ 恰为 exp(-h_top)，h_top 为实二次族在中心参数的拓扑熵，这建立了分岔测度与熵的显式等式。
