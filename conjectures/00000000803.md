# 00000000803

**English.** Definition: The Steklov eigenvalues σ_k(Ω) are the spectrum of the boundary Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator. Conjecture: Among bodies of revolution, a maximizer of σ₃ exists and is not a sphere; its generating curve is given by the solution of an explicit two-point boundary value problem, and the shape approaches a dumbbell of two tangent spheres.

**中文。** 定义：Steklov 特征值 σ_k(Ω) 为边界到内 Dirichlet-to-Neumann 算子的谱。猜想：在旋转体限制下,σ₃ 的最大化形状存在且非球面,其母线由显式的两点边值问题的解给出;该形状逼近两球粘合的哑铃型。
