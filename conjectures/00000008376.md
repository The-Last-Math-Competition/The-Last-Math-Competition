# 00000008376

**TRP-26 ★** — source: 264_热带几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The McKay correspondence for the S_n action in tropical symmetry: the McKay graph mck_trop of the S_n action on cell structures (permutations). Conjecture: mck_trop (the McKay graph of the S_n action on orbits of points) is the cycle graph C_n (a cycle graph law); the orbit count (count of fixed points) is the Stirling combination k! S(n,k) (a Stirling combination law); the maximum stabilizer (the largest) is S_{n-1} (a one-point stabilizer law); and the stabilizer spectrum (all subgroups appearing) is complete for Young subgroups (a Young completeness law). (tropical McKay cycle Stirling Young stabilizer)

**中文。** 定义：热带对称性的 Sn 的作用的 McKay 对应：胞腔结构的 Sn 作用（置换）的 McKay 图 mck_trop。猜想：mck_trop（Sn 作用（点的轨道）的 McKay 图）为循环图 C_n（循环图律）；且轨道计数（不动点的计数）= k!·S(n,k) 的 Stirling 组合（Stirling 组合律）；轨道的稳定子（稳定子群的分布）的极大（最大稳定子）为 S_{n-1}（单点稳定律）；稳定子的谱（一切子群的出现）为 Young 子群的完全（Young 完全律）。
