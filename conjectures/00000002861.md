# 00000002861

**English.** Definition: The Atiyah–Singer index theorem: the analytic index of an elliptic operator equals its topological index. Conjecture: Localization rigidity of the index: the j-th term of the heat-kernel expansion of the index density is a local invariant polynomial, with unique normalized leading term; uniqueness follows from the index-side extension of Hopf-type theorems. (index density local uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：Atiyah–Singer 指标定理：椭圆算子的解析指标等于拓扑指标。猜想：指标的局部化刚性：指标密度的热核展开的第 j 项为局部不变多项式且多项式的规范化的首项唯一；唯一性由 Hopf 型定理的指标侧推广给出。（指标密度局部唯一）
