# 00000002565

**English.** Definition: Size-depth trade-offs for linear circuits. Conjecture: The optimal trade-off curve for matrix multiplication satisfies a lower bound of loglog S · D type; and the constant of the bound is a logarithmic window. (size-depth trade-off logarithmic bound)

**中文。** 定义：线性电路的尺寸-深度折衷。猜想：矩阵乘的最优折衷曲线满足 loglogS·D 的下界；且界的常数为对数窗。（尺寸深度折衷对数界）
