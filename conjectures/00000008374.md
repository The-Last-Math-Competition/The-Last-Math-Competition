# 00000008374

**TRP-24 ★** — source: 264_热带几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The crystallization of tropical intersection numbers: the conversion cryst_int of intersections of crystals (limits of snapshots). Conjecture: The multiple of cryst_int (crystalline intersection equal to classical intersection) is 1 (a unit multiple law); the correction of crystalline boundaries (boundary effects) is a Borel-Moore term (a B-M correction law); the coherence of the signs of correction terms is the coherence of flows (a flow coherence law); and the obstruction to associativity of higher crystalline intersections (three-fold and above) is a Maslov correction (a Maslov obstruction law), whose vanishing domain is the coherent lattice (a coherent lattice vanishing law). (crystalline intersection Borel-Moore Maslov lattice)

**中文。** 定义：热带相交数的结晶化：相交积的结晶（快照的极限）的换算 cryst_int。猜想：cryst_int（结晶相交 = 经典相交）的倍数为 1（单位倍数律）；且结晶的边缘（边界效应）的修正为 Borel–Moore 的项（B-M 修正律）；修正项的符号（±）的相干为流向的相干（流向相干律）；结晶的高次（3-交以上）的结合律的障碍为 Maslov 修正（Maslov 障碍律），障碍的消没域为协调格点（协调格点消没域律）。
