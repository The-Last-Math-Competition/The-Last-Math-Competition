**English.** Definition: The Willmore functional is the integral of the squared mean curvature, and the Willmore spectrum is its sequence of constrained minima. Conjecture: In the sphere, the ratio of the second smallest to the smallest value of the Willmore spectrum converges to the explicit constant 2, realized by two parallel spheres. (Willmore two-value ratio constant 2)

**中文。** 定义：Willmore 泛函指平均曲率平方积分,Willmore 谱指其约束极小值序列。猜想：球中 Willmore 谱第二极小值与首极小值之比收敛于显式常数 2,由两平行球实现。（Willmore 二值比常数 2）
