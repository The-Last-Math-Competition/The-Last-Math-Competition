# 00000001618

**SP-18 ★** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: The transfer operator L_φ of the hyperbolic rational map z ↦ z²+c acts on spaces of analytic functions. Conjecture: For c = −1 (basilica), the second eigenvalue of L_φ has modulus 1/√2; for c = −2 (Chebyshev) it is 0 (degenerate); for general hyperbolic parameters, the second eigenvalue is the reciprocal of the modulus of the derivative at a repelling fixed point of z²+c.

**中文。** 定义：双曲有理映射 z↦z²+c 的传递算子 L_φ 作用於解析函数空间。猜想：对 c=−1(basilica),L_φ 的第二特征值模长为 1/√2;c=−2(Chebyshev) 时为 0(退化);一般双曲参数下第二特征值为 z²+c 的 repelling 不动点导数模的倒数。
