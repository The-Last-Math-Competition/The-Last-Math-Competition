# 00000008114

**NAG-04 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Gelfand-Kirillov dimension spectrum GK_spec(A_1) of irreducible representations of the Smith algebra (the worst-case A_1-type hypertorus quantization). Conjecture: The GK spectrum is the integer set {2,3,4,...} together with the sequence of fractions 2 + 1/n (a completion of the Coutinho-Levasseur-Tremblay type theorem); the representation at each fractional point 2 + 1/n is unique (a unique irreducible at each point); the family of representations with GK dimension 3 is parametrized by Whittaker-type two-parameter data (a two-parameter Whittaker law); and the rigidity of the monodromy group of the analytic continuation of holomorphic representations with GK = 2 is SL(2,Z) (an SL_2 monodromy rigidity). (Smith GK fractional Whittaker monodromy)

**中文。** 定义：Smith 代数（worstcase A₁ 型超环面量子化）的既约表示的 Gelfand–Kirillov 维数谱 GK_spec(A₁)。猜想：GK 谱为整数集 {2,3,4,…} 加上分数 2+1/n 的序列（Coutinho–Levasseur–Tremblay 型定理的完备化）；且分数点 2+1/n 的表示的唯一性（每点唯一既约）；整数 3 的表示族的参数化由 Whittaker 型双参数（双参数 Whittaker 律）；GK=2 的全纯表示的解析延拓的单值群的刚性为 SL(2,Z)（SL₂ 单值刚性）。
