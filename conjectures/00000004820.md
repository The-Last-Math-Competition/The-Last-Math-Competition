**MF3-10 △** — source: 146_分析数论_模形式与L函数III.md

**English.** Definition: Zero statistics and coefficient means are the two statistical layers of L-functions. Conjecture: There exist two families with identical asymptotic coefficient means but different zero statistics limits, and the separation is realized by an explicit Artin family and its twists. (same coefficient means-zero statistics separation)

**中文。** 定义：零点统计与系数均值指 L 函数的两层统计。猜想：存在两族的系数均值渐近相同而零点统计极限不同,分离由显式阿廷族与其扭转实现。（系数均值同-零点统计分离）
