# 00000000393

**English.** Definition: The even index i(ξ) concerns approximation by continued fractions with all partial quotients even. Conjecture: The even index of ln 3 is 1 (i.e. ln 3 is approximable at rate 1/q² by even partial quotients, in parallel with the known identity for ln 2).

**中文。** 定义：奇偶指数 i(ξ)(部分商全为偶数的逼近)。猜想：ln 3 的奇偶指数为 1(即 ln 3 可被偶部分商以 1/q² 速度逼近,与 ln 2 的已知恒等式平行)。
