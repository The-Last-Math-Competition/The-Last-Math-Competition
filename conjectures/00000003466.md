# 00000003466

**GA3-06 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The triangle core is the core structure formed by triangle contractions of a graph, carrying the coloring constraints of the original. Conjecture: The triangle core is a coloring invariant of chordal graphs: the chromatic number is preserved under core contraction; for general graphs the chromatic loss under core contraction is at most one, with the complete classification of losses a subfamily of odd wheels. (triangle core invariant)

**中文。** 定义：三角核为图中三角形收缩构成的核结构，其染色承载原图的染色约束。猜想：三角核为弦图的染色不变量：色数在核收缩下保持；一般图核收缩损失的色数至多一且损失的完全分类为奇轮族的子族。（三角核不变量）
