# 00000005099

**English.** Definition: The generic platform counts the linear degeneracy types of general rank-two local systems on the base curve. Conjecture: The degeneracy-type count is an explicit product of partition-type modular coefficients given by the count of squarefree discriminants; the main order is two thirds of the discriminant degree; and the density of superdegenerate types is zero with logarithmic-square decay. (modular-coefficient product of spectral-cover degeneracy types)

**中文。** 定义：泛平台：底曲线上一般秩二局部系统的线性退化类型计数。猜想：退化类型计数为分割型的模系数显式积且由既约判别式计数给出；主阶为判别式次数的三分之二；超退化的密度为零且衰减为对数平方型。（谱覆盖退化类型模系数积）
