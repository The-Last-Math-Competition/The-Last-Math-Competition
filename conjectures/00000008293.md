# 00000008293

**English.** Definition: The classification index of monotone Lagrangians in Hurwitz-Seidel spaces: the count mono_count of Hamiltonian equivalence classes of monotone Lagrangian tori. Conjecture: mono_count (equivalence classes of monotone Lagrangians) over the class of CP^n is the Chekanov-Schlenk dichotomy with 2 classes (a dichotomy law); the completeness of classification for n at least 5 (full classification) is a finite family (a finite family law) with explicit cardinality upper bound 2^{n-1} (a two-power upper bound); and the complete set of classifying invariants is the triple of discriminant, Maslov class, and Stiefel-Whitney classes (a triple completeness law). (monotone Lagrangian Chekanov-Schlenk finite family)

**中文。** 定义：Hurwitz–Seidel 空间的单调拉格朗日的分类指标：单调 Lagrangian 环面 L(T^{n}) 的 Hamilton 等价类的计数 mono_count。猜想：mono_count（单调 Lagrangian 的等价类）在 {CP^n} 的类数为 2 的 Chekanov–Schlenk 二分（二分律）；且 n ≥ 5 的分类的完备（全分类）为有限族（有限族律），族的基数的显式上界为 2^{n-1}（二幂上界）；分类的不变量（区分的完全组）为 Discr 判别式 + Maslov 类 + Stiefel–Whitney 的三重组（三重组完全律）。
