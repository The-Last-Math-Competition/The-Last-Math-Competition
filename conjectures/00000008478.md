# 00000008478

**English.** Definition: The maximization of the Ruelle zeta function over system metrics: extremal measures for zero positions. Conjecture: The extremal measure is unique and supported on finitely many periodic orbits (a unique support law); examples of uniqueness breaking are given by symmetric functions on the full shift (a full shift example); the extremal measure spectrum is discrete; and the extremal count does not exceed the main term of the periodic orbit count. (unique support of Ruelle extremal measures)

**中文。** 定义：Ruelle zeta 函数关于系统度量的极大化问题：零点位置的极值度量。猜想：极值度量唯一且支撑在有限周期轨道上（唯一支撑律）；唯一性破缺的例子由全移位上的对称函数给出（全移位例）；极值测度谱为离散集；极值的计数不超过周期轨道计数的主项。
