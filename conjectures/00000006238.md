**English.** Definition: The closed-formula lattice is the lattice-operation structure, the closed-open duality of subobjects. Conjecture: The closed-open dual difference is the open complement, the combination of completeness being the complement involution; the square of the involution is the identity, with fixed points the clopen elements; and the multiplicative deficit of the double-Boolean structure is the failure of distributivity, with failure degree 1. (complement-involution closed-open lattice operations)

**中文。** 定义：闭公式格：格运算结构（子对象的闭开对偶）。猜想：闭开对偶差为开补且完全的组合为补对合；对合的平方为恒等且不动点为既闭又开的元素；格的双布尔结构的乘性亏损为分配律的破坏且破坏度为 1。（格运算补对合闭开对偶差）
