**English.** Definition: Optimal transport is the transport problem minimizing cost. Conjecture: The metric continuous flow is the classical distance for Gaussian families, and the kernel of the distance is the geometric mean of covariances. (covariance geometric-mean Gaussian distance of optimal transport)

**中文。** 定义：最优传输指代价极小的运输问题。猜想：度规的连续流在高斯族时为经典距离；且距离的核为协方差几何平均。（最优传输协方差几何平均高斯距离）
