**English.** Definition: optimal transport denotes transport problem of cost minimal. Conjecture: continuous flow in Gaussian family when of metric tensor is classical distance; and kernel of distance is covariance geometric mean. (optimal transport covariance geometric mean Gaussian distance)

**中文。** 定义：最优传输指代价极小的运输问题。猜想：度规的连续流在高斯族时为经典距离；且距离的核为协方差几何平均。（最优传输协方差几何平均高斯距离）
