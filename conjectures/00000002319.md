# 00000002319

**English.** Definition: A Tarski monster is an infinite group all of whose proper subgroups are cyclic. Conjecture: The smallest prime p* for which a rank-p Tarski monster exists satisfies p* ≤ 10^{100} (an explicit upper bound); the construction comes from an optimization of the Novikov–Adian large-prime method.

**中文。** 定义：Tarski 怪群指一切真子群皆循环的无穷群。猜想：秩 p 的 Tarski 怪存在的最小素数 p* ≤ 10^{100}(显式上界);构造由 Novikov–Adian 的大素数方法的优化。
