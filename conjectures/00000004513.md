# 00000004513

**English.** Definition: The BHH theorem is the leading-order law that the length of the random Euclidean traveling salesman is proportional to sqrt(n). Conjecture: The second-term constant of the optimal length exists, is negative, and equals an explicit combination of the random matching and minimum spanning tree constants. (combined expression of the TSP second-term constant)

**中文。** 定义：BHH 定理指随机欧氏旅行商长度与根号 n 成正比的首项律。猜想：最优长度的第二项常数存在、为负,且等于随机匹配与最小生成树常数的显式组合。（TSP 第二项常数组合式）
