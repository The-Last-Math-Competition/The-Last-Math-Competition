# 00000002717

**SC-07 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Random simplicial complexes (the Linial–Meshulam model Y_d(n,p)): d-faces added with probability p to the (d−1)-skeleton. Conjecture: The homology-vanishing critical window of Y_d: p_c = c_d·log n/n with window width a logarithmic power correction of n^{−1}; within the window, the expected Betti numbers are tight constants, and the critical distribution converges to an explicit Poisson-type law. (Yd homology critical window)

**中文。** 定义：随机单纯复形（Linial–Meshulam 模型 Y_d(n,p)）：d 维面以概率 p 加入 (d−1) 骨架。猜想：Y_d 的同调消没临界窗：p_c = c_d log n/n 且窗口宽度为 n^{−1} 的对数幂修正；窗口内的 Betti 数的期望为紧致常数且临界分布收敛到显式 Poisson 型律。（Yd 同调临界窗）
