# 00000008298

**English.** Definition: The analytic continuation of SFT invariants of dual Hamiltonians: the analytic domain sft_dom of the potential function of symplectic field theory. Conjecture: sft_dom (the analytic domain of the SFT potential) is the explicit annulus {r < |lambda| < 1} in the lambda variable (an explicit annulus law); the inner and outer radii of the annulus are explicit as extremal actions of periodic orbits (an action annulus law); the singularities outside the annulus (poles of the potential) are located at the action values of periodic orbits (a pole-action correspondence law); and the residues of the singularities are the index-multiplicities of the orbits (an index residue law). (SFT annulus action poles index residues)

**中文。** 定义：对偶 Hamilton 的 SFT 的不变量的解析延拓：Symplectic field theory 的势函数的解析域 sft_dom。猜想：sft_dom（SFT 势的解析域）为 lambda 变量的圆环 {r<|λ|<1} 的显式环（显式环律）；且环的内外半径的显式为周期轨的作用量的极值（作用量环律）；环外的奇点（势的极点）的位置为周期轨的作用量值（极点-作用量对应律）；奇点的残数为轨的指标的重数化（指标残数律）。
