# 00000001457

**CX-07 △** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The Banach–Mazur distance d_BM(Δ_n, ℓ₁ⁿ) between the simplex Δ_n and the cross-polytope ℓ₁ⁿ. Conjecture: d_BM = n−1 (the lower end of the known gap n−1 ≤ d_BM ≤ n); that is, the cross-polytope is exactly the symmetric body asymptotically farthest from the simplex, at distance n−1.

**中文。** 定义：单纯形 Δ_n 与交叉多形体 ℓ₁ⁿ 的 Banach–Mazur 距离 d_BM(Δ_n, ℓ₁ⁿ)。猜想：d_BM = n−1(已知 n−1≤d_BM≤n 的缺口内取下端);即交叉多面体恰为到单纯形的渐近最远对称体,距 n−1。
