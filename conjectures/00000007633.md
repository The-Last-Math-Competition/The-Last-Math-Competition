# 00000007633

**English.** Definition: The regularity window of the method of characteristics is the time interval in which the solution remains a diffeomorphism. Conjecture: The endpoints of the regularity window are given explicitly by the reciprocal of the maximal initial gradient; and the extension of the solution outside the window is a shock. (explicit characteristic-window endpoints)

**中文。** 定义：特征线法的正则窗指解保持为微分同胚的时间段。猜想：正则窗的端点恰由初值梯度极大值的倒数显式给出；窗外解的延拓为激波。（特征正则窗显式端点）
