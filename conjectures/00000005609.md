# 00000005609

**EL2-19 ★** — source: 172_精确线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: The covering-packing gap is the ratio of covering radius to packing radius. Conjecture: The gap of three-dimensional lattices is always the explicit expression of the cell diameter ratio, minimal at the body-centered cubic; the minimum is the optimal covering-packing ratio, an algebraic number; and the spectrum of the gap in three dimensions is a closed interval with endpoints attained by the cubic lattice. (body-centered cubic minimal gap diameter ratio)

**中文。** 定义：覆盖-堆积间隙：覆盖半径与堆积半径之比。猜想：三维格的间隙恒为胞腔直径比的显式式且体心立方处极小；极小值为覆盖堆积的最优比且比值为代数数；间隙的谱在三维为闭区间且端点由立方格达到。（体心立方极小间隙直径比）
