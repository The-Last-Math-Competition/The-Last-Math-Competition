# 00000008177

**English.** Definition: The certificate length of APR-CL and ECPP for polynomial-time primality proving: the minimal length len_ECPP(p) of ECPP certificates (elliptic curve primality chains). Conjecture: len_ECPP(p) = O(log p) (a logarithmic certificate law); the constant of minimal realization is at most 5 (a five-constant bound); the logarithm of the density of the worst curves of ECPP (primes requiring the most steps) is -log log p times 1/2 (a half-logarithmic sparsity law); and the crossover of the complexities of ECPP and APR-CL (the performance balance point) is p of order 10^{500} (a five-hundred-digit crossover law). (ECPP logarithmic certificate crossover law)

**中文。** 定义：多项式时间素性判定的 APR-CL 与 ECPP 的证书长度：ECPP 证书（椭圆曲线原基链）的最小长度 len_ECPP(p)。猜想：len_ECPP(p) = O(log p)（对数证书律）；且证书的最小实现的常数 ≤ 5（五常数界）；ECPP 的最坏曲线（需最大步数的素数）的密度的对数为 -log log p·(1/2)（半对数稀疏律）；ECPP 与 APR-CL 的复杂度的交叉（性能平衡点）为 p ≈ 10^{500}（五百位交叉律）。
