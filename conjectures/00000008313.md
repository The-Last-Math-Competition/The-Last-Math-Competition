**English.** Definition: The Frechet-Lie deformation of quantum homology: the convergence domain qc_dom of the deformation of the quantum cup product (deformation parameter q). Conjecture: qc_dom (the convergence domain of quantum homology) is the convergence half-plane of the Novikov variable (a Novikov half-plane law); the boundary of the domain (the convergence boundary) is the accumulation limit of exponentially small actions (an accumulation limit law); the higher-order deformations (higher Maslov products) converge uniformly (a uniform Maslov convergence law); and the minimum convergence modulus (radius) is the normalized 1 (a unit minimum law). (quantum cup Novikov half-plane uniform Maslov)

**中文。** 定义：量子同调的 Fréchet–Lie 的形变：量子 cup 的形变（形变量 q）的收敛域 qc_dom。猜想：qc_dom（量子同调的收敛域）= Novikov 变量的收敛半平面（Novikov 半平面律）；且域的边界（收敛边界）为指数小作用量的累积极限（累积极限律）；形变的高阶（高阶 Maslov 乘法的收敛）的一致收敛（一致 Maslov 收敛律）；收敛的模（收敛半径）的最小为 1（单位最小律）的正规化。
