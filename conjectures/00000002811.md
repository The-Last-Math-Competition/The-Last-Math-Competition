# 00000002811

**English.** Definition: The moderate deviation principle: the scale between CLT and LDP. Conjecture: The adaptive window of moderate deviations: the critical α for window width n^{-α} is 1/2, beyond which the limit jumps from CLT to LDP, and the transitional distribution at the jump is a rescaled family of Edgeworth series. (moderate deviation critical window)

**中文。** 定义：中等偏差原理：CLT 与 LDP 之间的尺度。猜想：中等偏差的自适应窗：窗宽 n 的负 α 次幂的临界 α 为 1/2 且越界后极限从 CLT 跳到 LDP 且跳跃的过渡分布为 Edgeworth 级数的缩放族。（中等偏差临界窗）
