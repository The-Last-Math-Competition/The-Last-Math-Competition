# 00000004630

**MS2-30 △** — source: 139_几何测度IV_极小曲面系统.md

**English.** Definition: Jacobi fields and the deformation moduli space are the two structures of a minimal surface. Conjecture: There exist two minimal surfaces whose Jacobi field solution spaces coincide while their deformation moduli spaces are not isomorphic, and the separation is realized by pairs of isometric non-congruent surfaces. (Jacobi field-moduli space separation)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 场与形变模空间指极小曲面的两个结构。猜想：存在两极小曲面的 Jacobi 场解空间相同而形变模空间不同构,分离由等距不合同的曲面对实现。（Jacobi 场-模空间分离）
