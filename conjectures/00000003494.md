# 00000003494

**English.** Definition: The Atiyah class: the obstruction to holomorphic connections of vector bundles. Conjecture: The vanishing of the Atiyah class: the holomorphicity criterion of the metric connection is the vanishing of the Atiyah class, with the geometry of vanishing Hermite flatness. (Atiyah vanishing Hermite flat criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Atiyah 类：向量丛的全纯联络的障碍。猜想：Atiyah 类的消没：丛的度量联络的全纯性判据为 Atiyah 类零且零的几何为 Hermite 平坦。（Atiyah 消没 Hermite 平坦判据）
