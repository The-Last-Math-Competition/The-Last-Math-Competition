# 00000007320

**RT4-20 ★** — source: 229_随机张量.md

**English.** Definition: The dual norm of tensors with the dual of the spectral norm. Conjecture: The extreme points of the dual ball of the spectral norm are orthogonal families; and the face count of the dual ball is exactly the exponential lattice-point count of orthogonal-family bases. (orthogonal-family spectral dual-ball extremes)

**中文。** 定义：张量的对偶范数：谱范数的对偶。猜想：对偶的极值：谱范数的对偶球且球的极点为正交族。(张量谱范数正交族对偶球极点)；且对偶球的面数恰为正交族基的指数格点数。
