**English.** Definition: Dimensions of rigid measures: agreement of the dimension of Patterson measures with capacity. Conjecture: The lower bound on the Hausdorff dimension deficit of non-rigid measures: the dimension difference is at least the gap constant of the critical exponent, with the constant explicitly given by the spectral gap of the diffusion entropy of the group. (non-rigid dimension deficit lower bound)
