**ET3-25 ◆** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: L-functions as Frobenius products of characters. Conjecture: The weight-increase law of order derivatives is derivative order times weight one; and the exactness of the law is given by the Grothendieck-Ogg-Shafarevich formula. (L-derivative weight-increase law)

**中文。** 定义：L 函数为特征标的 Frobenius 积。猜想：阶导数的权增律为导数阶乘以权一；且增律的精确性由 Grothendieck–Ogg–Shafarevich 公式给出。（L 导数权增律）
