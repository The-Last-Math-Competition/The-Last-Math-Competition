**English.** Definition: The Iwasawa main branch is the Z_p-main component of the class group tower. Conjecture: An explicit family of real fields with rank jumps is given by the prime-discriminant family of totally real cubic fields, and the jump points are given by the explicit criterion that p splits completely.
