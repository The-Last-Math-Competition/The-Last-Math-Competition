# 00000004145

**English.** Definition: A mouse is a fragment of an inner model with an iteration strategy, and the coarseness comparison is the layer-by-layer comparison construction of two mice. Conjecture: The number of termination steps of the coarseness comparison is linearly bounded in the input length, and the exponent 1 is optimal. (linear termination of mouse comparison)

**中文。** 定义：mouse 指带迭代战略的内模型片段,厚度比较指两 mouse 的逐层对照构造。猜想：厚度比较的终止步数为输入长度的线性界,指数 1 最优。（mouse 比较线性终止）
