**English.** Definition: The complexes C[i,j] in the surgery formula (Ozsváth–Szabó). Conjecture: The homology-dimension table of C consists of the convolution of Δ-coefficients plus correction terms whose sign pattern has period at most 2 (periodicity of the corrections).
