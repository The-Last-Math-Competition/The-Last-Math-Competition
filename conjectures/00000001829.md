# 00000001829

**English.** Definition: The Tate conjecture asserts that the algebraic closure of ℓ-adic cohomology together with Q_ℓ(1) spans the whole space. Conjecture: Tate holds unconditionally for K3 surfaces over Q; the first-instance criterion for Picard-number-1 K3 surfaces is given explicitly (via a chain of congruence criteria on the moduli space).

**中文。** 定义：Tate 猜想指 ℓ-进上同调的代数闭包与 ℚ_ℓ(1) 张成相等。猜想：Tate 对 Q 上 K3 曲面无条件成立;Picard 数 1 的 K3 的首例判据(通过模空间的同余判据链)显式给出。
