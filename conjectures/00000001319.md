# 00000001319

**CC-19 ◆** — source: 29_多复变与复几何.md

**English.** Definition: Kobayashi hyperbolicity means the infinitesimal Kobayashi metric between any two points is a distance. Conjecture: A very general hypersurface of degree d ≥ 2n+1 in CPⁿ is holomorphically hyperbolic, and the dimension of the exceptional hyperbolicity locus is n−1; the currently known results cover a neighborhood of d ≥ 2n².

**中文。** 定义：Kobayashi 双曲性指任意两点的无穷小 Kobayashi 度量均为距离。猜想：ℂℙⁿ 中次数 d≥2n+1 的非常一般超曲面为全纯双曲,且其双曲性例外层的维数为 n−1;当前已知 d≥2n² 邻域的结果。
