# 00000004923

**MD3-23 ★** — source: 149_随机过程III_马氏决策.md

**English.** Definition: The Lipschitz constant of the value function measures spatial regularity. Conjecture: The optimal Lipschitz constant of continuous-state MDPs is an explicit maximal composition of the reward and transition parameters, given by the worst coupling and attainable. (maximal composition of value function Lipschitz constants)

**中文。** 定义：值函数的利普希茨常数指空间正则性度量。猜想：连续状态 MDP 的最优利普希茨常数为奖励与转移参数的显式极大合成,由最坏耦合给出且可达。（值函数利普希茨极大合成）
