**English.** Definition: The sphere packing density is the supremal fraction of space filled by non-overlapping unit balls. Conjecture: The densest lattice packings in dimensions five, six, and seven are given by the D lattices; and the determination is settled through Voronoi cell volumes. (D-lattice Voronoi volume optimality in dimensions 5-7)

**中文。** 定义：球堆积密度为不重叠单位球的空间填充上确界。猜想：五六七维的最密格堆积由 D 格给出；且判定的核为 Voronoi 胞体积。（D 格 Voronoi 体积五六七维最密）
