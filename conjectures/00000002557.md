# 00000002557

**GZ-27 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Quantitative Lebesgue density theorem: the scale spectrum of the bad-point set. Conjecture: The bad-point set where density deviates from 1 satisfies a Carleson-type packing estimate; the critical exponent of the packing volume is 1, attained by the equidistribution of Voronoi flowers. (density theorem quantitative packing)

**中文。** 定义：Lebesgue 密度定理的量化：坏点集的尺度谱。猜想：密度偏离一的坏点集满足 Carleson 型装箱估计；装箱体积的临界指数为 1 且临界性由 Voronoi 花的等分布达到。（密度定理量化装箱）
