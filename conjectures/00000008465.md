# 00000008465

**English.** Definition: The Arf invariant is the mod-two quadratic form class of a knot. Conjecture: The Arf invariant is always determined by the evaluation of the Jones polynomial at i; and (2,n)-torus knots display a period-two sequence. (closed periodic spectrum of Arf invariants)

**中文。** 定义：Arf 不变量指结的模二二次型类。猜想：Arf 恒由 Jones 多项式在 i 处的赋值判定；且 (2,n)-环面结呈周期二序列。（Arf 周期谱封闭）
