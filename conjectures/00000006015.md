**English.** Definition: The twist formula is the criterion structure of invariant curves. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the Diophantine type of the rotation number, with no smooth curves beyond the irrationality-exponent bound; the Diophantine bound is of golden-type second root, given by the linear-approximation constant; and smoothness in the neighborhood of the bound is undetermined, the undetermined strip of exponentially small width. (Diophantine-type constant conversion of rotation numbers)

**中文。** 定义：扭转公式：不变曲线的判据结构。猜想：换算常数为旋转数的丢番图型显式且无理指数超界处无光滑曲线；丢番图指数的界为黄金型的 2 次根且界由线性逼近常数给出；指数在界的邻域的光滑性未定且未定域的宽度为指数小。（旋转数丢番图型常数换算）
