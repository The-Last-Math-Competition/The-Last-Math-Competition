# 00000007828

**English.** Definition: Tropical Hurwitz numbers H_trop_{g,mu} count tropical curves with ramification, weighted by the product of local indices. Conjecture: Tropical and classical Hurwitz numbers are linked by the integer multiplier relation H_{g,mu} = N! H_trop_{g,mu} c (N the total ramification), and the multiplier equals 1 for all (g,mu), with no genus correction; if a non-unit multiplier ever occurs, its first appearance requires g at least 3 and |mu| at least 10, and its correction factor is a power of 2. (tropical-classical Hurwitz multiplier)

**中文。** 定义：热带 Hurwitz 数 H_trop_{g,μ} 为热带曲线的带分歧计数，含多重性（局部指标积）。猜想： tropical 与经典 Hurwitz 数由 H_{g,μ}=N!·H_trop_{g,μ}·c 的整数乘子关系连接（N 为总分歧），且乘子在一切 (g,μ) 上为 1（无亏格修正）；若出现非 1 乘子，其首次出现的最小例子必为 g≥3 且 |μ|≥10，其修正因子为 2 的幂。
