# 00000002762

**PI-22 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: PI with exits: identities with outer automorphisms and weights (twisted PI). Conjecture: The basis theorem for twisted PI: twisted T-ideals are finitely based, with basis size polynomial in the twist-parameter space; minimality of the basis in the single-twist case is decided by the rank of the capacity matrix. (twisted PI finite basis)

**中文。** 定义：带出口的 PI：含外部自同构与权的恒等式（twisted PI）。猜想：twisted PI 的基定理：扭曲 T-理想由有限基生成且基的规模为扭曲参数空间的多项式；基的极小性在单扭曲时由容度矩阵的秩判定。（twisted PI 有限基）
