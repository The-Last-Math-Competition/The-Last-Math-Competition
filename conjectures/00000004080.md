# 00000004080

**English.** Definition: The normal-form coefficients are the remaining coefficients after nonresonant terms are eliminated by near-identity transformations. Conjecture: There exist two bifurcation points whose normal-form coefficients of every order coincide while the orbit topologies are not homeomorphic, with minimal phase-space dimension 3. (dimension of normal-form-topology separation)

**中文。** 定义：规范形系数指近恒同变换消去非共振项后的剩余系数。猜想：存在两分叉点其任意阶规范形系数全同而轨道拓扑不同胚,最小相空间维数为 3。（规范形-拓扑分离维数）
