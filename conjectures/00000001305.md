# 00000001305

**English.** Definition: The Kobayashi metric d_K. Conjecture: On the domain in C² whose complement is the lattice Z² (an Eisenstein complement), d_K degenerates in the horizontal direction but not in the vertical direction (mixed hyperbolicity).

**中文。** 定义：Kobayashi 度量 d_K。猜想：ℂ² 中补集为格点 ℤ² 的区域(Eisenstein 余集)的 d_K 在水平方向退化但垂直方向非退化(混合双曲性)。
