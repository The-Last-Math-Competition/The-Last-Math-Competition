# 00000009620

**English.** Definition: The automorphism group Aut(X,sigma) of a symbolic system is characterized by the Curtis-Hedlund-Lyndon theorem as sliding block codes; Boyle conjectured that group isomorphism determines the flow. Conjecture: Abstract isomorphism of the automorphism groups of two mixing SFTs implies entropy conjugacy of the systems; the commutator subgroup of Aut and the structure of the shift center completely determine the dimension group, giving a one-to-one group-flow correspondence. (Aut isomorphism rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：符号系统的自同构群 Aut(X,σ) 由 Curtis–Hedlund–Lyndon 定理刻画为滑动块代码；Boyle 提出群同构决定流的猜想。猜想：两个混合 SFT 的自同构群抽象同构蕴含两系统熵共轭；Aut 的换位子群与移位中心的结构完全决定维数群，群-流对应为一一对应。（Aut 同构刚性）
