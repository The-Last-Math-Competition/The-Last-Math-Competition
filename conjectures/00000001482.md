# 00000001482

**English.** Definition: Ruelle–Pollicott resonances are the discrete spectral points of transfer operators on suitable function spaces. Conjecture: On compact negatively curved manifolds, the width of the resonance-free strip for the geodesic flow equals the convex-dual slope of the pressure function at the critical exponent; the strip width is monotone nonincreasing in the volume and locked by the first resonance's imaginary part. (resonance gap pressure duality formula)

**中文。** 定义：Ruelle–Pollicott 共振指传递算子在适当函数空间上的离散谱点。猜想：紧负曲率流形上测地流的共振自由条带宽度等于压强函数在临界指数处的凸对偶斜率；且条带宽度随流形体积单调不增而由第一共振虚部锁定。（共振间隙压强对偶公式）
