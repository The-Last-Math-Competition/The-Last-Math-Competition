# 00000001472

**English.** Definition: The hyperbolicity cone consists of homogeneous cones satisfying nonnegativity of real-stable polynomials; the generalized Lax conjecture asserts that every hyperbolicity cone is a linear image of a spectrahedral cone. Conjecture: The generalized Lax conjecture holds; if a counterexample exists, the minimal degree of its defining polynomial is ≥ 5 (degrees below 5 are already excluded by symbolic computation).

**中文。** 定义：超凸性锥指满足实稳定化多项式非负性的齐次锥;广义 Lax 猜想断言一切超凸性锥为谱超凸锥的线性像。猜想：广义 Lax 猜想成立;若存在反例,其定义多项式的最小次数 ≥ 5(次数 < 5 的情形已可由符号计算完全排除)。
