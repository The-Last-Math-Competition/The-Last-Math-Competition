# 00000002900

**F3-10 ★** — source: 82_有限域上的几何III.md

**English.** Definition: The sum-product phenomenon over finite fields: lower bounds on |A+A| times |A.A|. Conjecture: The finite-field threshold of sum-product: the lower bound of the sum-product estimate for subgroups A is an improved exponent |A|^{13/9}, with limiting improved exponent 3/2; the limit is blocked by rigid constructions in matrix space. (finite sum-product exponent limit)

**中文。** 定义：有限域上的和积现象：|A+A|·|A·A| 的下界。猜想：和积的有限域阈值：子群 A 的和积积的下界为 |A| 的 13/9 次幂的改进指数且改进的极限指数为 3/2；极限由矩阵空间的刚体构造阻隔。（有限和积指数极限）
