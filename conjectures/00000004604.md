# 00000004604

**English.** Definition: An isoperimetric partition is the minimal-interface-area partition of a region into n chambers of equal volume. Conjecture: The leading asymptotic term of its area is an explicit correction of the single-ball constant by a fractional power of n, with the correction coefficient given explicitly by the honeycomb partition density. (power-law correction of isoperimetric partitions)

**中文。** 定义：等周剖分指将区域分为 n 个等体积腔的极小界面积剖分。猜想：其面积渐近主项为单球常数的 n 的分数幂型显式修正,修正系数由蜂巢剖分密度显式给出。（等周剖分幂律修正）
