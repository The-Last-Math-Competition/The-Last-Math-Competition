# 00000002116

**PS-06 ★** — source: 56_解析组合.md

**English.** Definition: Balance of a two-color urn means the ratio of dominant eigenvalues is 1. Conjecture: The characteristic function of the limiting distribution is φ(θ) = Σ_k c_k e^{ikθlog₂} (with the Fourier coefficients c_k of the periodic log₂ function explicit); the distribution is of non-normal periodic type.

**中文。** 定义：两色 urn 的平衡指占优特征值比为 1。猜想：收敛分布的特征函数为 φ(θ) = \sum_{k}c_k e^{ikθlog_2}(周期 log_2 函数的 Fourier 系数 c_k 显式);分布为非正态的周期型。
