# 00000005827

**English.** Definition: The crystalline curvature is the discrete curvature of polygonal polar diagrams, with facet motion. Conjecture: The facet-motion formula is the explicit ODE of the reciprocal curvature, linear at planar-wave solutions; the weighting of the reciprocal is the facet width, with conserved weight sum; and the Wulff shape of the motion is self-similar, with similarity rate constant 1/2. (reciprocal-curvature ODE motion of facets)

**中文。** 定义：晶体曲率：多边形极图的离散曲率（ crystalline curvature）；晶面运动。猜想：晶面运动公式为曲率倒数的显式常微且平面波解处线性；倒数的加权为晶面宽度且权重和守恒；运动的 Wulff 形保持自相似且相似速率常数为 1/2。（晶面曲率倒数常微运动）
