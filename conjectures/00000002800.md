**English.** Definition: The limit language of asymptotically finite dimension: transfinite-inductive constructions. Conjecture: Transfinite rigidity of HI spaces: all operators on a hereditarily indecomposable space are scalar plus compact, and the rank of the compact part is an explicit function of the dense language. (HI operator transfinite rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：渐近有限维的极限语言：超限归纳构造。猜想：HI 空间的超限刚性：hereditarily indecomposable 空间的一切算子为标量加紧且紧部分的秩为稠密语言的显式函数。（HI 算子超限刚性）
