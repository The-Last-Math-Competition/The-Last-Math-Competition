# 00000008384

**English.** Definition: The expected measure: the average of the motivic measure normalized by the Gauss domain on arc spaces. Conjecture: The motivic lift of the Euler characteristic of the expected measure is a tower sum of ascending chain structures; each layer of the tower is the motivic lift of the integration-by-parts formula of the Kontsevich integral; and the tower closes in finitely many steps if and only if the singularity is of quasihomogeneous type. (expected Euler tower closure)

**中文。** 定义：期望测度指弧空间上按 Gauss 域规范化的母题测度的平均。猜想：期望测度的 Euler 特征的母题化为升链结构的塔和；塔和的每一层为 Kontsevich 积分的分部积分公式的母题提升，且塔在有限步闭合当且仅当奇点为李克特型。（期望 Euler 塔和闭合）
