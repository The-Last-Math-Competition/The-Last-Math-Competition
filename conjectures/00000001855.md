# 00000001855

**RM-15 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The 2-rank of a random symmetric matrix over F_q is its rank mod 2. Conjecture: The probability that the 2-rank equals r is (1−q^{-1})·q^{-C(r,2)}/∏(1−q^{-i}) (a negative binomial distribution), compatible with the MacWilliams identities.

**中文。** 定义：F_q 上随机对称矩阵的 2-rank 指其 mod 2 的秩。猜想：2-rank 为 r 的概率为 (1−q^{-1})\·q^{-C{r}{2}}/\prod(1−q^{-i})(负二项分布);与 MacWilliams 恒等相容。
