# 00000000524

**CA-24 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: For a local ring in characteristic p, the stable index of the descending chain of test ideals τ(p^e) is the least e with τ(p^e) = τ(p^{e+1}), denoted ctp(R). Conjecture: For local rings with two-dimensional rational singularities, ctp(R) ≤ ⌈multiplicity(R)/2⌉, with equality attained on minimal-multiplicity rings.

**中文。** 定义：特征 p 的 test ideal 的下降链 τ(p^e) 对 e 的稳定指数指使 τ(p^e)=τ(p^{e+1}) 首次成立的最小 e,记 ctp(R)。猜想：对二维有理奇点的局部环,ctp(R) ≤ ⌈multiplicity(R)/2⌉,且等号在最小多重性环上达到。
