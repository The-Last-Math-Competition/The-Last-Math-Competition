# 00000003178

**English.** Definition: Graph solutions of sparse linear systems: Laplacian solvers. Conjecture: Near-linear solution of Laplacian systems: the solution time is m·log^{O(1)} n, with the optimal exponent the logarithmic tower of 1. (Laplacian near-linear solution tower)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏线性方程组的图求解：Laplacian 求解器。猜想：Laplacian 系统的近线性求解：求解时间为 m log^{O(1)} n 且指数的最优为 1 的对数塔。（Laplacian 近线性求解塔）
