# 00000008767

**English.** Definition: Analytic K-theory: the topological K of Frechet algebras. Conjecture: Phillips' inverse limit theorem holds under invariance of dense subalgebras of Banach algebras; and the connected components of the image of the exponential map determine the homotopy groups. (Phillips inverse limit exponential image)

**中文。** 定义：解析 K 理论指 Fréchet 代数的拓扑 K。猜想：Phillips 逆极限定理在 Banach 代数的稠密子代数不变性下成立；且指数映射的像的连通分量为确定同伦群。（Phillips 逆极限与指数像）
