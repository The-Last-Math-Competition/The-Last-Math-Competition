# 00000005305

**English.** Definition: Tensor coefficients are the decomposition multiplicities of products of symmetric-group representations, and saturation is nonzero propagation under scaling. Conjecture: The saturation criterion of stable tensor coefficients is an explicit positivity condition given by the character formula of rectangular partitions; the saturation threshold exists and is at most a linear multiple of the partition length; and the positivity criterion is combinatorial, with the kernel of the condition a finite lattice. (rectangular saturation positivity of tensor coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：张量系数：对称群表示积的分解重数；饱和性指尺度放大的非零传播。猜想：稳定张量系数的饱和判定为显式正性条件且由矩形分拆的特征公式给出；饱和阈值存在且不超过分拆长度的线性倍数；正性判据为组合条件且条件的核为有限格。（张量系数矩形饱和正性）
