**ERO-09 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: body of extreme value of Birkhoff type average of arithmetic Mañé: polynomial orbit {P(n) mod 1}. Conjecture: arithmetic Lagrange spectrum and classical Lagrange spectrum dual (dual spectrum); and Hausdorff dimension of difference set of two spectrum is arithmetic closure of 1/2 (dimension difference); difference set is most small endpoint of closed interval (closed interval closure); closed interval is explicit (endpoint) of 1/2.

**中文。** 定义：算术 Mañé：多项式轨道 {P(n) mod 1} 的 Birkhoff 型平均的极值的全体取值。猜想：算术 Lagrange 谱与经典 Lagrange 谱对偶（对偶谱律）；且两谱的差集的 Hausdorff 维数为 1/2（半维差律）；差集的算术闭包为闭区间（闭区间闭包律）；闭区间的最小端点为 1/2 的显式值（半端点律）。
