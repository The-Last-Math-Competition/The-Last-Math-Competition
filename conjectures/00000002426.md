# 00000002426

**English.** Definition: The E-polynomial of the character variety of a surface group. Conjecture: The E-polynomial is a product correction of a combinatorial count; and the correction factors are powers of (1+q). (surface character variety combinatorial product correction)

**中文。** 定义：表面群特征簇的 E-多项式。猜想：E-多项式为组合计数的乘积修正；且修正的因子为 (1+q) 幂。（表面特征簇组合乘积修正）
