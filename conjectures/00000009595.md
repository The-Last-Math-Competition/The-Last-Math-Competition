# 00000009595

**MSH-15 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: Triply periodic minimal surfaces (TPMS) in R^3 ordered by quotient-surface genus g; the realizable range of the genus spectrum is unknown. Conjecture: The genus spectrum of TPMS has no gaps: for every g at least 3 there exists a TPMS of genus g; and the ratio of the minimal cell area to the genus is asymptotically an explicit pi g^{1/2} law, attained asymptotically by the Schwarz P surface family. (TPMS genus spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：三周期极小曲面（TPMS）在 R^3 中按商曲面亏格 g 排列；亏格谱的实现范围未知。猜想：TPMS 的亏格谱无间隙：每个 g ≥ 3 都存在亏格 g 的 TPMS；最小面积胞的面积与亏格之比渐近为 π·g^{1/2} 型显式律，由 Schwarz P 曲面族渐近达到。（TPMS 亏格谱）
