# 00000009910

**GBR-30 △** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Complexity of isomorphism of quiver representations: the complexity classification of isomorphism testing. Conjecture: Isomorphism testing for a fixed quiver is graph-isomorphism complete, and with unfixed dimensions it is polynomial-time exactly for Dynkin quivers; for wild type, computing the minimal field extension degree is undecidable at unbounded dimension. (representation isomorphism complexity dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：箭图表示同构的复杂度：表示同构判定的复杂度分类。猜想：固定箭图的表示同构判定为图同构完备且维数不固定时判定为多项式时间当且仅当箭图为 Dynkin 型；野型的最小域扩张次数的计算在维数无界时不可判定。（表示同构复杂度二分）
