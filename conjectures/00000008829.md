**CVX-29 ★** — source: 279_凸优化的迭代复杂度.md

**English.** Definition: The active set of constraints: identification of active constraints. Conjecture: Active set identification occurs in finite time (an identification law); acceleration gives earlier identification (an earliness law); the perturbation of early identification (noise identification) is noisy (a noise law); and noisy identification stabilizes uniformly (a uniformity law). (active set finite identification noise uniformity)

**中文。** 定义：约束的活跃集：活动约束的识别。猜想：活动集的识别（有限时间的识别）的（识别律）；且识别的提前（加速识别的）的（提前律）；提前的扰动（噪声识别）的（噪声律）；噪声的稳定（一致识别）的（一致律）。
