# 00000007741

**FRV-21 ★** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral measure of the product of k independent uniform random reflections on C^n converges as n grows. Conjecture: The limit is the (k-1)-fold additive convolution of the Bernoulli law (delta_1 + delta_{-1})/2 with a uniform-arc composition; the density on the unit circle is the normalized form of |sin(k theta/2)/sin(theta/2)|^{2/k}; and the outlying mass outside the unit circle is a truncation of max(0, 1-k/n) type, an explicit function of gamma in the k/n to gamma limit. (reflection product spectral limit)

**中文。** 定义：随机反射的乘积：R_1,…,R_k 为 C^n 上独立均匀随机反射（秩 1 修改的酉），乘积 R_1⋯R_k 的谱测度在 n→∞ 时收敛。猜想：极限为 Bernoulli 律 (δ_1+δ_{-1})/2 与其自身经 ⊞ 的 k-1 重卷积再与均匀圆弧律的复合；谱在单位圆上的密度为 1/(2π)·|sin(kθ/2)/sin(θ/2)|^{2/(k)} 的规范化形；且特征值在单位圆外的溢出质量恰为 max(0, 1-k/n) 型截断（在 k/n→γ 极限下为 γ 的显式函数）。
