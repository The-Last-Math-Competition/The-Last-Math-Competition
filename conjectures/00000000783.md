**PA-83 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: Rigid analytic spaces are the spaces of non-Archimedean analytic geometry. Conjecture: Every closed subspace of the affine line has dimension zero or one; the only exceptions to this dimension dichotomy are unions of discs; and the two-dimensional exceptions are finite unions of discs and intervals. (affine line dimension dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：刚性解析空间为非阿分析几何的空间。猜想：仿射线的闭子空间维数为零或一；且维数二分的例外为圆盘并。；且二维例外的构造为圆盘与区间的有限并。（仿射线维数二分）
