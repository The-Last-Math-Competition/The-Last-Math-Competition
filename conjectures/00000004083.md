# 00000004083

**BF2-23 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: A delayed Hopf bifurcation is the crossing of the imaginary axis by conjugate roots of the characteristic quasi-polynomial. Conjecture: The critical delay is tau_c = theta(mu)/Im lambda(mu), where theta is the argument function, the first bifurcation corresponds to the smallest positive argument solution, and the conversion formula is universal. (critical spectral conversion of delayed Hopf bifurcations)

**中文。** 定义：时滞 Hopf 分叉指特征拟多项式的共轭根穿越虚轴。猜想：临界延迟 τ_c = θ(μ)/Imλ(μ),θ 为幅角函数,首次分叉对应最小正幅角解,换算公式普适。（延迟 Hopf 临界谱换算）
