**DIA-09 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The Lehmer turnaround of Mahler measures of algebraic numbers: the taming path lm_path of the lower bound on M(alpha) > 1. Conjecture: lm_path (the tame-topological equivalence of the Lehmer conjecture) is the isolation of the Lehmer number 1.176... (an isolation equivalence law); the existence of non-Lehmer families (M < 1.17 infinitely often) is equivalent to the negation of large cardinals (a large cardinal equivalence law); the minimal Mahler measure M_d of degree d is monotone (M_{d+1} <= M_d, a monotonicity law); and beyond the known threshold d <= 44, the first degree of strict monotonicity is at most 52 (a fifty-two prediction law). (Lehmer isolation monotone fifty-two)

**中文。** 定义：代数数的 Mahler 测度的 Lehmer 折返：M(α)>1 的下界的 Lehmer 常数的驯化路径 lm_path。猜想：lm_path（Lehmer 猜想的驯拓扑等价）即 Lehmer 数 1.176…的孤立性（孤立性等价律）；且非 Lehmer（M<1.17 的无穷族）的存在与大基数的否定的等价（大基数等价律）；度数 d 的极小 Mahler 测度 M_d 的单调性（M_{d+1}≤M_d 的单调律）；单调的严格（严格递减）的证明阈为 d ≤ 44 的已知（44 已知阈）外的第一个严格点的度数 ≤ 52（五二预测律）。
