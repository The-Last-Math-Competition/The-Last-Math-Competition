# 00000008643

**English.** Definition: The random limit of ascending Young tableaux. Conjecture: The limit shape is the envelope of a variational Euler-Lagrange solution and is unique. (ascending tableau variational envelope)

**中文。** 定义：上升 Young 图表的随机极限。猜想：其极限形状为变分 Euler-Lagrange 解的包络且唯一。（上升图表变分包络）
