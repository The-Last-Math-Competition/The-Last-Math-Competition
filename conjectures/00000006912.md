# 00000006912

**English.** Definition: Bott periodicity with the periods of K-groups. Conjecture: The eightfold period of the groups holds, the realization of the period being Clifford algebras. (Clifford-realized eightfold Bott periodicity)

**中文。** 定义：Bott 周期性：K 群的周期。猜想：Bott 的周期：群的八重周期且期的实现为 Clifford 代数。（Bott 周期 Clifford 代数八重周期 K 群周期实现算子）
