# 00000008951

**ISO-01 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: The N-soliton solution of the KdV equation: the superposition of nondispersive traveling waves. Conjecture: Its interaction phase shifts satisfy two-body additivity, with total displacement an explicit logarithmic sum of velocity differences. (soliton phase additivity law)

**中文。** 定义：KdV 方程的 N-孤子解指非色散行波叠加。猜想：其相互作用相位移动满足两体可加性且总位移为速度差的 log 显式和。（孤子相位可加律）
