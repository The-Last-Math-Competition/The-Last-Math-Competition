# 00000004873

**English.** Definition: Reverse mathematics studies the equivalence of principles between subsystems of second-order arithmetic. Conjecture: The equivalence spectrum of Ramsey-type principles contains exactly five standard subsystems; the adjacencies of the spectrum are given by explicit jumping principles with each adjacency edge independent over omega-models; the restricted-coloring version shrinks to three points with the split point the dual Ramsey principle. (five-point structure of the Ramsey equivalence spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：逆数学：二阶算术子系统间的原理等价研究。猜想：拉姆齐型原理的等价谱恰含五个标准子系统；谱的邻接由显式跳跃原理给出且每条邻接边独立于 ω 模型；受限色数版本的谱缩为三点且分裂点为对偶拉姆齐原理。（拉姆齐等价谱五点结构）
