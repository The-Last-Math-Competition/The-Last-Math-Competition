# 00000008334

**DIA-14 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The convergence threshold of the Khintchine theorem: the critical oscillation frequency khi_osc of the failure of Khintchine outside the monotone domain. Conjecture: khi_osc (the minimal oscillation frequency for nonmonotone Khintchine failure) is two-scale in psi (a two-scale critical law); the correction of the failure (the Beresnevich-Velani extension beyond monotonicity) has unit constant in the divergence condition (a unit constant law); and the dimension of the convergence-side counterexamples (breaking zero-measure convergence) is 1/2 (a half-dimensional counterexample law). (Khintchine two-scale Beresnevich half-dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Khintchine 定理的收敛阈值：逼近函数 ψ 的单调域外的 Khintchine 的失效的临界振频 khi_osc。猜想：khi_osc（非单调的 Khintchine 的失效的最小振荡频率）为 ψ 的双尺度（双尺度临界律）；且失效的修正（非单调的 Beresnevich–Velani 的推广）的常数（ divergence 条件的常数）为 1 的单位（单位常数律）；收敛侧的推广（convergence 的零测的破缺）的反例的维数为 1/2（半维反例律）。
