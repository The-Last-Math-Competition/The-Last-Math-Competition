**English.** Definition: Loopy games: games allowing cycles. Conjecture: The trichotomy of values into on, off, and dud is complete; and the combination of the completeness is the graph cycle structure. (loopy graph cycle trichotomy complete)

**中文。** 定义：loopy games 为允许循环的游戏。猜想：值的三分为 on、off 与 dud 且三分为完全；且完全的组合为图回路。（loopy 图回路三分完全）
