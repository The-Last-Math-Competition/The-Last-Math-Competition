**English.** Definition: Spectral norm of tensors: maximal gain. Conjecture: The spectral norm is the maximal multilinear gain, characterized as an extremal problem: the norm is the maximum over unit vectors, and the characterization closes under the extremal formulation. (tensor spectral norm extremal problem)
