# 00000003892

**English.** Definition: Witt cohomology is the Witt-coefficient version of crystalline cohomology H^i(WΩ^j). Conjecture: The torsion subgroup of the Witt cohomology of a smooth projective variety is finite, with the p-valuation of its order growing strictly linearly in i−j with slope 1. (Witt torsion valuation linear growth)

**中文。** 定义：Witt 上同调指晶体上同调的 Witt 系数化 H^i(WΩ^j)。猜想：光滑射影簇的 Witt 上同调的挠子群有限,其阶的 p-赋值随 i−j 严格线性增长且斜率为 1。（Witt 挠赋值线性增长）
