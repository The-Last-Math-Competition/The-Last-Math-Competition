**English.** Definition: The tangent kernel is the linearized kernel of wide networks. Conjecture: In the infinite-width limit it is a deterministic kernel with convergence rate width negative one half, the kernel of the rate being the spectral concentration. (spectral-concentration rate of infinite-width tangent kernels)
