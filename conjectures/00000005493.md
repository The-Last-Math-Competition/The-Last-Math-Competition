# 00000005493

**IF2-23 ★** — source: 168_数学物理IV_可积场论.md

**English.** Definition: The finite-volume energy shift is the finite-size effect formula. Conjecture: The two-body energy shift is always the explicit expression of the momentum difference of the two-particle state; exact in the elastic region, with corrections controlled by the winding order. (winding-order momentum difference of two-body shifts)

**中文。** 定义：有限体积能移指尺寸效应公式。猜想：二体能移恒为双粒子态动量差的显式式；弹性区精确且修正由绕圈阶控制。（二体能移动量差绕圈）
