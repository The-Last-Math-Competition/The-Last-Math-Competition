**English.** Definition: Root separation is the minimal pairwise distance of the roots of a polynomial. Conjecture: The real root separation lower bound of four-term sparse polynomials is an explicit power of the coefficient heights, of dyadic decay type, attained by binomial families. (dyadic power bound of sparse real root separation)

**中文。** 定义：根分离指多项式根两两间的最小距离。猜想：四项稀疏多项式的实根分离下界为系数高度的显式幂,幂呈二进衰减型且界由二项式族达到。（稀疏实根分离二进幂界）
