# 00000001745

**English.** Definition: The approximation exponent spectrum (dimension of subsets). Conjecture: For points on the Veronese curve, the dimension spectrum of the realizable Dirichlet-improvable sets is the complete interval [0, 1] (spectrum completeness).

**中文。** 定义：逼近指数谱(维数子集)。猜想：Veronese 曲线上点的可实现的 Dirichlet 改进集的维数谱的完整区间为 [0, 1](谱完备)。
