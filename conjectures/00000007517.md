**ET3-07 ★** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The quantitative remainder of weak Lefschetz is the homological structure of the remainder. Conjecture: The remainder is given by the boundary quotient of middle cohomology and the quotient spectrum is finite-dimensional; and the vanishing of the remainder is equivalent to Hodge symmetry. (middle-quotient Lefschetz remainder)

**中文。** 定义：弱 Lefschetz 的定量残余为余项的同调结构。猜想：余项由中间上同调的边界商给出且商谱为有限维；且余项的消失等价于 Hodge 对称。（Lefschetz 余项中间商）
