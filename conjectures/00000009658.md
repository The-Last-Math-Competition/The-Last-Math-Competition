# 00000009658

**HSS-18 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: Local eigenvalue statistics of adjacency matrices of random d-regular graphs of fixed degree d. Conjecture: Bulk local statistics of random d-regular graph eigenvalues converge to the sine kernel process, consistent with GOE; edge extreme value statistics are Tracy-Widom without correction parameters, with no exceptions at d = 3. (regular graph sine universality)

**中文。** 定义：固定度 d 的随机 d-正则图邻接矩阵的特征值局部统计。猜想：随机 d-正则图的特征值在 bulk 的局部统计收敛到 sine 核过程，与 GOE 一致；边缘的极值分布为 Tracy–Widom 且无修正参数，d=3 无例外。（正则图 sine 普适）
