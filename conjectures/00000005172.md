**English.** Definition: Ind-completion is the completion construction adding filtered colimits. Conjecture: The conversion constant of compact objects is the explicit cofinality characteristic of the cardinal, which equals itself for regular cardinals, and the bound is optimal. (cofinality-characteristic conversion of ind-completion)
