**CG-98 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Euler's Briot–Bouquet q-difference equations. Conjecture: The coefficients of the entire formal solutions of the q-difference f(qx) − f(x) = x^k are q-binomials (f-q solution coefficients).

**中文。** 定义：Euler 的 Briot–Bouquet q-差分。猜想：q-差分 f(qx) − f(x) = x^k 的整形式解的系数为 q-binomial(f-q 解系数)。
