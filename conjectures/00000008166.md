**English.** Definition: The ordering error of the operator spectrum under the truncation of the Wilson format of effective field theory: the magnitude split_eft of the degeneracy splitting of operator dimension ordering in the EFT expansion. Conjecture: split_eft (the splitting of degenerate operators) is dominated by the one-loop correction factor 1/16 pi^2 (a one-loop splitting law); the minimal splitting gap is the double ratio Lambda^2/m_phi^2 (a double ratio formula); and the worst position of the ordering error (overlap of truncation orders) is at the dimension-6 operator spectrum (a dimension-6 worst law), with worst overlap rate 1/2 (a half-overlap upper bound). (EFT one-loop splitting dimension-six worst)
