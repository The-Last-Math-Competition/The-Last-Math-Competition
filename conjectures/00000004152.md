# 00000004152

**CM3-02 ◆** — source: 124_解析数论III_圆法.md

**English.** Definition: The Waring singular series is the local Euler product of the representation count by sums of k-th powers. Conjecture: The smallest power for which it takes a negative value is 4, and the negative values for fourth powers come only from the local factor at a single prime. (minimal Waring power with negative singular series)

**中文。** 定义：Waring 奇异级数指 k 次幂和表示数的局部 Euler 积。猜想：其取负值的最小方次数为 4,且四次幂的负值仅来自单个素数处的局部因子。（Waring 负奇异最小方次）
