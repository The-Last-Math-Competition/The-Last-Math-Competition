# 00000007913

**English.** Definition: The Howe duality correspondence of theta lifts: the characterization index kappa(pi) of the lift image of the theta kernel Theta for the dual pair (O(V), Sp(W)) of orthogonal and symplectic groups. Conjecture: The Howe conjecture (maximality of the image) holds for all split cases over p-adic fields, and the codimension of the image is concentrated at the first wall of the conservation relation: kappa(pi) = dim V - 2 min(theta-walls), the minimal wall being an explicit function of the Witt index; and the index is a complete linear wall for hypergeometric invariant lifts. (Howe conservation wall codimension)

**中文。** 定义：theta 提升的 Howe 对偶对应：辛群与正交群的对偶对 (O(V),Sp(W)) 的 theta 核 Θ 的提升像 Image(θ_ψ) 的刻画指标 κ(π)。猜想：Howe 猜想（像为极大）在 p-adic 域的全部分裂情形成立，且像的余维数集中由 conservation relation 的第一壁给出：κ(π)=dim V−2·min(θ-walls)，壁的最小值为 Witt 指数的显式函数；该指数在超几何不变量提升时为线性的完全壁。
