# 00000003464

**GA3-04 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: L(2,1)-labeling is distance-constrained coloring: points at distance one differ by at least two and at distance two by at least one, with span λ the minimal label range. Conjecture: The L(2,1) span satisfies λ(G) ≤ Δ², with asymptotic equality attained only by Moore graphs of diameter two; the optimal upper bound Δ²−Δ+2 is exact for prime-power Moore graphs, and general graphs improve by a logarithmic correction in Δ. (L21 span Moore law)

**中文。** 定义：L(2,1)-标号为距离约束染色：距离一的点标号差至少二、距离二的差至少一，跨度 λ 为标号极差最小值。猜想：L(2,1) 跨度满足 λ(G) ≤ Δ² 且等号渐近仅由直径二的 Moore 图取得；最优上界 Δ²−Δ+2 对素数幂阶 Moore 图精确且一般图可改进 Δ 的对数修正。（L21 跨度 Moore 律）
