**English.** Definition: The Dehn function is the area function of the word problem, compared with the thinness constant of hyperbolicity. Conjecture: The attainable interval of the ratio of the optimal Dehn constant of hyperbolic groups to the thinness constant is the closed interval [1,2], with ratio 1 attained by free groups and ratio 2 by extremal triangulation groups; the ratio spectrum is a connected interval with no gaps; and for nonhyperbolic groups the ratio diverges, with divergence rate inversely proportional to the polynomial Dehn exponent. (Dehn-thinness ratio spectrum from one to two)

**中文。** 定义：Dehn 函数：词问题的面积函数；与双曲性瘦常数之比。猜想：双曲群的最优 Dehn 常数与瘦常数之比的可达区间为闭区间 [1,2] 且比值 1 在自由群、比值 2 在极端三角剖分群达到；比值谱为连通区间且无间隙；非双曲群的比值发散且发散率与多项式 Dehn 指数成反比。（Dehn-瘦常数比值谱一二）
