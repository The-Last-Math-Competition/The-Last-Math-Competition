# 00000002441

**TA-01 ★** — source: 67_拓扑群与大基数交互.md

**English.** Definition: The Tukey type of a Polish group is the directed-set type of its cofinal subsets. Conjecture: The Tukey spectrum of non-locally-compact Polish groups gains the explicit new class {ℵ₁×ω} (a nontrivial type); the extension of the spectrum is realized by the automorphism group of the measure algebra.

**中文。** 定义：Polish 群的 Tukey 类型指其 cofinal 子集的有向集型。猜想：非局部紧 Polish 群的 Tukey 谱新增 {ℵ₁×ω} 的显式类(非平凡型);谱的扩展由测度代数自同构群实现。
