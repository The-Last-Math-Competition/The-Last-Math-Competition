**FL2-02 ◆** — source: 161_非线性偏微分II_流体II.md

**English.** Definition: Convex integration is the iterative perturbation method constructing nonunique weak solutions. Conjecture: The supremum of Holder exponents for three-dimensional nonuniqueness is one third, the upper bound given by the energy-inequality obstruction, the lower bound attained by explicit constructions of mixing operators, and not further increasable. (Holder supremum one third of convex integration)

**中文。** 定义：凸积分指构造非唯一弱解的迭代扰动方法。猜想：三维非唯一性的赫尔德指数上确界为三分之一,上界由能量不等式障碍给出,下界由混合算子的显式构造达到且不可再升。（凸积分赫尔德上确界三分之一）
