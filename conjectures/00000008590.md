**English.** Definition: operator matrix completion of positive complement problem: part block given of operator matrix is of positive operator. Conjecture: of positive complement by Parrott type inequality gives (Parrott); and of most small norm radius of complement and constant is 2 (and); minimal complement unique if and only if complement field of block located at angle (angle unique); ring block is convex set (convex complement field).
