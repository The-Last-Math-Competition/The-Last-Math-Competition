**HG3-06 ★** — source: 147_几何群论III_双曲群.md

**English.** Definition: The distortion function of a subgroup converts the subgroup word length into the ambient word length. Conjecture: The attainable polynomial distortion exponents are exactly all positive integers; the exponent k is realized by a cancellation-corrected Baumslag-Solitar type subgroup; in hyperbolic groups the distortion spectrum collapses to the quasiconvex type (exponent 1) and the distortion of any non-quasiconvex subgroup is at least exponential. (full spectrum of polynomial distortion exponents)

**中文。** 定义：子群的失真函数：子群词长相对母群词长的换算。猜想：可达的多项式失真指数恰为全部正整数；指数 k 由 Baumslag–Solitar 型子群的消除修正实现；双曲群中失真谱坍缩为拟凸型（指数 1）且任何非拟凸子群的失真至少为指数型。（失真多项式指数全谱）
