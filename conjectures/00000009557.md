**TCH-07 ★** — source: 304_Teichmuller动力.md

**English.** Definition: Affine invariant submanifolds are GL^+(2,R) orbit closures; the EMM theorem asserts they are linear submanifolds, and Filip further proved them algebraic; finiteness of the classification at fixed topological type (g,n) is open. Conjecture: At fixed (g,n) there are finitely many isomorphism classes of affine invariant submanifolds, with maximal codimension of proper submanifolds 3g - 3 + n - 2; and submanifolds of dimension at least 3g - 4 + n require only a finite complement to the fully ramified list. (affine submanifold finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：仿射不变子流形为 GL^+(2,R) 轨道闭包；EMM 定理断言其为线性子流形，Filip 进一步证明其为代数；固定拓扑型 (g,n) 时的分类有限性未知。猜想：固定 (g,n) 时仿射不变子流形的同构类有限，真子流形的最大余维为 3g−3+n−2；维数 ≥3g−4+n 的高维子流形仅剩完全分支列表的有限补充。（仿射子流形有限性）
