# 00000002334

**English.** Definition: The number of real roots of mixed-sign systems refers to random sign combinations. Conjecture: The leading correction of the Edgeworth expansion is always −σ³/6·3 times the third Hermite polynomial at zero; and the leading term is given by the mixed-degree functional. (mixed Edgeworth third-order Hermite)

**中文。** 定义：混合符号系统的实根数指随机符号组合。猜想：Edgeworth 展开的首修正恒为负 σ 三次除六乘三阶 Hermite 在零的值；且首项由混合度泛函给出。（混合 Edgeworth 三阶 Hermite）
