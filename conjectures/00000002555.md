# 00000002555

**GZ-25 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: The rectifiability spectrum: the layered structure of rectifiable dimensions of a set. Conjecture: There exist sets whose s-dimensional rectifiability layers form a strict infinite chain; the index set of the chain is closed, and the gaps of the index set are dual to the dimension spectrum of the Furstenberg direction sets. (rectifiability layered chain)

**中文。** 定义：可校正谱：集合的可校正维数层叠结构。猜想：存在集合使 s 维可校正层叠为严格的无限链；链的指标集为闭集且指标集的间隙与 Furstenberg 方向集合的维数谱对偶。（可校正层叠链）
