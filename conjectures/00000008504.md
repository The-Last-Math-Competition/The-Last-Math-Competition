# 00000008504

**English.** Definition: A strongly normal extension is a Picard-Vessiot extension generated in finitely many steps by exponentials, integrals, and algebraic extensions. Conjecture: The Galois group of a strongly normal extension is a finite-dimensional algebraic group; its differential Galois correspondence is the Kolchin correspondence; and the nesting depth of the extension is at most dim G, with the bound optimal. (strongly normal nesting depth)

**中文。** 定义：强正规扩张指有限步由指数、积分与代数扩张生成的 Picard–Vessiot 扩张。猜想：强正规扩张的 Galois 群为有限维代数群；其微分 Galois 对应为 Kolchin 对应；扩张的嵌套深度 ≤ dim G 且界最优。（强正规嵌套深度）
