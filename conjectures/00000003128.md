**English.** Definition: Branch points and cut points: the local structure of the boundary. Conjecture: The cut-point rigidity of the boundary: the boundary of a hyperbolic group has no cut points if and only if the group is one-ended thick, and the existence of cut points is the complete criterion for nontrivial splittings. (boundary cut point splitting criterion)

**中文。** 定义：分支（branch）与割点：边界的局部结构。猜想：边界的割点刚性：双曲群边界无割点当且仅当群为单厚度且割点的存在为非平凡分裂的完全判据。（边界割点分裂判据）
