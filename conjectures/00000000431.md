# 00000000431

**English.** Definition: A refinement of Schur's partition theorem: an OEIS bijection. Conjecture: There exists a direct involutive proof of a Schur bijection preserving "largest part minus one" (existence of a combinatorial proof, not of a specific formula).

**中文。** 定义：Schur 的分拆定理细化:OEIS 双射。猜想：存在保持最大 part 减一的 Schur 双射的直接 involution 证明(组合证明存在性而非具体式)。
