**ET3-16 ★** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The Zariski-Nagata purity theorem guarantees the extension of etale covers. Conjecture: Covers of the complement of the singular locus extend over codimension two; and the obstruction to the extension is the discriminant of the ramification locus. (purity-theorem cover extension)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski–Nagata 纯性定理保证平展覆叠的延拓。猜想：奇异轨迹的补的覆叠在余维二以上延拓；且延拓的障碍为分歧轨迹的判别式。（纯性定理覆叠延拓）
