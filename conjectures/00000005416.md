# 00000005416

**HM2-06 ★** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: Minimality is the condition that the measure is minimal for the harmonic structure. Conjecture: The criterion of the screw construction is given explicitly by the capacity of the trapping flow, necessary and sufficient, and the dimension of the construction has no exceptional values; the dimension of the screw construction equals exactly the codimension of the vanishing trapping capacity. (trapping-capacity criterion of the screw construction)

**中文。** 定义：极小性指测度为调和结构极小的条件。猜想：螺丝构造的判据由捕获流的容量显式给出,判据充分必要且构造的维数无例外值。(螺丝捕获容量充要判据)；且螺丝构造维数恰等于捕获容量消失的余维数。
