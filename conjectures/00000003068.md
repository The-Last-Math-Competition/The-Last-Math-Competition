# 00000003068

**English.** Definition: Integrable quantization tests: the quantum preservation of classical integrability. Conjecture: The Yang–Baxterization of quantum integrability: quantization of the classical r-matrix exists if and only if the classical Yang–Baxter equation holds, and the convergence radius of the quantization parameter is a finite explicit value. (YB-ization convergence radius explicit value)

**中文。** 定义：可积量子化检验：经典可积性的量子保持。猜想：量子可积的 Yang–Baxter 化：经典 r 矩阵的量子化的存在当且仅当经典 Yang–Baxter 方程成立且量子化的形变参数的收敛半径为有限显式值。（YB 化收敛半径显式值）
