# 00000003907

**English.** Definition: The test ideal chain is the descending chain of ideals tau(R^{1/p^e}) as e grows. Conjecture: The chain stabilizes within e <= dim R + 1, and there exist examples stabilizing exactly at e = dim R + 1, so the bound cannot be improved. (optimal test chain stabilization bound)

**中文。** 定义：test ideal 链指 τ(R^{1/p^e}) 随 e 的递降理想列。猜想：该链在 e ≤ dimR + 1 内稳定,且存在例子恰在 e = dimR + 1 才稳定,界不可改进。（test 链稳定最优界）
