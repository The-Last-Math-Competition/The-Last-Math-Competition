**English.** Definition: Fractal interpolation function graphs are IFS constructions. Conjecture: The turning point of the linear-growth segment of the graph dimension is the inverse square root of the horizontal compression ratio; and the combination of the turning is the vertical-horizontal balance. (interpolation graph turning inverse square root)

**中文。** 定义：分形插值函数图像为 IFS 构造。猜想：图像维数线性增长段的转折点为水平压缩比的平方根倒数；且转折的组合为纵横向平衡。（插值图像转折平方根倒数）
