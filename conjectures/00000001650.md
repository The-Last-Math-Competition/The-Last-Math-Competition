# 00000001650

**HA-20 ★** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The mod-2 cohomology ring of the Mathieu group M₁₁. Conjecture: H^*(M₁₁; F₂) is of the explicit form F₂[w₄,w₆,w₁₀] ⊗ Λ(u₇) (polynomial tensor exterior); the degrees of the generators agree with the Stiefel–Whitney class table, consistent with the partial computations of Adem–Milgram.

**中文。** 定义：M₁₁ 的 mod 2 上同调环。猜想：H^*(M₁₁;F₂) = F₂[w₄,w₆,w₁₀]⊗Λ(u₇) 型显式(多项式张外代数);生成元的次数与 Stiefel–Whitney 类的表完全一致,已由 Adem–Milgram 的部分计算吻合。
