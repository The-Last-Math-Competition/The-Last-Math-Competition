# 00000009925

**English.** Definition: Zigzag persistent homology: interval decompositions of bidirectional filtrations. Conjecture: The algorithmic complexity lower bound of zigzag interval decomposition is linear time, attained by optimal algorithms; the lower bound is given by explicit recursive families of interval-length-sum input sizes. (zigzag linear lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：zigzag 持久同调：双向过滤的区间分解。猜想：zigzag 区间分解的算法复杂度下界为线性时间且最优算法达到；下界由区间长度和的输入规模的显式递归族给出。（zigzag 线性下界）
