# 00000001994

**English.** Definition: Landau damping is the long-time electrostatic decay of collisionless plasmas. Conjecture: For polynomial-tail distributions, the damping rate is exactly the real part of the linear Landau eigenvalue (an exact match); and the optimal tail exponent for the match is determined by the regularity threshold of the initial data.

**中文。** 定义：Landau 阻尼指无碰撞等离子体的长时间静电衰减。猜想：多项式尾分布的阻尼率恰为线性 Landau 特征值的实部(精确匹配);匹配的最优尾指数由初始数据的正则性阈值确定。
