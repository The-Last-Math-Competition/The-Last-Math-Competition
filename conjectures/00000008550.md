**English.** Definition: The kernel of a lattice is a kernel ideal. Conjecture: Kernel ideals correspond one-to-one with congruences; and the maximal chain length of a kernel is the tower height of the lattice, decreasing under passage to sublattices. (kernel ideal congruence correspondence)
