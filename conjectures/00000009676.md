# 00000009676

**English.** Definition: Counting real zeros of exponential polynomials sum a_i e^{b_i z}: the case of algebraic coefficients and exponents. Conjecture: The count of real zeros is bounded above by an explicit polynomial function of the number of exponents and the height; the bound is optimal, and the density of imaginary parts of zeros within vertical strips has an explicit average law. (exponential polynomial zero density)

**中文。** 定义：指数多项式 Σ a_i e^{b_i z} 的实零点计数：系数与指数均为代数数的情形。猜想：实零点个数的上界为指数个数与高度的多项式显式函数；该界为最优且零点虚部分布在垂直带内的密度有显式平均律。（指数多项式零点密度）
