# 00000005060

**English.** Definition: Spectral edge types and diffusion exponents are two scaling layers. Conjecture: There exist two random potentials with the same spectral edge type but different diffusion exponents, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of potential tails. (same spectral edge-different diffusion exponent separation)

**中文。** 定义：谱边缘类型与扩散指数指两层标度。猜想：存在两随机位势的谱边缘同型而扩散指数不同,分离由位势尾的显式对实现。（谱边缘同-扩散指数异分离）
