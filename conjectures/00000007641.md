# 00000007641

**English.** Definition: The energy conservation of the wave equation as the space-time identity of product structure. Conjecture: The deficit of the energy identity is controlled by the regularity threshold of weak solutions; and the criticality of the threshold is the Besov-space index. (Besov-threshold energy identity)

**中文。** 定义：波动方程的能量守恒为乘积结构的时空恒等式。猜想：能量恒等式的亏损由弱解的正则门槛控制；且门槛的临界为 Besov 空间指标。（能量恒等 Besov 门槛）
