**RM3-28 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The symmetrized ensemble is the joint process of a β-gas and its mirror points (negative weight); the symmetrized gap probability is the joint no-point probability. Conjecture: The symmetrized gap probability satisfies the folding identity G_sym(s) = G_β(s/2)·G_{2β}(s/2). (symmetrization folding identity)

**中文。** 定义：对称化系综指 β-气与其镜像点(负权)的联合过程,对称化 gap 概率指其联合无点概率。猜想：对称化 gap 概率满足折叠恒等式 G_sym(s) = G_β(s/2)·G_{2β}(s/2)。（对称化折叠恒等式）
