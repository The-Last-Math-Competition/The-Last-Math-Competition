**English.** Definition: The covering density of the Leech lattice is the candidate thinnest lattice covering. Conjecture: The thinnest lattice covering in twenty-four dimensions is given by the Leech lattice; and the exact covering density is an explicit value of lattice points and integrals. (Leech thinnest covering optimal)

**中文。** 定义：Leech 格的覆盖密度为最薄格覆盖候选。猜想：二十四维最薄格覆盖由 Leech 格给出；且覆盖密度的精确值为显式格点和积分。（Leech 覆盖最薄最优）
