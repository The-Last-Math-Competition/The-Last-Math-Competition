# 00000002190

**English.** Definition: The Sperner capacity is the zero-error version of Shannon capacity. Conjecture: The Sperner capacity of C_{2k+1} equals cos(π/(2k+1)) (Gargano–Körner–Vaccaro–Simonyi); the exact value follows from the SDP bound of k-step chain analysis.

**中文。** 定义：Sperner 容量指无圈版本的 Shannon 容量。猜想：C_{2k+1} 的 Sperner 容量 = cos(π/(2k+1))(Gargano–Körner–Vaccaro–Simonyi);精确值由 k 步链分析的 SDP 界。
