**English.** Definition: Morse index spectrum: critical plane index of free boundary minimal surface is 4. Conjecture: the- k index of free boundary minimal surface is arithmetic series; every index of 2k+2 by rotation symmetric surface family realized and without jump, index spectrum completely connected. (free boundary index spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：自由边界极小曲面的 Morse 指标谱：临界悬链面指标为 4。猜想：自由边界极小曲面的第 k 个可达指标为 2k+2 的算术级数；每个指标值由旋转对称曲面族实现且无跳跃间隙，指标谱完全连通。（自由边界指标谱）
