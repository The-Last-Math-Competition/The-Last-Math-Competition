**OA-05 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The principal graph Γ(M,N) is the inclusion graph of the standard invariants of a subfactor. Conjecture: The characteristic polynomial of the second-adjacency matrix of the principal graph of a finite-depth subfactor is a product of cyclotomic polynomials; and the finiteness of the amplitudes is completely determined by the graph's bi-adjacency. (principal graph cyclotomic characteristic polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：主图 Γ(M,N) 为子因子标准不变量的包含图。猜想：有限深度子因子的主图的第二邻矩阵的特征多项式为分圆多项式乘积；且振幅的有限性由图的双邻接性完全决定。（主图分圆特征多项式）
