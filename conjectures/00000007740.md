# 00000007740

**FRV-20 △** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: Normalized free sums of free iid heavy-tailed self-adjoint elements with tail P(X>t) ~ t^{-alpha} L(t) converge to the free stable law S_alpha; the convergence rate is the W_1 distance decay. Conjecture: The rate is O(h(n)^{-1/2}) controlled by the regular variation of the tail balance function h(t)=t^alpha L(t); when L is constant the exact rate is n^{-min(alpha-1,1/2)/alpha}, with a phase transition at alpha=3/2 whose two sides are dominated by jump-center and diffusion-center error structures. (heavy-tail free stable rate)

**中文。** 定义：重尾自由独立元的正规化和：取自由 iid 自伴 X_i，尾部 P(X>t) ~ t^{-α}L(t)（缓变 L），正规化 n^{-1/α} 后的自由和收敛到自由稳定 S_α。收敛速率指 W_1 距离到 S_α 的衰减。猜想：速率由尾平衡函数 h(t)=t^α L(t) 的正则变化指数控制，为 O(h(n)^{-1/2})；当 L 为常数时精确速率为 n^{-min(α-1,1/2)/α}，且相变发生在 α=3/2 处，两侧的误差结构分别由跳-中心与扩散中心主导。
