# 00000005873

**English.** Definition: Anisotropic error analysis is the direction-dependent error estimate. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit matrix of the two-direction extrema, given by the singular values of the Jacobian; the main direction of the error is the maximal-singular-value direction, with proportion its square; and the standard estimate fails when the anisotropy ratio exceeds the threshold, twice the degree. (singular-value matrix conversion of anisotropy)

**中文。** 定义：各向异性误差分析：方向依赖的误差估计。猜想：换算公式为两方向极值的显式矩阵且由雅可比奇异值给出；误差的主方向为最大奇异值方向且占比为其平方；各向异性比超过阈值时标准估计失效且阈值为次数的二倍。（各向异性奇异值矩阵换算）
