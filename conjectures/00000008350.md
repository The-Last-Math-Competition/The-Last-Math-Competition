**English.** Definition: The stratification of Schnirelmann psi-density: the interaction schn_layer of Schnirelmann density of sequences (minimal segments of positive density) with the Dirichlet divisor problem. Conjecture: The complete stratification of schn_layer has logarithmically many layers (a logarithmic layer law); the transition between layers (density jumps) has threshold the reciprocal of the Mertens product (a Mertens threshold law); the layer influence on the distribution of divisors (the density correction of the Dirichlet error term) is the correction Delta(x) x^{-1/3} (a one-third correction law); and the sign of the correction (skewness of the error) follows the symmetric return law of random walks returning at plus or minus 1 (a symmetric return law). (Schnirelmann Mertens layer one-third return)

**中文。** 定义：Schnirelmann 的 ψ-密度的层：序列的 Schnirelmann 密度（正密度的最小段）与 Dirichlet 除数问题的交互 schn_layer。猜想：schn_layer（ψ-密度的层级的完全分类）的层的计数为对数层（对数层律）；且层与层的转移（密度的跳变）的阈值为 Mertens 积的倒数（Mertens 阈律）；除数的分布（Dirichlet 的误差项）的层的影响（误差的密度修正）为 Δ(x)·x^{-1/3} 的修正（三分之修正律）；修正的符号（误差的偏态）的随机游走的回复（回复定律）为 ±1 的对称（对称回复律）。
