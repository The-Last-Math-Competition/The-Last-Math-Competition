# 00000008156

**English.** Definition: The superconvergence of exact renormalization for hierarchical models: the superconvergence factor hier_conv of the transformation spectrum of Dyson hierarchical models. Conjecture: The exact value of hier_conv is (2^d - 1)^{-1} (a superconvergence formula); the error of the agreement between the hierarchical model and the translation-invariant model (an equivalence theorem) is a logarithmic correction log L; the correction vanishes (strict equivalence) uniformly for d at least 4 (a 4-dimensional equivalence law); and the complete list of positions of Borel-summability singularities of the hierarchical model is the arithmetic lattice 2 pi Z i (a hierarchical singularity lattice law). (Dyson hierarchical superconvergence lattice)

**中文。** 定义：层次（ hierarchical）模型的精确重整化的超收敛：Dyson 层次模型的变换的谱的超收敛因子 hier_conv。猜想：hier_conv 的精确值为 (2^d-1)^{-1}（超收敛公式）；且层次模型与平移模型的一致（等价定理）的误差为对数修正 log L（对数等价修正）；修正的消失（严格等价）在 d ≥ 4 一致（4 维等价律）；层次模型的 Borel 可和性的奇点的位置的完全列表为 2πZ·i 的算术格点（层次奇点格点律）。
