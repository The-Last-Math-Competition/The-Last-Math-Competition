# 00000008970

**English.** Definition: Virasoro constraints: symmetry algebra constraints. Conjecture: They uniquely determine the generalized Toda tau function, with the number of free constants the Hurwitz dimension. (Virasoro uniqueness Toda)

**中文。** 定义：Virasoro 约束指对称代数约束。猜想：其唯一确定广义 Toda τ 函数且自由常数为 Hurwitz 维数。（Virasoro 唯一 Toda）
