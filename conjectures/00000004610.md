# 00000004610

**English.** Definition: The interface area and the topological type are the two invariants of a bubble cluster. Conjecture: There exist two minimal bubble clusters with the same total interface area but different chamber topologies, and the minimal dimension of the separation is three, realized by spiral dissections. (three-dimensional area-topology separation)

**中文。** 定义：界面积分与拓扑型指泡团的两个不变量。猜想：存在两个极小泡团总界面积相同而腔室拓扑不同,分离的最小维数为三且由螺旋剖分实现。（界面积-拓扑三维分离）
