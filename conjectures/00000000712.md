# 00000000712

**English.** Definition: The p-adic exponential exp_p has radius of convergence p^{−1/(p−1)}. Conjecture: exp_p takes transcendental values at every nonzero algebraic point; and the proof path for transcendence is Mahler's method. (p-adic exponential transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：p 进指数 exp_p 的收敛半径为 p^{−1/(p−1)}。猜想：exp_p 在非零代数点的取值为超越数；且超越性的证明路径为 Mahler 方法。（p 进指数超越性）
