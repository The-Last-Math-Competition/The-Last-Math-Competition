**FLO-01 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The nondegenerate lower bound of the Arnold conjecture: the lower bound n_fix(M) on the count of fixed points of Hamiltonian isotopies of a symplectic manifold (M,omega). Conjecture: n_fix is at least the cup length of the sum of Betti numbers b_i(M;Z_2) plus one (a cup length law) after deformation; the correction for degeneracy (critical fixed points) is the multiplicity of the generating function of isolated multiple roots (a generating multiplicity law); and the saturation of the nondegenerate lower bound (the minimal example attaining it) is the torus T^{2n} (a torus saturation law), the saturated count being the power 2^{2n} (a two-power law). (Arnold cup-length torus saturation)

**中文。** 定义：Arnold 猜想的非退化下界：辛流形 (M,ω) 的 Hamilton 同痕的不动点的计数的下界 n_fix(M)。猜想：n_fix ≥ Σ b_i(M; Z₂) 的 cup-length 的加一（cup 长律）在形变后成立；且退化（临界不动点）的修正为孤立多重根的生成函数的重数（生成重数律）；非退化下界的饱和（达到的最小例）为环面 T^{2n}（环面饱和律），饱和的计数为 2^{2n} 的幂（二幂律）。
