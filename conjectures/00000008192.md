# 00000008192

**CNT-22 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: The homogenization of transcendence criteria: the computational version mahler_alg(n) of the criterion for algebraicity of p-automatic sequences by Mahler's method. Conjecture: mahler_alg (a verifiable certificate for transcendence of values of Mahler functions at algebraic points) has polynomial length (a polynomial certificate law); the randomized verification of certificates has one-sided error 1/3 (a one-sided one-third law); and the minimal example of transcendence (the shortest proof of transcendence) — the value of the sum of z^{2^k} at z = 1/2 — has proof length on the order of 10^4 steps (a shortest proof-step prediction). (Mahler polynomial certificate 2^k example)

**中文。** 定义：超越数判别的齐次化：Mahler 方法的 p-自动序列的代数性的判别的计算版 mahler_alg(n)。猜想：mahler_alg（Mahler 函数在代数点的值的超越性的可验证证书）的长度为多项式（多项式证书律）；且证书的验证的随机化为 1-sided 错误 1/3（单侧三之一律）；超越的极小例子（超越性的最短证明）为 Σz^{2^k} 在 z=1/2 的值的证明长度为 10⁴ 步的量级（最短证明步长预测）。
