**GBR-16 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: The Balmer spectrum: the prime ideal spectrum of tensor triangular geometry. Conjecture: The Balmer spectrum of the stable category of a self-injective algebra is an explicit set of supporting subcategories; the topology of the spectrum is a finite stratification of the Zariski topology, and the stratification is given by the dimension-spectrum function of preprojective components. (stable category Balmer spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Balmer 谱：张量三角几何的素理想谱。猜想：自内射代数的稳定范畴的 Balmer 谱为支撑子范畴的显式集合；谱的拓扑为 Zariski 拓扑的有限层化且层化由预投射分量的维数谱函数给出。（稳定范畴 Balmer 谱）
