**IG2-22 ★** — source: 178_统计推断的几何_信息几何.md

**English.** Definition: The correction term is the second order of the complexity penalty, of geometric Akaike type. Conjecture: The two-dimensional correction formula is explicit in the logarithmic derivative of the volume, half the dimension times the log sample size for regular families; the consistency of the correction in nested-family selection is guaranteed by sample-exponential growth, with rate the squared dimension; and the sign of the correction is always negative, the sign given by the curvature contraction. (two-dimensional logarithmic-derivative correction of volume)

**中文。** 定义：修正项：罚量（复杂度罚）的次阶；几何赤池型修正。猜想：二维的修正公式为体积对数导数的显式且正则族时为半维乘对数样本；修正在嵌套族的选择中的一致性由样本指数增长保证且增长率为维数的平方；修正的符号恒为负且负号由曲率收缩给出。（二维体积对数导数修正）
