# 00000008189

**CNT-19 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: The computation of Bernoulli numbers and irregular pairs: the distribution irr_pairs(X) of pairs (p,k) with B_k = 0 mod p. Conjecture: The density of irr_pairs (the frequency of irregular primes p) is 1 - e^{-1/2} (an e-minus-half law); the irregularity index of p (the number of zeros) is Poisson(1/2) (a half-Poisson law); the density of primes with large irregularity index (at least 3) is e^{-1/2} (1/2)^3/3! (an explicit sparsity); and the expected number of counterexamples in the next exponential extension of the verified threshold 10^9 of the Vandiver conjecture (to 10^{11}) is 0.00 (a zero expected-counterexample law). (irregular pairs half-Poisson Vandiver expectation)

**中文。** 定义：Bernoulli 数与不规则素对的计算：B_k mod p = 0 的（p,k）对的分布 irr_pairs(X)。猜想：irr_pairs 的密度（p 为不规则的频率）为 1-e^{-1/2}（e-负半律）；且 p 的不规则指数（零的个数）为 1 的 Poisson(1/2)（半泊松律）；极大不规则指数的素数（≥3 的）的密度为 e^{-1/2}·(1/2)^3/3!（稀疏显式）；Vandiver 猜想的验证（p_k 的全消没）的已验证阈 10^{9} 的指数扩展（下一步 10^{11}）的预期反例数为 0.00（反例期望零律）。
