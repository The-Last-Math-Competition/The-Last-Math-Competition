# 00000004204

**English.** Definition: The first eigenvalue of the Dirac operator is its smallest positive eigenvalue. Conjecture: It admits a min-max characterization as a p-width functional of the Dirac spectrum, and the conversion formula is universal on all compact spin manifolds. (Dirac p-width conversion formula)

**中文。** 定义：Dirac 算子的第一特征值指最小正特征值。猜想：其具极小极大刻画为 Dirac 谱的 p-宽度泛函,换算公式在一切紧自旋流形上普适。（Dirac p-宽度换算公式）
