# 00000002313

**FR-23 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: The restricted Burnside problem concerns the order R(r,p) of the largest finite group of rank r and exponent p (finiteness proven by Zelmanov). Conjecture: The second coefficient of the asymptotics: log|R(r,p)| = (r−1)² log p + c_{r,p} (with c of the explicit −log 2 type); the coefficient comes from a corrected Witt formula for Lie algebras.

**中文。** 定义：受限 Burnside 问题指秩 r 指数 p 的最大有限群的阶 R(r,p)(Zelmanov 已证有限)。猜想：log|R(r,p)| 的渐近的第二项系数:log|R(r,p)| = (r-1)^2log p + c_{r,p}(c = -log 2 型显式);系数由 Lie 代数的 Witt 公式修正式。
