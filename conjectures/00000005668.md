# 00000005668

**RW2-18 ★** — source: 174_随机游走II_混合与覆盖.md

**English.** Definition: The short-distance asymptotics is the Green value at neighboring points. Conjecture: The Green value is always the explicit rational function of the neighbor vector, normalized to 1 at the axis diagonal; and the nearest-neighbor values are rational with denominator at most the factorial of the coordination number. (rational neighborhood of short-distance Green values)

**中文。** 定义：短距渐近：邻近点的格林函数值。猜想：格林值恒为邻格向量的显式有理函数；轴对角处归一化为 1；近邻的值为有理数且分母不超过格点的配位数阶乘。（短距格林有理邻域）
