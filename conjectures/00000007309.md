# 00000007309

**English.** Definition: The statistical stability of tensor CP decompositions. Conjecture: The perturbation sensitivity is the condition number, and the constant of the number is the product of factor matrices. (factor-product CP condition number)

**中文。** 定义：张量 CP 分解的统计稳定性。猜想：分解的稳定性：扰动的敏感度为条件数且数的常数为因子矩阵的乘积。（张量 CP 分解因子矩阵乘积敏感度条件数扰动稳定性）
