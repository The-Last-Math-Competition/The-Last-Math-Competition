# 00000005599

**English.** Definition: Bit growth is the swell of output coefficients. Conjecture: The bit-growth upper bound of sparse determinants is the explicit product of the nonzero spectrum, attained at triangular supports, and the bound is tight. (triangular-support product bound of sparse bit growth)

**中文。** 定义：位增长指输出系数的膨胀量。猜想：稀疏行列式的位增长上界为非零谱的显式积,三角支持处达到且上界紧。（稀疏三角支持位增长积界）
