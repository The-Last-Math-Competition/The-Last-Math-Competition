# 00000006343

**English.** Definition: The canonical basis with the genus relative to the classical basis. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the genus with the classical basis and the genus generating series is rational; the poles of the series are roots of unity; and the minimum of the genus is at parabolic Verma modules. (rational-series conversion of canonical genus)

**中文。** 定义：典范基：量子群的典范基与经典基的亏格。猜想：换算常数为与经典基的亏格显式且亏格生成级数为有理；级数的极点为单位根；亏格的极小在抛物 Verma 模处。（典范亏格有理级数换算）
