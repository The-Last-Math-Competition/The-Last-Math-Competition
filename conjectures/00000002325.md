**English.** Definition: The condition number μ is the Lipschitz constant of the inverse operation. Conjecture: The heavy-tail exponent of the distribution of μ is −(m+1) (exact); the correction in the projective case is −m (projection removes the scale).

**中文。** 定义：条件数 μ 指逆运算的 Lipschitz 常数。猜想：μ 的分布的重尾指数为 -(m+1)(精确);修正在射影情形为 -(m)(投影除掉尺度)。
