# 00000007528

**English.** Definition: The weight of etale cohomology as the modulus of Frobenius eigenvalues. Conjecture: The cohomology of smooth varieties is pure weight, the weight spectrum being the grading of the mixed structure; and the mixed weight gap is given by the boundary of the compactification. (Frobenius weight-spectrum stratification)

**中文。** 定义：平展上同调的权为 Frobenius 作用的特征值模。猜想：光滑簇的上同调为纯权且权的谱为混合结构的分次；且混合的权间隙由紧化的边界给出。（Frobenius 权谱分层）
