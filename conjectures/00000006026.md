**English.** Definition: The steepness exponent is the worst power of growth. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit structure of the exponent pair, controlled by the minimal codimension of gradient vanishing; the lower bound of the codimension is 2; and the steepness exponent is invariant under symplectic coordinate changes, the invariant being the full steepness spectrum. (minimal-codimension conversion of steepness exponents)
