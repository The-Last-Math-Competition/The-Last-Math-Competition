# 00000004578

**English.** Definition: The boundary of a Bers slice is its compactification boundary surface. Conjecture: The orbit of a boundary point under the scaling automorphism is dense if and only if the corresponding lamination is uniquely ergodic, and the criterion is complete and can be verified finitely from the switch vector. (unique-ergodicity density criterion of the Bers boundary)

**中文。** 定义：Bers 切片的边界指其紧化边界曲面。猜想：边界点在缩放自同构下的轨道稠密当且仅当对应层汐唯一遍历,判据完全且可由切换向量有限验证。（Bers 边界唯一遍历稠密判据）
