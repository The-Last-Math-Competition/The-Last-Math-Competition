# 00000002421

**English.** Definition: A harmonic covering is the covering type of a harmonic morphism. Conjecture: The degree spectrum of harmonic coverings of a genus-g curve is an explicit set; and the gaps of the set are given by Riemann–Hurwitz. (curve harmonic covering degree spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：调和覆盖为 harmonic morphism 的覆叠型。猜想：亏格 g 曲线的调和覆盖度数谱为显式集合；且集合的间隙由 Riemann–Hurwitz 给出。（曲线调和覆盖度数谱）
