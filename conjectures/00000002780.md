# 00000002780

**English.** Definition: Milman's quotient theorem: high-dimensional quotients of arbitrary spaces contain ℓ_p^n-type structure. Conjecture: The dimension threshold of Milman quotients: the lower bound for k in (1−ε)ℓ_p^k quotients of n-dimensional spaces is an explicit power of log n, with exponent decided by cotype. (Milman quotient dimension lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Milman 商定理：任意空间的高维商含 ℓ_p^n 型结构。猜想：Milman 商的维数阈值：n 维空间的 (1−ε)ℓ_p^{k} 商的 k 的下界为 log n 的显式幂且幂指数由 cotype 决定。（Milman 商维数下界）
