# 00000003894

**CA3-14 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: The comparison theorem of prismatic cohomology is its canonical isomorphism with étale cohomology via A_crys. Conjecture: The comparison isomorphism can be obtained without Tor-independence assumptions, and in the semistable case the dimensions of the gradeds of the prismatic filtration are exactly the multiplicities of the Hodge–Tate weights. (prismatic graded-weight multiplicity)

**中文。** 定义：棱镜上同调的比较定理指其与 étale 上同调经 A_crys 的典范同构。猜想：比较同构可不以 Tor 独立性为前提,且半稳定情形棱镜滤子的分次的维数恰为相应 Hodge–Tate 权重的重数。（棱镜分次-权重重数）
