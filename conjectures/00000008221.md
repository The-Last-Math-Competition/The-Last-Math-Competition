# 00000008221

**English.** Definition: The Shintani basis of Whittaker functionals: the convergence shintani_conv of the analytic continuation of bases of Whittaker modules. Conjecture: shintani_conv (the convergence radius of the series of the Whittaker basis) is 1 (a unit radius law); the Gram determinant of the orthogonalized basis (the Shintani determinant) is explicit as a rational product of gamma factors (a gamma product law); and the zeros of the determinant (Shintani singularities) are distributed as an equidistant lattice on the imaginary axis (an equidistant lattice law) with spacing pi/2 (a half-circle spacing law). (Shintani gamma product equidistant lattice)

**中文。** 定义：Whittaker 泛函的 Shintani 基：Whittaker 模的基的解析延拓的收敛 shintani_conv。猜想：shintani_conv（Whittaker 基的级数的收敛半径）= 1（单位半径律）；且基的正交化的 Gram 行列式（Shintani 行列式）的显式为 gamma 因子的有理乘积（gamma 乘积律）；行列式的零点（Shintani 的奇点）的分布为虚轴的等距格点（等距格点律），格距为 π/2（半圆格距律）。
