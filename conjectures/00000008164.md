# 00000008164

**English.** Definition: The preservation of the area law of Wilson loops under block renormalization of lattice gauge theory: wl_keep. Conjecture: The string tension renormalization constant of wl_keep (RG preservation of the string tension) is b^{-sigma} (a tension scaling law); the exactness of preservation (the infinite cascade of the area law) has Perimeter divergence cancellation constant (P-cancellation) equal to 1 (a complete cancellation law); and the minimal gauge group where cancellation fails (perimeter divergence residue) is SU(3) (an SU3 residue exception), the logarithmic coefficient of the residue being the normalization of N_c/2 (a large-N gauge residue law). (Wilson loop area law SU3 residue)

**中文。** 定义：格点规范理论的块重整化的 Wilson 圈的面积律的保持 wl_keep。猜想：wl_keep（弦张力的 RG 保持）的张力重整化常数为 b^{-σ}（张力尺度律）；且保持的精确性（面积律的无穷级联）的 Wilson 圈的 Perimeter 发散的相消常数（P-相消）为 1（完全相消律）；相消的失效（周长发散残余）的最小规范群为 SU(3)（SU3 残余例外），残余的对数的系数为 N_c/2 的规范化（大 N 规范残余律）。
