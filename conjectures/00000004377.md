**English.** Definition: The Waldhausen additivity theorem is the additivity of K-groups for sifted sequences. Conjecture: The additivity deficit term is the higher Waldhausen torsion, the deficit vanishes for two-fold sifted sequences, and the threshold 2 is optimal. (two-fold sifted threshold of the K additivity deficit)

**中文。** 定义：Waldhausen 加性定理指 K 群对筛序列的加性。猜想：加性亏损项为高阶 Waldhausen 挠,亏损在二可筛时消失,阈值 2 最优。（K 加性亏损二筛阈）
