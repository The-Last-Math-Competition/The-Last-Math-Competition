**English.** Definition: Chains of Artin–Schreier–Witt extensions. Conjecture: The trace-norm recursion of canonical generators of lifts of Z/p^n-extensions is an explicit Witt-vector addition (a Witt–Artin–Schreier recursion).

**中文。** 定义：Artin–Schreier–Witt 扩张链。猜想：ℤ/p^n-扩张的规范生成元 lift 的迹范数递推为 Witt 向量加法的显式(Witt-AS 递推)。
