**RG4-26 ◆** — source: 191_随机图计数II_渐近方法.md

**English.** Definition: The stability version of extremal formulas. Conjecture: Near-extremal graphs have small squared structural distance with explicit dimension-reduction step, the realization of the reduction being random edge deletion. (random-deletion dimension reduction of near-extremal stability)

**中文。** 定义：极端公式的稳定性版本。猜想：近极值图的结构距离平方小且降维步显式；且降维的实现为随机删边。（近极值稳定随机删边降维）
