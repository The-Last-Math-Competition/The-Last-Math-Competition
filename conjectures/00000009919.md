**PHT-09 ★** — source: 316_持久同调II_统计与稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: Gromov-Hausdorff stability: persistent homology control under metric perturbation without ambient space. Conjecture: The bottleneck distance of persistent homology under GH perturbation has a lower bound: there exist compact metric spaces only GH-distance delta apart whose persistence diagrams differ by an explicit multiple of delta; the multiple is tight, realized by separating two-point spaces from the circle. (GH stability lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Gromov–Hausdorff 稳定性：无环境空间的度量扰动下的持久同调控制。猜想：GH 扰动下持久同调的瓶颈距离有下界：存在仅 GH 距离 δ 接近的紧度量空间其持久图相距 δ 的显式倍数；倍数为紧且由双点空间与圆周的分离实现。（GH 稳定性下界）
