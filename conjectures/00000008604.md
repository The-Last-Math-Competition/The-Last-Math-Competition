# 00000008604

**English.** Definition: Wasserstein distance as an estimation loss. Conjecture: W_1 density estimation for d at least 3 suffers the curse at rate n^{-1/d}; and W_2 for smooth distributions is n^{-2/(d+4)} with explicit optimal bandwidth. (transport estimation rate dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：Wasserstein 距离作为估计损失。猜想：d ≥ 3 的 W₁ 密度估计为 n^{−1/d} 诅咒；光滑分布 W₂ 为 n^{−2/(d+4)} 且最优带宽显式。（传输估计速率分界）
