# 00000001429

**MC-09 ◆** — source: 33_概率论II_马氏链与粒子系统.md

**English.** Definition: The radius R_n of a DLA cluster is the maximal inradius after n particles attach. Conjecture: R_n ≤ n^{1/2}·exp(c·log^{1/2}n) for some absolute constant c, and R_n ≥ n^{1/2}·(log n)^{-1}; that is, the DLA radius has half-power main term with a subexponential logarithmic correction.

**中文。** 定义：DLA 簇的半径 R_n 指附着 n 个粒子后的最大内切半径。猜想：R_n ≤ n^{1/2}·exp(c·log^{1/2}n) 对某绝对常数 c;且 R_n ≥ n^{1/2}·(log n)^{-1};即 DLA 的半径以半幂律为主项,对数修正为次指数级。
