**English.** Definition: Full normalization is the layer-by-layer normalization sequence of the mouse order. Conjecture: The minimal number of normalization steps needed for the full mice spectrum is omega layers, the obstacle measure strictly increases at each step, and the number of steps is incompressible. (minimal number of full normalization steps)
