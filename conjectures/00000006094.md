# 00000006094

**NC3-24 ★** — source: 188_非凸数值优化.md

**English.** Definition: The bias-variance tradeoff is the balance of divergence and capacity terms of the excess risk in empirical risk minimization. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit balance of divergence with capacity terms, the main order the square root of divergence over sample size; the two terms are equal at the balance point; and for infinite second moments of heavy tails the balance degenerates to single-term type, the degeneration given by Cauchy principal values. (square-root main order of the divergence-capacity balance)

**中文。** 定义：偏差-方差平衡：经验风险最小化中超额风险的散度项与容量项的折中。猜想：换算常数为散度与容量项的显式平衡且主阶为根号散度除以样本量；平衡点的两项相等；重尾二阶矩无穷时平衡退化为单项型且退化由 Cauchy 主值给出。（散度容量平衡根号主阶）
