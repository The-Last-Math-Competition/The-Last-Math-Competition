# 00000004431

**English.** Definition: The frozen boundary of a lozenge tiling is the edge of the limiting liquid domain. Conjecture: The boundary curvature is given by an explicit differential-geometric formula of the saddle point of the partition function, and the curvature has a square-root singularity at tangency points with exact exponent. (square-root singularity of the frozen boundary curvature)

**中文。** 定义：菱形密铺的冻结边界指极限液态域的边缘。猜想：边界曲率由配分函数鞍点的显式微分几何公式给出,曲率在切点处为平方根奇性且指数精确。（冻结边界曲率平方根奇性）
