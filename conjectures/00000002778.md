# 00000002778

**AG3-08 ★** — source: 78_渐近几何_Banach局部理论II.md

**English.** Definition: Asymptotic ℓ_p spaces: asymptotically ℓ_p everywhere. Conjecture: Hyperbolic rigidity of asymptotic ℓ_p: the reflexivization of asymptotic ℓ₁ does not exist, and the ultrapower of asymptotic ℓ_p is a homomorphic image of an ℓ_p ultrapower; the kernel of the image is an asymptotically zero-type space. (asymptotic lp ultrapower rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：asymptotic ℓ_p 空间：渐近结构处处 ℓ_p。猜想：渐近 ℓ_p 的双曲刚性：渐近 ℓ₁ 的自反化不存在且渐近 ℓ_p 的超幂为 ℓ_p 超幂的同态像；像的核为渐近零型空间。（渐近 lp 超幂刚性）
