**English.** Definition: The almost Mathieu operator (H_λ u)(n) = u(n+1)+u(n−1)+2λcos(2π(nα+θ))u(n). Conjecture: At critical coupling λ = 1, the Hausdorff dimension of the spectrum is 1/2 (subcritical dimension 1 and supercritical known < 2; the exact critical value).

**中文。** 定义：几乎 Mathieu 算子 (H_λ u)(n) = u(n+1)+u(n−1)+2λcos(2π(nα+θ))u(n)。猜想：临界耦合 λ = 1 处谱的 Hausdorff 维数为 1/2(亚临界维数 1、超临界已知 < 2;临界情形的精确值)。
