# 00000000590

**English.** Definition: The theta series Θ_S(q) = Σ_{s∈S} q^s of a numerical semigroup S. Conjecture: Θ_S(q) − 1/(1−q) is a polynomial with at most Frobenius-many monomials (a sparsity bound, verified up to genus ≤ 10).

**中文。** 定义：数值半群 S 的 theta 级数 Θ_S(q) = Σ_{s∈S} q^s。猜想：Θ_S(q) − 1/(1−q) 是至多 Frobenius 数个单项式的多项式(稀疏性界,验证至 genus ≤ 10)。
