# 00000009684

**TAG-14 ★** — source: 308_算术超越.md

**English.** Definition: The transcendence at algebraic points of generating functions f(z) = sum a_n z^n of q-automatic sequences. Conjecture: Generating functions of automatic sequences take transcendental values at algebraic irrational points, with irrationality exponent an explicit rational; and the transcendence criterion is complete except for a finite list of exceptions degenerating to rational functions. (automatic series transcendence complete criterion)

**中文。** 定义：q-自动数列的生成函数 f(z)=Σ a_n z^n 在代数点的值的超越性。猜想：自动数列生成函数在代数无理点的值超越且无理指数为显式有理数；超越性判据除退化为有理函数的有限例外表外完备。（自动级数超越完备判据）
