# 00000009767

**English.** Definition: Gelfand numbers and Kolmogorov numbers: two kinds of s-numbers of identity maps between Banach spaces. Conjecture: The Gelfand and Kolmogorov numbers of identity maps from l_p^n to l_q^n have explicit formulas when p < q; the termwise ratio of the two sequences converges to an explicit constant of a Legendre transcendental function. (Gelfand-Kolmogorov formula)

**中文。** 定义：Gelfand 数与 Kolmogorov 数：Banach 空间恒等映射的两种 s-数。猜想：恒等映射从 ℓ_p^n 到 ℓ_q^n 的 Gelfand 数与 Kolmogorov 数在 p<q 时有显式公式；两序列的逐项比值收敛到 Legendre 超越函数的显式常数。（Gelfand-Kolmogorov 公式）
