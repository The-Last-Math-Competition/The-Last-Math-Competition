# 00000004855

**English.** Definition: A non-cylindrical action is a group action with uniform kernel. Conjecture: The existence criterion of a universal non-cylindrical constant is an explicit exclusion condition on rank-two subactions, verifiable finitely, with the constant given by the separation lower bound. (existence criterion of the universal non-cylindrical constant)

**中文。** 定义：非柱面作用指带一致核的群作用。猜想：普适非柱面常数的存在判据为秩二子作用的显式排除条件,条件可有限验证且常数由分离下界给出。（非柱面普适常数存在判据）
