# 00000001140

**LT-40 △** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The flatness of the representation category of Frobenius kernels T_n in characteristic p means the functor to sheaves on the flag variety preserves coherence. Conjecture: There exists a p-curve (a flat cover of height p over the flag variety) such that the T₁-module category is equivalent to the category of quasicoherent sheaves on this cover, and for p = 2 the equivalence is given by explicit Weyl descent.

**中文。** 定义：特征 p 的 Frobenius 核 T_n 的表示范畴的平坦性指其到旗簇上层的函子保持凝聚性。猜想：存在 p-曲线(.flag 簇上高度 p 的平坦覆盖)使 T₁-模范畴等价于该覆盖上的拟凝聚层范畴,且该等价在 p=2 时由显式Weyl 下降给出。
