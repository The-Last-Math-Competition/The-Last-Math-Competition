**English.** Definition: The breakup threshold is the critical perturbation at which tori disappear. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit lower envelope of the critical function, given by the series truncation of conjugate-point conditions, and exact in the small-twist limit. (conjugate-point envelope formula of breakup thresholds)

**中文。** 定义：破裂阈值指环面消失的临界扰动量。猜想：换算公式为临界函数的显式下包络,由共轭点条件的级数截断给出且小扭转极限精确。（破裂临界共轭点包络式）
