**English.** Definition: Barycentric subdivision: the chain complex of faces. Conjecture: Flag-ization under barycentric subdivision: the barycentric subdivision of every complex is a flag complex; subdivision preserves homology, and the growth of the g-vector under subdivision follows an explicit graded Fibonacci law. (barycentric subdivision flag-ization law)

**中文。** 定义：barycentric 细分：面的链复形。猜想：重心细分的旗性：一切复形的重心细分为旗复形；细分保持同调且 g-向量在细分下的增长为分次 Fibonacci 型显式律。（重心细分旗化律）
