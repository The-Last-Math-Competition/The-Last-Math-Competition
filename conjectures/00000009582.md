# 00000009582

**English.** Definition: The growth relation between Morse index and area: the asymptotic behavior of index/area for sequences of minimal hypersurfaces with index tending to infinity. Conjecture: In closed manifolds with general metrics, index(a_k)/a_k converges to a positive constant c — the Weyl law holds; c is given by a universal function of dimension and the Ricci lower bound, with c = 1/(2 pi) for constant-curvature spheres and strictly larger c for hyperbolic manifolds. (index-area Weyl law)

**中文。** 定义：Morse 指标与面积的增长关系：指标趋于无穷的极小超曲面序列的 index/area 渐近行为。猜想：一般度量的闭流形中 index(a_k)/a_k 收敛到正常数 c，Weyl 律成立；c 由维数与 Ricci 下界的普适函数给出，常曲率球面时 c=1/(2π)，双曲流形时 c 严格增大。（指标-面积 Weyl 律）
