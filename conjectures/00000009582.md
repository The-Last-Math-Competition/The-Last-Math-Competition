**English.** Definition: Morse index and index/area asymptotic row of minimal hypersurface sequence of growth relations: index tends to infinite of area is. Conjecture: closed manifold in index(a_k)/a_k of general metric converges to positive constant c, Weyl becomes; c by dimension and universal function of Ricci lower bound gives, curvature ball plane when c=1/(2π), hyperbolic manifold when c strictly increases. (index - area Weyl)
