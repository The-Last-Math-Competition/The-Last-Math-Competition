**GT-26 ★** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: Kempe equivalence classes are the classes of 3-colorings connected by two-color chain exchanges. Conjecture: The 3-colorings of planar triangulations are connected under Kempe equivalence (known for cubic graphs); the general triangulation case follows by the connectivity of exchange graphs of the dual hexagonal systems.

**中文。** 定义：Kempe 等价类指 3-染色经双链交换连通的类。猜想：平面三角剖分的 3-染色在 Kempe 等价下连通(三次图情形已知);一般三角剖分由对偶六角系统的交换图连通性传递。
