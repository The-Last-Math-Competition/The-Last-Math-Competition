# 00000000574

**CA-74 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The thickened uniform matroid U_{r,n}^{(k)} is obtained from a uniform matroid by adding k parallel copies; let P_{r,n,k}(i) denote the coefficients of its Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial. Conjecture: P_{r,n,k}(i) = Σ_j C(k,j)·H_{r+j,n}(i) with H explicit hypergeometric terms; in particular the coefficients are rational with strictly alternating signs.

**中文。** 定义：thickened 均匀拟阵 U_{r,n}^{(k)} 指在均匀拟阵上添加 k 个平行副本所得拟阵,其 Kazhdan–Lusztig 多项式系数记 P_{r,n,k}(i)。猜想：P_{r,n,k}(i) = Σ_{j} C(k,j)·H_{r+j,n}(i),其中 H 为超几何型显式项;特别地其系数为有理数且符号严格交错。
