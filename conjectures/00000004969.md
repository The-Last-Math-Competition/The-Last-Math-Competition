**English.** Definition: The cactus rank is the minimal number of scheme points of a pointwise decomposition, contrasted with the ordinary rank of monomial terms. Conjecture: The maximal value of the cactus-rank-to-rank difference of three-variable monomials is an explicit constant attained by binomial-type monomials; the realizable set of the difference is an explicit subset of {0,1,2}; each difference value is decided by the elementary symmetric functions of the exponents, with decision threshold a power of 2. (maximal cactus-rank difference of three variables)

**中文。** 定义：cactus 秩：多项式秩分解为点态展开的最少概形点数；与普通秩（最少单项式项数）对照。猜想：三变量单项式的 cactus 秩与秩之差的最大值为显式常数且由二项式型单项式达到；差值的可实现集为 {0,1,2} 的显式子集；每个差值由指数的初等对称函数判别且判别阈值为 2 的幂。（三变量 cactus-秩差极大值）
