**English.** Definition: For a mixed tiling by cubes of two edge lengths {1,s}, the contact number N(n,s) is the maximum number of adjacent (face-sharing) pairs. Conjecture: For s=1/2 in R^n, N(n,1/2) = 3*2^{n-1} + n - 2; and for irrational s the contact number of any mixed tiling is at most two thirds of the same-dimensional Keller-type bound. (contact numbers of mixed cube tilings)

**中文。** 定义：两种边长 {1,s} 的立方体混合铺砌的接触数指相邻（共面接触）立体对的最大数目 N(n, s)。猜想：s=1/2 时 R^n 中单位立方体与半边长立方体的混合铺砌接触数 N(n,1/2)=3·2^{n-1}+n-2；s 为无理数时任意混合铺砌的接触数不超过同维 Keller 型上界的 2/3。
