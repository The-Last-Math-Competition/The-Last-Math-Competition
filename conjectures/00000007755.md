# 00000007755

**ADY-05 ◆** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: A postcritically finite (PCF) polynomial is one all of whose critical points are preperiodic. Conjecture: For each fixed degree d there are only finitely many PCF polynomials with algebraic coefficients; and for d=2 the count N(H) of PCF parameters of height at most H is asymptotic to C H^{1/2}, with the constant C given explicitly by the decay of the leading Bottcher coefficient. (PCF counting asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：postcritically finite（PCF）多项式指所有临界点均预周期的映射。猜想：每个固定次数 d 的 PCF 多项式（系数为代数数）只有有限多个；且 d=2 时 PCF 参数在高度 H 内的个数 N(H) 渐近为 C·H^{1/2}，常数 C 由 Böttcher 坐标首项系数的衰减显式给出。
