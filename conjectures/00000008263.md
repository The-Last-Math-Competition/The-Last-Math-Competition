**SFL-03 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The collapse threshold of particle filters (sequential Monte Carlo): the collapse rate coll_rate of the effective sample size N_eff. Conjecture: coll_rate (per-step effective particle decay) is the explicit 1 - kappa^2 (kappa the incremental information, a kappa-squared decay law); the optimal resampling (systematic versus multinomial) halves the variance (a variance halving law); and the constant in the particle-count law for the consensus of particle counts (L^2 error of the filtering distribution equal to c/N) is half of Var(weight) times the dimension (a Var-dimension half law). (particle collapse kappa-squared variance halving)

**中文。** 定义：粒子滤波（sequential Monte Carlo）的粒子数塌缩的阈值：有效样本数 N_eff 的塌缩率 coll_rate。猜想：coll_rate（每步的有效粒子衰减）= 1-κ² 的显式（κ 为增量信息，κ² 衰减律）；且重采样的最优（系统重采样 vs 多项式）的方差的减半（方差减半律）；粒子数的共识（滤波分布的 L² 误差 = c/N 的粒子数定律）的常数 c = Var(权重)·维度的半数（Var-维度半数律）。
