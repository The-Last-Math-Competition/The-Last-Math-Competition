**English.** Definition: The factorization of a graphon is the kernel decomposition by spectral projections. Conjecture: The constant of the local factor lemma is exactly the reciprocal of the minimal spectral gap, and the constant is optimal on interval kernels and cannot be improved. (spectral gap constant of factorization)
