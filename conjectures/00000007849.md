# 00000007849

**RAM-09 △** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The satisfiability threshold alpha_c(k) of random k-SAT in the density parameter. Conjecture: The asymptotic expansion of alpha_c(k) is 2^k log 2 - (1+log 2)/2 + O(2^{-k}) with the second coefficient exact; alpha_c(k) is irrational for all k at least 3; and the threshold window width is Theta(n^{-1/2+2^{-k}}), the 2^{-k} correction in the exponent coming from the second-order effect of covering-gap saturation. (k-SAT threshold second-order expansion)

**中文。** 定义：随机 SAT 的饱和阈值 α_c(k)（k-SAT 密度阈值）。猜想：α_c(k) 的渐近展开为 2^k log 2 - (1+log 2)/2 + O(2^{-k})，第二项系数精确；且 α_c(k) 对一切 k≥3 为无理数；阈值窗口宽度为 Θ(n^{-1/2+2^{-k}})，指数中的 2^{-k} 修正来自覆盖间隙饱和的二阶效应。
