# 00000009530

**English.** Definition: The Petz Renyi relative entropy D_alpha(rho||sigma) = (1/(alpha-1)) log tr(rho^alpha sigma^{1-alpha}) for alpha in (0,2); the data-processing inequality says D_alpha contracts under quantum channels, with equality cases the equidistant channels. Conjecture: For each alpha in (0,1), alpha-equidistant channels come in only two classes: unitary rotations on the support of sigma, and collapse channels mapping into a one-dimensional space outside the support of sigma, with no intermediate cases; for alpha at least 2 a third class generated by partial transpose structures appears, and the three-class classification is complete. (Renyi equidistance rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Petz Rényi 相对熵 D_α(ρ‖σ)=(1/(α−1))log tr(ρ^α σ^{1−α})，α∈(0,2)；数据加工不等式说 D_α 在量子信道下收缩，保距情形即等号成立的信道。猜想：对每个 α∈(0,1)，α-保距信道只有两类：σ 支撑上的酉旋转与映入 σ 支撑外一维的崩溃信道，无中间情形；α≥2 时新增的第三类由部分转置结构生成，三类分类完备。（Renyi 保距刚性）
