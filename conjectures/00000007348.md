# 00000007348

**English.** Definition: Nonstandard harmonic analysis with the infinitesimal side of Fourier. Conjecture: The hyper version of Shannon's theorem holds, and the infinitesimal error of the version is the band-limited residual. (band-limited nonstandard Shannon sampling)

**中文。** 定义：非标准调和分析：Fourier 的无穷小侧。猜想：非标准采样：Shannon 定理的超版本且本的无穷小误差为带限残差。（非标准 Shannon 带限残差超版本采样无穷小误差）
