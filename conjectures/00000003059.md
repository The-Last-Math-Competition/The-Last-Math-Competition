# 00000003059

**IP-19 ★** — source: 87_非线性PDE的可积检验.md

**English.** Definition: Bäcklund transformation tests: nonlinear superposition formulae. Conjecture: The existence of superposition formulae: the nonlinear superposition formula of an integrable equation exists if and only if the commutation relation closes, and the algebra of the closure is the nilpotent lattice of an infinite-dimensional group. (Backlund superposition nilpotent lattice)

**中文。** 定义：Bäcklund 变换的检验：非线性叠加公式。猜想：叠加公式的存在：可积方程的非线性叠加公式的存在当且仅当交换关系封闭且封闭的代数为无穷维群的幂零格。（Backlund 叠加幂零格）
