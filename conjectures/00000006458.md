**English.** Definition: Identification with the digit count of algebraic numbers. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the minimal digit count and the algebraic degree is linear in the digits; the coefficient is given by the classical metric; and the lower bound of the digits is the degree times the logarithmic height. (linear degree-digit coefficient conversion)

**中文。** 定义：识别：代数数的识别位数。猜想：换算常数为最小位数的显式且代数度与位数线性；系数由经典度量给出；位数的下界为代数度乘对数高度。（代数度位数线性系数换算）
