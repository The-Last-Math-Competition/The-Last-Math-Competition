**GZ-11 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Predual rigidity: the equivalence of measure-level weak boundedness of the Riesz transform with the geometric side. Conjecture: The Riesz transform is weakly bounded on a measure if and only if the measure is of David–Semmes type; the quantitative constant of weak boundedness is an explicit exponential function of the first eigenvalue of the Corominas measure. (predual rigidity characterization)

**中文。** 定义：预对偶刚性：Riesz 变换的测度弱有界性与几何侧的等价。猜想：Riesz 变换的测度弱有界当且仅当测度为 David–Semmes 型；弱有界性的量化常数为 Corominas 测度的第一特征量的显式指数函数。（预对偶刚性刻画）
