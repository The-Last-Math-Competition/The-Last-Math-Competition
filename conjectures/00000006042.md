**English.** Definition: A switching graph is the graph-family structure of correctness. Conjecture: Connected components are indexed by the crossing count of atomic pairings, the counting is closed, and the combination of the index is the cyclic enumeration. (cyclic-enumeration component indexing of switching graphs)
