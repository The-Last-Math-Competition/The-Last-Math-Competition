# 00000007871

**HCA-01 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The t-structure region of a stable infinity-category C is its (p,q) admissible region P(C) in Z^2 under numeration (numerically stable positive semidefinite quadratic forms). Conjecture: P(C) is a finitely generated convex lattice polytope; the vertex count of P(C) is uniformly controlled by the number of extremal points of the Bridgeland slope function, the two being equal for algebraic-geometric sources (derived categories of threefolds); and the area of the region gives a uniform upper bound for the Serre dimension of C. (t-structure region polytope law)

**中文。** 定义：稳定 ∞-范畴 C 上的 t-结构区域定义为其在数值化（数值化极稳定半正定二次型）下的（p,q）允许区域 P(C)⊂Z²。猜想：P(C) 为有限生成的凸格多胞形；且 P(C) 的顶点数被 Bridgeland 斜率函数的极值点个数一致控制，两者在代数几何来源（三维簇的导出范畴）时相等；区域面积给出 C 的 Serre 维数的一致上界。
