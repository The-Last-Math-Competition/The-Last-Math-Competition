**VR4-19 ★** — source: 227_变分法II.md

**English.** Definition: The Maupertuis principle with the geometry of the action. Conjecture: The geodesization of natural systems holds, and the metric of the geodesization is of Jacobi type. (Jacobi-type Maupertuis geodesization)

**中文。** 定义：Maupertuis 原理：作用量的几何。猜想：Maupertuis 的测地化：自然系统的测地化且化的度量为 Jacobi 型。（Maupertuis Jacobi 型测地化自然系统度量）
