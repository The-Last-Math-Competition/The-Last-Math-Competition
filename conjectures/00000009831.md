**WEX-11 ★** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: Multi-parameter maximal functions: weighted constants of multiparameter dyadic maxima. Conjecture: The ratio of the m-parameter weighted maximal constant to the product of single-parameter constants tends to 1; the convergence speed is an explicit exponential decay in the parameter count, controlled by the covering number of independent half-spaces. (multiparameter maximal multiplicativity law)

**中文。** 定义：多参数最大函数：多参数 dyadic 最大的加权常数。猜想：m 参数加权最大的常数与各单参数常数之积的比值趋于 1；收敛速度为参数数目的显式指数衰减且衰减由独立半空间的覆盖数控制。（多参数最大乘性律）
