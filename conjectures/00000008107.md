# 00000008107

**English.** Definition: The dimension spectrum of Quillen complexes of fusion systems: the connectivity dimension q_dim(F) of the Quillen complex (the complex of maximal elementary abelian subgroups) of |L|. Conjecture: q_dim(F) >= 2 (a connectivity rigidity, the fusion version of the Quillen conjecture); the criterion for simple connectivity of the complex is rank at least 3 (three-rank simple connectivity); and the exceptions to vanishing of higher homology of the complex (Quillen sphericity) are three of the seven rank-2 systems (a seven-system sphericity trichotomy: three spherical, four nonspherical). (Quillen complex connectivity three-rank trichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 Quillen 类的维数谱：|L| 的 Quillen 复形（极大初等交换子群复形）的连通维数 q_dim(F)。猜想：q_dim(F) ≥ 2（连通刚性，Quillen 猜想融合版）；且复形的单连通的判据为 rank ≥3（三秩单连通）；复形的高维同调的消没（Quillen 球状性）的例外为秩 2 的七系中的三个（七系球状三分律：三球状-四非球状）。
