# 00000000300

**English.** Definition: Invariant subspaces of polynomially compact operators. Conjecture: If p(T) is compact, then a nontrivial invariant subspace exists; and the determination is settled through West's decomposition. (polynomially compact invariant subspace via West decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：多项式紧算子的不变子空间。猜想：p(T) 紧蕴含非平凡不变子空间；且判定的核为 West 分解。（多项式紧 West 分解不变子空间）
