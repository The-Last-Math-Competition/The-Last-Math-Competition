**English.** Definition: The Wasserstein distance is the order-matching metric of persistence diagrams. Conjecture: The second-order conversion is of order-reciprocal type for Lipschitz functions, and the kernel of the reciprocal is the radius regularity exponent. (radius-exponent reciprocal of second-order Wasserstein)

**中文。** 定义：Wasserstein 距离指持久图的阶匹配度量。猜想：二阶的换算为 Lipschitz 函数时阶倒数型；且倒数的核为半径正则指数。（二阶 Wasserstein 半径指数倒数）
