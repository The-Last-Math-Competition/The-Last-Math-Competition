# 00000006883

**English.** Definition: The complement of an orbit closure is the core object of classifying geometry. Conjecture: The degree of the irreducible components of the complement has a polynomial upper bound, the exponent of the bound being the codimension of the variety; and the tightness of the polynomial bound is attained by the determinant variety. (polynomial degree bound of closure-complement components)

**中文。** 定义：轨道闭包的补集为分类几何的核心对象。猜想：补集的不可约分支的度数有多项式上界且界的指数为簇的余维；且多项式界的紧性由行列式簇达到。（闭包补集分支度数多项式界）
