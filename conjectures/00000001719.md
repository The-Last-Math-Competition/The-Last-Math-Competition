# 00000001719

**English.** Definition: A pro-nilsystem is an inverse limit of nilsystems; the equicontinuous factor is the maximal equicontinuous quotient. Conjecture: The equicontinuous factor of a minimal system (X,T) is pro-nil if and only if the return-time functions of all of its regionally recurrent sets are of uniformly bounded variation; the criterion is purely combinatorial, explicit via return-time variations by level.

**中文。** 定义：pro-nilsystem 指逆向极限的 nilsystem;等度连续因子指极大等度连续商系统。猜想：极小系统 (X,T) 的等度连续因子为 pro-nil 当且仅当其一切区域递归集的返回时间函数为一致有界变差;判据为纯组合,由逐阶返回时间变差显式。
