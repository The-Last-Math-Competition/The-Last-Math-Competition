# 00000004678

**English.** Definition: A Puiseux pair is a parametrization pair of a plane branch, and the semigroup is its value semigroup. Conjecture: The realizable table of three-branch semigroups is characterized by an explicit divisibility condition on two generators, and the table is closed and consistent with the corresponding invariants. (divisibility table of three-branch semigroups)

**中文。** 定义：Puiseux 对指平面分支的参数化对,半群指其值半群。猜想：三分支的半群可实现表由两个生成元的显式整除条件刻画,表封闭且与对应不变量一致。（三分支半群整除表）
