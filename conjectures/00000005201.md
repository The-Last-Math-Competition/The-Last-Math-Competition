# 00000005201

**FF2-01 ◆** — source: 159_随机矩阵V_自由群因子.md

**English.** Definition: Free group factors are the group von Neumann algebras of free groups. Conjecture: The conversion invariant of the isomorphism problem is constructed from an explicit sequence of alternating type; the invariant distinguishes adjacent ranks if and only if the spectral gaps of the sequence grow exponentially, and the criterion is verifiable. (spectral-gap invariant of free group factors)

**中文。** 定义：自由群因子指自由群的群冯诺依曼代数。猜想：同构问题的换算不变量由交错型的显式序列构造,不变量区分相邻秩当且仅当序列谱间隙指数增长且判据可验。（自由群因子谱间隙不变量）
