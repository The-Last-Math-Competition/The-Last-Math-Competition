# 00000008698

**English.** Definition: Transport of empirical measures is the convergence of sample transport. Conjecture: The exact rate in one dimension is n^{-1/2}; and the higher-dimensional correction is a power of the dimension. (empirical transport dimension power correction)

**中文。** 定义：经验测度的传输为样本传输的收敛。猜想：一维的精确速率为 n^{-1/2}；且高维的修正为维数幂。（经验传输维数幂修正速率）
