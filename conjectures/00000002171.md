# 00000002171

**EC-01 ◆** — source: 58_极值集合论与组合.md

**English.** Definition: Frankl's union-closed conjecture states that a union-closed family contains an element appearing in at least half its members. Conjecture: The conjecture holds; and the ground-set size of a minimal counterexample family is always at least twelve, with twelve attained. (Frankl counterexample ground size twelve)

**中文。** 定义：Frankl 并闭猜想指并闭族中存在出现于至少一半成员的元素。猜想：猜想成立；且最小反例族的域尺寸下界恒为十二并达到。（Frankl 反例域尺寸十二）
