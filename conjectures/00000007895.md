# 00000007895

**English.** Definition: The categorical slope functional of wall-crossing: the Hausel-type count HC(sigma,t) of a stability structure sigma with central charge Z, counting Donaldson-Thomas type weighted counts of stable objects at slope t. Conjecture: The correction of HC(sigma,t) under wall-crossing equals exactly the categorification of the Kontsevich-Soibelman integral identity; and the generating series of the invariants in the BPS heart is the WKB solution of the second-order differential equation y'' = Q(z) y (Q a quartic polynomial with coefficients from the periods of Z), whose Stokes constants are the integer invariant quantum of HC. (categorical KS Stokes invariants)

**中文。** 定义：畴壁穿越（wall-crossing）的范畴化斜率泛函：稳定结构 σ 的中心电荷 Z 与范畴的稠稳定性结构的哈钦斯（Hausel）型计数 HC(σ,t)。猜想：HC(σ,t)（斜率 t 处的稳定对象的 Donaldson–Thomas 型加权计数）在墙穿越时的修正恒等于 Kontsevich–Soibelman 的积分恒等式的范畴化；且 BPS 心脏中的不变量的生成级数为二次微分方程 y″=Q(z)y（Q 为四次多项式，系数由 Z 的周期给出）的 WKB 解，其 Stokes 常数为 HC 的整数不变量子。
