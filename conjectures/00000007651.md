# 00000007651

**English.** Definition: Resonant interaction as three-wave phase matching. Conjecture: The energy-exchange period of resonant three-wave groups is given explicitly by coupling coefficients; and the conservation of the period is the Manley-Rowe relation. (Manley-Rowe three-wave resonance)

**中文。** 定义：共振相互作用为三波相位匹配。猜想：共振三波组的能量交换周期由耦合系数显式给出；且周期的守恒为 Manley–Rowe 关系。（三波共振 Manley-Rowe）
