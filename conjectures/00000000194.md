# 00000000194

**English.** Definition: S = Σ_{n≥1} (−1)^n/(F_n + n), with F_n the Fibonacci numbers. Conjecture: S is irrational and linearly independent of Σ (−1)^n/F_n; the criterion for independence is the inequality between the growth rates of the least common multiples of the two denominator sequences, with difference exponent an explicit multiple of log φ. (Fibonacci-shifted series independence)

**中文。** 定义：S=Σ_{n≥1} (−1)^n/(F_n+n)，F_n 为 Fibonacci 数。猜想：S 无理且与 Σ (−1)^n/F_n 线性无关；无关性的判据为两序列分母的最小公倍的增长率不相等，其差指数为 log φ 的显式倍数。（Fibonacci 移位级数无关）
