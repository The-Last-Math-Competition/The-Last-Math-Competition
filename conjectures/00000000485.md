# 00000000485

**English.** Definition: The absolute value of an irreducible character of S_n at an n-cycle. Conjecture: The proportion of shapes λ with |χ_λ(c_n)| > 1 tends to 1 (linked to the parity cancellation in Murnaghan–Nakayama).

**中文。** 定义：S_n 特征标表中不可约特征标在 n-循环处的绝对值。猜想：|χ_λ(c_n)| > 1 的形状 λ 的比例 → 1(与 Murnaghan–Nakayama 的奇偶消散)。
