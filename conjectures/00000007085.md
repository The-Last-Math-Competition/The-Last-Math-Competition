**English.** Definition: The mirror of spheres with the homological mirror test of Fukaya. Conjecture: The Fukaya category of the two-dimensional sphere is the homology of the cotangent, and the algebra of the homology is the representation ring of the group. (cotangent representation-ring sphere mirror)
