# 00000001708

**EG-18 ★** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Conjecture: The possible values of the harmonic supremum of the maximal gap sequence of minimal strictly ergodic measures of Toeplitz flows form an explicit set of type {1/m : m | N} (the Toeplitz gap set).

**中文。** 猜想：Toeplitz 流的极小严格遍历测度的最大 gap 序列的调和上确界的可能值为 {1/m: m|N} 型显式(Toeplitz gap 集)。
