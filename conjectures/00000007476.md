**English.** Definition: The spectra of operator semigroups with generators. Conjecture: The spectral mapping of the semigroup holds, the image being the exponential image; and the kernel of the spectral mapping is exactly the resolvent residual set of the generator spectrum. (exponential-image semigroup spectral mapping)

**中文。** 定义：算子半群的谱：生成元。猜想：生成元的谱：半群的谱映射且射的像为指数像。(生成元谱映射半群指数像算子半群)；且谱映射的核恰为生成元谱的预解式残差集。
