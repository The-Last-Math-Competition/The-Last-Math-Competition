# 00000008103

**English.** Definition: The etale descent of local coefficient systems of fusion systems: the sheaf descent index des(F) of higher etale descent for coefficient systems of |L|. Conjecture: des(F) <= 2 (a second-order descent sufficiency law); the vanishing of the sheaf automorphisms in second-order descent is guaranteed by the centralizer transitivity of F (a transitivity-vanishing link); and the exceptions to descent (where third order is necessary) are exotic systems at p = 2 (a 2-exotic third-order exception), whose block Brauer correspondences split into three (a triple splitting rigidity). (etale descent second-order transitivity law)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的局部系数系统的平展下降：|L| 的系数系统的高阶平展下降的层下降指数 des(F)。猜想：des(F) ≤ 2（二阶下降充分律）；且二阶下降的层的自同构的消没由 F 的中心化传递性保证（传递-消没）；下降的例外（三阶必要）的系统为 exotic 且 p=2（2-exotic 三阶例外），该例外的块的 Brauer 对应的分裂为三次（三次分裂刚性）。
