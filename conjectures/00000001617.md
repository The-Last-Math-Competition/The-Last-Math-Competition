# 00000001617

**English.** Definition: The equilateral triangle torus is the flat torus with threefold symmetry. Conjecture: The off-diagonal terms of the gap statistics of eigenvalue pairs are always given explicitly by the norm distribution over the Eisenstein integers; and the distribution density is sixfold symmetric. (triangle gaps Eisenstein norms)

**中文。** 定义：等边三角形环面指具三重对称的平环面。猜想：特征值对的间隙统计的非对角项恒由 Eisenstein 整环的范数分布显式给出；且分布密度为六重对称。（三角形间隙 Eisenstein 范数）
