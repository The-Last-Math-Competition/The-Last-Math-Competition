# 00000002731

**English.** Definition: Combinatorial rigidity of simplicial maps: dimension-preserving maps between complexes. Conjecture: The arithmetic of existence of simplicial maps: a dimension-preserving surjection from a d-complex onto a simplex exists by the graded chromatic number criterion, the chromatic number being the maximal ascending segment of the h-vector. (dimension-preserving surjection chromatic criterion)

**中文。** 定义：单纯映射的组合刚体：两复形间保维映射。猜想：单纯映射的存在性数论：d 维复形到单纯形的保维满射的存在由分次染色数判据且染色数为 h-向量的最大上升段。（保维满射染色判据）
