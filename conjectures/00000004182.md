**GM3-02 ◆** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: A min-max minimal surface is a critical surface produced by a min-max construction, and the multiplicity is the multiplicity factor of the integral current. Conjecture: For bumpy metrics on closed three-manifolds the min-max minimal surfaces are always embedded with multiplicity one, and the smallest dimension where multiplicity one fails is 5. (bumpy embeddedness and the dimension of multiplicity one)

**中文。** 定义：极小极大极小曲面指由极小极大构造产生的临界曲面,重数指积分流量的重数因子。猜想：三维闭流形的 bumpy 度量上极小极大极小曲面恒嵌入且重数为一,重数一失效的最小维数为 5。（bumpy 嵌入与重数一维数）
