# 00000003882

**CA3-02 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: Strong F-regularity means the ring has no torsion-free free contraction for any Frobenius power. Conjecture: Strong F-regularity is preserved under purely transcendental base extension, its negation is preserved under completion, and neither can be strengthened to arbitrary faithfully flat extensions. (strong F-regular extension boundary)

**中文。** 定义：强 F-正则指对一切 Frobenius 幂无挠自由收缩的环。猜想：强 F-正则性在纯超越基扩张下保持,其否定在完备化下保持,且两者均不可加强为任意忠实平坦扩张。（强 F-正则扩张边界）
