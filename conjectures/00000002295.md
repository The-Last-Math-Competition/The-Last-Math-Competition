# 00000002295

**English.** Definition: An exotic fusion system is a saturated system not isomorphic to that of any finite group. Conjecture: The rank of the first exotic example for p ≥ 5 is 3 (constructed by the failure of p'-covers of exceptional Lie type); the minimal rank for p = 2 remains 2 (Solomon).

**中文。** 定义：exotic 融合系统指不同构于任何有限群融合系统的饱和系统。猜想：p≥5 的 exotic 首例的秩为 3(由 exceptional Lie 型的 p'-覆盖的失败构造);p=2 的最小秩仍为 2(Solomon)。
