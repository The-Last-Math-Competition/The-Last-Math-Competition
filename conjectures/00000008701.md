# 00000008701

**English.** Definition: Nonlinear transport (Young measures): transport for mean-field limits of microscopic oscillations. Conjecture: The relaxed transport of Young measures agrees with the original (a relaxation law); the limit of relaxation recovers single-point measures (a recovery law); the recovery rate (recovery of convexity) is exponential (an exponential law); and the critical recovery rate is 1/2 (a half-critical law). (Young measure relaxation recovery half-critical)

**中文。** 定义：非线性传输（Young 测度）：微观振荡的平均场极限的传输。猜想：Young 测度的松弛传输（松弛=原始）的（松弛律）；且松弛的极限（单点测度的回复）的（回复律）；回复的速率（凸性的回复）的指数的（指数律）；指数的临界（临界回复率）的 1/2 的（半临界律）。
