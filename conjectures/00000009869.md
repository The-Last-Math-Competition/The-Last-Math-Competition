# 00000009869

**English.** Definition: Currents of ASEP on rings: long-time fluctuations of accumulated currents under periodic boundary. Conjecture: The accumulated-current fluctuations of ring ASEP converge, for arbitrary initial profiles, to a universal limit law; the limit law is classified by the variation functional of the profile, the classification being a finite list with each class corresponding to an explicit Levy process. (ring current universal classification)

**中文。** 定义：环上 ASEP 的电流：周期边界下累积电流的长时间涨落。猜想：环 ASEP 的累积电流涨落对任意初始轮廓收敛到普适极限律；极限律由轮廓的变差泛函分类且分类为有限列表，每类与显式 Lévy 过程对应。（环电流普适分类）
