# 00000008171

**English.** Definition: The density of shortest-vector candidates in class groups of quadratic fields: the density rho_3(X) of elements without nontrivial solutions in the 3-part of class groups of discriminant |D| <= X. Conjecture: rho_3(X) tends to the Cohen-Lenstra type constant 1 minus the product over p of (1 - 1/p^2) (the CL limit for the odd part); the convergence error is O(X^{-1/2+epsilon}) (square-root convergence); the sign distribution of the second error term is uniform (a sign symmetry law); and the distribution of the 3-rank (the frequency of r_3 = k) is the explicit 2^{-k^2-k} times product constant (a 3-rank frequency formula), with the verification lower bound extendable to X >= 10^{20} (a computationally reachable threshold). (Cohen-Lenstra 3-rank square-root convergence)

**中文。** 定义：二次域类群的最短向量候选密度：判别式 |D|≤X 的类群的 3-部分的无平凡解的元素密度 ρ₃(X)。猜想：ρ₃(X) → 1 - ∏_{p}(1-1/p²) 型 Cohen–Lenstra 常数（奇部分的 CL 极限）；且收敛的误差为 O(X^{-1/2+ε})（平方根收敛）；误差的第二项的符号分布均匀（符号对称律）；3-秩的分布（r₃=k 的频率）为 2^{-k^{2}-k}·∏ 常数的显式（3-秩频率公式），公式的验证下界可推至 X ≥ 10^{20}（计算可达阈）。
