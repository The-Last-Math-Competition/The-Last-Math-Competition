# 00000000512

**English.** Definition: Multigraded regularity is the region concept for bigraded modules. Conjecture: The regularity region of a bigraded ideal is a convex polyhedron whose vertex count is bounded by a constant depending only on the type; and the bound is linear in the number of types. (bigraded regularity convex region)

**中文。** 定义：多分次正则性为双分次模的分次区域概念。猜想：bigraded 理想的正则性区域为凸多面体且顶点数对固定类型为有界常数；且界的阶为类型数的线性。（双分次正则性凸区域）
