# 00000001874

**English.** Definition: An NSOP₁ theory is a first-order theory without the tree-order property one; Kim independence is the independence relation on it. Conjecture: There exist strictly NSOP₁ non-simple new natural examples (beyond the Kim-decomposition class of random abelian groups); candidates are given by the quantifier-elimination theories of universal Frobenius groups.

**中文。** 定义：NSOP₁ 理论指不具树序性质一阶理论;Kim 独立性为其上的独立关系。猜想：存在严格 NSOP₁ 非简单的新自然例(超越随机可换群的 Kim-分解类);候选由泛 Frobenius 群的量词消去理论给出。
