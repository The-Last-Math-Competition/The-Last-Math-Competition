# 00000000446

**English.** Definition: Hivert's p-symmetric functions form the modified ring of permutation-symmetric functions. Conjecture: For p = 3, the dimension generating function of the ring is rational with explicit denominator; and the pole spectrum of rationality lies on an integer lattice in p. (p-symmetric rational generating function)

**中文。** 定义：Hivert 的 p-对称函数为置换对称的修正环。猜想：p 等于三时环的维数母函数为有理函数且分母显式；且有理性的极点谱为 p 的整格。（p-对称母函数有理）
