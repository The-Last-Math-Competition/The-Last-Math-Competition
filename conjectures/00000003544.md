**English.** Definition: The combinatorics of ordinal fixed points: enumeration functions. Conjecture: The normality of fixed-point enumeration: the enumeration of fixed points of α↦ω^α is a normal function, with the derivative of the function the transfinite limit. (fixed point enumeration normal transfinite limit derivative)

**中文。** 定义：序数不动点的组合：枚举函数。猜想：不动点枚举的正规性：α→ω^α 的不动点的枚举为正规函数且函数的导数为超极限。（不动点枚举正规超极限导数）
