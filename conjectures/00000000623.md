# 00000000623

**English.** Definition: vol(K) is the hyperbolic volume of a knot. Conjecture: The difference between vol(twist knot with n twists) and log²n converges to an explicit constant (the constant term after the leading vol ~ 2v₈·log²n asymptotics).

**中文。** 定义：vol(K) 为双曲纽结体积。猜想：vol(twist knot with n twists) 与 log²n 之差收敛于显式常数(渐进中 vol ~ 2v₈·log²n 之后的常数项)。
