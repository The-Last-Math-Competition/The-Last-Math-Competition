# 00000006019

**English.** Definition: The counterexample constant is the quantified bound of nonintegrability counterexamples, the splitting of heteroclinic orbits. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit sum of derivative variations, vanishing in the bounded-variation class; the vanishing threshold is the cumulative bound of the variation, of logarithmic type; and the exponentially small splitting angle is given by the Melnikov integral, with nonzero leading coefficient. (derivative-variation vanishing-criterion conversion)

**中文。** 定义：反例常数：不可积性反例（异宿轨道的分裂）的量化界。猜想：换算公式为导数变差的显式和且有界变差类中消没；消没的阈值为变差的累积界且界为对数型；分裂角的指数小量由 Melnikov 积分给出且积分的首项系数非零。（导数变差和消没判据换算）
