# 00000006886

**English.** Definition: Kronecker coefficients are the multiplicities of symmetric-group representation composition. Conjecture: The saturation of the positivity domain of Kronecker coefficients is asymptotically complete within semi-rectangular indices; and the saturation threshold is a linear function of the partition length. (Kronecker positivity saturation domain)

**中文。** 定义：Kronecker 系数为对称群表示复合的重数。猜想：Kronecker 系数的正性域的饱和在半矩形指标内渐近完全；且饱和阈值为分拆长度的线性函数。（Kronecker 正性饱和域）
