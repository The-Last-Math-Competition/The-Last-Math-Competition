# 00000008476

**English.** Definition: Thermodynamic formalism: nonanalytic points of the pressure function P(t) = sup(integral f d mu + t h(mu)), over ergodic invariant measures, are phase transitions. Conjecture: The existence of a phase transition is equivalent to nonuniqueness of equilibrium states (a nonuniqueness equivalence law); the critical temperature t_c is explicitly the intersection of the entropy-integral ratios of two candidate equilibrium measures (an entropy-ratio intersection law); phase transitions of nonhyperbolic systems are generically second order (a second-order universality law); and the entropy difference of coexisting measures at first-order transitions is an explicit constant. (phase transition entropy-ratio intersection)

**中文。** 定义：热力学形式：压力函数 P(t)=sup(∫f dμ + t·h(μ))（上确界遍历不变测度）的非解析点称为相变。猜想：相变的存在等价于平衡态不唯一（非唯一等价律）；且临界温度 t_c 的显式为两个候选平衡测度的熵-积分比的交点（熵比交点律）；非双曲系统的相变阶数普遍为二阶（二阶普适律）；一阶相变的共存测度的熵差为显式常数。
