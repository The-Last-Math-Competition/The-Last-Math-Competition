# 00000008368

**English.** Definition: The middle diffraction of tropical homology: the dimension trop_hoch of the symplectic cohomology of tropical spaces (corresponding to Berkovich deformations). Conjecture: trop_hoch (the dimension of tropical cohomology) is given by the Betti numbers of the intersection complex (an intersection Betti law); the vanishing of the torsion of homology (Z-coefficient torsion) is 2-torsion only (a two-torsion law); the free ranks (the free part) convert the spectrum of the intersection complex (a conversion law); and the obstruction to associativity of the multiplication (blow-up correction of intersection products) is homotopy associators (an A-infinity obstruction law), the minimal obstruction being the 3-cell (a three-cell obstruction example). (tropical homology intersection two-torsion associator)

**中文。** 定义：热带同调的中衍射：热带空间的辛上同调（对应于 Berkovich 的形变）的维度 trop_hoch。猜想：trop_hoch（热带上同调的维数）= 交复形的 Betti（交 Betti 律）；且同调的挠（Z-系数的挠部分）的消没为 2-挠（二挠律）；非挠的自由秩（自由部分的谱）为交复形的谱的换算（换算律）；同调的乘法（交积的爆破修正）的结合律的障碍为同伦结合子（A_∞ 障碍律），障碍的极小为 3-胞腔（三胞腔障碍例）。
