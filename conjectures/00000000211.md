# 00000000211

**SQ-11 ◆** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: The lucky numbers are obtained from the positive integers by the lucky sieve (at each round deleting the s_k-th position, where s_k is the k-th surviving number). Conjecture: The density of the intersection of lucky numbers and primes is asymptotically an explicit Euler-type constant, and the intersection contains infinitely many twin pairs; the constant is given by the first-order correction to independence of the two sieves. (lucky-prime intersection density)

**中文。** 定义：幸运数由自然数列经幸运筛（每轮删除第 s_k 位置，s_k 为幸存的第 k 个数）所得。猜想：幸运数与素数序列的交集密度渐近为显式 Euler 型常数，且该交集中的孪生对无穷；交集密度的常数由两筛的独立性偏差的一阶修正给出。（幸运-素数交集密度）
