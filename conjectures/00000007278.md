**English.** Definition: The Murnaghan-Nakayama rule with the combinatorial computation of characters. Conjecture: The ribbon counting of characters holds, and the sign of the count is the parity of the ribbon. (ribbon-parity MN boundary rule)

**中文。** 定义：Murnaghan–Nakayama 规则：特征标的组合计算。猜想：MN 的边界条：特征标的条带计数且数的符号为条带的公约。（Murnaghan-Nakayama 公约条带边界特征标计数符号）
