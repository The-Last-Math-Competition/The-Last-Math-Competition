**English.** Definition: Ritt–Kolch theorem of differential ideal denotes its characteristic sequence decomposition. Conjecture: characteristic sequence length ≤ d · (d+1)/2 (d of differential ideal decomposition is differential polynomial order number); and uniqueness module of decomposition becomes. (Ritt characteristic sequence length bound)
