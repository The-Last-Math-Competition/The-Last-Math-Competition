# 00000008506

**English.** Definition: The Ritt-Kolchin theorem for differential ideals is their characteristic set decomposition. Conjecture: The length of the characteristic set decomposition of the radical of a differential ideal is at most d(d+1)/2 (d the order of the differential polynomials); and the decomposition is unique up to reordering. (Ritt characteristic set length bound)

**中文。** 定义：微分理想的 Ritt–Kolch 定理指其特征列分解。猜想：微分理想根分解的特征列长度 ≤ d·(d+1)/2（d 为微分多项式阶数）；且分解的唯一性模重排成立。（Ritt 特征列长度界）
