# 00000008012

**English.** Definition: The Cordes-Kilpelainen type inequality for degenerate elliptic systems: the failure threshold p_sys(n) for interior estimates of vector-valued p-systems (nonlinear elliptic systems). Conjecture: C^{1,alpha} regularity of p-systems fails outside p in (2n/(n+2), 2 + 2/(n-2)) (the nonlinearity threshold for systems); within the threshold the optimal alpha is a system correction of the scalar alpha(p) (a half-order reduction alpha_sys = alpha(p)/2); and the explicit singularity of outer counterexamples (a power-law separation of components: the alternating law u_1 ~ r^gamma, u_2 ~ r^{gamma+1/2}) agrees with the resonance index of free integration (the resonance point gamma = 1/(p-1)). (p-system half-order Holder reduction)

**中文。** 定义：方程组的退化椭圆的 Cordes-Kilpeläinen 型不等式：向量值 p-系统（非线性椭圆系统）的内 estimate 的失效阈值 p_sys(n)。猜想：p-系统的 C^{1,α} 正则性在 p∉(2n/(n+2), 2+2/(n-2)) 外失效（系统的非线性阈值）；且阈值内最优 α 为标量的 α(p) 的系统修正（降半次的 α_sys = α(p)/2）；失效的外部反例的奇性显式（分量的幂律分离：u₁ ~ r^γ, u₂ ~ r^{γ+1/2} 的交错律）与自由积分的共振指数（γ = 1/(p-1) 的共振点）一致。
