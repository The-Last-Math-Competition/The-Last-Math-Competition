**English.** Definition: The algebraic structure of symmetric functions with the Lambda ring. Conjecture: The polynomiality of the symmetric-function ring holds, and the basis of the polynomiality is the complete homogeneous plus elementary functions. (homogeneous-elementary Lambda basis)

**中文。** 定义：对称函数的代数结构：Lambda 环。猜想：Lambda 的结构：对称函数环的多项式性且性的基为完全齐次加初等。（Lambda 完全齐次初等多项式基环结构）
