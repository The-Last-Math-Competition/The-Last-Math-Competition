**English.** Definition: Bangert's rigidity with minimal geodesics. Conjecture: The rigidity of minimal geodesics on the torus holds, and the completeness of the rigidity is the principal-block case. (principal-block Bangert rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Bangert 的刚性：极小测地。猜想：Bangert 刚性：环面的极小测地的刚性且性的完全为主基情形。（Bangert 主基情形刚性行为完全极小测地环面）
