# 00000005744

**English.** Definition: Stability is the fixed structure of the Frobenius action. Conjecture: The rank of the fixed subsheaf is given by the quotient of the group action of the double cover, and the combination of the quotient is the trace projection. (trace-projection rank quotient of fixed subsheaves)

**中文。** 定义：稳定性指 Frobenius 作用的不动结构。猜想：不动子层的秩由二层覆叠的群作用商给出；且商的组合为迹投影。（不动子层迹投影秩商显式）
