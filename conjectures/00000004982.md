# 00000004982

**English.** Definition: The curse of dimensionality is the exponential dependence of the error on dimension. Conjecture: Polynomial networks escape the curse when the smoothness exceeds half the dimension, the threshold is half the dimension, and it is supported by the matching lower bound of tensor product families. (half-dimension smoothness threshold of the curse)

**中文。** 定义：维数灾难指误差对维数的指数依赖。猜想：多项式网络在光滑度超过维数之半时可摆脱灾难,阈值为维数之半且由张量积族的匹配下界支撑。（维数灾难半维光滑阈值）
