**English.** Definition: Schrödinger semigroups: heat kernels with potential perturbation. Conjecture: The rigidity of the Feynman–Kac formula: the kernel of the potential semigroup is the explicit exponential of a path integral, with the convergence of the exponential the Kato class. (Feynman-Kac Kato class convergence)
