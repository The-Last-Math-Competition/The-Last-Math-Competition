**FM2-26 ★** — source: 153_有限模型论.md

**English.** Definition: Query enumeration delay is the time to output the first item. Conjecture: The delay constant table of constant-depth enumerable combinatorial classes is given by bounded-depth disjunctive normal forms, the delay is constant, and the table is closed under ternary join queries. (closed table of enumeration delay constant classes)

**中文。** 定义：查询枚举延迟指输出首项所需时间。猜想：常数深可枚举组合类的延迟常数表由有界深析取范式给出,延迟为常数且表在三元连接查询下封闭。（枚举延迟常数类封闭表）
