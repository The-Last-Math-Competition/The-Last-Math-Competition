**English.** Definition: The Frobenius character with the symmetric-function realization of symmetric-group characters. Conjecture: The map from characters to symmetric functions holds, and the equivalence of the map is the basis change of class functions. (class-function Frobenius basis change)

**中文。** 定义：Frobenius 特征标：对称群特征标的对称函数实现。猜想：Frobenius 对应：特征标的对称函数的映射且射的等价为类函数的基变换。（Frobenius 类函数基变换特征标对称函数映射等价）
