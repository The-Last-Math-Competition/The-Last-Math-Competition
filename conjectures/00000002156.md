**English.** Definition: hom(F,G) counts homomorphisms from F to G (Lovász: all F-counts determine G). Conjecture: Bounded-degree graphs are determined by #hom(F,G) with family size |F| ≤ C·n (C = 2^{d+1}); an explicit upper bound for the determining family size.
