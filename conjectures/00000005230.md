# 00000005230

**English.** Definition: The limiting distribution and the joint moment rate are two layers of statistics. Conjecture: There exist two matrix models with the same limiting distribution but different joint moment rates, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of independent and correlated randomness. (same limiting distribution-different joint moment rate separation)

**中文。** 定义：极限分布与联合矩速率指两层统计。猜想：存在两矩阵模型的极限分布相同而联合矩速率不同,分离由独立与相关随机性的显式对实现。（极限分布同-联合矩速率异分离）
