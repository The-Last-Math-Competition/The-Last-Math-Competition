**English.** Definition: The theta functions are the global section functions of the cluster structure, and the tropical limit is their degeneration limit. Conjecture: The base-change constant is an explicit ratio of breakpoint densities, and for two-variable clusters the constant is 2 and exact. (theta tropical base-change ratio 2)

**中文。** 定义：theta 函数指 cluster 结构的整体截断函数,tropical 极限指其退化极限。猜想：基变换常数为折点密度的显式比,二变量簇时常数为 2 且精确。（theta tropical 基变换比 2）
