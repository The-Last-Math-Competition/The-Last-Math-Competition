**BF2-26 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: A Turing bifurcation is a diffusion-induced instability in a reaction-diffusion system, and the preferred wavelength is the spatial period of the pattern. Conjecture: The preferred wavelength is given by an explicit closed form in the two diffusivities and the entries of the reaction Jacobian, is monotone in the ratio of diffusivities, and has a derivative that diverges at the critical ratio. (Turing wavelength closed form and monotonicity)

**中文。** 定义：Turing 分叉指反应扩散系统中扩散诱导失稳,首选波长指模式空间周期。猜想：首选波长由两扩散率与反应雅可比元素的显式闭式给出,关于扩散率之比单调且导数在临界比处发散。（Turing 波长闭式与单调性）
