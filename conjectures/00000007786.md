# 00000007786

**English.** Definition: The constant of the Bourgain-Milman inequality: the reciprocal c_BM of the optimal constant C in vol(K)vol(K^circ) <= C^n vol(B)^2. Conjecture: The optimal c_BM = 1/e, attained asymptotically by the simplex and consistent with the Mahler conjecture ratio; any improvement of c_BM beyond 1/e would imply the Mahler conjecture itself; and any weak limit of extremal sequences for c_BM is either a simplex sequence or a box sequence. (Bourgain-Milman optimal constant 1/e)

**中文。** 定义：Bourgain–Milman 不等式的常数：体积乘不等式 vol(K)vol(K°)≤C^n vol(B)^2 的最优常数 C 的倒数 c_BM。猜想：最优 c_BM = 1/e（由单纯形渐近达到，与 Mahler 猜想的比值一致），且 c_BM>1/e 的任何改进将蕴含 Mahler 猜想本身；进一步 c_BM 的极值序列的任何弱极限要么是单纯形列要么是长方体列。
