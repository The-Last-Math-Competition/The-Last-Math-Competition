**English.** Definition: Bochner technique: the geometry of integration by parts. Conjecture: Vanishing theorems: the vanishing of harmonic forms, with the content the positivity of curvature. (Bochner vanishing theorem)

**中文。** 定义：Bochner 技术：分部积分的几何。猜想：消没定理：调和形式的消没且没的组合为曲率正性。（Bochner 消没定理调和形式曲率正性组合技术分部积分几何）
