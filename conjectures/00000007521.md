**ET3-11 ◆** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: Lisse sheaves as locally constant sheaves of the etale topology. Conjecture: The lisse criterion is the finiteness of the geometric monodromy representation; and the verification of the criterion is the finite generation of the quotient of the group ring. (finite-monodromy lisse criterion)

**中文。** 定义：lisse 层为平展拓扑的局部常值层。猜想：lisse 的判据为几何单值表示的有限性；且判据的验证为群环的商的有限生成。（lisse 有限单值判据）
