# 00000009803

**English.** Definition: Discrete Schwarz-Christoffel maps: combinatorial conformal maps from polygons to disks. Conjecture: The boundary error of discrete Schwarz-Christoffel maps is O(h^{1/2}); the exponent 1/2 cannot be improved at corner points, and along smooth boundary segments the error improves to O(h). (discrete SC boundary error)

**中文。** 定义：离散 Schwarz–Christoffel 映射：多边形到圆盘的组合共形映射。猜想：离散 Schwarz–Christoffel 映射的边界误差为 O(h^{1/2})；指数 1/2 在角点处不可改进且光滑边界段处误差升为 O(h)。（离散 SC 边界误差）
