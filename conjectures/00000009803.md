# 00000009803

**DDG-13 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: combinatorial map of discrete Schwarz–Christoffel map: many edge. Conjecture: boundary error of discrete Schwarz–Christoffel map is O(h^{1/2}); exponent 1/2 in angle point at cannot be improved and smooth boundary error is O(h). (discrete SC boundary error)

**中文。** 定义：离散 Schwarz–Christoffel 映射：多边形到圆盘的组合共形映射。猜想：离散 Schwarz–Christoffel 映射的边界误差为 O(h^{1/2})；指数 1/2 在角点处不可改进且光滑边界段处误差升为 O(h)。（离散 SC 边界误差）
