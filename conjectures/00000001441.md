# 00000001441

**English.** Definition: The linearly edge-reinforced walk increases the weight of an edge by δ at each crossing (initial weight 1). Conjecture: For δ < 1 the walk is recurrent on Z; for δ ≥ 1 it localizes with probability 1 on a finite edge set; the phase transition occurs exactly at δ = 1, degenerating at δ = 0 to the recurrent simple walk.

**中文。** 定义：线性边增强游走指每次通过使边权增加 δ 的过程(初始权 1)。猜想：δ<1 时游走在 ℤ 上常返,δ≥1 时以概率 1 局部化于有限边集;相变严格发生在 δ=1,δ=0 时退化为常返简单游走。
