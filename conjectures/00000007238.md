# 00000007238

**English.** Definition: Multiple L-means with the convexity of mean values. Conjecture: The convexity bound of multiple sums holds, and the saturation of the bound is the spectral maximum point. (spectral-maximum multiple-mean convexity)

**中文。** 定义：多重 L-均值：均值子的凸性。猜想：均值子的凸性：多重和的凸性界且界的饱和为谱的极大点。（均值子谱极大点饱和多重凸性界）
