# 00000005309

**RS2-19 ◆** — source: 162_组合的代数_表示稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: The numerical boundary is the scale limit of computer verification. Conjecture: Verification extends to the explicit lattice of order eighteen, given by orbit computation of symmetric reduction, and the smallest counterexample beyond the bound exceeds eighteen, with the longest stabilizer chain of the orbit computation having length exactly eighteen. (order-eighteen orbit verification bound of Saxl)

**中文。** 定义：数值边界指计算机验证的规模上限。猜想：验证可推至十八阶的显式格点,由对称约化的轨道计算给出且界外最小反例规模超过十八。(Saxl 十八阶轨道验证界)；且轨道计算的最长稳定子链长度恰为十八。
