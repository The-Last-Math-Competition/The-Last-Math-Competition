# 00000008273

**SFL-13 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The transfer of regularity of kernels in rough Volterra equations: the regularity vol_reg of solutions of Volterra rough paths with kernel K. Conjecture: vol_reg (solution regularity) follows the minimum of the kernel regularity and the path roughness (a minimum law); the lower bound of the effective interval of the Hurst exponent H for singular kernels (fractional kernels (t-s)^{H-1/2}) is 1/4 (an H quarter lower bound); the order of the expansion needed when the lower bound breaks (H < 1/4, renormalization) is twice H (a double-H renormalization law); and the weight correction of the signature of the renormalized solution is the trigonometric explicit function gamma(H) (a gamma(H) formula). (rough Volterra minimum quarter renormalization)

**中文。** 定义：粗糙 Volterra 方程的核的正则性的传递：Volterra 粗糙路径（核 K）的解的正则性 vol_reg。猜想：vol_reg（解的正则性 = min(核的正则性, 路径的粗糙度) 的最小律）；且核的奇异（fractional 核 (t-s)^{H-1/2}）的 Hurst 指数 H 的有效区间的下界为 1/4（H-四分之一下界）；下界的破缺（H<1/4 的重整化）的展开的阶为 H 的 2 倍（双 H 重整律）；重整后的解的签名的权重修正为 γ(H) 的三角函数显式（γ(H) 公式）。
