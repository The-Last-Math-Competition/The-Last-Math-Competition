# 00000001510

**HY-30 ★** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The wind-tree flow is the straight-flow model on the plane with Z²-periodic obstacles. Conjecture: The rescaled limit spectrum of the wind-tree model is pure point; and the eigenvalues are lattice values of a quadratic form in the obstacle density and the speed ratio. (wind-tree pure point spectrum lattice quadratic form)

**中文。** 定义：风滚动流指 Z² 对称障碍平面上的直线流模型。猜想：风滚动模型的重标度极限谱为纯点；且特征值为障碍密度与速度比的二次型格点值。（风滚动纯点谱格点二次型）
