**English.** Definition: Language equivalence of algebras: elementary equivalence and PI. Conjecture: Model completeness of PI classes: elementary equivalence classes of matrix algebras are separated by transfinite rank invariants, and the minimal family of invariants is rank plus characteristic. (PI model completeness separation)
