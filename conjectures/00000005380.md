**HG4-30 △** — source: 164_几何群论IV_双曲几何III.md

**English.** Definition: The spectrum and virtual fibering are two three-dimensional layers. Conjecture: There exist two hyperbolic three-manifolds with the same Laplace spectrum but different virtual fibering properties, and the separation is realized by an explicit isometry-construction pair, with fibering decided by invariants. (same spectrum-different virtual fibering separation)

**中文。** 定义：谱与虚拟纤维化指两层三维性质。猜想：存在两双曲三维流形的拉普拉斯谱相同而虚拟纤维化性质不同,分离由等距构造的显式对实现且纤维性由不变量判定。（谱同-虚拟纤维化异分离）
