**English.** Conjecture: The profile of the concentrating solution in the soliton resolution of 5D energy-supercritical waves is of explicit self-similar type: profile = trivial solution plus a weighted sum of one bubble; the weight coefficients come from the resonance structure of angular momentum, with no asymptotically free term.
