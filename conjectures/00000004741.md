# 00000004741

**English.** Definition: The higher Brauer-Manin obstruction is the extended obstruction of the placement of zero-dimensional cycles. Conjecture: The obstruction computation of degree-eight classes can be reduced by an explicit Skolem reduction to a finite point check, with polynomial complexity and lossless reduction. (polynomial Skolem reduction of degree-eight classes)

**中文。** 定义：高次 Brauer–Manin 障碍指零维循环选址的扩展障碍。猜想：八次类的障碍计算可由显式 Skolem 归约为有限点检查,复杂度为多项式且归约无损。（八次类 Skolem 多项式归约）
