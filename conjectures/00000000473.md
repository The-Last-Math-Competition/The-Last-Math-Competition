**English.** Definition: The weak order lattice is the lattice of permutations ordered by inversion sets. Conjecture: The Sperner number of the weak order lattice equals the size of the middle layer; and the proof of the strong Sperner property is a symmetric chain decomposition. (weak order strong Sperner)

**中文。** 定义：弱序格为置换按逆序数的格。猜想：弱序格的 Sperner 数等于中位层大小；且强 Sperner 性的证明为对称链分解。（弱序强 Sperner）
