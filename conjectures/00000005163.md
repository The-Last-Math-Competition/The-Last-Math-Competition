# 00000005163

**English.** Definition: The weight-filtration grading is the associated grading of a mixed structure with respect to the weight filtration, with the graded dimensions of open varieties. Conjecture: The weight-graded dimensions of an open variety are an explicit alternating sum of combinatorial numbers and intersection cohomology; in the affine case the alternating sum simplifies to a pointwise expression; and the sign pattern of the alternating sum is plus-minus alternating with a finite summation domain. (alternating-sum formula of open-variety weight gradings)
