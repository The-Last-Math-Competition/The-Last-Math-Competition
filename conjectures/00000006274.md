# 00000006274

**English.** Definition: The weakening of name counting is the concentration structure of barrier name counting, the weak Bernoulli criterion. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit concentration of name counts and the proportion of typical names is uniform; the deviation is exponential, the exponent given by the mixing rate; and the length of the concentration window is the reciprocal of the entropy rate, the window end being the barrier. (uniform exponential deviation conversion of name counting)

**中文。** 定义：名字计数弱化：barrier 名字计数的集中结构（弱 Bernoulli 判据）。猜想：换算常数为名字计数的显式集中且典型名字比例一致；偏差为指数式且指数由混合率给出；集中窗的长度为熵率的倒数且窗端为 barrier。（名字计数一致偏差指数换算）
