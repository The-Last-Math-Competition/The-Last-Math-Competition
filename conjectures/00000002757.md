# 00000002757

**PI-17 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: The PI version of the finite-dimensionality conjecture: global dimension of PI algebras. Conjecture: The finiteness equivalence of global dimension: a PI algebra of finite global dimension is an affine quotient of Azumaya type, with the sum of squared ranks of the quotient identically equal to the generation rank. (PI global dimension equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：有限维数猜想 PI 版：PI 代数的整体维数。猜想：PI 代数的整体维数的有限性等价：有限整体维数的 PI 代数为 Azumaya 型的仿射商且商的秩平方和恒等于生成秩。（PI 整体维数等价）
