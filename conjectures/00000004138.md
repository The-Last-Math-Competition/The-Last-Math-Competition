# 00000004138

**FA2-18 ★** — source: 123_高等数理逻辑_强制法与公理.md

**English.** Definition: The supercompact version of the HOD hypothesis is the principle that supercompactness is inherited within HOD. Conjecture: The smallest large cardinal for this hypothesis is an extendible cardinal, and the hypothesis is equivalent to HOD being a fragment of a supercompact inner model. (HOD supercompact minimal cardinal equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：HOD 假设的超紧版本指超紧性在 HOD 中的可继承原理。猜想：该假设的最小大基数为可扩基数,且等价于 HOD 为超紧内模型的片段。（HOD 超紧最小基数等价）
