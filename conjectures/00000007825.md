# 00000007825

**MSE-15 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The Weil-Petersson volume vol WP(Mbar_{g,n}) is the symplectic volume of the hyperbolic moduli space, with generating function given by the Mirzakhani formula. Conjecture: The lattice-counting error of WP volumes (Mirzakhani-type counting of polygons by simple closed geodesic lattices) has the exact order O(T^{2+2g+n-delta}), where delta is a Diophantine correction from prime geodesics; and the main error term is a period polynomial whose coefficients are non-homogeneous linear combinations of WP volumes with uniformly nonnegative sign. (Mirzakhani lattice counting error)

**中文。** 定义：Weil–Petersson 体积 vol WP(Mbar_{g,n}) 为双曲模空间的辛体积。Mirzakhani 公式给出其生成函数。猜想：WP 体积格点计数误差（Mirzakhani 型计数：简单闭测地线格的多边形计数）为 O(T^{2+2g+n-δ}) 型精确指数（δ 为素测地线的 Diophantine 修正）；且误差主项为周期多项式，其系数为 WP 体积的非齐次线性组合，符号恒非负。
