# 00000003896

**English.** Definition: The tilt of a perfectoid space is the equivalence (X, O) ↦ (X, O^♭) with characteristic-p spaces. Conjecture: Different untilts of the same tilt space differ by at most a perfectoid unit-group automorphism, and this rigidity fails with a 3-point space as the minimal counterexample once the perfectoid hypothesis is dropped. (untilt rigidity and counterexample)

**中文。** 定义：perfectoid 空间的倾斜指其与特征 p 空间的等价 (X, O) ↦ (X, O^♭)。猜想：同一倾斜空间的不同 untilt 至多相差一个 perfectoid 单位群的自同构,且该刚性在去掉 perfectoid 假设后以 3 点空间为最小反例。（untilt 刚性与反例）
