# 00000009024

**English.** Definition: The limit law of coefficient distributions. Conjecture: The semicircular-type distribution is given by Weyl group symmetry; and the constant of the distribution is the weight function. (coefficient semicircle Weyl weight function)

**中文。** 定义：系数分布的极限律。猜想：半圆型分布由 Weyl 群对称性给出；且分布的常数为权函数。（系数半圆 Weyl 权函数）
