**English.** Definition: Leavitt path algebras: algebraizations of graph C*-correspondences. Conjecture: The isomorphism classification of Leavitt path algebras is completely determined by the symbolized K_0 group plus the unit, and every realizable symbol group is uniquely realized; the realization algorithm is an elementary sequence of graph transformations, with sequence length a linear function of edges. (Leavitt algebra K-group classification)

**中文。** 定义：Leavitt 路代数：图的代数化 C*-对应。猜想：Leavitt 路代数的同构分类由符号化 K_0 组加单位元完全决定且每个可实现的符号组唯一实现；实现算法为图变换的初等序列且序列长度为边的线性函数。（Leavitt 代数 K 群分类）
