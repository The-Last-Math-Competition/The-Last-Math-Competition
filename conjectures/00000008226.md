# 00000008226

**English.** Definition: The failure of spectral synthesis for unitary representations: the incomplete subspace synth_fail(G) of the spectral synthesis (direct integral decomposition into irreducibles) of L^2(G). Conjecture: The minimal group realizing nonzero synth_fail (a dimension index of closed invariant subspaces where spectral synthesis fails) is the product of the Heisenberg group with itself (a double Heisenberg example); the construction of failing subspaces (nonsynthesizable sets) has spectral set of minimal measure equal to the infimum of atomic masses of the spectral measure (an atomic infimum law); and the complete classification of group classes where spectral synthesis succeeds (synthesis groups) is: commutator subgroup compact or abelian (a synthesis classification law). (spectral synthesis double Heisenberg classification)

**中文。** 定义：幺正表示的谱综合失败：L²(G) 的谱综合（不可约的直积分分解）的不完备子空间 synth_fail(G)。猜想：synth_fail（谱综合失败的闭不变子空间的维数指标）的非零实现的最小群为 Heisenberg 群 × Heisenberg 群（双 Heisenberg 例）；且失败子空间的构造（非综合集）的谱集的最小测度为谱测度的原子质量的下确界（原子下确界律）；谱综合成功的群类（综合群）的完全分类为 [G,G] 紧或交换（综合分类律）。
