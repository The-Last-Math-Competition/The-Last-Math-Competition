**English.** Definition: Ordinal arithmetic: addition, multiplication, and exponentiation of transfinite ordinals. Conjecture: The valuation ring of ordinal values: the factorization domain of ordinal polynomials is the lattice-ization of Hessenberg natural sums, with the operation of lattices the transfinite polynomial ring. (Hessenberg natural sum lattice ring)

**中文。** 定义：序数算术：超穷序数的加乘幂运算。猜想：序数赋值的赋值环：序数多项式的分解域为 Hessenberg 自然和的格点化且格点的运算为超穷多项式环。（Hessenberg 自然和格点环）
