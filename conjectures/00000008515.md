# 00000008515

**SDG-15 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: Hyperasymptotics is the high-precision truncation of divergent series. Conjecture: The optimal truncation term count of the hyperasymptotic expansion of a divergent series is N* equal to the negative first power of |A| epsilon; and N* is the nearest integer minimizing the remainder. (hyperasymptotic optimal truncation)

**中文。** 定义：超渐近指发散级数的高精度截断。猜想：发散级数的超渐近展开的最优截断项数 N* = |A|·ε 的负一次幂；且 N* 为残差最小的整数最近整数。（超渐近最优截断）
