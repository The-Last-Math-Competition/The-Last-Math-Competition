**English.** Definition: The Gromov renormalized distance is a box-distance-type invariant of metric measure spaces. Conjecture: Compactness of graphon classes under this distance is controlled by an explicit upper bound on the entropy, and the bound is optimal, attained by subsequences of step tower graphs. (entropy-controlled compactness bound of the Gromov distance)

**中文。** 定义：Gromov 重整化距离指度量测度空间的盒距离型不变量。猜想：graphon 类在该距离下的紧性由熵的显式上界控制,界由阶跃塔图子列达到最优。（Gromov 距离熵控紧界）
