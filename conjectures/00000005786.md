# 00000005786

**English.** Definition: The parametric divergence family is the divergence sequence with parameters. Conjecture: The leading term of the classical divergence at zero parameter is half the squared information distance, and the combination of the half is the Fisher metric. (information-distance half leading term of the parametric family)

**中文。** 定义：参数散度族指带参数的散度列。猜想：零参数的经典散度首项为信息距离平方之半；且半的组合为 Fisher 度量。（参数族 Fisher 度量信息距离半首项）
