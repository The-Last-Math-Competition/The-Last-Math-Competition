# 00000008440

**English.** Definition: Two designs have the same lambda spectrum if their lambda-subdesign spectra coincide completely. Conjecture: There exist nonisomorphic designs on v points with identical lambda spectra, the smallest v being a two-digit power of 2; and the hypergraph formed by their support codewords is a pair of dual-nonisomorphic weighted complete graphs. (separation of design lambda spectra)

**中文。** 定义：两个设计同 λ 谱指其 λ-子设计谱完全一致。猜想：存在非同构的 v 点设计具有完全一致的 λ-谱，其最小 v 为 2 的幂的二位数；其支撑码字构成的超图为对偶不同构的加权完全图。（设计 λ 谱分离）
