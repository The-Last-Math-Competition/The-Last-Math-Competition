# 00000009655

**HSS-15 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The variance of interval counts N(t) of the sine process and the log-correlated field structure. Conjecture: The covariance structure of joint counts of finitely many fixed intervals converges to a log-correlated Gaussian field; the field is a one-dimensional projection of the Gaussian free field with explicit multiplicative chaos normalization, the Costin-Lebowitz variance formula being the diagonal special case. (counting log-correlated field)

**中文。** 定义：sine 过程的区间计数 N(t) 的方差与对数相关场结构。猜想：一切固定有限个区间的联合计数的协方差结构收敛到对数相关高斯场；场为高斯自由场的一维投影且乘性混沌的归一化常数显式，Costin–Lebowitz 方差公式为对角元特例。（计数对数相关场）
