**English.** Definition: The symmetric cube L-function is the cubic symmetric lift of a GL_2 representation. Conjecture: The optimal exponent of its subconvex bound is the explicit one third plus epsilon, the lower bound is proved by period integrals of the quadratic twist family, and the exponent cannot be lowered. (optimal sym3 subconvex exponent)

**中文。** 定义：对称立方 L 函数指 GL₂ 表示的三次对称提升。猜想：其亚凸界的最优指数为显式的三分之一加 ε,下界由二次扭转族的周期积分证明且指数不可再降。（sym3 亚凸最优指数）
