# 00000002245

**English.** Conjecture: The converse of Malmquist's theorem (a rational-coefficient criterion for differential equations): exceptional solutions of quadratic equations outside the admissible-value table of rational coefficients are explicit of Painlevé type (Malmquist converse).

**中文。** 猜想：Malmquist 定理(微分方程的有理系数判据)的逆:有理系数的许可值表外的二次方程的例外解为 Painlevé 型显式(Malmquist 逆)。
