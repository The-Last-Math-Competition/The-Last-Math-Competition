# 00000005278

**English.** Definition: The scalar third-order law is the turbulence law for advected scalars. Conjecture: Its constant is a universal scale-invariant value, given by the hierarchical conversion of flux conservation, and valid for arbitrary source scales. (universal constant of the scalar third-order law)

**中文。** 定义：标量三阶律指导标量的湍流律。猜想：其常数为标度不变的普适值,由通量守恒的层次换算给出且对任意源尺度成立。（标量三阶律普适常数）
