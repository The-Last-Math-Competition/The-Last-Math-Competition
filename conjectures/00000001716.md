# 00000001716

**EG-26 ★** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: The extendible map of an analytic flow is the transfer operator of its phase-space map. Conjecture: The constant of its spectral gap is of Fefferman–Graham type: gap ≥ (2π/θ_max)², where θ_max is the maximal angular deviation of the map; the gap constant is explicit in the compression coefficient of the map.

**中文。** 定义：解析流的可扩展映射指其相空间映射的传递算子。猜想：其谱隙的常数为 Fefferman–Graham 型:gap ≥ (2π/θ_max)^2,θ_max 为映射的最大角偏差;间隙常数由映射的压缩系数显式。
