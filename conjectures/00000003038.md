# 00000003038

**English.** Definition: Entanglement measurement protocols: experimental reconstruction of many-body entanglement. Conjecture: Minimal measurements for reconstruction: pure-state reconstruction of an n-body density matrix requires 4^n measurement settings, minimized to 3^n plus corrections in diagonal bases. (pure state reconstruction minimal settings)

**中文。** 定义：纠缠测量协议：多体纠缠的实验重构。猜想：重构的最小测量：n 体密度矩阵的纯态重构需要 4^n 个测量设置且设置的极小化在对角基下为 3^n 加修正。（纯态重构最小设置）
