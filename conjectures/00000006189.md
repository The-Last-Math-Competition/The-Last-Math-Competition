**RG4-29 ◆** — source: 191_随机图计数II_渐近方法.md

**English.** Definition: The Sidorenko conjecture asserts that the density of a bipartite graph H in every graph is at least the edge density to the edge-count power. Conjecture: The boundary between known and open classes is always updatable, and subdivisions of trees and unions of even cycles are closed in the known class. (updatable boundary of Sidorenko known classes)

**中文。** 定义：Sidorenko 猜想指二部图 H 的密度在一切图中不小于边密度边数幂的下界族。猜想：已知类与开放类的边界恒可更新；树的细分与偶圈并在已知类中封闭。（Sidorenko 已知开放边界更新）
