**English.** Definition: Ldistribution of ipschitz constant in ball on of metric projection P_K: R^n -> K of concentration: K of high dimension metric projection. Conjecture: variance ≤ c · (1-|P_K(x)|²/n), of log of Jacobi of pair all ball point x, P_K and the inequality is gradient concentration expression; K of strictly is half-space, this when metric projection degenerate is linear projection, concentration bound and Brown leading out distribution uniform of ball plane on.

**中文。** 定义：高维度量投影的集中：K 的度量投影 P_K: R^n→K 的 Lipschitz 常数在球上的分布。猜想：对均匀球点 x，P_K 的雅可比的 log 的方差 ≤ c·(1-|P_K(x)|²/n)，且该不等式为严格改进的梯度集中形式；等号情形 K 为半空间，此时度量投影退化为线性投影，集中界与半球面上的 Brown 首出分布一致。
