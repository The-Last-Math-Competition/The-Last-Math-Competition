# 00000007810

**ACG-30 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: Concentration of high-dimensional metric projection: the distribution on the sphere of the Jacobian of the metric projection P_K from R^n to K. Conjecture: For uniform sphere points x, the variance of the log-Jacobian of P_K is at most c(1 - |P_K(x)|^2/n), a strictly improved gradient-concentration form; and equality holds for the halfspace, where the metric projection degenerates to a linear projection and the concentration bound matches the Brownian exit distribution on the hemisphere. (metric projection Jacobian concentration)

**中文。** 定义：高维度量投影的集中：K 的度量投影 P_K: R^n→K 的 Lipschitz 常数在球上的分布。猜想：对均匀球点 x，P_K 的雅可比的 log 的方差 ≤ c·(1-|P_K(x)|²/n)，且该不等式为严格改进的梯度集中形式；等号情形 K 为半空间，此时度量投影退化为线性投影，集中界与半球面上的 Brown 首出分布一致。
