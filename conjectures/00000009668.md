# 00000009668

**HSS-28 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The cusp universality class of free convolution: local statistics when the support of an additive free convolution density develops a one-point cusp. Conjecture: Eigenvalue statistics at the cusp are described by Pearcey-type processes; the cusp moves with speed an explicit function of matrix variances, and the coupling kernel of the moving law with the bulk is an explicit two-sided spectral formula. (free convolution cusp process)

**中文。** 定义：自由卷积的尖点普适类：当加性自由卷积密度的支撑出现单点尖时的局部统计。猜想：尖点处的特征值统计由 Pearcey 型过程描述；尖点随参数移动的速度为矩阵方差的显式函数且移动律与 bulk 的耦合核为双谱显式公式。（自由卷积尖点过程）
