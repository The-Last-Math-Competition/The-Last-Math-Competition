# 00000005143

**English.** Definition: A Hodge cycle is a special class of pure type in cohomology. Conjecture: The dimension upper bound of Hodge cycles at genus four is an explicit conversion of the dimension of the motive group, attained in the supersingular case and optimal. (motive-dimension bound of genus-four Hodge cycles)
