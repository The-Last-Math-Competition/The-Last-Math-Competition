**TL2-30 △** — source: 135_范畴逻辑_拓扑斯.md

**English.** Definition: Constructive invariants are all the point-free definable invariants of locales. Conjecture: There exist two locales whose constructive invariants all coincide while their classical topologies are not isomorphic, the separation is given by the difference in the existence of ultrafilters, and the smallest example is two constructivizations of the interval. (constructive invariant-ultrafilter separation example)

**中文。** 定义：构造性不变量指 Locale 的无点可定义不变量全体。猜想：存在两个 Locale 的一切构造性不变量相同而经典拓扑不同构,分离由超滤子的存在性差异给出,最小例为区间的两个构造化。（构造不变量-超滤分离例）
