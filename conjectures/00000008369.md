# 00000008369

**English.** Definition: The tangent flow of tropical dynamical systems: the limit tang_lim of the tangential evolution (the entropy of the heat flow) of zero sets of tropical polynomials. Conjecture: The count of limit cycles of tang_lim is at most 2^d - 1 (a two-power upper bound law); the full-measure stability of cycles (basin measure) is universal (an attraction universality law); the flow entropy (topological entropy) is zero for polynomial flows (a polynomial zero-entropy law); and the minimal positive-entropy correction (piecewise-linear positive entropy) is piecewise linear (a piecewise positive entropy example). (tropical tangent two-power piecewise entropy)

**中文。** 定义：热带动力系统的切向流：热带多项式的零点集的切向演化（热流的熵）的极限 tang_lim。猜想：tang_lim（切向流的极限环）的计数 ≤ 2^{d}-1 的上界（二幂上界律）；且环的稳定性（吸弓域的测度）的满测（满测吸引）的普遍性（吸引普遍律）；流的熵（拓扑熵）的零（零熵）在多项式流（多项式零熵律）；熵的正（非多项式的正熵）的最小修正为分片线性（分片正熵例）。
