**English.** Definition: The electromagnetic eigenproblem is the discrete eigenvalue problem. Conjecture: The gradient subspace is exact with no spurious modes; the realization of exactness is the discrete orthogonal decomposition; and the kernel of the discrete orthogonal decomposition is the gradient-potential subspace. (exact gradient kernel without spurious modes)
