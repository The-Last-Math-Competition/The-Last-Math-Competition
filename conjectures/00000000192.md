# 00000000192

**CS-26 ★** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: S = Σ_{n≥1} 1/(n!+1). Conjecture: The runs of zeros in the binary expansion of S are unbounded, with longest run Θ(log n); the growth is explicitly controlled by the binary digit structure of n!+1, giving a constructive proof of irrationality. (n!+1 series run law)

**中文。** 定义：S=Σ_{n≥1} 1/(n!+1)。猜想：S 的二进制展开的零游程无界且最长游程为 Θ(log n)；增长由 n!+1 的二进位结构显式控制并给出无理性的构造性证明。（n!+1 级数游程律）
