# 00000007053

**English.** Definition: Continuous sum-product with the metric side. Conjecture: The measure version of sum-product holds, the combination of the product being Brunn-Minkowski. (Brunn-Minkowski measure-version sum-product)

**中文。** 定义：连续和积：度量侧。猜想：连续和积：测度版的和积且积的组合为 Brunn–Minkowski。（连续和积 Brunn-Minkowski 测度版连续和积组合和积测度版）
