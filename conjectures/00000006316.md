# 00000006316

**English.** Definition: The lower bound is that of the permanent of doubly stochastic matrices, van der Waerden. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the uniqueness of equality, attained by the all-one matrix; the stability constant is one half; and the deficit of the minimal neighborhood of the equality matrix is a positive-definite quadratic form. (half-stability equality conversion constant)

**中文。** 定义：下界：双随机矩阵积和式的下界（van der Waerden）。猜想：换算常数为等号唯一性的显式且全一矩阵达到等号；稳定常数为半；等号矩阵的极小邻域的亏格为二次型且型正定。（等号唯一稳定半换算常数）
