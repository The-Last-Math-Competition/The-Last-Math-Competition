# 00000000748

**English.** Definition: The Teichmüller lift w: F_p → Z_p. Conjecture: The only polynomials P of minimal degree satisfying P∘w = w∘P̄ (P̄ = P mod p) are the identity and Frobenius (classification of lift-commuting polynomials).

**中文。** 定义：Techmüller 代表提升 w: F_p → ℤ_p。猜想：任一多项式 P 满足 P∘w = w∘P̄ 的最小次数为 p(P̄ 为 P mod p)只有恒等与 Frobenius(提升交换的多项式分类)。
