**AE4-10 ★** — source: 226_算术遍历II.md

**English.** Definition: Marklof with the statistics of values of definite quadratic forms. Conjecture: The universal limit of value statistics holds, and the kernel of the limit is the Haar measure of the homogeneous space. (Haar-universal Marklof limit)

**中文。** 定义：Marklof：定二次型的统计。猜想：Marklof 的普适：值的统计的普适极限且限的核为齐性空间的 Haar。（Marklof Haar 齐性普适极限值统计）
