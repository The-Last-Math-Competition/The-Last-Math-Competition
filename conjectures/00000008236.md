**English.** Definition: The smoothness analysis of the price of anarchy: the tight parameters smooth_par of (lambda,mu)-smoothness. Conjecture: The complete classification of smooth_par (the tight smooth class) is a class of linear-quadratic forms (a linear-quadratic class law); the supremum of the tight POA (worst equilibria over all mechanisms) is the explicit 1/(1-mu-lambda) (a smoothness ratio formula); and the value spectrum of the tight POA of atomic congestion games (tight values over all cost functions) is the reciprocal lattice of the Hurwicz zeta function (a zeta lattice law), whose first value is 4/3 (a 4/3 first law, consistent with Braess in traffic). (smooth POA linear-quadratic zeta lattice)
