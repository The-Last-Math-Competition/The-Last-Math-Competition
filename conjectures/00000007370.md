# 00000007370

**OD4-10 ★** — source: 231_数值积分_常微II.md

**English.** Definition: Numerics for delay equations with functional differential equations. Conjecture: The sufficiency of convergence is delay tracking, and the resolution of the tracking is the step-size ratio. (step-ratio delay-tracking resolution)

**中文。** 定义：延迟方程的数值：泛函微分。猜想：延迟的步进：方法的收敛的充分为延迟的跟踪且踪的分辨率为步长比。（延迟方程步长比跟踪分辨率收敛充分数值步进）
