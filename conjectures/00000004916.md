**English.** Definition: Thompson sampling is the Bayesian policy of posterior randomization. Conjecture: The Bayesian regret bound constant of beta priors is an explicit rational function of the prior parameters, degenerating for the uniform prior, and the bound is tight. (rational constant of Thompson beta priors)

**中文。** 定义：Thompson 采样指后验随机化的贝叶斯策略。猜想：贝塔先验的贝叶斯悔值界常数为先验参数的显式有理函数,均匀先验时退化且界紧。（Thompson 贝塔先验有理常数）
