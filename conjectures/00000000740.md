**PA-40 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: Vandiver's conjecture asserts p ∤ h^+(Q(ζ_p)). Conjecture: In the natural probabilistic model treating Bernoulli numbers B_{2k} mod p as independent random variables, the probability of p² | h^+(Q(ζ_p)) is p^{−(p−3)/2}; hence the first Vandiver counterexample (if any) lies beyond 10^{18}.

**中文。** 定义：Vandiver 猜想断言 p∤h^+(Q(ζ_p))。猜想：在把 Bernoulli 数 B_{2k} mod p 视为独立随机变量的自然概率模型下,p²|h^+(Q(ζ_p)) 的概率为 p^{-(p−3)/2};由此首个 Vandiver 反例(若存在)超出 10^{18}。
