# 00000004723

**English.** Definition: An Azumaya algebra is a sheaf of local central simple algebras of square rank. Conjecture: Its rank realizable domain on a surface is the set of squares divisible by the index, and for a fixed Brauer class it is an explicit interval with attainable endpoints. (interval realization of Azumaya ranks)

**中文。** 定义：Azumaya 代数指秩为平方数的局部中心单代数层。猜想：其在曲面上的秩可实现域为指标整除的平方数集,固定 Brauer 类时为显式区间且端点可达。（Azumaya 秩区间实现）
