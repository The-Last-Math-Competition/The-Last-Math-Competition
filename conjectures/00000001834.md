# 00000001834

**English.** Definition: The Mumford–Tate exception dimension of a Jacobian is the amount by which the general MT group drops dimension. Conjecture: The exception dimension is g minus one; and the configurations are completely indexed by the order types of endomorphism rings, without redundancy. (MT exception dimension g-1 order-type index)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobian 的 Mumford–Tate 群例外维数指一般 MT 群的降维量。猜想：例外维数为 g 减一；且构型由自同态环的序型完全索引而无冗余。（MT 例外维 g-1 序型索引）
