# 00000002533

**GZ-03 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Marstrand's projection theorem: projections of a 1-dimensional set of R² onto almost every line have positive length. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of exceptional projection directions is completely determined: the exceptional dimension for an s-dimensional set is of the 2s−1 type; the correction has a logarithmic anomaly at s = 1, and the exceptional set has Assouad dimension 1 but strictly smaller Hausdorff dimension. (projection exceptional dimension spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Marstrand 投影定理：R² 的 1 维集到几乎一切直线的投影有正长度。猜想：投影例外方向的集合的维数谱完全确定：s 维集的投影例外维为 2s−1 型修正；修正项在 s=1 有对数异常且例外集的 Assouad 维数为 1 而 Hausdorff 维数严格小。（投影例外维数谱）
