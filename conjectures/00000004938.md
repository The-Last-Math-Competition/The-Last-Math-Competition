**HT4-08 ◆** — source: 150_代数拓扑IV_同伦论深化.md

**English.** Definition: The Picard group of the category of spectra is the group of homotopy classes of invertible objects. Conjecture: The local Picard group of height one and prime p is isomorphic to the direct sum of Z with Z/(2p-2), with the torsion part generated by the image of sphere automorphisms; the pi_0 of the hyper-Picard group is an explicit extension of this group; the extension splits at p = 2 but not at odd p. (generation structure of the K_1-local Picard group)

**中文。** 定义：谱范畴的 Picard 群：可逆对象的同伦类群。猜想：高度一素 p 的局部 Picard 群同构于 Z 与 Z/(2p-2) 的直和且挠部分由球面自同构像生成；超 Picard 群的 π₀ 为该群的显式扩张；p=2 时扩张分裂而 p 奇时不分裂。（K1 局部 Picard 生成结构）
