# 00000004551

**TR3-11 ◆** — source: 137_数论V_超越方法.md

**English.** Definition: A linear form in logarithms is an integer-coefficient combination of logarithms of algebraic numbers. Conjecture: The Baker-type lower bound can be improved to an explicit linear form in the coefficient heights, in the two-logarithm case it matches the spectral bound of Gaussian sums and cannot be improved further. (linear-form improvement of Baker lower bounds)

**中文。** 定义：对数线性型指代数数对数的整系数组合。猜想：Baker 型下界可改进为系数高度的显式线性型,两组对数情形与高斯和的谱界匹配且不可再进。（Baker 下界线性型改进）
