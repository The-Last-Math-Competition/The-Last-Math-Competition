**English.** Definition: Submodularity of entropy: decreasing conditional entropy. Conjecture: Submodular entropy: the monotonicity of conditional entropy, with the equivalence of monotonicity and submodularity; and the equivalence is verified by termwise vanishing in the chain rule of conditional mutual information. (entropy submodularity conditional monotonicity chain verification)

**中文。** 定义：熵的次模性：条件熵递减。猜想：次模的熵：条件熵的单调且调的等价为次模。(熵次模条件熵单调次模等价性次模熵)；且等价性由条件互信息的链式法则的逐项消没验证。
