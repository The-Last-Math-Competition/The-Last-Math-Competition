# 00000006595

**English.** Definition: Semiorthogonal decomposition is the direct-sum stratification structure. Conjecture: The length for rational surfaces is given by the genus and the lower bound is attained by line bundles; and the combination of the attainment is Beilinson type. (Beilinson line-bundle lower bound of rational surfaces)

**中文。** 定义：半正交分解为直和分层结构。猜想：有理曲面的长度由亏格给出且下界达于直线簇；且达界的组合为 Beilinson。（有理面 Beilinson 直线簇下界）
