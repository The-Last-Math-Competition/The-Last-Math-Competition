# 00000008726

**English.** Definition: Floquet engineering: the effective Hamiltonian of periodically driven systems. Conjecture: The high-frequency expansion converges exponentially in the reciprocal frequency (an exponential convergence law); small-denominator resonances are eliminated by MBL (an elimination law); Floquet topological phases support anomalous edge states (an anomalous edge law); and the resonance density is a power law in the driving amplitude (a resonance density law). (Floquet high-frequency exponential anomalous edge)

**中文。** 定义：Floquet 工程：周期驱动系统的有效哈密顿量。猜想：高频展开以频率倒数指数收敛（指数收敛律）；且小分母共振由 MBL 消除（消除律）；Floquet 拓扑相支持异常边缘态（异常边律）；共振的密度为驱动幅度的幂律（共振密度律）。
