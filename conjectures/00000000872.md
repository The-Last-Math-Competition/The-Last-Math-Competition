**English.** Definition: The spectral gap α(Ω) = inf_{k≥2} √λ_k − √λ_{k−1}. Conjecture: General domains have zero gap, but arithmetic domains (rational polygons) have an explicit gap lower bound of exponent −1/2 (a spectral gap dichotomy).
