# 00000006014

**HS2-04 ◆** — source: 186_哈密顿系统.md

**English.** Definition: A twist map is the classical area-preserving map type. Conjecture: The existence of invariant curves is controlled by the ratio of maximal to minimal twist, the kernel of the control being the variation functional; the critical value of the ratio is 1, above which invariant curves exist; and blow-up of the variation breaks the curves, the breakage rate of the same order as the variation. (variation-ratio control of twist invariant curves)

**中文。** 定义：扭转映射：保面积的经典映射型。猜想：不变曲线存在性由最大最小扭转差之比控制且控制的核为变差泛函；比的临界值为 1 且临界上不变曲线存在；变差的爆破导致曲线断裂且断裂的速率与变差同阶。（扭转不变曲线变差比控制）
