# 00000004690

**SG3-30 △** — source: 141_交换代数IV_奇点理论.md

**English.** Definition: The local zeta function and the topology are two singularity invariants. Conjecture: There exist two singularities whose local zeta functions agree for all primes while their embedded topologies differ, and the minimal dimension of the separation is two. (two-dimensional local zeta-topology separation)

**中文。** 定义：局部 zeta 函数与拓扑指两个奇点不变量。猜想：存在两奇点的一切素数的局部 zeta 函数相同而嵌入拓扑不同,分离的最小维数为二。（局部 zeta-拓扑二维分离）
