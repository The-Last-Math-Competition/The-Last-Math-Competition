**English.** Definition: The anticanonical cubic type is the system of cubic section equations. Conjecture: In dimension three its solvability criterion is a joint explicit condition of index and degree, complete for the quadratic family and effectively verifiable. (solvability criterion of the anticanonical cubic)

**中文。** 定义：反典范立方型指三次截面方程组。猜想：三维情形的可解性判据为指数与度的联合显式条件,判据在二次型族完全且有效可验。（反典范立方可解判据）
