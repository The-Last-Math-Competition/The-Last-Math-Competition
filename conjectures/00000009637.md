**English.** Definition: The natural symbolic coding of interval exchange maps produces low-complexity words; the linear recurrence (LR) property bridges measurable and topological structure. Conjecture: Symbolic codings of typical IETs are linearly recurrent words of complexity n + O(1); linear recurrence is closed under Rauzy-Veech induction, and LR words have full measure among IET codings. (IET linear recurrence full measure)
