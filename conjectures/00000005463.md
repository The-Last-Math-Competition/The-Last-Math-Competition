**English.** Definition: The Chinese remainder theorem is the reconstruction of congruence systems, with the optimal constant of the three-modulus case. Conjecture: The optimal computation constant of three-modulus reconstruction is the explicit doubled mixed-radix conversion, attained at small-modulus towers; with pairwise coprime moduli the reconstruction is unique, the uniqueness given by the bijection of the mixed radix; and the factor-of-two gap comes from the table compression of precomputation, with compression ratio 2. (mixed-radix doubled constant of three moduli)

**中文。** 定义：中国剩余定理：同余系的重建；三模数情形的最优常数。猜想：三模数重建的最优计算常数为混合基转换的显式二倍且小模数塔处达到；模数两两互素时重建为唯一且唯一性由混合基的双射给出；二倍的间隙来自预计算的表压缩且压缩比为 2。（三模数混合基二倍常数）
