# 00000000873

**English.** Definition: The period spectrum of periodic solutions of the wave equation on the equilateral triangle is the set of periods for which solutions are periodic. Conjecture: The completely integrable structure gives an explicit characterization: the periods are exactly the lattice of integer multiples of twice the triangle height divided by √3; the gaps (aperiodic resonances) have density zero in the spectrum and count O(T^{1/2}) by wave number.

**中文。** 定义：等边三角形上波动方程的周期解的周期谱指使解为周期的周期集合。猜想：完全可积结构给出周期谱的显式刻画:周期恰为三角形高的二倍除以 sqrt(3) 的整数倍的格点集,间隙(非周期共振)在谱中密度为零且按波数计数为 O(T^{1/2})。
