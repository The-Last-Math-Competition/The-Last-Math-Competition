# 00000004078

**English.** Definition: Bifurcation hysteresis is the non-coincidence of bifurcation curves as the parameter travels back and forth, and the hysteresis area is the enclosed area. Conjecture: Under small-amplitude periodic parameter paths the hysteresis area is asymptotically equal to the cube of the amplitude, and the exponent three is universal and irreducible. (cubic law of bifurcation hysteresis areas)

**中文。** 定义：分叉滞后指参数往返时分叉曲线不重合的现象，滞后面积指所围面积。猜想：小振幅周期参数路径下滞后面积渐近等于振幅的三次幂；幂次三普适且不可降。（分叉滞后面积三次律）
