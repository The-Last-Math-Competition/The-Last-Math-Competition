# 00000004648

**English.** Definition: Arakawa categorification is the equivalence between the adapted representation category of a W algebra and the geometric category of Whittaker D modules. Conjecture: The equivalence preserves fusion rules under minimal nilpotent orbit reduction with identical fusion coefficients; the weight shift converts as a half-shift with the shift invariant under the equivalence; and the kernel of the equivalence is a finite vanishing chain of irreducible objects with chain length at most the rank of the group. (half-weight conversion of Arakawa categorification)

**中文。** 定义：Arakawa 范畴化：W 代数的适配表示范畴与 Whittaker D 模几何范畴的等价。猜想：等价在极小幂零轨道归约下保持融合规则且融合系数相同；权的移位换算为半移位且移位量在范畴等价下不变；等价的核为有限条不可约对象的消失链且链长不超过群的秩。（Arakawa 范畴化半权换算）
