**English.** Definition: Algorithms for Zariski closures: polynomial computation of orbit closures. Conjecture: The minimal degree of the generating ideal of an orbit Zariski closure is polynomial in the orbit length, with computational complexity cubic in the dimension; the minimal polynomials are unique up to isomorphism. (orbit closure algorithm bound)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 闭包的算法：轨道闭包的多项式计算。猜想：轨道 Zariski 闭包的生成理想的最小次数为轨道长度的多项式且计算复杂度为维数的三次幂；极小多项式在同构意义下唯一。（轨道闭包算法界）
