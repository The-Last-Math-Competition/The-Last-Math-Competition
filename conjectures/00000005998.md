# 00000005998

**English.** Definition: Connectedness measures that any two points lie on a low-degree curve, related to the minimal degree of very free curves. Conjecture: The conversion formula is explicit in the minimal degree of very free curves, given by the chain covering of the discriminant complement; connectedness is at most the minimal degree plus one, the plus one attained by degree-six del Pezzo fibrations; and quotient singularities increase connectedness by at most 1. (discriminant-complement conversion of connectedness)

**中文。** 定义：连通度：任意两点落在低次曲线内的度量；与非常自由曲线最小度的关系。猜想：换算公式为非常自由曲线最小度的显式且由判别式补集的链覆盖给出；连通度不超过最小度加一且加一由六次 del Pezzo 纤维化达到；商奇点的存在使连通度增值至多 1。（连通度最小度判别补集换算）
