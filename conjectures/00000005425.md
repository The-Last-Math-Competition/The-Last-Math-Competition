# 00000005425

**HM2-15 ★** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: The boundary constant is the coefficient of metric comparison. Conjecture: The constant table of convex domains is always given by the sine of the interior angle, normalized at the half-plane, and the function is monotone and closed. (interior-angle sine constant table of convex domains)

**中文。** 定义：边界常数指度量比较的系数。猜想：凸域的常数表恒由内角的正弦给出；且半平面处归一而函数单调封闭。（凸域内角正弦常数表）
