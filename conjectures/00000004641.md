# 00000004641

**English.** Definition: A logarithmic VOA is a VOA whose modules contain Jordan blocks, and the R matrix is the braiding-action matrix. Conjecture: The first explicit R matrix is given on the genus-one modules of a member of the triplet family, the matrix is a constant times a Jordan block, and the braid image is finite. (first example of a triplet R matrix)

**中文。** 定义：对数 VOA 指模含 Jordan 块的 VOA,R 矩阵指辫作用矩阵。猜想：首个显式 R 矩阵在 triplet 家族成员的亏格一模上给出,矩阵为常数乘 Jordan 块且辫像有限。（triplet R 矩阵首例）
