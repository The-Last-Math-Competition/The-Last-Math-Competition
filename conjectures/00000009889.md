# 00000009889

**English.** Definition: Tilting equivalence: the complete invariant problem for tilting equivalences of derived categories. Conjecture: For derived-equivalent finite-dimensional algebras, the sum of Hochschild cohomology dimensions is bounded below by the number of quiver vertices; the bound is attained by the minimal complex support length of derived equivalences, with equality algebras being derived-tilted uniserial. (derived equivalence lower bound invariant)
