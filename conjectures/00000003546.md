# 00000003546

**OC3-26 ★** — source: 103_序数与组合数论III.md

**English.** Definition: Ordinals and automata: ordinal recognizability. Conjecture: The rigidity of ordinal automata: the ordinal sets recognized by ω-automata are countable, with the completeness of recognition the Büchi condition. (ordinal automata Buchi countable recognition)

**中文。** 定义：序数与自动机：序数可识别性。猜想：序数自动机的刚性：ω-自动机识别的序数集合为可数且识别的完全性为 Büchi 条件。（序数自动机 Buchi 可数识别）
