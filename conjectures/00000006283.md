# 00000006283

**English.** Definition: Stability is the stable bound of noncommutative K-groups under matrix extension. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit increment of the ring dimension and the stable bound of coefficient rings is dimension-plus-one type; the tight example of the bound is polynomial rings, with increment 1; and any improvement of the stable range needs commutativity, with the commutative bound halved. (dimension-increment conversion of stable bounds)

**中文。** 定义：稳定性：非交换 K 群在矩阵扩张下的稳定界。猜想：换算常数为环维数的显式增值且系数环的稳定界为维数加一型；界的紧例为多项式环且增值为 1；稳定范围的任何改进需交换性且交换情形的界减半。（环维数稳定界增值换算）
