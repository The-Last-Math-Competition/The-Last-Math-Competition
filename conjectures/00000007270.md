# 00000007270

**VR4-30 △** — source: 227_变分法II.md

**English.** Definition: Hyperbolic variation with the variational structure of wave equations. Conjecture: The stationary points of the hyperbolic functional are wave solutions, and the conservation of the solutions is the zero divergence of the energy flux. (zero-divergence wave-action stationarity)

**中文。** 定义：双曲变分：波方程的变分结构。猜想：波的作用泛函：双曲泛函的驻点为波解且解的守恒为能量流的散度为零。（波作用泛函能量流散度零双曲驻点守恒波解）
