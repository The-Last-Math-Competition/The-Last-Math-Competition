# 00000008102

**English.** Definition: The Picard group of the self-equivalence group of a fusion system: the group Pic(F) of stable homotopy classes of self-equivalences of |L|_p-hat. Conjecture: Pic(F) is finitely generated with rank equal to the count of Morita equivalence classes of blocks of F minus 1 (a rank-Morita count); the prime factors of the torsion subgroup of Pic are contained in {p} union block-invariant primes (a torsion-prime rigidity); and the complete list of minimal systems for computing Pic is the seven systems of rank at most 2 (a seven-system Picard completion), the product of the orders of their Pic groups being 2^{12} 3^5 (an explicit product constant). (fusion Picard rank-Morita seven-system completion)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的自等价群的 Picard 群：|L|_p^ 的自等价的稳定同伦类群 Pic(F)。猜想：Pic(F) 为有限生成且其秩 = F 的块的 Morita 等价类的计数 - 1（秩-Morita 计数）；且 Pic 的扭子群的素因子 ⊆ {p} ∪ (块不变量素)（扭素刚性）；Pic 的计算的最小系统的完整列表为秩 ≤2 的 7 系（七系 Pic 完备化），七系的 Pic 群的阶的乘积为 2^{12}·3^5（显式积常数）。
