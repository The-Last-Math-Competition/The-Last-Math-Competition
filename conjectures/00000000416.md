# 00000000416

**SA-16 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The evacuation operator e acts on standard Young tableaux of two-row shape (n,n); its orbits are the cycles of the iterates of e. Conjecture: The number of orbits is 2^{n-1}, every orbit length divides 2n, and the number of orbits of length exactly 2 is the Fibonacci number F_{n-1}.

**中文。** 定义：evacuation 算子 e 作用于两行形状 (n,n) 的标准 Young 表,其轨道指 e 的迭代循环。猜想：轨道数为 2^{n-1},且每条轨道长度整除 2n;长度恰为 2 的轨道条数为 Fibonacci 数 F_{n-1}。
