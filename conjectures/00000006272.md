# 00000006272

**English.** Definition: Isomorphism with the finite criterion of measure conjugacy of Ornstein type. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit convergence of coupling distances and the distance converges exponentially; the convergence rate is given by the spectral gap, finite; and the optimal coupling distance is d-bar, with lower bound zero if and only if isomorphic. (exponential convergence conversion of coupling distance)

**中文。** 定义：同构：测度共轭的有限判据（Ornstein 型）。猜想：换算常数为耦合距离的显式收敛且距离指数收敛；收敛的速率由谱隙给出且谱隙有限；耦合的最优距离为 d̄ 且 d̄ 的下界为零当且仅当同构。（耦合距离指数收敛速率换算）
