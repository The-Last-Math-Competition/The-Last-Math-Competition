**GZ-06 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Furstenberg sets E_α: planar sets carried at each point by an α-dimensional family of direction lines. Conjecture: The dimension lower bound of Furstenberg sets is (3α+1)/2, tight simultaneously at α = 1 and α = 0; for intermediate α the true bound is 2α + c with c an explicit gain from Orponen-type projection mechanisms. (Furstenberg dimension lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Furstenberg 集 E_α：每点有 α 维方向线族承载的平面集。猜想：Furstenberg 集的维数下界为 (3α+1)/2 且该界在 α=1 与 α=0 两端同时紧；中间 α 的真下界为 2α+c 修正且修正常数为 Orponen 型投影机制的显式增益。（Furstenberg 维数下界）
