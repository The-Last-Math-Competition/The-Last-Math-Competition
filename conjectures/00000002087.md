# 00000002087

**English.** Definition: The weak Arnold number N(H) is the lower bound for the number of periodic points of a Hamiltonian diffeomorphism. Conjecture: On rational symplectic manifolds, the characteristic-type lower bound for N(H) is χ(M) + Σ(−1)^{c_i} (a sum of periodic-orbit correction terms); the bound comes from surjective counting in local Floer homology.

**中文。** 定义：弱 Arnold 数 N(H) 指 Hamilton 微分同胚的周期点数下界。猜想：有理辛流形上 N(H) 的示性数型下界为 χ(M)+Σ(-1)^{c_i}(周期轨道修正项的和);下界由局部 Floer 同调的满射计数。
