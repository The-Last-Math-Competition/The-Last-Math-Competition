# 00000009601

**English.** Definition: Minimal Heegaard surfaces: the area-minimizing surfaces in Heegaard decompositions of 3-manifolds. Conjecture: Closed 3-manifolds admit minimal Heegaard surfaces, unique for general metrics; manifolds whose minimal Heegaard genus equals the Heegaard genus form a dense open set of hyperbolic type, with exceptions a complete list of Seifert type. (minimal Heegaard dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：极小 Heegaard 曲面：3-流形的 Heegaard 分解中面积最小的曲面。猜想：闭 3-流形的极小 Heegaard 曲面存在且一般度量下唯一；极小 Heegaard 亏格与 Heegaard 亏格相等的流形为双曲的稠密开集，例外为 Seifert 型完全列表。（极小 Heegaard 二分）
