# 00000001507

**English.** Definition: The directional distribution of the translation flow is the statistical structure of the flow along unit directions. Conjecture: The limit of the directional distribution is always a Markov-type process; and the transition kernel is completely given by the count of stratum cycle classes. (directional flow Markov kernel formula)
