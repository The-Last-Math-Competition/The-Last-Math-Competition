# 00000008170

**RNG-30 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The intersection structure of multicritical points under renormalization: the intersection angle het_angle of the stable manifolds of adjacent fixed points (heteroclinic orbits). Conjecture: het_angle (the intersection angle of heteroclinic connections between fixed points) is the universal constant theta_het = 2 pi/7 (a sevenths-angle law); the robustness constant of the stability of the angle (preservation under perturbation) is the minimum of the gap spectrum (a gap robustness law); the minimal dimension of the transverse intersection of heteroclinic orbits (non-collinear) is d = 2 (a 2-dimensional transverse minimum law); and the arithmetic type of the collinearity condition of high-dimensional degeneracy (heteroclinic collinearization) is the quasiperiodicity of solutions of a difference equation (a quasiperiodic collinearity law). (heteroclinic sevenths-angle quasiperiodic collinearity)

**中文。** 定义：多临界点的重整化的交点结构：相邻不动点的稳定流形的交（异宿轨道）的交角 het_angle。猜想：het_angle（不动点间的异宿连接的交角）为普适常数 θ_het = 2π/7（7 分角律）；且交角的稳定性（微扰下的保持）的鲁棒常数为间隙谱的最小值（间隙鲁棒律）；异宿轨道的横截交（非共线）的维数谱的最小维数为 d=2（2 维横截最小律）；高维的简并（异宿共线化）的共线化条件的算术型为差分方程的解的准周期（准周期共线律）。
