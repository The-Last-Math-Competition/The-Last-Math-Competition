**GL2-19 ★** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: Step approximation is the approximation of a graphon by step functions. Conjecture: The optimal upper bound of the number of steps achieving L^1 error epsilon is a constant multiple of 1/epsilon^2, with optimal constant 1, attained by random-type graphons. (optimal reciprocal-epsilon-squared bound on step counts)

**中文。** 定义：步逼近指以阶跃函数逼近 graphon。猜想：达到 L¹ 误差 ε 的最优步数上界为 1/ε² 的常数倍,常数 1 最优且由随机型 graphon 达到。（最优步数 ε 平方倒数界）
