# 00000003818

**English.** Definition: Γ-convergence: the topology of variational convergence. Conjecture: The stability of Γ-limits: convergence of minima, with the error of convergence the mean difference of functionals. (Gamma minimum convergence functional mean difference error stability)

**中文。** 定义：Γ-收敛：变分收敛的拓扑。猜想：Γ-极限的稳定性：极小值的收敛且收敛的误差为泛函的均差。（Gamma 极小收敛泛函均差误差稳定性）
