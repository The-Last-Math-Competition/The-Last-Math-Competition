# 00000007964

**CD3-04 ★** — source: 251_编码理论III_列表解码与局部译码.md

**English.** Definition: The classification extension of perfect codes: nontrivial q-ary perfect codes (covering radius 1, balls fill the space) have known types the Hamming codes and the binary/ternary Golay codes; define relaxed quasi-perfect codes (Q-uniform ball measures) with tight uniform shell distribution at covering radius r+1. Conjecture: The gap constant of the radius spectrum of quasi-perfect codes is at most 2 (covering efficiency at least 1/2); and binary linear quasi-perfect codes of length n exist if and only if n is of type 2^k - 1 or in a finite exceptional list (the Levenshtein set), completely enumerating the spectrum. (quasi-perfect Levenshtein spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：完美码的分类延拓：非平凡的 q 元完美码（覆盖半径 1，球填满空间）已知类型为 Hamming 与二进制/三进制 Golay。定义广义完美（Q-均匀球测度）的松弛完美码（quasi-perfect：覆盖半径 r+1 的紧壳均匀分布）。猜想：quasi-perfect 码的半径谱的间隙常数 ≤ 2（即覆盖效率 ≥ 1/2）；且二元线性 quasi-perfect 码在长 n 的存在性当且仅当 n 为 2^k-1 型或例外集合（Levenshtein 集合）中的有限列表（谱的完全列表化）。
