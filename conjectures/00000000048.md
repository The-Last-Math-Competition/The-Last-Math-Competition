# 00000000048

**GR-04 ★** — source: 03_图论与离散结构.md

**English.** Conjecture: (Prime graceful tree) Every tree with m edges admits an injective labeling f: V → [0, C·m log m] such that the values |f(u)−f(v)| over the tree edges are distinct primes.

**中文。** 猜想：(素优雅树)每棵 m 条边的树都有单射标号 f: V → [0, C·m log m],使树边上的 |f(u)−f(v)| 为互异素数。
