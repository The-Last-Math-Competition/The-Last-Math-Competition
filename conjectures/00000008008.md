# 00000008008

**English.** Definition: The modulus of quadratic convexity of the energy of solutions of the obstacle problem: the strong convexity modulus delta_Obst of the energy functional E(v) = integral |grad v|^2 under the obstacle constraint. Conjecture: delta_Obst = 1/(1+L^2) (L the Lipschitz constant of the obstacle); the rate of optimal convergence (gradient methods) is an explicit function of the spectral gap of the delta ratio; and the relaxation time distribution of the motion of the free boundary (quasi-static relaxation) is heavy-tailed with exponent 3/2 (a Levy-type law for obstacle relaxation), the tail constant being an explicit integral of the capacity of the contact set. (obstacle energy convexity Levy relaxation)

**中文。** 定义：障碍问题的解的能量的二次型凸性的模：能量泛函 E(v)=∫|∇v|² 的障碍约束下的强凸模 δ_Obst。猜想：δ_Obst = 1/(1+L²)（L 为障碍的 Lipschitz 常数）；且最优收敛（梯度法）的速率为 δ 比率的谱 Gap 的显式函数；自由边界的运动（拟静态松弛）的松弛时间的分布为重尾指数 3/2（障碍松弛的 Levy 型律），尾常数为接触集的容量的显式积分。
