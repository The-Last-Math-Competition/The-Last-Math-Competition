# 00000005595

**EL2-05 ★** — source: 172_精确线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: A linear Diophantine equation requires the computation of integer solutions. Conjecture: The minimal number of basis elements of the solutions equals the explicit value of the dimension minus the rank, with basis bit lengths given by LLL-type reduction of the kernel lattice; the bit-length bound of the reduction is a polynomial of the input bit length of degree 4; and the choice of the minimal basis is unique up to unimodular transformation. (minimal-basis formula of Diophantine kernel lattices)

**中文。** 定义：线性丢番图方程：整数解的计算。猜想：解基的极小个数等于维数减秩的显式值且基的位长由核格的 LLL 型规约给出；规约的位长界为输入位长的多项式且次数为 4；极小基的选取在幺模变换下唯一。（丢番图核格极小基公式）
