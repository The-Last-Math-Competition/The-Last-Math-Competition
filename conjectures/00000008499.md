# 00000008499

**English.** Definition: Optimization of isoperimetric profiles of spaces of ergodic measures: the minimization refactoring of the Cheeger constant at fixed measure. Conjecture: The minimization by monotone rearrangement of the isoperimetric profile is attained by spherically symmetric measures (a sphere law); the existence domain of nonspherical extremal measures is multimodal measures (a multimodal exception domain law); the explicit configuration of multimodal extrema is the double ball (a double-ball configuration law); and the critical mass ratio for the transition from double ball to single ball is 1/2 (a half-critical law). (Cheeger rearrangement double-ball half-critical)

**中文。** 定义：遍历测度空间的等周剖面的优化：固定测度的 Cheeger 常数的极小化重构。猜想：等周剖面的单调重排的极小化由球对称测度取得（球律）；非球极值测度的存在域为多峰测度（多峰例外域律）；多峰极值的显式构型为双球（双球构型律）；双球到单球的转变的临界质量比为 1/2（半临界律）。
