**English.** Definition: Cotangent complexes: derived invariants of deformation. Conjecture: The composition law of cotangent complexes: the tensor product of cotangent complexes of categories is the graded tensor sum, and the translation of the sum is the object-level symplectic structure. (cotangent complex composition symplectic translation)

**中文。** 定义：余切复形（cotangent complex）：形变的导出不变量。猜想：余切复合成律：范畴的余切复形的张量积为分次张量和且和的平移为对象级的辛结构。（余切复合成辛平移）
