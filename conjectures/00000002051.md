# 00000002051

**English.** Definition: The fine spectrum f_V(n) of a variety V is the cardinality of its n-generated free algebra. Conjecture: The fine spectrum of the semigroup variety ⟨x² = x⟩ is the explicit closed form f(n) = 2^{n}·C_n (C_n the Catalan numbers); the spectrum is controlled by the connected-component count of band semigroups.

**中文。** 定义：簇 V 的细谱 f_V(n) 指 n 元自由代数的基数。猜想：半群簇 ⟨x²=x⟩ 的细谱为 f(n) = 2^{n}· C_n(C_n 为 Catalan 数)的显式闭式;谱由带状半群的连通分量计数控制。
