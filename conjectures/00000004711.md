# 00000004711

**English.** Definition: An odometer is a minimal equicontinuous system of a countable group. Conjecture: Its sampling entropy conversion formula is the logarithm of the sampling density, the sampling entropy is zero if and only if the density is square summable, and the formula is exact. (density formula of odometer sampling entropy)

**中文。** 定义：里程计指可数群的极小等度连续系统。猜想：其采样的熵换算公式为采样密度的对数,采样熵为零当且仅当密度平方可和且公式精确。（里程计采样熵密度公式）
