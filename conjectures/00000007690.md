# 00000007690

**QDE-30 ◆** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: Hecke type decomposition coefficient of mock theta function f(q)=Σ (-1)^n q^{n(3n-1)/2}/(q;q)_n is Fourier coefficient a(n) of its real analytic positive term. Conjecture: a(n) in n is prime and n≡1 (mod 4) when always positive, in n≡3 (mod 4) when sign is exactly negative; and mean value of |a(p)| with respect to prime p is quantity level, its fluctuation of p^{1/2+o(1)} by Frobenius angle distribution controls of fixed CM elliptic curve.

**中文。** 定义：mock theta 函数 f(q)=Σ (-1)^n q^{n(3n-1)/2}/(q;q)_n 的 Hecke 型分解系数为其实解析修正项的 Fourier 系数 a(n)。猜想：a(n) 在 n 为素数且 n≡1 (mod 4) 时恒正，在 n≡3 (mod 4) 时符号恰为负；且 |a(p)| 关于素数 p 的均值为 p^{1/2+o(1)} 的量级，其波动由固定 CM 椭圆曲线的 Frobenius 角分布控制。
