# 00000007690

**QDE-30 ◆** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: For the mock theta function f(q)=sum (-1)^n q^{n(3n-1)/2}/(q;q)_n, the Hecke-type decomposition coefficients a(n) are the Fourier coefficients of its real-analytic correction. Conjecture: a(n) is always positive when n is a prime congruent to 1 mod 4 and has sign exactly negative when n is 3 mod 4; and |a(p)| has mean order p^{1/2+o(1)} over primes p, with fluctuations controlled by the Frobenius angle distribution of a fixed CM elliptic curve. (sign pattern of mock theta Hecke coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：mock theta 函数 f(q)=Σ (-1)^n q^{n(3n-1)/2}/(q;q)_n 的 Hecke 型分解系数为其实解析修正项的 Fourier 系数 a(n)。猜想：a(n) 在 n 为素数且 n≡1 (mod 4) 时恒正，在 n≡3 (mod 4) 时符号恰为负；且 |a(p)| 关于素数 p 的均值为 p^{1/2+o(1)} 的量级，其波动由固定 CM 椭圆曲线的 Frobenius 角分布控制。
