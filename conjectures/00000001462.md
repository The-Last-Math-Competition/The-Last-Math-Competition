# 00000001462

**English.** Definition: The product of the best constants of the Sobolev duality family. Conjecture: The interpolation product of the family of fractional Sobolev inequalities at the critical exponent is identically 1 (a critical interpolation identity).

**中文。** 定义：Sobolev 对偶族的最佳常数乘积。猜想：分数次 Sobolev 不等式族在临界指标处的插值乘积恒等式恒为 1(临界插值恒等)。
