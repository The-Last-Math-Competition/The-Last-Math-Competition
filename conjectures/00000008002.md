# 00000008002

**DEL-12 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: Absolutely minimizing extension for the infinity-Laplacian equation: the sharpening of the characteristic constants of comparison cones for AMLEs (absolutely minimizing Lipschitz extensions). Conjecture: The cone constant in the comparison principle for AMLEs is half the complementary angle of the opening angle of the tangent cone (Sharp Comparison with Cones); the gradient lines of AMLEs are geodesics (the geodesicity of Aronsson characteristics), and at separation points (branching points) of the flow lines the branching angle has the explicit value 2 arctan(1/2) (a rigid branching angle); and the dimension upper bound of branching points is n-2 (branching sparsity). (AMLE cone comparison and branching angle)

**中文。** 定义：无穷调和（∞-Laplacian）方程的绝对极小延拓：AMLE（absolutely minimizing Lipschitz extension）的比较锥的特征常数的锐化。猜想：AMLE 的比较原理的锥常数为切锥的张角的 1/2 余角（Sharp Comparison with Cones）；且 AMLE 的流线（gradient lines）为测地线（ARonsson 的特征线的测地性）在流线的分离点（分叉点）处的分叉角的显式值为 2arctan(1/2)（分叉刚角）；分叉点的维数上界为 n-2（分叉稀疏性）。
