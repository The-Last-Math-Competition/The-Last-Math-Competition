# 00000001493

**HY-13 ◆** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Palis's conjecture asserts that typical systems have finitely many attractors with stable statistical asymptotics. Conjecture: In the space of surface diffeomorphisms, the set of parameters simultaneously possessing finitely many physical measures is residual (topologically typical) and of full Lebesgue measure; the exceptional parameter set is of first category.

**中文。** 定义：Palis 猜想断言典型系统具有有限个吸引子且统计渐近行为稳定。猜想：曲面微分同胚的空间中,同时拥有有限多个物理测度的参数集合为剩余(拓扑上典型)且满 Lebesgue 测度;例外参数集为第一纲集。
