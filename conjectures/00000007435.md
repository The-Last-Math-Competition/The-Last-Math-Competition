# 00000007435

**English.** Definition: The counting of arithmetic progressions, Roth. Conjecture: The density threshold of progressions holds, and the improvement of the threshold is a logarithmic power. (log-power Roth density improvement)

**中文。** 定义：算术级数的计数：Roth。猜想：Roth 的密度：级数的密度阈值且值的改进为对数幂。（Roth 对数幂级数密度阈值密度计数改进）
