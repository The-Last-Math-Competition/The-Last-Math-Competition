# 00000003475

**English.** Definition: Permutation graphs are permutation-intersection structures, whose h-vectors relate to Euler subgraph counts. Conjecture: A bijection exists between h-vectors of permutation graphs and Euler subgraph counts: h_i is the number of permutation decompositions with exactly i descents; the bijection is constructed explicitly by the Foata transformation and is uniform over all permutation graphs. (permutation h-Euler bijection)

**中文。** 定义：置换图为置换交图结构，其 h-向量与 Euler 子图计数相关。猜想：置换图的 h-向量与 Euler 子图计数之间存在双射：h_i 为恰含 i 个下降的置换分解数；双射由 Foata 转换显式构造且对一切置换图一致。（置换 h-Euler 双射）
