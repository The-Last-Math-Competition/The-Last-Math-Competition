# 00000002850

**English.** Definition: Nonnegative matrix factorization (NMF): the algebraic geometry of nonnegativity constraints. Conjecture: Rank rigidity of NMF: the separation of nonnegative rank from ordinary rank starts at rank 4, with the minimal separating matrix an explicit 4×4 nonnegative matrix; the geometric characterization of the separation is a non-triangulable pair of nested polytopes. (nonnegative rank separation geometry)

**中文。** 定义：非负矩阵分解（NMF）：非负约束的代数几何。猜想：NMF 的秩刚性：非负秩与普通秩的分离在秩 4 起发生且最小分离矩阵为 Sl = 显式 4×4 非负矩阵；分离的几何刻画为嵌套多面体的非可三角化对。（非负秩分离几何）
