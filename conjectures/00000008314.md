**English.** Definition: The deepening of homological mirror symmetry: the depth index dms_deep of the deep extension of the equivalence between the Fukaya category and the derived category. Conjecture: dms_deep (the existence of deep levels of the HMS equivalence) is depth 1 for compact Calabi-Yau (a depth-one law); the lifting of depth (noncompact depth) equals the codimension of the skeleton (a codimension depth law); and the coherence obstruction of deep equivalences (commutation of Gysin sequences) is the automorphisms of the sphere spectrum (a sphere spectrum obstruction law), the order of the obstruction group being the stable homotopy at 24 (a 24 stable homotopy law). (HMS deep extension codimension 24 obstruction)
