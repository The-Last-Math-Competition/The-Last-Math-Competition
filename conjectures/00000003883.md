# 00000003883

**CA3-03 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: The F-pure threshold fpt(I) is the supremum of critical parameters at which no jump of the truncated test ideal occurs. Conjecture: For the binomial ideal (x^a, y^b) with gcd(a,b)=1, fpt = min(a,b) when p does not divide min(a,b); otherwise fpt = min(a,b) minus an explicit correction term depending only on the p-adic valuation of min(a,b). (binomial fpt formula)

**中文。** 定义：F-纯阈值 fpt(I) 指使 test ideal 截断不发生跳跃的临界参数上确界。猜想：二元单项式理想 (x^a, y^b) 在 gcd(a,b)=1 时 fpt = min(a,b) 当 p 不整除 min(a,b),否则 fpt = min(a,b) 减去仅依赖 min(a,b) 的 p-进赋值的显式修正项。（二元单项式 fpt 公式）
