# 00000002860

**AS-30 △** — source: 80_代数统计与张量分解的几何.md

**English.** Definition: The statistical stability of higher-moment tensor decompositions: the algebraic structure of cumulants. Conjecture: Stability rigidity of cumulant decompositions: the stability condition for orthogonal decompositions of fourth-order cumulant tensors is an eigenvalue gap exceeding twice the noise variance, and under the gap condition the perturbation bound is linear. (cumulant decomposition stability bound)

**中文。** 定义：高阶矩张量的分解的统计稳定性：累积量的代数结构。猜想：累积量分解的稳定性刚性：四阶累积量张量的正交分解的稳定性条件为特征间隙大于噪声方差的两倍且间隙条件下扰动界为线性。（累积量分解稳定界）
