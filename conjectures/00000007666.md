# 00000007666

**QDE-06 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: For the q-difference operator D_q f(z)=(f(qz)-f(z))/((q-1)z) on the partition generating function P(z), define the k-th q-difference coefficient b^{(k)}_n as the coefficient limit of D_q^k P(z) as z approaches 1 along the real axis after a Stirling change of variables. Conjecture: For each k>=1 the sequence {b^{(k)}_n mod 2} is eventually periodic with minimal period 2^{c(k)}, where c(k) is explicitly characterized by the binary digit sum of k; and the critical value of the difference order is exactly the reciprocal of the partition convergence radius. (parity periodicity of q-difference partition coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：对分拆数的 q-差分算子 D_q f(z)=(f(qz)-f(z))/((q-1)z)，记 p(n) 的生成函数 P(z)=Π_{m≥1}(1-z^m)^{-1}，并定义 k 阶 q-差分系数 b^{(k)}_n 为 D_q^k P(z)|_{z→1 沿实轴} 的系数极限经 Stirling 换元后的值。猜想：对每个 k≥1，序列 {b^{(k)}_n mod 2} 最终周期，且最小周期为 2^{c(k)}，其中 c(k) 为 k 的二进制数字和显式的结构刻画；且差分阶的临界值恰为分拆数的转换半径倒数。
