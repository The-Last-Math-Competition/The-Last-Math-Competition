**PI-25 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: Strict identities and non-strict inequalities: the inequality side of PI. Conjecture: The PI semantics of strict inequalities: the existence of everywhere-nonzero polynomials is characterized by the algebra's truth degree not exceeding one algebraic closure, and the complement of the nonzero domain is a finite union of algebraic varieties. (nonzero polynomial existence characterization)

**中文。** 定义：严格恒等式与非严格（不等式）：PI 的不等式侧。猜想：严格不等式的 PI 语义：处处非零的多项式的存在域刻画为代数的真实度不超过一代数闭包且非零域的补集为代数簇的有限并。（非零多项式存在刻画）
