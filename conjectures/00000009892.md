**GBR-12 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Exchange graphs of cluster-tilting objects: connectivity of exchange graphs of 2-Calabi-Yau categories (proven by Iyama-Yoshino). Conjecture: The diameter of the exchange graph of cluster-tilting objects has an explicit upper bound: a linear combination of the cluster variable count and the rank of the category; the bound is attained by extremal words of elementary transformation word lengths of ring-type cluster algebras. (exchange graph diameter upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：簇倾斜对象的交换图：2-Calabi–Yau 范畴的交换图的连通性（Iyama–Yoshino 已证连通）。猜想：簇倾斜对象的交换图的直径有显式上界：界为簇变量个数与范畴的秩的线性组合；界由环型簇代数的初等变换字长的极值字达到。（交换图直径上界）
