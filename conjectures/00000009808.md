# 00000009808

**English.** Definition: Koenigs dual meshes: the dual edge-length conserving structure of quadrilateral meshes. Conjecture: A quadrilateral mesh admits a Koenigs dual if and only if the cross-ratio of edge-cycle length differences is constant; the criterion is solvability of a linear system, and the dual mesh is unique up to isomorphism. (Koenigs duality criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Königs 对偶网格：四边形网格的对偶边长守恒结构。猜想：四边形网格容许 Königs 对偶当且仅当边圈长差的交比为常值；判据为线性方程组的可解性且对偶网格在同构意义下唯一。（Koenigs 对偶判据）
