# 00000006168

**English.** Definition: The saddle-point method is the estimation framework of fast-growing coefficients. Conjecture: The saddle-point window of exponential-type generating functions is of half-power type with endpoints controlled by tail integrals, the window being the relative width. (half-power relative width of saddle windows)

**中文。** 定义：鞍点法为快速增长系数的估计框架。猜想：指数型母函数的鞍点窗为半幂型且端点由尾部积分控制；且控制的窗为相对宽度。（鞍点窗半幂相对宽度）
