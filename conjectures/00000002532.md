# 00000002532

**English.** Definition: Tangent measures: the family of local blow-up limit measures of a set at a point. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of tangent-measure families degenerates to a single point at n-uniform measure points; for general Radon measures the minimal closed invariant subfamily of the tangent family is a lattice of convex combinations of affine k-plane measures, and the lattice is at most countable. (tangent measure invariant subfamily lattice)

**中文。** 定义：切测度（tangent measure）：集合在一点的局部缩放极限测度族。猜想：切测度族的维数谱在 n-均匀测度点退化为单点；一般 Radon 测度的切测度族的极小闭不变子族为仿射 k 平面测度的凸组合格且格的基数为至多可数。（切测度不变子族格）
