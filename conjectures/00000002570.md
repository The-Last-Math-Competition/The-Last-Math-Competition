# 00000002570

**CM-10 △** — source: 71_计算复杂性中的数学.md

**English.** Definition: Circuit complexity of families of natural mathematical objects. Conjecture: The circuit size of character-table families has an exponential lower bound proved by spectral methods; and the kernel of the spectral method is Fourier multiplicity. (character table spectral circuit lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：自然数学对象族的电路复杂度。猜想：特征标表族的电路尺寸为指数下界且证明为谱方法；且谱方法的核为 Fourier 重数。（特征标表谱下界电路）
