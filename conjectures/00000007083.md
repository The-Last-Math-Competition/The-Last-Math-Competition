**FK3-23 ★** — source: 221_FukayaII.md

**English.** Definition: The categorical side of symplectic flips with the birational Fukaya. Conjecture: The categorical equivalence of symplectic flips is the gluing of semiorthogonal complements, and the kernel of the gluing is the objects of the flip center. (center-kernel semiorthogonal flip gluing)

**中文。** 定义：辛翻转的范畴侧：Fukaya 的双有理。猜想：翻转的范畴：辛翻转的范畴等价为半正交补的粘合且粘的核为翻转中心的对象。（辛翻转半正交粘合中心核范畴等价）
