# 00000006307

**English.** Definition: The link between K-groups of number fields and function values. Conjecture: The class-number-formula bridge is equivalent to rank zero for totally real fields; the bridge coefficient is derivative regularization; and the constant of derivative regularization is the square root of the discriminant. (rank-zero bridge coefficient of totally real fields)

**中文。** 定义：数域的群与函数值的联系。猜想：类数公式的桥在全实域秩零等价；且桥系数为导数正则化；且导数正则化的常数为判别式的平方根。（全实域秩零桥系数）
