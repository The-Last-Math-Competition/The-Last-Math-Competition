**English.** Definition: Full replica symmetry breaking is the order-parameter function structure of infinitely many layers. Conjecture: The limiting order parameter of random matching is a purely jumping function with dense support, it converges by a Murray-type recursion, and the limit is unique. (unique dense limit of the full-RSB order parameter)
