# 00000006675

**English.** Definition: The perturbation-elimination structure of the flow formula. Conjecture: The elimination steps are polynomial with explicit exponent; the threshold of the exponent is the capacity ratio; and the window ends of the capacity ratio are controlled by the augmentation-path length. (polynomial exponent of amplification steps)

**中文。** 定义：流公式的扰动消除结构。猜想：消除步为多项式且指数显式；且指数的门槛为容量比；且容量比的窗端由增广路径长度控制。（放大步多项式指数）
