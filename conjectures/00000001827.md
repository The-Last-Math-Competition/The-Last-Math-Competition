# 00000001827

**English.** Definition: The secant-variety dimension defect of the rational normal curve is the gap between the actual and expected dimensions. Conjecture: The first case of positive defect is the classical pentaspherical orbit; apart from it the defect is zero (dimensions are as expected); the classification table is exhausted by orbit analysis of the Veronese embedding.

**中文。** 定义：有理正规曲线的割线簇维数偏差指其实际维数与预期维数之差。猜想：偏差 > 0 的首例为古典五球面型(Pentaspherical)轨道;除此之外零偏差(即割线簇维数预期);分类表由 Veronese 嵌入的轨道分析穷尽。
