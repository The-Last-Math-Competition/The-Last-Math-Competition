**MT2-01 ★** — source: 148_数学的元问题.md

**English.** Definition: An independence certificate is a complete argument proving the independence of a statement. Conjecture: The combinatorial size lower bound of any independence certificate of the continuum hypothesis is an explicit quantity characterized by the minimal branching structure of the conditional tree, attained and not compressible. (size lower bound of CH independence certificates)

**中文。** 定义：独立证书指证明陈述独立性的完整论证。猜想：连续统假设的任何独立证书的组合尺寸下界为条件树的最小分支结构所刻画的显式量,下界达到且不可再压。（CH 独立证书尺寸下界）
