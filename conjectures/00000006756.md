**TL3-26 ★** — source: 210_代数表示论IV_倾斜理论II.md

**English.** Definition: Repetitive algebras with the realization structure of stable categories. Conjecture: The stable-category realization is unique and the closed bridge is the preprojective image; the kernel of the preprojective image is stable one-point vanishing; and the realization isomorphism is equivariant under translation. (unique stable realization of repetitive algebras)

**中文。** 定义：重复代数：稳定范畴的实现结构。猜想：稳定范畴实现唯一且封闭的桥为预投射像；预投射像的核为稳定单点消没；实现的同构在平移下等变。（重复代数稳定唯一实现）
