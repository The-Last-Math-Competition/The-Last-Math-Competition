# 00000008417

**DFG-07 ◆** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: A Steiner system S(t,k,n) is a design in which every t-element subset lies in exactly one block. Conjecture: S(t,k,n) exists if and only if the classical divisibility conditions hold and n is at least n_0(t,k); n_0(t,k) admits an explicit bound of type (kt)^{ct}, and the lower bound n_0(5,6) > 12 is given by a concrete divisibility obstruction. (explicit threshold for Steiner systems)

**中文。** 定义：Steiner 系 S(t,k,n) 指每个 t 元子集含于恰一个区组的设计。猜想：S(t,k,n) 存在当且仅当经典整除条件满足且 n ≥ n₀(t,k)；n₀(t,k) ≤ (kt)^{ct} 型显式界，且下界 n₀(5,6) > 12 由具体整除障碍给出。（Steiner 系显式阈值）
