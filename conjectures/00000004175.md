# 00000004175

**English.** Definition: The freedom allocation is the assignment of degrees of freedom to the congruence constraints in efficient congruencing. Conjecture: The feasible allocation region is a rational polytope whose vertices are given exactly by all permutations, and the total number of vertices is k factorial. (vertices of the EC freedom polytope)

**中文。** 定义：自由度分配指高效同余法中各同余约束的自由度指派。猜想：可行指派域为有理多面体,顶点恰由全部置换给出,顶点总数为 k 的阶乘。（EC 自由度多面体顶点）
