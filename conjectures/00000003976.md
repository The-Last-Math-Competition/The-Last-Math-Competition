**English.** Definition: bounded depth Frege denotes AC type proof system of depth d. Conjecture: exact exponent of length lower bound of parity expression in depth d Frege is constant factor of 2^{n^{1/(d−1)}}, where exponent 1/(d−1) and d without pass. (depth Frege parity exact exponent)

**中文。** 定义：有界深度 Frege 指深度 d 受限的 AC 型证明系统。猜想：parity 重言式在深度 d Frege 的长度下界的精确指数为 2^{n^{1/(d−1)}},其中指数 1/(d−1) 的常数因子与 d 无关。（深度 Frege parity 精确指数）
