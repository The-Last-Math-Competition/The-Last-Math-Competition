**PC2-06 ◆** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: Bounded-depth Frege is the AC-type proof system with depth restricted to d. Conjecture: The exact exponent in the length lower bound for parity tautologies in depth-d Frege is 2^{n^{1/(d-1)}}, where the constant factor of the exponent 1/(d-1) is independent of d. (exact depth-Frege parity exponent)

**中文。** 定义：有界深度 Frege 指深度 d 受限的 AC 型证明系统。猜想：parity 重言式在深度 d Frege 的长度下界的精确指数为 2^{n^{1/(d−1)}},其中指数 1/(d−1) 的常数因子与 d 无关。（深度 Frege parity 精确指数）
