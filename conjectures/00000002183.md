# 00000002183

**English.** Definition: The quantitative bound of the triangle removal lemma is the functional relation between ε and δ. Conjecture: The bound is superpolynomial (i.e. δ(ε) is not a polynomial in ε); the constructive obstruction comes from ε-regular subsets of Behrend-type graph families.

**中文。** 定义：三角移除引理的定量界指 ε 与 δ 的函数关系。猜想：界为超多项式(即 δ(ε) 不为 ε 的多项式);构造型障碍由 Behrend 型图族的 ε-正则子集给出。
