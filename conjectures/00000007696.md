# 00000007696

**English.** Definition: In a self-similar tiling with inflation factor a PV number lambda, the area ratios of the tiles form the area spectrum S(lambda). Conjecture: When lambda is a cubic PV number, S(lambda) is the additive semigroup generated by units of norm <= lambda in the totally positive cone of Q(lambda); and two distinct cubic PV numbers give the same area spectrum if and only if their minimal polynomials agree after Galois conjugation. (PV area spectra)

**中文。** 定义：膨胀因子为 PV 数 λ 的自相似铺砌中，各 tile 面积之比构成面积谱 S(λ)。猜想：当 λ 为 3 次 PV 数时 S(λ) 是 Q(λ) 的完全正锥中由范数 ≤λ 的单位生成的加法半群；两个不同 3 次 PV 数给出相同面积谱当且仅当它们的极小多项式经 Galois 共轭后一致。
