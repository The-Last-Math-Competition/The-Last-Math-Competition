**English.** Definition: Density matrix renormalization group (DMRG): variational one-dimensional algorithms. Conjecture: The convergence bottleneck of DMRG: the iteration count of convergence is exponential in the entanglement, and the breaking of the bottleneck is the branching factor of tree tensor networks. (DMRG entanglement exponential bottleneck)
