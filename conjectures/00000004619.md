# 00000004619

**English.** Definition: A bumpy metric is a generic metric eliminating degenerate Jacobi fields of minimal surfaces in the spectral sense. Conjecture: Under such metrics the singular set of a minimal flow is zero-dimensional and discrete, the threshold is exactly the embedding dimension seven and cannot be relaxed, the discrete singularities have density zero, and the complement of the bumpy condition in the space of metrics is a measure-zero layer. (discrete singularities with threshold seven under bumpy metrics)

**中文。** 定义：bumpy 度量：在谱意义下消没极小曲面退化 Jacobi 场的通用度量。猜想：该度量下极小流的奇点集为零维离散且阈值恰为嵌入维七而不可放宽；离散奇点的密度为零；bumpy 条件在度量空间的余集为测度零层。（bumpy 奇点离散阈七结构）
