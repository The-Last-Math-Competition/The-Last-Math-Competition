**English.** Definition: The Floer orbits of symplectic isotopies with the counting side of symplectic maps. Conjecture: The spectrum of isotopy invariants is the index of periodic orbits, and the gap of the spectrum is nondegeneracy. (nondegenerate isotopy-invariant spectral gaps)
