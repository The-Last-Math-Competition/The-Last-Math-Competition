# 00000007231

**English.** Definition: Rudnick-Sarnak with quantum equidistribution. Conjecture: The equidistribution of high-energy eigenstates holds, and the remainder is the spectral gap. (spectral-gap Rudnick-Sarnak quantum equidistribution)

**中文。** 定义：Rudnick–Sarnak：量子等分布。猜想：量子遍历的本征态：高能本征态的等分布且布的余项为谱间隙。（Rudnick-Sarnak 谱间隙高能本征态等分布余项量子遍历）
