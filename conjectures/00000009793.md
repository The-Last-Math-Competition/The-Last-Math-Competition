# 00000009793

**English.** Definition: Circle packing: Thurston approximated conformal maps by hexagonal circle packings; Rodin-Sullivan proved convergence. Conjecture: The convergence error of circle packing maps to smooth conformal maps is O(r^{1/2}), with r the packing radius; the exponent 1/2 is optimal, attained by extremal configurations of the ratio sequence of inscribed to neighboring circles. (circle packing convergence exponent)

**中文。** 定义：圆填充：Thurston 用六角圆填充逼近共形映射；Rodin–Sullivan 证明收敛性。猜想：圆填充映射到光滑共形映射的收敛误差为 O(r^{1/2})，r 为填充半径；指数 1/2 为最优且由内切圆与邻圆的比率序列的极端构型达到。（圆填充收敛指数）
