# 00000009917

**English.** Definition: Central limit theorems for Betti curves of random Cech complexes. Conjecture: The k-th Betti number of Poisson Cech complexes satisfies a CLT with asymptotic variance an explicit kernel integral; the normalized limit is Gaussian, and the variance-to-mean ratio of the Gaussian tends to the universal constant 1/2. (random Cech CLT)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Čech 复形的 Betti 曲线的中心极限定理。猜想：泊松 Čech 复形的第 k 维 Betti 数满足 CLT 且方差渐近为显式核积分；归一化极限为高斯且高斯的方差-均值比趋于普适常数 1/2。（随机 Cech CLT）
