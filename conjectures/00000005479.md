**English.** Definition: The spectral parameter difference is the rapidity gap of momenta. Conjecture: The conversion constant of the fusion angle with the rapidity difference is explicit of inverse-hyperbolic type of the mass ratio, equality at the equal-mass chain, and the conversion is unique. (inverse-hyperbolic conversion constant of fusion rapidities)
