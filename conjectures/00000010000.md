**NCR-30 △** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Entropy of algebraic automorphisms: algebraic entropy growth of ring automorphisms. Conjecture: The dichotomy — zero or infinite algebraic entropy of automorphisms of finitely generated algebras — holds within the PI class; the minimal algebra with intermediate entropy outside the PI class is of Veronese type, with entropy a logarithmic algebraic number. (algebraic entropy dichotomy threshold)

**中文。** 定义：代数自同构的熵：环自同构的代数熵增长。猜想：有限生成代数自同构的代数熵为零或无穷的二分在 PI 类内成立；非 PI 类的中间熵例子的最小代数为 Veronese 型且其熵为对数代数数。（代数熵二分阈值）
