# 00000006176

**English.** Definition: Connected counting is the asymptotics of the block-partition structure. Conjecture: The singularity exponent is controlled by the block-size distribution, the combination of the control being the exponential functional; the lower bound of the exponent is 1/2, attained by the uniform block distribution; and the correction in the critical window is Poissonization with window width the inverse standard deviation. (block-distribution exponent functional of singularities)

**中文。** 定义：连通计数：块分割结构的渐近（图 组合类的奇点）。猜想：奇点指数由块大小分布控制且控制的组合为指数泛函；指数的下界为 1/2 且界由均匀块分布达到；临界窗内的修正为泊松化且窗宽为标准差的逆。（块分布指数泛函奇点）
