# 00000008111

**NAG-01 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Hilbert lattice of the point module space of an Artin-Schelter regular algebra A (connected graded, polynomial growth, Gorenstein parameter d). Conjecture: For d = 3 the indecomposable classification of point modules of AS-regular algebras is completely given by rational points of an elliptic curve or integer points of a parametric curve (an elliptic-hyperbolic dichotomy); the dimension of the point module space in the hyperbolic type (parametric curve) is always 1; and the order of the automorphism group of a point module of elliptic type is at most 24 (a noncommutative realization of the Hurwitz bound for elliptic automorphisms). (AS-regular elliptic-hyperbolic point modules)

**中文。** 定义：Artin–Schelter 正则代数 A（连通分次、多项式增长、Gorenstein 参数 d）的点模空间的 Hilbert 格。猜想：d=3 的 AS-正则代数的点模的不可分解分类由椭圆曲线的有理点或参数曲线的整点完全给出（椭圆-双曲二分）；且双曲型（参数曲线）的点模空间的维数恒为 1；椭圆型的点模的自同构群的阶 ≤ 24（椭圆自同构 Hurwitz 界的非交换实现）。
