# 00000004453

**SY3-03 ◆** — source: 134_辛几何III_镜像对称深化.md

**English.** Definition: The enumerative invariants of punctured curves are the wrapped Floer counts of pointed Riemann surfaces. Conjecture: The invariants are explicit polynomials in the number of punctures with interval-combinatorial coefficients, and as the puncture number tends to infinity the leading coefficient is asymptotic to a power of 2. (coefficients of the punctured enumerative polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：穿孔曲线的枚举不变量指带点黎曼面的包裹 Floer 计数。猜想：不变量为穿孔数的显式多项式,系数为区间组合数,穿孔数趋于无穷时首项系数以 2 的幂渐近。（穿孔枚举多项式系数）
