**English.** Definition: enumerative invariant of curve denotes Floer counting of point Riemann surface. Conjecture: invariant is explicit polynomial, coefficient of number is power asymptotic of interval binomial coefficient, number tends to infinite when leading term coefficient 2. (polynomial coefficient)

**中文。** 定义：穿孔曲线的枚举不变量指带点黎曼面的包裹 Floer 计数。猜想：不变量为穿孔数的显式多项式,系数为区间组合数,穿孔数趋于无穷时首项系数以 2 的幂渐近。（穿孔枚举多项式系数）
