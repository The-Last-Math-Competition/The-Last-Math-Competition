# 00000000887

**English.** Definition: The Yudovich log-Lipschitz class is the critical class for two-dimensional fluid regularity. Conjecture: The double-exponential growth bound is optimal and cannot be improved; and the counterexample to optimality is a composition of shear flows. (log-Lipschitz optimal double exponential)

**中文。** 定义：Yudovich 对数 Lipschitz 涡度为二维流体正则性的临界类。猜想：双指数增长界为最优且不可改进；且最优性的反例为剪切流复合。（log-Lipschitz 最优双指数）
