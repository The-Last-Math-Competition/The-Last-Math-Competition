# 00000006195

**English.** Definition: The energy threshold is the minimal energy of blow-up solutions, the ground-state gate. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit critical ratio of the threshold, the minimal energy of blow-up solutions given by the ground state; after normalization the ratio is always one; and solutions below the threshold exist globally and scatter. (normalized unit ratio of the ground-state threshold)

**中文。** 定义：能量门阈：爆破解的最小能量（基态门槛）。猜想：换算常数为门阈的显式临界比且爆破解的最小能量由基态给出；归一化后比值恒为一；能量低于门阈的解整体存在且散射。（基态门阈比值归一常一）
