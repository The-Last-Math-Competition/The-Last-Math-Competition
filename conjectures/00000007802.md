# 00000007802

**ACG-22 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: concentration: L_K as of isotropy constant is variance of function in random perturbation (random vertex) under of K. Conjecture: pair K= all random polytope (N element ball plane point), Var(L_{K_N}) = O(n^2/N^2), namely isotropy constant concentration, variance strictly less than volume concentration exponent; and point concentration of the bound in N=n^{3/2} generates phase transition, this module variance n/N.

**中文。** 定义：迷向常数的超集中：L_K 作为 K 的函数在随机扰动（如随机顶点删除）下的方差。猜想：对 K=均匀随机多胞形（N 个球面点），Var(L_{K_N}) = O(n^2/N^2)，即迷向常数呈超集中，方差严格小于体积集中指数；且该界在 N=n^{3/2} 处发生相变，越过此规模方差呈 n/N 的单点集中律。
