# 00000004615

**English.** Definition: A Lawson cone is a quadratic cone family of area-minimizing type. Conjecture: Its stability is completely classified by the explicit sign of a series function, the classification is closed for all dimension pairs and consistent with the known rows, and the exceptional dimension pairs are exactly the codimension vectors of the Simons cone. (complete classification of Lawson stability)

**中文。** 定义：Lawson 锥指面积极小的二次锥族。猜想：其稳定性由级数函数的显式符号完全分类,分类对一切维数对封闭且与已知行一致。(Lawson 稳定性完全分类)；且例外维数对恰为 Simons 锥的余维向量。
