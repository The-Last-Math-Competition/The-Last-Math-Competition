# 00000004258

**English.** Definition: The Givental decomposition is the reconstruction of a cohomological field theory from a two-dimensional theory and an R matrix. Conjecture: The renormalization constants are given explicitly by the pole residues of the spectral curve, they satisfy the symplectic condition equations, and they are unique under that constraint. (Givental renormalization pole residues)

**中文。** 定义：Givental 分解指上同调场论由二维理论与 R 矩阵的重构。猜想：重整化常数由谱曲线极点残差显式给出,常数满足辛条件方程且在该约束下唯一。（Givental 重整极点残差）
