**DEL-11 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The p-harmonic topological degree: the computation formula and index of the p-harmonic degree deg_p(grad u, Omega, 0) of the map grad u. Conjecture: deg_p is given by the topological degree of the p-normalized normal map of the boundary values (degree formula); the sum of indices of isolated singularities (a Poincare-Hopf type theorem) is a p-invariant correction of chi(Omega); the correction vanishes in the p to 2 limit (recovery of the classical harmonic case), and in the p to infinity limit it becomes the Cheeger index of the absolute gradient (the value-function geometry of infinity-harmonic functions). (p-harmonic degree Poincare-Hopf correction)

**中文。** 定义：p-调和代数拓扑度：映射 ∇u 的 p-调和度 deg_p(∇u,Ω,0) 的计算公式与指标。猜想：deg_p 由边界值的 p-规范化法映射的拓扑度给出（度公式）；且孤立奇点的指标的和（Poincaré–Hopf 型定理）为 χ(Ω) 的 p-不变修正；修正项在 p→2 的极限为消失（经典调和的恢复），p→∞ 的极限为绝对值梯度的 Cheeger 指标（∞-harmonic 的值函数几何）。
