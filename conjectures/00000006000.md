**English.** Definition: Birational rigidity and connectedness are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two varieties with the same rigidity but different rational connectedness, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same rigidity but different equivalence-class counts. (same rigidity-different connectedness equivalence-class separation)

**中文。** 定义：双有理刚度与连通度指两层。猜想：存在两簇的刚度相同而有理连通度不同,分离由同刚异等价类数的显式对实现。（刚度同-连通度异等价类分离）
