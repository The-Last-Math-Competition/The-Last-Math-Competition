**BR4-17 ◆** — source: 185_代数几何VII_双有理几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Rational connectedness means any two points are joinable by a rational curve. Conjecture: The degree spectrum of free curves is controlled by the equivalence-class count, the table is bounded, and the constant of the bound is the moduli-space dimension. (moduli-dimension control of rational connectedness)

**中文。** 定义：有理连通为两点可由有理曲线连接。猜想：自由曲线度的谱由等价类数控制且表有界；且界的常数为模空间维数。（有理连通模空间维数控制）
