**English.** Definition: The two-stage min-max is the two-layer width construction, first on submanifolds and then globally. Conjecture: The two-stage width difference is zero if and only if the intermediate minimal surface is a double cover, the decision condition is complete, and when the difference is positive it is a spectral-gap-type functional. (criterion for the two-stage width difference)
