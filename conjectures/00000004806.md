**FA4-26 ◆** — source: 145_代数几何IV_Fano簇.md

**English.** Definition: The isomorphism-class count of four-dimensional reflexive polytopes is known to be 473800776. Conjecture: This count has an Euler-type product factorization with factors an explicit product of small prime powers; the uniqueness of the factorization is determined by the Hodge data of the generating function; the corresponding five-dimensional count is finite, and its residue modulo 10 is an explicitly computable value. (Euler factorization of the four-dimensional count)

**中文。** 定义：四维反射多面体的同构类计数（已知为 473800776）。猜想：该计数有 Euler 型乘积分解且因子为小素数幂的显式积；分解的唯一性由母函数的 Hodge 数据确定；五维对应计数为有限且其模 10 的余数为可显式计算的值。（四维计数 Euler 分解）
