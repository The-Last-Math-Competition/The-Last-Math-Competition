**TIL-29 △** — source: 242_离散几何II_镶嵌与铺砌.md

**English.** Definition: Under a local long-range rearrangement of a Poisson-Voronoi tessellation (adding and removing a point, a Gibbs sampling step), the mean changes in edge count delta_e and vertex count delta_v satisfy the Euler balance 2 delta_e - 3 delta_v = 0. Conjecture: For the planar unit-intensity process, delta_e = 6 + O(1/r) in the rearrangement radius r, with the correction given explicitly by the Fisher information of the joint area-edge distribution under Lewis's law; in three dimensions the limit of delta_e is 2(1+sqrt 2)/3. (Euler balance of Voronoi resampling)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Voronoi 铺砌的拓扑量变化：在泊萨点过程加一点与去一点的局部长程重排（Gibbs 采样步）中，改变的平均边数 δ_e 与平均顶点数 δ_v 满足 Euler 平衡 2δ_e-3δ_v=0。猜想：二维单位强度过程中 δ_e=6+O(1/r)（r 为重排半径）且修正项由 Lewis 律的面积-边联合分布的 Fisher 信息显式给出；三维时 δ_e 的极限值为 2(1+√2)/3。
