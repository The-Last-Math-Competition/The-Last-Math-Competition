**FM-04 ★** — source: 65_分形几何与测度.md

**English.** Definition: The dimension spectrum of a Moran set is the distribution of local dimensions. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of uniform Moran sets is concave if and only if the geometric mean of the contraction ratios satisfies a logarithmic mean inequality; and the exception to concavity is oscillatory. (Moran spectrum concavity geometric-mean criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Moran 集维数谱为局部维数分布。猜想：均匀 Moran 的维数谱为凹当且仅当压缩比的几何平均满足对数均值不等式；且凹的例外为振荡型。（Moran 谱凹几何平均判据）
