**CA-62 △** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: A phantom morphism is a morphism of complexes that vanishes on homology. Conjecture: The phantom ideal is nilpotent if and only if the corresponding glueing of derived categories splits; and the criterion for structural rigidity is a direct-sum decomposition of the recollement. (phantom ideal nilpotence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：幻影态射为在同调上消没的复形态射。猜想：幻影理想幂零当且仅当导出范畴的粘合分裂；且结构刚性的判据为 recollement 的直和分解。（幻影理想幂零判据）
