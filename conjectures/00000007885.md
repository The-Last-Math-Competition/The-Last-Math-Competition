# 00000007885

**HCA-15 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: homotopy limit type A^{S^n} of high order iteration THH^{(n)}(A)=THH(THH^{(n-1)}(A)) of topology Hochschild homology. Conjecture: p- completion of homotopy groups of pair ball spectrum S, THH^{(n)}(S) has graded of filtration is symmetric tridiagonal system; its graded counting polynomial P_n(t) of three dimension coordinates (weight, stem, n) satisfies dimension generating function of spectrum homology of P_{n+1}=P_n · (1+t^{2^n}) (self-similar times), namely iteration THH is infinite product Π(1+t^{2^k}), and binary partition number uniform.

**中文。** 定义：拓扑 Hochschild 同调的高阶迭代 THH^{(n)}(A)=THH(THH^{(n-1)}(A)) 的同伦极限型 A^{S^n}。猜想：对球谱 S，THH^{(n)}(S) 的同伦群的 p-完备化有 filtration 的分次为三维坐标（weight, stem, n）的斜对称三对角系；其分次计数多项式 P_n(t) 满足 P_{n+1}=P_n·(1+t^{2^n})（自相似倍增律），即迭代 THH 的谱同调的维数生成函数为无穷乘积 Π(1+t^{2^k})，与二进制分拆数一致。
