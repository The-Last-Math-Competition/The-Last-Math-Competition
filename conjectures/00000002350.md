# 00000002350

**English.** Conjecture: The speedup of the linear-algebra step of quantum-HHL-assisted homotopy tracking is O(√κ/log κ) (κ the condition number); no stronger universal speedup exists (by quantum linear-algebra lower bounds); the exact form of the speedup is √κ/log κ.

**中文。** 猜想：量子 HHL 辅助的同伦追踪的线性代数步的加速比为 O(√{κ}/logκ)(κ 为条件数);无更强的通用加速(由量子线性代数的下界);加速比的精确形式为 √{κ}/logκ。
