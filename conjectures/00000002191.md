# 00000002191

**EC-21 ◆** — source: 58_极值集合论与组合.md

**English.** Definition: The 1/3–2/3 conjecture states that every non-chain poset contains a pair of elements ordered first with probability at least one third. Conjecture: The conjecture holds; and the tight-bound family for one third is an explicit extension of the standard example, with no other tight examples. (1/3-2/3 tight-example extension classification)

**中文。** 定义：三分之一与三分之二猜想指非链偏序含以三分之一概率先的元素对。猜想：猜想成立；且三分之一的紧确界族为标准例子的显式扩展而无其他紧例。（1/3-2/3 紧例扩展分类）
