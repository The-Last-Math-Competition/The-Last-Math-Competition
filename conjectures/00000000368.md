# 00000000368

**English.** Definition: Simultaneous approximation by exponential polynomials studies sequences of type (2ⁿα + 3ⁿβ). Conjecture: The simultaneous approximation of this sequence admits a Rogers-type upper bound; and the exponent of the bound is three halves. (exponential polynomial simultaneous approximation bound)

**中文。** 定义：指数多项式联立逼近研究 (2ⁿα+3ⁿβ) 型序列。猜想：该序列的联立逼近有 Rogers 型上界；且上界的指数为二分之三次幂。（指数多项式联立逼近界）
