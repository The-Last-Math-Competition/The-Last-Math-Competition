# 00000008468

**English.** Definition: The LMO invariant is the graph series invariant of 3-manifolds. Conjecture: The degree-2 part of LMO is Turaev-Viro type and trivalent; the degree-3 part is a q-series of the hyperbolic volume; the two jointly determine the first three layers of Ohtsuki coefficients, and the number of layers cannot be reduced. (LMO degree stratification determinacy)

**中文。** 定义：LMO 不变量指三流形的图形级数不变量。猜想：LMO 度 2 部分为 Turaev–Viro 型三值；度 3 部分为双曲体积的 q-级数；两者联合决定 Ohtsuki 系数前三层且层数不可降。（LMO 度分层决定）
