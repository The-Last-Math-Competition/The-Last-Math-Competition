# 00000004598

**English.** Definition: The hyperbolic isoperimetric deficit is the remainder functional of the isoperimetric inequality. Conjecture: The minimal positive lower bound of the isoperimetric deficit of domains with holes is an explicit function of the number of holes, attained by symmetric double-circular-arc domains and exact. (hole-count lower bound of the isoperimetric deficit)

**中文。** 定义：双曲等周亏指等周不等式的余量泛函。猜想：带孔区域的等周亏的最小正下界为孔数的显式函数,界由对称双圆弧域达到且精确。（带孔等周亏孔数下界）
