# 00000008214

**ORB-14 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The symmetry breaking operators of branching laws: the dimension sbc_dim of the space of symmetry breaking operators D(G,H) for a pair (G,H). Conjecture: sbc_dim (the breaking dimension at generic position) is 1 (a single breaking law); when (G,H) is a dual pair (Howe duality) the breaking is multiplicity-free (a multiplicity-free law); and the minimal pair with multiplicity greater than 1 is the Veronese embedding pair (SO(5), SO(3)) (a Veronese minimal multiplicity pair), whose maximal multiplicity is 2 (a Veronese multiplicity-two rigidity). (symmetry breaking Veronese multiplicity-two pair)

**中文。** 定义：表示的分歧律的对称破缺算子：对 (G,H) 的对称破缺算子空间 D(G,H) 的维数 sbc_dim。猜想：sbc_dim（一般位置的破缺维数）= 1（单破缺律）；且 (G,H) 为对偶对（Howe 对偶）时破缺为 multiplicity-free（自由重数律）；重数 > 1 的最小对为 (SO(5), SO(3)) 的 Veronese 嵌入对（Veronese 最小重数对），该对的最大重数为 2（Veronese 二重数刚性）。
