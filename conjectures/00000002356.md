# 00000002356

**OF-06 ★** — source: 64_算子理论II_函数演算与自由分析.md

**English.** Definition: The zero free variety of a free polynomial is the matrix level of its nc zero set. Conjecture: The matrix-convex hull of the zero variety of a two-generator free polynomial is an explicit domain; and the boundary of the domain is given by rank-one representations. (zero free variety matrix-convex hull)

**中文。** 定义：自由多项式的零点自由簇为 nc 零点集的矩阵层。猜想：两生成元自由多项式的零点簇的矩阵凸包为显式域；且域的边界为秩一表示。（零点自由簇矩阵凸包）
