# 00000005111

**English.** Definition: A Krylov subspace is the subspace spanned by powers of a matrix. Conjecture: The Krylov approximation error of the matrix exponential is controlled by an explicit hypergeometric bound of the spectral interval and the dimension, with equality at purely imaginary spectra and optimal constant, and the matching construction takes the Wilkinson counterexample family of Chebyshev spectra. (optimal hypergeometric bound of exponential Krylov approximation)

**中文。** 定义：Krylov 子空间指由矩阵幂张成的子空间。猜想：矩阵指数的 Krylov 逼近误差由谱区间与维数的显式超几何界控制,纯虚谱处等号且常数最优。(指数 Krylov 超几何最优界)；且匹配构造恰取 Chebyshev 谱的 Wilkinson 反例族。
