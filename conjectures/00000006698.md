**OT3-28 ★** — source: 208_最优传输II_离散.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal map with the regularity-window structure. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the regularity window and the jump set has measure zero; the window is given by the density ratio with the bound of the ratio the L-infinity ratio of the two measures; and outside the window the jump set is codimension one. (density-ratio window conversion of jump sets)

**中文。** 定义：最优映射：正则性窗结构。猜想：换算常数为正则窗的显式且跳跃集测度零；窗口由密度比给出且比的界为双测度的 L∞ 比；窗外的跳跃集为余维一。（跳跃集测度零密度比窗换算）
