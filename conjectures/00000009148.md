**English.** Definition: Polynomial order: the growth law of orders. Conjecture: Polynomial order: the growth of module orders, with the degree of the growth the representation rank; and the leading coefficient always equals the sum of squared representation weights divided by the rank. (polynomial order representation rank leading coefficient)

**中文。** 定义：多项式阶：序的增长律。猜想：多项式阶：模的阶的增长且长的度为表示的秩。(多项式阶表示秩模阶增长度序增长律)；且首项系数恒等于表示权的平方和除以秩。
