# 00000005601

**English.** Definition: Polynomial reduction is the polynomial-time basis reduction algorithm. Conjecture: The worst value of the reduction depth, the number of consecutive swap steps, is an explicit count, attained at Fibonacci lattices, and the expected depth is the logarithm of the worst case. (Fibonacci count of reduction depth)

**中文。** 定义：多项式规约指多项式时间的基规约算法。猜想：规约深度 (连续交换步数) 的最坏值为显式计数,斐波那契格处达到且期望深度为最坏的对数。（规约深度斐波那契计数）
