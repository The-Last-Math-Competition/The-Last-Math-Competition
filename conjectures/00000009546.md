# 00000009546

**QIC-26 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: Catalytic resources are auxiliaries that must be returned unchanged after use; catalytic entanglement can be strictly stronger than ordinary entanglement in LOCC transformations. Conjecture: Catalytic entanglement does not increase Shannon-type capacities of channels — the catalytic invariance law holds; but catalytic entanglement can increase the zero-error capacity C_0 by arbitrary polynomial factors, with the minimal gain channel of dimension 4 given by the Cabello graph structure. (catalytic capacity invariance law)

**中文。** 定义：催化资源指使用前后须原样归还的辅助；催化纠缠在 LOCC 转化中可严格强于普通纠缠。猜想：催化纠缠不增大信道的 Shannon 类容量，催化不变律成立；但催化纠缠可将零误差容量 C_0 增大任意多项式因子，最小增益信道的维数为 4 且由 Cabello 图结构给出。（催化容量不变律）
