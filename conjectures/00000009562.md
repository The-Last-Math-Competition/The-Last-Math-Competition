# 00000009562

**English.** Definition: The Rauzy-Veech-Zorich induction map is the accelerated renormalization preserving the natural measure, of Markov type; the decay of its correlation functions governs the statistics of billiard flows and IET sections. Conjecture: The correlations of Holder observables under the induction decay exponentially, with exponent controlled by an explicit function of the KZ spectral gap lambda_1 - lambda_2; and the decay spectrum acquires logarithmic corrections at the cusps of the compactification, with the correction exactly first order. (Zorich induction decay spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Rauzy–Veech–Zorich 诱导映射是强化化后保持自然测度的 Markov 型诱导；其关联函数衰减刻画台球流与 IET 断面的统计。猜想：诱导映射的 Hölder 可观测关联衰减为指数型，指数由 KZ 谱隙 λ_1−λ_2 的显式函数控制；衰减谱在紧化边界的尖点处出现对数修正且修正恰为一阶。（Zorich 诱导衰减谱）
