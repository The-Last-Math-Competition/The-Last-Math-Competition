# 00000003293

**English.** Definition: Clausen functions: polylogarithmic-type functions. Conjecture: The multiplication formula of Clausen: the double-angle formula of Cl₂ is the convolution of Bernoulli polynomials, with the closure of the convolution the double-sine identity. (Clausen double angle double-sine closure)

**中文。** 定义：Clausen 函数：多角函数。猜想：Clausen 的乘法公式：Cl₂ 的倍角公式为 Bernoulli 多项式的卷积且卷积的封闭为双正弦恒等式。（Clausen 倍角双正弦封闭）
