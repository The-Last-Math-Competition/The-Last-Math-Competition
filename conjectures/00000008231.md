# 00000008231

**EGT-01 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The regularity index of equilibrium price vectors: the regularity of the zero z(p*) = 0 of the excess demand z(p) of a pure exchange economy (n goods, m agents). Conjecture: A quantitative version of the genericity of regular economies (the measure of nonregular economies is a null set, proven by Debreu): the measure of nonregular economies decays double-exponentially in the number of agents m (a double-exponential sparsity law); and the mod 2 congruence of the count of equilibria (for regular economies) is the congruence of the Euler class (a parity congruence law): the number of equilibria is always congruent to 1 mod 2 (an odd-equilibrium rigidity). (regular economies double-exponential odd equilibria)

**中文。** 定义：平衡市场价格的不动点价格向量 p*：纯交换经济（n 商品，m 代理）的超额需求 z(p) 的零点 z(p*)=0 的正则指数 reg_ex。猜想：reg_ex（正则经济的普遍性）的临界（非正则经济的测度）为零集（Debreu 已证）的定量版本：非正则经济的测度的衰减为代理数 m 的双指数（双指数稀疏律）；且均衡的计数（正则经济的均衡数）的模 2 同余为 Euler 类的同余（奇偶同余律）：均衡数 ≡ 1 mod 2 恒成立（奇数均衡刚性）。
