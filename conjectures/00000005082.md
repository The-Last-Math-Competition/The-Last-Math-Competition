**English.** Definition: The geometric fundamental lemma is the geometrization of orbital-integral identities. Conjecture: Its conversion is an explicit combination of vanishing theorems, the dimensions of the pieces are given by the singularity spectrum of the fibers, and at the identity element it degenerates to elementary counting. (piece-dimension conversion of the geometric fundamental lemma)
