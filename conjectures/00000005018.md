# 00000005018

**English.** Definition: The parallel spinors are the space of parallel solutions of the spinor equation. Conjecture: Their dimension is stable under deformation of the structure, the stability domain is an explicit open set of the three-form differential, and instability occurs only at degenerate closed structures. (deformation-stable open set of parallel spinors)

**中文。** 定义：平行旋量指旋量方程的平行解空间。猜想：其维数在结构形变下稳定,稳定域为三形式微分的显式开集,失稳仅在退化闭结构处发生。（平行旋量形变稳定开集）
