**English.** Definition: The double Burnside ring is the algebraic model of stable homotopy. Conjecture: The unit group is explicit in the fusion self-equivalence classes, counted at rank two by characteristic subgroups, and the kernel of the count is the automorphism lattice. (characteristic-subgroup automorphism-lattice count of the double Burnside ring)
