# 00000000901

**English.** Definition: The free group factors L(Fₙ). Conjecture: L(Fₙ) ≇ L(F_m) for all n ≠ m; equivalently, the free group factor invariant log(m−1)/log(n−1) distinguishes all free group factors.

**中文。** 定义：自由群因子 𝕃(Fₙ)。猜想：𝕃(Fₙ) ≇ 𝕃(F_m) 对一切 n≠m;等价地,自由群因子的微缩不变量 log(m−1)/log(n−1) 区分一切自由群因子。
