**English.** Definition: K-functionals of Schatten classes: explicit renormalization of real interpolation. Conjecture: The real interpolation K-functional of Schatten class pairs is given by an explicit Lorentz renormalization of partial sums of singular values; the logarithmic correction coefficient of the K-functional at endpoints is unique and consistent with the sequence-space case. (Schatten K-functional explicit)

**中文。** 定义：Schatten 类的 K-泛函：实插值的显式重整化。猜想：Schatten 类对的实插值 K-泛函由奇异值的部分和的显式 Lorentz 重整给出；K-泛函在端点的对数修正系数唯一且与序列空间情形一致。（Schatten K 泛函显式）
