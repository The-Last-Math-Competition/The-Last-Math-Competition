# 00000001338

**English.** Definition: The distribution of the values of L(s, χ) at s = 1/2. Conjecture: For the quadratic-twist family {L(1/2, χ_d)} (d over fundamental discriminants) with fixed χ, the variance coefficient of the logarithmic moments has the explicit correction 2Σ_p 1/p² (the variance formula for the random-matrix χ analogue).

**中文。** 定义：L(s, χ) 在 s = 1/2 的值的分布。猜想：对固定 χ 的扭曲族 {L(1/2, χ_d)}(d 遍历基本判别式),对数矩的方差系数为 2Σ_p 1/p² 的显式修正(随机矩阵 χ 对应的方差公式)。
