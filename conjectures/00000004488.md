**English.** Definition: SDGR is the model topos of synthetic differential geometry. Conjecture: All SDGR models have mutually equivalent consistency strength, equivalent to bounded Zermelo set theory, the strength difference is always negligible, and the smallest differing example requires noncompact infinitesimal objects. (equivalence bound of SDGR consistency strength)
