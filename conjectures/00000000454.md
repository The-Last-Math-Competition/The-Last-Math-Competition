# 00000000454

**English.** Definition: The sweep map is the combinatorial correspondence for rational Catalan numbers. Conjecture: The expected number of iterations of the inverse of the sweep map is at most the square root of n; and the bound on the expectation follows from the reflection principle for random lattice paths. (sweep inverse iteration expectation bound)

**中文。** 定义：sweep 映射为有理 Catalan 的组合对应。猜想：sweep 的逆的迭代数期望不超过根号 n；且期望的界由随机格路的反射原理给出。（sweep 逆迭代期望界）
