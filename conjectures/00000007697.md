**TIL-07 △** — source: 242_离散几何II_镶嵌与铺砌.md

**English.** Definition: The tileability of a free n-omino P means some rectangle R is tiled by translation copies of P. Conjecture: The probability that a uniformly random free n-omino tiles some rectangle tends to 0 as n grows, decaying exponentially, with decay exponent exactly the logarithmic density of monotone path counts filling an {n/2} x {n/2} box. (exponential decay of n-omino tileability)

**中文。** 定义：自由 n-omino P 的可铺性指存在矩形 R 使 P 的平移拷贝铺满 R。猜想：均匀随机自由 n-omino 可铺某矩形的概率在 n→∞ 时趋于 0，且以指数速度衰减，衰减指数恰为铺满 {n/2}×{n/2} 方盒的单调路径计数的对数密度。
