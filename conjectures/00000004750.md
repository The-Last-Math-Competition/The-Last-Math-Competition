# 00000004750

**English.** Definition: Local real-point invariants and global Brauer classes are the two layers of arithmetic data. Conjecture: There exist two Azumaya algebras whose local invariants agree at all real points while their global Brauer classes differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair over an imaginary quadratic field. (same real points-global class separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：局部实点不变量与整体 Brauer 类指两层算术数据。猜想：存在两个 Azumaya 代数在一切实点的局部不变量相同而整体 Brauer 类不同,分离由虚二次域的显式对实现。（实点同-整体类分离对）
