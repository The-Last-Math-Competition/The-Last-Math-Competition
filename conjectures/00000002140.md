# 00000002140

**PS-30 ★** — source: 56_解析组合.md

**English.** Conjecture: The asymptotic density of the maximal saturated submatrices of a Hadamard matrix is 1/2 − Θ(n^{-1/2}); the second-order constant is explicit (from a tightening of the determinant inequality).

**中文。** 猜想：Hadamard 矩阵的最大饱和子矩阵的渐近密度为 1/2-\Theta(n^{-1/2});次首项的常数显式(由行列式不等式的紧化)。
