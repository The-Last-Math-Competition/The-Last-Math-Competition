**English.** Definition: The RH problem of the Kronecker quiver is Bridgeland's Riemann–Hilbert correspondence for DT invariants. Conjecture: Its Stokes constants are units of the quadratic field Q(√5) (the explicit unit-type value φ^{1/2}); the constants are computed from the principal-axis stability of the wall-crossing structure.

**中文。** 定义：Kronecker 箭图的 RH 问题指 Bridgeland 的 DT 不变量的 Riemann–Hilbert 对应。猜想：其 Stokes 常数为二次域 Q(√5) 的单位(单位型的显式值 φ^{1/2});常数由 wall-crossing 结构的主轴稳定性计算。
