**English.** Definition: Almost-invariant half-spaces. Conjecture: The almost invariance of half-spaces holds, the combination of the invariance being exponentially small deviations. (exponentially-small half-space deviations)

**中文。** 定义：几乎不变半空间：half-space。猜想：几乎不变的半空间：半空间的几乎不变且变的组合为指数小偏差。（几乎不变半空间指数小偏差半空间几乎不变组合半空间偏差指数几乎不变偏差）
