# 00000007228

**English.** Definition: Special points of Shimura varieties with equidistribution. Conjecture: The equidistribution of CM points holds, and the weight of the distribution is the volume of the weight lattice. (weight-lattice CM equidistribution)

**中文。** 定义：Shimura 簇的特殊点：等分布。猜想：特殊点的等分布：CM 点的等分布且布的权为权格的体积。（Shimura CM 权格体积特殊点等分布权）
