# 00000000256

**English.** Definition: The Birch–Swinneler-Dyer conjecture gives a precise identity among L(E,1), periods, Sha, and the torsion group. Conjecture: The BSD identity holds for every elliptic curve of conductor at most 100 (each instance can be verified numerically, but no general proof is known).

**中文。** 定义：Birch–Swinneler-Dyer 猜想给出 L(E,1)、周期、Sha、挠群的精确等式。猜想：BSD 等式对导子 ≤ 100 的全部椭圆曲线逐条成立(可逐条数值验证但无一般证明)。
