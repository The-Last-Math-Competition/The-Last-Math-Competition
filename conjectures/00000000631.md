# 00000000631

**KT-31 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The isotopy-class multiplicity of equal-volume hyperbolic knots. Conjecture: The number of isotopy classes decays exponentially in the volume; and the rate of decay is the sparsity of the volume spectrum, whose kernel is the gap law of the spectrum. (volume spectrum multiplicity exponential sparsity)

**中文。** 定义：等体积双曲纽结的同痕类重数。猜想：同痕类数目按体积增长指数衰减；且衰减的率为体积谱稀疏；且稀疏的核为体积谱的间隙律。（体积谱重数指数稀疏）
