# 00000009717

**SCA-17 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Modulation estimates near solitons: isolated eigenvalues of the linearized spectrum and the dynamics of modulation parameters. Conjecture: Modulation parameters satisfy ODE systems with exponential convergence, with convergence exponent the smallest-modulus nonzero eigenvalue of the linearized spectrum; when the spectral gap vanishes, critical modulation converges polynomially with exponent given by the resonance width. (modulation spectral gap convergence law)

**中文。** 定义：孤子附近的调制估计：线性化谱的孤立本征值与调制参数的动力学。猜想：调制参数满足指数收敛的常微分方程组且收敛指数为线性化谱的模最小非零特征值；谱隙消失时的临界调制为多项式收敛且指数由共振宽度给出。（调制谱隙收敛律）
