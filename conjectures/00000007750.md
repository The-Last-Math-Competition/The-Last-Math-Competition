# 00000007750

**FRV-30 ★** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The third level (path level) of the large deviation principle concerns the rate function I_3(mu,nu) for the joint empirical object of eigenvalues (measure mu) and eigenvectors (distribution nu) of Wigner ensembles. Conjecture: I_3 decomposes as I_3(mu,nu) = I_2(mu) + J(nu|mu), where J is a relative-entropy-type functional of Haar independence of eigenvectors; J = 0 for all nu only when the distribution matches the Gaussian ensemble; and the smallest positive value of J is given explicitly by (atom mass of mu)^2 log(atom ratio). (path-level LDP decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：大偏差原理的第三层（轨道层）：Wigner 系综的联合（特征值+特征向量）经验对象的速率函数 I_3(μ,ν)（μ 为谱测度、ν 为特征向量分布的联合）。猜想：I_3 分解为 I_3(μ,ν)=I_2(μ)+J(ν|μ)，其中 J 为特征向量 Haar 独立性的相对熵型泛函；当且仅当分布匹配高斯系综时 J=0 对一切 ν 成立；J 的最小正值由 (μ 的原子质量)²·log(原子比) 显式给出。
