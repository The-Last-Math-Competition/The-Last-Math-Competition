# 00000001736

**English.** Definition: S-integral points of hyperbolic curves. Conjecture: The count of q-integral points of P¹ ∖ {0,1,∞} is at most exp(O((log q)²)) with an explicit optimal constant (an optimal S-integral bound).

**中文。** 定义：双曲曲线的 S-整点。猜想：ℙ¹ ∖ {0,1,∞} 的 q-整点数 ≤ exp(O((log q)²)) 的最优常数显式(S-整点最优界)。
