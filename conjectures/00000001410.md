# 00000001410

**HT-20 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: The Bost conjecture is the surjectivity, under restricted topology, of the coarse assembly map with coefficients. Conjecture: The coarse-assembly surjectivity for hyperbolic groups is verified by an explicit constant in the controlled-compression inequality H^n(G) ≤ c·H^n_∞(G) on the Rips complex; the compression constant c depends only on the generating set.

**中文。** 定义：Bost 猜想指带系数的粗装配映射在受限拓扑下的满射性。猜想：双曲群的 coarse assembly 满射由 Rips 复形的受控挤压(coarse compression)不等式 H^n(G)≤c·H^n_∞(G) 的显式常数验证;挤压常数 c 只依赖于生成集。
