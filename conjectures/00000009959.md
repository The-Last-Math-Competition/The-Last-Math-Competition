# 00000009959

**MRG-19 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Equidistribution rates of periodic measures: convergence rates of diagonal flows along periodic orbits. Conjecture: The explicit rate of convergence of periodic-orbit measures to volume is the negative half power of the length times a correction of the periodic counting density; the correction term is given explicitly by the trace of the representation theory of the commensurability class of the geodesic. (periodic equidistribution explicit rate)

**中文。** 定义：周期测度的等分布率：对角流沿周期轨道的收敛速率。猜想：周期轨道测度对体积的收敛的显式率为长度的负半幂乘周期计数密度的修正；修正项由测地线的同痕类的表示论的迹显式给出。（周期等分布显式率）
