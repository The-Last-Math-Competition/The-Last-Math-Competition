**English.** Definition: Mahler's 3/2 problem studies the liminf of ‖(3/2)ⁿ‖. Conjecture: For every ε > 0, ‖(3/2)ⁿ‖ < n^{−1+ε} for infinitely many n; and the lower bound for the approximation frequency is a logarithmic density. (Mahler 3/2 dense approximation)

**中文。** 定义：Mahler 三分之二问题研究 ‖(3/2)ⁿ‖ 的下极限。猜想：对每个 ε>0 有 ‖(3/2)ⁿ‖<n^{−1+ε} 对无穷多 n 成立；且逼近频率的下界为对数密度。（Mahler 3/2 密集逼近）
