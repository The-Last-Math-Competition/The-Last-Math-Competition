# 00000004323

**AN4-23 ★** — source: 129_数论IV_代数数论显式方法.md

**English.** Definition: The Hilbert symbol is the explicit symbol of the quadratic-form pairing over local fields. Conjecture: The shortest formula for the quartic Hilbert symbol consists of exactly 4 explicit terms, and the lower bound on the number of terms is given by the entropy of the symbol table. (four-term shortest quartic Hilbert formula)

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert 符号指局部域二次型配对的显式符号。猜想：四次 Hilbert 符号的最短公式恰由 4 个显式项组成,项数下界由符号表的熵给出。（四次 Hilbert 四项最短公式）
