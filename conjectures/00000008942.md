**English.** Definition: The graph side of entropy: graph entropy. Conjecture: Graph entropy: the entropy of graph functions, with the characterization of the entropy vertex transitivity; and the entropy value realized by vertex-transitive graphs is exactly the fractional value of the optimal stable cut. (graph entropy vertex-transitive stable cut fraction)

**中文。** 定义：熵的图侧：图熵。猜想：图熵：图函数的熵且熵的刻画为顶点传递。(图熵顶点传递图函数熵刻画传递刻画)；且顶点传递实现的熵值恰为最优稳定割的分数值。
