# 00000004166

**English.** Definition: The joint singular series is the Euler product for the joint representation as a sum of two squares plus a prime. Conjecture: Averaged over the diameter it converges to an explicit Euler-product constant, with convergence rate the reciprocal of the logarithm of the diameter parameter. (mean convergence value of the joint singular series)
