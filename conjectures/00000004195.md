**GM3-15 ★** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: An AC layered solution is an entire solution whose interface consists of nested parallel surfaces. Conjecture: The minimal number of layers of a layered solution in a ball grows linearly with the diameter with the explicit slope 1/(pi epsilon), and the layer spacing is uniform in the interior. (layer-count slope of AC layered solutions)

**中文。** 定义：AC 分层解指界面为嵌套平行面的整解。猜想：球内分层解的最少层数随直径线性增长,斜率为 1/(πε) 型显式值且层间距在内部均匀。（AC 分层层数斜率）
