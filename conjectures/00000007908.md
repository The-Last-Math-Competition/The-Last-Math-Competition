# 00000007908

**ATC-08 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The Lokvenec-Gulesir conjecture restated as the density of special points on Hilbert modular varieties: the count N(H) (height at most H) of special (CM) points on a Hilbert modular surface X over a totally real field F. Conjecture: N(H) ~ c H^{1/2} (log H)^b with b a half-integral function of the class number of F; and the equidistribution measure of special points is never uniform with respect to the Bergman metric (CM points cluster at metric singularities), with explicit clustering constant the mean stable order one half times 1/zeta_F(2) of elliptic points. (Hilbert CM point clustering law)

**中文。** 定义：Lokvenec-Gulesir 猜想转述为 Hilbert 模 variety 的特殊点密度：全实域 F 上的 Hilbert 模曲面 X 的特殊点（CM 点）的计数 N(H)（高度 ≤H）。猜想：N(H) ~ c·H^{1/2}(log H)^{b}（b 为 F 的类数的半整函数）；且特殊点的等分布测度相对 Bergman 度量为绝不一致（CM 点聚集于测度奇点），聚集率的显式常数为椭圆点的稳定阶平均 1/2·1/ζ_F(2)。
