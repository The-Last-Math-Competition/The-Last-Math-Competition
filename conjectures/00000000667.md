# 00000000667

**KT-67 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The fraction p/q of a rational tangle (continued fraction). Conjecture: Tangles whose numerator p is prime correspond bijectively, under surgery, to knots with prime determinant (a bijection between primes and prime tangles).

**中文。** 定义：有理缠结的分数 p/q(连分数)。猜想：分子 p 为素数的缠结在手术下给出素行列式的纽结的对应为双射(素数与素缠结的一一对应)。
