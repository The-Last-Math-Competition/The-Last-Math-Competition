# 00000002308

**English.** Definition: A complete group satisfies G = Aut(G). Conjecture: The order of the smallest nontrivial solvable complete group is 2^{11}·3 (an explicit discovery); its structure is a minimal example of a semidirect product of a 2-group.

**中文。** 定义：完全群指 G=Aut(G) 的群。猜想：最小非显然可解完全群的阶为 2^{11}·3(显式发现);其结构为 2-群的半直积的极小例。
