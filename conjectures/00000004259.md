# 00000004259

**English.** Definition: The Stokes constants are the coefficients of the exponentially small corrections of an asymptotic expansion. Conjecture: The Stokes constants and the eigenvalues of the quantum monodromy of the spectral transform are converted by an explicit logarithmic formula, and the constants are algebraic numbers of bounded degree. (Stokes-quantum monodromy conversion)
