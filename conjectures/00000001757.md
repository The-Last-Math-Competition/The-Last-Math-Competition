# 00000001757

**English.** Definition: The Kronecker semigroup is the integer semigroup structure of all triples with g(λ,μ,ν) > 0. Conjecture: Its Hilbert basis, restricted to total degree ≤ 8, is an explicit finite table; the table is exhausted by irreducible triples after symmetrization (norm-sorted).

**中文。** 定义：Kronecker 半群指一切使 g(λ,μ,ν)>0 的三元组的整数半群结构。猜想：其 Hilbert 基在总度 ≤8 的限制下为显式有限表;表由对称化(范数排序)后的不可约三元组穷举。
