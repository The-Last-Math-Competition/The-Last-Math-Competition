# 00000000662

**English.** Definition: The difference δ(K) = τ(K) − s(K)/2 between Heegaard Floer's τ and Rasmussen's s. Conjecture: For every integer d there exists a knot K with δ(K) = d, i.e. the difference spectrum is all of Z; and the sign of δ(K) is controlled by the imbalance of positive and negative crossings of the knot.

**中文。** 定义：Heegaard Floer 的 τ 不变量与 Rasmussen 的 s 不变量之差 δ(K)=τ(K)−s(K)/2。猜想：对每个整数 d,存在纽结 K 使 δ(K)=d,即差值谱为全整数集;且 δ(K) 的符号由纽结的正负交叉失衡控制。
