# 00000002722

**SC-12 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Flag complexes: clique complexes completely determined by the graph. Conjecture: Nonnegativity of the Charney–Davis quantity Σ(−1)^i g_i of flag complexes; the combinatorial path to nonnegativity is the transverse monotonization of the perfect Chern class, and the quantity vanishes on flag manifolds. (Charney-Davis nonnegativity)

**中文。** 定义：旗复形（flag complex）：团复形由图完全决定。猜想：旗复形的 Charney–Davis 量 Σ(−1)^i g_i 的非负性；非负性的组合路径为完美国示性类的横截单调化且量在旗流形等式为零。（Charney-Davis 非负）
