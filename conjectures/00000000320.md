# 00000000320

**English.** Definition: The double-radical spectrum M(1,2,5) is the Markov spectrum of numbers whose partial quotients are restricted to {1,2,5}. Conjecture: This sub-spectrum is a Cantor set whose Hall ray begins before 4; and the exact starting point of the ray is the spectral radius of an explicit transition matrix of the three-symbol system. (restricted spectrum Hall ray shifted forward)

**中文。** 定义：双根号谱 M(1,2,5) 指部分商限制在 {1,2,5} 的 Markov 谱。猜想：该子谱为 Cantor 集且其 Hall 射线起点小于 4；且射线起点的精确值为该三符号系统的显式转移矩阵谱半径。（限制谱 Hall 射线前移）
