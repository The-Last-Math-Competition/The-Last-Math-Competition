# 00000009939

**English.** Definition: Persistence entropy: the Shannon functional of persistence bar length distributions. Conjecture: The persistence entropy satisfies concentration inequalities and is mutually bounded with the Shannon entropy of the longest bar: the entropy minus longest-bar contribution is bounded by half the logarithm of the bar count; the constant of the bound is tight. (persistence entropy concentration mutual bound)

**中文。** 定义：持久熵：持久条的长度分布的 Shannon 泛函。猜想：持久熵满足集中不等式且与最长条的 Shannon 熵互界：熵减最长条贡献界为条数对数的一半；界的常数为紧。（持久熵集中互界）
