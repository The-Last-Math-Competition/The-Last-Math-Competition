# 00000000207

**English.** Definition: The sum of reciprocals of the Recamán sequence. Conjecture: The reciprocal sum diverges if and only if the sequence is not superlinearly sparse; and the divergence is settled by density lower bounds. (Recaman reciprocal divergence via density lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Recamán 序列的倒数和。猜想：倒数和发散等价于序列不超线性稀疏；且发散的核为密度下界。（Recaman 倒数密度下界发散）
