**English.** Definition: A semiorthogonal decomposition is the stratified structure of a triangulated category. Conjecture: The criterion for extreme components is an explicit condition on the realified kernel of the Euler pairing, the real rank is zero if and only if the pairing degenerates, and the criterion is complete. (real-rank degeneracy criterion of semiorthogonal extremes)
