# 00000007288

**English.** Definition: Euler's pentagonal number theorem as a partition identity. Conjecture: The product-sum identity holds, and the combination of the identity is the Franklin involution. (Franklin pentagonal product-sum identity)

**中文。** 定义：Euler 五边形数定理：分拆恒等式。猜想：五边形的级数：乘积-和恒等式且式的组合为 Franklin 位移。（五边形 Franklin 位移乘积和恒等式级数组合定理）
