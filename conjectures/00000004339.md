**FP2-09 ★** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: The Cramer-Rao functional bound is the universal lower bound for the variance of unbiased estimators. Conjecture: The families of estimators saturating the bound are exactly the explicit orbits of exponential families, non-exponential families never saturate, and the deficit has an explicit lower bound via the curvature tensor. (C-R saturation exponential-orbit criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Cramér–Rao 泛函界指无偏估计方差的通用下界。猜想：界被饱和的估计族恰为指数族的显式轨道,非指数族永不饱和且亏损由曲率张量显式下界。（C-R 饱和指数轨道判据）
