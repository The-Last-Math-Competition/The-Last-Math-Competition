# 00000005244

**English.** Definition: Irreducibility is the connected indecomposability of the moduli space. Conjecture: The rank-r degree-d moduli space is always irreducible for genus at least 2; at genus 2 the exceptional decomposition has two irreducible components with dimensions differing by 1; and the intersection of the components is the Lange locus, with discriminant degree 2. (irreducibility criterion and exception by rank and degree)

**中文。** 定义：不可约性：模空间的连通无分解性。猜想：秩 r 度 d 模空间在亏格至少 2 时恒不可约；亏格 2 的例外分解为两个不可约分支且分支维数相差 1；分支的交为朗格轨迹且轨迹的判别度为 2。（秩公因子不可约判据例外）
