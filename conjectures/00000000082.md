# 00000000082

**English.** Definition: A prime-length closed geodesic is a closed orbit whose length is a prime number. Conjecture: On every closed negatively curved manifold there are infinitely many closed geodesics of prime length; moreover, the counting asymptotics agree with a prime-number-theorem type main term. (infinitude of prime closed geodesics)

**中文。** 定义：素数长度闭测地线指长度为素数的闭轨。猜想：任何闭负曲率流形上长度为素数的闭测地线有无穷多条；且计数渐近与素数定理型主项一致。（素数闭测地线无穷）
