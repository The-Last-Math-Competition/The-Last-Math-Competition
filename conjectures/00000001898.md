**ML-28 ★** — source: 48_逻辑与模型论II.md

**English.** Definition: Erdős–Rado polarized canonization is the decomposition property of two-color/canonical functions. Conjecture: The closed formula for the tight coloring number of two-dimensional canonization is an iterated power of κ (2^n cofinal powers); the formula closes over all regular κ, and the minimal closure is attainable.

**中文。** 定义：Erdős–Rado 极化 canonization 指二色/规范函数的分解性质。猜想：二维 canonization 的紧色数的闭包公式为 κ 的迭代幂(2^n 次共尾幂);公式在一切正则 κ 上封闭,极小闭包可达。
