# 00000004239

**English.** Definition: The tree expansion of the renormalization group is the perturbation expansion indexed by spanning trees. Conjecture: The combinatorial normalization of the expansion coefficients is the product of reciprocals of factorials of tree factors, and the normalization constant is given explicitly by the loop-free index. (factorial normalization of tree expansions)

**中文。** 定义：重整化群的树展开指以生成树索引的摄动展开。猜想：展开系数的组合归一化为树因子的阶乘倒数乘积,归一化常数由无圈指数显式给出。（树展开阶乘归一化）
