# 00000002389

**English.** Definition: The Vitushkin removability criterion is an analytic-continuation criterion. Conjecture: Removability for monogenic function systems is equivalent to vanishing of area density; and the constant of the equivalence is the analytic capacity. (monogenic removability area-density equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Vitushkin 可去性判据为解析延拓判据。猜想：单演函数系统的可去性等价于面积密度消没；且等价的常数为解析容量。（单演可去面积密度等价）
