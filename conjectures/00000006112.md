# 00000006112

**English.** Definition: Hypercontractivity is the exponent structure of higher-moment compression. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit region of exponent pairs with boundary the noise-parameter square law; the contraction of the region is self-similar with similarity ratio the noise parameter; and the interpolation exponent on the boundary is unique, given by Riesz-Thorin linearization. (square-boundary conversion of exponent-pair regions)

**中文。** 定义：超收缩：高阶矩压缩的时刻指标结构。猜想：换算常数为指标对的显式区域且边界为噪声参数平方律；区域的收缩自相似且相似比为噪声参数；边界上的插值指标为唯一且由 Riesz–Thorin 线性化给出。（指标对区域平方边界换算）
