**English.** Definition: The coset is the commutant of a vertex algebra over a subalgebra. Conjecture: The attainable values of the central-charge difference are twice the conformal-weight differences of the coset modules, and for rational central charges the attainable set is finite with the first non-attainable value explicit. (finite attainable set of coset differences)

**中文。** 定义：coset 指顶点代数与子代数的交换子。猜想：中心荷差的可达值为 coset 模共形权差的二倍,有理中心荷时可达集有限且首不可达值显式。（coset 差值有限可达集）
