# 00000004909

**English.** Definition: The belief MDP is the equivalent decision process on the belief space. Conjecture: For two states, the dimension of the closure of the reachable belief set is characterized explicitly by the intersection of the supports of the transition branches, the criterion is complete and continuous at the boundary, and the boundary case is exactly the merged chain of the cyclic classes of the transition matrix. (support criterion of the reachable belief set dimension)

**中文。** 定义：信念 MDP 指信念空间上的等价决策过程。猜想：两状态情形可达信念集闭包的维数由转移分支支撑交集显式刻画,判据完全且边界情形连续。(可达信念集维数支撑判据)；且边界情形恰为转移矩阵循环类的合并链。
