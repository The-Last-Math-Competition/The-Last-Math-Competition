**OIE-02 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative Khintchine inequality: norm control of Rademacher sums in Schatten classes (Lust-Piquard gave optimal constants). Conjecture: Improvement of the p = infinity endpoint of the noncommutative Khintchine inequality: the optimal exponent function of the modulus of uniform convexity is an explicit rational function of p; the optimal exponent is given by the embedding constants of two-subspace symmetric sums. (noncommutative Khintchine endpoint)

**中文。** 定义：非交换 Khintchine 不等式：Schatten 类中 Rademacher 和的范数控制（Lust-Piquard 给出最优常数）。猜想：非交换 Khintchine 的 p=∞ 端点改进：一致凸性模的最优指数函数为 p 的显式有理函数；最优指数由两子空间对称和的嵌入常数给出。（非交换 Khintchine 端点）
