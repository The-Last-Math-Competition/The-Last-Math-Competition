# 00000002890

**IX-30 △** — source: 81_微分算子的指标II.md

**English.** Definition: Rigidity of supersymmetric indices: the deformation invariance of the Witten index. Conjecture: Convexity of the Witten index: the index is invariant under convex deformations of the superpotential, with breaking points being the annihilation of zero-energy states; the annihilation criterion is the index sum of Morse critical points. (Witten index convex invariance)

**中文。** 定义：超对称指标的刚性：Witten 指标的形变不变性。猜想：Witten 指标的凸性：指标在超势的凸形变下不变且不变性的破坏点为零能态的湮灭；湮灭的判据为 Morse 临界点的指标和。（Witten 指标凸不变）
