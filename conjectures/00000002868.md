# 00000002868

**English.** Definition: Heat kernel expansions: the short-time asymptotics of traces of elliptic operators. Conjecture: Universality of heat coefficients a_j: a_j is a local invariant of the metric, vanishing on closed manifolds for odd j; the counterexamples to vanishing are manifolds with boundary, with corrections as boundary terms. (heat coefficient odd vanishing)

**中文。** 定义：热核展开：椭圆算子的迹的短时渐近。猜想：热系数 a_j 的普适性：a_j 为度量的局部不变量且 j 为奇数时在无边流形上消没；消没的反例为带边流形且反例修正为边界项。（热系数奇数消没）
