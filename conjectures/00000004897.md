# 00000004897

**English.** Definition: Goedel completeness is the equivalence of satisfiability and consistency. Conjecture: The completeness conversion for finite models is verified at small scale by explicit enumeration, with verification complexity double-exponential in the number of structures and the bound tight. (double-exponential verification bound of finite completeness)

**中文。** 定义：哥德尔完备性指可满足与一致的等价。猜想：有限模型的完备换算在小规模由显式枚举验证,验证复杂度为结构数的双指数且界紧。（有限完备性双指数验证界）
