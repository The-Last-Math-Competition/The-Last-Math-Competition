# 00000002608

**English.** Definition: The dimension of a poset (Dushnik–Miller): the minimal number of linear orders realizing P. Conjecture: Criticality of the standard examples S_n: dim(S_n) = n, and posets with S_n as minimal obstacle are exactly the standard-example family; the dimension of random posets concentrates at 2 with window of scale n^{-1/3}. (dimension standard example criticality)

**中文。** 定义：偏序集的维数（Dushnik–Miller）：实现 P 的线性序的最小个数。猜想：标准例子 S_n 的维数临界性：dim(S_n)=n 且以 S_n 为极小障碍的偏序恰为标准例子族；随机偏序的维数集中于 2 且集中窗口为 n 的负三分之一次幂尺度。（维数标准例子临界）
