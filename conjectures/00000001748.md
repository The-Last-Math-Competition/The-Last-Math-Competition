# 00000001748

**English.** Definition: An effective version of the small-points equidistribution theorem (Szpiro–Ullmo–Zhang type). Conjecture: The equidistribution rate of N small points is O(N^{-1/2}·√(log N)); optimality is explicit via Arakelov-geometric quantities of the period lattice, and in the semisimple case via approximation by the Faltings δ function.

**中文。** 定义：小点等分布(阿巴克尔–波特定理)的有效化。猜想：N 个小点的等分布率为 O(N^{-1/2}·√(log N));最优性由周期格的 Arakelov 几何量显式,半格情形可由 Faltings δ 函数逼近。
