# 00000004065

**BF2-05 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: The inclination quantity of a homoclinic orbit is the divergence rate of the stable and unstable manifolds along the orbit. Conjecture: The codimension-2 normal-form coefficient of an inclination flip is in an explicit linear relation with the Melnikov integral, and the saddle index of the periodic orbit produced by the flip is exactly 1/2. (inclination-flip coefficient and index)

**中文。** 定义：同宿轨道的倾斜量指稳定与不稳定流形沿轨道的发散率。猜想：倾斜量过零的余维 2 规范形系数与 Melnikov 积分为显式线性关系,翻转产生的周期轨道鞍点指数恰为 1/2。（倾斜翻转系数-指数）
