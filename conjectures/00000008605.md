# 00000008605

**English.** Definition: Minimax testing is the optimal discrimination between two classes of hypotheses. Conjecture: The condition for the detection gap rho_n is that rho_n^2 exceeds the U-statistic spectrum of the quadratic functional; and the separation threshold is controlled by the leading eigenvalue of the inner product kernel. (detection threshold spectral characterization)

**中文。** 定义：minimax 检验指两类假设的最优区分。猜想：检测间隙 ρ_n 的条件为 ρ_n² 超过二次泛函的 U-统计谱；分界阈值由内积核首特征值控制。（检测阈值谱刻画）
