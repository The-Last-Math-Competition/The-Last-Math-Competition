**English.** Definition: The local Langlands correspondence for GL₂(Q_{p²}). Conjecture: The correspondence for supercuspidal representations is always completed by rank-two irreducible étale (φ,Γ)-modules; and it is constructed via Breuil–Mézard type combinatorial formulas. (supercuspidal LLC completion)
