# 00000000657

**KT-57 ◆** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: An L-space knot is one all of whose sufficiently large Dehn surgeries are L-spaces. Conjecture: All L-space knots are torus knots or iterated torus knots; furthermore, the minimal crossing number of a non-torus L-space knot exceeds 21 (i.e. at least 22, if one exists).

**中文。** 定义：L-空间纽结指其一切足够大 Dehn 手术为 L-空间的纽结。猜想：L-空间纽结皆为 torus 结或 iterated torus 结;进一步,非 torus 型 L-空间纽结的最小交叉数 > 21(即若存在则交叉数至少 22)。
