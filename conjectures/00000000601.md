# 00000000601

**English.** Definition: V_K(t) is the Jones polynomial. Conjecture: The intersection {V_K(−1) : K prime knot} ∩ (the set of primes) is infinite (there are infinitely many knots with det K = |Δ_K(−1)| prime, and the subfamily with det K ≡ ±1 mod 8 is also infinite).

**中文。** 定义：V_K(t) 为 Jones 多项式。猜想：{V_K(−1) : K 素纽结} ∩ 素数集合无穷(det K = |Δ_K(−1)| 为素数的纽结无穷多,且同时 det K ≡ ±1 mod 8 的子族无穷)。
