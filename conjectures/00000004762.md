**FP3-12 ★** — source: 144_随机矩阵IV_自由概率.md

**English.** Definition: The block sizes of a noncrossing partition are the cardinalities of its blocks. Conjecture: The limiting shape of the joint distribution of normalized block sizes is a Poisson-Dirichlet type with explicit parameter one, realized by cycle counting. (PD limit of NC block sizes)

**中文。** 定义：非交叉分拆的块大小指各分块基数。猜想：归一化块大小联合分布的极限形状为显式参数的 Poisson–Dirichlet 型,参数为一且由循环计数实现。（NC 块大小 PD 极限）
