# 00000002200

**English.** Conjecture: The limit of the maximal-element distribution of multiple poset products (lexicographic sums) is Poisson(λ = 1); the explicit computation of λ follows from the independence of maximal elements of random posets.

**中文。** 猜想：多重偏序积(lexicographic sum)的极大元分布的极限为 Poisson(λ = 1);λ 的显式计算由随机偏序的极大元的独立性。
