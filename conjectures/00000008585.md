# 00000008585

**English.** Definition: Ergodic properties of the Berezin transform on Fock space. Conjecture: The Berezin transform converges to the identity as the scaling parameter tends to zero (a Gaussian convergence law); the convergence rate is controlled by the spectral gap of the operator (a spectral gap rate law); the Fock spectral gap constant is 1 (a Fock saturation law); and the spectral gap of weighted Fock spaces can degenerate (a weighted exception law), with critical weight exponent 1/2. (Berezin Fock spectral gap critical weight)

**中文。** 定义：Fock 空间上的 Berezin 变换的遍历性质。猜想：Berezin 变换在缩放参数趋于零时收敛到恒等（高斯收敛律）；且收敛速率由算子的谱隙控制（谱隙速率律）；Fock 情形的谱隙常数为 1（Fock 饱和律）；加权 Fock 空间的谱隙可退化（加权例外律），退化的临界权指数为 1/2。
