# 00000000978

**English.** Definition: The Kippenhahn curve (the algebraic boundary of the numerical range of a matrix). Conjecture: The number of real points of the Kippenhahn curve is bounded above by the combination n(n−1)/2 of eigenvalue multiplicities (real-point counting).

**中文。** 定义：Kippenhahn 曲线(矩阵数值域的代数边界)。猜想：Kippenhahn 曲线的实点数为特征值的重数的组合上界 n(n−1)/2(实点计数)。
