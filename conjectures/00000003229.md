**English.** Definition: Lyapunov exponents: the asymptotic rates of random products. Conjecture: The positivity domain of Lyapunov exponents: the exponent of quasiperiodic Schrödinger is a continuous function of the frequency, with the boundary of the positivity domain the subcritical circle. (Lyapunov positivity domain subcritical boundary)
