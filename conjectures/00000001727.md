# 00000001727

**English.** Definition: A semiabelian variety is the product of an abelian variety with G_m; the Bogomolov index is the infimum of the Néron–Tate height of its small points. Conjecture: The index is controlled by the high algebraicity constants of theta functions: the lower bound is c·exp(−c·h(A)), with c given by the Mumford gauge.

**中文。** 定义：半交换簇指交换簇与 𝔾_m 的乘积;Bogomolov 指数指其小点的 Néron–Tate 高的下确界。猜想：该指数由 theta 函数的高代数性常数控制:下界为 c·exp(−c·h(A)),c 由 Mumford 尺度给出。
