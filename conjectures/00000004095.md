# 00000004095

**CG4-05 ★** — source: 122_组合几何II_配置空间.md

**English.** Definition: The mixed Hodge structure is the graded structure of the Deligne weight filtration. Conjecture: For the homology of Conf_n(X) of a smooth variety X the weight is at most 2n - 2, the weight supremum is an explicit linear expression in n and the dimension, and it is attained for general X. (weight supremum formula of configuration MHS)

**中文。** 定义：混合 Hodge 结构指 Deligne 的权滤子分次结构。猜想：光滑簇 X 的 Conf_n(X) 同调权不超过 2n − 2,权上确界为 n 与维数的显式线性式,且对一般 X 达到。（配置 MHS 权上确界公式）
