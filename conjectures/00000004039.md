# 00000004039

**English.** Definition: A regular component is an AR-component isomorphic to the translation quiver ZA_infinity, its rank being the width of its tube core. Conjecture: The rank spectrum of the regular components of a representation-infinite quiver is the full spectrum from 1 to n, and the multiplicity of each rank is given explicitly by the defect delta(Q) of the quiver. (regular component rank spectrum and defect)

**中文。** 定义：正则分量指同构于平移箭图 ZA_∞ 的 AR-分量,秩为其管心宽度。猜想：表示无限箭图的正则分量秩谱为 1 到 n 的全谱,每个秩的重数由箭图亏数 δ(Q) 显式给出。（正则分量秩谱-亏数）
