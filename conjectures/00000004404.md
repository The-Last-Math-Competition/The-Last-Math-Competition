# 00000004404

**English.** Definition: Local-global convergence is neighborhood convergence strengthened by convergence of environment distributions. Conjecture: Its minimal characterizing operator is the third-order truncation of the environment Markov kernel, and the truncated characterization is complete and irreducible on Bernoulli-type graph limits. (third-order truncation characterization of local-global convergence)

**中文。** 定义：局部-全局收敛指邻域收敛加强环境分布的收敛。猜想：其极小刻画算子为环境马尔可夫核的三阶截断,截断刻画在伯努利型图限上完全且不可降阶。（局部-全局三阶截断刻画）
