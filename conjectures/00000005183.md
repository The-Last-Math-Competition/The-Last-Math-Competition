# 00000005183

**English.** Definition: The K-genus of a phantom is its Gauss homology. Conjecture: The genus formula of semiorthogonal components is the explicit linear expression of the total space minus the exceptional components, verified at reflexive varieties and closed. (linear subtraction formula of phantom genera)

**中文。** 定义：幻影的 K 亏格指其高斯同调。猜想：半正交分量的亏格公式为全空间减例外分量的显式线性式,自反簇处验证且封闭。（幻影亏格线性减法公式）
