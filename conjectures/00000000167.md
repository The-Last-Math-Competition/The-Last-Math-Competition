# 00000000167

**English.** Definition: S = Σ_{n≥1} n^{−n} is the sophomore's dream constant; the identity S = ∫₀¹ x^{−x} dx is known. Conjecture: The irrationality measure of S satisfies μ(S) ≤ 3; the bound is attained by an explicit sequence of rational approximations with denominators of the form n!^n, the approximation error exponent being exactly −(1+o(1))n log n. (sophomore measure upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：S=Σ_{n≥1} n^{−n} 为大学新生之梦常数，恒等式 S=∫₀¹ x^{−x}dx 已知。猜想：S 的无理性度量满足 μ(S) ≤ 3；上界由分母取 n!^n 型截断的显式有理逼近序列达到，且逼近误差的指数恰为 −(1+o(1))n log n。（Sophomore 度量上界）
