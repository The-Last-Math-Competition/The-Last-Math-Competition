# 00000002217

**English.** Definition: The D_{m,n} ring is the construction where polynomial values fall in mR plus nK. Conjecture: The smallest nontrivial table of the irreducible-element spectrum of D_{m,n} is always finite; and the table is indexed by partitions of coprime pairs (m, n). (D_mn irreducible spectrum coprime index)

**中文。** 定义：D_{m,n} 环指多项式值落入 mR 加 nK 的构造。猜想：D_{m,n} 的不可约元谱的最小非平凡表恒有限；且表由 m 与 n 的互素对的分拆索引。（D_mn 不可约谱互素索引）
