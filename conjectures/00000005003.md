# 00000005003

**English.** Definition: A coassociative submanifold is a four-dimensional calibrated manifold of G_2. Conjecture: Its deformation dimension is an explicit formula of the truncated dimension of self-dual two-forms, it simplifies when the normal bundle is trivial, and the moduli space is unconditionally smooth. (explicit coassociative deformation dimension formula)
