# 00000002610

**OL-20 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: The semantic spectrum of lattice identities: the classes of lattices satisfying an identity. Conjecture: Lattice identities admit a strict infinite refinement chain: every identity has a strictly stronger one; the construction of the chains unifies via the interval-doubling technique, and the minimal number of doublings is a linear bound in the complexity of the identity. (lattice identity infinite refinement)

**中文。** 定义：格恒等式的语义谱：满足恒等式的格类。猜想：格恒等式存在严格无穷细分链：每条恒等式有严格更强的恒等式；链的构造统一于区间加倍技巧且加倍的最小次数为恒等式复杂度的线性界。（格恒等式无穷细分）
