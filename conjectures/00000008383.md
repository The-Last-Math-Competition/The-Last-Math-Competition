# 00000008383

**English.** Definition: The two definitions of the motivic measure: the Denef-Loeser arc space motivic measure and the Clashof-Hrushovski type configuration space measure. Conjecture: The two definitions agree on local singularities of smooth varieties; the proof of agreement reduces to matching invariants under deformation to the normal cone; and the deviation terms concentrate on exceptional strata of discrete value parameters, whose motives are direct sums of Artin motives. (double measure matching)

**中文。** 定义：母题测度的两种定义指 Denef–Loeser 的弧空间母题测度与 Clashof–Hrushovski 型构形空间测度。猜想：两种定义在光滑簇的局部奇点上给出同值；同值证明可约化为形变召唤的不变量匹配；匹配的偏差项集中于离散值参数的例外层，例外层的母题为 Artin 母题的直和。（双测度同值匹配）
