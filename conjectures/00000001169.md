**English.** Definition: The Kashiwara crystal B(∞) is the combinatorial skeleton of the canonical basis. Conjecture: The character of B(∞) is an explicit generating function of pseudo-square counts; and the numerator of the generating function is the Euler product of the partition function. (B-infinity character formula)

**中文。** 定义：Kashiwara 晶体 B(∞) 为典范基的组合骨架。猜想：B(∞) 的特征为伪方格计数的显式生成函数；且生成的分子为分拆函数的 Euler 积。（B-无穷特征公式）
