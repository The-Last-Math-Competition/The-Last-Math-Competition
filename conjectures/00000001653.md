# 00000001653

**English.** Definition: The Witt group W(E) of an elliptic curve E is the cohomological Witt ring of quadratic forms over it. Conjecture: W(E) is an explicit extension of Z/2[Pic(E)] modulo the spherical-type relations: W(E) ≅ (Z/2)⁴ ⊕ Pic(E)[2] ⊕ Z (real type); the decomposition is realized explicitly by chains of squares of fundamental ideals.

**中文。** 定义：椭圆曲线 E 的 Witt 群 W(E) 指其上二次型的上同调 Witt 环。猜想：W(E) 为 Z/2[Pic(E)]/(球面型关系) 的显式扩张:W(E) ≅ (Z/2)⁴⊕Pic(E)[2]⊕Z(实型);分解由基本理想的平方链显式实现。
