# 00000005951

**English.** Definition: Delocalization is the uniform distribution of eigenvector mass. Conjecture: The maximal squared coordinate of uniform mass is logarithmic in the bulk, with power-type correction at the edge, and the kernel of the correction is the Airy window. (bulk-logarithmic Airy-window power-corrected coordinates)

**中文。** 定义：去局域化指特征向量质量均匀分布。猜想：一致质量的极大坐标平方和在体中为对数级且边缘为幂次修正型；且修正的核为 Airy 窗。（体对数级 Airy 窗幂修正坐标）
