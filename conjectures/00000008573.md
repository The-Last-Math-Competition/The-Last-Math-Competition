**NSP-13 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: sign limit: Szegő the- one limit theorem of infinite Toeplitz matrix and Widom spectrum formula. Conjecture: Toeplitz spectrum by bounded connected component constitutes (Widom formula); of this range complement set of sign and existence of spectral measure density by Szegő (log sign can and positive of) (Szegő); when is spectrum of multiplicity of Fisher–Hartwig singularity (F-H positive); singularity is integer (integer multiplicity).

**中文。** 定义：无穷 Toeplitz 矩阵的符号极限：Szegő 第一极限定理与 Widom 谱公式。猜想：Toeplitz 谱由符号的本性值域加补集的有界连通分量构成（Widom 公式律）；且谱测度密度的存在性由 Szegő 条件（log 符号可和）保证（Szegő 条件律）；条件破缺时的修正为 Fisher–Hartwig 奇点贡献（F-H 修正律）；奇点的重数的谱为半整数（半整数重数律）。
