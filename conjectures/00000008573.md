# 00000008573

**NSP-13 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The symbol limits of infinite Toeplitz matrices: the Szego first limit theorem and the Widom spectral formula. Conjecture: The Toeplitz spectrum consists of the essential range of the symbol plus the bounded connected components of its complement (the Widom formula law); the existence of the density of the spectral measure is guaranteed by the Szego condition (log summability of the symbol, a Szego condition law); the correction upon failure of the condition is the Fisher-Hartwig singularity contribution (an F-H correction law); and the multiplicity spectrum of singularities is half-integral (a half-integer multiplicity law). (Toeplitz Widom Fisher-Hartwig correction)

**中文。** 定义：无穷 Toeplitz 矩阵的符号极限：Szegő 第一极限定理与 Widom 谱公式。猜想：Toeplitz 谱由符号的本性值域加补集的有界连通分量构成（Widom 公式律）；且谱测度密度的存在性由 Szegő 条件（log 符号可和）保证（Szegő 条件律）；条件破缺时的修正为 Fisher–Hartwig 奇点贡献（F-H 修正律）；奇点的重数的谱为半整数（半整数重数律）。
