# 00000008124

**NAG-14 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Beilinson type spectral decomposition of noncommutative varieties: the spectral index spec_len(A) of full exceptional sequences (E_1,...,E_N). Conjecture: spec_len(noncommutative deformations of P^n) = n+1 (a length rigidity); the number of linear extensions of the partial order of the spectrum (the minimal exceptional generators) equals the Betti numbers of the Hochschild subalgebra (a poset-Betti correspondence); and the determinant of the mutual exceptional matrix (the Ext vanishing matrix) of the spectral decomposition is plus or minus 1 (a unit determinant rigidity), the exception to the determinant being the Clifford fixed point (a -1 Clifford exception law). (Beilinson length poset-Betti unit determinant)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的 Beilinson 型谱分解：完全例外序列 (E₁,…,E_N) 的谱指标 spec_len(A)。猜想：spec_len(P^n 的非交换形变) = n+1（长度刚性）；且例外的极小生成的谱的偏序（谱偏序）的线性扩展数 = Hochschild 子代数的 Betti 数（偏序-Betti 对应）；谱分解的互例外矩阵（Ext 消没矩阵）的行列式 = ±1（单位行列式刚性），行列式的例外为 Clifford 内点（-1 例外 Clifford 律）。
