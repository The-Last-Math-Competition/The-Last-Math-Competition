# 00000001454

**English.** Definition: The slicing problem asks whether a convex body of volume 1 contains sections of area ≥ c in some ℓ dimension (c independent of dimension). Conjecture: The slicing constant of a convex graph K = {(x, f(x))} obeys the explicit conversion ℓ₁(K) ≥ c·sup(λ_min D²f) with c = 1/2; the slicing bound for convex graphs is completely controlled by second-order information.

**中文。** 定义：切片问题问体积 1 的凸体是否含面积 ≥ c 的 ℓ 维截面(c 与维数无关)。猜想：凸图 K={(x,f(x))} 的切片常数与 f 的 Hessian 的各向异性常数满足显式换算 ℓ₁(K) ≥ c·sup(λ_min D²f),c=1/2;凸图情形的切片界由二阶信息完全控制。
