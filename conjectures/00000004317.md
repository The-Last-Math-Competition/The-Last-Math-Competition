**English.** Definition: Gorenstein's F-adjunction is the chain of ideal lifts constructing F-compatible assignments. Conjecture: The minimal size of F-generators is an explicit function of the binary digit count of the field degree, and minimality is attained by the family of quadratic fields. (minimal size of F-adjunction generators)
