**English.** Definition: The LDP of Gibbs measures: the deviation structure of equilibrium statistical mechanics. Conjecture: The phase-transition layer of Gibbs rates: for first-order transitions the double-well structure of the rate has well depth equal to the free-energy difference, and the minimal switching rate between wells is the explicit saddle-point value. (Gibbs double-well switching constant)

**中文。** 定义：Gibbs 测度的 LDP：平衡态统计力学的偏差结构。猜想：Gibbs 速率的相变层：一级相变的速率函数双井结构的井深为自由能差且井间最小切换轨道的速率常数为鞍点显式值。（Gibbs 双井切换常数）
