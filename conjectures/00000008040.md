**English.** Definition: Tamed first-order rigidity: the cardinality spectrum Sp(R_exp) of the classification space of the first-order theory of the structure R_exp. Conjecture: Sp is a single point (full categorical rigidity of the model-complete theory); the minimal cardinality for saturation of countable models of R_exp is aleph_1 (the infimum of compactness saturation); the count of isomorphism classes of nonsaturated countable models is 0 (the taming rigidity of unique countable saturation), and the failure of rigidity occurs only after expanding the language (adding Sin or Zilber powers), the minimal expanding function being Gamma_0 (the first layer of the power function tower). (R_exp categorical singleton spectrum rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：驯化的一阶刚性：结构 R_exp 的一阶理论的分类空间的基数谱 Sp(R_exp)。猜想：Sp 为单点（模型完备理论的全范畴刚性）；且 R_exp 的可数模型的饱和性的极小势 ℵ₁（紧致性饱和的下确界）；非饱和可数模型的同构类的计数为 0（唯一可数饱和的驯化刚性），刚性的破坏仅在扩张语言（添加 Sin 或 Zilber 幂）后发生，破坏的极小扩张函数为 Γ₀（幂函数塔的第一层）。
