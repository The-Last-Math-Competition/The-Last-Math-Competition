# 00000000240

**GE-09 ◆** — source: 15_图与极值参数.md

**English.** Definition: The Mahler volume of a symmetric convex body K is vol(K)·vol(K°) (K° the polar body). Conjecture (Mahler): vol(K)vol(K°) ≥ 4ⁿ/n!, with equality for symmetric bodies attained only by linear images of the cube or cross-polytope (known for n ≤ 3).

**中文。** 猜想：定义:对称凸体 K 的 Mahler 体积 vol(K)·vol(K°)(K° 为极体)。猜想(Mahler):vol(K)vol(K°) ≥ 4ⁿ/n!,对称体达到下界当且仅当立方体/交叉多面体的线性像(已知 n ≤ 3)。
