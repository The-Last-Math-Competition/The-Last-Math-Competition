# 00000007789

**English.** Definition: The quantitative log-concave CLT: the worst Kolmogorov distance d_n from one-dimensional projections of an isotropic log-concave X to the standard normal, over X and directions. Conjecture: d_n = O(n^{-1/2}), the same order as classical independent sums without log factors; and the extremal measure is the generalized simplex (exponential distribution on the simplex), with projecting directions concentrated near the central ray of the simplex, d_n being convex in the deviation from the central direction. (extremal log-concave CLT distance)

**中文。** 定义：log-concave 中心极限定理的定量形式：迷向 log-concave X 的一维投影 <X,θ> 的分布到标准正态的 Kolmogorov 距离 d_n 的最坏值（遍历 X 与 θ）。猜想：d_n = O(n^{-1/2})（与经典独立和同阶，无 log 因子）；且达到最坏 d_n 的极值测度为广义单纯形（指数分布在单纯形上），其投影方向集中在单纯形的中心射线附近，偏离中心方向的 d_n 依方向为凸函数。
