# 00000009646

**English.** Definition: The spike spectrum of sample covariance matrices XX^T/n: BBP transitions and clustering structures when the population has outlier eigenvalues. Conjecture: The limit positions of multiple spikes above the critical value satisfy an explicit algebraic equation; at criticality the spike fluctuations are asymmetric Tracy-Widom, cluster spacings are of the third-order scale O(n^{-1/3}), and the within-cluster statistics are the Airy point process. (spike clustering third-order statistics)

**中文。** 定义：样本协方差矩阵 XX^T/n 的尖钉（spike）谱：总体含离群特征值时的 BBP 相变与丛聚结构。猜想：多重尖钉在临界值上方的极限位置满足显式代数方程；临界处的尖钉波动为非对称 Tracy–Widom 且丛聚间距为 O(n^{−1/3}) 三阶尺度，丛聚内部统计为 Airy 点过程。（尖钉丛聚三阶统计）
