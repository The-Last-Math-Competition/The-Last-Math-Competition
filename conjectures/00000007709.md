**English.** Definition: The rep-tile order of a polygon is the value k for which the polygon dissects into k copies similar to itself. Conjecture: The order k of a convex rep-tile must be a perfect square or k=2; among convex quadrilaterals the class of rep-tile order exactly 4 consists of parallelograms and three exceptional families, whose parameter spaces are two-dimensional. (rep-tile orders of convex polygons)

**中文。** 定义：多边形的 rep-tile 阶指使多边形可剖分为 k 个与自身相似的拷贝的 k 值。猜想：凸 rep-tile 的阶 k 必为完全平方数或 k=2；凸四边形中 rep-tile 阶恰为 4 的类由平行四边形与三族例外四边形构成，例外族的参数空间维数为 2。
