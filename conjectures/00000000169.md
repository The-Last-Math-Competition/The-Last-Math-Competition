**English.** Definition: The Euler–Gompertz constant δ = ∫₀^∞ e^{−x}/(1+x) dx ≈ 0.5963. Conjecture: δ is irrational with irrationality exponent two; the unboundedness of its continued-fraction partial quotients can be verified via the Padé structure of the exponential integral. (Gompertz irrationality exponent two)
