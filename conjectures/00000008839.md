**MON-09 ★** — source: 280_单调算子与算子分裂.md

**English.** Definition: Yosida regularization of solution. Conjecture: error of Yosida approximate is always λ times monotone operator norm; and convergent in λ tends to zero when of approximate solution is one order. (Yosida one order error approximation)

**中文。** 定义：预解子的 Yosida 正则化。猜想：Yosida 近似的误差恒为 λ 乘单调算子范数；且近似解的收敛在 λ 趋于零时为一阶。（Yosida 一阶误差逼近）
