# 00000008839

**English.** Definition: Yosida regularization of resolvents. Conjecture: The Yosida approximation error is always lambda times the operator norm; and the convergence of approximate solutions is first order as lambda tends to zero. (Yosida first-order error approximation)

**中文。** 定义：预解子的 Yosida 正则化。猜想：Yosida 近似的误差恒为 λ 乘单调算子范数；且近似解的收敛在 λ 趋于零时为一阶。（Yosida 一阶误差逼近）
