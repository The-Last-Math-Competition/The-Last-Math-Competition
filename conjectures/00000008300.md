# 00000008300

**English.** Definition: The multiplicity uncertainty of Gromov-Floer intersections of Lagrangians: the local multiplicity mult_loc of isolated intersection points. Conjecture: mult_loc (the dimension of local Floer cohomology) is at least 1 (a multiplicity lower bound law); the minimal dimension for multiplicity greater than 1 (multiple intersections) is 2 for Pin arcs (a Pin arc example); the correction of multiplicities (nontransversal correction) is the Milnor number (a Milnor correction law); and the universality of the correction (all isolated-point multiplicities equal Milnor numbers) holds for analytic arcs (an analytic arc universality law). (Gromov-Floer Pin arc Milnor multiplicity)

**中文。** 定义：Lagrangian 相交的 Gromov–Floer 的重数的不确定性：孤立相交点的局部重数 mult_loc。猜想：mult_loc（局部 Floer 上同调的维数）≥ 1（重数下界律）；且重数 > 1（多重相交）的最小维数为 2 的 Pin 弧（Pin 弧例）；重的修正（非横截的修正）为 Milnor 数（Milnor 修正律）；修正的普适（一切孤点重数 = Milnor 数）在解析弧（解析弧普适律）下成立。
