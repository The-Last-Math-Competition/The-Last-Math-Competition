**SE-10 ★** — source: 93_随机环境的谱理论.md

**English.** Definition: Random Jacobi matrices: two-parameter disorder on and off the diagonal. Conjecture: The spectral dichotomy: off-diagonal disorder gives continuous-type spectra and diagonal disorder gives pure point spectra, with the boundary of the dichotomy the symmetry-breaking point. (Jacobi dichotomy symmetry-breaking boundary)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Jacobi 矩阵：双参数无序的对角与非对角。猜想：谱的两岐：非对角无序的谱的连续型且对角无序的纯点谱的两岐边界为对称破缺点。（Jacobi 两岐对称破缺边界）
