# 00000004347

**English.** Definition: The HWI inequality is the three-way inequality of entropy, Fisher information, and transport distance. Conjecture: The HWI constant of the infinite-dimensional Wiener measure is the explicit value 1/2, the constant is universal on abstract Wiener space and attained by the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup. (infinite-dimensional HWI constant 1/2)

**中文。** 定义：HWI 不等式指熵、费歇尔信息与传输距离的三方不等式。猜想：无穷维维纳测度的 HWI 常数为显式的 1/2,常数在抽象维纳空间普适且由 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 半群达到。（无穷维 HWI 常数 1/2）
