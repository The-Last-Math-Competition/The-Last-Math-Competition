# 00000001811

**English.** Definition: A flip is the operation in birational transformations that flips singular points; MMP termination means the flip chain is finite. Conjecture: The MMP terminates in dimension four; and the classification table of remaining four-dimensional terminal isolated singularities is completed by the Donaldson–Friedan index obstruction. (four-dimensional MMP termination and index obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：flip 指双有理变换中翻转奇异点的操作，MMP 终止指翻转链有限。猜想：MMP 在四维恒终止；且剩余四维终端孤立奇点由 Donaldson–Friedan 指数障碍补齐分类表。（四维 MMP 终止与指数障碍）
