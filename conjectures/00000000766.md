# 00000000766

**PA-66 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: A Breuil–Kisin module is the semilinear-algebra model of crystalline representations. Conjecture: The multiset of weights of a crystalline representation is completely recovered from the elementary divisors of the Kisin module; and the recovery algorithm runs in polynomial time. (completeness of weight recovery)

**中文。** 定义：Breuil–Kisin 模为结晶表示的半线性代数模型。猜想：结晶表示的权重多重集由 Kisin 模初等因子完全恢复；且恢复的算法为多项式时间。（权重恢复完全性）
