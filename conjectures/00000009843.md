**English.** Definition: Variable exponent spaces L^{p(.)}: the exponent-functionalized weighted theory. Conjecture: The complete criterion for boundedness of variable-exponent singular integrals is logarithmic Hoelder plus an integrability condition; necessity follows from exponent staircase constructions, and any missing condition makes the operator unbounded. (variable exponent complete criterion)

**中文。** 定义：变指标空间 L^{p(·)}：指数函数化的加权理论。猜想：变指标奇异积分有界的完全判据为对数 Hölder 加积分条件；判据的必要性由指数阶梯构造且任何缺失均导致算子无界。（变指标完全判据）
