# 00000000474

**English.** Definition: A random sorting network is a random sequence of adjacent transpositions reducing a uniform permutation of length n to the identity; the first-row trajectory is the time-position curve of the first strand. Conjecture: The expected number of inflection points of the trajectory is n/2 + O(n^{1/3}), and the empirical distribution of inflection positions on the normalized time axis converges to the product of the uniform distribution on [0,1] with the derivative of the limit shape.

**中文。** 定义：随机排序网络指对长 n 的均匀排列做相邻换位的随机序列,使其成为缩减词,首行轨迹指第一条线的时间-位置曲线。猜想：轨迹的拐点(斜率改变点)的个数的期望为 n/2+O(n^{1/3}),且拐点位置在归一化时间轴上的经验分布收敛到 [0,1] 均匀分布与极限形状导数的乘积密度。
