# 00000006409

**English.** Definition: Equimeasurable partitioning is the symmetric-difference characterization of two equimeasurable sets, by double integrals. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit difference of double integrals given by the hyperbolic integral of the distribution; the coefficient is one; and the difference is minimized if and only if the distributions coincide. (coefficient conversion of the double hyperbolic integral)

**中文。** 定义：等测分割：等测两集的对称差刻画（二重积分）。猜想：换算常数为二重积分的显式差且由分布的双曲积分给出；系数为一；差的极小化当且仅当分布相同。（二重双曲积分系数换算）
