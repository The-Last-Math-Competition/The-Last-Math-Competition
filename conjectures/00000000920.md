# 00000000920

**OA-20 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: Real rank zero means self-adjoint elements are approximable by invertibles. Conjecture: Real rank zero of an AH algebra is equivalent to density of the projection semigroup among self-adjoint elements; and the completion of the criterion is given by the projection cancellation theorem. (AH real rank zero projection density criterion)

**中文。** 定义：实秩零指自伴元可被可逆元逼近。猜想：AH 代数的实秩零等价于投影半格在自伴元中稠密；且判据的完全化由投影消没定理给出。（AH 实秩零投影稠密判据）
