# 00000007281

**English.** Definition: The Hardy-Ramanujan asymptotics of the partition function. Conjecture: The exact asymptotic of partitions is the main term plus series corrections, and the constant of the correction is the Bessel inversion. (Bessel-inverted exact partition asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：分拆函数的 Hardy–Ramanujan 渐近。猜想：分拆的精确渐近：主项加级数修正且正的常数为 Bessel 反演。（分拆 Bessel 反演主项级数修正精确渐近）
