# 00000009589

**English.** Definition: Yau's conjecture on the first eigenvalue of minimal hypersurfaces in spheres. Conjecture: lambda_1(M) = n for every closed minimal hypersurface of S^{n+1} (Choi-Wang proved lambda_1 at least n/2); and when the multiplicity is exactly n+1 the hypersurface M must be an equatorial sphere, while the surfaces with second eigenvalue lambda_2 = n+2 form a complete list of Clifford torus type. (spherical minimal first eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：Yau 关于球面极小超曲面第一特征值。猜想：S^{n+1} 的闭极小超曲面 λ_1 = n（Choi–Wang 已证 λ_1 ≥ n/2）。猜想：λ_1(M) = n 对一切闭极小超曲面成立；且重数恰为 n+1 时 M 必为赤道球面，第二小特征值 λ_2 = n+2 的曲面为 Clifford 环面型完全列表。（球面极小第一特征值）
