# 00000001836

**English.** Definition: Betti-number stability of relative motives is the motive-decomposition structure of projective bundles. Conjecture: The polynomial-degree count of the motive decomposition of the P-infinity bundle is an explicit binomial; and the count corresponds one-to-one with the Künneth components. (motive decomposition binomial count)
