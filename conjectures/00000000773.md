# 00000000773

**English.** Definition: The Galois spectrum is the spectrum of the adjoint action of the maximal p-extension G(p) of Q_p on its normal subgroups. Conjecture: The spectrum is pure point, its eigenvalues are all (p−1)-st roots of unity with finite-dimensional eigenspaces; and rigidity of the spectrum is equivalent to the Burnside group structure theorem for G(p).

**中文。** 定义：Q_p 的最大 p-扩张 G(p) 在其正规子群上的伴随作用给出的谱称为 Galois 谱。猜想：该谱为纯点谱,本征值为 p-1 次单位根的全体,且每个本征空间有限维;谱的刚性等价于 G(p) 的 Burnside 群结构定理。
