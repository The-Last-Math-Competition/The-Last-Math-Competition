**English.** Definition: h-vectors and g-vectors: transformation coefficients of the f-polynomial. Conjecture: The extension of g-nonnegativity (the g-conjecture, proven for combinatorial polytopes) to simplicial complexes: the g-vectors of flag complexes are nonnegative, with the combinatorial proof given by minimal free resolutions of graded algebras generated by ideals. (flag complex g-nonnegativity)
