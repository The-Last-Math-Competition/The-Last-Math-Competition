**LD-05 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: Schilder's theorem: the LDP on the path space of Brownian motion. Conjecture: Cameron–Martin rigidity of the Schilder rate: the rate function vanishes if and only if the path lies in the Cameron–Martin unit ball, and the spectral distribution of the rate on the sphere is an explicit chi-square-type law. (Schilder rate spectral distribution)

**中文。** 定义：Schilder 定理：Brown 运动的路径空间 LDP。猜想：Schilder 速率的 Cameron–Martin 刚性：速率函数为零当且仅当路径为 Cameron–Martin 单位球内且球面的速率谱分布为卡方型显式律。（Schilder 速率谱分布）
