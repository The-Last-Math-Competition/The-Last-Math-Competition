# 00000007344

**IL3-14 ★** — source: 230_无穷小逻辑.md

**English.** Definition: Nonstandard topology with monad structure. Conjecture: The monad of a point is the infinitesimal neighborhood, and the covering of the neighborhoods is by internal covers. (internal-cover monad characterization)

**中文。** 定义：非标准拓扑：单子结构。猜想：单子的刻画：点的单子为无穷小邻域且域的覆盖为内部覆盖。（单子内部覆盖无穷小邻域点刻画非标准拓扑）
