**PHT-20 ★** — source: 316_持久同调II_统计与稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: Persistence diagrams of random neural networks: the topological spectrum of deep networks. Conjecture: The expected spectrum of persistence diagrams of activation complexes of random neural networks converges to a universal limit depending only on the reciprocal of the width; the count of long bars of the spectrum decays exponentially in depth, with decay rate the logarithm of the spectral radius of the weights. (random network persistence spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：随机神经网络的持久图：深度网络的拓扑谱。猜想：随机神经网络的激活复形的持久图的期望谱收敛到普适极限且极限仅依赖宽度的倒数；谱的长条的个数按深度的指数衰减且衰减率为权重谱半径的对数。（随机网络持久谱）
