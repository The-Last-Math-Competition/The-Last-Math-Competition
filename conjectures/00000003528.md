# 00000003528

**OC3-08 ★** — source: 103_序数与组合数论III.md

**English.** Definition: The ordinal side of combinatorial number theory: Ramsey ordinals. Conjecture: Large Ramsey ordinals: the separation chain of indescribable ordinals and Ramsey ordinals, with the strictness of the chain the Erdős–Rado lower bound. (large Ramsey Erdos-Rado separation chain)

**中文。** 定义：组合数论的序数侧：Ramsey 序数。猜想：大 Ramsey 序数：不可描述序数与 Ramsey 序数的分离链且链的严格性为 Erdős–Rado 的下界。（大 Ramsey Erdos-Rado 分离链）
