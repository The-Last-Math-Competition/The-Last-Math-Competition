# 00000002978

**English.** Definition: The real-rootedness of adjacency-type polynomials: the matching polynomial of random graphs. Conjecture: Real-root universality of the matching polynomial: the empirical distribution of the roots of the matching polynomial of G(n,p) converges to a semicircle-type corrected law, the correction being the Laplace correction of path counting. (matching roots semicircle correction)

**中文。** 定义：邻接多项式的实根化：随机图的匹配多项式。猜想：匹配多项式的实根普适：G(n,p) 的匹配多项式的根的经验分布收敛到半圆型修正律且修正为路径计数的 Laplace 修正。（匹配根半圆修正）
