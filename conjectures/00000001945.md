# 00000001945

**English.** Definition: The spectral gap is the uniform spectral lower bound of the nontrivial part of the regular representation. Conjecture: The minimum of the spectral gap for SL₃(R) lattice actions is attained by uniform lattices (i.e. arithmetic lattices are extremal for the spectral gap); the extremal value is a generalization of Selberg's constant.

**中文。** 定义：谱隙指正则表示中不平凡部分的均匀谱下界。猜想：SL₃(R) 格作用的谱隙的最小值由均匀格取得(即算术格为谱隙极值);极值为 Selberg 常数的推广。
