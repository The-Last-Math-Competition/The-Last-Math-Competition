**FA2-01 ★** — source: 123_高等数理逻辑_强制法与公理.md

**English.** Definition: The finite-support iteration is the chain-condition-preserving construction taking finite supports level by level. Conjecture: In an iteration of length theta the worst antichain size is an explicit product formula in the supremum of the level sizes, and it is attained by iterations of Suslin trees. (worst-case antichain growth formula of iterations)

**中文。** 定义：有限支撑迭代指逐层取有限支撑的链条件保持构造。猜想：长度 θ 的迭代中最坏反链尺寸为各层尺寸的上确界的显式乘积式,且由 Suslin 树迭代达到。（迭代反链最坏增长公式）
