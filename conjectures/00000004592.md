**English.** Definition: Scattering resonances are the spectral resonance poles of noncompact hyperbolic manifolds. Conjecture: The main constant of their counting law is an explicit combination of the volume and the boundary area, and the constant is exact and continuous under convex-cocompact deformations. (volume-area constant of resonance counting)

**中文。** 定义：散射共振指非紧双曲流形的谱共振极点。猜想：其计数律的主常数为体积与边界面积的显式组合,常数精确且对凸余紧形变连续。（共振计数体积面积常数）
