**English.** Definition: The singular set is the degeneracy set of a minimal flow. Conjecture: The Hausdorff dimension upper bound of the singular set of a seven-dimensional minimal hypersurface is five, and the bound is attained by cone-direct-sum families and cannot be lowered. (codimension-two upper bound of seven-dimensional singular sets)
