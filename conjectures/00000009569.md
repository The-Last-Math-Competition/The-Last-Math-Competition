# 00000009569

**English.** Definition: The systole of modular surfaces under the WP metric (twice the shortest separating geodesic length) has a maximum that decays asymptotically at genus g. Conjecture: The maximum of the WP systole is asymptotically c/sqrt g, with the constant c given exactly by the self-intersection length of a single separating curve; the surface attaining the maximum is unique up to isometry and is a Fireburn-type symmetric surface. (WP systole asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：模曲面在 WP 度量下的 systole（最短分离测地线长的两倍）的最大值在亏格 g 时渐近衰减。猜想：WP systole 的最大值渐近为 c/√g 且常数 c 由单个分隔曲线的自相交长度精确给出；达到最大值的曲面模等距类唯一且为 Fireburn 型对称曲面。（WP systole 渐近）
