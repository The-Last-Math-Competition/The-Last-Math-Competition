# 00000002000

**English.** Definition: The vortex-pair annihilation rate of GP is the speed at which vortices disappear during the evolution. Conjecture: The annihilation rate is t^{-1/2}; and the rate constant is the one-fourth power of the vortex-core energy. (vortex annihilation core-energy rate law)

**中文。** 定义：GP 的涡旋对湮灭率指涡旋在演化中的消失速度。猜想：湮灭率为 t 的负二分之一次幂；且速率常数为涡旋核能量的四分之一次方。（涡旋湮灭核能量速率律）
