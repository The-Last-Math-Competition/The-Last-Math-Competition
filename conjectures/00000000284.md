**GC-11 ★** — source: 17_几何与动力学常数.md

**English.** Definition: The Fransén–Robinson constant F = ∫₀^∞ dx/Γ(x) ≈ 2.8078 is the integral of the reciprocal Gamma function over the positive real line. Conjecture: F is transcendental and algebraically independent of e and π; the independence is equivalent to algebraic independence of the values at algebraic points of the Mellin transform of the reciprocal Gamma function, and the exponential decay of the integral guarantees an effective irrationality measure. (Fransen-Robinson transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：Fransén–Robinson 常数 F=∫₀^∞ dx/Γ(x)≈2.8078 为 Gamma 倒函数的全直线积分。猜想：F 是超越数且与 e、π 代数无关；无关性等价于 Gamma 倒函数的 Mellin 变换在代数点取值的代数无关性，积分的指数衰减保证无理性度量有效。（Fransen-Robinson 超越）
