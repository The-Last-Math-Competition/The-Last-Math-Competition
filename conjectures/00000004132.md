# 00000004132

**English.** Definition: PFA is the proper forcing axiom, and chain reflection is the reflection of stationary sets at smaller cardinals. Conjecture: The spectrum of chain-reflection lengths implied by PFA is exactly all regular cardinals up to aleph_2, and the spectrum does not enlarge further under Martin maximum. (completeness of the PFA chain-reflection spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：PFA 指 proper 力迫公理,链反射指平稳集在更小基数的反射。猜想：PFA 蕴含的链反射长度谱恰为不超过 ℵ₂ 的全部正则基数,谱在 Martin 极大下不再扩大。（PFA 链反射谱完全性）
