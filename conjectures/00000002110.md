**English.** Definition: Counting tight contact structures on hyperbolic complement manifolds. Conjecture: The count equals a certain coefficient of a Khovanov-type invariant of an embedded link (with an explicit normalization for the coefficient); and the categorified identity of the count is controlled by the homological spectrum of the contact invariant.
