# 00000005936

**English.** Definition: The stationary-type distribution is the limit law of steady-state initial data. Conjecture: At parameter zero it degenerates to Gaussian, with endpoints of classical-ensemble type, and the kernel of the degeneration is the Brownian bridge. (Brownian-bridge Gaussian degeneration at parameter zero)
