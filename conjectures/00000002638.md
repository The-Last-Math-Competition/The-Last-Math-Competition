# 00000002638

**English.** Definition: Zagier's traces of singular moduli: the trace function of values of j(τ) at CM points. Conjecture: The Jacobization of trace functions: the horizontal lift of singular-moduli traces is an explicit finite combination of Jacobi forms with product-of-class-numbers coefficients; the weight of the lift is the quadratic character of the CM discriminant. (singular moduli trace Jacobization)

**中文。** 定义：Zagier 的奇异模轨迹：j(τ) 在 CM 点的值的轨迹函数。猜想：轨迹函数的 Jacobi 化：奇异模轨迹的水平提升为 Jacobi 形式的显式有限组合且组合系数为类数的乘积；提升的权为 CM 点判别式的二次特征。（奇异模轨迹 Jacobi 化）
