# 00000004373

**English.** Definition: The LQ-induced map to TC is the Lichtenbaum-Quillen type canonical comparison map. Conjecture: The lowest nonzero homotopy of its fiber spectrum lies in degree 0, the group is an explicit quotient of F_p, and the quotient is given by the Brauer class of etale cohomology. (Brauer quotient of the LQ fiber leading term)

**中文。** 定义：LQ 诱导向 TC 的映射指 Lichtenbaum–Quillen 型的典范比较映射。猜想：其纤维谱的最低非零同伦在度 0,群为 𝔽_p 的显式商,商由 étale 上同调的 Brauer 类给出。（LQ 纤维首项 Brauer 商）
