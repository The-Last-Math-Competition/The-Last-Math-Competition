# 00000000536

**English.** Definition: A unipotent peak is a Frobenius monodromy block of local cohomology. Conjecture: The size of a monodromy block is bounded by an explicit polynomial in the Crystalline rank; and the exceptions to geometric monodromy rigidity are hypersurface singularities. (monodromy block polynomial bound)

**中文。** 定义：unipotent 峰为局部上同调的 Frobenius 单值块。猜想：单值块大小不超过 Crystalline 秩的显式多项式界；且几何单值刚性的例外为超曲面奇点。（单值块多项式界）
