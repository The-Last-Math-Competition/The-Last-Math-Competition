# 00000006226

**English.** Definition: Infinitesimal categories are the object structures of synthetic microgeometry. Conjecture: The sheaf spectrum of nilpotent ideals is indexed by the nilpotence order of coordinates and the spectrum is finite, the bound of finiteness being the element count. (finite element-count bound of nilpotent-ideal spectra)
