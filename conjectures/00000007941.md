**English.** Definition: The spectral gap function of Whitham averaging: the dynamics of the Whitham slow moduli of the spectral gaps [E_{2j-1}, E_{2j}] of quasiperiodic KdV solutions. Conjecture: The Riemann invariants of the Whitham equations are the E_i; and the entropy production of the averaging map (fast to slow), the sum of log(E_{2j} - E_{2j-1}), is a strictly increasing convex function; in the high-genus limit (infinitely many gaps) the thermodynamic functional converges to the Yang-Yang functional of sine-Gordon, whose minimum is given by a statistical-mechanical Bethe ratio (the complete form of the free energy density). (Whitham Yang-Yang thermodynamic limit)

**中文。** 定义：Whitham 平均化的谱隙函数：准周期 KdV 解的谱隙 [E_{2j-1},E_{2j}] 的 Whitham 慢变 moduli 的动力学。猜想：Whitham 方程的 Riemann 不变量为 E_i；且平均化映射（快变到慢变）的熵产量 Σ log(E_{2j}-E_{2j-1}) 为严格递增的凸函数；其在高极限（无限隙）下的热力学泛函收敛到 sine-Gordon 的 Yang–Yang 泛函，其极小值由统计力学式的 Bethe 比式给出（自由能密度的完全形式）。
