# 00000002761

**English.** Definition: Central polynomials of free algebras: multivariate expressions that are not identities but take values in the center. Conjecture: The minimal degree of central polynomials: the minimum for M_n is n² (Formanek–Razmyslov), the moduli space of polynomials attaining it is irreducible; higher central polynomials are generated by symmetrization of the Cayley–Hamilton-type kernel. (central polynomial minimal degree)

**中文。** 定义：自由代数的中心多项式：非恒等但求值落在中心的多元式。猜想：中心多项式的最小次数：M_n 的中心多项式的最小次数为 n²（Formanek–Razmyslov）且达到次数的多项式的模空间不可约；高次中心多项式的生成为 Cayley–Hamilton 型核的对称化。（中心多项式最小次数）
