# 00000009960

**English.** Definition: Spectral rigidity and commensurability: the extent to which the Laplace spectrum of negatively curved quotients determines the commensurability class. Conjecture: Isospectrality of negatively curved quotients implies commensurability if and only if the dimension is at least three and the volume spectrum of the quotient is multiplicity-free; the two-dimensional exception is an explicit counting family of Sunada pairs that are isospectral but incommensurable. (isospectral-commensurable threshold dimension)

**中文。** 定义：谱刚性与同痕：负曲率商的 Laplace 谱决定同痕类的范围。猜想：负曲率商的同谱蕴含同痕当且仅当维数至少为三且商的体积谱无重数；二维例外为同谱不同痕的 Sunada 对的显式计数族。（同谱-同痕阈值维数）
