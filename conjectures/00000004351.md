# 00000004351

**English.** Definition: The phi^4_2 model is the two-dimensional Euclidean field with quartic self-interaction. Conjecture: The convergence threshold for the construction of its Schwinger functions is an explicit combination of the ratio of the coupling to the mass squared, and the threshold tends to zero, scaled by that ratio, as the mass tends to zero. (mass scaling of the phi^4_2 convergence threshold)

**中文。** 定义：φ⁴₂ 模型指二维四次自作用欧氏场。猜想：其 Schwinger 函数构造的收敛阈值为耦合与质量平方比的显式组合,阈值在质量趋于零时按该比例标度趋于零。（φ⁴₂ 收敛阈值质量标度）
