# 00000008354

**English.** Definition: Mixed subdivisions of tropical hypersurfaces and completely nontransversal pairs: the triangulation count mixed_trig of mixed cell subdivisions (subdivisions of Cayley embeddings). Conjecture: mixed_trig (the count of mixed subdivisions) equals the count of flatness degenerations of toric invariants (a flat count law); the coarsest triangulation (minimal vertices of coarse subdivisions) is the GKZ A-discriminant (a GKZ coarse subdivision law); the minimal length of chains of refinements (coarse to fine) is linear in d (a linear chain law); and the maximal chain (finest) is counted by a constant h-polynomial (an h-chain counting law). (mixed subdivision GKZ flat linear chain)

**中文。** 定义：热带超曲面的混合细分与完全非交配对：混合胞腔的剖分（Cayley 嵌入的细分）的三角化的计数 mixed_trig。猜想：mixed_trig（混合剖分的计数）= 环面不变量的Flatness（平坦退化）的计数（平坦计数律）；且三角化的最粗（粗剖分的最小顶点）为 GKZ 的 A-判别式（GKZ 粗剖律）；剖分的连续（粗到细的加细的链）的长度的极小为 d 的线性（线性链律）；链的极大（最细）的计数为常数的 h-多项式（h-链计数律）。
