**English.** Definition: The central term of the gap distribution mu of the sine kernel process: exact representations and transcendence of crossing probabilities. Conjecture: The Fourier transform of the sine process gap distribution is an explicit combination of hypergeometric functions and Painleve III; the central gap probability is transcendental and algebraically independent of any polynomial relation between pi and e. (central gap transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：sine 核过程的间隙分布 μ 的中心项：跨点概率的精确表示与超越性。猜想：sine 过程间隙分布的傅里叶变换为超几何与 Painlevé 第三型的显式组合；中心间隙概率为超越数且代数无关于 π 与 e 的任何多项式关系。（中心间隙超越性）
