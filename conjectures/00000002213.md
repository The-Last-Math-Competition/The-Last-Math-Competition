**English.** Definition: The delta set Δ(H) is the set of factorization-length differences of a numerical semigroup. Conjecture: The delta set is eventually periodic; and the period divides the Apéry-set length of the smallest generator. (delta period Apéry divisibility)

**中文。** 定义：delta 集 Δ(H) 指数值半群因子化长度差的集合。猜想：delta 集最终周期；且周期整除最小生成元的 Apéry 集长度。（delta 周期 Apéry 整除）
