**English.** Definition: The free Stein equation is the free analogue of the classical Stein equation. Conjecture: The regularity criterion for its solutions is an explicit Sobolev exponent threshold of the density, the threshold is attained at the semicircle law, and the criterion is complete. (Sobolev threshold of free Stein solutions)

**中文。** 定义：自由 Stein 方程指经典 Stein 方程的自由模拟。猜想：其解的正则性判据为密度的 Sobolev 指数显式阈值,阈值在半圆律达到且判据完全。（Stein 解 Sobolev 阈值）
