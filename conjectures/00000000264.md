# 00000000264

**AA-11 ◆** — source: 16_代数与算术对象.md

**English.** Definition: A Büchi sequence is a sequence of integer squares (x_n) whose second difference is identically 2 (x_{n+2} − 2x_{n+1} + x_n = 2 for all n). Büchi's problem: there exists M such that every nontrivial Büchi sequence of length M is of the "trivial" form (n + k)². Conjecture: M = 5 suffices, i.e. no nontrivial Büchi sequence of length 5 exists; its negation is equivalent to the negation of decidability of Diophantine equations over Z.

**中文。** 定义：Büchi 序列:整数的平方列 (x_n) 满足二阶差分恒为常数 2(即 x_{n+2} − 2x_{n+1} + x_n = 2 对所有 n)。Büchi 问题:存在 M 使每个长为 M 的非平凡 Büchi 序列都是"平凡"形 (n + k)²。猜想：M = 5 即可,即长为 5 的非平凡 Büchi 序列不存在;其否定等价于 ℤ 上丢番图可判定性的否定。
