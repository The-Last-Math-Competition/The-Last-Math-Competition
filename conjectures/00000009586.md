# 00000009586

**MSH-06 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The Almgren-Pitts width sequence W_k satisfies the Liokumovich-Marques-Neves Weyl asymptotics W_k ~ c k^{1/(n+1)}; the joint spectrum of widths and realizing-surface indices is unknown. Conjecture: W_k/a_k tends to 1 and the index of the k-th width's realizing surface is at most k; and the two-sided control index(a_k) at least k - C k^{1/2} holds for general metrics, with exceptions given by Clifford-type high-symmetry metrics. (width-index two-sided control)

**中文。** 定义：Almgren–Pitts 宽度序列 W_k 满足 Liokumovich–Marques–Neves 的 Weyl 渐近 W_k ~ c·k^{1/(n+1)}；宽度与实现曲面指标的联合谱未知。猜想：W_k/a_k → 1 且第 k 宽度的实现曲面指标 ≤ k；两谱的双侧控制 index(a_k) ≥ k − C k^{1/2} 在一般度量成立，例外由 Clifford 型高对称度量给出。（宽度-指标双侧控制）
