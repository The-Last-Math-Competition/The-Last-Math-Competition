# 00000008064

**PRF-14 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The algorithmic information theory of proof retrieval: the ratio rho(T) = min{|pi|}/K(phi) of shortest proof length to statement complexity (pi a proof of phi in T, K the Kolmogorov complexity). Conjecture: rho(T) is an invariant of T with supremum 1 (a strengthening of Chaitin-type completeness: almost all true statements have proof length at least K(phi)); the inverse of the infimum of rho (the density of the most easily provable statements) is the inverse of the tower function F_{|T|} (a sparsity law for extreme statements); and the consistency of the ratio across ZFC and ZFC plus large cardinals (absoluteness of the invariance) is compensated by the density of reflection schemata in the Levy hierarchy. (Chaitin proof-ratio absoluteness)

**中文。** 定义：证明检索的算法信息论：最短证明长度与语句复杂度的比率 ρ(T)=min{|π|}/K(φ)（π 为 T 中 φ 的证明，K 为 Kolmogorov 复杂度）。猜想：ρ(T) 为 T 的不变量且其上确界为 1（Chaitin 型完备定理的加强：几乎所有真语句的证明长度 ≥K(φ)）；且 ρ 的下确界（最易证语句的密度）的倒数为塔函数 F_{|T|} 的反函数（极端语句的稀疏律）；该比率对 ZFC 与 ZFC+大基数的一致性（不变性的绝对性）由 Levy 层级的反射模式的密度补偿。
