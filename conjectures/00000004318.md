**English.** Definition: The average rank is the mean of the ranks of a family of elliptic curves. Conjecture: The average rank of the full family is exactly 1/2, and any further descent requires an explicit correction from the distribution of 3-torsion, with leading correction term 1/12. (average rank 1/2 and leading correction)

**中文。** 定义：均值秩指椭圆曲线族秩的平均值。猜想：全族均值秩恰为 1/2,任何进一步下降需要 3 挠分布的显式修正,修正项首值为 1/12。（均值秩 1/2 与修正首值）
