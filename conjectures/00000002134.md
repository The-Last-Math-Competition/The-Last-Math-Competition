# 00000002134

**English.** Definition: The unimodal structure of the coefficients of the q-Catalan polynomial. Conjecture: The unimodal transition point is at the golden-ratio position of the index; and the transition window width is constant. (q-Catalan unimodal golden position)

**中文。** 定义：q-Catalan 的系数单峰结构。猜想：单峰转移点为指标的黄金分割位；且转移的窗宽为常数。（q-Catalan 单峰黄金位）
