# 00000004243

**English.** Definition: The Seiberg-Witten equations are the coupled equations of a spinor and a self-dual form, and their heat flow is a smoothing flow. Conjecture: The convergence rate of the flow is of spectral-gap exponential type, with the gap constant an explicit combination of the Euler characteristic and the signature, and it is positive if and only if b^+ is at least 2. (SW heat flow spectral gap constant)

**中文。** 定义：Seiberg–Witten 方程指旋量与自对偶形式的耦合方程,其热流为光滑化流。猜想：流的收敛速率为谱隙型指数,谱隙常数由欧拉示性与符号差的显式组合给出,且为正当且仅当 bc⁺ 至少为 2。（SW 热流谱隙常数）
