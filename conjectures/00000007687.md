# 00000007687

**QDE-27 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: A WZ pair (F,G) satisfies F(n+1,k)-F(n,k)=G(n,k+1)-G(n,k), with the q-analogue replacing shifts by q-shifts. Conjecture: For every hypergeometric term t(n,k), the parameter space of q-deformations t_q admitting a q-WZ pair with q-rational certificate is a Zariski open set whose complement is a union of hyperplanes determined by the pole types of the ratio function of t; in particular the complement for Gamma-type t always has dimension 1. (Zariski-open q-WZ deformation space)

**中文。** 定义：WZ 对 (F,G) 满足 F(n+1,k)-F(n,k)=G(n,k+1)-G(n,k)；其 q-模拟以 q-移位代替普通移位。猜想：对每个超几何项 t(n,k)，使得 t 存在 q-WZ 对且证书为 q-有理函数的 q-形变 t_q 的参数空间是一个 Zariski 开集，其补集为由 t 的比率函数的极点型决定的超平面并；特别地 Gamma 函数型 t 的补集维数恒为 1。
