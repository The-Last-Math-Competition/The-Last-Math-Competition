# 00000005198

**ST2-28 ◆** — source: 158_高阶范畴II_稳定无穷范畴.md

**English.** Definition: The Deligne conjecture asserts the compatibility of the center with the bracket, that is, the lift of the Gerstenhaber structure to E_2. Conjecture: The conversion is an explicit matrix of second-order structure constants, equivariant under the braid action; the conversion at third-order cohomology has no residue and the residue group vanishes; and the symmetric part of the constants is given by Koszul duality with the antisymmetric part unique. (second-order structure-constant matrix of Deligne)

**中文。** 定义：Deligne。猜想：中心与括号的相容性（Gerstenhaber 结构到 E₂ 的提升）。猜想：换算为二阶结构常数的显式矩阵且矩阵在辫作用下等变；三阶上同调处的换算无残差且残差群消没；常数的对称化部分由 Koszul 对偶给出且反对称部分唯一。（Deligne 二阶结构常数矩阵）
