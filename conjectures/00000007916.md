# 00000007916

**English.** Definition: The Gan-Gross-Prasad periods of relative cohomology: the rigidity spectrum (families of representations with nonzero period) of the period integral P(f) = integral over H of f(h) dh on a spherical variety G/H. Conjecture: The representations with nonzero period are exactly the intersection of functorial theta lift images with automorphically preserved varieties; the ratio of the square of the period to the local density of the L-value L(1/2,pi) is one, with the shape of the ratio an explicit product of Ichino-Ikeda type factors; and the ramified local contribution of the factors is a compact (1+1/q) type factor with global convergence. (GGP period Ichino-Ikeda ratio)

**中文。** 定义：相对上同调的 Gan–Gross–Prasad 周期：球簇 G/H 上的周期积分 P(f)=∫_H f(h)dh 的刚性谱（非零周期出现的表示族）。猜想：非零周期的表示恰为 functorial 的 theta 提升像与自守保留簇的交；周期的平方相对 L 值 L(1/2,π) 的局部密度比值为一，且该比的形状为 Ichino–Ikeda 型显式因子乘积；因子的分歧局部贡献为 (1+1/q) 型紧因子，整体收敛。
