# 00000004302

**AN4-02 ★** — source: 129_数论IV_代数数论显式方法.md

**English.** Definition: The simple unit of a real quadratic field is the fundamental unit given by the period of the continued fraction. Conjecture: The mean of the continued-fraction periods of real quadratic fields of discriminant at most X is an explicit asymptotic of square-root-of-logarithm type, with the constant given by an explicit functional of the mean of L(1, chi). (mean formula of real quadratic periods)

**中文。** 定义：实二次域的简单单位指由连分数周期给出的基本单位。猜想：判别式不超过 X 的实二次域连分数周期均值为根号对数型显式渐近,常数由 L(1,χ) 均值的显式泛函给出。（实二次周期均值公式）
