**English.** Definition: Fourier coefficients are the expansion coefficients of Maass forms. Conjecture: The optimal-exponent target and the current progress stand side by side and the wall of progress is the exponential sum; the current progress is of 7/64 plus epsilon type with per-step gain 1/64; and the wall has three layers. (exponential-sum wall of coefficient exponents)
