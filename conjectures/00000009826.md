# 00000009826

**English.** Definition: Rubio de Francia extrapolation: deriving the full family of weighted p-inequalities from a single p_0 inequality; the quantitative loss is unknown. Conjecture: The exponent loss of self-extrapolation A_p to A_q is double-logarithmic: norms grow like the p-prime power of [w] times a double-log correction of the log coefficient; the correction is optimal, attained by staircase constructions of power-law weights. (extrapolation double-log loss)

**中文。** 定义：Rubio de Francia 外插：由单一 p₀ 不等式推出全族 p 的加权不等式；定量损失未知。猜想：自外插 A_p→A_q 的指数损失为双对数型：范数增长如 [w] 的 p′ 幂乘 log 系数的双对数修正；修正为最优且由幂律权的阶梯构造达到。（外插双对数损失）
