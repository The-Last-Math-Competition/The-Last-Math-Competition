**English.** Definition: Ulam(1, n) denotes the Ulam sequence beginning with 1 and n. Conjecture: For every n ≥ 2 the sequence Ulam(1, n) is infinite; and the infinitude is settled by density estimates. (Ulam infinitude via density estimates)

**中文。** 定义：Ulam(1,n) 为以一与 n 开头的 Ulam 序列。猜想：对每个 n 不小于二 Ulam(1,n) 无穷；且无穷的组合为密度估计。（Ulam 无穷密度估计组合）
