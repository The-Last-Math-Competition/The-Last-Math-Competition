# 00000001803

**CV-23 ★** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: The Hartogs domain D×_h Ω (with holomorphic envelope condition h). Conjecture: Its holomorphic envelope is an explicit generalized domain: the denominator degree of the envelope is at most deg h; and the envelope function extends linearly from the zero structure of h.

**中文。** 定义：Hartogs 域 D\×_{h}Ω(h 全纯包络条件)。猜想：其全纯包络为显式广义域:包络的分母次数 ≤deg h;包络函数由 h 的零点结构线性扩张。
