# 00000003980

**PC2-10 △** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: Mutual simulation means that two proof systems polynomially simulate each other, with separation exponent being the supremum of length ratios. Conjecture: There exists a fixed explicit diagonalization construction which, for every pair of proof systems, produces a natural family of formulas with superpolynomial separation. (diagonal natural separation family)

**中文。** 定义：互模拟指两证明系统的多项式互相模拟,分离指数为长度比上确界。猜想：存在固定的显式对角化构造,其对每个证明系统对生成超多项式分离的自然公式族。（对角化自然分离族）
