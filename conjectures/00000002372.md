# 00000002372

**English.** Definition: The tensor reflexive subspace of polynomially bounded operators. Conjecture: The existence of the reflexive subspace is equivalent to polynomial convexity of the joint spectrum; and the criterion for convexity is the vanishing of holes in the spectrum. (tensor reflexive subspace convexity equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：多项式有界算子的张量反射子空间。猜想：反射子空间的存在等价于联合谱的多项式凸；且凸性的判据为谱的洞消没。（张量反射子空间凸性等价）
