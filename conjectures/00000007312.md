# 00000007312

**English.** Definition: Critical point counting of tensors, Beltrami type. Conjecture: The expected critical-point count of Gaussian tensors holds, and the exponent of the expectation is the alternating sum of indices. (index-alternating Gaussian critical-point count)

**中文。** 定义：张量的临界点计数：Beltrami 型。猜想：临界点的计数：高斯张量的临界点的期望且望的指数为指标的交替和。（张量临界点指标交替和高斯期望计数）
