**CS-04 ◆** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: Khinchin's constant K ≈ 2.6854 is the limit of the geometric mean of the continued-fraction partial quotients of almost every real number. Conjecture: The continued fraction of K itself satisfies the self-Khinchin property: the geometric mean of the first n partial quotients of K converges to K, at rate n^{−1/2+o(1)}, consistent with the typical deviation scale. (Khinchin self-similarity)

**中文。** 定义：Khinchin 常数 K≈2.6854 为几乎处处实数的连分数部分商几何平均极限。猜想：K 自身的连分数展开满足自 Khinchin 性：K 的前 n 个部分商的几何平均收敛到 K；收敛速率为 n^{−1/2+o(1)}，与其典型偏差尺度一致。（Khinchin 自相似性）
