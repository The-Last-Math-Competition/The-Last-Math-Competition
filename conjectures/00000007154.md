# 00000007154

**English.** Definition: Horn inequalities as the complete system of eigenvalue inequalities. Conjecture: The completeness of the system is the nonvanishing table of LR coefficients, and the saturation of the table is the saturation conjecture. (LR-saturated Horn recursion completeness)

**中文。** 定义：Horn 不等式：特征值不等式的完全系统。猜想：Horn 递归的饱和：不等式系统的完全性为 LR 系数的非零表且表的饱和为饱和猜想。（Horn LR 非零表饱和完全性递归）
