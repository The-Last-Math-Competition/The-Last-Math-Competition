# 00000002249

**English.** Definition: Arakelian sets are closed sets for uniform rational approximation. Conjecture: The minimal fragment count of fragmentation is an explicit irrational function of the area condition (fragment count).

**中文。** 定义：Arakelian 集(一致有理逼近的闭集)。猜想：片段化(fragmentation)的最小片段计数为面积条件的显式无理函数(片段计数)。
