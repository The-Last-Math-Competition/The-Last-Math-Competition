**English.** Definition: The scaling exponents and the correlation structure are two layers of statistics. Conjecture: There exist two turbulent fields with the same scaling exponent spectrum but different higher-order correlations, and the separation is realized by explicit modulation of synthetic fields. (same scaling spectrum-different higher-order correlation separation)
