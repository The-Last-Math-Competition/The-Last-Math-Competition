**PV-70 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The torsion function of the spectral fractional Laplacian is the spectral realization of virtual mass. Conjecture: The L∞ norm of the torsion function satisfies an explicit identity with the s-th harmonic mean of the spectrum; and the two-sided bounds of the identity are given by the leading eigenvalue term. (s-torsion spectral formula)

**中文。** 定义：谱分数 Laplacian 的扭转函数为虚拟质量的谱实现。猜想：扭转函数的 L∞ 范数与谱的 s 次调和平均满足显式等式；且等式的双向界由特征值首项给出。（s-torsion 谱公式）
