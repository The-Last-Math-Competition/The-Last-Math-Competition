# 00000005645

**English.** Definition: Bisection is the equal splitting of measures, of ham-sandwich type, with the complexity of bisecting curves. Conjecture: The conversion of the three-measure bisecting curve is the explicit formula of the parametric curve degree, of cubic type for degenerate (atomic) measures; the lower bound of the curve degree is 3, attained by cycloid types; and the number of bisection points is odd, the parity given by the connectivity of the measure. (parametric degree conversion formula of three measures)

**中文。** 定义：等分：测度均分的操作（火腿三明治型）；等分曲线的复杂度。猜想：三测度的等分曲线的换算为参数曲线度的显式公式且退化（原子）测度时为三次型；曲线度的下界为 3 且界由摆线型达到；等分点的个数为奇数且奇偶性由测度的连通性给出。（三测度参数度换算公式）
