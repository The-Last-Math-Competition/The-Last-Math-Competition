**English.** Definition: The quantification of the proof-theoretic constant of Tarski's undefinability: the minimal definable domain undef(T) of the undefinability of the truth predicate. Conjecture: The hierarchical constant of the definable fragments (Sigma_n-truth) of the truth predicate T* is the minimal disconnected point of the Stone duality of the reflection algebra (a Stone minimal-point law); the infimum of the Turing degree spectrum of T* (degrees of all consistent extensions' truth predicates) is 0-prime (the infimum rigidity of truth-predicate degrees); and the combinatorial density of the countable models of the saturated domain of consistent complete truth predicates (satisfaction classes) is 2^{-omega_1} (a model-theoretic quantification of the transfinite density constant). (Tarski Stone minimal truth-degree infimum)
