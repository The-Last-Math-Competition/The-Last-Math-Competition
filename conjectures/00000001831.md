# 00000001831

**English.** Definition: Kimura finite-dimensionality is the finiteness of the symmetric/antisymmetric powers of a motive. Conjecture: Motives of all surfaces are finite-dimensional; Kimura for hyperelliptic surfaces follows directly from the direct-sum decomposition of their fibration structure (without the full theory for general surfaces).

**中文。** 定义：Kimura 有限维性指母题的对称/反对称幂的有限性。猜想：一切曲面的母题有限维;超椭圆曲面的 Kimura 由其 fibration 结构的直和分解直接推演(不需要 general surface 的完整理论)。
