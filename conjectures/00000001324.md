# 00000001324

**CC-24 ★** — source: 29_多复变与复几何.md

**English.** Definition: The jumping layers of the Kodaira dimension are the dimension layers of direct images under deformation. Conjecture: The higher direct-image dimensions of the jumping layers are always given by the critical-value sets of the relative deformation; and the weight of the critical layer is one. (jumping layer critical direct-image dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Kodaira 维数的跳跃层指形变下直像的维数层。猜想：跳跃层的高阶直像维数恒由相对形变的临界值集给出；且临界层的权重为一。（跳跃层临界直像维数）
