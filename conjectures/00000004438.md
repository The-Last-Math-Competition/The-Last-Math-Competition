# 00000004438

**English.** Definition: Reflected Brownian motion is the multi-particle Brownian system with wall reflection, and the Karlin-McGregor property is the non-crossing of trajectories. Conjecture: Its limit shape is an explicit affine image of the Weyl chamber, with structure group the type-A root system. (Weyl chamber affine image of reflected Brownian motion)

**中文。** 定义：反射布朗运动指带壁反射的多粒子布朗系,Karlin–McGregor 性质指轨道不交。猜想：其极限形状为 Weyl 室的显式仿射像,像的结构群为 A 型根系统。（反射布朗 Weyl 室仿射像）
