**English.** Definition: The Mahler measure of a univariate integer polynomial P is m(P) = ∏ max(1, |α_i|) over the roots α_i. It is proven that m(1+x+y) = (3√3/4π)L(χ_{−3}, 2). Conjecture (Boyd): m(1+x+y+z) = (7/2π²)·L(E_{15}, 1), where E_{15} is the conductor-15 elliptic curve 15a1; more generally, m(1+x₁+…+x_n) is an algebraic multiple of the L-value of some mixed Tate motive.
