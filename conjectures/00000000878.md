# 00000000878

**PV-78 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The wave-envelope equation for double-negative metamaterials is the scalar reduced model of Maxwell systems with negative effective permittivity. Conjecture: The regularity threshold of its solutions is decided by the sign flip of the effective mass: for negative sign, H¹ solutions lose one order of regularity within time 2L; for positive sign, solutions are globally smooth; and the threshold case is exactly the linear degeneracy at zero dispersion.

**中文。** 定义：双负折射超材料的波动包络方程指有效介电常数为负的 Maxwell 系统的标量简化模型。猜想：该方程解的正则性阈值由有效质量符号翻转决定:符号为负时 H¹ 解在时间 2L 内失去一阶正则性,为正时整体光滑;阈值情形恰为零色散的线性退化。
