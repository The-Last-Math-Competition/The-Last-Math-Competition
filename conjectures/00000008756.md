# 00000008756

**MKT-16 ★** — source: 277_代数K理论与MilnorK.md

**English.** Definition: Geometric models of K-theory: cobordism quotients of bundles. Conjecture: The geometric model is equivalent to the Quillen spectrum (an equivalence law); the Tate model has an infinite loop structure (an infiniteness law); the relations of p-groups are explicit (a relation law); and the geometric realization is rigorously constructible (a rigorization law). (geometric K model Tate infinite loop)

**中文。** 定义：K 理论的几何模型：丛的配边商。猜想：几何模型与 Quillen 谱的等价（等价律）；且 Tate 模型的无穷环结构（无穷律）；p 群的关系的显式（关系律）；几何实现的严格化（严格化律）。
