**English.** Definition: Borsuk's problem asks for partitions of bounded sets into pieces of smaller diameter. Conjecture: The minimal dimension of a counterexample is some value not exceeding sixty-five; and the determination is settled through the double wrapping of Larman sets. (Borsuk-Larman double wrap minimal counterexample dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Borsuk 问题为有界集的小直径分割。猜想：有限维的最小反例维数为某个不超过六十五的值；且判定的核为 Larman 集双包。（Borsuk-Larman 双包最小反例维数）
