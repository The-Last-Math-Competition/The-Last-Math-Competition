# 00000008038

**English.** Definition: The semialgebraic index of the o-minimal extension of Nash functions: the definable complexity Nash_idx(f) of the graph of a Nash function (real analytic with semialgebraic graph), the minimal depth of a cell decomposition. Conjecture: Nash_idx(f) is at most the logarithm of deg f (logarithmic taming of Nash functions); the minimal examples attaining the logarithmic bound are Koshysh-type compositions (nested square-root chains); the conversion rate between chain length and algebraic degree is the bijection 2^{depth} = deg (complete conversion of Nash complexity); and the exception family to the conversion law is Chebyshev iteration (Chebyshev depth compression: depth = log log deg). (Nash logarithmic cell depth conversion)

**中文。** 定义：Nash 函数的 o-极小延拓的半代数索引：Nash 函数（实解析且半代数图）的图的可定义复杂度 Nash_idx(f)（cell 分解的最小深度）。猜想：Nash_idx(f) ≤ deg f 的对数（Nash 的对数驯化）；且达到对数界的 Nash 函数的极小例子为 Koshysh 型复合（嵌套平方根链）；链长与代数次数的换算率为 2^{depth}=deg 的双射（Nash 复杂度的完全换算）；换算律的例外族为 Chebyshev 迭代（Chebyshev 的深度压缩：depth=log log deg）。
