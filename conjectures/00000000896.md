**PV-96 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: Point-vortex solutions of surface quasi-geostrophic flow are singular solutions of the active scalar. Conjecture: The number of relative equilibria of N point vortices is finite and given by the algebraic degree of central configurations; and the counting bound is the rational degree of the configuration space. (SQG central configuration counting)

**中文。** 定义：表面准地流的点涡解为主动标量的奇点解。猜想：N-点涡的相对平衡数为有限且由中心构型的代数度给出；且计数的上界为构型空间的有理度。（SQG 中心构型计数）
