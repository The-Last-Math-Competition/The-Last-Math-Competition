**HA-09 ★** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The bottleneck distance d_B of persistence modules versus the L∞ distance of intervals. Conjecture: Under the dual representation of single-parameter interval modules M = ⊕I_i, d_B(M,N) exactly equals the minimal maximal deviation of endpoint matchings (i.e. the exact formula d_B = d_∞); the optimal matching is obtained by a greedy algorithm.

**中文。** 定义：持久模块的瓶颈距离 d_B 与区间的 L∞ 距离。猜想：单参数区间模块 M=⊕I_i 的对偶表示下,d_B(M,N) 恰等于区间端点匹配的极小最大偏差(即 d_B=d_∞ 的精确公式);匹配最优解可由贪心算法取得。
