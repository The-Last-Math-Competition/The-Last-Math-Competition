# 00000000319

**English.** Definition: The one-sided Markov constants M⁺ and M⁻ are the asymptotic suprema as q → +∞ and q → −∞ respectively. Conjecture: There exists α with M⁺(α) ≠ M⁻(α), both values lying in the Markov spectrum; and such asymmetric numbers form a dense Gδ set in the reals. (asymmetric one-sided Markov constants exist)

**中文。** 定义：左右 Markov 常数 M⁺ 与 M⁻ 分别取 q→+∞ 与 q→−∞ 的渐近上确界。猜想：存在 α 使 M⁺(α)≠M⁻(α) 且两值都落在 Markov 谱中；且此类不对称数在实数中为稠密 Gδ。（左右 Markov 不对称存在）
