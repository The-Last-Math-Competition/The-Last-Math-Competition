# 00000008574

**NSP-14 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: three angle constant of norm of Schatten class S_p. Conjecture: p=1 when three angle constant is norm constant of 1 (unit one); general p is explicit (one p); of 2^{1-1/p} and the constant by rank one operator attains (rank one and); p -> ∞ norm degenerate is operator norm (degenerate).

**中文。** 定义：Schatten 类 S_p 的准范数的三角常数。猜想：p=1 时三角常数为 1（单位一律）；一般 p 的准范数常数为 2^{1-1/p} 的显式（双一 p 律）；且该常数由秩一算子取得（秩一饱和律）；p→∞ 时准范数退化为算子范数（退化律）。
