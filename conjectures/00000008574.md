# 00000008574

**NSP-14 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The triangle constant of the quasi-norm of Schatten classes S_p. Conjecture: The triangle constant is 1 at p = 1 (a unit law); for general p the quasi-norm constant is the explicit 2^{1-1/p} (a two-minus-one-over-p law); the constant is attained by rank-one operators (a rank-one saturation law); and as p grows the quasi-norm degenerates to the operator norm (a degeneration law). (Schatten quasi-norm triangle constant)

**中文。** 定义：Schatten 类 S_p 的准范数的三角常数。猜想：p=1 时三角常数为 1（单位一律）；一般 p 的准范数常数为 2^{1-1/p} 的显式（双一 p 律）；且该常数由秩一算子取得（秩一饱和律）；p→∞ 时准范数退化为算子范数（退化律）。
