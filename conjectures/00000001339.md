# 00000001339

**AN2-09 ◆** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Definition: The sixth moment of the symmetric fourth power L-function L(s, sym⁴ f) of a holomorphic cusp form f is the integral average of its sixth power of modulus on the critical line. Conjecture: This moment is asymptotic to conductor-and-length polynomial factors times a degree-5 logarithmic polynomial with explicit combinatorial coefficients; this is the first complete statement of a sym⁴ moment formula.

**中文。** 定义：全纯尖形式 f 的对称四次 L 函数 L(s,sym⁴f) 的 6 次矩指其临界线上模长六次幂的积分平均。猜想：该矩渐近为导子与长度多项式因子乘以 5 次 log 多项式,多项式系数有显式组合公式;这是 sym⁴ 矩公式的首次完整陈述。
