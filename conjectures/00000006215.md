# 00000006215

**English.** Definition: Invariance is the preservation of the measure under the flow. Conjecture: Finite-dimensional truncations approximate and the limit-exchange error is explicit, the kernel of the error being the energy drift. (energy-drift exchange error of invariant approximation)

**中文。** 定义：不变性为测度在流下保持。猜想：有限维截断逼近且极限交换误差显式；且误差的核为能量漂移。（不变逼近能量漂移交换误差）
