# 00000002933

**English.** Definition: The hierarchy of ordinal recursive functions: the fast-growing hierarchy F_alpha. Conjecture: The separation witness of hierarchy classes: the witness function separating F_alpha from F_{alpha+1} is an Ackermann-type jump, and the minimal domain size of the witness is a tower of levels. (fast-growing separation witness)
