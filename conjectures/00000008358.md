# 00000008358

**English.** Definition: The passage contraction of tropical Hurwitz numbers: the fixed points pass_fix of the contraction operator on the passage number. Conjecture: The count of pass_fix (fixed-point curves of passage contraction) is rational spirals (a rational spiral law); the logarithmic convergence step count of the monotone contraction (energy monotone decrease) is O(log d) (a logarithmic step law); the threshold for D-finiteness failure of the generating function of Hurwitz numbers (non-D-finite) is ramification profile length at least 3 (a three-length threshold law); and the non-D-finiteness proof path is the density of singularities (a dense singularity path). (tropical Hurwitz passage spiral dense singularities)

**中文。** 定义：热带Hurwitz 数的通道收缩：通道数（passage number）的收缩算子的不动点 pass_fix。猜想：pass_fix（通道收缩的不动点曲线）的计数为有理螺线（有理螺线律）；且收缩的算子的单调（能量的单调减）的收敛步数为 O(log d)（对数步律）；Hurwitz 数的生成函数的 D-finite 的失效（非 D-finite）的阈为分歧轮廓的长度 ≥ 3（三长阈律）；非 D-finite 的证明路径为奇点分布的稠密（稠密奇点路径）。
