# 00000005381

**English.** Definition: The canonical height is the standard height function of a dynamical system. Conjecture: The minimal positive height lower bound of maps of degree at least two is an explicit negative-quadratic quantity of the degree, attained by totally invariant monomial families, and compatible with the classical constant. (lower bound of the dynamical minimal height constant)

**中文。** 定义：典范高度指动力系统的标准高度函数。猜想：度至少二映射的极小正高度下界为度量的负二次型显式量,由全不变单项式族达到且与经典常数相容。（动力学极小高度常数下界）
