# 00000004422

**English.** Definition: q-TASEP is the exclusion process with q-deformed jump rates. Conjecture: The leading closed-form constant of its moment recursion is an explicit finite product of reciprocals of q-Pochhammer symbols, and the recursion is stable if and only if q is less than 1. (q-TASEP finite-product moment leading term)

**中文。** 定义：q-TASEP 指 q 化跳率的排斥过程。猜想：其矩递推的闭式首项常数为 q-Pochhammer 倒数的显式有限积,递推稳定当且仅当 q 小于 1。（q-TASEP 矩首项有限积）
