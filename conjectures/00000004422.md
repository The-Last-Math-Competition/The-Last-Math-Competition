**IP2-02 ★** — source: 133_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: q-TASEP denotes repulsion process of q. Conjecture: closed-form leading term constant of its moment recurrence is explicit finite product, recurrence stable of q-Pochhammer reciprocal if and only if q less than 1. (q-TASEP moment leading term finite product)

**中文。** 定义：q-TASEP 指 q 化跳率的排斥过程。猜想：其矩递推的闭式首项常数为 q-Pochhammer 倒数的显式有限积,递推稳定当且仅当 q 小于 1。（q-TASEP 矩首项有限积）
