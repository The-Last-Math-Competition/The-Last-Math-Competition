**SMI-26 ★** — source: 287_子流形几何不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Superminimal surfaces: horizontal lifts to the Twistor space. Conjecture: The twistor description inside spheres is given by projective lines; and the bijection of the description is horizontality. (superminimal twistor projective description)

**中文。** 定义：超极小曲面为 Twistor 上的水平提升。猜想：球面内的 Twistor 描述由射影直线给出；且描述的双射为水平性。（superminimal Twistor 射影描述）
