# 00000009011

**MFH-01 ★** — source: 286_半整权模形式.md

**English.** Definition: Half-integral weight modular forms: modular forms of half-integral weight. Conjecture: The main coefficient of the square mean of their Hecke coefficients is an explicit functional in the central value of L(1, sym^2 g). (coefficient square-mean functional)

**中文。** 定义：半整权模形式指权为半整数的模形式。猜想：其 Hecke 系数的平方平均的主项系数为 L(1,sym² g) 的中心值的显式泛函。（系数平方平均泛函）
