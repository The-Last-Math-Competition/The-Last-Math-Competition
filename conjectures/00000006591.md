# 00000006591

**English.** Definition: Exceptional objects are the rigid structures without self-extensions. Conjecture: The moduli of exceptional families is locally constant and the dimension-deficit table is complete, the kernel of the table being the Hilbert lattice. (Hilbert-lattice deficit table of exceptional families)

**中文。** 定义：例外对象为无自扩张的刚体结构。猜想：例外族的模空间为局部常且维数亏格表完全；且表的核为 Hilbert 格。（例外族 Hilbert 格亏格表）
