**FP2-19 ★** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: The Follmer entropy drift is the construction of a Brownian drift with fixed relative entropy. Conjecture: The minimal-energy drift is the explicit Doob transform, the energy lower bound is of logarithmic type in half the square of the entropy, and the lower bound is attained only by exponential drifts. (Follmer minimal-energy Doob solution)

**中文。** 定义：Föllmer 熵漂移指以固定相对熵构造布朗运动的漂移。猜想：最小能量漂移为显式的 Doob 变换,能量下界为熵平方之半的下对数型,下界仅由指数漂移达到。（Föllmer 最小能量 Doob 解）
