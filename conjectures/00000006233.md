# 00000006233

**GT5-13 ◆** — source: 193_几何范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: The essential image is the full-image structure of geometric morphisms. Conjecture: The faithful-full case is equivalent to proper geometric morphisms, the criterion is complete, and the kernel of the equivalence is density. (dense faithful-full equivalence of essential images)

**中文。** 定义：本质像为几何态射的满像结构。猜想：忠实满情形与正几何态射等价且判据完全；且等价的核为稠密性。（本质像稠密忠实满等价）
