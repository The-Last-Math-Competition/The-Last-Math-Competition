# 00000004417

**English.** Definition: The heat kernel of a graphon is the kernel of the heat semigroup of its integral operator. Conjecture: The leading term of the short-time diagonal trace asymptotic is the explicit expression 1/sqrt(4 pi t), the next term is the one-loop correction by the integral of the square of the kernel, and both terms are universal. (two short-time terms of the graphon heat kernel)

**中文。** 定义：graphon 的热核指其积分算子热半群的核。猜想：短时对角迹渐近首项为 1 除以 √(4πt) 的显式式,次项为核平方积分的单圈修正,两项均普适。（graphon 热核短时双项）
