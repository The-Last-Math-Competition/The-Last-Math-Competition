# 00000001634

**English.** Definition: The representation dimension repdim of the exterior algebra Λ(V) = TV/⟨v⊗w+w⊗v⟩ is the shape dimension of its minimal exact category. Conjecture: repdim Λ(V) = dim V (the bounds dim V−1 and dim V are known); equality holds unconditionally exactly when dim V is even.

**中文。** 定义：外代数 Λ(V)=TV/⟨v⊗w+w⊗v⟩ 的表示维数 repdim 指其极小正合范畴的形维。猜想：repdim Λ(V)=dim V(已知下界 dim V−1 与上界 dim V);等号恰在 dim V 为偶数时无条件成立。
