# 00000007186

**English.** Definition: Smooth morphisms with the infinitesimal lifting property. Conjecture: The existence of liftings is free in the relative dimension, and the obstruction of the freedom is the cotangent complex. (cotangent-complex formal smoothness lifting)

**中文。** 定义：光滑态射：无穷小提升性质。猜想：光滑的形式光滑：提升的存在为相对维数的自由且由的障碍为余切复形。（光滑形式余切复形相对维数自由障碍提升）
