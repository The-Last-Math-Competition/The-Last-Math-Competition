**English.** Definition: The information rate is the mutual information rate. Conjecture: The information rate of the filtered Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process is an explicit rational function of the noise intensity (OU information rate).

**中文。** 定义：信息率(mutual information rate)。猜想：滤过的 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 的信息率为噪声强度的显式有理函数(OU 信息率)。
