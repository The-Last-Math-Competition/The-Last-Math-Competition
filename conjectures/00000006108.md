# 00000006108

**English.** Definition: Dimension-freeness means the constant does not depend on dimension. Conjecture: The convex one-Lipschitz class is closed with constant one, and the combination of the closure is convex translation invariance. (translation-invariant constant-one closure of convex classes)

**中文。** 定义：维数无关指常数不依赖维数。猜想：凸一 Lipschitz 类闭且常数为一；且闭包的组合为凸平移不变。（凸类闭包平移不变常数一）
