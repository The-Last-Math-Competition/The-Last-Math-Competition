# 00000003436

**English.** Definition: Aleksandrov widths: widths of nonlinear approximation. Conjecture: The stability of Aleksandrov widths: widths are Lipschitz continuous under small perturbation, with the constant the convexity modulus of the set. (Aleksandrov convexity modulus Lipschitz width)

**中文。** 定义：Aleksandrov 宽度：非线形逼近的宽度。猜想：Aleksandrov 宽度的稳定性：小扰动下宽度的 Lipschitz 连续且常数为集合的凸性模。（Aleksandrov 凸性模 Lipschitz 宽度）
