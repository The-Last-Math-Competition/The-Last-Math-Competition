# 00000000796

**English.** Definition: The Fermat quotient q_p(a) = (a^{p−1}−1)/p. Conjecture: Two Fermat quotients mod p are jointly independent whenever the product of the bases is not a power; and the exceptions to independence are power-relation pairs. (Fermat quotient independence)

**中文。** 定义：Fermat 商 q_p(a) 为 (a^{p−1}−1)/p。猜想：两 Fermat 商模 p 联合独立当底乘积非幂；且独立性的例外为幂关系对。（Fermat 商独立性）
