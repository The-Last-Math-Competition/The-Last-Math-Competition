# 00000007250

**VR4-10 ★** — source: 227_变分法II.md

**English.** Definition: The saddle points of variational inequalities with mixed extrema. Conjecture: The saddle-type variational solutions hold, and the approximation of the solutions is alternating minimax. (alternating-minimax mixed saddle solutions)

**中文。** 定义：变分不等式的鞍点：混合极值。猜想：混合鞍点：鞍点型的变分解且解的逼近为交替极小极大。（混合交替极小极大鞍点逼近变分解）
