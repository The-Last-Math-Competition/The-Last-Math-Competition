# 00000005693

**TW2-13 ★** — source: 175_范畴的表示_2表示论.md

**English.** Definition: The Schur-type indicator is the discriminant quantity of self-dual objects. Conjecture: The indicator table of 2-groups is the explicit count of involution orbits, two-valued at plus-minus one for balanced types; the modulus of the indicator is 1; and indicator multiplicativity is preserved under tensor products, the preservation given by the elementary identity of Frobenius-Schur indicators. (involution-orbit indicator counting of 2-groups)

**中文。** 定义：舒尔型指标：自对偶对象的判别量（指标）。猜想：双群的指标表为对合轨道的显式计数且平衡型时取值正负一的二值；指标的模为 1；指标乘性在张量积下保持且保持由 Frobenius–Schur 指标的初等恒等式给出。（双群对合轨道指标计数）
