# 00000007817

**English.** Definition: Vafa-Witten invariants count Euler-weighted moduli spaces of instantons on a surface S; the S-duality conjecture asserts the generating function is a half-integral weight modular form. Conjecture: For a minimal surface S of general type, the VW generating function is a modular form (constant-coefficient combination of chi(O_S), c_1^2, c_2) times a rational q-series F_S(q), where F_S(q) = 1 + O(q) has coefficients the SU(3)-type Donaldson invariants of S; and the order of the zero of the denominator of F_S at q=1 is exactly the first Betti number, an enumerative signature of rigidity. (Vafa-Witten factorization and Betti zero)

**中文。** 定义：Vafa–Witten 不变量计数曲面 S 上瞬子模空间的 Euler 加权。S-duality 猜想断言其母函数为半整权模形式。猜想：对一般型极小曲面 S，VW 母函数 = 模形式（仅依赖 χ(O_S), c_1², c_2 的常数系数组合）× 有理函数 q-级数 F_S(q)，且 F_S(q) = 1+O(q) 的系数为 S 的 SU(3) 型 Donaldson 不变量；F_S 的分母在 q=1 处的零点阶恰为第一 Betti 数，此为流形刚性条件的枚举签名。
