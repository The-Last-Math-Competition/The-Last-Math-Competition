# 00000000538

**English.** Definition: The reduced bipartite graph of G is obtained by successively contracting each independent vertex pair; pd I(G) is the projective dimension of its edge ideal. Conjecture: For reduced graphs without isolated vertices, pd R/I(G) = 2 if and only if G is bipartite and some Hall-path closure is a forest; this criterion advances the Froberg chordal-complement theorem to the pd = 2 layer.

**中文。** 定义：图的约化二部图指把每个独立顶点对依次压缩后得到的二部图;pd I(G) 为其边理想的投射维数。猜想：对约化后无孤立点的图,pd R/I(G)=2 当且仅当 G 为二部且其某个 hall 途径 closure 为森林;该判据把 Fröberg 弦补图定理推进到 pd=2 层。
