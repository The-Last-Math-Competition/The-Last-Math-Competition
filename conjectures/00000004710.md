# 00000004710

**English.** Definition: Polynomial correlations and mixing rates are the two statistical layers of a system. Conjecture: There exist two ergodic systems whose polynomial correlation limits all coincide while their mixing rates differ, and the separation is realized by a Markov pair with the same conjugacy but different spectral gaps. (correlation limit-mixing rate separation)

**中文。** 定义：多项式相关与混合速率指系统的两个统计层。猜想：存在两遍历系统的一切多项式相关极限相同而混合速率相异,分离由同共轭异谱隙的马尔可夫对实现。（相关极限-混合速率分离）
