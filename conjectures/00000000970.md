# 00000000970

**OA-70 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral measure of a self-adjoint polynomial P(X₁,…,X_d) in free group generators. Conjecture: The set of atomic masses of the spectral measure of P is exactly a finite set of type {m/2^k}, the total mass explicitly controlled by the count of critical values of the leading homogeneous part of P on the unit sphere; and the total atomic mass of a quadratic polynomial is at most 1/2.

**中文。** 定义：自共轭多项式 P(X₁,…,X_d) 在自由群生成元上的谱测度。猜想：P 的谱测度的原子质量集合恰为 {m/2^k} 型有限集,质量总和由 P 的首齐次部分在单位球上的临界值计数显式控制;二次多项式的原子总质量 ≤ 1/2。
