# 00000003877

**RM3-27 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The support endpoints are the boundary points of the support of the equilibrium density. Conjecture: For polynomial potentials, the support endpoints as functions of potential coefficients are always algebraic, with minimal-polynomial degrees at most twice the potential degree. (support endpoint algebraicity)

**中文。** 定义：支撑端点指平衡密度的支撑边界点。猜想：多项式位势的支撑端点作为位势系数的函数恒为代数函数,且其极小多项式次数不超过位势次数的两倍。（支撑端点代数性）
