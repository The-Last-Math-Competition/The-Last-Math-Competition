# 00000009797

**DDG-07 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Conditioning of Delaunay triangulations: spectral rigidity indices of meshes. Conjecture: The ratio of extreme eigenvalues of the cotan Laplacian of Delaunay triangulations has a uniform lower bound depending only on the combinatorics of the triangulation and the boundary; the lower bound is an explicit exponential function of interior angle bounds, asymptotically attained by degenerate Delaunay families. (Delaunay spectral conditioning lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Delaunay 三角化的条件数：网格的谱刚性指标。猜想：Delaunay 三角化的 cotan Laplacian 的最大最小特征值比有仅依赖三角剖分组合与边界的一致下界；下界由内角下界的显式指数函数给出且被退化 Delaunay 族渐近达到。（Delaunay 谱条件下界）
