**UA-24 ★** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Factorization homology is homology over factorization categories. Conjecture: Its value on a genus-g surface is an explicit linear combination of central tensor invariants of the coefficient category (the coefficients being central terms of the groupoid category); the formula is uniform for all g ≥ 0.

**中文。** 定义：分解同调指因子化范畴上的同调。猜想：其在亏格 g 曲面上的值为系数范畴的中心张量不变量的显式线性和(系数 = 群范畴的中心项);公式对一切 g≥0 一致。
