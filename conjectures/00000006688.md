**English.** Definition: Newton's method with the second-order spectrum of the semidiscrete problem. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the second-order spectrum and the condition number is given by measure regularity; the spectrum bound closes with closed endpoint the farthest-cell ratio; and the growth of the condition number is linear in the dimension. (regularity spectrum-bound conversion of condition numbers)
