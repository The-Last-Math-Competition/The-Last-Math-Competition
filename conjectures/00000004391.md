# 00000004391

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of a graphon is the eigenvalue sequence of its integral operator, and the spectral radius is the largest eigenvalue. Conjecture: Among graphons of fixed edge density, the maximum spectral radius is attained only by the twin-clique graphon and the minimum only by the half-graph graphon, both unique up to zero-measure modification. (uniqueness of the spectral radius extremes: twin-clique and half-graph)

**中文。** 定义：graphon 的谱指其积分算子的特征值序列,谱半径指最大特征值。猜想：固定边密度的 graphon 的谱半径最大值仅由双团 graphon 达到,最小值仅由半图 graphon 达到,两端在零测修改意义下唯一。（谱半径极值双团-半图唯一）
