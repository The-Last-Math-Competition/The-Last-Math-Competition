# 00000007207

**SC6-27 ★** — source: 225_概形论II.md

**English.** Definition: The Galois classification of etale fundamental groups. Conjecture: The comparison of the spectra of finite quotients of the fundamental group in positive characteristic with characteristic zero holds, and the rigidity of the comparison is specialization. (specialization-rigid etale quotient comparison)

**中文。** 定义：平展基本群的 Galois 分类。猜想：基本群的刚性：正特征的基本群的有限商的谱与特征零的比较且较的刚性为特殊化。（平展基本特殊化有限商谱比较刚性）
