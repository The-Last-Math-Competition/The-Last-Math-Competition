**KT-45 ◆** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The unknotting number u(K). Conjecture (Scharlemann): additivity u(K#K') = u(K)+u(K') (the main statement); strengthened: knots of unknotting number 1 have density tending to 0 as crossing numbers grow.

**中文。** 猜想：定义:解结数 u(K)。猜想(Scharlemann):u(K#K') = u(K)+u(K') 的加性(收录本体);加强:解结数 1 的纽结在交叉数 → ∞ 中密度 → 0。
