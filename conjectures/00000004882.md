# 00000004882

**English.** Definition: An independent statement is a true combinatorial sentence unprovable in arithmetic. Conjecture: The combinatorial complexity lower bound of the smallest independent statement of the Ramsey class is an explicit constant, given by the saturation verification of decidable intervals, and the bound rises monotonically as verification progresses. (lower bound of the minimal PA-independent combinatorial complexity)

**中文。** 定义：独立陈述指算术不可证的真组合句。猜想：拉姆齐类最小独立陈述的组合复杂度下界为显式常数,由可判定区间的饱和验证给出且界随验证推进单调上升。（PA 独立最小组合复杂度下界）
