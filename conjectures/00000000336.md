# 00000000336

**English.** Definition: Spherical uniform approximation replaces the coprimality condition by spherical separation. Conjecture: The spherical Duffin–Schaeffer theorem holds with full measure from divergence in dimension at least two; and the constants are controlled by the L² entropy of spherical harmonics. (spherical DS divergence full measure)
