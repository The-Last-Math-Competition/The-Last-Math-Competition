# 00000001988

**MP-28 ★** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: The Lieb–Robinson speed v_LR is the upper bound on information propagation in quantum systems with local interactions. Conjecture: The exact value of v_LR for the XXZ chain is a functional of the Bethe root density: v = 2·ρ(0) (ρ the root density); the value is determined by the maximum group velocity of quasiparticles.

**中文。** 定义：Lieb–Robinson 速度 v_LR 指局域相互作用的量子系统的信息传播上限。猜想：XXZ 链的 v_LR 的精确值为 Bethe 根密度的泛函:v = 2·ρ(0)(ρ 为根密度);值由准粒子的群速度的最大值确定。
