# 00000008664

**CGT-14 ★** — source: 274_计算群论与字的几何.md

**English.** Definition: Subgroup growth is the count a_n(G) of subgroups of index n. Conjecture: a_n has polynomial growth if and only if G is virtually nilpotent; and the realization spectrum of intermediate growth has a strict characterization with every increasing function realized by a floor of a power of n. (intermediate subgroup growth realization spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：subgroup growth 指指数 n 的子群计数 a_n(G)。猜想：a_n 多项式增长当且仅当 G 几乎幂零；中间增长的实现谱有严格刻画且每个递增函数以 n 的幂下取整实现。（中间 subgroup growth 实现谱）
