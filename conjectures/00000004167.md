**English.** Definition: A mixed power sum is a representation with variables of different powers. Conjecture: The minimal total number of variables is 7 for the mixed system (2,3) and 11 for the system (2,3,4), and the lower bound for a general mixed system is given by an explicit function of the sum of reciprocal degrees. (variable-count formula for mixed power sums)

**中文。** 定义：混合幂和指不同次数幂的联合变量表示。猜想：混合组 (2,3) 的最小总变量数为 7,组 (2,3,4) 为 11,一般混合组的下界由次数倒数和的显式函数给出。（混合幂变量数公式）
