# 00000001605

**English.** Definition: The sum Σgap of open gap lengths of Jacobi/Hill operators of period n. Conjecture: Σgap ≤ 4√n·‖q‖_{L²}·(1+1/n) (a uniform upper bound with optimal constant 4); equality is approached asymptotically by the single-frequency parameterization of the cosine potential.
