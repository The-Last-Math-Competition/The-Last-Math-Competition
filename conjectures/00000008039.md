**English.** Definition: The tame regularity of definable isoperimetric profiles: the definable regularity class IP_Omega of the isoperimetric profile h_Omega(r) of definable domains. Conjecture: h_Omega is a definable analytic function (analytic on intervals); the countability of the concavity-convexity transition points (isoperimetric phase transitions) corresponds one-to-one with the reflection law of the geodesic flow; the dimension of the singular leaves (singular fibers) of the definable foliation by isoperimetric minimal surfaces is at most n-3 (a codimension-3 law for isoperimetric singularities), and the quotient of the leaf space is a definable simplicial complex (a taming of the leaf space). (definable isoperimetric codimension-three singularities)
