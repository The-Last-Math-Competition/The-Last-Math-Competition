**English.** Definition: The Moyal plane: the geometry of noncommutative R². Conjecture: The spectral distance of Moyal: the explicit formula of the spectral distance of the plane is a function of the angle parameter, with the formula converging to the Euclidean distance at the lattice limit. (Moyal spectral distance lattice Euclidean limit)
