# 00000006105

**English.** Definition: The first-order transport inequality is the Talagrand-type control of the W_1 distance by entropy, with heavy-tail failure. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit limit of the mean, failing at heavy-tail critical exponent two; the failure point is the convergence radius of the moment-generating function; and the divergence order of the constant in the critical window is exactly the reciprocal of the exponent. (heavy-tail critical failure of first-order transport)

**中文。** 定义：一阶运输不等式：W₁ 距离与熵的 Talagrand 型控制；重尾失效。猜想：换算常数为均值的显式极限且重尾临界指数二处失效；失效点为矩生成函数的收敛半径；临界窗内的常数发散阶恰为指数的倒数。（一阶运输重尾临界失效）
