# 00000002628

**English.** Definition: Jacobi theta series and representation numbers of lattices: θ_L(τ,z) attached to an even lattice L. Conjecture: Spectral rigidity of representation numbers: the Jacobi theta series of a positive-definite even lattice uniquely determines the lattice when the rank is at least 12 and the discriminant is prime; the density of uniqueness counterexamples decays exponentially in the rank. (lattice representation spectral rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi θ 级数与格的表示数：θ_L(τ,z) 附着于偶格 L。猜想：表示数的谱刚性：正定偶格的 Jacobi θ 级数唯一确定格当秩至少 12 且判别式为素数；唯一性的反例密度随秩指数衰减。（格表示数谱刚性）
