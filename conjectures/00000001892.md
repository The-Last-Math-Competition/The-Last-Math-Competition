# 00000001892

**English.** Definition: A parametrized Picard–Vessiot extension is the Galois-theoretic structure of differential equations with parameters. Conjecture: The parametrized Galois group of the second-order parametrized Airy equation y'' = ty is G_a ⋉ SL₂ (semidirect); the group structure is an explicit parametrized subgroup of a linear algebraic group.

**中文。** 定义：参数化 Picard–Vessiot 扩张指微分方程含参数的 Galois 理论结构。猜想：二阶参数化 Airy 方程 y''=ty 的参数化 Galois 群为 G_a×SL₂(半直);群结构显式为线性代数群的参数化子群。
