# 00000003373

**NC-03 ★** — source: 98_非交换几何II_谱三元组.md

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative measure: the Dixmier trace. Conjecture: Choice-independence of the Dixmier trace: the value of the trace is independent of the generalized limit when the ζ function of the operator has an analytic-continuation residue at 1, with the independence a conformal invariant. (Dixmier residue conformal invariant independence)

**中文。** 定义：非交换测度：Dixmier 迹。猜想：Dixmier 迹的独立选择：迹的值为广义极限的选择独立当算子的 ζ 函数在 1 处解析延拓残差且独立性为共形不变量。（Dixmier 残差共形不变独立）
