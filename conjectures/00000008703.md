# 00000008703

**English.** Definition: Transport isoperimetry: the isoperimetric inequality of Wasserstein space. Conjecture: The spherical isoperimetric inequality holds, with the combination of balls a Gaussian product; and the kernel of the product is radial projection. (Wasserstein radial projection spherical isoperimetry)

**中文。** 定义：传输的等周为 Wasserstein 空间的等周不等式。猜想：球形等周成立且球的组合为高斯积；且积的核为径向投影。（Wasserstein 径向投影球形等周）
