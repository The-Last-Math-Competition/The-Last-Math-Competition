**English.** Definition: The center structure of the pure braid group. Conjecture: The central-action invariants of higher cohomology are explicit cohomology classes; and the table of the classes is given by representative cycles. (pure braid central-action cohomology invariant)

**中文。** 定义：纯 braid 群的中心结构。猜想：高阶上同调的中心作用不变量为显式上同调类；且类的表为代表循环。（纯辫中心作用上同调不变量）
