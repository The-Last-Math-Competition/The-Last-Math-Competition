# 00000008146

**RNG-06 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The chain structure of the system of equations for Green functions of the Dyson-Schwinger equations: the leading exponent ds_exp of the asymptotic behavior of solutions of DS chains. Conjecture: ds_exp (the logarithmic growth exponent) is a reduced rational fraction q/r (a rational exponent law); the denominator r is at most 3 (a denominator-3 upper bound); the graph families realizing denominator 3 are ladder-nested hybrid graphs (a ladder-nested hybrid law); and the numerator q is bounded by the loop depth of the perturbative expansion (a loop-depth bound). (Dyson-Schwinger rational ladder-nested exponents)

**中文。** 定义：Dyson–Schwinger 方程的 Green 函数的方程组的链结构：DS 链的解的渐近行为的首指数 ds_exp。猜想：ds_exp（对数增长指数）为有理数 q/r 的既约分数（有理指数律）；且指数的分母 r ≤ 3（分母 3 上界）；分母 3 的实现的图族为"梯-嵌套"混合图（梯嵌套混合律）；指数的分子 q 的上界为微扰展开的圈的深度（圈深界）。
