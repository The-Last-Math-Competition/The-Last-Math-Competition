**English.** Definition: leading exponent ds_exp of asymptotic row is of solution of structure: DS of system of equations of Green function of Dyson–Schwinger equation. Conjecture: ds_exp (logarithm growth exponent) is reduced part number (rational exponent); of rational number q/r and graph family of realized of r ≤ 3 (3 upper bound); 3 of exponent is upper bound of q of " - nested " mixing graph (nested mixing); exponent is depth (bound) of of expansion.

**中文。** 定义：Dyson–Schwinger 方程的 Green 函数的方程组的链结构：DS 链的解的渐近行为的首指数 ds_exp。猜想：ds_exp（对数增长指数）为有理数 q/r 的既约分数（有理指数律）；且指数的分母 r ≤ 3（分母 3 上界）；分母 3 的实现的图族为"梯-嵌套"混合图（梯嵌套混合律）；指数的分子 q 的上界为微扰展开的圈的深度（圈深界）。
