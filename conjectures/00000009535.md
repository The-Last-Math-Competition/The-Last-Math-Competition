# 00000009535

**English.** Definition: Quantum expanders are sequences of quantum channels whose spectral gap (the ratio of the second-largest to largest singular value) is uniformly below 1 - epsilon; random quantum channels are expanders with high probability. Conjecture: There exist polynomial-time computable explicit quantum expander families attaining a (1 - epsilon) fraction of the random spectral gap; and the spectral gap of any explicit construction family is limited by an e^{-Omega(sqrt(log d))} algebraic obstruction, so the explicit-random gap has an exponential lower bound. (quantum expander explicit construction)

**中文。** 定义：量子扩张图是谱隙（次最大奇异值与最大值之比）一致小于 1−ε 的量子信道序列；随机量子信道以高概率为扩张图。猜想：存在多项式时间可计算的显式量子扩张图族达到随机谱隙的 (1−ε) 倍，且任何显式构造族的谱隙受 e^{−Ω(√log d)} 型代数障碍限制，显式-随机差距具指数下界。（量子扩张显式构造）
