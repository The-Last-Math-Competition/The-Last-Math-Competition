# 00000003251

**English.** Definition: Immersions: smooth maps with injective differential. Conjecture: The immersion dimension law: immersions of n-manifolds into 2n-dimensional Euclidean space exist, with the classification of codimension-one immersions the stable isotopy classes of the normal bundle. (Whitney codimension-one immersion normal bundle classification)

**中文。** 定义：浸入（immersion）：切映射单射的微分手。猜想：浸入维数律：n 流形到 2n 维欧氏空间的浸入存在且余一浸入的分类为法丛的稳定同痕类。（Whitney 余一浸入法丛分类）
