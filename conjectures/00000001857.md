# 00000001857

**English.** Definition: The spanning-tree count τ(G) of a random d = 3 regular graph. Conjecture: τ(G) is asymptotically c₃^n (an exponential constant) with c₃ = ((√3−1)/2)²·exp(∫log(3−2cos θ)dθ/4π) (a McKay-type formula); the numerical limit is 1.175…, and the closed form is computable.

**中文。** 定义：随机 d=3 正则图的生成树数 τ(G)。猜想：τ(G) 的渐近为 c₃^n(指数常数),c₃ = ((√3−1)/2)^{2}·exp(∫log(3−2\cos θ)dθ/4π)(McKay– norm 型公式);数值极限为 1.175…,显式闭式可计算。
