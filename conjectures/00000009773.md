# 00000009773

**English.** Definition: The gap of Weyl product inequalities: the realizable range of ratios of eigenvalue products to singular value products. Conjecture: The realizable interval of the ratio of the product of the first n eigenvalue moduli to the product of the first n singular values is closed; interval endpoints are realized explicitly by direct sums of two-dimensional Jordan blocks, and intermediate values by weighted mixtures. (product ratio realizable interval)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl 乘积不等式的间隙：特征值乘积与奇异值乘积的比的可实现范围。猜想：前 n 个特征值模乘积与前 n 个奇异值乘积之比的可实现区间为闭区间；区间端点由二维 Jordan 型块的直和显式实现且中间值由加权混合实现。（乘积比可实现区间）
