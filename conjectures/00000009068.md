# 00000009068

**SMI-28 ★** — source: 287_子流形几何不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Biconservative submanifolds: weak parallelism of the stress-energy tensor. Conjecture: The stress-energy tensor has parallel divergence with finitely many hypersurface classifications; and the parameter of the classification is the sign of scalar curvature. (biconservative stress parallel classification)

**中文。** 定义：双保守子流形为应力能量张量的弱平行。猜想：应力能量张量的散度平行且超曲面分类有限；且分类的参数为数量曲率符号。（biconservative 应力平行分类）
