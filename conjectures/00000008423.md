# 00000008423

**English.** Definition: Subspace designs: the analogue of t-designs on the Grassmann lattice. Conjecture: For every prime power q and t at least 2 there exists a t-subspace design made of k-dimensional subspaces of a v-dimensional space; its minimal block count is at most C binom(v,t) q^{t(t+1)/2}; and at q = 2 the block count of the first nontrivial t = 2 design is an explicit value. (existence bound for subspace designs)

**中文。** 定义：子空间设计指 Grassmann 格上的 t-设计的模拟。猜想：对每个素数幂 q 与 t ≥ 2 存在 v-维空间上由 k-维子空间构成的 t-子空间设计；其最小区组数 ≤ C·binom(v,t)·q^{t(t+1)/2}；q = 2 时第一个非平凡 t = 2 设计的区组数为显式值。（子空间设计存在界）
