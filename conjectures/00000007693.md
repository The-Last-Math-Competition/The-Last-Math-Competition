**English.** Definition: A strongly aperiodic monotile in three-dimensional Euclidean space is a single polyhedron (no reflections allowed) whose translation tiling set is nonempty but contains no periodic tiling. Conjecture: A convex strongly aperiodic monotile exists; and the vertex count of the smallest-volume such block is at most 92, this bound arising from the 3-prismation of the two-dimensional hat tile. (convex strongly aperiodic monotile)

**中文。** 定义：三维欧氏空间中的强非周期单调块指单个多面体（不允许反射）其平移铺砌集非空但不含任何周期铺砌。猜想：存在凸的强非周期单调块；且其中体积最小的块的顶点数不超过 92，该上界由二维帽状瓦片顶点数的 3-棱柱化给出。
