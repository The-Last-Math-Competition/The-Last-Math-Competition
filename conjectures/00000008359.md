# 00000008359

**English.** Definition: The lattice index of tropical Poincare-Hopf: the index ind_trop of zeros of tropical vector fields. Conjecture: ind_trop (the index of a tropical vector field) lies in {0, plus or minus 1} (a three-value law); the sum of squared indices (the modulus of total index) equals the modulus of the Euler characteristic (a modular Euler law); the balance of the index distribution (ratio of positive to negative) is the random 1:1 (a random balance law); and the construction breaking the balance (systematic bias) is monotone vector fields (a monotone bias law), with supremum bias chi/2 (a half supremum law). (tropical Poincare-Hopf three-value monotone bias)

**中文。** 定义：热带 Poincaré–Hopst 的格点指标：热带向量场的零点的指标 ind_trop。猜想：ind_trop（热带向量场的指标）∈{0,±1}（三值律）；且指标的平方和（全指标的模）= Euler 特征的模（模 Euler 律）；指标的分布（正负的比）的平衡为 1:1 的随机（随机平衡律）；平衡的破缺（系统偏置）的构造为单调向量场（单调偏置律），偏置的上确界为 χ/2 的半（半偏置上确界律）。
