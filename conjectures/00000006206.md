**English.** Definition: The asymptotic soliton count is the soliton component count of the solution at infinity. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit constancy of the asymptotic soliton count, the correction given by the scattering coefficient of radiation; the soliton count divides the count of discrete eigenvalues of the spectrum; and the sign of the correction is always negative. (scattering-correction conversion of asymptotic soliton counts)

**中文。** 定义：渐近孤子数：解在无穷远的孤子分量数。猜想：换算常数为渐近孤子数的恒定显式且修正由辐射的散射系数给出；孤子数整除谱的离散特征值数；修正的符号恒为负。（渐近孤子数恒定散射修正换算）
