**LH2-15 ★** — source: 163_范畴化不变量_链接同调.md

**English.** Definition: The bigraded plane is the plane of the two grading variables of the homology. Conjecture: The diagonal slope table at low crossings (at most 12) is an explicit linear expression of the coloring weights, mirror-symmetric (slope negated); the value domain of slopes is finite, constrained by thickness; and slope flipping corresponds one-to-one with flipping knots. (linear table of bigraded diagonal slopes)

**中文。** 定义：双分次平面：同调的两个分次变量的平面。猜想：低交叉（不超过 12）的对角斜率表为染色权的显式线性式且镜像对称（斜率取负）；斜率的取值域为有限且由厚度约束；斜率翻转与翻转纽结的对应为一一对应。（双分次对角斜率线性表）
