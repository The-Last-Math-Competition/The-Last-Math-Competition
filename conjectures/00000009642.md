**HSS-02 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: Central limit theorems for linear statistics sum f(lambda_i): the variance structure was given in general frameworks by Diaconis-Evans and Sinai-Soshnikov. Conjecture: For general bounded-variation test functions the variance is the squared integral of an explicit Sobolev seminorm, with the variance formula uniform over all universality classes of Wigner matrices; and the difference between fourth moment and variance is exactly a single explicit kurtosis correction term. (linear statistic variance formula)

**中文。** 定义：线性统计量 Σ f(λ_i) 的中心极限定理：方差结构由 Diaconis–Evans 与 Sinai–Soshnikov 给出一般框架。猜想：一般有界变差检验函数的方差为显式 Sobolev 半范数的平方积分，方差公式对一切普适类 Wigner 矩阵一致；四次矩与方差之差恰为峰度修正的单项显式公式。（线性统计方差公式）
