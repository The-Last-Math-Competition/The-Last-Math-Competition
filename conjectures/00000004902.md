# 00000004902

**English.** Definition: Policy iteration is the classical algorithm of stepwise policy improvement. Conjecture: Its convergence rate is explicitly controlled by the discounted spectral gap, the per-iteration error reduction factor is an explicit function, and the bound is optimal, attained by two-state examples. (spectral-gap reduction factor of policy iteration)

**中文。** 定义：策略迭代指逐步改进策略的经典算法。猜想：其收敛速率由折扣谱隙显式控制,每次迭代的误差缩减因子为显式函数,界由双状态例达到最优。（策略迭代谱隙缩减因子）
