# 00000007742

**English.** Definition: The Fuglede-Kadison determinant Delta(p)=exp(int log|z| d mu_p) uses the spectral measure of p(s_1,...,s_d); the L^2-torsion tau^{(2)}(G) of a one-relator group G is the alternating sum of L^2-Betti numbers of its chain complex. Conjecture: For one-relator groups with nontrivial reduced relator r, tau^{(2)}(G) = Delta(r), equaling the square root of the Mahler measure M(r) when finite; consequently beta_1^{(2)} = d-1 and all other L^2-Betti numbers vanish. (L2-torsion determinant identity)

**中文。** 定义：多项式 p 的 Fuglede–Kadison 行列式 Δ(p)=exp(∫ log|z| dμ_p)，μ_p 为 p(s_1,…,s_d) 的谱分布；单关系群 G=⟨x_1,…,x_d | r⟩ 的 L²-挠率 τ^{(2)}(G) 由其链复形的 L²-Betti 数交替和给出。猜想：对单关系群（r 非平凡既约）有 τ^{(2)}(G)=Δ(r)，且该值当 r 的 Mahler 测度 M(r) 有限时等于 M(r) 的平方根；由此推出单关系群的 L²-Betti 数满足 β_1^{(2)}=d-1 且其余为零。
