**English.** Definition: The Lyapunov exponents lambda_1 at least ... at least lambda_g of the Kontsevich-Zorich cocycle control Hodge norm growth of cohomology classes along the geodesic flow; simplicity of exponents is proven by Avila-Viana, but specific values are known only for isolated strata. Conjecture: The KZ exponents of all strata are algebraic numbers, explicitly computable by finite combinatorial rules on Forni matrices (local unimodal models near cusps); and the second exponent of H(2) is a quadratic irrational expression involving sqrt 13. (KZ exponent algebraicity)

**中文。** 定义：Kontsevich–Zorich 上闭链的 Lyapunov 指数 λ_1≥…≥λ_g 控制上同调类沿测地流的 Hodge 范数增长；指数的简洁性由 Avila–Viana 证明，但具体数值仅个别 stratum 已知。猜想：一切 stratum 的 KZ 指数皆为代数数，且由 Forni 矩阵（尖点邻域的局部单峰模型）的有限组合规则显式计算；H(2) 的第二指数为含 √13 的二次无理式。（KZ 指数代数性）
