# 00000007922

**ATC-22 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The Hilbert-Samuel function of double-coset Hecke algebras: the Samuel function H(n) of the filtration structure of the local Hecke algebra H(G//K). Conjecture: H(n) is a polynomial of exact degree r (the split rank of the group) with leading coefficient 1/r!; the combinatorial interpretation of H is the integer-point count of the dominant cone of the Cartan decomposition; and the Koszul dual of the associated graded of the filtration is the quantum-group representation ring of the spherical WCW unitary, the two Poincare series being linked by the functional equation p(t) p(t^{-1}) = 1. (Hecke Hilbert-Samuel polynomial law)

**中文。** 定义：双陪集 Hecke 代数的 Hilbert–Samuel 函数：局部 Hecke 代数 H(G//K) 的滤结构的 Samuel 函数 H(n)。猜想：H(n) 为次数 r（群的分裂秩）的多项式，次数精确且首项系数为 1/r!；且 H 的组合解释为 Cartan 分解的支配锥的整点计数；滤子的伴随分次的 Koszul 对偶为球面的 WCW 酉的量子群表示环，两者的 Poincare 级数由函数方程 p(t)·p(t^{-1})=1 相连。
