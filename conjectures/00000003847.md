# 00000003847

**English.** Definition: An m-fold multivariate crystal is a structure on one vertex set with m pairwise-commuting arrow families, each a finite-type crystal. Conjecture: Every finite m-fold multivariate crystal decomposes into a direct sum of highest-weight multivariate crystals, and the multiset of component highest weights is uniquely determined by the joint character of the m arrow families. (multivariate crystal decomposition unique)

**中文。** 定义：m-重多元晶体指同一顶点集上带 m 族两两交换的箭头且每族各自构成有限型晶体的结构。猜想：任何有限 m-重多元晶体可分解为最高权多元晶体的直和,且分量最高权的多重集由 m 族箭头的联合字符唯一决定。（多元晶体分解唯一）
