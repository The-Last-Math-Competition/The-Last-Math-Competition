**English.** Definition: The chromatic stratification is the layering by height, and the thickness spectrum is the thickness classification of the layers. Conjecture: The thickness spectrum of height at most two consists of two explicit chains with endpoints the field spectrum and the sphere spectrum, and the spectrum is complete with no gaps. (complete two chains of the low-height thickness spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：色谱分层指按高度分层,厚度谱指层的厚度分类。猜想：高度不超过二的厚度谱为两条显式链,端点为域谱与球谱且谱无缺口完全。（低高度厚度谱双链完全）
