# 00000000176

**English.** Definition: The ratios ζ(2n+1)/π^{2n+1} of odd zeta values to powers of π. Conjecture: The first three ratios ζ(3)/π³, ζ(5)/π⁵, ζ(7)/π⁷, together with 1, are linearly independent, and the maximal provable length of independence within the prime Euler-product framework is exactly 4; the controlling kernel is the rank of the Möbius inversion matrix of odd zeta values. (independence of odd zeta ratios)

**中文。** 定义：奇 ζ 值与 π 的幂的比值 ζ(2n+1)/π^{2n+1}。猜想：前三个比值 ζ(3)/π³、ζ(5)/π⁵、ζ(7)/π⁷ 连同 1 线性无关，且素数 Euler 积框架内可证无关的极大长度恰为 4；控制核为奇 ζ 值的 Möbius 反演矩阵的秩。（奇 zeta 比值无关组）
