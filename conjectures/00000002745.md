**PI-05 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: GPI (generalized polynomial identities): identities with inverses and traces. Conjecture: The quantitative version of Herstein's structure theorem for GPI of prime algebras: the central extension degree of a GPI algebra is constant, and the degree bound of the rational function field extension is polynomial in the parameters. (GPI central extension bound)

**中文。** 定义：GPI（广义多项式恒等式）：含逆与迹的恒等式。猜想：素代数的 GPI 的 Herstein 结构定理的定量版：GPI 代数的中心扩张次数为常量且有理函数域扩张的次数上界为多项式个参数。（GPI 中心扩张界）
