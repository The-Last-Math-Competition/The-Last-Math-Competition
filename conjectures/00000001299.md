# 00000001299

**CG-99 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: A Ducci sequence is the iteration of taking absolute differences of n-tuples term by term. Conjecture: For n a power of two the sequence reaches zero in finitely many steps, while for non-powers of two nontrivial cycles exist; and the minimal cycle length is an odd factor of n. (Ducci zero criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Ducci 序列为 n 元组逐项取绝对差的迭代。猜想：n 为二的幂时序列在有限步归零且非二幂时存在非平凡环；且环的最小长度为 n 的奇因子。（Ducci 归零判据）
