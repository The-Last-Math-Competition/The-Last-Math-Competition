# 00000008777

**English.** Definition: Randomized eigenvalues: spectral approximation by random algorithms. Conjecture: The error of one-pass spectral approximation is 1 + epsilon (an epsilon law); the two-pass improvement is 1 + epsilon^2 (an epsilon-squared law); the streaming space has a lower bound (a space law); and the sketch size attains the lower bound (a size law). (one-pass epsilon-squared streaming sketch size)

**中文。** 定义：随机特征值：随机算法的谱逼近。猜想：单向（one-pass）谱逼近的误差为 1+ε 的（ε 律）；且双向的改进的 1+ε² 的（ε² 律）；流式（streaming）的空间的下界（空间律）；下界的达到的 sketch 的尺寸（尺寸律）。
