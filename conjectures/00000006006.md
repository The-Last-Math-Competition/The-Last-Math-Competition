**English.** Definition: A fibration is the terminal projection of the canonical ring. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit joint of fiber dimension with base type, unique at rank one; the general-type criterion for fibers is the total dimension minus the base dimension; and the failure of uniqueness appears only in the rank-two product type, with direct-product surfaces as failing examples. (base-type joint unique conversion of fibrations)

**中文。** 定义：纤维化：典范环的终态投射（纤维化结构）。猜想：换算常数为纤维维数与基型的显式联合且秩一时唯一；纤维的一般型判据为母维数减基维数；唯一性的破坏仅出现在秩二的乘积型且破坏例为直积曲面。（纤维基型联合唯一换算）
