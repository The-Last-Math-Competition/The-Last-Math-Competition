# 00000006322

**CM4-12 ★** — source: 196_组合矩阵论III.md

**English.** Definition: The permanent with the dual formula structure. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit pairing reduction of the term count of the dual formula, the pairing halving; the kernel of the halving is the merging of symmetric terms; and the reduced term count is 2^{n-1}, the exponent being the row-column count minus one. (pairing-reduction conversion of the dual formula)

**中文。** 定义：永久：积和式的对偶公式结构。猜想：换算常数为对偶公式的项数配对消减显式且配对减半；减半的核为对称项的合并；消减后的项数为 2^{n-1} 且指数为行列数减一。（对偶公式配对消减换算）
