# 00000002647

**MF-27 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: Inner products of Jacobi forms: the elliptic realization of the Petersson inner product. Conjecture: The elliptic kernel of the Petersson inner product: the kernel is an explicit combination of theta series, its convergence domain controlled by the Hermite constants of the index; the value of the kernel at CM points generates the ramification tower of the class field. (inner product elliptic kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式的内积：Petersson 内积的椭圆侧实现。猜想：Petersson 内积的椭圆核：内积核为 θ 级数的显式组合且组合的收敛域由指标的 Hermite 常数控制；核在 CM 点的值生成类域的分歧塔。（内积椭圆核）
