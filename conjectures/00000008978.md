**ISO-28 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: Ablowitz-Ladik: the integrable discretization of discrete NLS. Conjecture: The norm-repulsive weights of the scattering data are always closed under products; and the combination law of the closure is unitary factorization. (Ablowitz-Ladik scattering weight product closure)

**中文。** 定义：Ablowitz–Ladik 指离散 NLS 的可积离散化。猜想：散射数据的范数斥型权恒在乘积下封闭；且封闭的组合律为酉因子分解。（AL 散射权乘积封闭）
