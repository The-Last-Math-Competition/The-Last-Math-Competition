# 00000004063

**BF2-03 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: A Bogdanov-Takens point is a codimension-two bifurcation point where the Jacobian has a double zero eigenvalue. Conjecture: The first-order Melnikov quantity of the homoclinic curve depends only on the ratio of the quadratic coefficients, and the ratio one quarter separates the two topological classes. (critical ratio 1/4 for BT homoclinics)

**中文。** 定义：Bogdanov–Takens 点指雅可比具双零特征值的余维二分叉点。猜想：同宿曲线的一阶 Melnikov 量恒只依赖二次项系数比；且比值四分之一分隔两类拓扑。（BT 同宿比值临界 1/4）
