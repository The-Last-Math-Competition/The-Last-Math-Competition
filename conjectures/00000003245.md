# 00000003245

**English.** Definition: Boundary value problems on random domains: the spectrum of mixed boundaries. Conjecture: Randomization of boundary values: the spectrum of random boundary values on Lipschitz domains converges uniformly, with convergence rate an explicit power of boundary roughness. (boundary random roughness convergence power)

**中文。** 定义：随机区域的边值问题：混合边界的谱。猜想：边值随机化：Lipschitz 域的随机边值的谱收敛为一致极限且收敛率为边界粗糙度的显式幂。（边值随机粗糙度收敛幂）
