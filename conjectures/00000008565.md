# 00000008565

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of a Cayley graph of a group G: the integrality of adjacency operator eigenvalues as algebraic integers. Conjecture: All Cayley graph spectra consist of algebraic integers (an algebraic integer law); simple spectrum (no multiplicity) is generic for cyclic generating sets of abelian groups (an abelian simple spectrum law); the lower bound on spectral multiplicities for nonabelian groups is the dimension of irreducible representations (a representation dimension lower bound law); and the extremal spectral norm is given by the growth rate of the ball length function of the group (a ball length extremal law). (Cayley spectrum algebraic integrality representation bound)

**中文。** 定义：群 G 的 Cayley 图的谱：邻接算子特征值作为代数整数的整性。猜想：一切 Cayley 图谱为代数整数（代数整数律）；且单重谱（无重数）在阿贝尔群的循环生成集下普遍（Abel 单谱律）；非交换群的谱重数的下界为不可约表示维数（表示维数下界律）；谱范数的极值由群的球长函数增长率给出（球长极值律）。
