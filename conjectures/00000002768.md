**English.** Definition: The second homology of matrix algebras: the White-module structure of H₂(M_n). Conjecture: The dimension formula for H₂: the dimension of the second Hochschild homology of M_n(K) is a counting function of the prime group, explicitly related to the root count of the Weyl group. (second homology dimension formula)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵代数的第二同调：H₂(M_n) 的白模结构。猜想：H₂ 的维数公式：M_n(K) 的第二 Hochschild 同调的维数为素群的计数函数且公式与 Weyl 群的根数显式相关。（二阶同调维数公式）
