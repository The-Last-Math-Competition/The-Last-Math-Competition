# 00000005897

**PH2-07 ★** — source: 182_持久同调II.md

**English.** Definition: Barcode counting is the combinatorial count of possible barcodes. Conjecture: The two-dimensional counting formula is explicit in Young-tableau hook lengths, binomial coefficients for fixed endpoints; log-concavity is preserved for fixed endpoints; and the three-dimensional extension of the hook-length formula is of plane-partition type, with rational generating function. (hook-length barcode counting in two dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：条形码计数：可能条形码的组合数。猜想：二维计数公式为杨表钩长的显式且固定端点时为二项系数；计数的对数凹性在端点固定时保持；钩长公式的三维推广为平面分拆型且分拆的母函数为有理。（二维钩长条形码计数）
