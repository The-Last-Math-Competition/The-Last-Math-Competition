**MF-03 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: Jacobi Eisenstein series: E_{k,m} constructed by lattice sums. Conjecture: The explicit formula for the coefficients of Jacobi Eisenstein series is a convolution of divisor sums with characters; the Hecke-cusp correction coefficients of the convolution are negative, and the corrections appear only at the critical layers of the prime factors of the index. (Jacobi Eisenstein coefficient formula)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi Eisenstein 级数：E_{k,m} 由格和构造。猜想：Jacobi Eisenstein 系数的显式公式为除数和与特征标的卷积；卷积的 Hecke 尖点修正项系数为负且修正仅出现在指标素因子的临界层。（Jacobi Eisenstein 系数公式）
