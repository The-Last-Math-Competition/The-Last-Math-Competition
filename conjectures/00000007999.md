# 00000007999

**DEL-09 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The asymptotic shape of gradient blow-up for the prescribed mean curvature equation: the cone type set K_blow of blow-up limits of solutions at blow-up points. Conjecture: K_blow is a finite list of cones (n+1 types), the types classified by the frequency value at the blow-up point; and the catastrophe set of cone-type stability is a codimension-2 stratified surface; the contact angles on the intersection curves of the stratification satisfy the differential form of Young's identity (conservation of the contact-angle-gradient-curvature triple), the conservation law vanishing logarithmically at corner points. (mean curvature blow-up cone stratification)

**中文。** 定义：平均曲率方程的梯度爆炸的渐近形状： prescribed mean curvature 方程的解在爆破点的 blow-up 极限的锥类型集 K_blow。猜想：K_blow 为有限的锥列表（n+1 个类型），类型由爆破点的频率值分类；且锥类型的稳定性的突变集为余维 2 的分层曲面；分层的交线的接触角满足 Young 恒等式的微分形式（接触角-梯度-曲率三重的守恒），守恒律在角点处的奇性消没为对数型。
