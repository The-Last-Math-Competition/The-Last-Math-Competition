**English.** Definition: Monopole Floer homology is the Floer-type invariant of the SW equations with a module structure over polynomial coefficients. Conjecture: Its grading spectrum is a continuous interval with endpoints given explicitly by Froyshov-type invariants, and the interval length equals the rank of the cone homology. (monopole grading spectrum interval formula)

**中文。** 定义：monopole Floer 同调指 SW 方程的 Floer 型不变量,带多项式系数模结构。猜想：其分级谱为连续区间,端点由 Frøyshov 型不变量显式给出,区间长度等于锥同调的秩。（monopole 分级谱区间公式）
