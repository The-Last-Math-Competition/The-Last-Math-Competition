# 00000006450

**English.** Definition: Coefficient mean squares and the zero spectrum are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two forms with the same mean-square asymptotics but different zero statistics, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same mean square but different symmetric powers. (same mean square-different zero-spectrum separation)

**中文。** 定义：系数均方与零点谱指两层。猜想：存在两形式的均方渐近相同而零点统计不同,分离由同均方异对称幂的显式对实现。（均方同-零点谱异分离）
