# 00000004164

**CM3-14 ◆** — source: 124_解析数论III_圆法.md

**English.** Definition: The prime Waring number G(k) is the least number of variables for representations by sums of k-th powers of primes. Conjecture: For fourth powers G(4) = 15, and with 14 variables there exists a prime-modulus obstruction that makes the representation fail. (WG fourth-power G value 15)

**中文。** 定义：素数 Waring 数 G(k) 指使 k 次幂素数和表示成立的最少变量数。猜想：四位幂情形 G(4) = 15,且 14 个变量时存在模素障碍使表示失败。（WG 四次 G 值 15）
