**English.** Definition: Thickness is the window-width structure of coexisting infinitely many attractors, tangential thickness. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in tangential thickness and the window width of coexisting infinitely many attractors is given by renormalization; the renormalization fixed point of the window width is Feigenbaum type, the type value unique; and the lower bound of the thickness product is 4, attained by affine images. (renormalization window conversion of tangential thickness)

**中文。** 定义：厚度：共存无穷吸引子的窗宽结构（切线厚度）。猜想：换算常数为切线厚度的显式且共存无穷吸引子的窗宽由重整化给出；窗宽的重整化不动点为 Feigenbaum 型且型值唯一；厚度积的下界为 4 且界由仿射像达到。（切线厚度重整化窗宽换算）
