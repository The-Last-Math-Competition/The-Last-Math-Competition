# 00000009651

**English.** Definition: Density regularity of multiplicative free convolution powers: the density shape of the n-th free multiplicative convolution of a measure. Conjecture: The multiplicative convolution powers of a general compactly supported initial measure have analytic densities after k steps, with support of stick-decomposition structure; the required number of steps k is at most C log n, with C explicitly given by the minimal gap of the initial measure. (multiplicative convolution stick regularity)

**中文。** 定义：乘性自由卷积幂的密度正则性：测度的 n 次自由乘积卷积的密度形状。猜想：一般紧支撑初始测度的乘性卷积幂在 k 次后密度解析且支撑呈棍棒分解结构；所需次数 k ≤ C log n 且常数 C 由初始测度的最小间隙显式给出。（乘性卷积棍棒正则）
