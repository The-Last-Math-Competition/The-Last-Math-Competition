**English.** Definition: The minimal dihedral block is the block with defect group the dihedral group of order eight. Conjecture: The invariant conversion formula is explicit as the trunk count plus the extraordinary involution count, given at the three subtypes; the three subtypes are separated by the extraordinary index; and the sum of the invariants is the constant 6, the constant being the logarithm of the block-algebra dimension. (three-subtype invariants of the minimal dihedral block)

**中文。** 定义：最小二面体块：八阶二面体亏群的块。猜想：不变量换算公式为树干数加非凡对合数的显式且三子型处给出；三子型由非凡指标分离；不变量的和为常数 6 且常数为块代数维数的对数。（最小二面体三子型不变量）
