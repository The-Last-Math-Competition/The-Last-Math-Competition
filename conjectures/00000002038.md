# 00000002038

**AD-18 △** — source: 53_加性数论II.md

**English.** Conjecture: The set of squared differences of primes {p − p' = n²} is infinite (prime squared differences); and the construction is given by Rankin–Selberg pairs of quadratic twists of elliptic curve families.

**中文。** 猜想：模移位的平方差集合 {p − p' = n²} 为无穷(素数平方差)；且构造由椭圆曲线族的二次扭的 Rankin-Selberg 对给出。
