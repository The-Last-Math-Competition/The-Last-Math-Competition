# 00000009877

**KPU-27 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: 2+1-dimensional directed polymers: the exponent structure near the critical dimension. Conjecture: The drift and overlap exponents of 2+1-dimensional directed polymers satisfy the KPZ relation chi = 2 xi - 1, and both exponents are numerically exact to two decimal places in 2+1 dimensions; the straight-line relation holds strictly in all dimensions. (polymer KPZ straight-line relation)

**中文。** 定义：2+1 维定向聚合物：临界维数附近的指数结构。猜想：2+1 维定向聚合物的漂移指数与重叠指数满足 KPZ 关系 χ=2ξ−1 且两指数在 2+1 维的数值精确到百分位；直线关系在一切维数严格成立。（聚合物 KPZ 直线关系）
