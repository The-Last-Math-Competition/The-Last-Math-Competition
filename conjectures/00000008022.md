**English.** Definition: The exponential tower height of o-minimal structures: the transcendence base h_exp of the structure R_exp (the model completion of real exponentiation), the minimal tower height of transcendental functions needed for the definition. Conjecture: h_exp = 1 (single-layer rigidity of the exponential function); the epsilon-exponent of the Pila bound for the counting function of R_exp admits a logarithmic correction of 1/2 (a special bound for the exponential field); and the converse of Schanuel's conjecture (SCH) implying o-minimality of R_exp (known) fails: o-minimality of R_exp plus power rigidity does not imply SCH (independence, separated by a compactification construction of Zilber pseudo-power models). (R_exp single tower height independence)
