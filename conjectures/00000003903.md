**CA3-23 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: toric F- point denotes such that corresponds to toric ring is parameter lattice point of F-. Conjecture: F- lattice point counting of polytope is polynomial, period is most small common multiple, of vertex coordinates and and Ehrhart polynomial difference pointwise nonnegative. (toric F- polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：toric F-纯点指使对应 toric 环为 F-纯的参数格点。猜想：多面体的 F-纯格点计数为拟多项式,周期为顶点坐标分母的最小公倍数,且与 Ehrhart 拟多项式之差逐点非负。（toric F-纯拟多项式）
