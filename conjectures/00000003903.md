# 00000003903

**English.** Definition: A toric F-pure point is a parameter lattice point for which the corresponding toric ring is F-pure. Conjecture: The number of F-pure lattice points of a polytope is a quasi-polynomial whose period is the least common multiple of the denominators of the vertex coordinates, and its difference from the Ehrhart quasi-polynomial is pointwise nonnegative. (toric F-pure quasi-polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：toric F-纯点指使对应 toric 环为 F-纯的参数格点。猜想：多面体的 F-纯格点计数为拟多项式,周期为顶点坐标分母的最小公倍数,且与 Ehrhart 拟多项式之差逐点非负。（toric F-纯拟多项式）
