# 00000004935

**English.** Definition: Morava K-theory is the formal group cohomology K(n) of height n, together with the local sphere homotopy groups. Conjecture: The local sphere homotopy groups of height two and prime three form finite sets with elements labeled explicitly by invariants of the Picard group action; the positive period is the explicit constant of type 2 * 3^{n+1}; and the minimal stem of a nonzero element is exactly 3. (invariant table of K_2-local three-primary homotopy)

**中文。** 定义：Morava K 理论：高度 n 的形式群上同调 K(n)；局部球面同伦群。猜想：高度二素三的局部球面同伦群为有限集且元素由 Picard 群作用的不变量显式标注；正周期为 2·3^{n+1} 型显式常数；非零元素的最小 stem 恰为 3。（K2 局部三进同伦不变量表）
