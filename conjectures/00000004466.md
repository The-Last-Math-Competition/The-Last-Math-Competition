# 00000004466

**English.** Definition: The Riemann-Hilbert problem of GMN reconstructs the monodromy representation from Stokes data. Conjecture: The Stokes constants are DT-type integers, the asymptotic prefactor of the unique solution is an explicit product of Gamma functions, and the leading constant is of Euler-constant type. (GMN Stokes Gamma prefactor)

**中文。** 定义：GMN 的黎曼–希尔伯特问题指由 Stokes 数据重建单值表示。猜想：Stokes 常数为 DT 型整数,唯一解的渐向前因子为 Gamma 函数显式积,首常数为 Euler 常数型。（GMN Stokes Gamma 前因子）
