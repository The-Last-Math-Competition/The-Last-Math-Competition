**GA5-29 ★** — source: 239_几何代数III.md

**English.** Definition: Basis changes as orthogonal-basis regrouping structure. Conjecture: The rotor realization of basis changes is unique; the exception of uniqueness is orientation reversal; and the exception of orientation reversal is mirror composition. (rotor-unique basis-change realization)

**中文。** 定义：基底变换为正交基重组结构。猜想：基变换的转子实现唯一；且唯一的例外为定向反转；且定向反转的例外为镜像复合。（基变换转子唯一实现）
