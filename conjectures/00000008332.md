# 00000008332

**English.** Definition: The effective version of the Schmidt subspace theorem: the constant ss_eff of effective bounds for the exceptional subspaces. Conjecture: ss_eff (the constant of the effective subspace theorem) is polynomial (a polynomial constant law); the explicit shape of the constant (depending on d,n,epsilon) is a tower lower bound d^{O(n)} (a tower lower bound law); the application to S-unit equations (explicit solution-count bounds) gives the double exponential exp(exp(C |S|)) (a double-exponential solution count); and the tightening to actual counts is cubic |S|^{3+o(1)} (a cubic tightening law). (effective subspace tower double-exponential cubic)

**中文。** 定义：Schmidt 子空间定理的有效化：子空间定理的例外子空间的有效界的常数 ss_eff。猜想：ss_eff（有效子空间定理的常数）为多项式（多项式常数律）；且常数的显式（依赖 d, n, ε）的形状为 d^{O(n)} 的塔下界（塔下界律）；有效化的应用（S-单位方程的解数上界的显式化）的解数的显式为 exp(exp(C·|S|)) 的双指数（双指数解数）；双指数的紧化（实际解数）为 |S|^{3+o(1)} 的三次（三次紧化律）。
