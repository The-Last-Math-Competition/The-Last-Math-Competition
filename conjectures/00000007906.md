# 00000007906

**English.** Definition: The shape parameters of Taylor-Wiles systems: the dimension spectrum d(R) of the tangent space of the deformation ring in a lifting system R to T of automorphic Galois representations under p-local conditions. Conjecture: d(R) equals the Selmer group dimension of the deformation problem, with d(R) = 2 at unramified representations (minimal); and rigidification of the lifting (existence of a rigidified base point) makes the Fitting length of the Eisenstein ideal I_e equal to the order of the Selmer group of modular forms, with both constant values given by the interpolation formula of the p-adic L-function of Dirichlet characters (the dimension of the main conjecture). (Taylor-Wiles Selmer dimension identity)

**中文。** 定义：Taylor–Wiles 系的形状参数：在 p-局部条件下自守 Galois 表示的提升系 R→T 的形变环切空间的维数谱 d(R)。猜想：d(R) 为形变问题的 Selmer 群维数，满足 d(R)=2 在非分歧表示处（极小）；且提升的刚性化（刚性化基点的存在）使得 Eisenstein 理想 I_e 的 Fitting 长度等于模形式的 Selmer 群阶，两边的常数值由 Dirichlet 特征的 p-adic L 函数的插值公式给出（Main conjecture 的量纲）。
