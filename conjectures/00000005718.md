# 00000005718

**English.** Definition: The thin obstacle problem constrains only on a lower-dimensional set, of Signorini type. Conjecture: The three-dimensional regularity is the explicit combination of normal half-power plus tangential logarithm, simplifying to pure half-power at degenerate points; the optimal regularity exponent is 1/2 plus a logarithmic correction; and the coefficient of the correction is a functional of the contact-set density, bounded. (half-power logarithmic regularity combination of thin obstacles)

**中文。** 定义：薄障碍：约束仅在低维集合上的障碍问题（ Signorini 型）。猜想：三维的正则性表为法向半幂加切向对数的显式组合且退化点处化简为纯半幂；最优正则指数为 1/2 加对数修正；修正项的系数为接触集密度的泛函且泛函有界。（薄障碍半幂对数正则组合）
