**English.** Definition: The minimal form factor is the pole-free solution. Conjecture: Its formula is the explicit integral expression of the two-body scattering amplitude, unique by the positivity of the kernel, with closed form at the self-dual point. (unique closed form of the minimal form-factor integral)
