# 00000000731

**English.** Definition: A tower of multiple Z_p-extensions has a two-dimensional Galois group. Conjecture: The main term of the class-number growth is exactly a linear combination of the two-variable λ; the coefficients are given by the ranks of the non-cyclotomic branches; and the stability of the coefficients is monotone across the levels of the tower. (two-variable lambda explicit dominance)

**中文。** 定义：多重 ℤ_p-扩张的塔具二维伽罗瓦群。猜想：类数递增的主项恰为双变量 λ 的线性组合；且系数由不定分支的秩给出。；且系数的稳定性在塔的层间单调。（双变量 λ 显式主导）
