**English.** Definition: Lie sphere geometry: the sphere manifolds of contact Lie groups. Conjecture: The adaptivity of Lie transformations: the complete integral of third-order contact conditions is a curvature-type equation, with the closure of the integral the Darboux system. (Lie sphere Darboux closure)
