# 00000006276

**English.** Definition: The finitary formula gives the tail law of code lengths. Conjecture: Binary systems have exponential tails with exact coefficient, the combination of exactness being balanced bits, and the combination of balanced bits being symmetric prefix codes. (balanced-bit coefficient of code-length tails)

**中文。** 定义：有限性公式的码长尾律。猜想：二进系统尾部指数且系数精确；且精确的组合为平衡位；且平衡位的组合为对称前缀码。（码长尾指数平衡位系数）
