# 00000009743

**English.** Definition: BBP-type digit extraction formulas: direct computation of the n-th digit of a series without earlier digits. Conjecture: A constant xi has a base-b BBP-type extraction formula if and only if xi is a hypergeometric term sum whose term ratios mod b satisfy the square-factor condition on denominators; the criterion is a finite computation covering all known examples. (BBP existence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：BBP 型位提取公式：级数第 n 位的直接计算不需求前面位。猜想：常数 ξ 有 b 进制 BBP 型提取公式当且仅当 ξ 为超几何项和且项比值模 b 的分母结构满足平方因子条件；判据为有限计算且覆盖一切已知例子。（BBP 存在性判据）
