# 00000008501

**English.** Definition: A Picard-Vessiot extension is the minimal differential field extension (with algebraically closed constant field) of a linear differential system y' = Ay, whose differential Galois group is G. Conjecture: Solvability of the identity component of G is equivalent to Liouville solvability of the equation; in the solvable case the minimal number of exponential-integral terms in the expression of the solutions equals the rank of the character group of G, and that rank does not exceed the order of the matrix A. (Liouville criterion and character rank)

**中文。** 定义：Picard–Vessiot 扩张指线性微分方程组 y′=Ay 的最小微分域扩张（常域代数闭），其微分 Galois 群为 G。猜想：G 的恒等连通分量可解等价于方程的 Liouville 可解性；可解情形解的表达中指数积分项的极小个数等于 G 的特征标群的秩，且该秩不超过矩阵 A 的阶。（Liouville 判据与特征标秩）
