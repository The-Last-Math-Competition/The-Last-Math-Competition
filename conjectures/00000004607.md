# 00000004607

**English.** Definition: The isoperimetric profile is the minimal interface area needed to enclose a given volume. Conjecture: Its concavity correction is an explicit nonnegative function of the volume, vanishing only at zero and at the total volume, and the function is convex and symmetric. (double-zero concavity correction of the isoperimetric profile)

**中文。** 定义：等周函数指隔出给定体积所需的最小界面积。猜想：其凹性修正为体积的显式非负函数,仅在零与全体积两点为零且函数凸对称。（等周凹性修正双零点）
