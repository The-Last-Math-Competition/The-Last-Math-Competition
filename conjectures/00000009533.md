# 00000009533

**QIC-13 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: The partial transpose map Gamma acts on channel outputs giving T_phi(rho) = (phi(rho))^Gamma; if T_phi is positive the channel phi is PPT-binding, and all its transmitted states are PPT. Conjecture: The quantum capacity upper bound Q at most log d_min given by PPT constraints has, at dimension d = 5, channel instances strictly below all known single-letter bounds; and the maximal growth of this PPT binding gap with dimension is Theta(log log d), a double-logarithmic barrier that cannot be exceeded. (PPT capacity bound)

**中文。** 定义：部分转置映射 Γ 对信道输出作用得 T_φ(ρ)=(φ(ρ))^Γ；若 T_φ 为正映射则称 φ 为 PPT-束缚信道，其通过态均为 PPT。猜想：PPT 约束给出的量子容量上界 Q≤log d_min 在维数 d=5 存在严格小于一切已知单字母界的信道实例；该 PPT 束缚间隙随维数的最大增长为 Θ(log log d)，双对数壁垒不可超越。（PPT 容量界）
