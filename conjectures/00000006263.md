# 00000006263

**EG3-13 ◆** — source: 194_遍历论III_熵公式.md

**English.** Definition: The entropy gap is the difference of topological and measure entropy. Conjecture: The gap-existence criterion is given by the repulsiveness of critical orbits, the combination of the criterion being expanding points; the measure of expanding points is positive if and only if the gap is zero; and the lower bound of the gap is the logarithm of the expansion rate, attained on Markov systems. (critical expanding-point criterion of the entropy gap)

**中文。** 定义：熵缺口：拓扑熵与测度熵之差。猜想：缺口存在判据由临界轨道排斥性给出且判据的组合为膨胀点；膨胀点的测度正当且仅当缺口为零；缺口的下界为膨胀率的对数且界在 Markov 系统达到。（缺口临界膨胀点判据）
