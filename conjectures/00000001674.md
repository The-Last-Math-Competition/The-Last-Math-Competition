# 00000001674

**GT-14 ★** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: The grid theorem bounds the number of edges of graphs excluding a k×k grid minor. Conjecture: The optimal bound 2^{O(k log k)} cannot be improved to a polynomial; that is, there exist graphs excluding the k×k grid with at least k²·log^{(1−o(1))k} edges — the logarithmic factor is essential.

**中文。** 定义：网格定理指排除 k×k 网格子式的图的边数上界。猜想：最优界为 2^{O(k log k)} 不可改进为多项式;即存在排除 k×k 网格但边数 ≥ k^{2}·log^{(1−o(1))k} 的图族——对数因子为本质。
