# 00000006396

**MX2-26 ★** — source: 198_有限元III_混合元.md

**English.** Definition: Spurious modes with the pseudoeigenvalue structure of spectral elements and the frequency window. Conjecture: The window ends are given by the mesh scale and the closed window is the diameter square; the diameter-square window agrees with the reciprocal wavelength; and the spurious-mode count is at most the boundary degrees of freedom. (closed mesh-endpoint pseudofrequency window)

**中文。** 定义：伪模式：谱元的伪特征值结构；频率窗。猜想：窗端由网格尺度给出且封闭的窗为直径平方；直径平方的窗与波长倒数一致；伪模式的个数不超过边界自由度。（伪频率窗网格端点封闭）
