**English.** Definition: An exceptional collection is a sequentially strong generating sequence with pairwise vanishing extensions. Conjecture: The minimal exceptional generation table of Fano threefold hypersurfaces is given by an explicit list of generators of the residual category, with cubic hypersurfaces generated by four objects; the lower bound of the object count is the motive rank; and the generation numbers of the low-degree table increase linearly with increment exactly 1. (minimal exceptional generation table of Fanos)
