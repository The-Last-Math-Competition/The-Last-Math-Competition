# 00000001467

**CX-17 ◆** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The diameter of a polytope graph is the diameter of its vertex-adjacency graph. Conjecture: The graph diameter of 0/1-polytopes (vertex coordinates in {0,1}) is at most 2n − d (n vertices, dimension d); beyond the general counterexamples to the Hirsch conjecture, the 0/1 case has a strictly linear bound in n with optimal coefficient 2.

**中文。** 定义：多胞体图的直径指其顶点邻接图的直径。猜想：0/1-多胞体(顶点坐标 ∈{0,1})的图直径 ≤ 2n−d(n 顶点维数 d);Hirsch 猜想的一般反例之外,0/1 情形的线性界为 n 的严格线性,系数 2 最优。
