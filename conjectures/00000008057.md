**English.** Definition: The bridge of ordinal analysis of Turing degrees: the Spector bound sp(T) of computable approximations of well-founded relations (the admissible-set level of computable approximations to the ordinal strength of T). Conjecture: The alpha of the minimal admissible set L_alpha of sp(T) is the Sigma_1 stabilization of |T| (a fixed-point law); the enumeration of provable generics for Sigma_1-soundness of T is alpha-recursive but not recursive (a strict separation); and the minimal theory where the exchange diagram between the admissible hierarchy and the proof hierarchy (alpha versus |T|) fails is Delta-1-2-CA_0 (the exceptionality of the second bounding theory). (Spector admissible fixed-point stabilization)
