# 00000005187

**English.** Definition: Strong exceptionality means total vanishing of higher extensions of exceptional objects. Conjecture: The strength criterion converts from vanishing of second-order extensions to an explicit nondegeneracy condition of a quadratic pairing, complete for quadric bundles and computable. (quadratic nondegeneracy criterion of strong exceptionality)

**中文。** 定义：强例外指高阶扩张全消没的例外对象。猜想：强性判据由二阶扩张消没换算为二次配对非退化的显式条件,二次曲面簇完全且可计算。（强例外二次非退化判据）
