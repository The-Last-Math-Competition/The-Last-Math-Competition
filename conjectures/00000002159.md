# 00000002159

**English.** Definition: R(C₄, K_n) is the off-diagonal Ramsey number (no C₄ or a K_n). Conjecture: R(C₄,K_n) = (n−1)²/(2log n) + O(n²/log² n); the second-term coefficient is 1/(4log² n) (from the deletion correction of random graphs).

**中文。** 定义：R(C₄, K_n) 指对角 Ramsey 数(无 C₄ 或有 K_n)。猜想：R(C₄,K_n) = (n-1)^2/(2log n)+O(n^2/log^2 n);第二项系数为 1/(4log^2 n)(由随机图的 deletion 修正)。
