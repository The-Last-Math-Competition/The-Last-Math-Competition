**English.** Definition: Crystal string lengths are the string structure of the crystal basis. Conjecture: The round-off formula of string lengths is exact and the verification of closure is the local rule; the closure of local rules is completed on affine diagrams; and the parity of string lengths is controlled by the weight difference. (exact closed round-off structure of string lengths)
