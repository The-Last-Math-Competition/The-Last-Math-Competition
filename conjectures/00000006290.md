# 00000006290

**NK2-10 △** — source: 195_代数K理论III_非交换.md

**English.** Definition: Zeroth and first groups are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two rings with isomorphic zeroth groups but non-isomorphic first groups, and the separation is realized by an explicit ring pair with the same projection lattice but different elementary groups. (same zeroth-different first ring-pair separation)

**中文。** 定义：零阶与一阶群指两层。猜想：存在两环的零阶群同构而一阶群不同构,分离由同投影格异初等群的显式双环实现。（零阶同-一阶异双环分离）
