# 00000002145

**English.** Definition: ACM trees with minimal-polynomial multiplicities. Conjecture: Non-ACM trees have density tending to one among randomly labeled trees; and the kernel of the tendency is the descent transformation. (ACM descent transformation random-labeling density tends to one)

**中文。** 定义：绝对最小多项式重数的 ACM 树。猜想：非 ACM 树在随机标记树中密度趋一；且趋一的核为下降变换。（ACM 下降变换随机标记密度趋一）
