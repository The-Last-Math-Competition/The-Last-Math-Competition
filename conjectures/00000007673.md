# 00000007673

**English.** Definition: Askey-Wilson polynomials p_n(x;a,b,c,d|q) are orthogonal on [-1,1] with weight w(x); the logarithmic potential of zeros is U_n(z)=sum log|z-x_{n,k}|/n. Conjecture: The limit equilibrium constant, the monotone constant of the harmonic conjugate of U_n outside the zero set, is transcendental when a,b,c,d,q are all algebraic, except in degenerate cases where the potential is a quadratic polynomial. (transcendent equilibrium constant of Askey-Wilson zeros)

**中文。** 定义：Askey–Wilson 多项式 p_n(x;a,b,c,d|q) 关于权函数 w(x) 正交于 [-1,1]。其零点 x_{n,k} 的对数势 U_n(z)=Σ_k log|z-x_{n,k}|/n 在 n→∞ 时收敛到平衡测度的势。猜想：极限平衡常数（即 U_n 在零点集外的调和共轭的单调化常数）当 a,b,c,d,q 均为代数数时为超越数，除非退化情形使势函数为二次多项式。
