# 00000003191

**English.** Definition: Gröbner bases: reduced standard bases of polynomial ideals. Conjecture: The degree growth of Gröbner bases: the degree upper bound for generic coordinates is double exponential, with the average case a single-exponential gap of d^{O(n)}. (Groebner average single-exponential gap)

**中文。** 定义：Gröbner 基：多项式理想的约化标准基。猜想：Gröbner 的度增长：一般坐标的度上界为双重指数且平均情形为 d^{O(n)} 的单指数间隙。（Groebner 平均单指数间隙）
