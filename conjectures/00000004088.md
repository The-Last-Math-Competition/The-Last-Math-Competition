# 00000004088

**English.** Definition: A stochastic bifurcation is the displacement of a deterministic bifurcation point caused by noise. Conjecture: At small noise intensity the expected displacement of the Hopf point is a constant multiple of the square of the noise intensity, the constant being given explicitly by a bilinear expression in the cubic coefficients of the vector field and the noise covariance. (displacement constant of the stochastic Hopf bifurcation)

**中文。** 定义：随机分叉指噪声引起的确定性分叉点位移。猜想：小噪声强度下 Hopf 点位移期望为噪声强度平方的常数倍,常数由向量场三次系数与噪声协方差的双线性式显式给出。（随机 Hopf 位移常数）
