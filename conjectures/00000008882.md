# 00000008882

**English.** Definition: Subtract-a-square: the subtraction game of squares. Conjecture: The density of P-positions is zero and the zero density is strict; and the kernel of strictness is the growth of square gaps. (subtract-square gap strict zero density)

**中文。** 定义：subtract-a-square 为减平方游戏。猜想：P-位置的密度为零且零密度严格；且严格的核为平方间隔增长。（减平方平方间隔零密度严格）
