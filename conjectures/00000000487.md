# 00000000487

**SA-87 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: ‖v_μ‖ is the Euclidean norm of the conjugacy-class column vector v_μ = (χ_λ(μ))_λ of the character table. Conjecture: When μ ranges uniformly over partitions of n, ‖v_μ‖²/z_μ (with z_μ = ∏m_i!·i^{m_i}) concentrates around the constant 1 with variance O(n^{-1/2}); the concentration of column norms directly reflects the Plancherel structure of the character table.

**中文。** 定义：特征标表的共轭类列向量 v_μ=(χ_λ(μ))_λ 的 Euclid 范数 ‖v_μ‖。猜想：‖v_μ‖²/z_μ(其中 z_μ=∏m_i!·i^{m_i})在 μ 均匀遍历 n 的分拆时集中于常数 1,且方差为 O(n^{-1/2});列范数的集中是特征标表的 Plancherel 结构的直接反映。
