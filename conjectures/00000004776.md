**English.** Definition: Free determinacy is the property that the distribution determines the conjugacy class of an operator. Conjecture: The attainable domain of its propagation deficit is an explicit staircase of rank differences, the staircase closes at rank two and is closed for seminormal operators. (staircase domain of free determinacy deficits)

**中文。** 定义：自由决定性指由分布决定算子共轭类的性质。猜想：其传播亏值的可达域为秩差的显式阶梯,阶梯在秩二闭合且对半正规算子封闭。（自由决定亏值阶梯域）
