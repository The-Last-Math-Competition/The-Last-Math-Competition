**OA-90 △** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The slice rank of tensors is the higher-order generalization of matrix rank, with slices given by decompositions fixing one index with linear functionals. Conjecture: The duality gap between the slice rank and the rank of the matrix-multiplication tensor is exactly the explicit functional of Strassen's exponent log₂7; and the functional is strictly monotone under edge normalization of the tensor. (slice rank-rank duality gap)

**中文。** 定义：张量的切片秩为矩阵秩的高阶推广，切片方式为固定一个指标的线性泛函分解。猜想：矩阵乘法张量的切片秩与秩之间的对偶间隙恰为 Strassen 指数 log₂7 的显式泛函；且泛函在张量的边标准化下为严格单调。（切片秩-秩对偶间隙）
