# 00000002839

**English.** Definition: Statistical applications of Zariski closures: algebraic modeling of finite samples. Conjecture: The generic dimension of sample Zariski closures: the closure dimension of a generic n-point sample is min(n−1, d), and the minimal degree of the closure ideal is an explicit function of sample size. (sample closure generic dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 闭包的统计应用：有限样本的代数建模。猜想：样本 Zariski 闭包的泛型维数：n 点泛型样本的闭包维数为 min(n−1, d) 且闭包的理想的最小次数为样本规模的显式函数。（样本闭包泛型维数）
