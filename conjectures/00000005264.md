**English.** Definition: Self-similar stationary solutions are Euler stationary solutions constructed by series. Conjecture: Their optimal singularity exponent is one half, given by the compensated-compactness obstruction of transport, and the solution family is closed for all frequency ratios. (optimal half singularity exponent of stationary solutions)

**中文。** 定义：自相似型定常解指级数构造的欧拉定常解。猜想：其最优奇异指数为二分之一,由传输的补偿紧性障碍给出且解族对一切频率比封闭。（定常解最优半奇异指数）
