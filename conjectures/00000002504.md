# 00000002504

**English.** Definition: Uniqueness of tensor decompositions: identifiability. Conjecture: Generic identifiability is governed by the Kruskal condition: the CP decomposition of a general tensor of rank r is unique when the Kruskal ranks satisfy k₁+k₂+k₃ ≥ 2r+2. (tensor decomposition uniqueness Kruskal condition)
