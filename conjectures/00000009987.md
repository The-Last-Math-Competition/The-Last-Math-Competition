# 00000009987

**NCR-17 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Period and index of Azumaya algebras: invariants of central simple algebras over schemes. Conjecture: The index of an Azumaya algebra on a three-dimensional smooth scheme is controlled by the square of the period, and the power bound is tight; tightness is given by explicit computation of the differential lengths of the Gersten spectral sequence. (three-dimensional period-index square bound)

**中文。** 定义：Azumaya 代数的周期与指标：概形上中心单代数的不变量。猜想：三维光滑概形上的 Azumaya 代数的指标被周期的平方控制且方幕界为紧；紧性由 Gersten 谱序列的微分长度的显式计算给出。（三维周期-指标平方界）
