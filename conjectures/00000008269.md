# 00000008269

**English.** Definition: The gap between expectation propagation (EP) and variational methods: the gap ep_gap between EP fixed points and the variational free-energy minimum. Conjecture: ep_gap (the energy difference between EP and variational) is nonnegative (a variational lower bound law); the zeros of the gap (EP equals variational) form the Gaussian class (a Gaussian equivalence law); the family of distributions maximizing the gap (worst EP) is bimodal mixtures (a bimodal worst law); and the contraction rate of EP convergence (contraction of message updates) is 1 - kappa in information-geometric terms (an information contraction law). (EP variational gap Gaussian bimodal worst)

**中文。** 定义：期望传播（EP）与变分的间隙：EP 的固定点与变分自由能的极小的间隙 ep_gap。猜想：ep_gap（EP 与变分的能量差）≥ 0（变分下界律）；且间隙的零点（EP=变分）的高斯类（高斯等价律）；间隙的最大（最坏 EP）的分布族为双峰混合（双峰最坏律）；EP 的收敛（消息更新的收缩）的收缩率为 1-κ 的信息几何（信息收缩律）。
