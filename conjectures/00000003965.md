# 00000003965

**G4-25 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The boxicity of a graph is the minimal dimension in which the graph is represented as an intersection of interval boxes. Conjecture: Every graph G satisfies |box(G) - box(complement of G)| <= 2 with respect to its complement, and the difference 2 is attained by an explicit construction. (complement box-distance upper bound 2)

**中文。** 定义：boxicity 指图表示为区间盒交的最小维数。猜想：任意图 G 与其补图的盒子性满足 |box(G) − box(Ḡ)| ≤ 2,且差 2 由显式构造达到。（补图盒距上界 2）
