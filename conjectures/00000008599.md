# 00000008599

**MLC-09 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: Honest confidence sets are sets with uniform coverage probability. Conjecture: The optimal radius of honest confidence sets always exceeds the estimation rate by n^{-1/2}; and the constant of the difference is given by the least favorable Bayes prior. (honest confidence radius deficit)

**中文。** 定义：诚实置信集指覆盖概率一致的集合。猜想：诚实置信集的最优半径与估计速率恒差 n 的负二分之一次幂；且差的常数由最不利 Bayes 先验给出。（诚实置信半径亏）
