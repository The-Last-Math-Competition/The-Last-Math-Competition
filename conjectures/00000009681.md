# 00000009681

**English.** Definition: The count of S-integer solutions of the S-unit equation x + y = 1 over number fields: Evertse gave exponential bounds. Conjecture: The solution count of S-unit equations has the polynomial bound (|S| d)^C; the polynomial exponent C = 2 is optimal, attained by constructions of degenerate S sets. (S-unit polynomial bound)

**中文。** 定义：S 单位方程 x+y=1 在数域 S 整数解的个数：Evertse 给出指数型界。猜想：S 单位方程的解数有多项式界 (|S|·d)^{C}；多项式指数 C=2 为最优，由退化 S 集合的构造达到。（S 单位多项式界）
