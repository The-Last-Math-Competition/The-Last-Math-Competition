**English.** Definition: Acylindrical hyperbolicity: the universal framework of weak hyperbolicity. Conjecture: The obstruction to non-acylindricality: a group is acylindrically hyperbolic if and only if it has no virtually cyclic exceptional structure, and the elimination of the obstruction is the count of general-position elements. (acylindrical exception elimination count)
