# 00000004221

**English.** Definition: The combinatorial Hodge decomposition is the orthogonal decomposition of the cochain space into kernel and image of the Laplacian. Conjecture: The spectral gap of the k-coboundary Laplacian has a lower bound that is an explicit function of homotopy invariants, and the bound is optimal, attained by simplicial sphere triangulations. (combinatorial Hodge spectral gap lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：组合 Hodge 分解指上链空间按拉普拉斯核与像的正交分解。猜想：k-上边缘拉普拉斯的谱隙下界为同伦不变量的显式函数,界由单纯球面剖分达到最优。（组合 Hodge 谱隙下界）
