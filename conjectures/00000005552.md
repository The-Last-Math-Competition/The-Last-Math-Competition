**English.** Definition: The root-of-unity conversion is the p-adic valuation of characters. Conjecture: The structure of the conversion is always given by the cyclotomic decomposition of the Frobenius characteristic polynomial; it is closed with components in bijection when the weights are uniform. (cyclotomic decomposition at roots of unity)

**中文。** 定义：单位根换算指特征标的 p 进值化。猜想：换算的结构恒由 Frobenius 特征多项式的分圆分解给出；权一致时分解封闭且分量一一对应。（单位根分圆分解）
