# 00000008305

**English.** Definition: The Piunikhin-Salamon-Schwarz map pss_iso on closed symplectic manifolds: the ring-level map from quantum homology QH to Hamilton Floer homology HF. Conjecture: pss_iso is a ring isomorphism on every closed symplectic manifold; in the induced cup product spectrum, the weights of minimal nonzero quantum length are given by two-point Gromov-Witten invariants and form a finite set; and the Massey corrections of higher A-infinity multiplications have rational coefficients whose denominators divide the least common multiple of the action spectrum of periodic orbits. (PSS ring isomorphism Masley rational denominators)

**中文。** 定义：闭辛流形上 Piunikhin–Salamon–Schwarz 映射 pss_iso：量子同调 QH 到 Hamilton Floer 同调 HF 的环层映射。猜想：pss_iso 在一切闭辛流形上为环同构；其诱导 cup 积谱中极小非零量子长度的权重由两点 Gromov–Witten 不变量给出且权重集为有限；高阶 A 无穷乘法的 Massey 修正具有有理系数且分母整除周期轨作用量谱的最小公倍数。
