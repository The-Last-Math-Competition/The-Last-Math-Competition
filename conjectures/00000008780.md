# 00000008780

**English.** Definition: The structured random Fourier transform kernel SRFT (Rokhlin-Tygert type): the downsampled projection of the form A Pi F D, with Pi a random sign diagonal matrix, F the discrete Fourier matrix, and D column selection. Conjecture: SRFT approximates matrix multiplication and least squares at O(nd log d) complexity, with relative spectral-norm error at most (1 + sqrt(r/m)) times the Gaussian projection error with probability at least 0.99; the approximation quality is equivalent to the fully Gaussian kernel in the second-moment sense; and any random kernel preserving the fast-transform structure requires at least logarithmic-in-r seed bits, attained exactly by the diagonal sign design of SRFT. (SRFT Fourier sign seed entropy bound)

**中文。** 定义：结构随机傅里叶变换核 SRFT（Rokhlin–Tygert 型）：形如 AΠFD 的下采样投影，Π 为随机符号对角阵、F 为离散傅里叶矩阵、D 为列抽取。猜想：SRFT 以 O(nd log d) 复杂度逼近矩阵乘法与最小二乘，谱范数相对误差以概率至少 0.99 不超过高斯投影误差的 (1+√(r/m)) 倍；逼近质量与完全高斯核在二阶矩意义下等价；任何保持快速变换结构的随机核所需种子位数至少为 r 的对数阶且 SRFT 的对角符号设计恰达到该熵下界。
