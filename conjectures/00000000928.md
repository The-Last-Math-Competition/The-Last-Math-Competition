# 00000000928

**English.** Conjecture: There exists a Hilbert-space operator whose point spectrum is dense in the unit circle, whose spectrum is the whole disk, and which has no invariant subspace (an extreme counterexample structure for the invariant subspace problem).

**中文。** 猜想：存在 Hilbert 空间算子其点谱在单位圆稠密而谱为整个圆盘且无不变子空间(不变子空间的极端反例结构)。
