# 00000004131

**English.** Definition: The Martin axiom MA_kappa asserts the existence of filters meeting kappa many dense sets for ccc forcing. Conjecture: The minimal height of a Suslin tree destroyed by MA_kappa is kappa^+, the tree is explicit in L, and the compatibility of MA_{aleph_1} with the Suslin hypothesis holds only at kappa = aleph_1. (minimal height of Suslin trees destroyed by MA)

**中文。** 定义：Martin 公理 MA_κ 指 ccc 力迫对 κ 个稠密集的滤子存在性。猜想：MA_κ 破坏的最小 Suslin 树高度为 κ⁺,树在 L 中显式,且 MA_{ℵ₁} 与 Suslin 假设的相容仅在 κ = ℵ₁ 处成立。（MA 破坏树最小高度）
