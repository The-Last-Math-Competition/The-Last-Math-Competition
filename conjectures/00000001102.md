# 00000001102

**LT-02 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The R-polynomials of KL theory are defined by the recursion R_{x,w} = qR_{xs,w} + R_{x,ws}. Conjecture: For all x ≤ w, max R_{x,w} = 2^{ℓ(w)−ℓ(x)}·(1 − 2^{−⌈(ℓ(w)−ℓ(x))/2⌉}); that is, the peak decays as a power of two, with decay exponent the ceiling of half the length difference.

**中文。** 定义：KL 理论的 R-多项式 R_{x,w}(q) 由递推 R_{x,w}=qR_{xs,w}+R_{x,ws} 定义。猜想：对一切 x≤w,max R_{x,w} = 2^{ℓ(w)−ℓ(x)}·(1−2^{−⌈(ℓ(w)−ℓ(x))/2⌉}),即峰值以 2 的幂衰减且衰减指数由长度差的一半向上取整给出。
