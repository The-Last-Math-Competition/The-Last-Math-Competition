# 00000008769

**MKT-29 ★** — source: 277_代数K理论与MilnorK.md

**English.** Definition: The generator count of perfect complexes: Thomason's density. Conjecture: Generators exist (an existence law); there is an explicit bound on the generator count (a bound law); homogeneous coordinate rings are computable (a computability law); and the saturation conjecture is verifiable (a verifiability law). (perfect complex generators Thomason density)

**中文。** 定义：完美复形的生成数：Thomason 的稠密性。猜想：生成元的存在（存在律）；且显式生成数的界（界律）；齐次坐标环的计算（计算律）；饱和猜想的验证（验证律）。
