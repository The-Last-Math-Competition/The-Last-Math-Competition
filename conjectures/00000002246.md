**NV-16 ★** — source: 60_亚纯函数与NevanlinnaII.md

**English.** Definition: An algebroid function satisfies an irreducible P(z, w) = 0. Conjecture: The spectrum of the second deficiency θ in the deficiency relation Σ(δ + θ) ≤ 2ν of algebroid functions is realizable (algebroid second deficiency).

**中文。** 定义：代数体函数(algebroid,满足不可约 P(z, w) = 0)。猜想：代数体函数的缺度关系 Σ(δ + θ) ≤ 2ν 的第二亏量 θ 的谱实现(代数体第二亏量)。
