# 00000003889

**CA3-09 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: The Frobenius closure I^F is the intersection of I with its Frobenius expansions. Conjecture: The F-closure of a monomial ideal is always monomial, with exponent set the intersection of p-multiples of the original exponent set with the Newton polyhedron; the initial ideal of the F-closure of a general ideal is given by the same formula. (F-closure initial term formula)

**中文。** 定义：Frobenius 闭包 I^F 指 I 与其 Frobenius 扩张的交。猜想：单项式理想的 F-闭包恒为单项式理想,其指数集为原指数集 p 倍与牛顿多面体的交;一般理想的 F-闭包的首项理想恰由该公式给出。（F-闭包首项公式）
