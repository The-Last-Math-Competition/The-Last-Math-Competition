# 00000003489

**GA3-29 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The third-term correction of chromatic invariants of surface-embedded graphs (band d-invariants). Conjecture: The third term of the band d-invariant is 2^{2g} times a unit correction, with the correction completely determined by the parity of the winding numbers of the embedding; the third term vanishes if and only if the embedding has zero winding everywhere. (band third term correction)

**中文。** 定义：曲面嵌入图的染色不变量的第三项修正（环带 d-不变量）。猜想：环带 d-不变量的第三项为 2^{2g} 乘单位修正且修正项由嵌入旋绕数的奇偶完全确定；第三项消失当且仅当嵌入处处零旋绕。（环带第三项修正）
