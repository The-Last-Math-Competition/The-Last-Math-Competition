**English.** Definition: Transform subtraction is the subtraction constant of renormalization. Conjecture: The subtraction spectrum of arbitrary polynomial noise is an explicit linear table in the degree, verified at quadratic noise, and closed under linear interpolation. (linear spectrum table of polynomial-noise subtraction)

**中文。** 定义：变换减除指重整化的减除常数。猜想：任意多项式噪声的减除谱为次数的显式线性表,二次噪声处验证且线性插值封闭。（多项式噪声减除线性谱表）
