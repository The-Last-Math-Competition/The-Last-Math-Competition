# 00000002025

**English.** Definition: A + A is the sumset of a set; the order s of an asymptotic basis is the s-fold sum needed to cover all large integers. Conjecture: P ∪ Squares forms an asymptotic basis of order 3; moreover, the representation count at order 3 has the density lower bound ≥ c·N^{1/2}/log N (with c explicit as a singular-series lower bound).

**中文。** 定义：A + A 指集合的和集;渐近基的阶 s 指覆盖一切大整数所需 s 重和。猜想：P ∪ Squares 构成阶 3 的渐近基;进一步,阶 3 的表示数的密度下界 ≥ c·N^{1/2}/log N(c 显式为奇异级数下界)。
