# 00000008660

**English.** Definition: Stable commutator length scl is the infimum of limiting lengths of elements under commutator representations. Conjecture: scl values in free groups are always rational and computable in polynomial time; groups with positive scl spectral gap include mapping class groups and free groups; and the minimal positive value of scl is 1/2, attained by binder type elements. (scl rationality and spectral gap)

**中文。** 定义：稳定交换子长度 scl 指元素在交换子表示下的极限长度下确界。猜想：自由群的 scl 值恒为有理数且可多项式时间计算；scl 的谱隙正的群包含映射类群与自由群；非零 scl 的最小值为 1/2 且由 binder 型元素达到。（scl 有理谱隙）
