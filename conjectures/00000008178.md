# 00000008178

**CNT-08 ★** — source: 258_计算数论.md

**English.** Definition: The efficiency of the search for ABC triples: the counting density abc_dens(epsilon) of ABC hits with Rad(n) <= n^{1-epsilon}. Conjecture: abc_dens(epsilon) = O(X^{epsilon(1-epsilon)}) as an exact exponent (a density exponent formula); the smallest ABC hit (the smallest a + b = c) is the classical type (2, 3^{10} times 109, 23^5) (a minimal hit rigidity); and the optimal algorithm for the search complexity (enumerating all triples within X) is X^{2/3+epsilon} (a two-thirds law), the proof path being the modular surface of triples (a modular surface two-thirds duality). (ABC hit density two-thirds modular surface)

**中文。** 定义：ABC 三元组的搜索的效率：Rad(n) ≤ n^{1-ε} 的 ABC-hit 的计数密度 abc_dens(ε)。猜想：abc_dens(ε) = O(X^{ε(1-ε)}) 型精确指数（密度指数公式）；且 ABC-hits 的最小（a+b=c 的最小命中）为 (2,3^10·109, 23^5) 型经典（最小命中刚性）；搜索的复杂度（枚举一切 X 内三元组）的最优算法为 X^{2/3+ε}（三分之二律），律的证明路径为三元组的模曲面（模曲面-三分之二对偶）。
