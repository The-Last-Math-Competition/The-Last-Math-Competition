# 00000004381

**English.** Definition: Quillen-Lichtenbaum convergence is the range of validity of the comparison isomorphism of algebraic K with etale K. Conjecture: The minimal prime needed for convergence has the explicit lower bound of type 2n+1, and the bound is optimal, attained by integer rings containing an imaginary quadratic field. (QL minimal prime lower bound formula)

**中文。** 定义：Quillen–Lichtenbaum 收敛指代数 K 与 étale K 的比较同构成立范围。猜想：收敛所需的最小素数为 2n 加 1 型显式下界,界由含虚二次域的整数环达到最优。（QL 最小素下界公式）
