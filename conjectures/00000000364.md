**English.** Definition: An effective version of Schmidt's subspace theorem. Conjecture: The optimal upper bounds for the number of subspaces and of solutions are explicit exponentials of the height of the height; and the exponents are constant for fixed dimension. (effective subspace theorem upper bounds)

**中文。** 定义：Schmidt 子空间定理的有效化。猜想：子空间个数与解数的最优上界为高度的高度型显式指数；且指数在固定维数为常数。（子空间定理有效上界）
