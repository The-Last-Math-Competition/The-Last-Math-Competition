# 00000000278

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic–geometric mean AGM(a, b) is the common limit of the iteration x_{n+1} = (x_n+y_n)/2, y_{n+1} = √(x_n y_n); the Legendre relation between elliptic periods and the AGM ties it deeply to π and Gamma values. Conjecture: AGM(1,2) and π are algebraically independent; more strongly, for all nonzero algebraic a, b, the field generated by AGM(a,b), π, and log 4 has transcendence degree exactly 2, with the lower bound uniquely constrained by elliptic period relations. (AGM-pi algebraic independence)

**中文。** 定义：算术–几何平均 AGM(a,b) 指迭代 x_{n+1}=(x_n+y_n)/2、y_{n+1}=√(x_n y_n) 的公共极限；椭圆积分周期与 AGM 的 Legendre 关系给出 AGM 与 π、Γ 值的深度纠缠。猜想：AGM(1,2) 与 π 代数无关；更强地，对一切非零代数数 a、b，数 AGM(a,b) 与 π 及 log 4 生成的域的超越次数恰为 2，超越次数的下界由椭圆周期关系唯一约束。（AGM-pi 代数无关）
