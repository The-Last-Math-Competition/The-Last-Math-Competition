# 00000008398

**MIB-18 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The convergence tower of motivic integrals: the stabilization order of truncation limits of the Kontsevich integral. Conjecture: The truncated integral stabilizes after order n when the motivic measure of the singularity is of convergence type; the stabilization order equals the dimension of the motivic measure of the singularity; and the regularization of divergent motivic integrals is unique, the regularized value being a motive. (integral stabilization order)

**中文。** 定义：母题积分的收敛性塔指 Kontsevich 积分的截断极限的稳定阶。猜想：截断积分在第 n 阶后稳定当奇点的母题测度为收敛型；稳定阶等于奇点母题测度的维数；发散型母题积分的正规化唯一且正规化值为母题。（积分稳定阶）
