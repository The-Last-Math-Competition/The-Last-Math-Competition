**PS-13 ★** — source: 56_解析组合.md

**English.** Definition: The relation between site and bond percolation volume thresholds on the triangular lattice. Conjecture: The critical volumes of the two models are connected by an explicit correspondence; and the constant of the correspondence is duality-algebraic. (site-bond triangular lattice correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：三角格 site 与 bond 渗流的体积阈值关系。猜想：两模型的临界体积由显式对应连接；且对应的常数为对偶代数。（site-bond 三角格对应）
