**English.** Definition: The free Majorana model is the simplest free fermion field theory. Conjecture: The finite-temperature conversion is the explicit kernel of finite-interval determinants, compatible with the spectral shift of the zero-temperature solution, and the conversion is closed. (spectral-shift determinant kernel at finite temperature)

**中文。** 定义：自由马约拉纳模型指最简自由费米场论。猜想：有限温度的换算为有限区间行列式的显式核,与零温解的谱平移相容且换算封闭。（有限温度谱平移行列式核）
