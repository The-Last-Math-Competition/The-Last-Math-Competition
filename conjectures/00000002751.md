**English.** Definition: Regev's tensor product theorem: the additivity of exponents for tensor products of two PI algebra classes. Conjecture: The deficit of exponent additivity: the tensor-product exponent is exactly the sum, with the encoding carrying a logarithmic correction in mixed characteristic; the correction is the Kemer-function difference of the mixed torsion part. (Regev exponent additivity correction)

**中文。** 定义：Regev 张量积定理：两类 PI 代数张量积的指数加法。猜想：指数加法的亏损：张量积指数恰为两指数之和且和的编码在混合特征下带对数修正；修正项为混合挠部分的 Kemer 函数差。（Regev 指数加法修正）
