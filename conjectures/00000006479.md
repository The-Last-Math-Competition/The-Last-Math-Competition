# 00000006479

**GP3-19 ◆** — source: 201_高斯过程II.md

**English.** Definition: Banach-valued Gaussian processes take values in infinite-dimensional structures. Conjecture: The value pair is given by the process support and the kernel of the pair is the type-cotype pair; spaces of type 2 and cotype 2 are isomorphic to Hilbert spaces; and the feasible domain of the pair is the intersection of moment inequalities. (type-cotype pair of Banach values)

**中文。** 定义：Banach 值：取值于无穷维结构的高斯过程。猜想：值域二元组由过程支撑给出且二元组的核为型余型对；型为 2 且余型为 2 的空间同构于希尔伯特空间；二元组的可行域为矩不等式的交。（Banach 值型余型二元组）
