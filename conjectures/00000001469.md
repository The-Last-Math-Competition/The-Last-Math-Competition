# 00000001469

**English.** Definition: The Unique Games Conjecture (UGC) asserts that the value of the approximate-vertex game can be approximated by unique distinguishers. Conjecture: The weakest combinatorial form of UGC — every graph admits a near-optimal two-vertex game with a unique 2-label solution — if it fails, the minimal counterexample graph has more than 2^{40} vertices; this numerical lower bound is given by the explicit count of a recursive amplification construction.

**中文。** 定义：唯一博弈猜想(UGC)断言近似顶点游戏的值可被唯一区分器逼近。猜想：UGC 的最弱组合形式——每个图的两顶点近似最优博弈存在唯一 2-标签解——若失败,其最小反例图的顶点数 > 2^{40};该数值下界由递归放大构造的显式计数给出。
