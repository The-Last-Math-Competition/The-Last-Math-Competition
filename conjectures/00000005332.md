# 00000005332

**English.** Definition: The model is the geometric structure driven by a potential. Conjecture: Its conversion constant is the explicit Milnor number of the potential, in degenerate cases replaced by the spectrum number, and the conversion preserves triangulated equivalence. (Milnor-spectrum conversion of models)

**中文。** 定义：模型指位势驱动的几何结构。猜想：其换算常数为位势的米尔诺数显式,简并情形由谱数替代且换算保持三角等价。（模型米尔诺谱数换算）
