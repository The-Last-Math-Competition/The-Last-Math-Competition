# 00000002369

**OF-19 ★** — source: 64_算子理论II_函数演算与自由分析.md

**English.** Definition: The positivity cone of operator-valued self-adjoint free polynomials. Conjecture: The positivity cone of two-variable quadratic free polynomials is a projection of a semidefinite program; and the dimension of the projection is explicit. (two-variable quadratic positivity cone projection)

**中文。** 定义：算子值自伴自由多项式的正性锥。猜想：二元二次自由多项式的正性锥为半定规划的射影；且射影的维数为显式。（二元二次正性锥射影）
