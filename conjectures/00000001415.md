# 00000001415

**HT-25 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: The dichotomy of rational homotopy growth means growth is either finite or exponential (Félix–Halperin). Conjecture: The supremum of the rational homotopy growth rate of a compact simply connected manifold equals the supremum of the degrees of the differentials of its minimal model; and this value is zero exactly for formal manifolds. (growth rate minimal-model width formula)

**中文。** 定义：有理同伦增长的二分指有限或指数增长（Félix–Halperin）。猜想：紧单连通流形的有理同伦增长率上确界等于极小模型的微分度数的上确界；且该值在形式流形处取零。（增长率极小模型宽度公式）
