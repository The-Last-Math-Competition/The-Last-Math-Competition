# 00000005008

**English.** Definition: A twist sum is a G_2 construction gluing two matching pieces. Conjecture: The Betti number recursion of twist sums of the K3 times three-sphere product type is an explicit linear expression of the matching data, closed for all matchings and satisfying a global parity constraint. (linear Betti recursion of twist sums)

**中文。** 定义：解拧和指由匹配的两块拼合的 G₂ 构造。猜想：K3 与三维球积型的解拧和贝蒂数递推为匹配数据的显式线性式,对一切匹配封闭且满足全域奇偶约束。（解拧和贝蒂数线性递推式）
