**NLA-14 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Streaming low-rank approximation: single-pass rank approximation. Conjecture: The space of a one-plus-epsilon approximation is of order k times the reciprocal of epsilon; and the kernel of the space is the frequent directions sketch. (streaming frequent directions space k-over-epsilon)

**中文。** 定义：流式低秩逼近为单遍的秩逼近。猜想：一加 ε 逼近的空间为 k 乘 ε 倒数阶；且空间的核为频繁方向 sketch。（流式频繁方向空间 k 倒 ε）
