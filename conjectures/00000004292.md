**English.** Definition: The difference of the minimal number of generators and the rank is d(A) - r(A). Conjecture: Its distribution weighted by Ulm length converges to a Poisson law with parameter lambda given explicitly by the length, and the convergence rate is the reciprocal square. (Poisson limit of the generator-rank difference)
