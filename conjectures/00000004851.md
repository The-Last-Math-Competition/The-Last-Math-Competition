# 00000004851

**English.** Definition: The JSJ decomposition is the canonical splitting along cylindrical subgroups. Conjecture: The classification of rigid vertices at genus two consists of two explicit types, the table is closed, and the uniqueness of the decomposition is preserved up to equivalence. (closed two-type table of JSJ rigid vertices)

**中文。** 定义：JSJ 分解指沿圆柱子群的典范分裂。猜想：刚性顶点的分类在亏格二由两个显式型组成,表封闭且分解的唯一性在等价下保持。（JSJ 刚性顶点二型封闭表）
