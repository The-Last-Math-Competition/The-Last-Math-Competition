**English.** Definition: Finite-field plane duality: invariants under point-line duality. Conjecture: The complete list of duality invariants: crossing numbers, line-bundle numbers, and spectra of incidence matrices are preserved under duality, proved by the transpose isometry of the incidence matrix. (plane duality invariant list)

**中文。** 定义：有限域平面对偶：点线对偶下的不变量。猜想：对偶不变量的完全列表：交叉数、束数、关联矩阵的谱在对偶下保持且保持的证明为关联矩阵的转置等距。（平面对偶不变量列表）
