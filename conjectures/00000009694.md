**English.** Definition: Multiple zeta values MZVs are values of convergent multiple series zeta(s_1,...,s_k); their algebraic relation structure is the core of period theory. Conjecture: The monodromization splitting of the motivic MZV algebra has an explicit formula: the splitting map is an explicit combination of Fay trilinear relations and regularization sums; after splitting, the dimension of each weight space is exactly the value of the Euler-Zagier counting formula. (MZV monodromization splitting)

**中文。** 定义：多 ζ 值 MZV 为收敛多重级数 ζ(s_1,…,s_k) 的值；其代数关系结构是周期理论核心。猜想：motivic MZV 代数的单值化分裂存在显式公式：分裂映射由 Fay 三关系与正则化和的显式组合给出；分裂后每个权空间的维数恰为 Euler–Zagier 计数公式的值。（MZV 单值化分裂）
