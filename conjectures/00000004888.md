**English.** Definition: Projective absoluteness is the forcing invariance of projective sentences. Conjecture: Its combinatorial equivalence table is given explicitly by the closure conditions of proper forcing classes, closed at the third projective layer with each layer's equivalence unique. (forcing closure equivalence table of projective absoluteness)
