# 00000002726

**SC-16 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Pinched complexes: evolution of invariants under vertex pinching. Conjecture: The evolution of h-vectors under pinching is a linear transformation with a Steinberg-type triangular matrix; the fixed points of the evolution are cone complexes, and the dimension spectrum of the fixed points is given by the connectivity of the pinching kernel. (pinching h evolution matrix)

**中文。** 定义：捏合复形（pinched complex）：顶点捏合下的不变量演化。猜想：捏合的 h-向量演化为线性变换且变换矩阵为 Steinberg 型三角矩阵；演化的不动点为锥复形且不动点的维数谱由捏合核的连通度给出。（捏合 h 演化矩阵）
