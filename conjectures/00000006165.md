# 00000006165

**English.** Definition: The singular leading term is the leading asymptotic structure of algebraic singularities. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit formula of the algebraic singular leading term, controlled by the minimal exponent; the sub-leading exponent difference is the integer 1, and the coefficient ratio is a continued fraction of the main exponent; and the leading constant is uniquely determined by the singular amplitude. (minimal-exponent sub-leading conversion of algebraic singularities)

**中文。** 定义：奇异首项：代数奇异的首项渐近结构。猜想：换算常数为代数奇异首项的显式公式且由最小指数控制；次项指数差为整数 1 且系数比为主指数的连分数；首项常数由奇异振幅唯一确定。（代数奇异最小指数次项换算）
