# 00000001825

**English.** Definition: The Green–Lazarsfeld minimal decomposition conjecture concerns the minimality of the property-driven decomposition of curves. Conjecture: The minimal genus g* of a counterexample (if any) is at least 9; concretely, the minimal decomposition conjecture holds for general-type curves with g* ≤ 8.

**中文。** 定义：Green–Lazarsfeld 极小分解猜想指曲线的捕捉分解的极小性。猜想：反例(如存在)的最小亏格 g* ≥ 9;具体地 g*≤8 的极小分解猜想在一般型曲线上成立。
