# 00000005699

**English.** Definition: A 2-fusion category is the semisimple two-layer structure, the fusion of 2-group representations. Conjecture: The 2-fusion rules are given by the tube-type pairing, the kernel of the pairing being the lifting operator; the image of the kernel is the automorphism group of the unit, a finite group; and the nonnegativity of fusion coefficients is guaranteed by the semisimplicity of the tube algebra, with semisimplicity threshold at rank 2. (tube pairing kernel of 2-fusion)

**中文。** 定义：双重融合范畴：半单的双层结构（2-群表示的融合）。猜想：双层融合规则由电视（tube）型配对给出且配对的核为提升算子；核的像为单位的自同构群且群为有限；融合系数的非负性由管代数的半单性保证且半单性阈值在秩 2。（双层融合电视配对核）
