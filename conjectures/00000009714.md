# 00000009714

**SCA-14 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Classification of nonscattering solutions: compactness deficits and soliton convergence of energy-critical equations. Conjecture: The profiles of all nonscattering global solutions converge, modulo the symmetry group, to elements of a finite soliton list; the soliton list is the complete classification of harmonic solitons of the equation, with no hidden members. (nonscattering soliton convergence)

**中文。** 定义：非散射解的分类：能量临界方程的紧性亏损与孤子收敛。猜想：一切非散射的整体解的轮廓在模对称群后收敛到有限孤子列表的元素；孤子列表为该方程的调和孤子的完全分类且无隐成员。（非散射孤子收敛）
