# 00000002053

**English.** Definition: Congruence-lattice representation means every algebraic lattice is realized as the congruence lattice of some algebra (Grätzer–Schmidt). Conjecture: The minimal cardinality of an algebra simultaneously realizing a lattice L and a prescribed sublattice spectrum is max(spectrum)+1; representation minimality follows from a free-product truncation construction.

**中文。** 定义：同余格的表示指每个代数格可实现为某代数的同余格(Grätzer–Schmidt)。猜想：同时实现格 L 与给定子格谱的最小代数的基数为 max(谱)+1;表示最小化由自由积截断构造。
