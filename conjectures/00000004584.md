**HA3-14 ◆** — source: 138_双曲几何的解析面.md

**English.** Definition: The geodesic prime number theorem is the asymptotic law for counting closed geodesics. Conjecture: Its second-term constant is always given explicitly by the real part of the first zero of the Selberg zeta function, and the error exponents correspond one-to-one with the leading zeros. (resonance constant of the geodesic prime second term)

**中文。** 定义：测地素数定理指封闭测地线计数的渐近律。猜想：其第二项常数恒由 Selberg zeta 首零点实部显式给出；误差指数与首零点一一对应。（测地素数二项共振常数）
