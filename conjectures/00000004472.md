**English.** Definition: The GZ system is the Gaiotto-Zamolodchikov type difference equation. Conjecture: Its integrability conversion constant is an explicit combination of tau functions, and in the automorphic limit the constant degenerates to 1 and is exact. (GZ tau-function conversion normalization)

**中文。** 定义：GZ 体系指 Gaiotto–Zamolodchikov 型差分方程。猜想：其可积性换算常数为 τ 函数的显式组合,自守极限下常数退化为 1 且精确。（GZ τ 函数换算归一）
