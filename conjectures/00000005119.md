**English.** Definition: The principal logarithm branch is the principal branch with restricted spectral angle. Conjecture: For purely imaginary spectra the principal-branch conversion is given explicitly by the standard formula, continuously extended to the spectral-angle boundary, and the conversion is unique without ambiguity. (unique conversion of the principal logarithm at purely imaginary spectra)

**中文。** 定义：主对数分支指谱角限制的主值分支。猜想：纯虚谱的主支换算由标准公式显式给出,连续延拓到谱角边界且换算唯一无歧义。（主对数纯虚谱唯一换算）
