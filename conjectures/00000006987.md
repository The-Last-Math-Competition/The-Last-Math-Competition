**English.** Definition: Weak convergence is convergence in the testing sense. Conjecture: The dimension bound of concentration localization is given by spherical symmetry; the extremum of the bound is polar concentration; and the bound of polar concentration is attained at radial functions. (spherical dimension bound of concentration localization)
