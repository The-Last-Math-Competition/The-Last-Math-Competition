# 00000002702

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic side of entropy: the fold entropy of algebraic dynamical systems. Conjecture: Rationality of fold entropy: the fold entropy of an integral endomorphism is a rational multiple of the logarithm of the degree, the denominator dividing the periodic exponent of the map; the exceptional maps to rationality are compositions of Chebyshev and Lattès. (fold entropy rational classification)

**中文。** 定义：熵的算术侧：代数动力系统的折叠熵。猜想：折叠熵的有理性：整自同态的折叠熵为映射度对数的有理倍数且倍数分母被映射的周期指数整除；有理性的例外映射为 Chebyshev 与 Lattès 的复合。（折叠熵有理分类）
