# 00000008562

**NSP-02 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral type of the adjacency operator A_G of a locally finite graph G on l^2(V): the trichotomy of pure point, absolutely continuous, or singular continuous. Conjecture: On subcritical Galton-Watson trees the spectral type is pure point (a subcritical pure point law); on critical trees the spectrum contains a singular continuous part (a critical law); on supercritical trees the spectrum is absolutely continuous plus finite point spectrum (a supercritical law); and the boundary of spectral types (weightings near criticality) is decided by the sign of the Lyapunov exponent of the weights (a Lyapunov sign criterion). (Galton-Watson spectral type Lyapunov criterion)

**中文。** 定义：局部有限图 G 的邻接算子 A_G 在 ℓ²(V) 上的谱型：纯点、绝对连续或奇异连续的三分类。猜想：亚临界 Galton–Watson 树上谱型为纯点（亚临界纯点律）；临界树上谱含奇异连续部分（临界律）；超临界树的谱为绝对连续加有限点谱（超临界律）；谱型的边界（临界附近的加权）由权重的 Lyapunov 指数符号决定（Lyapunov 符号判据）。
