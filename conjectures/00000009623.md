# 00000009623

**SMA-13 ★** — source: 306_Smale空间与符号动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Cellular automata (CA) are continuous commuting factors of the one-dimensional shift; damage propagation speed (Lyapunov exponent) characterizes stability. Conjecture: The damage propagation speed of surjective CA under Bernoulli measures is an explicit function of the Boolean Fourier spectrum of the local rule table; the speed is zero if and only if the rule is a permutative factor, and slow-damage (sublinear propagation) CA have measure zero within the surjective class. (CA damage propagation speed)

**中文。** 定义：元胞自动机（CA）为一维移位上的连续交换因子；损伤传播速度（Lyapunov 指数）刻画稳定性。猜想：满射元胞自动机在 Bernoulli 测度下的损伤传播速度为局部规则表的布尔 Fourier 谱的显式函数；速度为零当且仅当规则为置换型因子，慢损伤（次线性传播）的 CA 在满射类中为零测度。（CA 损伤传播速度）
