# 00000006521

**English.** Definition: The local index formula converts the index density into heat-kernel coefficients. Conjecture: The algebraic explicit form of the local density is given by cyclic-cohomology representatives, and the normalization of the representatives is the cancellation of localization. (cyclic local index density)

**中文。** 定义：局部指标公式把指标密度化为热核系数。猜想：局部密度的代数显式由循环上同调的代表给出；且代表的规范化为局域化消去。（局部指标循环密度）
