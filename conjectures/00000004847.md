# 00000004847

**English.** Definition: The Rips construction is the small-cancellation construction with an arbitrary finite group as quotient. Conjecture: The cancellation-length lower bound of the output hyperbolic group is an explicit improvement in the logarithm of the order of the quotient, the improvement cannot be reduced, and the construction preserves hyperbolicity. (logarithmic cancellation-length lower bound of Rips)

**中文。** 定义：Rips 构造指以任意有限群为商的小消除构造。猜想：其输出双曲群的消除长度下界为商群阶对数的显式改进,改进不可再缩且构造保持双曲性。（Rips 消除长度对数下界）
