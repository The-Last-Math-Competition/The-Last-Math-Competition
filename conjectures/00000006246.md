**English.** Definition: The structure sheaf with the adjoint generation of the spectral structure, the co-geometry. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the structure co-geometry and the spectrum is given by the structure-sheaf adjunction; the unit of the adjunction is an equivalence, the test of equivalence being the stalks at points; and the rank of the co-geometry is the global dimension, finite. (spectrum-adjunction conversion of structure co-geometry)

**中文。** 定义：结构层：谱结构层的伴随生成；结构余几何。猜想：换算常数为结构余几何的显式且谱由结构层伴随给出；伴随的单位为等价且等价的检验为点上的茎；余几何的秩为整体维数且维数有限。（结构余几何谱伴随换算）
