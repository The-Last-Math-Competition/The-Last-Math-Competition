# 00000002137

**PS-27 ★** — source: 56_解析组合.md

**English.** Definition: The phase transition of a bivariate generating function is a singularity mutation. Conjecture: The critical exponents of power-law classes are rational; the jumps of the exponents are half-integers; and the spectrum of half-integer jumps is discrete within the admissible classes. (phase transition rational critical exponents)

**中文。** 定义：双变量生成函数的相位转移为奇异性突变。猜想：幂律类的临界指数为有理数；且指数的跳跃为半整数；且半整数跳跃的谱在允许类离散。（相位转移有理临界指数）
