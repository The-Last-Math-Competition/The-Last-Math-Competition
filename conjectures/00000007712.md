**TIL-22 △** — source: 242_离散几何II_镶嵌与铺砌.md

**English.** Definition: The minimal entropy h_min is the infimum of tiling-space entropy over all aperiodic Wang tile sets. Conjecture: h_min = 0, that is, there exist aperiodic Wang sets W_k with h(W_k) tending to 0 at rate h(W_k) = O(k^{-2}) in the tile count k; and every 11-tile aperiodic set of Jeandel-Rao type has entropy bounded below by a positive constant of order 10^{-5}. (zero minimal entropy of aperiodic Wang sets)

**中文。** 定义：非周期 Wang 集 W 的最小熵 h(W) 指遍历一切非周期 Wang 瓦集的铺砌空间熵的下确界。猜想：h_min = 0，即存在非周期 Wang 集 W_k 使 h(W_k)→0，其速率 h(W_k) = O(k^{-2})，其中 k 为瓦片数；且任何 11 瓦片非周期集（Jeandel–Rao 型）的熵下界为 10^{-5} 量级的正数。
