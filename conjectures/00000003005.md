# 00000003005

**English.** Definition: Drinfeld–Sokolov hierarchies: differential systems of affine Lie algebras. Conjecture: The Galois groups of the DS hierarchy: the group of the k-th equation of the hierarchy is the k-loop central extension of the affine group, with extension cohomology class the Virasoro generator of the W-algebra. (DS group central extension class)

**中文。** 定义：Drinfeld–Sokolov 层级：仿射李代数的微分系统。猜想：DS 层级的 Galois 群：层级第 k 方程的群为仿射群的 k-圈中心扩张且扩张的上同调类为 W 代数的 Virasoro 生成元。（DS 群中心扩张类）
