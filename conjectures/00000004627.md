**English.** Definition: A constant-mean-curvature free-boundary surface is a CMC surface meeting the boundary at right angles. Conjecture: It satisfies an explicit isoperimetric lower bound, the bound is attained by spherical-cap disks, and the inequality is compatible with the Reilly-type identity. (CMC free-boundary isoperimetric lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：常平均曲率自由边界面指交角直角的 CMC 曲面。猜想：其满足显式等周下界,界由球冠碟片达到且不等式与 Reilly 型恒等式相容。（CMC 自由边界等周下界）
