**English.** Definition: The telescope terminal object is the terminal object of type n in the category of periodic self-map localizations. Conjecture: The type-two telescope is not a finite smash product, and its difference from the K(2)-localization is given explicitly by the fractional Ext groups of the reciprocal-period tower; the second-page differentials of the tower are controlled by the Adams trace of the v_2 self-map, and the low-stem part is a finite explicit list of objects. (fractional Ext tower of telescope terminals)
