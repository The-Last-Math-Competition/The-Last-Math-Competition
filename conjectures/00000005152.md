# 00000005152

**English.** Definition: The locus is the trajectory of extra cycles in the period image. Conjecture: The count of this locus at genus three is an explicit modular-form series of the corresponding numbers, the coefficients are modular forms, and the count is exactly closed. (modular-series count of the genus-three locus)

**中文。** 定义：位置指周期像中额外圈的轨迹。猜想：亏格三的该位置计数为相应数的显式模形式级数,系数为模形式且计数精确封闭。（亏格三位置模级数计数）
