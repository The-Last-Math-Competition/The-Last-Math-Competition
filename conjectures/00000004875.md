**English.** Definition: Proof search is the machine enumeration of shortest proofs. Conjecture: The search lower bound of two-quantifier sentences on finite structures is an explicit exponential of half the number of structures, given by the invariance of symmetric structures and consistent with the satisfiability reduction. (symmetric-structure lower bound of proof search)
