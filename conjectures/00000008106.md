# 00000008106

**English.** Definition: The realization of Stiefel-Whitney classes of fusion systems: the sign functional sw(F) of the fusion image of the first Stiefel-Whitney class w_1 of real representations. Conjecture: The kernel of sw(F) is the kernel of the characteristic-2 real blocks of the fusion (a kernel-real block duality); the vanishing of the fusion image of w_1 is equivalent to the orientability of |L| (an orientation-vanishing link); and the minimal non-group source among oriented fusion systems is exactly the exotic two-rank system at p = 2 (a two-rank orientation exception), the orientation count of the exception being exactly 2 (a two-orientation counting law). (Stiefel-Whitney orientation two-rank exception)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 Stiefel–Whitney 类的实现：实表示的第一 Stiefel–Whitney 类 w₁ 的融合像的符号 functional sw(F)。猜想：sw(F) 的核为融合的特征 2 的实块的核（核-实块对偶）；且 w₁ 的融合像的消没等价于 |L| 的定向性（定向-消没）；定向的融合系统的极小非群源为且仅为 p=2 的 exotic 双秩系（双秩定向例外），例外的定向的计数恰为 2（双定向计数律）。
