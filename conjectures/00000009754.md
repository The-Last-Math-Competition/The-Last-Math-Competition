**MAD-24 ★** — source: 310_数学的自动发现.md

**English.** Definition: Markov decision processes of theorem proving: value-function regularity of reinforcement-learning search. Conjecture: The Lipschitz constant of the optimal value function of proof-search MDPs is linearly controlled by the average branching degree of proof trees; above a threshold branching degree the value function degenerates to a constant, with critical branching degree 6. (proof MDP value regularity)

**中文。** 定义：定理证明的马尔可夫决策过程：强化学习搜索的值函数正则性。猜想：证明搜索 MDP 的最优值函数的 Lipschitz 常数由证明树的平均分支度线性控制；分支度高于阈值时值函数退化为常数且学习失效的临界分支度为 6。（证明 MDP 值正则性）
