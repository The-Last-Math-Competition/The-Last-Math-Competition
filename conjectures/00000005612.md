**English.** Definition: The subresultant is the discriminant sequence of elimination. Conjecture: The bit-swell upper bound is an explicit linear expression of the coefficient bit length, tight for monic subdiagonal cases, improving the classical bound by a constant factor. (tight linear swell bound of subresultants)
