# 00000001801

**English.** Definition: The Nevanlinna deficiency relation for holomorphic curves entering the blowup Bl₀C². Conjecture: When the divisor is the exceptional divisor plus d lines in general position, the deficiency relation is a Cartan-type correction (blowup deficiencies).

**中文。** 定义：全纯曲线进入 blowup Bl₀ℂ² 的 Nevanlinna 缺项关系。猜想：除子为例外除数 + d 条一般直线时的缺陷关系为 Cartan 型修正(blowup 缺项)。
