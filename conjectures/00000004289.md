# 00000004289

**AG5-19 ◆** — source: 128_交换群论.md

**English.** Definition: An almost free abelian group is a non-free group all of whose subgroups of cardinality strictly below kappa are free, and flat-free means all subgroups below kappa are flat. Conjecture: The smallest cardinality at which an almost free but not flat-free group exists is aleph_omega, and this separation is provable in ZFC; at aleph_1 almost free implies flat-free; the counterexample at aleph_omega is constructed via the Shelah black box, and existence at any smaller cardinal is independent of ZFC. (almost free-flat-free separation cardinal)

**中文。** 定义：几乎自由交换群：每个基数严格小于 κ 的子群均自由的非自由群；♭-自由群：每个小于 κ 的子群均平坦。猜想：几乎自由而不 ♭-自由的群存在的最小基数为 ℵ_ω 且该分离在 ZFC 内可证；在 ℵ_1 处几乎自由蕴含 ♭-自由；ℵ_ω 处的反例由 Shelah 黑盒构造给出且任何更小基数处的存在性独立于 ZFC。（几乎自由-♭自由分离基数）
