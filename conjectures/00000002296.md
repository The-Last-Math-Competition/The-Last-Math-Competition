# 00000002296

**FR-06 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: The double Burnside ring of a fusion system is the algebraic structure of its stable homotopy classes. Conjecture: The unit group of the double Burnside ring of rank-2 exotic systems is an explicit finite table (from Monroe-type atomic decompositions); the order of the unit group is at most 2^8.

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的双 Burnside 环指其稳定同伦类的代数结构。猜想：秩 2 exotic 系统的双 Burnside 的单位群为显式有限表(表由门罗型原子分解);单位群的阶 ≤ 2^8。
