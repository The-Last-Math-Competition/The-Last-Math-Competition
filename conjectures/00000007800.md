# 00000007800

**ACG-20 △** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: Brownileading out position Y of distribution density p_K of an motion leading out isotropy body K of leading out distribution: from point of isotropy body on Brownian motion. Conjecture: explicit function on under, bound of L_K² of p_K in ball plane boundary point θ is (2π L_K²)^{-n/2} · exp(-θ²/2L_K²) type Gaussian envelope; and most large entropy approximation order of p_K is O(n^{-1}), approximation error constant equals K and symmetric difference volume ratio of John ball.

**中文。** 定义：迷向体上布朗运动的首出分布：从原点出发的布朗运动首出迷向体 K 的首出位置 Y 的分布密度 p_K。猜想：p_K 在球面边界点 θ 的值被 L_K² 的显式函数上下夹逼，界为 (2π L_K²)^{-n/2}·exp(-θ²/2L_K²) 型高斯包络；且 p_K 的最大熵逼近阶为 O(n^{-1})，逼近误差常数等于 K 与 John 椭球的对称差体积比。
