# 00000007800

**English.** Definition: Exit distributions of Brownian motion from isotropic bodies: the density p_K of the exit position Y of Brownian motion started at the origin from an isotropic body K. Conjecture: p_K at a boundary direction theta is sandwiched by explicit functions of L_K^2, with Gaussian envelope of type (2 pi L_K^2)^{-n/2} exp(-theta^2/2L_K^2); and the maximum-entropy approximation order of p_K is O(n^{-1}), the approximation error constant equaling the symmetric-difference volume ratio of K with its John ellipsoid. (Gaussian envelope of Brownian exit densities)

**中文。** 定义：迷向体上布朗运动的首出分布：从原点出发的布朗运动首出迷向体 K 的首出位置 Y 的分布密度 p_K。猜想：p_K 在球面边界点 θ 的值被 L_K² 的显式函数上下夹逼，界为 (2π L_K²)^{-n/2}·exp(-θ²/2L_K²) 型高斯包络；且 p_K 的最大熵逼近阶为 O(n^{-1})，逼近误差常数等于 K 与 John 椭球的对称差体积比。
