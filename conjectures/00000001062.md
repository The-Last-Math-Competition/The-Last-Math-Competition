# 00000001062

**FG-62 △** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: Let N_q(g) denote the number of isomorphism classes of genus-g curves over F_q(t). Conjecture: N_q(g) = q^{5(g−1)}·(q²−1)·(q³−q)/24·(1+O(q^{-1})), i.e. the closed-form limit of Poonen's recursion; the error term for g ≥ 2 is explicitly controlled by the contribution of unstable curves in the moduli space.

**中文。** 定义：F_q(t) 上亏格 g 曲线的同构类个数记 N_q(g)。猜想：N_q(g) = q^{5(g−1)}·(q²−1)·(q^{3}−q)/24·(1+O(q^{-1})),即 Poonen 递归的闭式极限;误差项在 g≥2 时由模空间上不稳定曲线的贡献显式控制。
