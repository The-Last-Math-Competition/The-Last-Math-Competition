# 00000009547

**QIC-27 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: The two-assisted quantum capacity Q_2(phi) allows arbitrary LOCC interactive protocols between sender and receiver, equaling an infimum-type measure of the distillable entanglement of the channel's Choi state. Conjecture: Under PPT constraints Q_2 equals the PPT regularization of the relative entropy of entanglement E_R; without constraints, Q_2 at least (1/2) E_R(Choi) always holds, with the halving constant 1/2 attained with equality by the completely depolarizing direct-sum corrected channel. (bidirectional capacity relative entropy formula)

**中文。** 定义：双辅助量子容量 Q_2(φ) 允许收发双方任意 LOCC 交互协议，等于信道 Choi 态的可蒸馏纠缠的下确界型量度。猜想：在 PPT 约束下 Q_2 等于相对熵纠缠 E_R 的 PPT 正则化；无约束时 Q_2≥(1/2)E_R(Choi) 恒成立且减半常数 1/2 由完全去极化直和修正信道取得等号。（双向容量相对熵公式）
