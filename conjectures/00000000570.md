# 00000000570

**CA-70 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The Chebyshev-normalized invariant. Conjecture: The width of the x-degree support of the Laurent expansion of any acyclic cluster algebra is at most 2·rank (locality of degrees).

**中文。** 定义：Chebyshev 归一化不变量。猜想：任意 acyclic cluster 代数的 Laurent 展开的 x-度支撑集的宽度 ≤ 2·rank(度的局部性)。
