# 00000008589

**NSP-29 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: Well-posedness of the abstract Cauchy problem: existence and uniqueness of solutions of dU/dt = AU. Conjecture: Well-posedness is equivalent to A being the generator of a C_0 semigroup (a Hille criterion law); the constant bound of the abstract Hille-Yosida condition is the inverse bound of the spectrum (an inverse bound law); the minimal dimension of ill-posed examples (null space type) is infinite (an infinite-dimensional example law); and the regularization recovery rate of ill-posed problems is the reciprocal of the ill-posed order (a regularization reciprocal law). (abstract Cauchy Hille generator regularization)

**中文。** 定义：抽象 Cauchy 问题的适定性：dU/dt=AU 的解的存在唯一性。猜想：适定性等价于 A 为 C₀ 半群生成元（Hille 判据律）；且抽象 Hille–Yosida 条件的常数界为谱的逆界（逆界律）；不适定例（零空间）的最小维数为无穷（无穷维例律）；不适定问题的正则化恢复率为 Ill-posed 阶的倒数（正则化倒数律）。
