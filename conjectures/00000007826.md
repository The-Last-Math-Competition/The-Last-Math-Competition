# 00000007826

**English.** Definition: Gopakumar-Vaidenhardt (GV) invariants n_{g,beta} are integers counting BPS states of genus g and class beta on a Calabi-Yau threefold, defined via the state density of MV invariants. Conjecture: GV integrality holds on every smooth Calabi-Yau threefold (the dual MV system admits a Z-basis); and n_{g,beta} vanishes for fixed beta as g grows, with vanishing threshold g_max(beta) = floor(beta c_1(S)/2) + 1 exactly when beta is a surface class supported on a family of surfaces S. (GV integrality and vanishing threshold)

**中文。** 定义：Gopakumar–Vatten（GV）不变量 n_{g,β}∈Z 为 Calabi–Yau 三维簇上亏格 g、类 β 的 BPS 态计数，经 MV 不变量的态密度定义。猜想：GV 整性在任意光滑 Calabi–Yau 三维簇上成立（对偶 MV 系统可 Z-基解）；且 n_{g,β} 在固定 β、g→∞ 时为零，其消没阈 g_max(β) = floor(β·c_1(S)/2)+1（S 为支撑曲面族）当 β 为曲面类时精确。
