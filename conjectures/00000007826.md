**English.** Definition: Gopakumar–Vatten (GV) invariant n_{g,β}∈Z is state density definition of BPS state counting, MV invariant of Calabi–Yau three dimension variety on genus g, class β. Conjecture: GV in arbitrary smooth Calabi–Yau three dimension variety on becomes (dual MV system can Z- solution); and n_{g,β} in fixed β, g -> ∞ when is zero, its vanishes g_max(β) = floor(β · c_1(S)/2)+1 (S is support surface family) when β is surface exact.

**中文。** 定义：Gopakumar–Vatten（GV）不变量 n_{g,β}∈Z 为 Calabi–Yau 三维簇上亏格 g、类 β 的 BPS 态计数，经 MV 不变量的态密度定义。猜想：GV 整性在任意光滑 Calabi–Yau 三维簇上成立（对偶 MV 系统可 Z-基解）；且 n_{g,β} 在固定 β、g→∞ 时为零，其消没阈 g_max(β) = floor(β·c_1(S)/2)+1（S 为支撑曲面族）当 β 为曲面类时精确。
