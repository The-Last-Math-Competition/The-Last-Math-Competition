# 00000001568

**GM-28 ★** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: A geometric thrackle is a straight-edge graph in the plane in which every two edges intersect exactly once. Conjecture: The maximum number of edges of a geometric thrackle is at most the number of vertices (the geometric version of Conway's thrackle conjecture); the strict bound ⌊1.393·n⌋ of Lovász–Pach–Szegedy can be improved to n.

**中文。** 定义：几何 thrackle 指平面上每对边恰相交一次的直边图。猜想：几何 thrackle 的最大边数 ≤ 顶点数(Conway thrackle 猜想的几何版);严格紧值为 ⌊1.393·n⌋ 的 Lovász–Pach–Szegedy 上界可改进到 n。
