**English.** Definition: The layer is the central-product structure of components, with its conversion. Conjecture: The conversion formula is explicit in the prime count of center intersections, a single prime for two layers; the additivity of the prime count is preserved under layer merging, with constant 1; and for three or more layers the count is multiplicative, with multiplicative deficit given by the associativity of intersections. (prime-count conversion of layer center intersections)

**中文。** 定义：层：分量的中心积结构；层换算。猜想：换算公式为中心交的素数计数显式且两层时为单素数；素数计数的加性在层的合并下保持且保持常数为 1；三层以上的计数为乘性且乘性亏损由交的结合律给出。（层中心交素数计数换算）
