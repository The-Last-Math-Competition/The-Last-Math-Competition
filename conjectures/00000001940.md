# 00000001940

**English.** Definition: The cyclic covers of a hyperbolic 3-manifold are the covers around a knot. Conjecture: The density of commutator classes is always negative logarithmic density; and the negative sign is verified from the root distribution of the Alexander polynomial. (cyclic cover commutator negative log density)

**中文。** 定义：双曲 3-流形的循环覆盖指绕纽结的覆叠。猜想：换位类的密度恒为负对数密度；且负号的验证由 Alexander 多项式的根分布。（循环覆盖换位负对数密度）
