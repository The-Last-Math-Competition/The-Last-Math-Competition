**English.** Definition: Randomized Cholesky: randomized incomplete factorization. Conjecture: Fill reduction is logarithmic and the combination is by random permutations; and the kernel of the permutation is graph coloring. (random Cholesky graph coloring logarithmic fill)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Cholesky 为不完全分解随机化。猜想：填充减少为对数级且组合为随机置换；且置换的核为图着色。（随机 Cholesky 图着色对数填充）
