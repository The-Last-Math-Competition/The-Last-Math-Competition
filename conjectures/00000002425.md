**English.** Definition: The G-character variety is the GIT quotient of representations. Conjecture: The E-polynomial of the SL₂ character variety of a closed surface group is of mixed Tate type; and the pole spectrum of the polynomial is the lattice of weights. (character variety mixed Tate E-polynomial)
