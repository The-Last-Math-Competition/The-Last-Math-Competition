**OL-24 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: Combinatorial shadows of root lattices: the shadow lattice attached to a root system. Conjecture: The Möbius value of the shadow lattice equals the sign of the longest element of the Weyl group, the sign explicitly controlled by the parity of the Coxeter number. (root lattice shadow Mobius)

**中文。** 定义：根格的组合影子：根系伴随格的阴影格。猜想：阴影格的 Möbius 值等于 Weyl 群最长元的符号差且符号差由 Coxeter 数的奇偶显式控制。（根格影子 Mobius）
