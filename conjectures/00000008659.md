**CGT-09 ★** — source: 274_计算群论与字的几何.md

**English.** Definition: Cannon's conjecture concerns the boundary of hyperbolic groups and the Gromov zero-angle structure. Conjecture: The boundary of every cocompact hyperbolic manifold group is a sphere and the geodesic language is regular (a Cannon regularity law); the extension of the regularity criterion (completion by finite automata) has completion domain the rigid Hurwitz type (a Hurwitz domain law); the exceptions to completion (boundary spheres of non-Cannon groups) are non-rigid hyperbolic groups (a non-rigid exception law); and the fractal dimensions of exceptional boundaries form a dense spectrum (a dense spectrum law). (Cannon Hurwitz rigid completion dense spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Cannon 混沌。猜想：双曲群的边界与 Gromov 零角结构。猜想：一切（余紧双曲流形的基本群）的边界为球面且测地语言为正规（Cannon 正规律）；且正规性的判据的推广（有限自动机的完备）的完备域为刚性 Hurwitz 型（Hurwitz 域律）；完备的例外（非 Cannon 的边界球面）的例为非刚性双曲群的（非刚性例外律）；例外的维数（例外的边界的分形维数）的维数的稠密谱（稠密谱律）。
