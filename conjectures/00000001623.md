# 00000001623

**English.** Definition: The n-soliton potential of KdV is the Schrödinger potential with zero reflection coefficient and eigenvalues −κ_i² (known). Conjecture: The scattering matrix of a multisoliton potential is pure phase (transmission coefficient of modulus 1), with phase shift δ(k) = 2iΣ arctan(k/κ_i) an explicit symmetric function of the κ_i; the scattering phase is completely determined by the soliton parameters.

**中文。** 定义：KdV 的 n-孤子位势指反射系数为零、特征值 −κ_i² 的 Schrödinger 位势(已知)。猜想：多孤子位势的散射矩阵为纯相位(透射系数模长 1),且相位移 δ(k)=2iΣ arctan(k/κ_i) 为 κ_i 的显式对称函数;散射相位完全由孤子参数决定。
