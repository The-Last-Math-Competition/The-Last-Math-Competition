# 00000001536

**KK-26 ★** — source: 36_代数K理论与非交换几何.md

**English.** Conjecture: Nilpotence of the cup product in Hochschild cohomology HH^*(A): for smooth compact algebras, every element of HH¹ has vanishing cup square (smooth compact cup nilpotence).

**中文。** 猜想：Hochschild 上同调 HH^*(A) 的 cup 积的幂零性:光滑紧代数的 HH¹ 的任意元素 cup 自身为零(光滑紧 cup 幂零)。
