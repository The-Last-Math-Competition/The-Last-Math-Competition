**English.** Definition: A shrinker is a self-similar solution, with the spectrum of its linearized operator. Conjecture: The spectral conversion formula is the explicit shift of the weighted spectrum, given for cylinders by the double roots of Jacobi fields; the supremum of the real parts of the spectrum is 1/2, attained by the space of translation modes; and the generation speed of the negative spectrum is a linear function of the shrinking rate. (weighted-spectrum shift conversion of shrinkers)

**中文。** 定义：收缩子：自相似解；其线性化算子的谱。猜想：谱换算公式为加权谱的显式平移且柱面时由雅可比场的二重根给出；谱的实部上确界为 1/2 且上确界由平移模的空间达到；负谱的生成速度为收缩速率的线性函数。（收缩子加权谱平移换算）
