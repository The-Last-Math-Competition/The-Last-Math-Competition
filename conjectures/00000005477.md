**English.** Definition: Bound-state poles are the physical singularities of the scattering. Conjecture: The residues are given explicitly by the combinatorics of fusion diagrams; each row is a three-particle subdiagram, and the coefficients are always ratios of quantum dimensions. (fusion quantum-dimension ratios of residues)
