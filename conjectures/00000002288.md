# 00000002288

**MG-28 ★** — source: 61_鞅与随机分析.md

**English.** Conjecture: Under convex order, the pathwise uniqueness of the minimal coupling in the comparison theorem for continuous martingales is a monotonicity-type condition on the SDE coefficients (minimal coupling uniqueness).

**中文。** 猜想：连续鞅的排序(comparison)定理的凸序下的极小耦合的轨道唯一性为 SDE 系数的单调型条件(极小耦合唯一)。
