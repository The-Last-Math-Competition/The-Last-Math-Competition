**English.** Definition: The random source term of hyperbolic equations as noise-driven waves. Conjecture: The energy growth rate of stochastic hyperbolic solutions is the square of the noise intensity; and the saturation of growth is dissipative damping. (stochastic hyperbolic energy growth law)
