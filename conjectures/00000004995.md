# 00000004995

**English.** Definition: A non-Kaehler CY structure is a complex structure with holonomy contained in the special unitary group but not Kaehler. Conjecture: The Betti number upper bound of the three-dimensional case is an explicit combination of two Betti numbers, satisfied by balanced manifolds and tight. (combined Betti upper bound of non-Kaehler CY)

**中文。** 定义：非凯勒 CY 结构指和乐含于特殊酉群但非凯勒的复结构。猜想：三维情形的贝蒂数上界为两个贝蒂数的显式组合,由平衡流形的例子满足且界紧。（非凯勒 CY 贝蒂数组合上界）
