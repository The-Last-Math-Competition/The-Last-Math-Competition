**LG2-07 ★** — source: 90_李群微分方程.md

**English.** Definition: The Maurer–Cartan form: the basic Lie-algebra-valued 1-form of a Lie group. Conjecture: The structural rigidity of the MC equation: the integral form of the Jacobi identity for the structure constants is dθ + ½[θ,θ] = 0, with rigidity characterized by the cohomology of the Lie algebra. (MC integral Jacobi cohomology)

**中文。** 定义：Maurer–Cartan 形式：李群的基本值 1-形式。猜想：MC 方程的结构刚性：对偶常数的 Jacobi 恒等式的积分形式为 dθ+½[θ,θ]=0 且刚性由李代数的上同调刻画。（MC 积分 Jacobi 上同调）
