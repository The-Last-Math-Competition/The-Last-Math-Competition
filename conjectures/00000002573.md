# 00000002573

**English.** Definition: A self-complementary graph is isomorphic to its complement. Conjecture: The smallest closed class of Shannon-capacity formulas for self-complementary graphs is of the square-root type; and the extremum of the closure is the 5-cycle. (self-complementary Shannon square-root closure)

**中文。** 定义：自补图为与补同构的图。猜想：自补图的 Shannon 容量的公式类最小封闭为根号型；且封闭的极值为五循环。（自补 Shannon 根号封闭）
