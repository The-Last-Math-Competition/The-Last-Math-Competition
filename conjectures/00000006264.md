**English.** Definition: The gap lower bound is the linear relation of the entropy gap with the exponent. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit lower bound of the gap and the linear relation with the exponent has slope one after normalization; the tight example of the slope is the renormalization fixed point of the quadratic family; and the upper bound of the gap is the integral of local entropy. (unit-slope gap-exponent conversion)

**中文。** 定义：缺口下界：熵缺口与指数的线性关系。猜想：换算常数为缺口的显式下界且与指数的线性关系斜率一旦归一；斜率的紧例为二次族的重整化不动点；缺口的上界为局部熵的积分。（缺口指数线性斜率一换算）
