# 00000002782

**English.** Definition: The low-dimensional skeleton structure of Banach spaces. Conjecture: Monotonicity of skeleton dimensions: the monotone decay rate of the Banach–Mazur distances of k-dimensional skeletons is exponential, with decay exponent an explicit function of cotype. (skeleton monotone law)

**中文。** 定义：Banach 空间的低茂（loweurs）结构：低维骨架。猜想：低茂维数的单调性：空间的 k 维骨架的 Banach–Mazur 距离的单调下降率为指数且下降指数为 cotype 的显式函数。（低茂骨架单调律）
