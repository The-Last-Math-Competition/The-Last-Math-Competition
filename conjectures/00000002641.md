# 00000002641

**MF-21 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: The symmetric square of Jacobi forms: the multiplicative structure of two Jacobi forms. Conjecture: The index-additive decomposition of the symmetric square: the index of the product is the sum of the two indices, and the Fourier coefficients are explicit convolution-type sums; the combination structure of the sums is given by matrix elements of Köcher-type dual pairs. (symmetric square index decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式的对称平方：两个 Jacobi 形式的乘性结构。猜想：对称平方的指标加法分解：乘积的指标为两因子指标之和且 Fourier 系数为卷积型显式和；和的组合结构为 Köcher 型对偶对的矩阵元素。（对称平方指标分解）
