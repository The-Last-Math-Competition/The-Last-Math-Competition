# 00000003715

**English.** Definition: Integral graphs denote graphs all of whose Laplacian eigenvalues are integers. Conjecture: The complete classification of integral graphs is organized by maximal integral graphs; the classification has no duplicate members up to isomorphism. (integral maximal graph complete classification)

**中文。** 定义：整谱图指一切 Laplace 特征值为整数的图。猜想：整谱图的完全分类组合为极大整谱图；且分类在同构意义下无重复成员。（整谱极大图完全分类）
