# 00000008578

**NSP-18 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of Ruelle transfer operators: the isolation of the dominant eigenvalue of weighted transfer operators of hyperbolic systems. Conjecture: Under regular potentials the dominant eigenvalue is real, simple, and isolated (a dominant isolation law); isolation implies exponential mixing (an exponential mixing law); the mixing rate constant is the second derivative of the pressure functional (a pressure constant law); and Dolgopyat type estimates give the optimal saturation of the rate (a D-optimality law). (Ruelle transfer dominant isolation pressure)

**中文。** 定义：Ruelle 传递算子的谱：双曲系统的加权转移算子的主导本征值隔离。猜想：正则势下主导本征值实且单重且隔离（主导隔离律）；且隔离蕴含指数混合（指数混合律）；混合速率常数为压力泛函的二阶导数（压力常数律）；Dolgopyat 型估计给出速率的最优饱和（D-最优律）。
