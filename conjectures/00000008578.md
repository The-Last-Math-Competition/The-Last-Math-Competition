# 00000008578

**NSP-18 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: dominant eigenvalue of weighted transition operator of spectrum: hyperbolic system of Ruelle operator. Conjecture: regular under dominant eigenvalue real and and (dominant); and implies exponent mixing (exponent mixing); mixing rate constant is two order derivative (pressure constant); Dolgopyat type estimate of pressure functional gives optimal of rate and (D- optimal).

**中文。** 定义：Ruelle 传递算子的谱：双曲系统的加权转移算子的主导本征值隔离。猜想：正则势下主导本征值实且单重且隔离（主导隔离律）；且隔离蕴含指数混合（指数混合律）；混合速率常数为压力泛函的二阶导数（压力常数律）；Dolgopyat 型估计给出速率的最优饱和（D-最优律）。
