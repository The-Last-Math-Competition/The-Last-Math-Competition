**English.** Definition: Free Brownian motion connects to SLE via multiple free random variables. Conjecture: The endpoints of the real spectral support of the circular-law family of free multiple variables are explicit polynomials; and the degree of the polynomial is the product of the degrees of freedom. (free spectral endpoint polynomials)

**中文。** 定义：自由布朗运动与 SLE 通过多重复自由随机变量联系。猜想：自由多重随机变量的圆律族的实部谱支集端点为显式多项式；且多项式的次数为自由度积。（自由谱端点多项式）
