# 00000001724

**English.** Definition: The Zilber–Pink conjecture asserts that intersections with all positive-rank abelian subvarieties are non-anomalous (unlikely intersections). Conjecture: Zilber–Pink is valid for E² minus explicit integral elliptic curves; the exceptional-subvariety list of the smallest nontrivial instance is exhausted by symmetrized Hecke correspondences; and the exceptional subvarieties are always exhausted by Hecke-symmetrized cosets.

**中文。** 定义：Zilber–Pink 猜想指与一切正秩子交换簇的交为非异常的(非可能交)。猜想：Zilber–Pink 在 E²\(显式整点椭圆曲线) 的情形有效;最小非平凡实例的例外簇列表由对称化的 Hecke 对应穷举；且例外簇恒由 Hecke 对称化陪集穷举。
