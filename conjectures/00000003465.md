# 00000003465

**GA3-05 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: Acyclic coloring colors vertices with k colors so that every bichromatic subgraph is acyclic, with acyclic chromatic number a(G) introduced by Grünbaum. Conjecture: Every planar graph satisfies a(G) ≤ 5, and girth five or more leaves a(G) ≤ 3 with only one finite candidate list remaining; the edge upper bound of acyclic-critical graphs is the tight 2.5v−2, attained by the triangular double-wheel family. (acyclic coloring critical tight bound)

**中文。** 定义：无圈染色将顶点染 k 色使每条双染色子图无圈，无圈色数 a(G) 由 Grünbaum 提出。猜想：一切平面图 a(G) ≤ 5 且围长五以上 a(G) ≤ 3 的例外仅剩一个有限候选列表；无圈临界图的边数上界为 2.5v−2 的紧界且紧性由三角双轮族达到。（无圈染色临界紧界）
