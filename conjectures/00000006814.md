# 00000006814

**English.** Definition: Sparsity of chirp signals with the fractional Fourier transform. Conjecture: Sparse recovery of chirps holds, the domain of recovery being the fractional domain. (fractional-domain chirp sparse recovery)

**中文。** 定义：chirp 信号的稀疏：分数 Fourier。猜想：chirp 的稀疏：线性调频的稀疏恢复且复的域为分数域。（chirp 稀疏分数域线性调频稀疏恢复分数 Fourier 恢复域）
