# 00000002776

**English.** Definition: Gowers' block-sequence theorem: every infinite-dimensional space contains an unconditional basic sequence or a copy of ℓ₁ or c₀ (proven). Conjecture: The strengthening of the block dichotomy: every space contains either a symmetric basic block sequence or a hereditarily indecomposable substructure, the dichotomy criterion being the uniform bounded deviation of block projections. (Gowers dichotomy strengthening)

**中文。** 定义：Gowers 分块序列定理：无穷维空间含无条件基本序列或 ℓ₁ 或 c₀ 的副本（已证）。猜想：分块二分的加强：任意空间含对称基本块序列或 hereditarily indecomposable 子结构且二分判据为块投影的一致有界偏差。（Gowers 二分加强）
