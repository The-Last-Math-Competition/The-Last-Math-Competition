**English.** Definition: The Stein kernel is the vector-field kernel characterizing the regularity of a density. Conjecture: The conversion constant between the L^p bound of the Stein kernel and the Sobolev regularity of the density is an explicit dimension-free value, and the bound is optimal, attained by Gaussian measures. (optimal Stein-Sobolev conversion constant)

**中文。** 定义：Stein 核指刻画密度正则性的向量场核函数。猜想：Stein 核的 L^p 界与密度 Sobolev 正则性的换算常数为维数无关的显式值,界由高斯测度达到最优。（Stein-Sobolev 最优换算常数）
