**PHT-19 ★** — source: 316_持久同调II_统计与稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: Extreme statistics of topological noise: the asymptotic distribution of the longest persistent bar under pure noise. Conjecture: Under pure noise the longest persistent bar length scales by a Gumbel law with normalization constant an explicit reciprocal of logarithmic multiplicity; the location parameter of the Gumbel is given explicitly by the intensity integral of the point process. (noise extreme Gumbel law)

**中文。** 定义：拓扑噪声的极值统计：随机噪声的最长持久条的渐近分布。猜想：纯噪声下最长持久条的长度按 Gumbel 律标度且归一化常数为对数重数的显式倒数；Gumbel 的位置参数由点过程的强度积分显式给出。（噪声极值 Gumbel 律）
