# 00000006232

**English.** Definition: Sheafification is the covering-refinement structure of the sheafification process of presheaves. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the covering refinement and given by the covering basis of the site; the refinement step count is logarithmic with base the covering multiplicity; and the adjoint of sheafification is inclusion, the counit of the adjoint pair a local isomorphism. (covering-refinement sheafification conversion constant)

**中文。** 定义：层化：预层的层化过程的覆盖精细结构。猜想：换算常数为覆盖精细的显式且由位点的覆盖基给出；精细的步数为对数型且底为覆盖重数；层化的伴随为包含且伴随对的余单位为局部同构。（覆盖精细层化换算常数）
