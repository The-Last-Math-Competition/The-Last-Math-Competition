# 00000007756

**English.** Definition: Periodic structure modulo p: for an integer polynomial f and prime p, the functional graph of f on F_p consists of cycles with pendant trees. Conjecture: For fixed f(x)=x^2+1 as p ranges over primes, the frequency of shapes with tail length exactly 1 converges to the explicit constant lambda = 3 - 4 log 2; and the ratio of maximal cycle length to p converges to a Dickman-type depletion distribution, differing from the random mapping model only in the second-order correction, which is determined by the prime factors of the discriminant of f. (Poonen shape statistics)

**中文。** 定义：周期点模 p 的周期结构：对整系数多项式 f 与素数 p，映射 f: F_p→F_p 的函数图由循环带悬挂树构成。Poonen 分类给出形态类型。猜想：固定 f(x)=x^2+1，当 p 遍历素数时出现 tail 长度恰为 1 的形态频率收敛到 λ=3-4log2 型显式常数；且最大循环长与 p 的比值分布收敛到 Dickman 型 depletion 分布，与随机映射模型仅在第二阶修正项不同，修正由 f 的判别式素因子决定。
