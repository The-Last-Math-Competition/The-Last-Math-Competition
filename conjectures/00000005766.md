# 00000005766

**English.** Definition: The motivic homotopy category is the stable spectrum framework of motives. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the spectral gap of slice convergence, given for very effective sheaves by the zero-slice difference; the spectral gap is the convergence rate of the slice tower, no slower than the negative first power of t; and the convergence is exact within a finite t-range, the exact window being the range of effectivity. (zero-slice difference spectral-gap conversion constant)

**中文。** 定义：母题同伦范畴：稳定谱框架（ motives 的稳定同伦）。猜想：换算常数为切片收敛的谱隙显式且非常有效层时由零切片差给出；谱隙为切片塔的收敛速率且速率不慢于 t 的负一次幂；收敛在有限 t 范围内精确且精确窗为有效性的范围。（切片零差谱隙换算常数）
