**English.** Definition: Flat families: the algebraic criterion of deformation. Conjecture: The Hilbert-function invariance of flatness: a family is flat if and only if the Hilbert polynomial is constant, with the computation of the criterion the equidimensional verification of graded components. (flat Hilbert equidimensional verification)
