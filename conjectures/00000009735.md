**MAD-05 ★** — source: 310_数学的自动发现.md

**English.** Definition: Knuth-Bendix completion: the critical-pair convergence procedure for term rewriting systems. Conjecture: The termination probability of completion of randomly generated term rewriting systems is an explicit power law in alphabet size; the expected number of new rules needed for termination is finite and a linear function of critical pair density. (Knuth-Bendix completion termination probability)

**中文。** 定义：Knuth–Bendix 完备化：项重写系统的临界对收敛程序。猜想：随机生成项重写系统的完备化终止概率为字母表大小的显式幂律；终止所需的新规则数的期望有限且为临界对密度的线性函数。（KB 完备化终止概率）
