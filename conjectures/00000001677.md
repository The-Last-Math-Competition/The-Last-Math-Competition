# 00000001677

**English.** Definition: The spectral gap of Apollonian networks. Conjecture: The normalized-Laplacian spectral gap of the random Apollonian network converges to an explicit constant c > 1/2 (the Apollonian spectral gap).

**中文。** 定义：Apollonian 网络的谱隙。猜想：随机 Apollonian 网络的归一化 Laplacian 谱隙收敛于显式常数 c > 1/2(Apollonian 谱隙)。
