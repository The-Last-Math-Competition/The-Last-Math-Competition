**English.** Definition: The spectrum of the Jacobi operator of the stable category (the stabilization of homologically triangulated categories) of noncommutative varieties: the spectrum jac_spec(A) of cluster residues. Conjecture: jac_spec is a discrete set of algebraic numbers (an algebraic discreteness law); the infimum of the gaps of the spectrum is 1 (a gap lower bound), the limit points of the gap spectrum being Fekete-type sequences with unbounded denominators; and the spectrum agrees with the walls of the Fan structure of the g-vectors of the cluster algebra (a spectrum-wall agreement law), the exception being degenerate varieties (the unique exceptional point of the degeneration chain Gr_2^5 of the Grassmannian Gr(2,n)). (Jacobi spectrum Fan wall agreement exception)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的稳定范畴（同调三角化的稳定化）的 Jacobi 算子的谱：cluster 留数的谱 jac_spec(A)。猜想：jac_spec 为代数数的离散集（代数离散律）；且谱的间隙的下确界为 1（间隙下界），间隙谱的极限点为分母无界的 Fekete 型点列；谱与 cluster 代数的 g-向量的 Fan 结构的壁的一致（谱-壁一致律），一致性的例外为退化簇（Grassmannian Gr(2,n) 的退化链 Gr₂⁵ 的唯一例外点）。
