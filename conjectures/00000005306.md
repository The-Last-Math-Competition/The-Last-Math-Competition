**RS2-16 ◆** — source: 162_组合的代数_表示稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: The stable tensor spectrum is the nonzero pattern of limiting coefficients. Conjecture: The spectrum classification of rectangular partitions is characterized explicitly by the effective region of hyperbolic inequalities, the boundary given by the support function, and the classification is closed, with the vertices of the effective region being the maximal lattice points of the hook lengths of the partitions. (closed hyperbolic classification of the rectangular spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：稳定张量谱指极限系数的非零模式。猜想：矩形分拆的谱分类由双曲不等式的有效区域显式刻画,边界由支撑函数给出且分类封闭。(矩形谱双曲区域封闭分类)；且有效区域顶点为分拆钩长的极大格点。
