# 00000000836

**English.** Conjecture: In Gaussian space, the product of the optimal constants of the three-parameter interpolation family of fractional Sobolev and Gagliardo–Nirenberg inequalities satisfies an identity (a product identity for the Caffarelli–Kohn–Nirenberg family).

**中文。** 猜想：高斯空间中分数次 Sobolev 与 Gagliardo–Nirenberg 族的三参数插值不等式的最优常数乘积恒等式(Caffarelli–Kohn–Nirenberg 家族的乘积恒等)。
