# 00000000606

**English.** Definition: The Alexander polynomial Δ_K(t). Conjecture: There exists a genus-2 knot for which the Mahler measure of Δ_K(t) is transcendental (algebraic independence on the knot side of the Boyd relation between Mahler measure and hyperbolic volume).

**中文。** 定义：Alexander 多项式 Δ_K(t)。猜想：存在亏格 2 纽结使 Δ_K(t) 的 Mahler 测度为超越数(Mahler 测度与双曲体积的 Boyd 关系在纽结侧的代数独立性)。
