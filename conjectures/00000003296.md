# 00000003296

**English.** Definition: Jacobi elliptic functions: the sn, cn, dn analogues of trigonometry. Conjecture: The Jacobi imaginary transformation: the transformation of Jacobi functions at imaginary modulus is an explicit matrix of modular transformations, with the kernel of the matrix the square of the Landen transformation. (Jacobi imaginary transformation Landen square kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 椭圆函数：sn, cn, dn 三角类似。猜想：Jacobi 虚变换：虚模数的 Jacobi 函数的变换为模变换的显式矩阵且矩阵的核为 Landen 变换的平方。（Jacobi 虚变换 Landen 平方核）
