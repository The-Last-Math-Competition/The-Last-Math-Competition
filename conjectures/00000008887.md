**English.** Definition: Snort and Col: extremes of coloring games. Conjecture: Snort has complete values for graph spectra (a complete value law); Col is dual to Snort under left-right exchange (a duality law); the arithmetic of the duality is the reversal snort equals minus col (a reversal law); and the exceptions to reversal are asymmetric graphs (an exception law). (snort col duality reversal asymmetric exception)

**中文。** 定义：snort 与 col：着色游戏的极值。猜想：snort 的（图的谱的完全值）的（完全值律）；且 col 的（对偶（左-右交换）的）的（对偶律）；对偶的算术（snort=−col 的翻转）的（翻转律）；翻转的例外（非对称图）的（例外律）。
