# 00000007515

**English.** Definition: Poincare duality with the duality structure of etale cohomology. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the twist deficit of duality, the twist-correction coefficient being the tame symbol product; the values of the coefficient are roots of unity whose orders divide p minus one; and the deficit vanishes at geometric connectivity. (twist-deficit coefficient conversion)

**中文。** 定义：Poincare 对偶：etale 上同调的对偶结构。猜想：换算常数为对偶的扭转亏且扭转修正系数为 Tame 符号积；系数的取值为单位根且根的阶整除 p 减一；亏的消没在几何连通处。（扭转亏系数换算常数）
