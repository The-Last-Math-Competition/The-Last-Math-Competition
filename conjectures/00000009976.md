**NCR-06 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Prime spectra of PI rings: the Zariski topology of the set of prime ideals of noncommutative rings. Conjecture: The prime spectrum of a PI ring is a spectral topological space, and the comparison topology coincides with the Zariski topology; the coincidence follows from finiteness of prime localization chains, with finiteness a polynomial bound in the polynomial identity degree. (PI prime spectrum spectral topology coincidence)

**中文。** 定义：PI 环的素谱：非交换环的素理想集合的 Zariski 拓扑。猜想：PI 环的素谱为谱拓扑空间且比较拓扑与 Zariski 拓扑重合；重合性由素的局部化链的有限性给出且有限性为多项式恒等式次数的多项式界。（PI 素谱谱拓扑重合）
