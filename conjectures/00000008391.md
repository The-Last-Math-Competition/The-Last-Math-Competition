# 00000008391

**MIB-11 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: Slant invariants of motives: quantities not definable under rational equivalence but invariant under motivic equivalence. Conjecture: Slant invariants exist in the motivic category of threefolds, and the first example is given by the torsion of the middle Jacobian motive of a threefold hypersurface; the torsion slant invariant takes values in a group of order two and is semicontinuous in degenerating families. (slant invariant torsion first example)

**中文。** 定义：母题的斜不变量指在有理等价下不可定义但母题等价下不变的量。猜想：斜不变量在三维簇的母题范畴中存在且第一个例子由三维超曲面的中 Jacobi 母题的挠给出；挠斜不变量的取值为二阶群且在退化族中半连续。（斜不变量挠首例）
