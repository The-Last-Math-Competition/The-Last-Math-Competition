**English.** Definition: The criticality of the Matsubara frequency cutoff of finite-temperature renormalization: mat_crit. Conjecture: The loss constant of mat_crit (criticality loss from the Matsubara cutoff) is the cube of pi T/Lambda (a cubic loss law); the vanishing of the truncation error in the zero-temperature recovery (T to 0) is e^{-Lambda/T} (an exponential vanishing law); and the dimensional jump of the regularization of the infrared disaster of thermal field theory (the bosonic IR disaster) occurs at d = 3 (a 3-dimensional thermal IR law), with critical exponent of the jump 1/2 (a thermal jump half-exponent). (Matsubara cubic loss thermal IR jump)
