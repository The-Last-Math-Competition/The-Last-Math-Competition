# 00000000666

**English.** Definition: The Floer counterpart of Khovanov thinness. Conjecture: The symmetric difference between Kh-thin knots and quasi-alternating knots has positive but strictly sub-1/2 density as crossing numbers grow (the true extent of thinness).

**中文。** 定义：Khovanov 的 Thin 的 Floer 对应。猜想：Kh-thin 纽结与 quasi-alternating 纽结的差集在交叉数 → ∞ 的密度为正但 < 1/2(thin 的真实范围)。
