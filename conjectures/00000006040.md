**HS2-30 △** — source: 186_哈密顿系统.md

**English.** Definition: Conserved quantities and reduced phase spaces are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two constrained systems with identical conserved quantities but non-isomorphic reduced phase spaces, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same algebra but different isotropy types. (same conserved-different isotropy reduced-space separation)

**中文。** 定义：守恒量与约化相空间指两层。猜想：存在两约束系统的一切守恒量相同而约化相空间不同构,分离由同代数异迷向型的显式对实现。（守恒同-约化相空间异迷向分离）
