# 00000000866

**English.** Definition: A Cheeger set minimizes h(E) = |∂E|/|E|; the regularity of its convex hull is the boundary smoothness. Conjecture: The convex hull of a planar Cheeger set is a C^{1,1} domain with curvature norm controlled by 1/h(E); in three dimensions the corresponding hull boundary is C^{1,1} away from finitely many cone points.

**中文。** 定义：Cheeger 集合指最小化 h(E)=|∂E|/|E| 的集合,其凸壳的正则性指边界光滑度。猜想：平面 Cheeger 集的凸壳为 C^{1,1} 域,且曲率范数被 1/h(E) 控制;该正则性在三维对应为凸壳边界除有限个锥点外 C^{1,1}。
