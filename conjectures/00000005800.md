# 00000005800

**English.** Definition: The low-order moments and information geometry are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two models with identical low-order moments but non-isomorphic information geometries, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same moments but different mixing coefficients. (same moments-different information-geometric mixing separation)
