# 00000002560

**English.** Definition: Coronization decompositions: dyadic coron decompositions of uniformly rectifiable sets. Conjecture: The minimal number of coronas of the decomposition is polynomial in the David–Semmes deficit parameter of the set; the polynomial exponent is 2, and the optimal algorithm is the greedy contraction of Christ cubes. (Coronization complexity)

**中文。** 定义：Coronization 分解：一致可校正集的 Dyadic 腔体分解。猜想：分解的最小腔体数为集的 David–Semmes 亏损参数的多项式；多项式指数为 2 且最优算法由 Christ 立方的贪心收缩给出。（Coronization 复杂度）
