# 00000001726

**English.** Definition: An effective explicit version of Zannier's theorem (finiteness of integral points outside exceptional subvarieties in powers of C×E). Conjecture: The degree of exceptional subvarieties is at most 4; the extremal exceptional subvarieties are the unions of orbit images under all power maps in C×E, classified explicitly by a nondegeneracy criterion.

**中文。** 定义：Zannier 定理(ℂ×E 的幂中不属于例外子簇的整点有限性)的可行显式化。猜想：例外簇的次数 ≤4;极值例外簇为 ℂ×E 中的所有幂映射下的轨迹像的并,由无扰性判据显式分类。
