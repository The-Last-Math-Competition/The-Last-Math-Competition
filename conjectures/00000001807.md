# 00000001807

**CV-27 ★** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: Oka-ness of holomorphic line bundles on the torus T² means the flexibility of their holomorphic sections. Conjecture: A line bundle L with c₁(L) ≠ 0 is not Oka; the trivial bundle is Oka; Oka-ness is decided by the binary condition of whether the first Chern class vanishes.

**中文。** 定义：环面 T² 上的全纯线丛的 Oka 性指其全纯截面的弹性。猜想：线丛 L(c₁(L)≠0) 非 Oka;平凡丛为 Oka;Oka 性由第一 Chern 类是否为零二值判定。
