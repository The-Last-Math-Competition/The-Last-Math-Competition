# 00000004291

**AG5-21 ★** — source: 128_交换群论.md

**English.** Definition: The Jordan type of an automorphism is its nilpotent canonical form as an operator on the module. Conjecture: The number of automorphisms of a p-group with fixed Jordan type is a quasi-polynomial in p, with period the height of the type and leading coefficient depending only on the large blocks of the type. (Jordan quasi-polynomial of p-group automorphisms)

**中文。** 定义：自同构的 Jordan 型指其作为模算子的幂零标准型。猜想：具固定 Jordan 型的 p-群自同构个数为 p 的拟多项式,周期为型的高度,首项系数只依赖型的大块。（p-群自同构 Jordan 拟多项式）
