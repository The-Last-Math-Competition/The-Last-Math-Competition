# 00000007858

**English.** Definition: Defective coloring rounds in distributed randomization: in the LOCAL model each vertex randomly picks a color from its palette each round and keeps it unless conflicting with its neighborhood (defective coloring allows d conflicts per vertex). Conjecture: Delta+1-distance-proper coloring on graphs with Delta at least Delta_0 needs exactly Theta(log Delta) rounds; the logarithmic correction of the constant lower bound log Delta/log log Delta is dictated by the iterative depth of local palette sparsification; and the randomized speedup is Theta(log* n), separated from deterministic rounds by 2^{Theta(log* n)}. (distributed coloring round complexity)

**中文。** 定义：分布式随机染色的缺陷轮数：LOCAL 模型下每轮每点随机从调色板选色、与邻域冲突则保持（defective coloring 允许每点 d 个冲突）。猜想：Δ+1 距离正则染色在 Δ≥Δ₀ 的图上需要且只需要 Θ(log Δ) 轮；常数下界为 log Δ/log log Δ 的对数修正由局部全染色（palette sparsification）的信息迭代深度决定；随机化的指数加速为 Θ(log* n)，与确定性轮数形成 2^{Θ(log* n)} 的分离。
