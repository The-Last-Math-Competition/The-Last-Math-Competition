**AN2-18 ★** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Definition: The Whittaker coefficients A(1,n) of GL₃ Maass forms. Conjecture: The mean square of |A(1,n)| is asymptotic to c times n; and the constant c is the Archimedean factor at the central point of the standard L-function. (GL3 Whittaker mean-square constant)

**中文。** 定义：GL₃ Maass 形的 Whittaker 系数 A(1,n)。猜想：|A(1,n)| 的均方渐近为 c 乘 n；且常数 c 为标准 L 函数在中心点的 Archimedes 因子。（GL3 Whittaker 均方常数）
