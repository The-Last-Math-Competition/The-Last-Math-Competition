**English.** Definition: A Boolean topos is a topos satisfying the law of excluded middle internally, and a fixed-point combinator is the self-reference operator of combinatory logic. Conjecture: The smallest topos admitting a Boolean internalized fixed-point combinator is an explicit extension of the category of two-point sets, with exactly 4 objects. (smallest Boolean fixed-point topos with four objects)

**中文。** 定义：布尔拓扑斯指内部满足排中律的拓扑斯,不动点组合子指组合逻辑的自指算子。猜想：容纳布尔内部化不动点组合子的最小拓扑斯为二点集合范畴的显式扩张,其对象数恰为 4。（布尔不动点最小四对象）
