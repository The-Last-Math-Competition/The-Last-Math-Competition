# 00000001664

**English.** Definition: Vizing's conjecture states γ(G□H) ≥ γ(G)γ(H) for domination numbers. Conjecture: Vizing's conjecture holds; the classification of equality graphs is complete for tree factors: γ(T□H) = γ(T)γ(H) if and only if the domination product at every vertex of T is attained by a matching.
