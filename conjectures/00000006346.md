**QG2-06 ◆** — source: 197_量子群II.md

**English.** Definition: The global basis is the dual pairing of the canonical one. Conjecture: The transition matrix of the pairing is always the identity; the inverse of the genus part is an involution; and the square of the involution is the identity map. (identity transition of global canonical bases)

**中文。** 定义：全局基为典范的对偶配对。猜想：配对的转移矩阵恒为恒等；且亏格部分的逆为对合；且对合的平方为恒等映射。（全局典范转移恒等）
