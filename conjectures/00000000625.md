# 00000000625

**English.** Definition: A twist composition of height ≤ 2 is a knot diagram formed as the Minkowski sum of two twist regions. Conjecture: Every hyperbolic knot (non-torus, non-satellite) with volume at most 3v₈ is a twist composition of height ≤ 2, and this classification acquires exactly one additional exception at volume ≤ 4v₈.

**中文。** 定义：高度 ≤ 2 的扭结组合指由两个 twist 区域经闵可夫斯基和构成的纽结图。猜想：体积不超过 3v₈ 的双曲纽结全体(非 torus 非卫星)均为高度 ≤ 2 的扭结组合,且该分类在体积 ≤ 4v₈ 时恰好新增一个例外。
