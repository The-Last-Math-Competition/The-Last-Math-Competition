**DC3-08 ◆** — source: 205_代数几何IX_导出范畴II.md

**English.** Definition: Walls and chambers are the stability stratification structure. Conjecture: The two-dimensional walls are linear forms and the form spectrum is finite; the combination of the spectrum is the contraction lattice; and the combination of the contraction lattice is the descending manifold. (linear contraction-lattice spectrum of two-dimensional walls)

**中文。** 定义：壁与室为稳定分层结构。猜想：二维的壁为线性型且型谱有限；且谱的组合为缩并格；且缩并格的组合为下降流形。（二维壁线性缩并格谱）
