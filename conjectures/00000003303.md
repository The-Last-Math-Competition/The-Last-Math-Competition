**English.** Definition: Legendre functions: the radial part of spherical harmonics. Conjecture: The connection relations of Legendre: the connection coefficients of adjacent orders form a three-term chain, with the limit of the chain the Airy-type transition. (Legendre connection Airy transition limit)
