# 00000009522

**QIC-02 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: The quantum capacity Q(phi) is the supremal rate of reliable quantum state transmission with arbitrarily many uses, given by the LSD regularization Q(phi) = lim_n (1/n) max_rho I_c(rho, phi^{tensor n}), where I_c(rho,phi) = S(phi(rho)) - S(phi^c(rho)) is the coherent information and phi^c the complementary channel; the private capacity P(phi) is regularized similarly. Conjecture: P(phi) at least Q(phi) for every channel, and both strict inequalities P > Q and Q > 0 are positive-measure events in the channel space of dimension d at least 3; the ratio P(phi)/Q(phi) has no uniform upper bound at fixed dimension, i.e. there exist channel families making the ratio arbitrarily large. (private-quantum capacity separation)

**中文。** 定义：量子容量 Q(φ) 为无穷次使用下可靠量子态传输速率的上确界，由 LSD 正则化 Q(φ)=lim_n (1/n) max_ρ I_c(ρ,φ^{⊗n}) 给出，I_c(ρ,φ)=S(φ(ρ))−S(φ^c(ρ)) 为相干信息；私人容量 P(φ) 同样正则化定义，φ^c 为互补信道。猜想：P(φ)≥Q(φ) 对一切信道成立且严格不等式 P>Q 与 Q>0 在维数 d≥3 的信道空间中均为正测度事件；比值 P(φ)/Q(φ) 在固定维数下无一致上界，即可构造信道族使该比值任意大。（私人-量子容量分离）
