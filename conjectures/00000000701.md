**English.** Definition: The Browkin I algorithm (p-adic continued fractions: partial quotients taken from representatives of F_p with |a| < p/2). Conjecture: For almost every ξ ∈ Q_p, the Browkin partial quotients are uniformly distributed over the representative set (in the σ-finite Haar sense).
