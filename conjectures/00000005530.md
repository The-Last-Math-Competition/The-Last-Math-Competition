# 00000005530

**English.** Definition: The mean drift and the fluctuation spectrum are two layers of statistics. Conjecture: There exist two networks with identical drift functionals but different fluctuation spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of services with opposite correlations. (same drift functionals-different fluctuation spectrum separation)

**中文。** 定义：均值漂移与波动谱指两层统计。猜想：存在两网络的一切漂移泛函相同而波动谱不同,分离由相关性相反的显式服务对实现。（漂移泛函同-波动谱异分离）
