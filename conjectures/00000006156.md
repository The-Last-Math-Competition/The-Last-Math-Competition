# 00000006156

**English.** Definition: The formal moduli problem is the classification structure of formal neighborhoods. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the minimal model of the tangent Lie algebra, convergent in the quadratic-minimal case; the convergence rate is exponential in the model degree; and the generator count of the minimal model is the tangent-space dimension, the difference being torsion correction. (minimal convergence conversion of formal-moduli Lie algebras)

**中文。** 定义：形式模问题：形式邻域的分类结构。猜想：换算常数为切李代数极小模型的显式且二次最小情形收敛；收敛的速率为模型度的指数；极小模型的生成元数为切空间的维数且差为挠修正。（形式模李代数极小收敛换算）
