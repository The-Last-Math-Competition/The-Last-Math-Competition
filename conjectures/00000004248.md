**English.** Definition: The Bauer-Furuta refinement is the stable homotopy lift of the SW invariants. Conjecture: In dimension four the corresponding stable homotopy group is a finite quotient of the integer group, and the order of the quotient equals exactly the absolute value of the SW invariant. (BF stable group as SW quotient)

**中文。** 定义：Bauer–Furuta 精细化指 SW 不变量的稳定同伦提升。猜想：其稳定同伦群的四维情形为整数群的有限商,商的阶恰等于 SW 不变量的绝对值。（BF 稳定群=SW 商）
