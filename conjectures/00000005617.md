# 00000005617

**English.** Definition: The reconstruction threshold is the parameter bound of feasible reconstruction under a modulus system. Conjecture: The threshold formula is an explicit function of the moduli, and the correction on lost residues is a linear discount; the discount rate is the ratio of lost residues to the modulus count; and the tight example of the threshold is a geometric distribution of moduli with ratio 2. (linear-discount threshold of lost residues)

**中文。** 定义：重建阈值：模数系下可行重建（中国剩余回代）的参数界。猜想：阈值公式为模数的显式函数且余式丢失时的修正是线性折扣；折扣率为丢失余式个数占模数个数的比；阈值的紧例为模数几何级数分布且公比为 2。（余式丢失线性折扣阈值）
