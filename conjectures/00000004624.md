# 00000004624

**English.** Definition: Minimal surface counting is the number of embedded minimal surfaces in distinct isotopy classes. Conjecture: In a compact three-manifold the number of minimal surfaces of area at most L grows at least like L squared, and the lower bound is realized by truncated Weierstrass families. (quadratic lower bound of minimal surface counts)

**中文。** 定义：极小曲面计数指互不同痕的嵌入极小曲面个数。猜想：紧三维流形中面积不超过 L 的极小曲面个数至少按 L 的平方增长,下界由截断 Weierstrass 族实现。（极小曲面计数平方下界）
