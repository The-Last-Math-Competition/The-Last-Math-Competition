# 00000001960

**English.** Definition: The cokernel of the coarse Baum–Connes map is the K-theoretic difference of the assembly map. Conjecture: The torsion part of the cokernel is an explicit tower of Z/2 (its length given by the 2-rank of the lattice); there is no higher torsion (i.e. the cokernel is entirely 2-primary).

**中文。** 定义：粗 Baum–Connes 映射的余核指装配映射的 K 理论差。猜想：余核的扭部分为 Z/2 的显式塔(长度由格的 2-秩);无更高扭(即余核完全由 2-挠控制)。
