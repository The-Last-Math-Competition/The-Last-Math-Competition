**English.** Definition: Dimension-free comparison is the Gaussian comparison decoupled from dimension. Conjecture: The constant for convex-Lipschitz cases is the unit spectrum and the kernel of closure is the concentration window; the window width is the uniform bound on the variance; and the tight example of the constant is the Rademacher sum. (unit-spectrum concentration window of convex Lipschitz functions)

**中文。** 定义：维数无关比较：与维数脱钩的高斯比较。猜想：凸利普希茨情形的常数为单位谱且封闭的核为集中窗；窗宽为方差的一致界；常数的紧例为拉德马赫和。（凸利普希茨集中窗单位谱）
