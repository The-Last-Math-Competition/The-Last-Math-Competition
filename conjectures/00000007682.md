# 00000007682

**QDE-22 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: The linearization coefficients c_{i,j,k}(q) of dual q-Hahn polynomials are defined by R_i R_j = sum c_{i,j,k}(q) R_k. Conjecture: The coefficient-absolute-value sum of c_{i,j,k}(q) at fixed i+j attains its minimum at the two endpoints k=0 and k=i+j, and its profile in k is a log-concave sequence. (log-concave profile of q-Hahn linearization)

**中文。** 定义：对偶 q-Hahn 多项式的线性化系数 c_{i,j,k}(q) 由 R_i R_j=Σ_k c_{i,j,k}(q) R_k 定义。猜想：c_{i,j,k}(q) 的系数绝对值之和 Σ|[c_{i,j,k}]_m|（[·]_m 表示 q^m 的系数）在 i+j 固定时于 k=0 与 k=i+j 两端点取得最小值，且其关于 k 的轮廓为对数凹序列。
