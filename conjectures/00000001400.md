# 00000001400

**HT-10 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: The Picard group Pic(S) of the sphere spectrum is the group of invertible objects of its suspension-spectrum category. Conjecture: Pic(S) ≅ Z ⊕ Z/24 ⊕ (a finite 2-primary group), where Z/24 is realized by the Anderson spectrum and the torsion part has order exactly 2^{11}; the complete structure is given by an explicit map from the Picard group of tmf.

**中文。** 定义：球谱 S 的 Picard 群 Pic(S) 指其悬挂谱范畴的可逆对象群。猜想：Pic(S) ≅ Z ⊕ Z/24 ⊕(有限 2-准素群),其中 Z/24 由 Anderson 谱实现,扭部分的阶恰为 2^{11};完整结构由 tmf 的 Picard 群的显式映射给出。
