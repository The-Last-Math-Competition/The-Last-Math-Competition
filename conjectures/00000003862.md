# 00000003862

**RM3-12 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: Macdonald scaling is the passage from Macdonald polynomials to Jack polynomials in the limit t = q^θ, q → 1. Conjecture: In the scaling limit, the θ-dependence of normalized moments is only through powers of (θ−1)/θ, with the exponent exactly |λ| − ℓ(λ). (Macdonald scaling exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Macdonald 缩放指极限 t = q^θ、q → 1 下 Macdonald 多项式向 Jack 多项式的过渡。猜想：缩放极限中归一化矩的 θ 依赖仅为 (θ−1)/θ 的幂,幂次恰为 |λ| − ℓ(λ)。（Macdonald 缩放幂次）
