**English.** Definition: A Warfield group is a group containing a completely decomposable pure subgroup. Conjecture: The correction term of the local-global principle is the torsion sum of the Warfield invariants, and the difference between the global and local sides is exactly an explicit binary expansion of prime powers. (binary expansion of the Warfield correction term)

**中文。** 定义：Warfield 群指含完全分解纯子群的群。猜想：局部-全局原则的修正项为 Warfield 不变量的扭和,全局与局部之差恰为素数幂的显式二进展开。（Warfield 修正项二进展开）
