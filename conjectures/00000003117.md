**English.** Definition: The Cannon–Thurston map: the boundary extension of proper embeddings. Conjecture: The fiber structure of CT maps: the fibers of the boundary map of a normal subgroup form a boundary-type stratification of the quotient group, with the collapse of the stratification the equivariant retraction of the subgroup. (CT fiber stratification collapse)
