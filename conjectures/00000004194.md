# 00000004194

**English.** Definition: The epsilon^2 energy of the Gamma-convergence is the next-order expansion of the functional. Conjecture: The sub-leading term is the integral of the anisotropic weight of the interface times the square of the mean curvature, with explicit coefficient, and equality can be verified directly on spherically symmetric interfaces. (structure formula of the Gamma sub-leading term)
