# 00000001529

**English.** Definition: The Nikolaus–Scholze t-structure on TP (topological periodic cyclic homology over spherical Witt vectors). Conjecture: The symmetry of the image and kernel of the norm map of TP(Z_p) is an explicit Frobenius half-integer shift (TP symmetry).

**中文。** 定义：TP(球状 Witt 向量) 的 Nikolaus–Scholze t-结构。猜想：TP(ℤ_p) 的范数映射的像与核的对称性为 Frobenius 半整数移位的显式(TP 对称性)。
