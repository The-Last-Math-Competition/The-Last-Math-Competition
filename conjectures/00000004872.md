# 00000004872

**English.** Definition: An upper-bound certificate is the combinatorial data verifying an inequality. Conjecture: The length lower bound of any certificate of an upper bound of a finite Ramsey number is an explicit quantity of squared type in that number, consistent with the verified intervals and completely combinatorial. (length lower bound of Ramsey upper-bound certificates)

**中文。** 定义：上界证书指验证不等式的组合数据。猜想：有限拉姆齐数上界的任何证书长度下界为该数的平方型显式量,下界与已验证区间一致且组合表述完全。（拉姆齐上界证书长度下界）
