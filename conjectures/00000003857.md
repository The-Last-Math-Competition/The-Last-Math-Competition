**English.** Definition: The circular Jacobi β-ensemble denotes the β-gas on the circle with weight |z|^{2a}|1−z|^{2b}. Conjecture: Every k-point correlation function is given explicitly by Jack-polynomial coefficients, and the equilibrium density loses single-interval support exactly when |a| or |b| reaches 1/2. (Jacobi beta support criticality)

**中文。** 定义：circular Jacobi β-系综指圆上带 |z|^{2a}|1−z|^{2b} 型权的 β-气。猜想：任意 k-点相关函数由 Jack 多项式系数显式给出,且平衡密度失去单区间支撑当且仅当 |a| 或 |b| 达到 1/2。（Jacobi-β 支撑临界）
