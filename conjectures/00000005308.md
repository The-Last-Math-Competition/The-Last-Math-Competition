**English.** Definition: The Saxl conjecture asserts that the tensor of the staircase partition contains the sign representation. Conjecture: The staircase conversion above order nine is given by covering induction on triangular partitions, with induction step one, supported by small-example decompositions. (Saxl staircase triangular covering induction)

**中文。** 定义：Saxl 猜想指阶梯分拆张量含符号表示。猜想：阶梯的换算在九阶以上由三角分拆的覆盖归纳给出,归纳步长为一且由小例分解支撑。（Saxl 阶梯三角归纳覆盖）
