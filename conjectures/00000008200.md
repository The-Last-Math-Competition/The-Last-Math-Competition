# 00000008200

**English.** Definition: The nonabelian boundary of the hidden subgroup problem for quantum algorithms: the measurement count dih_meas(n) of the efficiency of HSP on dihedral groups (Kitaev's quantum measurement). Conjecture: dih_meas (the optimal number of measurements for HSP on Dih_{2n}) is O(log n) (a logarithmic measurement law); the classical complexity of the post-processing of measurements (circle-to-line reconstruction) is O(n^{1/2}) (a half-power post-processing law), the classical lower bound for the half-power law being n^{1/2-o(1)} (a lower-bound tightness); and the efficiency boundary of nonabelian HSP (the polynomial law for solvable groups) is polynomial for solvable groups (a solvable polynomial law), the minimal unsolvable group being A_5 (an A5 unsolvability threshold), with the necessary measurement count for HSP on A_5 at least n^c for a polynomial exponent c (an A5 polynomial exponent law). (dihedral HSP logarithmic A5 boundary)

**中文。** 定义：量子算法的隐藏子群问题的非阿贝尔边界：HSP 在二面体群上的效率（Kitaev 的量子测量）的测量次数 dih_meas(n)。猜想：dih_meas（Dih_{2n} 的 HSP 的测量的最优次数）为 O(log n)（对数测量律）；且测量的后处理（圆到直线的重构）的经典复杂度为 O(n^{1/2})（半次后处理律），半次律的下界（经典下界）为 n^{1/2-o(1)}（下界紧性）；非阿贝尔 HSP 的效率边界（可解群的多项式律）为可解群的多项式（可解多项式律），不可解的最小群为 A₅（A5 不可解阈），A₅ 的 HSP 的测量的必要次数 ≥ n^{c} 的多项式指数（A5 多项式指数律）。
