# 00000005475

**English.** Definition: The quantum group is the algebraic framework of the solutions. Conjecture: The coproduct conversion of third roots of unity is an explicit correction of Gauss sums, verified for small-order roots, and the conversion preserves cococommutativity. (Gauss-correction conversion of third-root coproducts)
