# 00000004675

**English.** Definition: A Wahl singularity is a cyclic quotient singularity admitting Q-Gorenstein deformation. Conjecture: Its sufficient criterion for appearance in deformation contractions is a divisibility condition on the discriminant of the group chain, and the criterion is explicit and complete for small-genus contractions. (divisibility criterion of Wahl discriminants)

**中文。** 定义：Wahl 奇点指容许 Q-Gorenstein 形变的循环商奇点。猜想：其在形变收缩中出现的充分判据为群链判别式的整除条件,判据显式且在小亏格收缩完全。（Wahl 判别式整除判据）
