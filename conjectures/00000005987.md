**English.** Definition: The geometric genus is the dimension of canonical sections. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit combination of the hypersurface singularity spectrum, a degree combination for general type; the genus is a subtraction of Milnor numbers for smooth hypersurfaces, with coefficient 1; and the union of singularity spectra determines the genus, the deficit being the sum of local genera. (singularity-spectrum combination conversion of the geometric genus)

**中文。** 定义：几何属：典范截面的维数（几何亏格）。猜想：换算公式为超曲面奇异谱的组合显式且一般型时为次数组合；亏格在光滑超曲面时为 Milnor 数的减式且减式的系数为 1；奇异谱的并集决定亏格且决定的亏损为局部亏格和。（几何属奇异谱组合换算）
