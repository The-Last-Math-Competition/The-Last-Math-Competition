# 00000009957

**MRG-17 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Joinings of diagonal flows: classification of common invariant measures of two systems (Mozes's algebraicity theorem). Conjecture: Joining sparsity under entropy-rank conditions: all sufficiently high-entropy joinings of two diagonal flows are algebraic products; below the entropy threshold nonalgebraic joinings exist, constructed from Toeplitz-type counting families. (joining entropy threshold sparsity)

**中文。** 定义：对角流的 joining：两系统的共同不变测度分类（Mozes 的代数性定理）。猜想：熵秩条件下的 joining 稀疏性：两对角流的一切熵足够大的 joining 为代数乘积；熵阈值以下存在非代数 joining 且其构造为 Toeplitz 型计数族。（joining 熵阈值稀疏性）
