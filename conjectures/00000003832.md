# 00000003832

**RC-12 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: A cylindrical crystal denotes the finite structure of an affine crystal quotient by cylindrical translations, and cylindrical partitions the corresponding sets on the cylindrical lattice. Conjecture: The area generating function of cylindrical partitions of type A_n^{(1)} always has finite-product shape, with exponents quasipolynomials of period n+1. (cylindrical partition product formula)

**中文。** 定义：柱面晶体指仿射晶体商掉柱面平移所得的有限结构,柱面分拆指其在圆柱格点上的对应集合。猜想：型 A_n^{(1)} 柱面分拆的面积生成函数恒具有限乘积形状,其指数为周期 n+1 的拟多项式。（柱面分拆乘积公式）
