# 00000004334

**English.** Definition: The loop space measure is the measure on the space of Brownian loops, and the heat kernel is the kernel of the diffusion on it. Conjecture: The short-time leading constant of the loop heat kernel is an explicit value of the three-dimensional Bessel potential, and the next constant is the one-loop curvature correction. (two short-time constants of the loop heat kernel)

**中文。** 定义：环路空间测度指布朗环路的空间测度,热核指其上扩散过程的核。猜想：环路热核的短时首常数为三维 Bessel 位势的显式值,次常数为曲率的单圈修正。（环路热核短时双常数）
