# 00000004883

**MT2-13 ★** — source: 148_数学的元问题.md

**English.** Definition: Goodstein-type statements are termination-type combinatorial statements descending along ordinals, stratified by proof-theoretic strength. Conjecture: The strength is stratified explicitly by the tree height of the generating tree, and within height three each layer is exactly a layer of the epsilon_0 ordinal tower; the interlayer strength differences are exponential with a model-theoretic characterization of each layer; the layer of height four is independent of first-order arithmetic and the independence reverses in second-order arithmetic. (Goodstein tree-height level tower)

**中文。** 定义：Goodstein 型陈述：依序数下降的终止型组合陈述的证明论强度分层。猜想：强度按生成树的树高显式分层且树高三以内各层恰为 ε₀ 序数塔的逐层；层间强度差为指数级且每层有模型论刻画；树高四的层独立于一阶算术且独立性在二阶算术中反转。（Goodstein 树高层级塔）
