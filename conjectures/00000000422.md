**English.** Definition: maj is the descent-position-weighted sum statistic. Conjecture: On a fixed shape, the joint distribution of maj and des is asymptotically bivariate normal with explicit covariance; and the covariance is given by the hook-sum formula of the shape. (joint normality of maj and des)
