**English.** Definition: Complex bad approximation Bad(C) is the set of complex numbers admitting no good approximation with Gaussian-integer denominators. Conjecture: The intersection of Bad(C) with any real line is empty; and the complement of Bad(C) has full measure on every line, with convergence rates controlled by the lattice density of the Gaussian integers. (complex bad approximation empty line intersection)

**中文。** 定义：复坏逼近 Bad(ℂ) 为高斯整数分母下无良好逼近的复数集合。猜想：Bad(ℂ) 与任意实直线的交集为空；且 Bad(ℂ) 的补在每条直线上为全测度且收敛速度由 Gauss 整数的格密度控制。（复坏逼近直线交集空）
