# 00000002696

**English.** Definition: Modular distribution of periodic points: equidistribution of periodic points of endomorphisms (dynatomic measures). Conjecture: Weak convergence of dynatomic measures: periodic-point equidistribution measures converge to the Green measure with L∞ rate the inverse first power of the degree; the exceptional maps are Lattès type, with second-order convergence there. (dynatomic Green convergence law)

**中文。** 定义：周期点的模分布：自同态周期点的等分布（Dynatomic 测度）。猜想：dynatomic 测度的弱收敛：周期点等分布测度收敛到 Green 测度且收敛的 L∞ 速率为度数的负一次幂；例外映射为 Lattès 型且例外时收敛为二阶幂。（dynatomic Green 收敛律）
