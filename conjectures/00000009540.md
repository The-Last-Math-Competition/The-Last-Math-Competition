# 00000009540

**QIC-20 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: Energy-constrained capacity requires input states with average photon number tr(rho N) at most E; the constrained capacity of the single-mode pure-loss channel phi_kappa is g(kappa E), where g(x) = (x+1) log(x+1) - x log x. Conjecture: The entanglement-assisted capacity of multimode general loss networks is the sum form g(sum kappa_i E_i), invariant under arbitrary interferometer unitary couplings; and the gap between the upper and lower capacity bounds of thermal-noise channels does not exceed log(1 + N_th), a uniform thermal gap upper bound. (bosonic energy capacity network law)

**中文。** 定义：能量约束容量要求输入态满足平均光子数 tr(ρN)≤E；单模纯衰减信道 φ_κ 的约束容量为 g(κE)，g(x)=(x+1)log(x+1)−xlog x。猜想：多模一般衰减网络的纠缠辅助容量为 g(Σκ_i E_i) 的和形式，且该和形式在任意干涉仪酉耦合下不变；热噪声信道容量上下界之差不超过 log(1+N_th)，热隙上界一致成立。（玻色能量容量网络律）
