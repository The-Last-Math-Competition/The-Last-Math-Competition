# 00000004165

**English.** Definition: The shifted energy of the squares is the additive energy of the set of squares and its shift. Conjecture: The main-term coefficient of the asymptotic of the shifted energy is the explicit constant 4 pi^{-2}, and the remainder decays like the square root of the shift parameter and cannot be improved. (main constant of the shifted energy of squares)

**中文。** 定义：平方数的移位能量指集合与其平移的加性能量。猜想：移位能量的渐近主项系数为 4π^{−2} 型显式常数,余项按移位参数平方根衰减且不可改进。（平方移位能量主常数）
