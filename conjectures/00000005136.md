**English.** Definition: The backward error is the minimal magnitude of data perturbation. Conjecture: The optimal rank-one backward error is an explicit norm ratio of the residual, equality for tridiagonal problems, and the formula is optimal. (norm-ratio formula of rank-one backward errors)
