# 00000006631

**English.** Definition: The KMS equations are the self-consistent structure of equilibrium measures. Conjecture: The extremal decomposition of solutions is unique, the combination of the decomposition being the Choquet theorem. (unique Choquet extremal decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：规范方程为平衡测度自洽结构。猜想：解的极值分解唯一；且分解的组合为 Choquet 定理。（极值分解 Choquet 唯一）
