# 00000008836

**English.** Definition: Bregman splitting: relative error methods. Conjecture: Splitting under Bregman distances always converges; and the convergence criterion is summability of the decreasing distance sequence. (Bregman distance summable criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Bregman 分裂为相对误差方法。猜想：Bregman 距离下的分裂恒收敛；收敛判据为距离递减序列的可和性。（Bregman 距离可和判据）
