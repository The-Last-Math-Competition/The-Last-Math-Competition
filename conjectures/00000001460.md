# 00000001460

**CX-10 ★** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The entropy power inequality (EPI). Conjecture: The reverse EPI holds if and only if the ratio of the Fisher informations of the two distributions is bounded (a reverse EPI criterion).

**中文。** 定义：熵幂不等式(EPI)。猜想：反向 EPI 成立的充要条件为两分布的 Fisher 信息比的界(反向 EPI 判据)。
