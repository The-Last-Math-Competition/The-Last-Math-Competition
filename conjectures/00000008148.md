**English.** Definition: The spectral preservation of the band-truncation evolution of Glazek-Wilson similarity renormalization (SRG): the spectral preservation exponent sp_pres of the band decay of H_diag. Conjecture: The optimal band decay rate of sp_pres is e^{-s^2/2} (a Gaussian bandwidth law); the completeness of spectral preservation (conservation of invariants) in the limit of band decay is guaranteed by the vanishing of the three-body efflux of the coupling (a three-body vanishing completeness); and the strength spectrum of the induced three-body forces (induced forces of the noncommutative SRG flow) has minimum double-exponentially small of order e^{-L} (a double-exponential smallness of induction). (SRG Gaussian band three-body induction)
