# 00000005343

**LH2-23 ★** — source: 163_范畴化不变量_链接同调.md

**English.** Definition: The bicategorical lift is the two-fold categorification structure of link homology. Conjecture: The diagram basis is always an explicit basis of two-morphism commutators, closed on commutator cones; the dimensions of the basis are linear in the partition numbers; and the compatibility constant of the closure is 1, equivariant under braiding. (commutator closure of bicategorical diagram bases)

**中文。** 定义：双范畴提升：链环同调的双范畴化结构。猜想：图形基恒为二形态交换的显式基且换位锥上闭合；基的维数为分拆数的线性；闭合的相容常数为 1 且在辫作用下等变。（双范畴图形基换位闭合）
