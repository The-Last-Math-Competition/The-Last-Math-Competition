# 00000007211

**English.** Definition: Arithmetic equidistribution with the orbit statistics of subgroups. Conjecture: The rate of weak convergence of orbit measures holds, and the correction of the rate is the harmonics of the class number. (class-number-harmonic closed-geodesic equidistribution rate)

**中文。** 定义：算术遍历：子群的轨道统计。猜想：闭测地线的等分布：轨道测度的弱收敛的率且率的修正为类数的调和。（闭测地线类数调和等分布率修正轨道弱收敛）
