# 00000001489

**HY-09 ◆** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Birkhoff's conjecture asserts that integrable billiard tables on the plane (all billiard orbits bounded) are ellipses. Conjecture: An explicit differential inequality exists as a second-variational obstruction to integrability: the second variation of the boundary function must satisfy the linearized mirror equation pointwise, whose unique solution family consists of ellipses; this reduces Birkhoff's conjecture to pointwise rigidity verification.

**中文。** 定义：Birkhoff 猜想断言平面上可积(一切弹子轨道均界)的弹子桌皆为椭圆。猜想：可积性的二阶变分障碍存在显式微分不等式:桌形函数的二阶变分必须逐点满足线性化镜像方程,其唯一解族为椭圆;该不等式把 Birkhoff 猜想化为逐点刚性验证。
