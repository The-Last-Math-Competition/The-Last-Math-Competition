# 00000000677

**KT-77 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The thickness ratio of 3-bridge knots is the ratio of ropelength to the shortest bridge-projection length. Conjecture: Every 3-bridge knot has ropelength at least 16.372 + c with explicit correction c = 0.02, the lower bound being attained by a 3-bridge representative construction of the trefoil; no 3-bridge knot has a strictly smaller thickness ratio.

**中文。** 定义：3-桥纽结的厚比指 ropelength 与桥投影最短长度的比值。猜想：一切 3-桥纽结的 ropelength ≥ 16.372 + c,c = 0.02 为显式修正,且下界由三叶结的 3-桥代表构造取得;不存在厚比严格小于该值的 3-桥纽结。
