# 00000001770

**English.** Definition: The cell modules of the q-Schur algebra are the quotient modules of its cell structure. Conjecture: A complete classification of the semisimplicity thresholds at q = ζ_m (m ≤ 5): cell modules are semisimple if and only if m does not divide the hook lengths; the classification table for m = 3, 4, 5 is exhausted by standard-table hybridization.

**中文。** 定义：q-Schur 代数的 cell 模指其按 cell 结构的商模。猜想：q=ζ_m(m≤5) 的半单阈值的完全分类:cell 模半单当且仅当 m∤hook 长度;分类表对 m=3,4,5 由标准表杂化穷举。
