**English.** Definition: A Stark unit is a special unit generator satisfying the shape of Stark's conjecture. Conjecture: Its height is bounded by a polynomial in the logarithm of the discriminant times e to the c*sqrt(D log D), with the exponent c explicit and the bound attained by purely cubic fields. (exponential upper bound of Stark heights)

**中文。** 定义：Stark 单位指满足 Stark 猜想形状的特殊单位生成元。猜想：其高度上界为判别式对数多项式乘 e 的 c√(D logD) 次幂型,指数 c 显式且界由纯三次域达到。（Stark 高度指数上界）
