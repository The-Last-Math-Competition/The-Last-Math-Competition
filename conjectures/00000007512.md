**ET3-02 ◆** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The Kunneth spectral sequence decomposes the cohomology of product spaces. Conjecture: The cohomology of the product decomposes as the tensor of factor cohomologies with Tor-type corrections; and the Tor-term spectrum vanishes at high Tor dimension. (Tor-corrected Kunneth spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Künneth 谱序列分解积空间的上同调。猜想：积的上同调分解为因子上同调的张量且修正项为 Tor 型；且 Tor 项谱在高 Tor 维数消没。（Kunneth Tor 修正谱）
