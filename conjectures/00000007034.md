**English.** Definition: The crossing number lemma with graph crossings. Conjecture: The lower bound of crossing numbers holds, the combination of the bound being edge density. (edge-density crossing-number lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：交叉数引理：图的交叉。猜想：交叉的下界：交叉数的下界且界的组合为边密度。（交叉数引理边密度交叉下界交叉组合和积图的交叉）
