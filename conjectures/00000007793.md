# 00000007793

**ACG-13 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The variance of R = |X-Y|, the distance between two independent isotropic uniform ball points. Conjecture: Var(R)/Var(|X|) = 2 - 2/sqrt 3 + O(n^{-1}) with first correction coefficient -1/(3 sqrt 3); the ratio strictly improves the thin-shell constant in the two-body case, with equality only for X, Y iid uniform on the ball, any correlated coupling increasing the ratio. (two-point distance variance ratio)

**中文。** 定义：两独立迷向均匀球点距离 R=|X-Y| 的方差。猜想：Var(R)/Var(|X|) = 2-2/√3+O(n^{-1})，且修正项的首阶系数为 -1/(3√3)；该比值刻画薄壳常数在二体情形的严格改进，等号仅当 X,Y 独立同分布于球体，任何相关的耦合使比值增大。
