**English.** Definition: Uniform mass density of Jacobi matrices (a UMD-type spectral condition). Conjecture: The difference between the number of zeros of the Jost solution and the number of negative eigenvalues of the operator is given explicitly by the winding number of the potential (a discrete sharpening of Levinson's theorem).
