# 00000006754

**English.** Definition: The term-count bound of composite objects. Conjecture: The composite term count is bounded and the bound is exact; the exact counting is slingshot type; and the slingshot-type counting is given by the arm-decomposition combinatorics. (exact slingshot bound of composite term counts)

**中文。** 定义：复合对象的项数界。猜想：复合的项数有界且界精确；且精确的计数为弹弓型；且弹弓型的计数由臂分解的组合给出。（复合项数弹弓精确界）
