**English.** Definition: The growth of the n-th coefficients of the colored Jones polynomial J_{K,n}(q) of a knot. Conjecture: limsup |J_{K,n}(q at q=1 expansion coefficients)|^{1/n²} is an algebraic number (one algebraic constant per knot K).

**中文。** 定义：纽结的 colored Jones J_{K,n}(q) 的第 n 系数的增长。猜想：limsup |J_{K,n}(q=1 展开系数)|^{1/n²} 为代数数(每个 K 对应一个代数常数)。
