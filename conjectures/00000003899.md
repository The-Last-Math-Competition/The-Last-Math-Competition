# 00000003899

**CA3-19 ★** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: The Nygaard completion is the multiplicative completion of the prismatic-cohomology filtration, with grading N^iH^j. Conjecture: For smooth proper prismaticizations, N^iH^j = 0 holds for j < i − 1, and the bound i − 1 is optimal. (Nygaard vanishing optimal bound)

**中文。** 定义：Nygaard 完备化指棱镜上同调滤子的乘法完备化,分次记 N^iH^j。猜想：对光滑真棱镜化,N^iH^j = 0 对 j < i − 1 成立,且界 i − 1 不可改进。（Nygaard 消没最优界）
