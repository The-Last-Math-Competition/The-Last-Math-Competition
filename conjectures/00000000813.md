# 00000000813

**English.** Definition: Courant-sharp eigenvalues (the k-th eigenfunction has exactly k nodal domains). Conjecture: On the isosceles right triangle, the Courant-sharp eigenvalues are finite in number with a complete list (partially known; the complete list is open).

**中文。** 定义：Courant sharp 特征值(第 k 特征函数恰有 k 个节域)。猜想：等腰直角三角形上 Courant sharp 的特征值个数有限且完全列表(部分已知;完整列表开放)。
