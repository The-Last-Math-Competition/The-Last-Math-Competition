# 00000004509

**English.** Definition: A cartesian closed structure is the adjunction structure of the internal Hom. Conjecture: The number of its naturality equivalence classes equals the number of connected components of the geometric morphism, the count is explicit and closed on locally connected toposes. (component count of cartesian closed natural classes)
