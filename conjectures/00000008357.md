# 00000008357

**English.** Definition: The maximum weight matching of tropical determinants: the weight trop_det of the max-plus determinant permanent. Conjecture: The uniqueness of trop_det (uniqueness of maximum weight perfect matchings) holds generically with full measure (a full-measure uniqueness law); the structure of the uniqueness-breaking locus (multisolution domain) is the image of a hypersimplex (a hypersimplex image law); the count of breaking domains (cells) equals the Ehrhart polynomial of the second hypersimplex (an Ehrhart cell law); and tie-breaking of multiple solutions (lexicographic) has lexicographic constant a power 2^n (a power lexicographic law). (tropical determinant hypersimplex Ehrhart lexicographic)

**中文。** 定义：热带行列式的最大权重匹配：(trop,+) 行列式的最大积和式的权重 trop_det。猜想：trop_det（最大权重完美匹配的唯一性）的普适（一般位置的唯一）的测度为 1（满测唯一律）；且唯一性的破缺（多解域）的胞腔的结构为次多面体（hypersimplex 的像）（次像律）；破缺域的计数（胞腔数）= 第二hypersimplex 的 Ehrhart 多项式（Ehrhart 胞腔律）；多解的仲裁（字典序打破）的字典常数为 2^{n} 的幂（幂字典律）。
