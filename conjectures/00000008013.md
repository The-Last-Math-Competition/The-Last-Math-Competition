# 00000008013

**DEL-23 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The geometric characterization of domains for the boundary Harnack inequality: the complete geometric criterion for the domain class BH_n for equations in non-divergence form. Conjecture: BH_n domains are exactly the domains satisfying the inner parallel condition (uniform plus inner parallel); the minimality of the criterion (no further weakening) has counterexample domains that are gapped snowflakes (snowflakes missing a fixed proportion) at the critical exponent s* = (n-1)/n; and the divergence rate of the boundary Harnack constant on that domain family is (s*-s)^{-1} (a divergence law with critical exponent -1). (inner-parallel boundary Harnack criterion)

**中文。** 定义：边界 Harnack 不等式的域的几何刻画：非发散形式方程的边界 Harnack 的域类 BH_n 的完全几何判据。猜想：BH_n 域 = 满足内平行条件（uniform + inner parallel）的域；且判据的极小性（不可再减弱）的反例域为缺口 snowflake（缺一个比例的 snowflake）在指数临界 s*=(n-1)/n 的边界；边界 Harnack 常数在该域族的发散率为 (s*-s)^{-1}（临界指数 -1 的发散律）。
