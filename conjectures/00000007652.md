# 00000007652

**English.** Definition: The Lax map of hyperbolic conservation laws as semigroup structure. Conjecture: The contraction of the Lax map is preserved in L^1 distance; and the contraction constant is the bound of the interaction functional. (L1-contraction Lax semigroup)

**中文。** 定义：双曲守恒律的 Lax 映射为半群结构。猜想：Lax 映射的压缩性在 L¹ 距离下保持；且压缩的常数为交互泛函的界。（Lax 半群 L1 压缩）
