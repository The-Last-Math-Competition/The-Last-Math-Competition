# 00000000166

**English.** Conjecture: Prime saturation of the combinatorial nullstellensatz: if f ∈ F_p[x_1,…,x_n] vanishes on the entire grid [0, p−1]^n and has total degree < (p−1)n, then the annihilating ideal of f in the monomial basis of degrees < (p−1)n contains an explicit polynomial of degree at most p^{⌈log n⌉}; the saturation exponent ⌈log n⌉ is optimal.

**中文。** 猜想：组合零点定理的素数饱和度:若 f∈F_p[x_1,…,x_n] 在整个网格 [0,p−1]^n 上为零且总度 < (p−1)n,则 f 在次数 < (p−1)n 的单项式基下的零化理想包含一个次数 ≤ p^{⌈log n⌉} 的显式多项式;该饱和度指数 ⌈log n⌉ 为最优。
