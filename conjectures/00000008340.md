# 00000008340

**English.** Definition: The Davenport-Schmidt spectrum of uniform approximation: the maximum ds_max of the spectrum of uniform approximation exponents. Conjecture: ds_max (the supremum of uniform approximation exponents) is the golden reciprocal (3 - sqrt 5)/2 (a golden reciprocal law); the numbers attaining the supremum (worst uniform approximation) form a measure-zero set (a zero-measure worst law); the continued fraction pattern of the worst-number construction (Davenport-Schmidt) is of [1,1,1,3,1,1,3,...] type (a Bugeaud pattern law); and the family of pattern variants (inserting k) completes the spectrum with insertion values from the finite list {1,2,3} (a three-value insertion law, a spectrum completion law). (Davenport-Schmidt golden Bugeaud three insertion)

**中文。** 定义：一致逼近的 Davenport–Schmidt 的谱：一致逼近指数的谱的极大值 ds_max。猜想：ds_max（一致逼近指数的上确界）= (3-√5)/2 的黄金倒数（黄金倒数律）；且达上确界的数（最坏一致逼近）的测度为零（零测最坏律）；最坏数的构造（Davenport–Schmidt 的构造）的连分数的规律为 [1,1,1,3,1,1,3,…] 型（Bugeaud 的模式律）；模式（三的插入周期）的变体（插入 k 的族）的谱的完备（谱完备律）的插入值的有限列表 {1,2,3}（三值插入律）。
