# 00000009980

**English.** Definition: Hochschild cohomology rings HH^*: the invariant ring structure of algebras. Conjecture: The Hochschild cohomology ring of a finite-dimensional algebra is finitely generated if and only if the center of the universal deformation bracket structure is finitely generated; the criterion is verified explicitly by termination of the first-order Massey obstruction chain. (HH finite generation criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Hochschild 上同调环 HH^*：代数的不变环结构。猜想：有限维代数的 Hochschild 上同调环有限生成当且仅当泛形变括号结构的中心为有限生成；判据由一次 Massey 障碍链的终止显式验证。（HH 有限生成判据）
