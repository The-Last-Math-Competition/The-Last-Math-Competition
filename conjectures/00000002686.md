# 00000002686

**English.** Definition: Minimal automorphisms: the dynamics of minimal maps of projective space. Conjecture: Uniqueness of the invariant measure of a minimal endomorphism: the invariant measure is a Lebesgue-type affine measure whose entropy equals the logarithm of the topological degree; uniqueness is strict when the dimension is at least two. (minimal automorphism measure uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：极小自同构：射影空间上极小映射的动力学。猜想：极小映射的不变测度的唯一性：极小自同态的不变测度为 Lebesgue 型仿射测度且测度的熵等于映射的拓扑度对数；唯一性在映射维数至少二时严格。（极小自同构测度唯一）
