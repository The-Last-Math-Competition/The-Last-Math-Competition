# 00000007638

**English.** Definition: The Riemann problem as the Cauchy problem of piecewise-constant initial values. Conjecture: The combination of Riemann solutions is the splicing of shocks, rarefactions, and tangential discontinuities; and the type count of the splicing is the power of the characteristic families. (Riemann splicing classification)

**中文。** 定义：Riemann 问题为分片常初值的柯西问题。猜想：Riemann 解的组合为激波、稀疏与切向间断的拼接；且拼接的种类数为特征族的幂。（Riemann 拼接组合分类）
