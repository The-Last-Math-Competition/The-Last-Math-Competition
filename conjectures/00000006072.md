# 00000006072

**English.** Definition: Spurious local minima are nonglobal minima. Conjecture: For matrix sensing the spurious minima form an explicit lattice of rank jumps, vanishing after overparametrization, the kernel of the vanishing being the rank surplus. (rank-jump spurious minima vanishing under overparametrization)

**中文。** 定义：伪局部极小为非全局的极小点。猜想：矩阵感知的伪极小为秩跳变的显式格且过参数化后消失；且消失的核为秩盈余。（伪极小秩跳格过参消失）
