# 00000001080

**English.** Definition: Entanglement-assisted quantum codes use pre-shared entanglement to relax quantum code parameters. Conjecture: Parameters and entanglement dimension e satisfy: [[n,k,d;e]] exists if and only if a classical [n,k+e,d] exists — a linear exchange of entanglement for dimension; and the optimal e is 2·(k_max − k).

**中文。** 定义：纠缠辅助纠错码用预共享纠缠对降低量子码的参数需求。猜想：辅助纠缠维数 e 与码参数满足 [[n,k,d;e]] 存在当且仅当经典 [n,k+e,d] 存在,即纠缠换算为维数的线性公式;e 的最优值为 2·(k_max−k)。
