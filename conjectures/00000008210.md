# 00000008210

**English.** Definition: The automorphic realization of tempered representations: the bound temp_level(pi) on the minimal automorphic level of a tempered representation. Conjecture: temp_level (the minimal level of a tempered representation) is at most a polynomial in the infinitesimal character of pi (a polynomial level bound); the verifiability of the temperedness criterion (boundedness of local L-functions) by finite checking has checking radius the logarithm of the conductor (a logarithmic checking law); and the explicit upper bound for the level of the minimal non-tempered automorphic representation (the first Jacquet-Whittaker exception) is 2^{16} (a first-exception bound). (tempered polynomial level Jacquet-Whittaker exception)

**中文。** 定义：Tempered 表示的扩张的自守实现： tempered 表示的极小自守水平的界 temp_level(π)。猜想：temp_level（tempered 表示的最小水平）≤ C(π 的无穷小特征标) 的多项式（多项式水平界）；且 tempered 的判据（局部 L 函数的有界性）的可验证性（有限检查）的检查半径为 conductor 的对数（对数检查律）；非 tempered（例外）的最小自守表示（Jacquet–Whittaker 的第一例外）的水平的显式上界为 2^{16}（第一例外界）。
