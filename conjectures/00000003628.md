**English.** Definition: Cyclicity: the limit-cycle counting of polynomial systems. Conjecture: Cyclicity at infinity: the upper bound of cycle counts at points at infinity is the focal-quantity count of the isolated point. (infinity focal quantity cycle count upper bound cyclicity)

**中文。** 定义：Cyclicity：多项式系统的极限环计数。猜想：无穷远的 cyclicity：无穷远点的环数的上界为孤立点的焦点量数。（无穷远焦点量环数上界 cyclicity）
