# 00000009887

**GBR-07 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Stable quotients of AR quivers: finiteness of Riedtmann translation quotients. Conjecture: The symmetry group of the stable quotient of the AR quiver of tubular type algebras is an explicit quotient of the affine Weyl group; the order of the quotient is the product of tube parameters, and the unimodal maps of each tube form a minimal generating set of the quotient. (tubular stable quotient correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：AR 箭图的稳定商：Riedtmann 平移商的有限性。猜想：管状型代数的 AR 箭图的稳定商的对称群为仿射 Weyl 群的显式商群；商群的阶为管参数的乘积且每管的单态射图构成商的极小生成集。（管型稳定商对应）
