# 00000005290

**FL2-30 △** — source: 161_非线性偏微分II_流体II.md

**English.** Definition: Energy, cross helicity, and magnetic helicity are the conserved triple of magnetohydrodynamics. Conjecture: There exist two solutions with the same energy and cross helicity but different magnetic helicity, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of initial data with helicity injection. (same energy and cross helicity-different magnetic helicity separation)

**中文。** 定义：能量、交叉螺旋与磁螺旋指磁流体的守恒三元组。猜想：存在两解的能量与交叉螺旋相同而磁螺旋不同,分离由螺旋注入的显式初值对实现。（能量交叉螺旋同-磁螺旋异分离）
