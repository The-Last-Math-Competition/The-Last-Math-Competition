# 00000008320

**FLO-30 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The symplectic counterpart of Hochschild cohomology of the Fukaya category: the isomorphism hh_quant between HH^*(Fuk) and quantum homology. Conjecture: hh_quant (the closed-open string isomorphism) holds in the monotone compact case (a monotone closed-open law); the degree conversion of the isomorphism (HH degree versus quantum degree) is 2:1 (a two-one conversion law); the exception to the conversion (misaligned degrees) is non-Calabi-Yau categories (a non-CY category exception law); and the correction of the category (the CY cover) has covering count 2 (a double cover law), the HH splitting of the covered category being a characteristic-2 splitting (a characteristic-two splitting law). (closed-open Hochschild two-one CY double cover)

**中文。** 定义：Fukaya 范畴的 Hochschild 上同调的辛对应：HH^*(Fuk) 与量子同调的同构 hh_quant。猜想：hh_quant（闭弦-开弦的同构）在单调紧成立（单调闭开律）；且同构的度的换算（HH 度与量子的度）为 2:1 的换算（二一换算律）；换算的例外（度不对齐）的范畴为非 Calabi–Yau 的畴（非 CY 畴例外律）；畴的修正（CY 覆盖）的覆盖次数为 2（双覆盖律），覆盖的范畴的 HH 的分裂为特征 2 的分裂（特征二分裂律）。
