**GC6-26 ★** — source: 215_计算复杂性的几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal shift count is the maximal depth of the subspace tower. Conjecture: The closed form of the optimal shift is given by the representation degree and is exact for the regular representation; and the exception of exactness is the torsion representation. (degree closed form of optimal shifts)

**中文。** 定义：最优移位次数为子空间塔的极大深度。猜想：最优移位的闭式由表示度数给出且闭式在正则表示精确；且精确性的例外为挠表示。（最优移位度数闭式）
