**English.** Definition: optimal place -fold number is maximal depth of subspace. Conjecture: closed-form of optimal place by representation degree gives and closed-form in regular representation exact; and exception of exact is torsion representation. (optimal place degree closed-form)

**中文。** 定义：最优移位次数为子空间塔的极大深度。猜想：最优移位的闭式由表示度数给出且闭式在正则表示精确；且精确性的例外为挠表示。（最优移位度数闭式）
