# 00000007936

**English.** Definition: The scattering data and inverse problem of the Toda lattice: the scattering map S from the eigenvalues lambda_i of the Lax matrix of the finite nonperiodic Toda together with the decay rates of the normalization factors. Conjecture: S is an algebraic map (an isomorphism from the initial data space of the symmetric power map to the eigenvalue space); in the periodic Toda (affine A_n) the image of S is the spectral hypersurface of the Calogero-Moser space; and the absolute value of the Jacobian of the scattering map is the ratio of the absolute value of the eigenvalue discriminant to the normalization factor, a ratio identically equal to 1 in the group-like coordinates of the bi-Hamiltonian structure. (Toda scattering algebraic Jacobian)

**中文。** 定义：Toda 格的散射数据与逆问题：有限非周期 Toda 的 Lax 矩阵的特征值 λ_i 与规范因子的衰减率构成散射映射 S。猜想：S 为代数映射（对称幂映射的初始数据空间到特征值空间的同构）；且在周期 Toda（仿射 A_n）时 S 的像为 Calogero–Moser 空间的谱超曲面；散射映射的 Jacobian 的绝对值为特征值判别式的绝对值与规范化因子的比，比值在双哈密顿结构的 group-like 坐标下恒为 1。
