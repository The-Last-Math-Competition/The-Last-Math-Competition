**English.** Definition: A local coefficient system is a rank-one system with nontrivial monodromy. Conjecture: The vanishing region of the local-coefficient homology of Conf_n(X) is the complement of a union of hyperplanes in the parameter lattice, and the vanishing holds simultaneously for all n >= 2. (structure of the local-coefficient vanishing region)

**中文。** 定义：局部系数系统指带非平凡 monodromy 的秩 1 系。猜想：Conf_n(X) 的局部系数同调的消没域为参数格点中超平面并的补,消没对一切 n ≥ 2 同时成立。（局部系数消没域结构）
