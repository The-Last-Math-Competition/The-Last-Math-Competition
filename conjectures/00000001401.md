# 00000001401

**HT-11 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: The high-dimensional knot cobordism group C_n consists of cobordism classes of knots. Conjecture: The infinite-order elements of C_{4k+1} are given explicitly by signature-type families at odd primes; and each odd prime contributes exactly one independent generator. (odd-dimensional cobordism odd-prime signature family)

**中文。** 定义：高维纽结配边群 C_n 指纽结的配边等价类。猜想：C_{4k+1} 的无限阶元素由奇素数的签名型族显式给出；且每个奇素数贡献恰好一个独立生成元。（奇维配边奇素签名族）
