# 00000001585

**IS-15 ★** — source: 38_可积系统与精确可解模型.md

**English.** Definition: The Taylor coefficients c_n at t = 0 of the Painlevé tau function. Conjecture: {c_n} satisfies an explicit tridiagonal Hankel-type recursion c_n = Σ_k a_k c_{n−k}·H(n,k), where H(n,k) is a bandwidth-3 Hankel kernel; the recursion is independent of the two-dimensional Toda structure.

**中文。** 定义：Painlevé τ 函数在 t=0 的 Taylor 系数 c_n。猜想：{c_n} 满足显式三对角 Hankel 型递推:c_n = Σ_{k} a_k c_{n-k}·H(n,k),其中 H(n,k) 为带宽 3 的 Hankel 核;该递推独立于二维 Toda 结构。
