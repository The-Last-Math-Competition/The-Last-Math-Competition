**English.** Definition: Periodic counting and topological entropy are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two maps with identical periodic-orbit counts but different topological entropy, and the separation is realized by an explicit shift pair with the same periodic spectrum but different growth rate. (same periodic spectrum-different entropy shift separation)
