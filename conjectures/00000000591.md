# 00000000591

**English.** Definition: Wilf's conjecture. Conjecture: e·n(S) ≥ g(S) + e (n(S) the number of nongaps, g the Frobenius number). New-direction conjecture: on embedding-dimension-3 semigroups the ratio of the surplus e·n(S) − g(S) − e to g tends to 0, with an explicit convergence rate.

**中文。** 定义：Wilf。猜想：e·n(S) ≥ g(S) + e(n(S) 为非隙数,g 为 Frobenius 数)。新方向猜想:嵌入维 3 半群上盈余 e·n(S) − g(S) − e 与 g 的比值趋于 0,且收敛速率显式。
