# 00000001842

**English.** Definition: Correlated Wishart matrices are sample covariance matrices with population covariance Σ ≠ I. Conjecture: When Σ is not the identity, the renormalized limit of the smallest eigenvalue is an explicit convex combination of the TW-β law and white noise: the weight is controlled by δ, the ratio of the two smallest eigenvalues of Σ, degenerating to pure TW as δ → 1.

**中文。** 定义：相关 Wishart 指总体协方差 Σ≠I 的样本协方差矩阵。猜想：Σ 非单位时最小特征值的重整化极限为 TW-β 律与白噪声的显式凸组合:权重由 Σ 的最小两特征值之比 δ 控制,极限在 δ→1 时退化为纯 TW。
