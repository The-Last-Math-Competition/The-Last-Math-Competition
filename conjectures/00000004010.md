# 00000004010

**English.** Definition: The joint signature is the signature of each order of the joint path of two processes. Conjecture: There exist two processes whose joint signature distributions of all orders coincide while the process distributions differ, and the smallest realization is the infinite-level case for two-dimensional processes. (joint signature-distribution separation)

**中文。** 定义：联合签名指两过程联合路径的各阶签名。猜想：存在两过程其一切阶联合签名分布相同而过程分布不同,最小实现为二维过程的无穷层情形。（联合签名-分布分离）
