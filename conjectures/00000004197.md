# 00000004197

**English.** Definition: The AC linearized spectrum is the spectrum of the operator epsilon^2 Delta + 1 - 3u^2. Conjecture: The first negative eigenvalue and the minimum of the mean curvature of the interface satisfy the asymptotic equality lambda_1 proportional to -epsilon||H||, with an explicit universal proportionality constant. (AC first negative eigenvalue-curvature proportionality)

**中文。** 定义：AC 线性化谱指算子 ε²Δ + 1 − 3u² 的特征值。猜想：第一负特征值与界面平均曲率最小值满足 λ₁ 与 −ε‖H‖ 成比例的渐近等式,比例常数显式且普适。（AC 首负值-曲率比例）
