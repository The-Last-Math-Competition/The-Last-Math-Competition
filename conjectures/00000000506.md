# 00000000506

**CA-06 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The second row β_{1,j} of the Betti table records the graded distribution of the first syzygy module. Conjecture: For a monomial ideal, the support of β_{1,j} is a connected set without isolated points; and the connected structure of the support is completely determined by the merging mechanism of the Taylor resolution. (connectivity of first Betti support)

**中文。** 定义：Betti 表的第二行 β_{1,j} 记录一次 syzygy 模的分次维数分布。猜想：单项式理想的 β_{1,j} 的支撑集为无孤立点的连通集；支撑的连通结构由 Taylor 分解的合并机制完全决定。（一次 Betti 支撑连通）
