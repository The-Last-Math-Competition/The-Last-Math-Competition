# 00000000793

**English.** Definition: The p-adic digit sum s_p(n) is the digit-summing function. Conjecture: The limiting distribution of the digit sums of primes modulo p−1 is always uniform; the deviation from uniformity is controlled by carry correlations; and the deviation term is explicitly controlled by the correlation sum of the carry chain. (prime digit sum uniformity)

**中文。** 定义：p 进数字和 s_p(n) 为数码求和函数。猜想：素数的数字和模 p−1 的极限分布恒为均匀分布；且均匀的偏差由进位相关控制。；且偏差项由进位链的相关和显式控制。（素数数字和均匀）
