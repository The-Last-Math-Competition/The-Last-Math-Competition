# 00000008776

**NLA-06 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Randomized Kaczmarz iteration: sequentially projecting onto hyperplanes of equations sampled with probability proportional to squared row norms, for consistent overdetermined systems Ax = b. Conjecture: The expected error decay factor is exactly 1 - kappa(A)^{-2}, where kappa is the weighted row condition number; the speedup of block size b does not exceed sqrt b with optimal block size sqrt m (m the row count); and stochastic gradient descent under the optimal sampling distribution proportional to diagonally preconditioned residuals always converges faster than uniform sampling, with improvement ratio equal to the harmonic mean ratio of row-norm variances. (Kaczmarz block optimal sampling harmonic ratio)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Kaczmarz 迭代：按行范数平方概率抽取方程后向该超平面投影，对相容超定系统 Ax=b 的逐次逼近。猜想：期望误差衰减因子恰为 1-κ(A)^{-2}，其中 κ 为行的加权条件数；块大小 b 的加速比不超过 √b 且最优块大小为 √m（m 为行数）；随机梯度下降在按对角预条件残差成比例的最优采样分布下的收敛率恒优于均匀采样，改进比等于行范数方差的调和平均比。
