**NLA-06 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: random Kaczmarz approximation of iteration: norm square probability equation back to the hyperplane projection, pair compatible system Ax=b. Conjecture: expectation error decay factor is exactly 1-κ(A)^{-2}, where κ is acceleration ratio of weighted condition number; block size b of row does not exceed √b and optimal block size is √m (m is row number); stochastic gradient under in pair angle preconditioner residual becomes rate of convergence of optimal distribution under of proportion always in all, ratio equals harmonic average ratio of row norm variance.

**中文。** 定义：随机 Kaczmarz 迭代：按行范数平方概率抽取方程后向该超平面投影，对相容超定系统 Ax=b 的逐次逼近。猜想：期望误差衰减因子恰为 1-κ(A)^{-2}，其中 κ 为行的加权条件数；块大小 b 的加速比不超过 √b 且最优块大小为 √m（m 为行数）；随机梯度下降在按对角预条件残差成比例的最优采样分布下的收敛率恒优于均匀采样，改进比等于行范数方差的调和平均比。
