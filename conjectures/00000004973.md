# 00000004973

**English.** Definition: Negative weights are negative coefficients in quadrature formulas. Conjecture: The minimal correction eliminating negative weights in dimension four is one-dimensional refinement of at most two dimensions, after which positivity is restored and the accuracy order does not drop. (sufficiency of two-dimensional refinement for negative-weight elimination)

**中文。** 定义：负权重指求积公式中的负值系数。猜想：四维的负权消除最小修正为至多两维的单维加密,消除后正性恢复且精度阶不降。（负权消除两维加密充分）
