**SP-01 ◆** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: The Gauss–Kuzmin–Wirsing operator is the continued-fraction transfer operator, with λ₂ its subleading eigenvalue. Conjecture: λ₂ is transcendental and algebraically independent over Q of the adjacent eigenvalue λ₃; the proof path for independence is the solution-space dimension of a differential equation. (Wirsing constant algebraic independence)

**中文。** 定义：Gauss–Kuzmin–Wirsing 算子指连分数转移算子，λ₂ 为次大特征值。猜想：λ₂ 恒为超越数且与相邻特征值 λ₃ 在 Q 上代数无关；且无关性的证明路径为微分方程的解空间维数。（Wirsing 常数代数无关）
