# 00000008437

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of hypergraph designs: the combinatorial spectrum of the hypergraph serving as blocks. Conjecture: The number of decompositions of the complete t-hypergraph into specified isomorphism classes is double-exponential for prime v; and the fixed-point design spectrum of the automorphism group of a decomposition is the prime factor spectrum. (double-exponential hypergraph decompositions)

**中文。** 定义：超图设计的谱指其作为区组的超图的组合谱。猜想：完全 t-超图分解为指定同构类的分解数在素数 v 下为双指数；分解的自同构群的不动点设计谱为素因子谱。（超图分解双指数）
