# 00000002819

**LD-19 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: Large deviations of subadditive processes: Hammersley-type functionals. Conjecture: For the independent case the large-deviation rate of subadditive functionals is always a linear function; the subadditive correction of the rate is the reciprocal of the correlation length. (subadditive rate correlation correction)

**中文。** 定义：子加性过程的大偏差指 Hammersley 类型泛函。猜想：独立情形的子加性泛函的大偏差速率恒为线性函数；速率的次可加修正由相关长度的倒数给出。（子加性速率相关修正）
