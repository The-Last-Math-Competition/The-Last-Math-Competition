# 00000006411

**English.** Definition: The reverse inequality is the reversed strengthening of the classical product inequality. Conjecture: The compensation of the regularity deficit is given by regular balls of affine parametrization, and the constant of compensation is a linear function of the dimension. (affine regular-ball compensation of reverse inequalities)

**中文。** 定义：逆向不等式为经典乘积不等式的反向强化。猜想：正则性亏格的补偿由仿射参数化的正则球给出；且补偿的常数为维数的线性函数。（逆向正则球仿射补偿）
