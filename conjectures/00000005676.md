**RW2-26 ★** — source: 174_随机游走II_混合与覆盖.md

**English.** Definition: A biased walk is a walk in an environment with slope. Conjecture: The drift conversion formula is the explicit sign function of the slope, with a phase transition at critical value one, and drift zero and full on the two sides. (critical sign-function transition of bias)

**中文。** 定义：偏斜游走指带斜率的环境行走。猜想：漂移换算公式为斜率的显式符号函数,临界一处相变且两侧漂移为零与满值。（偏斜临界相变符号函数）
