# 00000007813

**English.** Definition: The double ramification cycle DR_{g,A} on the moduli space of stable maps Mbar_{g,n}(P^r,d), for an n-tuple A with sum zero, is the cycle class where mu^*(relative divisor) = sum A_i x_i. Conjecture: The coefficients of the tautological expression of DR_{g,A} satisfy a tower recursion over tree decompositions, and the topological intersection product obeys the explicit formula DR_{g,A} times DR_{g,B} = (sum A_i B_i / 2) DR_{g,A union B}; the formula is the governing law of the poly-filtration structure on the limit. (double ramification intersection formula)

**中文。** 定义：稳定映射模空间 Mbar_{g,n}(P^r,d) 上的分歧轨迹（double ramification cycle）DR_{g,A}（A 为满足 ΣA=0 的 n 元组）定义为其上满足 μ^*(相对除子)=ΣA_i x_i 的循环类。猜想：DR_{g,A} 的重言类表达式的系数在树分解上满足塔形递推，且 DR_{g,A}·DR_{g,B}（拓扑交积）= (ΣA_i B_i/2)·DR_{g,A∪B} 的显式化；该公式为极限的 poly-filtration 结构的支配律。
