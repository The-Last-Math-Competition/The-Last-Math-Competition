# 00000002897

**English.** Definition: Torsion points of elliptic curves: the group structure of finite-field points. Conjecture: Uniformity of torsion-point distributions: the probability distribution of p-torsion subgroups of random elliptic curves is given by the kernel distribution of Hurwitz class fields, with explicit entropy formula for the limit. (p-torsion Hurwitz distribution)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆曲线的扭点：有限域点的群结构。猜想：扭点分布的均匀性：随机椭圆曲线的 p-扭群的概率分布由 Hurwitz 类域的核分布给出且分布的极限为显式熵公式。（p-扭群 Hurwitz 分布）
