# 00000007980

**English.** Definition: Spreads and subspace codes: the maximal cardinality of a spread (subspace tiling) of a fixed-dimension space. Conjecture: The critical radius of random subspace tilings (the maximal dimension ratio where tilings exist) is (1/2) log_q(q-1) + o(1); and the automorphism group of optimal tilings (normal spreads) is an extension of the Singer group, whose Galois duality is classified by the parabolic-type minimal outer nilpotent kernel (the order of the nilpotent kernel being the prime part of q). (spread Singer nilpotent classification)

**中文。** 定义：spreads 与子空间码：plucking（子空间铺砌）的最大基数（固定维数空间的铺砌数）。猜想：随机子空间铺砌的临界半径（铺砌存在的最大维数比）为 1/2·log_q(q-1)+o(1)；且最优铺砌（正规 spreads）的自同构群为 Singer 群的扩张，扩张的 Galois 对偶由抛物线型的极小外加幂零核分类（幂零核的阶为 q 的素数部分）。
