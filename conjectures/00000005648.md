# 00000005648

**CP2-28 ★** — source: 173_组合几何III_凸位置.md

**English.** Definition: The structural condition of convex position is the local condition of subdivision vertices, the extension of the empty-circle and empty-sphere criteria. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit formula of the covering multiplicity of witness points, with constant 1 for three points on one circle; the multiplicity upper bound is 3, attained by Delaunay triangulations; and the globalization of the local condition holds if and only if the point set is in convex position. (covering-multiplicity conversion constant of witness points)

**中文。** 定义：凸位置的结构条件：剖分顶点的局部条件（空圆/空球判据的推广）。猜想：换算常数为证点集覆盖重数的显式公式且一圆三点情形常数为 1；重数的上界为常数 3 且界由 Delaunay 三角剖分达到；局部条件的全局化当且仅当点集处于凸位置。（证点覆盖重数换算常数）
