**English.** Definition: The p-adic logarithm log_p is defined on Z_p^×. Conjecture: log_p 2 is transcendental and algebraically independent of the p-adic pi π_p; moreover, {log_p q : q ≤ p² prime} is linearly independent over Q, with the proof requiring only a univariate Lambert-type method beyond p-adic Schanuel.

**中文。** 定义：p 进对数 log_p 定义在 Z_p^× 上。猜想：log_p 2 为超越数且与 p 进圆周率 π_p 代数无关;进一步,{log_p q : q ≤ p^2 素数} 在 Q 上线性无关,其证明只需 p 进 Schanuel 之外的单变量 Lambert 型方法。
