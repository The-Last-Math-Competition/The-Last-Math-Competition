# 00000001683

**GT-23 △** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: Ulam reconstruction for 3-uniform hypergraphs means reassembling them from their vertex-deleted hypergraphs. Conjecture: 3-uniform hypergraphs are reconstructible for n ≥ 8 (no counterexamples); any counterexample must be a twin pair of Steiner triple systems whose construction requires the 3-design parameters (v,3,1) to satisfy v ≡ 1,3 mod 6.

**中文。** 定义：3-均匀超图的 Ulam 重构指由其点删超图重组。猜想：3-均匀超图在 n≥8 时可重构(无反例);任何反例必为 Steiner 三元系的孪生对,其构造要求 3-设计的参数 (v,3,1) 满足 v≡1,3 mod 6。
