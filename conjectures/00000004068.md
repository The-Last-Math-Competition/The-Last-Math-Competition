# 00000004068

**English.** Definition: A blue-sky catastrophe is a singularity where the period of an invariant set tends to infinity at a finite parameter value. Conjecture: Near the catastrophe the period satisfies T(mu) proportional to (mu_c - mu)^{-1/2}, and the exponent 1/2 is universal and determined by the first-order coefficient of the return map. (half-exponent law of blue-sky period growth)

**中文。** 定义：蓝天突变指不变集在有限参数处周期趋于无穷的突变。猜想：突变附近周期满足 T(μ) 与 (μ_c − μ)^{−1/2} 成正比,指数 1/2 普适且由回归映射一阶系数决定。（蓝天周期增长半指数律）
