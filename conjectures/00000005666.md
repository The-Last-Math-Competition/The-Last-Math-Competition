**English.** Definition: The potential kernel is the difference kernel of the Green function of recurrent lattices. Conjecture: The three-dimensional lattice potential-kernel asymptotics is always the Newton-kernel leading term plus an explicit lattice correction; the correction term is given by the analytic-continuation coefficients of the Epstein zeta function, and the coefficients are algebraic numbers; and the correction decays as the negative third power of r, with explicit decay constant. (zeta lattice correction of the three-dimensional potential kernel)

**中文。** 定义：位势核：常返格的格林函数差值核。猜想：三维格的位势核渐近恒为牛顿核主项加显式格点修正；修正项由 Epstein zeta 的解析延拓系数给出且系数为代数数；修正的衰减为 r 的负三次幂且衰减常数显式。（三维位势 zeta 格点修正）
