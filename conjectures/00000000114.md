**English.** Conjecture: (Prime-gap family) For every k ≥ 2, the joint distribution of the consecutive-gap vectors (g_n, g_{n+1}, …, g_{n+k−1}) converges to an i.i.d. exponential distribution (Gallagher's family of conjectures).

**中文。** 猜想：(素数间隙族)对每个 k ≥ 2,相邻素数间隙向量 (g_n, g_{n+1}, …, g_{n+k−1}) 的联合分布收敛于 i.i.d. 指数分布(Gallagher 猜想族)。
