# 00000004261

**English.** Definition: A holomorphic VOA is a self-dual rational vertex operator algebra, and its rank is its central charge. Conjecture: The isomorphism classes of holomorphic VOAs of rank 48 are finite in number, at most 2 to the 10th power, and each can be constructed by orbit decomposition from the Leech lattice VOA. (finiteness of rank-48 holomorphic VOAs)

**中文。** 定义：全纯 VOA 指自对偶的有理顶点算子代数,秩指其中心荷。猜想：秩 48 的全纯 VOA 同构类有限,个数不超过 2 的 10 次幂,且每个均可由 Leech 格 VOA 的轨道分解构造。（秩 48 全纯 VOA 有限性）
