**English.** Definition: most few sample number of sample complexity: from noise data closed-form formula of sign recurrence. Conjecture: sign formula sample number of depth d is n^{c · d} and exponent constant c=1 is logarithm factor of optimal; sample complexity in noise δ under increases log(1/δ) and order not. (sign recurrence sample complexity)
