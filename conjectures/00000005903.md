# 00000005903

**PH2-13 ★** — source: 182_持久同调II.md

**English.** Definition: Two-parameter indecomposables are the indecomposable objects. Conjecture: The conversion formula is explicit in the edge paths of band intervals, single-edge paths for band modules, and closed for finite bands. (edge-path conversion formula of band indecomposables)

**中文。** 定义：双参数不可分解指不可分解对象。猜想：换算公式为带状区间边路径的显式,带状模时为单边路径且有限带下封闭。（带状边路径不可分解换算式）
