# 00000006361

**English.** Definition: The small quantum group is the quotient structure at root points. Conjecture: The representation count of exceptional types is a vertex-dimension closed form; the verification of the closed form is orthogonality; and the kernel of orthogonality is the character inner product. (vertex-dimension counting of small quantum groups)

**中文。** 定义：小量子为根点的商结构。猜想：例外型的表示计数为顶点维数闭式；且闭式的验证为正交性；且正交性的核为特征标内积。（小量子计数顶点维数）
