# 00000000992

**English.** Definition: Expander mixing lemmas control the deviation of random walks on graphs. Conjecture: The quantum expander mixing lemma is explicitly controlled by maximal overlap; the control constant agrees with the classical version and saturates on quantum complete graphs; and the overlap constant decays inversely with the square root of dimension. (quantum expander mixing lemma)

**中文。** 定义：扩展子混引理控制图上随机游走的偏差。猜想：量子扩展子的混引理由最大重叠显式控制；且控制常数与经典版一致且在量子完全图饱和；且重叠常数与维数的平方根成反比地衰减。（量子扩展子混引理）
