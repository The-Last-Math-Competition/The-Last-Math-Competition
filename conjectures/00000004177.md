# 00000004177

**English.** Definition: The minor-arc distribution of primes is the lower bound of prime exponential sums on the minor arcs. Conjecture: The mean-square lower bound of the prime exponential sum on minor arcs is x divided by log squared times an explicit density factor, and the exponent part cannot be compressed further. (mean-square lower bound of prime sums on minor arcs)

**中文。** 定义：素数的次弧分布指素数指数和在次弧上的下界。猜想：素数指数和在次弧的均方下界为 x 除以 log 平方乘显式密度因子,指数部分不可再压。（素数次弧均方下界）
