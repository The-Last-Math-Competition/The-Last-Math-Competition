**GT2-28 ★** — source: 127_数学物理II_规范理论的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The twisted sectors of an orbifold are the modules of its twisted sectors. Conjecture: The attainable table of minimal nontrivial twist orders is exactly the set of primes dividing the central charge, and twists of order above 7 occur only in VOAs of rank at least 32. (attainable table of twisted orders)

**中文。** 定义：orbifold 的 twisted sector 指扭转扇区的模。猜想：最小非平凡扭转阶数的可达表恰为整除中心荷的素数集,阶数超过 7 的扭转只出现在秩不小于 32 的 VOA。（twisted 阶数可达表）
