**NF2-23 ◆** — source: 218_非线性傅里叶分析.md

**English.** Definition: Commutation inequalities are the interchange of integrals along manifolds. Conjecture: The curvature window is given by the nondegeneracy criterion; the width of the window is the torsion; and the width of the torsion window is of constant order. (torsion-width curvature window of commutation)

**中文。** 定义：换序不等式为沿流形的积分换序。猜想：曲率窗由非退化充要条件给出；且窗口的宽度为挠率；且挠率窗口的宽度为常数级。（换序曲率窗挠率宽度）
