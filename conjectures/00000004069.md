# 00000004069

**English.** Definition: A Gavrilov-Guckenheimer bifurcation is a three-dimensional codimension-2 point where a complex pair and a real eigenvalue cross zero simultaneously. Conjecture: The quadratic terms of its normal form can be normalized to a two-parameter family, and the number of remaining invariants after normalization is exactly 1. (number of remaining invariants of the GG normal form)

**中文。** 定义：Gavrilov–Guckenheimer 分叉指一对复与一实特征值同时过零的三维余维 2 点。猜想：其规范形二次项可规范化为两参数族,规范化后的剩余不变量个数恰为 1。（GG 规范形剩余不变量数）
