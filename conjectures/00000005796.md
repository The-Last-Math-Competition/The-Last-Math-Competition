**English.** Definition: The learning coefficient is the analytic invariant of the singularity. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit leading order of the poles of the zeta function, given by the singularity spectrum for degenerate analytic sets, and the coefficient is unique. (zeta-pole leading-order conversion of learning coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：学习系数指奇点的解析不变量。猜想：换算公式为配边 zeta 极点的首阶显式,退化解析集时由奇点谱给出且系数唯一。（学习系数 zeta 极点首阶换算）
