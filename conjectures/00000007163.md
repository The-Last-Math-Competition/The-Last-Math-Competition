# 00000007163

**English.** Definition: Perron-Frobenius with the extremal spectra of positive matrices. Conjecture: The gap between the spectral radius and the next eigenvalue requires almost irreducible primitivity, and the bound of the gap is the positivity modulus. (positivity-modulus PF spectral gap)

**中文。** 定义：Perron–Frobenius：正矩阵的极值谱。猜想：PF 的极值间隙：谱半径与次大的间隙为原几乎不可约且隙的界为正性模。（PF 原几乎不可约正性模谱间隙界）
