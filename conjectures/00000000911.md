**English.** Definition: Singularity means the extreme noncommutativity M′ ∩ M^ω = C. Conjecture: The group factor L(SL₃(Z)) is a single factor; and its singularity gives the first purely operator-theoretic proof of rigidity. (singularity of the SL3Z group factor)
