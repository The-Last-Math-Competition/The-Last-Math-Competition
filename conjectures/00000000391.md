**English.** Definition: The irrationality measure of Catalan's constant G = β(2) is unknown. Conjecture: μ(G) is exactly two; and the attainment of two follows from effective linear forms supplied by the alternating structure of the series for G. (Catalan irrationality measure exactly two)

**中文。** 定义：Catalan 常数 G=β(2) 的无理度未知。猜想：μ(G) 恰为二；且二的达成为 G 的级数交错结构给出有效线性型。（Catalan 无理度恰为二）
