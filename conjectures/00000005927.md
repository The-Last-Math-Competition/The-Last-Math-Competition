# 00000005927

**English.** Definition: The space-time kernel is the transition kernel of the limit process. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the classical kernel (Brownian remainder) plus an explicit correction, complete for narrow-wedge initial data; the self-similarity exponent of the kernel is z = 3/2; and the support of the correction is inside the space-time cone, the cone angle locked by the scaling. (Brownian-remainder conversion of the space-time kernel)

**中文。** 定义：空时核：极限过程的转移核。猜想：换算公式为经典核（Brownian 余项）加显式修正且窄楔初值时为完全型；核的自相似指数为 z=3/2；修正项的支撑为时空锥内且锥角由标度锁定。（空时核布朗余项换算）
