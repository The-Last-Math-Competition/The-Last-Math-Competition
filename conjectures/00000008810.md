# 00000008810

**English.** Definition: Approximate projection: the error-tolerant oracle framework. Conjecture: The cumulative tolerance bound is a square-root window of the total error; and the tolerance allocation is a decreasing sequence. (inexact oracle square-root tolerance accumulation)

**中文。** 定义：近似投影为误差容忍的预言机框架。猜想：容差的累积界为总误差的根号窗；且容差的分配为递减序列。（不精确预言机根号容差累积）
