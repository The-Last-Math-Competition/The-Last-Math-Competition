# 00000000952

**OA-52 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: Erasures (deletion robustness of frames). Conjecture: Among n-dimensional uniform tight frames, the worst spectral deviation upon deleting k frame vectors is at most k/n, with equality precisely for equiangular tight frames (ETFs) (erasure optimality).

**中文。** 定义：erasures(帧的删除鲁棒性)。猜想：n 维均匀紧框架删除 k 帧向量的最坏谱偏差 ≤ k/n 的最优框架为等角紧框架(ETF)(删除最优性)。
