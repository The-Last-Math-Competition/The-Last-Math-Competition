# 00000007648

**HY4-18 ★** — source: 240_微分算子IV_双曲型.md

**English.** Definition: Implicit shock tracking as the free-boundary evolution of shock surfaces. Conjecture: The evolution equation of the shock surface is the normal RH condition; and the curvature correction of the surface is second order. (normal-RH shock-surface evolution)

**中文。** 定义：隐式激波追踪为激波面的自由边界演化。猜想：激波面的演化方程为法向 RH 条件；且面的曲率修正为二阶量。（激波面法向 RH 演化）
