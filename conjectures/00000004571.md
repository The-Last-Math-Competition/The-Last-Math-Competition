**HA3-01 ◆** — source: 138_双曲几何的解析面.md

**English.** Definition: The Teichmuller geodesic flow is the flow on moduli space determined by a quadratic differential. Conjecture: The metric entropy of its Liouville measure is a universal explicit constant, the entropy takes the same value on all holomorphic quadratic differentials, and it can be computed directly from the period graph. (universal constant of Teichmuller entropy)

**中文。** 定义：Teichmüller 测地流指二次微分确定的模空间流。猜想：其 Liouville 测度的度量熵为普适显式常数,熵在一切全纯二次微分上取同值且可由周期图直接算出。（Teichmüller 熵普适常数）
