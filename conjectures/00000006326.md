**English.** Definition: The sparse truncation of inclusion-exclusion is the correction structure of the inclusion-exclusion principle under sparse support. Conjecture: The correction is support-power type and the closed combination is the truncation ideal of the sparse lattice; the generators of the ideal are support monomials; and the truncation error is super-power type, with power the support size. (sparse-lattice power truncation of supports)
