**PS-19 ★** — source: 56_解析组合.md

**English.** Definition: The bond percolation critical point of random planar dual maps. Conjecture: The critical point is a convex correction of the self-dual point, p_c = p_sd + c/n; and the explicit value of c is three quarters. (dual map critical convex correction)

**中文。** 定义：随机平面对偶地图的键渗流临界点。猜想：临界点为自对偶点的凸修正 p_c=p_sd+c/n；且 c 的显式值为四分之三。（对偶地图临界凸修正）
