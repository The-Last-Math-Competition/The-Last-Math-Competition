# 00000008288

**SFL-28 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The viscosity filtering of Hamilton-Jacobi small-noise large deviations: the HJ representation hj_rate of the rate function of small-noise filtering. Conjecture: hj_rate (the large-deviation rate of the filtering error) is the Lagrangian form of the minimal action (a Lagrangian action law); the correction of the convexity of the rate (upper convexification of the nonconvex part) has domain the multimodal posteriors (a multimodal convexification domain law); and the constant of the logarithmic law of Freidlin-Wentzell escape of filtering errors (escape time from potential wells) is twice the potential barrier height (a double barrier law). (Hamilton-Jacobi filtering Lagrangian double barrier)

**中文。** 定义：大偏差的 Hamilton–Jacobi 的粘性滤波：小噪声滤波的大偏差的速率函数的 HJ 表示 hj_rate。猜想：hj_rate（滤波误差的大偏差速率）= 极小作用量的 Lagrange 型（Lagrange 作用律）；且速率的凸性（速率函数的凸包）的修正（非凸部分的上凸化）的域为多峰后验（多峰凸化域律）；滤波误差的 Freidlin–Wentzell 的逃逸（势阱的逃逸时间）的对数律的常数为势垒高度的双倍（双势垒律）。
