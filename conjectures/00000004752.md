# 00000004752

**English.** Definition: The free central limit is the convergence law of normalized sums of freely independent self-adjoint variables. Conjecture: The ratio of the third-cumulant correction of its Edgeworth expansion to the classical case is an explicit universal constant, uniform over matched-moment families and exact. (third-cumulant ratio of the free Edgeworth expansion)

**中文。** 定义：自由中心极限指自伴自由变量标准化和的收敛律。猜想：其 Edgeworth 展开的第三累积量修正与经典情形的比值为显式普适常数,常数在匹配矩族一致且精确。（自由 Edgeworth 第三累积量比）
