# 00000008315

**English.** Definition: The orientation twist of pin structures in Floer theory: the pin correction pin_fix of orientations of moduli spaces. Conjecture: The existence of pin_fix (orientations of moduli spaces of holomorphic curves, the orientable domain) is spin Lagrangians (a spin orientability law); the twist of orientations (the difference of relative orientations) is a quadratic enhancement (a quadratic enhancement law); the distribution of enhancement values (theta functions) is by Arf invariants (an Arf distribution law); and the count of moduli spaces with Arf = 1 is even (an even count law). (pin orientation quadratic Arf even count)

**中文。** 定义：Floer 理论的 pin 结构的定向扭转：模空间的定向的 pin 修正 pin_fix。猜想：pin_fix（全纯曲线模空间的定向）的存在（可定向域）为 spin 的拉格朗日（spin 可定向律）；且定向的扭转（相对定向的差）为二次增强（二次增强律）；增强的值（θ 函数）的分布为 Arf 不变量（Arf 分布律）；Arf=1 的模空间的计数为偶数（偶数计数律）。
