# 00000003477

**GA3-17 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The parity of domino tilings: the parity of the perfect-matching count of planar graphs and the vanishing of Kasteleyn signs. Conjecture: The domino parity of a planar graph is uniquely decided by the class count of face colorings, with the minimal operation flipping parity a single-face flip chain; in the nonplanar generalization parity with Petersen-class counts is a complete invariant. (domino parity criterion)

**中文。** 定义：多米诺重合的奇偶：平面图完美匹配数的奇偶与 Kasteleyn 符号消没。猜想：平面图多米诺奇偶由面染色的类数唯一决定且奇偶翻转的最小操作为单面翻转链；非平面推广时奇偶以 Petersen 类计数为完全不变量。（多米诺奇偶判据）
