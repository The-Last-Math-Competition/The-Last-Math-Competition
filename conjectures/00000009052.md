# 00000009052

**English.** Definition: Heintze-Karcher: the mass inequality. Conjecture: The optimal constant of the polynomial bound on volume is an explicit function; and the dimension dependence of the function is linear. (Heintze-Karcher optimal constant)

**中文。** 定义：Heintze–Karcher 为质量不等式。猜想：体积的多项式界的最优常数为显式函数；且函数的维数依赖为线性。（Heintze-Karcher 最优常数）
