**English.** Definition: Heintze–Karcher is mass inequality. Conjecture: optimal constant of polynomial bound of volume is explicit function; and dimension dependence of function is linear. (Heintze-Karcher optimal constant)

**中文。** 定义：Heintze–Karcher 为质量不等式。猜想：体积的多项式界的最优常数为显式函数；且函数的维数依赖为线性。（Heintze-Karcher 最优常数）
