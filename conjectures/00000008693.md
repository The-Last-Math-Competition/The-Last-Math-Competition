# 00000008693

**English.** Definition: The Wasserstein barycenter: the measure minimizing the squared W_2 average. Conjecture: The barycenter is unique in general dimension (a uniqueness law); the uniqueness exceptions (multiple barycenters) are pairs of symmetric distributions (a symmetric pair example law); regularity is preserved (absolute continuity, a preservation law); and the computation converges linearly by gradient flow (a linear convergence law). (barycenter uniqueness symmetric gradient linear convergence)

**中文。** 定义：Wasserstein 重心：极小化平方 W₂ 的平均测度。猜想：重心的唯一性（一般维数的唯一性）的唯一（唯一律）；且唯一的例外（多重重心）的例为对称分布对的（对称对例律）；重心的正则（AC 保持）的（保持律）；重心的计算（梯度流的收敛）的线性收敛的（线性收敛律）。
