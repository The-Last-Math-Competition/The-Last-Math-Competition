# 00000009865

**KPU-15 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: Second-class particles: position fluctuations of tagged perturbation particles in TASEP. Conjecture: The position of a second-class particle is superdiffusive as t^{2/3}, with limit distribution the first-passage representation of a Baik-Ben Aries type process; the left and right tails of the distribution are piecewise exponential and Gaussian. (second-class particle limit distribution)

**中文。** 定义：第二类粒子：TASEP 中示踪扰动粒子的位置涨落。猜想：第二类粒子的位置按 t^{2/3} 超扩散且极限分布为 Baik–Ben Aries 型过程的首通过表示；分布的左右尾渐近为指数与高斯的分段。（第二类粒子极限分布）
