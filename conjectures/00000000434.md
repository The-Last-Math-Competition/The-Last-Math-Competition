**SA-34 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: Rank and crank are two core statistics on partitions. Conjecture: For fixed modulus m, the joint distribution of rank and crank is asymptotically independent; and the error of independence is an Euler-constant-type correction in the modulus. (rank-crank asymptotic independence)

**中文。** 定义：rank 与 crank 为分拆的两个核心统计量。猜想：固定模 m 下 rank 与 crank 的联合分布渐近独立；且独立的误差为模的欧拉常数型修正。（rank-crank 渐近独立）
