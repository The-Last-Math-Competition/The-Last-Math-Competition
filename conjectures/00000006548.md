**English.** Definition: The spectral index formula with the power-sum convergence structure. Conjecture: The ladder difference is always the index and the convergence order is exact; the exact order is the dimension reciprocal; and the exactness of the convergence order is given by the cancellation of oscillatory traces. (power-sum convergence order of the spectral index)

**中文。** 定义：谱指标公式的幂和收敛结构。猜想：阶梯差恒为指标且收敛阶精确；且精确的阶为维数倒数；且收敛阶的精确性由振荡迹对消给出。（谱指标幂和收敛阶）
