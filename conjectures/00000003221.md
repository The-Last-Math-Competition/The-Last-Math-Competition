**English.** Definition: Random Schrödinger operators: the spectral structure of operators with random potentials. Conjecture: The spectral characterization of Anderson localization: the low-energy region has pure point spectrum for disorder strength above threshold, with the evidence of pure pointedness the fractional-moment self-consistency. (Anderson fractional moment self-consistency evidence)
