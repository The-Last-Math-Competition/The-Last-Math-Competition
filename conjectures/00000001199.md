**LT-99 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Conjecture: The coefficients of the Jack polynomial J^{(α)} at the rational specialization α = (k+1)/(k−1) are products of classical combinatorial numbers (a rational-alpha product formula).

**中文。** 猜想：Jack 多项式 J^{(α)} 的系数在 α = (k+1)/(k−1) 的特殊化为经典组合数的乘积(有理 α 乘积公式)。
