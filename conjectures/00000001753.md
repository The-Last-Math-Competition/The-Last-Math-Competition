# 00000001753

**English.** Definition: The spin characters of 2·S_n are the projective characters of the double cover. Conjecture: The spin character values on alternating classes satisfy |⟨χ,g⟩| ≤ 2^{⌊n/4⌋−1}; controlled by the log-concavity of spin character degrees, improving the order-n bound 2^{(n/2)−1} by the decay factor 2^{⌊n/4⌋}.

**中文。** 定义：2·S_n 的自旋特征标指双覆盖群的射影特征标。猜想：交错类上的自旋特征标值 |⟨χ,g⟩| ≤ 2^{⌊n/4⌋−1};由自旋特征标度的对数凹性控制,阶 n 的界为 2^{(n/2)-1} 的改进(衰减因子 2^{⌊n/4⌋})。
