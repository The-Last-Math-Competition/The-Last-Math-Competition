# 00000005142

**English.** Definition: The Tate conjecture asserts the algebraicity of cohomology classes of specific type. Conjecture: The new row for genus-three self-dual products is given by an explicit Kummer-type family, verified by computing kernel dimensions of cohomology, and the row is closed; the Tate classes are always fully realized in the kernel of the three-layer Selmer group. (Kummer new row of the genus-three Tate conjecture)

**中文。** 定义：Tate 猜想指特定型上同调类的代数性断言。猜想：亏格三的自对偶积的新行由显式库默尔型族给出,经上同调核维数计算验证且行封闭。(Tate 亏格三库默尔新行)；且 Tate 类在三层 Selmer 群核中恒完全实现。
