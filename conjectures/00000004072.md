# 00000004072

**English.** Definition: The switching sequence of a homoclinic network is the word of successive visits of an orbit to the list of equilibria. Conjecture: The count of feasible words is given by powers of the transition matrix determined by the connection residence spectrum, and the exponential growth rate of the count equals the spectral radius of the network. (switching-word count and spectral radius)

**中文。** 定义：同宿网络的切换序列指轨道在平衡点列间的相继访问字。猜想：可行字的计数由连接驻留谱决定的转移矩阵幂给出,计数的指数增长率等于网络谱半径。（切换字计数-谱半径）
