# 00000009108

**English.** Definition: The Crofton formula: the integral geometry of projection counting. Conjecture: The average projection constant is always given by the coefficients of the principal kinematic formula; the formula is closed under superposition of submanifolds; and the combined formula preserves nonnegative coefficients under iteration over submanifolds. (Crofton kinematic constants)

**中文。** 定义：Crofton 公式为投影计数的积分几何。猜想：平均投影常数恒由主运动学公式系数给出；且公式在子流形迭加下封闭；且封闭的组合公式在子流形迭代下保持系数非负。（crofton 运动学常数）
