**English.** Definition: The Arnold exceptional series is the sequence of exceptional families in the classification of singularities. Conjecture: Its member-count formula as a function of dimension is of explicit exponential type, holds for all dimensions, and agrees with the classical low-dimensional tables. (counting formula of Arnold exceptional families)
