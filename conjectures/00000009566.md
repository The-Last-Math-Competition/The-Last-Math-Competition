# 00000009566

**English.** Definition: Quantum unique ergodicity for Veech surfaces: equidistribution of global Laplace eigenfunctions. Conjecture: QUE holds for Laplace eigenfunctions on all Veech surfaces with error rate lambda to the minus one-half plus epsilon; the error exponent 1/2 is optimal, with the extremal example the bottleneck measure in the parallel-flow direction; and the modular-surface proof method (entropy lifting) requires rigid lattice-structure input on general Veech surfaces. (Veech surface QUE error rate)
