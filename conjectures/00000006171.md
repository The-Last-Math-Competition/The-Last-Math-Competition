**RG4-11 ◆** — source: 191_随机图计数II_渐近方法.md

**English.** Definition: Cayley formula is classical counting of tree. Conjecture: height of all tree is size type and constant exact; and kernel of exact is Brownian bridge limit. (tree limit)

**中文。** 定义：Cayley 公式为标号树的经典计数。猜想：均匀标号树的高度为根号尺寸型且常数精确；且精确的核为布朗桥极限。（标号树高根号布朗极限）
