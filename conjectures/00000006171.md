# 00000006171

**English.** Definition: Cayley's formula is the classical count of labeled trees. Conjecture: The height of uniform labeled trees is of root-size type with exact constant, and the kernel of the exactness is the Brownian bridge limit. (root-size Brownian limit of labeled tree heights)

**中文。** 定义：Cayley 公式为标号树的经典计数。猜想：均匀标号树的高度为根号尺寸型且常数精确；且精确的核为布朗桥极限。（标号树高根号布朗极限）
