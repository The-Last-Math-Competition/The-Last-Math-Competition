**English.** Definition: The odd-order theorem states that even order forces an involution. Conjecture: The local obstruction of the minimal counterexample is given by the odd-order closure of the three-layer transitive chain, and the kernel of the closure is the signalizer subgroup. (three-layer transitive closure obstruction of odd order)
