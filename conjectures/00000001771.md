# 00000001771

**English.** Definition: Norton's generalized moonshine refers to the generalized correspondence of twisted McKay–Thompson series of generalized Monstrous modular forms. Conjecture: The smallest nonvanishing example of the principal-part coefficients of the twisted series of commuting pairs (a,b) occurs at the 2A×2A involution pair: the leading coefficient is 2^8; the example is constructed via the kernel of invariant differential operators.

**中文。** 定义：Norton 广义月光指广义 Monstrous 模形式的 twisted McKay–Thompson 级数的广义对应。猜想：交换对 (a,b) 的 twisted 级数的主部系数的最小非零示例在 2A×2A 对合对上:首项系数为 2^8;示例由不变微分算子的核构造。
