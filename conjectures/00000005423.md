# 00000005423

**HM2-13 ★** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: Uniqueness is the essential uniqueness of the extremal metric. Conjecture: The uniqueness criterion is explicit of covering type: unique for the covering type, with an explicit parameter family otherwise, and the criterion is complete. (covering-type uniqueness criterion of extremal metrics)

**中文。** 定义：唯一性指极值度量的本质唯一性。猜想：唯一判据为极值曲线的覆盖型显式,覆盖型时唯一,非覆盖型时存在显式参数族且判据完全。（极值度量覆盖型唯一判据）
