# 00000001326

**English.** Definition: The Futaki invariant is the obstruction quantity for K-stability. Conjecture: K-stability of quotient varieties by discrete groups is always decided by the sign pattern of a weight polynomial; and a flip of the pattern corresponds to wall crossing. (quotient variety weight-pattern stability criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Futaki 不变量指 K 稳定性的障碍量。猜想：离散群商簇的 K 稳定性恒由权多项式的符号模式判定；且模式的翻转对应壁穿越。（商簇权模式稳定判据）
