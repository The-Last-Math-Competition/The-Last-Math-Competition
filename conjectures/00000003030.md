# 00000003030

**English.** Definition: Quantum assertions for many-body systems: sets of local projection measurements. Conjecture: Completeness of assertion sets: local observables decide completely if and only if any two states are separable and the product of separating projections is full rank. (local assertion completeness criterion)

**中文。** 定义：多体系统的量子断言：局域投影测量集。猜想：断言集的完备性：局域可观测量的完备判定当且仅当任意两态可分且分离投影的积为满秩。（局域断言完备判据）
