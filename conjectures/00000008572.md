# 00000008572

**NSP-12 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The complete Pick property of reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces: the condition for complete Pick embedding of normalized kernel matrices, i.e. the Schur complement condition for isometric embedding into Drury-Arveson space. Conjecture: The complete Pick property holds if and only if the kernel embeds isometrically into Drury-Arveson space; the decision can be made in polynomial time in the size of the kernel matrix; the multiplier algebra is generated by one element in one variable and by at most d coordinates in d variables; and the maximal ideal space of the spectrum is homeomorphic to the closure of the embedding image, with the boundary dimension of the image exactly the rank of the kernel. (complete Pick Drury-Arveson embedding decision)

**中文。** 定义：再生核 Hilbert 空间的完备 Pick 性：归一化核矩阵的完全 Pick 嵌入条件，即等距嵌入 Drury–Arveson 空间的 Schur 补条件。猜想：完备 Pick 性当且仅当核可等距嵌入 Drury–Arveson 空间；判定可在核矩阵规模的多项式时间内完成；乘子代数在单变量时由一个元素生成而在 d 变量时需至多 d 个坐标生成；其谱极大理想空间同胚于嵌入像的闭包且像边界维数恰为核的秩。
