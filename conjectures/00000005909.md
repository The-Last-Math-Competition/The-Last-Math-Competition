**PH2-19 ★** — source: 182_持久同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The relative constant is the stability quantity of relative persistent homology. Conjecture: The conversion formula is explicit in the cone length of the inclusion map, one for strong deformation retracts; the lower bound of the cone length is the dimension difference of the two spaces; and the Lipschitz property of the constant is preserved under perturbation, with constant 1. (unit cone-length conversion of the relative constant)

**中文。** 定义：相对常数：相对持久同调的稳定量。猜想：换算公式为包含映射锥长度的显式且强形变收缩时为一；锥长的下界为两空间的维数差；常数的 Lipschitz 性在扰动下保持且常数为 1。（相对锥长度常一换算）
