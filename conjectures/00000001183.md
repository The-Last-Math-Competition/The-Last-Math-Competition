# 00000001183

**English.** Definition: The table of maximal subgroups of G₂(q) is a building block of finite-group classification. Conjecture: The number of classes of triangularly irreducible maximal subgroups of G₂(q) is two; and the two classes are constructed from SL₃ and PGL₂ embeddings. (G2 triangularly irreducible two classes)

**中文。** 定义：G₂(q) 的极大子群表为有限群的分类构件。猜想：G₂(q) 的极大子群的三角不可约子群类个数为二；且两类的构造为 SL₃ 与 PGL₂ 嵌入。（G2 三角不可约两类）
