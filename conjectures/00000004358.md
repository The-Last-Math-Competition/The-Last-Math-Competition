**English.** Definition: The Feynman-Kac formula is the measure representation of the potential-weighted semigroup. Conjecture: The explicit hyperbolic bound of the semigroup norm is the exponential of the infimum of the potential times t, and the optimality of the bound is verified pointwise by solvable families of potentials. (optimality of the hyperbolic Feynman-Kac norm bound)

**中文。** 定义：Feynman–Kac 公式指位势加权半群的测度表示。猜想：半群范数的显式双曲界为势下确界的指数乘 t,界的最优性由可解势族逐点验证。（Feynman–Kac 范数双曲最优）
