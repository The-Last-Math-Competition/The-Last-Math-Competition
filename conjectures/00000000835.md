**English.** Definition: The transport-entropy inequality T₂(C). Conjecture: The T₂ constant of a measure on a compact manifold equals the reciprocal of the Ricci lower bound times an explicit spectral constant (an exact identity between T₂ and Ricci).

**中文。** 定义：transport-entropy 不等式 T₂(C)。猜想：紧流形测度的 T₂ 常数等于 Ricci 下界的倒数乘显式谱常数(T₂ 与 Ricci 的精确等式)。
