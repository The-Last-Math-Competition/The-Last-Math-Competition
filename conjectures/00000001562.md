# 00000001562

**English.** Definition: The plank problem (widths and covering). Conjecture: The covering lower bound by unequal-width planks in complex normed spaces is an explicit convex combination (the complex version of Bang's theorem).

**中文。** 定义：plank problem(宽度和覆盖)。猜想：复赋范空间中不等宽 plank 覆盖的下界为显式凸组合(Bang 定理的复版本)。
