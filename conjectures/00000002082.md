# 00000002082

**SY-02 ★** — source: 55_辛几何与镜像对称.md

**English.** Definition: The EHZ capacity is the Ekeland–Hofer–Zehnder capacity of Lagrangian cylinders. Conjecture: The EHZ capacity of the product of simplices Δ¹×Δ^{n−1} is a radical rational number (concretely the explicit closed form 1/√n); the value is obtained by a Billroth-type geodesic exhaustion.

**中文。** 定义：EHZ 容量指拉格朗日柱体的 Ekeland–Hofer–Zehnder 容量。猜想：单纯形乘积 Δ¹×Δ^{n-1} 的 EHZ 容量为根式有理数(具体为 1/√n 的显式闭式);值由 Billroth 型测地线穷举。
