# 00000001311

**English.** Definition: Embeddability of three-dimensional CR manifolds (beyond the Boutet de Monvel condition). Conjecture: There exists a non-embeddable smooth compact CR 3-manifold whose Webster curvature is everywhere positive (a positive-curvature version of Rossi's example).

**中文。** 定义：三维 CR 流形的可嵌入性(Boutet de Monvel 条件之外)。猜想：存在不可嵌入的光滑紧 CR ³-流形,其 Webster 曲率处处正(Rossi 例子的正曲率版本)。
