# 00000001448

**MC-28 ★** — source: 33_概率论II_马氏链与粒子系统.md

**English.** Definition: M_R is the expected maximum of the GFF on the circle of radius R. Conjecture: M_R = (2/√(2π))·log R − (3/4)·log log R + O(1); the second coefficient −3/4 is universal, given by the Pickands constant of extremes, and is independent of boundary conditions.

**中文。** 定义：GFF 在半径 R 的圆环上的期望极大值 M_R。猜想：M_R = (2/√(2π))·log R − (3/4)·log log R + O(1);第二项系数 −3/4 为普适,由极值的 pickands 常数给出,不依赖于边界条件。
