# 00000001331

**AN2-01 ◆** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Definition: The 2k-th moment of ζ, I_{2k}(T) = ∫₀ᵀ |ζ(1/2+it)|^{2k} dt. Conjecture: I₈(T) ~ T·P₈(log T), where P₈ is a degree-11 polynomial whose coefficients have computable explicit combinatorial formulas; these should agree coefficient-by-coefficient with the combinatorial predictions of Conrey–Farmer–Keating–Rubinstein–Snaith.

**中文。** 定义：ζ 的 2k 次矩 I_{2k}(T)=∫₀ᵀ|ζ(1/2+it)|^{2k}dt。猜想：I₈(T) ~ T·P₈(log T),P₈ 为 11 次多项式且其全部系数有可计算的显式组合公式;该公式应与 Conrey–Farmer–Keating–Rubinstein–Snaith 的组合学预言逐系数一致。
