# 00000001615

**SP-15 △** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: The Hilbert–Pólya program realizes the zeros of ζ as eigenvalues of a self-adjoint operator; Berry–Keating proposed H = xp. Conjecture: There exists a self-adjoint extension of xp with boundary conditions whose spectrum is exactly the imaginary parts of the nontrivial zeros of ζ; both halves (self-adjointness and coincidence with zeros) are currently unproven.

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert–Pólya 纲领指以自伴算子的特征值实现 ζ 零点的方案;Berry–Keating 提出 H=xp。猜想：存在 xp 的自伴延拓与边界条件使其谱恰为 ζ 非平凡零点的虚部;对应的两半(自伴性与零点重合)目前均无证明。
