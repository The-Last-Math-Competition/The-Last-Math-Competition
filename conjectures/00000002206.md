# 00000002206

**English.** Definition: The w-divisor is the w-closure image of fractional ideals. Conjecture: For strongly Mori domains, the finiteness of the rank of the w-divisor group is in explicit equality with the t-dimension of the coefficient field (w-divisor rank).

**中文。** 定义：w-除子(分式理想的 w-闭包像)。猜想：强 Mori 域的 w-除子群的秩的有限性与系数域的 t-维数显式等式(w-除子秩)。
