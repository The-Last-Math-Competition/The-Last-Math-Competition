**SC6-07 ★** — source: 225_概形论II.md

**English.** Definition: Hilbert schemes as the moduli of closed subschemes. Conjecture: The tangent space is the explicit Hom, and the dimension is minimal at flat points. (flat-minimal Hilbert tangent Hom)

**中文。** 定义：Hilbert 概形：闭子概形的模。猜想：Hilbert 的切空间：切空间为 Hom 的显式且式的维数在平坦点为极小。（Hilbert 切空间 Hom 平坦极小显式维数）
