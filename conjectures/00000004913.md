**English.** Definition: A high-probability bound is the error control under uniform probability. Conjecture: The high-probability constant of two-action Q-learning is an explicit multiple of the logarithmic factor, given by the tight constant of concentration inequalities and matching the lower bound. (tight high-probability constant of Q-learning)
