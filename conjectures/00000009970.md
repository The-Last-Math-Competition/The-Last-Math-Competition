# 00000009970

**MRG-30 △** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Arithmetic quantum limits: quantum ergodicity of eigenfunctions of arithmetic surfaces (proven by Lindenstrauss). Conjecture: The mass concentration obstruction of quantum limits of convex-cocompact nonarithmetic hyperbolic surfaces is explicit: the codimension lower bound of the support of quantum limits is one, the obstruction constructed explicitly from the diffusion rate of the normal flow; and the lower bound is tight on cocompact surfaces. (nonarithmetic quantum limit codimension)

**中文。** 定义：算术量子极限：算术曲面本征函数的量子遍历（Lindenstrauss 已证）。猜想：凸余紧非算术双曲曲面的量子极限的质量集中障碍显式化：量子极限支撑的余维下界为一且障碍由法向流的扩散速率显式构造；下界在余紧曲面为紧。（非算术量子极限余维）
