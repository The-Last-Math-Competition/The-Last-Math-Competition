# 00000001120

**English.** Definition: The Witt ring of the character ring is the quadratic-form structure on real-valued characters. Conjecture: The Witt structure of SU(n) is determined by explicit relations among Chern classes; and the minimal generating set of the relations is the symmetric polynomials in the Chern classes. (SU character Witt structure)

**中文。** 定义：特征标环的 Witt 环为实值特征标的二次型结构。猜想：SU(n) 的 Witt 结构由 Chern 类的显式关系决定；且关系的极小生成集为陈类的对称多项式。（SU 特征标 Witt 结构）
