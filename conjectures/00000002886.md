# 00000002886

**English.** Definition: The duality of K-homology: the operator realization of Poincaré duality. Conjecture: The index realization of dual classes: the K-homology representative of the fundamental class is the Dirac class, and the index of every elliptic operator is given by pairing with the Dirac class; nondegeneracy of the pairing is the operator side of Poincaré duality. (Dirac fundamental class realization)

**中文。** 定义：K-同调的对偶：Poincaré 对偶的算子实现。猜想：对偶类的指标实现：基本类的 K-同调代表为 Dirac 类且一切椭圆算子的指标由与 Dirac 类的配对给出；配对的非退化性为 Poincaré 对偶的算子侧。（Dirac 基本类实现）
