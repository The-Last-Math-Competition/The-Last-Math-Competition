**English.** Definition: Compact generators are the sets of elements generating a category by compact objects. Conjecture: The minimal count of generator pairs for a doubly generated stable category is two, characterized by the nonvanishing of the orthogonal complement, and the lower bound is attainable. (minimal pair count two of double generation)
