# 00000007347

**English.** Definition: Nonstandard number theory with hyper versions of Diophantine equations. Conjecture: Hyperfinite solutions of equations exist, and the standard part of the solutions is a genuine solution. (standard-part hyper-Diophantine solutions)

**中文。** 定义：非标准数论：丢番图的超版本。猜想：超丢番图：方程的超有限解且解的标准部分为真解。（超丢番图标准部分超有限方程真解）
