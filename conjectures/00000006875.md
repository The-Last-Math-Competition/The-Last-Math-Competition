# 00000006875

**English.** Definition: Free concentration with noncommutative concentration. Conjecture: The concentration of measures holds, the combination of concentration being Lipschitz functionals. (Lipschitz-functional free concentration)

**中文。** 定义：自由的集中：非交换集中。猜想：自由的集中：测度的集中且中的组合为 Lipschitz 泛函。（自由集中 Lipschitz 泛函测度集中组合集中泛函 Lipschitz 测度泛函集中自由 Lipschitz）
