**SA-99 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The hitting probability of vicious walkers is the probability p_n that n nonintersecting simple random walks go from fixed starting points to fixed endpoints without ever intersecting. Conjecture: p_n is identically equal to the value, at the corresponding endpoints, of the joint density of the ordered eigenvalues of an n×n GUE matrix, times the explicit Vandermonde factor ∏_{i<j}(y_j−y_i−(x_j−x_i)); this identity holds for all times T > 0.

**中文。** 定义：vicious walkers 的到达概率指 n 条非交简单随机行走从固定起点到固定终点、全程不相交的概率 p_n。猜想：p_n 恒等于 GUE n×n 矩阵的次序特征值联合密度在相应端点处的值乘以显式 Vandermonde 因子 ∏_{i<j}(y_j−y_i−(x_j−x_i));该恒等式对一切时间 T>0 成立。
