**English.** Definition: The cusped space is the conical geometric model of a relatively hyperbolic group. Conjecture: For the case of a two-group periphery, the estimate of the hyperbolicity constant is an explicit quadratic expression in the peripheral parameters, monotone and optimal. (quadratic estimate of the cusped hyperbolicity constant)
