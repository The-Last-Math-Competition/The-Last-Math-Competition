**English.** Definition: The Feynman-Kac of transport: the path integral of stochastic transport. Conjecture: The minimal orbit of path OT is the least-action orbit of the Schroedinger system; and the caustic set of the semiclassical WKB correction is the folding set of the Legendre map. (path OT WKB caustic)
