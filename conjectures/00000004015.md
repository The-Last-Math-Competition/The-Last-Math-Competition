# 00000004015

**RP2-15 ★** — source: 119_随机分析II_粗路径.md

**English.** Definition: A rough Volterra equation is a rough integral equation with a memory kernel. Conjecture: The regularity rho of the kernel and the regularity sigma of the solution satisfy the conservation law sigma + rho = 1/p, which breaks at the singular endpoints of the kernel by exactly half an order. (Volterra regularity conservation)

**中文。** 定义：粗糙 Volterra 方程指带记忆核的粗糙积分方程。猜想：核的正则性 ρ 与解的正则性 σ 满足守恒 σ + ρ = 1/p,守恒在核的奇异端点破缺半阶且恰为半阶。（Volterra 正则性守恒）
