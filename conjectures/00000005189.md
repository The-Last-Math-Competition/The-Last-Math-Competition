# 00000005189

**English.** Definition: Mutation is the orbit of an exceptional collection under the braid group action. Conjecture: The period table of mutation orbits is indexed explicitly by the kernel of the braid element, and the braid period of the cubic threefold is six; the lower bound of the period is 3; the period equals the motive rank of the residual category, with deviation only in rank-2 exceptions. (braid-kernel period structure of mutations)

**中文。** 定义：变异：例外集在辫群作用下的轨道。猜想：变异轨道的周期表由辫元素的核显式索引且立方三次超曲面的辫元周期为六；周期的下界为 3；周期与残余范畴的母题秩相等且偏差仅出现在秩 2 的例外。（变异辫元核周期结构）
