# 00000003969

**English.** Definition: The cop number is the minimal number of cops guaranteeing capture in the pursuit game. Conjecture: The cop number of a one-face embedded graph on a surface of genus g is at most floor(2*sqrt(g)) + 3, and this bound is attained by explicit construction families. (square-root bound for cop number on surfaces)

**中文。** 定义：cop 数指追捕博弈中保证捕获的最小警察数。猜想：亏格 g 曲面上单面嵌入图的 cop 数不超过 ⌊2√g⌋ + 3,且该界由显式构造族达到。（曲面 cop 数平方根界）
