# 00000002733

**SC-23 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: The T-construction and bistellar flips: elementary operations on combinatorial manifolds. Conjecture: Connectivity of bistellar flows: the number of bistellar equivalence classes of d-dimensional combinatorial manifolds is at most two for d ≥ 4, decided by the parity of middle Betti numbers; for d = 3 the class count has a logarithmic upper bound in the vertex count. (bistellar class count dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：T-construction 与 bistellar flip：组合流形的初等操作。猜想：bistellar 流的连通性：d 维组合流形的 bistellar 等价类的个数在 d≥4 为至多两个且两类的判据为中间 Betti 数的奇偶；d=3 的类数为顶点数的对数型上界。（bistellar 类数二分）
