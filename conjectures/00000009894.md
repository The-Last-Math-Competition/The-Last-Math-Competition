# 00000009894

**English.** Definition: Branches of moduli spaces: irreducible branches of representation varieties at given dimension vectors (Schofield gave the parametrization). Conjecture: Two branches intersect nonemptily if and only if the Schofield inequality system of the corresponding general subrepresentation chains has a solution; the criterion is linear programming, and the expected dimension of branch intersection is explicit. (branch intersection combinatorial criterion)

**中文。** 定义：模空间的分支：给定维数向量的表示簇的不可约分支（Schofield 给出参数化）。猜想：两个分支相交非空当且仅当对应的一般子表示链的 Schofield 不等式组的解存在；判据为线性规划且分支相交的期望维数显式。（分支相交组合判据）
