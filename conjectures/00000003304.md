# 00000003304

**English.** Definition: Hurwitz zeta: ζ with shifted parameter. Conjecture: Rational points of Hurwitz: Hurwitz with rational parameter is a combination of Dirichlet characters, with the weights of the combination Ramanujan sums. (Hurwitz Ramanujan sum combination weights)

**中文。** 定义：Hurwitz zeta：带参数平移的 ζ。猜想：Hurwitz 的有理点：有理参数的 Hurwitz 为 Dirichlet 特征的组合且组合的权为 Ramanujan 和。（Hurwitz Ramanujan 和组合权）
