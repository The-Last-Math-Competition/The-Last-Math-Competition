# 00000008482

**English.** Definition: The convergence error of Kingman's subadditive ergodic theorem: the decay order of |n^{-1}E[X_n] - gamma| in n. Conjecture: The universal upper bound for bounded subadditive families is O(log n/n) (a logarithmic harmonic law); the bound cannot be improved, extremal examples being realized by discretization deviations of Sturmian balanced words (a Sturmian extremal law); and in the unbounded case the error can decay arbitrarily slowly (an arbitrary slowness law). (Kingman Sturmian extremal logarithmic decay)

**中文。** 定义：Kingman 次可加遍历定理的收敛误差：|n^{-1}E[X_n]-γ| 关于 n 的衰减阶。猜想：有界次可加族的普适上界为 O(log n/n)（对数调和律）；且该界不可改进，极值例由 Sturmian 平衡词的离散化偏差实现（Sturmian 极值律）；无界情形误差可衰减任意慢（任意慢律）。
