**English.** Definition: The AR-quiver is the almost-split quiver of an algebra. Conjecture: There exist two derived equivalent algebras whose AR-quivers are not isomorphic, and the precise invariant of the AR-quiver under derived equivalence is the translation quiver of the singularity category. (exact derived-equivalence invariant of the AR-quiver)
