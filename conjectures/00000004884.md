**English.** Definition: A hybrid system is a dynamical system mixing continuous and discrete parts. Conjecture: The topological criterion of its independent statements is an explicit combinatorial condition of limit cycles and infinite switching, complete in dimension two, and the decision reduces to finite-automaton reachability.
