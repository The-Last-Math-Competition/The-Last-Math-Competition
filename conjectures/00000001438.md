# 00000001438

**English.** Conjecture: The distribution of the final size of SIR epidemics on the configuration model is a two-point mixture: an atom at w plus a continuous part, with weights given by explicit percolation flows (final-size mixture).

**中文。** 猜想：SIR 在配置模型上的最终规模的分布为双峰混合:原子 w + 连续部分,权重为显式过渗流量(最终规模混合)。
