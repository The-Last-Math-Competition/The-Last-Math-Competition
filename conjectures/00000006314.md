# 00000006314

**English.** Definition: Doubly stochastic scaling is the nonnegative matrix scaling of matrices, with the spectral gap. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit value of the scaling spectral gap, the gap being of classical triangular type; the minimum of the gap is at torus uniformization of transition matrices; and the convergence rate of the scaling is half the gap, the half constant being the geometric rate of Sinkhorn scaling. (triangular conversion of scaling spectral gaps)

**中文。** 定义：双随机缩放：矩阵的非负矩阵缩放与谱隙。猜想：换算常数为缩放谱隙的显式值且谱隙为经典三角型；谱隙的极小在转移矩阵的环面均匀化处；缩放的收敛率为谱隙之半且半常数为 Sinkhorn 定标的几何率。（缩放谱隙三角值换算常数）
