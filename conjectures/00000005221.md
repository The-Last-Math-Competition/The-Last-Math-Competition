# 00000005221

**English.** Definition: The approximation rate is the convergence speed of random matrices to the limit algebra. Conjecture: The spectral approximation rate of free group factors is an explicit expression of the negative one-half power of the dimension times a double logarithm, controlled by the lower bound of the smallest spectral value and matching the single-point spectrum. (double-logarithm approximation rate of free factors)

**中文。** 定义：逼近速率指随机矩阵到极限代数的收敛速度。猜想：自由群因子的谱逼近速率为维数负二分之一次幂乘双对数型显式式,由最小谱值下界控制且匹配单点谱。（自由因子双对数逼近速率）
