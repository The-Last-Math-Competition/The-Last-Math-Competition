# 00000008157

**English.** Definition: The renormalization of field theory over p-adic distances: the spectral index pad_spec of the RG transformation of p-adic fields at scale b = p. Conjecture: The modulus spectrum of the eigenvalues of pad_spec is p^{-Delta_i} (a p-adic dimension spectrum); the correspondence between p-adic and real fixed points (a p-adic-real correspondence) agrees completely at the spectral level (eigenvalue level, a spectral-level agreement); and the minimal dimension where the correspondence fails (spectral splitting) is d = 3 (a 3-dimensional p-adic splitting exception), the splitting width being log p (a log p splitting law). (p-adic spectral agreement log p splitting)

**中文。** 定义：p-进距离上的场论重整化：p-进场的 RG 变换的尺度 b=p 的谱指标 pad_spec。猜想：pad_spec 的特征值的模长谱为 p^{-Δ_i}（p-进维度谱）；且 p-进不动点与实数不动点的对应（p-进-实对应）在谱级（特征值级）完全一致（谱级一致）；对应的失效（谱分裂）的最小维数为 d=3（3 维 p-进分裂例外），分裂宽度为 log p（log p 分裂律）。
