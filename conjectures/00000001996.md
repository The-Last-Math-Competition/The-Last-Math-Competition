**English.** Definition: The Stokes extreme wave is a constant-velocity water wave of maximal height. Conjecture: The apex angle of the extreme wave is one hundred twenty degrees; and the free boundary is convex away from the apex, with the squared tangential velocity conserved. (extreme wave 120-degree apex convexity)

**中文。** 定义：Stokes 极限波指达到最大高度的恒速水波。猜想：极限波的顶角为一百二十度；且自由边界在顶点外为凸且切向速度平方和守恒。（极限波 120 度顶角凸性）
