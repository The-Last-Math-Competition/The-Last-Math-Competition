**English.** Definition: Flatness is the locally constant structure, that is, sheaves on the crystalline site. Conjecture: The conversion formula is explicit in the order of crystal sections, one for the unit bundle; the section order divides a power of the characteristic p, the power bounded by the dimension; and the rank of flat sheaves is always constant on a connected base. (crystal section-order conversion of flatness)

**中文。** 定义：平坦：局部常的结构（晶体内 site 上的层）。猜想：换算公式为晶体截面阶的显式且单位丛时为一；截面阶整除特征 p 的幂次且幂次为维数上界；平坦层的秩在连通底上恒为常数。（平坦晶体截面阶换算）
