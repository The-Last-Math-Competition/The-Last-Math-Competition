# 00000008015

**DEL-25 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The turning-point theorem for the Muskat problem of Hele-Shaw free boundaries: the explicit domain of the critical slope parameter sigma_c(t) of two-phase Muskat solutions at which the turnover (from stable to Rayleigh-Taylor unstable) occurs. Conjecture: The critical slope is exactly the L-infinity norm of the interface slope with value 1 (precise threshold sigma_c = 1); the singularity time of the evolution of the unstable interface (after turnover) is an explicit multiple of O((sigma-1)^{-1}); and global existence in the stable domain passes to the zero-viscosity-difference limit (the Euler limit of Hele-Shaw) with interchange rate the square root of the viscosity ratio. (Muskat critical slope turnover law)

**中文。** 定义：Hele-Shaw 自由边界的 Muskat 问题的中点定理：两相 Muskat 的解的临界斜率的显式域：斜率参数 σ_c(t) 使翻转（由稳定到 Rayleigh–Taylor 不稳定）发生。猜想：临界斜率的显式值为界面斜率的 L∞ 范数的 1（精确阈值 σ_c=1）；且不稳定界面的演化（翻转后）的奇性发生时间为 O(σ-1)^{-1} 的显式倍数；稳定域的解的全局存在性在零粘度差的极限交换到零（Hele-Shaw 的 Eulers 极限），交换速率为粘度比的 1/2 次幂。
