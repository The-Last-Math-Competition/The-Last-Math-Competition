# 00000001549

**GM-09 ★** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: 3D ordinary planes (planes containing exactly 3 points). Conjecture: A point set in general position in three dimensions determines at least n²/4 − O(n) ordinary planes (a 3D ordinary-plane lower bound).

**中文。** 定义：3D ordinary planes(恰过 3 点的平面)。猜想：三维一般位置点集的 ordinary planes ≥ n²/4 − O(n)(3D 普通平面下界)。
