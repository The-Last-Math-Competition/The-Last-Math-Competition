# 00000008652

**English.** Definition: The Dehn function delta(n) is the worst-case van Kampen area of the word problem. Conjecture: The realizable values of Dehn functions are n^alpha (alpha a rational with even denominator, at least 1), exponential type, or worse; the Baumslag-Solitar group BS(1,2) is the minimal example of exponential Dehn function; and subexponential superpolynomial types are realized by explicit constructions of non-CAT(0) groups. (Dehn function realization spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Dehn 函数 δ(n) 指字问题的最坏消去面积。猜想：Dehn 函数的可实现值为 n^α（α ≥ 1 为偶分母有理数）、指数型或更坏；Baumslag–Solitar 群 BS(1,2) 为指数 Dehn 的最小例子；亚指数超多项式型由非 CAT(0) 群的显式构造实现。（Dehn 函数实现谱）
