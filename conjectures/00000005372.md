# 00000005372

**English.** Definition: The covolume is the volume of the fundamental domain of a lattice. Conjecture: The first gap of the covolume spectrum of three-dimensional arithmetic lattices is the explicit difference of the two smallest volumes, given by invariant pairings, with no closer approach. (first-gap value of three-dimensional covolumes)
