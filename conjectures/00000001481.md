# 00000001481

**English.** Definition: The standard map T_p(x,y) = (x+y+p sin x, y+p sin x). Conjecture: For Lebesgue-almost every parameter p, the map is ergodic with respect to area; moreover, as p → 0 the lower envelope of the average positive Lyapunov exponent starts as c·√p (exponential growth starts with a square-root law in the parameter).

**中文。** 定义：标准映射 T_p(x,y)=(x+y+p sin x, y+p sin x)。猜想：对 Lebesgue-几乎处处的参数 p,映射关于面积测度遍历;进一步,小参数 p→0 时正 Lyapunov 指数的平均下包络为 c·√p(指数增长随参数以平方根律启动)。
