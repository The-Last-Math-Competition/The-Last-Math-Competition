# 00000002844

**English.** Definition: Polynomial systems of tensor eigenvalues: the algebraic degree of E-eigenvalues. Conjecture: The counting law of tensor eigenvalues: the number of E-eigenvalues of an order-d n-dimensional symmetric tensor is ((d−1)^n−1)/(d−2), with multiplicity structure given explicitly by the stratification of the discriminant. (E-eigenvalue counting formula)

**中文。** 定义：张量特征值的多项式方程组：EE 特征值的代数度。猜想：张量特征值的计数律：d 阶 n 维对称张量的 E-特征值个数为 ((d−1)^n−1)/(d−2) 且重数结构由判别式的分层显式给出。（E 特征值计数公式）
