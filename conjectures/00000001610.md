# 00000001610

**SP-10 ★** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: The Sturm–Liouville half-line problem concerns spectral inversion of potentials. Conjecture: For q ∈ L¹, the first k asymptotic coefficients of the Weyl function m are completely determined by the first k moments of q; and the alignment is a bijection. (moment-coefficient alignment bijective)

**中文。** 定义：Sturm–Liouville 半轴问题指位势的谱反演。猜想：q 属于 L¹ 时 Weyl 系数 m 的前 k 个渐近系数恒由 q 的前 k 个矩完全确定；且对齐为一一对应。（矩-系数对齐一一）
