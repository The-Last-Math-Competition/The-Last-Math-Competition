# 00000002067

**UA-17 ★** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Isbell duality is the adjoint dualization of algebras and geometric objects. Conjecture: The commutative rings of self-Isbell objects (fixed points of the dual composite) are compact Jacobson rings (an explicit classification); the class contains all finite-type rings of number rings.

**中文。** 定义：Isbell 对偶指代数与几何对象的伴随对偶化。猜想：自 Isbell 对象(对偶复合的不动点)的交换环为紧 Jacobson 环(显式分类);该类含一切数环的有限型生成。
