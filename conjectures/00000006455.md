# 00000006455

**MF4-25 ★** — source: 200_模形式III_Maass形.md

**English.** Definition: high precision is computation structure of number of digits. Conjecture: linear product of rounding and by condition number controls; and normal of controls is interval arithmetic. (rounding linear product interval arithmetic)

**中文。** 定义：高精度为超多位数的计算结构。猜想：舍入的线性累积且爆炸由条件数控制；且控制的方法为区间算术。（舍入线性累积区间算术防爆）
