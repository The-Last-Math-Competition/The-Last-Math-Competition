# 00000006455

**English.** Definition: High precision is the computation structure of very many digits. Conjecture: Rounding accumulates linearly and the explosion is controlled by the condition number, the method of control being interval arithmetic. (interval-arithmetic explosion control of linear rounding)

**中文。** 定义：高精度为超多位数的计算结构。猜想：舍入的线性累积且爆炸由条件数控制；且控制的方法为区间算术。（舍入线性累积区间算术防爆）
