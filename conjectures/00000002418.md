**English.** Definition: The layer structure of the 2-class field towers of imaginary quadratic fields. Conjecture: The distribution of the orders of the 2-class groups at the n-th layer is of Cohen–Lenstra type; and the mean of the distribution is the quadratic imaginary character. (2-class field tower layer order CL distribution)

**中文。** 定义：虚二次域的二类域塔的层结构。猜想：第 n 层二类群的阶的分布为 Cohen–Lenstra 型；且分布的均值为二次虚特征。（二类域塔层阶 CL 分布）
