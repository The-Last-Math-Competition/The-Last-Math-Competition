**English.** Definition: The Li–Yau constant is the optimal factor in gradient estimates for the heat kernel. Conjecture: On convex domains, the optimal constant in the Li–Yau inequality is replaced explicitly by the eigenvalue ratio; and the equivalence of the improvements preserves parabolic scaling. (Li-Yau constant equivalent improvement)
