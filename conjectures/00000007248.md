# 00000007248

**English.** Definition: The linking theorem as the extension of the mountain-pass type. Conjecture: The classification of topological links holds with the pseudo-gradient as criterion; and link classes are completely distinguished by the Conley-index pairs of the pseudo-gradient flow. (Conley-distinguished link classification)

**中文。** 定义：链接定理：山路型的推广。猜想：链接的分类：拓扑链接的类别且别的判据为伪梯度。(链接伪梯度拓扑类别判据分类)；且链接类别由伪梯度流的 Conley 指标对完全区分。
