# 00000009720

**SCA-20 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Minimal components of profile decompositions for scattering: energy decomposed as direct sums of linear profiles. Conjecture: The minimal component count of the profile decomposition of the initial energy is explicitly given by the number of isolated frequencies of the linear spectrum; the component count is nondecreasing under time evolution and exactly conserved in the scattering case. (profile component counting)

**中文。** 定义：散射的剖面分解的最小分量：能量分解为线性轮廓的直和。猜想：初始能量的剖面分解的最小分量数由线性谱的孤立频率个数显式给出；分量数在时间演化下不减且散射情形恰好守恒。（剖面分量计数）
