# 00000005483

**English.** Definition: The normalization of the minimal solution is the scaling constant of the minimal form factor. Conjecture: The semi-normalized constant table is an explicit product of Gamma functions, degenerating to one at the free-boson limit; the poles of the Gamma product vanish at nonpositive integers, the vanishing given by functional equations; and the constant is homogeneous of degree zero under mass-ratio scaling. (semi-normalized Gamma constant product)

**中文。** 定义：最小解的定标：归一常数（最小形式因子的标度）。猜想：半定标的常数表为 Gamma 函数显式积且自由玻色子极限处退化为一；Gamma 积的极点在非正整数处消没且消没由泛函方程给出；常数在质量比的缩放下齐次且齐次度为零。（半定标伽马常数积）
