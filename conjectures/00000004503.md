# 00000004503

**English.** Definition: Transfinite replacement is the constructively restricted form of the replacement axiom. Conjecture: The lower bound of its bounded ordinals is a transfinite elementary function of the Church-Kleene ordinal, the bound is explicit and attained in models of second-order arithmetic. (Church-Kleene bound of transfinite replacement)

**中文。** 定义：超限替代指替换公理的构造性受限形式。猜想：其有界序数的下界为 Church–Kleene 序数的超限初等函数,界显式且在二阶算术模型中达到。（超限替代 Church-Kleene 界）
