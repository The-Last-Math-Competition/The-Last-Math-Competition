**AC3-05 ★** — source: 105_代数编码II_卷积与格码.md

**English.** Definition: The covering radius of lattices: the farthest lattice-point distance. Conjecture: The union of covering and packing: the ratio of covering radius to packing radius is the dual of Voronoi cells, with the extremum of the ratio body-centered lattices. (covering packing Voronoi body-centered extremum ratio)

**中文。** 定义：格的覆盖半径：最远格点距离。猜想：覆盖与填满的联合：覆盖半径与填满半径的比为 Voronoi 胞的对偶且比的极值为体心格。（覆盖填满 Voronoi 体心极值比）
