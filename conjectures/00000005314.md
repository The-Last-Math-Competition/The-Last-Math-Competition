**English.** Definition: Spectral stability is the convergence of the cohomology spectra of families. Conjecture: The spectral conversion is an explicit expression of the eigenvalue gaps of the monodromy operator, stability holds at smooth truncations, and the conversion preserves the mixed structure. (eigenvalue-gap stable conversion of spectral families)
