# 00000006341

**English.** Definition: The ordered monomial basis is the classical-type basis of the enveloping algebra. Conjecture: The rank-two coefficients of the transition matrix are classical polynomials with positive sign, and the proof of positivity is the pairing identity. (positive classical transition coefficients)
