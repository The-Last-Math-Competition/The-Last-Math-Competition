# 00000005511

**QN2-11 ★** — source: 169_排队网络的图极限.md

**English.** Definition: The mean-field limit is the deterministic limit as the node count grows. Conjecture: The convergence rate of the empirical measure is the negative one-half power of the node count, controlled by the Lipschitz constant of the drift, and the constant is attained. (half-power convergence Lipschitz constant of mean fields)

**中文。** 定义：平均场极限指节点数增大的确定极限。猜想：经验测度的收敛速率为节点数的负二分之一次幂,由漂移的利普希茨常数控制且常数达到。（平均场半幂收敛利普希茨常数）
