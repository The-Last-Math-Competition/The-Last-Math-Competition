# 00000006210

**English.** Definition: The soliton spectrum and the weak-turbulence rate are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two equations with the same soliton spectrum but different weak-turbulence rates, and the separation is realized by an explicit lattice pair with the same spectrum but different resonance truncation. (same soliton spectrum-different truncation separation)

**中文。** 定义：孤立子谱与弱湍流率指两层。猜想：存在两方程的孤子谱相同而弱湍流率不同,分离由同谱异共振截断的显式格对实现。（孤谱同-湍流率异截断分离）
