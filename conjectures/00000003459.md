# 00000003459

**NA2-29 ★** — source: 100_赋范空间与逼近论II.md

**English.** Definition: The joint approximation of several functions: simultaneous rational interpolation. Conjecture: The law of simultaneous approximation: the law of vector-valued approximation is the weighting of components, with the optimal weights the balance of component norms. (simultaneous approximation component norm balanced weights)

**中文。** 定义：有理插值的误定：多个函数的联立逼近。猜想：联立逼近的律：向量值逼近的律为分量的加权且权的最优为分量范数的平衡。（联立逼近分量范数平衡权）
