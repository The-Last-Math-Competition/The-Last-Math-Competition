# 00000007996

**DEL-06 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The limit spectrum of the Almgren frequency function on free boundaries: the value spectrum Spec_FB of the r to 0 limit of the frequency N(r) = r D(r)/H(r) (energy-height ratio) at free boundary points. Conjecture: Spec_FB is a countable discrete set with minimal element 3/2 (the half-integral rigidity of Signorini); frequency values correspond one-to-one to tangent cones, the correspondence being the degree-dual of homogeneous harmonic functions; the infimum of gaps of the spectrum is 1/2 (a half-integral lattice), and the inverse of the monotonicity of the frequency (rectifiability as r tends to infinity) is a quantitative form of the unique continuation theorem. (Almgren frequency half-integral spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：自由边界的 Almgren 频率函数的极限谱：解 u 的频率 N(r)=rD(r)/H(r)（能量-高度比）的 r→0 极限在自由边界点的取值谱 Spec_FB。猜想：Spec_FB 为可数离散集且最小元为 3/2（Signorini 的半整刚性）；频率的取值与切锥一一对应，对应映射为齐次调和函数的次数-dual；谱的间隙的下确界为 1/2（半整分格），频率的单调性的逆（r→∞ 的整流性）为唯一延拓定理的定量形式。
