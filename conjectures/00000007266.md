# 00000007266

**English.** Definition: The variational spectrum with the min-max of eigenvalues. Conjecture: The maximin characterization of eigenvalues holds, and the equivalence of the characterization is the Rayleigh quotient. (Rayleigh-equivalent spectral min-max)

**中文。** 定义：变分谱：特征值的极小极大。猜想：谱的极小极大：特征值的极大极小刻画且画的等价为 Rayleigh 商。（谱 Rayleigh 商极小极大特征值等价刻画）
