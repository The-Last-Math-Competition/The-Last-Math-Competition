# 00000002829

**English.** Definition: Ergodic decomposition of deviations: the LDP stratification of mixing systems. Conjecture: The rate stratification of ergodic decompositions: the LDP rate of convex combinations of invariant measures is the infimum envelope of component rates, and the minimizer set of the envelope is the set of extreme points of the decomposition. (ergodic decomposition rate envelope)

**中文。** 定义：偏差的遍历分解：混合系统的 LDP 分层。猜想：遍历分解的速率分层：不变测度凸组合的 LDP 速率为分量速率的下确界包络且包络的极小点集为分解的极端点。（遍历分解速率包络）
