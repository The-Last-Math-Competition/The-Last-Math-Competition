# 00000006601

**English.** Definition: Hochschild homology is the trace-type invariant of the category. Conjecture: The weight spectrum of the formal sum for smooth varieties is explicit and the kernel of the spectrum is the HKR decomposition; the weight of the decomposition is the Hodge numbers; and the dimension of trace homology is the cohomological dimension. (HKR weight-spectrum decomposition of trace homology)

**中文。** 定义：迹同调：范畴的迹型不变量（Hochschild 同调）。猜想：光滑簇的形式和权谱显式且谱的核为 HKR 分解；分解的权为 Hodge 数；迹同调的维数为上同调维数。（迹同调 HKR 权谱分解）
