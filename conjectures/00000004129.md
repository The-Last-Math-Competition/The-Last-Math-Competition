**FA2-09 ★** — source: 123_高等数理逻辑_强制法与公理.md

**English.** Definition: The density of a complete Boolean algebra is the cardinality of its smallest dense subset. Conjecture: Density and chain length satisfy an explicit conversion inequality, and the constant in the inequality is optimized by the Cohen algebra. (density-chain conversion of complete Boolean algebras)

**中文。** 定义：完备布尔代数的密度指最小稠密集的基数。猜想：密度与链长满足显式换算不等式,且该不等式的常数由 Cohen 代数达到最优。（完备布尔密度-链换算）
