**HT2-15 ★** — source: 126_组合代数拓扑_高阶树.md

**English.** Definition: The higher Dirichlet-Neumann operator is the boundary response operator on k-chains. Conjecture: The multiplicity of its zero eigenvalue equals exactly the count of k-spanning trees of the boundary, and the multiplicity is one if and only if the boundary is a sphere triangulation. (higher DN zero-multiplicity identity)

**中文。** 定义：高阶 Dirichlet–Neumann 算子指 k-链边界响应算子。猜想：其零特征值重数恰等于边界 k-生成树的计数,重数为一当且仅当边界为球面剖分。（高阶 DN 零重数恒等）
