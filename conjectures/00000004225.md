**English.** Definition: high order Dirichlet–Neumann operator denotes k- boundary operator. Conjecture: its zero eigenvalue multiplicity equals counting, multiplicity of exactly boundary k- spanning tree is one if and only if boundary is ball plane triangulation. (high order DN zero multiplicity identical)
