# 00000001714

**English.** Definition: The stable attracting domains of ergodic sums are the stable limit types of heavy-tailed functions. Conjecture: The family of realizable stable exponents θ ∈ (0,2) is completely characterized by the Fourier coefficient decay of α: θ = min(1, β) with β the optimal Hölder exponent of f; the realizable set is (0,1) ∪ {2}.

**中文。** 定义：遍历和的稳定吸引域指重尾函数的稳定极限类型。猜想：可实现的稳定指数 θ ∈ (0,2) 的族由 α 的 Fourier 系数衰减完全刻画:θ = min(1, β),β 为 f 的最优 Hölder 指数;可实现集为 (0,1)∪\{2\}。
