**English.** Definition: Macdonald process denotes process of Macdonald compatible polynomial family construction. Conjecture: covariance of its q-Whittaker limit is Plancherel type explicit functional, at covariance degenerate is one dimension universal. (Macdonald covariance degenerate)

**中文。** 定义：Macdonald 过程指以 Macdonald 相容多项式族构造的过程。猜想：其 q-Whittaker 极限的涨落协方差为 Plancherel 型显式泛函,领头粒子处协方差退化为一维普适律。（Macdonald 协方差退化律）
