**IP2-06 ◆** — source: 133_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: A Macdonald process is the process constructed from Macdonald-compatible polynomial families. Conjecture: The fluctuation covariance of its q-Whittaker limit is an explicit Plancherel-type functional, and at the leading particle the covariance degenerates to a one-dimensional universal law. (Macdonald covariance degeneration law)

**中文。** 定义：Macdonald 过程指以 Macdonald 相容多项式族构造的过程。猜想：其 q-Whittaker 极限的涨落协方差为 Plancherel 型显式泛函,领头粒子处协方差退化为一维普适律。（Macdonald 协方差退化律）
