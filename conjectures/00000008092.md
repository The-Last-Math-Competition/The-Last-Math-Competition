# 00000008092

**FUS-12 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The marked equivalence of the Burnside ring of a fusion: the spectrum ker_F of the kernel of the mark homomorphism mu_F: A(F) to the product of A_F(P) (marks over F-closed subgroups). Conjecture: The minimal number of generators of the ideal structure of ker_F equals the number of minimal chains of the fusion (a chain-ideal correspondence); the prime spectrum of the Burnside ring corresponds one-to-one with the F-essential subgroups (a prime-essential duality); and Dress decomposability (the separation of connectedness of the prime spectrum) is preserved in fusion systems (a Dress fusion preservation law), the preservation constant being the dimension of the automorphic quotient of the fusion (the Dress constant). (Burnside mark chain-ideal prime duality)

**中文。** 定义：融合的 Burnside 环的标记等价：F 的 mark 同态 μ_F: A(F)→∏ A_F(P)（在 F-闭子群上的标记）的核的谱 ker_F。猜想：ker_F 的理想结构的极小生成元数为 F 的异类的极小链数（链-理想对应）；且 Burnside 环的素谱与 F 的"F-本质"子群一一对应（素-本质对偶）；Dress 的可分解性（素谱的连通性的分离）在融合系统中保持（Dress 融合保持律），保持的常数为融合的自守商的维数（Dress 常数）。
