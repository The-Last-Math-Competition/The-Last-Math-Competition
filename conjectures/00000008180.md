# 00000008180

**English.** Definition: The computational complexity of the modular polynomials in the Schoof-Elkies-Atkin algorithm: the complexity sea_c(l) of the dominant term (evaluation of the modular polynomial Phi_l) of SEA point counting. Conjecture: sea_c(l) = O(l^{3+epsilon}) (a cubic dominance law); the speedup factor of the replacement of the modular polynomial (fast evaluation of the isogeny graph of Atkin's X_0(l)) is l/log l (an l-over-log speedup law); and the predictive model of the actual running time of SEA (for large primes q of order 2^{256}) is the explicit l^2 M(log q)(1+o(1)) (an l-squared model), with model error below 5% (model tightness). (SEA cubic dominance l-squared model)

**中文。** 定义：Schoof–Elkies–Atkin 算法的模多项式的计算复杂度：SEA 的点计数的主导项（模多项式 Φ_l 的求值）的复杂度 sea_c(l)。猜想：sea_c(l) = O(l^{3+ε})（三次主导律）；且模多项式的替代（Atkin 的 X_0(l) 的同源图的快速求值）的加速因子为 l/log l（l/log 加速律）；SEA 的实际（大素数 q ≈ 2^{256}）的运行时间的预测模型为 l²·M(log q)·(1+o(1)) 的显式（l 平方模型），模型的误差 < 5%（模型紧性）。
