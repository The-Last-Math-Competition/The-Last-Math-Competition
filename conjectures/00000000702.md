# 00000000702

**English.** Definition: The Browkin expansion is a continued-fraction-like expansion in the p-adics. Conjecture: The Browkin expansion of an algebraic number is never eventually periodic; and the evidence for non-periodicity is the unboundedness of its partial quotients. (p-adic algebraic non-periodicity)

**中文。** 定义：Browkin 展开为 p 进连分数的类连分数展开。猜想：代数数的 Browkin 展开为非终周期；且非周期的证据为部分商的无界性。（p 进代数非周期）
