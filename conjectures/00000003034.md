# 00000003034

**English.** Definition: Renormalization of the localization edge: the flow of disorder and interactions. Conjecture: The localization-edge flow: the direction of the flow near the particle-hole symmetric point is decided explicitly by the sign of the interaction, and the fixed points of the flow are the critical points of the renormalization group. (localization edge RG flow direction)

**中文。** 定义：局域化边的重整化：无序相互作用的流。猜想：局域化边流：粒子-空穴对称点附近的流的方向由相互作用的符号显式决定且流的不动点为重整化群的临界点。（局域化边 RG 流向）
