# 00000005650

**English.** Definition: The center depth and the subdivision structure are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two point sets with identical center depths but different subdivision structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of shear transformations. (same depth-different subdivision shear separation)

**中文。** 定义：中心深度与剖分结构指两层。猜想：存在两点集的一切中心深度相同而剖分结构不同,分离由剪切变换的显式对实现。（深度同-剖分异剪切分离）
