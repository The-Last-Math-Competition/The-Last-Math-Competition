**English.** Definition: matching constant γ_p denotes limit of Power-of-p weight under most small perfect matching. Conjecture: γ_p with respect to p strictly decreasing and convex, limit converges to explicit constant, convexity is two order stability of equivalent to Parisi functional. (γ_p decreasing convexity and limit)

**中文。** 定义：匹配常数 γ_p 指 Power-of-p 权重下最小完美匹配的极限。猜想：γ_p 关于 p 严格递减且凸,极限收敛于显式常数,凸性等价于 Parisi 泛函的二阶稳定性。（γ_p 递减凸性与极限）
