# 00000004516

**English.** Definition: The matching constant gamma_p is the limit of the minimum perfect matching under power-of-p weights. Conjecture: gamma_p is strictly decreasing and convex in p, the limit converges to an explicit constant, and the convexity is equivalent to the second-order stability of the Parisi functional. (monotone convexity and limit of gamma_p)

**中文。** 定义：匹配常数 γ_p 指 Power-of-p 权重下最小完美匹配的极限。猜想：γ_p 关于 p 严格递减且凸,极限收敛于显式常数,凸性等价于 Parisi 泛函的二阶稳定性。（γ_p 递减凸性与极限）
