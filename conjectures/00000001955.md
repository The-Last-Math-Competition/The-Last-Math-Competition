# 00000001955

**LG-25 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: Building lattices act on affine buildings over local fields. Conjecture: The minimal covolume of a type ~A₂ building lattice is explicit via a local-ramification Euler product (the product being an integer multiple of ∏ℓ_v(Q(√−3))); the minimum is attained by the Cartwright–Steger lattice.

**中文。** 定义：建筑格指在局部域的仿射建筑上作用的格。猜想：类型 ~A₂ 的建筑格的最小协体积由局部分歧的 Euler 积显式(积为 ∏\ell_v(Q(√−3)) 的整倍);最小者由 Cartwright–Steger 格取得。
