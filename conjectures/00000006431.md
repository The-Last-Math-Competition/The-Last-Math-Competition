# 00000006431

**MF4-01 ◆** — source: 200_模形式III_Maass形.md

**English.** Definition: Maass forms are nonholomorphic automorphic forms. Conjecture: The first eigenvalue of the modular surface is a known numerical value and the infimum over arithmetic surfaces is separated from the exceptional value, the kernel of the separation being the potential window. (arithmetic separation window of the modular first eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：Maass 形式为非全纯的自守形式。猜想：模面的第一特征值为已知数值且算术面的下确界与例外值分离；且分离的核为位势窗。（模面首值算术分离窗）
