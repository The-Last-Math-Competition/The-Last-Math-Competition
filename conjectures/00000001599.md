# 00000001599

**IS-29 ★** — source: 38_可积系统与精确可解模型.md

**English.** Definition: Spectral curves of the elliptic Calogero–Moser system. Conjecture: The count of torsion points in the Abel–Jacobi image of the spectral curve equals the torsion points of the elliptic curve times the root-system dual (a spectral torsion-point count).

**中文。** 定义：椭圆 Calogero–Moser 的谱曲线。猜想：谱曲线的 Abel–Jacobi 像的挠点计数 = 椭圆曲线挠点 × 根系对偶(谱挠点计数)。
