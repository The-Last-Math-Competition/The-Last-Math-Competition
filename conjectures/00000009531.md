# 00000009531

**QIC-11 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: The Belavkin-Staszewski divergence D_BS(rho||sigma) = -log tr(sigma sigma^{-1/2} rho sigma^{-1/2}) is the arithmetic-geometric mean variant of the Petz convex divergence, with D_BS at least D_Petz always. Conjecture: The gap D_BS - D_Petz at fixed spectral gap delta has maximum log(1 + delta^2/4), attained by mixtures of two-dimensional noncommuting projections, with extremal rigidity holding only in the qubit case. (BS divergence extremum)

**中文。** 定义：Belavkin–Staszewski 散度 D_BS(ρ‖σ)=−log tr(σ·σ^{−1/2}ρσ^{−1/2}) 是 Petz 凹散度的算术-几何平均变体，恒有 D_BS≥D_Petz。猜想：差距 D_BS−D_Petz 在谱间隙 δ 固定时的最大值为 log(1+δ²/4)，达到最大值的态对为二维非交换投影的混合，极值刚性仅在 qubit 情形成立。（BS 散度极值）
