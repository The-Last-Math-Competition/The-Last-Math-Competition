# 00000004346

**FP2-16 ◆** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: The T_2 transport inequality W_2(nu, gamma)^2 <= 2t*D(nu||gamma) and the logarithmic Sobolev inequality D(nu||gamma) <= (1/2c)*I(nu||gamma), with optimal constants. Conjecture: For Gaussian measure the two inequalities imply each other and the conversion ratio in both directions is 2; the ratio 2 is attained by the standard Gaussian and cannot be improved; on nonconvex potentials T_2 can hold while LSI fails, and the smallest failing example is a heavy-tailed double-exponential-type measure. (optimal conversion ratio 2 between T_2 and LSI)

**中文。** 定义：T₂ 传输不等式 W₂(ν,γ)² ≤ 2t·D(ν‖γ) 与对数 Sobolev 不等式 D(ν‖γ) ≤ (1/2c)·I(ν‖γ) 的最优常数关系。猜想：高斯测度上两个不等式相互蕴含且两个方向的换算比均为 2；比 2 由标准高斯达到而不可改进；非凸位势上 T₂ 可成立而 LSI 失效且最小失效例子为重尾二重指数型测度。（T₂-LSI 换算比 2 最优）
