**SP-06 ◆** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: Anderson localization is the pure point spectrum with exponentially decaying eigenfunctions of Schrödinger operators with random potentials. Conjecture: The two-dimensional Bernoulli-potential lattice Schrödinger operator localizes at all energies; minimal-dimension obstruction: any proof of extended states must, in dimension ≥ 3, use resonance chains of the multiscale analysis — in two dimensions the geometric obstruction makes the chain length diverge.

**中文。** 定义：Anderson 局域化指随机位势 Schrödinger 算子的纯点谱与指数衰减特征函数。猜想：二维 Bernoulli 位势格点 Schrödinger 算子对一切能量局域化;最小维度障碍:任何扩展态存在的证明必须在维度 ≥3 时使用多尺度展开的共振链,二维的几何禁阻使该链长度发散。
