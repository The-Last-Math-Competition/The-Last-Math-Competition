# 00000005844

**MR3-14 ★** — source: 180_模表示II.md

**English.** Definition: The covering constant is the weighted structure of block covering. Conjecture: The character extension degree is given by the defect ratio of the covering pair, the combination of the ratio being the subgroup-order ratio; the extension degree divides the index ratio, with quotient 1 if and only if the covering is equi-defective; and the rational values of the defect ratio are powers of p, with bounded exponent differences. (covering extension defect-ratio subgroup-order ratio)

**中文。** 定义：覆盖常数：块的覆盖（子群块被母群块覆盖）的加权结构。猜想：特征延伸次数由覆盖对的亏比给出且亏比的组合为子群阶比；延伸次数整除指标比且整除的商为 1 当且仅当覆盖为等亏；亏比的有理值为 p 的幂且幂差有界。（覆盖延伸亏比子群阶比）
