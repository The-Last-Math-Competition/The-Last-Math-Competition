**English.** Definition: The random transverse field Ising model: power-law dynamics of Griffiths phases. Conjecture: The dynamical exponent z diverges with disorder (a divergence law); the explicit form of z(W) is a logarithmic integral (an explicitness law); the gap distribution in the ordered phase has a power-law tail (a power-law tail law); and the upper bound on z in three dimensions is finite (an upper bound law). (Griffiths dynamical exponent divergence)

**中文。** 定义：随机横向场 Ising 模型： Griffiths 相的幂律动力学。猜想：动力学指数 z 随无序发散（发散律）；且 z(W) 的显式为对数积分（显式律）；有序相的隙分布为幂律尾（幂律律）；三维的 z 的上界有限（上界律）。
