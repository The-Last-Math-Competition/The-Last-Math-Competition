# 00000004060

**AR3-30 △** — source: 120_代数表示论III_AuslanderReiten.md

**English.** Definition: The 2-limit of stable categories is the bilimit of a sequence of algebras under exact functors. Conjecture: There exists a sequence of algebras whose stable 2-limit is not isomorphic to the stable category of any algebra, and the smallest algebra dimension exhibiting the phenomenon is 5. (minimal exception of stable 2-limits)

**中文。** 定义：稳定范畴的 2-极限指代数列在正合函子下的双极限。猜想：存在代数列的稳定 2-极限不同构于任何代数的稳定范畴,出现该现象的最小代数维数为 5。（稳定 2-极限最小例外）
