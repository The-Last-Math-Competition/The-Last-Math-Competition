# 00000002770

**English.** Definition: The PI-zation of Kostkin sequences: standard monomials of alternated polynomials. Conjecture: Linear independence of standard monomials: the maximal linearly independent length of Kostkin sequences over matrix algebras is an explicit combinatorial function of the matrix size, given by the hook-length formula of partitions. (Kostkin maximal independent length)

**中文。** 定义：Kostkin 序列的 PI 化：交错化多项式的标准单项式。猜想：标准单项式的线性无关性：矩阵代数上 Kostkin 序列的极大线性无关长度为矩阵规模的显式组合函数且函数由分拆的钩长公式给出。（Kostkin 极大无关长度）
