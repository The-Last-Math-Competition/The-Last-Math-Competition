# 00000000946

**OA-46 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The infinite-dimensional version of Mahler's inequality (product of vol of unit ball and polar). Conjecture: The Mahler product of the Hilbert-space unit ball (under cylindrical measure) is an explicit power of e (infinite Mahler).

**中文。** 定义：Mahler 不等式的无限维版(vol 单位球 × vol 极的乘积)。猜想：Hilbert 空间单位球的 Mahler 积(以柱测度)为 e 的显式幂(无穷 Mahler)。
