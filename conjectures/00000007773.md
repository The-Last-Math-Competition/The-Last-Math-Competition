# 00000007773

**ADY-23 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Periodic density of endomorphisms of abelian varieties: for an abelian variety A over a number field with an endomorphism phi of minimal degree at least 2, the periodic set Per(phi) is an inverse-limit structure in A(Qbar). Conjecture: The prime-order distribution of the torsion part of Per(phi) is uniform; and Per(phi) intersect A(K) is finite with cardinality given by an explicit exponential formula in (deg phi)^r, where r is the quotient of the Mordell-Weil rank of K by the least common multiple of the map slopes. (finite periodic intersection formula)

**中文。** 定义：阿贝尔簇自同射的周期密度：设 A 为定义在数域的阿贝尔簇，φ 为其上的极小次数 ≥2 自同射。周期点 Per(φ) 在 A(Qbar) 中为逆极限结构。猜想：Per(φ) 的 torsion 部分的素阶分布均匀；且 Per(φ)∩A(K) 为有限集，其基数为 (deg φ)^{r} 的显式指数公式，其中 r 为 K 的 Mordell-Weil 秩与映射斜率的最小公倍数之商。
