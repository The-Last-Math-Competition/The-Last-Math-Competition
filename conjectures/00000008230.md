# 00000008230

**English.** Definition: The Selberg type transform of Adams operations on characters: the spectral realization adams_k of the Adams operation psi^k on characters. Conjecture: The support of the spectral measure of adams_k (the Adams prime image) is scaled by the factor k (a scaling law); the basis of fixed points of Adams (class functions fixed by psi^k) is the k-regular classes (a k-regular basis law); the dimension of the fixed subspace equals the number of k-regular classes (a regular count law); and the composition law of Adams has sign correction the Stiefel correction (-1)^{k-1} (k-1)/2 (a Stiefel correction formula). (Adams k-regular basis Stiefel correction)

**中文。** 定义：表示的 Adams 操作的 Selberg 型变换：特征标的 Adams 操作 ψ^k 的谱实现 adams_k。猜想：adams_k（特征标的 Adams 素像）的谱测度的支撑缩放 k 倍（缩放律）；且 Adams 的不动点（ψ^k 固定的类函数）的基为 k-正则类（k-正则基律）；不动的维数（不动子空间的维数）= k-正则类数（正则计数律）；Adams 的复合律的符号修正为 (-1)^{k-1}·(k-1)/2 的 Stiefel 修正（Stiefel 修正公式）。
