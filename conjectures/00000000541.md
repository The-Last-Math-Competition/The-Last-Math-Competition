**CA-41 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: Constructible-layer shellability is a combinatorial regularity of complexes. Conjecture: Shellability of the independence complex of a bipartite graph is equivalent to the chordality of the graph; and the criterion is complete over the class of chordal bipartite graphs. (independence complex shellability criterion)

**中文。** 定义：可构分层 shellability 为复形的组合正则性。猜想：二部图的独立复形的 shellability 等价于图的 chordality；且判据在弦二部图类完备。（独立复形 shellable 判据）
