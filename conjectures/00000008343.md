# 00000008343

**English.** Definition: Zaremba: the reachable domain zar_dom of denominators 2^k whose reduced fractions admit continued fraction expansions with complete quotients restricted to {1,2,3,4,5}. Conjecture: zar_dom covers all denominators of the form 2^k j; shrinking the restriction set to {1,2,3,4} leaves denominators of positive density uncovered; the restriction {1,2,3} covers only a zero-density subset; and the Bourgain-Kontorovich 2/3 density result can be strengthened to full coverage, the proof path reducing to uniform L^p spectral gap lower bounds for exponential sums with the gap constant uniform and explicit in k. (Zaremba full coverage spectral gap)

**中文。** 定义：Zaremba。猜想：分母为 2^k 的既约分数的连分数展开中完全商限制在 {1,2,3,4,5} 内的可达域 zar_dom。猜想：zar_dom 覆盖一切形如 2^k·j 的分母；限制集缩为 {1,2,3,4} 后不可覆盖的分母具有正密度；限制集 {1,2,3} 仅覆盖零密度子集；Bourgain–Kontorovich 的 2/3 密度结果可强化为全覆盖，证明路径归结为指数和的均匀 L^p 谱隙下界且谱隙常数对 k 一致而显式。
