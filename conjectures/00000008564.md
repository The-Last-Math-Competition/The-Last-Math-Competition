# 00000008564

**NSP-04 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: CMV matrices: the five-diagonal canonical representation of one-sided infinite unitary operators, completely encoded by Verblunsky coefficients alpha_n. Conjecture: Verblunsky coefficients and spectral measures are in bijection (the Killip-Simon bijection law); the Lipschitz constant of the spectral measure under small perturbations of the coefficients is controlled by the sum of |alpha_n| (an alpha-sum control law); at the critical case where alpha_n tends to unit modulus the spectral measure develops singularities (a critical singularity law); and the mass distribution of the singularities is of Cantor type (a Cantor mass law). (CMV Killip-Simon Cantor mass law)

**中文。** 定义：CMV 矩阵：单边无限酉算子的五对角典范表示，由 Verblunsky 系数 α_n 完全编码。猜想：Verblunsky 系数与谱测度之间为一一对应（Killip–Simon 双射律）；且系数小扰动的谱测度的 Lipschitz 常数受控于 Σ|α_n|（α 和控制律）；α_n 趋于单位模的临界情形的谱测度产生奇点（临界奇点律）；奇点的质量分布为 Cantor 型（Cantor 质量律）。
