# 00000009901

**English.** Definition: Covering theory: faithfulness of universal covers of locally bounded algebras. Conjecture: Existence of faithful covers is given by a local finiteness criterion of the interacting group action, computable in polynomial time from combinatorial quantities of the covering graph; and faithful covers are unique up to isomorphism. (covering faithfulness combinatorial criterion)

**中文。** 定义：覆盖理论：局部有界代数的普遍覆盖的忠实性。猜想：忠实覆盖的存在性由交互群作用的局部有限性判据给出且判据为覆盖图的组合量的多项式时间计算；忠实覆盖在同构意义下唯一。（覆盖忠实组合判据）
