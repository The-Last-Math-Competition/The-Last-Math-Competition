# 00000004451

**English.** Definition: A scattering diagram is the wall structure with attached functions on the tangent polytope, and wall crossing is the coordinate transformation across walls. Conjecture: For the five-vertex case the structure functions are explicit exponential-type formulas, generated order by order from the commutators of the initial walls and closed at third-order truncation. (third-order closure of five-vertex scattering)

**中文。** 定义：散射图指切多面体上的墙结构与附着函数,墙穿越指跨墙的坐标变换。猜想：五顶点情形的结构函数为指数型显式公式,由初墙的交换子逐阶生成且在三阶截断封闭。（五顶点散射三阶封闭）
