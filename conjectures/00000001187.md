# 00000001187

**LT-87 △** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The Springer concordance classes of an algebraic group G are the merged classes of the fibers of the Springer correspondence on unipotent orbits. Conjecture: The number of concordance classes equals the number of unipotent orbits of G if and only if all Springer fibers of the Weyl group of G are irreducible; and the exceptions are exactly the very-even orbits of even Sp and SO types, one each.

**中文。** 定义：代数群 G 的 Springer 通约等价类指幺幂轨道在 Springer 对应下的纤维的合并类。猜想：通约等价类个数恰等于 G 的幺幂轨道个数当且仅当 G 的 Weyl 群的所有 Springer 纤维不可约;且例外恰为偶 Sp 与 SO 型的十分裂轨道各一次。
