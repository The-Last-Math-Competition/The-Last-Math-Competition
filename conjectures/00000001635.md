# 00000001635

**English.** Definition: The singularity category of the weighted projective line is the quotient of its derived category by the vector-bundle subcategory. Conjecture: The equivalence classes of singularity categories of all weight types (a,b,c) form an explicit cross table: two are equivalent if and only if the triples agree after permutation and coprime reduction; the cross table is explicitly indexed by the elementary divisors of the triangle groups (a,b,c).

**中文。** 定义：加权射影直线的奇性范畴指其导出范畴对向量子范畴的商。猜想：一切权型 (a,b,c) 的奇性范畴两两间的等价类构成显式交叉表:等价当且仅当三元组经置换与互素化后相同;交叉表由三角形群 (a,b,c) 的初等因子显式索引。
