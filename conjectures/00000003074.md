# 00000003074

**English.** Definition: ∞-topoi: ∞-categories satisfying descent. Conjecture: The boundary of hypercompletion: the hypercompletion of an ∞-topos is the rectification of shape invariants, and the complement of the rectification is witnessed by nontrivial holes of Eilenberg–Mac Lane sheaves. (hypercompletion rectification boundary witness)

**中文。** 定义：∞-拓扑斯（∞-topos）：满足 descent 的 ∞-范畴。猜想：脱朗（hypercompletion）的边界：无穷拓扑斯的超完备化为形状不变的整流且整流的补为由 Eilenberg–Mac Lane 层的非平凡洞见证。（超完备整流边界见证）
