# 00000002368

**English.** Definition: The Arveson extension is the completely positive extension of CP maps. Conjecture: The minimal ambient dimension is the product of boundary representations; and the compression of the product is the minimal boundary. (Arveson minimal ambient dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Arveson 延拓为 CP 映射的完全正延拓。猜想：最小环境维数为边界表示的乘积；且乘积的压缩为极小边界。（Arveson 最小环境维数）
