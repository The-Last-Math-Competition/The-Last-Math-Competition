# 00000005522

**English.** Definition: The heavy-traffic regime is the diffusion-limit regime as the load approaches one. Conjecture: The variance parameter is always a linear expression of the second service moment and the arrival intensity, with the two-station coefficient table explicit; the intercept of the linear expression is the residual service variance; and the stationary distribution of the diffusion is two-dimensional Ornstein-Uhlenbeck type for two stations, with spectral gap twice the service rate difference. (second-moment linear expression of heavy traffic)

**中文。** 定义：重话务体制：负载趋近一时的扩散极限体制。猜想：方差参数恒为服务二阶矩与到达强度的线性式且两站情形的系数表为显式；线性式的截距为剩余服务方差；扩散过程的平稳分布在两站时为二维 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 型且谱隙为服务率差的二倍。（重话务二阶矩线性式）
