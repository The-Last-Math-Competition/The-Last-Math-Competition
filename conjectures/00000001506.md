**English.** Definition: The Lyapunov spectrum of a translation surface is the exponent spectrum of the cocycle. Conjecture: The Lyapunov spectra of all strata are simple; the only remaining unchecked cases involve high-dimensional strata with nontrivial hyperelliptic pairs. (complete verification of Lyapunov spectrum simplicity)
