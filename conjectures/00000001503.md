# 00000001503

**HY-23 ★** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The periodic data of a hyperbolic toral automorphism is the characteristic polynomial of its induced matrix. Conjecture: The minimal regularity r* for C^r conjugacy of two hyperbolic toral automorphisms is given explicitly by the coincidence height of the periodic data: r* = 1 if and only if the spectral ratios are non-units (in Levy's sense), and r* = ∞ exactly when the eigenvalues are Pisot numbers.

**中文。** 定义：双曲环面自同构的周期数据指其诱导矩阵的特征多项式。猜想：两个双曲环面自同构 C^r-共轭的最小正则性 r* 由周期数据的重合高度显式给出:r*=1 当且仅当谱比非单位(Levy 意义),r*=∞ 恰在特征值为 Pisot 时。
