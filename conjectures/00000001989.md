# 00000001989

**MP-29 ★** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: The Kubo–Mori state is the KMS equilibrium state of a quantum spin system. Conjecture: The existence-uniqueness of KM states in 1D extends to higher dimensions: all equilibrium KMS states in d > 1 have a unique thermodynamic limit; the extension goes from Araki's d = 1 construction via cluster expansions.

**中文。** 定义：Kubo–Mori 态指量子自旋系统的 KMS 平衡态。猜想：1D 的 KM 态的存在唯一推广到高维:d>1 的一切平衡 KMS 态有唯一的热力学极限;推广由 Araki 的 d=1 构造经簇展开。
