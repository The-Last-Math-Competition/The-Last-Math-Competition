# 00000001972

**English.** Definition: The minimal model M(p,p+1) is the unitary CFT of central charge c = 1−6/(p(p+1)). Conjecture: The rigorous unitary construction of its fusion category has a uniform bound: for arbitrary p, the operator norms of the construction are at most C (a bound depending only on an upper bound for c); the bound follows from the uniformity of Wess–Zumino vertices.

**中文。** 定义：极小模型 M(p,p+1) 指中心荷 c=1−6/(p(p+1)) 的酉 CFT。猜想：融合范畴的严格酉构造的 uniform 界:p 任意时构造的算子范数 ≤C(仅依赖 c 的上界);界由 Wess–Zumino 顶点的一致性给出。
