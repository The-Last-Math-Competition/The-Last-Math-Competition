# 00000003468

**English.** Definition: Complete n-arcs in the projective plane PG(2,q) are non-extendable point sets, whose maximal sizes and exceptional configurations are core problems of finite geometry. Conjecture: Beyond the hyperelliptic (q+2)-arcs the maximal complete arc size is asymptotically q−c·q^{2/3}; the complete list of projective exceptional arcs closes at q ≤ 25 with no larger exceptions. (projective complete arc asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：射影平面 PG(2,q) 中完全 n-弧为不能扩张的点集，其最大规模与例外构形是有限几何核心问题。猜想：超椭圆 (q+2)-弧之外的最大完全弧规模渐近为 q−c·q^{2/3}；射影例外弧的完全列表在 q≤25 封闭且不存在更大例外。（射影完全弧渐近）
