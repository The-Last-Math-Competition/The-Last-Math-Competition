# 00000002619

**English.** Definition: Euler posets: the symmetry of rank generating functions. Conjecture: Rank symmetry of Euler posets is characterized by Dehn–Sommerville type relations; the number of independent variables of the relations is the Euler genus of the poset, given by the homological dimension of maximal chains. (Euler rank symmetry genus)

**中文。** 定义：Euler 偏序集：秩生成函数的对称性。猜想：Euler 偏序的秩对称由 Dehn–Sommerville 型关系刻画；关系的独立变量个数为偏序的 Euler 亏格且亏格由极大链的同调维数给出。（Euler 秩对称亏格）
