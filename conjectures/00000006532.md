# 00000006532

**English.** Definition: The equivariant index with the correction for non-isolated fixed points. Conjecture: The correction order is always explicit and the combination of the correction is the stratification of singular varieties; the combination is compatible with the stratum dimension, with compatibility difference 1; and the main term of the correction is the localization of equivariant characteristic classes. (non-isolated correction-order structure of the equivariant index)

**中文。** 定义：等变指标：非孤立不动点的修正。猜想：修正阶恒显式且修正的组合为奇异簇的分层；修正的组合与分层维数相容且相容差为 1；修正的主项为等变示性类的局部化。（等变非孤立修正阶结构）
