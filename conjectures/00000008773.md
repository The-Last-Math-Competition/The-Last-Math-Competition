# 00000008773

**NLA-03 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: CUR decomposition: low-rank approximation by column-row sampling. Conjecture: The gap between the expected CUR error and the SVD is of leverage type (1 + sqrt(k+1)/sqrt(n-k))^{1/2} (a leverage law); the optimal sampling distribution for leverage scores is exact leverage (an exact leverage law); the correction for integerized sampling is 1 + epsilon (an epsilon correction law); and the worst instances of the correction are heavy-tailed leverage (a heavy-tail law). (CUR leverage exact heavy-tail correction)

**中文。** 定义：CUR 分解：列-行抽样的低秩逼近。猜想：CUR 的期望误差与 SVD 的间隙为 (1+√(k+1)/√(n-k))^{1/2} 型杠杆律（杠杆律）；且杠杆得分的抽样的最优分布为精确杠杆（精确杠杆律）；整化（整数抽样的）的修正为 1+ε（ε 修正律）；修正的最坏实例为重尾杠杆（重尾律）。
