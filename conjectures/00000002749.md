# 00000002749

**PI-09 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: Prime decomposition of T-ideals: the semantic layer of PI algebras. Conjecture: The prime-ideal lattice of T-ideals: the prime spectrum of the T-ideal of M_n is a chain of length the number of prime factors of n; the completeness of the lattice follows from the monotonicity of the Kemer function. (T-ideal prime spectrum chain)

**中文。** 定义：T-理想的素分解：PI 代数的语义层。猜想：T-理想的素理想格：M_n 的 T-理想的素谱为链且链长为 n 的素因子数；格的完备性由 Kemer 函数的单调性给出。（T-理想素谱链）
