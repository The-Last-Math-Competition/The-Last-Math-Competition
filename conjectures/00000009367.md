# 00000009367

**English.** Definition: Exchangeable extremes: extremal indices. Conjecture: Extremal indices: the extremes of exchangeable sequences, with the index of the extremes the tail index. (exchangeable tail index extremal sequence)

**中文。** 定义：可交换的极值：极值指标。猜想：极值指标：可交换序列的极值且值的指标为尾指标。（可交换极值尾指标可交换序列极值指标值）
