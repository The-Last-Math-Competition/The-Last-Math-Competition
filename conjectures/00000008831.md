**English.** Definition: Douglas–Rachford iteration is intersection composition of two reflection. Conjecture: DR sequence of general monotone operator always converges weakly; strong Lipschitz when convergent is linear and rate by step size product controls; Borwein ring counterexample by parameter α less than one. (DR ring counterexample)

**中文。** 定义：Douglas–Rachford 迭代为两反射的交替复合。猜想：一般单调算子的 DR 序列恒弱收敛；强单调加 Lipschitz 时收敛为线性且速率由反称步长积控制；Borwein 型循环反例由弛豫参数 α 小于一修复。（DR 弛豫修复循环反例）
