# 00000008831

**English.** Definition: Douglas-Rachford iteration: the alternating composition of two reflections. Conjecture: DR sequences for general monotone operators always converge weakly; with strong monotonicity plus Lipschitzness the convergence is linear with rate controlled by the antisymmetric step-size product; and Borwein-type cyclic counterexamples are repaired by relaxation parameters alpha below one. (DR relaxation cyclic counterexample repair)

**中文。** 定义：Douglas–Rachford 迭代为两反射的交替复合。猜想：一般单调算子的 DR 序列恒弱收敛；强单调加 Lipschitz 时收敛为线性且速率由反称步长积控制；Borwein 型循环反例由弛豫参数 α 小于一修复。（DR 弛豫修复循环反例）
