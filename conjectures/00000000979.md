# 00000000979

**English.** Definition: The essential spectrum of an almost-normal operator is stable under finite perturbations. Conjecture: Every component of the complement of the essential spectrum of an almost-normal operator contains either point spectrum or residual spectrum (a dichotomy); and the exception to the dichotomy rigidity is Fredholm with zero index. (spectral dichotomy rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：几乎正常算子的本质谱为有限摄动下的稳定量。猜想：几乎正常算子的本质谱的补集的每个分支含点谱或剩余谱之一；且二分刚性的例外为 Fredholm 零指标。（谱二分刚性）
