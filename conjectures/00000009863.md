**English.** Definition: Multi-species ASEP: stationary states and dynamics of multi-labeled particles. Conjecture: The stationary measure of multi-species ASEP has a universal matrix-product representation whose algebra is a degenerate limit of the Hecke algebra; the representation recurs over Catalan-type tree structures as species counts grow. (multispecies matrix-product universality)

**中文。** 定义：多物种 ASEP：粒子的多种类标记的稳态与动力学。猜想：多物种 ASEP 的稳态测度有普适矩阵积表示且表示的代数为 Hecke 代数的退化极限；表示在物种数增大时按 Catalan 型树结构递归。（多物种矩阵积普适）
