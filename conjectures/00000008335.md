# 00000008335

**DIA-15 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The explicit construction of algebraic independence: the proof path ai_path for the algebraic independence of the pair (e, pi). Conjecture: The equivalent proof framework of ai_path is the symmetry breaking of the Lindemann-Weierstrass group (a symmetry breaking framework law); the provable weaker transcendence base (degree at least 2) of the group (Gamma(1/4), Gamma(3/4), pi) (a provable base law); the next strengthening of Nesterenko's bound (degree at least 3 for pi, e^pi, Gamma(1/4)) extends to degree at least 4 over the field extended by e^e (an e^e extension law); and the number of algebraic independence candidates for the Chowla-Selberg values of the CM-domain modular functions is at least 2 (a two-candidate law). (e-pi symmetry breaking Nesterenko e^e extension)

**中文。** 定义：代数无关的显式构造：值的组 (e, π) 的代数无关的证明路径 ai_path。猜想：ai_path（e 与 π 的代数无关）的等价证明框架为 Lindemann–Weierstrass 的群对称破缺（对称破缺框架律）；且更弱的组 (Γ(1/4), Γ(3/4), π) 的超越基（超越次数 ≥ 2）的可证（可证基律）；Nesterenko 的界（π, e^π, Γ(1/4) 的次数 ≥ 3）的强化的下一步（次数 ≥ 4）的扩域为 e^{e}（e^e 扩域律）；扩域的模（CM 域的模函数）的 Chowla–Selberg 的值的代数无关的候选数 ≥ 2（二候选律）。
