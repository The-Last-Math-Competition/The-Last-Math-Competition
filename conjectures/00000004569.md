**English.** Definition: A Bessel moment is the integral moment of the product of a Bessel function and a power. Conjecture: Its transcendence criterion is an explicit coprimality condition on the moment indices, the condition is complete, and the decision threshold is given explicitly by the density. (coprimality transcendence criterion of Bessel moments)

**中文。** 定义：贝塞尔矩指贝塞尔函数与幂乘积的积分矩。猜想：其超越判据为矩指标的互素性显式条件,条件完全且判别阈值由密度显式给出。（贝塞尔矩互素超越判据）
