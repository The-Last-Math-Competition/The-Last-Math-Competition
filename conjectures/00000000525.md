# 00000000525

**English.** Definition: The F-pure threshold fpt(f) (the characteristic-p logarithmic threshold). Conjecture: The limit of fpt(x²+y³) as p → ∞ equals its characteristic-zero log threshold, and for p ≡ 2 mod 3, fpt = 5/6 (an explicit interpolation formula).
