# 00000008520

**English.** Definition: Vanishing theorems are algebraic constraints on solutions of differential equations. Conjecture: Equations with rigid differential Galois groups have global solutions on compact Riemann surfaces that are algebraic functions; their algebraic degree is controlled by the simplicity of the group. (algebraicity of rigid global solutions)

**中文。** 定义：消没定理指微分方程解的代数约束。猜想：具有刚性微分 Galois 群的方程在紧黎曼面上的整体解为代数函数；其代数度由群的单性控制。（刚性整体解代数性）
