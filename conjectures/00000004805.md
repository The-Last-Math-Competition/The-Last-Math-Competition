# 00000004805

**English.** Definition: A reflexive polytope is a dual polytope all of whose vertices are integral. Conjecture: The counting formula of four-dimensional toric Fanos has the structure of a root-system orbit product, with explicit factors and total count consistent with the known values. (orbit-product structure of reflexive polytopes)

**中文。** 定义：反射多面体指极点全整的对偶多面体。猜想：四维环面 Fano 的计数公式结构为根系统轨道积,因子显式且总数与已知值一致。（反射多面体轨道积结构）
