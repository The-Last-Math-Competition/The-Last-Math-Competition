**GBR-19 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: The tame-wild dichotomy: the moduli space structure of tame algebras after Drozd's theorem. Conjecture: The discriminant of one-parameter families of indecomposables of tame algebras is an explicit equation of an irreducible curve; the degree of the discriminant is that of the tame polynomial's discriminant, and the genus of the moduli curve is given by the signature sum of the Euler form. (tame family discriminant explicit equation)

**中文。** 定义：温野二分：Drozd 定理之后温代数的模空间结构。猜想：温代数的不可分解的一维参数族的判别式为不可约曲线的显式方程；判别式的次数为温多项式的判别式次数且模空间的亏格由欧拉形式的符号和给出。（温族判别式显式方程）
