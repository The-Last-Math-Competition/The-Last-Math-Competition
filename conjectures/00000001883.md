# 00000001883

**ML-13 ◆** — source: 48_逻辑与模型论II.md

**English.** Definition: The existence of a model companion of the complex exponential field (Macintyre's problem). Conjecture: The model companion of (C, exp) exists; under the Schanuel assumption, it is given by the uniform completion of Zilber pseudo-exponentiation fields (its existence proof can be made conditional).

**中文。** 定义：复指数域的模型伴侣存在性(Macintyre 问题)。猜想：(C,exp) 的模型伴侣存在;在 Schanuel 假设下,由 Zilber 伪指数域的一致完备化构造给出(其存在性证明可条件化)。
