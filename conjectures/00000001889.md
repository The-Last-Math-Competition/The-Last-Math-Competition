# 00000001889

**English.** Definition: The differential Galois inverse problem asks which algebraic groups are realizable as Galois groups of differential equations in positive characteristic. Conjecture: The realizable groups in char p are the reductive groups; the realizable subgroups of GL₂(F_p(t)) are connected reductive subgroups and a finite dihedral list; irreducible but non-realizable ones are characterized by the p-exponent exception.

**中文。** 定义：微分 Galois 逆问题问哪些代数群可实现为正特征微分方程的 Galois 群。猜想：char p 的可实现群为约化群;GL₂(F_p(t)) 的可实现子群为连通约化子群及二面体的有限列表;不可约但不可实现者由 p-指数的例外刻画。
