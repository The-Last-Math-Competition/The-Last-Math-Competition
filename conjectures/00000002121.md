**English.** Definition: The connective constant μ of Z² self-avoiding walks is the growth limit. Conjecture: μ is transcendental; the evidence for transcendence is a lower bound on the irrationality measure; and the irrationality-measure lower bound is two plus a little. (SAW connective constant transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：Z² 自避行走的连接常数 μ 为增长极限。猜想：μ 为超越数；且超越性的证据为无理度量的下界；且无理度量的下界为二加小量。（SAW 连接常数超越性）
