# 00000006415

**SD3-15 ◆** — source: 199_几何测度V_对称差问题.md

**English.** Definition: Spherical asymmetry is the difference measure from a ball. Conjecture: The deficit and asymmetry control each other in both directions with optimal constants, attained by rotationally symmetric bodies. (bidirectional deficit-asymmetry control)

**中文。** 定义：球不对称度为与球的差测度量。猜想：亏格与不对称度互相双向控制且常数最优；且最优由旋转对称体达到。（亏格-不对称双向控制）
