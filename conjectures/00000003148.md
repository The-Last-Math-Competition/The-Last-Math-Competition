# 00000003148

**English.** Definition: The Laplacian spectrum of Lie groups: harmonic analysis on the root space. Conjecture: The multiplicity formula of the spectrum: the multiplicity of an eigenvalue is the dimension of the weight space, with the count of dimensions the Freudenthal recursion. (Laplacian multiplicity Freudenthal recursion)

**中文。** 定义：李群的 Laplacian 谱：根空间的调和分析。猜想：谱的重数公式：特征值的重数为权空间的维数且维数的计数为 Freudenthal 递推。（Laplacian 重数 Freudenthal 递推）
