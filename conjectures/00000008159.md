# 00000008159

**RNG-19 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The renormalization of the asymptotic freedom of the nonlinear sigma model: the convergence exponent nls_geom of the geometric expansion (curvature terms of the metric) of the loop diagrams of the beta function of NLSM. Conjecture: The convergence of the nls_geom expansion (geometric series convergence) is controlled by the positivity of the Ricci curvature of the target manifold (a positive-Ricci convergence criterion); the convergence radius is the reciprocal of the squared curvature (a curvature-reciprocal radius law); and the minimal target of divergence for negative Ricci (anomalous dimensions, breaking of asymptotic freedom) is the negative mirror of S^2 (an S^2 anti-mirror example), the anomalous exponent of the counterexample being 1/2 (a half-anomaly law). (sigma model Ricci curvature half-anomaly)

**中文。** 定义：非线性 sigma 模型的渐近自由的重整化：NLSM 的 β 函数的圈图的几何展开（度规的曲率项）的收尾指数 nls_geom。猜想：nls_geom 的展开的收敛（几何级数收敛）由目标流形的 Ricci 曲率的正性（正 Ricci 收敛判据）控制；且收敛半径为曲率平方的倒数（曲率倒数半径律）；负 Ricci（反常维数）的发散（渐近自由的破坏）的最小目标为 S² 的负镜像（S² 反镜像例），反例的反常指数为 1/2（半反常律）。
