**F3-11 ★** — source: 82_有限域上的几何III.md

**English.** Definition: Spreads: pairwise disjoint subspace tilings of projective space. Conjecture: The rigidity question of spreads: the equivalence classes of k-spreads of d-dimensional space are a single monomial type when k+1 divides d+1; the count of equivalence classes follows the factorization law of Gauss binomials. (spread equivalence counting law)

**中文。** 定义：射影空间的互不相遇子空间铺砌（spread）。猜想：spread 的刚性问题：d 维空间的 k-spread 的等价类为单项式型的单一类当 k+1 整除 d+1；等价类的计数为 Gauss 二项式的因子分解律。（spread 等价计数律）
