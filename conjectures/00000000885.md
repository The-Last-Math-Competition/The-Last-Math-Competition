# 00000000885

**English.** Definition: A forward self-similar Leray solution of Navier–Stokes is a global weak solution of the form u(x,t) = λ(t)U(xλ(t)). Conjecture: All forward self-similar Leray solutions are trivial; beyond the exclusions of Nečas–Ružička–Šverák and Tsai, the remaining cases (non-axisymmetric, arbitrary swirl) do not exist either.

**中文。** 定义：Navier–Stokes 的前向自相似 Leray 解指形如 u(x,t)=λ(t)U(xλ(t)) 的整体弱解。猜想：一切前向自相似 Leray 解均为平凡解;Nečas–Ružička–Šverák 与 Tsai 的排除之外,剩余情形(非轴对称、swirl 任意)亦不存在。
