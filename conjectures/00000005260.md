# 00000005260

**MS3-30 △** — source: 160_代数几何V_模空间.md

**English.** Definition: The section dimension and symplectic homology are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two moduli spaces with identical dimension sequences but different symplectic homology, and the separation is realized by an explicit covering pair with the same sections but different topology. (same section dimensions-different symplectic homology separation)

**中文。** 定义：截面维数与辛同调指两层。猜想：存在两层模空间的维数序列相同而辛同调不同,分离由同截面异拓扑的显式覆盖对实现。（截面维数同-辛同调异分离）
