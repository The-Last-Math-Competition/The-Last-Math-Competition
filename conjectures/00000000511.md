# 00000000511

**English.** Definition: Let C be a smooth curve of genus g and degree d; the regularity reg(C) of its homogeneous coordinate ring relates to the last syzygies of its minimal free resolution. Conjecture: For hyperelliptic curves, reg(C) = 2 while the length of the tail of the minimal resolution equals Cliff(C) := min{deg L − 2(h⁰(L)−1) : h⁰(L) ≥ 2, h¹(L) ≥ 2}, and this identity holds uniformly for all hyperelliptic curves with g ≥ 2.

**中文。** 定义：设 C 为亏格 g、次数 d 的光滑曲线,其齐次坐标环的正则度 reg(C) 与极小自由分解的末端 syzygy 数相关。猜想：对超椭圆曲线,reg(C)=2 而其极小分解的末段长度恰为 Cliffs(C):=min{deg L−2(h⁰(L)−1):h⁰(L)≥2,h¹(L)≥2},且该等式对一切满足 g≥2 的超椭圆曲线统一成立。
