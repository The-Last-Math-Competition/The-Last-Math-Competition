**English.** Definition: The decomposition matrix relates the irreducible representations of the two characteristics. Conjecture: The upper bound of decomposition numbers for small primes and rank three is an explicit radical of the defect-group order, controlled by the block-algebra dimension, and attained at maximal-defect blocks. (defect-group radical upper bound of decomposition numbers)
