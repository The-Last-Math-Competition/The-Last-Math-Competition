# 00000007546

**English.** Definition: Free products as free amalgamation structure. Conjecture: The freeness law of products is dimension free decomposition, the decomposition unique; and the combination of uniqueness is rank additivity. (rank-additive unique free decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：自由积为自由合并结构。猜想：积的自由度律为维数自由分解且分解唯一；且唯一的组合为秩可加。（自由分解秩可加唯一）
