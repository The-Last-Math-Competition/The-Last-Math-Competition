# 00000009716

**English.** Definition: Energy convergence of Klein-Gordon on asymptotically flat backgrounds: the joint structure of mass term and decay. Conjecture: Massive Klein-Gordon energies converge to the free evolution at rate t^{-1-epsilon}; epsilon is given by an explicit trade-off formula of the mass and the background decay exponent. (Klein-Gordon energy convergence rate)

**中文。** 定义：渐近平坦背景 Klein–Gordon 的能量收敛：质量项与衰减的联合结构。猜想：带质量 Klein–Gordon 解的能量收敛到自由演化且速率为 t^{−1−ε}；ε 由质量与背景衰减指标的显式折衷公式给出。（KG 能量收敛速率）
