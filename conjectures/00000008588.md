# 00000008588

**NSP-28 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: Spectral rigidity of II_1 factors (Popa): the property that spectral distance conditions on a factor and its diagonal tensor embedding imply isomorphism rigidity of crossed products. Conjecture: II_1 factors with the strong rectification property are always spectrally rigid, with rectification critical constant 1/4; spectral rigidity implies relative spectral gaps of subfactors of width at least the square of the rectification constant; non-rigid examples are given by group measure space constructions whose first L^2-Betti number is always zero; and free group factors of rank at least 2 are all spectrally rigid, with rigidity uniquely determining the factor under tensor decompositions. (Popa spectral rigidity rectification Betti)

**中文。** 定义：II₁ 因子的谱刚性（Popa）：由因子与其对角张量嵌入的谱距离条件推出交叉积同构刚性的性质。猜想：具有强整流性质的 II₁ 因子恒为谱刚性且整流临界常数为 1/4；谱刚性蕴含子因子的相对谱隙且隙宽不小于整流常数的平方；非刚性例子由群测度空间构造给出且其第一 L²-Betti 数恒为零；自由群因子在秩至少 2 时全部谱刚性且刚性在张量分解下唯一确定因子。
