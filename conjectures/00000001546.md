# 00000001546

**GM-06 △** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: A bisector line of an n-point set is a line splitting the remaining points into equal halves. Conjecture: The maximum number of bisector lines is n·e^{O(√log n)}; that is, the upper bound matches the known lower bound n·e^{Ω(√log n)} on the doubly-exponential scale, and no polynomial upper bound exists.

**中文。** 定义：平分线指 n 点集中恰平分其余点的直线。猜想：平分线最大数为 n·e^{O(√log n)};即上界与已知下界 n·e^{Ω(√log n)} 在双重指数尺度匹配,不存在多项式上界。
