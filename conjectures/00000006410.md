**English.** Definition: The difference spectrum and shape are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two equimeasurable sets with the same translation-difference spectrum but different shapes, and the separation is realized by an explicit rotation pair with the same spectrum but different polarization. (same difference spectrum-different shape rotation separation)

**中文。** 定义：差谱与形状指两层。猜想：存在两等测集的平移差谱相同而形状不同,分离由同谱异极化的显式旋转对实现。（差谱同-形状异旋转分离）
