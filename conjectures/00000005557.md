# 00000005557

**English.** Definition: The index theorem is the Euler-characteristic formula of modules. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit product of local indices, the multiplicity sum at isolated fixed points, and the normalization constant is one once fixed. (unit normalization of local index products)

**中文。** 定义：指标定理指模的欧拉指标公式。猜想：换算常数为局部指标的显式乘积,孤立不动点处为重数和且归一常数为一旦定。（指标局部乘积归一常一）
