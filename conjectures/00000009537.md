# 00000009537

**QIC-17 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: The hypothesis testing relative entropy D_H^epsilon(rho||sigma) is the relative entropy of the optimal test under a type-one error constraint; the quantum Stein and Hoeffding limits are governed by the Umegaki entropy and D_alpha respectively. Conjecture: The second-order correction of channel capacity at finite block length n is -sqrt(nV) times the inverse Gaussian quantile Phi^{-1}(epsilon), with the information variance V given by an explicit algebraic formula; and the extremal channels for the variance-to-mean ratio V/D are asymmetric qubit erasure channels, attaining the extremal constant 2. (hypothesis relative entropy second order)

**中文。** 定义：假设检验相对熵 D_H^ε(ρ‖σ) 为第一类错误 ≤ε 约束下最优检验的相对熵；量子 Stein 与 Hoeffding 极限分别由 Umegaki 熵与 D_α 控制。猜想：信道容量在块长 n 有限时的二阶修正为 −√(nV)·Φ^{−1}(ε)，信息方差 V 有显式代数公式；方差与均值之比 V/D 的极值信道为非对称 qubit 删除信道，极值常数达到 2。（检验相对熵二阶）
