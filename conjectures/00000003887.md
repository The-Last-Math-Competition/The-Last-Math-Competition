# 00000003887

**English.** Definition: The relative Frobenius is the Frobenius action after base change. Conjecture: F-flatness is always preserved under faithfully flat descent; and the minimal counterexample must involve a non-reduced base. (relative F-flat descent criterion)

**中文。** 定义：相对 Frobenius 指基变换后的 Frobenius 作用。猜想：F-平坦性恒在忠实平坦下降下保持；且失败的最小例子必然出现非既约基。（相对 F-平坦下降判据）
