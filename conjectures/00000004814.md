**English.** Definition: A zero gap is the difference of adjacent nontrivial zeros. Conjecture: The limiting law of normalized gaps is the symplectic-ensemble gap distribution, the universality class is independent of the Satake parameters, and the distribution function is explicit. (symplectic universal limiting law of zero gaps)

**中文。** 定义：零点间隙指相邻非平凡零点之差。猜想：归一化间隙的极限律为辛系综间隙分布,普适类不依赖 Satake 参数且分布函数显式。（零点间隙辛普适极限律）
