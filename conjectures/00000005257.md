# 00000005257

**English.** Definition: The symplectic form is the natural closed form of the moduli space. Conjecture: The symplectic structure of rank two is given by the explicit pairing of the canonical class, is the standard form on the genus-one moduli, and the construction is globally nondegenerate. (canonical construction of the rank-two symplectic pairing)

**中文。** 定义：辛形式指模空间的自然闭形式。猜想：秩二的辛构造由典范类的显式配对给出,亏格一模上为标准形式且构造整体非退化。（秩二辛配对典范构造）
