**English.** Definition: The component-by-component construction is the greedy algorithm selecting generators dimension by dimension. Conjecture: The critical dimension count in dimension three is an explicit staircase function of the error weights, with at most three steps, and the construction is polynomial time. (three-step staircase of critical dimensions in component-by-component construction)
