**English.** Definition: The David–Semmes susceptibility question (SQP): the rectifiability-lifting problem for vector fields. Conjecture: The complete positive extension of SQP in codimension one: every measure-level susceptibility lifting of David–Semmes principles is feasible; the proof path is the finite vanishing of the ε-approximation lattices of Carleson measures. (susceptibility codimension-one extension)

**中文。** 定义：David–Semmes 单值性问题（SQP）：向量场的可校正性提升问题。猜想：SQP 在余维一的肯定的完整推广：一切 David–Semmes 原则的测度单值提升可行；可行性的证明路径为 Carleson 测度的 ε-逼近格的有限消没。（单值性余维一推广）
