# 00000005621

**English.** Definition: The partial intersection theorem is the common-point theorem of multiple convex blocks. Conjecture: The type of the three-part intersection is controlled by the equivariant-topological upper bound; in convex position the intersection is always a single point with multiplicity three. (equivariant single-point multiplicity three of triple intersections)

**中文。** 定义：部分相交定理指多个凸块剖分的公共点定理。猜想：三部分交的型由等变拓扑的上界控制；凸位置时交恒为单点且重数为三。（三重交等变单点重数三）
