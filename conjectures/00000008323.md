# 00000008323

**English.** Definition: The exceptional set LW_exc of the Littlewood conjecture degenerate domain: pairs (alpha,beta) where inf liminf n ||n alpha|| ||n beta|| = 0 fails. Conjecture: The Hausdorff dimension of LW_exc is zero (a zero-dimensional exception law); and the nonemptiness of the exception (existence of a counterexample) is equivalent to the breaking of the invariance of the Ein-Lindenstrauss quasi-flow (an equivalence law); the universality of the weaker degeneration (pseudo-Littlewood: subexponential growth of the product, a pseudo-LW universality law) has proof threshold three-infinitesimal (a three-infinitesimal threshold); and the boundary case of pairs (alpha,beta) from a quadratic field (pairs from Q(sqrt d)) is solvable by an algebraic identity (an algebraic identity solvability law). (Littlewood zero-dimensional quasi-flow equivalence)
