**English.** Definition: The double bubble is the standard minimal cluster with two volume constraints, and the spectral gap is the first positive eigenvalue of the second-variation operator. Conjecture: The double-bubble spectral gap is maximal at equal volumes and decreases monotonically with the volume ratio, and the maximum is unique and explicit. (double bubble spectral gap maximal at equal volumes)

**中文。** 定义：双泡指两体积约束的标准极小泡团,谱隙指第二变分算子的首正特征值。猜想：双泡谱隙在等体积时最大、随体积比单调下降,极大值唯一且显式。（双泡谱隙等体积最大）
