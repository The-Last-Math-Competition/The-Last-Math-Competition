**English.** Definition: The mod-p Lefschetz phenomenon for face rings: characteristic-p combinatorics. Conjecture: The complete decision of mod-p Hall–Lefschetz: decided by the fixed-point dimension of the Frobenius action on the face ring, equal to the invariant of the p-adic barycentric subdivision. (mod p Lefschetz decision)
