**KPU-10 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: Baik–Rains limit of steady state KPZ: Brownian initial condition. Conjecture: mixing correlated decay of space process of steady state solution is correlated time of logarithm growth: process in length L is exp(cL); mixing constant is explicit function of drift parameter and in zero drift at phase transition. (steady state mixing correlated)

**中文。** 定义：稳态 KPZ：双侧 Brownian 初始条件的 Baik–Rains 极限。猜想：稳态解的空间过程的混合相关衰减为对数增长：过程在长度 L 的相关时间为 exp(cL)；混合常数为漂移参数的显式分段函数且在零漂移处相变。（稳态混合相关律）
