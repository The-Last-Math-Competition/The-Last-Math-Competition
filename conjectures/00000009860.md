**KPU-10 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: Stationary KPZ: the Baik-Rains limit for two-sided Brownian initial conditions. Conjecture: The mixing correlation decay of the spatial process of stationary solutions grows logarithmically: the correlation time over length L is exp(cL); the mixing constant is an explicit piecewise function of the drift parameter with a phase transition at zero drift. (stationary mixing correlation law)

**中文。** 定义：稳态 KPZ：双侧 Brownian 初始条件的 Baik–Rains 极限。猜想：稳态解的空间过程的混合相关衰减为对数增长：过程在长度 L 的相关时间为 exp(cL)；混合常数为漂移参数的显式分段函数且在零漂移处相变。（稳态混合相关律）
