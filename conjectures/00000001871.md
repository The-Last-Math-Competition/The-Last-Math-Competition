# 00000001871

**ML-01 ◆** — source: 48_逻辑与模型论II.md

**English.** Definition: Shelah's categoricity conjecture states that abstract elementary classes (AEC) are categorical at sufficiently large cardinals. Conjecture: The categoricity spectrum of a countable-LS AEC below the Hanf number H₁ is exhausted by three modes (never categorical / linear growth / immediately categorical); the explicit simplification of the Hanf lower bound is H₁ = ℶ_{(2^{ℵ₀})^+}.

**中文。** 定义：Shelah 范畴性猜想指抽象初等类(AEC)在足够大基数下范畴。猜想：可数 LS(AEC) 的范畴性谱在 Hanf 数 H₁ 之下由三种模式穷举(完全不范畴/线性增长/立即范畴);Hanf 下界的显式简化为 H₁=ℶ_{(2^{ℵ0})^+}。
