# 00000004274

**AG5-04 ★** — source: 128_交换群论.md

**English.** Definition: The center of the automorphism group of a countable p-group consists of the elements commuting with all automorphisms. Conjecture: The center is nontrivial if and only if a characteristic element exists, the count of central elements is read off explicitly from the Ulm sequence, and the formula is a termwise product of powers of 2. (counting the automorphism center of p-groups)

**中文。** 定义：可数 p-群的自同构群中心指与一切自同构交换的元。猜想：中心非平凡当且仅当存在特征元素,中心元的计数由 Ulm 序列的显式读出,公式为逐项 2 的幂积。（p-群自同构中心计数）
