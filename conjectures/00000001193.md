# 00000001193

**English.** Definition: The Q'-branch of Hall–Littlewood theory consists of translation-symmetric functions. Conjecture: The branching translation rule for Q'ₙ is explicit; and the combinatorial interpretation of the rule is the counting of signed tableaux. (Q-prime translation rule)

**中文。** 定义：Hall–Littlewood 的 Q' 分支为平移对称函数。猜想：Q'ₙ 的分支平移规则为显式；且规则的组合解释为带符号表计数。（Q-prime 平移规则）
