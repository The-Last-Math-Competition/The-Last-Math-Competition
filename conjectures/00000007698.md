# 00000007698

**TIL-08 ◆** — source: 242_离散几何II_镶嵌与铺砌.md

**English.** Definition: A Delone set Y in R^d satisfies rY>=r (pairwise distances >= r) and RY<=R (every ball of radius R meets Y); its covering density theta(Y) is the covering density of the closed balls. Conjecture: For d>=3 the infimum of theta(Y) over Delone sets with fixed R/r is attained by lattice Delone sets, the optimal lattice at ratio 1 being A_d^*. (lattice-optimal Delone covering)

**中文。** 定义：R^d 中 Delone 集 Y 满足 rY≥r（任意两点距 ≥r）与 RY≤R（任意半径 R 球含 Y 的点）。其覆盖密度 θ(Y) 为闭球 {B(y,R)}_{y∈Y} 的覆盖密度上确界。猜想：d≥3 时 inf θ(Y)（遍历所有 R/r 固定比值下的 Delone 集）由格 Delone 集取得，且最优格在比值为 1 时为 A_d* 格。
