# 00000006968

**English.** Definition: Conditional Monte Carlo with conditioning. Conjecture: The simulation of conditional expectations holds, the variance of the simulation being the conditional variance. (conditional-variance conditional Monte Carlo)

**中文。** 定义：条件 Monte Carlo：条件化。猜想：条件的方差：条件期望的模拟且拟的方差为条件方差。（条件 Monte Carlo 条件方差条件期望模拟方差条件化模拟 QMC）
