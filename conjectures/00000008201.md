# 00000008201

**English.** Definition: The Kirillov orbit method: the correspondence between the unitary dual of a nilpotent Lie group G and coadjoint orbits O, sending O to the representation pi_O. Conjecture: For every exponential nilpotent group the correspondence is a bijection (completeness of the orbit method); the formal character (the Fourier transform of the symplectic potential of the orbit) equals the character distribution of pi_O (global validity of the Kirillov formula); and the convergence domain of the formula (analytic continuation of the character distribution) contains a holomorphic neighborhood of G with window radius the reciprocal of the Gelfand-Kirillov dimension times 1/e (a window radius formula). (orbit method completeness window radius)

**中文。** 定义：Kirillov 轨道方法：幂零李群 G 的酉对偶 Ĝ 与余伴随轨道 O 的对应 π_O。猜想：对一切指数幂零群，对应为双射（轨道方法完全性）；且形式特征（轨道的辛势的 Fourier 变换）= π_O 的特征分布（Kirillov 公式的全局有效性）；公式的收敛域（特征分布的解析延拓）包含 G 的全纯邻域半径 1/e 的 Gelfand–Kirillov 维数倒数的窗（窗半径公式）。
