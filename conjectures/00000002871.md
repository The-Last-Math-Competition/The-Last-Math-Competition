**English.** Definition: Callias-type indices: indices of Dirac operators with potentials. Conjecture: The boundary formula for Callias indices: the index equals the winding number of the boundary map at infinity, computed as the topological degree of a Clifford-valued map. (Callias boundary winding formula)

**中文。** 定义：Callias 型指标：带位势的 Dirac 算子的指标。猜想：Callias 指标的边界公式：指标等于无穷远边界的映射的缠绕数且缠绕数的计算为 Clifford 值映射的拓扑度。（Callias 边界缠绕公式）
