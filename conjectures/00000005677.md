# 00000005677

**English.** Definition: Long-range jumps have power-law jump-distance distribution. Conjecture: The scaling at critical exponent two is always root-time times a logarithm; the correction is given by the boundary of the stable law. (logarithmic correction scaling of long-range criticality)

**中文。** 定义：长程跳跃指幂律尾的跳距分布。猜想：临界指数二处的标度恒为根号时间乘对数型；修正由稳定律边界给出。（长程临界对数修正标度）
