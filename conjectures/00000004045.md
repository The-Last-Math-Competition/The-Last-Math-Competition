**English.** Definition: The exponential growth rate of the derivation algebra is the exponential order of its dimension sequence. Conjecture: The derivation algebra growth rate of a quiver algebra takes only the two discrete values 0 and 1, and no intermediate exponential growth exists. (dichotomy of derivation growth rates)
