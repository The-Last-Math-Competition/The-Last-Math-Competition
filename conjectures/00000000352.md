**English.** Definition: The Minkowski–Hlawka lower bound: there exists a lattice Λ with density ≥ ζ(n)/2^{n-1}. Conjecture: There exists an explicit constructive family attaining (1−o(1))ζ(n)/2^{n-1} (an explicit version of Minkowski).
