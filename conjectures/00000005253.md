# 00000005253

**English.** Definition: The stability wall is the switching hypersurface of stability parameters. Conjecture: The semiparametric wall table is an explicit sign-flip table of intersection pairings, the flips are given by rational functions of elementary walls, and it is closed. (sign-flip table of semiparametric walls)

**中文。** 定义：稳定壁指稳定性参数的切换面。猜想：半参量的壁表为相交配对的显式符号翻转表,翻转由初等壁的有理函数给出且封闭。（半参量壁符号翻转表）
