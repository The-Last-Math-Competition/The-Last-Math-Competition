# 00000004012

**RP2-12 ★** — source: 119_随机分析II_粗路径.md

**English.** Definition: The Ito map is the map from the driver and the initial value to the solution. Conjecture: The Ito map is locally Holder in the p-variation topology with exponent of the form gamma - 1/p, where gamma is the regularity of the vector fields, and this exponent is optimal. (optimal Holder exponent of the Ito map)

**中文。** 定义：Itô 映射指从驱动与初值到解的映射。猜想：Itô 映射在 p-变差拓扑下局部 Hölder,指数为 γ − 1/p 型,γ 为向量场正则性,该指数最优。（Itô 映射最优 Hölder 指数）
