**English.** Definition: Kervaire–Milnor: the group structure of homotopy spheres. Conjecture: The homotopy sphere group: the order of the subgroup of the stable homotopy group of spheres is the explicit numerator formula of Bernoulli numbers, with the integrality of the formula the Kervaire–Milnor homology. (bP Bernoulli numerator homology integrality)
