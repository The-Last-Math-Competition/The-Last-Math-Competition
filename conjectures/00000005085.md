# 00000005085

**GL3-05 ★** — source: 155_几何Langlands的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The vertex count of geometric Satake weight polytopes is the combinatorial invariant of loop equivalence classes. Conjecture: The vertex count of rank-two groups is an explicit Gelfand-type sum with highest coefficient given by the branch count of the weight; the generating function of the count is rational with poles at roots of unity; and the mod p reduction of the vertex count is indexed explicitly by the p-core of the weight. (Gelfand counting of polytope vertices)

**中文。** 定义：几何 Satake 权多面体的顶点计数：圈等价类的组合不变量。猜想：秩二群的顶点计数为盖尔范德型显式和且最高次系数由权的分支计数；计数的生成函数为有理函数且极点为单位根；顶点数的模 p 约化由权的 p 核显式索引。（多面体顶点盖尔范德计数）
