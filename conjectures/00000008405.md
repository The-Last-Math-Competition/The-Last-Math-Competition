**English.** Definition: The Burnside motive: the orbit motive of quotients by finite group actions. Conjecture: The Burnside motive ring is the Grothendieck ring of the categorical sum of all finite group actions; the prime spectrum of the ring is the union of the cyclic-group prime spectrum and the prime number spectrum; and the kernel of the marks homomorphism is the ideal of noncyclic stable automorphisms, whose principal generator is the maximal-torus fixed motive. (Burnside motive ring spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Burnside 母题指有限群作用商的轨道母题。猜想：Burnside 母题环为一切有限群作用的范畴化和的 Grothendieck 环；环的素谱为循环群素数谱与素数谱的并；marks 同态的核为非循环稳定自同构的理想，理想的主生成元为极大环面不动母题。（Burnside 母题环谱）
