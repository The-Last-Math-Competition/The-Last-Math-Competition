**English.** Definition: Tensor network contraction: compressed representations of many-body wave functions. Conjecture: Rigidity of contraction complexity: the minimal cost of network contraction is controlled by an exponential in treewidth, with optimal orders one-to-one with tree decompositions; the entanglement dimension of the quantum system equals the treewidth of the network. (network contraction treewidth correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：张量网络收缩：多体波函数的压缩表示。猜想：收缩复杂度刚性：网络收缩的最小代价由树宽的指数控制且最优顺序与树分解一一对应；量子系统的纠缠维数等于网络的树宽。（网络收缩树宽对应）
