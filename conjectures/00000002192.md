# 00000002192

**EC-22 ★** — source: 58_极值集合论与组合.md

**English.** Definition: The dimension of a poset is the minimal number of linear extensions whose intersection is the poset. Conjecture: The maximal dimension of height-2 posets is ⌈log₂ log₂ n⌉ (exact); the bound follows from the deep nesting of standard examples of random posets.

**中文。** 定义：偏序的维数指线性扩展的交的最小个数。猜想：高度 2 的偏序的最大维数为 ⌈log₂ log₂ n⌉(精确);界由随机偏序的标准例子的深嵌套。
