# 00000002028

**AD-08 △** — source: 53_加性数论II.md

**English.** Definition: Short-interval Goldbach means prime pairs (p, N−p) both lying in an interval of length L. Conjecture: For all large even N, among the Goldbach prime pairs there is one pair in an interval of length N^{θ} (θ = 0.525); θ can be compressed by improvements of exponential sums.

**中文。** 定义：短区间 Goldbach 指素数对 (p, N−p) 皆位于长度 L 的区间。猜想：一切大偶 N 的 Goldbach 素数对中存在一对位于长度 N^{θ} 的区间(θ = 0.525);θ 可由指数和的改进压缩。
