# 00000008808

**English.** Definition: Cubic regularization: the Nesterov-Polyak cubic model method. Conjecture: Global iteration convergence follows a double-logarithmic law; local realization is superlinear; and the superlinear realization is a local quadratic window. (cubic regularization double-log global convergence)

**中文。** 定义：立方正则为 Nesterov–Polyak 的立方模型方法。猜想：全局迭代的收敛为双对数律；且局部的实现为超线性；且超线性的实现为局部二次窗。（立方正则双对数全局收敛）
