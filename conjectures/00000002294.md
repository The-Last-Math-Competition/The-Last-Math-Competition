**English.** Definition: An Oliver group satisfies the constraints of GL_n(F_p) actions (admitting Oliver subgroups). Conjecture: The complete version of the classification table of Oliver groups for p = 3: the table is exhausted by 14 items of solvable and nonsolvable types; the invariant lists of each item are explicit.
