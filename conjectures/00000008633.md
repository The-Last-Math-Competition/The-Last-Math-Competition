**INP-13 ★** — source: 273_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: The stochastic six-vertex model is the randomly weighted model. Conjecture: The rate function of sharp deviations is given by a variational formula; and the kernel of the variational problem is the convex conjugate of the free energy. (stochastic six-vertex free energy convex conjugate rate)

**中文。** 定义：随机六顶点指随机权重模型。猜想：尖锐偏差的速率函数由变分式给出；且变分的核为自由能凸共轭。（随机六顶点自由能凸共轭速率）
