# 00000002804

**English.** Definition: The Gärtner–Ellis theorem: the rate given by the Legendre–Fenchel transform of the log-moment function. Conjecture: Repairing the failure of differentiability in the GE theorem: at the extreme points of the convex hull of the nondifferentiable set of the log-moment function, the rate has subgradient jumps with jump magnitudes of phase-transition-latent-heat type. (GE nondifferentiable point jump magnitude)

**中文。** 定义：Gärtner–Ellis 定理：由母函数的 Legendre–Fenchel 变换给出的速率。猜想：GE 定理的可微性失效的修复：母函数不可微点集的凸包的极点处速率有次梯度跳跃且跳跃量为相变潜热型常数。（GE 不可微点跳跃量）
