# 00000001036

**FG-36 △** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: The uniform distribution of perfect codes means the shell-count is of uniform type. Conjecture: The integer solutions of the parameter equations form a complete list; and the combination of the list is the set of integer points of the Lloyd polynomial. (uniform Lloyd polynomial integer-point list)

**中文。** 定义：完全码的均匀分布为球壳计数均匀型。猜想：参数方程的整数解为完全列表；且列表的组合为 Lloyd 多项式整点。（均匀 Lloyd 多项式整点列表）
