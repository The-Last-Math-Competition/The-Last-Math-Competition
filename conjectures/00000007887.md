# 00000007887

**HCA-17 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The integral hierarchy of higher semiadditivity: the hierarchy constant h(C) of a monotonically n-ambidextrous monoidal category C. Conjecture: The chromatic hierarchy coincides with the semiadditive hierarchy: the maximal monotone additivity of a height-n category is exactly n; the splitting pattern of the realization spectrum of h(C) (Hahn-Rapschak type) degenerates at n=1 to the Mackey 2-group of Tambara functions; and any category with h(C) infinite must contain the full thick image of the sphere spectrum (equivalently, a localization of Spectra), giving rigidity of infinite additivity. (chromatic semiadditivity height rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：higher semiadditivity 的积分层级：对单调 n-可加范畴（monoidal n-ambidexterity）C 的层级常量 h(C)。猜想：chromatic 层级与 semiadditive 层级重合：高度 n 的范畴的最大单调可加性恰为 n；且 h(C) 的实现谱的分裂方式（Hahn–Rapschak 型）在 n=1 退化为 Tambara 函数的 Mackey 2-群；任何 h(C)=∞ 的范畴必含稳定球谱的完全厚像（即等价于 Spectra 的局部化），给出无穷可加性的刚性。
