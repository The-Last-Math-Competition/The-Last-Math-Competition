# 00000000231

**English.** Definition: A Wall–Sun–Sun prime is a prime for which the Fibonacci period does not divide p². Conjecture: No such prime exists below 10^{17}, and their infinitude is open; and the detection is settled through the discriminant. (WSS discriminant detection, infinitude open)

**中文。** 定义：Wall–Sun–Sun 素数为 Fibonacci 周期不整除 p² 的素。猜想：该类素数在十的十七次方内不存在且无穷性开放；且检测的核为判别式。（WSS-判别式检测无穷开放）
