# 00000003867

**RM3-17 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The local relaxation time of Dyson Brownian motion is the time order needed for bulk statistics to equilibrate. Conjecture: The local relaxation time is c_β N² with c_β = 1/(2β) times the diffusion rate, the constant being invariant under all analytic potentials. (DBM relaxation constant universality)

**中文。** 定义：Dyson 布朗运动的局部松弛时间指 bulk 统计达到平衡所需的时刻阶。猜想：局部松弛时间 = c_β N²,常数 c_β = 1/(2β) 乘扩散率,且该常数对一切解析位势不变。（DBM 松弛常数普适）
