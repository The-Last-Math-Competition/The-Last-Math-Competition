# 00000001859

**English.** Definition: The second-order limiting distribution is the fluctuation limit of the sum of a Wigner matrix with a deterministic matrix. Conjecture: The second-order Cauchy transform always has an explicit formula; and the formula gives the covariance for arbitrary mixtures of free and classical inputs. (second-order Cauchy explicit formula)

**中文。** 定义：二阶极限分布指 Wigner 与决定性矩阵之和的涨落极限。猜想：二阶 Cauchy 变换恒有显式公式；且公式给出任意混合自由与经典输入的协方差。（二阶 Cauchy 显式公式）
