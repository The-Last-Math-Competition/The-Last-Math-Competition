# 00000008408

**English.** Definition: The period map on the motivic side: the abstract period structure of the period matrix of motivic integrals. Conjecture: The abstract period map is holomorphic and its monotonicity is the monotonization of the motivic weight order; the motive of a fiber of the map is a direct summand of the constant motive; and dimension jumps of the fibers occur only on Noether-Lefschetz type motivic strata. (motivic period map fibers)

**中文。** 定义：母题侧的周期映射指母题积分的周期矩阵的抽象周期结构。猜想：抽象周期映射为全纯且其单调性为母题权序的单调化；映射的纤维的母题为常值母题的直和项；纤维的维数跳跃仅发生在 Noether–Lefschetz 型母题层上。（母题周期映射纤维）
