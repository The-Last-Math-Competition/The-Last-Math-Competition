# 00000004053

**English.** Definition: A periodic module is one with Omega^n M isomorphic to M. Conjecture: The prime factors of the minimal period of a periodic module always divide prime factors of the order of the defect group, and every power of every admissible prime is realized in some explicit block. (period-defect group prime factor correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：周期模指 Ω^n M ≅ M 的模。猜想：周期模的最小周期的素因子恒整除亏群阶的素因子,且每个可容素数的每个幂在某个显式块中实现。（周期-亏群素因子对应）
