# 00000005341

**English.** Definition: The genus bound is the genus upper bound read off from the homology. Conjecture: The genus bound of the positive torus family is exact and not improvable, the negative torus family is linearly corrected by a thickness term with explicit correction, and the thickness coefficient equals the Milnor number of the torus fiber. (thickness-linear correction of genus bounds)

**中文。** 定义：亏格界指由同调读出的亏格上界。猜想：正环面族的亏格界精确不可改进,负环面族由厚度项线性修正且修正显式。(亏格界厚度线性修正)；且厚度项系数等于环面纤维的 Milnor 数。
