**RT4-07 ★** — source: 229_随机张量.md

**English.** Definition: Extreme statistics of random tensors with the largest singular value. Conjecture: The tensor version of the Tracy-Widom limit of the largest singular value holds, and the exponent of the version is the coupling exponent. (coupling-exponent tensor Tracy-Widom limit)

**中文。** 定义：随机张量的极端统计：最大奇异值。猜想：最大奇异的极限：最大奇异值的 Tracy–Widom 的张量版且版的指数为耦合指数。（张量最大奇异值耦合指数极限版本）
