# 00000007175

**English.** Definition: The inverse problem of eigenvalue placement with tridiagonal matrices. Conjecture: Any real spectrum is realizable by a symmetric tridiagonal, and the positivity of the tridiagonal is the principal-minor chain. (principal-minor-chain tridiagonal realizability)

**中文。** 定义：特征值位置的逆问题：三对角。猜想：三对角可实现：任意实谱的可实现为对称三对角且角的正定为主子式链。（三对角主子式链正定实谱可实现任意）
