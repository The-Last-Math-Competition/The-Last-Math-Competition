# 00000009958

**MRG-18 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Zero-entropy rigidity of Cartan actions: supports of zero-entropy measures for maximal torus actions (Katok-Spatzier theory). Conjecture: The support of zero-entropy invariant measures of Cartan actions is a homogeneous closure; the minimal algebraic subgroup of the support is explicitly determined by the weight lattice of the atomic group of the measure, with the rank of the weight lattice equal to the rank of the action minus the atomic dimension. (Cartan zero-entropy support rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Cartan 作用的零熵刚性：极大环面作用的零熵测度支撑（Katok–Spatzier 理论）。猜想：Cartan 作用的零熵不变测度的支撑为齐性闭包；支撑的最小代数子群由测度的原子群的权格显式确定且权格的秩等于作用的秩减原子维数。（Cartan 零熵支撑刚性）
