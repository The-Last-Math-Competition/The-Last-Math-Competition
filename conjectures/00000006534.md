**English.** Definition: The nondegeneracy window is the degeneration structure of index computation. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the nondegeneracy window and the window ends are given by the degeneration structure; the window closes and the kernel of the closure is the Morse functional; and the repair of degeneration is by perturbation, with the norm of the perturbation being the window width. (conversion constant of nondegeneracy window ends)

**中文。** 定义：非退化窗：指标计算的退化结构。猜想：换算常数为非退化窗的显式且窗端由退化结构给出；窗口封闭且封闭的核为 Morse 泛函；退化的修复由微扰且微扰的范数为窗宽。（非退化窗端换算常数）
