# 00000007822

**MSE-12 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The isoperiodic foliation on M_g is the foliation defined by fixing the periods of an Abel differential. Conjecture: Every leaf is a complete Lagrangian for the symplectic geometry of T^*M_g; the closed leaves are exactly the lifts of Teichmueller curves in strata of squares of differentials, with counts agreeing with cusp numbers; and the closure of a leaf is the whole stratum if and only if the corresponding differential lies in the principal stratum with at most 2 zeros. (isoperiodic Lagrangian leaf closure)

**中文。** 定义：等周期叶状结构：M_g 上由固定 Abel 微分的周期条件定义的叶（isoperiodic foliation）。猜想：每片叶在 T^*M_g 的辛几何下为完全 Lagrangian；闭叶恰由平方微分 stratum 的 Teichmüller 曲线的提升构成，其计数与 cusp 数一致；叶的闭包为整叶（整个 stratum）当且仅当对应微分属于主 stratum 且零点个数 ≤2。
