# 00000003015

**English.** Definition: Matrix product states (MPS): low-rank parametrizations of one-dimensional entanglement. Conjecture: The approximation rate of MPS: the MPS approximation error for gapped one-dimensional systems decays exponentially in the rank, with the product of the decay rate and the spectral gap a universal constant. (MPS approximation exponential rate)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵积态（MPS）：一维纠缠的低秩参数化。猜想：MPS 的逼近速率：gapped 一维系统的 MPS 逼近误差为秩的指数衰减且衰减率与谱隙的乘积为普适常数。（MPS 逼近指数速率）
