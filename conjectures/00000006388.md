**English.** Definition: Curl elements with the degree structure of the two Nedelec classes. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the degree gain of the second class and the curl space gains one degree; the check of the gain is the exactness of the de Rham complex; and the interpolation points of the two classes coincide, with weight 1. (degree-gain conversion constant of the second class)

**中文。** 定义：旋度元：Nédélec 旋度元两类的次数结构。猜想：换算常数为第二类次数增益的显式且旋度空间增益一次；增益的校验为 de Rham 复形的正合性；两类的插值点重合且重合的权为 1。（第二类次数增益换算常数）
