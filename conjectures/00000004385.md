**English.** Definition: The cdh topology is the complete-intersection topology, and KH is homotopy K-theory. Conjecture: The cdh convergence order of K to KH is of dimension-reciprocal type, and the order is optimal and non-improvable on semistable rings. (optimal order of cdh convergence)

**中文。** 定义：cdh 拓扑指完全交拓扑,KH 指同伦 K 理论。猜想：K 到 KH 的 cdh 收敛阶为维数倒数型,阶在半稳定环达到最优且不可改进。（cdh 收敛最优阶）
