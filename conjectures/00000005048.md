**English.** Definition: The definability-computability conversion is the correspondence of logical definability with algorithmic computability. Conjecture: In the monadic case the conversion is explicitly indexed by rank-one invariants and preserves polynomial relations; the image of the index is a dense subset of polynomial-time computable functions; the conversion first fails at binary existential quantifiers, with the failure constructed explicitly by pairing functions. (definability-computability conversion index of the monadic case)

**中文。** 定义：可定义与可计算的换算：逻辑可定义性与算法可计算性的对应。猜想：单子情形的换算由秩一不变量显式索引且换算保持多项式关系；索引的像为多项式时间可计算函数的稠密子集；换算失效首次出现在二元存在量词且失效由配对函数显式构造。（单子定义-计算换算索引）
