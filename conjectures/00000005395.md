**English.** Definition: The convergence constant of p-adic arguments is the rate parameter of p-adic convergence in dynamical Mordell-Lang type arguments about orbits meeting subvarieties. Conjecture: The convergence constant is an explicit exponential of the attraction depth, identically one for quadratic polynomial families; the exponent grows linearly with attraction depth, with slope 1; and in the superattracting case the exponent doubles, with doubling factor exactly 2. (attraction-depth exponential constant of dynamics)

**中文。** 定义：进值论证的收敛常数：动力莫德尔–朗型轨道与子簇交论证中 p 进收敛的速率参数。猜想：收敛常数为吸附深度的显式指数且二次多项式族时指数恒为一；指数随吸附深度线性增长且斜率为 1；超吸引情形指数翻倍且翻倍因子恰为 2。（动力吸附深度指数常数）
