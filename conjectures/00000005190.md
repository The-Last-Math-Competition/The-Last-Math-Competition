**ST2-20 △** — source: 158_高阶范畴II_稳定无穷范畴.md

**English.** Definition: Mutation orbits and generated categories are two layers of structure. Conjecture: There exist two exceptional collections with isomorphic orbits but different generated categories, and the separation is realized by an explicit gluing pair with the same orbit but different realizations. (same orbit-different generated category gluing separation)

**中文。** 定义：变异轨道与生成范畴指两层结构。猜想：存在两例外集的轨道同构而生成范畴不同,分离由同轨道异实现的显式粘合对实现。（轨道同-生成范畴异粘合分离）
