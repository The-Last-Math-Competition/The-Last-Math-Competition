# 00000007656

**HY4-26 ★** — source: 240_微分算子IV_双曲型.md

**English.** Definition: The transmission error as the energy-leakage measure of truncated boundaries. Conjecture: The transmission error law is always the exponential decay law of the truncation order; and the decay rate is an explicit rational function of the wave number. (exponential transmission-error law)

**中文。** 定义：透射误差为截断边界的能量泄漏度量。猜想：传递误差律恒为截断阶的指数衰减律；衰减率为波数的显式有理函数。（传递误差指数律）
