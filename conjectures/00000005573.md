# 00000005573

**English.** Definition: The normal form is the canonical presentation of the functor. Conjecture: The two-generated normal form is the explicit chain product of abelian graphs, diagonal under orthogonal bases; the diagonal entries are the prime factors of the orders of the generators; and the length of the normal form is the generator count plus the rank, with every term of the sum nonnegative. (chain-product diagonal normal form of two generators)

**中文。** 定义：标准型：函子的规范表示（信号函子的标准呈现）。猜想：二生成情形的标准型为交换图链积的显式且正交基下对角；对角元为生成元的阶的素因子；标准型的长度为生成元个数加秩且和式的每项非负。（二生成链积对角标准型）
