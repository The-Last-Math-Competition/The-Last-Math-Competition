# 00000007841

**English.** Definition: The success probability p(G) of Karger's contraction algorithm on an n-vertex graph is the probability that the random contraction sequence outputs a minimum cut; the optimal contraction schedule ratio rho(G) is the infimum over connected n-vertex graphs of p(G) relative to the theoretical extremum 2/n^2. Conjecture: The limit constant of rho is e^{-1/2}, attained asymptotically by near-complete graphs; and for every simple graph the explicit bound p(G) >= 2(1-epsilon)/n^2 with epsilon(n) = O(1/n) follows from the independent repetition combinatorial bound, tight on the cycle. (Karger contraction extremal ratio)

**中文。** 定义：Karger 收缩算法在 n 点图上的成功概率 p(G) 为随机收缩序列产生最小割的概率。定义最优收缩调度比 ρ(G) 为 p(G) 与理论极值 2/n² 之比在一切 n 点连通图上的下确界。猜想：ρ 的极限常数为 e^{-1/2}（由完全图的近似下界达到渐近），且对一切简单图 p(G) ≥ 2(1-ε)/n² 的显式 ε(n)=O(1/n) 可由独立重复的组合下界给出，界在圈图上精确取等。
