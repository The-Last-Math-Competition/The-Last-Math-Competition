**English.** Definition: Tangent data and etale neighborhoods are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two derived stacks with the same tangent data but non-isomorphic etale neighborhoods, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same tangent but different characteristic lifts. (same tangent-different characteristic-lift neighborhood separation)
