**English.** Definition: The Le Cam two-point method is the two-point argument for lower bounds. Conjecture: The two-point argument is insufficient for the exact constant on l_1 balls; and the necessary dimension for a three-point mixture argument is d at least 3. (limitation of Le Cam two points)
