# 00000009124

**English.** Definition: Bernstein's inequality: gradient control of polynomials. Conjecture: The constant of the spherical extension is always an explicit function of dimension; and the saturation of the function is the Chebyshev leading term. (Bernstein spherical constant)

**中文。** 定义：Bernstein 不等式为多项式的梯度控制。猜想：球面扩展的常数恒为维数的显式函数；且函数的饱和为切比雪夫首项。（Bernstein 球面常数）
