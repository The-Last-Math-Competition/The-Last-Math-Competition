# 00000009671

**English.** Definition: Schanuel's conjecture asserts that for Q-linearly independent algebraic numbers x_i the exponential field has transcendence degree at least the count of i; its quantitative effective form is unknown. Conjecture: The Schanuel lower bound can be strengthened to an explicit height version: the deficit of the transcendence degree lower bound is controlled by an explicit polynomial function of the product of Weil heights of the x_i; and the deficit is zero in the compact-set case of uniformly bounded heights. (quantitative Schanuel strengthening)

**中文。** 定义：Schanuel 猜想断言 Q 线性无关代数数 x_i 的指数域的超越次数至少为 i 数；其定量有效形式未知。猜想：Schanuel 下界可加强为显式高度版本：超越次数下界的亏损由 x_i 的 Weil 高度乘积的显式多项式函数控制；亏损为零的条件为高度一致有界的紧集情形。（Schanuel 定量强化）
