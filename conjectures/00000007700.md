**English.** Definition: A Danzer set is a point set D in R^d such that every convex body of volume >= 1 contains a point of D. Conjecture: For every d>=2 there exists a Danzer set of bounded density; and D can be chosen to also be a Delone set satisfying the growth density O(log^d t) visiting-time bound (every volume-1 convex body is visited before time t). (bounded-density Danzer-Delone sets)

**中文。** 定义：Danzer 集指 R^d 中点集 D 使得每个体积 ≥1 的凸体都含 D 的点。猜想：对每个 d≥2 存在具有有界密度的 Danzer 集；且可取 D 同时为 Delone 集并满足增长密度 O(log^d t) 的访问时间界（即每个体积 1 凸体在时刻 t 前被访问）。
