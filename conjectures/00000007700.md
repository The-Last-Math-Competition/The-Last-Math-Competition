**TIL-10 ◆** — source: 242_离散几何II_镶嵌与铺砌.md

**English.** Definition: Danzer set denotes R^d in point set D such that point of convex body all contains D of every volume ≥1. Conjecture: Danzer set; of pair every d≥2 exists possesses bounded density and D simultaneously is Delone set and satisfies time bound (namely every volume 1 convex body in t) of growth density O(log^d t).

**中文。** 定义：Danzer 集指 R^d 中点集 D 使得每个体积 ≥1 的凸体都含 D 的点。猜想：对每个 d≥2 存在具有有界密度的 Danzer 集；且可取 D 同时为 Delone 集并满足增长密度 O(log^d t) 的访问时间界（即每个体积 1 凸体在时刻 t 前被访问）。
