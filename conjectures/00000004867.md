**English.** Definition: The Green function is the sum function of return probabilities of the random walk. Conjecture: The Green decay exponent of a two-generator hyperbolic group is related to the boundary dimension by an explicit conversion, with conversion constant one after normalization, verified on lattices. (conversion of the Green exponent and the boundary dimension)
