# 00000006741

**English.** Definition: Infinitely generated tilting is the tilting structure of non-finite generation. Conjecture: The existence spectrum is completely classified by cycle quivers; the parameter of the classification is exponent zero; and the classification table is completely closed for quiver dimension at most three. (cycle-quiver classification of infinite tilting)

**中文。** 定义：无穷生成倾斜为非有限生成的倾斜结构。猜想：存在谱由循环箭图分类完全；且分类的参数为指数为零；且分类表在箭图维度不超过三时完全封闭。（循环箭图无穷倾斜分类）
