**English.** Definition: The algebraic statistics of random generating sets: limits of random monomial models. Conjecture: Saturation of random toric models: the saturation threshold of generic monomial ideals is an explicit integer of exponential scale, below which the model is a full exponential family. (random model saturation threshold)
