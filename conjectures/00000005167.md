# 00000005167

**English.** Definition: A polarized noncommutative Hodge structure carries a positive-definite form. Conjecture: The three-dimensional stability criterion is an explicit inequality of the window interval with the tilt angle, verified at cubic hypersurfaces and complete. (window stability criterion of three-dimensional noncommutative Hodge theory)

**中文。** 定义：极化非交换 Hodge 指带正定形式的结构。猜想：三维的稳定性判据为窗口区间与倾斜角的显式不等式,三次超曲面处验证且判据完全。（非交换三维窗口稳定判据）
