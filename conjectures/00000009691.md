# 00000009691

**English.** Definition: The algebraic approximation spectrum of transcendental numbers: the exact structure of approximation exponents by degree-d algebraic numbers of a transcendental alpha. Conjecture: The best exponent of degree-d algebraic approximation of e is exactly d+1, the exponent spectrum has no gaps with each exponent attained by explicit Pade approximations; and the spectrum of pi is isomorphic to that of e, the isomorphism given by elliptic theta approximation. (algebraic approximation spectrum of e)

**中文。** 定义：超越数的代数逼近谱：次数 d 代数数对超越数 α 的逼近指数的精确结构。猜想：e 的 d 次代数逼近的最佳指数恰为 d+1，指数谱无间隙且每个指数由显式 Padé 逼近达到；π 的谱与 e 同构且同构由椭圆 Theta 逼近给出。（e 的代数逼近谱）
