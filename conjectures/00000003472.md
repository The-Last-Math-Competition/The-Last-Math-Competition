# 00000003472

**English.** Definition: Combinatorial interpretations of negative values of the Tutte flow polynomial (e.g. the relation with unsigned bicovers). Conjecture: The value of the flow polynomial at −2 is the correction of the bicover index: flow roots in the interval [−4,0] follow the Beraha accumulation law, with accumulation endpoints equivalent to bicover existence; the flow values at negative integers are cover counts of cellular embeddings. (flow negative value bicover interpretation)

**中文。** 定义：Tutte 流多项式的负值点的组合解释（如与无符号双覆盖的关系）。猜想：流多项式在负二的值为双覆盖指标的修正：流根落在区间 [−4,0] 的分布服从 Beraha 聚集律且聚集端点与双覆盖存在性等价；负整点的流值计数为胞腔嵌入的覆盖数。（流负值双覆盖解释）
