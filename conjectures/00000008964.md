**ISO-14 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: Dressing: constructing new solutions from bare ones. Conjecture: Its kernel has finite rank if and only if the initial data is of N-soliton type, with rank exactly the soliton count. (dressing finite-rank criterion)

**中文。** 定义：dressing 指由裸解构造新解。猜想：其核为有限秩当且仅当初值为 N-孤子型且秩恰为孤子数。（dressing 有限秩判定）
