# 00000008696

**OTW-16 ★** — source: 275_最优传输与Wasserstein几何.md

**English.** Definition: Convexity of transport potentials: regularity of c-convex functions. Conjecture: The minimal regularity of c-convexity is the Ma-Trudinger-Wang condition A3 (an A3 law); A3 is equivalent to the four-point condition (weak A3, an equivalence law); the breaking of the equivalence gives singularities (a singularity law); and the singularities are classified in the interior regularity theory (a classification law). (MTW A3 four-point singularity classification)

**中文。** 定义：传输位的凸性：c-凸函数的正则。猜想：c-凸的极小正则（Ma–Trudinger–Wang 条件 A3）的（A3 律）；且 A3 的四点条件（弱 A3）的等价（等价律）；等价的破缺（非 A3 的奇点）的（奇点律）；奇点的分类（内部正则性的分类）的（分类律）。
