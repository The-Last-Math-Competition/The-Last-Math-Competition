# 00000002873

**IX-13 ★** — source: 81_微分算子的指标II.md

**English.** Definition: K-theory classes of elliptic operators: the classification of symbol classes. Conjecture: The softening of symbol classification: homotopy classes of maps from the principal symbol space to spheres completely classify elliptic operators, stable under homotopy and compact perturbations; the boundary of stability is the splitting of higher symbols. (symbol classification homotopy stability)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆算子的 K-理论类：象征类的分类。猜想：象征分类的软化：主象征空间到球面映射的同伦类完全分类椭圆算子且分类在同伦与紧扰动下稳定；稳定性的边界为高阶象征的分裂。（象征分类同伦稳定）
