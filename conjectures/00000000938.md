# 00000000938

**English.** Definition: d_n is the diameter of all n-dimensional Banach spaces under the Banach–Mazur distance. Conjecture: The asymptotic d_n = n is exact: d_n/n → 1 and d_n ≥ n·(1−c·log n/n) for all n; the distance between ℓ₁ⁿ and ℓ∞ⁿ is exactly √n (the Goldstine ratio) but they are not the diameter's realizORS — the diameter is attained by the mixed-norm space ℓ₁^{⌊n/2⌋}⊕ℓ∞^{⌈n/2⌉}.

**中文。** 定义：n 维 Banach 空间全体在 Banach–Mazur 距离下的直径 d_n。猜想：d_n = n 的渐近精确成立:d_n/n → 1,且对一切 n,d_n ≥ n·(1−c·log n/n);ℓ₁ⁿ 与 ℓ∞ⁿ 的距离恰为 √n·(Goldstine 比) 而非直径的实现者,直径由混合范数空间 ℓ₁^{⌊n/2⌋}⊕ℓ∞^{⌈n/2⌉} 达到。
