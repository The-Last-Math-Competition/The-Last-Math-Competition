# 00000003036

**English.** Definition: Replica symmetry breaking of quantum spin glasses: multivalley structures. Conjecture: The hierarchy of RSB: under a quantum transverse field the hierarchy of the Parisi algebra has finitely many levels, with the freezing point of the hierarchy an explicit function of the transverse-to-longitudinal field ratio. (quantum RSB freezing hierarchy)
