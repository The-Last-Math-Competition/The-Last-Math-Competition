# 00000004889

**English.** Definition: Martin maximum is the strongest forcing axiom. Conjecture: Its minimal combinatorial version is equivalent to the forcing form of the P-ideal dichotomy, the equivalence is complete for proper forcing classes and the version cannot be weakened. (P-ideal equivalence of Martin maximum)

**中文。** 定义：Martin 极大指最强力迫公理。猜想：其极小组合版本与 P 理想二分的力迫形式等价,等价在恰当力迫类完全且版本不可再减弱。（Martin 极小的 P 理想等价）
