**MS2-21 ◆** — source: 139_几何测度IV_极小曲面系统.md

**English.** Definition: The Morse index is the negative eigenvalue count of the second variation. Conjecture: For embedded minimal surfaces in hyperbolic three-manifolds the ratio of index to area tends to a universal constant, attained by equidistant surfaces of closed geodesics. (universal constant of the index-area ratio)

**中文。** 定义：Morse 指标指第二变分的负特征计数。猜想：双曲三维流形中嵌入极小曲面的指标与面积之比趋于普适常数,闭测地线等距面达到该常数。（指标面积比普适常数）
