**English.** Definition: Kra's holomorphic Fredholm determinant is the kernel determinant of the uniformization map. Conjecture: The first k imaginary parts of its zeros are always given by the explicit Selberg zeta spectrum; and the spectral isomorphism is established via the iso-spectrality of the Kra operator with the Laplacian. (Kra determinant Selberg spectrum)
