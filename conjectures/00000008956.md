# 00000008956

**English.** Definition: The AKNS system: the ZS-type spectral problem. Conjecture: The scattering map for norm-repulsive potentials is always injective; and the inverse map is a contraction with constant the reciprocal of the potential norm. (AKNS scattering injective contraction)

**中文。** 定义：AKNS 系统指 ZS 型谱问题。猜想：范数斥型位势的散射映射恒为单射；且逆映射为压缩映射而压缩常数为位势范数的逆。（AKNS 散射单射压缩）
