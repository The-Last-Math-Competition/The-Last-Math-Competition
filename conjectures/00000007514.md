# 00000007514

**English.** Definition: Poincare duality as the perfect pairing of cohomology with compactly supported cohomology. Conjecture: The trace characterization is completely determined by the fundamental class of the trace map; and the characterization carries a sign correction in odd dimension with correction sign minus one. (PD trace fundamental-class sign correction)

**中文。** 定义：Poincaré 对偶为上同调与带紧支撑上同调的完美配对。猜想：对偶的迹刻画由迹映射的基类完全确定；且刻画在奇维数时带符号修正且修正符号为负一。（PD 迹基类符号修正）
