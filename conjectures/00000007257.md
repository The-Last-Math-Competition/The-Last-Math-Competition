# 00000007257

**English.** Definition: The principle of least action with Lagrangian mechanics. Conjecture: The existence of elliptic orbits holds, and the minimizer of the existence is the rotation-number constraint. (rotation-constrained least-action minimization)

**中文。** 定义：最小作用原理：Lagrange 力学。猜想：最小作用的极小：椭圆轨道的存在且在的极小为转数约束。（最小作用转数约束椭圆轨道存在极小）
