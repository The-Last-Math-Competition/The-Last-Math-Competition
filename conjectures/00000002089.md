# 00000002089

**SY-09 ★** — source: 55_辛几何与镜像对称.md

**English.** Conjecture: In monotone symplectic manifolds, the existence of index-k monomial Lagrangians is bounded by k ≤ 3; monomial Lagrangians of index 4 and above do not exist (by the arithmetic rigidity obstruction of spectral values).

**中文。** 猜想：单调辛流形中指数 k 的单项式拉格朗日的存在性受 k≤3 的上界;指数 4 以上的单项式拉格朗日不存在(由谱值的算术刚性障碍)。
