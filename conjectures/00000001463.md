# 00000001463

**CX-13 △** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Conjecture: The supremum of the projection constants of ℓ_pⁿ over p ∈ [1, ∞] is c√n uniformly (with an explicit formula for c), attained asymptotically by both ℓ₁ⁿ and ℓ∞ⁿ (a universal upper bound for projection constants).

**中文。** 猜想：ℓ_pⁿ 的投影常数的上确界为 c√n 对 p ∈ [1, ∞] 一致(c 公式),且由 ℓ₁ⁿ 与 ℓ∞ⁿ 同时渐近达到(投影常数的普适上界)。
