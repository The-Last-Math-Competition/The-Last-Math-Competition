# 00000003526

**English.** Definition: Combinatorial principles of large ordinals: trees and partitions. Conjecture: The ordinal version of the tree lemma: the vanishing of descending chains of ordinal trees is well-foundedness, with the rank of well-foundedness the ordinal height. (ordinal tree well-founded rank height vanishing)
