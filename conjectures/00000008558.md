# 00000008558

**English.** Definition: The embedding dimension of a lattice is the minimal dimension of a Boolean lattice containing it as a sublattice. Conjecture: The embedding dimension is at most |J(L)| (J the join-irreducible elements); and the embedding dimension of a finite lattice equals its minimal number of generators. (lattice embedding dimension formula)

**中文。** 定义：格的嵌入维数指其作为子格嵌入布尔格的最小维。猜想：嵌入维数 ≤ |J(L)|（J 为 join 不可约元）；且有限格的嵌入维数等于其极小生成元数。（格嵌入维公式）
