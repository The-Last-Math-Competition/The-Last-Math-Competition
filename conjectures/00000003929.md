# 00000003929

**English.** Definition: The pearl complex is the complex formed by holomorphic chains of pearls on an embedded Lagrangian. Conjecture: After base change of the Novikov coefficients to the complex field, pearl homology is canonically isomorphic to HF(L, L), with isomorphism constant 1 and independent of the choice of embedding. (canonicity of pearl base change)

**中文。** 定义：Pearl complex 指嵌入 Lagrange 上全纯珠链构成的复形。猜想：pearl 同调在 Novikov 系数基变换到复数域后典范同构于 HF(L,L),同构常数为 1 且不依赖嵌入选取。（pearl 基变换典范性）
