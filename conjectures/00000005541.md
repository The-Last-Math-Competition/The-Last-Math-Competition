**English.** Definition: The Riemann-Hilbert correspondence is the equivalence of regular modules with perverse sheaves. Conjecture: The monodromy correspondence multiplicity table is given explicitly by the normal forms of Stokes matrices, closed at rank three by exceptional types, with multiplicity the multiplier dimension. (Stokes normal-form multiplicity table of monodromy)
