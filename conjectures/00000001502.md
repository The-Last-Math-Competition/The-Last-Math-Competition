# 00000001502

**English.** Definition: Local rigidity of higher-rank Anosov actions means smooth conjugacy of lattice actions with algebraic models. Conjecture: All counterexamples to rigidity in the non-semisimple group case come from explicit twist lifts; and the lift order of each counterexample divides the central rank of the group. (complete classification of twist-lift counterexamples)

**中文。** 定义：高阶 Anosov 作用的局部刚性指格作用与代数模型的光滑共轭。猜想：非半单群情形的刚性失败反例全部来自显式扭转提升；且每个反例的提升阶整除群的中心秩。（扭转提升反例完全分类）
