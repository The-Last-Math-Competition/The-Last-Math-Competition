**English.** Definition: Nonlocal operators are fractional-order Laplacian-type operators. Conjecture: Their hitting-distribution conversion is the explicit kernel of stable processes, closed in the half-space case, and the conversion preserves scaling. (closed-form kernel conversion of fractional hitting distributions)
