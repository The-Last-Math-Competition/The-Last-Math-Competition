# 00000004282

**English.** Definition: A Whitehead group is a group A with Ext(A, Z) = 0. Conjecture: The construction of a non-free Whitehead group always requires a full omega_1 tower of black boxes, and the lower bound of the tower height is provable in ZFC. (tower height lower bound of the Whitehead black box)

**中文。** 定义：Whitehead 群指 Ext(A,Z) 为零的群。猜想：非自由 Whitehead 群的构造恒需完整的 ω₁ 高黑盒塔；且塔高下界在 ZFC 可证。（Whitehead 黑盒塔高下界）
