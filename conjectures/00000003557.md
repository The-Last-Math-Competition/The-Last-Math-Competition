# 00000003557

**GF2-07 ★** — source: 104_几何泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The geometric side of Dvoretzky's theorem: high-dimensional ball sections. Conjecture: Approximate Euclidean ball sections: middle- and low-dimensional sections of arbitrary high-dimensional balls are nearly Euclidean, with the closeness dimension the logarithm law. (ball section Euclidean logarithm closeness law)

**中文。** 定义：Dvoretzky 定理的几何侧：高维球截面。猜想：球截面的近似欧氏：任意高维球体的中低维截面接近欧氏球且接近的维数为对数律。（球截面欧氏对数接近律）
