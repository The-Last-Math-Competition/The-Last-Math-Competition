# 00000000810

**English.** Definition: Kirchhoff eigenvalues of quantum graphs are the spectra of metric graphs with Kirchhoff conditions. Conjecture: Among graphs of fixed total length and vertex count, the minimum of the first eigenvalue is attained by the star graph; and the extremizer is strictly unique for more than three vertices. (quantum graph star extremizer)

**中文。** 定义：量子图的 Kirchhoff 特征值为度量图上带基尔霍夫条件的谱。猜想：固定总长与顶点数的图中第一特征值的最小值由星图达成；且极值的唯一性在顶点数三以上严格。（量子图星图极值）
