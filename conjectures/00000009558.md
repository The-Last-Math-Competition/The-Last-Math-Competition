**TCH-08 ★** — source: 304_Teichmuller动力.md

**English.** Definition: Veech surfaces are translation surfaces whose Veech group is a lattice; the Veech group is a discrete subgroup of SL(2,R), and the relation between its order and the genus was the subject of the original Veech conjecture. Conjecture: The order of the Veech group of a genus-g Veech surface with n marked points is at most 4g + 2n + 2, with equality attained only by (2,3,infinity) triangle-type lattice surfaces; and the maximal order of non-triangle type drops to 4g + 2n - 4. (Veech group order upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：Veech 曲面为 Veech 群为格的平移曲面；Veech 群是 SL(2,R) 的离散子群，其阶与亏格的关系是原 Veech 猜想主题。猜想：亏格 g 带 n 个标记点的 Veech 曲面的 Veech 群阶 ≤ 4g+2n+2，等号仅由 (2,3,∞) 三角型格曲面取得；非三角型的最大阶降到 4g+2n−4。（Veech 群阶上界）
