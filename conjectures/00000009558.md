**English.** Definition: Veech surface is Veech group is translation surface; Veech group of lattice is discrete subgroup, its order of SL(2,R) and relations of genus is Veech conjecture topic. Conjecture: Veech group order ≤ 4g+2n+2, only of Veech surface of genus g n denote point by (2,3,∞) three angle type lattice surface attains most large order 4g+2n−4 of; non- three angle type. (Veech group order upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：Veech 曲面为 Veech 群为格的平移曲面；Veech 群是 SL(2,R) 的离散子群，其阶与亏格的关系是原 Veech 猜想主题。猜想：亏格 g 带 n 个标记点的 Veech 曲面的 Veech 群阶 ≤ 4g+2n+2，等号仅由 (2,3,∞) 三角型格曲面取得；非三角型的最大阶降到 4g+2n−4。（Veech 群阶上界）
