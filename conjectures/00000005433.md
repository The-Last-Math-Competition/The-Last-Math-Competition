# 00000005433

**English.** Definition: The boundary convergence theorem concerns nontangential limits of boundary values. Conjecture: The convergence domain of higher-dimensional annular domains is characterized explicitly by the critical exponent of the approach angle, which is one half, and at the critical value there exists an explicit divergent harmonic nova. (critical approach-angle characterization of convergence domains)

**中文。** 定义：边界收敛定理指边界值的非切向极限。猜想：高维环型域的收敛域由切角的临界指数显式刻画,指数二分之一且临界处存在发散的显式调和新星。（临界切角收敛域刻画）
