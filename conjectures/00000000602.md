# 00000000602

**KT-02 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: V_K is the Jones polynomial, ω a primitive fourth root of unity. Conjecture: For every alternating knot K, |V_K(ω)|² divides det(K)², and the quotient det(K)²/|V_K(ω)|² equals the square of |V_K(i)|.

**中文。** 定义：V_K 为 Jones 多项式,ω 为 4 次本原单位根。猜想：对一切交替纽结 K,|V_K(ω)|² 整除 det(K)²,且商 det(K)²/|V_K(ω)|² 为 V_K(i) 的绝对值的平方。
