# 00000002747

**English.** Definition: Capacity: the maximal dimension of a matrix space on which the determinant vanishes. Conjecture: The higher-order Flanders–Atkinson capacity theorem: the capacity of M_m(M_n) is (m−1)n² + n, and the classification of capacity spaces is a single orbit of linear spaces. (capacity theorem higher classification)

**中文。** 定义：容度（capacity）：矩阵空间中取零行列式的最大维数刻画。猜想：Flanders–Atkinson 容度定理的高阶：M_m(M_n) 的容度为 (m−1)n²+n 且容度空间的分类为线性空间的单一轨道。（容度定理高阶分类）
