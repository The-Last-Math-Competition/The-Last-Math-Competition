# 00000001259

**English.** Definition: The Tribonacci word is the fixed point of the substitution a→ab, b→ac, c→a; the spectral hull of its substitution matrix is the algebraic hull of the spectrum. Conjecture: The spectral hull has dimension 3 (generated by three real eigenvalues); the second eigenvector corresponds to the second-largest eigenvalue and gives an algebraic characterization of the combinatorial correction coefficient 0.54… of the word-frequency vector.

**中文。** 定义：Tribonacci 词为替换 a→ab, b→ac, c→a 的不动点,其替换矩阵的谱壳指谱的代数壳。猜想：谱壳维数为 3(由三个实特征值生成);谱壳的第二特征向量对应次大特征值,给出词频率向量的组合修正系数 0.54… 的代数刻画。
