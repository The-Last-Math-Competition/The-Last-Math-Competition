# 00000004359

**FP2-29 ★** — source: 130_泛函积分与路径测度.md

**English.** Definition: The saddle-point approximation is the infinite-dimensional extension of the stationary phase method. Conjecture: Its error exponent is the explicit power 3/2 of the reciprocal of the second variation of the phase, drops to the 1/2 power at resonant critical points, and the constants at both places are universal. (constants of the saddle-point error resonance degradation)

**中文。** 定义：鞍点近似指驻相法的无穷维推广。猜想：其误差指数为相位二阶变差倒数的 3/2 显式幂,在共振临界点处降为 1/2 幂且两处常数均普适。（鞍点误差共振降阶常数）
