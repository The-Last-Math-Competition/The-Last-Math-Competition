# 00000009032

**MFH-22 ★** — source: 286_半整权模形式.md

**English.** Definition: The Waldspurger correspondence: the square-twist correspondence between half-integral weight coefficients and twisted L-values. Conjecture: The square-twist sums of coefficients are always class number functionals; and the constant of the functional equals the real period of the corresponding elliptic curve. (square-twist class number elliptic period)

**中文。** 定义：Waldspurger 对应为半整权系数与扭 L 值的平方扭对应。猜想：系数的平方扭和恒为类数泛函；泛函的常数等于相应椭圆曲线的实周期。（平方扭类数椭圆周期）
