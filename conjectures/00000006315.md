**English.** Definition: The van der Waerden lower bound is the classical lower bound of the permanent. Conjecture: The improvement for block-diagonal support is product type and optimal, the combination of the optimality being the permanental extremum. (permanental optimal support-block improvement)

**中文。** 定义：van der Waerden 下界为永久的经典下界。猜想：块对角支撑的改进为积型且积最优；且最优的组合为积和式极值。（支撑块改进积和式最优）
