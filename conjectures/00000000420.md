**SA-20 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: A jeu de taquin sliding path is the sequence of cells swept by the vacancy during sliding; its angle is the argument of the displacement vector of the path. Conjecture: The angular distribution of sliding paths from a uniformly random tableau converges to the density ρ(θ) = (2/π)·cos θ·(1−sin θ)^{1/2} on θ ∈ [−π/2, π/2], an explicit Cauchy-type distribution determined by the tangent directions of the limit shape.

**中文。** 定义：jeu de taquin 滑动路径指空格在表的滑动中扫过的格子序列,其角度指路径位移向量的辐角。猜想：均匀随机表出发的滑动路径的角度分布收敛到密度 ρ(θ) = (2/π)·cos θ·(1−sin θ)^{1/2},θ∈[−π/2,π/2],即由极限形状切向的 Cauchy 型分布显式给出。
