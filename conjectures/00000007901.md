# 00000007901

**English.** Definition: The low-height spectrum of Maass forms: the n-th small value r_n of the spectral parameter r of Maass-Hecke eigenforms on modular curves with Laplace eigenvalue lambda = 1/4 + r^2. Conjecture: The smallest value r_1 = 9.5337... is the unique eigenvalue in the isolated height interval [0, r_1 + 1/4] (rigidity of the first gap); and the distribution of the gap ratio (r_{k+1} - r_k)/log r_k of the k-th eigenvalues converges to the exponential distribution (a random-matrix-type level spacing law with GUE local statistics). (Maass first gap rigidity and GUE spacing)

**中文。** 定义：Maass 形式的低高度谱：模曲线上 Laplace 特征值 λ=1/4+r² 的 Maass-Hecke 特征形式，其 r 的第 n 个小值 r_n。猜想：最小 r_1 = 9.5337…对应的特征值为孤立高度区间 [0, r_1+1/4] 内唯一（第一间隙的刚性）；且第 k 个本征值的间隙比 (r_{k+1}-r_k)/log r_k 的分布收敛到指数分布（类随机矩阵的水平间距律，局部统计 GUE）。
