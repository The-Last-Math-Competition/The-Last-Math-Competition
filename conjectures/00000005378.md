**English.** Definition: Separability is the property of a subgroup being separated by finite quotients. Conjecture: The residual bound of surface subgroups is an explicit exponential expression of the word length, given by the combinatorial lemma of separable growth, and attained at arithmetic lattices. (word-length exponential bound of surface residuality)
