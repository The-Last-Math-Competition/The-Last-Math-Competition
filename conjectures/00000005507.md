# 00000005507

**English.** Definition: A symmetric queue is the symmetrized structure of first-come-first-served. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the joint ratio of arrival rate and service rate, degenerating to one in the single-class case, and it preserves the stationary product. (joint-ratio conversion constant of symmetric queues)

**中文。** 定义：对称队列指首来先服务的对称化结构。猜想：换算常数为到达率与服务速率的联合比例显式,单类时退化为一且保持平稳积。（对称队列联合比例换算常数）
