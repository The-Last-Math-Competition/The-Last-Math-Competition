# 00000008641

**INP-21 ★** — source: 273_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: Koornwinder denotes parameter Macdonald. Conjecture: kernel of plane interpolation is explicit product; and combinatorial of product is dual. (Koornwinder dual product interpolation kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Koornwinder 指双参数 Macdonald 推广。猜想：平面插值的核为显式双积；且双积的组合为根对偶。（Koornwinder 根对偶双积插值核）
