# 00000008641

**English.** Definition: Koornwinder polynomials are the two-parameter Macdonald generalization. Conjecture: The kernel of plane interpolation is an explicit double product; and the combination of the double product is root duality. (Koornwinder root dual double product interpolation kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Koornwinder 指双参数 Macdonald 推广。猜想：平面插值的核为显式双积；且双积的组合为根对偶。（Koornwinder 根对偶双积插值核）
