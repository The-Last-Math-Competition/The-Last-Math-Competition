# 00000002122

**English.** Definition: The scaling limit of SAWs is their convergence to SLE_{8/3}. Conjecture: The convergence holds; the Hölder regularity exponent of the one-point driving function of SLE_{8/3} has the exact value 1/2 (with zero error); the driving exponent is exactly 1/2.

**中文。** 定义：SAW 的标度极限指其收敛到 SLE_{8/3}。猜想：收敛成立;SLE_{8/3} 的单点驱动函数的 Hölder 正则指数的精确值为 1/2-(误差 0);驱动指数恰 1/2。
