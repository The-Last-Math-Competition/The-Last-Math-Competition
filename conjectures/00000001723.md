**DG-03 ★** — source: 43_丢番图几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Torsion-anomalous points are positive-dimensional intersections of C with nontrivial torsion subvarieties (with larger height infimum than ordinary points). Conjecture: The uniform lower bound of the Néron–Tate height of tor-anomalous points is c·max{ĥ(A), 2}; the bound is optimal (in the semisimple case it follows explicitly from the Mumford–Tate group structure).

**中文。** 定义：挠异常点指 C 与非平凡挠子簇的正维交点(高度下确界比常点更大)。猜想：tor-anomalous 点的 Néron–Tate 高的一致下界为 c·max{ĥ(A),2};界为最优(半格情形可由 Mumford–Tate 群结构显式)。
