**GA-30 △** — source: 57_代数图论II.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral Loewner inverse problem seeks symmetric nonnegative matrices. Conjecture: Solvability is decided by a polyhedral condition on adjacent eigenvalue differences; and the kernel of the condition is diagonal splitting. (Loewner diagonal-splitting difference polyhedron criterion)

**中文。** 定义：谱的 Loewner 逆问题求对称非负矩阵。猜想：可解性由相邻特征值差的多面体条件；且条件的核为对角拆分。（Loewner 对角拆分差多面体判据）
