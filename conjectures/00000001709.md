# 00000001709

**English.** Definition: The joining of the diagonal intersection of the horocycle flow with a skew flow concerns the classification of their joint closures. Conjecture: The joining is governed explicitly by the equation e^{2t} = e^{βs} (β the slope); beyond the known Ratner classification, in the two-dimensional case the joining set is an explicit hyperbola family.

**中文。** 定义：horocycle 流与斜流的对角交的连接指其联合闭包分类。猜想：连接由方程 e^{2t}=e^{βs} 显式控制(β 为斜率);除已知 Ratner 分类外,二维情形的连接集为显式双曲线族。
