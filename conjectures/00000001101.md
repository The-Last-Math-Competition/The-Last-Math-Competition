# 00000001101

**LT-01 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials P_{x,w}(q) of Weyl groups. Conjecture: For every root system there exist x, w such that the leading coefficient of P_{x,w} contains an odd prime factor beyond powers of two (diversity of prime factors of KL coefficients).

**中文。** 定义：Kazhdan–Lusztig 多项式 P_{x,w}(q)(Weyl 群)。猜想：对每个根系,存在 x, w 使 P_{x,w} 的首项系数为 2 的指数以外的奇素因子(KL 系数的素因子多样)。
