# 00000005418

**HM2-08 ★** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: The dimension supremum is the maximal possible measure dimension over all domains. Conjecture: The supremum for planar domains is two and unattainable, the approaching sequence given by an explicit family of nested slits, with dimension deficit exponentially small. (unattainable dimension supremum two)

**中文。** 定义：维数上确界指一切域的最大可能测度维数。猜想：平面域的上确界为二且不可达,逼近序列由嵌套裂隙的显式族给出且维数亏为指数小。（维数上确界二不可达）
