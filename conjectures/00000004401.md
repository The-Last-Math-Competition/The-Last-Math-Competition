**GL2-11 ★** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: A p-adic graphon is a graph-limit object taking values in a p-adic compact group. Conjecture: The base-change constant to real-valued graphons is an explicit p-power given by the Haar measure normalization factor, and the conversion is injective on kernels with values in compact-open subgroups. (p-adic base-change power injectivity)

**中文。** 定义：p-进 graphon 指取值于 p 进紧群的图限对象。猜想：其到实值 graphon 的基变换常数为 Haar 测度归一因子的显式 p 幂,换算在紧开子群值核上单射。（p-进基变换幂次单射）
