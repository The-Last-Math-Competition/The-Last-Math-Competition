**SD3-04 ◆** — source: 199_几何测度V_对称差问题.md

**English.** Definition: The classical strengthened type is the bridge-type strengthening of the isoperimetric inequality. Conjecture: The bridge term of the deficit is bounded below by the squared difference of the circumradius and the inradius, optimal at the disk, and the optimal constant is pi squared over four. (Bonnesen bridge-term disk optimality)

**中文。** 定义：经典强化型为等周不等式的桥型加强。猜想：亏格的桥项由外接与内切圆差的平方下界给出且圆盘处最优；且最优的常数为四分之π平方。（Bonnesen 桥项圆盘最优）
