# 00000001146

**English.** Definition: The harmonic forms of a compact semisimple Lie group G are the complement of the multiplication by the Killing metric in the de Rham complex. Conjecture: The graded dimension of harmonic forms equals that of the cohomology H^*(G), and the dimension table of harmonic representatives agrees with the Poincare polynomial of the exterior algebra Λ(odd-degree generators), with weights given by the root-system exponents.

**中文。** 定义：紧半单李群 G 的谐和形式指 de Rham 复形中与 Killing 度量的乘法补空间。猜想：谐和形式的分次维数等于 G 的模空间 H^*(G) 的分维数,且谐和代表元的维数表与 G 的外代数 Λ(奇度生成元) 的 Poincaré 多项式一致,权由根系指数给出。
