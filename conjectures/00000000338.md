# 00000000338

**English.** Definition: Restricted-digit Khinchin theory considers denominators whose decimal expansions omit one digit. Conjecture: In this model, the critical exponent shifts from one to the explicit value log9/log10; the tightness of the shift is locked by the dimension of the missing-digit Cantor set, and the shifted value is optimal. (missing-one-digit critical exponent)

**中文。** 定义：限制数字 Khinchin 指分母仅使用缺一个数字的十进展开。猜想：该模型的临界指数从一移位到 log9 除以 log10 的显式值；移位的紧性由缺数字 Cantor 集的维数锁定且移位值为最优。（缺一数字临界指数）
