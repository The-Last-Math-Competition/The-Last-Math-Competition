**OMN-17 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The tamed law of the iterated logarithm: the taming constant LIL_def of the LIL for definable martingales (martingale difference sequences over definable fields). Conjecture: The upper-limit constant of the LIL is sqrt 2 (tamed preservation of the classical constant); the tamed tail bound (for definable sub-Gaussian martingales) is exp(-c t^2) with a definable constant; the optimal value of c (tamed Hoeffding) is a definable version of 1/(2 sigma^2), and the failure of taming (degeneration of c into a non-definable function) occurs only when the dimension of the support of the martingale differences blows up (unbounded cell depth). (tamed LIL constant preservation)

**中文。** 定义：驯化的重对数律：可定义鞅（可定义域上的鞅差序列）的重对数律的驯化常数 LIL_def。猜想：LIL 的上极限常数为 √2（经典常数的驯化保持）；且驯化的尾概率界（可定义子高斯鞅）为 exp(-c t²) 的可定义常数；常数 c 的最优值（驯 Hoeffding）为 1/(2σ²) 的可定义化，驯化的失效（c 的退化为非可定义函数）仅在鞅差的支撑的维数爆炸（cell 深度无界）时发生。
