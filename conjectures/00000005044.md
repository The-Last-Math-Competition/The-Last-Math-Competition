**English.** Definition: Neighborhood equivalence is the equivalence of structures with equal ball counts. Conjecture: The equivalence class count for the two-ball case is an explicit expression of the generating function of ball isomorphism types, closed for all degrees and termwise computable. (generating function of two-ball neighborhood equivalence classes)
