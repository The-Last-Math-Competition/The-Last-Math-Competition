# 00000003836

**English.** Definition: The signature algorithm denotes the classical process of successively deleting adjacent descent pairs from a reading word of a cylindrical embedding to obtain the i-signature. Conjecture: The number of plus signs in the output signature is invariant under any cyclic rotation of the reading word and always equals the string parameter ε_i. (signature rotation invariance)

**中文。** 定义：签名算法指从柱面嵌入读字中依序删去相邻降对得到 i-签名的经典过程。猜想：输出签名中正号的个数与读字起点的任何轮换选取无关,且恒等于字符串参数 ε_i。（签名轮换不变性）
