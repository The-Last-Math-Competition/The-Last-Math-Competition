**English.** Definition: The Rankin-Selberg L-function is the L-function of the product of two automorphic representations. Conjecture: The optimal convexity-breaking exponent in weight four is the explicit one third, matched by the fourth-moment lower bound and not improvable. (optimal weight-four RS subconvex exponent)
