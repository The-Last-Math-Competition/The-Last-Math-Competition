**English.** Definition: The Seidel representation is the group homomorphism from the symplectic loop group to the automorphism group of quantum cohomology. Conjecture: The rank of the homological image of the Seidel representation equals exactly the rank of the symplectic isotropic-area homology of the manifold, and its kernel is exactly the identity component of the Hamiltonian group. (Seidel image rank and area homology)

**中文。** 定义：Seidel 表示指辛回路群到量子同调自同构群的群同态。猜想：Seidel 表示的同调像的秩恰等于流形的辛迷向面积同调的秩,且核恰为 Hamilton 群的单位分量。（Seidel 像秩-面积同调）
