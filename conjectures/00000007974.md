# 00000007974

**CD3-14 ★** — source: 251_编码理论III_列表解码与局部译码.md

**English.** Definition: The combinatorial rigidity of code shells: the structure of the shell graphs of the weight shells (Hamming spheres) of simplex codes (induced graphs of codewords of equal weight). Conjecture: The induced graph on the weight-w shell of a simplex code is a strongly regular graph with parameters (v,k,lambda,mu) explicit in (n,w); and the spectral gap of the bipartite graphs between different-weight shells equals the smallest modulus of zeros outside [-2,2] of the Jacobi polynomial of the absolute weight difference; this spectral formula gives the complete spectral characterization of the expander constants of shell graphs (a proof path for the Ramanujan property of simplex codes). (simplex shell strongly regular spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：码的壳层的组合刚性：单纯形码的权壳（Hamming 球壳）的壳层图的结构（同权码字的诱导图）。猜想：单纯形码的权 w 壳层的诱导图为强正则图，其参数 (v,k,λ,μ) 显式由 (n,w) 给出；且非壳层（不同权）间的 bipartite 图的谱间隙等于权差的绝对值的 Jacobi 多项式在 [-2,2] 外的最小零点模；该谱公式给出壳层图的 expander 常数的完整谱刻画（单纯形码的 Ramanujan 性的证明路径）。
