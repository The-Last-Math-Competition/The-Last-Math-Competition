# 00000003564

**GF2-14 ★** — source: 104_几何泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: Smooth points of Banach spaces: the differentiability of norms. Conjecture: The density of smooth points: smooth points of separable spaces are dense, with the obstruction to density the nonseparable conjugate. (smooth point conjugate nonseparable density obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：Banach 空间的光滑点：范数的可微性。猜想：光滑点的稠密：可分空间的光滑点稠密且稠密的障碍为不可分共轭。（光滑点共轭不可分稠密障碍）
