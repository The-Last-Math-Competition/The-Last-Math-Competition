**English.** Definition: Inflationary fixed points are the layer-by-layer growing fixed-point operators, and partial fixed points are the backtracking iteration version. Conjecture: The two are always separated in expressive power by linear-order counting sentences, with the partial side polynomially expressible and the inflationary side requiring superpolynomial rounds. (inflationary-partial fixed-point separating sentences)

**中文。** 定义：膨胀不动点指逐层增长的不动点算子，偏不动点指可逆迭代版本。猜想：两者的表达力非交叠恒由线性序计数句子分离；且偏侧多项式可表达而膨胀侧需超多项式轮。（膨胀-偏不动点分离句）
