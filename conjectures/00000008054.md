# 00000008054

**PRF-04 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The modal algebra of provability logic: the free algebra F_GL(n) of the algebraic duality (Magari algebras) of the countable class of frames of the Godel-Lob logic GL. Conjecture: The cardinality spectrum of F_GL(n) is countable (finitely generated free Magari algebras are countable); the decidability of its word problem goes through polynomial-time decision of Sigma formulas (the modalization of Sigma_1 completeness) (the failure of P for the word problem being separated from the non-recursiveness of the compactification of Bernstein infinite trees); and the height of the transfinite hierarchy of ultrafilters of F_GL(1) equals the Cantor-Bendixson rank of the notation space of epsilon_0 (a coincidence law of CB rank and proof-theoretic ordinal). (Magari free algebra CB-epsilon_0 coincidence)

**中文。** 定义：可证性逻辑的模态代数：Gödel–Löb 逻辑 GL 的可数框架类的代数对偶（Magari 代数）的自由代数 F_GL(n)。猜想：F_GL(n) 的基数谱为可数（有限生成的自由 Magari 代数可数）；且其字问题的可解性可通过 Sigma 公式（Sigma_1-完备性的模态化）的多项式时间判定（字问题的 P 失效与 Bernstein 无穷树的紧致化的非递归性分离）；F_GL(1) 的素滤子的超限层级的高度为 ε₀ 的记号空间的 Cantor-Bendixson 秩（CB 秩与证明论序数重合律）。
