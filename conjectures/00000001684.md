# 00000001684

**English.** Definition: p_{2t}(v,v) is the return probability of the random walk on a weighted vertex-transitive graph. Conjecture: Its subexponential decay is controlled by the tail of the spectral measure μ of the generating set: p_{2t} ~ exp(−t^{1/3}·L(t)), where L is an explicit logarithmic correction with a regularly-varying I^α-type behavior of μ at the spectral edge.

**中文。** 定义：加权点传递图上随机游走的返回概率 p_{2t}(v,v)。猜想：其次指数衰减由生成集的谱测度 μ 的尾部控制:p_{2t}∼exp(−t^{1/3}·L(t)),L 为 μ 在谱边缘 I^α 型变慢函数的显式对数修正。
