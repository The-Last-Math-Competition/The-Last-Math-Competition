# 00000002186

**English.** Conjecture: There exist n-vertex graphs with clique number and independence number both ≤ n^{1/2−ε} (with an explicit ε > 0); the attainable value of ε is 10^{-4} (from known randomized lower bounds of algebraic constructions); explicit constructions come from norm graphs over finite fields.

**中文。** 猜想：存在 n 点图使团数与独立数同时 ≤n^{1/2-ε}(ε>0 公式);ε 的可达到值为 10^{-4}(由代数构造的已知随机下界);显式构造由有限域上的范数图。
