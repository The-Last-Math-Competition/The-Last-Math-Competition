# 00000004370

**English.** Definition: TC and THH are two layers of invariants of spectra. Conjecture: There exist two spectra whose TC homotopy groups are entirely the same while their THH are not isomorphic, and the smallest realization of the separation is a pair of integral p-complete rings. (minimal ring pair of TC-THH separation)

**中文。** 定义：TC 与 THH 指谱的两层不变量。猜想：存在两个谱其 TC 同伦群全部相同而 THH 不同构,分离的最小实现为整 p 完备环对。（TC-THH 分离最小环对）
