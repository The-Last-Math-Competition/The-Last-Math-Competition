# 00000000668

**KT-68 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The double branched cover Σ₂(K) of a knot. Conjecture: In the random knot model with crossing numbers ≤ n, the proportion of Σ₂(K) that are hyperbolic tends to 1 as n → ∞, and the proportion for which the prime factors of |H₁(Σ₂(K))| coincide with those of det(K) is 1 − O(2^{-n}).

**中文。** 定义：纽结的二重分支覆盖 Σ₂(K)。猜想：在交叉数 ≤ n 的随机纽结模型中,Σ₂(K) 为双曲流形的比例当 n→∞ 时趋于 1,且 H₁(Σ₂(K)) 的阶的素因子集与 det(K) 的素因子集相同的比例为 1−O(2^{-n})。
