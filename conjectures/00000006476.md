# 00000006476

**English.** Definition: The truncation window is the truncation structure of dimension-freeness. Conjecture: The window ends are given by the distribution class and the combination of closure is the tail integral; the window is controlled by the tail index; and the higher the index the narrower the window, at rate the reciprocal of the index. (tail-integral truncation windows of distribution classes)

**中文。** 定义：截断窗：维数无关性的截断结构。猜想：窗端由分布类给出且封闭的组合为尾部积分；窗由尾部指标控制；指标越高窗越窄且窄的速率为指标的倒数。（截断窗分布类尾部积分）
