# 00000005179

**English.** Definition: Differential graded tilting lifts tilting to the graded level. Conjecture: The conversion constant of its endomorphism algebra is an explicit product of the morphism spectra, one in the coherent case, preserving quasi-isomorphism types. (morphism-spectrum conversion product of graded tilting)
