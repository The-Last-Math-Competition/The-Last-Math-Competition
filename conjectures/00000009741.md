# 00000009741

**English.** Definition: Wilf-Zeilberger certificates: existence criteria for machine proofs of hypergeometric identities. Conjecture: Summation identities of hypergeometric terms have WZ certificates if and only if the term ratios are rational functions of order at most two; the minimal order of the certificate is a function of the summation index difference, decidable in polynomial time by Gosper's algorithm. (WZ certificate existence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Wilf–Zeilberger 证书：超几何恒等式的机器证明的存在判据。猜想：超几何项的和式恒等式有 Wilf–Zeilberger 证书当且仅当项的比值为有理函数且阶不超过两项；证书的最小阶为求和指标差的函数并可由 Gosper 算法在多项式时间判定。（WZ 证书存在判据）
