# 00000006185

**English.** Definition: Extremal graphs are maximal structure classes with forbidden subgraphs. Conjecture: The structure with complete bipartite forbidden graphs is characterized by spectra with explicit coefficient, and the combination of the characterization is the eigenvector. (eigenvector spectral characterization of bipartite extremality)

**中文。** 定义：极端图为禁子图的极大结构类。猜想：完全二部禁图的结构由谱刻画且系数显式；且刻画的组合为特征向量。（禁二部极值谱特征向量）
