# 00000006873

**English.** Definition: The von Neumann entropy of free group factors with noncommutative Shannon theory. Conjecture: The noncommutative version of the entropy holds, the combination of the version being the subfactor index. (subfactor-index noncommutative Shannon entropy)

**中文。** 定义：自由群因子的 von Neumann 熵：非交换 Shannon。猜想：自由的 Shannon：熵的非交换版本且本的组合为子因子指标。（自由群 von-Neumann 熵子因子指标非交换 Shannon 组合自由 Shannon 指标子因子熵指标群）
