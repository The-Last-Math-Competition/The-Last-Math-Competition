**English.** Definition: Approximation on manifolds: the approximation theory of tangent spaces. Conjecture: The curvature deficit of manifold approximation: the deficit of tangent-space approximation is an explicit quadratic form of principal curvatures, with the extremum of the form the sphere. (manifold principal curvature sphere deficit extremum)
