# 00000007143

**English.** Definition: The Galois group of a polytope with realization automorphisms. Conjecture: The Galois group of the volume is a subgroup of the symmetric group, and the minimal group is the quadratic splitting field. (quadratic-splitting realization-volume Galois group)

**中文。** 定义：多面体的 Galois 群：实现的自同构。猜想：实现的 Galois：体积的 Galois 群为对称群的子群且群的极小为二次分裂域。（实现体积 Galois 对称子群二次分裂极小）
