# 00000007659

**English.** Definition: The rays of variable-coefficient hyperbolic operators as high-frequency energy trajectories. Conjecture: The dispersion law of ray tracing is always the explicit integral of coefficient gradients; and the law spectrum is a finite frequency set. (integral-spectrum ray dispersion law)

**中文。** 定义：变系数双曲算子的射线为高频能量轨迹。猜想：射线追踪的色散律恒为系数梯度的显式积分；律谱为有限频率集合。（射线色散律积分谱）
