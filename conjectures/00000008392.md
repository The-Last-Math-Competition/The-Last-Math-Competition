# 00000008392

**MIB-12 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The tower dimension of motivic measures: the recursion depth of arc space measures along nested singularity resolutions. Conjecture: The tower dimension is 0 for smooth varieties and 1 for nodal singularities; for hypersurfaces with isolated singularities it equals the logarithm of the Milnor number; and in general the tower dimension is controlled by the embedding dimension, with the bound optimal. (tower dimension stratified bound)

**中文。** 定义：母题测度的塔维数指弧空间测度沿嵌套奇点分解的递归深度。猜想：塔维数在光滑簇为 0，在节点奇点为 1；塔维数对超孤立奇点等于 Milnor 数的对数；一般奇点的塔维数被嵌入维数控制且界最优。（塔维数分层界）
