# 00000009798

**English.** Definition: Polyhedral geodesics: bend-point counts of discrete shortest paths. Conjecture: The bend-point count of shortest paths between two points on a polyhedron is a linear function of face count and height; the algebraic lengths of shortest paths involve sums of radicals and their comparison is doable in polynomial time. (geodesic bend-point count bound)

**中文。** 定义：多面体测地线：离散最短路径的折点计数。猜想：多面体上两点的最短路径的折点数为面数与高度的线性函数；最短路径的代数长度涉及根式和且其比较可在多项式时间完成。（测地线折点计数界）
