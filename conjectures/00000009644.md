**English.** Definition: The spectrum of random tensors: eigenvalues of a third-order random tensor T are the scalars lambda with T v tensor v tensor v = lambda v, the spectrum being a real algebraic set. Conjecture: The empirical eigenvalue measure of random third-order tensors converges to an explicit limiting measure; the density support is a symmetric interval with endpoints explicit roots of the hyperdeterminant equation, and the limit measure is characterized as the unique solution of a free moment problem. (tensor spectral limit measure)

**中文。** 定义：随机张量的谱：三阶随机张量 T 的特征值为使 T v⊗v⊗v = λ v 的标量 λ 集合，谱为实代数集。猜想：随机三阶张量的特征值经验测度收敛到显式极限测度；密度支撑为对称区间且端点为超行列式方程的显式根，极限测度由自由矩问题的唯一解刻画。（张量谱极限测度）
