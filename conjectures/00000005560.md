# 00000005560

**DM2-30 △** — source: 170_表示论的几何II_D模.md

**English.** Definition: The characteristic exponents and the monodromy spectrum are two local layers. Conjecture: There exist two modules with identical characteristic exponents but different monodromy spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of Stokes constants. (same exponents-different monodromy Stokes separation)

**中文。** 定义：特征指数与单值谱指两层局部分解。猜想：存在两模的一切特征指数相同而单值谱不同,分离由斯托克斯常数的显式对实现。（指数同-单值谱异斯托克斯分离）
