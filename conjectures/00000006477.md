**English.** Definition: Isometric embedding is the distance-preserving map structure. Conjecture: The dimension threshold of spherical isometry is complete and the realization of the threshold is positive-correlation embedding; the threshold is n plus 2; and the tightness of the embedding is the Welch bound. (positive-correlation threshold of spherical isometries)
