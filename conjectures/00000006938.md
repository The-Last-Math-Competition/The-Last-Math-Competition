**English.** Definition: Morita equivalence with the invariance of K. Conjecture: The K-groups of equivalent algebras are isomorphic, the combination of the isomorphism being equivalence functors. (equivalence-functor Morita invariance of K)

**中文。** 定义：Morita 等价：K 的不变性。猜想：Morita 的 K 不变：等价的 K 群同构且构的组合为等价函子。（Morita 等价 K 群同构等价函子不变性组合等价算子）
