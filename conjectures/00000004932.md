# 00000004932

**English.** Definition: The p-local sphere homotopy consists of the stable homotopy groups localized at the prime p. Conjecture: The three-primary exponent upper bound can be improved to an explicit one-quarter-type density in the stem, attained by periodic families and consistent with the gaps of the spectral sequence. (density improvement of three-local homotopy exponents)

**中文。** 定义：p 局部球面同伦指素 p 局部化的稳定同伦群。猜想：三进制的指数上界可改进为 stem 的四分之一型显式密度,密度由周期族达到且与谱序列的间隙一致。（三局部同伦指数密度改进）
