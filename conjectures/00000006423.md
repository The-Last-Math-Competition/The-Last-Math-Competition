# 00000006423

**English.** Definition: The functional product inequality is the functional version of measure convolution. Conjecture: The explicit construction of the midpoint function is geometric and equality is attained by the Gauss kernel, the extremality of Gauss being by log-concavity. (Gauss equality of functional midpoints)

**中文。** 定义：泛函乘积不等式为测度卷积的泛函版本。猜想：中点函数的显式构造为几何中点且等号由 Gauss 核取得；且 Gauss 的极值性由对数凹。（泛函中点 Gauss 等号）
