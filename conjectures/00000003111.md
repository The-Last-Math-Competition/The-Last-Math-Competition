**English.** Definition: Boundary homology: the homological rigidity of the boundary. Conjecture: The Cheeger–Gromov invariant of the boundary: the ℓ²-Betti numbers of boundary homology vanish except in the middle dimension, with the exception to vanishing being the dimensional symmetry of the boundary. (boundary l2 middle-dimension exception)
