# 00000004034

**English.** Definition: A brick is a module whose endomorphism ring is the base field, and the brick labeling is the canonical correspondence from tau-tilting modules to bricks. Conjecture: The brick-labeling morphism is a bijection if and only if the algebra is brick-finite; in the general case the minimal dimension of an injectivity counterexample is at most 5. (dimension bound for brick-labeling counterexamples)

**中文。** 定义：brick 指自同态环为基域的模,brick 标记指 τ-倾斜模到 brick 的典范对应。猜想：brick 标记的态射为双射当且仅当代数 brick 有限;一般情形注入性的最小反例维数不超过 5。（brick 标记反例维数界）
