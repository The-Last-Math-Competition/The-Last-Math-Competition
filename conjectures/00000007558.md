**English.** Definition: Prime decomposition with the primality structure of factors. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the uniqueness of prime decomposition and the validity domain of unique decomposition is open; the location of the domain is the index spectrum; and the count of prime factors is logarithmic. (unique-decomposition-domain conversion)
