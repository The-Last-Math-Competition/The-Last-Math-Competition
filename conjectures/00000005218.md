**English.** Definition: The subfactor depth is the diameter-type measure of the principal graph. Conjecture: The depth conversion constant is twice the bijection distance in the graph, the constant is two, and it is verified optimal at tree-type principal graphs. (doubled-distance conversion constant of depth)
