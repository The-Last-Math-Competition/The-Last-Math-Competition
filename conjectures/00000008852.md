# 00000008852

**MON-22 ★** — source: 280_单调算子与算子分裂.md

**English.** Definition: Perturbation stability of monotone operators: continuous dependence of the zero x_delta of a strongly monotone operator A after a norm-delta perturbation. Conjecture: Under small norm perturbations the zero is always continuous with modulus the explicit power delta/gamma of the strong monotonicity constant gamma; the perturbed operator remains maximal monotone exactly when delta does not exceed the strong monotonicity constant of A; and the length of the zero trajectory is at most the integral of delta/gamma, with the limiting curve as gamma tends to 0 being a 1-Lipschitz selection curve. (strong monotone perturbation zero continuity)

**中文。** 定义：单调算子的扰动稳定性：强单调算子 A 加范数 δ 的扰动后零点 x_δ 的连续依赖。猜想：范数小扰动下零点恒连续且连续模由强单调常数 γ 的显式幂 δ/γ 给出；扰动算子保持极大单调的阈值为 δ 不超过 A 的强单调常数；零点轨迹的长度不超过 δ/γ 的积分且在 γ→0 时的极限曲线为 1-Lipschitz 的选择曲线。
