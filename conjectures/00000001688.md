# 00000001688

**English.** Definition: The #P-counting of graph contractions counts the contraction maps between two graphs. Conjecture: The count can be done exactly in O((t+1)^{n}) time on treewidth-t graphs with optimal exponent; the coefficient cannot be compressed to (t+1)^{n−1} (by grid lower bounds).

**中文。** 定义：图收缩的 #P-计数指两图间收缩映射个数。猜想：树宽 t 图上的计数可在 O((t+1)^{n}) 时间内精确完成且指数最优;系数不可压至 (t+1)^{n−1}(由网格下界)。
