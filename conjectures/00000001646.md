# 00000001646

**English.** Definition: The l²-vanishing of the mapping class group Mod(Σ_g) means the vanishing of the l²-Betti numbers of its cohomology (known). Conjecture: A quantitative form of the vanishing: the dimension of H^i(Mod(Σ_g);Q) decays to zero exponentially in g within the stable range i ≤ (2g−2)/3; the decay constant is −log 2.

**中文。** 定义：映射类群 Mod(Σ_g) 的 l²-消没指其上同调的 l²-Betti 数为零(已知)。猜想：消没的定量形式:H^i(Mod(Σ_g);Q) 在稳定区 i≤(2g−2)/3 的维数以 g 的指数速度衰减趋零;衰减常数为 −log 2。
