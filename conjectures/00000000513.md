# 00000000513

**English.** Definition: A Boij–Söderberg decomposition expresses a Betti table as a cone combination of purity sequences. Conjecture: Every purity sequence is realized by a monomial ideal; and the complete characterization of realizability is given by the matching structure of pure graphs. (monomial realization of purity sequences)

**中文。** 定义：Boij–Söderberg 分解把 Betti 表分解为纯度序列的锥组合。猜想：任何纯度序列可由单项式理想实现；且可实现刻画的完全化为纯图的匹配结构。（纯度序列单项式实现）
