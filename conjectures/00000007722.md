# 00000007722

**English.** Definition: The p-compression mu_p of a measure mu is the distribution induced by compression with a family of free projections of rank ratio p; closure of free infinitely divisible and free selfdecomposable laws under compression is partially known. Conjecture: All p-compressions for p in (0,1) are free infinitely divisible if and only if mu is free selfdecomposable; and there exist laws whose mu_p is free infinitely divisible only for reciprocal-integer p, failing for general p, with the smallest such example having exactly 3 atoms. (compression characterization of free selfdecomposability)

**中文。** 定义：测度 μ 的 p-压缩 μ_p 指以秩比为 p 的自由投影族作压缩后诱导的分布。自由无穷可分律集与自由自分解律集在压缩下的封闭性已有部分结果。猜想：μ 的一切 p∈(0,1) 压缩均为自由无穷可分当且仅当 μ 为自由自分解律；且存在律 μ 使 μ_p 为自由无穷可分仅对倒数整数 p 成立而对一般 p 失效，此类最小例子的原子个数恰为 3。
