**English.** Definition: The transport error is the deviation of the realization from the limit. Conjecture: The error formula in the one-variable case is an explicit expression of the supremum of spectral differences, given by root distances of characteristic polynomials, and exponentially convergent. (root-distance error formula of one-variable transport)
