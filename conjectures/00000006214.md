**English.** Definition: The Gibbs measure with its support structure. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the compact-support index of the measure, the critical-dimension phase transition controlled by field tightness; the tightness index is s greater than d/2; and the threshold dimension of the transition is 3, above which the measure is supported on rough fields. (field-tightness critical threshold conversion)
