# 00000000856

**English.** Definition: Plateau's problem is the variational existence of minimal surfaces. Conjecture: For nonembedded boundaries, the singular set of the minimizer consists of finitely many even-order crossing points; and the local classification of the singularity structure is by half-integer densities. (Plateau singularity structure)

**中文。** 定义：Plateau 问题为极小曲面的变分存在。猜想：非嵌边界的极小元奇点集为有限偶数交叉点；且奇点结构的局部分类为半整数密度。（Plateau 奇点结构）
