# 00000002594

**OL-04 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: The LYM inequality and the Sperner property: maximal antichains of posets. Conjecture: Multiplicative closure of LYM posets: the direct product of two LYM posets retains the LYM property, and the permutation structure of normalized matchings is the braiding of local matchings of the factors; the braiding has genus zero. (LYM multiplicative closure law)

**中文。** 定义：LYM 不等式与 Sperner 性：偏序集的极大反链。猜想：LYM 偏序集的乘性封闭：两个 LYM 偏序集的直积保持 LYM 性且正规匹配的置换结构为积的局部匹配的编织；编织的亏格为零。（LYM 乘性封闭律）
