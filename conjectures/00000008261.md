# 00000008261

**English.** Definition: The regularity index of the Zakai filtering equation (the SPDE of conditional densities under observation noise). Conjecture: zakai_reg (the Sobolev regularity gain of Zakai solutions) is half an order beyond the noise-driven level, 1/2 - alpha (alpha the roughness, a half-order gain law); the long-time behavior of the filtering density (stable filtering) has exponentially convergent ergodic limits (an exponential stability law); and the rate constant is the infimum of the minimal Fisher information of the observation channel (a Fisher rate constant). (Zakai half-order regularity Fisher stability)

**中文。** 定义：Zakai 滤波方程（观测噪声下的条件密度的 SPDE）的解的正则性指标 zakai_reg。猜想：zakai_reg（Zakai 解的 Sobolev 正则性提升）= 噪声驱动的一阶半（1/2-α，α 为粗糙度）（半阶提升律）；且滤波密度的长时间行为（稳定滤波）的遍历极限的收敛速率为指数（指数稳定律）；速率常数为观测通道的最小 Fisher 信息的下确界（Fisher 速率常数）。
