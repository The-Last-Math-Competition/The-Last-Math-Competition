# 00000007599

**English.** Definition: The sampling theory of persistent homology as the sample complexity of topological recovery. Conjecture: The sample complexity of topological recovery is the logarithmic power of the rescaling radius; and the exponent of the power is the homological dimension. (logarithmic topological-recovery sample complexity)

**中文。** 定义：持续同调的采样理论为拓扑恢复样本复杂度。猜想：拓扑恢复的样本复杂度为重标半径的对数幂；且幂的指数为同调维数。（拓扑恢复对数样本复杂度）
