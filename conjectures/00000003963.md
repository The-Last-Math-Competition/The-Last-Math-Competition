**G4-23 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: A cube layer is the set of vertices of fixed weight in the hypercube. Conjecture: The algebraic connectivity of the induced graph of a layer, a Johnson graph, attains its minimum over all layers at l = n/2, of order Theta(log n / n^2). (algebraic connectivity of cube layers)

**中文。** 定义：立方层指超立方体中固定权重的顶点集。猜想：层 ℓ 的诱导图(Johnson 图)的代数连通度在一切层中最小者于 ℓ = n/2 达到,量级为 Θ(log n/n²)。（立方层代数连通度）
