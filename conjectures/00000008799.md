# 00000008799

**NLA-29 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: random kernel approximation is RKHS of random characteristic. Conjecture: error of kernel is characteristic number reciprocal; and kernel of reciprocal is Monte Carlo variance. (random characteristic Monte Carlo variance error)

**中文。** 定义：随机 kernel 逼近为随机特征的 RKHS。猜想：通用核的误差为特征数根号倒数；且倒数的核为 Monte Carlo 方差。（随机特征 Monte Carlo 方差根号误差）
