# 00000007540

**English.** Definition: L-spectra and monodromy spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two sheaf families with the same L-spectrum but different monodromy spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit twist-reflection pair with the same L but different monodromy. (same L-different monodromy twist separation)

**中文。** 定义：L 谱与单值谱指两层。猜想：存在两层族的 L 谱相同而单值谱不同,分离由同 L 异单值的显式扭反射对实现。（L 同-单值异扭反射分离）
