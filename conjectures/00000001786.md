# 00000001786

**English.** Definition: Donnelly–Fefferman type effective very ampleness refers to the explicit factors of L² estimates for jet extension. Conjecture: The effective jet-extension bound for singular metrics on toric varieties is (n+1)log(1/ε); the bound is explicitly controlled by the maximum of the toric Robin function.

**中文。** 定义：Donnelly–Fefferman 型有效非常丰富性指 jet 延拓的 L² 估计的显式因子。猜想：toric 簇上奇异度量的有效 Jet 延拓界为 (n+1)log(1/ε);界由 toric Robin 函数的极大值显式控制。
