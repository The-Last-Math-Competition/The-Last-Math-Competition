# 00000008451

**English.** Definition: The HOMFLY-PT moment: the expectation under difference operators in the two variables of the polynomial. Conjecture: The moments are algebraic numbers; the joint spectrum of two-variable moments is finite and classified by W-algebra eigenvalues; and the leading coefficient is a rational fraction. (algebraicity of HOMFLY moments)

**中文。** 定义：HOMFLY-PT 矩量指该多项式沿两变量的差分算子的期望。猜想：矩量为代数数；双变量矩量联合谱有限且由 W-代数特征值分类；首系数为有理分式。（HOMFLY 矩量代数性）
