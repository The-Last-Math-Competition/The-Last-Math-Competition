**English.** Definition: The pressure regularity domain is the well-posed range of the pressure equation. Conjecture: The lower end of the two-dimensional exponent interval is the critical exponent; smoothness holds above the critical value and degenerates at it. (critical interval regularity of two-dimensional pressure)

**中文。** 定义：压力正则域指压力方程的适定范围。猜想：二维指数区间下端为临界指数；光滑性在超过临界值时成立且临界处退化。（二维压力临界区间正则）
