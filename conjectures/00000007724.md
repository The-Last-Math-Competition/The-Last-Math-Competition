**English.** Definition: The free entropy dimension delta_0(X_1,...,X_m) is defined by Voiculescu's matrix microstate quantities and measures the free entropy codimension of generators of free group factors L(F_m). Conjecture: For the standard generators of L(F_m), delta_0 = m; and for finite generator tuples of L(F_infinity), delta_0 is always either 0 or at least 1, with no values strictly between 0 and 1. (zero-one gap of free entropy dimension)

**中文。** 定义：自由熵维数 δ_0(X_1,…,X_m) 由 Voiculescu 的矩阵微扰量定义，刻画自由群因子 L(F_m) 生成元的自由熵余维。猜想：对 L(F_m) 的标准生成元组，δ_0 = m；进一步对 L(F_∞) 的有限生成组恒有 δ_0 为 0 或至少为 1，即不存在介于 0 与 1 之间的取值。
