# 00000009603

**English.** Definition: Free-boundary minimal surfaces in the unit ball: equatorial disks and critical catenoids are the basic examples; the Fraser-Li classification conjecture characterizes surfaces whose coordinate functions are first eigenfunctions. Conjecture: In B^3 the free-boundary minimal surfaces all of whose coordinate functions are first eigenfunctions are only the equatorial disk and the critical catenoid; this rigid list gains no new members in B^n — no exceptional surfaces in higher dimensions. (Fraser-Li rigid classification)

**中文。** 定义：单位球中自由边界极小曲面：赤道盘与临界悬链面为基本例子；Fraser–Li 分类猜想刻画坐标函数为第一特征函数的曲面。猜想：B^3 中坐标函数均为第一特征函数的自由边界极小曲面只有赤道盘与临界悬链面；该刚性列表在 B^n 中不增新成员，高维无例外曲面。（Fraser-Li 刚性分类）
