**MS2-03 ★** — source: 139_几何测度IV_极小曲面系统.md

**English.** Definition: A triple junction is the common intersection line of three interfaces. Conjecture: Its migration rate constant is an explicit projection of the tension-equilibrium vector of the three interfaces; the constant is zero for equal tensions and a universal coefficient of one third otherwise. (one-third law of triple-junction migration)

**中文。** 定义：triple junction 指三界面的公共交线。猜想：其迁移速率常数为三界面张力平衡向量的显式投影,等张力时常数为零且不等张力时为三分之型普适系数。（triple 迁移速率三分律）
