# 00000008663

**English.** Definition: Divergence div(n) is the divergence behavior of geodesic rays. Conjecture: The classification of divergence functions: linear, exponential, and polynomial (a classification law); the realizable degrees of polynomial divergence are bounded by 2d-1 (a degree bound law); groups with exponential divergence are rigid (hyperbolic type, a rigidity law); and intermediate divergence between polynomial and exponential is unrealizable (an intermediate prohibition law). (divergence classification degree bound prohibition)

**中文。** 定义：压缩谱（divergence）：测地线的分歧行为 div(n)。猜想：div 的函数族的分类：线性、指数与多项式（分类律）；且多项式 divergence 的次数的可实现值 ≤ 2d-1 的界（次界律）；指数 divergence 的群的刚性（双曲型）的（刚性律）；divergence 的混合（多项式与指数的中间）的不可实现性（中间禁阻律）。
