# 00000006318

**CM4-08 ★** — source: 196_组合矩阵论III.md

**English.** Definition: Total unimodularity is the matrix class whose minors take values zero, plus, and minus one, with the total minor count. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit upper bound of the total minor count and the count of this class is exponential type; the bound is attained by tight examples (network matrices); and the generating function of the count is rational with poles at roots of unity. (exponential tight conversion of minor counting)

**中文。** 定义：完全幺模：子式取值零正负一的矩阵类；子式总数。猜想：换算常数为子式总数的显式上界且该类的计数为指数型；界为紧例（网络矩阵）达到；计数的母函数为有理且极点为单位根。（子式计数指数紧界换算）
