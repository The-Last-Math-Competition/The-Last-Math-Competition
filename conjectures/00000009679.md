# 00000009679

**TAG-09 ★** — source: 308_算术超越.md

**English.** Definition: Values of modular forms at algebraic points: Nesterenko proved the transcendence structure of pi and e^{pi sqrt d}. Conjecture: Every nonzero cusp modular form of weight k at least 2 takes transcendental values at algebraic points outside imaginary quadratic fields; at CM points the values are algebraic multiples of periods, with the transcendence degree of the multiple an explicit function of the class number of the CM field. (modular form value transcendence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：模形式在代数点的值：Nesterenko 证明 π 与 e^{π√d} 的超越结构。猜想：任意权 k≥2 非零尖点模形式在虚二次域外的代数点的值超越；CM 点处模形式值为周期代数倍且倍数的超越次数由 CM 域类数的显式函数给出。（模形式值超越判据）
