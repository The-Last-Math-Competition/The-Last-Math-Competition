# 00000005389

**English.** Definition: The Neron-Tate height is the canonical height of abelian varieties. Conjecture: Positivity is controlled by the rank: for positive rank the nonzero spectrum of the height diverges, and the gap of the minimal positive value has an explicit lower bound by the determinant of the finitely generated basis. (positive-rank gap lower bound of Neron-Tate heights)

**中文。** 定义：范龙高度指交换簇的典范高度。猜想：正定性由秩控制:正秩时高度的非零谱发散且极小正值的间隙由有限生成基的行列式显式下界。（范龙高度正秩间隙下界）
