# 00000008327

**DIA-07 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The Margulis duality of homogeneous dynamics: the rigidity MG_rigid of orbit closures of diagonal flows (duals of unipotents). Conjecture: MG_rigid (a quantitative strengthening of Ratner's theorem) gives polynomial bounds on the approximation rate of orbit closures (a polynomial approximation law); the worst approximation (slowly approximating orbits) is constructed by escaping orbits (an escape construction law); the distribution of orbit crossings (cusp crossing frequency) is exponentially mixing (an exponential mixing law); and the mixing rate is explicit in the spectral gap (a spectral gap rate law). (Margulis Ratner polynomial escape exponential mixing)

**中文。** 定义：齐性动力学的 Margulis 对偶：对角流（unipotent 的对偶）的轨道闭包的刚性 MG_rigid。猜想：MG_rigid（Ratner 定理的定量强化）的轨道闭包的逼近速率的界为多项式（多项式逼近律）；且逼近的最坏（慢逼近轨道）的构造为逃逸轨道（逃逸构造律）；轨道穿越（cusp 的穿越频率）的分布为指数混合（指数混合律）；混合的速率为谱隙的显式（谱隙速率律）。
