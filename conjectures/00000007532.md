**ET3-22 ★** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: Compactly supported cohomology as the relative cohomology of compactifications. Conjecture: Poincare duality of compactly supported cohomology carries weight corrections in the noncompact case; and the weight shift of the correction is one. (weight-corrected compact-support duality)

**中文。** 定义：紧支撑上同调为紧化的相对上同调。猜想：紧支撑上同调的 Poincaré 对偶在非紧情形带权修正；且修正的权位移为一。（紧支撑对偶权修正）
