# 00000003130

**English.** Definition: The rigidity of ergodic measures of hyperbolic groups: the measure dynamics on the boundary. Conjecture: Unique ergodicity of boundary measures: uniqueness of ergodic measures of the boundary action of quasi-isometries is the quasiconformal type of the measure, with the breaking of uniqueness being lattice embeddings. (boundary ergodic unique quasiconformal type)
