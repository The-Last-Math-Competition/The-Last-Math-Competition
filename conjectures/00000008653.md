# 00000008653

**English.** Definition: The growth function: the count of balls |B(n)|. Conjecture: Riley's conjecture: subexponentially growing finitely generated groups are virtually nilpotent (a virtual nilpotence law); the integrality of the degree of polynomial growth (Gromov: polynomial iff nilpotent) gives the degree spectrum (a degree spectrum law); the exact growth of the minimal intermediate-growth example (the Grigorchuk group) is e^{n^alpha} with the transcendence of alpha = 0.52... (an alpha transcendence law); and the possible spectrum of alpha for intermediate growth is dense (an alpha spectrum density law). (Gromov Grigorchuk intermediate alpha transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：生长函数：球 |B(n)| 的计数。猜想：Riley 的猜测：亚指数生长的有限生成群为虚幂零（虚幂零律）；且多项式生长的度的整数性（Gromov 已证多项式 iff 幂零）的度谱（度谱律）；中间生长的最小例子（Grigorchuk 群）的生长的精确刻画 e^{n^α} 的 α=0.52… 的超越性（α 超越律）；中间生长的可能的 α 的谱的稠密性（α 谱稠密律）。
