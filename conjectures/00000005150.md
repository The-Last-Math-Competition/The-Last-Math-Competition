# 00000005150

**English.** Definition: Periods and motive groups are two layers of Hodge data. Conjecture: There exist two Hodge structures whose period integrals all coincide but whose motive groups differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of substructures of the generic type. (same periods-different motive group separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：周期与母题群指两层 Hodge 数据。猜想：存在两 Hodge 结构的周期积分全同而母题群不同,分离由通用型的显式子结构对实现。（周期同-母题群异分离对）
