# 00000005159

**English.** Definition: The discriminant locus is the critical image of the period map. Conjecture: The genus-three discriminant equation is an explicit polynomial of discriminant-type hypersurfaces, composed from the coefficients of singular fiber types, with exact degree. (discriminant polynomial of genus three)
