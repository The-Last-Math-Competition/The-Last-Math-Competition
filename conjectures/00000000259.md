# 00000000259

**AA-06 ◆** — source: 16_代数与算术对象.md

**English.** Definition: For a fundamental discriminant d ≡ 1 (mod 4), the solvability of the negative Pell equation x² − dy² = −1 is determined by the parity of the wide class number. Conjecture (Stevenhagen): The density of primes p ≡ 1 (mod 4) for which the negative Pell equation is solvable equals 1 − ∏_{k≥2}(1−2^{−k}) (the 4-rank part is proven by Fouvry–Klüners; the density constant itself is open).

**中文。** 猜想：定义:d ≡ 1 (mod 4) 的基本判别式,其负 Pell 方程 x² − dy² = −1 的可解性由宽类数奇偶决定。猜想(Stevenhagen):负 Pell 方程可解的素数 p ≡ 1 (mod 4) 的密度等于 1−∏_{k≥2}(1−2^{−k})(Fouvry–Klüners 已证其 4-rank 部分,密度常数本身开放)。
