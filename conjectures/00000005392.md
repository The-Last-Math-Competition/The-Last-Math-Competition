# 00000005392

**English.** Definition: The non-Archimedean measure is the equidistribution limit of p-adic analysis. Conjecture: Its conversion is the explicit support formula of the potential minimizer, a discrete atom set for piecewise affine potentials, and the conversion is unique. (minimizer support formula of non-Archimedean measures)

**中文。** 定义：非阿测度指进值分析的等分布极限。猜想：其换算为位势极小化子的显式支撑公式,分段仿射位势时为离散原子集且换算唯一。（非阿测度极小子支撑公式）
