# 00000001492

**HY-12 ★** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Jakobson's theorem asserts that the parameter set of the Colonnatz map family with absolutely continuous invariant measures has positive measure. Conjecture: The local density of the complement of this parameter set at the Feigenbaum accumulation point is the negative first power of the universal constant δ; and this density is the same for the quadratic family and for general unimodal families. (boundary density universal delta law)

**中文。** 定义：Jakobson 定理断言 Colonnetz 映射族绝对连续不变测度的参数集正测度。猜想：参数集的补集在 Feigenbaum 累积点处的局部密度为普适常数 δ 的负一次幂；且该密度在二次族与一般单峰族上一致。（边界密度普适 δ 律）
