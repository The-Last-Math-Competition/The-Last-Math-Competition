# 00000007223

**English.** Definition: Chowla with the autocorrelation of Mobius. Conjecture: The vanishing of autocorrelations holds, and the extension of the vanishing is the higher cumulants. (higher-cumulant Chowla recursion)

**中文。** 定义：Chowla。猜想：Möbius 的自相关。猜想：Chowla 的递推：自相关的消失且失的推广为高阶累积量。（Chowla 高阶累积量自相关消失递推推广）
