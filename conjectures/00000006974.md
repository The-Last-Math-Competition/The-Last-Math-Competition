# 00000006974

**English.** Definition: Inverse scattering is the inversion from data to potential. Conjecture: The stability window of inversion is given by data regularity; the minimum of the window is half-differentiability; and the constant of the window minimum is the one-half Sobolev exponent. (regularity stability window of inverse scattering)

**中文。** 定义：逆散射为数据到势的反演。猜想：反演稳定窗由数据正则给出；且窗口的极小为二分之一次可微；且窗口极小的常数为二分之一 Sobolev 指数。（逆散射稳定窗正则）
