**HG3-04 ◆** — source: 147_几何群论III_双曲群.md

**English.** Definition: A two-dimensional subgroup is a virtual closed-surface subgroup. Conjecture: A hyperbolic group contains a virtual surface subgroup if and only if its boundary contains a nonseparating circle, and the correspondence is given by an explicit separation construction and complete. (surface subgroup-nonseparating circle correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：二维子群指虚拟闭曲面子群。猜想：双曲群含虚拟曲面子群当且仅当其边界含非分离圆,对应由显式的分离构造给出且完备。（曲面子群-非分离圆对应）
