# 00000003210

**English.** Definition: Subresultant chains: structured computation of GCDs. Conjecture: The structure theorem of subresultants: the vanishing of principal subresultant coefficients indicates common roots, with the multiplicity of vanishing the gap of the difference chain. (subresultant principal coefficient difference gap)
