# 00000008347

**DIA-27 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The Rademacher complexity of Diophantine approximation: the spectrum radem_c of optimal constants of approximation orders (the Hurwitz constant spectrum). Conjecture: radem_c (the closure of the spectrum of Hurwitz constants 1/nu(alpha) over all irrationals) is the closed interval [1/sqrt 5, 1/2] (a closed interval spectrum law); the realization of the interval (number sets attaining each constant) has dimension 1 (a full-dimensional realization law); the extremal value 1/sqrt 5 is uniquely attained (uniqueness of the golden ratio, an isolated extremum law); and the largest spectral gap in the neighborhood of the isolated point (near-extremal spectrum) is Lehmer-type 0.09... (a spectral gap constant law). (Hurwitz closed interval golden isolation gap)

**中文。** 定义：丢番图的 Rademacher 复杂度：逼近阶的最优常数（Hurwitz 常数的谱）radem_c。猜想：radem_c（Hurwitz 常数的谱（一切无理的 1/ν(α) 的值）的闭包）= [1/√5, 1/2] 的闭区间（闭区间谱律）；且区间的实现（每个常数的达成的数集）的维数为 1（满维实现律）；极值 1/√5 的唯一（黄金比的唯一性）的孤立点（孤立极值律）；孤立点的邻居（接近极值的谱）的空隙（谱隙）的最大为 Lehmer 型 0.09…（谱隙常律）。
