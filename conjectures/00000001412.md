# 00000001412

**English.** Definition: The almost-flat K-class K^0_af(X) is the K-theoretic image of Connes–Moscovici almost-flat bundles. Conjecture: The exponential map sends K^0_af(X) into the image of harmonic forms of the differential Chern character, and this map is injective; the image is exactly the closed sublattice of de Rham classes whose Chern character lies in the integral lattice of H^{even}(X).

**中文。** 定义：几乎平坦 K-类 K^0_af(X) 指 Connes–Moscovici 几乎平坦丛的 K-理论像。猜想：指数映射把 K^0_af(X) 映到微分陈特征的调和形式的像,且该映射为单射;像恰为陈特征落在 H^{even}(X) 的整格的 de Rham 类的闭子格。
