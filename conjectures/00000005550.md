**English.** Definition: The monodromy representation and the localized sheaf are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two modules with equivalent monodromy representations but non-isomorphic localized sheaves, and the separation is realized by an explicit Tate-type twist pair. (same monodromy-different localization twist separation)
