**MKT-08 ★** — source: 277_代数K理论与MilnorK.md

**English.** Definition: The redshift conjecture asserts that K(i)-equivalences of Morava K-theory transfer to K(i+1)-equivalences. Conjecture: Redshift holds for every height i; and the characterization of the equivalence is given by the vanishing of nil K-theory, with the noncommutative generalization preserved under Blanchette transfer of dg categories. (redshift nil vanishing characterization)

**中文。** 定义：红移猜想断言 Morava K-理论的 K(i)-等价传递到 K(i+1)-等价。猜想：红移对一切高度 i 恒成立；且等价的刻画由 nil K-理论的消没给出，非交换推广在 dg 范畴的 Blanchette 传递下保持。（红移 nil 消没刻画）
