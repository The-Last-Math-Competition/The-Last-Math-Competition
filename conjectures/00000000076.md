# 00000000076

**English.** Definition: The prime-time Birkhoff mean denotes the time average taken along the sequence of primes. Conjecture: For the rotation x↦x+α (α badly approximable) and Lipschitz f, the difference between the prime-time mean and the full-time mean is an explicit power of x divided by log. (quantitative rate for prime Birkhoff averages)

**中文。** 定义：素数时刻 Birkhoff 均值指沿素数序列的平均。猜想：对旋转 x↦x+α（α 坏逼近）与 Lipschitz f，素数时刻均值与全时刻均值之差为 x 除以 log 的显式幂。（素数 Birkhoff 定量速率）
