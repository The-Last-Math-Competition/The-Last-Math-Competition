# 00000006551

**English.** Definition: First-order splitting is the first-order approximation scheme of fractional stepping. Conjecture: The error coefficient of the leading commutator is always one half, and the coefficient saturates in the noncommuting case. (leading-commutator error of first-order splitting)

**中文。** 定义：一阶分裂为分步求解的一阶逼近格式。猜想：首换位子的误差系数恒为二分之一；且无交换情形的系数饱和。（一阶分裂首换位子误差）
