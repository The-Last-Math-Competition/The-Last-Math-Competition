# 00000006338

**CM4-28 ★** — source: 196_组合矩阵论III.md

**English.** Definition: Design multipliers are the multiplier-group structure of difference-set designs. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the multiplier group, the group being a subgroup of the unit group of a number field; the group order divides the difference-set parameters, the divisibility complete; and the fixed-point count of multipliers is a linear function of the parameters. (divisibility conversion of multiplier groups)

**中文。** 定义：设计乘子：差集设计的乘子群结构。猜想：换算常数为乘子群的显式且群为数域单位群的子群；群阶整除差集参数且整除关系完全；乘子的不动点数为参数的线性函数。（乘子群整除换算常数）
