# 00000004540

**SO2-30 △** — source: 136_随机组合优化.md

**English.** Definition: Phase transition thresholds and universality classes are the combinations of critical locations and critical exponents. Conjecture: There exist two random models with exactly the same thresholds but different critical exponents, and the separation is realized by explicitly coupled spin and satisfiability families. (same threshold-universality class separation)

**中文。** 定义：相变阈值与普适类指临界位置与临界指数组合。猜想：存在两个随机模型的阈值精确相同而临界指数不同,分离由显式耦合的自旋与可满足族实现。（阈值同-普适类分离）
