# 00000001516

**KK-06 △** — source: 36_代数K理论与非交换几何.md

**English.** Definition: Negative K-groups K_{−n}(R) are the Quillen K-groups in negative degree. Conjecture: For noncommutative motives of one-point glued categories, the smallest dimension where negative K is nonzero is always two; higher negative groups vanish layer by layer on geometric categories. (negative K minimal nonzero dimension two)

**中文。** 定义：负 K 群 K_{−n}(R) 指负指标的 Quillen K 群。猜想：单点凝聚范畴的非交换母题的负 K 的最小非零出现维数恒为二；且更高负群在几何范畴上逐层消没。（负 K 最小非零维二）
