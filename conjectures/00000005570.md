# 00000005570

**English.** Definition: The centric linking and the double Burnside are two algebraic models. Conjecture: There exist two fusion systems with isomorphic linking systems but non-isomorphic ring structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of differences of stable elements. (same linking-different ring stable-element separation)

**中文。** 定义：中心链接与双伯恩赛德指两层代数模型。猜想：存在两融合系统的链接同构而环结构不同构,分离由稳定元素的显式差异对实现。（链接同-环异稳定元素分离）
