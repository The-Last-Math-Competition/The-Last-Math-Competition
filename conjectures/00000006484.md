# 00000006484

**English.** Definition: Superconcentration with the variance-dependent concentration structure. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the variance-dependent joint spectrum and equality is given by independent opposites; the boundary of the joint spectrum is Cauchy-Schwarz type; and the stability of equality is quadratic. (variance-dependent joint-spectrum conversion)

**中文。** 定义：超集中：方差相关的集中结构。猜想：换算常数为方差相关联合谱的显式且等号由独立对立给出；联合谱的边界为 Cauchy–Schwarz 型；等号的稳定性为平方型。（方差相关联合谱换算）
