# 00000003936

**FL-26 ★** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: The decomposition length of a cobordism is the minimal number of factors when it is written as a concatenation of simple cobordisms. Conjecture: Every indecomposable cobordism class contains at least one saddle, and there exist high-genus Legendrians for which every cobordism decomposition has length at least 3. (lower bound for cobordism decomposition length)

**中文。** 定义：余边界的分解长度指写成简单余边界串联的最短因子个数。猜想：每个不可分解余边界类至少含一个鞍点;存在高亏格 Legendrian 使一切余边界分解长度不小于 3。（余边界分解长度下界）
