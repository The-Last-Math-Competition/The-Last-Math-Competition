# 00000001333

**AN2-03 ◆** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Definition: A Siegel zero is a real zero 1−β of a Dirichlet L-function of a real character close to s = 1. Conjecture (a strengthening of Landau–Siegel): For a primitive quadratic character χ of modulus q, the largest real zero satisfies 1−β ≤ c·q^{-1/2}·log^{-2}q with an effective absolute constant c.

**中文。** 猜想：定义:Siegel 零点指实特征 Dirichlet L 函数接近 s=1 的实零点 1−β。猜想(Landau–Siegel 强化):对模 q 的本原二次特征 χ,其最大实零点满足 1−β ≤ c·q^{-1/2}·log^{-2}q,c 为有效绝对常数。
