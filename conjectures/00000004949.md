**English.** Definition: Brown's representation theorem represents homology theories by spectra. Conjecture: The equivalence classes of the representing category are classified at low order explicitly by Bousfield classes plus Picard torsion, and the table is closed with no mixed types. (low-order classification table of the Brown representing category)
