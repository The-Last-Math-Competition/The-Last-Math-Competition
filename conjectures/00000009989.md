# 00000009989

**NCR-19 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Crossed product algebras: the correspondence of representations and blocks of crossed products of groups over rings. Conjecture: The correspondence between blocks of crossed product algebras and representations of normal subgroups is an explicit correction of a bijection; the correction index is given by the orbit count of inertia groups of Clifford theory, and the orbit count is an explicit quotient of a transitive group. (crossed product block bijection correction)

**中文。** 定义：交叉积代数：群在环上的交叉积的表示与块的对应。猜想：交叉积代数的块与正规子群表示的对应为双射的显式修正；修正指标由 Clifford 理论的惯性群的轨道计数且轨道计数为可迁群的显式商。（交叉积块双射修正）
