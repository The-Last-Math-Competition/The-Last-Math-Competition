# 00000000820

**English.** Definition: The vanishing order of an eigenfunction on the square torus T² is the degree of its polynomial leading part at a zero. Conjecture: Except for indices with l = a²+b² a triangular number and gcd = 2, the vanishing order is at most 1; the zero locations are explicitly given by the projections of the lattice points (a, b) onto S¹ — the zero set is a finite union of projection fibers.

**中文。** 定义：方环面 T² 特征函数的零点阶(vanishing order)指其在零点处的多项式主部的次数。猜想：除指标为三角数(l=a²+b², gcd=2)的特征外零点阶 ≤ 1;零点集中位置由格点 (a,b) 在 S¹ 上的投影落点显式给出,即零点集为投影纤维的有限并。
