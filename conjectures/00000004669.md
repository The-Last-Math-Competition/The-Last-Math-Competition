# 00000004669

**SG3-09 ★** — source: 141_交换代数IV_奇点理论.md

**English.** Definition: The modality of a curve singularity is the parameter dimension of its equivalence-class family, related to the Milnor number mu. Conjecture: The modality upper bound is (mu - 1)/2, attained by ordinary multiple-point families; the ratio of modality to mu converges to 1/2 as mu grows; germs of modality zero do not exist for mu at least 15, and the smallest non-rigid example has mu exactly 8. (quadratic upper bound of curve modality)

**中文。** 定义：曲线奇点的模数：奇点等价类族的参数维数 mod(S) 与 Milnor 数 μ 的关系。猜想：模数上界为 (μ-1)/2 且界由普通多重点族达到；模数与 μ 之比随 μ 增大收敛于 1/2；模数为零的芽在 μ ≥ 15 时不存在且最小非刚性例的 μ 恰为 8。（曲线模数二次上界）
