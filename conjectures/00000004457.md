**SY3-07 ★** — source: 134_辛几何III_镜像对称深化.md

**English.** Definition: The symplectic cohomology ring SH*(W) of a Weinstein domain W with torus boundary is the T-equivariant homology algebra of holomorphic orbit chains. Conjecture: For abelian skeletons the SH ring is isomorphic to the localization of the semigroup algebra of the dual fan with all structure constants integers; the multiplication constants are given explicitly by directed-edge counts of the dual graph; for nonabelian skeletons denominators appear and divide the exponent of the one-parameter-subgroup lattice of the skeleton. (integer table of torus SH multiplication)

**中文。** 定义：环面边界 Weinstein 域 W 的辛同调环 SH*(W)：全纯轨道链的 T 同调代数。猜想：阿贝尔骨架情形 SH 环同构于对偶扇半群代数的局部化且全部结构常数为整数；乘法常数由对偶图的有向边计数显式给出；非阿贝尔骨架的常数出现分母且分母整除骨架单参数子群格的指数。（环面 SH 乘法整数表）
