# 00000004866

**English.** Definition: The Martin kernel is the quotient functional of the random-walk Green function. Conjecture: The regularity exponent of its harmonic extension to the boundary is an explicit function of the measure tail, matching the Green decay, and the extension is unique. (regularity exponent of the Martin kernel extension)

**中文。** 定义：Martin 核指随机游走格林函数的比值泛函。猜想：其在边界上的调和延拓的正则性指数为测度尾的显式函数,与格林衰减匹配且延拓唯一。（Martin 核延拓正则指数）
