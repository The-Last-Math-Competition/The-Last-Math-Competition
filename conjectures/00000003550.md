# 00000003550

**English.** Definition: The Cantor normal form of ordinals: the standard expansion of ordinals. Conjecture: The uniqueness of the Cantor normal form: the uniqueness of expansion is strict decrease of exponents, with the length of decrease the depth of expansion. (Cantor normal form expansion depth unique decrease)

**中文。** 定义：序数的 Cantor 范式：序数的标准展开。猜想：Cantor 范式的唯一性：展开的唯一为指数的严格递减且递减的长度为展开深度。（Cantor 范式展开深度唯一递减）
