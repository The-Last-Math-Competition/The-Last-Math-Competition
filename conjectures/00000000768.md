# 00000000768

**English.** Definition: The Sen operator Θ is the infinitesimal generator of a p-adic representation. Conjecture: The multiplicity of each root of the characteristic polynomial of Θ exactly equals the multiplicity of the corresponding Hodge weight; the agreement is exact in the semistable case; and the deviation from agreement is absorbed by the ramification layers of the representation. (Sen weights complete)

**中文。** 定义：Sen 算子 Θ 为 p 进表示的无穷小生成元。猜想：Θ 的特征多项式根的重数恰等于 Hodge 权重的重数；且一致性在半稳定情形精确。；且一致性的偏差由表示的分歧层吸收。（Sen 权重完全）
