**English.** Definition: The brick graph: the graph of proper monomorphisms between bricks. Conjecture: The number of connected components of the brick graph equals the count of faithful classes of support tau-tilting modules; component diameters are bounded by twice the Loewy length of the algebra. (brick graph connected components)

**中文。** 定义：brick 图：brick 之间的真单态射构成的图。猜想：brick 图的连通分支数等于支持 τ-倾斜模的 faithful 类的计数；分支的直径有界且界为代数的 Loewy 长度的两倍。（brick 图连通分量）
