**English.** Definition: For the regular tiling {p,q} of the hyperbolic plane, the vertex corona count C_k is the number of vertices at graph distance at most k from a fixed vertex. Conjecture: For the two-parameter family with p,q both tending to infinity, the growth rate of C_k is given by a quadratic form in 2cosh, with exponential growth rate lambda(p,q) satisfying the transcendental equation lambda+lambda^{-1}=(p-2)(q-2)-2, and lambda is a quadratic irrational only when (p-2)(q-2) lies in {4,5,6,10}. (transcendental corona growth equation)

**中文。** 定义：双曲平面的正则铺砌 {p,q} 的顶点冠层计数 C_k 为距固定顶点图距离 ≤k 的顶点数。猜想：对 {p,q} 中 p,q 均趋于 ∞ 的双参数族，C_k 的增长率由 2cosh 的二次型给出，且 C_k 的指数增长率 λ(p,q) 满足超越方程 λ+λ^{-1}=(p-2)(q-2)-2，λ 的算术性仅当 (p-2)(q-2)∈{4,5,6,10} 时为二次无理数。
