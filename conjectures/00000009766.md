# 00000009766

**English.** Definition: Hadamard-type inequalities for Fredholm determinants det(I - T): bounds on determinant moduli of trace-class operators. Conjecture: The supremum of Fredholm determinant moduli at fixed trace norm is attained on normal operators with exact value an explicit exponential of the noise operator; the equality case is pure-phase unitary eigenvalues times uniform measure distributions. (Fredholm determinant extremum)

**中文。** 定义：Fredholm 行列式 det(I−T) 的 Hadamard 型不等式：迹类算子的行列式模的界。猜想：迹范数固定的 Fredholm 行列式模的上确界在正规算子上取得且精确值为噪声算子的显式指数；等号情形为纯相位酉特征值乘均匀测度分布。（Fredholm 行列式极值）
