# 00000000988

**English.** Definition: The Peres–Horodecki criterion detects separability via the positive partial transpose. Conjecture: The boundary measure between k-separability and genuinely k-partite entanglement forms a monotone chain in the dimension product; and the jump points of the chain occur exactly at prime factorizations. (separability boundary monotone chain)

**中文。** 定义：Peres–Horodecki 判据用部分转正检测可分性。猜想：k-可分与真正 k 部纠缠的边界测度在维数乘积上为单调链；且链的跳变点为素因子分解处。（可分边界单调链）
