**SDG-12 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: Difference algebra is an algebraic structure with an automorphism sigma, and difference Galois theory is its Galois correspondence. Conjecture: The intersection of the difference and differential Galois groups (for mixed systems) is the intersection group of the two; and the connected component of a mixed system is the differential connected part. (mixed difference-differential Galois group)

**中文。** 定义：差分代数指带自同构 σ 的代数结构，差分 Galois 理论为其 Galois 对应。猜想：差分与微分 Galois 群的交（混合系统）为两者的交群；且混合系统的连通分量为微分连通部分。（混合差分微分 Galois 群）
