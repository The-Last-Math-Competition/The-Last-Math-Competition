**English.** Definition: Yangians and differential equations: the algebraic side of integrable systems. Conjecture: The Galois realization of the Yangian: the envelope of the Galois groups of the Lax pairs of integrable equations is the representation image of the Yangian, with the index of the image the pole order of the spectral parameter. (Yangian Galois realization index)
