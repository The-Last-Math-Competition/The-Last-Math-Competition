# 00000003357

**English.** Definition: Equidistributed periodic points: weak convergence of periodic measures. Conjecture: The deviation of periodic equidistribution: the convergence rate of periodic-point measures is the counting deficit of preperiodic points, with the order of the deficit the characteristic exponent. (periodic equidistribution characteristic exponent deviation)
