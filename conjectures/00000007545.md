**English.** Definition: The endpoint structure of interpolation formulas. Conjecture: The endpoints of continuous families are group-algebra type and the endpoint spectrum is finite; the realization of endpoints is the parameter extrema; and the parameter extrema are realized as single-parameter endpoints. (group-algebra-type continuous endpoints)
