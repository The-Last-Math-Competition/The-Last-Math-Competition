# 00000004300

**AG5-30 △** — source: 128_交换群论.md

**English.** Definition: Ziegler spectrum homeomorphism and pure equivalence mean topological equivalence and categorical equivalence respectively. Conjecture: There exist two abelian groups whose Ziegler spectra are homeomorphic while their module categories are not purely equivalent, and the smallest group cardinality of the separation is aleph_0. (homeomorphic spectrum-pure inequivalence separation)

**中文。** 定义：Ziegler 谱同胚与纯粹等价分别指拓扑等价与范畴等价。猜想：存在两个交换群其 Ziegler 谱同胚而模范畴纯粹不等价,分离的最小群基数为 ℵ₀。（谱同胚-纯粹不等价分离）
