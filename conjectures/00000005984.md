# 00000005984

**English.** Definition: The Kodaira dimension is the growth order of pluricanonical forms. Conjecture: In three dimensions it is computed from finitely many polynomials with explicit bound, and the combination of the bound is the elementary decision of sections. (polynomial computation bound of the Kodaira dimension)

**中文。** 定义：小平维数为多重典范增长阶。猜想：三维时由有限多项式计算且界显式；且界的组合为截面的初等判定。（小平维数多项式计算界）
