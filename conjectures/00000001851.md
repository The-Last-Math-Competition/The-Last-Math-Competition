# 00000001851

**RM-11 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The characteristic polynomial of the CUE P_N(θ) = det(U − θ). Conjecture: The two-point joint moments E[P_N(θ₁)^k·conj{P_N(θ₂)}^l] have an explicit formula — a combination of Fisher–Hartwig singular functions with Barnes G; the formula is closed-form for |θ₁|, |θ₂| < 1.

**中文。** 定义：CUE 特征多项式 P_N(θ)=det(U−θ)。猜想：P_N(θ₁),P_N(θ₂) 的两点联合矩 E[P_N(θ₁)^k共轭{P_N(θ₂)}^l] 有显式公式:为 Fisher–Hartwig 奇异函数与 Barnes G 的组合;公式在 |θ_1|,|θ_2|<1 时闭式。
