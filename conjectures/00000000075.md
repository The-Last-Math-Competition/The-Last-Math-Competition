**English.** Conjecture: The functional graph of the polynomial dynamics x ↦ x²+c (with c outside {0,−2} and similar exceptions) over F_p obeys random-map statistics: the number of cyclic points is ~ √(πp/2), the image size is ~ (1−√(π/8))·p, and the maximum tail depth is of the same order.
