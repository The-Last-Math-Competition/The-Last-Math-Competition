**English.** Definition: Brown measure of three angle matrix denotes spectrum distribution of on three angle random matrix. Conjecture: its density constant is explicit functional, standard when of pair angle is all and support is ring. (three angle Brown density functional)

**中文。** 定义：三角矩阵的 Brown 测度指上三角随机矩阵的谱分布。猜想：其密度常数为对角元律的显式泛函,标准圆元时为圆盘均匀且支集为圆环。（三角 Brown 密度泛函）
