# 00000004772

**English.** Definition: The Brown measure of a triangular matrix is the spectral distribution of an upper triangular random matrix. Conjecture: Its density constant is an explicit functional of the diagonal-entry law, and for standard circular entries it is uniform on the disk with annular support. (density functional of triangular Brown measures)

**中文。** 定义：三角矩阵的 Brown 测度指上三角随机矩阵的谱分布。猜想：其密度常数为对角元律的显式泛函,标准圆元时为圆盘均匀且支集为圆环。（三角 Brown 密度泛函）
