**FM-03 ★** — source: 65_分形几何与测度.md

**English.** Definition: The gap set is the size spectrum of connected components of the complement of a self-similar set. Conjecture: Under strong separation, the decay rate of the spectral measure has a power times a logarithmic correction; and the exponent of the correction is negative one half. (gap spectrum logarithmic half correction decay)

**中文。** 定义：间隔集为自相似集补的连通分量尺寸谱。猜想：强分离情形的谱测度衰减率为幂乘对数修正；且修正的指数为负二分之一。（间隔谱对数半修正衰减）
