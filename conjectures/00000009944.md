**English.** Definition: Superrigidity: representation extension of higher-rank lattices (proven by Margulis); barrier phenomena of rank-one groups. Conjecture: Representation rigidity of rank-one groups is characterized by quasiconformal conservation of boundary actions: rigidity holds if and only if the Furstenberg entropy of boundary measures takes the equality value; the barrier is crossed explicitly by perturbation families of the rank-one dimension. (rank-one representation rigidity criterion)

**中文。** 定义：超刚性：高秩格的表示延拓（Margulis 已证）；秩一群的壁垒现象。猜想：秩一群的表示刚性以边界作用的拟共形守恒刻画：表示刚性当且仅当边界测度的 Furstenberg 熵取等式值；壁垒由秩一维数的扰动族显式穿越。（秩一表示刚性判据）
