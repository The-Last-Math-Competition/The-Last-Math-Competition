**English.** Definition: Horocycle limit measures: orbit measure limits of horocycle flows on lattices (proven by Dani-Smillie). Conjecture: The atomic mass counting formula of horocycle orbit measure limits: the atom count is the period count of periodic orbits, with explicit mass values the reciprocals of period lengths; and the entropy of nonatomic limiting measures is an explicit fraction of maximal entropy. (horocycle atomic counting formula)

**中文。** 定义：horocycle 极限测度：格上 horocycle 流的轨道测度极限（Dani–Smillie 已证）。猜想：horocycle 轨道测度极限的原子质量计数公式：原子数为周期轨道的周期计数且质量的显式值为周期长度的倒数；非原子极限测度的熵为极大熵的显式分数。（horocycle 原子计数公式）
