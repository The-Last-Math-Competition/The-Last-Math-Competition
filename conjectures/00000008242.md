# 00000008242

**EGT-12 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The global stability of Walrasian price adjustment: the Lyapunov function of the price-adjustment dynamics. Conjecture: The existence of a global Lyapunov function holds if and only if the economy is in the gross substitutes class (a GS criterion); the convergence rate of the adjustment in the GS class is exponential in the Lipschitz constant tau of the Hilbert metric (a Hilbert convergence law); and the minimal commodity-space dimension for oscillations in the non-GS case (limit cycles of the adjustment) is 3 (a three-good cycle example), the order of magnitude of the cycle period being the reciprocal of the price elasticity (an elasticity-reciprocal period law). (Walrasian gross-substitutes Hilbert three-good cycle)

**中文。** 定义：一般均衡的tâtonnement 的全局稳定：价格调整的动力学的 Lyapunov 函数 lyap_tat。猜想：lyap_tat（整体 Lyapunov 函数的存在）当且仅当经济为 gross substitute 类（GS 判据）；且 GS 类的调整的收敛速率为 Hilbert 度量的 Lipschitz 常数 τ 的指数（Hilbert 收敛律）；非 GS 的振荡（tâtonnement 的极限环）的最小维数的商品空间为 3（三商品环例），环的周期的数量级为价格弹性的倒数（弹性倒数周期律）。
