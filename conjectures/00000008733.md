**English.** Definition: Low-frequency noise spectrum: the frequency dependence S(omega) of local observable fluctuations. Conjecture: The MBL phase is white noise (a white noise law); the thermal phase is 1/omega type (a 1/f law); the transition region is omega^{-1/2} type (a half-exponent spectrum law); and the universal description is of Dyson-Schmidt class (a D-S law). (noise spectrum white 1/f Dyson-Schmidt)
