**NSP-19 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: Malliavin spectrum of dimension space on Ornstein–Uhlenbeck operator. Conjecture: spectrum is nonnegative integer (Hermite spectrum); and this multiplicity is asymptotic of partition number (partition multiplicity); spectrum counting of corresponds to -fold number by Hardy–Ramanujan partition asymptotic gives (H-R asymptotic); the- binomial positive is Rademacher series (R- positive).

**中文。** 定义：维纳空间上 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 算子的 Malliavin 谱。猜想：谱为非负整数（Hermite 谱律）；且本征重数为对应次数的分拆数（分拆重数律）；谱计数的渐近由 Hardy–Ramanujan 分拆渐近给出（H-R 渐近律）；第二项修正为 Rademacher 级数（R-修正律）。
