# 00000000495

**English.** Definition: The count ASM_n of alternating sign matrices is a six-vertex-model partition function. Conjecture: The largest prime factor of ASM_n does not exceed n²; and the smoothness of the counting sequence is consistent with Somos-type recursions. (ASM counting smoothness)

**中文。** 定义：交替符号矩阵计数 ASM_n 为六顶点模型配分函数。猜想：ASM_n 的最大素因子不超过 n²；且计数数列的光滑性与 Somos 型递推自洽。（ASM 计数光滑性）
