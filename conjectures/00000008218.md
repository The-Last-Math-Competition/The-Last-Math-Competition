# 00000008218

**ORB-18 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: Representation zeta functions: the convergence domain rep_zeta(G) of the series sum_n a_n n^{-s} (a_n the number of irreducibles of dimension n) for groups of finite representation growth. Conjecture: The abscissa of convergence of rep_zeta is rational (a rational abscissa law); the value spectrum of the abscissa (over all nilpotent compact p-adic groups) is a finite set of rationals with repeated realizations (a finite spectrum repetition law); and the ratio of the abscissa of the zeta function to the degree of the Higman PORC polynomial of the group equals (1/2) rank (a degree-rank half-ratio law). (representation zeta rational abscissa PORC ratio)

**中文。** 定义：表示 zeta 函数：有限表示增长群的 Σ_{n} a_n n^{-s}（a_n 为 n 维不可约数）的收敛域 rep_zeta(G)。猜想：rep_zeta 的收敛横坐标为有理数（有理横坐标律）；且横坐标的值谱（一切幂零紧 p-adic 群的 σ）为有限的有理数集的重复实现（有限谱重复律）；zeta 的 abscissa 与群的 Higman PORC 的多项式的次数的比 = 1/2·rank（次数-秩半比律）。
