**MIB-16 ◆** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The distribution of motivic Frobenius: the distribution of conjugacy classes of the geometric Frobenius of a fixed motive inside the motivic Galois group. Conjecture: The distribution is of uniform Chebotarev type; the unconditional error exponent of its effective version is 1/2 + 1/(2d) (d the dimension of the motive), saturating to 1/2 in the large-dimension limit; and the loss in the effective bound is controlled by the motivic obstruction of Siegel zeros. (effective Frobenius Chebotarev)

**中文。** 定义：母题 Frobenius 的分布指固定母题的几何 Frobenius 共轭类在母题 Galois 群中的分布。猜想：分布为一致 Chebotarev 型，其有效版本的无条件误差指数为 1/2 + 1/(2d)（d 为母题维数），且指数在大维数极限饱和到 1/2；有效界的丢失由 Siegel 零点的母题化障碍控制。（Frobenius 有效 Chebotarev）
