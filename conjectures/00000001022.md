# 00000001022

**English.** Conjecture: Beyond the MacWilliams identities for support weights of subcode subsets of any [n,k]_q code, the lower bound for trellis complexity is min(k, n−k)·⌈log₂ q⌉ (a trellis lower bound).

**中文。** 猜想：任意 [n, k]_q 的子码子集的支撑重量的 MacWilliams 恒等式之外,trellis 复杂度的下界为 min(k, n−k)·⌈log₂ q⌉(trellis 下界)。
