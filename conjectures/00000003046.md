**English.** Definition: Bi-Hamiltonian tests: the existence of compatible Poisson pairs. Conjecture: The spectral rigidity of Poisson pairs: the recursion basis of Poisson brackets of the basic sequence of a compatible pair is the Lenard chain, and the closure of the chain is Magri integrability. (Lenard chain Magri closure)

**中文。** 定义：双 Hamiltonian 检验：相容 Poisson 对的存在。猜想：Poisson 对的谱刚性：相容对的基本序列的 Poisson 括号的递推基为 Lenard 链且链的封闭为 Magri 可积性。（Lenard 链 Magri 封闭）
