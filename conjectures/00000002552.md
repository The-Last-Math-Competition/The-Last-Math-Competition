# 00000002552

**GZ-22 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: The projection classification theorem: simultaneous positive measure of projections in all dimensions versus rectifiability. Conjecture: The simultaneous positivity of a fixed family of m-dimensional projections implies rectifiability if and only if the Calderón coefficients of the projection family are nonzero; when a coefficient vanishes there exists a purely 1-dimensional Furstenberg counterexample. (Preiss finite projection criterion)

**中文。** 定义：射影分类定理：各维投影的同时正测度性与可校正性。猜想：固定 m 维投影族的正测度同时性蕴含可校正的充要条件为投影族的 Calderón 系数非零；系数为零时存在纯一维 Furstenberg 反例。（Preiss 有限投影判据）
