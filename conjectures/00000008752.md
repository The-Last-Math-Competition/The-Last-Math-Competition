# 00000008752

**English.** Definition: Quillen-Lichtenbaum: the comparison of K-groups with etale cohomology. Conjecture: The comparison holds strictly for i at most 2 (a threshold law); the inverse limit has a convergent spectrum (a convergence law); the proof goes through the Voevodsky-Rost Bloch-Kato theorem (a VR law); and there is the convergence domain of the motivic spectrum (a convergence domain law). (Quillen-Lichtenbaum threshold Voevodsky-Rost)

**中文。** 定义：Quillen–Lichtenbaum。猜想：K 群与 étale 上同调的比较。猜想：比较在 i ≤ 2 严格成立（阈律）；且逆向极限的收敛谱（收敛律）；证明由 Voevodsky–Rost 的 Bloch–Kato（VR 律）；motivic 谱的收敛域（收敛域律）。
