**English.** Definition: A Galois orbit is the set of all algebraic conjugates of a point. Conjecture: The convergence rate of small-point orbit equidistribution is the negative one-half power of the degree, controlled by the spectral gap of the potential kernel, and verified in the elliptic case. (negative half-power convergence rate of orbit equidistribution)
