# 00000008223

**ORB-23 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The homological dimension of block structure of representation categories: the projective dimension blk_pd of blocks of category O. Conjecture: blk_pd is at most the dimension of the flag (a flag dimension law); the complete classification of block equivalence classes is given by the linkage classes of central characters (a linkage classification law); the count of connected components of the linkage graph (the number of blocks) equals the number of Weyl group orbits of finite central characters (an orbit-block count); and the minimal dimension of a single block (minimal irreducible block) is 1 (a single-block uniformity). (category O linkage flag dimension blocks)

**中文。** 定义：表示范畴的分块结构的同调维数：范畴 O 的块的投射维数 blk_pd(O_χ)。猜想：blk_pd ≤ dim flag（旗维数律）；且块的等价类的完全分类由中心特征标的 linkage 类（linkage 分类律）；linkage 的图的连通分量的计数（分块数）= 有限中心 characters 的 Weyl 群轨道数（轨道-分块计数）；分块的单块（最小不可约块）的极小维数 = 1（单块一律）。
