# 00000008642

**English.** Definition: Cylindrical partitions are cylinder lattice configurations. Conjecture: The area generating function is of finite product form with exponent a quasi-polynomial of period; and the kernel of the period is cylindrical symmetry. (cylindrical partition symmetric quasi-polynomial product)

**中文。** 定义：柱面分拆指圆柱格点配置。猜想：面积生成函数为有限乘积形状且指数为周期拟多项式；且周期的核为柱对称。（柱面分拆柱对称拟多项式乘积）
