# 00000000414

**SA-14 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: The hook walk. Conjecture: Starting from a uniform point, the probability of visiting the cell (i,j) deviates from the content c(i,j) = j − i by at most n^{-1/2} (mixing property of the hook walk).

**中文。** 定义：钩行走(Hook walk)。猜想：从均匀起点出发,访问格子 (i,j) 的概率与内容 c(i,j) = j − i 的最大偏差 ≤ n^{-1/2}(钩行走的混合性)。
