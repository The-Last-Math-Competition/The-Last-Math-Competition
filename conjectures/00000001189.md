# 00000001189

**English.** Definition: The minimal support of a two-element-generated representation of SL_n(F_q) is the minimum of the dimension of the fixed-point space of its image in the natural n-dimensional representation. Conjecture: There exists a generating pair (A, B) whose common fixed space has dimension 0 while each of A and B fixes at most a line, giving support n²−1; the pair is explicitly constructible from a Singer cycle and its dual.

**中文。** 定义：SL_n(F_q) 的两元素生成表示的最小支撑指其像在自然 n 维表示中的不动点空间维数的极小值。猜想：存在生成对 (A,B) 使其公共不动空间维数为 0 且 A,B 各自的固定空间维数 ≤ 1,支撑为 n²−1;该对可由 Singer 循环与其对偶显式构造。
