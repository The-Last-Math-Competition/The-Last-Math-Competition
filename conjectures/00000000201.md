# 00000000201

**SQ-01 ◆** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: A Piatetski-Shapiro prime is a prime of the form ⌊n^c⌋ with 1 < c < 2 non-integer; their existence is known for c close to 1. Conjecture: The gap distribution of consecutive Piatetski-Shapiro primes converges to a Poisson process of explicit intensity, with mean gap of order c·N^{1−1/c} with a logarithmic correction; the joint distribution of gaps of order k agrees with the Cramér model, with corrections only in the small-gap spectrum. (Shapiro gap limit law)

**中文。** 定义：Piatetski-Shapiro 素数为形如 ⌊n^c⌋（1<c<2 非整）的素数，其存在性已知于 c 靠近 1 的区间。猜想：相邻 Shapiro 素数的间隙分布收敛到显式强度的 Poisson 过程，平均间隙为 c·N^{1−1/c}·log 修正；间隙的 k 阶联合分布与 Cramér 模型一致且修正仅出现在小间隙谱。（Shapiro 间隙极限律）
