# 00000001212

**English.** Definition: Two-row Chomp is the chocolate-bar game restricted to two rows. Conjecture: Among first-player-losing positions, the smaller coordinate takes exactly the values of a shifted Zeckendorf representation; and the losing-position sequence has density zero. (two-row Chomp Zeckendorf losing positions)

**中文。** 定义：两行 Chomp 为仅在两行的巧克力刮取博弈。猜想：先手败的位置中较小坐标的取值恰为 Zeckendorf 表示的移位；且败位置序列的密度为零。（两行 Chomp Zeckendorf 败位）
