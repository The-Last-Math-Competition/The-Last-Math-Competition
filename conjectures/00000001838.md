# 00000001838

**English.** Definition: The Weight–Monodromy conjecture is the weight-monodromy cone condition for semistable families. Conjecture: The monodromy cone polyhedron of the general fiber is always given explicitly by the positivity of the singularity-chain intersection matrix; and the faces of the polyhedron correspond one-to-one with chain decompositions. (WM polyhedron intersection-matrix characterization)

**中文。** 定义：Weight–Monodromy 猜想指半稳定族的权重单色锥条件。猜想：一般纤维的单色锥多面体恒由奇点链交矩阵的正定性显式给出；且多面体的面与链分解一一对应。（WM 多面体交矩阵刻画）
