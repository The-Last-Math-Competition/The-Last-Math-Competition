# 00000007573

**English.** Definition: The distortion correction of the nerve lemma as the quantitative deficit of equivalence. Conjecture: The deficit term is given by the Betti numbers of intersection holes and the deficit spectrum is finite; and the verification of finiteness is the local homological dimension. (finite-distortion nerve deficit)

**中文。** 定义：神经引理的失真修正为等价的定量亏损。猜想：亏项由交空洞的 Betti 数给出且亏谱有限；且有限的验证为局部同调维数。（神经失真亏项有限）
