**English.** Definition: The symmetric rank is the minimal number of symmetric summands of a symmetric tensor. Conjecture: The classification of equality of symmetric and general ranks of cubic monomials is given explicitly by a parity-type criterion of the indices, closed with no exceptional points, and the equality criterion always holds when the catalecticant matrix has full rank. (classification of cubic symmetric rank-general rank equality)

**中文。** 定义：对称秩指对称张量的对称分解最小数。猜想：三次单项式的对称秩与一般秩相等的分类由指标奇偶型判据显式给出,判据封闭且无例外点。(三次对称秩-一般秩相等分类)；且相等判据在 catalecticant 矩阵满秩时恒成立。
