# 00000002411

**GC2-01 ★** — source: 66_Galois覆盖与基本群.md

**English.** Definition: Abhyankar groups are finite quotient groups realizable on the affine line in characteristic p. Conjecture: The groups realizable on the affine line are quasi-prime group extensions; and the combination of the extensions is a completely classified dichotomy. (Abhyankar quasi-prime extension classification)

**中文。** 定义：Abhyankar 群为特征 p 仿射线上可实现的有限商群。猜想：仿射线上可实现的群为拟素群扩张；且扩张的组合为二分法完全分类。（Abhyankar 拟素扩张分类）
