# 00000002211

**MI-11 ★** — source: 59_交换环与乘法理想论.md

**English.** Definition: The elasticity ρ(R) is the ratio of longest to shortest factorizations. Conjecture: For imaginary quadratic integer rings Z[√−d], ρ has an explicit table of the type (smallest prime factor of d) (a quadratic-order elasticity table).

**中文。** 定义：弹性 ρ(R)(最长因子化与最短之比)。猜想：虚二次整数环 ℤ[√−d] 的 ρ 为 (d 的最小素因子) 型显式表(二次阶弹性表)。
