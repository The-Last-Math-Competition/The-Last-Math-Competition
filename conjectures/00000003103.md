**English.** Definition: Visual metrics: fractal metrics on the boundary. Conjecture: Ahlfors regularity of visual metrics: the Hausdorff dimension of the boundary is a function of the divergence exponent, with regularity constant an explicit correction of the growth rate of the group. (visual Ahlfors dimension formula)
