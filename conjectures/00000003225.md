**English.** Definition: Dynamical localization: time decay of eigenfunctions. Conjecture: The strong exponent of dynamical localization: the strong dynamic decay of eigenfunction evolution is exponential, with exponent inversely proportional to the localization length. (dynamic decay localization length inverse)

**中文。** 定义：动态局域化：本征函数的时间衰减。猜想：动态局域化的强指数：本征函数演化的强动态衰减为指数型且指数与局域化长度成反比。（动态衰减局域长度反比）
