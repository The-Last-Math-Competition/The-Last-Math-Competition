# 00000004742

**English.** Definition: The Brauer-Manin obstruction is the global obstruction of Brauer evaluations to rational points. Conjecture: Over the rationals the obstruction is complete for families of K3 surfaces, and the first incomplete example requires transcendental Brauer classes with explicit combinatorial structure. (completeness of the BM obstruction for K3 surfaces)

**中文。** 定义：Brauer–Manin 障碍指 Brauer 评价对有理点的整体阻碍。猜想：有理数域上该障碍对 K3 曲面族完备,首个不完备例需超越 Brauer 类与显式组合结构。（K3 的 BM 障碍完备性）
