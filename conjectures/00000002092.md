# 00000002092

**English.** Conjecture: The ring structure of the wrapped Fukaya category of a plumbing tree T is a unital quotient of the path algebra (the quotient eliminating interior idempotents); the generating relations of the quotient are an explicit list from the vertex connectivity of T.

**中文。** 猜想：wrapped Fukaya 范畴的 plumbing 树 T 的环结构为路径代数的幺正商(quotient 为内部 idempotent 消除);商的生成关系为 T 的顶点连通的显式列表。
