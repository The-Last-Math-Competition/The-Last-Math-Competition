# 00000002173

**EC-03 ★** — source: 58_极值集合论与组合.md

**English.** Definition: Signed EKR concerns extremal intersecting families over permutations/subspaces. Conjecture: The sliding-hyperplane family is the unique extremizer of signed intersecting families (in the maximal-dimensional spherical case); uniqueness is controlled by the strict decrease of eigenvector orderings.

**中文。** 定义：带符号 EKR 指在置换/子空间上的交族极值。猜想：滑超平面族为带符号交族的唯一极值(在最大维球面情形);唯一性由特征向量排序的严格递降控制。
