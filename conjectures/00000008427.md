# 00000008427

**DFG-17 ★** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The Reach bound of difference families: the gap of their density differences. Conjecture: For a prime p and t = 2, the explicit Reach upper bound on the minimal density difference of difference families is attainable for p at least 5; and the attaining difference families are constructed from quadratic-type partitions of cyclotomic classes. (attainability of the difference family Reach bound)

**中文。** 定义：差族的 Reach 界指其密度差的 Gap。猜想：对素数 p 与 t = 2 的差族的极小密度差的显式 Reach 上界在 p ≥ 5 时可达；可达的差族由分圆类的二次型分划构造。（差族 Reach 上界可达）
