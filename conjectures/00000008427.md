**English.** Definition: Reach bound of difference family denotes Gap of its density difference. Conjecture: pair prime p and difference family of explicit Reach upper bound in p ≥ 5; of minimal density difference of difference family of t = 2 by quadratic construction of class. (difference family Reach upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：差族的 Reach 界指其密度差的 Gap。猜想：对素数 p 与 t = 2 的差族的极小密度差的显式 Reach 上界在 p ≥ 5 时可达；可达的差族由分圆类的二次型分划构造。（差族 Reach 上界可达）
