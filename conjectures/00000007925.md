# 00000007925

**ATC-25 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The singularity index of Siegel Eisenstein series: the residual pole structure I_chi of the degenerate Eisenstein series E(z,s;chi) at special points of s. Conjecture: I_chi is controlled by the parity of the index delta(chi) = sum dim sigma(chi_v): odd index gives a simple pole with residue the theta integral of the Siegel-Weil formula, even index gives regularity; and the special-point values (half-integral points of s) in the regular case are algebraic multiples of CM periods, the multiple being the Toral period of the automorphic form f, with norm the square root of a power of 2. (Siegel Eisenstein parity pole structure)

**中文。** 定义：siegel Eisenstein 级数的奇异性指标：退化 Eisenstein E(z,s;χ) 在 s 的特殊点的残差极点结构 I_χ。猜想：I_χ 由指标 δ(χ)=Σ dim σ(χ_v) 的奇偶性控制：奇指标时残差为单极点且残数为 Siegel–Weil 公式的 theta 积分，偶指标时正则；且正则值的特殊点求值（s 的半整数点）为 CM 周期的代数倍数，倍数为自守形式 f 的 Toral 周期，其范数为 2 的幂次的平方根。
