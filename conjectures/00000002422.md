# 00000002422

**English.** Definition: The ramification-index structure of coverings of smooth curves. Conjecture: The realizable multisets are completely characterized by Riemann–Hurwitz together with singularity bundles; and the combination of the characterization is the union of orbits. (ramification multiset realizability characterization)

**中文。** 定义：光滑曲线覆盖的分歧指标结构。猜想：可实现的多重集由 Riemann–Hurwitz 与奇性束完全刻画；且刻画的组合为轨道并。（分歧多重集可实现刻画）
