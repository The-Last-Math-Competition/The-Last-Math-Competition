**English.** Definition: Knot surgery is the slant-product surgery along an embedded knot. Conjecture: The surgery change factor of the SW invariants is an explicit combination of the Alexander polynomial and the signature function of the knot, and the factor satisfies a multiplicative law on disjoint unions of links. (multiplicativity of knot surgery factors)

**中文。** 定义：结手术指沿嵌入结的斜积手术。猜想：SW 不变量的手术变化因子由结的 Alexander 多项式与符号差函数的显式组合给出,因子在链环并上满足乘性律。（结手术因子的乘性）
