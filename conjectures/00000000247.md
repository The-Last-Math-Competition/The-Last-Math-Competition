# 00000000247

**English.** Definition: A graph H has the Sidorenko property if in every graph the density of H is at least the e(H)-th power of the edge density (random graphs attain the minimum). Conjecture (Sidorenko): All bipartite graphs have the Sidorenko property (known for trees, even cycles, cubes, and other large classes).

**中文。** 猜想：定义:图 H 的 Sidorensky 性质:均匀随机图中 H 的密度 ≥ 该图边密度的 e(H) 次幂(随机图达到极小)。猜想(Sidorenko):所有二部图具有 Sidorenko 性质(已知树、偶圈、立方图等大类)。
