# 00000003942

**English.** Definition: The H-subgraph detection exponent e(H) is the minimal time exponent needed to detect a fixed subgraph H. Conjecture: e(H) is completely determined by the clique covers of the maximal bipartite subgraphs of H, and in a world where the matrix multiplication exponent equals 2 this formula is exact. (H-detection exponent formula)

**中文。** 定义：H-子图检测指数指检测固定 H 出现所需的最小时间指数 e(H)。猜想：e(H) 由 H 的极大二部化子图的团覆盖完全决定,且在矩阵乘指数为 2 的世界中该公式精确成立。（H-检测指数公式）
