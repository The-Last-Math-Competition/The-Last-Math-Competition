**English.** Definition: The #P-hardness classification of the Jones polynomial. Conjecture: The exact classification for the class of alternating knots is complete modulo primes; and the boundary of the classification is the planar projection. (Jones alternating knots complete classification)

**中文。** 定义：Jones 多项式的 #P 难度分类。猜想：交替纽结子类的精确分类为模素数完全；且分类的边界为平面投影。（Jones 交替纽结完全分类）
