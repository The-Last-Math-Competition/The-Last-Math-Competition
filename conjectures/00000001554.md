# 00000001554

**GM-14 ◆** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: Euclidean Ramsey properties mean that every two-coloring of the plane contains a monochromatic copy of a specified configuration. Conjecture: Every red-blue coloring of the plane contains a monochromatic unit equilateral triangle; and the unit version follows from Gallai's similarity images plus rational-affine combinatorial closure. (unit triangle Euclidean Ramsey)

**中文。** 定义：欧氏 Ramsey 性质指任何二染色平面含单色指定构型。猜想：平面任何红蓝染色恒含单色单位等边三角形；且单位版本由 Gallai 相似像加有理仿射组合封闭。（单位三角形欧氏 Ramsey）
