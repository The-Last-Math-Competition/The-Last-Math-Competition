**English.** Definition: Shear coordinates parametrize the boundary subgroups of the surface, and the bending lamination is the bending structure of the convex core boundary. Conjecture: The conversion Jacobian from shear to bending is the explicit law of doubled half-angle sines, and the formula is continuous and invertible for all laminations. (half-angle sine law of bending versus shear)

**中文。** 定义：剪切坐标指曲面边界子群的参数化,弯曲层汐指凸核边界的弯曲结构。猜想：剪切到弯曲的换算雅可比为二倍半角正弦型显式律,公式对一切层汐连续且可逆。（弯曲-剪切半角正弦律）
