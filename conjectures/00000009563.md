# 00000009563

**English.** Definition: The wind-tree model is the periodic-cell model of billiards with periodically distributed square obstacles in the plane, whose diffusion behavior is controlled by the KZ exponents of the level-set dynamics. Conjecture: The wind-tree model has normal diffusion (mean square displacement ~ t), with diffusion constant given by an explicit quadratic form in the Lyapunov spectrum of the section stratum; the degenerate parameter-zero direction exhibits superdiffusion, with the superdiffusion window width tending to zero as the period grows. (wind-tree diffusion constant)

**中文。** 定义：wind-tree 模型为平面上周期分布方形障碍台球的周期胞模型，其扩散行为由水平集动力系统的 KZ 指数控制。猜想：wind-tree 模型为正常扩散（均方位移 ~ t），扩散常数由截面 stratum 的 Lyapunov 谱的显式二次型给出；参数为 0 的退化方向出现超扩散，超扩散窗口的宽度随周期趋于零。（wind-tree 扩散常数）
