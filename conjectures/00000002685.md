**AD2-05 ★** — source: 75_代数动力学_ArzelaZariski.md

**English.** Definition: Zariski-dense orbits: orbit closures of semigroup actions. Conjecture: The dichotomy of orbit closures of multidimensional endomorphism semigroups: the closure is either the preperiodic point set or Zariski dense; the criterion is emptiness of the multiplicative zero set of the Jacobian determinants, verifiable computably for at most two maps. (semigroup orbit closure dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 密度轨道：半群作用的轨道闭包。猜想：多维自同态半群的轨道闭包二分：闭包或为预周期点集或为 Zariski 稠密；二分的判据为 Jacobi 行列式的乘性零点集为空且判据在不超过两个映射时可计算验证。（半群轨道闭包二分）
