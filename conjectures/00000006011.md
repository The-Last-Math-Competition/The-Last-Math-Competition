# 00000006011

**HS2-01 ◆** — source: 186_哈密顿系统.md

**English.** Definition: The near-integrable preservation theorem is the survival of invariant tori under small perturbations. Conjecture: The torus-measure conversion formula is the explicit power-law correction of the perturbation, the correction exponent given by the Diophantine exponent of the frequency vector, and the coefficient is exact. (Diophantine power-correction formula of torus measure)

**中文。** 定义：近可积保持定理指不变环面的小扰动存留。猜想：环面测度换算公式为扰动的显式幂律修正,修正指数由频率向量的丢番图指数给出且系数精确。（环面测度丢番图幂修正式）
