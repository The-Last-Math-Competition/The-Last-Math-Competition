**English.** Definition: Polynuclear growth (PNG) droplets: the spatial fluctuation structure of zero-temperature surface growth models. Conjecture: Higher-order description of the spatial process of the height field of PNG droplets at the edge: the local limit at the edge is a slope transform of the Airy process, and the kernel of the slope transform is of Bowden-Kent form. (PNG edge higher-order process)
