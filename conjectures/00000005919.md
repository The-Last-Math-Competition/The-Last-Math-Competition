# 00000005919

**English.** Definition: Statistical inference is the confidence structure of resampling. Conjecture: For sub-Gaussian noise the bootstrap consistency threshold is given by the sampling density, and the kernel of the density is the log-log window. (bootstrap density log-log window threshold)

**中文。** 定义：统计推断指重采样的置信结构。猜想：亚高斯噪声时自助相合阈值由采样密度给出；且密度的核为重对数窗。（自助相合采样密度重对数窗阈）
