# 00000008684

**English.** Definition: The Brenier map: the optimal map for quadratic cost is a gradient map grad u. Conjecture: u is convex (Brenier's theorem, a convexity law); the regularity of the map is C^{0,alpha} (an alpha-regularity law); the critical regularity of solutions of the Monge-Ampere equation requires strictly convex support plus bounded density (a biconditional law); and the necessity counterexamples (Caffarelli) occur in dimension at least 2 (a two-dimensional counterexample law). (Brenier convex Caffarelli biconditional)

**中文。** 定义：Brenier 极限映射：二次代价的最优映射为梯度映射 ∇u。猜想：u 的凸性（Brenier 定理的）的（凸律）；且凸性的正则（映射的正则性）的 C^{0,α}（α-正则律）；正则的 Monge–Ampère 的解的正则的临界（临界正则律）的支撑的严格凸+密度的有界（双条件律）；条件的必要性的反例（Caffarelli 反例）的维数 ≥ 2 的（二维反例律）。
