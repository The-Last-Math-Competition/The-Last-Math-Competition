**English.** Definition: Anosov boundary maps: the dynamics of boundary automorphisms. Conjecture: Markov decompositions of boundary maps: Markov partitions exist for boundary automorphisms of hyperbolic groups, with the spectral radius of the transition matrix equal to the entropy of the map. (boundary Markov spectral radius entropy)

**中文。** 定义：Anosov 边界映射：边界自同构的动力学。猜想：边界映射的 Markov 分解：双曲群边界自同构的 Markov 划分存在且划分的转移矩阵的谱半径为映射的熵。（边界 Markov 谱半径熵）
