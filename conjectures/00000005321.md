# 00000005321

**English.** Definition: Khovanov homology is the categorification of the Jones polynomial, and thickness is the width of the grading span. Conjecture: The thickness upper bound of links of crossing number c is the explicit linear expression c/2 plus 2, attained by torus knot families; the minimal thickness is 2, realized by thin (alternating) structures; and the parity of thickness is invariant under isotopy, with minimal variation step 1. (linear optimal bound of link homology thickness)

**中文。** 定义：Khovanov 同调：Jones 多项式的范畴化；厚度指分次跨度的宽度。猜想：交叉数 c 的链环厚度上界为 c/2 加 2 的显式线性式且环面结族达到；厚度的最小值为 2 且由薄结构（交替）实现；厚度的奇偶在狭义同伦下不变且变化的最小步长为 1。（链环同调厚度线性最优界）
