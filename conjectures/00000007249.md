# 00000007249

**English.** Definition: Poincare-Melnikov with the variation of subharmonics. Conjecture: The periodic orbits of minima of the action functional exist, and the minimal count is the star count. (star-counted subharmonic action minima)

**中文。** 定义：Poincaré–Melnikov：次谐的变分。猜想：次谐的极小：作用泛函的极小的周期轨道且道的极小数为星形数。（次谐星形数作用泛函周期轨道极小）
