**EGT-27 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The sample complexity of revenue-optimal mechanisms: the sample bound sample_rev for learning revenue guarantees of optimal mechanisms from m samples. Conjecture: The sample count for epsilon-learning revenue, sample_rev, is Theta((H/epsilon)^2 log n) (a double-logarithmic sample law, H the valuation upper bound); the competitive ratio of prior-free mechanisms (Dhangwatnotai) is the double correction (1/4)(log n)^{-1} (a logarithmic competition law); and the optimal use of samples (variance of double-difference estimation) converges at 1/m (an m-convergence law). (revenue sample complexity prior-free competition)

**中文。** 定义：机制的收入最优的样本复杂度：从 m 个样本学习最优机制的收入保证的样本界 sample_rev。猜想：sample_rev（收入的 ε-学习的样本数）= Θ((H/ε)²·(log n)) 的双对数（H 为估值上界，双对数样本律）；且无分布（Dhangwatnotai 的先验自由）的机制的收入的竞争比为 1/4·(log n)^{-1} 的双修正（对数竞争律）；样本的最优利用（双重差分估计的方差）为 1/m 的收敛（m 收敛律）。
