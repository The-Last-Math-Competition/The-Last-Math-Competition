# 00000005145

**English.** Definition: A non-absolute Hodge cycle violates absoluteness. Conjecture: The minimal field of its existence is an explicit quadratic extension of the rationals, characterized by the difference of Galois dimensions of Chow groups, and the field criterion is complete. (minimal quadratic field of non-absolute Hodge cycles)

**中文。** 定义：非绝对 Hodge 圈指违反绝对性的圈。猜想：其存在的最小域为有理数的显式二次扩域,由周群伽罗瓦维数差刻画且域判据完全。（非绝对 Hodge 最小二次域）
