# 00000008596

**English.** Definition: Cross-validated bandwidths are data-driven bandwidths for local polynomials. Conjecture: The relative error of CV bandwidths is always the power n^{-1/(2s+d)} and optimal; and full adaptivity requires no smoothness prior. (optimal CV bandwidth relative error)

**中文。** 定义：交叉验证带宽指数据驱动的局部多项式带宽。猜想：CV 带宽的相对误差恒为 n 的负 2s 加 d 分之一次幂且最优；且完全自适应不需光滑度先验。（CV 带宽相对误差最优）
