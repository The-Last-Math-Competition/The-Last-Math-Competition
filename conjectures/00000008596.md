**MLC-06 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: local polynomial of cross-validation exponent. Conjecture: mutual pair error of CV is negative 2s d one -fold power of always n and optimal; and completely adaptive not requires smooth a priori. (CV mutual pair error optimal)

**中文。** 定义：交叉验证带宽指数据驱动的局部多项式带宽。猜想：CV 带宽的相对误差恒为 n 的负 2s 加 d 分之一次幂且最优；且完全自适应不需光滑度先验。（CV 带宽相对误差最优）
