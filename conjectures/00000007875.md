# 00000007875

**English.** Definition: The comparison map rho from the Balmer spectrum Spc(C) to the homological spectrum Spec_h(C) (defined by the fixed-point ideals of homology objects) in tensor triangular geometry. Conjecture: For every essentially small, rigid, idempotent-complete stable category, rho is a homeomorphism; and the proof reduces to finite Balmer spectra (finitely many points), where the modularity (diamond property) of the lattice of thick subcategories of homotopy fibers holds, with Mobius function (-1)^r C_r (r the rank, C_r the r-th Catalan number). (Balmer homological homeomorphism)

**中文。** 定义：tensor 三角几何的 Balmer 谱 Spc(C) 与同调谱 Spec_h(C)（由同调对象的不动点理想定义）的比较映射 ρ: Spc(C)→Spec_h(C)。猜想：对一切本质小、刚性、范畴化的稳定范畴，ρ 为同胚；且该定理的证明可约化为有限 Balmer 谱（有限点情形），其点上同调纤维的厚子范畴格的模性（diamond property）成立，格的 Möbius 函数为 (−1)^r·C_r（r 为秩，C_r 为 r-Catalan 数）。
