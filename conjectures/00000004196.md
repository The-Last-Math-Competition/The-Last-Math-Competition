**English.** Definition: Wang-Wei convergence is the distance-decay law from the AC interface to the minimal surface. Conjecture: The decay rate is epsilon^2 times the logarithm of the spectral gap of the interface, the rate constant is explicit, and it diverges in the critical Nitsche case. (divergence of the WW convergence rate constant)

**中文。** 定义：Wang–Wei 收敛指 AC 界面到极小曲面的距离衰减律。猜想：衰减率为 ε² 乘界面谱间隙的对数,速率常数显式,并在临界 Nitsche 情形发散。（WW 收敛速率常数发散）
