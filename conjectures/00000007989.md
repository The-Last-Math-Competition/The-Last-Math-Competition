# 00000007989

**English.** Definition: The minimal universal code family: the parameter scale |U(n)| of a minimal family U of codes achieving capacity on all BMS channels. Conjecture: |U(n)| = O(log n) (a logarithmic-scale family suffices, via rate-polarization hybrid constructions); and the lower bound |U(n)| = Omega(log log n) (from the VC dimension lower bound of the channel space); and the construction of minimal families is equivalent to the existence of nested chains of polarization kernel matrices of length log n, the depth lower bound for recursive nesting of kernel matrices being controlled by the tower height of prime factors of field extensions. (universal polarization kernel chains)

**中文。** 定义：信道普遍性（universality）极小码族：在一切 BMS 信道上达到容量的极小码族 U 的参数规模 |U(n)|。猜想：|U(n)| = O(log n)（对数规模族足够，速率-极性混合构造）；且下界 |U(n)| = Ω(log log n)（由信道空间的 VC 维的下界）；极小族的构造等价于极化核矩阵的嵌套链的存在，链长为 log n（核矩阵的递归嵌套的深度下界由域扩张的素因子塔高控制）。
