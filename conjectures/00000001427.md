**English.** Definition: The slow bond of TASEP is a defect with bond rate r < 1 at the origin. Conjecture: The critical current ratio in stationarity is J(r)/J(1) = r/(2−r); that is, the effective speed of the slow bond is given by bistable matching, and the correction relative to the r^{1/2} heuristic is an exact rational function.

**中文。** 定义：TASEP 的慢键指原点键速率为 r<1 的缺陷。猜想：稳态通过的临界电流比 J(r)/J(1) = r/(2−r);即慢键的有效速度由双稳态匹配给出,修正项相对 r^{1/2} 启发为精确有理式。
