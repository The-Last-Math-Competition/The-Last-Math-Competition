# 00000008863

**English.** Definition: The Sprague-Grundy number is the value of an impartial game. Conjecture: The Grundy number distribution of random finite games tends to a geometric type with ratio controlled by the window; and the kernel of the control is the subgame count. (Grundy subgame geometric window)

**中文。** 定义：Sprague–Grundy 数为公平游戏的值。猜想：随机有限博弈的 Grundy 数分布趋于几何型且比率为窗口控制；且控制的核为子博弈数。（Grundy 子博弈数几何分布窗）
