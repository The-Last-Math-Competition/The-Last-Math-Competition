**English.** Definition: The isoperimetric deficit is the difference quantity of the perimeter-area ratio. Conjecture: The graded lower bound of the deficit is given by bridge-type chains with explicit per-level constants, and the closure of the chain is dimension-free. (graded bridge chains of isoperimetric deficits)

**中文。** 定义：等周亏格为周长面积比的差量。猜想：亏格的分级下界由桥型链给出且每级常数显式；且链的封闭为维数无关。（等周亏格桥链分级）
