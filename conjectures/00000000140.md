**English.** Conjecture: Prime-weighted minimal spanning tree: if the edge weights of K_n are i.i.d. with the "prime distribution" (natural density on the primes), then the MST total weight divided by n converges to an explicit constant c (given by a Stieltjes integral involving li).

**中文。** 猜想：素数权最小生成树:K_n 各边 iid 服从"素数分布"(取素数的自然密度),则 MST 总重/n 收敛到显式常数 c(由 li 的 Stieltjes 积分给出)。
