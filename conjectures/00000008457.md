**KH3-17 ★** — source: 267_纽结不变量与低维拓扑III.md

**English.** Definition: The distance d of a Heegaard splitting is the distance of its splitting surface in the curve complex. Conjecture: When d is at least 2g-2 the splitting is unique; and the volume of manifolds with high-distance splittings has an exponential lower bound in d. (volume lower bound from Heegaard distance)

**中文。** 定义：Heegaard 分裂距离 d 指其分裂曲面在曲线复形的距离。猜想：d ≥ 2g−2 时分裂唯一；高距离分裂的流形体积有 d 的指数型下界。（Heegaard 距离体积下界）
