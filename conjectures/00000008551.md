# 00000008551

**English.** Definition: The chromatic polynomial of a lattice is the polynomial of its intersection-disjoint maps. Conjecture: The roots of the chromatic polynomial of a geometric lattice alternate in sign; and the lower bound on moduli is controlled by an explicit expression of the critical exponent. (lattice chromatic polynomial root bounds)

**中文。** 定义：格的色多项式为其交不相交映射的多项式。猜想：几何格的色多项式的根符号交替；且模长下界由临界指数的显式式控制。(格色多项式根界)；且根模长下界恒由首项系数符号积控制。
