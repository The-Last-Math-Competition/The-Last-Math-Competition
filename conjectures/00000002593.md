# 00000002593

**OL-03 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: Dedekind's problem: the number M(n) of monotone Boolean functions (the cardinality of the free distributive lattice). Conjecture: The second-order expansion of the known main term of log₂ M(n) exists, with the second term the middle binomial coefficient times an explicit constant correction; the correction comes from the limiting distribution of symmetry classes in the duality decomposition. (Dedekind number second order)

**中文。** 定义：Dedekind 问题：单调布尔函数个数 M(n)（即自由分配格的元素数）。猜想：log₂ M(n) 的已知主项的次阶展开存在且次项为中间二项式系数乘以显式常数修正；修正常数由对偶分解的对称类的极限分布给出。（Dedekind 数次阶）
