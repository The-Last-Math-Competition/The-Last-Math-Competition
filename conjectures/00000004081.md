# 00000004081

**English.** Definition: An equivariant bifurcation is a bifurcation of a compact-group symmetric system, with isotropy subgroups classifying the fixed-point types. Conjecture: For the three-dimensional SO(3)-equivariant Hopf bifurcation the maximal strict chain length of isotropy subgroups is 3 and the count of chains is 12, both following from the subgroup lattice. (counting equivariant isotropy chains)

**中文。** 定义：等变分叉指紧群对称系统的分叉,迷向子群刻画不动点型。猜想：三维 SO(3) 等变 Hopf 的迷向子群最大严格链长为 3,链的计数为 12,由子群格组合给出。（等变迷向链计数）
