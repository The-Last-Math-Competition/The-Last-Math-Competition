# 00000006306

**English.** Definition: The spectral leading layer of the prismatic formula. Conjecture: The leading layer of the filtration is the canonical sheaf; the exactness of the coefficient is the flatness of the connection; and the criterion of the flatness is the Gauss-Manin vanishing. (canonical leading-layer coefficients of prismatic spectra)
