**English.** Definition: transcendental: τ(Γ) (Γ of τ function (Jimbo–Miwa–Ueno τ) in algebra initial data under of isomonodromy deformation is uniformization parameter lattice point). Conjecture: logarithm derivative (d/dt)log τ of τ function in algebra point of nondegenerate isomonodromy system is transcendental function; its constant (Stokes constant) is transcendental number or algebraically independent set; exception by pair set of algebra solution completely classification, its Stokes constant is square (number theory rigidity) of Salem number.

**中文。** 定义：isomonodromy 形变的 τ 函数（Jimbo–Miwa–Ueno τ）在代数初值下的超越性：τ(Γ)（Γ 为单值化参数格点）。猜想：非退化 isomonodromy 系统的 τ 函数在代数点的对数导数 (d/dt)log τ 为超越函数；其单值性常数（Stokes 常数）为超越数或代数无关组；例外情形由代数解完全分类，其 Stokes 常数的绝对值集合为 Salem 数的平方（数论刚性）。
