# 00000007937

**English.** Definition: Transcendence of isomonodromic deformation tau functions (Jimbo-Miwa-Ueno tau) at algebraic initial data: tau(Gamma) with Gamma a uniformization parameter lattice. Conjecture: The logarithmic derivative (d/dt) log tau of the tau function of a nondegenerate isomonodromy system at algebraic points is transcendental; its monodromy constants (Stokes constants) are transcendental or algebraically independent sets; and the exceptional cases are completely classified by algebraic solutions, the absolute value set of whose Stokes constants are squares of Salem numbers (arithmetic rigidity). (isomonodromic tau Salem Stokes rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：isomonodromy 形变的 τ 函数（Jimbo–Miwa–Ueno τ）在代数初值下的超越性：τ(Γ)（Γ 为单值化参数格点）。猜想：非退化 isomonodromy 系统的 τ 函数在代数点的对数导数 (d/dt)log τ 为超越函数；其单值性常数（Stokes 常数）为超越数或代数无关组；例外情形由代数解完全分类，其 Stokes 常数的绝对值集合为 Salem 数的平方（数论刚性）。
