# 00000008426

**English.** Definition: The design-type orthogonality of Latin squares: the superposition of two Latin squares. Conjecture: For n at least 4n_0 there exist exactly n-1 mutually orthogonal Latin squares if and only if n is a prime power; beyond prime powers the size of the largest orthogonal family is at most n - 1 - log_2(log n). (non-prime-power deficit of the MOLS bound)

**中文。** 定义：拉丁方的设计型正交指两个拉丁方的叠合。猜想：n ≥ 4n₀ 后恰有 n−1 个两两正交的拉丁方当且仅当 n 为素数幂；素数幂之外最大正交族的大小 ≤ n − 1 − log₂(log n)。（MOLS 界的非素幂亏）
