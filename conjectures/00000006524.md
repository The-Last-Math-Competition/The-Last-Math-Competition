# 00000006524

**English.** Definition: Getzler rescaling is the rescaling of Clifford variables. Conjecture: The supertrace limit is always the local index density; the order selection of the rescaling is dimension balancing; and the convergence rate of the rescaling limit is of root order. (rescaled supertrace local limit)

**中文。** 定义：Getzler 重整为 Clifford 变量的再标度。猜想：超迹极限恒为局部指标密度；且重整的阶选择为维数配平；且重整极限的收敛速率为根号阶。（重整超迹局部极限）
