# 00000009759

**English.** Definition: PSLQ integer relation detection: the necessary precision for deciding numerical identities. Conjecture: The decimal precision needed to recover an integer relation of degree d and height H is d times log H plus a constant; the precision lower bound is tight, and below it relation recovery is impossible in the signal-to-noise sense. (PSLQ precision lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：PSLQ 整数关系探测：数值恒等式判定的必要精度。猜想：恢复次数 d 高度 H 的整数关系所需的十进制精度为 d 乘 log H 加常数；精度下界为紧且低于该精度时关系恢复在信噪比意义下不可能。（PSLQ 精度下界）
