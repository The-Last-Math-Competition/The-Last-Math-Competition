**English.** Definition: The dimension of tensors: geometric dimension. Conjecture: The dimension of tensor spaces is governed by parameter counting: the dimension of the rank-r locus is the naive parameter count unless it exceeds the ambient dimension, and the count closes under the parameters. (tensor dimension parameter counting)
