# 00000008213

**English.** Definition: The BGG resolution of Harish-Chandra modules: the length bgg_len(pi) of the projective resolution of principal series. Conjecture: bgg_len equals the logarithm of |W| (a Weyl logarithm law); the union of the support cones of the weights of the Verma modules in the resolution is the totally positive cone (a total positivity union law); the combinatorics of the arrows of the BGG resolution of Verma modules (the quiver) is the Bruhat graph (a Bruhat graph law); and the length of the shortest form of the resolution (the minimal resolution) equals the length of the longest Weyl element (a longest-element length law). (BGG Weyl logarithm Bruhat resolution)

**中文。** 定义：Harish-Chandra 模的射影维数的 BGG 分解：主系列的射影分解的长度 bgg_len(π)。猜想：bgg_len = |W| 的对数（Weyl 对数律）；且分解的项的 Verma 模的权的支撑锥的并 = 全正锥（全正并律）；Verma 的 BGG 分辨的箭头的组合（箭图）为 Bruhat 图（Bruhat 图律）；分辨的最短形式（极小分辨）的长度 = 最长 Weyl 元的长度（最长元长度律）。
