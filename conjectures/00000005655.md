**English.** Definition: The cover time is the time to visit all vertices. Conjecture: The cover formula of graph families is the explicit tightening of the classical bounds, the tightening given by the maximal hitting-time ratio, attained at path graphs. (hitting-ratio tightened formula of cover times)

**中文。** 定义：覆盖时间指访问全部顶点的时间。猜想：图族的覆盖公式为经典上下界的显式紧化,紧化由最大击中时间比给出且路图处达到。（覆盖时间击中比紧化公式）
