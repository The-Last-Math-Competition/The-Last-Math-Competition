# 00000008447

**English.** Definition: h(V) denotes the number of isomorphism classes of hyperbolic knots with volume at most V. Conjecture: h(V) <= exp((3/pi)V), with the constant 3/pi optimal as attained by the growth of link families; and the Jorgensen-Thurston limit law of h(V) is exponential. (volume counting exponent of hyperbolic knots)

**中文。** 定义：h(V) 指体积至多 V 的双曲结同构类个数。猜想：h(V) ≤ exp((3/π)V) 且常数 3/π 由链环族的增长达到最优；h(V) 的 Jorgensen–Thurston 极限律为指数型。（双曲结体积计数指数）
