# 00000007421

**English.** Definition: The arithmetic of combinatorics II with the deepening of additive combinatorics. Conjecture: The convex structure of integer sumsets holds, and the dimension of the structure is the Freiman dimension. (Freiman-dimension sumset convexity)

**中文。** 定义：组合的算术 II：加性组合的深化。猜想：sumset 的凸域：整数集合的和集的凸结构且构的维数为 Freiman 维。（sumset Freiman 维整数和集凸结构维数）
