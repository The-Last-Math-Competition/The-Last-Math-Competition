**English.** Definition: Heterogeneous service has servers of unequal rates. Conjecture: The mean-field uniqueness criterion is the explicit condition of connected support of the rate distribution, two fixed points coexist for separated supports, and the criterion is necessary and sufficient. (connected-support uniqueness criterion of heterogeneity)
