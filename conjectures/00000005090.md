# 00000005090

**English.** Definition: The eigensheaf category and the Satake category are two layers of geometric objects. Conjecture: There exist two groups with equivalent eigensheaf categories but non-isomorphic Satake categories, and the separation is realized explicitly by a pair of same-rank different-root-system groups. (same eigensheaves-different Satake root-system separation)

**中文。** 定义：特征层范畴与 Satake 范畴指两层几何对象。猜想：存在两群的特征层范畴等价而 Satake 范畴不同构,分离由同秩异根系的对显式实现。（特征层同-Satake 异根系分离）
