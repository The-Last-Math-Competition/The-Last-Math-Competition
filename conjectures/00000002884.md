# 00000002884

**English.** Definition: Indices with singularities: the elliptic theory of conically singular spaces. Conjecture: The index correction of conical points: the index is that of the smooth part plus the contribution of cone coefficients, the contribution being the index of the asymptotic operator plus an explicit eta correction; the finiteness of the correction is the convergence of the cone spectrum. (cone index asymptotic correction)

**中文。** 定义：带奇异性的指标：conically 奇异空间的椭圆理论。猜想：锥奇点的指标修正：指标为光滑部分的指标加锥系数的贡献且贡献为渐进算子的指标加显式 eta 修正；修正的有限性为锥谱的收敛。（锥指标渐进修正）
