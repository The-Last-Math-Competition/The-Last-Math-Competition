**English.** Definition: Series poles are the analytic continuation singularities of growth series. Conjecture: The pole multiplicity table of lattice groups is given by resonance coincidences of closed geodesics, with multiplicities an explicit set of prime powers; the table at low genus (at most 3) is finite; and the first example of multiplicity above 1 is at genus 2 with a double pole. (resonance prime-power table of pole multiplicities)

**中文。** 定义：级数极点：增长级数的解析延拓奇点。猜想：格群的极点重数表由闭测地线的共振重合给出且重数为素数幂的显式集；低亏格（不超过 3）的表为有限；重数大于 1 的首例在亏格 2 且极点为二重。（极点重数共振素幂表）
