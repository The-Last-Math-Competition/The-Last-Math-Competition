# 00000002278

**English.** Conjecture: The equality between the heavy-tail exponent α of the renewal measure of a subordinator and the tail exponent of the first-exit-time distribution is an explicit conjugate of α (renewal-first-exit identity).

**中文。** 猜想：次ordinator的更新测度的重尾指数 α 与首出时间分布的尾指数的等式为 α 的显式共轭(更新-首出等式)。
