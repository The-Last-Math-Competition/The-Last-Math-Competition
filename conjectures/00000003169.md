**English.** Definition: The algebraic-geometric side of restricted isometry: zeros of sparse polynomial systems. Conjecture: The Kushnirenko count for sparse polynomials: the number of isolated zeros of a system is the mixed volume of Newton polytopes, with the tightness of the count complete intersections. (Kushnirenko mixed volume tightness)

**中文。** 定义：受限等距的代数几何侧：稀疏多项式系统的零点。猜想：稀疏多项式的 Kushnirenko 计数：方程组的孤立零点数为 Newton 多面体的混合体积且计数的紧性为完全交。（Kushnirenko 混合体积紧性）
