**English.** Definition: The quasipolynomial degree is the growth order of the characteristic function. Conjecture: Within three generators, the degree upper bound is an explicit quadratic expression of the generator count and the presentation length, attained by monomial quotient modules, and closed. (quadratic degree upper bound of quasipolynomials)
