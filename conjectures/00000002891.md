# 00000002891

**English.** Definition: Finite projective planes PG(2,q): point-line incidence structures over fields of order q³. Conjecture: The asymptotic sparsity of non-Desarguesian planes: among planes of prime-power order the number of non-Desarguesian isomorphism classes is asymptotically q^{q²/2} times little-o; the sparsity count is constrained by the automorphism group of coordinatizations. (non-Desarguesian asymptotic count)

**中文。** 定义：有限射影平面 PG(2,q)：q³ 阶有限域上的点线关联结构。猜想：非 Desargues 平面的渐近稀疏：阶为素数幂的平面中非 Desargues 的同构类数为 q 的 q²/2 次幂乘小 o 的渐近；稀疏性的计数由坐标化的自同构群约束。（非 Desargues 渐近计数）
