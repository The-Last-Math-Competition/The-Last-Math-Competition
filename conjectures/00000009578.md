# 00000009578

**English.** Definition: The relation between the entropy of non-atomic ergodic SL(2,R)-invariant measures and the dimension of their support is the core of entropy rigidity. Conjecture: Every SL(2,R)-invariant ergodic measure satisfies the linear lower bound entropy at least (1/2) dim(support); equality holds if and only if the support is a linear submanifold and the measure the affine Lebesgue restriction, so equality rigidity completely characterizes the Lagrangian case. (entropy-dimension linear inequality)

**中文。** 定义：非原子遍历 SL(2,R) 不变测度的熵与其支撑维数的关系是熵刚性方向的核心问题。猜想：一切 SL(2,R) 不变遍历测度满足熵 ≥ (1/2)·dim(支撑) 的线性下界；等号当且仅当支撑为线性子流形且测度为仿射 Lebesgue 限制，等号刚性完全刻画拉格朗日情形。（熵-维数线性不等式）
