# 00000007737

**English.** Definition: The squared singular values of truncated products of k independent Haar unitaries converge to the k-fold free multiplicative convolution mu_k of the uniform law nu on [0,1]. Conjecture: The lower endpoint x_k of the support of mu_k satisfies the explicit formula x_k = exp(-2k+2) k^{2k/(k-1)}, the density has exponent -1/2 singularities at both endpoints; and the Stieltjes transform of mu_k satisfies a degree-k polynomial equation with coefficients that are integer-coefficient polynomials in k. (support endpoint of unitary product convolutions)

**中文。** 定义：k 个独立 Haar 酉矩阵的截断乘积 U_1⋯U_k 的奇异值平方分布收敛到 [0,1] 上均匀律 ν 的 k 重自由乘法卷积 μ_k=ν ⊠ … ⊠ ν。猜想：μ_k 的密度支撑的下端点 x_k 满足显式公式 x_k = exp(-2k+2)·k^{2k/(k-1)}，密度在支撑两端点的奇性指数均为 -1/2；μ_k 的 Stieltjes 变换满足一个次数为 k 的多项式方程，其系数为 k 的整系数多项式。
