**English.** Definition: singular value square distribution of truncation product U_1⋯U_k of k element independent Haar unitary matrix converges k -fold self- of to [0,1] on all ν by times normal convolution μ_k=ν ⊠ … ⊠ ν. Conjecture: under endpoint x_k of density support of μ_k satisfies exponent all of explicit formula x_k = exp(-2k+2) · k^{2k/(k-1)}, density in support two endpoint is Stieltjes of -1/2; μ_k satisfies a -fold number is polynomial equation, its coefficient of k is coefficient polynomial of k.

**中文。** 定义：k 个独立 Haar 酉矩阵的截断乘积 U_1⋯U_k 的奇异值平方分布收敛到 [0,1] 上均匀律 ν 的 k 重自由乘法卷积 μ_k=ν ⊠ … ⊠ ν。猜想：μ_k 的密度支撑的下端点 x_k 满足显式公式 x_k = exp(-2k+2)·k^{2k/(k-1)}，密度在支撑两端点的奇性指数均为 -1/2；μ_k 的 Stieltjes 变换满足一个次数为 k 的多项式方程，其系数为 k 的整系数多项式。
