# 00000001407

**HT-17 △** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral gap of the Cayley graph of the braid group B_n (standard Artin generators) is the first nontrivial eigenvalue of its adjacency operator; the spectrum of a knot is the Laplace spectrum of its complement. Conjecture: For a knot K presented as a closed braid, the spectral gap is at least c·δ(B), where δ is the Cayley spectral gap of its braid word and c a universal constant; the spectrum-knot correspondence yields a lower bound on the hyperbolic volume.

**中文。** 定义：辫群 B_n 的 Cayley 图(标准 Artin 生成元)的谱间隙指其邻接算子的第一非平凡特征值;纽结的谱指其补空间 Laplace 谱。猜想：闭辫表示的纽结 K 的谱间隙 ≥ c·δ(B),δ 为其辫字的 Cayley 谱间隙,c 为普适常数;谱-纽结对应给出双曲体积的下界。
