**TR3-28 ★** — source: 137_数论V_超越方法.md

**English.** Definition: The critical values of an L-function are the special values at the central symmetric point. Conjecture: The first infinite algebraically independent family is the set of values at one of the Dirichlet L-functions with prime indices, and the independence follows from the extension of the elliptic method to non-CM characters. (infinite prime family of critical L-values)

**中文。** 定义：L 函数临界值指中心对称点的特殊值。猜想：首个无穷代数无关族为素数指标狄利克雷 L 在一处的值集,无关性由椭圆方法向非复乘特征的推广给出。（L 临界值素数无穷族）
