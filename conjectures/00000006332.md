# 00000006332

**English.** Definition: The construction class is the product-closed construction of Hadamard-type designs. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the product closure of the construction class, and the covering density of the quadratic-residue difference-set construction tends to one; the asymptotic error is of squared-reciprocal type; and the order of the product is the product of the factors. (asymptotic covering-density conversion of product closures)

**中文。** 定义：构造类：Hadamard 型设计的乘积封闭构造。猜想：换算常数为构造类的乘积封闭显式且二次剩余差集构造的覆盖密度渐近一；渐近的误差为平方倒数型；乘积的阶为因子的乘积。（乘积封闭覆盖密度渐近换算）
