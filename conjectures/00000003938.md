# 00000003938

**English.** Definition: The barcode is the interval decomposition of a Floer persistence module, and the bottleneck distance is the worst half-width over matchings of intervals. Conjecture: The bottleneck distance of a symplectic isotopy is controlled by the Hofer distance with constant 1, and 1 is optimal, attained by the two-point space. (barcode-Hofer optimal constant)

**中文。** 定义：条形码指 Floer 持续性模的区间分解,bottleneck 距离指区间匹配的最劣半宽。猜想：辛同痕的瓶颈距离被 Hofer 距离以常数 1 一致控制,且 1 为最优,由两点空间达到。（条形码-Hofer 最优常数）
