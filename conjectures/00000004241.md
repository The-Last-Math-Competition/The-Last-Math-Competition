**English.** Definition: The Donaldson series is the generating function of the Donaldson invariants, a modular form. Conjecture: The constant of its leading Fourier coefficient is an explicit combination of a Rokhlin-type invariant of the manifold and the perturbation data, and along low-genus directions the series terminates finitely. (leading-term formula of the Donaldson series)

**中文。** 定义：Donaldson 级数指 Donaldson 不变量的母函数,为模形式。猜想：其 Fourier 系数的首项常数由流形的 Rokhlin 型不变量与扰动数据的显式组合给出,且沿低亏格方向级数有限终止。（Donaldson 级数首项公式）
