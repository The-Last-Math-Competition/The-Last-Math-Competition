**English.** Definition: interval commutative map (IET) with respect to Lebesgue measure and Rauzy–Veech measure almost everywhere weak mixing, by mixing unknown of Avila–Forni proof; strong. Conjecture: L^p of IET for all p<∞ is mixing, and decay not in logarithm rate; mixing rate without uniform exponent lower bound, by logarithm of period approximate gives counterexample family. (IET logarithm mixing)

**中文。** 定义：典型区间交换映射（IET）关于 Lebesgue 测度与 Rauzy–Veech 测度几乎处处弱混合，由 Avila–Forni 证明；更强 的混合性未知。猜想：典型 IET 对一切 p<∞ 的 L^p 可观测为混合，且关联衰减不快于对数速率；混合速率无一致指数下界，由周期近似的对数慢化给出反例族。（IET 对数混合）
