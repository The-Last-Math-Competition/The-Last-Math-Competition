**English.** Definition: Typical interval exchange transformations (IETs) are almost everywhere weakly mixing with respect to Lebesgue and Rauzy-Veech measure, proven by Avila-Forni; stronger mixing properties are unknown. Conjecture: Typical IETs mix for all L^p observables with p finite, and the correlation decay is no faster than logarithmic; and there is no uniform exponential lower bound on the mixing rate, with counterexample families given by logarithmic slowing under periodic approximation. (IET logarithmic mixing)

**中文。** 定义：典型区间交换映射（IET）关于 Lebesgue 测度与 Rauzy–Veech 测度几乎处处弱混合，由 Avila–Forni 证明；更强 的混合性未知。猜想：典型 IET 对一切 p<∞ 的 L^p 可观测为混合，且关联衰减不快于对数速率；混合速率无一致指数下界，由周期近似的对数慢化给出反例族。（IET 对数混合）
