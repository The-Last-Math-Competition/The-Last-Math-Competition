**GL3-11 ★** — source: 155_几何Langlands的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The representation category is the geometric analogue of the representation category of Lie theory. Conjecture: The block numbering of the first layer of GL_3 is always converted by double cosets of the affine Weyl group, and the conversion preserves central characters. (block-numbering formula of the first GL_3 layer)
