**English.** Definition: Motivic stability is the stabilization of sequences of motives. Conjecture: The stable-range upper bound of two-parameter families is an explicit linear expression of the sum of degree and weight, given by higher conversion, and the bound is tight. (weight-sum linear upper bound of motivic stability)
