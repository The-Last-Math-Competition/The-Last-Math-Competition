**English.** Definition: Distance clouds with the compactness structure of Gromov-Hausdorff distance. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the compactness of distance with compact bounds under bounded offset; the bound spectrum is explicit with spectrum endpoints the offset constants; and the loss of compactness is at dimension blow-up. (bounded-offset compact-distance conversion)
