# 00000004539

**English.** Definition: The random ground-state degeneracy is the number of ground-state configurations of a random constraint system. Conjecture: The entropy-rate exponent of the degeneracy has an explicit formula for random k-SAT, the exponent is continuous in the density and remains continuous at the threshold, and the left derivative of the entropy rate at the condensation threshold equals the entropy of the warning-propagation fixed point. (continuity of the ground-state degeneracy entropy rate)

**中文。** 定义：随机基态简并度指随机约束系统基态构型数。猜想：简并度的熵率指数在随机 k-SAT 有显式公式,指数随密度连续且在阈值处保持连续。(基态简并熵率连续性)；且熵率在凝聚阈值的左导数等于警告传播不动点的熵。
