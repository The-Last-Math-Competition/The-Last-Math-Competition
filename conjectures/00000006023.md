# 00000006023

**HS2-13 ◆** — source: 186_哈密顿系统.md

**English.** Definition: Exponential stability is the Nekhoroshev-type estimate of long-time action freezing. Conjecture: The exponent pair is the optimal tradeoff curve under steepness, the kernel of the tradeoff being the directional minimum; the tradeoff curve is convex, the convexity given by the monotonicity of the steepness exponent; and the product of the two exponents is bounded above by a dimension-type constant. (directional-minimum steepness tradeoff of exponential stability)

**中文。** 定义：指数稳定：Nekhoroshev 型长时间作用量冻结的估计。猜想：指数对在陡性下为最优权衡曲线且权衡的核为方向极小；权衡曲线为凸且凸性由陡性指数的单调性给出；两指数的乘积有上界且上界为维数型常数。（指数稳定方向极小陡性权衡）
