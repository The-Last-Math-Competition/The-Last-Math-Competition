**English.** Definition: Homology and quantum cohomology are two layers of algebraic structure. Conjecture: There exist two Fanos with isomorphic integral homology rings but non-isomorphic quantum cohomology, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same Betti numbers but different spectral radii.
