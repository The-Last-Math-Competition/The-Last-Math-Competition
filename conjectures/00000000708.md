**PA-08 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: Square-periodicity means the partial quotients are eventually periodic and the associated quadratic form represents a square over Q_p. Conjecture: ξ is square-periodic if and only if ξ lies in a quadratic extension; and the criterion is given by the local representation law of the quadratic form. (square-periodicity quadratic field criterion)

**中文。** 定义：平方周期指部分商终周期且对应二次型在 Q_p 上表示平方。猜想：ξ 为平方周期当且仅当 ξ 属于某二次扩域；且判据由二次型的局部表示律给出。（平方周期二次域判据）
