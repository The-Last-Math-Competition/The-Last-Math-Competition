# 00000002289

**English.** Definition: Peacocks are processes increasing in convex order. Conjecture: The Kellerer stopping-time chain of any peacock (known to exist) can be chosen as a minimal-entropy coupling chain (peacock minimal chain).

**中文。** 定义：Peacocks(凸序单增的过程)。猜想：任意 peacock 的 Kellerer 停时链(已知存在)可选取为最小熵耦合链(peacock 极小链)。
