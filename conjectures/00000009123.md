# 00000009123

**English.** Definition: The real algebraic side of the Weyl tube formula. Conjecture: The saddle-point asymptotics of sublevel set volumes is an explicit exponent; and the critical locus of the exponent is the Newton polygon face. (sublevel saddle-point exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl 管公式的实代数侧。猜想：子水平集体积的鞍点渐近为显式指数；且指数的临界为 Newton 多边形面。（子水平鞍点指数）
