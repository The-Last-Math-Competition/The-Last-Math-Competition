# 00000008557

**English.** Definition: The center of a lattice is its direct product decomposition. Conjecture: The lower bound on the number of subdirectly irreducible factors of a lattice is the number of atoms of its congruence lattice; and the upper bound on the atom count is log_2 |L|. (lattice center atom count)

**中文。** 定义：格的中心为直积分拆。猜想：格可分拆为亚直不可约个数的下界为其同余格的原子数；原子个数的上界为 log₂|L|。（格中心原子数）
