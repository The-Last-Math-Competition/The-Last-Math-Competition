# 00000008066

**English.** Definition: The proof-retrieval complexity of Herbrand's theorem: the minimal size hrd(phi) of the Herbrand expansion of first-order valid formulas. Conjecture: The double-exponential gap between hrd(phi) (Herbrand complexity) and the size of cut-free proofs is tight; the double-exponential gap is compressed by half an exponent by the polarization strategy of the dual calculus (Miller's focused proofs, a focusing half-law); and the combinatorial constant of the Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski spectrum theorem (the transfer of finite models) is the depth of Skolem functions (a depth conversion of EM constants). (Herbrand focusing half-exponent compression)

**中文。** 定义：Herbrand 定理的证明检索复杂度：一阶有效式的 Herbrand 扩展式的最小规模 hrd(φ)。猜想：hrd(φ)（Herbrand 复杂度）与切消证明的规模的双指数间隙为紧；且双指数间隙由对偶演算（Miller 的聚焦证明）的极化策略压缩半指数（聚焦压缩半律）；Ehrenfeucht–Mostowski 的谱定理（有限模型的迁移）的组合常数为 Skolem 函数的深度（EM 常数的深度换算）。
