# 00000004362

**English.** Definition: Bokstedt periodicity is the 2-periodicity phenomenon of the periodic element in the coefficients of THH(F_p). Conjecture: The periodic cells of the THH of higher-sphere-tensored ring spectra form a two-dimensional lattice indexed by primes, generated by the periodic element and an explicit degree-one class, with no higher periodicity. (two-dimensional lattice of higher THH periodicity)

**中文。** 定义：Bökstedt 周期性指 THH(𝔽_p) 系数中周期元的 2-周期现象。猜想：高维球张量环谱的 THH 的周期单元构成素数索引的二维格,格由周期元与显式一次类生成,无更高周期。（THH 高维周期二维格）
