# 00000006525

**English.** Definition: Rescaling with the renormalization structure of the spectral action. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit window interval with endpoints given by the curvature scale; the interval closes and the kernel of the closure is the truncation of the heat-kernel expansion; and the truncation error is exponentially small. (curvature-scale window conversion of renormalization)

**中文。** 定义：重整：谱作用量的重整结构。猜想：换算常数为窗口的显式区间且端点由曲率尺度给出；区间封闭且封闭的核为热核展开的截断；截断的误差为指数小。（重整窗口曲率尺度换算）
