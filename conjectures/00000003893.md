# 00000003893

**English.** Definition: A prism (A,I) is a δ-ring A with an invertible ideal I. Conjecture: Prism structures over a fixed perfect ring are at most countable up to isomorphism, and new prisms over (Z_p)_^q correspond one-to-one with choices of topological generators of tilt spaces. (prism structure countability)

**中文。** 定义：棱镜 (A,I) 指 δ-环 A 带可逆理想 I 的结构。猜想：固定完美环上的棱镜结构在同构意义下至多可数,且 (Z_p)_q 上的新棱镜与倾斜空间的拓扑生成元选取一一对应。（棱镜结构可数性）
