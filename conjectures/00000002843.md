# 00000002843

**English.** Definition: Spectral hypergraphs and tensor eigenvalues: the spectrum of the adjacency tensor of a hypergraph. Conjecture: Rigidity of hypergraph spectra: the count of Z-eigenvalues of the adjacency tensor is a Bernstein–Zelevinsky type product, and the symmetries of the spectrum are completely determined by the automorphism group of the hypergraph. (hypergraph spectrum symmetry rigidity)
