**SMA-07 ★** — source: 306_Smale空间与符号动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Sofic shifts are factor images of SFTs, presented by finite graphs with output (hidden Markov models); the Fisher minimal realization is unique. Conjecture: The Fisher minimal state count of a sofic shift equals the rank of the Hankel matrix of its language; the rank is computable in polynomial time by a local criterion on words of length 2n+1, and the minimal realization graph structure is unique. (Fisher minimal realization)

**中文。** 定义：sofic 移位为 SFT 的因子像，由带输出的有限图（隐 Markov 模型）呈现；Fisher 最小实现唯一。猜想：sofic 移位的 Fisher 最小状态数为该移位的语言 Hankel 矩阵的秩；秩可由长 2n+1 词的局部判据在多项式时间内计算，最小实现的图结构唯一。（Fisher 最小实现）
