# 00000004548

**English.** Definition: A linear recurrence sequence is an integer sequence with a constant-coefficient recurrence. Conjecture: The p-adic approximation exponent of its terms is the explicit value one plus the reciprocal of the rank, and the exceptional terms are finite with count bounded by an explicit function of the recurrence order. (p-adic approximation exponent of recurrences)

**中文。** 定义：线性递推序列指常系数递推的整序列。猜想：其项的 p 进逼近指数为一加秩倒数型显式值,例外项有限且个数界为递推阶的显式函数。（递推 p 进逼近指数）
