# 00000001297

**English.** Definition: The fluctuation of Hofstadter's recursion a(n) = a(n−a(n−1)) + a(n−a(n−2)) is the deviation process b(n) = a(n) − n/2. Conjecture: The variance of b(n) diverges: Var(b(n)) ~ c·n^{1/2} with c explicit of the type ζ(3/2)/2π; that is, the fluctuation is long-range correlated and fails the CLT scaling.

**中文。** 定义：Hofstadter 递推 a(n)=a(n−a(n−1))+a(n−a(n−2)) 的波动指偏差过程 b(n)=a(n)−n/2。猜想：b(n) 的方差发散:Var(b(n)) ~ c·n^{1/2},c = ζ(3/2)/2π 型显式;即波动为长程相关且不满足 CLT 标度。
