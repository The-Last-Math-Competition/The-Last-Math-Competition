# 00000008194

**English.** Definition: The table of Manin constants via the modular symbol method: the distribution manin_law of the Manin constant c(f) of newforms f. Conjecture: manin_law (the frequency of c = 1) is 1 (an almost-everywhere-one law: c(f) = 1 almost always); the minimal prime factor of nonzero values of c lies in {2,3} (a two-prime law); the infinitude of c(f) = 2 (the two-constant infinitude) is implied by the Ribet type construction for CM forms (a Ribet construction); and the upper bound c(f) <= 2^{omega(N)} (omega the prime factor count, an omega upper-bound law) is tight (equality) for totally ramified cases with 2 omega (a total ramification tight example). (Manin constant almost-one Ribet omega bound)

**中文。** 定义：模符号法的 Manin 常数的表：新形式 f 的 Manin 常数 c(f) 的分布 manin_law。猜想：manin_law（c=1 的频率）为 1（几乎恒一律：c(f)=1 几乎处处）；且 c 的非 1 值的极小素因子 ∈ {2,3}（双素律）；c(f)=2 的无穷性（二常数无穷）由 CM 形式的 Ribet 型构造（Ribet 构造）暗示；c 的上界 c(f) ≤ 2^{ω(N)}（ω 为素因子数，ω-上界律），上界的紧性（等号）的例为 2·ω 的全分歧（全分歧紧例）。
