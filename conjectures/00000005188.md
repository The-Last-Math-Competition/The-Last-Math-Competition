# 00000005188

**English.** Definition: Full exceptionality means an exceptional collection generating the whole derived category. Conjecture: Counterexamples to fullness (exceptional but not full) are given by explicit pairs with nonzero semiorthogonal complement, the smallest being a four-dimensional hypersurface; the minimal thickness of the complement is 1; and the criterion of the counterexamples is given by the motive rank of the residual component with threshold 2. (minimal counterexample to full exceptionality)

**中文。** 定义：完全例外：生成整个导出范畴的例外集。猜想：非满的反例（例外而不完全）由半正交补非零的显式对给出且最小反例为四维超曲面；补的最小厚度为 1；反例的判据由残余分量的母题秩给出且阈值为 2。（完全例外非满最小反例）
