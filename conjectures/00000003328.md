**English.** Definition: Matrix Schubert ideals: affine versions of Schubert varieties. Conjecture: Schubert polynomials: the Gröbner degeneration of matrix Schubert ideals gives subword formulae, with the monomials of the formulae the Rothe diagrams of permutations. (Schubert Rothe subword Groebner degeneration)
