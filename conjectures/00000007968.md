# 00000007968

**CD3-08 ★** — source: 251_编码理论III_列表解码与局部译码.md

**English.** Definition: The complexity wall of list decoding: the joint trade-off curve W(R,epsilon) between polynomial-time solvability beyond capacity radius epsilon and list size L(epsilon). Conjecture: Above capacity by epsilon > 0, any deterministic algorithm with polynomial list size has query complexity n^{Omega(epsilon)}; and the algorithmic wall at capacity can be broken by folded RS up to a corrected radius of epsilon^2 (the Pareto front of radius versus list size), the exact shape of the front curve being piecewise linear in epsilon (two segments of slopes -1 and -2 meeting at the folding-parameter critical epsilon = 1/2). (folded RS Pareto front shape)

**中文。** 定义：列表解码的复杂度墙：容量半径 ε 之上的列表译码的多项式时间可解性与列表大小 L(ε) 的联合权衡曲线 W(R,ε)。猜想：在 ε>0 的容量之上，任何多项式列表大小的确定性算法的查询复杂度为 n^{Ω(ε)}；且容量处的算法墙可由折叠 RS 突破至 ε² 的修正半径（半径-列表大小的帕累托前沿），前沿曲线的精确形状为 ε 的分段线性（斜率 -1 与 -2 的两段，交点在 ε=1/2 的折叠参数临界）。
