# 00000001763

**RT-13 ★** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: The dimension spectrum of indecomposable modules of SL₂(F_{p²}) in defining characteristic p. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum is piecewise linear in the L-norm ℓ: dim = (ℓ+1)p²·b_ℓ(ℓ+1), with b completely determined by the Langlands parameter; the depth classification of the spectrum is given by the Weyl exchange graph.

**中文。** 定义：SL₂(F_{p²}) 在定义特征 p 的不可分解模的维数谱。猜想：不可分解模的维数谱在 L-范数 ℓ 上为分段线性:dim = (ℓ+1)p²·b_ℓ(ℓ+1),b 由 Langlands 参数完全决定;维数谱的深分类由 wether 交换图给出。
