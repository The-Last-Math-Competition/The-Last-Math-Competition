# 00000002292

**English.** Definition: A signalizer functor assigns p'-subgroups to each p-local subgroup. Conjecture: The minimal rank of a counterexample group to the completeness of balanced signalizer functors is ≥ 4; concretely, balanced functors of rank ≤ 3 are necessarily complete (the obstruction first appears in rank ≥ 4 Weyl-type fusion systems).

**中文。** 定义：signalizer functor 指在每个 p-局部子群上指派 p'-子群的函子。猜想：均衡信号函子的完备性的最小反例群的秩 ≥4;具体地,秩 ≤3 的均衡函子必完备(障碍在秩 ≥4 的 Weyl 型融合系统中首次出现)。
