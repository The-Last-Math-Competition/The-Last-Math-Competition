# 00000004185

**English.** Definition: Min-max with obstacle is the width construction under an obstacle constraint, and the free boundary is the edge of the contact set. Conjecture: The optimal regularity of the free boundary is of C^{1,alpha} type, the Hausdorff dimension of the singular set is at most n - 7, and 7-dimensional singularities do occur. (obstacle free-boundary singularity dimension)

**中文。** 定义：带障碍的极小极大指障碍约束下的宽度构造,自由边界指接触集的边缘。猜想：自由边界最优正则性为 C^{1,α} 型,奇点集的 Hausdorff 维数不超过 n − 7 且 7 维奇点确实出现。（障碍自由边界奇点维数）
