**English.** Definition: Burnside series are generating series of nilpotent quotient counts. Conjecture: The critical value of the convergence radius of the series is always one; the prime-level product of the Euler product converges with analytic continuation having the unit circle as natural boundary, the exception vanishing by tower coincidence. (Burnside series unit circle boundary)

**中文。** 定义：Burnside 级数指幂零商计数的生成级数。猜想：级数的收敛半径的临界值恒为一；Euler 乘积的素层乘积收敛且解析延拓的自然边界为单位圆，例外由塔重合消没。（Burnside 级数单位圆边界）
