# 00000002463

**English.** Definition: cf([ℵ_ω]^{ℵ_0}) is the covering cofinality of the family of countable subsets. Conjecture: The minimal pcf scale of the Schmidt-type counterexample to its equality with ℵ_{ω+1} is 8 (explicitly minimal); the counterexample is constructed from the pcf structure of 8 primes.

**中文。** 定义：cf([ℵ_ω]^{ℵ_0}) 指子集族的覆盖共尾数。猜想：其 =ℵ_{ω+1} 的 Schmidt 型反例的最小 pcf 尺度 = 8(显式最小);反例由 8 个素数的 pcf 结构构造。
