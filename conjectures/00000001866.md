# 00000001866

**RM-26 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The eigenvalue distribution of random permutation matrices (the CLT is known by Diaconis–Shahshahani). Conjecture: The correction term of higher moments is a logarithmic moment of μ: the k-th centered moment is a polynomial in (log n)^{k−1}; the polynomial coefficients are explicit via Bell polynomials of the cycle structure of μ.

**中文。** 定义：随机置换矩阵的特征值分布(Diaconis–Shahshahani 已知 CLT)。猜想：高阶矩的修正项为 μ 的对数矩:第 k 矩的中心化为 (log n)^{k-1} 的多项式;多项式系数由 μ 的 cycle 结构的 Bell 多项式显式。
