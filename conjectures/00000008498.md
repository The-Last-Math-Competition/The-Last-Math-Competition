# 00000008498

**ERO-28 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: The consistency of Abel and Cesaro limits of the ergodic Bellman recursion (the dynamic programming equation of multistep optimal averages). Conjecture: The two limits are always equal (a uniformity law); the decay of their difference is O(1/n) (a harmonic decay law); the optimality of the decay is attained by locking sequences of two-state systems (a two-state matching law); and the decay constant for multistate systems is the reciprocal of the spectral gap of the transition matrix (a spectral gap reciprocal law). (Bellman Abel Cesaro gap reciprocal)

**中文。** 定义：遍历 Bellman 递归（多步最优平均的动态规划方程）的 Abel 极限与 Cesàro 极限的一致性。猜想：两极限恒相等（一致律）；其差的衰减为 O(1/n)（调和衰减律）；衰减的最优性由双态系统的锁定序列取得（双态匹配律）；多状态系统的衰减常数为转移矩阵谱隙的倒数（谱隙倒数律）。
