# 00000008524

**English.** Definition: The dimension of a differential ideal is the differential analogue of the dimension of algebraic varieties. Conjecture: The differential dimension polynomial has rational coefficients; its degree is the number of variables and its leading coefficient is an integral multiple of 1/n!. (differential Hilbert polynomial)

**中文。** 定义：微分理想的维数指其代数簇的微分模拟。猜想：微分维数多项式为有理系数；次数为变量的个数且首项系数为 1/n! 的整倍。（微分 Hilbert 多项式）
