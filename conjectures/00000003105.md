# 00000003105

**GG-05 ★** — source: 89_几何群论II_Gromov双曲与边界.md

**English.** Definition: Cannon's conjecture. Conjecture: If the boundary of a hyperbolic group is the two-sphere then the group is virtually a lattice: the equivalent form — a group with boundary S² acting cocompactly on H³ is equivalent to the combinatorial rigidity of boundary isomorphism, with the rigidity criterion the Moore uniqueness of convergence group actions. (Cannon convergence Moore criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Cannon。猜想：双曲群边界为二维球面则群为虚拟格。猜想：Cannon 的等价形式：边界为 S² 的群作用在 H³ 的余紧等价与边界同构的组合刚性且刚性判据为收敛群作用的 Moore 唯一性。（Cannon 收敛 Moore 判据）
