**English.** Definition: Bohr inequalities with the spectra of trigonometric polynomials. Conjecture: The two-dimensional Bohr radius is an explicit algebraic number, and the high-dimensional extension is the hypersphere integral. (hypersphere-integral exact Bohr radius)
