**TL2-13 ★** — source: 135_范畴逻辑_拓扑斯.md

**English.** Definition: A univalent fibration is a type fibration satisfying Voevodsky's univalence. Conjecture: Univalence is stable under pullback if and only if the path object is injective, and the injectivity constant is given explicitly by the unit of the infinitesimal generator. (injectivity stability criterion of univalent fibrations)

**中文。** 定义：单价纤维指满足 Voevodsky 单价性的类型纤维化。猜想：单价性在拉回下稳定当且仅当路径对象为内射,内射常数由无穷小生成元的单位显式给出。（单价纤维内射稳定判据）
