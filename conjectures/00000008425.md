# 00000008425

**DFG-15 ★** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The strength of regular subgraph covers of graphs: the analogue of covering designs. Conjecture: The density spectrum of lambda-regular t-tree covers is an explicit interval in lambda with the design density as infimum; and the two endpoints of the spectrum are complete covering and exact covering. (density spectrum of regular covers)

**中文。** 定义：图的正则子图覆盖的强度指覆盖设计的模拟。猜想：λ-正则 t-树覆盖的密度谱为 λ 的显式区间且以设计密度为下确界；谱的两个端点为完全覆盖与恰好覆盖。（正则覆盖密度谱）
