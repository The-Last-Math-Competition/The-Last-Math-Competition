**LG2-02 ★** — source: 90_李群微分方程.md

**English.** Definition: The Peter–Weyl theorem: representation decompositions of compact Lie groups. Conjecture: The frequency spectrum of representations: the lattice-point distribution of the highest weights of irreducible representations is the Voronoi stratification of the Weyl group, with the count of the stratification the modular form-ization of the partition function. (Peter-Weyl weight lattice-point distribution)

**中文。** 定义：Peter–Weyl 定理：紧李群的表示分解。猜想：表示的频率谱：不可约表示的最高权的格子点分布为 Weyl 群的 Voronoi 分层且分层的计数为分区函数的模形式化。（Peter-Weyl 权格点分布）
