**English.** Definition: The algebraic independence of elliptic logarithms is their transcendental independence structure. Conjecture: The first instance is the quadruple of periods and quasi-periods of two distinct complex-multiplication elliptic curves, the quadruple is algebraically independent, and the proof needs only the modular method. (first instance of the CM two-curve quadruple)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆对数的代数无关性指其超越独立结构。猜想：首个算例为两条不同复乘椭圆曲线的周期与拟周期的四元组,四元组代数无关且证明仅需模方法。（复乘双曲线四元组首例）
