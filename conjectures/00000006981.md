**English.** Definition: Bilinear estimates are the norm control of products. Conjecture: The sharp constant is given by the wave-speed ratio; the window of the ratio is logarithmic; and the logarithmic window of the wave-speed ratio saturates on the focusing line. (wave-speed-ratio sharp bilinear constant)

**中文。** 定义：双线性估计为乘积的范数控制。猜想：锐常数由波速比给出；且比的窗为对数；且波速比的对数窗在聚焦线饱和。（双线性锐常数波速比）
