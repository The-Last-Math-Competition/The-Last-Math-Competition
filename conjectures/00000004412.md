**GL2-22 ◆** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral distribution of an exchangeable random graph limit is the spectral measure of its graphon integral operator. Conjecture: The characteristic function of the expected spectral measure is given explicitly by the logarithm of the Fredholm determinant of the kernel, and the determinant series converges if and only if the kernel is square integrable. (Fredholm logarithm characterization of exchangeable spectra)

**中文。** 定义：可交换随机图限的谱分布指其 graphon 积分算子的谱测度。猜想：期望谱测度的特征函数由核的 Fredholm 行列式的对数显式给出,行列式级数收敛当且仅当核平方可积。（可交换谱 Fredholm 对数刻画）
