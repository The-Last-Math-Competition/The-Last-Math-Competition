**EC4-06 ★** — source: 142_遍历论的组合_熵与计数.md

**English.** Definition: The singularity exponent is the return-strength parameter of an infinite-measure system. Conjecture: The attainable domain of the exponent of conservative infinite-measure systems is dense between zero and one, and both endpoints are unattainable and approached by logarithmic sequences. (dense endpoints unattainable of the singularity exponent)

**中文。** 定义：奇性指数指无限测度系统的回归强度参数。猜想：保守无限测度系统的奇性指数可达域在零与一间稠密,两端不可达且以对数序列逼近。（奇性指数稠密端不可达）
