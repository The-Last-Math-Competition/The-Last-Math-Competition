# 00000001079

**FG-79 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: CSS-type quantum LDPC codes are built from paired classical codes; their girth is that of the Tanner graph. Conjecture: The ratio of minimum distance to girth of CSS codes is at most log(g)·c with explicit c = 1/2; the extremal ratio is attained by hyperbolic Curtis codes, and all higher-girth families have ratios decaying logarithmically.

**中文。** 定义：CSS 型量子 LDPC 码由成对经典码构成,其围长为 Tanner 图的 girth。猜想：CSS 码的最小距离/围长之比 ≤ log(g)·c,c=1/2 显式;该比值的极值由双曲 Curtis 码取得,一切更高围长族的比值按对数衰减。
