# 00000001239

**English.** Definition: The Merino–Welsh invariant (spanning trees versus connected hyperplanes). Conjecture: All equality cases of τ(G) ≥ τ_c(G) + τ_a(G) − 1 are series-parallel graphs (MW equality graphs).
