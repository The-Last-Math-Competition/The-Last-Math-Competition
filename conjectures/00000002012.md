**English.** Conjecture: The variance of the CLT correction scale in stochastic homogenization is explicit via the effective tensor A_eff of the covariance function: Var = A_eff·log ε (a logarithmic correction); the correction appears in two dimensions (vanishing in higher dimensions).

**中文。** 猜想：随机均匀化的 CLT 修正尺度的方差由协方差函数的有效张量 A_eff 显式:Var = A_eff· log ε(对数修正);修正在二维出现(高维消失)。
