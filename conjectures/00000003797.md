# 00000003797

**English.** Definition: The projected gradient method denotes the projected iteration scheme for variational inequalities. Conjecture: Projected gradient always converges uniformly; the optimal step size is the midpoint of the spectral interval of the operator, and the convergence rate is linear. (projected gradient spectral midpoint step size)

**中文。** 定义：投影梯度法指变分不等式的投影迭代格式。猜想：投影梯度恒一致收敛；且最优步长为算子谱区间的中点且收敛率为线性。（投影梯度谱中点步长）
