# 00000006980

**English.** Definition: Scattering data and the squared norm are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two potentials whose partial scattering data (spectrum only) completely coincide while the squared norms differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit symmetric potential pair with the same spectrum but different norm. (same partial data-different norm separation)

**中文。** 定义：散射数据与平方范数指两层。猜想：存在两势的部分散射数据 (仅谱) 完全相同而平方范数不同,分离由同谱异范数的显式对称势对实现。（部分数据同谱异范数分离）
