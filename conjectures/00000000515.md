# 00000000515

**CA-15 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The Herzog–Kühl relations are proportional identities among the dimensions of homology modules. Conjecture: The extremal solutions of the HK relations are exactly the Betti tables of pure monomial ideals; and the characterization by these identities is complete in rank two. (HK extremal pure monomial characterization)

**中文。** 定义：Herzog–Kühl 关系为同调模维数的比例恒等式。猜想：HK 关系的极值解恰为纯单项式理想的 Betti 表；且等式刻画在秩二时完备。（HK 极值纯单项式刻画）
