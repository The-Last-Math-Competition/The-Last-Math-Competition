**English.** Definition: The McLean volume is the volume of the moduli-space metric. Conjecture: The special Lagrangian moduli volume of a three-dimensional CY is asymptotically an explicit fraction of the polarization volume, the fraction is one for product fibrations, and the formula is exact. (polarization fraction formula of McLean volumes)

**中文。** 定义：McLean 体积指模空间度量的体积。猜想：三维 CY 的特殊拉格朗日模体积渐近为极化体积的显式分式,直积纤维时分式为一且公式精确。（McLean 体积极化分式公式）
