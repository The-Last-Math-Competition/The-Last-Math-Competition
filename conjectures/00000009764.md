# 00000009764

**OIE-04 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Duality of approximation numbers a_n and entropy numbers e_n: comparison of the two sequences quantifying compactness. Conjecture: The supremum of the entropy-to-approximation number ratio of diagonal operators over superpolynomially decaying classes is asymptotically a trade-off of the n-th root of 2; the exact limit of the ratio is explicitly given by the interpolation formula of Lorentz indices. (entropy-approximation number ratio)

**中文。** 定义：逼近数 a_n 与熵数 e_n 的对偶关系：两种量化紧性的序列的比较。猜想：对角算子的熵数与逼近数的比值在超多项式衰减类上的确界渐近为 2 的 n 次根折衷；比值的精确极限由 Lorentz 指标的插值公式显式给出。（熵数-逼近数比值）
