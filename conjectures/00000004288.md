# 00000004288

**English.** Definition: omega-separable means every element is contained in a countable direct summand. Conjecture: The separation of its realizability under the diamond principle versus the Martin axiom is controlled by an explicit class of omega_1 stationary subsets, and the smallest invariant of the separation is the difference of that class. (axiom separation invariant of omega-separability)

**中文。** 定义：ω-可分指每个元素含于可数直和项。猜想：其可实现性在钻石原理与马丁公理下的分离由 ℵ₁ 平稳子集的显式类控制,分离的最小不变量为该类的差。（ω-可分的公理分离不变量）
