# 00000008775

**English.** Definition: Communication avoidance (CA): optimization of communication lower bounds of algorithms. Conjecture: The CA lower bound for matrix multiplication communication is Omega(n^3/sqrt M) (a communication law); the bound is attained by 3D algorithms (an attainment law); TSQR (communication-avoiding QR) is stable (a stability law); and the backward error lower bound is O(epsilon) (an error law). (communication avoiding 3D TSQR stability)

**中文。** 定义：通信避免（CA）：算法的通信下界的优化。猜想：CA 的下界（矩阵乘的通信）为 Ω(n³/√M)（通信律）；且下界的达到（3D 算法）的达到（达到律）；TSQR 的（QR 的通信避免）的稳定（稳定律）；稳定的下界（向后误差的）为 O(ε)（误差律）。
