**OL-25 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: Tree lattices and priority orders: lattice models for combinatorial search. Conjecture: The main asymptotics of the number of linear extensions of a tree poset is factorial divided by the hook product; the q-analogue of the hook formula holds for all d-complete tree lattices, and the q-count has the partition function as its generating kernel. (tree lattice hook q-analogue)

**中文。** 定义：树格与优先序：组合搜索的格模型。猜想：树偏序的线性扩张数的主渐近为阶乘除以 hook 乘积；hook 公式的 q-类比对一切 d-完备树格成立且 q-计数为分拆函数的生成核。（树格 hook q 类比）
