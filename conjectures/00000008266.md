# 00000008266

**SFL-06 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The density existence from nondegeneracy conditions of Malliavin stochastic calculus: the lower bound mall_low on the smallest eigenvalue of the Malliavin covariance matrix. Conjecture: The existence of mall_low (a nondegeneracy lower bound) implies existence and smoothness of the density (a smooth density law); the equivalence of the Hormander condition (bracket generation of vector fields) with Malliavin nondegeneracy has, in the noiseless regime, a broken equivalence whose regularization scale is the square of the noise strength sigma (a square regularization law); and the constant of the (sub-Gaussian) tail of the density is the minimal loss of the Hormander brackets (a minimal-loss tail constant). (Malliavin nondegeneracy Hormander square regularization)

**中文。** 定义：Malliavin 随机微积分的非退化条件的密度存在：Malliavin 协方差矩阵的最小特征值的下界 mall_low。猜想：mall_low（非退化的下界）的存在蕴含密度的存在且密度为光滑（光滑密度律）；且 Hörmander 条件（向量场的括号生成）与 Malliavin 非退化的等价（无噪声情形的等价破缺：噪声的正则化阈）的正则化尺度为噪声强度 σ 的平方（平方正则化律）；密度的尾（亚高斯）的常数为 Hörmander 括号的最小损失（最小损失尾常数）。
