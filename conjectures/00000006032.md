**English.** Definition: The vertices of the image are the extreme-point structure of the momentum image. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit identity of vertex count with isotropy-subgroup count, matching one by one under the action; the deviation of the matching is zero if and only if the action is locally free; and the angle sum of vertices is an integer multiple of 2 pi, the multiple being the number of isotropy types. (isotropy-subgroup identity conversion of vertices)

**中文。** 定义：像的顶点：动量映射像的极点结构。猜想：换算公式为顶点数与迷向子群数的显式等式且作用下逐一对应；对应的偏差为零当且仅当作用为局部自由；顶点的角度和为 2π 的整数倍且倍数为迷向型的个数。（顶点迷向子群等式换算）
