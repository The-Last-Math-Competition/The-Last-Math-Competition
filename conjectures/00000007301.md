# 00000007301

**RT4-01 ★** — source: 229_随机张量.md

**English.** Definition: Random tensors with the spectra and geometry of high-dimensional random arrays. Conjecture: The limit of the extreme-value distribution of symmetric Gaussian tensors is the Parisi functional of spin glasses. (Parisi-limit tensor spectral extrema)

**中文。** 定义：随机张量：高维随机数组的谱与几何。猜想：张量谱的极限：对称高斯张量的极值分布的极限为自旋玻璃的 Parisi 泛函。（张量谱 Parisi 泛函对称高斯极值分布极限）
