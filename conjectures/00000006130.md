# 00000006130

**CG5-30 △** — source: 189_高维概率_集中不等式II.md

**English.** Definition: Quadratic-form concentration and anisotropy are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two log-concave measures with identical quadratic-form constants but different anisotropy, and the separation is realized by an explicit shear pair invariant under quadratic forms. (same quadratic forms-different anisotropy shear separation)

**中文。** 定义：二次型集中与各向异性指两层。猜想：存在两对数凹测度的一切二次型常数相同而各向异性不同,分离由二次型不变的显式剪切对实现。（二次型同-各向异性异剪切分离）
