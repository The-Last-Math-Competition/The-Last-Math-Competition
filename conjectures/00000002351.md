# 00000002351

**OF-01 ★** — source: 64_算子理论II_函数演算与自由分析.md

**English.** Definition: The completely bounded norm of a free polynomial p is its matrix-value supremum. Conjecture: The attainable spectrum of the norm ratio ‖p‖_{cb}/‖p‖ is an interval in the two-variable case; and the endpoints of the interval are given by lexicographic monomials. (cb norm ratio attainable interval)

**中文。** 定义：自由多项式 p 的完全有界范数为其矩阵值上确界。猜想：范数比 ‖p‖_{cb}/‖p‖ 的可达谱在二元情形为区间；且区间的端点由字典单项式给出。（cb 范数比可达区间）
