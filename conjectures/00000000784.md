**English.** Definition: Raynaud semistable degeneration. Conjecture: The number of vertices of the degeneration polyhedron of the family y² = x³ + t^k is explicitly the number of divisors of k (a degeneration polyhedron count).

**中文。** 定义：Raynaud 半稳定退化。猜想：曲线族 y² = x³ + t^k 的退化多面体的顶点数为 k 的因子个数显式(退化多面体计数)。
