**LD-06 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: Freidlin–Wentzell theory: escape rates of small-noise stochastic dynamical systems. Conjecture: Explicitness of the FW quasipotential: for gradient fields the quasipotential is an explicit multiple of the potential difference, and the escape-time logarithmic asymptotics are twice the potential difference over the noise intensity; for non-gradient fields the quasipotential is the minimum of a variational functional whose minimizers satisfy Euler–Lagrange type equations. (FW quasipotential explicit law)

**中文。** 定义：Freidlin–Wentzell 理论：小噪声随机动力系统的逃逸速率。猜想：FW 拟势的显式性：梯度场的拟势为势函数差的显式倍数且逃逸时间对数渐近为二倍势差除噪声强度；非梯度场的拟势为变分泛函极小值且极小轨道满足 Euler–Lagrange 型方程。（FW 拟势显式律）
