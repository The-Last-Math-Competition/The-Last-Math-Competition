# 00000002748

**English.** Definition: The kernel of the standard polynomial: the structure of the kernel of s_k on M_n. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of kernels: dim ker s_k(M_n) is a combination of symmetric-group representations, with the dimension formula given by hook sums of Young diagrams; the zero region of the spectrum is the union of intervals k < 2n. (standard polynomial kernel dimension spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：标准多项式的核：s_k 在 M_n 上的核空间的结构。猜想：核空间的维数谱：dim ker s_k(M_n) 为对称群表示的组合量且维数公式由 Young 图的钩和给出；谱的零区为 k<2n 的区间并。（标准多项式核维数谱）
