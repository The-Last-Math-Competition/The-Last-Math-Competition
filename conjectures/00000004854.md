# 00000004854

**English.** Definition: Filling invariants are the joint control quantities of area and diameter for relative homotopies. Conjecture: They are additive under free products, the constant is the explicit maximal composition of the components, and there is no cross-correction term. (free-product additivity of filling invariants)

**中文。** 定义：填充不变量指相对同伦的面积与直径联合控制量。猜想：其在自由积下满足加性,常数为分量的显式极大合成且无交叉修正项。（填充不变量自由积加性）
