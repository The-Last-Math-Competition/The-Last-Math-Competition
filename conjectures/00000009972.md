**English.** Definition: finite of Brauer group: kernel of p field on smooth projective curve and order. Conjecture: Brauer group finite of local field on smooth projective curve and order is zeta function in 0 of curve and formula of explicit product; order of 1 at order by Newton many edge explicit computation of special fiber. (curve Brauer finite order formula)

**中文。** 定义：p 进域上光滑射影曲线的 Brauer 群：核的有限性与阶。猜想：局部域上光滑射影曲线的 Brauer 群有限且阶为曲线的 zeta 函数在 0 与 1 处阶的显式乘积；阶的公式由特殊纤维的 Newton 多边形显式计算。（曲线 Brauer 有限阶公式）
