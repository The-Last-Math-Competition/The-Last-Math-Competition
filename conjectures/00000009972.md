# 00000009972

**English.** Definition: Brauer groups of smooth projective curves over p-adic fields: finiteness and order of the kernel. Conjecture: The Brauer group of a smooth projective curve over a local field is finite, with order an explicit product of the orders of the curve's zeta function at 0 and 1; the order formula is computable explicitly from the Newton polygon of the special fiber. (curve Brauer finite order formula)

**中文。** 定义：p 进域上光滑射影曲线的 Brauer 群：核的有限性与阶。猜想：局部域上光滑射影曲线的 Brauer 群有限且阶为曲线的 zeta 函数在 0 与 1 处阶的显式乘积；阶的公式由特殊纤维的 Newton 多边形显式计算。（曲线 Brauer 有限阶公式）
