# 00000001300

**CG-100 △** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Orbit-automaton separation means finding a computable map whose orbit structure cannot be simulated by any finite-state summary. Conjecture: The orbit structure of the map f(n) = (n²+1)/2 cannot be simulated by the output of any finite-state automaton; that is, there exists an initial value for which the complement of the orbit visited is dense in every automaton summary.

**中文。** 定义：轨道-自动机分离指寻找不能用有限状态摘要模拟其轨道结构的可计算映射。猜想：映射 f(n)=(n²+1)/2 的轨道结构不能被任何有限状态自动机的输出模拟;即存在初值使其轨道访问的余集在任何自动机摘要中稠密。
