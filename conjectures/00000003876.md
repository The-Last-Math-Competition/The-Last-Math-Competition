# 00000003876

**RM3-26 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The two-component pairing ensemble is a β-gas of two particle types with intra-type β interaction and inter-type coupling g. Conjecture: The joint equilibrium measure is connected for g < g_c and splits into two branches for g > g_c, with g_c explicitly solved from the two component strengths and symmetric in them. (pairing phase separation critical)

**中文。** 定义：两分量配对系综指两类粒子带组内 β 作用与组间耦合 g 的 β-气。猜想：联合平衡测度在 g < g_c 时连通、g > g_c 时分裂为两支,临界值 g_c 由两分量强度显式解出且关于两强度对称。（配对相分离临界）
