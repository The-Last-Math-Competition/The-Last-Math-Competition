# 00000008212

**ORB-12 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The Thomas-Vas obstruction: the index tv_obs of the quantum correction of the geometric obstruction to Lagrangian distributions. Conjecture: tv_obs (the geometric obstruction to Lagrangian realization of a representation) is quantized in integer multiples of 1/2 (a half-integral quantization law); the obstruction vanishes (Lagrangian realization) if and only if the Maslov class is cohomologically trivialized (a Maslov trivialization criterion); and the minimal nonzero example of the obstruction is the order-2 obstruction of the metaplectic correction (a metaplectic order-2 example), whose correction (lifting to the covering group) has unique covering count 2 (a unique double-cover law). (Thomas-Vas half-integral metaplectic obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：Thomas–Vas 阻碍：Lagrange 分布的几何阻碍的量子修正的指标 tv_obs。猜想：tv_obs（表示的 Lagrange 实现的几何障碍）的量子化为 1/2 的整数倍（半整量子律）；且障碍的消没（Lagrange 实现）当且仅当 Maslov 类的上同调平凡化（Maslov 平凡判据）；障碍的最小非零例为 metaplectic 修正的 2 阻碍（metaplectic 二阻碍例），例的修正（覆盖群的提升）的唯一覆盖次数为 2（双覆盖唯一律）。
