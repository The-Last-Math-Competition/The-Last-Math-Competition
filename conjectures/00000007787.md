**English.** Definition: (centroid) body Γ_μ K is symmetrization image of to quantity integral ∫_K⟨x, · ⟩^2 type. Conjecture: volume ratio vol(Γ_μ K)/vol(K) in μ=Lebesgue and all measure family on with respect to Minkowski normal logarithm concave; and E (exponent) and limit relations of volume ratio in p -> ∞ when of L_p (L_p, p≥1) is strictly monotone interpolation, endpoint respectively corresponds to point measure and all ball.

**中文。** 定义：浮身（centroid）体 Γ_μ K 为向量积分 ∫_K⟨x,·⟩^2 型的对称化像。猜想：浮身体积比 vol(Γ_μ K)/vol(K) 在 μ=Lebesgue 与均匀测度族上关于 Minkowski 加法对数凹；且 E 版（指数浮身）与 L_p 版（L_p 浮身，p≥1）的体积比在 p→∞ 时的极限关系为严格单调插值，端点值分别对应单点测度与均匀球。
