# 00000007787

**English.** Definition: The centroid body Gamma_mu K is the symmetrization of the vector integral int_K <x,.>^2. Conjecture: The volume ratio vol(Gamma_mu K)/vol(K) is log-concave under Minkowski addition over the family of Lebesgue and uniform measures; and the E-version (exponential centroid) and L_p version (L_p centroid, p>=1) have volume ratios strictly monotonically interpolating as p tends to infinity, with endpoint values corresponding to point masses and the uniform ball respectively. (log-concave centroid volume ratios)

**中文。** 定义：浮身（centroid）体 Γ_μ K 为向量积分 ∫_K⟨x,·⟩^2 型的对称化像。猜想：浮身体积比 vol(Γ_μ K)/vol(K) 在 μ=Lebesgue 与均匀测度族上关于 Minkowski 加法对数凹；且 E 版（指数浮身）与 L_p 版（L_p 浮身，p≥1）的体积比在 p→∞ 时的极限关系为严格单调插值，端点值分别对应单点测度与均匀球。
