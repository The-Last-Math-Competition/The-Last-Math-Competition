**English.** Definition: The Radon transform: the integration operator over hyperplanes. Conjecture: The support spectrum of the Radon kernel is parity-split by spherical harmonics; and the criterion of the split is vanishing at even degrees. (Radon kernel parity spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Radon 变换为超平面上的积分算子。猜想：Radon 核的支撑谱为球面调和的奇偶分型；且分型的判据为偶次消没。（Radon 核奇偶谱）
