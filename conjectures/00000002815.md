**English.** Definition: Entropy-production inequalities: the deviation form of fluctuation relations. Conjecture: The difference of the positive and negative deviation rates is always the entropy production rate itself, exact in the Markov framework; correction terms of the difference law appear only on the time-reversal-breaking phase-transition layer. (entropy production difference law exactness)

**中文。** 定义：熵产率不等式：涨落关系的偏差形式。猜想：正负偏差的速率之差恒为熵产率本身且差律在马尔可夫框架精确；差律的修正项仅在时间反演破缺的相变层出现。（熵产率差律精确性）
