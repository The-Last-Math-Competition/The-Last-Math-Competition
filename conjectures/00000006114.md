**English.** Definition: Contraction is the moment-compression constant structure of nonlinear maps. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit cube-root law of three parameters, the leading coefficient of the near-end expansion exact; the tight example of the cube-root law is sign-function contraction; and the next term of the expansion is logarithmic with nonnegative coefficient. (near-end expansion constant of the cube-root law)
