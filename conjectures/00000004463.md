**English.** Definition: S-duality is the electric-magnetic duality of gauge theory. Conjecture: The DT generating function is covariant under an explicit subgroup of the modular group with covariant weight minus one half, and the operator spectrum is completely permuted under the transformation. (DT modular covariant weight minus one half)

**中文。** 定义：S-对偶指规范理论的电磁对偶。猜想：DT 母函数在模群的显式子群下协变,协变权为负二分之一,算子谱在变换下完全置换。（DT 模群协变权负半）
