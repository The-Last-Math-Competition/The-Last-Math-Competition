# 00000003435

**NA2-05 ★** — source: 100_赋范空间与逼近论II.md

**English.** Definition: Bernstein widths: widths compared with balls. Conjecture: The extremum of Bernstein widths: the Bernstein width of symmetric sets is the norm of extremal polynomials, with the computation of the norm the Chebyshev principle. (Bernstein Chebyshev extremal principle)

**中文。** 定义：Bernstein 宽度：与球比较的宽度。猜想：Bernstein 宽度的极值：对称集合的 Bernstein 宽度为极值多项式的范数且范数的计算为 Chebyshev 原理。（Bernstein Chebyshev 极值原理）
