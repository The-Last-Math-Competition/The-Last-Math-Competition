# 00000005737

**English.** Definition: The stratification is the singularity hierarchy of the free boundary. Conjecture: The three-dimensional blow-up set splits into three explicit layers, regular, symmetric, and tip, separated by frequency values; the frequency interval of the regular layer is open with endpoints excluded; and the minimal frequency of the tip layer is 2, locked by the symmetry of cone solutions. (three-layer frequency separation structure in three dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：分层：自由边界的奇点层级。猜想：三维的爆破集分为正则层、对称层与顶尖层三层显式结构且由频率值分隔；正则层的频率区间为开且端点不属于；顶尖层的最小频率为 2 且值由锥解的对称性锁定。（三维频率三层分隔结构）
