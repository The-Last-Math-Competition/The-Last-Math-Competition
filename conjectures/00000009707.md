**English.** Definition: NLS on hyperbolic space: Morawetz estimates with interactive growth control scattering. Conjecture: The time-integrated interaction term of the imaginary Morawetz identity for the three-dimensional cubic NLS on hyperbolic space is bounded above by C E with optimal constant; the bound is given explicitly by the isoperimetric constant of hyperbolic volume growth. (hyperbolic Morawetz constant)
