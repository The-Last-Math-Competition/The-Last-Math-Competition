**English.** Definition: The Cartan form is the graded symmetric structure of categorical homology. Conjecture: The twist-product coefficients are dimension-type and the closed combination is graded symmetry; the weight of graded symmetry is the Koszul duality; and the sign of the coefficients is positive. (graded-symmetric twist-product coefficients of the Cartan form)

**中文。** 定义：卡氏形式：范畴同调的分次对称结构。猜想：扭积系数为维数型且封闭的组合为分次对称；分次对称的权为 Koszul 双对偶；系数的符号为正。（卡氏扭积分次对称系数）
