**SY-06 ★** — source: 55_辛几何与镜像对称.md

**English.** Definition: The Gromov width of a toric domain is the supremum of diameters of embedded balls. Conjecture: The width is given explicitly by inscribed-disk paths of the moment polytope; and the combination of paths is an edge chain. (toric edge-chain inscribed-disk width formula)

**中文。** 定义：toric 域的 Gromov 宽为嵌入球直径上确界。猜想：宽由 moment 多面形的内切圆盘路径显式；且路径的组合为棱链。（toric 棱链内切圆盘宽度公式）
