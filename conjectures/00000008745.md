# 00000008745

**English.** Definition: Cheeger-Chern-Simons classes: characteristic classes with integral structure from K-theory to de Rham cohomology. Conjecture: Explicit representatives of CCS classes in K-groups exist (a representative law); the compatibility constant with the Borel regulator is 1 (a compatibility law); the obstruction to extending the integral structure is 2-torsion (an obstruction law); and there is a dual for the Bokstedt periodic classes of higher weight (a duality law). (Cheeger-Chern-Simons representative compatibility torsion)

**中文。** 定义：Cheeger–Chern–Simons 类：从 K 理论到 de Rham 上同调的带整结构的示性类。猜想：CCS 类在 K 群的显式代表元存在（代表元律）；且与 Borel regulator 的相容常数为 1（相容律）；整结构的扩展障碍为 2-挠（障碍律）；高权的 Bokstedt 周期类的对偶（对偶律）。
