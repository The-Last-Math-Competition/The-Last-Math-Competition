# 00000007827

**English.** Definition: The singular fiber count s(X) of an SYZ Lagrangian torus fibration of a semi-rich Calabi-Yau under SYZ polarization: the total number of nodal and focus singular fibers. Conjecture: s(X) is determined by the stable annihilation numbers of the derived category of the mirror pair, of the corrected order 24 + h^{1,1}(X) - h^{2,1}(X), the same order as the Euler number; and the distribution density of nodal fibers along the discriminant locus satisfies, in the skeleton limit on the toric diagram, a completely determined transport equation dual to the KS operator of wall-crossing. (SYZ singular fiber transport equation)

**中文。** 定义：SYZ 塌缩的奇异纤维计数：半富 Calabi–Yau 的 SYZ 极化下 Lagrangian 环面纤维化的奇异纤维（结点/焦点）总数 s(X)。猜想：s(X) 由镜像对的导出范畴的稳定湮灭数决定：s(X) = 24+h^{1,1}(X)-h^{2,1}(X) 的修正量级，即与 Euler 数同阶；且结点纤维沿判别轨迹的分布密度在 toric 图上的骨架极限满足一个完全确定的传输方程（与 wall-crossing 的 KS 算子对偶）。
