**English.** Definition: The horofunction boundary is the boundary of the image space of distance functions. Conjecture: Its topological dimension and the hyperbolicity constant are in an explicit monotone decreasing relation, the extreme is attained by the free group, and the relation is given by belt separation. (monotonicity law of the horofunction dimension)

**中文。** 定义：horofunction 边界指距离函数像空间的边界。猜想：其拓扑维数与双曲常数为显式单调下降关系,极端在自由群达到且关系由腰带分离给出。（horofunction 维数单调律）
