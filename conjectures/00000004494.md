# 00000004494

**English.** Definition: Higher inductive types are inductive types with path generators. Conjecture: The categorical semantics of their elimination principle is an explicit fusion of an initial algebra with a coalgebra, the fusion exists uniquely, and the interval type is the smallest realization. (unique fusion of higher inductive elimination)

**中文。** 定义：高阶归纳类型指带路径生成元的归纳类型。猜想：其消除原理的范畴语义为初始代数与余代数的显式融合,融合存在唯一,区间类型为最小实现。（高阶归纳消除融合唯一）
