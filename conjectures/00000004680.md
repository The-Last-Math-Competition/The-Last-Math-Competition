# 00000004680

**English.** Definition: The topology of the arc space and the deformation moduli space are two geometric objects. Conjecture: There exist two singularities whose arc spaces are homeomorphic while their deformation moduli spaces are not isomorphic, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with equal Milnor numbers but different socles. (arc space-moduli space separation)

**中文。** 定义：弧空间拓扑与形变模空间指两个几何对象。猜想：存在两奇点的弧空间同胚而形变模空间不同构,分离由同 Milnor 数不同基座的显式对实现。（弧空间-模空间分离）
