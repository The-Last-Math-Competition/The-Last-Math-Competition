# 00000005921

**KZ2-01 ◆** — source: 183_KPZII.md

**English.** Definition: Stochastic growth equations are evolution equations driven by noise. Conjecture: The height-difference rate conversion constant of stationary solutions is the explicit ratio of noise strength to the curvature parameter, asymptotically the rate constant of the one-third power of time for strong noise, and the formula is exact. (explicit ratio formula of stationary rate constants)

**中文。** 定义：随机生长方程指驱动噪声的演化方程。猜想：稳态解的高度差率换算常数为噪声强度与曲率参数的显式比,强噪声渐近时为时间三分之一次幂的率常数且公式精确。（稳态率常数显式比公式）
