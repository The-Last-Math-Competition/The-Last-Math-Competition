# 00000002370

**English.** Definition: The calculus-norm spectral structure of contraction operators. Conjecture: There exists a contraction T whose calculus norms simultaneously approach the upper and lower limits; and the simultaneity of the pair is given by the Paulsen system. (contraction calculus norm limit pair)

**中文。** 定义：压缩算子的演算范数谱结构。猜想：存在压缩 T 使演算范数同时逼近上下界的极限对；且对的同时性为 Paulsen 系统。（压缩演算范数极限对）
