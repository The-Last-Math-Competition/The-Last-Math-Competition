# 00000002661

**English.** Definition: Almost maximal radius: quantitative rigidity. Conjecture: Almost-rigidity of spheres: radius near maximal gives rigidity, with second-order loss. (almost maximal radius sphere rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：几乎极大半径：刚性的定量。猜想：几乎刚性的球面：半径接近极大的刚性且性的损失为二阶。（几乎极大半径球面刚性二阶损失定量刚性的球面）
