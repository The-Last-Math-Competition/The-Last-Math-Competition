# 00000004925

**English.** Definition: A mean-field game is the limit game model of infinitely many players. Conjecture: The convergence error of the coupling quantity of finitely many players is the negative one-half power of the player number, the constant is given by the smoothing of the interaction, and it matches the central limit. (coupling limit constant of mean fields)

**中文。** 定义：平均场博弈指无限参与者的极限博弈模型。猜想：有限参与者的耦合量收敛误差为参与数的负二分之一次幂,常数由相互作用光滑化给出且匹配中心极限。（平均场耦合极限常数）
