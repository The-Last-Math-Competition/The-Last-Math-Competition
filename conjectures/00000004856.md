**English.** Definition: The universal theory is the set of all universal first-order sentences. Conjecture: The model-group spectrum of the universal theory of hyperbolic groups is an explicit hyperbolically closed class, whose boundary consists of an explicit counterexample family of non-CAT(0) groups, and the characterization is complete. (characterization of the universal theory model spectrum)
