# 00000003850

**English.** Definition: The quotient-chain limit spectrum of a monoid is the limit of representation-dimension generating functions along chains of finite quotients. Conjecture: Two non-isomorphic finite generated monoids, neither a quotient of the other, can share the same limit spectrum; yet the plactic and hypoplactic monoids have distinct limit spectra. (quotient chain limit spectrum separation)

**中文。** 定义：幺半群的商列极限谱指沿有限商链各商的表示维数母函数的极限。猜想：存在两个不同构且互不为商的有限生成幺半群具有相同的极限谱;而 plactic 幺半群与 hypoplactic 幺半群的极限谱不同。（商列极限谱分离）
