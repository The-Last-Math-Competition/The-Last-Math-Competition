**English.** Definition: The Arnold geometry of the Euler equations: geodesics of incompressible flows. Conjecture: The Wasserstein dual is given by Lagrangian coordinates; and the kernel of the coordinates is the incompressible projection. (Euler incompressible projection Lagrangian duality)
