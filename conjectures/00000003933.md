# 00000003933

**English.** Definition: A ruling is a combinatorial configuration of the front projection of a Legendrian. Conjecture: A ruling exists if and only if the DGA admits an augmentation, and this holds for base manifolds of genus zero; at genus one a smallest counterexample exists. (genus boundary of ruling-augmentation equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：ruling 指 Legendrian 前向投影的组合配置。猜想：ruling 存在当且仅当 DGA 有增强,对底流形亏格为零成立;亏格 1 时存在最小反例。（ruling-增强等价的亏格边界）
