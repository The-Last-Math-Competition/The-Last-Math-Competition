# 00000003312

**English.** Definition: The Stanley–Reisner correspondence: simplicial complexes and squarefree monomial ideals. Conjecture: The homology of face rings: the simplicial homology of a complex is the mod-two component of the local cohomology of the face ring, with the count of components the reduced f-polynomial. (face ring local cohomology f-vector)
