**English.** Definition: The barycenter is the barycenter structure of measure sets. Conjecture: The barycenter of a finite family exists with explicit support bound; the constant of the bound is the dimension inverse; and the dimension-inverse constant is given by the simplex barycenter. (finite barycenter support bound)
