**English.** Definition: global minimal graph in n≤7 of Bernstein problem: R^n is linear, n=8 has Bombieri–De Giorgi–Giusti counterexample graph. Conjecture: nonlinear minimal graph in module isometry of n=8 and dilation under unique is BDG graph; all dimension counterexample by symmetric reduction is this counterexample dimension of the 8 dimension basic counterexample, without new. (Bernstein counterexample uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：Bernstein 问题：R^n 的整体极小图在 n≤7 为线性，n=8 起有 Bombieri–De Giorgi–Giusti 反例图。猜想：n=8 的非线性极小图在模等距与伸缩下唯一为 BDG 图；一切更高维反例由对称约化归结为该 8 维基本反例，无新的本征反例维数。（Bernstein 反例唯一性）
