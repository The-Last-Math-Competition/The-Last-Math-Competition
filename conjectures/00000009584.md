**MSH-04 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The Bernstein problem: entire minimal graphs in R^n are linear for n at most 7, and from n = 8 there are Bombieri-De Giorgi-Giusti counterexample graphs. Conjecture: The nonlinear minimal graph at n = 8 is unique up to isometry and dilation — the BDG graph; all higher-dimensional counterexamples reduce by symmetry to this 8-dimensional basic counterexample, with no new intrinsic counterexample dimensions. (Bernstein counterexample uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：Bernstein 问题：R^n 的整体极小图在 n≤7 为线性，n=8 起有 Bombieri–De Giorgi–Giusti 反例图。猜想：n=8 的非线性极小图在模等距与伸缩下唯一为 BDG 图；一切更高维反例由对称约化归结为该 8 维基本反例，无新的本征反例维数。（Bernstein 反例唯一性）
