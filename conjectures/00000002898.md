**English.** Definition: The moment curve: the finite-field arc {(1,t,t²,…,t^d)}. Conjecture: The MDS duality of moment arcs: the parameters of the dual arc are the minimum distance of Reed–Solomon codes, the equality in the Singleton bound geometricizing as maximality of arcs; the complementary geometry of maximal arcs is the hyperplane intersection count. (moment arc MDS duality)

**中文。** 定义：moment 曲线：{ (1,t,t²,…,t^d) } 的有限域弧。猜想：moment 弧的 MDS 对偶：对偶弧的参数为 Reed–Solomon 码的最小距离且距离-维数的 Singleton 界的等号几何化为弧的极大性；极大弧的补几何为超平面的相遇计数。（moment 弧 MDS 对偶）
