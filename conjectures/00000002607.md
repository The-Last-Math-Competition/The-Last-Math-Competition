# 00000002607

**OL-17 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: The order polynomial Ω(P,t): the count of order-preserving maps from P to a chain. Conjecture: The mixed (partially strict) order polynomial is an Ehrhart-type quasipolynomial whose period is the least common multiple of the two-dimensional interval structure of P. (mixed order polynomial period)

**中文。** 定义：序多项式 Ω(P,t)：P 到链的保序映射计数。猜想：混合（部分严格）序多项式为 Ehrhart 型拟多项式且周期为 P 的二维区间结构的最小公倍数。（混合序多项式周期）
