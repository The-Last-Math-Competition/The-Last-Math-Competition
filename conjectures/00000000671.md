# 00000000671

**KT-71 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The Kauffman bracket vector of a tangle consists of the boundary-expansion coefficients; the bracket pairing of two tangles by inner product gives the polynomial of the closed knot. Conjecture: For adequate tangle pairs, all components of the paired vector are nonnegative — adequate tangle closures preserve coefficient signs; and this positivity fails on general tangles exactly once.

**中文。** 定义：缠结的 Kauffman 括号向量指其边界展开系数组成的二元向量,两缠结的括号拼合的内积给出闭合纽结的多项式。猜想：对 adequate 缠结对,该内积向量的全部分量为非负,即 adequate 缠结的闭合保持系数符号;该正性在一般缠结上可失败恰一次。
