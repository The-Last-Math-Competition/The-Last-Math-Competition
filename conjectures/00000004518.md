# 00000004518

**English.** Definition: The chromatic number of a random graph is the optimal legal coloring number. Conjecture: With high probability the chromatic number is concentrated on two explicit adjacent integers, the probability of taking the lower value converges to an explicit constant, and the window width is one. (two-point concentration and explicit probability of the chromatic number)
