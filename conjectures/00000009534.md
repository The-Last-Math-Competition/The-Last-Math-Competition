# 00000009534

**English.** Definition: A Hadamard channel is one whose complementary channel is entanglement-breaking, equivalently a measure-and-prepare implementation; its quantum capacity is zero but its private capacity can be positive. Conjecture: The maximal private capacity of d-dimensional Hadamard channels is log d - Theta(1); the channels attaining it form, up to anti-unitary equivalence, the discrete Weyl covariant family; and the minimal channel dimension for the private-quantum separation P > 0 with Q = 0 is 3. (Hadamard private separation)

**中文。** 定义：Hadamard 信道指互补信道为纠缠破坏信道者，等价于可测量-制备实现；其量子容量为零但私人容量可为正。猜想：d 维 Hadamard 信道的私人容量最大值为 log d−Θ(1)，达到该值的信道在模反酉等价下为离散 Weyl 协变族，且私密-量子分离 P>0 而 Q=0 的最小信道维数为 3。（Hadamard 私密分离）
