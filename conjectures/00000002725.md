# 00000002725

**English.** Definition: Kühnel-type inequalities: the relation between vertex counts and Betti numbers of manifold complexes. Conjecture: The strengthening of the half-Euler-characteristic inequality: 2k-dimensional manifold complexes satisfy the corrected f₀ ≥ (2k+3)β_k, with the correction an explicit function of torsion coefficients; the equality complexes are classified as (2k+2)-point nome type. (Kuhnel strengthened classification)

**中文。** 定义：Kühnel 型不等式：流形复形的顶点数与 Betti 数的关系。猜想：半数 Euler 特征不等式的加强：2k 维流形复形满足 f₀ ≥ (2k+3)β_k 的修正版且修正项为挠系数的显式函数；等号复形的分类为 (2k+2)-点 nome 型。（Kuhnel 加强分类）
