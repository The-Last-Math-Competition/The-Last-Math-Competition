# 00000005528

**English.** Definition: Heavy-tailed service has infinite variance. Conjecture: The waiting asymptotics is the explicit stable law of the residual lifetime, the exponent shifted by the service tail, and the coefficient is the mean ratio. (residual-lifetime stable law of heavy-tailed waiting)

**中文。** 定义：重尾服务指方差无穷的分布。猜想：等待渐近为剩余寿命的稳定律显式,指数由服务尾平移且系数为均值比。（重尾等待剩余寿命稳定律）
