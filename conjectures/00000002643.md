# 00000002643

**English.** Definition: The skew-symmetrized genus-1 Siegel lowering: from degree 2 to degree 1. Conjecture: The dimension of the fibers of the lowering map is exactly the dimension of the index-1 space, and the isomorphism classes of the fibers are explicitly separated by the parity of the weight mod 2. (Siegel lowering fiber dimension)

**中文。** 定义：斜对称化的 Genus 1 Siegel 降格：从 2 阶到 1 阶。猜想：降格映射的纤维的维数恰为指标 1 空间的维数且纤维的同构类由权 mod 2 的宇显式分离。（Siegel 降格纤维维数）
