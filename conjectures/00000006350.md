# 00000006350

**English.** Definition: The classical basis and the canonical basis are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two quantum groups with the same classical basis but different canonical bases, and the separation is realized by an explicit deformation with the same basis but different twisting. (same classical-different canonical twist-deformation separation)

**中文。** 定义：经典基与典范基指两层。猜想：存在两量子的经典基相同而典范基不同,分离由同基异扭的显式形变实现。（经典基同-典范异扭形变分离）
