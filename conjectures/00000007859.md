# 00000007859

**English.** Definition: The weighted tail of reservoir sampling: the inclusion probability p_i = w_i^alpha / sum w^alpha of the A-Res algorithm for weighted sampling without replacement. Conjecture: The sup deviation of the empirical distribution of an A-Res sample against the weighted distribution W satisfies the Kolmogorov-Smirnov type concentration P(sup|Fhat - F| > epsilon) <= 2 exp(-2 m epsilon^2/H_{w,alpha}) with H the harmonic weighted entropy; and the adaptive choice of the optimal alpha minimizing H solves the balance equation alpha* = log(w_max/w_min)/log m. (weighted reservoir KS concentration)

**中文。** 定义：reservoir sampling 的加权尾部：加权无放回抽样（A-Res 算法）的包含概率 p_i=w_i^α/Σw^α 的分布尾部。猜想：A-Res 样本的经验分布函数对加权分布 W 的 sup 偏差满足 D_K-S 型集中：P(sup|F̂-F|>ε) ≤ 2exp(-2mε²/H_{w,α})（H 为调和加权熵）；且最优 α 的自适应选择使 H 最小化，解满足 α*=log(w_max/w_min)/log m 的平衡方程。
