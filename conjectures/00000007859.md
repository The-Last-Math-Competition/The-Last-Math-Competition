**English.** Definition: distribution tail of contains probability p_i=w_i^α/Σw^α of weighted tail: weighted without (A-Res algorithm) of reservoir sampling. Conjecture: sup deviation of distribution function pair weighted distribution W of A-Res sample satisfies D_K-S type concentration: P(sup|F̂-F|>ε) ≤ 2exp(-2mε²/H_{w,α}) (H is harmonic weighted entropy); and adaptive of optimal α such that H minimizes, solution satisfies equilibrium equation of α*=log(w_max/w_min)/log m.

**中文。** 定义：reservoir sampling 的加权尾部：加权无放回抽样（A-Res 算法）的包含概率 p_i=w_i^α/Σw^α 的分布尾部。猜想：A-Res 样本的经验分布函数对加权分布 W 的 sup 偏差满足 D_K-S 型集中：P(sup|F̂-F|>ε) ≤ 2exp(-2mε²/H_{w,α})（H 为调和加权熵）；且最优 α 的自适应选择使 H 最小化，解满足 α*=log(w_max/w_min)/log m 的平衡方程。
