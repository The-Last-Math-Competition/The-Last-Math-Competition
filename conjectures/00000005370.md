**HG4-20 △** — source: 164_几何群论IV_双曲几何III.md

**English.** Definition: The boundary quasiconformal type and the volume entropy are two geometric quantities. Conjecture: There exist two hyperbolic groups with the same boundary type but different volume entropies, and the separation is realized by an explicit covering pair with the same type but different entropy. (same boundary type-different volume entropy separation)

**中文。** 定义：边界拟共形型与体积熵指两个几何量。猜想：存在两双曲群的边界型相同而体积熵不同,分离由同型异熵的显式覆叠对实现。（边界型同-体积熵异分离）
