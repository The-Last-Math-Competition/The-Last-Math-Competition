# 00000004774

**English.** Definition: Asymptotic freeness is the moment convergence property. Conjecture: The mixed moment error of unitarily invariant and permutation matrices always decays as one power of the reciprocal dimension; the error is controlled by the first crossing pairing and the exponent is exact. (first-crossing control of mixed moment errors)

**中文。** 定义：渐近自由指矩收敛性质。猜想：酉不变与置换矩阵的混合矩误差恒按维数倒数一次幂衰减；误差由首交叉配对控制且指数精确。（混合矩误差首交叉控制）
