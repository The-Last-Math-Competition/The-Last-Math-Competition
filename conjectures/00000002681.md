# 00000002681

**English.** Definition: The Arzelà–Ascoli theorem: the uniform compactness criterion for equicontinuous families on compact spaces. Conjecture: A quantitative Arzelà–Ascoli: the optimal trade-off between the equicontinuity modulus ω and the compactness deviation δ is the linear law δ ≤ C·ω, with C the covering dimension plus one; the trade-off law is attained by the optimal discretization of Kolmogorov ε-entropy. (Arzela-Ascoli quantitative trade-off)

**中文。** 定义：Arzelà–Ascoli 定理：紧空间上等度连续函数族的一致紧性判据。猜想：Arzelà–Ascoli 的定量版：等度连续模 ω 与紧性的偏差 δ 之间的最优折衷为 δ ≤ C·ω 的线性律且常数 C 为紧空间的覆盖维数加一；折衷律由离散化的 Kolmogorov ε-熵最优达到。（Arzela-Ascoli 定量折衷）
