**English.** Definition: The relative index on noncompact manifolds: Gromov's relative index theory. Conjecture: The trichotomy of relative indices: the relative index of three operators satisfies a cyclic-sum identity, proved via an alternating-sum construction of Fredholm pairs. (relative index cyclic sum)
