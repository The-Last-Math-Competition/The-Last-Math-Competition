# 00000009064

**SMI-24 ★** — source: 287_子流形几何不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Tight submanifolds: embeddings of minimal total absolute curvature. Conjecture: The classification of tight embeddings in dimension two is torus type; and the uniqueness of the classification is up to affine equivalence. (tight two-dimensional torus classification)

**中文。** 定义：tight 子流形为极小全绝对曲率嵌入。猜想：tight 嵌入在维数二的分类为环面型；且分类的唯一性模仿射等价。（tight 二维环面分类）
