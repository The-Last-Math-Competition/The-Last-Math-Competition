# 00000001355

**English.** Definition: A trace formula is an identity between the spectral and geometric sides. Conjecture: The sign-change density of traces of Hecke operators on the newspace has an explicit limit in q; and the limiting value is a quadratic polynomial in the number of prime factors. (trace sign density quadratic law)

**中文。** 定义：迹公式指谱侧与几何侧的恒等。猜想：Hecke 算子在 newspace 的迹的符号变化密度随 q 有显式极限；且极限值为素因子个数的二次多项式。（迹符号密度二次律）
