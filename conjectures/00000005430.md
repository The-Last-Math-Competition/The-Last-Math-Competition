# 00000005430

**HM2-20 △** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: The elliptic measure and the harmonic measure are two hitting distributions. Conjecture: There exist two domains with the same elliptic measure but different Laplace measures, and the separation is realized by an explicit two-domain pair of variable-coefficient operators. (same elliptic-different harmonic variable-coefficient separation)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆测度与调和测度指两个击中分布。猜想：存在两域的椭圆测度相同而拉普拉斯测度不同,分离由变系数算子的显式两域对实现。（椭圆同-调和异变系数分离）
