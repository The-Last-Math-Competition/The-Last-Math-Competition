# 00000009909

**English.** Definition: Hall polynomials: field-independent representation-counting polynomials. Conjecture: The combinatorial interpretation of higher-order terms of Hall polynomials is recursive counting with flagged flags; the counting function of the interpretation is the number of maximal chains of a poset, and the leading coefficient returns to Green's flag counting. (Hall higher-order combinatorial interpretation)

**中文。** 定义：Hall 多项式：域无关的表示计数多项式。猜想：Hall 多项式的高阶项的组合解释为带标志旗的递归计数；解释的计数函数为偏序集的极大链数且首项系数回到 Green 的旗计数。（Hall 高阶组合解释）
