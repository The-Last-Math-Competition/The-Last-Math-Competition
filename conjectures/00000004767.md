# 00000004767

**English.** Definition: The Narayana numbers count the rank distribution of noncrossing partitions. Conjecture: The central limit variance of its two-parameter extension is the explicit harmonic mean of the two parameters, the limit is normal, and the variance formula is exact. (harmonic variance of two-parameter Narayana numbers)

**中文。** 定义：Narayana 数指非交叉分拆的秩分布计数。猜想：其双参数推广的中心极限方差为两参数的调和平均型显式值,极限为正态且方差公式精确。（Narayana 双参调和方差）
