# 00000009683

**English.** Definition: Liouville numbers are reals approximable by algebraic numbers beyond all orders; their algebraic independence structure is unknown. Conjecture: There exists a countable algebraically independent basis of Liouville numbers given by explicit series constructions; the sum of any two basis elements is a transcendental non-Liouville number, so closure fails under addition. (Liouville algebraically independent basis)

**中文。** 定义：Liouville 数为可被代数数超逼近的实数；其代数无关结构未知。猜想：存在由显式构造级数给出的 Liouville 数的可数代数无关基；基中任意两数的和为非 Liouville 的超越数，封闭性在加法下破裂。（Liouville 代数无关基）
