# 00000003996

**English.** Definition: The formalization of the Ramsey principle is the contradictory statement that a monochromatic k-clique exists in any 2-coloring of a graph. Conjecture: Its resolution length lower bound is 2^{Omega(k)}, and the choice of the diagonal parameter n = k^2 makes the exponent constants of the upper and lower bounds match. (matching Ramsey resolution exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Ramsey 原理的公式化指任意 2 色图中同色 k 团存在的矛盾式。猜想：其消解长度下界为 2^{Ω(k)},对角参数 n = k² 的选取使上界与下界的指数常数匹配。（Ramsey 消解指数匹配）
