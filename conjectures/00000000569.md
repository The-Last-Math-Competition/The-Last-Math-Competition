# 00000000569

**English.** Definition: For the pattern of Y-variables (y_i) of a cluster algebra, the return constant ρ is the constant by which the total scaling product ∏ y_i is multiplied upon returning to the original seed. Conjecture: For all finite and affine types, ρ = 1; and ρ is always a root of unity given by the value at 1 of the corresponding generalized Chebyshev polynomial, hence explicitly computable from the spectrum of the exchange matrix.

**中文。** 定义：簇代数的 Y-变量模式 (y_i) 在种子突变下的总体标度积 ∏ y_i 在回归原种子时乘以的常数称回还常数 ρ。猜想：对一切有限型与仿射型,ρ=1;且 ρ 恒为相应的广义 Chebyshev 多式在 1 处值的单位根,因而可由 exchange 矩阵的谱显式计算。
