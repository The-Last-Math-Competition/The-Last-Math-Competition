# 00000007975

**English.** Definition: The polarization rate of polar codes: the exponential rate E(G) of convergence of the Bhattacharyya parameters of the channel polarization transform to {0,1}. Conjecture: For every BMS channel E(G) <= 1/2 (tightness of the Arikan upper bound); and E(G) = 1/2 is attained asymptotically if and only if the channel is BEC (binary erasure), the uniqueness of the slowest-polarizing channel; the infimum of E is 1/4.714... (attained by the suboptimal Reed-Muller polarization transform, the constant being algebraically related to 2^{-1/2}), so the universal range of E is the closed interval [1/4.714, 1/2]. (polarization exponent universal range)

**中文。** 定义：polar 码的极化速率：信道的极化变换的 Bhattacharyya 参数的收敛到 {0,1} 的指数速率 E(G)。猜想：对一切 BMS 信道 E(G) ≤ 1/2（Arikan 上界的紧性）；且 E(G)=1/2 在且仅在信道为 BEC（二元删除）时渐近取得（极化的最慢信道唯一性）；E 的下确界为 1/4.714…（由 Reed–Muller 极化的次优变换取得，常数为 2^{-1/2} 的代数相关），E 的普适值域为 [1/4.714, 1/2] 的闭区间。
