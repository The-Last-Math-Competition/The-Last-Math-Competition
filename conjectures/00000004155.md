**CM3-05 ◆** — source: 124_解析数论III_圆法.md

**English.** Definition: The Hardy-Littlewood constant is the singular series of the representation count of prime tuples. Conjecture: For fixed diameter, the admissible tuple minimizing the constant is of the initial-segment-plus-two-point-correction type, and the minimum decays by an explicit power law in the diameter. (structure of HL extremal tuples)

**中文。** 定义：Hardy–Littlewood 常数指素数元组表示数的奇异级数。猜想：固定直径下使常数取极小的容许元组为区间前段加两点修正型,极小值按直径的显式幂律衰减。（HL 极值元组结构）
