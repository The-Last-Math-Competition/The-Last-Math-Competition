**English.** Definition: Second-order products with the deficit spectrum of oscillatory integrals. Conjecture: The oscillation spectrum gives the deficit and the gap of the spectrum is the characteristic-frequency difference; the gap spectrum is an overlap-free lattice; and the deficit counting is weighted lattice-point counting. (oscillatory deficit spectrum of second-order products)

**中文。** 定义：二阶积：振荡积分的亏格谱。猜想：振荡谱给出亏格且谱的间隙为特征频率差；间隙谱为无重叠格；亏格的计数为格点的带权计数型。（二阶积亏格振荡谱结构）
