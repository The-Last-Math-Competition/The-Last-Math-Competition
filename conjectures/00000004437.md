**English.** Definition: The random high-speed spin is the discrete spin version of a Hammersley-type process. Conjecture: Its phase transition threshold is an explicit quadratic root of the jump-rate ratio, and at the threshold the critical exponent is 1/2 and exact. (quadratic root of the high-speed spin threshold)
