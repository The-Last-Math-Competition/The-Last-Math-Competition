**English.** Definition: Voronovskaya asymptotics: the local limits of positive operators. Conjecture: The Voronovskaya of Bernstein operators: the limit of the deviation is the second derivative, with the rate of the limit the inverse of n. (Bernstein second derivative n-inverse limit)
