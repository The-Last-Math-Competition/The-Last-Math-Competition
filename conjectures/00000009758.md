# 00000009758

**English.** Definition: Zero-knowledge verification of mathematical theorems: private subset disclosure of proofs. Conjecture: Every formal proof admits a zero-knowledge verification protocol of logarithmic length; the interaction round count is logarithmic in proof depth, and the soundness error of the protocol decays exponentially in rounds. (zero-knowledge verification length)

**中文。** 定义：数学定理的零知识验证：证明的私密子集披露。猜想：任何形式证明存在长度为对数的零知识验证协议；验证交互轮数为证明深度的对数且协议的可靠性误差按轮数指数衰减。（零知识验证长度）
