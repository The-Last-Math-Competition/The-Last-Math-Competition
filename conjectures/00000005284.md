# 00000005284

**English.** Definition: The physical vacuum is the boundary where density decays quadratically with distance. Conjecture: The criterion for global existence of isentropic compressible flow is physical vacuum plus irrotational initial data, controlled by the explicit loss of weighted energy, and necessary and sufficient. (necessary and sufficient criterion of isentropic physical vacuum)

**中文。** 定义：物理真空指密度按距离平方衰减的边界。猜想：等熵可压整体存在的判据为物理真空加无旋初值,由加权能量的显式损失控制且必要充分。（等熵物理真空充要判据）
