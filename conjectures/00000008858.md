# 00000008858

**MON-28 ★** — source: 280_单调算子与算子分裂.md

**English.** Definition: Alternating projections for convex feasibility: the convergence structure of alternating orthogonal projection sequences between two closed convex sets A and B. Conjecture: Alternating projection sequences always converge weakly to nearest points in the intersection; the criterion for strong convergence is ultrapower regularity of the two sets, with a uniform lower bound of the regularity modulus giving a linear rate; when the angle between the two convex sets is acute, the rate is geometric in cos-squared theta, degenerating to O(1/k) as theta approaches a right angle; and with empty intersection the sequence approaches the distance between the sets with asymptotic direction converging to a separating normal vector. (alternating projection regularity geometric angle separation)

**中文。** 定义：凸可行问题的交替投影：两闭凸集 A、B 间交替正交投影序列的收敛结构。猜想：交替投影序列恒弱收敛到交集中的最近点；强收敛的判据为两集的超幂正则性且正则性模的一致下界给出线性率；两凸集夹角为锐角时收敛率为 cos²θ 的几何率且 θ 趋于直角时退化为 O(1/k)；交为空时序列逼近两集间的距离且渐近方向收敛到分离法向量。
