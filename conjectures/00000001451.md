# 00000001451

**English.** Definition: A polytope P is an ε-approximation of a convex body K if vol(K△P) ≤ ε·vol K. Conjecture: The number of facets of the minimal ε-approximating polytope of the unit ball in R^d is c_d·ε^{(d−1)/2} with the exact constant c_d explicit (the constant in the optimal approximation order).

**中文。** 定义：多胞体 P 对凸体 K 的 ε-近似(vol(K△P) ≤ ε·vol K)。猜想：ℝ^d 中单位球的最小 ε-近似多胞体的面数为 c_d·ε^{(d−1)/2} 的精确常数 c_d 显式(最优逼近阶的常数)。
