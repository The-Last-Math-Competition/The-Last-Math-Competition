# 00000001567

**GM-27 ★** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: Allowable sequences (projection sequences of point sets). Conjecture: Every allowable sequence contains at least (n choose 2)/2 − O(n) crossings in depth (allowable sequence depth).

**中文。** 定义：允许序列(allowable sequences,点集投影序列)。猜想：每个允许序列含 ≥ (n choose 2)/2 − O(n) 的交换深度(允许序列深度)。
