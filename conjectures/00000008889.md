# 00000008889

**English.** Definition: The conjunctive sum: composite games with synchronized alternating moves. Conjecture: The value of a synchronized sum is given by minimax composition of components; short-circuit elimination is decidable in polynomial time; and the value spectrum of selective compounds is finite. (conjunctive minimax composition)

**中文。** 定义：conjunctive sum 指轮流同步的复合博弈。猜想：同步和的值由分量的 minimax 复合给出；短路的剔除可多项式时间判定且选择性复合的值谱有限。（同步和 minimax 复合）
