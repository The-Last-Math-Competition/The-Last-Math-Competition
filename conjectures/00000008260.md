**English.** Definition: The implementation cost of the second welfare theorem of general equilibrium: the smooth realization smooth_cost of supporting prices. Conjecture: smooth_cost (the convexification cost of the second theorem) is the distributional distance of preference convexification (a convexification distance law); the equivalence of the existence radius epsilon of approximate support (epsilon-supporting hyperplanes) for nonconvex preferences to the convexification distance (an epsilon equivalence law); the strengthening of the second theorem (exact global support) holds if and only if preferences are convex and monotone (a biconditional law); and the convergence rate of convexification iterations (preference averaging) is the harmonic 1/t (a harmonic convergence law). (second welfare theorem convexification harmonic)

**中文。** 定义：一般均衡的福利经济学的第二定理的实现代价：支撑价格的可微实现 smooth_cost。猜想：smooth_cost（第二定理的凸化代价）为偏好凸化的分布距离（凸化距离律）；且非凸偏好的近似支撑（ε-支撑超平面）的存在半径 ε 与凸化的距离的等价（ε-等价律）；第二定理的强化（整体的精确支撑）当且仅当偏好为凸与单调的双条件（双条件律）；凸化的迭代（偏好平均）的收敛速率为 1/t 的调和（调和收敛律）。
