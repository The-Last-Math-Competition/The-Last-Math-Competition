# 00000004062

**English.** Definition: The universal unfolding of a Bautin point is the four-parameter degenerate Hopf unfolding. Conjecture: In the unfolding parameter space the stratified hypersurfaces where the number of limit cycles jumps are pairwise transverse, the intersection angles at the origin have universal values, and the number of angles is exactly 4. (number of transverse angles in Bautin unfoldings)

**中文。** 定义：Bautin 点的普适开折指四参数退化 Hopf 开折。猜想：开折参数空间中极限环个数跃变的分层超曲面两两横截,在原点的相交角有普适值,且角数恰为 4。（Bautin 开折横截角数）
