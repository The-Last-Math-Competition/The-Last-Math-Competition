# 00000008296

**English.** Definition: The minimal family of generators of wrapped Fukaya categories: the minimal twist count gen_min for Strommenfelt generation (generation by twists of local systems). Conjecture: gen_min (the minimal generating family of the wrapped category) equals the number of indecomposables of the singular leaves of the skeleton of the Lagrangian submanifold (an indecomposable count law); the codimension of skeleton singularities is at least 2 (a codimension-2 singularity law); and the domain of the fullness test of the verification of generation (the splitting of Abouzaid's criterion, testing surjectivity of homology) is the normal bundle of the skeleton (a normal bundle test domain law). (wrapped Fukaya skeleton indecomposable codimension)

**中文。** 定义：wrapped Fukaya 范畴的生成元的极小族：Strommenfelt 生成（局部系统的 twist 生成）的最小 twist 数 gen_min。猜想：gen_min（wrapped 的最小生成族）= Lagrangian 子流形的 skeleton 的奇异层的不可分解数（不可分解计数律）；且 skeleton 的奇点的余维 ≥ 2（余 2 奇点律）；生成的验证（生成判据）的分裂（Abouzaid 的判据）的检验同调的满性（满性检验）的域为 skeleton 的法丛（法丛检验域律）。
