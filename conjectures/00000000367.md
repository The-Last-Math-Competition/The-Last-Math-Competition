# 00000000367

**English.** Definition: The approximation exponent of a linear recurrence sequence measures how close its general term is to an integer. Conjecture: The general term of a nondegenerate linear recurrence satisfies ‖α_n‖ > n^{−C} for an explicit C; and C is determined by the gap between the moduli of the characteristic roots. (recurrence sequence approximation lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：线性递推序列的逼近指数刻画其通项近整数程度。猜想：非退化线性递推的通项满足 ‖α_n‖>n^{−C} 的显式 C；且 C 由特征根的模间隙给出。（递推序列逼近下界）
