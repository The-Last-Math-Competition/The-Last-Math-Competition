# 00000002436

**English.** Definition: The trivial-character spectrum of the fundamental group of the complement of a projective plane curve. Conjecture: The dimension of the spectrum is d²−3d+2; and the realization of the dimension is the lattice of abelian coverings. (curve complement trivial-character dimension formula)
