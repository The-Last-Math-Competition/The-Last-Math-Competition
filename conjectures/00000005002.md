# 00000005002

**English.** Definition: The associative moduli space is the deformation space of associative submanifolds. Conjecture: The rigidity criterion of low-dimensional associatives is an explicit threshold of the first eigenvalue of its operator, attained by tubular examples, and the criterion is complete. (eigenvalue threshold criterion of associative rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：结合模空间指结合子流形的形变空间。猜想：低维结合的刚性判据为其算子第一特征值的显式阈值,管状例达到等号且判据完全。（结合刚性特征值阈值判据）
