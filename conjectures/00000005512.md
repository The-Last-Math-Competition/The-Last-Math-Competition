# 00000005512

**QN2-12 ◆** — source: 169_排队网络的图极限.md

**English.** Definition: The supermarket model is the power-of-two load balancing strategy, sampling two queues at arrival and joining the shorter. Conjecture: The fixed point is an explicit hyperbolic-type distribution with tail a power law in the selection count, doubling the queue-length tail exponent; the location parameter of the hyperbolic fixed point is the ratio of arrival rate to total service rate; and the power-tail exponent diverges linearly as the sampling count tends to infinity. (supermarket hyperbolic fixed point with power tail)

**中文。** 定义：超市模型：两选一的负载平衡策略（到达时抽查两队列选短者）。猜想：固定点为显式双曲型分布且尾部为选择数次幂律——队长尾概率的指数加倍；双曲固定点的位置参数为到达率与总服务率之比；幂尾指数在抽查数趋于无穷时发散且发散率为线性。（超市双曲固定点幂尾）
