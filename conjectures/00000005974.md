**English.** Definition: Tensor eigenvectors are the solutions of higher-order eigen-equations. Conjecture: For supercritical signals the finding probability tends to one, the spin radius is given by the signal, and the kernel of the finding is the energy gap. (energy-gap finding probability of tensor spin radii)
