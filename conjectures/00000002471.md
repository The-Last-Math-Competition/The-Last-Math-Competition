**English.** Definition: Universality of partitions: universal congruences of the partition function, with the modular-form side supplying the mechanism. Conjecture: Every congruence of the partition function p(n) arising from the modular-form side is captured by a finite union of arithmetic progressions; the progressions are governed by the CM structure of the generating function. (partition function universal congruences modular-form side)
