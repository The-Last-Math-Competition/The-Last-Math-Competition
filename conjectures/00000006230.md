# 00000006230

**English.** Definition: Point categories and toposes are two structural layers. Conjecture: There exist two toposes with equivalent point categories but globally non-isomorphic, and the separation is realized explicitly by the same points with different sheaf thickness. (same point category-different topos sheaf-thickness separation)

**中文。** 定义：点范畴与拓扑斯指两层结构。猜想：存在两拓扑斯的点范畴等价而整体不同构,分离由同点异层厚的显式实现。（点范畴同-拓扑斯异层厚分离）
