**English.** Definition: The SYZ correction is the modification of the semi-flat mirror by instanton corrections. Conjecture: The leading correction is an explicit sum of logarithmic monomials with coefficients the open GW invariants, and on the two-torus fibration the leading term is the single point-disk contribution. (logarithmic leading sum of SYZ corrections)
