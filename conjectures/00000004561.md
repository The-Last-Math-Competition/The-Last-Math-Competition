# 00000004561

**English.** Definition: A period is the integral value of an algebraic differential form on an algebraic chain. Conjecture: Any finite subset of the logarithms of all primes is algebraically independent, forming an infinite subset of a transcendence basis. (finite independence of prime logarithms)

**中文。** 定义：周期指代数微分形式在代数链上的积分值。猜想：全体素数的对数中任意有限子集代数无关,构成超越基的无穷子集。（素对数任意有限无关）
