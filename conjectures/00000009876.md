# 00000009876

**English.** Definition: General first-passage percolation: variance exponents of nonintegrable FPP. Conjecture: Passage-time variances of general finite-variance-weight FPP grow as the one-half power of distance; the universality of the exponent 1/2 holds under finite exponential moments, and heavy tails deform it continuously with the tail exponent. (FPP variance exponent universality)

**中文。** 定义：一般首通过渗透：非可积 FPP 的方差指数。猜想：有限方差权重的一般 FPP 的通过时间方差按距离的 1/2 次幂增长；指数 1/2 的普适性在指数矩有限条件下成立且重尾时按尾指数连续变形。（FPP 方差指数普适）
