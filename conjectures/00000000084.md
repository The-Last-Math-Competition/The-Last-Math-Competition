# 00000000084

**English.** Conjecture: Classification of perfectly nonlinear (PN) functions: for p sufficiently large, every PN function over F_p is equivalent to one of Dembowski–Ostrom type (in the sense of polynomials x ↦ Σ aᵢx^{p^i+1}).

**中文。** 猜想：完全非线性(PN)函数分类:当 p 充分大时,F_p 上每个 PN 函数均等价于 Dembowski–Ostrom 型(多项式 x ↦ Σ aᵢx^{p^i+1} 意义下)。
