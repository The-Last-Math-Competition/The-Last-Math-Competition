# 00000007263

**English.** Definition: Liapunov-Schmidt with infinite-dimensional reduction. Conjecture: The finite-dimensional reduction of the bifurcation equation holds, and the kernel of the reduction is the eigenspace. (eigenspace-kernel Liapunov-Schmidt reduction)

**中文。** 定义：Liapunov–Schmidt：无穷维降维。猜想：LS 的约化：分岔方程的有限维约化且约的核为特征子空间。（Liapunov-Schmidt 特征子空间分岔方程核有限维约化）
