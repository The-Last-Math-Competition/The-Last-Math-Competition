# 00000008386

**English.** Definition: The motivic decomposition of rationally connected varieties: the decomposition of their Chow motives into direct sums of smooth projective motives. Conjecture: The unit motive of a rationally connected variety occurs with multiplicity 1; the complement of the decomposition is completely controlled, up to motivic equivalence, by the lattice of algebraic equivalence classes; and the complement is zero if and only if the variety is motivically trivial, the smallest nonrational example of motivic triviality being a three-dimensional birationally rigid variety. (RC motivic complement characterization)

**中文。** 定义：有理连通簇的母题分解指其 Chow 母题在平滑投射母题直和下的分解。猜想：有理连通簇的单位母题重数为 1；分解的余部分在母题等价下由代数等价类的格完全控制；余为零当且仅当簇为母题平凡，母题平凡的最小非有理例为三维双有理刚性簇。（RC 母题余刻画）
