# 00000006142

**English.** Definition: The formal moduli problem classifies formal completions, dual to differential graded Lie algebras. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit duality of formal moduli with Lie algebras, single-generated cases corresponding to complete ones; the commutator is zero if and only if the moduli space is formally smooth; and the minimal noncommutative example with two generators is the formal loop moduli of spheres, with tangent spectrum a plus-minus pair. (single-generated duality of formal moduli and Lie algebras)
