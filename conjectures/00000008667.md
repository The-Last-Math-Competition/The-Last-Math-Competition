# 00000008667

**English.** Definition: The Benjamini-Schramm limit is the local weak limit of random Cayley graphs. Conjecture: The spectral measure of the intrinsic convergence limit of solvable groups is always atomless or has atomic masses equal to reciprocals of finite-index subgroup counts; and the spectrum of free product limits is given by free convolution. (solvable limit spectrum free convolution)

**中文。** 定义：Benjamini–Schramm 极限指随机 Cayley 图的局部弱极限。猜想：可解群的本性收敛极限的谱测度恒为无原子或原子质量为有限指数子群数倒数；自由积极限的谱由自由卷积给出。（可解极限谱自由卷积）
