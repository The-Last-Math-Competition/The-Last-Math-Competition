# 00000008277

**English.** Definition: The Van Trees bound on the minimal error of nonlinear filtering: the tightening vt_tight of the Van Trees (Bayesian Cramer-Rao) bound. Conjecture: The domain of vt_tight (tightening of the Van Trees bound) is unimodal posteriors (a unimodal tight domain law); the asymptotic validity of the bound (large samples) is the Cramer-Rao bound corrected by 1 + 1/(nI) (a 1/nI correction law); and the explicit lower bound for the steady-state error of nonlinear filtering is the harmonic mean of observation Fisher information and process noise (a harmonic mean lower bound formula). (Van Trees unimodal harmonic mean lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：非线性滤波的最小误差的 Van Trees 界：贝叶斯误差的 Van Trees（贝叶斯 Cramér–Rao）界的紧化 vt_tight。猜想：vt_tight（Van Trees 界的紧化的域）为单峰后验（单峰紧域律）；且界的渐近（大样本）的有效性 = Cramér–Rao 的 1+1/(nI) 的修正（1/nI 修正律）；非线性滤波的稳态误差的下界的显式为观测的 Fisher 信息与过程噪声的调和平均（调和平均下界公式）。
