# 00000002540

**GZ-10 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Uniform measures: Radon measures with uniformly bounded ball measures. Conjecture: Planar uniform measures are completely classified into four basic types; a new infinite family of 3-dimensional uniform measures exists, and the tangent-cone closures of all members of the family converge to finite mixtures of the four basic types. (three-dimensional uniform measure family)

**中文。** 定义：均匀测度（uniform measure）：球测度一致有界的 Radon 测度。猜想：平面均匀测度完全分类为四类基本型；三维均匀测度的新无限族存在且族的一切成员的切锥闭包收敛到四基本型的有限混合。（三维均匀测度族）
