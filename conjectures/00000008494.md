# 00000008494

**English.** Definition: Stability of the class of absolutely winning sets of Schmidt games under invariant measures of ergodic systems. Conjecture: The intersection of an absolutely winning set with any full-dimensional Cantor set remains absolutely winning (a Cantor stability law); the class is closed under countable unions and countable intersections (a countable closure law); measures equivalent to absolutely winning sets assign them full measure (an equivalence full-measure law); and zero measure under singular measures is the only exceptional case (a singular exception law). (absolute winning Cantor stability closure)

**中文。** 定义：Schmidt 博弈的绝对赢集类在遍历系统不变测度下的稳定性。猜想：绝对赢集与任意全维 Cantor 集的交仍绝对赢（Cantor 稳定律）；该类对可数并与可数交封闭（可数封闭律）；与绝对赢集等价的测度赋予其全测度（等价全测律）；奇异测度下的零测为唯一例外情形（奇异例外律）。
