# 00000007644

**English.** Definition: The CFL condition as the stability condition of explicit schemes. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the implicit exemption domain and the unconditional stability of implicit schemes; the complete classification of the domain is the linear case; and the cost of exemption is linear-system solving with cost order 1. (implicit-exemption conversion)

**中文。** 定义：CFL 条件：显式格式的稳定性条件。猜想：换算常数为隐式豁免域且隐式格式的无条件稳定；域的完全分类为线性情形；豁免的代价为线性方程组求解且代价的次数为 1。（隐式豁免换算常数结构）
