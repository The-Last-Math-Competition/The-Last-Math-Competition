**SQ-19 ◆** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: The Fermat quotient q_p(2) = (2^{p−1}−1)/p measures arithmetic statistics modulo p. Conjecture: The bias of the distribution of q_p(2) mod p is completely controlled by the Wieferich primes: among the first X primes, the proportion with deviation exceeding t has an explicit exponential tail, and the exponent of the tail is reciprocal to the logarithmic density of the Wieferich count. (Fermat quotient deviation tail law)

**中文。** 定义：Fermat 商 q_p(2)=(2^{p−1}−1)/p 度量模 p 的算术统计。猜想：q_p(2) mod p 分布的偏差由 Wieferich 素数完全控制：前 X 个素数中偏差超 t 的比例为显式指数尾，且尾的指数与 Wieferich 计数的对数密度互为倒数。（Fermat 商偏差尾律）
