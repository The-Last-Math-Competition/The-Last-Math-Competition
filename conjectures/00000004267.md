# 00000004267

**English.** Definition: Zhu's C_2-cofiniteness is the finite dimensionality of the C_2 quotient space. Conjecture: The new criterion is that the characters are modular forms with finite-dimensional weight spaces; in the strongly rational case this criterion is equivalent to Zhu's condition and can be verified independently. (modular form criterion of C_2-cofiniteness)

**中文。** 定义：Zhu 的 C₂ 余有限性指 C₂ 商空间有限维的性质。猜想：新判据为特征标是模形式且权空间有限维,该判据在强有理情形与 Zhu 条件等价且可独立验证。（C₂ 余有限的模形式判据）
