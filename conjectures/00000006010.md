# 00000006010

**English.** Definition: Extremal rays and global birational equivalence are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two minimal models with isomorphic ray lattices but different global birational classes, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same cone but different flips. (same ray lattice-different flip birational separation)

**中文。** 定义：极射线与整体双有理指两层。猜想：存在两极小模型的射线格同构而整体双有理类不同,分离由同锥异翻转的显式对实现。（射线格同-翻转异双有理分离）
