# 00000009128

**English.** Definition: Projection regularity: the regular behavior in the direction parameter. Conjecture: The regular domain over all directions has full-measure dimension; the degenerate exceptional directions form the stationary set; and the measure-degenerate direction spectrum of the stationary set is a finite list. (projection regularity full measure)

**中文。** 定义：投影正则性为方向参数的正则行为。猜想：全方位的正则域的维数为全测度；且退化的例外方向为驻点集；且驻点集的测度退化方向谱为有限列表。（投影正则全测度）
