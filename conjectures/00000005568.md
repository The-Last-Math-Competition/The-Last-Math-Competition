# 00000005568

**LP2-08 ◆** — source: 171_有限群的局部分析与融合.md

**English.** Definition: Exotic systems are fusion structures without group realization. Conjecture: For primes at least five the minimal exotic rank is three, realized by special group extensions, no rank-two nonabelian exotics exist, and the boundary is exact. (rank-three boundary of exotics at large primes)

**中文。** 定义：例外系统指无群实现的融合结构。猜想：素数不小于五时最小例外秩为三且由特殊群扩张实现,秩二非交换例外不存在且分界精确。（大素数例外秩三分界）
