# 00000005804

**English.** Definition: Type-two singularities have unbounded rates, with the classification of translators. Conjecture: The translator types are given by an explicit family of slopes with maximal slope one; for convex curves the translators are the unique grim-reaper family; and the slope spectrum for general curves is a closed interval with endpoints given by the asymptotes. (slope-family structure of type-two translators)

**中文。** 定义：二型奇点：速率无界的奇点；平移子的分类。猜想：平移子型由坡度的显式族给出且最大坡度为一；凸曲线情形的平移子为 grim reaper 型的唯一族；坡度谱在一般曲线情形为闭区间且端点由渐近线给出。（二型平移子坡度族结构）
