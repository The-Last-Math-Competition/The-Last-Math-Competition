**English.** Definition: The critical point count of eigenfunctions on a random flat torus is a Morse statistic. Conjecture: The critical point count concentrates around an explicit constant times λ² in mean; and the variance of the Nicolaescu statistic is a universal constant. (critical point count concentration law)
