**English.** Definition: Sheafification is the iteration of the plus construction of the adjoint functor. Conjecture: The upper bound on the termination step count of the plus construction is aleph_0, finite-step termination holds exactly when the adjunction splits, and the splitting criterion is explicit and stepwise verifiable. (sheafification termination splitting criterion)
