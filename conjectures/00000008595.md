# 00000008595

**MLC-05 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: The minimax rate of sparse regression in sparsity s and dimension p. Conjecture: The minimax risk of s-sparse linear regression is 2 sigma^2 s log(p/s)/n with the constant 2 optimal; and the rate correction for inseparable correlated designs is a harmonic expression of the correlation spectrum. (sparse regression constant two)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏回归的 minimax 速率在 sparsity s 与维数 p 下。猜想：s-稀疏线性回归的 minimax 风险为 2σ²s log(p/s)/n 且常数 2 最优；不可分相关设计的速率修正为相关谱调和式。（稀疏回归常数二）
