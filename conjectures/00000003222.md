**English.** Definition: Wegner estimates: finiteness control of spectral measures. Conjecture: The Wegner constant: the Wegner estimate constant for Lipschitz potentials is the supremum of the distribution density, with tightness attained by the uniform distribution. (Wegner constant uniform distribution tightness)

**中文。** 定义：Wegner 估计：谱测度的有限性控制。猜想：Wegner 常数：Lipschitz 位势的 Wegner 估计常数为分布密度的上确界且常数的紧性为均匀分布。（Wegner 常数均匀分布紧性）
