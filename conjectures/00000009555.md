**English.** Definition: The Siegel-Veech constant cSV measures the cylinder-counting asymptotics N(T) ~ cSV T^2 for cylinders of height at most T on translation surfaces, obtained by summing principal boundary contributions of Veech degenerations. Conjecture: The Siegel-Veech constant of each stratum is an explicit rational linear combination of the volumes of the stratum and its principal boundary, with combination coefficients graph-theoretic data of boundary types; and the ratio of the cylinder-count and saddle-connection SV constants is a rational function depending only on the singularity type. (Siegel-Veech rational formula)

**中文。** 定义：Siegel–Veech 常数 cSV 度量平移曲面上高度 ≤T 的柱面计数渐近 N(T)~cSV·T²；它由 Veech 边界退化的主边界贡献求和得到。猜想：每个 stratum 的 Siegel–Veech 常数为该 stratum 与其主边界体积的显式有理线性组合，组合系数为边界型的图论数据；柱面数与鞍点联络数两个 SV 常数之比为只依赖奇点型的有理函数。（Siegel-Veech 有理公式）
