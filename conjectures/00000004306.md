**English.** Definition: The Cohen-Lenstra heuristics are the random-matrix distribution model of the p-part of class groups. Conjecture: After an explicit correction of the 2-rank distribution for p = 2 by genus theory, it agrees with the model, numerically confirmed up to discriminants of 10 to the 12th power. (confirmation of the CL 2-rank genus correction)

**中文。** 定义：Cohen–Lenstra 启发式指类群 p 部分的随机矩阵分布模型。猜想：p = 2 的 2 秩分布经 genus 理论显式修正后与模型吻合,数值确证到判别式 10 的 12 次幂。（CL 二秩 genus 修正确证）
