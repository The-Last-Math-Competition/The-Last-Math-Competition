**PHT-11 ★** — source: 316_持久同调II_统计与稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: Subsampled topological inference: the validity domain of m-out-of-n resampling. Conjecture: The uniform validity domain of subsampling is m = o(n/log n), with the window boundary tight; the bias outside the window is explicitly characterized by divergence of the expected count of extreme persistence bars. (subsampling validity window)

**中文。** 定义：子抽样拓扑推断：m out of n 重抽样的有效性域。猜想：子样本率的一致有效域为 m=o(n/log n) 且窗口的边界紧；窗口外的偏差由极端持久条的期望数的发散显式刻画。（子抽样有效窗口）
