**English.** Definition: Minorization is the lower-bound structure of separated-set counting. Conjecture: The domain of the ratio of the supremum over separated sets to the chain functional closes; the constant of the ratio is the dual pair; and the realization of the dual pair is the minimax measure. (dual-pair constant of the minorization chain ratio)

**中文。** 定义：极小化为分离集计数的下界结构。猜想：分离集上确界与链函数之比的域封闭；且比的常数为对偶对；且对偶对的实现为极小极大测度。（极小化链比对偶对常数）
