**TAG-05 ★** — source: 308_算术超越.md

**English.** Definition: Values of the Gamma function at rationals: Chudnovsky proved the algebraic relation structure of powers of Gamma(1/3) and Gamma(1/4) with pi. Conjecture: The transcendence degree of Gamma(1/5) and Gamma(2/5) is exactly 2; more generally Gamma(1/N) and Gamma(2/N) are algebraically independent for N at least 7, and when the denominator contains more than two distinct prime factors the transcendence degree is half the Euler phi value of the denominator. (Gamma transcendence degree spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Γ 函数在有理点的值：Chudnovsky 已证 Γ(1/3) 与 Γ(1/4) 的幂与 π 的代数关系结构。猜想：Γ(1/5) 与 Γ(2/5) 的超越次数恰为 2；更一般地 Γ(1/N) 与 Γ(2/N) 在 N≥7 时代数无关，且分母含两个以上不同素因子时超越次数为分母的 Euler 函数值的一半。（Gamma 超越次数谱）
