# 00000007358

**English.** Definition: Nonstandard difference equations with the continuous limits of discrete equations. Conjecture: The hyperfinite limit of discrete equations holds, and the solution of the limit is the standard solution. (standard-solved hyperfinite difference limits)

**中文。** 定义：非标准差分方程：离散的连续极限。猜想：差分的极限：离散方程的超有限极限且限的解为标准解。（差分方程超有限标准解极限离散连续）
