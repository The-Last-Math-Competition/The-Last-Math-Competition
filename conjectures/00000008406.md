**English.** Definition: The convergent power of the motivic measure: the motivic convergence radius of the power series expansion of the arc measure. Conjecture: The convergence radius is the reciprocal of the logarithmic threshold 1 of the singularity; the motives of the power series coefficients are symmetric power motives; and the motivic criterion for the convergence threshold is the boundedness of the dual motivic measure. (motivic measure convergent power)

**中文。** 定义：母题测度的收敛幂指弧测度的幂级数展开的母题收敛半径。猜想：收敛半径为奇点对数阈值 1 的倒数；幂级数系数的母题为对称幂母题；收敛阈值的母题判据为对偶母题测度的有界性。（母题测度收敛幂）
