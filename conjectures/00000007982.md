# 00000007982

**CD3-22 ★** — source: 251_编码理论III_列表解码与局部译码.md

**English.** Definition: The algebraic geometry of error-correcting pairs: the existence of pairs (C_1, C_2) for a code C with C_1 C_2 contained in C-perp (componentwise product). Conjecture: The existence of error-correcting pairs for AG codes is decided by the adaptability criterion of Weierstrass gaps of divisors on the curve; the tightness of the criterion is uniquely determined by the diagonalization of the gap graph of limit curves (Ferrers type); and complete decoding of codes without error-correcting pairs is NP-hard (an equivalent translation of dual rigidity and complexity), with the reduction maps preserving the weight-3 gap structure. (AG error-correcting pair gap criterion)

**中文。** 定义：错误校正对的代数几何：码 C 的错误校正对（C₁,C₂）使 C₁*C₂ ⊆ C^⊥（分量积）的存在性。猜想：AG 码的错误校正对的存在由曲线的除子的 Weierstrass 间隙的适配性判据；判据的紧性由极限曲线（Ferrers 型）的间隙图的对角化唯一决定；且无错误校正对的码的完全译码为 NP 困难（对偶刚性与复杂度的等价转化），NP 困难性的归约映射保持权 3 的间隙结构。
