**English.** Definition: The boundary height field of the Z² dimer is the random process of boundary heights. Conjecture: Its maximum distribution is of explicit Gumbel type; and the location parameter is locked by the Bethe ansatz of the log gas. (dimer extremal Gumbel location parameter)
