**English.** Definition: Polytope graphs with the combinatorial structure of one-dimensional skeletons. Conjecture: Steinitz's theorem holds, the graphs of three-dimensional polytopes being three-connected planar graphs, and the uniqueness of realization is embedding equivalence. (three-connected planar Steinitz realization)

**中文。** 定义：多面体图：一维骨架的组合结构。猜想：Steinitz 定理：三维多面体图为三连通平面图且图的实现的组合唯一性为嵌入等价。（Steinitz 三连通平面嵌入实现唯一组合）
