# 00000000006

**NT-06 ★** — source: 01_数论.md

**English.** Conjecture: Let A ⊂ [N] be such that the average of any two of its elements of the same parity is never prime; then |A| ≤ (1/3+ε)N, and the lower bound 1/3 is asymptotically attained by the multiples of 3.

**中文。** 猜想：设 A ⊂ [N],且 A 中任意两个同奇偶元素的平均数都不是素数,则 |A| ≤ (1/3+ε)N;而下界 1/3 由 3 的倍数渐近达到。
