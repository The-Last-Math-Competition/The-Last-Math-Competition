**English.** Definition: The halting problem of Abelian networks is the termination decision of chip processes. Conjecture: The universal critical exponent of the halting problem is two; and the universality of the exponent is independent of network topology. (Abelian network halting exponent)

**中文。** 定义：阿贝尔网络的停机问题为芯片过程的终止判定。猜想：停机问题的普适临界指数为二；且指数的普适性与网络拓扑无关。（阿贝尔网络停机指数）
