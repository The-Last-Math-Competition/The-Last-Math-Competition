# 00000006204

**English.** Definition: Interaction phase shifts are the phase movement of multi-soliton interaction. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit law of interaction phase shifts, the pairwise shifts of logarithmic-difference type; the logarithm coefficient is the coupling-constant ratio; and the sum of phase shifts is zero in the center-of-mass frame. (logarithmic phase-shift coefficient conversion of multi-solitons)

**中文。** 定义：交互相移：多孤子相互作用的相位移动。猜想：换算常数为交互相移的显式律且两两相移为对数差型；对数的系数为耦合常数比；相移的和在质心系下为零。（多孤子对数相移系数换算）
