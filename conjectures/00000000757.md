# 00000000757

**English.** Definition: The ε-factor is the root-number invariant of local analysis. Conjecture: For a family of automorphic representations with fixed additive character, the ε-values are equidistributed on the unit circle; and the distribution's angle is Haar-uniform. (epsilon-factor unit-circle equidistribution)

**中文。** 定义：ε-因子为局部分析的根数不变量。猜想：对固定加性特征的自守表示族的 ε 在单位圆上等分布；且分布的角度为 Haar 均匀。（ε 因子单位圆等分布）
