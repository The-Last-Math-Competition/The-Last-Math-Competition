**English.** Definition: The rank of a Drinfeld module is its number of function-field slopes. Conjecture: The prime-ideal congruence distribution of rank two agrees with a two-dimensional representation; and the kernel of the agreement is the equidistribution window. (Drinfeld rank two equidistribution window congruence)
