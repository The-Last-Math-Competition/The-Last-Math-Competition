# 00000002154

**GA-14 ★** — source: 57_代数图论II.md

**English.** Definition: The number of cyclic factors of the critical group Jac(G) (the sandpile group) is the number of its cyclic Z-components. Conjecture: The classification of graphs with zero cyclic factors (i.e. Jac entirely cyclic-free): exactly subdivisions of closed Eulerian graphs (trivial exceptions exhausted).

**中文。** 定义：临界群 Jac(G)(sandpile 群)的循环因子数指其 Z-循环分量的个数。猜想：循环因子数 =0 的图(即 Jac 完全无循环分量)的分类:恰为闭欧拉图的细分(平凡例外穷尽)。
