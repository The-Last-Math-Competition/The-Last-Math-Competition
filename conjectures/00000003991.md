# 00000003991

**PC2-21 ◆** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: The functional pigeonhole principle PHP_n is the formalization of the absence of an injection from n+1 to n. Conjecture: The cutting plane proof length lower bound of PHP_n is 2^{n/8}, and the exponential constant 1/8 cannot be improved under cutting plane rules with bounded coefficients. (functional PHP cutting plane exponent)

**中文。** 定义：泛函鸽笼 PHP_n 指 n+1 到 n 无单射的公式化。猜想：PHP_n 在切平面系统的证明长度下界为 2^{n/8},且指数常数 1/8 在系数有界的切平面规则下不可改进。（泛函 PHP 切平面指数）
