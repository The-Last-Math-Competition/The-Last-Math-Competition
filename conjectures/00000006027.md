# 00000006027

**English.** Definition: The positive-exponent measure is the proportion of chaotic orbits. Conjecture: In near-integrable domains it is exponentially small, given by the stratified integral of broken tori, and the kernel of the integral is the residual-gap lattice. (residual-gap broken-torus integral of positive exponents)

**中文。** 定义：正指数测度指混沌轨道的比例。猜想：近可积域中指数小且由破裂环面分层积分给出；且积分的核为残隙格。（正指数残隙格破裂环面积分）
