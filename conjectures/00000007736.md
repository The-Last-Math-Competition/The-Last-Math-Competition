# 00000007736

**English.** Definition: The free max convolution boxed is defined by the Ben Arous-Voiculescu geometric construction, the noncommutative analogue of classical order statistics. Conjecture: Under suitable linear normalization, free maxima of free iid sequences converge to a free Gumbel law whose R-transform satisfies a deformation of the cubic R^3 + tR^2 - 1 = 0; and the tail exponent ratio between the free and classical theories is always 2. (free Gumbel limit and exponent duality)

**中文。** 定义：自由最大卷积 ⊡ 由 Ben Arous–Voiculescu 的几何构造定义：对自由独立自伴 X,Y，X ⊡ Y 的分布由其谱测度经自由几何最大值机制给出，是经典次序统计量的非交换类比。猜想：在适当线性归一化下自由 iid 序列的自由最大值收敛到自由 Gumbel 律，该律的 R-变换满足三次方程 R³+tR²-1=0 的形变；其尾部指数在自由与经典两个理论中的比率恒为 2。
