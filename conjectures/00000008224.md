# 00000008224

**English.** Definition: The p-adic depth of the Kirillov formula for representations of p-adic groups: the orbit correspondence dep_orb of the depth of a representation. Conjecture: dep_orb (the correspondence between depth and coadjoint orbits) is the minimal realization of the ramification depth of orbits (a minimal realization law); the depth spectrum (values of depths over all representations) is (1/2) Z_{>=0} (a half-lattice depth spectrum law); and the completeness of the construction of depth-zero representations (Camille type constructions covering all depth-zero) is given by the minimal lattice covering of the Moy-Prasad stratification of admissible orbits (a minimal lattice covering law). (p-adic depth half-lattice Moy-Prasad covering)

**中文。** 定义：分支群的表示的 Kirillov 公式的 p-adic 深度：p-adic 群表示的深度 depth(π) 的轨道对应 dep_orb。猜想：dep_orb（深度与余伴随轨道的对应）为轨道的分歧深度的最小实现（最小实现律）；且深度谱（一切表示的深度的值）= 1/2·Z_{≥0}（半格深度谱律）；深度零表示（depth-zero）的构造（Camille 型构造）的完备性（一切 depth-zero 的构造覆盖）由容许轨道的 Moy–Prasad 分层的极小格点覆盖（极小格覆盖律）。
