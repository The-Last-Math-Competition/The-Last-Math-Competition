# 00000005449

**English.** Definition: Random verification is the error probability of probabilistic group-structure checks such as commutativity spot checks. Conjecture: The error probability of k independent checks is explicit negative exponential, with the exponential coefficient corrected by the count of nontrivial automorphisms; the detection power of a single check is at least one half at noncommutative witnesses; and the correction coefficient is exactly 1 when the automorphism group is trivial. (automorphism-corrected probability of random verification)

**中文。** 定义：随机验证：概率性的群结构检查（如交换性抽查）的误差概率。猜想：k 次独立检查的误差概率为负指数显式且指数系数由非平凡自同构的计数修正；单次检查的检测力在非交换 witness 处至少为二分之一；修正系数当自同构群平凡时恰为 1。（随机验证自同构修正概率）
