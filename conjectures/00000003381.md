# 00000003381

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative measure theory: noncommutative Lp spaces. Conjecture: The uniform convexity of noncommutative Lp: for p > 1 noncommutative Lp is uniformly convex, with the exponent of the convexity modulus the conjugate extremum of p. (noncommutative Lp conjugate extremum convexity modulus)

**中文。** 定义：非交换测度论：非交换 Lp 空间。猜想：非交换 Lp 的均匀凸性：p>1 的非交换 Lp 为一致凸且凸性模的指数为 p 的共轭极值。（非交换 Lp 共轭极值凸模）
