# 00000001777

**English.** Definition: The Fontaine–Mazur conjecture states that geometric Galois representations are potentially semistable. Conjecture: The FM criterion for two-dimensional odd representations ρ over quadratic fields is: det ρ is a twist of the cyclotomic character and ρ|_{I_p} is of Steinberg type from a finite set; the criterion is explicitly verifiable item by item.

**中文。** 定义：Fontaine–Mazur 猜想指几何 Galois 表示潜在半稳定。猜想：二次域上的二维奇表示 ρ 的 FM 判据为:det ρ 为分圆特征的 twists·ρ|_{Ip}=已由有限集的 Steinberg 类型;判据可显式逐条验证。
