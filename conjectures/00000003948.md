**G4-08 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: Dynamic connectivity maintains connectivity queries under edge insertions and deletions. Conjecture: The optimal update-query tradeoff curve is pieced together from its two endpoints and a middle polynomial segment, and no algorithm is simultaneously subpolynomial in both operations. (dynamic connectivity tradeoff curve)

**中文。** 定义：动态连通性指边插入删除下维护连通查询。猜想：最优更新-查询权衡曲线恰由两个端点与中间的多项式段拼出,不存在两端同时亚多项式的算法。（动态连通权衡曲线）
