# 00000008400

**English.** Definition: The configuration space motive: the formal motivic limit of truncated configuration spaces, whose measure is the motivic lift of the configuration integral. Conjecture: The generating function of Euler characteristics of configuration motives is the motivic lift of the exponential; the configuration motive measure decomposes in the collision-free limit into products of symmetric powers; and the decomposition coefficients are values of the motivic pairing of a quadratic form. (configuration motive measure decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：构形空间母题指删失构型空间的形式母题极限，其测度为构型积分的母题化。猜想：构型母题的 Euler 特征的生成函数为 exp 的母题提升；构型母题测度在无碰撞极限下分解为对称幂的乘积；分解的系数为二次型的母题配对的值。（构型母题测度分解）
