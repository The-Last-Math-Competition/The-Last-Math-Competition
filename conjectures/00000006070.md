**English.** Definition: Definable functions and resource sensitivity are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two linear systems with identical definable functions but different resource sensitivities, and the separation is realized by an explicit embedding with the same functions but different renormalization types. (same definable-different sensitivity embedding separation)
