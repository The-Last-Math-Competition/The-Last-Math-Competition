**English.** Definition: The deficit functional is the deficit of cascade decomposition, the Ginzburg-Landau type cascade of NLS. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit sum of deficit functionals and the cascade-compartment sum is cubic type; the coefficient of the cubic is the coupling constant; and the compartment-count upper bound is the modulus, given by conservation laws. (closed cubic-sum conversion of cascade deficits)
