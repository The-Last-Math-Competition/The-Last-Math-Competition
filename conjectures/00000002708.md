**English.** Definition: Dynamical Brauer–Manin: compatibility of orbit points with the Brauer group. Conjecture: The Brauer criterion for orbit points: global solvability of local orbit points is completely decided by the valuation obstruction of the constant part of the Brauer group, and the vanishing field of the obstruction is a cyclic extension. (dynamical Brauer complete decision)

**中文。** 定义：Dynamical Brauer–Manin：轨道点与 Brauer 群的相容性。猜想：轨道点的 Brauer 准则：局部轨道点的整体可解性由 Brauer 群的常值部分的赋值 obstruction 完全判定且 obstruction 的消没域为循环扩张。（动力 Brauer 完全判定）
