# 00000001148

**English.** Definition: The Feigin–Odesskii elliptic r-matrix r(z) is an elliptic-function solution valued in sl_n ⊗ sl_n. Conjecture: The period lattice of the spectral parameter of its quantization R(z) coincides with that of the Hecke elliptic curve E_τ, and the eigenvalue gap of R(z) at half-period points equals the explicit value of the degree mod n of the n-torsion line bundle on E_τ.

**中文。** 定义：Feigin–Odesskii 椭圆 r-矩阵 r(z) 为取值于 sl_n⊗sl_n 的椭圆函数解。猜想：其量子化 R(z) 的谱参数周期格与 Hecke 椭圆曲线 E_τ 的周期格一致,且 R(z) 在半周期点处的本征值间隙等于 E_τ 上 n-除子线丛的度 mod n 的显式值。
