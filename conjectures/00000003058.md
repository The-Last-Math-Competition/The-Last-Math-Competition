# 00000003058

**IP-18 ★** — source: 87_非线性PDE的可积检验.md

**English.** Definition: Asymptotic classification of soliton solutions: long-time behavior. Conjecture: The asymptotic decomposition of N solitons: the long-time solution is the superposition of translated single solitons, with the total phase shift conserved; the conservation count is the permutation of the velocity order. (soliton asymptotic phase shift conservation)

**中文。** 定义：孤子解的渐近分类：长时行为。猜想：N 孤子的渐近分解：长时解为单孤子的平移叠加且叠加的相移总和守恒；守恒的计数为速度序的置换。（孤子渐近相移守恒）
