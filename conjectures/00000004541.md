# 00000004541

**English.** Definition: A Gelfond-Schneider constant is a value of the type of an algebraic base raised to an irrational algebraic exponent. Conjecture: The distribution deviation of the partial quotients of its continued fraction has the uniform bound of type N to the negative 1/2+epsilon, and the deviation exponent cannot be improved and holds uniformly over the whole GS class. (deviation exponent of GS continued fractions)

**中文。** 定义：Gelfond–Schneider 常数指代数底的无理代数指数幂型值。猜想：其连分数部分商的分布偏差为 N 的负二分之一加 ε 次幂型均匀界,偏差指数不可改进且对 GS 类全体一致成立。（GS 连分数偏差指数）
