# 00000000958

**English.** Definition: The minimum-error discrimination of POVMs is the information content of a quantum measurement. Conjecture: The minimum-error discrimination of a quantum POVM is explicitly controlled by the frame's spectral gap; equality is exact for equiangular tight frames; and the control constant attains its optimal value at uniform POVMs. (discrimination-gap identity)

**中文。** 定义：POVM 的最小误差判别为量子测量的信息量。猜想：量子测量 POVM 的最小误差判别由框架的谱间隙显式控制；且等式在等角紧框架时精确；且控制常数在均匀 POVM 时达到最优值。（判别-间隙等式）
