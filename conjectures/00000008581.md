# 00000008581

**English.** Definition: Finite-band periodic Jacobi matrices: real symmetric tridiagonal matrices whose spectrum is a union of finitely many intervals. Conjecture: The criterion for N-band potentials is given by the Sodin-Yuditskii transfer function condition (an S-Y criterion law); the solution of the inverse spectral problem is unique up to mirror symmetry (a mirror uniqueness law); uniqueness fails in the hole case (spectral intervals degenerating to points, a hole exception law); and the parameter dimension of hole examples is 1 (a one-dimensional parameter law). (finite-band Jacobi Sodin-Yuditskii mirror)

**中文。** 定义：有限带周期 Jacobi 矩阵：谱为有限个区间的并的实对称三对角矩阵。猜想：N-带位势的判据由 Sodin–Yuditskii 的传递函数条件给出（S-Y 判据律）；且逆谱问题的解在镜像对称下唯一（镜像唯一律）；带孔（谱区间退化为点）情形的唯一性失效（带孔例外律）；带孔例的参数维数为 1（单维参数律）。
