# 00000009966

**MRG-26 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Repetitivity functions of Delone sets: growth of return radii of local configurations (the linear repetitivity question). Conjecture: The equivalence of linear growth of the repetitivity function with pure point diffraction holds within the uniquely ergodic framework; the upper bound of the linear coefficient is the reciprocal of the window volume, attained by model sets with irrational slopes. (linear repetitivity pure point equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Delone 集的重复性函数：局部构型回归半径的增长（线性重复性问题）。猜想：重复性函数线性增长与纯点衍射的等价在唯一 ergodic 框架内成立；线性系数的上界由窗口体积的倒数给出且无理斜率的模型集达到上界。（线性重复-纯点等价）
