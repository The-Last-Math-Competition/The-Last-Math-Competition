# 00000007940

**INT-10 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The integrable restatement of Fernando-Luna inverse spectral rigidity: the complete integrability criterion for the Lax spectrum (lambda_i(t)) of skew-symmetric tops (Euler top type). Conjecture: The evolution of the spectrum is closed if and only if the compatibility equation of the Casimir functions of the rigid-body group and the inertia tensor satisfies an involution condition; and the algebraic obstruction to involution (rank deficit of the Poisson bracket) vanishes exactly when the isotropy representation of the Cartan decomposition of the Lie algebra is one of the four exceptional strongly orthogonal root systems (corresponding to the completion of the classical list of integrable tops). (Euler top strongly orthogonal completion)

**中文。** 定义：Fernando–Luna 逆谱刚性的可积转述：斜对称陀螺（Euler 陀螺型）的 Lax 谱 (λ_i(t)) 的完全可积判据。猜想：谱的演化封闭当且仅当刚体群的 Casimir 函数与惯性张量的相容方程满足对合条件；且对合性的代数障碍（Poisson 括号的秩亏）在李代数的 Cartan 分解的迷向表示恰为强正交根系的四类例外之一时消没（对应经典可积陀螺列表的完全化）。
