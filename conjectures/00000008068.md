**English.** Definition: The proof-theoretic cost of Borel determinacy: the stratification of proof strength of the determinacy hierarchy BDT_alpha of Borel games. Conjecture: The proof-theoretic strength of BDT_alpha is of the same order as the notation complexity of alpha (a hierarchy synchronization law); the minimal large-cardinal axiom beyond second-order arithmetic needed for Pi-1-1 determinacy is a weakening of measurability (the existence of sharps); and the proof-theoretic constant of Martin's pure tree lemma is the first fixed point of the range of the collapsing function for Sigma-1-1 well-ordering (the Martin constant). (Borel determinacy sharp Martin constant)
