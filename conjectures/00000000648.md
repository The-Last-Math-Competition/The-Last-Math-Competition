# 00000000648

**English.** Definition: The smooth knot concordance group 𝒞₁. Conjecture: 𝒞₁ contains a free subgroup of infinite rank that splits, and the spectrum of its prime-order torsion contains all primes; the image of the splitting map from the algebraic concordance group to 𝒞₁ is, after localization at every prime p, an arithmetic group of exponential growth (Zariski density of the coefficients).

**中文。** 定义：光滑纽结配边群 𝒞₁。猜想：𝒞₁ 含有秩无穷的自由分裂子群,且其素数阶扭部分的谱包含一切素数;由代数配边群到 𝒞₁ 的分裂映射的像在一切素数 p 局部化为指数增长的算术群(系数的 Zariski 稠密)。
