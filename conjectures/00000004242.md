**English.** Definition: The Witten conversion is the equivalence formula between Donaldson and Seiberg-Witten invariants. Conjecture: The complete table of conversion constants is always given by combinations of pairings of the SW fundamental class with the mu map, and it is universal for all simply connected four-manifolds. (universal D-SW conversion table)

**中文。** 定义：Witten 换算指 Donaldson 与 Seiberg–Witten 不变量间的等价公式。猜想：换算常数全表恒由 SW 基本类与 μ 映射配对的组合给出；且对一切单连通四维流形普适。（D-SW 换算普适表）
