**English.** Definition: The Griffiths singularity is the singular contribution of rare regions. Conjecture: The asymptotics of its exponent distribution is an explicit power-law tail, the tail exponent is given by the length generating function of rare regions, and the moment divergence threshold is exact. (generating function of the Griffiths power-law tail)
