# 00000000640

**English.** Definition: The Cayley graph Cay(Mod, generating set D). Conjecture: The spectral gap of Mod(Σ_g) with respect to Dehn twist generators is at least c/g (the decay rate of the lower bound on the spectral gap).

**中文。** 定义：Cayley 图 Cay(Mod, 生成集 D)。猜想：Mod(Σ_g) 的关于 Dehn twist 生成的谱隙 ≥ c/g(谱隙的下界衰减率)。
