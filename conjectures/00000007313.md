**RT4-13 ★** — source: 229_随机张量.md

**English.** Definition: Exponential tensors with diagonal extrema. Conjecture: The diagonal control of independent exponential terms holds, and the law of the control is the Poisson point process. (Poisson-diagonal exponential-tensor extrema)

**中文。** 定义：指数张量：对角极值。猜想：指数的极值：独立指数项的对角控制且制的律为 Poisson 点过程。（指数张量 Poisson 点过程对角控制独立极值律）
