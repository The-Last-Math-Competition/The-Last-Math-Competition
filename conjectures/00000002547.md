# 00000002547

**English.** Definition: The local isoperimetric profile of measures: the Cheeger constant of n-uniform measures. Conjecture: The local Cheeger constant of n-uniform measures is uniformly bounded, with the bound given explicitly by the minimizer of the tangent-cone class; the isoperimetric profile converges to that of the tangent cone at a power-law rate in the scaling factor. (measure isoperimetric rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：测度的局部等周剖面：n-均匀测度的 Cheeger 常数。猜想：n-均匀测度的局部 Cheeger 常数一致有界且界由切锥类的极小元显式给出；等周剖面的收敛到切锥剖面的速率为缩放因子的幂律。（测度等周刚性）
