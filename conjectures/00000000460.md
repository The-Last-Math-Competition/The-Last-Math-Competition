# 00000000460

**English.** Definition: A matching symmetric-function statistic is an equivariant map from perfect matchings to the ring of symmetric functions. Conjecture: All equivariant matching statistics of bounded degree have asymptotically the same distribution on uniform random matchings; and the joint limit is given by the moments of the Gaussian free field. (matching statistics universal limit)

**中文。** 定义：匹配对称函数统计为把完美匹配映到对称函数环的等变映射。猜想：一切有界度数的等变匹配统计量在均匀随机匹配上的分布渐近相同；且极限的联合为高斯自由场的矩。（匹配统计普适极限）
