**HS2-08 ◆** — source: 186_哈密顿系统.md

**English.** Definition: Herman's counterexample is a diffeomorphism not topologically conjugate. Conjecture: The interpolation of Cantor-set measures vanishes at high smoothness, the low-smoothness bound given by the variation functional, and the kernel of the vanishing is the regularity deficit. (regularity-deficit measure of Herman's Cantor interpolation)

**中文。** 定义：Herman 反例指非拓扑共轭的微分同胚。猜想：康托集测度的插值在高光滑时消失且低光滑界由变差泛函给出；且消失的核为正则性亏。（Herman 康托插值正则亏测度）
