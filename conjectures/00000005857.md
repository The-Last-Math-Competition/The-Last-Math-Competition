# 00000005857

**English.** Definition: Generalized quaternion defect is the block of quaternion-type defect groups. Conjecture: It is separated into three subtypes by the minimal module dimension, the kernel of the separation being the dimension spectrum; the minimal module dimension is the dichotomy of 1 and 2; and the subtype count is at most one quarter of the defect-group order. (minimal-module dimension trichotomy of quaternion groups)

**中文。** 定义：广义四元数亏：四元数型亏群的块。猜想：由极小模维数分离为三子型且分离的核为维数谱；极小模维数的取值为 1 与 2 的二择；三子型的个数不超过亏群阶的四分之一。（四元数极小模维数三分）
