**LD-09 ★** — source: 79_随机过程的大偏差.md

**English.** Definition: Ergodic large deviations of Markov processes: Donsker–Varadhan theory. Conjecture: The spectral characterization of the DV rate: the DV rate of the Markov semigroup on a compact space is the variation of the Dirichlet form, with variational minimum the spectral gap; the level sets of the rate are measure projections of Sobolev balls. (DV rate spectral gap variation)

**中文。** 定义：马尔可夫过程的遍历大偏差：Donsker–Varadhan 理论。猜想：DV 速率的谱刻画：紧空间的 Markov 半群的 DV 速率为 Dirichlet 型的变分且变分极小值为谱隙；速率水平集为 Sobolev 球的测度投影。（DV 速率谱隙变分）
