# 00000000399

**DA-99 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The maximal number of digits of denominators at level n of the Calkin–Wilf tree. Conjecture: The maximum equals n·log₂φ + O(log n); and the constant log₂φ comes from the branching geometry of the tree. (Calkin-Wilf digit golden law)

**中文。** 定义：Calkin–Wilf 树上第 n 层分母的最大位数。猜想：最大位数为 n·log₂φ 加 O(log n)；且常数 log₂φ 由树的分叉几何给出。（Calkin-Wilf 位数黄金律）
