**English.** Definition: The stability of the Willmore conjecture: rigidity of minimal surfaces. Conjecture: The modal spectrum of the second variation is always finite; and the finite dimension is the minimax dimension. (Willmore second variation finite modality)

**中文。** 定义：Willmore 猜想的稳定性为极小流形刚性。猜想：第二变分的模态谱恒为有限；且有限的维数为极小极大维数。（Willmore 第二变分有限模态）
