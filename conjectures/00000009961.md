# 00000009961

**MRG-21 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: rigidity of Patterson–Sullivan measure of boundary measure family: hyperbolic group. Conjecture: rigidity: structure group of measure family is structure in boundary dimension of quasi-isometry group greater than exception structure of measure class under unique; uniqueness of one is torsion obstacle of Thurston type. (boundary measure uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：边界拟共形测度族：双曲群的 Patterson–Sullivan 测度的刚性。猜想：拟共形测度族的刚性：结构群为拟等距群的共形结构在边界维数大于一的测度类下唯一；唯一性失效的例外结构为 Thurston 型的挠率障碍。（边界测度共形唯一性）
