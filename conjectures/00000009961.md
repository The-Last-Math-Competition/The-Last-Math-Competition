# 00000009961

**English.** Definition: Boundary quasiconformal measure families: rigidity of Patterson-Sullivan measures of hyperbolic groups. Conjecture: Rigidity of quasiconformal measure families: the conformal structure of the structure group is unique within measure classes of boundary dimension above one for quasi-isometry groups; the exceptional structures where uniqueness fails are Thurston-type torsion obstructions. (boundary measure conformal uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：边界拟共形测度族：双曲群的 Patterson–Sullivan 测度的刚性。猜想：拟共形测度族的刚性：结构群为拟等距群的共形结构在边界维数大于一的测度类下唯一；唯一性失效的例外结构为 Thurston 型的挠率障碍。（边界测度共形唯一性）
