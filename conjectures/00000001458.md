# 00000001458

**CX-08 ★** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The volume ratio vr(K) = vol(K)/vol(maximal inscribed ellipsoid), normalized. Conjecture: Between the asymmetric minimal volume ratio at the simplex (normalized vr = Θ(n^{n/2})) and the symmetric case (n^{n/2}), the interpolation is realized by an explicit ℓ_p-unit-ball family with p(n) = log n − log log n + o(1); and this family is minimal.

**中文。** 定义：凸体 K 的体积比 vr(K)=vol(K)/vol(最大内接椭球) 的归一化。猜想：非对称最小体积比在单纯形(vr=Θ(n^{n/2}) 归一)与对称情形(n^{n/2}) 之间由显式的 ℓ_p-单位球插值族 p(n) 实现:p(n)=log n−log log n+o(1),且该族为极小。
