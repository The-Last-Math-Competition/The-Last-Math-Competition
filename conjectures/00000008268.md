**SFL-08 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The bifurcation of filters: the bifurcation index bif_ch of channel degeneration for the existence of steady-state filters. Conjecture: bif_ch (the bifurcation index of channel degeneration) is 1/2 (a half-exponent law); the hysteresis of the bifurcation (lag under slowly varying noise) has loop width the one-third power of the variation rate (a one-third hysteresis law); and the cusp position of the bifurcation diagram of the filtering error (the S-curve of error versus information parameter) is at the Fisher-information half-critical (a Fisher half-critical cusp law). (filter channel bifurcation hysteresis cusp)

**中文。** 定义：滤波的分岔：稳态滤波的存在性的通道分岔（观测通道的退化）的分岔指数 bif_ch。猜想：bif_ch（通道退化的分岔指数）= 1/2（半指数律）；且分岔的滞后（噪声缓变的迟滞）的环的宽度为缓变率的 1/3 次幂（1/3 迟滞律）；滤波误差的分岔图（误差-信息参数的 S 曲线）的尖点（cusp）的位置为 Fisher 信息的 1/2 临界（Fisher 半临界尖点律）。
