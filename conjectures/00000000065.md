**English.** Conjecture: A central limit theorem for prime rotations: for every irrational α and every smooth f with ∫f = 0, the sum S(x) = Σ_{p≤x} f({αp}) satisfies S(x)/√(x/log x) ⇒ N(0, σ²(f)) with σ²(f) explicit.
