**English.** Definition: The Dunford integral is the spectral decomposition of the resolvent along contours. Conjecture: The infimum of the norm of Riesz projections for spectral splitting is explicitly controlled by the spectral separation; and the control constant is the reciprocal of the spectral gap. (Riesz projection spectral-distance stability)

**中文。** 定义：Dunford 积分为预解式沿围道的谱分解。猜想：谱分割的 Riesz 投影的范数的下确界由谱间距显式控制；且控制的常数为谱间隙的倒数。（Riesz 投影谱距稳定性）
