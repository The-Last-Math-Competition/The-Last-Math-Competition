# 00000009768

**OIE-08 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Zeta regularization of compact operator eigenvalues: the exact relation between the convergence domain of zeta_A(s) = sum |lambda_n|^s and singular value decay. Conjecture: Cases where the convergence exponent of the eigenvalue zeta is strictly greater than the singular value exponent are rare exceptions; exceptional operators must have double-exponentially decaying singular values, and the classification of exceptions is the trace-class hyperdiagonal family. (eigenvalue zeta exponent)

**中文。** 定义：紧算子特征值的 zeta 正则化：ζ_A(s)=Σ|λ_n|^s 的收敛域与奇异值衰减的精确关系。猜想：特征值 zeta 的收敛指标严格大于奇异值指标的情形为罕见例外；例外算子的奇异值必需双重指数衰减且例外的分类为迹类超对角族。（特征值 zeta 指标）
