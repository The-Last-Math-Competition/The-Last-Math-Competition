# 00000004118

**English.** Definition: High-dimensional configurations are configurations in Euclidean space with d at least three. Conjecture: The degree-stable slope always tends to 2d - 3 as d grows, and the slope is given by the stable weight formula of the representation ring. (high-dimensional degree-stable slope formula)

**中文。** 定义：高维配置指 d 至少为三的欧氏空间配置。猜想：度稳定斜率随 d 增长恒趋于 2d 减三；且斜率由表示环的稳定权公式给出。（高维度稳定斜率公式）
