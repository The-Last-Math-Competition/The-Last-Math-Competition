# 00000000424

**SA-24 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: RSK for involutions: an involutive permutation corresponds to a single tableau. Conjecture: The shape of the tableau of a uniform involution converges to the involution version of the Ω curve, with first-row length ~ 2√n·c₀, where c₀ is an explicit integral constant associated with the involution limit shape Ω̃.

**中文。** 定义：对合的 RSK:对合排列对应单表。猜想：均匀对合对应表的形状趋于 Ω 曲线的对合版本,首行长度 ~ 2√n·c₀,其中 c₀ 为与对合极限形状 Ω̃ 相关的显式积分常数。
