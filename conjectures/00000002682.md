# 00000002682

**English.** Definition: The Zariski topology: closed sets of the prime spectrum of a commutative ring are of type V(I). Conjecture: A categorical characterization of compactness of the Zariski topology: Noetherian spectra are spectral spaces (Hochster); a quantitative relaxation of Noetherianity: the descent rate of the Zariski chain condition agrees with the Krull dimension of the ideal lattice, with deviation the count of minimal primes after localization. (Zariski spectrum Noetherianization)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 拓扑：交换环的素谱上的闭集为 V(I) 型集合。猜想：Zariski 拓扑的紧性的范畴刻画：Noether 谱为 spectral 空间（Hochster 定理）；Noether 条件的定量放松：谱的 Zariski 链条件下降率与理想格的 Krull 维数一致且一致偏差为局部化的极小素数计数。（Zariski 谱 Noether 化）
