# 00000004148

**English.** Definition: The Steel core model induction is the inductive construction from the core model, layer by layer of obstacle measures. Conjecture: The explicit enumeration of the obstacle measures is countable, the first obstacle appears at the Woodin layer, and the measures strictly increase layer by layer. (Steel induction obstacle measure spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Steel 核模型归纳指从核模型出发逐障碍测度的归纳构造。猜想：障碍测度的显式列举为可数集,首个障碍出现于 Woodin 层且逐层严格递增。（Steel 归纳障碍测度谱）
