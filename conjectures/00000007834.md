# 00000007834

**MSE-24 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The intersection geometry of Hecke orbits on modular curves: the intersection numbers I_{p,q} = T_p dot T_q of the image curves of Hecke correspondences T_p on Mbar_1(N). Conjecture: The diagonalization of I_{p,q} is given by Hecke eigenforms: I_{p,q} is the normalized truncated sum over f of c_f(p) c_f(q)/<f,f> with weight and level constraints; and for p = q the normalized self-intersection contribution is a log p transcendental constant, the height limit of the modular height being exactly the logarithmic prime contribution. (Hecke intersection eigenform diagonalization)

**中文。** 定义：模曲线的 Hecke 轨迹的交几何：Hecke 对应 T_p 在 Mbar_1(N) 上的像曲线的交数 I_p,q = T_p·T_q（相交数）。猜想：I_p,q 的对角化由 Hecke 特征形式给出：I_{p,q}=Σ_f c_f(p)c_f(q)/⟨f,f⟩ 的规范化的截断（按权与水平约束）；且当 p=q 时自交的正规化贡献为 log p 型超越常数，即模高度的高度极限恰为对数素数贡献。
