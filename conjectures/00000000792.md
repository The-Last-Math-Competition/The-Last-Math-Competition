# 00000000792

**PA-92 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: The hierarchy B_cris ⊂ B_st ⊂ B_dR of Fontaine's period rings and their filtration structure. Conjecture: B_dR is the smallest period ring through which every crystalline representation realizes its Hodge structure — any period ring realizing the de Rham comparison theorem for p-adic Galois representations must receive a homomorphism from B_dR; no intermediate proper extension can be omitted.

**中文。** 定义：Fontaine 周期环的层级 B_cris⊂B_st⊂B_dR 及其滤过结构。猜想：B_dR 是使一切晶体表示均实现其 Hodge 结构的最小周期环,即任何实现 p 进 Galois 表示的 de Rham 比较定理的周期环必然接收 B_dR 的同态;不存在可省略的中间真扩张。
