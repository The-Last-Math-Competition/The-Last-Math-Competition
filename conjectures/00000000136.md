# 00000000136

**English.** Definition: The Rademacher prime series denotes the random-sign series Σ ±1/p_n. Conjecture: Its distribution is absolutely continuous with an everywhere continuous density; the tails of the density decay at a law-of-the-iterated-logarithm rate. (absolute continuity of the prime series)

**中文。** 定义：Rademacher 素级数指 Σ±1/p_n 的随机符号级数。猜想：其分布为绝对连续且密度恒连续；密度的尾部衰减为重对数律型。（素级数绝对连续密度）
