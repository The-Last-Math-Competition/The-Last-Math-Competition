# 00000000823

**PV-23 △** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The eigenspace structure at doubled eigenvalues. Conjecture: On domains where a double eigenvalue has multiplicity 2, suitable linear combinations of eigenfunctions produce arbitrarily large vanishing order (singularities induced by multiplicity).

**中文。** 定义：双倍特征值处的特征空间结构。猜想：双特征值的重数为 2 的域可由特征函数的线性组合产生任意大 vanishing order(重数诱导的奇点)。
