**SMA-08 ★** — source: 306_Smale空间与符号动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Equilibrium states of Holder potentials on mixing SFTs: uniqueness and the variational principle are known; analyticity of the pressure functional and absence of phase transitions are the core of the thermodynamic formalism. Conjecture: The pressure functional of every Holder potential on a mixing SFT is real analytic with unique equilibrium state and no phase transitions; analyticity fails for countable-state Markov chains at the critical decay rate, with the failure threshold coinciding with the recurrence boundary of the graph. (pressure analyticity without phase transition)

**中文。** 定义：混合 SFT 上 Hölder 势的均衡态：唯一性与变分原理已知；压力泛函的解析性与相变缺失为热力学形式的核心。猜想：混合 SFT 上一切 Hölder 势的压力泛函实解析且均衡态唯一，无相变；解析性对可数状态 Markov 链的临界递减速率失效，失效阈值与图的常返性边界重合。（压力解析无相变）
