**English.** Definition: The complete criterion for the unitarity of cohomologically induced representations A_q(lambda): the Vogan conjecture. Conjecture: vogan_uni (the unitarity criterion for cohomological induction) is a completely explicit inequality in lambda (an explicit criterion law); the lambda on the boundary of the inequality (critical unitarity) form the boundary of the Wallach set (a Wallach boundary law); the signature of the invariant Hermitian form of boundary representations (critical representations) equals (-1)^k (a signature formula, k the cohomological degree); and the minimal deformation flipping the signature (sign change of the form) is a displacement of 1/2 (a half-displacement sign law). (Vogan unitarity Wallach boundary signature)
