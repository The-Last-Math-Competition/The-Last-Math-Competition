# 00000004163

**English.** Definition: The local obstruction of Waring-Goldbach is a congruence class with no representation modulo q. Conjecture: For sums of seven cubic powers of primes the local obstruction classes are exactly the three non-representable classes modulo 9, and adding one variable removes all obstructions. (exact table of WG cubic local obstructions)
