# 00000005889

**English.** Definition: Isogeometric analysis is the numerical method with the same basis for geometry and analysis. Conjecture: At maximal smoothness the approximation always reaches the optimal order, and the kernel of the order is the removal mechanism of spline multiplicity. (spline-multiplicity removal optimal order of isogeometric analysis)

**中文。** 定义：等几何分析指几何与分析同基的数值方法。猜想：最大光滑时逼近恒达最优阶；阶的核为样条重复度的消去机制。（等几何样条重复度消去最优阶）
