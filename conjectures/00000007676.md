# 00000007676

**QDE-16 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: The q-analogue Eulerian polynomial A_n(q,t)=sum q^{maj} t^{des} has the gamma-expansion A_n(q,t)=sum gamma_{n,j}(q) t^j(1+t)^{n-1-2j}. Conjecture: gamma_{n,j}(q) has nonnegative coefficients for all n, j, and the number of monomials of gamma_{n,j}(q) equals the cardinality of the peak-set family {S subset [n] : |S|=j, no adjacent elements}, the nonnegativity having a combinatorial proof by a weighted peak-set bijection. (peak-set monomial count of gamma coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：Eulerian 多项式的 q-模拟 A_n(q,t)=Σ_{π∈S_n} q^{maj(π)} t^{des(π)} 的 γ-展开为 A_n(q,t)=Σ_j γ_{n,j}(q) t^j(1+t)^{n-1-2j}。猜想：γ_{n,j}(q) ∈ N[q]（系数非负）对一切 n,j 成立，且 γ_{n,j}(q) 的单项式个数等于峰集合族 {S⊆[n]：|S|=j 且无相邻元素} 的基数，其非负性组合证明由带权峰集合的双射给出。
