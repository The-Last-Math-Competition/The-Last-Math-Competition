# 00000004122

**English.** Definition: Properness is the property that forcing preserves stationary sets of uncountable sets, and its game characterization is the half-game where the defender alternately chooses conditions. Conjecture: The minimal game length needed to characterize properness is exactly aleph_0, and no finite-round game characterization suffices. (minimal game length for properness)

**中文。** 定义：properness 指强制对不可数集保平稳集的性质,其博弈刻画指守方交替选条件的半游戏。猜想：刻画 properness 所需的最小游戏长度恰为 ℵ₀,任何有限回合的游戏刻画均不充分。（properness 博弈最小长度）
