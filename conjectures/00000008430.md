# 00000008430

**DFG-20 ★** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: A Schubert configuration of subspaces is a chain determined by dimension conditions. Conjecture: The count of Schubert configurations over finite fields is a q-polynomial; the leading coefficient of the q-polynomial is 1 and its roots are integers. (integer roots of Schubert counts)

**中文。** 定义：子空间的 Schubert 构形指由维数条件确定的链。猜想：Schubert 构形在有限域的计数为 q-多项式；q-多项式的首项系数为 1 且根为整数。（Schubert 计数整根）
