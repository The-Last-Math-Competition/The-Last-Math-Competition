**English.** Definition: Schubert of subspace denotes by dimension deterministic. Conjecture: counting of Schubert in finite field is leading term coefficient of q- polynomial; q- polynomial is 1 and is integer. (Schubert counting)

**中文。** 定义：子空间的 Schubert 构形指由维数条件确定的链。猜想：Schubert 构形在有限域的计数为 q-多项式；q-多项式的首项系数为 1 且根为整数。（Schubert 计数整根）
