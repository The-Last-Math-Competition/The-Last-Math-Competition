**English.** Definition: The curve complex has curves up to isotopy as vertices. Conjecture: The optimal value of its Gromov hyperbolicity constant is an explicit small constant, the bound is attained by an explicit filling-split construction and cannot be decreased. (optimal hyperbolicity constant of the curve complex)
