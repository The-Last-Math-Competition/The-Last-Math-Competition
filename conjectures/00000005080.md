**English.** Definition: Ground-state degeneracy and many-body localization stability are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two interacting systems with the same ground-state degeneracy but different many-body localization stability, and the separation is realized by an explicit construction of rare resonances. (same degeneracy-different localization stability separation)

**中文。** 定义：基态简并与多体局域稳定性指两层。猜想：存在两交互系统的基态简并度相同而多体局域稳定性不同,分离由稀有共振的显式构造实现。（简并同-局域稳定异分离）
