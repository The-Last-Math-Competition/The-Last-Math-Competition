**EC4-25 ★** — source: 142_遍历论的组合_熵与计数.md

**English.** Definition: Strict ergodicity means having exactly one invariant measure. Conjecture: The unique measure of a strictly ergodic subshift can be computed explicitly, the algorithm works by the convergent differences of uniform return times, and the convergence rate is an explicit reciprocal of the complexity. (explicit computation of strictly ergodic measures)

**中文。** 定义：严格遍历指恰有唯一不变测度。猜想：严格遍历子移位的唯一测度可显式计算,算法由均匀返回时间的差分收敛,收敛率为复杂度的显式倒数。（严格遍历测度显式计算）
