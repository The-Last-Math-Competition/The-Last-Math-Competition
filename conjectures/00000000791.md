# 00000000791

**English.** Definition: The p-adic Simpson correspondence is a bridge between representations and line bundles. Conjecture: The union of line-bundle slopes covers the entire spectral interval; overlaps occur only at critical slopes; and the covering count equals the lattice-point count of the weight space. (Simpson slope covering)
