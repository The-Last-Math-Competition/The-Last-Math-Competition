**English.** Definition: The Tait graph. Conjecture: The number of flyping classes of a prime alternating diagram equals the order of the automorphism group of the Tait graph (partially covered by Thistlethwaite's theory — new: the average number of flyping classes is O(1) and concentrated at 1).
