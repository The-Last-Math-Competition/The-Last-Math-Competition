**English.** Definition: The Dedekind η function: the 24th root of the modular discriminant. Conjecture: The transformation formula of η: the multiplier of η under modular transformations is the explicit Dedekind sum of a 24th root of unity, with the reciprocity of the sum the extension of elementary number theory. (eta Dedekind sum reciprocity)

**中文。** 定义：Dedekind η 函数：模判别式的 24 次根。猜想：η 的变换公式：模变换下的 η 的乘子为 24 次单位根的显式 Dedekind 和且和的互反律为初等数论的推广。（eta Dedekind 和互反律）
