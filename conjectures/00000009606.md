# 00000009606

**English.** Definition: The weak solution theory of inverse mean curvature flow (IMCF): Huisken-Ilmanen used it for the Penrose inequality; the high-dimensional regularity threshold is not fully determined. Conjecture: IMCF weak solutions are smooth from star-shaped initial data for n at most 7, with extinction time an explicit logarithm of the initial area; and the second-order correction of the asymptotic mass is an explicit integral of the Willmore energy functional. (inverse mean curvature high-dimensional regularity)

**中文。** 定义：逆平均曲率流（IMCF）的弱解理论：Huisken–Ilmanen 用于 Penrose 不等式；高维正则性门槛未完全确定。猜想：IMCF 弱解在 n≤7 从星形初值出发光滑且爆清时间为初值面积的显式对数；渐进质量的第二项修正为 Willmore 能量泛函的显式积分。（逆平均曲率高维正则）
