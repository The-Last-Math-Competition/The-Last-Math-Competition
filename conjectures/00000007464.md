# 00000007464

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of compact operators with the countable spectral structure. Conjecture: The countability of the spectrum holds, the sole accumulation point is zero; and the rate of convergence of the accumulation point to zero is controlled by the Schatten class membership of the compact operator. (Schatten-controlled compact spectral accumulation)

**中文。** 定义：紧算子的谱：可数谱结构。猜想：紧谱的聚点：谱的可数性且性的聚点唯一为零。(紧算子谱可数聚点唯一零紧谱结构)；且聚点趋于零的速率由紧算子的 Schatten 类属控制。
