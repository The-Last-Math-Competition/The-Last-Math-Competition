**English.** Definition: Convergence of isoperimetric profiles: the rigidity side. Conjecture: Convergence of profiles to the cone profile; the conical limit is given by tangent-cone convergence of Ricci solitons. (isoperimetric profile conical convergence)

**中文。** 定义：等周剖面的收敛：刚性侧。猜想：等周的收敛：剖面的收敛且敛的极限为锥面。(等周剖面锥面收敛收敛极限刚性等周)；且锥面极限由 Ricci 孤子的切锥收敛给出。
