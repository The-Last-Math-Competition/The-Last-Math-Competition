# 00000002625

**English.** Definition: Taylor expansion of Jacobi forms: the z-expansion coefficients are modular forms. Conjecture: The modularity deficit of Taylor coefficients: the r-th coefficient is a modular form of weight k+r plus a correction; the correction is given explicitly by Bernoulli polynomials of the index, and the deficit vanishes when r is odd. (Taylor coefficient modularity deficit)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式的 Taylor 展开：z-展开系数为模形式。猜想：Taylor 系数的模性亏损：第 r 层系数为权 k+r 的模形式加修正项；修正项由指标的 Bernoulli 多项式显式给出且亏损消没当 r 为奇数。（Taylor 系数模性亏损）
