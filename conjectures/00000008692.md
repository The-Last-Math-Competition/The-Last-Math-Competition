# 00000008692

**English.** Definition: The Sinkhorn algorithm: alternating projections for entropy-regularized OT. Conjecture: Sinkhorn converges linearly (a linearity law); the convergence rate is geometric in the Kullback and Hilbert metrics (a geometric rate law); the rate has a spectral characterization (a spectral gap characterization); and the saturation (optimal step) occurs at the half step length (a half step law). (Sinkhorn linear geometric spectral half step)

**中文。** 定义：Sinkhorn 算法：熵正则 OT 的交替投影。猜想：Sinkhorn 的线性收敛（线性律）；且收敛率（Kullback 与 Hilbert 度量）的几何（几何率律）；率的谱刻画（谱隙刻画）的（刻画律）；刻画的饱和（最优步长）的 1/2 步长的（半步长律）。
