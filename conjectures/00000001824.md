# 00000001824

**AG2-14 ★** — source: 46_代数几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The wall-chamber structure of double Hurwitz numbers is their piecewise-polynomial structure as the branch-profile parameters vary. Conjecture: The number of chambers in degree d is d(d−1)/2 + 1; computed from the critical angles of the resolution walls, the chamber-count formula is a quadratic polynomial in d.

**中文。** 定义：双 Hurwitz 数的壁腔结构指其随分歧轮廓参数变化的分段多项式结构。猜想：度 d 的腔数为 d(d−1)/2+1;由分辨率的壁的临界角计算,腔数公式为关于 d 的二次多项式。
