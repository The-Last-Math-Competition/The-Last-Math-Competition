**English.** Definition: Time reversibility is the deficit of the measure under time reversal. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the time-reversibility deficit and the entropy-production upper bound of approximate measures is superlinearly small; the deficit vanishes if and only if the measure is Hamiltonian type; and the entropy-production upper bound is the negative first power of t. (upper-bound conversion of reversibility-deficit entropy production)

**中文。** 定义：时间可逆：测度在时间反演下的亏损。猜想：换算常数为时间可逆亏损的显式且近似测度的熵产率上限为超线性小；亏损的消失当且仅当测度为哈密顿型；熵产率的上界为 t 的负一次幂。（可逆亏损熵产率上限换算）
