**English.** Definition: Chern classes are the characteristic classes of complex vector bundles. Conjecture: The axioms of Chern classes form an axiomatic system of classes, and the combination of the system is the Whitney sum formula. (Whitney-sum axioms of Chern classes)

**中文。** 定义：Chern 类：复向量丛的示性类。猜想：Chern 的公理：类的公理系统且统的组合为 Whitney 和。（Chern 类公理 Whitney 复向量丛示性类公理系统组合 Chern Whitney 系统 Whitney 示性类系统复丛）
