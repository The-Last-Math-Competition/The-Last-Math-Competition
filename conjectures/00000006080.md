# 00000006080

**NC3-10 △** — source: 188_非凸数值优化.md

**English.** Definition: Minima and saddle structures are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two networks with identical minimum positions and values but different saddle spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit perturbation with the same minima but different saddles. (same minima-different saddle perturbation separation)

**中文。** 定义：极小与鞍结构指两层。猜想：存在两网络的一切极小位置与值相同而鞍点谱不同,分离由同极小异鞍的显式扰动实现。（极小同-鞍谱异扰动分离）
