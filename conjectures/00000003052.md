**English.** Definition: Inverse spectral tests: uniqueness of spectral data of potentials. Conjecture: Spectral rigidity of potentials: compactly supported potentials are completely determined by the reflection coefficient plus bound states, with stability of determination the Lipschitz correction of the spectral norm. (potential spectral Lipschitz correction)

**中文。** 定义：逆谱检验：位势的谱数据的唯一性。猜想：位势的谱刚性：紧支撑位势由反射系数加束缚态完全决定且决定的稳定度为谱范数的 Lipschitz 修正。（位势谱 Lipschitz 修正）
