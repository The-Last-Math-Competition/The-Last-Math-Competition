# 00000000494

**English.** Definition: The partition function Z_n of the six-vertex model with domain-wall boundary. Conjecture: The asymptotics of Z_n/Z_{n-1} is an explicit 2^{n}·θ₃(q) (a limit closed form of Izergin–Korepin type).

**中文。** 定义：六顶点模型(domain wall) 配分函数 Z_n。猜想：Z_n/Z_{n-1} 的渐近为 2^{n}·θ₃(q) 的显式(Izergin–Korepin 的极限闭式)。
