**English.** Definition: Ultra-high moments are moments E(TrX^k) with moment order k reaching order N². Conjecture: The ultra-high moments of β-Hermite still equal the free-probability prediction at k = cN² (c < 1/4), with the divergence threshold exactly at k ~ N²logN. (high moment divergence threshold)

**中文。** 定义：超高阶矩指矩阶 k 达到 N² 量级的 E(TrX^k)。猜想：β-Hermite 的超高阶矩在 k = cN²(c < 1/4) 时仍等于自由概率预测值,发散阈值恰在 k ~ N²logN。（高阶矩发散阈值）
