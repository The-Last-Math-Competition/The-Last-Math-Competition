# 00000006633

**English.** Definition: Uniqueness is the singleness of infinite-volume states. Conjecture: High temperature gives uniqueness and the temperature threshold is given by the information deficit; the window of the deficit is the mutual-information window; and the ends of the mutual-information window are capacity thresholds. (information-deficit uniqueness threshold)

**中文。** 定义：唯一性为无穷体积态单一性。猜想：高温唯一且温度阈由信息亏格给出；且亏格的窗为互信息窗；且互信息窗的端为容量阈值。（唯一温度阈信息亏格）
