# 00000001656

**English.** Definition: Saturatedness of the derived category D^b(Coh X) means Ext-finiteness with a Serre functor. Conjecture: The saturation criterion for del Pezzo surfaces X: for degree d(X) ≥ 3, saturation always holds; the exceptions at degrees 1 and 2 are decided explicitly by the vanishing of Ext² on (−1)-curves of self-intersection −1.

**中文。** 定义：导范畴 D^b(Coh X) 的饱和性指其 Ext 有限且 Serre 函子存在。猜想：del Pezzo 曲面 X 的饱和判据为:度 d(X)≥3 时恒饱和;度 1,2 的例外由自交数为 −1 的例外曲线的 Ext² 消没显式判定。
