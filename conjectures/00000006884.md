**English.** Definition: The separation of determinant and permanent is the core problem of algebraic complexity theory. Conjecture: A construction window for polynomial-identity obstructions exists, with explicit exponential endpoints; and every polynomial inside the window distinguishes the two classes of varieties. (det-perm obstruction construction window)

**中文。** 定义：行列式与永久的分离为代数复杂性理论的核心问题。猜想：多项式恒等障碍的构造窗存在且窗端点为显式指数函数；且窗内一切多项式均区分两类簇。（行列式永久障碍构造窗）
