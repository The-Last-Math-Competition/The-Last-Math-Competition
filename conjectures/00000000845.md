**English.** Definition: Geodesic convexity of the Wasserstein space P(Ω) is the geometry of optimal transport. Conjecture: A lower curvature bound of P(Ω) is equivalent to a Ricci lower bound of Ω; and the characterization of equality extends to metric measure spaces in the nonsmooth setting. (Wasserstein curvature equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Wasserstein 空间 P(Ω) 的测地凸性为最优传输几何。猜想：P(Ω) 的曲率下界等价于 Ω 的 Ricci 下界；且等式刻画在非光滑域推广为度量测度。（Wasserstein 曲率等价）
