# 00000001056

**FG-56 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: A Frobenius-atypical curve is one on whose Jacobian Frobenius preserves no semisimple lattice structure. Conjecture: For atypical curves, #C(F_q) = q+1−Στ_i where all τ_i are explicit sums of 2√q·cos(2πj/(q+1)); and the maximal genus is q²−q+1.

**中文。** 定义：Frobenius 非典型曲线指 Frobenius 作用不保持任何半格结构的曲线。猜想：非典型曲线的点数为 #(C(F_q)) = q+1−Στ_i 且一切 τ_i 均可写为 2√q·cos(2πj/(q+1)) 的显式和;其最大亏格为 q²−q+1。
