**English.** Definition: The John ellipsoid: the maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid. Conjecture: John's decomposition: the decomposition identity of contact points of the ellipsoid, with the contact-point count at most n(n+3)/2. (John contact point decomposition upper bound count)

**中文。** 定义：John 椭球：最大体积内接椭球。猜想：John 分解：椭球的接触点的分解恒等式且恒等式的接触点数不超过 n(n+3)/2。（John 接触点分解上界数）
