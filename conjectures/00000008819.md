# 00000008819

**English.** Definition: Forward-backward splitting F-B and its accelerated version FISTA: proximal operator composite iterations with Nesterov inertial sequences for closed proper convex minimization. Conjecture: F-B converges at rate O(1/k) under Lipschitz gradients and cocoercivity, with convergence domain all closed proper convex objectives; FISTA attains the optimal O(1/k^2) rate with inertia parameter sequence beta_k = (k-1)/(k+2) the unique optimal choice; and the heavy-ball damping coefficient 3 is the threshold of perturbation stability — below 3 the error decay rate equals exactly the linear rate given by the spectral radius. (FISTA unique inertia heavy-ball threshold three)

**中文。** 定义：前向-后向分裂 F-B 与其加速版 FISTA：邻近算子复合迭代与 Nesterov 惯性序列对闭真凸最小化的收敛。猜想：F-B 在 Lipschitz 梯度与余强制性下收敛率为 O(1/k) 且收敛域为全部闭真凸目标；FISTA 达到 O(1/k²) 的最优率且惯性参数序列 β_k=(k-1)/(k+2) 为唯一最优选择；重球阻尼系数 3 为扰动稳定性的阈值——系数小于 3 时误差衰减率恰等于谱半径给定的线性速率。
