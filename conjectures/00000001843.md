**RM-03 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The rigidity of β-ensembles is the deviation of eigenvalues from classical (quantile) positions. Conjecture: For β-ensembles with general convex potentials V, the optimal rigidity is |λ_i − γ_i| ≤ (log N)^{1+ε}·(N min(i, N−i))^{-1/2+ε} (improving the log factor to nearly optimal); tight rigidity for N×N matrices has a log log N level correction.

**中文。** 定义：β-系综的刚性指特征值相对经典位置(分位数)的偏差。猜想：一般凸位势 V 的 β-系综的最优刚性为 |λ_i−γ_i| ≤ (log N)^{1+ε}\·(Nmin(i,N-i))^{-1/2+ε}(改进 log 因子至几乎最优);N×N 矩阵的紧刚性为 log log N 级修正。
