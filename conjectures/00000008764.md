**MKT-24 ★** — source: 277_代数K理论与MilnorK.md

**English.** Definition: The Quillen-Suslin theorem asserts that projective modules over polynomial rings over fields are free. Conjecture: Projective modules over the quantum plane are free when the parameter is a non-root of unity; and the minimal rank of stabilization equals the number of generators. (quantum plane freeness and minimal rank)

**中文。** 定义：Quillen–Suslin 定理断言域上多项式环的投射模自由。猜想：量子平面的投射模在参数为非单位根时自由；且稳定化的最小秩等于生成元数。（量子平面自由性与最小秩）
