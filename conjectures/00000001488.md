# 00000001488

**English.** Definition: Open systems (escape). Conjecture: The identity between the escape rate of an open hyperbolic system and the first resonance modulus has, in the nonequilibrium setting, its first correction given by the second derivative of the pressure (an escape-rate correction).

**中文。** 定义：open systems(逃逸)。猜想：开双曲系统的逃逸率与共振首模的等式在非平衡态设置下的第一修正为压强的二阶导数(逃逸率修正)。
