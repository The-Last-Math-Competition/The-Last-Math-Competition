**English.** Definition: Differential Galois theory of exponential function fields: modular functions and modular equations. Conjecture: The Galois group of the modular equation: the Galois group of the differential equation of the modular lambda function is an arithmetic subgroup of SL_2, and the group corresponds one-to-one with the commensurability classes of elliptic curves. (lambda Galois arithmetic subgroup)
