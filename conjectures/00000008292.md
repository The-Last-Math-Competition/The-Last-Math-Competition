# 00000008292

**FLO-02 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The rigidity of spectral invariants of the displacement energy of Lagrangians: the ratio disp_ratio of the displacement energy e(L) of a Lagrangian submanifold L to the spectral invariant c(L,p). Conjecture: disp_ratio (the spectral-to-displacement ratio) is at least 1 (a spectral lower bound law) with supremum 2 (a double upper bound law); equality 2 (the worst Lagrangian) is realized by the Clifford torus (a Clifford worst law); and the spectrum of disp_ratio (over all Lagrangians) is dense in [1,2] (a dense spectrum law), density realized by Chekanov rotations (a Chekanov family). (displacement spectral Clifford Chekanov density)

**中文。** 定义：Lagrangian 位移能量的谱不变量的刚性：Lagrange 子流形 L 的位移能量 e(L) 与谱不变量 c(L,p) 的比 disp_ratio。猜想：disp_ratio（谱与位移的比）≥ 1（谱下界律）且比的上确界为 2（双倍上界律）；且等号 2 的实现（最坏 Lagrangian）为 Clifford 环面（Clifford 最坏律）；比的谱（一切 Lagrangian 的 disp_ratio 的值）为 [1,2] 的稠密（稠密谱律），稠密的实现由 Chekanov 旋转（Chekanov 族）给出。
