# 00000004304

**English.** Definition: L(1, chi) is the value at 1 of a real character. Conjecture: In families of totally real fields its extreme-value distribution is log-normal, the mean and variance are explicit combinations from the class number formula, and the right-tail exponent is 1 and optimal. (log-normal law of L(1) extremes)

**中文。** 定义：L(1, χ) 指实特征在 1 处的值。猜想：全实域族中其极值分布为对数正态,均值方差由类数公式的显式组合给出,右尾指数为 1 且最优。（L1 极值对数正态律）
