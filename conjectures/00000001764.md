# 00000001764

**RT-14 ★** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: Multiplicity-free restriction means restriction to a subgroup without multiplicities. Conjecture: The classification of remaining stable Gelfand pairs: beyond the known list (O(n),O(n−1) etc.), the last undecided pair (Sp(2n), Sp(2n−2)·T) is multiplicity-free; completing Kobayashi's program.

**中文。** 定义：表示的分枝多重自由指限制到子群时无重数。猜想：剩余的稳定 Gelfand 对分类:除已知列表(O(n),O(n−1) 等),最后一个未决对 (Sp(2n),Sp(2n−2)·T) 为多重自由;Kobayashi 程序的收尾。
