# 00000008786

**English.** Definition: RandBLAS: the formalization of randomized basic linear algebra subprograms. Conjecture: The complete list of RandBLAS primitives has at most 10 elements (a list law); compositions of primitives carry guarantees (a combination law); the guarantees are composable (a composability law); and there is an optimal lower bound for compositions (a lower bound law). (RandBLAS primitive list composable guarantees)

**中文。** 定义：RandBLAS：随机基本线性代数子程序的形式化。猜想：RandBLAS 的原语的完全列表 ≤ 10 的（列表律）；且原语的组合的保证（组合律）；保证的可组合性（可组合律）；组合的最优下界（下界律）。
