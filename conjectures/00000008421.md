# 00000008421

**English.** Definition: Sequence covering strength: the uniformity with which a design covers all s-element subsets. Conjecture: The covering number C(n,k,t) (the minimum number of k-element blocks covering all t-element subsets) satisfies C(n,k,t) = binom(n,t)/binom(k,t)(1 + Theta(log t)), with the correction term for t at least 2 given by the combinatorial deficit of dual covers. (asymptotic correction of covering numbers)

**中文。** 定义：序列覆盖强度指设计覆盖一切 s 元子集的均匀性。猜想：覆盖数 C(n,k,t)（覆盖 t 元子集的最少 k 元区组数）满足 C(n,k,t) = binom(n,t)/binom(k,t)·(1+Θ(log t)) 且修正项在 t ≥ 2 时由对偶覆盖的组合亏给出。（覆盖数渐近修正）
