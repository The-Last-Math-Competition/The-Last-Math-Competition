# 00000004250

**GT2-10 △** — source: 127_数学物理II_规范理论的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The normalized ratio of SW and Donaldson invariants is their ratio after normalization by the Witten conversion. Conjecture: There exists a family of manifolds for which the ratio deviates from the conversion prediction under blow-up, the deviation appears only at b^+ = 1, and its magnitude is uniformly bounded. (blow-up ratio deviation condition)

**中文。** 定义：SW 与 Donaldson 不变量的标准化比值指两者经 Witten 换算归一后的比。猜想：存在流形族使比值在 blow-up 下偏离换算预测,偏离仅在 bc⁺ = 1 出现且幅值被一致有界。（blow-up 比值偏离条件）
