# 00000007770

**English.** Definition: The dynatomic curve X_n is defined by the vanishing of the dynatomic polynomial in parameter-coordinate space. Conjecture: The number of irreducible components v(n) is a multiplicative function; the genus satisfies g(X_n) = 1 + O(n 2^n) with g(X_n)=0 only for n at most 3; and the rational points of X_n for n at least 4 consist exactly of cusp points and parameters with degenerate critical orbits. (dynatomic curve irreducibility and rational points)

**中文。** 定义：dynatomic 曲线 Φ_n(φ,c)=0（φ 为 φ_c(0) 型参数化点）的不可约性：曲线 X_n 定义为 dynatomic 多项式在参数-坐标空间的零点。猜想：X_n 的不可约分支个数 v(n) 为乘性函数；且 X_n 的亏格 g(X_n) 满足 g(X_n)=1+O(n·2^n) 且 g(X_n)=0 仅当 n≤3；X_n 的有理点集在 n≥4 时由 cusp 点与临界轨迹退化参数完全给出。
