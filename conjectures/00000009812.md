# 00000009812

**English.** Definition: Ollivier coarse curvature: graph curvature defined by optimal transport. Conjecture: Ollivier curvature converges under mesh refinement to smooth Ricci curvature; the rate is O(h), and at the critical value 1/2 of the random-walk laziness parameter the curvature sign stabilizes. (Ollivier curvature convergence rate)
