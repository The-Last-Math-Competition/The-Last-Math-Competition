# 00000003013

**English.** Definition: SLOCC classification: equivalence classes under stochastic local operations and classical communication. Conjecture: Minimal representatives of SLOCC classes: the six SLOCC classes of three qubits have a complete list of minimal representatives, while the four-qubit class count is infinite but with parametrized families; the dimension of the families is the Young-diagram freedom of cumulants. (SLOCC class minimal representative system)

**中文。** 定义：SLOCC 分类：随机局域操作与经典通信的等价类。猜想：SLOCC 类的极小代表：三量子比特的六个 SLOCC 类的极小代表为完全列表且四比特的类数为无限但带参数化族；族的维数为累积量的 Young 图自由度。（SLOCC 类极小代表系）
