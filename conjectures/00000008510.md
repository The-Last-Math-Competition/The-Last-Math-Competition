**SDG-10 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: A differential Tannakian category is a rigid tensor differential category with a fiber functor. Conjecture: The group of the differential Tannakian correspondence is a linear algebraic group; and the order of the correspondence agrees with the minimal number of generators of the differential module. (differential Tannakian correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：Tannakian 微分范畴指带纤维函子的刚性张量微分范畴。猜想：微分 Tannakian 对应的群为线性代数群；且对应的阶与微分模的极小生成元数一致。（微分 Tannakian 对应）
