# 00000007771

**ADY-21 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: A p-curvature Grothendieck-Katz-type dynamical criterion: a family of dynamical systems f_t over a number field has vanishing p-curvature at algebraic t. Conjecture: The family is isotrivial (constant after an algebraic parameter change) if and only if the p-curvature vanishes for almost all primes p; and for nonisotrivial families the density of primes with nonzero p-curvature is bounded below by (1/2)/[K(t):K]. (dynamical Katz criterion)

**中文。** 定义：p-曲率与几何有限性（Grothendieck–Katz 型动力版本）：一族定义在数域上的动力系统 f_t 在 t 代数点处的 p-曲率恒消失。猜想：族为 isotrivial（经代数参数变换化为常映射）当且仅当 p-曲率对几乎所有素数 p 恒为零；且非 isotrivial 族的 p-曲率非零素数的密度下界为 1/2·1/[K(t):K]。
