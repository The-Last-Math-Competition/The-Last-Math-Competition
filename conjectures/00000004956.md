**HT4-26 ★** — source: 150_代数拓扑IV_同伦论深化.md

**English.** Definition: Framed manifolds are manifolds with tangential frames, corresponding via Pontryagin-Thom to the stable homotopy groups. Conjecture: The homotopy classification of five-dimensional framed manifolds is given explicitly by a codimension-two subgroup of the kernel of the J homomorphism; the torsion part of the kernel is 2-torsion and the classification table is finite; two manifolds are cobordant if and only if their Stiefel-Whitney numbers agree, and the criterion is complete in codimension two. (codimension classification of the five-dimensional framed kernel)

**中文。** 定义：带框流形：具切向标架的流形；经 Pontryagin–Thom 对应于稳定同伦群。猜想：五维带框流形的同伦分类由 J 同态核的余维二子群显式给出；核的挠部分为 2 挠且分类表为有限；两流形配边当且仅当 Stiefel–Whitney 数相同且该判据在余维二中完备。（五维带框流形核余维分类）
