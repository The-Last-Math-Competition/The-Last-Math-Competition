**English.** Definition: The double obstacle problem constrains the solution between two obstacles. Conjecture: The two-dimensional regularity domain is the explicit nonemptiness condition of the obstacle gap, necessary and sufficient when the contact sets separate, and the domain is open. (nonempty regularity condition of double-obstacle gaps)
