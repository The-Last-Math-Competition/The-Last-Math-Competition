**English.** Definition: Extremal Steklov eigenvalues and free-boundary minimal surfaces: Fraser-Schoen theory connects suprema with extremal surfaces. Conjecture: The maximum of sigma_1(L) times L is realized by free-boundary minimal surfaces, with extremal surfaces classified as a complete list of equatorial disks; the second extremal surface is the critical catenoid, and the spectral gap is an explicit constant. (Steklov extremal classification)
