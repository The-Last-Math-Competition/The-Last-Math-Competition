# 00000005157

**English.** Definition: The transcendence degree is the size of a minimal transcendence basis of a field. Conjecture: The conversion of the transcendence degree of period points with the height is an explicit log-linear expression, degenerating at special points, with coefficients consistent with the transcendence theorems. (log-linear conversion of period transcendence degrees)

**中文。** 定义：超越度指域的极小超越基规模。猜想：周期点的超越度与高度的换算为对数线性显式式,特殊点处退化且系数与超越定理一致。（周期超越度对数线性换算）
