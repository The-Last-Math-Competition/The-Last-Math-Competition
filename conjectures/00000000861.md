**PV-61 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The first eigenfunction u₁ of the p-Laplacian. Conjecture: Convexity of the level sets of u₁ on convex domains holds if and only if p ≥ 2 (a Payne-type convexity threshold; counterexamples are known outside p < 2).

**中文。** 定义：p-Laplace 第一特征函数 u₁。猜想：u₁ 的水平集的凸性在凸域成立当且仅当 p ≥ 2(Payne 型凸性阈值;已知反例 p < 2 之外)。
