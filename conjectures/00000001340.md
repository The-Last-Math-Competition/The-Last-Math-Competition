# 00000001340

**English.** Definition: The Rankin–Selberg L-function L(s, f × ḡ) of two cusp forms. Conjecture: The subconvex-breaking exponent can always be taken as one-eighteenth plus ε; and this exponent is locked by the limits of averaging methods. (Rankin-Selberg subconvex 1/18)

**中文。** 定义：Rankin–Selberg L(s,f×ḡ) 指两尖点形式的乘积 L 函数。猜想：亚凸破除指数恒可取十八分之一加 ε；且该指数由平均方法的极限锁定。（Rankin-Selberg 亚凸 1/18）
