**English.** Definition: The asymptotics of representation dimensions with Littlewood complexity. Conjecture: The saddle-point asymptotics of the hook formula holds, and the asymptotic dimension is the entropy of the partition. (Littlewood-entropy hook-dimension asymptotics)
