**English.** Definition: The additive closure of tensor direct sums is the asymptotic structure of rank. Conjecture: The asymptotic law of direct sums is given by the fusion product and the fusion spectrum is an explicit linear expression; and the law agrees with the Strassen asymptotic rank. (fusion law of direct-sum asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：张量直和的加法闭包为秩的渐近结构。猜想：直和的渐近律由融合积给出且融合谱为显式线性式；且律与 Strassen 渐近秩一致。（直和渐近融合律）
