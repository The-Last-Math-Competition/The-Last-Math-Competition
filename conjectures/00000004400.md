# 00000004400

**English.** Definition: The local tree count is the tree frequency of rooted neighborhoods, and the spectral measure is the adjacency spectral measure. Conjecture: There exist two non-isomorphic bounded-degree graph limits whose tree counts and cycle counts all agree while their spectral measures differ, and the minimal degree of the separation is 3. (tree-cycle count-spectral measure separation)

**中文。** 定义：局部树计数指根邻域树频,谱测度指邻接谱测度。猜想：存在两个不同构的有界度图限其一切树计数与圈计数相同但谱测度不同,分离的最小度为 3。（树圈计数-谱测度分离）
