# 00000006740

**English.** Definition: The finitistic dimension and the tilting-class lattice are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two algebras with the same dimension but different tilting lattices, and the separation is realized by an explicit covering pair with the same dimension but different quivers. (same dimension-different tilting covering separation)
