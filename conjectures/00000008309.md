# 00000008309

**FLO-19 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The sheaf-theoretic wall crossing of the Fukaya category: the degree sheaf_deg of the equivalence between the microlocal sheaf category and the Fukaya category. Conjecture: sheaf_deg (the equivalence of microlocal sheaves with the wrapped Fukaya category) holds for analytic Weinstein sectors (a Nadler-Zaslow type equivalence, an NZ equivalence law); the computability of the equivalence (the algorithm for vanishing cycles of sheaves) has polynomial complexity (a polynomial algorithm law); the sheaf correction across walls is by spherical twists (a spherical twist law); and the coefficients of twist compositions (cross-ratio corrections) are explicit cross ratios (a cross-ratio coefficient law). (microlocal Nadler-Zaslow spherical twist cross-ratio)

**中文。** 定义：Fukaya 范畴的畴壁穿越的史（sheaf-theoretic）：microlocal sheaf 的范畴与 Fukaya 的等价的度 sheaf_deg。猜想：sheaf_deg（microlocal 层与 wrapped Fukaya 的等价）在 Weinstein 的解析（Nadler–Zaslow 型等价）成立（NZ 等价律）；且等价的可计算性（层的 Vanishing cycle 的算法）的复杂度为多项式（多项式算法律）；穿越的墙（wall）的 sheaf 的修正为球面 twists（球面 twist 律）；twist 的复合（cross-ratio 的修正）的系数为 Cross ratio 的显式（Cross ratio 系数律）。
