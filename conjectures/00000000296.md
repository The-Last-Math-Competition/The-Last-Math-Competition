**English.** Definition: The busy beaver function Σ(n) is the maximum number of 1's written by an n-state Turing machine on a blank tape. It is known that Σ(5) = 47,176,870 (2024, bbchallenge). Conjecture: The value of Σ(6) is independent of ZFC (Σ(6) > 10↑↑15 is known; by direct simulation of a 748-state ZFC-independence machine, Σ(n) is independent for all sufficiently large n, and 6 is the smallest candidate).

**中文。** 定义：忙海狸函数 Σ(n)(n 态图灵机在空白带上最多写下的 1 的个数)。已知 Σ(5) = 47,176,870(2024, bbchallenge)。猜想：Σ(6) 的值独立于 ZFC(已知 Σ(6) > 10↑↑15;由 748 态 ZFC 独立性机的直接模拟,存在充分大的 n 使 Σ(n) 独立,6 为最小候选)。
