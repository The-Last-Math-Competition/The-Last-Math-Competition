# 00000008082

**FUS-02 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The homotopy dimension dim_h(L) of the nerve (classifying space) of a centric linking system L. Conjecture: For every saturated fusion system there is a unique (up to homotopy) centric linking system (existence and uniqueness, the partially proven Oliver correction); the p-completed homotopy groups of |L| are finitely generated in their p-parts; and the torsion part of the Whitehead group of the p-completion vanishes (a Whitehead vanishing law), with a quantitative upper bound on the vanishing that is linear in the rank of S. (centric linking Whitehead vanishing linearity)

**中文。** 定义：centric linking 系统 L 的 nerve（分类空间）的同伦维数 dim_h(L)。猜想：对每个饱和融合系统存在唯一（至同伦）的 centric linking 系统（存在唯一性，已部分证明的 Oliver 修正）；且 |L| 的 p-完备化的同伦群的 p-部分有限生成；同伦白headhead 群（p-完备化的 Whitehead 群）的 torsion 部分消失（Whitehead 消没律），消没的定量上界为 S 的秩的线性。
