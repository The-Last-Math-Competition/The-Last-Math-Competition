# 00000009825

**English.** Definition: Matrix weights: dimension dependence of weighted inequalities for matrix-valued Muckenhoupt W in A_p. Conjecture: The constant of the principal singular value inequality in the matrix A_2 case is an explicit power d^{1/2} of the dimension d; the power is optimal, attained by the matrix harmonic measure construction of random orthogonal weight families. (matrix A2 dimension power)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵权：矩阵值 Muckenhoupt W∈A_p 的加权不等式的维度依赖。猜想：矩阵 A_2 情形的主奇异值不等式常数为维数 d 的显式幂 d^{1/2}；幂最优且由随机正交权族的矩阵调和测度构造达到。（矩阵 A2 维数幂）
