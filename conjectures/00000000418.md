# 00000000418

**English.** Definition: A Yamanouchi (lattice) word of weight λ is a word in which, in every prefix, the occurrence counts of each letter form a partition; a lattice word is one appearing as the reading word of a standard tableau. Conjecture: The proportion of lattice words of weight λ among Yamanouchi words is exactly f^λ/n! · K^{-1}_{λ,λ}, and this ratio attains its maximum 1/2^{n-1} when λ is a rectangle.

**中文。** 定义：权 λ 的 Yamanouchi(格子)词指每个前缀中字母 i 的出现次数构成分拆的词;格子词指出现在某标准表读词中的词。猜想：权 λ 的格子词在 Yamanouchi 词中的比例恰为 f^λ/n!·K^{-1}_{λ,λ},且该比值在 λ 取方框形状时达到最大值 1/2^{n-1}。
