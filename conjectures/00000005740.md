# 00000005740

**English.** Definition: The tangent cone and the stratification structure are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two free boundaries with identical tangent cones but different stratification structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit family with the same cones but different frequency paths. (same cone-different stratification frequency-path separation)

**中文。** 定义：切锥与分层结构指两层。猜想：存在两自由边界的一切切锥相同而分层结构不同,分离由同锥异频率路径的显式族实现。（切锥同-分层异频率路径分离）
