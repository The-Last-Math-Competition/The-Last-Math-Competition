# 00000007212

**English.** Definition: The prime geodesic theorem with the counting of prime closed geodesics. Conjecture: The asymptotic of the counting is the main term plus oscillation, and the amplitude of the oscillation is the zero contribution. (zero-contribution prime-geodesic main term)

**中文。** 定义：素测地线定理：素闭测地线的计数。猜想：素测地线的主项：计数的渐近为主项加振荡且荡的振幅为零点的贡献。（素测地线零点贡献主项振荡渐近计数）
