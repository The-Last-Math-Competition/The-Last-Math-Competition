**HT2-26 ★** — source: 126_组合代数拓扑_高阶树.md

**English.** Definition: An optimal 1-cycle is the nontrivial closed chain of minimal weight. Conjecture: Its weight equals the minimum residual edge weight after cutting away the shortest-path tree, the identity holds strictly for nonnegative weights, and it can be solved by linear programming. (optimal cycle-shortest path tree identity)

**中文。** 定义：最优 1-循环指权最小的非平凡闭链。猜想：其权等于割去最短路树后的最小剩余边权,关系在非负权下严格成立且可线性规划求解。（最优循环-最短路树恒等）
