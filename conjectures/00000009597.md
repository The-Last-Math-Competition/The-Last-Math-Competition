# 00000009597

**English.** Definition: The Weyl asymptotic main term of the p-width spectrum is known; the explicit form of the secondary term is the quantitative frontier of min-max theory. Conjecture: The secondary term of the p-width is negative and explicitly given by a first-order curvature integral of the second fundamental form of the realizing minimal hypersurfaces; the secondary term is strictly negative for general metrics, degenerating to zero at symmetric metrics. (p-width secondary term)

**中文。** 定义：p-宽度谱的 Weyl 渐近主项已知；次主项的显式形式是 min-max 理论的定量前沿。猜想：p-宽度的次主项为负且由实现极小超曲面第二基本形的一次曲率积分显式给出；次主项在一般度量下严格负，对称度量处退化为零。（p-宽度次主项）
