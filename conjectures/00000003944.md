# 00000003944

**English.** Definition: A Kempe switch is the local recoloring operation flipping a two-colored chain. Conjecture: For Delta >= 5 the mixing time of random Kempe switches is n^{O(Delta)}, whereas for Delta <= 4 there exist colorings whose switch graph never mixes. (Delta threshold for Kempe mixing)

**中文。** 定义：Kempe 切换指翻转双色链的着色局部操作。猜想：Δ ≥ 5 时随机 Kempe 切换的混合时间为 n^{O(Δ)};而 Δ ≤ 4 时存在切换图永不混合的着色。（Kempe 混合的 Δ 阈值）
