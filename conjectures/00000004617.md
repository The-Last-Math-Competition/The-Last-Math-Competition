**English.** Definition: Hardt-Simon local uniqueness is the rigidity of a minimal flow in a neighborhood of the tangent cone. Conjecture: In the boundary case the boundary condition is the constant-mean-curvature condition on the cross-section of the tangent cone, and this condition is sufficient and necessary. (Hardt-Simon boundary CMC necessity and sufficiency)

**中文。** 定义：Hardt–Simon 局部唯一性指极小流在切锥邻域的刚性。猜想：带边情形的边界条件为切锥截面的常平均曲率条件,该条件充分且必要。（Hardt–Simon 边界 CMC 充要）
