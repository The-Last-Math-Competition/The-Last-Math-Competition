# 00000000613

**English.** Definition: The spectral sequence of Khovanov homology converges from Kh to Lee homology page by page; Bar-Natan's knight-move conjecture asserts that successive differentials move in directions (1,2) or (2,−1). Conjecture: The kernel of every nonzero differential in the knight-move conjecture has dimension a power of two, and the exponent equals the number of Reidemeister moves associated with the corresponding knight move.

**中文。** 定义：Khovanov 同调的谱序列从 Kh 到 Lee 同调逐页收敛,Barnatan 的骑士移动猜想断言相继微分沿 (1,2) 或 (2,−1) 方向移动。猜想：骑士移动猜想的每个非零微分的核的维数为 2 的幂,且该幂指数等于相应骑士移动所联系的 Reidemeister 移动数。
