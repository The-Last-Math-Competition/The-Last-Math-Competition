**QIC-30 △** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: Self-testing uniquely determines (up to local equivalence) the underlying state and measurements from black-box statistics; maximal CHSH violation self-tests the Bell state and Pauli measurements. Conjecture: The complete classification of self-testable statistics in the two-body two-outcome two-measurement scenario is a finite list with exactly 9 nonlocal vertices; and in the three-body scenario, the statistics achieving device-independent key rate of 1 bit per round are unique up to local equivalence, namely GHZ perfect-correlation type. (self-testing finite classification)
