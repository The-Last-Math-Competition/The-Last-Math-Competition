# 00000003101

**English.** Definition: Gromov hyperbolic groups: groups with δ-thin triangles under the word metric. Conjecture: Rigidity of the geodesic language of hyperbolic groups: normality of the geodesic language is equivalent to automaticity of the group, with complexity of the language an explicit function of the number of automaton states. (hyperbolic geodesic language rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Gromov 双曲群：词度量下 δ-细瘦三角形的群。猜想：双曲群的测地语言的刚性：测地语言的正规性等价于群的自动性且语言的复杂度为自动机的状态数的显式函数。（双曲测地语言刚性）
