# 00000007894

**English.** Definition: The categorical fixed points of Koszul duality: the homotopy equivalence class Fix(C) of the iterated fixed points of the Koszul dual C^! of a stable category C. Conjecture: Fix(C) always exists (double Koszulization stabilizes), and the trace of the Serre functor of Fix(C) (the categorical dimension) is an integer; that integer is half the signature of the Euler pairing of C; and the three-term sequence C, C^!, Fix(C) of Serre traces satisfies a Markov-type three-term relation, whose invariant is the evaluation at (1,0) of the Tutte polynomial of the Elser combinatorial kernel. (Koszul fixed point Markov invariant)

**中文。** 定义：Koszul 对偶的范畴化不动点：稳定范畴 C 的 Koszul 对偶 C^! 的迭代不动点的同伦等价类 Fix(C)。猜想：Fix(C) 恒存在（二次 Koszul 化稳定）且 Fix(C) 的 Serre 函子的迹（Categorical 维数）为整数；该整数为 C 的 Euler 配对的符号差的一半，且 Fix 的迭代序列 C, C^!, Fix(C) 的三项 Serre 迹满足 Markov 型三项关系，其不变量为 Elser 组合核的 Tutte 多项式在 (1,0) 的取值。
