# 00000003930

**FL-20 △** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: Relative Gromov-Witten invariants count the holomorphic disks emanating from a Lagrangian. Conjecture: There exist two Lagrangians whose complete relative invariants coincide while their HF(L, L) differ, and the minimal dimension for such a separation is 4. (minimal dimension of GW-HF separation)

**中文。** 定义：相对 Gromov–Witten 不变量指从 Lagrange 出发的全纯盘计数。猜想：存在两条 Lagrange 的全部相对不变量相同而 HF(L,L) 相异,最小维数为 4。（GW-HF 分离最小维数）
