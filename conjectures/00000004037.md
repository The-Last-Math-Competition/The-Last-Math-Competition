# 00000004037

**English.** Definition: The Coxeter transformation is the negative transpose of the Cartan matrix times its inverse. Conjecture: Its spectral radius equals 1 if and only if the quiver is of Dynkin or extended Dynkin type; when the spectral radius exceeds 1 it is always an algebraic integer, with the criterion given by the principal minors of the Cartan matrix. (Coxeter spectral radius criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Coxeter 变换指 Cartan 矩阵负转置乘其逆。猜想：其谱半径为 1 当且仅当箭图为 Dynkin 或扩展 Dynkin 型;谱半径大于 1 时恒为代数整数,判据由 Cartan 主子式给出。（Coxeter 谱半径判据）
