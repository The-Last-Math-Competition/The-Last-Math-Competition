# 00000000974

**OA-74 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The distance condition d(p,q) < 1 implies similarity of idempotents. Conjecture: The supremum of the similarity norm of a pair of idempotents is at most √3 times the inverse of d(p,q); and the constant √3 is optimal, attained by two-dimensional idempotent pairs. (idempotent similarity norm bound)

**中文。** 定义：幂等元的距离条件 d(p,q)<1 蕴含相似。猜想：幂等元对的相似范数的上确界不超过 √3 乘 d(p,q) 的逆；且常数 √3 为最优并由二维幂等对达到。（幂等相似范数界）
