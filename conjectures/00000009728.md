# 00000009728

**SCA-28 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Critically time-damped wave equations: the dichotomy at critical scaling of damping coefficient t^{-1}. Conjecture: The global dynamics of critically damped waves obeys a dichotomy: solutions with bounded effective total energy scatter to free waves with corrected phase; the phase correction is an explicit logarithmic integral of type t^{-1}, vanishing at noncritical exponents. (critical damping dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：临界时变阻尼波方程：阻尼系数 t^{−1} 的临界标度下的二分律。猜想：临界阻尼波的整体动力学二分：有效总能量有界的解散射到带修正相位的自由波；相位修正为 t^{−1} 型显式对数积分且非临界指标下修正消失。（临界阻尼二分律）
