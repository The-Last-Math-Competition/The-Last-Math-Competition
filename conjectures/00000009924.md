# 00000009924

**English.** Definition: Persistent Laplacians: the spectrum of Hodge Laplacians varying with the filtration parameter. Conjecture: The eigenvalues of persistent Laplacians are Lipschitz in the filtration parameter, with constant the maximal degree of the simplex insertion sequence; the derivative of an eigenvalue is an explicit combinatorial quantity of boundary pairings of new simplices. (persistent Laplacian spectral Lipschitz)

**中文。** 定义：持久 Laplacian：随过滤参数变化的 Hodge Laplacian 谱。猜想：持久 Laplacian 的特征值关于过滤参数为 Lipschitz 且常数为单形插入序列的度数最大值；特征值的导数为新单形的边界配对的显式组合量。（持久 Laplacian 谱 Lipschitz）
