# 00000009544

**English.** Definition: The zero-error classical capacity C_0 is given by regularizing the independence number alpha(phi^{tensor n}); the quantum version regularizes the quantum independence number alpha_q (the maximal number of distinguishable output states). Conjecture: The zero-error quantum capacity can strictly exceed the corresponding classical value, with the minimal gain channel the dimension-3 non-complete graph channel; and at fixed dimension d the maximal gain ratio C_0^q/C_0^c is the floor of (d-1) over 2, coinciding with the Kochen-Specker minimal dimension 3. (zero-error quantum gain)

**中文。** 定义：零误差经典容量 C_0 由独立数 α(φ^{⊗n}) 的正则化给出；量子版由量子独立数 α_q（最大可区分输出态数）正则化定义。猜想：零误差量子容量可严格大于对应经典值，最小增益信道为维数 3 非完全图信道；固定维数 d 时增益比 C_0^q/C_0^c 的最大值为 d−1 的整数除 2 下取整，且与 Kochen–Specker 集最小维数 3 重合。（零误差量子增益）
