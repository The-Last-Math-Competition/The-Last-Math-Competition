# 00000007754

**ADY-04 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The arboreal representation rho_f: G_Q to Aut(T) of a polynomial f in Q[x] comes from its iterated inverse tree, with the index [Aut(T):rho_f(G_Q)] measuring the deficit. Conjecture: For all quadratic polynomials with square leading coefficient, nonzero discriminant, and no quadratic factor, the index is finite and at most 8*2^{ord(-b)}; and the index is unbounded exactly for families with degenerate critical orbits (critical point preperiodic or escaping to infinity), giving a complete combinatorial criterion for arboreal finiteness. (arboreal index criterion)

**中文。** 定义：多项式 f∈Q[x] 的 arboreal 表示 ρ_f: G_Q→Aut(T) 由 f 的迭代逆树作用给出，其像的指标 [Aut(T):ρ_f(G_Q)] 度量树表示的亏损。猜想：对一切首项系数为平方数、判别式非零且无二次因子的二次多项式 f，指标有限且 ≤ 8·2^{ord(−b)}；且指标无界的二次多项式族恰为临界轨道退化的情形（即 critical point 预周期或逃逸到 ∞ 的例外参数族的像），该刻画给出 arboreal 有限性的组合完全判据。
