# 00000008834

**English.** Definition: Viscosity approximation: the smoothing of monotone inclusions. Conjecture: Viscosity solution trajectories always converge to the zero set; the convergence rate is the square root of the error function with coefficient given by the strong monotonicity modulus. (viscosity trajectory root error rate)

**中文。** 定义：粘滞逼近为单调包含的平滑化。猜想：粘滞解的轨道恒收敛到零集；收敛速率为误差函数的平方根且系数由强单调模给出。（粘滞轨道误差根速率）
