# 00000005329

**English.** Definition: The free genus is the categorified genus obstruction quantity. Conjecture: Its conversion is the explicit half-difference of slice invariants, verified at unions of compact surfaces, and the conversion is effective in both directions. (bidirectional half-difference conversion of free genera)

**中文。** 定义：自由亏格指范畴化的亏格障碍量。猜想：其换算为切片不变量的显式半差,紧曲面并处验证且换算双向有效。（自由亏格半差双向换算）
