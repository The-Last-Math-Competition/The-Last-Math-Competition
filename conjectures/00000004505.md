# 00000004505

**English.** Definition: The constructive characterization of local compactness is local compactness without the axiom of choice. Conjecture: Its consistency with the classical notion is controlled by the finiteness threshold of compact generators, and the threshold 1 is optimal and verified by the family of one-point spaces. (compact generator threshold 1 of local compactness)

**中文。** 定义：局部紧的构造性刻画指无选择公理情形的局部紧性。猜想：其与经典重合的一致性由紧生成元的有限性阈值控制,阈值 1 最优且由单点空间族验证。（局部紧紧生成阈值 1）
