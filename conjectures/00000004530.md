**SO2-20 △** — source: 136_随机组合优化.md

**English.** Definition: The fixed-point set and the iteration trajectory of message passing. Conjecture: There exist two algorithms with the same fixed-point set but different convergence trajectories, and the separation is constructed by an explicit frequency-sweep family of damping parameters. (fixed-point set-trajectory damping separation)

**中文。** 定义：消息传递的不动点集与迭代轨迹。猜想：存在两个算法的不动点集相同而收敛轨迹不同,分离由阻尼参数的显式扫频族构造。（不动点集-轨迹阻尼分离）
