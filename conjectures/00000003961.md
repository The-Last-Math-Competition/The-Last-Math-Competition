# 00000003961

**English.** Definition: The Johnson graph J(n, k) has k-element subsets as vertices, two vertices adjacent exactly when their symmetric difference has size 2. Conjecture: For all k <= n/2, the lazy random walk on J(n, k) exhibits total variation cutoff, with cutoff time an explicit multiple of (1/2) log C(n, k) times the inverse spectral gap. (Johnson cutoff time formula)

**中文。** 定义：Johnson 图 J(n,k) 指 k 元子集为顶点、两顶点相邻当且仅当对称差恰为 2 的图。猜想：对一切 k ≤ n/2,J(n,k) 上的懒惰随机游走存在总变差截断,截断时刻为谱隙倒数的 (1/2)log C(n,k) 的显式倍数。（Johnson 截断时刻公式）
