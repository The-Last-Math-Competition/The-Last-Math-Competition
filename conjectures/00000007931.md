# 00000007931

**INT-01 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: Integrality of the Schur expansion coefficients (Plucker coordinates) of the tau function of the KP equation at rational initial data: tau(t) = sum_lambda c_lambda s_lambda(t). Conjecture: The coefficient vector (c_lambda), propagated by the recursion of the Hirota bilinear equations, is always rational at rational initial data; and the support of the Schur expansion of the normalized tau function (the set of lambda with nonzero c_lambda) forms an ideal of the Young lattice whose boundary function is piecewise constant with jump count equal to the soliton number. (KP Plucker integrality and Young ideals)

**中文。** 定义：KP 方程的 τ 函数的 Schur 展开系数（Plücker 坐标）在初值为有理点时的整性：τ(t)=Σ_λ c_λ s_λ(t)。猜想：系数向量 (c_λ) 经 Hirota 双线性方程的递推在有理初值下恒为有数；且经规范化的 τ 的 Schur 展开的支撑（非零 c_λ 的 λ 集）构成 Young 格的理想，理想的边界函数为分片常数且跳变次数等于孤子数。
