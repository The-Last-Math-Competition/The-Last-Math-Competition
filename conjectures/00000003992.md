# 00000003992

**PC2-22 ◆** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: The Tseitin formula is the contradictory system labeling the vertices of a connected graph with odd charges. Conjecture: Its resolution width equals exactly the number of vertices of the smallest odd-charge subgraph of the graph, the conversion requiring no additive constant term. (exact Tseitin width-odd subgraph)

**中文。** 定义：Tseitin 公式指连通图上奇电荷顶点标的矛盾系统。猜想：其消解宽度恰等于图的最小奇电荷子图顶点数,换算无需任何加性常数项。（Tseitin 宽度-奇子图精确）
