# 00000001320

**English.** Definition: A hyperbolicity criterion for the complement Cⁿ ∖ E of a set E of codimension ≥ 2. Conjecture: If E contains a union of 2n−2 complex hyperplanes whose normal directions form a direction set of positive real measure in Cⁿ, then Cⁿ ∖ E is complete hyperbolic; the sufficient condition fails as the spherical measure of the direction set drops to 0.

**中文。** 定义：ℂⁿ 中余codimension ≥ 2 的集合 E 的补集 ℂⁿ∖E 的双曲性判据。猜想：若 E 包含一族 2n−2 个复超平面的并,使其法方向构成 ℂⁿ 的实测度 > 0 的方向集,则 ℂⁿ∖E 为完全双曲;该充分条件在方向集的球面测度下降为 0 时失效。
