# 00000004652

**English.** Definition: Umbral moonshine is the correspondence between the mock modular form coefficients of rank-24 even rootless lattices and Niemeier discriminants. Conjecture: The second moment of the nonzero coefficients is an explicit Gamma-functional asymptotic with leading coefficient 1/pi; the consistency of the coefficients with the product of Niemeier discriminants holds for all rootless lattices; and the density of zero coefficients converges to zero as the weight grows, with logarithmic decay. (second moment of umbral moonshine and discriminant products)

**中文。** 定义：影月射：秩 24 偶无根格的 mock 模形式系数与 Niemeier 判别式的对应。猜想：非零系数的二阶矩为 Gamma 泛函的显式渐近且主项系数为 1/π；系数与 Niemeier 判别式积的一致性在全部无根格上成立；零系数的密度随权增长收敛于零且衰减为对数型。（影月射二阶矩判别式积）
