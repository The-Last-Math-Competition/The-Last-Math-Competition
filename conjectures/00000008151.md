# 00000008151

**English.** Definition: The renormalization of nonunitary CFT at the Yang-Lee edge singularity: the stability index nu_stab of nonunitary fixed points (c < 0). Conjecture: The list of marginal (critically stable) nonunitary fixed points of nu_stab is of Yang-Lee type (c = -22/5) together with its minimal model cascade (the cascade formula c = -22/5 (m-2)/(m+1)); the stability along the cascade alternates (a stable-unstable alternation law) with period 2; and the most negative conformal dimension of a nonunitary fixed point is Delta_min = -1/5 (a Yang-Lee most-negative dimension rigidity). (Yang-Lee cascade alternation minus one-fifth)

**中文。** 定义：Yang–Lee 边缘奇点的非幺正 CFT 的重整化：非幺正不动点 (c<0) 的稳定性指标 nu_stab。猜想：nu_stab 的边缘（临界稳定）的非幺正不动点的列表为 Yang–Lee 型（c=-22/5）与它的极小模型级联（级联公式 c=-22/5·(m-2)/(m+1)）；且级联的稳定性的交替（稳定-不稳定交替律）的周期为 2；非幺正不动点的共形维数的最负值 Δ_min = -1/5（Yang–Lee 最负维数刚性）。
