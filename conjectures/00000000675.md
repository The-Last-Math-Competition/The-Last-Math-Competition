# 00000000675

**English.** Definition: The second critical point of the Möbius energy is the next energy-minimizing knot type after the circle. Conjecture: This second critical point is the two-component link (the closure of the Hopf link type), with explicit energy value 2π² + 4; the infimum of the Möbius energy over all nontrivial knot types is at least this value.

**中文。** 定义：Möbius 能量的第二临界点指除圆外的下一能量极小纽结型。猜想：该第二临界点为双环结(hopf link 的闭包型),其能量值为 2π²+4 的显式组合;一切非平凡纽结型的 Möbius 能量下确界 ≥ 该值。
