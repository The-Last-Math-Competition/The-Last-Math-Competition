**English.** Definition: Spectral freedom from spurious modes is the criterion for spectral schemes. Conjecture: The combined criterion is necessary and sufficient and the complete verification is kernel orthogonality; the verification of kernel orthogonality is the vanishing of boundary degrees of freedom; and the zero frequency of spurious modes is unique. (complete necessary-sufficient no-spurious criterion)
