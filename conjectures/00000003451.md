**NA2-21 ★** — source: 100_赋范空间与逼近论II.md

**English.** Definition: Approximation numbers of operators: the Banach-space version. Conjecture: The multiplicativity of approximation numbers: the approximation numbers of composite operators are the product correction of the two factors, with the correction the commutator difference norm. (approximation number composition commutator difference correction)

**中文。** 定义：算子的逼近数：Banach 空间版本。猜想：逼近数的乘性：复合算子的逼近数为两因子的乘积修正且修正为可交换的差范数。（逼近数复合交换差修正）
