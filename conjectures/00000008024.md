# 00000008024

**OMN-04 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The counting gap of the Wilkie conjecture: the optimal exponent g(X) of the rational point count N(X,H) of images of exponential polynomial maps (the known bound N <= H^{(n+1)/2+epsilon}). Conjecture: The count of zeros (height at most H) of one-variable exponential polynomials is O(log H) (the one-dimensional Wilkie conjecture); and the optimal higher-dimensional exponent is an integralization of n/2 (the Wilkie bound (n+1)/2 can drop by half an order); the half-order drop is obstructed by the Marmon-type density of the semialgebraic exceptional subvariety (Marmon counting on the exceptional set), the density constant being an explicit integral of the real root density of Sturm sequences. (Wilkie half-order exponential counting gap)

**中文。** 定义：Wilkie 猜想的计数间隙：指数多项式映像集 X 的有理点计数 N(X,H) 的最优指数 g(X)（已知 N≤H^{(n+1)/2+ε}）。猜想：单变量指数多项式的零点的计数（高度 ≤H）为 O(log H)（Wilkie 猜想一维版）；且高维最优指数为 n/2 的整化（(n+1)/2 的 Wilkie 界可降半次）；降半次的障碍由半代数例外簇的 Marmon 类密度（例外簇上的 Marmon 计数）决定，密度常数为 Sturm 序列的实根密度的显式积分。
