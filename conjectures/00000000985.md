**OA-85 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: A SIC-POVM is a complete quantum measurement made of d² equiangular vectors, with Weyl–Heisenberg orbits as candidates. Conjecture: A Weyl–Heisenberg SIC exists in d = 43, its phase parameters being algebraic numbers in the explicit number field Q(√(43·(11+2√29))); the WH-SIC phases of all prime dimensions below 50 lie in quadratic subfields of cyclotomic extensions.

**中文。** 定义：SIC-POVM 指由 d² 个等角向量构成的完备量子测量,Weyl–Heisenberg 轨迹为候选。猜想：Weyl-Heisenberg SIC 在 d=43 存在,其相位参数为满足显式数域 Q(√(43·(11+2√29))) 中的代数数;一切素维数 d<50 的 WH-SIC 相位均落在分圆扩张的二次子域中。
