# 00000004517

**English.** Definition: The connectivity of the k-SAT solution space is the connectivity of the solution graph, and the satisfiability threshold is the critical density. Conjecture: The deviation between the connectivity threshold and the satisfiability threshold has the explicit asymptotic form log 2 divided by k, with exact leading coefficient. (asymptotic deviation of the SAT connectivity threshold)

**中文。** 定义：k-SAT 的解空间连通指解图的连通性,可满足阈值指密度临界值。猜想：连通阈值与可满足阈值的偏离为 log2 除以 k 型显式渐近,首项系数精确。（SAT 连通阈值偏离渐近）
