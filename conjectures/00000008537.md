**English.** Definition: A modular lattice is a lattice satisfying the modular law. Conjecture: A finite modular lattice embeds into a subspace lattice with linear dimension at most |L|; and the minimal embeddings are of projective geometry type. (linear embedding dimension of modular lattices)
