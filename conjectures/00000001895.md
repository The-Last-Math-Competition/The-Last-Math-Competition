# 00000001895

**English.** Definition: Shelah's Strong Hypothesis (SSH) asserts pp(κ) = κ⁺ for all singular cardinals κ. Conjecture: The realizable values of the jump points of the pp function are controlled by explicit conditions on the pcf spectrum: pp(κ) = κ^{++} implies κ is of ℵ_ω-type limit (a spectral constraint on pp jumps).

**中文。** 定义：Shelah 强假设(SSH)断言对一切奇异基数 κ 有 pp(κ)=κ⁺。猜想：pp 函数的跳跃点的可实现值由 pcf 谱的显式条件控制:pp(κ)=κ^{++} 蕴含 κ 为 ℵ_ω 型极限(pp 跳跃的谱约束)。
