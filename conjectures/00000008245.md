# 00000008245

**EGT-15 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The implicit payments of algorithmic games: the feedback payoff_fb of differential games via Taylor expansion. Conjecture: payoff_fb (the explicit feedback payment) is a Riccati correction of a quadratic form in the state (a Riccati correction law); the bound on the difference between open-loop and closed-loop equilibria of differential games is the square of the time window (a square window difference bound); and the time threshold for existence of equilibria of stochastic differential games (noisy games) is hyperbolic in the reciprocal of the noise strength sigma (a hyperbolic threshold law). (differential game Riccati feedback square window)

**中文。** 定义：算法博弈的隐式付款的计算：Taylor 展开的微分博弈的支付的反馈 payoff_fb。猜想：payoff_fb（反馈支付的显式化）为状态的二次型的 Riccati 修正（Riccati 修正律）；且微分博弈的开环与闭环的均衡的差的界为时间窗的平方（平方窗差界）；随机微分博弈（噪声博弈）的均衡的存在的时刻阈为噪声强度 σ 的倒数的双曲（双曲阈律）。
