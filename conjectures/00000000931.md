**English.** Definition: Almost-normal matrices (n×n with ‖T*T − TT*‖ ≤ ε). Conjecture: The matching of the spectrum of an ε-normal matrix with a normal spectrum in Hausdorff distance ≤ √ε is optimal (a sharpening of Lin's theorem).
