**English.** Definition: The distribution of generators of class groups via Hafner-McCurley: the quality hmc_q(n) of LLL outputs for the distribution of bases of random relation lattices. Conjecture: The expectation of hmc_q (the length ratio of the LLL output basis) is 1.02^n (n/2 pi e)^{n/4} (the HMC formula); the verification of the expectation (deviation of simulation from theory) is below 3% (a three-percent tightness); and the total complexity of class group computation (the HMC algorithm) is L_D[1/2, sqrt 2] (a root-two constant law), the tightening path for the constant being repeated sampling of relations (a repeated-sampling tightening). (Hafner-McCurley LLL root-two tightness)

**中文。** 定义：Hafner–McCurley 的类群的生成元分布：随机关系格的基的分布的 LLL 的输出的质量 hmc_q(n)。猜想：hmc_q（LLL 的输出的基的长度比）的期望为 1.02^{n}·(n/2πe)^{n/4}（HMC 公式）；且期望的验证（模拟与理论的偏差）< 3%（三百分比紧性）；类群的计算的全复杂度（HMC 算法）为 L_D[1/2,√2]（根二常数律），常数的紧化路径为关系的重复采样（重复采样紧化）。
