# 00000007067

**English.** Definition: Deformations of the Fukaya category with the Novikov constant. Conjecture: The deformation classification is the class of first cohomology, and the symplectic side of the class is the globalization of closed two-forms. (closed-form first-cohomology deformation classification)

**中文。** 定义：Fukaya 范畴的形变：非正式常数。猜想：形变的平移：范畴的形变分类为一阶上同调的类且类的辛侧为闭二形式的整体化。（Fukaya 一阶上同调闭形式形变平移分类）
