# 00000006682

**OT3-12 ★** — source: 208_最优传输II_离散.md

**English.** Definition: Semidiscrete with the cell-matching structure of measures. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the gradient structure and the cell-measure matching equation is unique; the solution of the equation is the weight vector, with strict convexity; and the Jacobian of the gradient is the cell volume. (gradient-equation conversion of cell measures)

**中文。** 定义：半离散：测度的胞腔匹配结构。猜想：换算常数为梯度结构的显式且胞腔测度匹配的方程唯一；方程的解为权向量且解的凸性严格；梯度的雅比为胞腔体积。（胞腔测度梯度方程换算）
