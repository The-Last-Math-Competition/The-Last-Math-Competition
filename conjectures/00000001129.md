# 00000001129

**LT-29 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The Richardson variety X_w^v = X_w ∩ X^v is the intersection of two Schubert varieties. Conjecture: For all v ≤ w, X_w^v has rational singularities; equivalently, the higher local cohomology of its structure sheaf vanishes, and the restriction maps on the irreducible components of a Bott–Samelson resolution all split.

**中文。** 定义：Richardson 簇 X_w^v = X_w ∩ X^v(两 Schubert 簇的交)。猜想：对一切 v≤w,X_w^v 具有 rational 奇点;等价地,其结构层的高阶局部上同调消没,且该性质在 Bott–Samelson 剖分的不可约分支上的限制映射均为分裂。
