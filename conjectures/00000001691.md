**English.** Definition: Host–Kra characteristic factors capture multiple ergodic averages (nilsystems). Conjecture: The characteristic factor of the prime-weighted average E_{n<N}Λ(n)f₁(n)f₂(2n) is a hierarchy of prime Weyl systems: the order-1 component is the linear phase e(βn), and components of order ≥ 2 vanish; the hierarchy is listed explicitly via the Green–Tao reduction to nilsequences.
