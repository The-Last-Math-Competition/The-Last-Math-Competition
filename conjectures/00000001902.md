# 00000001902

**English.** Definition: The local smoothing conjecture concerns the time gain of L^p norms of wave-equation solutions. Conjecture: It holds in all dimensions; and the local smoothing exponent for cone-degenerate surfaces is always one half minus ε, with ε non-improvable. (local smoothing cone-degenerate exponent)

**中文。** 定义：局部平滑猜想指波方程解的 L^p 范数的时间增益。猜想：一般维数成立；且锥型退化曲面的局部平滑指数恒为二分之一减 ε 而 ε 不可改进。（局部平滑锥退化指数）
