**English.** Definition: The mobility edge is the energy boundary between localization and extension. Conjecture: The attainability criterion of a positive-measure mobility edge in three dimensions is an explicit threshold of the disorder strength, given by the bifurcation equation of self-consistent theory and sharp on both sides. (positive-measure attainability threshold of the mobility edge)

**中文。** 定义：迁移边指局域与扩展的能量分界。猜想：三维正测度迁移边的可达判据为无序强度的显式阈值,由自洽理论的分叉方程给出且两侧尖锐。（迁移边正测度可达阈值）
