# 00000004338

**English.** Definition: The Gaussian free field is the generalized random field whose covariance is the Green operator. Conjecture: For the infinite-volume GFF, conditional independence on a finite domain is explicitly equivalent to the harmonic extension kernel of that domain, and the equivalence requires an additional zero-boundary energy condition on unbounded domains. (harmonic characterization of conditional domains of the infinite GFF)
