# 00000005505

**QN2-05 ★** — source: 169_排队网络的图极限.md

**English.** Definition: Balance recursion is the layer-by-layer summation computation, with its termination property. Conjecture: The termination threshold is an explicit linear expression of the load parameters, below which the recursion is stable; the threshold is the phase transition point of the queueing network, at which the recursion period doubles; and for two stations the threshold is the critical value 1 of the sum of the two loads. (linear termination threshold of balance recursion)

**中文。** 定义：平衡递推：逐层求和的计算过程；其终止性。猜想：终止阈值为负荷参数的显式线性式且阈值下递推稳定；阈值为排队网络的相变点且点处递推周期翻倍；两站情形的阈值为两负荷之和的临界值 1。（平衡递推线性终止阈值）
