**English.** Definition: A chain product lattice is the Cartesian product of total order chains. Conjecture: The Sperner number of a chain product lattice is the maximum multinomial coefficient; and the maximal antichain is unique, namely the middle layer. (uniqueness of chain product Sperner)

**中文。** 定义：链积格为全序链的笛卡尔积。猜想：链积格的 Sperner 数为多项式系数的最大值；且反链的最大取法唯一为中间层。（链积 Sperner 唯一）
