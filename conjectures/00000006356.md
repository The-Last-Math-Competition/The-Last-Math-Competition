# 00000006356

**QG2-16 ★** — source: 197_量子群II.md

**English.** Definition: The quasitriangular weight-lattice structure of the multivalued associator constant. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the series coefficients and the weight lattice of the multivalued constant closes; the coefficient is one; and the kernel of the closure is the braid relation, the compatibility constant of the relation being a root of unity. (weight-lattice coefficient conversion of associators)

**中文。** 定义：拟三角：结合子多值常数的权格结构。猜想：换算常数为级数系数的显式且多值常数的权格封闭；系数为一；封闭的核为辫关系且关系的相容常数为单位根。（结合子多值权格系数换算）
