# 00000002813

**English.** Definition: Deviation recursion of the renormalization group: the LDP chain of block spins. Conjecture: The rate convergence of RG recursions: the sequence of rate functions of block transformations decreases monotonically to the fixed-point rate, and the fixed point is the Onsager–Machlup functional of the Gaussian field. (RG rate fixed point)

**中文。** 定义：重整化群的偏差递推：块自旋的 LDP 链。猜想：RG 递推的速率收敛：块变换的速率函数列单调下降收敛到不动点速率且不动点为高斯场的 Onsager–Machlup 泛函。（RG 速率不动点）
