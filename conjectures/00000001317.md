# 00000001317

**English.** Definition: Normal forms of CR maps (Walker type). Conjecture: CR maps between real-analytic hyperspheres are rational, with degree explicitly bounded in terms of the mapping degree (a quantitative version beyond Huang's theorem).

**中文。** 定义：CR 映射的正规形式(Walker 型)。猜想：实解析超球面间的 CR 映射为有理映射且次数由映射度显式界(Huang 定理之外的定量版)。
