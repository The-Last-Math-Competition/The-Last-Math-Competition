# 00000002047

**English.** Definition: Stepanov power sums are S_k = Σ_{x∈F_p}x^k χ(x³−x) (χ the quadratic character). Conjecture: The normalized distribution of S_k converges to the Bessel distribution of the unitary Whittaker model; the convergence follows from uniform limits of Weil-type bounds.

**中文。** 定义：Stepanov 幂和指 S_k = Σ_{x\in F_p}x^k\chi(x³−x)(χ 为二次特征)。猜想：S_k 的归一化分布收敛到酉 Whittaker 模型的 Bessel 分布;收敛由 Weil 型上界的一致极限。
