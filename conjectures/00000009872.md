# 00000009872

**English.** Definition: Ballistic annihilation: the dynamics of two-species particles annihilating on contact. Conjecture: The decay exponent of the surviving particle density for velocity distributions of ballistic annihilation is 1, with correction t^{-1/2}; the correction coefficient is uniquely determined by the expansion of the characteristic function of the velocity distribution near zero. (ballistic annihilation density decay)

**中文。** 定义：弹道湮灭：双类粒子相遇即灭的动力学。猜想：弹道湮灭的速度分布的幸存粒子密度衰减指数为 1 且修正为 t^{−1/2}；修正系数由速度分布的特征函数在零点附近的展开唯一确定。（弹道湮灭密度衰减）
