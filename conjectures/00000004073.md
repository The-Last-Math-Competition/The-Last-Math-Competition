# 00000004073

**BF2-13 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: A Bykov cycle is a heteroclinic cycle between two saddle-foci. Conjecture: After the cycle breaks, the period of the periodic orbits is asymptotically logarithmic, T(epsilon) = -C log epsilon + O(1), with the constant C given explicitly by the difference of the real parts of the two foci. (logarithmic period constant of Bykov cycles)

**中文。** 定义：Bykov 环指两鞍焦点之间的异宿环。猜想：环破裂后周期轨道的周期渐近为对数律 T(ε) = −C·log ε + O(1),常数 C 由两焦点实部之差显式给出。（Bykov 对数周期常数）
