# 00000005878

**English.** Definition: The residual estimator is the computable upper bound of jump-weighted type. Conjecture: On hanging-node meshes it is corrected by jump coefficients and closed, and the kernel of the correction is the hanging degrees of freedom. (hanging-degree-of-freedom jump-coefficient correction of residual estimators)
