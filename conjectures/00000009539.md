# 00000009539

**English.** Definition: Quantum feedback capacity Q_FB allows the receiver to send quantum systems back to the sender — the bidirectional quantum channel model; classical feedback capacity C_FB allows only classical bits back. Conjecture: Q_FB is at most 2Q for every channel, with the constant 2 asymptotically attained by the bidirectional qubit cavity model; and classical feedback does not increase the classical capacity of entanglement-breaking channels, the feedback invariance law holding completely on the EB class. (feedback doubling upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：量子反馈容量 Q_FB 允许接收端向发送端回传量子系统，即双向量子信道模型；经典反馈容量 C_FB 只允许回传经典比特。猜想：Q_FB≤2Q 对一切信道成立且常数 2 由双向 qubit 腔模型渐近达到；经典反馈不增大纠缠破坏信道的经典容量，反馈不变律在 EB 类上完全成立。（反馈倍增上界）
