**MIB-24 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The motivic stationary state: the minimal stable form of a motivic measure under the involution closure. Conjecture: The stationary state is reached in finitely many steps, with step count bounded by the logarithm of the Milnor number of the analytic type of the singularity; the motive of the stationary state is the unique set of indecomposable direct summands of the singularity motive; and the uniqueness of the decomposition is equivalent to the Krull-Schmidt property of the category of motives. (motivic stationary decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：母题稳态指母题测度在对合包下的极小稳定型。猜想：稳态在有限步内达到，步数为奇点解析型的 Milnor 数的对数上界；稳态的母题为奇点母题的不可分解直和项的唯一集合；分解的唯一性等价于母题范畴的 Krull–Schmidt 性质。（母题稳态分解）
