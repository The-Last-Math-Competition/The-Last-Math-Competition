**English.** Definition: The spectral sequence of motivic cohomology with K-theory. Conjecture: The two-dimensional collapse point is the cone point at order three, and the combination of the order is the Bloch-Lichtenbaum tower. (cone order-three collapse tower of motivic K-theory)
