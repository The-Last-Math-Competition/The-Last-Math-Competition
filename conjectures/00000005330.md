**LH2-10 △** — source: 163_范畴化不变量_链接同调.md

**English.** Definition: Homology and the Seifert genus are two layers of knot data. Conjecture: There exist two knots with isomorphic homology but different Seifert genera, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of knot mutations with genus difference one. (same homology-different Seifert genus separation)

**中文。** 定义：同调与塞弗特亏格指两层纽结数据。猜想：存在两纽结的同调同构而塞弗特亏格不同,分离由变结的显式对实现且亏格差为一。（同调同-塞弗特亏格异分离）
