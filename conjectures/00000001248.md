# 00000001248

**CG-48 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: TASEP is the totally asymmetric simple exclusion process; the second-class particle is the seat-referenced tracer particle. Conjecture: Under sparse initial conditions, the position of the second-class particle equals t/2 plus √t times an explicit distribution function; that function is a shifted Gaussian with shift constant of Euler-constant type. (second-class particle trajectory formula)

**中文。** 定义：TASEP 为全不对称简单排斥粒子系统，二阶粒子为座位参考的示踪粒子。猜想：稀疏初始下二阶粒子位置等于 t/2 加 √t 乘显式分布函数；该分布函数为高斯的平移且平移常数为欧拉常数型值。（二阶粒子轨迹公式）
