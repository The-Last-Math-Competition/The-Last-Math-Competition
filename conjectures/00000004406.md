**English.** Definition: An admissible flow on a graphing is a circulation under edge-capacity constraints. Conjecture: Its extreme value is converted explicitly by the minimum cut with conversion ratio 1, equality is attained on tree-like graphings, and it is a tight bound in general. (graphing flow-cut conversion ratio 1)
