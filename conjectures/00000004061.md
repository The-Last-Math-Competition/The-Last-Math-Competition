# 00000004061

**English.** Definition: A Hopf bifurcation is a conjugate pair of eigenvalues crossing the imaginary axis, and the focal values are the successive Lyapunov coefficients. Conjecture: A Bautin point, where the focal values l_1 and l_2 vanish simultaneously while l_3 is nonzero, occurs in quadratic polynomial vector fields if and only if the quadratic coefficients satisfy the intersection condition of two explicit algebraic curves. (algebraic characterization of Bautin points)

**中文。** 定义：Hopf 分叉指共轭特征值对穿越虚轴,焦点量指逐阶 Lyapunov 系数。猜想：焦点量 l₁ 与 l₂ 同时为零而 l₃ 非零的 Bautin 点在二次多项式向量场中出现当且仅当二次项系数满足两条显式代数曲线的相交条件。（Bautin 点代数刻画）
