# 00000009528

**English.** Definition: A quantum broadcast channel has one sender and several receivers, with capacity region the set of all simultaneously attainable rate vectors; degraded broadcast means the receiver channels form a noise-ordering chain. Conjecture: The entanglement-assisted capacity region of degraded quantum broadcast channels single-letterizes with at most m factorial extreme points for m receivers; and the region for classical-quantum hybrid sending is polyhedral while for pure quantum sending it is a strictly convex surface — a structural dichotomy. (quantum broadcast single-letterization)

**中文。** 定义：量子广播信道有一个发送端与多个接收端，容量区域为全部同时可达速率向量的集合；degraded 广播指接收端信道构成噪声偏序链。猜想：degraded 量子广播的纠缠辅助容量区域单字母化，且极点个数不超过接收端数 m 的阶乘 m!；经典-量子混合发送的区域为多面体而纯量子发送的区域为严格凸曲面，两者结构二分。（量子广播单字母化）
