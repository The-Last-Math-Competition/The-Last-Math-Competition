**DA-78 △** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The distribution of partial quotients in the Schneider p-adic continued-fraction expansion. Conjecture: For almost every p-adic number, the distribution of Schneider partial quotients is explicit and compatible with Haar measure; and the constants of the distribution are logarithmic powers of p. (Schneider continued fraction equidistribution)

**中文。** 定义：p 进连分数的 Schneider 展开的部分商分布。猜想：对几乎处处的 p 进数其 Schneider 部分商的分布显式给出且与 Haar 测度相容；且分布的常数为 p 的对数幂。（Schneider 连分数等分布）
