**KZ2-21 ◆** — source: 183_KPZII.md

**English.** Definition: Time reversal is the time-inversion symmetry of the dynamics, the KPZ reflection. Conjecture: In the fixed-point framework, time reversal is equivalent to spatial reflection, the combination of the reflection being the mirrored initial data; the invariant measure of the reversal is white noise, stationary under the flow; and the reversal commutes with the Lax-Oleinik duality of Burgers, with commutation constant -1. (mirrored-initial reflection of time reversal)

**中文。** 定义：时间倒置：动力学的时间反演对称（KPZ 的 reflection）。猜想：不动点框架下时间倒置等价于空间反射且反射的组合为镜像初值；倒置的不变测度为白噪声且噪声在流下稳态；倒置与 Burgers 的 Lax–Oleinik 对偶可交换且交换常数为 -1。（时间倒置镜像初值反射）
