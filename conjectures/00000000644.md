**KT-44 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The count of holomorphic disks in Sym^d(Σ) is the Floer-type count in the geometric realization of the Lawrence–Krammer–Bigelow representation. Conjecture: The matrix entries given by this count in the Hill basis make the character of the Burau representation positive at real points — all eigenvalues have real part ≥ −1/2; this positivity is equivalent to a hyperbolic-volume bound for the corresponding braid.

**中文。** 定义：Sym^d(Σ) 中的全纯圆盘计数指 Lawrence–Krammer–Bigelow 表示的几何实现中的 Floer 型计数。猜想：该计数在 Hill 基下给出的矩阵元素使 Burau 表示的特征标在实值点取正值,即一切本征值的实部 ≥ −1/2;该正性等价于相应辫的双曲体积的界。
