# 00000005164

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative Hodge theory is the categorified Hodge theory. Conjecture: Its classification conversion is an explicit pair of the heart of the category with the period map, consistent with derived-variety types and faithful in both directions. (heart-period conversion pair of noncommutative Hodge theory)

**中文。** 定义：非交换 Hodge 指范畴化的 Hodge 理论。猜想：其分类换算为范畴的心与周期映射的显式对,导出簇型一致且换算双向忠实。（非交换 Hodge 心-周期换算对）
