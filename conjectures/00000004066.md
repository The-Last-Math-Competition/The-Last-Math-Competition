**BF2-06 ★** — source: 121_微分方程分叉理论.md

**English.** Definition: A Naimark-Sacker bifurcation is a closed orbit losing stability and producing an invariant torus, and a resonance tongue is a frequency-locking parameter wedge. Conjecture: The width of the q-th resonance tongue is of order q^{-3} in the unfolding parameter, with the coefficient given explicitly by the cubic term of the resonant normal form. (q^{-3} law of resonance tongue widths)

**中文。** 定义：Naimark–Sacker 分叉指闭轨失稳产生不变环面,共振舌指锁频参数楔。猜想：第 q 次共振舌的宽度为开折参数的 q^{−3} 阶,系数由共振规范形三次项显式给出。（共振舌宽度 q 负三次律）
