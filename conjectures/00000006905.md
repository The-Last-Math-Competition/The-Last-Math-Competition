**GC6-25 ◆** — source: 215_计算复杂性的几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Shifted partial derivatives are the subspace towers after differentiation. Conjecture: The jump trajectory of the dimension spectrum is an explicit staircase and the jump positions are given by the inflection points of the Hilbert function; and the monotonicity of the trajectory is the positivity of the Betti table. (dimension-jump trajectory of shifted derivatives)

**中文。** 定义：移位部分导数为求导后的子空间塔。猜想：维数谱的跳跃轨迹为显式阶梯且跳跃位置由 Hilbert 函数的拐点给出；且轨迹的单调性为 Betti 表正性。（移位导数维数跳跃轨迹）
