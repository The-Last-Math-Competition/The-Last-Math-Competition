# 00000000597

**CA-97 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The Steenbrink spectrum is the weight set {i/q+1} of an isolated singularity, q a common multiple of the Milnor numbers. Conjecture: The full set of differences of adjacent spectral weights is exactly the multiples of the reciprocal of the least common multiple; and the rigidity of the spectrum is given by the eigenvalue gaps of the monodromy operator. (Steenbrink spectrum difference set)

**中文。** 定义：Steenbrink 谱为孤立奇点的谱权重集 {i/q+1}，q 为 Milnor 数公倍数。猜想：谱的相邻权重之差的全集恰为最小公倍数分之一的所有倍数；且谱的刚性由单值算子的特征值间隔给出。（Steenbrink 谱差全集）
