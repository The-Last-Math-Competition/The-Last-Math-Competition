# 00000007863

**RAM-23 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The query complexity of randomized submodular maximization: the expected number of queries q(n,k) needed for a (1-1/e)-approximation of max_{|S|<=k} f(S) with f normalized, monotone, submodular, under a value oracle. Conjecture: q(n,k) = Theta(n), a linear lower bound matching the greedy upper bound, with constant exactly 1 (every point queried at least once); and when a (1-1/e+epsilon) approximation is allowed the query count jumps to n^{Omega(log(1/epsilon)/epsilon^3)}, the jump exponent being doubly logarithmic and uniquely determined by the information-theoretic duality of the symmetric submodular game. (submodular query complexity jump)

**中文。** 定义：随机化子模函数最大化的查询复杂度：在值 oracle 模型下 max_{|S|≤k} f(S)（f 归一化单调子模）的 (1-1/e)-近似所需期望查询数 q(n,k)。猜想：q(n,k)=Θ(n)（线性下界匹配贪心的上界），且常数恰为 1（每点至少询问一次）；当允许 (1-1/e+ε) 近似时查询数跳升为 n^{Ω(log(1/ε)/ε^{3})}，跳变指数为双对数型，由信息论对偶（对称子模对策）唯一确定。
