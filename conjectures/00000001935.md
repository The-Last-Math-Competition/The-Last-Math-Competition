# 00000001935

**LG-05 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: A fake projective plane is a hyperbolic surface with the same Betti numbers as CP² (a minimal-volume surface). Conjecture: The arithmetic list of fake projective planes is complete (50 known examples); the minimal volume is attained uniquely among all hyperbolic surfaces (i.e. fake projective planes are volume-minimizing).

**中文。** 定义：伪投影平面指与 CP² 同 Betti 数的双曲曲面(最小体积曲面)。猜想：伪投影平面的算术列表为完全(已知 50 例);最小体积在一切双曲曲面中唯一取得(即伪投影平面为体积极小)。
