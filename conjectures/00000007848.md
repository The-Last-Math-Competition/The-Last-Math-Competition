**English.** Definition: optimal T(t) of Markov family) on of state: energy (spectral gap λ(s) temperature s changes of module. Conjecture: optimal satisfies variational equation dT/dt = -T²λ(T) · (d log π_T(E)/dT)^{-1}; its solution such that always mixing ∫dT/(T²λ(T)) is most small; in under analytic solution is time of arctan type trajectory, saddle point is always 1/4 · always time, the 1/4 universal in all parameter family.

**中文。** 定义：模拟退火的准静态冷却：能量景观（谱隙 λ(s) 随温度 s 变化的 Markov 链族）上的最优冷却调度 T(t)。猜想：最优调度满足变分方程 dT/dt = -T²λ(T)·(d log π_T(E)/dT)^{-1}；其解使总混合预算 ∫dT/(T²λ(T)) 为最小；在双井景观下解析解为 arctan 型轨迹，穿越鞍点的时间份额恒为 1/4·总时间，该 1/4 律普适于一切单参数双井族。
