**RAM-08 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: Quasi-static cooling schedules for simulated annealing: the optimal cooling schedule T(t) on an energy landscape whose Markov chain family has spectral gap lambda(s) varying with temperature. Conjecture: The optimal schedule satisfies the variational equation dT/dt = -T^2 lambda(T) (d log pi_T(E)/dT)^{-1}, whose solution minimizes the total mixing budget the integral of dT/(T^2 lambda(T)); in a double-well landscape the analytic solution is an arctan-type trajectory whose saddle-crossing time share is universally one quarter of the total time, this one-quarter law holding for all one-parameter double-well families. (annealing variational schedule and quarter law)

**中文。** 定义：模拟退火的准静态冷却：能量景观（谱隙 λ(s) 随温度 s 变化的 Markov 链族）上的最优冷却调度 T(t)。猜想：最优调度满足变分方程 dT/dt = -T²λ(T)·(d log π_T(E)/dT)^{-1}；其解使总混合预算 ∫dT/(T²λ(T)) 为最小；在双井景观下解析解为 arctan 型轨迹，穿越鞍点的时间份额恒为 1/4·总时间，该 1/4 律普适于一切单参数双井族。
