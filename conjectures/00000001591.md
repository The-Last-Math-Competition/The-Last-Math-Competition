# 00000001591

**English.** Definition: A skew brace is the algebraic model of set-theoretic Yang–Baxter solutions with a double group structure. Conjecture: The count of braces of prime-cubed order is an explicit polynomial; and the coefficients of the polynomial are given by the third-order classification of abelian groups. (p-cubed order brace polynomial count)

**中文。** 定义：skew brace 指带双群结构的集合论 Yang–Baxter 解的代数模型。猜想：素立方阶的 brace 计数为显式多项式；且多项式的系数由交换群的三阶分类给出。（p3 阶 brace 多项式计数）
