# 00000004961

**English.** Definition: Prony's method is the classical algorithm recovering exponential sums from equidistant samples. Conjecture: The noise threshold of three-term exponential sum recovery is given by an explicit expression in the cube of the separation, the bound is tight, and the criterion is the smallest singular value. (three-term noise threshold of Prony)

**中文。** 定义：Prony 方法指由等距采样恢复指数和的经典算法。猜想：三项指数和恢复的噪声阈值由分离度的三次幂型显式式给出,界紧且以最小奇异值为判据。（Prony 三项噪声阈值）
