# 00000003860

**English.** Definition: Bulk universality is the property that local bulk statistics do not depend on the potential. Conjecture: There exist real-analytic potentials whose bulk statistics depend on the potential, with the necessary and sufficient condition the loss of analyticity of the reciprocal equilibrium density inside the support. (beta non-universality criterion)

**中文。** 定义：bulk 普适性指体内局部统计不依赖位势的性质。猜想：存在实解析位势使 bulk 统计依赖位势,其充要条件是平衡密度的倒数在支撑内部失去解析性。（β-非普适判据）
