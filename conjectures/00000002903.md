# 00000002903

**English.** Definition: The density of rational points on rational varieties over finite fields. Conjecture: Saturation of rational-point density: the density limit of geometrically irreducible varieties is the average of the reciprocal of the Galois group order, with deviation controlled by the root counts of quadratic twists. (rational point Galois density)

**中文。** 定义：有限域上的有理簇的有理点密度。猜想：有理点密度的饱和：几何不可约簇的密度极限为 Galois 群阶倒数的平均且平均的偏差由二次扭的根数控制。（有理点 Galois 密度）
