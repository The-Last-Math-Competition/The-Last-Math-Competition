**English.** Definition: The measure entropy of a graphing is the measure entropy of its generator subsystem. Conjecture: The conversion of local to global entropy is an explicit logarithmic ratio, equality holds if and only if the graphing is amenable, and in the non-amenable case the direction of the strict inequality is determined. (amenable criterion of local-global entropy conversion)

**中文。** 定义：graphing 的测度熵指其生成子系统的测度熵。猜想：局部熵与全局熵的换算为显式对数比,等式当且仅当 graphing 顺从,非顺从时严格不等式方向确定。（局部-全局熵换算顺从判据）
