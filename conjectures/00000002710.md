**English.** Definition: The Arzelà–Zariski duality framework: the correspondence between functional compactness and spectral geometry. Conjecture: The dual correspondence: continuity of the spectral radius of equicontinuous families is equivalent to normality of the spectral topology, and the proof of the equivalence is given by the compactification layer of Grothendieck biduality. (AZ duality spectral continuity)
