**English.** Definition: The metric of the Sklyanin backdrop of separation of variables (SoV): the normalization coefficient alpha of the symplectic form in separated coordinates (lambda_i, mu_i) of an integrable model. Conjecture: alpha is always n (the number of separated variables); and the algebraic transcendence degree of the separation equations mu_i = f(lambda_i, t) at algebraic t is 0 (separation of variables concentrates transcendence in mu); the separability criterion (existence of separated variables) is equivalent to the commutativity of the ring generated by the spectral polynomials of the Lax matrix (the Burbirko criterion), and the spectral radius spectrum of that ring is the set of integer points of a conic (an algebraic-curve integer point classification). (Sklyanin separation conic integrality)
