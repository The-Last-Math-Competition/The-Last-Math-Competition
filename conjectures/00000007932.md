# 00000007932

**INT-02 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The octahedral symmetry of Jimbo algebraic solutions of Painleve VI: the Picard-type lattices of the parameters (a,b,c,d) of algebraic solutions of PVI and the finite list of icosahedral solutions. Conjecture: The complete list of irreducible algebraic solutions of PVI corresponds to the conjugacy classes of the icosahedral group (17 classes of solutions, including generalizations of known special solutions); and for each algebraic solution y(t), the product of the local monodromy matrices at t = 0, 1, infinity is the identity (Malgrange irreducibility), the eigenvalues of the monodromy generate a sublattice of the PVI parameter lattice, and the index is exactly the trivialization at 1/2. (PVI icosahedral solution classification)

**中文。** 定义：Painlevé VI 的 Jimbo 代数解的八面体对称：PVI 的代数解的参数 (a,b,c,d) 的 Picard 型格点与 icosahedral 解的有限列表。猜想：PVI 的不可约代数解的完整列表对应 icosahedral 群的共轭类（共 17 类解，含已知特解的推广）；且每个代数解的解函数 y(t) 在 t=0,1,∞ 的局部单项式矩阵的乘积为恒等（Malgrange 无挠性），单项式的特征值生成 PVI 参数格的子格，指标恰为 1/2 的平凡化。
