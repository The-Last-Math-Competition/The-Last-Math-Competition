# 00000008232

**English.** Definition: The weight axiomatization of coalition structures for the Shapley value: the extreme rays ext_ray of the weight vectors of the weighted Shapley value phi_w. Conjecture: ext_ray (extreme rays of weights) is the family of point-mass vectors (a delta-mass family law); the number of vertices of the core of convex geometry (anti-convex games) is 2^{n-1} (a vertex count law); the count of vertices (of the core polytope) equals the number of chains of feasible coalitions (a chain-vertex correspondence); and the barycentric coordinates of the position of the Shapley value inside the core are explicitly the entropy of the coalition structure (an entropy barycenter formula). (Shapley delta rays chain vertices entropy barycenter)

**中文。** 定义：Shapley 值的联盟结构的权重公理化：加权 Shapley 值 φ_w 的权重向量的极端射线 ext_ray。猜想：ext_ray（权重的极端射线）为单点质量向量族（δ-质量族律）；且凸几何（反凸博弈）的核的顶点数为 2^{n-1}（顶点数律）；顶点的计数（核的多面体的顶点）= 可行联盟的链数（链-顶点对应）；Shapley 值的核内位置的重心坐标的显式为联盟结构的熵（熵重心公式）。
