**English.** Definition: The minimal independent groups of axioms for cooperative solution concepts: the minimal groups axiom_min of independence for the axioms of the core, nucleolus, and Shapley value. Conjecture: The size of axiom_min (minimal independent group of Shapley axioms) is 3 (a three-axiom law); the shortest derivation length of redundant axioms (derivable axioms) is 7 steps (a seven-step derivation law); and the exponential downgrade of the verification complexity of the Kohlberg criterion of the nucleolus (characterization by balanced sets) to a polynomial-time verifiable subclass holds for convex games (a convex-class polynomial verification law). (Shapley three axioms seven-step Kohlberg)
