**English.** Definition: Ihara zeta ζ_X(u) of spectral graph corresponds to: k- regular graph X of Ihara zeta and explicit (Hashimoto formula) of Laplacian spectrum of graph. Conjecture: pole (reciprocal of ζ_X(u) is spectral radius |u|=1/(2√(k-1)); of function) in random regular graph on distribution in free group of nontrivial spectrum of graph and deviation of ζ_X in critical on by fluctuation controls, deviation exponent 1/2 of Cheeger constant of bijection cover (new graphs) of graph and Alon–Boppana constant uniform.
