# 00000009817

**DDG-27 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Weighted Voronoi-Delaunay duality: conformal structures of power diagrams. Conjecture: The weighted circle patterns of the power diagrams of weighted Delaunay duals are discrete conformal structures; the Laplace equation for the weights has a unique solution, and the moduli space of solutions is an open cell of the positive cone. (weighted duality conformal structure)

**中文。** 定义：加权 Voronoi–Delaunay 对偶：幂图的共形结构。猜想：加权 Delaunay 对偶的幂图的加权圆模式为离散共形结构；权重的拉普拉斯方程的解的存在唯一且解的模空间为正锥的开胞腔。（加权对偶共形结构）
