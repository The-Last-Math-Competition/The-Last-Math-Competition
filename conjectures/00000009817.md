**English.** Definition: structure of weighted Voronoi–Delaunay dual: power graph. Conjecture: weighted module expression of power graph of weighted Delaunay dual is exists unique of solution of Laplace equation of discrete structure; weight and moduli space of solution is of positive cone. (weighted dual structure)

**中文。** 定义：加权 Voronoi–Delaunay 对偶：幂图的共形结构。猜想：加权 Delaunay 对偶的幂图的加权圆模式为离散共形结构；权重的拉普拉斯方程的解的存在唯一且解的模空间为正锥的开胞腔。（加权对偶共形结构）
