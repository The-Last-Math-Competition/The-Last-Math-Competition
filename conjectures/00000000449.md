**English.** Definition: The q,t-Catalan is Haiman's bisymmetric generalization of the Catalan numbers. Conjecture: The coefficient sequence of C_n(q,0) never has three adjacent decreasing coefficients followed immediately by a rise; and the oscillation pattern is described by the area statistic of Dyck paths. (q-Catalan oscillation pattern)

**中文。** 定义：q,t-Catalan 为 Haiman 的双对称 Catalan 推广。猜想：C_n(q,0) 的系数序列无相邻三项递减后立即回升；且振荡模式的描述为 Dyck 路的面积统计。（q-Catalan 振荡模式）
