**KZ2-17 ★** — source: 183_KPZII.md

**English.** Definition: The central distribution is the stationary law at parameter zero, of Tracy-Widom type at the narrow-wedge center. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit ratio of stationary rates, coinciding with the classical rate in the limit; the deviation of the coincidence is the negative one-third power of t, the power locked by self-similarity; and the tail exponents of the distribution are of type 3/2, with asymmetric tails. (stationary-rate ratio conversion of the central distribution)

**中文。** 定义：中心分布：参数零的平稳律（窄楔中心的 Tracy–Widom 型）。猜想：换算常数为平稳率的显式比且极限下与经典率重合；重合的偏差为 t 的负三分之一幂且幂由自相似锁定；分布的尾部指数为 3/2 型且两尾不对称。（中心分布平稳率比换算）
