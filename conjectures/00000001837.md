**English.** Definition: The cohomology ring of a toric variety and the f-vector of the convex polytope. Conjecture: Macaulay monotonicity is complete under the bijection between the two; and the monotone chains are generated by the refined closure of Hilbert functions. (toric f-vector Macaulay completely monotone)
