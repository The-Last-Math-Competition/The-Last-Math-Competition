# 00000005245

**English.** Definition: The irreducibility genus threshold is the minimal genus where the criterion holds. Conjecture: The threshold at genus two is decided by the explicit decomposition of etale covers, and the threshold is two; the decomposition types are indexed by the monodromy group of the cover, with a finite index set; and the lifting of the threshold is a linear function of the genus with slope 2. (cover-decomposition threshold at genus two)

**中文。** 定义：不可约亏格阈值：判据成立的最低亏格。猜想：亏格二的阈值由平展覆叠的显式分解判定且阈值为二；分解类型由覆叠的单值群索引且索引集为有限；阈值的提升为亏格的线性函数且斜率为 2。（亏格二覆叠分解阈值）
