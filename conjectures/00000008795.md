**English.** Definition: Random matrix perturbation: the randomized Davis-Kahan theory. Conjecture: The Weyl improvement of spectral spacing is of order epsilon and the combination is gap concentration; and the kernel of concentration is the semicircle law. (random perturbation semicircle gap concentration Weyl)

**中文。** 定义：随机矩阵的扰动为 Davis–Kahan 随机版本。猜想：谱间距的 Weyl 改进为 ε 级且组合为间隙集中；且集中的核为半圆律。（随机扰动半圆律间隙集中 Weyl 改进）
