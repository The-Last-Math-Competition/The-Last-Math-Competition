# 00000001614

**SP-14 ★** — source: 39_泛函分析III_算子谱问题.md

**English.** Definition: The Hill equation −y'' + q(x)y = λy with q periodic. Conjecture: For the trigonometric potential q(x) = a·cos x, the total number of instability intervals is at most ⌊2√a⌋ + 1, and this is attained (gap counting for trigonometric potentials).

**中文。** 定义：Hill 方程 -y'' + q(x)y = λy(q 周期)。猜想：三角 q(x) = a·cos x 的不稳定性区间总数 ≤ ⌊2√a⌋ + 1 且达到(三角势隙计数)。
