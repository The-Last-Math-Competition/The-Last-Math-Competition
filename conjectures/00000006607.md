**English.** Definition: The tensor-ideal spectrum is the spatial structure of ideals. Conjecture: The spectrum dimensions of perfect complexes are equivalent and the equality is topologically closed, the combination of the closure being Thom. (Thom topological closure of spectrum dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：张量理想谱为理想的空间结构。猜想：完美复形的谱维数等价且等式拓扑封闭；且封闭的组合为 Thom。（谱维数 Thom 拓扑封闭）
