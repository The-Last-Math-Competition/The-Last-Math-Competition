# 00000006502

**English.** Definition: Characteristic numbers are the numbers of closed manifolds. Conjecture: The rigidity of characteristic numbers is the multiplicativity of the numbers, and the combination of the multiplicativity is the covering formula. (multiplicative covering formula of characteristic numbers)
