# 00000005634

**CP2-14 ★** — source: 173_组合几何III_凸位置.md

**English.** Definition: The piercing theorem controls the common point of intersecting families, where every d+1 intersecting implies a common piercing point. Conjecture: The quantitative bound of the three-intersection case is an explicit linear expression of the piercing count, given by the partition parameter; the piercing count is at most the logarithm of the family size with base 2; and the tight example of the bound is the binary subdivision of interval families, with subdivision depth equal to the bound. (linear piercing bound of triple intersections)

**中文。** 定义：穿刺定理：交族的公共点控制（一族若每 d+1 个相交则有公共穿刺点）。猜想：三交的定量界为穿刺数的显式线性式且界由剖分参数给出；穿刺数不超过族数的对数且对数底为 2；界的紧例为区间族的二叉剖分且剖分深度为界。（三交穿刺线性界）
