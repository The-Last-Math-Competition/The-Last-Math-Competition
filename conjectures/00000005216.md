**English.** Definition: Non-integer indices of subfactors are index values above 4 that are not integers. Conjecture: Within index 5, the realizable non-integer domain is the explicit union of intervals of squared-cosine values with finite point sets; the boundary points are cosine-type algebraic numbers; and the cumulative count of isolated points follows Weyl word lengths, with polynomial-type growth. (cosine boundary of non-integer indices within five)

**中文。** 定义：子因子的非整数指标：落在 4 之上且不为整数的指标值。猜想：指标 5 以内的非整数可实现域为余弦平方值的显式区间并与有限点集；边界点为余弦型代数数；孤立点的累计计数按外尔字长度给出且增长为多项式型。（指标五非整数余弦边界）
