# 00000008183

**English.** Definition: The computation of solvability of Galois groups of polynomials: the stabilization step count gal_steps(f) of the factorization of resolvent polynomials. Conjecture: gal_steps (the length of the resolvent chain) is at most deg f - 2 (a degree-minus-two law); the product of the degrees of the resolvents at each step is at most (deg f)! (a factorial product bound); the computation of the solvability criterion (complete splitting of the chain) has polynomial complexity (a polynomial criterion law); and the minimal example of unsolvability (chain failure) has degree 5 (a degree-5 failure rigidity). (resolvent chain degree-minus-two factorial bound)

**中文。** 定义：多项式的 Galois 群的可解性计算：resolvent 多项式的分解的稳定化步数 gal_steps(f)。猜想：gal_steps（resolvent 链的长度）≤ deg f - 2（次数减二律）；且链的每一步的 resolvent 的次数的乘积 ≤ (deg f)!（阶乘积界）；可解群的判据（链的完全分裂）的计算复杂度为多项式（多项式判据律）；不可解的最小例子（链失效）的次数为 5（五次失效刚性）。
