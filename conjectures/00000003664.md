**English.** Definition: Asymptotic convex geometry: limits of random projections. Conjecture: The Poisson limit of random projections: the Poisson convergence of random projections of high-dimensional bodies, with the canonical convexity of convergence the U-statistic limit. (random projection U-statistic canonical convex limit)

**中文。** 定义：渐近凸几何：随机投影的极限。猜想：随机投影的 Poisson 极限：高维体的随机投影的 Poisson 收敛且收敛的典则凸为 U-统计极限。（随机投影 U-统计典则凸极限）
