**English.** Definition: A toric Fano is a Fano variety containing a dense torus. Conjecture: Its K-stability criterion is an explicit system of inequalities on the barycenter of the polytope, the inequalities are given by weighted sums of vertices, and the criterion is completely equivalent. (barycenter criterion of toric K-stability)

**中文。** 定义：环面 Fano 指含稠密环面的 Fano 簇。猜想：其 K 稳定性判据为多面体重心的显式不等式组,不等式由顶点加权和给出且判据完全等价。（环面 K 稳定重心判据）
