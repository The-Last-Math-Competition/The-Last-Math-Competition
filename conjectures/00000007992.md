# 00000007992

**DEL-02 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The refined p-capacity version of the Wiener criterion: the regularity of boundary points of a domain Omega by the divergence criterion of the series of p-capacities cap(B_{2^{-k}} minus Omega). Conjecture: The equivalence of p-harmonic measure and capacity holds uniformly for p in (1,infinity): mu_x(E) <= C cap_p(E intersect boundary Omega, Omega)/cap_p(B,Omega); the constant is an explicit bounded function of p, returning to the classical 1 at p = 2 (Kellogg exactness); and the dimension upper bound of the complement of the regular point set (irregular points) is dim_Haus at most n - p (the dimension spectrum of the capacity measure). (p-Wiener capacity equivalence constant)

**中文。** 定义：Wiener 判据的 p-容量精细版：区域 Ω 的边界点的正则性由 p-容量的级数 Σ cap(B_{2^{-k}}\Ω) 的发散判据。猜想：p-调和测度与容量的等价性在 p∈(1,∞) 的一致性：μ_x(E) ≤ C cap_p(E∩∂Ω,Ω)/cap_p(B,Ω)；且常数为 p 的显式有界函数，p=2 时回到经典的 1（Kellogg 精确）；边界点的正则点集的余集（irregular 点）的维数上界为 dim Haus ≤ n-p（容量测度的维数谱）。
