# 00000007631

**English.** Definition: Quasilinear hyperbolic systems with the blow-up structure of wave-type first-order quasilinear systems. Conjecture: The lower bound of blow-up time is given by the reciprocal law of initial slopes with counterexample to exactness the constant state; the constant of the reciprocal law is the derivative of characteristic speed; and the location of blow-up is the characteristic envelope. (blowup-time reciprocal law)

**中文。** 定义：拟线性双曲：波型一阶拟线性系统的爆破结构。猜想：爆破时间的下界由初值斜率的倒数律给出且精确性的反例为常数态；倒数律的常数为特征速率的导数；爆破的位置为特征包络。（爆破时间倒数律结构）
