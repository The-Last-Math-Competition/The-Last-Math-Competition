# 00000006132

**English.** Definition: The derived fiber product is the product in the homotopy-limit sense. Conjecture: For flat pullbacks the amplitude is zero with bound controlled by the length of the regular sequence, the combination of the control being the Tor layers. (Tor-layer amplitude bound of fiber products)
