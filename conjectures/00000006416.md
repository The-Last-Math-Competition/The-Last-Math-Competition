**English.** Definition: Asymmetry is the asymmetry measure of the symmetric-difference metric. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the minimal body and the near-ball minimal bodies are rotationally symmetric ellipsoids; the parameter window is explicit with endpoints axis ratios 1 plus-minus epsilon; and the eccentricity of the ellipsoid is a linear function of the window. (rotational-ellipsoid parameter-window conversion of minimal bodies)
