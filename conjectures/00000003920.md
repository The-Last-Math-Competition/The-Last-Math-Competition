# 00000003920

**English.** Definition: The action spectrum is the set of action values of all Hamiltonian orbits. Conjecture: There exist two Hamiltonian isotopy classes on a closed symplectic manifold whose action spectra coincide completely while the length spectra of their fixed points differ; the coarsest invariant preserved under equality of spectra is exactly the spectral norm gamma. (HF spectrum-length separation)

**中文。** 定义：作用谱指全体 Hamilton 轨道作用值之集。猜想：存在闭辛流形上两个 Hamilton 同痕类作用谱完全相同而其不动点的长度谱相异;谱相同的最粗不变量恰为谱范数 γ。（HF 谱-长度分离）
