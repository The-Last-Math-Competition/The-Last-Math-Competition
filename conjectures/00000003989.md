# 00000003989

**English.** Definition: A monotone circuit is a circuit without negation gates, and a monotone proof is the corresponding system using only monotone reasoning. Conjecture: Every explicit formula family with a monotone circuit lower bound can be translated verbatim into an exponential monotone proof length lower bound, and the translation preserves the exponential order. (monotone circuit to monotone proof correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：单调电路指不含否定门的电路,单调证明指仅含单调推理的对应系统。猜想：每个单调电路下界的显式公式族可逐字翻译为指数级单调证明长度下界,且翻译保持指数阶。（单调电路-单调证明对应）
