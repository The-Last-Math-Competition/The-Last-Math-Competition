**OL-06 ★** — source: 72_序与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: The free lattice FL(n): the word problem of the free lattice on n generators. Conjecture: The complexity of the word problem of free lattices is exactly linear time; the optimal decision algorithm is a Whitehead-type reduction whose step count is a linear function of word length. (free lattice word problem linear)

**中文。** 定义：自由格 FL(n)：n 生成子的自由格的词问题。猜想：自由格的字问题的复杂性恰为线性时间；判定算法的最优形式为 Whitehead 型约简且约简步数为词长的线性函数。（自由格词问题线性）
