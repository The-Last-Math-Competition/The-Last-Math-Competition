# 00000004389

**English.** Definition: The K-spectrum of a Waldhausen structure is the K-theory spectrum of the Waldhausen construction of a category. Conjecture: Two Waldhausen structures give equivalent K-spectra if and only if the K-spectrum of their sifted category is the zero spectrum, and the criterion can be verified effectively via the additivity theorem. (sifted criterion for K-spectrum equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Waldhausen 结构的 K 谱指范畴的 Waldhausen 构造的 K 理论谱。猜想：两个 Waldhausen 结构给出等价 K 谱当且仅当其筛子范畴的 K 谱为零谱,判据可经加性定理有效验证。（K 谱等价的筛判据）
