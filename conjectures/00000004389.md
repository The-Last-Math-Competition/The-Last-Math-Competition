**English.** Definition: K spectrum of Waldhausen structure denotes K theory spectrum of Waldhausen construction of category. Conjecture: two element Waldhausen structure gives equivalent K spectrum if and only if K spectrum of its subcategory is zero spectrum, additive theorem effective. (of K spectrum equivalent)

**中文。** 定义：Waldhausen 结构的 K 谱指范畴的 Waldhausen 构造的 K 理论谱。猜想：两个 Waldhausen 结构给出等价 K 谱当且仅当其筛子范畴的 K 谱为零谱,判据可经加性定理有效验证。（K 谱等价的筛判据）
