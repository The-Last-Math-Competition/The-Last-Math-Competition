# 00000004290

**English.** Definition: The direct-sum decomposition of an extension is the two-term analysis of Ext. Conjecture: There exists an extension whose decomposition interference term is independent in ZFC, and the minimal support cardinality of the interference term is aleph_1. (independence of extension interference terms)
