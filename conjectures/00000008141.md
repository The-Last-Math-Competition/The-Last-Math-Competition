# 00000008141

**RNG-01 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: space: Rf(x)=λ f²(λ^{-1}x) (λ of Feigenbaum–Coullet–Tresser renormalization operator R in interval map is factor). Conjecture: codimension of stable manifold of hyperbolic fixed point (Feigenbaum function g) of R is 1 (universal constant δ is unstable eigenvalue); and stable dimension spectrum δ_k of renormalization fixed point of high order period times satisfies topological entropy of δ_k = δ · (2^{k}-1)^{-1/2} (formula), all fixed point is zero (zero entropy fixed point).

**中文。** 定义：Feigenbaum–Coullet–Tresser 重整化算子 R 作用于单峰区间映射的空间：Rf(x)=λ f²(λ^{-1}x)（λ 为尺度因子）。猜想：R 的双曲不动点（Feigenbaum 函数 g）的稳定流形的余维为 1（普适常数 δ 为不稳定特征值）；且高阶周期倍增的重整化不动点的稳定维数谱 δ_k 满足 δ_k = δ·(2^{k}-1)^{-1/2} 型递降（递降公式），一切不动点的拓扑熵为零（零熵不动点律）。
