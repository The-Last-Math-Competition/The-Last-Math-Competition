# 00000008141

**RNG-01 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The Feigenbaum-Coullet-Tresser renormalization operator R acting on the space of unimodal interval maps: Rf(x) = lambda f^2(lambda^{-1} x) (lambda the scaling factor). Conjecture: The stable manifold of the hyperbolic fixed point of R (the Feigenbaum function g) has codimension 1 (the universal constant delta is the unstable eigenvalue); the stable dimension spectra delta_k of renormalization fixed points of higher-order period doubling satisfy the descending formula delta_k = delta (2^k - 1)^{-1/2}; and the topological entropy of all fixed points is zero (a zero-entropy fixed-point law). (Feigenbaum codimension-one descending spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Feigenbaum–Coullet–Tresser 重整化算子 R 作用于单峰区间映射的空间：Rf(x)=λ f²(λ^{-1}x)（λ 为尺度因子）。猜想：R 的双曲不动点（Feigenbaum 函数 g）的稳定流形的余维为 1（普适常数 δ 为不稳定特征值）；且高阶周期倍增的重整化不动点的稳定维数谱 δ_k 满足 δ_k = δ·(2^{k}-1)^{-1/2} 型递降（递降公式），一切不动点的拓扑熵为零（零熵不动点律）。
