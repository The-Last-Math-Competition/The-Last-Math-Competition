**English.** Definition: The combinatorial symmetry group of polytopes with automorphism counting. Conjecture: The geometric realization of combinatorial symmetries is equivalent, and the obstruction of the equivalence is the affine embedding of the symmetry. (affine-embedding obstruction to combinatorial symmetry)
