**GA3-09 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: A pseudoforest is a graph each of whose components contains at most one cycle; the pseudoarboricity is the minimal number of pseudoforests covering all edges. Conjecture: The fractional pseudoarboricity has a complete solution: its value is the halved correction of the ceiling of the maximal edge density; the minimal overlap vertex count of pseudoforest decompositions equals the rank deficit of the cycle space, with uniqueness of decomposition characterized by the basis-choice lattice. (fractional pseudoarboricity formula)

**中文。** 定义：伪林为每个连通分支至多含一个圈的图，伪林数为覆盖全部边所需伪林的最少个数。猜想：分数伪林数有完全解：其值为最大边密度上取整的减半修正；伪林分解的最小重叠顶点数等于圈空间的秩亏且分解的唯一性由基底选择格刻画。（分数伪林公式）
