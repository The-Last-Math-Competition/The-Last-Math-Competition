**English.** Definition: Dense sum-product for dense sets. Conjecture: The sum-product of dense sets holds, the combination of the product being the density deficit. (density-deficit dense sum-product)

**中文。** 定义：稠密的和积：稠密集合。猜想：稠密的和积：稠密集的和积且积的组合为稠密性亏。（稠密和积稠密集稠密性亏组合稠密性亏和积稠密集）
