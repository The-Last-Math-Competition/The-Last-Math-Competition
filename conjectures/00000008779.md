# 00000008779

**English.** Definition: Randomized least squares (LSQR): Blendenpik-type solvers. Conjecture: The expected iteration count of Blendenpik is O(log(1/epsilon)) (a logarithmic law); the logarithmic constant of the sketch distribution is 2 (a two-constant law); the quality of the preconditioner (spectral concentration) is 1 + epsilon (a spectral concentration law); and the failure probability of concentration is exponential (an exponential law). (Blendenpik logarithmic spectral concentration)

**中文。** 定义：随机最小二乘（LSQR）：Blendenpik 型求解器。猜想：Blendenpik 的迭代期望 O(log(1/ε)) 的（对数律）；且对数的常数（sketch 分布的）为 2 的（二常数律）；预条件子的质量（谱的集中）为 1+ε（谱集中律）；集中的失败概率的指数（指数律）。
