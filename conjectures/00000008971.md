**English.** Definition: Hankel determinants: moment determinants. Conjecture: Their conversion with orthogonal polynomials is always an explicit double product; and the double duality is realized by the Heine formula. (Hankel-Heine double product duality)

**中文。** 定义：Hankel 行列式指矩量行列式。猜想：其与正交多项式的换算恒为显式双积；且双对偶由 Heine 公式实现。（Hankel-Heine 双积对偶）
