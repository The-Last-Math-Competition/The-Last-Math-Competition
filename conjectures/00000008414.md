**DFG-04 ◆** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: ovoid of PG(3,q) denotes q²+1 point set of without three line. Conjecture: completely classification of ovoid is elliptic quadratic surface and q is Suzuki–Tits ovoid; q of power of 2 is prime power when all ovoid is elliptic quadratic surface. (projective space ovoid completely classification)

**中文。** 定义：PG(3,q) 的 ovoid 指无三共线的 q²+1 点集。猜想：ovoid 的完全分类为椭圆二次曲面与 q 为 2 的幂的 Suzuki–Tits ovoid；q 为奇素数幂时一切 ovoid 为椭圆二次曲面。（射影空间 ovoid 完全分类）
