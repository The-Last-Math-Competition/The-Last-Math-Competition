**DFG-04 ◆** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: An ovoid of PG(3,q) is a set of q^2+1 points with no three collinear. Conjecture: The complete classification of ovoids consists of elliptic quadrics and the Suzuki-Tits ovoids when q is a power of 2; and when q is an odd prime power every ovoid is an elliptic quadric. (complete classification of projective space ovoids)

**中文。** 定义：PG(3,q) 的 ovoid 指无三共线的 q²+1 点集。猜想：ovoid 的完全分类为椭圆二次曲面与 q 为 2 的幂的 Suzuki–Tits ovoid；q 为奇素数幂时一切 ovoid 为椭圆二次曲面。（射影空间 ovoid 完全分类）
