**English.** Definition: Entanglement witnesses: detection by observable inequalities. Conjecture: Optimization of witnesses: the maximal detection domain of witness families is the family of tangent planes of the convex body, with the choice of tangent planes the dual solution of convex optimization. (witness maximal detection dual solution)

**中文。** 定义：纠缠见证：可观测不等式的探测。猜想：见证的最优化：见证族的极大探测域为凸体的切面族且切面的选择为凸优化的对偶解。（见证极大探测对偶解）
