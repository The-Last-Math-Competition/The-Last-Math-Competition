# 00000007966

**English.** Definition: Extremes of rank-metric codes: the solvability index of the automorphism groups of MRD codes (rank distance attaining the Singleton bound). Conjecture: Non-Gabidulin MRD codes (with nontrivial automorphisms) under factorization of the extension degree m exist only when m is composite (for prime m every MRD code is Gabidulin type, MRD rigidity); and the F_q-dimension of the linear automorphism group of every MRD code is at most an m^2 phi(m)/m type upper bound, with equality attained by the semilinear group of Gabidulin codes. (MRD Gabidulin rigidity bound)

**中文。** 定义：rank-metric 码的极值：MRD 码（秩距离达到 Singleton 界）的自同构群的可解性指标。猜想：非 Gabidulin 型的 MRD 码（非平凡自同构）在扩域次数 m 的因子分解下仅当 m 为合数时存在（m 为素数时 MRD 必为 Gabidulin 型，MRD 刚性）；且一切 MRD 码的线性自同构群的 F_q-维数 ≤ m²·φ(m)/m 型上界，等号由 Gabidulin 的半线性群取得。
