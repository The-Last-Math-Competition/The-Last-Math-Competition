# 00000007801

**ACG-21 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: k dimension volume mean value G_k(K) of vertex set constitutes of explicit: d dimension convex body K of Godbersen vertex satisfies G_k(K) ≤ c_{d,k} vol(K). Conjecture: affine not exactly in of optimal constant c_{d,k}=d!/((d-k)! · k!) · vol()/vol(K) attains, bound is G_k(K) ≤ (d choose k) · V(S) (S is standard); and stability error O(ε^{1/2}) of middle dimension k=⌊d/2⌋ by concentration controls of simplicial approximation.

**中文。** 定义：Godbersen 顶点猜测的显式版：d 维凸体 K 的顶点集构成的 k 维单形体积均值 G_k(K) 满足 G_k(K) ≤ c_{d,k} vol(K)。猜想：最优常数 c_{d,k}=d!/((d-k)!·k!)·vol(单形)/vol(K) 的仿射不变比值恰在单纯形取得，界为 G_k(K) ≤ (d choose k)·V(S)（S 为标准单形）；且中间维 k=⌊d/2⌋ 的稳定性误差带 O(ε^{1/2}) 由 simplicial approximation 的棱长集中控制。
