# 00000007801

**ACG-21 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The explicit form of the Godbersen vertex conjecture: the mean volume G_k(K) of the k-dimensional simplices spanned by vertices of a d-dimensional convex body K should satisfy G_k(K) <= c_{d,k} vol(K). Conjecture: The optimal constant c_{d,k}=d!/((d-k)!k!) vol(simplex)/vol(K), an affine-invariant ratio, is attained exactly at the simplex, so G_k(K) <= (d choose k) V(S) with S the standard simplex; and at the middle dimension k=floor(d/2) the stability error band O(epsilon^{1/2}) is controlled by edge-length concentration of simplicial approximation. (Godbersen simplex-optimal vertex volumes)

**中文。** 定义：Godbersen 顶点猜测的显式版：d 维凸体 K 的顶点集构成的 k 维单形体积均值 G_k(K) 满足 G_k(K) ≤ c_{d,k} vol(K)。猜想：最优常数 c_{d,k}=d!/((d-k)!·k!)·vol(单形)/vol(K) 的仿射不变比值恰在单纯形取得，界为 G_k(K) ≤ (d choose k)·V(S)（S 为标准单形）；且中间维 k=⌊d/2⌋ 的稳定性误差带 O(ε^{1/2}) 由 simplicial approximation 的棱长集中控制。
