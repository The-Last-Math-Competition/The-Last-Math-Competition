# 00000006277

**English.** Definition: Complete unpredictability is the equivalent characterization of positive-entropy ergodic structures, the name-prediction deficit. Conjecture: The explicit limit of the deficit order under refinement exists, the combination of the limit being partition refinement; the diameter of the refinement tends to zero at exponential speed; and the prediction success rate converges to one half. (refinement limit of the prediction deficit)

**中文。** 定义：完全不可预测：正熵遍历结构的等价刻画（名预测亏格）。猜想：亏格阶的显式细分极限且极限的组合为分割细化；细化的直径趋于零且零速为指数型；预测的成功率收敛于二分之一。（亏格细分分割细化极限）
