# 00000002077

**UA-27 ★** — source: 54_泛代数与范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: A 3-cocycle of a monoidal bicategory is a garland cohomology class of double categories. Conjecture: Its torsion classes admit an explicit classification by Picard 2-groups (the group being the categorification of the Picard group of the category); the classification table is exhaustive in the braided case.

**中文。** 定义：monoidal 双范畴的 3-cocycle 指双重范畴的带上同调类。猜想：其括张类为 Picard 2-群的显式分类(群为范畴的 Picard 群的范畴化);分类表对 braided 情形穷尽。
