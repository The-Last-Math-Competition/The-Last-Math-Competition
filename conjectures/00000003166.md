**CS2-06 ★** — source: 91_稀疏化与压缩感知的数学.md

**English.** Definition: Graph sparsification: spectral sparse approximations of graphs. Conjecture: The existence of Ramanujan sparsifiers: every graph has spectral sparsifiers of degree d that are near-Ramanujan, with the cost of sparsification the minimax of reweighting. (Ramanujan sparsification cost)

**中文。** 定义：图稀疏化：图的谱稀疏近似。猜想：Ramanujan 稀疏化的存在：一切图的谱稀疏化为度数 d 的近 Ramanujan 且稀疏化的代价为重加权的最小最大。（Ramanujan 稀疏化代价）
