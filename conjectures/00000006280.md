# 00000006280

**English.** Definition: Entropy invariants and spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two systems with completely identical entropy invariants but non-isomorphic spectra, and the separation is realized by weakly mixing ladders with the same entropy but different spectra. (same entropy invariant-different spectrum ladder separation)

**中文。** 定义：熵不变量与谱指两层。猜想：存在两系统的熵不变量完全相同而谱不同构,分离由同熵异谱的弱混合阶梯实现。（熵不变量同-谱异阶梯分离）
