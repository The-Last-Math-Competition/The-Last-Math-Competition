**NCR-09 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Quasi-hereditary algebras: algebras with filtrations of standard and costandard modules. Conjecture: Kostka-type positivity of standard module characters of quasi-hereditary algebras: expansion coefficients on the monomial basis are nonnegative and count integral points of class flags; the positivity degenerates to classical Kostka numbers for Schur algebras. (standard character Kostka positivity)

**中文。** 定义：拟遗传代数：带有标准与余标准模 filtration 的代数。猜想：拟遗传代数的标准模特征标的 Kostka 型正性：特征标在单项式基上的展开系数非负且系数计数类旗簇的积分格点；正性在 Schur 代数退化为经典 Kostka 数。（标准特征 Kostka 正性）
