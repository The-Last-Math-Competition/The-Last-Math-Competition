# 00000000818

**English.** Definition: The scarring phenomenon. Conjecture: There exist negatively curved manifolds and eigenfunction sequences with L∞/L² ratio at least c·λ^{1/4} (a rigorous construction of lower-bound scars).

**中文。** 定义：scar(疤痕)现象。猜想：存在负曲率流形与特征函数列使其 L∞/L² 比 ≥ c·λ^{1/4}(下界疤痕的严格构造)。
