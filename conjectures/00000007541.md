**English.** Definition: Free group factors as the weak closure of group algebras. Conjecture: The factor-classification spectrum is open at the isomorphism problem; the domain of the classification is core-amalgam free decomposition; and the domain of core-amalgam free decomposition consists of the two types of amalgamated products. (open classification spectrum of free factors)

**中文。** 定义：自由群因子为群代数的弱闭包。猜想：因子分类谱在同构问题处开放；且分类的域为核并自由分解；且核并自由分解的域为混合积的两类。（自由群因子分类谱开放）
