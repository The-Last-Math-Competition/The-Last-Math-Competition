# 00000002591

**English.** Definition: The Dedekind–MacNeille completion DM(P) is the smallest complete extension of a poset P preserving all existing joins and meets. Conjecture: The size law of MacNeille completions: |DM(P)| ≤ 2^{|P|} with the upper bound attained by unbounded dense antichains; the isomorphism types of P attaining the bound are superpolynomial in number, with the minimal generator characterization by three-chain cross products. (DM completion size law)

**中文。** 定义：Dedekind–MacNeille 完备化：偏序集 P 的最小完备扩张 DM(P)，保持一切现存并交。猜想：MacNeille 完备化的尺寸律：|DM(P)| ≤ 2^{|P|} 且上界由无界稠密反偏序达到；达到上界的 P 的同构类型数为超多项式且最小生成元刻画为三链交叉积。（DM 完备化尺寸律）
