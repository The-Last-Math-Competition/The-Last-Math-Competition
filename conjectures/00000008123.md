# 00000008123

**NAG-13 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Blanchard vanishing of noncommutative varieties: the vanishing index bl(A) of the noncommutative Rosinger duality of derived pushforwards. Conjecture: The bl vanishing (compatible vanishing of higher direct images) holds if and only if the leading coefficients of the Hilbert polynomials of the nc fibers match (a polynomial matching criterion); the exceptions to vanishing are given by twist moves of base change of the quantum family (a twist exception law); and the verifiability of vanishing (an algorithmic criterion) has polynomial Grobner basis complexity (a vanishing-GB complexity). (Blanchard polynomial matching Grobner criterion)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的 Blanchard 消没：导出 pushforward 的非交换 Rosinger 对偶的消没指标 bl(A)。猜想：bl 消没（高直像的相容消没）当且仅当 nc 纤维的 Hilbert 多项式的首项系数的匹配（多项式匹配判据）；且消没的例外由量子家的基变换的 twist 移动给出（twist 例外律）；消没的可验证性（算法判据）为 Grobner 基的多项式复杂度（消没-GB 复杂度）。
