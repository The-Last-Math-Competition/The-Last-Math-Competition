# 00000008495

**ERO-25 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: The dependence spectrum of the Hausdorff dimension of frozen measures on the observable function. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum (over all Holder observables) is the full image of a closed interval (a full image law); every dimension value is realized by some Holder observable (a realization law); the dependence of dimension on continuous deformations of the observable is lower semicontinuous (a lower semicontinuity law); and the jump size at discontinuity points is 1/2 (a half-jump law). (frozen dimension realization semicontinuous half-jump)

**中文。** 定义：冻结测度的 Hausdorff 维数关于观测函数的依赖谱。猜想：维数谱（遍历一切 Hölder 观测的取值）为闭区间全像（全像律）；每个维数值由某 Hölder 观测实现（实现律）；维数对观测的连续形变的依赖为下半连续（下半连续律）；依赖的断点跳变量为 1/2（半跳律）。
