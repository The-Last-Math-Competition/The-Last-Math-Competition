# 00000004735

**English.** Definition: Morita trivialization is the algebraic untwisting of twisted sheaves via an Azumaya algebra. Conjecture: In the quadratic case the trivialization conversion is the Clifford model, and the conversion is functorial under quadratic covers and invertible. (Clifford conversion of Morita trivialization)

**中文。** 定义：Morita 平凡化指扭曲层经 Azumaya 代数的代数消扭。猜想：二次情形的平凡化换算为 Clifford 模型,换算在二次覆叠下函子化且可逆。（Morita 平凡化 Clifford 换算）
