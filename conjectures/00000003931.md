# 00000003931

**FL-21 ◆** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: The Chekanov-Eliashberg DGA is the enhanced differential graded algebra of a Legendrian, and a stable tame isomorphism is an isomorphism preserving the class of generators. Conjecture: The DGA isomorphism classes of compact Legendrians on the (m,3)-torus are completely classified by m together with the pair of rotation numbers, and new stable tame classes appear once m >= 7. (complete classification of torus DGAs)

**中文。** 定义：Chekanov–Eliashberg DGA 指 Legendrian 的增强微分分次代数,stable tame 同构指其保持生成元的同构类。猜想：(m,3)-环面上紧 Legendrian 的 DGA 同构类由 m 与扭转数二元组完全分类;m ≥ 7 时出现新的 stable tame 类。（环面 DGA 完全分类）
