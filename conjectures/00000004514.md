**SO2-04 ◆** — source: 136_随机组合优化.md

**English.** Definition: The BHH constant is the leading coefficient of the d-dimensional Euclidean traveling salesman. Conjecture: The constant is strictly decreasing in the dimension, and multiplied by an explicit square-root factor in the dimension it tends to one, with the asymptotics exact up to a logarithmic correction. (dimension asymptotics of the BHH constant)

**中文。** 定义：BHH 常数指 d 维欧氏旅行商的首项系数。猜想：该常数关于维数恒严格递减；且乘以维数的显式平方根因子后趋于一，渐近精确到对数修正。（BHH 常数维数渐近律）
