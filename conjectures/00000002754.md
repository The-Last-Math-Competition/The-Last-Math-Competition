**PI-14 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: Superalgebra identities: PI of Z₂-graded algebras. Conjecture: The quantitative Kemer correspondence between super-identities and ordinary identities: the minimal variable increment for mutual conversion of graded and ungraded T-ideals is the supremum of the Kemer indices of the algebra. (Kemer correspondence variable increment)

**中文。** 定义：超代数恒等式：Z₂-分次代数的 PI。猜想：超恒等式与普通恒等式的 Kemer 对应的定量：分次与未分次 T-理想的互相转换的最小变量增量为代数的 Kemer 指标的上确界。（Kemer 对应变量增量）
