# 00000002736

**SC-26 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Endocollapsibility of simplicial complexes: collapsing from the ambient complex. Conjecture: The genus of endo-collapsible manifolds: the minimal number of collapsing steps is a linear function of the manifold genus with linear coefficient the reciprocal of the dimension; the genus doubles under double suspension. (endo-collapse genus linear law)

**中文。** 定义：单纯复形的 endocollapsibility：从外围复合形的塌缩。猜想：endo-可塌缩流形的亏格：塌缩的极小步数为流形亏格的线性函数且线性系数为维数的倒数；亏格在双悬浮下翻倍。（endo-塌缩亏格线性律）
