**English.** Definition: Demazure operators are difference operators on crystals. Conjecture: The maximal length of a Demazure chain equals the length of the longest element; and the extremal length is realized by the tail chain of a reduced word. (Demazure extremal-length chain)

**中文。** 定义：Demazure 算子为晶体上的差分算子。猜想：Demazure 链的极大长度等于最长元的长度；且极长的实现由既约字的尾链给出。（Demazure 极长链）
