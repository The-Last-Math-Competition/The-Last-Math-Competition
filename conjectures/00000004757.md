**English.** Definition: Asymptotic freeness means convergence of mixed moments to free moments. Conjecture: The moment convergence rate of independent unitarily invariant models is of explicit reciprocal-square-of-dimension type, the spectral correction is a linear expression in the reciprocal dimension, and the two are consistent across both model classes. (reciprocal-square rate of moment convergence)

**中文。** 定义：渐近自由指混合矩收敛于自由矩。猜想：独立酉不变模型的矩收敛速率为维数平方倒数型显式,谱修正为维数倒数的线性式且两类模型一致。（矩收敛平方倒数速率）
