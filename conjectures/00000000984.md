**OA-84 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: Commutators of Hankel and Toeplitz operators characterize the regularity of symbols. Conjecture: The Schatten norm of the commutator is two-sidedly controlled by the Besov norm of the symbol product; the two-sided constant is exact in the antisymmetric case; and the two-sided control order matches exactly when the Besov norm equals one. (commutator Besov two-sided bound)

**中文。** 定义：Hankel 与 Toeplitz 交换子刻画符号的正则性。猜想：交换子的 Schatten 范数由符号乘积的 Besov 范数双向控制；且双向常数在 Antisymmetric 情形精确；且双向控制的量级在 Besov 范数为一时精确匹配。（交换子 Besov 双向）
