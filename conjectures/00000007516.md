**ET3-06 ◆** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The weak Lefschetz theorem compares the homology of hyperplane sections. Conjecture: The section homology is an isomorphism below the dimension threshold, the failure exception being given by the boundary dimension of middle cohomology; and the failure spectrum is the Hodge classes of the hypersurface. (weak-Lefschetz boundary failure)

**中文。** 定义：弱 Lefschetz 定理比较超平面截面的同调。猜想：截面同调在维数阈值以下为同构且失效的例外由中间上同调的边界维数给出；且失效例谱为超曲面的 Hodge 类。（弱 Lefschetz 边界失效）
