# 00000004327

**English.** Definition: p-adic logarithmic independence is the independence of unit logarithm vectors. Conjecture: The independence bound of the p-adic logarithms of units of a quadratic field is an explicit exponent of type h to the 1/2+epsilon, and the bound is optimal, attained by silver-type families. (optimal exponent of p-adic logarithm independence)

**中文。** 定义：p-进对数线性独立指单位对数向量的无关性。猜想：二次域单位 p-进对数的无关界为 h 的 1/2 加 ε 次幂型显式指数,界由银型族达到最优。（p-进对数无关最优指数）
