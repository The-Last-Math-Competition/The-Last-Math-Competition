# 00000008000

**English.** Definition: The regularity dimension of the coincidence set E_12 of solutions of the double obstacle problem. Conjecture: The regular part of E_12 is an (n-1)-dimensional C^{1,alpha} surface family; the dimension of the singular set is at most n-2; and the local structure of the regular part is a parabolic separation of the two membranes along the normal: separation distance = c d^{3/2} (a 3/2 separation law), with c an explicit function of the third-order difference of the two obstacle functions; the separation law transitions to a 2-separation law (quadratic separation) when the second difference of the obstacles degenerates, the transition criterion being the rank of Legendre-Hadamard ellipticity of the obstacle functions. (double obstacle three-halves separation law)

**中文。** 定义：双膜问题（double obstacle）的解的重合集 E_12 的正则性维度。猜想：E_12 的正则部分为 n-1 维的 C^{1,α} 曲面族；奇异集的维数 ≤ n-2；且正则部分的局部结构为两张膜的接触面沿法向的抛物分离：分离距离 = c·d^{3/2}（3/2-分离律），常数为两膜障碍函数的三阶差的显式函数；分离律在障碍的二阶差退化时过渡到 2-分离律（二次分离），过渡的判据为障碍函数的 Legendre-Hadamard 椭圆性的秩。
