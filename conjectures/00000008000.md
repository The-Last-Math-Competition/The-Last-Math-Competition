**English.** Definition: regularity dimension of set E_12 of solution of problem (double obstacle). Conjecture: regular part of E_12 is dimension ≤ n-2; of C^{1,α} surface family; singular set of n-1 dimension and local structure of regular part is parabolic: distance = c · d^{3/2} (3/2-), constant of plane normal to of two is two order difference degenerate of 2- (quadratic), of explicit function; in obstacle of three order difference of two barrier function is rank of Legendre-Hadamard elliptic of barrier function.

**中文。** 定义：双膜问题（double obstacle）的解的重合集 E_12 的正则性维度。猜想：E_12 的正则部分为 n-1 维的 C^{1,α} 曲面族；奇异集的维数 ≤ n-2；且正则部分的局部结构为两张膜的接触面沿法向的抛物分离：分离距离 = c·d^{3/2}（3/2-分离律），常数为两膜障碍函数的三阶差的显式函数；分离律在障碍的二阶差退化时过渡到 2-分离律（二次分离），过渡的判据为障碍函数的 Legendre-Hadamard 椭圆性的秩。
