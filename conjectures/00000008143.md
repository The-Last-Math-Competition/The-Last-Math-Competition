# 00000008143

**RNG-03 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral stability of block-spin renormalization: the decay rate dec(b) of the transformed couplings under the block transformation T_b (b-block summing and compression) on the hypercubic lattice Z^d. Conjecture: dec(b) = b^{-d-y_tau} (a spectral decay of the tau exponent); the lattice constant c(d) in the proof of the stability of the decay (fixed-point invariance) is an explicit rational number (a rational lattice-constant law), with c(4) = 1/16 (a four-dimensional correction); and c(d) tends to 1 in the high-dimensional limit (high-dimensional recovery), at recovery rate 1/d^2 (a square recovery law). (block-spin spectral decay rational constant)

**中文。** 定义：块自旋重整化的谱稳定性：超立方格 Z^d 的块变换 T_b（b-块求和再压缩）的变换后耦合的衰减率 dec(b)。猜想：dec(b) = b^{-d-y_τ}（τ 指数的谱衰减）；且衰减的稳定性（不动点不动性）的证明的格子常数 c(d) 为显式有理数（格常数有理律），c(4)=1/16（四维修正）；格常数的渐近 c(d)→1（高维极限恢复），恢复的速率 1/d²（平方恢复律）。
