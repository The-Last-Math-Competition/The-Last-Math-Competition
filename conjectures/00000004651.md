# 00000004651

**English.** Definition: A Rademacher sum is the exact approximation series of modular coefficients. Conjecture: The leading Rademacher constant of moonshine coefficients is an explicit Gamma factor of the exponent, the constant is verified on the leading coefficient of the j function, and the residual exponent is small. (Gamma factor of the Rademacher leading constant)

**中文。** 定义：Rademacher 和指模系数的精确逼近级数。猜想：moonshine 系数的 Rademacher 渐近主常数为指数的显式 Gamma 因子,常数在 j 函数首系数验证且残差指数小。（Rademacher 主常数 Gamma 因子）
