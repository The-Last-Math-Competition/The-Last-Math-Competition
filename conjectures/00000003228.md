**English.** Definition: The Ten Martini problem: the universality of Cantor spectra. Conjecture: The rigidity of Cantor spectra: the AMO spectrum for nonperiodic Diophantine frequencies is Cantor, with the proof the analytic duality of Aubry duality. (Ten Martini Aubry duality rigidity)
