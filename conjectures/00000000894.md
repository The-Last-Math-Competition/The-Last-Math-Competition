**PV-94 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The Lamb vector l = u × ω of incompressible fluids. Conjecture: An integral identity for the divergence of l yields the equality between the variational derivative of helicity and the vortex knot energy (a helicity-energy bridge).

**中文。** 定义：不可压流体的 Lamb 向量 l = u × ω。猜想：l 的散度的积分恒等式给出 helicity 的变分导数与涡旋纽结能量的等式(helicity-energy 桥)。
