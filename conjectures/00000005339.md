# 00000005339

**English.** Definition: The coloring stability domain is the stable region as the coloring grows. Conjecture: The stability domain of symmetric-square coloring is given explicitly by a linear family of grading shifts, with slope two and starting point a function of the crossing number. (linear stability family of symmetric-square colorings)

**中文。** 定义：染色稳定域指染色趋于大的稳定区。猜想：对称平方染色的稳定域由分次移位的线性族显式给出,斜率为二且起点为交叉数的函数。（对称平方染色稳定线性族）
