**GL3-04 ★** — source: 155_几何Langlands的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The Schubert dimension is the homological dimension of Schubert pieces. Conjecture: The dimension table of the second layer of GL_3 is always given by the combination of affine Weyl group length and weight depth, closed within depth two. (second-layer Schubert dimension table of GL_3)

**中文。** 定义：舒伯特维数指舒伯特块的同调维数。猜想：GL₃ 第二层的维数表恒由仿射外尔群长度与权深度的组合给出；且深度二内封闭。（GL3 层二舒伯特维数表）
