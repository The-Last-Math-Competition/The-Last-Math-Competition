# 00000002556

**English.** Definition: Garnett's question: the comparability of analytic capacity and length for 1-dimensional sets. Conjecture: Analytic capacity and H¹ are comparable on AD-regular sets; for non-regular sets the ratio's infimum and supremum separate: the simultaneous realization of infinite supremum and zero infimum is on Ivanov-type two-way snowflakes. (Garnett ratio two extremes)

**中文。** 定义：Garnitt 之问：1 维集的解析容量与长度的可比性。猜想：解析容量与 H¹ 在 AD 规则集上可比；非规则集的比值上下确界分离：上确界无穷且下确界为零的同时实现集为 Ivanov 型双向雪花。（Garnett 比值双极值）
