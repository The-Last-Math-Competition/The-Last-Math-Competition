**English.** Definition: The nullity of the signless Laplacian and bipartiteness. Conjecture: For general graphs, the dimension of the zero eigenspace is the count of bipartite components; and the kernel of the count is an arbitrary bipartite base graph. (signless arbitrary bipartite base zero-dimension count)

**中文。** 定义：signless Laplacian 零重数与二部性。猜想：一般图的零特征空间维数为分支二部性计数；且计数的核为任意二部母图。（signless 任意二部母图零维计数）
