**GR-08 ◆** — source: 03_图论与离散结构.md

**English.** Definition: R(3,k) is the Ramsey number of three versus k. Conjecture: R(3,k) is asymptotically k²/(4 log k); moreover, the leading constant of this asymptotic matches the random-graph lower bound. (R3k logarithmic denominator asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：R(3,k) 为三与 k 的 Ramsey 数。猜想：R(3,k) 渐近等于 k² 除以四倍 log k；且该渐近的主项常数与随机图下界匹配。（R3k 对数分母渐近）
