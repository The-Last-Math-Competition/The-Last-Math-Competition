**OD4-29 ★** — source: 231_数值积分_常微II.md

**English.** Definition: Affine covariance with the geometric invariants of numerics. Conjecture: The covariance of Newton methods holds, and the algebra of the covariance is the tangent-space model. (tangent-modeled affine-covariant Newton)

**中文。** 定义：仿射协变：数值的几何不变量。猜想：仿射协变 Newton：方法的协变性且性的代数为切空间模型。（仿射协变切空间模型 Newton 方法协变性数值几何不变量代数）
