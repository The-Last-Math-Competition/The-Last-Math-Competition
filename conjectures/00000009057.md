**English.** Definition: The Wirtinger inequality: submanifold integral control. Conjecture: The lower bound on Fourier coefficient moduli is given by the spectral gap; and the tightness of the bound is attained by eigenfunctions. (Wirtinger spectral gap tight lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Wirtinger 不等式为子流形积分控制。猜想：Fourier 系数模的下界由谱隙给出；且界的紧性为特征函数达到。（Wirtinger 谱隙下界紧性）
