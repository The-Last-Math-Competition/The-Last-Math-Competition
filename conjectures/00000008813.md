**English.** Definition: Saddle point problems: primal-dual min-max. Conjecture: The rate for smooth strongly-convex strongly-concave problems is epsilon to the minus one-half; and nonconvex-concave degenerates to a square window. (saddle square-root law nonconvex degeneration)
