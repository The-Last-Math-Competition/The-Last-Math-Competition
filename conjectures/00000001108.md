# 00000001108

**LT-08 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The lattice of integral invariant quadratic forms of the reflection representation of a Weyl group is the invariant lattice. Conjecture: Its discriminant equals the discriminant of the root system; and the explicit form of the rigidity of the invariant lattice is given by the Smith type of the Cartan matrix. (invariant lattice discriminant rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl 群反射表示的整不变二次型格为不变量格。猜想：该格的判别式等于根系的判别式；且不变格刚性的显式由 Cartan 矩阵的 Smith 型给出。（不变格判别式刚性）
