# 00000007246

**VR4-06 ★** — source: 227_变分法II.md

**English.** Definition: The index theory of the calculus of variations with the second variation. Conjecture: The Morse index of nondegenerate critical points is the multiplicity sum, and the finiteness of the index is the compact perturbation. (compact-perturbation Morse index finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：变分法的指标理论：二阶变分。猜想：Morse 指标：非退化临界点的指标的重数和为无穷维的指标且标的有限为紧扰动。（Morse 紧扰动非退化临界指标有限无穷维重数和）
