# 00000004285

**English.** Definition: n-free means every subset of size at most n is contained in a free pure subgroup. Conjecture: The smallest cardinality of a group that is n-free but not n+1-free is aleph with subscript n-1, the cardinal spectrum is strictly increasing, and the bound is optimal. (minimal cardinal spectrum of n-free groups)

**中文。** 定义：n-游离指每个不超过 n 的子集含于自由纯子群。猜想：n-游离而非 n+1 游离的群最小基数为 ℵ 的 n−1 次下标,基数谱严格递增且界最优。（n-游离最小基数谱）
