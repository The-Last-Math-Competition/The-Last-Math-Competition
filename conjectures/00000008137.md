# 00000008137

**English.** Definition: The noncommutative projectivization of Stafford's theorem for noncommutative varieties: the projective formula staff(A) for projective dimensions in qgr. Conjecture: The staff dimension is at most 1 (a Stafford projective bound); the criterion for dimension 0 (globally projective) is that the center is a curve (a curve-dimension-0 criterion); the tightness of the bound 1 is given by the indecomposability of the torsion-free classification of point modules of ncP^2 (an indecomposable tightness law); and the proof path is the sheafification of Stafford's 2-generator theorem (a 2-generator sheafification path). (Stafford projective curve indecomposable tightness)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的 Stafford 的定理的非交换射影化：qgr 的投射维数的射影公式 staff(A)。猜想：staff 维数 ≤ 1（Stafford 射影界）；且维数 0（整体投射）的判据为中心为曲线（曲线-维 0 判据）；界 1 的紧性由 ncP² 的点模的挠自由分类的不可分解性（不可分解紧律）给出；界的证明路径为 Stafford 的 2-生成定理的非交换 sheaf 化（2-生成 sheaf 化路径）。
