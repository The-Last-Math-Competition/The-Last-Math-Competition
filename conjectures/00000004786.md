# 00000004786

**English.** Definition: The K moduli space is the moduli space of stable Fanos. Conjecture: The boundary points of the two-dimensional K moduli are classified into two explicit degeneration chains, consisting of wall-type and cone-type degenerations, and the classification is completely closed. (two-chain classification of the K moduli boundary)

**中文。** 定义：K 模空间指稳定 Fano 的模空间。猜想：二维 K 模的边界点分类为两条显式退化链,链由壁型与锥型退化组成且分类完全封闭。（K 模边界双链分类）
