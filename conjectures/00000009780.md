# 00000009780

**English.** Definition: Finite-dimensional approximation of numerical ranges: convergence of k-dimensional truncations of W(A). Conjecture: The polynomially convex hull of the numerical range is approximated by Ando representations of compressions, with convergence a power law in dimension; the rate exponent is decided by the smoothness class of the range boundary, degenerating to logarithmic at corner points. (numerical range approximation rate)

**中文。** 定义：数值值域的有限维逼近：W(A) 的 k 维截断的收敛。猜想：数值值域的多项式凸包由压缩的 Ando 表示逼近且收敛速率为维数的幂律；速率指数由值域边界的光滑类别决定且角点情形降为对数。（数值域逼近速率）
