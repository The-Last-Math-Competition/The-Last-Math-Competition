**HY-18 ★** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Conjecture: For partially hyperbolic systems with uniformly hyperbolic skew products, the boundary of the sign region of the central Lyapunov exponent in parameter space is a fractal of explicit dimension (a central-exponent fractal boundary).

**中文。** 猜想：一致双斜积的部分双曲系统的中心 Lyapunov 的符号在参数域中的边界为分形显式维数(中心指数分形边界)。
