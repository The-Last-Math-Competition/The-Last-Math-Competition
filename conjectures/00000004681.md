**SG3-21 ★** — source: 141_交换代数IV_奇点理论.md

**English.** Definition: F-purity is the regularity-lifting property in positive characteristic. Conjecture: The F-purity criterion of four-dimensional hypersurfaces is an explicit positivity table of the coefficients of the characteristic polynomial, and the table agrees at large characteristic with the characteristic-zero purity criterion. (coefficient positivity table of four-dimensional F-purity)

**中文。** 定义：F-纯正指正特征下的正则性提升性质。猜想：四维超曲面的 F-纯正判据为特征多项式系数的显式正性表,表在大特征与特征零的纯正判据一致。（四维 F-纯正系数正性表）
