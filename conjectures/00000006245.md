**English.** Definition: The topos spectrum is the spectrum construction of ring objects, Spec in a topos. Conjecture: The inverse system of spectral points is given by point types and the combination of completeness is the local lattice; the kernel of the local lattice is the prime-ideal spectrum and its Zariski topology is constructive; and the limit of the inverse system is dual to affine schemes, the deviation of the duality being Stone duality. (local-lattice inverse system of spectral points)
