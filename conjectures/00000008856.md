# 00000008856

**MON-26 ★** — source: 280_单调算子与算子分裂.md

**English.** Definition: Long-time behavior of nonexpansive semigroups: convergence of Cesaro average orbits of nonexpansive map families on Hilbert space. Conjecture: Average orbits of nonexpansive semigroups always converge weakly, with limit the metric projection of the initial value onto the common fixed point set; linear convergence holds if and only if the semigroup satisfies an angular density condition, with rate constant the reciprocal of the regularity modulus; and strong convergence of asymptotically regular semigroups is equivalent to the fixed point set being a singleton or the space having the Kadec-Klee property. (nonexpansive semigroup Cesaro angular Kadec-Klee)

**中文。** 定义：非扩张半群的长时行为：Hilbert 空间上非扩张映射族的 Cesàro 平均轨道的收敛性。猜想：非扩张半群的平均轨道恒弱收敛且收敛落点为半群公共不动点集到初值的度量投影；线性收敛率当且仅当半群满足角的密集性条件且率常数等于正则性模的倒数；渐近正则半群的强收敛等价于不动点集为单点或空间具有 Kadec–Klee 性质。
