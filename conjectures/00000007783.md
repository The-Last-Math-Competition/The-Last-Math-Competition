# 00000007783

**ACG-03 △** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The thin-shell constant sigma_n is the supremum over isotropic convex bodies of the standard deviation of |X| for an isotropic uniform sample. Conjecture: sigma_n = Theta(n^{-1/4}), so the known upper-bound order cannot be improved; and the sigma values of balls and simplices are n^{-1/2} and c n^{-1/4}, all isotropic bodies interpolating between the two strictly monotonically, with interpolation law given by the volume-concentration exponent of a two-parameter random simplex family. (thin-shell interpolation law)

**中文。** 定义：薄壳常数 σ_n 定义为 |X|（均匀凸体迷向样点的范数）的标准差在一切迷向凸体上的上确界。猜想：σ_n = Θ(n^{-1/4})，即已知上界阶不可改进；且球与单纯形的 σ 分别为 n^{-1/2} 与 c n^{-1/4}，一切迷向体的薄壳常数夹在两者之间并以严格单调方式插值，插值律由双参数随机单纯形族的体积集中指数给出。
