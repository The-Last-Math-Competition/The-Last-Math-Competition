**English.** Definition: The tilting-cotilting duality is the reverse structure of categories. Conjecture: The commutative closure of the opposite quiver realizes the duality; the kernel pair of the commutation is the middle torsion; and the dimension of the commutative kernel is given by the generator count of the middle torsion. (opposite-quiver tilting-cotilting duality)

**中文。** 定义：倾斜余倾斜对偶为范畴的反向结构。猜想：反箭图的交换闭合实现对偶；且交换的核对为中间挠；且交换核的维数由中间挠的生成元数给出。（倾斜余倾斜对偶反箭图）
