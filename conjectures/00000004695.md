# 00000004695

**English.** Definition: The entropy structure is the recovery-sequence tower of entropy functionals. Conjecture: The entropy structure of a zero-dimensional system is lower semicontinuous if and only if the entropy map is upper semicontinuous for invariant measures, and the conversion is given explicitly by the uniform convergence of the recovery sequences. (equivalence conversion of entropy structure semicontinuity)

**中文。** 定义：熵结构指熵泛函的恢复序列塔。猜想：零维系统的熵结构下半连续当且仅当熵映射对不变测度上半连续,换算由恢复列的一致收敛显式给出。（熵结构半连续等价换算）
