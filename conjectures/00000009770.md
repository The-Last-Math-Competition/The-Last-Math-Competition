# 00000009770

**English.** Definition: Equality cases of the triangle inequality for trace norms: extremal structure of the Schatten one-norm. Conjecture: Equality in the trace-norm triangle inequality holds if and only if the polar measures of the two operators share support and phase; the equality set is an explicit family of exposed faces of the Schatten unit ball, each face supported by a trace functional. (trace norm equality classification)

**中文。** 定义：迹范数三角不等式的等号情形：Schatten 一范数的极值结构。猜想：迹范数三角不等式等号当且仅当两算子的极化测度同支撑同相位；等号集合为 Schatten 单位球的暴露面的显式族且每一暴露面由迹泛函支撑。（迹范数等号分类）
