# 00000001965

**English.** Definition: The ground-state energy e(ρ) of the hardcore Bose gas is the per-particle ground energy at density ρ. Conjecture: The second virial correction coefficient b₂ in the 3D low-density expansion has an explicit integral value: b₂ = 2π·(4π/3)^{1/3} (a Clausius-type integral over hard-sphere configuration space); the second coefficient is determined by this closed form.

**中文。** 定义：Hardcore 玻色气的基态能量 e(ρ) 指密度 ρ 的每粒子基态能。猜想：3D 低密度展开的第二维里修正系数 b₂ 的积分值显式:b₂ = 2π·(4π/3)^{1/3}(由硬球位形空间的 Clausius 型积分);第二项系数由该闭式确定。
