# 00000001918

**HR-18 ★** — source: 49_调和分析II.md

**English.** Definition: Projections of fractal measures (the L² dimension of projections). Conjecture: For measures on arbitrary one-dimensional sets, the exceptional directions where the almost-everywhere projection dimension differs from min(1, s) form an algebraic curve (exceptional projection directions).

**中文。** 定义：projections of fractal measures(射影的 L² 维数)。猜想：任意 1 维集上的测度的几乎处处的射影维数 = min(1, s) 的例外方向为代数曲线(射影例外方向)。
