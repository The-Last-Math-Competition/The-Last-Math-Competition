# 00000000086

**AL-04 ◆** — source: 06_代数表示与有限结构.md

**English.** Definition: The free Burnside group B(m,p) denotes the largest m-generated group satisfying x^p = 1. Conjecture: log|B(m,p)| is asymptotic to (m−1)²/2 · log p; and this asymptotic holds as p tends to infinity. (Burnside order asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：自由 Burnside 群 B(m,p) 指满足 x^p=1 的最大 m 生成群。猜想：log|B(m,p)| 渐近等于 (m−1)²/2 乘 log p；且该渐近在 p 趋于无穷时成立。（Burnside 阶渐近）
