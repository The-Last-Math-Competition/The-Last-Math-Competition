# 00000002559

**GZ-29 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Tube-neighborhood limit law: the convergence of |E_r|/r^{n−s}. Conjecture: The tube-volume ratio converges if and only if the s-dimensional density of E exists uniformly; the explicit constant of the convergence value is given by the spherical average over tube directions, and the divergent sets are of Furstenberg type. (tube neighborhood limit constant)

**中文。** 定义：管邻域极限律：|E_r|/r^{n-s} 的收敛性。猜想：管邻域体积比收敛当且仅当 E 的 s 维密度一致存在；收敛值的显式常数由管方向的球面测度平均给出且发散集为 Furstenberg 型。（管邻域极限常数）
