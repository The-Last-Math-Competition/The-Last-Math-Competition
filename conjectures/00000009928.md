**PHT-18 ★** — source: 316_持久同调II_统计与稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: Frechet means of persistence diagrams: existence and uniqueness of mean sets. Conjecture: The Frechet mean set of a persistence diagram measure is convex and unique for generic measures; the dimension collapse of the mean set happens exactly when the symmetry group of the measure is nontrivial. (Frechet mean convexity)

**中文。** 定义：持久图的 Fréchet 均值：均值集合的存在唯一。猜想：持久图测度的 Fréchet 均值集合为凸集且在一般测度下唯一；均值集合的维数坍缩恰在测度的对称群非平凡时发生。（Fréchet 均值凸性）
