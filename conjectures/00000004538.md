**English.** Definition: Zero-temperature Glauber dynamics is the local dynamics of monotone energy decrease. Conjecture: On random d-regular graphs its mixing time is of subexponential type, the exponential constant is controlled explicitly by the forbidden-graph gradient flow, and the upper and lower bounds match. (matching subexponential bounds of zero-temperature mixing)
