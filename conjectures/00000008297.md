# 00000008297

**English.** Definition: The staircase of capacities of symplectic embeddings: the dimension spectrum of the ratio cap_ratio of the Ekeland-Hofer-Zehnder cylinder capacity to the ball volume capacity. Conjecture: The value spectrum of cap_ratio (the EHZ-to-volume ratio) is a dense subset of (0,1) (a density law), and the complete description of the exact value domain at n = 4 is the full interval (a four-dimensional full spectrum law); the asymptotic limit of the dimension dependence of the ratio at large n is 1 (a large-dimension limit-one law); and the convergence rate to the limit is the square reciprocal 1/n^2 (a square reciprocal convergence law). (capacity ratio dense four-dimensional full spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：辛嵌入的包络（capacity）的阶梯：柱容量 c_EZ 与体积容量 c_B 的比 cap_ratio 的维数谱。猜想：cap_ratio（EZ 与体积的比）的值谱为介于 0 与 1 之间的稠密集（稠密律）且 n=4 的精确值域的完备刻画为该区间的全谱（四维全谱律）；且比的维数依赖（n 大）的渐近极限为 1（大维极限 1 律）；极限的收敛速率为 1/n² 的平方倒数（平方倒数收敛律）。
