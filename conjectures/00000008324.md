# 00000008324

**DIA-04 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal constant et_const of the reciprocity inequality of Erdos-Turan for uniform distribution. Conjecture: The optimal constant of et_const is the Kershner type 1 + (1/2) sum (a Kershner correction law); the saturation of the inequality (attaining sequences) is the Kronecker sequence (n alpha) with optimal alpha (an optimal alpha law), the continued fraction expansion of alpha being of Barbilian type (a Barbilian expansion law); and the asymptotic constant of the star discrepancy of the sequence matches the Schmidt lower bound (a Schmidt matching law). (Erdos-Turan Kershner Barbilian Schmidt matching)

**中文。** 定义：一致分布的 Erdős–Turán 的倒易不等式的最优常数 et_const。猜想：et_const（Erdős–Turán 界的常数的最优）= 1 + 1/2·Σ 的 Kershner 型（Kershner 修正律）；且不等式的饱和（达到的序列）为 Kronecker 序列 (nα) 的最优 α（最优 α 律），α 的连分数的展开为 Barbilian 型（Barbilian 展开律）；序列的 star-discrepancy 的渐近常数为 Schmidt 下界的匹配（Schmidt 匹配律）。
