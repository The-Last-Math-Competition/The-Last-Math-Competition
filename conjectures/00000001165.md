**English.** Definition: The critical exponent of a hyperbolic lattice is the exponential growth rate of orbit counting. Conjecture: The critical exponent of SU(1,n;Z[i]) equals an explicit ratio with the volume entropy; and the constant of the ratio is the leading coefficient of the adjoint representation. (critical exponent volume-entropy ratio)
