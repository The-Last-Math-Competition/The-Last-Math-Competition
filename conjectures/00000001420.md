**English.** Definition: Formality (a dg algebra homotopy equivalent to its cohomology). Conjecture: Beyond the degeneration of spectral sequences for compact Kähler formality, the converse: formality implies that the multiplicative structure of the rational minimal model is completely determined by H^*(X) (the converse of formality).
