# 00000002835

**AS-05 ★** — source: 80_代数统计与张量分解的几何.md

**English.** Definition: The algebraic side of matrix completion: deterministic conditions for low-rank matrix recovery. Conjecture: Deterministic completion: a rank-r matrix is uniquely recoverable on the support of graph G if and only if G contains an r-regular supported subgraph; the minimal observation count is the combinatorial correction of 2nr−r². (graph support unique recovery criterion)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵完备的代数侧：低秩矩阵恢复的确定性条件。猜想：确定性完备：秩 r 矩阵在图 G 的支撑上的唯一可恢复当且仅当 G 含 r-正则支撑子图；恢复的最小观测数为 2nr−r² 的组合修正。（图支撑唯一恢复判据）
