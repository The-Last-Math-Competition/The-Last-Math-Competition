# 00000009848

**English.** Definition: Weighted martingale inequalities: weighted versions of Burkholder-Davis-Gundy. Conjecture: The weighted BDG constant is controlled by the joint optimum of weight and convex function Phi; the optimum is attained at explicit stopping times of random walks, and the explicit formula for the constant is the integral of the conjugate of Phi. (weighted BDG joint optimality)

**中文。** 定义：加权鞅不等式：Burkholder–Davis–Gundy 的权版本。猜想：加权 BDG 常数由权与凸函数 Φ 的联合最优二元组控制；最优在随机游走的显式停止时刻达到且常数的显式公式为 Φ 的共轭的积分。（加权 BDG 联合最优）
