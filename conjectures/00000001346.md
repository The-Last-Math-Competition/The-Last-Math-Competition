**AN2-16 ★** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Conjecture: The gaps between sign changes of the Fourier coefficients a_n of GL₂ holomorphic cusp forms are O(√n·log n) with lower bound Ω(√n) (the order of sign-change gaps).

**中文。** 猜想：GL₂ 全纯尖形式的 Fourier 系数 a_n 的符号变化间隔为 O(√n·log n) 且下界 Ω(√n)(符号变化间隔的阶)。
