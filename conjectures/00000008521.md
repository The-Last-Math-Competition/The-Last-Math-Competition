# 00000008521

**SDG-21 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: The convergence domain of Mahler solutions of difference equations. Conjecture: The convergence radius of solutions of Mahler equations is given by the contraction ratio of the Mahler inverse; and the distribution of singularities on the boundary is a self-similar fractal. (self-similarity of Mahler convergence domains)

**中文。** 定义：差分方程的 Mahler 解的收敛域。猜想：Mahler 方程解的收敛半径由 Mahler 逆的收缩比给出；且边界上奇点的分布为自相似分形。（Mahler 收敛域自相似）
