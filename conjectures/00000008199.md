# 00000008199

**English.** Definition: The polynomial-time computation of ring class fields: the scale ringfield_c(D) of the coefficients of the generating polynomials of ring class fields. Conjecture: The digit count of the coefficients of ringfield_c is at most c |D|^{1/2} log|D| (a half-power digit law); the separation of the roots of the generating polynomial (the class polynomial H_D, minimal complex spacing) is at least e^{-c|D|^{1/2}} (an exponential separation law); the complexity of the search for the shortest representation of a prime p by the complex multiplication (CM) method (the smallest d in p = x^2 + d y^2) is polynomial (a CM polynomial law); and the practical threshold of the CM method (the maximal computable |D|) is 10^{15} (a quadrillion threshold law). (ring class field CM quadrillion threshold)

**中文。** 定义：多项式时间的环类域的计算：环类域的生成多项式的系数的规模 ringfield_c(D)。猜想：ringfield_c 的系数的位数 ≤ c·|D|^{1/2}·log|D|（半次位数律）；且生成的多项式（类多项式 H_D）的根的分离（复平面的最小间距）≥ e^{-c|D|^{1/2}}（指数分离律）；复乘（CM）方法的素数 p 的最短表示（p=x²+d·y² 的最小 d）的搜索的复杂度为多项式（CM 多项式律）；CM 方法的实际阈（可计算的 |D| 极大）为 10^{15}（千万亿阈律）。
