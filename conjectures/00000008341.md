**DIA-21 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The Besicovitch renormalization of the Jarnik fractal: the dimension spectrum jarnik_dim of the Besicovitch characterization of the measure functions of Jarnik sets. Conjecture: jarnik_dim (the Jarnik-Besicovitch dimension spectrum) is the conversion 2 - (w+1) (a conversion law), with the difference between the uniform and nonuniform versions being the half-difference 1/2 (a half-difference law); the cancellation of the half-difference (agreement of uniform and nonuniform) holds for monotone convex psi (a monotone convex agreement law); and for broken convexity (nonconvex psi), the lower bound on the dimension of the difference set is 1/2 (a half-dimension lower bound law). (Jarnik-Besicovitch half-difference convex agreement)

**中文。** 定义：分形的 Jarnik 的 Besicovitch 的重整：Jarnik 集的测度函数的 Besicovitch 刻画的维数谱 jarnik_dim。猜想：jarnik_dim（Jarnik–Besicovitch 的维数谱）= 2-(w+1) 的换算（换算律）在一致（均匀）与非一致（非均匀）的两个版本的差为 1/2 的半差（半差律）；且半差的消去（一致与非一致的重合）在 ψ 的单调凸（单调凸重合律）；凸的破缺（非凸 ψ）的差集的维数的下界为 1/2（半维下界律）。
