# 00000008719

**MBL-09 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: The strong disorder renormalization group (SDRG): a stochastic real-space RG eliminating levels one by one. Conjecture: The infinite-randomness fixed point of SDRG is rigorously constructed on one-dimensional random chains (a construction law); the fixed-point exponent is the Fisher exponent (a Fisher law); the exponent correction for high-dimensional random systems is logarithmic (a logarithmic correction law); and finite-size exponent convergence carries log corrections (a log convergence law). (SDRG infinite-randomness Fisher logarithm)

**中文。** 定义：强无序重整化群（SDRG）：逐能级消元的随机实空间 RG。猜想：SDRG 的无限随机不动点在一维随机链严格构造（构造律）；不动点指数为 Fisher 指数（Fisher 律）；高维随机系统的指数修正为对数型（对数修正律）；有限尺寸的指数收敛含 log 修正（log 收敛律）。
