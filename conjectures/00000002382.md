# 00000002382

**FM-02 ★** — source: 65_分形几何与测度.md

**English.** Definition: A degenerate self-similar system is an iterated function system containing identity limits. Conjecture: The limit-set dimension of planar degenerate systems can realize the whole interval; and the realization of the interval is a one-parameter family. (degenerate limit-set dimension full interval)

**中文。** 定义：退化自相似系统为含恒等极限的叠函数系统。猜想：平面退化系统的极限集维数可实现全区间；且区间的实现为单参数族。（退化极限集维数全区间）
