**MF3-20 △** — source: 146_分析数论_模形式与L函数III.md

**English.** Definition: Coefficient means and higher moments are two layers of statistics. Conjecture: There exist two normalized eigenforms with the same coefficient means and second moments but different fourth moments, and the separation is realized by a CM and a non-CM pairing with equal means. (same mean and second moment-fourth moment separation)

**中文。** 定义：系数均值与高阶矩指两层统计量。猜想：存在两正规化本征形式的系数均值与二阶矩相同而四阶矩不同,分离由 CM 与非 CM 的同均值配对实现。（均值二矩同-四矩分离）
