# 00000006533

**IX3-13 ◆** — source: 203_非交换几何III_指标定理的几何侧.md

**English.** Definition: The relative version of the fixed-point formula is the covering sum. Conjecture: The pseudo-equivariant covering sum is always the relative index; the coefficient of the sum is the discriminant; and the index meaning of the covering sum is explained by pairings. (relative covering sum of fixed points)

**中文。** 定义：不动点公式的相对版本为覆叠和。猜想：伪等变的覆叠和恒为相对指标；且和的系数为判别式；且覆叠和的指标意义由配对解释。（不动点相对覆叠和）
