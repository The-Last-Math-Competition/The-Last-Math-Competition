# 00000003864

**RM3-14 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The Cauchy β-ensemble is the one-dimensional β-gas with bipower weight ∏(1+x_i²)^{−s}. Conjecture: Its equilibrium measure always has full-line support; every k-point function, after a Cauchy transform, is given explicitly by a hyperbolic kernel and is a rational function of s. (Cauchy beta kernel rationality)

**中文。** 定义：Cauchy β-系综指带双幂权 ∏(1+x_i²)^{−s} 的一维 β-气。猜想：其平衡测度支撑恒为全轴,任意 k-点函数经 Cauchy 变换后由双曲核显式表出,且 k-点函数为 s 的有理函数。（Cauchy-β 核有理性）
