# 00000005687

**English.** Definition: The symmetric center is the subcategory commuting with all objects of the category. Conjecture: The dimension ratio of the braided-commutativity criterion is always 1 when the center is one-dimensional; the dimension-ratio table is finite at low rank (at most 3 generators); and the extremum of the ratio is attained by the braiding of the order-two cyclic group, with value 2. (dimension-ratio structure of symmetric centers)

**中文。** 定义：交换中心：与范畴中全体对象交换的子范畴。猜想：辫交换判据的维数比在中心一维时恒等于 1；低秩（生成元不超过 3）的维数比表为有限；比值的极值在由二阶循环群辫化时达到且值为 2。（交换中心维数比结构）
