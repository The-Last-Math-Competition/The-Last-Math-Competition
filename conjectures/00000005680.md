# 00000005680

**English.** Definition: The scaling limit and finite corrections are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two walks with the same scaling limit but different finite-scale corrections, and the separation is realized by an explicit mirror pair of periodic modulation. (same limit-different correction periodic mirror separation)

**中文。** 定义：标度极限与有限修正指两层。猜想：存在两行走的标度极限相同而有限尺度修正不同,分离由周期调制的显式镜像对实现。（极限同-修正异周期镜像分离）
