**English.** Definition: The empty-system probability is the probability that all queues are empty. Conjecture: Its value is the explicit curve of the first coordinate of the fixed point, the curve is concave, and the slope diverges at the critical load with divergence exponent one. (critical slope divergence of the concave empty-system curve)
