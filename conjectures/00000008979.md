# 00000008979

**ISO-29 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: Finite gap solutions: periodic solutions with finitely many spectral gaps. Conjecture: The dimension of real periodic solutions of the Abel inverse of the spectral curve is always the gap count; and the counting is given by the basis of real Abel differentials. (finite gap real periodic gap-count dimension)

**中文。** 定义：finite gap 解指有限谱隙的周期解。猜想：谱曲线 Abel 逆的实周期解的维数恒为隙数；且计数由实 Abel 微分的基给出。（有限隙实周期维隙数）
