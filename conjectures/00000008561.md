# 00000008561

**English.** Definition: The epsilon-pseudospectrum sigma_epsilon(T) of an operator T is the set of complex numbers where the norm of the resolvent exceeds 1/epsilon. Conjecture: The pseudospectral area of a nonnormal operator satisfies the lower bound Area(sigma_epsilon) >= pi epsilon^2 kappa^2, where kappa is the eigenvector condition number (an area lower bound law); the bound is saturated by Jordan blocks (a Jordan saturation law); and for large epsilon the pseudospectrum is always connected (a connectivity law), with multicomponent pseudospectra produced only by direct-sum operators (a direct sum exception law). (pseudospectral area eigenvector condition bound)

**中文。** 定义：算子 T 的 ε-伪谱 σ_ε(T) 定义为使预解式范数超过 1/ε 的复数集合。猜想：非正规算子的伪谱面积满足下界 Area(σ_ε) ≥ π·ε²·κ²，其中 κ 为特征向量条件数（面积下界律）；且下界的饱和由 Jordan 块取得（Jordan 饱和律）；大 ε 时伪谱恒连通（连通律），多分量伪谱仅由直和算子产生（直和例外律）。
