# 00000008561

**NSP-01 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: ε- spectrum σ_ε(T) of operator T is defined to be such that complex number set of resolvent norm exceeds 1/ε. Conjecture: spectrum area of nonpositive operator satisfies lower bound Area(σ_ε) ≥ π · ε² · κ², where κ is eigenvector condition number (area lower bound); and of lower bound and by Jordan block attains (Jordan and); large ε spectrum always connected (connected), quantity spectrum only by direct sum operator (direct sum exception).

**中文。** 定义：算子 T 的 ε-伪谱 σ_ε(T) 定义为使预解式范数超过 1/ε 的复数集合。猜想：非正规算子的伪谱面积满足下界 Area(σ_ε) ≥ π·ε²·κ²，其中 κ 为特征向量条件数（面积下界律）；且下界的饱和由 Jordan 块取得（Jordan 饱和律）；大 ε 时伪谱恒连通（连通律），多分量伪谱仅由直和算子产生（直和例外律）。
