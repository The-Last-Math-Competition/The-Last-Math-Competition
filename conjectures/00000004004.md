# 00000004004

**English.** Definition: The log signature is the Lie series obtained by taking the logarithm of the signature. Conjecture: On discrete partitions the approximation error of the Chen identity is the p-th power of the mesh times an explicit constant depending on the p-variation of the path, and the exponent p is optimal and cannot be improved. (optimal order of the Chen approximation error)

**中文。** 定义：log 签名指签名取对数后的李级数。猜想：离散分割上 Chen 恒等式的逼近误差为 mesh 的 p 次幂乘路径 p-变差的显式常数,且指数 p 最优不可改进。（Chen 逼近误差最优阶）
