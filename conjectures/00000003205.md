# 00000003205

**English.** Definition: Characteristic sets (the Wu method): machine proofs of geometry theorems. Conjecture: The completeness of characteristic sets: deciding the algebraic truth of geometric propositions is the zero-point stratified test of the characteristic set, with the branch count of the test the characteristic-set length. (Wu method zero-point stratification branch count)
