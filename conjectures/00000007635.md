# 00000007635

**English.** Definition: Shock formation with the geometric criterion of blow-up. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the geometric criterion of blow-up and is the Riccati-type derivative law; the spectrum is finite with spectrum endpoints the compression strength; and the destabilization of the criterion is at multi-characteristic intersections. (derivative-law conversion)

**中文。** 定义：激波形成：爆破的几何判据。猜想：换算常数为爆破的几何判据且为 Riccati 型导数律；谱有限且谱的端点为压缩强度；判据的失稳在多特征相交处。（导数律换算常数结构）
