# 00000004041

**English.** Definition: The representation dimension repdim is the infimum of the dimensions of controlling subcategories. Conjecture: A cluster-tilted algebra has repdim at most 3 if and only if its mutation graph is finite, and in that case the repdim is exactly 2 or 3. (binary repdim criterion for cluster-tilted algebras)

**中文。** 定义：表示维数 repdim 指控制子范畴的维数下确界。猜想：cluster-倾斜代数的 repdim ≤ 3 当且仅当其 mutation 图有限,且此时 repdim 恰为 2 或 3。（cluster 代数 repdim 二值判定）
