# 00000004650

**English.** Definition: Braided equivalence and central charge are the two layers of a logarithmic category. Conjecture: There exist two logarithmic VOAs whose representation categories are braided equivalent while their central charges differ, and the separation is realized by symplectic fermion tensor products over dual parameter pairs. (dual separation of braided equivalence and central charge)

**中文。** 定义：辫等价与中心荷指对数范畴的两层。猜想：存在两对数 VOA 的表示范畴辫等价而中心荷不同,分离由对偶参数对的辛费米子张量积实现。（辫等价-中心荷对偶分离）
