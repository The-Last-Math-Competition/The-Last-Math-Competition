# 00000000366

**DA-66 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The Jarník upper bound for the count N_E(H) of rational points of height ≤ H on an elliptic curve E states that any arc of length ℓ contains O(ℓ^{2/3}H^{1/3}+1) rational points. Conjecture: For every E of Mordell–Weil rank ≥ 2, there are infinitely many H for which some arc attains a constant multiple of this bound, i.e. the Jarník bound is exponent-optimal on elliptic curves.

**中文。** 定义：椭圆曲线 E 上高度 ≤ H 的有理点计数 N_E(H) 的 Jarník 上界指任何长度为 ℓ 的弧上有理点数为 O(ℓ^{2/3}H^{1/3}+1)。猜想：对一切 Mordell-Weil 秩 ≥ 2 的 E,存在无穷多 H 使某段弧达到该上界的常数倍,即 Jarník 界在椭圆曲线上指数最优。
