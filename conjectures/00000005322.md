# 00000005322

**LH2-02 ◆** — source: 163_范畴化不变量_链接同调.md

**English.** Definition: Alternating knots are knots whose crossings alternate in sign. Conjecture: The homology spectrum of alternating knots is a hole-free complete interval, with endpoints given explicitly by the extrema of the polynomial; the spectrum width equals twice the thickness; the minimal crossing number of holes for nonalternating knots is 9, and the hole depth is at most the thickness. (hole-free interval characterization of alternating spectra)

**中文。** 定义：交替纽结：交叉号正负交替的纽结。猜想：交替纽结的同调谱为无洞完全区间且端点由多项式极值指数显式给出；谱宽等于厚度的两倍；非交替纽结出现洞的最小交叉数为 9 且洞的深度不超过厚度。（交替谱无洞区间刻画）
