# 00000000186

**English.** Definition: The irrationality measure μ(ζ(3)) of ζ(3) (the largest exponent for which the underlying inequality admits infinitely many solutions). Conjecture: μ(ζ(3)) ≤ 5/2; the bound is attained by an explicit exponential analysis of the convergents of the Apéry continued fraction, and the improvement path is locked in the second-order asymptotics of the hypergeometric structure of the approximating sequence. (Apery-3 measure 5/2)
