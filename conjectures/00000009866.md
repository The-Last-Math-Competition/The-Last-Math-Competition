# 00000009866

**English.** Definition: General-weight LPP: passage times with non-exactly-integrable distributions. Conjecture: One-point fluctuations of LPP with general independent weights of finite variance still converge to Tracy-Widom; the matching rate of convergence is t^{-1/6}, with the rate constant explicitly related to the skewness of the weights. (general weight Tracy-Widom universality)

**中文。** 定义：一般权重 LPP：非精确可积分布的通过时间。猜想：具有一般独立权重（有限方差）的 LPP 的单点涨落仍收敛到 Tracy–Widom；收敛的匹配速率为 t^{−1/6} 且速率常数与权重的偏度显式相关。（一般权重 TW 普适）
