**English.** Definition: An apartness space is a constructive space with a strong inequality relation. Conjecture: The minimal number of steps of its regularization is the metric resolution order, the regularization is closed within that many steps, and the step count is optimal. (apartness regularization resolution order)

**中文。** 定义：apartness 空间指带强不等关系的构造性空间。猜想：其正则化的最小步数为度量分辨阶,正则化在分辨阶步内封闭且步数最优。（apartness 正则化分辨阶）
