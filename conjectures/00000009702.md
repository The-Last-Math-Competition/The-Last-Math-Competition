**SCA-02 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Soliton resolution. Conjecture: The long-time behavior of critical equations is described by finitely many solitons plus radiation; the complete resolution of type-II blowup is known only sporadically: every type-II blowup solution of the three-dimensional focusing energy-critical wave equation decomposes, modulo symmetry, into a single harmonic soliton plus t^{-1} radiation; the decay correction of the modulation is t^{-2} times a universal constant given explicitly by the first resonance of the linearized spectrum. (type-II blowup soliton resolution)

**中文。** 定义：soliton 分解。猜想：临界方程的长时间行为由有限个孤子加辐射刻画；type-II 爆破的完全分辨已知零星。猜想：三维聚焦能量临界波方程的一切 type-II 爆破解在模对称后分解为单个调和孤子加 t^{−1} 辐射；调制的衰减修正项为 t^{−2} 乘普适常数且常数由线性化谱的第一共振显式给出。（二型爆破孤子分辨）
