# 00000008397

**English.** Definition: The noncommutative motivic lattice: the lattice structure of motives of dg categories under direct sum and tensor product. Conjecture: The lattice of blowup-invariant noncommutative motives is distributive with Mobius function of absolute value at most 1; the atoms of the lattice are unions of point motives and exceptional dual pairs; and the atom count equals the cardinality of the set of lengths of semiorthogonal decompositions. (noncommutative motivic lattice)

**中文。** 定义：非交换母题格指 dg 范畴的母题在直和与张量下的格结构。猜想：爆破不变的非交换母题的格为分配格且 Möbius 函数绝对值 ≤ 1；格的原子为单点母题与 exceptional 对偶对的并；原子计数等于半正交分解的长度集合的势。（非交换母题格）
