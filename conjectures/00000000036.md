# 00000000036

**AC-10 ★** — source: 02_组合数论与加性组合.md

**English.** Conjecture: If A ⊂ [N] is such that the sum (when ≥ 4) and the difference (when ≥ 2) of any two of its elements are both composite, then |A| ≤ (1/3+ε)N; the residue classes {±1 mod 6} give 1/3.

**中文。** 猜想：若 A ⊂ [N] 中任意两元素之和(≥4)与差(≥2)均为合数,则 |A| ≤ (1/3+ε)N;剩余类 {±1 mod 6} 给出 1/3。
