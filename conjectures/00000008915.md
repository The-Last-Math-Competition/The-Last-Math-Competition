**English.** Definition: Universal graphs: the universality of homomorphisms. Conjecture: Universal graphs: the universal objects of homomorphisms, with the size of the object the saturation count; and the saturation count is exactly the family size of the tight realizations of Hall conditions. (universal graph homomorphism saturation Hall tightness)

**中文。** 定义：普适图：同态的普适。猜想：普适图：同态的普适对象且象的规模为饱和计数。(普适图同态普适饱和计数对象规模)；且饱和计数恰为 Hall 条件的紧致实现族规模。
