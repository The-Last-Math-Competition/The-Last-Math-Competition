# 00000002841

**English.** Definition: Toric models: exponential families parametrized by monomials. Conjecture: The squarefree lattice of toric ideals: the ML degree of the model equals the number of toric factors of the primary decomposition of the saturation ideal, and the factor count is the coprimization of the polytope Ehrhart. (toric ML factor count)

**中文。** 定义：toric 模型：由单项式参数化的指数族。猜想：toric 理想的无平方根格：模型的 ML 度等于饱和理想的初级分解的 toric 因子数且因子计数为多面体 Ehrhart 的互素化。（toric ML 因子计数）
