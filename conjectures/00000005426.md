# 00000005426

**English.** Definition: The quasihyperbolic metric is the interior metric weighted by distance. Conjecture: The comparison constant of uniform domains is given explicitly by the chain-condition parameter, attained at horn domains, and the bound cannot be improved. (chain-condition comparison constant of uniform domains)

**中文。** 定义：拟双曲度量指距离加权的内部度量。猜想：一致域的比较常数由链条件参数显式给出,喇叭域处达到且界不可改进。（一致域链条件比较常数）
