**English.** Definition: Log-concavity of the Tutte polynomial under specializations of type (q+1, q−1). Conjecture: For every prime power q, T_G(q+1, q−1) > 0 and yields an effective count (an arithmetization of the combinatorial positivity interpretation).

**中文。** 定义：Tutte 多项式的对数凹性在 (q+1, q−1) 型特殊化。猜想：对每个素数幂 q,T_G(q+1, q−1) > 0 且给出有效计数(组合正性解释的算术化)。
