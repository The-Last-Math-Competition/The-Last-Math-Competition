# 00000002003

**English.** Conjecture: The equality case of the positive mass theorem with angular momentum occurs only for Kerr (i.e. the rigidity behavior of m² ≥ E² + J²); initial data sets attaining equality are Kerr data (up to a second-order flattening).

**中文。** 猜想：带角动量的正质量定理的等式情形仅 Kerr(即 m²≥E²+J² 的刚性行为);等式成立的初始数据集为 Kerr 数据(至差一个二阶展平)。
