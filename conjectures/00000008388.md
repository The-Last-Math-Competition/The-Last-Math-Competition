# 00000008388

**MIB-08 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The motivic Galois group: the automorphism group of realizations of the Tannakian category; the chessboard is the Ext^1 space of its representations. Conjecture: The dimension of Ext^1 of a fixed weight-1 motive is given by the dimension of extensions of the Hodge structure; Ext^1 of motives of weight at least 2 is always zero in the category of numerical motives, with counterexamples to vanishing appearing only beyond the numerical layer, the first counterexample having weight 3. (motivic Ext chessboard dimension)

**中文。** 定义：母题 Galois 群指 Tannakian 范畴的实现自同构群，棋盘指其表示的 Ext¹ 空间。猜想：固定权 1 母题的 Ext¹ 的维数由 Hodge 结构的扩张的维数给出；权 ≥ 2 的母题 Ext¹ 在数值母题范畴中恒为零，零性的反例只在非数值母题层出现且第一个反例的权为 3。（母题 Ext 棋盘维数）
