# 00000004416

**English.** Definition: The Berry-Esseen constant is the uniform error constant of the central limit approximation. Conjecture: For subgraph-counting distributions of sampled graphs this constant is an explicit expression in the kernel norm times the reciprocal of the variance, and it is optimal on independent-block graphons. (Berry-Esseen optimal constant of sampled counts)

**中文。** 定义：Berry–Esseen 常数指中心极限逼近的均匀误差常数。猜想：采样图子图计数分布的该常数为核范数乘方差倒数的显式式,在独立块 graphon 上达到最优。（采样计数的 Berry-Esseen 最优常数）
