# 00000005938

**English.** Definition: Second-order fluctuations are the next-order fluctuation structure. Conjecture: The leading correction of the one-third time power is logarithmic with exact coefficient, the combination of the exactness being the conformal invariant. (conformal-invariant logarithmic correction coefficient of second order)

**中文。** 定义：次阶涨落指涨落的次阶结构。猜想：时间三分之一次幂修正的首修正常数为对数型且系数精确；且精确的组合为共形不变量。（次阶共形不变量对数修正系数）
