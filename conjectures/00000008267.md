# 00000008267

**SFL-07 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The strong convergence error of numerical methods for SDEs: the explicit constant milstein_c of the strong error of the Milstein scheme. Conjecture: milstein_c (the strong error constant) is explicit of type L sigma sqrt h (L the Lipschitz constant, an explicit constant law); the robust domain of convergence of tamed Euler (superlinear growth coefficients) has threshold beta = 3/2 (a 3/2 robustness threshold); and the expected explosion time when the threshold breaks (untamed explosion) diverges as h^{-1/2} (a half-exponential divergence law). (Milstein explicit constant tamed Euler threshold)

**中文。** 定义：随机微分方程的数值方法的强收敛误差：Milstein 方案的强误差的显式常数 milstein_c。猜想：milstein_c（强误差常数）= L·σ·√h 型显式（L 为 Lipschitz，显式常数律）；且 tamed Euler（驯服欧拉）的收敛的稳健域（超线性增长系数）的阈值 β=3/2（3/2 稳健阈）；阈的破缺（无驯服的爆炸）的时刻的期望为 h^{-1/2} 的发散（半指数发散律）。
