**English.** Definition: Diagram statistics are the probability functionals of persistence diagrams. Conjecture: The support of the mean functional is finite-dimensional for atomic diagrams, and the kernel of the finiteness is the stratification of the diagram. (finite-dimensional support of mean functionals on atomic diagrams)
