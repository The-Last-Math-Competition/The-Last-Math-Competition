# 00000007726

**English.** Definition: Traffic distributions are described by trace functionals of noncommutative polynomials in matrix entries with graph variables; traffic independence unifies the algebraic structure of random matrix limits. Conjecture: The fluctuation functionals of unitarily invariant ensembles (second-order limits) are exactly the families independent and infinitely divisible in the traffic sense; their fluctuation covariances are characterized by spectra of graph-gluing operators, and all covariances are integer-coefficient combinatorial quantities. (traffic infinite divisibility of fluctuations)

**中文。** 定义：traffic 分布由矩阵集合的非交换多项式与图变量的迹泛函刻画；traffic 独立性统一描述随机矩阵极限的代数结构。猜想：酉不变系综的波动泛函（second-order 极限）恰为在 traffic 意义下独立且为 traffic 无穷可分的族；其波动协方差由图粘连算子的谱刻画，且所有协方差皆为整系数的组合量。
