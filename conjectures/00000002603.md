**English.** Definition: Formal Concept Analysis (FCA): the concept lattice of an object-attribute binary relation. Conjecture: The node count of the concept lattice of a random binary relation (density p) concentrates at an exponential law of the minimum of the two ends, with concentration constant an explicit function of the entropy function. (concept lattice random size law)

**中文。** 定义：形式概念分析（FCA）：对象-属性二元关系的概念格。猜想：随机二元关系（密度 p）的概念格的节点数集中为两端最小值的指数律且集中常数为熵函数的显式函数。（概念格随机规模律）
