**English.** Definition: Planted coloring is a hidden q-coloring of a random graph. Conjecture: Its information-theoretic detection threshold is an explicit function of q, given by the spectral singularity of the KL divergence, and it coincides with the spectral-method threshold for q at least 5. (KL spectral singularity threshold of planted coloring)

**中文。** 定义：种植染色指随机图中隐藏的 q 染色。猜想：其检测的信息论阈值为 q 的显式函数,由 KL 散度谱奇点给出,与谱方法阈值在 q 不小于 5 时重合。（种植染色 KL 谱奇点阈值）
