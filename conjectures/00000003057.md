**IP-17 ★** — source: 87_非线性PDE的可积检验.md

**English.** Definition: The existence test for action-angle variables: symplectic complete integrability. Conjecture: Explicit construction of action-angle variables: the action variables as functions of energy are explicit integrals of Abelian differentials, and the monodromy of angle variables is the explicit inverse of Abel's theorem. (action-angle Abel inverse construction)

**中文。** 定义：作用-角变量的存在检验：辛的完全可积性。猜想：作用-角的显式构造：作用变量的周期能量为 Abel 微分的显式积分且角变量的单值为 Abel 定理的显式逆。（作用角 Abel 逆构造）
