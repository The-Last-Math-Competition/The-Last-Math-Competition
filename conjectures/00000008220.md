# 00000008220

**ORB-20 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The normalization constant of stabilized orbital integrals in branching laws: the constant of the stabilization. Conjecture: The stable normalization constant is explicit as a power of 2 times a power of the root count (a two-root power law); the ramification of the constant (normalization of ramified orbits) has ramification index with prime factors contained in {2, l} (a two-prime ramification law); and the verification domain of local consistency of the normalization (agreement of normalizations of global orbits) is an extension of integral domains (an integral domain verification law) of degree at most 2 (a double-degree extension law). (stabilized orbital two-root normalization)

**中文。** 定义：分支定律的轨道积分的稳定化的 Višnupada 常数：稳定轨道积分的规范化的常数 viš_const。猜想：stab_const（稳定化的规范常数）为 2 的幂·根数幂的显式（二-根幂律）；且常数的分歧（分歧轨道的规范化）的分歧指数的素因子 ⊆ {2, ℓ}（双素分歧律）；规范化的局部一致性（整体轨道的规范的一致）的验证域为整环的扩张（整环验证域律），扩张的次数 ≤ 2（双次扩张律）。
