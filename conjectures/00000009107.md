# 00000009107

**English.** Definition: Integral geometry studies invariance of random geometric quantities. Conjecture: The normalization constant of rotation-invariant measures is always the spherical volume; and the uniqueness of normalization is by Haar measure. (rotation-invariant normalization constant)

**中文。** 定义：积分几何研究随机几何量的不变性。猜想：旋转不变测度的规范常数恒为球面体积；且规范化的唯一性由 Haar 测度。（旋转不变规范常数）
