# 00000001846

**RM-06 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The distribution of the maximal real eigenvalue of the real Ginibre ensemble. Conjecture: Its distribution is an explicit mixture of TW-β=1 and Gumbel: F(x) = Φ_TW(x)·Φ_Gumbel(x^{3/2})^{1/2}; the mixture weight is 1/2 (from the balance of real and complex eigenvalues).

**中文。** 定义：real Ginibre 的最大实特征值分布。猜想：其密度为 TW-β=1 与 Gumbel 的显式混合:F(x) = Φ_TW(x)·Φ_Gumbel(x^{3/2})^{1/2};混合权重为 1/2(由实/复特征值的平衡)。
