# 00000004839

**English.** Definition: The local Langlands correspondence is the bijection of local representations with Weil parameters. Conjecture: The global obstruction to its inverse recovery is an explicit inner twisting group, the group is of order two and completely controlled by the constraint of the central character. (inner twisting obstruction to the Langlands inverse)

**中文。** 定义：局部朗兰兹对应指局部表示与韦伊参数的双射。猜想：其逆向恢复的整体障碍为显式内部扭转群,群为二阶且由中心特征的约束完全控制。（朗兰兹逆向内部扭转障碍）
