**English.** Definition: Galois group: q- difference part of Mahler equation and Mahler intersection. Conjecture: dimension of Zariski closure of Galois group of Mahler equation is explicit function of minimal polynomial -fold number of Mahler operator and function by Diophantine approximation controls of Engel coefficient. (Mahler Galois dimension formula)
