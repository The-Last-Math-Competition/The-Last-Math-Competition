# 00000001118

**LT-18 △** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The average degree A_n(q) = |Irr|^{-1}Σ_χ χ(1) of irreducible characters of Sp_{2n}(F_q). Conjecture: A_n(q) ~ q^{n²/2+n/2}/(c·q^{1/2}), where the constant c is the q → ∞ limit of the explicit Euler product ∏_{i≥2}(1−q^{1-i}), namely c = 1; and the error term is O(q^{n²/2−1/2}).

**中文。** 定义：Sp_{2n}(F_q) 的不可约特征标的平均次数 A_n(q)=|Irr|^{-1}Σ_χ χ(1)。猜想：A_n(q) ~ q^{n²/2+n/2}/(c·q^{1/2}),常数 c 为显式 Euler 积 Π_{i≥2}(1−q^{1-i}) 的 q→∞ 极限,即 c=1;且误差项为 O(q^{n²/2-1/2})。
