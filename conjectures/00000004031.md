# 00000004031

**AR3-01 ★** — source: 120_代数表示论III_AuslanderReiten.md

**English.** Definition: An AR-sequence 0 -> tau M -> E -> M -> 0 is an almost-split exact sequence. Conjecture: For the path algebra kQ, the number of indecomposable direct summands of the middle term E of an AR-sequence is at most the sum of the injective dimension and the projective dimension of M, and the bound is attained optimally by exterior algebras. (upper bound on AR middle-term summands)

**中文。** 定义：AR-序列 0 → τM → E → M → 0 指几乎分裂正合列。猜想：路代数 kQ 的 AR-序列中间项 E 的不可分解直和项个数不超过 M 的入射维数与射影维数之和,且该界由外代数达到最优。（AR 中间项个数上界）
