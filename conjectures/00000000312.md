**DA-12 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: The Hall ray is the half-line (c_Hall, ∞) contained in the Markov spectrum, with c_Hall its optimal starting point. Conjecture: c_Hall itself belongs to the Markov spectrum — i.e. the Markov spectrum contains the half-line from c_Hall to infinity — and c_Hall is the Markov constant M(w) of a balanced word w over partial quotients {1,2}, uniquely determinable by a finite search within length ≤ 60.

**中文。** 定义：Hall 射线指 Markov 谱包含的半直线 (c_Hall, ∞),c_Hall 为其最优起点。猜想：c_Hall 本身属于 Markov 谱,即 Markov 谱包含从 c_Hall 到无穷的半直线,且 c_Hall 是某个由部分商 {1,2} 构成的平衡词 w 的 Markov 常数 M(w),w 可经有限搜索在长度 ≤ 60 内唯一确定。
