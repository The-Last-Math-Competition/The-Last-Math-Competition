# 00000001409

**English.** Definition: Novikov's conjecture asserts that higher signatures are homotopy invariants. Conjecture: The integral Novikov conjecture holds for all hyperbolic groups; for hyperbolic 3-manifold groups the proof follows from the surjectivity of the coarse Baum–Connes assembly map on the Rips complex, and this surjection can be constructed explicitly via controlled analysis.

**中文。** 定义：Novikov 猜想断言高阶符号是同伦不变量。猜想：整体 Novikov 猜想对一切双曲群成立;对双曲 3-流形群,其证明由粗 Baum–Connes 装配映射在 Rips 复形上的满射性给出,且该满射可经控制分析显式构造。
