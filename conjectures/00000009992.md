# 00000009992

**English.** Definition: Indecomposable dimension distributions of wild algebras: the mass distribution after the second Brauer-Thrall. Conjecture: The dimension of the parameter space of indecomposables of a wild algebra at given dimension grows as a power law in the dimension, with power exponent the maximal directed cycle length of the quiver of the algebra; the tail of the distribution is polynomial with no logarithmic correction. (wild algebra dimension power-law distribution)

**中文。** 定义：野代数的不可分解维数分布：第二 Brauer–Thrall 之后的质量分布。猜想：野代数的给定维数的不可分解的参数空间维数按维数呈幂律增长且幂指数为代数的箭图的最大定向圈长；分布的尾行为多项式型且无对数修正。（野代数维数幂律分布）
