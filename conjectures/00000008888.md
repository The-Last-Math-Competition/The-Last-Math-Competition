# 00000008888

**CGB-28 ★** — source: 281_组合博弈论II.md

**English.** Definition: The downsum operation on descending chains: the termwise sum-type composition for strictly descending real chains and its extension to ordinal descending chains. Conjecture: Downsum is associative on all finite descending chains, with associativity holding exactly on repetition-free chain families; extended to ordinal chains, downsum agrees with ordinal natural sum on finite segments and differs on transfinite segments by exactly one limit-term correction; the value spectrum of downsum on dense real chains is dense without isolated values; and the total number of corrections equals the count of limit ordinals in the chain. (downsum associativity ordinal limit correction density)

**中文。** 定义：递降链的 downsum 运算：对严格下降实数链的逐项求和型复合及其在序数下降链上的扩张。猜想：downsum 在一切有限下降链上满足结合律且结合律的成立域恰为无重复项的链族；扩张到序数链时 downsum 与序数自然和在有限段上相等且在超限段相差恰一个极限项修正；实数稠密链上 downsum 的值谱为稠密集且不含孤立值；修正项总数等于链中极限序数的个数。
