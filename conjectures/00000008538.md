**POS-08 ★** — source: 270_偏序集与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: A lattice algebra is the incidence algebra of a lattice. Conjecture: The Jacobson radical of an incidence algebra is its strictly lower triangular part; and its quotient is the Mobius algebra, of dimension equal to the number of lattice elements. (radical and quotient of incidence algebras)

**中文。** 定义：格代数指格的关联代数。猜想：关联代数的 Jacobson 根为严格下三角部分；且其商为 Möbius 代数且维数等于格元数。（关联代数根商）
