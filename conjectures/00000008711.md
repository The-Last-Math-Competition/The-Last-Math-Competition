**MBL-01 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: Anderson localization: the spectrum of the random-potential Schroedinger operator (H psi)(x) = psi(x+1) + psi(x-1) + V(x) psi(x) is pure point. Conjecture: For three-dimensional random Schroedinger operators with disorder strength above threshold, pure point spectrum holds strictly (an extended domain law); the mobility edge exists as an energy threshold and is analytic (an analytic edge law); and the exponent of the Lifshitz tail near the edge is an explicit energy functional (a Lifshitz exponent law). (Anderson mobility edge Lifshitz tail)

**中文。** 定义：Anderson 局域化：随机位势薛定谔算子 (Hψ)(x)=ψ(x+1)+ψ(x-1)+V(x)ψ(x) 的谱为纯点谱。猜想：三维随机薛定谔在扰动强度大于阈值时纯点谱严格成立（扩展域律）；且迁移率边作为能量阈值存在并具解析性（解析边律）；边附近的 Lifshitz 尾的指数为显式能量泛函（Lifshitz 指数律）。
