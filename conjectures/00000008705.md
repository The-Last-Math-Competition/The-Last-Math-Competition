**English.** Definition: approximation of optimal transport is approximation of neural network. Conjecture: transport graph approximation of ReLU network is n^{-1/2} rate; and combinatorial of rate is complexity. (ReLU- complexity transport approximation)

**中文。** 定义：最优传输的神经逼近为神经网络的逼近率。猜想：ReLU 网络的传输图逼近为 n^{-1/2} 速率；且速率的组合为采样复杂度。（ReLU-采样复杂度传输逼近率）
