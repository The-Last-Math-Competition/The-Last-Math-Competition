# 00000008705

**English.** Definition: Neural approximation of optimal transport: the approximation rate of neural networks. Conjecture: The transport map approximation by ReLU networks has rate n^{-1/2}; and the combination of the rate is the sampling complexity. (ReLU sampling complexity transport rate)

**中文。** 定义：最优传输的神经逼近为神经网络的逼近率。猜想：ReLU 网络的传输图逼近为 n^{-1/2} 速率；且速率的组合为采样复杂度。（ReLU-采样复杂度传输逼近率）
