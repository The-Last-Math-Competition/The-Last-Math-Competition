# 00000001863

**English.** Definition: The automorphic boundary of free multiplicative convolution. Conjecture: The boundary of automorphic measures realizable by determinant point processes is given explicitly by the fixed-point equation of subordination (the automorphic boundary); and the solution of the fixed-point equation is exactly the Berezin–Karpelevich limit.

**中文。** 定义：自由多重卷积的自守边界。猜想：行列式点过程可实现的自守测度的边界由 subordination 的不动点方程显式(自守边界)；且不动点方程的解恰为 Berezin-Karpelevich 极限。
