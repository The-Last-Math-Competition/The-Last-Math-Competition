**English.** Definition: Wigner-type deviations: the empirical spectral LDP of random-matrix eigenvalues. Conjecture: The rate functional of the spectral LDP: the explicit second-term correction of the one-dimensional Coulomb-gas rate is the diagonal entropy, making the Hessian of the rate at the semicircle law the inverse kernel of the logarithmic potential.
