# 00000004454

**English.** Definition: The localization of a stop is the wrapped-category functor removing the points of the Weinstein skeleton. Conjecture: The count of indecomposable objects of the localized category is given explicitly by the chain complex of the linking of the stop, the count being a linking-Euler-type integral. (linking count of stop localization)

**中文。** 定义：stop 的局部化指移去 Weinstein 骨架点的包裹范畴函子。猜想：局部化范畴的不可分解对象计数由 stop 链接的链复形显式给出,计数为链接 Euler 型积分。（stop 局部化链接计数）
