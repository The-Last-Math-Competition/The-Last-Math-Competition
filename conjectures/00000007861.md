# 00000007861

**RAM-21 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The expected number F_n of flips in randomized incremental Delaunay construction on a planar Poisson point process. Conjecture: E[F_n] = (12 pi^2/7 + 1) n + O(n^{1/2}) with an explicit transcendental leading coefficient; the variance of F_n is sigma^2 n + O(n^{2/3}) with sigma^2 a positive real given by the integral of a quadratic association function, and the fluctuation limit is Gaussian; and the constant coincides with the triangulation entropy constant of the four-coloring count of the plane. (Delaunay flip count transcendental constant)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Delaunay 增量构造的期望翻转数 F_n（平面 Poisson 点过程）。猜想：E[F_n] = (12π²/7+1)·n+O(n^{1/2})，系数为显式超越常数；且 F_n 的方差为 σ²n+O(n^{2/3})，σ² 为由二次型关联函数的积分给出的正实数，波动极限为高斯；该常数与平面的 4-染色计数三角剖分熵常数恒等。
