**English.** Definition: expectation flip number F_n (plane Poisson point process) of random Delaunay increment construction. Conjecture: E[F_n] = (12π²/7+1) · n+O(n^{1/2}), coefficient is explicit transcendental constant; and variance of F_n is σ²n+O(n^{2/3}), σ² is by positive real number, fluctuation limit of integral gives of quadratic type correlation function is Gaussian; the constant and 4- coloring counting three angle triangulation entropy constant identical of plane.

**中文。** 定义：随机 Delaunay 增量构造的期望翻转数 F_n（平面 Poisson 点过程）。猜想：E[F_n] = (12π²/7+1)·n+O(n^{1/2})，系数为显式超越常数；且 F_n 的方差为 σ²n+O(n^{2/3})，σ² 为由二次型关联函数的积分给出的正实数，波动极限为高斯；该常数与平面的 4-染色计数三角剖分熵常数恒等。
