# 00000005581

**English.** Definition: Quasithin groups are finite simple groups of local structure restricted to rank at most two. Conjecture: The classification verification converts to the explicit composition series of modular representations, the type of equidimensional blocks controlled by cyclic groups; the number of equidimensional blocks is the divisor count of the cyclic part; and the length of the composition series is a linear function of the blocks, with slope 1. (cyclic-type structure of quasithin equidimensional blocks)

**中文。** 定义：拟薄群：秩至多二的局部结构受限有限单群。猜想：分类验证换算为模表示合成系列的显式且等维块的型由循环群控制；等维块（同维数的不可约块）的个数为循环部分的因子数；合成系列的长为块的线性函数且斜率为 1。（拟薄等维块循环型结构）
