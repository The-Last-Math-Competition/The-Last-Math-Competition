**English.** Definition: The plurigenus is the global section count of canonical powers. Conjecture: The three-dimensional asymptotics is an explicit polynomial, monic in the maximal-dimensional case, and the coefficients of the polynomial are combinations of characteristic numbers. (monic polynomial of three-dimensional plurigenera)
