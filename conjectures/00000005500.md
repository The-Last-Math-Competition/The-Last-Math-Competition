# 00000005500

**English.** Definition: The deformed energy levels and the seed structure are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two models with identical deformed energy levels but different seed-theory structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit seed pair with spectrum-equivalent but different operator products. (same deformed levels-different seed separation)
