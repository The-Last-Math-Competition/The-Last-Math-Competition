# 00000000301

**DA-01 ◆** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: Bad is the set of badly approximable numbers, and λ·Bad+μ denotes its translate and dilate. Conjecture: For every λ ≠ 0 and μ ∈ R, the set (λ·Bad+μ) ∩ Bad is winning in the sense of Schmidt games and has Hausdorff dimension 1; in particular, translates of two badly approximable sets retain uncountable winning intersection.

**中文。** 定义：Bad 为坏逼近数集,仿射像 λ·Bad+μ 指其平移与伸缩。猜想：对任意 λ≠0 与 μ∈R,集合 (λ·Bad+μ) ∩ Bad 在 Schmidt 博弈意义下为赢集,且 Hausdorff 维数为 1;特别地两个坏逼近集的平移交保持不可数赢性。
