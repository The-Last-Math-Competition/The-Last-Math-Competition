# 00000002420

**GC2-10 ★** — source: 66_Galois覆盖与基本群.md

**English.** Conjecture: There exist non-isomorphic pairs (C₁, C₂) of smooth projective curves with isomorphic π₁^ét and identical ℓ-local data for every ℓ, yet globally non-isomorphic (a complete global-local separation); the construction uses undetectable differences of torsors.

**中文。** 猜想：存在非同构的光滑射影曲线对 (C₁,C₂) 使 π₁^{ét} 同构且一切 ℓ 的 ℓ-局部相同但整体不同构(整体-局部完全分离);构造由扭转子的不可检测差异。
