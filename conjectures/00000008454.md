# 00000008454

**English.** Definition: The slice genus g_4 and the Seifert genus g_3 of a knot. Conjecture: Three-strand braid knots always satisfy g_4 = g_3; the minimal separating example beyond three strands is of five-strand Bennequin type with g_3 - g_4 = 1. (slice equality boundary for three-strand braids)

**中文。** 定义：slice 亏格 g₄ 与 Seifert 亏格 g₃。猜想：三股辫结恒有 g₄ = g₃；三股之外的最小分离例为五股 Bennequin 型且 g₃ − g₄ = 1。（三股辫 slice 等式边界）
