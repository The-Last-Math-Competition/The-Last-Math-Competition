# 00000008490

**English.** Definition: The computational complexity of the maximum cycle mean problem for weighted digraphs. Conjecture: The O(nm) complexity of Karp's algorithm is optimal (a matching lower bound law); special structures of weighted acyclic classes admit a logarithmic improvement (a logarithmic improvement law); the parallel model complexity lower bound is the logarithm of the diameter (a diameter logarithm law); and the constant of the average complexity on random graphs is 1/3 (a one-third constant law). (Karp maximum cycle mean matching third)

**中文。** 定义：带权有向图的最大圈平均问题的计算复杂度。猜想：Karp 算法的 O(nm) 复杂度为最优（匹配下界律）；且加权无环类的特殊结构存在对数改进（对数改进律）；并行模型的复杂度下界为直径的对数（直径对数律）；随机图的平均复杂度的常数为 1/3（三分之一常数律）。
