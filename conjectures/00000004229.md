**English.** Definition: The twisted Laplacian is the cochain Laplacian with coefficients in a group representation. Conjecture: The representation-multiplicity weighted generating function of twisted tree counts factors into a product of cyclotomic polynomials, indexed explicitly by the dimensions of the irreducible representations. (twisted tree-representation generating function)

**中文。** 定义：twisted Laplacian 指带群表示系数的上链拉普拉斯。猜想：twisted 树计数的表示重数加权母函数分解为分圆多项式乘积,因子由不可约表示维数显式索引。（twisted 树-表示母函数）
