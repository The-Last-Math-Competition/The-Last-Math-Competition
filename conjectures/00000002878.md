# 00000002878

**English.** Definition: Higher index theorems: ℓ²-invariants of noncommutative covers (Lück). Conjecture: Continuity of higher φ-indices: the index is approximated by freezing constants under compact group actions, with approximation error a measure-concentration deviation; the vanishing of the deviation is the uniform summability of spectral projections. (higher index approximation error)

**中文。** 定义：高阶指标定理：非交换覆盖的 c-不变量（Lück）。猜想：高阶 φ 指标的连续性：指标在紧群作用下的冻结常数逼近且逼近的误差为测度集中偏差；偏差的消失为谱投影的一致可和性。（高阶指标逼近误差）
