**NLA-02 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: The Halko-Martinsson-Tropp framework: range-finder approximation A approximately QQ^*A. Conjecture: The optimal exponent of the error probability bound of HMT is of exp(-r) type (an exponential law); the constant of the high-probability Chernoff-type optimization is 2 (a Chernoff constant law); adaptive ranges (adaptive doubling of r) have optimal expected complexity (an optimality law); and the lower bound on complexity matches (a matching law). (HMT range Chernoff adaptive matching)

**中文。** 定义：Halko–Martinsson–Tropp 框架：范围求逼近 A≈QQ^*A。猜想：HMT 的误差概率界的最优指数为 exp(-r) 型（指数律）；且界的高概率优化的 Chernoff 型的常数为 2（Chernoff 常数律）；自适应的范围（r 的自适应加倍）的期望复杂度最优（最优律）；复杂度的下界匹配（匹配律）。
