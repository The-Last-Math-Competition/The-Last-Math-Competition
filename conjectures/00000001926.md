# 00000001926

**HR-26 ★** — source: 49_调和分析II.md

**English.** Definition: Non-parallel sets (nonlinear Roth). Conjecture: The density lower bound for sets of density δ containing isosceles triples of three points on the sphere is c·δ^{K} with the optimal K (a spherical Roth exponent).

**中文。** 定义：不平行集合(非线性 Roth)。猜想：球面上三点的等距三元组的密度 δ 集合的下界为 c·δ^{K} 的最优 K(球面 Roth 指数)。
