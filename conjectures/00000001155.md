# 00000001155

**LT-55 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The Lubotzky–Phillips–Sarnak graphs X^{p,q} are (p+1)-regular Ramanujan graphs. Conjecture: The deviation of the first nontrivial Laplace eigenvalue of X^{p,q} from 2√p is at most C·p^{-1/6}, and the fluctuation of this deviation as q varies over quadratic-residue classes is O(p^{-1/2+ε}).

**中文。** 定义：Lubotzky–Phillips–Sarnak 图 X^{p,q} 为 (p+1)-正则 Ramanujan 图。猜想：X^{p,q} 的第一非平凡 Laplace 特征值与 2√p 的偏差 ≤ C·p^{-1/6},且该偏差在 q 沿二次剩余类变化时的波动幅度为 O(p^{-1/2+ε})。
