# 00000000455

**English.** Definition: The tunnel number of a Dyck path. Conjecture: For a uniformly random Dyck path, the logarithm of the product of tunnel lengths is asymptotically normal, with explicit mean and variance constants.

**中文。** 定义：Dyck 路的 tunnel 数。猜想：均匀随机 Dyck 路的 tunnel 长度之积取对数后渐近正态,均值与方差为显式常数。
