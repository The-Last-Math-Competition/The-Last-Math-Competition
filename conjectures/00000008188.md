# 00000008188

**English.** Definition: The Riemann-Siegel type correction for the fast evaluation of L-functions: the number rs_terms(t) of correction terms for evaluating the central value L(1/2+it). Conjecture: rs_terms = O(t^{1/6}) (a sixth-root law); the coefficient of the leading correction term is the Stokes point t^{1/6} of the divergent point of the asymptotic series (a Stokes sixth-root correspondence); the window width of the vanishing series controlling the rounding error of evaluation (smoothing) is t^theta with optimal theta = 1/3 (a cubic window law); and the bit-complexity lower bound of evaluation is Omega(t^{1/4+epsilon}) (a quarter-power lower bound), the model of the lower bound being the quadratic phase of exponential sums (a quadratic phase lower bound). (Riemann-Siegel sixth-root Stokes cubic window)

**中文。** 定义：L-函数的快速求值的黎曼-西格尔型修正：中心值 L(1/2+it) 的求值的修正项的个数 rs_terms(t)。猜想：rs_terms = O(t^{1/6})（六次根律）；且修正的主项的系数为渐近级数的发散点（Stokes 点）t^{1/6}（Stokes-六次对应）；求值的舍入误差的控制的消没级数（Smooth 化）的窗宽 t^{θ}（θ=1/3 最优，三次窗律）；求值的比特复杂度的下界为 Ω(t^{1/4+ε})（四分之一次幂下界），下界的模型为指数和的二次相位（二次相位下界）。
