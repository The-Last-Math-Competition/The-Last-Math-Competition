**HCA-04 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The analyticity degree of Goodwillie calculus: the polynomial degree n(F) of a functor F of spectra is the convergence codimension of its Taylor tower. Conjecture: For every representable functor F(X) = X^{wedge k} wedge Y one has n(F) = k, and the connectivity lower bound for the fiber of the stabilization map of the Taylor coefficients is the quadratic c(k) = (k-1)(k+2)/2 (Kaliman-type connectivity growth of the compact factorization), this quadratic bound being exact and realized by the Whitehead spectral duality of the (k-1)-splitting maps of symmetric groups. (Goodwillie fiber connectivity quadratic)

**中文。** 定义：Goodwillie 演算的解析度：从谱范畴到谱范畴的函子 F 的解析多项式度 n(F) 定义为泰勒塔的收敛余维。猜想：对一切可表函子 F(X)=X^{∧k}∧Y，n(F)=k，且泰勒系数的稳定化映射的纤维的连通度下界为 c(k)= (k-1)(k+2)/2（紧因子分解的 Kaliman 型连通增长），该二次型上界精确且由对称群的 (k-1) 分裂映射的 Whitehead 谱对偶实现。
