# 00000008248

**English.** Definition: The compactness of bidding languages in combinatorial auctions: the minimal size bid_lang of a language compactly expressing all valuations. Conjecture: The information-theoretic lower bound of bid_lang (the minimal size for compact expression) is 2^n n (an information-theoretic lower bound law); the size of a compact language covering the class of monotone submodular valuations is the product n log n (a submodular product law); and the critical language of the expressiveness-computability trade-off (expressiveness exponent versus NP-hardness) is the depth-2 OR language (a depth-two critical law). (bidding language submodular product depth-two)

**中文。** 定义：组合拍卖的投标语言的紧凑性：表达一切估价的紧凑语言的最小规模 bid_lang。猜想：bid_lang（紧凑表达的最小规模的熵下界）= 2^{n}·n 的信息论下界（信息论下界律）；且覆盖估值类（单调次模）的紧凑语言的规模为 n·log n 的乘积（次模乘积律）；语言的表达力-计算性的权衡（表达力指数 vs 计算 NP 难）的临界语言为 OR 语言的深度 2（深度二临界律）。
