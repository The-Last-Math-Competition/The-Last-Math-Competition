# 00000002225

**English.** Conjecture: Uniquely clean rings (every element has a unique decomposition as idempotent plus unit) admit, in the finite commutative case, a complete classification as continuous images of Z₂-algebras (clean classification).

**中文。** 猜想：uniquely clean 环(每元为幂等 + 单位的唯一分解)在有限交换情形为 ℤ₂-代数的连续像 的完全分类(clean 分类)。
