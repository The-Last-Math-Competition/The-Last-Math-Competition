# 00000005853

**English.** Definition: Dihedral defect groups are defect groups of dihedral type. Conjecture: They are partitioned into three subtypes indexed by the extraordinary index, the minimal dihedral table is complete, and the kernel of the partition is the index parity. (three-subtype index parity of dihedral groups)
