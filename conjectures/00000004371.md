# 00000004371

**English.** Definition: The Goodwillie identity is the rational equivalence of relative algebraic K with negative cyclic homology. Conjecture: The minimal stable class for convergence of the identity is the complete class of Nil extensions, and every smaller class admits explicit counterexamples. (minimality of the Nil class of Goodwillie convergence)

**中文。** 定义：Goodwillie 恒等式指相对代数 K 与负循环同调的有理等价。猜想：恒等式收敛的最小稳定类为 Nil 扩张完备类,任何更小的类均存在显式反例。（Goodwillie 收敛 Nil 类极小）
