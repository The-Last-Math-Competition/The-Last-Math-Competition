# 00000001049

**English.** Definition: The Waring number g(k,q) is the least number of k-th powers needed to represent all of F_q. Conjecture: g(k,q) ≤ k·(1+o(1)), with the limiting constant explicit in the smallest prime factor of k (a sharp Waring upper bound).

**中文。** 定义：Waring 数 g(k, q)(F_q 中 k 次幂基的最小数)。猜想：g(k, q) ≤ k·(1 + o(1)) 且极限常数由 k 的最小素因子显式(Waring 上界精确)。
