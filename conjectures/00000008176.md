# 00000008176

**English.** Definition: The density table for computational verification of Artin's primitive root conjecture: the explicit values of the density delta_A(q) of primes q being a primitive root (Hooley explicit). Conjecture: delta_A(2) = 0.3739558136... (the explicit 2-value of the Artin constant, the product of (1 - 1/(p(p-1))) with the Legendre correction); the complete classification of the values of delta_A(q) (zero versus nonzero) is decided by q not congruent to 1 mod p^2 for all p (a non-Wieferich criterion); the minimal positive value of delta is attained at q = 2 (a 2-minimality law); and the arithmeticity of the density (rationality of delta): delta_A(q) is rational if and only if q is a perfect square or -4 times a square (a rationality criterion), the list of rational values being finite (a finite rational list). (Artin Hooley density rational classification)

**中文。** 定义：Artin 原根猜想的计算验证的密度表：素数 q 为原根的密度 δ_A(q) 的显式值（Hooley 显式）。猜想：δ_A(2) = 0.3739558136…（Artin 常数的 2-值的显式化：∏(1-1/(p(p-1))) 的勒让德修正）；且 δ_A(q) 的值的完全分类（零与非零）的判据为 q ≠ 1 mod p² 的一切 p（非 Wieferich 判据）；δ 的最小正值在 q=2 取得（2 最小律）；密度的算术性（δ 的有理性）的判定：δ_A(q) 为有理数当且仅当 q 为完全平方或 -4·平方（有理判据），有理值的列表有限（有限有理列表）。
