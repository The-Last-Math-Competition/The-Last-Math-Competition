**English.** Definition: The fundamental group is the topological fundamental group of the variety. Conjecture: Birational invariance holds for smooth projective types, it is trivial for rationally connected ones, and it is preserved under resolution in general. (rationally connected trivial dichotomy of the fundamental group)

**中文。** 定义：基本群为簇的拓扑基本群。猜想：光滑投射型的双有理不变性成立且有理连通时平凡；且一般情形由消解保持。（基本群有理连通平凡二分）
