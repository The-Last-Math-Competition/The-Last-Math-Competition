# 00000001792

**CV-12 ★** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: The k-Hessian of the complex Monge–Ampère operator is the real Monge–Ampère analogue of (dd^c)^k. Conjecture: On toric manifolds, solutions of the k-Hessian equation in arbitrary Kähler classes are algebraic functions (from the algebraic structure of the moment polytope); the regularity is C^∞ toric algebraicity.

**中文。** 定义：复 Monge–Ampère 算子的 k-Hessian 指 (dd^c)^k 的实 Monge–Ampère 类比。猜想：toric 流形上 k-Hessian 方程在任意 Kähler 类的解为代数函数(由 moment 多面体的代数结构);正则性为 C^∞ 的 toric 代数性。
