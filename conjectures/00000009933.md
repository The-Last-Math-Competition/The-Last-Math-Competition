# 00000009933

**English.** Definition: Manifold persistent homology estimation: minimax rates of recovering topological features from samples. Conjecture: The minimax rate of persistent homology estimation is the negative one-fifth power of the sample size; the exact exponent of the rate is given by the trade-off of dimension and boundary regularity, and the matching rate is tight. (manifold estimation minimax exponent)

**中文。** 定义：流形持久同调估计：从样本恢复拓扑特征的极小极大率。猜想：持久同调估计的极小极大率为样本量的负五分之一次幂；率的精确指数由维数与边界正则性的折衷给出且匹配率达到指数为紧。（流形估计极小极大指数）
