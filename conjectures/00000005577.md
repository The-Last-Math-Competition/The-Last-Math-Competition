# 00000005577

**English.** Definition: Composition factors are the components of the simple-group decomposition. Conjecture: The counting conversion is an explicit combination of partition numbers, corrected at the prime two by the distinctness condition of components, and the count is closed. (distinctness-corrected counting table of composition factors)
