**English.** Definition: Brascamp–Lieb constants (optimal constants of multivariate integrals for BL data). Conjecture: The constant of rank-1 BL data has a radical closed form if and only if the fractional matching polytope of its combinatorial matrix is integral (an integer-programming characterization of solvability).

**中文。** 定义：Brascamp–Lieb 常数(BL 数据的多元积分最优常数)。猜想：秩 1 BL 数据的常数为根式闭式当且仅当其组合矩阵的分数匹配多面体整(可解性判据的整数规划刻画)。
