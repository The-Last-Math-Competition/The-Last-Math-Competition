# 00000007623

**English.** Definition: Nilpotent elements as nilpotent structure. Conjecture: The nilpotency index is at most two and the index spectrum is complete; the exception of completeness is the degenerate type; and the exception of the degenerate type is nilpotency index three. (nilpotency-index-two degenerate exception)

**中文。** 定义：幂零元为幂零结构。猜想：幂零指数不超过二且指数谱完全；且完全的例外为退化型；且退化型的例外为幂零指数三。（幂零指数二例外退化）
