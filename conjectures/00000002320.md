# 00000002320

**English.** Conjecture: The spectrum of the probability P(G) of uniformly random generating pairs over all finite groups is an explicit dense subsequence of [1/4, 1]; the subsequence is realized by the PSL(2,p) family (P → 1/4 as p → ∞).

**中文。** 猜想：有限群的一致随机生成对的概率 P(G) 的谱(遍历一切有限群)为 [1/4,1] 的显式稠密子列;子列由 PSL(2,p) 族实现(P →1/4 当 p→∞)。
