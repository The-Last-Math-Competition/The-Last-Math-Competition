**English.** Definition: Toric ideals: kernel ideals of polytopal lattice configurations. Conjecture: The generation of toric ideals: generators are quadratic circuit relations of lattice points, with the minimal system of relations the Markov bases of the cycle space. (toric Markov basis circuit minimal system)
