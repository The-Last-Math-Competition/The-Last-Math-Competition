# 00000001975

**MP-15 ★** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: The RT invariant is the Reshetikhin–Turaev topological invariant at root q = e^{2πi/N}. Conjecture: The leading asymptotic of RT(M,q) is exp(N·(Vol+CS)/2π); the coefficient is the explicit Ray–Singer constant (the RT version of the Volume Conjecture); the phase is given by the CS invariant.

**中文。** 定义：RT 不变量指 Reshetikhin–Turaev 的根 q=e^{2πi/N} 下的拓扑不变量。猜想：RT(M,q) 的渐近主项为 exp(N·(Vol+CS)/2π);系数为显式 Ray–Singer 常数(即 Volume Conjecture 的 RT 版);相位由 CS 不变量给出。
