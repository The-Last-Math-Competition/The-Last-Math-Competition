# 00000008790

**NLA-20 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Randomized rounding of LP relaxations for integer programming: independent Bernoulli rounding with the fractional solution as expectation, and its pairwise correction (pipage rounding). Conjecture: The expected approximation ratio of randomized rounding for maximum-coverage-type problems is exactly 1 - 1/e, and pipage derandomization preserves the same ratio while eliminating variance; the objective difference between derandomized and independent rounding is at most 1; monotone perturbation of the LP constraints with radius delta eliminates worst-case instances, with delta always below n^{-2}; and pairwise negatively correlated rounding is always feasible over any down-closed constraint family. (pipage rounding negative correlation down-closed feasibility)

**中文。** 定义：整数规划的 LP 松解随机舍入：以小数解为期望的独立 Bernoulli 取整及其成对修正（pipage 舍入）。猜想：最大覆盖型问题随机舍入的期望近似比恰为 1-1/e 且 pipage 确定化保持同一比值并消除方差；确定化结果与独立取整的目标差不超过 1；以半径 δ 的单调扰动抖动 LP 约束可消除最坏实例且所需 δ 恒小于 n^{-2}；成对负相关舍入在任何向下闭包约束族上恒可行。
