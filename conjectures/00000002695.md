**English.** Definition: Arzelà–Zariski intersections: the joint structure of compactness conditions and spectral topology. Conjecture: Spectral stratification of equicontinuous function sheaves: the stratified dimension of the Zariski spectrum of the function sheaf equals the inflection count of the convex conjugate of the equicontinuity modulus; the inflections are combinatorially the independent parameters of extremal function families. (function sheaf spectral stratification dimension)
