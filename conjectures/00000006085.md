**English.** Definition: Acceleration is the momentum-driven fast method. Conjecture: The acceleration gain for nonconvex potentials is two for steep initial segments and square-root for chaotic landscapes, the combination of the gain being the curvature ratio. (curvature-ratio two-square-root gain window of acceleration)
