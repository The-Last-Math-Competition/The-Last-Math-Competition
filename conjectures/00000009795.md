# 00000009795

**DDG-05 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Spectral convergence of the cotan Laplacian: eigenvalue approximation of the cotangent discrete operator on triangulations. Conjecture: On uniformly mass meshes the first k eigenvalues of the cotan Laplacian converge to the smooth spectrum with error O(h) plus mesh quality factors; the mass-doubling scheme raises the error to O(h squared) and is stable on nonuniform meshes. (cotan spectral convergence order)

**中文。** 定义：cotan Laplacian 的谱收敛：三角剖分上余切离散算子的特征值逼近光滑谱。猜想：一致质量网格下 cotan Laplacian 的前 k 个特征值收敛到光滑谱且误差为 O(h) 加网格质量因子；加倍质量矩阵的方案误差升为 O(h²) 且对非均匀网格稳定。（cotan 谱收敛阶）
