**English.** Definition: eigenvalue approximation smooth spectrum of spectrum convergent: three angle triangulation on cotangent discrete operator of cotan Laplacian. Conjecture: front k element eigenvalue of uniform mass mesh under cotan Laplacian converges to smooth spectrum and error is error of O(h) mesh mass factor; doubling mass matrix is O(h²) and pair nonuniform mesh stable. (cotan spectrum order of convergence)

**中文。** 定义：cotan Laplacian 的谱收敛：三角剖分上余切离散算子的特征值逼近光滑谱。猜想：一致质量网格下 cotan Laplacian 的前 k 个特征值收敛到光滑谱且误差为 O(h) 加网格质量因子；加倍质量矩阵的方案误差升为 O(h²) 且对非均匀网格稳定。（cotan 谱收敛阶）
