# 00000007973

**English.** Definition: The modular-form representation of weights of algebraic-geometric codes (Goppa codes) under the complete Weierstrass semigroup spectrum of the curve: the Fourier expansion coefficients of the weight enumerator of the code C(X,D,G). Conjecture: The weight enumerator equals a theta series (a lattice-ized modular form) times an exponential-sum correction, the correction term being a finite convolution of the coefficients of the Hasse-Weil L-function of the curve (Osterle type); the leading term of the weights (the minimum weight) is deg D - g (exact Riemann-Roch); and the second-smallest weight list is uniquely determined by the neighborhood structure of the Weierstrass gap set (the Weierstrass realization of the complete weight spectrum of Goppa codes). (Goppa theta L-convolution weight spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：代数几何码（Goppa 码）在曲线的 Weierstrass 半群完全谱下的权重的模形式表示：码 C(X,D,G) 的重量枚举的 Fourier 展开系数。猜想：重量计数器 = theta 级数（格化的模形式）· 指数和修正；修正项为曲线的 Hasse–Weil L 函数的系数的有限次卷积（Oesterlé 型）；且权重的首项（最小权）= deg D-g（Riemann–Roch 精确）；次小权列表由 Weierstrass 间隙集的邻域结构唯一决定（Goppa 码的完全权谱的 Weierstrass 实现）。
