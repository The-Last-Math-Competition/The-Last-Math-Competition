# 00000005562

**English.** Definition: Exotic fusion systems are fusion types not realized by groups. Conjecture: The embedding criterion of rank-two exotic fusion is an explicit condition of classical group type, with special linear type completely delimited by the parity of the field; in odd characteristic the embedding is unique, the uniqueness given by the vanishing of automorphisms; and in even characteristic the counterexamples are finite in number, a power of 2. (parity-delimited embedding criterion of exotic fusion)

**中文。** 定义：例外融合：非群实现的融合型（融合系）。猜想：秩二例外融合的内嵌判据为典型群型的显式条件且特殊线性型由域的奇偶完全分界；奇特征域的内嵌为唯一且唯一性由自同构的消没给出；偶特征的反例为有限个且个数为 2 的幂。（例外内嵌奇偶分界判据）
