# 00000009918

**English.** Definition: Critical windows of random complex topology: the asymptotic shape of Betti number peaks. Conjecture: The peak height of Betti numbers of Poisson Cech complexes has an explicit limit, and the peak width shrinks as the negative one-fifth power of n; the limit curve of the peak shape is a symmetric log-concave function. (Betti peak limit shape)

**中文。** 定义：随机复形拓扑的临界窗口：Betti 数峰值的渐近形状。猜想：泊松 Čech 的 Betti 数峰值高度有显式极限且峰宽按 n 的负五分之一幂收缩；峰值形状的极限曲线为对称的对数上凸函数。（Betti 峰值极限形状）
