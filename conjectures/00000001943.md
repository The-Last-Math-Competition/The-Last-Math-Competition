# 00000001943

**LG-13 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: The equidistribution of CM points (Duke's theorem gives their equidistribution on modular curves). Conjecture: The proportion of CM points of discriminant −d at distance > ε from the boundary is exp(−c√d) (with the optimal exponent 1/2); this rate is the optimal effective version of Duke uniformization.

**中文。** 定义：CM 点的等分布(Duke 定理指其在 modular 曲线上等分布)。猜想：判别式 −d 的 CM 点到边界的距离 >ε 的比例为 exp(−c\sqrt d)(最优指数 1/2);该率为 Duke 均匀化的最优有效版。
