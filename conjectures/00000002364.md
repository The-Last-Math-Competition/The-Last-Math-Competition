**English.** Definition: The Taylor calculus is the Koszul-complex function calculus of several operators. Conjecture: Its exactness (solvability) holds automatically outside the Taylor joint spectrum on convex domains; the minimal extension is the two-layer polynomial convex hull (the layer count is exact).

**中文。** 定义：Taylor 演算指多算子的 Koszul 复形函数演算。猜想：其精确性(可解性)在凸域的 Taylor 联合谱外自动成立;最小扩展为多项式凸包的 2 层(层数精确)。
