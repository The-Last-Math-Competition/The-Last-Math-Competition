# 00000006248

**GT5-28 ★** — source: 193_几何范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Homotopy sheaves are the homotopy-group structure of sheaves, with the pasting law. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the pasting law and the sheaf-version classical pasting theorem holds; the coefficient of pasting is unique, the deviation of uniqueness being the refinement of covers; and the stalk of homotopy-sheaf groups is the homotopy group of the point, with vanishing of stalks at locally contractible points. (unique pasting-coefficient conversion of homotopy sheaves)

**中文。** 定义：同伦层：层的同伦群结构；粘贴律。猜想：换算常数为粘贴律的显式且层版的经典粘贴定理成立；粘贴的系数唯一且唯一的偏差为覆盖的 refinement；同伦群层的茎为点的同伦群且茎的消没在局部可缩处。（同伦群层粘贴系数唯一换算）
