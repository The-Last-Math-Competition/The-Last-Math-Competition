# 00000009590

**MSH-10 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The Chern gap. Conjecture: The gap phenomenon for minimal hypersurfaces of S^{n+1} with bounded ||A||^2, where ||A||^2 = n gives the Clifford torus; there exists an explicit epsilon_n > 0 such that minimal hypersurfaces with ||A||^2 at most n + epsilon_n are only of the Clifford torus and equatorial sphere types; and the optimal value of the second gap epsilon_n is an explicit rational number from the Cartan coefficient table. (Chern second gap)

**中文。** 定义：Chern 间隙。猜想：S^{n+1} 中 ||A||² 有界的极小超曲面的间隙现象；已知 ||A||²=n 时为 Clifford 环面。猜想：存在显式 ε_n>0 使 ||A||² ≤ n+ε_n 的极小超曲面只有 Clifford 环面与赤道球面两类；第二间隙 ε_n 的最优值为 Cartan 系数表的显式有理数。（Chern 第二间隙）
