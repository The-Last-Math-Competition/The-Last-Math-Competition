**English.** Definition: Schwinger functions are the correlation functions of a Euclidean field theory. Conjecture: The characteristic scale of their Euclidean invariance is read off explicitly from the correlation decay length, and the scale diverges at the phase transition point with divergence exponent 1. (critical exponent of the Schwinger characteristic scale)

**中文。** 定义：Schwinger 函数指欧氏场论的关联函数。猜想：其欧氏不变性的特征尺度由关联衰减长度显式读出,尺度在相变点发散且发散指数为 1。（Schwinger 特征尺度临界指数）
