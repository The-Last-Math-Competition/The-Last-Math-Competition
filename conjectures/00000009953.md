# 00000009953

**English.** Definition: The entropy gap: positive entropy of invariant measures of diagonal flows on lattice quotients implies algebraicity (several cases proven). Conjecture: The algebraicity of positive-entropy invariant measures is complete at the critical case of entropy one: measures with entropy exactly maximal are Haar, and the algebraic approximation rate of perturbed measures is controlled by an explicit exponent of the entropy deficit. (critical entropy algebraic rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：熵隙：对角流在格商上的不变测度的正熵蕴含代数性（多项已证）。猜想：正熵不变测度的代数性在熵为一的临界情形完全：熵恰为极大的测度为 Haar 且扰动测度的代数逼近率由熵亏损的显式指数控制。（临界熵代数刚性）
