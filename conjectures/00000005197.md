**English.** Definition: A Gerstenhaber structure is a graded commutative structure with a bracket. Conjecture: Its conversion is given explicitly by the second-order action structure, closed at third-order Hochschild cohomology with bracket normalization constant one. (second-order action closure of Gerstenhaber structures)

**中文。** 定义：格斯滕哈伯结构指带括号的分次交换结构。猜想：其换算由二阶作用结构显式给出,三阶 Hochschild 处闭合且括号归一常数为一。（格斯滕哈伯二阶作用闭合）
