**English.** Definition: Campana points are rational points satisfying orbifold denominator divisibility conditions. Conjecture: The count of Campana points of (P¹, 2[0]+2[1]+2[∞]) is always c times the square root of B divided by the square root of log B; and the logarithmic correction is given by the additive-energy duality. (Campana point count logarithmic correction)

**中文。** 定义：Campana 点指满足 orbifold 分母整除条件的有理点。猜想：(P1,2[0]+2[1]+2[∞]) 的 Campana 点计数恒为 c 乘 B 的二分之一次幂除 logB 的二分之一次幂；且对数修正由加性能量对偶给出。（Campana 点计数对数修正）
