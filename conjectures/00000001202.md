**English.** Definition: The octal game 0.07 (each move removes 1 or 2 tokens, with the post-removal constraint encoded by 0.07). Conjecture: Its SG sequence is eventually periodic with period 12 and pre-period length 4; i.e. g(n+12) = g(n) for all n ≥ 4.
