# 00000007851

**English.** Definition: The optimal target dimension m_{epsilon,n} of a Johnson-Lindenstrauss random projection embedding n points with epsilon-separation. Conjecture: The optimal lower bound m >= min(n-1, c(1+epsilon^2)^{-1} log n log(1/epsilon)) matches the upper bound; and the optimal sparsity of the sparse JL transform is s* = Theta(epsilon^{-1}), with any sparser distribution suffering a failure probability jumping to the explicit exponential 1 - epsilon^{-s} on worst-case point sets, the jump width determined by the antipodal K_2 structure count of the point set. (JL dimension and sparse jump)

**中文。** 定义：Johnson–Lindenstrauss 随机投影的最优常数维度：将 n 点 ε-分离嵌入所需的随机目标维度 m_ε,n。猜想：最优下界 m ≥ min(n-1, c(1+ε²)^{-1}·log n·log(1/ε)) 与上界匹配；且稀疏 JL 变换的最优稀疏度 s*=Θ(ε^{-1})，任何更稀疏的分布在最坏点集上失败概率跳升为 1-ε^{-s} 的显式指数；该跳变的宽度由点集的Antipodal K_2 结构计数决定。
