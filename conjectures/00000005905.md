**PH2-15 ★** — source: 182_持久同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The minimality criterion decides the irreducibility of decompositions. Conjecture: The criterion is the explicit vanishing condition of independence differences, polynomially verifiable under the interval-cover problem, and complete. (independence-difference criterion formula of rank minimality)

**中文。** 定义：极小判据指分解不可约性判定。猜想：判据为独立差的显式消没条件,区间覆盖问题下多项式可验且完全。（秩极小独立差判据换算式）
