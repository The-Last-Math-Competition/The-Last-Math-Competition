# 00000002735

**SC-25 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Stacked spheres: obtained by repeatedly coning over faces. Conjecture: The Upper Bound Theorem for stacked spheres (Novik): stacked spheres attain the f-vector upper bound, with the effective range of the bound an explicit interval in the overlap degree s; outside the interval, the extremal complexes are minimal corrections of cyclic polytopes. (stacked upper bound theorem interval)

**中文。** 定义：s-叠（stacked）球面：反复在面上加锥所得。猜想：s-叠球面的 Upper Bound 定理（Novik）：s-叠球面达到 f-向量上界且上界的有效范围与重叠度 s 的关系为显式区间；区间外的极值复形为循环多面体的极小修正。（s-叠上界定理区间）
