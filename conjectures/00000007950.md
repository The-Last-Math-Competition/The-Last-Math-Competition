# 00000007950

**INT-20 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The isomorphism classes of transcendents: the rigidity of Painleve transcendents (classical solutions y(t) of Painleve VI are new transcendental functions). Conjecture: Solutions of PVI with different parameters are inequivalent under algebraic correspondences t to (at+b)/(ct+d) and fractional transformations of y, except for a finite exceptional group (icosahedral exceptions); and the density criterion for the image of the monodromy representation of the analytic continuation of PVI in SU(2) is given by explicit integrals of rigid-body orbit connections (Tyrrell curves of isomonodromy); in the special case c = 0 the monodromy of the hypergeometricized solution is a triangle group (p,q,r), and the algebraicity criterion 1/p + 1/q + 1/r > 1 for PVI corresponds exactly to the Schwarz triangle list. (PVI transcendent rigidity and Schwarz list)

**中文。** 定义：transcendenta 的同构类：Painlevé 超越量的刚性（Painlevé VI 的经典解 y(t) 为新的超越函数）。猜想：不同参数的 PVI 解在代数对应 t→(at+b)/(ct+d) 与分式变换 y→… 下不等价，除有限例外群（icosahedral 例外）；且 PVI 的解析延拓的单值表示的像在 SU(2) 的稠密性判据由刚体轨道连接（isomonodromy 的 Tyrrell 曲线）的显式积分给出；特例 c=0 的超几何化的解的 monodromy 为三角群 (p,q,r)，PVI 的代数性判据 (1/p+1/q+1/r>1) 完全对应 Schwarz 三角列表。
