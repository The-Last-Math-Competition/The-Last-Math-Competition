# 00000004870

**English.** Definition: The Green decay and the boundary dimension are two group invariants. Conjecture: There exist two hyperbolic groups with the same Green decay exponent but different boundary dimensions, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of groups with the same exponent and different fractal dimensions. (same Green exponent-boundary dimension separation)
