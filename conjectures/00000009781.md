**OIE-21 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Pseudospectral growth and nonnormality: areas of epsilon-pseudospectra and the Kreiss constant. Conjecture: The growth rate of pseudospectral areas is controlled by the Kreiss constant with exponentially tight bound; operators attaining exponential growth are constructed by explicit ladders of shifted weighted powers, with ladder weights geometric progressions. (pseudospectrum-Kreiss exponential law)

**中文。** 定义：伪谱的增长与非正规性：ε-伪谱的面积与 Kreiss 常数。猜想：伪谱面积的增长率受 Kreiss 常数控制且界为指数级紧；达到指数增长的算子由移位的加权幂的显式梯子构造且梯子的权重为几何级数。（伪谱-Kreiss 指数律）
