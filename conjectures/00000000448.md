# 00000000448

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative Schur functions form the Schur-like basis of NCSF. Conjecture: The value of the noncommutative s_λ under the principal specialization agrees with the commutative case; and the agreement is preserved by a determinant identity. (noncommutative specialization compatibility)
