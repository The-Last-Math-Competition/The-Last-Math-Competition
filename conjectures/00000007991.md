# 00000007991

**DEL-01 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The optimal exponent alpha(p) of C^{1,alpha} regularity of solutions of the p-Laplace equation div(|grad u|^{p-2} grad u) = 0. Conjecture: The exact value of alpha(p) is the explicit expression min((p-1)/(2p-2 + p lambda_0), 1/(p-1)) with lambda_0 an explicit eigenvalue; alpha(p) returns to 1 at p = 2 (the harmonic limit); the slope limits of the graph of alpha(p) at the two endpoints p to infinity and p to 1 are -infinity and +infinity (endpoint cusps); and alpha(p) is a strictly decreasing continuous function of p on the segment p > 2. (p-Laplace optimal Holder exponent)

**中文。** 定义：p-Laplace 方程 div(|∇u|^{p-2}∇u)=0 的解的 C^{1,α} 正则性的最优指数 α(p)。猜想：α(p) 的精确值为 min((p-1)/(2p-2 + p·λ₀), 1/(p-1)) 型显式式（λ₀ 为某显式特征值）；且 α(p) 在 p=2 回到 1（调和极限）；α(p) 的图像在 p→∞ 与 p→1 两个端点的斜率极限为 -∞ 与 +∞（端点尖性）；α(p) 为 p 的严格递减连续函数（在 p>2 段）。
