**English.** Definition: The border rank is the rank of the lowest degeneration target orbit. Conjecture: The equation domain of the border rank is a multilinear system and the symmetrization spectrum gives explicit generation; and the dimension of the domain is computed by the Hilbert function. (symmetrized border-rank equations)
