**English.** Definition: Monotonicity of mean field games (MFG). Conjecture: Uniqueness of MFG solutions is equivalent to Lasry–Lions monotonicity, and explicit non-monotone counterexamples exist (the boundary of uniqueness).

**中文。** 定义：平均场博弈(MFG)的单调性。猜想：MFG 的唯一解与 Lasry–Lions 单调性等价且存在非单调的显式反例(唯一性边界)。
