**English.** Definition: A two-cell spectrum is a finite spectrum built from two cells. Conjecture: Its classification is a finite table given by all admissible types of Adams differentials, closed with no unknown types. (finite closed classification table of two-cell spectra)

**中文。** 定义：双胞谱指由两个胞腔构成的有限谱。猜想：其分类为有限表,由 Adams 微分的全部容许型给出且分类封闭无未知型。（双胞谱分类有限封闭表）
