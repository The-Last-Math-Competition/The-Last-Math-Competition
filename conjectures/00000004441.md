**English.** Definition: The duality of the KPZ fixed point is the space-time inversion symmetry. Conjecture: Its explicit conversion is the composition of a Galilean transformation with inversion, the conversion coefficient is an explicit combination in 1/2, and it is closed under stationary initial data. (closed coefficients of the KPZ duality conversion)
