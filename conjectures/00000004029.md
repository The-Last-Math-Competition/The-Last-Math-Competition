**RP2-29 ★** — source: 119_随机分析II_粗路径.md

**English.** Definition: A fractal path is a random curve of Hausdorff dimension beta > 1. Conjecture: The n-th level of the signature satisfies the self-similarity S^n(cX) = c^{n/beta} S^n(X), and the critical dimension for a nontrivial renormalized limit is beta = 2n/(n+1). (fractal signature self-similarity threshold)

**中文。** 定义：分形路径指 Hausdorff 维数 β > 1 的随机曲线。猜想：签名的第 n 层满足自相似 S^n(cX) = c^{n/β}S^n(X),重整化极限非平凡的临界维数为 β = 2n/(n+1)。（分形签名自相似阈）
