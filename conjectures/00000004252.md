**English.** Definition: The DT-PT conversion is the wall-crossing formula between Donaldson-Thomas and stable-pair invariants. Conjecture: The conversion factor is an explicit power of the MacMahon function, with exponent given by the D^6 coefficient of the curve class, and the formula is universal for all threefolds. (DT-PT MacMahon power formula)

**中文。** 定义：DT-PT 换算指 Donaldson–Thomas 与稳定对不变量间的壁穿越公式。猜想：换算因子为 MacMahon 函数的显式幂,幂指数由曲线类的 D⁶ 系数给出,公式对一切三维折叠普适。（DT-PT MacMahon 幂公式）
