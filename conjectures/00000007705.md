# 00000007705

**English.** Definition: The decidability boundary of tiling logic: for fixed dimension d and monotone second-order fragment F, comp(d,F) denotes the complexity of tiling satisfiability in F. Conjecture: comp(2,F)=PSPACE when F has alternation depth at most 2, and becomes undecidable with a third quantifier layer; for d>=3 the first-order tiling theory is already undecidable, with the minimal undecidable predicate set being the pair {adjacency, periodicity}. (quantifier-depth decidability boundary)

**中文。** 定义：铺砌逻辑的可判定性边界：对固定维数 d 与单调二阶逻辑片段 F，F 中铺砌可满足性问题的复杂度记 comp(d,F)。猜想：comp(2,F)=PSPACE 当 F 限于 ∀∃ 前束深度 ≤2，而加入第三层量词后为不可判定；d≥3 时一阶铺砌理论已不可判定，其最小不可判定谓词集为 {邻接, 周期性} 二元组。
