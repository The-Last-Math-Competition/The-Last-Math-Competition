**RM3-30 △** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The Poisson–WD transition is the parametrized transition of point processes from Poisson to Wigner–Dyson statistics. Conjecture: There exist explicit sequences of potentials and β such that bulk statistics undergo infinitely many Poisson-to-WD round trips, with adjacent round-trip parameter intervals decreasing geometrically. (infinite transition round-trip sequence)

**中文。** 定义：Poisson–WD 过渡指点过程从 Poisson 到 Wigner–Dyson 统计的参数化过渡。猜想：存在位势与 β 的显式序列使 bulk 统计沿序列经历无穷多次 Poisson 与 WD 的往返,且相邻往返的参数区间长度按几何级数递减。（无穷过渡往返序列）
