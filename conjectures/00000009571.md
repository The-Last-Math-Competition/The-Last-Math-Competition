# 00000009571

**English.** Definition: Teichmueller curves are isometrically embedded algebraic curves in modular surfaces; the complete classification at genus at least 3 is unfinished. Conjecture: All Teichmueller curves form a finite list with finite maximal algebraic genus; Teichmueller curves with extra real multiplication have density zero among isomorphism classes and are filtered by the arithmetic height of intersections with Hilbert modular surfaces. (Teichmueller curve finiteness)

**中文。** 定义：Teichmüller 曲线为模曲面内的等距嵌入代数曲线；亏格 ≥3 的完全分类未完成。猜想：一切 Teichmüller 曲线构成有限列表，最大代数亏格有限；带额外实乘法的 Teichmüller 曲线在同构类下密度为零且由 Hilbert 模曲面交点的算术高度过滤。（Teichmüller 曲线有限性）
