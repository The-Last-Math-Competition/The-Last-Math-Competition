# 00000009884

**English.** Definition: Cluster categories and the Caldero-Chapoton map: the correspondence between cluster variables and cluster coefficients of rigid objects. Conjecture: A cluster coefficient formula exists for higher cluster categories: coefficients are polynomial functionals of the Euler form of R^{(m)}-homogeneous objects; the formula returns to Caldero-Chapoton at m = 1, and the exponents of the coefficients are given by the m-cluster structure of the object. (higher cluster coefficient formula)

**中文。** 定义：Cluster 范畴与 Caldero–Chapoton 映射：簇变量与刚性对象的簇系数对应。猜想：高阶簇范畴的簇系数公式存在：系数为 R^{(m)}-齐次对象的 Euler 形式的多项式泛函；公式在 m=1 回到 Caldero–Chapoton 且系数的指数由对象的 m-丛结构给出。（高阶簇系数公式）
