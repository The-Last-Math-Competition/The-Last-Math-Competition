# 00000009873

**English.** Definition: Fractional-time KPZ: the KPZ equation with the time derivative replaced by a fractional one. Conjecture: The fluctuation exponent of fractional-time KPZ is the time-fractional index gamma times the KPZ exponent: fluctuations grow as t^{gamma/3}; the spatial correlation length grows as t^{2 gamma/3}, and the ratio stays 1:2 for all gamma. (fractional KPZ exponent spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：分数时间 KPZ：时间分数导数替换的 KPZ 方程。猜想：分数时间 KPZ 的涨落指数为时间分数指标 γ 乘以 KPZ 指数：涨落按 t^{γ/3}；空间相关长度按 t^{2γ/3} 且比值在一切 γ 保持 1:2。（分数 KPZ 指数谱）
