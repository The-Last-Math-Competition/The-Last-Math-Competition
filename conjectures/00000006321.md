# 00000006321

**English.** Definition: Permanent approximation is the polynomial randomized approximation of counting. Conjecture: Nonnegative matrices are approximable but signed matrices are not, with explicit transition bound, the kernel of the transition being the sign lattice. (sign-lattice transition of permanent approximation)

**中文。** 定义：永久近似为计数的多项式随机近似。猜想：非负矩阵可近似而带号矩阵不可且相变界显式；且相变的核为符号格。（永久近似符号格相变）
