# 00000009935

**English.** Definition: Regularity of expected persistence measures: absolute continuity of persistence measures of random point processes. Conjecture: The expected persistence measure is absolutely continuous if and only if the overlap function of the point process decays as O(n^{-1}); the exact threshold form is given by the persistence-pair counting kernel of the diagonal neighborhood. (persistence measure absolute continuity threshold)

**中文。** 定义：期望持久测度的正则性：随机点过程的持久测度的绝对连续。猜想：期望持久测度绝对连续当且仅当点过程的重叠函数满足 O(n^{-1}) 衰减；阈值的精确形式由对角线邻域的持久对计数核给出。（持久测度绝对连续阈值）
