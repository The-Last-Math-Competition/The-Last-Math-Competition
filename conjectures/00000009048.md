**English.** Definition: Lawson's inequality: the area-Willmore competition for minimal surfaces. Conjecture: The competition bound between area and the Willmore functional always holds; and equality in the bound is the Veronese surface. (Lawson competition Veronese equality)

**中文。** 定义：Lawson 不等式为极小曲面的面积-Willmore 竞争。猜想：面积与 Willmore 泛函的竞争界恒成立；且界的等号为 Veronese 曲面。（Lawson 竞争界 Veronese 等号）
