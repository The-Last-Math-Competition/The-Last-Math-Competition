# 00000005481

**IF2-11 ★** — source: 168_数学物理IV_可积场论.md

**English.** Definition: Form factors are scattering matrix element functions satisfying the Watson-type axiom system. Conjecture: The residue conversion of the three-particle pole is an explicit elementary expression of the fusion recursion, verified consistently for orthogonal groups; the polarization structure of the residue is given by the symmetrization of the fusion channel, with factorial-type symmetrization constant; and the closed layer count of the recursion is linear in the particle number with slope 1. (three-pole fusion recursion of form factors)

**中文。** 定义：形式因子：满足公理系（ Watson 型）的散射矩阵元函数。猜想：三粒子极点的留数换算为聚合递推的显式初等式且正交群情形验证一致；留数的极化结构由聚合通道的对称化给出且对称化常数为阶乘型；递推的封闭层数为粒子数的线性且斜率为 1。（形式因子三极点聚合递推）
