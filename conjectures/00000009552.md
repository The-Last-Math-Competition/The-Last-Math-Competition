# 00000009552

**TCH-02 ★** — source: 304_Teichmuller动力.md

**English.** Definition: The Masur-Veech volume vol of a stratum H(a_1,...,a_n) is given by Eskin-Okounkov counting; the asymptotic shape of volumes as singularity orders grow proportionally is unknown. Conjecture: The main exponent of the volume asymptotics is the dimension formula 2(sum a_i + n) - 1, secondary correction coefficients are rational functions of vertex counts of periodic graphs, and all coefficients admit explicit recursion; and the volume ratio vol H(2)/vol H(1,1) is an explicit rational number near 3/4. (stratum volume asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：stratum H(a_1,…,a_n) 的 Masur–Veech 体积 vol 由 Eskin–Okounkov 通过计数给出；体积在奇点阶数按比例增长时的渐近形状未知。猜想：体积渐近的主指数为 2(Σa_i+n)−1 的维数公式，次级修正系数为周期图顶点计数的有理函数，全部系数可显式递推；体积比 vol H(2)/vol H(1,1) 的精确值为 3/4 附近的显式有理数。（stratum 体积渐近）
