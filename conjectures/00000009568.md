**English.** Definition: The main term of the Mirzakhani-Zograf asymptotics of Weil-Petersson volumes V_{g,n} is known; the explicitness of all lower-order coefficients is incomplete. Conjecture: The correction sequence of the WP volume asymptotics is an explicit combination of polynomials in pi times zeta values, with the correction series diverging after truncation at the g^{2/3} term in 1/g; within the truncation window all coefficients have closed forms. (WP volume full explicitness)

**中文。** 定义：Weil–Petersson 体积 V_{g,n} 的 Mirzakhani–Zograf 渐近主项已知，全部低阶系数的显式性未完成。猜想：WP 体积渐近的修正项序列为 π 的多项式乘 zeta 值的显式组合，修正级数截断于 1/g 的 g^{2/3} 项后发散；截断窗口内系数全部有闭式。（WP 体积全显式化）
