# 00000008328

**English.** Definition: The depth rp_deep of Robin approximation of quadratic irrationals: the preperiodic depth of continued fraction cycles (periods). Conjecture: rp_deep (the upper bound on period depth of discriminant D) is the double correction O(sqrt D log log D) (a double correction bound); the extremal distribution of depth (deepest D) is anticorrelated with the class number (an anticorrelation law); the asymptotics of the complexity of D (the sum of depth and period length l) is the product log D sqrt D (a product law); and the density of the family of minimal-complexity D (shallow and short) is the Euler factor 1/zeta(2) (an Euler factor density law). (quadratic period depth class number anticorrelation)

**中文。** 定义：二次无理数的 Robin 逼近的深度：连分数的圈（周期）的预周期深度 rp_deep。猜想：rp_deep（判别式 D 的周期深度的上界）= O(√D·log log D) 的双修正（双修正界）；且深度的极值（最深的 D）的分布为类数的反相关（反相关律）；深度与周期长 ℓ 的和（D 的复杂度）的渐近为 log D·√D 的乘积（乘积律）；极小复杂度的 D（浅且短）的族的密度为 1/ζ(2) 的 Euler 因子（Euler 因子密度律）。
