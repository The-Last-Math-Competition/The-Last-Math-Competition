**ET3-09 ★** — source: 236_平展上同调II.md

**English.** Definition: The Gysin sequence characterizes the cohomological shift of closed embeddings. Conjecture: The shift deficit equals the codimension, the deficit spectrum being given explicitly by characteristic classes; and the generators of the spectrum are powers of the Euler class. (codimension-deficit Gysin shift)

**中文。** 定义：Gysin 序列刻画闭嵌入的上同调位移。猜想：位移的亏格等于余维数且亏谱由示性类显式给出；且谱的生成元为 Euler 类的幂。（Gysin 位移亏格余维）
