**INP-03 ★** — source: 273_可积概率.md

**English.** Definition: ASEP is the partially asymmetric exclusion process. Conjecture: The crossing count of critical points of the amplitude function always differs by 1 across the phase transition line; and the jump locus is an explicit algebraic curve. (ASEP crossing jump curve)

**中文。** 定义：ASEP 指部分不对称排斥过程。猜想：振幅函数的临界点交叉计数在相变线两侧恒差 1；跳变点为显式代数曲线。（ASEP 交叉跳变曲线）
