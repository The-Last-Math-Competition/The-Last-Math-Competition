**MIB-21 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The motivic Hodge ideal is the motivic lift of the Hodge ideal of a singularity. Conjecture: On weighted homogeneous singularities the motivic Hodge ideal is completely determined by the motivic polynomial of the spectrum; the coefficients of the spectrum polynomial are integers, nonnegative term by term; and the motivic layers of the ideal form a motivic layer chain in degenerating families, the number of saturation steps of the chain being the number of discrete values of the singularity. (motivic Hodge ideal spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：母题 Hodge 理想指奇点 Hodge 理想的母题化。猜想：母题 Hodge 理想在加权齐次奇点上由谱的母题多项式完全决定；谱多项式的系数为整数且逐项非负；理想的母题层在退化族中构成母题层链，链的饱和步数为奇点离散值的个数。（母题 Hodge 理想谱）
