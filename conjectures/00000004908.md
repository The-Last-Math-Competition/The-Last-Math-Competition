# 00000004908

**English.** Definition: The value function of a partially observable MDP is the optimal expected value function. Conjecture: Its number of piecewise-linear segments grows double-exponentially in the time step, with base an explicit power of two in the observation dimension, and the lower bound is realized by an explicit construction. (double-exponential growth of POMDP segments)

**中文。** 定义：部分可观察 MDP 的值函数指最优期望值函数。猜想：其分段线性段数按时步双指数增长,增长底为二的观测维数次幂型显式且下界由显式结构实现。（POMDP 段数双指数增长）
