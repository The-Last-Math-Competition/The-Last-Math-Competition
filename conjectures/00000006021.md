**English.** Definition: Arnold diffusion is the slow drift phenomenon. Conjecture: The upper-bound chain of diffusion rates is controlled by the splitting-angle exponents of heteroclinic orbits, the order of each connection explicit, and the kernel of the order is the splitting power law. (heteroclinic-chain upper bound of splitting power laws)
