**FL-27 ★** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: A stop of the wrapped Fukaya category is a geometric obstruction point removed in a partial compactification. Conjecture: Two stops give isomorphic wrapped categories if and only if the Legendrians given by their conormal lifts are isotopic, and the isotopy criterion is furnished by explicit invariants of the contact neighborhoods. (stop isotopy equivalence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：wrapped Fukaya 范畴的 stop 指部分紧化中被删去的几何阻碍点。猜想：两个 stop 给出同构的 wrapped 范畴当且仅当其余法提升的 Legendrian 同痕;同痕判据由切触邻域的显式不变量给出。（stop 同痕等价判据）
