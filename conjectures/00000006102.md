# 00000006102

**English.** Definition: The logarithmic Sobolev inequality relates entropy and energy. Conjecture: The sphere constant is the explicit dimension-reciprocal interpolation tending to the Gaussian value at large dimension, and the error is quadratic in the reciprocal. (dimension-interpolation Gaussian limit of the spherical LSI)

**中文。** 定义：对数 Sobolev 为熵与能量的不等式。猜想：球面常数为维数倒数的显式插值且大维极限趋于高斯值；且误差为倒数二次。（球面 LSI 维数插值高斯极限）
