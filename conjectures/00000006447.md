**English.** Definition: Subconvexity is the convexity-breaking property, the subconvex bound. Conjecture: The subconvex-exponent target and the current progress stand side by side and the method of progress is the index sum; the target is 1/6 and the current is 1/4 minus epsilon; and the gap between the poles is controlled by the cancellation degree of exponential sums. (index-sum progress of subconvex exponents)
