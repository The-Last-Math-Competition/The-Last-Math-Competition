# 00000009666

**English.** Definition: Random Toeplitz matrices: the limit of empirical eigenvalue distributions of matrices with iid constant diagonals. Conjecture: The empirical spectral measure of random Toeplitz matrices converges to an explicit non-universal symmetric limit measure; the density is bounded with support endpoints explicit values of complete elliptic integrals of the first kind, and the Toeplitz and Hankel limit measures are mirror images of each other. (Toeplitz limit measure explicit)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Toeplitz 矩阵：常对角线独立同分布元素的谱的经验分布极限。猜想：随机 Toeplitz 矩阵的谱经验测度收敛到显式非普适对称极限测度；密度有界且支撑端点为第一类完全椭圆积分的显式值，Toeplitz 与 Hankel 的极限测度互为镜像。（Toeplitz 极限测度显式）
