# 00000000355

**English.** Definition: The shortest-vector distribution of random lattices under the Siegel measure. Conjecture: The proportion of random lattices with four prescribed independent shortest vectors is an explicit rational value; and the denominator of that rational divides 24. (random lattice shortest-vector proportion)

**中文。** 定义：随机格在 Siegel 测度下的最短向量分布。猜想：指定四个独立最短向量的随机格比例为显式有理值；且有理值的分母为二十四的因子。（随机格最短向量比例）
