**English.** Definition: Lattice path enumeration: skew-shape paths in Young's lattice. Conjecture: The count of nonsymmetric skew tableaux is a flagged-type degeneration of Schur functions, the degeneration uniquely determined by the minimal covering of the flags. (skew tableau nonsymmetric counting)
