**English.** Definition: The entropy capacity is the canning-type upper-bound variant of entropy. Conjecture: The conversion constant of capacity to topological entropy is an explicit function of the variation exponent, it takes one for hyperbolic systems, and the bound cannot be improved. (variation conversion function of entropy capacity)
