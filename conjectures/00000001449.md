# 00000001449

**English.** Definition: The intensity of conformal loop ensembles. Conjecture: The fractal exponent of the loop intensity of CLE_κ, for κ in the interval from 8/3 to 4 (left-closed, right-open), is (8−κ)(3κ−8)/(32κ) (the CLE loop exponent).

**中文。** 定义：Brownian 共形 loop ensemble 的强度。猜想：CLE_κ 的环强度的分形指数在 κ 从 8/3 到 4 的左开右闭区间的公式为 (8 − κ)(3κ − 8)/(32κ)(CLE 环指数)。
