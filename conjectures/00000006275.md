# 00000006275

**English.** Definition: Finitary isomorphism is isomorphism with continuous codes. Conjecture: Code lengths are integrable and the expectation is controlled by the information dimension; the bound is the reciprocal integral of entropy; and the bound of the reciprocal integral is given by the information density. (information-dimension bound of integrable code lengths)

**中文。** 定义：有限性同构为带连续代码的同构。猜想：码长可积且期望由信息维控制；且控制的界为熵倒积；且熵倒积的界由信息密度给出。（码长可积信息维界）
