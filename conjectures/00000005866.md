**English.** Definition: The stability condition is the inf-sup condition of mixed schemes. Conjecture: The stability constant of the classical velocity-pressure pair attains a uniform lower bound on regular families, and the kernel of the bound is the Fortin projection. (regular uniform lower bound of the inf-sup Fortin projection)
