# 00000001873

**English.** Definition: A bad group is a connected finite-Morley-rank group with non-algebraic group structure. Conjecture: The Morley rank of a bad group is at least 3 (ranks 1 and 2 are algebraic); if a rank-3 bad group exists, it has no nontrivial definable subgroups (a strong constraint).

**中文。** 定义：bad group 指非代数群结构的连通有限 Morley 秩群。猜想：bad 群的 Morley 秩 r ≥ 3(秩 1、2 皆为代数);秩 3 的 bad 群若存在,则其无非平凡可定义子群(强约束)。
