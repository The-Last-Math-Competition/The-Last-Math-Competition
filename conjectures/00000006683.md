# 00000006683

**English.** Definition: Weighted diagrams with the weighted dual tessellation structure. Conjecture: The correspondence of the two tessellations is one-to-one; the bridge of the correspondence is the weight translation; and the bridge of the weight translation preserves the marginal identity. (one-to-one bridge of weighted tessellations)

**中文。** 定义：加权图为加权的对偶剖分结构。猜想：两种剖分的对应为一一；且对应的桥为权平移；且权平移的桥保持边际恒等。（加权剖分一一对应桥）
