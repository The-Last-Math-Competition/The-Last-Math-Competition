**English.** Definition: The discrete product inequality is the counting version for finite sets. Conjecture: The lower bound of the counting deficit is optimal at the classical constant and attained at prime moduli, the combination of attainment being the logarithmic structure. (optimal discrete counting deficits)

**中文。** 定义：离散乘积不等式为有限集的计数版本。猜想：计数亏格的下界为经典常数最优且素模达到；且达到的组合为对数结构。（离散计数亏格最优）
