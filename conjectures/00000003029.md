**QM-19 ★** — source: 86_多体量子数学.md

**English.** Definition: Quasiparticle lifetimes of nonintegrable systems: interaction cascades. Conjecture: Quasiparticle decay rates: under nonrelativistic scaling the decay rate is an explicit power of the phase-space volume, with exponent corrected by the spatial dimension. (quasiparticle decay power law)

**中文。** 定义：非可积系统的准粒子寿命：相互作用级联。猜想：准粒子衰变率：非相对论标度下的衰变率为相空间体积的显式幂且幂指数为空间维数的修正。（准粒子衰变幂律）
