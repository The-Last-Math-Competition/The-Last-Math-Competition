# 00000003953

**English.** Definition: An immersion is an embedding preorder allowing edge substitutions, and the forbidden minimal elements are the minimal complements of immersion-closed classes. Conjecture: The cardinality of the immersion-forbidden minimal set of the n-spoke wheel is the Fibonacci number F_{n+1}, and all minimal elements are of the spoke-bisection type. (Fibonacci counting of immersion-minimal elements)

**中文。** 定义：浸入指允许边替代的嵌入前序,禁用极小指浸入封闭类的极小补元素。猜想：n 辐条轮图的浸入禁用极小集的基数为 Fibonacci 数 F_{n+1},且全部极小元为辐条二分剖分型。（浸入极小 Fibonacci 计数）
