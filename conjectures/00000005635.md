# 00000005635

**CP2-15 ★** — source: 173_组合几何III_凸位置.md

**English.** Definition: The intersection property is that a fixed number of the family intersect, and the piercing number is the count of controlling points. Conjecture: The two-dimensional piercing formula is an explicit rational expression, degenerating to the matching number for binary parameters; the piercing number is at most the intersection count plus one, the plus one from parity correction; and the extremum of the formula is attained at convex-position families, with value twice the matching number. (rational piercing formula of two-dimensional intersections)

**中文。** 定义：相交性质：族中固定份数相交；穿透数指控制所需的点数。猜想：二维的穿透数公式为显式有理式且二元参数时退化为匹配数；穿透数不超过交数加一且加一由奇偶校正；公式的极值在凸位置族处达到且达到值为匹配数的两倍。（二维相交有理穿透公式）
