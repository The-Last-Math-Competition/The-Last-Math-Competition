# 00000008073

**PRF-23 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The proof-theoretic strengthening of the 0-1 law of finite model theory: the limit measure lambda_lim(T) of the first-order theory of random finite structures. Conjecture: The convergence rate spectrum beyond lambda_lim(T) in {0,1} (the proven 0-1 law): the limit convergence rate of any first-order sentence is O(2^{-n^{1/d}}) (d a quantifier-depth-type exponent); the tightening of the convergence rate is given by the combinatorial constant of Samuels' problem of walker games (the critical density of horses-and-stables type sets, a tightening-game constant duality); and the minimal dimension where the 0-1 law fails for transitive closure logic (TC) (graphs versus general structures) is 2 (the rigidity of TC failure at graphs). (0-1 law Samuels walker tightening)

**中文。** 定义：有限模型论的 0-1 律的证明论强化：随机有限结构的一阶理论的极限测度 λ_lim(T)。猜想：λ_lim(T) ∈{0,1}（0-1 律已证）的收敛速率的谱：任意一阶句的极限收敛速率为 O(2^{-n^{1/d}})（d 为量词深度型指数）；且收敛速率的紧化由游走（walker）对策的 Samuels 问题的组合常数（马子集合的临界密度）给出（紧化-对策常数对偶）；带传递闭包逻辑（TC）的 0-1 律的失效的最小维数（图与一般结构）为 2（图例外的 TC 失效刚性）。
