**English.** Definition: Saturation coefficients with the saturation gap of geometric complexity theory. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the saturation gap and the nonsaturated domain is explicit; the gap spectrum is finite with the spectrum upper bound the weight count of the representation; and the realization of the gap is given by rectangular representation pairs. (gap-spectrum conversion of the nonsaturated domain)
