# 00000008530

**SDG-30 △** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: Beyond Galois: super Galois structures. Conjecture: There exist differential systems whose solutions generate non-Galois extensions (not of Picard-Vessiot type); and their invariants are categorical structures of non-algebraic groups. (super Galois non-algebraic structure)

**中文。** 定义：Galois 之外指超 Galois 结构。猜想：存在微分系统其解生成非 Galois 扩张（非 Picard–Vessiot 型）；且其不变量为非代数群的范畴结构。（超 Galois 非代数结构）
