**English.** Definition: The partial-sum method is the term-by-term summation framework of asymptotic counting. Conjecture: The sub-leading term of two-parameter families is the negative-first power law with optimal error, and the condition of applicability is differentiability of the quotient series. (negative-first sub-leading term of partial sums)
