# 00000001894

**ML-24 △** — source: 48_逻辑与模型论II.md

**English.** Conjecture: The prime model of the higher differentially closed field DCF_n (n ≥ 2) has an explicit basis (algebraically independent sets of general solutions of n-th order differential equations); the basis construction can be given by an inductive Cohen-type model.

**中文。** 猜想：高阶可微闭域 DCF_n(n≥2) 的素模型有显式基(由 n 阶常微方程的通解的代数无关组);基的构造可由 Cohen 型模型的归纳化给出。
