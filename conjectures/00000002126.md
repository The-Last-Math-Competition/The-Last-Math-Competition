**English.** Definition: The maximum process of the GFF is the renormalized supremum limit. Conjecture: The Poissonization intensity constant of the extremes is an explicit logarithmic functional; and the kernel of the functional is the inverse of the correlation function. (GFF extreme Poisson intensity)

**中文。** 定义：GFF 的最大值过程为上确界重整极限。猜想：极值的 Poisson 化强度常数为显式对数泛函；且泛函的核为相关函数逆。（GFF 极值 Poisson 强度）
