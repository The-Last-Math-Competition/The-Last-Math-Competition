# 00000004537

**SO2-27 ◆** — source: 136_随机组合优化.md

**English.** Definition: The overlap gap property is the phenomenon that overlaps of optimal solutions avoid some interval. Conjecture: On models where the property holds, the approximation-ratio upper bound of any polynomial algorithm is an explicit function of the overlap gap, and the bound is optimal for the random regular XOR satisfiability problem. (optimal OGAP approximation bound)

**中文。** 定义：重叠间隔性质指最优解重叠避开某区间的现象。猜想：该性质成立的模型上任何多项式算法的逼近比上界为重叠间隙的显式函数,界在随机正则异或可满足问题达到最优。（OGAP 逼近比最优界）
