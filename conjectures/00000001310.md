# 00000001310

**English.** Definition: The Bergman kernel of a torus-invariant domain in Cⁿ is its reproducing kernel. Conjecture: The asymptotics of the kernel are always given by an explicit Laplacian of the Monge–Ampère measure of the toric potential. (toric domain kernel asymptotic formula)

**中文。** 定义：Cⁿ 中环面不变域的 Bergman 核指其上的再生核。猜想：核的渐近恒由 toric 位势的 Monge–Ampère 测度的显式拉普拉斯给出。（环面域核渐近公式）
