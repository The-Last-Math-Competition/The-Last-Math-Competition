# 00000008094

**FUS-14 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The F-saturated centralizer chains of subgroups of a fusion system: the stabilization length sat(F) of the descending chain S containing C_F(S) containing C_F(C_F(S)) and so on. Conjecture: sat(F) <= log_p |S| (a logarithmic stabilization law); the stabilized quotient (the quotient of the chain ends) is F-centric (a centric criterion); and the fusion systems realizing the extremal chain length (attaining the logarithm) are iterated wreath extremes (wreath realization), the list of minimal primes realizing them being {2,3} (a two-prime realization law). (centralizer chain logarithmic wreath realization)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的子群的 F-饱和的中心化链：中心化子的下降链 S ⊵ C_F(S) ⊵ C_F(C_F(S))… 的稳定长 sat(F)。猜想：sat(F) ≤ log_p |S|（对数稳定律）；且稳定商（链的两端商）为 F-中心（中心判据）；链长的极值（对数达到）的融合系统的实现为 iterated wreath 极值（圈积实现），实现的极小素数 p 的列表为 {2,3}（双素实现律）。
