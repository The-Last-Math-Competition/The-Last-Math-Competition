# 00000004813

**English.** Definition: The coefficients of an automorphic L-function are the coefficients of its Dirichlet series expansion. Conjecture: The error of the mean value theorem of coefficients over quadratic fields is optimal of square-root plus epsilon type, the bound is equivalent to the zero-density hypothesis and tight. (optimal error bound of coefficient means)

**中文。** 定义：自守 L 的系数指其狄利克雷级数展开系数。猜想：二次域系数的均值定理误差为平方根加 ε 型最优,界与零点密度假设等价且紧致。（系数均值最优误差界）
