**English.** Definition: The dimensional classification of local Picard groups is their structural type by prime. Conjecture: For height two and primes greater than three, the Picard group is a trivial extension of the integer group, the torsion part is only of order two, and it is controlled by the graph structure of supersingular points. (two-torsion structure of the K_2 large-prime Picard group)

**中文。** 定义：局部 Picard 群的维数分类指其按素数的结构型。猜想：高度二且素数大于三的 Picard 群为整数群的平凡扩张,挠部分仅二阶且由超奇异点的图结构控制。（K2 大素 Picard 二挠结构）
