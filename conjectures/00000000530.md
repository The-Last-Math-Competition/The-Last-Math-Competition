# 00000000530

**CA-30 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: Lyubeznik numbers λ_{i,j}(R) are numerical invariants derived from local cohomology modules. Conjecture: The Lyubeznik numbers in characteristic zero and in characteristic p agree for all sufficiently large p; and the stabilization threshold is explicitly controlled by the discriminant of the singularity. (Lyubeznik characteristic stability)

**中文。** 定义：Lyubeznik 数 λ_{i,j}(R) 为局部上同调模导出的数值不变量族。猜想：特征零与特征 p 的 Lyubeznik 数在 p 充分大时恒等于同一数组；稳定阈值由奇点的判别式显式控制。（Lyubeznik 特征稳定）
