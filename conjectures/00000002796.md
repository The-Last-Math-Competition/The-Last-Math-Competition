**English.** Definition: Spreading models: asymptotic limit spaces of Banach spaces. Conjecture: The spectrum of spreading models: the set of all spreading models of a space is a closed lattice, with minimal element a symmetric-basis space; the bifurcation points of the spectrum are the block oscillation rates of the space. (spreading model closed lattice)

**中文。** 定义：Spreading 模型：Banach 空间的渐近极限空间。猜想：spreading 模型的谱：空间的一切 spreading 模型的集合为闭格且格的极小元为对称基空间；谱的分歧点为空间的块振荡率。（spreading 模型闭格）
