# 00000008566

**NSP-06 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: Unitary dilations of operators: the minimal realization of a contraction T inside a unitary operator (Sz.-Nagy dilation). Conjecture: Every contraction operator has a second-order unitary dilation (a double-order law); the minimal dimension of the dilation is 2 unless T is already unitary (a unitary exception law); the explicit Schaeffer construction has polynomial complexity (a polynomial construction law); and the continuous semigroup version of the dilation exists within the H-infinity functional calculus framework (a continuous dilation law). (Sz.-Nagy double dilation polynomial construction)

**中文。** 定义：算子的酉膨胀：T 压缩到酉算子的最小实现（Sz.-Nagy 膨胀）。猜想：一切压缩算子具有二阶酉膨胀（双阶律）；膨胀的最小维数为 2 除非 T 已为酉（酉例外律）；Schaeffer 显式构造的复杂度为多项式（多项式构造律）；连续半群版本的膨胀存在于 H∞ 泛函演算框架（连续膨胀律）。
