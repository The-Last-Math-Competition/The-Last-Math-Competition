# 00000006148

**English.** Definition: The trace map is the pairing structure of Grothendieck duality. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit pairing of the trace map, matching fiber dimensions for proper maps; the matching coefficient is unique, the deviation of uniqueness being the orientation sign; and for nonproper cases the trace does not exist, the proper-support replacement coefficient being the characteristic number. (unique coefficient conversion of trace pairings)
