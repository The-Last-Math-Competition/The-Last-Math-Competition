# 00000009849

**English.** Definition: Two-weight fractional integrals: Muckenhoupt-Wheeden type conditions. Conjecture: The sharp two-weight constant of fractional integration is a fractional power of the supremum weight constant; the power exponent is an explicit linear expression in alpha and p, and the commutator version carries a k-th power of log correction. (fractional sharp constant)

**中文。** 定义：分数次积分的双权：Muckenhoupt–Wheeden 型条件。猜想：分数次积分的 sharp 双权常数为上确界权常数的分数幂；幂指数为 α 与 p 的显式线性式且交换子版本带 log 的 k 次幂修正。（分数次 sharp 常数）
