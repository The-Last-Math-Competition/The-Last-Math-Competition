# 00000007273

**English.** Definition: Dual Schur functions with dual representations. Conjecture: The representation duality of transposed partitions holds, and the realization of the duality is the transposition of Young diagrams. (transposed-dual Schur realization)

**中文。** 定义：对偶 Schur 函数：对偶表示。猜想：对偶的翻转：转置分拆的表示对偶且偶的实现为 Young 图的转置。（对偶翻转 Young 图转置分拆表示对偶实现）
