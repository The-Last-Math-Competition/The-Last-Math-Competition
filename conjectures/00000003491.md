**English.** Definition: Holomorphic vector bundles: holomorphic section sheaves over complex manifolds. Conjecture: The rigidity theorem of bundles: the moduli of stable bundles over high-genus curves is quasiprojective, with the dimension of the moduli explicitly Riemann–Roch.
