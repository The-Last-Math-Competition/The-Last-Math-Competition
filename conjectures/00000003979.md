# 00000003979

**PC2-09 ★** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: An ILP infeasibility certificate is a bit-string proof of integer programming infeasibility. Conjecture: There exists an explicit family of polytopes for which any certificate requires n^{2+epsilon} bits, the ratio lower bound being given by the Hadamard gaps of the coefficient subdeterminants. (ILP certificate subdeterminant lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：ILP 不可行证书指整数规划不可行性的位串证明。猜想：存在显式多面体族使任何证书需 n^{2+ε} 位,比值下界由系数子行列式的 Hadamard 间隙给出。（ILP 证书子行列式下界）
