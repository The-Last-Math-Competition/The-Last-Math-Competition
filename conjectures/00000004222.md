# 00000004222

**English.** Definition: The Duval-Reiner identity is the correspondence between tree counts and the Laplacian characteristic polynomial. Conjecture: In its higher-dimensional extension the tree count equals the explicit derivative of the characteristic polynomial at 1, with the order of the derivative exactly the number of faces of that dimension. (Duval-Reiner higher derivative formula)

**中文。** 定义：Duval–Reiner 恒等式指树计数与拉普拉斯特征多项式的对应。猜想：其高维推广中树计数等于特征多项式在 1 处的显式导数值,导数阶数恰为该维面数。（Duval–Reiner 高维导数式）
