# 00000007912

**ATC-12 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The Deligne conjecture type for periods: the ratio R(pi) = p^±(pi)/a(pi) of the periods p^±(pi) of a cusp representation pi (Betti-de Rham comparison) and the algebraic part a(pi) of L(1/2,pi). Conjecture: R(pi) is an algebraic number times a root of unity (Tate-conjecture-type rigidity of Hodge structures); and the class field theory part of R(pi) is uniquely determined by interpolation of minimal central values over the twists pi tensor chi, whose p-adic valuations give the codimension of the orthogonal complement of the Bloch-Kato Selmer group (dimensional conservation of the Iwasawa main conjecture). (period ratio root-of-unity rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：period 的 Deligne 猜想型：尖点表示 π 的周期 p^±(π)（Betti–de Rham 比较）与其 L 值 L(1/2,π) 的代数部分 a(π) 的比值 R(π)=p^±(π)/a(π)。猜想：R(π) 为单位根的代数数倍（Hodge 结构的 Tate 猜想型刚性）；且 R(π) 的类域论部分由在扭表示 π⊗χ 的极小中心值的插值唯一确定，其 p-adic 赋值给出 Bloch–Kato Selmer 群的正交补维数（Iwasawa 主猜想的量纲守恒）。
