# 00000009855

**English.** Definition: The Airy process family: the Airy_2 spatial process and the multi-time joint distributions of Airy_1 and Airy_stat. Conjecture: All finite-dimensional joint distributions of Airy_1 and Airy_stat admit a unified Fredholm Pfaffian representation; the kernel of the representation is an explicit linear combination of the Airy kernel and the edge kernel, complete without omissions. (Airy family unified Pfaffian representation)

**中文。** 定义：Airy 过程族：Airy_2 空间过程与 Airy_1、Airy_stat 的多时间联合分布。猜想：Airy_1 与 Airy_stat 的一切有限维联合分布有统一的 Fredholm Pfaffian 表示；表示的核为 Airy 核与边缘核的显式线性组合且完备无遗漏。（Airy 族统一 Pfaffian 表示）
