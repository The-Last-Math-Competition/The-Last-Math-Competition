# 00000007157

**EE4-07 ★** — source: 224_特征值极值II.md

**English.** Definition: Wielandt's min-max principle with the modulus eigenvalues of complex matrices. Conjecture: The spectral boundary of nonnormal matrices is a semicircle, and the radius of the circle is the numerical radius. (numerical-radius Wielandt semicircle bound)

**中文。** 定义：Wielandt 极小原理：复矩阵的模特征值。猜想：Wielandt 的界：非正规矩阵的谱的边界半圆且圆的半径为数值半径。（Wielandt 数值半径谱边界半圆界）
