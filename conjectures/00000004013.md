# 00000004013

**English.** Definition: The flow of an RDE is the two-variable map of the solution in the initial value and in time. Conjecture: The flow is C^{floor(1/p)-1} differentiable, the threshold floor(1/p) - 1 is optimal, and exactly one order is lost at reciprocal-integer values of p. (optimal differentiability threshold of flows)

**中文。** 定义：RDE 的流指解对初值与时间的二元映射。猜想：流为 C^{⌊1/p⌋−1} 可微,阈值 ⌊1/p⌋ − 1 最优,恰在整数倒数 p 处损失一阶。（流可微性阈值最优）
