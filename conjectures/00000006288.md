# 00000006288

**English.** Definition: The rank-finiteness conjecture constrains zeroth K-groups of group rings. Conjecture: The vanishing domain of the rank functional is given by torsion deficit, the compensation of the deficit being saturation, and the compensation of the saturation deficit being the splitting of torsion subgroups. (torsion-deficit compensation of rank functionals)

**中文。** 定义：秩有限猜想约束群环零阶群。猜想：秩泛函的消没域由挠项亏损给出；且亏损的补偿为饱和化；且饱和化亏损的补偿为挠子群的分裂。（秩泛函挠亏损补偿）
