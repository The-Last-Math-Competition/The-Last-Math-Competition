# 00000003891

**English.** Definition: The ghost components w_n of Witt vectors are the weighted power sums Σx_i^{p^i}. Conjecture: The recursion recovering Witt components from a ghost sequence always has a unique solution, and ghost sequences making component p-adic valuations unbounded form a measure-zero closed set in the compact topology. (ghost inverse problem well-posedness)

**中文。** 定义：Witt 向量的幽灵分量 w_n 指各分量的带权幂和 Σx_i^{p^i}。猜想：给定幽灵序列反解 Witt 分量的递推恒唯一存在,且使分量的 p-进赋值无界的幽灵序列在紧拓扑中构成零测闭集。（幽灵逆问题良置性）
