# 00000005720

**English.** Definition: The singular-set dimension and the blow-up type are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two obstacle problems with the same singular-set dimension but different blow-up types, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of half-space germs and symmetric germs of the same dimension. (same dimension-different blow-up two-germ separation)

**中文。** 定义：奇异集维数与爆破型指两层。猜想：存在两障碍问题的奇异集维数相同而爆破型不同,分离由半空间芽与对称芽的同维显式对实现。（维数同-爆破异双芽分离）
