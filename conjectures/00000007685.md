# 00000007685

**QDE-25 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: For the theta quotient T(z)=theta_3(zq)/theta_3(z), the Jackson integral J_q = int_0^1 T(z) d_q z uses the Jackson measure. Conjecture: J_q is transcendental when q in (0,1) is algebraic, and the leading coefficient of the Puiseux expansion of J_q in q-1 is Gamma(1/4)^2/(2 pi^{3/2}). (transcendence of Jackson theta integrals)

**中文。** 定义：theta 商 T(z)=θ_3(z q)/θ_3(z)（θ_3(w)=Σ q^{n²} w^n）的 Jackson 积分 J_q=∫_0^1 T(z) d_q z，其中 d_q z 为 (1-q)Σ z q^n 型 Jackson 测度。猜想：J_q 当 q∈(0,1) 为代数数时为超越数，且 J_q 关于 q-1 的 Puiseux 展开的主项系数为 Γ(1/4)²/(2π^{3/2})。
