**English.** Definition: Jackson integral J_q=∫_0^1 T(z) d_q z, where d_q z of theta quotient T(z)=θ_3(z q)/θ_3(z) (θ_3(w)=Σ q^{n²} w^n) is (1-q)Σ z q^n type Jackson measure.
