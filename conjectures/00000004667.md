**English.** Definition: The geometric genus is the dimension of the higher cohomology of the structure sheaf. Conjecture: The geometric genus upper bound of four-dimensional absolutely isolated singularities is an explicit linear function of the Milnor number with slope one eighth, attained by the Arnold series. (slope upper bound of the four-dimensional genus)

**中文。** 定义：几何亏格指结构层的高阶上同调维数。猜想：四维绝对孤立奇点的几何亏格上界为 Milnor 数的显式线性函数,斜率八分之一且由 Arnold 系列达到。（四维亏格斜率上界）
