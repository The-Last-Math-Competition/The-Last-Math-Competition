# 00000006593

**English.** Definition: Full exceptional collections with the ordering spectrum of generating sequences. Conjecture: The ordering spectrum is given by braid orbits and the combination of the orbit is the helical word; the realization of the helical word is the helix sequence; and the period of the orbit is the rotation number of the word. (helical-word orbits of ordering braids)

**中文。** 定义：完全例外集：生成整个范畴的例外列结构（排序谱）。猜想：排序谱由辫群轨道给出且轨道的组合为螺旋字；螺旋字的实现为螺旋序列；轨道的周期为字的旋转数。（排序辫群螺旋字轨道）
