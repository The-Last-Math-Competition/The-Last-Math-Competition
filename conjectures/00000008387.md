# 00000008387

**English.** Definition: The isogeny tower of elliptic curves: the tower of extensions of curves within a fixed isogeny class; the motivic cascade is the chain of images of the corresponding motives under isogeny. Conjecture: The motivic cascade length of the isogeny class of a non-CM elliptic curve is always 1; the cascade length of a CM curve is an explicit value within a logarithmic upper bound of the class number; and the motives of the cascade are simply transitive under the action of the class group of the CM field. (isogeny motivic cascade length)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆曲线同源塔指固定同源类中曲线的扩张塔，母题级联指对应母题在同源下的像链。猜想：无 CM 椭圆曲线的同源类的母题级联长度恒为 1；CM 曲线的级联长度为类数的对数上界内的显式值，且级联的母题在 CM 域的类群作用下单传递。（同源母题级联长度）
