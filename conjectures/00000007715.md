**English.** Definition: The isohedral count I(k) is the number of transitivity types of monohedral tilings with exactly k translation classes of tiles. Conjecture: I(k) is finite for each fixed k, and the generating function sum I(k) x^k is rational with denominator the product of (1-x^2)^{81} and nine low-degree polynomials. (rational generating function of isohedral types)

**中文。** 定义：单块 k 次铺砌传递类（isohedral）指平面上恰有 k 个平移等价类 tile 的单面铺砌的传递型数 I(k)。猜想：I(k) 对每个固定 k 有限，且 I(k) 的生成函数 Σ I(k) x^k 为有理函数，其分母由 (1-x^2)^{81} 与 9 个低次多项式的乘积给出。
