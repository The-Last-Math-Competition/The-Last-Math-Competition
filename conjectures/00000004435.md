# 00000004435

**English.** Definition: The q-Whittaker process is the process of the q-Whittaker-compatible family. Conjecture: Its normalization of the conversion with the quantum open Toda chain is an explicit Gamma integral, the integral is verified at the degeneration t = q, and the constant is exact. (Whittaker-Toda Gamma normalization)

**中文。** 定义：q-Whittaker 过程指 q-Whittaker 相容族的过程。猜想：其与量子开源 Toda 链的换算归一为显式 Gamma 积分,积分在 t 等于 q 的退化处验证且常数精确。（Whittaker-Toda Gamma 归一）
