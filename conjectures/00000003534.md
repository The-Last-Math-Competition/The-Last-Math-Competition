**English.** Definition: Suslin trees and Suslin lines: tree combinatorics. Conjecture: The independence of the Suslin problem: the existence of Suslin trees is independent of ZFC, with the combinatorial side of independence the antichain-chain dichotomy of trees. (Suslin antichain chain dichotomy independence)
