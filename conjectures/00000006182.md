# 00000006182

**English.** Definition: Strictly balanced subgraphs are graphs where every proper subgraph has lower density, with the extremal structure of expectations. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit ratio of balanced densities, and the expectation is monotone for strictly balanced subgraphs; the extremal point is unique and exactly the threshold itself; and two strictly balanced subgraphs share the threshold if and only if their densities are equal, in which case the expectation ratio is constant. (monotone extremal conversion of strictly balanced expectations)

**中文。** 定义：严格平衡子图：每个真子图密度低于本身的图；期望的极值结构。猜想：换算常数为平衡密度的显式比且严格平衡时期望单调；极值点唯一且恰为阈值本身；两个严格平衡图共享阈值当且仅当密度相等且此时期望比恒为常数。（严格平衡期望单调极值换算）
