**OA-98 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The completely bounded norm (cb). Conjecture: The supremum of the ratio of the cb norm to the operator norm over the observable algebra of three qubits equals √2 (the cb ratio).

**中文。** 定义：完全有界范数(cb)。猜想：三量子比特的可观测量代数的 cb 范数与算子范数比值的上确界 = √2(cb 比)。
