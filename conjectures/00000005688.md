# 00000005688

**TW2-08 ★** — source: 175_范畴的表示_2表示论.md

**English.** Definition: A multifusion category is a semisimple category with non-simple unit. Conjecture: The representation dimension table is the explicit sum of unit decompositions, closed under simple-module decomposition; the length of the unit is 2, and the sum of squared dimensions of the two simple units equals the global dimension of the category; and the orthogonality of the decomposition is given by the local units of the unit components, which are unique. (unit-decomposition dimensions of multifusion categories)

**中文。** 定义：多重融合：单位对象非简单的半单范畴。猜想：表示维数表为单位分解的显式和且单模分解下封闭；单位的长度为 2 且两个单纯单位的维数平方和等于范畴的整体维数；分解的正交性由单位分量的局部单位给出且局部单位唯一。（多重融合单位分解维数）
