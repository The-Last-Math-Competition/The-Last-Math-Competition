# 00000000195

**CS-29 ★** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: Θ_p = Σ 2^{−p_n} (the real number with 1's exactly at prime-indexed binary positions). Conjecture: Θ_p belongs to Mahler's class S, and its S-measure is an explicit function of the limsup of the generating exponent of the prime counting function; Θ_p is not a Liouville-type solution of any fixed-degree integer-coefficient equation. (prime binary Mahler class)

**中文。** 定义：Θ_p=Σ 2^{−p_n}（素数下标的二进制数）。猜想：Θ_p 属 Mahler S 类且其 S-度量为素数计数函数生成指数的 limsup 的显式函数；Θ_p 不是任何次数固定的整系数方程的 Liouville 型解。（素数二进制 Mahler 类）
