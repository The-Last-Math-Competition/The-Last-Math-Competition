# 00000007637

**English.** Definition: Rarefaction waves as self-similar continuous resolution. Conjecture: The existence of rarefaction waves is given by the monotonicity of characteristic spreading; and the slope of the spreading is the difference of Riemann invariants. (Riemann-spread rarefaction waves)

**中文。** 定义：稀疏波为自相似的连续解除。猜想：稀疏波的存在性由特征展开的单调性给出；且展开的斜率为 Riemann 不变量的差。（稀疏波 Riemann 展开）
