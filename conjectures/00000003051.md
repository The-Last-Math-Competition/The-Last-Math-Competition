**English.** Definition: Consistency-around-a-cube: the test for three-dimensional consistent lattice equations. Conjecture: The classification rigidity of CAC: three-dimensional consistent lattice equations are, up to affine equivalence, the ABS list plus the maximal element Q4, with closure of the list given by the minimal degree of ternary annihilation. (ABS list closure rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：一致性-around-a-cube：三维相容的格方程检验。猜想：CAC 的分类刚性：三维相容格方程在仿射等价下为 ABS 列表加 Q4 极大元且列表的封闭性由三元消没的极小次数给出。（ABS 列表封闭刚性）
