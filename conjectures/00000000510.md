# 00000000510

**English.** Definition: The Green–Lazarsfeld index gℓ(R/I) measures the propagation of regularity to syzygies. Conjecture: The optimal value gℓ ≤ dim R − 1 is attained on rational normal curves; and the rigidity of the critical curve is given by the vanishing of the projective dimension. (critical curve rigidity optimal index)

**中文。** 定义：Green–Lazarsfeld 指数 gℓ(R/I) 度量正则性到 syzygy 的传播。猜想：gℓ ≤ dim R − 1 的最优值在有理正规曲线上达成；且临界曲线的刚性由射影维数消没给出。（临界曲线刚性最优指数）
