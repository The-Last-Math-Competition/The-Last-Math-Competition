# 00000004529

**English.** Definition: A local algorithm is a randomized algorithm depending only on constant-radius neighborhoods. Conjecture: On random MAX-k-SAT the approximation-ratio upper bound of local algorithms is an explicit constant plus a logarithmic correction in the neighborhood radius, and the correction cannot be removed. (logarithmic correction bound of local approximation ratios)

**中文。** 定义：局部算法指仅依赖常数半径邻域的随机算法。猜想：随机 MAX-k-SAT 上局部算法的逼近比上界为显式常数加邻域半径的对数修正,修正项不可去除。（局部逼近比对数修正界）
