# 00000009556

**English.** Definition: A zero exponent appears in the KZ spectrum if and only if the corresponding monotone cocycle component degenerates; known examples come from square-tiled cyclic covers and the Forni-Matheus-Zorich series. Conjecture: The multiplicity of zero exponents is explicitly determined by a dimension-combination formula for Hodge substructures of relative cohomology; and the minimal-rank examples with zero exponents (spectrum containing exactly one zero) are finite up to isomorphism, all on a genus at most 6 list. (zero exponent complete classification)

**中文。** 定义：KZ 谱中出现零指数当且仅当对应单调 cocycle 分量退化；已知例子由方砖循环覆盖与 Forni–Matheus–Zorich 系列给出。猜想：零指数的重数由相对上同调的 Hodge 子结构的维数组合公式显式确定；带零指数的极小秩例子（谱恰含一个零）在同构类意义下仅有限个，全部为 genus ≤6 的列表。（零指数完全分类）
