# 00000002178

**EC-08 ★** — source: 58_极值集合论与组合.md

**English.** Definition: The p-biased complete intersection theorem is the extremal theory of intersecting families under p-biased measures. Conjecture: The extremum switches from r-stars at an explicit threshold in p (of the 2^{-r}/p type); the switch follows from Ahlswede–Khachatrian dynamic programming.

**中文。** 定义：p-偏向完全交定理指 p-偏向测度下的交族极值。猜想：极值由 r-星形在 p 的显式阈值处切替(阈值 = 2^{-r}/p 型);切替由 Ahlswede–Khachatrian 的动态规划。
