**English.** Definition: Voronoi tessellations: nearest-neighbor geometric partitions. Conjecture: The vertex count of the typical Voronoi cell: the mean facet count of the typical cell is 2 plus the Euler correction, with the correction universal in dimension. (Voronoi typical cell Euler correction facet count)
