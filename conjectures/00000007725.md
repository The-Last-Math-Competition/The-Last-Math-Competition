# 00000007725

**English.** Definition: The free multiplicative Brown motion G_t satisfies a free SDE dG_t = G_t dW_t + dW_t G_t - (t/2) G_t dt, whose Brown measure support and density are open problems. Conjecture: The Brown measure support of G_t is a star-shaped domain centered at the origin with analytic Jordan boundary, whose outer radius rho(t) satisfies rho'(t) = rho + rho^{-1}; the density is analytic in the interior with square-root singularity at the boundary. (star-shaped Brown measure of free multiplicative Brown motion)

**中文。** 定义：自由乘法 Brown 运动 G_t 满足 dG_t = G_t dW_t + dW_t G_t - (t/2)G_t dt 形式的自由 SDE，其 Brown 测度（非交换谱分布）的支撑与密度是公开问题。猜想：G_t 的 Brown 测度支撑为以原点为中心的星形域，其边界为解析 Jordan 曲线且外径恰为 exp(1+2W(t)) 的自由类比公式 ρ(t) 满足 ρ'(t)=ρ+ρ^{-1}；密度在星形内部解析且在边界处有平方根奇性。
