# 00000003935

**English.** Definition: The kappa-invariant is a bi-invariant of the Legendrian contact isotopy class taking values in the positive real numbers. Conjecture: The rational values of kappa are dense in the positive real axis, and there exist Legendrians with irrational kappa, constructible explicitly by spiral winding. (kappa density and irrational realization)

**中文。** 定义：κ-不变量指 Legendrian 切触同痕类的双不变量,取值正实数。猜想：κ 的有理值在正实轴稠密,存在 κ 为无理数的 Legendrian,其可由螺旋缠绕显式构造。（κ 稠密与无理值实现）
