# 00000008356

**English.** Definition: The tropical Abel-Jacobi graph Jacobian: the determinant det_J of the lattice of the Jacobian of a metric graph Gamma (graph complexity). Conjecture: det_J (the determinant of the graph Jacobian lattice) equals the spanning tree count tau(Gamma) (the matrix tree formula); the modulus of periodicity of the mod-p count of tau (p-local) has the form p^d - 1 (a period modulus law); the complete classification of Jacobian structures (group isomorphism types) is incomplete outside 3-regular graphs (a three-regular exception law); and the criterion for fullness in the real-quadratic-field analogue of class groups (real graphs with full Jacobians) is non-bipartiteness (a non-bipartite full criterion law). (graph Jacobian matrix tree period real fullness)

**中文。** 定义：热带 Abel–Jacobi 的图雅可比：度量图 Γ 的雅可比的格点的行列式 det_J（图复杂度）。猜想：det_J（图雅可比的格的行列式）= 生成树的计数 τ(Γ)（矩阵树公式）；且 τ 的模 p 计数（p-局部）的周期性的模数（周期模）为 p^{d}-1 的形（周期模律）；雅可比的结构（群的同构型）的完全分类在 3-正则图外不完全（三正则例外律）；类群的实二次图的类比（实图与满雅可比）的满性的判据为非二分（非二分满判据律）。
