# 00000009526

**QIC-06 ★** — source: 303_量子信道容量.md

**English.** Definition: A degradable channel admits a trace-preserving contraction theta with phi^c = theta composed with phi, and its quantum capacity single-letterizes at sup I_c; asymptotically degradable channels are strong operator topology limits of degradable ones. Conjecture: Asymptotically degradable channels preserve single-letterization; and in the genuinely non-degradable region there are explicit analytic curve families phi_t for which the gap between Q(phi_t) and sup I_c is Theta(t^{1/2}) as t tends to 0, the slowest possible rate of single-letterization failure. (degradable single-letter criterion)

**中文。** 定义：可降解信道指存在保迹收缩 θ 使 φ^c=θ∘φ，其量子容量单字母化于 sup I_c；渐近可降解指 φ 为可降解信道的强算子拓扑极限。猜想：渐近可降解信道保持单字母化，且真正不可降解区域中存在显式解析曲线族 φ_t 使 Q(φ_t) 与 sup I_c 之差在 t→0 时为 Θ(t^{1/2})，这是单字母化失效的最慢可能速率。（可降解单字母判据）
