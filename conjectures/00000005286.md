# 00000005286

**English.** Definition: The symmetric combination variables are the diagonalizing variables of magnetohydrodynamics. Conjecture: Their regularity threshold is the critical value one half, with global existence above and explicit blow-up below, given by the symmetric cancellation structure, and sharp on both sides. (half-regularity critical threshold of symmetric variables)
