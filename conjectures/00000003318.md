**English.** Definition: Borel monomial ideals: the combinatoratorial structure of Galligo-fixed point ideals. Conjecture: The minimal resolution of Borel: the minimal free resolution of a Borel ideal is of Eliahou–Kervaire type, with the term count the index set of maximal generators. (Eliahou-Kervaire index term count resolution)
