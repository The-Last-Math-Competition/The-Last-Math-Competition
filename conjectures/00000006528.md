**English.** Definition: The determinant metric is the canonical metric of the determinant line bundle. Conjecture: The analytic-geometric metric difference is always the analytic torsion, and the quadratic form of the torsion is the Bismut-Freed curvature. (analytic-torsion determinant metric)

**中文。** 定义：行列式度量为行列式线丛的典范度量。猜想：解析几何度量差恒为解析挠率；且挠率的二次型为 Bismut–Freed 曲率。（行列式度量解析挠率）
