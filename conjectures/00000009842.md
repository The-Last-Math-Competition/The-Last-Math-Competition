**English.** Definition: John-Nirenberg Bellman: optimal constants of BMO tail distributions (Slavin-Vasyunin gave explicit forms). Conjecture: The transcendence degrees of the algebraic branches of the three-parameter John-Nirenberg Bellman function are bounded; the phase-transition curves where branches glue are algebraic curves with finitely many real branches. (John-Nirenberg Bellman transcendence degree)

**中文。** 定义：John–Nirenberg Bellman：BMO 尾分布的最优常数（Slavin–Vasyunin 已给显式）。猜想：三参数 John–Nirenberg Bellman 函数的各代数分支的超越度为有界；分支拼接的相变曲线为代数曲线且实分支数为有限列表。（JN Bellman 超越度）
