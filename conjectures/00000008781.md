# 00000008781

**NLA-11 ★** — source: 278_随机化数值线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: Randomized tensor compression: randomized algorithms for TT and Tucker decompositions. Conjecture: The error of randomized Tucker is 1 + epsilon (an epsilon law); randomized SVD for TT is stable (a stability law); streaming tensors have a space lower bound (a space law); and the lower bound is attained by single-pass methods (a single-pass law). (randomized Tucker TT streaming single-pass)

**中文。** 定义：张量的随机压缩：TT 与 Tucker 的随机算法。猜想：随机 Tucker 的误差的 (1+ε) 的（ε 律）；且 TT 的随机 SVD 的稳定（稳定律）；流式张量的空间的下界（空间律）；下界的达到（single-pass）的（单程律）。
