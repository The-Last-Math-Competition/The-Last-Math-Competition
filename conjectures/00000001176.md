# 00000001176

**English.** Definition: Geometric crystals are logarithmic semifield realizations of crystals. Conjecture: The tropicalization of a geometric crystal gives an explicit arithmetic structure of the combinatorial crystal; and the bridge is the values of the boundary-reaching operators. (tropical crystal correspondence)

**中文。** 定义：几何晶体为晶体的对数半环实现。猜想：几何晶体的热带化给出组合晶体的显式算术结构；且对应的桥为尽界算子的取值。（tropical 晶体对应）
