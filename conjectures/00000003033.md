# 00000003033

**English.** Definition: Limits of many-body quantum metrology: entanglement-enhanced parameter estimation. Conjecture: Breaking of additivity of quantum Fisher information: the metrological gain of entangled probes scales as √n in the Heisenberg regime, with saturation of the scaling locked by an explicit noise-gating threshold. (metrology Heisenberg noise threshold)

**中文。** 定义：多体量子计量的极限：参数估计的纠缠增强。猜想：量子 Fisher 信息的可加性破缺：纠缠探针的计量增益为 √n 的 Heisenberg 标度且标度的饱和由噪声门控的显式阈值锁定。（计量 Heisenberg 噪声阈值）
