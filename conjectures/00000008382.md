**MIB-02 ★** — source: 265_母题积分与双有理不变量.md

**English.** Definition: The Bernstein basis: the subalgebra of the motivic algebra K_0(Var) spanned by classes of smooth projective varieties; effectivity of motivic integration means the motivic measure is completely evaluable on this algebra. Conjecture: The evaluation functional on the Bernstein basis is unique and multiplicative; the realization of the evaluation is the p to infinity limit of p-adic volumes of the arc space; and the existence of that limit is equivalent to the purity of the weight spectrum of the Hodge realization. (Bernstein evaluation uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：Bernstein 基指母题代数 K₀(Var) 中由光滑射影簇的类张成的子代数，母题积分的有效性指母题测度可在此代数上完全求值。猜想：Bernstein 基上的求值泛函唯一且保持乘法；求值的实化为弧空间的 p-进体积在 p→∞ 的极限；该极限的存在性与 Hodge 实现的权谱的纯性等价。（Bernstein 求值唯一性）
