# 00000002375

**OF-25 ★** — source: 64_算子理论II_函数演算与自由分析.md

**English.** Definition: The essential commutator measures subnormality. Conjecture: The subnormal weight spectrum of strict convex combinations is a closed lattice; and the extrema of the lattice are sum-of-squares decompositions; and the weights of the decomposition are trace-norm allocations. (subnormal weight convex-combination lattice)

**中文。** 定义：本质换位子为半正规性度量。猜想：严格凸组合的亚正规权重谱为闭格；且格的极值为平方和分解；且平方和分解的权重为迹范数分配。（亚正规权重凸组合格）
