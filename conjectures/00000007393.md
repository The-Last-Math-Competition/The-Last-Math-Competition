# 00000007393

**English.** Definition: The super Killing form with the super-invariant bilinear form. Conjecture: The degeneracy criterion is the vanishing of odd roots, and the exception of the vanishing is the strange phantom. (strange-phantom super-Killing degeneracy)

**中文。** 定义：超 Killing 型：超不变双线性型。猜想：超 Killing 的退化：退化的判据为奇怪根的消没且没的例外为奇怪的幻。（超 Killing 奇怪幻退化判据例外奇根消没）
