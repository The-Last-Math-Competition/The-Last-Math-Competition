# 00000002094

**English.** Definition: Unitality of symplectic homology means the SH multiplication has a unit. Conjecture: Unitality is equivalent to subcriticality of the filling, and the chain of equivalences is given by cone operations; and the kernel of the chain is handle cancellation. (SH-subcritical handle-cancellation cone-chain criterion)

**中文。** 定义：辛同调的含幺性指 SH 乘法幺元存在。猜想：含幺性等价于填充的亚临界性且等价链为锥操作；且链的核为 HANDLE 消去。（SH-亚临界 HANDLE 消去锥链充要）
