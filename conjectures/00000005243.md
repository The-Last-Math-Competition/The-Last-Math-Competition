**MS3-13 ★** — source: 160_代数几何V_模空间.md

**English.** Definition: The Picard rank is the rank of the Picard group. Conjecture: The Picard rank of the rank-four moduli space is an explicit linear expression of the genus, verified by the independence of quotient bundles; the slope of the linear expression is 1; and the exceptional value at genus 2 is the constant 1, explained by hyperelliptic specialization. (genus-linear formula of the rank-four Picard rank)

**中文。** 定义：皮卡秩：皮卡群的秩。猜想：秩四模空间的皮卡秩为亏格的显式线性式且由商丛无关性验证；线性式的斜率为 1；亏格 2 的例外值为常数 1 且例外由超椭圆特殊化解释。（秩四皮卡亏格线性公式）
