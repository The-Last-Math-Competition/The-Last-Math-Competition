**English.** Definition: The Bismut–Lott heat supertrace: the analytic torsion of flat bundles. Conjecture: The flatness of supertraces: the Bismut–Lott form is closed under the flat affine structure of the base, and the characteristic class of the closed form is the differential realization of Reidemeister torsion. (Bismut-Lott flat closedness)
