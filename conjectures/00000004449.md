**English.** Definition: The antiferroelectric boundary of the six-vertex model is the phase boundary of the ordered domain. Conjecture: Its correlation length obeys different laws on the two sides of the interface, one the reciprocal of the logarithm of the difference and the other a power law, with both exponents exact. (two laws of the antiferroelectric correlation length)

**中文。** 定义：六顶点的反铁电边界指有序域的相边界。猜想：其相关长度在界面两侧服从不同律,一侧为差值的对数倒数,另一侧为幂律,两律指数精确。（反铁电相关长度双律）
