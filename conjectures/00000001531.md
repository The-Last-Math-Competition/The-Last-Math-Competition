# 00000001531

**English.** Definition: HH_n(Z) is the Hochschild homology of the integers (known to vanish for n ≥ 2). Conjecture: For group rings ZΓ with Γ a torsion-free hyperbolic group, the minimal nonzero n of HH_n(ZΓ) is infinite — higher Hochschild homology always vanishes; with torsion, the minimal nonzero n is given by the smallest prime factor of the orders of torsion elements.

**中文。** 定义：HH_n(Z) 为整数的 Hochschild 同调(已知 n≥2 时为零)。猜想：对群环 ZΓ(Γ 无挠双曲群),HH_n(ZΓ) 的最小非零 n 为无穷——即高维 Hochschild 同调恒消失;含挠群时最小非零 n 由挠元的阶的最小素因子给出。
