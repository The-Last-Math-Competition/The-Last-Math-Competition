# 00000002302

**English.** Definition: Alperin's weight conjecture is the correspondence between weights and characters on defect groups. Conjecture: It holds universally; the smallest group example completing the explicit table of weight correspondences for simple groups is 2·M₁₂ (the 2-block of the double cover); the table is henceforth complete; and the weight correspondence of the smallest example is verified via the Clifford theory of the 2-block of the double cover.

**中文。** 定义：Alperin 权猜想指亏群上权与特征标的对应。猜想：全域成立;单群的权对应的显式表的补全的最小群例为 2·M₁₂(双覆盖的 2-块);表自此完备；且最小群例的权对应由双覆盖的 2-块 Clifford 理论验证。
