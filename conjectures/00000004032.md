# 00000004032

**English.** Definition: A tubular algebra is one of the four weight types of finite-dimensional algebras whose stable AR-quiver consists of families of tubes. Conjecture: The stratification of the tubes by slope corresponds one-to-one with the orbits of the action of the modular group PGL(2, Z), parametrized by explicit fractional linear transformations. (tubular tube stratification and modular orbits)

**中文。** 定义：tubular 代数指四个权型 tubular 有限维代数,其稳定 AR-箭图由管族构成。猜想：管族按斜率的分层与模群 PGL(2,Z) 的作用轨道一一对应,参数化为显式分数线性变换。（tubular 管分层-模群轨道）
