**English.** Definition: Tree-izations of ordinal notations: notations and trees. Conjecture: The well-foundedness of notation trees: the verification of well-ordering of notations is descending chains of trees, with the length of chains linear in notation height. (notation tree descending chain linear height well-foundedness verification)

**中文。** 定义：ordinal notations 的树化：记号与树。猜想：记号树的良基：记号的良序的验证为树的下降链且链的长度为记号高度的线性。（记号树下降链线性高度良基验证）
