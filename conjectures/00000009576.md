# 00000009576

**English.** Definition: The curve graph C(S) has isotopy classes as vertices and disjointness as edges; its Gromov hyperbolicity is proven, and the precise value of the ball growth rate (graph entropy) is unknown. Conjecture: The exponential growth rate h(C(S_{g,n})) is an explicit rational function of 2g - 2 + n, equal to the volume entropy of the Teichmueller metric; the identity of the two entropies is transferred by equality on covering families of square-tiled completely periodic surfaces. (curve graph growth entropy)

**中文。** 定义：曲线图 C(S) 为以同痕类为顶点、不相交为边的图；其 Gromov 双曲性已证，球增长速率（图熵）未知精确值。猜想：曲线图的指数增长率 h(C(S_{g,n})) 为 2g−2+n 的显式有理函数，且与 Teichmüller 度量的体积熵相等；两熵的恒等由方砖型完全周期曲面的覆盖族实现等式传递。（曲线图增长熵）
