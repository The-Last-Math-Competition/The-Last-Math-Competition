**English.** Definition: Engulfing: Stallings' topological extension technique. Conjecture: The dimension threshold of engulfing: m-dimensional engulfing requires dimension at least five, with the tightness of the threshold the double-cap configuration of four-dimensional counterexamples. (Stallings engulfing dimension threshold)
