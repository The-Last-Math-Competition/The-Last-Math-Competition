# 00000002837

**English.** Definition: The algebraic geometry of mixture models: the parameter-space structure of finite mixture distributions. Conjecture: The dimension formula of mixture varieties: the dimension of the observed variety of a k-mixture is the explicit hyperbolic correction of min(k·d, D−1), with correction appearing on the non-identifiable parameter layer. (mixture variety dimension correction)

**中文。** 定义：混合模型的代数几何：有限混合分布的参数空间结构。猜想：混合簇的维数公式：k 混合的观测簇的维数为 min(k·d, D−1) 的显式双曲修正且修正项在不可分参数层出现。（混合簇维数修正）
