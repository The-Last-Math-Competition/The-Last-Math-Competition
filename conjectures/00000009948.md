# 00000009948

**English.** Definition: Mostow rigidity: commensurability classes of finite-volume negatively curved quotients are determined by the fundamental group; the quantitative form of local rigidity. Conjecture: The quantitative Mostow rigidity: the speed at which deformation paths leave the commensurability class is an explicit function of the initial deformation velocity; the speed function is strictly increasing and convex, controlled by the first eigenvalue of the curvature tensor. (Mostow quantitative movement law)

**中文。** 定义：Mostow 刚性：体积有限的负曲率商的同痕类由基本群决定；局部刚性的定量形式。猜想：Mostow 刚性的定量版：形变路径离开同痕类的移动速率为形变初始速度的显式函数；速率函数为严格增的凸函数且由曲率张量的第一特征值控制。（Mostow 定量移动律）
