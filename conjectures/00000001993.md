# 00000001993

**English.** Definition: Soft potentials of the Boltzmann equation are cross-sections decaying like v^{−γ} at infinity in relative velocity (γ ∈ (0,2)). Conjecture: The convergence rate near equilibrium for soft potentials is polynomial: t^{−η} with the explicit exponent η of the 2/(2−γ) type; the exponent is uniquely determined by the softness of the potential.

**中文。** 定义：Boltzmann 方程的软位势指截面在相对速度无穷远处衰减如 v^{-γ}(γ∈(0,2))。猜想：软位势的平衡态附近的收敛率为多项式:t^{-η}(η=2/(2-γ)型显式指数);指数由位势的软化度唯一确定。
