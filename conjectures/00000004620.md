**English.** Definition: Tangent cone sequences and density are the two quantities of singularity analysis. Conjecture: There exists a minimal surface with two tangent cone sequences converging to different cones while the limit densities coincide, and a non-unique sequence of density two is realized by a splitting-type construction. (same density, non-unique tangent cone sequences)

**中文。** 定义：切锥序列与密度指奇点分析的两个量。猜想：存在极小曲面的两切锥序列收敛到不同锥而极限密度相同,密度二的非唯一序列由分裂型构造实现。（切锥序列-密度同分非唯一）
