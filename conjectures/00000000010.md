# 00000000010

**English.** Definition: The second-order structure of the prime-value count of a square-free integer polynomial f: the Bateman–Horn constant C_f and the leading deviation term. Conjecture: The count of prime values satisfies Π_f(x) = C_f·Li(x^{1/2}) + O(x^{1/4+ε}), where C_f is the product of local densities with convergence exponent the logarithm of the discriminant; the deviation exponent 1/4 is optimal — there is an explicit family of f for which the mean-square deviation is at least x^{1/2}/log²x; and C_f admits the explicit bounds 1/(2 log D_f) ≤ C_f ≤ 2 log D_f.

**中文。** 定义：无重根整系数多项式 f 的素值计数的二阶结构：Bateman–Horn 常数 C_f 与偏差主项。猜想：f 取素值的计数满足 Π_f(x) = C_f·Li(x^{1/2})+O(x^{1/4+ε})，其中 C_f 为局部密度乘积且收敛指数为判别式的对数；偏差项指数 1/4 为最优——存在显式 f 族使均方偏差不低于 x^{1/2}/log²x；C_f 的显式上下界为 1/(2 log D_f) ≤ C_f ≤ 2 log D_f。
