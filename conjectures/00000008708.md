**English.** Definition: The matrix version of transport inequalities: transport of matrix-valued measures. Conjecture: The noncommutative cost is given by the Frobenius norm; and the kernel of the norm is the trace of matrix couplings. (matrix Frobenius trace coupling noncommutative cost)
