# 00000005387

**English.** Definition: Difference-sequence convergence is the approach speed of the limit. Conjecture: The rate is the explicit reciprocal of the degree, the leading error is the preperiod depth of the critical point, and the convergence is monotone without oscillation. (reciprocal rate law of difference-sequence convergence)

**中文。** 定义：差序列收敛指极限的逼近速度。猜想：速率为度量的倒数显式,误差首项为临界点的预周期深度且收敛单调无振荡。（差序列收敛倒数速率律）
