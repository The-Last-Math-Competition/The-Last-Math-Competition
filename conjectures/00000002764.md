# 00000002764

**PI-24 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: The Kemer index vector: the complete asymptotic invariant of T-ideals. Conjecture: Realizability of index vectors: a pair (α,β) is the Kemer index of some T-ideal if and only if the components satisfy the monotonicity constraints of superalgebra dimensions; the realizable domain is the integer points of a graded lattice. (Kemer index realizability lattice)

**中文。** 定义：Kemer 指标向量：T-理想的完整渐近不变量。猜想：指标向量的可实现性：一对 (α,β) 可为某 T-理想的 Kemer 指标当且仅当分量满足超代数维数的单调约束；可实现域为分格的整点。（Kemer 指标可实现格）
