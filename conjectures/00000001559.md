# 00000001559

**GM-19 ★** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: The n-disk covering radius r_n of the unit disk is the minimal radius of n equal disks covering it. Conjecture: r₆ is an explicit quadratic algebraic number of the (1+√3)/2·(√3−1) type, realized by one central disk and five symmetric annular disks; and r₇ is a cubic algebraic number.

**中文。** 定义：单位圆盘的 n-盘覆盖半径 r_n 指覆盖单位圆盘的 n 个等半径盘的最小半径。猜想：r₆ = (1+√3)/2·(√3−1) 型显式二次代数数(由一个中心盘与五个环形盘的对称构型实现);r₇ 为三次代数数。
