# 00000004384

**English.** Definition: The spectral sequence of the Tate construction is the spectral sequence induced by the equivariant filtration. Conjecture: Its vanishing threshold is an explicit function of the rank of the group, and for elementary abelian p-groups the threshold is the rank minus one, which is optimal. (rank threshold of Tate vanishing for abelian groups)

**中文。** 定义：Tate 构造的谱序列指等变过滤导出的谱序列。猜想：其消没阈值为群秩的显式函数,初等交换 p 群作用时阈值为秩减一,最优。（Tate 消失交换群秩阈值）
