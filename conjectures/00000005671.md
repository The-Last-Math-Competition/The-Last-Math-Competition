**English.** Definition: The renormalization limit is the convergence of scale-by-scale renormalization, with the computation of critical exponents. Conjecture: The renormalization conversion constant of two-dimensional fractal walks is the explicit formula of the linearized spectrum of the renormalization map, with leading eigenvalue the reciprocal of the critical exponent; the modulus of the second eigenvalue is strictly below 1, and the spectral gap controls the convergence rate; and the fixed point of the renormalization map is superattracting, with attracting basin the whole parameter simplex. (linearized-spectrum conversion of two-dimensional renormalization)

**中文。** 定义：重整极限：逐尺度重整的收敛；临界指数的计算。猜想：二维分形游走的重整换算常数为重整映射线性化谱的显式公式且首特征值为临界指数的倒数；次特征值的模严格小于 1 且谱隙控制收敛速率；重整映射的不动点为超吸引型且吸引域为整个参数单形。（二维重整线性化谱换算）
