# 00000008597

**English.** Definition: The minimax rate of support estimation under Hausdorff distance. Conjecture: For boundaries of smoothness beta the rate is log n over n raised to beta over 2 beta plus d, with the logarithmic factor necessary; and the adaptive version loses the square of the logarithm. (support estimation necessary logarithm)

**中文。** 定义：支撑估计的极小极大速率在 Hausdorff 距离下。猜想：β 光滑边界的速率为 logn 除 n 的 β 除 2β 加 d 次幂且对数因子必要；且自适应版本亏对数平方。（支撑估计对数必要）
