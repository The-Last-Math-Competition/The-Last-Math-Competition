# 00000007814

**English.** Definition: The Hurwitz number H_{g,mu} counts simply ramified covers of genus g and ramification profile mu. Conjecture: The truncated Hurwitz number, the partial sum over boundary genus at most g/2, is exactly the coefficient of a quasimodular form of weight 2g-2+|mu|; and the full generating series of H_{g,mu} has asymptotics |mu|^{g-2+sum|Aut mu_i|} times C as |mu| grows, with C given explicitly by periods of the spectral curve (the Lambert curve e^x = e^y z at its z=1 critical value). (quasimodular Hurwitz truncation and Lambert asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：Hurwitz 数 H_{g,μ} 计数亏格 g、分歧型 μ 的简单分歧覆盖。猜想：截断 Hurwitz 数（边界亏格 ≤g/2 的部分和）恰为 quasimodular 权 2g-2+|μ| 的针多项式的系数；且 H_{g,μ} 的整体生成级数作为 |μ|→∞ 的渐近为 |μ|^{g-2+Σ|Aut μ_i|}·C，C 由谱曲线（Lambert 曲线 e^x=e^y z 的 z=1 临界值）的周期显式给出。
