# 00000004322

**English.** Definition: The Artin-Hasse power series is the corrected exponential series of the p-canonical lift. Conjecture: The p-power distribution of the denominators of its coefficients is the geometric law with parameter 1/p, and the exceptional coefficient occurs uniquely at the exponent p-1. (AH denominator geometric law and unique exception)

**中文。** 定义：Artin–Hasse 幂级数指 p 典范提升的修正指数级数。猜想：其系数分母的 p 幂分布为参数 1/p 的几何律,例外系数恰在指数 p−1 处唯一出现。（AH 分母几何律与唯一例外）
