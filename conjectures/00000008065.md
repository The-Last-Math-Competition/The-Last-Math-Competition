# 00000008065

**PRF-15 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The ordinal measure of strong normalization of lambda calculus: the supremum sequence sl(n) of strong reduction lengths of typed lambda terms (order n polymorphic types). Conjecture: The exact growth of sl(n) is the hierarchy of F_{omega^omega^{...}} (an n-layer tower) (a tightening of Statman-type theorems); the size of the normalizer under the optimal (shortest) reduction strategy is linear in term length (linearity of the optimal strategy); and the combinatorial constant of the semantic method for strong normalization (domain theory) is the realizability number of disjoint sums (a combinatorial interpretation of the domain constant), agreeing with the logarithm of the cardinality of Scott domains. (Statman tower optimal strategy linearity)

**中文。** 定义：λ-演算的强正规化的序数测度：类型 λ-项的强归约长度的上确界序列 sl(n)（阶 n 多态类型）。猜想：sl(n) 的精确增长为的 F_{ω^ω^…}（n 层塔）的层级（Statman 型定理的紧化）；且归约策略的最优性（最短归约策略）的规范化子的规模为项长的线性（最优策略的线性性）；强正规化证明的语义方法（domain 理论）的组合常数为不交和的可实现数（domain 常数的组合解释）与 Scott 域的基数的对数一致。
