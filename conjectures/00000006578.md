# 00000006578

**English.** Definition: The stochastic formula with the noise-reordering deficit structure. Conjecture: The noise-reordering deficit is step-size type with exact coefficient; the verification of exactness is the jitter expansion; and the coefficient of the jitter expansion is the iterated expectation. (step-size coefficient of the noise-reordering deficit)

**中文。** 定义：随机公式的噪声重排亏结构。猜想：噪声重排亏为步长型且系数精确；且精确的验证为抖动展开；且抖动展开的系数为迭代期望。（噪声重排亏步长系数）
