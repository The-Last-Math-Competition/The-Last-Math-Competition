# 00000004075

**English.** Definition: A Newhouse region is an open set in which parameters with homoclinic tangencies are dense. Conjecture: The Hausdorff dimension of the set of quadratic-tangency parameters is constantly full in the interior of a Newhouse region and decays continuously to zero at the boundary. (full-dimension interior law of Newhouse regions)

**中文。** 定义：Newhouse 区域指同宿切线在其中参数稠密的开集。猜想：二次切线参数集的 Hausdorff 维数在 Newhouse 区域内部恒为满维,并连续衰减至边界处为零。（Newhouse 维数满维内部律）
