**SC6-08 ★** — source: 225_概形论II.md

**English.** Definition: The Picard scheme with the moduli structure of line bundles. Conjecture: The representability of the functor is projective flat, and the obstruction to representability is the Brauer class. (Brauer-obstructed Picard representability)

**中文。** 定义：Picard 概形：线丛的模结构。猜想：Picard 的可表性：函子的可表为射影平坦且表的障碍为 Brauer 类。（Picard 可表 Brauer 障碍射影平坦函子）
