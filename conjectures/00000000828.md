# 00000000828

**English.** Definition: The fractional Sobolev constant is the best factor in the critical embedding. Conjecture: The optimal constant is attained uniquely by the dilation-invariant bubble family; and uniqueness is proved by the moving-plane method. (fractional extremizer uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：分数 Sobolev 常数为临界嵌入的最佳因子。猜想：最优常数由拉伸不变 bubble 族唯一达成；且唯一性的证明为移动平面法。（分数极值唯一性）
