# 00000005461

**English.** Definition: The modular square root is the root extraction modulo a prime. Conjecture: The average complexity of the classical algorithm is the explicit expectation of the dyadic valuation, the expectation converges to a constant with the size of the prime, and the formula is exact with no correction term. (dyadic-valuation expectation formula of modular square roots)

**中文。** 定义：模平方根指模素数的开方运算。猜想：经典算法的平均复杂度为二进赋位的显式期望式,期望随素数尺寸收敛到常数且公式精确无修正项。（模开方二进赋位期望公式）
