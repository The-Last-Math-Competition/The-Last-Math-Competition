# 00000005050

**English.** Definition: First-order and higher-order queries are two layers of expressive power. Conjecture: There exist two classes of finite structures where all first-order query results agree but some second-order query results differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit parity-counting extension. (same first-order-different second-order parity separation)

**中文。** 定义：一阶与高阶查询指两层表达力。猜想：存在两有限结构类的一切一阶查询结果相同而某二阶查询结果不同,分离由奇偶计数的显式扩张实现。（一阶同-二阶异奇偶分离）
