# 00000001977

**MP-17 ★** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: The Witten index is the index of supersymmetric quantum mechanics. Conjecture: For sigma models with flag-manifold targets, the Witten index is identically the q-power series of the elliptic genus; the leading power is n(n−1)/2. (flag manifold Witten elliptic index)

**中文。** 定义：Witten 指标指超对称量子力学的指标。猜想：目标为旗流形的 sigma 模型的 Witten 指标恒等于椭圆亏格的 q 幂级数；幂次为 n(n−1)/2。（旗流形 Witten 椭圆指标）
