# 00000002869

**IX-09 ★** — source: 81_微分算子的指标II.md

**English.** Definition: Indices of elliptic complexes: Euler-type invariants of de Rham and Dolbeault complexes. Conjecture: Multiplicativity of complex indices: the index of a tensor product complex is a correction of the Euler product, the correction being the dimension difference of kernels of boundary operators; the geometric meaning is the splitting of supertraces. (complex index supertrace splitting)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆复形的指标：de Rham 与 Dolbeault 复形的 Euler 型不变量。猜想：复形指标的乘性：张量复形的指标为 Euler 乘积的修正且修正项为边界算子的核的维数差；修正的几何意义为超迹的分裂。（复形指标超迹分裂）
