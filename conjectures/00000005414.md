**HM2-04 ◆** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: The absolute continuity criterion characterizes the full continuity of the measure with respect to area. Conjecture: There is an explicit slit-density threshold condition, at which absolute continuity turns to singularity, and the criterion is complete without gaps, with the exceptional domains exactly the finite unions of polynomial CREILE domains. (slit-density threshold criterion of absolute continuity)

**中文。** 定义：绝对连续判据指测度对面积的全连续刻画。猜想：阈裂隙密度的显式条件,阈值处绝对连续向奇性转变且判据完全无空隙。(绝对连续裂隙密度阈值判据)；且例外域恰为多项式 CREILE 域的有限并。
