# 00000005313

**RS2-23 ★** — source: 162_组合的代数_表示稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: Minimal generation is an indecomposable generating set. Conjecture: The conversion constant of two generators is an explicit polynomial of the chain length, of degree the generation degree, and the constant is exact. (chain-length polynomial of two ordered generators)

**中文。** 定义：极小生成指不可分解的生成集合。猜想：二生成的换算常数为链长的显式多项式,次数为生成度且常数精确。（有序模二生成链长多项式）
