# 00000004737

**English.** Definition: The differential Brauer group is the group of holomorphic Brauer classes of a complex manifold. Conjecture: Its three-torsion computation reduces to an explicit quotient of exponential sheaf cohomology, and the quotient is finite on compact three-dimensional complex manifolds and computable term by term. (quotient computation of differential Brauer three-torsion)

**中文。** 定义：微分 Brauer 群指复流形的全纯 Brauer 类群。猜想：其三挠的计算归约为指数层上同调的显式商,商在紧三维复流形上有限且可逐项计算。（微分 Brauer 三挠商计算）
