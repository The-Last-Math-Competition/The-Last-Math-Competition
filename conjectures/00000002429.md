**English.** Definition: An orbifold covering is the universal covering of an orbifold. Conjecture: The realizing groups of two-dimensional orbifolds are extensions of discrete rotation groups; and the table of the extensions is the list of triangle groups. (two-dimensional orbifold realization triangle group table)
