**English.** Definition: The Zassenhaus combination of the Conway polynomial coefficients Delta_n. Conjecture: The Hoste inequality sum of Delta_{2i} at most binom(n,2) is tight for (n,n-1)-torus links, and the equality spectrum is uniquely the torus family. (equality spectrum of the Conway Hoste inequality)
