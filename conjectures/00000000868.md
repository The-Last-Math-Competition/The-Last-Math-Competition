# 00000000868

**English.** Definition: The anisotropic p-Laplacian. Conjecture: The Wulff shape is the extremizer of all anisotropic inequalities, and the product of anisotropic Sobolev constants satisfies an identity (Wulff universality).

**中文。** 定义：anisotropic p-Laplacian。猜想：Wulff 形状为所有各向异性不等式的极值,且各向异性 Sobolev 常数的乘积恒等(Wulff 普适性)。
