# 00000007708

**English.** Definition: The k-th moment m_k of the Poisson-Voronoi cell is the k-th moment of the diameter of the typical cell in the Voronoi decomposition of a unit-intensity Poisson process. Conjecture: The moment generating function sum m_k t^k/k! is not D-finite; the coefficient asymptotics m_k ~ k! C rho^{-k} k^{-3/2} has rho the smallest positive root of a cubic polynomial and C a transcendental constant containing Gamma(1/3). (non-D-finiteness of Voronoi moment series)

**中文。** 定义：Poisson–Voronoi 胞腔的 k 阶矩 m_k 为单位强度 Poisson 点过程 Voronoi 分解中典型胞腔直径的 k 阶矩。猜想：矩母函数 Σ m_k t^k/k! 不是 D-有限的（不满足常系数线性微分方程），其系数的渐近 m_k ~ k! C ρ^{-k} k^{-3/2} 中 ρ 为 3 次多项式的最小正根，C 为含有 Γ(1/3) 的超越常数。
