# 00000000884

**English.** Definition: The time-average measure of semilinear waves is an invariant measure on the energy surface. Conjecture: The rate of ergodization is always controlled by the geometric ergodic rate; and the correction for nonuniform ergodicity is an explicit power of the spectral gap. (nonuniform ergodization rate)

**中文。** 定义：半线性波的时间平均测度为能量面的不变测度。猜想：遍历化率恒由几何遍历率控制；且非均匀遍历的修正为谱间隙的显式幂。（非均匀遍历率）
