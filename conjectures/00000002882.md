**English.** Definition: Symbol calculus of elliptic pseudodifferential operators: the multiplication formula. Conjecture: The index corollary of associativity of symbol multiplication: the trace of the Poisson bracket of principal symbols is zero (the trace formula), with correction terms being commutator densities of sub-symbols. (symbol Poisson trace zero)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆伪微分算子的符号计算：乘法公式。猜想：符号乘法的结合律的指标推论：主符号的 Poisson 括号的迹为零（轨迹公式）且公式的修正项为次象征的交换子密度。（符号 Poisson 迹零）
