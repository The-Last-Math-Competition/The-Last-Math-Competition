# 00000006262

**English.** Definition: The variational principle with the variational approximation of topological entropy. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit sequence of approximating measures, polynomial approximation when periodic measures are dense; the coefficient of approximation is exact, one over the orbit length; and the approximation error is zero on entropy-continuous systems. (polynomial coefficient conversion of periodic approximation)

**中文。** 定义：变分原理：拓扑熵的变分逼近。猜想：换算常数为逼近测度的显式序列且周期测度稠密时多项式逼近；逼近的系数精确且系数为轨道长度分之一；逼近的误差在熵连续系统上为零。（周期逼近多项式系数换算）
