# 00000007689

**QDE-29 ★** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: The two-parameter count a_{r,s} of the Bailey sliding chain is the number of distinct identities after sliding the a parameter r times and the b parameter s times. Conjecture: a_{r,s} satisfies the Delannoy recursion a_{r,s} = a_{r-1,s} + a_{r,s-1} + a_{r-1,s-1} with a_{r,s} = D(r,s) the Delannoy numbers; the diagonal grows as a_{m,m} ~ c 3^m m^{-1/2}; and the minimal factor count on the product side at level (r,s) is r+s+1, attained by purely directional paths. (Delannoy counting of sliding chains)

**中文。** 定义：Bailey 滑动链的双参数计数 a_{r,s} 指同时滑动 a 参数 r 次、b 参数 s 次后得到的互异恒等式个数。猜想：a_{r,s} 满足 Delannoy 递推 a_{r,s} = a_{r-1,s} + a_{r,s-1} + a_{r-1,s-1} 且 a_{r,s} = D(r,s) 为 Delannoy 数;对角线 a_{m,m} 呈中心 Delannoy 增长 a_{m,m} ~ c·3^m·m^{-1/2},且第 (r,s) 层恒等式乘积侧最小因子数为 r+s+1,下界由纯方向路径达到。（滑动链 Delannoy 计数）
