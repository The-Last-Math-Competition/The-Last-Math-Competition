# 00000009990

**NCR-20 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Global dimension of graded rings: the difference between graded and ungraded global dimension. Conjecture: The ungraded global dimension of a Noetherian graded ring is controlled by the graded global dimension plus the supremum of generation degrees; the bound is tight, attained by explicit families of Veronese subrings. (graded-ungraded dimension difference bound)

**中文。** 定义：分次环的整体维数：分次与不分次的整体维数的差异。猜想：Noetherian 分次环的不分次整体维数被分次整体维数加生成次数上确界控制；界为紧且达到的例子为 Veronese 子环的显式族。（分次-不分次维数差界）
