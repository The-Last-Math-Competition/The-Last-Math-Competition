# 00000007796

**ACG-16 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Levi limiting shape of convex body sequences: the scaling limit h_inf of the support functions h_n(theta), defined on the infinite-dimensional sphere limit. Conjecture: Any Levi limiting shape of a minimal Mahler-product sequence is invariant under Legendre duality (self-dual), and the self-dual limiting shapes are exactly the convex solutions p of the PDE p p^* identically 2; the piecewise-linearizations of this solution are exactly the Hanner polytope family. (self-dual Levi limits and Hanner)

**中文。** 定义：凸体序列的 Levi 极限形状：高维凸体 K_n 的支承函数 h_n(θ) 的缩放极限 h_∞ 定义在无限维球面极限上。猜想：任何极小 Mahler 乘积序列的 Levi 极限形状在 Legendre 对偶下不变（自对偶），且自对偶极限形状为唯一对称凸函数 p 满足 p·p^*≡2 的偏微分方程的凸解；该解的分片线性化恰为 Hanner 体族。
