# 00000008517

**English.** Definition: Reducibility of mixed differential-difference systems. Conjecture: The existence domain of mixed irreducible systems is a parameter lattice; and the minimal dimension of systems that are purely differentialally irreducible and purely difference irreducible but mixed reducible is 3. (minimal dimension of mixed reducibility)
