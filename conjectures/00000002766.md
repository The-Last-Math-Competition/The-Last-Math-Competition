# 00000002766

**English.** Definition: Density of small-rank matrices: PI of varieties of matrices of rank at most r. Conjecture: The minimal identity degree of the rank-r matrix variety is 2r+2, realized by the alternating determinant kernel; the coefficient-growth exponent of the identity is exactly r². (small-rank variety identity degree)

**中文。** 定义：小秩矩阵的稠密：秩不超过 r 的矩阵簇的 PI。猜想：秩 r 矩阵簇的极小恒等式次数为 2r+2 且次数的实现由交错化的行列式核给出；恒等式的系数增长指数恰为 r²。（小秩簇恒等式次数）
