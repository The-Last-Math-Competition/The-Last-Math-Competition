# 00000009663

**English.** Definition: The jump structure of the free Poisson process: the free Levy process realization of the Marchenko-Pastur distribution. Conjecture: The discretized realization of jump times of the free Poisson process is unique, generated by the square-root skeleton of a classical Poisson process; the Erlang distribution parameters of the skeleton correspond explicitly to jump rates, with no other realization. (free Poisson skeleton uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：自由泊松过程的跳结构：Marchenko–Pastur 分布的自由 Lévy 过程实现。猜想：自由泊松过程的跳时刻的离散化实现唯一：由经典 Poisson 过程的平方根骨架生成；骨架的 Erlang 分布参数与跳率显式对应且无其它实现。（自由泊松骨架唯一）
