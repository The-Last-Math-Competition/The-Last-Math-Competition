# 00000005478

**IF2-08 ★** — source: 168_数学物理IV_可积场论.md

**English.** Definition: The fusion angles are the vertex structures of the fusion triangles. Conjecture: The fusion angles of A_3 are explicit cosine expressions of exterior products, degenerating to a single angle at chained fusion; the cosine of the fusion angle is an algebraic number of degree at most 4; and the angle spectrum converges to zero as the fusion level grows, with quadratic convergence. (chained cosine structure of A_3 fusion angles)

**中文。** 定义：聚合角：束缚三角（fusion）的顶角结构。猜想：A₃ 的聚合角为外积的显式余弦式且链式聚合处退化为单角；聚合角的余弦为代数数且次数不超过 4；角度谱在聚合级趋大时收敛于零且收敛为平方型。（A3 聚合角链式余弦结构）
