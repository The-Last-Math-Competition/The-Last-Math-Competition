**ADY-09 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: height zeta function: Z_f(s)=Σ_{P∈Per(f)} h(P)^{-s} (Per(f) of system is periodic point set, h of f is pair Weil height), d=deg f. Conjecture: convergent coordinates of Z_f is σ_c=1/log d; and Z_f in Re s>1/log d in analytic and neighborhood, pole exactly in s=1/log d+2πik/log d (k∈Z) at, number of can extension contains the line is always mass constant of distribution measure of f.

**中文。** 定义：动力系统的高度 zeta 函数：Z_f(s)=Σ_{P∈Per(f)} h(P)^{-s}（Per(f) 为 f 的周期点集，h 为绝对 Weil 高度），d=deg f。猜想：Z_f 的收敛横坐标为 σ_c=1/log d；且 Z_f 在 Re s>1/log d 内解析并可延拓到含该直线的邻域，极点恰在 s=1/log d+2πik/log d（k∈Z）处，残数为 f 的等分布测度的总质量常数。
