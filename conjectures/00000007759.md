# 00000007759

**ADY-09 ★** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The dynamical height zeta function is Z_f(s) = sum h(P)^{-s} over periodic points P with absolute Weil height h, for d = deg f. Conjecture: The convergence abscissa of Z_f is sigma_c = 1/log d; Z_f is analytic on Re s > 1/log d and extends to a neighborhood of that line, with poles exactly at s = 1/log d + 2 pi i k/log d for integers k, with residues the total mass constant of the equidistribution measure of f. (pole lattice of dynamical height zeta)

**中文。** 定义：动力系统的高度 zeta 函数：Z_f(s)=Σ_{P∈Per(f)} h(P)^{-s}（Per(f) 为 f 的周期点集，h 为绝对 Weil 高度），d=deg f。猜想：Z_f 的收敛横坐标为 σ_c=1/log d；且 Z_f 在 Re s>1/log d 内解析并可延拓到含该直线的邻域，极点恰在 s=1/log d+2πik/log d（k∈Z）处，残数为 f 的等分布测度的总质量常数。
