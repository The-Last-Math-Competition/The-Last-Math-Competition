# 00000000025

**NT-25 ★** — source: 01_数论.md

**English.** Definition: The repulsion structure of Chebotarev zeros: the coexistence pattern of the real zeros of Artin L-functions in the interval from 1−c/log D to 1. Conjecture: The L-functions attached to two non-overlapping irreducible characters of the same Galois extension never simultaneously possess zeros in the interval from 1−c/log D to 1 (Deuring–Heilbronn repulsion); the repulsion is logarithmic — the distance from the secondary zero to the primary zero is bounded below by c·log log D / log D; an exceptional zero exists if and only if the associated character is real, and that Siegel-type zero is unique within the extension. (Deuring repulsion of Chebotarev zeros)

**中文。** 定义：Chebotarev 零点的排斥结构：Artin L 函数的实零点在从 1-c/log D 到 1 的区间内的共存模式。猜想：同一 Galois 扩张的两个非交叠不可约特征对应的 L 函数不同时拥有从 1-c/log D 到 1 区间内的零点（Deuring–Heilbronn 排斥）；排斥为对数型——次零点距主零点的距离下界为 c·log log D 除以 log D；例外零点存在当且仅当相应特征为实特征且该 Siegel 型零点在扩群内唯一。（Chebotarev 零点 Deuring 排斥）
