# 00000004234

**English.** Definition: The simplicial torsion is the torsion subgroup of the homology of a triangulation. Conjecture: The prime factors of the torsion are bounded by a linear function of the number of points, the bound is attained by lens space triangulations, and the prime-factor spectrum is asymptotically uniform in the large-prime limit. (linear bound on simplicial torsion prime factors)

**中文。** 定义：单形挠指剖分同调的挠子群。猜想：挠的素因子不超过点数的线性函数,界由透镜空间剖分达到,素因子谱在大素数极限下渐近均匀。（单形挠素因子线性界）
