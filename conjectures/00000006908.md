# 00000006908

**English.** Definition: The minimal projection size is the infimum of optimal decompositions. Conjecture: Monomial families attain the size bound and the equality spectrum is closed; and the exception of attainment is given by ultrametric-type tensors. (monomial attainment of minimal projections)

**中文。** 定义：极小投影尺寸为最优分解的下确界。猜想：单项式族达到尺寸界且等式谱为闭集；且达界的例外由超度量型张量给出。（极小投影单项达界）
