**English.** Definition: The obstacle problem is the variational problem constraining the solution above an obstacle, with the singularities of the contact set. Conjecture: The singularity classification of the four-dimensional contact set is an explicit two-class partition delimited by the frequency value 3/2; below the threshold singularities are smooth type and above branch type; and the density of branch-type singularities is a constant multiple of 3/2, with the multiple explicit. (two-class frequency delimitation of four-dimensional singularities)

**中文。** 定义：障碍问题：解约束在障碍之上的变分问题；接触集的奇点。猜想：四维接触集的奇点分类为二类的显式分划且由频率值 3/2 阈分界；阈值以下奇点为光滑型且以上为分支型；分支型奇点的密度为 3/2 的常数倍且倍数显式。（四维奇点二类频率分界）
