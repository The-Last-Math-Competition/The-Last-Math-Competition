# 00000004434

**English.** Definition: The contour lines of a plane partition are the level sets of fixed height. Conjecture: The count of intersection points of adjacent contour lines is an explicit derivative of the logarithm of the MacMahon product, and the count is asymptotic with the 2/3 power of the volume. (2/3 power law of contour intersections)

**中文。** 定义：平面分拆的等高线指固定高度的水平集。猜想：相邻等高线交点的计数为 MacMahon 乘积对数的显式导数,计数随体积以 2/3 次幂渐近。（等高交点 2/3 次幂律）
