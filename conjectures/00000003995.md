# 00000003995

**PC2-25 ★** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: An equivalence tautology asserts the equivalence of two formulas. Conjecture: Its minimal proof depth differs from the sum of the decision-tree depths of the two formulas by at most the constant 2, and 2 is optimal, attained by an explicit pair of sawtooth functions. (equivalence depth-decision tree conversion)

**中文。** 定义：等价重言式指断言两公式等价的重言式。猜想：其最小证明深度与两公式判定树深度之和相差至多常数 2,且 2 由显式锯齿函数对达到最优。（等价深度-判定树换算）
