# 00000000270

**English.** Definition: An elliptic divisibility sequence is the sequence of division-polynomial values of multiples of a point on an elliptic curve. Conjecture: For every nondegenerate such sequence, the largest prime factor of its terms tends to infinity; and the growth rate is at least log log. (EDS largest prime factor growth)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆 divisibility 序列指椭圆曲线点的除子多项式值序列。猜想：任一非退化序列的项的最大素因子趋于无穷；且增长速率至少为 loglog。（EDS 最大素因子增长）
