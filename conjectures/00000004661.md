# 00000004661

**English.** Definition: The multiplicity is the leading coefficient of the Hilbert function of a local ring. Conjecture: A ring of multiplicity two is always a hypersurface, non-hypersurface rings of multiplicity three are completely classified in dimension at most six, new families appear from dimension seven on, and the boundary is optimal. (dimension-six classification bound of multiplicity three)

**中文。** 定义：重数指局部环希尔伯特函数的首项系数。猜想：重数为二的环恒为超曲面,重数为三的非超曲面环在维数不超过六时分类完全,维数七起出现新族且分界最优。（重数三的六维分类界）
