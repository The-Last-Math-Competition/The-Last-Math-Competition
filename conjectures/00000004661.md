**SG3-01 ★** — source: 141_交换代数IV_奇点理论.md

**English.** Definition: multiplicity denotes leading term coefficient of local ring function. Conjecture: multiplicity is ring of two is always hypersurface, multiplicity is non- hypersurface ring in dimension of three does not exceed six when classification completely, dimension seven new family and separates optimal. (six dimension classification bound of multiplicity three)

**中文。** 定义：重数指局部环希尔伯特函数的首项系数。猜想：重数为二的环恒为超曲面,重数为三的非超曲面环在维数不超过六时分类完全,维数七起出现新族且分界最优。（重数三的六维分类界）
