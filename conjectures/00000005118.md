**English.** Definition: The matrix logarithm is the inverse branch of the exponential map. Conjecture: In the nonnormal case the lower bound of the logarithm condition number is an explicit reciprocal expression of the spectral angular separation, equality at Jordan blocks, and not improvable. (angular-separation lower bound of the logarithm condition number)
