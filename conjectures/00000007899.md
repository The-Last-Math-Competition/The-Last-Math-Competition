# 00000007899

**English.** Definition: The boundary walls of the thick subcategories of the sphere spectrum: the topology of the generization graph of the prime spectrum of thick(S). Conjecture: The generization order is the full linear order omega+1 (the Hopkins-Smith order); and under the primary decomposition the K-groups of the boundary walls (the quotient layers between adjacent primes) are finite p-local groups, of order an integral multiple of the degree polynomial product over i=1 to n of (p^i - 1) of the v_n self-map; that multiple equals 1 exactly for walls of type (n, n+1). (sphere spectrum wall K-group orders)

**中文。** 定义：球谱的厚子范畴的边界墙：thick(S) 的素谱的 generization 图的拓扑。猜想：generization 序为全线性序 ω+1（Hopkins–Smith 序）；且在准素分解下的边界墙（两相邻素间的商层）的 K-群为 p-局部有限群，阶为 v_n 的自映射的次数多项式 Π_{i=1}^{n}(p^i-1) 的整倍数；该倍数在墙的类型为 type (n, n+1) 时为 1（取等）。
