**English.** Definition: Seepage with the mixed scheme of Darcy flow. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit norm ratio of the flow-pressure duality and the norm equivalence of conforming elements holds; the ratio is tight, the tight example being Raviart-Thomas elements; and the kernel of the duality is the solenoidal field. (tight norm-ratio conversion of flow-pressure duality)
