**English.** Definition: The DT-RH problem of the loop quiver (tube category) is the RH construction of tubular DT invariants. Conjecture: Its solution is an explicit tau function of a principal homogeneous space (G, ∇); the tau function solves a Painlevé III type equation (via the similarity degeneration of the KP equations).
