# 00000003305

**English.** Definition: Orthogonality of polynomial families: the Askey scheme. Conjecture: The limits of the Askey tree: the limit contraction of orthogonal polynomial families is the limit chain of parameters, with the topology of the chain the planar embedding of the Hasse diagram. (Askey limit chain Hasse planar embedding)
