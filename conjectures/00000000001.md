# 00000000001

**English.** Definition: The decimal digit sum s(p) of a prime p is the sum of its digits. Conjecture: There exist infinitely many primes p such that s(p) is a perfect square; moreover, the density of such primes equals the value predicted by the logarithm-squared heuristic. (prime digit-square density)

**中文。** 定义：素数的十进制数字和 s(p) 为各位数字之和。猜想：存在无穷多个素数 p 使 s(p) 为完全平方数；且此类素数的密度为对数平方窗内的预期值。（素数数字平方密度）
