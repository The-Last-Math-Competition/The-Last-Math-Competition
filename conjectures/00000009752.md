# 00000009752

**English.** Definition: Algebraic certificates of combinatorial infeasibility: polynomial-method proofs of SAT. Conjecture: Below the phase transition point of random 3-SAT, Nullstellensatz certificates have polynomial sparsity; the certificate degree threshold coincides with the spectral gap of the hypergraph adjacency spectrum, jumping to linear above the transition. (Nullstellensatz sparsity)

**中文。** 定义：组合可行性的代数证书：多项式方法的 SAT 证明。猜想：随机 3-SAT 在相变点以下时 Nullstellensatz 证书的稀疏度为多项式；证书度数阈值与超图邻接谱的谱隙重合且在相变点上方跳到线性。（Nullstellensatz 稀疏度）
