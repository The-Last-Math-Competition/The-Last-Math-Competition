# 00000009874

**KPU-24 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: Reaction-diffusion A+B to nothing: fluctuation universality for separated initial conditions. Conjecture: The fluctuation limit of reaction-diffusion with separated initial conditions is an explicit functional of a birth-death point process; the density autocorrelation exponent of fluctuations is 1/4, independent of reaction rate and controlled solely by the transport exponent. (reaction-diffusion fluctuation functional)

**中文。** 定义：反应扩散 A+B→∅：分离初条件的涨落普适。猜想：分离初条件的反应扩散的涨落极限为生灭点过程的显式泛函；涨落的密度自相关指数为 1/4 且与反应率无关，仅由输运指数控制。（反应扩散涨落泛函）
