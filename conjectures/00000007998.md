# 00000007998

**English.** Definition: The degenerate extension of the Bernstein problem for minimal graphs: the dimension threshold n_B = 7 for entire solutions of the minimal graph equation div(grad u/sqrt(1+|grad u|^2)) = 0 (the known Bernstein theorem boundary). Conjecture: The singular set of singular minimal graphs for n at least 8 has dimension lower bound n-8 (Simons cone rigidity), and examples attaining the bound (cone-type graphs) are isolated in the compactified graph space; and the proof obstruction to rigidity of entire solutions at n = 7 is equivalent to the codimension-1 stability of the Simons cone, with an explicit bound -3/2 + sqrt(17/4) for the first negative eigenvalue of the second variation spectrum of stability (the cone spectrum constant). (Bernstein threshold Simons cone spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：极小图方程的 Bernstein 问题的退化延拓：极小图方程 div(∇u/√(1+|∇u|²))=0 的整体解的维数阈值 n_B=7（已知 Bernstein 定理边界）。猜想：n≥8 的奇异极小图的奇点集的维数下界为 n-8（Simons 锥刚性）且达到下界的例子（锥型图）在紧化的图空间中孤立；且 n=7 的整体解的刚性的证明障碍与 Simons 锥的余维 1 稳定性等价，稳定性的二阶变分的谱的第一个负特征值的显式界为 -3/2+√(17/4)（锥谱常数）。
