# 00000008438

**English.** Definition: The strength t of an orthogonal array: the uniformity of any t rows. Conjecture: The maximal number of columns of a strength-t OA is N(n,t) at most n^2 - n + t; the bound is attained when n is a prime power; and for non-prime-powers the deficit is at least n^{1/2}. (maximal column count of orthogonal arrays)

**中文。** 定义：正交表的强度 t 指任意 t 行的均匀性。猜想：强度 t 的 OA 的最大列数 N(n,t) ≤ n²−n+t；素数幂 n 时该界可达；非素数幂时亏量 ≥ n^{1/2}。（正交表最大列数）
