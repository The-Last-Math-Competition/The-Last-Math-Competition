# 00000008438

**DFG-28 ★** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: strong t of orthogonal table denotes all of arbitrary t row. Conjecture: most number N(n,t) ≤ n²−n+t; prime power n when the bound; non- prime power quantity ≥ n^{1/2} of OA of strong t. (orthogonal table most number)

**中文。** 定义：正交表的强度 t 指任意 t 行的均匀性。猜想：强度 t 的 OA 的最大列数 N(n,t) ≤ n²−n+t；素数幂 n 时该界可达；非素数幂时亏量 ≥ n^{1/2}。（正交表最大列数）
