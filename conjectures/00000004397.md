**GL2-07 ★** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: The flag algebra is the semidefinite programming framework based on partially labeled graphs, and the positive cone is the cone of its nonnegative elements. Conjecture: The convergence rate of finite approximations of the positive cone is polynomial, with the exponent linearly related to the number of labels, and the lower bound of the approximation error is explicit and cannot be compressed further. (polynomial convergence rate of the flag positive cone)

**中文。** 定义：旗代数指以部分标号图为基的半定规划框架,正锥指其非负元素锥。猜想：正锥有限逼近的收敛速率为多项式,指数与标号数线性相关,逼近误差的下界显式不可再压。（旗正锥多项式收敛率）
