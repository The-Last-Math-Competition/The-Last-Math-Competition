# 00000001094

**FG-94 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: The Welch bound of difference sets (a lower bound on periodic autocorrelation). Conjecture: There are infinitely many sequence families saturating the Welch bound, given by Legendre families (Welch-saturation infinitude).

**中文。** 定义：差异集的 Welch 界(周期自相关的下界)。猜想：最优序列族的 Welch 界饱和序列为 Legendre 族无穷多(Welch 饱和无穷性)。
