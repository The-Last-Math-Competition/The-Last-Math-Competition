**English.** Definition: The Dolbeault moduli space is the moduli space of Higgs pairs. Conjecture: The Poincare polynomial of rank two is an explicit polynomial of the genus, given by the topological count of chains and verified at low genus. (genus polynomial of rank-two Dolbeault Poincare polynomials)

**中文。** 定义：Dolbeault 模空间指希格斯对的模空间。猜想：秩二的庞加莱多项式为亏格的显式多项式,由链的拓扑计数给出且低亏格验证。（秩二 Dolbeault 庞加莱亏格多项式）
