**English.** Definition: Miller's theorem concerns the unsolvability degree of the group isomorphism problem. Conjecture: The isomorphism problem for hyperbolic groups has a polynomial-time algorithm; and after the GAP solution of nilpotent group isomorphism, the largest remaining open class is 9-dimensional nilpotent groups. (hyperbolic isomorphism polynomial algorithm)

**中文。** 定义：Miller 定理指群同构问题的不可解度。猜想：双曲群的同构问题有多项式时间算法；幂零群同构的 GAP 解决后剩余最大未决类为九维幂零群。（双曲同构多项式算法）
