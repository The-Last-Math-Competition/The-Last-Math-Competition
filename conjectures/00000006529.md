**English.** Definition: The equivariant correction of the torsion is the noncommutative-case structure. Conjecture: The correction spectrum of the noncommutative case closes; the correction constant is the absolute value of the weight difference; and the minimal element of the correction spectrum is realized by the two-element group. (closed equivariant correction spectrum of torsion)
