# 00000006518

**English.** Definition: The ramification of characteristic classes with the covering side. Conjecture: The characteristic classes of coverings hold, and the combination of the classes is the Riemann-Hurwitz formula. (Riemann-Hurwitz characteristic classes of coverings)

**中文。** 定义：示性类的分歧：覆盖侧。猜想：分歧的示性：覆盖的示性类且类的组合为 Riemann–Hurwitz。（示性类分歧覆盖侧 Riemann-Hurwitz 覆盖示性组合分歧 Riemann-Hurwitz 示性覆盖 Hurwitz 示性覆盖）
