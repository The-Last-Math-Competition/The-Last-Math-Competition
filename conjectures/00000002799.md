# 00000002799

**English.** Definition: The subspace-quotient symmetry of Banach local theory: duality of constructions. Conjecture: The symmetry law of subspaces and quotients: the product of the BM distances of a high-dimensional subspace and a high-dimensional quotient of an arbitrary space has an explicit root lower bound in the dimension, the root given by the volume ratio. (subspace-quotient product lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Banach 局部理论的商-子空间对称：duality of constructions。猜想：子空间与商的对称律：任意空间的高维子空间与高维商的 BM 距离的乘积下界为维数的显式根且根由体积比给出。（子商乘积下界）
