# 00000003833

**English.** Definition: A crystal realization of Macdonald polynomials P_λ(x;q,t) denotes the arrow graph of their combinatorial structure in the (q,t)→(0,∞) limit; the weight count denotes the generating function of vertex weights. Conjecture: The weight count admits an explicit positive formula generalizing q,t-Kostka numbers. (Macdonald crystal weight count)

**中文。** 定义：Macdonald 多项式 P_λ(x;q,t) 的晶体实现指其组合结构在 (q,t)→(0,∞) 极限下的箭图,权计数指顶点权重的生成函数。猜想：该权计数具有 Kostka 数的 q,t-Kostka 推广的显式正公式(Macdonald 晶体权计数)。
