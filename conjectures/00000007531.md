**English.** Definition: Monodromy as the action representation of fundamental-group loops. Conjecture: The weight spectrum of monodromy operators is a finite set, weight semisimplicity being slope control; and the spectral gap is given by the monodromy theorem. (finite-gap monodromy weight spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：单值为基本群回路的作用表示。猜想：单值算子的权谱为有限集且权单化为斜率的控制；且谱的间隙由 Monodromy 定理给出。（单值权谱有限间隙）
