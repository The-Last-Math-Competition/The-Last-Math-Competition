**English.** Definition: The second Neumann eigenvalue μ₂(Ω) (Szegő–Weinberger: the maximum is attained by the disk). Conjecture: The maximum of μ₃ is attained by the union of two equal-volume disjoint disks (the extremal shape for the third Neumann eigenvalue).
