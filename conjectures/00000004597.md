# 00000004597

**English.** Definition: The Ruelle zeta function is the one-dimensional zeta function of prime geodesics. Conjecture: Its differentiability criterion is an explicit nonresonance condition on the geodesic length spectrum, the condition is always true in the convex-cocompact case and decidable in general. (Ruelle differentiability nonresonance criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Ruelle zeta 指素测地线的一维 zeta 函数。猜想：其可微性判据为测地长谱的非共振显式条件,条件在凸余紧情形恒真且一般可判。（Ruelle 可微非共振判据）
