**English.** Definition: Best low-rank approximation of tensors. Conjecture: The existence of best low-rank approximations is governed by closure defects: unlike matrices, the set of bounded-rank tensors is not closed, and the approximation fails via limiting divergence. (tensor best low-rank approximation closure defect)
