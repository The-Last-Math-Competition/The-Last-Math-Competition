# 00000005591

**English.** Definition: The Smith normal form is the diagonalization of integer matrices. Conjecture: Its computational complexity is an explicit logarithmic multiple of matrix-multiplication complexity, the multiple is controlled by the bit growth of invariant factors, and the bounds match under nearly quadratic multiplication. (multiplication-log multiple complexity of Smith forms)

**中文。** 定义：史密斯标准型指整矩阵的对角化。猜想：其计算复杂度为矩阵乘法复杂度的显式对数倍,倍数由不变因子的位增长控制且上下界在近平方乘法下匹配。（史密斯乘法对数倍复杂度）
