**English.** Definition: object of q- central limit theorem is self-adjoint extension spectral measure μ_q of operator (D_q f)(x)=(f(qx)-f(x))/((q-1)x). Conjecture: n order moment m_n(q) of μ_q is coefficient polynomial, -fold number of q is 2n, leading term coefficient is (-1)^n order times type (2n-1)!!; and all of m_n(q) lies in unit |q|=1 on, and spectrum uniform corresponds to of q-Gauss contraction group.

**中文。** 定义：q-中心极限定理的对象为算子 (D_q f)(x)=(f(qx)-f(x))/((q-1)x) 的自伴扩张谱测度 μ_q。猜想：μ_q 的 n 阶矩 m_n(q) 是 q 的整系数多项式，次数为 2n，首项系数为 (-1)^n 双阶乘型 (2n-1)!!；且 m_n(q) 的根全部落在单位圆 |q|=1 上，与 q-Gauss 收缩半群的谱一致性对应。
