# 00000007688

**QDE-28 △** — source: 241_解析组合III_q差分.md

**English.** Definition: The q-central limit theorem concerns the spectral measure mu_q of the self-adjoint extension of (D_q f)(x)=(f(qx)-f(x))/((q-1)x). Conjecture: The n-th moment m_n(q) of mu_q is a polynomial in q with integer coefficients, degree 2n, and leading coefficient (-1)^n times the double factorial (2n-1)!!; and all roots of m_n(q) lie on the unit circle |q|=1, in correspondence with the spectrum of the q-Gaussian contraction semigroup. (moment polynomials of the q-Gaussian)

**中文。** 定义：q-中心极限定理的对象为算子 (D_q f)(x)=(f(qx)-f(x))/((q-1)x) 的自伴扩张谱测度 μ_q。猜想：μ_q 的 n 阶矩 m_n(q) 是 q 的整系数多项式，次数为 2n，首项系数为 (-1)^n 双阶乘型 (2n-1)!!；且 m_n(q) 的根全部落在单位圆 |q|=1 上，与 q-Gauss 收缩半群的谱一致性对应。
