# 00000008484

**English.** Definition: The Lagrange duality of ergodic optimization: the gap of the pressure duality in measure optimization with mean constraints. Conjecture: Strong duality holds under bounded observations, so the gap is zero (a strong duality law); examples where the gap is positive exist for infinite-dimensional observations (a gap existence law); the supremum of gap values is the explicit constant 1/2 (a half-gap law); and the regularization constant eliminating the gap by entropy regularization is the temperature parameter (a temperature elimination law). (pressure duality half-gap temperature)

**中文。** 定义：遍历优化的 Lagrange 对偶：带均值约束的测度优化问题中压力对偶的间隙。猜想：有界观测下强对偶成立即间隙为零（强对偶律）；无限维观测的间隙可达正值的例存在（间隙存在律）；间隙值的上确界为 1/2 的显式常数（半间隙律）；熵正则化消除间隙的正则化常数为温度参数（温度消除律）。
