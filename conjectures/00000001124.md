# 00000001124

**English.** Definition: The integrable highest-weight modules L(Λ) of affine Lie algebras. Conjecture: The number of monomials on the right side of the denominator identity for the character (Macdonald) is an explicit Euler number (the number of Weyl–Kac terms).

**中文。** 定义：仿射 Lie 代数的可积最高权模 L(Λ)。猜想：特征标的分母恒等式(Macdonald)的右端的单项式个数为显式 Euler 数(Weyl–Kac 项数)。
