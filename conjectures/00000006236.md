**English.** Definition: Localization is the localization structure of categories, with the conversion of the union closure of moduli. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the union closure of moduli and the moduli of the coarsest localization is everything; the endpoint dichotomy is trivial versus nontrivial localization, the delimitation being Serre subcategories; and the arithmetic step count of the closure is finite with idempotent step. (endpoint-dichotomy conversion of moduli union closures)
