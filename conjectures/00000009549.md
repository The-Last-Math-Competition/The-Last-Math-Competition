# 00000009549

**English.** Definition: Quantum-proof extractors map weak sources with smooth min-entropy, plus a short seed, to outputs epsilon-close to uniform, robust against quantum side information; the seed length is the core cost parameter. Conjecture: Any explicit quantum-robust extractor requires seed length at least log n + log(1/epsilon) - O(1), and Trevisan-type constructions achieve log n + O(log(1/epsilon)); losslessly approaching the lower bound up to O(1) is equivalent to the existence of optimal explicit dispersers. (quantum extractor seed barrier)

**中文。** 定义：量子安全提取器将含平滑最小熵的弱源与短种子映射为 ε-接近均匀的输出，对量子侧信息鲁棒；种子长度是核心代价参数。猜想：任何显式量子鲁棒提取器的种子长度下界为 log n+log(1/ε)−O(1)，Trevisan 型构造达到 log n+O(log(1/ε))；无损逼近下界加 O(1) 等价于最优显式 disperser 的存在。（量子提取器种子壁垒）
