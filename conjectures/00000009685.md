**English.** Definition: Padé–Hankel determinant growth: Nesterenko of algebra expression power series gives growth order upper bound. Conjecture: non- zero point of k order Hankel determinant of algebra power series is (k/ρ)(1+o(1)), ρ is o(1) term of its rational approximation exponent; Nesterenko bound is explicit polynomial reciprocal and not. (Hankel growth compact -ization)

**中文。** 定义：代数形式幂级数的 Padé–Hankel 行列式增长：Nesterenko 给出增长阶上界。猜想：代数幂级数的 k 阶 Hankel 行列式的非零起点为 (k/ρ)(1+o(1))，ρ 为其有理逼近指数；Nesterenko 界的 o(1) 项可改进为显式多项式倒数且不可再改进。（Hankel 增长紧化）
