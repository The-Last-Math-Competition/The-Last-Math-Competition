**TAG-15 ★** — source: 308_算术超越.md

**English.** Definition: Growth of Pade-Hankel determinants of algebraic formal power series: Nesterenko gave upper bounds on the growth order. Conjecture: The nonzero onset of k-th order Hankel determinants of an algebraic power series is (k/rho)(1+o(1)), with rho its rational approximation exponent; and the o(1) term of the Nesterenko bound can be improved to an explicit reciprocal polynomial, not further improvable. (Hankel growth tightening)

**中文。** 定义：代数形式幂级数的 Padé–Hankel 行列式增长：Nesterenko 给出增长阶上界。猜想：代数幂级数的 k 阶 Hankel 行列式的非零起点为 (k/ρ)(1+o(1))，ρ 为其有理逼近指数；Nesterenko 界的 o(1) 项可改进为显式多项式倒数且不可再改进。（Hankel 增长紧化）
