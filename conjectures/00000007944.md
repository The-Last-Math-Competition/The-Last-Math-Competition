# 00000007944

**English.** Definition: The algebraic entropy of Hirota bilinear residues: the degree growth exponent kappa_b of initial-value polynomials of solutions of bilinear equations. Conjecture: kappa_b = log lambda with lambda the spectral radius of the recursion matrix, and lambda is a Pisot number when the system is integrable; in the nonintegrable (chaotic) case the logarithm of lambda is of positive Liouville type; and the set of residue polynomials (the vacua of Sato theory in Hirota form) forms a semigroup in the integrable case, whose generating function is the p = 2 local factor of the zeta function of the Katz veil polynomial. (Hirota entropy Pisot dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：Hirota 双线性余项（residue）的代数熵：双线性方程的解的初值多项式的次数增长指数 κ_b（algebraic entropy）。猜想：κ_b=log λ（λ 为递推矩阵的谱半径）且 λ 为 Pisot 数当系统可积；非可积（混沌）时 λ 的对数为正的 Liouville 型；且余项式（Hirota 的 Sato 理论的 vacua）的集合在可积情形构成半群，半群的生成函数为 Katz 面纱多项式 zeta 函数的 p=2 局部因子。
