# 00000007935

**INT-05 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The conservation spectrum of the box-ball system (BBS), a reversible cellular automaton discretizing KdV solitons (a soliton with load k moves at speed k). Conjecture: The ratio of the explicit interaction phase shift formula of N-soliton solutions of BBS to the phase difference of continuous KdV solitons converges to 1 as the lattice spacing tends to 0; and the support patterns of the tau function of the Ultradiscrete limit of BBS correspond one-to-one to realized paths of the crystal base, with the generating function of path counts equal to 1 over the product of (1-q^k)^{p(k)} (p(k) the partition function). (box-ball crystal path generating function)

**中文。** 定义：box-ball 系统（BBS）的可逆元胞自动机的守恒谱：KdV 方程的孤子解的离散化（车辆数为 k 的孤子速度为 k）。猜想：BBS 的 N-孤子解的相互作用相位移动的显式公式与连续 KdV 孤子的相位差的比值在格距 →0 时收敛到 1；且 BBS 的 Ultradiscrete 极限的 τ 函数的支撑模式与晶体基（crystal base）的 realised paths 一一对应，路径计数的生成函数为 1/Π(1-q^k)^{p(k)}（p(k) 为分拆数）。
