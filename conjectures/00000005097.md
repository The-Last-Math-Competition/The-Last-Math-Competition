**English.** Definition: Hitchin fiber denotes map of lattice module spectrum. Conjecture: fiber constant of rank two is discriminant exponent, irreducible spectrum when exponent of spectrum characteristic polynomial is one and dimension. (Hitchin fiber discriminant exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Hitchin 纤维指希格斯模到谱底的映射。猜想：秩二的纤维换算常数为谱特征多项式的判别式指数,不可约谱时指数为一且换算保维数。（Hitchin 纤维判别式指数换算）
