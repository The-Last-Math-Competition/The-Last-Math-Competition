**GL3-17 ◆** — source: 155_几何Langlands的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The Hitchin fibration is the map of the Higgs moduli to the spectral base. Conjecture: The fiber conversion constant of rank two is the discriminant exponent of the spectral characteristic polynomial, the exponent is one for irreducible spectra, and the conversion preserves dimension. (discriminant-exponent conversion of Hitchin fibers)

**中文。** 定义：Hitchin 纤维指希格斯模到谱底的映射。猜想：秩二的纤维换算常数为谱特征多项式的判别式指数,不可约谱时指数为一且换算保维数。（Hitchin 纤维判别式指数换算）
