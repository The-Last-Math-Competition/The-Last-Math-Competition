# 00000008876

**CGB-16 ★** — source: 281_组合博弈论II.md

**English.** Definition: The hyperstar: a star-shaped composite game of several all-small branches. Conjecture: The value of a hyperstar has an explicit synthesis formula (a synthesis law); the temperature of the synthesis is dominated by the maximum branch temperature (a domination law); the exception to domination gives secondary temperatures (a secondary temperature law); and the secondary gap is at most 1/4 (a quarter-difference law). (hyperstar synthesis domination quarter gap)

**中文。** 定义：超星（hyperstar）：由多个微小分支组成的星型组合博弈。猜想：超星的（值的显式合成公式）的（合成律）；且合成的温度（分支温度的最大支配）的（支配律）；支配的例外（非支配组合的次温度）的（次温度律）；次的量化（差距 ≤1/4）的（四分之差律）。
