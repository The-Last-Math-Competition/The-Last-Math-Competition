**English.** Definition: Corner measures and Buffon projection counting. Conjecture: The generating function of the projection counts is the determinant of the Fourier coefficients of the corner measure; the rank deficit of the determinant is exactly the rectifiable dimension of the set, and the deficit's combinatorial interpretation is a maximal family of independent directions. (corner measure Buffon algebra)

**中文。** 定义：角测度（corner measure）与 Buffon 投影计数。猜想：投影计数的生成函数为角测度的 Fourier 系数的行列式；行列式的秩亏恰为集的可校正维数且亏量的组合解释为独立方向的极大族。（角测度 Buffon 代数）
