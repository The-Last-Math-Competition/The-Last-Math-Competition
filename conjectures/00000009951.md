**English.** Definition: Furstenberg boundaries and stationary measures: the central role of boundary measure classification. Conjecture: The entropy criterion for absolute continuity of boundary stationary measures: a measure is absolutely continuous if and only if the Furstenberg entropy equals the drift entropy; the criterion is algebraic for higher-rank groups and corrected by boundary fractal dimension for rank-one groups. (stationary measure entropy criterion)
