# 00000005307

**English.** Definition: Plethysm is the composition operation of symmetric functions. Conjecture: The first-row formula table at low degree (at most 5) is given by counting closed forms of partition pairs with interval-combinatorial coefficients; the first-row coefficients are always nonnegative; and at degree 6 saturation appears in the first-row coefficients, with saturation modulus 2. (interval counting closed form of plethysm first rows)

**中文。** 定义：合成运算：对称函数的复合。猜想：首行公式表在低次（不超过 5）由分拆配对的计数闭式给出且系数为区间组合数；首行系数恒非负；次数 6 的首行系数出现饱和且饱和模为 2。（合成首行区间计数闭式）
