# 00000008104

**FUS-24 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The LGF index of hyperfocal subgroups of fusion systems: the relaxation LGF(F) of hyperfocal restricted fusion systems (Libman's restriction index). Conjecture: LGF(F) has an exponential-type upper bound p^{rank} in the prime factor count of |S:R| (an LGF bound); the fusions attaining the bound are hyperfocal-free (a total-freeness rigidity); and the error term of the comparison between free fusion and the fusion of Borels of Lie type groups is the logarithm of the order of the Weyl group (a Borel error logarithm law). (hyperfocal Libman bound Borel error law)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 hyperfocal 子群的 LGF 指数：hyperfocal 限制融合系统的放宽（Libman 的限制指数）LGF(F)。猜想：LGF(F) = |S:R| 的素因子数的指数型上界 p^{rank}（LGF 上界）；且达到上界的融合为 hyperfocal-free（全自由刚性）；自由融合与 Lie 型群的 Borel 的融合的比对的误差项为 Weyl 群的阶的对数（Borel 误差对数律）。
