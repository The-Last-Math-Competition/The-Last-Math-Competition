# 00000009796

**English.** Definition: Disagreement of discrete mean curvature definitions: compatibility criteria between the cotan-vector definition and the vertex-normal projection definition. Conjecture: The two discrete mean curvatures are compatible if and only if the mesh satisfies the discrete Delaunay condition; at failure points the deviation is explicitly given by the edge-flip energy of poor edges, and flipping converges. (mean curvature compatibility criterion)

**中文。** 定义：离散平均曲率的定义分歧：cotan 向量定义与顶点法向投影定义的相容判据。猜想：两种离散平均曲率相容当且仅当网格满足离散 Delaunay 条件；条件失效处的偏差由劣质边的对角交换能量显式给出且交换收敛。（平均曲率相容判据）
