# 00000008508

**SDG-08 ★** — source: 269_微分代数与微分Galois.md

**English.** Definition: Maillet's theorem concerns the summability of divergent series of formal solutions of differential equations. Conjecture: The Gevrey order s of a formal solution is given by the maximal slope of the Newton polygon of the equation; and k-summability holds if and only if the slope spectrum is k-divisible. (Maillet summability criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Maillet 定理指微分方程的形式解的发散级数的可求和性。猜想：形式解的 Gevrey 序 s 由方程的 Newton 多边形的最大斜率给出；且可 k-求和当且仅当斜率谱的 k 可分性。（Maillet 可求和判据）
