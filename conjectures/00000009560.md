# 00000009560

**English.** Definition: Rational polygonal billiards correspond to translation cover surfaces; counting and existence of periodic orbits are controlled by the SV theory of the corresponding stratum. Conjecture: Every rational polygon has infinitely many periodic orbits, with counting N(T) ~ c T^2 where c is a rational multiple of the SV constant of the stratum containing the polygon; and for degenerate-angle polygons with more than a hundred angles, c has the explicit lower bound 1/(pi times area). (polygonal periodic universality)

**中文。** 定义：有理多边形台球对应平移覆盖曲面；周期轨道的计数与存在性由对应 stratum 的 SV 理论控制。猜想：每个有理多边形都有无穷多条周期轨道，且计数 N(T)~c·T² 的常数 c 为该多边形所属 stratum 的 SV 常数的有理倍数；百角以上的退化角型多边形的 c 有显式下界 1/(π·面积)。（多边形周期普适性）
