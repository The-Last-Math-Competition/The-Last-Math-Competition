# 00000009912

**English.** Definition: Expected persistence diagrams: the limit structure of empirical persistence measures of random point processes. Conjecture: The expected persistence measure of a Poisson point process converges to an absolutely continuous limit with density an explicit kernel on the birth-death plane; the support of the kernel is bounded above by twice the diameter, and the density diverges logarithmically at birth equals death. (expected persistence limit kernel)

**中文。** 定义：期望持久图：随机点过程的经验持久测度的极限结构。猜想：泊松点过程的期望持久测度收敛到绝对连续极限且密度为出生-死亡平面上的显式核；核的支集上界为直径的两倍且密度在出生等于死亡处对数发散。（期望持久极限核）
