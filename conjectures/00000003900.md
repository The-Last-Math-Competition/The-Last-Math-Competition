# 00000003900

**CA3-20 △** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: The prism structure count of a fixed ring is the number of pairwise non-isomorphic prism pairs over it. Conjecture: There exist perfect F_p-algebra families with arbitrarily large prism structure counts; yet the prism structure count over a perfect closed field is always 1. (prism structure count extremes)

**中文。** 定义：固定环的棱镜结构数指其上两两不同构的棱镜对个数。猜想：存在完美 F_p-代数族使棱镜结构数任意大;而完美闭域上的棱镜结构数恒为 1。（棱镜结构数的极端）
