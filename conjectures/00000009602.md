# 00000009602

**English.** Definition: The correspondence between inflection points of the isoperimetric spectrum of closed manifolds and stable minimal surfaces: isoperimetric profiles are realized by boundaries of isoperimetric regions. Conjecture: The area of the first inflection of the isoperimetric profile is exactly twice the area of the smallest stable minimal surface; and the isoperimetric-minimal constant (profile slope over minimal area) depends only on dimension and is an explicit rational number. (isoperimetric-minimal inflection)

**中文。** 定义：闭流形等周谱的拐点与稳定极小曲面的对应：等周轮廓线由等周问题的区域边界实现。猜想：等周轮廓的第一拐点面积恰为最小稳定极小曲面面积的两倍；等周-极小常数（轮廓斜率与极小面积之比）只依赖维数且为显式有理数。（等周-极小拐点）
