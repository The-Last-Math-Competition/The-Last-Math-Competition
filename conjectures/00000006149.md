**DAG2-19 ★** — source: 190_导出代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The derived scheme-theoretic image is the derived lifting structure of the image. Conjecture: The shortest length of projective resolutions is always finite, the shortest being the minimization of generator length; the minimal length equals the width of the cotangent spectrum; and the length is 1 in the smooth case, with increments controlled by the singularity order. (shortest generator length of projective images)

**中文。** 定义：导出闭图像：像的导出提升结构。猜想：投射分解的最短长度恒有限且最短的组合为生成元长度的极小化；极小长度等于余切谱的宽度；长度在光滑情形为 1 且增量由奇点阶控制。（图像投射最短生成长）
