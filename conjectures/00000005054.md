**English.** Definition: Quantum diffusion is the space-time propagation scaling of the wave packet. Conjecture: In dimension two the feasible domain of diffusion exponents is an explicit triangular domain, the family of moment exponents satisfies the rigid system of inequalities, and the domain boundary is attained by renormalized self-consistency. (triangular domain of two-dimensional quantum diffusion exponents)
