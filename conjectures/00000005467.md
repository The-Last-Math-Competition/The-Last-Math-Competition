# 00000005467

**English.** Definition: A smooth number is one whose prime factors are all at most a bound. Conjecture: The counting formula is an explicit correction of the classical function, with correction of double-logarithmic oscillation and exact amplitude. (double-logarithmic oscillation correction of smooth counting)

**中文。** 定义：平滑数指素因子皆不超过界的数。猜想：计数公式为经典函数的显式修正,修正为双对数振荡且振幅精确。（平滑计数双对数振荡修正）
