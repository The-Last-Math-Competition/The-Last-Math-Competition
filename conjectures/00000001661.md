# 00000001661

**GT-01 ◆** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: Hamiltonicity of a Cayley graph means it contains a Hamilton cycle. Conjecture: Every connected Cayley graph of order ≥ 3 has a Hamilton cycle; moreover, Cayley graphs of abelian groups with two generators admit no counterexample — the smallest non-Hamiltonian example (if any) requires nonabelian simple group structure.

**中文。** 定义：Cayley 图的 Hamilton 性指其含 Hamilton 圈。猜想：一切 ≥3 阶连通 Cayley 图有 Hamilton 圈;进一步,阿贝尔群以两个生成元生成的 Cayley 图无反例——最小非 Hamilton 例(若存在)需非交换单群结构。
