# 00000005440

**English.** Definition: Moments and boundary rigidity are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two domains with identical measure moments but different boundary rigidity, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of conformal images with the same moments but different rigidity. (same moments-different rigidity conformal separation)

**中文。** 定义：矩与边界刚性指两层。猜想：存在两域的一切测度矩相同而边界刚性不同,分离由同矩异刚性的显式共形像对实现。（矩同-边界刚性异共形分离）
