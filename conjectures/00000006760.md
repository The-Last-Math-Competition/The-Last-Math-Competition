# 00000006760

**English.** Definition: Derived categories and extension lattices are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two algebras with equivalent derived categories but different extension lattices, and the separation is realized by an explicit covering pair with the same derived category but different bases. (same derived-different covering separation)

**中文。** 定义：导出范畴与推广格指两层。猜想：存在两代数的导出范畴等价而推广格不同,分离由同导出异基的显式覆叠对实现。（导等同-推广格异覆叠分离）
