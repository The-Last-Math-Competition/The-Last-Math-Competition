**English.** Definition: Umbral moonshine refers to the mock modular forms of Niemeier root systems. Conjecture: The Lambert-series coefficients of umbral groups are always completely multiplicative at low weights; and multiplicativity is controlled by the splitting field of the coefficient field. (umbral multiplicativity at low weight)

**中文。** 定义：umbral 月光指 Niemeier 根系的 mock 模形式。猜想：umbral 群的 Lambert 级数系数恒在低权处乘性完全；且乘性由系数域的分裂域控制。（umbral 乘性低权）
