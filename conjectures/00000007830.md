# 00000007830

**English.** Definition: The semigroup spectrum of Weierstrass half-points on Brill-Noether general curves: the composite gap semigroups H of a general g^r_d. Conjecture: Under genericity the semigroup spectrum is determined exactly by the arithmetic genus and the rolling number rho; every non-semigroup gap pattern is realized: a numerical semigroup H with |H| = nu(g,r,d) is realized at some point of a general curve if and only if the genus of H equals g; and the weighted count of complementary semigroups is an explicit polynomial given by Young lattice paths. (numerical semigroup realization spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Brill–Noether 一般型曲线的 Weierstrass 半点的半群谱：一般曲线的 g^r_d 的复合隙半群 H(gap 集)。猜想：一般型条件下曲线的半群谱恰好由算术亏格与滚动数 (ρ) 决定，其中非半群隙完全实现：任何满足 |H|=ν(g,r,d) 的数值半群 H 在一般曲线某点实现当且仅当 H 的亏格等于 g；且回补半群的权重计数为用 Young 格路径给出的显式多项式。
