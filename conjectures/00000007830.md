**English.** Definition: composition group H(gap set) of g^r_d of group spectrum: general curve of Weierstrass point of Brill–Noether general type curve. Conjecture: group spectrum exactly of general under curve by arithmetic genus and number (ρ), where non- group completely realized: satisfies numerical group H in general curve some point realized of |H|=ν(g,r,d) if and only if genus of H equals g; and weight counting of complement group is explicit polynomial of using Young lattice path gives.
