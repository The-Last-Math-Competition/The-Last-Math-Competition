# 00000002396

**English.** Definition: A Furstenberg measure is a ×p-invariant Radon measure. Conjecture: The realizable set of support dimensions of measures that are simultaneously ×2-invariant and ×3-quasi-invariant is {0} ∪ [c, 1] (c an explicit positive constant, controlled by the Rudolph–Johnson entropy bound).

**中文。** 定义：Furstenberg 测度指 ×p 不变的 Radon 测度。猜想：同时 ×2-不变与 ×3-半不变的测度的支撑维数的可实现集 = {0}\cup[c,1](c 为显式正常数,由 Rudolph–Johnson 熵界控制)。
