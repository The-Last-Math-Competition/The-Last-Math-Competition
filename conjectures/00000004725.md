**English.** Definition: classification of lattice fibration of twist projective bundle: Brauer class α under index two twist. Conjecture: classification by explicit bijection of data (α,c₁,c₂) gives and consistency of bijection is finite; α=0 when degenerate is classical projective bundle classification and lattice of fiber discriminant uniform; twist Chern class is 2 times lattice of index and lattice exponent is exactly 2. (twist projective bundle classification bijection)

**中文。** 定义：扭曲射影丛：Brauer 类 α 下指标二扭转的塞格雷型纤维化的分类。猜想：分类由数据 (α,c₁,c₂) 的显式双射给出且双射的相容性为有限个判别条件；α=0 时退化为经典射影丛分类且纤维判别式一致；扭曲陈类的取值格为指标的 2 倍整格且格指数恰为 2。（扭曲射影丛分类双射）
