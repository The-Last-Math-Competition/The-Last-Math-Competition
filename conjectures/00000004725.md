# 00000004725

**English.** Definition: Twisted projective bundles are the classification of Segre-type fibrations of index-two twists under a Brauer class alpha. Conjecture: The classification is given by an explicit bijection of the data (alpha, c_1, c_2), with the compatibility of the bijection being finitely many discriminant conditions; at alpha = 0 it degenerates to the classical projective bundle classification with consistent fiber discriminants; the value lattice of the twisted Chern classes is the doubled integer lattice of the index, with lattice index exactly 2. (classification bijection of twisted projective bundles)

**中文。** 定义：扭曲射影丛：Brauer 类 α 下指标二扭转的塞格雷型纤维化的分类。猜想：分类由数据 (α,c₁,c₂) 的显式双射给出且双射的相容性为有限个判别条件；α=0 时退化为经典射影丛分类且纤维判别式一致；扭曲陈类的取值格为指标的 2 倍整格且格指数恰为 2。（扭曲射影丛分类双射）
