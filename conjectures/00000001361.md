# 00000001361

**DS-01 ◆** — source: 31_描述集合论与递归论.md

**English.** Definition: The cost C(G) of a countable group G is a Borel cardinal invariant of its generating graphs; the fixed price problem asks whether all generating graphs have the same cost. Conjecture: Hyperbolic groups have fixed price; in particular, every generating graph of the free group F_n (n ≥ 2) has cost n−1.

**中文。** 定义：可数群 G 的代价 C(G) 指其生成图的 Borel 基数不变量;固定价格问题问一切生成图的代价是否相等。猜想：双曲群具有固定价格;特别地,自由群 F_n(n≥2) 的任何生成图代价均为 n−1。
