**English.** Definition: The degree structure of harmonic coverings of graphs. Conjecture: The degree set equals the lattice of Euler-characteristic factors; and the remainder of the lattice is given by non-harmonic exceptions. (graph harmonic degree-Euler factor lattice)

**中文。** 定义：图的调和覆盖度数结构。猜想：度数集合等于 Euler 示性数因子的格；且格的余为由非调和例外。（图调和度数-Euler 因子格）
