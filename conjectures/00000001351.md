# 00000001351

**English.** Definition: The Chebyshev bias is the sign statistics of θ(x) − x. Conjecture: Under RH, the exact order of θ(x) − x is √x times logloglogx in Ω± oscillation; and the cumulative count of sign exchanges obeys a doubly-logarithmic law. (Littlewood strengthened exchange count)

**中文。** 定义：切比雪夫偏置指 θ(x)−x 的符号统计。猜想：RH 下 θ(x)−x 的精确阶为根号 x 乘 logloglogx 的 Ω± 振荡；且正负偏差的累计交换次数为对数双指数律。（Littlewood 加强交换计数）
