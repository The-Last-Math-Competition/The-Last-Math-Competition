# 00000009730

**English.** Definition: Quantitative scattering time: the time scale of scattering versus energy gaps. Conjecture: The minimal time from the initial moment until the solution enters an epsilon-neighborhood of scattering is polynomial in the energy gap, exp(C over theta to the alpha); the exponent alpha is explicitly given by the critical exponent and dimension, and small-sphere initial data attain that order. (scattering time gap law)

**中文。** 定义：定量散射时间：散射发生的时间尺度与能量间隙的关系。猜想：从初始时刻到解进入散射 ε 邻域的最小时间为能量间隙的多项式 exp(C/θ^{α})；指数 α 由临界指数与维数显式给出且极小球面初值达到该阶。（散射时间间隙律）
