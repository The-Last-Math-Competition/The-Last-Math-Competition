# 00000001255

**CG-55 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: A random perturbation of a cellular automaton on a finite ring is a Markov chain driven by Glauber-type noise. Conjecture: The support of the stationary measure is exactly a simplex of the graph; the extreme points of the simplex are deterministic fixed points, and the dimension is the minimal attractor dimension. (CA stationary support simplex)

**中文。** 定义：有限环上元胞自动机的随机扰动为 Glauber 型噪声驱动的马尔可夫链。猜想：平稳测度的支撑恰为图的单形；单形的极点为确定性不动点且维数为极小吸引子维数。（CA 平稳支撑单形）
