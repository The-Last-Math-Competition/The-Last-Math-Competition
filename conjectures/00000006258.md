**EG3-08 ★** — source: 194_遍历论III_熵公式.md

**English.** Definition: The entropy structure is the stratified structure of the entropy functional of Levine-Ledrappier type. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit descent of the corrected rank and the rank of failure points differs by one; the arithmetic progression of the descent is finite with common difference 1; and the minimal rank is zero, the zero set dense. (difference-one descent conversion of corrected rank)

**中文。** 定义：熵结构：熵泛函的层化结构（Levine–Ledrappier 型）。猜想：换算常数为修正秩的显式下降且失效点的秩差一；下降的算术级数为有限且级数的公差为 1；秩的极小值为零且零点集为稠密。（修正秩下降差一换算常数）
