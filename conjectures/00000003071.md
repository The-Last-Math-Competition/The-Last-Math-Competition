**English.** Definition: Quasi-categories: simplicial sets satisfying inner horn filling. Conjecture: Model completeness of quasi-categories: the Quillen equivalence of the Joyal and Bergner model structures translates as an adjoint pair of simplicial nerves, with the unit of the adjunction a weak equivalence of simplicial categories. (Joyal-Bergner translation equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：拟范畴（quasi-category）：满足内角填充的单纯集。猜想：拟范畴的模型完备性：Joyal 模型结构与 Bergner 结构的 Quillen 等价的翻译为单纯神经的伴随对且伴随的单位为单纯范畴的单位弱等价。（Joyal-Bergner 翻译等价）
