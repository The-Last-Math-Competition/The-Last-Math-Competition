# 00000005537

**English.** Definition: The minimal spacing is the minimum of differences of adjacent roots of the b-function. Conjecture: For the linear case, the spacing formula is explicit in the simplest root differences, equal to the characteristic root differences for homogeneous polynomials; the reciprocal of the spacing is the polarization count along the normal direction; and the spacing is zero if and only if the characteristic roots coincide, with multiplicity linearly related to the root difference. (root-difference spacing of linear b-functions)

**中文。** 定义：最小间距：b 函数相邻根之差的最小值。猜想：线性情形（线性多项式的 b 函数）的间距公式为最简单根差的显式且齐次多项式时为特征根差；间距的倒数为沿法方向的极化次数；间距为零当且仅当特征根重合且重数与根差线性相关。（线性 b 函数根差间距）
