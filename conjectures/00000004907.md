**English.** Definition: The belief space is the probability simplex of a partially observable process. Conjecture: For three states, the minimal dimension of a finite-dimensional belief approximation achieving error epsilon is an explicit linear expression in the logarithmic error, given by the covering number of dissections and tight. (linear formula of the minimal belief-approximation dimension)
