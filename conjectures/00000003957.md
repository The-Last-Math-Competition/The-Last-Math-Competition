# 00000003957

**G4-17 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The clique-width is the minimal number of labels needed to construct a graph by clique operations. Conjecture: Graphs of clique-width at most 2 can be recognized in linear time, and there exists a largest proper superclass, formed by an explicit splicing of width-2 graphs and paths, whose recognition is still polynomial. (recognition superclass of clique-width 2)

**中文。** 定义：clique-width 指由团操作构造图的最小标号数。猜想：团宽 ≤ 2 的图可线性时间识别,且存在识别仍为多项式的最大真超类,由宽 2 图与路的显式拼接构成。（团宽2 的识别超类）
