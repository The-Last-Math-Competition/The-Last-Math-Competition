# 00000002808

**English.** Definition: The contraction principle: transfer of LDP under continuous maps. Conjecture: The equality domain of contraction: surjectivity of the rate image holds if and only if the minimal lifted rates at every point agree, and the boundary of the surjectivity domain is the critical-value layer of the map. (contraction surjectivity critical layer)

**中文。** 定义：压缩原理：LDP 在连续映射下的传递。猜想：压缩的等式域：速率像的满射性当且仅当映射的每点提升的最小速率一致且满射域的边界为映射的临界值层。（压缩满射临界层）
