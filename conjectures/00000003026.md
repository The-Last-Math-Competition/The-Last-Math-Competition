**QM-16 ★** — source: 86_多体量子数学.md

**English.** Definition: Symmetry-protected topological phases (SPT): topological order without symmetry breaking. Conjecture: The complete classification of SPT: one-dimensional SPT phases are classified by group cohomology H²(G,U(1)) and two-dimensional ones by H³; the edge degeneracy equals the classification invariant. (SPT group cohomology classification)

**中文。** 定义：对称保护拓扑相（SPT）：无对称破缺的拓扑序。猜想：SPT 的完全分类：一维 SPT 的分类为群上同调 H²(G,U(1)) 且二维为 H³；边缘简并度等于分类的不变量。（SPT 群上同调分类）
