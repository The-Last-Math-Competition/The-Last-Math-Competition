**English.** Definition: Free Poisson approximation bounds the Kolmogorov distance between Wishart-type matrices (1/n) G G^* and the free compound Poisson law. Conjecture: The optimal error bound is C(sqrt(k/m) + 1/sqrt(kn)) with the two terms from the dimension ratio and sample size; at matching lower bound the free Stein method needs and needs only matching of second-order free cumulants, and the constant C can be taken as 1/2. (two-term free Poisson approximation bound)

**中文。** 定义：自由 Poisson 逼近：Wishart 型矩阵 (1/n) G G^*（G 为 k×m 独立元）到 MP 律的自由复合 Poisson 逼近误差（Kolmogorov 距离）。猜想：误差的最优上界为 C·(sqrt(k/m)+1/sqrt(kn))，其中两项分别来自维度比与样本量；下界匹配时，逼近的 Stein 方法的自由版本需要且只需要二阶自由累积量的匹配，常数 C 可取 1/2。
