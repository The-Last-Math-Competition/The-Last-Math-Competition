**SG3-14 ★** — source: 141_交换代数IV_奇点理论.md

**English.** Definition: The resolution graph is the intersection graph of the exceptional curves. Conjecture: For rank at most ten the realizable graphs are the explicit tree families satisfying the negative-definiteness and weight criteria, non-tree graphs first appear at rank eleven, and the boundary is exact. (tree bound rank eleven of resolution graphs)

**中文。** 定义：分解图指例外曲线的相交图。猜想：秩不超过十的可实现图为满足负定与权判据的显式树族,非树图首次出现在秩十一,分界精确。（分解图树界秩十一）
