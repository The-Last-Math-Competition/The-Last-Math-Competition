# 00000008175

**English.** Definition: The success probability curve of ECM (the elliptic curve method) for prime factors: the curve sigma(p) of the optimal parameter B_1 for ECM to find the prime factor p. Conjecture: The optimality of the constant 1/2 in the optimal B_1 = exp(sqrt(log p log log p (1/2 + o(1)))) of sigma(p) (a half-constant ECM law); the success rate curve at fixed cost is a double flip of the Dickman rho function (a double Dickman law); and the optimal distribution of curve parameters (Suyama parameter sigma) is uniform (a Suyama uniformity law), the attack surface from deviations from uniformity (the security impact of suboptimal sigma) being negligible (a sigma security negligibility law). (ECM half-constant double Dickman Suyama uniformity)

**中文。** 定义：ECM（椭圆曲线法）的素因子的成功概率曲线：ECM 找到素因子 p 的最优参数 B₁ 的曲线 σ(p)。猜想：σ(p) 的最优 B₁ = exp(√(log p·log log p·(1/2+o(1)))) 的常数 1/2 的最优性（半常数 ECM 律）；且成功率（固定成本）的曲线为 Dickman ρ 的双重翻转（双重 Dickman 律）；曲线参数（Suyama 参数 σ）的最优分布为均匀（Suyama 均匀律），均匀的偏离的攻击面（次优 σ 的安全影响）为可忽略（σ 安全忽略律）。
