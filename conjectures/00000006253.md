# 00000006253

**English.** Definition: The entropy infimum is the entropy-infimum formula of ergodic decompositions, the upper bound of closed systems. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit value of the closed-system upper bound, equal in the Bernoulli case; the general deficit is the ergodic-decomposition deficit term, the deficit being the atomicity of the measure; and the lower bound of the deficit is zero, zero if and only if the system is entropy-continuous. (ergodic-deficit conversion of closed-system upper bounds)

**中文。** 定义：熵下确界：遍历分解的熵下确界公式（闭系统的上界）。猜想：换算常数为闭系统上界的显式值且伯努利情形相等；一般亏损为遍历分解亏项且亏项为测度的原子性；亏损的下界为零且零性当且仅当系统为熵连续。（闭系统上界遍历亏项换算）
