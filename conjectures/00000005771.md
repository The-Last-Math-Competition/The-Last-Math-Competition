# 00000005771

**English.** Definition: The information metric is the canonical Riemannian tensor of the statistical manifold, the Fisher metric, with the manifold volume. Conjecture: The volume of the Gaussian family is the explicit Gamma product of pi, a closed value in three dimensions; the volume growth is factorial in the dimension, each added dimension multiplying by sqrt(2 pi); and the volume element of the normal family is of hyperbolic type in variance-mean coordinates, with explicit type constant. (Gamma product of the Gaussian information volume)

**中文。** 定义：信息度规：统计流形的典范黎曼张量（Fisher 度规）；流形的体积。猜想：高斯族的体积为圆周率的显式 Gamma 积且三维时为闭式值；体积的增长为维数的阶乘型且每增一维乘因子 √(2π)；正态族的体积元在方差-均值坐标下为双曲型且型常数显式。（高斯信息体积 Gamma 积）
