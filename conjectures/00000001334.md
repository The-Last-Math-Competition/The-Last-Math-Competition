# 00000001334

**AN2-04 ★** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Definition: The Burgess character sum Σ_{n≤N} χ(n). Conjecture: For every ε > 0, the "optimal Burgess" bound Σ_{n≤N} χ(n) ≪ N^{1/2+ε}q^{ε} is attained only in a q^{1/4}-neighborhood, and the bound cannot be attained at q^{1/4−δ} (precise optimality of Burgess).

**中文。** 定义：Burgess 特征和 Σ_{n≤N} χ(n)。猜想：对每个 ε > 0,Σ_{n≤N} χ(n) ≪ N^{1/2+ε}q^{ε} 的"最优 Burgess"仅在 q^{1/4} 邻域取到,且界不可在 q^{1/4−δ} 处达到(Burgess 最优性精确)。
