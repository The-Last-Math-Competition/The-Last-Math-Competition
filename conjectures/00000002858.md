# 00000002858

**English.** Definition: The algebraic dynamics of the EM algorithm: polynomial maps of latent-variable iteration. Conjecture: The algebraic characterization of EM convergence rates: the rate is given by the spectrum of the Jacobian at the fixed point, with maximal element an explicit function of the proportion of missing data. (EM convergence spectral explicit law)

**中文。** 定义：EM 算法的代数动力学：隐变量迭代的多项式映射。猜想：EM 的收敛速率的代数刻画：速率由 Jacobi 矩阵在不动点的谱给出且谱的最大元为数据缺失比例的显式函数。（EM 收敛谱显式律）
