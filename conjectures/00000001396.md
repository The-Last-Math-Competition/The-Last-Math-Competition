# 00000001396

**HT-06 ★** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: A d_r differential formula for the Adams spectral sequence is an algebraic closed form uniformly expressing all r-th differentials. Conjecture (a formalization of Mahowald's uncertainty principle): No uniformly valid d_r formula can simultaneously predict all differentials in both the v₁-periodic and v₂-periodic parts; any closed form must be incomplete on one of the two parts.

**中文。** 猜想：定义:Adams 谱序列的 d_r 微分公式指统一表达一切 r 阶微分的代数闭式。猜想(Mahowald 不确定性原理的形式化):不存在一致有效的 d_r 公式同时预测 v₁-周期与 v₂-周期两部分的全部微分;任何闭式必在两部分之一上不完备。
