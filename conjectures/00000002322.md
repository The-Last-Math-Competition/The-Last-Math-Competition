# 00000002322

**NG-02 ★** — source: 63_数值代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: α-theory is Smale's Newton convergence criterion α(f,x) = β(f,x)γ(f,x). Conjecture: Beyond α ≤ (13−3√17)/4 ≈ 0.157, the ratio of the volume of the convergence domain of multipoint Newton corrections to its convex hull is (1−2α)^{n} (an explicit volume ratio); the ratio is exact in the spherical case.

**中文。** 定义：α-理论指 Smale 的 Newton 收敛判据 α(f,x)=β(f,x)γ(f,x)。猜想：α ≤(13-3√17)/4 ≈ 0.157 之外的多点 Newton 校正的收敛域的体积与凸包之比为 (1-2α)^{n}(显式体积比);比值在球面情形精确。
