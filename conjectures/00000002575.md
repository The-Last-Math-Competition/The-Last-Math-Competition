# 00000002575

**CM-15 ★** — source: 71_计算复杂性中的数学.md

**English.** Definition: A two-source extractor extracts uniform bits from two weak sources. Conjecture: The entropy threshold of explicit two-source extractors is logarithmic-squared; and the window of the threshold is double-logarithmic. (two-source extractor entropy threshold)

**中文。** 定义：两源 extractor 为从两弱源提取均匀比特。猜想：显式两源 extractor 的熵阈为对数平方；且阈的窗为双对数。（两源 extractor 熵阈）
