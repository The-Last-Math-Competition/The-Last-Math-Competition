# 00000001848

**RM-08 △** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral norm of a third-order Gaussian tensor is the contraction value of its best rank-1 approximation. Conjecture: The spectral norm divided by E tends to c₃ (with E = c·N); the constant c₃ has a closed form (from an explicit computation of the maximum of the Gaussian field on the third-order sphere), c₃ = 2·3^{-1/6}·Γ(1/3)^{-1} (numerically verifiable).

**中文。** 定义：3 阶高斯张量的谱范数指最优 rank-1 逼近的收缩值。猜想：谱范数/E → c₃(其中 E = c· N);常数 c₃ 有闭式(由三阶球面上的最大 Gauss 场的显式计算),c₃ = 2· 3^{-1/6}· Γ(1/3)^{-1}(近似数值可对照)。
