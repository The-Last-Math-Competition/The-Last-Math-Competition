# 00000007763

**English.** Definition: Non-Archimedean Fatou components are connected components of the Fatou domains of an iterated map on the Berkovich space of P^1. Conjecture: Every periodic Fatou component of a polynomial over a p-adic field is either attracting or a Siegel disk defined over an extension, and for quadratic-irrational rotation numbers the periodicity condition is controlled by the explicit radius of the p-adic linearization domain; no p-adic Herman ring exists, and its degeneration at Berkovich exceptional points is characterized by branching index at most 2. (p-adic Fatou component trichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：非阿基米德 Fatou 分量为 P^1 的 Berkovich 空间上迭代映射的 Fatou 域连通分量。猜想：p-adic 域上多项式的每个周期 Fatou 分量要么吸引、要么是定义在扩域上的 Siegel 盘，且 Siegel 盘旋转数为二次无理数时周期性的旋转数条件由 p-adic 线性化域的显式半径控制；特别地不存在 p-adic Herman 环（已知）与其在 Berkovich 例外点处的退化情形由分支指标 ≤2 刻画。
