# 00000008279

**SFL-19 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The equivalence of Doob-type stability and contraction in filtering: the constant rcl_c of exponential contraction in total variation of filters. Conjecture: The lower bound of rcl_c (the contraction constant) is the complement of the Dobrushin coefficient of the Markov kernel (a Dobrushin complement law); contraction agrees with forgetting (the rate of forgetting the initial distribution, a contraction-forgetting equivalence law); the minimal chain breaking the equivalence (contraction without forgetting) is a periodic chain (a periodic chain example); and periodic correction (relaxation) recovers a contraction constant of 1/2 (a half recovery law). (filter contraction Dobrushin forgetting periodic)

**中文。** 定义：滤波稳定性的 DO（Doob-type）与 RCL（收缩）的等价：滤波收缩（总变差的指数收缩）的常数 rcl_c。猜想：rcl_c（收缩常数）的下界为 Markov 核的 Dobrushin 系数的余（Dobrushin 余律）；且收缩与遗忘（初始分布的遗忘速率）的一致（收缩-遗忘等价律）；等价的破缺（收缩而不遗忘）的最小链为周期链（周期链例）；周期修正（松弛化）的收缩常数的恢复为 1/2（半恢复律）。
