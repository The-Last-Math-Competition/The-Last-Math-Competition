# 00000008607

**MLC-17 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: sketching denotes random low dimension projection recurrence. Conjecture: optimal sketch dimension is linear of n and risk diverges of constant 1; low in the dimension is (n/m)^{1/2}. (sketch dimension critical)

**中文。** 定义：sketching 指随机低维投影回归。猜想：最优 sketch 维度为 n 的线性且常数 1；低于该维的风险发散率为 (n/m)^{1/2}。（sketch 维度临界）
