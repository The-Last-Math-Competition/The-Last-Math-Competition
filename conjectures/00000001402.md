# 00000001402

**English.** Definition: The Levine–Tristram signature is the sign-jump function along the diagonal knot. Conjecture: The number of jump positions of the signature function is at most the factor-chain length of the algebraic knot; and the bound is attained at Hessenberg-type knots. (jump count factor-chain upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：Levine–Tristram 签名指沿对角纽结的符号跳跃函数。猜想：签名函数的跳跃位置个数不超过代数结的因子链长；且界在 Hessenberg 型纽结处达到。（跳跃计数因子链上界）
