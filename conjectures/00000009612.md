# 00000009612

**English.** Definition: The rationality of the Ruelle zeta function zeta_R(s) = product over periodic orbits of (1 - e^{-sT}) for Axiom A systems; Parry-Pollicott proved rationality for hyperbolic flows. Conjecture: All higher-order Ruelle zetas of Smale spaces (Lefschetz type with representation coefficients) are rational functions, with pole sets an explicit subfamily of the resonance spectrum and multiplicities given by index-weighted sums over periodic orbits. (higher-order Ruelle rationality)

**中文。** 定义：公理 A 系统的 Ruelle zeta 函数 ζ_R(s)=Π_周期轨道 (1−e^{−sT}) 的有理性；Parry–Pollicott 证明双曲流的有理性。猜想：一切 Smale 空间的高阶 Ruelle zeta（带表示系数的 Lefschetz 型）均为有理函数，极点集为共振谱的显式子族且重数由周期轨道的指标加权和给出。（高阶 Ruelle 有理性）
