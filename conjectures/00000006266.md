**English.** Definition: The special-point formula gives the explicit entropy structure of symbolic sequences. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit entropy formula of symbolic sequences and the breakpoint correspondence is monotone; the kernel of the formula is the Perron root of the transition matrix, unique; and the breakage of monotonicity is at coincident breakpoints, the genus of the breakage one. (monotone correspondence conversion of symbolic entropy)
