# 00000001149

**English.** Definition: The fusion coefficients N_{ij}^{k}(p,q) of the Virasoro minimal model M(p,q) are the dimensions of the three-point structure constants of the primary fields. Conjecture: N_{ij}^{k}(p,q) is given by a p-adically convergent series whose m-th term is a q-binomial coefficient of p-adic absolute value p^{-m/2}; the series yields nonnegative integers whenever p and q are coprime.

**中文。** 定义：Virasoro 极小模型 M(p,q) 的融合系数 N_{ij}^{k}(p,q) 指相应初级场的三点结构常数的维数。猜想：N_{ij}^{k}(p,q) 由如下 p-进收敛的级数给出:每项为 q-二项系数且第 m 项的 p-进绝对值为 p^{-m/2};该级数在 p,q 互素时给出非负整数。
