# 00000008294

**FLO-04 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The stability of barcodes of Floer homology: the Lipschitz constant bott_lip of the bottleneck distance of persistence barcodes under Hamiltonian perturbation. Conjecture: bott_lip (perturbation stability of barcodes) is at most the L-infinity norm of the perturbation (a Lipschitz stability law); the count of long bars (essential bars) equals the rank of Lagrangian Floer homology (a rank count law); the asymptotics of the distribution of bar lengths (the length spectrum of long bars) are the energy levels of a quadratic-form exponential (an energy level distribution law); and the rigidity of the minimal death-birth gap of bars is the normalized unit gap delta = 1 (a unit gap rigidity). (barcode bottleneck Lipschitz rank energy levels)

**中文。** 定义：Floer 同调的瓶颈（barcode）的稳定性：Persistence barcode 的 bottleneck 距离的 Hamilton 扰动的 Lipschitz 常数 bott_lip。猜想：bott_lip（barcode 的扰动稳定性）≤ 扰动的 L∞ 范数（Lipschitz 稳定律）；且 barcode 的长条（essential bars）的计数 = Lagrangian Floer 的 rank（rank 计数律）；条长的渐近（长条的长度谱）的分布为二次型指数的能级（能级分布律）；条的最短（bar 的死亡-出生的间隙）的极小值的刚性为 δ=1（单位间隙刚性）的正规化。
