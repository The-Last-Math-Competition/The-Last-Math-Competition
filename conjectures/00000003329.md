# 00000003329

**English.** Definition: Forests and forest ideals: the combinatorics of acyclic subgraphs. Conjecture: The Betti of forest ideals: the support of the Betti table of the generating forest ideal is the antichain of forest subsets, with the antichain count the Stirling numbers of the second kind. (forest Stirling antichain Betti support)
