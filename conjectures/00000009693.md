# 00000009693

**TAG-23 ★** — source: 308_算术超越.md

**English.** Definition: Transcendence of values of the formal group logarithm expansion of elliptic curves at algebraic points. Conjecture: The formal group logarithm of a non-CM elliptic curve takes transcendental values at nonzero algebraic points; the transcendence degree of the value is explicitly tied to the tower structure of stable descent of the curve, and the CM case degenerates to algebraicity. (formal group logarithm transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆曲线的形式群对数展开在代数点的值的超越性。猜想：非 CM 椭圆曲线的形式群对数在非零代数点的值超越；值的超越度与曲线的稳定下降的塔结构显式相关，CM 情形退化为代数。（形式群对数超越）
