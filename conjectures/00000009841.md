**WEX-21 ★** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: The limit structure of A_infinity: weight class limits as p tends to infinity. Conjecture: Among sharp equivalent characterizations of A_infinity, the optimal exponent is of exponential type; the limit constant converges to the Tauberian constant as the exponent tends to infinity, monotonically and boundedly. (A-infinity exponent limit)

**中文。** 定义：A_∞ 的极限结构：p→∞ 的权类极限。猜想：A_∞ 的 sharp 等价刻画族中指数最优者为 e 指数型；极限常数在指数趋无穷时收敛到 Tauberian 常数且收敛为单调有界。（A 无穷指数极限）
