# 00000005102

**English.** Definition: The Fargues-Scholze correspondence is the p-adic geometrized correspondence. Conjecture: Its conversion to the base stack is an explicit localization of the condensed spectrum, the conversion preserves semisimple correspondences, and the normalization constant is one. (condensed-spectrum localization conversion of FS)

**中文。** 定义：Fargues–Scholze 对应指 p 进几何化对应。猜想：其到底栈的换算为凝聚谱的局部化显式,换算保持半单对应且归一常数为一。（FS 凝聚谱局部化换算）
