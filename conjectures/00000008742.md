# 00000008742

**English.** Definition: Milnor K-groups: K^M_n(F) is the n-th tensor power of F^* modulo Steinberg relations. Conjecture: The kernel of the natural map from Milnor K to Quillen K is exactly the 2-torsion (the Bloch-Kato law, proven); the exact structure of the 2-torsion is controlled by Beilinson-Soule vanishing (a structure law); exactness of the Milnor K residue complex holds for smooth rings of dimension 1 (an exactness law); and there is exceptional behavior over semilocal rings (an exception law). (Milnor Bloch-Kato torsion residue exactness)

**中文。** 定义：Milnor K-群：K^M_n(F) 为 F^* 的 n 次张量积模 Steinberg 关系。猜想：Milnor K 到 Quillen K 的自然映射的核恰为 2-挠（Bloch–Kato 律，已证）；且 2-挠的精确结构由 Beilinson–Soule 消没控制（结构律）；Milnor K 的 residue 复形的正合性在维度 1 的光滑环成立（正合律）；半局部环上的例外行为（例外律）。
