# 00000003966

**G4-26 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The book thickness is the minimal number of pages needed to distribute the edges after the vertices are arranged along the spine. Conjecture: The smallest nonplanar graph requiring 3 pages is K_{3,3}; every 1-planar graph needs at most 4 pages, and this bound is optimal. (optimal page bound for 1-planar graphs)

**中文。** 定义：book thickness 挂书厚度指顶点沿书脊排列后边分页的最少页数。猜想：需要 3 页的最小非平面图为 K_{3,3};一切 1-平面图的页数不超过 4 且该界最优。（1-平面页数最优界）
