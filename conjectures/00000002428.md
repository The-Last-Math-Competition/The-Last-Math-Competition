# 00000002428

**English.** Definition: Counting unramified coverings of minimal genus. Conjecture: The asymptotics of the count is a Hurwitz-number correction; and the exponent of the correction is the volume of the modular group. (minimal genus Hurwitz counting asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：无分歧覆盖的最小亏格计数。猜想：计数的渐近为 Hurwitz 数修正；且修正的指数为模群体积。（最小亏格 Hurwitz 计数渐近）
