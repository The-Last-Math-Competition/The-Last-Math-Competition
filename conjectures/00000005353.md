# 00000005353

**English.** Definition: A thin triangle is the triangle condition with controlled edge distance. Conjecture: The count of minimal combinatorial triangulations of the order-three skeleton is explicit of Catalan type, decaying to one at the extremal constant, and the bound is tight. (Catalan counting bound of thin triangles)
