# 00000002473

**English.** Definition: The rank and crank of partitions: two statistics. Conjecture: The rank and crank distinguish exactly the residue classes in the Ramanujan congruences — the two statistics agree outside the exceptional classes; and the distinguishing admits a near-bijective refinement. (rank crank statistics congruence distinguishing quasibijection)
