# 00000003500

**English.** Definition: Fourier–Mukai transforms: kernel-induced functors. Conjecture: The equivalence realization of FM: the autoequivalence group of the elliptic curve is the semidirect product of SL₂(Z), with the kernel of the realization the pullback of the Poincare bundle. (FM elliptic SL2 semidirect product kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Fourier–Mukai 变换：核导出的函子。猜想：FM 的等价实现：椭圆曲线的自等价群为 SL₂(Z) 的半直积且实现的核为 Poincare 丛的拉回。（FM 椭圆 SL2 半直积核）
