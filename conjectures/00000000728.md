**PA-28 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: Greenberg's conjecture asserts λ = μ = 0 for the cyclotomic Z_p-tower of a real quadratic field. Conjecture: Among fundamental discriminants d < X, the density for which Greenberg's conjecture holds tends to 1 with error O(X^{−1/2+ε}); and the count of exceptional d with λ_p(Q(√d)) > 0 is O(X^{1/4+ε}).

**中文。** 定义：Greenberg 猜想断言实二次域的分圆 Z_p-塔满足 λ=μ=0。猜想：在 d<X 的基本判别式中,Greenberg 猜想成立的密度趋于 1,误差为 O(X^{-1/2+ε});且 λ_p(Q(√d))>0 的例外 d 的计数为 O(X^{1/4+ε})。
