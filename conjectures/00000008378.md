**English.** Definition: The tropical Matsusaka effectivity: the criterion eff_trop for effectivity of tropical divisors (effective representatives of linear equivalence classes). Conjecture: eff_trop (the complete criterion for existence of effective representatives) is the Baker-Norine rank computation (a B-N criterion law); the saturation stability of effectivity (saturation of the effective cone) has an effective cone whose extremal ray count is at most 2^g (a double-power ray law); the explicit representatives of rays are tearing divisors (a tearing representative law); and the normalized volume of the cone is the harmonic g! product of (1/k) (a harmonic volume law). (tropical effectivity Baker-Norine tearing harmonic)
