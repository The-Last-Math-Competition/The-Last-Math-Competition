# 00000003828

**RC-08 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: The support polynomial of a one-dimensional configuration denotes the monomial sum with exponents given by the heights of semilattice paths corresponding to path-crystal elements. Conjecture: Highest-weight crystals of type A_n are uniquely determined up to isomorphism by the set of support polynomials of all elements, and each support polynomial is M-convex (hence Lorentzian). (configuration support Lorentzian property)

**中文。** 定义：一维配置的支撑多项式指以路径晶体元素对应半格点路的高度为指数的单项式和。猜想：型 A_n 的最高权晶体同构类由全体元素的支撑多项式集合唯一决定,且每个支撑多项式为 M-凸(从而 Lorentzian)多项式。（配置支撑 Lorentzian 性）
