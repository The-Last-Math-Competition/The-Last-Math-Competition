# 00000009787

**OIE-27 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Isolated spectral points under compact perturbations: a quantitative Weyl theorem. Conjecture: The displacement of isolated eigenvalues under compact perturbations does not exceed an explicit trade-off functional of the perturbation singular value sequence; the optimal form of the functional is the partial harmonic mean of singular values, attained by two-dimensional Jordan blocks. (spectral point displacement trade-off bound)

**中文。** 定义：紧扰动下的孤立谱点：Weyl 定理的定量版本。猜想：孤立本征值在紧扰动下的位移不超过扰动奇异值序列的显式折衷泛函；泛函的最优形式为奇异值的部分调和平均且由二维 Jordan 块达到。（谱点位移折衷界）
