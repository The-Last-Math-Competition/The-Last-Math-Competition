# 00000003886

**CA3-06 ◆** — source: 115_交换代数III_正特征.md

**English.** Definition: The F-signature s(R) is the limiting proportion of rank-one free summands in Frobenius expansions. Conjecture: For an affine toric ring T, s(T) = Σ_v vol(vertex simplex of v)/(n!·mult(T)), the sum over cone vertices v, with mult the multiplicity. (toric F-signature volume formula)

**中文。** 定义：F-signature s(R) 指秩一自由子和占 Frobenius 扩张的比例极限。猜想：仿射 toric 环 T 的 s(T) = Σ_v vol(v 的顶点单纯形)/(n!·mult(T)),其中 v 取遍锥的顶点,mult 为重数。（toric F-signature 体积公式）
