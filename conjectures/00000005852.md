# 00000005852

**English.** Definition: The Brauer tree is the tree-like encoding of cyclic blocks. Conjecture: In defect-two blocks the exceptional-point multiplicity is two, the combination of two being the duality mechanism of the exceptional point; the trunk count is the inertia index; and the order of the automorphism group of the tree divides the order of the defect group, with unique planar embedding. (trunk multiplicity exceptional duality structure)

**中文。** 定义：Brauer 树：循环块的树状编码结构。猜想：亏二块时例外点重数为二且二的组合为例外点的对偶机制；树干数为惯性指数；树的自同构群的阶整除亏群的阶且树的平面嵌入唯一。（树干重数例外对偶结构）
