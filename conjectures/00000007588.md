**English.** Definition: The normalized entropy of persistent homology as the Shannon functional of bar lengths. Conjecture: The constant of the concentration inequality of persistence entropy is controlled by the bar count; and the concentration deviation is the law of the iterated logarithm. (concentrated persistence-entropy deviation law)
