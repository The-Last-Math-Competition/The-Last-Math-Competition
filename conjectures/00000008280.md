**English.** Definition: The Markovianization of path-dependent options: the minimal lifting dimension markov_lift of state augmentation (adding path functionals). Conjecture: markov_lift (minimal Markovian lifting) is linear in the truncation level m of the signature (an m-linear law); and the infimum of the lifting dimension (minimal dimension for exact Markovianization) is 2 for Asian options (a two-dimensional Asian law), 2 for Lookback (a two-dimensional Lookback law), and 2 for Barrier (a two-dimensional Barrier law) — a three-twos law; beyond these (higher-order path dependence, e.g. signature level three and above) the growth bound of the minimal dimension is the double exponential 2^m (a double-m exponential law). (Markovian lifting signature three-twos double exponential)

**中文。** 定义：路径依赖期权的马尔可夫化：状态扩容（加入路径泛函）的最小扩容维数 markov_lift。猜想：markov_lift（最小马尔可夫扩容）= 签名的截断层数 m 的线性（m 线性律）；且扩容的维数的下确界（精确马尔可夫化的最小维）为 Asian 期权的 2（二维 Asian 律）；Lookback（极值）的 2（二维 Lookback 律）；Barrier 的 2（二维 Barrier 律）的三 2 律；三律之外（高阶路径依赖，如签名的第三层以上）的最小维数的增长的界为 2^{m} 的指数（双 m 指数律）。
