**English.** Definition: A k-edge-transitive spherical tiling is a tiling of the sphere by finitely many polygons with k edge orbits. Conjecture: For each fixed k there are only finitely many topological types of k-edge-transitive spherical tilings; the count N(k) satisfies N(k) <= 2^{2^{O(k)}}, and N(k) is even for k>=3. (double-exponential count of edge-transitive spherical tilings)

**中文。** 定义：k-边可传递的球面铺砌指由 k 条边轨道的有限球面多边形铺砌。猜想：对每个固定 k，k-边可传递球面铺砌的拓扑型只有有限多个；其计数 N(k) 满足 N(k)≤2^{2^{O(k)}} 且当 k≥3 时 N(k) 为偶数。
