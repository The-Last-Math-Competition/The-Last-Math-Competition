# 00000000173

**CS-07 ◆** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: The prime zeta function P(s) = Σ_p p^{−s}. Conjecture: P(2), P(3), P(4), P(5) are linearly independent over Q, and the maximal provable length of such independence within the prime Euler-product framework is 4; the controlling kernel is the rank of the Möbius inversion matrix of odd zeta values. (linear independence of prime zeta values)

**中文。** 定义：素数 zeta 函数 P(s)=Σ_p p^{−s}。猜想：P(2),P(3),P(4),P(5) 在 Q 上线性无关且无关性的极大长度在素数 Euler 积框架内为 4；控制核为奇 ζ 值的 Möbius 反演矩阵的秩。（素数 zeta 线性无关）
