**EXC-29 ★** — source: 297_可交换性与高阶概率.md

**English.** Definition: Functional limits: functional exchangeability. Conjecture: Functional exchangeability: the exchange limits of functionals, with the kernel of the limit the Brownian bridge. (functional Brownian bridge exchange limit kernel)

**中文。** 定义：泛函极限：泛函可交换。猜想：泛函可交换：泛函的交换极限且限的核为布朗桥。（泛函可交换布朗桥泛函交换极限核极限）
