# 00000004694

**EC4-04 ★** — source: 142_遍历论的组合_熵与计数.md

**English.** Definition: The realizable entropy spectrum is the set of entropy values of a family of actions. Conjecture: The entropy spectrum is countable if and only if the number of layers of the zero-dimensional dilation tower of the action is finite, and the criterion is given explicitly by the length of the recovery sequences of the entropy structure. (tower-layer criterion of countable entropy spectra)

**中文。** 定义：可实现熵谱指作用族的熵值全体。猜想：熵谱可数当且仅当作用的零维扩张塔层数有限,判据由熵结构的恢复序列长度显式给出。（熵谱可数塔层判据）
