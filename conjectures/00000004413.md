# 00000004413

**GL2-23 ★** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: The cycle count of a bounded-degree graph limit is the measure of cycles of fixed length. Conjecture: The count of cycles of length l is bounded above by the explicit expression (d-1)^l divided by 2l, where d is the degree bound, and the bound is attained and optimal for d-regular tree graph limits. (regular-tree optimal upper bound of cycle counts)

**中文。** 定义：有界度图限的圈计数指固定长度圈的测度。猜想：长度 ℓ 圈计数上界为 (d−1) 的 ℓ 次幂除以 2ℓ 型显式式,d 为度上界,界由 d 正则树图限达到且最优。（圈计数正则树最优上界）
