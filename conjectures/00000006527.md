**English.** Definition: The superconnection with the series convergence of the Quillen superconnection. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit convergence window of the series and the window is given by the spectral gap; the endpoints close and the closed recursion is the integration by parts of supertraces; and the convergence rate is the square of the spectral gap. (spectral-gap window conversion of superconnection series)

**中文。** 定义：超联络：Quillen 超联络的级数收敛。猜想：换算常数为级数收敛窗的显式且窗口由谱隙给出；端点封闭且封闭的递推为超迹的分部积分；收敛的速率为谱隙的平方。（超级数收敛谱隙窗换算）
