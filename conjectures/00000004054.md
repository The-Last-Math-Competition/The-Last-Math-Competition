**English.** Definition: A stable tube is a tubular component of the stable AR-quiver, its radius being the width of the tube. Conjecture: The radius spectrum of the stable tubes of a self-injective algebra is exactly all integers from 1 to the defect delta(Lambda). (stable tube radius spectrum and defect)

**中文。** 定义：稳定管指稳定 AR-箭图的管状分量,半径为管的宽度。猜想：自注入代数的稳定管半径谱恰为从 1 到亏数 δ(Λ) 的全部整数。（稳定管半径谱-亏数）
