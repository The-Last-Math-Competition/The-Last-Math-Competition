**English.** Definition: The K-groups of Toeplitz algebras: invariants of boundary C*-algebras. Conjecture: The six-term cycle of Toeplitz K-groups: the connecting map of the exact sequence of K-groups is the index map, with kernel the K-group of compact operators; the rigidity of the cycle persists in the groupoid framework. (Toeplitz six-term cycle)

**中文。** 定义：Toeplitz 代数的 K-群：边界 C*-代数的不变量。猜想：Toeplitz K-群的六项循环：K 群的正合列的连接映射为指标映射且映射的核为紧算子的 K 群；循环的刚性在群胚框架保持。（Toeplitz 六项循环）
