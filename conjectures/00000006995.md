# 00000006995

**English.** Definition: Box norms with the multilinear box-counting structure. Conjecture: The equivalence spectrum is given by the system type; the generators of the spectrum are box stratifications; and the generators of the box stratifications are nested intervals. (system-type equivalence spectrum of box norms)

**中文。** 定义：箱范数为多线性箱计数结构。猜想：等价谱由系统型给出；且谱的生成元为箱分层；且箱分层的生成元为嵌套区间。（箱范数系统型等价谱）
