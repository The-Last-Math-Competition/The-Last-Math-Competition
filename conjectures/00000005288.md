# 00000005288

**FL2-28 ★** — source: 161_非线性偏微分II_流体II.md

**English.** Definition: The magnetic Reynolds number is the ratio of advection to magnetic diffusion. Conjecture: The threshold of chaotic magnetization is the explicit ratio of the Lyapunov exponent to magnetic diffusion, verified at hyperbolic flows, and the magnetization transition is sharp across the threshold. (Lyapunov-ratio magnetization threshold)

**中文。** 定义：磁雷诺数指导磁率与磁扩散之比。猜想：混沌磁化的阈值为拉式指数对磁扩散的显式比,双曲流处验证且阈值上下磁化相变锐利。（磁化阈值拉式指数比）
