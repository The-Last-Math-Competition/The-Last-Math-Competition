# 00000001720

**EG-30 △** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: The joint centralizer of a commutative action is the group of measure-preserving transformations commuting with all its elements. Conjecture: The joint centralizer of zero-entropy Z^d actions is an algebraic Lie group; if the action is given by twists of rigid algebraic structures, the centralizer is an explicit closure of that algebraic group.

**中文。** 定义：交换作用的联合中心化子指与其一切元素交换的测度保持变换群。猜想：零熵 ℤ^d 作用的联合中心化子为代数李群;若作用由刚性代数结构的扭曲给出,则中心化子为该代数群的显式闭包。
