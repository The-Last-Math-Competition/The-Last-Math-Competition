# 00000009799

**DDG-09 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Discrete Hodge decomposition: orthogonal decomposition in the language of discrete differential forms. Conjecture: Stability of discrete Hodge decomposition under mesh refinement: the spectral projection errors of the three parts are O(h); the error jumps at degenerate meshes, with jump size explicitly controlled by the least-squares residual of edge flips. (discrete Hodge spectral stability)

**中文。** 定义：离散 Hodge 分解：离散微分形式语言中的正交分解。猜想：离散 Hodge 分解对网格细分的稳定性：分解三部分的谱投影误差为 O(h)；误差在退化网格处跳变且跳变量由对角交换的最小二乘残差显式控制。（离散 Hodge 谱稳定）
