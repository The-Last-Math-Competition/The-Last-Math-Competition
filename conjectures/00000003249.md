# 00000003249

**English.** Definition: Born–von Kármán boundaries: the spectrum of thermodynamic limits. Conjecture: The rate of Bloch convergence: the convergence rate of finite-box IDS is an inverse logarithmic power of the volume, with the exponent of the power the product of the Wegner constant and box side length. (Bloch convergence Wegner power exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Born–von Kármán 边界：热力学极限的谱。猜想：Bloch 收敛的速率：有限盒 IDS 的收敛速率为体积的逆对数幂且幂的指数为 Wegner 常数与盒边长的乘积。（Bloch 收敛 Wegner 幂指数）
