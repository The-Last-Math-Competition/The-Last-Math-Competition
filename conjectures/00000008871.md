# 00000008871

**English.** Definition: Canonical form: the simplified canonical form of a game. Conjecture: The canonical form is the unique shortest representative (a uniqueness law); the canonical length of games of size n is controlled (a length law); the length distribution of random games exists (a distribution law); and the tail of the distribution is exponential (an exponential tail law). (canonical unique length exponential tail)

**中文。** 定义：canonical form：博弈的简化典范形式。猜想：典范的（最短代表的唯一）的（唯一律）；且唯一的长度（大小 n 的游戏的典范长的）的（长度律）；长度的分布（随机游戏的）的（分布律）；分布的尾部（指数尾）的（指数尾律）。
