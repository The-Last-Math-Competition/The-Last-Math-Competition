# 00000001505

**English.** Definition: Orbit closures of the SL(2,R) action on strata of translation surfaces (characterized by EMM as affine invariant submanifolds). Conjecture: There are no genuine rank ≥ 2 closures (all closures are Veech-surface hulls or whole strata); the minimal evidence for rank-2 exclusion, in strata with at least two singularities, is verified in explicit period coordinates.

**中文。** 定义：平移曲面 strata 上的 SL(2,R) 作用的轨道闭包(EMM 定理刻画为仿射不变子流形)。猜想：不存在真正的秩 ≥2 闭包(一切闭包皆为 Veech 曲面壳或全 stratum);秩 2 排除的最小例证在最少两个奇点的 stratum 中由显式周期坐标验证。
