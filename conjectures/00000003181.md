# 00000003181

**English.** Definition: Sparse gradients of high-dimensional signals: total variation recovery. Conjecture: The fidelity of TV recovery: the minimal measurement count for TV recovery of piecewise constants is the jump count times log n, with the tightness of the constant the angular resolution of jumps. (TV jump measurement angular resolution)

**中文。** 定义：高维信号的稀疏梯度：全变差恢复。猜想：TV 恢复的保真：分片常数的 TV 恢复的最小测量数跳跃数 log n 且常数的紧性为跳跃的角分辨率。（TV 跳跃测量角分辨率）
