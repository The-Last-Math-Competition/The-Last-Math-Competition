**English.** Definition: The coding-theoretic limit of perfect t-designs (Steiner systems S(t,k,n)): the divisibility lattice for the existence of Steiner systems (an explicit version of the Keevash existence theorem). Conjecture: S(t,k,n) exists if and only if n satisfies the classical divisibility conditions and n >= n_0(t,k) (an explicit polynomial bound), with n_0(t,k) of explicit type O(tk)^t; and the maximal order of the automorphism group of a Steiner system is n^{log_2 n} type at t = 2 (affine plane designs), while for t at least 3 the maximum is the order of the 2-transitive action of the linear simple group PSL (a complete Lie-type classification of design groups). (Keevash threshold Steiner automorphism classification)

**中文。** 定义：完美 t-设计（Steiner 系 S(t,k,n)）的码论极限：Steiner 系的存在性的分割格点（Keevash 存在定理的显式版）。猜想：S(t,k,n) 存在当且仅当 n 满足经典整除条件且 n ≥ n₀(t,k)（显式多项式界）；n₀(t,k) = O(t·k)^{t} 型显式；且 Steiner 系的自同构群的最大阶在 t=2 为 n^{log₂ n} 型（仿射平面的设计），t≥3 的最大阶为线性单群 PSL 的 2-传递作用的阶（设计群的 Lie 型完全分类）。
