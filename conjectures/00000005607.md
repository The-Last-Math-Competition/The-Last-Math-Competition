**EL2-17 ★** — source: 172_精确线性代数.md

**English.** Definition: The relevant-vector upper bound controls their count. Conjecture: The upper bound is an explicit exponential in the dimension, attained at random lattices, and the dual-side bound matches, given by the Poisson summation functional of the lattice. (random exponential upper bound of relevant vectors)

**中文。** 定义：相关向量上界指个数的控制量。猜想：上界为维数的显式指数式,随机格处达到且对偶侧界匹配。(相关向量随机指数上界)；且对偶侧的界由格的 Poisson 求和泛函达到。
