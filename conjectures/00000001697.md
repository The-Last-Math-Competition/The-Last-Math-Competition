# 00000001697

**EG-07 △** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: Furstenberg's ×2×3 problem asks whether every probability measure jointly invariant under {×2,×3} is a sum of Haar measure and finite-index atoms. Conjecture: The classification holds; generalization: the jointly invariant measures of {×2,×3,×5} are still Haar plus atoms (higher dimensions are controlled by the same entropy barrier).

**中文。** 定义：Furstenberg 的 ×2×3 问题问 {×2,×3}-联合不变的概率测度是否皆为 Haar 与有限指数原子之和。猜想：该分类成立;推广:{×2,×3,×5} 的联合不变测度仍为 Haar 与原子(高维由同类熵垒控制)。
