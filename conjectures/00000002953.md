**English.** Definition: Hammersley percolation: the connectivity phenomenon of lattice edges open with probability p. Conjecture: The hyperscaling relation set of critical exponents: in two dimensions the four hyperscaling equations satisfied by the nine critical exponents have independence dimension three, and the universality class of the exponents is the union of the triangular and square lattices. (percolation hyperscaling independence dimension)
