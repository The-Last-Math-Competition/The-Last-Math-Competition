# 00000001819

**English.** Definition: Faber's conjecture (the tautological cohomology of M_g is Gorenstein, with socle in degree g−2). Conjecture: The complete quadratic table of tautological relations of M₄ (the relation space in degrees ≤ 6 is an explicit quadratic form, reduced by Pixton relations); the kernel and image dimensions of the table agree with Faber's Gorenstein prediction.

**中文。** 定义：Faber 猜想(M_g tautological 上同调 Gorenstein,socle 在度 g−2)。猜想：M₄ 的 tautological 关系的完全二次表(关系空间在度 ≤6 为显式二次型,由 Pixton 关系削减);表的核与像维数与 Faber 的 Gorenstein 预测一致。
