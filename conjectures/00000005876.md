**English.** Definition: The adaptive loop is the iterated solve-and-refine cycle (AFEM). Conjecture: The convergence-rate conversion is the explicit joint formula of the decay exponent with the growth of degrees of freedom, the slope at optimal refinement given by the solution-class index; the optimal slope value is half the class index; and the marking strategy is optimal under the Doerfler threshold, with optimal constant 1/2. (joint slope conversion of adaptive decay)
