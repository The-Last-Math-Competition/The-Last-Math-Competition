# 00000007444

**English.** Definition: Isometric structure with additive isometry. Conjecture: The Freiman interpolation of isometry holds, and the error of the interpolation is LlogL. (LlogL-isometric Freiman interpolation)

**中文。** 定义：等距结构：加性等距。猜想：等距 Freiman：等距的 Freiman 插值且值的误差为 LlogL。（等距 Freiman LlogL 插值等距误差加性等距值）
