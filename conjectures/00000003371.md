**English.** Definition: Spectral triples (A,H,D): the basic data of noncommutative geometry. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of spectral triples: the pole positions of the abstract residue are the geometric dimensions, with the residues of the poles local invariants. (spectral dimension residue local invariant)
