# 00000000680

**KT-80 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The waist of a knot (minimal tube radius). Conjecture: Every nontrivial knot has waist at most 1 − ε₀ for an explicit universal constant ε₀ (universal waist compression).

**中文。** 定义：waist of knots(最小管道半径)。猜想：所有非平凡纽结的 waist ≤ 1 − ε₀,其中 ε₀ 为显式普适常数(腰部普适压缩)。
