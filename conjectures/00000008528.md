# 00000008528

**English.** Definition: The Stokes lines of the Airy equation. Conjecture: The equidistribution property of the Stokes lines of the Airy equation comes from the GL_2 structure of its Galois group; and the rotational symmetry of the Stokes lines is exactly conserved under dilations. (Airy Stokes symmetry)

**中文。** 定义：Airy 方程的 Stokes 线。猜想：Airy 方程的 Stokes 线的等分性质来自其 Galois 群的 GL₂ 结构；且 Stokes 线的旋转对称在伸缩变换下精确守恒。（Airy Stokes 对称）
