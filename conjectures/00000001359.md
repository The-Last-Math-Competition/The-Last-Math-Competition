**English.** Definition: The Fouvry–Klückers bilinear model refers to divisor statistics at prime shifts. Conjecture: The main-term coefficient of the sum of squares of τ(p−1) is an explicit Euler product; and the coefficient carries a negative-sign term corresponding to the nontrivial null space. (prime-shift divisor square Euler coefficient)
