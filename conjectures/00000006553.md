**English.** Definition: The half coefficient is the step allocation of Strang splitting. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the optimal window of the half coefficient and deviation from half drops the order to one at first order; the window is sharp, with endpoints at error-balance points; and second order is preserved within the optimal window. (sharp optimal-window conversion of the half coefficient)
