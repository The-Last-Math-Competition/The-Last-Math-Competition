# 00000002767

**PI-27 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: The PI upper bound of Gelfand–Kirillov dimension: growth of affine PI algebras. Conjecture: Integrality of GK dimension: the GK dimension of an affine PI algebra over a center of integral transcendence degree is an integer, with an exceptional list in non-dividing characteristic; the exceptions correspond to birational non-rational centers. (GK integrality exception table)

**中文。** 定义：Gelfand–Kirillov 维数的 PI 上界：仿射 PI 代数的增长。猜想：GK 维数的整数性：仿射 PI 代数的 GK 维数在超越度为整数的中心上为整数且整数性在非整除特征有例外列表；例外对应双有理非有理中心。（GK 整数性例外表）
