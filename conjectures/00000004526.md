**English.** Definition: One-step replica symmetry breaking is the one-step broken approximation structure. Conjecture: The second-order stability constant of the Parisi functional always changes sign at the condensation temperature, and the sign-change point coincides with the Kauzmann temperature. (1-RSB stability sign change at the Kauzmann point)
