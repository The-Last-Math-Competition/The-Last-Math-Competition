# 00000001168

**English.** Definition: A Zoll metric is one all of whose geodesics are closed. Conjecture: The spectral multiplicity upper bound for Zoll metrics on SU(2) is two; and the counterexample to multiplicity rigidity is the family of rotated round metrics. (Zoll multiplicity rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Zoll 度量为一切测地线闭的度量。猜想：SU(2) 上 Zoll 度量的谱重数上界为二；且重数刚性的反例为圆度量旋转族。（Zoll 重数刚性）
