# 00000008129

**English.** Definition: The equivariant structure of deformations of noncommutative varieties: the noncommutative linearization lin(G,A) of automorphism group actions. Conjecture: The obstruction to the existence of a linearization (of the equivariant structure) is the torsor tors(G) of equivariant classes in HH^2; the count of trivializations of the torsor is exponential in the prime factor count of |G| (a trivialization exponent law); and the worst case of linearization (the minimal group acting non-linearizably) is the Klein group at n = 4 acting by translations on ncP^3 (minimal Klein non-linearizability), the exception to the action being given by the centralization of the Hecke algebra (a Hecke central exception law). (equivariant torsor Klein minimal non-linearizability)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的形变的等变结构：自同构群的非交换线性化 lin(G,A)。猜想：lin 的存在性（等变结构的线性化）的障碍为 HH² 的等变类的 torsor tors(G)；且 torsor 的平凡化的计数为 |G| 的素因子数的指数（平凡化指数律）；线性化的最坏（不可线性化的极小群）为 n=4 的 Klein 群在 ncP³ 的平移作用（Klein 极小不可线性），作用的例外由 Hecke 代数的中心化给出（Hecke 中心例外律）。
