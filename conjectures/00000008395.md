# 00000008395

**English.** Definition: The p-adic volume of the motivic measure: the realization via counting F_p points. Conjecture: The main term of the p to infinity asymptotics of the p-adic volume is the Hodge-Deligne polynomial; the support of the secondary term is the set of prime powers; and there is no infinite sequence of prime powers where the secondary term fails to exist. (p-adic volume main term)

**中文。** 定义：母题测度的 p-进体积指在 F_p 点计数实现的实化。猜想：p-adic 体积的 p→∞ 渐近的主项为 Hodge–Deligne 多项式；次项的支撑为素数幂集；不存在使次项不存在的素数幂无穷序列。（p-adic 体积主项）
