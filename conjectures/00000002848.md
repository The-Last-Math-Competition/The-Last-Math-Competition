# 00000002848

**AS-18 ★** — source: 80_代数统计与张量分解的几何.md

**English.** Definition: The algebraic geometry of phase retrieval: homogeneous systems of intensity equations. Conjecture: Universality of phase retrieval: universality of intensity functionals of n-dimensional complex signals holds under m ≥ 4n−4 random measurements, with the algebraic necessary and sufficient condition the empty intersection of the associated quadratic varieties. (phase retrieval algebraic criterion)

**中文。** 定义：相位恢复的代数几何：强度的齐次方程组。猜想：相位恢复的普适性：n 维复信号的强度泛函的普适性当 m ≥ 4n−4 的随机测量下成立且代数充要条件为相关二次簇的空交。（相位恢复代数充要）
