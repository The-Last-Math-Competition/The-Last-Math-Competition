**SA-61 ★** — source: 20_代数组合与对称函数.md

**English.** Definition: τ(KG(n,k)) is the number of spanning trees of the Kneser graph KG(n,k). Conjecture: For fixed k ≥ 2, τ(KG(n,k)) = (k(n−k))^{C(n,k)−1}·g_k(n), where the n^{1/k}-growth exponent of g_k(n) converges to an explicit limit γ_k, and γ_2 = 1/2.

**中文。** 定义：Kneser 图 KG(n,k) 的生成树数 τ(KG(n,k))。猜想：对固定 k≥2,τ(KG(n,k)) = (k(n−k))^{C(n,k)−1}·g_k(n),其中 g_k(n) 的 n^{1/k} 增长指数收敛到显式极限 γ_k,且 γ_2 = 1/2。
