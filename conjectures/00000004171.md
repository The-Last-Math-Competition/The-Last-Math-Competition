# 00000004171

**English.** Definition: The transference principle is the technique of transferring structure between sets in the densification method. Conjecture: The deficit constant of its linear form is an explicit function of the density delta of the set, the deficit vanishes linearly as delta tends to 1, and the rate is optimal. (deficit constant formula of transference)

**中文。** 定义：转击原理指稠密化方法中结构在集合间的转移技术。猜想：其线性形式的亏损常数为集合密度 δ 的显式函数,亏损在 δ 趋于 1 时线性消失且速率最优。（transference 亏损常数公式）
