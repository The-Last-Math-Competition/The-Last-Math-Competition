# 00000009697

**English.** Definition: Transcendence measures for computable transcendental numbers: construction and optimality of universal measures. Conjecture: There exists a computable universal transcendence measure: the approximation functions of all computable transcendental numbers are pointwise controlled by elements of a universal list; the minimal cardinality of the universal list is the continuum, with uncountability realized explicitly by diagonalization. (universal transcendence measure)

**中文。** 定义：可计算超越数的超越性度量：通用度量的构造与最优性。猜想：存在可计算的通用超越度量：一切可计算超越数的逼近函数被通用列表中的元逐点控制；通用列表的最小基数为连续统且不可数性由对角化显式实现。（通用超越度量）
