# 00000007939

**English.** Definition: The addition theorem spectrum of elliptic solutions: the elementary symmetric functions e_k(x(t)) of the evolution operator of elliptic solutions of the Calogero-Moser system (potential 1/sin^2). Conjecture: Each e_k is an elliptic function of t whose period lattice coincides with that of the potential; e_k satisfies an ordinary differential equation of order k+1 (higher-level integrability), with leading coefficient an explicit multiple of the (k+1)-st derivative of the Weierstrass p-function; and the spectral curve of the Lax pair of elliptic Calogero-Moser has genus n-1 (n the particle count), the isomorphism classes of spectral curves under initial-lattice translations forming an n-dimensional analytic family. (Calogero-Moser elliptic addition theorem)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆解的加法定理谱：Calogero–Moser 系（势 1/sin²）的椭圆解的演化算子的初等对称函数 e_k(x(t))。猜想：e_k 为 t 的椭圆函数且其周期格与势的格一致；且 e_k 满足阶 k+1 的常微分方程（高层次可积性），方程的首项系数为 Weierstrass p-函数的 k+1 阶导数的显式倍数；椭圆 Calogero–Moser 的 Lax 对的谱曲线的亏格为 n-1（n 为粒子数），谱曲线在初值格点平移下的同构类形成 n 维解析族。
