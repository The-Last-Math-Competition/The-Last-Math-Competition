# 00000002881

**English.** Definition: The index and positive scalar curvature: the quantitative side of the Gromov–Lawson conjecture. Conjecture: The uniform bound for kernel vanishing under positive curvature: on manifolds of scalar curvature ≥ k and diameter D, the Dirac kernel vanishes when kD² exceeds an explicit constant that is a quadratic function of the dimension. (positive curvature kernel vanishing bound)

**中文。** 定义：指标与正数量曲率：Gromov–Lawson 猜想的定量侧。猜想：正曲率下核消失的一致界：数量曲率 ≥ k 的流形上 Dirac 核维数在直径 D 下的消失当 kD² > 显式常数且常数为维数的二次函数。（正曲率核消失界）
