# 00000001280

**English.** Definition: The topological entropy of a minimal subshift is the growth rate of the system's complexity. Conjecture: The topological entropy of any minimal subshift is an algebraic number; and the exceptions to algebraicity occur only in pseudo-periodic systems outside the substitutive class. (minimal entropy algebraicity)

**中文。** 定义：极小子移位的拓扑熵为系统的复杂度增长率。猜想：任意极小子移位的拓扑熵为代数数；且代数性的例外仅在替换类之外的伪周期系统。（极小熵代数性）
