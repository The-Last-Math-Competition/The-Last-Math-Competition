# 00000001386

**English.** Definition: Given an ML-random point x under a noncomputable measure μ, the difference set from randomness under computable measures is D = {n : x↾n differs}. Conjecture: The Hausdorff dimension of every such D is at most 1 − h(μ) (h the effective entropy dimension of μ), and the bound is attained by the typical difference set of a binomial random drift.

**中文。** 定义：非可计算测度 μ 下 ML-随机点 x 与可计算测度下随机点的差异集 D={n: x↾n 不同}。猜想：一切此类 D 的 Hausdorff 维数上界为 1−h(μ)(h 为 μ 的有效熵维数),且该界由二项随机漂移的典型差异集取得。
