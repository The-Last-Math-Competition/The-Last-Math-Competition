**English.** Definition: The relation of widths and entropy: Carl's inequality. Conjecture: The converse of Carl: the lower bound of entropy numbers is the geometric mean of widths, with the exponent of the mean the dimension ratio. (Carl width geometric mean entropy lower bound)
