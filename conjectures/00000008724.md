# 00000008724

**MBL-14 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: The Mott activated conductivity of localized phases: the temperature dependence of conductivity with and without interactions. Conjecture: Without interactions the conductivity is activated of type exp(-T^{-1/2}) with exponent determined by the variable-range hopping dimension — on a d-dimensional lattice the exponent is exactly -(d+1)^{-1}; the interaction correction is of type exp(-T^{-1/4}), valid within the resolvable temperature window of cold atoms; and the high-temperature crossover is a power law T^alpha with alpha uniquely determined by the effective dimension of the phonon spectrum. (Mott activated conductivity hopping dimension)

**中文。** 定义：局域化相的 Mott 激活电导：无相互作用与相互作用情形下电导率随温度的依赖。猜想：无相互作用时电导率为 exp(-T^{-1/2}) 的激活型且指数由变程跳跃维数决定——d 维格点上指数恰为 -(d+1)^{-1}；相互作用修正为 exp(-T^{-1/4}) 型变体且在冷原子可分辨温度窗内成立；高温交叉区为幂律 T^α 且 α 由声子谱的有效维数唯一确定。
