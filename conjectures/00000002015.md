**English.** Definition: Crack propagation in peridynamics is the fracture evolution of nonlocal elasticity. Conjecture: The regularity of the crack path is C^{1,α} (α < 1/2); the upper bound comes from the convexity obstruction of the fracture energy (which cannot be exceeded at 1/2).

**中文。** 定义：peridynamics 的裂纹传播指非局部弹性的断裂演化。猜想：裂纹路径的正则性为 C^{1,α}(α<1/2);上界由断裂能量的凸性障碍给出(不可超过 1/2)。
