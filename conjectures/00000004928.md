# 00000004928

**English.** Definition: The ergodic limit of the risk-sensitive value is the limit of the long-term average. Conjecture: As the risk parameter tends to zero the ergodic limit constant is an explicit functional of the bias span, given by the large-deviation rate function and exact. (rate function constant of the risk ergodic limit)

**中文。** 定义：风险敏感值的遍历极限指长期平均的极限值。猜想：风险参数趋零时遍历极限常数为偏差跨度的显式泛函,由大偏差率函数给出且精确。（风险遍历极限率函数常数）
