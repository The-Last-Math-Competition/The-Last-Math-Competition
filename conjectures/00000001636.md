# 00000001636

**HA-06 ★** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: Aut(D^b(Coh C)) is the group of triangle autoequivalences of the derived category of a curve C. Conjecture: For genus g ≥ 2, Aut(D^b(Coh C)) ≅ Aut(C) ⋉ (Pic(C) ⊕ Z[2]); the Pic component acts diagonally on twist functors, its explicit action being of order 2 via the degree component of line bundles.

**中文。** 定义：曲线 C 的 D^b(Coh C) 的自同构群指三角范畴的自等价群。猜想：亏格 g≥2 时 Aut(D^b(Coh C)) ≅ Aut(C) ⋉ (Pic(C)⊕Z[2]);Pic 分量对角地作用于平移扭转函子,其显式作用由线丛的度数分量为 2 阶。
