**English.** Definition: An overdetermined problem is a boundary value problem with superfluous conditions. Conjecture: The feasible angles of the reflection method are explicit; for full overdetermination the ball solution is unique, and the partially overdetermined exception families are finite. (overdetermined reflection feasible-angle ball solutions)

**中文。** 定义：过定问题指条件超定的边值问题。猜想：反射方法的可行角显式；全过定时唯一球解且部分过定例外族有限。（过定反射可行角球解）
