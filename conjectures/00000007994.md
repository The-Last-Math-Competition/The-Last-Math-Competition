# 00000007994

**English.** Definition: The C^{1,alpha} exponent of the free boundary of the thin obstacle (Signorini) problem: the best Holder exponent alpha_S for gradient extension of solutions of the thin obstacle problem with obstacle on a lower-dimensional manifold gamma in R^n. Conjecture: alpha_S = 1/2 (a universal rigidity of the half power); and the higher smoothness threshold of the free boundary: the transition from C^{1,1/2} to C^{1,alpha} with alpha > 1/2 is completely determined by the sign of the leading term of the vanishing coefficient (Almgren stratification); analyticity after the transition is equivalent to nonnegativity of the first variation of the free boundary surface, the minimal example of violation being a four-point-contact singularity (the lowest-dimensional classification of singularities). (Signorini half-power Almgren stratification)

**中文。** 定义：thin obstacle（Signorini）问题的自由边界的 C^{1,α} 指数：障碍在低维流面 γ⊂R^n 上的薄障碍问题的解的梯度延拓的最佳 Hölder 指数 α_S。猜想：α_S = 1/2（半次的普适刚性）；且自由边界的高阶光滑性门槛：从 C^{1,1/2} 到 C^{1,α>1/2} 的跃迁由 vanishing 系数的首项符号完全决定（Almgren 分层）；跃迁后的解析性与自由边面的首变分的非负性等价，非负性破坏的极小示例为四点接触型奇点（奇点的最低维分类）。
