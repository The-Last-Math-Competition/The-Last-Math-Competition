# 00000004645

**English.** Definition: A negative-level affine VOA is an affine vertex algebra of negative rational level. Conjecture: The lower bound of its central-charge spectrum is an explicit negative constant, the bound is attained by the W sequence, and the spectrum is closed at the integer points of the level. (lower bound of the negative-level central-charge spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：负级仿射 VOA 指级数为负有理数的仿射顶点代数。猜想：其中心荷谱的下界为显式负常数,下界在 W 序列达到且谱在级数的整数点闭合。（负级中心荷谱下界）
