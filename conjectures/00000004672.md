# 00000004672

**SG3-12 ★** — source: 141_交换代数IV_奇点理论.md

**English.** Definition: A negative-weight deformation is a weighted deformation toward degeneration. Conjecture: A three-dimensional singularity admits a negative-weight deformation if and only if its spectrum contains an explicit value below minus one, and the criterion is effective and fails exactly at simple singularities. (spectral-value criterion of negative-weight deformations)

**中文。** 定义：负权形变指向退化方向的加权形变。猜想：三维奇点存在负权形变当且仅当其谱含小于负一的显式值,判据有效且在简单奇异处恰好失效。（负权形变谱值判据）
