**English.** Definition: KK-theory and the index pairing: the analytic realization of Kasparov's product. Conjecture: Continuity of the KK pairing: the pairing is continuous in the norm topology of operators, with continuity modulus explicitly controlled by commutator norms; tightness of the control is attained under elliptic regularity. (KK pairing continuity modulus)

**中文。** 定义：KK-理论与指标配对：Ku 的 Kasparov 积的解析实现。猜想：KK 配对的连续性：配对在算子的范数拓扑下连续且连续模由交换子的范数显式控制；控制的紧性在椭圆正则条件下达到。（KK 配对连续模）
