# 00000000932

**OA-32 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The numerical range W(T) of a compact operator is a compact convex set. Conjecture: A characterization exists for matrices whose numerical range has at most n−2 foci; and the extremal realization is by Jacobi-type perturbations. (numerical range focal point count characterization)

**中文。** 定义：紧算子的数值域 W(T) 为凸紧集。猜想：数值域焦点个数不超过 n−2 的矩阵刻画存在；且刻画的极值实现为 Jacobi 型摄动。（数值域焦点个数刻画）
