**MRG-03 ★** — source: 317_测度刚性.md

**English.** Definition: Measure limits of nondense orbits: the atomic structure of weak limits of orbit measures on homogeneous spaces (the Mozes-Shah theorem). Conjecture: The atomic mass concentration law of orbit measure limits: the entropy lower bound of the nonatomic part of a limiting measure is an explicit logarithmic function of the total atomic mass, and the bound is tight. (orbit limit atomic concentration law)

**中文。** 定义：非稠密轨道的测度极限：齐性空间上轨道测度的弱极限的原子结构（Mozes–Shah 定理）。猜想：轨道测度极限的原子质量集中律：极限测度的原子质量总和的非原子部分的熵下界为原子质量的显式对数函数且界为紧。（轨道极限原子集中律）
