# 00000001759

**RT-09 ★** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: The BMW algebra is the braid-group deformation of quantum groups (Birman–Murakami–Wenzl); a Markov trace is a self-adjoint linear functional on it. Conjecture: The BMW trace space has dimension at most 2 (generated by the Markov parameters q and a); the second trace is explicit in its relation with the F₀ factor of the Kauffman polynomial.

**中文。** 定义：BMW 代数指量子群的辫群变形(Birman–Murakami–Wenzl);Markov 迹指其上的自守线性函数。猜想：BMW 迹空间维数 ≤2(由 Markov 参数 q 与 a 生成);第二迹的显式为与 Kauffman 多项式的 F₀ 因子的关系。
