# 00000009859

**KPU-09 ★** — source: 314_KPZ普适类.md

**English.** Definition: The 2+1-dimensional KPZ transition: growth exponents vanish above the critical dimension and logarithmic roughness appears. Conjecture: In the smooth phase of weakly nonlinear 2+1-dimensional KPZ, height fluctuations grow as the square root of the logarithm: fluctuations are c sqrt(log t); the constant c is universal and independent of the nonlinearity strength, and the saturated height fluctuation at critical nonlinearity diverges as a power of the logarithm. (smooth phase logarithmic fluctuations)

**中文。** 定义：2+1 维 KPZ 相变：维数高于临界时生长指数消失、对数粗糙出现。猜想：2+1 维 KPZ 在弱非线性的光滑相中高度涨落按对数平方根增长：涨落为 c·√(log t)；常数 c 为普适且与非线性强度无关，临界非线性处的饱和高度涨落发散为对数幂。（光滑相对数涨落）
