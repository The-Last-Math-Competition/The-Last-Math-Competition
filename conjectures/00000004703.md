**English.** Definition: Multiple recurrence is the phenomenon of returning to the same set several times. Conjecture: The lower density of multiple recurrence time sets always has an explicit lower bound, and the bound decreases exponentially with the number of tower layers. (tower-type lower bound of multiple recurrence density)

**中文。** 定义：多重回归指多次返回同一集合的现象。猜想：多重回归时间集的下密度恒有显式下界；且界随塔层数指数递减。（多重回归密度塔型下界）
