# 00000004036

**English.** Definition: The components of the AR-quiver of the exterior algebra Lambda(V) are stratified by Schur powers. Conjecture: The tau-period of an indecomposable p-representation is given by an explicit divisor-type formula in the reduced dimension of p, and each period is realized uniquely in an explicit tube. (AR period formula for exterior algebras)

**中文。** 定义：外代数 Λ(V) 的 AR-箭图分量按 Schur 幂分层。猜想：不可分解 p-表示的 τ-周期为 p 的约化维数的显式因数型公式,且每个周期在显式管中唯一实现。（外代数 AR 周期公式）
