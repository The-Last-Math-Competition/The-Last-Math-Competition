**English.** Definition: The enigmatic characters of gl(m|n) are Serganova's geometric reduction of supercharacters. Conjecture: The enigmatic characters have an explicit formula: χ is a hook-quotient sum of sup-Schur(λ/μ) (the t = −1 specialization of supersymmetric Schur functions); the formula is a limit of supersymmetric Schur functions.

**中文。** 定义：gl(m|n) 的 enigmatic 特征标指 Serganova 的超特征标的几何约化。猜想：enigmatic 特征标有显式公式:χ = sup-Schur(λ/μ) 的钩商求和(supersymmetric Schur 的 t=-1 特化);公式为超对称 Schur 的极限。
