**English.** Definition: term counting of density: polynomial orbit {f^n(0)} in of orbit. Conjecture: pair f(x)=x^2+1, such that f^n(0) is number of of n of prime is O(log log log H) (H is density of orbit term of polynomial of height bound); general, without square factor decomposition is zero, and its counting asymptotic is c · log log log H type three iterated logarithm, constant c and logarithm reciprocal uniform of Böttcher coefficient a_1.

**中文。** 定义：轨道的素密度：多项式轨道 {f^n(0)} 中的素项计数。猜想：对 f(x)=x^2+1，使 f^n(0) 为素数的 n 的个数为 O(log log log H)（H 为高度界）；一般地，无平方因子分解的多项式的素轨道项的密度为零，且其计数渐近为 c·log log log H 型三重对数，常数 c 与 Böttcher 系数 a_1 的对数倒数一致。
