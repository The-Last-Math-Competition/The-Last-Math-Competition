# 00000007772

**ADY-22 △** — source: 244_算术动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: Prime density of orbits: counting prime terms in the polynomial orbit {f^n(0)}. Conjecture: For f(x)=x^2+1, the count of n with f^n(0) prime is O(log log log H) in the height bound H; in general the prime-orbit density of polynomials without square factor decompositions is zero, with count asymptotic to a triple logarithm c log log log H, the constant c agreeing with the reciprocal logarithm of the first Bottcher coefficient a_1. (triple-logarithm prime orbit count)

**中文。** 定义：轨道的素密度：多项式轨道 {f^n(0)} 中的素项计数。猜想：对 f(x)=x^2+1，使 f^n(0) 为素数的 n 的个数为 O(log log log H)（H 为高度界）；一般地，无平方因子分解的多项式的素轨道项的密度为零，且其计数渐近为 c·log log log H 型三重对数，常数 c 与 Böttcher 系数 a_1 的对数倒数一致。
