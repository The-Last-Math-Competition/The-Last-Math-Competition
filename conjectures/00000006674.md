# 00000006674

**English.** Definition: Network flow with the flow realization of transport on graphs. Conjecture: The reduction of transport to flow has linear size and preserves order; the order-preserving map is the forward edges; and the capacity of the forward edges is assigned explicitly by the marginal differences. (linear order-preserving transport-flow reduction)

**中文。** 定义：网络流为图上流的传输实现。猜想：传输到流的归约规模线性且归约保序；且保序的映射为前向边；且前向边的容量由边际差显式赋值。（传输流归约线性保序）
