# 00000000889

**PV-89 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The critical dissipative SQG equation is the surface quasi-geostrophic equation with fractional dissipation exactly at the critical order. Conjecture: Smooth solutions exist globally; moreover, no self-similar blow-up solutions exist — blow-ups of the form θ(x,t) = (t*−t)^{-α}Θ(x/(t*−t)^β) with α, β satisfying the scaling relation have no nonconstant solutions.

**中文。** 定义：临界耗散 SQG 方程指地表活性标量方程在分数耗散恰为临界阶的情形。猜想：光滑解整体存在;进一步,该方程不存在自相似爆破解,即形如 θ(x,t)=(t*−t)^{-α}Θ(x/(t*−t)^β) 的爆破在 α,β 满足标度关系时无非常数解。
