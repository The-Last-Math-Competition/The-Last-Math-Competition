**SMI-30 △** — source: 287_子流形几何不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Minimal algebraic inequalities: Newton transform structures. Conjecture: The constants of the divergence form of Newton transforms are always explicit; and the combination of the explicit values is the binomial coefficients. (Newton divergence explicit constants)

**中文。** 定义：极小代数不等式为 Newton 变换结构。猜想：Newton 变换的散度型的常数恒为显式；且显式的组合为二项式系数。（Newton 散度显式常数）
