# 00000004158

**English.** Definition: Efficient congruencing is the method of layer-by-layer congruence alignment on p-adic nodes. Conjecture: The optimal node distribution is k equidistant layers with node numbers allocated by binomial coefficients, and any deviation from this allocation loses at least an epsilon in the mean value bound. (binomial optimal allocation of EC nodes)

**中文。** 定义：efficient congruencing 指 p 进节点上的逐层同余对齐方法。猜想：最优节点分布为 k 层等距分层,各层节点数按二项系数分配,偏离该分配使中值界损失至少一个 ε。（EC 节点二项最优分配）
