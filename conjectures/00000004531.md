**English.** Definition: The condensation transition is the change of the solution space from exponentially many clusters to polynomially many. Conjecture: The condensation threshold is an explicit saddle point of the local entropy functional, the saddle-point equations are closed, and the threshold is located with error below one. (explicit saddle-point location of the condensation threshold)
