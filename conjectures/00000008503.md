# 00000008503

**English.** Definition: A differential module is a finitely generated module over a differential field with a derivation action. Conjecture: For an irreducible differential module, the dimension of the image of its differential Galois group under the adjoint representation determines the dimension of Ext^1: dim Ext^1 = n^2 - dim G; and Ext^1 = 0 if and only if the module is of trivial type under a rigid differential extension. (differential module Ext and rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：微分模指微分域上的有限生成模带导子运算。猜想：不可约微分模的微分 Galois 群在伴随表示下的像的维数决定其 Ext¹ 的维数：dim Ext¹ = n² − dim G；且 Ext¹ = 0 当且仅当模为刚性的微分扩张下的平凡型。（微分模 Ext 与刚性）
