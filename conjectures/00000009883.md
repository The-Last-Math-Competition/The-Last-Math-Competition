**GBR-03 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Tau-tilting finiteness: the complete criterion for finiteness of support tau-tilting modules of algebras. Conjecture: A quiver algebra is tau-tilting finite if and only if its brick set is finite; the criterion is polynomially verifiable from graph-theoretic conditions on the oriented path structure of the quiver, and is closed under gluing and direct sums. (tau-tilting finite graph criterion)

**中文。** 定义：τ-倾斜有限性：代数的支持 τ-倾斜模有限性的完全判据。猜想：箭图代数为 τ-倾斜有限当且仅当其 brick 集合有限且判据可由箭图的定向路结构的图论条件多项式验证；判据在粘合与直和下封闭。（tau-倾斜有限图判据）
