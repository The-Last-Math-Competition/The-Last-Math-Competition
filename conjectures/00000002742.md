**PI-02 ★** — source: 77_非交换多项式恒等_PI理论.md

**English.** Definition: The Amitsur–Levitzki theorem: M_n(K) satisfies s_{2n} = 0 with no lower-degree identities. Conjecture: The optimal length of Razmyslov's corrected identities: the minimal degree of the corrected identity family is 2n−1 plus a constant term, the constant being the counting supremum of admissible matrix pairs. (Razmyslov correction optimal length)

**中文。** 定义：Amitsur–Levitzki 定理：M_n(K) 满足 s_{2n}=0 且无更低次恒等式。猜想：AL 定理的 Razmyslov 修正恒等式的最优长度：修正恒等式族的极小次数为 2n−1 加常数项且常数为容许矩阵对的计数上确界。（Razmyslov 修正最优长度）
