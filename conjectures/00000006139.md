**English.** Definition: The obstruction formula quantifies the difference of two groups. Conjecture: For the negative-one shift of the tangent-obstruction dual the amplitude interval is explicit, and the combination of the interval is the composition law. (shift-amplitude composition law of tangent obstruction)
