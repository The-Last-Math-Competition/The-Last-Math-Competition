**English.** Definition: An energy barrier is the maximal energy threshold for a continuation map to hold, and the realizable spectrum is the set of all barrier values. Conjecture: The realizable spectrum forms an additive subsemigroup; it is discrete if and only if the manifold is monotone, and for non-monotone manifolds the barrier values are dense in the positive half-axis. (discreteness criterion for the barrier semigroup)

**中文。** 定义：能量屏障指 continuation 映射成立所需的最大能量阈值,可实现谱指全体屏障值。猜想：可实现谱构成加法子半群;其为离散的当且仅当流形单调,非单调时屏障值在正半轴稠密。（能障半群离散判据）
