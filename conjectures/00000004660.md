# 00000004660

**English.** Definition: The shadow and the principal part are the two end data of a mock form. Conjecture: There exist two mock modular forms with the same shadow but linearly independent principal parts, and the separation is realized explicitly by a Rademacher-corrected pair with equal shadows and different principal parts. (same-shadow different-principal-part separation)

**中文。** 定义：阴影与主部指 mock 形式的两端数据。猜想：存在两个 mock 模形式阴影相同而主部线性无关,分离由同影异主的 Rademacher 修正对显式实现。（同影异主分离对）
