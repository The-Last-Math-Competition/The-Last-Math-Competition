# 00000004439

**English.** Definition: Dyson Brownian motion is the Brownian dynamical system of the eigenvalues. Conjecture: The spectral conversion exponent of its non-collision condition is always beta/2, the conversion is of the same type for all positive beta and for endpoint reflection, and it is exact. (Dyson non-collision beta/2 exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Dyson 布朗运动指特征值的布朗动力系。猜想：其非碰撞条件的谱换算指数恒为 β/2,换算对一切正 β 与端点反射情形同型且精确。（Dyson 非碰撞 β/2 指数）
