# 00000002865

**English.** Definition: The L² index: the reduced index on noncompact covers (Atiyah). Conjecture: The Novikov–Shubin correction of the L² index: the convergence domain of the τ-exponents of the torsion part is the positive real axis, and the jump points of the exponents are explicit functions of the spectral radii of the covering group. (L2 index NS correction)

**中文。** 定义：L² 指标：非紧覆盖上的约化指标（Atiyah）。猜想：L² 指标的 Novikov–Shubin 修正：挠部分的 τ 指数的收敛域为正实轴且指数的跳跃点为覆盖群的谱半径的显式函数。（L2 指标 NS 修正）
