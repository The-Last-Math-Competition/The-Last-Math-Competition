**RM3-08 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: β-Manova denotes the β-gas of singular values of rectangular random matrices; the hard edge is the limiting process near the smallest singular value. Conjecture: The hard-edge limit is continuous in the two dimension parameters and converges to the β-Bessel process as one dimension tends to infinity. (beta Manova hard edge continuity)

**中文。** 定义：β-Manova 指矩形随机矩阵奇异值的 β-气,hard edge 指最小奇异值附近的极限过程。猜想：hard edge 极限随两维参数连续,并在一个维数趋于无穷时收敛到 β-Bessel 过程。（β-Manova hard edge 连续性）
