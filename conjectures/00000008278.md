# 00000008278

**SFL-18 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The optional stochastic calculus of jump-diffusions: the isometry iso_jump of stochastic integrals against compensated jump measures. Conjecture: The correction of iso_jump (the isometry of jump integrals) is the second moment of the Levy measure (a second moment isometry law); the decomposition of the error of optimal estimation for jump filtering (jump observations) is the orthogonal sum of continuous error plus jump error (an orthogonal decomposition law); and the information threshold for jump detection (separating jumps from diffusion in observations) is the ratio lambda/sigma^2 of jump intensity to diffusion noise (a lambda-sigma^2 threshold law), above which the detection error exponent is the Chernoff information (a Chernoff detection exponent). (jump isometry orthogonal Chernoff detection)

**中文。** 定义：跳扩散的可选随机分析：跳的补偿测度的随机积分的等距 iso_jump。猜想：iso_jump（跳积分的等距）的修正为 Lévy 测度的第二矩（第二矩等距律）；且跳滤波（含跳观测）的最优估计的误差的分解为连续误差+跳误差的正交和（正交分解律）；跳的检测（观测分离跳与扩散）的信息阈为跳强度 λ 与扩散噪声 σ² 的比 λ/σ² 的阈（λ-σ² 阈律），阈上的检测误差指数为 Chernoff 信息（Chernoff 检测指数）。
