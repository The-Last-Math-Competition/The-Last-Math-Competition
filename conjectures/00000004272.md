**English.** Definition: A rank-2 torsion-free group is a torsion-free abelian group of rank 2, and the real invariants are the pairs of real numbers classifying its equivalence classes. Conjecture: A pair of reals is realizable if and only if its difference satisfies an explicit rational linear condition involving sqrt(2), and the criterion is complete and redundancy-free. (realizability criterion of rank-2 real invariants)

**中文。** 定义：秩 2 无挠群指秩为 2 的无挠交换群,实不变量指其等价类的实数对。猜想：实数对可实现当且仅当其差满足与 √2 的显式有理线性条件,判据完全且无冗余。（秩 2 实不变量实现判据）
