# 00000005424

**English.** Definition: The boundary approximation of the hyperbolic metric is the boundary behavior of the interior-point metric. Conjecture: The boundary approximation conversion is always the explicit logarithmic formula of the boundary distance; uniform for convex domains, with normalization constant of type two. (hyperbolic boundary logarithm constant two)

**中文。** 定义：双曲度量的边界近似指内点度量的边界行为。猜想：边界近似的换算恒为边界距离的对数显式公式；凸域一致且归一常数为二型值。（双曲边界对数常二）
