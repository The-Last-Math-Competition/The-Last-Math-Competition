# 00000008958

**ISO-08 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: The sine-Gordon equation: a nonlinear Klein-Gordon equation. Conjecture: The stability of the breather spectrum is always given by the explicit Floquet threshold; and the threshold is twice the argument of the multiplier. (breather Floquet double-argument threshold)

**中文。** 定义：sine-Gordon 方程指非线性 Klein-Gordon 方程。猜想：呼吸子谱的稳定性恒由 Floquet 判据的显式阈值给出；且阈值为乘子辐角的二倍。（呼吸子 Floquet 双辐角阈）
