# 00000005695

**TW2-15 ★** — source: 175_范畴的表示_2表示论.md

**English.** Definition: A quadratic representation is a representation lifted to a quadratic extension. Conjecture: For imaginary quadratic fields it is graded by the discriminant, the classification is closed, and the kernel of the grading is the Clifford algebra. (Clifford discriminant grading of quadratic representations)

**中文。** 定义：二次表示为提升到二次扩张的表示。猜想：虚二次域时由判别式分度且分类封闭；且分度的核为 Clifford 代数。（二次表示 Clifford 判别分度）
