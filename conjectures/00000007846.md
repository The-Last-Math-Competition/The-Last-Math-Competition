# 00000007846

**English.** Definition: Two-choice load balancing: n balls each independently and uniformly choose two bins and are placed in the lighter one, with maximum load M(n,n). Conjecture: The limit law of M(n,n) minus log n/log log n is a Gumbel-type shifted law with location parameter c* = log 3 (the three-bin constant difference correction); and the tail of P(M(n,n) >= ceil(log n/log log n) + k) decays superexponentially as (e^{-k} k!)^{-1}, strictly separated from the double-exponential tail of the single-choice model. (two-choice Gumbel load law)

**中文。** 定义：two choices 负载均衡：n 个球依次独立均匀选两箱并放入较轻者，最大负载 M(n,n)。猜想：M(n,n)-log n/log log n 的极限律为 Gumbel 型位移律，其位置参数为 c*=log 3（三桶常数差分修正）；且 P(M(n,n)≥⌈log n/log log n⌉+k) 的尾衰减为 (e^{-k}·k!)^{-1} 型超指数，与单选择模型的 double-exponential 尾严格分离。
