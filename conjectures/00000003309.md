# 00000003309

**SF2-29 ★** — source: 95_特殊函数论II.md

**English.** Definition: Appell functions: two-variable hypergeometrics. Conjecture: The reduction system of Appell: the reduction relations of the four Appell functions are integral kernels of the univariate ₂F₁, with the explicit kernels Euler-type integrals. (Appell Euler integral kernel reductions)

**中文。** 定义：Appell 函数：两变量超几何。猜想：Appell 的约化系：四 Appell 函数的约化关系为单变量 ₂F₁ 的积分核且核的显式为 Euler 型积分。（Appell Euler 积分核约化系）
