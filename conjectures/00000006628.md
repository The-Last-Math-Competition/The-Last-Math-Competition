# 00000006628

**English.** Definition: Reflection positivity with the instability-window structure of mirror coupling. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the instability window and the mirror-coupling sign gives the window; the endpoints close and the verification of closure is sign flipping; and reflection positivity breaks outside the window. (mirror-coupling instability-window conversion)

**中文。** 定义：反射正性：镜像耦合的失稳窗结构。猜想：换算常数为失稳窗的显式且镜像耦合符号给出窗口；端点封闭且封闭的验证为符号翻转；窗外的反射正性破坏。（镜像耦合失稳窗换算）
