# 00000007293

**English.** Definition: The Nekrasov-Okounkov identity with partitions and random matrices. Conjecture: The product identity of partition sums holds, and the kernel of the identity is the fluctuation of hook lengths. (hook-fluctuation Nekrasov-Okounkov kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Nekrasov–Okounkov 恒等式：分拆与随机矩阵。猜想：恒等式的核：分拆和的乘积恒等式且式的核为 hook 长度的波动。（Nekrasov-Okounkov hook 波动分拆乘积恒等式核）
