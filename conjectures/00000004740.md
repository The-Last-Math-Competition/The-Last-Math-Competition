# 00000004740

**English.** Definition: Twisted K groups and stack equivalence are two layers of invariants. Conjecture: There exist two gerbes whose twisted K groups are all isomorphic yet which are not stack equivalent, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of stacks with equal homology but different homotopy. (twisted K isomorphism-stack equivalence separation)

**中文。** 定义：扭曲 K 群与扭等价指两层不变量。猜想：存在两 gerbe 的一切扭曲 K 群同构而非扭等价,分离由同调不同伦的显式叠对实现。（扭 K 同构-叠等价分离）
