# 00000008108

**English.** Definition: The invariants of Morita equivalence of block algebras of fusion systems: the complete set MI(F) of standard invariants of Morita equivalence classes. Conjecture: MI(F) is completely determined by the triple (defect group fusion type, inertial quotient, block fixed-point count) (a triple complete criterion); the count of different realizations of the same triple (non-equivalent blocks with the same triple) is at most 2 (a two-realization upper bound); examples of double realization are given by two non-equivalent exotic systems (a two-block exotic realization), the prime restriction on realizations being p in {2,3} (a two-prime realization restriction). (Morita triple two-realization bound)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的块代数的 Morita 等价的指标：Morita 等价类的标准不变量的完全组 MI(F)。猜想：MI(F) 由（亏群融合型、惯性商、块不动点数）三元组完全决定（三元完全判定）；且三元组的不同实现的计数（同一三元的非等价块数）≤ 2（双实现上界）；实现 2 的块的例子由两个非等价的 exotic 系给出（双块 exotic 实现），实现的存在的素数限制为 p ∈{2,3}（双素实现限制）。
