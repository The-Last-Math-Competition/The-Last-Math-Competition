# 00000008563

**NSP-03 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: Aronszajn–Krein theory of rank one perturbation A+α⟨ ·,φ⟩φ of self-adjoint operator A. Conjecture: perturbed point is integral diverges (Aronszajn); of spectral measure of perturbation to quantity and the finite rank Kato functional is (Kato functional); trace ccritical constant of lass infinite rank perturbed Kato functional can diverges, spectrum type mutation (trace class mutation); mutation is reciprocal (trace norm reciprocal) of trace norm.

**中文。** 定义：自伴算子 A 的秩一扰动 A+α⟨·,φ⟩φ 的 Aronszajn–Krein 理论。猜想：扰动的纯点性判据为扰动向量的谱测度的积分发散（Aronszajn 判据）；且该判据推广到有限秩时以 Kato 泛函为准（Kato 泛函律）；迹类无穷秩扰动的 Kato 泛函可发散，产生谱型突变（迹类突变律）；突变的临界常数为迹范数的倒数（迹范数倒数律）。
