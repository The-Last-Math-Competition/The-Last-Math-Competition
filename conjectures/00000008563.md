# 00000008563

**English.** Definition: The Aronszajn-Krein theory of rank-one perturbations A + alpha<.,phi>phi of a self-adjoint operator A. Conjecture: The criterion for pure point spectrum of the perturbation is divergence of the integral of the spectral measure of the perturbation vector (the Aronszajn criterion); the extension to finite rank is governed by the Kato functional (a Kato functional law); for trace-class infinite-rank perturbations the Kato functional may diverge, producing spectral-type transitions (a trace-class transition law); and the critical constant of the transition is the reciprocal of the trace norm (a trace norm reciprocal law). (Aronszajn-Krein Kato trace-class transition)

**中文。** 定义：自伴算子 A 的秩一扰动 A+α⟨·,φ⟩φ 的 Aronszajn–Krein 理论。猜想：扰动的纯点性判据为扰动向量的谱测度的积分发散（Aronszajn 判据）；且该判据推广到有限秩时以 Kato 泛函为准（Kato 泛函律）；迹类无穷秩扰动的 Kato 泛函可发散，产生谱型突变（迹类突变律）；突变的临界常数为迹范数的倒数（迹范数倒数律）。
