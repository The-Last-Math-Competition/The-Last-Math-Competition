# 00000004410

**GL2-20 △** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: The sampling distribution is the distribution of random sampling with a fixed number of windows, and the entropy rate is the limit of the per-window entropy. Conjecture: There exist two graphons whose finite-window sampling distributions all coincide while their entropy rates differ, with an explicit positive constant difference. (sampling distribution-entropy rate separation)

**中文。** 定义：采样分布指固定窗数的随机采样分布,熵率指其逐窗熵极限。猜想：存在两个 graphon 其一切有限窗采样分布相同但熵率不同,熵率差为显式正常数。（采样分布-熵率分离）
