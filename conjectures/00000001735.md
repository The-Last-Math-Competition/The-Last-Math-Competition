# 00000001735

**DG-15 ★** — source: 43_丢番图几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The extremum of the solution count N(k) of the Thue equation X³ − 2Y³ = k. Conjecture: max_k N(k) = 12; the list of k attaining 12 is finite (exhausted by the unit-group torsion condition of Thue, giving an explicit list).

**中文。** 定义：Thue 方程 X³−2Y³=k 的解数 N(k) 的极值。猜想：max_k N(k)=12;达到 12 的 k 的列表为有限(由 Thue 的单位群扭转条件穷举,给出显式列表)。
