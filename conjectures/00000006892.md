**English.** Definition: The rank formula is the dimension count of generic ranks. Conjecture: The full-measure domain of the maximal rank of general tensors has an explicit interval of length one; and the full-measure property is controlled by the complement codimension of a Zariski open set. (full-measure interval of maximal rank)
