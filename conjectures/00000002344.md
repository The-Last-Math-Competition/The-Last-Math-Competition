**English.** Definition: Optimized polynomial systems refer to the real-solution structure under relaxation. Conjecture: The threshold for arc-connectedness of real solutions is always 1/(2d) of the relaxation parameter (d the constraint degree); and exactly two branches merge at the threshold. (arc-connectedness threshold 1/2d)

**中文。** 定义：优化多项式系统指松弛化的实解结构。猜想：实解弧连通的阈值恒为松弛参数的二 d 分之一（d 为约束度数）；且阈值上恰两分支合并。（弧连通阈值 1/2d）
