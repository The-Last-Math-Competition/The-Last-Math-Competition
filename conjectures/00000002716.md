# 00000002716

**English.** Definition: Alexander duality: the homological duality of geometric realizations of complexes. Conjecture: The discrete quantitative duality: the deviation of the Betti-number equality under deletion of subcomplexes is a torsion correction of local chain groups; the correction is the depth genus of the deletion, computed by the Reese recursion. (Alexander duality torsion correction)

**中文。** 定义：Alexander 对偶：复形的几何实现的同调对偶。猜想：对偶的离散定量：同调对偶的 Betti 数等式在删除子复形下的偏差为局部链群的挠修正；修正项为 deletion 的深度亏格且亏格由 Reese 递推计算。（Alexander 对偶挠修正）
