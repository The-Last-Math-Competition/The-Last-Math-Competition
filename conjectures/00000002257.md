# 00000002257

**English.** Definition: A Julia direction is a ray direction of fastest growth. Conjecture: For entire functions of order ρ, the minimal number of Julia directions is 1 (known), and the asymptotic uniformity of their distribution holds if and only if ρ has denominator singularity (Julia direction distribution).

**中文。** 定义：Julia 方向(增长最快的射线方向)。猜想：级 ρ 的整函数的 Julia 方向的个数最小为 1(已知),其分布的渐近均匀性 ⟺ ρ 的分母奇性(Julia 方向分布)。
