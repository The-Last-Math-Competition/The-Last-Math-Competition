# 00000007985

**English.** Definition: The failure probability exponent of rateless recovery of fountain codes: the decoding failure probability P_f(k,Omega) of LT/Raptor codes (k message symbols, degree distribution Omega). Conjecture: The exponent of P_f at the optimal degree distribution Omega* is -k delta(Omega), delta(Omega) being the analytic entropy of the corrected ideal soliton distribution; the critical window of P_f (the covering phase transition) has width O(k^{1/3}) (consistent with the 2-core transition of random hypergraphs); and the extra symbol count for recovery at overhead epsilon is Theta(log k) (the precision of And-Or tree solutions), with constant an explicit multiple of the Euler constant gamma. (fountain soliton window exponent)

**中文。** 定义：闪码（fountain code）的无速率恢复的失败概率指数：LT/Raptor 码的译码失败概率 P_f(k,Ω)（k 个消息符号，度分布 Ω）。猜想：最优度分布 Ω* 的 P_f 的指数为 -k·δ(Ω)，δ(Ω) 为理想孤子分布的修正的解析熵；且 P_f 的临界窗（覆盖相变）宽度为 O(k^{1/3})（随机超图 2-核相变的一致）； overhead ε 的恢复的额外符号数为 Θ(log k)（And-Or 树的解的精度），常数为 Euler 常数 γ 的显式倍数。
