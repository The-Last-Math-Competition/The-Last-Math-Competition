**English.** Definition: Sliced Wasserstein distance: projection distances of persistence diagrams. Conjecture: The metric equivalence constants between sliced Wasserstein and bottleneck distances are explicit: SW at least c times bottleneck and SW at most C times bottleneck times the logarithm of bar count; the constant c is the infimum of the first moment of projection measures and is tight. (SW-bottleneck equivalence constants)

**中文。** 定义：切片 Wasserstein 距离：持久图的投影距离。猜想：切片 Wasserstein 与瓶颈距离的度量的等价常数为显式：SW ≥ c·瓶颈且 SW ≤ C·瓶颈乘条数的对数；常数 c 为投影测度的第一矩的下确界且为紧。（SW-瓶颈等价常数）
