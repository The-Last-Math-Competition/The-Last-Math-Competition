# 00000008033

**English.** Definition: The tame spectrum of Fourier analysis of definable groups: the growth rate R(U) of the dimension spectrum d_pi of the Peter-Weyl decomposition of a definable compact group U (a unitary group over a definable field). Conjecture: R(U) is polynomial (polynomial control of the dimension spectrum of definable representations) if and only if the rank of the Lie-ization of U is constant; the multiplicities of occurrences of definable representations are uniformly bounded (multiplicity uniformity); and the error term of the definable Weyl formula is controlled by the Fourier decay of definable measures (definable Rajchman property), with decay rate N(lambda)^{-1/2} (a definable realization of random-matrix-type decay). (definable Peter-Weyl polynomial spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：可定义群的傅里叶分析的驯谱：可定义紧群 U（可定义域上的酉群）的 Peter–Weyl 分解的维度谱 d_π 的增长率 R(U)。猜想：R(U) 为多项式（可定义表示的维度谱的多项式控制）当且仅当 U 的 Lie 化的秩为常数；且可定义表示的出现的重数一致有界（重数一致性）；谱的 Weyl 公式的可定义化的误差项由可定义测度的 Fourier 衰减（可定义 Rajchman 性）控制，衰减率为 N(λ)^{-1/2}（随机矩阵型衰减的可定义实现）。
