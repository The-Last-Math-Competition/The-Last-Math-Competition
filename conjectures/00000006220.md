# 00000006220

**English.** Definition: The equilibrium spectrum and the transport spectrum are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two equations with the same equilibrium spectrum but different transport spectra, and the separation is realized explicitly by the same equilibrium with different resonance configurations. (same equilibrium-different resonance transport separation)

**中文。** 定义：平衡谱与输运谱指两层。猜想：存在两方程的平衡谱相同而输运谱不同,分离由同平衡异共振配置的显式实现。（平衡谱同-输运谱异共振分离）
