# 00000001679

**English.** Definition: The acquaintance time a(G) is the minimal number of rounds of adjacent swaps after which everyone has met everyone. Conjecture: a(G) ≤ 2^{10}·n/log n; the constant cannot be improved to o(n/log n) (by the random-graph lower bound).

**中文。** 定义：相识时间 a(G) 指经逐轮相邻交换使全体两两相识的最少轮数。猜想：a(G)≤2^{10}·n/log n;常数不可改进至 o(n/log n)(由随机图的下界)。
