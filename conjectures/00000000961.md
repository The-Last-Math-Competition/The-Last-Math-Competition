# 00000000961

**English.** Definition: The Edgeworth expansion of the free central limit theorem is the series of correction terms to the semicircular approximation. Conjecture: The third-cumulant correction coefficient is κ₃ = √2 (the ratio of the third free cumulant to the third classical cumulant), the k-th expansion coefficient is given explicitly by the Möbius function of noncrossing pairings, and the series has radius of convergence 2.

**中文。** 定义：自由中心极限定理的 Edgeworth 展开指半圆分布逼近的修正项级数。猜想：第三累积量修正系数 κ₃ = √2(即第三自由累积量与第三经典累积量之比为 √2),且第 k 展开项的系数由非交叉配对的 Möbius 函数显式给出;该级数收敛半径为 2。
