# 00000001369

**DS-09 ★** — source: 31_描述集合论与递归论.md

**English.** Definition: Hjorth turbulence is the topological oscillation property of orbits of group actions in symbol spaces; it obstructs complete invariants for Borel reducibility. Conjecture: Non-turbulence of S_∞ actions has an explicit algebraic criterion: the action is non-turbulent if and only if the stabilizer tower of the orbit closures stabilizes at a finite level; the criterion depends only on the pointwise stabilizer chains of the orbits.

**中文。** 定义：Hjorth 湍流指群作用的轨道在符号空间中的拓扑振荡性质,它是 Borel 归约的完备不变量的障碍。猜想：S_∞ 作用的非湍流性有显式代数判据:作用非湍流当且仅当其轨道闭包的 stabilize 塔在有限层稳定;判据仅依赖轨道的逐点稳定子群链。
