# 00000005276

**English.** Definition: Anomalous dissipation means the dissipation does not vanish as viscosity goes to zero. Conjecture: The anomalous-dissipation exponent lower bound of passive scalars is one third, given by an explicit construction of quasi-self-similar flows, and the exponent cannot be raised. (dissipation exponent lower bound of passive scalars)

**中文。** 定义：反常耗散指粘性消失下耗散不趋零。猜想：被动标量的反常耗散指数下界为三分之一,由准自相似流的显式构造给出且指数不可再升。（被动标量耗散指数下界）
