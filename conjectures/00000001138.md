# 00000001138

**English.** Definition: The MV-cycles are the geometric basis of affine Grassmannian homology under geometric Satake equivalence. Conjecture: The multiplication structure constants of MV-cycles are always nonnegative; and their degeneration limit equals the structure constants of the algebra of class functions of the symmetric group. (MV-cycle nonnegative structure)

**中文。** 定义：MV-环量为几何 Satake 等价下仿射格拉斯曼同调的几何基。猜想：MV-环量乘法结构常数恒非负；且退化极限等于对称群类函数代数的结构常数。（MV 环量非负结构）
