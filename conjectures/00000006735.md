**English.** Definition: The finitistic dimension is the uniform bound of projective dimensions. Conjecture: The linear-type bound is attained in the quiver class and the slope of the bound is the proxy dimension; the optimality of the slope is by the monomial order. (linear-type finitistic-dimension bound)
