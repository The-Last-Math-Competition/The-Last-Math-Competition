# 00000008373

**English.** Definition: The descent of tropical vector bundles: the index trop_desc of the descent (translation invariance) of tropical vector bundles (piecewise-linear sheaves). Conjecture: trop_desc (the complete criterion for descent of tropical bundles) is the global nature of the covariance constant (a global covariance criterion law); the complete invariant classifying descended bundles is the image of the metric function (an image classification law); the minimal rank of non-descending bundles is 2 (a rank-two example); and the criterion for slope stability of bundles (maximal denominator of degree/rank) has maximal denominator at most 2 (a double denominator upper bound law). (tropical bundle descent covariance stability)

**中文。** 定义：热带向量丛的下降：热带向量丛（分片线性层）的下降（平移不变）的指标 trop_desc。猜想：trop_desc（热带丛的下降的完全判据）为协变常数的整体（整体协变判据律）；且丛的分类（下降丛的分类）的完备不变量为度规函数的像（像分类律）；非下降丛的最小秩为 2（秩二例）；丛的稳定（斜率稳定）的判据（度/秩 的极大分母）的极大分母 ≤ 2（双分母上界律）。
