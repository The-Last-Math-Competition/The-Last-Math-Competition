# 00000007679

**English.** Definition: For the Euler product (z;q)_infinity, the partial sums are S_N(z)=sum (-1)^n q^{n(n-1)/2} [N choose n]_q z^n. Conjecture: For each 0<q<1, the zero set of S_N(z) converges as N grows to a quasicircle Jordan curve whose shape-parameter envelope is controlled by the two points z=-q^{-N} and z=q^{-N}, and the conformal scaling constant of the quasicircle equals 1+2q. (quasicircle limit of partial q-product sums)

**中文。** 定义：Euler 乘积函数 (z;q)_∞=Π_{k≥0}(1-z q^k) 的部分和 S_N(z)=Σ_{n=0}^{N} (-1)^n q^{n(n-1)/2} [N choose n]_q z^n。猜想：对每个 0<q<1，S_N(z) 的零点集当 N→∞ 时收敛到一条拟圆 Jordan 曲线，其 Haskell 形状参数包络由 z=-q^{-N} 与 z=q^{-N} 两点控制的极限给出，且该拟圆的共形缩放常数等于 1+2q。
