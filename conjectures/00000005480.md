**English.** Definition: The pole structure and the bootstrap structure are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two scatterings with the same pole spectrum but different bootstrap-constraint solutions, and the separation is realized by an explicit sign-flip pair of residues. (same poles-different bootstrap sign separation)
