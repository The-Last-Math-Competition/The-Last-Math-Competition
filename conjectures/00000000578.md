**CA-78 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: The binomial matroid bin(n,i) is given by the i-subsets of {1,…,n} under symmetric difference; let χ_{n,i} denote its characteristic polynomial. Conjecture: For all 2 ≤ i ≤ n−2 and integers m ≥ 1, χ_{n,i}(−m) is divisible by i!·(n−i)!, and the quotient is a nonnegative combination of Eulerian numbers.

**中文。** 定义：二项拟阵 bin(n,i) 指由 {1,...,n} 的 i-子集按对称差给出的拟阵,其特征多项式记 χ_{n,i}。猜想：对一切 2≤i≤n−2 与整数 m≥1,χ_{n,i}(−m) 能被 i!·(n−i)! 整除,且商为 Eulerian 数的非负组合。
