**English.** Definition: Spheroidal wave functions: separation in ellipsoidal coordinates. Conjecture: The analytic continuation of spheroidals: the continuation of prolate and oblate functions is an analytic function of the parameter, with the branch points of the continuation the eigenvalue coincidence layers. (spheroidal continuation branch coincidence layer)
