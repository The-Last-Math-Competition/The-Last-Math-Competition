# 00000000798

**PA-98 △** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: The Hasse invariant is the local sign of a quadratic form. Conjecture: The Hasse invariants of random quadratic forms are equidistributed on ±1; independence of the distribution comes from the canonical-form coefficients; and the convergence rate of equidistribution is an inverse power of the dimension. (Hasse invariant equidistribution)

**中文。** 定义：Hasse 不变量为二次型的局部符号。猜想：随机二次型的 Hasse 不变量在 ±1 上等分布；且分布的独立性由典范型系数。；且等分布的收敛速率为维数的逆幂。（Hasse 不变量等分布）
