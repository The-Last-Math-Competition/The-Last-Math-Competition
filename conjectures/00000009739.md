**English.** Definition: Autoformalization: machine translation accuracy from natural-language mathematics to proof assistant languages. Conjecture: The semantic correctness rate of formalization translation decreases in segments with the logical complexity of the text, each segment dropping a fixed percentage; segment boundaries sit at the three complexity jumps of new definitions, diagrams, and higher-order induction. (formalization segmented correctness rate)
