**CG4-23 ★** — source: 122_组合几何II_配置空间.md

**English.** Definition: Formality means that homology determines the rational homotopy type, with Massey products as the obstruction. Conjecture: The configuration space of a graph is formal if and only if the graph is a tree, the first non-formal graph is K_{2,3}, and its shortest nontrivial Massey product has length 3. (formality dichotomy for graph configurations)

**中文。** 定义：形式性指同调决定有理同伦型,Massey 积为其障碍。猜想：图配置空间形式当且仅当图为树,首非形式图为 K_{2,3},其最小非平凡 Massey 积长度为 3。（图配置形式性二分）
