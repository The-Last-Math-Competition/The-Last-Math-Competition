# 00000004625

**English.** Definition: A free-boundary minimal surface is a minimal surface meeting the sphere at right angles. Conjecture: Free-boundary minimal surfaces of genus three with three boundary components exist in the unit ball, constructed from threefold-symmetric explicit Weierstrass data. (three-three construction of free-boundary surfaces)

**中文。** 定义：自由边界极小曲面指与球面交成直角的极小曲面。猜想：单位球内亏格三边界三的自由边界极小曲面存在,由三重对称的显式 Weierstrass 数据构造。（自由边界三三行构造）
