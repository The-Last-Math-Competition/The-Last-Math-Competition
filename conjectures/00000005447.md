**English.** Definition: Partial quotients are the successive terms of the continued fraction. Conjecture: The lower bound of the total bit length of the partial quotients of a quadratic irrational is explicit of log-linear type, controlled by the number of prime factors of the discriminant, and the bound is tight. (prime-factor lower bound of quadratic partial quotients)

**中文。** 定义：部分商指连分式的逐项。猜想：二次无理数的部分商位长总和下界为对数线性型显式,由判别式素因子数控制且界紧。（二次部分商素因子下界）
