**English.** Definition: Elliptic logarithms: linear independence of elliptic integral representations of algebraic points on an elliptic curve E (elliptic Baker theory). Conjecture: Lower bounds for linear forms in elliptic logarithms have explicit constants: the absolute value of a linear form with coefficient height B is at least exp(-C log B), with exponent 1 optimal; optimality is attained explicitly by constructions of near-congruent point sequences. (elliptic logarithm explicit lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：椭圆对数：椭圆曲线 E 上代数点的椭圆积分表示的线性无关性（椭圆 Baker 理论）。猜想：椭圆对数线性型的下界具有显式常数：系数高度 B 的线性型绝对值 ≥ exp(−C(log B)) 且指数 1 为最优；最优性由近同余点列的构造显式达到。（椭圆对数显式下界）
