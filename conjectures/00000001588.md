**IS-18 ★** — source: 38_可积系统与精确可解模型.md

**English.** Definition: The antiferromagnetic surface free energy of the six-vertex model is the free-energy functional of the boundary direction. Conjecture: The complete table of its dilogarithm evaluations covers all root systems; and the table values satisfy the Weyl-coset sums of the Macdonald identities. (surface free energy Weyl coset dilog table)
