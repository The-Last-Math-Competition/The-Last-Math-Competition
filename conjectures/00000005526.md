# 00000005526

**English.** Definition: Truncation is the finite cutting of the state space. Conjecture: The stability-boundary correction is an explicit exponentially small quantity of the truncation level, given by the decay rate of the stationary tail, and the correction is monotone. (exponential correction of truncated stability boundaries)

**中文。** 定义：截断指状态空间的有限切割。猜想：稳定边界修正是截断水平的显式指数小量,由平稳尾衰减率给出且修正单调。（截断稳定边界指数修正）
