**English.** Definition: Forni's deviation theorem gives subexponential bounds on the growth of cohomology classes along geodesics; the exact exponentiated deviation spectrum versus negative Lyapunov exponents is not fully quantified. Conjecture: The deviation exponent spectrum is explicitly given by piecewise-linear combinations lambda_{2g-k} of negative KZ exponents; and the distribution of the deviation functional along the flow converges to an explicit infinite-dimensional Gaussian field whose covariance operator is the compactified inverse of the Hodge star action. (Forni deviation exact spectrum)
