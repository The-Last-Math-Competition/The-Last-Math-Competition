# 00000008128

**NAG-18 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The rationality criterion of birational invariants of noncommutative varieties: the categorical criterion rat(A) for nc rationality. Conjecture: rat(A) holds if and only if D^b(qgr-A) has a full exceptional sequence and the rank of K_0 equals the generator count (an exceptional-rational equivalence); the dimension of the obstruction to rationality (the noncommutative intermediate Jacobian) is the dimension of HH^1 (a Jacobian-HH^1 dimension law); and the criterion for algebraicity of the Abelian variety type centralization of the obstruction (algebraicity of the intermediate Jacobian) is a signature splitting (a signature Abelian criterion), the rigidity of the criterion being verified on quartic hyperbolic varieties. (nc rationality exceptional Jacobian criterion)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的双有理不变量的有理性判据：nc 有理性的范畴判据 rat(A)。猜想：rat(A) 当且仅当 D^b(qgr-A) 有满例外序列且 K_0 的秩 = 生成数（例外-有理等价）；且有理性的障碍（非交换中间 Jacobian）的维数为 HH¹ 的维数（Jacobian-HH¹ 维数律）；障碍的 Abel 簇型的中心化（中间 Jacobian 的代数性）的判据为符号差分裂（符号差 Abel 判据），判据的刚性由 quartic 双曲簇验证。
