**English.** Definition: 2-representation theory is the representation theory of higher layers. Conjecture: At rank two it is given by the transversal count of conjugacy classes with automorphism pairs, closed, and the combination of the count is the orbital integral. (transversal conjugacy counting of 2-representations)
