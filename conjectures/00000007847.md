**RAM-07 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: independent rounding row number of r(A) (A of LP relaxation of approximate threshold: integer programming max{cx: Ax≤b, x∈{0,1}^n} of random rounding and function) of sequence number. Conjecture: exact characterizes of expectation of r(A) is 1-(1-1/m)^m (m is constraint number), and of deterministic rounding does not exceed in m=2 when of 1-1/e; randomization is zero, m≥3 when strictly is positive and increasing tends to e^{-1}.

**中文。** 定义：随机舍入的近似阈值：整数规划 max{cx: Ax≤b, x∈{0,1}^n} 的 LP 松弛的独立舍入比值 r(A)（A 的行数与列数的函数）。猜想：r(A) 的期望的精确刻画为 1-(1-1/m)^m（m 为约束数），且任何确定性舍入的比值不超过 1-1/e；随机化优势的分离在 m=2 时为零，m≥3 时严格为正且递增趋于 e^{-1}。
