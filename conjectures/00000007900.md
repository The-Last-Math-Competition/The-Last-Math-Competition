# 00000007900

**HCA-30 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The exponential series of perfect complexes (a categorified Dedekind eta): the categorified eta_C defined by zeta-normalization of the characters of the Serre functor, of type sum dim K_0(Perf_X) q^n. Conjecture: For a CY_n category C, eta_C is a quasimodular form of weight n/2; and the modular anomaly of eta_C (the phase under modular transformations) equals the global sign correction (-1)^{sum mu_i} of the counting invariants of C (mu_i the BPS multicover signs); and the case n = 1 recovers the classical Dedekind eta (realized by Perf of an elliptic curve) with the standard SL_2(Z) action. (categorified Dedekind eta modularity)

**中文。** 定义：完美复形的指数级数（categorified Dedekind η）：Σ dim K_0(Perf_X)·q^{n} 型级数的范畴化 η_C，由 Serre 函子的特征标的 zeta 正规化定义。猜想：对 CY_n 范畴 C，η_C 为权 n/2 的（拟）模形式；且 η_C 的模反常（模性变换的相位）等于 C 的计数不变量的整体符号修正 (−1)^{Σ μ_i}（μ_i 为 BPS 多重覆盖符号）；n=1 情形回到经典 Dedekind η（由椭圆曲线的 Perf 实现），模群为 SL₂(Z) 的标准作用。
