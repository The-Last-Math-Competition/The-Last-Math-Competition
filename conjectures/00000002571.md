**English.** Definition: The matrix-rank bound of communication complexity. Conjecture: An explicit form of the log-rank conjecture: D(f) ≤ c·log²rank (c = 16); the exponent 2 is minimal (i.e. the pure log conjecture is false). (log-rank conjecture explicit quadratic)
