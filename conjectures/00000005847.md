**English.** Definition: Equivalence with fusion is the enhanced categorical equivalence. Conjecture: At rank three it is given by the irreducibility of complexes, closed, and the combination of irreducibility is the direct summand. (irreducible direct-summand structure of fusion equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：带融合的等价为强化范畴等价。猜想：秩三时由复形的不可约性给出且封闭；且不可约的组合为直和项。（带融合复形不可约直和）
