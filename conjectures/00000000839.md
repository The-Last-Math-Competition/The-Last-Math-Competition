**English.** Definition: Quadratic-cost optimal transport maps ∇u (Brenier). Conjecture: The exponent α in u ∈ C^{1,α} is determined exactly by the regularity of the density ratio, with upper limit 1/(3n−1) (the exact value of the optimal exponent).

**中文。** 定义：二次代价最优输运映射 ∇u(Brenier)。猜想：u ∈ C^{1,α} 的 α 由密度比的正则性精确决定,且 α 的上限为 1/(3n−1)(最优指数的确切值)。
