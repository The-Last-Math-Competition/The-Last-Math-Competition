# 00000009753

**English.** Definition: Cost separation of discovery and verification: two complexity types of machine-discovered mathematical facts. Conjecture: The upper bound of the ratio of discovery cost to verification cost is logarithmic; the separation is tight when the search space is an explicitly measured subset, given by the self-similar scaling of evidence trees. (discovery-verification logarithmic separation)

**中文。** 定义：发现与验证的成本分离：机器发现数学事实的两类复杂度。猜想：数学对象发现的计算成本与验证成本之比的上界为对数型；该分离在搜索空间为显式度量子集时紧致且由证据树的自相似缩放给出。（发现-验证对数分离）
