# 00000004647

**English.** Definition: A W algebra is a Drinfeld-Sokolov reduction vertex algebra. Conjecture: The number of irreducible representations of a negative-level W algebra is an explicit formula in the number of weight-zero orbits, and the formula agrees with the categorical classification and holds for all ranks. (irreducible count of negative-level W algebras)

**中文。** 定义：W 代数指 Drinfeld–Sokolov 归约顶点代数。猜想：负级 W 代数的不可约表示个数为权零轨道数的显式公式,公式与分类范畴化一致且对一切秩成立。（负级 W 代数不可约计数）
