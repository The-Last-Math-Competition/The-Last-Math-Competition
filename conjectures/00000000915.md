**English.** Definition: The Blackadar accordion is a continuous function of the projection semigroup. Conjecture: The projection structure of tensor products C(X) ⊗ (Cuntz algebra) over compact Hausdorff spaces is completely controlled by the covering dimension; the accordion list closes at dimension two; and at dimension one the control degenerates to the classical accordion identity. (projection structure covering-dimension control)

**中文。** 定义：Blackadar 手风琴为投影半格的连续函数。猜想：紧豪斯多夫空间上 C(X) 与 Cuntz 代数张量积的投影结构由空间的覆盖维数完全控制；且手风琴列表在维数二封闭；且该控制在一维时退化为经典的手风琴恒等式。（投影结构覆盖维数控制）
