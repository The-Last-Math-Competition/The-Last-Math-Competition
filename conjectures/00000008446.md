# 00000008446

**KH3-06 ★** — source: 267_纽结不变量与低维拓扑III.md

**English.** Definition: An m-move is a generalized Reidemeister operation changing m parallel strands simultaneously. Conjecture: The set of m-move equivalence classes is finite if and only if m is at most 3; the number of classes at m = 3 is given by evaluations of the HOMFLY-PT polynomial at explicit values; and for m at least 4 the class spectrum is infinite and discrete. (boundary of m-move equivalence classes)

**中文。** 定义：m-move 指将 m 股平行弧同时变化的广义 Reidemeister 操作。猜想：m-move 等价类有限当且仅当 m ≤ 3；m=3 的类数由 HOMFLY-PT 多项式在显式值的赋值给出；m ≥ 4 时类谱无限离散。（m-move 等价类分界）
