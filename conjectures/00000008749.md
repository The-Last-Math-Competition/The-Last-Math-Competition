# 00000008749

**English.** Definition: Homotopy invariance of K-theory: K_i(R) is isomorphic to K_i(R[t]). Conjecture: Invariance holds for regular Noetherian rings (a regularity law); Weibel's characterization holds for rings of dimension below 2 (a Weibel law); the minimal dimension of counterexamples is 2 for simple rings (a counterexample law); and there is the behavior for noncommutative polynomial rings (a behavior law). (homotopy invariance regular Weibel counterexample)

**中文。** 定义：K 理论的同伦不变性：K_i(R)≅K_i(R[t])。猜想：不变性对正则 Noether 环成立（正则律）；且对维数小于 2 的环的 Weibel 刻画（Weibel 律）；反例的最小维数为 2 的单环（反例律）；非交换多项式环的行为（行为律）。
