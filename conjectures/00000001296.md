# 00000001296

**English.** Definition: The aliquot sequence is the iteration of s(n) = σ(n) − n. Conjecture: Every aliquot sequence is bounded or eventually periodic (Catalan–Dickson); moreover, if an unbounded (diverging) sequence exists, its smallest starting point exceeds 10^{10}, with the lower bound given by congruence-sieve structure outside the 276 family.

**中文。** 定义：aliquot 序列 s(n)=σ(n)−n 的迭代。猜想：一切 aliquot 序列有界或最终周期(Catalan–Dickson);进一步,发散(无界增长)序列若存在,其最小起点 > 10^{10},且该下界由 276 族以外的同余筛法结构给出。
