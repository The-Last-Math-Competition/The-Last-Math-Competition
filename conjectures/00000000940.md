# 00000000940

**English.** Definition: The Gordon–Lewis constant measures the obstruction to unconditional structure. Conjecture: The GL constant of projective tensor products has an explicit exponential lower bound in p; and the exponent saturates as p tends to two. (GL constant explicit exponent lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：Gordon–Lewis 常数度量无条件结构的障碍。猜想：投影张量积的 GL 常数的下界为 p 的显式指数；且指数在 p 趋于二时饱和。（GL 常数显式指数下界）
