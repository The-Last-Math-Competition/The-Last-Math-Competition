# 00000008158

**English.** Definition: The convergence domain of rigorous solutions of the Polchinski equation for effective interactions: polch_dom. Conjecture: polch_dom (the convergence domain of the rigorous RG) contains a neighborhood of the critical surface (a critical neighborhood inclusion law); the fractal dimension of the boundary of the domain is d_f = d + 1/2 (a boundary fractal law); the regularity radius of solutions inside the domain (analyticity) is a quadratic function of the scaling variable of the coupling (a quadratic analyticity radius); and the first singularity of the continuation outside the domain lies at negative coupling (a negative-coupling first singularity law), with position constant the positive root of a quadratic equation (a first-singularity equation). (Polchinski domain fractal quadratic radius)

**中文。** 定义：有效相互作用的 Polchinski 方程的严格解的收敛域 polch_dom。猜想：polch_dom（严格 RG 的收敛域）包含临界表面的邻域（临界邻域包含律）；且收敛域的边界的形状的分形维数为 d_f = d+1/2（边界分形律）；域内解的正则性（解析性）的半径为耦合的尺度变量的二次函数（二次解析半径）；域外的延拓（重整化迷向）的最小奇点的位置在负耦合（负耦合首奇点律），位置常数为 2 次多项式方程的正根（首奇点方程）。
