# 00000001758

**English.** Definition: The spin Kronecker coefficients are the expansion coefficients of spin tensor products of 2·S_n. Conjecture: Positivity for hook triples (λ, μ, ν) (all hooks) is completely characterized by the non-touching condition of the three two-row Young diagrams; otherwise it is zero; and the zero criterion is given by the contact points of the hook-boundary Young diagrams.

**中文。** 定义：自旋 Kronecker 系数指 2·S_n 的自旋张量积的展开系数。猜想：hook 三元组 (λ,μ,ν)(全 hook)的正性由三者的二元 Young 图的互不接触条件完全刻画;否则为零；且零判据由 hook 边界的 Young 图接触点给出。
