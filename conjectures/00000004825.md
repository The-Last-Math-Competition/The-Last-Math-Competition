# 00000004825

**English.** Definition: The nonzero density is the proportion of primes with nonzero eigenvalues. Conjecture: For CM-free forms the density of nonzero eigenvalues is one, with quantitative lower bound one minus the negative one-half power of the prime times an explicit constant, exactly matching Sato-Tate. (quantitative lower bound of nonzero eigenvalue density)

**中文。** 定义：非零密度指特征值非零的素数占比。猜想：无 CM 形式的非零特征值密度为一,定量下界为一减素数负二分之一次幂乘显式常数且与 Sato–Tate 精确匹配。（非零特征值密度定量下界）
