**English.** Definition: A holonomic module has Lagrangian characteristic variety. Conjecture: The upper bound of the number of irreducible components of the characteristic variety is an explicit polynomial of the characteristic number, controlled by the generation length, and attained at monomial modules. (generation-length upper bound of holonomic components)

**中文。** 定义：全纯模指特征簇为拉格朗日的模。猜想：特征簇的不可约分量数上界为示性数的显式多项式,由生成长度控制且单项式模处达到。（全holonomic 分量生成长度上界）
