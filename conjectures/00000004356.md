**English.** Definition: Lévy noise denotes independent increment noise of stable exponent α. Conjecture: can product threshold of its functional integral exists is α greater than d/2, threshold at three order moment diverges and heavy-tailed index critical. (Lévy can product threshold α greater than d/2)
