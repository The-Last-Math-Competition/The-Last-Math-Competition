**English.** Definition: Levy white noise is the independent-increment noise field of stability exponent alpha. Conjecture: The integrability threshold for the existence of its functional integrals is alpha greater than d/2; at the threshold the third moment diverges and the heavy-tail index is critical. (Levy integrability threshold alpha greater than d/2)

**中文。** 定义：Lévy 白噪声指稳定指数 α 的独立增量噪声场。猜想：其泛函积分存在的可积阈值为 α 大于 d/2,阈值处三阶矩发散且重尾指标临界。（Lévy 可积阈值 α 大于 d/2）
