# 00000001643

**HA-13 ★** — source: 40_同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The rank variety of an infinitesimal group scheme G is the projective variety of its support; the spectrum of the even cohomology ring is Spec H^{even}(G). Conjecture: For first-order infinitesimal group schemes G_{(1)}, the rank variety and Spec H^{even}(G_{(1)}) agree completely as sets and in topology; the agreement is verified at prime-power exceptions via the Friedlander–Parshall theorem.

**中文。** 定义：无穷小群方案 G 的秩簇指其支撑簇的射影簇;偶上同调环的谱指 Spec H^{even}(G)。猜想：一阶无穷小群方案 G_{(1)} 的秩簇与 Spec H^{even}(G_{(1)}) 作为集合与拓扑完全一致;一致性由 Friedlander–Parshall 的定理在素数次幂的例外处验证。
