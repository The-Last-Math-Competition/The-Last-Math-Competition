# 00000004608

**English.** Definition: A higher-codimension minimal surface is an area-minimizing submanifold of codimension at least two. Conjecture: It is fully regular in dimension at most five, the first singularity appears on an explicit cone-type variety in dimension six, and at codimension two the threshold shifts by one. (threshold shift of higher-codimension regularity)

**中文。** 定义：高余维极小曲面指余维至少为二的面积极小子流形。猜想：维数不超过五时全正则,首奇点出现在维数六的显式旋型簇,余维二时阈值平移一。（高余维正则阈值平移）
