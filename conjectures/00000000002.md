# 00000000002

**English.** Definition: A Beatty sequence is the integer sequence ⌊nα+β⌋. Conjecture: For every irrational α greater than one and every real β, the count of primes in the Beatty sequence satisfies the expected asymptotic density formula with an error term of logarithmic size. (Beatty prime asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：Beatty 序列指 ⌊nα+β⌋ 的整数序列。猜想：对每个无理 α 大于一与实数 β，Beatty 序列中的素数计数满足渐近密度的预期公式且误差为对数窗。（Beatty 素数渐近）
