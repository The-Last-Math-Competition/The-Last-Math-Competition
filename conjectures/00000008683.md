# 00000008683

**OTW-03 ★** — source: 275_最优传输与Wasserstein几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Schroedinger bridge: the coupling minimizing relative entropy (entropy-regularized transport). Conjecture: The convergence of the entropy regularization as epsilon tends to 0 has rate O(epsilon) (a linear rate law); the explicit constant of convergence is the Fisher information of the initial data (a Fisher constant law); the intermediate measure of the bridge evolves in Ornstein-Uhlenbeck type (an OU type law); and the explicit steady state is a Gaussian product (a Gaussian product law). (Schroedinger bridge linear Fisher OU steady state)

**中文。** 定义：Schrödinger 桥：相对熵极小的耦合（熵正则传输）。猜想：熵正则的收敛（ε→0 的传输图的收敛）的收敛速率 O(ε) 的（线性速率律）；且收敛的常数（常数的显式）为初值的 Fisher 信息的（Fisher 常数律）；桥的中间测度（边缘的演化）的 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 型（OU 型律）；OU 的稳态（稳态的显式）的高斯积（高斯积律）。
