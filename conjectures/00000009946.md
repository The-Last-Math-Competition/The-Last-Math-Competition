**English.** Definition: invariant measure of Borel density theorem: lattice in compact group on is Haar; quantity this open. Conjecture: quantity this: not approximate (convolution difference of Borel density less than ε) implies measure and difference distance of Haar by explicit power controls; power exponent of ε is reciprocal of rank of lattice and optimal. (quantity Borel density)
