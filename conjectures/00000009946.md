# 00000009946

**English.** Definition: Borel's density theorem: invariant measures of lattices on compact groups are Haar; quantitative versions are open. Conjecture: The quantitative Borel density theorem: approximate invariance (convolution difference below epsilon) implies the total variation distance to Haar is controlled by an explicit power of epsilon; the power exponent is the reciprocal of the lattice rank and is optimal. (quantitative Borel density)

**中文。** 定义：Borel 密度定理：格在紧群上的不变测度为 Haar；定量版本开放。猜想：Borel 密度的定量版本：不变性近似（卷积差小于 ε）蕴含测度与 Haar 的全变差距离由 ε 的显式幂控制；幂指数为格的秩的倒数且最优。（定量 Borel 密度）
