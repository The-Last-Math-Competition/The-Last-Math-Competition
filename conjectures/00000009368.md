**English.** Definition: Exchangeable martingales: the exchangeability of martingales. Conjecture: Exchangeable martingales: the exchangeability of martingale sequences, with the convergence of the exchange backward martingales. (exchangeable backward martingale convergence)

**中文。** 定义：可交换鞅：鞅的可交换。猜想：可交换鞅：鞅序列的可交换且换的收敛为倒向鞅。（可交换鞅倒向鞅鞅序列可交换收敛换）
