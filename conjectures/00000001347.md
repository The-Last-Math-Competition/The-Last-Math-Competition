# 00000001347

**English.** Definition: The half-integral weight coefficients a(n) are the Fourier coefficients under the Shimura correspondence. Conjecture: a(n) is always uniformly distributed among the admissible residue classes; and the deviation is controlled by the quadratic oscillation of Salié sums. (Waldspurger-side Salié deviation law)

**中文。** 定义：半整权系数 a(n) 指 Shimura 对应下的 Fourier 系数。猜想：a(n) 在允许剩余类中恒均匀分布；且偏差由 Salié 和的二次振荡控制。（Waldspurger 侧 Salié 偏差律）
