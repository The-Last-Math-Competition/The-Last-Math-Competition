# 00000002223

**English.** Definition: Amalgamated duplication is the doubled-copy ring of a prime ideal. Conjecture: The diameter of its zero-divisor graph is at most three; and the spectral condition on ideals attaining three is given by prime chains of length two. (duplicated ring zero-divisor graph diameter three)

**中文。** 定义：amalgamated duplication 指素理想的双副本环。猜想：其零因子图的直径不超过三；且达到三的理想谱条件由素链的长度二给出。（重复环零因子图直径三）
