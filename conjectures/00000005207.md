**English.** Definition: The gap between noncommutative and commutative rank is the difference of the two ranks of a tensor. Conjecture: Within order three (matrix size at most 3), the realizable values of the gap form the two-valued set {0,1}, each realized by explicit symbolic spaces; the minimal tensor size of gap 1 is exactly 3; and the gap is multiplicative under tensor products, with multiplicative deficit a power of 2. (two-valued symbolic realization of low-order gaps)

**中文。** 定义：非交换秩与交换秩的间隙：张量在两秩下的差值。猜想：三阶（矩阵规模不超过 3）以内间隙的可实现值为二值集 {0,1} 且每个值由显式符号空间实现；间隙 1 的最小张量规模恰为 3；间隙在张量积下乘性且乘性亏损为 2 的幂。（低阶间隙二值符号实现）
