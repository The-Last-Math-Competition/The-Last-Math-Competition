# 00000008849

**MON-19 ★** — source: 280_单调算子与算子分裂.md

**English.** Definition: Coupling of bimonotone systems: the structure of solution pairs of the simultaneous inclusion 0 in Ax + MBx coupling two maximal monotone operators A and B on Hilbert space through a coupling matrix M. Conjecture: Solution pairs of the coupled system always exist with solution set a complete lattice under the product order; the endpoint solutions of the lattice are epsilon-approximable in finitely many steps by monotone sweeping algorithms with iteration count at most O(log(1/epsilon)); and at the optimal permutation of the coupling matrix the overlap of the two component solutions is exactly the diameter of the common fixed point set. (bimonotone coupling lattice sweeping approximation)

**中文。** 定义：双单调系统的耦合：Hilbert 空间上两个极大单调算子 A、B 经耦合矩阵 M 联立的包含问题 0∈Ax+M Bx 的解对结构。猜想：耦合系统的解对恒存在且解集为乘积偏序下的完备格；格的端点解可由单调扫掠算法在有限步内 ε 逼近且迭代数不超过 O(log(1/ε))；耦合矩阵取最优置换时两分量解的重叠度恰为共同不动点集的直径。
