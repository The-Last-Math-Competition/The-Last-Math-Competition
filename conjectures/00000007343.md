# 00000007343

**IL3-13 ★** — source: 230_无穷小逻辑.md

**English.** Definition: Loeb integration with measure integration. Conjecture: The equivalence of hyperfinite sums with standard integrals holds, and the error of the equivalence is infinitesimal. (infinitesimal-error Loeb integral equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Loeb 积分：测度的积分。猜想：Loeb 积分的等价：超有限和与标准积分的等价且价的误差为无穷小。（Loeb 积分超有限和标准等价无穷小误差）
