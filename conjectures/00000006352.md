**English.** Definition: Tilting modules are the tensor-generating class of the global basis. Conjecture: The character cone is given by virtual combinations with positive coefficients; the generators of the cone are finite; and the finiteness of cone generation is the Hilbert basis. (positive combination generation of tilting character cones)
