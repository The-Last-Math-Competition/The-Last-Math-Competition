# 00000001969

**MP-09 ★** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Conjecture: The logarithmic coefficient of the entanglement entropy of the 2D free-fermion ground state is (log 2)/(√3) (corresponding to a 60° corner); the universality of the coefficient is determined by the local geometry of the Fermi surface.

**中文。** 猜想：2D 自由费米子基态的纠缠熵的对数项系数为 (log 2)/(√3)(对应角 60° 的拐角);系数的普适性由 Fermi 面的局部几何确定。
