# 00000000969

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative Khintchine inequality (Lust-Piquard). Conjecture: The optimal constant of the nc Khintchine inequality at even p = 2k is an explicit combination of Gaussian integrals (a closed form for the optimal constants).

**中文。** 定义：noncommutative Khintchine(Lust-Piquard)。猜想：nc Khintchine 最优常数在 p = 2k 偶数时为 Gauss 积分的显式组合(最优常数闭式)。
