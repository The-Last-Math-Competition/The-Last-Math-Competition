# 00000002546

**GZ-16 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Besicovitch projection: non-rectifiable sets must have zero-length projection directions. Conjecture: The quantitative deficit δ of non-rectifiability and the measure lower bound c(δ) of zero-length projection directions satisfy the polynomial relation c = δ^C; the optimal value of the exponent C is 2k, attained by cone-type sets. (Besicovitch quantitative projection)

**中文。** 定义：Besicovitch 投影：不可校正集的投影必有零长度方向。猜想：不可校正的定量亏损 δ 与投影零长度方向的测度下界 c(δ) 满足多项式关系 c=δ^{C}；指数 C 的最优值为 2k 且由锥型集达到。（Besicovitch 定量投影）
