# 00000005664

**English.** Definition: A varying-dimension graph mixes two local structures. Conjecture: The recurrence criterion is the explicit integral condition of the two-scale ratio, given by the harmonic mean of effective dimensions, and the boundary is strict. (two-scale harmonic-mean recurrence criterion)

**中文。** 定义：变维图指混合两种局部结构的图。猜想：常返判据为两尺度比例的显式积分条件,由有效维数的调和平均给出且分界严格。（两尺度调和平均常返判据）
