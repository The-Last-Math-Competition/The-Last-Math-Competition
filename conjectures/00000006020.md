**English.** Definition: Invariant sets and topological entropy are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two twist maps with identical invariant sets but different topological entropies, and the separation is realized by an explicit gluing pair with the same sets but different breakup bands. (same invariant sets-different entropy breakup-band separation)

**中文。** 定义：不变集与拓扑熵指两层。猜想：存在两扭转映射的一切不变集相同而拓扑熵不同,分离由同集异破裂带的显式粘合对实现。（不变集同-拓扑熵异破裂带分离）
