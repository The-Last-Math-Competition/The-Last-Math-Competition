# 00000004657

**English.** Definition: The principal part of a harmonic Maass form is its pole-expansion part. Conjecture: The principal-part coefficients are algebraic integers satisfying an explicit height inequality, and the bound is tight in rank one and cannot be relaxed. (height inequality of Maass principal parts)

**中文。** 定义：调和 Maass 形式的主部指其极点展开部分。猜想：主部系数为代数整数且满足显式高度不等式,界在秩一情形紧且不可放松。（Maass 主部高度不等式）
