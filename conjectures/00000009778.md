**English.** Definition: Singular values of the Volterra operator: the compact operator with interval-indicator integration kernel. Conjecture: The complete asymptotic expansion of Volterra singular values exists: the leading term is 1/(pi n) with corrections an odd-power series; each coefficient of the series is an explicit rational combination of Bernoulli numbers. (Volterra singular value full expansion)

**中文。** 定义：Volterra 算子的奇异值：积分核为区间示性的紧算子。猜想：Volterra 算子奇异值的完全渐近展开存在：主项为 1/(πn) 且修正项为奇次幂级数；级数的每个系数为 Bernoulli 数的显式有理组合。（Volterra 奇异值全展开）
