**English.** Definition: singular value: integral kernel of Volterra operator is compact operator of interval. Conjecture: completely asymptotic expansion exists: main term of Volterra operator singular value is 1/(πn) and positive term is every coefficient of power series; series is explicit rational combinatorial of Bernoulli number. (Volterra singular value expansion)

**中文。** 定义：Volterra 算子的奇异值：积分核为区间示性的紧算子。猜想：Volterra 算子奇异值的完全渐近展开存在：主项为 1/(πn) 且修正项为奇次幂级数；级数的每个系数为 Bernoulli 数的显式有理组合。（Volterra 奇异值全展开）
