# 00000004249

**English.** Definition: The family SW invariants are characteristic-class integrals over families of SW moduli spaces. Conjecture: The normalization constants of genus g satisfy an explicit recursion of type 2 to the power 2g, and the recursion is asymptotically stable at a closed form as the genus grows. (closed recursion of family SW normalization)

**中文。** 定义：族 SW 不变量指 SW 模空间族的示性类积分。猜想：亏格 g 的归一化常数满足显式递推,常数为 2 的 2g 幂型,递推在亏格增大时渐近稳定于闭式。（族 SW 归一化递推闭式）
