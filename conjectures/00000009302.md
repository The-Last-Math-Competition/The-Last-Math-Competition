**English.** Definition: The heat kernel of local limits. Conjecture: Heat kernel convergence: the convergence of graph heat kernels, with the rate of the convergence the inverse of the spectral gap. (local limit heat kernel inverse spectral gap convergence)

**中文。** 定义：局部极限的热核。猜想：热核的收敛：图的热核的收敛且敛的速率为谱隙的逆。（局部极限热核收敛谱隙逆图热核收敛速率收敛极限）
