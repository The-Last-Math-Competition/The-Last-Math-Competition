# 00000006060

**English.** Definition: Predicates and categories are two structural layers. Conjecture: There exist two models with identical definable predicates but non-isomorphic overall categories, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same predicates but different automorphism groups. (same predicates-different automorphism category separation)

**中文。** 定义：谓词与范畴指两层结构。猜想：存在两模型的一切可定义谓词相同而整体范畴不同构,分离由同谓词异自同构群的显式对实现。（谓词同-范畴异自同构分离）
