# 00000006537

**English.** Definition: The topological-side homomorphism is the realization from theory to homology. Conjecture: The topological-analytic isomorphism is multiplicative and the explicit form of the isomorphism is the Riesz map; the verification of multiplicativity is the preservation of pairings. (multiplicative topological-analytic isomorphism)

**中文。** 定义：拓扑侧同态为理论到同调的实现。猜想：拓扑解析同构为乘性且同构的显式为 Riesz 映射；且乘性的验证为配对保持。（拓扑解析乘性同构）
