# 00000005520

**English.** Definition: The steady-state distribution and the transient trajectory are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two schemes with the same steady state but different transient trajectories, and the separation is realized by an explicit swap pair of symmetric initial values. (same steady state-different transient initial-swap separation)

**中文。** 定义：稳态分布与瞬态轨迹指两层。猜想：存在两方案的稳态相同而瞬态轨迹不同,分离由对称初值的显式交换对实现。（稳态同-瞬态异初值交换分离）
