**DA-63 ★** — source: 19_丢番图逼近与度量数论.md

**English.** Definition: Wirsing approximation means approximation by algebraic numbers of degree at most n. Conjecture: The Wirsing exponent satisfies w*(2) = 2 (approximation of reals by quadratic algebraic numbers — the optimal exponent is still unresolved).

**中文。** 定义：Wirsing 逼近:次数 ≤ n 的代数数逼近。猜想：Wirsing 指数 w*(n) 满足 w*(2) = 2(实数被二次代数数逼近,最优指数仍未闭合)。
