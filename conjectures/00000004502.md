**TL2-22 ★** — source: 135_范畴逻辑_拓扑斯.md

**English.** Definition: Conditional set theory is a constructive variant of set construction. Conjecture: Its completeness conversion is given explicitly by the combinator of the realizability topos, the conversion fails at the negative ordinals with explicitly decidable failure points, and the failure points are exactly the embedded images of the supremum of the non-realizable Church-Kleene ordinals. (negative-ordinal failure of conditional set theory)

**中文。** 定义：条件集合论指构造集合论的直觉变体。猜想：其完全性换算由可实现性拓扑斯的结合子显式给出,换算在否定序数处失效且失效点显式可判。(条件集合论否定序数失效)；且失效点恰为不可实现序数的 Church-Kleene 上确界嵌入像。
