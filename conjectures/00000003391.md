**English.** Definition: The time evolution of spectral triples: modular group actions. Conjecture: The time evolution of modular automorphisms: the Hamiltonian of the evolution is the modular logarithmic derivative, with the spectrum of the derivative the Tomita–Takesaki modular group. (modular time evolution Tomita spectrum)
