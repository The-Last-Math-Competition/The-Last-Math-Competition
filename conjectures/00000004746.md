# 00000004746

**English.** Definition: The Skolem-Noether theorem is the conjugacy of simple embeddings of central simple algebras. Conjecture: The complexity of the explicit embedding algorithm is a cubic polynomial in the degree, and the lower bound is quadratic and attained by symbolic algebra families. (complexity bounds of Skolem-Noether embeddings)

**中文。** 定义：Skolem–Noether 定理指中心单代数单嵌入的共轭性。猜想：显式嵌入算法的复杂度为次数立方多项式,下界为二次且由符号代数族达到。（Skolem–Noether 嵌入复杂度界）
