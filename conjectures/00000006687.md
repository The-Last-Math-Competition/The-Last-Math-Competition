# 00000006687

**English.** Definition: Convex optimization methods with the convex solving of semidiscrete transport. Conjecture: Under positive measures the optimum is unique and the exception of uniqueness is atom collision; the atom-collision exception is eliminated by entropy regularization; and the elimination error is linear in the entropy parameter. (unique convex optimum of semidiscrete transport)

**中文。** 定义：凸优化方法：半离散传输的凸求解。猜想：正测度下最优唯一且唯一的例外为原子碰撞；原子碰撞的例外由熵正则化消除；消除的误差为熵参数的线性。（半离散凸最优唯一结构）
