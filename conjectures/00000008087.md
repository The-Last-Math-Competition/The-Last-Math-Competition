# 00000008087

**English.** Definition: The Cartan-Eilenberg type decomposition of fusion systems: the intersection lattice Lat(F) of maximal local subsystems of F. Conjecture: Lat(F) is a stratified lattice whose Mobius function has absolute value at most 1 (a stratified Mobius bound); the count of atoms of the lattice equals the count of maximal subsystems of F, at most an exponential-type upper bound C^{|S|} in p; and the lower bound on the atom count (nontrivial abundance) is log|S| (an abundance lower bound), with tightness attained by the count of parabolic subsystems of Weyl groups of Lie type groups (Weyl tightness). (local intersection stratified Mobius bound)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 Cartan–Eilenberg 型分解：F 的极大局部子系统的相交格 Lat(F)。猜想：Lat(F) 为分层格且其 Möbius 函数的绝对值 ≤ 1（层化 Möbius 界）；且格的原子的计数 = F 的极大子系统的计数 ≤ p 的指数型上界 C^{|S|}；原子数的下界（非平凡的丰富性）为 log|S|（丰富下界），下界的紧性由 Lie 型群的 Weyl 群的抛物子系统的计数取得（Weyl 紧性）。
