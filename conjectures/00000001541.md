# 00000001541

**GM-01 ◆** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: Let D(n) denote the number of distinct Euclidean distances determined by the n×n integer lattice. Conjecture: D(n) = n²/√log n·(c+o(1)) with c explicit, and the second-order correction is −c'·n²/log^{3/2}n (c' given by the analytic structure of the Landau–Ramanujan constant).

**中文。** 定义：n×n 整数格点阵的不同欧氏距离数记 D(n)。猜想：D(n) = n²/√log n·(c+o(1)),c 为显式常数,且第二项修正为 −c'·n²/log^{3/2}n(c' 由 Landau–Ramanujan 常数的解析结构给出)。
