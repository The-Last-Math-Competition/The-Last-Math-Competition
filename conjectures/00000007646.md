# 00000007646

**HY4-16 ★** — source: 240_微分算子IV_双曲型.md

**English.** Definition: The Friedlander transform converts the wave equation into a first-order system. Conjecture: The energy of the symmetric hyperbolic structure after transform is equivalent to the original wave energy; and the constant of the equivalence is the square of sound speed. (Friedlander symmetrized energy equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Friedlander 转换把波动方程化为一阶系统。猜想：转换后的对称双曲结构的能量等价于原波动能量；且等价的常数为声速平方。（Friedlander 对称化能量等价）
