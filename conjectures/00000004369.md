# 00000004369

**English.** Definition: HH is ordinary Hochschild homology, and the Gerstenhaber bracket is its degree minus-one Lie bracket. Conjecture: The first instance of a nondegenerate bracket appears in dimension 3, realized by the exterior algebra of the cohomology of the 3-sphere, and in dimension at most 2 it is always degenerate. (first dimension 3 of the Gerstenhaber bracket)

**中文。** 定义：HH 指普通 Hochschild 同调,Gerstenhaber 括号指其上的度负一李括号。猜想：括号非退化的首例出现在维数 3,由三维球面上同调的外代数实现,维数不超过 2 时恒退化。（Gerstenhaber 括号首例维 3）
