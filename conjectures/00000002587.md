# 00000002587

**CM-27 ★** — source: 71_计算复杂性中的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The generation problem for ideal class groups. Conjecture: The exponent relating spectral norm and shortest generator length is the logarithm of the discriminant; and the realization of the exponent is on imaginary quadratic fields. (class group generator length discriminant exponent)

**中文。** 定义：理想类群的生成问题。猜想：谱范数与最短生成长度的指数为判别式对数；且指数的实现为虚二次域。（类群生成长度判别式指数）
