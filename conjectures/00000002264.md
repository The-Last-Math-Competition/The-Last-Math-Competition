# 00000002264

**MG-04 ★** — source: 61_鞅与随机分析.md

**English.** Definition: The Skorokhod embedding stops Brownian motion with a prescribed distribution. Conjecture: The expected stopping time of the minimal residual embedding is an explicit integral of the convex-order difference (residual embedding time).

**中文。** 定义：Skorokhod 嵌入(给定分布停止 Brown)。猜想：最小残差嵌入(residual embedding)的期望停止时间为凸序差的显式积分(残差嵌入时间)。
