# 00000002355

**English.** Definition: The row contraction operator is a contraction of the Cuntz row. Conjecture: The bound of its free function calculus is always one; and the tightness of the bound is unitarily invariant for row contractions; and the verification of tightness is uniquely attained under row-normalized conditions. (row contraction calculus bound identically one)

**中文。** 定义：行压缩算子为 Cuntz 行的压缩。猜想：其自由函数演算的界恒为一；且界的紧性为行压缩的酉不变；且紧性的验证在行规范条件下唯一达到。（行压缩演算界恒一）
