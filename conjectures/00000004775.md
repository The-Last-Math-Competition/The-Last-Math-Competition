# 00000004775

**English.** Definition: The Weingarten function gives the coefficients of moments of Haar unitary matrix entries in an orthonormal basis of representations. Conjecture: The fifth-order asymptotic constant of the 1/n^5 expansion is an explicit rational function of the representation weights (lambda, mu), with the pole set of the rational function located at integer differences of the representation weights; the maximal order of fifth-degree poles is at most 3; and the denominator of the coefficients divides the least common multiple of the product of representation dimensions. (fifth-order rational expansion of Weingarten functions)

**中文。** 定义：Weingarten 函数：Haar 酉矩阵元素矩在表示正交基下的系数。猜想：1/n⁵ 阶展开的五阶渐近常数为表示权 (λ,μ) 的显式有理函数且有理函数的极点集落在表示权的整数差处；五阶极点的最高阶不超过 3；系数的分母整除表示维数乘积的最小公倍数。（Weingarten 五阶有理展开）
