# 00000004688

**English.** Definition: The local zeta function is the p-adic zeta integral of Igusa. Conjecture: The attainable values of the real parts of its poles are the explicit lattice of face weights of the Newton polyhedron, and the lattice is complete for nondegenerate singularities and parametrized by face distances. (face lattice of the real parts of local zeta poles)

**中文。** 定义：局部 zeta 函数指 Igusa 的 p 进 zeta 积分。猜想：其极点实部的可达值为牛顿多面体面权的显式格,格在非退化奇点完全且由面距离参数化。（局部 zeta 极点实部面格）
