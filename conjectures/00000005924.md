**English.** Definition: The free energy is the logarithmic rate of the partition function. Conjecture: The free energy of the log-gamma model is explicit in the Gamma function of the shape parameter, and the two-parameter limit degenerates to the classical rate. (shape-parameter two-parameter degeneration of log-gamma)
