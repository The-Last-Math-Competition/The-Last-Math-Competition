# 00000009996

**NCR-26 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Prime spectra of quantum affine spaces: the spectral structure of noncommutative torus-type deformations. Conjecture: The prime spectrum of a quantum affine space is catenary if and only if the multiplicative subgroup of deformation parameters has no repeated relations; catenarity is equivalent to Zariski Noetherianity, and verifying the equivalence is a finite computation on the parameter group. (quantum space catenary equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：量子仿射空间的素谱：非交换 torus 型变形的谱结构。猜想：量子仿射空间的素谱 catenary 当且仅当变形参数的乘性子群无重复关系；catenarity与 Zariski Noether 性等价且等价性的验证为参数群的有限计算。（量子空间 catenary 等价）
