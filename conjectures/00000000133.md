# 00000000133

**English.** Definition: The Zeckendorf representation of a prime is its unique decomposition as a sum of non-consecutive Fibonacci numbers. Conjecture: The number of terms in the representation of the n-th prime is asymptotic to c·log p_n; moreover, the fluctuations obey a central limit theorem. (Zeckendorf term-count law for primes)

**中文。** 定义：素数的 Zeckendorf 表示指以非连续 Fibonacci 数之和的唯一分解。猜想：第 n 个素数的表示项数渐近等于 c 乘 log p_n；且波动服从中心极限定理。（素数 Zeckendorf 项数律）
