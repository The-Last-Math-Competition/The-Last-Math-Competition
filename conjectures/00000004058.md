**AR3-28 ★** — source: 120_代数表示论III_AuslanderReiten.md

**English.** Definition: The singularity category is the stable derived category modulo homotopy of projectives. Conjecture: Its idempotent completion is the zero category if and only if the algebra is regular, and the dimension of the completed category equals an explicit invariant of the difference of global dimensions. (dimension invariant of the completed singularity category)

**中文。** 定义：singularity category 指投射模商去同伦的稳定导出范畴。猜想：其幂等完备化为零范畴当且仅当代数正则;完备化范畴的维数等于整体维数差的显式不变量。（奇范畴完备化维数不变量）
