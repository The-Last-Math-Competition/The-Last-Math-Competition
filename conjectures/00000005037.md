# 00000005037

**English.** Definition: Courcelle's theorem is the linear-time monadic second-order decision at bounded treewidth. Conjecture: The optimal constant at treewidth one is always an explicit exponent of the automaton rank, matching the known lower bound and not improvable. (treewidth-one rank constant of Courcelle)

**中文。** 定义：Courcelle 定理指有界树宽的单子二阶线性检测。猜想：树宽一时的最优常数恒由自动机秩的显式指数给出；且与已知的下界匹配不可改进。（Courcelle 树宽一秩常数）
