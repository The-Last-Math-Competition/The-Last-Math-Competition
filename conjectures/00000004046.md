**English.** Definition: Yoneda algebra E = Ext(S,S) denotes self- extension graded algebra of semisimple module set. Conjecture: Hilbert series of E is rational function if and only if algebra Koszul or all; is root of unity if and only if is equivalent to self- algebra. (Yoneda Hilbert rational)

**中文。** 定义：Yoneda 代数 E = Ext(S,S) 指半单模集的自扩张分次代数。猜想：E 的 Hilbert 级数为有理函数当且仅当代数 Koszul 或均质;分母根全为单位根当且仅当导出等价于自注入代数。（Yoneda Hilbert 有理性判据）
