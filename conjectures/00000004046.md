# 00000004046

**English.** Definition: The Yoneda algebra E = Ext(S, S) is the graded algebra of self-extensions of the collection of simple modules. Conjecture: The Hilbert series of E is rational if and only if the algebra is Koszul or homogeneous, and all roots of its denominator are roots of unity if and only if it is derived equivalent to a self-injective algebra. (rationality criterion of the Yoneda Hilbert series)

**中文。** 定义：Yoneda 代数 E = Ext(S,S) 指半单模集的自扩张分次代数。猜想：E 的 Hilbert 级数为有理函数当且仅当代数 Koszul 或均质;分母根全为单位根当且仅当导出等价于自注入代数。（Yoneda Hilbert 有理性判据）
