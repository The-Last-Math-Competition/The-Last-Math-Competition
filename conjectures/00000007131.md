# 00000007131

**English.** Definition: Ehrhart-Macdonald reciprocity with interior point counting. Conjecture: The quasi-polynomial of interior counts is the extension to negative indices, and the sign of the extension is alternating. (alternating negative-index Ehrhart reciprocity)

**中文。** 定义：Ehrhart–Macdonald 互反律：内部点的计数。猜想：互反的符号：内部计数的拟多项式为负指标的延拓且拓的符号为交替。（Ehrhart 互反负指标交替符号延拓）
