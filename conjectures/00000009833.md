# 00000009833

**WEX-13 ★** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: Multilinear Muckenhoupt conditions: weight classes of m-linear operators. Conjecture: The weighted constants of m-linear singular integrals satisfy a product upper bound, and the product form is exact: the constant is a geometric-mean (m-1)-power correction of the A_p constants of the weights; the correction exponent is explicitly given by the multilinear index vector. (multilinear product exact formula)

**中文。** 定义：多线性 Muckenhoupt 条件：m 线性算子的权类。猜想：m 线性奇异积分的加权常数满足乘积上界且乘积形式精确：常数为各权 A_p 常数几何平均的 m−1 次幂修正；修正指数由多线性指标向量显式给出。（多线性乘积精确公式）
