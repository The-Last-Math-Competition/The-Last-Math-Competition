**English.** Definition: Gauge is the twist-classification structure of Hopf algebras. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the twist classification and the Hopf-type classification is finite, controlled by characteristic numbers; the bound of finiteness is the exponential of the rank; and additivity of characteristic numbers is preserved under direct sums. (characteristic conversion of Hopf twist classification)
