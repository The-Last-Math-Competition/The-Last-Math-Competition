# 00000000902

**OA-02 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The fundamental group F(M) of a II₁ factor M is the set of all t ∈ R₊ with M^t ≅ M. Conjecture: There exists a II₁ factor whose fundamental group is an explicit uncountable proper subgroup of R₊ (for example, the dense proper subgroup generated by the closure of Q₊·e^Q); all known constructions produce only countable groups, and this example requires a mechanism beyond group-measure methods.

**中文。** 定义：II₁ 因子 M 的基本群 F(M) 指使 M^t≅M 成立的 t∈R₊ 全体。猜想：存在 II₁ 因子其基本群为 R₊ 的显式不可数真子群(例如 Q₊·e^Q 的闭包生成的稠密真子群);一切已知构造只产生可数群,该例需超越群体群方法的新机制。
