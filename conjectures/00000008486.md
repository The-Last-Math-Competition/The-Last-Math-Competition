**English.** Definition: structure of measure: weak limit point set of Gibbs measure family μ_t ∝ e^{-f/t} in t -> 0. Conjecture: limit point of measure always exists and maximum set of support contains in equilibrium state set; when f is periodic orbit limit unique and is the orbit distribution; non- limit can there does not exist and its oscillation period converges critical temperature of jump frequency; oscillation of to periodic orbit is at of exactly pressure function zero singular exponent.

**中文。** 定义：Gibbs 测度族 μ_t ∝ e^{-f/t} 在 t→0 的冻结测度：弱极限点集的结构。猜想：冻结测度的极限点恒存在且支撑含于平衡态集；当 f 的最大值集为周期轨道时极限唯一且为该轨道等分布；非锁定情形极限可不存在且其振荡周期收敛于双周期轨道间的跳跃频率；振荡出现的临界温度恰为压力函数零点奇异度指数的跃变处。
