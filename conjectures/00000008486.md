**ERO-16 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: The frozen measure of the Gibbs family mu_t proportional to e^{-f/t} as t tends to 0: the structure of the weak limit point set. Conjecture: Limit points of frozen measures always exist with support contained in the equilibrium set; when the maximum set of f is a periodic orbit the limit is unique and equals the equidistribution on that orbit; in the nonlocked case the limit may fail to exist and its oscillation period converges to the jump frequency between two periodic orbits; and the critical temperature where oscillation appears is exactly the jump of the singularity exponent of the zero of the pressure function. (frozen Gibbs limit oscillation criticality)

**中文。** 定义：Gibbs 测度族 μ_t ∝ e^{-f/t} 在 t→0 的冻结测度：弱极限点集的结构。猜想：冻结测度的极限点恒存在且支撑含于平衡态集；当 f 的最大值集为周期轨道时极限唯一且为该轨道等分布；非锁定情形极限可不存在且其振荡周期收敛于双周期轨道间的跳跃频率；振荡出现的临界温度恰为压力函数零点奇异度指数的跃变处。
