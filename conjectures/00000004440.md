# 00000004440

**English.** Definition: The sections of a Young diagram are the section distributions of partitions with fixed diagonal sums. Conjecture: There exist two integrable measures whose diagonal section distributions coincide completely while their partition functions differ, and the separation is realized by an explicit mixture of Hall-Littlewood and Schur measures. (section-partition separation by mixing)

**中文。** 定义：Young 图截面指固定对角和的分拆截面分布。猜想：存在两个可积测度其对角截面分布全同而配分函数不同,分离由 Hall–Littlewood 与 Schur 的显式混合实现。（截面-配分分离混合）
