# 00000005071

**English.** Definition: Interacting random systems are multiparticle random Hamiltonians. Conjecture: The critical interaction strength preserving localization is an explicit multiple of the reciprocal single-particle localization length, the multiple is half the interaction rank, and the transition is sharp on both sides. (critical interaction strength of stable localization)

**中文。** 定义：交互随机系统指多粒子随机哈密顿量。猜想：交互保局域化的临界强度为单粒子局域长度倒数的显式倍,倍数为交互秩之半且两侧转变尖锐。（交互稳定局域化临界强度）
