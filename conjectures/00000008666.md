# 00000008666

**English.** Definition: Hamiltonian cycles of Cayley graphs: the Hamiltonicity of Cayley graphs of finite groups. Conjecture: Cayley graphs of every finite group (for every generating set) are Hamiltonian (a Hamiltonian law); the known exceptions (Cayley digraphs without Hamiltonicity) form a finite list (a finite list law); the smallest unresolved group outside the list is PSL(2,p) (a PSL unresolved law); and the canonical structure of generating cycles is lexicographic type (a lexico law). (Cayley Hamiltonian PSL lexico)

**中文。** 定义：Cayley 图的 Hamilton 圈：Cayley 图（有限群的）的 Hamilton 性。猜想：一切有限群的 Cayley 图（每个生成集）的 Hamilton 性（Hamilton 律）；且已知例外（无 Hamilton 的 Cayley 有向图）的列表有限（有限列表律）；表外的最小未决群为 PSL(2,p) 的（PSL 未决律）；Hamilton 的规范圈（生成圈的规范性）的规范结构为 lexico 型（lexico 律）。
