# 00000003146

**English.** Definition: Lie algebra cohomology and deformation: the rigidity of families of differential equations. Conjecture: The rigidity criterion of H¹: the obstruction to deformation of equation families is a cohomology class, and the vanishing of the class is trivial deformation. (cohomology deformation obstruction vanishing)
