# 00000000182

**CS-16 ◆** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: The relation of π^π to the nearby values e^π and π^e in its Gelfond field. Conjecture: At least two of π^π, e^π, π^e are algebraically independent; the full classification is: π^π and e^π are algebraically independent if and only if π and log π are algebraically independent. (pi-pi field generation classification)

**中文。** 定义：π^π 与其 Gelfond 域的邻近值 e^π、π^e 的关系。猜想：π^π、e^π、π^e 三者中至少两个代数无关；更强的完全分类为：π^π 与 e^π 代数无关当且仅当 π 与 log π 代数无关。（pi-pi 域生成分类）
