# 00000009199

**English.** Definition: Random matrix statistics of level repulsion. Conjecture: Level repulsion: Poisson in the localized phase and Wigner-Dyson in the extended phase; and the width of the transition region is exactly the inverse Thouless scale. (level repulsion Poisson Wigner-Dyson Thouless transition)

**中文。** 定义：能级排斥的随机矩阵统计。猜想：能级排斥：局域化相的 Poisson 且广延相的 Wigner-Dyson。(能级排斥 Poisson Wigner-Dyson 局域化相广延相随机矩阵统计)；且过渡区宽度恰为 Thouless 尺度的倒数。
