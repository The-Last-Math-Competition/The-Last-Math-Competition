# 00000004048

**English.** Definition: The derived Picard group is the group of derived self-equivalence classes. Conjecture: The torus rank of the derived Picard group of a species quiver algebra equals its Z{n}-rank, its discrete part is exactly the quiver automorphism group, and the structure is a semidirect product. (structure theorem of derived Picard groups)
