# 00000001453

**CX-03 ★** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The random polytope K_n is the convex hull of n uniform points in R^d, with convex base body K. Conjecture: The volume deficit vol K − vol K_n has variance asymptotic to c_K·n^{-(d+3)/(d+1)}, where c_K = κ_d^{-2}·vol(∂K)²·σ_d² (σ_d a standard deviation constant of the unit sphere volume); and the normalized deficit satisfies a central limit theorem.

**中文。** 定义：随机多胞体 K_n 为 ℝ^d 中 n 个均匀点的凸包,K 为其凸体基。猜想：体积亏 vol K−vol K_n 的方差渐近为 c_K·n^{-(d+3)/(d+1)},其中 c_K = κ_d^{-2}·vol(∂K)²·σ_d²(σ_d 为单位球面体积的标准差常数);且归一化亏量满足中心极限定理。
