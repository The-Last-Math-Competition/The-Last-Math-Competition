**English.** Definition: The condition number is the reciprocal of the smallest singular value of the design matrix. Conjecture: The expected condition number of weighted least squares grows logarithmically in the number of polynomials with explicit constant, and the bound is attained by random alternating nodes. (logarithmic condition-number bound of weighted least squares)
