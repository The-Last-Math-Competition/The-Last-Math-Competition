**English.** Definition: Weighted Fourier restriction: weighted versions of restriction estimates. Conjecture: The dual weight condition for weighted Stein-Tomas to hold is the interpolation family of A_p with A_{p-prime}; the condition is exact, with counterexamples from spherically concentrated weights. (weighted restriction duality condition)

**中文。** 定义：加权 Fourier 限制：限制性估计的权版本。猜想：加权 Stein–Tomas 成立的对偶权条件为 A_p 与 A_{p′} 的插值族；条件精确且由球面聚集权构造反例。（加权限制对偶条件）
