**CM4-17 ◆** — source: 196_组合矩阵论III.md

**English.** Definition: The minor identity is the determinant identity of compound elimination of Sylvester type. Conjecture: The intermediate swell of integer arithmetic is the bit length of values and the kernel of exactness is division elimination; the swell bound is polynomial bit length of degree 4; and the ordered sequence of eliminations is a division chain, with chain length equal to the minor order. (division-elimination bit length of condensed swell)

**中文。** 定义：子式恒等式：复合消去的行列式恒等式（Sylvester 型）。猜想：整数算术的中间膨胀为值位长且精确的核为除法消去；膨胀的界为多项式位长且次数为 4；消去的有序序列为除法链且链长等于子式阶。（浓缩膨胀除法消去位长）
