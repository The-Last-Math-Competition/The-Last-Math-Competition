**English.** Definition: Decoupling is the family of decomposition inequalities at the critical exponent on neighborhoods of the paraboloid. Conjecture: At the critical exponent, near-equality is realized only by diagonal configurations, and the minimal spatial dimension of a realization is 2. (dimension of the decoupling equality configuration)
