# 00000001936

**LG-06 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: The per-volume Betti number limit of an arithmetic tower is the limit of the ratio of Betti numbers to covolume over a tower of lattices. Conjecture: The limit is c/(6π) (the Bergeron–Venkatesh prediction); c is given by a special regularization of the value of an L-function at the central point, and the limit value is exactly determined.

**中文。** 定义：算术塔的 per-volume Betti 数极限指格的塔中 Betti 数与协体积之比的极限。猜想：该极限为 c/(6π)(Bergeron–Venkatesh 预测);c 由 L 函数在中心点的值的特殊正则化给出,极限值精确确定。
