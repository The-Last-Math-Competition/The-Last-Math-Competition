**English.** Definition: The ordinal exponent of optimal proof-search strategies: the normalization index srch(T) of search trees for resolution and tableaux. Conjecture: srch(T) (the super-polynomial exponent of optimal proof search) is the logarithmic tower of the ordinal strength of T (a search-ordinal log-tower law); the optimal forgetting constant of strategy improvement (the forgetting rate of learned clauses) is 1 - 1/e (the optimal forgetting constant); and the completeness constant of the ART system of automated theorem proving (a unified ASL system) is the supremum of out-degrees of the notation tree of the ordinal omega^omega (the ASL completeness constant). (proof search log-tower forgetting constant)
