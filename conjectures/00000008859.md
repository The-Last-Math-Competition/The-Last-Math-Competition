**English.** Definition: Inertial parameters of accelerated splitting schemes: the influence of parameter sequences of inertial extrapolation steps on the stability of monotone operator splitting iterations. Conjecture: The supremum of inertial parameters is always the reciprocal of the inverse norm of the splitting operator; beyond the bound there exist explicit oscillating counterexamples whose amplitude divergence rate is exactly linear in the excess of the parameter; the optimal inertial sequence drives the iteration complexity to the lower bound Omega(1/sqrt epsilon) and matches the corresponding Nesterov parameters term by term; and within the bound the convergence rate is at worst a constant multiple of the inertia-free scheme. (inertial splitting bound oscillation Nesterov matching)

**中文。** 定义：加速分裂格式的惰性参数：惯性外推步的参数序列对单调算子分裂迭代稳定性的影响。猜想：惰性参数的上确界恒为分裂算子逆范数的倒数；参数超界时存在显式振荡反例且反例的振幅发散率恰为参数超出量的线性函数；最优惰性序列使分裂格式的迭代复杂度达到下界 Ω(1/√ε) 且与 Nesterov 格式的对应参数逐项相等；参数在界内时收敛率不劣于无惰性格式的常数倍。
