**English.** Definition: The spectrum of localizing invariants of noncommutative motives. Conjecture: The classification of invariants is always completely given by the K_0 of the monoid of natural transformations; and the classification is closed under Karoubi closure with no new equivalence classes. (invariants K_0 Karoubi closure)
