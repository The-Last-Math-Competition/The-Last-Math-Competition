# 00000008047

**English.** Definition: The tamed complexity of driven sets: the depth Str(X) of the equidimensional stratification (dimension stratification of fibers) of a definable parameter family (X_t)_{t in T}. Conjecture: Str(X) <= dim T (depth control of dimension stratification); the fibers of each stratum are uniformly regular (uniform tame regularity within strata); the codimension of the exceptional set of stratification overlap (multiparameter resonance) obeys a resonance law of at least 2; and the structure of the resonance exceptional set is given by the irreducible decomposition of the subresultant chain of the parameter domain, the length of the decomposition bounded by the logarithm of the Bezout number (a tamed Bezout). (driven subresultant resonance stratification)

**中文。** 定义：驱动的等分集（driven sets）的驯化复杂度：可定义参数族 (X_t)_{t∈T} 的等维数集（纤维的维数分层）的深度 Str(X)。猜想：Str(X) ≤ dim T（维数分层的深度控制）；且每层的纤维的正则一致（层内的一致驯正则）；分层的交叠（多参数共振）的例外集的余维数为 ≥ 2 的共振律；共振例外集的结构由参数域的子结式链（subresultant）的不可约分解给出，分解的长度的上界为 Bezout 数的对数（Bezout 驯化）。
