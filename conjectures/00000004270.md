# 00000004270

**English.** Definition: The character series of a VOA is the generating function of its modules, and the intertwining algebra is the fusion algebra. Conjecture: There exist two VOAs whose character series coincide completely while their fusion algebras are not isomorphic, realizable already with central charge at most 24. (character-fusion separation example)

**中文。** 定义：VOA 的特征标级数指模的母函数,intertwining 代数指融合代数。猜想：存在两个 VOA 特征标级数全同而融合代数不同构,最小中心荷不超过 24 即可实现。（特征标-融合分离例）
