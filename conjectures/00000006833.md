# 00000006833

**English.** Definition: The invariants of weighted shifts. Conjecture: The invariant subspaces of the shift hold, the combination of the subspaces being weight recursions. (weight-recursion invariants of weighted shifts)

**中文。** 定义：加权移位的不变。猜想：加权的不变：移位的不变子空间且间的组合为权重递推。（加权移位权重递推不变子空间加权组合不变递推权重子空间加权移位递推）
