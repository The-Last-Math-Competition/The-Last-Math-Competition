**English.** Definition: Conditional LDP: deviation structure given initial values or filtrations. Conjecture: Filtration compatibility of conditional rates: conditional rates converge along the filtration, with limit rate the unconditional rate minus the information gain; the difference is the explicit capacity of the filtering entropy. (conditional rate information gain)
