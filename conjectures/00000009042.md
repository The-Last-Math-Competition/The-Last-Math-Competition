**English.** Definition: Chern's conjecture: the characteristic numbers of minimal hypersurfaces. Conjecture: The integral of Gauss curvature of compact minimal hypersurfaces is bounded, with the bound an explicit function of the dimension. (Chern characteristic boundedness)

**中文。** 定义：Chern 猜想指极小超曲面的示性数。猜想：其紧极小超曲面的 Gauss 曲率积分有界且界为维数显式函数。（Chern 示性数有界）
