# 00000000089

**AL-07 ◆** — source: 06_代数表示与有限结构.md

**English.** Definition: Higman's PORC conjecture. Conjecture: For fixed n, the map p ↦ (the number of groups of order pⁿ) is given by finitely many polynomials in p (indexed by the residue classes of p).

**中文。** 定义：Higman PORC。猜想：固定 n,p ↦ (阶为 pⁿ 的群个数) 是有限多个关于 p 的多项式(按 p 的剩余类)。
