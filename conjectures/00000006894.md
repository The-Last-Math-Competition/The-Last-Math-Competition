# 00000006894

**English.** Definition: The count bound of generic ranks is the count of interval endpoints. Conjecture: The number of generic ranks of fixed size is at most a linear function of the size parameters; and the tightness of the bound is attained by rectangular tensors of intermediate size. (linear bound on the generic-rank count)

**中文。** 定义：典型秩的个数界为区间端点的计数。猜想：固定尺寸的典型秩个数不超过尺寸参数的线性函数；且界的紧性由中间尺寸的矩形张量达到。（典型秩个数线性界）
