# 00000005271

**English.** Definition: Boundary layer theory is the boundary approximation equation at high Reynolds number. Conjecture: The well-posedness convergence threshold is the explicit condition of analytic exterior flow plus weighted Sobolev spaces, below the threshold a monotone quantity exists, and the condition is optimal and not weakenable. (weighted Sobolev threshold of boundary layers)

**中文。** 定义：边界层理论指高雷诺数的边界近似方程。猜想：其适定的收敛阈值为解析外流加加权索伯列夫的显式条件,阈值下单调量存在且条件最优不可减弱。（边界层加权索伯列夫阈值）
