**FP3-08 ★** — source: 144_随机矩阵IV_自由概率.md

**English.** Definition: The pairing count of asymptotically free moments is the weighted asymptotics of crossing pairings in the expansion of unitarily invariant mixed moments. Conjecture: The asymptotic coefficients of mixed moments are given by a square-reciprocal power series in the crossing number, with leading term the noncrossing Catalan-type term; the coefficient of the one-crossing correction is exactly -1/2; the power series has convergence radius 1 and diverges on the circle of convergence. (crossing-weight series of mixed moments)

**中文。** 定义：渐近自由矩的配对计数：酉不变混合矩展开中交叉配对的带权渐近。猜想：混合矩的渐近系数由交叉数的平方倒数幂级数给出且首项为无交叉的 Catalan 型项；一交叉修正项的系数恰为 -1/2；幂级数的收敛半径为 1 且在收敛圆上发散。（混合矩交叉权级数）
