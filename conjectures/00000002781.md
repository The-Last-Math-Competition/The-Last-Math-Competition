**AG3-11 ★** — source: 78_渐近几何_Banach局部理论II.md

**English.** Definition: Vapnik–Chervonenkis dimension and local theory: the combinatorial complexity of convex bodies. Conjecture: The equivalence of VC dimension and Rademacher type for convex bodies: the VC dimension of the skeleton is at most n log n, attained by simplices; the constant of the equivalence is optimal when extreme points are uniformly distributed. (convex body VC-type equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Vapnik–Chervonenkis 维数与局部理论：凸体的组合复杂度。猜想：凸体的 VC 维与 Rademacher 型的等价：体的骨架的 VC 维为 n log n 的上界且上界由简单体达到；等价的常数在极点均匀分布时最优。（凸体 VC-型等价）
