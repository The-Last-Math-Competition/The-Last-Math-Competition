# 00000002298

**FR-08 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Conjecture: The threshold for the smallest non-locally-realizable p-rank is p²−1 (corrected from p²); concretely, fusion systems of rank < p²−1 are all locally realizable, and the first counterexample at rank = p²−1 is constructed from (p−1)×(p+1) matrix groups.

**中文。** 猜想：p-秩的最小非局部实现的阈值为 p²-1(修正自 p²);具体地,秩 < p²-1 的融合系统皆可实现为局部,秩 = p²-1 的首个反例由 (p-1)×(p+1) 矩阵群构造。
