**PHT-26 ★** — source: 316_持久同调II_统计与稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: Clique-filter persistent homology: topological statistics of clique complexes on random graphs. Conjecture: The conditional distribution of persistence bar lengths of Erdos-Renyi clique filtrations has exponential tails, with tail rate explicitly related to the spectral gap of clique counts; the expected longest bar grows as the one-third power of n, with tight exponent. (clique filtration bar exponential tail)

**中文。** 定义：团过滤持久同调：随机图上的团复形的拓扑统计。猜想：Erdős–Rényi 团过滤的持久条长度的条件分布为指数尾且尾率与团数的谱隙显式相关；最长条的期望按 n 的三分之一幂增长且指数紧。（团过滤条长指数尾）
