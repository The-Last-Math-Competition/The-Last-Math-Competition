**English.** Definition: Top-heaviness (Dowling–Wilson): the number of flats of rank ≤ i is at most the number of flats of rank ≥ r−i. Conjecture: The gap in the strict inequality at middle levels is monotone (top-heavy strengthened; verified for all r = 4).
