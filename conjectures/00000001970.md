# 00000001970

**MP-10 ★** — source: 51_数理物理.md

**English.** Definition: The form factors of sine-Gordon breathers are the matrix elements of their scattering. Conjecture: Bootstrap completeness holds: the denominator of the two-particle form factor has an explicit table of ∏(u²−n²) type (listed termwise by fusion angles θ_n); the complete system exhausts all asymptotic states.

**中文。** 定义：Sine-Gordon 呼吸子的 form factor 指其散射的矩阵元。猜想：bootstrap 的完备性成立:两粒子 form factor 的分母为 ∏(u²−n²) 型的显式表(表由融合角 θ_n 的逐项列出);完备系统穷尽一切渐近态。
