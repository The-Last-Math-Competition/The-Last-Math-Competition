# 00000008801

**English.** Definition: Nesterov accelerated gradient (NAG): a first-order acceleration method driven by momentum parameters. Conjecture: NAG matches the t-squared acceleration lower bound for smooth strongly convex problems (a t-squared matching law); the instance family attaining the bound is the quadratic chain (a chain family law); the heavy-ball generalization of NAG to non-strongly convex problems has stability properties (a stability law); and the optimal momentum schedule for stability is 1 - 1/t (a scheduling law). (Nesterov t-squared chain heavy-ball schedule)

**中文。** 定义：Nesterov 加速梯度法（NAG）：动量参数驱动的加速一阶方法。猜想：NAG 对光滑强凸问题的迭代下界匹配 t² 的加速（t² 匹配律）；且下界的达到的实例族为二次链（链族律）；NAG 在非强凸的推广（重球）的稳定性（稳定律）；稳定的最优动量为 1-1/t 的调度（调度律）。
