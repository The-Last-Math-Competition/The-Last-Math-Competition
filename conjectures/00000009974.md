# 00000009974

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative crepant resolutions (NCCR): End-algebra-type resolutions of singularities (Van den Bergh theory). Conjecture: Deformation uniqueness of NCCRs of three-dimensional quotient singularities: all NCCRs are pairwise derived Morita equivalent, with the count of equivalence classes an explicit count of twisting group parameters; the criterion is given by the intersection matrix of the exceptional bundle of the McKay correspondence. (NCCR deformation uniqueness)

**中文。** 定义：非交换 crepant 解析（NCCR）：奇点的 End 代数型解消（Van den Bergh 理论）。猜想：三维商奇点的 NCCR 的形变唯一：一切 NCCR 两两导 Morita 等价且等价的类数为扭曲群参数的显式计数；判据由 McKay 对应的例外簇的交矩阵给出。（NCCR 形变唯一性）
