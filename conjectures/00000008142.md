# 00000008142

**English.** Definition: Superconvergence of Fisher dimensions: the linearity lin(Delta) of the scaling exponent table Delta_k of k-point functions of the critical Ising model. Conjecture: lin is completely linear (Delta_k = k Delta_1 + a C(sigma)-symmetric correction) if and only if the model is integrable (an integrability-linearity equivalence); the minimal model breaking linearity (nonlinear contributions of anomalous dimensions) is the 3-state Potts model (minimal Potts nonlinearity); and the spectrum of the nonlinear coefficients is a quadratic form in the central charges of Virasoro minimal models (a c-quadratic formula), with discriminant -4(m+3)^2/(m(m+4)) (a minimal-model discriminant). (Fisher integrability-linearity c-quadratic form)

**中文。** 定义：Fisher 维数的超收敛：临界 Ising 模型的 k 点函数的尺度指数表 Δ_k 的线性度 lin(Δ)。猜想：lin 完全线性（Δ_k = k·Δ_1 + C(σ-对称修正)）当且仅当模型可积（可积-线性等价）；且线性破坏（反常维数的非线性贡献）的最小模型为 3 态 Potts（Potts 最小非线性）；非线性系数的谱为 Virasoro 极小模型的中央荷的二次型（c-二次型公式），二次型的判别式 = -4·(m+3)²/(m(m+4))（极小模型判别式）。
