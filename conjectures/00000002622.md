**English.** Definition: Theta blocks: Jacobi forms constructed from unary theta products. Conjecture: Completion of the table of shortest theta blocks (Alp–Kocaoglu–Poor–Yen): the minimal theta-block length of every weight-index pair is finite with upper bound twice the index; the minimal length is given explicitly by the elementary layers of the divisor function. (shortest theta block table)

**中文。** 定义：theta 块（theta block）：由一元 theta 乘积构造的 Jacobi 形式。猜想：最短 theta 块表（Alp–Kocaoglu–Poor–Yen）的完备化：一切权指标对的极小 theta 块长度有限且长度的上界为指标的两倍；极小长度由除数函数的初等层显式给出。（最短 theta 块表）
