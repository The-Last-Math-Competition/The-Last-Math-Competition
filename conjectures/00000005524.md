# 00000005524

**English.** Definition: Static priority is a fixed class ordering. Conjecture: The priority stability region of two-class networks is the explicit nesting of traffic inequalities, given by the lexicographic order of priorities, with first-come-first-served as the extremum. (lexicographic nested stability structure of priorities)

**中文。** 定义：静态优先级指固定的类次序。猜想：两类网络的优先稳定域为流量不等式的显式嵌套,由优先序字典序给出且先来先服务为极值。（优先字典嵌套稳定域结构）
