# 00000002321

**NG-01 ★** — source: 63_数值代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: Homotopy continuation tracks solutions while continuously deforming an easily solved system into the target system; the condition-length integral is the integral of the condition number along the path. Conjecture: The average condition length over full Bézout-number paths is D^{n+1}/ε^{n} (D the maximal degree); the exponent is exact (a polynomial-type average condition length).

**中文。** 定义：同伦延续法指从易解系统连续变形到目标系统的解追踪;条件长积分指沿路径的条件数的积分。猜想：满 Bézout 数路径的平均条件长为 D^{n+1}/ε^{n}(D 为最大度);指数精确(平均条件长的多项式型)。
