# 00000009986

**NCR-16 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Restricted quantum groups: blocks of small quantum groups at prime root orders. Conjecture: The blocks of restricted quantum groups are symmetric special double crosses, with block labels the coset count of the affine Weyl group; the cross type of the double cross is an explicit quotient of the translation group, and the block count is exactly the coset count. (small quantum group block double cross)

**中文。** 定义：restricted 量子群：素根阶的小量子群的块。猜想：restricted 量子群的块为对称特殊双十字且块的标签为仿射 Weyl 群的陪集计数；双十字的交叉型为平移群的显式商且块数恰为陪集数。（小量子群块双交叉）
