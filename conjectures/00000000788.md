# 00000000788

**PA-88 ★** — source: 23_p进分析与局部域.md

**English.** Definition: Rigid cohomology is the de Rham type cohomology in characteristic p. Conjecture: The weights in Poincaré duality for nonsmooth compactifications are monotone; and the correction of the weight structure is given by the intersection complex. (rigid duality weight monotonicity)

**中文。** 定义：刚性上同调为特征 p 的 de Rham 型上同调。猜想：Poincaré 对偶在非光滑紧化下的权为单调；且权结构的修正为交集复形。（刚性对偶权单调）
