# 00000004246

**English.** Definition: The Taubes equivalence is the equality between SW and contact Gromov counts on symplectic four-manifolds. Conjecture: The absolute value of the conversion constant is 1, the sign is determined by the chirality of the homology class, and the equality holds on all closed symplectic four-manifolds. (chirality sign of the Taubes conversion)

**中文。** 定义：Taubes 等价指辛四维流形上 SW 与切触 Gromov 计数的等式。猜想：换算常数的绝对值为 1,符号由同调类的手性决定,等式对一切闭辛四维流形成立。（Taubes 换算手性符号）
