# 00000007608

**English.** Definition: Reflections as hyperplane mirror structure. Conjecture: The composition of two reflections is a rotation and the decomposition is unique; the exception of uniqueness is parallel reflections; and the exception of parallel reflections is translation composition. (unique two-reflection rotation composition)

**中文。** 定义：反射为超平面镜像结构。猜想：两反射的合成为旋转且分解唯一；且唯一性的例外为平行反射；且平行反射的例外为平移复合。（两反射合成旋转唯一）
