# 00000006555

**English.** Definition: Palindromic splitting with the palindromic-coefficient recursion of higher-order splitting. Conjecture: The fourth-level recursion coefficients close and the generating function of the recursion is rational; the choice of recursion initial values is unique within four levels; and the poles of the rational function are roots of unity. (rational generation of palindromic recursion coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：回文分裂：高阶分裂的回文系数递归。猜想：四级递归系数封闭且递归的生成函数为有理；递归初值的选取在四级内唯一；有理函数的极点为单位根。（回文递归系数有理生成）
