**English.** Definition: The higher-dimensional semantics of proof equivalence: the invariant PN_inv of the geometric equivalence classes of proof nets (proof nets of Girard's linear logic), as homology classes of flow spaces. Conjecture: Two proof nets are equivalent if and only if the first homology classes of the compactifications of their flows are rationally equal (homology as a complete invariant); the complexity of the minimal word problem of the Dehornoy order on the combinatory algebra is linear (fastest decision of the order); and the inflation rate of the size of normal forms of the quotient of proof nets by bureaucratic equivalence is polynomial (polynomiality of de-bureaucratization). (proof net homology Dehornoy linear decision)
