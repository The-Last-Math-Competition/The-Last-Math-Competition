# 00000009949

**English.** Definition: Measure equivalence rigidity: measure equivalence classes of lattices (Furman proved the higher-rank classification); the rank-one case is open. Conjecture: Measure equivalence classes of hyperbolic lattices are finite, with class representatives an explicit list of arithmetic lattices; and the optimal compression constant of an equivalence coupling equals the ratio of the first l^2-Betti numbers of the two lattices. (hyperbolic lattice ME finite classes)
