**English.** Definition: The JSJ algorithm is the construction procedure of the canonical decomposition. Conjecture: Its complexity is a cubic polynomial in the number of generators, and the exponent three is proven optimal by explicit examples of rigid chains. (optimal cubic complexity of JSJ)
