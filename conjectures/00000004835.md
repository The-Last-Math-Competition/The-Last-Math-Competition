# 00000004835

**English.** Definition: Arithmetic quantum ergodicity is the equidistribution of Hecke eigenfunctions. Conjecture: The deviation of period integrals of quadratic twists of elliptic curves is the explicit negative one-quarter power of the level plus epsilon, given by the subconvex bound of the bridge conversion and tight. (AQE quadratic twist deviation exponent)
