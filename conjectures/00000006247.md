# 00000006247

**English.** Definition: Homotopy sheaves are higher-level sheaf structures. Conjecture: Hypercompletion is necessary and the convergence order of the tower is explicit, the kernel of the order being the truncation height, and the convergence of the truncation height being the homotopy-sheaf tower. (hypercompletion truncation-height convergence order of homotopy sheaves)

**中文。** 定义：同伦层为高阶层结构。猜想：超完备化必要且塔的收敛阶显式；且阶的核为截断高；且截断高的收敛为同伦层塔。（同伦群层超完备截断高收敛阶）
