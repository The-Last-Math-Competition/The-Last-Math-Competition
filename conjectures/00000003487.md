# 00000003487

**English.** Definition: Mod-p coloring of hypertrees: hyperedges as mod-p linear constraints. Conjecture: For p larger than the hyperedge size bound, the mod-p chromatic number coincides with the classical hypergraph chromatic number; the tightness of the threshold is attained by projective-plane hypergraphs, with the critical prime the smallest prime factor of the hyperedge capacity. (hypertree mod p threshold)

**中文。** 定义：超树的模 p 染色：超边为模 p 线性约束的染色问题。猜想：p 大于超边大小上界时模 p 染色数与经典超染色数一致；阈值的紧性由射影平面超图达到且临界素数为超边容量的最小素因子。（超树模 p 阈值）
