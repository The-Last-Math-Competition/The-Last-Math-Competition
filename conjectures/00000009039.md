**MFH-29 ★** — source: 286_半整权模形式.md

**English.** Definition: Bergé–Martin five weight. Conjecture: analytic continuation complete of 5/4 weight and pole is finite; and position of pole is real quadratic point. (5/4 weight real quadratic pole finite)

**中文。** 定义：Bergé–Martin 五季度权。猜想：5/4 权的解析延拓完备且极点为有限；且极点的位置为实二次点。（5/4 权实二次极点有限）
