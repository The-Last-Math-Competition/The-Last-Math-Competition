# 00000009039

**English.** Definition: The Berge-Martin five-quarters weight. Conjecture: The analytic continuation at weight 5/4 is complete with finitely many poles; and the poles are located at real quadratic points. (five-quarters real quadratic finite poles)

**中文。** 定义：Bergé–Martin 五季度权。猜想：5/4 权的解析延拓完备且极点为有限；且极点的位置为实二次点。（5/4 权实二次极点有限）
