**English.** Definition: Hierarchical models: Schroedinger operators on trees or hierarchical lattices. Conjecture: The critical coupling on trees is Wegner-type explicit (a criticality law); the boundary between pure point and singular continuous domains is an Abel-type criterion (an Abel law); the singular continuous spectrum is Cantor type (a Cantor law); and the fractal dimension of the spectrum is continuously tunable (a spectrum line law). (hierarchical Wegner Abel Cantor tunability)

**中文。** 定义：分层（hierarchical）模型：树上或分层晶格上的薛定谔算子。猜想：树上的临界耦合为 Wegner 型显式（临界律）；纯点域与奇异连续域的分界为 Abel 型判据（Abel 律）；奇异连续谱为 Cantor 型（Cantor 律）；谱的分形维数可连续调节（谱系律）。
