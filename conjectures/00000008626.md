**English.** Definition: The sine process is the bulk universal limit point process. Conjecture: The leading asymptotic constant of the Fredholm determinant of the two-point correlation function is a Gamma(2/3) functional; and the secondary constant comes from the Berry phase. (sine leading Gamma constant)
