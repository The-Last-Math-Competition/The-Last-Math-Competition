# 00000008018

**English.** Definition: The Markov-type bound on the renormalized energy of the obstacle problem: the renormalized remainder R(u) of the contribution of singular points (obstacle contact cusps) to the Dirichlet energy. Conjecture: R(u) is a sum of type sum over singular points of c_i rho_i^2 (rho_i the local radius of the cusp), with constants explicit functions of the frequency limit c(N_j) = (2N_j - 1) pi/2; and the dynamics of the variation of the renormalized energy (motion of singularities) is a Hamiltonian system whose Hamiltonian is the dual of the pairwise logarithmic interaction of the singularities (a mirror image of the log-gas), whose equilibrium configurations are weighted versions of Fekete point sets. (obstacle renormalized log-gas Fekete equilibrium)

**中文。** 定义：障碍问题的重整化能量的 Markov 型界：奇异点（障碍接触尖点）对 Dirichlet 能量的贡献的重整化剩余 R(u)。猜想：R(u) = Σ_{奇点} c_i·ρ_i² 型求和（ρ_i 为尖点的局部半径），常数为频率极限的显式函数 c(N_j)= (2N_j-1)·π/2；且重整化能量的变分（奇点运动）的动力学为 Hamilton 系统，哈密顿量为奇点两两之间的对数相互作用的对偶（log-gas 的镜像化），该系统的平衡构型为 Fekete 点集的加权版本。
