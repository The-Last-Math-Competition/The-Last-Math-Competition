**QG2-26 ★** — source: 197_量子群II.md

**English.** Definition: The Yangian with the polynomial-root structure of representations. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the pairing of polynomial roots, given by classical integers; the sum of pairings is the weight; and the imaginary part of the roots is zero. (classical pairing conversion of polynomial roots)

**中文。** 定义：杨代数：Yangian 表示的多项式根结构。猜想：换算常数为多项式根的配对显式且由经典整数给出；配对的和为权；根的虚部为零。（多项式根经典配对换算）
