**FL2-09 ★** — source: 161_非线性偏微分II_流体II.md

**English.** Definition: A relative equilibrium is a rigidly rotating vortex configuration. Conjecture: The count of relative equilibria of at most four vortices is an explicit enumeration of polynomial roots, the count for general strengths follows a power law of two, and stability is decided by a quadratic form. (power-law enumeration of low-vortex relative equilibria)

**中文。** 定义：相对平衡指刚体旋转的涡构型。猜想：四个以内涡的相对平衡计数为多项式根的显式枚举,一般强度下计数为二的幂律且稳定性由二次型判别。（低涡相对平衡幂律枚举）
