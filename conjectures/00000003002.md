# 00000003002

**English.** Definition: The Galois theory of parametric differential equations: parametrized Picard–Vessiot theory. Conjecture: The joint group of parametric PV: the intersection of the Galois groups of the fibers of the parameter space is the joint group, with the codimension of the intersection given by the specialization stratification of parameters. (parametric joint group specialization layer)
