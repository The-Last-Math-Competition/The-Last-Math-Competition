# 00000001428

**MC-08 ★** — source: 33_概率论II_马氏链与粒子系统.md

**English.** Definition: Coalescing random walks (merging upon meeting). Conjecture: On Z^d, the exponential decay rate λ_d of the total particle count equals the probability that two independent random walks never meet, and this probability has an explicit spectral representation (an identity between coalescence rate and non-collision probability).

**中文。** 定义：coalescing 随机游走(相遇即合并)。猜想：ℤ^d 上粒子总数的指数衰减率 λ_d 等于两个独立随机游走永不相遇的概率,且该概率有显式谱表示(凝聚率-余碰概率等式)。
