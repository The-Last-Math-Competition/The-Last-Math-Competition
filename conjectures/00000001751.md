# 00000001751

**RT-01 ◆** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: Saxl's conjecture states that the tensor square V(λ)⊗V(λ) of the staircase partition λₙ contains every irreducible representation of S_n for n sufficiently large. Conjecture: Saxl's conjecture holds with threshold n₀ ≤ 2^{18}; concretely, S_n up to order 2^{17} is computationally verifiable, and the remaining range is covered by spectral tools (mixing times).

**中文。** 定义：Saxl 猜想指阶梯分拆 λₙ 的张量平方 V(λ)⊗V(λ) 含 S_n 的一切不可约表示(n 充分大)。猜想：Saxl 猜想成立且阈值 n₀≤2^{18};具体地,2^{17} 阶以下的 S_n 已可由计算验证,剩余区间由谱工具(混合时间)补齐。
