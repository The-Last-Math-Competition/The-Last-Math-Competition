**DS-16 ★** — source: 31_描述集合论与递归论.md

**English.** Definition: The Hausdorff–Kuratowski hierarchy compares the difference hierarchy of Δ⁰₃ with the limit layer of Σ⁰₂. Conjecture: The two hierarchies are strictly different at the third level; the strictness is equivalent to a positive answer to the tree-automorphism problem (Marko's equivalence path).

**中文。** 定义：Hausdorff–Kuratowski 分层比较 Δ⁰₃ 的差分层与 Σ⁰₂ 极限层。猜想：两分层在第三层严格不同;该严格性等价于树自同构问题的正回答(Marko 的等价路径)。
