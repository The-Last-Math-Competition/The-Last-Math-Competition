# 00000005498

**English.** Definition: The deformed energy levels are the size dependence of the deformed spectrum, given by the deformation formula of finite-volume levels. Conjecture: The two-body formula is an explicit quadratic in the seed eigenvalues with momenta, degenerating in the narrow-torus limit to the relativistic correction; the coupling coefficient of the quadratic is a linear function of the deformation parameter; and level degeneracies split into two branches under deformation, with splitting width proportional to the deformation parameter. (seed quadratic formula of two-body deformations)

**中文。** 定义：形变能级：形变后谱的尺寸依赖（有限体积能级的形变公式）。猜想：二体公式为种子本征值与动量的显式二次式且窄环极限退化为相对论修正项；二次式的耦合系数为形变参数的线性函数；能级的简并在形变下分裂为两支且分裂宽与形变参数成正比。（形变二体种子二次公式）
