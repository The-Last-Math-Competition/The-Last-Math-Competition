# 00000004808

**English.** Definition: The YTD correspondence is the equivalence of K-stability with the existence of metrics. Conjecture: The extension of the equivalence to singular Fanos is K-polystability plus an explicit decay condition, complete for the class of cone singularities with no exceptions. (YTD singular polynomial extension)

**中文。** 定义：YTD 对应指 K 稳定性与度量存在的等价。猜想：奇异 Fano 的推广等价为 K 多项式稳定性加显式衰减条件,推广在锥奇点类完全且无例外。（YTD 奇异多项式推广）
