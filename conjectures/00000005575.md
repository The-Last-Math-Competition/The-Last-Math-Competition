**English.** Definition: A uniqueness subgroup controls local structure, with the obstruction to its existence. Conjecture: The obstruction dimension is explicit in the number of generator-permutation orbits, controlled by the simplicity of maximal local subgroups; the obstruction is zero when simplicity holds, and the uniqueness subgroup exists; and the first positive obstruction occurs at non-flag types of the rank-two abelian graph, with obstruction value 1. (orbit-dimension obstruction to uniqueness)
