**English.** Definition: The noncommutative prismatic decomposition of the prismatic framework. Conjecture: The generalized grading of spectral coefficients is of special type, the grading levels are finite, and the upper bound of the grading levels is the perfect prismatic depth. (finite grading of noncommutative prisms)

**中文。** 定义：棱镜框架的非交换分解。猜想：谱系数的广义分次为特殊型；且分次的层数有限；且分次层数的上界为棱镜的完美NESS深度。（非交换棱镜分次有限）
