# 00000008399

**English.** Definition: Motivic correlation: the nonvanishing of Ext groups between two motives. Conjecture: Ext^1 between motives of weights p and q is nonzero only when |p - q| <= 1; the dimension of Ext^1 is zero in numerical motives and computable from extensions of Hodge structures in the motivic category; and the first Ext^1 not of Hodge origin occurs between weights 2 and 3. (motivic correlation weight-difference bound)

**中文。** 定义：母题相关指两个母题之间 Ext 群的非平凡性。猜想：权 p 与权 q 母题间的 Ext¹ 仅当 |p−q| ≤ 1 时非零；Ext¹ 的维数在数值母题中为零、在母题范畴中可由 Hodge 结构的扩张计算；第一个非 Hodge 来源的 Ext¹ 出现在权 2 与权 3 之间。（母题相关权差界）
