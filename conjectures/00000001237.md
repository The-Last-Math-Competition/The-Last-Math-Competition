# 00000001237

**English.** Definition: The period of chip firing on a digraph is the length of the recurrent oscillation. Conjecture: The firing period of a directed cycle equals the lcm of the arc lengths; and the period formula extends to strongly connected digraphs via rotation numbers. (firing period least common multiple)

**中文。** 定义：有向图上的芯片发射的周期为回归振荡的长度。猜想：有向环的发射周期等于弧长的最小公倍数；且周期公式在强连通图推广为旋转数。（发射周期最小公倍）
