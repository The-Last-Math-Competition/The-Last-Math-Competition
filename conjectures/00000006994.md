# 00000006994

**English.** Definition: Commutation with the deficit structure of integral interchange. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the commutation deficit and given by the algebraic type of oscillatory integrals; the order of the deficit is 1/epsilon; and the kernel of the deficit is the measure of the resonance surface. (oscillatory-integral commutation conversion)

**中文。** 定义：换序：积分换序的亏损结构。猜想：换算常数为换序亏损的显式且由振荡积分的代数型给出；亏损的阶为 1/ε；亏损的核为共振面的测度。（换序振荡积分数换算常数）
