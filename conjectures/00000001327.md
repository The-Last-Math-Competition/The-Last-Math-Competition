# 00000001327

**English.** Definition: The L²-metric geodesic flow on the Kähler potential space of a torus is the Hamiltonian flow on that space. Conjecture: The entropy of the geodesic flow is always zero; and the evidence for zero-entropy rigidity is the commutativity of geodesics together with the convexity of the Mabuchi functional. (potential geodesic zero-entropy rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：环面上 Kähler 位势空间的 L² 度量测地流指位势空间上的哈密顿流。猜想：测地流的熵恒为零；且零熵刚性的证据为测地的可交换性与 Mabuchi 泛数的凸性。（位势测地零熵刚性）
