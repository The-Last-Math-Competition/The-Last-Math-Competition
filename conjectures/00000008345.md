# 00000008345

**DIA-25 ★** — source: 263_丢番图逼近II.md

**English.** Definition: The multiscale approximation of Cantor targets: the win-or-lose measure cantor_hit of Baker-type Cantor targets (intersections of psi-approximable sets with Cantor sets). Conjecture: cantor_hit (a sufficient condition for nonempty intersection) is the half-dimensional transcendence of psi (a half-dimensional sufficiency law); the tightness of the condition (optimal psi) has critical constant the ratio of Hausdorff dimensions (a dimension ratio constant law); the hit-or-miss transition (hit probability 0 to 1) has a logarithmically narrow critical window (a logarithmic narrow window law); and the universal scaling inside the window (scaling function) is of tanh type (a tanh scaling law). (Cantor target half-dimensional tanh window)

**中文。** 定义：Cantor 目标的多尺度逼近：Baker 型的 Cantor 目标（ψ-可逼近 × Cantor 的交）的测度的双赢 cantor_hit。猜想：cantor_hit（交的非空的充分条件）为 ψ 的超越 half-dimensional（半维充分律）；且条件的紧性（最优 ψ）的临界常数为 Hausdorff 维数的比（维数比常数律）；Hit or miss 的转移（命中概率 0→1）的临界窗为对数窄（对数窄窗律）；窗内的概率的普适标度（标度函数）为 tanh 型（tanh 标度律）。
