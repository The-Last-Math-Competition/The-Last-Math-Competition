# 00000001707

**EG-17 ★** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: The continuous spectrum of a Sturmian system is the non-pure-point part of its spectrum. Conjecture: For the Sturmian system of slope α (a quadratic irrational), the measure type of the continuous spectrum is an explicit singular measure whose Fourier transform does not decay to 0, given by truncations of a Lambert series.

**中文。** 定义：Sturmian 系统的连续谱指其谱的非纯点部分。猜想：以 slope α(二次无理数)的 Sturmian 系统的连续谱的测度类型为显式奇异测度:该测度的 Fourier 变换不衰减到 0,由 Lambert 级数的截断给出。
