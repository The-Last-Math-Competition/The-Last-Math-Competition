# 00000003595

**English.** Definition: The space of lattices is the topological space of all full-rank lattices on SL_n(R)/SL_n(Z). Conjecture: The space of lattices is always locally compact, with the boundary of its compactification consisting of degenerate lattices; the boundary stratification corresponds one-to-one with chains of standard parabolic subgroups. (lattice space parabolic boundary stratification)

**中文。** 定义：格空间指所有满秩格在 SL_n(R)/SL_n(Z) 上的拓扑空间。猜想：格空间恒为局部紧而其紧化的边界由退化格组成；且边界分层与标准 parabolic 子群链一一对应。（格空间 parabolic 边界分层）
