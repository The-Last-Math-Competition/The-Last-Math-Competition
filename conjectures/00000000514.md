**English.** Definition: The coefficients q_i of a BS decomposition carry arithmetic structure of their denominators. Conjecture: The denominator of q_i divides the Vandermonde determinant of the degree sequence; and the saturation of the divisibility is exact for pure decompositions. (BS coefficient Vandermonde divisibility)
