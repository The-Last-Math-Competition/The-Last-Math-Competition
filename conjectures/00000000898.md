# 00000000898

**PV-98 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The Stokes drag around an obstacle. Conjecture: Finite drag is minimized over regular domains by an explicit shape functional whose minimizer is the ball (the postulate of minimal drag).

**中文。** 定义：Stokes 围绕障碍的阻力。猜想：有限阻力由正则域的显式形状泛函最小化为球(Postulate of minimal drag)。
