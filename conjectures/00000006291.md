**English.** Definition: A regular ring is one where element equations are always solvable. Conjecture: The zeroth group is a complete invariant; the classification is given by the ordered groups of continuous geometry; and the dimension function of the ordered group is the coordinate lattice of continuous geometry. (complete ordered-group zeroth classification of regular rings)

**中文。** 定义：正则环为元素方程恒可解的环。猜想：零阶群为完备不变量；且分类由连续几何的有序群给出；且有序群的维数函数为连续几何的坐标格。（正则环零阶有序群完备）
