# 00000009882

**English.** Definition: Rigidity of wild quivers: the distribution of indecomposable rigid representations of wild hereditary quivers. Conjecture: A wild hereditary quiver has infinitely many indecomposable rigid representations at some dimension vector if and only if its underlying graph contains an oriented cycle; the criterion is a finite linear-algebra computation, and rigid families are parametrized by explicit varieties. (wild rigidity infinitude criterion)

**中文。** 定义：野箭图的刚性：wild 遗传箭图的不可分解刚性表示的分布。猜想：wild 遗传箭图对每个维数向量存在无穷多刚性不可分解当且仅当其骨架图含定向圈；判据为线性代数的有限计算且刚性族的参数化为显式簇。（野刚性无穷判据）
