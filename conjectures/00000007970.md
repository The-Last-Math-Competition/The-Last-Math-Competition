**English.** Definition: The dimensional obstruction of locally correctable codes (LCC): the decay rate of the exponential upper bound on the dimension K(n) of (k,delta)-LCCs. Conjecture: Every constant-query LCC has dimension K(n) <= n^{1-Omega(1)} (a quantitative sublinear-linear separation); and the upper bound for t-query LCCs is a tower-type descent n^{1-Omega(log^{1-t} 1)} (as t grows the exponential obstruction decays to sub-power-law); configurations matching the lower bound exist only in neighborhood structures of matching-realizable hypergraphs (reality of matching polynomial zero distributions), the structural criterion corresponding one-to-one to the tight instances of the Lovasz local lemma. (LCC dimension tower descent)

**中文。** 定义：局部可校正码（LCC）的维数障碍：(k,δ)-LCC 的维数 K(n) 的指数上界的降维率。猜想：任意常数查询 LCC 的维数 K(n) ≤ n^{1-Ω(1)}（次线性-线性分离的定量形式）；且 t-查询 LCC 的上界为 n^{1-Ω(log^{1-t} 1)} 型塔式递降（t 增大指数障碍衰减为亚幂律）；匹配下界的构型仅存在于匹配多项式零点分布的实性（matching realizable）超图的邻域结构中，结构判据与 Lovász local lemma 的紧实例一一对应。
