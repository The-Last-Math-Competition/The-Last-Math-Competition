# 00000009727

**English.** Definition: Inverse scattering for sine-Gordon: parameterized manifolds of breathers and kinks. Conjecture: The parameter set of breather solutions of sine-Gordon in weighted Sobolev classes is a finite-dimensional smooth manifold with direct-product structure in inverse scattering coordinates; the dimension of the parametrization is exactly the breather count plus twice the phase degrees of freedom. (sine-Gordon breather manifold)

**中文。** 定义：sine-Gordon 的逆散射：呼吸子与扭结的参数化流形。猜想：sine-Gordon 在加权 Sobolev 类的呼吸子解的参数集为有限维光滑流形且流形在逆散射坐标下为直积结构；参数化的维数恰为呼吸子个数加两倍相位自由度。（sG 呼吸子流形）
