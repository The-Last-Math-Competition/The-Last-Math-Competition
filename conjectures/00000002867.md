# 00000002867

**English.** Definition: The family index theorem: index bundles of families of elliptic operators over fibrations. Conjecture: The characteristic-class rigidity of family indices: the Chern character of the index bundle is the closure of the differential representative of the family index form, with closure uniqueness fixed by gauge normalization of the differential structure. (family index characteristic rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：族指标定理：纤维化上椭圆算子族的指标束。猜想：族指标的示性类的刚性：指标束的陈示性类为族指标形式的微分代表的闭包且闭包的唯一性为微分结构的规范固定。（族指标示性刚性）
