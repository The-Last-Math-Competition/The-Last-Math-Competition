# 00000009435

**English.** Definition: The duality of Moebius: mu and zeros. Conjecture: The spectral side of mu: the correspondence of functions with zeros, with the rigidity of the correspondence the explicit formula. (Moebius explicit formula zero correspondence duality)

**中文。** 定义：Möbius 的对偶：μ 与零点。猜想：μ 的谱侧：函数与零点的对应且应的刚性为显式公式。（Mobius 显式公式对偶谱侧函数零点对应刚性相关）
