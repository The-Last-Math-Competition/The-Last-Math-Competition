# 00000001231

**CG-31 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: The order of the Jacobian of the Johnson graph J(n,k). Conjecture: |Jac(J(n,k))| = (C(n−2,k−1))^{C(n,k)−1}·∏_i f(i), where f is the explicit small-factor polynomial f(i) = (i²−i+1)^{e_i}; the formula follows from the matrix-tree theorem for complete graphs restricted to Young subgroups.

**中文。** 定义：Johnson 图 J(n,k) 的雅可比群的阶。猜想：|Jac(J(n,k))| = (C(n-2,k-1))^{C(n,k)-1}·∏_{i} f(i),其中 f 为显式小因子多项式 f(i)=(i²−i+1)^{e_i};该公式由完全图的矩阵树定理经 Young 子群限制给出。
