**NF2-13 ◆** — source: 218_非线性傅里叶分析.md

**English.** Definition: Product estimates are the norm law of multiplication. Conjecture: The critical deficit is logarithmic type; the saturation of the deficit is at the endpoint; and the endpoint deficit closes by a double-logarithmic correction. (logarithmic critical deficit of products)

**中文。** 定义：乘积估计为乘法的范数律。猜想：临界亏损为对数型；且亏损的饱和为端点；且端点的亏损由双重对数修正闭合。（乘积临界对数亏损）
