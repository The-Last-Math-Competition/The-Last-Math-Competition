**GL3-23 ★** — source: 155_几何Langlands的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The condensed spectrum is the family of spectral coefficient objects of the geometric Langlands correspondence. Conjecture: The condensed-spectrum conversion of GL_2 is the explicit standard pairing of local parameters, with the pairing unique at supercuspidals; the norm of the pairing is always 1; and the cusp versus non-cusp boundary is given by the conductor of the local parameter with conductor threshold 2. (standard pairing of the GL_2 condensed spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：凝聚谱：几何 Langlands 对应的谱系数对象族。猜想：GL₂ 的凝聚谱换算为局部参数的标准配对显式且超尖点处配对唯一；配对的范数恒为 1；尖点与非尖点的分界由局部参数的导子给出且导子阈值为 2。（GL2 凝聚谱标准配对）
