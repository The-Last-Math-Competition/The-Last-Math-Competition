# 00000004076

**English.** Definition: A Wada boundary is a boundary whose boundary points belong simultaneously to the boundaries of three or more basins. Conjecture: On the plane a minimal realization of a Wada boundary requires exactly two quadratic tangencies, and the parameter-space dimension of the minimal realization family is 2. (dimension of minimal Wada realizations)

**中文。** 定义：Wada 边界指边界点同时属于三个及以上盆地的边界。猜想：平面上 Wada 边界的最小实现恰需两条二次切线,最小实现族的参数空间维数为 2。（Wada 最小实现维数）
