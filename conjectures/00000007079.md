**English.** Definition: The symplectic side of Riemann-Hilbert with the categorification of Stokes data. Conjecture: The category of Stokes local systems is equivalent to Fukaya, and the bridge of the equivalence is nonstopped radiation. (nonstopped-radiation Stokes equivalence)
