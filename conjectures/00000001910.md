# 00000001910

**HR-10 △** — source: 49_调和分析II.md

**English.** Definition: Weighted restriction (weights w ∈ A_p). Conjecture: The optimal exchange between the weighted constant of spherical restriction and the A_p characteristic of radial weights is an explicit interpolation formula (weighted restriction exchange).

**中文。** 定义：加权限制(权 w ∈ A_p)。猜想：球面限制的加权常数与径向权的 A_p 特征的最佳交换为显式插值公式(加权限制交换)。
