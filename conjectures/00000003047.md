# 00000003047

**English.** Definition: Symmetry tests: the existence of higher generalized symmetries. Conjecture: The tower growth law of symmetries: the orders of symmetries of integrable equations form an odd arithmetic progression, with common difference the order of the minimal master symmetry of the equation. (symmetry tower common difference law)

**中文。** 定义：对称性检验：高阶广义对称的存在性。猜想：对称的塔增长律：可积方程的对称的阶数为奇数算术级数且级数的公差等于方程的最小主对称的阶。（对称塔公差定律）
