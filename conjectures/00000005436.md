# 00000005436

**HM2-26 ★** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: The support equivalence is the coincidence of the Martin boundary with the hitting support. Conjecture: The equivalence criterion of bounded domains is the explicit uniform chain condition, valid for Lipschitz domains and explicitly failing for slit domains. (uniform chain criterion of support equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：支撑等价指马丁边界与击中支撑的重合。猜想：有界域的等价判据为一致链条件显式,利普希茨域成立且裂隙域显式失效。（支撑等价一致链判据）
