# 00000000464

**English.** Definition: P(τ(G)) is the largest prime factor of τ(G). Conjecture: For families of connected d-regular graphs (vertex count v → ∞), P(τ(G)) ≥ c(d)·log v for an explicit c(d) > 0; and on random d-regular graphs, the distribution of P(τ(G))/log v converges to an exponential distribution.

**中文。** 定义：τ(G) 的最大素因子 P(τ(G))。猜想：对 d-正则连通图族(顶点数 v→∞),P(τ(G)) ≥ c(d)·log v 对显式 c(d)>0 成立;且随机 d-正则图上 P(τ(G))/log v 的分布收敛到指数分布。
