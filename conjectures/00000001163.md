# 00000001163

**English.** Definition: The topological K-theory KO groups of the classifying space BE₈ of the E₈ lattice. Conjecture: The 2-primary part of KO^0(BE₈) has rank 3 with torsion part the three-fold direct sum of Z/2; the structure is explicitly determined by the Adams-operator spectrum of the mod-2 homology of E₈, with no odd torsion.

**中文。** 定义：E₈ 格的分类空间 BE₈ 的拓扑 K-理论 KO 群。猜想：KO^0(BE₈) 的 2-准素部分的秩为 3,其扭部分为 Z/2 的三次直和;该结构由 E₈ 的模 2 同调的 Adams 运算谱显式决定,且无奇素扭。
