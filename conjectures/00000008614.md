**English.** Definition: Early stopping is data-driven stopping of iterative regularization. Conjecture: One-dimensional stopping rules are minimax optimal on the Sobolev scale; the stopping deficit is logarithmic and attained by the Lepski criterion. (optimal early stopping rule)
