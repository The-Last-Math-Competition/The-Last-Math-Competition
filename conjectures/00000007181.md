**English.** Definition: Scheme theory II with the deepening of the Grothendieck framework. Conjecture: The lifting obstruction of affine open covers is the first Cech cohomology, and the vanishing of the obstruction is the flatness of quasicoherent sheaves. (flat-quasicoherent affine-covering obstruction vanishing)
