**English.** Definition: A G-function is a series with controlled divisibility of coefficient denominators. Conjecture: Its logarithmic approximation exponent at algebraic points is the explicit value one plus 1/n, the approximation is attained by diagonal Pade construction, and the bound cannot be compressed further. (tight bound of G-function approximation exponents)

**中文。** 定义：G 函数指系数分母整除性控制的级数。猜想：其在代数点的对数逼近指数为一加 n 分之一型显式值,逼近由对角 Padé 构造达到且界不可再压。（G 函数逼近指数紧界）
