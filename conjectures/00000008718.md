# 00000008718

**MBL-08 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: The random XY chain: a spin-1/2 chain with random couplings and fields, mappable to free fermions. Conjecture: Single-particle localization for the random XY chain holds rigorously (a rigorous law); quasiparticle localization remains stable at small coupling after adding interactions (a stability domain law); the critical coupling of the stability domain is an explicit function of the variance of the field distribution (an explicitness law); and the universal stability exponent is 1/2 (an exponent law). (random XY quasiparticle stability half exponent)

**中文。** 定义：随机 XY 链：随机耦合与随机场的自旋 1/2 链，可映射为自由费米子。猜想：随机 XY 的单粒子局域化严格成立（严格律）；且加入相互作用后准粒子局域化在小耦合稳定（稳定域律）；稳定域的临界耦合为场分布方差的显式函数（显式律）；稳定性的普适指数为 1/2（指数律）。
