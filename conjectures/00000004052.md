# 00000004052

**English.** Definition: The Dade group is the group of isomorphism classes of endo-permutation modules of a block. Conjecture: The rank of the Dade group is at most the dimension of the rank variety of the defect group, and equality is attained for abelian defect groups. (Dade rank-defect group dimension upper bound)

**中文。** 定义：Dade 群指块的末端置换模同构类群。猜想：Dade 群的秩不超过亏群的秩 variety 维数,且等号在交换亏群时达到。（Dade 秩-亏群维数上界）
