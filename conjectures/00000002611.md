# 00000002611

**English.** Definition: Maximal join-independent families of completely distributive lattices: the completely join-irreducible elements. Conjecture: The cardinality F(L) of maximal join-independent families of a completely distributive lattice can take every value in a cardinal interval; the realizability characterization is a continuum interpolation of prime-element layer counts. (maximal independent family spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：完备分配格的极大无关族：完全分配律的既约元。猜想：完全分配格的极大无关族的势 F(L) 可取基数区间内的一切值；可实现刻画为素元层数的连续统内插。（极大无关族谱）
