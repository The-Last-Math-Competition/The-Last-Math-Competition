# 00000006881

**English.** Definition: Geometric complexity theory studies the separation of VP and VNP via representation theory. Conjecture: An explicit family of obstruction tests for multisymmetric matrix varieties exists, with the degree bound of the obstruction polynomials being quasi-polynomial; and this bound is the lowest threshold for separation along the GCT route. (quasi-polynomial degree bound of GCT obstruction families)

**中文。** 定义：几何复杂性理论（GCT）以表示论研究 VP 与 VNP 的分离。猜想：多重对称矩阵簇的障碍检验的显式族存在且障碍多项式的次数界为拟多项式；且该界为 GCT 路线下分离的最低门槛。（GCT 障碍族拟多项式度数界）
