# 00000005504

**English.** Definition: A load-dependent network has service rates depending on load, with its balance function. Conjecture: The balance function equals the combinatorial count of admissible states, of Fibonacci-type recursion for tree topologies; for ring topologies the count is Lucas type, differing from Fibonacci type by the constant 2; and each increment of the load exponent doubles the recursion order, with multiplier 2. (tree-topology recursion of balance functions)

**中文。** 定义：负荷依赖网络：服务率随负载变化的网络；其平衡函数。猜想：平衡函数等于容许状态的组合计数且树拓扑时为 Fibonacci 型递推；环拓扑时计数为 Lucas 型且与 Fibonacci 型相差常数 2；负荷指数每增一阶递推阶数翻倍且倍数为 2。（平衡函数树拓扑递推）
