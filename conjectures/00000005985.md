**English.** Definition: The dimension criterion is the explicit determination of the Kodaira dimension. Conjecture: In three dimensions the characteristic-number combination is graded by the Euler characteristic with the signature, with grading weight one half; the critical value of the criterion is 2, general type above; and the Euler characteristic and signature agree in parity, the difference being a multiple of 24. (Euler-signature grading of three-dimensional characteristics)

**中文。** 定义：维数判据：小平维数的显式测定。猜想：三维的示性数组合由欧拉特征与符号差给出分度且分度的权为一半；判据的临界值为 2 且临界上为一般型；欧拉特征与符号差的奇偶一致且一致差为 24 的倍数。（三维示性欧拉符号分度）
