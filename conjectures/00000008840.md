# 00000008840

**English.** Definition: Solvability of monotone inclusions under splitting. Conjecture: The graph version of Minty's theorem always holds; the zero set is closed convex and given by fixed points of the graph reflection. (Minty graph reflection zero set)

**中文。** 定义：分裂的单调包含的可解性。猜想：Minty 定理的图版刻画恒成立；零点集为闭凸且由图反演的不动点给出。（Minty 图反演零点集）
