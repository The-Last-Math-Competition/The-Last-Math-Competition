# 00000008062

**English.** Definition: The Dickson-type bound for Higman's lemma: the combinatorial length function H_h(n) of the Higman (word embedding) lemma (the minimal escape length). Conjecture: The growth rate of H_h(n) is dominated by H_{omega^omega} in the Cichon hierarchy and the domination is tight (no faster growth necessary); and the conversion law between the Higman function H_k for alphabet size k and Erdos-Szekeres-type functions: H_{k+1} behaves as the iteration H_k composed with H_k (a composition law for alphabet growth); the normalization of length functions (de Bruijn base change) has base-conversion constant 2 (universality of binary conversion). (Higman Cichon composition base law)

**中文。** 定义：Higman 引理的迪金森型界：Higman（词嵌入）引理的组合长度函数 H_h(n)（最小逃逸长度）。猜想：H_h(n) 的增长率被 Cichon 层级中的 H_{ω^ω} 支配且支配为紧（无更快的必要增长）；且字母表大小 k 的 Higman 函数 H_k 与 Erdős–Szekeres 型函数的复合换算律：H_{k+1} ~ H_k∘H_k 型迭代（字母递增的合成律）；长度函数的均匀化（de Bruijn 的换基）的基变换常数为 2（二基换算的普适性）。
