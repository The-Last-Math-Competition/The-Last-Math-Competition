**English.** Definition: Lie semigroups: the Lie theory of semigroups. Conjecture: The reachability criterion: the interior of the reachable set of a control system is nonempty if and only if the generated Lie algebra is full-dimensional, with the verification of full dimensionality the Chow condition. (semigroup reachability Chow full dimension)

**中文。** 定义：Lie semigroup：半群的李理论。猜想：可达性判据：控制系统的可达集的内部非空当且仅当生成的李代数满维且满维的验证为 Chow 条件。（半群可达 Chow 满维）
