# 00000002387

**FM-07 ★** — source: 65_分形几何与测度.md

**English.** Definition: The growth rate of the fundamental group of the Sierpinski carpet. Conjecture: The supremum of the growth rate is a power of λ with λ an explicit product of the embedding lattice; and the combination of the product is the union of generators. (carpet group growth rate lattice product)

**中文。** 定义：Sierpinski 地毯基本群的增长率。猜想：增长率的上确界为 λ 的幂且 λ 为嵌入格的显式乘积；且乘积的组合为生成元并集。（地毯群增长率格乘积）
