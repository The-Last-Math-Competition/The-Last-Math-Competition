# 00000002066

**English.** Definition: A cohesive topos is a topos with shape modality ♭ ⊣ ♯. Conjecture: The correctness of the double flat-sharpening ♯∘♭ is locally the identity within the closure of differential cohesion (i.e. the double application is the identity); this condition is necessary and sufficient for cohesion.

**中文。** 定义：内聚拓扑斯指具形状模态 ♭⊣♯ 的拓扑斯。猜想：对偶平坦化 ♯∘♭ 的正确性在微分同调的闭包中为局部恒等(即二次应用恒等);该条件为内聚性的充要。
