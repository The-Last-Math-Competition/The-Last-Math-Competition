# 00000003050

**English.** Definition: The algebraic entropy test: vanishing of complexity growth of maps. Conjecture: The zero criterion for algebraic entropy: a map is integrable if and only if its algebraic entropy is zero, and the near-zero correction of the entropy is a log-periodic function. (algebraic entropy zero log-periodicity)

**中文。** 定义：代数熵检验：映射的复杂性增长的消失。猜想：代数熵的零判据：映射可积当且仅当代数熵为零且熵的近零修正为对数周期函数。（代数熵零对数周期）
