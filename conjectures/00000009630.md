# 00000009630

**English.** Definition: Ergodic optimization studies optimal measures for the supremal average functional integral f d mu; optimality of periodic measures is controlled by the regularity of the potential. Conjecture: On mixing SFTs, potentials with sufficiently small Lipschitz constant have periodic-orbit optimizing measures; the critical Lipschitz constant is an explicit function of the spectral gap, beyond which optimizing measures with full support exist and the phase transition occurs exactly once. (ergodic optimization critical value)

**中文。** 定义：遍历优化研究 supremum 平均泛函 ∫f dμ 的最优测度；周期测度最优性受势的正则性控制。猜想：混合 SFT 上 Lipschitz 常数充分小的势的优化测度为周期轨道；临界 Lipschitz 常数为谱隙的显式函数，超过临界值时存在具全支撑的优化测度且相变恰有一次。（遍历优化临界值）
