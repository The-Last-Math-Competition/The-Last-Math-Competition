# 00000005492

**English.** Definition: The finite-size conversion is the scaling relation from the lattice to the thermodynamic limit. Conjecture: The conversion is an explicit series of exponential corrections, the exponents given by the physical mass and imaginary momenta, and the leading coefficient is one. (leading term one of the finite-size exponential series)

**中文。** 定义：有限尺寸换算指格点到热极限的标度关系。猜想：换算为指数修正的显式级数,指数由物理质量与虚动量给出且首项系数为一。（有限尺寸指数级数首项一）
