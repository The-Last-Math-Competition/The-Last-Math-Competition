**ERO-05 ★** — source: 268_遍历优化.md

**English.** Definition: Bousch rigidity: the complete locking phenomenon of the structure of maximizing measures for a given Lipschitz function on interval maps. Conjecture: On the interval, locking is Baire-generic and the dominating periodic orbits number at most 3 (a three-period domination law); the minimal dimension for the failure of locking on higher-dimensional tori is 2 (a two-dimensional failure law); and the lower bound on the density of partial locking is 1/2, meaning at least half of the periodic orbits participate in the approximation of maximizing measures (a half-density law). (Bousch locking three-period half-density)

**中文。** 定义：Bousch 刚性：区间映射上关于给定 Lipschitz 函数的极大化测度结构的完全锁定现象。猜想：区间上锁定现象在 Baire 意义下普遍且支配周期轨道数不超过 3（三周期支配律）；高维圆环上锁定的失效的最小维数为 2（二维失效律）；部分锁定的密度下界为 1/2，即至少一半的周期轨道参与极大化测度的逼近（半密度律）。
