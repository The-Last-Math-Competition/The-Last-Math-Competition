# 00000006347

**QG2-07 ★** — source: 197_量子群II.md

**English.** Definition: The global basis with the leading-coefficient structure of the dual canonical basis. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit for the leading coefficients of the dual basis, the leading term one; higher genera close by the inversion series; and the convergence radius of the inversion is 1. (closed inversion conversion of dual leading terms)

**中文。** 定义：全局基：对偶典范基的首系数结构。猜想：换算常数为对偶基首系数的显式且首项为一；高阶亏格由反演级数封闭；反演的收敛半径为 1。（对偶首项反演封闭换算）
