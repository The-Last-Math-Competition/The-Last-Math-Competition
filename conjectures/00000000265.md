**AA-12 ◆** — source: 16_代数与算术对象.md

**English.** Definition: The rational distance problem concerns points in the plane whose distances to all four vertices of the unit square are rational. Conjecture: Apart from the symmetric points of the square itself, no such point exists; and in standard position there are exactly four symmetric points. (rational distance square has no solution)

**中文。** 定义：有理距离问题指平面上到单位正方形四顶点距离全为有理数的点。猜想：除正方形自身的对称点外不存在这样的点；且对称点在标准位置恰有四个。（有理距离方格无解）
