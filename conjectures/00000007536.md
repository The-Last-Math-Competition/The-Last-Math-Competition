# 00000007536

**English.** Definition: Special values of L-functions as the integrality of zeta values of etale cohomology. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the purity of special values with special-point weight integers; the integrality spectrum is complete with kernel the rank part of Beilinson's conjecture; and the denominator of the integrality is the index of the Abel-Jacobi image. (special-value integrality conversion)

**中文。** 定义：L 函数特殊值：etale 上同调的 zeta 函数值的整性。猜想：换算常数为特殊值的纯性且特殊点权整数；整性谱完全且谱的核为 Beilinson 猜想的秩部分；整性的分母为 Abel–Jacobi 像的指标。（特殊值整性换算常数）
