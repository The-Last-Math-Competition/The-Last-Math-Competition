**English.** Definition: generates pair of commutator structure: differential operator ring of Weyl algebra. Conjecture: Weyl algebra in commutator equation AB BA is solution pair of unit element is parameter of finite parameter family; family is trace positive of front two algebra independent element and parameter space is two dimension algebraic variety. (Weyl commutator finite parameter -ization)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl 代数的交换子结构：微分算子环的生成对。猜想：Weyl 代数中交换子方程 AB 减 BA 为单位元的解对为有限参数族；族的参数为前两代数独立元素的迹修正且参数空间为二维代数簇。（Weyl 交换子有限参数化）
