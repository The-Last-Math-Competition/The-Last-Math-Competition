# 00000009999

**English.** Definition: Commutator structure of Weyl algebras: generating pairs of rings of differential operators. Conjecture: Solution pairs of the commutator equation AB minus BA equals the identity in the Weyl algebra form a finite-parameter family; the parameters are trace corrections of the first two algebraically independent elements, and the parameter space is a two-dimensional algebraic variety. (Weyl commutator finite parametrization)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl 代数的交换子结构：微分算子环的生成对。猜想：Weyl 代数中交换子方程 AB 减 BA 为单位元的解对为有限参数族；族的参数为前两代数独立元素的迹修正且参数空间为二维代数簇。（Weyl 交换子有限参数化）
