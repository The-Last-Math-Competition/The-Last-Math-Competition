# 00000001500

**English.** Definition: Boundary rigidity means the boundary distance function determines the interior metric; a simple manifold is one all of whose geodesics have no conjugate points. Conjecture: The tomographic condition for boundary rigidity of negatively curved simple manifolds is: the ellipsoid condition on second tangential derivatives of the boundary distance function is nondegenerate everywhere; the condition is explicit and verifiable pointwise.

**中文。** 定义：边界刚性指流形的边界距离函数决定内部度量;简单流形指一切测地线无共轭点。猜想：负曲率的简单流形为边界刚性的层析条件为:边界距离函数在边界切向的二阶导数椭球条件处处非退化;该条件显式且可逐点验证。
