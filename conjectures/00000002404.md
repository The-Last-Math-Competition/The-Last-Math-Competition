# 00000002404

**English.** Conjecture: The Julia dimensions of compositions of polynomials satisfy dim J(f∘g) ≥ max(dim J(f), dim J(g)), with strict inequality when f, g are noncommuting; the parameter conditions for the strict family are explicit.

**中文。** 猜想：多项式的复合的 Julia 维数满足 dim J(f∘ g)≥\max(dim J(f),dim J(g))(不等式严格)当 f,g 非交换;严格族的参数条件显式。
