# 00000002896

**English.** Definition: The Hasse–Weil bound for curves over finite fields: the absolute bound on point counts. Conjecture: The tower structure of maximal curves: the limit of towers of curves attaining the Weil upper bound exists and equals an explicit constant of the Deligne–Lusztig lower bound; the complete classification of towers corresponds to the minimal representations of Suzuki and Ree groups. (maximal curve tower limit)

**中文。** 定义：有限域曲线的 Hasse–Weil 界：点计数的绝对值界。猜想：极大曲线的塔结构：达到 Weil 上界的曲线塔的极限存在且极限等于 Deligne–Lusztig 下界的显式常数；塔的完全分类对应于 Suzuki 与 Ree 群的极小表示。（极大曲线塔极限）
