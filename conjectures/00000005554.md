# 00000005554

**English.** Definition: The convergence radius is the boundary value of overconvergence. Conjecture: The radius lower bound is the explicit power of the spectral slope, with power the reciprocal of the weight, tight and not lowerable away from slopes. (slope-power radius lower bound of overconvergence)

**中文。** 定义：收敛半径指过收敛的边界值。猜想：半径下界为谱斜率的显式幂,幂为权倒数且非斜坡处紧不可再降。（过收敛斜率幂半径下界）
