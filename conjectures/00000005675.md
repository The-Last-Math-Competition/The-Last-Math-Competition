**RW2-25 ★** — source: 174_随机游走II_混合与覆盖.md

**English.** Definition: A nonreversible walk has circulation. Conjecture: The scaling limit is always the explicit matrix of the antisymmetric flow, given by the skew part of the transition differences, and the limit is a diffusion with drift. (skew-flow diffusion limit of nonreversible walks)

**中文。** 定义：非可逆游走指带环流的行走。猜想：标度极限恒为反对称流的显式矩阵；由转移差的对斜部分给出且极限为带流扩散。（非可逆对斜流扩散极限）
