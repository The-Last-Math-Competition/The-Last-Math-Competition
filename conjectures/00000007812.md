# 00000007812

**English.** Definition: The counts N_g(q) of genus g curves on the quintic threefold X_5 in P^4 are Gromov-Witten type invariants via the reduced GW theory. Conjecture: For each fixed g, N_g(q) is the coefficient of a quasimodular form of weight 2g-2+q; and N_g(0) = 0 for g at least 2, while N_g(-1), the irrational-curve Kahler correction, is the rational multiple (-1)^g(2g-2)! of the Hurwitz number H_g. (quintic curve counts quasimodularity)

**中文。** 定义：五次三维超曲面 X_5⊂P^4 的亏格 g 曲线计数 N_g(q) 为 Gromov–Witten 型不变量（经约化 GW 理论）。猜想：对每个固定 g，N_g(q) 为次数 2g-2+q 型的拟模形式系数；且 N_g(0)=0 对 g≥2 而 N_g(-1)（无理曲线 Kähler 修正）为 Hurwitz 数 H_g 的有理倍数，倍数为 (-1)^g(2g-2)!。
