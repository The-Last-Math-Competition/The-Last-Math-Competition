# 00000008081

**FUS-01 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The exoticness of a fusion system F (a saturated fusion system on a p-group S) means F is not isomorphic to the fusion system of any finite group. Conjecture: The minimal base rank of exotic fusion systems over freely abelian p-subgroups of 2-rank at least 3 is 3; the classification of the link structure of all known exotic systems (Solomon and Levy-Oliver types) converges to a finite list: for p = 2, the dimension of the Frattini quotient of O_2(F) of the automorphism group of the minimal exotic system is at least 2 (exceptional rigidity); and the first Chern class obstruction to the spatial realization of exotic systems in Morava E-theory is a quadratic form (the minimal example). (exotic base-rank three link rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统 F（在 p-群 S 上的饱和融合系统）的 exotic 性指 F 不同构于任何有限群的融合系统。猜想：在 2-秩 ≥3 的自由交换 p-子群上的 exotic 融合系统的极小基底秩为 3；且一切已知的 exotic 系（Solomon、Levy–Oliver 型）的链环结构分类收敛于有限列表：以 p=2 为例，极小 exotic 系的自同构群的 O_2(F) 的 Frattini 商的维数 ≥ 2（例外刚性）；且 exotic 系在 Morava E-理论的空间实现中的第一 Chern 类障碍为二次型（极小例子）。
