# 00000009665

**English.** Definition: Random band matrices: the localization-delocalization transition of n by n band matrices of bandwidth W. Conjecture: The Anderson-type transition occurs at W = n^{1/2}: for W much smaller than n^{1/2} eigenvectors localize at exponential rate, and for W much larger they delocalize completely with sine-universal spectral statistics; the critical window has logarithmic width n^{o(1)}. (band matrix critical bandwidth)

**中文。** 定义：随机带矩阵：带宽 W 的 n×n 带状矩阵的局域-离域转变。猜想：Anderson 型转变发生在 W = n^{1/2}：W 远小于 n^{1/2} 时特征向量以指数速率局域化，W 远大于 n^{1/2} 时完全离域且谱统计为 sine 普适；临界窗口宽为 n^{o(1)} 的对数尺度。（带矩阵临界带宽）
