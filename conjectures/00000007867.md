# 00000007867

**RAM-27 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The speedup separation of parallel randomized repetition: the success probability p_s(n^R) of an R-round parallel randomized algorithm (n independent samples per round) simulating a serial algorithm. Conjecture: The ratio rho_{R,n} of the success probability of R rounds with n samples each to Rn serial steps satisfies rho_{R,n} >= 1 - cR/n (near-serial) with optimal c = 1; and for systems with exponential-tail failure (rate e^{-t}), the optimal allocation of parallel redundancy is geometrically increasing n_i = n q^i with q = e^{-1}, giving a total cost saving factor (1-e^{-1})^{-1}. (parallel repetition geometric allocation)

**中文。** 定义：并行随机重复的加速分离：R-round 并行随机算法（每轮 n 次独立采样）模拟串行算法的成功概率 p_s(n^R)。猜想：R 轮-每轮 n 采样的并行模拟与 Rn 步串行的成功概率之比 ρ_{R,n} 满足 ρ_{R,n} ≥ 1-c·R/n（接近串行），且 c=1 为最优；对指数尾失败（失败率 e^{-t}）的系统，并行冗余的最优分配为等比递增 n_i=n·q^i（q=e^{-1}），总成本节省因子为 (1-e^{-1})^{-1}。
