# 00000008331

**English.** Definition: The trigonometric approximation of nonlinear Diophantine problems: the square cancellation wy_c of Weyl sums for uniform distribution of polynomial sequences P(n) mod 1. Conjecture: The universality of wy_c (square-root cancellation for all nonconstant integer-coefficient P) has proof threshold the non-quadratic leading coefficient (a non-quadratic threshold); for quadratic leading coefficients (bad alpha) the growth of enhanced sums is bounded by the three-quarters bound n^{3/4+epsilon} (a three-quarters bound law); and tightness (attaining P) is achieved by quadratic forms P(n) = alpha n^2 with optimal alpha (an optimal quadratic alpha law), alpha constructed by fractal continued fractions (a fractal continued fraction construction). (Weyl three-quarters fractal continued fraction)
