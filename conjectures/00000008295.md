# 00000008295

**English.** Definition: The quasi-morphism nature of Entov-Polterovich weights: the convexity quasi_ball of the unit ball (the distribution of weights) of the spectral pseudo-norm. Conjecture: The extreme points of quasi_ball (the unit ball of the quasi-morphism) are the extremal points of stabilization invariants (a stable extremal law); the value spectrum of the quasi-morphism (the weights of all Hamiltonians) is the closed interval [0,1] (a closed interval spectrum law); the correction to partial homogeneity (m-th order homogeneity) of weights is the discretization of the logarithm (a log discretization law); and the exceptional set where weights are differentiable (differentiability of generic Hamiltonians) is a fractal boundary (a fractal boundary exception law) of dimension 1/2 (a half-dimension law). (Entov-Polterovich quasi-morphism closed interval fractal)

**中文。** 定义：Entov–Polterovich 的重量的准态度：谱伪范数的单位球（重量的分布）的凸性 quasi_ball。猜想：quasi_ball（准态度的单位球的端点）为稳定化不变量的极值点（稳定极值律）；且准态度的值谱（一切 Hamilton 的重量的值）为 [0,1] 的闭区间（闭区间谱律）；重量的部分齐性（m 次齐性）的修正为 log 的离散化（log 离散化律）；重量的可微（一般 Hamilton 的可微性）的例外集为分形边界（分形边界例外律），边界的维数为 1/2（半维数律）。
