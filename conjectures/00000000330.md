**English.** Definition: The Khinchin sequence class Ψ consists of admissible sequences of variable errors ψ_j. Conjecture: Prime denominators and variable errors interchange: the prime zero-one law holds for every monotone admissible Ψ; and the criterion is the weighted divergence of prime sums. (prime variable-error zero-one law)

**中文。** 定义：Khinchin 序列类 Ψ 为可变误差 ψ_j 的容许序列。猜想：素数分母与可变误差可交换：素数版零一律对一切单调容许 Ψ 成立；且判据为素数和的加权发散。（素数可变误差零一律）
