**FUS-06 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The p-parity of the Z*-theorem for fusion systems: the fusion version of Glauberman's Z*-theorem for odd p — the lifting constant iso(F) of isolated involutions of the center of F. Conjecture: The Z*-theorem for fusion systems at odd primes holds (existence of the canonical lifting of isolated p-elements); and the complete classification of failures at p = 2 consists of exceptional pairs of symplectic-type automorphisms (the semidirect product exception of Q_8); the invariance of the canonicity of the lifting is a characteristic-2 block invariant of the fusion system (a half-integral law for the rank of Broue blocks). (odd-p Z-star symplectic exception list)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的 Z*-定理的 p-奇性：奇 p 的融合系统的 Z*-定理（Glauberman）的融合版：F 的中心的孤立元（isolated involution）的提升常 iso(F)。猜想：奇素数 p 的融合系统的 Z*-定理成立（孤立 p-元的规范提升的存在）；且 p=2 的失效的完全分类为 symplectic 型自同构的例外对（Q_8 的半直积例外）；提升的规范性的不变量为融合系统的特征 2 的块不变量（Broué 的块的秩的半整律）。
