# 00000003006

**GD-26 ★** — source: 85_微分方程的Galois理论.md

**English.** Definition: Closed forms of hypergeometric sums: the Gosper–Petkovsek criterion. Conjecture: The existence criterion for closed forms of hypergeometric-term sums: the sum is not hypergeometric if and only if the Gosper equation has no rational solution, and the verification of nonexistence is polynomial time. (Gosper polynomial criterion)

**中文。** 定义：超几何和的闭式：Gosper–Petkovsek 判据。猜想：超几何项的和的闭式存在判据：和不超几何当且仅当 Gosper 方程无有理解且无解的验证为多项式时间。（Gosper 多项式判据）
