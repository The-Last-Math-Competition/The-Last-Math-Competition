# 00000000959

**English.** Definition: The basis constant of a Schauder basis is the uniform norm of the partial-sum projections. Conjecture: The Marcinkiewicz basis constant (the optimal basis constant in the rearrangement-invariant sense) is equivalent to the ordinary basis constant under ergodic-average weighting: their ratio is bounded by 2, with equality exactly for spaces containing ℓ₁ block bases; this characterization makes the Marcinkiewicz invariant computable.

**中文。** 定义：Schauder 基的基常数指部分和投影的一致范数。猜想：Marcinkiewicz 基常数(在重排不变意义下的最优基常数)与通常基常数在遍历平均加权下等价:两者之比被 2 控制,且比值为 2 的空间恰含 ℓ₁ 的块基;该刻画给出 Marcinkiewicz 不变量的可计算性。
