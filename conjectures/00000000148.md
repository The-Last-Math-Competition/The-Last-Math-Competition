**English.** Definition: Equal mean order for prime rotations versus integer rotations. Conjecture: For algebraic irrational α, the difference between the prime-time and integer-time means is an explicit power of a logarithmic window; the equal order is controlled by the Diophantine exponent. (equal mean order of prime and integer rotations)

**中文。** 定义：素数旋转与整数旋转的均值同阶性。猜想：对代数无理 α，素数时刻与整数时刻的均值之差为对数窗内的显式幂；同阶性由丢番图指数控制。（素数整数旋转同阶）
