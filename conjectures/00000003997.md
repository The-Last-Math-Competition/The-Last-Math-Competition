# 00000003997

**English.** Definition: The translation of bounded arithmetic is the encoding of first-order arithmetic proofs as sequences of propositional proofs. Conjecture: The translation bit length of every bounded arithmetic Sigma theorem is O(n log n) in the theorem length, and the logarithmic factor cannot be removed. (linearithmic translation bit-length bound)

**中文。** 定义：有界算术的翻译指一阶算术证明到命题证明序列的编码。猜想：一切有界算术 Σ 定理的翻译位长为定理长度的 O(n log n),且对数因子不可去除。（翻译位长线性对数界）
