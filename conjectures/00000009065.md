# 00000009065

**SMI-25 ★** — source: 287_子流形几何不等式.md

**English.** Definition: k-degeneracy of submanifolds: relative nullity dimension. Conjecture: The spectrum of degenerate isotropic orders closes in low dimensions; the upper bound of the spectrum is the codimension; and the gaps of the spectrum are given by rank deficits of the second fundamental form. (k-degenerate spectrum low-dimensional closure)

**中文。** 定义：子流形的 k-退化为相对零化维数。猜想：退化迷向阶的谱在低维封闭；且谱的上界为余维数；且谱的间隙由第二基本形的秩亏给出。（k-退化谱低维封闭）
