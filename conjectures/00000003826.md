**RC-06 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: The tropicalization X_trop of a geometric crystal X denotes the integer graph obtained by letting rational-function arrow rules tend to infinity and taking logarithms. Conjecture: The number of connected components of X_trop equals the number of isolated critical points of the potential function of X. (tropical component-critical point)

**中文。** 定义：几何晶体 X 的 tropical 化 X_trop 指对有理函数箭头规则取值趋于无穷并取对数后所得的整数图。猜想：X_trop 的连通分量个数等于 X 的势函数的孤立临界点个数。（tropical 分量-临界点）
