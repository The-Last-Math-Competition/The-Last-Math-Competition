# 00000001270

**English.** Definition: Multidimensional Sturmian words are low-complexity cut-and-project words on Z²; their generating cone is the closed convex cone of all occurrence-frequency vectors. Conjecture: The generating cone of a multidimensional Sturmian word always has dimension 2 (the frequency cone is planar); beyond dimension 2, factors of complexity ≥ 2n must occur; this dimensional barrier beyond the Nivat conjecture is given by the geometry of cut-and-project.

**中文。** 定义：多维 Sturmian 词指 Z² 上的低复杂度切割投影词,其生成锥指一切出现频率向量的闭凸锥。猜想：多维 Sturmian 词的生成锥的维数恒为 2(即频率锥为平面锥),维数 3 以上的情形必须出现复杂度 ≥ 2n 的因子;Nivat 猜想之外的这一维数壁垒由切割投影的几何给出。
