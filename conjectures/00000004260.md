# 00000004260

**English.** Definition: The correlators of a cohomological field theory are the tensor sequences of its multiplicative structure. Conjecture: There exist two cohomological field theories whose one-point correlators all coincide while their higher multiplications differ, and the smallest realization appears in the difference of the genus-zero cubic potentials. (minimal genus of CohFT multiplication separation)

**中文。** 定义：上同调场论的关联子指其乘法结构张量列。猜想：存在两个上同调场论的一次关联子全同而高次乘法不同,最小实现出现在亏格零三次势的差。（CohFT 乘法分离最小亏格）
