# 00000004609

**English.** Definition: The phase number is the number of chambers of a minimal partition. Conjecture: In dimension d the phase-number upper bound for stable partitions is the explicit 2 to the d, and the bound is attained by orthogonal sphere configurations and cannot be exceeded. (phase number bound 2 to the d for stable partitions)

**中文。** 定义：相数指极小分区的腔室个数。猜想：维数 d 中稳定分区的相数上界为二的 d 次幂型显式,上界由正交球构型达到且不可超越。（稳定分区相数 2 的 d 次幂界）
