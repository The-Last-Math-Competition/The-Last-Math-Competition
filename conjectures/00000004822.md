# 00000004822

**English.** Definition: Normalized coefficients are the Fourier coefficients scaled by prime powers. Conjecture: The joint limiting distribution of integral and half-integral weight coefficients is the product of independent uniform circle and beta types, the fourth moment is explicit, and all mixed moments vanish. (joint limiting distribution of integral and half-integral weights)

**中文。** 定义：正规化系数指按素数幂缩放的傅里叶系数。猜想：整权与半整权系数的联合极限分布为独立圆周均匀与贝塔型之积,四阶矩显式且混合矩全部消失。（整半整权联合极限分布）
