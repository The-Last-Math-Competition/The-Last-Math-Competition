# 00000000197

**English.** Definition: S = Σ_{n≥1} 1/(F_n+1). Conjecture: The generating function of the continued-fraction partial quotients of S is transcendental; the transcendence follows from the interplay between the growth rate of the partial quotients and the Legendre constant of golden-ratio approximations. (Fibonacci+1 continued fraction transcendence)

**中文。** 定义：S=Σ_{n≥1} 1/(F_n+1)。猜想：S 的连分数部分商生成函数为超越函数；超越性由部分商增长率与黄金比例逼近的 Legendre 常数交互给出。（Fibonacci+1 连分数超越）
