# 00000004280

**English.** Definition: The endomorphism ring is the ring of additive homomorphisms of a group to itself. Conjecture: There exist two abelian groups whose endomorphism rings are isomorphic as rings while the groups are not isomorphic, and the minimal rank of realization is 3. (minimal rank of endomorphism ring-group separation)

**中文。** 定义：自同态环指群到自身的加法保持同态环。猜想：存在两个交换群其自同态环环同构而群不同构,实现的最小秩为 3。（自同态环-群分离最小秩）
