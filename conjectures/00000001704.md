**EG-14 ★** — source: 42_遍历论II.md

**English.** Definition: Mañé's lemma controls the difference between ∫f dμ_max and the cohomological supremum of f by a coboundary function. Conjecture: The explicit upper bound for the difference is at most Lip(f)·diam(M)/(2 sin(θ/2)), where θ is the coherence angle of the minimal action; the bound is attained by the critical function of rigid rotations.

**中文。** 定义：Mañé 引理指 ∫f dμ_max 与 f 的上同调上确界的差由共边函数控制。猜想：该差的显式上界为 ≤ Lip(f)·diam(M)/(2 sin(θ/2)),θ 为极小作用的相干角;界在刚性旋转的临界函数达到。
