# 00000002906

**English.** Definition: Algebraic-geometric codes (Goppa codes): Riemann–Roch system codes on curves. Conjecture: The double-Romania bound on the minimum distance of Goppa codes: the feasible domain of distance-dimension points is explicitly bounded by the genus trade-off of the curve, with tightness controlled by the extreme points of the Weierstrass semigroup; the extreme points are always paired by the complementary Frobenius numbers of the semigroup. (Goppa double-Romania feasible domain)

**中文。** 定义：代数几何码（Goppa 码）：曲线上的 Riemann–Roch 系统码。猜想：Goppa 码的最小距离的双罗马界：距离-维数点的可行域由曲线的亏格的折衷显式界定且界的紧性由 Weierstrass 半群的极端点控制。(Goppa 双罗马可行域)；且极端点恒由半群补 Frobenius 数配对给出。
