# 00000004770

**FP3-20 △** — source: 144_随机矩阵IV_自由概率.md

**English.** Definition: The Mobius spectrum and the lattice structure are two combinatorial invariants. Conjecture: There exist two families of noncrossing partitions with the same Mobius function spectrum but non-isomorphic lattices, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of an annular family and a boundary family. (Mobius spectrum-lattice separation pair)

**中文。** 定义：Moubius 谱与格结构指两个组合不变量。猜想：存在两非交叉分拆族的 Moubius 函数谱相同而格不同构,分离由环面与带边族的显式对实现。（Moubius 谱-格分离对）
