**English.** Conjecture: For the Bank–Laine equation y'' + A(z)y = 0, an equality-type bound holds between the genus of the meromorphic coefficient A and the exponent of convergence of the zeros of the product q = y₁y₂ (a BL exponent bound).

**中文。** 猜想：Bank–Laine 方程 y'' + A(z)y = 0 的亚纯系数 A 的亏格与解的乘积 q = y₁y₂ 的零点收敛指数的等式界(BL 指数界)。
