**SC-18 ★** — source: 76_组合拓扑_单纯复形.md

**English.** Definition: Wedge decompositions and Zeigler-type examples: combinatorial rigidity of non-PL spheres. Conjecture: The minimal vertex count of non-PL spheres: non-PL simplicial spheres exist from dimension five, with minimal vertex count an explicit lower bound of type d+8; the realizing complexes are minimal triangulations of double suspensions of non-orientable manifolds. (non-PL sphere minimal vertices)

**中文。** 定义：wedge 分解与 Zeigler 型例：非 PL 球面的组合刚性。猜想：非 PL 球面的最小顶点数：非 PL 的单纯球面在维数五起存在且最小顶点数为 d+8 的显式下界；下界的实现复形为双悬浮不可定向流形的极小三角化。（非 PL 球面极小顶点）
