# 00000006212

**English.** Definition: Stochastic scattering is the almost-sure scattering under random initial data. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit domain of almost-sure scattering, the Gaussian-type initial support covering supercritical cases; the failure set of scattering has measure zero, the evidence being the log-divergence exponent; and the scattering threshold of critical random fields is dimension 4. (Gaussian support-domain conversion of almost-sure scattering)

**中文。** 定义：随机散射：随机初值下散射的几乎必然性。猜想：换算常数为几乎必然散射的显式域且高斯型初值的支撑覆盖超临界；散射的失败集为零测且零测性的证据为对数发散指数；临界随机场的散射阈值为维数 4。（几乎必然散射高斯支撑域换算）
