# 00000008020

**English.** Definition: The cone restriction of the Evans-Krylov theorem for fully nonlinear equations: the cone constant epsilon_EK(n) for C^{2,alpha} regularity of concave/convex equations F(D^2 u) = 0. Conjecture: C^{2,alpha} solutions hold within uniform ellipticity cone ratio epsilon < epsilon_EK with an explicit lower bound c/n^2 for epsilon_EK(n); outside the cone (epsilon > epsilon_EK) the counterexample solutions have corner-type power-law singularities (singularity exponent 1 + alpha_neg); the minimal dimension of corner counterexamples is n = 3 (dimensional rigidity of W^{2,p} counterexamples), while at n = 2 global C^{1,1} regularity holds for all cone ratios (the two-dimensional survival law), via monotonicity of conformal invariants under smoothing of the complex structure. (Evans-Krylov cone bound two-dimensional survival)

**中文。** 定义：完全非线性方程的 Evans–Krylov 定理的锥限制：concave/convex 方程 F(D²u)=0 的 C^{2,α} 正则性的锥常数 ε_EK(n)。猜想：C^{2,α} 解在均匀椭圆锥的比值 ε < ε_EK 内成立且 ε_EK(n) 有显式下界 c/n²；且锥外（ε>ε_EK）的反例解的奇性为角点的幂律（奇性指数为 1+α_neg）；角点反例的最小维数 n=3（W^{2,p} 反例的维数刚性），n=2 的 C^{1,1} 的全局正则性在一切锥比值内成立（二维幸免律），幸免律的证明路径为复结构的光滑化的共形不变量的单调性。
