# 00000006620

**English.** Definition: Critical temperature and critical exponents are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two lattice models with the same critical temperature but different critical exponents, and the separation is realized by an explicit long-short-range pair with the same temperature but different universality classes. (same temperature-different exponent long-short separation)

**中文。** 定义：临界温度与临界指数指两层。猜想：存在两格模型的临界温度相同而临界指数不同,分离由同温度异普适类的显式长短程对实现。（温度同-指数异长短程分离）
