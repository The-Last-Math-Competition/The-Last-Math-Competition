# 00000007667

**English.** Definition: The Rogers-Ramanujan continued fraction is R(q)=q^{1/5}/(1+q/(1+q^2/(1+...))). Conjecture: For every algebraic q with 0<|q|<1, the value R(q) is transcendental; furthermore for any two distinct algebraic numbers q_1, q_2, the values R(q_1) and R(q_2) are algebraically independent over Q. (transcendence and algebraic independence of Rogers-Ramanujan values)

**中文。** 定义：Rogers–Ramanujan 连分数 R(q)=q^{1/5}/(1+q/(1+q²/(1+…)))。猜想：对任意代数数 q 满足 0<|q|<1，值 R(q) 为超越数；进一步对任意两个不同的代数数 q_1,q_2，值 R(q_1) 与 R(q_2) 在 Q 上代数无关。
