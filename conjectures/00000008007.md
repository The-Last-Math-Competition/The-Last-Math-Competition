# 00000008007

**English.** Definition: The stability spectrum of radial solutions of the Bernoulli free boundary problem: the eigenvalue spectrum Lambda_i of the linearization of radial solutions (spherical free boundary) of the two-phase Bernoulli problem. Conjecture: Lambda_i = i(i+n-2) (the spectrum of the spherical Laplacian) plus Q_i (a curvature-pressure coupling correction); the sign of the first eigenvalue Lambda_1 is a complete stability criterion (Lambda_1 > 0 stable), and the critical radius r_c of Lambda_1 makes Q_1 = -(n-1): the critical radius is the (n-1)-st root of the ratio of the pressure difference to the surface tension; and the first rupture mode of the evolution of the unstable branch (topological transition) is the m = 2 elliptic splitting (the first channel of the peanut-type transition). (Bernoulli radial peanut first mode)

**中文。** 定义：Bernoulli 问题的自由边界的径向解的稳定性谱：两相 Bernoulli 问题的径向解（球面自由边界）的线性化的特征值谱 Λ_i。猜想：Λ_i = i(i+n-2)（球面 Laplacian 的谱）+ Q_i（曲率-压力耦合修正）；且首特征值 Λ₁ 的符号为稳定性的完全判据（Λ₁>0 稳定），Λ₁ 的临界半径 r_c 使 Q₁ = -(n-1)：临界半径的显式值为压力差与表面张力的比值的 (n-1) 次方根；不稳定分支的演化（拓扑转变）的第一破裂模式为 m=2 的椭圆分裂（花生型转变的第一通道）。
