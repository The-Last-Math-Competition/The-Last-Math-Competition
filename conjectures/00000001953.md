# 00000001953

**LG-23 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: The diameter of Cayley graphs (word metrics of arithmetic groups). Conjecture: The diameter of SL_n(F_p) with respect to a fixed generating set is polynomial in p^{n²−1} (Helfgott-type growth; the exact exponent).

**中文。** 定义：Cayley 图的直径(算术群的字度量)。猜想：SL_n(𝔽_p) 关于固定生成集的直径为 p^{n²−1} 的多项式(Helfgott 型增长;精确指数)。
