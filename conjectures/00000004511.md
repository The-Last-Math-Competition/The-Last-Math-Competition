# 00000004511

**English.** Definition: Random assignment is the minimum-weight perfect matching of an iid exponential cost matrix, and the Parisi constant is its expected limit. Conjecture: In the variant with positional shifts of the cost band, the correction of the expected limit is an explicit quadratic function of the shift, the correction coefficient is universal, and at zero shift it degenerates to the Parisi constant. (universal quadratic shift correction of Parisi)

**中文。** 定义：随机分配指 iid 指数代价矩阵的最小权和匹配,Parisi 常数指其期望极限。猜想：代价带位置偏移的变体中期望极限的修正是偏移的显式二次函数,修正系数普适且零偏移时退化为 Parisi 常数。（Parisi 偏移二次修正普适）
