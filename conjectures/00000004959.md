# 00000004959

**HT4-29 ★** — source: 150_代数拓扑IV_同伦论深化.md

**English.** Definition: The Whitehead product is the canonical bilinear operation on homotopy groups. Conjecture: The product table in low dimensions (stem at most 3) is given by an explicit correction of the Jacobi identity with correction coefficients plus or minus 2; the product vanishes if and only if one factor is a suspension and the degree sum lies in the stable range; and the conversion formula agrees with the Toda bracket operations under the EHP sequence with deviation a quadratic commutator term. (low-dimensional correction of Whitehead products)

**中文。** 定义：Whitehead 乘积：同伦群上的典范双线性运算。猜想：低维（stem 不超过 3）的乘积表由 Jacobi 恒等式的显式修正给出且修正系数为正负 2；乘积消没当且仅当因子之一为 suspension 且次数和落在稳定域；换算公式与 EHP 序列下 Toda 括号运算一致且偏差为二次交换项。（Whitehead 乘积低维修正）
