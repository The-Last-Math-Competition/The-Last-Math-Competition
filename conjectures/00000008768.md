# 00000008768

**English.** Definition: The Adams sum of K(Z): the split orthogonal complement spectrum. Conjecture: The homotopy groups of the Adams sum, after p-localization, are always Gray modules of period 2(p-1); and the leading term of the period is exactly the image spectrum of Quillen operations. (Adams sum Gray periodic module)

**中文。** 定义：K(Z) 的 Adams 和指分裂的正交补谱。猜想：Adams 和的同伦群在 p 局部化后恒为周期 2(p−1) 的 Gray 模；且周期的首项恰为 Quillen 操作的像谱。（Adams 和 Gray 周期模）
