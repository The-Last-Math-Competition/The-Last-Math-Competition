**English.** Definition: Gröbner bases of differential ideals: the computational theory of differential polynomial rings. Conjecture: Termination of differential Gröbner bases: reduced bases of homogeneous differential ideals are finite under rank well-orders, with counterexamples to finiteness being Kolchin's non-Noetherian ring examples. (differential Grobner termination)
