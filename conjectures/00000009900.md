**GBR-20 ★** — source: 315_箭图表示与Auslander-Reiten理论.md

**English.** Definition: Hall algebras of cyclic quivers: the shuffle structure of affine quivers. Conjecture: The correction term of the shuffle product of the Hall algebra of a cyclic quiver is a commutator expansion of the quantum torus; the structure constants of the expansion are explicit combinations of Taylor coefficients of the quantum Dilogarithm, and the coefficients are positive. (cyclic Hall shuffle correction)

**中文。** 定义：循环箭图的 Hall 代数：仿射箭图的洗牌结构。猜想：循环箭图的 Hall 代数的 shuffle 积的修正项为量子环面的交换子展开；展开的结构常数为量子 Dilogarithm 的 Taylor 系数的显式组合且系数为正。（循环 Hall shuffle 修正）
