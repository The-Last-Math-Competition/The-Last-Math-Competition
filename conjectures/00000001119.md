# 00000001119

**English.** Definition: Kostka–Foulkes polynomials are t-weighted tableau counts. Conjecture: The degree of their specialization at t = 1 is at most the number of boxes in the shape difference; and tightness of the degree bound is attained by staircase shapes. (KF degree box bound)

**中文。** 定义：Kostka–Foulkes 多项式为 t-加权的表计数。猜想：该多项式在 t=1 处的下降多项式次数不超过形状差的方格数；且次数界的紧性由阶梯形状达到。（KF 次数方格界）
