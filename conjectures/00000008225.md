# 00000008225

**ORB-25 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The representation-theoretic reading of singular sets of D-modules: the dimension spectrum sing_spec(pi) of singularities of the characteristic variety. Conjecture: The minimal singularity dimension of sing_spec obeys the codimension-2 law dim O minus 2; complete vanishing of singularities (smooth characteristic variety) holds if and only if pi is finite-dimensional (a finite-dimensional smoothness law); the worst singularities (totally singular) occur for degenerations of Whittaker functionals (a degenerate Whittaker law); and the realization of the singularity spectrum (all values realized) is a complete realization of a finite set (a finite full realization law). (characteristic singularity codimension-two Whittaker)

**中文。** 定义：D-模的孤点集的表示论解释：特征簇的奇点的维数谱 sing_spec(π)。猜想：sing_spec 的极小奇点维数为 dim O - 2 的余 2 律；且奇点的完全消没（光滑特征簇）当且仅当 π 为有限维（有限维光滑律）；奇点的最坏（全奇异）的表示为 Whittaker 泛函的退化（退化 Whittaker 律）；奇点谱的实现（一切谱值的实现）为有限集的完全实现（有限全实现律）。
