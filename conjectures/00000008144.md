# 00000008144

**RNG-04 ★** — source: 257_重整化群的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The convergence radius of the operator spectrum of Kadanoff variables: the convergence index conv(RG) of the linearized spectrum rho_i of the renormalization operator in the expansion variables (temperature-field plane). Conjecture: conv (the uniform bound on the convergence radius of the spectrum) is given by the dimension of the least irrelevant operator (an irrelevant domination law); the limit of the gap spectrum (ratios of adjacent rho) is infinite powers of the golden ratio tau (a tau-spectrum law), the densification of the spectrum (vanishing gaps) occurring at d = 4 (the upper critical dimension, a 4-dimensional densification); and the critical exponent of densification is the square-root law epsilon^{1/2} (epsilon = 4 - d). (Kadanoff tau-spectrum four-dimensional densification)

**中文。** 定义：Kadanoff 的变量的算子谱的收敛半径：展开变量（温度-场平面）的重整化算子的线性化谱 ρ_i 的收敛指标 conv(RG)。猜想：conv（谱的收敛半径的一致界）由最不相关的算子的维度给出（irrelevant 支配律）；且谱的间隙谱（相邻 ρ 之比）的极限为黄金比 τ 的无穷幂（τ-谱律），谱的稠密化（间隙消失）在 d=4（上临界维数）发生（4 维稠密化）；稠密化的临界指数 ε^{1/2}（ε=4-d）的平方根律。
