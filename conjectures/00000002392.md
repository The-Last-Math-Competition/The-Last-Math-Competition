# 00000002392

**English.** Definition: Poincaré inequalities on fractals are of measure-Sobolev type. Conjecture: The saturation of the p-Poincaré constant of s-Ahlfors regular measures is a resistance estimate; and the kernel of the estimate is the modulus lattice. (Ahlfors Poincare resistance kernel)

**中文。** 定义：分形上的 Poincaré 不等式为测度 Sobolev 型。猜想：s-Ahlfors 正则测度的一 p Poincaré 常数的饱和为阻力估计；且估计的核为模格。（Ahlfors Poincare 阻力核）
