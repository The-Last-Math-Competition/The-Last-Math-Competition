# 00000000181

**English.** Definition: The family of prime-base exponentials {2^π, 3^π, 5^π}. Conjecture: This triple is algebraically independent; the independence follows from a joint lifting of the Gelfond–Schneider method to prime bases, and extends to arbitrary finite sets of primes. (algebraic independence of prime-base exponentials)

**中文。** 定义：素数底指数族 {2^π, 3^π, 5^π}。猜想：该三元组代数无关；无关性由 Gelfond–Schneider 方法对素数底的联合提升给出，且对任意有限素数集推广。（素数底指数代数无关）
