# 00000001166

**LT-66 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The representation deformation space of a lattice of rank ≥ 2 is the periphery of local rigidity. Conjecture: Outside local rigidity, the deformation space is always a finite-type scheme; and the exceptions to finite type are the exceptional isomorphism classes. (lattice deformation finite type)

**中文。** 定义：秩二以上格的表示形变空间为局部刚性外围。猜想：形变空间在局部刚性之外恒为有限型概形；且有限型的例外为例外同构类。（格形变有限型）
