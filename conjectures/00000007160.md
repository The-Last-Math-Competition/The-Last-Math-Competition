# 00000007160

**English.** Definition: Spectral variation with the Lipschitz dependence of eigenvalues. Conjecture: The spectra of normal matrices are Lipschitz with the Frobenius norm as constant. (Frobenius Hoffman-Wielandt spectral Lipschitz)

**中文。** 定义：谱变分：特征值的 Lipschitz 依赖。猜想：Hoffman–Wielandt：正规矩阵的谱的 Lipschitz 且常数为 Frobenius 范数。（Hoffman-Wielandt Frobenius 正规谱 Lipschitz 常数）
