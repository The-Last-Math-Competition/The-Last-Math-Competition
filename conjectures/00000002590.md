# 00000002590

**CM-30 △** — source: 71_计算复杂性中的数学.md

**English.** Conjecture: There exist mathematical constants (e.g. exponential growth rates of combinatorial sequences) for which any computational lower bound is equivalent to a transcendence criterion of the underlying series; the anchoring constant is of the Σ a^{-2^n} type. (computational constant transcendence anchoring)

**中文。** 猜想：存在数学常数(如组合序列的指数增长率)其任何计算下界等价于一元二次的超越性判据;锚定常数由 Σ a^{-2^n} 型。
