# 00000005908

**English.** Definition: Relative persistence is the persistent structure of relative homology. Conjecture: The explicit quotient of the pair filtration degenerates to absolute persistence when the subcomplex is constant, the kernel of the degeneration being the vanishing of inclusions. (absolute-degenerate quotient of relative persistence)

**中文。** 定义：相对持久指相对同调的持久结构。猜想：偶对过滤的显式商在子复形常值时退化为绝对持久；且退化的核为含入消没。（相对持久含入消没绝对退化商）
