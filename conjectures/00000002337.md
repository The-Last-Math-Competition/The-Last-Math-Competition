# 00000002337

**English.** Definition: The Krawczyk interval operator is the Newton-type verification of interval arithmetic. Conjecture: The success probability of tracking is at least exp(−L·γ) (L the condition length, γ the γ-number); the constant of the exponential bound is exact (no better bound).

**中文。** 定义：Krawczyk 区间算子指区间算术的 Newton 型验证。猜想：追踪的成功概率 ≥ exp(-L·γ)(L 为条件长,γ 为 γ-数);指数界的常数精确(无更优界)。
