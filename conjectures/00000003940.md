# 00000003940

**English.** Definition: A finite type invariant is a numerical invariant obtained from a truncation of the DGA. Conjecture: There exist two Legendrians whose DGAs are isomorphic at chain level while all their finite type invariants differ, with crossing number at most 12. (minimal DGA-finite type separation example)

**中文。** 定义：有限型不变量指 DGA 截断给出的数值不变量。猜想：存在两个 Legendrian 其 DGA 链水平同构而一切有限型不变量相异,最小交叉数不超过 12。（DGA-有限型分离最小例）
