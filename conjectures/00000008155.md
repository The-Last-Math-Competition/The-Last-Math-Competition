# 00000008155

**English.** Definition: The renormalization of OPE on the Borel plane: the manifold structure ope_flow of the RG equations for the coefficients C_{ijk} of the operator product expansion. Conjecture: The complete classification of the integral curves of ope_flow is by algebraic curves (OPE algebraicity); the genus of the algebraic curves is at most 1 (an elliptic-rational dichotomy); genus 1 is realized by the operator family of Ising plus magnetic field (an Ising ellipticity law); and the count of rational points of the curves is the density of rational values of the OPE coefficients (a rational-coefficient density duality). (OPE flow elliptic-rational coefficient duality)

**中文。** 定义：波莱尔平面上的 OPE 的重整化：算子乘积展开的系数 C_{ijk} 的 RG 方程的流形结构 ope_flow。猜想：ope_flow 的积分曲线的完全分类为代数曲线（OPE 代数性）；且代数曲线的亏格 ≤ 1（椭圆-有理二分律）；亏格 1 的实现为 Ising + 磁场的算子族（Ising 椭圆律）；曲线的有理点的计数为 OPE 系数的有理值的密度（有理-系数密度对偶）。
