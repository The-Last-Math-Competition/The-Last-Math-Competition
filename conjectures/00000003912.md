**English.** Definition: The spectral invariant c(a, H) is the infimum of action-functional values produced from a homology class a by the minimax principle. Conjecture: The optimal Lipschitz constant of c with respect to the oscillation seminorm is exactly 1, and equality is attained on the two-dimensional torus by a family of sine potentials. (optimal spectral Lipschitz constant)

**中文。** 定义：谱不变量 c(a,H) 指同调类 a 经极值原理给出的作用泛函下确界。猜想：c 关于振荡半范数的最优 Lipschitz 常数恰为 1,等号可在二维环面上以正弦位势族达到。（谱 Lipschitz 最优常数）
