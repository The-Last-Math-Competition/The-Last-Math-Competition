# 00000009772

**OIE-12 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Schatten criteria for convolution operators: spectral classes of translation-invariant integral operators on compact Lie groups. Conjecture: A convolution operator on a compact Lie group belongs to Schatten p if and only if the weighted l^p sums of the Fourier coefficients of the kernel converge; the critical exponent is explicitly given by the dimension of the group and the height difference of the root system. (convolution Schatten criterion)

**中文。** 定义：卷积算子的 Schatten 准则：紧李群上平移不变积分算子的谱类。猜想：紧李群上卷积算子属于 Schatten p 当且仅当卷积核的 Fourier 系数的 ℓ p 加权可和；临界指标由群的维数与根系的高度差显式给出。（卷积 Schatten 准则）
