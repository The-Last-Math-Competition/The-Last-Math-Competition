# 00000006522

**English.** Definition: The heat-kernel expansion is the tower of small-time asymptotic coefficients. Conjecture: The cyclic structure of the coefficients is sifted out by supertraces and the sifted coefficients are explicit, and the closure of the tower is the dimension truncation. (supertrace tower of heat-kernel coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：热核展开为小时间渐近系数的塔。猜想：系数的循环结构由超迹筛出且筛后系数显式；且塔的封闭为维数截断。（热核系数超迹塔）
