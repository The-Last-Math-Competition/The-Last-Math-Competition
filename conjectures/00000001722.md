# 00000001722

**English.** Definition: The number of torsion points #(C ∩ Jac_tors) of a genus g ≥ 2 curve C inside its Jacobian. Conjecture: The count is at most 8·g·log g; the coefficient 8 comes from an explicit form of Raynaud's theorem, and the log factor is tight on hyperelliptic curves.
