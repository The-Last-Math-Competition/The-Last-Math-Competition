**English.** Definition: Gowers' block structure is the organization of unconditional basic sequences in space families. Conjecture: Every infinite-dimensional Banach space contains either an ℓ_{p_n}-type block basis or a hereditarily indecomposable block; and the block dichotomy is a complete classification with no third class. (Gowers block dichotomy complete)
