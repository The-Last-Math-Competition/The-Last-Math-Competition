# 00000005519

**English.** Definition: The mixing rate is the speed of relaxation to steady state. Conjecture: The conversion constant is an explicit quadratic in the reciprocal of the rate range, given by the internal drifts of the fast and slow subgroups, and the bound is tight. (range-quadratic mixing conversion constant of heterogeneity)
