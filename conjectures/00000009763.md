**OIE-03 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Symmetric operator ideals are characterized by Lorentz sequences: ideals correspond one-to-one with singular value sequence spaces. Conjecture: The lattice of unitarily invariant ideals is contravariantly isomorphic to the lattice of weighted Lorentz sequence spaces; maximal proper ideals are given by the critical exponent families of Marcinkiewicz norms, and the maximal ideal is unique up to isomorphism. (ideal-Lorentz duality)

**中文。** 定义：对称算子理想由 Lorentz 序列特征化：理想与算子的奇异值序列空间一一对应。猜想：酉不变理想格与带权 Lorentz 序列格反变同构；极大真理想由 Marcinkiewicz 范数的临界指数族给出且极大理想在同构意义下唯一。（理想-Lorentz 对偶）
