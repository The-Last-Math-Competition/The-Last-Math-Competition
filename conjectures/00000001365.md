# 00000001365

**English.** Definition: The Λ structure (Thomas hierarchy) of the quotient space of countable Borel equivalence relations. Conjecture: The classification of quotients of Z² actions is completely explicit via SL₂(Z) orbits when the generating vectors have length ≤ 2 (a small-generation classification).

**中文。** 定义：countable Borel 等价关系的商空间的 Λ 结构(Thomas 分层)。猜想：ℤ² 作用的商的分类在生成向量长度 ≤ 2 时完全由 SL₂(ℤ) 的轨道显式(小生成分类)。
