# 00000005310

**English.** Definition: Plethysm and tensor are two operations on symmetric functions. Conjecture: There exist two partitions with identical plethysm coefficients but different tensor spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of involution partitions. (same plethysm-different tensor spectrum involution separation)

**中文。** 定义：合成与张量指两种对称函数运算。猜想：存在两分拆的合成系数全部相同而张量谱不同,分离由对合分拆的显式对实现。（合成同-张量谱异对合分离）
