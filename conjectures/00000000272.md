# 00000000272

**English.** Definition: The parity of the class number of a quadratic field is determined by genus theory. Conjecture: In the even class-number case, the distribution of the 2-Sylow structure of the class group always follows a sign-corrected Cohen–Lenstra measure; and the correction weight is a power of two given by the order of the automorphism group. (2-Sylow CL two-power correction)

**中文。** 定义：二次域的类数奇偶由 genus 理论决定。猜想：偶类数情形的类群二 Sylow 结构分布恒服从符号修正的 Cohen–Lenstra 测度；且修正权重为自同构群阶的二幂。（2-Sylow CL 二幂修正）
