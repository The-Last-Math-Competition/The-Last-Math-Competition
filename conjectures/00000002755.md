# 00000002755

**English.** Definition: Capelli identities: vanishing of alternated polynomials. Conjecture: The minimal index of the Capelli sequence: an algebra satisfies the Capelli identity of index n if and only if its PI exponent is at most n, and the minimal index is the ceiling of the exponent plus one. (Capelli minimal index)

**中文。** 定义：Capelli 恒等式：交错多项式的消失。猜想：Capelli 序列的最小指标：代数满足指标 n 的 Capelli 恒等式当且仅当其 PI 指数不超过 n 且最小指标为指数的上整数加一。（Capelli 最小指标）
