# 00000004500

**English.** Definition: The syntactic category and the semantic category are the two categorical images of a theory. Conjecture: There exist two type theories whose semantic categories are equivalent while their syntactic categories are not isomorphic, and the separation is realized explicitly by two theories whose universe counts differ by one. (semantic-syntactic separation by one universe)

**中文。** 定义：句法范畴与语义范畴指理论的两个范畴像。猜想：存在两个类型论的语义范畴等价而句法范畴不同构,分离由宇宙数差一的两个理论显式实现。（语义-句法分离宇宙差一）
