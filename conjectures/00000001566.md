# 00000001566

**GM-26 △** — source: 37_组合几何.md

**English.** Definition: A convex monohedral tile is a convex polyhedron that tiles R³. Conjecture: The total number of its isomorphism classes is finite and at most 400; the currently known monohedral tiles (prisms, Goldberg types, etc.) number over 300, with the remainder guaranteed by termination of a computer search.

**中文。** 定义：凸单铺砌单体指能铺满 ℝ³ 的凸多面体。猜想：其同构类总数有限且 ≤ 400;目前已知的铺砌单体(棱柱、Goldberg 型等)计数为 300 余,余量由计算机搜索的可终止性保证。
