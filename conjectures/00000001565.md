# 00000001565

**English.** Definition: A random 0/1-polytope is the convex hull of random vertices of the cube. Conjecture: The expected volume ratio to the unit cube always concentrates around √(log d)/d; and the width of concentration is controlled by the Poincaré constant. (random 01 volume ratio logarithmic concentration)
