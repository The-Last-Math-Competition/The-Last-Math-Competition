**BR4-15 ★** — source: 185_代数几何VII_双有理几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Superrigidity is the extreme property that all immersed curves are rigid. Conjecture: The closure of the family tree is attained at the classical exceptional types (quadrics and four-dimensional hypersurfaces), the combination of the exceptions being quadrics; superrigidity holds if and only if the curve-weighted H^1 vanishes; and the vanishing deficit of exceptional types is the contribution of one conic. (quadric exceptions of superrigidity)

**中文。** 定义：超刚性：任意浸入曲线均刚的极端性质。猜想：族谱的封闭性在经典例外型（二次超曲面与四维超曲面）达到且例外的组合为二次超曲面；超刚性当且仅当 H¹ 的曲线加权消没；例外型的消没差为一条二次曲线的贡献。（超刚性二次超曲面例外）
