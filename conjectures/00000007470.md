**English.** Definition: The spectra of Toeplitz operators with the symbol side. Conjecture: The Toeplitz spectrum is the symbol image plus the connectivity complement, and the criterion of the complement is the winding number. (winding-criterion Toeplitz spectral theorem)

**中文。** 定义：Toeplitz 算子的谱：符号侧。猜想：符号谱定理：Toeplitz 的谱为符号像加连通补且补的判据为绕数。（Toeplitz 符号像谱定理绕数连通补判据符号侧）
