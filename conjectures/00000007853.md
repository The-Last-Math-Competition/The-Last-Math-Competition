# 00000007853

**RAM-13 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The randomized competitive ratio of online algorithms under additive error (such as list update): distributional bounds on R_n. Conjecture: For metrical task systems on the uniform metric (n states) the optimal randomized competitive ratio is exactly 2H_n - 1 with H the harmonic number; the separation over any deterministic algorithm is H_n/2; and the correction constant gamma in H_n - gamma is uniquely determined by the dual integrality constant (equal to 1) of the covering linear program. (metrical task system exact ratio)

**中文。** 定义：加性误差下随机排序（如 list update 的 competitive ratio）的分布尾：在线算法的竞争比 R_n 的随机化界。猜想：对 metrical task systems 在均匀度量（n 个状态）上，最优随机竞争比的精确值为 2H_n-1（H 为调和数）；且此值不被任何确定性算法超越的分离量为 H_n/2；修正项 H_n-γ 的 γ 常数由覆盖 LP 的对偶积分常数（=1）唯一确定。
