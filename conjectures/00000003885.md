# 00000003885

**English.** Definition: The Cartier operator C is the degree-(p−1) component of the inverse Frobenius on graded rings. Conjecture: The dimension sequence of images of iterated C satisfies a linear recursion, and all prime factors of the minimal period divide the denominator of the corresponding F-pure threshold. (Cartier period-fpt divisibility)

**中文。** 定义：Cartier 算子 C 指分次环上逆 Frobenius 的次数降 p−1 分量。猜想：C 的高次复合的像维数序列满足线性递推,其极小周期的素因子均为对应 F-纯阈值的分母的因子。（Cartier 周期-fpt 整除）
