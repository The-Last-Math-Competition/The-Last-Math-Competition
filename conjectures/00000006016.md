**English.** Definition: Aubry-Mather sets are the Cantor families of invariant sets. Conjecture: The stratification of rotation numbers is indexed by monotone intervals, the structure is complete, and the combination of the completeness is the gap function. (rotation stratification of Aubry-Mather gap functions)

**中文。** 定义：Aubry–Mather 集指不变集的康托族。猜想：旋转数的分层由单调区间索引且结构完全；且完全的组合为间隙函数。（Aubry-Mather 间隙函数旋转分层）
