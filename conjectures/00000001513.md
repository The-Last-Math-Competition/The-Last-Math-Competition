# 00000001513

**KK-03 ◆** — source: 36_代数K理论与非交换几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Borel regulator is the volume map from number fields to the reals. Conjecture: The mod-two congruence chain of reg_{2k−1} is always an explicit combination of Bernoulli numbers and the square root of the discriminant; and the chain provides a mod-2 integrality test of Beilinson's conjecture. (Borel congruence Bernoulli combination)

**中文。** 定义：Borel regulator 指数域到实数的体积映射。猜想：reg_{2k−1} 模二的同余链恒为 Bernoulli 数与判别式平方根的显式组合；且链给出 Beilinson 猜想的二积分性检验。（Borel 同余 Bernoulli 组合）
