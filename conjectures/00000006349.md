**English.** Definition: The universal solution is the universal-solution structure of Yang-Baxter type equations. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the first-order spectrum of component solutions and the rational shift of the fundamental representation is explicit; the shift is the weight difference; and the degeneration points of the spectrum are resonances, the index being the multiplicity of the weights. (component rational-spectral-shift conversion constant)
