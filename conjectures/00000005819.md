# 00000005819

**English.** Definition: The extinction asymptotics is the shape approaching extinction. Conjecture: The two-dimensional conversion constant is the explicit exponent of the first eigenvalue, attained at separated-variable solutions, and the formula is exact. (eigenvalue exponent conversion of two-dimensional extinction)

**中文。** 定义：灭绝渐近型指逼近灭绝的形。猜想：二维换算常数为第一特征值的显式指数,分离变量解处达到且公式精确。（二维灭绝特征指数换算式）
