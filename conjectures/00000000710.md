# 00000000710

**English.** Definition: The series of the p-adic Möbius function. Conjecture: The partial sums Σ_{n ≤ x} μ_p(n) (in the sense of p^k ‖ n) are O(x^{1/2+ε}) (a p-adic Mertens conjecture).

**中文。** 定义：p 进 Mobius 函数的级数。猜想：Σ_{n ≤ x} μ_p(n)(p 进莫比乌)在 p^k || n 意义下的部分和为 O(x^{1/2+ε})(p 进 Mertens)。
