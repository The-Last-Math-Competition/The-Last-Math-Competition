# 00000002626

**English.** Definition: Weak Jacobi forms: allowing exponentially growing z-direction Fourier expansions. Conjecture: The minimal-weight generation theorem: the weak Jacobi ring of fixed index m is generated in weights 1 and 2, the minimal number of generators being exactly the rank of the factor lattice of m; the Hilbert series of the ring is rational with unit-root denominators. (weak Jacobi generated ring)

**中文。** 定义：弱 Jacobi 形式：允许指数增长的 z 方向 Fourier 展开。猜想：最小权生成定理：固定指标 m 的弱 Jacobi 环由权 1 与权 2 生成且生成元的极小个数恰为 m 的因子格的秩；环的 Hilbert 级数为有理函数且分母根为单位根。（弱 Jacobi 生成环）
