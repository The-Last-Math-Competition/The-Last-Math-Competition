**AN4-24 ★** — source: 129_数论IV_代数数论显式方法.md

**English.** Definition: The Coleman decomposition is the cyclotomic decomposition of a power series at roots of unity. Conjecture: The correction coefficient table is completely explicit for p at most 23, and the coefficients are Bernoulli-type rational numbers built from values of cyclotomic polynomials. (explicit table of Coleman correction coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：Coleman 分解指幂级数在单位根处的分圆分解。猜想：修正系数表在 p 不超过 23 完全显式,系数为分圆多项式值的 Bernoulli 型有理数。（Coleman 修正系数显式表）
