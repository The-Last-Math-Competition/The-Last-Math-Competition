**English.** Definition: Graph spectra and Brownian motion: continuous limits. Conjecture: The graph limit of Brownian motion: the functional limit of random walks on graphs is Brownian, with the covariance of limits spectral measures. (graph walk Brownian spectral measure covariance limit)
