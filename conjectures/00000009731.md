# 00000009731

**MAD-01 ★** — source: 310_数学的自动发现.md

**English.** Definition: The success-rate threshold of automated theorem proving: the complete-proof generation rate of formalization assistants on standard mathematical corpora. Conjecture: At training scales containing millions of formalized theorems, automated systems reach complete-proof success rates above ninety percent on new theorems; the remaining failures concentrate on proofs requiring new definitions, with their proportion decaying as the inverse square of corpus growth. (ATP success-rate threshold)

**中文。** 定义：自动定理证明的正确率门槛：形式化助手在标准数学语料上的完整证明生成率。猜想：在包含百万条已形式化定理的训练规模下，自动系统在新定理上的完整证明成功率达到百分之九十以上；剩余失败例集中于需要新定义构造的证明，其比例随语料增长按平方倒数衰减。（ATP 成功率门槛）
