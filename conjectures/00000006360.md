**QG2-20 △** — source: 197_量子群II.md

**English.** Definition: The tensor category and quantum dimension are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two Hopf algebras with equivalent categories but different dimension functions, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same category but different pivotal structures. (same category-different pivotal dimension separation)
