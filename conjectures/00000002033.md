# 00000002033

**English.** Conjecture: The distribution of quadratic character sums (on the √q scale) converges to an explicit random model (controlled by a central-limit type limit of random completely multiplicative functions); the model variance is (1/2π²)·(loglog q).

**中文。** 猜想：二次特征和的分布(在 √q 尺度上)收敛到显式随机模型(由随机完全积性函数的中心极限型极限控制);模型的方差为 (1/2π²)·(loglog q)。
