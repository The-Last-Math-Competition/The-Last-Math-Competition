# 00000005501

**QN2-01 ◆** — source: 169_排队网络的图极限.md

**English.** Definition: An open network is a Markovian network with exponential service. Conjecture: Near the stability boundary the queue length scales as the negative one-half power of the load deficit, with exponent given by the branching structure of the bottleneck and consistent with heavy-traffic diffusion; and the pre-exponential constant is exactly the load redistribution coefficient of the bottleneck branch. (half-power critical exponent of bottlenecks)

**中文。** 定义：开网络指指数服务的马尔可夫网络。猜想：稳定边界附近的队长按负载缺口的负二分之一次幂标度,指数由瓶颈的分支结构给出且与重话务扩散一致。(瓶颈临界指数半幂标度)；且指数前常数恰为瓶颈分支的负载重分配系数。
