# 00000008056

**PRF-06 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The realization degree of Hilbert's program in the epsilon-calculus: the combinatorial cost c_epsilon (the inflation rate of the epsilon-translation) of first-order equivalence of the epsilon-calculus (Hilbert's choice-operator calculus). Conjecture: c_epsilon is polynomial (linear-logarithmic) with tight magnitude |D| log|D|; the size of the constructive content of the second epsilon-theorem is an explicit multiple of Herbrand complexity; and the lower bound on the size of solutions of the minimal counterexamples to epsilon-term elimination (non-eliminable epsilon-critical formulas) and of the modern reconstruction of the Bernays problem from Ackermann's remarks is n^{log n} (a strict separation of subpolynomial from polynomial). (epsilon-calculus Herbrand elimination cost)

**中文。** 定义：ε-演算的希尔伯特纲领实现度：ε-演算（Hilbert 的选择算子演算）的一阶等价性的组合代价 c_ε（ε-翻译的膨胀率）。猜想：c_ε 为多项式（线性对数级）界的紧量级 |D|·log|D|；且 ε-演算的第二 ε-定理的构造性内容的规模为 Herbrand 复杂度的显式倍数；ε-项的消除的极小反例（不可消除的 ε-临界公式）与 Ackermann 注记的致谢问题（Bernays 问题的现代重构）的解的规模下界为 n^{log n}（次多项式-多项式的严格分离）。
