**GA-17 ★** — source: 57_代数图论II.md

**English.** Definition: The C₅ density among C₄-free graphs is the maximal relative density of C₅'s therein. Conjecture: The density is 1/5; the extremal construction is a balanced variant of polarity graphs; and the proof follows from the positivity of the SDP matrix in flag algebras.

**中文。** 定义：C₄-free 图的 C₅ 密度指其中 C₅ 的最大相对密度。猜想：该密度为 1/5;极值构造为 polarity 图的平衡变体;证明由 flag algebra 的 SDP 矩阵的正性给出。
