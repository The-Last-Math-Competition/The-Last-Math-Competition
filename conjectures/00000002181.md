# 00000002181

**English.** Definition: The Gotsman–Linial conjecture relates the sensitivity of Boolean functions to their spectral norm. Conjecture: Sensitivity is always at most √2·log n times the spectral norm; and the bound is controlled by the pointer maxima of real hypercubical faces. (sensitivity spectral-norm sqrt-two law)

**中文。** 定义：Gotsman–Linial 猜想指布尔函数的敏感度与谱范数的关系。猜想：敏感度恒不超过谱范数的根号二乘 logn 因子；且界由实超立方面的指针极大值控制。（敏感度谱范数根号二律）
