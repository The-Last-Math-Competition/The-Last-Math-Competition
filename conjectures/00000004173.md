# 00000004173

**English.** Definition: The eta-function pair is the function-pair system controlling the minor arcs in the circle method. Conjecture: The first layer of its energy stratification is constructed explicitly from Voronoi-type function pairs, and the transition between layers requires exactly k additional variables. (first-layer construction of eta-function pairs)
