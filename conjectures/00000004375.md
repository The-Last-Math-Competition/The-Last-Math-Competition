**KK2-15 ◆** — source: 131_代数K理论II_迹方法.md

**English.** Definition: Etale K-theory is the etale localization of the K-spectrum. Conjecture: The comparison remainder with l-adic cohomology is an explicit twist with coefficients in the l-adic unit group, and the remainder vanishes exactly when all l-th roots of unity are present. (twist formula of the etale comparison remainder)

**中文。** 定义：étale K 理论指 K 谱的 étale 局部化。猜想：与 ℓ 进上同调的比较余项为 ℓ 进单位群系数的显式扭转,余项在含全部 ℓ 次单位根时精确消失。（étale 比较余项扭转公式）
