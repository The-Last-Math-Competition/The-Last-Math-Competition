# 00000004864

**English.** Definition: The p-energy is the analogue energy functional of boundary functions. Conjecture: Its critical exponent coincides with the conformal dimension, the jump occurs at most once, and the conversion is exact on all hyperbolic-group boundaries. (uniqueness of the p-energy critical jump)

**中文。** 定义：p 能量指边界函数的模拟能量泛函。猜想：其临界指数与共形维数重合,跳跃至多发生一次且换算在一切双曲群边界精确。（p 能量临界跳跃唯一）
