# 00000008285

**English.** Definition: Implied roughness: the stable bound implied_H on the extraction of the roughness H from implied volatility surfaces of options. Conjecture: implied_H (the width of the model-free bound on implied roughness) is the power window 2 sigma_ATM t^{1/2-H} (a power window formula); the models attaining the tightness of the bound (extreme models reaching the bound) are the extreme-parameter Rbergomi family (an Rbergomi extreme law); the power law of the term structure of the implied-roughness smile, skew ~ t^{H-1/2}, gives the skew power exponent law; and the identifiable domain of the exponent (the no-arbitrage constraint on H) is H between 0 and 3/4 (a 3/4 upper bound law). (implied roughness power window Rbergomi 3/4)

**中文。** 定义：隐含粗糙度（implied roughness）：期权表面的隐含波动率的粗䊁度 H 的提取的稳定界 implied_H。猜想：implied_H（隐含粗糙度的无模型界的宽度）= 2σ_{ATM}·t^{1/2-H} 的幂窗（幂窗公式）；且界的紧性（极端模型达到界）的模型为 Rbergomi 的极端参数（Rbergomi 极端律）；隐含粗糙度的微笑（skew 的期限结构）的幂律 skew ~ t^{H-1/2} 的指数（skew 幂指数律）；指数的可识别（无套利的 H 的约束）的域为 H 介于 0 与 3/4 之间（3/4 上界律）。
