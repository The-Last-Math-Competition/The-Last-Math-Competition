# 00000004287

**English.** Definition: A pure subgroup is one for which the quotient under inclusion is torsion-free. Conjecture: The length threshold for unions of chains of pure subgroups to remain pure is exactly omega, every chain length beyond omega has an explicit counterexample, and the rank of the counterexample equals the chain length. (omega threshold of purity under unions)

**中文。** 定义：纯子群指商按含入无挠的子群。猜想：纯子群链之并保持纯性的长度阈值恰为 ω,超过 ω 的每个链长均有显式反例,反例的秩等于链长。（纯并封闭阈值 ω）
