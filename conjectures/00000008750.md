# 00000008750

**English.** Definition: The additivity theorem for nonconnective Waldhausen categories. Conjecture: The K-groups of glueing squares always satisfy a Mayer-Vietoris long exact sequence; and the boundary maps of the sequence are given by the naturality of simplicial approximation. (glueing Mayer-Vietoris boundary naturality)

**中文。** 定义：非连通 Waldhausen 范畴的加性定理。猜想：粘连方块的 K 群恒满足 Mayer–Vietoris 长正合列；且列的边缘映射由单纯逼近的自然性给出。（粘连 Mayer-Vietoris 边缘自然）
