**English.** Definition: Log-concave concentration is the dimension-dependent upper bound for log-concave measures. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit dimension-dependent upper bound consistent with the slice constant; the example of consistency is the simplex-vertex measure after isotropic normalization; and the degeneration of the bound to dimension-freeness holds if and only if the measure is the image of an exponential map. (slice-consistent conversion of dimension-dependent log-concavity)

**中文。** 定义：凹集中：对数凹测度的维数依赖上界。猜想：换算常数为维数依赖的显式上界且与切片常数一致；一致的下例为 isotropic 归一后的单纯形顶点测度；界的退化为维数无关当且仅当测度为指数映射的像。（凹维数依赖切片一致换算）
