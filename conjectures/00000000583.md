# 00000000583

**English.** Definition: The Orlik–Solomon algebra is the cohomology model of a matroid. Conjecture: The minimal number of generators of the characteristic ideal is bounded by a dimensional constant times n; and the constant of the uniform-generation bound is an explicit function of the rank. (OS algebra generator linear bound)

**中文。** 定义：Orlik–Solomon 代数为拟阵的上同调模型。猜想：特征理想的极小生成元数不超过维数常数乘 n；且均匀生成性界的常数为秩的显式函数。（OS 代数生成元线性界）
