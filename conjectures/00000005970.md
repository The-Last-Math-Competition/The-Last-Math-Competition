# 00000005970

**English.** Definition: Spike positions and eigenvector spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two spiked models with the same spike positions but different eigenvector spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same positions but different multiplicities. (same spike position-different multiplicity separation)

**中文。** 定义：尖峰位置与特征向量谱指两层。猜想：存在两尖峰模型的尖峰位置相同而特征向量谱不同,分离由同位置异多重态的显式对实现。（尖峰位置同-向量谱异多重分离）
