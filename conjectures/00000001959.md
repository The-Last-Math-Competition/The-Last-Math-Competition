**English.** Definition: The clique complex of an S-arithmetic lattice is the clique structure in the affine building. Conjecture: The clique-count generating function of a Q-rank-one group is always a rational function; and its pole spectrum coincides with the Poincare polynomial of the Weyl group. (clique generating function rational poles)
