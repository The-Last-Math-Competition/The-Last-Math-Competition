**English.** Definition: The separation singularity is the blow-up of boundary-layer solutions at separation points. Conjecture: The singularity exponent is one half with an explicit logarithmic correction as next term, given by asymptotic matching of critical profile families, and the correction is exact. (half-exponent logarithmic correction of separation singularities)
