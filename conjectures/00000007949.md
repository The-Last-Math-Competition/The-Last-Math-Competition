# 00000007949

**English.** Definition: The connection between q-difference Painleve equations and cluster algebras: the analogy of q-PVI with QRT-type maps on rational surfaces, and the seeds of the CLY presentation of q-Painleve. Conjecture: q-PVI is compatible with the tau-deformation of the cluster algebra of type A_3^(1) (a complete CLY structure); and the solution space of every q-Painleve equation is univalued and analytic inside each chamber of the chamber structure of the corresponding cluster algebra; solutions on chamber walls are algebraic (rational); and the count of nodes (seeds) of the skeleton of the cluster exchange graph under the affine Weyl group of type n is finite if and only if the Painleve equation is of additive type (an integrability regression law). (q-Painleve CLY chamber analyticity)

**中文。** 定义：q-差异 Painlevé 方程与簇代数的连接：q-PVI 的方程有理曲面上的 QRT 型映射的类比，q-Painlevé 的 CLY 表象的种子。猜想：q-PVI 相容于 A₃^(1) 型簇代数的 τ-形变（完全 CLY 结构）；且一切 q-Painlevé 方程的解空间为对应簇代数的 chamber 结构的每个 chamber 内的单值解析；chamber 墙上的解为代数（有理）；簇交换图的骨架的节点（seeds）的计数在 n 型仿射 Weyl 群下为有限当且仅当 Painlevé 方程为加法型（可积回归律）。
