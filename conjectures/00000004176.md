# 00000004176

**English.** Definition: The smooth Weyl sum is the polynomial exponential sum restricted to smooth numbers. Conjecture: The p-adic filtration constant of its mean-square main term is given explicitly by an Euler product, and the constant is an algebraic number of degree at most k. (p-adic filtration constant of smooth Weyl sums)

**中文。** 定义：光滑 Weyl 和指限制在光滑数上的多项式指数和。猜想：其均方主项的 p 进滤波常数由 Euler 乘积显式给出,常数为代数数且次数不超过 k。（光滑 Weyl p 进滤波常数）
