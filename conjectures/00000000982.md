# 00000000982

**English.** Definition: The Berezin transform. Conjecture: Fixed points of the Berezin transform on Fock space are radial functions, and the cardinality of the fixed-point set is at most 2 (fixed-point rigidity).

**中文。** 定义：Berezin 变换。猜想：Fock 空间上 Berezin 变换的不动点为径向函数且其不动点集的基数 ≤ 2(不动点刚性)。
