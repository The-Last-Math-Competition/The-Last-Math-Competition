**English.** Definition: The solution cohomology and the characteristic variety are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two modules whose solution cohomologies all agree but whose characteristic varieties are not isomorphic, and the separation is realized by an explicit phantom-type construction. (same solution cohomology-different characteristic variety phantom separation)
