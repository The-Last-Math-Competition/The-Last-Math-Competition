# 00000007247

**English.** Definition: Ekeland duality with the dual action. Conjecture: The equivalence of the dual problem holds, and the bridge of the equivalence is the Legendre transform. (Legendre-bridged Ekeland dual minimization)

**中文。** 定义：Ekeland 的对偶：对偶作用。猜想：对偶作用的极小：对偶问题的等价且价的桥为 Legendre 变换。（Ekeland 对偶 Legendre 极小等价桥变换）
