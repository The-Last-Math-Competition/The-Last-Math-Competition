**AE4-03 ★** — source: 226_算术遍历II.md

**English.** Definition: Lattice points on homogeneous spaces with counting theorems. Conjecture: The asymptotic of lattice points in balls has the spectral gap as error; and the oscillation term of the error is given termwise by the phase spectrum of the purely cuspidal eigenvalues. (spectral-gap lattice-point ball asymptotics)

**中文。** 定义：齐性空间上的格点：计数定理。猜想：格点计数：球内的格点的渐近且近的误差为谱的间隙。(格点计数谱间隙球内渐近误差)；且误差的振荡项由纯尖点特征值的相位谱逐项给出。
