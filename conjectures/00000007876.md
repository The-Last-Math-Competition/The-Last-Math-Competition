# 00000007876

**English.** Definition: Shape invariants of infinity-topoi: the shape shp(X) of Shv(X) (a pro-homotopy type) and the layer-separation degree d(X) = dim(shp) - dim(etale shp) of cohesion. Conjecture: For every cohesive infinity-topos, d(X) <= 1; and d(X) = 0 if and only if the base space is a Stone space; the recursive ascent of the separation degree under transfinite iteration of shape stabilizes at the first indefinite ordinal omega+1, the stabilization locus corresponding to the complete subtopos of cohesion. (cohesive shape separation stabilization)

**中文。** 定义：∞-topos 的形状不变量：Shv(X) 的形状 shp(X)（pro-同伦型）与凝聚性（cohesion）的层化分离度 d(X)=dim(shp) - dim(etale shp)。猜想：对一切凝聚 ∞-topos，d(X) ≤ 1；且 d(X)=0 当且仅当底空间为 Stone 空间；分离度的递归提升在谱的超限叠代（shp∘shp）上稳定于第一未定序数 ω+1，该稳定位对应凝聚的完备子拓扑。
