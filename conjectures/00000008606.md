# 00000008606

**MLC-16 ★** — source: 272_非参数统计的极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: The minimax recovery of Gaussian graphical model structure. Conjecture: The minimal sample size under maximal degree k is k log d; and the correction from positive-definiteness constraints is linear in the dimension. (graphical model sample size bound)

**中文。** 定义：高斯图模型结构恢复的 minimax。猜想：最大度 k 下最小样本量为 k log d；正定约束的修正为维数线性。（图模型样本量界）
