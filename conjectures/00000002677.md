# 00000002677

**English.** Definition: The Cheeger constant and spectrum: bounds on the spectral gap. Conjecture: Equivalence of the constant and the first eigenvalue, with diameter the precision. (Cheeger spectral gap diameter)

**中文。** 定义：Cheeger 常数与谱：谱隙的界。猜想：谱隙的界：常数与第一特征值的等价且价的精度为直径。（Cheeger 常数谱隙直径等价精度第一特征值界）
