# 00000002714

**English.** Definition: Buchsbaum complexes: the uniform version whose links are homology balls. Conjecture: The correction formula for Buchsbaum h*-vectors: the correction is a linear combination of Betti numbers with coefficients the Euler-characteristic corrections of links; the formula degenerates to the Novik–Swartz identity on manifold complexes. (Buchsbaum h correction formula)

**中文。** 定义：Buchsbaum 复形：链接为同调球的均匀版本。猜想：Buchbaum h*-向量的修正公式：修正量为 Betti 数的线性组合且组合系数为链接的 Euler 特征修正；公式在流形复形退化为 Novik–Swartz 恒等式。（Buchsbaum h 修正公式）
