**English.** Definition: The monotone deficit is the deficit of the entropy-type functional, considered for genus-two surfaces. Conjecture: The genus-two conversion constant is the explicit supremum of the energy density in the handle region, zero at the degenerate bridge; the supremum is attained at the symmetric points of the handle, two of them; and the lower bound of the deficit is the logarithm of the handle length, with the constant of the mean curvature flow. (handle-region density supremum at genus two)

**中文。** 定义：单调亏损：单调量（熵型泛函）的亏损；亏格二曲面的情形。猜想：亏格二的换算常数为柄区能量密度的显式上确界且退化桥处为零；上确界在柄的对称点达到且点数为 2；亏损的下界为柄长的对数且系数为平均曲率流的常数。（亏格二柄区密度上确界）
