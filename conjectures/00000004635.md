# 00000004635

**English.** Definition: An anyon is a simple object of a modular tensor category, and the quantum dimension is its Frobenius-Perron dimension. Conjecture: The prime factors of the sum of all squared quantum dimensions always divide the prime factors of the norm of the Gauss sum, with the constraint at equality for the Ising type. (norm constraint on anyon prime factors)

**中文。** 定义：任意子指模张量范畴的简单对象,量子维数指其 Frobenius–Perron 维。猜想：全体量子维数平方和的素因子恒整除 Gauss 和范数的素因子,约束在 Ising 型达等号。（任意子素因子范数约束）
