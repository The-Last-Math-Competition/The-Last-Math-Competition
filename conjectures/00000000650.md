# 00000000650

**KT-50 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The bridge number br(K) and the Seifert genus g₃(K) of a knot. Conjecture: The optimal form of br(K) ≥ 2 + ⌈g₃(K)/(br(K)−1)⌉ is br(K) ≥ 2 + ⌊g₃(K)/⌊br(K)−1⌋⌋, with equality attained pointwise on the family of (2,n)-torus knots; the inequality cannot be strengthened further.

**中文。** 定义：纽结的桥数 br(K) 与 Seifert 亏格 g₃(K)。猜想：对一切纽结,br(K) ≥ 2 + ⌈g₃(K)/(br(K)−1)⌉ 的最优形式为 br(K) ≥ 2 + ⌊g₃(K)/⌊(br(K)−1)⌋⌋,且等号在 (2,n)-torus 结族上逐点取得;该不等式不可再加强。
