# 00000002417

**GC2-07 ★** — source: 66_Galois覆盖与基本群.md

**English.** Definition: The Brauer group is the torsion group of central simple algebras. Conjecture: The Brauer group of a compact locally symmetric space is finite with order a function of the Betti numbers; and the verification of finiteness is the vanishing of Tate classes. (locally symmetric space Brauer finite)

**中文。** 定义：Brauer 群为中央单代数的扭转子。猜想：紧局部对称空间的 Brauer 群有限且阶为 Betti 数函数；且有限的验证为 Tate 类消失。（局部对称空间 Brauer 有限）
