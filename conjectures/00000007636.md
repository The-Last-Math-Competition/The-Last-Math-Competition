**English.** Definition: Rankine-Hugoniot conditions connect the states on both sides of a shock. Conjecture: The shock speed is the flux-jump ratio with uniqueness given by the characteristic direction; and the exception of tangential discontinuity is the linearly degenerate field. (RH shock-speed jump ratio)

**中文。** 定义：Rankine–Hugoniot 条件连接激波两侧的状态。猜想：激波速度为通量跳跃比且比的唯一性由特征方向给出；且切向间断的例外为线性退化场。（RH 激波速度跳跃比）
