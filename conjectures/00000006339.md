# 00000006339

**English.** Definition: Rigidity is the minimal number of changes altering the rank. Conjecture: The rigidity of explicit matrices exceeds the logarithmic square and is given by Fourier-type construction, the kernel of the construction being the RIP family. (Fourier RIP construction of explicit rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：刚性为改变秩的最少改动数。猜想：显式矩阵的刚性超对数平方且由傅里叶型构造；且构造的核为 RIP 族。（显式刚性傅里叶 RIP 构造）
