# 00000000660

**English.** Conjecture: The d-invariants of the Brieskorn spheres Σ(2,3,6n±1) form an arithmetic progression with common difference 2 and differ from the Rokhlin μ-invariant by multiples of 8 (a linearization of the μ-invariant).

**中文。** 猜想：Brieskorn 球 Σ(2,3,6n±1) 的 d-不变量构成公差为 2 的等差序列且与 Rokhlin μ 相差 8 的倍数(μ-不变量的线性化)。
