**English.** Definition: The square-sum identity of Jones β-numbers and Carleson measures (the Traveling Salesman Theorem). Conjecture: The asymmetric version of TST: the existence of a curve is characterized by the direction-divergence sum weighted by β-numbers, with critical exponent 1/2 for the divergence weights; at criticality the divergent sets are exactly tree-like fractals with multiplicative branching measure. (TST direction divergence critical)

**中文。** 定义：Jones β 数与 Carleson 测度的平方和恒等式（Traveling salesman 定理）。猜想：TST 的非对称版：曲线存在性由 β 数加权的方向分歧和刻画且分歧权的临界指数为 1/2；临界处的发散集恰为树状分形且分形的分岔测度为乘性。（TST 方向分歧临界）
