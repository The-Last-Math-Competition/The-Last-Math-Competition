# 00000002050

**English.** Definition: The triprime smooth numbers are {2^a·3^b·5^c ≤ x}. Conjecture: Their count has the second-order error term (log x)²·P(loglog x) (P an explicit periodic function whose period comes from the continued-fraction structure of log2/log3); the error is controlled by the Poissonization of three independent geometric distributions.

**中文。** 定义：三素平滑数指 {2^a3^b5^c≤ x}。猜想：其计数的二次误差项为 (log x)^2· P(loglog x)(P 为显式周期函数,周期由 log2/log3 的连分数结构);误差由三个独立几何分布的 Poisson 化控制。
