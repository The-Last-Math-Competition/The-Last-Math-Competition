**English.** Definition: Weight spectra and intersection-complex spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two perverse sheaves with the same weight spectrum but different intersection-complex spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit middle-extension perturbation with the same weight but different intersection. (same weight-different intersection extension separation)
