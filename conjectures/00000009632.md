# 00000009632

**English.** Definition: Multifractal analysis of the Birkhoff spectrum: the Hausdorff dimension spectrum of level sets where time averages converge to a given compatible vector. Conjecture: The dimension spectrum of all Birkhoff level sets of an SFT is the Legendre dual of the pressure rate and analytic; analyticity breaks only at phase transition points, the break points are finite in number, and agree with coset counts of the symmetry group of the potential. (exact multifractal formula)

**中文。** 定义：Birkhoff 谱的多重分形分析：水平集 {x: 时间平均收敛到给定相容向量} 的 Hausdorff 维数谱。猜想：SFT 的一切 Birkhoff 水平集的维数谱为压率的 Legendre 对偶且解析；解析性破裂仅在相变点发生且破裂点个数有限，与势的对称群陪集计数一致。（多重分形精确公式）
