**English.** Definition: Set-valued analysis: variational problems of multivalued maps. Conjecture: Kakutani fixed points: existence of fixed points of set-valued maps, with the conditions of existence convex values and closed graphs. (Kakutani convex value closed graph fixed point existence condition)
