# 00000008122

**NAG-12 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The rigidity of the Gerstenhaber structure of the Hochschild cohomology of noncommutative varieties: the rigidity index ger(A) of deformations of the multiplication on HH^*(A). Conjecture: The dimension of ger(A) equals the Hodge number h^{2,0} of the central variety (a central Hodge correspondence); the tangent dimension of the perfect obstruction theory of the subalgebra HH^2 (first-order deformations) equals Ext^1(O,O) of qgr (a tangent-Ext^1 correspondence); and the base of the universal family of deformations (Kuranishi space) is analytic of dimension at most dim HH^2 minus dim HH^3 (a Kuranishi bound), tightness realized by deformations of the 4-dimensional Sklyanin (Kuranishi tightness of noncommutative K3). (Gerstenhaber Hodge Kuranishi tightness)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的 Hochschild 上同调的 Gerstenhaber 结构的刚性：HH^*(A) 的乘法的形变的刚性指数 ger(A)。猜想：ger(A) 的维数 = 中心簇的 Hodge 数 h^{2,0}（中心 Hodge 对应）；且 HH² 的子代数（一阶形变）的完美阻碍理论的切维数 = qgr 的 Ext¹(𝒪,𝒪)（切-Ext¹ 对应）；形变的普适家族的基（Kuranishi 空间）为解析且维数 ≤ dim HH² - dim HH³（Kuranishi 界），界的紧性由 4 维 Sklyanin 的形变（非交换 K3 的 Kuranishi 紧性）实现。
