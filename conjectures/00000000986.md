# 00000000986

**English.** Definition: Mutually unbiased bases (MUB) are d+1 orthonormal bases with pairwise inner products of constant modulus 1/√d. Conjecture: No complete MUB exists in d = 6; equivalently, among the Weyl commutation equivalence classes of the 6-dimensional Fock space there is no 7-element mutually unbiased system; the nonexistence follows from algebraic-geometric constraints of trace forms over residue-class fields.

**中文。** 定义：互不偏基(MUB)指两两内积模长恒为 1/√d 的 d+1 个正交基。猜想：d=6 时不存在完备 MUB;等价地,6 维 Flock 空间的 Weyl 交换等价类中不存在 7 元互偏系。该不存在性由剩余类域上迹型的代数几何约束给出。
