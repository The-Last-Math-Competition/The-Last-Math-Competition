# 00000002329

**NG-09 ★** — source: 63_数值代数几何.md

**English.** Conjecture: The false-positive rate of redundant factors in numerical irreducible decomposition is d^{-2} (an inverse-square power of the degree); false positives are controlled by the Gaussian tail of random noise at threshold ε (P(false) = exp(−cε²)).

**中文。** 猜想：数值不可约分解的冗余因子的误报率为 d^{-2}(度数的负二次幂);误报由阈值 ε 的随机噪声 Gaussian 尾控制(P(误报)= exp(-cε²))。
