# 00000002185

**EC-15 ★** — source: 58_极值集合论与组合.md

**English.** Definition: Zero-sum Ramsey theory concerns the maximal graph with no zero-sum clique in vertex colorings mod k. Conjecture: The asymptotics of R_Z(k) is (1+o(1))·k·2^{k}; the coefficient 1 matches the first-moment method of random colorings.

**中文。** 定义：零和 Ramsey 指顶点 mod k 染色中无零和团的最大图。猜想：R_Z(k) 的渐近为 (1+o(1))· k· 2^{k};系数 1 由随机染色的第一矩方法匹配。
