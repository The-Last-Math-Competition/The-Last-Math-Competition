# 00000002544

**English.** Definition: The coarea formula: the integral identity for level-set measures of Lipschitz maps. Conjecture: The renormalized BV version of the coarea formula: the distributional derivative of the perimeter measure of level sets is the Hausdorff measure of the jump part with an explicit density correction, and the correction diverges logarithmically at degenerate critical points. (coarea BV renormalization)

**中文。** 定义：余面积公式：Lipschitz 映射的水平集测度的积分恒等式。猜想：余面积公式在 BV 框架的重整化版本：水平集的周长测度的分布导数为跳跃部的 Hausdorff 测度的显式密度修正且修正在退化临界点为对数发散。（余面积 BV 重整化）
