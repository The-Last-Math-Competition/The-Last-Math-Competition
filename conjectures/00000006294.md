# 00000006294

**English.** Definition: Bott two-periodicity is the two-periodicity of K-groups of stable algebras. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit two-type period and the two-periodicity isomorphism of stable algebras is canonical; the coefficient of the isomorphism is one; and the parity of the period breaks in the nonstable range, the recovery dimension being a quadratic-form invariant. (canonical coefficient conversion of two-periodicity)

**中文。** 定义：Bott 二周期：稳定代数的 K 群二周期。猜想：换算常数为周期的显式二型且稳定代数的二周期同构典范；同构的系数为一；周期的奇偶在非稳定范围破坏且破坏的恢复维数为二次型不变量。（二周期典范同构系数换算）
