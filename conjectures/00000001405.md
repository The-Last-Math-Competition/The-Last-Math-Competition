# 00000001405

**English.** Definition: The action of the mapping class group on cobordism is the cobordism operation induced by surface transformations. Conjecture: Two isotopic surface bands have equivalent cobordism structures if and only if the corresponding stable mapping classes are equivalent; and the criterion for equivalence is the intersection of the kernels of the Johnson homomorphisms. (stable equivalence Johnson criterion)

**中文。** 定义：映射类群在配边上的作用指曲面变换诱导的配边运算。猜想：两个同痕曲面带等价配边结构当且仅当对应稳定映射类等价；且等价的判据为 Johnson 同态核的交。（稳定等价 Johnson 判据）
