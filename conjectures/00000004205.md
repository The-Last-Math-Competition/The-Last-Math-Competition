**GM3-25 ★** — source: 125_几何测度III_极小极大.md

**English.** Definition: The biharmonic operator is Delta^2, and the biharmonic width is the min-max quantity of its eigenvalues. Conjecture: The Weyl constant of the biharmonic width spectrum and the Laplace width constant are related by a power conversion, with the exponent given explicitly by the dimension. (biharmonic width Weyl conversion exponent)

**中文。** 定义：双谐算子指 Δ²,双谐宽度指其特征值的极小极大量。猜想：双谐宽度谱的 Weyl 常数与 Laplace 宽度常数间为幂次换算,指数由维数显式给出。（双谐宽度 Weyl 换算指数）
