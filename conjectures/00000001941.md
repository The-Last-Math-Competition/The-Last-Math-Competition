# 00000001941

**LG-11 ◆** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: Joinings of diagonal flows {a_t} on rank-1 quotient spaces refer to the Ratner classification of orbit closures. Conjecture: All joinings are characterized by algebraic equations (Margulis–Tomanov); the first non-arithmetic example is given by the diagonal flow of the (2,3,7) triangle lattice (with joinings an explicit family of surfaces).

**中文。** 定义：对角流 {a_t} 在秩 1 商空间上的连接指其轨道闭包的 Ratner 分类。猜想：一切连接由代数方程刻画(Margulis–Tomanov);首个非算术例子由 (2,3,7) 三角形格的对角流给出(连接为显式曲面族)。
