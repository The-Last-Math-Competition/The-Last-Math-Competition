# 00000000447

**English.** Definition: The 0-Hecke algebra H_n(0). Conjecture: The number of isomorphism classes of indecomposable twisted modules of H_n(0) equals a Fibonacci number (a simple characterization), and the absolute Galois group acts trivially on them.

**中文。** 定义：0-Hecke 代数 H_n(0)。猜想：H_n(0) 的不可分解扭子模的同构类计数 = Fibonacci(简单特征化)且其绝对 Galois 群作用平凡。
