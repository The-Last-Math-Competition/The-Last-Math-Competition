# 00000004588

**English.** Definition: The Chern-Simons invariant is the characteristic-class integral of a three-manifold. Conjecture: For hyperbolic manifolds with a fixed volume cap the CS value distribution tends to uniform, with deviation an explicit small quantity of the volume boundary decaying linearly with the cap. (uniform linearly decaying CS distribution)

**中文。** 定义：Chern–Simons 不变量指三维流形的示性类积分。猜想：固定体积上限的双曲流形其 CS 值分布趋于均匀,偏差为体积边界的显式小量且随上限线性衰减。（CS 分布均匀线性衰减）
