**English.** Definition: The z-expansion coefficients p_{m,n}(K) of the HOMFLY-PT polynomial P(K;a,z). Conjecture: For each fixed m, the sequence p_{m,n} is a discretized correction of the partition function of the sl(m|1) fusion model of the Lie superalgebra; the correction vanishes at m = 1; and the absolute values of the coefficients form a unimodal sequence. (HOMFLY coefficients Lie super fusion)
