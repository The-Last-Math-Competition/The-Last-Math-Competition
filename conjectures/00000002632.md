# 00000002632

**MF-12 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: Vector bundles on Jacobi varieties: sections of line bundles on modular curve × elliptic curve. Conjecture: Index-m Jacobi forms are realized as sections of a line bundle; the vanishing theorem for the section sheaf holds for sufficiently large weight, with vanishing threshold given by the Serre-dual dimension of the index. (Jacobi section vanishing)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 簇上的矢丛：模曲线 × 椭圆曲线上的线丛截影。猜想：指标 m 的 Jacobi 形式实现为某线丛的截影；截影层的上同调消没定理在权充分大时成立且消没阈值由指标的 Serre 对偶维数给出。（Jacobi 截影消没）
