# 00000003801

**English.** Definition: The Palais–Smale condition: the completeness of variations. Conjecture: The compactness of Palais–Smale: the convergence of critical sequences of functionals, with the failure points of convergence mountain-pass points. (Palais-Smale mountain pass compactness critical sequence convergence)

**中文。** 定义：Palais–Smale 条件：变分的完备性。猜想：Palais–Smale 的紧性：泛函的临界序列的收敛且收敛的失效点为山道点。（Palais-Smale 山道紧性临界序列收敛）
