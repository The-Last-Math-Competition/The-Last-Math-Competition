# 00000008250

**EGT-20 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: The measure of the Braess paradox in network games: the explicit bound braess_bound on the selfish-routing deterioration ratio from adding an edge. Conjecture: braess_bound (the upper bound on the social cost deterioration ratio from a new edge) is floor(n/2) (a half-count upper bound law); the tightness of the bound (networks attaining floor(n/2)) is achieved by the Params trapezoid network family (a trapezoid tight family law); the density of the existence domain of the paradox (Braess sparsity of network subsets) is 1/6 (a one-sixth density law); and the noise threshold of robustness of the paradox (existence under perturbed networks) is epsilon = 1/(4d) (d the diameter, a four-diameter threshold). (Braess half-count trapezoid one-sixth density)

**中文。** 定义：网络博弈的 Napels Braess 悖论的计量：自私路由的 Braess 上界的显式 braess_bound。猜想：braess_bound（新增边的社会代价恶化比的上界）= ⌊n/2⌋（半数上界律）；且上界的紧性（达到 ⌊n/2⌋ 的网络）由 Params 梯形网络族取得（梯形紧族律）；悖论的存在域（网络的子集的 Braess 稀疏性）的密度为 1/6（六分之一密度律）；悖论的鲁棒性（扰动网络的存在性）的噪声阈为 ε=1/(4d)（d 为直径，四倍直径阈）。
