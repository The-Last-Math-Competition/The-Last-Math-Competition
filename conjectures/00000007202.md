# 00000007202

**English.** Definition: Zariski's main theorem with the extension of function fields. Conjecture: The obstruction to the extension is the deficit of the normalization, and the vanishing of the deficit is pseudo-convexity. (pseudo-convex Zariski extension obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：Zariski 主定理：函数域的延拓。猜想：Zariski 主定理的推广：延拓的障碍为正规化的亏格且格的消没为伪凸。（Zariski 主定理伪凸亏格正规化消没延拓障碍）
