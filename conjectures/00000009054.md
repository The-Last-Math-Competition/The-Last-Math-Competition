# 00000009054

**SMI-14 ★** — source: 287_子流形几何不等式.md

**English.** Definition: The Gauss map of a submanifold: the normalization of normals. Conjecture: Harmonicity is equivalent to the conjunction of minimality and cominimality; and the coordinates of the equivalence are componentwise harmonic. (Gauss map harmonic minimality equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：子流形的 Gauss 映射为法向的单位化。猜想：调和性等价于极小性与余极小性的联合；且等价的坐标为分量调和。（Gauss 映射调和极小等价）
