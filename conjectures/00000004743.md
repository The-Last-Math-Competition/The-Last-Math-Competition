**English.** Definition: A diagonal quartic surface is the quartic diagonal equation of coefficient type. Conjecture: The explicit table of its Brauer generators consists of finite symbolic families, configured explicitly through genus-one fibrations, and the small-coefficient case is complete. (family table of diagonal quartic Brauer elements)

**中文。** 定义：对角四次曲面指系数型的四次曲面对角方程。猜想：其 Brauer 生成元的显式表由有限符号族组成,族经亏格一纤维化配置显式且小系数情形完整。（对角四次 Brauer 元素族表）
