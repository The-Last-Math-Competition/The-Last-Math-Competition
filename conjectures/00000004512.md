# 00000004512

**English.** Definition: The lowest cost of a random assignment is the total weight of the optimal matching. Conjecture: The limit of its variance exists and can be represented explicitly through the Gamma values of a second Painleve connection problem, and the representation is unique. (Painleve representation of the assignment variance)
