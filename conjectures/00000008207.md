# 00000008207

**ORB-07 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The Clifford correspondence between irreducible representations of an inverse monoid M and representations of its unit group G(M): the fiber structure at the level of equivalence classes of the restriction map to G(M). Conjecture: The image of the restriction map consists exactly of the irreducible representations of G(M) fixed by the centralizer action of the maximal group congruence class; every fiber has cardinality exactly 2^k, where k is the number of non-group congruence components in the Rees quotient chain of M; and the smallest example where the correspondence fails for general monoids is the four-point truncation of the full transformation monoid, whose fibers exhibit a non-power branch count of 3. (inverse monoid Clifford power-of-two fibers)

**中文。** 定义：逆幺半群 M 的不可约表示与单位群 G(M) 表示的 Clifford 对应：限制到 G(M) 的映射在等价类层面的纤维结构。猜想：限制映射的像恰为 G(M) 中被最大群同余类的中心化子作用固定的不可约表示；每条纤维的基数恒等于 2^k，其中 k 为 M 的 Rees 商链中非群同余分量数；一般幺半群中对应失效的最小例子是满变换幺半群的四点截断，其纤维出现非平方分支数 3。
