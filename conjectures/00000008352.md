# 00000008352

**TRP-02 ★** — source: 264_热带几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The h-vector of the dual graph of tropicalization: the volume berg_vol of the h-polytope of tree-like complexes (Bergman fans). Conjecture: berg_vol (the subdivision volume of the Bergman fan) equals the beta-invariant of the corresponding matroid (a beta-invariant formula); the strict log-concavity of the h-vector holds for uniform matroids (a uniform strictness law); and the mod-p count of the volume (p-volume) equals the characteristic-p Tutte polynomial (a Tutte-p duality law), the proof path being the Crapo-Rota bracket (a C-R path). (Bergman beta-invariant uniform Tutte duality)

**中文。** 定义：热带化的对偶图的雪松（h-vector）：树状复形（Bergman fan）的 h-多面体的体积 berg_vol。猜想：berg_vol（Bergman fan 的细分体积）= 相应的 matroid 的 β-不变量（β-不变量公式）；且 h-向量的单峰性（对数凹）的严格（严格对数凹）的 matroid 为一致（一致严格律）；体积的模（特征 p）的计数（p-体积）= 特征 p 的 Tutte 多项式（Tutte-p 对偶律），对偶的证明路径为 Crapo–Rota 的括号（C-R 路径）。
