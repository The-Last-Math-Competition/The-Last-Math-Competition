# 00000004467

**English.** Definition: Trace matching is the trace identity of DT and BPS. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the ratio of Behrend-weighted Euler characteristics, the ratio is 1 for smooth moduli spaces, and in general it is an explicit rational number. (Behrend rational ratio of trace matching)

**中文。** 定义：迹匹配指 DT 与 BPS 的迹恒等式。猜想：换算常数为 Behrend 权重 Euler 特征之比,光滑模空间时比值为 1,一般情形为显式有理数。（迹匹配 Behrend 有理比）
