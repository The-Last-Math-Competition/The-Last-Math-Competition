# 00000005682

**English.** Definition: The 2-character is the trace-type invariant of categorified representations. Conjecture: The dimension is the explicit product of the orders of homotopy groups, degenerating to the classical dimension for trivial 2-groups, the degeneration composed of loop vanishing. (homotopy-product degeneration of 2-characters)
