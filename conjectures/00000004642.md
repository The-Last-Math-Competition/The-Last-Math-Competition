# 00000004642

**English.** Definition: The triplet algebra is the central extension of the even part of symplectic fermions. Conjecture: The projective rank of its representation category is the explicit formula of type p minus one over two, the rank is linear in p, and for small p it agrees with the known values. (linear formula of triplet projective ranks)

**中文。** 定义：triplet 代数指辛费米子偶部的中心扩张。猜想：其表示范畴的投射秩为 p 减一之半型显式公式,秩随 p 线性且在小 p 与已知值一致。（triplet 投射秩线性公式）
