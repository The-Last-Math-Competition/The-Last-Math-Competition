# 00000008428

**DFG-18 ★** — source: 266_组合设计与有限几何II.md

**English.** Definition: A perfect difference family is a difference family in which every element is covered exactly once. Conjecture: The existence domain of perfect difference families is an explicit triangular region in (v,k,1); and all integer points on the boundary are given by projective-line type difference families. (existence domain of perfect difference families)

**中文。** 定义：完美差族指每元素恰被覆盖一次的差族。猜想：完美差族的存在域为 (v,k,1) 的三角型显式区域；边界的整数点全部由射影直线型差族给出。（完美差族存在域）
