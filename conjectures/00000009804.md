**DDG-14 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Discrete Yamabe flow: the discrete conformal curvature flow within vertex-scaling classes. Conjecture: The discrete Yamabe flow converges globally within the class of attainable target curvatures, at polynomial rate; the rate exponent is explicitly given by the discrete conformal distance between initial and target metrics. (discrete Yamabe global convergence)

**中文。** 定义：离散 Yamabe 流：顶点缩放类中的离散共形曲率流。猜想：离散 Yamabe 流在可达目标曲率类内全局收敛且收敛为多项式率；率的指数由初始度量与目标度量的离散共形距离显式给出。（离散 Yamabe 全局收敛）
