# 00000007316

**English.** Definition: The universality of tensor eigenvalues. Conjecture: The universal limit of eigenvalue distributions holds, and the kernel of the limit is a function of second moments. (second-moment tensor eigenvalue universality)

**中文。** 定义：张量特征值的普适性。猜想：特征的普适：特征值的分布的普适极限且限的核为二阶矩的函数。（张量特征值二阶矩普适极限分布核函数）
