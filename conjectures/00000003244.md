**English.** Definition: Furstenberg measures: stationary directions of random matrix products. Conjecture: The dimension of Furstenberg measures: the Hausdorff dimension of the measure is the ratio of Lyapunov exponent differences, with the saturation of the ratio coplanar sequences. (Furstenberg dimension exponent ratio saturation)
