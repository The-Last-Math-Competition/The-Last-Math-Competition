# 00000008959

**English.** Definition: The KP equation: the two-dimensional generalization of KdV. Conjecture: The Schur expansion of its tau function has rational coefficients at rational initial data, with support a Young ideal. (KP Schur rationality)

**中文。** 定义：KP 方程指 KdV 的二维推广。猜想：其 τ 函数的 Schur 展开在有理初值下系数为有理数且支撑为 Young 理想。（KP Schur 有理性）
