# 00000007788

**ACG-08 ★** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: random polytope K_N=conv(X_1,…,X_N) (X_i independent all in K). Conjecture: quantity 1-E vol(K_N)/vol(B) asymptotic of pair ball B^n, E vol(K_N)/vol(B) is c_n N^{-2/(n-1)}, constant c_n can by exact computation, of ball plane point measure and c_n/c_{n-1} -> √(π/2); pair should exponent not constant doubling, two element constant difference is can solution combinatorial quantity of exactly angle point measure.

**中文。** 定义：随机多胞形 K_N=conv(X_1,…,X_N)（X_i 独立均匀于 K）。猜想：对球 B^n，E vol(K_N)/vol(B) 的缺失量 1-E vol(K_N)/vol(B) 渐近为 c_n N^{-2/(n-1)}，常数 c_n 可由球面单点测度的精确渐进计算，且 c_n/c_{n-1}→√(π/2)；对单纯形相应指数不变而常数翻倍，两个常数之差恰为角点测度贡献的可解释组合量。
