# 00000001891

**English.** Conjecture: The model companion of the difference-differential composite fields of characteristic zero exists (axiomatized by mixed ideals of difference and differential polynomials); the theory is simple but not supersimple (composite independence extends Kim independence).

**中文。** 猜想：正特征 0 的差分-微分复合域的模型伴侣存在(其公理化由差分多项式与微分多项式的混合理想);该理论为简单但不超简单(复合独立性由 Kim 独立推广)。
