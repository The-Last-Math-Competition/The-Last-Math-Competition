# 00000006048

**English.** Definition: MALL is the linear logic subsystem with additives. Conjecture: Branch weights are indexed by additive multisets, the combination of the index being the sum decomposition; the lower bound of the branch count is half the conclusion count; and the orthogonality of the decomposition is given by the linear negation of the additive unit, the negation unique. (additive-multiset sum-decomposition indexing of MALL branches)

**中文。** 定义：乘加片段：含加法的线性逻辑子系统（MALL）。猜想：分支权重由加法多重集索引且索引的组合为和分解；分支数的下界为结论数之半；分解的正交性由加法单位的线性否定给出且否定唯一。（乘加分支多重集和分解索引）
