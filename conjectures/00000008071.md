# 00000008071

**PRF-21 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The proof-theoretic size of program extraction: the inflation ap(pi) of the size of programs extracted from Pi_2 proofs of PA (the size of Kreisel's realizers). Conjecture: ap(pi) is tightly double-exponentially bounded (the double-exponential law of the Godel-Gentzen translation plus no-counterexample interpretation); direct extraction (modified realizability of the electorate style) inflates by a single exponential (a single-exponential advantage of improved realization); and the worst-case examples of the inflation rate correspond one-to-one with the arithmetic combination objects of Gelfond-Schneider-type transcendental statements (lower bounds on the size of elementary proofs of hypergeometric sums, a worst-transcendence duality). (Kreisel realizability double-exponential tightness)

**中文。** 定义：程序抽取的证明论规模：从 PA 的 Π₂ 证明抽取程序的规模的膨胀 ap(π)（Kreisel 的实现子的规模）。猜想：ap(π) 为双指数界紧（Goedel–Gentzen 翻译+no-counterexample 的双指数律）；且直接抽取（选民式 modified realizability）的膨胀为单指数（改良实现的单指数优势）；膨胀率的最坏例子与 Gelfond–Schneider 型超越语句的算术化的组合对象（超几何和的初等证明的规模下界）一一对应（最坏-超越对偶）。
