**English.** Definition: Coefficients and functorial structure are two layers of the L-function. Conjecture: There exist two L-functions agreeing in the first million coefficients but with different functorial structure, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair of a twisted zeta and a product L-function. (same coefficient prefix-functorial structure separation)
