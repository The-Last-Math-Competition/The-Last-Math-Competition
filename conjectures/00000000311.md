# 00000000311

**English.** Definition: The Lagrange spectrum L consists of isolated discrete points on the interval (3, 3.01), the open intervals between adjacent points being spectral gaps. Conjecture: Both endpoints of the first gap in (3, 3.01) are quadratic irrationals whose eventually periodic continued fractions are given by two palindromic words over the alphabet {1,2,3}; and the number of isolated points in this interval is exactly 13.

**中文。** 定义：Lagrange 谱 L 在区间 (3, 3.01) 内由离散的孤立点组成,相邻孤立点之间的开区间为谱间隙。猜想：(3, 3.01) 内第一个间隙的两个端点均为二次无理数,其连分数展开最终周期部分由字母 {1,2,3} 上的两个回文词给出;且该区间内孤立点个数恰为 13。
