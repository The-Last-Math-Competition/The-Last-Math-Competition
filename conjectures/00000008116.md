**NAG-06 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The Fourier-Mukai type invariant of derived equivalences of noncommutative varieties: the signature sig(D^b(A)) of the Euler pairing on derived cohomology. Conjecture: sig is a derived invariant (homological conservativity) and equals the Hodge signature of the central variety plus a noncommutative correction Delta; the explicit form of Delta is the signature of the even part of the Hochschild cohomology of A (an HH signature correction formula); and the correction vanishes if and only if A is Azumaya (an Azumaya vanishing criterion). (derived signature HH even correction)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的导出等价的 Fourier–Mukai 型不变的量：dcH（导出上同调）的 Euler 配对的符号差 sig(D^b(A))。猜想：sig 为导出不变量（同调保守性）且 sig = 中心簇的 Hodge 符号差的非交换修正 Δ；修正 Δ 的显式为 A 的 Hochschild 上同调的偶部的符号（HH-符号修正公式）；修正的消没当且仅当 A 为 Azumaya（Azumaya 消没判据）。
