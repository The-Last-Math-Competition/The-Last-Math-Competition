**English.** Definition: The index as the quantified structure of inclusion. Conjecture: The value spectrum of the index splits into discrete and continuous domains; the gap of the discrete domain is fourfold; and the attainment of the fourfold gap is the prime-index tower. (discrete-continuous index value spectrum)
