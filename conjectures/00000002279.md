**English.** Definition: Hypercontractivity of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup (Nelson, known). Conjecture: The optimal value of the perturbation bound for the hypercontractivity constant of the weighted OU semigroup is explicit in the Poincaré constant of the weight (weighted hypercontractivity).

**中文。** 定义：Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 半群的超压缩性(已知 Nelson)。猜想：加权 OU 的超压缩常数的扰动界的最优值为权重的 Poincaré 常数显式(加权超压缩)。
