# 00000001802

**English.** Definition: The polynomial hull of a compact set. Conjecture: The polynomial hull of the Hopf link (p,q) is an explicit fibration: hull = {(z,w) : |z| ≤ 1, |w| ≤ r(|z|)} with r a (p,q)-type weight function; the fibers are explicit Riemann surfaces (bounded parametrizations of algebraic curves).

**中文。** 定义：紧集的多项式凸包。猜想：Hopf 链环(p,q) 的多项式凸包为显式纤维化:凸包 = {(z,w):|z|≤1,|w|≤r(|z|)}(r 为 (p,q) 型权函数);纤维为显式 Riemann 面(由代数曲线的有界参数化)。
