# 00000004002

**RP2-02 ★** — source: 119_随机分析II_粗路径.md

**English.** Definition: Fractional Brownian motion is the self-similar Gaussian process with Hurst parameter H. Conjecture: In the logarithm of the expected signature only the first two levels are ever nonzero, and the two level values interpolate continuously in H between one half and one. (two-level property of the fBM expected signature)

**中文。** 定义：分数 Brown 运动指带 Hurst 参数 H 的自相似高斯过程。猜想：期望签名的对数中恒仅前两层非零；且两层值随 H 在二分之一与一间连续插值。（fBM 期望签名两层性）
