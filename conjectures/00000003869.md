**RM3-19 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: The β=1 skew kernel is the Fredholm–Pfaffian expansion kernel of GOE-type point processes. Conjecture: Each term of the expansion corresponds to a matching, the sign is exactly the power of (−1) by crossing count, and the crossing count is computable in polynomial time. (skew kernel crossing sign formula)

**中文。** 定义：β=1 的 skew 核指 GOE 型点过程的 Fredholm–Pfaffian 展开核。猜想：展开的每一项对应一个匹配,符号恰为 (−1) 的交叉数次幂,且交叉数可在多项式时间内计算。（skew 核交叉符号公式）
