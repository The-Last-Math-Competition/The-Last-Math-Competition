# 00000008522

**English.** Definition: The elementarity criterion for exponential integrals. Conjecture: The Liouville criterion for elementary solutions reduces to solvability of the differential Galois group; and the minimal number of nesting levels of the exponential product of solutions of a solvable group equals the derived length of the group. (exponential nesting level count)

**中文。** 定义：指数积分的初等性判据。猜想：Liouville 初等解的判据可约化为微分 Galois 群的可解性；可解群的解的嵌套指数积的极小层数等于群的导出长度。（指数嵌套层数）
