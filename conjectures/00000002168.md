# 00000002168

**GA-28 ★** — source: 57_代数图论II.md

**English.** Definition: The Frobenius–Perron vector is the positive eigenvector of the spectral radius; the peak is the position of the maximal component. Conjecture: The distribution of peak positions (on uniform trees) converges to an explicit distribution; the limiting density is the Beta(1/2,1/2) type C·x^{-1/2}(1−x)^{-1/2}.

**中文。** 定义：Frobenius–Perron 向量指谱半径对应的正特征向量;最大分量的位置为 peak。猜想：peak 位置的分布(均匀树上)收敛到显式分布;极限密度为 C·x^{-1/2}(1-x)^{-1/2} 的 Beta(1/2,1/2) 型。
