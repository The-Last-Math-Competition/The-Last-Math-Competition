# 00000001147

**English.** Definition: A triangular Lie algebra is a subalgebra of triangular matrices. Conjecture: The dimension of the second cohomology of a triangular Lie algebra is two; and the dimension is realized by one contribution each from the outer derivations and the center. (triangular H2 dimension two)

**中文。** 定义：三角李代数为三角矩阵组成的子代数。猜想：三角李代数的二阶上同调维数为二；且维数的实现由外导子与中心各贡献一维。（三角 H2 维数二）
