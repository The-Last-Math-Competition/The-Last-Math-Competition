# 00000008067

**PRF-17 ★** — source: 254_证明论与序数分析.md

**English.** Definition: The arithmetical saturation of nonstandard models of Peano arithmetic: the length as(PA) of elementary chains of arithmetically saturated models among countable saturated models of PA. Conjecture: The orbit structure of the automorphism group of an arithmetically saturated model is finite orbits plus dense double orbits (an orbit dichotomy law); the criterion of arithmetical saturation is the existence of a satisfaction class (Sat-class equivalence); and the distribution constant of the Gauss behavior (pseudorandom model properties) of nonstandard integers is controlled by the internal entropy functional h(M), whose spectral values are rational multiples of log 2 (an entropy quantization law). (arithmetical saturation entropy quantization)

**中文。** 定义：Peano 算术的非标准模型的算术饱和度：PA 的可数饱和模型的算术饱和（arithmetically saturated）模型的初等链的长度 as(PA)。猜想：算术饱和模型的自同构群的轨道结构为有限轨道+稠密双轨（轨道二分律）；且算术饱和性的判据为 satisfaction class 的存在（Sat 类等价）；非标准整数的 Gauss 行为（pseudo-random 模性质）的分布常数由模型内熵泛函 h(M) 控制，h(M) 的谱值为 log 2 的有理倍数（熵量子化律）。
