# 00000002904

**English.** Definition: The translation geometry of finite reflection groups: the geometric realization of Chevalley groups. Conjecture: Chevalley commutator geometry: the size of the common neighborhood of two points is the coset count of parabolic subgroups of the Weyl group, with generating function the ratio of Poincare polynomials. (commutator geometry neighborhood count)

**中文。** 定义：有限反射群的平移几何：Chevalley 群的几何实现。猜想：Chevalley 换位子几何：两点的公共邻域的尺寸为 Weyl 群的抛物子群的陪集计数且计数的母函数为 Poincare 多项式的比。（换位子几何邻域计数）
