# 00000008235

**EGT-05 ★** — source: 260_数理经济与博弈的数学.md

**English.** Definition: Revenue equivalence and optimal reserve prices in auctions: the distribution dependence of the Myerson optimal reserve price r* for first- and second-price auctions. Conjecture: The closed formula for r* (the Myerson reserve price with independent private values) is the virtual-value conversion F^{-1}(1 - 1/c) (a virtual value formula, c a constant); the explicit gain of the linkage principle for affiliated values is a quadratic function of the affiliation coefficient (a quadratic affiliation gain law); and the sign of the revenue difference between English and first-price auctions is always positive (an English advantage law), the asymptotic magnitude of the advantage (large n) being linear in the affiliation degree (a linear advantage magnitude). (Myerson virtual reserve linkage quadratic gain)

**中文。** 定义：拍卖的收入等价与最优保留价：一价/二价拍卖的期望收入的 Myerson 最优保留价 r* 的分布依赖。猜想：r*（独立私人价值的 Myerson 保留价）的封闭公式 = F^{-1}(1-1/c) 的虚值率换算（虚值率公式，c 为常量）；且关联价值（affiliated）的收入的 Linkage 原理的增益的显式为关联系数的二次函数（关联二次增益律）；English 与第一价的收入差的符号恒为正（English 优势律），优势的量的渐近（n 大）为关联度的线性（线性优势量）。
