# 00000005801

**NL3-01 ◆** — source: 179_非线性扩散_平均曲率与多孔介质.md

**English.** Definition: The mean curvature flow evolves the interface along the normal at curvature speed. Conjecture: The maximal-curvature blow-up of three-dimensional convex surfaces is an explicit ratio of the reciprocal of the shrinking time, one for shrinking spheres, and degenerating ellipsoids asymptotically one half. (curvature blow-up time-ratio formula of convex surfaces)

**中文。** 定义：平均曲率流指界面沿法向以曲率速度演化。猜想：三维凸曲面的最大曲率爆破为收缩时间倒数的显式比,球面收缩时为一且退化椭圆渐近半。（凸曲面曲率爆破时间比式）
