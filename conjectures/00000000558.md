# 00000000558

**English.** Definition: Koszul duality dualizes exterior algebras with symmetric algebras. Conjecture: For higher Koszul algebras, the graded dimensions of the matrix-factorization categories of an algebra and its dual are reciprocal; this dimension-exchange formula holds exactly on Clifford-type algebras. (Koszul duality dimension exchange)

**中文。** 定义：Koszul 对偶把外代数与对称代数相互对偶化。猜想：高阶 Koszul 代数的对偶的矩阵因子化范畴的分次维数互为倒数；该维数交换公式在 Clifford 型代数上精确成立。（Koszul 对偶维数交换）
