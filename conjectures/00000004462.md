**English.** Definition: The BPS counts of K3 surfaces are the stable-pair invariants. Conjecture: The all-genus formula is given by an explicit combination of Noether-Lefschetz numbers with generating functions, and the formula is closed and uniform for all primes. (closed all-genus K3 BPS formula)

**中文。** 定义：K3 曲面的 BPS 计数指稳定对不变量。猜想：全亏格公式由 Noether–Lefschetz 数与生成母函数的显式组合给出,公式封闭且对一切素数一致。（K3 BPS 全亏格封闭公式）
