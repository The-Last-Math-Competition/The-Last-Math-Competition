# 00000005112

**English.** Definition: Newton iteration for the matrix sign function is its classical quadratically convergent solution. Conjecture: The boundary of the convergence domain of the iteration is an explicit algebraic curve of the initial residual, given by a polynomial condition on the real parts of the spectrum, and the boundary is completely characterized. (algebraic boundary of sign-function Newton convergence)

**中文。** 定义：矩阵符号函数的牛顿迭代指其经典二次收敛求解。猜想：迭代收敛域边界为初始残差的显式代数曲线,由谱实部的多项式条件给出且边界完全刻画。（符号函数牛顿收敛代数边界）
