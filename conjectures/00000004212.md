**HT2-02 ◆** — source: 126_组合代数拓扑_高阶树.md

**English.** Definition: A higher-dimensional spanning forest is a supporting chain group of fixed torsion-free dimension. Conjecture: For triangulations of a d-sphere the generating function of the higher forest count is rational, with denominator an explicit power product of (1+t) in the numbers of faces. (rational generating function of higher forests)

**中文。** 定义：高维生成森林指固定无挠维数的支撑子链群。猜想：d-球面剖分的高维森林计数的母函数为有理函数,分母为 (1+t) 的面数次显式幂积。（高维森林有理母函数）
