# 00000001499

**HY-19 ◆** — source: 35_双曲动力系统.md

**English.** Definition: The marked length spectrum is the set of lengths of shortest representatives over free homotopy classes of the surface. Conjecture: The marked length spectrum of a closed surface determines its metric; moreover, there is an explicit determining set of O(g) closed geodesics (with coefficient 6g−6): their length spectrum uniquely determines the hyperbolic metric.

**中文。** 定义：标记长度谱指曲面自由同伦类的最短代表长度全体。猜想：闭曲面的标记长度谱决定其度量;进一步,存在 O(g) 条闭测地线(系数为 6g−6)的显式决定集:其长度谱唯一确定双曲度量。
