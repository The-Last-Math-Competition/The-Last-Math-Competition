# 00000001478

**CX-28 △** — source: 34_凸分析与最优化.md

**English.** Definition: The nuclear norm of a tensor is its minimal sum of rank-one tensors. Conjecture: The nuclear-norm unit ball of tensors in C^{2×2×2} is the image, under a polynomial map, of an explicit spectrahedral cone (a generalized inequality based on 4×4 symmetric matrices); this representation gives SDP computability of the 2×2×2 nuclear norm.

**中文。** 定义：张量的核范数为其秩一张量的最小和。猜想：ℂ^{2×2×2} 张量的核范数单位球为某显式谱超凸锥(基于 4×4 对称矩阵的广义不等式)在多项式映射下的像;该表示给出 2×2×2 核范数的 SDP 可计算性。
