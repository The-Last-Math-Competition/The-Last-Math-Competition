**HSS-27 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of products of independent Wigner matrices: Fuss-Catalan limits and local universality. Conjecture: The spectral measure of products converges to the Fuss-Catalan distribution, given by the unique solution of a free moment problem; bulk local statistics remain sine-universal, and edge extremes are Airy type with an explicit product-corrected kernel. (product spectrum Fuss-Catalan)

**中文。** 定义：独立 Wigner 矩阵乘积的谱：Fuss–Catalan 型极限与局部普适。猜想：乘积的谱测度收敛到 Fuss–Catalan 分布且该分布由自由矩问题的唯一解给出；bulk 的局部统计仍为 sine 普适，边缘的极值分布为 Airy 型带显式乘积修正核。（乘积谱 Fuss-Catalan）
