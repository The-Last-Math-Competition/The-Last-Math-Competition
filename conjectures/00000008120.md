# 00000008120

**English.** Definition: The Auslander constant of the noncommutative local rings of points of noncommutative varieties: the index aus(P) of the Cohen-Macaulay representation type of the local endomorphism ring. Conjecture: aus(P) (the criterion for finite CM type) is locally rigid: the CM type of a noncommutative point is finite if and only if its exceptional sequence length is at most 3 (a 3-exceptional criterion); the Auslander-Reiten quivers of points of finite CM type are of ADE type (an ADE local classification); and the minimal noncommutative realizations of type E (E_6, E_7, E_8) are the cubic points of P^3 (a cubic point-E type correspondence law). (Auslander ADE cubic point realization)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的点的非交换局部环的 Auslander 常数：局部端环的 Cohen–Macaulay 表示型的指标 aus(P)。猜想：aus(P)（有限 CM 型的判据）为局部刚性：非交换点的 CM 型有限当且仅当其"例外序列长度"≤ 3（3-例外判据）；且有限 CM 型的点的 Auslander–Reiten 箭图的型为 ADE（ADE 局部分类）；E 型（E₆,E₇,E₈）的最小非交换实现为 P³ 的三次点（三次点-E 型对应律）。
