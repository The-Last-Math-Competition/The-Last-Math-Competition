# 00000007815

**MSE-05 ★** — source: 246_模空间与枚举几何II.md

**English.** Definition: Semisimplicity of the quantum cohomology ring QH^*(X) means the Frobenius manifold multiplication is diagonalizable at a general point; the Dubrovin conjecture links semisimplicity to exceptional collections in the derived category. Conjecture: For a Fano threefold X, quantum cohomology is semisimple if and only if there is a full exceptional collection; and the Zariski closure of the monodromy representation of the Dubrovin connection on big quantum cohomology is the generalized group Gamma_X of Sym^n(C^*) type, whose number of connected components equals the Brill-Noether genericity index of X. (Dubrovin semisimplicity-exceptional equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：量子上同调环 QH^*(X) 的半单性指 Frobenius 流形的乘法在一般点可对角化。Dubrovin 猜想链接半单性与导出范畴的例外组。猜想：对 Fano 三维簇 X，量子上同调半单当且仅当存在满 exceptional collection；且分裂型（big quantum 同调的 Dubrovin 联络的单值表示）的 Zariski 闭包为 Sym^n(C^*) 的推广群 Γ_X，其连通分支数等于 X 的 Brill-Noether 一般型指数。
