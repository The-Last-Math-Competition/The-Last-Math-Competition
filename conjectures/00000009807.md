# 00000009807

**English.** Definition: Polyhedral Willmore energy: the discrete functional of edge dihedral deficits. Conjecture: The infimum of polyhedral Willmore energy on genus-one surfaces is the discrete 2 pi squared; the infimum is attained by explicit refinement sequences of triangulations, and the functional Gamma-converges to the smooth Willmore energy in the limit. (discrete Willmore infimum)

**中文。** 定义：多面体 Willmore 能量：棱二面角亏损的离散泛函。猜想：多面体 Willmore 能量在亏格一曲面上的下确界为离散 2π²；下确界由三角剖分加细的显式序列达到且泛函在极限下 Γ-收敛到光滑 Willmore 能量。（离散 Willmore 下确界）
