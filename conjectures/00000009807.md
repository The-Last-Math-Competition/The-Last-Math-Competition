# 00000009807

**DDG-17 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: discrete functional of polytope Willmore energy: two plane angle. Conjecture: infimum of polytope Willmore energy in genus one surface on is discrete 2π²; infimum by explicit sequence of three angle triangulation attains and functional in limit under Γ- converges to smooth Willmore energy. (discrete Willmore infimum)

**中文。** 定义：多面体 Willmore 能量：棱二面角亏损的离散泛函。猜想：多面体 Willmore 能量在亏格一曲面上的下确界为离散 2π²；下确界由三角剖分加细的显式序列达到且泛函在极限下 Γ-收敛到光滑 Willmore 能量。（离散 Willmore 下确界）
