**OIE-11 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Quantum monotonicity of noncommutative Renyi entropy: interpolation inequalities between Petz and minimal entropy. Conjecture: The optimal interpolation constants for log-convexity of the quantum Renyi entropy family in the parameter alpha exist explicitly; the constant of the alpha from 1 to 2 interpolation inequality is an explicit ratio of Vandermonde determinants and is tight. (quantum Renyi interpolation constant)

**中文。** 定义：非交换 Renyi 熵的量子单调性：Petz 与最小熵的插入不等式。猜想：量子 Rényi 熵族在参数 α 的对数凸性的最优插入常数存在显式；α 从 1 到 2 的插值不等式的常数为 Vandermonde 行列式的显式比且为紧。（量子 Renyi 插入常数）
