# 00000006984

**NF2-14 ★** — source: 218_非线性傅里叶分析.md

**English.** Definition: The explicit structure of critical corrections. Conjecture: The correction table is complete and closed; the combination of the closure is the geometric mean; and the geometric mean combination is preserved along product chains. (closed critical correction of products)

**中文。** 定义：临界修正的显式结构。猜想：修正表完全且封闭；且封闭的组合为几何平均；且几何平均的组合在乘积链保持。（乘积临界修正封闭）
