# 00000008753

**English.** Definition: Negative K-groups: the existence and dimension dependence of K_{-i}. Conjecture: The Weibel bound: negative K of schemes of dimension d vanishes for i > d (a Weibel law); there is the compactness characterization by compactified dimension (a compactness law); examples of negative K for noncommutative categories (a noncommutative example law); and a geometric interpretation of negative K (an interpretation law). (negative K Weibel dimension vanishing)

**中文。** 定义：负 K-群：K_{-i} 的存在与维数依赖。猜想：Weibel 界：维数 d 的方案的负 K 在 i>d 消没（Weibel 律）；且紧致维数的紧化刻画（紧致律）；非交换范畴的负 K 例子（非交换例律）；负 K 的几何解释（解释律）。
