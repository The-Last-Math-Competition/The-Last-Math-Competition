# 00000004269

**English.** Definition: A logarithmic VOA is a vertex algebra whose modules carry Jordan blocks, and the fusion rules are the module tensor products. Conjecture: The minimal central charge of non-semisimple fusion is -22/5, attained by an explicit member of the triplet family. (minimal central charge of non-semisimple logarithmic fusion)

**中文。** 定义：对数 VOA 指模带 Jordan 块的顶点代数,融合规则指模张量积。猜想：非半单融合的最小中心荷为 −22/5,由 triplet 家族的显式成员达到。（log 非半单最小中心荷）
