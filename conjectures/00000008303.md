# 00000008303

**FLO-13 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The h-principle classification imm_class of regular homotopy classes of Lagrangian immersions: the realization map from formal Lagrangian immersions (homotopy classes of Lagrangian lifts of tangent spaces) to genuine Lagrangian immersions. Conjecture: imm_class (regular homotopy classes of Lagrangian immersions) is completely characterized by the classification of formal Lagrangians (the Gromov-Lees criterion, a Lees complete characterization law); the minimal dimension of the embedding exception (the immersion-embedding gap) is 4 (a four-dimensional gap example); and the quantification of the gap (the embedding obstruction) is the Polterovich-Seyfarti squaring obstruction (a squaring obstruction law), whose vanishing criterion is two-sided Maslov classes (a two-sided criterion law). (Lagrangian h-principle Lees squaring obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：Lagrangian 浸入的正则同伦类的 h-原理分类 imm_class：形式 Lagrangian 浸入（切空间的拉格朗日提升的同伦类）到真实 Lagrangian 浸入的实现映射。猜想：imm_class（Lagrangian 浸入的正则同伦类）由形式 Lagrangian 的分类（Gromov–Lees 判据）完全刻画（Lees 完全刻画律）；且嵌入的例外（浸入-嵌入的间隙）的最小维数为 4（四维间隙例）；间隙的量化（嵌入的障碍）为 Polterovich–Seyfarti 的平方障碍（平方障碍律），障碍的消没的判据为双向法从（双向判据律）。
