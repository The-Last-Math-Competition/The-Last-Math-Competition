# 00000009619

**English.** Definition: The periodic specification property gives the time overhead T(epsilon,n) of gluing orbit segments; optimal control of specification time relates to finite-size effects in statistical physics. Conjecture: The specification time of a mixing SFT satisfies T(n) <= C log n, with C an explicit function of the inverse spectral gap of the transition matrix and not improvable; and systems with slow specification (polynomial but nonlogarithmic T(n)) form a non-dense second-category exception among mixing systems. (specification time optimal constant)

**中文。** 定义：周期规格性质给出轨道段拼接的时间开销 T(ε,n)；规格时间的最优控制关系到统计物理的有限尺寸效应。猜想：混合 SFT 的规格时间满足 T(n) ≤ C·log n，常数 C 为转移矩阵谱隙倒数的显式函数且不可改进；慢规格（T(n) 为多项式但非对数）的系统在混合系统内为非稠密的第二范畴例外。（规格时间最优常数）
