# 00000000620

**English.** Definition: A crossing change is the local operation of flipping the over-under relation at one crossing of a knot diagram. Conjecture: For every non-alternating knot, some crossing change simultaneously alters at least two Khovanov homology groups (no crossing perturbs exactly one graded group); for alternating knots, every crossing change perturbs exactly one group.

**中文。** 定义：单交叉变更指把纽结图中一个交叉的过欠关系翻转的局部操作。猜想：对一切非交替纽结,存在某个交叉变更同时改变至少两个 Khovanov 同调群(即不存在只扰动一个分次群的交叉);交替纽结的每个交叉变更恰扰动一个群。
