# 00000005124

**English.** Definition: MINRES is the minimal residual method for the symmetric case. Conjecture: Its residual norm is given by an explicit Chebyshev integral of the tridiagonal spectral spread, equality at two-point spectral clusters, and the conversion constant is one. (Chebyshev integral identity of MINRES)

**中文。** 定义：MINRES 指对称情形的极小残差法。猜想：其残差范数由三对角谱极差的显式切比雪夫积分给出,双点谱簇处等号且换算常数为一。（MINRES 切比雪夫积分恒等）
