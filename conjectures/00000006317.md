**English.** Definition: Total unimodularity means all minors are in the three-value set. Conjecture: The decomposition is composed of network and replication with explicit tree height, the kernel of the height being the Seymour depth. (Seymour tree height of total unimodularity)

**中文。** 定义：完全幺模指一切子式属三值集。猜想：分解由网络与复制合成且树高显式；且高的核为 Seymour 深度。（完全幺模 Seymour 树高）
