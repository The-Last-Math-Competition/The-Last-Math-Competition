**English.** Definition: The SFT potential is the generating function produced by touches and cylinders. Conjecture: The pole multiplicity spectrum of the potential is completely determined by the self-linking of the Reeb orbits, each pole multiplicity being equal to the factorial of the multiplicity of the corresponding orbit. (SFT pole multiplicity and orbits)

**中文。** 定义：SFT 势函数指由 touch 与柱面生成的生成函数。猜想：势函数的极点重数谱完全由 Reeb 轨道的自连接数决定,每个极点重数等于对应轨道的重数阶乘。（SFT 极点重数-轨道）
