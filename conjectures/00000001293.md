# 00000001293

**English.** Definition: The accelerated 3x+1 map g(x) = (3x+1)/2^{v}. Conjecture: The density of integers whose orbit converges to 1 is 1 with an explicit remainder O(2^{−√log x}) (remainder rate).

**中文。** 定义：3x+1 映射的加速 g(x) = (3x+1)/2^{v}。猜想：全轨收敛到 1 的整数密度为 1 之外的显式余项 O(2^{−√log x})(余项速率)。
