# 00000008211

**ORB-11 ★** — source: 259_李群表示与轨道方法.md

**English.** Definition: The monotonicity of Gelfand-Kirillov dimensions of representations: the index gk_mono of injectivity of GK dimension (transfer of the GK dimension of irreducible representations under subrepresentations). Conjecture: gk_mono holds (a GK injectivity law) and the GK dimension is an integer or half-integer (a half-integral lattice law); the groups realizing half-integrality are quadratic extension groups (a quadratic half-integral group law); and the equality family of the upper bound on GK dimension (GK at most dim G/2 for representations of algebraic groups) is the spherical representations (a spherical equality law), the classification of the family being the classification of spherical embeddings (a spherical classification correspondence). (GK injectivity half-integral spherical equality)

**中文。** 定义：表示的 Gelfand–Kirillov 维数的单径：GK 维数的单射性（既约表示的 GK 维数在子表示下的传递）的指标 gk_mono。猜想：gk_mono 成立（GK 单射律）且 GK 维数为整数或半整数（半整格律）；半整的实现的群为二次扩张群（二次半整群律）；GK 维数的上界（代数群的表示的 GK ≤ dim G/2）的等号族为球表示（球等号律），族的分类为球嵌入的分类（球分类对应）。
