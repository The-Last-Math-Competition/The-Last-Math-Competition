**English.** Definition: A sum-free set (A + A disjoint from A). Conjecture: The maximal sum-free subsets of F_p are exactly the intervals ((p+1)/3, 2(p−1)/3), uniquely up to dilation (the complete Diananda–Yap characterization over finite fields).
