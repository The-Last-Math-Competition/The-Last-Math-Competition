# 00000007920

**English.** Definition: The affine Grassmannian geometry of Steinberg representations: the combinatorial mirror Gamma_Sat of the Satake equivalence of perverse sheaves on the affine Grassmannian Gr_G. Conjecture: The entries of the transition matrix from the standard basis of the Grothendieck group of the Satake category to the spherical basis are Littlewood-Richardson numbers; the dimension of the kernel of the mod p reduction of the matrix (the Krull dimension of the p-torsion part) equals 0 on characteristic sheaves; and the endomorphism algebra of the Whip structure of the Steinberg sheaf is semisimple when p does not divide the order of the Weyl group (the semisimplicity threshold). (Satake LR matrix mod p kernel)

**中文。** 定义：Steinberg 表示的仿射 Grassmannian 几何：仿射 Grassmannian Gr_G 的 perverse 层的 Satake 等价的组合镜像 Γ_Sat。猜想：Satake 范畴的 Grothendieck 群的标准基到球面的过渡矩阵的元素为 Littlewood–Richardson 数；且矩阵的模 p 约化的核的维数为 p-挠部分的 Krull 维数在特征层上等于 0；Steinberg 层的 Whip 结构的自同态代数在 p 不整除 |Weyl 群| 时为半单（半单性阈值）。
