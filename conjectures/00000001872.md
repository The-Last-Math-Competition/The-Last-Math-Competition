# 00000001872

**English.** Definition: The Cherlin–Zilber conjecture states that infinite simple groups of finite Morley rank are algebraic groups. Conjecture: The classification of pseudolinear finite-Morley-rank groups is complete: every pseudolinear group is the purification of a centralizer of an algebraic group; the remaining closing case is the twisted exception of odd-type tori (no new classes).

**中文。** 定义：Cherlin–Zilber 猜想指有限 Morley 秩的无限单群为代数群。猜想：伪线性(pseudolinear)有限 Morley 秩群的分类完成:一切伪线性群为代数群的中心化子的纯化;剩余的收尾情形为奇类型 torus 的扭曲例外(无新类)。
