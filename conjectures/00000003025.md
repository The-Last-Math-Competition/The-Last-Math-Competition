# 00000003025

**English.** Definition: Many-body scattering: the transition structure from few-body to many-body. Conjecture: The cascade decomposition of quantum many-body scattering: the n-body S-matrix decomposes as the product correction of chained two-body scatterings, with the correction the connection phases of cascade vertices. (scattering cascade connection phases)

**中文。** 定义：多体散射：少体到多体的过渡结构。猜想：量子多体散射的级联分解：n 体 S 矩阵分解为链式二体散射的乘积修正且修正项为级联顶点的连接相位。（散射级联连接相位）
