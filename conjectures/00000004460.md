**SY3-10 △** — source: 134_辛几何III_镜像对称深化.md

**English.** Definition: The wrapped Fukaya category and the Weinstein skeleton are the category and its geometric skeleton. Conjecture: There exist two Weinstein manifolds with equivalent wrapped categories but non-homotopic skeletons, and the minimal dimension of the separation is 4. (minimal dimension 4 of the Fukaya-skeleton separation)

**中文。** 定义：包裹 Fukaya 范畴与 Weinstein 骨架指范畴与其几何骨架。猜想：存在两个 Weinstein 流形的包裹范畴等价而骨架不同伦,分离的最小维数为 4。（Fukaya-骨架分离最小维 4）
