# 00000002148

**English.** Definition: A (5,8)-cage is the smallest 5-regular graph of girth 8. Conjecture: Its order is 80; the construction is known, and the closure of the lower bound follows from a Moore-bound improvement for Brown-type graphs (lower bound 80).

**中文。** 定义：(5,8)-cage 指围长 8 的最小 5-正则图。猜想：其阶数为 80;构造已给出,下界的闭合由 Brown 型图的 Moore 界改进(下界 80)。
