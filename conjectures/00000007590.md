**English.** Definition: Confidence spectra and classical statistical spectra are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two distributions with the same confidence spectrum but different classical statistical spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit heavy-tail family with the same topology but different moments. (same topology-different moment separation)
