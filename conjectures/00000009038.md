**English.** Definition: The Iwaniec exponential sum bound. Conjecture: The subconvex bound for half-integral weight coefficients is 1/6 + epsilon; and the exponent wall is explicitly the vanishing of exponential sums. (Iwaniec subconvex 1/6 exponent wall)
