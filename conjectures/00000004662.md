**English.** Definition: The difference of the embedding dimension and the multiplicity is the difference of two local invariants. Conjecture: The difference takes all nonnegative integers, each value is realized by a monomial ring, and outside complete intersections the spectrum misses exactly one explicit value. (unique gap of the embedding-multiplicity difference spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：嵌入维数与重数之差指两个局部不变量的差。猜想：该差可取一切非负整数,每个值由单项式环实现,完备交之外的谱恰缺一个显式值。（嵌入重数差谱唯一缺口）
