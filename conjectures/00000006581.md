# 00000006581

**English.** Definition: The kernel transform is the categorical equivalence induced by Fourier-Mukai type kernel objects. Conjecture: The explicit spectrum of minimal kernel-representation dimensions is finite and the realization of minimality is generator reduction; the upper bound of the spectrum is the product of the dimensions of the two varieties; and the reduction step count is the logarithm of the spectrum. (generator reduction of minimal kernel dimensions)

**中文。** 定义：核变换：傅里叶–茂木型核对象诱导的范畴等价。猜想：核极小表示维数的显式谱有限且极小的实现为生成元约化；谱的上界为两簇维数之积；约化的步数为谱的对数。（核极小维数生成元约化）
