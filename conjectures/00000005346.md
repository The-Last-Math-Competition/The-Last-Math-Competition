**English.** Definition: Webs are the planar diagram structures of invariant tensors. Conjecture: The counting formula of five-point webs is an explicit combinatorial product, verified consistently at the pentagonal web; the growth of the count is Catalan type, with exponent the number of interior points; and the basis property of webs holds for standard boundary labels, with triangular basis change. (combinatorial product counting of five-point webs)

**中文。** 定义：蛛网：不变张量的平面图结构。猜想：五点网的计数公式为组合积显式且五边网处验证一致；计数的增长为 Catalan 型且指数为内点数；蛛网的基性质在边界标记为标准时成立且基变换为三角型。（五点蛛网组合积计数）
