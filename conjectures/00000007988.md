# 00000007988

**English.** Definition: The shape optimization of lattice quantization error: the stability of the minimizing configuration L_n of the normalized second moment of Voronoi cells of n-dimensional lattices. Conjecture: The Voronoi cell of the optimal lattice L_n is permutohedron type (equal Coxeter type) except for the degenerate exceptions n+1 in {2,3}; and the perturbation stability radius of optimality is of order n^{-1/2} (a positive-definite lower bound on the second variation); the infimum sequence of energy gaps (relative difference to the optimum) converges to 0 but with exponent -1 (not superlinear), and the limit point set of the gap spectrum is integral multiples of 1/n^2 (discreteness of the quantization spectrum). (Voronoi permutohedron optimality stability)

**中文。** 定义：格的量化误差的形状优化：n 维格的 Voronoi 胞腔的正规化二阶矩的最小化构型 L_n 的稳定性。猜想：最优格 L_n 的 Voronoi 胞腔为 permutohedron 型（等 COXETER 型）当 n+1 ∈ {2,3} 的超立方退化例外之外；且最优性的扰动稳定性半径为 n^{-1/2} 的量级（二阶变分的正定下界）；次优格的能隙（与最优的相对差）的下确界序列收敛到 0 但收敛指数为 -1（非超线性），能隙谱的极限点集为 1/n² 的整数倍（量子化谱的离散性）。
