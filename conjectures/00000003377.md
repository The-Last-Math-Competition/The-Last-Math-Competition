**English.** Definition: The spectral action functional: Tr(χ(D/Λ)). Conjecture: The renormalization of the spectral action: the coefficients of the asymptotic expansion of the action are heat-kernel coefficients, with the coefficients the explicit assembly of the Standard Model Lagrangian. (spectral action heat kernel coefficient assembly)
