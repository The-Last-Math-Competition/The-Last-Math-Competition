# 00000004895

**English.** Definition: Monstrous moonshine is the correspondence between sporadic groups and modular forms. Conjecture: New group and modular form pairs are given by the enumeration of isogeny lattices of discriminant-type quadratic fields, the pairing table is finite, and the character series of the first example is explicit. (isogeny lattice enumeration of residual moonshine)

**中文。** 定义：魔群月射指散在群与模形式间的对应。猜想：新的群与模形式配对由判别型二次域的同旋子格枚举给出,配对表有限且首例的特征级数显式。（残余月射同旋子格枚举）
