**English.** Definition: Partial fixed-point logic is the logic with backtrackable iteration. Conjecture: Its expressive domain on ordered structures coincides with polynomial space, and the smallest counterexample to coincidence with polynomial-time capture, if it exists, requires a nontrivial order structure and is explicit. (coincidence counterexample structure of partial fixed-point capture)

**中文。** 定义：偏不动点逻辑指迭代可回退的逻辑。猜想：其在带序结构上的表达域与多项式空间的等价保持,与多项式时间捕获重合的最小反例 (若存在) 需非平凡序结构且可显式。（偏不动点捕获重合反例结构）
