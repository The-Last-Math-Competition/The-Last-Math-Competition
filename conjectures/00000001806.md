# 00000001806

**English.** Definition: An Oka manifold satisfies holomorphic flexibility (all holomorphic maps from Stein manifolds into it are approximable and interpolable). Conjecture: The complement of an analytic subvariety of codimension ≥ 2 in C^n is Oka; the criterion is a Zariski-openness condition of full-measure-vanishing type (Oka-ness of the complement controlled by the dimension of the subvariety).

**中文。** 定义：Oka 流形指满足全纯弹性性质(一切源自 Stein 流形到它的全纯映射可逼近插值)的流形。猜想：C^n 中余维 ≥2 的解析子簇的补为 Oka;判据为簇的满维测度消没型的 Zariski 开性(补的 Oka 性由簇的维数条件控制)。
