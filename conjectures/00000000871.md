# 00000000871

**English.** Definition: The observability constant is the minimal control energy for the wave equation. Conjecture: On an interval, the control constant is given by an explicit infimum of a Fourier criterion and drops strictly at the critical time; and the slope of the drop is a function of the interval length. (control constant explicit drop)

**中文。** 定义：观控性常数为波浪方程的最小控制能量。猜想：区间上的控制常数由 Fourier 判据的显式下确界给出且在临界时刻处严格下降；且下降的斜率为区间长度函数。（控制常数显式下降）
