**RS2-12 ◆** — source: 162_组合的代数_表示稳定性.md

**English.** Definition: The limit shape is the continuous limit contour of random partitions. Conjecture: The shape conversion is given explicitly by the variational minimum of the logarithmic potential, is an explicit function under the classical measure, and the conversion is unique. (unique variational-minimum conversion of limit shapes)

**中文。** 定义：极限形状指随机分拆的连续极限轮廓。猜想：形状换算由对数势的变分极小显式给出,经典测度下为显式函数且换算唯一。（极限形状变分极小唯一换算）
