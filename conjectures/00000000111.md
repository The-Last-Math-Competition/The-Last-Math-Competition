**FA-06 ★** — source: 09_参数化猜想族.md

**English.** Conjecture: (Latin square family) For every prime p, let t_p be the infimum of the number of transversals of a Latin square of order p: conjecture t_p ≥ c^p, and that the limit lim t_p^{1/p} exists (see GR-14).

**中文。** 猜想：(拉丁方族)对每个素数 p,设 t_p 为 p 阶拉丁方横截个数的下确界:猜想 t_p ≥ c^p,且极限 lim t_p^{1/p} 存在(见 GR-14)。
