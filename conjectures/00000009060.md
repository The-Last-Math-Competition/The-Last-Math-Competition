**English.** Definition: Infinitesimal bendability: deformation stiffness of embeddings. Conjecture: The classification at low isotropic dimensions exists; and the threshold of the classification is codimension two; and the classification table is complete for codimension at most two. (infinitesimal bendability low-order classification)
