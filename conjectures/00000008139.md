# 00000008139

**English.** Definition: The Brown-Goodearl spectrum of noncommutative varieties: the continuity index bg(A) of the dimension function dim: Spec A to Z on the stratification of Spec A. Conjecture: The dim function is continuous on Spec (a BG continuity law) if and only if A is strongly Hopf invariant (a strong invariance criterion); the codimension of the jump set where continuity fails (dimension jumps) is at least 2 (a codimension-2 jump law); and the minimal example of the jump set is the quantum determinant complement of the quantum matrix algebra O_q(M_2) (a quantum determinant exception), the dimension jump of the exception being 1 (a single-jump rigidity). (BG continuity quantum determinant jump)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的 Brown–Goodearl 的谱空间：Spec A 的分层结构的维数函数 dim:Spec→Z 的连续性指标 bg(A)。猜想：dim 函数在 Spec 上连续（BG 连续律）当且仅当 A 为强 Hopf 不变（强不变判据）；且连续性的失效（维数跳跃）的跳跃集的余维数 ≥ 2（跳跃余 2 律）；跳跃集的极小例为量子矩阵代数 O_q(M₂) 的量子行列式补（量子行列式例外），例外的维数跳跃为 1（单跳刚性）。
