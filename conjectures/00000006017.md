# 00000006017

**HS2-07 ◆** — source: 186_哈密顿系统.md

**English.** Definition: Minimal sets are the minimal orbit sets of the action functional, with their quantification by the mean action. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit functional of convex conjugates, the Cantor value difference is exponentially small; the exponent of the difference is the Diophantine exponent of the rotation number; and the subdifferential of the convex conjugate is single-valued off the Cantor set, the single-valued domain open and dense. (exponentially small Cantor-difference conversion of convex conjugates)

**中文。** 定义：极小集：作用泛函的极小轨道集；其量化（平均作用）。猜想：换算常数为凸共轭的显式泛函且康托值差为指数小；差的指数为旋转数的丢番图指数；凸共轭的次微分在康托集的余集上单值且单值域为开稠密。（凸共轭康托差指数小换算）
