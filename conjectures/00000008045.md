# 00000008045

**OMN-25 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The tame regularity of definable Monge-Kantorovich transport: the definable regularity class OT_def of the optimal transport map T_c for definable costs c. Conjecture: T_c is a definable map and, under the Ma-Trudinger-Wang condition (A3) on the cost, a definable diffeomorphism; the regularity threshold of solutions of the Monge-Ampere obstacle problem for definable transport agrees with the cell depth of truncation; and the proof obstruction for the tamed codimension at least 2 of the singular set of the transport map (a sparsity law for singularities) is the Diophantine approximation of second derivatives of definable Alexandrov solutions (the approximation constant being the supremum of the partial quotients of the continued fraction of cell parameters). (definable transport MTW cell regularity)

**中文。** 定义：可定义传输的 Monge-Kantorovich 的驯正则：可定义代价 c 的最优传输映射 T_c 的可定义正则类 OT_def。猜想：T_c 为可定义映射且在代价的 Ma-Trudinger-Wang 条件（A3）下为可定义微分同胚；可定义传输的 Monge–Ampère 障碍问题的解的正则性阈值与截断（truncation）的 cell 深度一致；且传输映射的奇异集的余维数 ≥ 2 的驯化（奇点稀疏律）的证明障碍为可定义 Alexandrov 解的二阶导数的 Diophantine 逼近（逼近常数为 cell 参数的连分数的部分商的上确界）。
