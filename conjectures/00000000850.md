# 00000000850

**English.** Definition: The Alt–Caffarelli problem is an energy minimization with gradient jumps. Conjecture: The set of flat points of the free boundary is dense and the degenerate set has measure zero; and the completed structure theorem covers all dimensions. (Alt-Caffarelli free boundary structure)

**中文。** 定义：Alt–Caffarelli 问题为含梯度跳跃的能量极小。猜想：自由边界的平坦点集稠密且退化点集为零测；且结构定理的完整化覆盖全部维数。（AC 自由边界结构）
