**SQ-04 ◆** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: The Ulam sequence U as in SQ-03, with counting function A(x) = #(U ∩ [1,x]). Conjecture: A(x) = o(x) and A(x) ~ c·x/log x with c = 13/4; that is, the Ulam numbers are of the same order as the primes and have zero density.

**中文。** 定义：Ulam 序列 U 如 SQ-03,其计数函数 A(x)=#(U∩[1,x])。猜想：A(x) = o(x) 且 A(x) ~ c·x/log x,c = 13/4;即 Ulam 数与素数同阶且密度为零。
