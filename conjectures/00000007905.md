# 00000007905

**ATC-05 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: Spectral gluing of meromorphic automorphic forms: the pole spectrum P_phi of the scattering matrix phi(s) of the residual spectrum (Eisenstein residue) of noncuspidal forms. Conjecture: P_phi is a discrete set whose imaginary parts have a uniform limiting distribution (no high condensation points); the pole count N(T) = #{beta in P_phi : Im beta <= T} satisfies N(T) ~ (d^2/2 pi) T log T with d the rank of the group, with second term -d(d-1)/4 T; and no pole has Re beta > 1/2 (absolute convergence rigidity of the residual spectrum). (scattering pole counting law)

**中文。** 定义：亚纯自守形式的谱粘合：noncuspidal 的残余谱（Eisenstein 残差）的散射矩阵 φ(s) 的极点谱 P_φ。猜想：P_φ 为离散集且其虚部的极限分布均匀（无高凝聚点）；极点计数 N(T)={β∈P_φ: Im β≤T} 满足 N(T) ~ (d²/2π)T log T（d 为群秩），次主项为 −d(d−1)/4·T；且不存在 Re β > 1/2 的极点（残余谱的绝对收敛刚性）。
