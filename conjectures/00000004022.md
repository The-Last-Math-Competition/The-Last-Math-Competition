# 00000004022

**English.** Definition: The signature of a flow is the signature embedding of flow data over time windows. Conjecture: The heavy-tail stabilization of increment signatures holds if and only if the uniform second moment of the increment distribution is bounded, with the threshold 2 optimal and not lowerable. (heavy-tail stabilization threshold of signatures)

**中文。** 定义：流的签名指流数据按时间窗的签名嵌入。猜想：增量签名的重尾稳定当且仅当增量分布的一致二阶矩有界,阈值 2 最优且不可降低。（签名重尾稳定阈值）
