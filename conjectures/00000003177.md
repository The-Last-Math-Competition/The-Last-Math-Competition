# 00000003177

**English.** Definition: The Johnson–Lindenstrauss lemma: distance-preserving random projections. Conjecture: The dimension rigidity of JL: the dimension lower bound for preserving n point distances is log n over ε² plus a logarithmic correction, with the necessity of the correction by the volume argument. (JL dimension lower bound volume argument)

**中文。** 定义：Johnson–Lindenstrauss 引理：距离保持的随机投影。猜想：JL 引理的维数刚性：保持 n 点距离的维数下界为 log n 除以 ε² 加对数修正且修正的必要为体积论证。（JL 维数下界体积论证）
