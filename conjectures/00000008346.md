# 00000008346

**English.** Definition: The boundary of the Gelfond-Schneider domain: the strengthening range of algebraic independence for expressions alpha^beta. Conjecture: The algebraic independence strengthening for algebraic bases and algebraic exponents holds when the transcendence degree is at least two; and the boundary is closed under the value groups of the Gelfond-Schneider theorem. (Gelfond-Schneider boundary algebraic independence strengthening)

**中文。** 定义：Gelfond–Schneider 域的边界指 α^β 型代数无关性的强化范围。猜想：代数底与代数指数的代数无关强化在超越次数至少二时成立；且边界由 Gelfond–Schneider 定理的值组封闭。（G-S 域边界代数无关强化）
