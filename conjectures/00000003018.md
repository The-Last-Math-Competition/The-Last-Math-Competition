# 00000003018

**English.** Definition: Quantum belief propagation (QBP): message passing for quantum spin glasses. Conjecture: The convergence domain of QBP: message iteration converges if and only if the loop spectral radius is below one, with the quantum correction of the spectral radius being the coherence term of off-diagonal elements. (QBP loop spectrum coherence correction)

**中文。** 定义：量子置信传播（QBP）：量子自旋玻璃的消息传递。猜想：QBP 的收敛域：消息迭代的收敛当且仅当循环谱半径小于一且谱半径的量子修正为非对角元的相干项。（QBP 循环谱相干修正）
