**English.** Definition: The weak formulation of PDEs with the PDE side. Conjecture: The weak solutions of the equation exist, the existence being abstract calculus. (abstract-calculus existence of weak solutions)

**中文。** 定义：偏微的弱 formulation：PDE 侧。猜想：弱形式的 PDE：方程的弱解且解的存在为抽象演算。（偏微弱形式抽象演算方程弱解存在组合弱解存在抽象演算演算方程）
