**English.** Definition: 2k order moment M_{2k}=lim Σ_{p≤x} a_p^{2k}/π(x) of Frobenius trace a_p of moment spectrum: cusp expression f of Sato–Tate distribution. Conjecture: M_{2k} is 2k moment (Catalan type integer C_k=(2k)!/k!(k+1)!); of characteristic x of Sato–Tate group SU(2) and scaling limit of error term O(x^{-1/2+ε}) quadratic fluctuation completion: error is covariance by of Gaussian field; the in 2k=2 of gives of symmetric power L function in center point by distribution (Keating–Snaith type) completely characterizes of log L(1/2,sym² f).
