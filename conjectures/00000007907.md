# 00000007907

**English.** Definition: The moment spectrum of the Sato-Tate distribution: the 2k-th moment M_{2k} = lim sum_{p<=x} a_p^{2k}/pi(x) of the Frobenius traces a_p of a cusp form f. Conjecture: M_{2k} is the 2k-th moment of the character x of the Sato-Tate group SU(2) (the Catalan-type integer C_k = (2k)!/k!(k+1)!); and the error term O(x^{-1/2+epsilon}) improves to a complete quadratic fluctuation: the scaled limit of the error is a Gaussian field with covariance given by central values of symmetric power L-functions; for 2k = 2 the field is completely characterized by the distribution of log L(1/2, sym^2 f) (Keating-Snaith type). (Sato-Tate Catalan moments and Gaussian field)

**中文。** 定义：Sato–Tate 分布的矩谱：尖点形式 f 的 Frobenius 迹 a_p 的 2k 阶矩 M_{2k}=lim Σ_{p≤x} a_p^{2k}/π(x)。猜想：M_{2k} 为 Sato–Tate 群 SU(2) 的特征 x 的 2k 矩（Catalan 型整数 C_k=(2k)!/k!(k+1)!）；且误差项 O(x^{-1/2+ε}) 可改进至二次波动完备化：误差的缩放极限为协方差由对称幂 L 函数在中心点的值给出的高斯场；该场在 2k=2 的情形由 log L(1/2,sym² f) 的分布（ Keating–Snaith 型）完全刻画。
