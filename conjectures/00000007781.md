**English.** Definition: isotropy constant L_K of convex body K⊂R^n by quantity definition: K isotropy of volume normalization denotes ∫_K x x^T dx = L_K² |K| I_n. Bourgain tangent conjecture sup_K L_n bounded. Conjecture: optimal upper bound of L_n is (√2/π)e^{1/2} (by asymptotic attains), namely tangent constant in family attains extreme value; and extreme value body in Hausdorff limit under unique of all dimension.

**中文。** 定义：凸体 K⊂R^n 的迷向常数 L_K 由体积规范化的惯性张量定义：K 迷向指 ∫_K x x^T dx = L_K² |K| I_n。Bourgain 切片猜想断言 sup_K L_n 有界。猜想：L_n 的最优上界为 (√2/π)e^{1/2}（由单纯形渐近取得），即切片常数在单纯形族达到极值；且一切维数的极值体在 Hausdorff 极限意义下唯一。
