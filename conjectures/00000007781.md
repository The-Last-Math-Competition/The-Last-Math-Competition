# 00000007781

**ACG-01 ◆** — source: 245_渐近凸几何.md

**English.** Definition: The isotropic constant L_K of a convex body K in R^n is defined by the volume-normalized inertia tensor with K isotropic when int_K x x^T dx = L_K^2 |K| I_n; the Bourgain slicing conjecture asserts sup_K L_n is bounded. Conjecture: The optimal upper bound of L_n is (sqrt 2 / pi) e^{1/2}, attained asymptotically by the simplex, so the slicing constant is extremal at the simplex family; and the extremal body is unique in the Hausdorff-limit sense in every dimension. (simplex-optimal isotropic constant)

**中文。** 定义：凸体 K⊂R^n 的迷向常数 L_K 由体积规范化的惯性张量定义：K 迷向指 ∫_K x x^T dx = L_K² |K| I_n。Bourgain 切片猜想断言 sup_K L_n 有界。猜想：L_n 的最优上界为 (√2/π)e^{1/2}（由单纯形渐近取得），即切片常数在单纯形族达到极值；且一切维数的极值体在 Hausdorff 极限意义下唯一。
