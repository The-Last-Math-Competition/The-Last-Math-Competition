# 00000005445

**CN2-05 ★** — source: 167_计算数论_算法复杂性.md

**English.** Definition: The extended Euclidean algorithm computes the gcd with coefficients. Conjecture: The mean of the bit-length distribution of minimal coefficients is an explicit constant times the bit length, the variance is given by the distribution of partial quotients, and the formula is asymptotically exact. (bit-length distribution formula of Bezout coefficients)

**中文。** 定义：扩展欧几里得指带系数的最大公因子算法。猜想：极小系数的位长分布均值为显式常数乘位长,方差由部分商分布给出且公式渐近精确。（贝祖系数位长分布公式）
