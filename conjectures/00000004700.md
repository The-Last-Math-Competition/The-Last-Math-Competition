# 00000004700

**English.** Definition: Entropy isomorphism and the spectrum are the two classes of invariants of a system. Conjecture: There exist two systems with identical entropy-dimension data but non-isomorphic spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit pairing of Sturmian and Toeplitz systems. (explicit pair of entropy dimension-spectrum separation)

**中文。** 定义：熵同构与谱指系统的两类不变量。猜想：存在两系统的全部熵维数据相同而谱不同构,分离由 Sturmian 与 Toeplitz 的显式配对实现。（熵维-谱分离显式对）
