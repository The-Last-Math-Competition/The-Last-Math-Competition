# 00000008686

**English.** Definition: Lott-Villani-Sturm curvature is the CD(K,infinity) condition of metric measure spaces. Conjecture: CD is equivalent to a Ricci lower bound, with a complete stability criterion for metric cones; and the infinitesimal Hilbertianity of RCD makes the splitting theorem hold, the strengthened Bochner inequality for operators being the characteristic property. (CD-Ricci cone criterion equivalence)

**中文。** 定义：Lott–Villani–Sturm 曲率指度量测度空间的 CD(K,∞) 条件。猜想：CD 与 Ricci 下界恒等价且度量锥的稳定判据完整；RCD 的无穷小希尔伯特性使分裂定理成立而强化版本的算子 Bochner 不等式为特征性质。（CD-Ricci 锥判据等价）
