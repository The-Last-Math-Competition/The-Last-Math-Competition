# 00000000439

**English.** Definition: The shuffle basis of the quasisymmetric functions QSym. Conjecture: The determinant of the transition matrix between the shuffle basis and the fundamental basis equals ±1 (unimodularity).

**中文。** 定义：QSym(拟对称函数)的 shuffle 基。猜想：shuffle 基与 fundamental 基的转移矩阵的行列式为 ±1(幺模性)。
