# 00000002307

**FR-17 ★** — source: 62_有限群论II.md

**English.** Definition: The Fitting height and derived length are two invariants of solvable groups. Conjecture: Fitting height ≤ 2·(derived length) − 1 (a linear bound); the bound is controlled by layerwise projections of supersolvable towers; tightness is attained by extremal examples with total derived chain 2.

**中文。** 定义：Fitting 高与导群长指可解群的两种不变量。猜想：Fitting 高 ≤2·导群长-1(线性界);界由超可解塔的逐层投影控制;紧性由全导链为 2 的极端例子。
