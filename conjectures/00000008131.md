# 00000008131

**NAG-21 ★** — source: 256_非交换代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The variation of Donaldson-Thomas type invariants of noncommutative varieties: the integrality int_ncDT of wall-crossing of nc DT invariants. Conjecture: The wall-crossing corrections of nc DT are combinations of integer invariants (a noncommutative version of KS-type integrality); the correction term of the Behrend function weights of the generating series of invariants agrees with the weights of the central variety (a Behrend-central agreement); and the worst counterexamples to integrality (non-integer walls) occur in dimension at least 4 (a dimension-4 integrality law), 4-dimensional counterexamples being given by noncommutative ultra-varieties (a supersymmetric exception). (nc DT wall Behrend integrality)

**中文。** 定义：非交换簇的 Donaldson–Thomas 型不变量的变异：nc DT 的墙穿越的整数性 int_ncDT。猜想：nc DT 的墙穿越的修正为整数不变量的组合（KS 型整数性非交换版）；且不变量的生成级数的 Behrend 函数权重的修正项的中心簇的权一致（Behrend-中心一致）；整数性的最坏反例（非整数墙）的维数 ≥ 4（4 维以上整数律），4 维反例由非交换 ultra-簇（超对称例外）给出。
