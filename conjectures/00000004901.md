# 00000004901

**MD3-01 ★** — source: 149_随机过程III_马氏决策.md

**English.** Definition: Policy switching refers to the change points of the optimal deterministic policy on the time axis. Conjecture: The total number of switches of the optimal policy of a finite MDP is at most an explicit linear expression in the number of states and the horizon, attained by random-walk-type transitions and optimal. (linear upper bound of optimal policy switches)

**中文。** 定义：策略切换指最优确定策略在时间轴上的改变点。猜想：有限 MDP 最优策略的切换总次数不超过状态数与时域的显式线性式,界由随机游走型转移达到且最优。（最优策略切换线性上界）
