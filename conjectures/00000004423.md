**English.** Definition: The amplitude function of ASEP is the integrand of its exact distribution formula. Conjecture: The crossing count of the critical points of asymptotic steepest descent always differs by one on the two sides of the phase transition line, and the jump occurs on an explicit algebraic parameter curve. (ASEP crossing-count jump curve)

**中文。** 定义：ASEP 的振幅函数指其精确分布公式的被积函数。猜想：渐近最快下降临界点的交叉计数在相变线两侧恒相差一,跳变发生在显式代数参数曲线上。（ASEP 交叉计数跳变曲线）
