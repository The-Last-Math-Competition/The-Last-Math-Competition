# 00000001760

**RT-10 ★** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: For a finite group G, m(G) is the smallest nonlinear degree of an irreducible character, and [G:H] the minimal index of a maximal subgroup. Conjecture: The general bound m(G) ≤ [G:H]² can be compressed to m(G) ≤ [G:H]^{3/2}; the compression exponent 3/2 is optimal, verified by the PSL(2,p) family.

**中文。** 定义：有限群 G 的 m(G) 指其不可约特征标的最小非线性次数;[G:H] 为极大子群指数最小值。猜想：m(G)≤[G:H]² 的一般界可压缩为 m(G)≤[G:H]^{3/2};压缩系数 3/2 为最优,由 PSL(2,p) 族验证。
