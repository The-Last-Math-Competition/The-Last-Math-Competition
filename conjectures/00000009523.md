# 00000009523

**English.** Definition: The entanglement-assisted classical capacity C_E(phi) is given by the Bennett-Shor-Smolin-Thapliyal formula C_E(phi) = max_rho I(rho,phi), where I(rho,phi) = S(rho) + S(phi(rho)) - S((id tensor phi)(purification)) is the quantum mutual information. Conjecture: Except for the completely depolarizing constant channel, C_E(phi) > chi(phi) strictly for every channel; and the maximal ratio C_E/chi over d-dimensional channels is d, attained by trace-halving channels, with the attaining channel unique up to joint unitary equivalence. (entanglement-assisted mutual information formula)

**中文。** 定义：纠缠辅助经典容量 C_E(φ) 由 Bennett–Shor–Smolin–Thapliyal 公式 C_E(φ)=max_ρ I(ρ,φ) 给出，I(ρ,φ)=S(ρ)+S(φ(ρ))−S((id⊗φ)(纯化)) 为量子互信息。猜想：除完全去极化常数信道外 C_E(φ)>χ(φ) 对一切信道严格成立，且比值 C_E/χ 在 d 维信道上的最大值为 d，由迹半衰信道取得；取得最大值的信道在模联合酉等价下唯一。（纠缠辅助互信息公式）
