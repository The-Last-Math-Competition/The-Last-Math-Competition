**GZ-04 ★** — source: 70_几何测度论II.md

**English.** Definition: Uniformly rectifiable sets carry David–Semmes regularity. Conjecture: Uniform rectifiability is completely characterized by the quantitative criterion of Big Pieces of Lipschitz Images; the three classical conditions (β-numbers, Coronization decompositions, boundedness of singular integrals) are pairwise equivalent with explicitly related constants. (uniform rectifiability three criteria equivalent)

**中文。** 定义：一致可校正集（uniformly rectifiable）：带 David–Semmes 正则性的可校正集。猜想：一致可校正性由 Big Pieces of Lipschitz 图像的量化判据完全刻画；判据的三个经典条件（β 数、Coronized 分解、奇异积分有界）两两等价且等价常数显式相关。（一致可校正三判据等价）
