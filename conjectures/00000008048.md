# 00000008048

**OMN-28 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The taming of pregeometries (an o-minimal realization of combinatorial geometry): the definability index MG(cl) of the matroid of a commutative pregeometry cl. Conjecture: The pregeometry generated by the algebraic closure operator acl of an o-minimal structure is commutative (exchange plus locality); and the minimal structure where commutativity fails (noncommutativity of genuine matroids) is the hyper-exponential structure (a tower structure of double exponentials, R_exp,exp); the GF(2) representation of the matroid of the double-exponential structure is a finite list of classical binary matroids (the tamed exclusion of the Vamos matroid, the exclusion principle being tower-height control of the Mac Lane-Steinitz exchange law). (pregeometry Vamos tamed exclusion)

**中文。** 定义：预几何的驯化（combinatorial geometry 的 o-极小实现）：交换预几何 cl 的 matroid 的可定义性指标 MG(cl)。猜想：o-极小结构的代数闭包算子 acl 生成的预几何为交换（exchange+locality）；且交换性的失效（真 matroid 的非交换性）的最小结构为超指数结构（双指数函数的塔结构 R_exp,exp）；双指数结构的 matroid 的循环空间的 GF(2) 表现为经典二元拟阵的有限列表（Vámos 拟阵的驯化排除，排除原理为 Mac Lane–Steinitz 交换律的塔高控制）。
