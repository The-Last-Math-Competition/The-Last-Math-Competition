# 00000001043

**English.** Definition: The Carlitz rank is the number of AB-expression layers. Conjecture: The permutation rank over prime fields is at most four; and the combination of four is the norm-basis decomposition. (Carlitz rank four norm-basis decomposition)

**中文。** 定义：Carlitz 秩为 AB 表达层数。猜想：素域的置换秩不超过四；且四的组合为范数基分解。（Carlitz 秩四范数基分解）
