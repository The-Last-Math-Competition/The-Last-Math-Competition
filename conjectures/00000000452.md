**English.** Definition: Narayana numbers N(n,k) refine the Catalan count. Conjecture: The Narayana triangle is log-concave along antidiagonals; and the full unimodal network in both directions is 4-connected. (Narayana antidiagonal concavity)

**中文。** 定义：Narayana 数 N(n,k) 为 Catalan 的 refined 计数。猜想：Narayana 三角沿反对角线的序列为对数凹；且双重方向的完全单峰网络在四连通。（Narayana 反对角凹）
