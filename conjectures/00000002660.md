# 00000002660

**English.** Definition: Cheeger–Colding: limits under lower Ricci bounds. Conjecture: Regularity of limits: the limit structure is a metric cone, with rigidity to the volume cone; the metric-cone limit is unique and realized by torus-equivariant contraction. (Cheeger-Colding metric cone limit regularity)

**中文。** 定义：Cheeger–Colding：Ricci 下界的极限。猜想：极限的正则：度量锥的极限结构且构的刚体积锥。(Cheeger-Colding 度量锥极限正则刚体积锥 Ricci 下界极限结构)；且度量锥极限唯一且以环面等变收缩收敛实现。
