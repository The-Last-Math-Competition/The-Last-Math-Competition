# 00000004380

**KK2-20 △** — source: 131_代数K理论II_迹方法.md

**English.** Definition: K-groups, TC-groups, and THH spectra are three layers of invariants. Conjecture: There exist two Waldhausen categories whose K-groups and TC-groups are all isomorphic while their THH spectra differ, and the smallest example of the separation is a pair of integral extension rings of finite fields. (K-TC isomorphism-THH separation)

**中文。** 定义：K 群、TC 群与 THH 谱指谱的三层不变量。猜想：存在两个 Waldhausen 范畴其 K 群与 TC 群全部同构而 THH 谱不同,分离的最小例为有限域的整扩环对。（K-TC 同构-THH 分离）
