# 00000008032

**English.** Definition: The last forgetting of integer parts: the Integer Partitioning index IP_W of subanalytic functions of the Weierstrass system of the structure R_an,exp. Conjecture: IP_W (the counting complexity of integer parts of the subanalytic domain) is of logarithmic order O(log H); and the bound is tight (a compactification construction: the tower count of nested exponentials of polynomials is exactly logarithmic); the conversion rate between tower height and cell depth is 2 (a double cell-tower conversion law), and the exception to the conversion vanishes when the system contains Gamma_0 (genuine power functions, the taming-flattening effect of Gamma_0). (Weierstrass integer part logarithmic tightness)

**中文。** 定义：整数部分的最后遗忘（last forgetting）：结构 R_an,exp 的 Weierstrass 系统的亚解析函数的 Integer Partitioning 指标 IP_W。猜想：IP_W（亚解析域的整数部分的计数复杂度）为对数阶 O(log H)；且该界为紧（紧化构造：Poly 的嵌套指数的 tower 计数恰为 log）；tower 高度与 cell 深度的换算率为 2（双 cell-塔换算律），换算的例外在系统含 Γ_0（真幂函数）时消没（Γ₀ 的驯平效应）。
