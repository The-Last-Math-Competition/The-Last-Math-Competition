# 00000008730

**English.** Definition: The Lieb-Robinson bound is the upper limit on the speed of information propagation. Conjecture: Entanglement propagation in the thermal phase always has a linear light cone; the MBL phase has a logarithmic light cone with the transition region sublinear; and the anisotropy of high-dimensional cones is given by the nearest-neighbor structure of the lattice. (Lieb-Robinson linear-logarithmic light cone dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：Lieb–Robinson 约束指信息传播的速度上限。猜想：热相的纠缠传播恒为线性光锥；MBL 相为对数光锥且过渡区为次线性锥，高维锥的各向异性由格的最近邻结构给出。（LR 光锥线性-对数二分）
