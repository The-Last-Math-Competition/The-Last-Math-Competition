# 00000004520

**English.** Definition: First- and second-order asymptotics are the first two layers of the expansion of the optimal value. Conjecture: There exist two random Euclidean functionals with the same leading coefficient but different logarithmic powers, and the separation is realized explicitly by MST families with different weight tails. (same leading term-log-power separation)
