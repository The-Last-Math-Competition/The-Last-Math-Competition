# 00000006004

**English.** Definition: A contraction is the exceptional orbit of a birational morphism, with the classification of Mori contractions. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit pair of center dimension with exceptional type, projective bundles at the lowest type; the discrimination of contractions is the enumeration of four pairs (dim center, dim exceptional); and the example of each pair is unique up to deformation, with finitely many deformation classes. (center-exceptional pair conversion of contractions)

**中文。** 定义：收缩：双有理态射的例外轨道；Mori 收缩的分类。猜想：换算公式为中心维数与例外型的显式对且最低型时为射影束；收缩的判别为 (dim 中心, dim 例外) 的四对枚举；每对的例子唯一至形变且形变类的个数为有限。（收缩中心例外型对换算）
