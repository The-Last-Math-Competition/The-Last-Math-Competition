# 00000001849

**RM-09 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The singular-value edge of a product of k independent Wishart matrices. Conjecture: The recursion constants of the Meijer-G closed form for k ≥ 4 satisfy an explicit three-term recursion (from the pole residues of the Mellin transform); the constants can be written down step by step; and the three-term recursion coefficients are written out degree-by-degree from the Mellin-pole residues.

**中文。** 定义：k 个独立 Wishart 矩阵乘积的奇异值边缘。猜想：k≥4 的 Meijer-G 闭式的递推常数满足显式三项递推关系(由 Mellin 变换的极点残差);常数可逐次写出；且三项递推的系数由 Mellin 极点的留数逐阶写出。
