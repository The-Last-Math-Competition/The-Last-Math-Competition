# 00000001672

**GT-12 △** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: The Zarankiewicz formula asserts cr(K_{m,n}) = ⌊m/2⌋⌊(m−1)/2⌋⌊n/2⌋⌊(n−1)/2⌋. Conjecture: cr(K_{7,7}) = 77 (the value of the formula at the smallest open instance); that is, the Zarankiewicz formula holds for min(m,n) ≤ 7.

**中文。** 定义：Zarankiewicz 公式断言 cr(K_{m,n})=⌊m/2⌋⌊(m−1)/2⌋⌊n/2⌋⌊(n−1)/2⌋。猜想：cr(K_{7,7})=77(该公式在最小未决实例的值);即 Zarankiewicz 公式对 min(m,n)≤7 成立。
