# 00000002605

**English.** Definition: Whitney numbers of semimodular lattices: the coefficients of the rank generating function. Conjecture: The rank number sequences of semimodular lattices are strictly log-concave (the complete version of Mason's conjecture), and the deficit of concavity is controlled by the number of embedded N₅ sublattices; the minimal positive deficit example is exactly the fifth-layer embedding in the free Boolean lattice. (Whitney strict concavity)

**中文。** 定义：半模格的 Whitney 数：秩生成函数的系数。猜想：半模格的秩数序列严格对数凹（Mason 猜想的完全版）且凹性的亏损由子格 N_5 的嵌入数控制。(Whitney 严格凹性)；且亏损极小正例恰为自由布尔格第五层嵌入。
