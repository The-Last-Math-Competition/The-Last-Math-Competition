# 00000001979

**English.** Definition: Gribov copies are multiple solutions of gauge fixing. Conjecture: The number of Gribov copies of SU(2) on T³ is an explicit Euler-characteristic combination: N = ∏(sin(πn/3)/sin(πm/3)) type closed form; the count is given by the Atiyah–Bott fixed-point formula.

**中文。** 定义：Gribov 拷贝指规范固定的多重解。猜想：SU(2) 在 T³ 上的 Gribov 拷贝数为 Euler 特征数的显式组合:N = ∏(sin(πn/3)/sin(πm/3)) 型闭式;计数由 Atiyah–Bott 的不动点公式给出。
