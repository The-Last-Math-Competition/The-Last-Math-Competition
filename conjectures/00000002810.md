# 00000002810

**English.** Definition: Ziv–Merhav large deviations: deviations of compression complexity. Conjecture: The deviation rate of compression lengths: the large deviations of the redundancy of universal compression follow a logarithmically corrected Poisson-type law, with correction parameter the growth rate of the dictionary tree. (Ziv-Merhav deviation law)

**中文。** 定义：Ziv–Merhav 大偏差：压缩复杂度的偏差。猜想：压缩长度的偏差速率：通用压缩的冗余的大偏差为对数修正的 Poisson 型律且修正参数为字典树的增长率。（Ziv-Merhav 偏差律）
