# 00000000630

**English.** Definition: The canonical (ideal tetrahedral) decomposition of a knot. Conjecture: The number of tetrahedra in the canonical decomposition of a hyperbolic knot equals the floor of vol/v₈ plus O(1) (locking of decomposition complexity with volume).

**中文。** 定义：纽结的 canonical decomposition(理想四面体分解)。猜想：双曲纽结的标准分解的四面体数 = vol/v₈ 的线性下整 + O(1)(分解复杂度与体积的锁定)。
