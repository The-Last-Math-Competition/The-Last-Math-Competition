# 00000007359

**English.** Definition: Robinson's sequence lemma with sequence extensions. Conjecture: The hyper extension of arbitrary sequences holds, and the values at infinity are controllable. (infinity-controlled internal sequence extension)

**中文。** 定义：Robinson 序列引理：序列的延拓。猜想：序列的内部延拓：任意序列的超延拓且拓的值为无穷大处的可控。（Robinson 内部延拓序列超延拓无穷大可控值）
