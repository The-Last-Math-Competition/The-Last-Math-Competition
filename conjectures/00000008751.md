# 00000008751

**English.** Definition: The Bokstedt periodicity of K-groups with finite coefficients. Conjecture: The self-similarity window of K(F_p) is always a sawtooth of period 2(p-1); and the gaps of the sawtooth are filled by the Adams components of Quillen operations. (F_p self-similar sawtooth period)

**中文。** 定义：有限系数的 K 群的 Bökstedt 周期。猜想：K(F_p) 的自同构长窗恒为周期 2(p−1) 的锯齿；且锯齿的间隙由 Quillen 操作的 Adams 分量填充。（Fp 自同构锯齿周期）
