# 00000009791

**DDG-01 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Discrete Gaussian curvature of polyhedral surfaces: the angle deficit K_v = 2 pi minus the sum of interior angles at a vertex; the convergence of discrete to smooth curvature. Conjecture: Under mesh size h to 0 with uniformly bounded triangle quality, angle deficits divided by mixed areas converge to smooth Gaussian curvature with error O(h); the error constant is explicitly given by the circumradius-to-inradius ratio of triangles, and the ratio condition cannot be removed. (angle deficit convergence order)

**中文。** 定义：多面体曲面的离散高斯曲率：顶点处的角度亏损 K_v=2π−Σ内角；离散曲率与光滑曲率的收敛关系。猜想：在网格尺寸 h→0 且三角形质量一致有界下，角度亏损除以混合面积收敛到光滑高斯曲率且误差为 O(h)；误差常数由三角形外接圆与内切圆半径比显式给出且比率条件不可去。（角度亏损收敛阶）
