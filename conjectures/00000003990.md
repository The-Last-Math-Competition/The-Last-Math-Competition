# 00000003990

**PC2-20 △** — source: 118_证明复杂性II.md

**English.** Definition: The depth spectrum is the growth order of the shortest proof depth as formula size grows. Conjecture: There exists a formula family whose proof lengths are asymptotically equal while the depth spectra separate into Theta(log n) and Theta(1), and both spectra are simultaneously realized between resolution and extended Frege. (length-depth spectrum double separation)

**中文。** 定义：深度谱指最短证明深度随公式规模的增长阶。猜想：存在公式族其证明长度渐近相同而深度谱分离为 Θ(log n) 与 Θ(1),且两种谱在消解与 extended Frege 之间同时实现。（长度-深度谱双分离）
