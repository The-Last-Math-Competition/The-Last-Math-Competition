**English.** Definition: The connected components of tilting complexes are the equivalence classes of tilting objects within a derived equivalence class. Conjecture: The rank of the maximal automorphism torus of each connected component equals the defect of the algebra, and the order of its commutative part is an explicit polynomial in the defect. (tilting torus rank and defect)

**中文。** 定义：倾斜复形的连通分量指导出等价类中倾斜对象的等价类。猜想：每个连通分量的自同构极大环面秩等于代数的 defect,可交换部分的阶为 defect 的显式多项式。（倾斜环面秩-defect）
