# 00000008741

**English.** Definition: Quillen K-groups: the homotopy groups K_i(R) = pi_i(BGL(R)^+). Conjecture: The rank of K_i(Z) is 1 when i is congruent to 1 mod 4 and zero otherwise (the Borel law); there is a complete criterion for nontriviality of the 2-torsion of K_i(Z) when i is congruent to 3 mod 4 (a torsion criterion law); K_i(F_q) is Z at i = 0, cyclic at odd positive i, and zero at positive even i (a cyclicity law); and there is an explicit formula for high ranks over rings of integers (an explicitness law). (Borel rank torsion cyclicity explicit)

**中文。** 定义：Quillen K-群：K_i(R)=π_i(BGL(R)^+) 的同伦群。猜想：K_i(Z) 的秩当 i≡1 mod 4 时为 1、其余为零（Borel 律）；且 K_i(Z) 的 2-挠部分在 i≡3 mod 4 时非平凡的完整判定（挠判定律）；K_i(F_q) 为 i=0 时 Z、i 奇时循环群、i 正偶时为零（循环律）；数环的整数环的高秩的显式公式（显式律）。
