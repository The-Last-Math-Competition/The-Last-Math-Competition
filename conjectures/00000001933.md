# 00000001933

**English.** Definition: Superrigidity means representations of lattices into higher-rank groups are given by algebraic-group structure. Conjecture: All representations of cocompact lattices of PU(2,1) into PU(n,1) (n ≥ 2) are superrigid (the remaining exceptional type, after Corlette–Gromov–Schoen); PU(2,1) is the last open case.

**中文。** 定义：超刚性指格到高秩群的表示由代数群结构给出。猜想：PU(2,1) 的余紧格到 PU(n,1)(n≥2) 的一切表示的超刚性(其余例外型已由 Corlette–Gromov–Schoen 建立);PU(2,1) 为最后未决情形。
