# 00000009019

**MFH-09 ★** — source: 286_半整权模形式.md

**English.** Definition: Tunnell's theorem: the congruent number criterion. Conjecture: The criterion is given by the coefficients of two half-integral weight forms and is necessary and sufficient; and the proof of necessity is the Waldspurger bridge. (Tunnell Waldspurger necessary-sufficient criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Tunnell 定理为同余数判据。猜想：判据由两个半整权形式的系数给出且条件充分必要；且必要性的证明为 Waldspurger 桥。（Tunnell 判据 Waldspurger 充要）
