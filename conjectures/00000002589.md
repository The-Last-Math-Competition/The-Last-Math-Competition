# 00000002589

**English.** Definition: Approximate counting of rational points on Fano varieties. Conjecture: The relative-error constant is the inverse logarithm of the height; and the verification of the constant is by the Brauer–Manin obstruction. (Fano point counting height error)

**中文。** 定义：Fano 簇的有理点近似计数。猜想：相对误差常数为高度的逆对数；且常数的验证为 Brauer–Manio 障碍。（Fano 点计数高度误差）
