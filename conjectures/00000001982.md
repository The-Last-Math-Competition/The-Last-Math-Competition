# 00000001982

**English.** Definition: The entanglement spectrum is the eigenvalue spectrum of the reduced density matrix (Liouville space). Conjecture: The counting function of the entanglement spectrum of the 2D free-fermion ground state is a parabola plus a zero-mode correction: the parabola coefficient is 2/π (the Fermi velocity), and the correction is the topological-type (log 2)·Z₂ (switching with the presence or absence of zero modes).

**中文。** 定义：纠缠谱指约化密度矩阵的本征值谱(Liouville 空间)。猜想：2D 自由费米子基态的纠缠谱的计数函数为抛物线加零模式修正:抛物线系数 = 2/π(费米速度),修正项为拓扑型的 (log 2)· Z₂(在有无零模式之切换)。
