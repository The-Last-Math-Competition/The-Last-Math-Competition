# 00000004483

**English.** Definition: De Morgan's law is the internal validity of not p or not not p. Conjecture: A sheaf topos satisfies De Morgan's law if and only if the regular complement of its locale is closed under the lattice of dense open sets, and the criterion is decidable from the finite lattice of open sets. (dense open set criterion of De Morgan)

**中文。** 定义：De Morgan 律指非 p 或非非 p 的内部有效性。猜想：层拓扑斯满足 De Morgan 律当且仅当其 Locale 的正规补在稠密开集格下封闭,判据可由开集格有限判定。（De Morgan 稠密开集判据）
