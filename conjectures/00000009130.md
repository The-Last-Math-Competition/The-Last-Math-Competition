**English.** Definition: Five-point configurations: constrained configurations of planar points. Conjecture: The count of the planar five-point configuration spectrum is of type one; the realization of the count is by extremal convex position; and the counting contribution beyond convex position extrema is always zero. (five-point configuration type-one count)

**中文。** 定义：五点构型为平面点的约束构形。猜想：平面五点构型谱的计数为一型；且计数的实现由凸位置极值；且凸位置极值以外的计数贡献恒为零。（五点构型计数一型）
