# 00000001034

**English.** Definition: Perfect codes in the Lee metric are ball-packing partitions of F_q^n by Lee balls. Conjecture: Nontrivial perfect Lee codes exist only for n = 1 (q ≥ 5) and n = 3 (q = 2, the Golomb–Welch perfect codes); for n ≥ 4 all Lee perfect codes are trivial; in particular the Golomb–Welch counterexamples are excluded outside (q,n) = (5,3).

**中文。** 定义：Lee 度量的完美码指 F_q^n 中 Lee 球的球填充分割。猜想：非平凡完美 Lee 码仅在 n=1(q≥5)与 n=3(q=2, Golomb–Welch 完美码)存在;n≥4 时一切 Lee 完美码为平凡;特别排除 (q,n)=(5,3) 之外的 golomb-Welch 反例。
