# 00000004900

**MT2-30 △** — source: 148_数学的元问题.md

**English.** Definition: A translation functor is a faithful correspondence between two classes of structures. Conjecture: There exists a translation functor full in the forward direction but not faithful in the reverse, the asymmetry is given by an explicit vanishing family of invariants on one side, and the smallest example is the pair of bipartite and signed graphs. (full-faithful asymmetry separation of translation functors)

**中文。** 定义：翻译函子指两结构类间的忠实对应。猜想：存在翻译函子正向满而反向不忠实,不对称由一侧的显式不变量消失族给出,最小例为二部图与符号图对。（翻译函子不对称满忠实分离）
