**English.** Definition: A Du Bois singularity is the complex-geometric vanishing property of the cohomology of the de Rham complex. Conjecture: Its positive-characteristic analogue criterion is the surjectivity of Frobenius on local cohomology, and the criterion is complete in low dimension and stratifies with F-injectivity. (surjectivity criterion of positive-characteristic Du Bois)

**中文。** 定义：Du Bois 奇点指复几何的凋零上同调消没性质。猜想：其正特征类似判据为 Frobenius 在局部上同调的满射性,判据在低维完全且与 F-注入形成分层。（Du Bois 正特征满射判据）
