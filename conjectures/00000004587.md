**HA3-17 ◆** — source: 138_双曲几何的解析面.md

**English.** Definition: A cusped manifold is a finite-volume hyperbolic three-manifold with cusps. Conjecture: The orientable cusped manifold of minimal volume is unique, and the first ten manifolds in the volume ordering are completely determined and stable. (unique minimal cusped manifold and the first ten)

**中文。** 定义：Cusped 流形指带尖点的有限体积双曲三维流形。猜想：最小体积的可定向 cusped 流形唯一,体积排序前十的流形完全确定且稳定。（最小 cusped 唯一与前十）
