# 00000007902

**ATC-02 ★** — source: 249_自守表示与L函数.md

**English.** Definition: The zero-free region of the symmetric power L-functions L(s, sym^n f) for a weight 2 cusp form f: the zero set Z_n in the critical strip. Conjecture: The uniform zero-free band of Z_n to the left of Re s = 1 has width c/log(n Q) with Q the conductor; and the Selberg class degree of sym^n f at the first unknown case n = 10 beyond the known n at most 9 is n+1, and its exceptional zero (Landau-Siegel type) does not exist when the density of sign changes of the coefficients of f is positive. (symmetric power zero-free band)

**中文。** 定义：对称幂 L 函数 L(s, sym^n f)（f 为权 2 尖点形式）的无零点区域：临界带中的零点集 Z_n。猜想：Z_n 在 Re s=1 左侧的一致无零点带宽为 c/log(n·Q)（Q 为导子）；且 sym^n f 的 functoriality 在 n≤9 之外的第一个非已知情形 n=10 的 Selberg 类次数为 n+1，其例外零点（Landau–Siegel 型）不存在当 f 的系数符号变化密度为正。
