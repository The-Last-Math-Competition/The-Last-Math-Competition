# 00000004153

**English.** Definition: The Weyl sum is the polynomial exponential sum S(alpha) = sum of e(alpha x^k). Conjecture: The main-term coefficient of its cubic power average is the explicit constant Gamma(1+1/k)^3/Gamma(3/k), and the remainder is x to the power 3/k plus epsilon. (main constant of the cubic Weyl moment)

**中文。** 定义：Weyl 和指 S(α) = Σe(αx^k) 的多项式指数和。猜想：其三次幂平均的主项系数为 Γ(1+1/k)³/Γ(3/k) 型显式常数,余项为 x 的 3/k 次幂加 ε。（Weyl 和三次矩主常数）
