**English.** Definition: An affine sphere is a volume-minimizing open subset of a toric embedding. Conjecture: The volume lower bound of affine spheres of toric Fanos is an explicit reciprocal expression in the number of vertices, attained by single-point orbits and monotone. (vertex-reciprocal volume bound of affine spheres)
