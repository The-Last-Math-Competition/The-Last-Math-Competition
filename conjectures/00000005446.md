# 00000005446

**English.** Definition: The continued fraction expansion is the bit complexity of the continued fraction representation of rationals. Conjecture: The bit complexity of the continued fraction expansion is always equivalent to the complexity of the gcd (Euclidean type); with uniformly bounded partial quotients the complexity degenerates to linear, with degeneration constant the logarithm of the partial-quotient bound; and under the worst partial quotients (Fibonacci type) the complexity is quadratic, attaining the Euclidean lower bound. (gcd-equivalent complexity of continued fractions)

**中文。** 定义：连分数展开：有理数的连分式表示的位复杂度。猜想：连分数展开的位复杂度恒等价于最大公因子的复杂度（欧几里得型）；部分商一致有界时复杂度退化为线性且退化常数为部分商界的对数；最坏部分商（Fibonacci 型）下复杂度为平方型且达到欧几里得下界。（连分数公因子等价复杂度）
