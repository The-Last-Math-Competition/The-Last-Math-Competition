# 00000001582

**English.** Definition: The Jimbo asymptotics of the one-punctured elliptic isomonodromy are the connection formulae of its Stokes constants. Conjecture: In the identity between the Stokes constants and the reflection amplitudes of Liouville field theory, the proportionality constant is exactly 1 (the existence of the identity is the known direction); the exact constant is verified via the structure constants of the c = 1 theory.

**中文。** 定义：椭圆一点的 isomonodromy 的 Jimbo 渐近式指其 Stokes 常数的连结公式。猜想：Stokes 常数与 Liouville 场论反射振幅的等式中的比例常数恰为 1(已知方向为等式的存在);精确常数由 c=1 理论的结构常数显式验证。
