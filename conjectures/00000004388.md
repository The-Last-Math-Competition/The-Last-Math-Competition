# 00000004388

**KK2-28 ★** — source: 131_代数K理论II_迹方法.md

**English.** Definition: The canonical fibrations of algebraic K-theory are the families of homotopy fiber sequences derived from localization sequences and the additivity theorem. Conjecture: Every canonical fiber sequence is generated by composition of at most five steps from the three elementary types of localization, additivity, and transfer; the set of generating relations is finite with each relation of length at most 5; and the decomposition of the composition is unique up to homotopy if and only if the fiber sequence is of Euler type. (three-type generation of K fibrations)

**中文。** 定义：代数 K 理论的典范纤维化：由局部化序列与加性定理导出的同伦纤维列族。猜想：一切典范纤维列由局部化、加性与转移三类初等纤维列经不超过五步复合生成；生成关系集为有限且每条关系的长度不超过 5；复合的分解在同伦意义下唯一当且仅当纤维列为欧拉型。（K 纤维化三类生成）
