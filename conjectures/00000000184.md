# 00000000184

**CS-18 ◆** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: Algebraic independence of e and π. Conjecture: e and π are algebraically independent; more strongly, e, π, and e^π together generate a field of transcendence degree two. (e-pi-e^pi degree two)

**中文。** 定义：e 与 π 的代数无关性。猜想：e 与 π 代数无关；更强地 e、π 与 e^π 三者生成超越次数为二的域。（e-pi-e^pi 次数二）
