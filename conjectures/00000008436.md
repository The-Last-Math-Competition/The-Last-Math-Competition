# 00000008436

**English.** Definition: Mixed-type covers: covering designs with nonuniform block sizes. Conjecture: The block-size distribution of optimal mixed covers is an explicit staircase; the jump points of the staircase are integer points of ratios of binomial coefficients; and uniform covers are strictly suboptimal at the jump points. (staircase distribution of mixed covers)

**中文。** 定义：覆盖的混合型指区组大小不均匀的覆盖设计。猜想：最优混合覆盖的区组尺寸分布为显式阶梯型；阶梯的跳跃点为二项系数比的整数化；均匀覆盖在跳跃点处严格次优。（混合覆盖阶梯分布）
