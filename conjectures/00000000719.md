# 00000000719

**English.** Definition: The Barsky–Katz p-adic measure. Conjecture: The p-adic valuation of the values of the p-adic interpolation L_p(s, ω) at s = 1 + (p−1)k, divided by p, is uniformly distributed (value distribution beyond the Leopoldt zeros).

**中文。** 定义：Barsky–Katss p 进测度。猜想：ζ 的 p 进插值 L_p(s, ω) 在 s = 1 + (p−1)k 处的值 / p 的 p 进赋值均匀分布(Leopoldt 零点之外的值分布)。
