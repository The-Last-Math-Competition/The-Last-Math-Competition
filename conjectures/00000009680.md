# 00000009680

**TAG-10 ★** — source: 308_算术超越.md

**English.** Definition: Algebraic preimages of the exponential map: the logarithm branch structure of the set of multiple inverse images of exp at algebraic numbers. Conjecture: The Q-span of the set of logarithm branches of the same algebraic number is finite-dimensional with dimension equal to the lattice of multiples of 2 pi i; logarithm lattices of distinct algebraic numbers coincide if and only if the numbers are exponentially related. (logarithm branch lattice structure)

**中文。** 定义：指数映射的代数原像：exp(z) 在代数数处的多重逆像集合的对数分支结构。猜想：同一代数数的对数分支集合的 Q 张成为有限维且维数等于 2πi 的倍数格；不同代数数的对数格在且仅在指数相关时重合。（对数分支格结构）
