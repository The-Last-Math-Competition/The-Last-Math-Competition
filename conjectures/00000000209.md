**SQ-09 ◆** — source: 14_特定序列与递推.md

**English.** Definition: Gijswijt's sequence: a(1) = 1; a(n) is the largest k such that the word formed by the first n−1 terms can be written as Y^k. Conjecture: The digit 5 occurs in the sequence (4 is known to first occur at term 220; the first occurrence of 5 is estimated to be astronomically large).

**中文。** 定义：Gijswijt 序列:a(1) = 1;a(n) 为使前 n−1 项组成的词可写成 Y^k 的最大 k。猜想：数字 5 在序列中出现(已知 4 首现于第 220 项;5 的首现位置被估计为天文数字)。
