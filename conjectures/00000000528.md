# 00000000528

**CA-28 ★** — source: 21_交换代数与同调代数.md

**English.** Definition: Frobenius–Betti numbers are the renormalized Betti tables in characteristic p. Conjecture: For rational three-dimensional normal rings, the limit of the F-Betti table exists and, after renormalization, is of pure monomial type; and the convergence rate is a negative power of p. (F-Betti limit pure monomial type)

**中文。** 定义：Frobenius–Betti 数为特征 p 的 Betti 表重整化量。猜想：有理三维正规环的 F-Betti 表的极限存在且重整化后为纯单项式型；且收敛速率为 p 的负幂。（F-Betti 极限纯单项式型）
