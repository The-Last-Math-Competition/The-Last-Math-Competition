# 00000001372

**English.** Definition: Limits of measurable functions under cone measure. Conjecture: The first counterexample, beyond the dichotomy that hyperarithmetic invariant functions are almost everywhere equal to the identity or the jump under Martin measure, has complexity Δ^1_2 (the invariance hierarchy).

**中文。** 定义：锥测度下的可测函数的极限。猜想：超算术不变函数在 Martin 测度下几乎处处等于恒等或跳跃的二择一之外的第一个反例的复杂度为 Δ^1_2(不变性层次)。
