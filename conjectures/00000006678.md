**English.** Definition: Scaling iteration with matrix-scaling approximation. Conjecture: The linear convergence rate is given by the spectral gap and the rate table is complete; the exception of completeness is the zero spectrum; and the zero-spectrum exception is repaired by row randomization. (spectral-gap convergence rate of scaling iteration)

**中文。** 定义：比例迭代为矩阵比例逼近。猜想：线性收敛率由谱隙给出且率表完全；且完全的例外为零谱；且零谱的例外由行随机化修复。（比例迭代谱隙收敛率）
