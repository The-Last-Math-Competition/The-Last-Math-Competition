# 00000008355

**English.** Definition: Specht modules of tropical linear spaces: the stratification coefficients trop_coeff of Bergman complexes of valuated matroids. Conjecture: trop_coeff (cohomology coefficients of tropical linear spaces) are irreducible Specht modules of S_n (a Specht coefficient law); the multiplicities of the modules (multiplicities of Specht decompositions) are Littlewood-Richardson numbers (an LR multiplicity law); and the exceptions to vanishing of cohomology (nonvanishing middle strata) are indexed by rim hooks (a rim hook exception law), the exceptional strata having dimension 1 (a one-dimensional exception law). (tropical Specht Littlewood-Richardson rim hook)

**中文。** 定义：热带线性空间的斯佩希特 模：valuated matroid 的 Bergman 复形的层系数 trop_coeff。猜想：trop_coeff（热带线性空间的上同调系数）为 Sn 的不可约 Specht 模（Specht 系数律）；且模的重数（Specht 分解的重数）= Littlewood–Richardson 数（LR 重数律）；上同调的消没（中间层的消没）的例外（非消没层）的指标为边缘分拆（边缘分拆例外律），例外的层的维数为 1（单维例外律）。
