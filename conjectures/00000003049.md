# 00000003049

**English.** Definition: The elasticity test: the elastic law of soliton interactions. Conjecture: The three-soliton elastic law: multisoliton collisions of integrable systems are purely elastic, with the elasticity criterion the multiplicative preservation of scattering data; the radiation loss of nonintegrable systems is an explicit third-order quantity of the interaction. (three-soliton elastic radiation quantity)

**中文。** 定义：消相干检验：孤立子相互作用的弹性律。猜想：三孤子弹性律：可积系统的多孤子碰撞为纯弹性且弹性判据为散射数据的乘性保持；非可积的辐射损失为相互作用的显式三阶量。（三孤子弹性辐射量）
