# 00000003474

**GA3-14 ★** — source: 101_代数图论III_染色与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The interlace polynomial characterizes the interaction of the cycle space of a graph with Gaussian elimination. Conjecture: Multiple roots of the interlace polynomial occur only in the interval [−4,0], with each multiple root corresponding to one eliminable crossing of the medial embedding; the multiple-root spectrum coincides with the Jones root spectrum of indecomposable links and is a finite union. (interlace multiple root forbidden law)

**中文。** 定义：交织多项式刻画图的圈空间与高斯消去的互动结构。猜想：交织多项式的重根仅出现在区间 [−4,0] 且每个重根对应 medial 嵌入的一个可消没交叉；重根谱与不可分解链环的 Jones 根谱一致且为有限并。（交织重根禁用律）
