# 00000003964

**G4-24 ◆** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The diameter of a Cayley graph is the diameter with respect to the word metric of the generating set. Conjecture: The Cayley graph diameter of every finite simple group is at most 3 log^2 |G|; the smallest family of groups attaining the constant 1 is PSL(2, p). (Cayley diameter constant for simple groups)

**中文。** 定义：Cayley 图直径指关于生成元集的字度量直径。猜想：一切有限单群的 Cayley 图直径不超过 3·log²|G|;其中最小达到常数 1 的群族为 PSL(2,p)。（单群 Cayley 直径常数）
