**English.** Definition: Cayley graph diameter denotes word metric diameter of with respect to generator set. Conjecture: Cayley graph diameter of all finite simple group does not exceed 3 · log²|G|; where most small attains group family of constant 1 is PSL(2,p). (simple group Cayley diameter constant)
