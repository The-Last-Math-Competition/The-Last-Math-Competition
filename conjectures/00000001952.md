# 00000001952

**LG-22 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: The minimal index of a finite-index subgroup of Out(F_n). Conjecture: The minimal index is 2^n·binom(n,2) (an automorphism count of the Vogtmann complex); all smaller indices arise only as kernels of automorphism actions.

**中文。** 定义：Out(F_n) 的有限指数子群的最小指数。猜想：最小指数为 2^n\cdot\binom{n}{2}(Vogtmann 复形的自同构计数);一切更小的指数皆由自同构作用的核给出。
