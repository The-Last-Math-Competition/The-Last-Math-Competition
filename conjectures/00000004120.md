# 00000004120

**English.** Definition: The homology spectrum of an operad is the sequence of homology dimensions stratified by arity. Conjecture: There exist two operads whose homology spectra are identical while their homotopy types differ, and the minimal arity threshold of the separation is 3. (operad homology-homotopy separation)

**中文。** 定义：operad 的同调谱指按 arity 分层的同调维数列。猜想：存在两个 operad 同调谱恒同而同伦型不同,分离的最小 arity 阈为 3。（operad 同调-同伦分离）
