# 00000009605

**English.** Definition: Spectral rigidity of minimal surfaces in spheres: whether the Laplace spectrum uniquely determines the Clifford torus. Conjecture: Minimal tori in S^3 isospectral to the Clifford torus are necessarily isometric; isospectral non-isometric pairs of minimal tori do not exist in S^3, so spectral rigidity holds universally in the minimal category. (Clifford torus spectral rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：球面极小曲面的谱刚性：Laplace 谱是否唯一确定 Clifford 环面。猜想：S^3 中与 Clifford 环面同谱的极小环面必等距；同谱不等距的极小环面对在 S^3 中不存在，谱刚性在极小范畴内普遍成立。（Clifford 环面谱刚性）
