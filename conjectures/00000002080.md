**English.** Definition: The coherence of the lattices of Serre subcategories and localizing subcategories of module categories. Conjecture: An explicit characterization of the quotient of the two lattices exists; over finite-dimensional algebras it is decidable (a polynomial algorithm); the characterization is given by the dimensions of supporting-varieties.
