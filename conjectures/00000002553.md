# 00000002553

**English.** Definition: Comparison of analytic capacity γ(E) with continuous analytic capacity. Conjecture: The lower bound of the ratio of the two capacities is an explicit power of the dimension, with exponent 1/2 attained by corner-type sets; the equivalence domain of the two capacities is the rectifiable sets, and first separation occurs at logarithmic-spiral sets. (two analytic capacities ratio)

**中文。** 定义：解析容量 γ(E) 与连续解析容量的比较。猜想：两容量之比的下界为维数的显式幂且幂指数 1/2 由角状集达到；两容量的等价域为可校正集并在对数螺线集发生首次分离。（双解析容量比）
