# 00000001349

**AN2-19 ◆** — source: 30_解析数论II_L函数与自守.md

**English.** Definition: The Elliott–Halberstam conjecture asserts that primes in arithmetic progressions reach distribution level θ = 1. Conjecture: The conclusion that EH(θ = 1) implies liminf(p_{n+1} − p_n) ≤ 6 is the weakest corollary of EH: no version EH(θ) with θ < 1 suffices for the bound ≤ 6; that is, 6 is the infimal output of EH-type methods.

**中文。** 定义：Elliott–Halberstam 猜想断言素数在等差数列中的分布水平可达 θ=1。猜想：EH(θ=1) 蕴含素数间隙 liminf (p_{n+1}−p_n) ≤ 6 的结论是 EH 的最弱推论:任何 θ<1 的 EH(θ) 版本都不足以推出间隙 ≤ 6;即 6 是 EH 类方法的下确界输出。
