**English.** Definition: The extended process is the joint space-time framework of KPZ domain growth. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the stretching-direction scaling ratio, the spatial two-thirds combined with the temporal three-thirds; the self-consistency constant of the combination is 1; and any deviation of the scaling ratio is pulled back by the renormalization-group flow, the pull-back rate being the drift strength. (joint space-time scaling ratio of stretching)
