# 00000007110

**English.** Definition: Distributed first-order methods with communication optimization. Conjecture: The tradeoff of communication and computation holds, the combination of the tradeoff being round complexity. (round-complexity communication-computation tradeoff)

**中文。** 定义：一阶方法的分布式：通信优化。猜想：分布式的下降：通信与计算的折衷且衷的组合为轮次复杂度。（分布式一阶方法通信优化轮次复杂度通信计算折衷组合分布式轮次复杂度通信复杂度计算折衷）
