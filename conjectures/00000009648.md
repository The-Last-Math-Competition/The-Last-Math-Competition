# 00000009648

**HSS-08 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: Delocalization of eigenvectors of Wigner matrices: norms concentrate at n^{-1/2} (Erdos-Yau proved complete delocalization); the quantitative precision is unknown. Conjecture: Quantum unique ergodicity of eigenvectors holds with logarithmic-level ergodicity error: mass on any subset does not exceed the volume ratio times (log n)^c; and the logarithmic correction cannot be removed, by counterexamples with correlated barriers. (eigenvector logarithmic delocalization)

**中文。** 定义：Wigner 矩阵特征向量的离域化：范数集中于 n^{−1/2}（Erdős–Yau 已证完全离域）；定量精度未知。猜想：特征向量的量子唯一遍历成立且遍历性误差为对数级：任意子集上的质量不超过体积比乘以 (log n)^{c}；对数修正不可去，由相关势垒的反例给出。（特征向量对数离域）
