**CP2-13 ★** — source: 173_组合几何III_凸位置.md

**English.** Definition: The fractional conversion is the fractional extension of the parameters and dimension of the intersection theorem. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit tolerance expression of the multiple-intersection extension, given by inclusion-exclusion; the tolerance is a linear function of the intersection deficit, with slope the reciprocal of the dimension; and the tight examples of the fractional version are given by the double-cylinder construction, with finitely many constructions. (multiple-intersection tolerance fractional conversion)

**中文。** 定义：分数换算：相交定理的参数与维数的分数推广。猜想：换算公式为重交数推广的显式容差式且由容斥原理给出；容差为交数缺额的线性函数且斜率为维数的倒数；分数版的紧例由双圆柱构造给出且构造数为有限。（重交容差分数换算公式）
