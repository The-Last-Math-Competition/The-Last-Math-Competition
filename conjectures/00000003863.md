# 00000003863

**English.** Definition: The Selberg–Aomoto integral is the Jack-interpolation generalization of the Selberg integral, meromorphically extended in (α,β,γ). Conjecture: Its pole set consists exactly of the poles of Gamma factors and the negative-integer lattice of γ, with no other poles. (Selberg pole complete classification)

**中文。** 定义：Selberg–Aomoto 积指 Selberg 积的 Jack 插值推广族,作为 (α,β,γ) 的函数亚纯延拓。猜想：其极点集恰由 Gamma 因子的极点与 γ 的负整数格构成,不存在任何其他极点。（Selberg 极点完全分类）
