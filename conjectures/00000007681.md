**English.** Definition: q-Hermite polynomial H_n(x|q) satisfies three term recurrence 2x H_n=H_{n+1}+(1-q^n)H_{n-1}, its zero denote x_{n,1}<…<x_{n,n}. Conjecture: intersection zero identical expression x_{n+1,2k}<x_{n,k}<x_{n+1,2k+1} for all 0<q<1 becomes (classical takes limit q -> 1 obtains Hermite zero intersection), and difference sequence x_{n,k}-x_{n+1,2k} with respect to q decreasing 0 of.

**中文。** 定义：q-Hermite 多项式 H_n(x|q) 满足三项递推 2x H_n=H_{n+1}+(1-q^n)H_{n-1}，其零点记 x_{n,1}<…<x_{n,n}。猜想：交错零点恒等式 x_{n+1,2k}<x_{n,k}<x_{n+1,2k+1} 对一切 0<q<1 成立（经典取极限 q→1 得 Hermite 零点交错），且更精细的差序列 x_{n,k}-x_{n+1,2k} 关于 q 单调递减到 0。
