# 00000007681

**English.** Definition: The q-Hermite polynomials H_n(x|q) satisfy the three-term recursion 2x H_n = H_{n+1}+(1-q^n)H_{n-1}, with zeros x_{n,1}<...<x_{n,n}. Conjecture: The interlacing identity x_{n+1,2k} < x_{n,k} < x_{n+1,2k+1} holds for all 0<q<1 (with the classical Hermite interlacing at q to 1); moreover the finer difference sequence x_{n,k}-x_{n+1,2k} decreases monotonically to 0 in q. (q-Hermite interlacing and monotone spacing)

**中文。** 定义：q-Hermite 多项式 H_n(x|q) 满足三项递推 2x H_n=H_{n+1}+(1-q^n)H_{n-1}，其零点记 x_{n,1}<…<x_{n,n}。猜想：交错零点恒等式 x_{n+1,2k}<x_{n,k}<x_{n+1,2k+1} 对一切 0<q<1 成立（经典取极限 q→1 得 Hermite 零点交错），且更精细的差序列 x_{n,k}-x_{n+1,2k} 关于 q 单调递减到 0。
