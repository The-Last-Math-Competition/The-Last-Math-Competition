# 00000009598

**English.** Definition: The first eigenvalue of minimal hypersurfaces in negatively curved manifolds: the Schoen-type estimate lambda_1 at least c in terms of curvature. Conjecture: Minimal surfaces of closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds satisfy lambda_1 at least 1/4 + c over l-squared, where l is the longest geodesic length; and the improvement term vanishes asymptotically on deep minimal surfaces (normalized area tending to 4 pi/3). (hyperbolic minimal first eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：负曲率流形中极小超曲面的第一特征值：Schoen 型估计 λ_1 ≥ c 与曲率的关系。猜想：闭双曲 3-流形的极小曲面 λ_1 ≥ 1/4 + c/ℓ²，其中 ℓ 为最长测地线长；改进项在深层极小曲面（归一化面积趋于 4π/3）渐近消失。（双曲极小第一特征值）
