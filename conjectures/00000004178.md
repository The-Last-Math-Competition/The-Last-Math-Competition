# 00000004178

**English.** Definition: Type I and Type II sums are the linear-type and bilinear-type exponential sums. Conjecture: The optimal switching threshold between the two types of sums is the 1/3 power of the interval length, and the switching point produces a logarithmic-factor loss on both sides. (Type I-II switching threshold)

**中文。** 定义：Type I 与 Type II 和指线性型与双线性型指数和。猜想：两类和的最优切换阈值为区间长度的 1/3 幂,且切换点两端各产生对数因子损失。（Type I-II 切换阈值）
