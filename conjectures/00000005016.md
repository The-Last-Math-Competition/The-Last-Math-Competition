# 00000005016

**English.** Definition: The zero-mode count of the Dirac operator on a Spin(7) manifold is related by index to characteristic classes. Conjecture: For compact Spin(7) manifolds with b_1 = 0, the Dirac zero-mode count is the explicit integer-valued index of the A-hat genus plus a w_8 correction, and the value is nonnegative; the correction coefficient is 1; and the mod 2 reduction of the zero-mode dimension equals the mod 2 reduction of the integral of w_8. (Dirac zero-mode index of Spin(7))
