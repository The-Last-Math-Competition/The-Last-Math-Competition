# 00000005543

**English.** Definition: Sphere classes are the special module classes of sphere D-modules on the flag manifold, with the conversion between characteristics and orbits. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit sign expression of orbit-dimension differences, degenerating to one at closed orbits; the sign of an open orbit is the negative of the closed one, with equal absolute value; and the conversion product of two orbits is a constant multiple of the intersection number of the orbit closures, with multiplier 1. (sign conversion of orbit-dimension differences for spheres)

**中文。** 定义：球面类：旗流形上球面 D 模的特殊模类；其换算（特征与轨道的对应）。猜想：换算公式为轨道维数差的显式符号式且闭轨道处退化为一；开轨道的符号为闭轨道的负且绝对值相等；两个轨道的换算乘积为轨道闭包的交数的常数倍且倍数为 1。（球面轨道维差符号换算）
