# 00000009705

**English.** Definition: Price's decay law for wave equations on asymptotically flat backgrounds: tail decay of solutions outside black holes. Conjecture: Price's law is stable under nonlinear perturbations: the two-term structure of tail decay t^{-3} with secondary t^{-4} log t is universal for all initial data with decay exponent beta > 1/2; the nonlinear term changes only the second coefficient, given explicitly by the Newman-Penrose constant of the initial data. (nonlinear Price two-term law)

**中文。** 定义：渐近平坦背景上波动方程的 Price 衰减律：解在黑洞外部的尾部衰减。猜想：非线性微扰下 Price 律稳定：尾部衰减 t^{−3} 与次阶 t^{−4} log t 的双项结构对一切满足衰减指标 β>1/2 的初值普适；非线性项仅改变第二项系数且系数由初值的 Newman–Penrose 常数显式给出。（非线性 Price 双项律）
