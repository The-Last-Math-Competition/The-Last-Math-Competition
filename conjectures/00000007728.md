# 00000007728

**English.** Definition: The free multiplicative convolution MP_{c1} boxed MP_{c2} of two Marchenko-Pastur laws is explicitly known, with atoms only at 1. Conjecture: For MP_{c1} boxed ... boxed MP_{ck}, the largest zero of the density satisfies x_max = prod min(1,c_i)^{2/k}, the density behaves as (x_max - x)^{k-1/2} to the left of x_max; and the atom criterion is that at least two of the c_i are below 1. (largest zero of convolved MP laws)

**中文。** 定义：两个 Marchenko–Pastur 律 MP_c（参数 c>0）的自由乘法卷积 MP_c1 ⊠ MP_c2 的密度为已知显式形式；其原子部分仅出现在 1 处，质量为 max(0, 1-c1)(max(0,1-c2)) 型修正。猜想：MP_{c1} ⊠ … ⊠ MP_{ck} 的密度零点集中，最大零点 x_max 满足 x_max = Π min(1,c_i)^{2/k}，且密度在 x_max 左侧的行为为 (x_max - x)^{k-1/2}；原子判据为 k 个 c_i 中至少两个 <1。
