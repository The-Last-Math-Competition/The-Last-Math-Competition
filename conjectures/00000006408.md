# 00000006408

**English.** Definition: Equimeasurable sets are set pairs of the same measure. Conjecture: The rearrangement minimum is always attained by monotone rearrangement and the minimal value is the integral of the distribution-function difference; the optimality is by the Hardy-Littlewood inequality. (monotone-optimal rearrangement minimum)

**中文。** 定义：等测集为同测度的集合对。猜想：重排极小恒由单调重排达到且极小值为分布函数差的积分；且最优性由 Hardy–Littlewood 不等式。（重排极小单调最优）
