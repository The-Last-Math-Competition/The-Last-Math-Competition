# 00000008519

**English.** Definition: The classification of regular and irregular singularities of D-modules. Conjecture: The irregularity of D-modules satisfies conservation under the four functors: the product law of irregularity under f_shriek and f-pullback; and irregularity is an integer combination of the Levelt type classification. (D-module irregularity conservation)

**中文。** 定义：D-模的正则奇点与不规则奇点分类。猜想：D-模的不规则度在四函子下满足守恒：f_! 与 f^* 的不规则度乘积律；且不规则度为 Levelt 型分类的整数组合。（D-模不规则度守恒）
