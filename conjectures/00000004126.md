**English.** Definition: The juxtaposition forcing is the forcing whose conditions are coordinatewise unions. Conjecture: The smallest anomaly of the juxtaposition absorption law appears in three-coordinate interaction, the anomaly can be realized by an explicit forcing of cardinality aleph_1, and there is no anomaly in dimension two or below. (minimal anomaly of the juxtaposition absorption law)
