**English.** Definition: The index is the minimal splitting degree of a central simple algebra, and the period is the order of the Brauer class. Conjecture: The realizable values of the index-to-period ratio on a quadratic field form the explicit lattice of squarefree integers, given by aggregation conditions of the local invariants. (aggregation lattice of index-period ratios)

**中文。** 定义：指标指中心单代数分裂的最小次数,周期指 Brauer 类的阶。猜想：二次域上指标与周期之比的可实现值为平方自由整数的显式格,格由局部不变量的聚合条件给出。（指标周期比聚合格）
