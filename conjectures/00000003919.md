# 00000003919

**FL-09 ★** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: The open-closed map OC is the map from Lagrangian Floer cohomology to closed-string quantum cohomology. Conjecture: In an exact filling, surjectivity of OC forces the filling to be a homology sphere; this conclusion fails in dimension at least 6, and the smallest counterexample is a product of local tori. (open-closed surjectivity sphere criterion)

**中文。** 定义：开闭映射 OC 指 Lagrange Floer 上同调到闭弦量子同调的映射。猜想：exact 填充中 OC 满射迫使填充同调球;该结论在维数不小于 6 时失效,最小反例为局部环面的乘积。（open-closed 满射球判据）
