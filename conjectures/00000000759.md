# 00000000759

**English.** Definition: The type theory of SL₂(Q_p) is the classification skeleton of representations. Conjecture: The number of intertwined zero-supercuspidal types is at most two; the rigidity is realized by depth-zero supercuspidals; and this rigidity fails for positive-depth types, yielding a family of counterexamples. (zero-supercuspidal intertwining rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：SL₂(Q_p) 的类型理论为表示的分类骨架。猜想：零超点类型的缠结数不超过二；且刚性的实现为深度零的超尖点。；且该刚性在深度正的类型失效并给出反例族。（零超点缠结刚性）
