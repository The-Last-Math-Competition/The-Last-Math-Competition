**English.** Definition: The quantization of the Arnold-Liouville dimension of symplectic integrable systems: the quantum fixed point count q_int of integrable systems. Conjecture: q_int (the count of Bohr-Sommerfeld orbits) is at least the Arnold-Liouville topology of the Lagrangian foliation (a topology counting law); the equality of counts (BS equals topology) holds in dimension 2 (a two-dimensional equality domain law); the higher-dimensional correction is the Berry-Tabor symmetry correction (a BT correction law); and the statistics of the correction (level spacing) are Poisson for integrable systems (an integrable Poisson law), with the boundary to quantum chaos (nonintegrable GOE, the Wigner law) given by the rigidity of the geodesic flow (a rigidity boundary law). (Bohr-Sommerfeld Berry-Tabor Poisson boundary)

**中文。** 定义：辛维数的 Arnold–Liouville 的量子化：可积系统的量子不动点 q_int。猜想：q_int（Bohr–Sommerfeld 轨道的计数）≥ Lagrangian 叶的 Arnold–Liouville 拓扑（拓扑计数律）；且计数的等式（BS=拓扑）的维数域为 2 维（二维等式域律）；高维的修正为 Berry–Tabor 的对称修正（BT 修正律）；修正的统计（能级间距）为泊松（可积泊松律），量子混沌（非可积）的 GOE（Wigner 律）的分界为测地流的刚性（刚性分界律）。
