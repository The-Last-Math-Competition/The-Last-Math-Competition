**HY4-17 ◆** — source: 240_微分算子IV_双曲型.md

**English.** Definition: Hyperbolic boundary conditions as constraints on incoming characteristics. Conjecture: The criterion of well-posed boundary conditions is that the outgoing characteristic count equals the boundary-operator count; and the satisfaction of the criterion is maximal dissipativity. (hyperbolic boundary well-posedness criterion)

**中文。** 定义：双曲边界条件为入口特征的约束。猜想：适定边界条件的判据为出口特征数等于边界算子数；且判据的满足为最大耗散。（双曲边界适定判据）
