**GT5-20 △** — source: 193_几何范畴论.md

**English.** Definition: Closed-substructure lattices and open-complement structures are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two toposes with isomorphic closed lattices but different open-complement structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit bitopology with the same closed lattice but different complement operations. (same closed lattice-different complement separation)

**中文。** 定义：闭子结构格与开补结构指两层。猜想：存在两拓扑斯的闭格同构而开补结构不同,分离由同闭格异补运算的显式双拓扑实现。（闭格同-开补异运算分离）
