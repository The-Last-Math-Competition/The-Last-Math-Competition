**English.** Definition: The free transport inequality is the inequality between free entropy and transport cost. Conjecture: The optimal constant is an explicit dimension-free value attained by the semicircle law; the saturation value for semicircular input is exactly pi/2; the optimal constant is invariant under the free central limit, and any improvement of the constant sacrifices at least one multimodal input direction. (dimension-free constant of free transport)

**中文。** 定义：自由运输不等式：自由熵与运输代价之间的不等式。猜想：最优常数为维数无关的显式值且由半圆律达到；半圆输入下的饱和值恰为 π/2；最优常数在自由中心极限下不变且任何常数的改进至少牺牲一个多峰输入方向。（自由运输维数无关常数）
