**English.** Definition: Hopf plumbing is fibering and gluing along Hopf bands. Conjecture: Hopf plumbing generates all fibered knots; the shortest generating word has length at most 3g; and the shortest word is unique up to stabilization. (generation bound for fibered plumbing)

**中文。** 定义：Hopf plumbing 指沿 Hopf 带的纤维化和粘合。猜想：Hopf plumbing 生成一切 fibered 结；最短生成字长 ≤ 3g 且最短字在稳定化下唯一。（fibered plumbing 生成界）
