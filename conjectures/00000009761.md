**OIE-01 ★** — source: 311_算子理想与特征值不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Lidskii's theorem: the trace of a trace-class operator equals the sum of eigenvalues; the majorization relations between singular values and eigenvalues form Weyl-Lidskii theory. Conjecture: An arithmetic strengthening of the log-majorization control of eigenvalue moduli by singular values: the convex combination bound |lambda_n| <= ((product_{k<=n} s_k)^{1/n} + s_n)/2 is optimal; optimality is attained by second-order diagonal models, and the convex combination coefficients are unique. (Weyl majorization arithmetic strengthening)

**中文。** 定义：Lidskii 定理：迹类算子的迹等于特征值和；其奇异值与特征值的优超关系构成 Weyl–Lidskii 理论。猜想：特征值模被奇异值的对数优超控制的强化算术形式：|λ_n| ≤ ((Π_{k≤n} s_k)^{1/n} + s_n)/2 的凸组合界为最优；最优性由二阶对角模型达到且凸组合系数唯一。（Weyl 优超算术强化）
