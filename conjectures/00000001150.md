# 00000001150

**LT-50 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: Leibniz algebras (a non-antisymmetric generalization). Conjecture: The difference in the identity H^*(g) = HL^*(g) between Leibniz cohomology and Chevalley translation is a free module (the Leibniz–Chevalley difference).

**中文。** 定义：Leibniz 代数(非反对称推广)。猜想：Leibniz 上同调与 Chevalley 平移的等式 H^*(g) = HL^*(g) 的差为自由模(Leibniz-Chevalley 差)。
