# 00000008150

**English.** Definition: The systematic error of the Migdal-Kadanoff bond-moving approximation: the deviation err_MK(d) of critical exponents between MK and exact RG. Conjecture: err_MK(d) = c (d-2)^{-2} (an inverse-square error law with c explicit); the error vanishes at d = 2 (two-dimensional exactness: self-dual exactness of MK at d = 2); the saturation value err_inf of the upper bound at high dimension is the logarithm of 1/2 (a saturation logarithm law); and the error reduction factor of the MK correction (multiscale bond moving) is b^{-d} (b the block scale). (Migdal-Kadanoff inverse-square saturation law)

**中文。** 定义：Migdal–Kadanoff 的键移动近似的系统误差：MK 与精确 RG 的临界指数的偏差 err_MK(d)。猜想：err_MK(d) = c·(d-2)^{-2}（平方反比误差律，c 显式）；且误差的最小维数 d=2（二维精确：MK 在 d=2 的自对偶精确性）；误差的上界（高维）的饱和值 err_∞ 为 1/2 的对数（饱和对数律）；MK 的修正（多尺度键移动）的误差递降因子为 b^{-d}（b 为块尺度）。
