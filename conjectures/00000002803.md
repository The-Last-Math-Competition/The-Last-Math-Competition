**English.** Definition: Sanov's theorem: large deviations of empirical measures (relative-entropy rate). Conjecture: The strong-coupling deviation of the Sanov rate: the correction of the minimal deviation of the relative-entropy rate is the specific entropy density, and in the Markov case the correction is the variational relative entropy of the transition kernel. (Sanov Markov variational correction)

**中文。** 定义：Sanov 定理：经验测度的大偏差（相对熵速率）。猜想：Sanov 速率的强耦合偏差：相对熵速率的最小偏差的修正为比熵密度且修正项在 Markov 情形为转移核相对熵的变分。（Sanov Markov 变分修正）
