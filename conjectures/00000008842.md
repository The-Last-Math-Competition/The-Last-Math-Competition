# 00000008842

**MON-12 ★** — source: 280_单调算子与算子分裂.md

**English.** Definition: The dense domain theorem for maximal monotonicity. Conjecture: The domain of a maximal monotone operator is always dense in its closure; and the graph of the operator is a maximally monotone closed set in the product space. (maximal monotone domain density)

**中文。** 定义：极大单调的稠密域定理。猜想：极大单调算子的域恒在闭包内稠密；且算子图在乘积空间为极大单调闭集。（极大单调域稠密）
