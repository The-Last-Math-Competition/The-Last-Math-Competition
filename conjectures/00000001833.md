# 00000001833

**AG2-23 ◆** — source: 46_代数几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The section conjecture states that the section Galois group of a hyperbolic curve determines its geometry. Conjecture: Two hyperbolic curves have isomorphic section groups if and only if the curves are isomorphic (a first instance of nonabelian verification); this follows from the rigidity of the action of the absolute Galois group on the points of the curve.

**中文。** 定义：截段猜想指双曲曲线的截段 Galois 群决定其几何。猜想：两条双曲曲线的截段群同构 ⟺ 曲线同构(非交换验证的首例);由绝对 Galois 群在曲线上点的作用的刚性强推。
