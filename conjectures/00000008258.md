# 00000008258

**English.** Definition: The microstructure of dark pools and latency equilibria: the Kyle insider parameter kyle_gap of the bid-ask spread. Conjecture: kyle_gap (the half-spread of the Kyle model) is the explicit sigma sqrt(Delta t/8) (sigma the volatility, Delta t the interval, the Kyle formula); the explicit linear coefficient of price impact (Kyle's lambda) is the square root of the ratio of insider variance to noise variance (a variance-ratio square-root law); and the decrease of lambda with multiple insiders (competing insiders) is 1/sqrt(m+1) (m the number of insiders, a square-root decrease law). (Kyle half-spread variance-ratio insider decrease)

**中文。** 定义：暗池与延迟的均衡的微观结构：买卖价差的 Kyle 内幕参数 kyle_gap。猜想：kyle_gap（Kyle 模型的半价差）= σ·√(Δt/8) 的显式（σ 为波动，Δt 为间隔，Kyle 公式）；且价格冲击的线性系数（Kyle 的 λ）的显式为内幕方差与噪声方差的比的平方根（方差比平方根律）；多内幕（竞争内幕）的 λ 的递减为 1/√(m+1)（m 为内幕数，平方根递减律）。
