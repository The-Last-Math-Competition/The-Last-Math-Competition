# 00000007585

**English.** Definition: Perturbation stability as the jitter behavior of persistent barcodes under random perturbation. Conjecture: The variance of perturbed barcodes is always controlled by the square of the perturbation amplitude; and the control constant equals the bar-endpoint density. (variance-controlled perturbed barcodes)

**中文。** 定义：加扰稳定性指随机扰动下持久条码的抖动行为。猜想：加扰条码的方差恒由扰动幅的平方控制；控制的常数等于条端点密度。（加扰条码方差控制）
