# 00000008527

**English.** Definition: The differential Galois group of the hypergeometric equation 2F1. Conjecture: The dimension of the differential Galois group of 2F1 is completely classified by the Schwarz table; and the finiteness of the Schwarz table is equivalent to the complete classification of algebraic solutions of 2F1. (2F1 Galois classification)

**中文。** 定义：超几何方程 ₂F₁ 的微分 Galois 群。猜想：₂F₁ 的微分 Galois 群的维数可由 Schwarz 表完全分类；Schwarz 表的有限性等价于 ₂F₁ 代数解的完全分类。（₂F₁ Galois 分类）
