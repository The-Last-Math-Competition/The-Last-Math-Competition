**English.** Definition: The Ornstein-Uhlenbeck operator is the Gaussian perturbation operator on Wiener space. Conjecture: Its invariant measure is unique on abstract Wiener space, and the multiplicities of its spectrum are given by the explicit decomposition of symmetric tensors of the Cameron-Martin space. (OU spectral multiplicity decomposition)
