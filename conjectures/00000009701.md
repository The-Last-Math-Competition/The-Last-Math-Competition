# 00000009701

**SCA-01 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Energy scattering for the three-dimensional cubic defocusing nonlinear Schroedinger equation: the scattering theory of the energy-critical case (the Kenig-Merle dichotomy is proven); the quantitative form of the scattering rate is unknown. Conjecture: Scattering solutions' profiles converge to the linear free evolution at polynomial rate t^{-1-epsilon}, with the exponent optimal; the rate is controlled by an explicit function of the initial energy and the Sobolev norm of the linear profile. (scattering convergence rate)

**中文。** 定义：三维立方 defocusing 非线性薛定谔方程的能量解散射：能量临界情形的散射理论（Kenig–Merle 二分律已证）；散射速率的定量形式未知。猜想：散射解的轮廓收敛到线性自由演化的速率为 t^{−1−ε} 的多项式率且指数最优；速率由初始能量与线性剖面 Sobolev 范数的显式函数控制。（散射收敛速率）
