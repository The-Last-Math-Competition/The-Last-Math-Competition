**English.** Definition: An exploration schedule is the decaying strategy for visiting suboptimal choices. Conjecture: The optimal exploration decay exponent of Bernoulli domains is of the reciprocal-time type, it attains the regret lower-bound constant, and any faster decay construction has a failing example. (optimal exponent law of exploration decay)

**中文。** 定义：探索日程指访问次优选择的衰减策略。猜想：伯努利域的最优探索衰减指数为时间的倒数型,达到悔值下界常数且任何更快衰减构造失败例。（探索衰减最优指数律）
