**English.** Definition: Thermography: the temperature diagram of games. Conjecture: The mast value of the thermograph is unique (a uniqueness law); the unique right vertical is half-integral (a half-integrality law); the star-n exception gives non-integer walls (an exception law); and the exceptions have density zero among random games (a density-zero law). (thermograph mast half-integer star exception)
