# 00000005839

**MR3-09 ★** — source: 180_模表示II.md

**English.** Definition: Block counting is the problem of the number of blocks. Conjecture: The counting formula is the explicit sum of characteristic numbers, defect-zero blocks decided by the modular vanishing of character degrees; the number of defect-zero blocks divides the count of characters whose degree is not divisible by p; and the generating function of the count is a polynomial with nonnegative coefficients. (characteristic-sum formula of block counting)

**中文。** 定义：块计数：块的个数问题。猜想：计数公式为示性数的显式和且亏零块由特征标维数的模消没判定；亏零块的个数整除 p 不整除群阶的特征标数；计数的生成函数为系数非负的多项式。（块计数示性数求和公式）
