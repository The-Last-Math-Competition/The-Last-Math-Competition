**English.** Definition: The angular parameter of a shrinking curve is the evolving phase, with the parametrization of the shrinking circle. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit period of elliptic integrals, diverging at degenerate points; the period asymptotics are odd multiples of pi/2; and the modulus of the elliptic integral tends to 1 at degenerate points, with logarithmic convergence. (elliptic-integral period conversion of shrinking curves)

**中文。** 定义：收缩圈的角参数：演化相位；收缩圆的参数化。猜想：换算公式为椭圆积分的显式周期且退化点处发散；周期的渐近为 π/2 的奇数倍；椭圆积分的模在退化点趋于 1 且收敛为对数型。（收缩圈椭圆积分周期换算）
