# 00000001391

**HT-01 ◆** — source: 32_代数拓扑与同伦论.md

**English.** Definition: π_*^S denotes the stable homotopy groups of spheres; the exponent of the 2-primary part is the largest k for an element of order 2^k. Conjecture: The 2-primary exponent of π_n^S is at most 4·n^{log₂ n/4}; that is, the exponent grows as a log-polynomial in n, uniformly for all n.

**中文。** 定义：π_*^S 为球面稳定同伦群,其 2-准素部分的指数指 2^k 阶元素的最大 k。猜想：π_n^S 的 2-准素指数 ≤ 4·n^{log₂n/4};即指数以 n 的 log-多项式阶增长,界对一切 n 一致。
