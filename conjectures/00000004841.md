**English.** Definition: A hyperbolic group is a finitely generated group whose Cayley graph satisfies the thin-triangle condition, and the boundary is its ideal boundary. Conjecture: The boundary is locally connected if and only if the visual covering dimension of the boundary terminates in finitely many steps along an explicit descending chain, and the criterion is verifiable combinatorially. (visual-chain criterion of boundary local connectivity)

**中文。** 定义：双曲群指凯莱图满足瘦三角形条件的有限生成群,边界指其理想边界。猜想：边界局部连通当且仅当边界的视觉覆盖维数沿显式下降链在有限步终止,判据可由组合验证。（边界局部连通视觉链判据）
