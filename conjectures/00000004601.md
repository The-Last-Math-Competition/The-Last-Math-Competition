**MS2-01 ★** — source: 139_几何测度IV_极小曲面系统.md

**English.** Definition: A minimal partition system is a bubble cluster minimizing total interface area under multiple volume constraints. Conjecture: For a five-phase bubble cluster, the codimension of the interface intersection has the explicit lower bound two, the intersection is itself a minimal submanifold, and the codimension lower bound cannot be improved. (codimension-two lower bound of five-phase intersections)

**中文。** 定义：极小分区系统指多体积约束下极小化总界面积的泡团。猜想：五相泡团的界面交集的余维有显式下界二,交集本身为极小子流形且余维下界不可改进。（五相交集余维二下界）
