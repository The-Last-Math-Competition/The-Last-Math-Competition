# 00000003821

**RC-01 ★** — source: 113_表示论的组合侧_晶体与幺半群.md

**English.** Definition: The Demazure slice Σ_w denotes the finite subset of B(∞) obtained along any reduced word of a Weyl-group element w by requiring nonnegative string parameters, with cardinality d(w). Conjecture: d(w) is independent of the choice of reduced word; and Σ_{w∈W} d(w) q^ℓ(w) is a rational function with denominator a finite product of factors (1−q^k). (Demazure slice cardinality invariant)

**中文。** 定义：Demazure 切片 Σ_w 指对 B(∞) 沿 Weyl 群元 w 的任一简约字取字符串参数非负所得的有限子集,基数记 d(w)。猜想：d(w) 不依赖简约字的选取;且 Σ_{w∈W} d(w) q^ℓ(w) 是分母为 (1−q^k) 型有限乘积的有理函数。（Demazure 切片基数不变量）
