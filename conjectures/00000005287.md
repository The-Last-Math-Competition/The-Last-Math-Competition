**English.** Definition: Symmetrization is the geometric regrouping of variables. Conjecture: Its conversion constant is the explicit geometric mean of the two variables, degenerating to one at equilibrium, and the conversion preserves the energy identity. (geometric-mean energy identity of symmetrization)

**中文。** 定义：对称化指变量的几何重组。猜想：其换算常数为两变量的几何平均显式,平衡态处退化为一旦换算保持能量恒等式。（对称化几何平均能量恒等）
