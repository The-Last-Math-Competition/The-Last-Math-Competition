# 00000008443

**KH3-03 ★** — source: 267_纽结不变量与低维拓扑III.md

**English.** Definition: A periodic knot is a knot with rotation symmetry of prime order p; the Smith constraint is the inequality satisfied by the homology of periodic knots. Conjecture: For p = 2 the Smith constraint cannot be essentially improved; torus-type periodic knots make the constraint exactly tight at the middle homology level, and the equality configurations are exactly the torus family. (equality configurations of the Smith constraint)

**中文。** 定义：周期结指具素数 p 阶旋转对称的结，Smith 约束指周期结的同调所满足的不等式。猜想：p=2 的 Smith 约束不可本质改进；torus 型周期结在中间同调层使约束精确取等，等号构型完全为 torus 族。（Smith 约束等号构型）
