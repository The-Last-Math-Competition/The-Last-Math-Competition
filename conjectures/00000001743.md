# 00000001743

**DG-23 ★** — source: 43_丢番图几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The cubic Veronese curve is the embedding (t, t², t³) in R³. Conjecture: A Khintchine-type theorem holds on this curve: for monotone error functions ψ with Σψ(t) = ∞, almost every t admits infinitely many rational approximations; this follows from the Davenport–Schmidt nondegeneracy condition.

**中文。** 定义：三次 Veronese 曲线指 (t,t²,t³) 在 ℝ³ 的嵌入。猜想：Khintchine 型定理在该曲线上成立:单调误差函数 ψ 满足 Σψ(t)=∞ 时,几乎处处 t 有无穷多有理点逼近;由 Davenport–Schmidt 的非退化条件给出。
