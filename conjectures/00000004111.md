**English.** Definition: The K(pi,1) property is the vanishing of all higher homotopy groups. Conjecture: Deciding the K(pi,1) property for graph configuration spaces can be done in polynomial time, with the criterion being the conjunction of at most 2n explicit cycle-chain conditions. (polynomial K-pi-1 decision for graph configurations)
