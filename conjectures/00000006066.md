**English.** Definition: Differential extension is the linear logic system with differential operators. Conjecture: The Taylor-expansion coefficients are multilinear, the combination of linearity being the resource module; the basis of the resource module is the resource multiset, with polynomial-type basis counting; and the symmetry of Taylor coefficients is given by resource permutations, the kernel of the permutation being multiplicity. (multilinear Taylor resource modules of differential extension)
