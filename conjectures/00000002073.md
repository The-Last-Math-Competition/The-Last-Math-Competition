**English.** Definition: A fully dualizable object is a dual-closed object in the cobordism hypothesis framework. Conjecture: The framed fully dualizable objects of Cat_∞ are exactly locally presentable stable categories; their dual dimension is at most 1; the dimension-1 objects are of the ℓ-projective noncommutative-curve type.

**中文。** 定义：完全可对偶化对象指协边假设框架中的对偶封闭对象。猜想：Cat_∞ 中 framed fully dualizable 对象恰为局部 presentable 稳定范畴;其对偶维数 ≤1;维数 1 的对象为 ℓ-投射的非交换曲线型。
