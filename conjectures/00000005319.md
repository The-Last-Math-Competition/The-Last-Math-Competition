# 00000005319

**English.** Definition: Asymptotic classes are equivalence classes in the large-parameter limit, and class multiplication is the multiplicative structure of asymptotic decompositions. Conjecture: The genus table of class multiplication is an explicit sign function of degree differences, positive on classical families; the sign flip occurs only at difference 1 and is unique; and associativity of class multiplication is preserved in the limit, with associativity deficit exponentially small. (genus sign function of asymptotic multiplication)

**中文。** 定义：渐近类：大参数极限下的等价类；类乘法指渐近分解的乘性结构。猜想：类乘法的亏格表为次数差的显式符号函数且经典族处符号为正；符号翻转仅在差为 1 处出现且翻转为唯一；类乘法的结合性在极限中保持且结合亏损为指数小。（渐近乘法亏格符号函数）
