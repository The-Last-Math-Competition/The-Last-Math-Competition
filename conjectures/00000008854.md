# 00000008854

**English.** Definition: The generalization of projected gradient. Conjecture: The convergence rate of generalized projections is always O(1/t); and the optimal step size is the midpoint of the spectral interval of the metric matrix. (generalized projection spectral midpoint)

**中文。** 定义：投影梯度的一般化。猜想：广义投影的收敛率恒为 O(1/t)；最优步长由度量矩阵的谱区间中点给出。（广义投影谱中点）
