# 00000002585

**English.** Definition: The parametrized complexity of simplicial-complex homology uses the treewidth of the triangulation as the parameter. Conjecture: The cone-obstruction constant is a combinatorial quantity equal to two; the saturation of the constant is attained on the topological side of complete bipartite graphs. (cone obstruction constant two combinatorial)
