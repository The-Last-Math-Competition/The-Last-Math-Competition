**SFL-05 ★** — source: 261_随机滤波与粗糙路径.md

**English.** Definition: The unification of the Ito-Stratonovich correction in rough paths: the correction corr_IS of rough integration. Conjecture: The explicit form of corr_IS (the correction term) is (1/2) times the divergence of the diffusion tensor applied to [X,X] prime (a divergence correction formula); the vanishing of the correction (unification of semimartingales and geometric rough paths) holds if and only if the antisymmetric part of the quadratic variation of the path vanishes (an antisymmetric vanishing criterion); and the numerical error of the correction (order of discrete approximation) is the fractional order h^{2 alpha - 1} (a fractional error order law), alpha the roughness. (Ito-Stratonovich divergence antisymmetric criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Itô 与 Stratonovich 的修正项的粗糙路径的统一：粗糙积分的 Itô-Stratonovich 修正 corr_IS。猜想：corr_IS（修正项的显式）= (1/2)·[X,X]' 的扩散张量的散度（散度修正公式）；且修正的消失（半鞅与几何粗糙路径的统一）当且仅当路径的二次变差的反对称部分消失（反对称消失判据）；修正项的数值误差（离散近似的阶）为 h^{2α-1} 的分数阶（分数误差阶律），α 为粗糙度。
