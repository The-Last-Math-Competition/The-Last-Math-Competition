# 00000006501

**English.** Definition: Universal characteristic classes with classifying spaces. Conjecture: The universal classes classify the classifying space of classes, and the combination of the classes is the symmetric series. (symmetric-series classification of universal characteristic classes)

**中文。** 定义：通用示性类：分类空间。猜想：通用的类：类空间的分类且类的组合为对称级数。（通用示性类分类空间对称级数类空间组合通用对称级数分类空间级数对称分类空间类空间级数）
