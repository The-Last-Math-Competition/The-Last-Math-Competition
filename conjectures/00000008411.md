# 00000008411

**English.** Definition: A t-(v,k,lambda) design is a block system on a v-point set such that every t-element subset is contained in exactly lambda blocks. Conjecture: For fixed t at least 3 there are only finitely many isomorphism classes of designs whose automorphism group is t-transitive; the classification table consists of projective space designs, Hanani triple systems, and at most three exceptions, all contained among the sporadic designs known in the literature. (finite classification of highly transitive designs)

**中文。** 定义：t-(v,k,λ) 设计指 v 点集上使每个 t 元子集恰含于 λ 个区组的区组系。猜想：固定 t ≥ 3 的自同构群为 t-传递的设计仅有有限个同构类；分类表由射影空间设计、Hanani 三元系与至多三个例外组成，例外均含于文献已知的 sporadic 设计。（高传递设计有限分类）
