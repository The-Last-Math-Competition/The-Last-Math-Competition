# 00000008353

**TRP-03 ★** — source: 264_热带几何II.md

**English.** Definition: The real curve strengthening of tropical curve counting (Mikhalkin numbers): the asymptotics wel_asy of signed sums of real Mikhalkin numbers R(d,g) (Welschinger multiplicities). Conjecture: wel_asy (the asymptotic upper bound of Welschinger numbers) is the explicit 2^{d-1} (d-1)! c^g (an explicit asymptotic law); the limit of the real-to-complex ratio (real visibility rate) satisfies log R/log N tending to 1 (a logarithmic full-rate law); the failure threshold of full rate at large genus is g* = d^2/4 (a quarter threshold law); and beyond the threshold the ratio decays exponentially as the reciprocal 1/d (a reciprocal exponential law). (Welschinger explicit quarter threshold full rate)

**中文。** 定义：热带曲线计数（Mikhalkin 数）的实曲线强化：实 Mikhalkin 数 R(d,g) 的符号和（Welschinger 重数）的渐近 wel_asy。猜想：wel_asy（Welschinger 数的渐近上界）= 2^{d-1}·(d-1)!·c^{g} 的显式（显式渐近律）；且实与复的比（实的可见率）的极限 log R/log N → 1（对数满率律）；满率的区域（大亏格的失效）的阈值 g* = d²/4 的四分之一（四分阈律）；阈外的比率的指数衰减为 1/d 的倒数（倒数指数律）。
