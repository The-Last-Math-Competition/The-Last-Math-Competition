# 00000009721

**SCA-21 ★** — source: 309_非线性偏微分方程的散射.md

**English.** Definition: Scattering with random initial data: the probabilistic global theory of randomized data. Conjecture: Energy-supercritical defocusing NLS with randomized initial data always scatters with probability one; and the scattering probability is independent of the supercritical exponent. (random supercritical scattering)

**中文。** 定义：随机初值散射指随机化数据的概率整体理论。猜想：能量超临界 defocusing NLS 的随机化初值恒以概率一散射；且散射概率与超临界指数无关。（随机超临界散射）
