**English.** Definition: Factorial primes are p# ± 1 (p# the product of all primes up to p). Conjecture: The mean of the indicator that p#+1 is prime is asymptotically a corrected c/log²p with an explicit rational sieve constant; simultaneously, pairs for which both p#+1 and p#−1 are prime are only finitely many, and the list is explicitly closed by collisions of covering congruence systems. (factorial prime sieve constant)
