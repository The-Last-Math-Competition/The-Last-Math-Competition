**English.** Definition: Measure-theoretic tests of integrability: the existence of invariant measures. Conjecture: The density of invariant measures: the density of a multiplicative invariant measure of a map is the inverse of the Jacobian determinant, and absolute continuity of the inverse is rational invariance. (invariant measure density absolute continuity)

**中文。** 定义：可积性的测度论检验：不变测度的存在。猜想：不变测度的密度：映射的乘性不变测度的密度为 Jacobian 行列式的逆且逆的绝对连续性为有理不变性。（不变测度密度绝对连续）
