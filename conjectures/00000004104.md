# 00000004104

**English.** Definition: The surface braid group is the braid group on a surface. Conjecture: The center of the n-particle surface braid group on a torus of genus g is infinite cyclic, a generator has exponent 2g - 2 + n, and this agrees with the exponent in the center of the pure braid group. (center exponent formula of torus braids)

**中文。** 定义：表面辫群指曲面上的辫群。猜想：亏格 g 环面上 n 粒子表面辫群的中心为无限循环,生成元的指数为 2g − 2 + n,与纯辫群中心的指数一致。（环面辫中心指数公式）
