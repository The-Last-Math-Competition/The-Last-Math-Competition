# 00000009971

**NCR-01 ★** — source: 318_非交换环的分类.md

**English.** Definition: Artin-Schelter regular algebras: graded algebras of bijective Hilbert series and finite global dimension; the four-dimensional Noetherian classification is incomplete. Conjecture: The Hilbert series list of Noetherian AS-regular four-dimensional algebras is finite and of two types: commutative polynomial series and elliptic-type series; the parameter space of each type is a finite-dimensional explicit variety, with no third class. (AS-regular four-dimensional classification)

**中文。** 定义：Artin–Schelter 正则代数：分次双射 Hilbert 级数且整体维数有限的分次代数；四维 Noetherian 分类未完成。猜想：Noetherian AS 正则四维代数的 Hilbert 级数列表有限且列表为两种类型：交换多项式级数与椭圆型级数；每型的参数空间为有限维显式簇且无第三类。（AS 正则四维分类）
