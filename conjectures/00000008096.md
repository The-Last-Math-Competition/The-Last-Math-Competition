# 00000008096

**FUS-16 ★** — source: 255_有限群与融合系统.md

**English.** Definition: The transfer maps of subgroups of a fusion system: the fusion-invariant kernel ver_F of the transfer map ver: S to S/Phi(S). Conjecture: The rank of the image of ver_F(S) is at least rank(S) - 1 (a transfer-image rank lower bound); the triviality of the transfer kernel is equivalent to F being controlled (a control-transfer duality); and the minimal non-group-realized controlled fusion (exotic controlled) is a rank-3 2-system (the minimal exotic controlled dimension), whose block-theoretic counterpart (the inertial quotient of the block) is A_4 (a quaternionic inertial-quotient rigidity). (transfer control exotic A_4 quotient)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的子群的转移映射：transfer 映射 ver: S→S/Φ(S) 的融合不变核 ver_F。猜想：ver_F(S) 的像的秩 ≥ rank(S)-1（转移像秩下界）；且转移核的平凡性等价于 F 为 controlled（控制-转移对偶）；controlled fusion 的极小非群实现（exotic controlled）为秩 3 的 2-系统（极小 exotic controlled 维数），该系统的块理论对应（block 的 inertial 商）为 A_4（四元不变商刚性）。
