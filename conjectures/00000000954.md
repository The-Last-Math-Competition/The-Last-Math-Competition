# 00000000954

**OA-54 ★** — source: 25_算子代数与泛函分析.md

**English.** Definition: The generic measurement number for phase retrieval is the smallest m such that m generic linear measurements determine vectors in R^n or C^n up to phase. Conjecture: The complex case m = 4n−4 is optimal (known) and the real case m = 2n−1 is optimal (known); new part: if measurements are restricted to m generic pairs of orthogonal planes, the complex optimum drops to 2n, and this threshold cannot be lowered further.

**中文。** 定义：相位恢复的泛型测量数指使 m 个泛型线性测量在相差相位下唯一确定 R^n 或 C^n 中向量的最小 m。猜想：复情形 m=4n−4 为最优(已知),实情形 m=2n−1 为最优(已知);新部分:允许测量限制在 m 个泛型平面正交对时,复情形最优降至 2n,且该阈值不可再降。
