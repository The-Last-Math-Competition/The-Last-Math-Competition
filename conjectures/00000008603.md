# 00000008603

**English.** Definition: Covariate shift means the training and test distributions differ. Conjecture: The excess risk under shift is always controlled by the second moment of the density ratio; and when the shift is unidentifiable, every estimator has an explicit lower bound on its excess risk. (covariate shift excess risk characterization)

**中文。** 定义：协变量漂移指训练与测试分布不同。猜想：漂移下的过剩风险恒由密度比二阶矩控制；且漂移不可识别时任何估计子的过剩风险有显式下界。（漂移过剩风险刻画）
