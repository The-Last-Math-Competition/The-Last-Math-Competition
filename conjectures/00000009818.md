**English.** Definition: Poisson-Voronoi statistics: Lewis's law, the linear relation between cell area and edge count. Conjecture: The mean cell area of Poisson-Voronoi tessellations is a linear function of edge count (Lewis's law), with proportionality constant an explicit value of the reciprocal intensity; the second-order correction is negative and given by the leading term of the Edgeworth expansion. (Lewis law rigorous proof)

**中文。** 定义：Poisson–Voronoi 镖 STAT: Lewis 律：胞面积与边数的线性关系。猜想：Poisson–Voronoi 镶嵌的平均胞面积为边数的线性函数（Lewis 律）且比例常数为强度倒数的显式值；二阶修正为负且由 Edgeworth 展开的首项给出。（Lewis 律严格证明）
