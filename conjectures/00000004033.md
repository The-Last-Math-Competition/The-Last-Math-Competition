# 00000004033

**English.** Definition: The tau-tilting lattice is the poset of support tau-tilting modules, and the Cambrian structure is its correspondence with cosets of the Weyl group. Conjecture: Its automorphism group is isomorphic to the semidirect product of the partial automorphism group of the quiver with an anti-involution, and the formula holds for all cluster-finite types. (tau-Cambrian automorphism formula)

**中文。** 定义：τ-倾斜格指支撑 τ-倾斜模的偏序格,Cambrian 结构指与 Weyl 群陪集系的对应。猜想：其自同构群同构于箭图偏自同构群与反对合的半直积,公式对一切簇有限型成立。（τ-Cambrian 自同构公式）
