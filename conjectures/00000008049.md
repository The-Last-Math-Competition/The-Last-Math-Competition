**English.** Definition: The taming threshold of the model-theoretic Ramsey property: the o-minimal realization threshold Ram(R) of the Ramsey property of a structure R (the Nesetril-class Ramsey property). Conjecture: R_an,exp does not have the structural Ramsey property (a Ramsey obstruction for ordered structures); the minimal structure with the Ramsey obstruction is the pure order structure (R,<) (the non-Ramsey minimality of linear orders); the tamed Ramsey property (the distal Ramsey property of distal structures) holds in all distal structures (Ramsey universality of distal taming); and the epsilon-inversion of the distal regularization law is explicit of Legendre-function type (epsilon^{-1} a hyper-exponential lower bound of the distal kernel). (distal Ramsey universality threshold)
