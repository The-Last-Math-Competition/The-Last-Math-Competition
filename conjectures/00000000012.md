# 00000000012

**English.** Definition: The short-interval form of the Chebotarev density theorem. Conjecture: For every number field K, conjugacy class C, and θ greater than one half, the deviation of the Chebotarev count in short intervals from its main term is an explicit power of x divided by log. (short-interval Chebotarev)

**中文。** 定义：Chebotarev 密度定理的短区间形式。猜想：对每个数域 K、共轭类 C 与 θ 大于二分之一，短区间内的 Chebotarev 计数与主项之差为 x 除以 log 的显式幂。（短区间 Chebotarev）
