# 00000005606

**English.** Definition: Relevant vectors are the normal vectors of Voronoi cell vertices. Conjecture: The three-dimensional count is explicit in the dumbbell graph of the quadratic-form field, with vertex count two polynomial invariants; the edge count of the dumbbell graph is a constant multiple of the vertex count, with multiplier 3; and the connectivity of the graph is equivalent to the class number of the field being one. (dumbbell-graph count of three-dimensional relevant vectors)

**中文。** 定义：相关向量：胞腔（Voronoi）顶点的法向量。猜想：三维的计数为二次型域的哑铃图显式且顶点数为两个多项式不变量；哑铃图的边数为顶点数的常数倍且倍数为 3；图的连通性等价于域的类数为一。（三维哑铃图相关向量计数）
