# 00000001093

**FG-93 ★** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: Equivalence classes of Hadamard matrices are normalized by row/column signs and permutations. Conjecture: There is a unique equivalence class of Hadamard matrices of order 12; exactly 5 classes of order 16; and exactly 60 of order 24.

**中文。** 定义：Hadamard 矩阵的等价类由行列符号与置换归一化。猜想：12 阶 Hadamard 矩阵的等价类唯一;16 阶恰有 5 个等价类,24 阶恰有 60 个等价类。
