# 00000007720

**English.** Definition: The substitution matrix M induces a linear map M* on H^1(Omega_sigma), whose spectrum is the homological spectrum. Conjecture: The intersection of the homological and dynamical spectra is exactly the set of eigenvalues of M of modulus greater than 1; the pure-point part of the dynamical spectrum outside the homological spectrum consists of weak peaks with vanishing Bragg intensities, and the frequency set of weak peaks is dense in reciprocal space if and only if the group generated by the eigenvalues of M has rank at least 2. (homological-dynamical spectral intersection)

**中文。** 定义：铺砌空间的同调群作用：替换矩阵 M 诱导 H^1(Ω_σ) 上的线性映射 M^*，其谱称为同调谱。猜想：同调谱与动力谱的交集恰为 M 的模长 >1 的特征值全体；动力谱中不属于同调谱的纯点部分由 Bragg 峰强度极限为零的弱峰构成，且弱峰的频率集在倒易空间中稠密当且仅当替换矩阵的特征值生成的群秩 ≥2。
