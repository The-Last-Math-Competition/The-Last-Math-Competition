**WEX-25 ★** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: Weighted Poincare: the duality inequality between weights and capacities. Conjecture: The weighted Poincare constant equals the dual quotient of the p-capacity of the domain by the weight energy; the explicit formula for the quotient is exact in the Muckenhoupt weight class, attained by radial weights. (Poincare-capacity duality)

**中文。** 定义：加权 Poincaré：权与容度的对偶不等式。猜想：加权 Poincaré 常数等于区域的 p-容度与权能量的对偶商；商的显式公式在 Muckenhoupt 权类精确且由径向权达到。（Poincare-容度对偶）
