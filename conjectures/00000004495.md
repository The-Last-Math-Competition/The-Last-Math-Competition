# 00000004495

**English.** Definition: Cubical type theory is the combinatorial theory with the cubical interval as paths, and the regularity axiom is the consistency axiom of the cubical structure. Conjecture: Its smallest model is an explicit modification of the cubical-simplicial adjoint pair, the modified model verifies the regularity axiom and has minimal size. (simplicial correction of the smallest cubical model)

**中文。** 定义：立方类型论指以立方区间为路径的组合理论,正则公理指立方结构一致性公理。猜想：其最小模型为立方-单纯伴随对的显式修正,修正模型验证正则公理且尺寸最小。（立方最小模型单纯修正）
