# 00000005802

**English.** Definition: The monotone quantity is the Gaussian-weighted entropy-type functional. Conjecture: The optimal conversion formula is the explicit integral of the Gaussian density difference, zero at self-similar points, and the gradient estimate is exact. (Gaussian density-difference integral formula of monotone quantities)
