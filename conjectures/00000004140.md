**English.** Definition: Consistency strength is the preorder of consistency implications between axioms. Conjecture: There exist two axioms of the same consistency strength with different Pi_1_2 consequence sets, and the smallest example is given by two natural variants of the existence of a Suslin tree. (minimal strength-consequence separation example)
