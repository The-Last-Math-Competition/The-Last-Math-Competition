**OS3-23 ◆** — source: 204_算子分裂法_数值PDEII.md

**English.** Definition: The fractional solution scheme of equation splitting. Conjecture: Of three conserved quantities two are preserved and the third drifts explicitly; the compensation of the drift is the correction step; the weight of the correction step is given by the drift norm; and the bound of the drift norm is controlled by the square of the splitting step. (two-preserving drift formula of conservation)

**中文。** 定义：方程分裂的分步求解格式。猜想：三守恒量保二且第三漂移显式；且漂移的补偿为修正步；且修正步的权重由漂移范数给出；且漂移范数的界由分裂步长平方控制。（守恒保二漂移公式）
