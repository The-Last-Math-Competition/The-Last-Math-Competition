# 00000005384

**English.** Definition: Parity decomposition is the carry-structure decomposition of heights. Conjecture: The conversion constant of the times-one map is an explicit multiple of the orbit branch count, two for the full tree, and the conversion preserves carry positivity. (full-tree multiple conversion of parity heights)

**中文。** 定义：奇偶分解指高度的进位结构分解。猜想：倍一映射的换算常数为轨道分支数的显式倍,满树时为二且换算保持进位正性。（奇偶高度满树倍数换算）
