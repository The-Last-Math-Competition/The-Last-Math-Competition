**English.** Definition: A two-level Frobenius structure is the two-level bicommutative structure. Conjecture: In the binary case it is closed by snake relations, the relations are complete, and the verification of completeness is the adjunction law. (snake-relation completeness of two-level Frobenius structures)

**中文。** 定义：双层 Frobenius 结构为双层交换余交换结构。猜想：二元时由蛇关系闭合且关系完备；且完备的验证为伴随律。（双层 Frobenius 蛇关系完备）
