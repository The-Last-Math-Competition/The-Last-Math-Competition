**English.** Definition: Extended Frege is the Frege system augmented with extension variables. Conjecture: Any propositional proof system that polynomially simulates IPS also polynomially simulates extended Frege, so eF and IPS form the two main axes of the simulation order and meet at this point. (eF-IPS simulation meeting)

**中文。** 定义：extended Frege 指附扩展变量的 Frege 系统。猜想：任何多项式模拟 IPS 的命题证明系统亦多项式模拟 extended Frege,即 eF 与 IPS 构成模拟偏序的两条主轴并在此交汇。（eF-IPS 模拟交汇）
