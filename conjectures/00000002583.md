**CM-23 ★** — source: 71_计算复杂性中的数学.md

**English.** Definition: Circuit polynomials are the polynomial generators of the tangent cone. Conjecture: The minimal number of circuit polynomials of an explicit affine variety is degree times codimension; and the realization of the count is on quadratic varieties. (circuit polynomial degree-codimension product)

**中文。** 定义：电路多项式为切锥的多项式生成。猜想：显式仿射簇的电路多项式最小数为度乘余维；且计数的实现为二次簇。（电路多项式度余维乘积）
