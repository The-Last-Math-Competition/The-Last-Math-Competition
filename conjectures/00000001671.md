# 00000001671

**GT-11 ★** — source: 41_图论III.md

**English.** Definition: The basis number of a graph is the maximal edge multiplicity in a Mac Lane basis of its cycle space. Conjecture: The basis number of K_n is ⌈(n+3)/2⌉; the known lower bound matches, and the gap of 1 in the upper bound is closed by the standard basis of the cycle space.

**中文。** 定义：图的基础数指其循环空间的 Mac Lane 基中边最大重数。猜想：K_n 的基础数为 ⌈(n+3)/2⌉;已知下界与之匹配,上界差 1 的缺口由循环空间的标准基闭合。
