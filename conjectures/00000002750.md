# 00000002750

**English.** Definition: Locally bounded exponent (PI exponent): the asymptotic constant measuring closeness. Conjecture: Arithmeticity of the exponent: the PI exponent of an algebra is a half-integer, with the denominator the commutator rank of generating matrix pairs; the jump set of exponents is a finite lattice, with gaps controlled by the inflection points of Regev's symmetrization constants. (PI exponent half-integer rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：局部有界指数（PI exponent）：指数为接近度量的渐近常数。猜想：指数的算术性：PI 代数的指数为半整数且半整性的分母为生成矩阵对的交换子秩；指数的跳跃集为有限格且格的间隙由 Regev 对称化常数的拐点控制。（PI 指数半整刚性）
