# 00000005403

**English.** Definition: The random conversion is the Mobius analogue under a random model with independent zeros. Conjecture: The two-dimensional random conversion is always the explicit binomial count of unions of independent zeros; the expectation of the count is of hypergeometric type, with variance bounded by the binomial variance; and the deviation between the simulation and the true conversion decays exponentially with the period, with decay rate at least the soliton spectral gap. (binomial counting of random zero unions)

**中文。** 定义：随机换算：随机模型（独立零点）下的默比乌斯模拟。猜想：二维随机换算恒为独立零点并的显式二项计数；计数的期望为超几何型且方差为二项方差的上界；模拟与真实换算的偏差随周期以指数衰减且衰减率不小于孤立子谱隙。（随机零点并二项计数）
