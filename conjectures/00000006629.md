**English.** Definition: The chessboard estimate is the combinatorial corollary of positivity. Conjecture: The extremal configuration is of constant-diagonal type and unique; the verification of uniqueness is blockwise compression; and the extremal of blockwise compression is the diagonal chessboard. (unique diagonal chessboard extremum)

**中文。** 定义：棋盘估计为正性的组合推论。猜想：极值构型为常数对角型且唯一；且唯一的验证为逐块压缩；且逐块压缩的极值为对角棋盘。（棋盘极值对角唯一）
