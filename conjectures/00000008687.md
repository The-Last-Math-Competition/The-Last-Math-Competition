**English.** Definition: The Gromov-Wasserstein distance is the coupling distance of metric measure spaces. Conjecture: Computing GW is NP-hard with approximation window given by spectral relaxation; and the kernel of the relaxation is the graph Laplacian. (GW spectral relaxation Laplacian NP-hardness)

**中文。** 定义：Gromov–Wasserstein 距离为度量测度空间的耦合距离。猜想：GW 的计算为 NP 困难且近似窗为谱松弛；且松弛的核为图的拉普拉斯。（GW-谱松弛拉普拉斯 NP 困难）
