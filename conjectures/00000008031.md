# 00000008031

**English.** Definition: The taming of the Assouad dimension of definable metric spaces: the Assouad dimension dim_A(X,d) of a definable set X with definable metric d. Conjecture: dim_A is integral or non-integral (non-integral values allowed, breaking pure topological dimension) but controlled by definable approximation sequences of the upper and lower Minkowski dimensions; and the distribution spectrum of the values of dim_A (ranging over all definable metrics) is the full spectrum [0,n] (complete realization); the minimal model of realization is the definable family of snowflake metrics x^alpha, with the value spectrum of alpha a dense subset of (0,1) (transcendental parameters excluded from realization, algebraic parameters fully realized). (definable snowflake Assouad full spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：可定义度量空间的 Assouad 维数的驯化：可定义集 X 配可定义度量 d 的 Assouad 维数 dim_A(X,d)。猜想：dim_A 为整数或非整数（允许非整数，突破纯拓扑维数）但受控于 upper/lower Minkowski 维数的可定义逼近序列；且 dim_A 的值的分布谱（遍历一切可定义度量）为 [0,n] 的全谱（完全实现）；实现的最小模型为 snowflake 度量 x^α 的可定义族，α 的取值谱为介于 0 与 1 之间的稠密子集（超越参数的实现排除，代数参数的完全实现）。
