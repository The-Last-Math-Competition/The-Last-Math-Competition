# 00000006208

**English.** Definition: Pole dynamics is the flow of the poles, the discrete eigenvalues, of inverse scattering. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit law of pole dynamics, the eigenvalues stationary under the soliton flow; the normalization constants evolve exponentially, the exponent twice the real part of the eigenvalue; and pole collisions are elastic under the merging of new flows, with elasticity constant 1. (stationary-pole exponential-normalization conversion)

**中文。** 定义：极点动力学：逆散射的极点（离散特征值）的流。猜想：换算常数为极点动力学的显式律且特征值在孤子流下不动；归一常数指数演化且指数为特征值的实部两倍；极点的碰撞在新流的合并下弹性且弹性常数为 1。（极点不动归一指数换算）
