# 00000004064

**English.** Definition: Identifiability of a normal form is the well-posedness of recovering unfolding parameters from finite-order jet data. Conjecture: The normal form of a codimension-k bifurcation is identifiable if and only if k <= 3, and from codimension 4 on there exist parameter combinations that are never identifiable. (codimension threshold 3 of identifiability)

**中文。** 定义：规范形的可辨识性指由有限阶 jet 数据反解开折参数的适定性。猜想：余维 k 分叉的规范形可辨识当且仅当 k ≤ 3,余维 4 起存在恒不可辨识的参数组合。（可辨识性的余维阈值 3）
