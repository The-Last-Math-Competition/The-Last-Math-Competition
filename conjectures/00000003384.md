**English.** Definition: Noncommutative coverings: the operatorization of covering maps. Conjecture: Noncommutative deck transformations: the operatorization of the deck group of a covering is a crossed product, with the index of the product the branching count of the covering. (noncommutative deck crossed product branch index)
