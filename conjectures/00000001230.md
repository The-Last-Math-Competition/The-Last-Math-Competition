# 00000001230

**CG-30 ★** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Jac(K_{m,n}) is the sandpile group (Jacobian) of the complete bipartite graph. Conjecture: The Jacobian of the n-dimensional grid graph [m₁]×…×[m_n] is isomorphic to the explicit decomposition ⊕_{S}(Z/gcd(S))^{e(S)} over subsets S of the m_i; the two-dimensional case is given explicitly by the K_{m,n} formula and torus gluing.

**中文。** 定义：Jac(K_{m,n}) 为完全二部图的沙堆群(雅可比群)。猜想：n 维网格图 [m₁]×…×[m_n] 的雅可比群同构于 ⊕_{S} (Z/gcd(S))^{e(S)} 的显式分解,S 取 m_i 的子集;二维情形由 K_{m,n} 公式与环面粘合显式给出。
