# 00000009937

**English.** Definition: Vanishing thresholds of random complexes: homology vanishing in the Linial-Meshulam model. Conjecture: The critical window width of homology vanishing shrinks as a logarithmic power of n, with window exponent the reciprocal of dimension minus one; the phase transition curve inside the window is an explicit Gumbel-type distribution function. (vanishing window shrinkage exponent)

**中文。** 定义：随机复形的消没阈值：Linial–Meshulam 模型的同调消没。猜想：同调消没的临界窗口宽度按 n 的对数幂收缩且窗口指数为维数减一的倒数；窗口内的相变曲线为显式的 Gumbel 型分布函数。（消没窗口收缩指数）
