**English.** Definition: The Drinfeld limit of q-TASEP: the integrable limit of the moment structure. Conjecture: The generating functions of q-TASEP moments converge in the scaling limit to the Drinfeld curve representation of the quantum torus algebra; the spectrum of the central elements of the representation agrees with the label set of the Airy line ensemble. (q-TASEP Drinfeld spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：q-TASEP 的 Drinfeld 极限：矩结构的可积极限。猜想：q-TASEP 的矩的生成函数在标度极限收敛到 quantum torus 代数的 Drinfeld 曲线表示；表示的中心元谱与 Airy 线系综的标签集一致。（q-TASEP Drinfeld 谱）
