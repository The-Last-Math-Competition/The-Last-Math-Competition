**English.** Definition: The difference distance of independent families is the symmetric-difference metric structure. Conjecture: The difference distance of independent families always has multiplicative structure with coefficient one, and the degree of failure of multiplicativity is the correlation coefficient. (unit multiplicative-difference coefficient of independent families)

**中文。** 定义：独立族的差距离为对称差度量结构。猜想：独立族的差距离恒具乘性结构且系数为一；且乘性的破坏度为相关系数。（独立族乘性差系数一）
