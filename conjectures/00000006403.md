**SD3-03 ★** — source: 199_几何测度V_对称差问题.md

**English.** Definition: difference distance of independent family is symmetric difference metric structure. Conjecture: difference distance of independent family always multiplicative structure and coefficient is one; and of multiplicative is correlated coefficient. (independent family multiplicative difference coefficient one)

**中文。** 定义：独立族的差距离为对称差度量结构。猜想：独立族的差距离恒具乘性结构且系数为一；且乘性的破坏度为相关系数。（独立族乘性差系数一）
