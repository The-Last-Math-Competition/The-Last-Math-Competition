**English.** Definition: The Modica-Mortola slope parameter is the gradient-bound constant of the Gamma-convergence. Conjecture: In the weighted generalization the sharp constant is given explicitly by the weighted arc length of the potential, and for any positive Borel weight the constant is attainable and cannot be improved. (weighted sharp Modica-Mortola constant)

**中文。** 定义：Modica–Mortola 斜率参数指 Γ-收敛的梯度界常数。猜想：加权推广情形的 sharp 常数由势函数的加权弧长显式给出,权为任意正 Borel 函数时常数可达且不可改进。（MM 加权 sharp 常数）
