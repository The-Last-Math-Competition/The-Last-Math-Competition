# 00000003053

**IP-13 ★** — source: 87_非线性PDE的可积检验.md

**English.** Definition: The existence test for recursion operators: strong symmetry. Conjecture: The spectral polynomial of recursion operators: the spectral polynomial of a strong symmetry R coincides with that of the Lax matrix, and the breaking of the coincidence is a certificate of nonintegrability. (recursion-spectrum consistency certificate)

**中文。** 定义：递推算子的存在性检验：强对称性。猜想：递推算子的谱多项式：强对称 R 的谱多项式与 Lax 矩阵的谱多项式一致且一致性的破坏为不可积的证书。（递推-谱一致性证书）
