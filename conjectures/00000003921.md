# 00000003921

**FL-11 ★** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: HF(L_0, L_1) is the Floer homology of the intersection points of two Lagrangian submanifolds. Conjecture: For a monotone spin Lagrangian, the rank of HF(L, L) is at least the sum of the Betti numbers beta_i(L) minus an explicit boundary-obstruction correction term, and the correction term vanishes for real projective space. (sharp rank lower bound for Lagrangian HF)

**中文。** 定义：HF(L₀,L₁) 指两 Lagrange 子流形交点的 Floer 同调。猜想：单调自旋 Lagrange 的 HF(L,L) 的秩不小于 Σβᵢ(L) 减去边界障碍的显式修正项;对 real 射影空间修正项为零。（Lagrangian HF 秩的精确下界）
