# 00000002263

**MG-03 ★** — source: 61_鞅与随机分析.md

**English.** Definition: Optimal stopping maximizes E payoff over stopping times. Conjecture: The optimal boundary for Brownian motion with drift and linear cost is an explicit concatenation of parabolic arcs (stopping boundary); and the concatenation points solve the free-boundary equation of the drift-to-cost ratio.

**中文。** 定义：最优停止(停时最大化 E payoff)。猜想：带漂移 Brown 与线性代价的最优边界的显式形式为抛物线段拼接(停时边界)；且拼接点由漂移与代价比的自由边界方程解出。
