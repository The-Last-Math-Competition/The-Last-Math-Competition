# 00000001172

**English.** Definition: The quasi-R-matrix (Drinfeld). Conjecture: The components of the quasitriangular structure of a quantum dual pair belong to the saturation of the completed Z[q^{±1/2}] (integrality of saturation).

**中文。** 定义：quasi-R-matrix(Drinfeld)。猜想：量子对偶对的 quasitriangular 结构的分量属于完备化的 ℤ[q^{±1/2}] 的饱和(整饱和性)。
