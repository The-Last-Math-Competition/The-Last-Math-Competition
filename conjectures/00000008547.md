**English.** Definition: A Peck lattice is a rank-symmetric strictly Sperner lattice. Conjecture: The Young lattice of partitions is Peck; and the characteristic polynomials of all geometric Peck lattices are symmetric with the symmetry midpoint at half the rank. (Peck lattice characteristic symmetry)

**中文。** 定义：格的 peck 指秩对称且严格单射格。猜想：分拆格 Young 格为 peck；一切几何 peck 格的特征多项式对称且对称中点为其半秩。（peck 格特征对称）
