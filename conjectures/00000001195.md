# 00000001195

**LT-95 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: The inner (Kronecker) product of symmetric functions. Conjecture: ⟨p_λ, p_μ * p_ν⟩ = z_λ·δ with lexicographic counting (Kronecker power-sum orthogonality).

**中文。** 定义：对称函数的内部积(Kronecker)。猜想：⟨p_λ, p_μ * p_ν⟩ = z_λ·δ 的字典计数(Kronecker 幂和正交)。
