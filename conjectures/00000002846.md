**English.** Definition: The statistical side of rational normal curves: the algebraic geometry of binomial mixtures. Conjecture: Binomial mixture varieties: the degree of the parametrization image of k Bernoulli mixtures is the partition function of min(k, d), and the classification of singular points is by intersections of Veronese subvarieties. (binomial mixture variety degree)

**中文。** 定义：有理正常曲线的统计侧：二项混合的代数几何。猜想：二项混合簇：k 个伯努利混合的参数化像的次数为 min(k, d) 的分拆函数且像的蜜饯点的分类为 Veronese 子簇的交。（二项混合簇次数）
