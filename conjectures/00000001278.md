**English.** Definition: The zeta function of a symbolic system is the generating function of periodic-orbit counts. Conjecture: The zeta discriminant of a homotopy-conjugacy class of SFTs divides an exponential sum; and the quotient is the characteristic polynomial of a cyclic cover. (zeta discriminant divisibility)
