**G4-16 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: Multigraph isomorphism is the isomorphism decision problem for graphs allowing multiple edges. Conjecture: Multigraph isomorphism and simple graph isomorphism are equivalent in n^{polylog n} time; multigraph isomorphism for treewidth t costs 2^{O(t)} * n, with edge multiplicities contributing only a linear factor. (cost-free extension of multigraph isomorphism)

**中文。** 定义：多重图同构指允许重边的图的同构判定。猜想：多重图同构与素图同构在 n^{polylog n} 时间内等价完成;树宽 t 的多重图同构为 2^{O(t)}·n,重数仅贡献线性因子。（多重图同构无代价扩张）
