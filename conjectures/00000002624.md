# 00000002624

**MF-04 ★** — source: 73_模形式与Jacobi形式II.md

**English.** Definition: The Eichler–Zagier correspondence: a bijection between index-1 Jacobi forms and half-integral-weight modular forms. Conjecture: The high-index extension of the Eichler–Zagier correspondence: index-m Jacobi cusp forms are isomorphic to a subspace of the weight k−1/2 cusp space of Γ₀(m), whose orthogonal complement has dimension the number of prime factors of m. (high index EZ subspace)

**中文。** 定义：Eichler–Zagier 对应：index 1 Jacobi 形式与半整权模形式的双射。猜想：Eichler–Zagier 对应的高指标推广：指标 m 的 Jacobi 尖点形式同构于 Γ₀(m) 的权 k−1/2 尖点空间的子空间且子空间的正交补维数为 m 的素因子个数。（高指标 EZ 子空间）
