**English.** Definition: A very free curve is a rational curve with maximal splitting property. Conjecture: The minimal degree of a very free curve on a smooth Fano is an explicit linear expression in the dimension, attained by double-cover types with universal conversion. (minimal degree conversion of very free curves)
