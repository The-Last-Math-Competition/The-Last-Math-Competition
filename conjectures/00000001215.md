# 00000001215

**CG-15 ◆** — source: 28_组合博弈与离散动力学.md

**English.** Definition: Meyniel's conjecture asserts that the cop number of an n-vertex graph is at most ⌈√n⌉. Conjecture: The cop number of a planar graph is at most three and the bound is tight; the cop number of an outerplanar graph is at most two, with only finitely many graphs attaining the bound up to isomorphism. (planar cop number tight bound)

**中文。** 定义：Meyniel 猜想断言 n 顶点图的警察数不超过根号 n 的上取整。猜想：平面图的警察数不超过三且界紧；外平面图的警察数不超过二且达到此界的图在同构意义下有限。（平面警察数紧界）
