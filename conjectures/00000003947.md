**G4-07 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The min-cut query complexity is the number of edge-existence queries needed to decide the minimum cut. Conjecture: The randomized and deterministic query complexities of undirected min-cut differ by at most a logarithmic factor, and both are of order Theta(m). (min-cut query matching)

**中文。** 定义：min-cut 查询复杂度指以边存在询问判定最小割的次数。猜想：无向 min-cut 的随机与确定查询复杂度相差至多对数因子,且两者均为 Θ(m) 量级。（min-cut 查询匹配）
