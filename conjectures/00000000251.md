**English.** Definition: A harmonious labeling is an injective labeling of the vertices modulo m whose edge sums are bijective modulo m. Conjecture: All trees admit harmonious labelings; the determination is settled through the duality with edge-graceful labelings plus a same-trace cycle decomposition. (tree harmonious via edge-graceful duality)
