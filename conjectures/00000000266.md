**English.** Definition: A Kontsevich–Zagier period is the value of an integral of a rational-coefficient polynomial over a region defined by polynomial inequalities. Conjecture: Euler's constant γ is a period; and the period property of γ is equivalent to a representation via the exponential integral. (Euler constant period property)

**中文。** 定义：Kontsevich–Zagier period 指多项式不等式区域上有理系数多项式积分值的全体。猜想：欧拉常数 γ 是 period；且 γ 的 period 性质与指数积分的表示等价。（欧拉常数 period 性）
