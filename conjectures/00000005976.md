**English.** Definition: Doubly stochastic matrices have row and column sums one. Conjecture: The delocalization of the uniform state is completely uniform at maximal entropy, the perturbation law is closed, and the closure is the eigenvalue gap. (eigen-gap uniform delocalization of doubly stochastic matrices)

**中文。** 定义：双随机矩阵指行列和为一的矩阵。猜想：均匀态的去局域在熵最大时完全均匀且扰动律封闭；且封闭的组合为特征值间隙。（双随机熵最大特征间隙均匀去局域）
