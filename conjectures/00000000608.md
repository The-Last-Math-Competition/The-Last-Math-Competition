**KT-08 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The coefficients of the Kauffman bracket are combinatorial invariants of a knot. Conjecture: The absolute values of the Kauffman coefficient sequence of an alternating knot have no three adjacent strictly decreasing terms; and the combinatorial content of unimodality is alternating positivity. (Kauffman coefficient alternating unimodality)

**中文。** 定义：Kauffman 括号的系数为纽结的组合不变量。猜想：交替纽结的 Kauffman 系数序列绝对值无相邻三项严格下降；且单峰的组合为交错正性。（Kauffman 系数交错单峰）
