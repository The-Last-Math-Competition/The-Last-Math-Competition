# 00000002863

**IX-03 ★** — source: 81_微分算子的指标II.md

**English.** Definition: The η invariant: the regularization of the spectral asymmetry of Dirac operators. Conjecture: The mod-one reduction of η: η(D) mod Z is purely topological, depending only on characteristic classes; the variation formula of η is an integral of local forms, with integrand the sum of characteristic forms and Chern–Simons corrections. (eta mod-one topological rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：η 不变量：Dirac 算子的谱不对称性的正则化。猜想：η 的模一约化：η(D) mod Z 为纯拓扑且仅依赖示性类；η 的变差公式为局部形式的积分且积分密度为示性形式与 Chern–Simons 修正的和。（eta 模一拓扑刚性）
