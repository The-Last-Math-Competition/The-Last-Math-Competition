**English.** Definition: Ideal lattices of AF algebras: combinatorial realizations by Bratteli diagrams. Conjecture: The closed ideal lattice of an AF algebra is isomorphic to the hereditary-subset lattice of its Bratteli diagram; the isomorphism preserves stratification of dimension vectors, and the length of maximal ideal chains equals the supremum of path depths of the diagram. (AF ideal lattice combinatorial realization)

**中文。** 定义：AF 代数的理想格：Bratteli 图的组合实现。猜想：AF 代数的闭理想格与 Bratteli 图的遗传子集格同构；同构保持维数向量的层化且极大理想链的长度等于图的路径深度上确界。（AF 理想格组合实现）
