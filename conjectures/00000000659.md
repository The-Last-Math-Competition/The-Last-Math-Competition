# 00000000659

**English.** Definition: The rank of instanton homology is a knot shadow invariant. Conjecture: The rank is at least the one-half power of the bridge number minus one; the bound is attained for shadow-minimal knots; and shadow minimality is realized by bridge decompositions. (instanton homology bridge-number lower bound)

**中文。** 定义：瞬时同调的秩为纽结侧影不变量。猜想：秩至少为桥数减一的二分之一次幂；且下界的达成为侧影极小；且侧影极小的实现为桥分解。（瞬时同调桥数下界）
