**English.** Definition: Boundedness means that fixed-dimensional Fanos form families of finite type. Conjecture: The explicit bound of four-dimensional boundedness is a joint polynomial of volume and Betti numbers, calibrated by quartic hypersurface families. (optimal boundedness bound of four-dimensional Fanos)
