# 00000007951

**INT-21 ★** — source: 250_可积系统II.md

**English.** Definition: The multi-scale consistency of integrable lattice equations (consistency-around-a-cube): the three-dimensional consistency index C_3(E) of a system of equations on a three-dimensional lattice restricted to its three two-dimensional faces. Conjecture: The classification of integrable lattice equations satisfying C_3: up to affine Q transformations only Q4 (elliptic potential) is the multi-parameter maximal type; all other integrable equations are degenerations of Q4; and the tau function of Q4 is a contour integral of a ternary elliptic theta function, and the level structure of the Hasse diagram of the degeneration tree (the structure of the discrete parameter space) is isomorphic to Arnold's classification lattice of catastrophe singularities (a catastrophe-integrability duality). (Q4 maximal type catastrophe duality)

**中文。** 定义：可积格方程的多重尺度一致性（consistency-around-a-cube）：三维格上的方程组在三个两维面的相容性指标 C_3(E)。猜想：C_3 成立的可积格方程的分类：在仿射 Q 四周变换意义下仅有 Q4（椭圆势）型为多参数极大型；其余可积方程均为 Q4 的退化；且 Q4 的 τ 函数为三元椭圆 theta 函数的围道积分，退化树的 Hasse 图的层次（离散参数空间的结构）与 Arnold 的突变论奇异性的分类格同构（突变-可积对偶）。
