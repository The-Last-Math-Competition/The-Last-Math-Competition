**English.** Definition: Approximation by square denominators studies the distribution of {n²α}. Conjecture: The divergence of Σψ(√n)/√n implies that for almost every α there are infinitely many approximations by square denominators; and the constant of the critical divergent series is optimal. (square-denominator Khinchin criterion)

**中文。** 定义：平方数分母逼近研究 {n²α} 的分布。猜想：Σψ(√n)/√n 发散蕴含几乎处处的 α 有无穷多平方分母逼近；且临界发散级数的常数最优。（平方分母 Khinchin 判据）
