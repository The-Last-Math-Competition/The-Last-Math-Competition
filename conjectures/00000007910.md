# 00000007910

**English.** Definition: The infinity-type exponents of a Hecke character theta and the algebraicity field E_theta of the special values theta(a). Conjecture: For a weight 1 Hecke character of a totally real field F, theta(a) lies in E_theta times a power of pi (a Stark root-of-unity correction), with the degree of E_theta uniformly controlled by [F:Q] 2^r; and the measure norm of the p-adic interpolation (distribution) of theta is at most the order of the Galois stabilization factor, with equality if and only if the conductor of theta is 1 (the minimal norm of unramified characters). (Hecke character algebraicity bound)

**中文。** 定义：Hecke 特征（Hecke character）θ 的无穷型指数与特殊值 θ(𝔞) 的代数性域 E_θ。猜想：对全实域 F 的权 1 Hecke 特征，θ(𝔞)∈E_θ·(π^{s}) 型（Stark 单位根修正），E_θ 的次数被 [F:Q]·2^{r} 一致控制；且 θ 的 p-adic 插值（分布）的测度范数 ≤ Galois 稳定化因子的阶，等号当且仅当 θ 的导子为 1（非分歧特征的极小范数）。
