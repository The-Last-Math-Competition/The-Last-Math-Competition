# 00000006097

**English.** Definition: The mean-field limit is the two-layer limit of infinitely many particles. Conjecture: For two layers it is the classical PDE with convergence rate the reciprocal of the particle count, and the kernel of the equation is the measure flow. (measure-flow limit of classical mean-field PDEs)

**中文。** 定义：平均场极限为粒子无限的双层极限。猜想：两层时为经典型偏微方程且收敛的率为粒子数倒数；且方程的核为测度流。（平均场经典偏微测度流极限）
