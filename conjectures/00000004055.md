# 00000004055

**AR3-25 ★** — source: 120_代数表示论III_AuslanderReiten.md

**English.** Definition: The syzygy Omega(M) is the kernel of the minimal projective cover, and the period is the minimal number of iterations first returning to an isomorphic module. Conjecture: On every self-injective algebra the syzygy period of any prescribed prime p can be realized, and the minimal period 2 is attained by open-chain modules. (realization spectrum of syzygy periods)

**中文。** 定义：syzygy Ω(M) 指极小投射覆盖的核,周期指迭代首次回到同构的最小步数。猜想：每个自注入代数上任意预给素数 p 的 syzygy 周期均可实现,最小周期 2 由开链模达到。（syzygy 周期实现谱）
