**English.** Definition: Randomized QR: the stabilization of Cholesky QR. Conjecture: The stability of iteration two is a squared-condition window; and the kernel of the window is residual orthogonalization. (Cholesky-QR2 residual orthogonal stability window)

**中文。** 定义：随机 QR 为 Cholesky QR 的稳定化。猜想：迭代二的稳定为条件数平方窗；且窗的核为残差正交化。（Cholesky-QR2 残差正交稳定窗）
