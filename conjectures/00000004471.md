# 00000004471

**English.** Definition: The monodromy of period integrals is the matrix representation of the mirror period transformation. Conjecture: The monodromy of the double logarithm is an explicit solution of a KZ-type equation, represented by iterated polylogarithm integrals and closed at weight 3. (KZ monodromy closure of the double logarithm)

**中文。** 定义：周期积分的单值指镜像周期变换的矩阵表示。猜想：双重对数的单值为 KZ 型方程的显式解,表示为 polylog 迭代积分且在权 3 处封闭。（双对数 KZ 单值封闭）
