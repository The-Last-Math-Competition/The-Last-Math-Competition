**English.** Definition: Mathieu functions: Floquet solutions of elliptic potentials. Conjecture: The characteristic bands of Mathieu: the band structure of eigenvalues is the periodic-boundary splitting, with the gap width of the splitting an explicit power series in q. (Mathieu characteristic band splitting width)
