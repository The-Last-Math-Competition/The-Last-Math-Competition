# 00000008009

**DEL-19 ★** — source: 252_退化椭圆与自由边界.md

**English.** Definition: The degeneracy threshold of the singular set of the heat flow of p-harmonic maps: the existence threshold p_str(m) for p-harmonic maps into the sphere S^{m-1}. Conjecture: For p at least m every map is p-harmonically homotopic to a constant (rigidity for p at least m), while for p < m nontrivial harmonic maps exist; near the threshold the minimal energy of harmonic maps is explicit (for p < m the conformal solutions have energy e_p = (p/(m-p))^p Area, a Duong-Zwiebach type formula); and the dimension of the singular set of the heat flow is at most m - [p] (the Strat dimension spectrum of singularities), the integer-point law of the spectrum agreeing with the residue mod m of the integer part of p. (p-harmonic sphere threshold conformal energy)

**中文。** 定义：p-调和映射的热流的奇性集的退化阈值：到球面 S^{m-1} 的 p-调和映射的存在阈值 p_str(m)。猜想：p ≥ m 时一切映射 p-调和同伦于常值（p≥m 的刚性），p<m 时非平凡调和映射存在；且阈值附近的调和映射的极小能量的显式式（p<m 的共形解的能量 e_p=(p/(m-p))^{p}·Area 型 Duong-Zwiebach 式）；热流的奇性集的维数 ≤ m-[p]（奇性的 Strat 维数谱），谱的整点律与 p 的整数部分的模 m 剩余一致。
