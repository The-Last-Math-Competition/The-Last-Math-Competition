# 00000002138

**PS-28 ★** — source: 56_解析组合.md

**English.** Definition: The saddle-point equation of the even generating function of random Cayley trees. Conjecture: The uniqueness of the solution is given by a monotonicity criterion of the log-derivative; and the window of the criterion is the exponential parameter domain. (Cayley saddle uniqueness criterion)

**中文。** 定义：随机 Cayley 树的偶生成函数鞍点方程。猜想：解的唯一性由 log 导数的单调性判据给出；且判据的窗为指数参数域。（Cayley 鞍点唯一判据）
