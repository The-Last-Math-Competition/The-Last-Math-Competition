# 00000003962

**G4-22 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: The Kneser graph KG(n, k) is the graph whose k-subsets are adjacent exactly when they are disjoint. Conjecture: The ratio of its L-infinity mixing time to the inverse spectral gap is Theta(log C(n, k)), and the precutoff window has width O(n). (Kneser precutoff window)

**中文。** 定义：Kneser 图 KG(n,k) 指 k 子集相邻当且仅当不交的图。猜想：其懒惰随机游走的 L∞ 混合时间与谱隙倒数的比为 Θ(log C(n,k)),预截断窗口宽度为 O(n)。（Kneser 预截断窗口）
