# 00000003962

**G4-22 ★** — source: 117_图论IV_算法与结构.md

**English.** Definition: Kneser graph KG(n,k) denotes k subset if and only if graph of not intersection. Conjecture: L∞ mixing time of its random walk and ratio of spectral gap reciprocal is Θ(log C(n,k)), truncation window width is O(n). (Kneser truncation window)

**中文。** 定义：Kneser 图 KG(n,k) 指 k 子集相邻当且仅当不交的图。猜想：其懒惰随机游走的 L∞ 混合时间与谱隙倒数的比为 Θ(log C(n,k)),预截断窗口宽度为 O(n)。（Kneser 预截断窗口）
