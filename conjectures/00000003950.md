**English.** Definition: A randomized-deterministic separation is a polynomial gap between the complexities of two computational models. Conjecture: Triangle detection has an n^{Omega(1)} separation between the randomized and deterministic word models, and no natural graph problem admits a reverse deterministic superlinear advantage. (randomized-deterministic separation in graph algorithms)

**中文。** 定义：随机-确定分离指两计算模型复杂度的多项式差距。猜想：三角检测在随机字模型与确定字模型之间有 n^{Ω(1)} 的分离;且任何自然图问题不存在反向的确定超线性优势。（图算法随机-确定分离）
