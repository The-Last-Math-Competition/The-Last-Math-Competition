# 00000006299

**English.** Definition: The manifold-rigidity conjecture is homotopy-topological rigidity. Conjecture: Homotopy equivalences of higher-dimensional hyperbolic manifolds rigidify to isometries; the exceptional dimension is four; and the counterexample of the exceptional dimension is constructed by Spivak fibrations. (hyperbolic isometry exception at dimension four)

**中文。** 定义：流形刚性猜想的同伦-拓扑刚性。猜想：高维双曲的同伦等价刚化为等距；且例外维数为四；且例外维数的反例由 Spivak 纤维化构造。（双曲同伦等距例外维四）
