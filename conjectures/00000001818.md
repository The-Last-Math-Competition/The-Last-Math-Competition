# 00000001818

**English.** Definition: Terminal quotient singularities are the terminal singularities of quotients C^n/G. Conjecture: For n ≥ 5, the group list of isolated terminal quotient singularities is an explicit finite table, induced by the Gorenstein types of finite subgroups of SL_n(Z) (exhausted by the table); non-isolated cases do not exist for n ≥ 5.

**中文。** 定义：终端商奇点指商 C^n/G 的终端奇点。猜想：n≥5 的孤立终端商奇点的群列表为显式有限表:由 SL_n(Z) 的有限子群的 Gorenstein 型诱导(表格穷举);非孤立情形在 n≥5 不存在。
