**HD2-09 ★** — source: 157_复几何II_Hodge理论.md

**English.** Definition: The monodromy weight filtration is the weight structure induced by monodromy. Conjecture: The weight-two conversion constant is an explicit factorial ratio of the characteristic exponents, the ratio is one for tame monodromy, and the formula is closed in the quasi-unipotent case. (factorial-ratio conversion of weight-two monodromy)

**中文。** 定义：单值权滤过指单值诱导的权重结构。猜想：权二的换算常数为特征指数的显式阶乘比,温顺单值时比值为一且公式在拟幺模情形封闭。（单值权二阶乘比换算式）
