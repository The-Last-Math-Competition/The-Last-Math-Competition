# 00000006304

**English.** Definition: The trace formula is the compatibility of the Hochschild-type trace with the grading. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the induced grading and the trace preserves the filtration; the grading differs by one, with difference constant 1; and the composition of the trace with K_0 is the Euler characteristic, an integer. (filtration-preserving difference-one trace conversion)

**中文。** 定义：迹公式：Hochschild 型迹与分次的相容。猜想：换算常数为诱导分次的显式且迹保滤子；分次差一且差的常数为 1；迹与 K_0 的合成为 Euler 示性数且示性数整数。（迹保滤子分次差一换算）
