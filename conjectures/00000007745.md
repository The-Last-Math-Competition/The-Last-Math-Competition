# 00000007745

**FRV-25 ★** — source: 243_高维随机矩阵VIII_自由随机变量.md

**English.** Definition: The information monotonicity of the free compression semigroup states that the relative free entropy D(mu|nu) does not increase under ratio-p compression. Conjecture: The compression inequality D(mu_p|nu_p) <= p D(mu|nu) + h(p) holds with universal Shannon-type correction h(p) = -p log p; equality holds if and only if mu is a member of the free exponential family relative to nu; and the free Fisher information satisfies the concavified bound I(mu_p) >= p^{-1} I(mu). (Shannon-corrected free compression inequality)

**中文。** 定义：自由压缩半群的信息单调性：相对自由熵 D(μ|ν) 在比率 p 压缩下不增。猜想：压缩不等式 D(μ_p|ν_p) ≤ p·D(μ|ν) + h(p)，其中 h(p) 为普适的 Shannon 型修正项 h(p)=-p log p；等号成立当且仅当 μ 为相对于 ν 的自由指数族成员；由此推出自由 Fisher 信息 I 满足 I(μ_p) ≥ p^{-1} I(μ) 的凹化版本。
