# 00000004393

**English.** Definition: A graphing is a measure-preserving system serving as the limit of bounded-degree graphs. Conjecture: The universal covering of its local tree decomposition is isomorphic to the boundary system of a free group action, and the measure entropy of the covering is an explicit functional of the Borel cycle measure. (free group universal covering of graphings)

**中文。** 定义：graphing 指有界度图限的测度保持系统。猜想：其局部树剖分的普适覆盖同构于自由群作用的边界系统,覆盖的测度熵由 Borel 圈测度的显式泛函给出。（graphing 自由群普适覆盖）
