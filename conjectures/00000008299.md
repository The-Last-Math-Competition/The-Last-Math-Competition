**English.** Definition: The virtual fundamental class of nonregular Hamiltonians: the dimension vir_dim of the virtual fundamental class of Kuranishi structures. Conjecture: vir_dim (the virtual dimension formula) is the explicit mu(index) - mu(exit) + n - 3 (an index difference formula); the gluing consistency of the virtual class (a generalized associativity) makes the square of the boundary homomorphism of the moduli spaces zero (a square-zero law); and the obstruction to proving square-zero (multiplicities of multiple covers) is corrected by a sign correction (a sign correction law), the coherence of the correction (sign coherence) being given by the orientation of rigid rotations of holomorphic curves (a rigid rotation orientation law). (virtual class index gluing square-zero signs)
