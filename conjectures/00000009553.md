**TCH-03 ★** — source: 304_Teichmuller动力.md

**English.** Definition: N(L) counts closed Teichmueller geodesics of length at most L within a stratum, with length defined in Hodge norm coordinates, closed geodesics corresponding to stable saddle connection graphs. Conjecture: N(L) is asymptotic to e^L/L with exponential rate exactly 1, equal to the flow entropy of the Masur-Veech measure; and the secondary term is the -1 power L^{-1} with an explicit constant given by codimension descent recursion over the principal boundary. (closed geodesic counting)

**中文。** 定义：stratum 内长度 ≤L 的闭 Teichmüller 测地线计数 N(L)；这里长度由 Hodge 范数坐标系定义，闭测地线对应稳定鞍点联络图。猜想：N(L) ~ e^{L}/L 且指数率恰为 1，等于 Masur–Veech 测度的流熵；次主项为 −1 次幂 L^{−1} 带显式常数，由主边界的余维下降递推给出。（闭测地线计数）
