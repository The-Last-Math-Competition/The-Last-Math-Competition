# 00000001768

**RT-18 ★** — source: 44_表示论II.md

**English.** Definition: The characters of standard modules of the rational Cherednik category O of sl₂ at t = 1. Conjecture: These characters are always given by positive combinations of bisymmetric functions; and the combination coefficients are given by pseudopermutation counts of Hurwitz numbers. (spin-Schur character positive combination)

**中文。** 定义：sl₂ 的有理 Cherednik 范畴 O 在 t=1 的标准模特征。猜想：该特征恒由双对称函数的正组合给出；且组合系数由 Hurwitz 数的伪置换计数。（spin-Schur 特征正组合）
