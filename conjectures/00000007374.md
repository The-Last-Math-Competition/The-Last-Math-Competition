# 00000007374

**English.** Definition: Numerics for rough paths with nonsmooth driving. Conjecture: The convergence order of the Euler scheme holds, and the constant of the order is the path roughness. (roughness-constant rough-Euler order)

**中文。** 定义：粗糙路径的数值：驱动的非光滑。猜想：粗糙的 Euler：格式的收敛阶且阶的常数为路径的粗糙度。（粗糙路径粗糙度 Euler 格式收敛阶常数驱动非光滑数值）
