# 00000001013

**FG-13 ◆** — source: 26_有限几何与编码理论.md

**English.** Definition: The MDS conjecture asserts that q-ary MDS codes have length at most q+1, with the exception q even and k ∈ {3, q−1} giving q+2. Conjecture: The maximal length of weighted MDS codes (coordinate multiplicity ≤ 2) is q + O(√q), with the correction Θ(√q) given exactly by the Hasse bound on elliptic rational points.

**中文。** 定义：MDS 猜想断言 q 元 MDS 码长度 ≤ q+1,例外为 q 偶且 k∈{3,q−1} 时的 q+2。猜想：加权 MDS 码(坐标重数 ≤2)的最大长度为 q+O(√q),且修正项 Θ(√q) 由椭圆有理点数的 Hasse 界精确给出。
