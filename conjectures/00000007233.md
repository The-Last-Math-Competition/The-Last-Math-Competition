# 00000007233

**English.** Definition: Michel-Venkatesh with subconvexity. Conjecture: The subconvex bound for the symmetric cube holds with exponent one eighth; and the one-eighth exponent is locked by the optimal amplitude of the Michel-Venkatesh averaging method. (symmetric-cube one-eighth subconvex exponent)

**中文。** 定义：Michel–Venkatesh：子凸性。猜想：子凸界：对称立方的子凸界且界的指数为八分之一。(对称立方八分子凸指数界)；且八分之一指数由 Michel-Venkatesh 均值方法的最优振幅锁定。
