# 00000001607

**English.** Definition: The Anderson model on the Bethe lattice is the random-potential operator on a tree. Conjecture: The density of states at the edge of the absolutely continuous spectrum always has a square-root singularity; and the singularity constant is an explicit function of the lattice branching number. (Bethe edge square-root singularity)

**中文。** 定义：Bethe 格上的 Anderson 模型指树上随机位势算子。猜想：绝对连续谱边缘的态密度恒具有平方根奇性；且奇性常数为格分叉度的显式函数。（Bethe 边缘平方根奇性）
