**English.** Definition: The displacement energy is the minimal amplitude of a Hamiltonian displacement of a Lagrangian. Conjecture: The energy of the Clifford torus in a closed ball is controlled by a volume obstruction; and the kernel of the obstruction is the Gromov area. (Clifford Gromov-area displacement energy obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：位移能量指拉格朗日被 Hamilton 位移的最小振幅。猜想：闭球中 Clifford 环面的能量由体积障碍控制；且障碍的核为 Gromov 面积。（Clifford-Gromov 面积位移能量障碍）
