**English.** Definition: The plus construction is the elimination construction of fundamental groups. Conjecture: The vanishing chain of higher commutator groups is given by inclusion-induced equivalences, and the kernel of the vanishing is the Whitehead group. (Whitehead kernel of the plus construction)

**中文。** 定义：加构造为基本群的消去构造。猜想：高交换群的消没链由含入诱导等价给出；且消没的核为 Whitehead 群。（加构造 Whitehead 消没核）
