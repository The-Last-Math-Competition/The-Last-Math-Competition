**C2-12 ★** — source: 12_组合代数与几何II.md

**English.** Conjecture: Sum covers by multiplicative subgroups: for d | p−1, the minimal k for which the k-fold sumset of the subgroup H of d-th powers in F_p^{*} covers F_p is (1+o(1))·log p/(log p − log d) (as d → ∞ with d < p^{1−ε}).

**中文。** 猜想：乘法子群的和覆盖:对 d | p−1,k 次幂子群 H ⊂ F_p^{*} 的 k 重和集覆盖 F_p 的最小 k 为 (1+o(1))·log p/(log p − log d)(d → ∞ 且 d < p^{1−ε})。
