# 00000001648

**English.** Definition: The Teichmüller complex of a group G is a subcomplex of the Sullivan minimal model of its cohomology. Conjecture: The ℓ-connectivity threshold of the complex is the commutator length ℓ(G): for m-solvable groups the complex is (ℓ(G)−1)-connected but not ℓ(G)-connected; connectivity is completely controlled by the solvability level.

**中文。** 定义：群 G 的 Teichmüller 复形指其上同调的 Sullivian 极小模型的子复形。猜想：复形的 ℓ-连通阈值为群的合长度 ℓ(G):m-可解群的复形为 (ℓ(G)−1)-连通但非 ℓ(G)-连通;连通性由可解性的层数完全控制。
