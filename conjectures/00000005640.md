# 00000005640

**English.** Definition: The intersection spectrum and the intersection graph are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two convex-set families with the same intersection spectrum but non-isomorphic intersection graphs, and the separation is realized by an explicit redrawing pair preserving intersections. (same intersection spectrum-different graph redrawing separation)
