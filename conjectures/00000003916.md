**FL-06 ◆** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: The spectral norm is gamma(H) = c(1, H) - c(pt, H). Conjecture: gamma is bounded on all Hamiltonians if and only if the fundamental group is finite; the smallest manifold on which it is unbounded is the two-dimensional torus, on which gamma can be made arbitrarily large. (finite pi_1 criterion for gamma boundedness)

**中文。** 定义：谱范数 γ(H) = c(1,H) − c(pt,H)。猜想：γ 在全体 Hamilton 上有界当且仅当基本群有限;无界性的最小流形为二维环面,其上 γ 可取任意大。（γ 有界的有限π₁ 判据）
