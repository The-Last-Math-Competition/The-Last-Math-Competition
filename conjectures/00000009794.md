**DDG-04 ★** — source: 312_离散微分几何.md

**English.** Definition: Discrete Ricci flow: Chow-Luo's combinatorial flow evolving edge weights by curvature; convergence and smooth limits. Conjecture: The discrete Ricci flow converges exponentially within the class of attainable target curvatures; the convergence exponent is controlled by the first eigenvalue of the combinatorial Laplacian, and the flow converges uniquely on spherical topology to the discrete uniform metric. (discrete Ricci exponential convergence)

**中文。** 定义：离散 Ricci 流：Chow–Luo 以曲率驱动边权演化的组合流；收敛性与光滑极限。猜想：离散 Ricci 流在目标曲率可达类内指数收敛；收敛指数由组合 Laplacian 的第一特征值控制且流在球面拓扑唯一收敛到离散均匀度量。（离散 Ricci 指数收敛）
