# 00000001196

**LT-96 ★** — source: 27_李理论与代数群.md

**English.** Definition: M(λ,μ) is the maximum of the Littlewood–Richardson coefficients N_{λμ}^{ν} of Schur Q-functions at fixed shapes. Conjecture: M(λ,μ) ≤ min(2^{ℓ(λ)}, 2^{ℓ(μ)}, f^{ν}), with value exactly 2^{k−1} at λ = μ = (k); the extremum is attained on shifted rectangles.

**中文。** 定义：Schur Q-函数的 Littlewood–Richardson 系数 N_{λμ}^{ν} 的极大值 M(λ,μ) 指固定形状下的最大系数。猜想：M(λ,μ) ≤ min(2^{ℓ(λ)}, 2^{ℓ(μ)}, f^{ν}) 且在 λ=μ=(k) 时恰取 2^{k-1};极值在 shifted 长方形上达到。
