# 00000006446

**MF4-16 ★** — source: 200_模形式III_Maass形.md

**English.** Definition: Symmetric powers with the pole structure of symmetric-power L-functions. Conjecture: The dihedral exceptions give the complete classification of poles and the combination of the exceptions is the leading term of induced representations; poles appear only at even symmetric powers; and the order of a pole is the multiplicity of the dihedral decomposition. (dihedral induced poles of symmetric powers)

**中文。** 定义：对称幂：对称幂 L 函数的极点结构。猜想：二面体例外给出极点的完全分类且例外的组合为诱导表示的首项；极点仅出现于偶对称幂；极点的阶为二面体分解的重数。（对称幂极点二面体诱导）
