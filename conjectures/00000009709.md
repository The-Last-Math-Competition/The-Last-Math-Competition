**English.** Definition: place reconstruction of nonlinear Schrodinger equation of inverse scattering: can product of DNLS. Conjecture: inverse scattering reconstruction of DNLS in weighted Sobolev data class is well-posed bijection; reflection coefficient and map of place is analytic isomorphism and derivative number of of to loss is exactly one. (DNLS inverse scattering well-posed)

**中文。** 定义：DNLS 的逆散射：可积变形的非线性薛定谔方程的位势重建。猜想：DNLS 在加权 Sobolev 数据类的逆散射重建为适定双射；反射系数与位势的映射为拟解析同构且反向损失的导数个数恰为一。（DNLS 逆散射适定）
