# 00000009709

**English.** Definition: Inverse scattering for DNLS: potential reconstruction for the integrable deformation of the nonlinear Schroedinger equation. Conjecture: Inverse scattering reconstruction of DNLS is a well-posed bijection on weighted Sobolev data classes; the reflection-coefficient-to-potential map is a quasianalytic isomorphism, with exactly one derivative lost in reverse. (DNLS inverse scattering well-posedness)

**中文。** 定义：DNLS 的逆散射：可积变形的非线性薛定谔方程的位势重建。猜想：DNLS 在加权 Sobolev 数据类的逆散射重建为适定双射；反射系数与位势的映射为拟解析同构且反向损失的导数个数恰为一。（DNLS 逆散射适定）
