# 00000007355

**English.** Definition: Keisler's foundation theorem with the existence of functionals. Conjecture: The infinitesimal version of the interchange of integrals holds, and the error of the version is infinitesimal. (infinitesimal-error nonstandard Fubini)

**中文。** 定义：Keisler 的基础定理：泛函的存在。猜想：Fubini 的非标准：积分交换的无穷小版本且本的误差为无穷小。（Keisler Fubini 无穷小积分交换泛函存在版本误差）
