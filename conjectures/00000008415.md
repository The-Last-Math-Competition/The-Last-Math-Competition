# 00000008415

**English.** Definition: A Hadamard matrix is a square matrix with entries plus or minus 1 and pairwise orthogonal rows. Conjecture: Hadamard matrices of order 4n exist for all n; the number of equivalence classes h(4n) satisfies h(4n) at least 2^{4n^2(1+o(1))}; and the logarithmic asymptotics of h(4n) is attained by the counting lower bound from t-design covers. (Hadamard existence and counting)

**中文。** 定义：Hadamard 矩阵指元素 ±1 且行两两正交的方阵。猜想：4n 阶 Hadamard 矩阵对一切 n 存在；其等价类个数 h(4n) 满足 h(4n) ≥ 2^{4n²(1+o(1))}；h(4n) 的对数渐近由 t-设计覆盖的计数下界达到。（Hadamard 存在与计数）
