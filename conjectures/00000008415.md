**English.** Definition: Hadamard matrix denotes element ±1 and of row two two orthogonal. Conjecture: 4n order Hadamard matrix for all n exists; its equivalence class number of h(4n) satisfies logarithm asymptotic of h(4n) ≥ 2^{4n²(1+o(1))}; h(4n) by counting lower bound of t- let cover attains. (Hadamard exists and counting)

**中文。** 定义：Hadamard 矩阵指元素 ±1 且行两两正交的方阵。猜想：4n 阶 Hadamard 矩阵对一切 n 存在；其等价类个数 h(4n) 满足 h(4n) ≥ 2^{4n²(1+o(1))}；h(4n) 的对数渐近由 t-设计覆盖的计数下界达到。（Hadamard 存在与计数）
