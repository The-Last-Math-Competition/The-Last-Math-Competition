**MD3-19 ★** — source: 149_随机过程III_马氏决策.md

**English.** Definition: Linear approximation is the class of parametric linear representations of value functions. Conjecture: The threshold at which a two-parameter linear approximation can exactly represent the optimal policy is characterized explicitly by the invariant subspaces of the Bellman operator, and the condition is completely verifiable; the basis vectors of the invariant subspace equal the roots of the characteristic polynomial of the discount factor. (invariant subspace threshold of linear approximation)

**中文。** 定义：线性逼近指值函数的参数线性表示类。猜想：两参数线性逼近可精确表示最优策略的阈值由贝尔曼算子的不变子空间显式刻画,条件完全可验。(线性逼近不变子空间阈值)；且不变子空间基向量等于折扣因子特征多项式的根。
