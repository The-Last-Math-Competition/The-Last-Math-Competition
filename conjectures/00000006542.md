**English.** Definition: The graph index with the correspondence of the index on graphs with the spectrum. Conjecture: The correspondence domain is finite and the bridge of the correspondence is the poles of the Ihara zeta function; the reciprocals of the poles are the adjacency spectrum; and the kernel of the correspondence is the covering structure of regular graphs. (Ihara correspondence structure of the graph index)

**中文。** 定义：图指标：图上指标与谱的对应。猜想：对应域有限且对应的桥为 Ihara zeta 的极点；极点的倒数为邻接谱；对应的核为正则图的覆盖结构。（图指标谱 Ihara 对应结构）
