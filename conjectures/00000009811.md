**English.** Definition: Bricard flexible octahedra: the complete classification of self-intersecting flexible polyhedra. Conjecture: The complete list of flexible polyhedra is the three Bricard types plus vertex-gluing composites; non-self-intersecting flexible polyhedra are only the Connelly type and its Steffen subalgebra families. (flexible polyhedron classification)

**中文。** 定义：Bricard 挠曲八面体：自交挠曲多面体的完全分类。猜想：挠曲多面体的完全列表为 Bricard 三型加顶点粘贴复合；非自交的挠曲多面体仅 Connelly 型及其 Steffen 子代数族。（挠曲多面体分类）
