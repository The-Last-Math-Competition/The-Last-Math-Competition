**English.** Definition: The slice bound is the lower-bound quantity of the four-dimensional genus. Conjecture: The tightened table of three-braids is an explicit function of the signature sum of the braid word, with equality at torus closures; the saturation threshold of the signature sum is 3; and the smallest strict inequality has a braid word with two crossings of opposite signs. (signature-sum tightening of three-braid slice bounds)
