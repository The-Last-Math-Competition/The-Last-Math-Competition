**English.** Definition: The volume spectrum of polytopes with the distribution of normalized volumes. Conjecture: The limit of the volume spectrum of random simple polytopes is the Marchal-type distribution, and the support of the distribution is exponential scale. (Marchal random-simple volume-spectrum limit)
