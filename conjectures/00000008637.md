# 00000008637

**English.** Definition: Stokes constants are the jump coefficients of asymptotic expansions. Conjecture: In integrable models they are positive with product conserved with the intersection angle of separatrices; and the combination of the conservation is the connection number. (Stokes connection number positivity angle conservation)

**中文。** 定义：Stokes 常数指渐近展开跳变系数。猜想：可积模型中为正数且与分界线交角乘积守恒；且守恒的组合为连接数。（Stokes 连接数正性交角守恒）
