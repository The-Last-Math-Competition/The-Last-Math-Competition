**NL2-03 ★** — source: 156_数值线性代数II.md

**English.** Definition: A Sylvester equation is a matrix equation of the form AX + XB = C. Conjecture: The complexity lower bound of its solution is an explicit cubic quantity of the total number of matrix-vector products, valid for dense inputs and proven by algebraic techniques. (cubic complexity lower bound of Sylvester equations)

**中文。** 定义：Sylvester 方程指形如 AX 加 XB 等于 C 的矩阵方程。猜想：其求解的复杂度下界为矩阵向量乘总数的立方型显式量,稠密输入下成立且由代数技术证明。（Sylvester 方程立方复杂度下界）
