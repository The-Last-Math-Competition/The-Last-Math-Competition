# 00000000275

**English.** Definition: The thin-shell constant: the typical fluctuation scale σ_n of |X| − √n for an isotropic log-concave random vector X. Conjecture: σ_n = O(1) (a stronger square-root fluctuation version appears in the literature); and the supremum of σ_n is attained by shell-equivariant log-concave measures.

**中文。** 定义：thin-shell 常数:各向同性 log-凹随机向量 |X| − √n 的典型波动尺度 σ_n。猜想：σ_n = O(1)(更强的平方根波动版本见各文献)；且 σ_n 的上确界由球壳等变 log-凹测度达到。
