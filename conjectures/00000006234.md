# 00000006234

**English.** Definition: The essential image concerns the relation of a functor's essential image with adjoint conservativity. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in adjoint conservativity and regularity is reflected by the inverse image; the criterion of conservativity is the double faithfulness of the adjoint pair, the minimal counterexample being field extension; and the kernel of the inverse-image reflection is the equalizer. (adjoint-conservative regular inverse-image conversion)

**中文。** 定义：本质像：函子的本质像与伴随保守性的关系。猜想：换算常数为伴随保守性的显式且正则性由逆像反射；保守的判据为伴随对的双忠诚且判据的极小反例为域扩张；逆像反射的核为等价子。（伴随保守正则逆像换算）
