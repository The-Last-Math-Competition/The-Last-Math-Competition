# 00000009998

**English.** Definition: Central polynomials: central-valued polynomials of matrix algebras (Formanek's construction proves existence). Conjecture: The minimal degree of a central polynomial of the n by n matrix algebra is exactly n squared, and the attaining polynomial is unique up to isomorphism and transpose; uniqueness exceptions appear from n = 4, forming finite-parameter families. (central polynomial minimal degree)

**中文。** 定义：中心多项式：矩阵代数的中心值多项式（Formanek 构造存在性已知）。猜想：n 阶矩阵代数的中心多项式的最小次数恰为 n 的平方且达到的多项式在同构与转置下唯一；唯一性的例外从 n=4 起出现且例外为有限参数族。（中心多项式最小次数）
