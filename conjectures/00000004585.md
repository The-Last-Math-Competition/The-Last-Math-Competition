# 00000004585

**HA3-15 ◆** — source: 138_双曲几何的解析面.md

**English.** Definition: The first eigenvalue is the smallest positive spectral value of the Laplacian. Conjecture: The first eigenvalue of a closed hyperbolic three-manifold can be arbitrarily close to one but never exceeds it, the supremum is attained by sequences of arithmetic manifolds, and one is not attainable. (unattainable supremum one of the first eigenvalue)

**中文。** 定义：第一特征值指拉普拉斯算子的最小正谱值。猜想：闭双曲三维流形的第一特征值可任意接近一但恒不超过一,上确界由算术流形序列实现且一不可达。（λ₁ 上确界一不可达）
