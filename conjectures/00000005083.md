# 00000005083

**English.** Definition: The affine Grassmannian is the homogeneous space of the loop group. Conjecture: The weight conversion of GL_3 is always given explicitly by the correspondence of highest weights with polytopes, closed in the small-weight range and preserving weight products. (GL_3 Satake weight polytopes)

**中文。** 定义：仿射格拉斯曼指环路群的齐性空间。猜想：GL₃ 的权换算恒由最高权与多面体的对应显式给出；小权范围封闭且保持权积。（GL3 Satake 权多面体）
