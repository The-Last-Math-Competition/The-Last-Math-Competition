# 00000000196

**CS-30 ★** — source: 13_常数与级数.md

**English.** Definition: Θ₁ = Σ 2^{−⌊n log n⌋} and Θ₂ = Σ 2^{−⌊n²/2⌋} (Mahler-type sparse series). Conjecture: Θ₁ and Θ₂ are algebraically independent; the independence follows from explicit verification of Mahler's gap condition for the two exponent sets, the gap ratio being the incommensurability of log n with n. (algebraic independence of sparse series)

**中文。** 定义：Θ₁=Σ 2^{−⌊n log n⌋} 与 Θ₂=Σ 2^{−⌊n²/2⌋}（Mahler 型稀疏级数）。猜想：Θ₁ 与 Θ₂ 代数无关；无关性由指数集合的 Mahler 间隙条件的显式验证给出，间隙比为 log n 与 n 的不可通约性。（稀疏级数代数无关）
