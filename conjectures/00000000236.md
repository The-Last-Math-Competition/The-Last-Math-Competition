**GE-05 ◆** — source: 15_图与极值参数.md

**English.** Definition: D(n) is the maximum absolute value of the determinant of an n-th order ±1 matrix. Conjecture: (i) Hadamard matrices exist for every 4 | n (the Hadamard conjecture); (ii) D(n)/n^{n/2} → 1.

**中文。** 定义：D(n) = n 阶 ±1 方阵行列式的最大绝对值。猜想：(i) Hadamard 矩阵对一切 4 | n 存在(Hadamard 猜想);(ii) D(n)/n^{n/2} → 1。
