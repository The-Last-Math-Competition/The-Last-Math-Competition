# 00000001572

**English.** Definition: The modified KP equation is the bilinear companion system of KP. Conjecture: The positroidization of mKP soliton solutions is always parameterized by the Lagrangian cells of the degenerate Grassmannian; and the cell dimensions agree with the Grassmann necklace length. (mKP positroid cell parameterization)

**中文。** 定义：修正 KP 方程指 KP 的双线性伴随系统。猜想：mKP 的孤子解的 positroid 化恒由退化 Grassmannian 的 Lagrangian 胞腔参数化；且胞腔维数与 Grassmann 项链长度一致。（mKP positroid 胞腔参数化）
