# 00000005464

**English.** Definition: The redundancy of a residue system is the excess of the representation. Conjecture: The minimal-redundancy formula is the explicit difference of the product logarithm with the covering radius, given by rounding bounds, and optimal. (covering-radius redundancy formula of residue systems)

**中文。** 定义：剩余系统的冗余指表示的多余度。猜想：冗余最小公式为乘积对数与覆盖半径的显式差,由舍入界给出且最优。（剩余系统冗余覆盖半径公式）
