# 00000001938

**LG-08 ★** — source: 50_李群离散子群.md

**English.** Definition: Torsion homology growth is the tower limit of |H^i(Γ_n, Z)_tors|/vol(Γ_n\G). Conjecture: The limit exists and is an explicit ratio of L-values (the ratio of L(∂-operator, i) to the regulator); the computation of the limit is made explicit by the Franquevin–Sinnott probe technique.

**中文。** 定义：挠上同调增长指 |H^i(Γ_n,Z)_tors|/vol(Γ_n\G) 的塔极限。猜想：该极限存在且为 L 值的显式比(由 L(∂\bar∂,i) 与调节子的比值);极限的计算可由 Franquevin–Sinnott 的探针技术显式化。
