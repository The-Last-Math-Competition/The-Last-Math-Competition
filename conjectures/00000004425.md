**English.** Definition: The Hall-Littlewood measure is the partition measure weighted by HL polynomials. Conjecture: The one-point distribution of its marginals is an explicit q-series degenerating to geometric type, and the formula is closed for every row truncation. (closed q-series of HL marginals)

**中文。** 定义：Hall–Littlewood 测度指以 HL 多项式为权的分拆测度。猜想：其边缘的单点分布为退化为几何型的显式 q 级数,公式对一切行截断封闭。（HL 边缘 q 级数封闭式）
