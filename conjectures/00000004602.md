**English.** Definition: Plateau's law is the classical rule that three soap films meet at one hundred twenty degrees. Conjecture: In the anisotropic-tension variant, the three-interface angles are given by an explicit cosine formula of the tension vectors, degenerating continuously to one hundred twenty degrees for equal tensions. (anisotropic Plateau cosine formula)

**中文。** 定义：Plateau 法则指三肥皂膜交于一百二十度的经典规则。猜想：各向异性张力变体中三界面角由张力向量的显式余弦公式给出,等张力时连续退化为一百二十度。（各向异性 Plateau 余弦公式）
