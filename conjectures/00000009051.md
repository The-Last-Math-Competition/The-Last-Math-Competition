# 00000009051

**SMI-11 ★** — source: 287_子流形几何不等式.md

**English.** Definition: Poincare inequalities with potential. Conjecture: The optimal constant is always given by the first Dirichlet eigenvalue; and the threshold it gives is the sign of the potential. (potential Poincare optimal constant)

**中文。** 定义：带位势的 Poincaré 不等式。猜想：最优常数恒由第一 Dirichlet 特征值给出；且给出的门槛为位势符号。（位势 Poincare 最优常数）
