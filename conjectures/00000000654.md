**KT-54 ★** — source: 22_纽结与低维拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The isotopy genus controls the four-dimensional genus of a knot. Conjecture: The isotopy genus does not exceed the average of three and the four-dimensional genus; and tightness of the bound is attained on algebraic curves. (isotopy genus algebraic-curve mean bound)

**中文。** 定义：合痕亏格为纽结的四维亏格控制量。猜想：合痕亏格不超过三与四维亏格和的均值；且界的紧性为代数簇。（合痕亏格代数簇均值界）
