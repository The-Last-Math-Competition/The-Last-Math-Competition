# 00000007896

**HCA-26 ★** — source: 248_高阶范畴与同调代数II.md

**English.** Definition: The geometrical descending chain of the Brauer group of derived Azumaya algebras: the Postnikov tower from the Brauer 3-group dAz(X) to the torsion classes of H^2(X,G_m). Conjecture: The k-invariant of the second layer of the tower is the Beilinson Tate-Saito type characteristic 2-form; and the order of the corrected Brauer group of a derived enhancement (with nontrivial cotangent complex) exactly doubles on varieties with (-1)-shifted structure (a dichotomy law), the two branches being controlled by the quadratic-form signatures of symmetric powers of the singularity. (derived Azumaya Brauer dichotomy)

**中文。** 定义：导出 Azumaya 代数的 Brauer 群的 geomerical 降链：dAz(X) 的 Brauer 3-群到 H²(X,G_m) 扭类的 Postnikov 塔。猜想：塔的第二层的 k-不变量为 Beilinson 的 Tate-Saito 型特征 2 形式；且导出增强（余切复形非平凡）的修正 Brauer 群的阶在 (-1)-移位结构的簇上恰增 2 倍（二分律），二分的两支由奇点的对称积的二次型符号控制。
