**English.** Definition: The index classification of the partial quotients of π means grouping them by prime versus composite indices. Conjecture: The two groups of partial quotients always obey the same Gauss–Kuzmin distribution; independence of the grouping is certified by the absence of index correlation. (equal distribution of partial quotients by index type)

**中文。** 定义：π 的部分商的指标分类指按素数与合数指标分组统计。猜想：两组部分商恒服从同一 Gauss–Kuzmin 分布；分组独立性由指标无相关验证。（部分商素合同分布）
