**English.** Definition: self-adjoint minimal operator of Rubio de Francia operator: extrapolation. Conjecture: most small norm of fixed point of extrapolation operator is explicit function; function of norm is explicit kernel expression of integral equation of unique solution and positive of kernel is equivalent to weight class embedding. (extrapolation operator fixed point norm)

**中文。** 定义：Rubio de Francia 算子：外插的自伴极小算子。猜想：外插算子的不动点的最小范数为初始范数的显式单调函数；函数为唯一解的积分方程的显式核形式且核的正性等价于权类嵌入。（外插算子不动点范数）
