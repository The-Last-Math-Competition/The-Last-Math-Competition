**WEX-30 △** — source: 313_加权不等式与外插.md

**English.** Definition: The Rubio de Francia operator: the self-adjoint minimal operator of extrapolation. Conjecture: The minimal norm of fixed points of the extrapolation operator is an explicit monotone function of the initial norm; the function is the explicit kernel form of a uniquely solvable integral equation, and the positivity of the kernel is equivalent to the weight class embedding. (extrapolation operator fixed-point norm)

**中文。** 定义：Rubio de Francia 算子：外插的自伴极小算子。猜想：外插算子的不动点的最小范数为初始范数的显式单调函数；函数为唯一解的积分方程的显式核形式且核的正性等价于权类嵌入。（外插算子不动点范数）
