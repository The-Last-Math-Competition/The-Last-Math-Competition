**English.** Definition: Voronoi evolution: the dynamics of random tessellations. Conjecture: The equilibrium of Voronoi flows: the invariant distribution of dynamical evolution of tessellations is Poisson, with the mixing rate of evolution the renovation rate of cells. (Voronoi renovation rate Poisson invariant mixing)

**中文。** 定义：Voronoi 演化：随机镶嵌的动力学。猜想：Voronoi 流的平衡：镶嵌的动力学演化的不变分布为 Poisson 且演化的混合率为胞的翻新率。（Voronoi 翻新率 Poisson 不变混合）
