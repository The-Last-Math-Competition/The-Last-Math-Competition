# 00000002639

**English.** Definition: Degeneration of Jacobi forms at exponent 0: the restriction as modular forms at z = 0. Conjecture: The generation of the kernel of the restriction map: the kernel is controlled by a lower bound on the degree in z, and the minimal generators of the kernel are explicit χ_{k,m} type Jacobi forms; the weight spectrum of the generators is the integer points of [k, k+m]. (degeneration kernel generation spectrum)

**中文。** 定义：Jacobi 形式在指数 0 的退化：z=0 的模形式限制。猜想：限制映射的核的生成：核由变量 z 的次数下界控制且核的最小生成元为 χ_{k,m} 型显式 Jacobi 形式；生成元的权谱为区间 [k, k+m] 的整点。（退化核生成谱）
