# 00000001762

**English.** Definition: The character of a tilting module T(λ) of an algebraic group is its formal character (depending only on the p-canonical basis of Weyl modules). Conjecture: The dimension of T((p−1)ρ+μ) equals p^{#Δ}·dim T₁(μ) (tensor Steinberg); and the character of general T(λ) is given explicitly by coefficient recursions of the p-canonical basis.

**中文。** 定义：代数群的 tilting 模 T(λ) 的特征指其形式特征标(仅依赖 Weyl 模的 p-canonical 基)。猜想：T((p−1)ρ+μ) 的维数 = p^{#Δ}·dim T_1(μ)(张量 Steinberg);一般的 T(λ) 特征由 p-canonical 基的系数递归显式。
