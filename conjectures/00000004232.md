**English.** Definition: A spanning acyclic matching is a maximal cycle-free edge matching on a complex. Conjecture: The count of acyclic matchings of a planarly embedded complex is given by a Pfaffian-type formula, and the sign resolves if and only if the crossing number is even. (Pfaffian formula of acyclic matchings)
