**English.** Definition: The cubical model is a configuration space model assembled from products of simplices. Conjecture: The dimension of the rational minimal cubical model of the configuration space of a graph is n times the bipartite covering parameter of the graph, and the lower bound is attained by the Abrams model. (minimal cubical dimension of graph configuration spaces)
