# 00000002876

**English.** Definition: Novikov-type inequalities: the index side of Morse theory for closed 1-forms. Conjecture: Additivity of Novikov numbers: the index sum of gradient-type flows is the Euler characteristic over the Novikov ring, with additivity deficit given by the multiplicities of resonant layers. (Novikov additive resonance deficit)

**中文。** 定义：Novikov 型不等式：闭 1-形式的 Morse 理论的指标侧。猜想：Novikov 数的可加性：梯度型流的指标和为 Novikov 环上的 Euler 且可加性的亏损由共振层的重数给出。（Novikov 可加共振亏损）
