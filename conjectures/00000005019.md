# 00000005019

**English.** Definition: The Berger holonomy list is the complete classification of irreducible holonomies. Conjecture: The holonomy group is completely determined by the dimension of the parallel spinor space plus one cubic invariant, the determination table is closed over the whole list with no collisions, and the cubic invariant degenerates to the cross-product structure-constant invariant in the G_2 case. (spinor dimension plus cubic determines holonomy)

**中文。** 定义：Berger 和乐列表指不可约和乐的完全分类。猜想：和乐群由平行旋量空间维数加一个三次型不变量完全决定,决定表对全类封闭且无碰撞。(旋量维数加三次型决定和乐)；且三次型在 G₂ 情形退化为叉积结构常数不变量。
