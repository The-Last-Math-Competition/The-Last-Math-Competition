**English.** Definition: algebra structure (Derksen–Weyman theory) of invariant of invariant ring: quiver representation. Conjecture: Hilbert series of invariant ring is explicit positive formula: series is inclusion-exclusion expansion of tree monomial and every term coefficient is pole spectrum of nonnegative combinatorial quantity; series and dimension to quantity spectrum uniform of Schofield. (invariant Hilbert positive)

**中文。** 定义：半不变量环：箭图表示的半不变量的代数结构（Derksen–Weyman 理论）。猜想：半不变量环的 Hilbert 级数为显式正性公式：级数为树单项式的容斥展开且每项系数为非负组合量；级数的极点谱与 Schofield 链的维数向量谱一致。（半不变量 Hilbert 正性）
