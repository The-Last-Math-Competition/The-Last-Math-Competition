# 00000008953

**ISO-03 ★** — source: 284_孤立子与逆散射的代数.md

**English.** Definition: A Lax pair: a commuting pair of operators. Conjecture: Its spectral curve has genus N-1 and forms an N-dimensional analytic family under initial-data translations. (Lax spectral curve genus family)

**中文。** 定义：Lax 对指可交换的算子对。猜想：其谱曲线的亏格为 N-1 且在初值平移下形成 N 维解析族。（Lax 谱曲线亏格族）
