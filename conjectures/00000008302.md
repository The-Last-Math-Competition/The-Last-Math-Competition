# 00000008302

**FLO-12 ★** — source: 262_Floer同调与辛刚性.md

**English.** Definition: The Charette bound for Reeb orbit indices in symplectic homology: the minimal index min_idx of the symplectic homology of a contact manifold (Y,xi). Conjecture: min_idx (the lower bound for the Conley-Zehnder indices of Reeb orbits) is at least the conversion 2(dim Y + 1)/2 - 3 (a conversion lower bound); the tightness of the bound (the smallest contact manifold attaining it) is the standard sphere S^{2n-1} (a standard sphere tightness law); and the stability domain of the spectral gaps of the filtered symplectic homology is the nondegenerate locus (a nondegenerate stability law), with the collapse outside renormalized by Borman orbits (a Borman renormalization law). (Reeb index Charette standard sphere tightness)

**中文。** 定义：辛同调的 Reeb 轨道指数的 Charette 界：接触流形 (Y,ξ) 的辛同调的极小指数 min_idx。猜想：min_idx（Reeb 轨道的 Conley–Zehnder 指数的下界）≥ 2·(dim Y+1)/2-3 的换算（换算下界）；且界的紧（达到的最小接触流形）为标准球 S^{2n-1}（标准球紧律）；辛同调的滤过的谱的间隙（谱间隙）的稳定域为非简并（非简并稳定域律），域外的坍塌（简并坍塌）的重整化为 Borman 轨道（Borman 重整律）。
