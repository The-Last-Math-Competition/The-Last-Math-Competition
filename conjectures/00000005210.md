# 00000005210

**English.** Definition: Noncommutative rank and tensor rigidity are two strength measures. Conjecture: There exist two matrix spaces with the same noncommutative rank but different tensor rigidity, and the separation is realized by an explicit symbolic pair. (same noncommutative rank-different rigidity symbolic separation)

**中文。** 定义：非交换秩与张量刚性指两个强度度量。猜想：存在两矩阵空间的非交换秩相同而张量刚性不同,分离由显式符号对实现。（非交换秩同-刚性异符号分离）
