# 00000002268

**MG-08 ★** — source: 61_鞅与随机分析.md

**English.** Definition: A U-statistic is a symmetric mean of a kernel h. Conjecture: The Berry–Esseen constant of U-statistics with degenerate kernels is an explicit functional of the first eigenvalue of the kernel spectrum (U-statistic BE).

**中文。** 定义：U-统计量(核 h 的对称平均)。猜想：退化核的 U-统计量的 Berry–Esseen 常数为核的谱的第一特征值的显式泛函(U-统计 BE)。
