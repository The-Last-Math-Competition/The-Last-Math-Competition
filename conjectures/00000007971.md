# 00000007971

**CD3-11 ★** — source: 251_编码理论III_列表解码与局部译码.md

**English.** Definition: The threshold saturation analysis of LDPC codes in the waterfall region: the gap between the message-passing decoding threshold sigma_MP on BMS channels and the MAP threshold sigma_MAP. Conjecture: Spatially coupled LDPC codes saturate to the MAP threshold (the saturation principle), with convergence rate 1/L^2 (L the coupling length); the error exponent of the waterfall curve (the rising exponent of the error floor) is -n E(sigma), E(sigma) being the saddle-point value of an explicit EXIT functional; and the expansion of E has a square-root singularity at the threshold (mean-field phase transition class), with universal critical exponent 1/2 for all symmetric channels. (coupled LDPC threshold saturation rate)

**中文。** 定义：LDPC 码的瀑布区分析的门限饱和：消息传递译码在 BMS 信道上的译码门限 σ_MP 与 MAP 门限 σ_MAP 的差距。猜想：空间耦合 LDPC 码的门限饱和到 MAP 门限（饱和原理），且收敛速度为 1/L²（L 为耦合长度）；瀑布曲线的误差指数（error floor 的上升指数）为 -n·E(σ)，E(σ) 为显式 EXIT 泛函的鞍点值；E 的展开在门限处有平方根奇性（相变均场类），临界指数 1/2 对一切对称信道普适。
