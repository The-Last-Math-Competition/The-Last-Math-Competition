**English.** Definition: Persistence landscapes as embeddings of barcodes into function spaces. Conjecture: The reconstruction error of landscapes is the inverse power of the truncation order with power exponent the dimension harmonics; and the distribution of the error is uniform in the truncation window. (inverse-power landscape truncation error)
