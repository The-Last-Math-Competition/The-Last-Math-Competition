# 00000009545

**English.** Definition: The channel simulation cost R(phi to psi) is the minimal per-copy number of uses of phi, with free shared entanglement, needed to simulate psi; simulating the quantum identity by bidirectional classical channels is teleportation. Conjecture: The cost of simulating the d-dimensional quantum identity by classical bits plus entanglement is exactly 2 log d bits, the constant 2 being the exact characterization of the information causality principle; and the simulation costs of general channel pairs form a pseudo-norm duality quotient satisfying the triangle inequality with no ties. (channel simulation cost)

**中文。** 定义：信道模拟成本 R(φ→ψ) 为用信道 φ 加免费共享纠缠模拟 ψ 的最小每拷贝使用次数；双向经典信道模拟量子恒等即远程传态。猜想：用经典比特加纠缠模拟 d 维量子恒等的成本恰为 2 log d 比特，常数 2 是信息因果原理的精确表征；一般信道对的模拟成本构成伪范数对偶商，满足三角不等式且无平局。（信道模拟成本）
