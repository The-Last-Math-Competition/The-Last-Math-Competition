**English.** Definition: Quasiperiodic operators: the spectrum of the almost Mathieu operator. Conjecture: The spectral purity of AMO: the almost Mathieu operator has pure point spectrum for coupling above 2, with the spectrum at critical coupling singular continuous by an explicit criterion. (AMO critical singular continuous criterion)
