# 00000006406

**English.** Definition: The complement operation is the symmetric-difference structure of complements. Conjecture: The complement operation is always an isometry of the difference metric and the composition of complements is an involution, the verification of the isometry being the De Morgan identity. (isometric involution of the complement operation)

**中文。** 定义：余运算为补集的对称差结构。猜想：余运算恒为差度量的等距且余的复合为对合；且等距的验证为德摩根恒等式。（余运算等距对合）
