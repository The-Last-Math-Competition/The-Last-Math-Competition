# 00000008287

**English.** Definition: The universal approximation depth of filters: the depth dependence depth_fil of the approximation rate of recurrent-network filters. Conjecture: depth_fil (the depth exponent of the approximation rate) is the two-parameter O(k^{-2} log(1/epsilon)) (a two-parameter approximation law); the optimal depth-width trade-off (most economical width per layer) is the square root of the width exponent (a square-root trade-off law); and the lower bound on the parameter count of the minimal architecture for filter universality (approximation of all finite-dimensional filters) is quadratic in the state dimension (a quadratic parameter law). (filter depth two-parameter square-root trade-off)

**中文。** 定义：滤波的深度的普适逼近：递归网络的滤波的逼近率的深度依赖 depth_fil。猜想：depth_fil（逼近率的深度指数）= O(k^{-2}·log(1/ε)) 的双参（双参逼近律）；且深度的最优（每层的宽度的最省）的权衡为宽度的指数的根号（根号权衡律）；滤波的普适（一切有限维滤波的逼近）的最小架构的参数数的下界为状态维数的二次（二次参数律）。
