**English.** Definition: Cut-free proof counting is the enumeration of cut-free proof nets. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit generating function, rational for small fragments (the unit fragment); the counting asymptotics is Catalan type, with exponent the conclusion count; and rationality fails when exponentials are included, the failure of transcendental type. (rational counting conversion of cut-free proofs)

**中文。** 定义：切自由证明计数：无 cut 证明网的枚举。猜想：换算公式为生成函数的显式且小片段（单位片段）时为有理函数；计数的渐近为 Catalan 型且指数为结论数；有理性在含指数时失效且失效为超越型。（切自由证明有理计数换算）
