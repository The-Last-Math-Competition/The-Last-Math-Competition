**English.** Definition: Ergodic recurrence along twin primes denotes the convergence of multiple-recurrence averages taken along twin-prime pairs. Conjecture: For every measure-preserving system and every set of positive measure, the recurrence limit along twin-prime pairs always exists and equals the square of the measure. (twin-prime ergodic recurrence)

**中文。** 定义：孪生素数的遍历递归指多重递归在孪生素数对上的收敛。猜想：对每个保测系统与正测度集，孪生素数对的递归极限恒存在且等于测度平方。（孪生素数遍历递归）
