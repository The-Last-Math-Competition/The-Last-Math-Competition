# 00000008034

**OMN-14 ★** — source: 253_o极小与驯拓扑.md

**English.** Definition: The o-minimal boundary of differential algebraic geometry: the minimal structure D of the DAE-closure (o-minimal closure of solution sets of differential polynomial systems, differential algebraic sets over R). Conjecture: D is an extension of R_exp and the extension is conservative (counting bounds preserved); the Pila-type counting for differential algebraic curves is H^epsilon; the exception: when solutions containing dx/dt = x (genuine exponentials) are included, the exponent of the compactified counting bound rises by 1/2 (a differential exponent-counting exchange law), the exchange being controlled by the decay of Mahler measures of Taylor coefficients. (differential algebraic conservative exponent exchange)

**中文。** 定义：微分代数几何的 o-极小边界：微分多项式方程组的解集（R 上微分代数集）的 o-极小闭包 DAE-闭包的最小结构 D。猜想：D 为 R_exp 的扩张且扩张为保守（保持 counting 界）；微分代数曲线的 Pila 型计数为 H^{ε}；例外：包含 dx/dt=x 的解（真指数）时计数紧化界的指数为 1/2 的提升（微分指数-计数交换律），交换律由 Taylor 系数的 Mahler 测度的衰减控制。
