# 00000008531

**POS-01 ★** — source: 270_偏序集与格理论.md

**English.** Definition: The Dedekind number M(n) is the number of elements of the free distributive lattice (the number of monotone Boolean functions). Conjecture: log M(n) = binom(n, floor(n/2))(1+o(1)) (the main term central law); the second term is a Kleitman type logarithmic-loss correction, and the exact asymptotic constant after correction exists; and the reachable threshold of numerical verification is n at most 11. (Dedekind asymptotic constant)

**中文。** 定义：Dedekind 数 M(n) 指自由分配格的元素个数（单调布尔函数个数）。猜想：log M(n) = binom(n,⌊n/2⌋)·(1+o(1)) 的主项中心律；次主项为对数损失的 Kleitman 型修正且修正后的精确渐近常数存在；数值验证的可达阈为 n ≤ 11。（Dedekind 渐近常数）
