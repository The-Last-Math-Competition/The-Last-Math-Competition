**AG5-08 ★** — source: 128_交换群论.md

**English.** Definition: A completely decomposable group is a direct sum of rank-1 groups, and the direct-summand characterization is the type characterization of its direct summands. Conjecture: The family of necessary conditions characterizing direct summands is mutually independent, the minimal rank of an independence counterexample is 4, and the counterexamples are constructed explicitly via Corner realization by rings. (independent counterexamples of direct-summand characterization)

**中文。** 定义：完全分解群指秩 1 群的直和,直和项刻画指其直和项的型刻画。猜想：刻画直和项的必要条件族相互独立,独立反例的最小秩为 4,反例由环的 Corner 实现显式构造。（直和项刻画独立反例）
