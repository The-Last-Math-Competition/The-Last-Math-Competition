# 00000002563

**CM-03 ★** — source: 71_计算复杂性中的数学.md

**English.** Definition: Fourier rigidity is the difficulty of sparse Fourier approximation of a function. Conjecture: Explicit function families f_n require t ≥ n^{1/2}·log n terms for Fourier-sparse approximation (with explicit constants); the lower bound follows from the L¹ concentration obstruction to Parseval. (Fourier rigidity sparse approximation)

**中文。** 定义：Fourier 刚性指函数的 Fourier 稀疏逼近的难度。猜想：显式函数族 f_n 的 Fourier 稀疏逼近需 t ≥ n^{1/2}·log n 项(显式常数);下界由 Parseval 的 L¹ 集中障碍。
