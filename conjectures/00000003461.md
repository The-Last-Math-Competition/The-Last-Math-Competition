# 00000003461

**English.** Definition: DP-coloring of graphs is the multiplicative generalization of coloring (corresponding to point-cover simulated coloring of corresponding covers), with DP-chromatic number χ_DP defined by Bernshteyn–Kostochka–Pron. Conjecture: The DP-chromatic number of planar graphs satisfies χ_DP ≤ 5, and planar graphs of girth at least five satisfy χ_DP ≤ 3 in the DP framework; the difference spectrum between DP and classical chromatic numbers takes exactly the two values zero and one on the planar class, with the smallest graph attaining one the three-dimensional prism. (DP planar threshold)

**中文。** 定义：图的 DP-染色为染色的乘性推广（对应点划覆盖的模拟染色），DP-色数 χ_DP 由 Bernshteyn–Kostochka–Pron 定义。猜想：平面图的 DP-色数满足 χ_DP ≤ 5 且 DP-框架下围长至少五的平面图 χ_DP ≤ 3；DP 与经典色数的差谱在平面类上恰取零与一两个值且取一的最小图为三维双棱柱。（DP 平面阈值）
