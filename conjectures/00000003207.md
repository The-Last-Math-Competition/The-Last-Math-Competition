# 00000003207

**English.** Definition: Syzygies of modules: generation of relations. Conjecture: Minimal generation of syzygies: the length of the minimal free resolution is the projective dimension, and the Betti table of the resolution records the graded Betti numbers. (syzygy Betti table projective dimension)

**中文。** 定义：模的 Syzygy：关系的生成。猜想：Syzygy 的极小生成：极小自由分解的长度为投射维数且分解的 Betti 表为分次贝蒂数的记录。（Syzygy Betti 表投射维数）
