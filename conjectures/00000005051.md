# 00000005051

**English.** Definition: The Anderson model is the Schrodinger operator of a lattice random potential. Conjecture: In the deep-energy region inside the three-dimensional band, the asymptotic constant of position moments is an explicit power of the diffusion coefficient, the power is half the moment order, and the constant agrees with the homogenization limit. (homogenization constant of Anderson position moments)

**中文。** 定义：Anderson 模型指格点随机位势的薛定谔算子。猜想：三维带内深能区的位置矩渐近常数为扩散系数的显式幂,幂为矩阶之半且常数与均匀化极限一致。（Anderson 位置矩均匀化常数）
