# 00000007462

**English.** Definition: The spectrum of the numerical range, Wintner. Conjecture: The spectrum lies in the closure of the numerical range, and the extremum of the closure is the normal operator. (normal-extremal Wintner inclusion)

**中文。** 定义：数值域的谱：Wintner。猜想：数值域包含：谱含于数值域闭包且包的极值为正常算子。（数值域 Wintner 正常算子谱包含闭包极值谱）
