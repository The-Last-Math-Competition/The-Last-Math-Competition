# 00000001788

**CV-08 ★** — source: 45_多复变II.md

**English.** Definition: The relative capacity cap(E, Ω). Conjecture: The relative capacity of the ellipsoid in the ball is a Gamma-function closed form (the ellipsoid capacity); and the closed form is a Gamma-function product in the axis ratio of the ellipsoid.

**中文。** 定义：相对容量 cap(E, Ω)。猜想：椭球在球中的相对容量为 Γ 函数闭式(椭球容量)；且闭式由椭球轴比的 Gamma 函数乘积表示。
