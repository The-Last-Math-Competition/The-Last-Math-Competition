# 00000005250

**English.** Definition: The theta divisor and the moduli space are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two moduli spaces with isomorphic theta divisors but non-isomorphic moduli spaces, and the separation is realized by an explicit covering pair with the same theta but different moduli. (same theta-different moduli covering separation)

**中文。** 定义：θ 除子与模空间指两层。猜想：存在两模空间的 θ 除子同构而模空间不同构,分离由同 θ 异模的显式覆叠对实现。（θ 同构-模异覆叠分离）
