# 00000009585

**MSH-05 ★** — source: 305_高维极小曲面.md

**English.** Definition: The Simons cone C in R^8 is a minimal cone of the area functional and unstable; among all codimension-1 minimal cones the Simons cone is fundamental for singularity analysis. Conjecture: The Simons cone is isolated in the space of minimal cones: any sufficiently C^1-close minimal cone coincides with it; the isolation radius is given by an explicit constant of the spherical spectral gap, the gap between two adjacent Dirichlet eigenvalues. (Simons cone isolation rigidity)

**中文。** 定义：Simons 锥 C⊂R^8 为面积泛函的极小锥且不稳定；一切余维 1 极小锥中 Simons 锥的地位是奇点分析的基础。猜想：Simons 锥在极小锥空间中孤立：任何充分 C^1 接近的极小锥与重合；孤立半径由球面谱间隙的显式常数给出，谱间隙为两相邻 Dirichlet 特征值之差。（Simons 锥孤立刚性）
