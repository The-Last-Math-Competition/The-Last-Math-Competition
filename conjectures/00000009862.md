# 00000009862

**English.** Definition: The spectral gap of ASEP: the Markov chain gap on rings of length L. Conjecture: The spectral gap of ASEP at length L is asymptotically pi squared over L squared times an explicit constant; the constant is continuous in the asymmetry parameter and returns to the known value of the simple exclusion chain in the fully symmetric limit. (ASEP spectral gap explicit constant)

**中文。** 定义：ASEP 的谱隙：长度 L 环上的马尔可夫链间隙。猜想：ASEP 在长度 L 的谱隙渐近为 π²/L² 乘显式常数；常数在任意不对称参数下连续且在完全对称极限回到简单排队的已知值。（ASEP 谱隙显式常数）
