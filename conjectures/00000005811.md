**English.** Definition: A self-similar source-type solution is the explicit fundamental solution of the porous medium equation. Conjecture: The support radius is given by the explicit integral of the conserved scaling, the constant is exact, and the solution is identically zero outside the support. (conservation integral of source-solution supports)
