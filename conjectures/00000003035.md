# 00000003035

**English.** Definition: Spectral statistics of many-body Anderson models: from Poisson to GOE. Conjecture: The transition of spectral statistics: under the disorder-interaction competition the spectral-gap ratio distribution transitions from Poisson to GOE along an explicit interpolation curve, with the midpoint of the interpolation the critical disorder. (gap ratio transition interpolation curve)
