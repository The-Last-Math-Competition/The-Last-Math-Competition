**English.** Definition: Harnack constant: mesh geometry of discrete harmonic function and relations of estimate constant. Conjecture: discrete Harnack constant is explicit exponent function; constant in θ_min -> 0 when diverges of most small in angle θ_min and diverges is constant of logarithm type, positive three angle mesh is uniformly bounded. (discrete Harnack explicit constant)
