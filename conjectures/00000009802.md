# 00000009802

**English.** Definition: Harnack constants of discrete harmonic functions: the relation between mesh geometry and estimate constants. Conjecture: The discrete Harnack constant is an explicit exponential function of the minimal interior angle theta_min; the constant diverges as theta_min tends to 0 at logarithmic rate, and for positive (non-obtuse) triangle meshes the constant is uniformly bounded. (discrete Harnack explicit constant)

**中文。** 定义：离散调和函数的 Harnack 常数：网格几何与估计常数的关系。猜想：离散 Harnack 常数为最小内角 θ_min 的显式指数函数；常数在 θ_min→0 时发散且发散率为对数型，正三角网格的常数为一致有界。（离散 Harnack 显式常数）
