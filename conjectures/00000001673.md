# 00000001673

**English.** Definition: The inducibility of C₆ is its maximal asymptotic density as an induced subgraph of n-vertex graphs. Conjecture: ind(C₆) = (√3−1)/2; the extremal configuration is the balanced blow-up of the triangular prism, uniquely; and uniqueness is verified by the stabilizer structure of triangular-prism blow-ups.

**中文。** 定义：C₆ 的诱导度 inducibility 指其在 n 点图中作为诱导子图的最大渐近密度。猜想：ind(C₆)=(√3−1)/2;极值构型为三角柱的平衡 blow-up,唯一；且唯一性由三角柱 blow-up 的稳定子结构验证。
