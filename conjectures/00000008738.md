# 00000008738

**MBL-28 ★** — source: 276_多体局域化与随机薛定谔.md

**English.** Definition: Locally accessible information: the distinguishable information about initial states carried by remote observables. Conjecture: In the thermal phase local information tends to zero (a zero-tendency law); in the MBL phase local information is finite (a finiteness law); the upper bound on local information is given by l-bit overlaps (an upper bound law); and equality saturates in deep MBL (a saturation law). (locally accessible information l-bit saturation)

**中文。** 定义：局域可及信息：远端观测量对初态的可分辨信息。猜想：热相中局域信息趋零（趋零律）；且 MBL 相中局域信息有限（有限律）；局域信息的上界由 l-比特 重叠给出（上界律）；等号在深 MBL 饱和（饱和律）。
