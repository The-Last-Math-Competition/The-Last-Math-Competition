# 00000005588

**English.** Definition: The high-rank completeness theorem is the signalizer theorem for rank at least three. Conjecture: The squared closure of balanced generators is given at rank three by pairwise-balanced triangular chains, and the kernel of the chain is the family of root subgroups. (triangular balance chain of high-rank completeness)

**中文。** 定义：高秩完备定理指秩不小于三的信号定理。猜想：均衡生成子的平方闭包在秩三时由两两平衡的三角链给出；且链的核为根子群系。（高秩完备根子群系三角平衡链）
