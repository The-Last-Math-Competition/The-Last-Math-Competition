**FA2-07 ★** — source: 123_高等数理逻辑_强制法与公理.md

**English.** Definition: A symmetric submodel is the submodel obtained by fixing a forcing fragment under a symmetry group. Conjecture: The minimal truncation of the rank function of a symmetric model that is definable is level omega, and every finite-level truncation is non-definable. (definability threshold of symmetric rank truncations)

**中文。** 定义：对称子模型指经对称群固定力迫片段所得的子模型。猜想：对称模型秩函数截断的可定义性最小截断为第 ω 层,一切有限层截断均不可定义。（对称秩截断可定义阈）
