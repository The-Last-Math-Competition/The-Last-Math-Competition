**English.** Definition: The BPS transformation spectrum is the wall-crossing structure data of a quiver. Conjecture: There exist two quivers with completely identical BPS spectra that are not isomorphic, and the minimal number of vertices of the separation is 3. (BPS spectrum-quiver separation with 3 vertices)
