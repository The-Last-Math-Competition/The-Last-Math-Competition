**GL2-17 ◆** — source: 132_图极限与网络数学.md

**English.** Definition: A W-random graph is the window-sampled random graph with a graphon as kernel. Conjecture: The error constant of its counting lemma is an explicit function of the L-infinity norm of the kernel, the error decays exponentially in the reciprocal of the kernel regularity, and the constant is 1. (W-random counting error constant 1)

**中文。** 定义：W-随机图指以 graphon 为核的按窗采样随机图。猜想：其计数引理的误差常数为核 L 无穷范数的显式函数,误差随核正则性倒数指数衰减且常数为 1。（W-随机计数误差常数 1）
