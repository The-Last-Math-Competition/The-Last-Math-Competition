**English.** Definition: The Bercovici-Pata correspondence is the correspondence between classical and freely infinitely divisible laws. Conjecture: The classical limit error of the correspondence is an explicit power of the reciprocal of the variance, consistent with the small-variance expansion and with explicitly controlled remainder. (power law of the BP correspondence error)

**中文。** 定义：Bercovici–Pata 对应指经典与自由无穷可分律间的对应。猜想：对应的经典极限误差为方差倒数的显式幂次,与小方差展开一致且余项显式控制。（BP 对应误差幂次律）
