# 00000009044

**English.** Definition: The Reilly functional: higher-order curvature integrals. Conjecture: Its extrema are attained on spheres, and the dimension of the negative subspace of the spectrum of the second variation is the index of the submanifold. (Reilly sphere extremum index)

**中文。** 定义：Reilly 泛函指高阶曲率积分。猜想：其极值在球面达到且第二变分的谱的负子空间维数为曲面子流形指标。（Reilly 球面极值指标）
