# 00000008105

**English.** Definition: The p-local rigidity of homological Euler classes of fusion systems: the polynomial rigidity poly(F) of the ring structure of H^*(F;F_p). Conjecture: The Krull dimension of poly(F) equals rank(S) (a dimension-rank formula); the Castelnuovo-Mumford regularity of H^* is -1 (no regularity defect); and the minimal fusion where the Evens-Feshbach type depth-dimension equality (depth = dimension) fails is a rank-3 exotic system (minimal exotic rank 3 for the depth defect), the defect being quantized at 1 modulo p (defect quantization). (cohomology dimension-rank depth defect quantization)

**中文。** 定义：融合系统的同调 Euler 类的 p-局部刚性：H^*(F;F_p) 的环结构的多项式刚性 poly(F)。猜想：poly(F) 的 Krull 维数 = rank(S)（维数-秩公式）；且 H^* 的 Castelnuovo–Mumford 正则度为 -1（无正则度缺陷）；Evens–Feshbach 型的深度-维数的等式（深度=维数）的失效的最小融合为秩 3 的 exotic 系（深度缺陷的最小 exotic 秩 3），缺陷量为 1 的模 p（缺陷量子化）。
