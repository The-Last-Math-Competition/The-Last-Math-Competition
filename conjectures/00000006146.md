**DAG2-16 ★** — source: 190_导出代数几何.md

**English.** Definition: The tangent complex is the chain of tangent spaces of derived structures. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit zeroth-order term of the classical tangent space, degenerating to concentration in degree zero when smooth; higher terms are corrected by obstructions, the dimension of the correction equaling the excess of embedding dimension over smooth dimension; and the difference of kernel and cokernel dimensions of the deformation-obstruction map is the generator count. (zeroth-order obstruction correction of tangent spaces)

**中文。** 定义：切复形：导出结构的切空间链条。猜想：换算常数为经典切空间的显式零阶项且光滑时退化为集中度一处；高阶项由障碍修正且修正的维数等于嵌入维超出光滑维数之差；形变障碍映射的核与余核维数差为生成元数。（切空间零阶障碍修正）
