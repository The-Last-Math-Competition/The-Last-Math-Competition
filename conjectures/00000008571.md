# 00000008571

**NSP-11 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: The Berger measure of a subnormal operator: the spectral measure of its minimal normal extension. Conjecture: The Berger measure is uniquely determined by the operator (a uniqueness law); the support of the measure is recursively determined by the moment sequence (a recursion law); the convergence rate of the recursion is quadratic (a quadratic rate law); and the support is finite if and only if the operator is an extension of a finite-rank normal part (a finite support criterion). (Berger measure moment recursion support)

**中文。** 定义：亚正规算子的 Berger 测度：最小正规扩张的谱测度。猜想：Berger 测度由算子唯一决定（唯一律）；且测度支撑由矩序列递归确定（递归律）；递归的收敛速率为二次（二次速率律）；支撑为有限集当且仅当算子为有限秩正规部分的扩张（有限支撑判据）。
