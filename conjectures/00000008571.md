**NSP-11 ★** — source: 271_非自伴算子与无限图谱.md

**English.** Definition: spectral measure of Berger measure: most small normal extension of normal operator. Conjecture: Berger measure by operator unique (unique); and measure support by convergent rate of moment sequence recurrence deterministic (recurrence); recurrence is quadratic (quadratic rate); support is finite set if and only if operator is extension (finite support) of finite rank normal part.

**中文。** 定义：亚正规算子的 Berger 测度：最小正规扩张的谱测度。猜想：Berger 测度由算子唯一决定（唯一律）；且测度支撑由矩序列递归确定（递归律）；递归的收敛速率为二次（二次速率律）；支撑为有限集当且仅当算子为有限秩正规部分的扩张（有限支撑判据）。
