# 00000007527

**English.** Definition: Mixed sheaves as filtered sheaves of mixed weight. Conjecture: Mixedness is closed under the four functors and the closure domain is a triangulated subcategory of the derived category; and the finiteness of the closure is controlled by weight gaps. (four-functor mixed closure)

**中文。** 定义：混合层为权混合的带滤过层。猜想：混性在四函子下封闭且封闭域为导出范畴的三角子范畴；且封闭的有限性由权重间隙控制。（混性四函子闭包）
