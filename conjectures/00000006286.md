**English.** Definition: Model spaces are the combinatorial models of triangulated group systems. Conjecture: The homotopy type of the nerve of subgroup lattices is unique; the realization of uniqueness is spectral homotopy equivalence; and the realization of spectral homotopy equivalence is simplicial approximation. (unique subgroup-lattice nerve homotopy)
