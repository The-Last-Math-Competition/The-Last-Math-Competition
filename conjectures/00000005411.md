# 00000005411

**HM2-01 ◆** — source: 166_分析II_调和测度.md

**English.** Definition: Harmonic measure is the distribution of the first boundary hit of Brownian motion, with the maximal atom weight in the finite-atom case. Conjecture: When the harmonic measure of a planar domain has finitely many atoms, the maximal atom weight is explicit of arctangent type, with the extremal domain attained by unions of slits; the atom weight of a slit at the origin is exactly 1/2; and the number of atoms is at most the number of slits, each slit contributing exactly one atom. (slit extremal of finite atom weights)

**中文。** 定义：调和测度：布朗运动首中边界的分布；有限原子情形的最大原子权重。猜想：平面域调和测度取有限原子时最大原子权重为反正切型显式且极值域由裂隙的并达到；原点处裂隙的原子权重恰为 1/2；原子数不超过裂隙数且每个裂隙贡献恰一个原子。（有限原子权重裂隙极值）
