# 00000005623

**CP2-03 ★** — source: 173_组合几何III_凸位置.md

**English.** Definition: The prime necessity is the phenomenon that parameters must be prime powers. Conjecture: The counterexample family of non-prime-power parameters is realized by explicit torsion configurations, the change of variables of the configuration space is explicit, and the family is closed, with the Jacobian of the change of variables being exactly the torsion-group determinant. (torsion-configuration counterexample family of non-prime powers)

**中文。** 定义：素数必要性指参数须素数幂的现象。猜想：非素幂参数的反例族由显式挠配置实现,配置空间的积分换元显式且族封闭。(非素幂挠配置反例族)；且配置空间换元的雅可比恰为挠群行列式。
