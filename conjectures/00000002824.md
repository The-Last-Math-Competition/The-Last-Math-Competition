**English.** Definition: Large deviations of queueing networks: the structure of congestion paths. Conjecture: The large-deviation paths of queues: the most likely congestion path is the inversion of the deterministic fluid limit, and the rate constant of the path is the harmonic mean of service rates. (queueing fluid inversion path)

**中文。** 定义：排队网络的大偏差：拥塞路径的结构。猜想：排队的大偏差路径：拥塞的最可能路径为确定性流体极限的反演且路径的速率常数为服务率的调和均值。（排队流体反演路径）
