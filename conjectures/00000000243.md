**English.** Definition: A Petersen coloring of a bridgeless cubic graph is a vertex-preserving map from its edges to the edges of the Petersen graph. Conjecture (Petersen coloring conjecture): Every bridgeless cubic graph admits a Petersen coloring (which implies the cycle double cover and the 5-flow conjecture).

**中文。** 猜想：定义:Petersen 染色:桥-free 三正则图的边到 Petersen 图边的保顶点映射。猜想(Petersen 染色猜想):每个无桥三正则图有 Petersen 染色(蕴含双圈覆盖与 5-流)。
