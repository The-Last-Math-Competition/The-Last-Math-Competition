# 00000001920

**English.** Definition: Bourgain's discrete restriction is the restriction estimate for the paraboloid over Z/pZ. Conjecture: The periodic behavior of the restriction constant as p → ∞ is always given explicitly by the phase of p mod 4; and the phase flips at prime squares. (discrete restriction mod-four phase)

**中文。** 定义：Bourgain 的离散限制指 Z/pZ 上抛物面的限制估计。猜想：p 趋于无穷的限制常数的周期恒由 p 模四的相位显式给出；且相位的翻转在素数平方处。（离散限制模四相位）
