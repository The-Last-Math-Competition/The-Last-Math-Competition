# 00000003918

**English.** Definition: The Batalin-Vilkovisky operator Delta is the square-zero operator of degree -1 on Floer cohomology. Conjecture: Higher operators Delta_k of degree -k exist on every closed symplectic manifold and, for k <= n, together with Delta generate a Gerstenhaber structure; no nonzero operator of degree below -n exists. (BV higher operator threshold)

**中文。** 定义：Batalin–Vilkovisky 算子 Δ 指 Floer 上同调上度 −1 的平方为零算子。猜想：度为 −k 的高阶算子 Δ_k 存在于一切闭辛流形且对 k ≤ n 与 Δ 生成 Gerstenhaber 结构;不存在度低于 −n 的非零算子。（BV 高阶算子阈）
