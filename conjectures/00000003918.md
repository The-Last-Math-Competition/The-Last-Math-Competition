# 00000003918

**FL-08 ★** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: Batalin–Vilkovisky operator Δ denotes square of Floer cohomology on −1 is zero operator. Conjecture: is high order operator Δ_k exists in all closed symplectic manifold of −k and pair k ≤ n and non- zero operator of Δ generates Gerstenhaber structure; there does not exist in −n. (BV high order operator)

**中文。** 定义：Batalin–Vilkovisky 算子 Δ 指 Floer 上同调上度 −1 的平方为零算子。猜想：度为 −k 的高阶算子 Δ_k 存在于一切闭辛流形且对 k ≤ n 与 Δ 生成 Gerstenhaber 结构;不存在度低于 −n 的非零算子。（BV 高阶算子阈）
