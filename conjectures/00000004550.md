# 00000004550

**TR3-10 △** — source: 137_数论V_超越方法.md

**English.** Definition: Super-approximation is the construction of irrationality exponents strictly exceeding two. Conjecture: There exists an explicit family of Mahler function values whose irrationality exponents diverge at any prescribed rate, and the divergent construction is unified and free of exponential towers. (divergence spectrum of Mahler super-approximation)

**中文。** 定义：超逼近指无理度指数严格超过二的构造。猜想：存在 Mahler 函数值的显式族其无理度指数按任意预给速率发散,发散构造统一且不含指数塔。（超逼近 Mahler 发散谱）
