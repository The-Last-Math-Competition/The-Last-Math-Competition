**English.** Definition: The cobordism ring and the motivic spectrum are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two motivic spectra with isomorphic cobordism rings but non-isomorphic spectra, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same ring but different unit spectra. (same cobordism ring-different motivic spectrum unit separation)
