# 00000002373

**English.** Definition: A spectral maximal space is a maximal spectral subspace. Conjecture: For nonnormal operators, the dimension spectrum of spectral maximal spaces equals the spectral multiplicity; and the spectral multiplicity is a chain of minimal polynomials. (spectral maximal space dimension spectral multiplicity)
