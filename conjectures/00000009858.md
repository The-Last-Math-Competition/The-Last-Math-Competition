**English.** Definition: Intermediate disorder: polymer exponent drift within the critical temperature window. Conjecture: The temperature width of the intermediate-disorder window shrinks as the negative fourth power of the system size; within the window the limit is the diffusive directed polymer, and the leading Edgeworth correction coefficient relates explicitly to the fourth cumulant. (intermediate disorder window width)

**中文。** 定义：中间无序：临界温度窗口的聚合物指数漂移。猜想：中间无序窗口的温度宽度按系统尺寸的负四分之一次幂收缩；窗口内极限为扩散定向聚合物且 Edgeworth 修正的首系数与第四累积量显式相关。（中间无序窗口宽度）
