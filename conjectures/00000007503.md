# 00000007503

**MP6-23 ★** — source: 235_母题与周期II.md

**English.** Definition: The l-independence of motives as the prime independence of realizations. Conjecture: The independence of realizations always holds on compact varieties; and the dimension of the exceptional variety is strictly smaller than the dimension of the motive. (l-independence exceptional-variety deficit)

**中文。** 定义：母题的 ℓ-独立指实现的素数无关性。猜想：实现的无关性在紧簇上恒成立；且例外簇的维数严格小于母题的维数。（l-独立例外簇维数亏）
