# 00000005020

**English.** Definition: The Dirac spectrum and counting invariants are two layers. Conjecture: There exist two Spin(7) manifolds with the same Dirac spectrum but different calibration counts, and the separation is realized by an explicit family of isospectral deformations. (same Dirac spectrum-different calibration count separation)

**中文。** 定义：狄拉克谱与计数不变量指两层。猜想：存在两 Spin(7) 流形的狄拉克谱相同而校准计数不同,分离由等谱变形的显式族实现。（狄拉克谱同-校准计数异分离）
