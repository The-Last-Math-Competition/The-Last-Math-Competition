**English.** Definition: A cartesian fibration is a fibred category with pullback lifts, and the Beck-Chevalley condition is the commutativity of summation with pullback. Conjecture: The minimal axiom set for this condition contains exactly one axiom, and this axiom is independent of the remaining axioms of the fibration. (independence of the single Beck-Chevalley axiom)

**中文。** 定义：笛卡尔纤维指带拉回提升的纤维范畴,Beck–Chevalley 条件指求和与拉回的交换律。猜想：该条件的最小公理集恰含一条,且这条独立于纤维化的其余公理。（Beck–Chevalley 单公理独立）
