# 00000007865

**RAM-25 ★** — source: 247_随机算法的概率分析.md

**English.** Definition: The variance reduction rate r(n,d) of randomized stratified grids for Monte Carlo integration (randomized Latin hypercube plus rank-1 lattice) relative to naive Monte Carlo. Conjecture: For integrand families of Sobolev mixed smoothness s one has r(n,d) = O(n^{-2 min(s,1)+1} (log n)^{d-1}), and the log factors cannot be entirely removed; and the distribution tail of the worst-case error of the optimal randomized lattice generator under random shifts is lognormal, with log-variance d/12 times the leading series term. (randomized QMC variance reduction tail)

**中文。** 定义：Monte Carlo 积分的随机化分层网格（randomized Latin hypercube + rank-1 lattice）的方差消减率 r(n,d)（相对朴素 MC 的方差比）。猜想：对 Sobolev 混合光滑 s 的被积函数族，r(n,d)=O(n^{-2 min(s,1)+1}·(log n)^{d-1})，且 log 因子不可全部去除；最优随机化格的生成向量在随机平移下的最坏-case 误差的分布尾为 lognormal，其 log-方差为 d/(12)·（级数首项）。
