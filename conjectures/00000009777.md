# 00000009777

**English.** Definition: Schatten norms of iterated commutators: norm constants of nested commutators. Conjecture: The Schatten p norm of double commutators satisfies an optimal-constant inequality with explicit constant c_p = 2^{1/p}; the constant is attained by pairs of Clifford algebra generators, and higher commutators grow by iterative factors. (double commutator optimal constant)

**中文。** 定义：多重复交换子的 Schatten 范数：嵌套交换子的范数常数。猜想：双重交换子的 Schatten p 范数满足最优常数不等式且常数 c_p=(2)^{1/p} 的显式值；常数由 Clifford 代数生成元对达到且高阶交换子按迭代因子增长。（双交换子最优常数）
