# 00000002009

**English.** Conjecture: The necessary and sufficient condition for unique continuation of the Ricci–DeTurck flow at conical singularities is that the tangent cone is rigid (i.e. a cone that is an isolated soliton up to isometry); the condition is decided explicitly by the uniqueness of self-similar solutions of the tangent cone.

**中文。** 猜想：Ricci–DeTurck 流在锥奇点处的唯一延拓的充要条件为切锥为刚性(即切锥为锥等度孤立子);条件由切锥的自相似解的唯一性显式判定。
