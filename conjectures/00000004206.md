# 00000004206

**English.** Definition: The linear rigidity group is the first-variation automorphism group of a minimal surface. Conjecture: The linear rigidity quotient size of an embedded minimal surface is always 1, and the smallest example with quotient size 2 is a surface of double-cover type over the projective plane. (two-valued classification of linear rigidity quotients)

**中文。** 定义：线性刚性群指极小曲面的一阶变分自同构群。猜想：嵌入极小曲面的线性刚性商尺寸恒为 1,商尺寸 2 的最小例为射影平面的双覆盖型曲面。（线性刚性商二值分类）
