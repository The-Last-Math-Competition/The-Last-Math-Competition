**English.** Definition: A quartic threefold is a quartic hypersurface in five-dimensional projective space, and conics are rational curves of degree two. Conjecture: Its conic count is given explicitly by Quot-scheme localization, the count is a polynomial of modular invariants and verified at low degree. (conic count of the quartic threefold)

**中文。** 定义：四次三维体指五维射影空间中的四次超曲面,圆锥曲线指二次有理曲线。猜想：其圆锥曲线计数由 Quot 方案局部化显式给出,计数为模不变量的多项式且低度验证。（四次三维体圆锥计数）
