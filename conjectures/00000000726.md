# 00000000726

**English.** Definition: The λ-invariant is the exponent of class-number growth in a Z_p-tower. Conjecture: The λ of the maximal real subfield tower is constant in the level; and the stabilization is attained already at level one. (lambda invariant early stabilization)

**中文。** 定义：λ-不变量为 ℤ_p-塔的类数增长指数。猜想：最大实子域塔的 λ 关于层数为常数；且稳定化的序为层数一即达。（λ 不变量早期稳定）
