# 00000009660

**HSS-20 ★** — source: 307_高维谱统计.md

**English.** Definition: The renormalized correlations of zeros of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line and the GUE correspondence (Montgomery's pair correlation conjecture). Conjecture: The two- through four-point correlation functions of zeta zeros converge, under the generalized Riemann hypothesis and quasiconformal assumptions, to the corresponding GUE kernels; the error term is an explicit prime-power correction affecting only the boundary layer of short-range correlations. (zeta zero GUE correlations)

**中文。** 定义：黎曼 zeta 函数临界线上零点的重整化关联与 GUE 对应（Montgomery 对关联猜想）。猜想：zeta 零点的二至四点关联函数在广义 Riemann 假设与拟共形假设下收敛到 GUE 的对应核；误差项为显式素数幂修正，修正仅影响短程关联的边界层。（zeta 零点 GUE 关联）
