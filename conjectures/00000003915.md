**FL-05 ★** — source: 116_辛拓扑II_Floer同调.md

**English.** Definition: The partial derivative of a spectral invariant is the right limit of c(a, epsilon H)/epsilon as epsilon tends to 0. Conjecture: This limit always exists, equals the Hofer-Zehnder type capacity dual value of the support of a, and the limiting process is monotone and convex. (spectral derivative-capacity duality)

**中文。** 定义：谱不变量的偏导指 ε → 0 时 c(a, εH)/ε 的右极限。猜想：该极限恒存在,等于 a 的支撑的 Hofer–Zehnder 型容量对偶值,且极限过程为单调凸。（谱偏导-容量对偶）
