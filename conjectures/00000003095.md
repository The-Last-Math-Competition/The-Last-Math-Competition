**English.** Definition: Derived geometry: structure sheaves of derived schemes. Conjecture: Affine covers of derived structures: the lifting obstruction of affine covers of derived schemes is the translation of the cotangent complex, and vanishing of the translation is flatness. (derived affine cover flatness obstruction)

**中文。** 定义：导出几何：导出概形的结构层。猜想：导出结构的仿射覆盖：导出概形的仿射覆盖的提升阻碍为余切复形的平移且平移的消没为平坦性。（导出仿射覆盖平坦阻碍）
