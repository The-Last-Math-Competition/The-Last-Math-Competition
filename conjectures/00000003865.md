# 00000003865

**RM3-15 ★** — source: 114_随机矩阵III_beta系综.md

**English.** Definition: β-Ginibre is the β-extension of non-Hermitian random matrices; real eigenvalues are those on the real axis. Conjecture: The expected number of real eigenvalues E(N,β) satisfies: E(N,β) − √(2N/π) is nondecreasing in β and tends to zero as β→∞. (beta Ginibre real eigenvalue monotonicity)

**中文。** 定义：β-Ginibre 指非厄米随机矩阵的 β 推广,实特征值指位于实轴上的特征值。猜想：实特征值期望个数 E(N,β) 满足 E(N,β) − √(2N/π) 为 β 的不减函数,且随 β→∞ 趋于零。（β-Ginibre 实特征值单调）
