# 00000000891

**English.** Definition: The energy of harmonic maps between spheres has a discrete spectrum. Conjecture: For each energy level k, the minimal-energy map is realized by a rotationally symmetric homotopy class; and the gaps of the energy ladder are non-integer multiples of eight π corrected. (harmonic map energy ladder realization)

**中文。** 定义：球面到球面的调和映照能量具有离散谱。猜想：每个能级 k 的极小能量映照由旋转对称同伦类实现；且能梯的间隙为八π的非整数倍修正。（调和映照能梯实现）
