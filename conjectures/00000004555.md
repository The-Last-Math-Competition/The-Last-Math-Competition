**English.** Definition: An exponential polynomial is a finite sum function with exponential terms. Conjecture: The upper bound of the multiplicities of its zeros is an explicit quadratic function of the number of terms and the degree difference, and the bound is given exactly by fewnomial theory and attained. (quadratic bound of exponential zero multiplicities)
