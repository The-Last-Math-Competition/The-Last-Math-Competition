# 00000000177

**English.** Definition: The joint arithmetic structure of ζ(5)/π⁵ and ζ(3)/π³. Conjecture: The numbers 1, ζ(3)/π³, ζ(5)/π⁵ form a Q-linearly independent triple; the independence is equivalent to the vanishing of the period relations between modular forms of weights 5 and 3, and that vanishing is decided by an explicit test of Hodge positions on the Eichler–Shimura variety. (weight 3–5 zeta triple)

**中文。** 定义：ζ(5)/π⁵ 与 ζ(3)/π³ 的联合算术结构。猜想：1、ζ(3)/π³、ζ(5)/π⁵ 构成 Q-线性无关三元组；无关性等价于权 5 与权 3 的模形式周期关系的消没，消没由 Eichler–Shimura 流形的 Hodge 位置的显式检验。（三五次 zeta 三元组）
