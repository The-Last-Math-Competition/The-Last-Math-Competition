**PV-62 ★** — source: 24_偏微分方程与变分法.md

**English.** Definition: The infinity Laplacian is the equation of absolutely minimizing Lipschitz extensions. Conjecture: The total length measure of the streamline characteristics of absolute minimizers is bounded by the diameter times a constant; and the measure-bound constant is a function of dimension. (streamline measure bound)

**中文。** 定义：无穷 Laplacian 为绝对极小元的方程。猜想：绝对极小元的分级线特征长度总测度不超过直径乘常数；且测度界的常数为维数函数。（分级线测度界）
