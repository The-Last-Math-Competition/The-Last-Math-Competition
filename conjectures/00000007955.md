# 00000007955

**English.** Definition: The spectral curve count of the tropical limit of p-reduced dissections: the footpoint correspondence of Gromov-Witten type tropical curve multiplicities (the dual of Welschinger weights). Conjecture: Each ray of the tropical limit corresponds to a cusp of the spectral curve, the tropical length of the cusp tangent vector equaling its log-normalization factor (the magnitude of the logarithm of the normalization factor); and the signed count of rays equals the corresponding Hurwitz number, the sign controlled by the parity of the Legendre term at the cusp; and the spectral decomposition of Hurwitz numbers is a Fourier truncation of floor(S/pi) (a tropical version of the Boutet de Monnel conjecture). (tropical spectral curve Hurwitz duality)

**中文。** 定义：p-reduced 分解的 tropical 极限的谱曲线计数：Gromov–Witten 型的 tropical 曲线的重数的脚标对应（Welschinger 重的对偶）。猜想：tropical 极限的每条射线对应谱曲线的一个尖点，尖点的切向量的 tropical 长度等于其 log-规范因子（规范因子的对数的量级）；且射线的计数带符号和恒等于对应的 Hurwitz 数，符号由尖点处的 Legendre 项的奇偶控制；Hurwitz 数的谱化分解为 floor(S/π) 的 Fourier 截断（Boutet de Monvel 猜想的 tropical 版）。
