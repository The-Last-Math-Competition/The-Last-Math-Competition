# 00000004753

**English.** Definition: The free Poisson law is the Marchenko-Pastur distribution. Conjecture: The stability constant of convolution powers always follows a half-exponent power law in the scale parameter, and the stability domain contains all freely infinitely divisible laws. (half-exponent stability of MP convolution powers)

**中文。** 定义：自由泊松律指 Marchenko–Pastur 分布。猜想：卷积幂的稳定常数恒按尺度参数的半指数幂律；且稳定域含一切自由无穷可分。（MP 卷积幂半指数稳定）
