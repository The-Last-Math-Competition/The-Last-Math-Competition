# 00000001841

**RM-01 ★** — source: 47_随机矩阵II.md

**English.** Definition: The bulk gap probability of the CUE is the probability of no eigenvalues in an arc of length s/N on the unit circle. Conjecture: The intermediate-scale asymptotics (s ≫ 1) is exp(−c(1)·s²) with constant c(1) = π²/6; the correction factor is an explicit ratio of Barnes G functions.

**中文。** 定义：CUE 的体间隙概率指单位圆上长度 s/N 的无特征值概率。猜想：中间标度 s≫1 的渐近为 exp(−c(1)·s²),常数 c(1)=π²/6;修正因子为 Barnes G 函数的显式比。
