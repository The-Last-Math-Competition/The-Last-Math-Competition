# 00000002013

**English.** Definition: The Calderón problem recovers equation coefficients from the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map. Conjecture: Uniqueness for anisotropic partial data is given by the light-geodesic injectivity condition on an open boundary subset; and the condition includes geodesic convexity of the open set. (Calderon light-geodesic injectivity condition)

**中文。** 定义：Calderón 问题指由 Dirichlet-to-Neumann 映射恢复方程系数。猜想：各向异性部分数据的唯一性由边界开集的光测地单射条件给出；且该条件包含开集的测地凸性。（Calderón 光测地单射条件）
