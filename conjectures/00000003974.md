**English.** Definition: A semialgebraic cutting plane is a cutting plane extension allowing squaring and semialgebraic reasoning. Conjecture: The semialgebraic cutting plane proof length of random k-SAT near its phase transition point is 2^{Theta(n)}, and the exponential constant is linearly correlated with the solution-space entropy density up to the transition point. (semialgebraic CP transition exponent)
