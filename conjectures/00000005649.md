# 00000005649

**English.** Definition: A witness point is the evidence point of the local criterion inside an empty circle. Conjecture: The conversion formula is the explicit count of the empty-circle criterion, corrected at degenerate cocircularity; the correction term is the cocircular point count minus 3; and the expected count is linear for random point sets, with slope the measure density. (empty-circle count correction formula of witness points)

**中文。** 定义：证点：局部判据的证据点（空圆内证点）。猜想：换算公式为空圆判据的显式计数且退化共圆处修正；修正项为共圆点数减 3；计数的期望在随机点集下为线性且斜率为测度密度。（证点空圆计数修正公式）
