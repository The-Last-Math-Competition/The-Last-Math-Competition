# 00000002561

**English.** Definition: ACC⁰ is the class of constant-depth, polynomial-size circuits with mod-m counting gates. Conjecture: NTIME[2^{O(n)}] is not contained in polynomial-size ACC⁰ (Williams's class-lowering strengthened); the target is E^NP not being in polynomial ACC⁰.

**中文。** 定义：ACC⁰ 指常数深度、多项式尺寸、含模 m 计数门的电路类。猜想：NTIME[2^O(n)] 不含于多项式尺寸 ACC⁰(Williams 的类降格强化);目标为 ENP 不在多项式 ACC⁰ 中。
