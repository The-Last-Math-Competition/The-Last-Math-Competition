# 00000006452

**English.** Definition: Leakage is the leakage error of spectral acceleration methods. Conjecture: The conversion constant is the explicit exponential of leakage decay given by the spectral parameter; the exponent is a linear function of the window width; and the kernel of the leakage is the discretization of the Poisson kernel. (spectral-parameter exponential conversion of leakage decay)
