# 00000003983

**English.** Definition: Degree-4 SOS is the certificate system of sums of squares of quartic polynomials. Conjecture: The approximation ratio of degree-4 SOS for the independence number of 3-uniform hypergraphs is at most 2 - epsilon with epsilon > 1/16, and epsilon is attained by an explicit spectral-gap construction of pseudodistributions. (SOS4 hypergraph approximation gap)

**中文。** 定义：度 4 SOS 指四次平方和证书系统。猜想：度 4 SOS 对 3-均匀超图独立数的近似比不超过 2 − ε 且 ε > 1/16,ε 由伪分布的显式谱间隙构造达到。（SOS4 超图近似比间隙）
