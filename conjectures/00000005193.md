# 00000005193

**ST2-23 ◆** — source: 158_高阶范畴II_稳定无穷范畴.md

**English.** Definition: The cobordism hypothesis classifies extended topological field theories by fully dualizable objects. Conjecture: The homotopy groups of the classification-value space in the framed case are an explicit quotient of the stable homotopy of the classifying space; the quotient table in low dimension (at most 3) is finite, with the kernel of the quotient given by the vanishing of twists over the circle; and the group of two-dimensional framed theories is the quotient of four copies of Z. (low-dimensional homotopy quotients of the framed cobordism hypothesis)

**中文。** 定义：余边界假设：扩展拓扑场论由全对偶对象分类。猜想：框架情形的分类值空间的同伦群为分类空间稳定同伦的显式商；低维（不超过 3）的商表为有限且商的核由圆上 twist 的消没给出；二维框架理论的群恰为 Z 的四拷贝之商。（余边框架低维同伦商）
