# 00000004421

**English.** Definition: TASEP is the totally asymmetric simple exclusion process, and labeled particles are the particles numbered by initial order. Conjecture: The second-order correction constant of labeled particle positions is an explicit integral of a GUE-type functional, and the constant is universal for step initial data and admits no further correction. (TASEP universal second-order constant)

**中文。** 定义：TASEP 指全不对称简单排斥过程,标签粒子指按初始次序标号的粒子。猜想：标签粒子位置的次阶修正常数为 GUE 型泛函的显式积分,常数在台阶初值普适且不可再修。（TASEP 标签次阶普适常数）
