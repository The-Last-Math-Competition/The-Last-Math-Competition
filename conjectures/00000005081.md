**English.** Definition: Hecke eigensheaves are the eigen-sheaves of Hecke operators. Conjecture: The irreducible eigensheaves of GL_2 are classified explicitly by the initial sheaves of irreducible local systems, and the classification table is closed for degree sheaves with no exceptional members. (explicit classification table of GL_2 eigensheaves)

**中文。** 定义：Hecke 特征层指 Hecke 算子的本征层。猜想：GL₂ 的不可约特征层由不可约局部系统的初值层显式分类,分类表对次数层封闭且无例外成员。（GL2 特征层显式分类表）
