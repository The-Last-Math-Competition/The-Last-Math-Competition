**HT2-07 ★** — source: 126_组合代数拓扑_高阶树.md

**English.** Definition: A unimodular triangulation is one all of whose chain matrices are unimodular. Conjecture: Closure of tree counts under divisibility by the number of points holds for unimodular triangulations in dimension at most 2, and the smallest counterexample in dimension 3 is the second barycentric subdivision of projective space. (counterexample dimension of unimodular divisibility)

**中文。** 定义：单模剖分指全体链矩阵均可幺模的剖分。猜想：单模剖分树计数对点数的整除封闭性在维数不超过 2 时成立,维数 3 的最小反例为射影空间的第二个重心剖分。（单模整除反例维数）
